App 为什么要申请“权限”（摄像头、定位等）？

Why do apps ask for 'permissions' like camera or location?

权限是 App 调用手机功能的许可，比如拍照要摄像头、导航要定位。但有些 App 要了用不上的权限就有风险，装好后在设置里收回不必要的，更安全。
Permissions let an app use phone features—camera for photos, location for maps. But apps asking for unneeded permissions are risky; revoke extras in settings.

什么是“广告追踪”，怎么减少它？

What is 'ad tracking' and how do you reduce it?

广告追踪用 Cookie 或设备 ID 记录你逛过哪些网站、搜过什么，好给你推精准广告。可在系统里开“限制广告跟踪”、清 Cookie、用防跟踪浏览器来减少。
Ad tracking uses cookies or device IDs to log sites you visit and searches, then targets ads. Turn on 'limit ad tracking', clear cookies and use anti-tracking browsers to cut it.

什么是“开源许可证”（如 GPL）？

What is an 'open-source license' like the GPL?

许可证规定你能怎么用开源代码。GPL 要求你改完再发布时也开源；MIT 等则很宽松。选错许可证可能让公司不小心公开了自己的代码，所以很关键。
A license says how you may use open code. GPL requires you open your changes too; MIT is permissive. Picking wrong can force a company to expose its own code, so it matters.

电脑病毒和木马有什么不一样？

How do computer viruses and trojans differ?

病毒会把自己附到别的文件上、自动复制传播；木马则装成有用程序骗你安装，偷偷开后门让坏人控制。两者都要靠不乱点链接、装杀毒来防。
A virus attaches to files and spreads by itself; a trojan disguises as useful software to trick install and opens a backdoor. Both are avoided by not clicking blind links and using antivirus.

免费 App 不收钱，它靠什么赚钱？

Free apps charge nothing—how do they make money?

免费 App 常靠广告、内购（买皮肤、去广告）或把你的数据用于推荐来赚钱。天下没有真免费，用之前看看它怎么赢利，留意隐私条款。
Free apps earn via ads, in-app purchases (skins, ad-free) or using your data for recommendations. Nothing is truly free—check how it makes money and the privacy terms.

什么是“开源软件”？

What is 'open-source software'?

开源软件把背后的源代码公开，谁都能看、改、分享，比如火狐浏览器、Linux。因为大家都能检查，漏洞往往修得快，也更可信。
Open-source software publishes its source code so anyone can view, modify and share it—like Firefox or Linux. Many eyes check it, so bugs get fixed fast and it's trustworthy.

什么是“应用商店”，为什么尽量从那里下载？

What is an 'app store' and why download from it?

应用商店是官方审核过的软件市场，里面的 App 大多经过安全检查。从它下载比随便找网页装，更不容易中病毒或被偷信息。
An app store is a curated, vetted market where apps are security-checked. Downloading there is far safer than random web links that may carry malware or data theft.

上网时说的“Cookie”是什么？

What is a 'cookie' when browsing?

Cookie 是网站存在你浏览器里的小纸条，记住你登录过、购物车有啥。它让上网更方便，但也可能被用来跟踪你看了什么。可在设置里清理或拒绝。
A cookie is a small note a site stores in your browser to remember you're logged in or your cart. It's convenient but can track what you viewed; you can clear or block them.

为什么 App 老是提示要“更新”？

Why do apps keep asking to 'update'?

更新会带来新功能、修掉出错的 bug、补上安全漏洞。就像给房子补漏补缺。更新前看一眼改了什么，重要的安全更新最好及时装。
Updates add features, fix bugs and patch security holes—like repairing a house. Check what changed; install important security updates promptly.

什么是“软件”？

What is 'software'?

软件是让硬件（手机、电脑）干活的指令集合，看得见摸不着。微信、游戏、浏览器都是软件；没有软件，机器只是一块没用的铁。
Software is the set of instructions that makes hardware (phone, PC) do things—invisible but essential. WeChat, games, browsers are software; without it, the machine is just dead metal.

为什么要给电脑装杀毒软件？

Why install antivirus on a computer?

杀毒软件像门卫，扫描下载的文件、拦住可疑程序，防止病毒偷密码或搞坏电脑。手机也类似，别乱装来路不明的 App。
Antivirus is like a guard that scans downloads and blocks suspicious programs, stopping viruses from stealing passwords or breaking the PC. On phones, avoid unknown apps.
