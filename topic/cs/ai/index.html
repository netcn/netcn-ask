人工智能是怎么学会认出小猫的？

How does AI learn to recognize a cat?

它看了成千上万张标好『这是猫』的照片，反复调整内部参数，直到能从新图片里找出猫的特征，就像小朋友看多了就认识了。
It looks at thousands of photos labeled 'this is a cat' and keeps adjusting its internal settings until it can spot cat features in new pictures—much like a child learning by seeing many examples.
