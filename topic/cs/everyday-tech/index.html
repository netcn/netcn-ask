为什么手机信号满格却上不了网，可能遇到了伪基站？

Why might you have full signal but no internet—could it be a fake base station?

伪基站会假装成运营商信号把你的手机“吸”过去，拦截短信、伪造来电或发诈骗信息。信号满却收不到正常短信、频繁掉线时要警惕，尽量少用手机并离开该区域。
A fake base station impersonates a carrier to hijack your phone, intercepting SMS or sending fraud. If you have bars but miss normal texts and drop often, be wary and move away.

什么是 NFC，为什么手机碰一下就能付款？

What is NFC, and why can phones pay by tapping?

NFC 是极短距离（几厘米）的无线技术。手机和收银机轻轻一碰就交换付款信息，比扫码更快，但需要双方都支持 NFC。
NFC is a very short-range (a few cm) wireless tech. Tap phone to terminal and they swap payment info—faster than scanning, but both sides must support NFC.

量子比特和经典比特有什么本质不同？

What is fundamentally different about a qubit versus a classical bit?

经典比特只有 0 或 1 两个确定状态；量子比特是量子态，可以处于 |0⟩ 和 |1⟩ 的叠加，测量时才以一定概率坍缩为其中之一。多个量子比特还可纠缠，使整体状态空间指数增长。正是这些性质让量子算法在某些问题上有独特优势——但也意味着结果本质是概率性的，需要反复测量和纠错，量子计算机并非在一切任务上都更快。
A classical bit is deterministically 0 or 1; a qubit is a quantum state in a superposition of |0⟩ and |1⟩, collapsing to one outcome with some probability only upon measurement. Qubits can also become entangled, making the joint state space grow exponentially. These properties give quantum algorithms unique advantages on certain problems—but results are inherently probabilistic, needing repeated measurement and error correction, and quantum computers are not faster at everything.

物联网（IoT）是什么？智能家居怎么工作？

What is the Internet of Things (IoT), and how do smart homes work?

物联网是把传感器、家电、设备接入互联网，让它们采集数据、接受远程控制。智能家居里，传感器（温度、人体感应）和开关通过 Wi-Fi、蓝牙、ZigBee 连到网关或云平台，你的指令和自动化规则（“湿度低就开加湿器”）在那里被解释执行。好处是自动化，风险是每个设备都可能是网络攻击面。
The IoT connects sensors, appliances, and gadgets to the internet so they collect data and accept remote control. In a smart home, sensors (temperature, motion) and switches link via Wi-Fi, Bluetooth, or ZigBee to a hub or cloud where your commands and automation rules (“if humidity drops, turn on the humidifier”) are interpreted and executed. The upside is automation; the risk is every device becomes an attack surface.

为什么有的界面好用、有的难用？“可用性”指什么？

Why are some interfaces easy to use and others not? What is usability?

可用性衡量一个产品对目标用户是否有效、高效、满意地完成任务。好界面遵循学习成本低、可记忆、容错、可恢复等原则，比如一致的操作、清晰的反馈、把常用操作放大。它背后是人机交互（HCI）研究，连按钮大小都有规律——费茨定律说明目标越大越近，点击越快越准。
Usability measures how effectively, efficiently, and satisfactorily a product lets target users finish tasks. Good interfaces follow principles like low learning cost, memorability, error tolerance, and recovery—consistent actions, clear feedback, and prominent placement of frequent operations. Behind it lies human-computer interaction (HCI) research, down to the physics of buttons: Fitts's law shows larger, closer targets are clicked faster and more accurately.

What is airplane mode?

Airplane mode switches off all wireless signals at once—calls, data, WiFi, Bluetooth—so the phone won't interfere on a plane, and it also saves battery or charges faster.

什么是屏幕分辨率？

What is screen resolution?

分辨率是屏幕上横竖各有多少个小亮点（像素）。同样大小，像素越多画面越细腻清楚，比如 1920×1080 比 1280×720 更清晰。
Resolution is how many tiny dots (pixels) a screen has across and down. More pixels in the same size means sharper images—1920×1080 is clearer than 1280×720.

脑机接口（BCI）是怎么把"想法"变成机器指令的？

How does a brain-computer interface (BCI) turn "thoughts" into machine commands?

BCI 用电极采集脑电信号（头皮 EEG 或皮层电极），经预处理去噪、提取特征（如运动想象时 mu 节律的变化），再用分类器或解码器把特征模式映射为光标移动、字符拼写或机械臂动作等指令，最后通过视觉或触觉反馈让用户学会调节脑信号，形成闭环。
BCIs acquire brain signals with electrodes (scalp EEG or cortical arrays), preprocess and denoise them, extract features such as mu-rhythm changes during motor imagery, then map the feature patterns to commands like cursor movement, character spelling or robotic-arm actions via classifiers or decoders. Visual or tactile feedback closes the loop so users learn to modulate their signals.

云计算的“虚拟化”是什么？一台服务器怎么变成很多台？

What is virtualization in cloud computing, and how does one server become many?

虚拟化用虚拟机监控程序（hypervisor）把一台物理机的 CPU、内存、磁盘切成多个相互隔离的虚拟机，每个虚拟机像独立电脑，可装自己的操作系统。云厂商靠它把硬件资源池化、动态分配给客户，随开随关。容器（如 Docker）是更轻的替代，共享宿主内核，只隔离应用环境。
Virtualization uses a hypervisor to carve one physical machine's CPU, memory, and disk into isolated virtual machines, each behaving like its own computer with its own OS. Cloud providers pool hardware this way and allocate it to customers on demand, creating and deleting VMs freely. Containers (like Docker) are a lighter alternative that share the host kernel and isolate only the application environment.

2.4G 和 5G 的 WiFi 有什么不一样？

What is the difference between 2.4GHz and 5GHz WiFi?

2.4G 穿墙远、速度慢；5G 速度快、但隔一堵墙信号就弱。打游戏、看高清视频靠近路由器时用 5G，隔着好几间房用 2.4G 更稳。
2.4GHz reaches farther through walls but is slower; 5GHz is faster but weak past one wall. Use 5GHz near the router for HD video, 2.4GHz across rooms.

快充会伤电池吗？

Does fast charging damage the battery?

快充会放热，而高温才是电池老化的主因。正规手机的快充有温控和限流保护，偶尔用没关系；长期边快充边打游戏、让手机很烫，才更伤电池。
Fast charging makes heat, and heat—not speed—is what ages batteries. Certified fast chargers limit current and temperature, so occasional use is fine; sustained heat while gaming is what harms it.

AR 和 VR 有什么区别？

What is the difference between AR and VR?

VR（虚拟现实）用头显完全取代你看到的现实，把你放进生成的虚拟世界，头部追踪让画面随视角变化；AR（增强现实）则在真实画面上叠加数字信息，比如手机地图的路标、试戴眼镜的效果。技术上 AR 需要实时感知环境（SLAM 定位），VR 更需要高刷新率、低延迟来防晕动。
VR (virtual reality) replaces your view of the real world entirely, placing you inside a generated environment where head tracking changes the scene with your gaze; AR (augmented reality) overlays digital information on top of the real world, like map arrows or virtual eyeglasses on your phone. Technically AR needs real-time environment perception (SLAM), while VR demands high refresh rates and low latency to prevent motion sickness.

游戏和电影里的"物理引擎"是怎样让物体看起来真实运动的？

How do physics engines in games and movies make objects move realistically?

物理引擎按固定时间步循环做两件事：用牛顿运动定律积分更新物体的位置与速度（重力、弹力、碰撞产生的冲量），再用碰撞检测找出并纠正重叠。为了性能，游戏常用简化的碰撞体（球体、盒体），限定步长并采用宽相位/窄相位两级检测，让上千个物体也能实时模拟。
Physics engines loop at fixed time steps doing two jobs: integrating position and velocity from Newton's laws (gravity, spring forces, collision impulses), and collision detection that finds and resolves overlaps. For performance, games use simplified collision shapes such as spheres and boxes, fixed timesteps, and broad-phase/narrow-phase detection so thousands of bodies simulate in real time.

手机充电到 100% 要马上拔掉吗？

Should you unplug your phone the moment it hits 100%?

现代手机充满后会自动停止充电，不必立刻拔。但长期让电池一直满电或一直高温，会稍微加快老化。平时随用随充、别经常用到 0% 最护电池。
Modern phones stop charging at 100%, so you don't need to unplug instantly. But keeping a battery full and hot for long slightly speeds aging. Top up often and avoid draining to 0%.

手机拍夜景为什么要举着几秒不动，拍出来还比眼睛看到的更亮？

Why must you hold a phone still for several seconds in night mode, and why is the photo brighter than the scene looked?

手机传感器很小，单张长曝光会因手抖而模糊，所以夜景模式改为连拍十几张较短曝光的照片，再逐张对齐叠加。多帧平均能把随机噪声降低约帧数的平方根倍，等效于一次长曝光却避开了运动模糊，举着不动是为了让对齐算法有稳定的参考。随后的色调映射会大幅提亮暗部并压缩高光，因此成片比肉眼观感更亮、细节更多，而拍摄期间移动的人容易出现重影。
A phone sensor is tiny, and a single long exposure would blur from hand shake, so night mode captures a burst of a dozen or more short frames and aligns and merges them. Averaging N frames cuts random noise by roughly the square root of N, giving the light-gathering of a long exposure without motion blur, and holding still gives the alignment stage something stable to work with. Tone mapping then lifts the shadows and compresses highlights, which is why the result looks brighter and more detailed than the scene did, and why anyone who moved during the burst comes out ghosted.

为什么手指一点屏幕就有反应，戴着厚手套点却没用？

Why does a screen respond to a bare fingertip but ignore a thick glove?

手机屏幕下面有一层看不见的细网格，一直在测量每个交叉点上能存住多少电。手指里有带盐的水，能导电，靠近时会把一点电引走，网格发现哪个点变了，就知道你点在哪里。厚手套是绝缘的，把手指挡得太远，网格察觉不到变化，所以没反应；触屏手套的指尖织进了导电的丝线，于是又能用了。
Under the glass there is an invisible grid that constantly measures how much electric charge each crossing point can hold. A fingertip contains salty water and conducts, so bringing it close pulls a little charge away, and the grid notices which point changed and reports where you touched. A thick glove is an insulator that keeps your finger too far away for the grid to sense any change, so nothing happens — touchscreen gloves work again because conductive thread is woven into their fingertips.

视频通话为什么是画面卡顿、声音断续，而不是像慢放一样整体变慢？

Why does a video call stutter and clip audio instead of simply playing back in slow motion?

实时通话必须按时播出，晚到的数据就失去价值了。接收端只维持一个几十到几百毫秒的抖动缓冲，用来吸收网络延迟波动；一旦某个包迟到或丢失超过这个窗口，播放器就直接跳过它，于是出现画面停顿、马赛克或声音断裂。相反，看点播视频可以提前缓冲几十秒，所以宁可转圈等待也能保证连续播放——这正是实时通信与流媒体的根本取舍。
A live call must play out on schedule, and data arriving late is worthless. The receiver keeps only a jitter buffer of tens to a few hundred milliseconds to absorb variation in network delay, and anything later than that window is skipped outright, which appears as a frozen frame, blockiness or a clipped syllable. On-demand video, by contrast, buffers many seconds ahead, so it shows a loading spinner instead of glitching — the fundamental trade-off between real-time communication and streaming.
