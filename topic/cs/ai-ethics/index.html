什么是 AI 对齐？

What is AI alignment?

AI 对齐指让系统的目标和行为与人类的真实意图和价值观一致，避免它为完成任务而采取有害手段。难点在于把模糊的人类偏好准确转化为可优化的目标。这是安全部署强大 AI 的核心研究课题。
AI alignment means making a system's goals and behavior match human intent and values, avoiding harmful shortcuts taken just to finish a task. The hard part is turning vague human preferences into an optimizable objective. It is central to safely deploying powerful AI.

公平性在算法上如何度量？

How is fairness measured for algorithms?

公平性常用统计指标衡量，如不同群体间的准确率、假阳性率是否接近（均等机会），或预测阳性比例是否一致（人口均等）。不同定义之间常互相冲突，难以同时满足。选择哪种取决于具体场景的价值观。
Fairness is often measured by statistics like equal accuracy or false-positive rates across groups (equal opportunity), or equal positive-prediction rates (demographic parity). These definitions frequently conflict and cannot all hold at once. The choice depends on the values of the specific context.

为什么“AI 为什么这么判断”是个难题？

Why is “why did the AI decide this” so hard?

深度神经网络把输入经过成千上万参数的非线性变换后输出结果，内部是高维中间表示，没有一个变量对应“可读的理由”，所以被称为黑箱。可解释性方法分两类：全局的（如注意力权重、特征重要性）和局部的（对单个预测给出归因，如 LIME、SHAP）。在医疗、信贷等高风险场景，可解释性直接关系到责任认定和监管合规。
A deep network pushes input through thousands of nonlinear parameters into high-dimensional intermediate representations where no single variable reads as a “reason,” which is why it is called a black box. Explainability splits into global methods (attention weights, feature importance) and local ones attributing individual predictions (LIME, SHAP). In high-stakes settings like medicine and credit, explainability ties directly to accountability and regulatory compliance.

用 AI 生成的内容反复训练下一代 AI，会发生什么？

What happens when each new generation of AI is trained mainly on the previous generation's output?

会出现模型崩塌：每一代都从上一代的采样输出中学习，采样误差与拟合误差逐代累积，分布的尾部（罕见事件、少数群体的表达）先被抹平，最终连主体部分也退化成方差极小的少数模式。Nature 2024 年发表的实验显示，即使每一代数据量充足，纯用递归生成数据训练的语言模型也会在几代之内开始输出无意义的重复内容。这意味着可溯源的真实人类语料具有长期价值，实践上需要保留原始语料、每轮混入真实数据，并对训练集做来源标识与过滤。
The outcome is model collapse: each generation learns from the previous one's samples, so sampling and approximation errors compound, first erasing the tails of the distribution — rare events and minority phrasings — and eventually degenerating even the bulk into a few low-variance modes. Experiments published in Nature in 2024 showed that language models trained purely on recursively generated data begin emitting meaningless repetition within a handful of generations, even with plentiful data each round. The implication is that provenance-tracked human data holds lasting value, so practitioners preserve original corpora, blend real data into every round, and filter training sets by source.

深度伪造（Deepfake）是怎么做出来的？为什么难防范？

How are deepfakes made, and why are they hard to stop?

深度伪造用生成对抗网络（GAN）等模型训练出能生成逼真假视频、假声音的网络：生成器不断造假，判别器不断鉴别，互相逼对方变强。它难防范是因为人眼和耳朵难以分辨，且生成成本越来越低；应对靠检测模型、内容来源认证和平台治理，本质上是一场“造假—检测”的军备竞赛。
Deepfakes are made with models like generative adversarial networks (GANs): a generator keeps forging, a discriminator keeps detecting, and each pushes the other to improve until fake video and audio look and sound real. They are hard to stop because humans struggle to tell, and the cost keeps dropping; defenses rely on detection models, provenance verification, and platform governance—an arms race between forgery and detection.

What privacy issues arise from AI training data?

C2PA 这类「内容凭证」标准能可靠证明一张图片的来龙去脉吗？

Can content-provenance standards such as C2PA reliably prove where an image came from?

C2PA 的做法是给媒体文件附加一份经数字签名的清单，记录拍摄设备、编辑软件和每一步修改，任何后续改动都要追加新的签名断言，形成可验证的链条。它能证明内容自某个签名点以来未被篡改，也能标注是否由 AI 生成，但对完全没有签名的文件无话可说，而截图、转码或社交平台的重压缩都会把凭证剥掉。因此它是提高伪造成本的工具，而不是鉴伪的万灵药，只有相机厂商、编辑软件和分发平台端到端支持才真正有效。
C2PA attaches a cryptographically signed manifest to a media file recording the capture device, editing tools and each modification, with every later change appending another signed assertion to build a verifiable chain. It can prove that content is unaltered since a given signing point and can flag AI generation, but it says nothing about unsigned files, and screenshots, transcoding or platform recompression strip the credentials entirely. So it raises the cost of undetected manipulation rather than detecting fakes, and only works when cameras, editors and distribution platforms all support it end to end.

一个「总体准确率 99%」的人脸识别系统，为什么对不同人群可能极不公平？

Why can a face recognition system with 99% overall accuracy still be grossly unfair across demographic groups?

总体准确率是各子群误差按其在数据中的比例加权的平均值，样本多的群体主导这个数字，样本少的群体即使误差高十倍也几乎不影响总分。Buolamwini 与 Gebru 2018 年的审计发现，商用性别分类在浅肤色男性上错误率不到 1%，在深肤色女性上高达 34.7%；NIST 对上百个算法的大规模评测同样确认，多数系统在不同人种、性别、年龄间的假阳性率相差一到两个数量级。因此评估必须分组报告误差，并区分假阳性与假阴性——手机解锁的假阴性只是不便，而比对检索中的假阳性可能把无关的人牵连进来。
Overall accuracy is an average of per-group error rates weighted by how common each group is in the data, so well-represented groups dominate the figure and a small subgroup can carry ten times the error without moving it. Buolamwini and Gebru's 2018 audit found commercial gender classifiers erring under 1% on lighter-skinned men but 34.7% on darker-skinned women, and NIST's evaluation of over a hundred algorithms confirmed false-positive rates differing by one to two orders of magnitude across race, sex and age. Fair evaluation therefore requires disaggregated error reporting and separating false positives from false negatives — a false negative at phone unlock is an inconvenience, while a false positive in an identification search can implicate the wrong person.
