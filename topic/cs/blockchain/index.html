比特币的最长链规则把分叉中落败的区块彻底丢弃,这带来了怎样的激励扭曲?GHOST 规则如何修正?

Bitcoin's longest-chain rule discards losing fork blocks entirely — what incentive distortion follows, and how does the GHOST rule correct it?

被丢弃的陈旧块让诚实矿工的算力白白损失,而大矿池产出陈旧块的比例更低,获得相对优势,这正为自私挖矿攻击提供了温床。GHOST 在选择主链时把陈旧块计入子树权重并给其矿工发放奖励,显著降低高速出块下的孤块率,以太坊据此在更短出块间隔下维持安全性。
Discarded stale blocks waste honest miners' work, while large pools suffer proportionally fewer orphans and gain a relative edge — fertile ground for selfish-mining attacks. GHOST weighs subtrees including stale blocks when selecting the main chain and rewards their miners, cutting orphan rates at high block frequencies, which is how Ethereum stays secure with faster block times.

#区块链 #共识协议

比特币轻节点只下载区块头也能确认收款,Merkle 证明在其中扮演什么角色?

Bitcoin light clients can confirm incoming payments while downloading nothing but block headers — what role do Merkle proofs play in this?

每个区块头的 80 字节里只存放交易树的根哈希,付款方随附一条 Merkle 路径,轻节点沿路重算哈希即可确认该笔交易确实被写入此块,开销仅为 O(log n) 次哈希、几百字节流量。轻节点因此不必存储或验证全部交易,只需跟踪最长链的累计难度来判断确认深度。这种以多数算力为信任前提的取舍,正是中本聪白皮书第 8 节描述的简化支付验证机制。
An 80-byte header carries only the Merkle root, so a payer hands over an authentication path and the node recomputes roughly log n hashes—about 500 bytes—to confirm the transaction is committed in that block. Light nodes therefore skip storing and executing all transactions, instead tracking cumulative difficulty along the longest chain to gauge confirmation depth. This trade-off of trusting majority hash power rather than full validation is exactly the simplified payment verification scheme of Section 8 of the Bitcoin whitepaper.

#默克尔树 #简化支付验证

掌握全网一半以下算力的攻击者究竟能做什么、又做不到什么?

With less than half of total hash power, what can a blockchain attacker actually do—and what stays out of reach?

共识规则由每个全节点独立校验,算力再大也造不出伪造签名或凭空增发的有效块,这类无效区块会被网络直接丢弃。多数算力真正买到的是对排序权的短期垄断:隐瞒区块使最近若干区块作废(链重组),以及对自己的历史交易反悔实施双花。攻击偷不走他人的私钥资产,却能摧毁市场信心,2019 年 Ethereum Classic 遭遇的 51% 攻击令交易所损失数百万美元即是实例。
Every full node independently validates consensus rules, so no amount of hash power mints valid-looking blocks with forged signatures or inflated supply—such blocks are simply discarded. What majority hashing actually buys is short-term monopoly on ordering: withholding blocks to orphan recent competitors (chain reorganization) and reversing one's own past transactions in a double-spend. The attack steals nobody's keys yet torches market confidence, as when Ethereum Classic suffered 51% attacks in 2019 costing exchanges millions of dollars.
