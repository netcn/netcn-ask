什么是电脑程序？

What is a computer program?

程序是一张写给电脑的菜谱，一步一步教它做菜。没有程序，电脑就不知道该干啥。
A program is a recipe written for the computer, teaching it step by step. Without a program, the computer would not know what to do.

电脑怎么记住我们看过的动画片？

How does a computer remember the cartoons we watched?

电脑把喜欢的画面藏进它的小仓库里，关掉也不丢。下次打开，它就又拿给你看。
The computer hides your favorite pictures in its little storage, and they stay even when it is off. Next time you open it, it shows them to you again.

电脑按下开关后是怎么醒过来的？

How does a computer wake up after you press the power?

开机后，电脑先跑一段小检查程序看看零件好不好。确认没问题，再把操作系统请出来站岗。
On startup the computer runs a small check to see if parts are well, then brings up the operating system to take charge. Only after that can you use it.

电脑的内存和硬盘有什么不一样？

What is the difference between memory and storage?

内存像你写作业的桌面，关机就清空；硬盘像书包，关了机东西还在。桌面越大，同时做的事越多。
Memory is like your desk where you work and clears when off; storage is like a backpack that keeps things even when off. A bigger desk lets you do more at once.

How do pictures appear on the screen?

Under the screen hides a little net that counts your fingers; when you tap, it reports the spot. The computer opens something right there.

电脑是怎么把字母变成能传送的数字的？

How does a computer turn letters into numbers it can send?

电脑用一本代码表，把每个字母换成专属数字，比如 A 是 65。这样文字就能像数数一样被电脑搬来搬去。
The computer uses a code table that swaps each letter for its own number, like A is 65. That lets text be moved around by the computer just like counting.

处理器（CPU）是怎么当电脑大脑的？

How does the processor (CPU) act as the computer's brain?

处理器是电脑里最爱动脑的小方块，每秒做几十亿次微小计算。它把每个任务拆开，排好队一个一个解决。
The processor is the busy little square that does billions of tiny calculations each second. It splits every task and solves them one by one in order.

电脑为什么会听我的话?我按字母A它就知道?

How does my computer listen when I press the letter A?

键盘下面有一排小开关。你按下A,开关就告诉电脑:A来啦!电脑用只有它懂的电信号飞快地查表,然后在屏幕上画出A。这一切只用眨眼的万分之一时间。
Little switches live under every key. Press A and the switch tells the computer: A is here! It checks its code super fast and draws an A on screen — all in one ten-thousandth of a blink.
