网址（链接地址）是什么？

What is a web address (URL)?

网址是网站的门牌号，像家里的地址一样。把它输进浏览器，就能找到那个网站。
A web address is the house number of a website, just like your home address. Type it into the browser and you reach that site.

手机上的小程序（App）是什么？

What is an app on a phone?

App 是手机里的小玩具或小工具，点一下就能画画、听歌或算数。每个 App 都会做一件特别的事。
An app is a tiny toy or tool inside the phone that you tap to draw, sing, or count. Each app does one special thing.

How does an email reach a friend?

你写好的信坐上互联网的小火车，叮咚一声跑到朋友的邮箱里。它比纸信快好多好多。

网址前面的小锁和 HTTPS 是什么？

What is the little lock and HTTPS before a web address?

HTTPS 给路上消息加了一层隐形信封，只有对方能拆。小锁亮着，说明你的话不会被路人偷看。
HTTPS wraps messages in an invisible envelope that only the other side can open. When the lock shows, your words can't be peeked at by passers-by.

How does a video call show far-away people on screen?

路由器是做什么的？

What does a router do?

路由器是家里网络的小邮差，把消息分给每部手机和电脑。没有它，大家就抢不着网。
A router is the little postman of your home network, handing messages to every phone and computer. Without it, nobody gets the internet.

我们发的消息是怎么被切成小包裹旅行的？

How are our messages split into little packets that travel?

长消息会被切成很多小包裹，每包写上收件地址分头走。到了终点，电脑再把它们拼回原样。
A long message is cut into many small packets, each labeled with the destination and sent by different paths. At the end the computer stitches them back together.

What is social media and how do posts reach everyone?

Wi-Fi 是什么，怎么让电脑上网？

What is Wi-Fi and how does it connect us?

Wi-Fi 像看不见的小翅膀，把网上消息变成飞进屋里的风。电脑接住风，就能和你远方的朋友说话。
Wi-Fi is like invisible little wings that turn internet messages into a breeze flying into your room. The computer catches the breeze and talks to far-away friends.

云存储是什么，东西存在哪里？

What is cloud storage and where do things go?

云存储是把照片存在别处的大电脑里，不是真的天上云。这样换手机也能拿到自己的画。
Cloud storage keeps your photos on big computers far away, not in the sky. So you can get your pictures even on a new phone.

我们打的网址是怎么变成 IP 地址的？

How does the web address turn into an IP address?

一个叫 DNS 的电话本把网址翻译成数字门牌号。电脑拿着号码，才找得到那台服务器。
A phone book called DNS translates the web address into a number address. With that number the computer can find the right server.
