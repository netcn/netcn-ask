Are online strangers safe?

People online may hide their real face, so we should not trust them easily. Always ask mom or dad before meeting anyone.

什么是网络钓鱼，怎么识破它？

What is phishing and how do we spot it?

钓鱼是坏人假装成熟人或银行骗你点链接给秘密。真机构不会这样要密码，看到就先问大人。
Phishing is when crooks pretend to be someone you know or a bank to trick you into giving secrets. Real groups never ask for passwords this way; when seen, ask a grown-up.

为什么用完电脑要退出自己的账号？

Why should we log off our account after using a computer?

#密码 #安全

什么是两步验证，为什么更安全？

What is two-step verification and why is it safer?

两步验证在密码之外再加一把临时小锁，比如手机验证码。就算钥匙被偷，坏人还是进不来。
Two-step verification adds a temporary second lock beyond the password, like a phone code. Even if the key is stolen, the crook still can't get in.
