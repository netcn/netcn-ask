什么是 HTTPS 中的公钥基础设施（PKI）？

What is the public key infrastructure (PKI) behind HTTPS?

PKI 由证书颁发机构（CA）、证书和密钥组成。浏览器信任内置 CA，CA 给网站签发证书，从而让你能确认网站身份并安全交换密钥。
PKI consists of certificate authorities (CAs), certificates, and keys. Browsers trust built-in CAs that vouch for sites, letting you verify identity and exchange keys safely.

什么是同态加密（Homomorphic Encryption）？

What is homomorphic encryption?

它允许直接在密文上做计算，结果解密后等于在明文上计算的结果。这样云服务器能帮你算数据却看不到数据内容。
It lets you compute on encrypted data; decrypting the result equals computing on the plaintext. A cloud can process your data without ever seeing it.

什么是 RSA？它基于什么数学难题？

What is RSA, and what math problem does it rely on?

RSA 是一种非对称算法，安全性建立在两个大质数相乘容易、反过来分解乘积极难这一数学难题上。
RSA is an asymmetric algorithm whose security rests on the hardness of factoring large integers: multiplying two big primes is easy, factoring their product is extremely hard.

区块链的工作量证明（PoW）和默克尔树（Merkle Tree）分别起什么作用？

What do proof-of-work (PoW) and Merkle trees do in a blockchain?

PoW 要求矿工做大量计算来争夺记账权，使篡改历史成本极高；默克尔树把大量交易压缩成一个根哈希，方便快速验证交易是否被包含。
PoW makes miners spend heavy computation to append blocks, so rewriting history is prohibitively costly. A Merkle tree compresses many transactions into one root hash for fast inclusion proofs.

量子计算对现有加密有什么威胁？什么是后量子密码？

What threat does quantum computing pose to current encryption, and what is post-quantum cryptography?

足够强的量子计算机能用 Shor 算法快速分解大数和求离散对数，威胁 RSA 与 ECC。后量子密码研究抗量子的新算法，如格密码。
A strong quantum computer could use Shor's algorithm to break RSA and ECC by factoring and discrete logs. Post-quantum cryptography builds quantum-resistant algorithms like lattice-based crypto.

什么是零信任（Zero Trust）安全模型？

What is the Zero Trust security model?

零信任假定网络内外都不可信，每次访问都要验证身份、设备和权限，遵循最小权限原则，不再默认内网即安全。
Zero Trust assumes no implicit trust inside or outside the network; every access is verified for identity, device, and permission, following least privilege.

什么是盐（Salt）和慢哈希（如 bcrypt）？

What are a salt and slow hashing (like bcrypt)?

盐是加在密码上的随机值，让相同密码也得到不同哈希，防止查表破解。bcrypt 等故意算得慢，增加暴力破解成本。
A salt is random data added to a password so identical passwords yield different hashes, defeating lookup tables. Slow hashes like bcrypt raise the cost of brute force.

什么是数字证书和 CA（证书颁发机构）？

What are digital certificates and a CA (certificate authority)?

CA 是受信任的第三方，用它的私钥给网站公钥签名生成证书。浏览器用内置的 CA 公钥验证证书，从而相信网站公钥是真的。
A CA is a trusted third party that signs a site's public key with its private key to make a certificate. Browsers use built-in CA public keys to verify it.

什么是 AES？为什么它被认为很安全？

What is AES, and why is it considered secure?

AES 是一种对称分组加密标准，用 128、192 或 256 位密钥对数据块多次混淆。它经过严格审查、无已知实用漏洞，且密钥空间巨大。
AES is a symmetric block cipher that scrambles data blocks with 128, 192, or 256-bit keys through many rounds. It is heavily vetted, has no known practical break, and a huge key space.

什么是密钥管理？为什么密钥要定期轮换？

What is key management, and why rotate keys regularly?

密钥管理包括生成、存储、分发和销毁密钥。定期轮换能缩小泄露后的影响范围，即使旧密钥被盗，损失也有限。
Key management covers generating, storing, distributing, and destroying keys. Regular rotation limits the blast radius of a leak, so a stolen old key does limited damage.

什么是沙箱（Sandbox）？

What is a sandbox?

沙箱是在隔离环境里运行不可信程序，限制它能访问的文件和网络，就像在玻璃箱里试危险品，坏了也不影响外面。
A sandbox runs untrusted code in an isolated space with limited file and network access, like testing dangerous material inside a glass box so the outside stays safe.

什么是哈希函数？为什么密码不能明文存？

What is a hashing function, and why shouldn't passwords be stored in plain text?

哈希把任意长度输入变成固定长度的指纹，且难以反推原文。存哈希而非明文，即使数据库泄露，攻击者也拿不到真实密码。
A hash turns input into a fixed-length fingerprint that is hard to reverse. Storing hashes, not plain text, means a leak won't expose real passwords.

什么是端到端加密（E2EE）？

What is end-to-end encryption (E2EE)?

端到端加密中，消息只在你的设备和对方设备上加解密，连提供服务的中间服务器也看不到内容，所以服务商无法偷看。
In E2EE, messages are encrypted only on your device and the recipient's, so even the service's servers can't read them; the provider can't peek.

对称加密和非对称加密有什么区别？

What is the difference between symmetric and asymmetric encryption?

对称加密用同一把钥匙加解密，快但难安全传钥匙；非对称用公钥加密、私钥解密，解决了传钥匙问题但更慢。实际常用混合加密。
Symmetric uses one key for both steps, fast but hard to share safely. Asymmetric uses a public key to encrypt and a private key to decrypt, solving sharing but slower. Real systems mix both.
