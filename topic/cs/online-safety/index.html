什么是“撞库”攻击？

What is a 'credential stuffing' (password spraying) attack?

撞库是坏人拿别处泄露的“账号+密码”清单，去其他网站批量试登录，因为很多人各网站用同一密码。对付它最好的办法是每个网站用不同密码。
Credential stuffing uses leaked 'account+password' lists to try logging into other sites, since many reuse passwords. The fix is a unique password per site.

什么是社交工程诈骗？

What is social engineering fraud?

社交工程是骗子不攻电脑、而是“骗人”：装成客服、警察或熟人，利用害怕或贪心让你自己交出验证码、转账。识破的关键是——官方不会要你的密码和验证码。
Social engineering tricks the person, not the machine: scammers pose as support, police or friends and exploit fear or greed to get codes or transfers. Rule: real officials never ask for your password or code.

什么是 SIM 卡交换攻击？

What is a SIM swap attack?

骗子先套取到你的运营商信息，骗营业厅把你的号码转到他的 SIM 卡上。这样你收不到验证码，他就能用短信验证码登录你的账号。给运营商设个额外密码可防此类攻击。
Scammers trick the carrier into moving your number to their SIM, so you stop getting codes and they log in via SMS. A carrier-side PIN adds protection.

什么是 VPN，它能保护我什么？

What is a VPN and what does it protect?

VPN 把你的上网数据先加密送到远方服务器再上网，别人更难偷看你在家或公共 WiFi 上的内容，也能隐藏你的真实位置。但它不是万能，别用它干坏事。
A VPN encrypts your traffic and routes it through a remote server, so others can't easily see what you do on home or public WiFi and your location is hidden. It's not a free pass for anything illegal.

什么样的密码比较安全？

What makes a password strong?

又长又乱最好：用 12 位以上、混合大小写字母、数字和符号，且每个网站不同。比起“Password123”，一句只有你知道的话（如“猫咪爱吃2只虾!”）更难被猜中。最好用密码管理器。
Long and random wins: 12+ characters mixing case, numbers and symbols, unique per site. A personal sentence like 'CatsEat2Shrimp!' beats 'Password123'. A password manager helps.

网站为什么必须用加盐的慢哈希存密码，而不是直接存 SHA-256？

Why must sites store passwords with a salted slow hash instead of a plain SHA-256?

SHA-256 的设计目标就是快，现代 GPU 每秒能算上百亿次，攻击者拿到数据库后可以用字典和彩虹表快速反推弱口令；不加盐时相同密码的哈希值完全相同，一张预计算表就能同时打穿所有用户。盐是每个用户独立的随机值，与密码一起参与哈希，使预计算失效并让相同密码呈现不同结果。更关键的是改用专门抗硬件破解的慢函数：Argon2id 与 scrypt 通过可调的内存开销抵御 GPU 和 ASIC，bcrypt 与 PBKDF2 靠高迭代次数拖慢每次尝试，OWASP 与 NIST 都建议随硬件进步定期上调参数。
SHA-256 is built for speed — a modern GPU computes tens of billions per second — so once a database leaks, attackers brute-force weak passwords with dictionaries and rainbow tables; without a salt, identical passwords hash identically and one precomputed table cracks every account at once. A salt is a per-user random value hashed together with the password, which invalidates precomputation and makes identical passwords look different. The decisive step is switching to a function deliberately slow against dedicated hardware: Argon2id and scrypt impose tunable memory cost that frustrates GPUs and ASICs, while bcrypt and PBKDF2 rely on high iteration counts, and both OWASP and NIST advise raising the parameters as hardware improves.

What is two-factor authentication (2FA) and why does it matter?

面对勒索软件，为什么说备份才是最后一道防线，「3-2-1」又是什么意思？

Why is backup the last line of defence against ransomware, and what does the 3-2-1 rule mean?

勒索软件会加密你的文件再索要赎金，加密本身无法硬破解，付钱也常常拿不到可用的密钥，所以真正能救回数据的只有一份未被感染的副本。3-2-1 原则是：至少保留 3 份数据，存放在 2 种不同介质上，其中 1 份放在异地。关键在于至少有一份是离线或不可改写的，比如拔下来的移动硬盘或写一次读多次的对象存储，否则勒索软件会连着映射的网络盘和自动同步的云盘一起加密；另外要定期做恢复演练，没验证过能还原的备份不算备份。
Ransomware encrypts your files and then demands payment, the encryption itself cannot be broken by force, and paying often fails to yield a working key, so the only reliable rescue is a copy the malware never reached. The 3-2-1 rule says keep at least 3 copies of the data on 2 different kinds of media with 1 copy stored off-site. What matters most is that at least one copy be offline or immutable — an unplugged external drive, or write-once object storage — because otherwise ransomware encrypts the mapped network share and the auto-syncing cloud folder along with everything else, and restores must be rehearsed, since a backup you have never restored is not yet a backup.

通行密钥（passkey）凭什么说比「密码加短信验证码」更安全？

What makes a passkey safer than a password plus an SMS code?

通行密钥用公钥密码学：注册时设备生成一对密钥，私钥留在手机或安全芯片里永不外传，只把公钥交给网站；登录时网站发来一个随机挑战，设备在你用指纹或面容解锁后签名返回。因为不存在可被复用的共享秘密，服务器即使被拖库也拿不到能用来登录的凭据；而且签名绑定了具体域名，浏览器不会在仿冒站点上使用它，钓鱼因此直接失效。短信验证码则是可读、可转述的共享秘密，钓鱼页面能实时中转，SIM 卡交换攻击也可能截获。
A passkey uses public-key cryptography: at registration the device generates a key pair, keeps the private key inside the phone or a secure element where it never leaves, and hands the site only the public key; at login the site sends a random challenge that the device signs after you unlock it with a fingerprint or face. Because no reusable shared secret exists, a breached server yields nothing an attacker can log in with, and the signature is bound to the exact domain so the browser simply refuses to use it on a lookalike site, which defeats phishing outright. An SMS code, by contrast, is a human-readable shared secret that a phishing page can relay in real time and that SIM-swap attacks can intercept.
