确定性时间层次定理的内容是什么？为什么由它可以推出 P 是 EXPTIME 的真子集？

What does the deterministic time hierarchy theorem state, and why does it imply that P is a proper subset of EXPTIME?

定理断言：对任意时间可构造函数 t(n)，存在一个语言可在 O(t(n)) 时间内判定，却不能在 o(t(n)/log t(n)) 时间内判定；证明采用对角化方法，模拟所有运行时间较短的机器并对每个结果取反，构造出一个与之不同的语言。取 t(n)=2^n 可得一个属于 EXPTIME 的语言，而任何固定多项式 n^k 都满足 n^k = o(2^n/n)，故该语言不属于任何 DTIME(n^k)，也就不在 P 中，于是 P 是 EXPTIME 的真子集。
The theorem asserts that for every time-constructible function t(n), some language is decidable in O(t(n)) time but not in o(t(n)/log t(n)) time; the proof diagonalizes — it simulates each machine running within the smaller bound and flips its answer to define a new language. Choosing t(n) = 2^n yields a language in EXPTIME, while every fixed polynomial satisfies n^k = o(2^n/n), so that language lies outside every DTIME(n^k) and hence outside P. Therefore P is strictly contained in EXPTIME.

Toda 定理（1991）陈述了什么结论？它为什么说明 #P 计数问题的计算能力极强？

What does Toda's theorem (1991) state, and why does it show that #P counting problems are computationally very powerful?

该定理断言 PH ⊆ P^#P：整座多项式层级都可归约到带 #P 预言机的多项式时间。#P 函数统计非确定性图灵机的接受路径数目。证明以 Valiant–Vazirani 归约与 BP· 算子把量词交替压缩成一次计数询问。既然 PH 被普遍认为远超 NP，一次计数即可模拟全层级，足见计数之强。
Toda's theorem asserts PH ⊆ P^#P: the entire polynomial hierarchy collapses into polynomial time with a #P oracle, where #P functions count accepting paths of nondeterministic polynomial-time machines. The proof combines the Valiant–Vazirani unique-SAT reduction with the BP· operator to replace quantifier alternation by a single counting query. Since PH is widely believed to far exceed NP, one counting query simulating the whole hierarchy shows how powerful counting is.

什么是 NP 完全问题？Cook–Levin 定理为何被视为计算复杂性理论的基石？

What is an NP-complete problem, and why is the Cook–Levin theorem regarded as a cornerstone of computational complexity theory?

NP 完全问题是 NP 中最难的一层：任何 NP 问题都可多项式时间归约到它。1971 年 Cook 证明布尔可满足性问题是 NP 完全的，Levin 也独立得到同一结论，首次确认此类问题存在。此后从 SAT 等已知完全问题构造多项式归约即可证出新问题的 NP 完全性；任一 NP 完全问题若有多项式算法即有 P=NP，大量实际难题的命运由此绑定。
An NP-complete problem is among the hardest in NP: every language in NP reduces to it in polynomial time. In 1971 Cook proved that Boolean satisfiability (SAT) is NP-complete, and Levin independently obtained the same result, establishing that such problems exist at all. Afterwards, proving a new problem NP-complete only requires a polynomial reduction from SAT or another known complete problem. Because a polynomial-time algorithm for any single NP-complete problem would imply P = NP, the theorem ties the fate of thousands of practical optimization problems together.
