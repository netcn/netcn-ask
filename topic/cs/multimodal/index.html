多模态模型如何把文本和图像“对齐”到同一套表示里？

How do multimodal models align text and images into one representation space?

通常分别用文本编码器和图像编码器把两者变成向量，再通过对比学习让“相配”的图片-文字向量靠近、不配的远离，于是同一语义在两种模态下落到相近的位置。
A text encoder and an image encoder turn each modality into vectors; contrastive learning pulls matching image-text pairs closer and pushes mismatches apart, so the same meaning lands near the same spot across modalities.

什么是视觉语言模型（VLM），它和普通大模型有何不同？

What is a vision-language model (VLM) and how does it differ from a plain LLM?

VLM 在语言模型之外接入了图像编码器，能把图片转成模型能读的“视觉词元”，于是你可以拿着一张图问它问题、让它描述或推理，而不只是处理纯文字。
A VLM adds an image encoder to a language model, turning a picture into 'visual tokens' the model can read, so you can ask about, describe, or reason over an image—not just plain text.

CLIP 的训练目标是什么？

What is the training objective of CLIP?

CLIP 用海量“图片-文字”对做对比学习：同一对的图文向量被拉到一起，不同对的被打散。训练后只需用文字向量就能去检索或分类图像，无需额外标注。
CLIP is trained contrastively on massive image-text pairs: matching pairs are pulled together, non-matching pairs pushed apart. After training, a text vector alone can retrieve or classify images without extra labels.
