默克尔树（Merkle tree）在区块链里具体用来做什么？

What exactly does a Merkle tree do in a blockchain?

它把大量交易两两哈希、层层向上聚成一个根哈希，作为整批交易的“指纹”。轻节点只需保存根哈希，就能用很短的“成员证明”验证某笔交易是否真的在该区块里，而不必下载全部交易。
It hashes transactions pairwise up to a single root hash acting as a fingerprint of the batch. A light node keeps only the root and can verify any transaction's inclusion with a short proof, without downloading all transactions.

区块链的“不可篡改”特性主要来自哪里？

Where does a blockchain's 'immutability' mainly come from?

每个区块都包含上一个区块的哈希，像锁链一样环环相扣；一旦改了某个历史区块，后面所有哈希都会失效，且要改写需控制全网多数算力或权益，因此成本高到不划算。
Each block contains the hash of the previous one, chaining them together; altering a past block breaks every later hash, and rewriting history would require controlling the majority of the network's power or stake—prohibitively costly.

什么是零知识证明（ZKP）？

What is a zero-knowledge proof (ZKP)?

零知识证明让一方（证明者）向另一方证明“我知道某个秘密或某命题为真”，却不泄露秘密本身。比如你能证明自己知道密码而不说出密码，常用于隐私币和身份验证。
A zero-knowledge proof lets a prover convince a verifier that a statement is true—or that they know a secret—without revealing the secret itself, used in privacy coins and identity checks.

什么是智能合约？

What is a smart contract?

智能合约是写在区块链上、满足条件就自动执行的程序，像一台没人能偷偷改动的自动售货机：规则公开、到点就按约定办事，无需中间人。
A smart contract is a program on a blockchain that runs automatically when conditions are met—like a tamper-proof vending machine: the rules are public and it executes as agreed, with no middleman.
