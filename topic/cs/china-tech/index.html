量子通信为什么被认为“不可窃听”？

Why is quantum communication said to be 'un-eavesdroppable'?

它基于量子不可克隆定理和测不准原理：窃听会改变量子态，通信双方能察觉异常。注意它分发的是密钥，并不靠超光速传递信息。
It rests on the no-cloning theorem and uncertainty principle: eavesdropping disturbs the quantum state and is detectable. It distributes keys, not information faster than light.

GB 2312、GBK 和 GB 18030 三种中文编码之间是什么关系？

How are the Chinese encodings GB 2312, GBK and GB 18030 related to one another?

三者构成一条逐层向后兼容的链条：GB 2312-1980 用双字节收录 6763 个简体汉字和 682 个符号，够日常使用但缺生僻字与繁体；GBK 在其编码空间上扩展，收录两万多个字，同时保持 GB 2312 的字节序列仍然有效。GB 18030 采用单、双、四字节混合结构，把整个 Unicode 码位空间映射进来，因此能表示繁体字、少数民族文字和罕用汉字。现行的 GB 18030-2022 是强制性国家标准，要求实现覆盖更完整的汉字集（含 CJK 扩展 B 区），是面向中文市场软件的编码基线。
They form a chain of backward-compatible encodings: GB 2312-1980 uses two bytes for 6,763 simplified characters plus 682 symbols, adequate for everyday text but missing rare and traditional forms; GBK extends that code space to more than twenty thousand characters while keeping GB 2312 byte sequences valid. GB 18030 mixes one-, two- and four-byte sequences to map the entire Unicode code space, so it can encode traditional characters, minority scripts and rare hanzi. The current GB 18030-2022 is a mandatory national standard requiring a larger repertoire including CJK Extension B, and it is the baseline encoding for software sold into the Chinese market.

中文搜索和文本处理为什么绕不开「分词」这一步？

Why do Chinese search and text processing depend on a word segmentation step?

中文书写不用空格分隔词，同一串字「研究生命起源」既能切成研究/生命/起源，也能切成研究生/命/起源，机器必须先判断边界才能建索引、做检索和翻译。经典方法是基于词典的正向最大匹配；现代做法把它转成逐字序列标注，给每个字打 B/M/E/S 标签，再用条件随机场或神经网络在标注语料上学习，标准评测的 F 值可达 96% 以上。近年字级与子词建模让部分下游任务可以跳过显式分词，但搜索引擎的索引与召回仍普遍依赖它。
Chinese text has no spaces between words, so one character string can be cut in more than one valid way, and a machine must resolve those boundaries before it can index, retrieve or translate. The classic approach is dictionary-based forward maximum matching; the modern one recasts segmentation as per-character sequence labelling with B/M/E/S tags learned by conditional random fields or neural networks on annotated corpora, reaching F-scores above 96% on standard benchmarks. Character-level and subword models now let some downstream tasks skip explicit segmentation, but search engine indexing and recall still rely on it heavily.

「东数西算」工程要解决的是什么工程问题？

What engineering problem is the East Data West Computing plan meant to solve?

数据中心耗电大、需要大量冷却，而算力需求集中在东部沿海，那里电价高、土地紧张、能耗指标受限；西部则有充裕的水电、风电与光伏，气温低还能省下制冷用电。该方案在京津冀、长三角、粤港澳、成渝以及内蒙古、贵州、甘肃、宁夏布局八大算力枢纽和十个国家数据中心集群，把对时延不敏感的离线训练、数据备份、视频渲染等任务西迁。留在东部的是实时交互业务，因为跨越数千公里的光纤往返延迟无法用工程手段消除。
Data centres consume enormous electricity and cooling, yet demand concentrates in the eastern coastal provinces where power is expensive, land is scarce and energy quotas are tight, while western regions offer abundant hydro, wind and solar power plus cool climates that cut cooling load. The plan designates eight national computing hubs and ten data centre clusters across Beijing-Tianjin-Hebei, the Yangtze and Pearl River deltas, Chengdu-Chongqing, Inner Mongolia, Guizhou, Gansu and Ningxia, relocating latency-tolerant work such as offline training, backup and video rendering westward. Interactive services stay in the east because the fibre round-trip delay across thousands of kilometres cannot be engineered away.
