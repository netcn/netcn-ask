托卡马克为什么要用强磁场把上亿度的聚变等离子体“关”在真空里？

Why does a tokamak use strong magnetic fields to cage 100-million-degree fusion plasma in a vacuum?

没有任何材料能直接接触上亿度的等离子体而不被汽化，所以靠环形磁场让带电粒子绕磁力线转圈、待在中心。真空则避免等离子体撞到残余气体而降温散掉。
No material could touch 100-million-degree plasma without vaporizing, so a toroidal field makes charged particles spiral along field lines and stay centered. Vacuum keeps residual gas from cooling the plasma on contact.

Why does lightning glow?

A huge electric field ionizes air, heating it so it glows and thunders.

等离子体的“磁约束”和“惯性约束”两种聚变路线，差别在哪？

What's the difference between 'magnetic' and 'inertial' confinement fusion?

磁约束（如托卡马克）用磁场长时间“托住”稀薄的等离子体；惯性约束（如激光点火）则用多束激光在极短时间内把小靶丸压缩到聚变条件，靠惯性还来不及散开就反应了。一个靠“撑住”，一个靠“压炸”。
Magnetic confinement (tokamak) holds a tenuous plasma with fields for a long time; inertial confinement (laser ignition) compresses a tiny pellet in a billionth of a second so fusion happens before it can fly apart. One 'holds', the other 'squeezes'.

闪电那一瞬，空气其实被变成了什么状态？

In a lightning flash, what state does the air actually turn into?

强大的电场把空气分子电离，正负电荷分开乱窜，空气瞬间变成灼热的等离子体通道。正是这团发光发热的等离子体，构成了我们看到的闪电。
The huge electric field ionizes air molecules, freeing charges that race around, turning the air into a hot plasma channel. That glowing, searing plasma is the lightning we see.

#闪电 #电离 #等离子体

为什么恒星内部和霓虹灯里都有等离子体，温度却差了上亿倍？

Both star interiors and neon signs contain plasma — why do their temperatures differ by hundreds of millions of degrees?

只要原子被电离出自由电子，就算等离子体，对“温度”没有下限。霓虹灯只是温和电离、灯管凉凉的；恒星靠极高温度和压强维持完全电离，所以热到发光。是否等离子体和有多热，是两回事。
Plasma only needs freed electrons, with no minimum temperature. A neon sign is mildly ionized and stays cool; a star is fully ionized by extreme temperature and pressure, hence blazing hot. Being plasma and being hot are separate questions.

#电离度 #温度 #恒星

为什么人造核聚变要上亿度，而氢弹用化学炸药就能点燃？

Why does lab fusion need 100 million degrees, yet a hydrogen bomb ignites with chemical explosives?

氢弹靠原子弹的 X 射线和冲击波在一瞬间把燃料压缩到极高密度和温度，是“猛压”出来的。实验室里没法造原子弹当打火机，只能靠稳态磁约束慢慢把稀薄等离子体加热到上亿度。
A hydrogen bomb uses an atomic bomb's X-rays and shockwave to compress fuel to extreme density and temperature in an instant. Labs can't use a fission bomb as a lighter, so they must steadily heat tenuous plasma to 100 million degrees instead.

等离子体为什么被称为物质的“第四态”？

Why is plasma called the 'fourth state' of matter?

给气体不断加热或加电，电子会被扯离原子，变成一群带正电的离子和自由电子混在一起——这团“带电雾”就是等离子体，和固液气都不同，所以算第四态。
Heat or energize a gas enough and electrons rip free from atoms, leaving a mix of positive ions and free electrons — an electrified 'fog' unlike solid, liquid or gas, hence the fourth state.

电焊时的亮光和闪电一样，都短暂地把空气变成了什么？

Welding sparks and lightning are alike — both briefly turn air into what?

都靠强大的电把空气里的原子“打”出电子，变成发光发热的等离子体。那一瞬的空气，已经不是普通气体，而是一团带电的火光。
Both use strong electricity to knock electrons off air atoms, making glowing, hot plasma. For that instant the air isn't an ordinary gas but a charged, blazing light.

为什么霓虹灯、日常见到的“电离子”颜色，和里面充的气体有关？

Why does a neon sign's color depend on the gas inside?

不同气体的原子，电子从激发态落回时放出的光颜色不同：氖发红光，氩偏蓝紫，加上荧光粉还能变出各种颜色。所以换种气体，就换了灯的颜色。
Different gases emit different colors when their electrons drop from excited states: neon glows red, argon bluish-violet, and phosphors add more hues. Change the gas, change the color.

商场里的“等离子球”用手一碰，电丝为什么就聚到手指上？

In a plasma ball, why do the electric filaments gather toward your finger when you touch it?

球里的高压电把气体变成发光的等离子体丝。你的手是导体，把电场“吸引”过来，丝就顺着更顺的路聚到你指尖了。
High voltage inside turns the gas into glowing plasma filaments. Your conductive hand pulls the electric field toward it, so the filaments stream to your fingertip along the easier path.
