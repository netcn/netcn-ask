为什么圆锥形沙堆正下方地面上的法向压强出现中心凹陷,而不是静水模型预言的最大值?

Why does the normal stress measured under the apex of a conical sandpile show a central dip instead of the maximum predicted by a hydrostatic model?

颗粒堆通过空间上高度不均匀的力链网络传递重力,其应力状态无法用各向同性的静水压描述。堆内摩擦形成的拱形支撑会把载荷优先导向侧向并重新分配到边缘,使得堆底中心的竖直应力反而低于两侧。这种依赖加载历史的非局域响应是颗粒物质区别于普通流体的标志性现象之一。
A granular heap carries its weight through a highly inhomogeneous network of force chains, so its stress state cannot be captured by isotropic hydrostatic pressure. Frictional arching inside the pile preferentially redirects load sideways and redistributes it toward the rim, leaving the vertical stress beneath the apex smaller than at either side. This history-dependent, nonlocal response is one of the hallmark phenomena distinguishing granular matter from ordinary fluids.

#力链 #压强凹陷

沙堆底部的法向应力为什么不在中心最大,而是在两侧出现两个对称的峰?这对粮仓等储料容器的设计意味着什么?

Why is the normal stress under a sandpile not maximal at its center but instead shows two symmetric peaks toward the edges, and what does this imply for designing silos and similar storage vessels?

颗粒堆内部自发形成力链网络,载荷沿着接触链优先传递而非均匀扩散,于是底部应力出现指向两侧的对称双峰,即所谓压力凹陷。这一现象违反了各向同性线弹性介质的预言,说明颗粒介质中存在冻结的残余剪应力,必须用非经典本构关系描述。工程上,Janssen早在1895年就指出仓壁摩擦会把物料的部分重量转嫁给侧壁,使仓底压力随装料高度呈指数饱和而非线性增长,这正是钢制筒仓可以做得又高又薄的理论依据。
Granular piles spontaneously build force-chain networks, so load travels preferentially along contact chains instead of spreading uniformly, producing symmetric twin peaks at the base known as the pressure dip. This contradicts isotropic linear elasticity, showing that frozen residual shear stresses exist in granular matter and demand non-classical constitutive descriptions. On the engineering side, Janssen showed already in 1895 that wall friction diverts part of the fill weight to the silo walls, making bottom pressure saturate exponentially with height rather than grow linearly, which is why steel silos can be built tall yet thin-walled.

#颗粒物质 #力链网络

为什么沙堆静止时可以保持很陡的坡度，而倾角一旦超过某个临界值就会发生雪崩式滑落？

Why can a sandpile hold a steep slope at rest, yet avalanche suddenly once its tilt exceeds a critical angle?

颗粒介质存在静态休止角与动态休止角之差：静止时颗粒间的摩擦与几何互锁能够支撑比流动状态更陡的坡面。当倾角超过临界值时，局部失稳会触发链式滑移，坡面经雪崩回落到稳定角附近。这类自组织临界行为使沙堆成为研究地震、滑坡等事件规模统计规律的原型系统。
Granular media possess distinct static and dynamic angles of repose: inter-particle friction and geometric interlocking support slopes steeper than those sustainable during flow. Once the tilt passes the critical value, local failure triggers chain-reaction sliding, and avalanches relax the surface back toward the stable angle. Such self-organized critical behavior made the sandpile a prototype system for studying the size statistics of earthquakes and landslides.
