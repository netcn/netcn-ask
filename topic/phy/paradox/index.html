量子擦除实验如何“恢复”已经消失的干涉？

How does a quantum eraser 'restore' interference that already vanished?

若记录哪条缝的信息后来被“擦除”（使路径变得不可区分），干涉条纹会重新出现，哪怕决定在光子通过后才做出；说明可获取的路径信息、而非物理扰动，才是干涉消失的关键。
If which-path information is later erased (made indistinguishable), fringes reappear even after the photon passed; it shows accessible path information, not physical disturbance, kills interference.

薇格纳朋友与“客观实在”之争焦点在哪？

Where does the Wigner's friend debate about objective reality focus?

若朋友在密闭实验室里测了量子系统，门外薇格纳用“整个实验室”做叠加描述——两人对“是否发生确定结果”看法冲突，逼问测量与意识、相对论参考系的边界。
If a friend measures a system inside a lab, Wigner outside can describe the whole lab as a superposition—they disagree on whether a definite outcome occurred, probing the boundary of measurement, objectivity and reference frames.

为什么麦克斯韦方程组在伽利略变换下不协变，却长期被认为正确？

Why were Maxwell's equations long accepted though not Galilean covariant?

19世纪以以太为绝对静止参考系，方程在该系成立；只有当爱因斯坦用洛伦兹变换与光速不变重建时空，才揭示电磁与力学的统一框架，旧协变问题随之消解。
In the 19th century the aether was the absolute frame where the equations held; Einstein's Lorentz transformation and light-speed invariance unified EM with mechanics, dissolving the covariance issue.

为什么在密闭车厢里向上跳起还会落回原地，而不是被甩在后面？

Why, jumping up in a moving closed train, do you land in the same spot rather than being left behind?

你和车厢共享相同水平速度，跳起后水平惯性让你保持该速度（无水平外力）；伽利略相对性说明匀速参考系内竖直运动与静止时无异，所以落回原地。
You share the train's horizontal velocity; with no horizontal force you keep it while airborne. Galilean relativity says vertical motion in a uniform frame is the same as at rest, so you land in place.

为什么可逆卡诺热机效率最高却不现实？

Why is the reversible Carnot engine the most efficient yet impractical?

卡诺效率 1-Tc/Th 是可逆极限，要求所有传热在无穷小温差下进行，意味着功率趋零、循环无限慢；实际引擎用有限温差换功率，效率低于卡诺。
Carnot efficiency 1−Tc/Th is the reversible limit needing infinitesimal temperature gaps, so power tends to zero and cycles infinitely slow; real engines trade efficiency for finite power.

为什么在加速电梯里分不清是重力还是加速度（等效原理的日常版）？

Why can't you tell gravity from acceleration inside an accelerating elevator?

在密闭电梯里，向下加速 a 会减少你对地板的压力，与重力减弱等效；向上加速则增重，与重力增强等效；局域上两者产生的力学效应完全相同，这就是等效原理。
In a closed elevator, downward acceleration reduces your apparent weight like weaker gravity; upward acceleration adds weight like stronger gravity. Locally the effects are identical—the equivalence principle.

麦克斯韦妖如果真的存在，会推翻热力学第二定律吗？

If Maxwell's demon really existed, would it overturn the second law?

不会。妖要“看”并记住分子信息，擦除记忆必然产生至少同等热量（朗道尔极限），整体熵不减，第二定律依然成立。
No. The demon must observe and remember; erasing that memory costs at least as much heat (Landauer limit), so total entropy still rises and the second law holds.

为什么把冰和温水放进保温杯摇晃，水温不会降到0℃以下？

Why doesn't shaking ice and warm water in a thermos drop below 0°C?

冰融化吸热把水冷却，但只要有冰存在平衡温度就是0℃；摇晃只加速热交换不会创造更低温度，除非额外做功或相变，否则不会低于冰点。
Melting ice cools the water, but with ice present the equilibrium is 0°C; shaking only speeds exchange, it can't make it colder without extra work or phase change.

为什么从桥上丢下的石头和羽毛在真空中同时落地？

Why do a stone and a feather hit the ground together in vacuum?

自由落体加速度 g=GM/r² 与质量无关，所有物体在真空中以相同加速度下落；羽毛落后只是空气阻力（与形状、面积有关）造成的，真空中没有空气就同时落地。
Free-fall acceleration g=GM/r² is mass-independent; in vacuum everything falls together. A feather lags only because of air resistance, absent in vacuum.

双缝实验里“观察”为什么会让波坍缩成粒子？

Why does observation in the double-slit experiment collapse the wave into particles?

若用设备分辨光子走哪条缝，就引入了与路径相关的信息（退相干），干涉条纹消失；不测量路径时，概率幅叠加产生明暗条纹。测量本身破坏了相干。
Detecting which slit entangles the photon with path information (decoherence) and kills interference; without path measurement the probability amplitudes add and fringes appear. Measurement destroys coherence.

为什么骑自行车不扶把也不会倒？

Why doesn't a bike fall over even without hands on the bars?

前进时轮子像陀螺，倾斜会触发前轮转向使接触点回到重心下方（陀螺加转向自稳）；速度足够时这种自校正盖过不稳，所以能不扶把骑行。
Moving wheels act like gyroscopes; a lean steers the front wheel so the contact point returns under the center of mass. Above a speed this self-correction stabilizes the bike.

薛定谔猫实验真正想挑战的是什么？

What was Schrodinger's cat experiment really challenging?

它把微观叠加放大到宏观猫的生死，质问“测量何时让叠加坍缩”、以及宏观世界为何看不到叠加——推动了多世界、退相干等诠释的发展。
It blows a microscopic superposition up to a cat's life or death, questioning when measurement collapses a state and why we never see macro superpositions—spurring many-worlds and decoherence views.

为什么从行驶的车里看，近处的树往后跑、远山却几乎不动？

Why do near trees seem to rush backward but distant hills barely move from a moving car?

近处东西在你的视野里角度变化快，所以显得飞快后退；远处山离得远，同样车程造成的角度变化很小，看起来就几乎不动。
Near things change angle in your view quickly, so they seem to rush by; far hills shift angle very little for the same trip, so they look almost still.

为什么热水有时比冷水结冰更快（姆潘巴现象）？

Why can hot water sometimes freeze faster than cold—the Mpemba effect?

这不是普遍定律，但在某些条件下成立：热水蒸发更快减少水量、温差大加速顶部冷却、溶质浓度与容器结霜差异等都可能让较热水先结冰。
Not a universal law, but under some conditions it holds: faster evaporation reduces mass, stronger cooling at the top, and differences in solutes/frost can let hotter water freeze first.

薛定谔的猫到底是死是活？

Is Schrodinger's cat dead or alive?

这是思想实验：在没打开盒子看之前，猫被说成“又死又活”的叠加，用来说明量子世界很奇怪。
It is a thought experiment: before you open the box, the cat is described as both alive and dead, showing how odd quantum rules are.
