对称性破缺与拓扑相变（如 KT 相变）有何不同？

How do symmetry-breaking and topological transitions (like KT) differ?

常规相变靠序参量连续或突变、常伴随对称性破缺；Kosterlitz–Thouless 相变不破任何对称，而是靠涡旋、反涡旋对的结合与解离，属拓扑序的范畴。
Ordinary transitions use an order parameter and break symmetry; the Kosterlitz–Thouless transition breaks none, instead pairing and unbinding vortices—an example of topological order.

一级相变中的“临界核”与过冷、过热为何存在？

Why do critical nuclei and supercooling/superheating exist in first-order transitions?

新相要自发出现须先形成大于临界尺寸的“核”以抵消表面能；小于它的核会消失，所以纯净液体可过冷、纯净金属可过热，直到扰动触发突变。
A new phase must grow a critical nucleus to outweigh surface energy; smaller ones vanish, so pure liquids supercool and metals superheat until a fluctuation triggers the jump.

超流和超导算不算相变？

Are superfluidity and superconductivity phase transitions?

算。液氦在 λ 点变成超流（零黏滞），某些金属冷却后电阻突降为零（超导），都是对称性破缺型相变，也有自己的临界温度。
Yes. Helium becomes superfluid (zero viscosity) at the lambda point and some metals lose all resistance (superconductivity); both are symmetry-breaking phase transitions with their own critical temperatures.

临界现象里的“标度律”是什么？

What is the scaling law in critical phenomena?

在相变临界点附近，许多物理量按幂律变化，指数互相约束且与微观细节无关，这叫普适性——磁铁、液体、合金在临界点“长得像”。
Near a critical point quantities follow power laws whose exponents are linked and independent of details, a universality that makes magnets, fluids and alloys look alike at the critical point.

水结冰和磁铁消磁算不算一类现象？

Are water freezing and a magnet losing power the same kind of thing?

都叫“相变”——系统宏观性质在某条件下突然改变，比如水从流动变固体，或金属失去磁性。
Both are phase transitions, where a system's big-scale properties snap to a new state, like water freezing or a magnet losing power.
