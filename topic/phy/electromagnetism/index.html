无线电、可见光和 X 光本质上有什么共同点？

What do radio waves, visible light, and X-rays have in common at heart?

它们都是电磁波，只是频率与波长不同。无线电波长、频率低，X 光波长短、频率高；整个排列就是电磁波谱，由麦克斯韦理论统一描述。
They are all electromagnetic waves differing only in frequency and wavelength. Radio waves are long and low-frequency, X-rays short and high-frequency; the whole ordering is the electromagnetic spectrum described by Maxwell's theory.

麦克斯韦方程组描述了什么？

What do Maxwell's equations describe?

麦克斯韦方程组用四个方程统一描述了电场、磁场及其与电荷和电流的关系，并预言了电磁波的存在。它是经典电磁学的基石，也奠定了现代通信的理论基础。
Maxwell's equations use four relations to describe electric and magnetic fields and their links to charges and currents, predicting electromagnetic waves. They are the foundation of classical electromagnetism and modern communications.

麦克斯韦方程组统一了哪两件事，并预言了什么？

What two things do Maxwell's equations unify, and what did they predict?

麦克斯韦方程把电、磁与光统一成同一种电磁现象，并预言变化的电场会产生磁场、变化的磁场产生电场，从而电磁波可在真空中以光速传播。
Maxwell's equations unify electricity, magnetism, and light as one electromagnetic phenomenon, and predict that changing electric fields make magnetic fields and vice versa, so electromagnetic waves travel through vacuum at the speed of light.

为什么强磁铁在厚铜管里会慢慢下落？

Why does a strong magnet fall slowly through a thick copper tube?

磁铁下落时，铜管内的磁通持续变化，管壁中因此产生一圈圈闭合的感应电流，叫涡电流。根据楞次定律，涡电流的磁场总是反抗磁铁的运动，产生向上的阻力，与重力平衡后磁铁便以很低的恒定速度匀速下落。磁悬浮列车和电梯的电磁制动利用的正是这种涡流阻力，感应加热炉则反过来利用涡流在金属内部发热来熔化金属。
As the magnet falls, the changing flux induces closed loops of current — eddy currents — in the copper walls. By Lenz's law these currents create a field that opposes the motion, giving an upward drag that balances gravity, so the magnet settles into a slow constant fall. Electromagnetic brakes on maglev trains and elevators exploit exactly this drag, while induction furnaces run it in reverse, using eddy-current heating to melt metal.

为什么电容器让交流电"通过"，却把直流电挡住？

Why does a capacitor pass alternating current but block direct current?

电容器靠两极板间的电场储存能量。面对交变电压，极板上的电荷不断增减，导线上便持续有电流流进流出，看起来交流"穿过"了电容器；而面对恒定的直流电压，电容器充电完成后电流就降为零。电容器对交流的阻碍叫容抗，与频率成反比，电感则相反、感抗与频率成正比。这两者与电阻一起，共同决定交流电路中电流与电压的相位关系。
A capacitor stores energy in the field between its plates. Under alternating voltage the plates continuously charge and discharge, so current keeps flowing through the wires and effectively "passes" the capacitor; with steady DC the current dies once charging is complete. A capacitor's AC opposition, capacitive reactance, falls with frequency, while an inductor's inductive reactance rises with it. Together with resistance, these set the phase relationship between current and voltage in AC circuits.

为什么说麦克斯韦方程组是物理学最重要的方程之一？

Why are Maxwell's equations counted among the most important in all of physics?

四个方程把电学、磁学和光学统一成一个理论：高斯定律描述电荷如何产生电场，磁场无源说明不存在孤立的磁单极，法拉第定律指出变化的磁场如何激发电场，安培-麦克斯韦定律则说明电流和变化的电场如何产生磁场。麦克斯韦由此预言了电磁波并算出其速度等于光速，爱因斯坦又在此基础上创立了狭义相对论。从无线电到智能手机，几乎所有现代技术都建立在这组方程之上。
Four equations unify electricity, magnetism and optics: Gauss's law tells how charges create electric fields, the no-monopole law says magnetic field lines have no sources, Faraday's law shows how changing magnetic fields generate electric ones, and the Ampère–Maxwell law explains how currents and changing electric fields create magnetic fields. Maxwell predicted electromagnetic waves and found their speed equals light's, and Einstein built special relativity on the same ideas. Nearly all modern technology, from radio to smartphones, rests on these equations.

超导体除了零电阻，还有什么更奇特的性质？

Besides zero resistance, what stranger property do superconductors have?

超导体会完全排斥内部磁场（迈斯纳效应），使磁体悬浮其上。这不只是“没有电阻”，而是主动把磁通排出体外，是区别于理想导体的本质特征。
A superconductor expels magnetic field from its interior (the Meissner effect), letting a magnet levitate above it. This is not just zero resistance but an active pushing-out of flux, what truly distinguishes it from a perfect conductor.

超导体是如何把磁场“排出”自身的？

How do superconductors expel magnetic fields from themselves?

低于临界温度后，超导体内部感应出表面屏蔽电流，使磁感应强度在体相中降为零，这一现象叫迈斯纳效应。Meissner 与 Ochsenfeld 于 1933 年用实验证实，它说明超导态是真正的抗磁态而非单纯零电阻。
Below a critical temperature a superconductor develops surface screening currents that cancel the interior magnetic field, driving it to zero—the Meissner effect. Meissner and Ochsenfeld demonstrated this in 1933, showing superconductivity is a true diamagnetic state, not merely zero resistance.

人类从没到过恒星，怎么知道太阳由什么元素组成？

Humans have never visited a star — how do we know what elements the Sun contains?

每种元素的原子都只能吸收或发射特定波长的光，形成一套特征谱线，如同指纹。把太阳光经过棱镜或光栅分光后，会看到一道道暗的吸收线——那是太阳外层大气中的原子吸收了对应波长的光。分析这些吸收线的波长和强弱，就能确定元素种类与丰度。1868年日食期间，科学家正是靠光谱在地球上看到了氦——它先在天上被发现，之后才在地球上找到。
Every element's atoms absorb or emit light only at specific wavelengths, producing a set of spectral lines that act as fingerprints. When sunlight is spread by a prism or grating, dark absorption lines appear where atoms in the Sun's outer atmosphere swallowed particular wavelengths. Reading the lines' positions and strengths reveals the elements and their abundances. During the 1868 eclipse, scientists spotted helium this way — it was found in the sky before it was found on Earth.

物理学家为什么一直在寻找「磁单极子」？它存在吗？

Why do physicists still search for magnetic monopoles, and do they exist?

自然界里磁极总是南北成对出现，没有单独存在的「磁荷」，这与电荷不对称。狄拉克1931年指出，若磁单极子存在，就能自然解释电荷为何都是基本电荷的整数倍（电荷量子化）。尽管粒子数据组（PDG）迄今未确认其存在，大型强子对撞机与宇宙线实验仍在搜寻，因为它关乎规范对称与早期宇宙。
Magnets always come with north and south poles together—no isolated 'magnetic charge'—asymmetrically unlike electric charge. Dirac showed in 1931 that if a monopole existed, it would naturally explain why all charges are integer multiples of the elementary charge (charge quantization). The Particle Data Group has found no confirmed monopole, yet the LHC and cosmic-ray experiments keep searching because it bears on gauge symmetry and the early universe.

What principle lets a generator turn spinning motion into electricity?

Faraday's law says a changing magnetic flux through a coil induces a voltage. A generator spins a coil in a magnetic field so the flux keeps changing, producing a continuous current.

闪电为什么是弯弯曲曲、分叉的锯齿状，而不是一条直线？

Why is lightning jagged and forked rather than a straight line?

闪电始于云层中的「阶梯先导」：电荷沿最易被击穿的空气通道一段段向下试探，每段约几十米，方向被局部电场和随机的离子不均匀性决定，于是呈锯齿状。当某条通道接近地面触发上行连接，主放电沿这条既成路径返回，就形成我们看到的闪电线，并常在半途分出叉枝。
Lightning begins with a 'stepped leader': charge probes downward in jagged segments of tens of meters, each step bent by local fields and random irregularities in ionized air. When one branch nears the ground and connects, the main discharge races back up that established path, producing the visible bolt—with forks where side branches formed.

从无线电波到伽马射线都是电磁波，它们之间到底有什么区别？

Radio waves and gamma rays are both electromagnetic — so what really distinguishes them?

它们的本质相同，唯一区别在于频率和波长。频率越高，每个光子携带的能量越大，穿透力越强：无线电波用于广播和通信，微波用来加热食物，红外线用于夜视，可见光让我们看见世界，X射线能穿透软组织拍摄医学影像，伽马射线能量最高，可用于放疗。整个电磁波谱跨越了几十个数量级的频率范围。
They are the same kind of wave, differing only in frequency and wavelength. Higher frequency means more energy per photon and greater penetrating power: radio for broadcasting and communication, microwaves for cooking, infrared for night vision, visible light for sight, X-rays for medical imaging through soft tissue, and gamma rays, the most energetic, for radiotherapy. The full spectrum spans dozens of orders of magnitude in frequency.

交流电和直流电有什么区别？

What is the difference between AC and DC?

直流电（DC）电流方向不变，如电池；交流电（AC）电流方向和大小周期性变化，如家用插座。交流电便于用变压器升压远距离输电。
Direct current (DC) flows one way, like from a battery. Alternating current (AC) reverses direction periodically, as in wall outlets, and is easy to step up for long-distance transmission.

法拉第电磁感应告诉我们什么？

What does Faraday's law of electromagnetic induction tell us?

它说穿过一个回路的磁通量发生变化时，回路中就会产生感应电动势，其大小等于磁通变化率（楞次定律决定方向，总是阻碍变化）。发电厂让线圈在磁场中转动、改变磁通，正是靠这条原理发电。法拉第于1831年发现，是电动机与发电机的理论基础。
It states that when magnetic flux through a loop changes, an electromotive force is induced, equal in magnitude to the rate of flux change (Lenz's law gives the direction, opposing the change). Power plants spin coils in magnetic fields to vary flux and generate electricity by this law. Faraday discovered it in 1831, founding motors and generators.

楞次定律说的“阻碍变化”到底指什么？

What does Lenz's law mean by opposing the change?

感应电流的方向总是使其磁场去抵抗引起它的磁通量变化，这正是能量守恒的要求。比如磁铁插入线圈时，线圈感应出的磁极会排斥磁铁，让你更费力。
The induced current flows so its own magnetic field resists the change in flux that caused it, as required by energy conservation. Pushing a magnet into a coil, the coil's poles repel it, costing you extra effort.

手机和电动牙刷的无线充电靠的是什么物理原理？

What physical principle powers wireless charging in phones and toothbrushes?

它基于电磁感应（法拉第定律）：充电板里的线圈通交流电产生交变磁场，设备里的接收线圈「感」到变化磁通，便感应出电流给电池充电。把两个线圈靠近、对准，能量就通过磁场隔空传递，无需金属触点。国际标准如 IEC 61980 规定了电动汽车无线充电的线圈与频率。
It relies on electromagnetic induction (Faraday's law): an AC current in the pad's coil makes an alternating magnetic field, and the receiving coil in the device picks up the changing flux and induces a current to charge the battery. Aligning the coils transfers energy through the magnetic field without metal contacts. Standards like IEC 61980 specify coil and frequency for wireless EV charging.

变压器是怎么把电压升高或降低的？

How does a transformer step voltage up or down?

变压器由绕在同一个铁芯上的两组线圈构成。交变电流通过原线圈时产生交变的磁场，铁芯把这个磁通传递到副线圈，副线圈中就感应出电压。两个线圈的电压之比等于匝数之比：匝数多的一侧电压高而电流小。由于变压器只能作用于变化的磁场，直流电无法通过它升压，这也是电网坚持采用交流电的原因之一。
A transformer has two coils wound on a shared iron core. Alternating current in the primary coil creates an oscillating magnetic field, the core channels the flux to the secondary coil, and a voltage is induced there. The voltage ratio equals the turns ratio: the side with more turns gets higher voltage and lower current. Because transformers need a changing field, they cannot handle DC, which is one reason power grids use alternating current.

为什么说光其实就是一种电磁波？

Why is light said to be nothing but an electromagnetic wave?

麦克斯韦从自己的方程组推出：变化的电场会激发磁场，变化的磁场又会激发电场，两者交替产生并以波的形式在空间中传播，而且这种波的速度恰好等于当时实测的光速。他由此断定光就是电磁波。电磁波谱从无线电波、微波、红外一路延伸到可见光、紫外、X射线和伽马射线，可见光只是其中很窄的一段。
From his equations, Maxwell showed that a changing electric field generates a magnetic one and vice versa, and the two regenerate each other, traveling as waves through space at a speed that matched the measured speed of light exactly. He concluded that light is an electromagnetic wave. The spectrum runs from radio, microwave and infrared through visible light to ultraviolet, X-rays and gamma rays, with visible light only a narrow slice.

磁铁靠近线圈时，为什么线圈会"反抗"磁铁的运动？

Why does a coil "resist" the motion of a magnet approaching it?

这是楞次定律的体现：感应电流的方向总是让自己产生的磁场去阻碍引起它的磁通变化。磁铁靠近时，线圈产生一个排斥磁铁的磁场；磁铁远离时，线圈又产生一个吸引它的磁场。所以把强磁铁推进铜管时能感到明显的阻力。正是这股"别扭"的力量，让电磁感应成为发电机和电磁制动的根基。
Lenz's law: induced current always flows so that its own magnetic field opposes the flux change that created it. A magnet approaching a coil induces a repelling field; one moving away induces an attracting one, which is why a strong magnet pushed through a copper tube feels real drag. This stubbornness is the very effect that makes induction drive generators and electromagnetic brakes.

微波炉是怎么把食物加热的？为什么盘子却不烫？

How does a microwave oven cook food, and why does the plate stay cool?

微波炉发出频率约2.45GHz的微波，使水分子这类带正负电荷的极性分子随电场高频转动、彼此碰撞摩擦生热。食物富含水分，所以热得快；普通陶瓷碟子不含极性分子，微波穿过而不被吸收，所以它本身不热。水分极少的物体（如干木片）在微波里几乎加热不了。
A microwave oven radiates about 2.45 GHz microwaves that make polar molecules like water tumble and collide, generating heat. Food is packed with water, so it heats quickly; an ordinary ceramic plate has no polar molecules, so microwaves pass through and it barely warms itself. Objects with almost no water — dry wood, for instance — hardly heat in a microwave at all.

为什么远距离输电要用几十万伏的高压？

Why is electricity transmitted over long distances at hundreds of thousands of volts?

输电线的电阻会把电能变成热，损耗的功率与电流的平方成正比。用变压器把电压升到几十万伏之后，输送同样功率所需的电流大大减小，线路损耗就按平方关系急剧下降。电到达城市后，再通过变压器逐级降回家庭使用的220伏。提高电压、压低电流，正是电网省电的核心手段。
Transmission lines turn electricity into heat, wasting power proportional to the square of the current. Stepping the voltage up to hundreds of kilovolts lets the same power flow with far less current, slashing resistive losses quadratically. Near cities, transformers step it back down to the 220 volts homes use. Boosting voltage to shrink current is the central trick of efficient power grids.

为什么微波炉里不能放金属餐具？

Why must you never put metal utensils in a microwave oven?

金属里的自由电子会被微波电场驱动，在表面形成强感应电流，而薄而尖的金属（如铝箔、镶金边的盘子）容易在边缘尖端积聚电荷、放电打火花，可能损坏微波源甚至引发危险。厚实的金属碗还会把微波反射回去，导致食物加热不均。所以微波炉要用无金属的容器。
Free electrons in metal are driven by the microwave's electric field into strong surface currents, and thin or pointed metal — foil, gold-trimmed plates — concentrates charge at edges where it can arc and spark, damaging the magnetron or creating a hazard. Thick metal bowls also reflect microwaves, leaving food unevenly heated. That's why microwave-safe dishes contain no metal.

发电机是怎么把机械能变成电能的？

How does a generator convert mechanical energy into electricity?

根据法拉第电磁感应定律，穿过线圈的磁通量一旦变化，线圈中就会产生感应电动势。发电机让线圈在磁场中持续转动，或者让磁铁在线圈内旋转，磁通量随之周期性变化，于是输出不断换向的交流电。发电站用水轮机、汽轮机或风力机带动转子旋转，机械能就这样源源不断地转化为电能。
Faraday's law of induction says a changing magnetic flux through a coil induces an electromotive force. A generator spins the coil inside a magnetic field, or spins the magnet within the coil, so the flux oscillates and alternating current flows. Turbines driven by water, steam or wind turn the rotor, continuously converting mechanical energy into electricity.

手机和Wi-Fi是怎么把信息塞进空气里传出去的？

How do phones and Wi-Fi pack information into the air and send it?

它们用无线电波——电磁波的一种——来传信息。发送端先把文字、图片变成二进制的0和1，再用这些数据去调制高频无线电波，天线把载有数据的电波发射出去；接收端的天线收到后解调，还原出原始数据，变成你看到的画面和文字。电磁波以光速传播，所以信息几乎瞬间到达。
They use radio waves, a form of electromagnetic radiation. The transmitter first turns text and images into binary 0s and 1s, then uses those bits to modulate a high-frequency radio carrier; the antenna radiates the data-laden waves, and the receiver's antenna picks them up and demodulates them back into the original content. Since EM waves travel at light speed, the information arrives almost instantly.

电磁波是如何传播的？

How do electromagnetic waves propagate?

变化的电场产生磁场，变化的磁场又产生电场，二者相互激发并向前传播，形成横波。它不需要介质，可在真空中以光速传播。电场与磁场方向互相垂直，且都垂直于传播方向。
A changing electric field creates a magnetic field, which in turn creates a changing electric field, and the two mutually excite and propagate forward as a transverse wave. It needs no medium and travels through vacuum at light speed. The electric and magnetic fields are perpendicular to each other and to the direction of travel.

两根平行的通电导线为什么会同向相吸、反向相斥，安培力本质上来自什么？

Why do two parallel current-carrying wires attract when currents are parallel and repel when antiparallel, and what is the Ampère force really from?

一根导线的电流在周围产生环形磁场，另一根导线中的载流子在该磁场中受洛伦兹力，宏观上就是 F = IL×B。用右手定则可验证：电流同向时第二根导线受力指向第一根，故相吸；电流反向时受力朝外，故相斥。相距 1 米的两根长直导线各通 1 安培时，每米作用力约 2×10⁻⁷ N，历史上正是用这条关系定义安培的。
The current in one wire sets up a circular magnetic field, and the moving carriers in the other wire feel a Lorentz force in it, which macroscopically is F = IL×B. The right-hand rule shows that for parallel currents this force points toward the first wire (attraction), while for antiparallel currents it points outward (repulsion). Two long straight wires 1 m apart each carrying 1 A feel about 2×10⁻⁷ N per metre—the relation once used to define the ampere.

为什么在电梯里手机常常收不到信号？

Why do phones often lose signal inside an elevator?

电梯是金属围成的箱子，金属外壳会感应出与外来电场相反的电荷，把内部空间里的电磁场抵消掉，形成法拉第笼，无线电波进不来，手机自然没有信号。反过来看，这个原理也在保护我们：避雷针让雷电流走金属外壳入地，医院手术室用屏蔽墙挡住外来电磁波。
An elevator is a metal box, and the metal responds to an incoming electric field by rearranging surface charges that cancel the field inside — a Faraday cage. Radio waves can't get in, so the phone finds no signal. The same idea protects us elsewhere: lightning rods route current through metal to the ground, and shielded operating rooms keep external electromagnetic waves out.

为什么肥皂泡和光盘表面会泛出五彩的颜色？

Why do soap bubbles and the surface of a CD shimmer with color?

光盘表面刻着间距与光波长相当的密集凹槽，反射光在沟槽间发生干涉——不同波长的光在某些角度加强、在某些角度抵消，于是不同角度显出不同颜色，这叫衍射光栅效应。肥皂泡则是前后两个膜面反射的光发生薄膜干涉产生颜色。两者的颜色都不是颜料染的，而是光波叠加的结果。
A CD's surface carries fine grooves spaced about as far apart as light's wavelength; light reflecting off them interferes, strengthening some wavelengths and cancelling others at each viewing angle, so different angles show different colors — diffraction-grating color. A soap bubble colors light through thin-film interference between its two reflecting surfaces. Neither color comes from pigment; both are pure wave superpositions.
