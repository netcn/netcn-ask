十二平均律为什么把八度切成 12 个相等半音，却让每件乐器都“稍微”不准？

Why does twelve-tone equal temperament split an octave into 12 equal steps, leaving every instrument 'slightly' off?

纯律里最协和的和声要求频率比是有理数（如 3:2），但要在所有调上都好听，只能妥协成“每个半音乘相同系数”。这样除了八度，其他音程都和标准有理数略有偏差，换来可以随意转调。
Just intonation's sweetest intervals need rational frequency ratios (e.g. 3:2), but to sound good in every key you compromise by multiplying by one equal factor per semitone. So except the octave, intervals drift slightly from the ideal ratios — the price of free modulation.

调琴师为什么故意把钢琴的高音调得偏高、低音调得偏低？

Why do tuners deliberately stretch a piano's treble up and bass down?

真实琴弦有刚度，泛音频率略高于基频的整数倍，这叫非谐性。人耳判断八度是否准靠的是泛音是否对齐，因此按泛音对齐来调，高音区就自然被拉高、低音区被压低，这套做法叫拉伸调音。
Real strings have stiffness, so their overtones sit slightly above exact integer multiples — inharmonicity. Since the ear judges octaves by matching overtones, tuning to those partials naturally stretches the treble upward and the bass downward.

弦乐器按弦改变的是有效长度，为什么同一根弦能拉出整条音阶？

Pressing a string changes its effective length — why can one string produce a whole scale?

手指按下去把振动段缩短，频率随长度反比升高，于是同一根弦能奏出不同音。要恰好得到十二平均律的音，按压位置按固定比例移动即可，这也是品丝和指板距离的设计依据。
A finger shortens the vibrating segment, raising frequency inversely with length, so one string yields many notes. To hit equal-temperament pitches, the stopping points shift by fixed ratios — the very logic behind frets and fingerboard spacing.

小提琴为什么一定要有共鸣箱和两个f形孔？

Why does a violin need a body and two f-holes?

细弦本身推动的空气太少，声音很弱。琴弦的振动经琴码传到面板，面板带动整块箱体和箱内空气一起振动，把振动有效地耦合到空气中；f孔让箱内空气柱参与共振，尤其加强低音。
A thin string moves almost no air on its own. The bridge passes its vibration to the top plate, which drives the whole box and the air inside, coupling the energy into the room; the f-holes let the internal air resonate and reinforce the low notes.

管乐器的“超吹”为什么能不按键就吹出高八度的音？

Why can a wind player 'overblow' to get a higher octave without pressing keys?

管里的空气柱本来以基频振动，用力吹会激发“第二泛音”（频率加倍），听感就是高八度。相当于让同一根空气柱多绕一个波节，音就跳上去。
The air column normally vibrates at its fundamental; blowing harder excites the second harmonic (double the frequency), heard as an octave up. It's the same air column gaining one more node, jumping the pitch up.

为什么把耳朵贴在长管一端、敲另一端，听起来比敲短管低沉？

Why does tapping one end of a long tube sound deeper than tapping a short one?

管子里的空气柱越长，能装下的“半波”越长，基频越低，声音就越闷越低。短管的空气柱短，基频高，听起来更脆。
A longer air column fits a longer half-wavelength, lowering the fundamental and the tone. A short tube's column gives a higher fundamental, so it sounds brighter and thinner.

两把同型号的吉他弹同一个音，为什么听起来还是不一样？

Why do two identical-model guitars still sound different on the same note?

同型号吉他的音色差异主要来自“音色”而非音高——音色由泛音的相对强弱（泛音谱）与琴体的共鸣方式决定。即便型号相同，木材、做工、硬件和调校的微小差别也会改变共鸣峰，使同一个音含有不同的频率组合与衰减。所以人耳仍能分辨出两把琴。
Two same-model guitars differ in timbre, not pitch: timbre is set by the relative strengths of the overtones (the overtone spectrum) and how each body resonates. Even with the same model, tiny differences in wood, construction, hardware, and setup shift those resonances, giving the same note a different mix of frequencies and decay. That's why the ear can still tell two instruments apart.

管乐器在冷天为什么音会偏低？

Why do wind instruments play flat in the cold?

管乐的音高由管内空气柱的驻波决定，频率正比于空气中的声速。温度每降低约1℃，声速大约减少0.6米每秒，音高就随之下沉，所以乐手上场前要先把乐器吹热。
Their pitch comes from standing waves in the air column, and that frequency scales with the speed of sound. Sound slows by roughly 0.6 m/s per degree of cooling, so the pitch sags — which is why players warm the instrument up first.

吉他把弦拧紧一点，音就变高，这是为什么？

Why does tightening a guitar string raise its pitch?

弦的基频与张力的平方根成正比，与长度和线密度成反比。拧紧增加张力，横波在弦上跑得更快，每秒完成的振动次数就更多，听起来音更高。
A string's fundamental frequency rises with the square root of tension and falls with length and mass per unit length. Tightening it makes the wave travel faster, so it vibrates more times per second.

钢琴里的琴弦为什么有的又长又粗，有的又短又细？

Why are some piano strings long and thick while others are short and thin?

又长又粗的弦晃得慢，发出低沉的声音；又短又细的弦晃得快，声音就又高又亮。钢琴把它们排在一起，才能从最低音弹到最高音。
Long, thick strings wiggle slowly and give deep notes, while short, thin ones wiggle fast and sound high and bright. A piano lines them up so it can play from the lowest note to the highest.

为什么同一首曲子用钢琴和小提琴奏出来“味道”完全不同？

Why does the same tune sound totally different on piano versus violin?

音高由基频决定，但乐器还同时发出许多成整数倍的“泛音”，各自强弱不同，这就是“音色”。钢琴和小提琴的泛音组合不一样，所以哪怕音一样，我们也能一眼（耳）认出是谁在奏。
Pitch comes from the fundamental, but instruments also emit many integer-multiple overtones with different strengths — that's timbre. Piano and violin have different overtone recipes, so even on the same note we tell them apart by ear.

为什么敲一下玻璃杯边，杯里水越多声音越低？

Why does tapping a glass rim sound lower when it holds more water?

杯子和水一起振动，水越多，要带动的“重量”越大，振动就越慢，声音也就越低。空杯轻巧，振动快，声音就尖。
The glass and water vibrate together; more water means more mass to move, so it vibrates slower and sounds lower. An empty glass is light, vibrates fast and sounds higher.
