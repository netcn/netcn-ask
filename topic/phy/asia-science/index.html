日本物理学家汤川秀树因什么工作获得诺贝尔奖？

For what work did Japanese physicist Hideki Yukawa win the Nobel Prize?

他预言强相互作用靠一种新的粒子（介子，后被证实为π介子）传递，为理解原子核内的力打开了大门。
He predicted the strong force is carried by a new particle, the meson (later the pion), opening the door to understanding the nuclear force.

印度天体物理学家钱德拉塞卡因什么闻名？

What is Indian astrophysicist Subrahmanyan Chandrasekhar famous for?

他算出白矮星有个最大质量（钱德拉塞卡极限），超过它就会塌成中子星或黑洞，改变了恒星演化理论。
He found a maximum mass for white dwarfs, the Chandrasekhar limit; above it a star collapses to a neutron star or black hole, reshaping stellar evolution.

杨振宁和李政道因什么发现获诺贝尔奖？

For what discovery did Yang and Lee win the Nobel Prize?

他们提出在弱相互作用中“宇称”不守恒，即左右对称在微观某些过程里会被打破，颠覆了旧观念。
They proposed that parity is not conserved in weak interactions—left-right symmetry can be broken in some microscopic processes, overturning old belief.

美籍华人崔琦因什么发现获诺贝尔物理学奖？

For what discovery did Chinese-American physicist Daniel Tsui win the Nobel Prize in Physics?

他发现分数量子霍尔效应：在极强磁场和极低温下，二维电子气的电阻出现由分数电荷载流子引起的平台。
He discovered the fractional quantum Hall effect: at extreme fields and low temperatures, a 2D electron gas shows plateaus from fractionally charged carriers.

吴健雄如何用实验验证杨、李的宇称不守恒？

How did Chien-Shiung Wu experimentally verify Yang and Lee's parity violation?

她用极低温下排列整齐的钴-60原子核做β衰变，发现电子更爱朝一个方向飞，证明弱作用里左右真的不对称。
Using cobalt-60 nuclei aligned at very low temperature, she found beta electrons preferred one direction, proving weak interactions are not left-right symmetric.

应县木塔历经近千年地震而不倒，木结构有什么物理巧思？

Why has the Yingxian Wooden Pagoda survived millennia of earthquakes?

它用斗拱把构件咬合在一起，地震时木材的柔性和节点摩擦能吸收和耗散晃动能量，整体像会“卸力”的弹簧。
Its bracket sets (dougong) lock parts together; wood flexibility and joint friction dissipate shaking energy, so the structure sheds quake forces like a spring.

阿拉伯学者伊本·海赛姆对光学有什么贡献？

What did the Arab scholar Ibn al-Haytham contribute to optics?

他系统用实验研究光与视觉，解释了小孔成像（暗箱），并提出光沿直线传播、由物体进入眼睛，奠定了现代光学方法。
He studied light and vision experimentally, explained the camera obscura (pinhole imaging), and argued light travels straight from objects into the eye, founding modern optics.

What physics and materials ideas sit behind papermaking and printing among China's four great inventions?

Papermaking disperses fibers in water then drains them into a mat; printing uses ink wetting and capillarity to transfer characters—both lean on fluid and surface physics.

张衡的地动仪真的能“预测”地震方向吗，靠什么原理？

Could Zhang Heng's seismoscope indicate a quake's direction, and by what principle?

地动仪内部有悬垂的摆，远处地震传来的地面振动让摆倒向震源方向，拨动对应方向的机关。这是利用惯性感知地面运动。
Inside, a suspended pendulum lurched toward the incoming seismic wave and tripped the mechanism on that side—using inertia to sense ground motion.
