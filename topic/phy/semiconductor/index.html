当晶体管缩到原子尺度，量子隧穿为什么会让“关不掉”的漏电流变严重？

As transistors reach atomic scales, why does quantum tunneling make 'off' leakage worse?

栅极和沟道之间的绝缘薄层薄到几个原子时，电子有一定概率直接“穿墙”过去，不再受开关控制。这种隧穿电流随厚度指数增长，是继续微缩的物理天花板之一。
When the insulating layer between gate and channel is only a few atoms thick, electrons can tunnel straight through it regardless of the switch. That tunneling current grows exponentially as it thins — a hard floor for shrinking.

摩尔定律本质上遇到的，是量子力学瓶颈还是热力学瓶颈？

Is Moore's Law fundamentally hitting a quantum-mechanics limit or a thermodynamics limit?

两者都有。量子隧穿让“关断”越来越难（量子极限），而每一次开关都要耗散能量、产生热，散热跟不上就烧坏芯片（热力学极限）。所以瓶颈既是物理的，也是能量的。
Both. Tunneling makes 'off' harder (quantum limit), while every switch dissipates energy as heat and cooling can't keep up (thermodynamic limit). The wall is physical and energetic at once.

How does a transistor work?

Why build chips in cleaner-than-surgery cleanrooms, yet defects still happen?

因为最细的线条只有几个纳米，一粒落到上面的灰尘就是一座“山”，会让电路断线。即使层层过滤，残余分子和光刻本身的统计涨落也会留下少量缺陷，所以良率永远在和物理极限搏斗。

纯硅在接近绝对零度时几乎不导电，为什么温度一升高它的导电能力就迅速变强？

Pure silicon barely conducts near absolute zero, so why does its conductivity rise quickly as temperature increases?

纯半导体里几乎没有自由载流子，但温度升高会不断把价带电子激发越过约 1.1 eV 的带隙进入导带，同时留下带正电的空穴。载流子数量随温度呈指数增长，所以导电性急剧增强——这与金属恰好相反。
An intrinsic semiconductor has almost no free carriers, but heating continually excites electrons across its ~1.1 eV band gap into the conduction band, leaving positively charged holes. The carrier number grows exponentially with temperature, so conductivity rises sharply—the opposite of what metals do.

Silicon's band gap is about 1.1 eV — does that number decide how much heat a chip can tolerate?

禁带是电子从“被绑住”到“能自由导电”要跨过的能量门槛。室温下热运动很难把电子推过去，所以硅是半导体；温度太高时大量电子被热“吵醒”乱跑，芯片就会漏电、失控。
The band gap is the energy an electron needs to break free and conduct. At room temperature thermal energy rarely bridges it, so silicon stays semiconducting; too much heat excites many electrons and the chip leaks and misbehaves.

PN结为什么在正向偏置时电流大增、反向偏置时几乎截止——二极管整流靠的就是它？

Why does a p–n junction pass large current under forward bias but nearly block it in reverse—is this how a diode rectifies?

两者接触处形成内建电场和耗尽层。正向偏置削弱内建场、缩小耗尽层，多数载流子得以顺畅通过；反向偏置则加宽耗尽层、几乎堵死电流。这种单向导通正是二极管把交流整成直流的物理基础。
At the junction an internal electric field and depletion region form. Forward bias weakens that field and narrows the region, letting majority carriers flow; reverse bias widens it and nearly blocks current. This one-way conduction is exactly how a diode rectifies AC into DC.

硅为什么是半导体——既不像铜那样随便导电，也不像橡胶那样完全绝缘？

Why is silicon a semiconductor—neither conducting freely like copper nor insulating completely like rubber?

铜这类金属的导带本身未填满，电子不必越过任何能隙就能导电；橡胶等绝缘体的能隙宽达数电子伏，室温下几乎没有电子越得过去。硅的价带与导带之间只隔约 1.1 eV，室温热运动就能把少量电子送过去，导电能力因此介于两者之间。正因这道“适中”的能隙，掺杂、光照或外加电场都能大幅改变硅的载流子数目，使其导电性可被精细调控。
In a metal like copper the conduction band is only partly filled, so electrons conduct without crossing any gap at all; in an insulator such as rubber the gap is several electron-volts wide and essentially no electron can cross it at room temperature. Silicon's valence and conduction bands are separated by only about 1.1 eV, so ordinary thermal energy lifts a small number of electrons across, putting its conductivity in between. Because that gap is 'moderate', doping, illumination or an applied field can change silicon's carrier number drastically, making its conductivity finely controllable.

芯片里的“沙子”怎么变成大脑？

How does the sand in a chip become a brain?

沙子提炼出硅，做成能控制电流开关的晶体管，几十亿个晶体管排成电路，就成了处理器。
Sand yields silicon, shaped into transistors that switch current; billions wired together form a processor.

芯片工厂为什么连一粒灰尘都怕？

Why are chip factories afraid of even a single speck of dust?

因为现在最细的电路线比灰尘还细，一粒灰尘落到硅片上，就可能让一根“电线”断掉或短路。所以工厂要在比手术室还干净的无尘室里造芯片。
Today's finest circuit lines are thinner than dust, so one speck landing on the wafer can break or short a 'wire'. That's why chips are made in cleanrooms cleaner than operating theaters.

掺了磷或硼的硅，为什么会多出“自由”的电子或空穴？

Why does silicon doped with phosphorus or boron gain free electrons or holes?

磷原子有五个价电子，放进硅里会多出一个“闲着”的电子，容易到处跑，叫 N 型。硼只有三个价电子，留下一个缺电子的“空穴”，别的电子可以补进来，等效于空穴在移动，叫 P 型。
Phosphorus has five valence electrons, leaving one loosely bound 'extra' electron free to move (N-type). Boron has three, leaving a hole that other electrons fill, so the hole appears to move (P-type).
