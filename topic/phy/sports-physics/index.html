游泳时身体位置稍微下沉一点，阻力为什么会明显变化？

Why does swimming a little deeper change drag so much?

贴着水面游会不断制造波浪，这部分兴波阻力随速度的高次幂增长；稍微下沉可以减少造波，但迎水面积和摩擦阻力又会增加。顶级泳姿是在这两者之间找平衡。
Swimming right at the surface constantly generates waves, and this wave-making drag grows very steeply with speed; going slightly deeper reduces wave making but adds frontal and friction drag. Elite technique balances the two.

自由潜水员下潜到100米，为什么身体没被压坏？

Why is a free diver at 100 metres not crushed?

水是不可压缩的，人体约 60% 也是水。下潜到 100 米时外界静水压约 10 个大气压，但潜水员通过均衡（equalization）让肺内气体与外界压力一致，体内含气腔（肺、鼻窦、中耳）被压缩而非被“压爆”。软组织因主要由水组成几乎不被压缩，骨骼和实心组织也能承受。真正危险的是气体体积变化导致的肺挤压伤（若未均衡）和氮麻醉/缺氧，而非单纯外部压力把身体压坏。
Water is nearly incompressible, and about 60% of the human body is water. At 100 m the hydrostatic pressure is about 10 atmospheres, but a freediver equalizes the air spaces (lungs, sinuses, middle ears) so internal pressure matches the outside; the gas-filled cavities are compressed rather than crushed. Soft tissue, being mostly water, barely compresses, and bone and solid tissue tolerate the load. The real dangers are barotrauma from unequalized air spaces and nitrogen narcosis/hypoxia, not the external pressure physically crushing the body.

跳远运动员在空中拼命摆臂收腿，真的能跳更远吗？

Do long jumpers really gain distance by swinging their arms in mid-air?

跳远时运动员在空中摆臂、收腿，是角动量守恒下的“补偿动作”：上半身向后摆，下半身相对向前，使双腿能前伸、身体翻转到利于落地的姿势。但这并不能改变整体重心按抛体运动的抛物线轨迹，只优化落地姿态、减少脚跟越线前的“前伸距离”损失，从而多争取几厘米。所以摆臂有用，但增益来自姿态而非凭空增加远度。
In the long jump, mid-air arm swings and leg tucks are counter-movements governed by angular-momentum conservation: swinging the upper body back lets the legs come forward, rotating the body into a landing posture before the feet hit. This does not alter the center-of-mass parabola—that follows projectile motion—but it optimizes the landing position and reduces lost distance from the heels crossing the line, gaining a few centimeters. So the swing helps, but by posture, not by magically adding range.

跑鞋里那块碳板到底怎么帮人省力？

How does the carbon plate in a running shoe actually save energy?

碳板加上高回弹泡棉，能把落地时压缩的能量更多地还回来，减少泡棉内部的黏性损耗。它还让前脚掌变硬，减少脚趾关节做的负功，相当于把脚变成一根更长的杠杆。
The stiff plate plus resilient foam returns more of the energy stored at landing instead of wasting it in the foam. It also stiffens the forefoot so the toe joints do less negative work, effectively lengthening the foot's lever.

撑杆跳能跳到六米多，能量是怎么一步步转换的？

How does energy flow through a six-metre pole vault?

助跑把化学能变成动能，插杆后玻璃纤维杆弯曲，把动能存成弹性势能；杆回弹时又把能量交还给运动员，转成越过横杆的重力势能。
The run-up turns muscle energy into kinetic energy, the bending fibreglass pole stores it as elastic energy, and the recoil hands it back as the gravitational potential energy needed to clear the bar.

足球里的香蕉球或棒球的曲球为什么会拐弯？请用马格努斯效应解释其受力机制。

Why does a curling free kick in soccer or a curveball in baseball swerve sideways? Explain the mechanism in terms of the Magnus effect.

旋转的球体通过摩擦带动周围空气随之旋转，使两侧边界层相对来流一侧顺转、一侧逆转，分离点位置因而不同：一侧气流贴体更久、流速更快、压强更低，另一侧相反。两侧压强差产生垂直于飞行方向的侧向力，即马格努斯力，其大小随转速和球速增大，轨迹因此弯成弧线；改变旋转方向，弧线也随之反弯。
A spinning ball entrains nearby air through friction, so the relative rotation of the boundary layers differs on its two sides and the separation points shift: on one side the flow stays attached longer, moves faster and has lower pressure, while the other side behaves oppositely. The resulting pressure difference produces a lateral force perpendicular to the flight direction — the Magnus force — whose magnitude grows with spin rate and ball speed, bending the trajectory into a curve; reversing the spin bends it the other way.

花样滑冰选手收紧手臂时转速明显加快，展开手臂又慢下来。这背后是哪条守恒定律？转快之后多出的动能又从何而来？

A figure skater spins noticeably faster when pulling her arms in, and slows down again when extending them. Which conservation law explains this, and where does the extra kinetic energy come from?

忽略冰面摩擦时，人体绕竖直轴的角动量 L=Iω 守恒。收臂让质量向转轴集中，转动惯量 I 变小，角速度 ω 相应增大，转速成倍上升。而转动动能 E=Iω^2=L^2/(2I)，I 减小时动能不降反升，这份额外能量来自肌肉克服离心趋势所做的功；展臂时过程反转，肌肉做负功，动能随之回落。
With ice friction negligible, the skater's angular momentum about the vertical axis, L = Iω, is conserved. Drawing the arms in concentrates mass near the rotation axis, shrinking the moment of inertia I, so the angular velocity ω rises and the spin rate multiplies accordingly. Since rotational kinetic energy is E = Iω^2 = L^2/(2I), a smaller I means higher kinetic energy: the surplus comes from muscular work done against centrifugal pull. Extending the arms reverses the process — muscles do negative work and the kinetic energy drops back.

乒乓球的上旋球为什么过网后会突然往下扎？

Why does a topspin table-tennis ball dive down after crossing the net?

球向前旋转时，上方空气被带着走、下方被顶着走，形成上下压力差，产生一个向下的马格努斯力。这个力叠加在重力上，球的下落就显得特别急。
Forward spin speeds the air over the top and slows it underneath, creating a pressure difference and a downward Magnus force. Added to gravity, it makes the ball drop unusually sharply.

百米起跑为什么要蹬起跑器，而不是站着起跑？

Why do sprinters push off starting blocks instead of standing?

起跑器提供一个稳固的斜面，让运动员能在短时间内施加很大的向后作用力，地面的反作用力就把人向前推。这段较大的冲量使起跑的速度增量更快。
The blocks give a solid inclined surface against which the sprinter can push hard backwards, and the ground's reaction pushes him forward. The larger impulse over that short time produces a faster gain in speed.

花样滑冰运动员一收手臂，为什么立刻转得飞快？

Why does a figure skater spin faster the moment she pulls in her arms?

旋转时角动量几乎不变。手臂收拢让质量更靠近转轴，转动惯量变小，于是转速必须变大来保持角动量守恒。
Her angular momentum stays nearly constant. Pulling the arms in brings mass closer to the axis, lowering the moment of inertia, so the rotation rate must rise to compensate.

举重时为什么必须让杠铃紧贴身体上提？

Why must a weightlifter keep the bar close to the body?

杠铃离身体越远，重力对髋和腰形成的力臂越长，需要的力矩越大。贴身上提能把力臂压到最小，既省力也降低腰部受伤风险。
The farther the bar drifts from the body, the longer the lever arm about the hips and spine, and the larger the required torque. Keeping it close minimises that lever arm, saving effort and protecting the back.
