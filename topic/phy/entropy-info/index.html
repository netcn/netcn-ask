香农熵与热力学熵是同一回事吗？

Are Shannon entropy and thermodynamic entropy the same thing?

形式相似但层次不同：香农熵度量信息的不确定性，热力学熵度量微观状态的混乱；朗道尔原理在“擦除信息须耗能”处把两者桥接起来。
They look alike but differ in level: Shannon entropy measures informational uncertainty, thermodynamic entropy measures microscopic disorder; Landauer's principle bridges them via the energy cost of erasing.

麦克斯韦妖与朗道尔原理如何闭合“信息与熵”的循环？

How do Maxwell's demon and Landauer's principle close the information-entropy loop?

妖靠获得分子信息来减熵，但记忆终须擦除，擦除按朗道尔极限放热，使总熵不减；信息因此被纳入热力学，计算机耗能有了下限。
The demon lowers entropy using information, but erasure costs Landauer heat, keeping total entropy up; information enters thermodynamics and sets a lower bound on computing energy.

黑洞的“贝肯斯坦–霍金熵”意味着什么？

What does Bekenstein-Hawking black-hole entropy mean?

黑洞的熵正比于视界面积而非体积，暗示信息可能“写”在表面上；这与热力学熵呼应，也引发“信息悖论”——掉进去的信息去哪了。
Black-hole entropy scales with horizon area, not volume, hinting information lives on a surface; it mirrors thermodynamic entropy and sparks the information paradox of what falls in.

信息会被“销毁”吗，还是只是扩散了？

Is information destroyed or just spread out?

朗道尔原理说：每擦除 1 比特确定信息，至少向环境释放 kT·ln2 的热量。信息并非消失，而是变成更分散、更难回收的热无序。
Landauer's principle says erasing one bit costs at least kT·ln2 of heat. Information is not destroyed but spread into harder-to-recover thermal disorder.

“熵”是不是就是混乱？

Is entropy just messiness?

粗略说，熵衡量一个系统有多少种排布方式；方式越多越“乱”，热量也越难变回有用的功。
Roughly, entropy counts how many ways a system can be arranged; more ways means more disorder and less usable energy.
