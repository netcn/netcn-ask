为什么回旋镖能飞出去又转着圈飞回投掷者手中？

Why does a boomerang fly out and then curve back to the thrower?

回旋镖两翼旋转时，一侧相对空气速度更大、升力更强，产生一个使飞行面偏转的力矩。配合初始自旋，形成类似陀螺进动的弯曲轨迹，于是绕圈返回。
As the boomerang spins, one wing moves faster through the air than the other, creating unequal lift that tilts its plane of flight. Combined with its initial spin, this produces a gyroscopic precession that curves the path back to the thrower.

为什么松开手的悠悠球能顺着绳子自动回到手里？

Why does a yo-yo climb back up the string to your hand on its own?

悠悠球下落到最低点时，轴的凹槽与绳摩擦使它在原地空转，储存的旋转动能通过绳子重新缠绕把球拉回。靠手腕一抖改变绳的张力，就能让它回弹。
At the bottom the yo-yo's axle groove lets it spin in place, and the stored rotational energy rewinds the string to pull it back up. A flick of the wrist changes the string tension and triggers the return.

为什么旋转起来的陀螺不容易倒，停下来反而会倒？

Why does a spinning top stay upright and only falls when it slows down?

高速旋转的陀螺具有角动量，根据角动量守恒，它会抵抗方向改变而保持自转轴稳定。转速越低角动量越小，进动加剧，最终失去平衡倒下。
A fast-spinning top has angular momentum, and by conservation of angular momentum its spin axis resists changing direction and stays stable. As it slows, the angular momentum drops, precession grows, and it eventually loses balance and falls.

为什么用手一搓竹蜻蜓的杆，它就能飞起来？

Why does a bamboo dragonfly fly up when you spin its stick?

快速转动的叶片像小螺旋桨，把空气向下推，空气反作用力给叶片一个向上的升力。当升力大于自身重力，竹蜻蜓就腾空而起。
The rapidly spinning blades act like a tiny propeller, pushing air downward so the air pushes back upward on the blades. When that lift exceeds its weight, the bamboo dragonfly lifts off.

为什么吹出来的肥皂泡总是近似球形？

Why are soap bubbles always roughly spherical?

表面张力会让液体表面收缩到最小面积，而在体积相同时球形的表面积最小。肥皂泡内外气压差也促使它趋向球形以平衡张力。
Surface tension pulls a liquid surface toward the smallest possible area, and for a given volume a sphere has the minimum surface area. The pressure difference across the film also drives the bubble toward a sphere to balance the tension.

为什么风筝逆着风跑起来反而能飞上天空？

Why does a kite rise into the sky when you run against the wind?

风筝面与风向成一定夹角，气流经过时上下表面流速不同形成压强差，产生向上的升力。跑动让相对风速增大，升力超过重力时风筝就升起来了。
A kite is held at an angle to the wind, and the different air speeds above and below its surface create a pressure difference that produces lift. Running increases the relative wind speed, and once lift exceeds weight the kite rises.

为什么弹簧玩具能从楼梯上自己一级级翻下去？

Why does a coiled spring toy walk down a staircase by itself one step at a time?

弹簧圈一端搭在下一级台阶时，重心前移使整条弹簧翻滚过去，能量在金属圈间连续传递。每一翻转都把势能变成滚动的动能，于是不断翻跟头下楼。
When one end of the coil reaches the next step, its center of mass shifts forward and the whole spring tumbles over, with energy passing continuously along the coils. Each flip converts potential energy into rolling motion, so it keeps tumbling down.

为什么阳光下的肥皂泡会显现出彩虹般的彩色？

Why do soap bubbles show rainbow-like colors in sunlight?

肥皂膜很薄，前后表面的反射光会发生干涉，某些波长的光相互加强、某些相互抵消。膜的厚度不均，于是不同位置显现出不同颜色，形成彩纹。
A soap film is very thin, and light reflected from its front and back surfaces interferes, reinforcing some wavelengths and canceling others. Because the film thickness varies, different spots show different colors, producing the swirling pattern.

为什么松开充气的气球，它会一边喷气一边乱飞？

Why does a released inflated balloon zoom around while jetting air?

气球收缩把内部空气高速向后喷出，根据动量守恒，气球获得反方向的反冲力。喷口方向不断变化，反冲方向也随之改变，于是气球乱窜。
The balloon's skin squeezes air out the neck at high speed, and by conservation of momentum the balloon gets a recoil force in the opposite direction. As the nozzle swings around, the recoil direction changes too, so it darts about.

为什么不倒翁怎么推都倒不下去？

Why does a roly-poly toy never fall over no matter how you push it?

不倒翁底部重、重心很低，被推歪时重心升高，重力产生一个把它拉回正立的恢复力矩。只要重心低于支撑点附近，它就会自动回正。
A roly-poly has a heavy bottom and a very low center of gravity. When tipped, its center of mass rises, and gravity creates a restoring torque that pulls it back upright, so it self-rights as long as the center stays low.

为什么把气球在头发上摩擦几下，就能吸在墙壁上？

Why does a balloon rubbed on your hair stick to a wall?

摩擦使气球带上静电荷，靠近墙壁时吸引墙面上相反的感应电荷。静电吸引力足以克服气球重力，让它短暂贴在墙上。
Rubbing charges the balloon with static electricity, and when it nears the wall it attracts opposite induced charges on the surface. The electrostatic attraction is enough to overcome the balloon's weight and hold it there briefly.
