单透镜成像为什么会有色差？工程上怎么克服它？

Why do single lenses suffer chromatic aberration, and how do engineers correct it?

玻璃的折射率随波长变化，所以蓝光和红光经过同一透镜后会聚在不同位置，像的边缘出现彩色，这就是色差。经典解决方案是消色差双合透镜：把折射率与色散能力不同的两种玻璃胶合在一起，让两者的色散相互抵消，使两种波长的光会聚到同一点。此外还有球差、彗差、像散等几何像差，需要用多片透镜组合和特殊面型来校正。
Because a lens's refractive index depends on wavelength, blue and red light focus at different points, smearing the image edges with color — chromatic aberration. The classic fix is an achromatic doublet, two lenses of different dispersion glued together so their color shifts cancel and two wavelengths focus together. Other geometric aberrations, such as spherical aberration, coma and astigmatism, call for multi-element designs and aspheric surfaces.

激光为什么能保持一条极细的光束传很远，而普通灯光很快发散开？

Why can a laser stay a narrow beam over long distances while ordinary light spreads out quickly?

激光的光子几乎同频率、同相位、同方向，这种高度的空间与时间相干性让它以稳定的高斯光束传播、几乎不发散。普通光源发出的光相位杂乱、方向各异，所以迅速散开。相干性也正是激光能用于精密测距和全息的原因。
Laser light consists of photons with nearly the same frequency, phase, and direction; this strong spatial and temporal coherence lets it propagate as a stable Gaussian beam that hardly diverges. Ordinary sources emit light with random phases and directions, so they spread quickly. Coherence is also why lasers enable precise ranging and holography.

激光和普通灯光最根本的区别是什么？

What is the fundamental difference between a laser and ordinary light?

Laser light comes from stimulated emission, so all photons share frequency, phase, and direction—a coherent, monochromatic, narrow beam. Ordinary lamps emit random, incoherent light from countless atoms. That ordered beam is what allows precise cutting and ranging.

Why do deserts or hot roads sometimes show a fake pool of water or shimmering scene in the distance?

全息照片为什么有立体感？它和普通照片的记录方式有什么不同？

Why do holograms look three-dimensional, and how does their recording differ from ordinary photography?

普通照片只记录光的强度，丢失了相位信息。全息拍摄让物体反射的光与一束参考光在感光材料上发生干涉，把光场的振幅和相位一并编码成干涉条纹。用参考光照射这张图样时，干涉条纹会重新放出物体原来的光波，观察者移动视角时能看到视差，获得真实的立体感。钞票防伪全息图也是同样的干涉原理。
An ordinary photograph records only intensity and throws away phase. In holography, light scattered by the object interferes with a reference beam on the recording material, encoding both amplitude and phase as interference fringes. Illuminating that pattern with the reference beam reconstructs the object's original wavefront, and moving your viewpoint reveals real parallax, so the image looks solid. The holograms on banknotes use the same interference principle.

衍射光栅为什么能把白光精确地分解成光谱？

Why does a diffraction grating split white light into precise spectra?

光栅表面刻有数千条间距均匀的细槽，每条槽的衍射光之间发生干涉，只有在相邻光程差恰好等于整数倍波长的方向才会出现明亮的谱线。由于这个条件依赖波长，不同颜色的光被送到不同角度，白光因此被展开成光谱。光栅的角分辨率远高于棱镜，还能同时看到多级光谱，所以光谱仪普遍用它来分析星光和物质的成分。
A grating carries thousands of evenly spaced grooves, and the light diffracted from them interferes: bright lines appear only in directions where the path difference between neighboring grooves is an integer number of wavelengths. Because that condition depends on wavelength, each color emerges at its own angle and white light fans into a spectrum. Gratings resolve angles far better than prisms and show multiple orders, which is why spectrometers use them to analyze starlight and material composition.

迈克尔逊干涉仪是怎么测出比波长还小的长度变化的？

How does a Michelson interferometer measure length changes far smaller than the wavelength?

一束光经分光镜分成两路，分别射向两面镜子后返回并重新会合，两臂的光程差决定干涉条纹的明暗。只要一面镜子移动半个波长，光的往返光程就变化一个波长，条纹恰好移动一个完整周期，因此亚微米乃至更小的位移都能被精确读出。引力波探测器LIGO正是把这一原理放大到数公里长的双臂，测出了比原子核直径还小的时空伸缩。
A beam splitter divides light into two arms that reflect off mirrors and recombine, and the arm-length difference sets the interference pattern. Moving one mirror by half a wavelength changes the round-trip path by a full wavelength, shifting the fringes by exactly one period, so displacements far below a micron are readable. The gravitational-wave detector LIGO scales the same idea to multi-kilometer arms and senses stretches smaller than an atomic nucleus.
