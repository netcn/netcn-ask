马约拉纳零模为何被视为拓扑量子比特的“天然纠错”？

Why are Majorana zero modes seen as natural error correction for topological qubits?

马约拉纳零模成对出现、非局域地编码量子信息，局部扰动难以翻转它，相当于自带拓扑保护，有望实现抗错的拓扑量子计算。
Majorana zero modes come in pairs and store quantum info non-locally, so local noise cannot easily flip them—built-in topological protection for fault-tolerant qubits.

量子自旋霍尔效应与量子霍尔效应关键区别？

What is the key difference between quantum spin Hall and quantum Hall effects?

量子霍尔需外加磁场并破缺时间反演对称；量子自旋霍尔靠自旋–轨道耦合，在无磁场下让相反自旋沿相反边缘走，时间反演对称被保留，更易在实验中稳定。
Quantum Hall needs an external field and breaks time-reversal symmetry; quantum spin Hall uses spin-orbit coupling so opposite spins travel opposite edges with time-reversal symmetry intact, easier to stabilize.

量子霍尔效应和拓扑有什么关系？

What is the link between the quantum Hall effect and topology?

在强磁场低温下，二维电子气出现量子化霍尔电导，其边缘导电通道受拓扑不变量（陈数）保护，对杂质免疫，是现代拓扑物态的雏形。
In strong fields and low temperatures a 2D electron gas shows quantized Hall conductance; its edge channels are protected by a topological invariant (Chern number) and immune to disorder—an early topological state.

拓扑绝缘体的表面为什么“刀枪不入”？

Why is a topological insulator's surface so robust?

拓扑绝缘体内部绝缘、表面却导电，而且这种表面态受拓扑保护，普通杂质和缺陷无法轻易关掉它，像带“护盾”的电线。
A topological insulator is insulating inside but conducts on its surface; those surface states are topologically protected, so ordinary dirt and defects cannot easily shut them off.

“拓扑”和我们平时的保护有什么关系？

What does topology have to do with protection?

拓扑研究物体被拉伸时保持不变的性质，比如甜甜圈的洞。某些材料靠这种“洞”保护电子通道不被杂质打乱。
Topology studies properties unchanged by stretching, like a donut's hole. Some materials use such features to protect electron paths from dirt.
