摩擦过的气球为什么能吸起小纸屑？

Why does a rubbed balloon pick up paper bits?

气球在头发上摩擦后会带电，电就把轻轻的纸屑吸了过来。
After rubbing on hair, the balloon gets electric charge that pulls tiny paper bits to it.

Why can magnets attract iron?

磁铁有磁场，能改变铁内部微小磁域的方向，使铁被吸引。
A magnet's field aligns tiny domains inside iron, pulling it closer.

How is static electricity actually made?

摩擦时电子从一个物体跳到另一个物体上，两边就带上了相反的静电。
Rubbing makes electrons jump from one object to another, leaving both with opposite static charges.

磁铁为什么能吸引铁？

Why can a magnet attract iron?

铁等铁磁材料内部有许多微小的磁畴，平时方向杂乱。外磁场会让这些磁畴趋向对齐，使铁整体被磁化并受到磁场吸引。一旦撤去外场，部分磁性可能保留，这就是永磁体。
Ferromagnetic materials like iron contain many tiny magnetic domains that are normally random. An external field aligns these domains, magnetizing the iron so it is attracted. Removing the field can leave some magnetization behind, which is how permanent magnets form.

开关怎么控制灯亮和灯灭？

How does a switch control a light turning on and off?

开关合上时电路连成一圈，电通了灯就亮；断开时电停了灯就灭。
Closing the switch completes the loop so electricity flows and the light turns on; opening it stops the flow and the light goes off.
