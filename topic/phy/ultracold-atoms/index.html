什么是玻色-爱因斯坦凝聚？为什么说它是一种宏观量子现象？

What is Bose-Einstein condensation, and in what sense is it a macroscopic quantum phenomenon?

玻色子不受泡利不相容原理限制。当稀薄玻色气体冷却到临界温度以下时，占相当大比例的全同原子集体落入同一个最低能量量子态，由单一多体波函数描述，这就是玻色-爱因斯坦凝聚。稀薄原子气体的临界温度约为百纳开尔文量级，1995年在铷-87蒸气中首次实现，如今是研究超流、量子相变和物质波干涉的高度可控平台。
Bosons are exempt from the Pauli exclusion principle. When a dilute gas of bosons is cooled below a critical temperature, a sizable fraction of identical atoms collapses into a single lowest-energy quantum state described by one many-body wavefunction; this is Bose-Einstein condensation. Dilute atomic gases reach the critical temperature at hundreds of nanokelvin; the effect was first realized in rubidium-87 vapor in 1995 and now serves as a highly controllable platform for superfluidity, quantum phase transitions and matter-wave interferometry.

激光冷却已能把原子降到微开尔文量级，为什么制备玻色-爱因斯坦凝聚还必须补上“蒸发冷却”这一步？

Laser cooling already chills atoms to microkelvin temperatures — why is an extra evaporative-cooling stage still indispensable for reaching Bose-Einstein condensation?

光学方法受多普勒极限与反冲极限约束，通常止步于微开尔文量级，仍比多数原子的凝聚温度高一两个数量级，强散射光还持续扰动气体。蒸发冷却则逐步降低磁阱阱深，放走动能最高的原子，余下的经弹性碰撞重新热化，每轮都带走熵并降温；循环数十秒后温度降到百纳开尔文级，热德布罗意波长超过原子间距，宏观比例的原子涌入同一基态形成凝聚体。
Optical methods hit the Doppler and recoil limits near the microkelvin range — still typically one to two orders of magnitude above the condensation temperatures of most species — while strongly scattered light keeps disturbing the gas. Evaporative cooling instead progressively lowers the depth of a magnetic trap so the most energetic atoms escape, and elastic collisions rethermalize those left behind, removing entropy and heat each round. After tens of seconds the gas sits at hundreds of nanokelvin, the thermal de Broglie wavelength exceeds the interatomic spacing, and a macroscopic fraction collapses into one common ground state.

如何通过旋转被囚禁的超冷原子气体直接证明玻色-爱因斯坦凝聚体的超流性？

How does spinning a trapped ultracold atomic gas directly demonstrate the superfluidity of a Bose-Einstein condensate?

缓慢旋转阱中的凝聚体时，经典流体式的整体转动被量子化环流条件禁止：凝聚体只能通过生成涡旋来响应，每根涡旋的环量严格等于h/m，芯部原子密度降为零。转速升高后，大量涡旋自发排成规则的三角形阿布里科索夫点阵。2001年Abo-Shaeer等人首次在钠凝聚体中拍摄到这种涡旋点阵，直观展示了宏观波函数的相位相干性与超流本质。
When the condensate rotates slowly, classical solid-body rotation is forbidden by the quantized-circulation condition: the gas responds by nucleating vortices whose circulation equals exactly h/m, with vanishing density at each core. As the rotation rate rises, many vortices spontaneously arrange into a regular triangular Abrikosov lattice. In 2001 Abo-Shaeer and coworkers imaged such vortex lattices in a sodium condensate for the first time, directly revealing the phase coherence of the macroscopic wavefunction and its superfluid nature.
