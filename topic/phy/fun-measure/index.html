在微波炉里放一块巧克力，看哪融化，真能“量”出光速吗？

Can you really 'measure' the speed of light with chocolate in a microwave?

微波炉里的微波形成驻波，只有某些点最热。量出两块融化区的距离就是半个波长，再乘已知的微波频率，就能算出光速。方法粗糙但原理和科学家一样。
Microwave energy forms standing waves with hot spots. Measuring the gap between two melted spots gives half a wavelength; multiply by the known microwave frequency and you get the speed of light. Crude, but the principle matches the real method.

为什么先看到闪电、后听到雷声，还能用它估出雷暴离你多远？

Why do we see lightning before thunder — and can that tell how far the storm is?

光比声音快得多：闪电的光几乎瞬间到，雷声却要慢慢爬。看到闪后数几秒才听到雷，用“秒数÷3”大约就是多少公里远。
Light is far faster than sound: the flash arrives instantly, thunder crawls. Count seconds after the flash until thunder; divide by 3 for roughly the distance in kilometers.

用一根棍子和它的影子，怎么在没有尺子时估出大树的高度？

With just a stick and its shadow, how can you estimate a tree's height without a ruler?

同一时刻，实物和影子的长度比例对谁都一样（相似三角形）。量出小棍长、小棍影、大树影，用“树高/树影 = 棍长/棍影”一算就出来了。
At the same moment, the ratio of object to shadow is the same for everything (similar triangles). Measure stick, stick-shadow and tree-shadow, then tree height = stick × (tree-shadow / stick-shadow).
