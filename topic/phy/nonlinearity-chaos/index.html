孤子为什么能在非线性介质中稳定传播？

Why can solitons propagate stably in nonlinear media?

孤子是色散（使波展宽）与非线性（使波陡化）相互平衡形成的局域波包，能在长距离传播中保持形状和速度不变，常见于光纤通信和水面波。
A soliton is a localized wave packet where dispersion (broadening) balances nonlinearity (steepening), preserving its shape and speed over long distances; seen in optical fibers and water waves.

湍流为何被称为经典物理未解难题？

Why is turbulence called an unsolved problem of classical physics?

湍流是流体在雷诺数高时出现的多尺度涡旋级联，能量从大尺度层层传到小尺度耗散，缺乏普适封闭解，其统计理论至今未能完全解决。
Turbulence is the multi-scale vortex cascade at high Reynolds number, where energy cascades from large to small scales and dissipates; no universal closed solution exists, so its statistical theory remains unsolved.

费根鲍姆常数描述了混沌的什么普适规律？

What universal law of chaos does the Feigenbaum constant describe?

在单峰映射等系统中，周期加倍出现混沌的间隔比趋近普适常数δ≈4.669，表明不同非线性系统走向混沌的方式具有相同的标度规律。
In unimodal maps, the ratio of intervals between period-doubling approaches the universal constant δ≈4.669, showing diverse nonlinear systems reach chaos by the same scaling law.

洛伦兹吸引子为何被视为混沌的象征？

Why is the Lorenz attractor seen as an icon of chaos?

洛伦兹从简化大气对流方程得到三维确定性系统，其轨迹在双涡结构中永不重复却受限于有限区域，对初值极度敏感，是「蝴蝶效应」的数学来源。
From a simplified atmospheric convection model, Lorenz found a deterministic 3D system whose trajectory never repeats yet stays bounded in a double-spiral structure, exquisitely sensitive to initial conditions — the math behind the 'butterfly effect'.

什么是「蝴蝶效应」？

What is the 'butterfly effect'?

它指确定性混沌系统中，初值的微小差异会随指数增长放大，导致长期预测失效；名字源于「巴西蝴蝶扇翅可能引发得州龙卷风」的比喻。
It means that in deterministic chaotic systems tiny differences in initial conditions grow exponentially, defeating long-term prediction; the name comes from the metaphor that a butterfly's flap could trigger a distant tornado.

为什么双摆的运动难以预测？

Why is the motion of a double pendulum hard to predict?

双摆是确定性的非线性系统，但其相空间轨迹对初始角度极度敏感，微小差别会迅速放大，短期内即偏离预测，是混沌的直观演示。
The double pendulum is a deterministic nonlinear system whose trajectories are extremely sensitive to initial angles; tiny differences amplify fast, deviating from prediction quickly — a vivid demo of chaos.
