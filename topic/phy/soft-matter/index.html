同一条高分子链,为什么在 θ 溶剂中末端距按 N 的 0.5 次方标度,换到良溶剂里却变成约 0.588 次方?

For the same polymer chain, why does the end-to-end distance scale as N to the 0.5 power in a theta solvent but about 0.588 in a good solvent?

θ 温度下单体间的排除体积作用被溶剂吸引精确抵消,链等价于理想无规行走,R ∝ N^(1/2)。良溶剂中排除体积仍然存在,链必须自回避膨胀,Flory 平均场把熵弹性能与两体排斥能配平,得 ν = 3/(d+2),三维为 3/5 ≈ 0.588,重整化群精确值为 0.5877。这一标度指数已被光散射与小角中子散射证实,是高分子溶液理论的基石。
At the theta temperature solvent attraction exactly cancels excluded-volume interactions between monomers, so the chain reduces to an ideal random walk with R proportional to N^(1/2). In a good solvent excluded volume survives and the chain must swell as a self-avoiding walk; balancing entropic elasticity against two-body repulsion in Flory's mean-field argument gives ν = 3/(d+2), i.e. 3/5 ≈ 0.588 in three dimensions, refined to 0.5877 by renormalization group. Light scattering and SANS confirm this exponent, which anchors polymer solution theory.

How is the 'jamming transition' in granular matter described?

当颗粒堆积密度越过阈值，系统从可流动流体态突变为刚性固体态，称为堵塞转变。它在零温极限下表现为一种具有特有标度行为的临界相变。
As packing density crosses a threshold, granular systems abruptly change from a flowing fluid to a rigid solid — the jamming transition. At zero temperature it appears as a critical phase with characteristic scaling behavior.

Why can a gel form a semi-solid network from a liquid?

为什么水能在很细的管子里自发上升？

Why does water rise spontaneously in a very narrow tube?

这是毛细现象：液体与管壁间的附着力使弯月面弯曲，表面张力沿管壁向上的分量与重力平衡，使液柱上升，管越细上升越高。
This is capillarity: adhesion to the wall bends the meniscus, and the upward component of surface tension balances gravity, lifting the liquid column — higher in narrower tubes.

橡胶的弹性主要源于能量还是熵？

Does rubber elasticity mainly come from energy or entropy?

橡胶拉伸时高分子链从卷曲变为伸展，构象数减少、熵下降；回弹是系统恢复高熵状态的趋势主导，因此橡胶弹性本质上是熵弹性而非键能变化。
Stretching rubber straightens polymer chains, reducing conformations and entropy; the snap-back is driven by the tendency to regain high entropy, so rubber elasticity is fundamentally entropic, not from bond-energy change.

Why does a cornstarch-water mixture get 'harder' when struck harder?

This is a shear-thickening non-Newtonian fluid: under fast shear particles cannot rearrange, so friction forms a transient rigid network that resists like a solid, then flows again when shear stops.

Why can soft matter spontaneously self-assemble into ordered structures?

在熵或能量驱动下，组分如两亲分子、胶体通过局部相互作用最小化自由能，自发形成胶束、囊泡等有序结构，无需外部蓝图。这种能力是生命材料和纳米制造的基础。

水滴落在荷叶上会缩成近乎完美的球还能滚来滚去,荷叶表面到底做对了什么?

A droplet on a lotus leaf beads up almost perfectly spherical and rolls freely; what exactly does the leaf surface achieve?

荷叶表面布满 5 到 10 微米的乳突,乳突上又覆盖纳米级蜡晶,微纳复合粗糙结构加上低表面能把表观接触角推到 150° 以上。水滴仅搭在乳突顶端、下方截留空气,处于 Cassie-Baxter 态,固液接触面积极小,滚动角只有几度。滚走的水珠顺带带走灰尘,这就是自清洁荷叶效应的物理核心。
The leaf is carpeted with 5 to 10 μm papillae coated in nanoscale wax crystals, and this hierarchical roughness plus low surface energy pushes the apparent contact angle above 150°. The droplet rests only on papillae tips with air trapped beneath—the Cassie-Baxter state—so the solid-liquid contact area is tiny and the roll-off angle just a few degrees. Rolling drops carry dust along, which is the physics of the self-cleaning lotus effect.

蛋黄酱静置时能像固体一样保持形状，抹开时却顺畅流动，这种屈服应力行为在高浓度乳液中是怎样形成的？

Mayonnaise holds its shape like a solid at rest yet flows smoothly when spread out — how does such yield-stress behavior emerge in a concentrated emulsion?

蛋黄酱是油滴体积分数极高的分散体系，液滴相互挤压构成瞬态网络，只有外应力突破临界值才能拆散网络并让液滴重排。低于该阈值时材料只发生弹性形变，表现为固体；一旦超过，网络的瓦解与重建持续进行，宏观上呈现黏性流动。临界应力的大小取决于液滴浓度、粒径分布以及界面吸附层的强弱。
Mayonnaise is a dispersion whose oil droplets occupy a very high volume fraction and jam into a transient network, so a critical stress must be exceeded before droplets can rearrange and slip past one another. Below that threshold the sample deforms elastically and appears solid; above it, the network keeps breaking and rebuilding, giving macroscopic viscous flow. The yield stress is set by droplet volume fraction, size distribution, and the strength of the adsorbed interfacial layers.

向列相液晶显示器的像素为什么只需区区几伏电压就能完成明暗状态的切换？

Why can a mere few volts switch a pixel between bright and dark states in a nematic liquid-crystal display?

向列相分子兼具介电各向异性与接近液体的低转动阻力，透明电极建立的微小电场所产生的力矩足以克服微弱的弹性形变能与黏性耗散，把整个指向矢场扭转到新取向。夹层偏振片随后把这一取向变化转换成透过率的开关。阈值电压由介电力矩与Frank弹性常数的竞争决定，通常落在1至2伏之间。
Nematic molecules pair dielectric anisotropy with liquid-like rotational mobility, so the torque generated by a small field across transparent electrodes overcomes weak elastic distortion and viscous drag, rotating the whole director field into a new orientation. Sandwiching polarizers then convert that reorientation into an on-off change of transmission. The threshold voltage follows from the competition between dielectric torque and the Frank elastic constants, typically landing between one and two volts.

颗粒物质从流动状态突然变成能承重的固体，这类堵塞转变和普通的玻璃化转变关键区别在哪里？

Granular matter suddenly stops flowing and starts bearing load at the jamming point—what separates this transition from an ordinary glass transition?

Liu与Nagel的堵塞相图表明，堆积密度、剪切应力和温度三者共同决定体系能否承受剪切。当堆积分数逼近临界值φc时，颗粒间接触网络突然具备力学刚性，平均配位数跳升越过等静压阈值，剪切模量从零跃迁为有限值。与热致玻璃化不同，这里起支配作用的是构型空间的几何约束而非热激活，所以室温下的沙堆同样表现出锐利的屈服点。
In the jamming phase diagram of Liu and Nagel, packing density, shear stress, and temperature jointly decide whether the system resists shear. As the packing fraction approaches a critical value φc, the contact network acquires rigidity abruptly, the mean coordination number jumps above the isostatic threshold, and the shear modulus rises from zero to finite. Geometry of configuration space rather than thermal activation governs the change, which is why a sand pile exhibits a sharp yield point even at room temperature.

液晶为什么既像液体一样会流动,又能像晶体那样改变光的偏振?

Why can liquid crystals flow like liquids yet twist light like crystals?

液晶分子的关键在于「部分有序」:它们不像普通液体那样完全杂乱,也不像晶体那样三维修固,而是取向趋于一致(向列相)甚至分层排列(近晶相),同时还能整体流动。外电场可以扭转分子的共同指向,而指向的变化会改变材料对光偏振方向的旋转能力——液晶屏正是靠电压逐像素控制这种旋光,配合偏振片来调节明暗。流动性来自位置无序,光学各向异性来自取向有序,两者并存造就了液晶的独特用途。
The key to liquid crystals is partial order: unlike ordinary liquids their rod-like molecules are not fully random, yet unlike crystals they are not locked in 3D — they align collectively (nematic) or layer up (smectic) while still flowing. An applied voltage reorients the common direction of the molecules, and because optical rotation of polarization depends on that direction, each pixel of an LCD can switch bright or dark between polarizers. Fluidity comes from positional disorder; optical anisotropy from orientational order — having both is what makes liquid crystals useful.

沙漏里的沙子越漏越少,为什么单位时间漏下的量却几乎不变?

Why does sand flow through an hourglass at a nearly constant rate even as the level drops?

水从桶底小孔流出时流速随水位下降而变慢,因为液体压强由水位决定。颗粒物质不同:沙粒间存在持续的颗粒接触力链,孔口附近会自然形成拱形结构,流量由拱的尺寸与重力共同锁定,对上方沙堆压强几乎不敏感。实验与模拟都表明,当孔径远大于粒径(约5倍以上)且沙层足够厚时,质量流量只依赖孔径、粒径与密度,这就是沙漏能稳定计时的物理基础。
Water draining from a hole slows down as the level falls because liquid pressure depends on depth. Granular matter behaves differently: persistent contact force chains among grains let a stable arch form near the orifice, locking the flow rate to the arch size and gravity rather than to the pressure of the pile above. Experiments and simulations show that for orifices more than about five grain diameters wide and sufficiently deep fills, the mass flow depends only on orifice size, grain size and density — the physical basis of hourglass timekeeping.

果冻为什么一晃就「颤抖」,却不至于散开流走?

Why does jelly wobble when you shake it, yet still hold its shape instead of flowing apart?

果冻是一种典型的软物质凝胶:明胶分子在热水中溶解后,冷却时相互缠绕并形成三维交联网络,把大量水分子「锁」在网络空隙里。轻摇时,交联点像小弹簧一样储存又释放能量,于是整块果冻弹性颤动;只有用力超过网络的屈服强度,键才被撕断,它才会破裂流动。这种「固体骨架+液体成分」的组合,让果冻兼具弹性与柔软,也是橡胶、黏液、细胞骨架等软物质的共同特征。
Jelly is a classic soft-matter gel: gelatin molecules dissolve in hot water and, on cooling, entangle into a three-dimensional cross-linked network that traps large amounts of water in its pores. A gentle shake makes the cross-links act like tiny springs that store and release energy, so the whole block wobbles elastically; only when the force exceeds the network's yield strength do bonds tear and the jelly breaks and flows. This 'solid skeleton plus liquid component' combination gives jelly both elasticity and softness — a signature shared by rubber, mucus, and the cell cytoskeleton.

湿沙子能堆出陡峭的城堡,干沙子却只能摊成缓坡,水做了什么?

Why does damp sand build steep castles while dry sand slumps into gentle slopes?

干燥沙粒之间只有摩擦与重力,堆到约30至35度就会滑坡,这个极限角叫安息角。加入少量水后情况突变:水不在颗粒间流动,而是在接触点形成微小的液桥,液桥的表面张力像几十枚微型胶圈把相邻颗粒拉在一起,形成毛细内聚力。沙堡越重压得越实、接触点越多、强度越高;但水太多时液桥连通成水膜,润滑反而让城堡坍塌——最佳含水量只有百分之几。潮汐退去后滩涂恰好处于这个甜蜜区间,这正是湿沙城堡只在海边常见的原因。
Dry grains feel only friction against gravity and slump once the pile reaches roughly thirty to thirty-five degrees, the angle of repose. A little water changes everything: instead of flowing, it forms tiny liquid bridges at each contact point whose surface tension acts like dozens of micro rubber bands binding neighboring grains — capillary cohesion. Packing harder creates more contacts and more strength; too much water, though, merges bridges into films that lubricate collapse. The sweet spot is only a few percent water — exactly where a retreating tide leaves the beach.
