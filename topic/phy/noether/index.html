当对称性“自发破缺”时，诺特定理还成立吗？

When symmetry is spontaneously broken, does Noether's theorem still hold?

成立，但会伴随出现零质量的 Goldstone 模式（全局破缺）或被人吃掉的规范玻色子（局域破缺，希格斯机制）；守恒律与对称性的对应依旧，只是表现更微妙。
Yes, but it births massless Goldstone modes (global breaking) or gauge bosons eaten via the Higgs mechanism (local breaking); the symmetry-conservation link remains, only more subtle.

规范对称如何“生”出传递力的粒子？

How does gauge symmetry give birth to force-carrying particles?

要求局域规范对称（每点独立旋转）会强制引入补偿场，这些场的量子就是力的载体：光子、W/Z 玻色子、胶子分别传递电磁、弱、强力。
Demanding local gauge symmetry forces the introduction of compensating fields whose quanta carry forces: photons, W/Z and gluons mediate electromagnetism, the weak and strong forces.

角动量守恒对应哪种对称？

Which symmetry corresponds to conservation of angular momentum?

对应空间旋转对称（各方向物理规律相同）。因为宇宙没有特殊的“优先方向”，所以孤立系统的总角动量保持不变。
It corresponds to rotational symmetry of space. Because no direction is privileged, the total angular momentum of an isolated system is conserved.

为什么能量守恒和“时间平移对称”是一回事？

Why is energy conservation the same as time-translation symmetry?

诺特定理说：每种连续对称都对应一个守恒量。时间处处一样，就得到能量守恒；空间平移对称，就得到动量守恒。
Noether's theorem links every continuous symmetry to a conservation law: time symmetry gives energy conservation, spatial symmetry gives momentum.
