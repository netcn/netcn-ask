什么是光学倍频?为什么它必须在强光条件下才能明显发生?

What is second-harmonic generation, and why does it become appreciable only under intense light?

光学倍频又称二次谐波产生,是指频率为ω的强激光通过非线性晶体时,介质中的电极化强度出现正比于光场平方的P⁽²⁾项,从而辐射出频率为2ω的新光。在弱场下极化的高阶项远小于线性项,产生的二次谐波功率与基频光强平方成正比但绝对值微乎其微。只有当激光提供足够高的场强,且晶体满足相位匹配使基频与倍频光保持同相传播时,能量转换效率才能达到百分之几十的可观水平。
Second-harmonic generation is a nonlinear process in which intense light at frequency ω passing through a nonlinear crystal drives a polarization component proportional to E², radiating new light at 2ω. At weak fields the quadratic polarization term is negligible compared with the linear one, and although the generated power scales with the square of the fundamental intensity it remains vanishingly small. Only when a laser supplies sufficiently high field strength and the crystal satisfies phase matching—so that fundamental and harmonic waves stay in step—can conversion efficiencies reach sizable tens of percent.

#二次谐波产生 #非线性极化

相位匹配是高效二次谐波产生的前提,请说明其物理含义以及双折射晶体如何满足该条件。

Phase matching is the prerequisite for efficient second-harmonic generation — explain its physical meaning and how birefringent crystals satisfy it.

相位匹配要求基频光与二次谐波在介质中以相同的相速度传播,使各处产生的谐波同相叠加;否则相干长度仅数十微米,转换效率随距离振荡而非累积。数学上等价于 k(2ω) = 2k(ω),失配 Δk 为零时功率按介质长度的平方增长。双折射晶体利用寻常光与非常光折射率随方向和偏振的差异,通过调节晶体角度或温度,令某一偏振组合满足 n(ω) 与 n(2ω) 相等。
Phase matching demands that the fundamental and the second harmonic propagate at equal phase velocity so harmonic contributions everywhere add in phase; otherwise the coherence length is only tens of micrometers and conversion oscillates rather than accumulates. Mathematically it is k(2ω) = 2k(ω), and at zero mismatch Δk the output power grows quadratically with crystal length. Birefringent crystals exploit the direction- and polarization-dependent refractive index of ordinary and extraordinary waves, tuning angle or temperature until some polarization combination satisfies n(ω) = n(2ω).

#相位匹配 #二次谐波产生

倍频(SHG)效率为什么强烈依赖基频光强与相位匹配条件?

Why does second-harmonic generation efficiency depend strongly on both the fundamental intensity and the phase-matching condition?

二次谐波强度按 I₂ ∝ I₁² d_eff² L² 增长,来自介质的二阶非线性极化,因此基频光强的微小提升会使倍频输出平方级增强。同时,只有当基频与倍频波在晶体中以相同相速度传播(折射率 n(ω) = n(2ω))时,各处产生的谐波才同相叠加;失配 Δk ≠ 0 时输出随 L 出现 sin²(ΔkL/2) 振荡并被相干长度限制。实用上通过温度调谐或角度调谐双折射晶体实现相位匹配。
The second-harmonic intensity grows as I₂ ∝ I₁² d_eff² L² because it originates from the second-order nonlinear polarization, so even small increases in the fundamental power boost the output quadratically. Efficient buildup additionally requires the fundamental and harmonic waves to propagate with the same phase velocity — i.e. n(ω) = n(2ω) — so that contributions generated along the crystal add in phase; when Δk ≠ 0 the output oscillates as sin²(ΔkL/2) and is capped by the coherence length. In practice phase matching is achieved by temperature or angle tuning of birefringent crystals.
