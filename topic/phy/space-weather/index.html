地磁暴为什么会影响电网和卫星？

Why do geomagnetic storms affect power grids and satellites?

强太阳活动注入磁层能量，使地磁场剧烈扰动，在长距离导体（如输电电缆）中感应地磁感应电流，损坏变压器；同时高能粒子会损伤卫星电子器件。
Strong solar activity dumps energy into the magnetosphere, violently disturbing the field and inducing geomagnetically induced currents in long conductors like power lines, damaging transformers; energetic particles also harm satellite electronics.

空间天气预报依赖哪些观测与模型？

What observations and models does space weather forecasting rely on?

预报综合太阳观测卫星、行星际探测器与地磁台网数据，用磁流体和粒子输运模型模拟日冕物质抛射的传播与地磁响应，提前数小时到数天发出警报。
Forecasting combines solar observatories, interplanetary probes, and geomagnetic networks, using MHD and particle-transport models to simulate CME propagation and geomagnetic response, issuing alerts hours to days ahead.

地球磁层如何保护我们？

How does Earth's magnetosphere protect us?

地磁场在太阳风压迫下形成水滴状磁层，偏转大部分带电粒子，使其沿磁力线进入极区，从而保护地表生物免受高能粒子与宇宙辐射直接轰击。
Earth's magnetic field forms a teardrop magnetosphere against the solar wind, deflecting most charged particles toward the poles, shielding the surface from direct high-energy and cosmic radiation.

日冕物质抛射为何是空间天气的主要威胁？

Why are coronal mass ejections the main threat in space weather?

日冕物质抛射把数十亿吨日冕等离子体和磁场以高速抛向行星际空间，若撞击地球会剧烈压缩磁层、引发强地磁暴和辐射带增强，威胁航天与通信。
A coronal mass ejection hurls billions of tons of coronal plasma and magnetic field into interplanetary space; an Earth-directed one sharply compresses the magnetosphere, causing intense storms and radiation-belt enhancement that threaten spaceflight and communications.

太阳风是怎么产生的？

How is the solar wind produced?

太阳日冕温度极高、粒子动能超过引力束缚，持续向外膨胀形成由质子和电子组成的超音速带电粒子流，即为太阳风。
The solar corona is so hot that particles exceed gravitational binding and stream outward as a supersonic flow of protons and electrons — the solar wind.

范艾伦辐射带是什么？

What are the Van Allen radiation belts?

它们是地球磁层内被磁场俘获的高能带电粒子区域，分内外两层，主要含质子和电子，是航天器通过时必须规避的高辐射环境。
They are regions in Earth's magnetosphere where magnetic fields trap energetic charged particles, in inner and outer belts of mainly protons and electrons — a high-radiation zone spacecraft must avoid.

太阳耀斑为何会干扰地球无线电？

Why do solar flares disrupt Earth's radio communications?

耀斑是太阳大气磁重联释放的剧烈辐射爆发，伴随X射线和极紫外增强，电离低层大气D层，吸收短波无线电信号，造成短波通信中断。
A flare is a burst of radiation from magnetic reconnection; enhanced X-rays and EUV ionize the lower atmosphere (D-layer), absorbing shortwave radio and causing blackouts.

极光发光的具体物理机制是什么？

What is the specific physical mechanism of auroral light?

太阳风带电粒子沿极区磁力线沉降，与高层大气氧、氮原子碰撞激发，原子退激时发射特定波长光子（如氧原子630纳米红光、557纳米绿光），形成极光。
Charged particles precipitate along polar field lines and collide with oxygen and nitrogen atoms in the upper atmosphere, exciting them; as atoms de-excite they emit specific wavelengths — oxygen 630 nm red, 557 nm green — forming auroras.
