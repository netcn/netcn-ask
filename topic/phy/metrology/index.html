光钟为何可能取代铯钟成为秒的新基准？

Why might optical clocks replace cesium clocks as the new second?

光钟使用更高频率的光学跃迁，频率不确定度比铯钟低两个数量级以上，稳定性更好。国际计量界正推动以光频跃迁重新定义秒。
Optical clocks use higher-frequency optical transitions with uncertainties over a hundred times smaller than cesium, offering far better stability. The metrology community is moving to redefine the second by an optical transition.

量纲分析为什么能在不解方程的情况下猜出公式？

Why can dimensional analysis guess a formula without solving any equations?

物理规律必须在单位改变时保持形式，这个约束本身就大幅限制了变量的组合方式。例如只用绳长和重力加速度构造时间量纲，就唯一得到根号下长度除以重力，正是单摆周期的形式，只差一个无量纲系数。
A physical law must keep its form when units change, and that constraint alone sharply limits how variables can combine. From only length and gravity, the sole combination with the dimension of time is the square root of length over g — exactly the pendulum period up to a dimensionless factor.

LIGO 如何探测到引力波？

How does LIGO detect gravitational waves?

LIGO 用公里级迈克尔逊激光干涉仪，引力波经过时使互相垂直的双臂长度产生极微小的相对变化（约质子直径的千分之一），通过激光相位差检测出来。2015年首次直接探测到黑洞并合产生的引力波。
LIGO uses kilometer-scale Michelson laser interferometers; a passing gravitational wave changes the relative arm lengths by tiny fractions — about a thousandth of a proton diameter — detected as laser phase shifts. In 2015 it first directly observed waves from a black-hole merger.

开尔文现在由玻尔兹曼常数定义，这跟“水的三相点”有什么不同？

The kelvin now rests on the Boltzmann constant — how does that differ from the triple point of water?

旧定义把水的三相点定为273.16 K，但真实水的同位素组成和杂质会影响这个点的复现精度。新定义直接固定玻尔兹曼常数，把温度化为能量单位的换算，任何能测量热运动能量的方法都可作为基准，摆脱了对特定物质的依赖。
The old definition assigned 273.16 K to water's triple point, yet isotopic composition and impurities limited how well that point could be reproduced. Fixing the Boltzmann constant instead makes temperature a conversion into energy units, so any measurement of thermal energy can realise the kelvin without relying on one substance.

Why is the kilogram no longer a platinum-iridium cylinder in Paris?

Why is the fine-structure constant α≈1/137 regarded as a profound fundamental constant in physics?

千克改为依据普朗克常数定义之后，基布尔天平通过怎样的两阶段操作把一个实物砝码的质量与普朗克常数联系起来？

After the kilogram became tied to the Planck constant, how does the two-phase operation of a Kibble balance connect the mass of a physical test weight to that constant?

第一阶段为称量模式：线圈载流后在磁场中受到的洛伦兹力恰好抵消砝码重力。第二阶段为速度模式：让线圈以恒定速度在同一磁场中运动并记录感应电动势。两式相乘消去线圈与磁场的几何因子，机械功率便与电压、电阻挂钩，而这些电学量经由约瑟夫森效应和量子化霍尔效应溯源至普朗克常数与基本电荷，最终质量的不确定度可达10^-8量级。
In the weighing phase, the Lorentz force on a current-carrying coil suspended in a magnetic field exactly balances the weight of the test mass. In the velocity phase, the coil is driven at constant speed through the same field while the induced voltage is recorded. Multiplying the two measurements cancels the coil-and-field geometry factor, linking mechanical power to voltage and resistance, which are themselves realized through the Josephson effect and the quantum Hall effect against the Planck constant and the elementary charge; relative uncertainties reach the 10^-8 level.

摄氏度和开尔文的“一度”为什么正好一样大？

Why is one degree Celsius exactly the same size as one kelvin?

摄氏度和开尔文的单位大小完全相同，因为开尔文的定义就是“1 开尔文 = 1 摄氏度”，两者只差一个固定偏移（0 °C = 273.15 K）。因此温度变化 1 °C 恰好等于变化 1 K，二者度量的是同一段温度间隔。只有零点位置不同，度的“大小”并没有变。
The Celsius and kelvin scales use exactly the same unit size because the kelvin was defined so that one kelvin equals one degree Celsius; they differ only by an offset (0 °C = 273.15 K). A change of 1 °C is therefore exactly a change of 1 K, since both measure the same temperature interval. Only the zero point is shifted, not the size of a degree.

一米现在是怎么定义的？

How is the metre defined today?

先固定真空中光速为299792458米每秒，再规定一米就是光在1/299792458秒内走过的距离。因为秒可以用原子钟极精确地实现，长度基准也就跟着变得极其稳定。
The speed of light in vacuum is fixed at 299792458 m/s, and the metre is then the distance light travels in 1/299792458 of a second. Since the second is realised superbly well by atomic clocks, the length standard inherits that stability.

那块国际千克原器会随时间微量失重或沾污，且全世界都要依赖它，风险集中。新定义用基布尔秤把质量与普朗克常数 h 及精确固定的电学单位挂钩，使千克可由自然常数复现，任何实验室都能自洽地得到，不再依赖单一实物。

“秒”现在是用什么物理过程来定义的，为什么偏偏选铯-133原子的某种跳动？

What physical process now defines the 'second,' and why specifically a certain transition of cesium-133?

自 1967 年起，1 秒被定义为铯-133原子基态超精细能级间跃迁辐射的 9,192,631,770 个周期。这一微波频率极其稳定且可复现，不受地点、环境实物影响，因此成为最精确的时间基准。现代原子钟就是数这个跳动来计时的。
Since 1967 the second is defined as 9,192,631,770 periods of the radiation from the hyperfine transition of cesium-133. This microwave frequency is extremely stable and reproducible, independent of place or artifacts, making it the most precise time base. Modern atomic clocks literally count this ticking.

一秒为什么要用铯原子来定义，而不是地球自转？

Why define the second with a caesium atom rather than Earth's rotation?

地球自转会因潮汐摩擦、地核运动和大气变化而不均匀，一天的长度每年都在微小地改变。铯-133基态两个超精细能级之间的跃迁频率恒定且可复现，规定其为9192631770周即为一秒，稳定性高出许多个数量级。
Earth's spin is uneven because of tidal friction, core motion and atmospheric shifts, so the length of a day wobbles year to year. The hyperfine transition of caesium-133 is constant and reproducible, so the second is defined as 9192631770 of its cycles — many orders of magnitude steadier.

基布尔天平凭什么能把千克的质量基准挂到普朗克常数这样一个量子量上？

By what reasoning does the Kibble balance tie the kilogram, a macroscopic mass artifact, to the quantum quantity Planck's constant?

仪器分两步运行：先用通电线圈所受洛伦兹力抵消砝码重力，再让线圈匀速运动测量感应电动势。借助约瑟夫森电压标准和量子化霍尔电阻标准，两步里的电学量都可写成频率乘以普朗克常数的形式，速度项消去重力与几何因子后质量便唯一地由h确定。2019年修订的国际单位制据此按固定值h = 6.62607015×10⁻³⁴ J·s复现千克。
The instrument runs in two phases: it first cancels a test mass's weight with the Lorentz force on a current-carrying coil, then measures the voltage induced when the coil moves at constant velocity. Using the Josephson voltage standard and the quantum Hall resistance standard, both electrical quantities reduce to frequency multiplied by Planck's constant, so after velocity terms cancel gravity and geometric factors the mass fixes itself uniquely to h. The revised SI of 2019 realizes the kilogram from the fixed value h = 6.62607015×10⁻³⁴ J·s.

如果工程师忘记修正相对论,GPS 每天会错出多远?

If engineers forgot relativistic corrections, how far would GPS drift per day?

两个效应方向相反都要算。狭义相对论:卫星时速近1.4万公里,星载钟比地面慢约每天7微秒;广义相对论:轨道高度引力较弱,星载钟快约每天45微秒;净效果是快38微秒。导航定位依赖纳秒级时间测量——38微秒对应约11公里的测距误差,而且逐日累积,不出几分钟定位就不可信。因此卫星出厂前把时钟频率预先调低,入轨后正好走准;接收机还要实时扣除信号穿过电离层与大气折射的延迟。GPS 是普通人每天都在用的相对论实验。
Two opposite effects must both be booked. Special relativity slows satellite clocks about seven microseconds daily at fourteen thousand km/h; general relativity speeds them up about forty-five microseconds in weaker gravity; net gain thirty-eight microseconds. Ranging needs nanosecond timing, so thirty-eight microseconds maps to roughly eleven kilometers of error per day — useless within minutes without correction. Satellites therefore fly with factory-prelowered clock frequencies that land exactly right on orbit, and receivers still strip out ionospheric and tropospheric delays. GPS is a relativistic experiment you use daily.

计算结果为什么不能想写多少位小数就写多少位？

Why can't you keep as many decimal places as your calculator shows?

计算结果不可能比输入数据中最不精确的那一项更准确，多写小数位只是凭空制造了数据并不支持的“精确感”。有效数字规则让答案如实反映测量不确定度，而不是把计算器产生的多余数字当成真实精度。用两位小数测量得到的量，却报出五位小数，是误导人的。
A result can only be as precise as the least-precise measurement that went into it; extra decimal places imply a certainty the data don't support. Significant-figure rules keep the answer honest about uncertainty instead of letting calculator noise masquerade as real precision. Reporting five decimal places when your input was measured to two digits is misleading.

科学家为什么爱写 3.0×10⁸ 而不是 300000000？

Why do scientists write 3.0×10⁸ instead of 300000000?

科学记数法把数量级和有效数字分开，既不容易数错零，也一眼看出精度。3.0×10⁸ 表示只有两位有效数字可信，而写一长串零反而暗示后面每一位都精确。
Scientific notation separates the order of magnitude from the significant figures, so you neither miscount zeros nor lose sight of precision. Writing 3.0×10⁸ says only two digits are trustworthy, while a long string of zeros wrongly implies every digit is exact.

物理实验为什么每个测量结果都要写上不确定度？

Why must every measured result carry an uncertainty?

任何测量都有仪器分辨率、环境波动和读数偏差，单个数字无法说明它可信到什么程度。写成“1.24±0.03秒”才能让别人判断两组结果是否真的矛盾，也是重复实验和理论比较的前提。
Every measurement is limited by instrument resolution, environmental drift and reading bias, so a bare number says nothing about how far to trust it. Reporting 1.24 ± 0.03 s lets others judge whether two results genuinely disagree, which is essential for replication and comparison with theory.
