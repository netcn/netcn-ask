泡泡为什么五颜六色？

Why are bubbles so colorful?

泡泡薄薄的皮把光照出许多颜色，所以看上去花花绿绿。
A bubble's thin skin bends the light in a special way, so it glows with many shifting colors like a rainbow.

游乐园转盘为什么把人甩出去？

Why does a spinning ride fling people outward?

转得快时，身体想一直直直往前冲，就被甩向外面。
When it spins fast, your body wants to keep going straight and gets flung outward.

气球吹太大为什么会爆？

Why does a balloon pop when blown too big?

气球皮被拉得太紧，再也撑不住，就啪地破了。
The balloon skin is stretched too tight to hold and bursts with a pop.

泡泡为什么是球形的？

Why are bubbles shaped like spheres?

水的表面张力把泡泡皮往里收，球形用最少的皮包住最多的空气。
Surface tension pulls the bubble skin inward, and a sphere holds the most air with the least skin.

泡泡为什么会破掉？

Why do bubbles pop?

泡泡的皮非常薄，被风一吹或手指一碰，就破成了小水珠。
A bubble's skin is very thin and breaks into tiny drops when wind or a finger touches it.

车轮为什么做成圆的？

Why are wheels made round?

圆轮转动时，轮心到地面的距离始终不变，所以滚起来又平又省力。
A round wheel keeps the same distance from its center to the ground, so it rolls smoothly and with little effort.
