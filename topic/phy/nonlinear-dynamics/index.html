为什么双摆的运动看似随机、无法长期预测？

Why does a double pendulum appear random and become unpredictable over time?

双摆是一个经典的混沌系统：它对初始条件具有指数级的敏感依赖（蝴蝶效应的机械版本）。即使两个初始角度只差十万分之一度，演化出的轨迹也会在数秒后完全不同。其运动虽由确定性方程（拉格朗日方程）支配，却没有解析周期解，长期行为本质上不可预测。
The double pendulum is a classic chaotic system with exponential sensitivity to initial conditions—a mechanical version of the butterfly effect. Two setups differing by one hundred-thousandth of a degree diverge completely within seconds. Though governed by deterministic Lagrange equations, it has no analytic periodic solution, so its long-term behavior is essentially unpredictable.

为什么搅拌咖啡会出现乱糟糟的“湍流”？

Why does stirring coffee create messy turbulence?

当流速快、黏性相对弱时，流动失稳、出现大小旋涡层层嵌套，这就是湍流。它高度非线性、难以精确预测，是经典物理里著名的难题。
When flow is fast and viscosity relatively weak, it goes unstable and breaks into nested swirls of many sizes—turbulence. Highly nonlinear and hard to predict exactly, it is a famous hard problem in classical physics.

“蝴蝶效应”在物理里指什么？

What does the butterfly effect mean in physics?

它指对混沌系统初值的极度敏感：一点点微小差别会被指数式放大，长期行为变得不可预测。这是非线性动力学而非神秘巧合。
It means extreme sensitivity to initial conditions in a chaotic system: a tiny difference is amplified exponentially, making long-term behavior unpredictable. It is nonlinear dynamics, not mysticism.

什么是分形，雪花轮廓为什么算分形？

What is a fractal, and why is a snowflake edge one?

分形是局部与整体自相似、细节在不同尺度重复的形状，维度往往不是整数。雪花、海岸线在放大后仍有相似的凹凸，正是分形的体现。
A fractal is a shape self-similar at every scale, with detail repeating at different sizes and often a non-integer dimension. Snowflakes and coastlines keep similar bumps when magnified—classic fractals.
