坡印廷矢量如何说明导线中的能量其实沿空间而非导线内部流动？

How does the Poynting vector show energy flows in space, not inside wires?

坡印廷矢量 S=E×B 给出能流密度；在载流导线外，由表面电荷建立的径向电场与环向磁场叉乘，得到沿导线方向的能流，能量实际在导线周围的电磁场中传输，导线只引导场。
S=E×B gives energy flux; outside a current-carrying wire the radial E (from surface charge) crossed with the azimuthal B yields flux along the wire—energy travels in the surrounding EM field, the wire only guides it.

为何良导体表面附近的电场必须垂直于表面？

Why must the electric field be perpendicular to a conductor surface?

静电平衡下导体内部电场为零；若有切向分量，表面自由电荷会沿表面移动直到切向场被抵消，只剩法向分量，于是表面等势、场线垂直。
In electrostatic equilibrium the interior E is zero; any tangential component would drive surface charges until canceled, leaving only the normal component, so the surface is equipotential.

位移电流为何必须加入安培定律？

Why must displacement current be added to Ampère's law?

原安培定律对含电容的回路在非稳恒时不满足连续性方程（散度不为零）。麦克斯韦加入 ε0 ∂E/∂t 项后，方程满足电荷守恒，并预言变化的电场能产生磁场、支持电磁波传播。
The original law fails charge conservation for capacitive loops in time-varying cases. Maxwell added ε0 ∂E/∂t so ∇·J stays consistent and changing E fields generate B, enabling EM waves.

电磁波为什么能在真空中传播而不需要介质？

Why can EM waves propagate in vacuum without a medium?

变化的电场产生磁场、变化的磁场产生电场，二者自我维持向前传播，不需要粒子介质；真空中波速 c=1/√(ε0μ0)。当年以太假设被迈克尔逊-莫雷实验否定。
A changing E field makes a B field and vice versa, self-sustaining propagation with no material medium; in vacuum c=1/√(ε0μ0). The aether was ruled out by Michelson–Morley.

什么是趋肤效应，为什么高频电流只在导线表层走？

What is the skin effect and why do high-frequency currents stay near the surface?

交变电流产生的磁场在导线内部感应出涡电流，抵消中心处的电流并增强表层电流，使有效截面缩小、电阻上升；趋肤深度 δ≈√(2/(ωμσ)) 随频率升高而变薄。
An alternating current's own magnetic field induces eddies that cancel the core current and boost surface current, shrinking effective area and raising resistance; skin depth δ≈√(2/(ωμσ)) shrinks with frequency.

磁单极子若存在将如何改变麦克斯韦方程？

How would magnetic monopoles change Maxwell's equations?

需加入磁荷密度 ρm 与磁流 Jm：∇·B=ρm、∇×E=-∂B/∂t-Jm，使电与磁完全对称（对偶对称），并解释电荷量子化的狄拉克条件 eg=nℏ/2。
One adds magnetic charge ρm and current Jm: ∇·B=ρm and ∇×E=-(∂B/∂t+Jm), making electricity and magnetism dual-symmetric and motivating Dirac's charge quantization eg=nℏ/2.

什么是电磁感应，发电机靠它怎么工作？

What is electromagnetic induction and how do generators use it?

法拉第定律说磁通变化会在回路里感应电动势；发电机让线圈在磁场中转动，磁通周期性变化，于是产生交变电压，把机械能变成电能。
Faraday's law: changing magnetic flux induces an EMF. A generator spins a coil in a field so flux oscillates, producing AC voltage—converting mechanical to electrical energy.

为什么电容器在交流电路里会通高频阻低频？

Why does a capacitor pass high frequencies but block low ones?

容抗 Xc=1/(ωC) 随频率升高而减小；频率越高，电荷在极板间来回翻转越容易，等效阻碍越小，所以电容对高频信号像短路、对直流像开路。
Capacitive reactance Xc=1/(ωC) drops with frequency; higher frequencies flip charge more easily, so a capacitor looks like a short to AC and open to DC.

为什么两根平行通电导线会相互吸引或排斥？

Why do two parallel current-carrying wires attract or repel?

每根导线的电流产生环绕自身的磁场，另一根导线中的运动电荷在该磁场中受洛伦兹力；同向电流相吸、反向相斥，这正是安培力的来源，也是安培被定义为基本单位的历史依据。
Each current makes a magnetic field; moving charges in the other wire feel a Lorentz force. Same-direction currents attract, opposite repel—this is Ampère's force.

为什么磁铁能隔着纸吸住回形针？

Why can a magnet pick up a paperclip through paper?

磁铁的磁场能穿过纸张（非磁性材料基本不影响磁场），到达回形针后把它磁化，于是回形针也被吸引，隔着薄纸依然有效。
The magnetic field passes through paper (non-magnetic), magnetizes the paperclip, and attracts it, so it works through thin paper.

闪电为什么通常先向上或向下分叉着走？

Why does lightning usually branch upward or downward?

云底积累电荷，电场在空气里制造出电离通道（阶梯先导），它像树一样随机分叉寻找电阻最小的路径到达地面，回击沿已形成的通道点亮整条闪电。
Charge builds in clouds; the field creates ionized stepped leaders that fork randomly seeking the lowest-resistance path to ground, then the return stroke lights the channel.

电磁炉为什么只对铁锅管用？

Why does an induction cooker only work with iron pans?

电磁炉用变化的磁场在铁锅里感应出电流（涡流）发热；玻璃、铝锅感应弱，就不怎么热。
A changing magnetic field induces eddy currents that heat iron pans; glass or aluminum respond too weakly to warm up.

为什么有时候脱毛衣会有小火花和噼啪声？

Why do sparks and crackles happen when taking off a sweater?

毛衣和衣服摩擦把电子从这里搬到那里，积起静电；电荷太多时会在空气中突然跳过去，发出小火花和噼啪声，这就是静电放电。
Rubbing moves electrons and builds static charge; when too much builds up it jumps through air as a tiny spark with a crackle—static discharge.
