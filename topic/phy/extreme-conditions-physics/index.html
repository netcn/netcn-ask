液氦为何表现出超流性？

Why does liquid helium show superfluidity?

在约2.17 K以下，液氦-4发生玻色-爱因斯坦凝聚，部分原子进入零黏度的宏观量子基态，可无摩擦流动、爬壁并导热极快，称为超流。
Below about 2.17 K, helium-4 undergoes Bose–Einstein condensation; part of the atoms enter a zero-viscosity macroscopic quantum ground state that flows without friction, climbs walls, and conducts heat rapidly — superfluidity.

德拜模型如何解释低温下固体热容？

How does the Debye model explain low-temperature solid heat capacity?

德拜把晶格振动视为声子气体，低温下只有低频声子被激发，热容与温度的三次方成正比（德拜T³定律），成功解释实验上热容随温度趋零的行为。
Debye treats lattice vibrations as a gas of phonons; at low temperature only low-frequency phonons are excited, giving heat capacity proportional to T cubed (Debye T³ law), explaining the observed vanishing heat capacity.

在极高压下氢可能发生什么相变？

What phase transition can hydrogen undergo under extreme pressure?

理论预言在数百万大气压下分子氢会变为原子金属氢，表现为室温超导体候选；实验室虽多次报道但样品恢复到常压易失稳，证据仍存争议。
Theory predicts molecular hydrogen becomes atomic metallic hydrogen above millions of atmospheres, a candidate room-temperature superconductor; lab reports exist but samples are unstable on release, so evidence remains debated.

微重力环境如何改变对流和燃烧？

How does microgravity change convection and combustion?

在微重力下浮力对流几乎消失，热传递主要靠传导和辐射，火焰呈球形、燃烧更慢更完全。这既为研究基础物理提供条件，也关乎航天器安全。
In microgravity buoyancy convection nearly vanishes; heat transfer is by conduction and radiation, flames are spherical and burn slower and more completely — useful for fundamental physics and relevant to spacecraft safety.

什么是超导现象？

What is superconductivity?

某些材料在低于临界温度时电阻完全消失，并排斥内部磁场（迈斯纳效应），电流可无损耗长期维持，应用于磁共振成像和粒子加速器。
Below a critical temperature some materials lose all electrical resistance and expel internal magnetic fields (Meissner effect), letting current persist without loss, used in MRI and accelerators.

金刚石对顶砧如何产生极高压力？

How does a diamond anvil cell generate extreme pressure?

它用两颗相对抛光的金刚石尖端挤压微小样品，把吨级力集中在微米级面积上，可产生数百万大气压，是研究地球深部和合成新材料的关键工具。
It squeezes a tiny sample between two polished diamond tips, focusing tons of force onto a micrometer area to reach millions of atmospheres — key for Earth's interior studies and new materials.

目前常压下超导体最高临界温度大约是多少？

Roughly what is the highest critical temperature for superconductors at ambient pressure?

常压下铜氧化物高温超导体（如汞钡钙铜氧）临界温度约133 K，仍远低于室温；部分氢化物在极高压下可达接近室温但需高压维持。
At ambient pressure cuprate high-Tc superconductors (e.g. HgBaCaCuO) reach about 133 K, far below room temperature; some hydrides approach room temperature only under extreme pressure.
