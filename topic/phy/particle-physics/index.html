量子色动力学（QCD）研究什么？

What does quantum chromodynamics (QCD) study?

QCD 是描述强相互作用的量子场论，用“色荷”和胶子说明夸克如何被绑成质子和中子。它还有“渐近自由”：能量越高，强作用力反而越弱。
QCD is the quantum field theory of the strong force; with color charge and gluons it explains how quarks bind into protons and neutrons. It also shows asymptotic freedom: at higher energy the strong force gets weaker.

世界的基本粒子有哪些？为什么质子和中子不算基本粒子？

What are the fundamental particles of nature, and why aren't protons and neutrons among them?

标准模型把基本粒子分成费米子和玻色子两大类。费米子构成物质，包括6种夸克（上、下、粲、奇、顶、底）和6种轻子（电子、μ子、τ子以及对应的三种中微子）；玻色子传递相互作用，如光子、胶子、W/Z玻色子和希格斯玻色子。质子和中子由夸克组成，所以不是基本粒子；电子没有已知的内部结构，才是真正的基本粒子。
The Standard Model divides elementary particles into fermions and bosons. Fermions make up matter: six quarks (up, down, charm, strange, top, bottom) and six leptons (electron, muon, tau, plus three neutrinos); bosons carry the forces — the photon, gluons, W and Z bosons, and the Higgs. Protons and neutrons are built from quarks, so they are composite; the electron has no known substructure and is genuinely elementary.

弱相互作用中宇称为什么不守恒？

Why is parity not conserved in the weak interaction?

1956 年李政道与杨振宁指出弱相互作用可能破坏左右对称，吴健雄次年用钴-60 的极化β衰变实验证实宇称确实不守恒。这是首次发现自然界基本相互作用偏爱手性。
In 1956 Lee and Yang proposed that the weak interaction might violate left-right symmetry, and Wu confirmed it the next year with polarized cobalt-60 beta decay. It was the first time a fundamental interaction was found to favor chirality.

为什么从来没有人观察到单独存在的夸克？

Why has no one ever observed a free, isolated quark?

夸克之间的强相互作用力会随距离增大而增强，就像被一根橡皮筋拉着：你想把两个夸克拉开，就必须注入巨大能量，而这些能量会长出一对新的夸克—反夸克，形成新的粒子。因此夸克只能成团地禁闭在质子、中子等强子内部，永远无法被单独观察到，这就是色禁闭。
The strong force between quarks grows stronger with distance, like being tied by a rubber band: pulling two quarks apart demands enormous energy, and that energy materializes as a new quark-antiquark pair, forming new particles. So quarks are permanently confined inside hadrons such as protons and neutrons and can never be seen alone — color confinement.

中微子为什么能轻松穿过整个地球？

Why can a neutrino pass straight through the whole Earth?

中微子只参与弱相互作用和引力，几乎不与物质碰撞，截面极小。每秒有万亿计的中微子穿过你身体而毫无感觉，大多来自太阳核聚变。
Neutrinos feel only the weak force and gravity, so they almost never hit matter—their cross-section is tiny. Trillions pass through you each second, mostly from the Sun's fusion, unnoticed.

希格斯玻色子是怎么给基本粒子「赋予质量」的？

How does the Higgs boson 'give mass' to elementary particles?

根据电弱统一理论，真空里充满希格斯场；有质量的粒子（如 W/Z 玻色子、夸克、轻子）与这个场耦合，运动时就受到「拖拽」而表现为惯性质量，耦合越强质量越大，而无质量的光子不与它耦合。希格斯玻色子是这个场的量子激发。2012年 ATLAS 与 CMS 在 LHC 上发现它， Englert 与 Higgs 获2013年诺贝尔奖。
In electroweak theory the vacuum is filled with the Higgs field; massive particles (W/Z bosons, quarks, leptons) couple to it and feel 'drag' that shows up as inertial mass—stronger coupling means more mass—while the massless photon does not couple. The Higgs boson is the field's quantum excitation. ATLAS and CMS discovered it at the LHC in 2012; Englert and Higgs won the 2013 Nobel Prize.

四种基本力里的弱相互作用到底干什么用？

What does the weak interaction, one of the four fundamental forces, actually do?

弱相互作用作用距离极短，专门负责改变夸克的味道，比如中子衰变成质子、电子和反中微子（β衰变）就靠它。太阳聚变的第一步——两个质子融合成氘——也必须通过弱相互作用，这一步慢得惊人，正因为如此，太阳才像文火慢炖一样稳定燃烧了几十亿年，而不是瞬间炸光。
The weak interaction acts only over tiny distances and changes the flavor of quarks: a neutron decaying into a proton, electron, and antineutrino (beta decay) is its work. The first step of solar fusion, two protons fusing into deuterium, also needs the weak force, and it is so excruciatingly slow that the Sun burns steadily for billions of years instead of exploding all at once.

CP破坏在粒子物理与宇宙学中为何重要，它在标准模型中如何出现？

Why is CP violation important in particle physics and cosmology, and how does it appear in the Standard Model?

CP破坏使物质与反物质演化不对称，是解释宇宙中物质占优所需的条件之一。标准模型中它源于夸克混合矩阵（CKM矩阵）的不可约复相位，在 K 与 B 介子系统中已被实验观测到。
CP violation makes matter and antimatter evolve asymmetrically and is one of the conditions needed to explain the matter dominance of the universe. In the Standard Model it arises from the irreducible complex phase of the quark mixing (CKM) matrix, observed in K and B meson systems.

自然界有哪四种基本相互作用，强弱各如何？

What are the four fundamental forces, and how do their strengths compare?

强相互作用、弱相互作用、电磁力和引力。强相互作用束缚原子核、最强；电磁力管原子与光；弱相互作用管放射性衰变；引力最弱却作用最远，决定天体运动。
They are the strong and weak nuclear forces, electromagnetism, and gravity. The strong force binds nuclei and is strongest; electromagnetism runs atoms and light; the weak force drives radioactive decay; gravity is weakest but reaches farthest, ruling the cosmos.

中微子振荡现象说明了中微子的哪一条基本性质，并如何推翻「中微子无质量」的旧假设？

What fundamental property does neutrino oscillation reveal, and how does it overturn the old assumption of massless neutrinos?

中微子振荡指一种味本征态（如电子中微子）在传播中转化为另一种味的概率性现象，只能在质量本征态与味本征态不同且中微子具有非零质量差时才发生。该现象证明至少两种中微子有质量，从而修正了标准模型中无质量中微子的设定。
Neutrino oscillation is the probabilistic conversion of one flavor eigenstate (e.g., electron neutrino) into another during propagation, which occurs only when flavor and mass eigenstates differ and neutrinos have non-zero mass-squared differences. It proves at least two neutrino species are massive, revising the massless-neutrino assumption in the Standard Model.

希格斯玻色子为什么那么重要？它和质量有什么关系？

Why is the Higgs boson so important, and what does it have to do with mass?

希格斯玻色子是2012年在CERN的大型强子对撞机上发现的。根据希格斯机制，宇宙中充满希格斯场，不同基本粒子与它相互作用的强弱不同，因而获得不同的质量；光子不参与这种作用，所以静质量为零。希格斯玻色子正是希格斯场的量子激发，它自身也有质量。没有它，电子和夸克都会以光速飞行，原子和恒星都无法形成。
The Higgs boson was discovered at CERN's Large Hadron Collider in 2012. In the Higgs mechanism, a field permeates all space, and particles acquire different masses by interacting with it more or less strongly; the photon doesn't interact and stays massless. The Higgs boson is the quantum excitation of that field and has mass itself. Without it, electrons and quarks would fly at light speed and atoms and stars could never form.

强相互作用的“渐近自由”是什么意思？

What does 'asymptotic freedom' of the strong interaction mean?

量子色动力学（QCD）预言，夸克之间距离越近、能量越高，强耦合常数越小，于是表现得几乎自由；距离拉开时耦合反而变强，导致色禁闭。Gross、Politzer 与 Wilczek 于 1973 年算出这一性质并获 2004 年诺奖。
Quantum chromodynamics predicts that the strong coupling grows weak at very short distance and high energy, so quarks act nearly free—asymptotic freedom—while it strengthens at larger distance, causing color confinement. Gross, Politzer and Wilczek computed this in 1973 (Nobel 2004).

Do neutrinos have mass?

标准模型最初假设中微子无质量，但 Super-Kamiokande 等实验在 1998 年观测到中微子振荡——即不同味之间互相转变，而振荡只有在粒子有质量时才可能。2015 年诺贝尔物理学奖授予了这一发现者。
The original Standard Model assumed massless neutrinos, but Super-Kamiokande and others observed neutrino oscillations in 1998—flavors converting into one another, possible only if they have mass. The 2015 Nobel Prize honored this discovery.

希格斯机制如何在不破坏整体规范不变性的前提下，赋予 W、Z 玻色子质量？

How does the Higgs mechanism give mass to the W and Z bosons without breaking overall gauge invariance?

希格斯场在电弱对称破缺中取得非零真空期望值，其与规范场的耦合项转化为规范玻色子的质量项，而拉格朗日量整体仍规范不变。W± 与 Z 玻色子由此获得质量，光子保持无质量，这一机制于2012年由CMS与ATLAS实验发现希格斯玻色子证实。
The Higgs field acquires a non-zero vacuum expectation value in electroweak symmetry breaking, and its coupling to gauge fields becomes a mass term for the gauge bosons while the full Lagrangian remains gauge invariant. The W± and Z bosons gain mass while the photon stays massless; the mechanism was confirmed by the 2012 CMS and ATLAS discovery of the Higgs boson.

量子色动力学中的「色禁闭」为何使我们无法观测到自由夸克？

Why does color confinement in quantum chromodynamics prevent us from observing free quarks?

QCD中耦合常数随距离增大（红外奴役），拉开夸克需注入的能量会在真空中产生新的夸克-反夸克对，故能量用于产生介子而非分离单个夸克。因此色荷被禁闭在强子内部，只能观测到无色的强子。
In QCD the coupling grows with distance (infrared slavery); pulling quarks apart injects energy that creates new quark-antiquark pairs from the vacuum, so the energy goes into making mesons rather than isolating a quark. Color charge is therefore confined inside colorless hadrons and only hadrons are observable.

What is antimatter, and what happens when it meets ordinary matter?

每种粒子都有对应的反粒子：电荷等量子数相反、质量相同，比如正电子是电子的反粒子。当粒子与反粒子相遇时，两者一起湮灭，质量按 E=mc² 全部转化为能量（通常是高能光子）。安德森1932年在宇宙线中发现正电子，是反物质存在的首个证据；今天大型强子对撞机也能制造反质子等。
Every particle has an antiparticle with opposite charge and same mass—e.g., the positron is the electron's antiparticle. When a particle meets its antiparticle they annihilate, converting their mass entirely to energy (usually high-energy photons) per E=mc². Anderson discovered the positron in cosmic rays in 1932, the first evidence of antimatter; the LHC now makes antiprotons too.
