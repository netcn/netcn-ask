什么是约束力与广义坐标？

What are constraint forces and generalized coordinates?

约束限制物体的可能运动方式，为维持约束而产生的力叫约束力（如绳张力、桌面支持力）。广义坐标是能独立描述系统位形的最少变量，可自然消去理想约束力。拉格朗日方程正是建立在广义坐标之上。
Constraints limit how a body can move, and the forces that maintain them are constraint forces (like string tension or normal support). Generalized coordinates are the minimal independent variables describing a system's configuration and can naturally eliminate ideal constraint forces. Lagrange's equations are built on generalized coordinates.

哈密顿力学与辛几何有什么关系？

What is the relation between Hamiltonian mechanics and symplectic geometry?

哈密顿力学把运动写成正则方程，其相空间天然带有辛结构——一种保持面积的几何。演化由哈密顿量生成的辛变换描述，使相体积守恒（刘维尔定理）。辛几何正是这一结构在现代数学中的抽象。
Hamiltonian mechanics writes motion as canonical equations, and its phase space carries a natural symplectic structure—a geometry that preserves area. Evolution is a symplectic transformation generated by the Hamiltonian, conserving phase volume (Liouville's theorem). Symplectic geometry is the modern mathematical abstraction of this structure.

最小作用量原理怎样统一力学？

How does the principle of least action unify mechanics?

最小作用量原理要求真实运动使作用量 S=∫L dt 取极值，对所有力学系统形式统一。从它可导出牛顿定律、拉格朗日方程和哈密顿方程，也与量子力学的路径积分一脉相承。它是连接经典与量子、理论与几何的枢纽。
The principle of least action requires that the true motion makes the action S=∫L dt extremal, with a uniform form for all mechanical systems. From it one derives Newton's laws, Lagrange's and Hamilton's equations, and it connects directly to the quantum path integral. It is the hub linking classical and quantum, theory and geometry.

拉格朗日力学如何替代牛顿力学？

How does Lagrangian mechanics replace Newtonian mechanics?

拉格朗日力学用系统的动能与势能之差 L=T−V 构造作用量，并以作用量取极值的最小作用量原理给出运动方程。它不依赖力的分解，特别适合约束系统和复杂坐标。这种变分视角也是量子力学和场论的基础。
Lagrangian mechanics builds an action from L=T−V, the difference of kinetic and potential energy, and derives motion from the principle that the action is extremal. It avoids force decomposition and suits constrained systems and complex coordinates. This variational viewpoint also underlies quantum mechanics and field theory.
