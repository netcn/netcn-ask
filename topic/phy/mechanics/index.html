刚体绕固定轴转动时如何描述？

How is rotation of a rigid body about a fixed axis described?

刚体定轴转动可用角位移、角速度和角加速度描述，转动惯量相当于平动中的质量。转动定律为合外力矩等于转动惯量乘以角加速度。
Fixed-axis rotation of a rigid body is described by angular displacement, angular velocity, and angular acceleration, with the moment of inertia playing the role of mass. The rotational law states net torque equals moment of inertia times angular acceleration.

什么是简谐运动？

What is simple harmonic motion?

简谐运动是一种回复力与位移成正比且方向相反的周期性运动，如弹簧振子和单摆的小角度摆动。其位移随时间按正弦或余弦规律变化，周期与振幅无关。
Simple harmonic motion is periodic motion where the restoring force is proportional to and opposite the displacement, as in a mass on a spring or a pendulum at small angles. The displacement varies as a sine or cosine, and the period is independent of amplitude.

什么是终端速度？

What is terminal velocity?

物体下落时空气阻力随速度增大，当阻力与重力相等时合力为零，速度不再增加，这个最大速度叫终端速度。
As an object falls, air drag grows with speed until it equals gravity, so the net force is zero and speed stops increasing. That limiting speed is terminal velocity.

What is conservation of angular momentum?

角动量守恒指在没有外力矩作用时，系统的总角动量保持不变。花样滑冰运动员收拢手臂时转动加快，正是角动量守恒的结果。
Conservation of angular momentum states that a system's total angular momentum stays constant when no external torque acts on it. A spinning skater speeds up when pulling in their arms, a direct result of this law.

阻尼振动与受迫振动的共振条件是什么？

What are the resonance conditions for damped and forced vibrations?

受迫振动中，当驱动频率接近系统的固有频率且阻尼较小时，振幅会显著增大，这就是共振。阻尼会抑制共振峰并使其略向低频偏移。桥梁、乐器乃至原子都可能出现共振现象。
In forced vibration, when the driving frequency nears the system's natural frequency with small damping, the amplitude grows markedly—this is resonance. Damping suppresses the resonance peak and shifts it slightly to lower frequency. Resonance appears in bridges, musical instruments, and even atoms.

刘维尔定理如何描述相体积的守恒？

How does Liouville's theorem describe the conservation of phase volume?

刘维尔定理指出，哈密顿系统的相点云在演化中其相体积保持不变（不可压缩）。这源于相空间流的无散度性质，与辛结构一致。它是统计力学中系综演化的基础。
Liouville's theorem states that the phase volume occupied by a cloud of points in a Hamiltonian system is conserved (incompressible) during evolution. This follows from the divergence-free nature of phase-space flow and matches its symplectic structure. It underlies ensemble evolution in statistical mechanics.

What does conservation of momentum mean?

In a system with no external force, the total momentum (mass × velocity) stays constant before and after collisions. It is a fundamental conservation law.

什么是参考系，为什么它很重要？

What is a reference frame and why does it matter?

参考系是描述物体运动所选取的坐标和观察者背景。同一个运动在不同参考系下可能呈现为静止、匀速或加速，因此速度、时间等都不是绝对的。选择方便的参考系能极大简化物理分析。
A reference frame is the coordinate and observer background chosen to describe motion. The same motion can appear stationary, uniform, or accelerated in different frames, so quantities like velocity and time are not absolute. Picking a convenient frame greatly simplifies physical analysis.

做圆周运动的物体为什么需要向心力？

Why does an object in circular motion need centripetal force?

物体做圆周运动时速度方向不断改变，因此需要指向圆心的合力来改变运动方向，这个力叫向心力。向心力由绳子拉力、重力或摩擦力等提供，并不产生新的力。
In circular motion the velocity direction keeps changing, so a net force pointing toward the center is needed to alter that direction; this is the centripetal force. It is supplied by tension, gravity, friction, etc., and is not a new kind of force.

汽车转弯时，为什么我们感觉被向外推？

Why do we feel pushed outward when a car turns?

这是惯性造成的“离心”感觉：身体想沿直线运动，车却转向，于是相对车被甩向外侧。严格说离心力是惯性在非惯性系中的表现。
It is inertia: your body wants to keep going straight while the car turns, so relative to the car you seem flung outward. The 'centrifugal force' is an apparent force in the turning frame.

A fast-spinning object has angular momentum that resists changes in orientation (gyroscopic stability). A tilt makes it precess rather than topple.

Why does a spinning ball curve in flight (a banana kick)?

Spin makes air move faster on one side, lowering pressure there. The pressure difference pushes the ball sideways—the Magnus effect.

宇航员在太空站里为什么会飘起来?是没有重力了吗?

Why do astronauts float inside the space station — is gravity gone?

不是!空间站高度的重力仍有地面的九成左右。真正原因是空间站在做自由落体式的绕圈运动:它向地球掉落,又因横向速度太快而总也掉不到地面。舱内一切物体和宇航员一起下落,相互之间显得没有重量,这叫失重或微重力。就像电梯缆绳断掉的瞬间,你会短暂飘起来一样。
No — gravity up there is still about ninety percent of ground level. The station keeps falling toward Earth while moving sideways so fast it always misses; everything inside falls together and seems weightless: microgravity. Like the instant an elevator cable snaps, you float briefly.

What is projectile motion?

Projectile motion is the curved flight of an object launched and then acted on only by gravity, like a thrown stone or an arrow. It moves at constant speed horizontally while accelerating downward vertically.

为什么单脚站立比双脚站立更难保持平衡？

Why is it harder to balance on one foot than on two?

双脚站立时支撑面更大，身体重心更容易落在支撑范围内。单脚时支撑面变小，重心稍偏就会倒。
Two feet make a wider base of support, so your center of gravity stays within it. One foot gives a tiny base, so small shifts make you tip.

旋转的陀螺为什么不会立刻倒下，而是歪着慢慢画圈（进动）？

Why does a spinning top not fall over immediately, but instead lean and slowly trace circles (precession)?

高速自转让陀螺具有很大的角动量，方向大致沿自转轴。当重力试图把它扳倒时，这个力矩并不让它直接倒下，而是改变角动量的方向，使轴缓慢地绕竖直方向画圈，这就是进动。转速越低，进动越明显，最后才倒下。
Fast spin gives the top a large angular momentum along its axis. When gravity tries to topple it, the torque does not make it fall straight down; instead it changes the direction of the angular momentum, so the axis slowly circles the vertical—this is precession. As the spin slows, precession grows until the top finally falls.

What are work and energy?

撑杆跳高运动员把杆压成弓形时，为什么杆能像弹簧一样把人重新弹得更高？

When a pole vaulter bends the pole into a bow, why can the pole spring back and launch the athlete even higher?

运动员助跑的动能先转化为杆的弹性势能，杆被压弯储存了能量。随后杆回弹，把这部分势能交还给人体，转化为上升的动能和重力势能。所以人能越过比手握杆点高得多的横杆。
The runner's kinetic energy is first stored as elastic potential energy when the pole bends. As the pole straightens, it returns that energy to the athlete as upward kinetic and gravitational potential energy. This lets the vaulter clear a bar well above the hands.

在匀速直线前进的高铁里，你竖直向上跳起，为什么落地时还在原地，没有被甩到车尾？

If you jump straight up inside a train moving at constant speed, why do you land in the same spot instead of being left behind at the back?

你和列车一起拥有相同的水平速度，起跳后这个水平速度并不会消失，所以你始终和车厢保持同步前进。这是惯性参考系的表现：在匀速直线运动的车厢里，物理规律与地面静止时完全一样。只要列车不加速、不转弯，竖直起跳就只改变上下运动。
You and the train share the same horizontal speed, and jumping straight up does not cancel that forward motion, so you keep moving with the carriage. This is what an inertial frame means: inside a vehicle moving at constant velocity, physics works exactly as it does on the ground. As long as the train does not accelerate or turn, the jump only affects vertical motion.

牛顿第二定律说的是什么？

What does Newton's second law state?

牛顿第二定律说，物体的加速度与它受到的合力成正比，与它的质量成反比，公式为 F=ma。也就是说，力越大、物体越轻，加速就越快。
Newton's second law says an object's acceleration is proportional to the net force on it and inversely proportional to its mass, expressed as F=ma. In short, more force or less mass means faster acceleration.

Conservation of momentum means the total momentum of a system stays constant when no external force acts on it. For example, after two billiard balls collide, their combined momentum equals what it was before.
