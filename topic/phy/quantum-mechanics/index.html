量子密钥分发为什么能保证密钥"不可窃听"？

Why does quantum key distribution make an eavesdropping-proof key possible?

量子密钥分发用单光子来传送密钥：一个光子只能携带一个量子比特，窃听者想复制或测量它，就必须先与它发生相互作用，而这必然改变光子的量子态。收发双方核对部分样本时，任何窃听痕迹都会暴露，被污染的密钥随即丢弃重发，量子不可克隆定理保证了这一安全性。我国的"墨子号"卫星已经在千公里尺度上实现了星地之间的量子密钥分发。
Quantum key distribution sends the key one photon at a time: a photon carries a single qubit, and an eavesdropper who tries to copy or measure it must interact with it, inevitably disturbing its quantum state. When the two parties compare test samples, any interception leaves a detectable trace, so the compromised key is discarded and re-sent — guaranteed by the no-cloning theorem. China's Micius satellite has demonstrated satellite-to-ground key distribution across thousands of kilometers.

量子叠加态如何被数学描述？

How is a quantum superposition described mathematically?

一个量子态用希尔伯特空间中的态矢量表示，叠加态是多个基矢量的线性组合：|ψ⟩=α|0⟩+β|1⟩。测量时以|α|²、|β|²的概率得到对应本征态。这种线性结构是量子并行与干涉的根源。
A quantum state is a state vector in Hilbert space, and a superposition is a linear combination of basis vectors: |ψ⟩=α|0⟩+β|1⟩. Upon measurement the outcome is an eigenstate with probabilities |α|² and |β|². This linear structure underlies quantum parallelism and interference.

In a heavily doped p–n junction the bands overlap enough that carriers tunnel straight through the barrier, producing a negative-resistance region where current first rises then falls with voltage. This negative differential resistance lets the device switch state extremely fast, so it is used in microwave and ultra-high-speed circuits. It is a direct application of quantum tunneling in semiconductors.

太阳核心只有1500万度，质子凭什么越过静电斥力发生聚变？

The Sun's core is only about 15 million degrees — how do protons get past electrostatic repulsion to fuse?

按经典物理，两个质子必须爬过库仑势垒才能靠近到足以聚变，而1500万度对应的平均动能远远不够。量子隧穿解决了这个矛盾：即使能量低于势垒，质子的波函数也会渗过势垒，让它以小概率直接出现在近距离处。单个事件概率极低，但太阳里质子数量极其庞大，每秒仍有海量聚变发生。可以说，没有量子隧穿，太阳根本燃不起来。
Classically, two protons must climb over the Coulomb barrier to get close enough to fuse, and 15 million degrees provides far too little energy on average. Quantum tunneling rescues the Sun: even below the barrier, a proton's wavefunction leaks through it, so the proton occasionally appears at fusion distance. Each individual event is extraordinarily rare, but the Sun holds so many protons that fusion proceeds at a colossal rate. Without tunneling, the Sun simply could not ignite.

做贝尔实验检验量子纠缠时，那一对对“纠缠光子”通常是从哪儿来的？

In Bell-test experiments on quantum entanglement, where do the pairs of 'entangled photons' usually come from?

如今的标准做法是让泵浦光子在非线性晶体（如 BBO）中发生自发参量下转换，一个高频光子分裂为两个低频光子，二者能量、动量互补，可制备成偏振纠缠态。但阿斯佩 1982 年的经典实验用的并不是这种源，而是钙原子级联辐射产生的光子对。参量下转换自 1988 年前后才逐步取代原子级联，1995 年 Kwiat 等人的高亮度纠缠源使贝尔检验效率大幅提升。
Today's standard source is spontaneous parametric down-conversion in a nonlinear crystal such as BBO, where one pump photon splits into two lower-frequency photons that are complementary in energy and momentum and can be prepared in a polarization-entangled state. Aspect's classic 1982 experiment, however, did not use this source: its photon pairs came from a calcium atomic cascade. Down-conversion only replaced atomic cascades from around 1988, and the bright entangled source of Kwiat et al. in 1995 sharply improved the efficiency of Bell tests.

海森堡绘景与薛定谔绘景有何不同？

How do the Heisenberg and Schrödinger pictures differ?

在薛定谔绘景中态矢量随时间演化，而算符固定；在海森堡绘景中态固定，算符随时间演化。两者通过幺正变换相联系，对物理预言完全一致。选择哪种只是计算方便的约定。
In the Schrödinger picture the state vector evolves in time while operators are fixed; in the Heisenberg picture the state is fixed and operators evolve. They are linked by a unitary transformation and make identical physical predictions. The choice is merely a matter of calculational convenience.

扫描隧道显微镜是怎样"看见"单个原子的？

How does a scanning tunneling microscope "see" individual atoms?

它利用量子隧穿效应：当极细的金属针尖贴近导电样品表面时，电子能"穿过"针尖与样品之间的真空缝隙，形成微弱电流。隧穿电流对间距极其敏感，针尖与样品的距离每改变一个原子直径，电流就变化约十倍。通过逐点扫描并记录针尖的升降，仪器就能把样品表面的原子排列还原成图像。宾尼希与罗雷尔因此获得1986年诺贝尔物理学奖。
It exploits quantum tunneling: when an atomically sharp metal tip approaches a conducting surface, electrons tunnel across the vacuum gap and form a tiny current. This current is exquisitely sensitive — it changes about tenfold for every change in separation of one atomic diameter. Scanning point by point and recording the tip height reconstructs the surface's atomic arrangement as an image. Binnig and Rohrer won the 1986 Nobel Prize in Physics for this work.

普朗克的「量子假说」是怎样诞生的，解决了什么危机？

How did Planck's quantum hypothesis arise, and what crisis did it resolve?

19世纪末，用经典物理推算「黑体辐射」在高频段会发散（紫外灾难），与实验完全不符。普朗克1900年为拟合数据，大胆假设能量不是连续的，而是以 E = hν 的最小单位（量子）一份份发射吸收，由此得到与实验完美吻合的曲线。这开创了量子论，h 也成为自然界的基本常数。
In the late 19th century classical physics predicted blackbody radiation would diverge at high frequencies (the ultraviolet catastrophe), contradicting experiment. To fit the data, Planck proposed in 1900 that energy is not continuous but emitted and absorbed in minimal quanta E = hν, yielding a curve that matched perfectly. This founded quantum theory and made h a fundamental constant.

什么是波函数坍缩？

What is wavefunction collapse?

在量子力学中，系统的状态由波函数描述，它给出各结果的概率。进行测量时，波函数会从多个可能结果的概率分布突然变为某个确定结果，称为波函数坍缩。
In quantum mechanics a system's state is described by a wavefunction giving probabilities of outcomes. Upon measurement, the wavefunction suddenly changes from a spread of possibilities to one definite result, called collapse.

A quantum state evolves deterministically by Schrödinger's equation before measurement, so why does measurement bring an irreversible 'collapse'?

冯·诺依曼把量子过程分成两段：测量前的幺正演化连续且可逆，测量时系统与宏观仪器的纠缠使叠加在可观测结果上“退相干”，表现为不可逆的投影坍缩。测量问题至今没有完全定论，但它清楚区分了演化与读取两个环节。
Von Neumann split quantum processes into two parts: a unitary, continuous, reversible evolution before measurement, and an irreversible projection at measurement, where entanglement with a macroscopic apparatus decoheres the superposition. The measurement problem remains unsettled, but it clearly separates evolution from readout.

量子计算机凭什么可能比普通计算机快得多？

光由一个个光子组成，每个光子携带的能量正比于它的频率。电子要挣脱金属需要获得一份最低能量（逸出功），而且它只能一次性吸收一个光子，所以只有光子能量大于逸出功时才能把电子打出来。频率太低的光子能量不足，光照再强也无济于事；而频率足够高的光哪怕很微弱也能打出电子。爱因斯坦因此提出光量子假说，并荣获1921年诺贝尔物理学奖。
Light is made of photons, each carrying energy proportional to its frequency. An electron needs a minimum energy, the work function, to escape, and it absorbs photons one at a time, so only photons above that energy can eject it. Low-frequency photons can't do the job however intense the beam, while high-frequency light works even when dim. Einstein's photon hypothesis explained this and won him the 1921 Nobel Prize in Physics.

Two particles can share an entangled state: each one's property, like spin direction, is undetermined beforehand, yet the two are strictly linked. Bell tests later proved the correlation is real and can't be explained by pre-agreed hidden variables — yet it breaks no causality and can't transmit information faster than light.

Semiconductors have a narrow gap, which is why light, temperature or doping can dramatically change their conductivity.
