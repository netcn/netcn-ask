啁啾脉冲放大（CPA）如何实现拍瓦级激光而不烧坏放大器？

How does chirped-pulse amplification reach petawatts without destroying the amplifier?

先把脉冲在时间上“展宽”降低峰值功率再放大，最后用压缩器还原极短高峰值脉冲；这避免了材料在放大时被峰值功率击穿，是超强激光主流方案。
Chirped-pulse amplification stretches the pulse to lower peak power, amplifies it safely, then recompresses it—avoiding damage from peak intensity and enabling petawatt lasers.

激光的“受激辐射”和普通发光有什么本质区别？

What is the key difference between stimulated emission and ordinary light?

普通发光是原子随机掉能级、光波杂乱（自发辐射）；受激辐射是被光子“刺激”后发出完全相同、同方向同相位的光，因此激光高度相干。
Ordinary light is random spontaneous emission with messy waves; stimulated emission makes atoms copy an incoming photon exactly in direction and phase, giving laser its coherence.

激光冷却原子为什么能把温度降到接近绝对零度？

Why can laser cooling bring atoms near absolute zero?

让激光频率略低于原子跃迁频率（红失谐）。多普勒效应使迎面冲向光束的原子优先吸收光子，光子的动量冲击总是与运动方向相反、原子越快减速越明显，就像“逆风中的小球”。反复吸收-发射后原子减速到微开尔文量级；再配合亚多普勒冷却技术可降至纳开尔文。
Tune the laser slightly below the atomic transition (red detuning). The Doppler effect makes atoms moving into the beam absorb more photons, so the photon kicks always oppose the motion — the faster an atom is, the harder it is braked, like a ball against the wind. After repeated absorb-and-emit cycles the atoms slow to the microkelvin range; with sub-Doppler schemes, down to the nanokelvin.

飞秒激光怎么用来“雕刻”角膜或加工玻璃？

How do femtosecond lasers sculpt corneas or machine glass?

极短脉冲在聚焦处瞬间功率极高，通过多光子吸收在极小体积内电离材料，精度达微米级且不伤周围，常用于近视手术和精密打孔。
Ultra-short pulses deliver huge peak power so multiphoton absorption ionizes a tiny volume, carving with micron precision and no heat damage—used in eye surgery and precision drilling.

激光笔的红光为什么又直又亮？

Why is a laser-pointer beam straight and bright?

激光里所有光波步调一致、颜色单一，所以能聚成又直又窄的光束，不像手电筒那样散开。
In a laser all the light waves march in step at one color, so the beam stays straight and narrow instead of spreading out.
