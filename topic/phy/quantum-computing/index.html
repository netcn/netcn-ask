表面码（surface code）为何成为纠错主流方案？

Why has the surface code become the leading error-correction scheme?

表面码把逻辑比特铺在二维方格的多个物理比特上，错误以局部“任意子”激发形式出现，只需近邻测量即可诊断，阈值约 1% 且工程上较易布线。
The surface code spreads a logical qubit over a 2D lattice; errors show as local anyon excitations diagnosed by neighbor measurements, with a ~1% threshold and practical wiring, making it the leading scheme.

量子退火和门模型量子计算本质区别在哪？

What is the essential difference between quantum annealing and gate-model computing?

门模型用可控的逻辑门序列构造算法（如 Shor、Grover）；量子退火把问题编码成能量地形，让系统从高温基态慢慢“滑”到最低能量解，适合组合优化而非通用计算。
Gate models run algorithms via controlled logic gates (Shor, Grover); quantum annealing encodes a problem as an energy landscape and lets the system relax to the minimum, suiting optimization rather than universal computing.

退相干时间 T1 与 T2 为何是量子计算机实用化的“命门”？

Why are decoherence times T1 and T2 the crux of practical quantum computing?

T1 是能量弛豫（0 变 1）、T2 是相位相干丢失的时间；运算必须在远小于 T2 内完成并纠错，否则信息蒸发。材料缺陷、热噪声与电磁干扰都在压缩这两个时间。
T1 is energy relaxation and T2 is dephasing time; computations must finish and be corrected well within T2 or information evaporates. Defects, heat and noise all shrink these times—the crux of practical qubits.

Grover 搜索为何是“平方加速”而非指数加速？

Why is Grover search a square-root speedup, not exponential?

经典搜索无序列表要试约 N 次，Grover 算法用量子振幅放大约根号 N 次就能找到目标，是平方根级加速，适合查找但非指数飞跃。
A classical search needs about N tries; Grover's algorithm uses amplitude amplification to find the target in about sqrt(N) steps—a quadratic speedup useful for search but not exponential.

量子隐形传态能瞬间把人送走吗？

Can quantum teleportation send a person instantly?

不能。它只传送量子态的信息，且必须配合一条经典信道和一份预先共享的纠缠；原物会被销毁，速度也不超光速。
No. It transfers a quantum state using pre-shared entanglement plus a classical channel; the original is destroyed and nothing travels faster than light.

为什么量子计算机容易出错，又要做“纠错”？

Why are quantum computers error-prone and need error correction?

量子比特极易被环境扰动（退相干），单个比特出错率高。量子纠错用多个物理比特编码一个逻辑比特，靠冗余和测量来发现并纠正错误。
Qubits decohere easily, so single-bit error rates are high. Quantum error correction encodes one logical qubit in many physical ones, using redundancy and measurement to detect and fix faults.

量子计算机能取代家里的电脑吗？

Can a quantum computer replace the computer at home?

暂时不能。它只在特定难题（如大数分解、模拟分子）上快，普通上网打字还是经典电脑更合适。
Not yet. It only wins on special hard tasks like factoring or molecule simulation; everyday typing is still better on classical PCs.

量子计算机和家里的电脑有什么不一样？

How is a quantum computer different from the computer at home?

普通电脑用 0 或 1 的小开关；量子计算机用“量子比特”，能同时是 0 和 1 的叠加，像一次尝试很多种可能。
Normal computers use bits that are 0 or 1; a quantum computer uses qubits that can be both at once, exploring many possibilities in parallel.
