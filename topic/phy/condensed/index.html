玻色-爱因斯坦凝聚为什么只在极低温出现？

Why does Bose-Einstein condensation occur only at very low temperature?

当热德布罗意波长 λdB=h/√(2πmkT) 增长到粒子平均间距量级时，全同玻色子会宏观占据同一基态；临界温度 Tc∝(n/ζ(3/2))^(2/3)ℏ²/(2πmk)，通常需纳开量级。
When the thermal de Broglie wavelength reaches the interparticle spacing, identical bosons macroscopically occupy the ground state; the critical temperature is so low (nK scale) that it needs extreme cooling.

安德森局域化如何说明无序也能阻止导电？

How does Anderson localization show disorder can stop conduction?

在一维或强无序系统中，电子波的多次散射产生相干背向散射，使波函数指数局域在某个区域，扩散常数趋于零，即使无相互作用也会出现金属-绝缘体转变。
In 1D or strongly disordered systems coherent backscattering localizes electron waves exponentially, killing diffusion and causing a metal-insulator transition even without interactions.

能带理论如何解释导体、半导体与绝缘体的区别？

How does band theory distinguish conductors, semiconductors, insulators?

晶体中电子能级展宽为能带；价带与导带之间若有重叠或部份填充则为导体；有较小带隙（约1 eV）的是半导体，可热或光激发导电；带隙大（大于几 eV）的为绝缘体。
In crystals levels broaden into bands; overlapping or partly filled bands give conductors; a small gap (~1 eV) gives semiconductors excitable by heat/light; a large gap yields insulators.

准晶体为何违反传统晶体学平移对称性却仍可有衍射峰？

Why do quasicrystals break translational symmetry yet still show sharp diffraction?

准晶体具有长程有序但无周期平移对称（如五重旋转对称），其结构由拼接规则生成；数学上它仍有离散傅里叶分量，因此电子或X射线衍射给出明锐而非连续的峰。
Quasicrystals have long-range order without periodic translation (e.g. fivefold symmetry); their aperiodic tiling still yields discrete Fourier components, giving sharp diffraction peaks.

为什么石墨软而金刚石极硬？

Why is graphite soft but diamond extremely hard?

石墨是层状结构，层内强共价、层间仅靠弱范德华力，容易滑动；金刚石中每个碳与四个邻居形成三维强共价网络，方向键处处咬合，所以极硬。
Graphite's layers are weakly bound by van der Waals forces and slide easily; diamond has a 3D covalent network locking every carbon, making it extremely hard.

为什么金属能导电而橡胶几乎不能？

Why do metals conduct but rubber hardly does?

金属外层电子离域成可在晶格中游走的电子海，施加电场便整体漂移形成电流；橡胶的电子被牢牢束缚在共价键里，没有自由载流子，所以是绝缘体。
Metals have delocalized valence electrons free to drift under an electric field; rubber's electrons are bound in covalent bonds with no free carriers, making it an insulator.

What is the Meissner effect in superconductors?

超导体进入超导态时会把内部磁场排出（完全抗磁），而不仅仅是零电阻；这区分了理想导体与真正的超导体，并可用磁通排斥解释磁悬浮。
A superconductor expels internal magnetic field (perfect diamagnetism), not merely zero resistance; this distinguishes it from an ideal conductor and explains magnetic levitation.

为什么掺杂质（掺杂）能把绝缘的硅变成有用的半导体？

Why does doping turn insulating silicon into a useful semiconductor?

纯硅带隙约1.1 eV、本征载流子少；掺入磷（施主）提供多余电子，或掺硼（受主）制造空穴，使室温下有大量可动载流子，导电性可控，这是晶体管的基础。
Pure Si (gap ~1.1 eV) has few carriers; doping with phosphorus (donors) or boron (acceptors) supplies electrons or holes, giving controllable conductivity—the basis of transistors.

为什么玻璃是固体却能像液体一样透明？

Why is glass a solid yet transparent like a liquid?

玻璃是非晶态固体，内部原子无序但被冻住不流动；可见光光子能量不足以激发其电子跃迁，也几乎不被散射，于是多数光直接穿过，看起来透明。
Glass is an amorphous solid—its atoms are frozen in disorder, not flowing. Visible photons lack energy to excite its electrons and are barely scattered, so light passes through.

为什么磁铁能粘在冰箱上，陶瓷碗却不行？

Why does a magnet stick to the fridge but not to a ceramic bowl?

冰箱门多是含铁的金属，会被磁化而吸住磁铁；陶瓷碗不是铁磁性材料，磁场穿过去不留手，所以粘不住。
The fridge is iron-based metal that gets magnetized and holds the magnet; a ceramic bowl isn't ferromagnetic, so the field passes through and it won't stick.
