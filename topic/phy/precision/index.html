原子钟用铯还是用光晶格钟，为什么光钟有望比铯钟准上百倍？

Cesium or optical lattice clock — why might an optical clock beat cesium by a hundredfold?

铯钟“滴答”在微波频率，每秒约九十亿次；光钟用可见光，频率高了几万倍，相当于“秒针”跑得更快、能分得更细。加上把原子关进光晶格里更安静，误差就更小。
A cesium clock ticks in the microwave range (~9 billion/s); an optical clock uses visible light, tens of thousands of times higher, so its 'hand' moves far finer. Trapping atoms in an optical lattice also quiets perturbations, shrinking the error.

卡文迪许的扭秤当年怎么测出引力常数 G，从而“称”出地球质量？

How did Cavendish's torsion balance measure G and thereby 'weigh' the Earth?

他在细丝下吊一根横杆，两端各有一个小铅球，旁边放两个大铅球。大球用引力轻轻扭动横杆，测出扭转角度就能算出 G，再结合地表重力就反推出地球的质量。
He suspended a rod with small lead balls on a fine wire and placed large lead balls nearby. Their gravity twisted the rod minutely; from the twist angle he found G, and with surface gravity he inferred Earth's mass.

千克现在用“基布尔秤”来定义，它怎么把质量和普朗克常数连起来？

The kilogram is now defined with a Kibble balance — how does it link mass to Planck's constant?

基布尔秤先让通电线圈在磁场里受的力等于重力，再用同一套线圈在运动中产生电压，两步走把“质量”换算成电学量。因为普朗克常数已被固定为准确值，质量就经由量子标准被定义。
A Kibble balance first balances gravitational force on a coil with magnetic force, then relates that to a voltage from the same coil in motion. This converts mass into electrical quantities tied to Planck's constant, now fixed exactly, defining the kilogram quantum-mechanically.

#基布尔秤 #普朗克常数 #千克

LIGO 用两条四公里长的臂组成激光干涉仪，光程差小到质子直径级别是怎么测出来的？

LIGO's interferometer has two 4-km arms — how can it measure length changes as tiny as a proton's diameter?

引力波经过时会极其轻微地改变两条臂的长度。激光在臂里来回反射几百次把差异放大，再让两束光干涉：臂长差一点，明暗条纹就移动，由此读出十万亿分之一的伸长。
A passing gravitational wave slightly changes one arm's length versus the other. The laser bounces hundreds of times to amplify the difference, then the two beams interfere; a tiny arm-length change shifts the fringe pattern, revealing a stretch of one part in 10^21.

#LIGO #干涉 #引力波

激光干涉引力波天文台（LIGO）为什么建两条互相垂直的臂，而不是一条？

Why does LIGO use two perpendicular arms instead of one?

引力波会在一个方向拉长、垂直方向压短。两条互相垂直的臂能同时看到“一边长一边短”的差值，把极微小的形变变成可测的干涉条纹移动；单臂则无法区分这种拉伸与压缩。
A gravitational wave stretches one direction while squeezing the perpendicular one. Two perpendicular arms reveal the 'one longer, one shorter' difference as a measurable fringe shift; a single arm couldn't separate stretch from compression.

原子钟靠什么“滴答”——为什么用原子而不是钟摆？

What makes an atomic clock 'tick' — and why atoms instead of a pendulum?

原子里的电子在两个能级间跳跃，会吸收或放出频率极其稳定的光，这个频率就是天然的“秒针”。比钟摆受温度、重力影响稳得多，所以能几千万年不差一秒。
Electrons in an atom jump between two levels, absorbing or emitting light of an extremely stable frequency — that frequency is the natural 'second hand'. It's far steadier than a pendulum affected by temperature and gravity, so the clock barely drifts for millions of years.

#原子钟 #能级 #频率

为什么石英表比机械表准那么多——晶体的“压电”怎么当钟用？

Why is a quartz watch far more accurate than a mechanical one — how does piezoelectric crystal act as a clock?

石英晶体会以非常稳定的频率来回伸缩（压电效应），通电能让它“振”个不停。电子电路数它的振动次数来计秒，比靠发条和齿轮的机械表稳得多。
A quartz crystal oscillates at a very stable frequency (the piezoelectric effect), driven by electricity. The circuit counts those vibrations to keep seconds, far steadier than a spring-and-gear mechanical movement.

#石英 #压电 #振荡

为什么测量越精密，越要把仪器放在真空中、埋在地下、隔振到极致？

Why does more precise measurement demand vacuum, underground sites and extreme vibration isolation?

因为空气分子会撞、地面会颤、温度变化会伸缩——任何一点扰动都会盖过要测的微弱信号。真空去掉分子撞击，地下躲开人多车闹，隔振台滤掉震动，信号才显现。
Air molecules jostle, the ground trembles, temperature makes things expand — any disturbance can drown the faint signal. Vacuum removes molecular hits, underground sites dodge noise, isolation tables filter vibration, letting the signal emerge.

#隔振 #真空 #精密

为什么今天的一米，是用光在真空里跑一段极短时间来定义的？

Why is today's meter defined by light traveling a tiny time in vacuum?

光速在真空中是固定不变的准确值，所以让“米 = 光在 1/299792458 秒内走的距离”，定义既稳定又全世界一致，不依赖某把具体的尺子。
The speed of light in vacuum is a fixed, exact value, so defining the meter as the distance light travels in 1/299,792,458 seconds is stable and identical everywhere — no physical ruler required.

#米 #光速 #定义

科学家为什么说“秒”是靠原子“数”出来的？

Why do scientists say the 'second' is counted out by atoms?

因为现在的一秒，定义为某种原子振动了九十多亿次的时间。科学家让原子帮我们“数拍子”，数到那个数就是一秒，比看太阳或摆钟都准。
Today one second is defined as a set number of vibrations of a certain atom — over nine billion. Atoms 'keep the beat' for us; count to that number and it's one second, more reliable than the Sun or a pendulum.
