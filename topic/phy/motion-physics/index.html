为什么高尔夫球表面布满小凹坑，而不是光滑的？

Why does a golf ball have dimples instead of a smooth surface?

凹坑能扰乱球体后方的气流，让湍流边界层贴着球面更久，从而减小尾流区的低压阻力。结果球飞得更远更稳，比光滑球省力。
Dimples disturb the airflow so the turbulent boundary layer stays attached to the ball longer, reducing the low-pressure drag behind it. The ball then flies farther and more steadily than a smooth one.

为什么乒乓球很容易被搓出强烈的旋转，并影响落点？

Why is a ping-pong ball so easy to impart heavy spin to, and how does it affect the bounce?

乒乓球很轻、表面积相对大，击球时拍面摩擦就能让它高速自转。旋转改变球周围气流，利用马格努斯效应使落点和弧线偏离直线轨迹。
A ping-pong ball is very light with a relatively large surface, so a brushing stroke makes it spin fast. The spin changes the airflow around it, and the Magnus effect bends its arc and landing point away from a straight path.

为什么撑杆跳运动员能跳到比自己身高高得多的高度？

Why can a pole vaulter clear a height far above their own body?

运动员把助跑的动能储存在弯曲的撑杆里变成弹性势能，杆伸直时再把这些能量释放，把人高高弹起。配合身体翻越动作，重心得以越过横杆。
The vaulter stores the run-up's kinetic energy as elastic potential energy in the bent pole, which then releases it to fling the body upward. With a skillful turnover, the center of mass clears the bar well above head height.

为什么转动汽车方向盘，车就会朝相应方向转弯？

Why does turning the steering wheel make the car turn in that direction?

方向盘通过转向机构改变前轮朝向，车轮与地面的侧向摩擦力提供向心力，把车拉离原直线轨迹。轮子的偏转角度决定了转弯的半径和方向。
The wheel turns the front wheels via the steering linkage; the tires' sideways friction with the road supplies a centripetal force that pulls the car off its straight path. The wheel angle sets the turn's radius and direction.

为什么足球里的香蕉球会边飞边拐弯？

Why does a soccer banana kick curve sideways as it flies?

球员踢球时让球强烈旋转，旋转侧与空气摩擦带动气流，使球两侧气压不同。压强差形成侧向力，球便沿弧线飞行，这就是马格努斯效应。
The player gives the ball heavy spin, and the spinning side drags the air so the pressures on the two sides differ. The pressure difference creates a sideways force, bending the ball's path, an effect called the Magnus effect.

为什么跳高运动员要先把速度跑起来再起跳？

Why do high jumpers build up speed before they take off?

助跑把身体的水平动能转化为起跳时的竖直动能和转动动能，使人跳得更高。动能越大，能克服重力做的功越多，重心就能升得更高。
The run-up converts the body's horizontal kinetic energy into vertical and rotational energy at takeoff, letting the athlete jump higher. More kinetic energy means more work against gravity, raising the center of mass further.

为什么在跑步机上原地跑和在地面往前跑，身体感觉不太一样？

Why does running on a treadmill feel different from running forward on the ground?

在地面跑你要克服惯性把自己整体向前加速，还要对抗空气阻力；跑步机只是把腿往后蹬、带子把你往后拉回原位，相对空气几乎静止。少了空气阻力和整体位移，体感更轻松。
On the ground you must accelerate your whole body forward against inertia and air resistance; on a treadmill you only push your legs back while the belt pulls you to the same spot, nearly stationary relative to the air. Without air drag and net displacement, it feels easier.
