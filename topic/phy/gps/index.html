萨奈克效应在卫星与光纤陀螺中如何体现？

How does the Sagnac effect show up in satellites and fiber gyros?

绕闭合回路旋转时，顺逆时针光程因相对论性时间、长度差而不同，产生相位偏移；卫星和环形激光、光纤陀螺都靠此感知角速度。
Rotation around a closed loop makes counter-propagating light paths differ by relativistic time/length shifts, yielding a phase offset; satellites and ring laser/fiber gyros sense rotation this way.

除了相对论，GPS 还要克服哪些物理误差？

Besides relativity, what physical errors must GPS overcome?

电离层和对流层延迟、卫星钟老化、多径反射、地球自转（萨奈克效应）都会造成偏差；系统靠多频校正和地面监控网实时修正。
Ionosphere and troposphere delays, clock drift, multipath reflection and Earth's rotation (Sagnac effect) all bias readings; multi-frequency correction and ground monitors fix them live.

GPS 同时用到狭义和广义相对论，差多少？

How much do special and general relativity each matter for GPS?

卫星以约一万四千公里时速运动（狭义相对论使钟变慢）又处在更弱引力中（广义相对论使钟变快），净效果是星上钟每天快约 38 微秒，不修正定位会每天漂约 10 公里。
At 14,000 km/h and weaker gravity, satellite clocks net about 38 microseconds fast per day; without correction GPS would drift roughly 10 km daily.

手机导航为什么和爱因斯坦有关系？

Why does phone navigation have to do with Einstein?

GPS 卫星飞得快、引力又弱，时钟比地面走得稍快一点，必须按相对论修正，否则导航会差出好几公里。
GPS satellites move fast and feel weaker gravity, so their clocks tick slightly faster; relativity corrections keep your position from drifting by kilometers.
