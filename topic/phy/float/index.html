为什么冰会浮在水上？

Why does ice float on water?

水结冰时体积膨胀、密度变小，所以冰比液态水轻，能浮起来。
Freezing expands water and lowers its density, so ice is lighter than liquid water.

潜水艇怎么控制自己下沉和上浮？

How does a submarine control sinking and rising?

它用压载水舱装水或把水排掉，改变自身的轻重，从而下沉或上浮。
It fills or empties ballast tanks with water to change its weight and so sinks or rises.

救生圈为什么能托住人？

Why does a life ring hold a person up?

救生圈里装满空气，比水轻很多，能把人托在水面上。
A life ring is full of air and much lighter than water, so it holds a person at the surface.

大船为什么浮在水上？

Why does a big boat float on water?

船的身体里装了很多空气，整体比水轻，就浮在上面。
A boat's body holds lots of air, so altogether it is lighter than water and floats.

为什么有的东西沉、有的东西浮？

Why do some things sink while others float?

比同体积水更密的东西往下沉，比水轻的被水托住浮起来。
Things denser than the same volume of water sink; lighter ones are held up by the water and float.

木头为什么浮在水上？

Why does wood float on water?

木头比同体积的水轻，水就把它托在上面，不会沉。
Wood is lighter than the same amount of water, so the water holds it up instead of sinking it.

为什么洗澡时小鸭子玩具总浮在水面上?

Why does my rubber ducky always float in the bath?

小鸭子里藏着一个秘密——空气!它的橡胶壳里是空心的,比同样大小的水轻得多。水会托起所有比自己轻的东西,所以小鸭子稳稳漂着,石头那样重的小家伙就只能沉底啦。
Your duck hides a secret — air! Its hollow body weighs much less than the water it pushes aside, and water lifts anything lighter. So the duck floats while heavy pebbles sink to the bottom.
