LIGO 怎么测出比原子核还小的晃动？

How does LIGO measure a wobble smaller than an atom's nucleus?

LIGO 用两条 4 公里长的垂直真空臂，激光在两端来回比长度。引力波经过时两臂长度差改变约千分之一质子直径，干涉条纹随之移动。
LIGO uses two 4-km vacuum arms and compares lengths with laser light. A passing wave changes their difference by about a thousandth of a proton's width, shifting the interference pattern.

我们怎么“听”到黑洞碰撞？

How do we 'hear' black holes collide?

两颗黑洞合并会抖出引力波，探测器用激光测出比质子还小的长度变化，再转成声音让我们“听”到。
Merging black holes shake out gravitational waves; detectors catch length changes smaller than a proton and turn them into sound.

引力波到底是什么“声音”？

What kind of 'sound' are gravitational waves?

引力波不是声音，而是时空像水面一样被黑洞碰撞、超新星等大事件“晃”出来的波纹，要用巨大的探测器才能捕捉到。
Gravitational waves are not sound but ripples in spacetime stirred up when black holes collide or stars explode, caught only by giant detectors.
