为什么快充会让电池内部更容易长出“锂枝晶”，戳破隔膜就短路？

Why does fast charging promote 'lithium dendrites' that can pierce the separator and short the cell?

充电太快时，锂离子来不及均匀嵌进负极，就直接在表面堆积成针状结晶（枝晶）。枝晶越长越尖，可能刺穿隔膜碰到对面电极，造成内部短路甚至起火。
Too-fast charging leaves lithium no time to embed evenly, so it plates as needle-like crystals (dendrites). They can grow sharp enough to pierce the separator and touch the other electrode, causing an internal short or fire.

固态电池把液态电解液换成固体，为什么能同时追求更高能量和更安全？

Why can solid-state batteries chase both higher energy and better safety by replacing liquid electrolyte with a solid?

液态电解液易燃，还可能长出锂枝晶刺穿隔膜导致短路。换成稳定的固体电解质后，既能用能量密度更高的金属锂做负极，又不易燃、不易短路，所以又强又稳。
Liquid electrolyte is flammable and can grow lithium dendrites that pierce the separator. A stable solid electrolyte allows a high-energy lithium-metal anode while being non-flammable and short-resistant — stronger and safer.

超级电容器和锂电池有什么不同——一个快一个慢的物理原因是什么？

How do supercapacitors and Li-ion batteries differ — what makes one fast and the other slow?

电池靠化学反应把离子搬来搬去，过程慢但能存很多能量；超级电容靠电荷直接贴在电极表面，几乎瞬间完成，但存得少。所以电容适合快充快放，电池适合长时间供电。
Batteries move ions through slow chemical reactions — lots of stored energy but slow. Supercapacitors just pile charge on electrode surfaces — nearly instant, but less energy. Caps are for quick bursts, batteries for lasting power.

为什么一次性电池（干电池）不能像手机电池那样反复充电再用？

Why can't disposable batteries be recharged like a phone battery?

一次性电池里的化学反应大多是“单向”的，放电后产物散开、结构变了，充不回去。可充电电池专门选了能来回折腾的化学反应，所以能反复使用。
Disposable cells use largely one-way reactions; after discharge the products scatter and the structure changes, so they can't be reversed. Rechargeables pick reactions that happily run both ways.

电池两极之间的“电势差”到底是怎么由化学反应产生的？

How does a chemical reaction actually create the voltage between a battery's terminals?

正负极材料对电子的“亲疏”不同：一端容易把电子交出去（负极），另一端抢着要电子（正极）。电子被这种化学势差“推”着走，就形成了我们量到的电压。
The two electrode materials differ in how strongly they hold electrons: one readily gives them up (anode), the other grabs them (cathode). That chemical potential difference 'pushes' electrons, giving the measured voltage.

电池充电时，锂离子到底在怎么“搬家”？

How exactly do lithium ions 'move house' while a battery charges?

充电时，锂离子从正极脱出，穿过中间的电解液和隔膜，钻进负极的层状结构里“住下”；放电时再倒着搬回去，途中经过外电路就推动了电流。
While charging, lithium ions leave the cathode, cross the electrolyte and separator, and lodge in the anode's layered structure; discharging reverses the trip, and their passage through the external circuit drives current.

为什么旧电池用久了会鼓包？

Why does an old battery sometimes bulge?

电池里面发生的化学反应会慢慢产生一点点气体，越积越多就把外壳顶鼓了。就像气球被悄悄打进太多气。
The cell's chemistry slowly makes a little gas; as it builds up, the casing swells — like a balloon quietly overinflated.

为什么冬天的手机电池会“虚电”，明明满格却突然关机？

Why does a phone battery show full charge in winter yet shut off suddenly?

低温下电池里的化学反应变慢，锂离子跑得不利索，电压被暂时“压”低。显示的格子是按常温算的，于是看起来满电，实际一用力电压就掉到关机线以下。
Cold slows the cell's chemistry so lithium ions move sluggishly and the voltage sags. The indicator is calibrated for room temperature, so it looks full, yet under load the voltage drops below the shutoff point.
