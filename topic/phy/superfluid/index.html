超流体和超导体有什么共同的物理本质？

What physical essence do superfluids and superconductors share?

它们都来自“大量粒子凝聚到同一个量子态”：超流体是氦原子集体进入同一态、没有黏性；超导体是电子成对进入同一态、没有电阻。宏观尺度上能看到量子行为，是它们最像的地方。
Both arise from many particles condensing into one quantum state: superfluid helium atoms share a state with zero viscosity, superconducting electron pairs share one with zero resistance. Macroscopic quantum behavior is their common heart.
