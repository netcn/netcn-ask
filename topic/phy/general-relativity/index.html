引力波是如何由加速质量产生的？

How are gravitational waves produced by accelerating masses?

根据广义相对论，当质量分布以非球对称方式加速变化（如双星绕转）时，会扰动时空并以光速向外辐射引力波。它们是时空本身的涟漪，极其微弱，直到2015年才被直接探测到。这证实了爱因斯坦百年前的预言。
In general relativity, when a mass distribution accelerates and changes non-spherically (such as a binary orbit), it ripples spacetime and radiates gravitational waves at light speed. They are ripples of spacetime itself, so faint that they were directly detected only in 2015. This confirmed Einstein's century-old prediction.

爱因斯坦场方程的核心思想是什么？

What is the core idea of Einstein's field equations?

爱因斯坦场方程写成 G_{μν}+Λg_{μν}=(8πG/c⁴)T_{μν}，左边是时空的曲率，右边是物质与能量的分布。它把物质告诉时空如何弯曲、时空告诉物质如何运动定量化。这是广义相对论的灵魂。
Einstein's field equations read G_{μν}+Λg_{μν}=(8πG/c⁴)T_{μν}, where the left side is spacetime curvature and the right side is the distribution of matter and energy. They quantify 'matter tells spacetime how to curve, spacetime tells matter how to move.' This is the soul of general relativity.

黑洞的事件视界是什么？

What is the event horizon of a black hole?

事件视界是黑洞周围的一个边界，一旦越过它，包括光在内的任何东西都无法逃离。它是因果的分界线，而非实体表面。视界的大小由黑洞的质量决定，称为史瓦西半径。
The event horizon is a boundary around a black hole beyond which nothing, not even light, can escape. It is a causal dividing line rather than a physical surface. Its size, set by the black hole's mass, is called the Schwarzschild radius.

等效原理在广义相对论中扮演什么角色？

What role does the equivalence principle play in general relativity?

等效原理指出，在局部小范围内，匀加速参考系中的物理效应与均匀引力场无法区分。它把引力与加速度等同起来，是广义相对论的出发点。由此才能把引力重新理解为时空的几何性质。
The equivalence principle states that locally, the physical effects in a uniformly accelerating frame are indistinguishable from those in a uniform gravitational field. It equates gravity with acceleration and is the starting point of general relativity. From it, gravity is reinterpreted as the geometry of spacetime.

引力时间膨胀如何被GPS验证？

How is gravitational time dilation confirmed by GPS?

GPS卫星处在较弱的引力场中，其原子钟比地面钟走得快；同时由于轨道运动又有狭义相对论的速度时间膨胀使其变慢。两种效应叠加约每天快38微秒，若不修正，定位误差会迅速累积到公里级。这正是相对论日常应用的例子。
GPS satellites sit in a weaker gravitational field, so their atomic clocks tick faster than ground clocks; the orbital motion's special-relativistic time dilation slows them slightly. The net effect is about 38 microseconds per day faster, and without correction positioning errors would accumulate to kilometers. This is a daily application of relativity.

引力如何弯曲时空？

How does gravity bend spacetime?

广义相对论认为，质量和能量告诉时空如何弯曲，弯曲的时空告诉物质如何运动。在地球附近，时空被压出类似漏斗的几何，使自由落体的物体沿最短路径（测地线）运动，看起来就像被引力吸引。引力不是传统意义上的力，而是几何效应。
General relativity holds that mass and energy tell spacetime how to curve, and curved spacetime tells matter how to move. Near Earth, spacetime is distorted into a funnel-like geometry, so freely falling objects follow the shortest paths (geodesics), appearing as if pulled by gravity. Gravity is not a force in the traditional sense but a geometric effect.

什么是引力红移？

What is gravitational redshift?

引力红移指光从强引力场逃向弱引力场时，频率降低、波长变长，向红端偏移的现象。它本质上是引力时间膨胀的另一种表现：在强场处时间流得更慢，钟频更低。这一效应已在实验室和天文观测中被精确测量。
Gravitational redshift is the phenomenon where light escaping from a strong gravitational field to a weaker one lowers its frequency and lengthens its wavelength, shifting toward the red. It is another face of gravitational time dilation: time flows slower in stronger fields, so clocks there tick at lower frequency. This effect is measured precisely in labs and astronomy.
