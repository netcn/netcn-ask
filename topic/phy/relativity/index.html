什么是时空弯曲？

What is the curvature of spacetime?

在广义相对论中，时空不是平坦的背景，而是可被质量能量弯曲的四维结构。物体和光都沿着弯曲时空中的测地线运动，引力即表现为时空的弯曲。
In general relativity, spacetime is not a flat background but a four-dimensional structure curved by mass and energy. Objects and light follow geodesics in this curved spacetime, and gravity is precisely that curvature.

闵可夫斯基时空的几何结构是怎样的？

What is the geometric structure of Minkowski spacetime?

闵可夫斯基时空把三维空间和一维时间合并为四维流形，其度量带有负号：ds²=c²dt²−dx²−dy²−dz²。这种伪黎曼几何让光锥划分出类时、类光和类空区域，决定了因果可达性。它是狭义相对论的几何语言。
Minkowski spacetime merges three space dimensions and one time dimension into a four-dimensional manifold with a metric carrying a minus sign: ds²=c²dt²−dx²−dy²−dz². This pseudo-Riemannian geometry uses light cones to separate timelike, lightlike, and spacelike regions, fixing causal accessibility. It is the geometric language of special relativity.

What is a black hole's event horizon, and why is there no return past it?

The event horizon is the boundary where escape speed exceeds light; cross it and not even light gets out, because spacetime is bent that sharply. It is not a solid surface but a one-way point of no return.

四维时空中的时空间隔为什么是不变量？

Why is the spacetime interval invariant in four-dimensional spacetime?

在闵可夫斯基时空中，两个事件之间的间隔定义为c²Δt²−Δx²−Δy²−Δz²。洛伦兹变换保持这个量不变，就像旋转保持欧几里得距离不变一样。因此所有惯性观察者都同意时空间隔，尽管他们对时间和空间的单独测量可能不同。
In Minkowski spacetime the interval between two events is defined as c²Δt²−Δx²−Δy²−Δz². Lorentz transformations preserve this quantity, just as rotations preserve Euclidean distance. Thus all inertial observers agree on the interval even though their separate measures of time and space differ.

Because γ → ∞ as v → c, accelerating a massive object to light speed would take infinite energy, which is why c is the speed limit.
