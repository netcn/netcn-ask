驱动蛋白在微管上的步长约为8纳米，与热涨落的尺度相当，它凭借什么机制保证长期单向行进而不显著后退？

Kinesin advances along a microtubule in steps of roughly eight nanometers, a scale comparable to thermal fluctuations — what mechanism lets it march persistently in one direction instead of wandering backward?

驱动蛋白的两个头部沿微管交替结合，ATP水解促使颈链接合，构象偏摆把后侧头部甩向前方，构成动力冲程。化学循环与机械步进严格门控：前一头未结合ATP时后一头难以脱离，形成时序上的棘轮约束。每个ATP释放的自由能因此被定向使用，向后滑移的概率被压得很低，净单向行走得以维持。
The two heads of kinesin bind the microtubule alternately, and ATP hydrolysis docks the neck linker so that a conformational power stroke swings the rear head forward. The chemical cycle is tightly gated with stepping: the trailing head cannot detach until the leading head has bound ATP, imposing a timing-ratchet constraint. Free energy from each ATP molecule is thereby spent directionally, backward excursions stay rare, and net unidirectional motion persists.

Near 65 pN the contour length of dsDNA jumps by roughly seventy percent while the tension barely changes. Early interpretations split between an intact-base-pair conversion to S-DNA and a rupture of interstrand hydrogen bonds yielding parallel single strands. Later torque-constrained stretching and nick-dependent fluorescence assays established that strand separation contributes substantially at least in nicked molecules, with transition enthalpy and its salt dependence backing a partial-melting picture; the transition now serves as a standard probe of sequence-specific melting energies and protein-induced forces.

驱动蛋白沿微管行走时每步约8纳米，这个步长与微管蛋白二聚体的排列周期有什么对应关系？ATP水解又是怎样推动其构象循环的？

Kinesin advances roughly 8 nm per step along a microtubule—how does this stride map onto the tubulin dimer lattice, and how does ATP hydrolysis drive its conformational cycle?

微管由α/β微管蛋白异二聚体首尾相接组装而成，纵向重复周期恰好是8纳米，驱动蛋白的两个头部正好落在相邻结合位点上交替前进。ATP与落后头部结合触发颈链对接，将它甩到前方位点；到达后该头部水解释放磷酸，而后头部在下一次ATP结合时才释放ADP。这种握手式门控保证了步进的定向性和高持续性。
Microtubules assemble from head-to-tail α/β-tubulin heterodimers whose longitudinal repeat is exactly 8 nm, so the two heads of kinesin land on successive binding sites and alternate. ATP binding to the trailing head docks its neck linker and swings it to the forward site; after arrival it hydrolyzes and releases phosphate, while the rear head releases ADP only when the next ATP binds. This hand-over-hand gating keeps stepping directional and highly processive.

双光子显微镜为什么能在活体组织中比共聚焦显微镜看得更深,同时光损伤更小?

Why can two-photon microscopy image deeper into living tissue than confocal microscopy while causing less photodamage?

因为激发只发生在焦点。双光子吸收需两个近红外光子几乎同时打到同一分子,概率正比于光强平方,仅焦点亮度足够,焦点外组织不被激发,天然具备光学层切能力,也无需共聚焦针孔。近红外光散射更弱,穿透可达数百微米;单个光子能量更低,光漂白与光毒性随之明显下降。Denk等人1990年据此奠定了该技术。
Because excitation occurs only at the focus. Two-photon absorption needs two near-infrared photons to hit one molecule almost simultaneously, with probability scaling as intensity squared; only the focus is bright enough, tissue elsewhere stays unexcited, giving intrinsic optical sectioning without a pinhole. Near-infrared light scatters less and penetrates several hundred micrometers, while lower photon energy markedly reduces photobleaching and phototoxicity. Denk and colleagues laid the foundation of this technique in 1990.

光学相干层析(OCT)为何特意采用低相干光源,反而能看清组织内部的分层结构?

Why does optical coherence tomography deliberately use a low-coherence source, and how does that reveal layered structures inside tissue?

因为深度被编码在相干长度之中。低相干光仅在样品臂与参考臂光程差小于相干长度(数到数十微米)时才干涉,平移参考镜便逐深度读出背散射信号,相干性本身成了深度选通的门。配合对回波的外差探测,灵敏度足以捕捉微弱的散射光,于是在视网膜这类透明度差的生物组织里也能重建毫米量深的横断面图像,无须切片。
Because depth is encoded in the coherence length. Low-coherence light interferes only when the path difference between sample and reference arms stays below the coherence length of a few to tens of micrometers, so translating the reference mirror reads out backscattered signals depth by depth, with coherence acting as a depth gate. Together with heterodyne detection of the returning wave, the sensitivity captures faint scattering, reconstructing millimeter-scale cross-sectional images even in poorly transparent biological tissue such as the retina without any sectioning.
