量子力学和广义相对论为什么这么难缝在一起？

Why is it so hard to sew quantum mechanics and general relativity together?

量子场论把时空当作固定舞台，广义相对论却让时空本身成为动力学对象，两者对背景的处理根本冲突。直接量子化引力会出现无法用有限个参数消除的发散，而普朗克尺度的实验又几乎无法企及，理论缺少数据约束。
Quantum field theory treats spacetime as a fixed stage, whereas general relativity makes spacetime itself dynamical, and the two views of the background clash. Naively quantising gravity gives divergences that no finite set of parameters can absorb, and Planck-scale experiments are practically out of reach, so the theories go untested.

大型对撞机运行多年，为什么还没找到超出标准模型的新粒子？

After years of collider running, why has no particle beyond the Standard Model shown up?

可能新粒子的质量高于现有对撞能量，也可能它们与已知物质耦合极弱，或藏在难以分辨的本底之中。这让“自然性”问题更加尖锐，也推动实验转向精密测量、稀有衰变和暗区搜寻等互补路线。
New states may simply be heavier than current collision energies, or couple so weakly that they hide, or sit under irreducible backgrounds. That sharpens the naturalness puzzle and is pushing experiments toward precision measurements, rare decays and dark-sector searches.

数学为什么能如此惊人地准确描述物理世界？

Why is mathematics so unreasonably effective at describing the physical world?

物理学家维格纳称之为“数学在自然中惊人的有效性”：本为纯抽象而发展的数学，竟能极其精确地描述物理实在。一种解释是，我们只保留了贴合现实的数学，失败的模型被淘汰，而宇宙本身具有数学可捕捉的简单规律。至于数学究竟是“发现于”自然还是由心智“强加于”自然，仍是未解的科哲难题。
Physicist Eugene Wigner called it the 'unreasonable effectiveness of mathematics': math developed for pure abstraction turns out to describe physical reality with stunning accuracy. One answer is that we keep only the math that fits—failed models are discarded—and that the universe has simple, regular structures our math can capture. Whether math is 'discovered' in nature or imposed by mind remains a live philosophical puzzle.

意识能不能最终用物理定律解释？

Can consciousness ultimately be explained by physical law?

神经科学已能把注意、记忆等功能与具体的电化学过程对应起来，但为什么这些过程会伴随主观体验，仍被称为“难问题”。目前的整合信息论、全局工作空间等方案都还缺乏可判定的实验预言，量子意识假说也没有可靠证据。
Neuroscience can map attention or memory onto specific electrochemical processes, yet why any process is accompanied by subjective experience is still called the hard problem. Integrated information theory, global workspace models and quantum proposals all lack decisive experimental tests.

既然基本方程对时间反演几乎对称，我们为什么只记得过去？

If the fundamental equations are nearly time-reversal symmetric, why do we remember only the past?

记忆的形成要在环境中留下不可逆的痕迹，伴随熵的增加，因此记忆的方向与熵增方向绑在一起。而熵为什么会增加，最终指向宇宙初始状态的熵极低这一事实，这个低熵起点本身还没有被解释。
Forming a memory leaves an irreversible record and raises entropy, so the direction of remembering is tied to the direction of entropy growth. Why entropy grows traces back to the extraordinarily low-entropy initial state of the universe, and that starting condition itself remains unexplained.

物理“常数”真的一直不变吗，有办法检验吗？

Are the physical constants really constant, and how could we check?

检验常数是否不变，方法之一是比较遥远类星体古老星光中的原子谱线与实验室数值；谱线偏移就意味着精细结构常数在变化。其他手段包括奥克洛天然反应堆、宇宙微波背景和陨石同位素。迄今多数证据表明，在数十亿年尺度上常数高度稳定，但极微小的变化仍是开放且持续被检验的问题。
Physicists test constancy by comparing the same atomic transitions in ancient light from distant quasars with lab values; a shift in spectral lines would reveal a changing fine-structure constant. Other probes use the Oklo natural reactor, the cosmic microwave background, and meteorite isotopes. So far most evidence shows the constants are constant to high precision over billions of years, but tiny variations remain an open, actively tested question.

物理常数为什么“刚好”允许恒星和生命存在？

Why do the physical constants happen to allow stars and life?

自然常数（如精细结构常数、引力强度）落在一段极窄的范围内，恰好允许原子、稳定的恒星和化学反应存在；稍有偏移，生命便不可能出现。这种“精细调节”引出了人类原则（宇宙之所以如此，是因为只有这样的宇宙才有观察者）和多宇宙假说（存在无数常数不同的宇宙，我们恰好身处可宜居的一个）。它是否需要超越偶然的解释，目前仍有争议。
The constants of nature (like the fine-structure constant or gravitational strength) sit in a narrow range that permits atoms, stable stars, and chemistry; tiny shifts would make life impossible. This 'fine-tuning' prompts the anthropic principle (we observe such a universe because only it allows observers) and multiverse hypotheses (many universes with different constants, we happen to be in a habitable one). Whether it needs an explanation beyond chance remains debated.

#精细调节 #人类原则 #多重宇宙

时间是最基本的东西，还是从别的结构里“涌现”出来的？

Is time fundamental, or does it emerge from something deeper?

在量子引力的某些方案里，惠勒-德威特方程不含时间参数，时间似乎只是子系统之间关联的一种描述方式，这被称为“时间问题”。热时间假说、纠缠涌现时空等思路都主张时间是派生量，但都还没有实验判据。
In several approaches to quantum gravity the Wheeler–DeWitt equation contains no time parameter, suggesting time is only a way of describing correlations between subsystems — the problem of time. Thermal-time and entanglement-based proposals treat time as derived, yet none has an experimental test.
