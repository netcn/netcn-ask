电子之间本来相斥，为什么在超低温下却能两两“手拉手”组成库珀对通过晶格？

Electrons normally repel — why at very low temperature do they pair up as Cooper pairs via the lattice?

一个电子经过正离子晶格时会把格子微微拉近，造成局部正电荷聚集，吸引另一个电子过来。这个“声子当红娘”的间接吸引，在低温下胜过直接排斥，于是电子成对、无阻力地流动。
An electron nudges the positive lattice inward, creating a local positive patch that attracts a second electron. This phonon-mediated attraction wins over direct repulsion at low temperature, so electrons pair and flow without resistance.

SQUID 这种超灵敏磁强计，为什么能测到人体大脑活动产生的微弱磁场？

Why can a SQUID magnetometer detect the faint magnetic fields from human brain activity?

SQUID 利用超导环里磁通的量子化，对极微小的磁场变化都能产生可测的电压。大脑的神经元电流只有极弱的磁场，普通仪器测不到，SQUID 却灵敏到能分辨。
A SQUID exploits quantization of magnetic flux in a superconducting loop, turning tiny field changes into measurable voltage. Neuronal currents make only a faint field, but a SQUID is sensitive enough to resolve it.

第一类和第二类超导体，为什么只有第二类能扛住强磁场去做强磁体？

Why can only type-II superconductors withstand strong fields to make powerful magnets?

第一类超导体一碰到较强磁场就整体失去超导（迈斯纳态被破）。第二类允许磁场以“量子磁通线”的形式局部穿入，只要不超过上限就能继续超导，所以能做核磁共振和聚变堆里的大磁体。
Type-I loses superconductivity entirely above a modest field. Type-II lets magnetic flux enter as quantized vortices while staying superconducting below an upper limit, enabling the strong magnets in MRI and fusion.

为什么超导磁体可以用很小的电源「充」出持续数年的强大磁场?

How can a superconducting magnet hold a powerful field for years after being charged by a small power supply?

超导线圈没有电阻,电流在其中流动几乎不消耗能量。建磁场时电源缓慢给线圈充电,达到目标电流后用超导开关把线圈两端短接,形成一个无阻闭合回路——电流便在这圈超导线里持续环流,磁场随之保持。衰减率主要取决于接头残余电阻与机械微扰,好的MRI或核磁共振谱仪磁体每年电流衰减可低至百万分之一量级,因此只需小电源完成「充电」,之后即可撤去,这正是常规铜线圈无法企及的。
A superconducting winding has no resistance, so current flows without dissipation. The supply slowly ramps the coil to its target current, then a superconducting switch shorts the terminals into a closed resistanceless loop; the current keeps circulating and the field persists. Decay is dominated by joint residual resistance and mechanical disturbances — good MRI and NMR spectrometer magnets lose on the order of one part per million per year. Only a small supply is needed for charging, something copper coils can never match.

#超导磁体 #持久电流 #应用超导

磁悬浮列车用的超导磁体，为什么通常要泡在液氦或液氮里？

Why are the superconducting magnets in maglev trains usually soaked in liquid helium or nitrogen?

超导只在足够低的温度下才出现，液氦（约 -269℃）和液氮（约 -196℃）能提供这种极冷环境。保持低温，磁体才能零电阻地产生强大稳定的磁场。
Superconductivity needs very low temperature, which liquid helium (~-269°C) or nitrogen (~-196°C) supplies. Staying cold lets the magnet carry huge current with zero resistance.

为什么“高温超导”（如能用液氮冷却的那类）的发现，让超导离日常应用近了一大步？

Why did 'high-temperature' superconductors (coolable by liquid nitrogen) bring superconductivity much closer to everyday use?

以前超导要液氦，又贵又难伺候；液氮却便宜又常见。能用液氮冷却，意味着超导电缆、磁体和器件在成本和工程上更容易推广。
Old superconductors needed expensive, fussy liquid helium; liquid nitrogen is cheap and common. Cooling with it makes superconducting cables and magnets far more practical to deploy.

超导体的“临界温度”是什么意思——超过它为什么就“不超导”了？

What is a superconductor's critical temperature — why does it stop superconducting above it?

临界温度是材料从“零电阻”变回普通金属的温度界线。低于它时电子成对、完美导电；一旦升温超过临界值，这对“解散”，电阻就回来了。
The critical temperature is the line below which a material has zero resistance. Below it electrons pair up and conduct perfectly; above it the pairs break and ordinary resistance returns.

#临界温度 #零电阻 #相变

超导的迈斯纳效应说明了什么？

What does the Meissner effect of superconductors show?

迈斯纳效应指超导体在转变温度下会把内部磁场完全排出，表现为完全抗磁。这说明超导态不只是零电阻，更是一个具有宏观量子相干的态。它使超导体能悬浮在磁体之上。
The Meissner effect means a superconductor below its transition temperature expels all internal magnetic field, showing perfect diamagnetism. This shows the superconducting state is not just zero resistance but a macroscopically quantum-coherent state. It lets a superconductor levitate above a magnet.

磁通钉扎为什么能让一小块超导体“卡”在半空，不掉下来？

Why does flux pinning let a small superconductor hang frozen in mid-air?

第二类超导体里的缺陷会“钉住”穿过的磁通线。要移动它，就得强行把磁通线从缺陷里拽出来，需要很大外力，于是它就被稳稳地悬在磁场中的某个位置。
Defects in a type-II superconductor pin the flux lines passing through. Moving it means tearing those lines free, which takes great force, so it stays locked in place within the magnetic field.

#磁通钉扎 #悬浮 #缺陷

第一类和第二类超导体在混合态行为上的核心区别是什么?决定这一区别的关键参数是什么?

What distinguishes type-I from type-II superconductors in their mixed-state behavior, and which key parameters decide this distinction?

第一类超导体只有一个临界场,超过后迈斯纳效应完全破坏;第二类超导体存在下临界场 Hc1 和上临界场 Hc2,在两者之间磁场以量子化磁通涡旋的形式部分穿透体内。区别由京茨堡-朗道参量 κ = λ/ξ 决定:κ < 1/√2 为第一类,κ > 1/√2 为第二类。实用超导材料如 NbTi 和 Nb3Sn 都是第二类,依靠钉扎磁通涡旋来承载大电流并保持零电阻。
A type-I superconductor has a single critical field above which superconductivity collapses entirely, whereas a type-II has lower and upper critical fields Hc1 and Hc2 between which flux penetrates as quantized vortices. The distinction is set by the Ginzburg–Landau parameter κ = λ/ξ: κ < 1/√2 gives type-I, κ > 1/√2 gives type-II. Practical conductors such as NbTi and Nb3Sn are type-II, relying on vortex pinning to carry large currents at zero resistance.

#磁通涡旋 #金兹堡-朗道理论

迈斯纳效应为什么说明超导不只是「零电阻」,而是完全抗磁?

Why does the Meissner effect show superconductivity is more than zero resistance — it is perfect diamagnetism?

如果超导只是「电阻为零的理想导体」,那么按电磁感应规律,降温进入超导态之前就存在的磁场磁通应当被「冻结」保留在体内。但1933年迈斯纳和奥克森菲尔德的实验显示:无论先加磁场还是先降温,只要进入超导态,体内磁感应强度一律被排空为零,磁场被挤到表面薄层内。这种与历史路径无关的完全抗磁,只能用超导基态的热力学性质解释,是判断真正超导电性的标志之一,磁悬浮演示正是它的直观体现。
If a superconductor were merely a perfect conductor with zero resistance, electromagnetic induction would freeze in any magnetic flux present before cooling. But in 1933 Meissner and Ochsenfeld showed that regardless of whether field-cooled or zero-field-cooled, once the material goes superconducting the interior flux is expelled to zero, confined to a thin surface layer. This path-independent perfect diamagnetism follows from the thermodynamics of the superconducting ground state, is a hallmark used to identify genuine superconductivity, and powers the familiar levitation demonstrations.

#超导 #迈斯纳效应 #抗磁性

为什么有的金属冷到零下两百多度，电流流过它却一点也不发热？

Why does some metal, when cooled to below -200°C, carry current without getting hot at all?

因为那种极冷让金属变成了“超导体”，电流可以毫无阻碍地流过去，不浪费一点能量变成热。就像小珠子在光滑到底的轨道上滚，永远不减速。
At that extreme cold the metal becomes a 'superconductor', so current flows with zero resistance and no energy wasted as heat — like a bead rolling on a perfectly smooth track, never slowing.
