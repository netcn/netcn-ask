三棱镜怎么变出颜色？

How does a prism make colors?

三棱镜把白光分开，变成红橙黄绿蓝靛紫好多颜色，像小彩虹。
A prism splits white light into red, orange, yellow, green, blue, indigo, and violet, like a tiny rainbow.

彩虹为什么红色在最外圈？

Why is red on the outside of a rainbow?

阳光经过小水滴时，红光弯得少、紫光弯得多，所以红留在外圈、紫在内圈。
In water drops, red bends least and violet bends most, so red stays on the outside and violet inside.

玻璃为什么能看见对面？

Why can we see through glass?

玻璃很透明，光可以笔直穿过去，所以我们能看见另一边的东西。
Glass is clear, so light passes straight through and we can see what is on the other side.

影子为什么早上和傍晚特别长？

Why are shadows longest in the morning and evening?

太阳刚升起或快落下时斜着照，把影子拉得长长的。
When the sun is low in the sky, it shines sideways and stretches your shadow long.

放大镜为什么能把字变大？

Why does a magnifying glass make words look bigger?

凸透镜让光线弯折后形成一个放大的虚像，所以小字在眼里就显得更大了。
A convex lens bends the light to form an enlarged image, so the small words look bigger to your eye.

三棱镜怎样把白光拆成七种颜色？

How does a prism split white light into seven colors?

白光其实藏着各种颜色，三棱镜让每种颜色弯折的程度不同，便把它们散开成一道彩虹。
White light actually hides every color; a prism bends each color by a different amount, spreading them into a rainbow.

眼镜怎么帮我们看清东西？

How do glasses help us see?

镜片像小透镜，把光弯一弯聚好，在眼睛里照出清楚的图像。

傍晚的天空为什么红红的？

Why is the sky red at sunset?

太阳快下山时，光要走很远的路，蓝光跑散了，红光照到我们眼里。
When the sun is low, its light travels far; blue spreads away and red light reaches our eyes.

为什么会有影子呀？

Why do we have shadows?

光被我们的身体挡住，身后就留下黑黑的一块，那就是影子。
Light is blocked by our body, leaving a dark patch behind us, and that is a shadow.

我们为什么能看到各种各样的颜色？

Why do we see so many different colors?

物体只把某些颜色的光反射进眼睛，大脑再把这些信息看成红、绿、蓝等颜色。
Objects reflect only some colors of light into your eye, and your brain reads them as red, green, blue, and more.
