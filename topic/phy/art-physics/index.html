大理石雕像的皮肤为什么看起来带着“温润的血色”？

Why does marble skin in a sculpture look warm and almost translucent?

光进入大理石后并非立刻反射，而是在晶粒间多次散射几毫米再漫射出来，这叫次表面散射。它使明暗过渡柔和、边缘微微透光，与人体皮肤的光学行为相似，所以视觉上格外像活人。
Light entering marble is not reflected at once but scatters among the crystal grains for a few millimetres before diffusing back out — subsurface scattering. That softens the shading and lets edges glow faintly, mimicking the optics of real skin.

梵高画里的亮黄色为什么在一百多年里慢慢变褐了？

Why has Van Gogh's bright yellow slowly turned brown over a century?

梵高使用的铬黄是铬酸铅颜料，在光照下化学上并不稳定。可见光和紫外线驱动的光化学反应，使铬从六价向三价转化、并改变晶体形态，于是鲜艳的黄逐渐变成暗褐、橄榄色调。这正是他部分向日葵与田野画作百余年来变暗发褐的原因。
Van Gogh used chrome yellow (lead chromate), a pigment chemically unstable under light. Photochemical reactions driven by visible and UV light reduce chromium from Cr(VI) toward Cr(III) and alter the crystal form, shifting the bright yellow toward dull brown and olive tones. This degradation is why some of his sunflowers and fields have darkened over the past century.

颜料越混越暗，屏幕上的光越混越亮，为什么完全相反？

Why does mixing paint darken it while mixing screen light brightens it?

颜料是减色混合：每种色料吸收一部分波长，混在一起吸收的范围越来越宽，反射回来的光就越少。屏幕是加色混合：红绿蓝三种光相加，进入眼睛的能量增加，三者齐全时呈白色。
Paints mix subtractively: each pigment absorbs some wavelengths, so combining them absorbs an ever wider band and reflects less light. Screens mix additively: red, green and blue light add up, so more energy reaches the eye and all three together look white.

音乐厅的混响时间为什么不是越长越好？

Why isn't a longer reverberation time always better in a concert hall?

混响让声音丰满，但过长会使前后音符互相掩盖，语言和快速乐句变得模糊。交响乐厅通常取1.8到2.2秒，歌剧院略短，报告厅只要0.8秒左右，靠吸声材料和扩散体来调节。
Reverberation adds warmth, but too much lets successive notes mask each other, blurring speech and fast passages. Symphony halls usually aim for 1.8–2.2 s, opera houses less, lecture rooms about 0.8 s, tuned with absorbers and diffusers.

文艺复兴画家的“一点透视”背后是什么几何原理？

What geometry underlies the one-point perspective of Renaissance painters?

人眼看到的是三维场景在视网膜上的中心投影，平行线的像会交于同一个消失点。画家把画布当作视线与场景之间的一个平面截面，按投影几何确定物体缩小的比例，画面就与真实视觉一致。
The eye receives a central projection of a three-dimensional scene, in which parallel lines converge on a single vanishing point. Treating the canvas as a plane slicing the bundle of sight lines, the painter sizes objects by projective geometry so the image matches real vision.

为什么拍出的晚霞照片总不如亲眼看到的震撼？

Why do sunset photos never look as stunning as the real thing?

人眼的动态范围可以跨越十几个数量级，还能通过瞳孔和局部适应同时看清明亮天空和暗处剪影。相机传感器的动态范围窄得多，若不用包围曝光或HDR合成，亮部就过曝、暗部就死黑。
The eye spans an enormous range of brightness and adapts locally, so it sees both a glowing sky and dark silhouettes at once. A camera sensor's dynamic range is far narrower, so without bracketing or HDR merging the highlights blow out and the shadows go black.

博物馆为什么严格控制油画展厅的湿度和照度？

Why do museums strictly control humidity and light levels around oil paintings?

油画由有机胶料和多层颜料构成，会吸湿和释湿；湿度波动使画面反复膨胀收缩，容易导致开裂和剥落。光线（尤其紫外）会促使颜料和清漆褪色、发生化学分解，因此展厅照度被严格控制（通常很低并滤除紫外）。同时管控二者，可以减缓这种缓慢的老化化学过程。
Oil paintings are built from organic binders and layered paint that absorb and release moisture; humidity swings make them expand and contract, risking cracking and paint loss. Light—especially UV—drives fading and chemical breakdown of pigments and varnish, so illuminance is kept low and UV is filtered. Controlling both slows the slow chemistry of decay.

历史上蓝色颜料极其昂贵，为什么蓝闪蝶的翅膀不用色素？

Blue pigment was once priceless — so why does a blue morpho need no pigment at all?

蝴蝶翅膀靠的是结构色：鳞片上的纳米级多层结构让特定波长的反射光相干叠加而增强，其余波长被抵消。这种颜色来自几何而非化学，因此不会褪色，观察角度不同色调还会变化。
Its colour is structural: nanoscale multilayers on the scales make reflections of one wavelength interfere constructively while others cancel. Because the hue comes from geometry rather than chemistry it never fades, and it shifts with viewing angle.
