硬盘为什么放弃沿膜面磁化的纵向记录、转向磁化垂直于盘片的垂直记录？其关键的物理机制是什么？

Why did hard drives abandon longitudinal recording, where bits lie in the plane of the disk, in favor of perpendicular recording, and which physical mechanism makes it superior?

纵向记录中磁化沿薄膜平面排列，相邻位元交界处退磁场随密度提高不断增强，使过渡区展宽、读出噪声增大；垂直记录把易磁化轴转到膜面法向，并在记录层下加一层高磁导率软磁底层，与写头磁极构成近似闭合的磁通回路，退磁场被大幅抑制，位元边界更锐利，线密度随之倍增。2005年前后硬盘全面改用垂直记录，成为此后面密度持续攀升的关键转折。
In longitudinal recording the magnetization lies in-plane, so demagnetizing fields at the border between neighbouring bits strengthen as density climbs, widening the transition region and raising read-back noise. Perpendicular recording rotates the easy axis to the film normal and adds a high-permeability soft magnetic underlayer beneath the recording layer; together with the write pole this nearly closes the flux loop, suppressing demagnetization so transitions stay sharp and linear density multiplies. Drives switched over around 2005, the turning point behind today's ever-climbing areal densities.

硬盘中的磁性颗粒为什么不能无限缩小？由此产生的是哪一个物理限制？

Why cannot the magnetic grains in a hard disk be shrunk indefinitely, and which physical limit results?

磁颗粒的热稳定性由磁晶各向异性能垒KV决定，K为各向异性常数，V为颗粒体积。当V缩小到能垒与热能kBT可比时，热涨落会在记录时间尺度内自发翻转磁化方向，造成数据丢失，这一界限称为超顺磁极限。工程上采用高各向异性FePt（L1₀相）介质并结合热辅助磁记录，以继续提升存储面密度。
A grain's thermal stability is set by its magnetocrystalline anisotropy barrier KV, where K is the anisotropy constant and V the grain volume. Once V shrinks so far that the barrier approaches thermal energy kBT, thermal fluctuations spontaneously reverse the magnetization on recording timescales and erase data; this boundary is the superparamagnetic limit. Engineering counters it with high-anisotropy FePt (L1_0 phase) media combined with heat-assisted magnetic recording to keep raising areal density.

为什么现代硬盘读取头和MRAM存储单元普遍采用带MgO晶体势垒的磁性隧道结？

Why do modern hard-disk read heads and MRAM storage cells commonly adopt magnetic tunnel junctions with crystalline MgO barriers?

非晶氧化铝势垒的隧穿电导受界面态主导，室温隧穿磁电阻仅约70%；MgO(001)外延势垒则是自旋对称过滤器：多数自旋的Δ1态与铁磁电极匹配、可相干贯穿，少数自旋通道被抑制，理论上隧穿磁电阻可达上千%。2004年Yuasa等在高单晶Fe/MgO/Fe结中实现室温约113%的TMR(4.2 K下约180%),大幅提升了硬盘读头灵敏度与MRAM开关比。
In amorphous alumina barriers the tunnelling conductance is dominated by interface densities of states, limiting room-temperature TMR to roughly 70%. An epitaxial MgO(001) barrier works as a spin symmetry filter: majority-spin Δ1 states match the ferromagnetic electrodes and tunnel coherently, while minority-spin channels are suppressed, so theory predicts TMR above 1000%. In 2004 Yuasa et al. realized about 113% TMR at room temperature (180% at 4.2 K) in single-crystal Fe/MgO/Fe junctions, greatly improving read-head sensitivity and MRAM on/off ratios.
