重整化解决了量子场论中的什么问题？

What problem in quantum field theory does renormalization solve?

直接计算时，场的自能和真空涨落会导致无穷大的物理量。重整化通过把这些发散吸收到可观测的质量和电荷中，重新标度参数，得到有限且精确的预言。它使量子电动力学成为史上最精确的理论之一。
In direct calculation, a field's self-energy and vacuum fluctuations yield infinite physical quantities. Renormalization absorbs these divergences into observable mass and charge by rescaling parameters, giving finite and precise predictions. It made quantum electrodynamics one of the most accurate theories ever.

自旋真的是粒子绕自身轴的旋转吗？

Is spin really a particle rotating about its own axis?

自旋是一种内禀的角动量自由度，并不对应经典意义上的自转。它的值和单位（如ħ/2）无法用宏观旋转类比，却严格服从角动量的量子代数。它是相对论性量子场论的内在要求，而非几何旋转。
Spin is an intrinsic angular-momentum degree of freedom, not a classical spinning in the literal sense. Its values and units (such as ħ/2) have no macroscopic rotational analog yet obey the quantum algebra of angular momentum exactly. It is required intrinsically by relativistic quantum field theory, not by geometric rotation.

卡西米尔效应如何体现真空能量？

How does the Casimir effect manifest vacuum energy?

当两块不带电的金属板靠得极近时，板间只能容纳特定波长的真空电磁涨落，而板外涨落更多，于是产生把两板推向一起的微弱压力。这被视为真空零点能真实存在的证据。它虽小，却是可测量的量子场效应。
When two uncharged metal plates are brought extremely close, only certain vacuum electromagnetic fluctuations fit between them, while more fluctuations remain outside, producing a tiny pressure pulling the plates together. This is taken as evidence that vacuum zero-point energy is real. Small but measurable, it is a quantum field effect.
