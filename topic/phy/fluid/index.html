伯努利原理说的是什么？

What does Bernoulli's principle state?

在流速快的流体中压强较低，流速慢处压强较高。这一压强差能解释升力、喷雾器和飞机机翼等现象。
In a flowing fluid, faster speed means lower pressure, and slower speed means higher pressure. The pressure difference explains lift, atomizers, and more.

潜水员为什么会上“减压病”？

Why do divers get decompression sickness (the bends)?

深水下高压让更多氮气溶进血液；若上升太快，压力骤降使氮气析出成气泡堵住血管，就会关节疼痛甚至危险。
At depth, high pressure dissolves more nitrogen in blood; rising too fast drops pressure so the gas forms bubbles that block vessels, causing pain or danger.

为什么番茄酱有时候倒不出来，摇一摇又出来了？

Why is ketchup sometimes hard to pour but flows after shaking?

番茄酱是非牛顿流体，静止时黏稠像固体，受到冲击或晃动时变稀，所以摇一摇就容易倒出来。
Ketchup is a non-Newtonian fluid: it stays thick like a solid at rest but thins under shaking or impact, so it pours after you agitate it.

钢铁造的大船为什么能浮在水上？

Why does a steel ship float though steel sinks?

船体是空心的大肚子，排开的水很重。只要船和货物总重不超过排开水的重量，浮力就能把它托住（阿基米德原理）。
A hollow hull displaces a huge amount of water; as long as the ship's total weight is less than that displaced water, buoyancy holds it up (Archimedes' principle).

用吸管喝饮料，水是怎么跑到嘴里的？

How does liquid get into your mouth through a straw?

你吸的时候把吸管里的空气吸走，里面气压变小，外面的大气压就把饮料压进吸管、送到嘴里。
Sucking removes air from the straw, lowering its pressure; the higher outside air pressure then pushes the drink up and into your mouth.
