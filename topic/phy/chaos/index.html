庞加莱截面为何能把混沌“化简”成离散图像？

Why can a Poincare section reduce chaos to a discrete picture?

在高维轨迹上取一张截面，只记录每次穿越的点，就把连续流变成点序列；混沌系统在此呈现分形状的奇异吸引子，规律一目了然。
Slicing a high-dimensional trajectory with a plane and recording crossing points turns continuous flow into points; chaos shows up as a fractal strange attractor, revealing order at a glance.

费根鲍姆常数刻画了哪种“通向混沌”的普适道路？

What universal route to chaos does the Feigenbaum constant describe?

在倍周期分岔序列中，相邻分岔参数间距比趋于一个普适常数 δ≈4.669，与具体方程无关，揭示不同混沌系统共享同一条通往混沌的“路线图”。
In period-doubling cascades the spacing ratio of bifurcation parameters approaches the universal Feigenbaum constant δ≈4.669, independent of the equation—different systems share one route to chaos.

三体问题为什么没有通用解析解？

Why is there no general solution to the three-body problem?

三个相互引力作用的物体形成非线性动力系统，对初值极度敏感且一般不可积，只能用数值方法近似，长期轨道无法用公式精确写出。
Three gravitating bodies form a non-linear, non-integrable system exquisitely sensitive to initial conditions, so only numerical approximations exist and no general closed-form solution.

混沌理论中的蝴蝶效应是什么？

What is the butterfly effect in chaos theory?

蝴蝶效应指在某些确定性系统中，初始条件的微小差异会随时间的推进被指数级放大，导致长期结果完全不可预测。它说明确定性不等于可长期预测。天气就是典型的混沌系统例子。
The butterfly effect means that in some deterministic systems, tiny differences in initial conditions are amplified exponentially over time, making long-term outcomes unpredictable. It shows determinism does not equal long-term predictability. Weather is a classic example of a chaotic system.

蝴蝶扇翅膀真能引发台风吗？

Can a butterfly flapping its wings really cause a typhoon?

这是“混沌”的形象说法：某些系统对开头极敏感，小差别会被放大，长期结果很难精确预测。
It is a picture of chaos: some systems are so sensitive to their start that tiny differences grow and long forecasts fail.
