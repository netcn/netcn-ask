为什么保温瓶能长时间保温？

Why does a thermos keep drinks hot or cold for a long time?

保温瓶有双层真空壁，真空几乎不传导热，加上镀层反射辐射，所以内外热量很难交换，温度就保得住。
A thermos has a double wall with a vacuum between layers; the vacuum blocks conduction and the reflective coating reduces radiation, so heat barely transfers in or out.

#保温 #真空 #热传导

保温杯的真空夹层挡住了哪些传热通道?镀银内壁又是在对付剩下哪一种散热方式?

Which heat-transfer channels does the vacuum gap of a thermos flask block, and what remaining loss mechanism do the silvered walls target?

双层壁之间的真空同时切断了空气对流和气体分子碰撞导热,这两条通常是日常容器最主要的散热途径。剩下的主要是红外辐射,而内外壁银膜的发射率极低,能把大部分热辐射反射回原侧。瓶口的软木或泡沫塞导热系数很小,再补上最后一条沿固体接触漏热的通道。
The vacuum between the double walls cuts off both air convection and gas-molecule conduction, normally the dominant losses from an everyday container. What remains is chiefly infrared radiation, and the low-emissivity silver films on both walls reflect most of it back to its own side. A cork or foam stopper at the mouth, with its small conductivity, closes the last path of leakage through solid contacts.

为什么黑夜比白天冷？

Why is night colder than day?

白天地面吸收阳光积热，夜里没有日照又向天空散热。
Ground stores daytime heat but radiates it away with no sun at night.
