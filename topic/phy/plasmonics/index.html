表面等离激元为何能突破衍射极限把光场压缩到纳米尺度?其传播距离受限的根本原因是什么?

Why can surface plasmon polaritons confine light below the diffraction limit, and what fundamentally limits their propagation length?

表面等离激元是金属表面自由电子集体振荡与光场耦合形成的电磁模,其色散曲线位于真空中光线右侧,对应更大的波矢,因此可以在垂直界面方向把场压缩到远小于自由空间波长的尺度。这种亚波长约束来自金属负介电常数导致的边界条件,而非任何超越衍射极限的传播。代价是电子振荡伴随欧姆损耗与朗道阻尼,波矢越大场越局域,金属内吸收越强,传播长度按1/Im(k)缩短,这是材料复介电常数虚部决定的内在权衡。
Surface plasmon polaritons are hybrid electromagnetic modes formed by coupling light to collective oscillations of free electrons at a metal surface. Their dispersion curve lies to the right of the light line in vacuum, implying larger wavevectors, so the field can be squeezed normal to the interface into dimensions far smaller than the free-space wavelength. This subwavelength confinement stems from the boundary conditions set by the metal's negative permittivity rather than from any evasion of diffraction during propagation. The price is Ohmic and Landau damping of the electron motion: the tighter the confinement, the larger Im(k) and the stronger absorption inside the metal, so the propagation length shrinks roughly as 1/Im(k)—an intrinsic trade-off dictated by the imaginary part of the dielectric function.

#表面等离激元 #欧姆损耗

推导并说明金属-电介质界面表面等离激元的色散关系及其导致的场束缚特性。

Derive the surface plasmon dispersion relation at a metal–dielectric interface and explain the field confinement it implies.

对界面处的 TM 表面波求解麦克斯韦方程组并要求场在两侧指数衰减,得到 k_sp = k₀√(ε_m ε_d/(ε_m + ε_d)),其中 ε_m、ε_d 分别为金属与介质的介电常数。当 ε_m < -ε_d 时 k_sp > k₀√ε_d,即面内波矢大于同介质中的光子动量,普通自由空间光无法直接激发,需棱镜耦合(Kretschmann 结构)或光栅补偿动量。由于垂直方向波矢为虚数,能量被压缩到界面附近约半个波长的区域内,这正是等离激元实现亚波长聚焦与传感增强的根源。
Solving Maxwell's equations for a TM surface wave at the interface and requiring evanescent decay on both sides gives k_sp = k₀√(ε_m ε_d/(ε_m + ε_d)), with ε_m and ε_d the permittivities of the metal and dielectric. When ε_m < -ε_d this exceeds the photon momentum k₀√ε_d in the adjacent medium, so free-space light cannot excite the mode directly; prism coupling (the Kretschmann geometry) or grating coupling supplies the missing momentum. Because the perpendicular wavevector is imaginary, the energy is squeezed into a region of roughly half a wavelength around the interface, which underlies plasmonic subwavelength focusing and sensing enhancement.

#表面等离激元 #色散关系

什么是表面等离激元?它是如何在金属与介质界面上形成的?

What is a surface plasmon, and how does it form at the interface between a metal and a dielectric?

表面等离激元是沿金属-介质界面传播的集体电子振荡波,其电磁场在界面法线方向呈指数衰减,能量被高度束缚在亚波长尺度内。它的色散关系由金属和介质的介电函数共同决定,只有当两者实部符号相反且满足特定匹配条件时才能存在。这种强场束缚使表面等离激元能够突破衍射极限,实现纳米尺度的光场操控。
A surface plasmon is a collective electron oscillation that propagates along a metal-dielectric interface, with its electromagnetic field decaying exponentially normal to the surface and confined to subwavelength dimensions. Its dispersion relation depends on the dielectric functions of both media and exists only when their real parts have opposite signs under specific phase-matching conditions. This strong field confinement allows surface plasmons to beat the diffraction limit and manipulate light at the nanoscale.
