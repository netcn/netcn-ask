为什么轻轻吹过瓶口会发声，而用力吹过细缝边缘会“尖叫”（边棱音）？

Why does a gentle blow across a bottle sing, while a hard blow over a thin edge 'screams' (edge tone)?

气流被边缘反复切割，形成“喷流一会儿偏左、一会儿偏右”的振荡，像哨子一样周期性拍打空气发声。吹得越急，振荡频率越高，声音就越尖。这就是边棱音（管乐器很多靠它起振）。
The jet is sliced by the edge and flips side to side periodically, slapping the air like a whistle. The harder the blow, the higher the oscillation frequency and the shriller the tone — the edge tone that drives many wind instruments.

为什么轻轻拨动一端固定的绳子，只有在特定频率才会“站”出明显的波形（驻波）？

Why does plucking a string fixed at one end show a clear standing wave only at certain frequencies?

波在固定端会反射回来，和入射波叠加。只有当绳长正好是半波长的整数倍时，反射波和原波处处同相、互相加强，才形成不传播的“驻波”；别频率下正反相抵消，看不出形状。
The wave reflects at the fixed end and overlaps the incoming one. Only when the length is an integer multiple of a half-wavelength do they reinforce everywhere, forming a non-traveling standing wave; other frequencies cancel out and show no clear shape.

洗衣机甩干时衣服贴在桶壁上，为什么不会掉下来？

During spin-dry, why do clothes stay stuck to the drum instead of falling?

桶高速旋转，衣服要沿直线飞出去，却被桶壁挡住，于是被“离心”地压在壁上。需要的向内的力由桶壁提供，所以衣服贴着壁一起转，水却被甩出去。
Spinning fast, the clothes want to fly straight out but the drum wall holds them in, pressing them against it by 'centrifugal' effect. The wall supplies the inward force, so clothes ride the wall while water is flung out through the holes.

为什么旋转的雨伞会把水甩成一圈，而不是直直落下？

Why does a spinning umbrella fling water in a circle instead of straight down?

伞转起来，水珠随伞获得切向速度；离开伞边后它想沿切线飞出去，重力再把它往下拉，合起来就是斜着甩出的弧线，看起来像甩成一圈。
A spinning umbrella gives droplets tangential speed; once they leave the rim they want to fly off along the tangent while gravity pulls them down, combining into an arcing fling — looking like a spray ring.

#旋转 #切向 #惯性

陀螺高速旋转时，外力想让它倒，它却“拐弯”着转，这叫什么效应？

When a fast-spinning top is pushed to fall, it instead 'veers' around — what effect is this?

这叫“进动”。自旋的陀螺有角动量，外力的力矩不直接把它放倒，而是让角动量方向慢慢绕圈转，于是陀螺不是倒下，而是歪着画圈。
It's precession. A spinning top carries angular momentum; the external torque doesn't topple it directly but makes that momentum direction sweep around, so the top doesn't fall — it leans and circles.

#进动 #角动量 #陀螺

蝴蝶翅膀、蓝闪蝶、肥皂膜，为什么都能泛出不是颜料染出的彩虹色？

Why can butterfly wings, blue morphos and soap films show rainbow colors with no pigment?

它们靠的是“结构色”：表面有比光波长还细的层层结构，不同颜色的光在薄膜间互相加强或抵消，于是随角度看变出虹彩。这不是染料，是光和薄层“玩”出来的。
They use structural color: layers finer than a light wavelength make different colors reinforce or cancel by interference, shifting with viewing angle. It's not dye but light playing with thin structures.

泡泡为什么大多是圆的——表面张力怎么“挑选”形状？

Why are bubbles usually round — how does surface tension 'choose' the shape?

表面张力总想让“皮”的面积尽量小，同样多的气，球形表面积最小、最省能量。所以没外力干扰时，泡泡会自然缩成圆球。
Surface tension minimizes the film's area; for a fixed amount of gas the sphere has the smallest area and lowest energy. So undisturbed, a bubble naturally pulls itself into a round ball.

#表面张力 #球形 #最小面积

为什么对着空瓶口吹气，会“呜呜”响？

Why does blowing across an empty bottle's mouth make it 'hum'?

气流在瓶口被边缘切成一小股一股，在瓶里撞来撞去形成空气振动，瓶子里的空气柱就像一根看不见的弹簧，按自己的频率嗡嗡响。
The airstream is sliced by the rim into puffs that bounce inside, making the air column vibrate like an invisible spring at its own frequency, humming away.

为什么往水里扔石头会冒出一串泡泡？

Why does tossing a stone into water send up a string of bubbles?

石头砸下去把空气一起卷进水里，又被水挤得浮上来，沿途变成一串小泡泡冒出水面，噗噗地跑掉。
The stone drags air down with it; water squeezes that air back up, and on the way it forms a trail of little bubbles that pop out at the surface.

#泡泡 #空气 #水

两个相同音叉离得近，敲响一个另一个也响，这是怎么“偷”到能量的？

Two identical tuning forks nearby — strike one and the other rings too. How does it 'steal' the energy?

敲响的音叉在空气中发出和它固有频率相同的声波，另一个音叉被这同频声波“推”得跟着振动，这叫共振。能量通过空气一波波传过去，就带动了它。
The struck fork sends sound at its natural frequency; the nearby identical fork is 'pushed' by those matching waves into vibrating too — resonance. Energy rides the air wave across and sets the other ringing.

肥皂泡为什么一戳就破，可两只泡泡碰到一起会合成一个？

Why does a soap bubble pop at a touch, yet two bubbles merge into one when they meet?

泡泡皮薄得像影子，手指带的小脏东西或干处一碰就破。两只泡泡相遇时膜连成一片，表面张力让它们缩成更省能量的一个大泡泡。
The film is as thin as a shadow and breaks where a finger's dirt or dryness touches it. When two bubbles meet, their films join and surface tension shrinks them into one bigger, lower-energy bubble.
