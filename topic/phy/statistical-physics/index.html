一维与二维伊辛模型在相变行为上为何截然不同，昂萨格解说明了什么？

Why do one- and two-dimensional Ising models behave so differently regarding phase transitions, and what does the Onsager solution show?

一维伊辛链在有限温度下因涨落总能破坏长程序，故无有限温度相变；二维伊辛模型在低温存在自发磁化，并在临界温度发生相变。昂萨格给出二维伊辛模型的精确自由能，首次严格证明相变并揭示临界指数。
A one-dimensional Ising chain has no finite-temperature transition because fluctuations always destroy long-range order, whereas the two-dimensional Ising model develops spontaneous magnetization below a critical temperature. Onsager's exact solution of the 2D model gave the first rigorous demonstration of a phase transition and its critical exponents.

临界现象的标度律与普适类如何解释不同系统在相变点表现出相同临界指数？

How do scaling laws and universality classes explain why very different systems show the same critical exponents at a phase transition?

在临界点附近关联长度发散，系统的宏观行为仅由维度、对称性与相互作用短程性决定，与微观细节无关，据此归入普适类。标度假设使各临界指数通过标度关系相互关联，因此铁磁、液气与合金等系统可共享同一组指数。
Near a critical point the correlation length diverges, so macroscopic behavior depends only on dimensionality, symmetry, and short-range interaction range rather than microscopic detail, grouping systems into universality classes. The scaling hypothesis links critical exponents through scaling relations, so magnets, fluids, and alloys can share the same exponents.

涨落-耗散定理如何把系统的平衡涨落与其对微小扰动的耗散响应联系起来？

How does the fluctuation-dissipation theorem relate equilibrium fluctuations of a system to its dissipative response to small perturbations?

该定理指出，处于热平衡的线性响应系数（如电导率、磁化率）等于平衡态下相应涨落量的时间关联函数。换言之，系统对微扰的耗散越强，其在平衡时对应的自发涨落也越大，二者由温度统一标定。
The theorem states that a linear-response coefficient (such as conductivity or susceptibility) in thermal equilibrium equals the time-correlation function of the corresponding equilibrium fluctuation. In other words, stronger dissipative response to a perturbation implies larger spontaneous equilibrium fluctuations, both scaled by temperature.

对称性自发破缺在凝聚态与粒子物理中扮演什么共同角色？

What common role does spontaneous symmetry breaking play in condensed matter and particle physics?

当系统的基态对称性低于其动力学规律时即发生自发对称破缺，会产生无质量（或近无质量）的戈德斯通模，并为有序相提供序参量。在粒子物理中希格斯机制正是规范对称的自发破缺，赋予规范玻色子质量。
Spontaneous symmetry breaking occurs when the ground state has lower symmetry than the governing dynamics, producing massless (or nearly massless) Goldstone modes and an order parameter for the ordered phase. In particle physics the Higgs mechanism is precisely spontaneous breaking of a gauge symmetry that gives gauge bosons mass.

居里-外斯定律如何描述铁磁体在居里温度以上的磁化率行为？

How does the Curie-Weiss law describe the magnetic susceptibility of a ferromagnet above its Curie temperature?

在居里温度 T_c 以上，顺磁相的磁化率满足 χ = C/(T - T_c)，其中 C 为居里常数。这反映出短程铁磁关联使磁化率在接近 T_c 时发散，是二级相变的标度前兆。
Above the Curie temperature T_c the paramagnetic susceptibility follows χ = C/(T - T_c), with Curie constant C. This reflects short-range ferromagnetic correlations driving the susceptibility to diverge near T_c, a precursor of the second-order transition.

爱因斯坦1905年对布朗运动的理论如何为分子动理论提供了决定性证据？

How did Einstein's 1905 theory of Brownian motion provide decisive evidence for the kinetic theory of molecules?

爱因斯坦将悬浮微粒的无规运动归结为周围分子的随机碰撞，导出均方位移与时间的线性关系 ⟨x²⟩ = 2Dt。结合斯托克斯阻力可估算阿伏伽德罗常数，首次把不可见分子的统计量转化为可观测的宏观运动。
Einstein attributed the random motion of suspended particles to random molecular collisions and derived the linear relation ⟨x²⟩ = 2Dt between mean-square displacement and time. Combined with Stokes drag this let him estimate Avogadro's number, linking unobservable molecular statistics to measurable macroscopic motion.

玻尔兹曼分布描述了平衡态下粒子占据各能级的概率，其指数形式意味着什么？

The Boltzmann distribution gives the probability that particles occupy energy levels at equilibrium; what does its exponential form imply?

在温度 T 下，处于能级 E 的概率正比于 exp(-E/k_B T)，即能量越高的态被占据的概率按指数衰减。这反映了系统趋向最低能量与热涨落之间的竞争，是统计物理中解释热平衡分布的核心。
At temperature T the probability of occupying energy level E is proportional to exp(-E/k_B T), so higher-energy states are exponentially less populated. This reflects the competition between minimizing energy and thermal fluctuations and is central to equilibrium statistical physics.

朗道二级相变理论如何用对称破缺和序参量描述相变？

How does Landau's theory of second-order phase transitions describe transitions via symmetry breaking and an order parameter?

朗道理论把自由能展开为序参量 η 的幂级数，高温对称相取 η=0 为极小，低温相在 η≠0 处出现对称破缺的新极小。相变点附近序参量与比热随温度呈现特征奇异性，由此统一刻画二级相变。
Landau theory expands the free energy as a power series in an order parameter η; the high-temperature symmetric phase has a minimum at η=0, while the low-temperature phase develops a symmetry-broken minimum at η≠0. Near the transition the order parameter and specific heat show characteristic singularities, unifying second-order transitions.
