国际单位制为什么一而再地重写基本单位？

Why is the SI system repeatedly redefining its base units?

实物标准会磨损、会漂移，而自然常数（光速、普朗克常数、基本电荷等）永远不变。把单位锚定常数，任何实验室都能复现，最稳最公平。
Physical artifacts wear and drift, but natural constants (speed of light, Planck's constant, elementary charge) never change. Anchoring units to them lets any lab reproduce them exactly.

“千克”曾经靠一块铂铱合金圆柱体定义，后来为什么改了？

The kilogram was once a platinum-iridium cylinder; why was that changed?

那块“国际千克原器”会悄悄变重或变轻，难以保证永恒准确。2019年起千克改由普朗克常数和天平（基布尔秤）定义。
The international prototype could slowly gain or lose mass, so it was not eternally exact. Since 2019 the kilogram is defined via Planck's constant and a Kibble balance.

食物热量常用“卡路里”，科学上为什么更爱用“焦耳”？

Food energy uses calories, but why do scientists prefer joules?

焦耳直接来自力和位移（1焦耳=1牛·米），和其他能量单位统一；卡路里只是习惯保留在营养标签上。
The joule follows directly from force times distance and unifies with other energies; the calorie survives mainly on nutrition labels by habit.

“秒”现在为什么用铯原子来定义，而不再看地球转一圈？

Why is the second now defined by a cesium atom rather than Earth's rotation?

地球自转有快慢波动，不够稳；铯-133在两个能级间跃迁的频率极其固定，用它计时要准几亿倍。
Earth's spin wobbles and is not steady; the cesium-133 hyperfine transition has an extremely fixed frequency, making atomic time billions of times more accurate.

古人用“尺”和“步”量长短，为什么现代全世界统一用“米”？

Why did the world switch from rulers' feet and paces to the meter?

各地的“尺”长短不一，做生意、造桥容易乱。统一用米这样的标准，交流才不出错。
Local 'feet' differed in length, causing confusion in trade and building; a shared standard like the meter prevents mistakes.

“光年”是时间单位还是距离单位？

Is a light-year a unit of time or of distance?

是距离单位，指光在一年里跑的路程，约9.46万亿公里。名字带“年”只是因为它用时间算出来。
It is a distance: how far light travels in one year, about 9.46 trillion km. The 'year' is just because time is used to compute it.

温度为什么有摄氏、华氏、开尔文三种常见“尺子”？

Why are there three common temperature scales: Celsius, Fahrenheit, and Kelvin?

它们零点和刻度不同：摄氏以水的冰点和沸点为0和100，华氏以盐冰混合物和体温为基准，开尔文以“绝对零度”为0，方便不同用途。
They set zero and steps differently: Celsius places 0 °C at water's freezing point and 100 °C at its boiling point, Fahrenheit used a brine mix and body temperature, and Kelvin starts at absolute zero for science.

发动机功率常说“马力”，马真的有那么大的力气吗？

Engine power is given in 'horsepower'; is a horse really that strong?

马力是瓦特当年为推销蒸汽机、拿马和驴出力做比较而定的单位，1马力约746瓦，是平均值而非某匹马的最大值。
Horsepower was James Watt's marketing comparison of steam engines with horses; one horsepower is about 746 W, an average, not a single horse's peak.
