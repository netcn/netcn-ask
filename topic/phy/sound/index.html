乐器怎么发出不同的声音？

How do instruments make different sounds?

它们用不同办法让空气或琴弦抖动，声音就有高有低、有清有闷。
They shake air or strings in different ways, making sounds that are high, low, bright, or soft.

敲鼓怎么会响？

Why does a drum make a sound when we hit it?

鼓面被敲得快速抖动，带着空气一起抖，就发出了声音。
The drum skin shakes fast when hit and makes the air shake too, creating sound.

Why do we hear echoes in a valley?

声音撞到大山又弹回来，我们就好像又听了一遍。
Sound hits the mountain and bounces back, so we seem to hear it one more time.

Why can we hear sounds?

耳朵里有一层小鼓膜，声音一来它就抖动，把消息传给大脑。
Your ear has a tiny eardrum that shakes when sound arrives and sends the message to your brain.

蝙蝠怎么靠回声找路？

How do bats use echoes to find their way?

蝙蝠发出叫声，声音撞到东西弹回来，它听回音就知道前面有什么。
Bats send out calls that bounce off objects, and by listening to the echo they learn what is ahead.

声音跑得有多快？

How fast does sound travel?

声音在空气中大约每秒走340米，比光慢很多，所以远处我们先看见再听见。
Sound travels about 340 meters per second in air, far slower than light, so from far away we see before we hear.

Sound waves shake the eardrum and tiny ear bones, which turn the motion into nerve signals sent to the brain.
