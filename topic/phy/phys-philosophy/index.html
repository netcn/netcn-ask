为什么说对称性和守恒律是同一件事的两面？

Why are symmetry and conservation laws two sides of the same coin?

诺特定理说：时间平移对称对应能量守恒，空间平移对称对应动量守恒，旋转对称对应角动量守恒。一种对称就“生出”一条守恒律。
Noether's theorem links them: time-translation symmetry gives energy conservation, space-translation gives momentum, rotation gives angular momentum—each symmetry breeds a conservation law.

#诺特 #对称 #守恒

为什么我们既不能精确知道粒子的位置、又不能同时知道它的速度？

Why can't we know both a particle's position and its speed precisely?

海森堡不确定性原理说，位置和动量的不确定度乘积有个下限。这不是仪器太差，而是自然界本身的“模糊”，被测量得越准，另一个就越糊。
Heisenberg's uncertainty principle sets a floor on the product of position and momentum spreads; it is nature's fuzziness, not just bad instruments—sharpen one and the other blurs.

#不确定性 #测量 #量子

物理定律在时间上大多可逆，为什么现实却总走向更乱？

Most physics laws are time-reversible, so why does reality always get messier?

单个分子的运动可逆，但大量粒子的统计规律让“有序”态远少于“混乱”态，系统几乎必然走向高熵，这就是时间之箭。
Individual motions reverse, but statistically ordered states are far rarer than messy ones, so systems almost surely move to higher entropy—the arrow of time.

#熵 #时间 #不可逆

宇宙诞生时极微小的密度起伏，怎么长成了今天的星系和宇宙网？

How did tiny early density ripples grow into today's galaxies and cosmic web?

引力是不稳定的：稍密的地方引力稍强，吸引更多物质，起伏被越放越大（金斯不稳定性），最终塌成星系和星系团。
Gravity is unstable: slightly denser regions pull a bit harder, gathering more matter and amplifying the ripple (Jeans instability) until galaxies and clusters form.

#引力 #不稳定性 #宇宙

在相对论和量子论里，“力”这个概念为什么变模糊了？

Why does the idea of 'force' get blurry in relativity and quantum theory?

广义相对论里引力是时空弯曲，不是传统推力；量子论里相互作用靠交换粒子（如光子）实现。力更像“几何”和“交换”，不再是简单的拉与推。
In general relativity gravity is spacetime curvature, not a push; in quantum theory interactions happen by exchanging particles like photons. Force becomes geometry and exchange rather than simple pull and shove.

#力 #相对论 #量子

真空真的是“什么都没有”吗？

Is a vacuum truly 'nothing at all'?

不是。量子场论里真空充满涨落：粒子和反粒子不断成对出现又消失，还能产生可测的卡西米尔力。
No. In quantum field theory the vacuum seethes with fluctuations—particle pairs blink in and out, producing measurable effects like the Casimir force.

#真空 #涨落 #量子

测量这个动作本身，为什么会改变被看的量子系统？

Why does the act of measuring disturb the quantum system being watched?

在量子层面，测量必须让仪器和粒子相互作用，这一下就改变了粒子状态（观察者效应），所以“看”和“被看”分不开。
At the quantum level, measuring requires the instrument to interact with the particle, which changes its state (observer effect); seeing and being seen cannot be separated.

#观察者 #测量 #量子

为什么物理学家相信物理定律在宇宙各处、各时都一样？

Why do physicists believe laws are the same everywhere and everywhen?

这叫相对性原理（更广义叫对称性）。若定律随地随时变，科学预测就失去意义；迄今遥远星系的光谱都支持同一套规律。
Called the principle of relativity (a symmetry): if laws changed by place or time, prediction would be meaningless, and distant spectra so far obey the same rules.

物理学里“时间”到底怎么定义才严谨？

How can 'time' be defined rigorously in physics?

时间没有绝对定义：靠钟（周期性过程）来量；相对论里每个人有自己的“固有时”，热力学和宇宙膨胀又给时间定了方向。
Time has no absolute definition: we measure it by clocks (periodic processes); relativity gives each observer their own proper time, while thermodynamics and cosmic expansion give it a direction.
