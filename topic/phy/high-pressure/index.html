为什么高压能让本来绝缘的物质变成金属，甚至让氢变成“金属氢”？

Why can high pressure turn an insulator metallic — even turning hydrogen into 'metallic hydrogen'?

加压把原子挤得更近，电子的能带展宽、价带与导带交叠，电子就能自由流动，于是绝缘体变导体。理论预言极高压下氢分子会解离成共价金属网络，就是金属氢。
Pressure squeezes atoms closer, broadening bands until the valence and conduction bands overlap so electrons flow freely — an insulator becomes a metal. Theory predicts that extreme pressure dissociates hydrogen molecules into a metallic covalent network: metallic hydrogen.

为什么地心压力能把铁压成“固态”内核，尽管那里的温度比铁的熔点还高？

Why does Earth's core pressure keep iron solid despite temperatures above iron's melting point?

熔点本身会随压强升高而升高。地心约 360 万大气压把铁的熔点抬到比实际温度还高，于是铁在“本该熔化”的条件下反而被压成了固态内核。
The melting point rises with pressure. At Earth's core (~3.6 million atm) the melting point of iron is pushed above the actual temperature, so iron is squeezed into a solid inner core even though it would melt at that heat under low pressure.

钻石压腔（金刚石对顶砧）怎么用两颗小钻石造出地心级别的压力？

How does a diamond anvil cell use two tiny diamonds to reach Earth's-core pressures?

把样品夹在两颗金刚石的尖之间，用螺旋使劲往里压。因为钻石极硬又顶尖很小，力集中到极小面积上，压强就能飙到上百万个大气压，足以模拟地心。
A sample is squeezed between the tips of two diamonds and pressed hard. Diamond is ultra-hard and the tips are tiny, so force concentrates on a minuscule area, driving pressure to millions of atmospheres — enough to mimic Earth's core.

潜水越深耳朵越痛，每下潜大约 10 米增加多少大气压？

The deeper you dive, the more your ears hurt — about how much pressure per 10 meters down?

每下潜约 10 米，水柱就多压一个大气压，所以 10 米深约 2 个大气压（含原本的 1 个），30 米深就约 4 个。耳朵里的气囊被压扁，就会痛。
Roughly every 10 meters of water adds one atmosphere, so at 10 m you feel about 2 atm (including the 1 from air), and 4 atm at 30 m. The air spaces in your ears get squeezed, which hurts.
