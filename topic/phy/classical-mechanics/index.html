What is the Coriolis force, and why does it deflect large-scale airflow to the right in the Northern Hemisphere?

科里奥利力是地球自转带来的惯性效应，只在旋转参考系中“显现”。在北半球运动的物体相对地轴向东的分量会被甩向外，表现为向右偏转；南半球则相反。
The Coriolis force is an inertial effect of Earth's rotation, appearing only in the rotating frame. In the Northern Hemisphere, moving objects are deflected to the right; in the Southern Hemisphere, to the left.

单簧管和长笛吹出同一个音，为什么音色完全不同？

Why do a clarinet and a flute sound completely different when playing the same note?

音色由泛音的成分决定。管内的驻波可以同时存在多个模式：基频决定音高，而频率为基频整数倍的泛音各不相同。长笛两端开口，能支持全部谐波；单簧管一端封闭一端开口，由于边界条件限制只能保留奇次谐波，而且各泛音的强弱分布也不同。这些谐波按不同比例叠加出各自独特的波形，人耳便听出迥异的音色。
Timbre is set by the mix of overtones. Standing waves in a tube coexist in many modes: the fundamental fixes the pitch while harmonics at integer multiples of it differ between instruments. A flute, open at both ends, supports every harmonic; a clarinet, closed at one end, keeps mainly odd ones because of its boundary conditions, and the harmonic strengths differ too. These distinct harmonic blends shape each instrument's waveform, which the ear hears as different timbre.

旋转的足球踢出去后，为什么飞行轨迹会拐弯？

Why does a spinning football bend through the air instead of flying straight?

旋转的球会带动紧贴表面的空气一起转。一侧气流与旋转方向相同而加速、压强降低，另一侧被减速、压强升高，两侧的压差产生一个垂直于飞行方向的侧向力，使球弧线飞行，这就是马格努斯效应。更精确的机制是旋转改变了边界层与气流分离点的位置，伯努利关系只能给出直观图像。
A spinning ball drags a thin layer of air with it. On one side the airflow joins the rotation and speeds up, lowering pressure; on the other side it slows and pressure rises, and the net pressure difference makes a sideways force that curves the ball — the Magnus effect. The fuller mechanism involves the spin shifting the boundary layer and the separation point; Bernoulli gives only a handy intuition.

音叉的声音为什么纯净，而钢琴和小提琴弹同一个音却听得出区别？

Why does a tuning fork sound pure while piano and violin playing the same note sound different?

音叉的两个叉臂对称振动，高次谐波被强烈抑制，主要发出一个基频，所以音色纯净。琴弦或共鸣腔则同时振动出基频和许多整数倍频率的泛音，不同乐器泛音的强弱分布不同，构成各自独特的音色。耳朵对频率比例非常敏感，正是泛音比例让我们分清同音高来自哪种乐器。
A tuning fork's symmetric prongs suppress higher harmonics, so it emits almost pure fundamental and sounds clean. Strings and resonating boxes vibrate at the fundamental plus many integer-multiple overtones, and each instrument weights them differently, which is its timbre. Our ears are sensitive to frequency ratios, and those overtone patterns are what let us tell a piano from a violin at the same pitch.

分析力学为什么能用“广义坐标”来描述一个复杂系统的运动，而不必死盯着每个质点的位置？

Why can analytical mechanics describe a complicated system with 'generalized coordinates' instead of tracking every particle's position?

很多约束（如杆长固定、轮子只滚动）会减少系统的真实自由度，用贴合约束的坐标（角度、弧长等）就能完整描述运动。拉格朗日方程正是基于广义坐标和能量，把牛顿力学的繁复约束化简。这样多体问题往往变得清晰许多。
Many constraints—fixed rod length, pure rolling, and so on—reduce a system's true degrees of freedom, and coordinates that fit those constraints (angles, arc lengths) fully describe the motion. Lagrange's equations build on generalized coordinates and energy to remove the messy constraint forces of Newton's approach. Multi-body problems then become far clearer.

物理学中的「最小作用量原理」说的是什么？

What does the principle of least action in physics state?

它说一个力学系统从初态到末态的真实路径，是使「作用量」（拉格朗日量对时间的积分）取驻值的那一条。这把牛顿三定律浓缩成一个变分原理，并可推广到电磁场、广义相对论甚至量子力学（路径积分）。哈密顿原理即作用量的一阶变分为零。
It says the actual path a system takes between two states is the one that makes the 'action' (the time integral of the Lagrangian) stationary. This condenses Newton's laws into a single variational principle and extends to fields, general relativity, and quantum mechanics (path integrals). Hamilton's principle states that the first variation of the action vanishes.

双摆只有两个摆锤，运动为什么却难以预测？

A double pendulum has only two bobs — why is its motion so hard to predict?

双摆的运动方程是非线性的，它是最经典的混沌系统之一：两个初始角度哪怕只差一丝一毫，之后的轨迹也会以指数速度分道扬镳。但每个时刻的受力与运动都严格服从牛顿定律，系统本质上是确定性的——确定性和不可预测并不矛盾，因为结果对初始条件极其敏感。混沌不是无规律，而是初值敏感的有规律。
The double pendulum's equations are nonlinear, making it a classic chaotic system: two starts differing by the tiniest amount diverge exponentially in their trajectories. Yet every instant obeys Newton's laws, so the system is deterministic — determinism and unpredictability coexist because the outcome is hypersensitive to initial values. Chaos is not randomness; it is law-governed motion that is dramatically sensitive to where you start.

为什么血管只要稍微变窄，血流阻力就会成倍增加？

Why does even a slight narrowing of a blood vessel sharply increase flow resistance?

层流条件下，圆管流动的阻力与管径的四次方成反比（泊肃叶定律）：半径缩小一半，阻力变成原来的16倍。所以动脉粥样硬化造成的轻度狭窄，就可能让供血明显下降。血管通过舒张、收缩来调节阻力，全身血压的调配也依赖这一规律。
For laminar flow, resistance through a tube scales inversely with the fourth power of the radius (Poiseuille's law): halve the radius and resistance jumps 16-fold. That is why even mild narrowing from atherosclerosis can cut blood supply seriously. Vessels regulate flow by dilating and constricting, and the body's blood-pressure control leans on the same law.

刚体旋转的转动惯量如何计算？

How is the moment of inertia of a rigid body calculated?

转动惯量是刚体绕某轴旋转时惯性的度量，定义为各质元质量与其到轴距离平方的乘积之和 I=∫r²dm。它依赖于质量分布和转轴位置，而非只取决于总质量。同一物体对不同轴的转动惯量可以很不相同。
The moment of inertia measures a rigid body's inertia about a rotation axis, defined as the sum of each mass element times the square of its distance to the axis, I=∫r²dm. It depends on mass distribution and the axis location, not merely total mass. The same body can have very different moments about different axes.

陀螺快倒时，为什么转轴不是直接倒下去，而是绕竖直方向打转？

When a top starts to tilt, why does its axis sweep around the vertical instead of simply falling?

重力产生的力矩方向与角动量垂直，而不是与它平行。力矩的作用是改变角动量的方向而非大小，于是角动量矢量不断绕竖直方向转动，这个过程叫进动。自转越快，进动越慢，所以陀螺转得越快、倒下得越慢。同样的物理机制也解释了地球自转轴的岁差：太阳和月球的引力使地轴大约每26000年扫出一个圆锥。
Gravity's torque acts perpendicular to the angular momentum, not along it. Torque changes the direction of angular momentum rather than its magnitude, so the angular momentum vector keeps rotating around the vertical — that is precession. The faster the spin, the slower the precession, which is why a fast top topples slowly. The same physics drives Earth's axial precession: solar and lunar gravity make the pole sweep a cone roughly every 26,000 years.

同样质量的两根杆，为什么长的那根更难转动？

Why is a longer rod of the same mass harder to spin about its center?

因为转动惯量不仅取决于质量大小，还取决于质量相对转轴的分布。转动惯量等于每个质量元乘以它到转轴距离的平方再求和，即I等于Σmr²。质量离轴越远，对转动惯量的贡献按距离平方放大，所以同样质量的长杆转动惯量大得多。要让长杆获得相同的角加速度，就需要更大的力矩，这就是它"转起来费劲"的原因。
Moment of inertia reflects not just how much mass there is but how it is arranged around the axis. It sums each mass element times the square of its distance from the axis, I = Σmr². Mass farther out counts quadratically more, so a longer rod of equal mass has a far larger moment of inertia. Giving it the same angular acceleration then demands more torque, which is why it feels harder to spin.

为什么弹簧振子的往复运动被称为"简谐运动"？

Why is the back-and-forth motion of a mass on a spring called simple harmonic motion?

The spring's force is proportional to the displacement and always points back to equilibrium: F = −kx. Under such a linear restoring force the displacement follows a sine wave at a single frequency, which is why the motion is called "simple harmonic." Its period depends only on the mass and spring constant, never the amplitude, which is the basis for stable mechanical timekeeping.
