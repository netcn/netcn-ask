位移电流为什么是麦克斯韦方程中必需的？

Why is the displacement current necessary in Maxwell's equations?

在含电容的电路中，导线有电流而极板间无实电荷流动，安培定律若只算传导电流会不自洽。麦克斯韦引入由变化电场构成的位移电流，使定律在任意回路都成立，并由此预言了电磁波。
In circuits with capacitors, current flows in the wire but no real charge moves between plates, so Ampère's law with conduction current alone is inconsistent. Maxwell introduced the displacement current made of a changing electric field, making the law valid for any loop and thereby predicting electromagnetic waves.

坡印廷矢量如何描述电磁能量的流动？

How does the Poynting vector describe electromagnetic energy flow?

坡印廷矢量 S=E×B/μ₀ 表示单位面积、单位时间流过的电磁能量，方向即能量传播方向。它把场携带能量这一观念量化。例如导线外的电磁场才真正把能量从电源输送到负载。
The Poynting vector S=E×B/μ₀ gives the electromagnetic energy crossing a unit area per unit time, with direction along energy flow. It quantifies the idea that fields carry energy. For example, it is the field outside a wire that actually carries energy from source to load.

麦克斯韦方程组说明了什么？

What do Maxwell's equations tell us?

麦克斯韦方程组用四个方程统一描述电场、磁场及其与电荷、电流的关系，并预言了电磁波的存在。它们把电、磁、光统一在同一理论框架下。这是经典物理最伟大的综合之一。
Maxwell's equations use four equations to describe electric and magnetic fields and their relation to charges and currents, and they predict the existence of electromagnetic waves. They unify electricity, magnetism, and light in one framework. It is among the greatest syntheses of classical physics.

电磁学中的规范自由度意味着什么？

What does gauge freedom mean in electromagnetism?

电磁场的电势和磁矢势可以整体加上一个任意标量函数的梯度而不改变可观测的电场和磁场，这种冗余就是规范自由度。它看似多余，却深刻反映了电磁相互作用的对称性结构。现代物理把规范对称性视为力的来源。
The electric and magnetic potentials can be shifted by the gradient of an arbitrary scalar function without changing the observable fields; this redundancy is gauge freedom. It seems superfluous yet reflects the symmetry structure of electromagnetism deeply. Modern physics views gauge symmetry as the very origin of forces.

推迟势如何体现电磁作用的有限传播速度？

How do retarded potentials reflect the finite propagation speed of electromagnetism?

电荷的影响并非瞬时到达远处，而是以光速传播的。推迟势给出的场在时刻t取决于源在较早时刻 t−r/c 的状态，其中r是距离。这明确表达了因果以光速传播，也保证了理论符合相对论。
A charge's influence does not arrive instantly but propagates at light speed. Retarded potentials give fields at time t depending on the source's state at the earlier time t−r/c, where r is the distance. This explicitly expresses causality travels at light speed and keeps the theory consistent with relativity.
