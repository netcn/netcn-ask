量子自旋霍尔效应与整数量子霍尔效应在拓扑保护机制上有何根本区别？

How does the quantum spin Hall effect fundamentally differ from the integer quantum Hall effect in its topological protection?

整数量子霍尔效应依赖外磁场破缺时间反演对称，边缘态为手性通道；量子自旋霍尔效应在无外磁场下由时间反演对称保护，每个边缘存在一对手性相反、自旋锁定的螺旋边缘态。后者属于Z2拓扑相，对背散射免疫源于自旋-动量锁定。
The integer quantum Hall effect relies on an external magnetic field breaking time-reversal symmetry and yields chiral edge channels, whereas the quantum spin Hall effect is protected by time-reversal symmetry without a field and hosts a pair of counter-propagating, spin-locked helical edge modes. Its Z2 topology makes backscattering forbidden by spin-momentum locking.

BCS理论如何用电子-声子相互作用解释常规超导体的零电阻与迈斯纳效应？

How does BCS theory explain zero resistance and the Meissner effect in conventional superconductors via electron-phonon interaction?

BCS理论指出，费米面附近的两个电子通过交换虚声子产生弱的等效吸引，结成动量相反、自旋相反的库珀对。大量库珀对在玻色凝聚下形成宏观相干态，其能隙禁止单粒子激发，从而产生无耗散的超流电流与完全抗磁性。
BCS theory shows that two electrons near the Fermi surface experience an effective weak attraction by exchanging virtual phonons, forming Cooper pairs with opposite momentum and spin. The Bose-condensed pairs create a macroscopic coherent state with an energy gap that forbids single-particle excitations, yielding dissipationless current and perfect diamagnetism.

拓扑绝缘体为何在体能带绝缘的同时，表面却存在受拓扑保护的导电态？

Why does a topological insulator host conducting surface states protected by topology while its bulk remains insulating?

拓扑绝缘体的体能带具有非平庸的拓扑不变量（如Z2不变量），使得体-边对应要求边界上必然出现穿越能隙的狄拉克锥表面态。这些表面态受时间反演对称保护，对无序和相互作用具有鲁棒性，非磁性杂质无法使其局域化。
The bulk of a topological insulator carries a non-trivial topological invariant such as the Z2 invariant, and bulk-boundary correspondence forces gapless Dirac-cone surface states to appear at the boundary. Protected by time-reversal symmetry, these surface states are robust against disorder and non-magnetic perturbations.

拓扑序与朗道对称破缺序在刻画物质相的方式上有何不同？

How does topological order differ from Landau symmetry-breaking order in classifying phases of matter?

朗道范式用局域序参量与对称破缺来分类相变，而拓扑序不能用局域序参量描述，其特征为拓扑纠缠熵与任意子激发等整体量子性质。拓扑序在连续变形下保持不变，不对应任何对称性破缺。
The Landau paradigm classifies phases by local order parameters and symmetry breaking, whereas topological order cannot be captured by a local order parameter and is characterized by global properties such as topological entanglement entropy and anyonic excitations. Topological order persists under continuous deformation without symmetry breaking.

什么是库珀对？它为何能在金属中使两个带同号电荷的电子相互束缚？

What is a Cooper pair, and how can two same-charge electrons become bound in a metal?

库珀对是由两个动量相反、自旋相反的电子经电子-声子相互作用形成的束缚态。一个电子使晶格畸变，畸变吸引另一个电子，等效为吸引相互作用，从而克服库仑排斥形成配对。这种配对是超导微观起源。
A Cooper pair is a bound state of two electrons with opposite momentum and spin formed through electron-phonon interaction. One electron distorts the lattice, and the distortion attracts the other, producing an effective attraction that overcomes Coulomb repulsion. This pairing underlies superconductivity.

铜氧化物高温超导与常规BCS超导在配对机制和转变温度上有何主要差异？

What are the main differences in pairing mechanism and transition temperature between cuprate high-temperature superconductors and conventional BCS superconductors?

铜氧化物超导出现在反铁磁莫特绝缘体掺杂后，配对带有d波对称性且被认为源于电子强关联而非简单声子媒介。其临界温度可达液氮温区（约90至135 K），远高于常规超导体，但微观机制尚未完全解决。
Cuprate superconductivity emerges upon doping a Mott insulator and features d-wave pairing believed to arise from strong electron correlations rather than simple phonon mediation. Their critical temperatures reach the liquid-nitrogen range (about 90 to 135 K), far above conventional superconductors, though the full mechanism remains unresolved.

整数量子霍尔效应中，霍尔电导为何只能取整数的基本常数倍？

In the integer quantum Hall effect, why is the Hall conductance restricted to integer multiples of a fundamental constant?

在强磁场与低温下，二维电子气的朗道能级高度简并，边缘形成一维手性通道。霍尔电导为 σ_xy = ν e²/h，其中ν为填充因子整数，源于拓扑陈数与朗道能级的整体量子化。
Under strong magnetic fields and low temperatures, the two-dimensional electron gas forms highly degenerate Landau levels with one-dimensional chiral edge channels. The Hall conductance σ_xy = ν e²/h with integer filling factor ν arises from the topological Chern number of the Landau levels.

扭转双层石墨烯在「魔角」附近为何会出现关联绝缘体与非常规超导？

Why do twisted bilayer graphene near the magic angle exhibit correlated insulators and unconventional superconductivity?

当两层石墨烯扭转角接近约1.1度的魔角时，摩尔超晶格将费米速度压低、态密度显著增强，使电子间库仑相互作用相对于动能占主导。这导致平带中出现关联绝缘态，并在掺杂区域观察到类铜氧化物的非常规超导。
At twist angles near the magic angle of about 1.1 degrees, the moire superlattice flattens the bands and enhances the density of states, making electron-electron Coulomb interactions dominate over kinetic energy. This produces correlated insulating states and, upon doping, unconventional superconductivity reminiscent of cuprates.
