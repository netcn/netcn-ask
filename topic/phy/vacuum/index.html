“真空度”几帕和几纳帕差多少——为什么太空比冰箱里还空？

How big is the gap between 'a few pascals' and 'a few nanopascals' of vacuum — and why is space emptier than a fridge?

1 帕大约是大气压的十万分之一，1 纳帕又是它的十亿分之一，差出好多个数量级。冰箱里只是不太对流，其实空气满满；而近地太空每立方厘米可能只有几个原子，比冰箱“空”得多。
1 Pa is about one hundred-thousandth of atmospheric pressure, and 1 nPa is a billionth of that — many orders apart. A fridge just doesn't convect much but is full of air; near-Earth space may hold only a few atoms per cubic centimeter, far emptier.

保温瓶的夹层抽成真空，为什么就不容易传热了？

Why does a vacuum between a thermos's walls stop heat from getting through?

热要靠空气这样的“东西”来传导和对流。把夹层里的空气抽走变成真空，就没有介质帮忙传热了，再加上反光壁挡住辐射，热水就能保温很久。
Heat travels by conduction and convection through a medium like air. Suck the air out to make a vacuum and there's nothing to carry the heat; reflective walls also block radiation, so drinks stay hot long.
