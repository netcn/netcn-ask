规范对称性如何支配基本相互作用？

How do gauge symmetries govern the fundamental interactions?

要求局域规范对称性（不同地点可独立变换）会强制引入相应的规范场，这些场恰好对应自然界的作用力：光子、W/Z玻色子和胶子。因此电磁、弱、强相互作用都可统一在规范理论框架内。这是标准模型的核心思想。
Requiring local gauge symmetry (independent transformations at different points) forces the introduction of corresponding gauge fields, which turn out to be nature's forces: the photon, W/Z bosons, and gluons. Thus electromagnetism, the weak, and the strong interactions all fit a gauge-theory framework. This is the core idea of the Standard Model.

CKM 与 PMNS 矩阵分别描述什么“混合”？

What 'mixing' do the CKM and PMNS matrices describe?

CKM 矩阵描述夸克在上、下型之间的弱衰变混合（也是 CP 破坏来源之一）；PMNS 矩阵描述中微子味态与质量态的混合，是轻子扇区的混合参数。
The CKM matrix mixes quark flavors in weak decays (a source of CP violation); the PMNS matrix mixes neutrino flavor and mass states—mixing parameters of the quark and lepton sectors.

渐进自由为何让夸克在高能下反而“变自由”？

Why does asymptotic freedom make quarks freer at high energy?

强耦合常数随能量升高而减小（渐近自由），所以近距离或高能碰撞时夸克近乎自由；低能时耦合变大导致禁闭——同一理论两端行为迥异。
The strong coupling shrinks at high energy (asymptotic freedom), so quarks act nearly free in hard collisions; at low energy it grows and confines them—same theory, opposite regimes.

电荷、色荷、弱同位旋为何要“统一”起来想？

Why think of electric, color and weak charges as unified?

在更高能量下电磁与弱力合并为电弱力，理论进一步猜测三种力在某尺度统一；统一场试图用一个对称群把粒子和力收进同一框架。
At higher energy electromagnetism and the weak force merge into the electroweak force; theories further guess the three forces unify, seeking one symmetry group for particles and forces.

希格斯玻色子为什么被称为“质量的起源”？

Why is the Higgs boson called the origin of mass?

粒子在充满空间的希格斯场中运动时会受到“拖拽”，这种相互作用的强弱表现为质量。2012 年 LHC 找到的希格斯粒子正是该场的激发。
Particles moving through the all-pervading Higgs field feel a kind of drag whose strength shows up as mass. The Higgs boson found at the LHC in 2012 is a ripple of that field.

希格斯机制为何赋予W和Z玻色子质量，却让光子保持无质量？

Why does the Higgs mechanism give mass to the W and Z bosons while the photon stays massless?

电弱理论的规范对称性禁止直接写入矢量玻色子的质量项。希格斯场的真空期望值不为零，自发破缺了电弱对称性：W和Z玻色子通过与该场耦合获得质量（约80.4与91.2 GeV），而剩余的电磁U(1)对称性保持完好，对应的光子因而始终无质量。2012年ATLAS与CMS实验发现约125 GeV的希格斯玻色子，验证了该机制的核心预言。
Gauge symmetry in electroweak theory forbids writing explicit mass terms for vector bosons. The Higgs field takes a nonzero vacuum expectation value and spontaneously breaks the electroweak symmetry: the W and Z bosons acquire masses (about 80.4 and 91.2 GeV) by coupling to this field, while the residual electromagnetic U(1) symmetry stays intact, so the photon remains massless. In 2012 the ATLAS and CMS experiments found a Higgs boson near 125 GeV, confirming the mechanism's key prediction.

无论对撞机能量多高，实验都敲不出一颗孤立的"裸夸克"，这背后是什么物理机制？实验上又是如何间接确认夸克存在的？

No matter how high the collider energy, experiments never knock out an isolated bare quark — what physical mechanism lies behind this, and how is the existence of quarks confirmed indirectly?

量子色动力学中胶子自身携带色荷并相互吸引，色力线被压缩成连接两个夸克的"弦"，张力约为每飞米 1 GeV。想拉开的距离越远，弦内储存的能量越多；一旦超过产生新正反夸克对的阈值，弦便断裂成两颗普通强子，而不是放出一个自由夸克。因此高能碰撞中夸克只以喷注——方向高度关联的一束强子雨——的形式显露踪迹。
In quantum chromodynamics gluons themselves carry color charge and attract one another, squeezing the chromoelectric field into a narrow string joining two quarks, with a tension of about 1 GeV per femtometer. The farther apart you try to pull them, the more energy the string stores; once that exceeds the cost of creating a new quark-antiquark pair, the string snaps into two ordinary hadrons instead of releasing a free quark. In high-energy collisions quarks therefore show themselves only as jets — tightly collimated sprays of hadrons.

标准模型能解释世界上所有的力吗？

Can the Standard Model explain all the forces in the world?

它统一了电磁、弱、强三种力和已知的基本粒子，但还没有包括引力，也解释不了暗物质。
It unifies electromagnetism, the weak and strong forces and the known particles, but it leaves out gravity and dark matter.
