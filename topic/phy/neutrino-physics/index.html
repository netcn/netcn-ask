什么是太阳中微子的MSW效应?它如何解释高能与低能太阳中微子行为的差异?

What is the MSW effect in solar neutrino physics, and how does it explain why high-energy and low-energy solar neutrinos behave differently?

电子型中微子在日核稠密物质中与电子发生前向相干散射,附加势能改变了有效混合角与能级劈裂;物质项与真空项相消时出现共振,转化被大幅增强。高能硼8中微子产生于共振密度之上并绝热穿出,约七成转化为μ、τ味;低能pp中微子不经共振,只按真空振荡平均转化约四成。这一大混合角(LMA)图像后被KamLAND反应堆实验交叉证实。
In the dense solar core, electron neutrinos undergo coherent forward scattering off electrons; the added potential shifts the effective mixing angle and level splitting, and where the matter term cancels the vacuum term a resonance strongly enhances conversion. High-energy boron-8 neutrinos are created above the resonance and exit adiabatically, about seventy percent emerging as muon and tau flavors, while low-energy pp neutrinos never cross it and convert only partially, around forty percent on average, through vacuum oscillations. This large-mixing-angle picture was later confirmed by the reactor experiment KamLAND.

物理学家为什么要不惜代价寻找无中微子双贝塔衰变?它一旦现身将意味着什么?

Why are physicists going to such lengths to hunt for neutrinoless double beta decay, and what would its discovery mean?

因为它是检验中微子是否为自己反粒子的现实途径:一旦发现就证明轻子数不守恒、中微子是马约拉纳粒子,还能反推其绝对质量标度。普通双贝塔衰变由一对反中微子带走能量,而无中微子模式下两个虚中微子须直接湮没,末态只剩两个电子,其能谱在端点处呈尖锐单峰。当前吨级同位素实验已把半衰期下限推进到10^26年以上,仍未捕捉到信号。
It offers the practical test of whether the neutrino is its own antiparticle: observing it would prove lepton number violation, establish neutrinos as Majorana particles, and fix their absolute mass scale. Ordinary double beta decay lets an antineutrino pair carry energy away, whereas in the neutrinoless mode the two virtual neutrinos must annihilate directly, leaving two electrons alone and a sharp spike at the endpoint of their summed spectrum. Ton-scale isotope experiments now push the half-life limit beyond ten to the twenty-sixth years, still without a signal.

#无中微子双贝塔衰变 #马约拉纳中微子 #轻子数不守恒

萨德伯里中微子观测站(SNO)为什么能证明太阳中微子「失踪」源于味振荡而非太阳模型出错?

Why could the Sudbury Neutrino Observatory prove that the missing solar neutrinos were due to flavor oscillation rather than a flawed solar model?

因为重水让它能同时测两类反应:带电流只计入电子型中微子,中性流对三种味一视同仁。结果总通量与标准太阳模型一致,而电子型只占约三分之一,其余变成了μ、τ味。味会改变意味着三种中微子质量不同且相互混合,即中微子有质量,太阳模型反而得到验证。
Because heavy water allowed two reactions at once: the charged current counts only electron neutrinos while the neutral current treats all three flavors equally. The measured total flux matched the standard solar model, yet electron neutrinos accounted for only about one third, the rest having turned into muon and tau flavors. Changing flavor requires distinct masses and mixing among the neutrinos, so the result proved neutrinos have mass and vindicated the solar model.

#中微子振荡 #太阳中微子问题 #重水探测器

超级神冈探测器为什么能凭上行与下行大气μ子中微子数量的显著不对称,断定中微子在飞行途中发生了振荡?

Why did the sharp asymmetry between upward-going and downward-going atmospheric muon neutrinos let Super-Kamiokande conclude that neutrinos oscillate during flight?

因为几何条件让上下行构成天然对照:宇宙线在大气顶层均匀产生电子型和μ子型中微子,下行者只飞约20公里,上行者却要穿越近1.3万公里的地球。超级神冈发现上行电子型基本无损而μ子型明显亏缺,且亏缺随路程增长而加剧,正是μ子中微子按正弦规律转化为其他味的特征,由此定出质量平方差约2.5×10^-3 eV^2,确证中微子有质量并发生振荡。
Geometry supplies a built-in control: cosmic rays create electron and muon neutrinos evenly at the top of the atmosphere, yet a downward path spans only about twenty kilometers while an upward one crosses nearly thirteen thousand kilometers of Earth. Super-Kamiokande saw upward electron events essentially intact but upward muon events strongly depleted, the deficit deepening with distance - the signature of sinusoidal conversion into unseen flavors. The pattern pinned a mass-squared splitting near 2.5×10^-3 eV^2, establishing that neutrinos oscillate and hence have mass.
