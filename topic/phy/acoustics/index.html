为什么两个频率接近的音会听出“拍音”？

Why do two close frequencies produce a beating tone?

两个频率非常接近的音同时响，它们的声波会周期性地互相加强又抵消，听起来音量一强一弱地起伏，这就是拍音。
Two nearly equal frequencies interfere, periodically reinforcing and canceling, so the loudness pulses up and down—this wobble is a beat.

声学中的非线性项如何产生冲击波（如雷声、声爆）？

How do nonlinear terms create shock waves in acoustics?

声波大振幅时传播速度随局部压强升高而增大，波前追赶波后而变陡，最终破碎成不连续面（激波）；能量在很薄层内耗散，发出雷声般巨响与声爆。
At large amplitude the sound speed rises with local pressure, so wave crests catch up and steepen into a discontinuity (shock); energy dissipates in a thin layer, giving thunder-like booms and sonic cracks.

主动噪声控制（ANC）如何利用波的干涉在局部区域抵消低频噪声？

How does active noise control use wave interference to cancel low-frequency noise in a local region?

ANC用麦克风采集原噪声，由控制器生成相位相反、振幅相等的反相声波，与原声波叠加产生相消干涉，使声压在该区域趋近于零。由于声波波长限制，ANC在低频（长波长）效果好，且仅在误差传声器附近有限空间有效。
ANC picks up the original noise with a microphone and a controller generates an anti-phase sound wave of equal amplitude; destructive interference drives the local sound pressure toward zero. Limited by wavelength, ANC works best at low frequencies and only in the small region near the error microphone.

什么是多普勒效应？

What is the Doppler effect?

多普勒效应指波源与观察者相对运动时，观察者接收到的频率发生变化的现象。火车驶近时汽笛音调变高、远离时变低，就是这一效应。
The Doppler effect is the change in observed frequency when the source and observer move relative to each other. A train whistle sounds higher as it approaches and lower as it recedes.

Bragg 散射型声子晶体在千赫兹以下很难打开带隙,局域共振结构是怎样绕开这个限制的?

Bragg-scattering phononic crystals struggle to open band gaps below kilohertz frequencies; how do locally resonant structures get around this limit?

Bragg 机制要求波长与晶格常数同量级,音频低频对应的米级周期在工程上无法实现。局域共振单元在其固有频率附近与基体强耦合,使等效动态质量密度或等效模量变为负值,于是在远小于波长的晶格尺寸上也能打开完全带隙。带隙位置由共振子质量、刚度和填充率决定,基本与周期大小解耦。
The Bragg mechanism demands wavelengths comparable to the lattice constant, which would mean meter-scale periodicity at low audio frequencies. Locally resonant units couple strongly to the matrix near their natural frequency, driving the effective dynamic mass density or modulus negative and opening complete gaps on deeply subwavelength lattices. Gap positions are set by resonator mass, stiffness, and filling fraction, essentially decoupled from the period itself.

#声子晶体 #局域共振带隙

水中气泡的米纳尔特共振频率是什么？它为何让微小气泡成为水下声场的主导散射与吸收体？

What is the Minnaert resonance frequency of a gas bubble, and why does it make tiny bubbles dominant scatterers and absorbers of underwater sound?

气泡像一个振子：可压缩气体充当弹簧，周围水的惯性充当质量，共振频率f≈(1/2πR)√(3γP0/ρ)，与半径成反比。毫米级气泡共振在千赫兹量级，远低于同尺寸刚性球的瑞利散射预期；近共振时散射截面可比几何截面大几个数量级，因此舰船尾流和生物气泡层会显著衰减声呐信号，雨滴入海的气泡也构成海洋环境噪声源。
A bubble acts as an oscillator: the compressible gas supplies the spring and the inertia of the surrounding water supplies the mass, giving f≈(1/2πR)√(3γP0/ρ), inversely proportional to radius. Millimetre-sized bubbles resonate at kilohertz frequencies, far below what Rayleigh scattering from a rigid sphere of that size would imply, and near resonance the scattering cross-section can exceed the geometric cross-section by orders of magnitude. Ship wakes and biological bubble layers therefore strongly attenuate sonar, and bubbles from raindrops hitting the sea are a major source of ocean ambient noise.

救护车鸣笛靠近时音调变高、远离时变低，为什么？

Why does an ambulance siren sound higher as it approaches and lower as it leaves?

这是多普勒效应：声源靠近时波前被压缩、波长变短、频率变高；远离时被拉开、频率变低。我们据此判断车在靠近还是走远。
This is the Doppler effect: as the source approaches, wavefronts compress to shorter wavelengths and higher pitch; as it leaves, they stretch and the pitch drops. It tells us the siren is coming or going.

医学超声成像基于什么物理原理，为何能无损地观察体内组织？

On what physical principle does medical ultrasound imaging rely, and why can it image internal tissue non-invasively?

超声探头发射高频声波（通常1至15 MHz），遇到不同声阻抗的组织界面时发生反射，回波经压电换能器转为电信号并按时间延迟重建为断面图像。因其使用机械能而非电离辐射，对人体基本无损伤。
An ultrasound probe emits high-frequency sound (typically 1 to 15 MHz); reflections occur at tissue interfaces of differing acoustic impedance, and the echoes are converted by piezoelectric transducers into electrical signals reconstructed by time delay into cross-sectional images. Using mechanical energy rather than ionizing radiation, it is essentially non-damaging.

Why do soldiers break step rather than march in rhythm across a bridge?

不是。耳蜗有一套主动机制：感受声波的信号主要由单排内毛细胞转导，外围三排外毛细胞则借助膜蛋白prestin随电压伸缩，向行波注入机械能，可把弱声下的基底膜响应提高几十倍乃至上百倍。Gold在1948年就预言了这一主动过程，后来发现的耳声发射正是它泄漏回耳道的副产品。有了主动供能，人耳对微弱声音才既灵敏又能精细分辨频率。
No. The cochlea runs an active mechanism: sound signals are transduced mainly by the single row of inner hair cells, while the three outer rows act as motors. Their membrane protein prestin lets the cells lengthen and shorten with voltage, injecting mechanical energy into the traveling wave and boosting the basilar membrane's weak-sound response by tens to hundreds of times. Thomas Gold predicted this active process in 1948, and the otoacoustic emissions later detected in the ear canal are its leaked by-product. This powered feedback is what makes hearing at threshold both sensitive and finely tuned in frequency.

声音是怎么传播到我们耳朵里的？

How does sound travel to our ears?

物体振动会推动周围空气形成疏密相间的声波，声波通过空气等介质传到耳朵，使鼓膜振动并被大脑识别为声音。声音不能在真空中传播。

弹吉他时，为什么按住琴弦越靠后（有效长度越短），发出的音就越高？

When playing guitar, why does pressing the string shorter make the note higher?

弦振动的基本频率与其长度成反比，弦越短，来回振动一次越快，频率越高，听感上音就越尖。所以同一根弦，按在不同品丝上改变有效长度，就能奏出不同音高。这也是所有弦乐器定音的基础。
The fundamental frequency of a vibrating string is inversely proportional to its length; a shorter string completes each back-and-forth vibration faster, giving a higher frequency and a higher pitch. That is why pressing a string at different frets changes the note. It is the basis of tuning for all string instruments.

Why does a guitar string produce a fixed pitch instead of a random noise?

拨动琴弦时，只有那些能在弦长内恰好形成整数个「驻波」的振动模式被保留下来——波峰波谷被两端固定点锁住，来回反射叠加成稳定的形状。不同模式对应不同频率（基频和它的整数倍泛音），所以我们听到清晰的音高。驻波也出现在管乐器和鼓面上。
When you pluck a string, only vibrations that fit a whole number of 'standing waves' along its length survive—their peaks and nodes are pinned by the fixed ends and reinforce into a stable shape. Each mode has a frequency (the fundamental and its integer harmonics), giving a clear pitch. Standing waves also appear in wind instruments and drumheads.

为什么救护车开近时警笛听起来更尖，开远后变低沉？

Why does an ambulance siren sound higher as it approaches and lower as it leaves?

这是多普勒效应：声源朝你运动时，它发出的声波被压缩，到达你耳中的波长变短、频率变高，于是音调更尖；远离时声波被拉开，频率变低、音调下沉。同样的原理也用于雷达测速和天文学中测星系退行（光的多普勒红移）。它由克里斯蒂安·多普勒1842年提出。
This is the Doppler effect: as the source moves toward you, its sound waves compress, reaching your ear with shorter wavelength and higher frequency, so the pitch rises; moving away stretches them, lowering the frequency and pitch. The same idea is used in radar speed guns and in astronomy to measure receding galaxies (optical redshift). Christian Doppler proposed it in 1842.

用手指沾点水轻轻摩擦酒杯的杯口，为什么杯子会发出悠长而纯净的嗡鸣声？

Why does lightly rubbing a wet finger around a wine glass rim make it sing a long, pure tone?

手指沿杯口滑动时的“粘—滑”摩擦不断向杯壁输入能量，激起杯沿的弯曲振动模态——最低模态是带四个节点的“椭圆化”驻波。该模态频率由杯壁几何与材料决定、相当稳定，所以听到纯净持续的乐音；往杯里加水会让频率明显降低。玻璃琴就是靠同一机制发声的。
Stick–slip friction between the sliding wet finger and the rim keeps pumping energy into the glass, exciting a bending mode of the rim—the lowest one being an 'ovalling' standing wave with four nodes. That mode's frequency is fixed by the glass geometry and material and is quite stable, so the tone is pure and sustained; adding water to the glass lowers the pitch noticeably. The glass harmonica sounds by exactly the same mechanism.

对着大山喊一声,为什么能听到自己的回声?

Why can you hear your own echo when you shout at a mountain?

声音向前跑,碰到大山或高墙会被弹回来,这个现象叫反射。弹回来的声音就是回声。空旷的山谷里,回声要多跑一段路才回到耳朵,比喊声慢一点,听起来就像有人学你说话。教室的墙太近太软,回声混在喊声里,就不容易听出来啦。
Sound rushes forward and bounces back when it hits a big mountain or wall; bouncing back is called reflection. The bounced-back sound is an echo. In a wide valley the echo travels an extra stretch before returning, arriving a little late, so it sounds like someone copying you. In a classroom the walls are too close and too soft, so the echo hides inside your shout.
