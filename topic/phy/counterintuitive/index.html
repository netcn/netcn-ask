北半球的马桶冲水一定逆时针转吗？

Does toilet water in the Northern Hemisphere always swirl counterclockwise?

不一定。科里奥利力对这么小的水流影响极小，旋转方向主要取决于马桶形状和水流初始扰动，而不是地球自转。
Not really. The Coriolis force is far too weak at that scale; the spin direction is set by the toilet's shape and initial disturbance, not Earth's rotation.

为什么两个初始几乎完全相同的双摆，过一会儿就会走向完全不同的轨迹？

Why do two double pendulums started almost identically soon follow completely different paths?

双摆是混沌系统，对初始条件极端敏感：微小差别会被指数放大。因此长期不可预测，这正是混沌而非随机。
The double pendulum is chaotic and exquisitely sensitive to initial conditions: tiny differences amplify exponentially, making long-term behavior unpredictable yet deterministic.

为什么两艘并行行驶的船会不由自主地相互靠近？

Why do two ships sailing side by side drift toward each other?

两船之间的水流变快、压强变低，外侧较高的水压把船往中间推。这是伯努利原理，和飞机升力同源。
Water between the ships flows faster and its pressure drops, so higher outside pressure pushes them together. This is Bernoulli's principle, the same source as lift.

#迈斯纳 #超导 #悬浮

蝴蝶效应是说蝴蝶扇扇翅膀真能掀起龙卷风吗？

Does the butterfly effect really mean a butterfly's wing flap can stir up a tornado?

那是一种比喻，不是字面意思。天气是典型的非线性混沌系统：初始状态的微小差别会随时间指数放大，两三天内还能预报，几周后误差就吞没了信号。蝴蝶扇翅只是象征初始误差小到极致的情形。因此哪怕测量得再精确，天气的长期预报也不可能做到——这正是天气预报超过一周就不可靠的根本原因。
It's a metaphor, not a literal claim. The weather is a nonlinear, chaotic system: tiny differences in initial conditions grow exponentially, staying predictable for days but swamping the signal within weeks. The butterfly symbolizes an infinitesimally small initial difference. So no matter how precisely we measure, long-term weather prediction is impossible — the real reason forecasts beyond about a week can't be trusted.

在失重的太空里点蜡烛，火焰为什么是球形的？

In weightless space, why is a lit candle flame spherical?

地球上热烟上升造成对流，火焰被拉长；太空没有重力对流，靠扩散供氧，于是各方向对称，呈球形。
On Earth hot gas rises and convects, stretching the flame; in space there is no gravity-driven convection, so diffusion feeds it symmetrically into a sphere.

飞机机翼上表面弯、空气跑得更快，为什么那里的压强反而更低、把飞机托起？

The wing's top is curved and air moves faster there, yet pressure is lower and lifts the plane. Why?

伯努利原理说流速大的地方压强小。上下压强差产生向上的升力；同时机翼也把空气向下推，符合作用力与反作用力。
Bernoulli's principle says faster flow means lower pressure; the pressure difference gives lift, and the wing also pushes air downward (action–reaction).

#抢风 #升力 #帆船

把两张纸平行悬空、往中间吹气，为什么纸不但不会被吹开，反而会被吸到一起？

If you hold two sheets of paper parallel and blow between them, why are they drawn together instead of blown apart?

根据伯努利原理，流速快的地方压强更低。往两张纸中间吹气使缝隙里空气流速加大、压强下降，纸外侧的静止空气压强相对较高，就把纸往中间推。所以“吹”反而造成“吸”。
By Bernoulli's principle, faster flow means lower pressure. Blowing between the sheets speeds up the air there and drops its pressure, while the still air outside has higher pressure and pushes the sheets inward. So blowing produces suction rather than separation.

In a vacuum, when a feather and an iron ball fall together, does the feather still have weight?

有。重量来自地球引力，和有没有空气无关；真空里只是没有空气阻力，两者才以相同加速度下落。
Yes. Weight comes from gravity and has nothing to do with air; in vacuum there is simply no air resistance, so both fall with the same acceleration.

A cube of ice floats in a cup of water. After it melts, does the water level rise, fall, or stay the same?

水面几乎不动。因为浮着的冰排开的水，正好等于它融化后变成的水的体积。
The level stays about the same, because the floating ice already displaced exactly the volume of water it becomes when melted.

#浮力 #冰 #排开水

电梯的缆绳突然断了，你在电梯里会瞬间失去体重吗？为什么？

If an elevator's cable snaps, do you instantly lose your weight inside it? Why?

会感觉失重，因为电梯和你一起自由下落，你和电梯之间没有相互挤压。但地球对你的引力依然存在，只是没有“支撑力”表现出来。
You feel weightless because the elevator and you fall together, so there is no normal force pressing between you. Earth's gravity still acts on you; it just has nothing to push against.

Why are soap bubbles always round?

用吸管喝饮料时，真的是“吸力”把液体“拉”上来的吗，为什么瓶里液面却不会跟着上升？

When you drink through a straw, is the liquid really 'pulled' up by suction, and why doesn't the drink level in the bottle rise with it?

吸管里并没有凭空产生拉力，你只是把管内的空气吸走、降低气压。外面更高的外界大气压便把液体沿管子压了上来。所以本质上是大气压在“推”，而不是你在“拉”。
Nothing pulls the liquid up from inside; you only remove air and lower the pressure in the straw. The higher outside atmospheric pressure then pushes the liquid up the tube. So it is atmospheric pressure pushing, not you pulling.
