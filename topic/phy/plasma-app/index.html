拿到朗缪尔单探针的一条 I-V 特性曲线后,怎样从中读出电子温度和等离子体密度?

Given a single Langmuir probe I-V characteristic, how do you extract the electron temperature and the plasma density from it?

在远低于等离子体电位的区间,电子电流按 exp(eV/kTₑ) 指数增长,作 ln Iₑ 对探针电压的图,直线段斜率即给 kTₑ/e。曲线拐点(电子饱和流起点)定出等离子体电位,将饱和电子流除以探针面积并代入 Iₑsat = enₑA√(kTₑ/2πmₑ) 即得密度。悬浮电位处正负电流相等,可作为零电流参考用来校验读数。
Well below the plasma potential the electron current grows as exp(eV/kTₑ); plotting ln Iₑ against probe voltage yields kTₑ/e from the straight-line slope. The knee where electron saturation begins fixes the plasma potential, and dividing Iₑsat by the probe area in Iₑsat = enₑA√(kTₑ/2πmₑ) gives the density. At the floating potential ion and electron currents balance, providing a zero-current cross-check on the readings.

#等离子体诊断 #朗缪尔探针

电容耦合射频刻蚀腔中，功率电极上的直流自偏压是如何自发建立的？它又如何把离子能量与化学自由基通量解耦开来？

How does the dc self-bias on the powered electrode arise spontaneously in a capacitively coupled RF etcher, and how does it decouple ion energy from the chemical radical flux reaching the wafer?

小面积功率电极与大面积接地壁构成非对称回路，非线性鞘层使两侧压降不再相等，稳态下系统自动建立起直流自偏压以令正负半周的电流积分相等，功率电极因而悬浮在数百伏负电位。衬底处离子穿越增厚的高压鞘层被加速，撞击能量由偏压直接设定；而中性自由基的产生率取决于放电密度，几乎不受偏压影响。两个通道独立可调，正是各向异性刻蚀剖面与低损伤工艺得以兼顾的原因。
Because the small powered electrode pairs with large grounded walls, the nonlinear sheath responses unbalance the two voltage drops, and the discharge spontaneously develops a dc self-bias that equalizes current flow over positive and negative half-cycles, floating the powered electrode a few hundred volts negative. Ions crossing the thickened high-voltage sheath arrive with energies set directly by that bias, while neutral radical production tracks the discharge density and barely notices the bias. Tuning the two channels independently is what allows anisotropic profiles together with low substrate damage.

#等离子体刻蚀 #射频鞘层

托卡马克既然靠等离子体电流自己产生磁场,为什么还要再绕一圈庞大的环向场线圈?

Since a tokamak plasma generates its own field via the plasma current, why is a huge set of toroidal-field coils still needed?

只有电流产生的极向场时,磁力线旋转变换不足,无法形成稳定的嵌套磁面,扭曲模会迅速把等离子体推向第一壁。环向场线圈提供占主导的环向磁场,两者叠加成螺旋磁力线,给出所需的安全因子和磁面结构。因此环向场是约束的骨架,等离子体电流只负责补足旋转变换并提供欧姆加热。
The poloidal field from the current alone gives too little rotational transform: nested flux surfaces cannot form, and kink modes rapidly push the plasma into the first wall. The toroidal coils supply the dominant toroidal field, and the two superpose into helical field lines with the required safety factor and flux surfaces. The toroidal field is thus the backbone of confinement, while the current tops up the transform and provides ohmic heating.

#磁约束聚变 #托卡马克环向场

荧光灯管里的放电明明发的是紫外线,为什么我们看到的却是白光?

The discharge inside a fluorescent tube actually emits ultraviolet light, so why do we see white light?

低压汞蒸气放电中电子碰撞把汞原子激发到 6³P₁ 态,其主要跃迁辐射 254 nm 紫外光,人眼完全看不见。管壁荧光粉吸收这些紫外光子后经下转换发射红绿蓝混合的可见光。整灯因此是放电产紫外、荧光粉转白光的两步过程,发光效率远高于单纯加热灯丝的白炽灯。
In a low-pressure mercury discharge, electron impact excites Hg atoms to the 6³P₁ state, whose main transition radiates 254 nm UV that is invisible to the eye. The phosphor coating absorbs these UV photons and down-converts them into a red-green-blue mix of visible light. The lamp is thus a two-step emitter—discharge makes UV, phosphor makes white—which is far more efficient than heating a filament.

#气体放电 #低压汞灯发光

在芯片制造的刻蚀与沉积环节，为什么要借助等离子体而不是直接使用普通的反应性气体？

Why do etching and deposition steps in chip fabrication rely on plasmas rather than simply feeding in ordinary reactive gases?

室温下多数工艺气体化学性质近乎惰性，反应速率可以忽略；等离子体中的高能电子碰撞把气体解离为自由基和离子，使缓慢的化学反应在温和的晶圆温度下高速推进。与此同时，离子被鞘层电场加速后近乎垂直地轰击表面，赋予刻蚀很强的方向性并抑制侧向钻蚀。低温、高速与各向异性三者兼备，这是单纯的化学气相反应无法提供的组合。
Most process gases are chemically inert at room temperature, with negligible reaction rates, but energetic electrons in a plasma dissociate them into radicals and ions, driving fast chemistry at moderate wafer temperatures. At the same time, ions accelerated through the sheath strike surfaces nearly perpendicular, giving etching strong directionality and suppressing lateral undercutting. Low temperature, high rate, and anisotropy together form a combination that plain gas-phase chemistry cannot deliver.
