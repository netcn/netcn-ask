跳远运动员起跳前为什么要先跑一段“助跑”？

Why does a long jumper run up before takeoff?

助跑积累水平速度，起跳时把一部分水平速度转成向上的速度；水平越快，同样腾空时间里飞得越远。
The run-up builds horizontal speed, some of which converts to vertical at takeoff; more horizontal speed means more distance in the same air time.

短跑起跑为什么要用起跑器？

Why do sprinters use starting blocks?

起跑器给脚一个稳固的支撑，让腿能以更大角度猛蹬，获得更大的向前的反作用力，起跑更猛。
Blocks give a firm push-off so the leg drives at a better angle, producing a larger forward reaction force for a faster start.

网球拍线穿得松一点，为什么反而更“吃球”、更好控？

Why does a looser tennis string bed give more control and 'bite'?

松线让球在拍面停留更久，形变更大，能咬住球加更多旋转，也扩大甜区、减少失误。
Looser strings let the ball dwell longer and deform more, gripping it for extra spin and enlarging the sweet spot, which improves control.

射箭时为什么拉弓比直接用手扔箭更有力？

Why is drawing a bow more effective than throwing an arrow by hand?

弓把缓慢的拉力存成弹性势能，松手时瞬间全部传给箭；手扔的力作用时间短、能量少，箭飞不快。
The bow stores drawing energy as elastic potential and releases it all at once; a hand throw acts too briefly to impart much energy.

乒乓球拉上旋弧圈球，为什么落地后会往前“窜”？

Why does a topspin loop shot in table tennis kick forward after bouncing?

上旋飞行时马格努斯力向下压，落台瞬间球相对台面向上旋，摩擦把它往前上方弹，看起来像加速前冲。
Topspin's Magnus force pushes the ball down; at the bounce the upward spin grips the table and flings it forward, so it seems to accelerate.

高尔夫球表面满是凹坑，为什么反而比光滑的飞得更远？

Why do dimpled golf balls fly farther than smooth ones?

凹坑让表面气流变成湍流，推迟分离、缩小尾流，阻力更小；光滑球后面的大漩涡反而更费劲。
Dimples trip the flow into turbulence, delaying separation and shrinking the wake, cutting drag; a smooth ball's big wake costs more.

骑自行车为什么速度越快反而越稳，不容易倒？

Why is a bicycle steadier the faster you go?

转动的车轮有角动量（陀螺效应），加上前轮“拖距”让车头自动回正；速度高时这些稳定作用更明显。
Spinning wheels carry angular momentum (gyroscopic effect) and the front-wheel trail self-centers the steering; both stabilizing effects grow with speed.

骑自行车上坡为什么要换成小牙盘、大飞轮？

Why shift to a small front gear and large rear cog when cycling uphill?

这样每蹬一圈轮子转得少，但踩起来更省力——用转速换力矩，像用长扳手拧螺丝更省力。
Each pedal turn spins the wheel less but needs less force—you trade speed for torque, like a longer wrench needing less effort.

足球的“香蕉球”为什么会拐弯？

Why does a soccer 'banana' kick curve in the air?

球高速旋转，一侧空气被带着走、流速快，另一侧慢；压强差把球推向低压侧，这就是马格努斯效应。
A spinning ball drags air faster on one side, lowering pressure there; the pressure difference pushes it sideways—the Magnus effect.

撑杆跳高时，运动员为什么要把杆弯成弓形？

Why does a pole vaulter bend the pole into a bow?

助跑的动能先存进弯曲的杆里变成弹性势能，杆回弹时再把人“弹”得更高，比直接跳省劲得多。
Run-up kinetic energy is stored as elastic energy in the bent pole, which then springs the vaulter higher—far better than jumping unaided.

在蹦床上，为什么能跳得比原地起跳更高？

Why can you jump higher on a trampoline than from the ground?

网面把你下落的动能先存成弹性势能，再回弹帮你加速，相当于地面“借”了你一份力。
The bed stores your falling energy as elastic potential and gives it back, adding to your jump as if the ground pushed you up.

跳水运动员入水时，为什么要把身体绷直、手先插进水里？

Why do divers straighten up and enter hands-first?

流线型身体阻力小、速度损失少；手先入水把面积缩到最小，水花也最小，得分更高。
A streamlined body cuts drag and keeps speed; entering hands-first minimizes the area and splash, earning better scores.

篮球投篮加后旋，为什么更容易“柔和”地进筐？

Why does backspin help a basketball go in softly?

后旋球撞框或板时反弹角度更平、更易向下滚进；就算碰到筐沿也常被“吸”回去，容错更好。
Backspin makes the ball rebound more gently and downward off rim or board, and even on a rim it often rolls in, giving more margin.
