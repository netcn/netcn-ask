为什么电磁力比引力强得多？

Why is the electromagnetic force so much stronger than gravity?

在基本粒子尺度，两个质子间的电力约为引力的10的36次方倍。日常物质呈电中性，正负电荷相互抵消，使宏观上引力占主导；但在原子和分子层面，电磁力才是决定结构的主要力量。
At the particle scale the electric force between two protons is about 10^36 times their gravitational attraction. Everyday matter is electrically neutral, with charges canceling, so gravity dominates macroscopically; but at atomic and molecular scales electromagnetism governs structure.

When a spring is squashed or stretched, it wants to return to its shape and pushes back.

Why does a fast spinning top stay upright?

A spinning object carries angular momentum that resists being tipped, so the faster it spins the steadier it stays.

为什么在冰面上容易滑倒？

Why is it easy to slip on ice?

冰面很光滑，脚和地面之间的摩擦力很小，脚一蹬就容易向前滑而不是站稳。
Ice is very smooth, so there is little friction between your foot and the ground; you slide instead of gripping firmly.

Does a spring pull harder the more you stretch it?

Earth's gravity pulls nearby objects toward it, so when you let go they fall downward.
