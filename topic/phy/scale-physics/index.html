为什么在纳米尺度，水会表现出和平时很不一样的“脾气”？

Why does water behave oddly at the nanometer scale?

纳米空间里表面力压倒体积力，水会被界面“结构化”、流速在碳纳米管中异常变快，许多日常规律不再适用。
At nanometer sizes surface forces overwhelm bulk forces; water becomes structured near walls and can flow anomalously fast in nanotubes, breaking everyday rules.

为什么在细胞那么小的尺度，“黏性”说了算，而惯性的作用几乎看不见？

Why, at the tiny scale of a cell, does viscosity dominate while inertia hardly matters?

衡量惯性与黏性较量的雷诺数在微观下极小，流动由黏性主导；所以细菌游动后几乎立刻停下，不像鱼靠惯性滑行。
The Reynolds number, comparing inertia to viscosity, is tiny at cellular scales, so viscosity dominates and a bacterium stops almost at once when it stops swimming.

为什么星系转得那么快，却没被自己的离心力甩散，暗示有看不见的东西？

Why do galaxies spin so fast without flying apart, hinting at unseen matter?

按可见物质的引力，外侧恒星应转得慢；实际转速过快，说明存在不发光、只施引力的“暗物质”提供额外束缚。
Visible matter's gravity predicts slower outer stars, but they orbit too fast, implying invisible dark matter adds the extra binding pull.

#暗物质 #星系 #引力

为什么最远的星系看起来正以超过光速的速度远离我们，却没违背相对论？

Why do the most distant galaxies recede faster than light without breaking relativity?

它们不是在空间里“穿行”超光速，而是它们之间的空间本身在膨胀，把距离拉开。相对论限制的是物体在空间里的速度，不限空间自身的拉伸。
They are not moving through space faster than light; the space between us is expanding. Relativity limits motion through space, not the stretching of space itself.

为什么微观粒子的“位置测不准”在肉眼可见的宏观世界里几乎感觉不到？

Why is the uncertainty in a particle's position unnoticeable in the macroscopic world?

不确定性由普朗克常数决定，它极小；宏观物体动作量远大于它，相对误差微乎其微，所以世界看起来确定。
Uncertainty is set by Planck's constant, which is tiny; macroscopic actions are vastly larger, so the relative error is negligible and the world looks definite.

为什么地震波能绕地球跑一圈被对面测到，而你在房间里说话只是听到回声？

Why can seismic waves circle the globe yet your voice in a room only echoes?

地震波在地球内部传播、能量衰减小，能穿过整个星球；房间里声波波长太短、被墙壁快速吸收反射，只能形成近处回声。
Seismic waves travel through Earth and lose little energy, crossing the whole planet; room sound has short wavelengths, quickly absorbed and reflected into nearby echoes.

为什么细菌靠“晃”前进，而鱼靠“摆尾”前进——尺度决定了策略？

Why do bacteria wiggle to move while fish swish their tails—does scale decide the strategy?

细菌处在低雷诺数世界，惯性没用，只能靠持续摆动螺旋桨式鞭毛“爬”过黏性；鱼在高雷诺数下靠摆尾制造推力并利用惯性滑行。
Bacteria live at low Reynolds number where inertia is useless, so they must continuously crank flagella through viscosity; fish use tails for thrust and coast on inertia.

原子内部几乎是空的，为什么我们摸到桌子却是实实在在的？

Atoms are mostly empty space, so why does a table feel solid?

原子核虽小，但电子云充满原子；相邻原子的电子因电磁排斥和泡利不相容原理无法重叠，于是把手挡在外面。
The nucleus is tiny but the electron cloud fills the atom; neighboring electrons repel electromagnetically and cannot overlap by Pauli exclusion, blocking your hand.

小雨滴从很高处落下，为什么是圆滚滚的而不是拉成长条？

Why are falling raindrops rounded rather than stretched into long shapes?

表面张力总想缩小表面积，把小水滴拉成球；只有够大的雨滴才被空气压扁成上宽下窄的“馒头”形。
Surface tension pulls small drops into spheres to minimize area; only larger drops get flattened by air into a bun shape.

#表面张力 #雨滴 #尺度

蚂蚁能搬动比自己重好几十倍的东西，为什么大象做不到？

An ant can lift dozens of times its own weight, but an elephant cannot. Why?

力气来自肌肉横截面，约按体长平方增长；体重按体积、约按立方增长。动物越大，体重比力气涨得快，相对就弱了。
Strength scales with muscle cross-section (length squared) while weight scales with volume (length cubed); as animals grow, weight outpaces strength.

对小蚂蚁来说，水的表面张力为什么像一张能站人的地板？

For an ant, why is water's surface tension like a floor it can stand on?

蚂蚁很轻，水面那层看不见的“皮”（表面张力）足以托住它，所以它能踩在水上不沉。
An ant is so light that the invisible skin of surface tension holds it up, letting it walk on water without sinking.

小虫子从高处掉下来往往没事，人摔下去却会受伤，为什么？

Small bugs survive falls that would injure us. Why?

空气阻力随面积增长、体重随体积增长；虫子又轻又“大而轻”，很快达到很低的终端速度，落地不疼。
Air drag scales with area, weight with volume; a bug is light and area-rich, reaching a very low terminal speed, so landing is gentle.

星星本身不眨眼睛，为什么我们透过大气层看它会闪？

Stars don't really twinkle; why do they shimmer when we look through the atmosphere?

大气冷热不均，密度在变，光线被反复折射，方向轻微抖动，看起来就一闪一闪。太空中没有大气，星星是稳的。
Turbulent air of varying density bends starlight slightly and randomly, making it shimmer; in space, with no atmosphere, stars are steady points.
