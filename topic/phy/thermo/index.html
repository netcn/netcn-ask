自由膨胀中理想气体的熵变如何计算，既然Q=0？

How is entropy change computed for free expansion if Q=0?

自由膨胀不可逆且 Q=0，但熵是状态函数；可设想一条可逆等温膨胀连接相同初末态，ΔS=∫dQ_rev/T=nR ln(V2/V1)。不能用实际过程的 dQ 计算不可逆路径。
Free expansion is irreversible with Q=0, but entropy is a state function; use a reversible isothermal path between the same states: ΔS=nR ln(V2/V1). The actual dQ cannot be used.

为什么绝对零度（-273.15°C）只能无限逼近，却永远无法真正达到？

Why can absolute zero (−273.15 °C) only be approached but never actually reached?

热力学第三定律指出，任何有限步骤的制冷过程都无法把系统冷却到绝对零度。随着温度趋近零，可用的熵差和可利用的制冷“落差”都趋于消失，因此越接近零越难再降温。它是一条不可逾越的极限，而非单纯技术困难。
The third law of thermodynamics states that no finite sequence of cooling steps can bring a system to absolute zero. As temperature approaches zero, the available entropy differences and the cooling 'head' both vanish, making further cooling ever harder. It is a fundamental limit, not merely a technical difficulty.

What does the fluctuation-dissipation theorem mean?

它表明系统处于平衡时的自发涨落（噪声）与其对微扰的线性响应（耗散）由同一温度联系：越粘滞的响应，其热噪声越大。这是约翰逊噪声与电阻关系的来源。
It links equilibrium fluctuations (noise) to linear response (dissipation) at temperature T: stronger dissipative response implies larger thermal fluctuations—this underlies Johnson noise in resistors.

液氦在极低温下为什么会变成“超流体”——能沿着杯壁自己爬出来流走？

Why does liquid helium become a 'superfluid' at very low temperature, even climbing the walls of its container and escaping?

在约 2.17 K 以下，部分氦-4 原子凝聚到同一个量子基态，形成具有零黏度的玻色–爱因斯坦凝聚成分。这种超流体没有任何黏性阻力，能沿极薄的液膜爬上容器壁、从杯口溢出。它是宏观尺度上直接显现的量子现象。
Below about 2.17 K a fraction of helium-4 atoms condenses into the same quantum ground state, forming a viscosity-free Bose–Einstein condensate component. With no viscous resistance, the superfluid creeps up container walls in an ultra-thin film and can overflow the rim. It is a quantum phenomenon visible at macroscopic scale.

加热的铁块为什么会由暗红变成黄白色？这和量子论的诞生有什么关系？

Why does heated iron glow from dull red to yellow-white, and what does that have to do with the birth of quantum theory?

物体温度越高，辐射的电磁波峰值波长越短，所以铁块先发红光，温度升高后峰值移向黄白色，这就是维恩位移定律。但经典物理完全无法解释黑体辐射的能量分布曲线，所谓"紫外灾难"迫使普朗克大胆假设：辐射能量只能以量子hv为单位一份份地发出。这一假设给出与实验完全吻合的公式，成为量子力学的起点，也直接启发了爱因斯坦后来解释光电效应。
The hotter an object, the shorter the peak wavelength of its radiation, so iron glows red first and shifts toward yellow-white as it heats — Wien's displacement law. But classical physics could not explain the blackbody spectrum at all, the "ultraviolet catastrophe," and this forced Planck to assume that energy is emitted only in discrete quanta hv. That hypothesis reproduced the data exactly, launched quantum mechanics, and directly inspired Einstein's later explanation of the photoelectric effect.

麦克斯韦妖真的能让热从冷处流向热处，从而推翻热力学第二定律吗？

Could Maxwell's demon really make heat flow from cold to hot and break the second law of thermodynamics?

麦克斯韦设想的小妖能辨认单个分子的速度并开关一道小门：把快分子放行到一边、慢分子留在另一边，无需做功就能制造温差。但深入分析后发现，妖精必须测量、记忆并擦除分子信息，而这些信息处理环节都不可避免要消耗能量、产生熵。把测量和存储的代价计入后，系统总熵并不会减少，热力学第二定律依然成立。
Maxwell imagined a tiny demon that recognizes individual molecular speeds and opens a trapdoor, sorting fast molecules to one side and slow ones to the other to build a temperature difference without work. Deeper analysis showed the demon must measure, remember and erase information about the molecules, and each of these steps inevitably costs energy and produces entropy. Once the price of information processing is included, total entropy never decreases and the second law stands.

Why do old thermometers and irons use two bonded metal strips to sense temperature?

Different metals expand by different amounts when heated, so a bonded bimetallic strip curves toward the side that expands less. Calibrating that bend to temperature makes a thermometer, and attaching a switch lets it control heat.

What is the ideal gas law?

理想气体状态方程为 pV=nRT，其中 p 是压强、V 是体积、n 是物质的量、T 是温度、R 是常数。它描述了在分子相互作用可忽略时气体的宏观行为。
The ideal gas law is pV=nRT, where p is pressure, V is volume, n is amount of substance, T is temperature, and R is a constant. It describes gas behavior when molecular interactions are negligible.

熵到底是什么？为什么说孤立系统的熵只会增加？

What exactly is entropy, and why does it always increase in an isolated system?

熵衡量系统的混乱程度，更准确地说是：给定同一个宏观状态时，系统内部各种微观排列方式的数量。孤立系统总会自发走向微观状态数最多的那个方向，因此熵趋于增加，这就是热力学第二定律。热从高温流向低温、墨水在水中自动散开，都是熵增在日常生活里的体现。
Entropy measures disorder — more precisely, how many microscopic arrangements correspond to a given macroscopic state. An isolated system spontaneously moves toward the most numerous arrangements, so its entropy tends to grow; that is the second law of thermodynamics. Heat flowing from hot to cold and ink dispersing through water are everyday displays of increasing entropy.

为什么被100摄氏度的蒸汽烫伤，常常比被同样温度的热水更严重？

Why does steam at 100°C often scald worse than boiling water at the same temperature?

因为蒸汽在皮肤表面凝结成水时，会释放出大量潜热：每克蒸汽凝结大约放出540卡热量，相当于把同质量的水再加热五百多度。这股额外的凝结热迅速传给皮肤，造成比热水更深的烫伤。潜热就是物质在相变时吸收或放出的那笔巨大的热量，它解释了很多"烫得不合理"的现象。
When steam condenses on skin, it releases latent heat — about 540 calories per gram, enough to heat the same mass of water more than 500 degrees more. This condensation heat dumps into the skin quickly and causes deeper burns than hot water. Latent heat is the large energy absorbed or released during a phase change, and it explains many surprisingly dangerous burns.

为什么热机不可能把燃料的热能百分之百变成有用功？

Carnot proved that even the ideal cycle has an efficiency capped by the two reservoir temperatures: efficiency equals 1 minus the cold temperature over the hot temperature. Reaching 100% would require a cold reservoir at -273.15°C, which cannot exist, so real efficiency always falls short of 1.

热力学第一定律是什么？

What is the first law of thermodynamics?

热力学第一定律就是能量守恒在热现象中的表达：系统吸收的热量等于内能增加加上对外做的功。能量既不会凭空产生，也不会凭空消失。
The first law of thermodynamics is energy conservation applied to heat: heat added to a system equals its increase in internal energy plus the work it does. Energy is neither created nor destroyed.

受热物体辐射的峰值波长随温度升高而变短，这是维恩位移定律。温度低时峰值在红外、可见光只漏出红色；温度高时峰值移到更短波长，红橙黄绿蓝都变强，混合起来看着就发白。
The peak wavelength of a hot object's radiation shifts to shorter values as temperature rises, described by Wien's displacement law. At low temperature the peak is in the infrared and only red leaks into visible light; at high temperature the peak moves shorter, so more colors appear and the glow looks white.

水能变成哪三种不一样的样子?冰化了还能变回水吗?

What three different forms can water take, and can ice turn back into water?

平常的水会流动,是液体。放进冰箱冻一冻,水变成硬硬的冰,成了固体。烧开的水冒出白蒙蒙的水汽,里面藏着水蒸气,它是气体。温度一变,水就能在固体、液体、气体三种样子之间变来变去,冰化了还会变回水呢。
Everyday water flows, so it is a liquid. Put it in the freezer and it becomes hard ice, a solid. Boil it and white mist rises from the pot, hiding water vapor, which is a gas. When temperature changes, water switches among solid, liquid, and gas; melted ice turns right back into water.
