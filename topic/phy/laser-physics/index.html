激光器实现稳定单模运转时,空间烧孔效应为什么会威胁单纵模工作,有哪些常用抑制手段?

Why does spatial hole burning threaten single-longitudinal-mode laser operation, and what techniques are commonly used to suppress it?

驻波腔内纵模的场分布具有固定的波腹波节结构,增益介质中粒子数反转被消耗出与该驻波图样对应的空间周期性凹陷,即空间烧孔。其他纵模的波节恰落在已烧孔位置,可以从未被消耗的反转中获得净增益,从而引发多模振荡。抑制手段包括使用行波环形腔消除驻波图样、采用短增益长度配合强模牵引,以及在腔内插入标准具或双折射滤波片提高相邻纵模的损耗差。
A standing-wave cavity fixes antinodes and nodes of each longitudinal mode, so inversion in the gain medium is depleted into periodic spatial dips matching that pattern — spatial hole burning. Other longitudinal modes place nodes where holes were burned and can extract net gain from undepleted inversion, driving multimode oscillation. Remedies include traveling-wave ring cavities that eliminate the standing pattern, short gain lengths with strong mode pulling, and intracavity etalons or birefringent filters that raise the loss contrast between adjacent modes.

#空间烧孔 #单纵模运转

为什么普通光源发光是各向同性的,而激光具有高度的方向性?

Why does ordinary light emit in all directions while laser light is highly directional?

普通光源依靠自发辐射,大量原子或分子的跃迁彼此独立,相位、偏振和传播方向都是随机的,因此光向四面八方发散。激光则来自光学谐振腔内的受激辐射,新产生的光子与诱发光子具有完全相同的频率、相位和传播方向。谐振腔两端反射镜的几何结构只允许沿腔轴方向的光反复放大并形成振荡,偏离轴向的模式因多次衍射损耗而无法建立,所以输出光束的发散角通常仅毫弧度量级。
Ordinary light sources rely on spontaneous emission: countless independent atomic or molecular transitions have random phases, polarizations, and propagation directions, so the light spreads isotropically. Laser light instead arises from stimulated emission inside an optical resonator, where each emitted photon duplicates the frequency, phase, and direction of the photon that induced it. The two mirrors of the resonator only sustain modes along the cavity axis; off-axis components suffer repeated diffraction losses and cannot build up, which is why a laser beam typically diverges by only a few milliradians.

#受激辐射 #光学谐振腔

激光器中谐振腔的纵模间隔由什么决定,如何估算可振荡的纵模数目?

What determines the longitudinal mode spacing of a laser resonator, and how do you estimate how many longitudinal modes can oscillate?

相邻纵模的频率间隔为 Δν = c/(2L),其中 L 是腔的光学长度,由腔长和介质折射率共同决定。只有落在增益介质增益带宽之内、且小信号增益超过腔损耗的纵模才能起振,因此可振荡模式数约为增益线宽除以纵模间隔。例如腔长 30 cm 的氦氖激光器 Δν 约 500 MHz,而多普勒线宽约 1.5 GHz,故通常有 2–3 个纵模同时振荡。
Adjacent longitudinal modes are separated by Δν = c/(2L), where L is the optical length of the cavity set by its geometric length and the refractive index of the intracavity medium. A mode can oscillate only if it lies within the gain bandwidth of the medium and its small-signal gain exceeds the round-trip loss, so the number of oscillating modes is roughly the gain linewidth divided by the mode spacing. A 30 cm helium-neon laser has Δν ≈ 500 MHz against a Doppler width near 1.5 GHz, so two or three longitudinal modes typically oscillate simultaneously.
