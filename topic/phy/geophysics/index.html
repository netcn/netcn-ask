古地磁条带如何证明海底扩张？

How do paleomagnetic stripes prove seafloor spreading?

洋中脊喷出的岩石在冷却时记录当时地磁场方向；磁场反复翻转留下对称的正负磁条带，像磁带一样向两侧展开，直接证明板块张裂与新洋壳生成。
Rocks at mid-ocean ridges freeze in the ambient field direction; repeated reversals leave symmetric magnetic stripes spreading both ways, directly proving seafloor spreading.

俯冲带为什么既是强震源又是火山工厂？

Why is a subduction zone both a quake source and a volcano factory?

大洋板块俯冲下沉时摩擦锁住积累应力，突然滑脱引发特大地震；板块带下的水降低地幔熔点，生成岩浆上涌形成岛弧火山。
A subducting slab locks and stores stress, then slips in great earthquakes; fluids it carries lower the mantle's melting point, feeding magma that builds island-arc volcanoes.

Why do volcanoes erupt?

地下岩浆受压上升，气体膨胀把岩浆从裂缝喷出地表。
Underground magma rises under pressure; gas expansion drives it out.

地核到底是固体还是液体，怎么知道的？

Is Earth's core solid or liquid, and how do we know?

地震的 S 波（横波）不能穿过液体，而穿过地核的 S 波消失、P 波却拐弯，说明外核是液态、内核是固态——靠波的行为反推出来。
S-waves cannot cross liquid, yet they vanish through the core while P-waves bend, showing a liquid outer core and solid inner core inferred from wave behavior.

地球磁场为什么会“翻转”？

Why does Earth's magnetic field flip?

外核液态铁的对流像发电机产生磁场；对流不稳定时偶极会减弱、混乱，最终南北极对调，全程历时数千年，化石磁条记录了下来。
Convection of liquid iron in the outer core acts as a dynamo; when convection turns unstable the dipole weakens and scrambles, eventually swapping north and south over thousands of years, recorded in rock magnetism.

地震波为什么能帮我们“看”地球内部？

Why can seismic waves help us see inside the Earth?

地震波在岩石里传播速度不同，遇到液体（比如外核）还会改变，科学家据此画出地球内部的图层。
Seismic waves travel at different speeds through rock and change at liquids like the outer core, letting us map Earth's interior.
