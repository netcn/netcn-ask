哈密顿量与相空间是什么？

What are the Hamiltonian and phase space?

相空间用“位置+动量”描述系统全部状态；哈密顿量 H(q,p) 给出总能量，其方程给出状态随时间演化的流。它是经典力学与统计物理的语言。
Phase space uses position+momentum to describe a system's full state; the Hamiltonian H(q,p) gives total energy and its equations govern evolution—the language of mechanics.

辛几何与泊松括号是什么？

What are symplectic geometry and the Poisson bracket?

辛几何研究带有“辛形式”的偶数维流形，是哈密顿力学的几何骨架。泊松括号 {f,g} 衡量两个物理量沿对方生成的演化，是量子对易子的经典对应。
Symplectic geometry studies even-dimensional manifolds with a symplectic form, the skeleton of Hamiltonian mechanics; the Poisson bracket {f,g} is the classical counterpart of quantum commutators.

杨-米尔斯与质量缺口问题是什么？

What is the Yang–Mills mass gap problem?

杨-米尔斯理论是描述强相互作用（量子色动力学）的规范场论。其“质量缺口”问题要求严格证明真空与最低激发态之间存在正的能量间隔，是千禧年难题之一。
Yang–Mills theory underlies the strong force; the mass gap problem asks to rigorously prove a positive energy gap above the vacuum—a Millennium Prize problem.

#物理数学 #杨米尔斯

诺特定理说每个连续对称性对应一个守恒量。以时间平移不变性为例说明该定理的具体内容。

Noether's theorem associates every continuous symmetry with a conserved quantity. Illustrate it concretely through time-translation invariance.

若拉格朗日函数不显含时间,则沿运动方程的能量 H=Σ q̇ᵢ∂L/∂q̇ᵢ−L 守恒:dH/dt = −∂L/∂t = 0。诺特定理把这推广为:拉格朗日量的每一个光滑对称变换都给出一个守恒荷,空间平移对应动量、转动对应角动量,时间平移对应能量。
If the Lagrangian has no explicit time dependence, the energy H=Σ q̇ᵢ∂L/∂q̇ᵢ−L is conserved along solutions: dH/dt = −∂L/∂t = 0. The theorem generalizes this: every smooth symmetry of the Lagrangian yields a conserved charge — spatial translations give momentum, rotations angular momentum, and time translations energy.

#诺特定理 #守恒律

诺特定理如何把物理系统的连续对称性与守恒律联系起来？举一个力学中的具体对应。

How does Noether's theorem connect continuous symmetries of a physical system to conservation laws? Give a concrete correspondence from mechanics.

诺特定理指出，若拉格朗日量在某个连续变换下不变，则由该变换生成的量沿运动方程守恒。例如拉格朗日量不显含时间对应能量守恒，不显含某个广义坐标（平移对称）则对应的广义动量守恒，这正是动量守恒的对称性根源。
Noether's theorem states that if the Lagrangian is invariant under a continuous transformation, then the quantity generated by that transformation is conserved along the equations of motion. For instance, a Lagrangian with no explicit time dependence yields energy conservation; independence from a generalized coordinate (translation symmetry) conserves the conjugate momentum—the symmetry origin of momentum conservation.
