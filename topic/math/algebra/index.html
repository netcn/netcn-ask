什么是环（ring）？

What is a ring?

环是一个带加法和乘法（加法成交换群、乘法结合且对加法分配律成立）的集合，但乘法不一定有逆元或交换。整数集在加减乘下就是一个环。
A ring has addition forming a commutative group and associative multiplication distributing over addition; integers form a ring.

为什么有理数上的多项式环是一个欧几里得整环？

Why is the polynomial ring over the rationals a Euclidean domain?

因为对任意两个有理系数多项式都可以做带余除法（反复消去首项），且余式的次数严格小于除式的次数。这样“次数”就充当了欧几里得函数，反复做带余除法等价于辗转相除求最大公因式。因此 Q[x] 是欧几里得整环，从而是主理想整环和唯一分解整环：每个多项式都能唯一分解成不可约多项式之积。
Because any two rational polynomials admit division with remainder (repeatedly cancelling leading terms), the remainder having strictly smaller degree than the divisor. The degree then serves as a Euclidean function, and iterated division is exactly the Euclidean algorithm computing GCDs. Hence Q[x] is a Euclidean domain, therefore a principal ideal domain and a unique factorization domain: every polynomial factors uniquely into irreducibles.

秩-零化度定理说的是什么？

What does the rank-nullity theorem say?

对 m×n 矩阵 A（即线性映射 V→W），秩（像空间的维数）与零化度（核的维数）之和等于 n：rank(A)+nullity(A)=n。它表达了一种“守恒”：被映射保留的独立方向与被压到零的方向加起来必然等于输入空间的维数。例如三阶线性映射的秩为 2 时，齐次方程 Ax=0 必有 1 维解空间。
For an m×n matrix A (a linear map V→W), the rank (dimension of the image) plus the nullity (dimension of the kernel) equals n: rank(A)+nullity(A)=n. It expresses a conservation law: the independent directions preserved by the map plus the directions collapsed to zero must total the dimension of the input space. A rank-2 linear map on 3D space therefore has a 1-dimensional solution space for Ax=0.

为什么有限域的元素个数总是某个素数的幂？

Why must a finite field have a prime-power number of elements?

有限域必有素数特征 p：把 1 自加 p 次就得到 0，这 p 个元素构成素域 F_p。整个有限域可以看作 F_p 上的向量空间，设其维数为 n，元素个数就是 pⁿ。反过来，对任意素数 p 和正整数 n，同构意义下恰好存在一个 pⁿ 元有限域。有限域是纠错码（如里德-所罗门码）、密码学和组合设计的重要工具。
A finite field has prime characteristic p: adding 1 to itself p times gives 0, and these p elements form the prime field F_p. The whole field is a vector space over F_p; if its dimension is n, the field has pⁿ elements. Conversely, for every prime p and positive integer n there is exactly one field of order pⁿ up to isomorphism. Finite fields power error-correcting codes (e.g., Reed–Solomon), cryptography, and combinatorial designs.

伽罗瓦理论如何解释为什么五次以上方程没有一般根式解？

How does Galois theory explain why equations of degree five or higher lack a general radical solution?

伽罗瓦理论把多项式方程的根对称性与一个群（伽罗瓦群）对应，根式可解等价于该群可解。一般五次方程的伽罗瓦群是对称群S5，它不可解，因此不存在通用的根式公式。阿廷与斯图尔特的教材把这一对应讲得极清楚。
Galois theory associates the symmetries among a polynomial's roots with a group, its Galois group, and radical solvability is equivalent to that group being solvable. The generic quintic has Galois group S5, which is not solvable, so no general radical formula exists. Artin and Stewart present this correspondence clearly.

线性规划里为什么最优解一定出现在可行域的“顶点”上？

In linear programming, why must an optimal solution occur at a vertex of the feasible region?

目标函数是线性函数，其水平线（等值线）是一族平行直线，函数值沿一个方向单调变化。可行域由线性不等式围成，是凸多边形（高维下是凸多面体）。若最优在区域内部或边上，沿垂直于等值线的方向移动（或沿边滑动）总能让值继续增大或不变，直到撞到顶点——除非最优在整条边上取得，而那样的边两端仍是顶点。所以只需检查顶点，单纯形法正是从一个顶点沿边跳到更优顶点的算法，实际规模问题的求解都建立在这个几何事实上。
The objective is linear, so its level sets are parallel lines and the function changes monotonically in one direction. The feasible region, cut out by linear inequalities, is a convex polygon (polytope in higher dimensions). If an optimum lay strictly inside or on an edge, moving perpendicular to the level lines (or sliding along the edge) would keep improving or at least not decrease the value, until a vertex is hit — unless the optimum is attained along an entire edge, whose endpoints are vertices anyway. Hence only vertices need checking: the simplex method walks from vertex to vertex along improving edges, and virtually all practical LP solvers rest on this geometric fact.

把 n 个符号重新排列的所有方式组成什么结构？它的“奇偶性”又是什么？

What structure do all rearrangements of n symbols form, and what is their “parity”?

所有排列在“先做排列 A 再做排列 B”的复合下构成对称群 Sₙ，共有 n! 个元素。任何排列都能写成若干次两两交换（对换）的乘积，虽然写法不唯一，但所需对换次数的奇偶性是确定的：偶数次叫偶排列，奇数次叫奇排列。偶排列全体构成正规子群 Aₙ（交错群），它有 n!/2 个元素；符号函数把群映到 {±1}，是理解置换群表示和五次方程不可解的关键工具。
All permutations under composition form the symmetric group Sₙ, with n! elements. Every permutation factors into transpositions (swaps of two symbols); the factorization is not unique, but its length's parity is: even permutations need an even number, odd ones an odd number. The even permutations form a normal subgroup Aₙ, the alternating group, of size n!/2. The sign homomorphism mapping Sₙ onto {±1} is the key tool behind representation theory and the unsolvability of the quintic.

Representation theory bridges algebra, geometry, and physics (e.g., quantum angular momentum), and Fourier analysis is a special case of representing the circle group.

What is a matrix?

矩阵是按行、列排成的矩形数表，真正的身份却是线性变换的坐标。乘法定义成行乘列再相加，是为了让两次连续变换合成一个矩阵，先做 B 再做 A 等于做 AB；相乘也因此要求前者的列数与后者的行数相等。两点易混：它一般不满足交换律，AB 与 BA 常不相等，顺序必须交代；矩阵也不是行列式，后者只是从方阵算出的一个数。
A matrix is a rectangular table of numbers arranged in rows and columns, and its real identity is the coordinate description of a linear transformation. Multiplication is defined as row against column, summed, precisely so that two transformations in a row collapse into a single matrix, doing B and then A being the same as doing AB, which is also why a product needs the columns of the first factor to match the rows of the second. Keep two things apart: the multiplication is not commutative in general, since AB and BA often differ, so the order has to be stated; and a matrix is not a determinant, the latter being just a single number computed from a square matrix.

Why does the arithmetic–geometric mean inequality (AM-GM) hold, and when is equality attained?

因对数严格凹，詹森不等式给出 ln[(x_1+…+x_n)/n] ≥ [ln x_1+…+ln x_n]/n = ln((x_1⋯x_n)^(1/n))，取指数即知算术平均不小于几何平均。凹性严格，等号当且仅当诸数全相等。此式是极值问题的基石：总和给定时均分使乘积最大，乘积给定时均分使总和最小。
Since the logarithm is strictly concave, Jensen's inequality applied to positive numbers x_1,…,x_n gives ln[(x_1+…+x_n)/n] ≥ (ln x_1+…+ln x_n)/n = ln((x_1⋯x_n)^(1/n)); exponentiating both sides yields AM ≥ GM. Strict concavity forces equality if and only if all the numbers are equal. The inequality is a cornerstone of extremal problems: for a fixed sum, equal numbers maximize the product, and for a fixed product, equal numbers minimize the sum.

See 1/2 as 'half'; then (1/2)×(1/3) is 'a third of a half'—the whole split into 2×3=6 equal parts, taking 1, gives 1/6. Algebraically a/b·c/d=(ac)/(bd) is exactly what you get by cross-combining the b and d equal divisions, so numerators and denominators multiply separately.
