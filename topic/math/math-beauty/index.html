曼德博集的边缘为什么既美丽又无限复杂？

Why is the boundary of the Mandelbrot set both beautiful and infinitely complex?

它来自迭代z→z²+c，把不发散的复数c染成图案。边界在任何放大倍率下都出现新的自相似细节，这种无限复杂正是混沌与分形的标志。
It comes from iterating z→z²+c, coloring the c values that do not escape. At any zoom the boundary reveals new self-similar detail, the signature of chaos and fractals.

海岸线到底有多长，为什么答案取决于尺子？

How long is a coastline, and why does the answer depend on your ruler?

理查德森发现量得越细，海岸线越长，因为它有自相似的不规则。曼德博由此提出分形，用分形维数描述这种尺度相关的复杂长度。
Richardson found finer measurements give longer coastlines because of self-similar irregularity. Mandelbrot then proposed fractals, using fractal dimension to describe such scale-dependent length.

彭罗斯镶嵌只用两种菱形，为什么永远不重复？

Why does a Penrose tiling with just two rhombus shapes never repeat?

1974年彭罗斯用两种菱形按匹配规则铺平面，得到的图案具有五重对称却不具备平移周期，即非周期密铺。它后来还启发了准晶体的发现。
In 1974 Penrose tiled the plane with two rhombi under matching rules, producing fivefold symmetry with no translational period, a non-periodic tiling. It later inspired the discovery of quasicrystals.

海螺壳的螺旋为什么常常接近黄金螺旋？

Why do many seashells approximate a golden spiral?

海螺按固定比例持续生长，每圈放大相同倍数，形成等角螺线；当放大比接近黄金比时就接近黄金螺旋。这种自相似让它不论大小都形状一致。
A shell grows at a fixed ratio, scaling each turn by the same factor, forming a logarithmic spiral; near the golden ratio it approaches a golden spiral. Self-similarity keeps its shape at any size.

雪花为什么大多是六边形的？

Why are snowflakes mostly six-sided?

冰的晶体由水分子按六方晶格排列，氢键决定了六重对称。每片雪花在六条对称轴上生长分枝，所以天然是六角形。
Ice crystals arrange water molecules in a hexagonal lattice; hydrogen bonds fix sixfold symmetry. Each flake grows branches along those six axes, so it is naturally hexagonal.

为什么人类普遍觉得左右对称的东西好看？

Why do humans generally find left-right symmetry beautiful?

左右对称意味着身体结构完整、健康，进化让我们把对称当作可靠信号。数学上它对应反射对称，规律本身也带来舒适感。
Bilateral symmetry signals physical integrity and health, so evolution made us read it as trustworthy. Mathematically it is reflection symmetry, and order itself feels pleasing.

壁纸上的重复花纹，数学上算几种对称？

Mathematically, how many symmetry types do repeating wallpaper patterns have?

平面上的周期图案只有17种基本的壁纸群，穷尽了旋转、平移、反射、滑移反射的所有组合。看似千变万化的花纹，其实逃不出这17种。
Periodic plane patterns fall into just 17 wallpaper groups, exhausting all combinations of rotation, translation, reflection and glide reflection. Endless-looking designs are confined to these 17.

科赫雪花为什么面积是有限的、周长却是无限的？

Why does the Koch snowflake have finite area but infinite perimeter?

每一步把线段中间三分之一外凸成小三角，周长每次乘4/3不断变长，趋于无穷；而新增面积几何级数收敛，总面积有上限。它是分形的经典例子。
Each step replaces a third of every segment with a bump, multiplying perimeter by 4/3 toward infinity, while added area forms a convergent geometric series with a finite limit. A classic fractal.

谢尔宾斯基三角形怎么用不断挖洞得到？

How do you get a Sierpinski triangle by repeatedly removing holes?

从一个实心三角形开始，挖掉中央倒三角，再对剩下的三个小三角重复同样操作，无限做下去就得到处处有洞的谢尔宾斯基三角形。
Start with a solid triangle, remove the central upside-down one, then repeat on the three remaining smaller triangles; infinite repetition yields the hole-filled Sierpinski triangle.

骰子相对两面的点数为什么总是加起来等于7？

Why do opposite faces of a die always sum to 7?

标准骰子把1对6、2对5、3对4，这样无论怎么放，六面之和都平衡，滚动更公平也更对称。这是一种巧妙的对称设计。
A standard die pairs 1–6, 2–5, 3–4, so opposite faces sum to 7; this balances the six sides for fairer, more symmetric rolling. A neat symmetric design.

教堂的彩色玻璃窗为什么常用旋转对称？

Why do church stained-glass windows often use rotational symmetry?

把相同花纹绕中心转一定角度仍重合，画面既庄重又平衡。旋转对称是分形与镶嵌之外，人类常用来表达秩序之美的方式之一。
Repeating the same motif around a center by a fixed angle keeps the image balanced and solemn. Rotational symmetry is, beside fractals and tessellation, one of the ways humans commonly use to express orderly beauty.

莫比乌斯带只有一个面，怎么用纸条验证？

A Möbius strip has only one side; how can you verify it with paper?

把纸条一端扭转180度再粘成环，用笔画线沿中线一直画，会回到起点并涂满整条带子，说明它只有一个面、一条边。
Twist a strip 180° and tape it into a loop, then draw a center line all the way around; it returns to start having covered the whole surface, proving one side and one edge.
