图同构问题是什么？

What is the graph isomorphism problem?

它问两幅图是否只是顶点重命名后的同一结构。该问题既未被证明为NP完全，也未被证明有高效算法，是计算复杂性中的著名灰色地带。
It asks whether two graphs are the same up to renaming vertices. Despite huge effort, it is neither proved NP-complete nor known easy—a famous gray zone of complexity.

库拉托夫斯基定理是什么？

What is Kuratowski's theorem?

它给出平面图的特征：一个图是平面的，当且仅当它不含可收缩为 K₅（五顶点完全图）或 K₃,₃（三分图）的子图。这为平面性提供了判定标准。
A graph is planar iff it contains no subgraph contractible to K₅ or K₃,₃. This gives a clean criterion for planarity.

What is a Hamiltonian cycle?

哈密顿回路是经过每个顶点恰好一次并回到起点的回路。寻找它属于 NP 完全问题，与欧拉回路（走边）不同，它要求走遍顶点。
A Hamiltonian cycle visits every vertex exactly once and returns; finding one is NP-complete, unlike Euler circuits which traverse edges.

什么是无向图的最大流—最小割定理?它和线性规划的对偶性有何联系?

State the max-flow min-cut theorem for undirected networks and relate it to linear programming duality.

定理断言网络中 s 到 t 的最大流量等于把 s 与 t 分开的最小割的容量。将最大流写成线性规划后,其对偶问题恰是最小割的整数规划松弛,且约束矩阵的全单模性保证对偶最优解取整值,从而对偶间隙为零——这是 LP 对偶在组合优化中最经典的实例之一。
The theorem says the maximum flow from s to t equals the minimum capacity of an s-t cut. Formulating max-flow as a linear program, its dual is precisely the relaxation of the min-cut integer program; total unimodularity of the constraint matrix guarantees integral dual optima, so there is no duality gap — one of the classic instances of LP duality in combinatorial optimization.

最短路径算法（如 Dijkstra）做什么？

What does a shortest-path algorithm (e.g. Dijkstra) do?

它在带权图上求从起点到其余各点的最短距离。做法是贪心：每轮从没定下最终距离的点里挑当前距离最小的一个钉死，再用它松弛相邻边。这一步成立的前提是边权全部非负；一旦出现负权边就可能给出错的答案，那得换 Bellman–Ford。代价看实现：朴素做法的开销随顶点数的平方增长，配最小堆只多出一个对数因子。它常被混成旅行商问题，其实后者至今没有已知的多项式算法。
It finds the shortest distances from a source to every other node in a weighted graph. The method is greedy: each round it fixes the unsettled vertex with the smallest current distance and relaxes its outgoing edges. That step is valid only when every edge weight is non-negative; a single negative edge can break the answer, and that is where Bellman-Ford belongs. Cost depends on implementation: the naive version grows with the square of the number of vertices, while a min-heap adds only a logarithmic factor. It often gets confused with the travelling salesman problem, which is the one with no known polynomial-time algorithm.

什么是无标度网络的幂律度分布？为什么这类网络中枢纽节点的存在会显著缩短平均路径长度？

What is the power-law degree distribution of a scale-free network, and why do hub nodes in such networks dramatically shorten average path lengths?

无标度网络的度分布满足 P(k) ∝ k^(−γ)，节点连接数没有典型尺度，少数高度数的枢纽与大量低度数节点共存。随机游走或最短路经过枢纽即可实现大跨度跳跃，使任意两点间的距离主要由“到枢纽的一两步”构成，故平均路径长度随网络增长仅呈对数级上升。
A scale-free network has degree distribution P(k) ∝ k^(−γ): there is no typical node degree, and a few high-degree hubs coexist with many low-degree nodes. Since shortest paths can hop across large distances via one or two hubs, distances between arbitrary nodes consist mostly of steps toward a hub, so average path length grows only logarithmically with network size.

地球上任意两个人之间真的大约只要六次朋友的朋友就能连上吗?

小世界实验给出过耐人寻味的支持:1967年米尔格拉姆让内布拉斯加的志愿者把信件只交给认识的人转寄,平均经五六人到达波士顿目标。1998年沃茨与斯托加茨用数学解释了它为何可能——只需在大量「近邻连接」中掺入极少数随机远程连接,网络的平均最短路径就会骤降,同时局部抱团结构保留。社交网络时代的亿级数据分析显示平均链长约四五跳,但「可达」不等于「可达且愿意转发」,六度更多揭示了人类关系网的稀疏枢纽结构,而非保证人人都能借道。
The small-world experiment offers suggestive support: in 1967 Milgram had Nebraska volunteers forward letters only through acquaintances, reaching Boston targets in five or six hops on average. In 1998 Watts and Strogatz explained why this is possible — adding just a few random long-range links to a heavily local network collapses average shortest paths while keeping tight clusters. Billion-scale social-network data puts mean chain length around four to six hops, yet reachability is not willingness-to-forward; six degrees exposes the sparse hub structure of human networks rather than guaranteeing any route.

完全不靠计算机,数学家怎样证明五种颜色必定够用?同样的论证又为何推不到四种颜色?

Without any computer at all, how did mathematicians prove that five colors always suffice, and why does the very same argument fall short at four?

平面图必有度数不超过5的顶点:先删掉它,把剩下的图递归地染好五色,再把顶点放回。若五个邻居没占满五种颜色,补上空色即可;若占满了,就在两种颜色的子图中沿连通块做肯普链交换,总能腾出一个颜色——五色定理由此纯手工成立。而染四色时,度5顶点的五个邻居只分得四种颜色,两条交换链会在交叉之处互相拆台,肯普1879年的证明恰恰漏在这里。
Every planar graph has a vertex of degree at most five: delete it, color the remaining graph recursively with five colors, and put the vertex back. If its five neighbors fail to use all five colors, fill in a free one; if they do, perform a Kempe-chain swap along connected components of some two colors and a slot always opens up — which proves the five-color theorem entirely by hand. With only four colors, however, the five neighbors share four colors and two swapping chains can cross and undo each other; Kempe's 1879 proof broke precisely here.

#肯普链 #五色定理 #平面图着色

肯普1879年的四色定理证明风光了十一年才被推翻——漏洞藏在哪里？它为何反而带来了五色定理？

Kempe announced a four color theorem proof in 1879 that stood for eleven years before being refuted — where did the flaw lie, and how did it still deliver the five color theorem?

漏洞藏在肯普设计的「肯普链」颜色交换操作里：他声称交换两种颜色总能消去一种多余的颜色，希伍德1890年举出反例，说明某些构型下两次交换会互相干扰、无法完成。不过整套方法只需放宽到五种颜色就完全成立，五色定理由此诞生。这段历史也表明，一个流传十余年、备受推崇的「证明」，仍可能埋藏着只在罕见情形才触发的缺陷。
The flaw hid in the color-switching move known as a Kempe chain: Kempe claimed that swapping two colors always eliminates one surplus color, but in 1890 Heawood exhibited configurations where two such swaps interfere and cannot be carried out. Relax the claim to five colors, however, and the whole machinery works perfectly — hence the five color theorem. The episode shows that even a celebrated proof admired for over a decade can harbor defects triggered only in rare cases.

#图论 #小世界

哥尼斯堡的七座桥为什么注定无法一次走遍而不重复?

Why was Konigsberg's seven-bridge walk doomed from the start?

1736年欧拉把陆地缩成点、桥画成线,发现能否一次走完只取决于每个点连出的线条数(度数):要想一笔不重复地走完,中途经过的点必须成进成出即度数为偶,只有起点和终点允许奇数。哥尼斯堡四块陆地全是奇度(3、3、3、5),一个奇点都不将就,所以任何路线必然重复某座桥。这项分析诞生了图论与拓扑学的思想雏形——问题的答案藏在连接方式里,与桥的长短宽窄毫无关系。
In 1736 Euler shrank each landmass to a point and each bridge to a line, showing that a single non-repeating tour depends only on how many lines meet at each point (its degree): every intermediate stop needs an even degree, with only start and end allowed odd. All four Konigsberg landmasses had odd degrees (3, 3, 3, 5), so some bridge must be repeated. That argument launched graph theory and prefigured topology — connectivity alone decides, lengths and widths are irrelevant.

#图论 #欧拉路径 #一笔画

为什么涂色地图的问题可以原封不动地搬进图论,变成给平面图的顶点染色?

Why can the map-coloring problem be translated wholesale into graph theory as coloring the vertices of a planar graph?

把每个国家看成一个点,两国接壤就连一条边,地图立刻化为一幅平面图;相邻国家不同色,恰好等价于相邻顶点不同色。转化之后,边界的歪斜形状、飞地之类的几何细节全部消失,只剩下纯粹的连接关系,还能借助欧拉公式推出平面图必有度数不超过5的顶点这样的利器。四色问题的全部现代研究,正是在这张对偶图上展开的。
Turn every country into a dot and join two dots whenever the countries share a border: the map becomes a planar graph, and requiring neighboring countries to differ in color is exactly requiring adjacent vertices to differ. After the translation, crooked borders and enclaves vanish, leaving pure connectivity, and tools such as Euler's formula apply — for instance, that every planar graph has a vertex of degree at most five. All modern work on the four-color problem unfolds on this dual graph.
