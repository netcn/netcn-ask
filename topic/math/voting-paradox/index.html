阿罗的不可能性定理究竟排除了什么——为什么选项达到三个及以上时，没有任何投票规则能同时守住非独裁等几条看似温和的公理？

Just what does Arrow's impossibility theorem rule out — why can no voting rule uphold non-dictatorship together with a few seemingly mild axioms once three or more options are on the table?

它排除了完美选举规则的存在。阿罗要求规则同时做到：接受任意个人排序（无限制定义域）、全体一致时从众（帕累托原则）、两候选间的胜负不受第三者进出影响（无关备择独立性），且无人拥有单方面定夺权（非独裁）；他1951年证明这些条件在三个及以上选项时必然冲突，总有某个人的偏好成为群体排序。这宣告任何投票制度都可能在特定偏好组合下失灵或遭策略操纵，机制设计只能权衡取舍。
It rules out the existence of a perfect electoral rule. Arrow asked a rule to accept any profile of individual rankings (unrestricted domain), follow unanimity (the Pareto principle), keep the contest between two candidates unaffected when a third enters or quits (independence of irrelevant alternatives), and grant nobody unilateral say (non-dictatorship). His 1951 proof shows these demands collide irreconcilably once three or more options exist: somebody's ranking always becomes society's. Every voting system thus breaks down or invites strategic manipulation under some configuration, leaving designers nothing but trade-offs.

#阿罗不可能定理 #社会选择 #无关备择独立性

选民把票投给并非自己最爱之人以扭转局势，这种策略性投票有可能被制度设计彻底杜绝吗？哪条定理断言：只要选项不少于三个，任何非独裁的规则都无法免疫操纵？

Voters backing someone other than their true favorite to swing the outcome — can institutional design ever stamp out such tactical voting? And which theorem asserts that once options number three or more, no non-dictatorial rule is immune to manipulation?

无法彻底杜绝——这正是吉伯德-萨特思韦特定理：当选项不少于三个时，任何满射且非独裁的投票规则必然存在被操纵的空间，即总能找到一类偏好分布，使某位选民谎报排序比诚实投票拿到更好的结果。它与阿罗不可能定理互为表里：阿罗排除了完美的集体排序，这条定理则直接封死防操纵规则的生存空间，现实选举只能在容忍策略行为与其他缺陷之间权衡取舍。
It cannot be stamped out — that is the Gibbard-Satterthwaite theorem: with three or more options, every onto and non-dictatorial voting rule leaves room for manipulation, meaning some preference profile always exists under which a voter gains by misreporting a ranking instead of voting honestly. It stands back to back with Arrow's impossibility theorem: Arrow ruled out a perfect collective ranking, while this result closes off manipulation-proof rules altogether, leaving real elections to weigh tolerated strategic behavior against other defects.

#策略性投票 #防操纵 #吉伯德-萨特思韦特定理

三位投票人面对三位候选人，多数决竟可能推出A胜B、B胜C、C又胜A的死循环——这一投票悖论叫什么名字？它暴露了多数决的哪个根本弱点？

With three voters choosing among three candidates, majority rule can produce a dead loop where A beats B, B beats C, yet C beats A — what is this voting paradox called, and what fundamental weakness of majority rule does it expose?

它叫孔多塞悖论，也叫投票循环。设三人偏好依次为A优于B优于C、B优于C优于A、C优于A优于B，则两两对决时A以二比一胜B，B以二比一胜C，C再以二比一胜A，群体偏好绕成闭环，选不出众望所归的赢家。这表明多数决无法把个人排序整合成一致的集体排序，而且议程安排权足以操纵胜负——控制先比哪一组，就能定制最后的赢家。
It is the Condorcet paradox, also called the voting cycle. Let the three voters rank A over B over C, B over C over A, and C over A over B. In head-to-head contests A beats B two to one, B beats C the same way, then C beats A, looping the collective preference into a closed ring with no consensus winner. Majority rule thus cannot weld individual rankings into one consistent social ordering, and agenda control becomes power itself: fix the order of the pairwise duels and you can manufacture whichever winner you want.
