房贷等额本息和等额本金，区别到底在哪？

What is the difference between equal-installment and equal-principal mortgages?

等额本息每月还款额固定，前期还的利息多；等额本金每月本金相同、月供递减，总利息更少但前期压力大。本质是两种不同的还款现金流。
Equal-installment keeps the monthly payment fixed with more interest early; equal-principal pays the same principal each month so payments shrink and total interest is lower, but early payments are higher. They are different cash-flow schedules.

等额本金一定比等额本息更省钱吗？

Is equal-principal always cheaper than equal-installment?

单看利息总额，等额本金更少。但若把前期多还的钱拿去投资、且收益高于贷款利率，等额本息反而更灵活。是否省钱取决于你的资金机会成本。
Equal-principal has less total interest, but if you invest the extra early payments at a return above the loan rate, equal-installment can be more flexible. Cheaper depends on your opportunity cost.

#理财 #房贷对比

无风险年利率为5%，按连续复利计。某股票现价为100，一年后价格只可能变为120或95。用单期二叉树模型计算执行价为105的一年期欧式看涨期权的无套利价格，并给出相应的复制策略。

With a continuously compounded risk-free rate of 5% per year, a stock priced at 100 today can only move to 120 or 95 in one year. Using the one-period binomial model, compute the no-arbitrage price of a one-year European call with strike 105, and describe the replicating strategy.

上升因子u=1.2，下降因子d=0.95，风险中性概率p=(e^0.05−d)/(u−d)=(1.0513−0.95)/0.25≈0.4051。期权到期支付在上涨态为120−105=15、下跌态为0，故价格=e^(−0.05)×0.4051×15≈5.78。这与复制策略一致：买入Δ=15/(100×0.25)=0.6股股票，同时借入约54.22的资金，今日净支出同样是5.78，任何价格偏离都会被套利抹平。
With up factor u = 1.2 and down factor d = 0.95, the risk-neutral probability is p = (e^0.05 − d)/(u − d) = (1.0513 − 0.95)/0.25 ≈ 0.4051. The payoff is 15 in the up state and 0 in the down state, so the call price equals e^(−0.05) × 0.4051 × 15 ≈ 5.78. This coincides with the replicating portfolio: buy Δ = 15/(100 × 0.25) = 0.6 shares financed by borrowing about 54.22, leaving the same net outlay of 5.78 today; any deviation would open an arbitrage.

连续复利公式 A=Pe^{rt} 是怎么来的？

How does the continuous-compounding formula A=Pe^{rt} arise?

把一年分成 n 段复利，极限 n→∞ 时 (1+r/n)^{nt}→e^{rt}，于是本金 P 在利率 r、时间 t 后变为 Pe^{rt}。这是 e 在金融中的自然登场。
Splitting a year into n compounding periods, as n→∞ we get (1+r/n)^{nt}→e^{rt}, so principal P becomes Pe^{rt} at rate r over time t. This is e's natural entrance into finance.

信用卡只还最低还款额，利息是怎么算的？

How does interest work if you only pay the minimum on a credit card?

未还清部分按日利率（约万分之五）从消费当天起全额计息，且下月利滚利。长期只还最低额，实际年化成本可能超过18%。
The unpaid balance accrues daily interest (about 0.05%) from the purchase date and compounds monthly. Paying only the minimum can mean an effective annual cost above 18%.

#理财 #复利

为什么叫72法则，它能告诉我们钱多久翻倍？

What is the rule of 72 and how does it estimate doubling time?

用72除以年化利率百分数，就约等于本金翻一倍所需的年数。例如年利8%，大约9年翻倍。它是复利的对数近似。
Divide 72 by the annual interest rate in percent to approximate the years needed to double your money. At 8% that is about 9 years. It is a logarithmic approximation of compounding.

保险公司是怎么靠概率赚钱的？

How do insurance companies make money from probability?

公司用大数定律把众多投保人的小额保费汇聚起来，赔付只是其中一部分，并预留利润与安全边际。只要事故概率估计准确，就能稳定盈利。
By the law of large numbers, many small premiums pool into a fund where payouts are only a fraction, with profit and safety margins reserved. Accurate risk estimates make the business steady.

#理财 #保险

期权定价为什么可以归结为一个偏微分方程?Black-Scholes 公式背后的核心思想是什么?

Why does option pricing reduce to a partial differential equation, and what is the core idea behind the Black-Scholes formula?

Black-Scholes 的核心是无套利原则下的动态对冲:通过连续买卖标的股票构造瞬时风险为零的组合,其收益必须恰好等于无风险利率,否则套利者会立刻抹平偏差。这一复制论证把期权价格约束成一条抛物型偏微分方程,解出的闭式表达式即为欧式看涨期权的定价公式。模型假定股价服从波动率恒定的几何布朗运动,Merton 与 Scholes 也因此获得 1997 年诺贝尔经济学奖。
The heart of Black-Scholes is dynamic hedging under the no-arbitrage principle: continuously trading the underlying stock builds a portfolio that is instantaneously riskless, so its return must equal the risk-free rate or arbitragers immediately erase the gap. This replication argument pins the option price down through a parabolic PDE whose closed-form solution prices a European call. The model assumes geometric Brownian motion with constant volatility, and Merton and Scholes were awarded the 1997 Nobel Memorial Prize in Economic Sciences for it.

#期权定价 #无套利原理

把100元存一年，年利率10%，年底有多少钱？如果每个月复利一次呢？

Put 100 yuan in the bank at 10% annual interest. How much at year end? What if compounded monthly?

一年一次复利得 100×1.10=110 元；按月复利得 100×(1+0.10/12)^12≈110.47 元。复利次数越多，最终金额略高，极限是连续复利 100×e^0.10≈110.52 元。
Annual compounding gives 100×1.10=110. Monthly gives 100×(1+0.10/12)^12≈110.47. More frequent compounding yields slightly more, converging to continuous compounding 100×e^0.10≈110.52.
