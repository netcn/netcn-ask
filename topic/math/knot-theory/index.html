在纽结理论中,右手三叶结的琼斯多项式是什么?如何用它判断三叶结与其镜像是否等价?

In knot theory, what is the Jones polynomial of the right-handed trefoil knot, and how can it be used to decide whether the trefoil is equivalent to its mirror image?

在标准归一化下,右手三叶结的琼斯多项式为 V(t)=t+t^3-t^4。把 t 替换为 t^(-1) 就得到镜像纽结的不变量 V(t)=t^(-1)+t^(-3)-t^(-4),两者并不相同。既然多项式不同,琼斯多项式便检测出手性差异,从而证明三叶结无法在三维空间中连续变形为自己的镜像。
Under the standard normalization the right-handed trefoil has Jones polynomial V(t) = t + t^3 - t^4. Substituting t -> t^-1 yields the invariant of its mirror image, V(t) = t^-1 + t^-3 - t^-4, which is different. Since the polynomials disagree, the Jones polynomial detects chirality, proving that the trefoil cannot be continuously deformed into its mirror image in three-dimensional space.

#纽结理论 #琼斯多项式

把绳子首尾相接得到一条封闭曲线后，“绳上是否真的打了结”就成为一个严格的数学问题。什么样的两条封闭曲线才算同一个纽结？最简单的三叶结为什么永远无法解开成无结的圆圈？

Once a rope's ends are joined into a closed curve, whether there is a genuine knot becomes a precise mathematical question. When do two closed curves count as the same knot, and why can the simplest one, the trefoil, never be untangled into an unknotted circle?

纽结理论研究圆周在三维空间中的嵌入：若存在保持外围空间的整体同胚把一条曲线映到另一条，二者就是同一个纽结。区分纽结依赖不变量，如亚历山大多项式、纽结群和琼斯多项式；三叶结的琼斯多项式既不同于无结圆圈的常数1，也不同于其镜像所对应的多项式，因此它是真正的纽结并具有手征性。这些不变量后来还在统计力学与量子场论中找到了意想不到的联系。
Knot theory studies embeddings of the circle into three-dimensional space: two knots are identical when an ambient homeomorphism carries one onto the other. Distinguishing them relies on invariants such as the Alexander polynomial, the knot group, and the Jones polynomial; the trefoil's Jones polynomial differs both from the constant 1 of the unknotted circle and from that of its mirror image, so it is genuinely knotted and chiral. These invariants later turned out to have unexpected connections to statistical mechanics and quantum field theory.
