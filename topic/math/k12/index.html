为什么1/3等于0.333…，但三乘0.333…却等于1？

Why is 1/3 equal to 0.333…, and three times that equals 1?

1/3写成小数就是0.333…无限循环，三份加起来是0.999…，而0.999…恰好等于1。无限循环让它没有缺掉的最后一小截。
1/3 is the repeating decimal 0.333…, and three of them sum to 0.999…, which equals 1 exactly. The infinite repeat leaves no missing final sliver.

#中小学 #无限小数

国际象棋盘上放麦粒，为什么最后会多到吓人？

Why do wheat grains on a chessboard become frighteningly many?

第一格1粒、第二格2粒、每格翻倍，到第64格是2的63次方粒。总数约1.8×10的19次方粒，远超全球年产量，这就是指数增长的威力。
One grain on the first square, two on the second, doubling each time; the 64th square alone has 2^63 grains. The total, about 1.8e19, dwarfs world harvests, showing exponential growth.

#中小学 #指数增长

为什么圆周率既不是整数也不是分数？

Why is pi neither an integer nor a fraction?

1761年兰伯特证明π是无理数，不能写成分数，小数无限不循环。后来还证明它是超越数，所以尺规作图也无法化圆为方。
Lambert proved in 1761 that pi is irrational, not expressible as a fraction with non-repeating decimals. It is also transcendental, which is why squaring the circle by straightedge is impossible.

#中小学 #圆周率

负负得正，怎么直观理解？

Why does a negative times a negative give a positive, intuitively?

可以把负号看作方向反转：欠债是负，再取消一笔欠债就是收入，即正。或想成数轴上连续两次反向，回到正方向。
Read the minus sign as a reversal of direction: debt is negative, cancelling a debt is income, hence positive. Or on a number line, two reversals bring you back to the positive direction.

#中小学 #负数

负数能开平方吗？

Can you take the square root of a negative number?

在实数范围内不能，因为平方总非负。但引入虚数单位i（i²=−1）后，负数也能开平方，比如√−4=2i，这打开了复数世界。
Not within real numbers, since squares are never negative. But with the imaginary unit i where i²=−1, negatives get square roots, e.g. √−4=2i, opening the world of complex numbers.

#中小学 #复数

用拼图怎么理解(a+b)的平方？

How does a puzzle picture explain (a+b) squared?

把边长为a+b的大正方形分成四块：a²、b²和两个ab的长方形，加起来就是a²+2ab+b²。图形一摆，公式就自己出来了。
A big square of side a+b splits into a², b² and two rectangles ab, totaling a²+2ab+b². Lay out the pieces and the formula appears by itself.

#中小学 #代数直观

钟表和算盘为什么用十二进制而不是十进制？

Why do clocks and the abacus use base 12 rather than base 10?

12能被2、3、4、6整除，分起东西来比10更方便，所以一小时分12刻、一打是12个沿用至今。算盘上珠以一代五，也暗合这种分法。
12 divides by 2,3,4,6, making sharing easier than 10, so an hour has 12 parts and a dozen is 12. The abacus upper bead standing for five also fits this scheme.

#中小学 #进制

为什么三角形面积等于底乘高除以二？

Why is a triangle's area base times height divided by two?

两个完全相同的三角形可以拼成一个平行四边形，平行四边形面积是底乘高，所以一个三角形就取其一半。剪拼一下就能看明白。
Two identical triangles form a parallelogram whose area is base times height, so one triangle is half of that. Cut and rearrange to see it.

#中小学 #几何直观

为什么任何非零数的0次方都等于1？

Why is any nonzero number to the power 0 equal to 1?

看规律：2的3、2、1次方是8、4、2，每降一次除以2，那么2的0次方自然是1，规律才连续。也可由a^m÷a^m=1且等于a^(m−m)=a^0推出。
Look at the pattern: 2^3,2^2,2^1 are 8,4,2, halving each step, so 2^0 must be 1 to keep it continuous. Or a^m÷a^m=1 equals a^(m−m)=a^0.

#中小学 #指数

为什么除以一个分数，等于乘以它的倒数？

Why is dividing by a fraction the same as multiplying by its reciprocal?

除以一个数就是乘以它的倒数，分数也不例外。比如÷1/2等于×2，相当于问这个数里包含几个二分之一，自然翻倍。
Dividing by a number means multiplying by its reciprocal, and fractions are no exception. Dividing by 1/2 equals times 2, asking how many halves fit, which doubles the amount.

#中小学 #分数

温度计上的负数该怎么读？

How do you read negative numbers on a thermometer?

零度以下就是负数，比如零下5度写作−5℃，表示比0度还冷5度。负数在这里就是零以下的距离。
Below zero is negative; minus 5°C means 5 degrees colder than zero. Here negatives are simply distances below zero.

两点之间线段最短，怎么一眼就看出来？

Why is the straight line between two points shortest, at a glance?

把两点拉一根绷紧的绳子，它自然成直线，因为任何弯路都比直着走要长。这是几何里最直观的一条真理。
Pull a taut string between two points and it forms a straight line, since any detour is longer. It is the most intuitive truth in geometry.

#中小学 #几何直观

为什么0.5比0.25数字少，却更大？

Why is 0.5 bigger than 0.25 even though it has fewer digits?

小数看的是几分之几：0.5是二分之一，0.25是四分之一。半个比四分之一个当然大，数字位数多少和大小没关系。
Decimals are fractions: 0.5 is one half, 0.25 is one quarter. Half is more than a quarter, so the number of digits does not decide size.

#中小学 #小数

分数通分到底在做什么？

What are we really doing when we find a common denominator?

通分是把不同分母的分数换成相同的分母，好让它们分母一样、只比分子。本质是找公倍数作统一刻度，才能相加相减。
Finding a common denominator rewrites fractions with the same bottom number so you compare numerators only. It uses a common multiple as a shared scale, enabling addition and subtraction.
