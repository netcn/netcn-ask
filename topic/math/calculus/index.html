为什么很多复杂函数都能被多项式“逼真模仿”？

Why can many complicated functions be imitated very accurately by polynomials?

在一点附近，光滑函数 f 可以用它的泰勒级数表示：f(x)=Σ f^(k)(a)/k!·(x-a)^k。项越多，多项式在 a 附近对 f 的逼近越好——误差是 (x-a)^(n+1) 的高阶小量。例如 e^x=1+x+x²/2!+x³/3!+…，取前几项就足够精确。这正是计算器算 sin、e^x 等函数的方式：把超越函数换成可加减乘除的多项式。
Near a point, a smooth function f equals its Taylor series: f(x)=Σ f^(k)(a)/k!·(x-a)^k. More terms give better approximation of f near a, with error of order (x-a)^(n+1). For instance e^x=1+x+x²/2!+x³/3!+…, and the first few terms already suffice. This is exactly how calculators evaluate sin, e^x, and other transcendental functions — by replacing them with polynomials built from addition and multiplication.

“趋于”这种模糊说法怎么才能变成精确的数学定义？

How can the vague phrase “approaches” be turned into a precise mathematical definition?

柯西和魏尔斯特拉斯用 ε-δ 语言做到这一点：称 lim f(x)=L（当 x→a）当且仅当，对任意小的误差容忍度 ε>0，都能找到对应的 δ>0，使所有满足 0<|x-a|<δ 的 x 都有 |f(x)-L|<ε。这里 ε 先给定、δ 随之选择，“任意 ε”保证无限逼近，“存在 δ”保证确实能做到。整个微积分的严格化都以这个定义为基石。
Cauchy and Weierstrass achieved it with ε-δ language: lim f(x)=L as x→a means that for every tolerance ε>0, however small, there exists a δ>0 such that whenever 0<|x-a|<δ, one has |f(x)-L|<ε. The ε is chosen first and δ is found for it; “for all ε” forces arbitrarily close approach, “there exists δ” guarantees it is attainable. The entire rigorization of calculus rests on this definition.

斯托克斯定理为何被看作高维的微积分基本定理？

Why is Stokes' theorem viewed as a higher-dimensional Fundamental Theorem of Calculus?

斯托克斯定理把一个曲面边界上的线积分等于曲面内旋度的面积分，统一了格林、高斯等经典公式。它把沿边界的积分与区域内部的微分联系起来，是微分形式理论的雏形。斯皮瓦克与芒克斯在流形分析教材中把这一思想推广到任意维。
Stokes' theorem equates the line integral around a surface's boundary to the surface integral of the curl, unifying Green's and Gauss's classical formulas. It links an integral over a boundary to the derivative inside the region and foreshadows differential forms. Spivak and Munkres extend this idea to arbitrary dimensions in their manifold texts.

多元链式法则如何用雅可比矩阵表达多个变量的复合求导？

How does the multivariable chain rule express differentiation of compositions via Jacobian matrices?

当变量通过中间变量依赖另一些变量时，多元链式法则把外层梯度与内层雅可比矩阵相乘，得到总导数。它把一元链式法则推广到向量值函数，是优化与物理场变换的基础。斯皮瓦克在《流形上的微积分》中给出了清晰的处理。
When variables depend on others through intermediate variables, the multivariable chain rule multiplies the outer gradient by the inner Jacobian to give the total derivative. It generalizes the one-variable rule to vector-valued functions and underlies optimization and field transformations. Spivak presents it cleanly in Calculus on Manifolds.

What is the implicit function theorem?

The implicit function theorem: if ∂F/∂y≠0 near a point of F(x,y)=0, then y can be expressed locally as a unique function of x; it turns constraints into functions.

散度定理（高斯定理）怎样把“体积内的总源强”与“表面的总通量”联系起来？

How does the divergence theorem (Gauss's theorem) link 'total source strength inside a volume' to 'total flux through its surface'?

散度定理断言 ∭_V (∇·F) dV = ∬_∂V F·dS：向量场在体积 V 内的散度积分，等于它穿过边界 ∂V 的通量。它说明“内部产生的量”必等于“从边界流出的净量”，是静电学高斯定律与流体连续方程的数学核心，也是微积分基本定理向三维的推广。
The divergence theorem states ∭_V (∇·F) dV = ∬_∂V F·dS: the integral of a field's divergence over volume V equals its flux through the boundary ∂V. It says what is created inside must flow out across the boundary, underlying Gauss's law in electrostatics and fluid continuity, and generalizes the fundamental theorem to three dimensions.

一致连续是什么？

What is uniform continuity?

一致连续要求存在一个只依赖 ε 的 δ，使定义域内任意相距足够近的点函数值都接近。它与普通连续不同：普通连续允许 δ 随点的位置变化，一致连续则“统一”控制。
Uniform continuity needs a single δ(ε) working everywhere; unlike pointwise continuity, the allowed closeness doesn't depend on the location in the domain.

The gamma function is defined by an integral and satisfies Gamma(n+1) equals n factorial, continuously extending factorial to positive reals and beyond. Stirling's formula gives the factorial for large n asymptotically as the square root of 2πn times (n/e) to the n, a key tool in probability and combinatorics. Whittaker & Watson and Abramowitz & Stegun's Handbook record the standard results.

为什么弹簧振子的周期与振幅无关？

Why is a spring's period independent of amplitude?

由胡克定律，运动满足 mx''+kx=0，即 x''+ω²x=0（ω=√(k/m)）。这是一个线性常系数微分方程，通解 x(t)=A cos(ωt+φ) 中的频率完全由弹簧刚度 k 与质量 m 决定，与振幅 A 无关。这正是“等时性”，伽利略与惠更斯靠它设计出摆钟；一旦弹簧非线性，等时性就会被破坏。
By Hooke's law the motion satisfies mx''+kx=0, i.e., x''+ω²x=0 with ω=√(k/m). As a linear constant-coefficient equation its general solution x(t)=A cos(ωt+φ) has frequency determined solely by stiffness k and mass m—independent of the amplitude A. This is isochronism, the property Galileo and Huygens exploited in pendulum clocks; nonlinear springs break it.

牛顿迭代法为什么通常每走一步就能让答案的正确位数翻倍,它在什么情况下会失灵?

因为它具有二次收敛性:牛顿法用切线代替曲线,只要当前点足够靠近单根且导数不为零,下一步误差就约等于上一步误差的平方再乘一个常数,正确位数因此每步翻倍。但若初值离根太远,或根附近导数趋于零(如重根、平台区),切线方向会严重失准,迭代可能停滞、来回振荡甚至发散,这时需要换初值或改用阻尼牛顿法。
Because it converges quadratically: Newton's method replaces the curve with its tangent, and near a simple root with nonzero derivative the next error equals roughly a constant times the square of the previous error, so the count of correct digits doubles every step. If the starting guess is far away, or the derivative vanishes nearby as at multiple roots or flat regions, tangents misfire and iterates can stall, oscillate, or diverge; a fresh start or damping cures this.
