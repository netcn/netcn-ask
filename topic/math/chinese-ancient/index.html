祖暅原理说了什么？和卡瓦列里原理一样吗？

What does Zu Geng's principle say, and is it like Cavalieri's principle?

祖暅提出幂势既同则积不容异：夹在两平行面间的两个立体，若每一处的截面积都相等，则体积相等。这与西方卡瓦列里原理一致，早约1100年，用于求球体积。
Zu Geng stated that solids between parallel planes with equal cross-sectional area at every height have equal volume. This matches Cavalieri's principle, about 1100 years earlier, and was used to find the sphere's volume.

祖冲之是怎么把圆周率算到小数点后7位的？

How did Zu Chongzhi compute pi to seven decimal places?

He used Liu Hui's circle-cutting method, extending the inscribed polygon to 24576 sides and repeatedly applying the Pythagorean theorem, obtaining 3.1415926 < pi < 3.1415927, plus the approximations 22/7 and the remarkably accurate 355/113.

北宋沈括的“隙积术”怎样用“堆起来的东西”逼近高阶等差级数求和？

How did Shen Kuo's 11th-century 'xi ji shu' (gap-stacking) approximate sums of higher-order arithmetic series via stacked objects?

沈括在《梦溪笔谈》中提出“隙积术”，计算如酒坛层层堆成四棱台时“有空隙”的实体积：他用上底、下底、层数构造公式，处理的是二阶等差（方垛）求和，较此前“垛积术”更贴合实际堆叠。这是中国古代对高阶等差数列与体积近似的重要推进，早于欧洲同类讨论。
In 'Dream Pool Essays', Shen Kuo's 'xi ji shu' computes the real volume of objects stacked into a frustum (e.g., wine jars) with gaps, using top/base areas and layers to sum second-order arithmetic series—more practical than prior 'duo ji' stacking formulas. It advanced Chinese work on higher-order arithmetic progressions and volume approximation ahead of Europe.

杨辉三角和帕斯卡三角是同一个东西吗？

Is Yanghui's triangle the same as Pascal's triangle?

内容相同：每行数字是上一行相邻两数之和，用来展开二项式系数。杨辉在1261年《详解九章算法》中记载此图，比帕斯卡早约400年，所以中国称杨辉三角。
They are the same object: each entry is the sum of the two above it, giving binomial coefficients. Yang Hui documented it in 1261, about 400 years before Pascal, so in China it is called Yanghui's triangle.

《周髀算经》的“日高术”是怎样用影子长短和相似三角形测出太阳高度的？

How did the 'sun-height method' in the Zhoubi Suanjing measure the sun's height using shadow lengths and similar triangles?

《周髀算经》用“髀”（八尺表竿）立地测影，在不同地点同时量得影差，借助相似直角三角形（今称日高术公式 h = 表高×间距÷影差 + 表高）反推日高与日远。它是最早把勾股定理与比例关系用于天文测量的记录，体现了“以影测天”的中国古代数理天文思想。
The Zhoubi Suanjing set an 8-chi gnomon, measured shadow differences at two sites simultaneously, and used similar right triangles (the formula h = gnomon·distance/shadow-difference + gnomon) to infer the sun's height and distance. It is among the earliest records applying the Pythagorean relation and proportion to astronomy—'measuring heaven by shadow.'

为什么古中国用闰月来协调历法？这和数学有什么关系？

Why did ancient China use leap months, and what does that have to do with math?

一个朔望月约29.53天，12个月约354天，比回归年少约11天。通过19年7闰使历法与季节同步，背后是分数近似与同余思想的运用。
A lunar month is about 29.53 days, so 12 months miss the solar year by about 11 days. Inserting 7 leap months every 19 years keeps the calendar aligned, an application of fractional approximation and congruences.

《孙子算经》里的“物不知数”问题，怎样成了中国剩余定理的雏形？

How did the 'unknown number of objects' problem in the Sunzi Suanjing become a prototype of the Chinese remainder theorem?

《孙子算经》（约公元 4 世纪）问：“今有物不知其数，三三数之剩二，五五数之剩三，七七数之剩二，问几何？”它给出“三人同行七十稀，五树梅花廿一枝，七子团圆正月半”的口诀，用 70、21、15（各模其余两两互素数的特解）拼出答案，正是最早的中国剩余定理实例。
The 'Sunzi Suanjing' (c. 4th century) asks for a number that leaves remainders 2,3,2 when divided by 3,5,7, and gives the rhyme using 70,21,15 (special solutions modulo the co-prime counterparts) to assemble the answer—the earliest instance of the Chinese remainder theorem.

什么是割圆术？为什么边数越多越接近圆？

What is the circle-cutting method and why does more sides mean closer to a circle?

割圆术是刘徽提出的方法：用圆内接正多边形逼近圆周。边数越多，多边形每条边与圆弧之间的空隙越小，其周长就越接近圆周长。
Liu Hui's method approximates a circle with an inscribed regular polygon. As the number of sides grows, the gap between each polygon edge and the arc shrinks, so the perimeter approaches the circle's circumference.

How did ancient China represent zero?
