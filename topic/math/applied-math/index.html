什么是傅里叶变换？

What is the Fourier transform?

傅里叶变换把信号分解为不同频率的正弦波之和，广泛用于音频处理、图像压缩（如JPEG、MP3）和通信。
The Fourier transform decomposes a signal into sine waves of different frequencies; used in audio, image compression and signal processing.

纳维-斯托克斯方程是什么？

What are the Navier-Stokes equations?

它们描述粘性流体的运动，是流体力学的基本方程。证明其在一般情形下的光滑解存在性，是千禧年七大数学难题之一。
They describe viscous fluid motion, the core of fluid mechanics; proving smooth solutions exist in general is a Millennium Prize problem.

控制论研究什么？

What does control theory study?

控制论研究如何让系统按期望方式运行：用反馈不断修正误差，例如巡航定速和火箭姿态控制。核心是稳定性与最优化。
Control theory designs systems to behave as desired via feedback correcting error, e.g. cruise control and rocket stabilization; focus on stability.

香农信息论的基础思想是什么？

What is the core idea of Shannon's information theory?

信息论用“比特”度量信息量，越不确定的事件携带越多信息。基于此可定义信道容量与无损压缩极限，是现代通信的基石。
Information theory measures information in bits; more uncertain events carry more. It defines channel capacity and compression limits, basis of modern comms.

什么是微分方程？

What is a differential equation?

微分方程是含有未知函数及其导数的方程，用来描述物理、生物和工程中的变化规律，如人口增长、弹簧振动和天体运动。
A differential equation relates a function to its derivatives, modeling change in physics, biology and engineering.

阿罗不可能定理是什么？

What is Arrow's impossibility theorem?

阿罗定理说：在不少于三个选项时，不可能存在一种“公平”的投票规则同时满足完全序、无关选项独立、非独裁等几条看似合理的要求。它揭示了集体决策的固有矛盾。
Arrow's theorem: with ≥3 options no voting rule can satisfy a handful of reasonable criteria (ranking, independence of irrelevant alternatives, non-dictatorship) at once.

排队时多开一个窗口，为什么会快很多？

Why does opening one more checkout lane make the wait much shorter?

这是排队论：平均等待随服务台数增加而非线性下降，利用率越高时边际改善越大。所以从1个窗口加到2个，常比从5个加到6个更明显。
This is queueing theory: average wait drops nonlinearly as servers increase, and the gain is largest at high utilization. Going from 1 to 2 lanes often helps far more than from 5 to 6.

什么是复利？

What is compound interest?

复利是“利息也产生利息”的计算方式，公式为 A = P(1+r)^n。时间越长，增长越明显。
Compound interest earns interest on interest: A=P(1+r)^n; growth accelerates over time.

布莱克-斯科尔斯期权定价公式是什么？

What is the Black–Scholes option pricing formula?

它用几何布朗运动为期权定价，给出欧式看涨期权的封闭公式，核心变量是股价、行权价、期限、利率与波动率。1997 年获诺贝尔经济学奖。
The Black–Scholes formula prices European options via geometric Brownian motion, depending on price, strike, time, rate and volatility; it won the 1997 Nobel in Economics.

数值方法有什么用？

What are numerical methods used for?

很多方程无法写出精确解，数值方法用近似算法在计算机上求足够精确的解，如牛顿迭代求根、有限差分解方程。
When exact formulas fail, numerical methods compute accurate approximations, e.g. Newton's iteration for roots or finite differences for equations.

What is linear programming?

线性规划在线性约束下最大化或最小化一个线性目标函数，例如用最少成本调配资源。单纯形法是常用求解算法。
Linear programming optimizes a linear objective under linear constraints, e.g. cheapest resource allocation; solved by the simplex method.

How is math used when shopping?

买东西时用数学算总价、比较单价、找零和折扣。例如打八折就是原价乘以0.8。
Shopping uses math for totals, unit prices, change and discounts; a 20%-off sale means price × 0.8.

How is math used in cooking?

什么是斐波那契数列？

What is the Fibonacci sequence?

斐波那契数列从0、1开始，后面每项等于前两项之和：0,1,1,2,3,5,8,13…。它常出现在花瓣数和螺旋排列中。
The Fibonacci sequence starts 0,1 and each term is the sum of the previous two (0,1,1,2,3,5,8,13…), seen in petals and spirals.
