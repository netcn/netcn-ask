纳皮尔算筹（骨牌）是怎样帮人乘法的？

How did Napier's bones help with multiplication?

每根木条刻着一个数乘以1到9的结果，把对应木条排在一起，按对角线相加就能完成多位数乘法，是对数之前的手工计算神器。
Each rod shows a number times 1 through 9; lining up the rods and adding along diagonals performs multi-digit multiplication, a manual aid before logarithms.

对数计算尺为什么曾是工程师的标配？

Why was the slide rule once standard for engineers?

它把对数刻度刻在尺上，滑动就能做乘除和开方，速度快、便携。直到1970年代电子计算器普及，才渐渐退出舞台。
It engraved logarithmic scales so sliding performed multiplication, division and roots quickly and portably. It faded only when electronic calculators spread in the 1970s.

对数表是怎么让大航海更安全的？

How did logarithm tables make ocean navigation safer?

航海要用球面三角算经纬度，手工乘除极繁且易错。纳皮尔发明对数后，人们编出对数表，把复杂运算简化为加减，显著减少了导航错误。
Navigation needed spherical trigonometry with tedious, error-prone multiplication. After Napier's logarithms, printed tables reduced it to addition and subtraction, sharply cutting navigational mistakes.

为什么计算机算0.1加0.2不等于0.3？

Why does a computer get 0.1 + 0.2 not equal to 0.3?

计算机用二进制浮点数（IEEE 754）近似小数，而0.1、0.2在二进制下是无限循环小数，只能存近似值，累加后产生微小误差。这不是bug，而是表示的局限。
Computers use binary floating point (IEEE 754); 0.1 and 0.2 are repeating binaries stored only approximately, so their sum drifts. It is a representation limit, not a bug.

对数是怎么把乘法变成加法的？

How does a logarithm turn multiplication into addition?

对数满足log(ab)=log a+log b，于是两个数相乘可先取对数相加、再查反对数还原。这让没有计算器的时代也能快速算大数乘法。
Logarithms satisfy log(ab)=log a+log b, so to multiply you add the logs and then exponentiate back. This let people compute big products quickly before calculators.

算盘的进位思想和二进制有什么关系？

How is the abacus carry idea related to binary?

算盘每档满十进一，是十进制进位；二进制则是满二进一。两者都是位值制下的进位规则，只是基数不同，体现了进制的统一逻辑。
An abacus carries at ten per rod, decimal; binary carries at two. Both are place-value carry rules with different bases, showing the unified logic of numeral systems.

用尺规怎么把任意一个角平分？

How do you bisect any angle with compass and straightedge?

以顶点为圆心画弧交两边，再分别以两交点为圆心画等半径弧交于一点，连接顶点与该点即得角平分线。这是尺规作图的经典基本操作。
From the vertex draw an arc crossing both sides, then equal arcs from those crossings meet at a point; the line from vertex to that point bisects the angle. A classic straightedge-and-compass construction.

计算器内部为什么用二进制而不是十进制？

Why do calculators use binary internally instead of decimal?

电路只有开和关两种稳定状态，正好对应0和1，用二进制做逻辑与运算最简单可靠。显示时再转换回我们熟悉的十进制。
Circuits have two stable states, on and off, mapping naturally to 0 and 1, making binary logic simplest and most reliable. The result is converted back to decimal for display.

算盘上怎么表示很大的数和小数？

How does an abacus represent very large numbers and decimals?

算盘每根杆代表一个数位，靠拨珠表示0到9，数位向左延伸就能表示任意大的整数。小数则约定某根杆为小数点位，规则相同。
Each rod is a place value with beads for 0–9, so moving left extends to arbitrarily large integers. Decimals just fix one rod as the decimal point with the same rules.

世界上第一台机械计算器是谁造的？

Who built the first mechanical calculator?

1642年法国数学家帕斯卡为帮父亲算税，造出能加减的齿轮式计算器帕斯卡计算器。它开启了用机器代替手工计算的浪潮。
In 1642 the French mathematician Pascal built a geared adding-subtracting machine to help his tax-collector father. It launched the wave of mechanical calculation.
