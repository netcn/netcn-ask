解析延拓为什么能让黎曼 zeta 函数定义到实部小于 1 的区域？

Why does analytic continuation let the Riemann zeta function be defined where its real part is less than 1?

zeta 函数的级数 $\sum n^{-s}$ 仅在 $\operatorname{Re}(s)>1$ 收敛，但通过函数方程 $\zeta(s)=2^s\pi^{s-1}\sin(\pi s/2)\Gamma(1-s)\zeta(1-s)$ 可唯一解析延拓到整个复平面（除 $s=1$ 的极点外），从而赋予 $\zeta(-1)=-1/12$ 等数值以严格意义。
The series $\sum n^{-s}$ converges only for $\operatorname{Re}(s)>1$, but the functional equation $\zeta(s)=2^s\pi^{s-1}\sin(\pi s/2)\Gamma(1-s)\zeta(1-s)$ gives a unique analytic continuation to the whole plane except a pole at $s=1$, giving rigorous meaning to values like $\zeta(-1)=-1/12$.

留数定理如何把闭合围道积分转化为内部奇点的留数之和？

How does the residue theorem turn a closed contour integral into a sum of residues at interior singularities?

若 $f$ 在围道 $C$ 内除有限个孤立奇点 $a_k$ 外解析，则 $\oint_C f(z)\,dz=2\pi i\sum_k\operatorname{Res}(f,a_k)$。它把实积分、求和等问题转化为计算奇点处的留数，是复分析的核心工具。
If $f$ is analytic inside $C$ except for finitely many isolated singularities $a_k$, then $\oint_C f(z)\,dz=2\pi i\sum_k\operatorname{Res}(f,a_k)$. It reduces real integrals and sums to computing residues at singularities, a core tool of complex analysis.

黎曼映射定理对单连通区域给出了什么深刻的几何结论？

What deep geometric conclusion does the Riemann mapping theorem give for simply connected domains?

任何不等于整个复平面的单连通开区域，都共形等价于单位圆盘——即存在双全纯（解析且可逆）映射。这说明单连通区域的「内部形状」在复意义下只有一种，但边界性质会决定映射的正则程度。
Any simply connected open domain other than the whole plane is conformally equivalent to the unit disk—there exists a biholomorphic (analytic and invertible) map. It means simply connected domains have, in the complex sense, only one internal shape, though boundary regularity affects the map.

柯西积分公式如何由边界值确定解析函数的内部值？

How does Cauchy's integral formula determine interior values of an analytic function from its boundary values?

若 $f$ 在简单闭曲线 $C$ 内及其上解析，$z_0$ 在内部，则 $f(z_0)=\frac{1}{2\pi i}\oint_C\frac{f(z)}{z-z_0}\,dz$。这表明解析函数由其边界值完全决定，并由此可导出任意阶导数的积分表达式。
If $f$ is analytic inside and on a simple closed curve $C$, and $z_0$ lies inside, then $f(z_0)=\frac{1}{2\pi i}\oint_C\frac{f(z)}{z-z_0}\,dz$. This shows an analytic function is fully determined by its boundary values and yields integral formulas for all derivatives.

如何用留数定理计算形如 $\int_{-\infty}^{\infty}\frac{\sin x}{x}\,dx$ 的实积分？

How can the residue theorem evaluate real integrals such as $\int_{-\infty}^{\infty}\frac{\sin x}{x}\,dx$?

考虑复函数 $e^{iz}/z$ 沿上半平面大半圆围道积分，由留数定理与 Jordan 引理得 $\int_{-\infty}^{\infty}\frac{e^{ix}}{x}\,dx=\pi i$（取主值），取虚部即得 $\int_{-\infty}^{\infty}\frac{\sin x}{x}\,dx=\pi$，即著名的 Dirichlet 积分。
Consider $e^{iz}/z$ over a large semicircular contour in the upper half-plane; by the residue theorem and Jordan's lemma the principal value $\int_{-\infty}^{\infty}\frac{e^{ix}}{x}\,dx=\pi i$, and taking the imaginary part gives $\int_{-\infty}^{\infty}\frac{\sin x}{x}\,dx=\pi$, the Dirichlet integral.

最大模原理说明了解析函数的模具有怎样的极值性质？

What extremal property of the modulus does the maximum modulus principle describe for analytic functions?

若 $f$ 在区域 $D$ 内解析且非常数，则 $|f|$ 不能在 $D$ 的内部取到最大值，只能在边界上达到。这一原理常被用来估计解析函数的上界，可直接由柯西积分公式（平均值原理）推出。
If $f$ is analytic and nonconstant in a domain $D$, then $|f|$ cannot attain a maximum in the interior of $D$; it is reached only on the boundary. This principle is often used to bound analytic functions and follows directly from Cauchy's integral formula (the mean value property).

复分析中的刘维尔定理对整函数给出了什么限制？

What restriction does Liouville's theorem impose on entire functions in complex analysis?

刘维尔定理指出：在整个复平面上解析且有界的函数必为常数。据此可简洁证明代数基本定理——非常数多项式必有根，因为任何无根的多项式倒数将是整且有界。
Liouville's theorem states that a function entire and bounded on the whole plane must be constant. It yields a neat proof of the fundamental theorem of algebra: a nonconstant polynomial must have a root, since otherwise its reciprocal would be entire and bounded.
