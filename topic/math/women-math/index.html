埃米·诺特发现了对称与守恒的什么关系？

What symmetry-conservation link did Emmy Noether discover?

诺特定理说：每一个连续的对称都对应一个守恒量（如时间平移对称对应能量守恒，空间平移对称对应动量守恒）。它是现代物理的基石之一。
Noether's theorem states every continuous symmetry corresponds to a conservation law (time-translation ↔ energy, space-translation ↔ momentum). It is a cornerstone of modern physics.

阿达·洛芙莱斯为什么被称为第一位程序员？

Why is Ada Lovelace called the first programmer?

她为巴贝奇的分析机写了算法（计算伯努利数的步骤），并预见到机器不止能算数、还能处理符号与音乐，被视为史上第一个计算机程序。
She wrote an algorithm (for Bernoulli numbers) for Babbage's Analytical Engine and foresaw machines handling symbols and music, not just numbers—regarded as the first computer program.

索菲·热尔曼是谁？她做了什么数学？

Who was Sophie Germain, and what mathematics did she do?

她是自学成才的法国女数学家，在数论（以‘热尔曼素数’闻名）和弹性力学上做出贡献，曾以男性化名与高斯通信。她的工作推动了费马大定理的研究。
A self-taught French mathematician, she contributed to number theory (famous for 'Germain primes') and elasticity, corresponding with Gauss under a male pseudonym. Her work advanced the study of Fermat's Last Theorem.
