什么是黎曼流形？度量张量在其中的作用是什么？

What is a Riemannian manifold, and what role does the metric tensor play in it?

黎曼流形是在每点切空间上装备了一个正定内积（度量张量 $g_{ij}$）的光滑流形。度量张量定义了局部长度、角度与体积，从而能计算测地线、曲率等几何量，是把微积分推广到弯曲空间的框架。
A Riemannian manifold is a smooth manifold equipped at each tangent space with a positive-definite inner product, the metric tensor $g_{ij}$. The metric defines local lengths, angles, and volumes, enabling the computation of geodesics, curvature, and other geometric quantities, extending calculus to curved spaces.

陈述高斯-博内定理，并解释它如何把曲面的局部几何与整体拓扑联系起来。

State the Gauss-Bonnet theorem and explain how it connects the local geometry of a surface with its global topology.

紧致定向曲面 M 的高斯-博内公式为 $\int_M K\,dA=2\pi\chi(M)$，其中 $\chi$ 是欧拉示性数。它表明曲面上高斯曲率的积分只取决于拓扑不变量 $\chi$，与具体度量无关：正曲率总量由「洞」的个数决定，例如球面 $\chi=2$ 总曲率为 $4\pi$。
For a compact oriented surface M the Gauss-Bonnet formula reads $\int_M K\,dA=2\pi\chi(M)$, where $\chi$ is the Euler characteristic. It shows the integral of Gaussian curvature depends only on the topological invariant $\chi$, not on the metric: total positive curvature is fixed by the number of holes, e.g. the sphere has $\chi=2$ and total curvature $4\pi$.

#高斯-博内 #欧拉示性数 #曲率

球面上每一点的高斯曲率都是正常数，而马鞍面处处为负；这种符号差异为什么会体现在测地三角形的内角和上？

A sphere has constant positive Gaussian curvature while a saddle surface is negatively curved everywhere; why does this sign difference manifest itself in the angle sums of geodesic triangles?

高斯曲率控制测地三角形的角盈与角亏：单位球面上内角和大于 π 且超出部分恰为面积，负曲率曲面上内角和则小于 π。Theorema Egregium 表明该曲率是内蕴量，与曲面在空间中的嵌入方式无关，因此局限于曲面上的二维观察者仅凭测角与量面积就能把它测出来。这正是 Gauss-Bonnet 公式所定量刻画的内容。
Gaussian curvature governs the angular excess or defect of geodesic triangles: on a unit sphere the interior angles sum to more than π with the excess equal to the area, while on a negatively curved surface they sum to less than π. By the Theorema Egregium this curvature is intrinsic, independent of how the surface sits in ambient space, so a two-dimensional observer confined to the surface can measure it from angles and areas alone. This is exactly what the Gauss-Bonnet formula makes quantitative.

什么是高斯曲率？球面上任一点的高斯曲率为何是正的？

What is Gaussian curvature, and why is the Gaussian curvature at every point of a sphere positive?

高斯曲率是曲面在一点的两个主曲率的乘积 $K=\kappa_1\kappa_2$。球面半径 R 的两个主曲率均为 $1/R$，故 $K=1/R^2>0$，说明球面在每点都向同侧弯曲，是局部凸的正曲率曲面。
Gaussian curvature is the product of the two principal curvatures at a point, $K=\kappa_1\kappa_2$. For a sphere of radius R both principal curvatures equal $1/R$, so $K=1/R^2>0$, meaning the sphere bends the same way in all directions and is locally positively curved.

什么是流形？地球表面为何常被建模为二维流形？

What is a manifold, and why is Earth's surface often modeled as a two-dimensional manifold?

流形是局部同胚于欧氏空间的拓扑空间，每点附近可用坐标卡描述。地球表面在局部（足够小的区域）近似平面，可用经纬度坐标，但整体不可展平，因此是二维流形而非二维欧氏空间。
A manifold is a topological space locally homeomorphic to Euclidean space, described near each point by coordinate charts. Earth's surface looks flat locally (over small regions) and can be given latitude-longitude coordinates, yet cannot be flattened globally, so it is a two-dimensional manifold rather than Euclidean 2-space.

为什么薯片（双曲抛物面）掰起来特别脆？

Why do saddle-shaped (hyperbolic paraboloid) potato chips snap so crisply?

它的两个主曲率方向相反（一正一负），形成‘抗弯’的双向刚度，不易弯折只能断裂，所以口感脆。这是负高斯曲率结构的妙用。
Its two principal curvatures point opposite ways (one positive, one negative), creating two-way stiffness that resists bending and tends to fracture, giving a crisp snap. A neat use of negative Gaussian curvature.

什么是里奇流？它在庞加莱猜想证明中起了什么作用？

What is Ricci flow, and what role did it play in the proof of the Poincaré conjecture?

里奇流是由哈密顿引入的演化方程 $\partial_t g_{ij}=-2R_{ij}$，让度量沿时间演化以平滑曲率，类比热方程扩散曲率。佩雷尔曼用里奇流配合手术方法，证明了三维闭流形若单连通则同胚于三维球面，从而解决庞加莱猜想；相关工作发表于 arXiv 并由学界验证。
Ricci flow is the evolution equation $\partial_t g_{ij}=-2R_{ij}$ introduced by Hamilton, letting the metric evolve to smooth out curvature, analogous to heat diffusion. Perelman used Ricci flow with surgery to prove that a simply connected closed 3-manifold is homeomorphic to the 3-sphere, resolving the Poincaré conjecture; the work appeared on arXiv and was verified by the community.

什么是曲率？在平面曲线中曲率如何衡量弯曲程度？

What is curvature, and how does it measure bending for a plane curve?

平面曲线的曲率 $\kappa$ 描述单位弧长上切线方向改变的速度，$\kappa=|d\mathbf{T}/ds|$。直线曲率为 0，圆半径为 R 的曲率为 $1/R$：半径越小弯曲越剧烈。曲率直观反映了曲线偏离直线的程度。
For a plane curve curvature $\kappa$ measures how fast the tangent direction changes per unit arc length, $\kappa=|d\mathbf{T}/ds|$. A straight line has curvature 0, while a circle of radius R has curvature $1/R$: smaller radius means sharper bending. Curvature quantifies deviation from a straight line.

什么是截面曲率？它如何刻画黎曼流形的几何形状？

What is sectional curvature, and how does it characterize the geometry of a Riemannian manifold?

截面曲率是黎曼流形中每对线性无关切向量所张二维切平面上的高斯曲率。常截面曲率空间分为三类：正曲率如球面（三角形角和大于 $\pi$）、零曲率如欧氏空间、负曲率如双曲空间（角和小于 $\pi$），决定了局部三角形与平行线的行为。
Sectional curvature is the Gaussian curvature of the two-dimensional tangent plane spanned by any pair of linearly independent tangent vectors in a Riemannian manifold. Spaces of constant sectional curvature fall into three types: positive (sphere, triangle angle sum $>\pi$), zero (Euclidean), and negative (hyperbolic, sum $<\pi$), governing the local behavior of triangles and parallels.

什么是测地线？在球面上两点间的最短路径是什么？

What is a geodesic, and what is the shortest path between two points on a sphere?

测地线是曲面上局部长度最短的曲线，等价于「直线」的推广。球面上的测地线是大圆（过球心的平面与球面的交线）的弧段，因此地球上两点间的最短航线沿大圆而非恒定经线。
A geodesic is a curve on a surface that is locally length-minimizing, generalizing the notion of a straight line. On a sphere the geodesics are arcs of great circles (intersections of the sphere with planes through its center), so the shortest flight between two points on Earth follows a great circle rather than a line of constant longitude.

#测地线 #大圆 #最短路径

把圆柱面沿一条母线剪开摊平,竟可以得到一块平面而全程毫无拉伸——为什么这两种看上去弯曲程度不同的曲面在局部上算是同一种?第一基本形式在其中扮演什么角色?

Slit a cylinder along a ruling and unroll it, and you obtain a flat patch with zero stretching throughout. Why do these two surfaces, seemingly curved to different degrees, count as locally identical, and what part does the first fundamental form play?

第一基本形式 I = E du^2 + 2F du dv + G dv^2 度量的是沿曲面自身测得的长度、夹角与面积,完全不关心曲面在周围空间中如何弯。取圆柱参数化 r(u,v)=(cos u, sin u, v),直接计算得 E=G=1 且 F=0,与平面直角坐标下的度量逐点相同,于是摊平映射保持一切内蕴量,是严格的等距。高斯绝妙定理指出高斯曲率仅由第一基本形式决定,圆柱与平面的高斯曲率恒为零,这正是任何内蕴实验都无法在局部区分二者的根本原因。
The first fundamental form I = E du^2 + 2F du dv + G dv^2 records only the lengths, angles, and areas measured along the surface itself, entirely indifferent to how the surface bends in the surrounding space. Parametrize the cylinder as r(u,v) = (cos u, sin u, v); direct computation gives E = G = 1 and F = 0, matching the planar Cartesian metric pointwise, so the unrolling map preserves every intrinsic quantity and is a genuine isometry. Gauss's Theorema Egregium states that Gaussian curvature depends solely on the first fundamental form, and both the cylinder and the plane carry identically zero curvature — the exact reason no intrinsic experiment can tell them apart locally.
