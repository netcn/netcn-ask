中心极限定理为什么是统计学推断的理论基石？

Why is the central limit theorem the theoretical cornerstone of statistical inference?

中心极限定理表明，大量独立同分布随机变量之和在标准化后趋近于正态分布，与原始分布形状无关。它解释了现实中许多量呈钟形分布，并使基于正态分布的置信区间与假设检验合法化。费勒与迪雷特的教材给出了不同条件下的证明。
The central limit theorem says the standardized sum of many independent, identically distributed variables tends to a normal distribution regardless of the original shape. It explains why so many real quantities look bell-shaped and justifies normal-based confidence intervals and tests. Feller and Durrett give proofs under various conditions.

马尔可夫链蒙特卡洛（MCMC）是什么？

What is Markov chain Monte Carlo (MCMC)?

MCMC 用一条巧妙设计的马尔可夫链产生服从目标分布的随机样本，从而在高维空间中做贝叶斯推断。它是现代统计学与机器学习的核心引擎之一。
MCMC uses a crafted Markov chain to sample from a target distribution, enabling Bayesian inference in high dimensions. It is a core engine of modern statistics and ML.

布朗运动是什么？

What is Brownian motion?

布朗运动是粒子受无数微观碰撞形成的随机连续运动轨迹，也是数学上连续却处处不可导的典型过程。它奠定了期权定价等金融模型的基础。
Brownian motion is the jittery random path of particles under countless microscopic collisions, continuous yet nowhere differentiable. It underpins financial models like option pricing.

最小二乘法估计回归直线的思想最早由谁提出？

Who first proposed the idea behind least-squares estimation of a regression line?

最小二乘法由勒让德在1805年和高斯在1809年分别独立提出，通过最小化观测值与模型预测值之差的平方和来拟合直线。它在存在测量误差时给出最优线性无偏估计，至今仍是回归分析的基础。高斯与勒让德的原始论文是标准出处。
The method of least squares was proposed independently by Legendre in 1805 and Gauss in 1809, fitting a line by minimizing the sum of squared deviations between observations and the model. It gives the best linear unbiased estimate under measurement error and remains the basis of regression. The original papers of Gauss and Legendre are the standard source.

大数定律与中心极限定理分别刻画了样本平均的什么行为？

What different behaviors of the sample average do the law of large numbers and the central limit theorem describe?

大数定律说明随着样本量增大，样本平均依概率收敛到真实期望，关注长期稳定的位置。中心极限定理进一步描述其围绕期望的波动呈正态分布，关注偏离的形状。费勒与比林斯利的著作分别细致论证了这两条定理。
The law of large numbers says the sample average converges in probability to the true expectation as the sample grows, concerning long-run location. The central limit theorem further describes the fluctuation around that expectation as normal, concerning the shape of deviation. Feller and Billingsley argue these theorems in detail.

贝叶斯定理是什么？

What is Bayes' theorem?

贝叶斯定理用新证据更新事件发生的概率：P(A|B) = P(B|A)P(A)/P(B)。它是医学检测、垃圾邮件过滤和机器学习推断的基础。
Bayes' theorem updates a probability with new evidence: P(A|B)=P(B|A)P(A)/P(B). It underpins medical testing, spam filters and ML inference.

全概率公式是什么？

What is the law of total probability?

它把一个事件的概率按一组互斥且完备的情形分解求和：P(A) = Σ P(A|B_i)P(B_i)。它是计算复杂概率与推导贝叶斯公式的桥梁。
It breaks an event's probability across mutually exclusive, exhaustive cases: P(A)=Σ P(A|B_i)P(B_i). It links complex probabilities to Bayes' theorem.

蒙提霍尔问题为什么反直觉，正确策略是什么？

Why is the Monty Hall problem counterintuitive, and what is the correct strategy?

在蒙提霍尔问题中，主持人打开一扇后面是山羊的门后，换门会把中奖概率从三分之一提升到三分之二。直觉常误以为剩下两门机会相等，但主持人的信息改变了条件概率。塞尔文1975年的短文与沃斯·萨万特的专栏让这一问题广为人知。
In the Monty Hall problem, after the host opens a goat door, switching raises the win chance from one-third to two-thirds. Intuition wrongly assumes the two remaining doors are equally likely, but the host's information changes the conditional probabilities. Selvin's 1975 note and vos Savant's column made it famous.

What is random sampling?

p值在假设检验中的确切含义是什么，它常被怎样误读？

What exactly does a p-value mean in hypothesis testing, and how is it often misread?

p值是在原假设为真时，观察到当前数据或更极端数据的概率，并非原假设本身为真的概率。常见误读是把它当作效应存在或不存在的证据强度。费舍尔1925年的方法与卡塞拉、伯杰的教材都强调其条件性。
A p-value is the probability, under the null hypothesis, of seeing the observed data or something more extreme, not the probability that the null itself is true. A common misuse is treating it as the strength of evidence for or against an effect. Fisher's 1925 method and Casella & Berger's text stress its conditional nature.

What is the probability of heads when flipping a fair coin?

A fair coin has only two equally likely outcomes, so the probability of heads is 1/2, or 50%. This means that in the long run, about half of the flips will come up heads.

贝叶斯定理如何根据新证据更新事件的概率？

How does Bayes' theorem update the probability of an event in light of new evidence?

贝叶斯定理用先验概率乘以似然再除以证据的总概率，得到后验概率，从而把新观察纳入判断。它是在条件概率框架下把信念随数据修正的核心公式。贝叶斯1763年的论文与杰恩斯的书是经典出处。
Bayes' theorem multiplies a prior probability by the likelihood and divides by the total probability of the evidence to yield the posterior, incorporating new observation into judgment. It is the core formula for revising belief with data under conditional probability. Bayes's 1763 essay and Jaynes's book are classic references.
