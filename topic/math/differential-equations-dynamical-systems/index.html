什么是庞加莱-本迪克松定理？它如何限制平面动力系统可能出现的混沌？

What is the Poincaré-Bendixson theorem, and how does it restrict the kinds of chaos possible in planar dynamical systems?

庞加莱-本迪克松定理指出：平面上有界轨道若不包含平衡点，则必趋于周期轨道或平衡点的连接。因此二维连续动力系统不会出现奇怪吸引子式的混沌，混沌至少需要三维相空间——这解释了洛伦兹吸引子为何存在于三维系统中。
The Poincaré-Bendixson theorem states that a bounded orbit in the plane that contains no equilibrium must approach a periodic orbit or a connection of equilibria. Hence two-dimensional continuous systems cannot exhibit strange-attractor chaos; chaos requires at least three dimensions, which is why the Lorenz attractor lives in a 3D system.

什么是李雅普诺夫稳定性？如何用李雅普诺夫函数判断平衡点的稳定性？

What is Lyapunov stability, and how can a Lyapunov function determine the stability of an equilibrium point?

李雅普诺夫稳定性指系统轨迹在平衡点附近始终靠近且不会任意偏离。若存在正定函数 $V(x)$ 沿系统轨线导数 $\dot V\le0$，则平衡点稳定；若进一步 $\dot V<0$（除平衡点外），则渐近稳定。该方法无需求解方程即可判定稳定性。
Lyapunov stability means trajectories starting near an equilibrium stay nearby and do not diverge arbitrarily. If a positive-definite function $V(x)$ has derivative $\dot V\le0$ along trajectories, the equilibrium is stable; if $\dot V<0$ away from it, the equilibrium is asymptotically stable. This method checks stability without solving the equation.

什么是洛伦兹吸引子？它为何被视为确定性混沌的标志性例子？

What is the Lorenz attractor, and why is it regarded as a hallmark example of deterministic chaos?

洛伦兹吸引子来自描述对流的三维常微分方程组，其轨线在相空间中形成永不重复却又被局限的蝴蝶状集合。它对初值极度敏感（蝴蝶效应），确定性方程却产生不可长期预测的轨迹，是混沌理论最著名的实例。
The Lorenz attractor arises from a three-dimensional ODE system modeling convection; its trajectories form a butterfly-shaped set in phase space that never repeats yet stays bounded. It is exquisitely sensitive to initial conditions (the butterfly effect), so deterministic equations yield unpredictability over long times, making it the iconic example of chaos.

什么是偏微分方程？它与常微分方程的关键区别是什么？

What is a partial differential equation, and what is the key difference from an ordinary differential equation?

偏微分方程（PDE）的未知函数依赖多个自变量，方程中出现偏导数，如热方程 $u_t=\alpha u_{xx}$。与 ODE 只含一个自变量的导数不同，PDE 描述场随空间与时间的变化，求解常需边界与初始条件配合。
A partial differential equation involves an unknown function of several variables with partial derivatives, such as the heat equation $u_t=\alpha u_{xx}$. Unlike an ODE, which has derivatives in one variable, a PDE describes how a field varies in space and time and typically needs boundary and initial conditions.

什么是谐波振子方程？它的解为什么是正弦与余弦的叠加？

What is the harmonic oscillator equation, and why is its solution a superposition of sine and cosine?

无阻尼谐波振子方程为 $x''+\omega^2 x=0$。其特征方程 $r^2+\omega^2=0$ 有纯虚根 $\pm i\omega$，故通解为 $x(t)=A\cos(\omega t)+B\sin(\omega t)$，即两种相位不同的简谐振动的线性组合，参数 A、B 由初值决定。
The undamped harmonic oscillator obeys $x''+\omega^2 x=0$. Its characteristic equation $r^2+\omega^2=0$ has purely imaginary roots $\pm i\omega$, so the general solution is $x(t)=A\cos(\omega t)+B\sin(\omega t)$, a linear combination of two phase-shifted simple harmonics with A and B set by initial conditions.

什么是 logistic 方程？它如何描述种群增长的饱和现象？

What is the logistic equation, and how does it model the saturation of population growth?

logistic 方程为 $dx/dt=rx(1-x/K)$，其中 r 是内禀增长率、K 是环境容纳量。当 x 远小于 K 时近似指数增长，随 x 接近 K 增长放缓并趋于平衡；它刻画了资源受限下种群从爆发到饱和的 S 形曲线。
The logistic equation is $dx/dt=rx(1-x/K)$, with intrinsic growth rate r and carrying capacity K. When x is far below K growth is nearly exponential, then slows as x approaches K and settles at equilibrium, capturing the S-shaped saturation of a resource-limited population.

什么是常微分方程？举例说明指数衰减模型如何由一个一阶方程描述。

What is an ordinary differential equation, and how does an exponential decay model arise from a first-order equation?

常微分方程是未知函数只依赖一个自变量且其导数满足的方程。放射性衰变满足 $dy/dt=-ky$，解为 $y(t)=y_0 e^{-kt}$，其中 k 为衰变常数，描述半衰期现象，是典型的线性一阶常微分方程。
An ordinary differential equation involves an unknown function of a single variable together with its derivatives. Radioactive decay obeys $dy/dt=-ky$, solved by $y(t)=y_0 e^{-kt}$ with decay constant k, describing half-life and forming a classic linear first-order ODE.

什么是傅里叶方法求解热方程？分离变量法的基本思想是什么？

What is the Fourier method for solving the heat equation, and what is the basic idea of separation of variables?

分离变量法假设解可写为空间函数与时间函数的乘积 $u(x,t)=X(x)T(t)$，代入热方程 $u_t=\alpha u_{xx}$ 后两边分别只依赖 t 与 x，从而等于同一常数，化为两个常微分方程。空间部分给出正弦本征函数，时间部分给出指数衰减，叠加成傅里叶级数即完整解。
Separation of variables assumes a solution as a product $u(x,t)=X(x)T(t)$; substituting into $u_t=\alpha u_{xx}$ makes each side depend on only one variable, so both equal a constant, splitting into two ODEs. The spatial part yields sine eigenfunctions and the temporal part exponential decay; superposition gives a Fourier series, the full solution.

洛特卡-沃尔泰拉模型是什么？

What is the Lotka–Volterra model?

它是描述捕食者与猎物数量互作的微分方程组：猎物增长被捕食压制，捕食者随猎物增多而增长。它解释了种群围绕平衡点的周期性振荡。
The Lotka–Volterra equations model predator–prey dynamics, producing oscillatory cycles around equilibrium—a foundation of mathematical ecology.

传染病 SIR 模型是什么？

What is the SIR epidemic model?

SIR 把人群分为易感(S)、感染(I)、康复(R)三类，用微分方程描述传播。其基本再生数 R₀ 判断是否会出现大流行：R₀>1 时疫情会扩散。
The SIR model splits a population into Susceptible, Infected, Recovered; its basic reproduction number R₀>1 signals an epidemic will spread.
