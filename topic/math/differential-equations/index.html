纳维-斯托克斯方程的存在性与光滑性为何被列为千禧年难题？

Why is the existence and smoothness of solutions to the Navier-Stokes equations a Millennium Prize problem?

纳维-斯托克斯方程描述黏性流体的运动，但三维情形下解是否始终存在且不出现奇点至今未被证明。费夫曼把这一问题列为克雷数学研究所的千禧年大奖难题，要求证明或反证光滑解的存在。康斯坦丁与福亚斯的专著讨论了其数学困难。
The Navier-Stokes equations describe viscous fluid motion, yet whether solutions always exist and stay smooth in three dimensions without singularities remains unproven. Fefferman listed it as a Clay Millennium Prize problem asking for a proof or disproof of smooth existence. Constantin & Foias's monograph discusses the mathematical difficulties.

拉普拉斯变换为何常用于求解常系数线性微分方程？

Why is the Laplace transform often used to solve linear differential equations with constant coefficients?

拉普拉斯变换把微分运算转换为代数中的乘法，从而把常微分方程化为关于像函数的代数方程。求解后再做反变换即得原函数，特别适合带初值与分段输入的工程问题。多伊奇与博伊斯、迪普里马的教材都把它作为标准技巧。
The Laplace transform turns differentiation into multiplication in algebra, converting an ordinary differential equation into an algebraic equation in the transformed function. After solving, an inverse transform recovers the original function, especially convenient for initial-value and piecewise-input engineering problems. Doetsch and Boyce & DiPrima present it as a standard technique.

逻辑斯蒂方程如何描述种群增长从指数到饱和的转变？

How does the logistic equation describe the shift of population growth from exponential to saturation?

逻辑斯蒂方程在指数增长项上乘以一个随密度上升而下降的因子，使增长率在接近环境容纳量时趋于零。它产生S形增长曲线，避免了无限膨胀，是生态建模的基础。韦尔赫斯特1838年的论文与默里的《数学生物学》给出了经典推导。
The logistic equation multiplies the exponential growth term by a factor that declines as density rises, driving the growth rate to zero near the carrying capacity. It yields an S-shaped curve that avoids unbounded expansion and is foundational in ecology. Verhulst's 1838 paper and Murray's Mathematical Biology give the classic derivation.
