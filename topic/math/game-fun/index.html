体育比赛用循环赛还是淘汰赛，哪种更公平？

In sports, is a round-robin or a knockout fairer?

循环赛让每两队都交手，偶然性小、更能反映真实实力，但场次多；淘汰赛场次少却容易被一次失误淘汰。两者是公平与效率的权衡。
Round-robin has every pair play, reducing luck and reflecting true strength but needing many games; knockout is efficient yet one bad day eliminates you. It is a trade-off of fairness versus efficiency.

How can you divide a cake so everyone feels they got a fair share?

一个经典办法是：一人切、另一人先选，切的人会尽量切平均以免自己拿到小的。这就是博弈论里的无嫉妒分配思路。
A classic method: one person cuts, the other chooses first, so the cutter splits evenly to avoid the smaller piece. This is the envy-free idea from fair-division game theory.
