埃米·诺特定理把对称性和什么联系在了一起？

What did Emmy Noether's theorem link symmetry with?

诺特定理（1918）指出：物理定律的每一个连续对称性都对应一个守恒量——如时间平移对称对应能量守恒，空间平移对称对应动量守恒。艾米·诺特作为抽象代数（环与理想理论）的奠基人之一，其工作成为现代理论物理与粒子守恒律的数学支柱，却长期因性别被学术机构排斥。
Noether's theorem (1918) states that every continuous symmetry of a physical law corresponds to a conserved quantity—time-translation symmetry gives energy conservation, spatial symmetry gives momentum conservation. A founder of abstract algebra (rings and ideals), Emmy Noether provided the mathematical backbone for modern theoretical physics and conservation laws, yet faced long exclusion from academia because of her gender.

「0」这个数字是怎么被发明出来的？

How was the digit '0' invented?

零的概念最早在古巴比伦和玛雅文明中以占位符号出现，但作为可运算的数字「0」由印度数学家在公元 5 世纪左右系统确立，并随阿拉伯学者（如花拉子米）的著作传入伊斯兰世界与欧洲，故称「阿拉伯数字」。它使位值制计数法与代数运算成为可能，被誉为人类最重要的数学发明之一。
The concept of zero appeared as a placeholder in Babylonian and Mayan civilizations, but the digit '0' as an operable number was systematically established by Indian mathematicians around the 5th century CE, then transmitted to the Islamic world and Europe via scholars like al-Khwarizmi—hence 'Arabic numerals.' It enabled place-value notation and algebra, among the most important mathematical inventions.

《几何原本》为什么重要？

Why is Euclid's Elements important?

《几何原本》是公元前300年左右欧几里得编写的巨著，用公理与逻辑推理组织几何与数论，是历史上影响最久的教科书。它确立了“公理化”这一科学范式。
Euclid's Elements (c. 300 BCE) organized geometry and number theory from axioms by strict logic—the most influential textbook in history. It established the axiomatic method.
