排队论里的 Little 定律是什么？它为什么厉害？

What is Little's Law in queueing theory and why is it powerful?

它说 系统中平均人数 L = 平均到达率 λ × 平均停留时间 W，即 L = λW。它不依赖具体到达或服务的分布，因此能跨系统通用地估算等待时间或队列长度。
It states the average number in the system L equals arrival rate λ times average time in system W: L = λW. It holds regardless of arrival/service distributions, so it estimates waiting time or queue length universally.

M/M/1 队列的平均等待时间公式是什么？

What is the average waiting time formula for an M/M/1 queue?

设到达率 λ、服务率 μ，利用系数 ρ=λ/μ，平均系统内时间 W=1/(μ−λ)，平均排队时间 Wq=ρ/(μ−λ)。当 ρ 接近1时等待时间急剧上升。
With arrival rate λ and service rate μ and utilization ρ=λ/μ, the mean time in system is W=1/(μ−λ) and mean queueing time Wq=ρ/(μ−λ). As ρ→1 the waiting time blows up.

什么是单纯形法？它为何在最坏情况下是指数时间却在实践中高效？

What is the simplex method, and why is it exponential in the worst case yet efficient in practice?

单纯形法沿多面体可行域的棱在顶点间移动，每步改进目标函数直至最优。最坏情形下（如 Klee-Minty 构造的退化例子）需遍历指数级顶点，但实测中顶点数远小于理论上界，平均步数近似多项式，因此实际极快。
The simplex method moves along edges of the feasible polytope from vertex to vertex, improving the objective each step until optimal. In the worst case (e.g. Klee-Minty degenerate examples) it visits exponentially many vertices, yet real instances have far fewer, with near-polynomial average steps, so it is fast in practice.

什么是拉格朗日对偶？强对偶性在优化中有什么意义？

What is Lagrangian duality, and what is the significance of strong duality in optimization?

拉格朗日对偶把原问题转化为极大极小对偶问题，给出原最优值的下界。若满足斯莱特条件等约束规范，强对偶成立即原问题与对偶问题最优值相等，可借对偶变量（价格）解释资源稀缺性，并支撑现代求解器。
Lagrangian duality turns the primal problem into a max-min dual, giving a lower bound on the primal optimum. Under constraint qualifications like Slater's condition, strong duality holds so the primal and dual optima coincide, letting dual variables (prices) interpret resource scarcity and supporting modern solvers.

什么是 NP 难问题？它与运筹优化中的组合爆炸有何关系？

What is an NP-hard problem, and how is it related to combinatorial explosion in operations research?

NP 难问题至少与 NP 中最难问题一样难，目前没有已知多项式时间算法。旅行商、整数规划等运筹问题属 NP 难，随着规模增大可行解数量组合爆炸，只能依赖近似算法、分支定界或启发式在可接受时间内求近优解。
An NP-hard problem is at least as hard as the hardest problems in NP, with no known polynomial-time algorithm. TSP and integer programming in operations research are NP-hard; as size grows the number of feasible solutions explodes combinatorially, so one relies on approximation, branch-and-bound, or heuristics for near-optimal solutions in reasonable time.

什么是随机梯度下降？它在大规模机器学习优化中为何重要？

What is stochastic gradient descent, and why is it important in large-scale machine-learning optimization?

随机梯度下降用单个（或小批量）样本估计梯度来更新参数，而非计算全数据集梯度。每次更新廉价且带噪声，但噪声有助于逃离尖锐极小，使其在海量数据下比批量梯度下降快得多，是现代深度学习的默认优化器基础。
Stochastic gradient descent updates parameters using the gradient estimated from a single (or mini-batch) sample rather than the full dataset. Each update is cheap and noisy, but the noise helps escape sharp minima, making it far faster than batch gradient descent on massive data and forming the basis of modern deep-learning optimizers.

什么是线性规划？它解决的是哪一类优化问题？

What is linear programming, and what class of optimization problems does it solve?

线性规划是在线性等式与不等式约束下，极小或极大化线性目标函数的问题，标准形为 $\min c^T x$ 满足 $Ax\le b$。它用于资源分配、生产排程等，可由单纯形法或内点法高效求解。
Linear programming minimizes or maximizes a linear objective $c^T x$ subject to linear equality and inequality constraints $Ax\le b$. It is used in resource allocation and scheduling and is solved efficiently by the simplex method or interior-point methods.

什么是整数规划？它和普通线性规划的区别是什么？

What is integer programming, and how does it differ from ordinary linear programming?

整数规划要求部分或全部变量取整数值。相比连续线性规划，整数约束使可行域变成分立点集，问题通常 NP 难。它用于排班、选址等必须取离散决策的场景，常通过分支定界求解。
Integer programming requires some or all variables to take integer values. Unlike continuous linear programming, the integer constraints make the feasible region a set of discrete points, usually rendering the problem NP-hard. It models scheduling and facility location where decisions must be discrete, often solved by branch-and-bound.

什么是凸优化？凸性为何能保证找到全局最优？

What is convex optimization, and why does convexity guarantee a global optimum?

凸优化是在凸集上极小化凸函数的问题。凸函数的任意局部极小也是全局极小：因连接任意两点的线段上的函数值不超过端点连线，故不存在欺骗性的局部谷底，梯度类与内点法都能可靠收敛到全局解。
Convex optimization minimizes a convex function over a convex set. Any local minimum of a convex function is also global: the function value on the segment between two points lies below the chord, so there are no deceptive local valleys, and gradient or interior-point methods reliably reach the global solution.

什么是最大流最小割定理？它在网络中有什么应用？

What is the max-flow min-cut theorem, and what is its application in networks?

最大流最小割定理指出：网络中从源到汇的最大流量等于分割源汇的最小割容量。它用于交通与通信网络的容量规划、二分图匹配（通过构造流网络）以及可靠性的瓶颈分析。
The max-flow min-cut theorem states that the maximum flow from source to sink in a network equals the capacity of the minimum cut separating them. It is used in capacity planning of transport and communication networks, bipartite matching (via flow networks), and bottleneck reliability analysis.
