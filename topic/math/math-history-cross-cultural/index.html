阿拉伯学者花拉子米的《代数学》对欧洲文艺复兴数学起了什么作用？

What role did al-Khwarizmi's Algebra play in the mathematics of Renaissance Europe?

花拉子米的《还原与对消计算简述》系统给出一次方程与二次方程的解法，并首创代数（al-jabr）一词。12 世纪该书被译为拉丁文，把印度-阿拉伯数字与解方程方法带入欧洲，直接催生了欧洲代数学的诞生与后来的符号代数革命。
Al-Khwarizmi's Al-Kitab al-Mukhtasar fi Hisab al-Jabr wa-l-Muqabala systematically presented solutions to linear and quadratic equations and coined the term algebra (al-jabr). Translated into Latin in the 12th century, it brought the Indo-Arabic numerals and equation-solving methods to Europe, directly spawning European algebra and the later symbolic-algebra revolution.

艾米·诺特的不变量定理对现代数学与物理有何深远影响？

What profound impact did Emmy Noether's theorem on invariants have on modern mathematics and physics?

诺特定理证明：每个连续的对称性都对应一个守恒量（如时间平移对应能量守恒、空间平移对应动量守恒）。它把群论、变分法与物理定律统一起来，成为粒子物理标准模型与守恒律的理论支柱，并深刻影响了抽象代数中的环论发展。
Noether's theorem proves that every continuous symmetry corresponds to a conserved quantity (time translation to energy, spatial translation to momentum). It unified group theory, the calculus of variations, and physical law, becoming the backbone of conservation laws and the Standard Model, while also deeply influencing ring theory in abstract algebra.

19世纪非欧几何的诞生为何动摇了“数学真理等同于物理真理”的传统信念？

Why did the emergence of non-Euclidean geometry in the 19th century undermine the traditional belief that mathematical truth coincides with physical truth?

罗巴切夫斯基与鲍耶在1830年前后独立构造出过直线外一点可引多条平行线的几何，其无矛盾性后来由贝尔特拉米借助伪球面模型予以证实。既然两种互相矛盾的几何都能自洽，“欧氏公理是先验真理”的康德式立场便难以为继。此后数学家把公理视为人为选定的假设，数学的真理性被收缩为公理系统内部的一致性。
Around 1830, Lobachevsky and Bolyai independently constructed geometries in which infinitely many parallels pass through a point off a given line, and Beltrami later confirmed their consistency with the pseudosphere model. Since two mutually contradictory geometries are both self-consistent, the Kantian position that Euclid's axioms are a priori truths became untenable. Mathematicians thereafter treated axioms as deliberately chosen hypotheses, reducing mathematical truth to consistency within an axiom system.

中国古代的「勾股定理」与毕达哥拉斯定理是否等价？它最早见于哪部文献？

Is the Chinese Gougu theorem equivalent to the Pythagorean theorem, and in which text did it first appear?

勾股定理即直角三角形两直角边平方和等于斜边平方 $a^2+b^2=c^2$，与毕达哥拉斯定理数学等价。中国最早系统记载见于《周髀算经》，三国赵爽用弦图给出证明，比西方同类记载更早以出入相补思想呈现。
The Gougu theorem states that the sum of squares of the legs equals the square of the hypotenuse, $a^2+b^2=c^2$, mathematically equivalent to the Pythagorean theorem. In China it was first systematically recorded in the Zhoubi Suanjing, and Zhao Shuang of the Three Kingdoms era proved it with a xian tu diagram using the principle of dissection.

斐波那契数列因何得名？它如何与印度数学的「狮子阵」问题相关？

Why is the Fibonacci sequence so named, and how is it related to the Indian lion's march problem?

斐波那契数列由比萨的列奥纳多（绰号斐波那契）在《算盘全书》中以兔子繁殖问题引入，递推 $F_n=F_{n-1}+F_{n-2}$。类似递推早在印度数学（如 Pingala 的韵律计量、12 世纪的狮子阵问题）中已出现，说明该数列有跨文化的独立起源。
The Fibonacci sequence was introduced by Leonardo of Pisa (nicknamed Fibonacci) in Liber Abaci via a rabbit-breeding problem, with recurrence $F_n=F_{n-1}+F_{n-2}$. Similar recurrences appeared earlier in Indian mathematics (e.g. Pingala's prosody and the 12th-century lion's march problem), showing independent cross-cultural origins.

什么是零的概念？它在印度数学中是如何被发明并传播到世界的？

What is the concept of zero, and how was it invented in Indian mathematics and spread worldwide?

零作为数字与占位符由古印度数学家在公元 5 世纪前后系统使用，最早见于婆罗摩笈多的著作，规定了零的运算规则。经阿拉伯学者（如花拉子米）中转，零的符号与十进制位值制传入欧洲，奠定了现代算术基础。
Zero as both a number and a placeholder was systematically used by Indian mathematicians around the 5th century CE, first treated with operational rules in Brahmagupta's works. Transmitted through Arab scholars such as al-Khwarizmi, the symbol and decimal place-value system reached Europe, laying the foundation of modern arithmetic.
