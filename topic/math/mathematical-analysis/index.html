巴拿赫-塔斯基悖论为何说一个球可以分成有限块重新拼成两个等大同球？

Why does the Banach-Tarski paradox say a ball can be cut into finitely many pieces and reassembled into two identical balls?

巴拿赫-塔斯基悖论依赖选择公理构造出不可测的非物质点集，并借助三维旋转群的自由作用把球分解为五块后重新拼合。它不违背质量守恒，因为所用碎片不是日常意义上的有体积物体。巴拿赫与塔斯基1924年的论文与瓦贡的书详述了这一构造。
The Banach-Tarski paradox uses the axiom of choice to build non-measurable point sets and exploits a free action of the 3D rotation group to split a ball into five pieces reassembled into two copies. It does not violate conservation of mass because the pieces are not ordinary volumetric objects. Banach & Tarski's 1924 paper and Wagon's book detail the construction.

傅里叶级数为何能把周期函数分解为正弦与余弦的叠加？

Why can a Fourier series decompose a periodic function into a sum of sines and cosines?

傅里叶级数利用三角函数族在区间上正交的性质，把周期函数展开成正弦余弦的无穷加权和。它使复杂的周期信号可被频率成分分析，是信号处理的数学基础。齐格蒙德与斯坦、沙克拉希的《傅里叶分析》都系统建立了收敛理论。
A Fourier series exploits the orthogonality of trigonometric functions on an interval to expand a periodic function into an infinite weighted sum of sines and cosines. It lets complex periodic signals be analyzed into frequency components and underlies signal processing. Zygmund and Stein & Shakarchi's Fourier Analysis build the convergence theory.

波尔查诺-魏尔斯特拉斯定理保证了什么样的序列必有收敛子列？

What sequences does the Bolzano-Weierstrass theorem guarantee have a convergent subsequence?

波尔查诺-魏尔斯特拉斯定理说，欧几里得空间中的有界序列必定含有收敛的子列。它把有界性与紧性联系起来，是极值存在性与函数分析的基础工具。鲁丁与阿博特的教材都把它作为分析学的支柱定理。
The Bolzano-Weierstrass theorem states that every bounded sequence in Euclidean space has a convergent subsequence. It links boundedness with compactness and is a basic tool for existence of extrema and functional analysis. Rudin and Abbott present it as a pillar of analysis.

康托尔集为何被称为无处稠密的完全集，它的测度是多少？

Why is the Cantor set called a perfect, nowhere-dense set, and what is its measure?

康托尔集由不断去掉线段中间三分之一构造，它不含任何区间故无处稠密，却又每个点都是聚点故为完全集。它的总长度是零，却含有不可数多个点，展示了无穷的微妙。康托尔1883年的论文与鲁丁的教材都讨论了它。
The Cantor set is built by repeatedly removing the middle third of intervals; it contains no interval, so it is nowhere dense, yet every point is a limit point, so it is perfect. Its total length is zero while it holds uncountably many points, showing subtleties of infinity. Cantor's 1883 paper and Rudin's text discuss it.

柯西序列为何能刻画实数系的完备性？

Why can a Cauchy sequence characterize the completeness of the real number system?

柯西序列指任意靠后的项彼此任意接近的序列；实数系的完备性正是说每个柯西序列都收敛到实数。有理数系不满足这一点，例如逼近根号2的有理数列在有理数内无极限。鲁丁与阿波斯托尔的《数学分析》都把它作为实数公理的等价刻画。
A Cauchy sequence is one whose terms become arbitrarily close to each other far enough along; the completeness of the reals is exactly that every Cauchy sequence converges to a real. The rationals fail this, as a rational sequence approaching the square root of 2 has no rational limit. Rudin and Apostol's Mathematical Analysis use it as an equivalent characterization of the reals.

函数列的逐点收敛与一致收敛的本质差别是什么？为什么一致收敛能把连续性传递给极限函数？

What is the essential difference between pointwise and uniform convergence of a sequence of functions, and why does uniform convergence pass continuity on to the limit?

逐点收敛中 N 可逐点选取，各处快慢不一；一致收敛要求同一个 N 对一切 x 同时有效，即 sup|f_n−f|→0。连续性传递靠三项估计：|f(x)−f(a)|≤‖f−f_n‖∞+|f_n(x)−f_n(a)|+‖f−f_n‖∞，首尾由一致收敛控制，中间项用 f_n 自身的连续性。缺此一致的界，逐点极限可能不连续。
Under pointwise convergence the index N may be chosen separately for each point, so the speed varies across the domain. Uniform convergence demands a single N working simultaneously for every x, i.e. sup |f_n − f| → 0. Continuity then transfers through a three-term estimate: |f(x) − f(a)| ≤ ‖f − f_n‖∞ + |f_n(x) − f_n(a)| + ‖f − f_n‖∞, where the outer terms are controlled by uniform convergence and the middle one by continuity of f_n itself. Pointwise convergence lacks this uniform bound, and its limit really can be discontinuous.

有理数在数轴上处处稠密，为什么数学分析还非用实数不可？完备性到底补上了什么？

The rationals are dense everywhere on the number line, so why does analysis absolutely need the real numbers — what exactly does completeness add?

稠密只保证缝隙旁都有有理数，却不保证极限留在原地。看逼近 √2 的数列 1, 1.4, 1.41, …：各项越来越接近（柯西列），但极限 √2 不在 ℚ 中，这列数在 ℚ 内没有终点，微积分依赖的极限运算随之失灵。完备性公理（如确界原理）封住缺口：实数中每个柯西列都收敛，单调有界数列必收敛，介值定理和极值定理由此奠基。
Density guarantees only that a rational stands beside every gap, not that limits stay put. Watch the rationals 1, 1.4, 1.41, … creeping toward √2: the terms grow ever closer (a Cauchy sequence), yet the limit √2 lies outside ℚ, so the sequence lands nowhere in the rational world and the limit operation on which calculus rests breaks down. Completeness seals the gap: in the reals every Cauchy sequence converges and every bounded monotone sequence converges, laying the foundation of the intermediate and extreme value theorems.
