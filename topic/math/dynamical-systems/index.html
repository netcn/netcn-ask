什么是逻辑斯蒂映射中的倍周期分岔道路？费根鲍姆常数在其中扮演什么角色？

What is the period-doubling route to chaos in the logistic map, and what role does the Feigenbaum constant play in it?

随着参数 r 增大，逻辑斯蒂映射的稳定不动点经历周期 2、4、8……的分岔，最终在 r≈3.5699 处进入混沌，这条路径称为倍周期道路。费根鲍姆发现相邻分岔参数间距之比 δ≈4.6692 趋于普适常数，且该常数对一大类单峰映射相同，说明混沌的出现具有超越具体模型的定量规律性。
As the parameter r grows, the stable fixed point of the logistic map undergoes bifurcations at periods 2, 4, 8, … until chaos onset near r ≈ 3.5699; this cascade is the period-doubling route. Feigenbaum found that ratios of successive bifurcation intervals converge to the universal constant δ ≈ 4.6692, identical across a broad class of unimodal maps—evidence that the onset of chaos obeys quantitative laws beyond any single model.

什么是洛伦兹系统中的“蝴蝶效应”？它为什么说明长期天气预报存在原理性极限？

What is the butterfly effect in the Lorenz system, and why does it imply a fundamental limit on long-range weather forecasting?

洛伦兹系统对初始条件极端敏感：初值相差千万分之一的两条轨迹会在短时间内指数分离。这意味着无论把测量精度提高到多少，可预报的时间窗口总是有限的，因此长期天气预报在原理上而非技术上受限。
The Lorenz system is extremely sensitive to initial conditions: two trajectories that start one ten-millionth apart separate exponentially fast. No matter how precisely you measure, the predictable time window stays finite, so long-range forecasting is limited in principle, not merely in practice.

#混沌 #初值敏感性

洛伦兹把模拟的中间数值少打了几个小数位，重跑的天气竟走向完全不同的结局——这种极端的初值敏感性被称为什么？它为什么宣判了长期天气预报的极限？

When Lorenz retyped an intermediate simulation value with fewer decimal digits, the restarted weather run diverged toward a completely different future — what is this extreme sensitivity to initial conditions called, and why does it cap long-range weather forecasting?

这就是蝴蝶效应，学名叫对初始条件的敏感依赖。洛伦兹1963年的大气对流模型只有三个变量，非线性项却使相邻轨道呈指数分离：误差每隔一段特征时间翻倍，微小差别迟早吞没全部预报技巧。由于观测精度永远有限，混沌系统的可预报期被硬性截断，这正是逐日天气预报的理论上限只有两周左右的原因。
It is the butterfly effect, technically sensitive dependence on initial conditions. Lorenz's 1963 atmospheric convection model carries only three variables, yet its nonlinear terms make neighboring trajectories split exponentially: errors double every characteristic interval, and tiny differences sooner or later wipe out all forecasting skill. Since observations are never infinitely precise, the predictable window of a chaotic system is hard-capped — exactly why day-to-day weather prediction tops out around two weeks even in theory.

#蝴蝶效应 #敏感依赖 #洛伦兹系统

迭代式 x→rx(1−x) 简单得一行就写得下，为什么参数一旦增大，它会先经历周期一次次翻倍、最终坠入混沌？这些分岔点的间距里为何还藏着同一个常数？

The iteration x→rx(1−x) fits on a single line, so why does cranking up its parameter first double the period again and again and finally plunge the system into chaos — and why does one and the same constant lurk in the spacing of those bifurcations?

因为迭代不断把区间拉伸后再折叠回去，参数越大折叠越剧烈：r较小时不动点稳定；越过约3后稳定周期翻倍为2，此后每跨过一处分岔阈值周期再翻倍，4、8、16……到r≈3.57附近周期趋于无穷，轨道再也安定不下来，这就是混沌。相邻阈值的间距按固定公比收缩，极限比率约为4.669，即费根鲍姆常数；它对一切单峰映射成立，说明通往混沌的道路具有普适性。
Because iteration keeps stretching the interval and folding it back, and larger parameters fold more violently: for small r the fixed point rests calmly; past about 3 its stability yields to a cycle of period 2, and each further threshold doubles the period anew — 4, 8, 16 — until near r ≈ 3.57 the period diverges and no orbit settles anymore, which is chaos. Successive thresholds shrink by a fixed ratio whose limit, about 4.669, is the Feigenbaum constant; it holds for every unimodal map, marking the road into chaos as universal.
