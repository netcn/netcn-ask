椭圆曲线密码（ECC）是什么？

What is elliptic-curve cryptography (ECC)?

ECC 利用椭圆曲线上点加法的“离散对数难解”来构造密钥。相比 RSA，它用更短的密钥达到同等安全，广泛用于手机与比特币。
ECC bases security on the hardness of the elliptic-curve discrete logarithm; it matches RSA security with much shorter keys, used in phones and Bitcoin.

零知识证明是什么？

What is a zero-knowledge proof?

零知识证明让一方向另一方证明“某个命题为真”而不泄露任何额外信息，如证明知道密码而不透露密码。它在区块链隐私与身份验证中日益重要。
A zero-knowledge proof convinces a verifier that a statement is true without revealing anything else—used in blockchain privacy and authentication.

格密码为什么能抗量子？

Why is lattice-based cryptography quantum-resistant?

格密码的安全性基于高维格中“最短向量问题（SVP）”的难解性，而目前没有已知的量子算法能高效求解它。因此它是后量子密码的主要候选。
Lattice-based crypto relies on the hardness of the shortest vector problem, for which no efficient quantum algorithm is known, making it a leading post-quantum candidate.

肖尔算法为何威胁现有密码？

Why does Shor's algorithm threaten current cryptography?

肖尔算法在量子计算机上能高效分解大整数和求离散对数，从而破解 RSA 与 ECC。这推动了对“后量子密码”的紧急研究。
Shor's algorithm factors integers and solves discrete logs efficiently on a quantum computer, breaking RSA and ECC—spurring post-quantum cryptography.
