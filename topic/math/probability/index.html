谷歌的 PageRank 和马尔可夫链有什么关系？

How is Google's PageRank related to Markov chains?

PageRank 把网络浏览者抽象成一个马尔可夫链：状态是网页，转移按网页上的链接等概率进行。PageRank 值正是该链的平稳分布——长期看访问每个页面的比例。为避免“死胡同”和循环陷阱，拉里·佩奇引入阻尼因子（约 0.85），让浏览者偶尔随机跳到任意网页，从而保证平稳分布存在且唯一，再按它给网页排序。
PageRank models a web surfer as a Markov chain: states are pages and transitions follow the page's links with equal probability. The PageRank value is the chain's stationary distribution—the long-run proportion of visits to each page. To handle dead ends and trapping cycles, Larry Page introduced a damping factor (about 0.85) letting the surfer occasionally jump to a random page, guaranteeing a unique stationary distribution used to rank pages.

为什么一维、二维随机游走迟早回到原点，而三维却不一定？

Why do 1D and 2D random walks return to the origin almost surely, but not in 3D?

这是波利亚定理（1921）。在一维和二维，随机游走是“常返”的：回到原点的概率为 1，而且会无限多次返回。三维中回到原点的概率只有约 34%，因此是“暂态”的。直观原因是维度越高可选方向越多，回到原点所需的巧合被大量稀释。它为扩散、高分子构象和网络上的随机搜索提供了数学模型。
This is Pólya's theorem (1921). In one and two dimensions the walk is recurrent: the probability of returning to the origin is 1, and it returns infinitely often. In three dimensions the return probability is only about 0.34, so the walk is transient. Intuitively, higher dimension offers more directions, diluting the coincidence needed to come back. This provides mathematical models for diffusion, polymer conformations, and random search on networks.

大偏差原理（large deviation principle）如何量化“极小概率事件”的指数衰减速度？

How does the large deviation principle quantify the exponential decay rate of very small probabilities?

大偏差原理用“速率函数” I(x) 描述随机变量偏离均值的概率：P(Sₙ/n ≈ x) ≈ e^(−nI(x))。它比中心极限定理更精细地刻画了尾部，说明极端偏离的概率随样本量 n 呈指数衰减；在统计物理、风险与通信中有广泛应用，源自理查德·埃利斯等人对 Cramér 定理的推广。
The large deviation principle describes rare deviations via a rate function I(x): P(Sₙ/n ≈ x) ≈ e^(−nI(x)). It refines the central limit theorem by capturing the tail, showing extreme deviations decay exponentially in the sample size n, with wide use in statistical physics, risk, and communications—built on Cramér's theorem.

什么是鞅（martingale）？

What is a martingale?

鞅是一种随机过程，其未来条件的期望等于当前值（无“免费趋势”）。在公平赌博中财富就是鞅，这也说明“加倍下注”无法保证获利。
A martingale's conditional future expectation equals its present value; fair-game wealth is a martingale, so doubling bets can't guarantee profit.

中心极限定理为什么被称为统计学的基石？

Why is the Central Limit Theorem called the cornerstone of statistics?

中心极限定理指出：大量相互独立、同分布（且方差有限）的随机变量之和，其标准化分布会趋近于正态分布，无论原始分布是什么形状。这解释了为何自然界与社会中大量「均值类」现象呈钟形曲线，并使基于正态分布的置信区间、假设检验等推断方法拥有了普遍适用的理论依据。
The Central Limit Theorem states that the standardized sum of many independent, identically distributed random variables (with finite variance) tends toward a normal distribution, regardless of the original shape. This explains why so many natural and social 'average-like' phenomena form bell curves, and gives the theoretical backbone for confidence intervals, hypothesis tests, and other normal-based inference.

双信封悖论是什么？

What is the two-envelope paradox?

两信封各装钱，一个正好是另一个的两倍。你拿一个后想“换”可能赚一倍也可能亏一半，期望却算成 1.25 倍——诱导你无限换。错误源于对任意金额都假设了对称分布。
Two envelopes, one with twice the other's amount; switching seems to gain 25% in expectation—but the same logic repeats forever. The flaw is assuming a symmetric distribution for any observed amount.

马尔可夫链刻画的是一类怎样的随机过程？

What kind of stochastic process do Markov chains describe?

马尔可夫链是一种随机过程，其下一个状态只依赖于当前状态，而与更早的历史无关——这称为「无记忆性」或马尔可夫性质。它由安德烈·马尔可夫在 20 世纪初提出，用于建模语言、股价和网页跳转等序列。PageRank 本质上就是在万维网上随机游走的马尔可夫链平稳分布。
A Markov chain is a stochastic process whose next state depends only on the current state, not on earlier history—the 'memoryless' or Markov property. Introduced by Andrey Markov in the early 20th century, it models sequences like language, stock prices, and web navigation. PageRank is essentially the stationary distribution of a random walk (Markov chain) over the web.

Sleeping Beauty is awakened and asked the coin outcome; she's woken once on heads, twice on tails (with amnesia). 'Thirders' say the chance of heads now is 1/3, 'halfers' say 1/2—a debate about how to define probability.

What is the St. Petersburg paradox?

一枚公平硬币反复抛，直到第一次正面出现；若第 n 次才正面，你赢得 2ⁿ 元。期望收益是无穷大，但没人愿付高价玩——揭示了“期望效用”比“期望金额”更合理。
A game pays 2ⁿ if the first head appears on toss n; expected payoff is infinite, yet nobody pays much to play—showing expected utility beats expected value.

为什么高维积分特别适合用蒙特卡洛方法？

Why is Monte Carlo especially suited to high-dimensional integrals?

网格法在 d 维空间中每个方向剖分 n 段，共需要 n^d 个点，随维数指数爆炸，这就是“维数灾难”。蒙特卡洛用随机抽样估计积分，由中心极限定理，误差与 √N 成反比，与维数无关。因此从统计物理、金融期权定价到贝叶斯推断，处理高维问题时蒙特卡洛几乎是唯一可行的路径。
Grid methods in d dimensions need n^d points when each direction is divided into n segments, exploding exponentially with dimension—the curse of dimensionality. Monte Carlo estimates integrals by random sampling; by the central limit theorem the error decays like 1/√N, independent of dimension. So for high-dimensional problems—statistical physics, option pricing, Bayesian inference—Monte Carlo is often the only feasible route.

鞅的停时定理（可选抽样定理）在什么条件下成立？为什么“公平赌局下任何停止策略都无法获利”这一直觉需要这些条件？

Under what conditions does the optional stopping theorem for martingales hold, and why does the intuition that no stopping strategy beats a fair game require those conditions?

可选抽样定理通常要求停时有界、或停时的期望有限且鞅增量一致有界等条件之一；此时 E[M_T] = E[M_0]。缺少这些条件时结论可能失效：例如在公平赌局中采用“翻倍加注直到赢”的策略，停时期望虽为正，但对应停时有无穷期望资金需求，定理前提被破坏。
The optional stopping theorem holds under conditions such as a bounded stopping time, or an integrable stopping time together with uniformly bounded martingale increments; then E[M_T] = E[M_0]. Without them the conclusion can fail: in a fair game, the martingale betting system—doubling stakes until a win—has positive expected gain at stopping, but the required capital has infinite expectation, violating the theorem's hypotheses.

#鞅 #可选抽样定理

蒙提霍尔问题（三门问题）里，换门为什么赢的概率更高？

In the Monty Hall problem, why does switching doors win more often?

在 Monty Hall 游戏中，你先选一扇门，主持人（知道门后情况）打开剩下两扇中一扇空门，问你是否换到最后一扇。你最初选中的概率只有 1/3，而奖品在「另两扇门」中的概率是 2/3；主持人排除一扇空门后，这 2/3 全集中到那扇未开的门上，因此换门赢面为 2/3。许多直觉误以为变成 1/2，正是忽略了主持人信息的非随机性。

这不是悖论，而是概率直觉的反差。先算反面：所有人都不相同。第 2 人避开第 1 人的概率是 364/365，第 3 人避开前两人的概率是 363/365，依此类推，23 个人乘起来的积约 0.49，所以“至少两人相同”约 51%。人数越多越逼近 1，60 人时已超过 99%。
This is not a paradox but a clash with intuition. Compute the complement: all birthdays distinct. Person 2 avoids person 1 with probability 364/365, person 3 avoids the first two with 363/365, and so on; the product over 23 people is about 0.49, so a match has probability roughly 51%. It climbs toward 1 quickly—over 99% by 60 people.

贝叶斯定理解决了什么类型的推理问题？

What kind of reasoning problem does Bayes' theorem address?

贝叶斯定理给出了在观察到新证据后更新某事件发生概率的公式：P(原因|证据) = P(证据|原因)·P(原因) / P(证据)。它把「先验概率」与「似然」结合得到「后验概率」，是医学检测、垃圾邮件过滤和机器学习中不确定性推理的核心。其思想源自托马斯·贝叶斯 18 世纪的遗稿，由拉普拉斯发扬光大。
Bayes' theorem gives the formula for updating the probability of a cause after observing evidence: P(cause|evidence) = P(evidence|cause)·P(cause) / P(evidence). It combines a prior probability with likelihood to yield a posterior, and is central to medical testing, spam filtering, and machine learning. The idea comes from Thomas Bayes's 18th-century posthumous work, developed by Laplace.

No. Each spin is independent; previous results do not affect the next one, so the probability of red on the next spin is unchanged. Treating “I am due for a win after a losing streak” as a rule is the famous gambler's fallacy. It confuses the probability of a whole sequence with that of a single event.

强大数定律与中心极限定理分别描述了独立同分布随机变量和的什么行为?两者在信息层次上有何差异?

What aspects of sums of i.i.d. random variables do the strong law of large numbers and the central limit theorem describe respectively, and how do they differ in informational content?

The strong law gives a qualitative almost-sure statement: (S_n−nμ)/n → 0. The central limit theorem quantifies the fluctuation scale: √n(S̄_n−μ) converges in distribution to a normal law. The former only says deviations vanish; the latter pins down their distributional shape, providing first-order accurate approximation of fluctuations — complementary results, neither implying the other.

正态分布（高斯分布）为什么在自然界如此常见？

Why is the normal (Gaussian) distribution so common in nature?

正态分布是呈钟形的对称连续分布，由均值和标准差两个参数决定。它常见的原因在于中心极限定理：许多独立微小随机因素叠加的结果往往趋近钟形。从人的身高到测量误差，大量现象都大致服从它，因此高斯分布成为统计建模与质量控制的基本假设。
The normal distribution is a symmetric bell-shaped continuous distribution determined by just its mean and standard deviation. Its ubiquity follows from the Central Limit Theorem: sums of many independent tiny random factors tend toward a bell shape. From human heights to measurement errors, countless phenomena are approximately normal, making it the default assumption in statistics and quality control.

为什么掷两颗公平骰子，点数之和为 7 比之和为 2 更可能出现？

Why is the sum 7 more likely than the sum 2 when rolling two fair dice?

两只骰子各有 6 面，共 36 种等可能结果；和为 2 只有 (1,1) 一种，概率 1/36，而和为 7 有 (1,6)(2,5)(3,4)(4,3)(5,2)(6,1) 六种，概率 6/36 = 1/6。组合数越多越可能，这正是两颗骰子点数之和呈现“中间高、两端低”的对称三角形分布的原因。
Two dice give 36 equally likely outcomes; sum 2 occurs only as (1,1), probability 1/36, while sum 7 occurs as six pairs (1,6)…(6,1), probability 6/36 = 1/6. More combinations mean higher probability—the sum of two fair dice follows a symmetric triangular distribution that peaks in the middle.

一个房间只要有23个人,就有超过一半概率存在两人同一天生日?这怎么可能?

Just twenty-three people make shared birthdays likelier than not? How is that possible?

直觉错在把问题当成有人和我同天生日(那确实需要253人);实际问的是任意两人配对,23人共有253对,每对撞日的独立概率约1/365,全部错开的概率连乘后只剩一半以下。这就是著名的生日悖论——并非逻辑矛盾,而是直觉对组合爆炸的低估。它的现实版本威力惊人:密码学中哈希碰撞攻击正基于同一原理,n位指纹只需约2的n/2次方次尝试就能找到碰撞,因此安全系统被迫使用更长的摘要。
Intuition misfires by reading the question as someone sharing YOUR birthday (needing 253 people); really any pair may collide, and twenty-three people form 253 pairs, each matching at about 1/365 independently — multiplying no-collision chances drops below half. The birthday paradox is thus no logical contradiction but intuition underestimating combinatorial explosion. Its practical twin bites cryptography: hash-collision attacks exploit the same math, needing only about two to the n-over-2 tries against n-bit fingerprints — which is why secure systems demand longer digests.
