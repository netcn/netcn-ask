若尔当曲线定理是什么，它为何显然却难证？

What is the Jordan curve theorem, and why is it obvious yet hard to prove?

平面上一条简单闭曲线把平面分成内部和外部两个区域。直觉很清楚，但严格证明需要处理任意扭曲曲线，相当技术化。
A simple closed curve in the plane divides it into an inside and an outside. Intuitively obvious, but rigorous proof must handle arbitrarily wild curves and is quite technical.

什么是纽结理论，三叶结为什么解不开？

What is knot theory, and why can't a trefoil knot be untied?

纽结理论研究绳圈在连续形变下的等价类。三叶结有非零的扭结不变量（如琼斯多项式），证明它不能在不剪断的情况下变成平凡圆。
Knot theory studies equivalence classes of loops under continuous deformation. The trefoil has a non-zero invariant (e.g., Jones polynomial), proving it cannot become an unknot without cutting.

博苏克-乌拉姆定理是什么，它和对跖点有什么关系？

What is the Borsuk-Ulam theorem, and what does it say about antipodal points?

它说把球面连续映射到平面，必存在一对对径点映射到点同一点。推论包括地球表面总有一对对跖点温度与气压同时相同。
It states that any continuous map from a sphere to the plane maps some pair of antipodal points to the same point. A corollary: there are always antipodal points on Earth with equal temperature and pressure.

庞加莱猜想说的是什么，为什么是千禧年难题？

What does the Poincaré conjecture state, and why was it a Millennium problem?

它猜想任何单连通的三维闭流形都同胚于三维球面。佩雷尔曼用里奇流在2003年前后证明了它，是拓扑学里程碑。
It conjectures that any simply connected closed 3-manifold is homeomorphic to the 3-sphere. Perelman proved it around 2003 using Ricci flow — a milestone in topology.

毛球定理说球面上的毛无法全部顺贴，这是为什么？

The hairy ball theorem says you cannot comb a hairy ball flat — why?

在偶数维球面（如地球）上，任何连续的切向量场必有零点（旋涡点）。所以毛发总会有一处打旋无法顺向。
On an even-dimensional sphere, every continuous tangent vector field has a zero (a whirl). So the hair must have at least one cowlick.

布劳威尔不动点定理讲了什么？搅拌咖啡能说明它吗？

What does Brouwer's fixed-point theorem say? Can stirring coffee illustrate it?

把茶杯连续映射到自身，必存在至少一点位置不变。搅拌咖啡后，总有一小点液体最终回到它出发的位置附近。
Any continuous map from a closed disk to itself has at least one fixed point. After stirring coffee, some tiny region ends up where it started.

怎样判断两个形状拓扑上相同？什么是拓扑不变量？

How do we tell two shapes are topologically the same? What are topological invariants?

若存在连续的双向逆映射（同胚），二者拓扑相同。用不变量（如连通分支数、亏格、基本群）可区分不同形状，这些量在形变下保持不变。
Two shapes are topologically the same if a continuous bijection with continuous inverse (homeomorphism) exists. Invariants like number of components, genus, or fundamental group stay fixed under deformation and help tell shapes apart.

纽结理论在生物学中有什么实际应用？

What real biological application does knot theory have?

细胞内的DNA在复制、重组时常被打结或套环，酶（拓扑异构酶）负责剪切再接以解开纽结。拓扑工具可量化DNA的缠绕状态。
During replication and recombination, DNA becomes knotted or catenated, and enzymes (topoisomerases) cut and rejoin to untangle it. Topology quantifies DNA's entanglement.

拓扑里亏格是什么意思，它怎么数洞？

In topology what is genus, and how does it count holes?

亏格是可连续形变下不变的洞数：球面亏格0，环面（甜甜圈）亏格1，双孔前圈亏格2。它是分类闭曲面的关键不变量。
Genus is the number of holes, invariant under deformation: sphere has genus 0, torus genus 1, double-holed surface genus 2. It classifies closed surfaces.

拓扑常被称橡皮几何，它不关心哪些性质？

Topology is called rubber-sheet geometry; which properties does it ignore?

它忽略长度、角度、面积等度量，只保留连通性、孔洞、边界等连续形变下不变的性质。三角形可被拉成圆，二者拓扑等价。
It ignores lengths, angles, and areas, keeping only properties like connectivity, holes, and boundaries that survive continuous deformation. A triangle can be stretched into a circle — topologically they match.

为什么说咖啡杯和甜甜圈在拓扑上是同一种东西？

Why is a coffee mug said to be topologically the same as a doughnut?

拓扑只关心连续形变下的不变性质，杯身加把手可连续捏成环面，二者都有一个洞（亏格1）。所以它们同胚。
Topology cares only about properties preserved under continuous deformation; a mug with a handle can be squished into a torus, both having one hole (genus 1). They are homeomorphic.

克莱因瓶是什么，为什么它没有内外之分？

What is a Klein bottle, and why does it have no inside or outside?

它是把莫比乌斯带的边界再接回自身的不可定向闭曲面，在三维空间里必须自相交才能画出来。在四维中它无边界、无内外。
It is a non-orientable closed surface made by gluing a Möbius strip's boundary to itself; in 3D it must self-intersect. In 4D it has no boundary and no distinct inside or outside.

柯尼斯堡七桥问题为什么无解，欧拉怎么解决的？

Why is the Königsberg seven-bridges problem unsolvable, and how did Euler solve it?

欧拉把陆地化为顶点、桥化为边，证明一笔画要求最多两个奇度顶点，而四块陆地都是奇数度。这开创了图论与拓扑。
Euler modeled land as vertices and bridges as edges, showing an Eulerian path needs at most two odd-degree vertices, but all four lands had odd degree. This founded graph theory and topology.
