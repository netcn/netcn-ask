为什么估计总体方差时，样本方差要除以 n-1 而不是 n？

Why does the sample variance divide by n-1 instead of n when estimating the population variance?

因为样本均值本身是从数据算出来的，它会把数据“拉向”自己，使残差平方和偏小：用 n 作分母得到的方差平均来说低于真方差。除以 n-1 恰好修正这个偏差，使 E[s²]=σ²，即得到无偏估计。自由度视角是：样本均值已知后，n 个残差里只有 n-1 个可以自由变化，最后一个被约束。n 较大时两者几乎相等，但小样本下差别显著。
The sample mean is itself computed from the data and pulls the data toward it, shrinking the sum of squared residuals: dividing by n yields an average below the true variance. Dividing by n-1 corrects exactly this bias, giving E[s²]=σ², an unbiased estimator. In degrees-of-freedom terms, once the sample mean is fixed, only n-1 of the n residuals can vary freely; the last is constrained. For large n the two nearly agree, but the difference matters for small samples.

贝叶斯因子（Bayes factor）是怎样用“数据支持度之比”来比较两个模型的？

How does the Bayes factor compare two models via 'the ratio of how much the data supports each'?

贝叶斯因子 BF₁₂ 等于在模型 M₁ 与 M₂ 下观测数据的边际似然之比 P(D|M₁)/P(D|M₂)，大于 1 表示数据更支持 M₁。它直接量化证据强度、不依赖先验概率的取舍，但边际似然需对参数空间积分，计算常依赖 MCMC；与 AIC/BIC 不同，它给出的是“证据倍数”而非点估计惩罚。
The Bayes factor BF₁₂ is the ratio of marginal likelihoods P(D|M₁)/P(D|M₂); above 1 the data favor M₁. It quantifies evidence strength directly, independent of prior model probabilities, but needs integration over parameter space (often via MCMC); unlike AIC/BIC it reports an 'odds multiple,' not a penalized point estimate.

为什么无数互不相干的随机量加起来，结果都服从正态分布？

Why do sums of countless unrelated random quantities always follow a normal distribution?

中心极限定理说：只要各随机量相互独立、方差有限，大量它们的平均值（标准化后）就近似服从标准正态分布，无论原分布长什么样。原因在于卷积的多次迭代会把分布的细节“抹平”，只保留均值和方差两个参数。标准误 σ/√n 也随之而来：样本量翻四倍，估计误差才减半。这是统计推断里置信区间和假设检验的数学根据。
The central limit theorem states that as long as random variables are independent with finite variance, the standardized average of many of them is approximately standard normal — no matter what the original distribution looks like. Convolution iterated many times smooths away distribution details, leaving only the mean and variance. The standard error σ/√n follows: quadruple the sample size and the estimation error only halves. This is the mathematical basis of confidence intervals and hypothesis testing.

什么是标准差？

What is standard deviation?

标准差衡量一组数据里每个值偏离平均数的典型幅度：各值与均值之差平方后取平均得方差，再开平方就是标准差。开方不只是走形式，它把量纲从平方单位还原成与原始数据相同，所以标准差能和均值直接比大小，方差不能。用处在于对照均值看离散：均值相同而标准差悬殊，均值的代表性就完全不同；单位不同时，还要改比它与均值的比值。
Standard deviation measures the typical way each value in a set departs from the mean: the differences from the mean are squared and averaged into the variance, and the square root of that is the standard deviation. The root is not a formality - it brings the units back from the squared scale to those of the original data, so the standard deviation can be compared directly with the mean while the variance cannot. Its use lies in reading spread against the mean: two groups with the same mean but very different standard deviations have means of quite different representativeness, and where the units differ, it is the ratio of standard deviation to mean that should be compared.

尺子量出的每个数都带误差，用它们算出的结果误差会更大吗？

Every number from a ruler carries error — does the result computed from them get even more inaccurate?

会，误差会传播。加减运算里绝对误差相加：比如两个各带 ±0.1 厘米的长度相加，结果误差约 ±0.2 厘米。乘除运算里则是相对误差（误差除以数值）相加：10±0.1 乘 20±0.2，相对误差约 1% 加 1%，共约 2%。理解这一点，实验和工程里才不会被“看似精确”的数字骗到。
Yes — errors propagate. In addition or subtraction, absolute errors add: two lengths each off by ±0.1 cm give a sum accurate to about ±0.2 cm. In multiplication or division, relative errors (error divided by value) add: 10±0.1 times 20±0.2 carries a relative error of about 1% plus 1%, roughly 2%. Knowing this keeps experiments and engineering from being fooled by seemingly precise numbers.

什么是统计功效（statistical power），为什么样本越大越容易检出真实效应？

What is statistical power, and why does a larger sample make it easier to detect a true effect?

统计功效是当原假设实际为假时，检验正确拒绝它的概率，等于 1−β（β 为第二类错误率）。效应量固定时，样本量越大、测量越精确，检验统计量的分布越分离，功效越高；这正是实验设计要预先估算“所需样本量”的原因。
Statistical power is the probability of correctly rejecting a false null hypothesis, equal to 1−β (β being the Type II error rate). With a fixed effect size, larger samples and less noise separate the test-statistic distributions more, raising power—which is why experiments pre-compute the required sample size.

刀切法（jackknife）是怎样通过“每次留掉一个样本”来估计偏差和方差的？

How does the jackknife estimate bias and variance by 'leaving out one sample at a time'?

刀切法依次从 n 个样本中去掉第 i 个、用其余 n−1 个重算统计量，得到 n 个“伪值”，再用伪值的均值与离散程度估计原估计的偏差与标准误。它是自助法的前身，思路直观、计算稳定，适合样本量不大时的误差评估。
The jackknife drops the i-th of n samples one at a time, recomputes the statistic on the remaining n−1, and forms n 'pseudovalues' whose mean and spread estimate the original estimator's bias and standard error. A precursor to the bootstrap, it is intuitive and stable for modest samples.

为什么很多测量数据会聚成一个“钟形”？

Why do many kinds of measurements bunch up in a bell shape?

身高、体重、测量误差等数据常围绕一个平均值铺开，中间高、两头低，形成钟形曲线，这就是正态分布。原因是这类数值往往是大量独立微小因素叠加的结果：根据中心极限定理，许多相互独立、方差有限的随机量相加，整体就近似服从正态分布，无论单个因素长什么样。它的形状只由平均值 μ 和标准差 σ 决定，约 68% 的数据落在 μ±σ 内，95% 落在 μ±2σ 内。
Heights, weights, and measurement errors spread around a mean — high in the middle, low at both tails — tracing a bell-shaped curve, the normal distribution. The reason is that such values aggregate many small independent effects: by the central limit theorem, sums of many independent random quantities with finite variance are approximately normal regardless of their individual shapes. Its shape is set entirely by the mean μ and standard deviation σ, with about 68% of data within μ±σ and 95% within μ±2σ.

质量检测中为什么常用“3σ 原则”作为控制线？

Why is the "3-sigma rule" commonly used as the control limit in quality inspection?

在统计过程控制（休哈特控制图）中，样本统计量（如每批的均值）在中心极限定理下近似服从正态分布，落在均值 ±3σ 之外的概率仅约 0.3%。因此控制图上的点一旦越出 3σ 线，就被视为过程可能失控的异常信号，提示需要停机检查，而不是随机波动。
In statistical process control (Shewhart charts), sample statistics such as batch means are approximately normal by the central limit theorem, so falling beyond mean ±3σ has probability only about 0.3%. A point crossing the 3σ limit on a control chart is therefore treated as a signal of possible out-of-control process—an assignable cause to investigate, not random variation.

什么是回归分析？

What is regression analysis?

回归分析是给变量间的关系配一条能算的线：先假设因变量随自变量怎样变，再调参数让预测与观测整体最接近，常用最小二乘，即把各点竖直偏差的平方和调到最小。平方让偏得远的点更吃重，代价是个别异常值就能把线拖走。它给出自变量每增一个单位时因变量平均变多少，如用身高估体重；但它只描述共同变化，不证明因果，向样本范围外推也不可靠。
Regression fits a calculable line to the relationship between variables: you propose how a dependent variable responds to the explanatory variables, then tune the parameters so that predictions match the observations as closely as possible overall, the standard choice being least squares, which drives the sum of the squared vertical deviations of the points to its minimum. Squaring puts heavier weight on the points that miss badly, and the price is that a few outliers can drag the whole line. What it delivers is the average change in the outcome for each unit change in the input, as when height is used to estimate weight; but it describes covariation only, it cannot prove cause, and extrapolating beyond the range the sample covers is unreliable.

极大似然估计在什么条件下相合且渐近正态？为什么它的渐近方差恰好是 Fisher 信息的倒数？

Under what conditions is a maximum likelihood estimator consistent and asymptotically normal, and why is its asymptotic variance exactly the inverse of the Fisher information?

在参数可识别、支撑集不随参数变化、对数似然二阶可微且求导与期望可交换等正则条件下，sqrt(n)·(θ_mle − θ0) 收敛到均值零、方差为 I(θ0)^(-1) 的正态分布，其中 I 为单次观测的 Fisher 信息。直观地说，Fisher 信息度量对数似然在真值附近的平均曲率：峰越尖，数据对参数的约束越强，估计越稳定。这也正是达到该方差的估计被称为渐近有效的原因。
Under regularity conditions such as identifiability, parameter-independent support, a twice differentiable log-likelihood with differentiation interchangeable with expectation, sqrt(n)·(θ_mle − θ0) converges to a normal distribution with mean zero and variance I(θ0)^(-1), where I is the per-observation Fisher information. Intuitively, the Fisher information measures the average curvature of the log-likelihood near the true value: the sharper the peak, the more strongly the data pin down the parameter. This is why estimators attaining that variance are called asymptotically efficient.

看到 p=0.03,许多人会解读为『原假设有 97% 的概率不成立』,这个读法错在哪一步?p 值真正度量的是什么对象?

Confronted with p=0.03, many people read it as a 97% chance that the null hypothesis is false. At which step does that reading break down, and what object does a p-value actually measure?

p 值的定义是:在假定原假设成立的前提下,出现与手头数据同等极端或更极端结果的概率,即 P(数据至少这么极端 | H0 为真),它谈论数据而非假设本身。把它误读成 P(H0 为假 | 数据) 颠倒了条件化的方向,并悄悄掺入了两个假设各自的先验可信度,这正是所谓检察官谬误的统计版本。同一个 p=0.03 在不同先验与不同样本量之下对应的可信结论可以相去甚远,美国统计学会 2016 年的正式声明因此明确禁止将 p 值解释为原假设为真或为假的概率。
By definition, a p-value is the probability, calculated assuming the null hypothesis holds, of obtaining results at least as extreme as those observed: P(data this extreme | H0 true) — a statement about the data, never about the hypothesis. Misreading it as P(H0 false | data) reverses the direction of conditioning and silently imports the prior plausibility of the competing hypotheses, the statistical face of the prosecutor's fallacy. One and the same p = 0.03 supports wildly different conclusions under different priors and sample sizes, and the American Statistical Association's 2016 formal statement accordingly forbids interpreting a p-value as the probability that the null hypothesis is true or false.

单因素方差分析为什么要用“组间均方 / 组内均方”之比构造 F 统计量，而不是对每两组直接做 t 检验？

Why does one-way ANOVA construct its F statistic as the ratio of the between-group mean square to the within-group mean square, rather than running a t-test on every pair of groups?

方差分析把总变异分解为组间（处理效应）与组内（随机误差）两部分。原假设“各组均值相等”成立时，两种均方估计的是同一误差方差，比值服从 F 分布；处理若真有效应，组间项被抬高，F 随之增大。两两 t 检验要做 k(k-1)/2 次，即便每次水平仅 0.05，累计第一类错误率也急剧膨胀；F 检验一次给出整体结论，并以全部残差估计误差。
ANOVA partitions total variability into a between-group part, which reflects treatment effects, and a within-group part, which reflects random error. Under the null hypothesis of equal group means, both mean squares estimate the same error variance, so their ratio follows an F distribution; if a treatment genuinely works, the between-group term inflates and F grows. Pairwise t-tests across k groups require k(k-1)/2 comparisons, and even at level 0.05 apiece the family-wise Type I error rate balloons. A single F test controls that overall rate while estimating error variance from all residuals at once.

随机化区组设计中，为什么按已知的干扰来源划分区组能提高处理间比较的精度？这种设计何时反而不如同等规模的完全随机设计？

In a randomized block design, why does grouping experimental units by a known nuisance source improve the precision of treatment comparisons, and when does the design fare worse than a completely randomized one of equal size?

把地块、批次、试验日等干扰来源划成内部均匀的区组，并在区组内随机分配处理，使处理比较都在同一区组内进行，区组间差异由此从误差项中剥离；误差平方和随之缩小，标准误变小，检验更灵敏。代价是比同等规模的完全随机设计少 b−1 个误差自由度，只有当区组间差异明显大于区内随机波动时，这一交换才划算。
Nuisance sources such as plots, batches, or testing days are divided into internally homogeneous blocks, and treatments are randomized within each block, so every treatment comparison happens inside a block while block-to-block variation is stripped out of the error term. The error sum of squares shrinks accordingly, standard errors of treatment means fall, and tests gain power. The cost is b−1 fewer error degrees of freedom than a completely randomized design of equal size; the trade pays off only when genuine block differences clearly exceed within-block noise.
