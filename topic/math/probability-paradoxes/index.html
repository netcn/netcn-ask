两个信封一个装x元另一个装2x元，你打开看到y元后，换信封的期望是1.25y元，那是否该一直换？

Two envelopes contain x and 2x; after seeing y you compute the other's expected value as 1.25y. Should you always switch?

不能，因为推导隐含假设看到的y可对应任意x的两种情形，但先验分布未定义导致期望计算无效。它揭示了无界分布下期望可能失真。
No; the calculation silently assumes y could be the smaller or larger amount for arbitrary x, but an undefined prior makes the expectation meaningless. It shows expectations can mislead under unbounded distributions.

什么是伯克森悖论，为什么医院里两种病看起来负相关其实未必？

What is Berkson's paradox, and why might two diseases appear negatively correlated in a hospital when they are not?

医院样本以至少患一种病为入院条件，会造成入院人群里两种病的共存被系统性低估，呈现虚假负相关。这是选择偏差的典型例子。
Hospital samples condition on having at least one disease, which systematically undercounts co-occurrence and creates a spurious negative correlation — a classic selection bias.

#伯克森 #选择偏差 #统计

圣彼得堡游戏的期望收益为何会发散，它揭示了什么？

Why does the St. Petersburg game have infinite expected value, and what does it reveal?

游戏奖金随抛到首次正面的次数指数增长，期望为无穷大，但没人愿付高价参与。它推动了期望效用理论的发展，说明期望金额不等于主观价值。
The payout grows exponentially with the waiting time, so expected value is infinite, yet nobody pays a fortune to play. It motivated expected utility theory: expected money is not the same as subjective value.

在圆内随机取一条弦，其长度超过内接等边三角形边长的概率是多少？为什么答案不唯一？

For a chord chosen at random in a circle, what is the probability it is longer than a side of the inscribed equilateral triangle, and why is the answer not unique?

答案取决于随机的定义，可以是1/3、1/2或1/4。它说明在连续概率中必须先明确概率测度，否则随机是模糊的。
The answer depends on how random is defined: 1/3, 1/2, or 1/4. It shows that in continuous probability you must first fix a probability measure.

什么是检察官谬误，为什么匹配概率极小不等于被告无罪概率极小？

What is the prosecutor's fallacy, and why does a tiny match probability not mean a tiny probability the defendant is innocent?

它把在随机人中特征匹配的概率误当成被告无罪时匹配的概率。要结合基础人数用贝叶斯定理，才能正确评估被告有罪的概率。
It confuses the probability of a match given innocence with the probability of innocence given a match. Bayes' theorem with the base rate is needed for the correct guilt probability.

什么是辛普森悖论，为什么分组看和合并看结论会相反？

What is Simpson's paradox, and why can grouped and combined data lead to opposite conclusions?

当数据按某个变量分层后，各层的趋势与汇总后的整体趋势可能完全相反。它常因各层样本量悬殊、混杂变量未被控制而产生。
In Simpson's paradox, trends in subgroups can reverse in the pooled data, usually because of unequal group sizes and an uncontrolled confounder.

A family has two children and at least one is a boy. What is the probability both are boys? And what if we also learn that boy was born on a Tuesday?

仅知至少一男孩时是1/3；加上星期二男孩这一更具体的信息后，概率变为13/27。额外细节改变了样本空间，从而改变了结果。
With only at least one boy the chance is 1/3; adding born on a Tuesday changes it to 13/27. The extra detail reshapes the sample space.

某病患病率1%，检测准确率99%，若检测呈阳性，你真患病的概率是多少？

A disease affects 1% of people and a test is 99% accurate. If you test positive, what is the chance you actually have the disease?

约50%。大量健康人被误报，阳性中真病人被稀释，必须用贝叶斯结合基础患病率。
About 50%. Most people are healthy, and even a 1% false-positive rate produces many false alarms, so Bayes' theorem with the base rate is essential.

Of three prisoners one will be freed; you ask the guard will B be executed and hear yes. Did your chance of freedom change?

你（A）的获释概率从1/3升到1/2，而C的概率也升到1/2。这与蒙提霍尔相似，守卫的回答提供了额外信息。
Your (A's) chance of freedom rises from 1/3 to 1/2, as does C's. Like Monty Hall, the guard's answer supplies new information.

蒙提霍尔（三门）问题该不该换门？

Should you switch doors in the Monty Hall (three-door) problem?

应该换。你最初选中的概率是1/3，奖品在另两扇门后的概率是2/3；主持人揭示一扇空门后，这2/3全转移到剩下那扇门上，换门胜率翻倍。
Yes, switch. Your first pick has 1/3 chance; the prize is behind the other two doors with 2/3. After the host reveals an empty door, that 2/3 transfers to the remaining door, doubling your win chance.

大数定律说抛硬币正面比例会趋近1/2，这是否意味着少量抛掷也会接近？

The law of large numbers says the heads proportion approaches 1/2, but does it mean a small number of tosses will also be close?

不会，大数定律只保证长期平均收敛，短期波动仍可能很大。样本越多，比例才越稳定地贴近真值。
No; it only guarantees long-run convergence of the average. Short-term fluctuations can still be large, and stability improves only with more trials.

#大数定律 #频率 #概率

抛硬币连续出现5次正面后，下一次更可能是反面吗？

After five heads in a row, is the next fair-coin toss more likely to be tails?

不会，每次抛掷独立，正面概率仍是1/2。赌徒谬误是把长期会平衡错误地当成短期会补偿。
No; each toss is independent and still has probability 1/2 for heads. The gambler's fallacy wrongly treats long-run balance as short-run compensation.

#赌徒谬误 #独立性 #概率

生日悖论在说什么？

What does the birthday paradox say?

在23人中，两人生日相同的概率已超过50%。因为要比较的是‘两两组合’约253对，而非与某特定人比较，所以冲突来得比直觉快得多。
In a group of 23, the chance two share a birthday exceeds 50%. We compare ~253 pairs, not against one fixed person, so collisions arrive far faster than intuition suggests.
