预言纸牌的魔术，为什么常常用到模运算？

Why do card-prediction tricks often use modular arithmetic?

把牌面映射成数字后，移牌、切牌等操作等价于在模某个数下做加减。魔术师用同余关系算出牌堆中某张固定位置的牌，从而看似预言。
Mapping cards to numbers turns cuts and deals into addition or subtraction modulo n. The magician uses congruences to track a card at a fixed position, seemingly predicting it.

扑克牌魔术里四张A怎么会凭空多出来？

In a card trick, how can four aces seem to multiply?

这类魔术多用强迫选牌、预先叠放和错位收牌等手法，配合观众注意力的转移。数学上靠排列组合的确定性，而非真的变出牌。
Such tricks rely on force selections, pre-stacked orders and false shuffles plus misdirection. Mathematically they use the determinism of permutations, not real multiplication of cards.

一个读心小魔术：想个三位数，把它重复成六位数，为什么一定能被7、11、13整除？

Magic trick: think of a 3-digit number, repeat it to make a 6-digit number, why is it always divisible by 7, 11 and 13?

把abc写成abcabc，等于abc×1001，而1001=7×11×13。所以无论想什么三位数，重复后都能被这三个数整除，魔术师据此猜出原数。
Writing abc as abcabc equals abc×1001, and 1001=7×11×13. So whatever 3-digit number you pick, the repeat is divisible by all three, letting the magician recover it.
