张益唐在孪生素数猜想上做出了什么突破性贡献？

What breakthrough did Yitang Zhang make on the twin prime conjecture?

孪生素数猜想认为存在无穷多对相差为 2 的素数（如 11 和 13）。2013 年，张益唐证明存在某个有限常数，使得存在无穷多对素数之差不超过该常数（他给出的界是 7000 万），首次将「无穷多对」与「有限间隔」联系起来。随后 Polymath 协作项目把该上界大幅缩小到 246。
The twin prime conjecture asserts infinitely many prime pairs differing by 2 (e.g., 11 and 13). In 2013 Yitang Zhang proved that there is some finite bound such that infinitely many prime pairs differ by no more than it—he gave 70 million—the first result linking 'infinitely many' with 'bounded gaps.' The Polymath project later reduced the bound to 246.

What does Fermat's Last Theorem state, and who finally proved it?

费马大定理为什么困扰了数学家三百多年？

Why did Fermat's Last Theorem baffle mathematicians for over 350 years?

费马 1637 年在书边写下“xⁿ+yⁿ=zⁿ 在 n>2 时无正整数解”，并声称有巧妙证明但未留下。欧拉解决了 n=3 的情形，n=4 可用费马的无穷递降法处理，此后每代数学家只攻克了个别指数。直到 1994 年怀尔斯（在泰勒协助下补正）借助椭圆曲线与模形式的联系完成证明，所用方法远超原题本身。
In 1637 Fermat wrote in a book margin that xⁿ+yⁿ=zⁿ has no positive integer solutions for n>2, claiming a marvelous proof he never recorded. Euler handled n=3; the n=4 case follows from Fermat's own infinite descent; each generation conquered one exponent at a time. Only in 1994 did Wiles—with Taylor's help to patch a gap—finish the proof using a link between elliptic curves and modular forms, methods far beyond the original problem.

费马小定理说 a^p≡a (mod p)，它为什么总是成立？

Fermat's little theorem says a^p≡a (mod p) — why does it always hold?

先用二项式定理：对 (a+1)^p 展开，中间所有二项式系数 C(p,k)（1≤k≤p-1）都含因子 p，因为 p 是质数，分子里的 p 不会被 1 到 p-1 约掉，所以 (a+1)^p≡a^p+1 (mod p)。对 a 从 0 开始归纳，结合 1^p≡1，就得 a^p≡a (mod p)。p 不整除 a 时两边除以 a 得 a^(p-1)≡1，这是 RSA 解密正确性的根基。
Expand (a+1)^p by the binomial theorem. Every middle coefficient C(p,k) for 1≤k≤p-1 is divisible by p, since p is prime and the factor p in the numerator is not canceled by any k in the denominator; hence (a+1)^p≡a^p+1 (mod p). Inducting on a from 1^p≡1 gives a^p≡a (mod p). When p does not divide a, divide by a to get a^(p-1)≡1, the cornerstone of RSA decryption.

黎曼猜想到底在猜什么？它和质数的分布有什么关系？

What exactly does the Riemann hypothesis claim, and how does it connect to the distribution of primes?

黎曼把欧拉的乘积公式 ζ(s)=Σ 1/n^s=Π(1-p^(-s))^(-1) 延拓到整个复平面，发现 ζ 的非平凡零点与质数分布密切相关。素数定理 π(x)~Li(x) 1900 年前后被证明，但它只给出主项；零点位置决定误差。黎曼猜想断言：所有非平凡零点都落在实部为 1/2 的临界线上。若成立，π(x) 与 Li(x) 的误差可降到约 √x·log x。它被列为千禧年七大难题之一，至今未解，且其成立与否都会对质数分布的精细结构产生巨大影响。
Riemann extended Euler's product formula ζ(s)=Σ 1/n^s=Π(1-p^(-s))^(-1) to the whole complex plane and showed the non-trivial zeros of ζ govern the distribution of primes. The prime number theorem π(x)~Li(x) was proved around 1900, but it gives only the leading term; the zeros dictate the error. The Riemann hypothesis asserts that every non-trivial zero lies on the critical line Re(s)=1/2. If true, the error between π(x) and Li(x) drops to about √x·log x. One of the seven Millennium Prize problems, it remains unsolved, and either outcome would profoundly shape the fine structure of prime distribution.

怎样用扩展欧几里得算法求出模逆元？

How does the extended Euclidean algorithm compute modular inverses?

以求满足 11x≡1 (mod 30) 的 x 为例。先对 (30,11) 辗转相除：30=2·11+8，11=1·8+3，8=2·3+2，3=1·2+1。再反向回代：1=3−1·2=3−(8−2·3)=3·3−8=(11−8)·3−8=3·11−4·8=3·11−4·(30−2·11)=11·11−4·30，于是 11·11≡1 (mod 30)，逆元是 11。一般地，只要 gcd(a,m)=1，回代总能把逆元表示为 a 与 m 的线性组合。
Take solving 11x≡1 (mod 30). First run Euclid's algorithm on (30,11): 30=2·11+8, 11=1·8+3, 8=2·3+2, 3=1·2+1. Then back-substitute: 1=3−1·2=3−(8−2·3)=3·3−8=(11−8)·3−8=3·11−4·8=3·11−4·(30−2·11)=11·11−4·30. Hence 11·11≡1 (mod 30) and the inverse is 11. In general, whenever gcd(a,m)=1, back-substitution expresses the inverse as a linear combination of a and m.

Euler's theorem says a^φ(n)≡1 (mod n) whenever gcd(a,n)=1, and since ed-1 is a multiple of φ(n), we get m^(ed)≡m (mod n) — the original text returns. 为什么欧几里得算法（辗转相除法）一定能求出两个正整数的最大公约数？

Why does the Euclidean algorithm always succeed in finding the greatest common divisor of two positive integers?

核心是恒等式 gcd(a,b)=gcd(b, a mod b)：设 a=bq+r，凡同时整除 a 与 b 的数必整除 r，反之亦然，所以新旧两对数的公因数集合完全相同，最大公约数保持不变。由于每步余数严格变小且非负，算法必在有限步内遇到余数 0，此时最后一个非零除数就是所求的 gcd，终止性与正确性由此同时得到保证。
The engine is the identity gcd(a,b)=gcd(b, a mod b): writing a=bq+r, any common divisor of a and b divides r, and conversely, so the old and new pairs share exactly the same set of common divisors and hence the same gcd. Because each remainder is strictly smaller and nonnegative, the algorithm reaches a zero remainder in finitely many steps; the last nonzero divisor is the answer. Termination and correctness follow together.

Why can the composite 341 = 11 × 31 fool the Fermat test, so that 2^340 ≡ 1 (mod 341) still holds — and what does this warn us about primality testing?

费马小定理只保证素数满足 2^(p-1) ≡ 1，逆命题不真。对 341：模 11 时 2 的阶为 10；模 31 时由 2^5 ≡ 1 知阶为 5；340 是两者的倍数，两模皆余 1，中国剩余定理合并得 2^340 ≡ 1 (mod 341)。这就是伪素数：单底检验误报，实用须换底数或用 Miller-Rabin。
Fermat's little theorem guarantees the congruence only for primes; its converse fails. For 341, modulo 11 the element 2 has order 10 and 340 is a multiple of 10; modulo 31, since 2^5 = 32 ≡ 1, its order is only 5 and 340 is likewise a multiple of 5. Both congruences hold, so the Chinese remainder theorem merges them into 2^340 ≡ 1 (mod 341). Such composites are pseudoprimes to base 2: a single-base Fermat test gives false positives, so practical testing varies the bases or turns to Miller-Rabin.
