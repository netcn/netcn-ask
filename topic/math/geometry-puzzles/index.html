两点之间小球最快滑下的路径是直线吗？

Is the straight line the fastest path for a ball to slide between two points?

不是。最速降线是一条摆线（圆滚线）：先陡降积累速度，再用曲线抄近路，总用时比直线更短。这是变分法的经典问题。
No. The brachistochrone is a cycloid: it drops steeply first to build speed, then curves to shorten the path, beating the straight line in time. A classic calculus-of-variations problem.

埃舍尔的圆极限系列与圆内的双曲几何有什么关系？

How are Escher's Circle Limit works related to hyperbolic geometry in a disk?

它们描绘了庞加莱圆盘模型：圆内越靠边距离越大，天使与魔鬼的尺寸在欧氏眼里缩小、在双曲意义下却相等。这是双曲平面在有限圆盘里的投影。
They depict the Poincaré disk model: near the rim distance grows, so angels and devils shrink in Euclidean view but are equal in hyperbolic sense. It is a hyperbolic plane projected into a finite disk.

埃舍尔的镶嵌画用了什么数学原理？

What mathematical principle do Escher's tessellations use?

他用平移、旋转、反射等对称把平面无缝铺满（密铺），常借助群论中的壁纸群描述重复模式。人与飞鸟可互为背景地填满整个平面。
He used translations, rotations, and reflections to tile the plane seamlessly (tessellation), often described by wallpaper groups in group theory. Figures and background interlock to fill the plane.

彭罗斯镶嵌奇在哪？

What is strange about Penrose tiling?

它用两种菱形就能铺满平面却绝不出现周期性重复，且具有五重旋转对称。这种‘准晶体’结构后来在真实材料中得到印证。
It tiles the plane with just two rhombus shapes yet never repeats periodically, and shows fivefold rotational symmetry. Such 'quasicrystal' structure was later found in real materials.

等周问题说给定周长，什么图形面积最大？

The isoperimetric problem: for a given perimeter, which shape has the largest area?

答案是圆。平面上给定周长的所有闭曲线中，圆围成面积最大；反之给定面积时圆周长最短。它可用变分法或对称化严格证明。
The answer is the circle: among all closed curves with fixed perimeter, the circle encloses the maximum area, and conversely it minimizes perimeter for fixed area. Proved via calculus of variations or symmetrization.

阿基米德螺旋有什么性质，和等角螺线有何不同？

What are the properties of the Archimedean spiral, and how does it differ from a logarithmic spiral?

阿基米德螺线满足极径与极角成正比（r=aθ），相邻圈间距恒定，常用于卷尺与凸轮。等角螺线r=a^θ的圈间距则按几何级数增长。
The Archimedean spiral has r=aθ, so successive turns are evenly spaced — used in roll tapes and cams. The logarithmic spiral r=a^θ spaces its turns geometrically.

蜜蜂蜂巢为什么是六边形而不是正方形或三角形？

Why are honeycombs hexagonal rather than square or triangular?

在给定周长下，六边形能以最少蜡壁围出最大面积，且能无空隙铺满平面。这是等周与密铺共同优化的结果，高效又省材料。
Among tilings, hexagons enclose the most area per wall length and tile the plane without gaps. It is the joint optimum of isoperimetry and tessellation — efficient and material-saving.

只用一种正多边形能铺满平面的有哪几种？

Which regular polygons alone can tile the plane?

只有正三角形、正方形和正六边形三种，因为它们的内角能整除360度。正五边形不行，会在拼缝处留空隙。
Only the equilateral triangle, square and regular hexagon work alone, because their interior angles divide 360°. The regular pentagon leaves gaps.
