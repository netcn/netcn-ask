高斯在统计学里留下了哪个核心概念？

What core statistics concept did Gauss leave us?

他系统发展了最小二乘法并奠定正态分布（高斯分布）的理论，用以处理天文观测误差。‘钟形曲线’由此成为误差与许多自然现象的模型。
He developed the method of least squares and the theory of the normal (Gaussian) distribution for astronomical observation errors. The bell curve became a model for errors and many natural phenomena.

伽罗瓦与群论有什么关系？

What is Galois's connection to group theory?

法国天才伽罗瓦在 19 世纪创立群论，用“对称结构”来判断多项式方程能否用根式求解。他证明了五次及以上的一般方程没有通用的根式解法，把代数方程问题转化为对群的研究。他的思想深刻影响了现代代数，却因决斗早逝，年仅 20 岁。
The French prodigy Galois founded group theory in the 19th century, using symmetry structures to decide whether polynomial equations can be solved by radicals. He proved that general equations of degree five or higher have no general radical solution, turning the problem into the study of groups. His ideas deeply shaped modern algebra, yet he died in a duel at only 20.

莱布尼茨在 1679 年提出的二进制，和中国的《易经》八卦有什么巧合与误解？

What coincidence and misconception links Leibniz's 1679 binary system with the I Ching's trigrams?

莱布尼茨发明 0/1 二进制后，看到传教士寄来的《易经》六十四卦图，发现卦象阴阳两爻恰好对应 0/1 的二进制排列，便在 1703 年发表文章盛赞这是“古老中国智慧”。但卦象本为占卜符号、并非计数系统，这只是跨文化的美丽误会，却让二进制更早为人所知。
After inventing 0/1 binary, Leibniz saw the I Ching's 64 hexagrams and noticed their yin-yang lines match binary 0/1 arrangements, publishing in 1703 praising it as ancient Chinese wisdom. The hexagrams were oracles, not a counting system—a beautiful cross-cultural misunderstanding that nonetheless spread binary ideas earlier.

牛顿和莱布尼茨关于微积分的争论是什么？

What was the Newton-Leibniz calculus dispute?

17 世纪末，英国的牛顿与德国的莱布尼茨各自独立发明了微积分，却因“谁先发明”爆发了激烈的优先权之争。两人记号体系不同：牛顿用点表示导数，莱布尼茨用 dx/dy，后者沿用至今。这场争论一度分裂了英欧数学界，但两人都被承认为微积分的奠基人。
In the late 17th century, Britain's Newton and Germany's Leibniz each independently invented calculus, but a fierce priority dispute erupted over who was first. Their notations differed: Newton used dots for derivatives, Leibniz used dx/dy, the latter surviving today. The feud split British and continental mathematics for a time, yet both are credited as founders of calculus.

斐波那契数列是什么，为什么有趣？

What is the Fibonacci sequence and why is it interesting?

斐波那契数列从 0、1 开始，每一项等于前两项之和：0,1,1,2,3,5,8…。它出现在许多自然现象中，如花瓣数和松果螺旋，相邻两项之比会趋近于黄金分割比。该数列由斐波那契在介绍印度-阿拉伯算术的书中引入西方。
The Fibonacci sequence starts 0, 1 and each term is the sum of the previous two: 0,1,1,2,3,5,8... It appears in many natural phenomena, such as petal counts and pinecone spirals, and the ratio of neighboring terms approaches the golden ratio. Fibonacci introduced it to the West in a book on Indian-Arabic arithmetic.

索菲·热尔曼（Sophie Germain）如何以男性笔名推进了费马大定理的研究？

How did Sophie Germain advance the study of Fermat's Last Theorem under a male pen name?

19 世纪初，热尔曼因女性身份被拒于学界门外，遂以“勒布朗先生”之名与高斯通信，并提出“热尔曼素数”（p 使 2p+1 也为素数）来处理费马大定理的第一种情形，证明了该情形下对一类素数成立。她的工作是首项重要的女性数学贡献，后获哥廷根大学荣誉博士。
In early 1800s, barred from academia as a woman, Germain corresponded with Gauss as 'M. Le Blanc' and introduced 'Germain primes' (p with 2p+1 also prime) to handle the first case of Fermat's Last Theorem, proving it for a class of primes. Her work was a pioneering female contribution and earned an honorary doctorate from Göttingen.

毕达哥拉斯定理（勾股定理）是什么？

What is the Pythagorean theorem?

直角三角形两直角边边长的平方和等于斜边边长的平方，即 a²+b²=c²。它既能用来求未知边长，也揭示了平面几何中距离的本质。该定理在世界多地独立被发现，是中国古代“勾三股四弦五”的推广。
In a right triangle, the sum of the squares of the two legs equals the square of the hypotenuse: a²+b²=c². It finds unknown side lengths and reveals the nature of distance in plane geometry. Discovered independently in many regions, it generalizes the ancient Chinese 3-4-5 triangle relation.

祖冲之在圆周率上做了什么贡献？

What did Zu Chongzhi contribute to pi?

中国南北朝数学家祖冲之在 5 世纪把圆周率精确计算到 3.1415926 与 3.1415927 之间，并提出约率 22/7 和密率 355/113。在当时的计算条件下，这一精度领先世界近千年。密率 355/113 是极好的分数近似。
The Chinese mathematician Zu Chongzhi, in the 5th century, computed pi to between 3.1415926 and 3.1415927 and proposed the rough ratio 22/7 and the precise ratio 355/113. Under the computational limits of his time, this accuracy led the world by nearly a millennium. The ratio 355/113 is an excellent fractional approximation.

笛卡尔坐标系是怎么把代数和几何联系起来的？

How does the Cartesian coordinate system link algebra and geometry?

法国数学家笛卡尔提出用一对数（x, y）表示平面上的点，从而把几何图形变成代数方程，把方程变成图形。这一“坐标几何”让曲线可以用公式研究，也为微积分和解析几何奠定基础。它至今是所有科学和工程绘图的语言。
The French mathematician Descartes proposed representing plane points by a pair (x, y), turning geometric figures into algebraic equations and equations into figures. This coordinate geometry lets curves be studied by formulas and laid the groundwork for calculus and analytic geometry. It remains the language of all scientific and engineering drawing.

阿基米德是谁，他有哪些成就？

Who was Archimedes and what did he achieve?

阿基米德是古希腊（公元前 3 世纪）的数学家与工程师，发现了浮力原理和杠杆原理，并近似计算出圆周率。他用“穷竭法”求面积和体积，是积分思想的先驱。传说他在浴缸中悟出浮力，惊呼“尤里卡”（我发现了）。
Archimedes was a Greek mathematician and engineer of the 3rd century BCE who discovered the principle of buoyancy and the law of the lever and approximated pi. Using the method of exhaustion for areas and volumes, he anticipated integral calculus. Legend says he discovered buoyancy in a bath and cried Eureka (I found it).

数学家欧拉是谁，他为何著名？

Who was the mathematician Euler and why is he famous?

莱昂哈德·欧拉是 18 世纪瑞士数学家，著作数量和影响力都极其惊人。他系统化了微积分、图论和数论，并留下了著名的欧拉公式 e^{iπ}+1=0。今天许多数学符号（如 π、e、i、f(x)）都源自他的工作。
Leonhard Euler was an 18th-century Swiss mathematician of astonishing output and influence. He systematized calculus, graph theory, and number theory and gave the famous Euler formula e^{iπ}+1=0. Many symbols we use today (π, e, i, f(x)) come from his work.

数学家高斯为何被称为“数学王子”？

Why is Gauss called the Prince of Mathematicians?

卡尔·弗里德里希·高斯是 19 世纪德国数学家，在数论、统计学、天文学和电磁学等领域都有奠基性贡献。他年仅 19 岁就证明了二次互反律并构造了正十七边形尺规作图。其著作严谨深刻，被誉为数学史上影响力最大的人物之一。
Carl Friedrich Gauss was a 19th-century German mathematician who made foundational contributions to number theory, statistics, astronomy, and electromagnetism. At 19 he proved the law of quadratic reciprocity and constructed the regular 17-gon by straightedge and compass. Renowned for rigor and depth, he is among the most influential figures in math history.

我们用的“阿拉伯数字”其实来自哪里？

Where do the Arabic numerals we use actually come from?

今天通用的 0、1、2…9 其实起源于印度，经阿拉伯学者翻译和传播后传入欧洲，因此欧洲人称之为“阿拉伯数字”。它采用位值制和零符号，极大简化了计算和记录。这一体系取代罗马数字成为现代世界的标准。
The 0,1,2...9 we use today actually originated in India and reached Europe through Arab scholars who translated and spread them, so Europeans called them Arabic numerals. Using place value and a zero symbol, they greatly simplified calculation and record-keeping. This system replaced Roman numerals as the world standard.

黄金分割是什么，为什么被认为美观？

What is the golden ratio and why is it considered beautiful?

黄金分割比 φ≈1.618，定义为把一条线段分成两部分，使整体与长段之比等于长段与短段之比。它出现在五角星、贝壳螺旋和许多建筑比例中。许多人觉得含 φ 的比例和谐悦目，但它“无处不在”的说法常被夸大。
The golden ratio φ≈1.618 is defined by dividing a segment so the whole-to-long part equals the long-to-short part. It appears in pentagrams, shell spirals, and many architectural proportions. Many find φ-based ratios harmonious, though claims that it is everywhere are often exaggerated.

柯尼斯堡七桥问题与图论有什么关系？

What is the Königsberg bridge problem's link to graph theory?

18 世纪柯尼斯堡有七座桥横跨两条河的分支，居民好奇能否不重复地走遍每座桥。欧拉把陆地抽象为点、桥抽象为线，证明这样的走法不可能，从而开创了图论。这是把现实问题转化为数学结构分析的经典范例。
In the 18th century Königsberg had seven bridges over branches of a river, and townsfolk wondered if one could cross each bridge exactly once. Euler abstracted land as points and bridges as lines, proved it impossible, and thereby founded graph theory. It is a classic example of turning a real problem into mathematical structure analysis.

传说中“在棋盘格上按倍数放麦粒”的故事，为什么最后一格的麦粒多到惊人？

In the legend of 'doubling grains of wheat on chessboard squares,' why does the last square hold an astonishing amount?

故事说第 1 格放 1 粒、第 2 格 2 粒、每格翻倍，到第 64 格就是 2⁶³ 粒，全部合计约 2⁶⁴−1 ≈ 1.8×10¹⁹ 粒，超过全球年产量。它用国际象棋 64 格讲清了“指数增长”的威力，是数学文化里最经典的指数爆炸例子。
The tale places 1 grain on square 1, 2 on square 2, doubling each time; square 64 alone holds 2⁶³ grains, and the total is about 2⁶⁴−1 ≈ 1.8×10¹⁹—more than world annual output. Using the chessboard's 64 squares, it shows the power of exponential growth, a classic math-culture example.

How did young Gauss quickly sum 1+2+…+100?

他把首尾配对：(1+100)+(2+99)+… 共50对，每对都是101，所以 50×101=5050。这就是等差数列求和的雏形。
He paired ends: (1+100)+(2+99)+… makes 50 pairs each summing to 101, so 50×101=5050. It is the seed of arithmetic-series summation.

什么是圆周率 π？

What is pi (π)?

π 是圆的周长与直径的比值，对任何大小的圆都相同，约等于 3.14159。它是一个无理数，小数无限不循环。π 不仅出现在几何里，也出现在概率、物理和许多看似无关的公式当中。
π is the ratio of a circle's circumference to its diameter, the same for every circle, approximately 3.14159. It is irrational with a non-repeating infinite decimal. π appears not only in geometry but also in probability, physics, and many seemingly unrelated formulas.

孪生素数猜想是什么？

What is the twin prime conjecture?

孪生素数指相差 2 的一对素数，如 (3,5)、(11,13)。孪生素数猜想认为这样的素数对有无穷多对，但至今未被证明。2013 年张益唐在弱形式上取得突破，证明存在间隔有限的无穷多素数对，引发广泛关注。
Twin primes are pairs of primes differing by 2, like (3,5) and (11,13). The twin prime conjecture says there are infinitely many such pairs, but it remains unproven. In 2013 Yitang Zhang made a breakthrough on a weaker form, proving infinitely many prime pairs with bounded gap, drawing wide attention.

庞加莱猜想是什么？

What is the Poincaré conjecture?

庞加莱猜想问：任何看起来像三维球面的封闭三维空间，是否本质上就是一个三维球面？它属于拓扑学，是千禧年七大难题之一。2003 年前后，佩雷尔曼用几何分析的方法给出了证明，并拒绝了菲尔兹奖。
The Poincaré conjecture asks whether any closed three-dimensional space that looks like a 3-sphere is essentially a 3-sphere. Belonging to topology, it is one of the seven Millennium problems. Around 2003 Perelman proved it using geometric analysis and declined the Fields Medal.

古埃及人怎样记数？

How did ancient Egyptians write numbers?

古埃及人使用十进制但非位值制，用不同的象形符号表示 1、10、100、1000 等，通过重复累加来表达数目。例如数字 23 就写成两个 10 的符号加三个 1 的符号。他们没有表示“零”的独立符号，运算也主要靠分数表。
Ancient Egyptians used a decimal but non-positional system, with distinct hieroglyphs for 1, 10, 100, 1000, etc., repeated and added to express quantities. For example, 23 was two 10 symbols plus three 1 symbols. They had no separate symbol for zero, and calculation relied heavily on tables of fractions.

谁发明了“零”？

Who invented the number zero?

零的概念在多个古文明中独立出现，但作为真正可参与运算的数字符号，主要由古印度数学家在公元 5 世纪左右完善，后经阿拉伯世界传入欧洲。玛雅文明也独立发展出零的符号。零的引入让位值记数法和现代算术成为可能。
The concept of zero appeared independently in several ancient civilizations, but as a true numerical symbol usable in calculation it was mainly perfected by Indian mathematicians around the 5th century CE and later transmitted to Europe via the Arab world. The Maya also developed a zero symbol independently. Zero made place-value notation and modern arithmetic possible.

算盘的历史和用途是什么？

What are the history and use of the abacus?

算盘是一种古老的手动计算工具，用珠子在杆上表示数字，在中国、日本、中东等地独立演化出不同形制。它通过位值制和心算配合，能快速完成加减乘除。在电子计算器普及前，算盘是商人和账房的主力工具。
The abacus is an ancient manual calculating tool using beads on rods to represent numbers, evolving independently in China, Japan, the Middle East, and elsewhere. With place value and mental arithmetic it performs addition, subtraction, multiplication, and division quickly. Before electronic calculators, it was the main tool of merchants and accountants.

什么是罗马数字？

What are Roman numerals?

罗马数字用字母 I、V、X、L、C、D、M 表示 1、5、10、50、100、500、1000，通过加减组合成其他数，例如 IV 是 4、IX 是 9。它不使用位值制和零，做乘除很不方便，现代主要用于钟表、序号和装饰。
Roman numerals use letters I, V, X, L, C, D, M for 1, 5, 10, 50, 100, 500, 1000, combined by addition and subtraction, e.g., IV is 4 and IX is 9. Without place value or zero, multiplication and division are awkward; today they appear mainly on clocks, outlines, and decorations.
