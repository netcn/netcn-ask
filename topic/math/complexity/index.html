为什么‘旅行商’和‘背包’这类问题让我们头疼？

Why do problems like TSP and knapsack give us headaches?

它们属于 NP 难：目前没有已知能在多项式时间内精确求解的算法，输入稍大就爆炸式变难。实际靠近似、动态规划或启发式在可接受误差内解决。
They are NP-hard: no known polynomial-time exact algorithm, and difficulty explodes with size. In practice we use approximation, dynamic programming or heuristics within acceptable error.

#复杂度 #NP难

P与NP问题到底在问什么？假如某个NP完全问题——例如旅行商问题的判定版本——被找到多项式时间算法，密码学与复杂性理论将发生怎样的连锁崩塌？

What is the P versus NP question really asking — and if some NP-complete problem, say the decision version of the traveling salesman problem, were found a polynomial-time algorithm, what chain reaction would sweep through cryptography and complexity theory?

它在问：凡是答案容易验证的问题，是否都同样容易解答？P类问题可在多项式时间内求出答案，NP类问题的候选答案可在多项式时间内核对；二者是否相等悬置逾五十年，位列克雷数学研究所千禧年七大难题之首。一旦某个NP完全问题获得多项式算法，依据库克-列文定理的多项式归约，所有NP问题随之可解，RSA等公钥体系的难度根基将即刻瓦解。
It asks whether every problem whose answers are easy to verify is also easy to solve. Problems in P admit polynomial-time solutions; candidate answers to problems in NP can be checked in polynomial time; whether the two classes coincide has hung open for over half a century, heading the Clay Mathematics Institute's seven Millennium Problems. The moment any NP-complete problem gains a polynomial algorithm, polynomial reductions via the Cook-Levin theorem make all NP problems tractable too, instantly dissolving the hardness foundation of public-key schemes such as RSA.

快速排序平均为什么是 O(n log n)？

Why is quicksort average O(n log n)?

每次划分把数组大致对半分，需约 log n 层，每层总计扫描 O(n) 个元素，故平均 O(n log n)。最坏（已排序且总选端点）会退化到 O(n²)。
Each partition splits the array roughly in half, needing about log n levels, and each level scans O(n) elements total, giving average O(n log n). The worst case (sorted input, bad pivot) degrades to O(n²).

傅里叶变换把信号拆成了什么？

What does the Fourier transform decompose a signal into?

它把随时间变化的信号分解成不同频率的正弦波叠加，让你看到‘含有哪些频率、各占多少’。音乐均衡器和图像压缩都建立在它之上。
It breaks a time signal into a sum of sine waves of different frequencies, revealing which frequencies are present and how strongly. Audio equalizers and image compression build on it.

动态规划和‘暴力枚举’差在哪？

How does dynamic programming differ from 'brute-force enumeration'?

暴力把子问题重复算很多遍；动态规划把已算的结果存起来复用（记忆化），把指数级开销压成多项式。典型如最长公共子序列、背包问题。
Brute force recomputes subproblems repeatedly; dynamic programming stores and reuses results (memoization), turning exponential cost into polynomial. Classic for longest common subsequence and knapsack.

为什么‘排序’是计算机里的基本功？

Why is 'sorting' a basic skill in computing?

许多任务（查找、去重、合并、中位数）都先排序会更快；而比较排序的理论下界是 O(n log n)，是算法课的第一个重要结论。
Many tasks (search, dedupe, merge, median) get faster after sorting; the theoretical lower bound for comparison sorting is O(n log n), a first key result in algorithms.
