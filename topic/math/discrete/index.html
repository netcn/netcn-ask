拉姆齐理论想说明「完全无序」为什么不可能？

How does Ramsey theory show that 'complete disorder' is impossible?

拉姆齐理论研究：在足够大的结构中，无论怎样染色或分类，必然会出现某种有序的局部模式。经典结论是拉姆齐数 R(3,3)=6——任意六个人的聚会中，必有三人彼此相识或彼此陌生。它揭示「任何足够大的系统都含强制性的规律」，应用于组合学、计算机科学与逻辑。
Ramsey theory studies how, in sufficiently large structures, any coloring or partition inevitably forces an orderly local pattern. The classic result is Ramsey number R(3,3)=6—at any party of six people, three mutually know each other or three mutually don't. It reveals that 'any large enough system contains forced regularity,' with uses in combinatorics, computer science, and logic.

P 与 NP 问题的核心疑问是什么？为什么它价值百万美元？

What is the core question of P versus NP, and why is it worth a million dollars?

P 指能在多项式时间内求解的问题类，NP 指答案能被多项式时间验证的问题类。P = NP 疑问在于：是否每个容易验证的问题也都容易求解？若 P = NP，许多密码体系与优化难题将瞬间可解，对安全与科学影响巨大；若 P ≠ NP，则确认了某些问题本质难解。它是克雷研究所七大千禧年难题之一。
P is the class of problems solvable in polynomial time, NP those whose answers can be verified in polynomial time. P vs NP asks whether every easily verifiable problem is also easily solvable. If P = NP, many cryptographic and optimization problems would become tractable, dramatically impacting security and science; if P ≠ NP, some problems are inherently hard. It is one of the Clay Institute's seven Millennium Problems.

旅行商问题为什么被认为是「难」的？

Why is the traveling salesman problem considered 'hard'?

旅行商问题要求找到访问一批城市各一次并回到起点的最短路线。随着城市数增加，可能的路线数以阶乘爆炸式增长，没有已知的高效（多项式时间）精确算法。它属于 NP 难问题，却被广泛用于物流、芯片布线和 DNA 测序；实践中常用近似与启发式算法求解。
The traveling salesman problem asks for the shortest route visiting each of a set of cities once and returning to the start. As cities increase, possible routes grow factorially, and no efficient (polynomial-time) exact algorithm is known. It is NP-hard yet widely used in logistics, circuit routing, and DNA sequencing, where approximation and heuristic methods are standard.

杨辉三角（帕斯卡三角）里藏着哪些有趣规律？

What interesting patterns hide in Pascal's (Yanghui's) triangle?

帕斯卡三角（中国称杨辉三角，南宋杨辉 1261 年记载）的每一行是二项式展开系数，且左右对称。它同时隐藏着斐波那契数列（斜向求和）、各行的和是 2 的幂、以及「曲棍球杆」恒等式等无数规律。它连接了组合数学、概率与代数，是数学中模式最丰富的结构之一。
Pascal's triangle (Yanghui's triangle in China, recorded by Yang Hui in 1261) gives binomial expansion coefficients, symmetric across each row. It also hides the Fibonacci numbers (diagonal sums), row sums that are powers of 2, and countless other patterns like the hockey-stick identity. It links combinatorics, probability, and algebra as one of math's richest pattern sources.
