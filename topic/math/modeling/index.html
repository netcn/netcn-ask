主成分分析（PCA）在做什么样的数学？

What mathematics does Principal Component Analysis (PCA) do?

它在高维数据中找方差最大的方向（特征向量），把数据投影到低维而尽量保留信息，本质是对协方差矩阵做特征值分解。是降维的核心工具。
It finds directions of maximal variance (eigenvectors) in high-dimensional data and projects onto them to keep information while reducing dimension—essentially eigendecomposition of the covariance matrix. A core dimensionality-reduction tool.

#优化 #线性规划

在没有出生与死亡的封闭人群中，经典 SIR 模型为什么预言疫情会在仍有许多易感者未感染时就自行终止？终态规模方程揭示了什么规律？

In a closed population with no births or deaths, why does the classical SIR model predict that an epidemic dies out while many susceptibles remain uninfected, and what does the final size equation reveal?

终态规模方程 ln(s∞/s₀)=−R₀(s₀−s∞)（s₀≈1 时即 ln s∞=−R₀(1−s∞)）把结束时剩余易感比例与 R₀ 联系起来；R₀>1 时除平凡根外仅有一支 s∞<1 的正根。传播停止并非因易感者耗尽，而是易感比例降至约 1/R₀、有效再生数跌破 1 后感染链逐渐衰减，故总会留下从未被感染的人群；由 R₀ 与初值即可唯一确定总感染规模。
The final size relation, ln(s∞/s0) = −R0(s0 − s∞), which reduces to ln s∞ = −R0(1 − s∞) when s0 ≈ 1, ties the fraction of people still susceptible when transmission fades to the basic reproduction number; for R0 > 1 it has, besides the trivial root, a unique solution below 1. An epidemic stops not because susceptibles run out, but because once susceptibility falls near 1/R0 the effective reproduction number drops below one and infection chains decay. Many individuals therefore remain never infected, and the attack rate is pinned down by R0 and the initial conditions alone.

Does 'six degrees of separation' have a mathematical basis?

In large random or scale-free networks, path length grows only logarithmically with nodes, so billions of people are only a few steps apart on average. Small-world network models explain it.

为什么‘相关’不等于‘因果’是建模的铁律？

Why is 'correlation is not causation' a hard rule in modeling?

两件事同时变化可能只因共同受第三因素驱动（如冰淇淋销量与溺水都随气温升）。把相关当因果会得出错误干预，建模必须谨慎区分。
Two things moving together may share a third driver (ice-cream sales and drownings both rise with temperature). Treating correlation as cause yields wrong interventions; modeling must distinguish them.

某地区人口按Logistic模型演化：dP/dt = 0.04·P·(1 − P/500)，其中P以万为单位，t以年为单位。若当前人口为100万，约需多少年达到250万？并解释建模中r与K的现实含义。

A region's population follows the logistic equation dP/dt = 0.04·P·(1 − P/500), with P measured in ten-thousands and t in years. If the current population is one million, how long until it reaches 2.5 million, and what real quantities do the parameters r and K represent?

方程的解为P(t)=K/(1+Ae^(−rt))，其中A=(K−P₀)/P₀=(500−100)/100=4。代入P=250得1+4e^(−0.04t)=2，解出t=ln4/0.04≈34.7，即约35年后达到250万。这里r=0.04是资源不受限制时的内禀人均增长率，K=500万是由食物、住房与环境承载能力决定的容纳量，且人口恰在K/2处增长最快。
The solution is P(t) = K/(1 + Ae^(−rt)) with A = (K − P₀)/P₀ = (500 − 100)/100 = 4. Substituting P = 250 gives 1 + 4e^(−0.04t) = 2, so t = ln4/0.04 ≈ 34.7, roughly 35 years. Here r = 0.04 is the intrinsic per-capita growth rate under unlimited resources, while K = 5 million is the carrying capacity set by food, housing and environmental limits; growth is fastest exactly at K/2.

#数学建模 #Logistic人口模型

如何用一组微分方程刻画传染病在人群中的传播,也就是 SIR 模型?基本再生数 R0 怎样决定疫情的走向?

How can a system of differential equations capture the spread of an infectious disease through the SIR model, and how does the basic reproduction number R0 govern the course of an epidemic?

SIR 模型把人群分成易感者 S、感染者 I 与移出者 R 三类,满足 dS/dt=-bSI/N、dI/dt=bSI/N-gI、dR/dt=gI,其中 b 为人均传染率,g 为恢复率。基本再生数 R0=b/g 衡量一名感染者在全易感人群中平均引发的继发感染人数:R0 大于 1 时感染规模初期呈指数增长,R0 小于 1 时疫情自行消退。同一阈值还给出达到群体免疫所需的最低免疫比例 1-1/R0。
The SIR model divides the population into susceptible S, infective I, and removed R classes obeying dS/dt = -bSI/N, dI/dt = bSI/N - gI, dR/dt = gI, where b is the per-capita transmission rate and g the recovery rate. The basic reproduction number R0 = b/g measures how many secondary infections one infective causes on average in a wholly susceptible population: when R0 exceeds 1 the infected count grows exponentially at first, while R0 below 1 lets the outbreak die out on its own. The same threshold yields the minimum immune fraction 1 - 1/R0 required for herd immunity.

#数学建模 #SIR传染病模型

在 SIR 流行病模型中，基本再生数 R0 如何决定疫情的走向？“群体免疫阈值 1-1/R0”又是怎么来的？

In the SIR epidemic model, how does the basic reproduction number R0 determine the course of an outbreak, and where does the herd immunity threshold 1 - 1/R0 come from?

SIR 模型把人群分为易感、感染、移除三类，以微分方程刻画转化。基本再生数 R0 指一名感染者在全易感人群中平均引起的二代病例数：R0≤1 时病例传不满一人，疫情自行消退；R0>1 时病例早期指数增长。令新增感染与移除相抵可知：易感比例跌破 1/R0，即约 1-1/R0 的人口免疫后疫情收缩，这就是群体免疫阈值的由来。
The SIR model sorts a population into susceptible, infectious, and removed classes linked by differential equations. The basic reproduction number R0 is the average number of secondary infections one case produces in a wholly susceptible population: if R0 ≤ 1, each case fails to replace itself and the outbreak dies out, while R0 > 1 gives early exponential growth. Equating new infections with recoveries shows the epidemic shrinks once the susceptible fraction falls below 1/R0 — that is, once a share 1 - 1/R0 of the population is immune, which is precisely the herd immunity threshold.

为什么疫情前期确诊人数看起来像直线、之后却突然飙升？

Why does an epidemic look like a slow straight line at first, then suddenly explode?

这是指数增长的特征：早期基数小，翻倍所需时间固定，所以绝对增量起初不明显；一旦基数变大，每单位时间新增的人会越来越多，曲线变得陡峭。
This is the signature of exponential growth: the doubling time is constant, so absolute daily additions stay small at first; once the base is large, each doubling adds far more cases and the curve steepens sharply.

Why does compound interest grow so much faster than simple interest?

单利只在本金上算利息，呈直线增长；复利在‘本金+已生利息’上再计息，呈指数增长。时间一长，二者的差距会被放大到惊人的程度。
Simple interest earns only on principal, growing linearly; compound interest earns on principal plus accumulated interest, growing exponentially. Over time the gap becomes enormous.

#博弈 #合作

为什么‘半衰期’描述的是指数衰减而不是线性衰减？

Why does 'half-life' describe exponential rather than linear decay?

因为每过固定时间剩余量减半，比例是恒定的，这正是指数衰减：N(t)=N0×(1/2)^(t/T)。线性衰减不会保持固定的‘减半周期’。
Because a fixed time always halves the remainder, a constant ratio, which is exponential decay: N(t)=N0×(1/2)^(t/T). Linear decay would not keep a fixed halving period.
