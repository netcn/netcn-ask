双曲几何可以用什么模型直观看到？

What model lets you visualize hyperbolic geometry?

常用庞加莱圆盘模型：圆盘内三角形内角和小于180度，越靠近边界长度与面积被无限拉伸，平行线可以‘分叉’无限多。
A common one is the Poincaré disk model: triangles inside have angle sum below 180°, lengths and areas stretch toward the boundary, and infinitely many parallels can diverge.

#非欧几何 #双曲

飞机为什么常走‘大圆航线’而不是直线？

Why do planes often follow a 'great circle' route rather than a straight line?

在球面上两点间最短路径是过球心的大圆的一段，而不是平面地图上的直线。地图投影会把大圆画成弯曲的弧。
On a sphere the shortest path between two points is an arc of the great circle through the center, not the straight line on a flat map. Map projections bend great circles into curves.

#非欧几何 #球面

地球的经线为什么在两极‘相交’却在赤道最远？

Why do Earth's meridians meet at the poles yet are farthest at the equator?

在球面上，‘直’（最短）的线是经线大圆，它们本不平行，必在两极汇合。这是球面几何与平面的根本区别：这里没有真正的平行线。
On a sphere the 'straight' (shortest) lines are meridian great circles, which are not parallel and must meet at the poles. A key difference from the plane: here true parallels do not exist.

#非欧几何 #球面

在非欧几何里，三角形内角和还是180度吗？

In non-Euclidean geometry, is the sum of a triangle's angles still 180 degrees?

不一定。在球面（正曲率）上内角和大于180度；在双曲面（负曲率）上小于180度。 Euclid 的平行公设在这里被替换了。
Not necessarily. On a sphere (positive curvature) it exceeds 180°; on a hyperbolic surface (negative curvature) it is less. Euclid's parallel postulate is replaced here.
