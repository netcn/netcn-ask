Is the golden ratio really everywhere in art?

The golden ratio about 1.618 does appear in some classic compositions, but many claimed golden rectangles are retrofitted. It is pleasing, not a universal law of the universe.

人类花了多久才普遍接受零这个概念？

How long did it take for zero to be widely accepted?

零最早在古印度作为数字出现，经阿拉伯传入欧洲后，还被怀疑了几百年。没有零，位值制和代数都难以发展。
Zero first appeared as a numeral in ancient India and, after reaching Europe via Arabs, was doubted for centuries. Without zero, place value and algebra could hardly develop.

音乐里为什么八度听起来像同一个音，和数学有什么关系？

Why does an octave sound like the same note, and what is the math?

高八度的频率正好是低音的两倍，十二平均律把一倍频程均分成12个等比半音。音程的比例关系，正是数学与听觉的共鸣。
The octave's frequency is exactly double, and equal temperament splits that octave into 12 geometrically equal semitones. Interval ratios are where math meets hearing.

#数学文化 #音乐

古埃及人没有乘法表，怎么算乘法？

How did ancient Egyptians multiply without a times table?

他们用倍加法：不断把一个数翻倍，再按另一数的二进制分解挑出对应倍数相加。本质上就是今天的二进制乘法。
They used doubling: repeatedly double one number, then add the doublings selected by the binary expansion of the other. It is binary multiplication in disguise.

向日葵的种子为什么排成螺旋，还常是34和55？

Why do sunflower seeds form spirals, often 34 and 55?

种子沿两条方向相反的螺旋排布，螺旋数多为相邻斐波那契数。这样能让种子在有限空间里最紧密、最均匀地铺开。
Seeds lie on two opposite spirals whose counts are often adjacent Fibonacci numbers. This packs them as tightly and evenly as possible in limited space.

菠萝和松果上的鳞片排列，为什么也是斐波那契数？

Why do pineapple and pinecone scales also follow Fibonacci numbers?

它们表面斜向的鳞甲排成两组螺旋，螺旋数常是相邻斐波那契数，如8和13。这是植物在生长中追求最紧密排布的自然结果。
Their scales form two families of spirals whose counts are often adjacent Fibonacci numbers like 8 and 13. It is nature packing growth as tightly as possible.

伊斯兰几何图案为什么那么对称好看？

Why are Islamic geometric patterns so symmetrically beautiful?

它们用圆规直尺作正多边形与星形，通过旋转、反射反复拼接成无限延伸的图案。数学上对应着平面对称群，既有秩序又有变化。

#数学文化 #几何图案

为什么电话键盘和数字键盘都是“3 列 4 行”、且 0 单独放在最下面？

Why are phone and calculator keypads laid out as '3 columns × 4 rows' with 0 alone at the bottom?

电话的 3×4 布局源自 20 世纪电话公司的实验：“123 在顶”便于快速拨号且误触少；而计算器的数字排列常与之相反（789 在顶、123 靠近底部），二者因历史路径不同而形成差异。0 单独成行放在最下方，是因为它代表“无/复位”，功能上区别于 1–9 的数字键。
The 3×4 phone layout came from 20th-century telephone-company tests favoring '123 on top' for speed and fewer errors; calculators later adopted a 3×4 grid but usually inverted it (789 on top, 123 near the bottom), diverging by historical path. Zero sits alone on its own bottom row because it means 'none/reset,' functionally distinct from the digits 1–9.

罗马数字为什么做算术特别麻烦？

Why is arithmetic hard with Roman numerals?

罗马数字没有位值制和零，加减靠符号拼接、乘法几乎要硬算。直到印度-阿拉伯数字带进位和零传入欧洲，日常计算才变容易。
Roman numerals lack place value and zero, so addition is symbol-shuffling and multiplication is near brute force. Arithmetic only eased after positional Hindu-Arabic digits with zero arrived in Europe.

#数学文化 #罗马数字

为什么要有“闰秒”，原子钟的秒和地球自转的日为什么会对不上？

Why do we need 'leap seconds,' and why does the atomic-clock second drift from the Earth-rotation day?

现代“秒”由铯原子跃迁定义（原子时，极稳），而“一天”来自地球自转（天文时，受潮汐摩擦缓慢变长）。两者累积差，国际地球自转服务（IERS）便在 6 月或 12 月末尾插入一闰秒，让协调世界时（UTC）不偏离太阳时太多。它是人类用纯数学计时对抗不规则自然的产物。
The modern second is defined by a cesium transition (atomic time, ultra-stable), while a 'day' comes from Earth's rotation (astronomical time, slowly lengthening via tidal friction). The gap accumulates, so the IERS inserts a leap second at June/December ends to keep UTC near solar time—humanity using pure math to fight irregular nature.

古巴比伦人是怎么表示分数的？

How did the Babylonians represent fractions?

他们用六十进制，把分数写成60的负次幂之和，比如1/2就是30/60。这和我们今天的时间、角度用60进制一脉相承。
They used base 60, writing fractions as sums of negative powers of 60, e.g. 1/2 as 30/60. This is the same sexagesimal system behind our hours and degrees.

#数学文化 #六十进制

为什么一周有7天、时间用60进制，都来自巴比伦？

Why do a 7-day week and base-60 time both trace to Babylon?

巴比伦人崇拜七大天体，定下一星期七天；他们的六十进制方便分割（60能被2、3、4、5、6整除），于是沿用到了分秒和角度。
Babylonians honored seven celestial bodies, giving the 7-day week; their base 60 divides neatly by 2,3,4,5,6, so it survived in minutes, seconds and degrees.

中国古代的算筹是怎样计数的？

How did ancient Chinese counting rods represent numbers?

算筹用纵横两式的小棍摆出数字，靠位置表示个十百千，已是十进位值制。它比算盘更早，是当时先进的计算工具。
Counting rods laid small sticks in two alternating patterns, using position for units, tens, hundreds, a decimal place-value system. Predating the abacus, they were an advanced tool of their era.

玛雅人为什么用二十进制？

Why did the Maya use a base-20 number system?

二十进制可能源于用手指加脚趾共二十个计数单位。玛雅人还用点横表示数字，并早早使用了零的符号。
Base 20 likely came from counting all fingers and toes, twenty in total. The Maya wrote numbers with dots and bars and used a symbol for zero remarkably early.

牛顿与莱布尼茨谁先发明微积分？

Who invented calculus first, Newton or Leibniz?

两人各自在17世纪末独立创立微积分：牛顿从物理运动的“流数”出发，莱布尼茨发展了更系统的记号（如 ∫ 与 d）。优先权之争曾长期困扰学界，如今公认两人共享功劳。
Newton and Leibniz each invented calculus independently in the late 1600s—Newton from fluxions of motion, Leibniz with systematic notation (∫, d). Credit is now shared.

#数学史 #微积分

为什么年份要四年一闰，但整百年却要四百年才闰一次？

Why leap every four years, yet a century year leaps only every 400?

地球公转约365.2422天，四年加一天仍略多，于是整百年不闰、四百年再闰一次来修正。这是用有理数逼近无理年的巧妙历法设计。
A year is about 365.2422 days; adding a day every four overshoots, so century years skip and every 400th keeps it, correcting the error. A clever rational approximation of an irrational year.

#数学文化 #闰年

为什么大多数骰子相对的两个面，点数加起来都等于 7？

Why do the opposite faces of most dice add up to 7?

标准骰子把 1 对 6、2 对 5、3 对 4 摆成相对面，每组都正好是 7。这样无论哪一面朝上，六面平均仍是 3.5，转动起来更“公平”也更对称好看；这是几个世纪流传下来的约定，不是数学定理，却是普遍的制作习惯。
A standard die places 1 opposite 6, 2 opposite 5, 3 opposite 4—each pair sums to 7. Then whichever face is up, the average of all faces stays 3.5, and the layout looks balanced; it is a centuries-old convention, not a theorem, but a near-universal manufacturing habit.

汉字数字和算盘有什么关系？

How are Chinese number characters related to the abacus?

汉字一二三用横画计数，和算盘上拨珠数数一样直观。算盘的每一档对应一个数位，正好配合中文的十进位说法。
Chinese characters for one, two, three use stroke counts, as intuitive as beads on an abacus. Each abacus column is a place value, matching the Chinese decimal way of speaking.

圆周率日为什么是3月14日？

Why is Pi Day on March 14?

因为圆周率π约等于3.14，美国写法3/14正好对应3月14日。这一天常用来庆祝数学，也有人吃派（pie）玩谐音。
Pi is about 3.14, and in the US date format 3/14 means March 14. It is celebrated as a math holiday, often with pie for the pun.
