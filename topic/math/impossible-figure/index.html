埃舍尔的画里，水为什么能永远往高处流？

In Escher's drawings, why can water flow uphill forever?

他用不可能图形把局部合理的阶梯首尾相接成闭环，每处看着向下、整体却回到起点。这是拓扑与视错觉结合的趣味艺术。
He links locally sensible staircases into a closed loop where each part looks downhill yet the whole returns to start. It is playful art mixing topology with visual illusion.

不可能三叉戟是什么图形，为什么看着怪？

What is the impossible trident and why does it look wrong?

它画出一个前端有三个圆柱、后端却只有两个圆柱的物体，靠局部合理的透视拼接出全局矛盾。大脑按二维线索强行解读，于是产生不可能感。
It depicts an object with three cylindrical prongs at one end but only two at the other, built from locally consistent perspectives that conflict globally. The brain forces a reading and senses the impossibility.

#不可能图形 #视觉

彭罗斯三角形为什么“不可能”？

Why is the Penrose triangle 'impossible'?

彭罗斯三角形把三个直角棱柱以看似合理的方式连接，局部每段都成立，但整体在三维中无法存在。它利用二维画面的视觉错觉制造“永不可能”的闭环。
The Penrose triangle joins three bars so each local part looks fine but the whole cannot exist in 3D—an impossible loop created by a 2D visual illusion.
