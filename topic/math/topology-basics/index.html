基本群如何用一个空间的环路结构区分不同拓扑形状？

How does the fundamental group distinguish different topological shapes via their loops?

基本群 $\pi_1(X)$ 由基于某点的闭道路在同伦意义下的等价类构成，群运算为路径拼接。圆 $S^1$ 的基本群是 $\mathbb Z$（绕圈数），而球面的基本群平凡，这正说明圆与球面在拓扑上不可同胚。
The fundamental group $\pi_1(X)$ consists of homotopy classes of loops based at a point, with group operation given by concatenation. The circle $S^1$ has $\pi_1\cong\mathbb Z$ (winding number), while the sphere has the trivial group, showing circle and sphere are not homeomorphic.

拓扑学中「同伦」概念如何刻画两条路径的连续可变性？

How does the notion of homotopy in topology capture the continuous deformability of two paths?

两条映射 $f,g:X\to Y$ 同伦，若存在连续映射 $H:X\times[0,1]\to Y$ 使 $H(x,0)=f(x)$、$H(x,1)=g(x)$，即一路径可在不「断裂」的情况下连续变形为另一路径。同伦是拓扑不变量研究的基本等价关系。
Two maps $f,g:X\to Y$ are homotopic if there is a continuous $H:X\times[0,1]\to Y$ with $H(x,0)=f(x)$ and $H(x,1)=g(x)$, so one deforms continuously into the other without breaking. Homotopy is a basic equivalence relation in studying topological invariants.

Heine-Borel 定理给出了欧氏空间中紧集的什么等价刻画？

What equivalent characterization of compact sets in Euclidean space does the Heine-Borel theorem give?

在 $\mathbb R^n$ 中，一个子集是紧的当且仅当它是闭且有界的。紧性保证任意开覆盖都有有限子覆盖，由此推出连续函数在紧集上必取到最大值与最小值（极值定理）。
In $\mathbb R^n$, a subset is compact iff it is closed and bounded. Compactness guarantees that every open cover has a finite subcover, from which follows that continuous functions attain their max and min on compact sets (extreme value theorem).

拓扑学中「连通空间」的直观含义是什么？

What is the intuitive meaning of a connected space in topology?

一个拓扑空间是连通的，当且仅当它不能表示为两个非空、互不相交的开集的并。直观上，连通空间是「一整块」，无法被分割成两个分离的开部分。实数轴 $\mathbb R$ 是连通的，而 $\{0\}\cup\{1\}$ 不连通。
A topological space is connected iff it cannot be written as the union of two nonempty disjoint open sets. Intuitively a connected space is all in one piece, with no separation into two open parts. The real line $\mathbb R$ is connected, while $\{0\}\cup\{1\}$ is not.

为什么在优化问题中紧性常被用来保证解的存在性？

Why is compactness often used to guarantee existence of solutions in optimization?

因为定义在紧集上的连续实值函数必有最大值和最小值（极值定理）。把可行域取为紧集，即可保证目标函数在该域上达到极值，从而优化问题至少存在一个最优解。
A continuous real-valued function on a compact set attains both a maximum and a minimum (extreme value theorem). Taking the feasible region to be compact thus guarantees the objective reaches an extremum, so an optimal solution exists.

拓扑学中「同胚」意味着两个空间具有怎样的等价关系？

What equivalence does homeomorphism mean between two topological spaces?

若存在连续的双射且其逆也连续，则两空间同胚，可视作「拓扑上相同」：它们共享所有拓扑性质（连通性、紧性、分离性等）。例如咖啡杯与甜甜圈表面同胚，都同胚于环面。
A homeomorphism is a continuous bijection with continuous inverse; two spaces related this way are topologically the same and share all topological properties (connectedness, compactness, separation). For example a coffee mug and a doughnut surface are homeomorphic, both to a torus.

什么是拓扑空间的连通分支？

What is a connected component of a topological space?

连通分支是空间中极大的连通子集：任意两点若能被一条完全落在空间内的连通子集连接，则属于同一连通分支。每个空间都可唯一分解为互不相交连通分支的并，例如有理数集 $\mathbb Q$ 的每个点都是孤立的连通分支。
A connected component is a maximal connected subset: two points belong to the same component if they are joined by a connected subset lying in the space. Every space decomposes uniquely into disjoint components; e.g. in $\mathbb Q$ each point is its own component.
