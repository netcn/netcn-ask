What is a fractal?

分形是具有自相似结构的几何图形，即局部放大后与整体形状相似，且通常具有非整数的维度。著名的曼德博集合和科赫雪花曲线都是分形例子。
A fractal is a geometric shape with self-similar structure, meaning a magnified part resembles the whole, and it often has a non-integer dimension. Famous examples include the Mandelbrot set and the Koch snowflake curve.

非欧几何是怎么被发现的？它颠覆了什么常识？

How was non-Euclidean geometry discovered, and what intuition did it overturn?

数学家们长期试图从欧几里得其余公理推导出「平行公设」，最终罗巴切夫斯基和波尔约在 19 世纪独立发现：若否定该公设也能得到自洽的几何（双曲几何）。黎曼随后建立椭圆几何，其中不存在平行线。这些几何后来成为爱因斯坦广义相对论的时空模型基础，证明「空间弯曲」在逻辑与物理上都是可能的。
Mathematicians long tried to derive Euclid's parallel postulate from his other axioms; Lobachevsky and Bolyai independently found in the 19th century that denying it yields a consistent geometry (hyperbolic). Riemann then built elliptic geometry, where no parallels exist. These geometries later became the basis of spacetime in Einstein's general relativity, showing that 'curved space' is both logically and physically possible.

巴拿赫-塔斯基悖论为什么能「把一个球变成两个同样大的球」？

Why can the Banach–Tarski paradox 'turn one ball into two identical balls'?

巴拿赫-塔斯基悖论（1924）指出：在三维空间中，可以把一个实心球分割成有限块，经刚体旋转和平移后重新拼成两个与原球完全相同大小的球。这个结论并不违反物理，因为它依赖选择公理构造出不可测的「病态」碎片，现实中无法真正切出。它揭示了选择公理与「体积」直觉之间的深刻冲突。
The Banach–Tarski paradox (1924) says a solid ball in 3D can be cut into finitely many pieces that, after rotations and translations, reassemble into two balls identical to the original. This doesn't violate physics because the pieces are non-measurable 'pathological' sets built using the Axiom of Choice and cannot be physically cut. It reveals a deep conflict between the Axiom of Choice and our intuition of volume.

#几何 #三角形

在球面上，三角形的内角和为什么可以大于 180°？大到多少？

On a sphere, why can a triangle's angle sum exceed 180°, and by how much?

球面上的“直线”是大圆，三个大圆围出的区域就是球面三角形。取球面上互相垂直的三条大圆（比如赤道和两条经线），围出的三角形三个角都是 90°，内角和 270°，超过 180° 的部分叫球面过剩。过剩量与三角形面积成正比（单位球上，面积恰等于过剩角的弧度数），这是球面几何里最深刻的定理之一，被用来从三角测量反推地球大小。
Great circles play the role of straight lines on a sphere, and three of them enclose a spherical triangle. Take three mutually perpendicular great circles (say the equator and two meridians): the triangle they enclose has three right angles, a sum of 270°. The excess over 180° is the spherical excess, and on the unit sphere it is exactly proportional to the triangle's area — in fact equal to it in radians. This deep result lets surveyors measure the size of the Earth from angle measurements.

分形几何用什么概念度量像海岸线这样不规则物体的维数？

射影几何里的帕斯卡定理（Pascal's theorem）说内接于圆锥曲线的六点有什么共线关系？

帕斯卡定理指出：若六边形（可退化为六点）的顶点都在同一条圆锥曲线上，则三组对边交点共线，这条线称为帕斯卡线。它是射影几何对“度量”完全不敏感的代表性结论，对偶于布里安雄定理（外切六边形的三线共点）。
Pascal's theorem states that if the six vertices of a hexagon lie on a single conic, then the three intersections of opposite sides are collinear—the Pascal line. It is a hallmark of projective geometry because it makes no use of distance or angle, and it is dual to Brianchon's theorem for circumscribed hexagons.

为什么正多面体（柏拉图立体）只有五种？

Why are there only five regular (Platonic) solids?

正多面体要求每个面是全等的相同正多边形，且每个顶点汇聚相同数量的面。由于多边形内角必须使若干个能拼成小于 360° 的立体角，几何上只允许三角形、正方形和五边形这三种面，并仅能组合出正四面体、立方体、正八面体、正十二面体和正二十面体五种。柏拉图曾把它们与地水火风及宇宙第五元素相对应。
A regular solid requires identical regular polygons as faces meeting equally at each vertex. Because the polygon angles must sum to less than 360° to form a solid corner, only triangles, squares, and pentagons work, yielding exactly five: tetrahedron, cube, octahedron, dodecahedron, and icosahedron. Plato associated them with earth, water, fire, air, and the cosmos's fifth element.
