突变理论是什么？

What is catastrophe theory?

突变理论研究系统参数连续变化时，结果却在某些临界点发生不连续“跳跃”的现象，如桥梁突然坍塌。它由托姆创立，用于建模相变与危机。
Catastrophe theory studies discontinuous 'jumps' in outcomes as parameters change smoothly, like a bridge suddenly collapsing. Thom created it to model phase transitions and crises.

纳维-斯托克斯方程为什么被列为千禧年难题？

Why is the Navier–Stokes equation a Millennium Prize Problem?

纳维-斯托克斯方程描述黏性流体（水、空气）的运动，是气象、航空与海洋科学的基石。千禧年难题要求证明：在三维空间中，对任意光滑初值，解是否始终存在且保持光滑（不存在奇点/湍流崩塌）。这个问题关系着湍流这一未解自然之谜，至今无人给出完整证明或反例。
The Navier–Stokes equations describe the motion of viscous fluids (water, air) and underpin meteorology, aerodynamics, and ocean science. The Millennium problem asks to prove that, in 3D, smooth solutions exist for all time for any smooth initial data (no singularities/turbulence blow-up). Tied to the unsolved mystery of turbulence, it remains unproved with no counterexample.

动力系统与李雅普诺夫指数是什么？

What are dynamical systems and Lyapunov exponents?

动力系统研究状态随时间演化的规律；李雅普诺夫指数衡量相邻轨道分离或收敛的快慢，正指数即意味着混沌。它们是理解天气、生态与经济的数学框架。
Dynamical systems study how states evolve over time; a Lyapunov exponent measures how nearby trajectories separate (positive ⇒ chaos). They frame weather, ecology and economics.

纳什均衡描述的是博弈中的什么状态？

What state in a game does the Nash equilibrium describe?

纳什均衡由约翰·纳什提出：在一个多人博弈中，若每个参与者在他人策略既定下都无法通过单方面改变策略而获益，则这组策略构成纳什均衡。它是一种「僵局」式的稳定状态，未必最优却难以偏离。博弈论由此广泛应用于经济学、进化生物学和国际冲突分析，纳什因此获 1994 年诺贝尔经济学奖。
Proposed by John Nash, a Nash equilibrium is a set of strategies where no player can benefit by unilaterally changing their own, given others' choices. It is a stable 'stalemate' that need not be optimal but is hard to leave. Game theory thus informs economics, evolutionary biology, and conflict analysis, earning Nash the 1994 Nobel Prize in Economics.

混沌理论与“蝴蝶效应”是什么？

What are chaos theory and the butterfly effect?

混沌系统对初值极度敏感：微小差别会指数级放大，导致长期不可预测，即“蝴蝶效应”。洛伦兹吸引子是经典例子。
Chaotic systems are exquisitely sensitive to initial conditions, so tiny differences amplify and defy long-term prediction—the butterfly effect. The Lorenz attractor is the classic example.

偏微分方程（PDE）是什么？

What are partial differential equations (PDEs)?

PDE 含多个自变量的未知函数及其偏导数，描述热、波、扩散与电磁等现象。绝大多数物理定律都以 PDE 形式表达。
PDEs involve functions of several variables and their partial derivatives, modeling heat, waves, diffusion and electromagnetism. Most physical laws take PDE form.

有限元方法是什么？

What is the finite element method?

有限元方法把复杂区域剖分为小单元，在每个单元上用简单函数近似解，再拼成全局解。它是工程仿真（如汽车碰撞、桥梁受力）的主力算法。
The finite element method meshes a region into small elements, approximates the solution locally, then assembles it globally. It is the workhorse of engineering simulation.

小波变换是什么？

What is the wavelet transform?

小波用可缩放的“小波”基同时捕捉信号的时间与频率局部特征，优于傅里叶的全局表示。它用于 JPEG2000 图像压缩与去噪。
Wavelets use scalable basis functions to capture both time and frequency locality, beating Fourier's global view. Used in JPEG2000 compression and denoising.
