什么是不可能有图形，它们为什么骗过大脑？

What are impossible figures, and why do they fool the brain?

不可能图形在局部都合理、整体却自相矛盾，如彭罗斯楼梯、不可能叉子。大脑按三维物体的先验解读局部线索，于是误以为整体成立。
Impossible figures are locally plausible but globally contradictory, like the Penrose stairs or impossible trident. The brain reads local cues as 3D objects and infers a coherent whole that cannot exist.

彭罗斯三角形为什么在二维画里成立、三维中不可能？

Why is the Penrose triangle valid in a 2D drawing but impossible in 3D?

它利用透视把三根直角的杆在图上首尾相接成三角，但这是视觉错位的把戏。在真实三维空间里，这种同时正交的连接无法存在。
It uses perspective to join three right-angled bars into a triangle on paper, but that is a visual trick. In real 3D space such simultaneously orthogonal connections cannot exist.

穆勒-莱尔错觉中两条等长线段为何看起来不等？

In the Müller-Lyer illusion, why do two equal segments look unequal?

向外的箭头让人联想到远的角，向内的箭头像近的角，大脑按深度经验补偿长度。其实两线等长，只是箭头朝向触发了距离误判。
Outward arrows suggest a receding corner, inward arrows a near corner, so the brain compensates for depth. The lines are equal; arrow direction triggers a distance misjudgment.

盯住红色方块再看白纸，为什么会出现绿色残影？

After staring at a red square then a white page, why do you see a green afterimage?

视网膜上对应的视锥细胞因持续刺激而疲劳，看白纸时疲劳通道反应减弱、互补通道相对增强，于是出现互补色（红对绿）后像。
The stimulated cones fatigue; on a white surface the tired channel responds less while its complementary channel dominates, producing a complementary (red→green) afterimage.

为什么静止的重复图案在移动时会产生似动？

Why do still repeating patterns appear to move (apparent motion)?

当高对比度格栅平移一点点，大脑用相邻相位解释成连续运动，产生漂动错觉（如周边漂移）。这与视觉系统对运动的边缘检测有关。
When a high-contrast grid shifts slightly, the brain interprets adjacent phases as continuous motion, producing a shimmer (e.g., peripheral drift). It relates to motion edge-detection in vision.

棋盘中A、B两块看似一深一浅，为什么其实亮度相同？

On a chessboard illusion, squares A and B look different in shade — why are they actually the same brightness?

这是同色错觉：B被圆柱体阴影盖住，大脑根据处在阴影中自动提亮补偿，于是觉得它比旁边的A浅。用取色器量二者灰度完全一致。
This is the checker-shadow illusion: B lies in the cylinder's shadow, so the brain brightens it to compensate, making it look lighter than A. A color picker shows both have identical gray values.

卡尼莎三角为什么让我们看见不存在的边？

Why does the Kanizsa triangle make us see edges that aren't there?

三个缺角的黑色Pac-Man形会触发大脑补全轮廓，于是一个亮白三角形凭空出现。这是格式塔封闭性原则的典型例子。
Three pac-man-like notched disks prompt the brain to complete contours, making a bright white triangle appear from nothing. It is a classic Gestalt principle of closure.

咖啡馆墙错觉里平行的横线为什么看起来弯曲？

In the café wall illusion, why do parallel horizontal lines look wavy?

交错排列的明暗方块在交界处产生对比加强，使视网膜对边界位置产生偏移感知。大脑把这种局部错位连成波浪，其实线都是直的。
Alternating light/dark bricks enhance contrast at boundaries, shifting perceived edge positions. The brain stitches these local offsets into waves, though the lines are straight.
