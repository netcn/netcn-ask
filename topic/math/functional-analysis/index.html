希尔伯特空间与一般的巴拿赫空间有什么关键区别？为什么量子力学和傅里叶分析偏爱希尔伯特空间？

What distinguishes a Hilbert space from a general Banach space, and why do quantum mechanics and Fourier analysis favor Hilbert spaces?

两类空间都是完备赋范空间；区别在于希尔伯特空间的范数由内积诱导。有了内积便有正交概念：可以做正交投影分解、展开傅里叶级数，并拥有平行四边形法则带来的几何直观。许多定理也更强，如里斯表示定理保证每个连续线性泛函都能用一个向量表示。量子力学中的态叠加恰好需要这种正交结构，因此数学物理偏爱希尔伯特空间。
Both are complete normed spaces; the difference is that a Hilbert space's norm arises from an inner product. With an inner product comes orthogonality: one can decompose via orthogonal projections, expand in Fourier series, and exploit the geometry encoded in the parallelogram law. Theorems grow stronger too — the Riesz representation theorem guarantees that every continuous linear functional is represented by a vector. Quantum superposition requires precisely such orthogonal structure, so mathematical physics favors Hilbert spaces.

泛函分析中，为什么线性算子的“连续”与“有界”是等价的？这里的“有界”究竟指什么？

In functional analysis, why are "continuous" and "bounded" equivalent for a linear operator, and what exactly does "bounded" mean here?

此处“有界”并非值域有界，而是把单位球映成有界集：存在 C 使 ||Tx||≤C||x|| 对一切 x 成立。线性使问题大为简化：由 T(x-y)=Tx-Ty，连续性只需在原点验证；再由范数的齐次性，这等价于 sup ||Tx||/||x|| 有限。于是连续这一局部定性条件化为由算子范数度量的定量条件，微分与积分方程中的许多算子由此纳入统一框架。
Bounded here does not mean bounded range but that the unit ball is mapped into a bounded set: there is a constant C with ||Tx|| ≤ C||x|| for every x. Linearity simplifies matters decisively — since T(x-y) = Tx - Ty, continuity needs checking only at the origin — and homogeneity of the norm turns continuity at the origin into finiteness of sup ||Tx||/||x|| over nonzero x. A local qualitative condition thus becomes a quantitative one measured by the operator norm C, bringing the many operators of differential and integral equations into a single framework.

什么是巴拿赫压缩映射原理？为什么在完备度量空间里反复迭代一定能收敛到唯一的不动点？

What is Banach's contraction mapping principle, and why does repeated iteration necessarily converge to a unique fixed point in a complete metric space?

该原理说：完备度量空间上的映射 T 如果把任意两点的距离至少按固定比例 0<k<1 压缩，那么 T 恰有一个不动点，且从任何初值出发的迭代都几何式地逼近它。关键是相邻两次迭代的距离按公比 k 衰减，使序列成为柯西列；完备性保证极限存在，T 的连续性保证极限就是不动点。它自带误差估计，是皮卡逐步逼近法解微分方程的核心。
Banach's contraction principle says that a map T on a complete metric space which shrinks every distance by a fixed factor 0<k<1 has exactly one fixed point, approached geometrically by iteration from any starting value. Distances between consecutive iterates decay at ratio k, so they form a Cauchy sequence; completeness supplies the limit, and continuity of T guarantees it is fixed. The theorem brings intrinsic error bounds and drives Picard iteration.
