为什么乘法口诀只背到九九八十一?古人的计算工具长什么样?

Why stop at nine times nine — what calculating tools did ancients use?

因为一位数相乘是地基,两位数乘法可以拆成几步一位数来完成。古人发明了算筹和算盘,熟练者拨珠如飞,曾赢过早期的电动计算器;中国的算盘用了上千年。今天计算机一秒万亿次,但理解为什么这样算的原理,永远比按键更重要。
Single-digit products are the foundation — multi-digit multiplication splits into steps of them. Ancients invented counting rods and the abacus; expert hands once outraced early electric calculators. Today's computers do trillions of operations per second, yet understanding why beats pressing keys.

我有五个手指,两只手一共有几个手指?十个手指有什么用?

One hand has five fingers — how many on two hands? What are ten fingers for?

五加五等于十!很久很久以前,人类就是掰着手指数数的,十进制就是十个手指教我们的。现在你也能用手指学加法:左手出三根,右手出四根,合在一起数一数,一共七根!
Five plus five makes ten! Long ago people counted on fingers, which taught us base ten. Try adding with hands now: three fingers here, four there, count them all together — seven.

0 是最小的数吗?负数比0还要小是怎么回事?

Is zero the smallest number? How can negatives be even smaller?

把数字想成温度计:0度往上正数爬升,往下负数钻探,可以一直钻到零下十度、零下一百度……数轴两头都是无限的,0只是中间的起点。负数不是不存在的数,而是比0更少的意思——欠一块钱就是负一块钱。
Think thermometer: zero is the middle start, positives climb, negatives drill below to minus ten or minus one hundred — endless both ways. Negatives are real numbers meaning less than nothing-owned: owing one yuan is minus one.
