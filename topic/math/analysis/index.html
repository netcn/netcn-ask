巴塞尔问题问的是什么？欧拉给出的惊人答案是什么？

What does the Basel problem ask, and what surprising answer did Euler give?

巴塞尔问题要求计算所有正整数平方倒数之和：1 + 1/4 + 1/9 + 1/16 + …。欧拉在 1734 年惊人地证明这个和等于 π²/6，首次把 π 与整数平方的倒数联系起来。他用了对正弦函数无穷乘积的巧妙分析，这一结果开启了 ζ 函数与素数深层联系的研究。
The Basel problem asks for the sum of the reciprocals of the squares of the positive integers: 1 + 1/4 + 1/9 + 1/16 + …. Euler astoundingly proved in 1734 that the sum equals π²/6, the first link between π and the reciprocals of squares. Using an infinite product for the sine function, his result opened the study of deep connections between the zeta function and primes.

康托尔为什么说「无穷大也有大小之分」？

Why did Cantor say that infinities come in different sizes?

康托尔用「一一对应」比较集合大小，发现自然数集与整数、有理数集可以一一对应（同属「可数无穷」），但实数集无法与自然数一一对应，因而是更大的「不可数无穷」。他进一步证明无穷的层次可以无限递增（如实数集的幂集更大），这一发现曾遭许多同时代数学家反对，却奠定了现代集合论。
Cantor compared set sizes by one-to-one correspondence and found the natural numbers, integers, and rationals can all be paired with the naturals (all 'countably infinite'), but the reals cannot, making them a larger 'uncountable infinity.' He showed infinities form an endless hierarchy (the power set of the reals is larger still). Opposed by many contemporaries, this founded modern set theory.

What is analytic continuation?

Analytic continuation extends a function's domain while preserving its analytic nature, uniquely. The Riemann zeta function is continued this way from Re(s)>1 to the whole plane.

贝尔纲定理是什么？

What is the Baire category theorem?

它说完备度量空间不能表示为可数个“无处稠密”子集的并，即“大多数”点不在那些稀疏集合中。它常用于证明存在性（如处处不可导的连续函数）。
A complete metric space is not the countable union of nowhere-dense sets, so 'most' points escape sparse sets. It proves existence results like continuous nowhere-differentiable functions.

一致收敛是什么？

What is uniform convergence?

函数项级数若在整体区间上一致收敛，则极限函数保留连续、可积等好性质。魏尔斯特拉斯M判别法提供了一致收敛的简便检验。
If a series of functions converges uniformly on an interval, the limit keeps properties like continuity and integrability. The Weierstrass M-test gives an easy check.

为什么一段任意复杂的周期信号总能拆成一族不同频率正弦与余弦的叠加？这种分解何时逐点收敛？十九世纪悬置的收敛性难题最终是如何解决的？

Why can an arbitrarily complicated periodic signal always be decomposed into a superposition of sines and cosines at different frequencies, when does this decomposition converge pointwise, and how was the convergence problem left hanging in the nineteenth century finally settled?

正交函数系{1,cos nx,sin nx}如同坐标系一样张成周期函数空间，傅里叶系数由积分内积唯一确定，因此分解是无穷维空间中的坐标表示而非近似技巧。狄利克雷条件保证分段光滑函数的级数逐点收敛，并在间断点收敛到左右极限的平均值。1966年Carleson证明了L²函数的傅里叶级数几乎处处收敛，Hunt随即推广到所有L^p（p>1）空间，这一问题至此彻底解决。
The orthogonal family {1, cos nx, sin nx} spans the space of periodic functions like a coordinate basis, and Fourier coefficients are pinned down uniquely by integral inner products, so the decomposition is a genuine coordinate representation in infinite dimensions rather than an approximation trick. Dirichlet's conditions give pointwise convergence for piecewise smooth functions, with the series at a jump converging to the average of the two one-sided limits. In 1966 Carleson proved that Fourier series of every L2 function converge almost everywhere, and Hunt soon extended the result to all L^p spaces with p>1, closing the question completely.

自然常数 e ≈ 2.718 为什么如此重要？

Why is the natural constant e ≈ 2.718 so important?

e 是使得函数 eˣ 的导数等于自身的唯一底数，也是极限 (1 + 1/n)ⁿ 当 n→∞ 的值，由雅各布·伯努利在研究复利时首次遇到。它出现在连续增长、放射性衰变、正态分布和欧拉恒等式 e^(iπ) + 1 = 0 中，被称为「自然」对数之底。欧拉在 18 世纪系统研究了它。
e is the unique base for which the derivative of eˣ equals itself, and is the limit of (1 + 1/n)ⁿ as n→∞—first encountered by Jacob Bernoulli studying compound interest. It appears in continuous growth, radioactive decay, the normal distribution, and Euler's identity e^(iπ) + 1 = 0, as the base of 'natural' logarithms. Euler systematically studied it in the 18th century.

调和级数 1 + 1/2 + 1/3 + … 为什么发散到无穷？

Why does the harmonic series 1 + 1/2 + 1/3 + … diverge to infinity?

调和级数每一项越来越小，但它们的和却无限增大——它发散。一个经典证明把项分组：1/3+1/4 > 1/2，1/5+…+1/8 > 1/2，依此类推，每组都超过 1/2，于是可得到任意多的「半份」使总和冲破任何界限。这与几何级数（公比小于1）的收敛形成鲜明对比，提醒我们「项趋于零」不足以保证级数收敛。
The harmonic series has terms shrinking toward zero, yet its sum grows without bound—it diverges. A classic proof groups terms: 1/3+1/4 > 1/2, 1/5+…+1/8 > 1/2, and so on, each group exceeding 1/2, so we can pile up arbitrarily many halves. This contrasts sharply with convergent geometric series and warns that 'terms → 0' does not guarantee convergence.

混沌理论中的「蝴蝶效应」是什么意思？

What does the 'butterfly effect' mean in chaos theory?

蝴蝶效应指在某些确定性动力系统中，初始条件的微小差异会随时间被指数级放大，导致长期预测彻底失效——洛伦兹形象地比喻「巴西一只蝴蝶扇动翅膀可能引发得州一场龙卷风」。这类系统并非随机，却对初始值极度敏感，典型例子包括天气和双摆。混沌理论由爱德华·洛伦兹在 1960 年代研究气象时奠定。
The butterfly effect means that in some deterministic dynamical systems, tiny differences in initial conditions are amplified exponentially over time, making long-term prediction impossible—Lorenz quipped a butterfly in Brazil might trigger a tornado in Texas. Such systems are not random but exquisitely sensitive to initial values; weather and the double pendulum are classic examples. Chaos theory was founded by Edward Lorenz studying meteorology in the 1960s.

傅里叶变换能把信号「拆开」成什么？它为什么无处不在？

What does the Fourier transform decompose a signal into, and why is it everywhere?

傅里叶变换把一个随时间变化的信号分解为不同频率正弦波的叠加，即把函数从「时间域」转换到「频率域」。约瑟夫·傅里叶在 19 世纪研究热传导时提出任何周期信号都可如此展开。今天它是音频压缩（MP3）、图像（JPEG）、医学成像（MRI）和量子力学的核心数学工具。
The Fourier transform decomposes a time-varying signal into a superposition of sine waves of different frequencies, switching a function from the time domain to the frequency domain. Joseph Fourier proposed in the 19th century that any periodic signal can be so expanded, while studying heat flow. Today it underpins audio compression (MP3), images (JPEG), medical imaging (MRI), and quantum mechanics.

For any vectors u, v, |⟨u,v⟩| ≤ ‖u‖·‖v‖: the inner product can't exceed the product of lengths. It underlies many inequalities in probability and geometry.

用傅里叶级数逼近方波时,为什么部分和在跳跃断点附近总会出现约百分之九的过冲?这种现象叫什么,不断叠加谐波项能消除它吗?

When a square wave is approximated by its Fourier series, why do partial sums always overshoot by roughly nine percent near the jump discontinuities? What is this behavior called, and does stacking up more harmonic terms remove it?

该现象称为吉布斯现象。任何有限项部分和都是连续函数,只能以一段越来越陡的过渡去贴合跳变,而过渡段的峰值恒约为跳变幅度的 8.95%。随着谐波项数增多,过冲被挤向间断点且宽度收窄,但峰值高度不再下降,因此无论叠加多少项都无法彻底消除。
This behavior is called the Gibbs phenomenon. Every finite partial sum is continuous, so it can hug the jump only through an ever steeper transition whose peak remains about 8.95% of the jump height. As more harmonics are added, the overshoot is squeezed toward the discontinuity and narrows, yet its peak never drops, so no amount of extra terms eliminates it completely.

微积分研究的核心问题是什么？牛顿和莱布尼茨各自做了什么？

What are the core problems of calculus, and what did Newton and Leibniz each do?

微积分围绕两个互逆的核心问题：求瞬时变化率（导数）与求曲线下面积/累积量（积分）。牛顿从物理学运动问题出发，莱布尼茨独立发明并确立了至今仍在使用的积分号 ∫ 与微分记号。两人各自的发现引发了优先权之争，但微积分后来成为现代科学与工程的通用语言。
Calculus centers on two inverse problems: finding instantaneous rates of change (derivatives) and finding areas/cumulative quantities under curves (integrals). Newton approached it from motion in physics, while Leibniz independently invented it and introduced the integral sign ∫ and differential notation still used today. Their separate discoveries triggered a priority dispute, but calculus became the universal language of modern science and engineering.

芝诺悖论「阿基里斯追龟」想说明什么？它错在哪里？

What does Zeno's 'Achilles and the tortoise' paradox intend to show, and where does it go wrong?

芝诺提出：跑得快的阿基里斯要追上前方落后的乌龟，必须先到达乌龟的起点，而此时乌龟又前进了一点，如此无限细分似乎永远追不上。这个悖论的根源在于混淆了「无限多个步骤」与「无限长的时间」——这些步骤所用时间构成一个收敛的无穷级数，其总和是有限的。它成为早期对无穷与极限的深刻思辨。
Zeno argued that swift Achilles, to catch a tortoise ahead, must first reach its starting point, by which time the tortoise has advanced, and so on ad infinitum, seemingly never catching up. The paradox confuses 'infinitely many steps' with 'infinite time'—the times form a convergent infinite series with a finite total. It is an early profound meditation on infinity and limits.
