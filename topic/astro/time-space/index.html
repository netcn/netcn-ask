什么是光锥和因果结构？

What are light cones and causal structure?

光锥用光在时空中的可能路径划分事件为可影响、可被影响和永远隔绝的区域，是理解因果和相对论极限的基础。
Light cones use light's possible paths in spacetime to divide events into affectable, affected and forever-separated regions—the basis for causality and relativistic limits.

什么是参考系拖拽（参考系拖曳）？

What is frame dragging?

旋转的大质量天体会像搅动蜂蜜一样拖拽周围的时空，使附近自由下落的陀螺自转轴缓慢进动，已被引力探测器B实验证实。
A rotating massive body drags nearby spacetime around it like stirring honey, slowly precessing a freely falling gyroscope—confirmed by the Gravity Probe B experiment.

什么是引力时间膨胀？

What is gravitational time dilation?

引力时间膨胀指处在更强引力场中的钟比远处走得更慢，已在地面高度差实验和GPS系统上被精确验证。
Gravitational time dilation means a clock in a stronger gravitational field ticks slower than one farther away, precisely verified in height-difference experiments and the GPS system.

黑洞附近时间会怎样？

What happens to time near a black hole?

在强引力下时间显著变慢，从远处看落入者会越走越慢、红光渐暗，这被称为引力时间膨胀。
Under strong gravity time slows markedly; to a distant viewer a falling object appears to slow and redden—this is gravitational time dilation.

双胞胎佯谬讲的是什么？

What does the twin paradox say?

双胞胎中一人乘近光速飞船远行返回，会比留在地球的兄弟更年轻，这是狭义相对论中时间膨胀的真实结果。
Of twins, one riding a near-light-speed ship returns younger than the one who stayed on Earth—a real consequence of time dilation in special relativity.

光跑得多快？

How fast does light travel?

真空中的光速约为每秒30万公里，是宇宙中所有信息和物质运动的极限速度。
Light in vacuum travels about 300,000 km per second—the ultimate speed limit for information and matter in the Universe.
