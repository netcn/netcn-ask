为什么「氧与甲烷共存」被当作潜在生物印记，其理论依据是什么？

Why is the coexistence of oxygen and methane considered a potential biosignature, and what is the theoretical basis?

在热力学上氧气是强氧化剂、甲烷是还原剂，二者会迅速反应而难以在平衡大气中同时高浓度共存。若观测到两者同时富集，在无强烈非生物来源（如光化学或地质释放）时，最自然的解释是存在持续的生物产氧与产甲烷过程。该判据依赖化学不平衡，因此必须排除恒星紫外驱动的产氧等非生物途径。
Thermodynamically, oxygen is a strong oxidant and methane a reductant, so they react rapidly and cannot coexist at high concentrations in chemical equilibrium. Observing both enriched simultaneously, absent strong abiotic sources such as photochemistry or geology, most naturally implies ongoing biological oxygen production and methanogenesis. Because the criterion rests on chemical disequilibrium, abiotic pathways such as stellar-UV-driven oxygen production must first be excluded.

詹姆斯·韦伯望远镜如何借助透射光谱探测系外行星大气中的二氧化碳？

How does the James Webb Telescope use transmission spectroscopy to detect carbon dioxide in exoplanet atmospheres?

韦伯的中红外仪器（MIRI 与 NIRSpec）覆盖 3–5 微米的分子吸收带，其中 4.3 微米的强 CO2 振动带受云层影响较小。通过分析凌星过程中恒星光在窄波段上的微弱减弱，韦伯在 WASP-39b 等热木星大气中明确识别出 CO2 的特征吸收，这是系外行星大气首次确证该分子。
Webb's mid-infrared instruments, MIRI and NIRSpec, cover the 3–5 micrometer molecular bands where the strong CO2 vibration band near 4.3 micrometers is comparatively less affected by clouds. By measuring the tiny wavelength-dependent dimming during transit, Webb unambiguously identified the CO2 absorption signature in the atmosphere of hot Jupiters such as WASP-39b, the first confirmed detection of the molecule in an exoplanet atmosphere.

系外行星大气中的云和霾会怎样干扰透射光谱的成分解读？

How do clouds and hazes in exoplanet atmospheres interfere with interpreting transmission spectra?

云与霾由悬浮微粒组成，会在宽波段上产生近乎平坦的消光，掩盖底层气体的精细吸收线，使分子特征变弱甚至消失。由于透射光谱对高层大气更敏感，建模时必须同时拟合微粒尺度分布与气体丰度，否则会系统性低估云下气体的真实含量。
Clouds and hazes made of suspended particles produce nearly flat extinction across broad bands, masking the fine absorption lines of underlying gases and weakening or erasing molecular features. Because transmission spectra are most sensitive to the upper atmosphere, models must jointly fit particle size distributions and gas abundances, or they will systematically underestimate the true abundances below the clouds.

什么是系外行星宜居性研究中的「生物印记」？

What are biosignatures in exoplanet habitability studies?

生物印记是指可由生命活动产生、且在无生命过程中难以维持的观测特征，典型例子是大气中氧与甲烷同时存在的化学不平衡。在透射光谱中检测到这类气体的异常富集，会被视为潜在生命信号，但必须由非生物学机制严格排除后才能确认。
A biosignature is an observable feature that living processes can produce and that is hard to maintain without life, a classic example being the chemical disequilibrium of oxygen and methane coexisting in an atmosphere. Detecting such anomalous gas enrichments in transmission spectra would be taken as a possible sign of life, but only after non-biological mechanisms are rigorously excluded.

天文学家所说的「类地系外行星」通常指什么？

What do astronomers usually mean by an Earth-like exoplanet?

类地系外行星一般指质量与半径接近地球、由岩石构成、且位于宿主恒星宜居带内的行星。这类行星被认为最可能具备液态水与适宜表面环境，是搜寻地外生命的首要目标，但「类地」描述的是物理规模与轨道位置，不等于确认存在生命或适宜大气。
An Earth-like exoplanet generally means a rocky planet with mass and radius close to Earth's and orbiting within its star's habitable zone. Such planets are considered the most likely to host liquid water and a suitable surface environment, making them the primary targets in the search for life, though Earth-like describes physical size and orbit, not a confirmed living or habitable atmosphere.

恒星周围的「宜居带」是怎样定义的？

How is the habitable zone around a star defined?

宜居带是恒星周围温度适中、使行星表面能维持液态水的轨道范围，又称「金发姑娘区」。它的内外边界取决于恒星光度与行星大气的温室效应，对暗弱的红矮星较近、对明亮的恒星较远。宜居带只是液态水存在的必要条件，并不保证行星真正宜居。
The habitable zone is the orbital region around a star where temperatures allow liquid water on a planet's surface, often called the Goldilocks zone. Its inner and outer edges depend on the star's luminosity and the planet's atmospheric greenhouse effect, lying closer in for faint red dwarfs and farther out for bright stars. The zone is only a necessary condition for liquid water and does not guarantee actual habitability.

TRAPPIST-1 行星系统为何受到系外行星研究的特别关注？

Why does the TRAPPIST-1 planetary system receive special attention in exoplanet research?

TRAPPIST-1 是一颗距地球约 40 光年的超冷红矮星，周围有七颗地球大小的岩石行星，其中多颗位于宜居带内。由于行星排布紧凑且频繁凌星，它成为韦伯望远镜用透射光谱逐个刻画大气成分的理想实验室，用于检验类地行星是否普遍拥有大气。
TRAPPIST-1 is an ultracool red dwarf about 40 light-years away with seven Earth-sized rocky planets, several of them inside the habitable zone. Because the planets are tightly packed and transit frequently, it is an ideal laboratory for Webb to characterize atmospheric composition one by one and to test whether Earth-like planets commonly retain atmospheres.

关于 K2-18b 大气中「二甲基硫醚」的探测存在哪些科学争议？

What scientific controversies surround the claimed detection of dimethyl sulfide in the atmosphere of K2-18b?

K2-18b 是一颗约 124 光年外的「Hycean」型亚海王星，韦伯 2023 年的透射光谱显示有甲烷和二氧化碳，并疑似存在二甲基硫醚（DMS），而 DMS 在地球上主要由海洋生物产生。但该信号统计显著性有限，且 DMS 的吸收与数据噪声及其他分子可能混淆，学界普遍认为还需更多观测才能确认，并将其视为「值得关注的候选」而非定论。
K2-18b is a Hycean-type sub-Neptune about 124 light-years away; Webb's 2023 transmission spectrum showed methane and carbon dioxide and a possible hint of dimethyl sulfide (DMS), a gas produced on Earth mainly by marine life. The signal has limited statistical significance and could be confused with noise or other molecules, so the community treats it as an intriguing candidate requiring more observations rather than a confirmed result.

什么是系外行星的「透射光谱」？

What is the transmission spectrum of an exoplanet?

当系外行星从母恒星前方经过时，恒星光线会穿过行星大气的边缘薄层，大气中的原子和分子会吸收特定波长的光。对比凌星前后恒星光谱的差异，就能得到随波长变化的大气不透明度曲线，即透射光谱，用以识别大气成分。
When an exoplanet transits in front of its host star, starlight filters through the thin limb of the planet's atmosphere, where atoms and molecules absorb specific wavelengths. Comparing the stellar spectrum before and during transit yields a wavelength-dependent opacity curve, the transmission spectrum, which reveals the atmospheric composition.
