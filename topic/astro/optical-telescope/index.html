为什么地面大口径光学望远镜的角分辨率并不随口径无限提高，而往往被限制在约一角秒的水平？

Why does the angular resolution of large ground-based optical telescopes stop improving with aperture and typically stay limited to about one arcsecond?

望远镜衍射极限约为1.22λ/D，口径增大本应不断锐化星像，但大气湍流使波前持续发生畸变。湍流相干长度r₀在地面台址上典型只有十几厘米，对应约一角秒的视宁度光斑；超过这一口径后星像尺寸趋于饱和，加大口径主要增加集光本领而非分辨本领。自适应光学借助变形镜实时补偿波前误差，才能使大望远镜逼近衍射极限。
The diffraction limit 1.22 lambda/D would keep sharpening images as aperture grows, but atmospheric turbulence continuously distorts the incoming wavefront. At visible wavelengths the turbulence coherence length r0 is typically only ten-odd centimeters even at good sites, corresponding to a seeing spot of roughly one arcsecond; beyond that aperture, image size saturates, so a larger mirror mainly gains light gathering rather than resolving power. Adaptive optics with deformable mirrors corrects the wavefront in real time, letting big telescopes approach their diffraction limit.

地基光学望远镜如何利用自适应光学克服大气湍流造成的成像模糊？

How do ground-based optical telescopes use adaptive optics to overcome image blur caused by atmospheric turbulence?

湍流让波前不停畸变，星像被抖散成一角秒左右的光斑。自适应光学用亮星或钠激光导星实时测量波前误差，驱动变形镜以每秒上千次频率施加反向补偿，把能量重新聚拢到接近衍射极限的核内。这使大型地基望远镜在近红外波段获得近乎太空的分辨本领，也是极大望远镜不可或缺的关键技术。
Turbulence keeps bending incoming wavefronts, smearing stars into blobs about one arcsecond wide. An adaptive optics system measures those errors in real time with a bright star or a sodium laser guide star, then drives a deformable mirror applying opposite corrections up to a thousand times per second, refocusing the light into a core approaching the diffraction limit. This gives large ground-based telescopes near-space resolution in the near-infrared and is indispensable for extremely large telescopes.

天文台的视宁度对观测有什么影响？

How does observatory seeing affect observations?

大气湍流使星光抖动、星像模糊，视宁度差会限制地面望远镜的分辨率，因此大望远镜常建于高处干燥之地或借助自适应光学补偿。
Atmospheric turbulence makes starlight shimmer and images blur; poor seeing limits ground-based resolution, so big telescopes sit on high dry sites or use adaptive optics to compensate.
