卫星导航为什么怕“电离层延迟”？

Why does satellite navigation worry about ionospheric delay?

信号穿过电离层时速度会变化，导致定位偏差；系统用双频信号来修正这种延迟提高精度。
Signals slow in the ionosphere, biasing position; systems use dual frequencies to correct this delay and improve accuracy.

手机定位为什么至少需要4颗卫星？

Why does phone positioning need at least four satellites?

3颗可算出经纬高三维位置，第4颗用来校正接收机时钟误差，才能得到准确的时间和位置。
Three give 3D position; a fourth corrects the receiver's clock error for accurate time and location.

北斗导航系统是用来做什么的？

What is the Beidou navigation system for?

北斗是中国建成的全球卫星导航系统，用卫星信号为手机、车辆和船只提供定位和授时服务。
Beidou is China's global satellite navigation system, using satellites to provide positioning and timing for phones, vehicles and ships.
