嫦娥工程在月球探测上取得了哪些突破？

What breakthroughs has the Chang'e program achieved in lunar exploration?

嫦娥三号实现月面软着陆，嫦娥四号首次登陆月球背面，嫦娥五号成功采样返回，为人类提供了宝贵的月壤样本。
Chang'e 3 achieved a soft landing, Chang'e 4 first landed on the lunar far side, and Chang'e 5 returned samples—providing valuable lunar soil for science.

帕克太阳探测器为何能近距离飞掠太阳？

How can the Parker Solar Probe fly so close to the Sun?

帕克探测器借助金星引力反复减速并压低近日点，用碳复合材料隔热盾承受上千万度高温（实际是周围粒子温度），成为首个触碰日冕的航天器。
Parker uses repeated Venus gravity assists to lower its perihelion and a carbon-composite heat shield to endure millions of degrees (particle temperature), becoming the first craft to touch the corona.

旅行者号探测器现在在哪？

Where are the Voyager probes now?

旅行者1号和2号已飞出日球层、进入星际空间，是迄今飞得最远的人造物体，仍向地球传回数据。
Voyagers 1 and 2 have crossed the heliosphere into interstellar space—the farthest human-made objects—and still send data home.

火星探测器面临哪些挑战？

What challenges do Mars rovers face?

火星距离远、通信延迟长，大气稀薄难以减速着陆，且表面辐射和沙尘都会影响设备寿命与供电。
Mars is far with long signal delays, its thin atmosphere makes landing hard to brake, and surface radiation and dust threaten power and hardware life.

人类登上过月球吗？

Have humans landed on the Moon?

登过。1969年阿波罗11号首次把宇航员送上月球，之后共有12人曾在月面行走。
Yes. Apollo 11 first landed astronauts on the Moon in 1969, and twelve people have since walked its surface.

国际空间站是做什么的？

What is the International Space Station for?

国际空间站是绕地球运行的大型实验室，宇航员在上面做失重、生物和材料等各类科学实验。
The ISS is a large lab orbiting Earth where astronauts run experiments on weightlessness, biology, materials and more.
