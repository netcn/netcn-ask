掉到地上的流星体叫什么？研究它有什么用？

What do we call a meteoroid that hits the ground, and why study it?

掉落地面的叫陨石。它保留了太阳系早期的物质成分，是研究行星形成和太阳系的“时间胶囊”。
One that reaches the ground is a meteorite. It preserves early solar-system material, a 'time capsule' for studying planet formation.

英仙座、双子座流星雨是以什么命名的？

How are the Perseids and Geminids named?

以流星看似发出的方向（辐射点）所在的星座命名：辐射点在英仙座就叫英仙座流星雨，在双子座就叫双子座流星雨。
They are named after the constellation containing the radiant—the point from which meteors appear to stream.

看流星雨最好的条件是什么？

What are the best conditions for watching a meteor shower?

选月亮不亮、天空晴朗、远离城市灯光的地方，躺下让视野尽量开阔，耐心等待即可，无需望远镜。
Choose a moonless, clear, dark site away from city lights, lie back for a wide view, and be patient—no telescope needed.

流星是怎么来的？

Where do shooting stars come from?

流星是太空里的尘埃或小石块闯入地球大气，高速摩擦发热发光划过夜空，并不是真正的星星掉下来。
A meteor is dust or a small rock from space entering our atmosphere and glowing from friction; it's not a real star falling.
