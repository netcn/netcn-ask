岁差对历法和农业有什么实际影响？

What practical effect does precession have on calendars and agriculture?

岁差使回归年（季节周期）略短于恒星年，长期累积会让节气相对恒星背景缓慢漂移；历法需以回归年为准，所以祖冲之等人要不断修正。
Precession makes the tropical year slightly shorter than the sidereal year, slowly shifting solar terms against the star background; calendars must use the tropical year, so scholars like Zu Chongzhi kept correcting them.

#岁差 #回归年

什么是'岁差'，它为什么会让北极星换人？

What is precession, and why does it change which star is the pole star?

地球自转轴的指向像陀螺一样缓慢画圈（约26000年一周），使天极在星空中缓慢漂移，所以如今的北极星未来会被织女星等取代。
Earth's spin axis slowly circles like a top (a ~26,000-year cycle), drifting the celestial pole across the sky, so today's Pole Star will later be replaced by Vega.

#岁差 #北极星

为什么几千年前建造的有些神庙对准的星星今天对不上了？

Why do stars aligned with ancient temples and pyramids no longer match today?

因为岁差让恒星相对天极和春分点缓慢移动，几千年来方向已偏移；这反而帮考古学家推算建筑的建造年代。
Precession slowly shifts stars relative to the pole and equinox over millennia, so alignments no longer match—which actually helps archaeologists date the structures.

#岁差 #考古天文

春分点为什么会'西移'？

Why is the vernal equinox said to 'move westward'?

岁差使地球自转轴进动，导致太阳沿黄道回到春分时，交点比上一年略向西（沿黄道逆向）挪动一点，约25800年绕天一周。
Precession makes Earth's axis wobble, so the Sun's crossing back to the equinox shifts slightly westward along the ecliptic each year, completing a loop in about 25,800 years.
