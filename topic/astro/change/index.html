嫦娥四号登陆月球背面为什么需要“鹊桥”中继星？

Why did Chang'e 4 need the Queqiao relay satellite to land on the far side?

月球背面永远背对地球，无法直接通信，鹊桥中继星停在地月拉格朗日点，把信号转发回地球。
The far side never faces Earth, so the Queqiao relay satellite at a Lagrange point bounces signals between the lander and Earth.

嫦娥五号做了什么特别的事？

What was special about Chang'e 5?

它在2020年从月球采集约1.7公斤月壤并成功带回地球，是人类时隔四十多年再次取回月球样品。
In 2020 it collected ~1.7 kg of lunar soil and returned it—the first Moon sample brought back in over four decades.

嫦娥工程是什么？

What is the Chang'e program?

嫦娥是中国探月工程的系列任务，用绕月、落月、采样返回等方式一步步探测月球。
The Chang'e program is China's lunar exploration series, stepping through orbiting, landing, and sample return.
