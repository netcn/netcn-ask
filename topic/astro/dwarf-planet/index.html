妊神星（Haumea）为什么形状像一颗被拉长的椭球？

Why is Haumea shaped like an elongated ellipsoid?

妊神星自转极快（约 4 小时一圈），离心力把它拉成椭球，还拥有光环和两颗卫星，是柯伊伯带中形态最奇特的天体之一。
Haumea spins extremely fast (about one rotation every 4 hours), and centrifugal force stretches it into an ellipsoid; it also has a ring and two moons, making it one of the oddest Kuiper Belt objects.

阋神星（Eris）的发现为何推动了冥王星的降级？

How did the discovery of Eris lead to Pluto's demotion?

2005 年发现的阋神星质量甚至略大于冥王星，说明外太阳系还有类似天体，促使天文学界重新定义行星，间接导致冥王星被归为矮行星。
Eris, found in 2005, is slightly more massive than Pluto, showing that similar bodies exist in the outer solar system; this pushed astronomers to redefine planet, indirectly demoting Pluto to a dwarf planet.

谷神星（Ceres）既是小行星又是矮行星，它特别在哪？

Ceres is both an asteroid and a dwarf planet—what makes it special?

谷神星是小行星带中最大的天体，直径约 940 公里，自身引力使其接近球形，内部可能有盐水储层，表面还发现了明亮盐斑。
Ceres is the largest object in the asteroid belt, about 940 km across; its gravity shapes it nearly spherical, it may host a briny reservoir inside, and it shows bright salt spots on its surface.
