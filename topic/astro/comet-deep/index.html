奥陌陌（Oumuamua）为何引发热议？

Why did Oumuamua stir so much debate?

它是首个被确认的星际天体，形状细长、缺少彗星气体尾，且出现非引力加速，有人猜测是氢冰或光帆，但更朴素解释是释气；其起源仍是谜。
It was the first confirmed interstellar object—elongated, with no gas tail, and showing non-gravitational acceleration. Some speculated hydrogen ice or a light sail, but outgassing is the simpler explanation; its origin remains mysterious.

鲍里索夫（Borisov）与奥陌陌有何不同？

How did Borisov differ from Oumuamua?

鲍里索夫是典型的星际彗星，有明显的彗发和气体活动，成分类似太阳系彗星；它证明星际空间中存在活跃天体，而奥陌陌更像无气体的岩石碎块。
Borisov was a typical interstellar comet with a clear coma and gas activity, much like Solar System comets; it proved active objects roam interstellar space, whereas Oumuamua looked like a gas-free rock.

彗星中发现了哪些与生命相关的物质？

What life-related materials have been found in comets?

彗星含水和多种有机分子（如氨基酸前体、甲醛），被视为地球早期水和有机物的可能输送者，支持了“生命原料天降”假说。
Comets carry water and many organic molecules (amino-acid precursors, formaldehyde) and are seen as possible deliverers of Earth's early water and organics, supporting the 'life ingredients from space' idea.

罗塞塔号探测器对彗星做了什么？

What did the Rosetta probe do to a comet?

欧空局的罗塞塔号于2014年环绕并登陆67P彗星，第一次在彗星旁仔细分析它的气体和尘埃，发现里面有复杂有机物和水冰。
ESA's Rosetta orbited and landed on comet 67P in 2014, analyzing its gas and dust up close for the first time and finding complex organics and water ice.
