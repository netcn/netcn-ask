多信使观测如何约束中子星的状态方程？

How do multi-messenger observations constrain the neutron-star equation of state?

GW170817的引力波波形给出了中子星“软化”程度的上限，配合千新星电磁观测估计抛射物质量，两者共同限制了核密度之上的物态方程。
The gravitational-wave signal of GW170817 bounds how 'soft' neutron-star matter can be, and kilonova light gives the ejected mass; together they constrain the equation of state above nuclear density.

超新星1987A探测到的中微子为何重要？

Why were the neutrinos from supernova 1987A important?

1987A是近代第一颗被探测到中微子的超新星，约20个中微子在光学亮起前数小时被捕获，证实了核心坍缩模型，并验证中微子几乎不受阻碍地穿出恒星。
SN 1987A was the first supernova from which neutrinos were detected; about 20 arrived hours before the optical light, confirming the core-collapse model and showing neutrinos escape the star almost unhindered.

高能宇宙射线从哪里来？

Where do high-energy cosmic rays come from?

宇宙射线是接近光速的带电粒子，能量最高的“超高能宇宙射线”来源仍不明，可能与活动星系核喷流或星暴星系有关；因带电会被磁场偏转，难以直接回溯其源地。
Cosmic rays are charged particles near light speed; the origin of the most energetic 'ultra-high-energy' ones is unknown, possibly active galactic nucleus jets or starburst galaxies. Because they are charged, magnetic fields deflect them, hiding their source.

2017年的GW170817事件为什么被视为多信使天文学的里程碑?

Why is the 2017 event GW170817 regarded as a milestone of multi-messenger astronomy?

GW170817首次同时探测到双中子星并合的引力波、伽马射线暴和千新星电磁对应体,LIGO/Virgo的引力波信号与费米卫星的伽马射线触发仅相隔约1.7秒。随后全球数十台望远镜锁定宿主星系NGC 4993,证实金、铂等重元素在并合中经快中子俘获过程产生,并给出了独立的哈勃常数测量。该事件表明引力波与电磁波联合观测能获取任何单一信使都无法提供的信息。
GW170817 detected gravitational waves from a binary neutron star merger together with its gamma-ray burst and kilonova counterpart, the LIGO/Virgo signal and Fermi's gamma-ray trigger separated by only about 1.7 seconds. Dozens of telescopes then pinned the event to host galaxy NGC 4993, confirming that heavy elements like gold and platinum are forged in the merger through rapid neutron capture and delivering an independent measurement of the Hubble constant. The event proved that combining gravitational-wave and electromagnetic observations yields information no single messenger can provide.

多信使天文学相比只看电磁波,能给天体物理研究带来什么新信息?

What new information does multi-messenger astronomy add compared with observing electromagnetic waves alone?

不同信使携带互补的物理信息:电磁波谱给出温度与化学成分,引力波直接反映致密天体并合时的动力学与质量,中微子能从不透明的恒星内部或极端加速区无损逃逸,宇宙线则记录最高能量的粒子过程。2017 年 GW170817 事件中,引力波探测与随后在多个波段找到的千新星光学对应体相结合,首次确证了双中子星并合是重元素(如锶)的主要产地之一。把各信使的时间戳、方向和能量放在一起分析,还能独立约束宇宙膨胀速率并检验广义相对论。
Different messengers carry complementary physics: the electromagnetic spectrum traces temperature and composition, gravitational waves reveal the dynamics and masses of compact-object mergers directly, neutrinos escape unscattered from opaque stellar interiors or extreme accelerators, and cosmic rays record the highest-energy particle processes. In the 2017 GW170817 event, combining the gravitational-wave detection with the kilonova counterpart found across multiple bands provided the first evidence that binary neutron star mergers are a major site for producing heavy elements such as strontium. Joint analysis of timing, sky position, and energy across messengers also independently constrains the Hubble constant and tests general relativity.

费米伽马射线空间望远镜是做什么的？

What does the Fermi Gamma-ray Space Telescope do?

费米在伽马波段巡天，探测脉冲星、伽马暴、活动星系核以及潜在的暗物质信号，是多信使天文中的关键电磁波设备。
Fermi surveys the sky in gamma rays, detecting pulsars, gamma-ray bursts, active galactic nuclei and possible dark-matter signals—a key electromagnetic instrument for multi-messenger work.

什么是多信使天文学？

What is multi-messenger astronomy?

多信使天文学同时用电磁波、引力波、中微子和宇宙线等多种“信使”来研究同一天体事件，能获得单一渠道无法得到的全貌。2017年的双中子星并合GW170817是这一领域的里程碑。
Multi-messenger astronomy studies the same cosmic event with several 'messengers' at once—electromagnetic light, gravitational waves, neutrinos and cosmic rays—revealing a fuller picture than any one channel alone. The 2017 neutron-star merger GW170817 was its landmark.
