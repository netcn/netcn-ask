脉冲星计时阵列是通过什么原理探测纳赫兹引力波的？

By what principle do pulsar timing arrays detect nanohertz gravitational waves?

毫秒脉冲星以极高稳定性发出射电脉冲，相当于宇宙中的天然时钟；当波长以光年计的纳赫兹引力波穿过地球与脉冲星之间的时空时，会周期性地改变脉冲到达地球的时间。NANOGrav 等阵列通过长期监测数十颗脉冲星的到达时间残差的相关性，在 2023 年报告了来自超大质量黑洞双星背景的纳赫兹引力波迹象。
Millisecond pulsars emit radio pulses with extraordinary clock-like stability, acting as natural cosmic clocks; when nanohertz gravitational waves with light-year wavelengths pass through the spacetime between Earth and a pulsar, they periodically alter the pulse arrival times at Earth. Arrays such as NANOGrav monitor the correlated timing residuals of dozens of pulsars over years and in 2023 reported evidence of a nanohertz gravitational-wave background from supermassive black hole binaries.

为什么引力波被称为测量哈勃常数的「标准汽笛」？

Why are gravitational waves called standard sirens for measuring the Hubble constant?

双中子星并合发出的引力波振幅直接给出其光度距离，而伴随的电磁对应体（如千新星）能提供红移，二者结合可在不依赖宇宙距离阶梯的情况下独立测定哈勃常数。这种方法绕开了传统测距的级联误差，被视为化解当前 H0 测量分歧的独立仲裁手段。
The amplitude of gravitational waves from a binary neutron star merger directly yields its luminosity distance, while the associated electromagnetic counterpart such as a kilonova supplies the redshift; together they determine the Hubble constant independently of the cosmic distance ladder. This approach avoids the cascading errors of traditional distance measurements and is seen as an independent arbiter of the current H0 tension.

科学家如何从一个引力波波形反推出黑洞的质量与自旋？

How do scientists infer the masses and spins of black holes from a gravitational-wave waveform?

引力波波形的频率扫掠速率与振幅由双星的总质量与质量比决定，而波形在并合前后的相位与调制蕴含自旋信息。研究人员把观测信号与广义相对论数值相对论库中的模板逐毫秒匹配，通过贝叶斯参数估计得到质量、自旋及距离的后验分布。
The chirp rate and amplitude of a gravitational-wave waveform are set by the binary's total mass and mass ratio, while the phase and modulation around merger encode the spins. Researchers match the observed signal against templates from numerical relativity in general relativity millisecond by millisecond, using Bayesian parameter estimation to obtain posterior distributions for masses, spins, and distance.

什么是「双黑洞并合」引力波事件？

What is a binary black hole merger gravitational-wave event?

双黑洞并合是指两颗相互绕转的黑洞因引力波辐射损失轨道能量而螺旋靠近、最终融合为一颗更大黑洞的过程。该过程会以引力波形式释放出相当于数个太阳质量对应的能量，被 LIGO/Virgo 等探测器以激光干涉方式记录为「啁啾」信号。
A binary black hole merger is the process in which two orbiting black holes spiral inward as they lose orbital energy to gravitational radiation and finally coalesce into a single larger black hole. The event releases energy equivalent to several solar masses as gravitational waves, recorded by detectors such as LIGO/Virgo as a chirp signal via laser interferometry.

事件视界望远镜（EHT）是如何拍到黑洞照片的？

How did the Event Horizon Telescope capture an image of a black hole?

EHT 把分布在全球的射电望远镜通过甚长基线干涉技术连成一台口径相当于地球直径的虚拟望远镜，从而具备足以分辨黑洞阴影的角分辨率。2019 年它发布了 M87 星系中心黑洞的环形图像，2022 年又发布了银河系中心人马座 A* 的图像。
The EHT links radio telescopes around the globe through very long baseline interferometry into a virtual telescope with an effective aperture the size of Earth, giving it the angular resolution to resolve a black hole shadow. In 2019 it released the ring-shaped image of the black hole in galaxy M87, and in 2022 the image of Sagittarius A* at the center of the Milky Way.

黑洞的「事件视界」是什么？

What is the event horizon of a black hole?

事件视界是黑洞周围的一个边界，越过它任何物质或光都无法逃脱黑洞的引力，因此外部观测者看不到视界内的信息。它的半径（史瓦西半径）正比于黑洞质量，是黑洞「只进不出」这一特性的几何体现。
The event horizon is the boundary around a black hole beyond which nothing, not even light, can escape its gravity, so an outside observer cannot see any information from within. Its radius, the Schwarzschild radius, is proportional to the black hole mass and embodies the geometric nature of the black hole's one-way passage.

双中子星并合与金、铂等重元素的起源有什么关系？

What is the connection between binary neutron star mergers and the origin of heavy elements like gold and platinum?

双中子星并合会抛出大量富含中子的物质，在极短时间内通过快中子俘获（r-过程）合成比铁重的元素，包括金、铂、铀等。GW170817 的千新星观测在光谱与亮度演化上与 r-过程产物高度吻合，有力支持这类并合是宇宙中重元素的重要产地。
Binary neutron star mergers eject large amounts of neutron-rich matter that, in a fraction of a second, synthesizes elements heavier than iron through rapid neutron capture, the r-process, including gold, platinum, and uranium. The kilonova of GW170817 matched r-process products in both spectrum and brightness evolution, strongly supporting such mergers as a major site of the Universe's heavy elements.
