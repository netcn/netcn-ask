如何用次级凌星测量行星温度？

How is secondary eclipse used to measure planetary temperature?

次级凌星指行星转到恒星背后、其自身热辐射被遮挡的时刻。比较行星在掩食前后与恒星叠加及单独恒星的亮度差，可得到行星的发射通量，进而推算其昼夜面平衡温度。结合光谱还能绘制温度随波长的变化。
A secondary eclipse occurs when the planet passes behind the star and its own thermal emission is blocked. Comparing the brightness with and without the planet's contribution yields the planet's emitted flux, from which day-side equilibrium temperature is derived. Spectrally resolved eclipses map temperature versus wavelength.

透射光谱法如何揭示系外行星的大气成分？

How does the transmission spectroscopy method reveal an exoplanet's atmospheric composition?

当行星凌星时，恒星光会穿过行星大气的边缘薄层，其中的原子和分子在特定波长吸收光，使该波长处的凌星深度略增。通过比对不同波长的凌星深度（即透射光谱），可辨认出钠、水、二氧化碳等成分。
During a transit, starlight passes through the thin limb of the planet's atmosphere, where atoms and molecules absorb at specific wavelengths and deepen the transit slightly there. Comparing transit depths across wavelengths (the transmission spectrum) reveals species such as sodium, water and carbon dioxide.

K2-18b的大气中发现了什么引人关注的信号？

What notable signals were found in K2-18b's atmosphere?

K2-18b是一颗位于宜居带的亚海王星，JWST的观测在其大气中同时探测到甲烷和二氧化碳，并报告了可能存在的二甲基硫（DMS）的暗示。若DMS被确认，将是潜在生物标志，但尚需更高质量数据。该行星也可能拥有富氢的海洋大气。
K2-18b is a habitable-zone sub-Neptune where JWST detected both methane and carbon dioxide, with a tentative hint of dimethyl sulfide (DMS). If confirmed, DMS would be a potential biosignature, though higher-quality data are needed. The planet may also harbor a hydrogen-rich ocean atmosphere.

热木星的大气环流有什么特点？

What characterizes the atmospheric circulation of hot Jupiters?

热木星因紧贴恒星而受热极强，白天侧温度可达数千开尔文，驱动强大的赤道急流将热量从昼面输运到夜面。其大气常出现平流层逆温，并在晨昏线附近形成金属云和矿物云。三维流体模拟显示其风带速度可达每秒数公里。
Hot Jupiters orbit so close to their stars that dayside temperatures reach thousands of kelvin, driving powerful equatorial jets that transport heat from day to night sides. Their atmospheres often show stratospheric inversions and form metal and mineral clouds near the terminator. Three-dimensional fluid simulations predict wind speeds of several kilometers per second.

大气逃逸中的瑞利散射尾说明了什么？

What does the Rayleigh scattering tail in atmospheric escape indicate?

在氢逃逸的凌星光谱中，波长越短吸收越强的瑞利散射特征表现为蓝端加深的“尾”。它揭示上层大气正在经历流体动力的氢流失，并可用于约束行星的高层大气温度和逃逸率。结合莱曼α测量能区分风致逃逸与辐射压效应。
In transit spectra of hydrogen escape, the Rayleigh scattering feature—stronger absorption at shorter wavelengths—appears as a deepened blue tail. It reveals hydrodynamic hydrogen loss in the upper atmosphere and constrains its temperature and escape rate. Combined with Lyman-alpha, it distinguishes wind-driven escape from radiation pressure.

为什么热木星的大气会“逃逸”？

Why does a hot Jupiter's atmosphere “escape”?

热木星接受恒星的强烈辐射，高层大气被加热到极高温度，部分轻气体（主要是氢）获得足够动能挣脱行星引力而流失。这种流体动力逃逸可改变行星的质量与成分。观测上常通过莱曼α的蓝移吸收来探测这种逃逸。
Hot Jupiters receive intense stellar radiation; their upper atmospheres heat to extreme temperatures so light gases, mainly hydrogen, gain enough energy to escape the planet's gravity. This hydrodynamic escape can alter the planet's mass and composition. It is often detected via blueshifted Lyman-alpha absorption.

系外行星大气中检测到水蒸气，为什么不等于那里有生命？

Why does detecting water vapor in an exoplanet atmosphere not mean life exists there?

水是普通分子，在巨行星、褐矮星甚至恒星大气中都能由化学平衡自然产生，并不要求生物活动。确认生命需要综合氧气、甲烷等不平衡气体的共存、季节变化与行星表面条件等多重证据，远超出单一分子检测。
Water is a common molecule that forms naturally by chemical equilibrium in giant planets, brown dwarfs and even stellar atmospheres, with no need for biology. Confirming life requires multiple lines of evidence — co-existing out-of-equilibrium gases like oxygen and methane, seasonal changes, surface conditions — far beyond a single molecule detection.

哪些大气信号可能指示生命？

Which atmospheric signals might indicate life?

生物标志物指可能由生命活动产生并可在大气光谱中检测的化学物种，例如氧气、臭氧与甲烷的非平衡共存。二氧化碳、水蒸气和氮气等也构成宜居背景。单一气体并不充分，需多种气体的化学不平衡组合来提高可信度。
Biosignatures are chemical species that life might produce and that can be detected in atmospheric spectra, such as a non-equilibrium coexistence of oxygen, ozone, and methane. Carbon dioxide, water vapor, and nitrogen form the habitable background. No single gas suffices; a chemically imbalanced combination strengthens the case.

比邻星b的大气可能是什么样的？

What might the atmosphere of Proxima Centauri b be like?

比邻星b是距我们最近的岩质系外行星，位于红矮星宜居带内，但恒星频繁的耀斑可能剥离其大气。若保有大气，成分可能受强烈紫外辐射改造，并以二氧化碳或氮为主。其真实大气状况仍是未解之谜，需未来望远镜直接探测。
Proxima Centauri b is the nearest rocky exoplanet, in the habitable zone of a red dwarf, but frequent stellar flares may strip its atmosphere. If retained, it could be dominated by carbon dioxide or nitrogen, altered by intense UV radiation. Its actual atmosphere remains unknown, awaiting future direct observations.

云和霾如何影响透射光谱？

How do clouds and hazes affect transmission spectra?

云和霾中的微粒会散射或吸收穿过大气边缘的光，压平分子吸收线的对比度并抬升光谱基线。厚云可掩盖大部分分子特征，使大气显得“平淡”。通过建模粒子大小和分布，研究者仍能从中提取温度和部分成分信息。
Particles in clouds and hazes scatter or absorb light passing through the atmospheric limb, flattening molecular absorption features and raising the spectral baseline. Thick clouds can mask most molecular signatures, making the atmosphere look featureless. Modeling particle size and distribution still extracts temperature and some composition.

“红边”为什么被视为潜在的生命信号？

Why is the “red edge” considered a potential sign of life?

地球植被中的叶绿素在约700纳米处对近红外光反射率骤增，形成独特的“红边”光谱特征。若在系外行星反射光谱中检测到类似陡变，可能暗示光合作用式生物圈。但它并非生命独有，需排除矿物等其他成因。
Chlorophyll in Earth's vegetation sharply raises reflectance near 700 nm, creating the distinctive “red edge.” Detecting a similar spectral jump in an exoplanet's reflected light could hint at a photosynthetic biosphere. It is not unique to life, so mineral causes must be excluded.

如何探测系外行星的大气？

How do we probe exoplanet atmospheres?

最常用的方法是凌星透射光谱：当行星从恒星前方经过时，部分星光穿过其大气边缘，特定分子会在光谱上留下吸收线。此外也可用发射光谱和反射光谱研究大气的温度与成分。地面和空间望远镜（如JWST）都能开展这类观测。
The most common method is transit transmission spectroscopy: as a planet passes in front of its star, some starlight filters through the atmosphere's limb, where specific molecules imprint absorption lines. Emission and reflection spectra also reveal temperature and composition. Both ground- and space-based telescopes, such as JWST, perform these observations.

什么是系外行星大气？

What is an exoplanet atmosphere?

系外行星大气是包裹在行星固体或液态表面之外的气体层，成分可能包括氢、氦、水蒸气、二氧化碳等。大气决定了行星表面的温度与是否可能宜居。通过星光穿过大气时的吸收特征，天文学家能推断其组成。
An exoplanet atmosphere is the gas layer enveloping a planet's solid or liquid surface, possibly containing hydrogen, helium, water vapor, carbon dioxide, and more. The atmosphere sets the surface temperature and habitability potential. By studying how starlight is absorbed passing through it, astronomers infer its composition.

为什么有些系外行星被称作超级地球？

Why are some exoplanets called super-Earths?

超级地球指质量介于地球与海王星之间（约 1–10 倍地球质量）、多半是岩质或富水成分的行星。它们在本星系中并不存在，却在系外非常常见，是研究行星形成与大气的重要样本。
Super-Earths are planets with masses between Earth's and Neptune's (roughly 1–10 Earth masses), mostly rocky or water-rich in composition. They do not exist in our Solar System but are very common among exoplanets, making them key samples for studying planet formation and atmospheres.

天文学家能直接“看到”系外行星吗？

Can astronomers directly “see” exoplanets?

大多数系外行星是通过间接方法（如凌星和视向速度）发现的，因为它们的星光相比恒星极其微弱。但直接成像技术已能拍到少数远离恒星、年轻且明亮的巨行星。未来如极大望远镜和掩星 Coronagraph 将提升直接成像能力。
Most exoplanets are found indirectly via transits or radial velocity because their light is extremely faint next to the star. Direct imaging has nevertheless captured a few young, bright giant planets far from their stars. Future extremely large telescopes and coronagraphs will improve direct imaging.

系外行星大气和地球大气有什么不同？

How do exoplanet atmospheres differ from Earth's?

地球大气以氮和氧为主、厚度适中且支持液态水与生命。许多系外行星大气则可能是氢氦主导、极度高温或高压，甚至布满矿物云。不同恒星辐照和行星质量造就了极为多样的行星大气类型。
Earth's atmosphere is nitrogen- and oxygen-rich, moderately thick, and supports liquid water and life. Many exoplanet atmospheres are instead hydrogen-helium dominated, extremely hot or high-pressure, and may be covered in mineral clouds. Different stellar irradiation and planet masses produce great atmospheric diversity.

系外行星上会有“天气”吗？

Can exoplanets have “weather”?

有。只要行星拥有大气，就可能出现风、云、风暴和温度变化等天气现象。例如热木星上的超强风和矿物云，以及类地行星上可能的水循环。天气的剧烈程度取决于行星温度、自转和大气成分。
Yes. Any planet with an atmosphere can show weather such as winds, clouds, storms, and temperature variations. Hot Jupiters exhibit extreme winds and mineral clouds, while Earth-like planets may have water cycles. The intensity depends on temperature, rotation, and atmospheric composition.
