阿波罗带回的月球岩石告诉了我们什么？

What did Apollo's Moon rocks tell us?

岩石同位素显示月球可能源于约45亿年前一颗火星大小天体撞击地球溅出的物质凝聚而成（大碰撞假说）。
Rock isotopes suggest the Moon formed about 4.5 billion years ago from debris of a Mars-sized body hitting Earth (giant-impact hypothesis).

宇航员在月球上为什么跳着走？

Why do astronauts on the Moon hop as they walk?

月球重力只有地球的约六分之一，身体很轻，加上笨重的航天服，小步跳跃反而比正常走路更稳当省力。
The Moon's gravity is about one-sixth of Earth's and the spacesuit is bulky, so hopping is steadier and easier than walking.

阿波罗计划一共成功登月几次？

How many successful crewed Moon landings did Apollo achieve?

阿波罗11、12、14、15、16、17号共6次成功把航天员送上月球表面，13号因故障未能登月但安全返回。
Apollo 11,12,14,15,16,17 successfully landed astronauts—six times; Apollo 13 failed to land but returned safely.

人类第一次登上月球是哪一年？

What year did humans first land on the Moon?

1969年，美国阿波罗11号任务把阿姆斯特朗和奥尔德林送上月球，是人类首次登月。
In 1969, NASA's Apollo 11 landed Armstrong and Aldrin on the Moon—the first crewed lunar landing.
