罗曼（Roman）太空望远镜如何研究暗能量？

How will the Roman Space Telescope study dark energy?

南希·格雷斯·罗曼望远镜用大视场红外巡天做弱引力透镜和超新星统计，以更高精度测绘暗能量如何随宇宙时间演化。
The Nancy Grace Roman Telescope will use wide-field infrared surveys for weak gravitational lensing and supernova statistics, mapping with higher precision how dark energy evolves over cosmic time.

蜻蜓（Dragonfly）任务为何去土卫六？

Why is the Dragonfly mission going to Titan?

NASA的蜻蜓是一架核动力旋翼飞行器，将飞越土卫六表面采样，研究其有机化学与潜在前生命环境，是首个地外天体上的多旋翼探测器。
NASA's Dragonfly is a nuclear-powered rotorcraft that will fly across Titan sampling its surface, studying organic chemistry and possible pre-life environments—the first multirotor explorer on another world.

LISA空间引力波探测器有何不同？

What is different about the LISA space gravitational-wave detector?

LISA由三颗相隔百万公里的航天器组成，用激光测距聆听毫赫兹引力波（如超大质量黑洞并合），填补地面探测器听不到的低频空白。
LISA uses three spacecraft a million kilometers apart, laser-ranging to listen for millihertz gravitational waves (e.g. supermassive black-hole mergers), filling the low-frequency gap ground detectors cannot hear.

木卫二快船（Europa Clipper）将研究什么？

What will Europa Clipper study?

它将多次飞掠木卫二，用雷达穿透冰壳探测地下海洋、评估其宜居性，是搜寻太阳系内生命的重要任务。
It will repeatedly fly past Europa, using radar to probe the ice shell and subsurface ocean and assess habitability—a major search for life within the Solar System.

阿尔忒弥斯（Artemis）计划目标是什么？

What is the goal of the Artemis program?

阿尔忒弥斯是美国牵头的新一轮探月计划，目标在2020年代让首位女性和下一位男性登陆南极，并建立月球门户以支撑未来火星任务。
Artemis is a US-led new lunar program aiming to land the first woman and next man near the south pole in the 2020s and build a lunar Gateway to support future Mars missions.

火星采样返回任务面临什么难题？

What challenges face a Mars sample-return mission?

需在火星起飞、轨道交会对接并将样本送回地球，技术链条极长且要严防地球生物污染，目前由NASA与ESA合作推进。
It must launch from Mars, dock in orbit and return samples to Earth—an extremely long chain requiring strict protection against Earth contamination; NASA and ESA are pursuing it together.

薇拉·鲁宾天文台（LSST）将做什么？

What will the Vera Rubin Observatory (LSST) do?

这台位于智利的大视场巡天望远镜每三夜扫描整片天空，十年积累海量数据，用于捕捉瞬变天体、近地小行星和暗能量演化。
This large-survey telescope in Chile will scan the whole sky every three nights, accumulating a decade of massive data to catch transient objects, near-Earth asteroids and dark-energy evolution.

星舰（Starship）为什么被认为会改变航天？

Why is Starship expected to change spaceflight?

星舰是SpaceX完全可重复使用、运载能力极大的飞船，目标是大幅降低入轨成本，支撑星座部署、月球基地与火星殖民。
Starship is SpaceX's fully reusable, very high-capacity vehicle aimed at drastically cutting launch cost, enabling satellite constellations, lunar bases and Mars colonization.
