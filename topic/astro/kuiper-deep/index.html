为什么柯伊伯带如今的总质量远低于其100公里级天体在原位吸积所需的原盘质量？这一质量亏损如何支持海王星外迁的动力学模型？

Why is the Kuiper Belt's present-day mass far below that needed for its 100-km bodies to have accreted in situ, and how does this mass deficit support models of Neptune's outward migration?

今日柯伊伯带总质量仅约0.02倍地球质量，而要在原位碰撞吸积出100公里级天体，原盘质量至少需高出一个数量级，且太阳系年龄内的并合也太慢。这说明该区曾富含物质后被清空：海王星外迁时经共振俘获与引力散射，把大部分天体抛入奥尔特云或星际空间。质量亏损因此成为尼斯模型行星迁移理论的关键证据。
The Kuiper Belt now contains only about 0.02 Earth masses, yet growing 100-km bodies in situ requires a primordial disk at least an order of magnitude more massive, and collisions today cannot assemble such objects within the Sun's lifetime. The deficit implies the region once held far more material, most of it removed when Neptune's outward migration captured survivors into resonances and scattered the rest into the Oort cloud or interstellar space, a cornerstone prediction of the Nice model.

阋神星（Eris）为何让冥王星被降级？

Why did Eris lead to Pluto's demotion?

2005年发现的阋神星质量比冥王星还大，迫使天文学家正视“什么是行星”问题，直接推动了IAU 2006年矮行星定义与冥王星除名。
The 2005 discovery of Eris, more massive than Pluto, forced astronomers to face 'what is a planet,' directly driving the IAU's 2006 dwarf-planet definition and Pluto's reclassification.

塞德娜（Sedna）为何被认为来自“内奥尔特云”？

Why is Sedna thought to come from the 'inner Oort Cloud'?

塞德娜拥有异常遥远、近日点极高的轨道（约76天文单位起），无法由海王星解释，可能受经过的恒星或行星九扰动，是内奥尔特云的证据。
Sedna has an extremely distant orbit with a very high perihelion (~76 AU), unexplained by Neptune; it may have been perturbed by a passing star or Planet Nine, evidence for an inner Oort Cloud.

柯伊伯带是什么？它和奥尔特云有何不同？

What is the Kuiper Belt, and how does it differ from the Oort Cloud?

柯伊伯带是位于海王星轨道外、由冰质小天体组成的盘状区域，短周期彗星源于此；奥尔特云是更遥远、近似球壳的彗星库。
The Kuiper Belt is a disk-shaped region of icy bodies beyond Neptune that supplies short-period comets, whereas the Oort Cloud is a far more distant, roughly spherical reservoir of comets.

长周期彗星的倾角近乎随机、还常见逆行，短周期彗星却几乎都顺行且贴近黄道面。这一对比暗示了怎样的两类彗星储库？

Long-period comets show nearly random inclinations, often retrograde, whereas short-period comets are almost always prograde and confined near the ecliptic. What two comet reservoirs does this contrast point to?

长周期彗星源于奥尔特云：早期被巨行星散射到数千至数万天文单位远的冰体，长期受银河系潮汐力作用，轨道面已充分随机化，故来向任意。短周期彗星则来自扁薄的柯伊伯带与散射盘，那里保留着原行星盘的几何形态，成员经共振或相互摄动迁入内太阳系时，仍带着小倾角与顺行特征。
Long-period comets come from the Oort Cloud: icy bodies flung by the giant planets to thousands of astronomical units, where the Galactic tide has spent billions of years randomizing their orbital planes, so they return from arbitrary directions. Short-period comets instead originate in the flattened Kuiper Belt and scattered disk, which retain the primordial disk's geometry; members nudged inward through resonances carry small inclinations and prograde motion.

柯伊伯带天体分为哪些类别？

What categories do Kuiper Belt objects fall into?

分为经典柯伊伯带天体（圆轨道）、共振天体（如与海王星2比3共振的冥族天体）和散射盘天体（轨道被扰动伸长），反映不同动力学历史。
They divide into classical KBOs (round orbits), resonant objects (like Pluto's 2:3 resonance with Neptune) and scattered-disk objects (stretched, perturbed orbits)—each reflecting a different dynamical history.
