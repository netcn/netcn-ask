EDGES实验探测到了什么争议信号？

What controversial signal did the EDGES experiment detect?

EDGES声称在21厘米吸收谱中看到比预期更深、更冷的信号，若属实意味着早期宇宙气体异常冷或存在额外背景辐射，结果仍存激烈争论。
EDGES reported a 21-cm absorption signal deeper and colder than expected; if real it implies the early gas was unusually cold or there was extra background radiation—a result still fiercely debated.

第一代恒星（星族 III）有什么特别？

What is special about the first stars (Population III)?

星族III是宇宙中最早诞生的恒星，由大爆炸后纯氢氦气体在暗物质晕中直接坍缩形成，几乎不含比氦更重的元素（金属丰度趋近于零）。由于没有重元素帮忙散热，它们质量极大（数十到数百倍太阳质量）、温度高、寿命极短。它们以强烈的紫外辐射和最早的重元素（经超新星）启动了宇宙再电离与后续星系的化学演化。
Population III stars were the very first stars, formed from pristine hydrogen–helium gas in early dark-matter halos with essentially no elements heavier than helium (near-zero metallicity). Because heavy elements help gas radiate away heat, their absence let the gas collapse into very massive (tens to hundreds of solar masses), hot, short-lived stars. Their intense ultraviolet light and the first heavy elements from their supernovae drove cosmic reionization and seeded later chemical evolution.

什么是21厘米线，它如何探测早期宇宙？

What is the 21-centimeter line and how does it probe the early Universe?

中性氢的超精细结构跃迁发出21厘米射电波，其红移后波长记录了再电离前后氢的分布，是绘制宇宙黎明的独特探针（如SKA、HERA实验）。
Neutral hydrogen's hyperfine transition emits 21-cm radio waves; their redshifted wavelengths record hydrogen distribution before and after reionization, a unique probe of cosmic dawn (e.g. SKA, HERA).

宇宙的再电离时期发生了什么？

What happened during the universe's reionization epoch?

在大爆炸后约38万年的微波背景形成、宇宙进入“黑暗时期”之后，第一批恒星和星系释放的紫外辐射逐渐把弥漫的中性氢重新电离成质子和自由电子。这一过程大约发生在红移 z≈6–20（宇宙年龄约4亿到10亿年），标志着宇宙从几乎全中性变为电离态。再电离也结束了宇宙黑暗时代，并深刻影响了此后星系的演化。
After the cosmic microwave background formed (~380,000 years after the Big Bang) and the universe entered its dark ages, the ultraviolet light from the first stars and galaxies gradually reionized the pervasive neutral hydrogen, splitting it back into protons and free electrons. This reionization epoch spanned roughly z≈6–20 (about 400–1000 million years after the Big Bang) and marks the transition from a mostly neutral to an ionized universe. It ended the cosmic dark ages and shaped all later galaxy evolution.

宇宙黎明指什么时候？

What era does cosmic dawn refer to?

宇宙黎明指第一批恒星和星系开始形成、向周围中性氢发出紫外辐射并启动再电离的时期，大约对应红移 z≈20–30（大爆炸后约1亿到2亿年）。它标志着宇宙黑暗时代的结束与“发光天体”时代的开端。这是连接原初宇宙与今日星系演化的关键过渡阶段。
Cosmic dawn refers to the era when the first stars and galaxies formed and began emitting ultraviolet light into the surrounding neutral hydrogen, initiating reionization—roughly z≈20–30, or about 100–200 million years after the Big Bang. It marks the end of the cosmic dark ages and the beginning of the luminous universe. It is a key transition between the primordial cosmos and today's galaxies.
