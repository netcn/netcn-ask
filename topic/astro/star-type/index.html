什么是蓝超巨星，它们为什么罕见？

What is a blue supergiant and why are they rare?

蓝超巨星是温度高、光度极大的大质量恒星，处于极短而剧烈的演化阶段，因此数量稀少且很快走向超新星。
Blue supergiants are extremely hot, luminous massive stars in a brief, violent evolutionary phase, so they are rare and quickly head toward supernova.

什么是沃尔夫-拉叶星？

What is a Wolf-Rayet star?

沃尔夫-拉叶星是大质量恒星演化晚期的特殊类型，以极强恒星风吹走外层，暴露出炽热富氦的核心，常是超新星或伽马暴的前身。
A Wolf-Rayet star is a late-stage massive star blowing off its outer layers with intense winds, exposing a hot helium-rich core; it is often a precursor to a supernova or gamma-ray burst.

什么是变星？

What is a variable star?

变星的亮度会随时间发生规律性变化，原因包括脉动、旋转或伴星掩食，是测量宇宙距离的重要工具。
A variable star changes brightness over time, due to pulsation, rotation, or eclipsing by a companion, and serves as a key tool for measuring cosmic distances.

什么是棕矮星，它为什么不算真正的恒星？

What is a brown dwarf and why isn't it a true star?

棕矮星质量介于行星和恒星之间（约13到80倍木星质量），核心温度不够高，无法持续进行氢核聚变，因此被称为失败的恒星。
A brown dwarf has a mass between a planet and a star (about 13–80 Jupiter masses) and its core is too cool to sustain hydrogen fusion, earning it the name failed star.

Starlight refracts through turbulent air, seeming to flicker.

什么是赫罗图，它有什么用？

What is the Hertzsprung-Russell diagram and what is it for?

赫罗图以恒星的光谱类型（温度）为横轴、光度为纵轴作图，能直观展示恒星的演化阶段和分类。
The Hertzsprung-Russell diagram plots stars by spectral type (temperature) against luminosity, revealing their evolutionary stages and classifications at a glance.

什么是主序星？

What is a main-sequence star?

主序星正处于稳定燃烧核心氢的阶段，占恒星寿命的约90%；太阳目前就是一颗主序星。
A main-sequence star is steadily burning hydrogen in its core and spends about 90% of its life there; the Sun is currently a main-sequence star.

夜空里的星星为什么颜色不同?一张图怎么把恒星的一生讲完?

Why do stars shine in different colors — how can a single diagram tell a star's whole life story?

颜色就是温度计:猎户座参宿四泛红(约3500度),天狼星蓝白(约9900度),规律与烧铁块相同——越热越蓝。把恒星按颜色(表面温度)与光度画在一张图上即赫罗图,1900年代初期赫茨普龙与罗素各自发现恒星并不随机散布,九成挤在从暗红到亮蓝的对角主序带上——那是稳定燃烧氢的中年期;右上方聚集着膨胀降温的巨星,左下方是熄火收缩的白矮星。恒星一生就沿着这张图移动:主序越长越久,离开主序便进入快进的晚年。读懂它等于拿到了恒星级谱与寿命表。
Color is a thermometer: Betelgeuse glows red (about thirty-five hundred kelvin), Sirius blue-white (near ten thousand), the same rule as heated iron — hotter means bluer. Plotting stars by color (surface temperature) against luminosity yields the Hertzsprung-Russell diagram, found independently in the early 1900s: stars scatter not randomly but with ninety percent crowded along the diagonal main sequence — stable hydrogen-burning middle age; swollen cooled giants sit upper right, shrunken white dwarfs lower left. A star's lifetime is movement across this chart: main sequence lasts longest, then late stages run fast. Reading it hands you stellar genealogy plus life expectancy.

什么是双星系统？

What is a binary star system?

双星系统由两颗相互绕转的恒星组成，银河系中超过半数恒星属于双星或多星系统。
A binary system consists of two stars orbiting each other; more than half the stars in the Milky Way belong to binary or multiple systems.

什么是红矮星？

What is a red dwarf?

红矮星是质量较小、表面较冷、发光暗弱的恒星，占银河系恒星的绝大多数，寿命可长达上万亿年。
A red dwarf is a small, cool, dim star that makes up the vast majority of stars in the Milky Way and can live for trillions of years.

什么是白矮星？

What is a white dwarf?

白矮星是中小质量恒星死亡后的致密残骸，体积像地球却拥有太阳级质量，靠电子简并压力支撑而不再核聚变。
A white dwarf is the dense remnant of a low-to-medium mass star, Earth-sized but Sun-mass, supported by electron degeneracy pressure and no longer fusing.
