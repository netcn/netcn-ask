GW170817事件为什么重要？

Why was the GW170817 event important?

GW170817是首次直接探测到的双中子星并合引力波事件，随后在全球望远镜中观测到对应的伽马射线暴与千新星光学信号。它首次实现引力波与电磁波的“多信使”联测，并独立测出哈勃常数、证实了重元素（如金）的r过程产地。
GW170817 was the first directly detected gravitational wave from a binary neutron star merger, followed by a corresponding gamma-ray burst and kilonova seen across the electromagnetic spectrum. It achieved the first multi-messenger observation linking gravitational and electromagnetic waves, independently measured the Hubble constant, and confirmed neutron-star mergers as sites of heavy-element r-process production like gold.

引力波有哪两种基本偏振模式？

What are the two basic polarization modes of gravitational waves?

在广义相对论中，引力波只有“加号”（plus）和“叉号”（cross）两种张量偏振，对应互相垂直的两个拉伸-压缩方向。其他引力理论还可能预言标量或矢量偏振。通过多探测器网络分析偏振，可检验广义相对论在强场下的有效性。
In general relativity, gravitational waves have only two tensor polarizations, “plus” and “cross,” corresponding to two perpendicular stretch-squeeze directions. Alternative gravity theories may predict scalar or vector polarizations. Polarization analysis with detector networks tests general relativity in the strong-field regime.

原初引力波与宇宙暴涨有何关系？

How are primordial gravitational waves related to cosmic inflation?

宇宙暴涨时期时空量子涨落被急剧拉伸，理论上会产生几乎尺度不变的随机引力波背景，即原初引力波。它们会在宇宙微波背景的偏振上留下特有的B模图案。探测这一信号将直接证实暴涨，但目前仍受前景辐射的强烈干扰。
During cosmic inflation, quantum spacetime fluctuations were stretched dramatically, theoretically producing a nearly scale-invariant stochastic background of gravitational waves—primordial gravitational waves. They would imprint a distinctive B-mode pattern on the cosmic microwave background's polarization. Detecting it would directly confirm inflation, but foreground emission strongly interferes.

用死亡恒星的节拍器探测时空涟漪?2023年纳赫兹引力波的发现是怎么回事?

Using dead stars as metronomes to sense spacetime ripples? What did 2023 bring?

毫秒脉冲星的自转稳定堪比原子钟,脉冲到达地球的时间可以精确到纳秒。当一列波长数光年的超低频引力波(如星系中心超大质量黑洞双星绕转产生)扫过银河系,它会让不同方向的脉冲星时钟出现关联性的微微失谐——有的提前有的推后,构成特征图案。NANOGrav等四个团队追踪15年、监测数十颗脉冲星,2023年同时宣布探测到这种纳赫兹引力波背景的统计证据,强度与星系并合模型预言吻合。这相当于把整个银河系变成一台星系尺度的引力波天线,打开了观察超巨黑洞舞蹈的新窗口。
Millisecond pulsars spin as steadily as atomic clocks, their pulses arriving to nanosecond precision. A train of ultralow-frequency gravitational waves — wavelengths of light-years, from orbiting supermassive black-hole binaries — would nudge different pulsar clocks in a correlated pattern, some early some late. Four teams including NANOGrav tracked dozens of pulsars across fifteen years and announced in 2023 statistical evidence for exactly such a nanohertz background, matching merger-model predictions. The galaxy itself became a galactic-scale antenna, opening a window onto the waltzes of supermassive black holes.

人类第一次探测到引力波是在什么时候？

When did humans first detect gravitational waves?

2015年9月，LIGO科学合作组首次直接探测到来自约13亿光年外两个黑洞合并产生的引力波，信号编号为GW150914。这一发现证实了爱因斯坦的百年预言，并打开了观测宇宙的全新窗口。相关三位科学家因此获得2017年诺贝尔物理学奖。
In September 2015 the LIGO Scientific Collaboration made the first direct detection of gravitational waves from two merging black holes about 1.3 billion light-years away, event GW150914. The discovery confirmed Einstein's century-old prediction and opened a new window on the universe. Three scientists won the 2017 Nobel Prize in Physics for it.

探测引力波为什么重要？

Why is detecting gravitational waves important?

引力波让我们能用一种全新的方式聆听宇宙，直接观测黑洞并合这类看不见的事件。它独立于光，可以穿透尘埃和物质，带来电磁波无法提供的信息。与望远镜结合，引力波开启了多信使天文学的新时代。
Gravitational waves let us listen to the universe in a wholly new way, directly observing invisible events like black-hole mergers. Independent of light, they penetrate dust and matter, delivering information no electromagnetic wave can. Combined with telescopes, they opened the era of multi-messenger astronomy.

引力波红移是什么意思？

What is gravitational wave redshift?

引力波红移指波的频率在传播中因宇宙膨胀而降低，与光波的红移类似。它把观测到的频率与源处固有频率联系起来，是推断波源距离和宇宙学参数的桥梁之一。结合电磁对应体测得的红移，可约束膨胀历史。
Gravitational wave redshift is the lowering of wave frequency during propagation due to cosmic expansion, analogous to light's redshift. It links observed and intrinsic frequencies and helps infer source distance and cosmological parameters. Combined with an electromagnetic counterpart's redshift, it constrains the expansion history.

引力波是如何产生的？

How are gravitational waves generated?

当质量分布发生非对称的加速变化时，例如双黑洞或双中子星相互绕转并螺旋靠近，就会辐射引力波带走能量。超新星不对称爆发等剧烈事件也能产生。波的频率和强度取决于系统的质量和运动速度。
Gravitational waves are emitted when a mass distribution accelerates asymmetrically, such as two black holes or neutron stars orbiting and spiraling inward, carrying away energy. Asymmetric supernova explosions can also produce them. The wave frequency and strength depend on the system's mass and speed.

爱因斯坦当年预言引力波了吗？

Did Einstein predict gravitational waves?

是的。爱因斯坦在1916年基于广义相对论场方程推导出弱场下的引力波解，但他一度怀疑其能否被探测。直到2015年LIGO才首次直接探测到双黑洞并合产生的引力波，印证了百年前的理论。
Yes. In 1916 Einstein derived gravitational wave solutions from the weak-field limit of general relativity, though he later doubted they could ever be detected. The first direct detection came in 2015 when LIGO observed waves from a binary black hole merger, confirming the century-old theory.

引力波主要来源于哪些天体事件？

What astrophysical events produce gravitational waves?

最主要的来源是致密双星系统的并合，包括双黑洞、双中子星以及黑洞与中子星的组合。双中子星并合还会伴随短伽马射线暴并抛洒金等重元素。此外，超新星不对称爆发和快速自转的形变中子星也会产生较弱的引力波。
The strongest sources are mergers of compact binaries: binary black holes, binary neutron stars, and black hole-neutron star pairs. Binary neutron-star mergers also produce short gamma-ray bursts and forge heavy elements like gold. Asymmetric supernovae and deformed, rapidly spinning neutron stars emit weaker waves too.

引力波会带走能量吗？

Do gravitational waves carry energy?

会。辐射引力波的系统会损失轨道能量，导致双天体逐渐靠近并最终并合，这是LIGO观测到的信号来源。脉冲双星PSR B1913+16的轨道衰减就与广义相对论预言的引力波能耗高度吻合。
Yes. A system radiating gravitational waves loses orbital energy, causing the binary to spiral inward and eventually merge—the source of LIGO signals. The orbital decay of the pulsar binary PSR B1913+16 matches general relativity's predicted gravitational-wave energy loss remarkably well.

为什么引力波非常难探测？

Why are gravitational waves so hard to detect?

引力波对空间的拉伸极其微小，经过地球时引起的长度变化只有质子直径的千分之一量级。因此需要数公里长的干涉臂和极敏感的激光设备才能测出。即便如此，信号也常被环境噪声淹没，需要精巧的隔振与数据处理。
Gravitational waves stretch space by an exceedingly tiny amount—only about a thousandth of a proton's diameter when passing Earth. Detecting this needs kilometer-long interferometer arms and extremely sensitive lasers. Even then, signals are buried in noise, requiring careful isolation and data analysis.
