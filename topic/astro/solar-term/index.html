二十四节气和黄道十二宫在日期上为何接近却不重合？

Why are the 24 solar terms close to but not aligned with the zodiac dates?

两者都按太阳黄经划分，但节气是每15度均匀分24段，而西方星座边界是历史上不规则划定的。
Both use solar longitude, but terms divide it evenly into 24 steps of 15°, while Western constellation borders were set irregularly.

二十四节气是根据什么定的？

On what basis are the 24 solar terms set?

节气依据太阳在黄道上的位置（地球绕日轨道）划分，反映气温、降水和物候变化，是农耕的重要参考。
Solar terms are based on the Sun's position along the ecliptic, marking temperature, rainfall and phenology changes—key guides for farming.

清明和冬至在天文上意味着什么？

What do Qingming and Dongzhi mean astronomically?

清明约在太阳到达黄经15度（春分之后的节气），冬至是太阳直射南回归线、北半球白昼最短的一天。
Qingming is near 15° ecliptic longitude after the equinox; Dongzhi is when the Sun reaches the Tropic of Capricorn, the shortest day north of the equator.

冬至一定在12月22日吗？

Is the winter solstice always on December 22?

不一定，多在12月21日或22日，偶尔在23日，取决于当年回归年长度和时差安排。
Not always—usually Dec 21 or 22, occasionally 23, depending on the tropical year length and timekeeping.
