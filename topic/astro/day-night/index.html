地球自转正在变慢吗？一天会变长吗？

Is Earth's rotation slowing down—will days get longer?

月球引起的潮汐摩擦正让地球自转极其缓慢地变慢，每世纪一天大约变长约1.7毫秒，远古时一天只有二十多小时。
Tidal friction from the Moon very slowly brakes Earth's spin—about 1.7 milliseconds longer per century; in the distant past a day was only ~20+ hours.

#日长 #潮汐摩擦

为什么夏天白天长、冬天白天短？

Why are days long in summer and short in winter?

地轴倾斜使夏季时某半球更多面向太阳、日照弧线更长，冬季则相反，于是昼夜长短随季节变化。
Axial tilt makes a hemisphere face the Sun more in summer, lengthening the daylight arc, and less in winter, changing day length by season.

#昼夜长短 #地轴

为什么会有白天和黑夜？

Why is there day and night?

因为地球不停地自转，朝向太阳的一面是白天，背向太阳的一面是黑夜。
Earth spins on its axis, so the side facing the Sun has day and the opposite side has night.

#昼夜 #自转

天上的星星数得清吗?为什么白天看不到它们?

Can anyone count all the stars? Why do they hide at daytime?

多得数不清!光银河系就有上千亿颗星星。白天它们没消失,是太阳太亮把星光盖住了,就像白天开灯看不出亮。等太阳落山、天空变黑,星星就一颗颗出来眨眼啦。
Too many to count! Our Milky Way alone holds hundreds of billions of stars. By day they never leave — the sun is just too bright, drowning starlight like lamps lost in daylight. When the sun sets and skies darken, stars come out blinking one by one.
