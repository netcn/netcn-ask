空间站为什么不用火箭一直推着也不会掉下来？

Why doesn't the space station fall even without constant thrust?

它在约400公里高度以每秒约7.7公里速度绕地做圆周运动，重力刚好提供向心力，靠“持续下落却不落地”维持轨道。
At ~400 km it orbits at ~7.7 km/s; gravity supplies the centripetal force, so it keeps 'falling around' Earth without extra thrust.

中国自己的空间站叫什么？

What is China's own space station called?

叫“天宫”，由天和核心舱及多个实验舱组成，长期有航天员驻留开展太空科学工作。
It's called Tiangong, built from the Tianhe core module and labs, with astronauts stationed long-term for space science.

在空间站上为什么人会飘起来？

Why do people float on the space station?

空间站和里面的人都在自由落向地球（绕地飞行），处于微重力状态，所以看起来像飘浮，并非没有重力。
The station and its crew are freely falling around Earth in microgravity, so they appear to float—not because there's no gravity.

国际空间站从地面看像什么，怎么知道它何时飞过？

What does the ISS look like from the ground, and how do you know when it passes?

天气好时它像一颗安静移动、不闪烁的亮星，几分钟横穿夜空；用航天追踪App或网站可查到当地过境时间。
On a clear night it looks like a steady, non-twinkling bright star gliding across in minutes; tracking apps and websites list local flyover times.
