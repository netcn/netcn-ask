强引力透镜、弱引力透镜和微引力透镜分别利用什么观测信号?各自最适合研究哪类天体物理问题?

What observational signals do strong lensing, weak lensing, and microlensing each exploit, and which astrophysical problems is each best suited to study?

强透镜产生多重像、爱因斯坦环和巨弧,可用来称量星系团质量并放大遥远的背景星系;弱透镜表现为背景星系形状的微小相干切变,是绘制暗物质大尺度结构和限制宇宙学参数的核心手段;微引力透镜则通过背景恒星的短暂增亮探测不可见致密天体,涵盖流浪行星与恒星级黑洞。三者共同把引力本身当作天然望远镜使用。
Strong lensing produces multiple images, Einstein rings, and giant arcs, letting astronomers weigh galaxy clusters and magnify faint background galaxies; weak lensing shows up as tiny coherent shears in the shapes of background galaxies and underpins maps of the cosmic web as well as tests of cosmological parameters; microlensing detects unseen compact objects, from free-floating planets to stellar-mass black holes, through the brief brightening of background stars. All three turn gravity itself into a natural telescope.

#引力透镜 #强透镜与弱透镜

引力透镜效应为什么会把同一个遥远星系变成多个像或拉成环状?

Why does gravitational lensing turn a single distant galaxy into multiple images or an elongated ring?

当前景星系或星系团的引力场弯曲时空时,来自更远光源的光线会沿多条测地线路径到达观测者,因此同一目标可以呈现为两到四个分离的像,几何条件合适时还会连成一个爱因斯坦环。像的分离角由爱因斯坦半径决定,它随透镜质量、以及源-透镜-观测者三者的距离组合而变化,所以测量这个角度可以直接估计透镜系统的质量。强透镜还能放大背景天体的亮度和尺度,让本来看不见的高红移星系变得可研究。
When the gravitational field of a foreground galaxy or cluster curves spacetime, light from a more distant source reaches the observer along several geodesic paths at once, so one target can appear as two to four separate images; under favorable geometry they merge into an Einstein ring. The image separation is set by the Einstein radius, which depends on the lens mass and the distances among source, lens, and observer, so measuring that angle yields a direct estimate of the lens system's mass. Strong lensing also magnifies the brightness and apparent size of background objects, bringing otherwise invisible high-redshift galaxies into reach.

#引力透镜 #爱因斯坦环

同一个类星体为什么会在天上出现两个?爱因斯坦环又是怎么来的?

Why can one quasar appear twice in the sky — and where do Einstein rings come from?

广义相对论允许大质量天体弯折光线,当前景星系恰好横在类星体与地球之间且近乎对准,星光沿两侧对称路径同时到达——我们看到两个同源像,光谱一致,只是时间延迟数天到数百天。若对准完美,像被拉成环绕前景天体的光弧,即爱因斯坦环。1979年沃尔什等人发现的孪生类星体Q0957+561是首个确认案例,透镜从此成为称重工具:像间距与环半径直接量出前景天体总质量(含不发光的暗物质),时间延迟还能倒推宇宙膨胀速率。
General relativity lets massive bodies bend light. When a foreground galaxy sits almost precisely between quasar and Earth, rays travel two symmetric paths around it and arrive together — two images of one source, spectra identical but delayed by days to hundreds of days. Near-perfect alignment stretches the image into a luminous ring around the lens: an Einstein ring. The 1979 double quasar Q0957+561 was the first confirmed case; lensing since became a weighing tool, because image separation and ring radii measure total lens mass including dark matter, and time delays even probe the universe's expansion rate.
