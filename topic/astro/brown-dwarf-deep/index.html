怎么区分一颗褐矮星和一颗巨行星？

How do you tell a brown dwarf from a giant planet?

质量在约13倍木星质量（氘燃烧下限）以上算褐矮星、以下算行星；但实际难以直接测质量，常靠形成方式（独立形成还是绕恒星）辅助判断。
Above about 13 Jupiter masses (the deuterium-burning limit) it is a brown dwarf, below it a planet; but mass is hard to measure directly, so formation history (isolated vs. orbiting a star) helps decide.

什么是Y型褐矮星？

What is a Y-type brown dwarf?

Y型是褐矮星中最冷的一类，表面温度可低至约300开尔文，接近室温，颜色偏红，是最接近行星质量的亚恒星天体。
Y-type is the coldest class of brown dwarf, with surface temperatures down to about 300 K—near room temperature—and reddish color, the most planet-like substellar objects.

褐矮星的大气里有什么云？

What clouds exist in brown-dwarf atmospheres?

由于温度介于行星与恒星之间，褐矮星大气可凝结出硅酸盐尘云、乃至铁云和刚玉云，使其亮度与颜色随自转而变。
Because their temperatures sit between planets and stars, brown-dwarf atmospheres can condense silicate dust clouds and even iron or corundum clouds, making their brightness and color change as they rotate.
