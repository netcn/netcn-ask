什么是赫罗图（H-R图）？

What is the Hertzsprung-Russell diagram?

赫罗图以恒星的光谱类型（对应温度）为横轴、光度为纵轴绘制，多数恒星落在主序带上。它能直观地反映恒星的演化阶段和物理性质。
The Hertzsprung-Russell diagram plots stars by spectral type (temperature) against luminosity, with most lying along the main sequence. It reveals a star's evolutionary stage and physical nature at a glance.

天文学家如何称量恒星的质量？

How do astronomers weigh a star?

单独一颗恒星的质量无法直接测量，但双星系统提供了途径。观测两颗星的轨道周期和半长轴，代入牛顿引力与开普勒第三定律，可以求出系统的总质量。如果还能测出每颗星相对质心的轨道半径，就能分别求出各自的质量。双星轨道测量因此是唯一能直接测定恒星质量的可靠途径。
The mass of an isolated star cannot be measured directly, but binary systems provide a way. Observing the orbital period and semimajor axis of two stars and applying Newtonian gravity with Kepler's third law yields the system's total mass. If each star's orbit around the barycenter is also measured, the individual masses can be separated. Binary orbit analysis is thus essentially the only reliable direct route to stellar masses.

什么是蓝离散星？它们为什么看起来比星团其他恒星年轻？

What are blue stragglers, and why do they look younger than other cluster stars?

蓝离散星出现在球状星团等年老星团中，却比同族恒星更蓝更亮，看似违反了“星团内所有恒星同龄”的预期。主流解释是，在星团拥挤的内部，它们通过与伴星并合或吸积物质而重新获得质量。质量增加使氢燃料供应和表面温度随之升高。因此它们在赫罗图上显得比同龄恒星更年轻。
Blue stragglers appear in old clusters such as globulars yet are bluer and brighter than their siblings, seemingly violating the expectation that all cluster stars formed together. The leading explanation is that in the crowded cluster interior they gain mass by merging with or accreting from companions. The added mass boosts their hydrogen supply and surface temperature. As a result they appear younger than their true age on the H-R diagram.

恒星磁场的强度是如何测出来的？

How is the strength of a star's magnetic field measured?

恒星磁场会使大气中的原子能级发生分裂，谱线由此出现多条精细分量，且各分量的偏振状态不同，这一效应称为泽曼效应。通过测量谱线的分裂间距和偏振，可以推算出磁场强度。除太阳外，天文学家已在许多恒星上测到比太阳强得多的磁场。磁星表面磁场甚至可达 10 的 15 次方高斯量级。
A stellar magnetic field splits the atomic energy levels in the star's atmosphere, producing multiple fine line components with distinct polarization, an effect called the Zeeman effect. Measuring the splitting and polarization of spectral lines reveals the field strength. Besides the Sun, astronomers have measured far stronger fields on many stars. Magnetar surfaces can reach up to 10^15 gauss.

为什么说球状星团是测定银河系年龄的“化石”？

Why are globular clusters regarded as fossils for dating the Milky Way?

球状星团几乎是同一时间形成的恒星集合，此后没有新一代恒星补充。因此，成员星在赫罗图上构成的“主序拐点”直接对应星团从形成至今的演化年龄。由恒星演化模型反推，最老的球状星团年龄约在 110 亿到 130 亿年之间。这为银河系乃至宇宙的年龄设定了下限，并与宇宙学给出的宇宙年龄相协调。
Globular clusters formed nearly all at once, with no subsequent generations of stars. As a result, the turn-off point of their member stars on the H-R diagram directly yields the cluster's evolutionary age. Stellar evolution models date the oldest clusters to roughly 11 to 13 billion years. This sets a lower limit on the age of the Milky Way and of the universe, consistent with cosmological age estimates.

造父变星如何被用来测量遥远星系的距离？

How are Cepheid variables used to measure distances to remote galaxies?

造父变星的光变周期与其真实光度之间存在严格的正比关系，称为周光关系。天文学家只要测出它的光变周期，就能推知其绝对光度。再把绝对光度与观测到的视亮度比较，利用平方反比定律即可算出距离。哈勃太空望远镜对造父变星的精确测量是建立星系距离尺度、测定哈勃常数的关键步骤，精度可达百分之几。
Cepheid variables obey a strict period-luminosity relation. By measuring a Cepheid's pulsation period, astronomers can infer its true luminosity. Comparing that absolute luminosity with the observed apparent brightness and applying the inverse-square law then yields the distance. Hubble Space Telescope measurements of Cepheids are a cornerstone of the extragalactic distance scale and the Hubble constant, with a precision of a few percent.

为什么 Ia 型超新星被称为“标准烛光”？

Why are Type Ia supernovae called standard candles?

Ia 型超新星是白矮星从伴星吸积物质、逼近钱德拉塞卡极限后引发的热核爆炸。由于爆炸发生在几乎相同的临界质量处，其峰值光度相当一致，因此被称为“标准烛光”。实际观测中还利用峰值光度与光变曲线衰减速度的关系进行校正。经过校正后距离测量精度大幅提高，可据此测出超新星所在遥远星系的距离。
A Type Ia supernova is the thermonuclear explosion of a white dwarf after it accretes matter from a companion and approaches the Chandrasekhar limit. Because the explosion happens at nearly the same critical mass, peak luminosities are quite uniform, earning it the name standard candle. In practice, the relation between peak luminosity and the light curve's decline rate provides further calibration. With that calibration, distance measurements become far more precise, allowing astronomers to gauge the distance to the supernova's host galaxy.

质子-质子链与 CNO 循环在恒星产能上有何区别？

How do the proton–proton chain and the CNO cycle differ in powering stars?

两者都是把氢聚变成氦的核反应链。质子-质子链在像太阳这样的中低质量、较冷恒星中占主导；而 CNO 循环以碳、氮、氧为催化剂，对温度极其敏感，在更热、质量更大的恒星中成为主要产能方式。
Both are nuclear chains fusing hydrogen into helium. The proton–proton chain dominates in cooler, lower-mass stars like the Sun, while the CNO cycle uses carbon, nitrogen, and oxygen as catalysts and is extremely temperature-sensitive, becoming the main energy source in hotter, more massive stars.

蟹状星云为什么被认为是超新星遗迹研究的样板？

Why is the Crab Nebula considered the benchmark supernova remnant?

蟹状星云对应 1054 年爆发的超新星，中国宋代天文记录准确记载了这次“客星”，是少数有明确历史记载的超新星之一。它由恒星爆发抛出的物质构成，目前仍以每秒约 1500 公里的速度膨胀。膨胀激波与周围星际介质相互作用，同时中心的中子星（脉冲星）持续向星云注入能量。这使得蟹状星云在几乎所有波段都持续辐射，成为超新星遗迹研究的样板。
The Crab Nebula is the remnant of the supernova seen in 1054, accurately recorded as a guest star in Song-dynasty Chinese annals, making it one of the few supernovae with definite historical records. It consists of material ejected in the blast, still expanding at about 1,500 km/s. The expanding shock interacts with the surrounding interstellar medium while the central neutron star (pulsar) keeps injecting energy into the nebula. Because it radiates across nearly every wavelength band, it is the benchmark supernova remnant.

褐矮星是什么，为何说它是“失败了的恒星”？

What are brown dwarfs, and why are they called “failed stars”?

褐矮星质量介于行星与恒星之间（约13至80倍木星质量），核心温度与压力不足以持续稳定的氢聚变，因此无法像真正的恒星那样长期发光。天文学家常用“锂检验”来识别它们：质量足够低的褐矮星在其内部保留了原本的锂。
Brown dwarfs have masses between planets and stars (about 13–80 times Jupiter's) and their cores are too cool and low-pressure to sustain stable hydrogen fusion, so they cannot shine like true stars. Astronomers often use the “lithium test”: brown dwarfs below a certain mass retain their original lithium.

恒星的金属丰度如何影响星族分类？

How does stellar metallicity relate to population classification?

天文学家按恒星的重元素含量将其分为星族I（较年轻、金属丰度较高）和星族II（较年老、金属丰度较低）。金属丰度反映了恒星形成时所在星系的化学演化阶段。
Astronomers split stars into Population I (younger, metal-rich) and Population II (older, metal-poor) by their heavy-element content. Metallicity records the chemical evolution stage of the galaxy in which a star formed.

主序星为什么能在几十亿年里既不塌缩也不炸开？

Why can a main-sequence star remain neither collapsing nor exploding for billions of years?

主序星处于流体静力学平衡：核心氢聚变产生的向外压力与引力向内的拉拽恰好抵消。这种平衡还能自我调节——一旦引力占了上风，核心被压缩、温度升高，聚变加快，压力随即回升把恒星重新撑开。只要核心还有氢可烧，这种负反馈就让它保持稳定。
A main-sequence star sits in hydrostatic equilibrium: the outward pressure from hydrogen fusion in its core exactly balances the inward pull of gravity. The balance is self-regulating — if gravity gains the upper hand, the core compresses and heats up, fusion speeds up, and the pressure rebounds to puff the star back out. As long as hydrogen remains in the core, that negative feedback keeps the star steady.

宇宙中的金、银等重金属是怎么形成的？

How do heavy metals like gold and silver form in the universe?

大爆炸只造出氢、氦和微量锂，恒星聚变最多做到铁为止，因为比铁更重的元素聚变反而要吸收能量。铁以上的元素要靠中子俘获来造，其中快速俘获的r过程主要发生在超新星爆发，以及中子星并合这样极端的天文事件里。2017年LIGO和室女座探测器观测到的双中子星并合事件，就伴随产生了大量金、银等元素。
The Big Bang produced only hydrogen, helium, and a trace of lithium, and stellar fusion stops at iron, since fusing anything heavier costs energy instead of releasing it. Elements above iron form by neutron capture, and the rapid r-process takes place mainly in supernovae and extreme events such as neutron-star mergers. The 2017 binary neutron-star merger detected by LIGO and Virgo was observed to forge large amounts of gold and silver.

钱德拉塞卡极限对白矮星的质量上限意味着什么？

What does the Chandrasekhar limit imply about the mass upper bound of white dwarfs?

钱德拉塞卡极限约为 1.4 倍太阳质量，是电子简并压能够支撑白矮星、使其不被自身引力坍缩的最大质量。超过这一极限，电子简并压失效，白矮星会继续坍缩成中子星或触发 Ia 型超新星。
The Chandrasekhar limit is about 1.4 solar masses, the maximum mass at which electron degeneracy pressure can support a white dwarf against its own gravity. Above it the degeneracy pressure fails and the white dwarf collapses into a neutron star or triggers a Type Ia supernova.

什么是恒星的主序阶段？

What is the main-sequence phase of a star?

主序阶段是恒星一生中最长、最稳定的时期，核心通过氢聚变为氦来产能。恒星的质量决定了它在主序上停留的时间长短和最终的归宿。
The main sequence is the longest and most stable phase of a star's life, when its core fuses hydrogen into helium for energy. A star's mass determines how long it stays there and how it will end.

恒星的质量与寿命之间有什么关系？

How are a star's mass and its lifetime related?

质量越大的恒星核心温度和压力越高，核聚变进行得越快，反而寿命越短。例如几十倍太阳质量的恒星只能存在几百万年，而小质量红矮星可燃烧上万亿年。
More massive stars have hotter, denser cores and burn their fuel much faster, so they live shorter lives. A star dozens of times the Sun's mass may last only millions of years, while a low-mass red dwarf can shine for trillions.

脉冲星为什么能转得那么快？

Why can pulsars spin so fast?

脉冲星是旋转的中子星。恒星核心坍缩时，半径从数千公里（接近地球大小）缩到约十公里，转动惯量急剧变小，按角动量守恒定律自转就猛烈加快，可以高达每秒数百圈。它磁极发出的射电束像灯塔一样扫过地球，每次扫过就被我们记录成一个脉冲，周期精确得像原子钟。
Pulsars are spinning neutron stars. When a stellar core collapses, its radius shrinks from a few thousand kilometres (roughly the size of Earth) to about ten, the moment of inertia plummets, and by conservation of angular momentum the spin speeds up dramatically, reaching hundreds of revolutions per second. Radio beams from the magnetic poles sweep across Earth like a lighthouse, registering as a pulse on each pass with a timing as steady as an atomic clock.

大质量恒星的最终归宿是什么？

What is the final fate of a massive star?

质量大于约8倍太阳的恒星在耗尽核燃料后会发生核心坍缩型超新星爆发，留下中子星或黑洞。这一过程还把大量重元素抛洒到星际空间。
Stars more than about eight times the Sun's mass end by exhausting their fuel and undergoing a core-collapse supernova, leaving behind a neutron star or black hole. The explosion also scatters heavy elements into space.

红巨星明明温度更高，为什么看起来反而更红？

A red giant has a hotter core, so why does it look redder?

红巨星是老年恒星，核心虽热，但外层膨胀到极大，表面温度反而降到只有几千摄氏度。恒星表面越冷，黑体辐射的峰值波长越长，偏向光谱的红端，所以它呈现橙红色。按维恩位移定律，颜色其实就是表面温度的直观反映。
A red giant is an old star whose core is hot but whose envelope has expanded enormously, so its surface temperature actually drops to a few thousand degrees. The cooler a star's surface, the longer the peak wavelength of its blackbody radiation, shifting toward the red end of the spectrum. By Wien's law, colour is really a direct readout of surface temperature.

红巨星是什么，太阳以后也会变成它吗？

What is a red giant, and will the Sun become one?

红巨星是恒星核心氢耗尽后外层膨胀、表面变冷变红的阶段，体积可膨胀到原来的上百倍。大约 50 亿年后，太阳核心氢烧完也会膨胀成红巨星，可能吞没水星和金星。
A red giant is the phase after a star exhausts core hydrogen, when its outer layers expand and the surface cools and reddens, swelling to over a hundred times its former size. In roughly 5 billion years the Sun will do the same and may engulf Mercury and Venus.

约50亿年后太阳将如何演化？

How will the Sun evolve about 5 billion years from now?

当太阳核心的氢耗尽后，它会膨胀成一颗红巨星，外层大幅扩张并可能吞没水星、金星乃至地球轨道。最终外层物质会以行星状星云形式抛散，留下一颗地球大小的白矮星。
When the Sun exhausts the hydrogen in its core, it will swell into a red giant, expanding so far that it may engulf Mercury, Venus, and possibly Earth's orbit. Its outer layers will later be shed as a planetary nebula, leaving an Earth-sized white dwarf.

什么是暗星云？马头星云为什么是黑色的？

What is a dark nebula, and why is the Horsehead Nebula black?

暗星云是浓密冰冷的星际气体尘埃云，其中的尘埃会吸收和散射背后恒星及星云的光，因此在明亮的背景下呈现剪影般的黑暗。马头星云正是这样一块位于猎户座的致密尘埃云，它挡住了背后红色发射星云的光，形成马头形状。
A dark nebula is a dense, cold cloud of interstellar gas and dust whose dust absorbs and scatters light from stars and nebulae behind it, appearing as a dark silhouette against a bright background. The Horsehead is exactly such a dense dust cloud in Orion, blocking the light of the red emission nebula behind it to form its shape.

红矮星会发生强烈的耀斑，这对围绕它们的行星意味着什么？

Red dwarfs produce powerful flares; what does that mean for planets around them?

红矮星表面磁场活动强烈，会频繁爆发 X 射线和紫外耀斑，能量比太阳耀斑强得多。耀斑会冲刷行星大气，甚至可能剥离行星的大气层；但另一方面，有些研究认为耀斑的紫外辐射反而可能促进某些前生物化学反应的进行，影响是复杂且双重的。
Red dwarfs have vigorous magnetic activity and erupt in X-ray and ultraviolet flares far more energetic than solar flares. These flares bombard planetary atmospheres and can even strip them away; on the other hand, some studies suggest flare ultraviolet radiation could drive certain prebiotic reactions, so the net effect is complex and double-edged.

恒星的个头能差多少？

How much can the sizes of stars differ?

恒星个头差距惊人。像史蒂文森2-18这样的红超巨星，半径约为太阳的2000倍，放到太阳的位置上会把土星轨道都吞进去；而中子星只有一座城市那么大，直径约二十公里。太阳在两者之间属于很普通的恒星。
The spread in stellar size is astonishing. A red hypergiant like Stephenson 2-18 is roughly 2,000 times the Sun's radius, so placed where the Sun is it would engulf Saturn's orbit, while a neutron star spans only about twenty kilometres, the size of a city. The Sun sits between these extremes as a very ordinary star.

赫罗图（H-R 图）在恒星研究中起什么作用？

What role does the Hertzsprung–Russell (H–R) diagram play in stellar astronomy?

赫罗图以恒星的光谱类型（或温度）为横轴、光度（或绝对星等）为纵轴绘制恒星。赫茨普龙与罗素独立发现，大多数恒星落在一条“主序”带上，由此揭示了恒星的质量、温度与光度之间的系统关系，是现代恒星天文学的基石。
The H–R diagram plots stars by spectral type (or temperature) against luminosity (or absolute magnitude). Hertzsprung and Russell independently found that most stars lie on a “main sequence,” revealing systematic links among a star's mass, temperature, and luminosity—a cornerstone of stellar astronomy.

大约50亿年后太阳变成红巨星，地球会怎样？

What will happen to Earth when the Sun becomes a red giant in about five billion years?

太阳膨胀时，水星和金星会被吞没，地球即使因为太阳质量流失而把轨道向外推一些，也很可能被吞掉或被烤成焦炭。就算侥幸幸存，地表温度也会高得让海洋全部蒸发，生命早已无法存续。
As the Sun swells, Mercury and Venus will be swallowed, and even if Earth's orbit drifts outward a little because the Sun loses mass, our planet will most likely be engulfed too or scorched to a cinder. Even if it survived, the surface would be far too hot, with every ocean boiled away, so life would be long gone.

为什么有些恒星会周期性地变暗再变亮？

Why do some stars periodically dim and brighten?

变暗变亮主要有两类原因：一类是几何原因，比如食双星（如大陵五）中两颗星互相绕转，一颗星周期性遮住另一颗；另一类是恒星自身的物理变化，比如米拉这类长周期变星，其外层会周期性膨胀收缩，改变温度和光度。通过观测光变曲线可以区分这两种机制。
There are two main causes. Geometric ones occur in eclipsing binaries such as Algol, where two stars orbit each other and one periodically covers the other; physical ones occur in pulsating stars like Mira, whose outer layers rhythmically expand and contract, changing temperature and luminosity. Light curves allow the two mechanisms to be distinguished.

褐矮星为什么不能算真正的恒星？

Why is a brown dwarf not considered a true star?

恒星的定义是核心持续进行氢聚变，而点燃并维持氢聚变至少需要约0.08倍太阳质量。质量不足的褐矮星达不到这个门槛，只能在形成初期短暂烧掉一点氘，随后靠收缩慢慢散热，越变越冷。它常常被称为失败的恒星。
A star is defined by sustaining hydrogen fusion in its core, which needs at least about 0.08 times the Sun's mass. A brown dwarf below that threshold never ignites proper hydrogen fusion; it briefly burns a little deuterium when young and then simply contracts and cools, fading steadily. That is why it is often called a failed star.

行星状星云与行星有什么关系？

What relationship do planetary nebulae have with planets?

行星状星云与行星毫无关系，只是早期望远镜中其圆润形态类似行星而得名。它们是中低质量恒星（如太阳）在生命末期抛出的发光气体壳层，中心残留的白矮星发出的紫外辐射使其发光。
Planetary nebulae have nothing to do with planets; they were named for their round, planet-like appearance in early telescopes. They are glowing shells of gas ejected by low- to intermediate-mass stars like the Sun at the end of life, lit up by ultraviolet light from the central white dwarf.

发射星云和反射星云有什么不同？

What is the difference between an emission nebula and a reflection nebula?

发射星云内部的炽热恒星电离周围的气体，气体在复合时发出特征颜色的光，比如猎户座大星云呈现红色（氢的发射线）。反射星云则本身不发光，只是尘埃把附近恒星的光散射出来，通常呈蓝色，因为蓝光更容易被尘埃散射。
An emission nebula's hot interior stars ionize surrounding gas, which glows in characteristic colors as it recombines; for example the Orion Nebula appears red from hydrogen's emission lines. A reflection nebula does not shine on its own but simply scatters light from a nearby star, usually appearing blue because blue light scatters more efficiently off the dust.

白矮星是什么？

What is a white dwarf?

白矮星是中小质量恒星演化到末期的残骸，靠电子简并压支撑，不再进行核聚变。它体积小、密度极高，一茶匙白矮星物质重达数吨。
A white dwarf is the remnant of a low- to medium-mass star, supported by electron degeneracy pressure and no longer fusing atoms. It is tiny and extremely dense; a teaspoon of its material weighs several tons.

恒星之间的“真空”里究竟有什么？

What actually fills the space between stars?

星际空间并非真空，而是充满极其稀薄的气体和尘埃，总称星际介质。气体以氢为主，尘埃是微米级的碳硅酸盐颗粒；星际介质约占银河系可见质量的百分之几，密度虽低，却决定了恒星如何形成和光线如何传播。
Interstellar space is not a vacuum; it is filled with extremely tenuous gas and dust collectively called the interstellar medium. The gas is mostly hydrogen, and the dust consists of micrometer-scale carbon and silicate grains; though sparse, this medium makes up a few percent of the Milky Way's visible mass and shapes both star formation and how light travels.

疏散星团和球状星团有什么主要区别？

What are the main differences between open clusters and globular clusters?

疏散星团由几百到几千颗年轻恒星组成，分布松散，多在银河系银盘里，如昴星团；球状星团则由几十万到上百万颗年老恒星挤成一个球体，分布在银晕中。两者的年龄和化学成分也不同，球状星团通常古老且金属含量低。
Open clusters hold a few hundred to a few thousand young stars loosely spread through the galactic disk, such as the Pleiades; globular clusters pack hundreds of thousands to millions of old stars into a ball in the halo. Their ages and compositions also differ: globulars are typically ancient and metal-poor.

恒星质量越大，寿命为什么反而越短？

Why do more massive stars have shorter lifetimes?

恒星越大，核心的温度和压强越高，氢聚变燃烧得越猛烈。恒星的光度大约随质量的3.5次方增长，所以一颗质量为太阳10倍的恒星，燃料消耗要快上千倍，寿命只有几千万年。太阳这种中等恒星反而能稳定燃烧约100亿年。
The more massive a star, the hotter and denser its core and the faster hydrogen fuses there. A star's luminosity grows roughly as the 3.5th power of its mass, so a star ten times the Sun's mass burns fuel a thousand times faster and lives only tens of millions of years. A middleweight star like the Sun can instead burn steadily for about ten billion years.

白矮星为什么那么重？一茶匙能有多重？

Why is a white dwarf so heavy, and how heavy is a teaspoon of it?

白矮星是太阳这类恒星死后的残骸，质量接近太阳却被压缩到地球大小，密度高达每立方厘米约100万克，一茶匙就有好几吨重。它靠电子的简并压力支撑，电子被挤到不能再靠近，才抵挡住引力继续坍缩。
A white dwarf is the leftover core of a star like the Sun, packing about a solar mass into an Earth-sized object, so its density reaches roughly one million grams per cubic centimetre and a teaspoonful weighs several tonnes. It is held up by electron degeneracy pressure: electrons squeezed so tightly they resist any closer packing, which stops gravity from crushing the star further.

猎户座大星云为什么是研究恒星形成的“天然实验室”？

Why is the Orion Nebula a natural laboratory for studying star formation?

猎户座大星云距离我们约 1350 光年，是离地球最近的大质量恒星形成区，其中不仅有发光的电离氢区，还包裹着正在坍缩的分子云和数百颗年轻的恒星。望远镜能直接分辨出原恒星、环绕的尘埃盘和被电离气体包围的年轻恒星，让天文学家得以观察恒星从诞生到成熟的整个过程。
About 1,350 light-years away, the Orion Nebula is the nearest large star-forming region. It contains a glowing ionized-hydrogen region plus the molecular cloud collapsing within it and hundreds of young stars. Telescopes can resolve protostars, surrounding dusty disks and young stars embedded in ionized gas, letting astronomers watch star formation from birth to maturity.

一颗恒星最终变成白矮星、中子星还是黑洞，由什么决定？

What decides whether a star ends up as a white dwarf, a neutron star, or a black hole?

起决定作用的是它诞生时的质量。质量约在8倍太阳质量以下的恒星，死亡后留下白矮星；约8到20多倍太阳质量的恒星会以超新星收场，核心坍缩成中子星；更大的恒星核心则直接坍缩成黑洞。太阳属于白矮星那一类。
The deciding factor is the star's mass at birth. Stars below roughly eight solar masses end their lives as white dwarfs; stars of about eight to twenty-plus solar masses blow up as supernovae and their cores collapse into neutron stars; still more massive cores collapse straight into black holes. Our Sun belongs to the white-dwarf category.

恒星一生的能量主要来自哪种核反应？

What nuclear reaction mainly powers a star throughout its life?

像太阳这样的主序星靠核心的质子—质子链（pp 链）把氢聚变成氦并释放能量。质量更大的恒星则主要通过碳氮氧（CNO）循环聚变氢；当核心氢耗尽，恒星会膨胀成红巨星，进入后续演化阶段。
Sun-like main-sequence stars are powered by the proton–proton (pp) chain in their cores, fusing hydrogen into helium and releasing energy. More massive stars mainly use the carbon–nitrogen–oxygen (CNO) cycle, and when core hydrogen is exhausted the star swells into a red giant and enters later evolutionary stages.

超新星是什么？

What is a supernova?

超新星是恒星生命末期发生的剧烈爆炸，瞬间的亮度可超过整个星系。它分为Ia型和核心坍缩型等，是宇宙中重元素的重要来源。
A supernova is a violent explosion at the end of a star's life, briefly outshining an entire galaxy. Types include Ia and core-collapse, and supernovae are a major source of the universe's heavy elements.

星星为什么一闪一闪的？

Why do stars twinkle?

星星本身不闪，是它们穿过地球大气层时，流动的空气把光线弯来弯去，我们看上去就一闪一闪了。在太空里的望远镜看星星，它们是不闪的。
Stars don't actually flicker; as their light passes through Earth's atmosphere, moving air bends it this way and that, so to us they appear to twinkle. Seen from a telescope in space, stars do not twinkle.
