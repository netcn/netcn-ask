我们能看到宇宙最初几分钟之后的样子吗？

Can we see the universe just after its first minutes?

我们无法看到光子退耦之前“最初几分钟”本身，因为那时的宇宙对光不透明；可观测的“快照”是约38万年后形成的宇宙微波背景和原初核合成留下的轻元素丰度。通过分析微波背景的涨落以及氢、氦、锂的比例，天文学家能反推出大爆炸后几分钟内宇宙的温度、密度与膨胀速率。也就是说，我们看到的不是那一刻的光，而是它留下的物理指纹。
We cannot directly see the first few minutes themselves, because before photon decoupling (about 380,000 years after the Big Bang) the cosmos was opaque to light. The observable fingerprints of that era are the cosmic microwave background and the light-element abundances left by Big Bang nucleosynthesis. By analyzing CMB fluctuations and the ratios of hydrogen, helium, and lithium, astronomers reconstruct the temperature, density, and expansion rate of the universe minutes after its beginning—not its light, but its physical signature.

宇宙微波背景温度功率谱上的多个声学峰，是怎样从早期宇宙的物理过程中形成的？

How were the multiple acoustic peaks in the temperature power spectrum of the cosmic microwave background forged by processes in the early universe?

复合之前，宇宙是炽热的光子—重子等离子体，引力和光子压强的拉锯使密度扰动像声波一样振荡。光子退耦那一刻，不同尺度的模式停在各自相位上：被压缩到极值的尺度留下最热的第一个峰，其后依次出现冷暖相间的峰谷，特征角尺度约一度。第一峰位置直接约束空间曲率，各级峰高之比给出重子与物质密度，是精确宇宙学的支柱。
Before recombination the universe was a hot photon-baryon plasma, and the tug-of-war between gravity and radiation pressure made density perturbations oscillate like sound waves. At the moment photons decoupled, modes of different scales froze at different phases: the scale caught at maximum compression left the hottest first peak, followed by alternating peaks and troughs at a characteristic angular scale near one degree. The first peak's position directly measures spatial curvature, and the ratios of peak heights fix the baryon and total matter densities - pillars of precision cosmology.

为什么说宇宙微波背景辐射是大爆炸的“余晖”？它的温度为何只剩下约2.7 K？

Why is the cosmic microwave background called the 'afterglow of the Big Bang', and why has its temperature faded to only about 2.7 K?

宇宙早期是高温等离子体，光子被自由电子反复散射，无法自由传播。约在大爆炸后38万年，温度降至3000 K上下，质子与电子复合成中性原子，光子解耦后一路传播至今。宇宙膨胀把这些光子的波长拉伸了约1100倍，黑体谱温度随之降为如今的约2.725 K，成为宇宙起源于炽热致密状态的最直接证据。
In the early Universe a hot plasma filled space, and photons were scattered over and over by free electrons, unable to travel freely. About 380,000 years after the Big Bang the temperature fell to roughly 3000 K, protons and electrons recombined into neutral atoms, and the photons decoupled and have streamed freely ever since. Cosmic expansion stretched their wavelengths by a factor of about 1100, cooling their blackbody spectrum to about 2.725 K today - the most direct evidence that the Universe began in a hot, dense state.

宇宙微波背景辐射是什么？它为什么重要？

What is the cosmic microwave background, and why is it important?

它是宇宙约 38 万岁时释放的第一缕光，随膨胀冷却到约 -270℃ 的微波背景，其细微温度涨落记录了宇宙最初的密度结构。
It is the first light released when the universe was about 380,000 years old, now cooled by expansion to microwave background at about -270°C; its tiny temperature fluctuations record the universe's earliest density structure.
