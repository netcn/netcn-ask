为什么星团能帮我们估算恒星年龄？

Why do clusters help estimate stellar ages?

同一星团的恒星几乎同龄但质量不同，质量大的演化更快；看哪些质量的星已离开主序就能反推年龄。
Cluster stars share an age but differ in mass; since massive ones evolve faster, seeing which masses left the main sequence reveals the age.

疏散星团和球状星团有什么不同？

How do open and globular clusters differ?

差别在数目、年龄和位置。疏散星团只有几百颗星，束缚松散、形状不规则，是近期由星际云坍缩而成，重元素偏多，多在银盘与旋臂上；球状星团有几十万到上百万颗，被自身引力压成致密球形，金属丰度低，年龄接近银河系本身，在银晕里。易混的是疏散星团终会瓦解：恒星逐个逃逸，加上与分子云擦肩的潮汐扰动，几亿年就散干净，所以太阳的出生星团早已不见；球状星团势阱深才撑到现在。
The differences lie in membership, age and location. An open cluster holds only a few hundred stars, loosely bound and irregular in shape; it condensed from interstellar gas relatively recently, is comparatively rich in heavy elements, and mostly sits in the galactic disk and spiral arms. A globular cluster may contain hundreds of thousands to over a million stars, compressed by its own gravity into a dense sphere, metal-poor and nearly as old as the Galaxy itself, orbiting in the halo. What gets mixed up is that open clusters are doomed to disperse: stars leak away one by one and tidal shocks from passing molecular clouds strip the rest, so a few hundred million years is enough to scatter them, which is why the Sun's birth cluster is long gone; globulars survive because their deeper potential wells hold on.

天上成片的一群星叫什么？

What do we call a group of stars together in the sky?

叫星团，是同一时期诞生、彼此靠引力松散聚集的一群恒星，如昴星团（七姊妹星团）。
They're star clusters—groups of stars born together and loosely bound by gravity, like the Pleiades.
