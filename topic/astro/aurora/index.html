为什么中低纬度偶尔也能看到极光？

Why can auroras sometimes be seen at mid-low latitudes?

强烈的太阳风暴（地磁暴）会压缩并扰动地球磁层，使极光带向低纬度扩张，这时南方地区也可能看到红绿色的极光。
Strong solar storms (geomagnetic storms) disturb Earth's magnetosphere and push the auroral oval toward lower latitudes, letting mid-latitude regions see red-green auroras.

#地磁暴 #低纬极光

为什么极光多出现在地球两极附近？

Why do auroras mainly appear near the Earth's poles?

地球磁场像漏斗把太阳粒子导向南北磁极，所以极光带集中在高纬度地区，如北极圈和南极圈。
Earth's magnetic field channels solar particles toward the magnetic poles, so auroral belts sit at high latitudes like the Arctic and Antarctic circles.

#磁极 #高纬度

除了地球，还有哪些行星有极光？

Besides Earth, which planets have auroras?

木星、土星、天王星、海王星等都有极光，它们源自各自磁场与太阳风或卫星火山粒子的相互作用。
Jupiter, Saturn, Uranus, and Neptune also have auroras, driven by their magnetic fields interacting with solar wind or moon-related particles.

#行星极光 #磁场

北极光（极光）是怎么形成的？

How do the northern lights (aurora) form?

太阳抛出的带电粒子被地球磁场引向两极，撞入高层大气使气体发光，就形成了五彩的极光。
Charged particles from the Sun are funneled by Earth's magnetic field to the poles, exciting atmospheric gases to glow as auroras.
