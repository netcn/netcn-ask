为什么星座的形状会随时间缓慢改变？

Why do constellation shapes slowly change over time?

恒星各自以不同速度和方向运动（自行），几万年累积下来亮星的相对位置会变化，星座形状也就慢慢变形。
Stars move at different speeds and directions (proper motion); over tens of thousands of years their relative positions shift, slowly reshaping constellations.

夜空中为什么会有那么多“星座”？

Why are there so many 'constellations' in the night sky?

星座是古人把天空中相邻亮星连成的图案，用来辨认方向和季节，并不是星星真的挤在一起。
Constellations are patterns people drew by connecting nearby bright stars to navigate and track seasons; the stars are not actually close together.
