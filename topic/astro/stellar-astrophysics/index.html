氦闪是什么现象，为什么只发生在低质量恒星演化晚期？

What is the helium flash, and why does it occur only in the late evolution of low-mass stars?

当低质量恒星（约0.8至2.0倍太阳质量）耗尽核心氢后，简并电子气体支撑的氦核逐渐升温，直到氦聚变的点火温度被突破。由于简并物质不靠热压支撑，能量释放不会膨胀降温，导致失控式的热核爆发，即氦闪。它只在电子简并显著的核心出现，因此大质量恒星因核心温和膨胀而避免这一现象。
When a low-mass star, roughly one to two solar masses, exhausts core hydrogen, its helium core supported by degenerate electrons heats until it reaches the ignition temperature for helium fusion. Because degenerate matter does not expand and cool in response to heating, the energy release runs away in a violent thermonuclear event called the helium flash. This only happens where electron degeneracy is significant, so more massive stars avoid it by gently expanding their cores instead.

磁星为何拥有宇宙中最强的磁场，这种磁场是如何产生的？

Why do magnetars possess the strongest magnetic fields in the universe, and how do they form?

磁星是一类特殊的中子星，表面磁场可达10的15次方高斯，远超普通脉冲星。主流理论认为，大质量恒星核心坍缩时剧烈的对流与快速自转把发电机机制放大，把磁场冻结并增强到极端强度。其磁能偶尔的重新连接会引发星震与伽马射线暴，即软伽马重复暴。
Magnetars are a special class of neutron stars with surface fields up to 10^15 gauss, far stronger than ordinary pulsars. The leading theory holds that during core collapse of a massive star, vigorous convection combined with rapid rotation drives a dynamo that amplifies and freezes in an enormous magnetic field. Occasional magnetic reconnection triggers starquakes and gamma-ray flares known as soft gamma repeaters.

恒星内部的CNO循环是什么，它在多大质量的恒星中占主导地位？

What is the CNO cycle in stars, and in which mass range does it dominate energy production?

CNO循环是氢通过碳、氮、氧作为催化剂逐步聚变成氦的核反应链，对温度极其敏感，温度每升高一点反应速率急剧上升。它在大质量恒星（约1.3倍太阳质量以上）的核心占能量产生的主要份额，而小质量恒星更多依赖质子-质子链。该机制由贝特在1939年系统阐明，是理解大质量恒星演化的基础。
The CNO cycle is a chain of nuclear reactions in which hydrogen is fused into helium using carbon, nitrogen, and oxygen as catalysts, and it is extremely temperature-sensitive so its rate rises steeply with core temperature. It dominates energy production in the cores of more massive stars, roughly above 1.3 solar masses, whereas lower-mass stars rely mainly on the proton-proton chain. Bethe laid out the mechanism in 1939, and it remains central to models of massive-star evolution.

白矮星的冷却过程如何被用作测定星团年龄的宇宙钟？

How is the cooling of white dwarfs used as a cosmic clock to date star clusters?

白矮星不再进行核反应，仅依靠简并电子气体的余热缓慢辐射冷却，其亮度随年龄单调下降。观测中只要找到最暗、因而最老的白矮星，就能定出星团冷却序列的截断年龄。温吉特等人1987年的研究把这一方法确立为测定球状星团年龄的可靠手段。
White dwarfs no longer fuse nuclei and cool slowly by radiating the residual heat of a degenerate electron gas, their brightness falling monotonically with age. Observing the faintest, hence oldest, white dwarfs sets the truncation age of the cluster's cooling sequence. Winget and colleagues established this method in 1987 as a reliable way to date globular clusters.

钱德拉塞卡极限是什么，它如何决定白矮星的最终命运？

What is the Chandrasekhar limit, and how does it determine the fate of a white dwarf?

钱德拉塞卡极限约为1.4倍太阳质量，是电子简并压力能够支撑恒星残骸不被自身引力坍缩的最大质量。当白矮星通过吸积超过这一界限，简并压力失效，核心坍缩并触发Ia型超新星爆发。这一极限由钱德拉塞卡在1930年代用量子简并气体理论推导，是恒星残骸分类的关键边界。
The Chandrasekhar limit is about 1.4 solar masses, the maximum mass that electron degeneracy pressure can support against gravitational collapse in a stellar remnant. When a white dwarf accretes beyond this bound, degeneracy pressure fails, the core collapses, and a Type Ia supernova is triggered. Chandrasekhar derived it in the 1930s using quantum statistics of a degenerate gas, and it marks the dividing line among stellar remnants.

宇宙中比铁重的元素主要通过什么过程产生，2017年的什么观测证实了它？

Through what process are elements heavier than iron mainly forged, and what 2017 observation confirmed it?

比铁重的元素主要通过快中子俘获过程（r过程）在极端中子流环境中合成，例如双中子星并合。伯比奇等人早在1957年就把重元素合成纳入恒星核合成框架。2017年LIGO与Virgo探测到双中子星并合引力波，同时望远镜观测到对应千新星中金、铂等元素的特征，直接证实了r过程。
Elements heavier than iron are mainly forged by the rapid neutron-capture process, or r-process, in extreme neutron-rich environments such as merging neutron stars. Burbidge and colleagues framed heavy-element synthesis within stellar nucleosynthesis as early as 1957. In 2017 LIGO and Virgo detected a neutron-star merger in gravitational waves while telescopes saw the matching kilonova rich in gold and platinum, directly confirming the r-process.

为什么Ia型超新星可以作为标准烛光来测量宇宙距离？

Why can Type Ia supernovae serve as standard candles for measuring cosmic distances?

Ia型超新星来自白矮星达到钱德拉塞卡极限后的热核爆发，其峰值光度由相同物理过程决定，因此本征亮度非常一致。菲利普斯关系进一步用光变曲线的衰减速率修正峰值亮度，使测距精度达到约百分之几。这让天文学家能够把它们的观测亮度转化为可靠的距离，并由此发现宇宙加速膨胀。
Type Ia supernovae arise from the thermonuclear disruption of a white dwarf near the Chandrasekhar limit, so their peak luminosity is set by a uniform physical process and is intrinsically consistent. The Phillips relation refines the peak brightness using the decline rate of the light curve, improving distance precision to a few percent. Astronomers convert their observed brightness into reliable distances, which is how the accelerating expansion was discovered.

赫罗图是什么，它为什么是恒星天文学的基石？

What is the Hertzsprung-Russell diagram, and why is it a cornerstone of stellar astronomy?

赫罗图以恒星的光谱型或温度为横轴、光度为纵轴画出每个恒星的位置，揭示出明显的演化序列。主序、巨星支和白矮区等结构反映出恒星质量与年龄的对应关系。借助它，天文学家能推断星团的年代、恒星的质量和演化阶段。
The Hertzsprung-Russell diagram plots stars by spectral type or temperature against luminosity, revealing distinct evolutionary sequences. Features like the main sequence, giant branch, and white-dwarf region reflect the links between stellar mass and age. With it, astronomers infer a cluster's age and a star's mass and evolutionary stage.
