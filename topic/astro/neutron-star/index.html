什么是中子星状态方程难题？

What is the neutron star equation-of-state problem?

中子星内部物质在核密度之上的状态至今不明，测量其质量与半径能约束极端条件下的物态方程，这是现代核天体物理前沿。
The behavior of matter above nuclear density inside neutron stars is still unknown; measuring their mass and radius constrains the equation of state under extreme conditions, a frontier of nuclear astrophysics.

磁星（Magnetar）的磁场强到什么地步？

How strong is a magnetar's magnetic field?

磁星是磁场极强的中子星，表面磁场可达地球磁场的万亿倍以上，偶尔的星震会释放剧烈伽马射线爆，是宇宙中磁场最强的已知天体。
A magnetar is a neutron star with a field over a trillion times Earth's; occasional starquakes unleash intense gamma-ray bursts, making it the most magnetic object known.

中子星有多密、自转多快？

How dense and how fast do neutron stars spin?

中子星是大质量恒星的核心坍缩遗迹：电子被压进质子合成中子，靠中子简并压与核力撑住，整个太阳质量塞进直径约 20 公里的天体，每立方厘米以亿吨计。核心本就在自转，半径骤减几个数量级，角动量守恒使它转得极快，毫秒脉冲星每秒能转数百圈。但它并不均匀，外壳远稀疏于核心；脉冲星也不会自行加速，毫秒级那批靠吸积伴星物质被重新加速。
A neutron star is the collapsed core of a massive star: electrons are pressed into protons to make neutrons, and neutron degeneracy plus the nuclear force holds it up, so an entire solar mass is squeezed into a body about 20 km across, hundreds of millions of tonnes per cubic centimeter. The core was already spinning, and shedding several orders of magnitude of radius spins it up by conservation of angular momentum, so millisecond pulsars can turn hundreds of times a second. It is not uniform, though - the crust is far less dense than the core. Nor does a pulsar accelerate on its own: the millisecond ones were re-spun by accreting matter from a companion.

恒星质量越大，寿命反而越短，为什么？

Why do more massive stars live shorter lives?

大质量恒星核心温度更高、核聚变更剧烈，燃料消耗远快于小质量恒星，因此虽燃料更多却往往只活几百万到几千万年，而小质量恒星可燃烧上千亿年。
Massive stars burn much hotter and faster, consuming fuel far quicker than low-mass stars; despite having more fuel they live only millions of years, while low-mass stars shine for hundreds of billions.

宇宙里真有一秒钟能闪三十次的「灯塔」吗?脉冲星凭什么这么准时?

Do cosmic lighthouses really flash thirty times a second — what keeps a pulsar so punctual?

1967年贝尔在射电数据里发现间隔1.337秒的规整脉冲,一度被猜成外星信号,最终确认是快速自转的中子星:大质量恒星坍缩把太阳级质量压进城市大小的球体,角动量守恒把自转加速到毫秒级,磁场随之压缩增强万亿倍。磁轴与自转轴通常不重合,沿磁极喷出的射电束像探照灯扫过地球,每转一圈收到一次闪光。中子星惯量巨大又近乎无摩擦,自转周期稳定胜过原子钟,毫秒脉冲星因而成为检验引力波与低频时间基准的天然实验室。
In 1967 Jocelyn Bell found perfectly regular 1.337-second pulses in radio data — briefly suspected as alien beacons — later understood as a spinning neutron star: a collapsed core packing solar-scale mass into a city-sized sphere whose spin, conserved from collapse, reaches millisecond periods while the magnetic field is amplified a trillionfold. Because magnetic and rotation axes misalign, radio beams from the magnetic poles sweep past Earth like searchlights, one flash per turn. Enormous moment of inertia plus near-frictionless interiors make periods steadier than atomic clocks long-term, turning millisecond pulsars into natural laboratories for gravitational-wave tests and low-frequency timing standards.

中子星到底有多致密？

Just how dense is a neutron star?

一颗中子星把约一个太阳的质量塞进直径仅约 20 公里的球里，一茶匙物质在地球上重达数亿吨，是除黑洞外最致密的天体。
A neutron star packs about one solar mass into a sphere only ~20 km across; a teaspoon of it would weigh hundreds of millions of tons on Earth—the densest object short of a black hole.
