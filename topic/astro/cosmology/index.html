宇宙学常数问题是什么？

What is the cosmological constant problem?

量子场论估算的真空能密度比观测到的暗能量密度大了约10的120次方倍，这一巨大差异称为宇宙学常数问题。它被视为理论物理最严重的失配之一。调和二者可能需要新的对称性或量子引力理论。
Quantum field theory estimates a vacuum energy density about 10^120 times larger than the observed dark energy density, a discrepancy known as the cosmological constant problem. It is considered one of the worst mismatches in theoretical physics. Reconciling them may require new symmetries or a quantum theory of gravity.

什么是弱引力透镜？欧几里得任务用它来测量什么？

What is weak gravitational lensing, and what does the Euclid mission measure with it?

与产生清晰弧像的强引力透镜不同，弱引力透镜使背景星系的形状产生微小而系统的拉伸。单个星系的变形几乎察觉不到，但统计数十亿个星系的平均形状，就能恢复出追踪前景暗物质分布的剪切场。欧几里得望远镜正是通过高精度测量星系形状的系统性变形，来绘制暗物质的大尺度分布图。由此可以检验宇宙学模型，并约束暗物质与暗能量的性质。
Unlike strong lensing, which produces dramatic arcs, weak lensing causes small, systematic distortions in the shapes of background galaxies. The effect is imperceptible for any single galaxy, but averaging billions of galaxies recovers the shear field that traces foreground dark matter. Euclid precisely measures these systematic shape distortions to map dark matter's large-scale distribution. The results test cosmological models and constrain the nature of dark matter and dark energy.

类星体为什么能发出比整个星系还亮的光芒？

Why can quasars outshine their entire host galaxies?

类星体是活动星系核中极端明亮的一类。中心超大质量黑洞把周围气体吸引过来，物质下落时释放的引力能转化为辐射，效率远超核聚变。因此，一个星系的核心就能发出相当于数百个星系的光。观测到的最远类星体红移超过 7，对应宇宙不足 10 亿岁的年代，说明超大质量黑洞在大爆炸后很快就已形成。
Quasars are the most luminous class of active galactic nucleus. The central supermassive black hole drags in surrounding gas, and the gravitational energy released as matter falls inward is converted into radiation far more efficiently than nuclear fusion. A single galactic core can therefore outshine hundreds of galaxies. The most distant quasars have redshifts beyond 7, from when the universe was less than a billion years old, showing that supermassive black holes formed very quickly after the Big Bang.

什么是宇宙的平坦性问题？

What is the flatness problem of the universe?

观测表明宇宙空间在大尺度上近乎几何平坦，但其密度需要在极早期被精确调校到极高精度才能保持平坦。暴胀理论通过极速膨胀将任何初始弯曲「拉平」，从而解释了这一巧合。
Observations show the universe is nearly geometrically flat on large scales, yet its density would have needed impossibly fine tuning early on to stay flat. Inflation solves this by stretching any initial curvature flat through rapid expansion.

星系旋转曲线如何揭示了暗物质的存在？

How did galaxy rotation curves reveal dark matter?

按照开普勒定律，旋涡星系外侧恒星绕银心旋转的速度应随距离增大而下降，就像太阳系行星那样。但 20 世纪 70 年代，鲁宾和福特观测发现，旋涡星系外围的旋转速度基本保持不变。这说明外围存在大量既不发光也不吸光、却参与引力作用的物质，其质量远超可见恒星。这就是暗物质最早的强证据之一。
Keplerian dynamics predict that rotation speeds in the outer parts of spiral galaxies should fall with distance, as planets do in the solar system. But in the 1970s Vera Rubin and Kent Ford observed that rotation curves in the outer regions stay essentially flat. The only explanation is a large amount of invisible matter that neither emits nor absorbs light yet participates gravitationally, outweighing the visible stars. This became one of the earliest strong pieces of evidence for dark matter.

哈勃常数张力（Hubble tension）指的是哪两种测量方法之间的不一致？

Between which two measurement methods does the Hubble tension arise?

哈勃常数张力指早期宇宙方法（如普朗克卫星对宇宙微波背景的拟合，约 67 km/s/Mpc）与本地距离阶梯方法（如 SH0ES 团队用造父变星与 Ia 型超新星，约 73 km/s/Mpc）所得 H0 值存在超过 5σ 的偏差。这一差异可能暗示超出标准 ΛCDM 模型的新物理。
The Hubble tension refers to the discrepancy, exceeding 5σ, between early-universe methods (e.g., Planck CMB fitting, ~67 km/s/Mpc) and local distance-ladder methods (e.g., SH0ES using Cepheids and Type Ia supernovae, ~73 km/s/Mpc). The gap may hint at new physics beyond standard ΛCDM.

暗物质既不发光又看不见，科学家凭什么说它存在？

Dark matter neither shines nor is seen, so on what grounds do scientists say it exists?

证据来自引力效应。星系旋转曲线显示，外缘恒星转得比可见物质引力能解释的快得多，说明星系四周裹着一层看不见的质量晕；引力透镜的弯曲程度也超过可见物质的预期。宇宙微波背景的温度起伏模式以及宇宙结构的演化，同样要求存在约占总能量27%的暗物质。它的粒子本质至今未知，正在被地下实验和加速器寻找。
The evidence is gravitational. Galaxy rotation curves show outer stars moving far faster than visible matter can explain, implying a halo of unseen mass; gravitational lensing is also stronger than expected from visible matter alone. The temperature-fluctuation pattern of the cosmic microwave background and the growth of cosmic structure likewise demand dark matter making up about 27 percent of the total energy. Its particle nature is still unknown and is being hunted in underground detectors and accelerators.

暗物质有哪些候选粒子？科学家如何在实验室直接寻找它们？

What are the candidate particles for dark matter, and how do scientists search for them directly in laboratories?

主流候选粒子包括弱相互作用大质量粒子（WIMP）和轴子。WIMP 只通过弱力和引力与普通物质相互作用，轴子则是为解决强 CP 问题提出的极轻粒子，两者都可能构成冷暗物质。直接探测实验把探测器深埋地下以屏蔽宇宙射线，用高灵敏度探测器等待暗物质粒子偶尔与原子核碰撞产生的极微弱反冲信号。例如 LUX-ZEPLIN 使用数吨液态氙探测 WIMP，ADMX 等实验则用谐振腔寻找轴子，目前尚无确凿信号。
Leading candidate particles include weakly interacting massive particles (WIMPs) and axions. WIMPs interact with ordinary matter only through the weak force and gravity, while axions are extremely light particles originally proposed to solve the strong CP problem, and either could make up cold dark matter. Direct-detection experiments are placed deep underground to block cosmic rays, with ultra-sensitive detectors waiting for the faint recoil of a dark-matter particle striking a nucleus. LUX-ZEPLIN uses several tonnes of liquid xenon to hunt WIMPs and experiments such as ADMX use resonant cavities to look for axions, so far without a confirmed signal.

宇宙射线是什么？它们从哪里来？

What are cosmic rays, and where do they come from?

宇宙射线是几乎以光速运动的高能带电粒子，主要是质子、原子核和少量电子。它们到达地球时还会与大气作用，产生次级粒子。能量较低的宇宙射线主要来自太阳和银河系内的超新星遗迹，后者通过激波加速机制把粒子推到极高能量。能量最高的极少数粒子可超过 10 的 20 次方电子伏特，被认为来自银河系外的活动星系核等极端天体。
Cosmic rays are high-energy charged particles, mostly protons, nuclei and a few electrons, moving at nearly the speed of light. On arrival they interact with Earth's atmosphere, producing secondary particles. Lower-energy cosmic rays come mainly from the Sun and from galactic supernova remnants, which accelerate particles through shock acceleration. The rarest ultra-high-energy particles exceed 10^20 electron volts and are thought to originate from extreme extragalactic sources such as active galactic nuclei.

宇宙纤维结构之间是什么？

What lies between the cosmic filaments?

宇宙大尺度结构呈“宇宙网”：星系沿纤维和节点分布，纤维之间是近乎空旷的“宇宙空洞”。空洞内物质密度极低（约为宇宙平均的十分之一或更少），气体稀薄而温度低，却并非绝对真空——它们仍是暗物质和少量气体的稀疏分布区。空洞会随宇宙膨胀而不断变大。
On the largest scales the universe forms a cosmic web: galaxies lie along filaments and clusters, and between those filaments sit nearly empty regions called cosmic voids. Voids have very low density (a tenth of the cosmic mean or less) with thin, cool gas, but they are not true vacuums—they still contain sparse dark matter and gas. Voids grow larger as the universe expands.

1998 年科学家如何发现宇宙在加速膨胀？

How did scientists discover in 1998 that the expansion of the universe is accelerating?

1998 年，两个团队用 Ia 型超新星作为标准烛光，测量极远超新星的红移和亮度。结果发现它们比预期的更暗，也就是距离比匀速膨胀模型预测的更远。结合红移数据推断，宇宙不仅在膨胀，而且膨胀正在加速。这说明存在一种具有负压、能量密度约占宇宙 70% 的成分，即暗能量；这一发现获得 2011 年诺贝尔物理学奖。

宇宙再电离纪元大约在红移多少结束，主要证据来自哪些观测？

At about what redshift did cosmic reionization end, and what observations primarily constrain it?

宇宙再电离主要由首批恒星和星系的紫外光子完成，整体在红移 z ≈ 6（约大爆炸后 10 亿年）基本结束，但过程从 z ~ 15 起持续了数亿年。主要约束来自类星体光谱的 Gunn–Peterson 吸收槽、WMAP/Planck 给出的 Thomson 光学深度，以及高红移星系的发射线。
Cosmic reionization, driven mainly by ultraviolet photons from the first stars and galaxies, was essentially complete by redshift z ≈ 6 (about 1 billion years after the Big Bang), though the process began around z ~ 15 and lasted hundreds of millions of years. Key constraints come from the Gunn–Peterson troughs in quasar spectra, the Thomson optical depth measured by WMAP and Planck, and emission lines in high-redshift galaxies.

为什么 CMB 的黑体谱是支持大爆炸而非稳恒态宇宙的关键证据？

Why is the blackbody spectrum of the CMB key evidence for the Big Bang rather than a steady-state universe?

COBE 卫星在 1990 年测得的 CMB 谱与 2.725 K 黑体辐射吻合到万分之几的精度，这种普适热平衡谱只能由早期高温致密宇宙冷却形成。稳恒态模型用星系际尘埃散射星光来解释 CMB，却无法自然产生如此精确的黑体形状。
The COBE satellite measured the CMB spectrum in 1990 to match a 2.725 K blackbody to better than a few parts in 10,000, a universal thermal-equilibrium shape that only arises from the cooling of an early hot, dense universe. Steady-state models invoked interstellar dust scattering starlight, but they cannot naturally produce such a precise blackbody form.

Massive objects, including invisible dark-matter halos, gravitationally distort the images of background galaxies. By statistically measuring systematic shape distortions (shear) across many galaxies, one reconstructs all-sky dark-matter mass maps independent of luminous matter. DES and the Euclid satellite map large-scale structure this way.

Dark energy is the mysterious force driving the universe's expansion to accelerate, making up about 68 percent of the total energy budget. It acts as a nearly uniform repulsive pressure filling space, the opposite of dark matter's clustering effect. It entered the mainstream after the 1998 discovery, from distant supernovae, that expansion is speeding up; it resembles Einstein's cosmological constant, though its true nature remains a mystery.

What is gravitational lensing, and why does it help astronomers measure mass?

根据广义相对论，大质量天体或星系团会弯曲它周围的时空，使经过的光线偏折，就像一面透镜。背景星系的光因此被扭曲、放大，甚至形成环状或弧状影像，透镜的强度和变形程度取决于前景天体的质量，因此可以测量出包括暗物质在内的总质量。
According to general relativity, a massive object or galaxy cluster warps surrounding spacetime and deflects passing light like a lens. Background galaxies appear distorted, magnified, or arranged into rings and arcs; the strength of this lensing depends on the foreground mass, so it measures total mass including dark matter.
