类星体和活动星系核的能量来源是什么？

What powers quasars and active galactic nuclei?

类星体是活动星系核的一种极端形式，其能量来自中央超大质量黑洞吸积周围气体时释放的引力能。物质在吸积盘中螺旋下落，摩擦升温到极高温度并发出从射电到X射线的宽谱辐射，部分以相对论性喷流形式喷出。施密特1963年测定3C 273的红移，确认它们是极遥远、极明亮的天体。
A quasar is an extreme form of active galactic nucleus powered by a central supermassive black hole releasing gravitational energy as it accretes surrounding gas. Material spirals inward through a disk, heated by friction to extreme temperatures and emitting broadband radiation from radio to X-rays, sometimes in relativistic jets. Schmidt's 1963 redshift of 3C 273 established them as extraordinarily distant, luminous objects.

银河系中心的棒旋结构是如何被证实的？

How was the barred structure at the center of the Milky Way confirmed?

长期的天体测量显示银河系中心方向存在非轴对称的质量分布，恒星的运动模式偏离纯旋臂盘。通过近红外测光穿透尘埃，以及盖亚卫星对恒星自行的精确测量，天文学家确认了贯穿银心的长棒结构。这一棒结构引导气体向内流动，促进了中央核球的形成。
Decades of astrometry revealed a non-axisymmetric mass distribution toward the Galactic center, with stellar motions deviating from a pure disk. Near-infrared photometry piercing the dust, together with Gaia's precise stellar proper motions, confirmed a long bar across the central region. This bar funnels gas inward and helps build up the central bulge.

人马座A*是什么，事件视界望远镜如何拍到它的影像？

What is Sagittarius A*, and how did the Event Horizon Telescope image it?

人马座A*是银河系中心质量约400万倍太阳的超大质量黑洞，长期通过周围恒星的轨道被精确认证。事件视界望远镜把全球射电望远镜组成相当于地球大小的干涉阵列，在1.3毫米波长捕捉到环绕黑洞阴影的发光环。2022年公布的影像直接验证了广义相对论在强引力区的预言。
Sagittarius A* is the Milky Way's central supermassive black hole, about 4 million solar masses, confirmed for decades through the orbits of nearby stars. The Event Horizon Telescope linked radio dishes worldwide into an Earth-sized interferometer and captured the glowing ring around the black hole's shadow at 1.3 mm wavelength. The 2022 image directly tested general relativity in the strong-gravity regime.
