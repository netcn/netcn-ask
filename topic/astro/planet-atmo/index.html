金星和火星的大气都以二氧化碳为主,为什么两者的表面温度却相差数百度?

The atmospheres of Venus and Mars are both dominated by carbon dioxide, so why do their surface temperatures differ by hundreds of degrees?

关键差异在于大气总量:金星的大气压约为地球的92倍,而火星只有地球的约0.6%,厚重的CO2大气让金星产生极强的温室效应。金星还覆盖着硫酸云层,进一步捕获红外辐射并反射阳光形成复杂的辐射平衡。因此金星表面约735 K,而火星因大气稀薄、热容量小,全球平均温度仅约210 K。
The key difference is total atmospheric mass: Venus's surface pressure is about 92 times Earth's, while Mars holds only about 0.6% of an atmosphere, and the thick CO2 envelope drives a runaway greenhouse effect on Venus. A global layer of sulfuric-acid clouds further traps outgoing infrared radiation while scattering sunlight into a complex radiative balance. As a result, Venus bakes at roughly 735 K, whereas Mars, with its thin air and low heat capacity, averages only about 210 K.

气态巨行星的大气如何分层？

How is a giant planet's atmosphere layered?

木星、土星大气按温度和凝结高度分带，氨云在最上、硫化和铵云在下，强纬向急流形成可见条纹与风暴。
Jupiter and Saturn atmospheres are banded by temperature and condensation height, with ammonia clouds on top and ammonium-hydrogen-sulfide clouds below; strong zonal jets form visible stripes and storms.

什么是系外行星的“半径-质量”关系？

What is the exoplanet radius–mass relation?

同一半径的行星质量可差很多：岩石行星密度高、蓬松的含大量气体；这条关系帮我们区分超级地球与水世界、迷你海王星。
Planets of the same radius can differ greatly in mass: rocky ones are dense, puffy ones hold lots of gas; this relation separates super-Earths from water worlds and mini-Neptunes.

土卫六（Titan）的大气有什么特别？

What is special about Titan's atmosphere?

土卫六拥有浓厚氮气大气和液态甲烷雨湖，是太阳系唯一有稳定表面液体的卫星，其化学被认为类似地球早期，是搜寻前生命化学的热点。
Titan has a thick nitrogen atmosphere and lakes of liquid methane rain—the only moon with stable surface liquids—and its chemistry resembles early Earth, a hotspot for pre-life chemistry.

热木星为何可能有“逆温层”？

Why might a hot Jupiter have a temperature inversion?

某些热木星大气高层吸收恒星紫外（如TiO、VO分子）而变热，形成温度随高度升高的逆温层，使某些吸收线从吸收变发射，是系外大气研究焦点。
In some hot Jupiters, high-altitude molecules (TiO, VO) absorb stellar UV and heat the upper layer, creating a temperature inversion where temperature rises with height—flipping some lines from absorption to emission, a focus of exoplanet study.

#热木星 #逆温层 #TiO

金星与地球大小相近,为什么表面环境却相差悬殊?关键差异来自哪里?

Venus is nearly Earth's twin in size, yet its surface environment is drastically different. What accounts for the key difference?

两颗行星个头确实相近——金星半径约比地球小5%、质量约小18%——但金星的二氧化碳大气压高达92个地球标准大气压,叠加强温室效应使表面温度维持在约740 K。金星几乎没有板块运动和磁场,水汽在早期被光解并经太阳风剥离,碳无法像地球上那样被硅酸盐风化和沉积循环封存。失控温室一旦启动,水丢失与碳积累相互加强,最终造就了今天的恶劣表面环境。
Venus is indeed close to Earth in size - about 5% smaller in radius and roughly 18% less massive - yet it sustains a 92-bar CO2 atmosphere whose runaway greenhouse keeps the surface near 740 K. Lacking plate tectonics and a strong magnetic field, Venus lost its water through photodissociation and solar-wind stripping, so carbon was never locked away by silicate weathering and carbonate deposition as on Earth. Once the runaway greenhouse started, water loss and carbon buildup reinforced each other, producing today's hostile surface.

#失控温室效应 #行星大气演化

金星、地球、火星的大气为何如此不同？

Why are Venus, Earth and Mars atmospheres so different?

三者起初都含二氧化碳与水，地球因板块与生命锁住碳并保有液态水；金星失控温室使海洋蒸发，火星因弱磁场和弱引力让大气被太阳风剥离。
All three started with CO2 and water; Earth locked carbon via plate tectonics and life and kept liquid water, Venus suffered runaway greenhouse evaporation, and Mars lost its air to solar wind because of weak magnetic field and gravity.
