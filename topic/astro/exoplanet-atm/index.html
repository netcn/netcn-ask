在能量限制的大气逃逸模型里,加热效率这个参数为什么总是难以准确标定?

Why does the heating-efficiency parameter remain so hard to pin down in energy-limited atmospheric escape models?

模型假设入射 XUV 辐射的固定比例转化为气体克服行星引力所做的功,但真实效率取决于辐射沉积深度、氢分子解离、冷却谱线以及电离复合平衡,不同行星之间可差出一个数量级。观测端还叠加了恒星 XUV 光度本身的大误差,以及莱曼阿尔法、氦10830埃三重线等逃逸探针随温度结构的强烈变化。于是同一颗行星用不同代码拟合,得到的效率值可以从百分之几一直散布到几十个百分点。
The framework assumes a fixed fraction of incident XUV energy becomes work done by gas against planetary gravity, yet the genuine efficiency depends on where radiation deposits, on hydrogen dissociation, on cooling lines, and on ionization-recombination balance, varying by an order of magnitude between planets. Stellar XUV luminosities add large uncertainties, while escape probes such as Lyman-alpha and the helium 10830 angstrom triplet respond strongly to the assumed thermal profile. Fitting the same planet with different codes consequently spreads the inferred efficiency from a few percent to tens of percent.

#大气逃逸 #XUV 加热

凌星期间恒星光擦过热木星大气外缘,为什么碱金属共振线会让行星看起来变大?

When starlight grazes the limb of a transiting hot Jupiter, why do alkali resonance lines make the planet appear larger?

凌星光穿过行星高层大气的环状路径,谱线中心的吸收截面远大于连续谱,使对应波长的等效行星半径增大,凌星深度随之加深。钠的双共振线位于589纳米光学波段,标高放大效应显著,成为最早被证认的地外行星大气特征之一。2002年哈勃STIS仪器在HD 209458b上测得的钠吸收正是这一机制的经典实证。
Transit light samples an annular path through the planet's upper atmosphere, where line-center absorption cross sections dwarf the continuum value, raising the apparent radius at those wavelengths and deepening the transit. The sodium doublet sits at 589 nm in the optical, so the scale-height amplification is strong enough to observe from orbit. Hubble STIS measurements of sodium absorption on HD 209458b in 2002 remain the classic demonstration of this mechanism.

#透射光谱 #碱金属共振线

凌星过程中行星大气如何让透过光谱出现随波长变化的额外半径信号？

How does a transiting planet's atmosphere imprint a wavelength-dependent extra radius on the transmission spectrum?

行星本体遮挡的是几何圆盘对应的恒星光，但高层大气在特定波长上有附加不透明度，恒星光穿过这些半透明层时会按吸收截面被削弱。波长越接近强吸收带，能穿透的大气层越高，等效遮蔽面积越大，表现为凌星深度在该波段系统性加深。比较不同波段的视半径就能反演出钠钾原子线、水汽或云霾等成分与悬浮物的信息。
The planet's opaque disk blocks starlight geometrically, but its upper atmosphere adds wavelength-specific opacity along the limb where starlight grazes semi-transparent layers. Near strong absorption bands the light penetrates higher, more tenuous layers, enlarging the effective occulting area and deepening the transit depth systematically at those wavelengths. Measuring the apparent radius versus wavelength therefore reveals species such as sodium, potassium, water vapor, or the presence of aerosols.

#透射光谱 #大气不透明度

行星本身不发光,天文学家怎么知道几百光年外它的「空气」里有什么?

Planets emit no light of their own — how do astronomers read the atmosphere of a world hundreds of light-years away?

靠「凌星透射光谱」。当行星从母恒星前方经过,恒星的光会有一小部分穿过行星大气边缘再到达我们;不同分子只在特定波长上吸收光,把凌星时与凌星外的光谱相除,吸收特征的「指纹」便浮现出来——钠钾的吸收线、水汽与甲烷的宽带、乃至金属蒸气。詹姆斯·韦布望远镜凭借6.5米口径与红外灵敏度,已能在一颗系外行星大气中清晰识别出二氧化碳。
Through transmission spectroscopy during transits. As a planet crosses its star's disk, a sliver of starlight skims through the planet's atmospheric limb before reaching us; molecules absorb only at characteristic wavelengths, so dividing the in-transit spectrum by the out-of-transit one reveals fingerprints — sodium and potassium lines, broad water and methane bands, even metal vapors. With its 6.5-meter mirror and infrared sensitivity, the James Webb Space Telescope has now cleanly identified carbon dioxide in an exoplanet atmosphere.
