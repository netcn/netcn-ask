千新星（kilonova）的红蓝双成分模型如何对应中子星并合？

How does the two-component red-and-blue kilonova model correspond to a neutron-star merger?

双中子星并合抛出的富中子物质发生快中子俘获（r 过程）合成重元素。蓝色成分来自低不透明度、高纬度的较薄抛射物；红色成分来自赤道方向含镧系元素、不透明度高的较厚抛射物，对应并合喷流与潮汐尾的不同几何。引力波事件 GW170817 的电磁对应体证实了该模型。
Ejected neutron-rich matter in a binary neutron-star merger undergoes r-process synthesis of heavy elements. The blue component comes from low-opacity, high-latitude thin ejecta; the red from equatorial, lanthanide-rich, high-opacity thick ejecta—different geometries of the jet and tidal tails. The GW170817 electromagnetic counterpart confirmed the model.

伽马射线暴的长暴/短暴二分如何对应不同的中心引擎？

How does the long/short gamma-ray burst dichotomy map to different central engines?

持续时间长于约 2 秒的长暴通常由大质量恒星核坍缩产生，伴以超新星；短暴（小于 2 秒）则对应双中子星或中子星–黑洞并合，伴以千新星，已被 GW170817 证实。两者喷流结构与余辉环境也明显不同。
Long bursts (>~2 s) typically arise from massive-star core collapse with a supernova; short bursts (<2 s) correspond to binary neutron-star (or NS–BH) mergers with a kilonova, confirmed by GW170817. Their jet structures and afterglow environments also differ markedly.

重复快速射电暴的色散量演化如何被用来研究星系际介质？

How is the dispersion-measure evolution of repeating FRBs used to probe the intergalactic medium?

重复 FRB 的多次爆发若来自固定源，其色散量的缓慢变化（排除本地贡献后）可反映源所在星系晕与弥散星系际介质的电子柱密度。结合红移可约束宇宙重子物质在温热弥漫介质中的占比，补充 X 射线对失踪重子的探测。
For a repeating FRB at a fixed source, slow DM changes (after removing local contributions) trace the electron column of the host halo and diffuse IGM. Combined with redshift, this constrains the fraction of cosmic baryons in the WHIM, complementing X-ray searches for missing baryons.

快速射电暴(FRB)为什么被认为可能来自磁星?目前有哪些观测证据?

Why are magnetars considered likely sources of fast radio bursts (FRBs), and what observational evidence supports this?

磁星拥有宇宙中最强的磁场(~10^14–10^15 G),其磁重联能以毫秒尺度释放巨大能量,正好匹配FRB的亮度和时标。2020年SGR 1935+2154爆发了与FRB高度相似的射电暴(SGR 1806-20在2004年的伽马耀发也伴随过类FRB信号),这是迄今最强的直接关联证据。此外CHIME对重复FRB的偏振、周期性活跃窗口等统计特征,也与年轻磁星的性质大体吻合,但单一磁星机制尚不能解释全部FRB。
Magnetars carry the strongest known magnetic fields (~10^14–10^15 G), and magnetic reconnection on them can release enormous energy on millisecond timescales, matching FRB luminosities and durations. In April 2020 SGR 1935+2154 produced a radio burst strikingly similar to an extragalactic FRB — the strongest direct association to date. Statistical properties of repeating FRBs measured by CHIME, such as polarization behavior and periodic activity windows, also fit young magnetar models, though a single magnetar mechanism does not yet explain every FRB.

磁星为什么被锁定为快速射电暴的头号嫌疑犯?

Why are magnetars the prime suspects behind fast radio bursts?

2020年4月28日,银河系磁星SGR 1935+2154被空间伽马射线监测器记录到硬X射线暴,同日又被CHIME与STARE2两台独立射电望远镜捕捉到毫秒射电暴——首次在家门口实现同一事件的双波段对应,能量虽远低于宇宙学FRB,机制却如出一辙。磁星拥有宇宙最强量级磁场,星壳应力突然释放可在磁层中激发相干的毫秒射电激波,天然满足FRB的全部苛刻条件;多个重复暴的宿主星系观测也支持年轻磁星起源。
On 28 April 2020 the Galactic magnetar SGR 1935+2154 fired a hard X-ray burst recorded by space monitors, and the same day two independent radio telescopes, CHIME and STARE2, caught a millisecond radio burst — the first same-event, two-band catch in our own backyard. Far weaker than cosmological FRBs yet mechanically identical, it matched theory: magnetars carry the strongest known surface magnetic fields, sudden crustal stress fires coherent millisecond radio waves through the magnetosphere, and host-galaxy studies of repeating FRBs point to young magnetars.

潮汐撕裂事件（TDE）是如何暴露星系中心黑洞的存在的？

How do tidal disruption events (TDEs) reveal central supermassive black holes?

当恒星过于靠近中心黑洞、被潮汐力撕碎时，约一半物质被抛射、另一半吸积并发出明亮的 X 射线与紫外耀发。TDE 的光变与谱线特征可用来估计黑洞质量与自旋，是探测宁静星系中心黑洞的探针。
When a star passes too close to a central black hole, tidal forces tear it apart; about half is ejected and half accretes, flaring brightly in X-ray and UV. TDE light curves and spectra estimate black-hole mass and spin, probing dormant central black holes.

Ia 型超新星为何被称为标准烛光？

Why are Type Ia supernovae called standard candles?

Ia 型超新星来自白矮星接近钱德拉塞卡极限的热核爆炸，峰值光度较均匀，且光变宽度与峰值亮度存在 Phillips 关系可校正。经校正后它们可作为标准烛光，用于测量遥远星系距离并发现宇宙加速膨胀。
Type Ia supernovae arise from thermonuclear explosions of white dwarfs near the Chandrasekhar limit, with fairly uniform peak luminosity; the width–luminosity Phillips relation allows correction. Calibrated, they serve as standard candles to measure distant galaxies and reveal accelerated expansion.

快速射电暴（FRB）是什么？它们来自哪里？

What are fast radio bursts (FRBs), and where do they come from?

FRB 是毫秒级的极强射电闪，多数来自银河系外，部分重复爆发已被定位到年轻磁星，但多数一次性暴的源头仍无定论。
FRBs are millisecond flashes of intense radio waves, mostly from beyond our galaxy; some repeaters trace to young magnetars, but the sources of most one-off bursts remain unsettled.

什么是时域天文学?它为什么在近十几年才迅速崛起?

What is time-domain astronomy, and why has it surged only in the past couple of decades?

时域天文学研究天体亮度、位置或光谱随时间的变化,关注超新星、变星、伽马暴和引力波事件等暂现源。它长期受限于望远镜难以对同一片天区反复巡天,数据存储与快速预警能力也不足。近十年来大视场巡天(如 ZTF)和自动化编目流水线让每晚发现成千上万个瞬变事件成为可能,该领域随之爆发式增长。
Time-domain astronomy studies how celestial objects change in brightness, position, or spectrum over time, focusing on transients such as supernovae, variable stars, gamma-ray bursts, and gravitational-wave events. For decades it was limited by telescopes that rarely revisited the same sky region and by insufficient data pipelines for rapid alerts. In recent years wide-field surveys like ZTF and automated classification pipelines that flag thousands of transient candidates per night have made the field explode.

快速射电暴只闪几毫秒,凭什么说它来自几十亿光年之外、能量还极其巨大?

Fast radio bursts flash for mere milliseconds — how do we know they come from billions of light-years away with enormous energy?

关键证据是色散量:射电脉冲穿越星际等离子体时,低频比高频走得慢,延迟正比于路径上的自由电子总数。银河系内电子总量有限,而实测FRB的色散量远超银道面贡献,说明大部分路程发生在河外,距离动辄数十亿光年。在此距离上,毫秒级爆发的射电能量仍抵得上太阳数周乃至数月的全部输出,如此极端必须来自致密天体的剧烈过程。
The key is dispersion. Radio pulses crossing ionized interstellar plasma slow down at lower frequencies, the delay measuring the number of free electrons along the path. The Milky Way cannot supply enough electrons to match observed dispersion measures, so most of the journey is extragalactic — often billions of light-years. At those distances even milliseconds of radio emission rival the Sun's total output over weeks to months, demanding extreme physics around compact objects.

耀变体（blazar）为何在时域上表现出剧烈而快速的光变？

Why do blazars show such violent, rapid variability in the time domain?

耀变体是喷流几乎正对地球的活动星系核，相对论性集束效应把光度与变幅放大、把时标压缩。喷流内激波与磁重联在近黑洞尺度上快速变化，因而在数小时到数天内就能观测到显著光变。
Blazars are active galactic nuclei whose jets point nearly at Earth; relativistic beaming amplifies luminosity and compresses timescales. Shocks and magnetic reconnection in the jet vary rapidly near the black hole, producing notable variability from hours to days.

快速射电暴（FRB）的主要观测特征是什么？

What are the main observational features of fast radio bursts (FRBs)?

FRB 是毫秒量级的强射电脉冲，色散量远超银河系贡献，表明源自河外甚至宇宙学距离。多数一次性爆发，少数（如 FRB 121102）为重复暴。其物理起源至今未明，磁星被认为是最可能的来源之一。
FRBs are millisecond-duration strong radio pulses with dispersion measures far exceeding the Milky Way's, indicating extragalactic or cosmological distances. Most are one-off; a few (e.g., FRB 121102) repeat. Their origin is unknown, with magnetars among the leading candidates.
