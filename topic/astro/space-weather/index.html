日球层电流片(行星际磁场扇形结构)是如何形成的?它对流星的观测有什么间接影响?

How does the heliospheric current sheet — the sector structure of the interplanetary magnetic field — form, and what indirect effect does it have on meteor observations?

太阳自转叠加偶极磁场,使磁力线被太阳风拖曳成帕克螺旋,而磁场极性反转面延伸出去形成一个波浪状的电流片,地球穿越其不同侧面时就观测到磁扇形交替。流星体本身不受电磁力影响,但雷达流星监测依赖电离尾迹,太阳风与地磁活动会改变高层大气电离状态与背景噪声。因此扇形结构经过时,雷达流星计数会出现可统计到的涨落,这是日地耦合研究中的一个经典课题。
Solar rotation acting on a dipolar field drags field lines into the Parker spiral, and the polarity-reversal surface extends outward as a wavy current sheet; as Earth crosses its different flanks, spacecraft observe alternating magnetic sectors. Meteoroids themselves are unaffected by electromagnetic forces, but radar meteor detection depends on ionized trails, and solar-wind conditions plus geomagnetic activity alter upper-atmosphere ionization and background noise. Statistically detectable fluctuations in radar meteor counts when a sector boundary passes are therefore a classic topic in Sun-Earth coupling research.

流星雨为什么会周期性地在每年大致相同的时间出现?

Why do meteor showers recur at roughly the same time every year?

流星雨源于彗星(少数来自小行星)在轨道上抛洒的尘埃颗粒带,当地球每年沿公转路径穿过这些颗粒云时,微粒高速进入高层大气烧蚀发光,便形成流星雨。由于母彗星的轨道是固定的,地球与其交会的时刻也基本固定,所以英仙座流星雨总是出现在8月中旬、双子座流星雨出现在12月中旬。辐射点的位置取决于地球运动方向与颗粒云相对速度的几何关系,这也是流星雨以其所在星座命名的原因。
Meteor showers come from dust streams shed by comets (occasionally asteroids). Each year Earth sweeps through these particle clouds along its orbit, and the grains burn up in the upper atmosphere to produce meteors. Because each parent body follows a fixed orbit, the crossing dates stay stable - hence the Perseids peak in mid-August and the Geminids in mid-December. The radiant direction is set by the geometry of Earth's motion relative to the stream, which is why showers take their names from constellations.

一次强烈的日冕物质抛射发生在1.5亿公里之外，它为什么会危及地面上的电网？

A violent coronal mass ejection erupts some 150 million kilometres away. Why can it endanger power grids here on the ground?

强磁暴期间，携带着南向行星际磁场的抛射物与地球磁层发生磁重联，大量能量注入使环电流增强、地表磁场剧烈起伏。依照法拉第电磁感应定律，快速变化的磁场会在长距离输电线路、油气管道等良导体中激发地磁感应电流，使变压器直流偏磁饱和、异常发热乃至损坏。1989年3月的大磁暴就让加拿大魁北克电网在约一分半钟内全线崩溃。
During a severe storm, ejecta carrying a southward interplanetary magnetic field reconnects with Earth's magnetosphere; the injected energy intensifies the ring current and makes the ground-level magnetic field fluctuate rapidly. By Faraday's law of induction, these swift variations drive geomagnetically induced currents along long conducting paths such as power lines and pipelines, biasing transformers into half-cycle saturation, overheating and damaging them. The great March 1989 storm collapsed the entire Quebec power grid in roughly ninety seconds.

太阳耀斑与日冕物质抛射有什么区别？它们对地球的影响方式为何不同？

What is the difference between a solar flare and a coronal mass ejection, and why do they affect Earth differently?

耀斑是日面局部磁能骤然释放形成的电磁辐射爆发，以光速约八分钟抵达地球，主要扰动电离层，干扰短波通信与导航。日冕物质抛射则是数十亿吨磁化等离子体被整体抛出，约一至四天到达，撞击地球磁场引发地磁暴，感应电流可损坏电网变压器并加重卫星轨道衰减。两者常相伴发生，但机理与时间尺度截然不同。
A flare is a sudden release of electromagnetic radiation when magnetic energy on the Sun snaps; moving at light speed, it reaches Earth in about eight minutes and mainly disturbs the ionosphere, disrupting shortwave communication and navigation. A coronal mass ejection instead throws billions of tons of magnetized plasma into interplanetary space, arriving in one to four days; striking Earth's magnetic field, it triggers geomagnetic storms whose induced currents can damage grid transformers and increase satellite drag. The two often occur together but differ sharply in mechanism and timing.

极光为何集中在南北磁纬较高地区？那些舞动的绿光和红光是如何产生的？

Why do auroras cluster around high magnetic latitudes, and how are their shimmering green and red lights produced?

太阳风持续挤压地球磁层，磁尾中的磁重联将电子加速，使其沿磁力线汇入南北半球高磁纬的高层大气。电子撞击氧原子与氮分子并令其激发，受激粒子回落时发出特征色光：约一两百千米高的原子氧贡献最常见的绿色，更高处的氧发出暗红光，氮则添上蓝紫色调。因粒子循磁力线聚集，极光呈现为环绕地磁极的明亮光带。
The solar wind continually presses against Earth's magnetosphere, and reconnection in the magnetotail accelerates electrons that stream down field lines into the upper atmosphere at high magnetic latitudes. There they excite oxygen atoms and nitrogen molecules; as the energized particles relax, characteristic colors appear: atomic oxygen around 100–200 kilometers altitude gives the familiar green, oxygen higher up glows deep red, and nitrogen adds blue-purple shades. Channeled along magnetic fields, the light forms ovals around each geomagnetic pole.

太阳脸上有时会长「黑斑」,那是什么?会影响我们吗?

The Sun sometimes grows dark spots — what are they and do they matter?

那些黑斑叫太阳黑子,是太阳表面温度稍低的区域(也有四千多度),因为比周围暗才显黑。黑子多的时候太阳更活跃,可能喷出带电粒子流扰动地球,让指南针乱摆、极光变亮,严重时影响电网和卫星。科学家每天观测它们做空间天气预报。
Those spots are sunspots — regions slightly cooler (still four thousand degrees!) appearing dark against brighter surroundings. Spot-rich times mean active Sun flinging charged particles that wiggle compasses, brighten auroras and sometimes trouble power grids and satellites. Scientists watch daily to forecast space weather.
