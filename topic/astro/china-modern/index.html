为什么中国空间站叫'天宫'，它和探月探火怎样配合？

Why is China's space station called 'Tiangong,' and how does it coordinate with lunar and Mars missions?

'天宫'取天上宫殿之意，是长期有人驻留的科研平台；它可验证长期驻留、再生生保等关键技术，为未来载人登月与深空探测打基础。
'Tiangong' means heavenly palace—a long-term crewed lab; it tests life-support and long-stay tech that future crewed lunar and deep-space missions will need.

北斗的'短报文'通信功能是做什么的，为什么多数国外导航系统没有？

What does Beidou's short-message communication function do, and why do most foreign navigation systems lack it?

北斗卫星能收发简短文字报文，让没有手机信号的海上、野外用户也能求救和联络；这需要卫星具备双向通信能力，增加了系统复杂度。
Beidou satellites can send and receive short text messages, letting users at sea or in the wild call for help without cell service; it requires two-way communication capability, adding system complexity.

为什么火星着陆比月球着陆更难？

Why is landing on Mars harder than on the Moon?

火星有稀薄大气需防热和降落伞减速，但大气又太薄无法轻柔着陆；加上地火通信延迟达十几分钟，无法实时操控，必须自动完成'恐怖七分钟'。
Mars has thin air needing heat shields and parachutes yet too thin for a soft landing; with 10+ minute light-delay, landing cannot be remote-controlled and must be fully automatic—the 'seven minutes of terror.'

羲和号、夸父号太阳探测卫星是观测什么的？

What do the Xihe and Kuafu solar observatories observe?

羲和号从特殊轨道观测太阳大气的磁场与速度，夸父号巡测太阳爆发和日地空间天气，二者共同守护地球的'空间环境'安全。
Xihe observes solar atmospheric magnetic fields and flows from a special orbit, while Kuafu monitors solar eruptions and space weather—together guarding Earth's space environment.

#羲和号 #太阳探测

为什么嫦娥五号要去月球'挖土'采样？

Why did Chang'e-5 go to the Moon to collect soil samples?

月球样品能帮助测定月球年龄、火山活动历史，并验证'月球晚期重轰炸'等关键假说，是仅靠遥感无法获得的直接证据。
Lunar samples reveal the Moon's age, volcanic history and test key ideas like the Late Heavy Bombardment—direct evidence remote sensing alone cannot provide.

为什么探月探火都要算'发射窗口'？

Why must lunar and Mars missions time their 'launch windows'?

地球、月球和火星位置不断变化，只有在特定几天出发，才能用最省燃料的轨道相遇；错过就要再等几个月甚至两年。
As Earth, Moon and Mars move, only on certain days can you take the most fuel-efficient path to meet them; miss it and you wait months or even two years.

中国'巡天'空间望远镜和哈勃有什么不同？

How does China's Xuntian space telescope differ from Hubble?

巡天望远镜视场比哈勃大300多倍，擅长对大范围天区快速拍照普查，并可与天宫空间站共轨、方便维护升级，而哈勃视场较窄、专注深场精测。
Xuntian has a field of view over 300 times wider than Hubble's, suited to fast wide-area surveys, and shares orbit with the Tiangong station for easy servicing; Hubble has a narrow field for deep precise imaging.

FAST'中国天眼'为什么建在贵州的山窝里？

Why was China's FAST radio telescope built in a mountain bowl in Guizhou?

贵州的喀斯特洼地天然像个巨碗，能托住500米口径的反射面，且当地人烟稀少、电磁干扰低，适合接收极其微弱的宇宙无线电信号。
Guizhou's karst depression naturally cradles the 500-meter dish, and its sparse population gives low radio interference—ideal for catching faint cosmic radio signals.

北斗卫星导航系统怎么知道你在哪里？

How does the Beidou system know where you are?

北斗由多颗卫星不停播发带精确时间戳的信号，接收机测出几颗卫星信号的到达时间差，用三角定位算出经纬度与高度。
Beidou satellites broadcast signals with precise timestamps; a receiver measures arrival-time differences from several satellites and triangulates your latitude, longitude and altitude.

火箭为什么能飞上天？

Why can a rocket fly into the sky?

火箭向下喷出高速气体，气体反过来把火箭往上推（作用力与反作用力），只要推力大于重力就能不断加速升空。
A rocket blasts gas downward, and the gas pushes the rocket upward (action and reaction); when thrust beats gravity it keeps accelerating upward.

'悟空'号暗物质粒子探测卫星是找什么的？

What is the Wukong dark-matter satellite searching for?

悟空号在太空中精确测量高能电子和伽马射线能谱，试图捕捉暗物质粒子湮灭或衰变产生的异常信号，因为暗物质不发光却占宇宙质量大部分。
Wukong precisely measures high-energy electron and gamma-ray spectra in space to catch anomalies from dark-matter annihilation or decay; dark matter is invisible yet dominates cosmic mass.

天问一号去火星做了什么？

What did Tianwen-1 do at Mars?

天问一号一次任务实现'绕、着、巡'：环绕火星、祝融号火星车着陆乌托邦平原并巡视探测，使中国成为第二个成功着陆火星的国家。
Tianwen-1 accomplished orbiting, landing and roving in one mission: its Zhurong rover touched down on Utopia Planitia, making China the second nation to land on Mars.
