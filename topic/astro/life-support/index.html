低重力对人体有哪些长期影响？

What are the long-term effects of low gravity on the body?

微重力导致骨钙流失、肌肉萎缩、体液上移和视力变化；长期月球（0.17g）或火星（0.38g）任务需要锻炼设备和人工重力等对抗措施。
Microgravity causes bone-calcium loss, muscle atrophy, fluid shifts upward and vision changes; long lunar (0.17g) or Mars (0.38g) missions need exercise gear and maybe artificial gravity to counter this.

太空辐射为何对宇航员危险？

Why is space radiation dangerous to astronauts?

地球磁场外有银河宇宙射线和太阳粒子事件，能损伤DNA、增加癌症风险；深空任务需用水、聚合物或磁场屏蔽加以防护。
Beyond Earth's magnetic field, galactic cosmic rays and solar particle events can damage DNA and raise cancer risk; deep-space missions need shielding with water, polymers or magnetic fields.

什么是闭环生命保障系统？

What is a closed-loop life support system?

闭环系统回收并再生水、氧气和食物（如用植物和微生物处理废水与二氧化碳），尽量减少从地球补给，是长期太空驻留和火星任务的必备。
A closed-loop system recovers and regenerates water, oxygen and food (using plants and microbes to process waste and CO2), minimizing resupply from Earth—essential for long stays and Mars missions.

宇航员在太空中如何获得水与氧气？

How do astronauts get water and oxygen in space?

国际空间站的氧气由电解水产生，水则来自回收的汗水、尿液和冷凝水，经多重过滤净化后循环使用，回收率超过90%。
The ISS makes oxygen by electrolyzing water, and its water comes from recycled sweat, urine and condensate, purified through multiple filters and reused—over 90% recovered.
