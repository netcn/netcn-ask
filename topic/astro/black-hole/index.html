引力红移与多普勒频移在黑洞吸积盘谱线中如何区分？

How are gravitational redshift and Doppler shift distinguished in black-hole accretion-disk spectra?

围绕黑洞高速公转的吸积盘内侧物质在朝向地球一侧产生强蓝移、背向一侧强红移，形成不对称的宽铁 Kα 线。叠加黑洞引力造成的系统红移后，整体谱线畸变（相对论性展宽）可反推最内稳定圆轨道（ISCO）与黑洞自旋，二者贡献在模型拟合中分离。
Disk material orbiting near the hole shows strong blueshift on the approaching side and redshift on the receding side, broadening the iron Kα line asymmetrically. Combined with the black hole's gravitational redshift, the relativistic line profile lets one infer the innermost stable circular orbit (ISCO) and spin, with the two contributions separated in modeling.

霍金辐射是如何让黑洞随时间蒸发的？

How does Hawking radiation cause a black hole to evaporate over time?

霍金在 1974 年指出，视界附近的量子涨落使一对虚粒子中一个落入黑洞、另一个逃逸，逃逸者表现为从视界外发出的热辐射。由于黑洞损失能量等效于损失质量，寿命极长的黑洞会极其缓慢地收缩、升温，直至最终爆发。
In 1974 Hawking showed that quantum fluctuations just outside the horizon let one member of a virtual particle pair fall in while the other escapes, appearing as thermal radiation emitted from beyond the horizon. Because losing energy means losing mass, an extremely long-lived black hole shrinks and heats up very slowly until it finally evaporates.

黑洞会“蒸发”消失吗？

Can black holes evaporate and disappear?

根据霍金辐射理论，黑洞会因量子效应缓慢损失能量并辐射粒子，质量越小蒸发越快；但恒星级黑洞的蒸发时间远超宇宙当前年龄，实际上难以观测。
Hawking radiation says black holes slowly lose energy via quantum effects; smaller ones evaporate faster, but stellar black holes would take vastly longer than the universe's age to vanish.

黑洞的引力并不会把周围一切都吸进去，为什么？

A black hole does not suck in everything around it — why not?

从远处看，黑洞的引力场和相同质量的普通天体完全一样，它并不会额外多拉周围的东西。远处绕行的恒星和气体在普通引力作用下就能稳定运动，只有闯进潮汐半径或视界以内的物质才会被吞掉。真正危险的只是它的视界内部，而不是黑洞本身有什么特别的吸力。
At a distance, a black hole's gravitational field is exactly the same as that of an ordinary object of equal mass; it exerts no extra pull. Stars and gas orbiting far away move stably under ordinary gravity, and only matter venturing inside the tidal region or the horizon is swallowed. What makes it dangerous is the interior beyond the horizon, not some special suction power of the hole itself.

为什么探测引力波需要几公里长的激光干涉仪？

Why does detecting gravitational waves require interferometers several kilometres long?

引力波引起的空间应变极其微小，典型只有10的负21次方量级，也就是在4公里的臂长上，长度变化只有约质子直径的千分之一。干涉臂越长，同样的应变造成的臂长差越大，越容易被检测到。为此，LIGO把测试质量用悬挂镜片隔振，在真空管道里运行，再用两臂的干涉相消来压制噪声。
Gravitational waves stretch space by only about one part in 10^21 — over a 4-kilometre arm, a change of roughly a thousandth of a proton's diameter. The longer the arms, the larger the length difference a given strain produces and the easier it is to measure. So LIGO suspends its mirrors as vibration-isolated test masses, runs the beams in vacuum tubes, and cancels noise by interfering the two arms.

克尔黑洞的能层为何可以提取旋转能量？

Why can rotational energy be extracted from a Kerr black hole's ergosphere?

旋转的克尔黑洞之外存在能层，其中时空被拖曳使任何粒子都无法保持静止。潘罗斯过程让粒子进入能层后分裂，一部分带着负能量落向黑洞、另一部分携走大于入射的总能量逃逸，从而从黑洞自转中偷走能量并轻微减慢其旋转。
Outside a rotating Kerr black hole lies the ergosphere, where frame-dragging forbids any particle from remaining at rest. In the Penrose process, a particle entering the ergosphere splits; one fragment with negative energy falls in while the other escapes with more energy than the whole, extracting rotational energy and slowing the hole.

引力波是怎么被LIGO探测到的？

How does LIGO detect gravitational waves?

两个黑洞并合时会让时空产生涟漪，即引力波，它经过地球时会把空间拉伸或压缩一点点。LIGO用两条各4公里的激光干涉臂来捕捉这种变形：引力波经过时，两臂的长度会交替变化，干涉条纹随之摆动。2015年9月，LIGO首次直接探测到约13亿光年外两个约30倍太阳质量黑洞的并合，名为GW150914。
Merging black holes send ripples through spacetime — gravitational waves — which stretch and squeeze space by a tiny amount as they pass Earth. LIGO catches that distortion with two 4-kilometre laser interferometer arms: when a wave passes, the arms' lengths alternate and the interference pattern shifts. In September 2015 LIGO made the first direct detection, GW150914, from the merger of two black holes of about 30 solar masses some 1.3 billion light-years away.

相对论喷流是如何被黑洞吸积系统加速到接近光速的？

How are relativistic jets accelerated to near light speed by black-hole accretion systems?

当吸积盘存在强有序磁场并带走黑洞自转的角动量（Blandford–Znajek 机制）或盘内磁离心加速（Blandford–Payne 机制）时，沿两极的磁化等离子体被准直并提取磁能与转动能，加速到接近光速。喷流功率与吸积率和黑洞自旋相关，是耀变体与射电星系的高能引擎。
With a strong ordered magnetic field threading the disk, black-hole spin energy (Blandford–Znajek) or disk magnetic-centrifugal launching (Blandford–Payne) channels magnetized plasma along the poles, collimating and accelerating it to near light speed. Jet power scales with accretion rate and spin, powering blazars and radio galaxies.

银河系中心的黑洞有多大？

How massive is the black hole at the centre of the Milky Way?

银河系中心的黑洞叫人马座A*，质量约为太阳的400万倍。虽然这么重，它的直径却只有大约2400万公里，比水星到太阳的距离还小，在宇宙尺度上算是一颗小而密的天体。2022年，事件视界望远镜拍到了它周围发光气体形成的甜甜圈状影像。
The Milky Way's central black hole is Sagittarius A*, with a mass of about four million Suns. Heavy as that is, its diameter is only about 24 million kilometres, smaller than Mercury's orbit, making it small and dense on cosmic scales. In 2022 the Event Horizon Telescope imaged the doughnut-like glow of gas surrounding it.

吸积盘为什么会发光，其颜色通常偏向哪一端？

Why does an accretion disk glow, and toward which end of the spectrum is it usually shifted?

落向致密天体（黑洞、中子星）的物质在吸积盘内因粘滞摩擦逐级释放引力势能，被加热到数百万开尔文，主要发出 X 射线与紫外辐射。因此吸积盘通常呈高温蓝色或紫外色，而非以可见光为主。
Matter spiraling into a compact object releases gravitational potential energy through viscous friction in the disk and is heated to millions of kelvin, emitting mainly X-rays and ultraviolet light. Accretion disks are therefore typically hot and blue/UV rather than visibly dominated.

事件视界望远镜（EHT）如何拍到黑洞的阴影？

How did the Event Horizon Telescope (EHT) image a black hole's shadow?

EHT 将横跨地球的多架射电望远镜用甚长基线干涉（VLBI）合成一架口径相当于地球直径的虚拟望远镜，在 1.3 毫米波长观测 M87 星系中心黑洞与银心人马座 A 星。亮环来自吸积盘光在强引力下的引力透镜，中心暗区即为黑洞阴影——事件视界附近光子捕获区的投影。
The EHT linked radio telescopes across Earth via very-long-baseline interferometry into a virtual telescope the size of the planet, observing the M87 central black hole and Sagittarius A* at 1.3 mm. The bright ring is gravitationally lensed disk emission; the central dark region is the black-hole shadow—the projection of the photon-capture zone near the horizon.

恒星级黑洞和星系中心的超大质量黑洞有什么不同？

How do stellar-mass black holes differ from the supermassive ones at galaxy centres?

恒星级黑洞由大质量恒星死后核心坍缩形成，质量只有几个到几十个太阳质量，在银河系里数量很多。超大质量黑洞质量可达数百万到上百亿个太阳，盘踞在星系中心，它的形成途径至今仍在研究中，可能靠合并增长，也可能由早期巨气体云直接坍缩。两者的基本物理相同，但尺度天差地别。
Stellar-mass black holes form from the collapsed cores of dead massive stars, weigh only a few to tens of solar masses, and are plentiful in the Milky Way. Supermassive black holes, up to hundreds of millions or tens of billions of solar masses, sit at galaxy centres; how they grow is still under study, possibly through mergers or direct collapse of giant gas clouds early on. The physics is the same, but the scales are vastly different.

引力波事件 GW150914 是如何证实黑洞真实存在的？

How did the gravitational-wave event GW150914 confirm that black holes really exist?

2015 年 LIGO 探测到两个约 30 倍太阳质量黑洞并合产生的时空涟漪，其波形与广义相对论双黑洞旋进—并合—铃宕的数值模拟精确吻合。这是人类首次直接探测到黑洞并合，也验证了黑洞作为动态天体真实存在。
In 2015 LIGO detected spacetime ripples from the merger of two black holes of about 30 solar masses each, with a waveform matching general-relativistic simulations of binary inspiral, merger and ringdown. It was the first direct detection of a black-hole merger and confirmed black holes as real dynamical objects.

事件视界到底是一道什么样的边界？

What kind of boundary is the event horizon?

事件视界是黑洞的边界，它不是固体表面，而是一个有去无回的临界位置。在那里，逃离黑洞所需的逃逸速度正好等于光速，所以连光也无法逃出。任何物质一旦越过视界，就再也无法把任何信息传回外面。
The event horizon is the boundary of a black hole. It is not a solid surface but a point of no return: there the escape velocity needed to get away equals the speed of light, so not even light can leave. Anything that crosses the horizon can never send information back out.

超大质量黑洞与星系核球质量之间为何存在相关性？

Why is there a correlation between supermassive black-hole mass and galactic bulge mass?

观测发现星系中心超大质量黑洞质量与宿主星系核球（bulge）质量、恒星速度弥散存在紧密的 M–σ 关系。这通常被解释为黑洞与宿主在并合与共演化过程中通过反馈（如活动星系核风）相互调节，避免气体无节制地落入中心。
Observations show a tight M–σ relation between supermassive black-hole mass and host bulge mass (or stellar velocity dispersion). It is usually interpreted as co-evolution: feedback such as AGN winds regulates growth of both, preventing runaway central gas inflow.

Because it is both tiny and twitchy: Sagittarius A* carries only about a thousandth of M87's central black hole's mass yet lies nearly two thousand times closer, so the two subtend similar angles on the sky; but matter circling it completes an orbit in minutes rather than days, and brightness and structure churn throughout hours-long exposures, like photographing a puppy that refuses to sit still. A plasma scattering screen in the Galactic plane smears detail and must be modeled away, dust forces millimetre-wave observing, and only supercomputer processing cross-checked by independent imaging teams settled the final picture.

如果一个人掉进黑洞，会经历什么？

What would happen to a person falling into a black hole?

最先是潮汐力把他拉成面条一样的长条：脚端的引力比头端强得多，身体被两头拉伸。越靠近超大质量黑洞，视界附近反而越平缓，但一旦越过视界，就永远无法返回，外界的观察者也再也收不到他的任何信号。

事件视界是什么，它与黑洞的大小有何关系？

What is the event horizon and how does it relate to a black hole's size?

事件视界是时空中的单向边界，任何越过它的物质或光都无法返回。对于不带电、不旋转的史瓦西黑洞，视界半径为 2GM 除以 c 平方，正比于质量——太阳质量黑洞约 3 公里，而星系中心超大质量黑洞可达数光时尺度。
The event horizon is a one-way boundary in spacetime; nothing that crosses it, even light, can return. For a non-rotating Schwarzschild black hole, its radius is 2GM/c², proportional to mass—about 3 km for a solar-mass hole and up to light-hours for a supermassive one.

黑洞本身是黑的，它周围为什么反而特别亮？

A black hole itself is black, so why is its surroundings extremely bright?

那圈亮光来自黑洞外面的吸积盘。落向黑洞的气体一边旋转一边被压缩和摩擦，温度升到几百万度甚至更高，发出强烈的光、紫外线甚至X射线。我们看到的亮光其实是视界之外正在下坠的气体，而不是黑洞本身。
The brightness comes from the accretion disk outside the hole. Gas spiralling inward is compressed and heated by friction until it reaches millions of degrees, pouring out intense light, ultraviolet, and even X-rays. What we see glowing is the gas still falling in, just outside the event horizon — not the black hole itself.

天文学家凭什么确信银河系中心潜藏着一个约四百万倍太阳质量的超大质量黑洞?

Why are astronomers convinced that a supermassive black hole of about four million solar masses lurks at the center of the Milky Way?

最直接的证据来自恒星轨道:近红外自适应光学与干涉测量跟踪到一群恒星围绕看不见的人马座A*运转,S2星每十六年掠过一次近心点,速度高达每秒七千余公里;由开普勒第三定律推算,这个引力焦点集中着约四百万倍太阳质量,又被约束在百余天文单位的狭小范围内,如此高的致密度排除了暗星团等替代解释,唯有一个超大质量黑洞最为合理。Genzel 与 Ghez 因此获得2020年诺贝尔物理学奖。
The decisive evidence comes from stellar orbits. Infrared adaptive optics and interferometry have traced dozens of stars racing around an invisible radio source, Sagittarius A*: the star S2 sweeps through pericenter every sixteen years at more than seven thousand kilometres per second on a clean Keplerian ellipse. Kepler's third law pins about four million solar masses inside a region about a hundred astronomical units across, far too dense for any hidden cluster of dark remnants and leaving a supermassive black hole as the only sensible reading. Genzel and Ghez shared the 2020 Nobel Prize in Physics for exactly this decades-long campaign.
