引力弹弓（引力助推）是怎样省燃料的？

How does a gravity assist (slingshot) save fuel?

探测器从行星旁掠过时，借助行星的引力改变自身速度和方向，就像被甩了一下的弹弓。相对太阳来看，探测器可以『窃取』行星的一小部分轨道动量来加速或减速，而行星自身几乎不受影响。旅行者号正是靠多次引力助推才飞出太阳系。
When a probe swings past a planet, the planet's gravity bends and changes its speed and direction, like a slingshot. In the Sun's frame the craft 'steals' a tiny bit of the planet's orbital momentum to speed up or slow down, while the planet is essentially unaffected. The Voyagers used repeated assists to leave the solar system.

低轨卫星为什么最终会坠回大气？

Why do low-orbit satellites eventually fall back into the atmosphere?

即使在数百公里高空，仍有极稀薄的大气分子产生微弱阻力，使卫星逐渐损失轨道能量、轨道高度缓慢下降。高度越低阻力越大，形成正反馈，最终再入烧毁。因此空间站需要定期抬升轨道来『续命』。
Even hundreds of kilometers up, a trace of atmosphere creates tiny drag that slowly bleeds orbital energy and lowers altitude. Lower altitude means more drag, a positive feedback that ends in reentry and burn-up. That is why stations periodically raise their orbit to stay alive.

火箭常用的推进剂有哪些种类？

What kinds of propellants do rockets commonly use?

火箭推进剂分液体和固体两大类：液体如液氧-煤油、液氢-液氧，比冲高、可节流关断；固体燃料结构简单、响应快但难控制。还有剧毒但可常温存贮的肼类，以及正在发展的甲烷燃料。不同任务会权衡性能、成本和安全性来选型。
Rocket propellants split into liquid and solid types: liquids like LOX-kerosene or LH2-LOX give high efficiency and throttle control, while solids are simple and quick but hard to control. There are also storable hydrazines and the emerging methane fuels. Missions weigh performance, cost and safety when choosing.

霍曼转移轨道为何是行星际航行中最省燃料的轨道？

Why is the Hohmann transfer orbit the most fuel-efficient path for interplanetary travel?

霍曼转移是一条与起始轨道和目标轨道同时相切的半椭圆轨道，只需在两个切点做两次速度脉冲，就利用最少的速度增量在共面圆轨道间转移。它用尽引力场的自然几何，使燃料消耗最小，是多数探测器的基本轨道方案。
A Hohmann transfer is a half-ellipse tangent to both the departure and arrival circular orbits, requiring only two velocity impulses at the tangent points to move between coplanar circular orbits with the least delta-v. By exploiting the natural geometry of the gravity field it minimizes fuel use and is the basic trajectory for most probes.

飞船返回地球时为什么会被烧得通红？

Why does a returning spacecraft glow red-hot?

返回舱以每秒约7.8公里的高速冲入大气，与空气剧烈摩擦并压缩空气，使前端温度骤升到上千摄氏度。这主要靠激波加热而非单纯摩擦。特殊防热材料会烧蚀牺牲自己，保护舱内的人和仪器。
A returning capsule plunges into the atmosphere at about 7.8 km/s, and compression plus friction heat its front to over a thousand degrees Celsius. The heating is mainly from shock compression, not simple friction. Ablative heat shields sacrifice themselves to protect crew and instruments.

国际空间站（ISS）为什么不会掉回地球？

Why doesn't the International Space Station (ISS) fall back down to Earth?

国际空间站在约 400 公里高度以约每秒 7.7 公里的速度绕地球做圆周运动，它的下落正好被地球弯曲的曲面接住，于是持续绕圈而不坠地。稀薄大气仍有阻力，所以需要货运飞船偶尔抬升轨道。
The ISS orbits about 400 km up at roughly 7.7 km/s, fast enough that its fall is continually matched by the curve of Earth's surface, so it keeps circling instead of dropping. Thin atmospheric drag still slows it, so cargo ships periodically reboost its orbit.

为什么发射火星探测器要等约26个月的窗口？

Why do Mars missions wait for a launch window every about 26 months?

地球和火星绕太阳公转速度不同，二者相对位置不断变化。只有当地球追上并大致与火星成特定角度时，用最省燃料的转移轨道才能高效抵达，这种最佳几何配置约每26个月出现一次。错过就要再等两年多。
Earth and Mars orbit at different speeds, so their relative positions keep changing. Only when Earth catches up and aligns at a specific angle can a fuel-efficient transfer orbit reach Mars, and this favorable geometry recurs about every 26 months. Miss it and you wait over two years.

为什么火箭需要多级才能把载荷送上太空？

Why do rockets need multiple stages to send payloads into space?

单级火箭要带着全部燃料和空壳飞完全程，质量太大难以达到轨道速度。多级火箭在每层燃料烧完后抛掉空壳，减轻后续质量，让剩余发动机更高效加速，这种丢包袱的策略是实现入轨的关键。
A single-stage rocket must carry all its fuel and empty tanks the whole way, making it too massive to reach orbital speed. A multi-stage rocket drops the empty shells after each stage burns out, lightening what remains so the surviving engines accelerate more efficiently — this staging is the key to reaching orbit.

第一宇宙速度是什么意思？

What is the first cosmic velocity?

第一宇宙速度约为每秒7.9公里，是物体在地球表面附近绕地球做圆周运动所需的最小速度，也就是成为人造卫星的速度。低于它就会落回地面，达到它就能进入近地轨道。再快到第二宇宙速度（约11.2公里每秒）就能逃离地球。
The first cosmic velocity is about 7.9 kilometers per second, the minimum speed needed to circle Earth near its surface and become a satellite. Below it the object falls back; at it, it enters low orbit. Faster still, at the second cosmic velocity of about 11.2 km/s, it escapes Earth entirely.

地球同步卫星为什么能『停』在天上不动？

Why can a geostationary satellite seem to hang still in the sky?

地球同步卫星运行在赤道上空约36000公里处，轨道周期恰好等于地球自转周期（24小时），方向与地球自转相同。从地面看，它始终停在同一经度上空，犹如静止。通信、电视和气象卫星常采用这种轨道。
A geostationary satellite orbits about 36,000 kilometers above the equator with a period equal to Earth's 24-hour rotation and in the same direction. From the ground it appears fixed over one longitude, as if motionless. Communications, TV and weather satellites often use this orbit.

什么是太空垃圾？它有什么危害？

What is space debris, and why is it a problem?

太空垃圾是失效卫星、火箭残骸和碰撞产生的碎片，主要聚集在近地轨道。它们以极高速度飞行，哪怕小碎片撞击卫星也会造成破坏。数量增多还可能引发链式碰撞，使轨道越来越危险。
Space debris is defunct satellites, spent rocket stages and fragments from collisions, concentrated in low Earth orbit. Moving at extreme speeds, even tiny pieces can wreck a satellite on impact. Growing numbers risk a chain reaction of collisions that makes orbits ever more hazardous.

火箭为什么通常垂直发射？

Why do rockets usually launch vertically?

火箭需要尽快穿过稠密的大气层以减少阻力和结构受力，垂直上升能最快获得高度。它还要加速到极高的水平速度才能入轨，垂直起飞便于随后逐步转向。若水平滑跑起飞，大气阻力和气动控制会困难得多。
A rocket must climb through the dense lower atmosphere quickly to reduce drag and structural stress, and vertical ascent gains altitude fastest. It still needs huge horizontal speed to orbit, so it pitches over gradually after liftoff. Horizontal takeoff would face far harder drag and control problems.

什么是逃逸速度？

What is escape velocity?

逃逸速度是从某天体表面出发、无需后续动力就能摆脱其引力束缚所需的最小初速度。地球的逃逸速度约每秒11.2公里，月球仅约2.4公里每秒。质量越大、半径越小，逃逸速度越高，例如黑洞附近的逃逸速度超过光速。
Escape velocity is the minimum starting speed needed to break free of a body's gravity without further thrust. For Earth it is about 11.2 km/s, but for the Moon only about 2.4 km/s. The larger the mass and the smaller the radius, the higher it is; near a black hole it exceeds the speed of light.

火箭为什么能在太空中推进？

How can a rocket propel itself in space?

火箭依靠动量守恒，也就是牛顿第三定律工作：它向后高速喷出气体，气体给火箭一个向前的反作用力。太空是真空并不影响这一过程，因为推进不依赖空气，只依赖喷出物质本身。这正是火箭比飞机更适合航天的原因。
A rocket works by conservation of momentum, Newton's third law: it ejects gas backward at high speed and the gas pushes the rocket forward. Vacuum does not matter, because propulsion relies on the expelled mass, not on surrounding air. That is why rockets, not airplanes, are suited to space.

人造卫星为什么常用太阳能板供电？

Why do satellites use solar panels for power?

太空中有近乎不间断的阳光，太阳能板可以把光能直接转成电能，为卫星长期供电。太空接近真空且没有天气遮挡，光伏效率稳定可靠。少数深入太阳系外、阳光微弱的探测器才会改用核电池。
In space sunlight is nearly continuous, so solar panels convert light directly into electricity to power a satellite for years. The near-vacuum and lack of weather make photovoltaic power steady and reliable. Only probes that travel far from the Sun, where sunlight is weak, switch to nuclear batteries.

国际空间站离地面有多高？

How high above Earth is the International Space Station?

国际空间站运行在约400公里高的近地轨道上，大约每90分钟绕地球一圈。这个高度仍在稀薄的大气层内，因此空间站需要定期点火抬升轨道以抵消阻力。从地面用肉眼有时能看到它像一颗缓慢移动的亮星划过夜空。
The International Space Station orbits at about 400 kilometers above Earth, circling the planet roughly every 90 minutes. This altitude is still inside the thin upper atmosphere, so the station periodically fires thrusters to counter drag. From the ground it can sometimes be seen by eye as a slow-moving bright star.

什么是轨道？卫星为什么不会掉下来？

What is an orbit, and why don't satellites fall down?

轨道是物体以足够高的横向速度绕另一天体做曲线运动时形成的路径。卫星并非不掉下来，而是以约每秒8公里的速度持续『下落』，只是地球表面也在它脚下同步弯曲，于是它永远落不到地面。这就像水平抛出的石头，速度越快落点越远，快到一定程度就绕地球转圈了。
An orbit is the path an object follows when it moves sideways fast enough to curve around another body. A satellite is not failing to fall; it is constantly 'falling' at about 8 kilometers per second while Earth's surface curves away beneath it, so it never reaches the ground. Like a stone thrown horizontally, the faster it goes the farther it lands, and beyond a certain speed it circles the Earth.
