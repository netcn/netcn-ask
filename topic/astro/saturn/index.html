土卫二恩克拉多斯为何被认为可能宜居？

Why is Enceladus thought possibly habitable?

土卫二南极喷出的冰喷泉含有水汽、盐分和简单有机分子，表明其冰壳之下存在液态海洋。潮汐加热为这片地下海提供了能量，而有机物和液态水是生命的基本要素。卡西尼号曾在喷流中检测到构成生命相关的化学成分。
Enceladus shoots ice plumes from its south pole containing water vapor, salts and simple organics, evidence of a liquid ocean beneath the ice. Tidal heating supplies energy to that subsurface sea, and organics plus liquid water are the ingredients of life. Cassini detected life-relevant chemistry in the plumes.

卡西尼号任务结束时为什么选择撞向土星？

Why did the Cassini mission choose to crash into Saturn at its end?

卡西尼号燃料即将耗尽，若任其漂流可能撞上可能有地下海的土卫二或泰坦，带来地球生物污染的风险。科学家因此让它主动冲入土星大气焚毁，这被称为『大决战』。这样既保护了可能孕育生命的卫星，又获得了最后一批大气探测数据。
With its fuel nearly gone, Cassini might have drifted into Enceladus or Titan, moons that may hide subsurface oceans, risking contamination by Earth life. Scientists instead sent it plunging into Saturn's atmosphere to burn up, the 'Grand Finale'. This protected possibly habitable moons and gathered final atmospheric data.

土卫六泰坦（Titan）上有什么让科学家着迷？

What about Titan fascinates scientists?

泰坦拥有浓厚氮气大气和液态甲烷湖泊与河流，是太阳系中除地球外唯一表面有稳定液体的天体，被视为研究前生命化学的天然实验室。
Titan has a thick nitrogen atmosphere and lakes and rivers of liquid methane—the only body besides Earth with stable surface liquids—making it a natural lab for prebiotic chemistry.

土卫六泰坦有什么奇特之处？

What is unusual about Titan, Saturn's moon?

泰坦是土星最大的卫星，也是太阳系中唯一拥有浓厚大气的卫星，大气以氮为主，表面还有液态甲烷形成的湖泊和河流。它的环境类似早期地球，被认为可能具备孕育生命的条件。探测器『卡西尼-惠更斯』曾登陆其表面。
Titan is Saturn's largest moon and the only moon with a thick atmosphere, mainly nitrogen, and it has lakes and rivers of liquid methane on its surface. Its environment resembles early Earth and may offer conditions for life. The Cassini-Huygens mission even landed a probe there.

土卫二恩克拉多斯（Enceladus）喷出的喷泉是什么？

What are the geysers erupting from Enceladus?

土卫二南极的裂缝喷出含水的冰晶羽流，源自冰壳下的液态海洋，其中检测到有机物和氢气，暗示可能具备支持生命的化学条件。
Fissures at Enceladus's south pole jet plumes of water-ice crystals from a subsurface ocean; organics and hydrogen detected there hint at chemistry that could support life.

土星为什么能浮在水面上？

Why could Saturn float in water?

土星的平均密度约为每立方厘米0.69克，比水的密度还小，所以理论上若有足够大的水池，土星能漂浮。这是因为它主要由氢氦等轻质气体构成，没有致密固态表面。它的体积虽大，质量却相对『轻』。
Saturn's average density is about 0.69 grams per cubic centimeter, less than water, so in theory it would float in a large enough bath. This is because it is made mostly of light gases like hydrogen and helium with no dense solid surface. Despite its huge size, it is relatively 'light'.

土星环主要由什么构成？

What are Saturn's rings mainly made of?

土星环几乎全由无数冰粒和岩石碎块组成，大小从微米到数米不等，在阳光下反射出明亮的光环。
Saturn's rings are made almost entirely of countless ice particles and rock fragments ranging from micrometers to meters across, which reflect sunlight into bright rings.

土星环是由什么组成的？

What are Saturn's rings made of?

土星环主要由无数冰块和岩石碎屑构成，大小从微米级的尘埃到数米甚至更大的冰块都有。这些物质在土星赤道面上绕其高速旋转，因反射阳光而显得明亮。环的整体厚度通常只有几十米。
Saturn's rings are made of countless chunks of ice and rock debris, ranging from micron-sized dust to blocks meters across. These particles orbit rapidly in Saturn's equatorial plane and shine by reflecting sunlight. The rings are usually only tens of meters thick overall.
