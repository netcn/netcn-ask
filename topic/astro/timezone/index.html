为什么有些国家会实行夏令时？

Why do some countries use daylight saving time?

夏令时把时钟拨快一小时，让傍晚多利用日光、减少照明用电；并非所有国家都采用，且起止日期不同。
Daylight saving shifts clocks forward an hour to use evening daylight and cut lighting energy; not all countries use it, and start/end dates vary.

为什么中国全国统一用北京时间？

Why does all of China use Beijing Time?

为便于全国协调，中国跨多个地理时区却统一采用东八区（北京时间），因此西部实际日出比时钟显示晚很多。
For national coordination China spans several geographic zones but uses one time (UTC+8), so the west sees sunrise much later by the clock.

什么是“国际日期变更线”？

What is the International Date Line?

它大致沿180度经线，向东跨过它日期减一天，向西跨过加一天，用来避免环球旅行时日期混乱。
Roughly along the 180° meridian, crossing it eastward subtracts a day and westward adds one, preventing date confusion when traveling around the world.

为什么有的地方比别的地方时间早或晚？

Why do some places have earlier or later times?

地球自西向东自转，不同经度看到太阳升起的时间不同，于是按经度划分时区，每15度大约差1小时。
Earth rotates eastward, so different longitudes see sunrise at different times; time zones are set by longitude, about 1 hour per 15 degrees.
