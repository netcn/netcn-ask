为什么探测超新星爆发的中微子对验证恒星坍缩模型至关重要？

Why is detecting supernova neutrinos crucial for testing stellar collapse models?

在核心坍缩超新星中，约 99% 的引力束缚能以中微子形式在数秒内释放，这是驱动爆发与合成重元素的能量来源，但光子要到数小时后才透出。1987A 超新星仅记录到 19 颗中微子，已能约束原中子星冷却时标；下一代探测器有望在银心事件中获得数万计数，从而区分不同爆炸机制。
In a core-collapse supernova, about 99% of the released gravitational binding energy emerges as neutrinos within seconds, powering the explosion and the synthesis of heavy elements, while photons escape only hours later. The 19 neutrinos from SN 1987A already constrained the protoneutron star cooling timescale, and next-generation detectors could record tens of thousands from a Galactic event, distinguishing rival explosion mechanisms.

中微子在「多信使」观测中相比引力波和电磁波有何独特优势？

What unique advantages do neutrinos offer in multimessenger observations compared with gravitational waves and light?

中微子几乎不与物质相互作用，因此能直接穿出致密天体（如坍缩超新星核心或并合喷流）的内部，而光子会被外层物质吸收、引力波对对称坍缩不敏感。这一穿透性使中微子成为窥视极端高温高密度环境的唯一探针，但代价是探测需要巨型地下探测器且难以精确定位。
Neutrinos interact so weakly that they escape directly from the interiors of dense objects such as collapsing supernova cores or merger jets, whereas photons are absorbed by overlying material and gravitational waves are blind to spherically symmetric collapse. This transparency makes neutrinos the only probe of extreme temperature and density environments, at the cost of requiring huge underground detectors and offering poor localization.

费米与 INTEGRAL 卫星在 GW170817 的伽马射线探测中提供了哪些关键约束？

What key constraints did the Fermi and INTEGRAL satellites provide on the gamma rays from GW170817?

费米 GBM 在引力波到达后约 1.7 秒探测到短伽马射线暴 GRB 170817A，确立了双中子星并合与短伽马暴的因果联系；INTEGRAL 则给出严格的伽马射线非探测上限，排除了明亮的普通短暴。两者共同表明该并合喷流相对地球观测者偏向较宽角度或能量偏低。
Fermi's GBM detected the short gamma-ray burst GRB 170817A only about 1.7 seconds after the gravitational waves, establishing the causal link between binary neutron star mergers and short gamma-ray bursts. INTEGRAL supplied a stringent non-detection upper limit, ruling out a bright ordinary short burst and indicating that the jet was viewed far off-axis or was intrinsically weak.

GW170817 事件为什么在天文学上具有里程碑意义？

Why is the GW170817 event a milestone in astronomy?

GW170817 是 2017 年首次被探测到的双中子星并合事件，LIGO/Virgo 先捕捉到引力波，随后全球望远镜在电磁波段找到了对应的短伽马射线暴和千新星。它首次实现了引力波与电磁辐射的联合观测，并证实了重元素（如金、铂）可由 r-过程在并合中合成。
GW170817 was the first detected merger of two neutron stars, recorded in 2017 when LIGO/Virgo captured the gravitational waves and telescopes worldwide then found the matching short gamma-ray burst and kilonova in light. It achieved the first joint gravitational-wave and electromagnetic observation and confirmed that heavy elements such as gold and platinum are forged by the r-process during such mergers.

IceCube 中微子天文台是通过什么方式捕捉宇宙中微子的？

How does the IceCube Neutrino Observatory detect cosmic neutrinos?

IceCube 建在南极冰盖之下，利用一立方千米的纯净冰体作为探测介质，当高能中微子与冰中原子核作用产生缪子时，会在冰中留下蓝色切伦科夫光，被光纤传感器阵列记录。它已探测到来自深空弥漫的天体中微子流，并把一颗高能中微子追溯到耀变体 TXS 0506+056。
IceCube is buried beneath the Antarctic ice sheet and uses a cubic kilometer of pure ice as detector medium; when a high-energy neutrino interacts with a nucleus to produce a muon, the muon emits blue Cherenkov light recorded by an array of optical sensors. It has detected a diffuse flux of astrophysical neutrinos and traced one high-energy neutrino back to the blazar TXS 0506+056.

科学家是如何把一颗高能中微子与耀变体 TXS 0506+056 联系起来的？

How did scientists link a high-energy neutrino to the blazar TXS 0506+056?

2017 年 IceCube 探测到一颗高能缪子中微子（IceCube-170922A），其到来方向指向耀变体 TXS 0506+056，而该天体当时正发生伽马射线耀发，多波段望远镜的协同观测显著提高了关联的可信度。此外回溯分析还发现该源在 2014–2015 年间曾有中微子超出，支持其作为宇宙线加速源的身份。
In 2017 IceCube detected a high-energy muon neutrino, IceCube-170922A, whose arrival direction pointed back to the blazar TXS 0506+056, which was then undergoing a gamma-ray flare, and coordinated multiwavelength observations strengthened the association. A retrospective analysis also found a neutrino excess from this source in 2014–2015, supporting its role as a cosmic-ray accelerator.

天文学中的「千新星」指的是什么现象？

What phenomenon does a kilonova describe in astronomy?

千新星是双中子星或中子星—黑洞并合后产生的光学/红外暂现源，其亮度约为新星的一千倍而得名。并合抛出的富中子物质通过 r-过程合成大量金、铂等重元素，并在数天到数周内逐渐变红变暗。
A kilonova is an optical and infrared transient produced after the merger of two neutron stars or a neutron star and a black hole, so named because its brightness is roughly a thousand times that of a classical nova. The neutron-rich ejecta forge large amounts of gold, platinum and other heavy elements via the r-process, glowing and fading over days to weeks.

什么是「多信使天文学」？

What is multimessenger astronomy?

多信使天文学指同时利用引力波、电磁辐射、中微子和宇宙线等多种「信使」来观测同一天体事件的研究方法。通过比对不同信使携带的信息，科学家能更完整地还原天体物理过程的物理本质，例如并合与爆发事件的内部机制。
Multimessenger astronomy observes the same astrophysical event through multiple messengers such as gravitational waves, electromagnetic radiation, neutrinos, and cosmic rays. By comparing the information each messenger carries, scientists can reconstruct the physical nature of events like mergers and explosions far more completely than with light alone.
