折射镜、反射镜、折反镜各有什么优缺点？

What are the pros and cons of refractors, reflectors and catadioptrics?

折射镜成像锐利好维护但大口径贵；反射镜口径大性价比高但需偶尔调光轴；折反镜紧凑便携适合摄影但价格较高。
Refractors give sharp, low-maintenance views but cost more per inch; reflectors are affordable and large but need collimation; catadioptrics are compact for imaging but pricier.

买天文望远镜最该看什么参数？

What matters most when buying a telescope?

口径（物镜直径）最重要，它决定集光能力和能看清多暗多细的目标，比放大倍数更关键。
Aperture (objective diameter) matters most—it sets light-gathering and detail, more important than magnification.

新手该买望远镜还是双筒望远镜？

Should a beginner buy a telescope or binoculars?

入门建议先买一副好的双筒望远镜，轻便易用、视野广，适合认星座和看流星；再考虑天文望远镜。
Beginners should start with good binoculars—light, easy, wide-field, great for constellations and meteors—before an astronomical telescope.
