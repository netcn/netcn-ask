国际天球参考系（ICRF）有什么作用？

What is the role of the International Celestial Reference Frame (ICRF)?

ICRF以遥远的类星体为基准定义天球坐标系，是天体位置和导航的精确框架。它基于甚长基线干涉测量测定的射电源，取代了更早的动力学参考系。
The ICRF defines the celestial coordinate system using distant quasars as anchors, providing the precise framework for object positions and navigation. Based on VLBI-measured radio sources, it replaced earlier dynamical reference frames.

什么是恒星的“自行”，其中运动最快的恒星是哪颗？

What is a star's “proper motion,” and which star moves fastest?

自行指恒星相对于太阳在天空平面上的表观横向运动，需长期精确观测才能察觉。自行最大的已知恒星是巴纳德星，每年在天空中移动约10.3角秒，是距离我们仅约6光年的红矮星，几百年的观测就足以看出明显位移。
Proper motion is a star's apparent sideways drift across the sky relative to the Sun, detectable only with long, precise observations. The fastest known is Barnard's Star, moving about 10.3 arcseconds per year; a red dwarf just ~6 light-years away whose shift becomes obvious over mere centuries.

欧空局的盖亚（Gaia）卫星在做什么？

What is ESA's Gaia satellite doing?

盖亚以极高精度测量超过十亿颗恒星的位置、视差、自行和亮度，绘制出最详尽的银河系三维地图，刷新了我们对银河系结构的认识。
Gaia measures positions, parallaxes, proper motions, and brightness for over a billion stars with extreme precision, building the most detailed 3D map of the Milky Way and reshaping our view of its structure.

What has ESA's Gaia mission achieved in astrometry?

盖亚卫星自2013年运行，以前所未有的精度测量了超过十亿颗恒星的位置、自行和视差，绘制出最详尽的银河系三维星图。这些数据大幅刷新了我们对银河系结构、恒星距离和动态历史的认识。

恒星自行指的是什么？

What does a star's proper motion refer to?

自行是恒星在天球上相对于背景的横向位置变化，反映它穿越空间的实际运动，常与视差、径向速度一起构建恒星的立体运动。
Proper motion is a star's transverse shift across the sky relative to the background, reflecting its true motion through space; with parallax and radial velocity it builds a full 3D motion.

周年视差怎样把恒星距离测量挂接到地球公转轨道上?为何地面视差观测长期以来只对较近的恒星有效?

How does annual parallax hook stellar distance measurement onto Earth's orbit, and why did ground-based parallax work long stay effective mainly for nearby stars?

恒星在相隔半年两次观测之间的角位移等于日地平均距离在该星处的张角,1 角秒视差定义为 1 秒差距,约合 3.26 光年。大气湍流与折射把单站地面测量的精度压在几十毫角秒,可靠的视差样本长期局限于几百光年之内。伊巴谷天测卫星把精度推进到约 1 毫角秒,盖亚巡天进一步进入亚毫角秒区间,使数以亿计的恒星拥有了纯几何距离。
The angular shift of a star between observations taken six months apart equals the angle subtended by the mean Earth-Sun distance at that star; one arcsecond of parallax defines one parsec, roughly 3.26 light-years. Atmospheric turbulence and refraction cap single-site ground-based precision at a few dozen milliarcseconds, confining reliable parallax samples to within a few hundred light-years for generations. The Hipparcos astrometry satellite pushed accuracy to about one milliarcsecond, and the Gaia survey now reaches sub-milliarcsecond level, giving hundreds of millions of stars purely geometric distances.

依巴谷卫星和盖亚任务的天体测量观测如何定义和改进了恒星的参考系?

How did astrometric observations from Hipparcos and Gaia define and refine the reference frame for stars?

天体测量的核心是建立一个惯性参考系,并把恒星位置、视差和自行以毫角秒乃至微角秒精度锚定在其中。1989 年发射的依巴谷卫星首次从太空完成全天巡测,给出了约 11.8 万颗恒星的精密星表;欧空局的盖亚任务在此基础上对约二十亿颗恒星重复扫描,亮源的单星视差精度可达数十微角秒。这些数据不仅让距离阶梯的第一级——三角视差——变得系统可靠,还通过准星星表把光学参考系与河外射电源定义的国际天球参考系直接连接起来。
The core task of astrometry is to establish an inertial reference frame and anchor stellar positions, parallaxes, and proper motions within it at milliarcsecond or even microarcsecond precision. Launched in 1989, Hipparcos performed the first all-sky survey from space, delivering a precise catalog of about 118,000 stars; ESA's Gaia mission repeated this scanning scheme for roughly two billion stars, reaching parallax accuracies of tens of microarcseconds for bright sources. These data make the first rung of the distance ladder—trigonometric parallax—systematically reliable, and through quasar catalogs they tie the optical frame directly to the International Celestial Reference Frame defined by extragalactic radio sources.

什么是天体测量学？

What is astrometry?

天体测量学是精确测量天体位置、距离和运动的分支学科。它为基础天文学提供恒星的自行、视差和历表，是导航与参考系的基石。
Astrometry is the branch of astronomy that precisely measures the positions, distances, and motions of celestial objects. It provides stellar proper motions, parallaxes, and ephemerides, underpinning navigation and reference frames.

天文学里的标准烛光是什么意思？

What does a standard candle mean in astronomy?

标准烛光是已知真实亮度的天体（如造父变星、Ia 型超新星），由观测到的明暗反推距离，是丈量宇宙尺度的核心工具。
A standard candle is an object of known intrinsic brightness (like Cepheids or Type Ia supernovae); comparing its apparent dimness to true brightness gives distance—a core tool for cosmic measuring.

什么是恒星视差？它如何测量距离？

What is stellar parallax, and how does it measure distance?

地球绕日公转时，近处恒星相对遥远背景会显出微小位移，半年视角差越大说明距离越近，视差角是距离（秒差距）的倒数。
As Earth orbits the Sun, nearby stars shift slightly against distant backgrounds; a larger half-year parallax angle means a closer star, with distance in parsecs equal to the reciprocal of the angle in arcseconds.
