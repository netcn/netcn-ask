什么是费米悖论？它提出了哪些可能的解答方向？

What is the Fermi paradox, and what possible resolutions have been proposed?

费米悖论指出：如果银河系中普遍存在地外文明，那么它们应该早已遍及整个星系，但人类至今没有观测到任何确凿的外星文明信号。可能的解释大致分为几类。其一，生命和文明在宇宙中极其罕见，地球甚至可能是孤例；其二，技术文明往往因战争、资源枯竭或自毁等原因在实现星际扩张前就消亡；其三，高等文明可能故意隐藏自己或放弃星际扩张。此外，也不排除人类当前搜索信号的方式本身存在局限。
The Fermi paradox notes that if extraterrestrial civilizations were common in the Milky Way, they should have spread across the whole galaxy, yet no confirmed alien signal has ever been detected. Proposed resolutions fall into several broad categories. Some argue that life and civilization are extraordinarily rare, possibly even unique to Earth; others that technological civilizations tend to self-destruct through war, resource depletion or self-inflicted catastrophe before interstellar expansion; still others that advanced civilizations deliberately conceal themselves or forgo expansion. A further possibility is that our current search strategies are simply too limited.

地球生命几乎只用左旋氨基酸构建蛋白质。这种“手性均一”现象为何被视为生命化学的重要特征？关于它最初如何产生，目前有哪些主流假说？

Terrestrial life builds proteins almost exclusively from left-handed amino acids. Why is this 'homochirality' regarded as an essential signature of living chemistry, and what are the leading hypotheses for how it first arose?

生物聚合物的有序结构依赖构象单一：若氨基酸旋光混杂，肽链难以折叠成稳定的活性形态，核酸螺旋也会失稳，因此旋光纯度被视为识别生命的化学判据。其起源尚无定论：主流假说包括恒星形成区圆偏振紫外光对两种对映体的选择性光解留下初始失衡、方解石等矿物表面对单一手性的选择性吸附富集，以及自催化反应把微小失衡指数式放大为宏观旋光纯度。
The orderly architecture of biopolymers demands uniform handedness: with mixed-chirality amino acids, peptide chains cannot fold into stable functional shapes and nucleic-acid helices lose integrity, so optical purity stands among the chemical criteria used to identify life. Its origin remains unresolved. Leading hypotheses include enantioselective photolysis by circularly polarized ultraviolet light from star-forming regions leaving an initial imbalance between mirror forms, preferential adsorption of one hand on mineral surfaces such as calcite, and autocatalytic networks amplifying a tiny initial excess exponentially into macroscopic homochirality.

土卫二羽流冰粒中为何含有复杂有机大分子？这对冰壳之下海洋的宜居性意味着什么？

Why do ice grains in Enceladus's plume contain complex organic macromolecules, and what does that mean for the habitability of the ocean beneath its ice shell?

卡西尼号尘埃分析仪在飞越羽流时，检出质量超过200道尔顿、难溶于水的复杂有机大分子，性质酷似有机物受高温或水热作用后留下的难溶残渣。这表明冰壳下的海水正与多孔岩核发生高温水热反应，为深层海洋的复杂碳化学提供能量与原料。不过非生命途径同样能生成此类分子，它们只证明化学环境的潜力，并不构成存在生命的证据。
During its plume flybys, Cassini's dust analyzer registered insoluble organic macromolecules heavier than 200 daltons, resembling refractory residues left when organic matter is heated or processed hydrothermally. Their presence implies that seawater beneath the ice shell reacts with a porous rocky core at high temperature, supplying energy and raw material for complex carbon chemistry in the deep ocean. Because abiotic pathways produce such molecules too, they attest to chemical potential rather than proving life.

天文学家所说的生物印记包括哪些信号，如何在系外行星大气中搜寻它们？

What signals do astronomers call biosignatures, and how are they searched for in exoplanet atmospheres?

生物印记指只能由生命活动维持、且非生物过程难以产生的化学不平衡，例如氧气与甲烷在大气中共存。通过透射光谱，望远镜在行星凌星时分析穿过大气的星光，识别这些气体的吸收特征。德马里等人提出的判据要求多个互补信号同时出现，以避免把地质过程误判为生命。
Biosignatures are chemical imbalances that life can maintain but non-biological processes struggle to produce, such as oxygen and methane coexisting in an atmosphere. Using transit transmission spectroscopy, telescopes analyze starlight passing through the atmosphere to spot these gases' absorption features. Des Marais and colleagues argued that multiple complementary signals must appear together to avoid mistaking geology for life.

2020 年科学家在金星大气中发现了什么可能有生命意义的分子？

What molecule with potential biological significance was reported in Venus' atmosphere in 2020?

2020 年，一个研究团队报告在金星大气约 50 至 60 公里高度探测到磷化氢气体，浓度约为十亿分之二十。在地球上，磷化氢主要由厌氧微生物产生，而当时未找到金星大气中能自然生成它的无机路径，因此它一度被视为候选生物标志物。但后续多个团队对原始探测信号和数据分析方法提出质疑，目前结论仍存在争议。
In 2020, a research team reported detecting phosphine at altitudes of roughly 50 to 60 km in Venus' atmosphere, at a mixing ratio of about 20 parts per billion. On Earth, phosphine is produced mainly by anaerobic microbes, and no known inorganic route could explain it in Venus' clouds at the time, so it was regarded as a candidate biosignature. However, subsequent teams questioned both the detection and the analysis methods, and the claim remains disputed.

Is there evidence yet for life beyond Earth?

迄今尚未发现确凿的地外生命证据，但在火星、木卫二、土卫二等地点找到过可能适宜的条件，搜寻仍在积极进行。
No conclusive evidence of extraterrestrial life exists yet, but Mars, Europa, Enceladus, and others show possibly habitable conditions, and the search is actively ongoing.

土卫二恩塞拉达斯有什么证据表明它存在地下海洋？

What evidence indicates that Enceladus has a subsurface ocean?

卡西尼号探测器多次飞掠土卫二，发现其南极喷出含有水、盐和有机分子的羽状喷流，喷出物质中还有与海底热液活动相关的微粒。这些喷流的源头只能是一个位于冰壳之下、与岩石核接触的液态水海洋。
The Cassini spacecraft flew past Enceladus repeatedly and observed plumes jetting from its south pole that contain water, salts and organic molecules, including grains linked to hydrothermal activity on the seafloor. The only plausible source of these jets is a liquid-water ocean beneath the icy crust, in contact with the rocky core.

什么是嗜极生物？它们对寻找地外生命有什么启发？

What are extremophiles, and how do they inform the search for extraterrestrial life?

嗜极生物是在高温、高压、强酸、高盐或辐射等极端环境中存活的微生物，比如深海热泉口一百多摄氏度的水中生活的细菌。它们证明生命的耐受范围远比想象的大，从而大大拓宽了宇宙中可能存在生命的场所。
Extremophiles are microbes that thrive in extreme conditions such as high temperature, high pressure, strong acidity, high salinity or radiation, for example bacteria living in water above one hundred degrees Celsius at deep-sea hydrothermal vents. They show that life's tolerance is far broader than once thought, widening the range of places where life might exist in the universe.

天文学家如何用韦布望远镜在系外行星大气中寻找生命迹象？

How do astronomers use the James Webb Space Telescope to search for signs of life in exoplanet atmospheres?

当系外行星从恒星前方经过时，星光会穿过行星大气，大气中的分子会留下特征性的指纹，韦布望远镜利用这一点识别水、二氧化碳、甲烷等分子。同时出现氧气和甲烷这种理论上难以长期共存的组合，被认为是可能的生命迹象，但仍需排除非生物成因。
When an exoplanet passes in front of its star, starlight travels through the planet's atmosphere and molecules imprint characteristic fingerprints, which JWST uses to identify water, carbon dioxide and methane. Combinations such as oxygen with methane, which should not persist for long, are treated as possible signs of life, though non-biological origins still have to be ruled out.

德雷克方程是用来估算什么的？它的各个因子代表什么？

What does the Drake equation estimate, and what do its factors represent?

德雷克方程估算银河系中能够与我们通信的文明数量，它把恒星形成率、有行星的恒星比例、每颗恒星宜居行星数、生命出现概率、智慧生命概率、文明寿命等多个未知因子相乘。方程的数值高度不确定，但它把“银河系里有多少地外文明”这一问题拆解成可以逐项研究的科学问题。
The Drake equation estimates the number of communicating civilizations in the Milky Way, multiplying factors such as the star formation rate, the fraction of stars with planets, the number of habitable planets per star, the probability that life and intelligence arise, and the lifetime of civilizations. Although its numerical result is highly uncertain, it breaks the question of extraterrestrial civilizations into terms that can be studied one by one.

地球上的极端微生物（如耐辐射奇球菌）的发现，如何改变了对宜居环境的界定？

How did discoveries of Earth's extremophiles, such as Deinococcus radiodurans, change how we define a habitable environment?

传统上宜居环境以地表液态水和温和温度为准。极端微生物能在强辐射、高温、高盐或深地下的环境中存活并高效修复DNA损伤，说明生命耐受边界远比想象更宽。天体生物学因此把地下卤水、冰壳之下的海洋等先前被排除的环境纳入搜寻范围，把宜居标准改写为液态水、可用能量与关键元素三者兼备。
Habitability was once judged by surface liquid water and mild temperatures. Extremophiles survive strong radiation, high heat, salinity, or deep subsurface conditions and repair DNA damage efficiently, showing life's physical limits are far broader than assumed. Astrobiology therefore includes subsurface brines and oceans beneath ice shells in its searches, redefining habitability as the simultaneous presence of liquid water, usable energy, and biologically key elements.

为什么科学家认为火星曾经有大量液态水？有什么直接证据？

Why do scientists think Mars once had abundant liquid water, and what is the direct evidence?

火星轨道器拍摄到了干涸的河谷网络、扇形三角洲和湖底层状沉积物，它们只有长期流动的液态水才能形成。好奇号与毅力号还在古湖床发现了河流冲刷形成的鹅卵石和只能在水中生成的黏土矿物。
Mars orbiters have imaged dried river valleys, fan-shaped deltas and layered lakebed sediments, which only long-lived flowing water could have created. Rovers Curiosity and Perseverance have additionally found water-worn pebbles and clay minerals that form only in water in ancient lake beds.

胚种论是什么假说，它面临哪些主要的科学质疑？

What is the panspermia hypothesis, and what are its main scientific objections?

胚种论认为生命或其前体分子可能由陨石、彗星或星际尘埃在行星之间传播，从而把生命带到地球。虽然星际有机分子和抗辐射微生物的存在提供了一定支持，但该假说并不能解释生命的起源，只是转移了问题发生地。此外，宇宙射线与再入高温对生命体的杀伤仍是重大障碍。
Panspermia holds that life, or its precursor molecules, may have been carried between planets by meteorites, comets, or interstellar dust, seeding life on Earth. The discovery of interstellar organics and radiation-resistant microbes lends some support, yet the hypothesis does not explain life's origin, only relocates it. Cosmic rays and the heat of atmospheric reentry remain serious obstacles for viable organisms.

为什么天体生物学家把寻找液态水作为判断天体宜居性的首要线索？

Why do astrobiologists treat the presence of liquid water as the primary clue to a world's habitability?

液态水是几乎所有已知生命化学反应的溶剂，细胞内的生化反应都需要在水环境中进行，因此“跟着水找生命”成为行星探测的基本策略。地球之外只要有长期存在的液态水，无论是地下海洋还是表面湖泊，都被视为可能存在生命的地方。
Liquid water is the solvent for nearly all known biochemistry; cellular reactions take place in aqueous environments, so following the water has become the core strategy of planetary exploration. Any world with long-lived liquid water, whether a subsurface ocean or a surface lake, is treated as a potential site for life.
