韦布发现的“过早”大质量星系为何让天文学家困惑？

Why do Webb's 'too-early' massive galaxies puzzle astronomers?

韦布在早期宇宙（z约7-10）看到比模型预测更多、更亮、更成熟的星系，若属实意味着星系形成比想像更快，可能需要修正暗物质晕吸积或恒星初始质量函数。
Webb sees more, brighter, more mature galaxies in the early Universe (z≈7–10) than models predict; if real, galaxy formation was faster than thought, perhaps needing revised dark-matter halo accretion or stellar initial mass function.

韦布首次在系外行星大气中探测到二氧化碳意味着什么？

What does Webb's first CO2 detection in an exoplanet atmosphere mean?

韦布在热木星WASP-39b的透射光谱中明确识别出CO2，是系外行星大气化学的突破，证明红外光谱能揭示行星形成时的盘内位置与成分。
Webb clearly identified CO2 in the transmission spectrum of the hot Jupiter WASP-39b—a breakthrough in exoplanet atmospheric chemistry, proving infrared spectra reveal where and how a planet formed.

NIRSpec和MIRI仪器各擅长什么？

What are NIRSpec and MIRI each good at?

NIRSpec在近红外做高分辨率光谱，分解星系与系外大气谱线；MIRI覆盖中红外，能穿透尘埃看恒星形成与暖尘，二者是韦布科学的核心。
NIRSpec takes high-resolution near-infrared spectra, resolving galaxy and exoplanet atmospheric lines; MIRI covers mid-infrared, seeing through dust to star formation and warm dust—both are central to Webb's science.

詹姆斯·韦布如何改变了我们对早期星系的认识？

How did JWST change our view of early galaxies?

韦布在JADES等巡天中发现红移z大于10、甚至z大于14的明亮星系，其数量与质量比现有宇宙学模型预期更多，挑战了早期星系形成理论。
In surveys like JADES, Webb found bright galaxies at redshifts z>10 and even z>14, more numerous and massive than current cosmological models predict, challenging theories of early galaxy formation.

詹姆斯·韦布发现了宇宙中最古老的星系吗？

Has JWST found the Universe's most ancient galaxies?

韦布在JADES巡天中认证了红移达z约14的星系（如JADES-GS-z14-0），对应宇宙仅约3亿岁时，是目前已知最古老的星系候选之一。
In JADES, Webb certified galaxies at redshift z≈14 (e.g. JADES-GS-z14-0), when the Universe was only about 300 million years old—among the most ancient galaxy candidates known.

詹姆斯·韦布空间望远镜为什么能看清极早期宇宙？

Why can the James Webb Space Telescope see the very early universe clearly?

韦布在红外波段工作，而高红移星系的光被拉长到红外，加上大口径和高灵敏度，它能捕捉到宇宙仅几亿岁时的微弱光芒。
Webb observes in infrared, where high-redshift galaxy light is stretched, and with a large aperture and high sensitivity it catches faint light from when the universe was only a few hundred million years old.

詹姆斯·韦布望远镜为何要在极低温下工作？

Why does JWST work at extremely low temperature?

韦布主要观测红外光，自身热量会淹没信号，因此主镜与仪器被遮阳板冷却到约零下233摄氏度（40开尔文），仅靠被动辐射即可维持。
Webb observes mostly infrared, so its own heat would drown the signal; a sunshield cools the mirror and instruments to about -233°C (40 K) by passive radiation alone.

詹姆斯·韦布空间望远镜为什么选择以红外观测为主,而不是像哈勃那样主打可见光?

Why does the James Webb Space Telescope observe mainly in infrared rather than focusing on visible light like Hubble?

因为它的核心目标——早期宇宙星系、恒星育婴房和冷天体——主要在红外发光。宇宙膨胀把最遥远星系的光拉伸到近红外;红外线又能穿透尘埃帷幕看清恒星形成区;原行星盘等低温结构同样在红外明亮。为此韦布配备6.5米镀金分段主镜与大型遮阳板,在日地第二拉格朗日点冷却到约40K运行。
Because its core targets, galaxies of the early universe, stellar nurseries and cold objects, shine chiefly in infrared. Cosmic expansion stretches light from the most distant galaxies into near-infrared wavelengths, infrared pierces dusty veils to reveal star-forming regions, and cool structures such as protoplanetary disks glow there as well. Webb therefore carries a gold-coated 6.5-meter segmented primary and a large sunshield, running cooled to about 40 kelvin at Sun-Earth Lagrange point 2.
