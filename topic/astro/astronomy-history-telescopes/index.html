事件视界望远镜如何拍到黑洞照片？

How did the Event Horizon Telescope capture black hole images?

事件视界望远镜将分布全球的多台射电望远镜通过甚长基线干涉连成口径相当于地球大小的虚拟望远镜，在1.3毫米波长观测黑洞周围发光的吸积环。结合原子钟同步与海量相关处理，重建出M87*和人马座A*的阴影图像。这是干涉技术和计算成像的巅峰之作。
The Event Horizon Telescope linked radio dishes worldwide via Very Long Baseline Interferometry into a virtual telescope the size of Earth, observing the glowing accretion ring around black holes at 1.3 mm. Using atomic-clock synchronization and massive correlation processing, it reconstructed shadow images of M87* and Sagittarius A*. It is a pinnacle of interferometry and computational imaging.

冰立方中微子望远镜如何工作？

How does the IceCube neutrino telescope work?

冰立方建在南极冰盖下，利用一立方公里的洁净冰作为探测介质，捕捉高能宇宙中微子与冰作用产生的切伦科夫光。它能在几乎全天空范围内识别来自遥远耀变体和超新星的中微子。中微子不带电且几乎不与物质作用，是穿透极强的高能信使。
IceCube is built deep in the Antarctic ice sheet, using a cubic kilometer of clear ice as detector medium to catch Cherenkov light from high-energy cosmic neutrinos interacting with the ice. It identifies neutrinos from distant blazars and supernovae over nearly the full sky. Neutrinos are uncharged and barely interact, making them extremely penetrating high-energy messengers.

视差法是怎么测量恒星距离的？

How does parallax measure the distances to stars?

地球绕太阳公转半年，观测者的位置移动了约3亿公里，近处的恒星相对背景恒星就会有一个微小的角度摆动，这个角度叫视差角。距离（以秒差距计）等于1除以视差角（以角秒计），比邻星的视差约0.77角秒，对应1.3秒差距。依巴谷卫星测了约12万颗恒星，盖亚卫星更是把精度推到微角秒量级。
As Earth orbits the Sun, the observer's position shifts by about 300 million kilometres over half a year, so a nearby star appears to swing by a tiny angle against background stars, called the parallax angle. Distance in parsecs equals one divided by the parallax angle in arcseconds: Proxima Centauri's 0.77-arcsecond parallax puts it at 1.3 parsecs. The Hipparcos satellite measured about 120,000 stars, and Gaia has pushed precision to microarcsecond levels.

夫琅禾费吸收线在恒星光谱学里为什么是奠基性发现？

Why were Fraunhofer absorption lines a foundational discovery in stellar spectroscopy?

1814 年夫琅禾费在太阳光谱中辨认出数百条暗线（后称夫琅禾费线），基尔霍夫与本生随后证明这些线对应特定元素在气体中的吸收。这让天文学家能凭光谱读出恒星的化学成分，开启了天体物理学的化学分析时代。
In 1814 Fraunhofer identified hundreds of dark lines in the solar spectrum (later called Fraunhofer lines), and Kirchhoff and Bunsen then proved they correspond to absorption by specific elements in gas. This let astronomers read a star's chemical composition from its spectrum, opening the chemical-analysis era of astrophysics.

为什么光谱被称作天体的身份证？

Why is the spectrum called the identity card of a celestial body?

每种元素都有自己独一无二的谱线位置，恒星光谱上的暗吸收线就像指纹一样，能指认恒星大气里有哪些元素。谱线的强度反映温度和元素丰度，谱线的移动还能暴露天体的视向速度。通过分析光谱，天文学家能得知恒星的温度、成分、密度甚至磁场，所以它被称为天体的身份证。
Every element has its own unique set of spectral-line positions, and the dark absorption lines in a star's spectrum act like fingerprints identifying which elements sit in its atmosphere. Line strengths reveal temperature and abundances, and line shifts expose radial velocity. By analysing spectra, astronomers learn a star's temperature, composition, density, and even its magnetic field, which is why the spectrum is called its identity card.

阿塔卡马大型毫米波阵列（ALMA）有什么特别？

What is special about the Atacama Large Millimeter Array (ALMA)?

ALMA是由数十台射电天线组成、位于海拔五千米沙漠的毫米波干涉阵，专门探测被尘埃遮蔽的冷气体与恒星形成区。其高分辨率和灵敏度能解析原行星盘的细节与早期星系的尘埃。它是研究宇宙“冷”成分最强大的地面设施之一。
ALMA is a millimeter-wave interferometric array of dozens of radio antennas at 5000 meters altitude in the desert, probing cold gas and star-forming regions hidden by dust. Its resolution and sensitivity resolve protoplanetary disks and dusty early galaxies. It is among the most powerful ground facilities for the universe's “cold” components.

引力透镜如何帮助天文观测？

How does gravitational lensing help astronomical observations?

大质量天体弯曲身后光源的光线，形成放大、增亮甚至多重成像的引力透镜效应。弱透镜的统计扭曲可测绘暗物质分布，强透镜能让我们看到极暗远的星系。它既是天然望远镜，也是探测暗物质的关键工具。
Massive objects bend light from background sources, producing gravitational lensing that magnifies, brightens, or even multiplies their images. Weak-lensing statistics map dark matter distribution, while strong lensing lets us see extremely faint, distant galaxies. It is both a natural telescope and a key tool for detecting dark matter.

自适应光学如何补偿大气扰动？

How does adaptive optics compensate for atmospheric turbulence?

自适应光学用可变形镜面以毫秒级速度实时校正大气湍流造成的波前畸变，通常需要以亮星或激光导星作为参考。它能把地基望远镜的角分辨率提升到接近太空水平。这项技术是现代大口径地基观测不可或缺的部分。
Adaptive optics uses a deformable mirror to correct, in milliseconds, the wavefront distortions caused by atmospheric turbulence, typically referencing a bright star or laser guide star. It pushes ground-based resolution close to space quality. The technique is indispensable for modern large ground-based observations.

为什么望远镜的口径决定了它看得多清楚？

Why does a telescope's aperture decide how sharp its view is?

光通过圆形口径时会发生衍射，任何一个点源都会被展成一个带光晕的小圆斑，其角半径大约为1.22乘以波长除以口径直径。口径越大，衍射斑越小，分辨本领越强，所以想看清细节就必须造更大的镜子；同时口径还决定集光能力，灵敏度随直径的平方增长。这正是天文学家不断建造更大望远镜的原因。
Light diffracts as it passes through a circular aperture, so any point source is smeared into a small haloed disc whose angular radius is roughly 1.22 times the wavelength divided by the aperture diameter. The larger the aperture, the smaller that diffraction disc and the finer the resolution, so sharper views demand bigger mirrors; the aperture also sets the collecting area, with sensitivity growing as the square of the diameter. That is why astronomers keep building ever larger telescopes.

光谱学在天文学中扮演什么角色？

What role does spectroscopy play in astronomy?

光谱学把星光分解成不同波长，从中可读取天体的化学组成、温度、视向速度和磁场等信息。没有光谱，就几乎无法判断遥远天体到底是什么。它是现代天体物理诊断的基石。
Spectroscopy splits starlight into wavelengths, revealing a body's chemical composition, temperature, radial velocity, and magnetic field. Without spectra, we could hardly tell what distant objects actually are. It is the cornerstone of modern astrophysical diagnosis.

射电望远镜能看见哪些可见光看不见的东西？

What can radio telescopes see that visible light cannot?

无线电波能穿透星际尘埃云，让天文学家看到光学望远镜被遮挡的恒星形成区、冷气体和分子云。射电波段还带来了许多划时代的发现，比如1967年发现的脉冲星、1965年发现的宇宙微波背景，以及遥远类星体。500米口径的FAST和智利的ALMA阵列都专门在这一波段工作。
Radio waves pass through interstellar dust clouds, letting astronomers see star-forming regions, cold gas, and molecular clouds that visible light cannot get through. The radio window also brought landmark discoveries: pulsars in 1967, the cosmic microwave background in 1965, and distant quasars. China's 500-metre FAST dish and the ALMA array in Chile both work in this band.

詹姆斯·韦伯空间望远镜主要观测什么？

What does the James Webb Space Telescope mainly observe?

JWST是红外空间望远镜，口径约6.5米，专长于穿透宇宙尘埃并观测极高红移的早期星系。它能解析系外行星大气透射光谱，并拍摄恒星形成区的精细结构。它是哈勃在红外波段的强力继任者。
JWST is an infrared space telescope with a 6.5-meter mirror, specialized in piercing cosmic dust and observing very high-redshift early galaxies. It resolves exoplanet transmission spectra and fine structures of star-forming regions. It is Hubble's powerful infrared successor.

哈勃空间望远镜的哈勃深场为何被视为观测宇宙学的里程碑？

Why is the Hubble Deep Field considered a milestone in observational cosmology?

1995 年哈勃在看似空白的一小块天区长时间曝光，拍到约三千个极遥远的星系，把可观测宇宙的可视历史大幅推前。它首次直观展示星系随红移的演化，并成为后来各类深场巡天的范本。
In 1995 Hubble stared at a seemingly empty patch of sky for a long exposure and captured about three thousand extremely distant galaxies, pushing the visible history of the universe far back. It gave the first direct view of galaxy evolution with redshift and became the template for later deep-field surveys.

钱德拉X射线天文台研究什么？

What does the Chandra X-ray Observatory study?

钱德拉是高分辨率X射线空间望远镜，专门观测宇宙中极高温、高能的过程，如黑洞吸积、星系团热气体和超新星遗迹。X射线被大气吸收，只能在太空中观测。它揭示了暗物质分布与黑洞周围极端环境的细节。
Chandra is a high-resolution X-ray space telescope observing extremely hot, high-energy processes such as black hole accretion, hot cluster gas, and supernova remnants. X-rays are absorbed by the atmosphere, so they must be observed from space. It revealed details of dark matter distribution and extreme environments near black holes.

勒维特发现造父变星的周光关系对测定宇宙尺度有何决定性意义？

What decisive role did Leavitt's discovery of the Cepheid period–luminosity relation play in measuring the scale of the universe?

1912 年勒维特在小麦哲伦云中发现造父变星的光变周期越长、本征亮度越亮，建立起可定标的周光关系。哈勃随后用它测出仙女座星系的距离，证明宇宙远超银河系，从而把星系确立为河外岛屿。
In 1912 Leavitt found in the Small Magellanic Cloud that Cepheid variables with longer pulsation periods are intrinsically brighter, establishing a calibratable period–luminosity relation. Hubble later used it to measure the distance to Andromeda, proving the universe extends far beyond the Milky Way and establishing galaxies as extragalactic islands.

哈勃定律的发现如何改变了人类对宇宙尺度的认识？

How did the discovery of Hubble's law change humanity's view of cosmic scale?

1929年哈勃测得星系退行速度与距离成正比，说明宇宙并非静态而是在膨胀。这一关系首次给出了星系距离的系统化标尺，把宇宙从恒定的背景变成演化的整体。它为日后大爆炸理论的确立铺平了道路，也暗示宇宙有一个有限的起点。
In 1929 Hubble found that galaxies recede proportionally to their distance, showing the universe is not static but expanding. The relation gave astronomers a systematic yardstick for galactic distances and turned the cosmos from a fixed backdrop into an evolving whole. It paved the way for the Big Bang model and implied a finite beginning.

反射望远镜与折射望远镜有何不同？

How do reflecting and refracting telescopes differ?

折射望远镜用透镜聚光，反射望远镜用凹面镜聚光。大口径透镜会因自重变形并存在色差，因此现代大型望远镜几乎都采用反射式。反射镜可造得更大更轻，更适合专业天文观测。
Refracting telescopes use lenses to focus light, while reflectors use concave mirrors. Large lenses sag under their own weight and suffer chromatic aberration, so modern large telescopes are almost all reflectors. Mirrors can be built larger and lighter, suiting professional astronomy.

What is the Square Kilometre Array (SKA) project?

SKA是正在建设、总收集面积达一平方公里的巨型射电望远镜阵列，分布在南非和澳大利亚。它将以空前灵敏度绘制中性氢分布、探测早期宇宙并搜索可能的地外信号。建成后将极大推动射电天文学的发展。
The SKA is a giant radio telescope array under construction with a total collecting area of one square kilometer, spanning South Africa and Australia. It will map neutral hydrogen, probe the early universe, and search for possible extraterrestrial signals with unprecedented sensitivity. Upon completion it will greatly advance radio astronomy.

What are the main contributions of the Hubble Space Telescope?

哈勃于1990年升空，避开了大气扰动，拍摄了深场图像并精确测定了哈勃常数与宇宙年龄。它揭示了星系演化、恒星形成区以及系外行星大气透射光谱等众多发现。作为在轨三十余年的旗舰望远镜，它重塑了现代天文学。
Launched in 1990, Hubble avoided atmospheric blur, captured deep-field images, and precisely measured the Hubble constant and cosmic age. It revealed galaxy evolution, star-forming regions, and exoplanet transmission spectra, among many findings. Operating in orbit for over three decades, it reshaped modern astronomy.

为什么要把哈勃望远镜放到太空里？

Why put the Hubble telescope in space?

大气层是天文观测的麻烦制造者：气流扰动让星象模糊抖动，水汽和臭氧又会吸收掉大部分紫外线以及部分红外线。升到太空后，哈勃摆脱了这些干扰，拍出的图像比地面清晰得多，还能观测到大气完全挡住的紫外线，1990年发射后它成为天文史上产出最多的望远镜之一。
The atmosphere is a nuisance for astronomers: turbulent air makes star images blurry and shimmer, while water vapour and ozone absorb most ultraviolet and much infrared light. Above it all, Hubble escapes both problems, imaging far more sharply than any ground telescope and opening the ultraviolet sky the atmosphere blocks entirely. Launched in 1990, it became one of the most productive instruments in astronomical history.

It mapped the universe's 3D large-scale structure and supplied core data for studies like baryon acoustic oscillations.

为什么大型天文台偏爱智利和夏威夷的高山顶？

Why do major observatories favour high mountaintops in Chile and Hawaii?

高山站海拔超过大气层的大部分水汽，空气又干又稀薄，星光被扰动和吸收得少，成像更清晰；智利的阿塔卡马沙漠一年有三百多个晴天，是地球上观星条件最好的地方之一。夏威夷莫纳克亚山海拔约4200米，同样干燥稳定。世界上几台最大的光学和射电望远镜都建在这两处。
High-altitude sites rise above most of the atmosphere's water vapour, and with the air so dry and thin, starlight is less distorted and absorbed, giving sharper images. Chile's Atacama Desert enjoys over 300 clear nights a year and is among the best stargazing spots on Earth, while Hawaii's Mauna Kea, at about 4,200 metres, is likewise dry and stable. Several of the world's largest optical and radio telescopes stand on these two sites.

折射望远镜和反射望远镜有什么不同？

How do refracting and reflecting telescopes differ?

折射望远镜用凸透镜把光弯折聚焦，伽利略时代的天文望远镜就是这种；它的缺点是不同颜色的光聚焦点略有不同，会产生色差，而且大透镜又厚又重，容易下垂变形。反射望远镜改用凹面镜反射聚焦，没有色差，镜面可以做得很大，现代大型望远镜包括韦布几乎都是反射式的。
A refractor uses a convex lens to bend and focus light, the kind of telescope Galileo used; its drawbacks are that different colours focus at slightly different points, causing chromatic aberration, and that large lenses are thick, heavy, and prone to sagging. A reflector instead focuses light with a concave mirror, which shows no chromatic aberration and can be built very large, which is why nearly all modern observatories, including Webb, are reflectors.

哥白尼革命的核心转变是什么，它对科学方法有何影响？

What was the central shift of the Copernican Revolution, and how did it affect the scientific method?

哥白尼把地球从宇宙中心移到绕太阳公转的行星之一，用日心体系取代托勒密的地心模型。这一转变不只是几何重排，更强调用观测和数学检验取代权威与哲学思辨。库恩指出，它奠定了现代科学以证据为本、不断自我修正的范式。
Copernicus moved Earth from the center of the universe to just one planet orbiting the Sun, replacing Ptolemy's geocentric model with a heliocentric one. The shift was more than geometric: it elevated observation and mathematical testing over authority and philosophical argument. Kuhn noted that it established the modern, evidence-based and self-correcting scientific paradigm.

望远镜越大就越好吗？

Is a bigger telescope always better?

一般来说，更大的口径能收集更多光、看清更暗更细的目标，因此更有优势。但大气湍流、光学误差和成本也会限制实际效果。现代大望远镜往往配合自适应光学和干涉技术来发挥大口径的潜力。
Generally, a larger aperture collects more light and resolves fainter, finer targets, so it has advantages. But atmospheric turbulence, optical errors, and cost also limit real performance. Modern large telescopes pair big apertures with adaptive optics and interferometry to realize their potential.

Who invented the first astronomical telescope?

In 1608 Dutch spectacle makers such as Hans Lippershey built the first refracting telescopes that magnified distant objects, and the next year Galileo turned one skyward with landmark discoveries. The telescope revolutionized humanity's view of the universe. Newton later invented the reflecting telescope.

什么是星表？

What is a star catalog?

星表是系统记录天体位置、亮度和其他参数的列表或数据库，是天文学研究的基础资料。从古代星官图到现代数字巡天（如依巴谷、盖亚），星表不断扩充和精确化。天文学家借助星表来比对和发现天体变化。
A star catalog is a systematic list or database recording celestial positions, brightness, and other parameters—fundamental reference material for astronomy. From ancient star charts to modern digital surveys like Hipparcos and Gaia, catalogs keep growing and improving in precision. Astronomers use them to compare and discover changes.
