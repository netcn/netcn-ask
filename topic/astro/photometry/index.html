多普勒效应如何把光谱线位移翻译成恒星的视向速度？

How does the Doppler effect translate spectral-line shifts into a star's radial velocity?

恒星沿视线运动会使吸收或发射谱线整体移动：靠近则蓝移（波长变短）、远离则红移。由波长偏移量除以静止波长等于视向速度除以光速，可解出视向速度。高精度（米/秒级）视向速度法是探测系外行星与双星的关键手段。
Line-of-sight motion shifts spectral lines: approaching yields blueshift (shorter wavelength), receding redshift. From Δλ/λ0 = v_r/c one solves the radial velocity v_r. High-precision (m/s) radial velocities are key to detecting exoplanets and binaries.

造父变星的周光关系为什么能充当宇宙距离阶梯的标尺？

Why can the Cepheid period–luminosity relation serve as a rung of the cosmic distance ladder?

造父变星脉动周期与其本征光度严格正相关（Leavitt 定律）。测得周期即可知绝对星等，再与视星等比较得到距离，误差可控制在约 5% 内。它是本地宇宙到邻近星系距离测量的关键一级阶梯。
A Cepheid's pulsation period correlates tightly with its intrinsic luminosity (Leavitt's law). Measuring the period gives absolute magnitude; comparing with apparent magnitude yields distance to ~5% precision. It is a key local rung of the distance ladder out to nearby galaxies.

金属丰度 [Fe/H] 在恒星天文学中如何定义和表示？

How is metallicity [Fe/H] defined and expressed in stellar astronomy?

[Fe/H] 等于恒星的铁氢对数丰度减去太阳的铁氢对数丰度，是以太阳为基准的相对值。负值表示比太阳更贫金属。它用于刻画恒星年龄、星系化学演化与星族差异。
[Fe/H] is the iron-to-hydrogen abundance of a star minus that of the Sun on a log scale; negative values mean more metal-poor. It traces stellar age, galactic chemical evolution, and population differences.

视星等与绝对星等的区别是什么，如何互相换算？

What is the difference between apparent and absolute magnitude, and how are they converted?

视星等描述地球上看到的亮度，绝对星等是天体置于 10 秒差距标准距离时的亮度，反映本征光度。两者通过距离模数（视星等减去绝对星等等于 5 倍对数距离比）联系，距离越远视星等越大（越暗）。
Apparent magnitude is the observed brightness from Earth; absolute magnitude is the brightness at a standard 10 pc distance, reflecting intrinsic luminosity. They connect via the distance modulus m−M=5 log10(d/10 pc); farther objects appear fainter.

滤光系统（如 Johnson–Cousins UBVRI）在测光中起什么作用？

What role do filter systems like Johnson–Cousins UBVRI play in photometry?

不同滤光片只透过特定波段的光，测得的星等组合（色指数如 B−V）反映恒星温度与红化。标准滤光系统使全球望远镜的测光可相互校准与比较，是恒星测光与变星监测的基础。
Different filters pass only specific wavelength bands; magnitude combinations (color indices like B−V) reveal temperature and reddening. Standard filter systems let photometry from telescopes worldwide be calibrated and compared—the basis of stellar photometry and variable-star monitoring.

盖亚（Gaia）卫星怎样通过天体测量建立三维星图？

How does the Gaia satellite build a 3D star map through astrometry?

盖亚以极高精度测量十亿余颗恒星的天球位置、自行与视差，视差直接给出距离，自行揭示横向运动，再结合测光得到温度与光度。其数据革命性地绘制了银河系的三维结构与运动学。
Gaia measures positions, proper motions, and parallaxes for over a billion stars with extreme precision; parallax gives distance directly, proper motion reveals transverse motion, and photometry yields temperature and luminosity—revolutionizing the 3D map and kinematics of the Milky Way.

恒星光谱型 OBAFGKM 的排序依据是什么？

What is the basis for ordering stellar spectral types OBAFGKM?

这一序列按恒星表面温度从高到低排列（O 最热、蓝，M 最冷、红），由吸收线特征决定：O 型以电离氦线为主，F/G 型见氢与金属的精细线，M 型则由分子带（如氧化钛）主导。它是哈佛分类法的核心。
The sequence orders stars by decreasing surface temperature (O hottest/blue to M coolest/red), diagnosed by absorption-line features: O shows ionized helium, F/G show hydrogen and metal lines, and M is dominated by molecular bands (e.g., TiO). It is the core of the Harvard classification.
