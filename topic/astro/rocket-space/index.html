宇航员在太空里怎么喝水?水会不会飘走?

How do astronauts drink in space? Does water float away?

会飘走哦!太空里没有重量,水不会乖乖待在杯子里,而是变成一颗颗亮晶晶的水球到处飞。宇航员用密封袋子插吸管喝,或者张嘴去追水球——像小鱼吃虫子一样好玩!
It floats away! With no weight in space, water refuses cups and turns into shiny floating balls. Astronauts sip from sealed pouches with straws or chase the balls mouth-first — fun as a fish catching bugs.
