观测暗弱天体时，统计噪声主要来自哪里？

For faint objects, where does the dominant statistical noise come from?

极暗光源到达探测器的光子数本身随机起伏（散粒噪声），遵循泊松统计，是限制测光精度的基本极限，常需长时间曝光来压制。
For very faint sources, the random arrival of photons (shot noise) follows Poisson statistics and sets the basic limit on photometric precision, usually beaten down by long exposures.

天文学家如何利用选择效应来解释观测样本中恒星质量的分布与银河系真实初始质量函数之间的差异?

How do astronomers use selection effects to explain why the observed stellar mass distribution in a survey sample differs from the Galaxy's true initial mass function?

任何巡天都对亮度、颜色和天空位置设有探测阈值,亮星和近距恒星因此被系统性高估。若不做统计改正,样本会严重偏向大质量、高光度的恒星,而暗弱的低质量恒星大量缺失。通过蒙特卡洛模拟注入人工恒星再重新回收,可以量化完备度随星等的变化,从而反推出真实的初始质量函数。
Every survey imposes detection thresholds on brightness, color, and sky position, systematically overrepresenting luminous and nearby stars. Without statistical corrections, samples become strongly biased toward massive, high-luminosity stars while faint low-mass stars go missing. By injecting artificial stars into the images and measuring how many are recovered, astronomers quantify completeness as a function of magnitude and invert the bias to recover the true initial mass function.

天文学家为什么普遍改用贝叶斯方法而不是最小二乘来拟合数据?

Why have astronomers largely shifted from least-squares fitting to Bayesian methods for data analysis?

天文数据常带有非高斯误差、删失(如探测上限)和复杂的选择效应,最小二乘的假设在这些情况下失效。贝叶斯框架能把先验知识、测量不确定度和选择函数统一写进似然函数,并用 MCMC 或嵌套采样给出参数的后验分布而非单点估计。这使得结果自带误差棒和模型比较能力,已成为 LSST 等大型巡天的标准工具链。
Astronomical data often carry non-Gaussian errors, censoring (e.g. detection limits), and complicated selection effects that violate least-squares assumptions. The Bayesian framework folds prior knowledge, measurement uncertainty, and the selection function into a single likelihood, then uses MCMC or nested sampling to deliver full posterior distributions instead of point estimates. This yields built-in error bars and model comparison, and it is now standard in the analysis pipelines of surveys like LSST.
