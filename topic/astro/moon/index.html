什么是“潮汐锁定”？

What is tidal locking?

潮汐锁定是天体因引力相互作用，自转周期与公转周期逐渐同步的现象，结果是它总以同一面朝向引力更强的伴侣；月球与许多卫星都如此。
Tidal locking is when an object's spin synchronizes with its orbit due to gravity, always showing one face to its partner; the Moon and many moons are tidally locked.

#潮汐锁定 #同步自转

月球正面的月海覆盖率远高于背面,这种二分性最可能的成因是什么?

Why do the lunar maria cover far more of the near side than the farside — what most likely explains this hemispheric dichotomy?

阿波罗地震仪和重力测量显示月球正面地壳厚约30公里,而背面可达60公里以上,这决定了撞击盆地能否被玄武岩充填成月海。主流模型认为岩浆洋结晶末期,最后残余的KREEP熔体因放射性生热元素富集而集中到正面中低纬区域,局部加热延长了那里的玄武岩喷发期。SPA巨型撞击对内部对流的扰动也可能参与其中,但热化学不对称目前是最受支持的解释。
Apollo seismometers and gravity mapping show the nearside crust is about 30 km thick while the farside exceeds 60 km, controlling whether impact basins could be flooded by basalt to form maria. In leading models, late-stage residual melts of the magma ocean enriched in radiogenic elements (KREEP) concentrated beneath the nearside mid-latitudes, and the extra heat prolonged basaltic volcanism there. The giant South Pole–Aitken impact may also have perturbed interior convection, but the thermochemical asymmetry is currently the best-supported explanation.

#月球二分性 #KREEP

为什么我们在地球上永远看不到月球的背面？

Why can we never see the Moon's far side from Earth?

月球被地球潮汐锁定，自转周期与公转周期相同，所以始终以同一面朝向地球，背面直到 1959 年才被探测器拍到。
The Moon is tidally locked to Earth, with its rotation period equal to its orbital period, so the same face always points toward us; the far side was first photographed by a probe in 1959.

#月球 #潮汐锁定 #背面

为什么有的月球撞击坑带有一圈明亮的辐射纹？

Why do some lunar craters have bright rays radiating outward?

辐射纹是撞击时抛射出的浅色物质，它们在月表呈放射状延伸，可以长达上千公里。较年轻的撞击坑（如第谷坑）辐射纹清晰，而古老的撞击坑辐射纹会因微陨石轰击和太阳风侵蚀而逐渐变暗消失，因此辐射纹的清晰度可以用来判断撞击坑的相对年龄。
Rays are light-colored material excavated and thrown outward during an impact, extending radially for up to thousands of kilometers. Young craters such as Tycho show crisp rays, while rays on old craters fade and disappear under micrometeorite bombardment and solar wind erosion, so ray prominence helps judge a crater's relative age.

月海是怎么形成的？为什么月海看起来比月陆暗？

How did lunar maria form, and why do they look darker than the highlands?

约 39 亿到 30 亿年前，大型小天体撞击月球正面，留下巨大的盆地，随后月幔部分熔融产生的玄武岩熔岩沿着撞击裂缝上涌，把盆地填平，形成月海。玄武岩富含铁、钛，颜色比月陆的斜长岩暗得多，所以月海在夜空中呈现暗色斑块。
Between roughly 3.9 and 3.0 billion years ago, giant impacts on the Moon's near side left huge basins; basaltic lava from partial melting of the mantle then rose through fractures and flooded them, forming the maria. Basalt is rich in iron and titanium and much darker than the highlands' anorthosite, so the maria appear as dark patches.

#月球 #月海 #玄武岩

月球背面最大的撞击盆地是什么？为什么它对月球研究很重要？

What is the largest impact basin on the Moon's far side, and why does it matter?

南极-艾特肯盆地是月球上最大、最古老的确认撞击盆地，直径约 2500 公里，深达 8 公里以上，深坑暴露了月球深部地壳甚至可能的上地幔物质。嫦娥四号就在盆地内的冯·卡门撞击坑软着陆，直接探测那里的月表物质，为研究月球内部结构提供了独特样本。
The South Pole-Aitken basin is the Moon's largest and oldest confirmed impact basin, roughly 2,500 km across and more than 8 km deep, exposing deep crustal and possibly upper-mantle material. China's Chang'e-4 landed softly inside it at Von Karman crater and directly probes the surface material there, offering unique samples for studying the Moon's interior.

#月球水 #水冰

为什么月球正面和背面的地形差异如此之大?

Why do the near side and far side of the Moon have such different terrain?

月球正面以暗色的月海玄武岩平原为主,而背面几乎全是明亮崎岖的高地,地壳也比正面厚约20公里。主流解释认为这种二分性源于早期岩浆洋结晶后月球被潮汐锁定,背面持续暴露在撞击与富含铝物质堆积之下,形成更厚的地壳,抑制了玄武岩溢流。2019年嫦娥四号在冯·卡门撞击坑的探测进一步表明,背面月幔中可能富集橄榄石等深部物质。
The lunar near side is dominated by dark basaltic maria, while the far side is almost entirely bright, rugged highlands with a crust roughly 20 km thicker. The leading explanation ties this dichotomy to lunar magma-ocean crystallization followed by tidal locking: the far side accumulated more aluminum-rich material and a thicker crust that suppressed later basalt flooding. Data from Chang'e 4 at Von Kármán crater in 2019 further suggests the far-side mantle may be enriched in deep-seated minerals like olivine.

月亮会影响海水吗？

Does the Moon affect the sea?

月球的引力会把海水往它那一侧拉，形成一天里的涨潮和退潮。太阳的引力也帮了一点忙，所以潮水有大有小。
The Moon's gravity tugs seawater toward its side, producing the daily rise and fall of the tides. The Sun pulls a little as well, which is why some tides are stronger than others.

Why does the Moon seem to follow us as we move?

The Moon is very far (~380,000 km), so our walking distance is tiny compared to it; its viewing angle barely changes, so it seems to follow us wherever we go.

#月球 #宇航员

月全食时月亮为什么会变成暗红色？

Why does the Moon turn dark red during a total lunar eclipse?

月球进入地球本影后，唯一能照到它的阳光都要先穿过地球大气。大气把蓝光散射掉，又把红光折射到月面上，于是月亮呈现暗红色。这也是它常被称作血月的原因。
Once the Moon moves into Earth's shadow, the only sunlight reaching it has passed through our atmosphere. The air scatters blue light away and bends red light onto the lunar surface, leaving the Moon a dim coppery red. That is why people call it a blood moon.

#月相 #朔望

为什么日全食只能在很窄的地带看到？

Why can a total solar eclipse be seen only in a narrow strip?

月球比地球小得多，它投在地面上的本影直径通常只有一两百公里。随着月球和地球运动，这小小的影子扫出一条狭长路径，只有路径内的人才能看到全食。附近的人只能看到日偏食。
The Moon is far smaller than Earth, so the dark inner shadow it casts on the ground is usually only one or two hundred kilometres wide. As the Moon and Earth move, that small shadow sweeps a long narrow track, and only people inside it see totality. Everyone nearby sees a partial eclipse instead.

#月相 #弯月 #圆月

为什么我们只能看到月亮的同一面？

Why do we only ever see one side of the Moon?

月球自转一圈和绕地球一圈用的时间一样长，这种情况叫潮汐锁定。于是它总把同一面朝着地球，另一面要靠探测器才能看到。
The Moon takes exactly as long to spin once as it does to circle Earth, a situation called tidal locking. That keeps the same face turned toward us, so spacecraft are needed to see the far side.

#月球 #潮汐锁定

月亮为什么有时看起来大、有时看起来小？

Why does the Moon sometimes look bigger and sometimes smaller?

月球绕地球的轨道是椭圆，离地球近时看起来稍大、远时稍小；另外靠近地平线时因参照物错觉，也会显得特别大。
The Moon's orbit is elliptical, so it looks slightly bigger when nearer and smaller when farther; near the horizon, an illusion also makes it seem larger.

月球表面为什么坑坑洼洼？

Why is the Moon's surface full of craters?

因为月球几乎没有大气和地质活动来抹平痕迹，数十亿年来小行星和彗星撞击留下的坑洞都被完好保留了下来。
The Moon has almost no atmosphere or geology to erase marks, so craters from billions of years of asteroid and comet impacts remain preserved.

主流理论认为月球是怎么形成的？

What is the leading theory for how the Moon formed?

大碰撞假说认为，约 45 亿年前一颗火星大小的天体（常称忒伊亚）撞击早期地球，抛出的物质在轨道上聚集形成了月球。
The giant-impact hypothesis holds that about 4.5 billion years ago a Mars-sized body (often called Theia) struck the early Earth, and debris from the impact coalesced into the Moon.
