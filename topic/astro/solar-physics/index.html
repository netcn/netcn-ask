太阳的11年活动周期背后的发电机机制是怎样工作的？

How does the dynamo mechanism behind the Sun's 11-year activity cycle work?

太阳内部较差自转把子午环流产生的极向磁场拉伸成环向磁场，这一过程由对流层的湍流对流不断放大与重组。磁场在表面浮现形成黑子和活动区，极性每11年翻转一次，构成22年的磁周期。帕克在1955年提出的发电机理论为解释这一周期奠定了基础。
Differential rotation inside the Sun stretches the poloidal field generated by meridional circulation into a toroidal field, while turbulent convection in the envelope continually amplifies and reorganizes it. Magnetic flux emerges at the surface as sunspots and active regions, with polarity flipping every 11 years, giving a 22-year magnetic cycle. Parker's 1955 dynamo theory laid the foundation for explaining this periodicity.

日冕加热问题指的是什么，目前主流的解释有哪些？

What is the coronal heating problem, and what are the leading explanations today?

太阳日冕温度高达百万开尔文，远高于其表面约5800开尔文的光球，能量如何从低温层向上传递成为长期难题。主流假说包括磁场重连引发的微耀斑，以及磁流体波把能量从对流区向上传输并在日冕耗散。SOHO和界面区成像摄谱仪等任务正在区分这两种机制的相对贡献。
The solar corona reaches millions of kelvin, far hotter than the roughly 5800 kelvin photosphere below, and how energy climbs from cool layers to heat it remains a long-standing puzzle. Leading hypotheses invoke magnetic reconnection in nanoflares and magnetohydrodynamic waves that carry energy upward from the convection zone and dissipate in the corona. Missions such as SOHO and IRIS are distinguishing the relative contributions of these mechanisms.

帕克太阳探测器如何近距离研究太阳风，它验证了什么理论？

How does the Parker Solar Probe study the solar wind up close, and what theory did it test?

帕克太阳探测器借助金星引力助推逐步俯冲到距日面仅数百万公里的近日点，直接采样太阳风与原初磁场。它发现日冕中存在翻转的磁性结构（磁开关），并测到比预期更快的加热与加速。这些观测检验了帕克1958年提出的日冕与太阳风动力学理论。
The Parker Solar Probe uses repeated Venus gravity assists to dive to perihelia just millions of kilometers above the solar surface, directly sampling the solar wind and pristine magnetic field. It found magnetic switchbacks in the corona and measured faster-than-expected heating and acceleration. These observations tested Parker's 1958 theory of the corona and solar wind.

日冕物质抛射对地球有什么影响，如何被监测和预警？

How do coronal mass ejections affect Earth, and how are they monitored and forecast?

日冕物质抛射是太阳抛出的数十亿吨带电粒子云，若撞击地球会压缩磁层并引发地磁暴。强烈的地磁暴可扰乱卫星运行、通信与电网，并在高纬度地区产生极光。NASA的SOHO与STEREO卫星通过实时成像追踪抛射物的轨迹，为地球提供数小时到数天的预警。
A coronal mass ejection is a cloud of billions of tons of charged particles hurled from the Sun, and if it hits Earth it compresses the magnetosphere and triggers geomagnetic storms. Strong storms can disrupt satellites, communications, and power grids and produce auroras at high latitudes. NASA's SOHO and STEREO spacecraft track these ejections in real time, giving hours to days of warning for Earth.
