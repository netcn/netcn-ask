拉格朗日点有哪些，有什么用？

What are the Lagrange points and what are they for?

两体系统中存在L1到L5五个引力平衡点；L1、L2适合放望远镜（如SOHO、韦布），L4、L5可存放“特洛伊”小行星。
In a two-body system there are five gravitational balance points, L1–L5; L1 and L2 suit telescopes (SOHO, Webb), while L4 and L5 can harbor 'Trojan' asteroids.

什么是比冲（specific impulse）？

What is specific impulse?

比冲衡量火箭发动机效率，等于单位推进剂产生的冲量（常换算成秒），越高代表同样燃料获得越多速度增量，是推进系统设计核心指标。
Specific impulse measures engine efficiency—impulse per unit propellant, often in seconds; higher means more velocity gain from the same fuel, a core figure of merit for propulsion design.

探测器飞掠行星时为什么会因引力弹弓效应获得加速，而行星自身却几乎不损失任何动能？

Why does a spacecraft gain speed through a gravity assist during a planetary flyby, while the planet itself loses essentially no kinetic energy?

在行星参考系里，双曲线飞掠只改变探测器速度的方向，进入与离开时的相对速率相同。但行星本身正以每秒数十公里的速度绕太阳运行，把两个参考系叠加起来看，探测器相对太阳的速度矢量被偏向了加速方向，日心系动能随之增加。这份能量取自行星绕日的轨道能量，由于行星质量极其巨大，其轨道改变小得永远无法测出。
In the planet's reference frame, a hyperbolic flyby merely rotates the spacecraft's velocity vector: the departure speed equals the arrival speed. The planet, however, orbits the Sun at tens of kilometers per second, so combining the two frames shows the heliocentric velocity bent toward a faster direction, raising its kinetic energy around the Sun. That energy comes from the planet's own orbital motion, whose change is far too tiny ever to measure.

多级火箭为什么必要？

Why are multi-stage rockets necessary?

火箭要把自身和剩余燃料一起加速，单级难达轨道速度；多级在部分燃料用尽后抛掉空壳，逐段提高质量比，使最终有效载荷入轨。
A rocket must accelerate itself plus leftover fuel, so a single stage rarely reaches orbital speed; staging jettisons empty shells as fuel is spent, raising the mass ratio step by step so the payload reaches orbit.

地球静止轨道与太阳同步轨道有何不同？

How do geostationary and sun-synchronous orbits differ?

地球静止轨道在赤道上空约3.6万公里、周期24小时，卫星看似不动，适合通信；太阳同步轨道让卫星总在同一地方时过赤道，适合对地观测。
Geostationary orbit sits ~36,000 km over the equator with a 24-hour period so the satellite appears fixed, good for communications; a sun-synchronous orbit always crosses the equator at the same local time, ideal for Earth observation.
