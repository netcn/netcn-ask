暗物质的“直接探测”实验如何工作？

How do dark-matter direct-detection experiments work?

地下实验室（如XENONnT、LZ、PandaX）用极纯液体闪烁体等待暗物质粒子撞上原子核产生微弱闪光，靠深埋地下屏蔽宇宙射线本底。
Underground labs (XENONnT, LZ, PandaX) use ultrapure scintillators to wait for a dark-matter particle to nudge a nucleus into a faint flash, shielded deep underground from cosmic-ray background.

什么是轴子（axion），它为何是热门候选？

What is an axion and why is it a popular candidate?

轴子是一种极轻、弱相互作用的假想粒子，原为解决强CP问题提出，其质量与耦合恰好可能解释暗物质，正被腔体实验（如ADMX）搜寻。
The axion is a hypothetical, extremely light, weakly interacting particle, originally proposed to solve the strong CP problem; its properties could explain dark matter and are hunted in cavity experiments like ADMX.

如何通过伽马射线“间接”寻找暗物质？

How can gamma rays indirectly reveal dark matter?

若暗物质粒子相互湮灭或衰变，会在银河系中心等密集区产生特征伽马射线能谱，费米望远镜正寻找这种超出本底的超出信号。
If dark-matter particles annihilate or decay, they would produce a characteristic gamma-ray spectrum in dense regions like the Galactic Center; Fermi searches for such an excess above known backgrounds.

原初黑洞能解释暗物质吗？

Can primordial black holes explain dark matter?

原初黑洞是宇宙极早期由密度涨落直接坍缩形成的黑洞，若质量分布合适可贡献部分暗物质，但微引力透镜与微波背景约束已排除了多数质量窗口。
Primordial black holes formed from density fluctuations in the very early Universe could account for some dark matter, but microlensing and CMB constraints have ruled out most mass windows.
