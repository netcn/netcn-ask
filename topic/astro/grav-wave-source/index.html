爱因斯坦望远镜和宇宙探险家将带来什么？

What will the Einstein Telescope and Cosmic Explorer bring?

这些是下一代地下引力波探测器，臂长更长、采用低温硅镜，灵敏度比LIGO提高约10倍，可探测到宇宙学距离的双星并合与早期宇宙信号。
These next-generation underground detectors use longer arms and cryogenic silicon mirrors, gaining about 10× LIGO's sensitivity to reach cosmological-distance mergers and early-Universe signals.

脉冲星计时阵列如何探测引力波背景？

How do pulsar timing arrays detect a gravitational-wave background?

利用毫秒脉冲星极其规律的脉冲作为银河系时钟，当纳赫兹引力波经过时会微扰其到达时间，NANOGrav等已报告一致的低频频谱证据。
Millisecond pulsars act as ultra-regular galactic clocks; nanohertz gravitational waves slightly perturb their arrival times, and arrays like NANOGrav have reported a consistent low-frequency signature.

什么是连续引力波？

What are continuous gravitational waves?

连续引力波来自非轴对称、自转不规则的快速中子星，发出稳定几乎单频的信号，LIGO正用长期积分搜寻这类“宇宙哨声”。
Continuous waves come from rapidly spinning but slightly asymmetric neutron stars, emitting an almost single-frequency hum; LIGO searches for them with long integrations—a 'cosmic whistle.'

什么是中子星-黑洞并合？

What is a neutron-star–black-hole merger?

这是中子星被黑洞吞噬的事件，可同时产生引力波和（若中子星被撕裂）电磁信号，能帮助测量中子星物态并研究重元素合成。
It is an event where a black hole devours a neutron star, yielding gravitational waves and, if the star is torn apart, electromagnetic light—probing the neutron-star equation of state and heavy-element synthesis.

什么是随机引力波背景？

What is a stochastic gravitational-wave background?

它是由无数遥远、不可分辨的并合与早期宇宙过程叠加成的引力波“白噪声”，像宇宙的背景海啸，脉冲星计时与未来探测器都可探测。
It is the 'white noise' of gravitational waves from countless unresolved mergers and early-Universe processes, a background ocean of ripples detectable by pulsar timing and future detectors.

引力波主要有哪些天体源？

What are the main astrophysical sources of gravitational waves?

主要源包括双黑洞并合、双中子星并合、中子星-黑洞并合，以及超新星不对称爆发和宇宙学随机背景，不同源有不同波形特征。
Main sources are binary black-hole mergers, binary neutron-star mergers, neutron-star–black-hole mergers, asymmetric supernovae, and a cosmological stochastic background—each with distinct wave signatures.
