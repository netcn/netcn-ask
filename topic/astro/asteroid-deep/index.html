小行星采矿为什么对深空探索有意义？

Why does asteroid mining matter for deep-space exploration?

在太空就地取材制造燃料和材料，能大幅降低从地球运货的成本，支撑更远的载人探索。
In-space resource use cuts the cost of hauling from Earth, enabling farther crewed exploration.

什么是近地小行星？它们为什么受关注？

What are near-Earth asteroids, and why do they matter?

近地小行星是轨道接近地球的小天体，既可能因撞击带来威胁，也蕴含水和金属资源，是未来探测与防御的重点对象。
Near-Earth asteroids have orbits close to Earth's; they pose impact risk but also hold water and metal resources, making them key targets for exploration and planetary defense.

小行星上有哪些值得开采的资源？

What resources are worth mining on asteroids?

近地小行星富含水冰（可拆成火箭燃料）、铁镍金属和铂族贵重金属，是未来太空资源的候选。
Near-Earth asteroids hold water ice (for fuel), iron-nickel metals and platinum-group metals—candidates for space resources.

谷神星和灶神星有什么特别？

What is special about Ceres and Vesta?

二者是小行星带中最大的天体，谷神星是矮行星（含地下盐水储层），灶神星是分异过的岩质天体，其陨石曾被带回地球研究。
They are the largest bodies in the asteroid belt: Ceres is a dwarf planet with a subsurface brine reservoir, while Vesta is a differentiated rocky world whose meteorites have reached Earth.

恐龙灭绝的小行星撞击发生在哪里？

Where did the dinosaur-killing asteroid impact happen?

约6600万年前一颗约10公里小行星撞在现今墨西哥尤卡坦半岛的希克苏鲁伯，引发全球气候灾变，导致非鸟恐龙灭绝和白垩纪结束。
About 66 million years ago a ~10 km asteroid hit the Yucatán peninsula (Chicxulub) in modern Mexico, triggering global climate catastrophe that ended the non-avian dinosaurs and the Cretaceous.

灵神星（Psyche）为何特殊？

Why is the asteroid Psyche special?

灵神星几乎全由金属构成，可能是某原行星被撞击后的裸露金属核，NASA的Psyche任务将首次探访这类“天体铁芯”。
Psyche is made almost entirely of metal and may be the exposed iron-nickel core of a shattered protoplanet; NASA's Psyche mission will be the first to visit such an 'iron world.'

小行星带里的石头会不会经常撞到一起或掉到地球？

Do the rocks in the asteroid belt frequently collide or fall to Earth?

小行星带看似密集，其实天体之间相距极远，碰撞罕见；其轨道相对稳定，只有少数受扰动才进入内太阳系成为近地小行星。
Although the belt looks crowded, its objects are vastly spaced apart and collisions are rare; orbits are fairly stable, and only a few perturbed objects enter the inner solar system as near-Earth asteroids.

什么是近地小行星，它们有危险吗？

What are near-Earth asteroids and are they dangerous?

近地小行星轨道接近地球，撞击可造成区域甚至全球灾难；DART任务已成功用撞击改变Dimorphos的轨道，验证动能偏转防御可行。
Near-Earth asteroids have orbits near Earth and impacts can cause regional or global catastrophe; the DART mission successfully shifted Dimorphos's orbit by impact, proving kinetic deflection defense works.

小行星如何按成分分类？

How are asteroids classified by composition?

主要分为碳质（C型，富含水和有机物、最暗）、硅质（S型，岩石金属）和金属（M型，富铁镍）三类，反映它们形成于太阳系不同位置。
Main types are carbonaceous (C-type, water- and organic-rich, darkest), silicaceous (S-type, rocky-metallic) and metallic (M-type, iron-nickel), reflecting where they formed in the Solar System.
