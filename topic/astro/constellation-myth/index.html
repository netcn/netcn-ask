为什么中国用'二十八宿'而西方用'黄道十二宫'？

Why did China develop 28 mansions while the West developed 12 zodiac signs?

中国以月亮约27.3天绕天一周来划分月站（28宿），重视月与行星的行度；西方以太阳周年路径分12段，重视太阳与黄道带，反映了各自观测重点。
China divided the sky by the Moon's ~27.3-day path (28 mansions), focusing on lunar and planetary motion; the West divided the Sun's annual path into 12, focusing on the ecliptic—reflecting different priorities.

#二十八宿 #黄道十二宫

北斗七星在中国和西方的名字一样吗？

Do the Big Dipper's seven stars have the same names in China and the West?

西方把七颗星看作大熊座的一部分（尾巴），中国则独立称为'北斗'，并据其形状定出'斗柄指东，天下皆春'等季节规律。
The West sees them as part of the Great Bear's tail; China calls them the Northern Dipper and used its handle's pointing to mark seasons ('when the handle points east, spring comes').

#北斗七星 #中西对比

占星学的黄道十二宫和天文学真正的星座对得上吗？

Do astrology's twelve zodiac signs match the real astronomical constellations?

由于岁差，春分点已西移约30度，今天的'白羊座'时段其实太阳位于双鱼座；且各星座大小不均，十二宫只是人为等分，与真实星空并不吻合。
Because of precession the vernal point has shifted ~30°, so the 'Aries' period now finds the Sun in Pisces; the 12 signs are equal slices that don't match the unequal real constellations.

#黄道十二宫 #岁差

为什么星星组成的图案其实并不在一起？

Why do the stars in a constellation pattern not actually lie together?

星座只是从地球看过去方向接近的投影，成员星可能相距光年之遥、彼此毫无关联，只是恰好出现在同一视线方向。
A constellation is just a projection of stars that happen to share a viewing direction from Earth; its members may be light-years apart and physically unrelated.

#星座 #视方向

南半球的星空和北半球一样吗？

Is the night sky in the Southern Hemisphere the same as in the North?

不一样。南半球能看到北极星看不到的南十字座、大小麦哲伦云，而北半球常见的北斗七星在南纬较高处就沉到地平线下。
No. The South sees the Southern Cross and Magellanic Clouds invisible from the North, while the Big Dipper sinks below the horizon at southern latitudes.

#南天 #星空

为什么星星好像会'眨眼睛'？

Why do stars seem to twinkle?

星光穿过地球动荡、冷热不均的大气层时被折射得忽明忽暗，就像隔着热水汽看灯光；行星因为是个小圆面，反而不太眨眼。
Starlight bends through Earth's turbulent, uneven air, flickering like a light seen through heat haze; planets show a disc, so they hardly twinkle.

#星星眨眼 #大气

不同文明看到的星座一样吗？

Do different civilizations see the same constellations?

星星位置相同，但连线与故事各异：希腊人看猎户，中国人把相近星群划入'参宿'和'白虎'，因纽特人则按极地生活看出驯鹿与海豹。
The stars are the same, but the lines and stories differ: Greeks saw Orion, Chinese grouped them into Shen and the White Tiger, and Inuit saw caribou and seals from polar life.

#星座文化 #不同文明

星座是怎么来的？

How did constellations come about?

古人把天上临近的亮星连成图案，赋予人物或动物名字方便记忆和导航，现代天文学则把全天划分为88个官方星座区域。
Ancient people linked nearby bright stars into shapes named for heroes or animals to aid memory and navigation; modern astronomy divides the sky into 88 official constellations.
