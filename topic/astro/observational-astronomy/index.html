薇拉·鲁宾天文台的大型综合巡天望远镜将如何改变天文观测？

How will the Vera C. Rubin Observatory's LSST transform astronomical surveying?

薇拉·鲁宾天文台以8.4米望远镜配合32亿像素相机，计划用十年时间反复扫描整个南天，生成史上最大的时域天文数据库。它能在单次巡天中同时追踪超新星、近地小行星、暗物质分布与银河系结构。这种宽视场、高频重访的模式让暂现天体与罕见事件无处遁形。
The Vera C. Rubin Observatory pairs an eight-meter-class telescope with a 3.2-gigapixel camera to repeatedly scan the entire southern sky over a decade, building the largest time-domain astronomy database yet. A single survey can simultaneously track supernovae, near-Earth asteroids, dark-matter distribution, and Galactic structure. Its wide field and frequent revisits leave transient and rare events nowhere to hide.

事件视界望远镜是如何给黑洞拍出首张照片的？

How did the Event Horizon Telescope produce the first photograph of a black hole?

事件视界望远镜把分布在全球的射电望远镜通过甚长基线干涉技术同步，等效出一台地球口径的虚拟望远镜。它在1.3毫米波长观测M87星系中心，收集跨越数千公里的相位信息，再用算法重建出环绕黑色阴影的光环。2019年公布的M87影像首次直接刻画了黑洞的事件视界轮廓。
The Event Horizon Telescope synchronized radio dishes across the globe using very-long-baseline interferometry, creating a virtual telescope the size of Earth. Observing the center of galaxy M87 at 1.3 mm, it gathered phase information spanning thousands of kilometers and reconstructed a bright ring around a dark shadow. The 2019 M87 image first directly outlined a black hole's event horizon.

引力透镜现象如何被用来探测暗物质和遥远星系？

How is gravitational lensing used to probe dark matter and distant galaxies?

根据广义相对论，大质量天体弯曲途经的光线，使背景星系的像被放大、拉伸成弧甚至多像。通过建模透镜的质量分布，可以绘出本身不可见的暗物质团块；强透镜还能把极遥远星系增亮到可被光谱分析的程度。哈勃空间望远镜利用这一效应发现了许多原本无法观测的早期宇宙天体。
General relativity says massive objects bend passing light, magnifying and stretching background galaxies into arcs or multiple images. Modeling the lens mass distribution maps invisible dark-matter clumps, while strong lensing brightens very remote galaxies enough for spectroscopy. The Hubble Space Telescope has used this effect to reveal early-universe objects otherwise too faint to study.

詹姆斯·韦伯空间望远镜的主要科学仪器有哪些，它们各自探测什么波段？

What are the main scientific instruments on the James Webb Space Telescope, and what wavelength ranges do they cover?

韦伯望远镜以6.5米镀金主镜工作于红外波段，其主仪器包括近红外相机NIRCam、近红外光谱仪NIRSpec和中红外仪器MIRI。NIRCam负责高灵敏度成像，NIRSpec能同时对上百个目标做光谱，MIRI则覆盖更长的中红外并自带日冕仪。这些仪器共同让望远镜能回望宇宙再电离时期的早期星系。
The Webb telescope uses a 6.5-meter gold-coated primary mirror operating in the infrared, with principal instruments NIRCam, NIRSpec, and MIRI. NIRCam provides high-sensitivity imaging, NIRSpec obtains simultaneous spectra of more than a hundred targets, and MIRI covers longer mid-infrared wavelengths with a coronagraph. Together they let the telescope look back at the earliest galaxies of the reionization era.
