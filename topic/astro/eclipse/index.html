食双星的光变曲线能告诉我们什么？

What can an eclipsing binary's light curve tell us?

当双星相互遮挡时，亮度出现规律凹缺，结合轨道周期可推算两星的大小、质量与温度，是精确测定恒星参数的重要手段。
When the two stars eclipse each other, the light dips regularly; with the orbital period this yields their sizes, masses, and temperatures—a precise way to determine stellar parameters.

为什么日全食时能看到太阳外层大气（日冕）？

Why can we see the Sun's outer atmosphere (corona) during a total eclipse?

平时太阳强光掩盖了暗淡的日冕，日全食时月球恰好遮住光球层，亮度骤降上百万倍，稀薄的日冕才显露出来。
Normally the bright photosphere hides the faint corona; during totality the Moon blocks the photosphere, dimming light a millionfold so the thin corona shows.

日全食和日环食有什么不同？

What is the difference between a total and an annular solar eclipse?

取决于月球离地球的远近：近时月盘能完全遮住太阳成日全食；远时月盘偏小，太阳边缘留一圈亮环成日环食。
It depends on the Moon's distance: when near, it fully covers the Sun (total); when far, a bright ring remains (annular).

为什么不是每个月都有日食和月食？

Why aren't there eclipses every month?

因为月球轨道相对地球公转轨道倾斜约5度，多数月份月球从太阳上方或下方掠过，只有交点附近才会三者恰成一线形成食。
The Moon's orbit is tilted about 5 degrees, so most months it passes above or below the Sun; eclipses occur only near the nodes where the three align.

日食是怎么回事？

What causes a solar eclipse?

当月球运行到太阳和地球之间，挡住太阳光时，地球上一部分地区就会看到日食。
A solar eclipse happens when the Moon passes between the Sun and Earth and blocks the Sun's light for some regions on Earth.
