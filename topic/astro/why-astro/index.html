为什么木星的大红斑能存在几百年不散？

Why has Jupiter's Great Red Spot lasted for centuries?

它是木星大气中的巨型反气旋风暴，因行星没有固态表面摩擦耗散、且内部热量持续供给，得以在流体中长久维持，只是尺寸在缓慢缩小。
It's a giant anticyclonic storm; with no solid surface to create friction and steady internal heat feeding it, it persists in the fluid atmosphere, only slowly shrinking.

#大红斑 #木星

为什么我们看到的宇宙其实都是'过去的'？

Why is everything we see in the universe actually from the past?

光传播需要时间，太阳的光要8分钟才到地球，遥远星系的光走了上亿年；所以我们望得越远，看到的就越是宇宙更早时期的样子。
Light takes time to travel—Sunlight needs 8 minutes, distant galaxies millions of years—so the farther we look, the earlier in cosmic history we see.

#光行时 #宇宙视界

为什么土星有显眼的光环，而多数行星没有？

Why does Saturn have prominent rings while most planets don't?

土星环由无数冰与岩石碎块组成，位于它的洛希极限内、无法聚成卫星；木星、天王星也有环，只是暗淡得多，不易看见。
Saturn's rings are countless ice and rock bits within its Roche limit, unable to clump into a moon; Jupiter and Uranus have fainter rings, harder to see.

#土星环 #洛希极限

为什么宇宙微波背景辐射被称为'宇宙婴儿照'？

Why is the cosmic microwave background called the 'baby photo of the universe'?

它是宇宙约38万岁时、物质与辐射脱耦后释放的光，如今红移成微波充满全天空，记录了最早期宇宙的温度起伏，是宇宙学的关键证据。
It's light released when the universe was ~380,000 years old, after matter and radiation decoupled; now redshifted to microwaves, it maps the infant universe's temperature ripples—key cosmological evidence.

#微波背景 #宇宙学

为什么天文台大多建在山顶或沙漠，而不在城市？

Why are observatories built on mountains or in deserts, not cities?

高处空气稀薄而稳定、水汽少，沙漠晴天多、湿度低，都能减少图像模糊与遮挡；远离城市还能避开灯光造成的光污染，看到更暗的星。
High, dry mountain sites have thin, steady air and little water vapor; deserts are clear and arid, reducing blur and blockage, while distance from cities avoids light pollution so fainter stars are visible.

#天文台选址 #光污染

为什么天文学家说'我们都是星尘做的'？

Why do astronomers say 'we are all made of stardust'?

人体中的碳、氧、铁等重元素都来自恒星内部的核聚变与超新星爆炸，再经星际物质汇聚形成地球和生命，所以构成我们的原子确实诞生于星辰。
The carbon, oxygen and iron in our bodies were forged by stellar fusion and supernovae, then gathered via interstellar matter into Earth and life—so our atoms truly were born in stars.

#星尘 #元素起源

为什么地球会有春夏秋冬四季？

Why does Earth have four seasons?

地球自转轴倾斜约23.5度，绕日时不同地区受太阳照射角度和时长随季节变化，造成冷暖交替；离太阳远近不是主因。
Earth's axis tilts ~23.5°; as it orbits, sunlight angle and day length change through the year, causing warm and cold seasons—distance from the Sun is not the main cause.

#四季 #地轴倾斜

为什么夏天的夜晚看起来星星比冬天多？

Why do summer nights seem to show more stars than winter?

夏天夜空朝向银河系中心方向，密集的恒星与星云尽收眼底；冬天朝向银盘边缘，恒星较稀疏，所以夏季星更密集明亮。
In summer the night side faces the Milky Way's center, rich with stars and nebulae; in winter it faces the disk's edge, sparser—so summer looks starrier.

#银河 #季节星图

为什么极光主要出现在地球两极附近？

Why do auroras appear mainly near Earth's poles?

太阳带电粒子被地球磁场引导，沿磁力线汇聚到南北磁极上空，与大气分子碰撞发光，所以极光多出现在高纬度地区。
Solar charged particles are steered by Earth's magnetic field to the magnetic poles, where they hit air molecules and glow—so auroras favor high latitudes.

#极光 #磁极

为什么月亮会有圆缺（月相）变化？

Why does the Moon show phases (waxing and waning)?

月球自己不发光，我们看到的亮面是它被太阳照亮、且朝向地球的那部分；随着月球绕地位置变化，可见亮面从新月到满月循环。
The Moon doesn't shine on its own; we see the sunlit part facing Earth, and as its position changes, the visible lit fraction cycles from new to full.

#月相 #照亮

为什么流星会一闪而过？

Why do meteors flash and vanish?

流星是太空尘埃或小石块以每秒十几到几十公里闯入大气，与空气剧烈摩擦燃烧发光，通常在高空几秒内烧尽，留下短暂光迹。
A meteor is dust or a pebble hitting the atmosphere at tens of km/s, burning up from friction and glowing for a few seconds before vanishing high above.

#流星 #大气燃烧

为什么恒星有的发红、有的发蓝？

Why are some stars red and others blue?

恒星颜色反映表面温度：蓝星最热（上万度），黄星如太阳居中，红星较冷（约三千度）；温度越高，发出的光越偏蓝白。
A star's color shows its surface temperature: blue stars are hottest (tens of thousands K), yellow like the Sun is mid, red is cooler (~3000 K); hotter means bluer-white light.

#恒星颜色 #温度

为什么天空白天是蓝色、傍晚却变红？

Why is the sky blue by day but red at dusk?

阳光被空气分子散射，蓝光波短更容易被散射到四面八方（所以天是蓝的）；傍晚阳光斜穿厚大气，蓝光被散射殆尽，剩下红光直达眼睛。
Air molecules scatter short blue light in all directions (blue sky); at dusk sunlight crosses thick air, blue is scattered away and red remains to reach your eyes.

#天空颜色 #散射

为什么月亮好像会跟着人走？

Why does the Moon seem to follow you when you walk?

月亮离我们非常远，你走一点点路相对它的角度几乎不变，所以无论怎么走它都像停在原处、跟着你移动。
The Moon is so far away that walking a little changes its angle hardly at all, so it seems to stay put and 'follow' you as you move.
