为什么有些海湾的潮差能超过十几米？

Why can tidal ranges exceed ten meters in some bays?

像芬迪湾这样的喇叭形浅湾会产生潮汐共振，把涌进来的潮水不断放大，形成世界罕见的超大潮差。
Funnel-shaped shallow bays like the Bay of Fundy resonate with the tide, amplifying the incoming water into record-large ranges.

#潮差 #共振

为什么农历初一和十五潮汐特别大（大潮）？

Why are tides especially large at new and full moon (spring tides)?

初一、十五时太阳、月球、地球近似成一线，两者引力叠加，使高潮更高、低潮更低，称为大潮。
At new and full moon the Sun, Moon and Earth align, their gravities combine, making higher highs and lower lows—spring tides.

#大潮 #朔望

什么是“小潮”？

What is a 'neap tide'?

上弦和下弦月时，太阳与月球引力方向接近垂直，相互抵消一部分，潮差最小，称为小潮。
At first and last quarter the Sun and Moon pull at right angles, partly canceling, producing the smallest tidal range—neap tides.

#小潮 #潮差

海水的涨落（潮汐）是怎么来的？

What causes the ocean's tides?

月球和太阳的引力把面向它们一侧的海水拉高形成涨潮，地球另一侧也因离心效应出现涨潮，于是一天通常有两次高潮。
The Moon and Sun's gravity pulls seawater up on the near side, and a bulge also forms on the far side from centrifugal effect, giving about two high tides a day.
