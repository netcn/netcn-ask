自适应光学技术如何抵消大气湍流对地基望远镜的干扰？

How does adaptive optics technology compensate for atmospheric turbulence in ground-based telescopes?

自适应光学用可变形镜实时修正被大气扰动的波前，其形状由波前传感器每秒上千次测量决定。系统以明亮参考星或人造激光导星为基准，把模糊的星像恢复成接近衍射极限的锐利图像。欧洲南方天文台的甚大望远镜凭借这一技术显著提升了地基观测的分辨率。
Adaptive optics uses a deformable mirror to correct the wavefront distorted by the atmosphere in real time, its shape set by a wavefront sensor sampling thousands of times per second. The system locks onto a bright reference star or an artificial laser guide star to recover near diffraction-limited sharp images from blurred ones. ESO's Very Large Telescope uses this technique to dramatically raise ground-based resolution.

欧洲极大望远镜（ELT）的主动与自适应光学面临哪些工程挑战？

What engineering challenges face the active and adaptive optics of the Extremely Large Telescope (ELT)?

ELT 主镜由近八百片六角形镜段拼成 39 米口径，主动光学须实时调节每片镜段的纳米级位置以保持整体面形；叠加的多共轭自适应光学还要校正三维大气湍流，需激光导星与极快计算。这些系统使 ELT 的角分辨率比哈勃提高约十六倍，但控制复杂度与造价极高。
The ELT's primary mirror is assembled from nearly 800 hexagonal segments to 39 meters, and its active optics must adjust each segment's position at the nanometer level in real time to preserve the overall figure; layered multi-conjugate adaptive optics must also correct three-dimensional turbulence using laser guide stars and very fast computing. These systems give the ELT about sixteen times Hubble's angular resolution, but at extreme control complexity and cost.

薇拉·鲁宾天文台的时域巡天将如何借助弱引力透镜研究暗能量？

How will the Vera C. Rubin Observatory's time-domain survey use weak gravitational lensing to study dark energy?

鲁宾天文台（原 LSST）将在十年内反复拍摄南天，获取数十亿星系的形状与位置。星系背景光的微弱畸变（弱引力透镜）由前景大尺度物质分布造成，统计这种畸变随红移的演化可约束暗能量的状态方程，为宇宙加速膨胀提供独立检验。
The Rubin Observatory, formerly LSST, will repeatedly image the southern sky over ten years, cataloging the shapes and positions of billions of galaxies. The subtle distortion of background galaxy light by weak gravitational lensing traces foreground large-scale matter, and statistically tracking how that distortion evolves with redshift constrains the dark energy equation of state, independently testing cosmic acceleration.

超导跃迁边缘探测器（TES）在远红外与亚毫米天文观测中为何重要？

Why are transition-edge superconducting (TES) detectors important in far-infrared and submillimeter astronomy?

TES 利用超导材料在临界温度附近电阻随温度剧烈变化的特性，把单个光子能量转化为可测电信号，实现近光子噪声极限的超高灵敏度计数。它们被用在远红外光谱仪中，能在冷宇宙尘埃辐射最亮的亚毫米波段以极高信噪比探测暗弱谱线。
A TES exploits how a superconductor's resistance changes sharply near its critical temperature, converting individual photon energies into measurable signals for near-photon-noise-limited, ultra-sensitive counting. Used in far-infrared spectrometers, they detect faint lines at very high signal-to-noise in the submillimeter bands where cold cosmic dust radiates most brightly.

为什么工作在中红外波段的空间望远镜必须对镜体与科学仪器进行深度制冷？

Why must space telescopes working in the mid-infrared deeply cool both their optics and their science instruments?

室温下约300 K的望远镜本身就像黑体，峰值辐射恰在十微米附近，会以自身热发光淹没同波段的天体信号。将主镜降至几十开尔文、探测器再主动制冷到数开尔文，能把仪器背景压低几个数量级，微弱红外源才能测量。这正是斯皮策耗尽液氦后仅剩最短波段可用、韦布主镜被动冷至约45 K而MIRI仍需额外制冷机的原因。
A telescope at room temperature near 300 K radiates like a blackbody peaking close to ten micrometers, and its own thermal glow would swamp celestial signals in exactly those bands. Dropping the primary mirror to a few dozen kelvin and actively chilling detectors to several kelvin suppresses instrumental background by orders of magnitude, making faint infrared sources measurable. That is why Spitzer retained only its shortest bands after the helium ran out, and why Webb's primary cools passively to about 45 K while MIRI needs dedicated refrigerators.

射电干涉测量中的“闭合相位”为何不受各天线自身大气与仪器相位误差的影响？这一性质对高分辨率成像有何意义？

In radio interferometry, why is the 'closure phase' immune to atmosphere- and instrument-induced phase errors at individual antennas, and why does this matter for high-resolution imaging?

三条基线构成闭合三角形时，观测到的可见度相位之和只由源的亮度分布决定；每台天线上的大气或接收机误差属于“天线项”，在三角形中一正一负恰好抵消，因此闭合相位不受其污染。它是绝对相位丢失时仍稳健保留的结构信息，为自校准提供初始约束，使ALMA与全球VLBI能重建毫角秒级的可信图像。
Three baselines forming a closed triangle yield a sum of visibility phases that depends only on the source brightness distribution. Errors from the atmosphere above a dish or from receiver electronics enter through individual antennas, so within the triangle each appears once positively and once negatively and cancels exactly. This invariant survives even when absolute phases are corrupted, anchoring self-calibration and letting arrays like ALMA and global VLBI recover credible images at milliarcsecond resolution.

天文学家使用的「光谱仪」能告诉我们什么？

What can a spectrograph tell astronomers?

光谱仪把天体光按波长展开成光谱，其中暗的吸收线与亮的发射线对应特定原子或分子。通过识别谱线与测量其位移（红移/蓝移），天文学家能确定天体的化学成分、温度、运动速度和磁场等物理性质。
A spectrograph spreads an object's light into a spectrum by wavelength, where dark absorption and bright emission lines correspond to specific atoms or molecules. By identifying those lines and measuring their shifts, red or blue, astronomers determine the object's chemical composition, temperature, motion, and magnetic field.

天文相机中常用的 CCD 探测器是如何工作的？

How does the CCD detector commonly used in astronomical cameras work?

CCD（电荷耦合器件）由大量光电二极管像素组成，光子打在半导体上产生电子并被储存在像素势阱中，再由移位寄存器逐行读出为电信号。它因量子效率高、噪声低而成为天文成像主流，不过在红外波段逐渐被汞镉碲等专用探测器取代。
A CCD, charge-coupled device, consists of many photodiode pixels; photons hitting the semiconductor generate electrons stored in pixel wells and then shifted out row by row through a register as electrical signals. Its high quantum efficiency and low noise made it the mainstream in astronomical imaging, though in the infrared it is increasingly replaced by specialized detectors such as mercury cadmium telluride.

天文学家如何用光谱的红移来推断星系的退行速度？

How do astronomers use spectral redshift to infer a galaxy's recession speed?

每种元素在光谱中留下固定位置的明线或暗线，构成天体的化学指纹。当星系远离我们时，这些特征线整体向长波方向平移，红移量通过多普勒效应换算成退行速度。斯里弗和哈勃的早期观测首次把这种红移与距离联系起来，成为宇宙膨胀的直接证据。
Each element leaves spectral lines at fixed wavelengths, forming a chemical fingerprint of the object. As a galaxy recedes, those features shift toward longer wavelengths, and the redshift converts to recession speed through the Doppler effect. Slipher and Hubble first linked such redshifts to distance, providing direct evidence for cosmic expansion.

中国 FAST 望远镜在灵敏度上有什么突出优势？

What outstanding sensitivity advantage does China's FAST telescope have?

FAST 是世界最大单口径球面射电望远镜，口径达五百米，接收面积远超此前的阿雷西博望远镜。其巨大的集光面积与主动反射面调形能力使其对暗弱射电源与窄带信号的灵敏度居世界前列，特别适合脉冲星搜索与中性氢巡天。
FAST is the world's largest single-dish spherical radio telescope, with a 500-meter aperture far exceeding the former Arecibo telescope in collecting area. Its huge area and actively shaped reflector give it among the highest sensitivities to faint radio sources and narrowband signals, especially suited to pulsar searches and neutral hydrogen surveys.

为什么把相距几千公里的射电望远镜联合观测，能比单台望远镜看得更精细？甚长基线干涉测量（VLBI）的分辨率由什么决定？

Why does combining radio telescopes thousands of kilometers apart reveal finer detail than any single dish, and what sets the angular resolution of Very Long Baseline Interferometry (VLBI)?

望远镜角分辨率约为波长除以口径，单台射电望远镜口径有限，长波下成像很粗。干涉技术把相距D的两台望远镜信号精确对时后相干叠加，等效于口径为D的望远镜，分辨率由波长除以基线长度决定。跨洲VLBI基线接近地球直径，分辨率达毫角秒级；事件视界望远镜在1.3毫米波段达到约20微角秒，首次拍到M87黑洞阴影。
A telescope's angular resolution is roughly wavelength divided by aperture, so single radio dishes are intrinsically blurry at long wavelengths. Interferometry instead correlates the signals of two dishes separated by a baseline D with precise timing, synthesizing the resolution of a D-sized aperture set by wavelength over baseline. Transcontinental VLBI approaches Earth's diameter and resolves milliarcseconds, while the Event Horizon Telescope at 1.3 mm reached about 20 microarcseconds, imaging the shadow of the M87 black hole for the first time.
