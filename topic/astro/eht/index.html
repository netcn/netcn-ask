甚长基线干涉（VLBI）如何工作？

How does very-long-baseline interferometry (VLBI) work?

VLBI把全球射电望远镜当作一台地球大小虚拟望远镜的各部分，用原子钟记录信号并事后相关，基线越长分辨率越高，是EHT成像的基础。
VLBI treats radio dishes worldwide as parts of one Earth-sized virtual telescope, recording signals with atomic clocks and correlating them later; longer baselines give higher resolution—the basis of EHT imaging.

EHT还观测了黑洞的喷流吗？

Has the EHT also observed black-hole jets?

是的。EHT对M87的研究结合了阴影与长达数千光年的相对论喷流，帮助理解超大质量黑洞如何将物质加速到近光速并反馈星系。
Yes. EHT work on M87 combined the shadow with its thousand-light-year relativistic jet, helping explain how supermassive black holes accelerate matter near light speed and feed back on their galaxies.

事件视界望远镜给银河系中心黑洞拍了照吗？

Did the Event Horizon Telescope image the Milky Way's central black hole?

是的。2022年EHT发布了人马座A*（Sagittarius A*）的图像，确认这个约400万太阳质量的黑洞与M87一样拥有明亮环与阴影，只是变化更快、更难成像。
Yes. In 2022 the EHT released an image of Sagittarius A*, the ~4-million-solar-mass black hole, confirming a bright ring and shadow like M87's, but faster-varying and harder to image.
