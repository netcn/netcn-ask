哈勃曾因主镜磨错而模糊，后来怎么修好的？

How was Hubble fixed after its mirror was ground wrong?

1993年航天员乘航天飞机上天，为它安装了一套矫正光学组件（像配眼镜），使其恢复清晰视力。
In 1993 astronauts flew up on a shuttle and installed corrective optics—like glasses—restoring its sharp vision.

什么是哈勃张力？

What is the Hubble tension?

哈勃张力是指测量当今宇宙膨胀速率（哈勃常数 H₀）的两种方法给出的数值不一致：基于微波背景和 ΛCDM 模型推得约 67 km/s/Mpc，而基于超新星等“距离阶梯”的局部测量约 73 km/s/Mpc，差距约 9%。这一分歧持续存在、难以用测量误差解释，可能意味着我们对暗能量、中微子或早期宇宙物理的理解需要修正。它是当代宇宙学最突出的未解之谜之一。
The Hubble tension is the mismatch between two ways of measuring today's expansion rate, the Hubble constant H₀. Early-universe inferences from the CMB within the ΛCDM model give about 67 km/s/Mpc, while local distance-ladder measurements using supernovae give about 73 km/s/Mpc—a ~9% discrepancy. Because it persists beyond measurement error, it may signal that our understanding of dark energy, neutrinos, or early-universe physics needs revision, making it one of cosmology's leading puzzles.

为什么要把望远镜放到太空中？

Why put a telescope in space?

大气会扭曲星光、吸收某些波段并带来光污染，太空里没有这些干扰，能看到更暗更清的天体。
Atmosphere blurs light, blocks bands and adds glow; in space there's no such interference, seeing fainter, clearer objects.

哈勃太空望远镜是干什么的？

What does the Hubble Space Telescope do?

它是一台在地球大气层外工作的望远镜，避开大气抖动拍出清晰深空照片，帮助人类看见遥远星系。
It's a telescope above the atmosphere that avoids blur to take sharp deep-space images, revealing distant galaxies.
