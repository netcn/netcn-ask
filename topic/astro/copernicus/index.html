哥白尼的模型和现代认识还有哪些差别？

How does Copernicus's model differ from today's understanding?

他仍认为行星沿正圆轨道匀速运动，且太阳在宇宙中心；后来开普勒发现椭圆轨道、牛顿给出引力解释才更完善。
He still used circular uniform orbits with the Sun at the universe's center; Kepler's ellipses and Newton's gravity later refined it.

#哥白尼 #椭圆轨道

日心说刚提出时为什么不被接受？

Why was the heliocentric model rejected at first?

它与日常感觉和当时权威的地球中心宇宙观冲突，也缺少直接证据，直到后来观测才被证实。
It clashed with everyday experience and the accepted Earth-centered view, and lacked direct proof until later observations confirmed it.

#日心说 #科学革命

以前的人认为太阳绕着地球转，后来是谁推翻的？

Who overturned the idea that the Sun orbits Earth?

哥白尼提出日心说，认为地球和其他行星都绕着太阳转，揭开了现代天文学的序幕。
Copernicus proposed the heliocentric model—Earth and planets orbit the Sun—opening modern astronomy.
