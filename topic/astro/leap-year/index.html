公历的闰年规则是谁在何时确定的？

Who and when set the Gregorian leap rule?

由教皇格里高利十三世在1582年改革儒略历而定，使日历与季节长期保持一致。
Pope Gregory XIII reformed the Julian calendar in 1582, keeping the calendar aligned with seasons long-term.

#格里高利历 #1582

为什么1900年不是闰年，而2000年是？

Why was 1900 not a leap year but 2000 was?

闰年规则：能被4整除，但整百年必须能被400整除才是闰年。1900不能被400整除，2000可以，所以2000年是闰年。
Rule: divisible by 4, but a century year must be divisible by 400. 1900 isn't, 2000 is, so 2000 was a leap year.

如果没有闰年，日历和季节会怎样慢慢错开？

What happens to the calendar and seasons without leap years?

每年少算约0.24天，约每4年差近1天，上百年后同一日历日期会对应完全不同的季节。
Without it we'd lose ~0.24 day per year, drifting nearly a day every 4 years, so the same date would fall in different seasons after centuries.

为什么会有闰年，每四年都闰吗？

Why do we have leap years, and is it every four years?

地球绕太阳一周约365.24天，每四年补一天（2月29日）来对齐季节；但整百年份须被400整除才闰，所以并非严格的四年一闰。
A year is ~365.24 days, so we add a day (Feb 29) every four years to stay aligned; but century years leap only if divisible by 400, so it's not strictly every four.
