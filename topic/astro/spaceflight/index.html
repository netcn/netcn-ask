引力弹弓为什么能让探测器不烧燃料就加速？

How does a gravity assist speed up a probe without burning fuel?

在行星的参考系里，探测器飞入和飞出时速度大小不变，只是方向改变，属于弹性散射；但换到太阳参考系看，行星本身以每秒十几公里的速度在轨道上运动，探测器就会被行星的轨道速度拖着改变速度，既可能加速也可能减速。木星绕太阳的速度约每秒13公里，旅行者号正是在它身后借力，把速度抬升到了逃逸太阳所需的水平。
In the planet's frame, the probe enters and leaves with equal speed, only its direction changes — an elastic scattering; but in the Sun's frame the planet itself moves at some ten-plus kilometres per second along its orbit, so the probe's velocity is dragged and altered by the planet's orbital speed, gaining or losing energy depending on the geometry. Jupiter travels around the Sun at about 13 kilometres per second, and Voyager slingshotted behind it to reach the speed needed to escape the Sun.

第二宇宙速度为什么恰好是第一宇宙速度的根号2倍？

Why is the second cosmic velocity exactly the square root of two times the first?

逃逸速度要求物体带着恰好为零的总机械能离开，也就是动能等于引力势能的绝对值。把方程对速度求解，得到根号下2GM除以r，而近地圆轨道速度是根号下GM除以r，两者正好相差根号2倍，地面处约为每秒11.2公里。这个比值只由能量关系决定，与星球密度无关。
Escape velocity requires the object to depart with exactly zero total mechanical energy, meaning its kinetic energy equals the magnitude of its gravitational potential energy. Solving for speed gives the square root of 2GM over r, while the low-orbit circular speed is the square root of GM over r, so the two differ by exactly the square root of two, about 11.2 kilometres per second at Earth's surface. The ratio follows purely from energy, independent of the planet's density.

为什么火箭要携带那么多燃料？齐奥尔科夫斯基方程说明了什么？

Why must rockets carry so much fuel, and what does the Tsiolkovsky equation reveal?

齐奥尔科夫斯基方程给出速度增量等于排气速度乘以质量比的自然对数。要入轨需要约每秒9.4公里的速度增量，而化学火箭排气速度只有每秒3公里上下，代入方程可算出初始质量约为空载质量的二十多倍，所以燃料占了火箭总重的绝大部分。更关键的是速度增量与质量比是对数关系，想多要一点速度，燃料就要按指数增长，分级正是为了绕开这个惩罚。
The Tsiolkovsky rocket equation gives the speed increment as the exhaust velocity times the natural log of the mass ratio. Reaching orbit needs about 9.4 kilometres per second of delta-v, while chemical rockets exhaust at only about three kilometres per second, so the equation yields an initial mass over twenty times the empty mass, which is why fuel dominates a rocket's weight. Crucially, delta-v grows logarithmically with the mass ratio, so each extra bit of speed demands exponentially more fuel; staging is how engineers dodge that penalty.

为什么“光速”是星际旅行的天然障碍？

Why is light speed a natural barrier for interstellar travel?

按相对论，越接近光速所需能量越大，且有时间膨胀效应，使跨越光年级距离极其困难。
Relativity says energy balloons as you approach light speed, with time dilation, making light-year trips extremely hard.

第一宇宙速度为什么是每秒7.9公里？

Why is the first cosmic velocity 7.9 kilometres per second?

要在近地轨道不掉下来，卫星必须让地球引力刚好充当向心力。令万有引力等于质量乘向心加速度，解出的速度约为每秒7.9公里，也就是贴着地面做圆形轨道的最低速度。低于这个速度，卫星会落回地面；高于它，轨道就会变成椭圆。
To stay in a low orbit without falling, a satellite must have Earth's gravity act exactly as the centripetal force. Equating gravity with mass times centripetal acceleration gives about 7.9 kilometres per second, the minimum speed for a circular orbit skimming the surface. Slower than that, the satellite falls back to the ground; faster, its orbit becomes an ellipse.

旅行者号探测器是怎么飞出太阳系的？

How did the Voyager probes manage to leave the Solar System?

旅行者号1977年发射，利用行星引力弹弓一次次加速：旅行者2号依次掠过木星、土星、天王星和海王星，完成了著名的行星大巡游。靠这种借力，探测器不需要携带大量燃料就获得了足够的逃逸速度。旅行者1号2012年、旅行者2号2018年先后穿过日球层顶，如今都运行在星际空间里。
Launched in 1977, the Voyagers repeatedly gained speed through planetary gravity assists: Voyager 2 swung past Jupiter, Saturn, Uranus, and Neptune in the famous grand tour. With this borrowed momentum the probes reached escape speed without hauling huge fuel tanks. Voyager 1 crossed the heliopause in 2012 and Voyager 2 in 2018, and both now operate in interstellar space.

火箭为什么几乎都是多级的？

Why are almost all rockets multi-stage?

单级火箭要把沉重的贮箱、发动机和壳体一路驮到入轨，绝大多数燃料都浪费在加速这些死重上。多级火箭燃料烧完就抛掉空壳体，让剩下的部分更轻，可以用同样多的燃料飞得更快。土星五号有三级，猎鹰九号也有两个大段，分级是航天上应对引力损失的标准做法。
A single-stage rocket would have to haul its heavy tanks, engines, and structure all the way to orbit, wasting most of its fuel on accelerating that dead weight. A multi-stage rocket drops the emptied shell once its fuel is spent, leaving a lighter vehicle that can fly faster with the same propellant. The Saturn V had three stages and the Falcon 9 has two big sections; staging is the standard way to beat the penalty of gravity.

火星车是怎么安全降落在这个荒凉星球上的？

How do rovers land safely on Mars?

着陆要经历进入、下降和着陆三步。探测器以约每小时2万公里冲入稀薄大气，靠隔热罩减速，然后打开超声速降落伞进一步减速，最后阶段各任务各有招数：勇气号、机遇号靠气囊弹跳，好奇号和毅力号则用空中吊车，在最后几十米把火星车用缆绳缓缓放到地面。
Landing runs through entry, descent, and touchdown. The probe slams into the thin atmosphere at about 20,000 kilometres per hour, sheds speed with a heat shield, then deploys a supersonic parachute. The final stage differs by mission: Spirit and Opportunity bounced in airbags, while Curiosity and Perseverance used the sky crane, lowering each rover on cables over the last few dozen metres.

宇宙飞船在太空中是怎么追上空间站并完成对接的？

How does a spacecraft in space catch up with the space station and dock with it?

关键在于空间站和飞船都在同一高度的轨道上以同样的速度飞行，彼此几乎没有相对速度，所以追赶并不需要高速冲刺。飞船先进入略低的轨道靠更快角速度追上，再在合适时机爬升到同一高度，随后用小型推进器一点点调整，最后以每秒几厘米的相对速度靠近并锁定在对接端口上。
The key is that the station and the spacecraft both circle at the same altitude and speed, so their relative velocity is almost nil and no high-speed chase is needed. The ship first drops into a slightly lower, faster orbit to catch up, then climbs back to the same height at the right moment, fine-tunes with small thrusters, and finally approaches at a few centimetres per second to latch onto the docking port.
