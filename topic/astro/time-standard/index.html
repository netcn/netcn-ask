为什么国际上决定未来取消闰秒？

Why did the world decide to eventually drop leap seconds?

闰秒会让金融、通信、导航系统每次都要特殊应对，易出故障；2022年国际计量大会决定最晚2035年起不再插入闰秒，改用更平滑的协调方式。
Leap seconds force finance, communications and navigation systems to make special adjustments and risk glitches; in 2022 the metrology assembly decided to stop inserting them by 2035, using smoother coordination.

为什么地球自转在变慢，一天真的在变长吗？

Why is Earth's rotation slowing—is a day really getting longer?

月球引起的潮汐摩擦把地球自转的角动量转移给月球轨道，使日长每世纪增加约1.7毫秒；远古一年有400多天，就是证据。
Tidal friction from the Moon transfers Earth's spin angular momentum to the lunar orbit, lengthening the day by ~1.7 ms per century; ancient years had over 400 days—proof.

#日长 #潮汐摩擦

卫星导航为什么必须考虑相对论？

Why must satellite navigation account for relativity?

卫星高速运动使钟因狭义相对论变慢，又因离地心远、引力弱使钟因广义相对论变快，二者叠加每天差约38微秒，不修正定位会每天漂移约10公里。
A satellite's speed slows its clock (special relativity) while weaker gravity speeds it (general relativity); the net ~38 microseconds/day must be corrected or positioning drifts ~10 km daily.

古代没有原子钟，人们靠什么来确定'标准时间'？

Without atomic clocks, how did ancients set 'standard time'?

他们靠日晷看日影定当地真太阳时，城市间用统一'标准钟'或天文台授时；中国历代设司天监、钦天监专门测时颁历。
They used sundials for local solar time and a unified civic clock or observatory for standards; China's astronomical bureaus measured and issued time and calendars.

为什么'经度'能靠观测星星和携带精确时钟算出来？

Why can longitude be found from the stars plus an accurate clock?

同一时刻不同经度的地方时不同；带着已知标准时的钟出海，比对当地正午（或恒星位置）与钟面时间差，就能算出所在经度，这正是航海钟的用处。
At one instant, local time differs by longitude; carrying a clock set to standard time, you compare local noon (or star positions) with the clock to compute longitude—the purpose of the marine chronometer.

#经度 #航海钟

我们日常用的'时间'到底由什么定义？

What actually defines the time we use every day?

现代秒长由铯-133 原子基态两个超精细能级之间跃迁所对应的辐射 9,192,631,500 个周期定义，全世界通过原子钟联网保持协调世界时(UTC)，再按经度划分时区得到本地时间。
The modern second is defined by 9,192,631,500 cycles of the radiation corresponding to the hyperfine transition of the cesium-133 ground state; atomic clocks worldwide keep UTC, and time zones offset it by longitude.

为什么国际日期变更线不在本初子午线上？

Why isn't the International Date Line at the prime meridian?

本初子午线（0°经线）定的是时刻起点，而日期变更线为避免把同一个国家分到两天、绕开陆地，特意在180°附近弯折，主要关乎'哪天'而非'几点'。
The prime meridian sets the time origin, but the date line bends near 180° to avoid splitting countries and crossing land—it's about 'which day,' not 'what hour.'

#日期变更线 #时区

为什么不同地区的时间不一样？

Why do different regions have different times?

地球自转让太阳依次照亮各地，为免都用同一时刻造成混乱，人们按经度把地球分成24个时区，每差15度大致差1小时。
Earth's rotation brings sunlight to places in turn; to avoid confusion, the world is split into 24 time zones roughly 15° apart, each about one hour different.

#时区 #地球自转

什么是'闰秒'，为什么要有它？

What is a 'leap second,' and why do we need it?

地球自转略不规律且长期变慢，原子钟定义的一天比地球自转快一点点，所以偶尔在6月或12月末加（或减）一秒，让协调世界时(UTC)跟上地球。
Earth's rotation is irregular and slowing, so atomic-clock days run slightly ahead; occasionally a second is added (or removed) at the end of June or December to keep UTC in step with Earth.

为什么有时候白天也能看到月亮？

Why can we sometimes see the Moon during the day?

月亮本身反射太阳光，只要它和太阳不在完全相反的方向、且天空不太亮，肉眼就能在白天找到它，尤其上弦前后在下午常见。
The Moon just reflects sunlight; when it's not opposite the Sun and the sky isn't too bright, you can spot it by day—common in the afternoon around first quarter.
