分子云中心温度只有十几开尔文，为什么还能合成甲醇这类复杂有机分子？

How can complex organic molecules like methanol form inside molecular cloud cores whose temperature is only about ten kelvin?

气相两体反应在这种温度下势垒太高，效率极低，所以主要化学过程发生在冰冷的尘埃颗粒表面。CO等分子冻结在粒度表面后，宇宙线或紫外光子电离氢气产生的氢原子可以在表面量子隧穿并加合，逐级把CO氢化成HCO、H2CO直至CH3OH。实验室低温冰实验已复现这一反应链，说明表面化学而非热平衡气相反应主导了冷云中复杂分子的生成。
Two-body gas-phase reactions face prohibitive barriers at such temperatures, so the dominant chemistry happens on cold dust-grain surfaces. Once CO freezes out, H atoms produced by cosmic-ray or UV ionization can tunnel and add onto the ice, hydrogenating CO stepwise to HCO, H2CO, and finally CH3OH. Laboratory experiments on low-temperature ices have reproduced this chain, confirming that surface chemistry rather than thermal gas-phase reactions builds complex molecules in cold clouds.

为什么宇宙里没有无限大的恒星,也几乎没有真正的失败者棕矮星泛滥?

Why are there no infinitely massive stars — and why no plague of failed brown dwarfs either?

两头都被物理卡死。上限:恒星越亮,辐射压对表面气体的推斥越猛,超过爱丁顿极限(约150倍太阳质量量级)时光压直接把外层吹散,超大原恒星还会靠爆发性吸积自我调节;观测最大值在两百太阳质量上下。下限:0.08倍太阳质量的核不足以压缩出数千万开尔文的氢点火温度,引力收缩的热散尽后只能靠氘燃烧苟且降温,沦为棕矮星——它们数量其实不少,只是又暗又冷难被看见。0.08与150这两个数字划定了主序星的整个王国。
Physics clamps both ends. The cap: brighter stars push radiation pressure harder against their own gas, and beyond the Eddington limit (around 150 solar masses) light blows away the envelope, while overmassive protostars self-regulate via eruptive accretion — observations top out near two hundred solar masses. The floor: below 0.08 solar masses cores never reach the tens of millions of kelvin needed for hydrogen ignition; after gravitational heat dissipates they limp along on deuterium, cooling as brown dwarfs — actually numerous, just too dim to census. Those two numbers, 0.08 and 150, bound the entire main-sequence kingdom.

什么是钱德拉塞卡极限？

What is the Chandrasekhar limit?

钱德拉塞卡极限约为1.4倍太阳质量，是白矮星依靠电子简并压力支撑自身不被引力压垮的最大质量上限。
The Chandrasekhar limit is about 1.4 solar masses—the maximum mass a white dwarf can support against gravity with electron degeneracy pressure.

什么是行星状星云？

What is a planetary nebula?

行星状星云是中小质量恒星在红巨星阶段末抛出的外层气壳，被中心白矮星紫外辐射照亮，与行星无关。
A planetary nebula is the outer shell ejected by a low-to-medium mass star at the end of its red-giant phase, lit by the central white dwarf's UV light—despite the name, it has nothing to do with planets.

恒星的质量如何决定它的寿命？

How does a star's mass determine its lifetime?

质量越大的恒星虽然燃料更多，但燃烧极其剧烈、消耗更快，寿命反而更短；太阳约能活100亿年，而大质量恒星可能只活几百万年。
More massive stars have more fuel but burn far more furiously and die sooner; the Sun will live about 10 billion years, while massive stars may last only millions.

#恒星质量 #恒星寿命

冷暗云中的氢分子几乎不发任何辐射,天文学家为何转而依赖一氧化碳来示踪这些分子气体?

Since molecular hydrogen emits almost nothing inside cold dark clouds, why do astronomers fall back on carbon monoxide to trace that molecular gas?

氢分子是同核双原子分子,没有永久偶极矩,电偶极转动能级跃迁被禁阻,而暗云只有约十开的温度,连它的四极跃迁也难以激发,因此自身近乎不可见。一氧化碳带有永久偶极矩,最低转动跃迁位于115吉赫,只需约五开就能激发,在冷分子气体中处处发出明亮谱线。借助丰度模型把一氧化碳强度折算成氢分子柱密度,就得到标准的天体物理转换因子方法。
As a homonuclear molecule, hydrogen lacks a permanent dipole moment, so its dipole rotational transitions are forbidden, and at the roughly ten-kelvin temperature of dark clouds even its quadrupole lines stay unexcited, leaving it effectively invisible. Carbon monoxide does carry a permanent dipole; its lowest rotational line at 115 GHz needs only about five kelvin to excite and shines brightly throughout cold molecular gas. Converting carbon-monoxide intensity into hydrogen column density through abundance models yields the standard astrophysical conversion factor method.

恒星一生会经历哪些主要阶段？

What are the main stages in a star's life?

典型恒星经历分子云坍缩、主序星、红巨星（或红超巨星），最后以白矮星、中子星或黑洞收场，取决于初始质量。
A typical star passes through molecular-cloud collapse, the main sequence, a red giant (or supergiant) phase, and ends as a white dwarf, neutron star, or black hole depending on its initial mass.

恒星靠什么发光？

What makes stars shine?

恒星核心在极高温度和压力下把氢聚变成氦，释放出巨大能量，这就是恒星光芒的来源。
In a star's core, extreme heat and pressure fuse hydrogen into helium, releasing enormous energy—that is what makes a star shine.

太阳会死吗？

Will the Sun die?

会。约50亿年后太阳将膨胀成红巨星，最终收缩为一颗白矮星，慢慢冷却变暗。
Yes. In about 5 billion years the Sun will swell into a red giant and finally shrink into a white dwarf that slowly cools and dims.
