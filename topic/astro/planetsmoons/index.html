海王星最大的卫星海卫一（Triton）为何与众不同？

What makes Triton, Neptune's largest moon, so unusual?

海卫一沿与海王星自转相反的逆行轨道运行，且表面有冰火山活动，很可能是被海王星俘获的柯伊伯带天体。
Triton orbits Neptune retrograde (backward) and shows cryovolcanic activity, strongly suggesting it is a captured Kuiper Belt object rather than an original moon.

火星的奥林帕斯山为何如此巨大？

Why is Mars's Olympus Mons so enormous?

奥林帕斯山是火星上的一座盾状火山，高约22公里，是太阳系已知最高的山。火星重力较弱且地壳板块不漂移，使火山能够长期在同一处堆积增长。
Olympus Mons is a shield volcano on Mars about 22 kilometers high, the tallest known mountain in the solar system. Mars's weaker gravity and lack of moving tectonic plates let the volcano grow in one spot for a very long time.

木星的卫星木卫一（伊奥）为何火山活动如此剧烈？

Why is Jupiter's moon Io so volcanically active?

木卫一处在木星与其他伽利略卫星的轨道共振中，木星强大的潮汐力不断拉伸和挤压它的内部，产生巨大摩擦热。这使它成为太阳系中火山活动最活跃的天体。
Io sits in an orbital resonance with Jupiter and the other Galilean moons, so Jupiter's powerful tides constantly flex its interior and generate enormous frictional heat. This makes Io the most volcanically active body in the solar system.

海王星和天王星为何被称为「冰巨星」？

Why are Neptune and Uranus called ice giants?

冰巨星的内部主要由水、氨和甲烷等「冰」物质（在高压下呈流体状态）构成，而不是像木星那样以氢和氦为主。它们的大气含甲烷，使二者呈现蓝色。
Ice giants are built mostly from so-called ices such as water, ammonia, and methane in fluid form under high pressure, rather than from hydrogen and helium like Jupiter. Methane in their atmospheres gives both planets a blue color.

土星的卫星土卫六（泰坦）为何令科学家着迷？

Why does Saturn's moon Titan fascinate scientists?

土卫六是太阳系中唯一拥有浓密大气层的卫星，表面有由液态甲烷形成的湖泊和河流。它被认为可能存在与地球不同的有机化学环境，是寻找地外生命的重要目标。
Titan is the only moon in the solar system with a dense atmosphere, and it has lakes and rivers of liquid methane on its surface. It may host an exotic organic chemistry unlike Earth's, making it a key target in the search for life beyond Earth.

什么是潮汐锁定，为何月球总以同一面朝向地球？

What is tidal locking, and why does the Moon always show us one face?

潮汐锁定指卫星的自转周期与公转周期相同，使同一面始终朝向主星。地球对月球的引力潮汐作用逐渐减慢了月球自转，最终使它被锁定。
Tidal locking occurs when a moon's rotation period matches its orbital period, so the same side always faces its planet. Earth's tidal pull gradually slowed the Moon's spin until it became locked.

金星的表面温度为什么比水星还高？

Why is Venus hotter than Mercury despite being farther from the Sun?

金星被浓厚的二氧化碳大气包裹，产生强烈的温室效应，把热量牢牢锁住。其表面温度约465摄氏度，比离太阳更近的水星还要热。
Venus is wrapped in a thick carbon-dioxide atmosphere that produces a runaway greenhouse effect, trapping heat efficiently. Its surface reaches about 465 degrees Celsius, hotter than Mercury even though Mercury is closer to the Sun.

火星为什么被称为「红色星球」？

Why is Mars called the Red Planet?

火星表面富含氧化铁，也就是铁锈，使土壤和岩石呈现红色。火星大气层稀薄，表面有巨大的峡谷和太阳系最高的火山。
Mars is rich in iron oxide, or rust, which gives its soil and rocks a red color. It has a thin atmosphere, enormous canyons, and the tallest volcanoes in the solar system.

木卫二（Europa）为何被列为搜寻地外生命的重点目标？

Why is Jupiter's moon Europa a top target in the search for extraterrestrial life?

伽利略探测与后续观测表明，木卫二冰壳之下存在全球性的液态咸水海洋，体积可能两倍于地球海洋，并有潮汐加热维持液态。冰下海洋加能量来源的组合，使它成为太阳系内最可能找到生命的地方之一。
Galileo and later observations show that beneath Europa's icy shell lies a global salty liquid-water ocean perhaps twice Earth's ocean volume, kept liquid by tidal heating. The combination of a subsurface ocean and an energy source makes it one of the most promising places in the Solar System to seek life.

八大行星可以分为哪两大类？

How are the eight planets divided into two groups?

八大行星按组成分为类地行星（水星、金星、地球、火星）和巨行星（木星、土星、天王星、海王星）。类地行星体积小、以岩石为主，巨行星质量大、以气体或冰为主。
The eight planets fall into terrestrial planets (Mercury, Venus, Earth, Mars) and giant planets (Jupiter, Saturn, Uranus, Neptune). Terrestrial planets are small and rocky, while giants are massive and made mostly of gas or ice.

水星的表面环境是怎样的？

What are conditions like on the surface of Mercury?

水星是离太阳最近的行星，几乎没有大气层，昼夜温差极大。白天温度可达430摄氏度，夜晚可降至零下180摄氏度。
Mercury is the closest planet to the Sun and has almost no atmosphere, leading to extreme temperature swings. Days can reach 430 degrees Celsius while nights drop to minus 180.

地球的唯一天然卫星叫什么？它如何造成潮汐？

What is Earth's only natural satellite, and how does it cause tides?

地球的天然卫星是月球，它与太阳的引力共同作用于地球海洋，形成潮汐。月球引力使正对和背对月球两侧的的海水微微隆起。
Earth's natural satellite is the Moon, whose gravity works with the Sun's to raise tides in the oceans. The Moon's pull makes the water bulge slightly on both the near and far sides of Earth.

土卫二（恩克拉多斯）为何被认为可能宜居？

Why is Saturn's moon Enceladus considered possibly habitable?

土卫二南极喷发出含盐分的水冰羽流，表明其冰壳之下存在液态海洋。羽流中检测到了有机物和氢气，具备生命所需的一些化学条件。
Enceladus shoots salty water-ice plumes from its south pole, evidence of a liquid ocean beneath its icy crust. The plumes contain organics and hydrogen, suggesting some of the chemistry life needs may be present.

哪颗行星拥有最显眼的行星环？

Which planet has the most prominent ring system?

土星拥有太阳系中最壮观、最明亮的行星环，主要由冰粒和岩石碎块组成。木星、天王星和海王星也拥有暗淡得多的环系。
Saturn has the most spectacular and brightest ring system in the solar system, made mainly of ice particles and rock fragments. Jupiter, Uranus, and Neptune have much fainter rings.

月球上为什么有那么多坑？

Why does the Moon have so many craters?

月球没有大气和板块运动来抹平表面，所以亿万年来被陨石撞击留下了大量环形山。地球上的坑则大多被风化、流水和板块运动抹去了。
The Moon has no atmosphere or plate tectonics to erase its surface, so billions of years of meteorite impacts left countless craters. On Earth, most craters have been worn away by weather, rivers, and tectonics.

我们生活的地球有什么特别之处？

What is special about our planet Earth?

地球是目前已知唯一拥有生命和液态水海洋的行星。它离太阳的距离不远不近，温度刚好让水保持液态。
Earth is the only planet known to harbor life and vast oceans of liquid water. Its distance from the Sun is just right for water to stay liquid.

太阳系里最大的卫星是哪一颗？

What is the largest moon in the Solar System?

最大的卫星是绕着木星转的木卫三（Ganymede），它甚至比水星还大一点点。它是唯一一颗自己拥有磁场的卫星，表面既有亮亮的老冰，也有暗暗的撞击坑。
The largest moon is Ganymede, which orbits Jupiter, and it is even a bit larger than Mercury. It is the only moon known to have its own magnetic field, with a surface of bright old ice and dark impact craters.
