「脏雪球」模型描述的是什么天体？

What object does the "dirty snowball" model describe?

「脏雪球」是天文学家对彗星的经典比喻，指彗星由冰（水、二氧化碳、甲烷）与尘埃岩石混合而成。这一模型由弗雷德·惠普尔提出，能解释彗星接近太阳时的喷发行为。
The "dirty snowball" is the classic analogy for a comet: a mixture of ices (water, carbon dioxide, methane) and dust or rock. Proposed by Fred Whipple, it explains why comets erupt as they approach the Sun.

通古斯事件可能由什么引起？

What likely caused the Tunguska event?

1908年发生在西伯利亚的通古斯大爆炸没有留下陨石坑，普遍被认为是一颗约50至60米的小天体在大气中高空爆炸所致。它夷平了约2000平方公里的森林。
The 1908 Tunguska explosion in Siberia left no impact crater and is widely thought to have been a roughly 50 to 60 meter object that detonated high in the atmosphere. It flattened about 2,000 square kilometers of forest.

彗星拖着的长尾巴是怎么形成的？

How does a comet get its long tail?

当彗星靠近太阳时，冰受热升华，喷射出气体和尘埃，并在太阳风和辐射压作用下形成指向背离太阳的彗尾。一颗彗星往往同时拥有尘埃尾和离子尾。
As a comet nears the Sun, its ice sublimates and releases gas and dust, which the solar wind and radiation pressure push into tails pointing away from the Sun. A comet often shows both a dust tail and an ion tail.

陨石通常分为哪几种主要类型？

What are the main types of meteorites?

陨石主要分为石陨石、铁陨石和石铁陨石。石陨石最常见，铁陨石来自小行星的金属核心，石铁陨石则介于两者之间。
Meteorites are mainly divided into stony, iron, and stony-iron types. Stony meteorites are the most common, iron ones come from the metal cores of asteroids, and stony-irons lie in between.

小行星带主要位于哪里？

Where is the main asteroid belt located?

主小行星带位于火星和木星轨道之间，包含数百万颗岩石与金属小天体。木星的强大引力阻止了它们聚集成为一颗行星。
The main asteroid belt lies between the orbits of Mars and Jupiter and holds millions of rocky and metallic bodies. Jupiter's strong gravity prevented them from assembling into a single planet.

流星和陨石有什么区别？

What is the difference between a meteor and a meteorite?

流星是太空里的小尘埃或小石块闯入大气层燃烧发出的光，也就是我们看到的「流星」。如果没烧完掉到地面上，剩下的石头就叫做陨石。
A meteor is a bit of dust or rock from space that burns up as it enters the atmosphere, producing the streak of light we see. If a piece survives and lands on the ground, the remaining rock is called a meteorite.
