什么是中微子振荡？它说明了什么？

What is neutrino oscillation, and what does it imply?

中微子在飞行中会在三种味之间转换，说明它们具有非零质量，这超出了最初的标准模型预言，是粒子物理的重要发现。
Neutrinos switch among three flavors while traveling, proving they have nonzero mass—beyond the original Standard Model prediction and a major particle-physics discovery.

太阳中微子是什么？为什么它能直接告诉我们太阳内部的情况？

What are solar neutrinos, and why do they reveal the Sun's interior directly?

太阳核心核聚变会同时产生中微子，它们几乎不与物质作用、瞬间穿过太阳和地球，因此携带着核心正在聚变的直接证据。
Solar fusion produces neutrinos that barely interact and stream straight through the Sun and Earth, carrying direct evidence that fusion is happening in the core right now.
