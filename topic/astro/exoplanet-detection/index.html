凌星法与视向速度法各自量出行星系统的哪些参数?两种方法结合为何能够锁定行星的平均密度并推断其成分?

Which parameters do the transit method and the radial-velocity method each deliver for a planetary system, and why does combining the two pin down a planet's bulk density and composition?

凌星光变曲线的深度给出行星半径与恒星半径之比,其形态和持续时间还编码轨道倾角与半长轴;视向速度的半振幅则给出质量乘以 sin 倾角的最小值。凌星的存在把倾角钉在接近九十度,sin 因子归一,于是真实质量与半径同时到手,平均密度随之可算,岩质、富水或厚气体包层的世界由此区分。开普勒望远镜产出数千个凌星候选体,ESPRESSO 等高稳定性阶梯光谱仪承担后续的质量复核。
The depth of a transit light curve gives the planet-to-star radius ratio, while its shape and duration encode orbital inclination and semi-major axis; the radial-velocity semi-amplitude yields mass multiplied by the sine of the inclination, a minimum value. Because a transit pins the inclination near ninety degrees, the sine factor normalizes, true mass and radius arrive together, and bulk density separates rocky, water-rich, or heavily gas-enveloped worlds. Kepler delivered thousands of transit candidates, with echelle spectrographs of extreme stability such as ESPRESSO performing follow-up mass verification.

不用看到行星本体,一颗星星短暂变亮也能暴露它的行星?微透镜法怎么工作?

A star briefly brightening can betray an unseen planet — how does microlensing work?

这是引力透镜的单星版:当一颗前景恒星恰好从背景恒星前掠过,其引力充当临时放大镜,背景星几天到几周内先亮后暗,光曲线形状由几何唯一决定。若前景星带着行星,行星的小透镜场会在主事件上叠加一个几小时到一天的尖峰——毛淑德与帕钦斯基1991年指出该信号能反推行星质量与轨道距离。方法的独特优势是对遥远暗淡的银河核球恒星敏感,专抓雪线以外的冷巨行星甚至自由漂浮行星;代价是不可重复:这样的对齐一生只有一次。
It is single-lens gravitational lensing: when a foreground star drifts across the line of sight to a background star, its gravity acts as a temporary magnifier and the source brightens then fades over days to weeks along a geometrically fixed curve. If the foreground star carries a planet, its own small lens adds a spike lasting hours to a day atop the main event — a signal Mao and Paczynski showed in 1991 encodes the planet's mass and orbital distance. The method uniquely senses dim distant stars toward the Galactic bulge, catching cold giant worlds beyond the snow line and even free-floating planets; the price is irrepeatability — the alignment happens once.

天文学家主要依靠哪两类观测效应来发现系外行星?各自有哪些代表性项目或探测成果?

Which two main kinds of observational effects do astronomers rely on to discover exoplanets, and what representative projects or results illustrate each?

绝大多数系外行星由凌星法和视向速度法发现:前者捕捉行星凌星时恒星亮度的周期性下降,NASA的开普勒与TESS任务借此累计找到数千颗行星;后者测量恒星光谱的多普勒摆动,能直接给出行星质量。两种方法联用可同时确定行星半径与质量,进而推算密度并判断其内部构成。
Most known exoplanets come from the transit and radial-velocity methods: the former catches the periodic dips in starlight when a planet crosses its star's disk, which is how NASA's Kepler and TESS missions have racked up thousands of planets, while the latter measures the Doppler wobble of stellar spectra and yields planet masses directly. Used together, the two methods pin down both radius and mass, so densities and interior compositions can be inferred.

#系外行星 #凌星与视向速度法

用凌星法找系外行星,为什么找到的大多是离恒星很近的「短周期」行星?

Why does the transit method mostly find planets hugging their stars on short periods?

两个因素叠加。其一,凌星要求行星轨道恰好侧向我们,轨道越小对齐概率越大(地球随机视角下仅约0.5%,近日行星可达百分之几);其二,确认行星至少要看到两次同深度、同周期的遮挡,周期越短,同样观测时长里重复越多、越易核实。开普勒卫星连拍四年,因此收获大量热木星与超级地球——它们未必代表宇宙常态,而是这种方法的舒适区,统计时需专门修正该选择效应。
Two factors stack. Geometrically, a transit requires the orbit to be edge-on within a narrow tilt window; the smaller the orbit, the higher that probability (Earth's is about 0.5 percent at random orientation, close-in planets reach several percent). Observationally, confirming a planet needs at least two matching dips, so shorter periods give more repeats per year of watching. Kepler's four-year stare therefore harvested hot Jupiters and super-Earths — not necessarily the galactic norm but this method's comfort zone, a selection effect statisticians must correct for.
