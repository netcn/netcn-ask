为什么中国古代天象记录对现代天文学很有价值？

Why are ancient Chinese sky records valuable to modern astronomy?

中国留下了世界上最长、最连续的新星、超新星和日食记录（如1054年超新星对应今天的蟹状星云），为研究恒星演化提供千年尺度的证据。
China has the world's longest continuous records of novae, supernovae and eclipses (e.g., the 1054 supernova now the Crab Nebula), giving millennia-scale evidence for stellar evolution.

为什么郭守敬的《授时历》能比西方早约300年达到同等精度？

Why did Guo Shoujing's calendar reach the same year-length accuracy as the West about 300 years earlier?

他采用更精确的圭表测影与多次冬至观测，并废除复杂的'上元积年'推算，直接用实测数据定朔，使回归年长度精确到365.2425天。
He used more precise gnomon shadow measurements and multiple winter-solstice observations, abandoned the cumbersome epoch reckoning, and set the calendar directly from data, fixing the tropical year at 365.2425 days.

阿拉伯和波斯天文在历史上如何影响中国？

How did Arabic and Persian astronomy influence China historically?

元代通过西域学者传入阿拉伯的历法、星表（如《万年历》）和仪器，郭守敬的授时历也吸收了其中的数学与测算方法。
In the Yuan dynasty, Persian and Arabic calendars, star catalogs and instruments arrived via scholars from the West, and Guo Shoujing's work absorbed their mathematics and methods.

什么是'干支'纪年，它和天文有关吗？

What is the sexagenary (ganzhi) cycle, and is it related to astronomy?

干支用10天干配12地支组成60年一轮的纪年法，其中12地支源自木星约12年绕天一周（岁星纪年），是早期天文观测的产物。
The ganzhi system pairs 10 heavenly stems with 12 earthly branches in a 60-year cycle; the 12 branches originated from Jupiter's roughly 12-year orbit (the 'year-star' reckoning), a product of early sky observation.

祖冲之在天文历法上有什么贡献？

What were Zu Chongzhi's contributions to astronomy and the calendar?

南朝的祖冲之编订《大明历》，首次把'岁差'引入中国历法计算，并提出更接近真实的交点月长度，提高了日月食预报精度。
The scholar Zu Chongzhi compiled the Daming Calendar, the first Chinese calendar to account for precession, and gave a more accurate nodal-month length, improving eclipse predictions.

中国古代的“客星”记录为什么对现代超新星遗迹研究具有独特价值?

Why are ancient Chinese records of "guest stars" (kexing) uniquely valuable for modern supernova remnant research?

中国史书连续两千余年系统记录了新见亮星的位置、颜色和可见时长,为公元185年、1006年、1054年等历史超新星提供了精确到月的时标。这些记录帮助现代天文学家把蟹状星云等遗迹与其母超新星直接对应起来,并据此测定遗迹的膨胀年龄。其他文明的记录大多缺乏这种连续性和定位精度,因此中国档案成为标定超新星频率的不可替代基准。
Chinese dynastic histories systematically logged the positions, colors, and visibility durations of newly appearing stars over more than two thousand years, providing month-level dates for historical supernovae such as those of AD 185, 1006, and 1054. These records allowed modern astronomers to link remnants like the Crab Nebula directly to their parent explosions and to derive remnant expansion ages. Few other civilizations kept records of comparable continuity and positional accuracy, making the Chinese archives an irreplaceable baseline for calibrating supernova rates.

#客星 #超新星遗迹

除了超新星记录,中国古代对彗星、太阳黑子和日月食的系统观测为什么在现代研究中仍不可替代?

Beyond supernova records, why do ancient Chinese systematic observations of comets, sunspots, and eclipses remain irreplaceable in modern research?

哈雷彗星两千余年的每次回归都见于中国史册,使现代天文学家得以反推其历史轨道演化;连续的黑子记载是重建蒙德极小期等千年尺度太阳活动的唯一独立基线;而跨越千年的日月食记录为研究地球自转速率的长期变化提供了不可复测的数据。这些长基线档案是任何现代仪器都无法回溯补测的。
Every return of Halley's Comet for two millennia appears in Chinese chronicles, letting modern astronomers reconstruct its historical orbit; the continuous sunspot record is the only independent baseline for reconstructing millennium-scale solar activity such as the Maunder Minimum; and eclipse records spanning centuries anchor studies of long-term changes in Earth's rotation rate. Such long-baseline archives cannot be retroactively supplied by any modern instrument.

为什么中国古代特别重视天象观测？

Why did ancient China pay special attention to observing celestial phenomena?

在'天人感应'观念下，日食、彗星、流星等被视为上天对帝王的警示，因此历代都设专职机构观测记录天象，既为编制历法也为占卜吉凶。
Under the belief that heaven reflected human affairs, eclipses, comets and meteors were seen as warnings to rulers, so each dynasty kept official astronomers both for calendar-making and for omens.

中国古代的'二十八宿'是什么？

What were the 'Twenty-Eight Mansions' in ancient Chinese astronomy?

二十八宿是把月亮和行星运行的黄道带分成28个天区，作为坐标参照，类似西方的黄道星座，是中国星官体系的基础。
The Twenty-Eight Mansions divided the zodiac belt into 28 sky zones used as coordinate markers for the Moon and planets—analogous to Western zodiac constellations and foundational to China's star-official system.

#二十八宿 #星官

郭守敬是谁，他有什么天文成就？

Who was Guo Shoujing and what were his astronomical achievements?

郭守敬是元代天文学家，主持编订《授时历》，一年长度精确到365.2425天，与现行公历相当；他还创制简仪、高表等仪器，并主持大规模四海测验。
Guo Shoujing was a Yuan-dynasty astronomer who led the Shoushi Calendar with a year of 365.2425 days—matching today's Gregorian calendar—and built instruments like the simplified armilla, conducting a vast geodetic survey.

古代中国人用星星做什么？

What did ancient Chinese people use the stars for?

他们用星星辨别方向、安排农时（看北斗指向定季节），并记录日食彗星等来推测吉凶。
They used stars to find direction, schedule farming by the Big Dipper's seasonal pointing, and record eclipses or comets as omens.

什么是'浑天说'和'浑天仪'？

What are the 'Huntian' (celestial sphere) theory and the armillary sphere?

浑天说认为天像鸡蛋壳包着大地，张衡改进并制造的浑天仪用水力驱动，能演示恒星每日东升西落，是早期的天球模型。
The Huntian theory viewed the sky as a sphere enclosing Earth like an egg's shell; Zhang Heng improved the water-powered armillary sphere that simulated the daily rotation of stars—an early celestial globe.

登封观星台是做什么用的？

What was the purpose of the Dengfeng Gaocheng Observatory?

它是元代郭守敬主持建造的观星与测影建筑，利用高表在夏至、冬至测量日影长短，用来定节气和回归年长度。
Built under Guo Shoujing in the Yuan dynasty, this observatory used a tall gnomon to measure solar shadow lengths at solstices, determining solar terms and the tropical year.
