伽马射线暴的瞬时辐射与其后的余辉,在辐射机制上有什么本质区别?

What fundamentally distinguishes the prompt emission of a gamma-ray burst from its afterglow?

瞬时辐射来自中心引擎(新生黑洞或磁星)驱动的超高洛伦兹因子外流内部的激波耗散或磁场重联,典型持续时间短于百秒。余辉则是这股外流扫过周边介质形成的外部激波,经由同步辐射从伽马段一路冷却到射电,其衰减行为遵循 Blandford-McKee 自相似解。Swift 卫星对 X 射线余辉的系统跟踪把两个阶段衔接起来,并揭示出暗示中心引擎持续活动的平台期成分。
Prompt emission comes from internal dissipation - shocks or magnetic reconnection within an ultra-relativistic outflow launched by a central engine such as a newborn black hole or magnetar. The afterglow instead forms when that ejecta sweeps up the surrounding medium in an external shock, cooling through synchrotron radiation from gamma rays down to radio while following the Blandford-McKee self-similar decay. Systematic Swift monitoring of X-ray afterglows bridged the two phases and revealed plateau features pointing to prolonged engine activity.

#伽马射线暴 #相对论激波

伽马射线暴的余辉为什么会从最初明亮的快速衰减变成缓慢的幂律式变暗？

Why does a gamma-ray burst afterglow fade from an initially brilliant rapid decline into a slow power-law dimming?

暴发生时相对论性外流以接近光速抛出，早期辐射来自内区致密物质相互碰撞产生的内激波，能流高但持续时间短。外流扫过星际介质后形成外激波，被加速的电子在磁场中同步辐射出X射线到射电波段。随着外流减速，激波能量注入率下降，同步特征频率和峰亮度一起降低，于是余辉流量按幂律随时间衰减。
The prompt emission comes from internal shocks in an ultra-relativistic outflow, which is luminous but brief. As the ejecta plows into the surrounding medium, an external shock accelerates electrons that synchrotron-radiate from X-ray to radio bands. The blast wave decelerates as it sweeps up material, lowering the energy injection rate and shifting the synchrotron peak downward, so the afterglow flux decays as a smooth power law in time.

活动星系核的喷流为什么能把能量输送到远超宿主星系尺度的距离？

How can jets from active galactic nuclei transport energy to distances far beyond their host galaxies?

黑洞吸积盘与旋转黑洞提取的能量沿磁化准直通道加速等离子体，使其以接近甚至表观超光速的速度外流。喷流内部磁场抑制了与星际介质的横向交换，端部形成的终端热斑只耗散一小部分能量，主体得以维持准直推进数十万秒差距。X射线观测到的巨型瓣状辐射和空腔证实这些结构能把数百万倍太阳质量的等效能量注入星系团介质。
Energy extracted from the accretion disk and spinning black hole accelerates plasma along a magnetized, collimated channel to relativistic speeds, sometimes with apparent superluminal motion. Strong internal magnetic fields limit lateral exchange with the interstellar medium, and only modest energy is lost at the terminal hot spot, letting the jet stay collimated for hundreds of kiloparsecs. X-ray lobes and cavities inflated by these flows confirm injection of energy equivalent to millions of solar masses into the intracluster medium.
