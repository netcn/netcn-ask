超新星遗迹中通过何种机制实现宇宙线的扩散激波加速（DSA）？

By what mechanism do supernova remnants achieve diffusive shock acceleration (DSA) of cosmic rays?

在遗迹激波前后，带电粒子被上游与下游磁场不规则性反复跨越激波面散射，每轮穿越获得与当地流速成比例的增益，能量按幂律累积——即一阶费米加速。当粒子能量高到能逃逸时形成观测到的硬幂律宇宙线谱，并伴随强同步辐射遗迹。
Across the remnant shock, charged particles are repeatedly scattered by upstream/downstream magnetic irregularities, crossing the shock front each cycle and gaining energy proportional to the flow speed—first-order Fermi acceleration. High-energy particles eventually escape, yielding the observed hard power-law spectrum and strong synchrotron remnants.

什么是 PeVatron，为什么探测银河系内的 PeV 宇宙线源很困难？

What is a PeVatron and why is detecting Galactic PeV cosmic-ray sources difficult?

PeVatron 是能把宇宙线加速到拍电子伏（10 的 15 次方电子伏）量级的天体源。困难在于：宇宙线带电粒子在银河系磁场中反复散射，到达地球时已失去方向信息；只能通过其撞击气体产生的伽马射线（强子成分）间接定位，且需排除轻子辐射混淆。
A PeVatron accelerates cosmic rays to peta-electronvolt (10^15 eV) energies. The difficulty: charged cosmic rays are scattered by Galactic magnetic fields and arrive isotropized, so direction is lost; one must infer sources indirectly from gamma rays from hadronic collisions with gas, while excluding leptonic-radiation confusion.

蟹状星云脉冲星风云（PWN）如何作为 TeV 耀发的标准校准源？

How does the Crab Nebula pulsar wind nebula (PWN) serve as a standard calibrator for TeV flares?

蟹状星云由公元 1054 年超新星遗迹中心的脉冲星驱动，其风星云在 GeV–TeV 波段有稳定且精确的能谱，被广泛用作地面切伦科夫望远镜与空间伽马望远镜的绝对流量校准基准。一旦蟹状星云自身出现耀发，便会挑战标准模型。
The Crab Nebula, powered by the pulsar from the AD 1054 supernova remnant, has a stable, precise GeV–TeV spectrum and is widely used as an absolute flux calibrator for ground and space gamma-ray telescopes. When the Crab itself flared, it challenged standard models.

宇宙线能谱为什么会在“膝”处明显变陡?这又如何支持超新星遗迹是银河系宇宙线的主要来源?

Why does the cosmic-ray energy spectrum steepen sharply at the knee, and how does this support supernova remnants as the dominant source of Galactic cosmic rays?

膝被视为银河系内加速器加速能力的上限:扩散激波加速所能达到的最高能量随磁场增强而抬高,若遗迹只握有微高斯级的普通星际磁场,质子远够不到膝部;观测却显示年轻遗迹的强激波能把磁场放大至上百微高斯,使质子恰好抵达三千万亿电子伏附近并率先挣脱银河系,能谱随之变陡。膝之上重核渐占上风,各元素的截断能量大致按电荷数成倍抬升,与理论预言吻合,这正是超新星遗迹主导银河系宇宙线的关键证据。
The knee marks the practical ceiling of accelerators inside our Galaxy. In diffusive shock acceleration the maximum reachable energy climbs with magnetic-field strength: holding only ordinary microgauss interstellar fields, remnant shocks would fall well short of it. Observations show that strong shocks in young remnants amplify their fields to hundreds of microgauss, letting protons just reach a few quadrillion electronvolts and break free first, which steepens the spectrum there. Above the knee heavier nuclei take over, with cutoff energies rising roughly in proportion to charge, matching theory and anchoring the case that supernova remnants dominate Galactic cosmic rays.

银河系中心的费米气泡是什么，如何被发现？

What are the Milky Way's Fermi bubbles and how were they discovered?

费米气泡是银河系中心上下对称、延伸约 25000 光年的巨大伽马射线与射电辐射结构。费米伽马射线空间望远镜在对全天巡天数据中剔除已知弥漫背景后意外发现，暗示数百万年前中心曾发生剧烈能量释放事件。
The Fermi bubbles are huge, bilaterally symmetric gamma-ray and radio structures extending ~25,000 light-years above and below the Galactic Center. Fermi discovered them serendipitously after subtracting known diffuse backgrounds from all-sky data, suggesting a violent energy-release episode millions of years ago.

大气切伦科夫望远镜（如 H.E.S.S.、MAGIC）如何探测高能伽马射线？

How do Imaging Atmospheric Cherenkov Telescopes (e.g., H.E.S.S., MAGIC) detect high-energy gamma-rays?

高能伽马光子进入大气后产生相对论性级联簇射，速度超过空气中光速，发射出短暂蓝色的切伦科夫光。地面大口径望远镜通过成像这些微弱蓝光闪烁，重建原伽马光子的能量与入射方向，覆盖从数十 GeV 到 TeV 的能段。
High-energy gamma photons create relativistic air showers that move faster than light in air, emitting brief blue Cherenkov light. Ground-based large telescopes image these faint flashes to reconstruct the primary gamma's energy and direction, covering tens of GeV to TeV.

太阳宇宙线与银河系宇宙线的主要区别是什么？

What is the main difference between solar and Galactic cosmic rays?

太阳宇宙线来自太阳耀斑与日冕物质抛射，能量较低（通常 MeV–GeV）、受太阳活动调制明显；银河系宇宙线来自超新星遗迹等河内加速源，能量可延伸至极高能并成分更重。二者在到达地球时受太阳风与地磁场的屏蔽程度不同。
Solar cosmic rays come from solar flares and CMEs, are lower energy (typically MeV–GeV), and strongly modulated by solar activity; Galactic cosmic rays originate in supernova remnants and reach far higher energies with heavier composition. They differ in how solar wind and Earth's field shield them.

宇宙线带电,乱糟糟的星际磁场一搅便迷失来路,那天文学家凭什么断言高能质子确实穿行在星际介质里并与之碰撞?

Cosmic rays are charged and tangled interstellar magnetic fields scramble their directions, so on what grounds do astronomers assert that high-energy protons really stream through the interstellar medium and collide with it?

直接证据来自伽马射线:高能质子撞击星际气体中的原子核,先产生中性π介子,再衰变为一对伽马光子,Fermi 卫星在银盘和多个分子云方向都测到与气体分布吻合的此类辐射,说明质子确实在与介质碰撞。另一条线索是电离:宇宙线能钻进紫外光照不进的稠密云核,H3+ 离子的吸收线由此推出可观的中性电离率。此外宇宙线压强显著支撑着星际气体,参与抵抗自引力坍缩。
The direct evidence comes from gamma rays: a high-energy proton striking a nucleus in interstellar gas produces a neutral pion that decays into a pair of gamma-ray photons, and the Fermi satellite has detected exactly this radiation across the Galactic plane and toward several molecular clouds, tracking the gas distribution and proving that protons really collide with the medium. Another clue is ionization: cosmic rays reach dense cloud cores that ultraviolet light cannot penetrate, and absorption lines of H3+ ions translate into a measurable ionization rate there. Cosmic-ray pressure also materially supports interstellar gas against self-gravity.

宇宙线中膝和踝结构分别意味着什么？

What do the knee and ankle features in the cosmic-ray spectrum mean?

宇宙线能谱在约 10 的 15 次方电子伏处斜率变陡称为膝，通常解释为银河系内加速源能量上限；在约 10 的 18 次方电子伏处斜率再次变平称为踝，常被视为河外宇宙线开始主导、并反映宇宙微波背景对极高能质子的 GZK 截断前过渡。
The cosmic-ray spectrum steepens at ~10^15 eV (the knee), usually interpreted as the energy limit of Galactic accelerators; it flattens again at ~10^18 eV (the ankle), often seen as extragalactic cosmic rays taking over, before the GZK cutoff from CMB interactions.
