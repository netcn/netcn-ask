什么是星系团和宇宙大尺度结构？

What are galaxy clusters and the cosmic web?

星系聚成星系团，再连成纤维状和墙状的宇宙网，中间是几乎空旷的巨洞，这是物质在引力下演化的结果。
Galaxies group into clusters and link into filamentary walls of the cosmic web, separated by near-empty voids—the result of matter evolving under gravity.

什么是星暴星系？

What is a starburst galaxy?

星暴星系在极短时间内以远高于正常的速度制造恒星，常由星系并合触发，这种爆发通常只能维持几亿年。
A starburst galaxy forms stars at a far higher rate than usual over a short span, often triggered by mergers, and such bursts typically last only a few hundred million years.

什么是星系并合，对星系演化有何影响？

What is galaxy merger and how does it affect evolution?

星系在引力下相撞并合，能触发猛烈恒星形成、重塑结构，并让中心黑洞共同增长，银河系未来就将与仙女座星系并合。
Galaxies collide and merge under gravity, triggering bursts of star formation, reshaping structure, and growing central black holes; the Milky Way will one day merge with Andromeda.

银河系有多大？

How big is the Milky Way?

银河系是直径约10万光年的棒旋星系，包含上千亿颗恒星，太阳位于距银心约2.7万光年的旋臂上。
The Milky Way is a barred spiral about 100,000 light-years across, with over a hundred billion stars; the Sun sits on an arm about 27,000 light-years from the center.

星系主要有哪些类型？

What are the main types of galaxies?

按形态可分为旋涡星系、椭圆星系和不规则星系；旋涡星系有显著的旋臂，椭圆星系则呈椭球状、恒星较老。
By shape they divide into spiral, elliptical and irregular galaxies; spirals show clear arms, while ellipticals are ellipsoidal with older stars.

我们在宇宙中是唯一的星系吗？

Are we the only galaxy in the Universe?

不是。可观测宇宙里就有数千亿个星系，每个都包含亿万颗恒星。
No. The observable Universe holds hundreds of billions of galaxies, each with billions of stars.
