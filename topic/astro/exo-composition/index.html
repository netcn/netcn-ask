原行星盘如何被观测？

How are protoplanetary disks observed?

ALMA等毫米波阵列能解析原行星盘内的环与缝隙，这些结构被认为是行星正在形成的证据，直接展示恒星与行星系统的“摇篮”。
Millimeter arrays like ALMA resolve rings and gaps inside protoplanetary disks; these structures are taken as evidence of planets forming, directly showing the 'cradle' of star and planet systems.

什么是地球相似指数（ESI）？

What is the Earth Similarity Index (ESI)?

ESI综合半径、密度、表面温度等参数给出与地球的相似度（0到1），用于筛选最可能宜居的候选，但只是粗略的排序工具。
The ESI combines radius, density and surface temperature into a similarity-to-Earth score (0 to 1), used to rank the most promising habitable candidates, though it is only a rough sorting tool.

“金发姑娘”宜居带只看温度吗？

Does the 'Goldilocks' habitable zone depend only on temperature?

宜居不仅要求液态水温度，还要合适的大气压、磁场、大气成分与恒星活动；综合这些的“宜居性”比单纯宜居带更严格。
Habitability needs not just liquid-water temperature but suitable atmospheric pressure, magnetic field, atmospheric composition and stellar activity; true habitability is stricter than the simple habitable zone.
