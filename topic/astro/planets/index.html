科学家如何证明木卫二冰壳之下存在海洋？

How did scientists demonstrate an ocean beneath Europa's icy crust?

伽利略号探测器发现，木卫二在木星磁场中穿行时会产生感应磁场，这是其内部存在导电层的最有力证据，而最合理的导电层就是咸水海洋。与此同时，木卫二表面的裂缝、碎冰块和“混沌地形”表明冰壳一直在缓慢活动，表面年龄极轻。潮汐加热则为海洋保持液态提供了持续能量。据此估算，木卫二的海洋深度可能达数十到上百公里。
The Galileo spacecraft found that Europa generates an induced magnetic field as it moves through Jupiter's magnetosphere, the strongest evidence for an electrically conductive layer beneath the surface, most plausibly a salty ocean. At the same time, the moon's surface cracks, ice rafts and chaotic terrain reveal ongoing crustal activity and a very young surface age. Tidal heating supplies the energy that keeps the ocean liquid. Estimates put the ocean's depth at tens to more than a hundred kilometers.

为什么木卫一拥有太阳系最活跃的火山？

Why does Io host the most volcanically active surface in the solar system?

木卫一与木卫二、木卫三处于轨道共振之中，导致木星的巨大潮汐力周期性挤压和拉伸它。潮汐摩擦把机械能转化为热量，使木卫一内部保持部分熔融。这些热量驱动了数百座活火山，喷出硫和二氧化硫，形成黄橙色的硫沉积地表。木卫一由此成为太阳系中火山活动最活跃的天体，也是除地球外唯一确认正在喷发的天体。
Io is locked in orbital resonance with Europa and Ganymede, so Jupiter's enormous tides alternately squeeze and stretch it. Tidal friction converts this mechanical energy into heat, keeping the interior partially molten. That heat drives hundreds of active volcanoes erupting sulfur and sulfur dioxide, coating the surface in yellow-orange sulfur deposits. Io is thus the most volcanically active body in the solar system and the only one besides Earth confirmed to be erupting today.

国际天文学联合会如何定义“行星”？

How does the IAU define a 'planet'?

按国际天文学联合会2006年的定义，行星须绕太阳公转、自身引力使其近似球形、并已清空轨道附近的其他天体。冥王星因未满足第三条而被归为矮行星。
Per the IAU's 2006 definition, a planet orbits the Sun, is round from its own gravity, and has cleared its orbital neighborhood. Pluto fails the third criterion and is a dwarf planet.

土星卡西尼环缝内缘处，环粒子公转周期恰约为卫星土卫一的一半。这种整数比对应背后是怎样的共振动力学？

At the inner edge of Saturn's Cassini Division, ring particles orbit with periods close to exactly half that of the moon Mimas. What resonance dynamics lie behind this integer ratio?

该半径构成与土卫一的2:1平运动共振：粒子每绕土星两圈，便在同一相对方位受到一次土卫一的引力牵引，同相位的重复摄动不断泵大轨道偏心率。偏心率增大使粒子径向运动加剧、碰撞更频繁，耗散与角动量交换遂将物质清出共振带，留下宽约4800千米、边缘锐利的空隙。
This radius forms a 2:1 mean-motion resonance with Mimas: every two orbits a particle completes about Saturn, it receives one tug from the moon at the same relative position. Perturbations recurring in phase coherently pump up orbital eccentricity. The enlarged radial excursions intensify collisions among neighboring particles, and the resulting dissipation and angular-momentum exchange sweep material out of the resonance zone, leaving a gap about 4,800 kilometers wide with sharply defined boundaries.

海王星上的『大黑斑』是什么？

What was the Great Dark Spot on Neptune?

大黑斑是1989年旅行者2号发现于海王星南半球的一个巨型风暴系统，面积接近地球大小。它类似木星大红斑，但由甲烷吸收使暗区呈现深蓝黑色。有趣的是，后来观测发现它已消失，说明海王星大气变化很快。
The Great Dark Spot was a giant storm in Neptune's southern hemisphere spotted by Voyager 2 in 1989, about the size of Earth. Like Jupiter's red spot but appearing dark blue-black from methane absorption, it later vanished in later observations, showing Neptune's atmosphere changes fast.

毅力号火星车在耶泽罗撞击坑发现了什么？

What has the Perseverance rover found at Jezero crater?

耶泽罗是约 38 亿年前的一个湖泊遗址，具备河流入湖的三角洲沉积。毅力号确认了这里的古三角洲和湖底碳酸盐岩，并在岩石中采集了多份样品管，计划由后续任务送回地球分析。这些样品可能保存着火星早期环境甚至生命活动的痕迹。
Jezero is the site of a lake that existed some 3.8 billion years ago, with a river delta feeding into it. Perseverance has confirmed the ancient delta and lakebed carbonates there, and has sealed rock samples in tubes for a future mission to return to Earth. Those samples may preserve traces of early Martian environments or life.

好奇号火星车发现了什么与生命相关的物质？

What life-related material has the Curiosity rover found?

好奇号在盖尔撞击坑的泥岩中检测到了多种有机分子，包括含碳的复杂化合物，并发现甲烷浓度会随季节变化。有机分子本身不等于生命，但它们是生命的前体物质，证明火星在过去能够保存有机碳。
Curiosity has detected a variety of organic molecules, carbon-bearing compounds, in the mudstones of Gale crater, and has observed that methane levels vary with the seasons. Organic molecules are not life itself, but they are building blocks of life and show that ancient Mars could preserve organic carbon.

水星那么热，为什么两极却可能有冰？

Mercury is scorching hot, so why might its poles still harbor ice?

水星自转轴几乎不倾斜，两极有些永久阴影的撞击坑从未被阳光直射，温度低至约 -200℃，从彗星撞击带来的水或太阳风氢氧结合的水得以以冰的形式保存。
Mercury's axis is barely tilted, so some polar impact craters stay in permanent shadow and never receive direct sunlight, reaching about -200°C; water delivered by comet impacts or formed from solar-wind hydrogen and oxygen can survive there as ice.

水星表面有什么特殊地貌？为什么它很热却仍可能藏有水冰？

What special features does Mercury's surface have, and why might it hold water ice despite extreme heat?

水星表面布满撞击坑，最大的撞击盆地是直径约 1550 公里的卡洛里斯盆地，内部由多环结构环绕。在水星两极的永久阴影坑内，雷达观测发现了疑似水冰的反射信号，那里的温度常年低于零下 170 摄氏度，冰可以长期保存。
Mercury's surface is heavily cratered; its largest impact basin, Caloris, is about 1,550 km wide and surrounded by concentric rings. Radar observations have detected bright reflections consistent with water ice inside polar craters in permanent shadow, where temperatures stay below about minus 170 degrees Celsius year-round, so ice can survive for long periods.

Why is Venus' surface so hot, and what kind of world is it?

金星被浓密的二氧化碳大气和硫酸云包裹，大气压约为地球的 90 倍，温室效应使其表面温度高达约 460 摄氏度，比水星还热。金星的表面布满火山地貌和缓慢流动的熔岩平原，过去可能长期处于火山活动状态。
Venus is wrapped in a dense carbon-dioxide atmosphere with sulfuric-acid clouds at about 90 times Earth's pressure; the runaway greenhouse effect heats its surface to about 460 degrees Celsius, hotter than Mercury. Its surface is covered with volcanic landforms and slowly moving lava plains, and it was probably volcanically active for long periods.

为什么巨行星的光环都紧贴行星本体，而卫星只能在更远处形成？洛希极限如何划出这条界线？

Why do giant planets' rings hug the planet itself while satellites form only farther out? How does the Roche limit draw this boundary?

行星引力随距离变化，天体近侧与远侧所受引力之差即潮汐力。在洛希极限以内，潮汐力超过冰质碎块的自引力，物质无法聚结成卫星，只能摊薄成环；越过极限后自引力重占上风，碎片便能吸积长大为卫星。土星明亮的主环恰位于其洛希极限之内，紧贴环系外缘的牧羊犬卫星正是这条分界线的直观标志。
A planet's pull weakens with distance, so the difference in gravity across a body—the tidal force—grows toward the planet. Inside the Roche limit this force exceeds the self-gravity binding icy rubble together, so material cannot coalesce into moons and spreads into rings instead; beyond the limit self-gravity wins and debris accretes into satellites. Saturn's bright main rings sit inside its Roche limit, and the shepherd moons hugging their outer edge neatly mark this boundary.

行星绕太阳的轨道是什么形状？

What shape are the orbits of planets around the Sun?

开普勒第一定律说行星的轨道是椭圆，太阳坐在其中一个焦点上，圆只是偏心率等于零的特例。平方反比引力下的束缚解本来就是圆锥曲线，椭圆是其中不逃逸的那一类。多数行星的偏心率小于 0.1，看着几乎正圆，偏心率最大的水星也才 0.2 出头。易混的是：太阳在焦点而非中心，行星到太阳的距离在一年里确有变化，但季节并不来自它；何况行星互相摄动，真实轨道也不是闭合的完美椭圆。
Kepler's first law says the orbit of a planet is an ellipse with the Sun at one of its two foci, and a circle is only the special case of zero eccentricity. The bound solutions of an inverse-square gravitational force are conic sections anyway, the ellipse being the class that does not escape. Most planets have eccentricities below 0.1, so their orbits look almost circular, and even Mercury, the most eccentric of them, is just over 0.2. The easy mistake: the Sun sits at a focus rather than the center, so a planet's distance from the Sun really does change over a year, but the seasons do not come from that; and since the planets perturb one another, a real orbit is not a perfectly closed ellipse either.

This seasonal carbon-dioxide cycle drives the annual waxing and waning of the Martian polar caps.

科学家如何根据撞击坑来推测行星表面的年龄？

How do scientists estimate the age of a planetary surface from impact craters?

原理是撞击坑越多，表面就越古老：古老表面经历了更长时间的陨石轰击，坑密度更高。用月球上已经精确测定年代的岩石对撞击坑密度进行校准，就可以把火星、水星等天体表面的坑密度换算成绝对年龄。
The principle is that more craters mean an older surface: ancient surfaces have endured bombardment longer and carry higher crater densities. By calibrating crater density against precisely dated lunar rocks, densities on Mars, Mercury and other bodies can be converted into absolute ages.

冥王星为什么不再算行星了？

Why is Pluto not called a planet anymore?

天文学家重新定下了行星的规矩，冥王星太小，轨道旁边还有很多小伙伴挤在一起。于是大家改叫它矮行星。
Astronomers agreed on new rules for planets, and Pluto is small with many icy neighbours sharing its path. So now it is called a dwarf planet.

海王星是怎样被算出来的？

How was Neptune calculated into existence?

19 世纪天文学家发现天王星轨道偏离预测，推断外侧有未知行星的引力扰动，勒维耶用数学算出位置，伽勒据此在 1846 年观测到海王星。
In the 19th century, observed deviations in Uranus's orbit implied an unseen perturbing planet; Le Verrier computed its position mathematically, and Galle observed Neptune in 1846 based on that prediction.

#海王星 #数学 #发现

类地行星和气态巨行星为什么差别这么大？

Why are the rocky planets and the gas giants so different?

太阳系形成时内圈温度很高，只有岩石和金属能凝结，所以水星到火星都是小而致密的岩石世界。外圈足够冷，冰和气体也能聚集，行星因此长得又大又轻。木星和土星就以氢和氦为主。
When the Solar System formed, the inner region was hot and only rock and metal could condense, so Mercury through Mars became small, dense worlds. Farther out it was cold enough for ice and gas to gather, letting planets grow huge and light. That is why Jupiter and Saturn are mostly hydrogen and helium.

#行星形成 #太阳系

什么样的天体才能叫行星？

What does an object need in order to be called a planet?

按国际天文学联合会的规定，行星要绕着太阳运行，自身引力把它压成接近球形，还要清空轨道附近的其他小天体。三条都满足，才算真正的行星。
The International Astronomical Union says a planet must orbit the Sun, be round because of its own gravity, and have cleared other small bodies from its orbit. Only an object that meets all three rules counts.

火星上会刮沙尘暴吗？

Does Mars have dust storms?

火星经常刮沙尘暴，有的大到笼罩整个星球，能持续好几个星期。那里的空气很稀薄，所以风虽然吹得远，力气却不算大。
Mars has frequent dust storms, and some grow large enough to cover the whole planet for weeks. Its air is very thin, so the wind travels far but does not push very hard.
