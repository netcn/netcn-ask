星系团的缺失质量是如何通过引力透镜被揭示的？

How was the missing mass in galaxy clusters revealed through gravitational lensing?

兹威基 1933 年从后发座星系团成员星系的速度弥散推断，所需引力质量远大于可见恒星质量。现代弱引力透镜通过背景星系的形状畸变直接测绘星系团总质量分布，证实大部分质量来自不发光暗物质。
In 1933 Zwicky inferred from the velocity dispersion of galaxies in the Coma Cluster that the gravity needed far exceeds the visible stellar mass. Modern weak gravitational lensing maps a cluster's total mass directly from the distorted shapes of background galaxies, confirming that most of the mass is non-luminous dark matter.

重子声学振荡是什么？

What are baryon acoustic oscillations?

重子声学振荡是早期宇宙中声波在光子-重子流体中留下的密度涟漪，在大爆炸后约三十八万年冻结下来。它在今天星系的关联函数中表现为约五亿光年的特征尺度，被用作宇宙学标准尺。通过测量这一尺度随红移的变化，可约束暗能量和膨胀历史。
Baryon acoustic oscillations are density ripples left by sound waves in the early-universe photon-baryon fluid, frozen in about 380,000 years after the Big Bang. They appear today as a characteristic scale of about 500 million light-years in galaxy correlations, used as a cosmological standard ruler. Tracking this scale with redshift constrains dark energy and the expansion history.

宇宙再电离是什么时候、如何发生的？

When and how did cosmic reionization occur?

宇宙再电离发生在红移约6到15之间，最早形成的恒星和星系释放的紫外光子逐渐电离了弥漫的中性氢。这一过程结束了宇宙黑暗时期，使宇宙对紫外光变得透明。莱曼α森林和21厘米线观测正在精细描绘其时空进程。
Cosmic reionization occurred between redshifts of about 6 and 15, as ultraviolet photons from the first stars and galaxies progressively ionized the diffuse neutral hydrogen. This ended the cosmic dark ages and made the universe transparent to UV light. The Lyman-alpha forest and 21-cm line observations are mapping its spacetime progress.

星系如何随红移演化？

How do galaxies evolve with redshift?

观测显示高红移（早期宇宙）星系普遍更小、更不规则且恒星形成率更高，随宇宙年龄增长通过并合与吸积逐渐变成今天的大型盘状或椭圆星系。恒星质量函数也随红移向低质量端演化。研究这种演化需结合深场巡天与宇宙学模拟。
Observations show that high-redshift (early-universe) galaxies are generally smaller, more irregular, and form stars at higher rates, gradually becoming today's large disks and ellipticals through mergers and accretion. The stellar mass function also shifts toward lower masses at high redshift. Studying this requires deep-field surveys combined with cosmological simulations.

星系化学演化研究什么？

What does galactic chemical evolution study?

星系化学演化追踪恒星核合成产生的元素如何生成、混合并再进入星际介质，从而改变星系的金属丰度分布。通过比较恒星与气体中的元素比值，可反推恒星形成历史和吸积率。它把星系动力学、恒星演化与观测丰度联系起来。
Galactic chemical evolution tracks how elements produced by stellar nucleosynthesis are generated, mixed, and returned to the interstellar medium, changing the galaxy's metallicity distribution. Comparing elemental ratios in stars and gas infers the star formation history and accretion rates. It links galaxy dynamics, stellar evolution, and observed abundances.

什么是莱曼α森林？

What is the Lyman-alpha forest?

莱曼α森林是遥远类星体光谱中一系列密集的吸收线，由沿途不同红移的中性氢云对莱曼α波长的吸收造成。它像是宇宙中的“指纹”，让天文学家测绘星系际介质的密度与分布。通过分析森林的统计特性，可约束宇宙再电离历史和物质功率谱。
The Lyman-alpha forest is a series of dense absorption lines in the spectra of distant quasars, caused by intervening neutral hydrogen clouds at various redshifts absorbing at the Lyman-alpha wavelength. It acts like a cosmic fingerprint, mapping the density and distribution of the intergalactic medium. Its statistics constrain the reionization history and the matter power spectrum.

旋涡星系和椭圆星系有何不同？

How do spiral and elliptical galaxies differ?

旋涡星系拥有旋转的盘状结构和明显的旋臂，恒星形成仍在活跃进行，富含气体和尘埃。椭圆星系则呈椭球状，几乎不含冷气体，恒星较年老，基本停止造星。这种形态差异反映了它们不同的形成与并合历史。
Spiral galaxies have a rotating disk with distinct arms where star formation is still active, rich in gas and dust. Elliptical galaxies are ellipsoidal, nearly devoid of cold gas, with older stars and little ongoing star formation. The morphological difference reflects their distinct formation and merger histories.

活动星系核是什么？

What is an active galactic nucleus?

活动星系核是星系中心区域因超大质量黑洞吸积而异常明亮的核，可释放出超越整个星系恒星总光度的能量。其表现形式包括类星体、耀变体和赛弗特星系等。喷流和辐射会显著影响其宿主星系的演化。
An active galactic nucleus is the unusually bright core of a galaxy powered by accretion onto a supermassive black hole, emitting energy that can outshine all its stars combined. Manifestations include quasars, blazars, and Seyfert galaxies. Its jets and radiation strongly influence the host galaxy's evolution.

矮星系在宇宙中扮演什么角色？

What role do dwarf galaxies play in the universe?

矮星系是数量最多、质量最小的星系，常作为大星系的“积木”通过并合促成星系成长。它们还保留着早期宇宙的化学与恒星形成记录，是研究星系形成的重要样本。许多矮星系被暗物质晕主导，是研究暗物质的天然实验室。
Dwarf galaxies are the most numerous and least massive systems, often acting as building blocks whose mergers grow larger galaxies. They also preserve records of early-universe chemistry and star formation, making them key samples for studying galaxy formation. Many are dark-matter-dominated, serving as natural laboratories for dark matter.

为什么星系自转曲线暗示暗物质的存在？

Why do galaxy rotation curves imply dark matter?

如果星系质量仅来自可见恒星和气体，外缘恒星的转速应随距离下降。但观测显示转速在很大半径内基本保持平坦，说明存在大量看不见的质量提供额外引力。这一证据是最早支持暗物质的观测之一。
If a galaxy's mass came only from visible stars and gas, outer stars' speeds should drop with distance. Instead, observations show flat rotation curves over large radii, implying unseen mass supplying extra gravity. This was among the earliest observational supports for dark matter.

星系团中的暗物质晕起什么作用？

What role does the dark matter halo play in galaxy clusters?

暗物质晕是包裹星系团的巨大引力势阱，其质量可达可见物质的五倍以上，是维持星系团整体结构的关键。星系和炽热气体都嵌在晕中，靠其引力束缚在一起。通过对引力透镜和X射线气体的测量，可推断晕的质量分布。
The dark matter halo is a vast gravitational well enveloping a galaxy cluster, with several times more mass than the visible matter and crucial for holding the cluster together. Galaxies and hot gas are embedded in it, bound by its gravity. Gravitational lensing and X-ray gas measurements reveal the halo's mass distribution.

银河系属于哪种类型的星系？

What type of galaxy is the Milky Way?

银河系是一个棒旋星系，具有中央棒状结构和多条旋臂，直径约 10 万光年，包含上千亿颗恒星。我们太阳系位于其中一条旋臂外缘，距银心约 2.7 万光年。
The Milky Way is a barred spiral galaxy with a central bar and several spiral arms, about 100,000 light-years across and containing hundreds of billions of stars. Our Solar System sits in the outer part of one arm, roughly 27,000 light-years from the Galactic center.

银河系属于哪个更大的结构？

What larger structure does the Milky Way belong to?

银河系是本星系群的一员，本星系群还包括仙女座星系、三角座星系等约八十个星系。本星系群又属于更大的室女座超星系团（本超星系团）。再往上，这些结构共同组成了拉尼亚凯亚超星系团。
The Milky Way is a member of the Local Group, which also includes the Andromeda Galaxy, the Triangulum Galaxy, and about eighty others. The Local Group in turn belongs to the larger Virgo Supercluster. On even larger scales these structures form the Laniakea Supercluster.

可观测宇宙里大约有多少星系？

Roughly how many galaxies are in the observable universe?

根据深空巡天的统计，可观测宇宙中星系的数量估计在两千亿个左右。每个星系平均含有数百亿颗恒星，因此恒星总数远超地球上沙粒的数量。随着望远镜更灵敏，这个数字仍在不断修正。
Deep-field surveys suggest there are on the order of 200 billion galaxies in the observable universe. Each typically holds tens of billions of stars, so the total number of stars vastly exceeds Earth's grains of sand. As telescopes become more sensitive, this estimate keeps being revised.

星系是什么，宇宙里大约有多少？

What is a galaxy, and roughly how many are there in the universe?

星系是由引力绑在一起的上千亿颗恒星、气体、尘埃和暗物质组成的大集合，比如我们的银河系。可观测宇宙里估计约有 2 万亿个星系，其中大多极其遥远。
A galaxy is a vast collection of hundreds of billions of stars, gas, dust and dark matter bound together by gravity, like our own Milky Way. The observable universe is estimated to contain about two trillion galaxies, most of them extremely distant.
