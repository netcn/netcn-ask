什么是千新星，它和金等重元素有什么关系？

What is a kilonova and how is it linked to gold and heavy elements?

千新星是两颗中子星并合时释放的短暂明亮爆发，其极端中子流通过r过程合成了金、铂等大量重元素。
A kilonova is a brief brilliant burst from merging neutron stars; its extreme neutron flux forges gold, platinum and many heavy elements via the r-process.

大质量恒星生命终结时会发生什么？

What happens when a massive star ends its life?

大质量恒星核心燃料耗尽后无法支撑自身引力，核心坍缩成中子星或黑洞，外层猛烈反弹形成核心坍缩超新星，并向太空抛射重元素。
When a massive star exhausts its fuel it can no longer support itself; the core collapses into a neutron star or black hole, and the rebounding outer layers explode as a core-collapse supernova, dispersing heavy elements.

为什么Ia型超新星被用作标准烛光？

Why are Type Ia supernovae used as standard candles?

Ia型超新星峰值光度几乎恒定，因为白矮星都在同一质量极限爆炸，所以比较观测亮度就能推算距离，帮助发现宇宙加速膨胀。
Type Ia supernovae have a nearly constant peak luminosity because white dwarfs explode at the same mass limit, so comparing observed brightness yields distance—key to discovering the accelerating Universe.

如果一颗超新星在距地球几十光年内爆发，会怎样？

What would happen if a supernova exploded within a few tens of light-years of Earth?

近距离超新星释放的强烈宇宙射线和伽马射线会破坏臭氧层、增加辐射，可能引发生物大灭绝，但目前已知近邻恒星都没有即将爆发的迹象。
A nearby supernova's intense cosmic rays and gamma rays could damage the ozone layer and raise radiation, possibly causing mass extinctions, but no known neighboring star is about to explode.

什么是超新星遗迹，比如蟹状星云？

What is a supernova remnant like the Crab Nebula?

超新星遗迹是爆炸抛出的气体在星际空间膨胀形成的云团；蟹状星云是1054年一颗超新星的遗骸，中心还藏着一颗快速自转的中子星。
A supernova remnant is the expanding cloud of gas from the blast; the Crab Nebula is the debris of a 1054 supernova and hides a rapidly spinning neutron star at its center.

Ia型与II型超新星有何区别？

How do Type Ia and Type II supernovae differ?

II型来自大质量恒星核心坍缩，富含氢；Ia型来自白矮星吸积到钱德拉塞卡极限后的热核爆炸，几乎不含氢且亮度高度一致。
Type II comes from the core collapse of a massive star and is hydrogen-rich; Type Ia is a thermonuclear explosion of a white dwarf reaching the Chandrasekhar limit, nearly hydrogen-free with a very uniform brightness.

超新星和新星一样吗？

Is a supernova the same as a nova?

不一样。超新星是恒星毁灭性的终结爆炸，新星则是白矮星表面氢的周期性爆发，恒星本身仍然存在。
No. A supernova is a star's catastrophic final explosion, while a nova is a periodic surface outburst of hydrogen on a white dwarf, which survives.
