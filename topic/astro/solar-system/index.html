太阳星云假说如何解释行星的成分分带（内类地、外巨行星）？

How does the solar nebula hypothesis explain the compositional zoning of the Solar System (terrestrial inside, giants outside)?

在原行星盘内区温度高，挥发性冰无法凝结，只剩难熔的岩石和金属形成类地行星。外区低于雪线，水冰等挥发物可以富集，于是形成以冰和气体为主的巨行星，这一温度梯度是成分分带的根本原因。
In the inner protoplanetary disk temperatures were too high for volatile ices to condense, leaving only refractory rock and metal to build terrestrial planets. Beyond the snow line, water ice and other volatiles could accumulate, enabling ice- and gas-rich giant planets; this temperature gradient is the root cause of the compositional zoning.

木卫一为什么会成为太阳系里火山活动最猛烈的天体？

Why is Io the most volcanically active body in the Solar System?

答案在轨道共振和潮汐加热。木卫一、木卫二和木卫三处于拉普拉斯共振中，公转周期成简单整数比，迫使木卫一的轨道保持偏心。轨道每圈被木星引力拉伸变形，内部因周期性弯折而摩擦生热，热量足以维持约400座活火山。它的表面不断被硫化合物重新覆盖，形成五彩斑斓的地貌。
The answer lies in orbital resonance and tidal heating. Io, Europa, and Ganymede are locked in the Laplace resonance, their orbital periods in simple integer ratios, which keeps Io's orbit eccentric. Each circuit, Jupiter's gravity flexes the moon, and the internal friction from repeated distortion generates heat enough to sustain about 400 active volcanoes. Its surface is endlessly resurfaced by sulphur compounds, producing a vivid, colourful terrain.

How do the Kuiper Belt and the Oort Cloud differ in their formation?

柯伊伯带位于海王星轨道之外约30至50个天文单位，是形成于原行星盘外缘的冰质小天体盘，轨道大致位于黄道面附近。奥尔特云则在远处呈球状分布，主要由早期被巨行星散射出去的天体组成，并受到银河系潮汐和恒星摄动的显著塑造。
The Kuiper Belt lies just beyond Neptune at about 30 to 50 astronomical units and is a disk of icy bodies formed at the edge of the protoplanetary disk, with orbits near the ecliptic. The Oort Cloud is a distant spherical reservoir made largely of objects scattered outward by the giant planets and later shaped by galactic tides and passing stars.

What is the planetary migration hypothesis (Nice model) for the solar system?

尼斯模型认为，早期巨行星的轨道曾经不稳定并发生过显著的迁移，把大量小天体抛向太阳系内外。这一过程被认为造成了后期的重轰炸事件，并塑造了外太阳系的结构。
The Nice model proposes that the giant planets' orbits were once unstable and underwent major migrations, flinging many small bodies inward and outward. This is thought to have caused the later heavy bombardment and shaped the structure of the outer solar system.

太阳系相对于银道面的倾角约是多少，这种几何关系有什么观测意义？

About what is the Solar System's inclination to the galactic plane, and what is the observational significance of this geometry?

太阳系黄道面与银河系银道面的夹角约 60 度，但因两平面交线恰靠近人马座，银心方向其实紧邻黄道。这一几何影响了星际尘埃消光在天空中的分布，也决定了巡天观测银道面附近天体时的策略与难度。
The ecliptic plane of the Solar System is inclined about 60 degrees to the galactic plane, yet because the line of intersection of the two planes lies near Sagittarius, the direction of the Galactic center actually sits close to the ecliptic. This geometry shapes the sky distribution of interstellar dust extinction and dictates survey strategies and difficulties when observing objects near the Galactic plane.

小行星带中的“柯克伍德空隙”是如何形成的？

How do the “Kirkwood gaps” in the asteroid belt form?

柯克伍德空隙是小行星带中几乎缺失小行星的特定半径区域，出现在与木星轨道周期成简单整数比（如3:1、2:1）的位置。在这些轨道共振点，木星引力周期性扰动使小行星轨道变得不稳定，最终将其散射出去。
Kirkwood gaps are regions in the asteroid belt nearly empty of asteroids, located where orbital periods are simple integer ratios (such as 3:1, 2:1) of Jupiter's. At these resonances, Jupiter's gravity periodically perturbs asteroids onto unstable orbits, scattering them away over time.

Where is the edge of the solar system?

太阳耀斑和日冕物质抛射有什么区别？哪个更容易引发地磁暴？

How do solar flares and coronal mass ejections differ, and which is more likely to trigger a geomagnetic storm?

太阳耀斑是太阳大气中磁重联释放能量产生的电磁辐射爆发，能量以光速以 X 射线和紫外形式到达地球，主要影响电离层。日冕物质抛射则把数十亿吨等离子体连同其冻结的磁场整个抛向太空，需要一到三天才能到达地球。正是 CME 携带的磁场与地球磁场相互作用，才产生了最强的地磁暴。因此，相比耀斑，日冕物质抛射更容易引发强地磁暴。
A solar flare is an explosive burst of electromagnetic radiation from magnetic reconnection in the solar atmosphere; its X-rays and ultraviolet arrive at light speed and mainly disturb the ionosphere. A coronal mass ejection (CME), by contrast, hurls billions of tons of plasma and its frozen-in magnetic field into space, taking one to three days to reach Earth. It is the CME's magnetic field interacting with Earth's magnetosphere that produces the strongest geomagnetic storms. A CME is therefore far more likely than a flare to trigger a major geomagnetic storm.

柯伊伯带和奥尔特云到底有什么区别？

What exactly distinguishes the Kuiper belt from the Oort cloud?

柯伊伯带是海王星轨道外30到50天文单位处的环状带，主体位于黄道面附近，包含冥王星、阋神星等冰质小天体，是短周期彗星的来源。奥尔特云则是包裹整个太阳系、范围从数千到约十万天文单位的球形云，内含上万亿颗冰体，靠附近恒星经过的引力扰动把长周期彗星抛向内太阳系。奥尔特云至今没有被直接观测到，只能从彗星轨道推断它的存在。
The Kuiper belt is a ring of icy bodies 30 to 50 astronomical units beyond Neptune, lying mostly near the ecliptic and home to Pluto, Eris, and others; it is the source of short-period comets. The Oort cloud is instead a spherical shell enveloping the whole Solar System, from a few thousand out to roughly 100,000 AU, holding trillions of icy objects that passing stars nudge inward as long-period comets. It has never been observed directly and is inferred solely from comet orbits.

The Kuiper Belt is a disk-shaped region just beyond Neptune, roughly 30 to 55 astronomical units from the Sun, containing many icy bodies including Pluto. The Oort Cloud is a vast spherical shell surrounding the entire Solar System, reaching tens of thousands of astronomical units, and is thought to be the source of long-period comets. They differ greatly in shape, distance, and composition.

太阳系的角动量分布为何被视为一个难题？

Why is the angular momentum distribution of the solar system considered a puzzle?

太阳占据了太阳系约99.8%的质量，却只拥有总角动量的约1%，绝大多数角动量集中在行星的轨道上。这一现象通常用吸积盘中磁场与物质的耦合（磁制动）来解释，是恒星形成研究中的经典问题。
The Sun holds about 99.8 percent of the solar system's mass but only roughly 1 percent of its angular momentum, with most of it locked in the planets' orbits. This is usually explained by magnetic braking, where the young star's magnetic field couples to the protoplanetary disk, and remains a classic problem in star formation.

流星雨是怎么形成的？为什么同一流星雨每年会在固定时间出现？

How do meteor showers form, and why do the same showers recur at fixed dates each year?

流星雨的来源是彗星：彗星在轨道上剥离出大量尘埃碎片，形成沿轨道分布的碎片流，地球每年在同一时间穿过该碎片流，碎片进入大气高速燃烧形成流星雨。因为地球在固定的轨道位置穿过碎片流，所以流星雨每年都在大致相同的日期出现。
Meteor showers come from comets, which shed streams of dust grains along their orbits. Each year Earth passes through the same debris stream at the same orbital location; grains burn up in the atmosphere at high speed, producing the shower. Because Earth crosses the stream at a fixed orbital position, a given shower returns at about the same calendar date every year.

Why do comets have long tails?

彗星是结冰的脏雪球，靠近太阳时表面的冰受热升华成气体和尘埃。太阳风和辐射压力把这些物质推离彗星，形成背向太阳的彗尾，有时还分出尘埃尾和离子尾。彗星越靠近太阳，尾巴就越长越亮。
Comets are dirty snowballs of ice that, when near the Sun, release gas and dust as their surface ices sublimate. Solar wind and radiation pressure push this material away from the comet, forming tails that point away from the Sun, sometimes splitting into a dust tail and an ion tail. The closer a comet gets to the Sun, the longer and brighter its tail becomes.

为什么冥王星被降级为矮行星？

Why was Pluto reclassified as a dwarf planet?

2006年国际天文学联合会规定，行星必须清空其轨道附近的其他天体。冥王星虽然绕太阳运行且近似球形，但未能清空柯伊伯带中众多类似天体，因此被归为矮行星。这一决定反映了对太阳系外缘小天体群体认知的深化。
In 2006 the IAU ruled that a planet must have cleared its orbital neighborhood of other bodies. Pluto orbits the Sun and is roughly round, but it has not cleared the many similar objects in the Kuiper Belt, so it was classed as a dwarf planet. The decision reflected deeper understanding of the small-body population in the outer Solar System.

The Sun is the closest star to Earth and the central body of the Solar System. It is composed mainly of hydrogen and helium, and it releases enormous light and heat through nuclear fusion in its core, where hydrogen is converted into helium. The Sun accounts for about 99.86% of the total mass of the Solar System, and its gravity keeps the eight planets and other bodies in their orbits.

金星表面温度高达约465°C，超过水星，主要原因是其浓厚的大气层几乎全部由二氧化碳构成，并伴随大量硫酸云，形成极强的温室效应。阳光能透入加热地表，但热量难以以红外辐射形式逸出，使热量不断积累。
Venus has a surface temperature of about 465 °C, hotter than Mercury, because its thick atmosphere is almost entirely carbon dioxide with sulfuric-acid clouds, producing an extreme greenhouse effect. Sunlight heats the surface, but the heat cannot easily escape as infrared radiation, so it builds up.
