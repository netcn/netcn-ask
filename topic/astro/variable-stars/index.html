什么是造父变星的周光关系？它为什么被称为丈量遥远距离的量天尺？

What is the period-luminosity relation of Cepheid variables, and why are these stars called yardsticks for measuring vast distances?

造父变星是有规律地径向胀缩的黄超巨星，脉动由氦部分电离区充当阀门来驱动：压缩时电离度升高、不透明度变大，辐射能被暂时储存，膨胀时再释放出来，从而维持稳定的振荡。造父变星光度越大，脉动周期越长；因此只需测出光变周期就能推知光度，再与视亮度比较便得到距离，把距离测量从银河系延伸到邻近星系。
Cepheids are yellow supergiants that rhythmically swell and shrink, their pulsation driven because a partially ionized helium layer acts as a valve: compression raises ionization and opacity so radiation is stored momentarily, then released during expansion, sustaining a steady oscillation. More luminous Cepheids pulse with longer periods, so the observed period reveals the true luminosity; comparing it with apparent brightness yields the distance, extending measurements from our Galaxy to nearby ones.
