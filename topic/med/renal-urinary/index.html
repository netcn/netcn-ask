透析（血液透析与腹膜透析）替代了肾脏的哪些功能？

What kidney functions do dialysis treatments replace?

透析通过半透膜清除血液中的尿素、肌酐等小分子毒素，并纠正水、电解质与酸碱失衡，部分替代肾小球的滤过与肾小管的调节功能，但不能完全复制肾脏的内分泌作用。
Dialysis uses a semipermeable membrane to remove uremic toxins like urea and creatinine and correct fluid, electrolyte and acid-base balance, partially replacing filtration and regulation - but not the kidney's endocrine roles.

什么是肾小球滤过率（GFR），它为什么能反映肾功能？

What is GFR and why does it reflect kidney function?

GFR 指单位时间内两侧肾脏生成的原尿量，受肾血流量和滤过膜面积影响。GFR 持续下降常提示肾单位受损，是慢性肾病分期的关键指标。
GFR is the volume of fluid filtered by both kidneys per unit time, depending on blood flow and filter surface. A sustained drop suggests nephron damage and is central to staging chronic kidney disease.

肾脏是如何过滤血液并生成尿液的？

How do the kidneys filter blood and make urine?

肾脏约有百万个肾单位，血液流经肾小球时被滤过，水、废物和小分子进入肾小管，有用物质被重吸收，最终形成尿液。这既排出代谢废物，也帮助调节体液与电解质平衡。
Each kidney has about a million nephrons. Blood is filtered at the glomerulus; water, wastes and small molecules enter the tubule, useful substances are reabsorbed, and the result becomes urine. This removes waste while balancing fluids and electrolytes.

肾结石是怎么形成的，为什么那么疼？

How do kidney stones form and why do they hurt so much?

当尿液中钙、草酸或尿酸等过饱和时，晶体析出并聚集成结石。结石移动或堵塞尿路时会刺激神经并引起痉挛，造成剧烈绞痛。多饮水有助于稀释尿液、减少风险。
When urine becomes supersaturated with calcium, oxalate or uric acid, crystals clump into stones. Moving or blocking stones irritate nerves and cause spasms - severe colic pain. Drinking enough water dilutes urine and lowers risk.

为什么憋尿太久对身体不好？

Why is holding urine too long unhealthy?

膀胱过度充盈会增加尿路感染和膀胱压力，长期可能削弱排尿功能；尿液滞留也给细菌繁殖创造条件。有尿意时及时排空更合适。
An overfull bladder raises infection risk and pressure, and prolonged retention lets bacteria multiply. Emptying when you feel the urge is healthier.

为什么我们每天都要排尿？

Why do we pee every day?

因为身体要把喝进来的多余水分和吃进来的废物变成尿排出去，这样身体才干净又舒服。
Your body turns extra water and food waste into pee so it can send the junk out and stay clean and comfy.
