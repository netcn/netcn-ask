什么是“运动性低血糖”，如何预防？

What is 'exercise-induced hypoglycemia' and how can it be prevented?

运动性低血糖指运动时血糖过低，出现头晕、手抖、乏力，多见于空腹或糖尿病患者。预防方法包括运动前适当进食、避免空腹剧烈运动并随身带糖。
Exercise-induced hypoglycemia is low blood sugar during activity, causing dizziness, trembling, and weakness, often in those exercising on an empty stomach or with diabetes. Prevention includes eating a light snack beforehand, avoiding intense fasting exercise, and carrying sugar.

什么是最大心率，如何粗略估算？

What is maximum heart rate and how is it roughly estimated?

最大心率是运动时心脏能达到的安全上限，常用“220减去年龄”粗略估算。运动时的目标心率通常保持在最大心率的一定比例区间内。
Maximum heart rate is the upper safe limit of the heart during exercise, roughly estimated as '220 minus your age'. Target training heart rate is usually kept within a certain percentage of this maximum.

伸展拉伸有什么作用？

What is the benefit of stretching?

拉伸能增加肌肉和关节的柔韧性，改善活动范围并缓解运动后的紧绷感。运动前后适度拉伸都有助于减少受伤。
Stretching increases the flexibility of muscles and joints, improves range of motion, and relieves post-exercise tightness. Moderate stretching before and after exercise helps reduce injury risk.

运动后肌肉酸痛正常吗？

Is muscle soreness after exercise normal?

运动后1到2天出现的延迟性肌肉酸痛通常是正常的，是肌肉适应运动的反应。适当拉伸、休息和补水有助于缓解。
Delayed muscle soreness appearing one to two days after exercise is usually normal and reflects the muscles adapting. Gentle stretching, rest, and hydration help relieve it.

运动后为什么要及时补水？

Why should we rehydrate after exercise?

运动时我们通过出汗流失水分和电解质，及时补水有助于维持体温和循环。少量多次、温水为宜，避免一次性猛灌。
During exercise we lose water and electrolytes through sweat, so rehydrating helps maintain body temperature and circulation. Drink small amounts frequently with lukewarm water rather than gulping all at once.

为什么说“久坐伤身”？

Why is 'sitting too long' bad for health?

长时间坐着会降低能量消耗、影响血液循环，并与肥胖和心血管疾病风险上升相关。建议每隔一段时间起身活动一下。
Sitting for long periods lowers energy expenditure and impairs circulation, and is linked to higher risks of obesity and cardiovascular disease. It is advised to stand and move around periodically.

为什么要多去户外玩耍和运动？

Why is it good to play and exercise outdoors more?

户外活动和运动能让身体更强壮，还能晒太阳帮助身体制造维生素D。和小朋友一起跑跳也有助于交朋友。
Outdoor play and exercise make the body stronger and let sunlight help the body make vitamin D. Running and jumping with friends also helps you make friends.

走路算运动吗？

Does walking count as exercise?

算。快走等中等强度步行能锻炼心肺、消耗热量，是适合大多数人的安全运动方式。每天累计走几千步对身体就有好处。
Yes. Brisk walking is a moderate-intensity activity that trains the heart and lungs and burns calories, making it a safe exercise for most people. Accumulating several thousand steps a day already benefits health.

运动对我们的身体有什么好处？

What are the benefits of exercise for our bodies?

运动能增强心肺功能、让骨骼和肌肉更强壮，还能让人心情更好。每天活动一会儿比久坐不动更健康。
Exercise strengthens the heart and lungs, builds stronger bones and muscles, and improves mood. Being active for a while each day is healthier than sitting still for long periods.
