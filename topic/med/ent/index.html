突发性感音神经性聋为什么强调在发病72小时内开始糖皮质激素治疗?

Why is glucocorticoid therapy emphasized within 72 hours of onset in sudden sensorineural hearing loss?

突聋的常见机制包括内耳微循环障碍、病毒感染与自身免疫炎症,毛细胞和螺旋神经节在缺血与炎症作用下会从可逆损伤转为不可逆损伤,时间窗很短。糖皮质激素通过减轻内耳炎症和水肿提高听力恢复率,研究显示发病两周内尤其72小时内开始治疗者完全恢复率显著更高。超过一个月后再治疗,自发改善与药物效应都明显下降。
Proposed mechanisms include inner-ear microvascular compromise, viral infection, and autoimmune inflammation; hair cells and spiral ganglion neurons shift from reversible to irreversible injury within a short window. Glucocorticoids raise recovery rates by reducing cochlear inflammation and edema, and studies show significantly higher complete-recovery rates when treatment starts within two weeks, especially 72 hours, of onset. After one month, both spontaneous improvement and drug effect fall off sharply.

良性阵发性位置性眩晕的典型发作有什么特点?Dix-Hallpike 试验是如何诱发的?

What characterizes a typical episode of benign paroxysmal positional vertigo, and how does the Dix-Hallpike maneuver provoke it?

发作由翻身、低头或躺下等头位变动触发,眩晕持续数秒至一分钟,可伴恶心与旋转性眼震。Dix-Hallpike 试验让患者从坐位快速后躺,头向一侧旋转45度、后仰使头部低于水平面约20度,后半规管耳石症会在短暂潜伏期后出现扭转性眼震。眼震朝向患侧,恢复坐位时常出现方向反转,据此可定位受累的半规管。
Episodes are triggered by head-position changes such as turning over in bed, bending down, or lying back; vertigo lasts seconds to under a minute and may come with nausea and torsional nystagmus. In the Dix-Hallpike maneuver the patient is moved quickly from sitting to supine with the head turned 45 degrees and extended, and posterior-canal BPPV produces torsional nystagmus after a brief latency. The nystagmus beats toward the affected ear and often reverses direction on sitting up, which localizes the involved canal.
