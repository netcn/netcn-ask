针灸试验里的"假针"对照是怎么做的？

How do acupuncture trials create a "sham needle" control?

常见做法有三类：用钝头可回缩的假针只压皮肤不刺入；在非穴位处浅刺；或刺入但不做提插捻转等手法。难点在于患者能感到"有没有被扎"，而针刺本身即是强刺激，所以"假针"往往并非真正的空白对照，这也是针灸研究结论常有争议的原因之一。
Common designs use blunt retractable needles that only press the skin, shallow needling at non-acupoints, or insertion without manipulation. The problem is that patients can often tell whether they were needled, and needling itself is a strong stimulus, so sham needles are rarely a true placebo — one reason acupuncture findings stay contested.

中医说的"气"，能用现代科学解释吗？

Can the traditional Chinese concept of "qi" be explained in modern scientific terms?

"气"是古代医家对生命活动、能量与功能状态的整体概括，并非现代生理学中某个可测量的单一物质。今天研究者通常把与"气"相关的描述拆解为代谢率、循环灌注、神经内分泌调节和主观疲劳感等可测指标来研究。把它当作一套历史悠久的临床语言，而不是等同于某种粒子或电流，更有助于科学对话。
"Qi" is a holistic ancient framework describing vitality, energy and functional state, not a single measurable substance in modern physiology. Researchers today translate qi-related descriptions into testable variables such as metabolic rate, tissue perfusion, neuroendocrine regulation and subjective fatigue. Treating it as a historical clinical language rather than a particle or current makes scientific dialogue easier.

#中医 #气 #科学解释

中医脉诊和现代的脉搏、血压测量有什么区别？

How does pulse diagnosis differ from modern pulse and blood-pressure measurement?

脉诊用手指在寸口分部位感受脉的深浅、力度、节律与形态，形成如"弦""滑""细"等定性描述，用于整体辨证。现代测量则量化心率、节律、血压、脉搏波传导速度等指标，可重复且可对比。研究发现不同医师对脉象判断的一致性有限，这是脉诊标准化研究的重点。
Pulse diagnosis uses fingers at wrist positions to sense depth, force, rhythm and quality, yielding qualitative labels like "wiry" or "slippery" for pattern differentiation. Modern measurement quantifies heart rate, rhythm, blood pressure and pulse-wave velocity, which are repeatable and comparable. Studies find limited agreement between practitioners on pulse qualities, a key target of standardisation research.

为什么黄连素（小檗碱）会被现代研究用于辅助降低2型糖尿病血糖？

Why has berberine (from Huanglian) been studied to help lower blood glucose in type 2 diabetes?

小檗碱是中药黄连的主要生物碱，现代研究发现它能激活AMPK通路、改善胰岛素敏感性和肠道菌群，降糖幅度在部分试验中接近二甲双胍。2008年《自然·医学》报道其降糖与调脂作用，但证据等级仍低于一线药物，不能替代规范治疗。它也是“本草来源化合物走向现代药理”的典型例子。
Berberine is the main alkaloid of the herb Coptis (Huanglian); modern work shows it activates AMPK, improves insulin sensitivity and shifts the gut microbiota, with glucose lowering in some trials near metformin's. A 2008 Nature Medicine paper reported its glucose- and lipid-lowering effects, but evidence is still below first-line drugs and it cannot replace standard care. It is a classic case of a herbal compound entering modern pharmacology.

中药复方为什么比单一成分药更难做临床试验？

Why are multi-herb formulas harder to test in clinical trials than single-compound drugs?

复方常含几十到上百种化学成分，批次间因产地、采收季节和炮制方式而波动，难以保证成分一致；处方还常按个体辨证调整，与随机对照试验要求的标准化干预冲突。此外气味明显使盲法困难。近年研究尝试用指纹图谱质控、固定配方和网络药理学来解决部分问题。
A formula may contain dozens to hundreds of compounds that vary by growing region, harvest season and processing, making batch consistency hard; prescriptions are also often individualised, clashing with the standardised interventions trials require. Strong taste and smell also make blinding difficult. Chemical fingerprinting, fixed formulations and network pharmacology are being used to address parts of this.

除了青蒿素，还有哪些现代药物源自传统药材？

Besides artemisinin, which modern drugs came from traditional medicinal plants?

麻黄中的麻黄碱启发了拟肾上腺素类药物；黄连中的小檗碱、雷公藤中的雷公藤甲素、砷剂（砒石）用于治疗急性早幼粒细胞白血病、以及从蟾酥、水蛭等药材中分离的活性物，都进入了现代研究。这条路径说明传统经验可以是发现新分子的线索库，但仍需经过纯化、剂量与安全性验证。
Ephedrine from ma huang inspired adrenergic drugs; berberine from Coptis, triptolide from Tripterygium, arsenic trioxide (from mineral drugs) for acute promyelocytic leukaemia, and compounds isolated from toad venom or leeches all entered modern research. Traditional experience can serve as a library of leads, but purification, dosing and safety testing remain essential.

#本草 #药物发现

"中药天然所以无毒"这种说法为什么不成立？

Why is "herbal means natural, so it's harmless" wrong?

植物会合成许多具生物活性的化合物，天然并不等于安全。含马兜铃酸的药材可致肾损伤与尿路上皮癌，附子中的乌头碱有心脏毒性，何首乌与部分减肥草药与肝损伤有关；草药还可能与处方药相互作用（如圣约翰草影响多种药物代谢）。使用前应告知医生你正在服用的所有药物与补充剂。
Plants make many bioactive compounds, and natural does not mean safe. Aristolochic acid–containing herbs cause kidney damage and urothelial cancer, aconitine in Aconitum is cardiotoxic, and Polygonum multiflorum plus some slimming herbs are linked to liver injury; herbs can also interact with prescriptions, as St John's wort does with many drugs. Tell your clinician about every medicine and supplement you take.

#中药安全 #马兜铃酸

针灸缓解疼痛，现代研究认为可能的机制是什么？

What mechanisms do modern studies propose for acupuncture's pain relief?

现有研究提出几条途径：针刺刺激皮下与肌肉的感受器，经脊髓的"闸门"机制抑制痛信号；触发内源性阿片肽（如内啡肽）与腺苷释放；并通过下行调控和大脑注意网络改变对疼痛的感知。这些机制可以部分解释镇痛效果，但不同病症的证据强度差别很大。
Proposed pathways include stimulation of subcutaneous and muscular receptors that engage spinal "gate control" inhibition, release of endogenous opioids such as endorphins and of adenosine locally, and top-down modulation that changes how the brain attends to pain. These can partly explain analgesia, though evidence strength varies a lot by condition.

#针灸 #镇痛机制

针灸对哪些情况的证据相对较强，哪些较弱？

For which conditions is acupuncture's evidence stronger, and where weaker?

较多综述认为针灸在慢性腰颈痛、膝骨关节炎疼痛、紧张性头痛与偏头痛预防、化疗相关恶心呕吐等方面可能带来中等程度改善，部分指南将其列为可选辅助。对戒烟、减肥、不孕、哮喘等，证据薄弱或与安慰对照差异不明显。选择时应考虑正规执业、无菌操作，并不替代必要的诊断与治疗。
Reviews suggest acupuncture may give moderate improvement for chronic low back and neck pain, knee osteoarthritis pain, tension headache and migraine prevention, and chemotherapy-related nausea, and some guidelines list it as an optional adjunct. For smoking cessation, weight loss, infertility and asthma the evidence is weak or not clearly better than sham. Choose licensed practitioners with sterile technique, and do not let it replace necessary diagnosis and treatment.

#针灸 #适应证 #证据强度

煎中药为什么要分"先煎"和"后下"？

Why are some herbs boiled first and others added last?

这是朴素的提取化学：矿物、贝壳和坚硬根茎类（如石膏、龟甲）需要更长时间高温才能溶出有效成分，所以先煎；含挥发油的芳香药材（如薄荷、砂仁）久煮会让活性成分随蒸汽跑掉，所以后下。有毒药材（如附子）先煎久煮还能降低毒性成分含量。
It is practical extraction chemistry: minerals, shells and hard roots such as gypsum or turtle shell need long high-heat boiling to release their constituents, so they go in first. Aromatic herbs rich in volatile oils, like mint or cardamom, lose actives to steam if boiled long, so they go in last. Toxic materials such as aconite are boiled long precisely to reduce toxic content.

#煎药 #提取化学

推拿正骨时听到的"咔哒"声是骨头回位了吗？

Is the "crack" heard during manipulation the sound of a bone going back into place?

多数情况下不是。这种声音通常来自关节被快速牵开时滑液中气体空腔的形成或塌陷，与"骨头错位复位"不是一回事。手法可能通过肌肉放松和神经反射带来短期舒适，但颈部高速旋转手法存在少见但严重的风险，应由受过训练的专业人员评估后进行。
Usually not. The sound typically comes from gas cavities forming or collapsing in joint fluid when the joint is quickly gapped — not a bone slipping back. Manipulation may relieve symptoms via muscle relaxation and reflexes, but high-velocity neck rotation carries rare serious risks and should only follow assessment by a trained professional.

#正骨 #关节响声

吃姜为什么会觉得暖？真的能"驱寒"吗？

Why does ginger make you feel warm, and does it really "drive out cold"?

姜中的姜辣素能激活口腔与消化道的TRPV1等温度/辣感受体，让大脑产生温热感，同时轻度促进外周血流。这是感觉与循环层面的变化，不等于提高核心体温或治疗感冒；姜对缓解轻度恶心（含孕吐）有一定证据。
Gingerols activate TRPV1-type heat/pungency receptors in the mouth and gut, so the brain registers warmth, and peripheral blood flow rises slightly. That is a sensory and circulatory change, not a rise in core temperature or a cure for colds; ginger does have modest evidence for easing mild nausea, including in pregnancy.

#生姜 #辛辣感受体

中医舌诊在看什么？现代医学也看舌头吗？

What does tongue diagnosis look at, and does modern medicine examine the tongue too?

舌诊观察舌色、舌形、舌苔厚薄与湿润度，中医据此判断证型。现代医学同样查看舌与口腔：苍白提示贫血、干燥提示脱水、白色斑块可能是念珠菌感染、光滑萎缩可与维生素B12或铁缺乏有关、不明白斑需排查癌前病变。两者观察相似，但解释框架不同。
Tongue diagnosis reads colour, shape, coating thickness and moisture to classify patterns. Modern medicine also inspects the tongue: pallor suggests anaemia, dryness dehydration, white plaques may be candidiasis, a smooth atrophic surface can reflect B12 or iron deficiency, and unexplained white patches need evaluation for precancerous change. Similar observations, different explanatory frameworks.

They are low-impact aerobic-plus-balance training that is gentle and sustainable — an advantage in itself.

#太极 #八段锦 #运动证据

青蒿素是怎么从古代本草记载走向现代药物的？

How did artemisinin travel from an ancient herbal text to a modern drug?

20世纪70年代，中国研究团队系统筛选传统方药寻找抗疟药，从《肘后备急方》"绞汁"服用青蒿的记载中受到启发，改用低温乙醚提取，成功获得高活性成分青蒿素。它后来成为全球疟疾治疗的核心药物，屠呦呦因此获得2015年诺贝尔生理学或医学奖。
In the 1970s Chinese researchers screened traditional remedies for antimalarials and were inspired by an ancient text describing wringing juice from qinghao (sweet wormwood); switching to low-temperature ether extraction yielded the highly active compound artemisinin. It became a cornerstone of global malaria treatment, and Tu Youyou received the 2015 Nobel Prize in Physiology or Medicine.

为什么含麻黄碱的中成药被严格限购以防制毒和用药风险？

Why are ephedrine-containing herbal medicines tightly restricted against misuse and drug-making?

麻黄中的麻黄碱、伪麻黄碱既是平喘解表的有效成分，也是合成甲基苯丙胺（冰毒）的前体，因此被列管。过量或不当使用可致心悸、高血压甚至心律失常，与某些药物同服风险更高。我国对含麻黄碱类复方制剂实行限购和实名登记，以防止流入非法制毒渠道。
Ephedrine and pseudoephedrine in Ephedra (Mahuang) are both effective bronchodilating ingredients and precursors for methamphetamine, so they are controlled. Too much can cause palpitations, hypertension or arrhythmia, and interactions raise the risk. China limits and registers ephedrine-containing compounds by real name to stop them feeding illegal drug manufacture.

"忌口"有科学依据吗？

Is there science behind avoiding certain foods when ill?

部分情况有：服用某些药物时确实需避开特定食物（如西柚汁影响多种药物代谢、华法林与富含维生素K的蔬菜、单胺氧化酶抑制剂与含酪胺食物），皮肤病或胃肠疾病患者也可能对辛辣、酒精敏感。但把"发物"泛化到几乎所有食物，缺乏证据，反而可能造成营养不足。具体饮食限制建议听经治医生说明。
Sometimes: certain medicines really do require avoiding specific foods — grapefruit juice alters many drugs' metabolism, warfarin interacts with vitamin K–rich vegetables, MAO inhibitors with tyramine-rich foods — and people with skin or gut conditions may react to spice or alcohol. But extending "trigger foods" to nearly everything lacks evidence and can cause nutritional gaps. Follow the specific advice of your treating clinician.

Does moxibustion work mainly through heat or through compounds in the mugwort?

目前研究更支持温热刺激是主要因素：局部升温可扩张血管、激活温度感受器（如TRPV通道）并影响痛觉传导。艾叶燃烧的挥发性成分是否有额外药理作用尚不明确，其烟雾还含有颗粒物，室内施灸需通风。

Whether volatile compounds from burning mugwort add pharmacological effects is unclear, and the smoke contains particulates, so ventilate the room.

"以形补形"，吃猪脑能补脑吗？

Does eating pig brain "nourish" your brain, as the like-cures-like idea suggests?

不能。食物在消化道会被分解成氨基酸、脂肪酸和小分子，身体按自身需要重新利用，不会因为吃了某个器官就优先修补同名器官。动物脑还含大量胆固醇。真正对大脑有益的是整体饮食质量、充足睡眠、运动和学习刺激。
No. Food is broken down into amino acids, fatty acids and small molecules, which the body reuses according to its own needs — eating an organ does not preferentially rebuild the same organ. Animal brain is also very high in cholesterol. What actually helps the brain is overall diet quality, sleep, exercise and mental stimulation.

拔火罐后留下的紫红色印子是什么？

What are the purple-red marks left after cupping?

罐内负压把皮肤和皮下组织吸起，使微小血管破裂、血液渗入组织间隙，形成类似瘀青的印记，通常几天到两周自行吸收。颜色深浅主要取决于负压大小和留罐时间，并不能可靠地反映"体内湿气或毒素多少"。皮肤破损、凝血异常或正在服抗凝药者应避免，必要时先咨询医生。
Suction pulls skin and underlying tissue upward, rupturing tiny vessels so blood leaks into the tissue, producing bruise-like marks that fade over days to two weeks. Their darkness mostly reflects suction strength and duration, not the amount of "dampness or toxins" inside. People with broken skin, clotting disorders or on anticoagulants should avoid it and ask a clinician first.

#拔罐 #瘀青

"穴位"在解剖上对应什么？

What do acupuncture points correspond to anatomically?

现代解剖学没有找到与穴位一一对应的独立结构，但研究发现许多常用穴位处存在神经末梢较密集、血管与肌间隔交汇、结缔组织层次特殊的特点，与"扳机点"位置也有部分重叠。也就是说，穴位更像是历代医家积累的"刺激效果较好的体表位置"，而非一套隐藏的器官系统。
Modern anatomy finds no separate structures matching points one-to-one, but many commonly used points sit where nerve endings are denser, where vessels and intermuscular septa meet, or where connective tissue layers are distinctive — and they partly overlap with myofascial trigger points. Points look more like empirically accumulated "good places to stimulate" than a hidden organ system.

它指一些既能作食物、也被传统医学用作药材的物品，如山药、红枣、生姜、桂圆、薏米。含义是日常饮食本身会影响身体状态，但"同源"不等于"能治病"：作为食物摄入的剂量通常远低于药用剂量。
It refers to items used both as food and as traditional medicine, such as Chinese yam, jujube, ginger, longan and Job's tears. The idea is that everyday diet shapes bodily state — but sharing an origin does not mean curing disease, since food-level amounts are usually far below medicinal doses.

#药食同源 #饮食

"春捂秋冻"有道理吗？

Is the saying "bundle up in spring, stay cool in autumn" sensible?

它抓住了两个现实：春季气温波动大、昼夜温差明显，过早减衣容易受凉；秋季逐步适应凉意有助于身体的温度调节适应。但"冻"不是越冷越好，老人、儿童、心血管病患者对寒冷更敏感，应以体感舒适、避免明显发抖为准。
It captures two real points: spring temperatures swing widely so shedding clothes too early invites chill, while gradual autumn cooling helps the body adapt its thermoregulation. But colder is not better — older adults, children and people with cardiovascular disease are more cold-sensitive, so comfort without shivering is the right guide.

刮痧"出痧"是排毒吗？

Does the redness raised by gua sha mean toxins are coming out?

"痧"其实是刮拭造成的皮下毛细血管出血点，属于机械性损伤反应，并没有把毒素排出体外。它可能通过局部血流增加、按压刺激和放松感带来短期舒适。刮拭力度过大会造成明显损伤，发热、皮肤病变或出血倾向时不宜进行。
The "sha" is petechial bleeding from capillaries broken by scraping — a mechanical injury response, not toxins leaving the body. Any short-term relief likely comes from increased local blood flow, pressure stimulation and relaxation. Excessive force causes real injury, and it should be avoided with fever, skin lesions or bleeding tendencies.

民间说的"上火"，在现代医学里对应什么？

What might the folk concept of "shanghuo" (excess heat) correspond to medically?

"上火"是一组主观体验的统称，常见对应包括口腔黏膜溃疡、咽部或牙龈的轻度炎症、便秘、痤疮加重、睡眠不足后的黏膜脆弱和脱水。它更像症状描述而非单一疾病，所以"清火"的效果往往来自多喝水、休息与减少刺激性食物。持续或加重的症状应就医评估。
"Shanghuo" bundles several subjective experiences: mouth ulcers, mild throat or gum inflammation, constipation, acne flares, and fragile mucosa after poor sleep or dehydration. It describes symptoms rather than one disease, so "cooling" remedies often help mainly through more fluids, rest and fewer irritating foods. Persistent or worsening symptoms deserve a medical check.
