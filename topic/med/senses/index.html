长时间戴耳机大音量听歌为什么会损伤听力？

Why does listening loudly through headphones for long periods damage hearing?

内耳耳蜗中的毛细胞负责把声波转换成神经信号，它们被过强或过久的声音刺激后会受损，而人类的毛细胞不能再生，损伤是永久的。世界卫生组织的“安全聆听”建议是：成人每周在80分贝以下累计不超过40小时，儿童更严格（约75分贝）。听后耳鸣、耳朵发闷或听声音发虚，就是已经过量的信号。实用做法是遵循“60-60原则”（音量不超过60%、连续不超过60分钟），并用降噪耳机以便在更低音量下听清。
Hair cells in the cochlea convert sound waves into nerve signals, and sound that is too loud or lasts too long damages them - in humans they cannot regenerate, so the loss is permanent. WHO's safe listening advice is no more than 40 hours per week at under 80 decibels for adults, with a stricter limit of about 75 decibels for children. Ringing, a blocked feeling or muffled hearing afterwards means the dose was already too high. A practical rule is 60-60: no more than 60% volume for no more than 60 minutes at a stretch, and noise-cancelling headphones help you hear clearly at lower volume.

为什么害羞时脸会红？

Why does the face turn red when we feel shy?

害羞或紧张时，身体会让脸部的小血管张开，更多血液流过，脸就红红的。这是很正常的反应，说明你很在意别人的看法。
When shy or nervous, the body opens tiny blood vessels in the face so more blood flows through and it turns red. This is a normal reaction showing you care about others' opinions.

为什么小朋友会一年比一年高？

Why do children grow taller each year?

身体里的骨头和肌肉一直在长大，尤其是腿骨慢慢变长，人就长高了。吃好、睡好、多运动，就能健康地长高。
The body's bones and muscles keep growing, especially the leg bones lengthening, so a child gets taller. Eating well, sleeping, and playing sports help healthy growth.

为什么我们会打嗝？

Why do we get hiccups?

打嗝是横膈膜突然抽了一下，让空气猛地冲进喉咙，发出"嗝"的声音。吃太快或喝气泡水常常会引起，一会儿自己就好。
A hiccup is the diaphragm twitching suddenly, making air rush into the throat with a "hic" sound. Eating too fast or drinking fizzy water often causes it, and it usually stops on its own.

为什么眼睛能看见东西？

Why can eyes see things?

眼睛像小照相机，把光收进来变成信号送给大脑，大脑就知道看见了什么。有了眼睛，我们就能认出爸爸妈妈和好玩的玩具。
Eyes work like little cameras, catching light and turning it into signals for the brain, which then knows what you see. With eyes, you can recognize parents and fun toys.

为什么舌头能尝出酸甜苦咸？

Why can the tongue taste sweet, sour, bitter, and salty?

舌头上有许多小味蕾，像小探测器分别认出不同味道，再报告给大脑。味道帮我们享受美食，也帮我们避开变质的东西。
The tongue has many tiny taste buds that act like detectors, each recognizing a flavor and reporting to the brain. Taste helps us enjoy food and avoid spoiled things.

为什么冷的时候胳膊上会起鸡皮疙瘩？

Why do we get goosebumps when cold?

受凉时皮肤下的小肌肉把汗毛立起来，想留住一层暖空气，皮肤就鼓起小颗粒。这是从毛茸茸祖先那里留下的小反应。
When cold, tiny muscles under the skin lift the hairs to trap a layer of warm air, making little bumps. It is a small reaction left over from furry ancestors.

为什么头发和指甲会一直长？

Why do hair and nails keep growing?

头发和指甲由一种叫角蛋白的硬材料做成，身体不断在根部制造新的，就把旧的往前推、长出来。它们像小盔甲，保护头皮和指尖。
Hair and nails are made of a tough material called keratin, and the body keeps making new material at the root, pushing the old part out. They act like little armor protecting scalp and fingertips.

为什么我们有两只耳朵？

Why do we have two ears?

两只耳朵收到声音的时间差，让大脑算出声音来自左边还是右边，这叫声音定位。两只耳朵还能在吵闹环境里互相帮助，听得更清楚。
The tiny time difference between two ears lets the brain calculate whether a sound comes from the left or right, called sound localization. Two ears also help each other hear clearly in noisy places.

晕车晕船是怎么产生的？

What causes motion sickness in cars and boats?

晕动症源于感觉信息冲突：内耳前庭器官感受到颠簸和加速，而眼睛看到的车内环境相对静止，肌肉与关节的位置感觉又给出第三种信息，大脑难以整合便触发恶心、出汗和头晕。因此看书或看手机会加重，看远处地平线、坐在颠簸较小的位置（汽车前排、船的中部）、保持通风则能缓解。必要时可在出行前使用抗组胺类或东莨菪碱类药物，反复乘坐后多数人会逐渐适应。
Motion sickness arises from conflicting sensory input: the vestibular organs in the inner ear feel bumps and acceleration, the eyes see a relatively still cabin, and muscles and joints report a third version of events, so the brain struggles to reconcile them and triggers nausea, sweating and dizziness. That is why reading or looking at a phone makes it worse, while watching the distant horizon, sitting where motion is least (front seat of a car, middle of a boat) and keeping fresh air moving all help. Antihistamine or scopolamine medication taken before travel can help, and most people gradually adapt with repeated exposure.

为什么耳朵能听到声音？

Why can ears hear sounds?

耳朵里有一层小薄膜，声音跑进来会让它轻轻震动，再变成信号告诉大脑。这样我们就能听到音乐、笑声和妈妈的呼唤。
Inside the ear is a tiny membrane that sound makes vibrate gently, turning into signals for the brain. That lets us hear music, laughter, and a mother's call.

为什么每个人的指纹都不一样？

Why is everyone's fingerprint different?

指纹是手指皮肤上的小纹路，在妈妈肚子里时就按独一无二的图案长成，连双胞胎也不同。它们还能增加摩擦力，帮我们更稳地抓东西。
Fingerprints are tiny ridges on the skin that form in a unique pattern before birth, different even in twins. They also add friction so we can grip things more steadily.

为什么鼻子能闻到味道？

Why can the nose smell things?

鼻子里面有一小块专门闻味的区域，气味分子飘进去会被它抓住并告诉大脑。好闻的味道提醒食物新鲜，难闻的味道提醒要小心。
High inside the nose is a small smelling zone that catches odor molecules and tells the brain. Pleasant smells signal fresh food, while bad ones warn us to be careful.
