“每天必须喝满8杯水”有根据吗？

Is '8 glasses of water a day' well-founded?

饮水需求因人而异，与体型、活动量和气候相关。口渴感和浅色尿液是实用的判断信号，食物中也含不少水分，不必硬凑8杯。
Needs vary with size, activity and climate. Thirst and pale urine are practical guides, and food supplies much water—no need to force eight glasses.

“吃糖让孩子多动”有科学依据吗？

Is there proof that sugar makes kids hyperactive?

严格对照研究并未发现糖直接导致多动；家长的预期和聚会环境等更易造成“看起来更兴奋”的偏差。当然，限制添加糖对长期健康仍有必要。
Controlled studies don't show sugar directly causes hyperactivity; parental expectation and party settings bias the impression. Limiting added sugar is still wise for long-term health.

“鸡蛋升高胆固醇，不能吃”过时了吗？

Is 'eggs raise cholesterol, avoid them' outdated?

对多数人，膳食胆固醇对血液胆固醇影响有限，饱和脂肪影响更大。健康人群适量吃蛋通常是安全的，整体饮食模式更关键。
For most people, dietary cholesterol barely moves blood cholesterol; saturated fat matters more. Moderate egg intake is usually fine; the overall diet pattern counts most.

#鸡蛋 #胆固醇

“发烧必须马上退烧”对吗？

Must we bring down a fever immediately?

低烧有助身体对抗感染，通常为了舒服才处理，而不是盯着数字。但若体温很高、持续不退，或婴儿发烧，应咨询医生。
Mild fever helps fight infection; we usually treat for comfort, not the number. Very high or persistent fever, or fever in an infant, warrants a doctor.

#发烧 #误区

“感冒时猛喝维生素C能治好”吗？

Does loading up on vitamin C cure a cold?

维生素C不能治愈感冒，若平时规律补充可能让病程略短。感冒主要靠休息和补水，抗生素对它无效。
Vitamin C can't cure a cold; regular use may shorten it slightly. Colds mainly need rest and fluids, and antibiotics don't work on them.

“掰指关节会导致关节炎”是真的吗？

Does cracking knuckles cause arthritis?

没有可靠证据把掰指关节和关节炎联系起来；那种声响是关节内气泡破裂，可能会吵到别人，但一般不伤关节。
No solid evidence links knuckle-cracking to arthritis; the sound is gas bubbles popping. It may annoy others but generally doesn't harm joints.

#关节炎 #误区

“出汗多就等于排毒多”对吗？

Does sweating more mean detoxing more?

出汗的主要作用是降温，只能排出极少量代谢物；真正的解毒靠肝脏和肾脏。过度出汗反而有脱水风险。
Sweat mainly cools you and excretes only trace metabolites; real detox is the liver's and kidneys' job. Over-sweating risks dehydration instead.

#出汗 #误区

“排毒”产品真的能排毒吗？

Do 'detox' products really remove toxins?

人体的肝脏和肾脏本就负责解毒，“排毒”产品大多缺乏证据且常非必需。均衡饮食、喝水和睡眠，比买产品更靠谱。
Your liver and kidneys already detox; 'detox' products mostly lack evidence and aren't needed. Balanced eating, water and sleep beat buying products.

“在暗处看书会弄坏眼睛”对吗？

Does reading in dim light ruin your eyes?

暗光下看书主要造成暂时的眼疲劳和不适，并不会造成永久性损伤。良好照明能减轻疲劳，但不用过度担心。
Dim light mainly causes temporary eye strain and discomfort, not permanent damage. Good lighting eases fatigue, but don't over-worry.

#用眼 #误区

“抗生素能治一切发炎”对吗？

Do antibiotics cure all kinds of inflammation?

不对。抗生素只针对细菌感染；病毒性疾病和普通炎症并不需要，滥用会催生耐药菌。是否使用应由医生判断。
No. Antibiotics target bacteria only; viral illnesses and ordinary inflammation don't need them, and misuse breeds resistance. Let a doctor decide.
