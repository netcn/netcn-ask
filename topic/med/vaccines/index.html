什么是“免疫记忆”及其持续时间？

What is immune memory and how long does it last?

免疫记忆由记忆B/T细胞维持，可在再次遇抗原时快速响应；其持久性因病原而异，有些终身，有些仅数年。
Immune memory is maintained by memory B and T cells that respond fast on re-exposure; its duration varies—some lifelong, others only a few years.

mRNA疫苗与传统疫苗有何不同？

How do mRNA vaccines differ from traditional ones?

mRNA疫苗把编码抗原的遗传指令送入细胞自行生产抗原，不含活病毒，起效快且易于调整，而传统疫苗多用减毒或灭活病原体制成。
mRNA vaccines deliver genetic instructions for cells to make the antigen themselves—no live virus—acting fast and being easy to adapt, unlike traditional vaccines made from weakened or inactivated pathogens.

结合疫苗为什么对婴幼儿特别有效？

Why are conjugate vaccines especially effective in infants?

肺炎链球菌、b型流感嗜血杆菌等细菌的荚膜多糖属于不依赖T细胞的抗原，2岁以下婴幼儿对它反应很弱，也难以形成免疫记忆。把多糖共价连接到载体蛋白（如破伤风类毒素、CRM197）后，抗原就能被T细胞辅助识别，从而产生高亲和力抗体和持久记忆。结合疫苗还能减少鼻咽部带菌，通过降低传播产生群体保护，这正是Hib疫苗和肺炎结合疫苗大幅减少儿童脑膜炎与肺炎的原因。
The capsular polysaccharides of bacteria such as pneumococcus and Haemophilus influenzae type b are T-cell-independent antigens, to which infants under two respond poorly and without forming lasting memory. Covalently linking the polysaccharide to a carrier protein such as tetanus toxoid or CRM197 lets T cells help recognize it, generating high-affinity antibodies and durable memory. Conjugate vaccines also reduce nasopharyngeal carriage, so less transmission gives community protection - which is why Hib and pneumococcal conjugate vaccines have sharply cut childhood meningitis and pneumonia.

为什么有人会对疫苗犹豫？

Why are some people vaccine-hesitant?

原因包括错误信息、对罕见副作用的担忧，以及信任缺口。对获批疫苗而言，其获益通常远大于风险，可靠信息和透明沟通能减少犹豫。
Causes include misinformation, worry about rare side effects and trust gaps. For approved vaccines, benefits generally far outweigh risks; reliable info and transparent communication reduce hesitation.

疫苗里的佐剂是做什么用的？

What are vaccine adjuvants for?

佐剂本身不提供抗原，而是通过激活先天免疫、延长抗原在注射部位的停留时间，把免疫应答放大并引导到需要的方向。常用的有铝盐、水包油乳剂MF59，以及含皂树皂苷QS-21的AS01系统（用于带状疱疹和疟疾疫苗），mRNA疫苗的脂质纳米颗粒本身也具有佐剂效应。佐剂的价值在于减少每剂抗原用量、减少接种针次、增强老年人应答；接种后局部红肿酸痛和短暂低热，部分正来自佐剂引发的免疫激活。
An adjuvant supplies no antigen itself; it amplifies and steers the immune response by activating innate immunity and keeping antigen at the injection site longer. Common ones include aluminium salts, the oil-in-water emulsion MF59 and the AS01 system containing the saponin QS-21 used in shingles and malaria vaccines, while the lipid nanoparticle of mRNA vaccines has intrinsic adjuvant activity. Adjuvants let each dose use less antigen, reduce the number of injections needed and boost responses in older adults - and part of the local soreness, swelling and brief low fever after vaccination comes precisely from this deliberate immune activation.

成年人还需要接种哪些疫苗？

Which vaccines do adults still need?

流感每年接种，破伤风类毒素每10年加强，50岁以上考虑带状疱疹疫苗，老人或高危人群考虑肺炎球菌疫苗。具体以当地免疫程序为准。
Get a yearly flu shot, a tetanus booster every 10 years, shingles vaccine from age 50, and pneumococcal for older/high-risk people. Follow your local immunization schedule.

Why do we need booster shots?

流感疫苗为什么要每年打？

Why get the flu vaccine every year?

流感病毒变异快，每年流行的毒株不同，且疫苗保护会随时间减弱，因此建议每年接种以匹配当季病毒。
Flu viruses mutate quickly and strains change yearly while vaccine protection fades, so annual vaccination matches the current season's virus.

HPV疫苗只是给女性打吗？

Is the HPV vaccine only for girls?

不是。HPV疫苗能预防男女相关的多种癌症与疣，通常推荐青少年和年轻成人接种，男女都受益。

过敏与免疫力低下是一回事吗？

Is an allergy the same as weak immunity?

不是。过敏是免疫系统对无害物反应过度，而免疫力低下是防御不足，两者机制不同，不能简单等同。
No—an allergy is an overreaction to harmless substances, while low immunity means weak defense; the mechanisms differ.

群体免疫的门槛是怎么算出来的?为什么麻疹门槛高达95%,流感却低得多?

How are herd-immunity thresholds computed? Why is the measles threshold around ninety-five percent while flu sits far lower?

关键参数叫基本传染数R0:一个病例在全部易感人群里平均传染几人。阻止流行的临界免疫比例等于1减去1/R0。麻疹R0高达12到18,门槛约92到95%;流感R0约1.3,门槛两成多即可显著压平曲线。这解释了两件事:为何疫苗接种率稍有下滑麻疹就卷土重来(门槛极高容不得缺口);为何流感疫苗保护力六成也值得打(降低传播即可削峰)。群体免疫保护的是无法接种的新生儿与免疫缺陷者——它是公共品,靠的是整个社区的接种率而非个人选择。
The key parameter is basic reproduction number R0: how many people one case infects in a fully susceptible population. The critical immune fraction equals one minus one over R0. Measles R0 of twelve to eighteen demands ninety-two to ninety-five percent; influenza R0 near 1.3 needs barely twenty-plus percent to flatten curves. This explains both why small vaccination dips let measles resurge (huge threshold, no slack) and why a sixty-percent flu vaccine still helps (any transmission cut flattens peaks). Herd immunity shields newborns and immunocompromised who cannot be vaccinated — it is a public good built on community coverage, not personal choice.

疫苗是怎么保护我们的？

How do vaccines protect us?

疫苗让免疫系统提前“认识”病原体，产生记忆细胞，将来真正感染时能更快消灭它。
Vaccines let the immune system meet a pathogen in advance and form memory cells, so it can fight the real infection faster later.

打疫苗后胳膊疼正常吗？

Is a sore arm after vaccination normal?

很常见，是局部免疫反应，几天内会消退。可休息、冷敷缓解；若出现严重过敏等罕见反应，应及时就医。
It's common—a local immune response that fades in days. Rest and a cool compress help; seek care for rare severe allergic reactions.
