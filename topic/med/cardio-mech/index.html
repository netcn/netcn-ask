为什么他汀类药物能降低心脏病风险？

How do statins lower heart-disease risk?

他汀抑制胆固醇合成的关键酶，降低低密度脂蛋白，并有一定稳定斑块、抗炎作用，从而减少动脉粥样硬化事件。
Statins block a key cholesterol-making enzyme, lowering LDL and offering some plaque-stabilizing and anti-inflammatory effects, reducing atherosclerotic events.

动脉粥样硬化是怎么发生的？

How does atherosclerosis develop?

血管内皮受损后，脂质沉积形成斑块，逐渐使血管变窄变硬；斑块破裂可诱发血栓，引发心梗或中风。
After endothelial injury, lipid deposits form plaques that narrow and stiffen arteries; a ruptured plaque can trigger a clot causing heart attack or stroke.

心脏的『Frank-Starling 定律』说的是什么？

What does the Frank-Starling law of the heart describe?

它指在一定范围内，心室舒张末容积越大（即回心血量越多），心肌纤维被拉得越长，下一次收缩力就越强，心输出量随之增加。这相当于心脏对静脉回心血量做出自动匹配，维持进出平衡。
Within limits, a larger end-diastolic ventricular volume—more blood returning—stretches the cardiac fibers and yields a stronger next contraction, raising stroke volume. It lets the heart automatically match its output to the venous return and keep inflow and outflow balanced.

为什么高血压常被称为“沉默的杀手”？

Why is high blood pressure called a 'silent killer'?

高血压早期常无明显症状，却长期损害心、脑、肾和血管；定期测量才能早发现。
High blood pressure often has no early symptoms yet quietly damages heart, brain, kidneys and vessels - so regular measurement is the only way to catch it.

心脏的四个腔室各做什么？

What does each of the heart's four chambers do?

右心把全身回来的缺氧血送到肺换气，左心把富氧血泵向全身；心房接收血液，心室负责泵出。
The right heart sends oxygen-poor blood to the lungs; the left heart pumps oxygen-rich blood to the body. Atria receive blood; ventricles do the pumping.
