为什么过度使用消毒剂可能不利？

Why can overuse of disinfectants be harmful?

过度消毒会破坏正常微生物接触、可能促进耐药菌筛选并刺激呼吸道，适度清洁与菌群平衡更有利长期免疫健康。
Over-disinfecting disrupts normal microbial exposure, may select resistant strains, and irritates airways; moderate cleaning and microbial balance better support long-term immune health.

防晒霜上的SPF和PA分别代表什么？

What do SPF and PA on sunscreen mean?

SPF衡量对UVB（主要致晒伤）的防护时长与强度，PA（如PA+++）表示对UVA（主要致老化）的防护等级。广谱（broad-spectrum）产品同时覆盖两者。
SPF measures protection against UVB (the main burn cause); PA (e.g., PA+++) grades protection against UVA (the main aging cause). 'Broad-spectrum' covers both.

Is sunscreen only to avoid tanning?

UV light speeds skin aging and raises skin-cancer risk. Besides sunscreen (SPF 30+ and reapply), hats, clothing, and avoiding peak UV hours matter too.

氟化物为什么能减少蛀牙？

Why does fluoride reduce tooth decay?

氟离子能与牙釉质中的羟磷灰石结合形成更耐酸的氟磷灰石，并促进早期脱矿部位的再矿化，同时抑制致龋细菌的产酸代谢。因此含氟牙膏是目前最重要、最经济的防龋措施，世界卫生组织也认可饮水氟化在适宜浓度下的公共卫生价值。氟的作用有剂量窗：长期摄入过量会造成牙齿氟斑，因此《饮用水水质准则》把饮用水氟的指导值定为1.5 mg/L，儿童用含氟牙膏也应控制用量并避免吞咽。
Fluoride ions combine with hydroxyapatite in enamel to form more acid-resistant fluorapatite, promote remineralization of early demineralized spots, and inhibit acid production by cavity-causing bacteria. Fluoride toothpaste is thus the single most important and economical anti-caries measure, and WHO recognizes the public health value of water fluoridation at appropriate concentrations. The effect has a dose window: chronic excess causes dental fluorosis, so the WHO Guidelines for Drinking-water Quality set a guideline value of 1.5 mg/L for fluoride, and children should use only a small amount of fluoride toothpaste and avoid swallowing it.

智齿为什么常常需要拔掉？

Why do wisdom teeth often need to be removed?

智齿是最后萌出的第三磨牙，多在17～25岁长出，而现代人颌骨相对变小，常常没有足够空间，于是出现阻生、斜向生长或只萌出一部分。这些位置容易堆积食物残渣和菌斑，引起冠周炎、邻牙龋坏和牙根吸收，反复肿痛。因此出现症状或影像上已造成损害时建议拔除；而位置正常、能清洁到位且无症状的智齿可以保留并定期复查。
Wisdom teeth are the third molars, the last to appear, usually between ages 17 and 25, and because modern jaws are relatively smaller there is often not enough room, so they become impacted, tilt sideways or erupt only partly. Such positions trap food debris and plaque, leading to pericoronitis, decay of the neighbouring tooth and root resorption, with repeated pain and swelling. Extraction is therefore advised when symptoms or imaging show damage, while a well-positioned, cleanable and symptom-free wisdom tooth can be kept under periodic review.

免洗洗手液能完全代替流水洗手吗？

Can hand sanitizer fully replace washing with soap and water?

含酒精的免洗洗手液能杀灭许多细菌与部分病毒，但对诺如病毒、艰难梭菌等效果有限，且去不掉肉眼可见的脏污。手明显脏时仍应优先用肥皂流水洗。
Alcohol-based sanitizers kill many bacteria and some viruses but are weak against norovirus and C. diff, and they don't remove visible dirt. When hands are dirty, soap and water are still best.

哪些时刻最该洗手？

When are the most important times to wash your hands?

饭前、便后、外出回家、咳嗽打喷嚏后、处理生肉或垃圾后都应洗手。用肥皂和流动水搓洗至少20秒，能显著减少病菌传播。
Wash hands before eating, after using the toilet, after coming home, after coughing/sneezing, and after handling raw meat or garbage. Scrub with soap and running water for at least 20 seconds to cut germ spread.

为什么含氯消毒剂需要对诺如病毒用比一般细菌更高的浓度才有效？

Why do chlorine disinfectants need higher concentrations against norovirus than against ordinary bacteria?

诺如病毒没有脂质包膜，结构更“结实”，对酒精和许多常用消毒剂不敏感，而含氯消毒剂能破坏其蛋白衣壳。美国疾控中心建议被呕吐物、粪便污染的表面用1000 ppm以上的含氯消毒液作用，远高于日常细菌消毒的浓度。处理时还要先清理可见污物再消毒，并戴手套。
Norovirus has no lipid envelope and is sturdier, resisting alcohol and many routine disinfectants, while chlorine breaks its protein coat. The US CDC advises over 1000 ppm chlorine on surfaces soiled by vomit or stool—far above everyday bacterial levels. First remove visible matter, then disinfect, wearing gloves.

为什么厨房里生熟食物要用不同的砧板和刀具以防交叉污染？

Why should raw and cooked foods use separate boards and knives to avoid cross-contamination?

生肉、禽、海鲜常带沙门菌、弯曲菌等致病微生物，切完若用同一块板处理熟食，细菌会直接落到即食食物上引起食物中毒。世界卫生组织“食品安全五要点”明确把生熟分开列为关键措施。最实作的办法是备两套砧板、用完热水洗消，或先处理熟食再处理生食。
Raw meat, poultry and seafood often carry Salmonella, Campylobacter and the like; using the same board for ready-to-eat food transfers bacteria straight onto it and causes food poisoning. The WHO's “Five keys to safer food” lists separating raw and cooked as essential. Practically, keep two boards, wash with hot water after use, or prep cooked food before raw.

What problems can bad posture cause?

How can we protect our eyes when using screens?

遵循“20-20-20”法则：每看屏幕20分钟，望向约6米外物体20秒。保持适当距离、充足照明，并多眨眼防止干涩。
Follow the 20-20-20 rule: every 20 minutes, look at something about 6 meters away for 20 seconds. Keep a good distance, adequate light, and blink often to avoid dryness.

How long and how should we brush our teeth?

为什么隐形眼镜护理液不能随便用自来水或唾液替代？

Why must contact-lens solution not be replaced by tap water or saliva?

自来水可能含阿米巴等微生物，用其冲洗或浸泡镜片可致严重的感染性角膜炎甚至失明；唾液同样带菌且不通无菌。隐形眼镜护理液含杀菌、保湿和缓冲成分，专为镜片与角膜安全设计。疾控部门强调要用专用溶液、勤洗镜盒并避免戴着镜片游泳或洗澡。
Tap water can hold amoebae that, on a lens, cause severe infectious keratitis and even blindness; saliva is also bacteria-laden and not sterile. Lens solution carries disinfecting, wetting and buffering agents made for the lens and cornea. CDC guidance stresses using only the proper solution, cleaning the case often, and not swimming or showering in lenses.

为什么牙齿上有小黑洞?牙齿蛀了还能长回来吗?

Why do teeth get little holes — can cavities grow back?

吃完糖不刷牙,嘴巴里的小小细菌会把糖变成酸,慢慢咬出小黑洞。乳牙掉了会长新牙接班,可接班的恒牙坏了就再也不长。睡前刷好牙,让细菌没糖吃!
If you skip brushing after candy, tiny germs turn sugar into acid that nibbles little holes in your teeth. Baby teeth make way for grown-up teeth — but once a grown-up tooth is damaged, it never grows back. Brush before bed so germs find no sugar!
