肿瘤的TNM分期在描述什么？

What does TNM cancer staging describe?

TNM是国际通用的实体肿瘤分期系统：T描述原发肿瘤的大小和侵犯深度，N描述区域淋巴结转移的范围，M表示有无远处转移。三者组合后归入I～IV期，分期越晚意味着病变范围越广、预后通常越差。它由国际抗癌联盟与美国癌症联合委员会维护并定期更新，是选择治疗方案、评估预后和比较不同研究结果的共同语言。
TNM is the internationally used staging system for solid tumours: T describes the size and local extent of the primary tumour, N the extent of regional lymph node involvement, and M whether distant metastases are present. The three are combined into stages I to IV, with later stages meaning wider disease and generally poorer prognosis. Maintained and periodically updated by the UICC and the AJCC, it is the shared language for choosing treatment, estimating outlook and comparing results across studies.

《癌症的标志》一文提出的『免疫逃逸』指什么？

What is 'immune evasion' in the Hallmarks of Cancer framework?

Hanahan 与 Weinberg 在 2011 年的更新版中指出，肿瘤除了无限增殖、促血管生成等特征，还会主动躲避免疫系统，例如下调抗原呈递、表达 PD-L1 抑制 T 细胞、或招募抑制性免疫细胞。理解这一点催生了免疫检查点抑制剂等疗法。
In their 2011 update, Hanahan and Weinberg listed immune evasion as a cancer hallmark: tumors actively dodge immunity by down-regulating antigen presentation, expressing PD-L1 to inhibit T cells, or recruiting suppressive immune cells. This insight spawned checkpoint-inhibitor therapies.

p53 蛋白为什么被称为『基因组守护者』？

Why is p53 called the guardian of the genome?

p53 是一种转录因子，在 DNA 受损时被激活，可暂停细胞周期让损伤修复，或诱导不可逆损伤的细胞凋亡。超过半数人类肿瘤都带有 p53 基因突变，使其失去这道关键防线。
p53 is a transcription factor that, when DNA is damaged, halts the cell cycle to allow repair or triggers apoptosis of irreparably harmed cells. More than half of human tumors carry p53 mutations, removing this key safeguard.

端粒和端粒酶与癌症有什么关系？

How are telomeres and telomerase linked to cancer?

正常细胞每分裂一次端粒就缩短一点，终老而停止分裂；多数癌细胞重新激活端粒酶，维持端粒长度从而获得近乎无限的增殖能力。Blackburn、Greider 与 Szostak 因揭示端粒保护机制获 2009 年诺贝尔奖。
Normal cells lose a bit of telomere length with each division and eventually stop; most cancer cells reactivate telomerase to maintain telomeres and gain near-limitless division. Blackburn, Greider, and Szostak won the 2009 Nobel Prize for explaining telomere protection.

癌细胞为什么即便在富氧下也偏好糖酵解？这就是『Warburg 效应』吗？

Why do cancer cells prefer glycolysis even with oxygen—is that the Warburg effect?

Warburg 在 1920 年代观察到肿瘤细胞即使在有氧时也大量摄取葡萄糖并转为乳酸，称为有氧糖酵解。这虽产能效率低，却能快速供给生物合成所需的中间产物，并适应肿瘤缺氧微环境。
Warburg noted in the 1920s that tumor cells consume huge amounts of glucose and convert it to lactate even when oxygen is present—aerobic glycolysis. It is energetically inefficient but rapidly supplies biosynthetic intermediates and suits the hypoxic tumor milieu.

肿瘤血管生成是什么，为什么它被称为癌症的标志之一？

What is tumor angiogenesis and why is it a cancer hallmark?

肿瘤长到一定体积后需要新血管供氧供养，于是分泌 VEGF 等因子促使周围血管长出新分支，这一过程由 Folkman 在 1971 年提出。阻断血管生成的『抗血管生成疗法』因此成为一类肿瘤治疗策略。
Once a tumor reaches a certain size it needs new vessels for oxygen and nutrients, so it secretes factors like VEGF to sprout new capillaries—a process Folkman described in 1971. Blocking this angiogenesis became a class of cancer therapy.

CAR-T 细胞疗法是如何让患者的 T 细胞去攻击肿瘤的？

How does CAR-T therapy turn a patient's own T cells into tumor attackers?

医生取出患者 T 细胞，用病毒载体导入编码嵌合抗原受体的基因，使 T 细胞表面长出能直接识别肿瘤抗原的『人造受体』，扩增后再回输体内。June 团队推动的这项技术对复发白血病和淋巴瘤展现出显著缓解，2017 年获 FDA 批准。
Clinicians harvest a patient's T cells, use a viral vector to insert a gene for a chimeric antigen receptor so the cells display an artificial receptor that recognizes tumor antigens, expand them, and infuse them back. June's team showed striking remissions in relapsed leukemia and lymphoma; the FDA approved it in 2017.

放射治疗为什么要分成很多次照射？

Why is radiotherapy delivered in many small fractions?

分次照射利用了正常组织与肿瘤对放射损伤修复能力的差异：每次治疗间隔期间，正常细胞修复亚致死损伤的能力通常强于肿瘤细胞。分次还让肿瘤内缺氧区域在体积缩小后重新氧合，从而对射线更敏感，并让处于抗辐射细胞周期阶段的细胞重新分布到敏感阶段。因此常规方案多为每天1.8～2 Gy、持续数周，这样在杀灭肿瘤的同时把晚期毒性控制在可接受范围。
Fractionation exploits the difference between normal tissue and tumour in repairing radiation damage: between sessions, healthy cells generally repair sublethal injury better than cancer cells. Splitting the dose also lets hypoxic regions inside a shrinking tumour re-oxygenate and become more radiosensitive, and allows cells sitting in radioresistant phases of the cell cycle to redistribute into sensitive ones. Hence typical schedules of 1.8-2 Gy per day over several weeks, which kill tumour cells while keeping late toxicity acceptable.

为什么癌症转移才是导致大多数患者死亡的原因？

Why does metastasis cause most cancer deaths?

原发肿瘤通常可被手术切除，但少数细胞会脱离、侵入血管或淋巴管，在远处器官定植并形成新病灶，使单一病灶变成系统性病害。转移需要完成上皮—间质转化、存活于循环、再定植等一系列艰难步骤，因而成为治疗难点。
Primary tumors are often removable, but a few cells detach, invade blood or lymph vessels, and colonize distant organs, turning one lesion into systemic disease. Metastasis demands epithelial-mesenchymal transition, survival in circulation, and re-colonization—a hard sequence that makes treatment difficult.

原癌基因和抑癌基因分别是如何推动细胞癌变的？

How do oncogenes and tumor-suppressor genes drive cancer?

原癌基因本是调控生长的『油门』，一旦被突变或扩增异常激活就变成致癌的癌基因；抑癌基因则是『刹车』（如 p53、Rb），一旦失活就失去约束增殖的能力。Bishop 与 Varmus 发现细胞癌基因源于正常原癌基因，获 1989 年诺贝尔奖。
Proto-oncogenes are the growth 'accelerator'; when mutated or amplified they become overactive oncogenes. Tumor suppressors are the 'brakes' (e.g., p53, Rb), and their inactivation removes proliferation control. Bishop and Varmus showed cellular oncogenes derive from normal proto-oncogenes, earning the 1989 Nobel Prize.

什么是肿瘤微环境，为什么它很重要？

What is the tumour microenvironment and why does it matter?

肿瘤不是一团纯粹的癌细胞，还包括被“驯化”的成纤维细胞、免疫细胞、新生血管、细胞外基质和它们分泌的各种因子，这个整体称为肿瘤微环境。它可以为癌细胞提供生长信号、诱导血管生成、制造缺氧和酸性环境，并通过免疫抑制细胞和检查点分子帮助肿瘤逃避免疫监视，还会阻碍药物渗透造成耐药。正因为如此，抗血管生成药和免疫检查点抑制剂等疗法针对的其实是微环境而非癌细胞本身。
A tumour is not a pure mass of cancer cells: it also contains co-opted fibroblasts, immune cells, newly formed vessels, extracellular matrix and the factors they secrete, together making up the tumour microenvironment. It supplies growth signals, drives angiogenesis, creates hypoxic and acidic conditions, and helps the tumour evade immune surveillance through suppressive cells and checkpoint molecules, while also hindering drug penetration and fostering resistance. That is why treatments such as anti-angiogenic drugs and immune checkpoint inhibitors actually target the microenvironment rather than the cancer cell itself.

化疗和放疗是怎样治疗癌症的？

How do chemotherapy and radiation treat cancer?

化疗用药物全身杀灭快速分裂的癌细胞，但也影响正常细胞故会引起脱发、恶心等副作用；放疗用高能射线局部照射肿瘤、破坏其 DNA。二者常联合手术或用于晚期控制。
Chemotherapy uses drugs to kill fast-dividing cancer cells throughout the body, but also affects normal cells, causing side effects like hair loss and nausea. Radiation uses high-energy rays to locally damage tumor DNA. Both are often combined with surgery or used for advanced control.

细胞凋亡失灵为何会让癌症得逞？

How does failed apoptosis let cancer succeed?

凋亡是细胞在受损或不再需要时有序自毁的程序；Kerr、Wyllie 与 Currie 在 1972 年定义这一概念。癌细胞常上调抗凋亡蛋白、下调促凋亡信号，从而逃过清除、持续存活并积累突变。
Apoptosis is the ordered self-destruction of damaged or unneeded cells, a concept Kerr, Wyllie, and Currie defined in 1972. Cancer cells often up-regulate anti-apoptotic proteins and silence pro-apoptotic signals, evading clearance and persisting with accumulating mutations.

癌症本质上是什么？

What is cancer at its core?

癌症是一组因细胞 DNA 突变失控而异常增殖的疾病，可形成肿块并侵袭周围组织，还能经血液淋巴转移到远处（转移）。不同癌症的诱因、治疗和预后差异很大。
Cancer is a group of diseases where cells grow out of control due to DNA mutations, forming masses that invade nearby tissue and can spread through blood or lymph to distant sites (metastasis). Causes, treatments, and outcomes vary widely.

An advanced cancer patient reports persistent breathlessness. Beyond treating the underlying disease, which pharmacological and non-pharmacological interventions can palliative care offer to relieve this symptom?

低剂量阿片类药物是缓解难治性呼吸困难的一线用药,能够减轻呼吸窘迫感,短期规范使用不会导致有临床意义的二氧化碳潴留。非药物手段包括风扇送风刺激面部三叉神经分布区、调整体位、缩唇呼吸与放松训练,焦虑表现突出时可短程联用苯二氮䓬类药物。氧疗主要使存在低氧血症的患者获益,血氧正常的患者同样能从冷风对流中获得主观缓解。
Low-dose opioids are first-line for refractory dyspnea, easing the distress of breathlessness without clinically significant carbon dioxide retention when used appropriately in the short term. Non-pharmacological measures include a fan directing airflow onto the face, upright positioning, pursed-lip breathing and relaxation training, with short benzodiazepine courses reserved for prominent anxiety. Supplemental oxygen benefits chiefly hypoxemic patients, whereas cool moving air brings subjective relief even to those with normal saturation.

化疗为什么常常会掉头发？

Why does chemotherapy often cause hair loss?

传统化疗药攻击的是快速分裂的细胞，而毛囊中的毛母质细胞分裂速度在人体内名列前茅，因此被“连带”损伤，生长期毛发脱落。同样的原理也解释了化疗引起口腔黏膜炎、恶心和白细胞下降。这类脱发通常是暂时的，疗程结束后数月内会重新长出，有时发质或卷曲度略有变化；部分方案可用头皮降温装置减轻脱发。
Conventional chemotherapy targets rapidly dividing cells, and the matrix cells in hair follicles are among the fastest dividers in the body, so they are hit as collateral damage and hairs in the growth phase fall out. The same principle explains chemotherapy-related mouth sores, nausea and low white cell counts. This hair loss is usually temporary, with regrowth within months after treatment ends - sometimes with a slightly different texture or curl - and scalp cooling can reduce it with some regimens.

如何初步辨别可疑的皮肤痣？

How can you spot a suspicious skin mole?

可用「ABCDE」法则：Asymmetry（不对称）、Border（边缘不规则）、Color（颜色不均）、Diameter（直径多大于 6 毫米）、Evolution（近期变化）。任何快速变化的痣都应尽早看皮肤科。
Use the "ABCDE" rule: Asymmetry, irregular Border, uneven Color, Diameter over 6 mm, and Evolution (recent change). Any rapidly changing mole should be checked by a dermatologist early.
