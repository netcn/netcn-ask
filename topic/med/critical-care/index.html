脓毒性休克患者的“1小时集束化治疗”包含哪些核心措施？为什么特别强调血乳酸测定与早期复苏？

What are the core elements of the hour-1 bundle for patients with septic shock, and why are lactate measurement and early resuscitation emphasized so strongly?

包括测乳酸（首查>2 mmol/L需复测）、抗菌药前留血培养、1小时内静脉给予广谱抗菌药、低血压或乳酸≥4时先输约30 mL/kg晶体液、仍低血压则用升压药维持平均动脉压65以上。机理：抗生素每延误一小时死亡风险递增；乳酸反映无氧代谢，既是启动复苏的门槛也是疗效指标，早期达标可改善预后。
The bundle includes measuring lactate (repeat if the first value exceeds 2 mmol/L), drawing blood cultures before antimicrobials, infusing broad-spectrum antibiotics within one hour, rapidly giving about 30 mL/kg of crystalloid for hypotension or lactate of 4 mmol/L or higher, and starting vasopressors when hypotension persists to keep mean arterial pressure at 65 mmHg or above. Every hour of antibiotic delay compounds mortality, while lactate mirrors anaerobic metabolism and serves both as a trigger for resuscitation and a gauge of its adequacy.

机械通气患者为什么每天都要接受撤机筛查并尝试自主呼吸试验，而不是由医生凭经验直接决定何时拔管？

Why should mechanically ventilated patients undergo daily weaning screening plus a spontaneous breathing trial instead of having clinicians decide extubation purely by experience?

机械通气期间呼吸肌只承担部分做功，仅凭氧合与潮气量无法判断患者能否脱离呼吸机。每日先筛出原发病好转、氧合循环稳定者，再以T管或低水平压力支持行30-120分钟自主呼吸试验，检验其能否独立承担全部通气负荷；浅快呼吸指数<105等指标有助预测成败。Ely的随机试验证实，该规范流程显著缩短带机时间并减少并发症。
During ventilation the machine assumes much of the respiratory muscles' work, so oxygenation and tidal volume alone cannot reveal whether the patient can breathe unaided. Each day, patients whose primary illness is improving with stable oxygenation and circulation are screened, then given a 30-120 minute spontaneous breathing trial via T-piece or low-level pressure support to test whether their muscles can carry the full ventilatory load; a rapid-shallow-breathing index below 105 helps predict success. Ely et al.'s randomized trial showed this standardized approach shortens ventilation time and reduces complications.

中重度急性呼吸窘迫综合征（ARDS）患者为什么推荐早期开展俯卧位通气？

Why is early prone-position ventilation recommended for patients with moderate-to-severe acute respiratory distress syndrome (ARDS)?

俯卧位借助重力使背侧萎陷肺泡复张，解除心脏和腹腔内容物对背侧肺的压迫，使通气血流更均匀，并降低跨肺压梯度与剪切应力，从而减轻呼吸机相关肺损伤。PROSEVA试验显示，PaO2/FiO2低于150的重症患者每日俯卧至少16小时，28天死亡率从约33%降至16%；但需团队规范操作，防范压疮与气管导管移位。
Prone positioning lets gravity recruit collapsed dorsal lung regions, lifts the heart and abdominal contents off the dependent lung, evens out ventilation-perfusion matching, and lowers transpulmonary pressure gradients and cyclic shear stress, thereby limiting ventilator-induced lung injury. The PROSEVA trial showed that in patients with PaO2/FiO2 below 150, proning for at least 16 hours daily cut 28-day mortality from about 33% to 16%; success requires trained teams watching for pressure ulcers and tube displacement.
