慢性阻塞性肺疾病（COPD）的核心病理是什么？

What is the core pathology of COPD?

长期有害气体（如吸烟）引发持续的气道和肺泡炎症，导致气流不可逆受限；戒烟和规范吸入治疗可延缓恶化。
Long-term irritants like smoke cause persistent airway and alveolar inflammation with irreversible airflow limitation; quitting smoke and inhaler therapy slow decline.

哮喘发作时在气道里发生了什么？

What happens in the airways during an asthma attack?

气道因过敏或刺激出现平滑肌收缩、黏膜水肿和黏液增多，使气流受限，表现为喘息和胸闷；规范用药可控制。
Allergens or irritants trigger muscle tightening, swelling and extra mucus in the airways, narrowing airflow - wheeze and chest tightness. Proper medication controls it.

为什么雾霾天要少出门？

Why stay indoors on hazy, polluted days?

PM2.5等细微颗粒物可深入肺部甚至进入血液，刺激呼吸道、加重心肺负担；敏感人群尤其要注意防护。
Fine particles like PM2.5 reach deep into the lungs and even the blood, irritating airways and straining heart and lungs - especially for sensitive groups.

为什么深呼吸能让人平静？

Why does deep breathing calm us down?

缓慢的深呼吸会激活副交感神经、降低心率与应激反应，帮助身体从“战斗或逃跑”切换到放松状态。
Slow deep breaths activate the parasympathetic nervous system, lowering heart rate and stress responses and shifting you from 'fight or flight' to calm.
