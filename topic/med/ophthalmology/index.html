青光眼为什么被称为“视力的小偷”？

Why is glaucoma called the thief of sight?

青光眼是视神经进行性损害，常与眼内压升高有关。它最先损失的是周边视野，中央视力在很长时间内保持清晰，而大脑会自动“填补”缺损，因此患者往往等到视野明显缩小才察觉，此时损伤已不可逆。定期检查眼压、视神经和视野是唯一可靠的早期发现方式，40岁以上、有家族史、高度近视者更应重视。及时使用降眼压滴眼液、激光或手术可以延缓进展，保住剩余视野。
Glaucoma is progressive damage to the optic nerve, usually associated with raised intraocular pressure. It takes the peripheral field first while central vision stays sharp for a long time, and the brain fills in the missing areas, so people often notice only when the field has narrowed markedly - by which point the damage is irreversible. Regular checks of eye pressure, the optic disc and the visual field are the only reliable way to catch it early, especially after age 40, with a family history or with high myopia. Pressure-lowering drops, laser or surgery started in time slow progression and protect the remaining field.

为什么急性闭角型青光眼容易在夜晚昏暗环境中突然发作?

Why does acute angle-closure glaucoma often strike suddenly in dim evening light?

昏暗环境下瞳孔散大,周边虹膜堆积,使本就狭窄的前房角进一步关闭,房水外流受阻导致眼压急剧升高。典型发作表现为剧烈眼痛、同侧头痛、视物模糊伴虹视,并常因刺激迷走神经而恶心呕吐。夜间长时间低头看手机或情绪激动都可能成为诱发因素。
In darkness the pupil dilates and the peripheral iris bunches up, closing an already narrow anterior chamber angle and blocking aqueous outflow, so intraocular pressure spikes abruptly. Typical attacks bring severe eye pain, ipsilateral headache, blurred vision with halos around lights, and often nausea and vomiting from vagal stimulation. Long sessions of reading a phone screen with the head bowed at night or emotional stress can act as triggers.

湿性老年黄斑变性患者定期玻璃体腔注射抗VEGF药物,究竟控制的是视力恶化的哪个环节?

In neovascular age-related macular degeneration, which step of vision decline do repeated intravitreal anti-VEGF injections actually control?

抗VEGF药物中和血管内皮生长因子,促使脉络膜新生血管退缩、减少渗漏与视网膜下积液,从而减轻光感受器受到的机械牵拉和缺血损害。它们无法逆转已萎缩的视网膜色素上皮和死亡的光感受器,因此疗效高度依赖早发现以及按方案规律注射。多数坚持治疗的患者可多年维持有用的阅读视力,自行拉长给药间隔常伴随渗出复发和视力回退。
Anti-VEGF agents neutralize vascular endothelial growth factor, driving regression of choroidal neovessels and reducing leakage and subretinal fluid, which limits mechanical traction and ischemic injury to photoreceptors. They cannot restore atrophic retinal pigment epithelium or lost photoreceptors, so outcomes depend on early detection and disciplined adherence to the injection schedule. Most compliant patients retain useful reading vision for years, while unilaterally stretched intervals invite recurrent exudation and vision loss.

急性闭角型青光眼发作时为什么会出现看灯有彩虹圈和剧烈眼痛?

Why do acute angle-closure glaucoma attacks cause rainbow halos around lights and severe eye pain?

发作时晶状体前移或瞳孔阻滞使房水无法经瞳孔进入前房,前房角骤然关闭,眼压可在数小时内升至50mmHg以上。角膜水肿使入眼光线发生散射,围绕光源形成彩虹样光环,即虹视。眼压骤升牵拉三叉神经末梢,引起剧烈眼胀痛、同侧头痛、恶心呕吐,常被误诊为胃肠炎或偏头痛而延误治疗。
During an attack, lens forward shift or pupillary block stops aqueous from reaching the anterior chamber, the angle closes abruptly, and intraocular pressure can exceed 50 mmHg within hours. Corneal edema scatters incoming light, forming rainbow-colored rings around lights, known as halos. The pressure surge stretches trigeminal endings, causing intense ocular pain with ipsilateral headache, nausea, and vomiting, which is often mistaken for gastroenteritis or migraine and delays sight-saving treatment.

白内障为什么会让视力变模糊？

Why does a cataract blur vision?

眼睛的晶状体原本高度透明，随年龄增长其中的蛋白质逐渐氧化、聚集，晶状体变得混浊发黄，光线无法清晰聚焦到视网膜上，于是视物模糊、怕光眩光、颜色变暗。紫外线暴露、吸烟、糖尿病和长期使用糖皮质激素会加速这一过程。白内障是全球致盲的首要原因，但手术摘除混浊晶状体并植入人工晶体是非常成熟有效的治疗，多数患者术后视力显著改善。
The eye's lens is normally crystal clear, but with age its proteins oxidize and clump, so the lens turns cloudy and yellow and can no longer focus light sharply on the retina - vision blurs, glare becomes troublesome and colours look dull. Ultraviolet exposure, smoking, diabetes and long-term corticosteroid use all speed the process. Cataract is the leading cause of blindness worldwide, yet removing the cloudy lens and implanting an artificial one is a mature, highly effective operation after which most people see much better.

为什么不能用手揉眼睛？

Why shouldn't we rub our eyes with our hands?

手上有很多看不见的细菌和病毒，揉眼睛会把它们送进眼里，可能引起眼睛红肿、发痒的“红眼病”。用力揉还会擦伤眼睛表面很薄的一层，让眼睛更痛更痒。眼睛不舒服时可以先洗手，再闭上眼睛用干净的凉毛巾轻轻敷一会儿，如果一直难受就要告诉大人去看医生。
Your hands carry lots of germs you cannot see, and rubbing pushes them into your eyes, which can cause the red, itchy, swollen eyes of conjunctivitis. Rubbing hard can also scratch the very thin surface layer of the eye and make it hurt and itch even more. If your eyes feel uncomfortable, wash your hands first, then close your eyes and hold a clean cool cloth over them for a while - and tell a grown-up so you can see a doctor if it keeps bothering you.
