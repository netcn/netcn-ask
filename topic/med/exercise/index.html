运动真的能增强免疫力吗？

Does exercise really boost immunity?

规律、适度的运动可降低感染风险并改善免疫调节；但长时间高强度运动后会有短暂的免疫“空窗期”。关键在于持之以恒、避免过度。
Regular moderate exercise lowers infection risk and improves immune regulation, though very long intense sessions briefly suppress immunity. Consistency and moderation are key.

为什么高原低氧训练能提升耐力运动员的携氧能力？

Why can altitude/hypoxia training raise an endurance athlete's oxygen-carrying capacity?

持续低氧刺激肾脏分泌促红细胞生成素（EPO），使红细胞和血红蛋白增多，从而提高血液携氧能力；同时线粒体和毛细血管密度也会适应性增加。运动员常在中等海拔（约2000–2500米）“住高练低”以平衡刺激与训练量。返回平原后这种“高原增益”约维持数周，但过低氧或过度训练反而抑制免疫与恢复。
Sustained hypoxia makes the kidney secrete erythropoietin (EPO), raising red cells and haemoglobin so blood carries more oxygen, while mitochondria and capillary density also adapt upward. Athletes often “live high, train low” around 2000–2500 m to balance stimulus with volume. The “altitude gain” lasts weeks at sea level, but too-low oxygen or overtraining suppresses immunity and recovery.

运动如何改善胰岛素敏感性？

How does exercise improve insulin sensitivity?

肌肉收缩时无需胰岛素即可摄取葡萄糖，并上调GLUT4转运蛋白，长期运动降低体脂、减轻炎症，从而提升全身胰岛素敏感性。
Contracting muscles take up glucose without insulin and upregulate GLUT4; long-term exercise cuts fat and inflammation, raising whole-body insulin sensitivity.

运动后肌肉酸痛（DOMS）的成因是什么？

What causes delayed-onset muscle soreness (DOMS)?

DOMS多因不熟悉的离心运动造成肌纤维微损伤与局部炎症，通常在运动后24–72小时达高峰，属可逆的适应性反应。
DOMS mainly comes from micro-tears and local inflammation after unfamiliar eccentric exercise, peaking 24–72 hours later—a reversible adaptation.

休息日为什么重要？

Why are rest days important?

肌肉是在休息时修复和变强的，长期不休息容易出现过度训练和运动损伤。一般每周安排1–2天完全或低强度休息。
Muscles rebuild and strengthen during rest; skipping it raises overuse-injury risk. Plan 1–2 rest or very light days each week.

什么是运动性横纹肌溶解？

What is exertional rhabdomyolysis?

指平时不习惯的极端运动使肌肉细胞破裂，释放肌红蛋白，可能堵塞并损伤肾脏。若出现严重肌肉剧痛、肿胀或酱油色尿，应及时就医。
It's when unaccustomed extreme exertion ruptures muscle cells, releasing myoglobin that can damage kidneys. Severe muscle pain, swelling or dark 'cola' urine warrants medical care.

Can I still exercise with bad knees?

可以。游泳、骑车、散步等低冲击运动，以及加强大腿肌肉的练习，能保护膝关节。若某动作引起疼痛应停止并咨询专业人员。
Yes. Low-impact activities like swimming, cycling and walking, plus thigh-strengthening, protect knees. Stop any move that causes pain and consult a professional.

How do I tell if exercise intensity is right?

中等强度时你能说话但唱不了歌；也可用“最大心率的50%–70%”作参考。更重要的是倾听身体，循序渐进。
At moderate intensity you can talk but not sing; about 50–70% of max heart rate is a guide. Most important: listen to your body and progress gradually.

什么是“最大心率”和训练区间？

What are maximum heart rate and training zones?

常用估算最大心率为220减年龄，中等强度约达最大心率的50–70%，高强度为70–85%，可据此调节运动负荷。
A common max-heart-rate estimate is 220 minus age; moderate intensity is about 50–70% and vigorous 70–85% of it, guiding training load.

运动为什么能让人心情变好？

Why does exercise improve your mood?

运动时身体会释放内啡肽和血清素，带来放松与愉悦感，还能促进脑源性神经营养因子（BDNF）生成，帮助神经元生长连接。规律运动同时改善睡眠、提升体能和自信，研究显示它与减轻抑郁焦虑症状相关。
Exercise triggers the release of endorphins and serotonin, bringing calm and a sense of well-being, and boosts brain-derived neurotrophic factor (BDNF), which supports neuron growth and connectivity. Regular activity also improves sleep, fitness, and self-confidence, and studies link it to reduced symptoms of depression and anxiety.

为什么运动后过量补充蛋白质反而会增加肾脏负担？

Why can over-supplementing protein after exercise burden the kidneys?

普通训练者每日约1.2–1.6 g/kg蛋白质已足够修复肌肉，超过需要的部分会被脱氨、以尿素形式经肾排出，并不变成更多肌肉。长期高蛋白加脱水会升高肾脏滤过负荷，对已有肾病者尤其不利。天然膳食（蛋、奶、豆、肉）通常已能满足，蛋白粉应视总摄入而非盲目加量。
For ordinary training, about 1.2–1.6 g/kg per day already repairs muscle; excess is deaminated and excreted as urea by the kidneys, not turned into more muscle. Long-term high protein with low fluid raises filtration load and harms anyone with kidney disease. Natural foods (egg, milk, bean, meat) usually suffice, so powders should follow total intake, not blind adding.

久坐为什么有害健康？

Why is sitting too long bad for health?

长时间久坐降低肌肉活动和能量消耗，与代谢综合征、静脉血栓和早死风险升高相关，建议每小时起身活动。
Prolonged sitting lowers muscle activity and energy use, linking to metabolic syndrome, blood clots, and higher early-death risk; stand hourly.

Can strength training make older adults safer?

Yes. Moderate strength training maintains muscle and bone and improves balance, lowering fall risk. Start light with good form, ideally guided by a professional.

Why warm up before exercise?

Warming up raises muscle temperature and blood flow and loosens joints, lowering strain risk. About 5–10 minutes of brisk walking plus dynamic stretches suffices.

有氧运动和力量训练有何不同？

How do aerobic and strength training differ?

有氧运动（如跑步）主要提升心肺耐力，力量训练（如举重）增加肌肉与骨密度，两者互补最有益健康。
Aerobic exercise (running) boosts cardio endurance, while strength training (lifting) builds muscle and bone density; both together are best.

How should we drink during exercise?

Sip before, during and after; for sessions over an hour, replace electrolytes too. Avoid gulping large amounts at once to prevent stomach discomfort.

#运动补水 #电解质

运动前后应该怎么吃？

What should you eat before and after exercise?

运动前1-2小时补充适量碳水化合物（如香蕉、燕麦、全麦面包）能为身体储备“燃料”；运动后尽快（30-60分钟内）摄入蛋白质加碳水化合物，有助于肌肉修复和糖原恢复。长时间耐力运动要及时补水补盐，大量出汗时还可选择含电解质的饮品。具体需要量取决于运动类型、强度和时长，普通健身者不必过度精算。
A carbohydrate snack an hour or two before exercise—banana, oats, or whole-wheat bread—fuels the session; after exercise, protein plus carbohydrate within 30–60 minutes helps repair muscle and replenish glycogen. Long endurance workouts call for timely fluid and sodium, and electrolyte drinks when sweating heavily. Exact amounts depend on the type, intensity, and duration of exercise, and casual exercisers don't need to over-calculate.

为什么运动前做动态拉伸比静态压腿更合适？

Why is dynamic stretching better than static stretching right before exercise?

动态拉伸通过可控的活动（如摆腿、高抬腿）升高体温、增加关节活动度和血供，让肌肉为运动做好准备。静态长时间压腿会在短时内降低肌肉爆发力与稳定性，更适合放在运动后放松。因此热身应以动态动作为主，把静态拉伸留给冷身阶段。
Dynamic stretching—leg swings, high knees and the like—raises temperature, range and blood flow so muscles are ready to move. Long static holds briefly lower power and stability, so they fit better after exercise for cooling down. Warm up with movement and save static stretches for the end.
