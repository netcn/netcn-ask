胰岛素和胰高血糖素如何共同维持血糖稳定？

How do insulin and glucagon keep blood sugar stable?

餐后血糖升高时胰岛β细胞释放胰岛素，促进葡萄糖进入细胞储存；饥饿时α细胞释放胰高血糖素，促使肝脏释放葡萄糖。二者相反相成，使血糖维持在一个较窄的范围。
After meals, beta cells release insulin to store glucose; when fasting, alpha cells release glucagon to free glucose from the liver. Working in opposition, they keep blood sugar in a narrow range.

1型和2型糖尿病的根本区别在哪里？

What is the fundamental difference between type 1 and type 2 diabetes?

1型因自身免疫破坏胰岛β细胞，导致胰岛素绝对缺乏；2型早期以胰岛素抵抗为主、后期分泌也不足。二者在病因、发病年龄与治疗策略上都不相同。
Type 1 is autoimmune destruction of beta cells causing absolute insulin deficiency; type 2 starts with insulin resistance and later reduced secretion. Their causes, age patterns and treatments differ.

为什么压力大时皮质醇会升高？

Why does cortisol rise when we are stressed?

面对压力，下丘脑 - 垂体 - 肾上腺轴被激活，肾上腺分泌皮质醇，帮助调动能量、暂时抑制非紧急功能。短期有益，长期偏高则可能损害免疫与代谢。
Stress activates the HPA axis, and the adrenals release cortisol to mobilize energy and pause non-urgent functions. Useful short-term, but chronically high levels can harm immunity and metabolism.

什么是激素的“负反馈”调节？

What is negative feedback in hormone control?

当血液中某种激素水平过高时，会反过来抑制上游腺体继续分泌，使其回落；过低则解除抑制。这种闭环让激素水平保持稳定。
When a hormone gets too high, it signals upstream glands to dial back; when too low, the brake lifts. This loop keeps hormone levels steady.

为什么更年期与雌激素下降有关？

Why is menopause linked to falling estrogen?

女性卵巢随年龄衰退，雌激素分泌减少，影响血管舒缩、骨代谢与情绪，于是出现潮热、骨量流失加快等变化。
As ovaries age, estrogen falls, affecting blood-vessel tone, bone turnover and mood - hence hot flashes and faster bone loss.

甲状腺有什么作用，为什么会让人怕热或怕冷？

What does the thyroid do, and why can it make you heat- or cold-sensitive?

甲状腺分泌激素调控全身代谢速率。激素过多（甲亢）时代谢加快、怕热心慌；过少（甲减）时代谢变慢、怕冷乏力。
The thyroid hormones set your metabolic rate. Too much (hyperthyroidism) speeds metabolism - heat intolerance and palpitations; too little (hypothyroidism) slows it - cold sensitivity and fatigue.

为什么有时候我们会突然长高？

Why do we sometimes grow taller suddenly?

因为身体里有种叫生长激素的东西，在我们睡觉和运动时悄悄帮忙长个子。
A hormone called growth hormone quietly helps you grow taller while you sleep and play.
