“Zone 2”低强度有氧训练有什么用？

What is the use of Zone 2 low-intensity aerobic training?

Zone 2 是约能轻松说话的中低强度有氧区间，长期训练提升线粒体数量和脂肪供能效率，被认为有助于耐力和代谢健康，是很多耐力运动员的基础训练。
Zone 2 is a moderate aerobic intensity where you can still talk; sustained training grows mitochondria and fat-burning efficiency, aiding endurance and metabolic health—a base for many endurance athletes.

生物钟（昼夜节律）如何被光照设定？

How is the circadian clock set by light?

大脑视交叉上核接收视网膜传来的光信号，把核心体温、激素（如皮质醇晨高夜低）和清醒度锁定到约 24 小时节律；晨光尤其有助于“校准”时钟。
The brain's suprachiasmatic nucleus receives light signals from the retina to lock core temperature, hormones (cortisol high in morning), and alertness to a ~24-hour rhythm; morning light best “calibrates” the clock.

高强度间歇训练（HIIT）为何高效？

Why is HIIT efficient?

HIIT 在短时高强度与恢复交替中进行，能在更短时间刺激心肺、线粒体和胰岛素敏感性，但强度大、需循序渐进，心血管疾病者须先评估。
HIIT alternates short intense bursts with recovery, efficiently stimulating cardio, mitochondria, and insulin sensitivity in less time—but it's demanding; those with heart disease should be evaluated first.

DASH 饮食为什么有助于降压？

Why does the DASH diet help lower blood pressure?

DASH 饮食强调低盐、高钾高镁高钙的蔬果全谷和瘦肉，通过减轻钠负荷、改善血管张力来帮助降低收缩压，是高血压管理的非药物基石。
The DASH diet emphasizes low salt plus potassium-, magnesium-, and calcium-rich produce, whole grains, and lean protein; by cutting sodium load and improving vessel tone, it lowers systolic pressure—a non-drug cornerstone of hypertension care.

为什么久坐即使“有运动”也有害？

Why is sitting harmful even if you “exercise”?

研究显示长时间静坐会独立地损害糖脂代谢和血管功能，哪怕每天也锻炼；因此“少坐、多站、常活动”与规律运动同样重要。
Prolonged sitting independently harms glucose/lipid metabolism and vessel function even if you exercise daily; so “sit less, stand more, move often” matters alongside formal workouts.

蓝光为什么会影响睡眠？

Why does blue light affect sleep?

夜间蓝光（手机、屏幕）会抑制褪黑素分泌、推迟生物钟，使入睡变难；睡前调暗屏幕、减少强光有助睡眠卫生。
Evening blue light (phones, screens) suppresses melatonin and delays the body clock, making sleep harder; dimming screens and avoiding bright light before bed supports sleep hygiene.

间歇性禁食（如 16:8）有哪些证据与风险？

What is the evidence and risk for intermittent fasting (e.g., 16:8)?

部分研究显示限时进食可改善胰岛素敏感性和体重，但证据强弱不一，且孕妇、进食障碍史者和儿童不宜；它并非人人适用的“魔法”，开始前最好咨询医生。
Some studies show time-restricted eating (e.g., 16:8) improves insulin sensitivity and weight, but evidence is mixed, and it's unsuitable for pregnancy, eating-disorder history, or children—not a universal fix; consult a doctor first.

什么是最大摄氧量（VO2max）？

What is VO2max?

VO2max 是身体在极限运动时每分钟每千克体重能利用的最大氧气量，是心肺耐力的黄金指标；通过规律有氧训练可提升。
VO2max is the maximum oxygen your body uses per minute per kilogram at peak exercise—a gold-standard of cardiorespiratory fitness—and rises with regular aerobic training.

酒精对睡眠的真实影响是什么？

What is alcohol's real effect on sleep?

酒精虽能让人更快入睡，却会破坏深睡和快速眼动睡眠、增加夜醒与打鼾，总体降低睡眠质量；所谓“助眠”是错觉。
Alcohol may help you fall asleep faster but disrupts deep and REM sleep and raises night awakenings and snoring, lowering overall quality—the “sleep aid” is an illusion.

什么是“地中海饮食”？

What is the Mediterranean diet?

地中海饮食以蔬果、全谷物、橄榄油、鱼类和坚果为主，红肉和加工食品较少，长期研究提示它有助于心脑健康和长寿。
The Mediterranean diet centers on vegetables, whole grains, olive oil, fish, and nuts, with little red or processed meat; long-term studies link it to heart, brain, and longevity benefits.

咖啡因在体内多久代谢掉一半？

How long does it take to metabolize half of caffeine?

咖啡因的平均半衰期约 3–5 小时，但受基因、肝脏酶和避孕药等因素影响很大；下午摄入可能延迟入睡，敏感者宜早些饮用。
Caffeine's average half-life is about 3–5 hours but varies with genes, liver enzymes, and contraceptives; afternoon intake can delay sleep, so sensitive people should drink earlier.

运动处方和普通锻炼计划有什么不同？

How does an exercise prescription differ from a workout plan?

运动处方像药方一样，依据个人健康目标和身体状况，规定频率、强度、时间和类型（FITT），有时由专业人员为慢病管理量身制定。
An exercise prescription, like a drug order, specifies frequency, intensity, time, and type (FITT) based on your health and goals, sometimes tailored by professionals for chronic-disease management.
