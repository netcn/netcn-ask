为什么眼动脱敏再加工（EMDR）会被用于治疗创伤后应激障碍（PTSD）？

Why is eye movement desensitization and reprocessing (EMDR) used for PTSD?

EMDR让患者在忆起创伤的同时进行双侧刺激（如指导眼动），被认为能促进大脑对记忆的“再加工”，削弱创伤画面的情绪负荷、重建更适应的联结。多项随机试验和WHO、APA指南将其列为PTSD的推荐心理治疗之一，效果不依赖详细暴露讲述。它需在受过训练的治疗师手中进行。
In EMDR the patient holds the trauma in mind while doing bilateral stimulation such as guided eye movements; this is thought to help the brain “reprocess” the memory, softening its emotional charge and building calmer links. Trials and WHO/APA guidelines list it among recommended PTSD therapies, working without detailed exposure narrative, but only with a trained therapist.

双相情感障碍的核心特征是什么？

What are the core features of bipolar disorder?

双相障碍以情绪在抑郁和躁狂或轻躁狂之间摆动为特征，躁狂期表现为精力亢进、睡眠减少和冲动。易与单纯抑郁混淆而误治。规范药物与心理治疗可稳定病情。
Bipolar disorder swings between depression and mania or hypomania, with manic phases of high energy, less sleep, and impulsivity. It is easily mistaken for plain depression and mistreated. Medication and therapy stabilize it.

认知行为疗法的基本原则是什么？

What are the basic principles of cognitive behavioral therapy?

CBT 认为情绪和行为受想法影响，通过识别并修正歪曲认知来改变感受。它结构清晰、有时限，常用于抑郁和焦虑。家庭作业和练习是重要环节。
CBT holds that thoughts drive emotion and behavior, so identifying and correcting distorted thinking changes feelings. It is structured and time-limited, used for depression and anxiety. Homework and practice are key parts.

心理危机干预的急性期目标是什么？

What are the acute goals of psychological crisis intervention?

急性期目标是稳定情绪、保证安全、恢复基本功能和连接支持资源，而非立即深挖创伤。采用倾听、正常化和实用问题解决。后续再转介持续治疗。
Acute crisis care aims to stabilize emotion, ensure safety, restore basic function, and connect supports, not to dig into trauma immediately. It uses listening, normalization, and practical problem-solving. Ongoing treatment follows.

童年不良经历如何影响成年心理健康？

How do adverse childhood experiences affect adult mental health?

童年虐待、忽视和家庭暴力等 ACE 会提高成年期抑郁、成瘾和躯体疾病风险，机制涉及应激系统长期敏化。早期识别与保护性关系可缓冲伤害。这是预防医学的重要方向。
Adverse childhood experiences like abuse, neglect, and domestic violence raise adult depression, addiction, and physical illness risk via long-term stress-system sensitization. Early identification and protective relationships buffer harm. It is key to prevention.

创伤后应激障碍的主要表现有哪些？

What are the main features of PTSD?

PTSD 在经历或目睹严重创伤后出现，表现为反复闯入性回忆、回避、警觉增高和负性认知。症状持续超过一个月并影响功能才考虑诊断。早期心理干预有助预防慢性化。
PTSD follows severe trauma with intrusive memories, avoidance, hyperarousal, and negative thoughts. Diagnosis needs symptoms over a month that impair function. Early psychological care helps prevent chronicity.

为什么产后情绪问题容易被忽视？

Why are postpartum mood problems easily overlooked?

产后抑郁和焦虑常被误认为正常的育儿疲惫，且产妇可能因自责而隐瞒。激素骤变、睡眠剥夺和社会支持不足共同推高风险。及时筛查和陪伴能改善预后。
Postpartum depression and anxiety are often mistaken for normal parenting fatigue, and mothers may hide them from guilt. Hormonal drops, sleep loss, and weak support raise risk. Timely screening and companionship improve outcomes.

强迫症和爱整洁有什么区别？

How does OCD differ from simply being tidy?

强迫症的核心是侵入性念头引发强烈焦虑，患者被迫重复仪式行为以缓解，且明知不合理却无法停止，耗费大量时间。普通爱整洁是有序偏好而不感痛苦。差异在于痛苦和功能受损。
OCD centers on intrusive thoughts causing strong anxiety, driving repetitive rituals to relieve, done knowingly yet uncontrollably and time-consuming. Plain tidiness is an orderly preference without distress. The difference is distress and impairment.

为什么述情障碍会让人难以识别和表达自己的情绪？

Why does alexithymia make it hard to recognise and express emotions?

述情障碍是一种在人群中并不少见的特质，表现为难以辨别身体里的情绪线索、找不到合适词语描述感受，常把情绪以头痛、胃痛等躯体不适表达。研究提示它与前额叶-边缘系统连接减弱有关，并会增加焦虑、抑郁和心身疾病风险。识别它有助于人们用更有效的途径管理情绪，而非只归咎于“性格”。
Alexithymia is a fairly common trait where people struggle to read emotional cues in the body and find words for feelings, often expressing distress as headaches or stomachaches. Work links it to weaker prefrontal–limbic connections and to higher anxiety, depression and psychosomatic risk. Naming it helps people manage emotion through better routes rather than blaming “character”.

正念冥想有什么用？

What is mindfulness meditation good for?

正念通过专注当下、不带评判地觉察，帮助减轻焦虑和改善注意。每天几分钟练习即可见益。它是辅助方法，严重问题仍需专业帮助。
Mindfulness means noticing the present without judgment, which eases anxiety and improves attention. A few minutes daily helps. It is supportive; serious issues still need professionals.

睡眠和情绪有什么关系？

What is the link between sleep and mood?

睡眠不足会放大负面情绪、降低情绪调节能力，而抑郁焦虑又常伴失眠。两者互相影响，形成恶性循环。改善一边常能带动另一边。
Poor sleep amplifies negative emotion and lowers regulation, while depression and anxiety often bring insomnia. They feed each other in a cycle. Improving one often helps the other.

抑郁症有哪些常见表现？

What are common signs of depression?

持续两周以上的情绪低落、兴趣减退、精力下降，并伴睡眠或食欲改变，可能是抑郁信号。它是一种常见的医学状况而非性格软弱。应及早就诊评估。
Low mood, lost interest, and low energy lasting over two weeks with sleep or appetite changes may signal depression. It is a common medical condition, not weakness. Seek evaluation early.

青少年为何更容易出现情绪问题？

Why are adolescents more prone to emotional problems?

青春期激素、大脑发育和社交压力叠加，使青少年情绪波动更大。学业、家庭和同伴关系都是影响因素。家庭理解和及时支持十分关键。
Puberty stacks hormones, brain development, and social pressure, making teens' moods swing more. School, family, and peers all play a role. Family understanding and timely support are key.

心理咨询和聊天有什么区别？

How does counseling differ from chatting?

心理咨询由受过训练的专业人员，用系统方法帮助处理情绪与行为问题。它目标明确、有保密原则，不同于朋友闲聊。有困扰时求助是积极的选择。
Counseling is done by trained professionals using structured methods for emotional and behavioral issues. It is goal-oriented and confidential, unlike casual talk. Seeking it when struggling is a positive choice.

为什么心理危机中“耐心倾听、不急于评判”比急于给建议更重要？

Why does "listening without judging" matter more than quick advice in a psychological crisis?

处在危机中的人首先需要感到被理解和安全，过早评判或说教会让他关闭沟通、更孤立。非评判性倾听能降低对方的生理性唤起、建立信任，为后续专业帮助铺路。我国心理危机干预指南把“倾听与共情”列为首要步骤，再评估风险并转介。
Someone in crisis first needs to feel understood and safe; early judgement or lecturing makes them shut down and feel more alone. Non-judgemental listening lowers arousal, builds trust and paves the way for professional help. Chinese crisis-intervention guidance lists “listen and empathise” as the first step before assessing risk and referring on.

怎么和感到抑郁的朋友相处？

How should you be with a friend who seems depressed?

倾听而不评判、不急于给建议，是支持抑郁朋友最好的方式。鼓励其寻求专业帮助而非独自硬扛。持续陪伴本身就有价值。
Listening without judgment and resisting quick advice is the best support for a depressed friend. Encourage professional help rather than struggling alone. Simply staying present is valuable.

运动对情绪有好处吗？

Is exercise good for mood?

规律运动能促进内啡肽等神经递质释放，缓解压力和低落情绪。每周数次中等强度活动即有助益。运动是辅助手段，不能替代专业治疗。
Regular exercise releases neurotransmitters like endorphins that ease stress and low mood. A few weekly sessions of moderate activity help. It is supportive, not a replacement for professional care.

焦虑和普通的担心有什么区别？

What is the difference between anxiety and normal worry?

正常担心通常有明确原因并随事情解决而消失。焦虑障碍则是持续、过度且难以控制的担忧，影响日常生活。若长期困扰应寻求专业帮助。
Normal worry usually has a clear cause and fades when resolved. An anxiety disorder is persistent, excessive, hard-to-control worry that disrupts daily life. If it lingers, seek professional help.

长期压力大有什么影响？

What are the effects of long-term stress?

持续高压可影响睡眠、食欲、免疫和心血管健康，并加重焦虑情绪。找到适合的减压方式很重要。必要时可寻求心理支持。
Chronic stress can affect sleep, appetite, immunity, and heart health, and worsen anxiety. Finding suitable stress relief matters. Seek psychological support when needed.

聊到自杀念头时该怎么办？

What should you do if someone mentions suicidal thoughts?

认真倾听、不轻视，并鼓励对方联系专业机构或求助热线。陪伴其远离危险并协助获得帮助。这是需要专业介入的紧急信号。
Listen seriously, do not dismiss it, and encourage contact with professionals or a helpline. Stay with the person, keep them safe, and help them get care. This is an emergency signal needing professional help.
