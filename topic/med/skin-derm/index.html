为什么夏天容易被晒伤？

Why do we get sunburned more in summer?

紫外线（尤其UVB）会损伤皮肤细胞DNA，引发红肿疼痛的炎症反应，也就是晒伤。涂防晒、避开强紫外线时段可减少伤害。
Ultraviolet light - especially UVB - damages skin-cell DNA and triggers the red, painful inflammation we call sunburn. Sunscreen and avoiding peak sun reduce harm.

湿疹（特应性皮炎）的核心机制是什么？

What is the core mechanism of eczema (atopic dermatitis)?

它常与皮肤屏障功能缺陷、免疫失衡（如2型炎症）和遗传易感相关，使皮肤易干痒、发炎和感染。规范保湿与抗炎治疗可显著改善。
It often involves a defective skin barrier, immune imbalance (e.g., type-2 inflammation) and genetic susceptibility, making skin dry, itchy, inflamed and infection-prone. Structured moisturizing and anti-inflammatory care help greatly.

为什么伤口结痂前保持适度湿润更利于愈合？

Why does a lightly moist wound heal better than a dry one?

适度湿润的环境利于表皮细胞迁移和生长因子发挥作用，愈合更快、瘢痕更轻；过度干燥会延缓上皮化。但过湿或感染则会适得其反。
A lightly moist environment helps skin cells migrate and growth factors work, speeding healing with less scarring; too dry slows it. But excess moisture or infection reverses the benefit.

为什么摔倒擦破皮会流血结痂？

Why do scrapes bleed and then form a scab?

皮肤破了，下面的小血管会流血；身体很快用血痂像小盖子一样把伤口盖住，保护它慢慢长好。
When skin breaks, tiny blood vessels bleed; your body quickly caps the spot with a scab like a little lid so it can heal.

皮肤是怎样保护我们身体的？

How does skin protect the body?

皮肤的角质层和皮脂膜构成物理与化学屏障，阻挡病原体和水分流失；其中的免疫细胞和神经还能感知伤害并启动防御。
The stratum corneum and skin oils form a physical and chemical barrier against germs and water loss; immune cells and nerves sense damage and launch defense.

为什么老年人皮肤容易干燥发痒？

Why does older skin get dry and itchy?

随着年龄增长，皮脂和汗液分泌减少、角质层保水能力下降，皮肤更易干燥；适度保湿、减少过热洗浴可缓解。
With age, less oil and sweat and weaker water retention make skin drier; gentle moisturizing and cooler baths help.
