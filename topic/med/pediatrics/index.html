为什么儿童比大人更容易得中耳炎？

Why do children get middle ear infections more often than adults?

连接中耳与鼻咽部的咽鼓管在儿童时期又短、又宽、位置更水平，鼻咽部的病原体和分泌物更容易进入中耳；加上腺样体较大、上呼吸道感染频繁、免疫系统尚在成熟，中耳炎因此高发。多数急性中耳炎会自行缓解，医生常先观察并镇痛，只在特定情况下使用抗生素。母乳喂养、避免二手烟、按时接种肺炎球菌和流感疫苗都能降低发病风险。
In childhood the Eustachian tube linking the middle ear to the nasopharynx is short, wide and more horizontal, so germs and secretions from the nose and throat reach the middle ear easily; larger adenoids, frequent upper respiratory infections and a still-maturing immune system add to the risk. Most acute otitis media resolves on its own, so doctors often start with observation and pain relief and reserve antibiotics for particular situations. Breastfeeding, avoiding second-hand smoke and receiving pneumococcal and influenza vaccines on schedule all lower the risk.

口服补液盐为什么能救回腹泻脱水的孩子？

Why can oral rehydration salts save a child dehydrated by diarrhoea?

关键在于小肠上皮的葡萄糖-钠共转运机制：即使在腹泻时，葡萄糖与钠一起被吸收仍能带动水分进入体内，这一发现被称为20世纪最重要的医学进展之一。世界卫生组织推荐的低渗配方每升约含钠75 mmol和葡萄糖75 mmol，比高糖饮料更有效且不会因渗透压过高加重腹泻。含糖饮料、运动饮料和纯水都不能替代补液盐；出现无尿、嗜睡、眼窝凹陷或抽搐等重度脱水表现须立即就医。
The key is sodium-glucose co-transport in the small-intestinal epithelium: even during diarrhoea, absorbing glucose together with sodium still drags water into the body - a discovery ranked among the most important medical advances of the twentieth century. The WHO-recommended low-osmolarity formula contains about 75 mmol of sodium and 75 mmol of glucose per litre, which works better than sugary drinks and will not worsen diarrhoea through excess osmolality. Soft drinks, sports drinks and plain water are not substitutes, and signs of severe dehydration such as no urine output, lethargy, sunken eyes or convulsions require immediate care.

儿童生长曲线该怎么看？

How should a child's growth chart be read?

生长曲线把孩子的身高、体重、头围按年龄和性别与参考人群比较，用百分位或Z分数表示。关键不是落在第25还是第75百分位，而是长期趋势是否沿着自己的曲线稳定上升；短期波动、遗传身高偏低都可能是正常的。若曲线明显向下跨越两条主要百分位线、体重长期不增或身高增速骤降，则提示需要评估营养、慢性疾病或内分泌问题。世界卫生组织的0～5岁生长标准描述的是母乳喂养儿童在良好条件下应有的生长模式。
A growth chart compares a child's height, weight and head circumference with a reference population by age and sex, expressed as percentiles or z-scores. What matters is not whether the child sits at the 25th or the 75th percentile but whether the long-term trend rises steadily along their own curve; short-term wobbles and familial short stature can be entirely normal. Crossing downwards through two major percentile lines, prolonged failure to gain weight or a sudden drop in height velocity signals a need to assess nutrition, chronic illness or endocrine problems. The WHO standards for ages 0-5 describe how breastfed children grow under favourable conditions.

为什么医生反复强调让婴儿仰卧睡觉？

Why do doctors insist that babies sleep on their backs?

大量流行病学证据显示，俯卧或侧卧睡眠会显著增加婴儿猝死综合征的风险，可能与二氧化碳重复吸入、觉醒反应减弱和体温过高有关。自20世纪90年代各国推行“仰睡”运动后，多国婴儿猝死率下降了一半以上。安全睡眠还包括：睡在平整坚实的床垫上，婴儿床内不放松软枕头、毛绒玩具和厚被，与父母同房不同床，避免烟草暴露和过度包裹。
Strong epidemiological evidence shows that prone or side sleeping markedly raises the risk of sudden infant death syndrome, probably through rebreathing carbon dioxide, blunted arousal responses and overheating. Since national “back to sleep” campaigns began in the 1990s, infant death rates from SIDS have fallen by more than half in many countries. Safe sleep also means a firm flat mattress, no soft pillows, plush toys or heavy quilts in the cot, room-sharing but not bed-sharing with parents, and avoiding tobacco exposure and overheating.
