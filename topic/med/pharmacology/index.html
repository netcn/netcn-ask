为什么葡萄柚会影响某些药物？

Why can grapefruit affect some medicines?

葡萄柚中的成分会抑制肠道和肝脏的CYP3A4酶，使部分药物（如某些他汀、降压药）在血中浓度异常升高。说明书提示“避免柚类”时就别吃。
Grapefruit blocks the CYP3A4 enzyme in gut and liver, raising blood levels of some drugs (certain statins, blood-pressure meds). If the label says avoid grapefruit, don't eat it.

靶向药和化疗的作用方式有什么本质区别？

What is the essential difference between targeted therapy and chemotherapy?

化疗药作用于所有分裂活跃的细胞，正常增殖组织（如毛囊、骨髓、肠黏膜）也难免受伤，所以有脱发、骨髓抑制和恶心等副作用。靶向药则针对癌细胞特有的分子异常，如特异的突变蛋白或受体——伊马替尼抑制BCR-ABL融合蛋白治疗慢性髓性白血病就是经典例子。因此靶向药选择性更强、毒性更小，但患者需要先做基因检测确认存在相应靶点。
Chemotherapy attacks all rapidly dividing cells, so healthy tissues that divide often — hair follicles, bone marrow, and gut lining — suffer collateral damage, causing hair loss, marrow suppression, and nausea. Targeted drugs instead block cancer-specific molecular abnormalities such as mutant proteins or receptors; imatinib, which inhibits the BCR-ABL fusion protein in chronic myeloid leukemia, is a classic example. Targeted drugs are therefore more selective and less toxic, but patients must first be tested to confirm the matching target.

口服药吃下去后在体内经历了什么？

What happens to an oral drug after you swallow it?

药物在体内的旅程可概括为ADME：吸收、分布、代谢、排泄。经肠道吸收的药物先通过门静脉进入肝脏，被CYP450酶系部分转化，首次通过就被代谢掉一部分，这叫"首过效应"。吸收后药物随血液循环分布到组织并到达靶点，血浆浓度随时间按"半衰期"衰减，半衰期决定了给药间隔。
A drug's journey is often summarized as ADME: absorption, distribution, metabolism, and excretion. Drugs absorbed from the gut first pass through the portal vein into the liver, where CYP450 enzymes transform a portion before it even reaches circulation — the first-pass effect. After absorption the drug distributes through the bloodstream to tissues and its target, and plasma levels then decay according to the drug's half-life, which determines the dosing interval.

药物在肝脏中是如何被代谢的？

How are drugs metabolized in the liver?

肝脏中的酶（尤其是细胞色素P450家族）会把药物化学修饰，使其更易溶于水以便经尿液或胆汁排出。这一过程的快慢因人而异，影响药效和毒性。
Enzymes in the liver, especially the cytochrome P450 family, chemically modify drugs so they become more water-soluble and can be excreted in urine or bile. The speed of this process varies between people, affecting efficacy and toxicity.

mRNA疫苗是怎么"训练"免疫系统的？

How does an mRNA vaccine train the immune system?

mRNA被包进脂质纳米粒，注射后被肌细胞摄取，细胞利用它翻译出病毒抗原蛋白（如刺突蛋白），经MHC分子在细胞表面展示。免疫系统把该蛋白当作异物，激活辅助T细胞并诱导出中和抗体和细胞毒性T细胞应答，同时留下记忆细胞。mRNA在细胞内很快降解，不会进入细胞核，所以不能改变基因组；常见反应多为局部酸痛和短期发热，属于正常免疫应答。
The mRNA is packaged in a lipid nanoparticle, taken up by muscle cells, and translated into the viral antigen (such as the spike protein), which is presented via MHC molecules on the cell surface. The immune system treats it as foreign, activating helper T cells and generating neutralizing antibody and cytotoxic T-cell responses while leaving behind memory cells. The mRNA degrades quickly inside cells and never enters the nucleus, so it cannot alter your genome; common reactions such as a sore arm or short-lived fever are normal immune responses.

什么是“治疗窗窄”的药物，为什么要监测血药浓度？

What are narrow therapeutic index drugs, and why is blood level monitoring needed?

治疗窗指有效浓度与中毒浓度之间的区间。华法林、地高辛、锂盐、苯妥英钠、茶碱和氨基糖苷类抗生素的这一区间很窄，剂量或代谢的小幅变化就可能从无效跳到中毒。因此临床需要治疗药物监测：华法林查INR，地高辛和锂盐测血药浓度，氨基糖苷类按峰谷值调整。患者应固定服药时间、不擅自换厂家或改剂量，并主动告知医生所有正在使用的药物和保健品。
The therapeutic window is the gap between effective and toxic concentrations. For warfarin, digoxin, lithium, phenytoin, theophylline and aminoglycoside antibiotics that gap is narrow, so a small change in dose or metabolism can swing the effect from useless to toxic. Clinical practice therefore relies on therapeutic drug monitoring: INR for warfarin, serum levels for digoxin and lithium, peak and trough values to adjust aminoglycosides. Patients should keep dosing times consistent, not switch brands or doses on their own, and tell their doctor about every drug and supplement they take.

纳洛酮为什么能救回阿片类药物过量的人？

Why can naloxone reverse an opioid overdose?

阿片类药物过量最危险的后果是作用于脑干μ受体导致呼吸抑制。纳洛酮是μ受体的竞争性拮抗剂，亲和力高，能把阿片类分子从受体上“挤下来”，静脉或鼻喷给药后数分钟内恢复呼吸。但它的半衰期比多数阿片类短，患者可能在药效消退后再次陷入呼吸抑制，因此使用后必须立即呼叫急救并持续观察，可能需要重复给药。许多国家已允许社区人群配备鼻喷纳洛酮以救助他人。
The most dangerous consequence of opioid overdose is respiratory depression from action on brainstem mu receptors. Naloxone is a high-affinity competitive antagonist at those receptors, physically displacing the opioid, and restores breathing within minutes when given intravenously or as a nasal spray. Its half-life is shorter than that of most opioids, however, so the patient can slip back into respiratory depression as it wears off - emergency services must be called immediately, monitoring continued and repeat doses may be needed. Many countries now let community members carry nasal naloxone to help others.

β受体阻滞剂这类药物为什么能改善心力衰竭的预后？

Why can beta-blockers improve outcomes in heart failure?

在慢性心衰中，交感神经长期过度兴奋会令心肌持续高负荷并促发重塑；β受体阻滞剂阻断肾上腺素能刺激，降低心率和耗氧，长期可逆转部分心室重构。MERIT-HF、CIBIS II 等大型试验证明，在标准治疗上加用美托洛尔或比索洛尔能显著降低死亡率。
In chronic heart failure, sustained sympathetic over-activation overloads the myocardium and drives remodeling. Beta-blockers blunt adrenergic stimulation, lowering heart rate and oxygen demand, and over time partially reverse ventricular remodeling. Trials such as MERIT-HF and CIBIS II showed that adding metoprolol or bisoprolol to standard therapy cuts mortality.

基因治疗是怎样实现的？目前用在哪些病？

How does gene therapy work, and what diseases does it treat?

基因治疗用经过改造的无害病毒载体（如AAV、慢病毒）把功能正常的基因送入患者细胞，补偿或纠正致病突变；或直接递送基因编辑工具（如CRISPR）在体内定点修复基因。已获批的治疗覆盖遗传性视网膜病、脊髓性肌萎缩（SMA）、β-地中海贫血等单基因病。挑战在于递送效率、长期表达稳定性和免疫反应，更多适应症正在临床试验中。
Gene therapy uses engineered harmless viral vectors—such as AAV and lentiviruses—to deliver a functional gene into the patient's cells to compensate for or correct a disease-causing mutation, or it delivers gene-editing tools like CRISPR to repair the gene in place. Approved treatments cover single-gene diseases such as inherited retinal disease, spinal muscular atrophy (SMA), and beta-thalassemia. Challenges include delivery efficiency, long-term expression stability, and immune responses, and many more indications are in clinical trials.

什么是药物的“首过效应”？

What is the first-pass effect of a drug?

口服药物在小肠吸收后先经门静脉进入肝脏，部分药物在到达全身循环之前就被肝酶大量代谢掉，这就是首过效应，它会显著降低生物利用度。硝酸甘油、利多卡因、普萘洛尔等药物首过消除很强，因此硝酸甘油需舌下含服、利多卡因用于抗心律失常时须静脉给药。静脉注射、舌下、经皮和部分直肠给药都能不同程度绕过首过代谢，这也是同一药物口服与注射剂量常常相差很大的原因。
An orally taken drug is absorbed in the small intestine and passes first through the portal vein into the liver, where hepatic enzymes may metabolize much of it before it ever reaches the systemic circulation - the first-pass effect, which sharply lowers bioavailability. Nitroglycerin, lidocaine and propranolol undergo heavy first-pass elimination, which is why nitroglycerin is given under the tongue and lidocaine intravenously when used for arrhythmias. Intravenous, sublingual, transdermal and partly rectal routes bypass this metabolism to varying degrees, explaining why oral and injected doses of the same drug often differ greatly.

常见止痛药大致分三类：对乙酰氨基酚类主要在中枢水平减轻痛觉信号传递；非甾体抗炎药（阿司匹林、布洛芬等）抑制环氧化酶COX，减少致痛致炎的前列腺素合成；阿片类（吗啡、曲马多等）作用于脑与脊髓阿片受体阻断痛觉传入。它们都缓解症状而非治因，应遵医嘱或按说明使用。
Common painkillers fall into three broad groups: acetaminophen-type agents, which act mainly at the central level to dampen pain-signal transmission; NSAIDs such as aspirin and ibuprofen, which block cyclooxygenase (COX) and cut the synthesis of prostaglandins that drive inflammation and pain; and opioids such as morphine and tramadol, which act on opioid receptors in the brain and spinal cord to block pain input. All relieve symptoms rather than treating the cause; use as directed by a doctor or the label.

为什么有些人喝一点酒就脸红？

Why do some people flush after just a little alcohol?

酒精先被乙醇脱氢酶转化为乙醛，再由乙醛脱氢酶2（ALDH2）分解为乙酸。东亚人群中相当比例携带活性很低的ALDH2变异型，乙醛迅速堆积，引起面部潮红、心跳加快、头痛和恶心。乙醛被国际癌症研究机构列为1类致癌物，因此这类人若长期饮酒，食管癌风险比一般人高出数倍。脸红不是“酒量能练出来”的表现，而是身体发出的代谢警告。
Alcohol is first converted to acetaldehyde by alcohol dehydrogenase, then broken down to acetate by aldehyde dehydrogenase 2 (ALDH2). A substantial share of East Asian people carry a low-activity ALDH2 variant, so acetaldehyde builds up quickly and causes facial flushing, a racing heart, headache and nausea. Acetaldehyde is classified as a Group 1 carcinogen by the International Agency for Research on Cancer, so flushers who drink regularly face a several-fold higher risk of oesophageal cancer. Flushing is not a sign that tolerance can be trained up - it is a metabolic warning.

儿童铅暴露为什么特别危险？

Why is lead exposure especially dangerous for children?

铅会干扰神经系统发育，影响神经元迁移、突触形成和髓鞘化，造成认知能力下降、注意力和行为问题，这些损害往往不可逆。儿童单位体重摄入更多、肠道对铅的吸收率远高于成人，加上爬行和把手放入口中的行为，暴露风险更大。世界卫生组织明确指出目前尚未发现“安全”的血铅水平。常见来源包括含铅涂料与粉尘、受污染土壤、非正规蓄电池回收、某些劣质化妆品与调味料以及老旧含铅管道。
Lead disrupts nervous system development, interfering with neuronal migration, synapse formation and myelination, and the resulting loss of cognitive ability and problems with attention and behaviour are often irreversible. Children take in more per kilogram of body weight and absorb lead from the gut far more efficiently than adults, and crawling and mouthing objects add to exposure. WHO states plainly that no safe blood lead level has been identified. Common sources include leaded paint and dust, contaminated soil, informal battery recycling, some low-quality cosmetics and spices, and old lead plumbing.

药为什么分饭前和饭后吃？

Why are some medicines taken before and others after meals?

有些药需要空腹好吸收（如某些抗生素），有些药对胃有刺激需要饭后吃以减少不适，所以要按说明服用。
Some medicines absorb better on an empty stomach (like certain antibiotics), while others irritate the stomach and are taken after meals; follow the instructions.
