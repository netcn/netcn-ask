詹纳的牛痘接种为什么被视为疫苗学的开端？

Why is Jenner's cowpox inoculation seen as the birth of vaccinology?

1796年，英国医生爱德华·詹纳注意到挤奶女工感染牛痘后不再得天花，他从牛痘脓液取材接种给男孩詹姆斯·菲普斯，随后接触天花物质而未发病，首次用可控实验证明了交叉免疫保护。“疫苗”（vaccine）一词就来自拉丁语vacca（牛）。此前虽有人痘接种，但詹纳的方法更安全、可推广，最终推动世界卫生组织领导的全球计划在1980年正式宣布天花根除——迄今唯一被消灭的人类传染病。
In 1796 the English physician Edward Jenner, noting that milkmaids who caught cowpox never developed smallpox, inoculated material from a cowpox lesion into a boy named James Phipps, who then resisted exposure to smallpox - the first controlled demonstration of cross-protective immunity. The word vaccine comes from the Latin vacca, meaning cow. Earlier variolation with human smallpox existed, but Jenner's method was safer and scalable, ultimately enabling the WHO-led global campaign that declared smallpox eradicated in 1980, still the only human infectious disease ever eliminated.

胰岛素是怎样被发现的？

How was insulin discovered?

1921年至1922年，在多伦多大学，班廷与贝斯特在麦克劳德的实验室提取胰腺内分泌物质，科利普进一步提纯，使其可安全用于人体。1922年1月，14岁的1型糖尿病男孩伦纳德·汤普森接受注射后血糖显著下降，原本必死的疾病第一次有了治疗方法。班廷与麦克劳德因此获得1923年诺贝尔生理学或医学奖，发现者以象征性的1美元转让专利，希望胰岛素尽快普及；今天可及性和价格仍是全球关注的公共卫生议题。
Between 1921 and 1922 at the University of Toronto, Banting and Best extracted the pancreatic secretion in Macleod's laboratory, and Collip purified it enough for safe human use. In January 1922, fourteen-year-old Leonard Thompson, dying of type 1 diabetes, received an injection and his blood glucose fell markedly - the first treatment for a previously fatal disease. Banting and Macleod received the 1923 Nobel Prize in Physiology or Medicine, and the discoverers assigned the patent for a symbolic one dollar so insulin could spread quickly; access and price nonetheless remain live global health issues today.

塞麦尔维斯为什么被称为“手卫生之父”？

Why is Semmelweis called the father of hand hygiene?

1847年，在维也纳总医院工作的伊格纳兹·塞麦尔维斯发现，由医生和医学生接产的产房，产妇因产褥热死亡的比例远高于助产士负责的产房；他推断原因是医生解剖尸体后未清洗双手带来了“尸体颗粒”。他要求用含氯石灰水洗手，产褥热死亡率随之大幅下降。当时细菌学说尚未建立，他的主张遭到同行排斥，直到巴斯德和李斯特之后才获公认。今天世界卫生组织把每年5月5日定为世界手卫生日，正是这一发现的延续。
In 1847 Ignaz Semmelweis, working at the Vienna General Hospital, found that mothers delivered by physicians and medical students died of puerperal fever far more often than those attended by midwives, and inferred that doctors were carrying “cadaverous particles” on unwashed hands from the autopsy room. He required handwashing in chlorinated lime solution, and deaths from puerperal fever fell dramatically. Germ theory did not yet exist, so his colleagues rejected the idea, and vindication came only after Pasteur and Lister. WHO's designation of 5 May as World Hand Hygiene Day carries that discovery forward.
