维生素B12为什么必须靠“内因子”才能吸收？

Why does vitamin B12 absorption require intrinsic factor?

B12分子较大且不稳定，需要胃壁细胞分泌的内因子与它结合，形成的复合物才能被回肠末端的特定受体识别并主动吸收。因此萎缩性胃炎、胃部手术、自身免疫破坏内因子（恶性贫血）或回肠病变都会导致缺乏，长期使用抑酸药和二甲双胍也会减少吸收。缺乏表现为大细胞性贫血、舌炎和四肢麻木等神经损害，神经症状若延误治疗可能不可逆，需按病因选择注射或大剂量口服补充。
The B12 molecule is large and fragile, so it must bind intrinsic factor secreted by gastric parietal cells, and only that complex is recognized and actively absorbed by specific receptors in the terminal ileum. Atrophic gastritis, gastric surgery, autoimmune destruction of intrinsic factor (pernicious anaemia) and ileal disease therefore cause deficiency, while long-term acid suppressants and metformin also reduce absorption. Deficiency shows up as macrocytic anaemia, a sore tongue and neurological damage such as numb limbs; neurological signs may become irreversible if treatment is delayed, so replacement is given by injection or in high oral doses depending on the cause.

What is dehydration and how does the body signal it?

为什么血液中同型半胱氨酸升高与叶酸、维生素B6和B12缺乏有关，并增加心脑血管病风险？

Why does elevated homocysteine link folate, B6 and B12 deficiency to higher cardiovascular risk?

同型半胱氨酸的再甲基化与转硫通路分别依赖叶酸（维生素B12为辅酶）和维生素B6，任一营养素缺乏都会使其代谢受阻而在血中堆积。观察性研究显示高同型半胱氨酸与动脉粥样硬化、静脉血栓风险相关，但降低它的随机试验并未一致减少心血管事件，提示它可能是危险标志而非直接因果。临床对反复血栓或早发动脉硬化者会检测并补充相应B族维生素。
Remethylation and transsulfuration of homocysteine depend on folate (with B12 as cofactor) and on B6, so a lack of any of them lets homocysteine accumulate. Observational studies tie high homocysteine to atherosclerosis and venous thrombosis, yet trials that lower it have not consistently cut cardiovascular events, suggesting it may be a marker rather than a direct cause. Clinics do test and replete B vitamins in people with recurrent clots or early atherosclerosis.

给长期饥饿或严重营养不良的患者重新启动营养支持时，为什么要在最初数日警惕低磷血症和心律失常？

When nutritional support is restarted in a severely malnourished patient, why must hypophosphatemia and arrhythmia be watched for closely during the first few days?

饥饿状态下胰岛素分泌受抑，体内总磷储备逐渐耗竭；重新供能后血糖刺激胰岛素骤增，驱动磷酸盐、钾、镁快速转入细胞内，血清磷随之骤降。低磷抑制ATP合成与膈肌功能，可诱发心律失常、呼吸衰竭甚至猝死。预防要点为低热量起始（约10-15 kcal/kg/天）、缓慢加量，并经验性补充磷、钾、镁和维生素B1。
During starvation, insulin secretion is suppressed and total-body phosphate stores become depleted. When calories restart, rising insulin drives phosphate, potassium and magnesium into cells and serum phosphate falls abruptly. Hypophosphatemia impairs ATP synthesis and diaphragmatic function, predisposing to arrhythmia, respiratory failure and sudden death. Prevention is to begin feeding at low calorie targets (roughly 10-15 kcal/kg/day), advance slowly, and replace phosphate, potassium, magnesium and thiamine empirically.

膳食纤维对健康有什么好处？

What are the health benefits of dietary fiber?

膳食纤维分为可溶性和不可溶性两类。可溶性纤维（如燕麦、豆类）在肠道形成凝胶，帮助降低胆固醇、平稳血糖；不可溶性纤维（如蔬菜、全谷物）增加粪便体积、促进排便。纤维还被肠道菌群发酵产生短链脂肪酸，有益肠道健康。大多数成年人摄入不足，多吃全谷物、蔬果和豆类是简单有效的方法。
Fiber comes in soluble and insoluble forms. Soluble fiber, in oats and beans, forms a gel that lowers cholesterol and steadies blood glucose; insoluble fiber, in vegetables and whole grains, adds bulk and prevents constipation. Fermenting fiber also feeds gut bacteria, producing beneficial short-chain fatty acids. Most adults fall short, so more whole grains, vegetables, and legumes is a simple fix.

糖摄入过多有什么危害？

What are the harms of too much sugar?

问题在于长期的量，不是偶然一口。添加糖热量高却几乎不提供其他营养，含糖饮料最容易让能量超标，多余部分转成脂肪，体重上升又加重胰岛素抵抗，2型糖尿病风险随之上升。牙菌斑吃糖产酸，酸反复冲刷牙面，溶蚀釉质就成了龋洞，所以吃的次数比一次吃多少更要紧。果糖主要经肝脏代谢，长期过量会推动脂肪在肝内堆积，这是脂肪肝的一条来路。
The trouble is the amount sustained over time, not a single accidental sweet. Added sugar is energy-dense yet supplies little else, and sugary drinks are the easiest way to overshoot the day's energy, with the surplus stored as fat, the extra weight in turn worsening insulin resistance and raising the risk of type 2 diabetes. Is an apple a day really sound advice — why does nutrition research keep flip-flopping?

苹果本身无害且有益,但营养学的反复源于方法论困境:随机对照试验让人长期按指定饮食生活几乎不可能,主流研究只能靠问卷回忆吃过的食物——记忆偏差巨大;而且吃大量蔬菜的人往往同时运动、不吸烟、收入更高(健康使用者偏倚),混淆因素难以剥离。这就是为什么观察研究说咖啡有害又有益、鸡蛋起起落落。相对可靠的是硬终点随机试验与机制研究。共识底线反而稳定:多吃完整蔬果与全谷、少超加工食品、控制总量——复杂的是头条,不是盘子。
Apples are fine, but nutrition whiplash stems from method: randomizing people to fixed diets for years is nearly impossible, so research leans on food-frequency questionnaires with huge recall bias; meanwhile vegetable-lovers also exercise, smoke less and earn more — healthy-user bias confounds everything. Hence coffee and eggs flip-flopping in headlines. Randomized trials with hard endpoints and mechanistic work are steadier. The stable floor remains boringly simple: whole vegetables, fruits and grains, fewer ultraprocessed foods, sensible totals — headlines are complicated, plates need not be.
