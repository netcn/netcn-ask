医疗AI为什么强调可解释性和前瞻性验证？

Why do clinical AI systems need explainability and prospective validation?

回顾性数据上的高准确率不足以证明临床价值：真实工作流中的图像质量、人机交互方式和医生对提示的依从度都会改变最终结果，因此需要前瞻性研究乃至随机对照试验，检验它对诊断准确率、报告时效和患者结局的实际影响。可解释性手段如热力图和特征归因，帮助医生识别模型是否依赖了不合理线索，并在出错时形成可追溯的责任链条。监管机构同时要求算法更新的变更管理和上线后的持续性能监测。
High accuracy on retrospective data does not prove clinical value, because image quality, how clinicians interact with the tool and whether they follow its prompts all change results in a real workflow; prospective studies and ideally randomised trials are needed to measure effects on diagnostic accuracy, turnaround time and patient outcomes. Explainability methods such as saliency maps and feature attribution help clinicians see whether the model relied on implausible cues and create a traceable chain of accountability when it errs. Regulators also require change management for algorithm updates and ongoing performance monitoring after deployment.

为什么医学AI模型换一家医院表现就明显下降？

Why does a medical AI model often perform much worse at a different hospital?

模型可能学到与疾病无关的「捷径特征」，例如设备型号、摄片体位、图像后处理风格甚至胶片上的标记，这些混杂因素在训练医院恰好与结局相关，换到数据分布不同的机构后失效。此外人群疾病谱、患病率和标注标准的差异也会改变阳性预测值，即使灵敏度特异度不变，实际可用性也会变差。因此多中心外部独立验证、数据分布监测和上线后的持续性能追踪是医疗AI落地的必要环节。
Models can latch onto shortcut features unrelated to disease, such as scanner model, patient positioning, post-processing style or even markers burned into the image, which happened to correlate with outcomes at the training site and stop working where the data distribution differs. Differences in case mix, prevalence and labelling standards also change positive predictive value, so a tool can become far less useful even when sensitivity and specificity look unchanged. Multi-centre external validation, monitoring of data drift and continuous post-deployment performance tracking are therefore essential.

远程医疗适合处理哪些问题？哪些情况必须去医院？

Which problems suit telemedicine, and when must you go to hospital in person?

远程医疗较适合慢病随访与用药调整、检查结果解读、皮肤和心理等以问诊或图像为主的评估，以及偏远地区的专科会诊，可减少交通与等候成本。需要体格检查、影像与化验、急救处置或病情不稳定时必须现场就诊，例如胸痛、呼吸困难、意识改变、抽搐、外伤大出血或高热伴精神差。一般建议把远程咨询作为分诊、随访和健康管理的补充，而不是替代所有面诊。
Telemedicine works well for chronic disease follow-up and medication adjustment, explaining test results, largely history- or image-based assessment such as dermatology and mental health, and specialist consultation for remote areas, cutting travel and waiting costs. In-person care is required when physical examination, imaging or laboratory tests, emergency treatment are needed or the patient is unstable, for example chest pain, breathlessness, altered consciousness, seizures, major trauma bleeding or high fever with lethargy. It is generally advised to use remote consultation as a complement for triage, follow-up and health management rather than a substitute for all visits.

糖尿病视网膜病变为什么成为AI筛查最成功的场景之一？

Why has diabetic retinopathy become one of the most successful uses of AI screening?

这类筛查任务边界清晰、图像标准化程度高，只需根据眼底照片判断分级和是否需要转诊，而全球需要定期筛查的糖尿病患者数量远超眼科医师的服务能力。深度学习算法在大规模数据集上达到了与眼科专家相当的灵敏度和特异度，已在部分国家进入初级卫生保健筛查流程。实际落地仍需关注图像质量控制、在不同族群中的验证以及筛查阳性后的转诊与治疗衔接。
The task is well defined and the images are highly standardised: a fundus photograph is graded to decide whether referral is needed, while the number of people with diabetes needing regular screening far exceeds available ophthalmologists. Deep learning algorithms have reached sensitivity and specificity comparable to specialists on large datasets and have entered primary care screening pathways in some countries. Real-world use still depends on image quality control, validation across different populations, and reliable referral and treatment after a positive screen.

数字疗法和普通健康类App有什么不同？

How do digital therapeutics differ from ordinary health apps?

数字疗法以软件本身作为干预手段，针对特定疾病提供治疗或疾病管理方案，需要临床试验证据并通过医疗器械或药品监管审批，例如用于失眠的认知行为治疗程序、成瘾行为干预和慢病管理系统。普通健康App多为记录、提醒和信息展示工具，不作治疗声称，也不受同等强度的监管。选择时应关注产品的适应症范围、证据强度以及是否需要医生处方或配合随访。
Digital therapeutics use the software itself as the intervention, delivering treatment or disease management for a specific condition, and they require clinical trial evidence and regulatory clearance as a medical device or therapeutic product; examples include cognitive behavioural programmes for insomnia, addiction interventions and chronic disease management systems. Ordinary health apps mostly log data, send reminders and present information without making therapeutic claims, and face far lighter regulation. When choosing, it is worth checking the approved indication, the strength of evidence, and whether a prescription or clinical follow-up is required.

医学影像AI是怎么被「训练」出来的？

How is a medical imaging AI model actually trained?

研究者先收集大量带标注的影像，标签通常来自放射科医师阅片、病理结果或临床随访结局，再用卷积神经网络等模型在训练集上学习图像特征与标签之间的映射关系，并用独立验证集调参、测试集评估。评价指标一般包括灵敏度、特异度和ROC曲线下面积，并与医师读片水平比较。数据的代表性和标注质量直接决定模型上限，这也是医学影像AI最核心的瓶颈。
Researchers first assemble large sets of annotated images, with labels from radiologist reading, pathology results or clinical follow-up outcomes, then train models such as convolutional neural networks to map image features to labels, tuning on a separate validation set and reporting performance on a held-out test set. Typical metrics are sensitivity, specificity and area under the ROC curve, often benchmarked against clinicians. How representative the data are and how accurate the labels are set the ceiling on performance, which is the central bottleneck in medical imaging AI.

用智能手表大规模筛查房颤，为什么不一定改善健康结局？

Why might large-scale smartwatch screening for atrial fibrillation not improve health outcomes?

大型前瞻研究显示，可穿戴设备的不规则脉搏提示确实能发现部分未被诊断的房颤，但收到提示的人中经心电贴片确认者比例有限，存在较多假阳性和随之而来的检查负担与焦虑。更关键的是，筛查发现的多为负荷较低的短阵房颤，其抗凝治疗能否减少卒中尚未在随机试验中证实。一般建议把设备提示视为就医评估的线索，由医生结合心电图和卒中风险评分决定后续处理。
Large prospective studies show that irregular-pulse notifications from wearables do identify some previously undiagnosed atrial fibrillation, but only a minority of alerts are confirmed on subsequent ECG patch monitoring, leaving many false positives with attendant testing burden and anxiety. More importantly, screen-detected episodes are often short and low-burden, and randomised trials have not established that anticoagulating them reduces stroke. It is generally advised to treat a device alert as a reason for medical assessment, letting a clinician decide next steps using ECG findings and stroke risk scores.

没有神经科医生的基层医院,如何借助远程卒中网络让患者仍能及时接受溶栓?

How can telestroke networks let patients at small hospitals without neurologists still receive timely thrombolysis?

靠中心医院专家的实时远程会诊把卒中决策送到基层床旁。基层完成头颅CT并上传后,中心端通过视频查体和影像共享判断有无出血、核对适应证与禁忌证,几分钟内明确能否静脉溶栓,基层团队立即就地给药。这种轮辐式网络填补了基层专科空白,免去长途转运延误,让更多患者在发病4.5小时时间窗内用上重组组织型纤溶酶原激活剂,预后显著改善。
Real-time remote consultation brings stroke expertise from a hub hospital to the bedside at spoke clinics. After the local team uploads a head CT, hub physicians assess the patient over video and review shared images to exclude hemorrhage, weigh indications against contraindications, and decide within minutes whether intravenous thrombolysis is justified; the spoke team then treats immediately. Such hub-and-spoke networks close the specialist gap, avoid transfer delays, and put many more patients on tissue plasminogen activator inside the 4.5-hour window with clearly better outcomes.
