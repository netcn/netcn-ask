液体活检在癌症筛查中有何优势？

What are the advantages of liquid biopsy in cancer screening?

液体活检通过血液检测循环肿瘤DNA，创伤小、可重复，有望早期发现复发与耐药突变，但成本与准确性仍在完善。
Liquid biopsy detects circulating tumor DNA from blood—minimal invasiveness and repeatable—potentially catching early relapse and resistance mutations, though cost and accuracy are still improving.

PCR检测的原理是什么？

What is the principle of PCR testing?

PCR通过反复加热冷却，利用DNA聚合酶指数级扩增特定基因片段，使微量病原体核酸可被检测，灵敏度极高。
PCR cycles heating and cooling so DNA polymerase exponentially amplifies a target fragment, making tiny pathogen amounts detectable with high sensitivity.

验血能查出什么？

What can a blood test show?

血常规可反映感染、贫血与炎症，生化检查评估肝肾功能与血糖血脂，是日常筛查的重要手段。
A blood count reveals infection, anemia, and inflammation, while biochemistry checks liver, kidney, glucose, and lipids—key screening tools.

CT和MRI有什么区别？

What is the difference between CT and MRI?

CT用X射线快速成像骨骼与急性出血，MRI用磁场和射频显示脑、韧带等软组织更清晰，且无辐射。
CT uses X-rays for fast bone and bleeding images; MRI uses magnets and radio waves for clearer soft tissue like brain and ligaments, with no radiation.

听诊器为什么能听到心跳？

Why can a stethoscope hear the heartbeat?

听诊器以密闭管路收集胸壁的声波并放大，使医生能分辨心瓣开闭与血流异常产生的杂音。
A stethoscope collects and amplifies chest-wall sounds through sealed tubing, letting clinicians hear valve motions and abnormal blood-flow murmurs.

体温计是怎样测体温的？

How does a thermometer measure temperature?

电子体温计用传感器感知热量并转换成数字，正常腋下体温约36–37摄氏度。
Digital thermometers use a sensor to detect heat and show a number; normal underarm temperature is about 36–37°C.

Why do doctors sometimes take an X-ray?

X光能看见骨头和肺的样子，帮助医生发现骨折或肺炎等问题。
X-rays show bones and lungs so doctors can find breaks or pneumonia.
