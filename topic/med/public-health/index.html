HIV感染今天能控制住吗？

Can HIV infection be controlled today?

可以。HIV会逆转录并整合进CD4+T细胞基因组，单靠免疫系统无法清除，但抗逆转录病毒治疗（ART）通过联合多种药物在逆转录、整合、蛋白成熟等不同环节阻断病毒复制。规律服药后病毒载量可降至检测不到，CD4细胞逐渐恢复。达到“检测不到”即不具备传染性（U=U），感染者可以长期健康生活，并阻断母婴和性传播。
Yes. HIV reverse-transcribes and integrates into the CD4+ T-cell genome, so the immune system alone cannot clear it, but antiretroviral therapy (ART) blocks viral replication at multiple steps—reverse transcription, integration, and protein maturation. With consistent treatment, viral load falls to undetectable and CD4 cells recover. Undetectable means untransmittable (U=U): people with HIV can live long, healthy lives and do not transmit the virus sexually or to their babies.

为什么烟草使用仍被称为全球第一大可预防的死因？

Why is tobacco still called the world's leading preventable cause of death?

全球每年约800万人死于烟草相关疾患，吸烟是肺癌、慢阻肺、冠心病和卒中的共同首要危险因素，二手烟还危害不吸烟者。GBD研究显示烟草导致的伤残调整寿命年（DALY）在所有危险因素中名列前茅。正因为其危害广、可明确预防，控烟被公认为性价比最高的公共卫生干预之一。
About 8 million people die each year from tobacco-related disease; smoking is the top shared risk for lung cancer, COPD, coronary disease and stroke, and secondhand smoke harms non-smokers. The GBD study ranks tobacco among the leading causes of disability-adjusted life years. Because the harm is vast yet clearly preventable, tobacco control is among the most cost-effective public-health actions.

疫苗覆盖率的阈值如何计算？

How is the vaccine coverage threshold calculated?

群体免疫阈值近似为 1 减 1 除以 R0，R0越高所需覆盖率越高。该简化公式未考虑人口混合不均和疫苗效力。实际规划需结合本地流行病学数据。
The herd immunity threshold is roughly 1 minus 1 over R0; higher R0 needs higher coverage. This simplified formula ignores uneven mixing and vaccine efficacy. Real planning uses local epidemiological data.

什么是耐多药结核（MDR-TB）？

What is multidrug-resistant tuberculosis (MDR-TB)?

MDR-TB指结核菌同时对最有效的两种一线药物——异烟肼和利福平耐药，主要由治疗不规范（擅自停药、单药暴露）或直接感染耐药株造成。治疗需改用二线药物、疗程长达18个月以上，副作用更多、治愈率更低、费用更高。规范完成全程督导治疗是预防耐药的关键，而耐药菌株的传播会进一步加重负担。
MDR-TB means the bacteria are resistant to both isoniazid and rifampicin—the two most effective first-line drugs—arising mainly from irregular treatment (stopping early, single-drug exposure) or infection with a resistant strain. Treatment requires second-line drugs for 18 months or more, with more side effects, lower cure rates, and higher costs. Completing the full supervised course is the key to preventing resistance, while transmission of resistant strains worsens the burden further.

为什么有些疫苗要打加强针？

Why do some vaccines require booster doses?

初次接种后机体形成记忆B细胞和记忆T细胞，但灭活类疫苗诱生的抗体滴度会随时间衰减。加强针相当于一次“模拟再感染”，再次激活记忆细胞，使抗体在短期内快速回升，并推动生发中心反应实现亲和力成熟，同时扩增长寿命骨髓浆细胞，把保护期明显拉长。这正是“免疫记忆”的临床运用——与人体再次接触病原体时的反应原理相同。
Initial vaccination generates memory B and T cells, but antibody titers from inactivated-type vaccines wane over time. A booster is like a simulated re-exposure: it re-activates memory cells, quickly raising antibody levels, drives germinal-center reactions for affinity maturation, and expands long-lived bone-marrow plasma cells to greatly extend protection. This is the clinical application of immunological memory—the same principle as the body's response to a real pathogen re-encounter.

为什么第二次感染登革热可能更危险？

Why can a second dengue infection be more dangerous?

登革病毒有4种血清型。首次感染产生的抗体对同型有中和作用，但针对其它型别的亚中和水平抗体反而会引发“抗体依赖性增强”（ADE）：抗体-病毒复合物更容易被巨噬细胞等通过Fc受体摄取，让病毒在这些细胞里大量复制，引发更剧烈的免疫反应，从而增加重症登革热（登革出血热/休克综合征）风险。这也是登革疫苗必须同时诱导4型均衡保护的原因。
Dengue virus has four serotypes. Antibodies from a first infection neutralize the same serotype, but sub-neutralizing antibodies against other serotypes can cause antibody-dependent enhancement (ADE): antibody-virus complexes are taken up more readily by macrophages via Fc receptors, allowing massive viral replication inside them and triggering a fiercer immune reaction—raising the risk of severe dengue (dengue hemorrhagic fever/shock syndrome). This is why dengue vaccines must induce balanced protection against all four serotypes.

一支疫苗从研发到上市要走哪些流程？

What steps does a vaccine go through from research to market?

疫苗研发一般先做临床前研究——用细胞和动物实验验证安全性与免疫原性，再依次进入I期（小样本安全与剂量）、II期（免疫应答与剂量范围）、III期（大样本有效性、安全性）临床试验。随后数据提交监管机构审评审批，获批上市后还持续进行IV期监测，长期跟踪罕见不良反应。紧急使用授权会加快流程，但仍要求现有证据显示获益大于风险。
Vaccine development begins with preclinical research — cell and animal studies of safety and immunogenicity — followed by clinical trials in phases: I (small groups, safety and dosing), II (immune response and dose range), and III (large trials of efficacy and safety). The data are then submitted for regulatory review and approval, and after market authorization continuous phase IV surveillance tracks rare adverse events long-term. Emergency use authorization accelerates the process but still requires the available evidence to show benefits outweigh risks.

为什么“相关不等于因果”？

Why doesn't correlation imply causation?

两个现象同时出现，可能是因为共同的第三因素（混杂）、可能结局先于暴露（反向因果），也可能只是巧合。判断因果关系需要排除混杂、确认时间先后，并有合理的生物学机制支撑。随机对照试验通过随机化消除混杂；观察性研究则借助多因素回归、倾向评分甚至孟德尔随机化等方法尽量逼近因果结论。
Two phenomena occurring together may share a common third factor (confounding), the "outcome" may have preceded the "exposure" (reverse causation), or it may be pure chance. Establishing causation requires ruling out confounding, confirming the correct time order, and supporting biological plausibility. RCTs remove confounding by randomization; observational studies approach causal conclusions with multivariable regression, propensity scores, or even Mendelian randomization.

队列研究和病例对照研究有什么区别？

Dengue is caused by dengue virus and spread by Aedes mosquitoes, mainly Aedes aegypti and Aedes albopictus. After an incubation of a few days, patients develop sudden high fever with severe headache, pain behind the eyes, muscle and joint aches, and a rash. Most recover within about a week, but a minority progress to severe dengue with bleeding and shock signs that need urgent care. Mosquito control is the most important preventive measure.

电子烟比传统香烟“安全”吗？

Are e-cigarettes safer than conventional cigarettes?

电子烟不燃烧烟草，因此某些燃烧产物较少，但绝不等于无害。其气雾中含有尼古丁（高度成瘾，并可损害青少年大脑发育）、超细颗粒物、羰基化合物和金属等有害成分，长期健康影响尚在观察中。一些证据提示在专业支持下电子烟可能帮助部分成年吸烟者戒烟，但世界卫生组织强调它对青少年和不吸烟者具有明显危害，主张严格监管、禁止面向青少年营销。从不吸烟的人不应以“更安全”为理由开始使用。

室内用柴火或煤做饭为什么危害健康？

Why is cooking indoors with wood or coal harmful to health?

固体燃料和煤油在通风不良的室内不完全燃烧，会产生高浓度PM2.5、一氧化碳和多环芳烃，其颗粒物浓度可远超室外空气污染标准。世界卫生组织估计家庭空气污染每年造成约320万人死亡，主要疾病包括儿童肺炎、慢性阻塞性肺疾病、肺癌、缺血性心脏病和中风，妇女和儿童因在灶边时间长而受害最深。改用液化气、电或生物质炉具等清洁燃料并改善排烟通风，是最直接有效的干预。
Solid fuels and kerosene burning incompletely in poorly ventilated rooms generate high concentrations of PM2.5, carbon monoxide and polycyclic aromatic hydrocarbons, with particle levels far above outdoor air quality limits. WHO estimates household air pollution causes about 3.2 million deaths a year, chiefly through childhood pneumonia, chronic obstructive pulmonary disease, lung cancer, ischaemic heart disease and stroke, with women and children hit hardest because they spend most time near the stove. Switching to clean fuels such as LPG or electricity, or to improved stoves, plus proper venting, is the most direct and effective intervention.

疟疾是怎样感染人体、如何诊断和治疗的？

How does malaria infect people, and how is it diagnosed and treated?

疟原虫经雌性按蚊叮咬进入血液，先在肝脏繁殖，再侵入红细胞反复扩增，引起周期性寒战、发热和出汗。诊断依靠血涂片镜检或快速诊断试纸。以青蒿素为基础的联合治疗（ACT）是当前标准方案；防蚊（蚊帐、驱蚊剂）是核心预防手段。
Malaria parasites enter the blood through the bite of female Anopheles mosquitoes, multiply first in the liver, then invade red blood cells in repeated cycles that cause periodic chills, fever, and sweating. Diagnosis relies on blood-smear microscopy or rapid tests. Artemisinin-based combination therapy (ACT) is the standard treatment; mosquito control—bed nets and repellents—is the key to prevention.

How is the workplace noise occupational exposure limit of 85 dB(A) defined, and what obligations follow for employers?

China's GBZ 2.2 sets the noise limit at an equivalent continuous sound level of 85 dB(A) averaged over a 40-hour working week, with instantaneous levels never exceeding 115 dB(A). Employers must measure noise periodically, provide hearing protectors for posts exceeding the limit, and arrange pre-placement and in-service occupational health examinations. An 8-hour equivalent level of 80 dB(A) or above already constitutes hazardous noise exposure requiring hazard disclosure and enrollment in a hearing conservation program.

头盔和安全带为什么能救命？

Why do helmets and seat-belts save lives?

撞击时人体会突然减速，头部和内脏承受巨大冲击力。头盔的缓冲层让减速过程延长几毫秒并把力分散到更大面积，安全带则把身体固定在座椅上，避免撞向方向盘或飞出车外，同时利用最坚固的骨骼承担力量。世界卫生组织的数据显示，正确佩戴头盔可使摩托车驾驶人死亡风险降低约42%、头部受伤风险降低约69%，安全带使前排乘员死亡风险降低约45%～50%，儿童安全座椅可降低幼儿死亡60%以上。
In a crash the body decelerates abruptly and the head and internal organs take enormous force. A helmet's crushable liner stretches that deceleration by a few milliseconds and spreads the load over a wider area, while a seat-belt holds the body in the seat, stops it striking the steering wheel or being thrown out, and channels force through the strongest bones. WHO data show that wearing a helmet correctly cuts a motorcyclist's risk of death by about 42% and of head injury by about 69%, seat-belts reduce front-seat occupant deaths by roughly 45-50%, and child restraints cut deaths in young children by more than 60%.

打针的针头为什么用一次就要丢掉？

Why is an injection needle thrown away after a single use?

针头刺入皮肤后会接触血液，如果重复使用或多人共用，乙型肝炎病毒、丙型肝炎病毒和艾滋病病毒等可能随残留血液传给下一个人。针尖用过一次也会变钝、可能被污染，再用更疼也更容易感染。因此规范做法是一人一针一管，用后立刻放入专用利器盒统一处理，世界卫生组织还推广用后自动失效的注射器来防止重复使用。
A needle touches blood once it goes through the skin, so reusing it or sharing it between people can pass on hepatitis B, hepatitis C or HIV in the traces of blood left behind. A used tip is also blunter and may be contaminated, making a second use more painful and more likely to cause infection. The rule is therefore one needle and one syringe per person, dropped straight into a dedicated sharps box afterwards, and WHO promotes auto-disable syringes that cannot be used twice.
