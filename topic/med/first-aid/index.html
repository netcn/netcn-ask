进行心肺复苏（CPR）时按压的深度和频率是多少？

What are the depth and rate of chest compressions in CPR?

对于成人，按压深度约为5到6厘米，频率为每分钟100到120次。每次按压后让胸廓完全回弹，并尽量减少中断。
For adults, compress the chest about 5 to 6 centimeters deep at a rate of 100 to 120 compressions per minute. Let the chest fully recoil after each push and minimize interruptions.

中暑（热射病）应该怎么处理？

How do we handle heat stroke?

热射病是急症：把人移到阴凉处、松衣、用冷水喷洒加扇风快速降温，清醒者可小口补水。若意识不清、无汗或体温极高，立即呼叫急救。
Heat stroke is an emergency: move to shade, loosen clothes, cool rapidly with water and fanning, and sip fluids if alert. Confusion, no sweating or very high temperature means call emergency services now.

什么是“黄金救援时间”？

What is the 'golden hour' of rescue?

“黄金救援时间”指突发重病或重伤后，最有可能挽救生命、减少后遗症的有限关键时段，常被认为约为伤后1小时。及时正确的急救在此窗口内尤为关键。
The 'golden hour' refers to the critical, limited window after a sudden severe illness or injury when lives are most likely to be saved and long-term damage minimized, often considered about the first hour. Timely, correct first aid matters most within this window.

如何判断心脏骤停并施行CPR？

How do you recognize cardiac arrest and do CPR?

若患者无反应且无正常呼吸，应立即呼叫急救并开始胸外按压（约每分钟100–120次、深度5–6厘米），配合人工呼吸直至除颤器或救援到达。（本回答仅为科普，实操请参加正规急救培训。）
If unresponsive with no normal breathing, call emergency services and start chest compressions (about 100–120/min, 5–6 cm deep), with rescue breaths until an AED or help arrives. (Educational only; get proper first-aid training for practice.)

What does the FAST test for stroke mean?

FAST stands for Face drooping, Arm weakness, Speech difficulty, and Time to call emergency—any sign means seek help fast.

For a sprain, ice or heat?

受伤后48小时内用RICE原则：休息、冰敷、加压、抬高；之后若仍僵硬可转为温热。若剧痛、肿得厉害或无法承重，应就医排除骨折。
In the first 48 hours use RICE: rest, ice, compression, elevation; later switch to warmth for stiffness. Severe pain, swelling or inability to bear weight needs medical check for fracture.

对乙酰氨基酚过量后,为什么N-乙酰半胱氨酸必须在摄入后尽早(最好8小时内)使用?

Why must N-acetylcysteine be started as early as possible—ideally within eight hours—after an acetaminophen overdose?

过量的对乙酰氨基酚经CYP2E1代谢生成NAPQI,后者耗竭肝细胞谷胱甘肽并与线粒体蛋白共价结合,引起小叶中心性肝坏死。N-乙酰半胱氨酸作为谷胱甘肽前体,只有在大量NAPQI尚未与组织结合前给药才能有效拦截毒性代谢物。超过8小时给药虽仍能改善凝血障碍和生存率,但肝毒性风险随延迟明显上升。
Excess acetaminophen is converted by CYP2E1 into NAPQI, which depletes hepatic glutathione and binds mitochondrial proteins, producing centrilobular necrosis. As a glutathione precursor, N-acetylcysteine intercepts circulating NAPQI efficiently only before large amounts bind covalently to tissue. Later treatment still improves coagulopathy and survival, yet hepatotoxicity risk climbs steeply as the delay lengthens.

潜水医学中，“保罗·伯特效应”与“洛兰·史密斯效应”分别指哪种氧中毒表现？为何在高压下呼吸时中枢神经型氧中毒会率先出现？

In diving medicine, which form of oxygen toxicity do the Paul Bert effect and the Lorrain Smith effect respectively describe, and why does central nervous system oxygen toxicity appear first when breathing under elevated ambient pressure?

保罗·伯特效应指高氧分压引发的急性中枢型氧中毒，表现为面颊抽动、眩晕乃至惊厥；洛兰·史密斯效应指长时间暴露所致的肺型氧中毒，表现为胸痛、咳嗽与肺活量下降。机制均为活性氧超出抗氧化防御能力，损伤神经元与肺泡上皮；环境压升高令吸入气氧分压成比例上升，脑组织先达发作阈值，故水下以惊厥型为主，规程据此限定各分压的暴露时限。
The Paul Bert effect is acute CNS oxygen toxicity from high inspired PO2, presenting as facial twitching, vertigo and convulsions; the Lorrain Smith effect is pulmonary oxygen toxicity after prolonged exposure, with retrosternal burning, cough and falling vital capacity. Both stem from reactive oxygen species overwhelming antioxidant defences, injuring neurons and alveolar epithelium; because higher ambient pressure raises inspired PO2 proportionally, the brain reaches its seizure threshold first underwater, so dive regulations cap exposure time for each PO2 level.

烫伤后第一时间的正确处理是什么？

What is the correct first response to a burn?

立即用流动的凉水冲洗伤口15-20分钟降温止痛，同时轻轻脱去衣物（粘在伤口上的不要硬撕）。不要涂牙膏、酱油或冰块，也不要用针刺破水疱；用干净纱布或保鲜膜松散覆盖后就医，面积大或起大水疱的更要尽快看医生。
Cool the burn under gently running tap water for 15–20 minutes to stop heat damage and ease pain, and remove clothing unless it is stuck to the skin. Do not apply toothpaste, soy sauce, or ice, and never pop blisters; loosely cover with clean gauze or plastic wrap and seek medical care, especially for large burns or large blisters.

被烫伤后应该怎么处理？

What should you do after a burn or scald?

立即用流动的凉水冲洗烫伤处至少10到20分钟，以减轻疼痛和损伤。不要涂牙膏或酱油，严重烫伤应尽快就医。
Immediately run cool (not ice-cold) water over the burn for at least 10 to 20 minutes to reduce pain and damage. Do not apply toothpaste or soy sauce, and seek medical care for serious burns.

为什么断离的手指或肢体保存要“干燥冷藏”而不能泡在生理盐水或冰水里？

Why should a severed finger or limb be kept "dry and cold", not soaked in saline or ice water?

再植成功靠保存离体组织的活力，理想是先用干净纱布包裹、再放入塑料袋、置于冰屑上（0–4°C）的“干燥冷藏”，避免冻伤和浸泡损伤。直接泡生理盐水或冰水会使细胞水肿、血管内皮破坏，降低再植成活率。同时伤员止血包扎、尽快送有显微外科能力的医院是关键。
Replantation depends on keeping the detached tissue alive: wrap it in clean gauze, seal in a plastic bag and set it on ice chips (0–4°C)—“dry cold”—to avoid freezing and soaking damage. Soaking in saline or ice water swells cells and ruins vessel lining, lowering success. Meanwhile control bleeding and rush to a microsurgery centre.

What should you do if someone is choking on food?

若患者能咳嗽发声，应鼓励其用力咳嗽；若无法呼吸，可对其实施海姆立克急救法（腹部冲击）。婴儿和成人的手法有所不同，必要时立即呼救。
If the person can cough and speak, encourage forceful coughing; if they cannot breathe, perform the Heimlich maneuver (abdominal thrusts). Techniques differ for infants and adults, and call for help if needed.

为什么1岁以下婴儿发生气道梗阻时要用拍背和压胸，而不能用成人的海姆立克法？

Why do infants under one year with airway obstruction need back slaps and chest thrusts, not the adult Heimlich?

婴儿肝大、膈肌高、胸腹结构娇嫩，对腹部猛挤的成人海姆立克法可能伤及肝脏或无效。指南推荐先5次俯卧拍背、再5次仰卧压胸，利用解剖位置把堵塞物推出。若婴儿仍能哭叫咳嗽说明气道未全堵，应鼓励咳而非急于操作；一旦无声、发绀须立即循环进行并呼叫急救。
Infants have a large liver, high diaphragm and delicate trunk, so the adult Heimlich abdominal thrust can injure the liver or fail. Guidelines use five back slaps then five chest thrusts to expel the object through anatomy. If the baby still cries or coughs, the airway is not fully blocked—let them cough; once silent and blue, repeat the cycle and call emergency.

外伤出血时该怎么止血？

How should you stop bleeding from a wound?

首选用干净的布或纱布直接用力压迫伤口止血，持续压迫直到出血停止或急救人员接手。尽量让伤口部位高于心脏，不要频繁松手查看。只有大动脉严重出血且压迫无效时才考虑止血带，并记录上带时间。
The first-line method is firm, direct pressure on the wound with clean cloth or gauze, held until bleeding stops or help arrives. Keep the injured area raised above the heart if possible, and avoid repeatedly lifting the cloth to peek. A tourniquet is reserved for severe arterial bleeding when pressure fails — note the time it was applied.

一氧化碳中毒患者的脉搏血氧饱和度可以完全正常,这是为什么?

Why can pulse oximetry read completely normal in a patient with carbon monoxide poisoning?

脉搏血氧仪仅用660nm与940nm两个波长区分氧合与还原血红蛋白,而碳氧血红蛋白在这两个波长下的吸光特性接近氧合血红蛋白,仪器便把它计入氧合部分。因此 SpO2 可显示98%以上,而血液实际携氧能力已大幅下降。怀疑中毒时应测定动脉血气加碳氧血红蛋白浓度,并立即给予高流量纯氧加速CO解离。
Pulse oximeters separate oxyhemoglobin from deoxyhemoglobin using only two wavelengths (660 and 940 nm), and carboxyhemoglobin absorbs light at these wavelengths much like oxyhemoglobin, so the device counts it as oxygenated. SpO2 can therefore read above 98% while the blood's actual oxygen-carrying capacity is markedly reduced. When CO poisoning is suspected, arterial blood gas with a measured carboxyhemoglobin level is required, and high-flow 100% oxygen should be started at once to hasten CO dissociation.

潜水员上浮过快为什么会得减压病？现场与后续的标准处理原则是什么？

Why does ascending too quickly cause decompression sickness, and what are the standard management principles both on scene and afterwards?

水下高压使氮气按亨利定律大量溶于血液和脂肪组织；上浮过快时环境压力骤降，氮气来不及经肺排出而过饱和析出成气泡。气泡堵塞微循环、牵拉神经组织并激活补体、凝血与炎症级联，引起关节痛（屈肢症）、皮疹、眩晕乃至截瘫。救治原则是立即改吸纯氧、充分补液，并尽快送入加压舱行再加压联合高压氧治疗。
Elevated underwater pressure drives large amounts of nitrogen into blood and fat according to Henry's law. When ascent outpaces elimination through the lungs, tissues become supersaturated and nitrogen bubbles form. Bubbles obstruct microcirculation, distend neural tissue, and ignite complement, coagulation and inflammatory cascades, producing joint pain (the bends), rash, vertigo, even paraplegia. Management calls for immediate 100% oxygen, generous fluids, and prompt transfer to a recompression chamber for compression combined with hyperbaric oxygen.

心肺复苏为什么要压得那么深那么快?按压中断几秒就会前功尽弃吗?

Why must CPR compressions go so deep and fast — do pauses really undo progress?

心脏骤停后每延迟一分钟除颤,生存率下降约一成;高质量按压是用胸廓当泵,把血手动挤向大脑与心脏本身。指南要求深度5至6厘米、频率每分钟100至120次,因为过浅过慢产生的血流不足以维持器官活性;按压中断(比如换人拖沓)会让冠脉灌注压断崖下跌,恢复需重新积累,所以强调最小化中断与每两分钟轮换防止疲劳。人工呼吸比过去淡化:未受训者持续按压优于犹豫;溺水与儿童等缺氧病因仍需通气。学一次CPR,你可能是除颤器到来前某人唯一的桥。
Survival drops about ten percent per minute without defibrillation; high-quality compression turns the chest into a pump, manually driving blood to brain and heart muscle. Guidelines demand five-to-six-centimeter depth at 100-120 per minute because shallower, slower pushes cannot sustain organ perfusion; interruptions crash coronary perfusion pressure that takes time to rebuild, hence minimal pauses and two-minute swaps against fatigue. Rescue breaths matter less than before: untrained bystanders should push continuously, though drowning and children still need air. Learn CPR — you may be the bridge until the defibrillator arrives.
