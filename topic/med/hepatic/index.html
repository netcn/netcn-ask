乙肝疫苗为什么能有效预防感染？

Why is the hepatitis B vaccine so effective?

疫苗诱导机体产生针对乙肝病毒表面抗原的抗体与免疫记忆，接触真病毒时可快速清除，从而预防慢性感染及相关肝癌风险。
The vaccine builds antibodies against the HBV surface antigen plus immune memory, so real virus is cleared fast - preventing chronic infection and liver-cancer risk.

脂肪肝是怎么回事，严重吗？

What is fatty liver, and is it serious?

当肝细胞内脂肪堆积过多就形成脂肪肝，多与肥胖、饮酒和代谢异常相关；早期可逆，持续进展可发展为肝炎甚至肝硬化。
Fatty liver means excess fat in liver cells, often tied to obesity, alcohol or metabolic issues. Early stages are reversible, but progression can lead to hepatitis or cirrhosis.

为什么喝酒伤肝？

Why does alcohol harm the liver?

酒精及其代谢产物在肝脏分解时会产生损伤并促使脂肪堆积，长期大量饮酒可引发从脂肪肝、肝炎到肝硬化的过程。
Alcohol and its breakdown products injure the liver and promote fat buildup; long-term heavy drinking can march from fatty liver to cirrhosis.

肝脏是怎样“解毒”的？

How does the liver 'detox'?

肝脏通过一系列酶把药物、酒精和代谢废物转化为易溶于水的形式，再由胆汁或尿液排出，这并非神话里的“排毒”。
The liver uses enzymes to convert drugs, alcohol and waste into water-soluble forms excreted in bile or urine - not the 'detox' of myths.

胆红素是什么，为什么肝病会让皮肤发黄？

What is bilirubin, and why does liver disease cause yellow skin?

胆红素是红细胞分解的产物，通常由肝脏处理排出；肝功能受损或胆管堵塞时胆红素堆积，导致皮肤、巩膜发黄，称为黄疸。
Bilirubin comes from broken-down red cells and is normally cleared by the liver; when the liver or bile ducts fail, it builds up and yellows the skin and eyes (jaundice).
