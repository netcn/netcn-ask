把溺水者救上岸后，第一步应该做什么？

What is the first step after pulling a drowning victim from the water?

溺水导致的心跳停止本质上源于缺氧，因此现场处理与一般心脏骤停略有不同：确认环境安全后立即检查反应和呼吸，若无正常呼吸应先给予人工呼吸，再开始按压与人工呼吸循环，并尽快呼叫急救。不要为了“把水倒出来”做腹部冲击或倒挂拍背，这会延误抢救并增加呕吐误吸风险。所有溺水者即使看似恢复也需就医观察，注意保暖并预防再次低体温。
Cardiac arrest in drowning stems from lack of oxygen, so on-scene care differs slightly from ordinary arrest: once the scene is safe, check response and breathing immediately and, if breathing is absent or abnormal, give rescue breaths first, then continue cycles of compressions and breaths while emergency help is summoned. Do not perform abdominal thrusts or hold the person upside down to “drain water” - this delays resuscitation and increases the risk of vomiting and aspiration. Every drowning victim needs medical assessment even if they seem to recover, and should be kept warm to prevent further hypothermia.

低体温和冻伤该怎么正确处理？

How should hypothermia and frostbite be handled correctly?

低体温者应尽快转移到温暖避风处，脱掉湿冷衣物，用毛毯包裹躯干为核心保温，清醒者可喝温热的含糖饮料，但要动作轻柔——重度低体温时剧烈搬动可能诱发致命心律失常，且不要给酒精。冻伤部位不要揉搓、不要用火烤或雪擦，可在37～39℃温水中缓慢复温；如果存在再次冻结的可能，应先保持冻结状态并尽快送医。两种情况都应及早寻求专业救治。
Move a hypothermic person to a warm, sheltered place, remove wet clothing and wrap the trunk in blankets to rewarm the core; if fully alert they may sip a warm sweet drink, but handle them gently - rough movement in severe hypothermia can trigger fatal arrhythmias - and never give alcohol. Frostbitten tissue should not be rubbed, held near a fire or scrubbed with snow; rewarm it slowly in water at 37-39 degrees Celsius, and if refreezing is possible, leave it frozen and get to medical care fast. Both conditions warrant professional treatment as early as possible.

怀疑颈椎或脊柱受伤时，为什么不要随意搬动伤者？

Why shouldn't you move someone with a suspected spinal injury?

脊柱骨折后椎体可能已不稳定，扭转、屈曲或拖拽会让骨块或碎片进一步压迫、切割脊髓，把原本可恢复的损伤变成永久性瘫痪。高处坠落、车祸、跳水受伤或伴颈部剧痛、四肢麻木无力时都应高度怀疑。正确做法是让伤者保持原位，用双手固定头颈使头、颈、躯干成一直线，等待专业救援；只有在火灾、溺水等即刻危险下才整体移动。但气道梗阻或没有呼吸时，抢救呼吸和循环优先。
After a spinal fracture the vertebrae may already be unstable, and twisting, bending or dragging can let bone fragments press or cut into the spinal cord, turning a recoverable injury into permanent paralysis. Suspect it strongly after falls from height, road crashes or diving injuries, or when there is severe neck pain, numbness or limb weakness. Keep the person where they are, steady the head and neck with your hands so head, neck and trunk stay in line, and wait for trained rescuers; move them as a unit only if there is immediate danger such as fire or water. If the airway is blocked or breathing has stopped, however, restoring breathing and circulation takes priority.

什么情况下需要使用止血带？

When should a tourniquet be used?

止血带用于四肢危及生命的大出血，例如喷射性出血或直接压迫仍无法控制的出血。使用要点是绑在伤口近心端约5～7厘米处、不要压在关节上，收紧到出血停止为止，并记录上带时间告知医护人员。不要间断松开“放血”，否则会加重失血；躯干、颈部出血无法使用止血带，应改用填塞加压。控制致命性出血往往比等待救护车更紧迫，因此许多国家推广公众止血培训。
A tourniquet is for life-threatening limb bleeding - spurting blood, or bleeding that direct pressure cannot control. Apply it about 5-7 cm above the wound, never over a joint, tighten until bleeding stops, and note the time so medical staff know. Do not loosen it intermittently to “let blood flow”, as that worsens blood loss; bleeding from the trunk or neck cannot be tourniqueted and needs wound packing with firm pressure instead. Stopping catastrophic bleeding is often more urgent than waiting for an ambulance, which is why many countries now train the public to do it.
