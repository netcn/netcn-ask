多组学（multi-omics）整合有什么意义？

Why does multi-omics integration matter?

多组学同时分析基因组、转录组、蛋白组和代谢组等层面，拼出更完整的疾病图景，有助于发现新分型和新靶点，但数据整合与解读仍是挑战。
Multi-omics jointly analyzes genome, transcriptome, proteome, and metabolome to build a fuller disease picture, aiding new subtypes and targets—though data integration and interpretation remain hard.

为什么有些影像需要造影剂？

Why do some scans need contrast agents?

造影剂（如含碘或钆的制剂）能改变组织对 X 光或磁共振的信号对比，让血管、肿瘤或炎症更清晰；使用前要评估肾功能和过敏风险。
Contrast agents (iodine- or gadolinium-based) alter how tissue appears on X-ray or MRI, sharpening vessels, tumors, or inflammation; kidney function and allergy risk are checked beforehand.

扩散张量成像（DTI）能看什么？

What can diffusion tensor imaging (DTI) show?

DTI 利用水沿神经纤维顺向扩散的特性，描绘大脑白质“线路”的走向与完整性，常用于研究脑损伤、中风和多发性硬化。
DTI exploits how water diffuses along nerve fibers to map the brain's white-matter “wiring” and its integrity, used in brain injury, stroke, and multiple sclerosis.

CRISPR 除了编辑基因还能用于快速诊断吗？

Beyond editing genes, can CRISPR be used for rapid diagnosis?

是的。SHERLOCK 和 DETECTR 等系统利用 CRISPR 蛋白（如 Cas13/Cas12）识别特定核酸并切割报告分子，可在资源有限处快速检测病毒或耐药基因。
Yes. Systems like SHERLOCK and DETECTR use CRISPR proteins (e.g., Cas13/Cas12) to recognize specific nucleic acids and cut a reporter, enabling rapid virus or resistance-gene detection even in low-resource settings.

全基因组测序（WGS）与全外显子测序（WES）有何不同？

How do whole-genome and whole-exome sequencing differ?

WGS 测全部 DNA 序列，包括基因间区域；WES 只测编码蛋白的外显子（约占基因组的 1–2%）但更集中、更经济，常用于找致病变异。
WGS sequences all DNA including non-coding regions; WES sequences only the protein-coding exons (~1–2% of the genome) but is more focused and cheaper, often used to find disease variants.

功能磁共振（fMRI）怎样“看”大脑活动？

How does fMRI “see” brain activity?

fMRI 不直接测神经放电，而是检测血氧水平依赖（BOLD）信号——活跃脑区耗氧增加、血流随之上升，间接反映哪些区域在“加班”。
fMRI doesn't measure firing directly; it detects blood-oxygen-level-dependent (BOLD) signals—active regions use more oxygen and get more blood flow—indirectly showing which areas are “working.”

PET-CT 是如何发现肿瘤的？

How does PET-CT detect tumors?

PET 先注射微量放射性糖示踪剂，代谢活跃的癌细胞摄取更多，便在影像上“点亮”；CT 提供解剖定位，二者结合能看到“在哪里、有多活跃”。
PET injects a trace radioactive sugar that metabolically active cancer cells take up more, “lighting up” on imaging; CT adds anatomical location, combining “where” with “how active.”

AI 在医学影像中能做什么？

What can AI do in medical imaging?

经过训练的算法可在胸片、眼底等影像中辅助识别病变、标注可疑区域，帮助医生提高效率；它仍是辅助工具，最终判断需由专业医师作出。
Trained algorithms can flag lesions and highlight suspicious areas in X-rays, retinal scans, etc., helping clinicians work faster; they remain aids, with final calls made by professionals.

什么是生物标志物（biomarker）？

What is a biomarker?

生物标志物是可测量的体内指标（如血液蛋白、基因特征），用来提示健康状态、疾病风险或治疗反应；好的标志物需稳定、可重复且与结局相关。
A biomarker is a measurable indicator (e.g., a blood protein or genetic signature) reflecting health, disease risk, or treatment response; good ones are stable, reproducible, and tied to outcomes.

连续血糖监测（CGM）是怎样工作的？

How does continuous glucose monitoring (CGM) work?

CGM 用皮下微型传感器间歇测量组织间液的葡萄糖，给出 24 小时血糖曲线，帮助糖尿病患者发现波动并调整饮食与用药。具体方案请与医生商定。
CGM uses a tiny under-skin sensor to read glucose in interstitial fluid, giving a 24-hour curve so people with diabetes can spot swings and adjust diet or medication. Plans should be set with a doctor.

数字化疗法（Digital Therapeutics）是什么？

What are digital therapeutics?

数字化疗法是基于软件、有临床证据的治疗干预，例如用 App 配合认知行为疗法治疗失眠或成瘾；它作为药物的补充，需经监管评估。
Digital therapeutics are software-based, evidence-backed interventions—e.g., an app delivering CBT for insomnia or addiction—used alongside drugs and requiring regulatory review.

可穿戴设备能监测哪些健康指标？

What health indicators can wearables monitor?

智能手表等可连续记录心率、步数、睡眠和血氧，部分还能测心电图或提示房颤；它们适合趋势监测，但不能替代诊断性医疗检查。
Wearables continuously track heart rate, steps, sleep, and blood oxygen, some even ECG or flag atrial fibrillation; they're good for trends but not a substitute for diagnostic care.

什么是“即时检验”（POCT）？

What is point-of-care testing (POCT)?

即时检验是在病人身边（如诊所、家中）快速得到结果的检测，比如血糖仪和抗原快筛，省去把样本送到中心实验室的等待。
Point-of-care testing gives quick results at the patient's side—like a glucose meter or antigen rapid test—without sending samples to a central lab.
