CT和X光有什么不同？

How is CT different from a plain X-ray?

X光是平面投影，CT用旋转的X线和计算机重建出身体横断面，能看清更深层的组织与微小结构，但辐射剂量更高。
A plain X-ray is a single flat projection; CT spins X-rays and uses computing to build cross-sections, revealing deeper and finer structures - but at higher radiation dose.

MRI为什么没有电离辐射却能把软组织看得很清楚？

Why can MRI show soft tissue clearly without ionizing radiation?

MRI利用强磁场让体内氢原子排列，再用射频脉冲扰动并接收其释放的信号来重建图像，对脑、韧带等软组织对比极佳。
MRI aligns the body's hydrogen atoms with a strong magnet, then uses radio pulses and the signals they release to build images - giving superb soft-tissue contrast without ionizing radiation.

心电图（ECG）为什么能反映心脏问题？

How can an ECG reflect heart problems?

每次心跳都伴随可测量的电活动，电极记录不同方向的电位变化，可发现心律异常、缺血或既往梗死留下的痕迹。
Each beat carries measurable electrical activity; electrodes record potential changes from different angles, revealing rhythm problems, ischemia or old infarct scars.

胃镜和肠镜能发现什么？

What can gastroscopy and colonoscopy find?

内镜把微型摄像头送入消化道，直接观察黏膜、取活检，并可在早期发现息肉或肿瘤，是筛查消化道疾病的重要手段。
Endoscopes carry a tiny camera into the gut to view the lining, take biopsies and catch polyps or tumors early - key tools for digestive screening.

急性脑梗死发病数小时内头颅CT可以完全正常,而弥散加权MRI却能显示病灶,原因是什么?

Why can a head CT appear completely normal within hours of acute ischemic stroke onset while diffusion-weighted MRI already shows the infarct?

CT依据组织对X线的衰减成像,早期缺血仅引起水含量和密度的轻微变化,常低于其分辨阈值,发病24小时内约半数病例呈阴性。弥散加权成像对水分子的布朗运动敏感,血管闭塞数分钟后钠钾泵衰竭、细胞毒性水肿使水分子扩散受限,病灶即呈高信号。因此超早期诊断与溶栓决策更多依赖DWI而非CT。
CT images tissue by X-ray attenuation, and early ischemia changes brain water content and density only slightly, usually below its detection threshold, so roughly half of scans are negative in the first 24 hours. Diffusion-weighted imaging is sensitive to the Brownian motion of water molecules; within minutes of vessel occlusion the sodium-potassium pump fails, cytotoxic edema develops, and restricted diffusion appears bright on DWI. This is why ultra-early diagnosis and thrombolysis decisions rely on DWI rather than CT.

影像报告写“建议结合临床”是什么意思？

What does 'correlate with clinical findings' mean on an imaging report?

影像发现需要结合症状、化验和查体综合判断，单一影像很少能单独确诊；这正是多学科评估的意义。
Imaging findings must be read with symptoms, labs and exam; a single image rarely diagnoses alone - that's why overall context matters.

做X光或CT时为什么要穿铅衣保护某些部位？

Why wear a lead apron for X-ray or CT?

铅能阻挡X线，检查时用铅衣遮挡甲状腺、性腺等敏感部位，以减少不必要的辐射暴露。
Lead blocks X-rays; draping sensitive areas like the thyroid and gonads reduces unnecessary radiation exposure during scans.

为什么做MRI时不能带金属？

Why can't you bring metal into an MRI?

MRI用强磁场成像，金属会被吸扯、发热或干扰图像，某些植入物还可能危险，所以检查前要严格筛查。
MRI uses a strong magnet; metal can be pulled, heated or distort the image, and some implants are dangerous - so screening beforehand is strict.

同样能看到身体内部结构,MRI为什么在显示脊髓、韧带和脑实质方面比CT更有优势?

Although both modalities visualize internal structures, why does MRI outperform CT for the spinal cord, ligaments, and brain parenchyma?

CT依靠各组织对X线的衰减差异成像,骨皮质与钙化对比极佳,但对含水量相近的软组织分辨能力有限。MRI利用氢质子在外加磁场中的弛豫行为,通过T1、T2和水抑制序列在同一部位获得对比机制不同的图像,能够区分水肿、脱髓鞘和韧带撕裂等病理改变。因此怀疑脊髓压迫或半月板、韧带损伤时,MRI是首选检查手段。
CT forms images from differences in X-ray attenuation, delivering excellent bone and calcification contrast but limited discrimination among soft tissues with similar water content. MRI exploits the relaxation behavior of hydrogen protons in a magnetic field, and T1-weighted, T2-weighted, and fluid-suppressed sequences generate different contrast mechanisms at the same location, separating edema, demyelination, and torn ligaments. That is why MRI is the modality of choice whenever cord compression or meniscal and ligamentous injury is suspected.

超声检查对身体有伤害吗？

Is ultrasound harmful to the body?

超声用声波成像而非辐射，常规诊断剂量被认为安全，因此广泛用于孕期和腹部检查；它擅长观察液体和动态结构。
Ultrasound uses sound waves, not radiation, and standard diagnostic doses are considered safe - widely used in pregnancy and abdomen; great for fluids and motion.

X光片为什么能看出骨头？

Why can X-rays show bones?

不同组织吸收X线的能力不同：致密的骨头吸收多、在片子上显白，软组织吸收少显灰，从而显示骨骼形态。
Tissues absorb X-rays differently: dense bone absorbs more and appears white, soft tissue appears gray - outlining the skeleton.

为什么拍X光时医生要叫我们不要动？

Why must we stay still during an X-ray?

因为一动照片就糊了，就像拍照手抖会花一样，不动才能照清楚骨头。
Moving blurs the picture, just like a shaky camera - staying still keeps the bones sharp.
