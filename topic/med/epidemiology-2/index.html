变异株是怎么产生的，为什么要关注？

How do variants arise and why do we track them?

病毒复制时随机突变，少数突变可能增强传播或逃逸免疫，形成变异株；监测它们有助于及时调整疫苗与防控措施。
Viruses mutate randomly as they copy; a few changes may spread better or dodge immunity, forming variants. Tracking them helps update vaccines and controls.

群体免疫阈值和R0有什么关系？

How is the herd-immunity threshold linked to R0?

阈值约等于 1 减 1/R0；R0越高，需要接种疫苗或曾感染的比例就越大，才能切断传播链。
The threshold is roughly 1 - 1/R0. The higher the R0, the larger the share that must be immune (via vaccine or prior infection) to break transmission.

什么是用“污水监测”来追踪传染病？

What is wastewater surveillance for infections?

通过检测污水中的病原核酸，可提前发现社区感染趋势，尤其适用于无症状传播，是一种低成本的“哨点”手段。
Testing sewage for pathogen genetic material can reveal community infection trends early - especially for silent spread - as a low-cost sentinel tool.

接触者追踪为什么能放慢疫情？

Why does contact tracing slow an outbreak?

通过找到并管理感染者接触过的人，可在其发病前就隔离，切断传播链，为疫苗和医疗争取时间。
By finding and managing people an infected person touched, you isolate them before they spread - cutting chains and buying time for vaccines and care.

什么是基本传染数R0？

What is the basic reproduction number R0?

R0指在无人免疫、无防控措施时，一个感染者平均会传染的人数；R0大于1疫情会扩大，小于1则会消退。
R0 is the average number one infected person spreads to when no one is immune and no measures are taken. Above 1 an outbreak grows; below 1 it fades.
