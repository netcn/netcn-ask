人体衰老的细胞和分子机制有哪些？

What are the cellular and molecular mechanisms of aging?

衰老由多种相互交织的机制驱动：端粒随细胞分裂逐渐缩短，达到临界长度后细胞进入复制性衰老；DNA损伤积累、表观遗传改变、线粒体功能障碍、蛋白质稳态下降和慢性低度炎症共同加速这一过程。细胞衰老本身是防癌的屏障，但衰老细胞持续分泌的炎症因子（SASP）反而促进组织老化。这些发现催生了清除衰老细胞等延缓衰老的试验性策略。
Aging is driven by intertwined mechanisms: telomeres shorten with each cell division and trigger replicative senescence at a critical length; DNA damage accumulation, epigenetic changes, mitochondrial dysfunction, declining proteostasis, and chronic low-grade inflammation accelerate the process together. Cellular senescence itself is a barrier against cancer, but the inflammatory factors senescent cells continuously secrete (the SASP) paradoxically promote tissue aging. These insights have spawned experimental strategies such as clearing senescent cells to slow aging.

更年期的"潮热"是怎么产生的？

What causes hot flushes in menopause?

雌激素下降改变了下丘脑体温调节中枢的敏感性，使"体温舒适区"变窄，轻微升温就被判定为过热，于是触发皮肤血管扩张和出汗散热，产生突发的热浪与潮红。研究还发现下丘脑中KNDy神经元活动增强参与其中，这也是新型非激素药物的作用靶点。治疗方案应与医生讨论。
Falling oestrogen alters the hypothalamic thermoregulatory set point, narrowing the comfortable temperature zone so small rises read as overheating, triggering skin vasodilation and sweating — the sudden wave of heat and flushing. Research implicates hyperactive KNDy neurons in the hypothalamus, now a target for non-hormonal drugs. Treatment choices should be discussed with a clinician.

端粒和衰老是什么关系？

How are telomeres related to ageing?

端粒是染色体末端的重复DNA序列，每次细胞分裂会缩短一点；缩到临界长度时细胞进入衰老状态或凋亡，这被视为细胞层面老化的一个标志。但衰老是多因素过程，还涉及线粒体功能、蛋白稳态、表观遗传改变和慢性炎症。端粒酶能延长端粒，然而过度激活与癌症风险相关，所以"延长端粒抗老"并非简单可行的方案。
Telomeres are repetitive DNA caps on chromosomes that shorten a little at each cell division; at a critical length cells become senescent or die, a hallmark of cellular ageing. But ageing is multifactorial, also involving mitochondrial function, proteostasis, epigenetic change and chronic inflammation. Telomerase can lengthen telomeres, yet excess activity is linked to cancer, so "lengthen telomeres to stay young" is not a straightforward strategy.

为什么人类女性会有绝经，而多数动物没有？

Why do human females go through menopause when most animals don't?

多数哺乳动物繁殖能力随寿命同步衰退，而人类女性在寿命中段就停止生育。一个被广泛讨论的解释是"祖母假说"：停止生育后把资源投入照顾子孙，可能提高家族整体后代存活率。虎鲸和领航鲸等少数具有复杂社会结构的物种也有类似现象，为该假说提供了旁证。
In most mammals fertility declines roughly in step with lifespan, whereas human females stop reproducing around mid-life. A widely discussed explanation is the grandmother hypothesis: ceasing reproduction to invest in grandchildren may raise the family's overall reproductive success. A few species with complex societies, such as killer whales and pilot whales, show something similar, lending indirect support.

为什么人类分娩比大多数动物更困难？

Why is human childbirth harder than in most other animals?

人类婴儿头部相对巨大，而直立行走要求骨盆相对狭窄、结构稳固，两者之间存在解剖学张力（常被称为"分娩困境"假说）。婴儿必须在产道中完成一系列旋转才能通过。这也解释了为何人类新生儿相对不成熟、需要长期照护，以及为何助产在人类社会中普遍存在。
Human infants have unusually large heads while upright walking favours a relatively narrow, sturdy pelvis — an anatomical tension often called the obstetrical dilemma. The baby must rotate through a series of positions to pass the birth canal. This also helps explain why human newborns are relatively immature, need prolonged care, and why assisted birth is near-universal in human societies.

生长激素在人体中起什么作用？

What does growth hormone do in the body?

生长激素由垂体分泌，是儿童和青少年骨骼与软组织生长的主要驱动力，同时调节蛋白质、糖和脂肪代谢。儿童期分泌不足会导致生长迟缓，分泌过多则可能引起巨人症；成年后它更多参与肌肉维持和代谢调节。生长激素主要在夜间深睡眠时分泌旺盛，因此规律睡眠对儿童发育很重要。
Growth hormone, made by the pituitary, is the main driver of bone and soft-tissue growth in children and adolescents, and it also regulates protein, glucose, and fat metabolism. Deficiency in childhood causes growth failure, while excess can cause gigantism; in adults it is more involved in maintaining muscle and regulating metabolism. It is secreted most during deep sleep at night, which is why regular sleep matters for growing children.

为什么很多人怀孕早期会孕吐？

Why do many people feel nauseated in early pregnancy?

最主要的相关因素是胎盘分泌的激素，特别是hCG和GDF15水平升高，作用于脑干呕吐中枢附近的区域，同时嗅觉与味觉敏感性上升。一种假说认为这在演化上有保护作用，让孕早期避开可能变质或有毒的食物。多数在孕中期缓解；若无法进食进水或体重明显下降，应及时就医。
The leading factors are placental hormones — notably rising hCG and GDF15 — acting near brainstem vomiting centres, together with heightened smell and taste sensitivity. One hypothesis is an evolutionary protective role, steering early pregnancy away from spoiled or toxic foods. It usually eases by the second trimester; seek care if you cannot keep fluids down or lose weight.

青春期身体会发生哪些变化？

What changes does the body go through during puberty?

青春期由性激素激增启动，女孩出现乳房发育和月经初潮，男孩出现睾丸增大和遗精，同时伴随生长突增、体毛出现和身体成分改变。发育开始的早晚个体差异很大，女孩通常在8-13岁、男孩9-14岁启动，偏早偏晚大多仍在正常范围。充足的睡眠、均衡营养和积极心理支持有助于平稳度过这一时期。
Puberty is set off by a surge of sex hormones: girls develop breast tissue and start menstruation, boys' testes enlarge and sperm production begins, along with a growth spurt, body hair, and changing body composition. The timing varies widely—girls typically start between 8 and 13 and boys between 9 and 14—and being early or late is usually still normal. Adequate sleep, balanced nutrition, and emotional support help make this transition smoother.

为什么孕期用药要格外谨慎？

Why does medication need extra caution in pregnancy?

胚胎器官形成主要集中在孕早期，此时对外来物质最敏感；部分药物可经胎盘影响胎儿发育，历史上的沙利度胺事件就是警示。但"完全不用药"也有风险：未控制的癫痫、感染、甲状腺疾病或严重抑郁同样危害母婴。原则是由医生权衡利弊后选择方案，不自行停药或加药。
Organs form mainly in the first trimester, when the embryo is most sensitive, and some drugs cross the placenta and affect development — the thalidomide tragedy is the historical warning. Yet taking nothing is also risky: uncontrolled epilepsy, infection, thyroid disease or severe depression harm both mother and baby. The rule is that a clinician weighs risks and benefits — never start or stop medicines on your own.

为什么老年人更容易缺乏维生素B12？

Why are older adults more prone to vitamin B12 deficiency?

随年龄增长，胃黏膜萎缩使内因子分泌减少、胃酸降低，导致食物中的B12难以从蛋白中释放并被回肠吸收，这种“食物结合型B12缺乏”在老人中很常见。B12缺乏可造成巨幼细胞贫血和神经髓鞘损害，表现为手脚麻木、记忆力下降。老年患者血B12和甲基丙二酸检测有助于早发现。
With age, gastric atrophy lowers intrinsic factor and acid, so B12 bound to food is poorly released and absorbed in the ileum—a common “food-cobalamin malabsorption” in elders. Deficiency causes megaloblastic anemia and myelin damage, showing as numbness and memory loss. Checking serum B12 and methylmalonic acid helps detect it early.

为什么叶酸要在怀孕前就开始补？

Why should folic acid be started before pregnancy, not after?

神经管在受孕后约第3—4周闭合，那时很多人还不知道自己怀孕。叶酸参与DNA合成与细胞快速分裂，孕前1—3个月开始每日补充可显著降低脊柱裂等神经管缺陷风险。具体剂量请遵医嘱，有相关家族史者可能需要更高剂量。
The neural tube closes around weeks 3–4 after conception, before many people know they are pregnant. Folate supports DNA synthesis and rapid cell division, so daily supplementation started one to three months before conception markedly lowers the risk of neural tube defects such as spina bifida. Dose should follow clinical advice; a family history may call for more.

月经周期是由哪些激素调控的？

Which hormones regulate the menstrual cycle?

月经周期由下丘脑-垂体-卵巢轴驱动：卵泡期雌激素逐渐升高，让子宫内膜增厚；排卵后黄体分泌孕激素，使内膜转为适合胚胎着床的分泌期；若未受孕，黄体萎缩、雌孕激素骤降，内膜脱落形成月经。平均周期约28天，但21-35天都属常见范围。
The menstrual cycle is driven by the hypothalamus-pituitary-ovary axis: during the follicular phase rising estrogen thickens the uterine lining; after ovulation the corpus luteum secretes progesterone, converting the lining to a secretory state ready for implantation. If pregnancy does not occur, the corpus luteum regresses, estrogen and progesterone fall sharply, and the lining sheds as menstruation. The average cycle is about 28 days, though 21–35 days is common.

为什么老年人更容易脱水？

Why are older adults more prone to dehydration?

随年龄增长，口渴感受变钝，即使体液不足也不觉得渴；肾脏浓缩尿液的能力下降，水分流失更多；体内总含水量本身也减少。加上部分人为减少起夜或行动不便而主动少喝水，风险叠加。天热或生病时更应主动定时补水。
With age the thirst sensation dulls, so fluid deficits go unnoticed; the kidneys concentrate urine less well, losing more water; and total body water is lower to begin with. Some also drink less deliberately to avoid night trips to the toilet or because mobility is limited, compounding the risk. In hot weather or illness, drinking on a schedule helps.

什么是肌少症，为什么对老年人特别重要？

What is sarcopenia and why does it matter for older adults?

肌少症指随年龄增长出现的肌肉质量与力量显著下降，通常从40岁后每十年流失约3%—8%，速度在久坐、蛋白摄入不足和疾病时加快。它直接影响行走速度、平衡与自理能力，是跌倒、骨折和失能的重要风险因素。抗阻训练加上充足蛋白质摄入是目前最有效的干预。
Sarcopenia is the age-related loss of muscle mass and strength — roughly 3–8% per decade after 40, faster with inactivity, low protein intake or illness. It directly affects walking speed, balance and independence, and is a major risk factor for falls, fractures and disability. Resistance training plus adequate protein intake is the most effective intervention known.

那是"囟门"，颅骨还没完全长合留下的缝隙。它让头部在出生时能稍微变形通过产道，也给快速长大的大脑留出空间。前囟一般在1—1.5岁闭合，日常轻柔触碰是安全的，但明显凹陷或鼓起时应就医。
That is the fontanelle, a gap where the skull bones have not yet fused. It lets the head mould slightly to pass through the birth canal and leaves room for the fast-growing brain. The front fontanelle usually closes by 12–18 months; gentle everyday touch is safe, but a clearly sunken or bulging spot should be checked by a clinician.

男孩青春期为什么会变声？

Why do boys' voices break during puberty?

睾酮升高使喉部软骨增大、声带变长变厚，振动频率随之降低，声音变低沉。变化期间喉部肌肉还不适应新的"乐器"尺寸，控制不稳就会出现忽高忽低的破音，通常几个月到两年内稳定。女孩声带也会变长，但幅度较小。
Rising testosterone enlarges the laryngeal cartilage and makes the vocal folds longer and thicker, so they vibrate at a lower frequency and the voice deepens. Meanwhile the laryngeal muscles are still adapting to the new instrument size, causing cracks and squeaks that usually settle within a few months to two years. Girls' folds also lengthen, but less.

为什么1岁以下的宝宝不能吃蜂蜜？

Why shouldn't babies under one year old eat honey?

蜂蜜可能含肉毒杆菌孢子。婴儿肠道菌群尚未成熟、胃酸较弱，孢子可能在肠道萌发并产生毒素，导致婴儿肉毒中毒，表现为便秘、吸吮无力、肌张力低下。普通加热无法可靠灭活孢子，因此1岁前应完全避免，包括含蜂蜜的加工食品。
Honey can carry Clostridium botulinum spores. An infant's gut flora is immature and stomach acid weaker, so spores may germinate and produce toxin, causing infant botulism with constipation, weak sucking and floppiness. Ordinary heating does not reliably kill spores, so avoid honey entirely before age one, including foods containing it.

为什么小孩发烧常比大人烧得更高？

Why do children often run higher fevers than adults?

儿童免疫系统对新病原反应更强烈，产生更多致热的细胞因子；同时他们体表面积与体重之比大、体温调节还不够稳定，体温波动幅度更大。体温高低并不直接等于病情严重程度，孩子的精神状态、进食和呼吸情况更值得关注；婴儿发热或出现异常嗜睡应尽快就医。
Children's immune systems react strongly to new pathogens, releasing more fever-inducing cytokines, and their larger surface-area-to-weight ratio with less stable thermoregulation makes temperature swing more. Height of fever does not equal severity — alertness, feeding and breathing matter more. Fever in infants, or unusual drowsiness, warrants prompt medical care.

为什么小朋友上幼儿园后老是生病？

Why do little kids get sick so often after starting nursery?

因为他们的免疫系统还在"上学"。每遇到一种新病毒，身体就学会认识它并记住，下次就更快打赢。幼儿园里孩子多、接触近，遇到的新病毒也多，所以一年感冒六到八次很常见，随着年龄增长会明显减少。
Their immune system is still in training. Each new virus teaches the body to recognise and remember it, so next time the fight is faster. Nursery means many children in close contact and many new viruses, so six to eight colds a year is common — and it drops noticeably as they grow.

为什么女孩的青春期生长突增通常比男孩来得早？

Why does the adolescent growth spurt usually start earlier in girls than in boys?

青春期启动由下丘脑-垂体-性腺轴驱动，女孩多在9–11岁、男孩多在11–13岁进入突增期。女孩突增早但持续时间短、总增益较少，男孩启动晚却突增更久更高，因此最终成人身高男性普遍高于女性。突增开始早晚差异大，只要骨龄与身高增速在合理范围即属正常。
Puberty is driven by the hypothalamic–pituitary–gonadal axis; girls typically enter the growth spurt at 9–11 and boys at 11–13 years. Girls start earlier but spurt for a shorter time with smaller total gain, while boys start later yet grow taller for longer, so adult men are on average taller. Timing varies widely, and what matters is that bone age and growth velocity stay reasonable.

为什么乳牙蛀牙也会影响以后恒牙的排列和健康？

Why can tooth decay in baby teeth affect the permanent teeth later?

乳牙下方的恒牙牙胚在颌骨中发育，乳牙严重龋坏或早失会改变咀嚼力和间隙，导致恒牙萌出空间不足、拥挤或异位。乳牙牙髓感染还可能波及下方的恒牙胚，造成釉质发育不良。因此从第一颗牙萌出起就要刷牙、限糖并定期看牙医。
The permanent tooth buds develop in the jaw beneath the baby teeth; severe decay or early loss of a primary tooth changes chewing forces and space, so the adult tooth may erupt crowded or out of place. Infection of a primary pulp can also reach the underlying bud and cause enamel defects. Brushing from the first tooth, limiting sugar and regular dental visits all matter.
