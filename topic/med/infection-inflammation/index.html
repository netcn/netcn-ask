为什么会出现抗生素耐药性？

Why does antibiotic resistance develop?

当抗生素使用不当或过度时，最敏感的细菌被杀死，而具有耐药基因的细菌存活并繁殖。耐药基因可在细菌间传播，最终导致药物失效。
When antibiotics are misused or overused, the most susceptible bacteria die while those carrying resistance genes survive and multiply. Resistance genes can spread among bacteria, eventually rendering drugs ineffective.

什么是败血症（脓毒症）？

What is sepsis?

败血症是感染引发全身失控的炎症反应，可导致器官衰竭和休克。它是急症，表现为高热或低体温、心率呼吸加快、神志改变，需立即抢救。
Sepsis is a life-threatening, body-wide runaway inflammatory response to infection that can lead to organ failure and shock. It is an emergency with high or low temperature, fast heart and breathing rates and confusion, requiring urgent care.

带状疱疹是什么？

What is shingles (herpes zoster)?

带状疱疹由潜伏在神经节里的水痘-带状疱疹病毒重新激活引起。它沿神经分布出现成簇疼痛水疱，常见于老年人或免疫力下降时。
Shingles is caused by reactivation of the varicella-zoster virus that has lain dormant in nerve ganglia. It produces clusters of painful blisters along a nerve distribution, often in older adults or when immunity drops.

真菌感染常见吗？

Are fungal infections common?

真菌感染很常见，从皮肤癣、脚气到阴道念珠菌都属此类。多数仅在体表，但免疫力低下者可发生严重的深部真菌感染。
Fungal infections are common, ranging from athlete's foot and ringworm to yeast infections. Most stay on the surface, but people with weak immunity can develop serious deep fungal infections.

人体正常菌群是什么？

What is the body's normal flora (microbiome)?

正常菌群是长期寄居在皮肤、口腔、肠道等部位的微生物群落，通常与人体互利。它们帮助抵御有害菌、参与代谢并训练免疫系统。
Normal flora are the microbial communities living on the skin, in the mouth, gut and elsewhere, usually in a mutually beneficial relationship. They help fend off harmful microbes, aid metabolism and train the immune system.

病毒和细菌有什么不同？

What is the difference between viruses and bacteria?

细菌是能独立生存和繁殖的单细胞生物，部分可引起疾病。病毒更小，必须进入宿主细胞内才能复制，常引起流感、新冠等感染。
Bacteria are single-celled organisms that can live and reproduce on their own, and some cause disease. Viruses are smaller and must enter host cells to replicate, causing infections such as flu and COVID-19.

抗生素是如何起作用的？

How do antibiotics work?

抗生素通过抑制细菌的细胞壁合成、蛋白质合成或DNA复制来杀灭或抑制细菌。它们对病毒无效，因此感冒等病毒感染不应使用。
Antibiotics kill or inhibit bacteria by blocking their cell-wall synthesis, protein synthesis or DNA replication. They do not work against viruses, so they should not be used for viral infections like the common cold.

发烧是好事还是坏事？

Is a fever a good or a bad thing?

发烧是免疫系统对抗感染的正常反应，适度升温可抑制病原体并增强免疫细胞活性。但过高或持续不退的发热需要就医查明原因。
A fever is the immune system's normal response to infection; moderate heat can slow pathogens and boost immune cells. But very high or persistent fever needs medical evaluation to find the cause.

为什么伤口会红肿热痛？

Why do wounds become red, swollen, hot and painful?

这是急性炎症的典型表现，受伤后局部血管扩张、血流增加并渗出液体。免疫细胞聚集清理细菌和坏死组织，同时刺激神经产生痛感。
These are classic signs of acute inflammation: after injury, local vessels widen, blood flow increases and fluid leaks out. Immune cells gather to clear bacteria and dead tissue while stimulating nerves to cause pain.

炎症是什么？

What is inflammation?

炎症是身体对损伤或感染的防御反应，表现为红、肿、热、痛和功能受限。它通过召集免疫细胞清除有害物并促进修复。
Inflammation is the body's defensive response to injury or infection, showing as redness, swelling, heat, pain and limited function. It recruits immune cells to clear the threat and promotes repair.

白细胞有什么作用？

What do white blood cells do?

白细胞是免疫系统的核心成员，负责识别并消灭细菌、病毒和异常细胞。包括中性粒细胞、淋巴细胞等多种类型，各有专长。
White blood cells are central to the immune system and identify and destroy bacteria, viruses and abnormal cells. Types such as neutrophils and lymphocytes each have specialized roles.
