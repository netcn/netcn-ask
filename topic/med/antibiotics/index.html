细菌产生耐药性的分子机制有哪些？

What molecular mechanisms cause bacterial resistance?

细菌可通过产生降解酶、改变药物靶点、外排泵排出药物及获得耐药质粒等机制抵抗抗生素，且耐药基因能在菌种间水平传播。
Bacteria resist via degrading enzymes, altered drug targets, efflux pumps, and resistance plasmids, and these genes can spread horizontally between species.

#耐药机制 #质粒

什么是“最后一线”抗生素及为何受保护？

What are last-resort antibiotics and why are they protected?

碳青霉烯类等“最后一线”药物用于多重耐药重症感染，因有效替代极少而被严格限用，以防广泛耐药后无药可治。
Last-resort drugs like carbapenems treat multi-drug-resistant severe infections; with few alternatives they are tightly restricted to preserve them against total resistance.

#最后一线 #碳青霉烯

从抗菌药物PK/PD角度看,为什么β内酰胺类按时间依赖性规律给药,而氨基糖苷类可以一日一次大剂量?

In pharmacokinetic/pharmacodynamic terms, why must beta-lactams be dosed in a time-dependent fashion while aminoglycosides allow once-daily high dosing?

关键在于各自的最大杀菌参数与抗生素后效应不同。β内酰胺类几乎无PAE,疗效取决于游离药物浓度超过MIC的时间占给药间隔的比例(fT>MIC),通常需40%以上,延长滴注或加密给药最有效;氨基糖苷类为浓度依赖性,杀菌速度随Cmax/MIC升高而加快且有较长PAE,单次足量给药维持高峰值,谷浓度回落期还能降低肾耳毒性,管理策略据此制定优化方案。
The key is which parameter predicts killing best and how strong the post-antibiotic effect is. Beta-lactams show almost no PAE, so efficacy tracks the fraction of the dosing interval with free drug above the MIC (fT>MIC, usually 40% or more), favoring prolonged infusions or shorter intervals. Aminoglycosides are concentration-dependent: killing accelerates as Cmax/MIC rises and a sustained PAE lets one large daily dose hold peaks high while low troughs limit nephrotoxicity and ototoxicity - the basis of stewardship dosing strategies.

#抗菌药物管理 #时间依赖性杀菌 #氨基糖苷类

从细胞因子调控的角度看，为什么细菌感染时降钙素原显著升高而多数病毒感染时保持低位？

Through the lens of cytokine regulation, why does procalcitonin rise steeply in bacterial infections yet usually stay low during viral ones?

因为两类病原触发的细胞因子环境对该基因的开关作用相反。细菌内毒素及IL-1β、TNF-α、IL-6等因子诱导肺、肠、脂肪等甲状腺外组织表达CALC-1基因，大量释放降钙素原；病毒感染以干扰素应答为主，IFN-γ直接阻断这条转录通路，故PCT几乎不升。加之其半衰期约一天、感染控制后迅速回落，医生可依据降钙素原动力学判断感染类型并指导停药，随机对照试验已证明这样能安全缩短抗菌疗程。
Because the cytokine environments the two pathogen classes create switch the same gene in opposite directions. Bacterial endotoxin together with interleukin-1 beta, TNF-alpha and IL-6 drives extra-thyroid tissues such as lung, gut and fat to express CALC-1 and release abundant procalcitonin, whereas the interferon-dominated antiviral response - chiefly IFN-gamma - directly blocks that transcription, keeping levels flat. With a half-life near twenty-four hours and a swift fall once infection is controlled, procalcitonin dynamics let clinicians judge infection type and guide cessation; randomized trials show this safely shortens antibiotic courses.

养殖场使用抗生素为何关乎人类健康？

Why does farm antibiotic use affect human health?

畜牧业促生长用药会富集环境中的耐药菌，并可能经食物链传播耐药基因，因此多国已限制非治疗性使用。
Farm use for growth promotes resistant bacteria in the environment and can pass resistance genes via food chains, so many countries limit non-therapeutic use.

#畜牧 #耐药

洗手与抗生素耐药有何关系？

How is handwashing linked to antibiotic resistance?

勤洗手减少感染发生，从而降低对抗生素的需求，间接减缓耐药菌的产生与扩散。
Frequent handwashing cuts infections and thus antibiotic demand, indirectly slowing resistant bacteria's rise and spread.

我们普通人如何帮助对抗耐药？

How can ordinary people help fight resistance?

不自行购药、不把抗生素当感冒药、遵医嘱用完疗程并按时接种，都是减缓耐药的重要日常行动。
Don't self-prescribe, don't use antibiotics for colds, finish prescribed courses, and vaccinate—everyday actions that slow resistance.

#耐药 #公众行动

为什么必须按疗程吃完抗生素？

Why must you finish the full antibiotic course?

提前停药可能留下耐药菌并导致感染复发，遵医嘱完成疗程有助于彻底清除并减少耐药产生。
Stopping early may leave resistant bacteria and cause relapse; finishing the course helps clear infection and limit resistance.

#抗生素 #依从性

抗生素能治感冒吗？

Can antibiotics cure a cold?

不能。感冒多由病毒引起，抗生素只对细菌有效，乱用反而助长耐药菌。
No—colds are usually viral; antibiotics only fight bacteria, and misuse fuels resistance.

#抗生素 #病毒

药水没吃完可以留着下次用吗？

Can you keep leftover medicine for next time?

不可以。剩下的药水可能变质或过期，下次生病也不一定对症，应遵医嘱使用或丢弃。
No—leftover medicine may spoil or expire and might not fit the next illness; follow your doctor and discard safely.
