长期睡眠不足会对身体做什么？

What does long-term sleep loss do to the body?

持续缺睡会损害记忆与情绪、削弱免疫力、扰乱代谢并增加意外风险；它还是心血管和肥胖问题的可控危险因素。优先保证睡眠是性价比很高的健康投资。
Chronic short sleep impairs memory and mood, weakens immunity, disrupts metabolism and raises accident risk; it's a modifiable risk factor for heart disease and obesity. Prioritizing sleep is a high-return investment.

为什么快速眼动（REM）睡眠被剥夺会选择性损害情绪记忆的巩固？

Why does REM sleep deprivation selectively impair emotional memory consolidation?

REM睡眠期间杏仁核与海马、新皮层的对话增强，负责把带有情绪色彩的经历“再加工”并存入长时记忆，同时平复过度唤起。实验显示剥夺REM后，人对负面刺激的记忆偏向和情绪反应会异常增高，白天的情绪调节能力下降。Walker等2009年的研究系统阐述了睡眠与记忆的这一关系。
During REM, the amygdala talks more with hippocampus and neocortex to re-process emotional experiences into long-term memory while calming over-arousal. Experiments show that after REM loss, memory bias toward negative stimuli and emotional reactivity rise and daytime regulation drops. Walker and colleagues (2009) laid out this sleep–memory relationship.

发作性睡病有哪些核心症状？

What are the core symptoms of narcolepsy?

发作性睡病以不可控制的日间嗜睡、猝倒、睡眠瘫痪和入睡前幻觉为核心表现，多与下丘脑分泌素缺失有关。需睡眠监测与多次小睡试验确诊。治疗以药物和行为管理结合。
Narcolepsy centers on uncontrollable daytime sleepiness, cataplexy, sleep paralysis, and hypnagogic hallucinations, often tied to lost hypocretin. Diagnosis needs sleep studies and the multiple sleep latency test. Treatment combines drugs and behavior.

#发作性睡病 #猝倒 #诊断

为什么治疗慢性失眠首选认知行为治疗（CBT-I）而不是安眠药？

Why is cognitive behavioral therapy for insomnia (CBT-I) preferred over sleeping pills?

慢性失眠往往由不良睡眠行为和对失眠的担忧“维持”。CBT-I通过睡眠限制（压缩卧床时间）、刺激控制（只在困时上床）等行为技术重建睡眠-觉醒节律，并结合认知重构消除“必须睡着”的焦虑。随机对照试验显示其效果与药物相当，但没有依赖、耐受和次日困倦问题，且疗效在停药后长期保持，因此国内外指南都把它列为一线治疗。
Chronic insomnia is usually maintained by poor sleep behaviors and worry about sleep itself. CBT-I rebuilds the sleep-wake rhythm with behavioral techniques such as sleep restriction (compressing time in bed) and stimulus control (going to bed only when sleepy), and adds cognitive restructuring to dismantle the anxiety of “must fall asleep.” RCTs show effects comparable to medication, but without dependence, tolerance, or next-day drowsiness, and the gains persist after treatment ends—so guidelines in many countries list CBT-I as first-line therapy.

1型发作性睡病患者为什么会出现猝倒？这与下丘脑食欲素（orexin）神经元丢失有什么关系？

Why does cataplexy occur in patients with narcolepsy type 1, and how is it related to the loss of hypothalamic orexin (hypocretin) neurons?

1型发作性睡病者下丘脑食欲素（orexin）神经元大量丢失，脑脊液食欲素A极低。食欲素维持清醒稳定并阻止REM睡眠提前入侵；缺失后觉醒不稳，白天嗜睡难以抗拒，情绪诱发的猝倒则是REM肌张力抑制通路被误触发的结果。该病与HLA-DQB1*06:02强相关，提示免疫机制参与；脑脊液食欲素A检测有助于确诊。
In narcolepsy type 1, selective loss of orexin (hypocretin) neurons in the lateral hypothalamus leaves cerebrospinal fluid orexin-A extremely low. Orexin normally stabilizes wakefulness and stops REM sleep from intruding too early; without it, arousal becomes unstable, causing irresistible daytime sleepiness, while emotion-triggered cataplexy reflects inappropriate activation of the REM muscle-atonia pathway. The strong HLA-DQB1*06:02 association points to autoimmune involvement, and low CSF orexin-A helps confirm the diagnosis.

睡眠呼吸暂停综合征如何诊断？

How is sleep apnea syndrome diagnosed?

多导睡眠监测是金标准，记录脑电、血氧、呼吸和心率等，判断暂停次数与缺氧程度。家庭便携监测适用于部分中高危患者。确诊后可用呼吸机或无创通气治疗。
Polysomnography is the gold standard, recording EEG, oxygen, breathing, and heart rate to count apneas and hypoxia. Home portable monitoring fits some moderate-high risk patients. CPAP or non-invasive ventilation follows diagnosis.

睡觉打鼾很响需要担心吗？

Should loud snoring be a concern?

单纯打鼾很常见，但如果鼾声很大、伴有呼吸暂停和憋醒、白天严重困倦，可能提示阻塞性睡眠呼吸暂停（OSA）。OSA使睡眠中反复缺氧、睡眠碎片化，长期会增加高血压、心血管疾病和认知问题的风险。怀疑时应做睡眠监测确诊；治疗首选持续气道正压通气（CPAP），减肥和侧卧也有帮助。
Simple snoring is common, but loud snoring with witnessed pauses, gasping awakenings, and severe daytime sleepiness may signal obstructive sleep apnea (OSA). OSA causes repeated hypoxia and fragmented sleep, and over time raises the risk of hypertension, cardiovascular disease, and cognitive problems. If suspected, a sleep study confirms it; continuous positive airway pressure (CPAP) is first-line treatment, with weight loss and side-sleeping also helping.

不宁腿综合征是什么？

What is restless legs syndrome?

不宁腿综合征表现为静息时下肢难以忍受的酸胀冲动，常在夜间加重，影响入睡。可能与缺铁、肾病或药物有关。确诊后可有针对性处理。
Restless legs syndrome brings an uncontrollable urge to move the legs at rest, worse at night and disrupting sleep. It may relate to iron deficiency, kidney disease, or medicines. Targeted care follows diagnosis.

人的睡眠分哪几个阶段？

What stages make up human sleep?

睡眠分为非快速眼动睡眠（NREM）和快速眼动睡眠（REM）。NREM又分浅睡（N1、N2）和深睡（N3）；深睡对体力恢复和免疫很重要，REM期大脑活跃、梦境最多。整夜以约90分钟为周期循环，前半夜深睡较多，后半夜REM比例升高，一个晚上会经历4-6个这样的周期。
Sleep consists of non-rapid eye movement (NREM) and rapid eye movement (REM) sleep. NREM includes light (N1, N2) and deep (N3) stages; deep sleep is key to physical recovery and immunity, while REM is when the brain is active and dreaming mostly occurs. Cycles last about 90 minutes and repeat 4–6 times a night—deep sleep dominates the early night and REM increases toward morning.

Is circadian disruption linked to metabolic disease?

长期昼夜节律紊乱，如频繁倒班或夜班，与肥胖、糖尿病和心血管风险升高相关。机制涉及激素分泌和进食时间的错位。尽量规律作息有助减轻影响。
Chronic circadian disruption, like frequent shift work, links to higher obesity, diabetes, and cardiovascular risk. The mechanism involves misaligned hormone release and meal timing. Keeping a regular schedule helps limit harm.

褪黑素分泌受什么调节？

What regulates melatonin secretion?

松果体在黑暗中分泌褪黑素，光线尤其蓝光强烈抑制其释放，因此夜间光照会推迟睡意。视交叉上核作为主生物钟协调这一节律。规律的光暗周期有助于稳定分泌。
The pineal gland secretes melatonin in darkness, and light especially blue light strongly suppresses it, so nighttime light delays sleepiness. The suprachiasmatic nucleus acts as master clock coordinating this rhythm. Regular light-dark cycles stabilize secretion.

Why does the upper airway collapse repeatedly at night in obstructive sleep apnea, and how does this explain daytime sleepiness and long-term hypertension risk?

清醒时颏舌肌等咽部扩张肌持续收缩，对抗吸气负压以维持气道开放；入睡后这种张力活动减弱，REM期近乎消失。若肥胖或下颌偏小已使气道狭窄，管腔便会反复塌陷闭塞，血氧下降触发微觉醒，导致睡眠碎片化并兴奋交感神经，长期增加高血压与心血管事件风险。经鼻持续气道正压（CPAP）像气体夹板一样撑开气道，是核心治疗。
When awake, pharyngeal dilator muscles such as the genioglossus contract continuously against inspiratory negative pressure to keep the airway open. Their tonic activity weakens at sleep onset and nearly disappears during REM sleep. In someone whose airway is already narrowed by obesity or a small jaw, the lumen collapses repeatedly; each episode lowers oxygen, triggers a micro-arousal that fragments sleep, and activates sympathetic output, raising long-term risks of hypertension and cardiovascular events. Nasal CPAP acts like a pneumatic splint and remains first-line therapy.

Sleep helps the brain sort memories, repair the body, and clear waste—essential maintenance time for health.

Why do we yawn?

科学家发现，打哈欠能让凉凉的空气进到嘴里，帮大脑降降温、让人更精神一点。困了或无聊时，身体就用打哈欠来提提神。
Scientists think yawning lets cool air into the mouth and helps cool the brain down a little, making us a bit more alert. When tired or bored, the body uses a yawn to perk up.

睡前看手机为什么影响睡眠？

Why does using a phone before bed affect sleep?

手机屏幕的蓝光会抑制褪黑素分泌，推迟睡意到来。内容与通知还可能引起兴奋或焦虑。建议在睡前一小时放下电子设备。
Blue light from phone screens suppresses melatonin and delays sleepiness. Content and notifications can also cause arousal or anxiety. It is best to put devices away an hour before bed.

为什么睡前做剧烈运动反而可能让人更难入睡？

Why can intense exercise right before bed make it harder to fall asleep?

剧烈运动使核心体温、心率和肾上腺素在短期内升高，身体需要时间冷却和放松才能进入睡意。此外运动后精神更亢奋，也会推迟入睡。一般建议把高强度训练放在睡前至少2–3小时，或改做拉伸、散步等舒缓活动帮助放松。
Hard exercise briefly raises core temperature, heart rate and adrenaline, and the body needs time to cool and settle before sleep comes. The mental buzz afterward also delays bedtime. Keep intense training at least 2–3 hours before sleep, or switch to gentle stretching and a walk to relax.

为什么天黑了妈妈就让我去睡觉?

Why does Mom tuck me in when it gets dark?

你的身体里住着一个小闹钟。天一黑,它就让眼睛告诉大脑:该休息啦!睡觉的时候,你的身体在悄悄长大,大脑在整理白天看到的好玩东西。睡得饱,第二天才有力气玩。
A little clock lives inside you. When it gets dark it tells your brain: time to rest! While you sleep your body grows and your brain tidies up all the fun things from today. Good sleep means more play tomorrow.

鬼压床是鬼吗?睡醒后动不了身体的科学解释是什么?

Sleep paralysis and ghosts — what's the real science of waking unable to move?

睡眠分深浅期和快速眼动期,做梦多发生在后者,此时大脑会主动锁住全身肌肉防止你把梦演出来。鬼压床就是意识提前醒了、肌肉锁还没解除的错位状态,几分钟内自动恢复。文化里把它想象成鬼怪压身,科学里它是常见的睡眠瘫痪,压力大、作息乱时更频繁,侧睡和规律作息能减少发作。
Sleep cycles include REM dreaming phases during which the brain paralyzes muscles so you cannot act dreams out. Sleep paralysis is waking before the lock releases: conscious but frozen for minutes. Cultures blamed spirits; science calls it common sleep paralysis, more frequent under stress and irregular schedules — side sleeping plus routine helps.
