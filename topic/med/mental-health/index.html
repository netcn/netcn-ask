抑郁和普通的悲伤有什么区别？

How is depression different from ordinary sadness?

抑郁是持续两周以上的情绪低落、兴趣丧失并伴睡眠食欲改变等，影响功能；普通悲伤通常有明确原因且会随时间缓解。
Depression is a persistent low mood and loss of interest lasting over two weeks with sleep/appetite changes that impair function; ordinary sadness has a clear cause and eases with time.

慢性压力如何改变大脑结构？

How does chronic stress change brain structure?

长期高皮质醇可萎缩海马（影响记忆）并放大杏仁核（增强恐惧反应），同时削弱前额叶的执行控制功能。
Chronic high cortisol can shrink the hippocampus (memory) and enlarge the amygdala (fear) while weakening prefrontal executive control.

抑郁症真的不是"想不开"吗？大脑里发生了什么？

Is depression really not just "thinking too negatively"? What happens in the brain?

抑郁症是生物、心理、社会因素共同作用的疾病。研究发现相关脑区（前额叶、海马、杏仁核）活动与体积改变，5-羟色胺、去甲肾上腺素等单胺类神经递质系统功能异常，慢性炎症与HPA轴过度激活也参与其中。它像高血压一样需要规范治疗，心理治疗和药物都有效。
Depression is an illness driven by biological, psychological, and social factors. Research shows altered activity and volume in the prefrontal cortex, hippocampus, and amygdala; dysfunction of monoamine systems such as serotonin and norepinephrine; and roles for chronic inflammation and HPA-axis overactivation. Like hypertension, it deserves proper treatment — both psychotherapy and medication are effective.

What is 'rumination' and how to break it?

认知行为疗法（CBT）具体是怎么起作用的？

How exactly does cognitive behavioral therapy (CBT) work?

CBT的核心假设是：情境不由人控制，但对情境的想法会影响情绪和行为。治疗师帮来访者识别自动化负性思维（如"我肯定做不好"），用证据检验并重构它，再通过行为激活和暴露练习逐步改变回避行为。它针对当前问题、结构明确，有大量随机对照试验支持其治疗焦虑、抑郁和失眠等疾病的效果。
CBT rests on a core idea: situations are not under your control, but your interpretation of them drives emotion and behavior. Therapists help clients spot automatic negative thoughts (e.g., "I'll definitely fail"), test them against evidence and reframe, then use behavioral activation and exposure to change avoidance. It is present-focused and structured, and a large body of randomized trials supports it for anxiety, depression, and insomnia.

焦虑发作时心跳加快、出汗、手抖是怎么回事？

What causes a racing heart, sweating, and trembling during anxiety?

大脑的杏仁核感知到威胁后激活下丘脑，进而启动交感神经系统——"战斗或逃跑"反应：肾上腺素释放，心率加快、血压升高、呼吸加深，血液流向肌肉。同时下丘脑-垂体-肾上腺轴升高皮质醇。适度焦虑是保护机制，只有过度、持续并影响生活时才需要干预。
When the amygdala perceives a threat, it alerts the hypothalamus, which triggers the sympathetic nervous system — the fight-or-flight response: adrenaline is released, heart rate and blood pressure rise, breathing deepens, and blood flows to muscles. The hypothalamic–pituitary–adrenal axis also raises cortisol. Some anxiety is protective; only when it becomes excessive, persistent, and impairing does it need treatment.

Why is teen depression often overlooked?

为什么电休克治疗（ECT）对难治性重度抑郁仍然有效？

Why does electroconvulsive therapy (ECT) still work for treatment-resistant severe depression?

电休克治疗在麻醉下给予短暂电流诱发一次受控的惊厥，能快速改变脑内神经递质、神经可塑性与神经环路活动，效果是药物难以企及的。它被推荐用于自杀风险高、拒食或孕期重度抑郁等对时间敏感的危重情况，现代改良技术已大幅降低记忆副作用。美国精神医学会将其列为难治性抑郁的一线躯体治疗之一。
Under anaesthesia, ECT delivers a brief current to provoke one controlled seizure, rapidly shifting neurotransmitters, plasticity and circuit activity in ways drugs cannot match. It is recommended for time-critical severe cases—high suicide risk, refusal to eat, or pregnancy—and modern modifications have cut memory side effects. The APA lists it among first-line somatic treatments for resistant depression.

What is the 'fight or flight' response?

“战斗或逃跑”是身体面对威胁时由交感神经系统触发的应激反应，会加快心率、升高血压以准备行动。它是人类进化出的生存保护机制。
The 'fight or flight' response is a stress reaction triggered by the sympathetic nervous system when facing a threat, raising heart rate and blood pressure to prepare for action. It is a survival mechanism evolved in humans.

为什么人际联系有益心理健康？

Why do social connections help mental health?

支持性关系能降低压力激素和抑郁风险，而长期孤独对健康的危害可与吸烟相当。主动维系朋友、家人或社群联系，是低成本的保护因素。
Supportive relationships lower stress hormones and depression risk, while chronic loneliness harms health about as much as smoking. Maintaining friends, family or community ties is low-cost protection.

物质成瘾真的能治好吗？怎么治？

Can substance addiction really be treated, and how?

成瘾是慢性、复发性的脑疾病，长期滥用物质会重塑中脑边缘多巴胺“奖赏通路”，使“想要”和“喜欢”脱节。有效治疗是行为治疗与药物辅助治疗的组合：认知行为疗法和动机访谈重建应对技能，美沙酮、丁丙诺啡用于阿片依赖，安非他酮/伐尼克兰用于烟草依赖，纳曲酮用于预防复发。和高血压一样，成瘾需要长期管理而非一次性“治愈”，复发不等于失败。
Addiction is a chronic, relapsing brain disease: chronic substance use reshapes the mesolimbic dopamine “reward pathway,” decoupling wanting from liking. Effective treatment combines behavioral therapy with medication: cognitive behavioral therapy and motivational interviewing rebuild coping skills, methadone and buprenorphine treat opioid dependence, bupropion/varenicline treat tobacco dependence, and naltrexone prevents relapse. Like hypertension, addiction needs long-term management rather than a one-time “cure”—relapse is not failure.

老话说「愁一愁,白了头」,长期精神压力真的会让头发提前变白吗?

As the saying goes, worry turns hair white—can chronic psychological stress really bring graying forward?

有科学依据。2020年发表于Nature的小鼠研究证实,应激使交感神经过度激活,释放的大量去甲肾上腺素会促使毛囊隆突部的黑色素干细胞异常增殖并流失,干细胞库耗竭后,新长出的毛发就永久失去颜色。人类的急性应激同样可能影响毛发色素,所谓「一夜白头」部分是黑发集中脱落、白发显出来的视觉效果。
There is solid evidence. A mouse study in Nature in 2020 showed that stress overactivates the sympathetic nervous system, and the surge of norepinephrine pushes melanocyte stem cells in the hair-follicle bulge into abnormal proliferation and depletion; once that reserve is spent, newly grown fur stays unpigmented. Acute stress appears capable of influencing human hair pigment too, while overnight whitening partly reflects pigmented hairs selectively falling out and revealing white ones.

Why does the heart beat faster when angry?

生气或害怕时，身体会分泌让心跳加快的激素，为"战斗或逃跑"做准备，所以你会觉得心怦怦跳。
When angry or scared, the body releases hormones that speed up the heart to prepare for "fight or flight," so you feel it pounding.
