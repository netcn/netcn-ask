为什么慢病随访要关注“多重用药”风险？

Why watch for polypharmacy in chronic care?

多种药物同服可能增加相互作用与不良反应，尤其对老年人；定期做用药审核可减掉不必要用药、降低伤害。
Taking many drugs raises interaction and side-effect risk, especially in older adults; regular medication review can drop unnecessary drugs and reduce harm.

为什么糖尿病管理要看“糖化血红蛋白”？

Why track HbA1c in diabetes care?

它反映过去约2 - 3个月的平均血糖，比单次血糖更稳定地体现长期控制水平，是调整治疗的重要依据。
HbA1c reflects average blood sugar over roughly 2-3 months, steadier than a single reading, making it key for adjusting treatment.

慢病管理为什么强调“自我管理”？

Why does chronic-disease care stress self-management?

高血压、糖尿病等需长期生活方式与用药配合，患者掌握监测与应对技能，比单纯就诊更能稳定控制病情。
Conditions like hypertension and diabetes need long-term lifestyle and medication teamwork; patients who learn monitoring and coping skills control them better than visits alone.

在家里测血压怎样才准？

How to measure blood pressure accurately at home?

静坐5分钟、手臂与心脏同高、袖带大小合适、不说话，取多次平均值更可靠；定期校准设备也有帮助。
Sit quietly 5 minutes, arm at heart level, correct cuff size, silent - then average several readings. Calibrating the device helps too.
