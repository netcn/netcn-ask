How does pulmonary rehab help COPD patients?

通过呼吸肌训练、节能技术与运动处方，改善通气效率和运动耐量，减少急性加重与住院。
Breathing-muscle training, energy-saving techniques and exercise prescription improve ventilation and exercise tolerance, cutting flare-ups and hospital stays.

中风后为什么要尽早开始康复训练？

Why start rehab early after a stroke?

大脑具有可塑性，早期科学康复可促进未受损区域代偿、减少瘫痪侧挛缩，改善行走与自理；过晚则恢复更难。
The brain is plastic; early, science-based rehab lets intact areas compensate and prevents contractures, improving walking and self-care. Delay makes recovery harder.

脑卒中偏瘫患者Brunnstrom分期达III期时,上肢屈肌共同运动的典型表现是什么?该阶段康复训练的核心策略是什么?

In hemiplegic stroke patients at Brunnstrom stage III, what is the typical presentation of the upper-limb flexor synergy, and what is the core training strategy at this stage?

III期的特征是随意运动重新出现但仍以共同运动为主,上肢屈肌协同典型表现为肩胛骨回缩、肩外展外旋、肘屈曲与前臂旋后。训练核心在于从共同运动模式中诱发出分离的选择性动作,可通过近端负重、姿势控制与任务导向性练习实现。同时要避免过度用力引发联合反应,防止异常运动模式被强化。
Stage III marks the return of voluntary movement still dominated by synergies; the classic flexor pattern combines scapular retraction, shoulder abduction with external rotation, elbow flexion, and forearm supination. Training aims to elicit selective movement breaking away from the synergy through proximal weight-bearing, postural control, and task-oriented practice. Excessive effort must be avoided because associated reactions reinforce the abnormal pattern.

为什么康复强调“循序渐进”？

Why does rehab stress 'step by step'?

组织修复和体能恢复都需要时间，过快容易再受伤、过慢容易萎缩；个体化、分阶段推进更安全有效。
Tissues and fitness need time - too fast risks re-injury, too slow risks atrophy. Individual, staged progress is safer and more effective.

脑卒中偏瘫康复中,任务导向性训练相对传统神经发育疗法(Bobath)有哪些优势?

Compared with traditional neurodevelopmental therapy such as Bobath, what advantages does task-oriented training bring to post-stroke hemiparetic rehabilitation?

任务导向性训练以真实生活动作(够物、坐站转移、上下台阶)为训练单元,强调高重复、难度递进和情境特异性。系统评价显示其在上肢功能与步行能力方面的改善优于以抑制异常姿势为核心的 Bobath 技术。其机制在于利用活动依赖性神经可塑性促进皮质重组,实施时应借助 Fugl-Meyer 等评估分层设定难度并配合家庭训练计划巩固效果。
Task-oriented training builds sessions around real-life actions such as reaching, sit-to-stand transfers, and stair climbing, stressing high repetition, graded difficulty, and context specificity. Systematic reviews show greater gains in upper-limb function and walking capacity than posture-suppression-centered Bobath techniques. Its mechanism relies on activity-dependent neuroplasticity driving cortical reorganization; in practice difficulty is stratified with tools like the Fugl-Meyer assessment and consolidated through a home exercise program.
