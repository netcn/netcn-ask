商业“detox 排毒疗程”能清除体内毒素吗？

Do commercial 'detox' programs clear toxins?

健康的肝肾功能本就持续清除代谢废物，多数商业“排毒”缺乏证据；与其依赖产品，不如保证睡眠、饮水与均衡饮食。
Healthy liver and kidneys already clear metabolic waste continuously; most commercial 'detox' lacks evidence. Sleep, water and balanced food beat the products.

“天然保健品绝对安全、可随便吃”对吗？

Are 'natural' supplements always safe to take freely?

不对。许多草本或补充剂也有肝肾风险，或会与处方药相互作用；“天然”不等于无害，使用前应咨询专业人士。
No. Many herbs and supplements risk liver or kidney harm or interact with prescription drugs; 'natural' isn't 'harmless' - check with a professional first.

“打疫苗会让人得这种病”是真的吗？

Can vaccines give you the disease they prevent?

正规的灭活或亚单位疫苗不含活病原，不会致病；减毒活疫苗极个别体弱者需评估，但整体而言获益远大于风险。
Proper inactivated or subunit vaccines contain no live germs and can't cause the disease; live vaccines need caution only in a few frail people - overall benefit far exceeds risk.

“吃碱性食物能改变体质酸碱度”有科学依据吗？

Can 'alkaline foods' change your body's acidity?

人体血液的酸碱由肺和肾精密调节，饮食很难撼动；所谓“酸碱性体质”已被科学界否定，均衡膳食更重要。
Blood acidity is tightly regulated by lungs and kidneys; diet barely shifts it. The 'acid-alkaline body' idea is rejected by science - balanced eating matters more.

“手机辐射会让人得脑瘤”是真的吗？

Does phone radiation cause brain tumors?

手机属非电离辐射，能量不足以破坏DNA；现有大规模研究未证实其与脑瘤有明确因果，但合理减少使用时长仍有益。
Phones use non-ionizing radiation too weak to break DNA; large studies haven't shown a clear causal link to brain tumors, though less screen time is still wise.

“发烧会烧坏脑子”是真的吗？

Does fever 'burn' the brain?

普通发烧是身体在和病菌打仗，一般不会烧坏脑子；只有极少数很高且持续的情况要注意，大多数发烧休息喝水就会好转。
A normal fever is your body fighting germs and rarely harms the brain; only very high, lasting fevers need worry. Most fevers ease with rest and fluids.
