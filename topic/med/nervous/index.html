什么是反射？为什么眨眼睛是反射？

What are reflexes, and why is blinking a reflex?

反射是不经过大脑思考的快速、固定反应，由脊髓等低级中枢完成；眨眼睛是保护眼睛免受异物和强光的防御反射。
A reflex is a fast, fixed response that bypasses the brain, handled by the spinal cord; blinking is a protective reflex shielding the eye from objects and bright light.

为什么触摸到烫的东西会立刻缩手？

Why do we pull our hand away from something hot?

碰到烫的东西，感觉神经立刻把信号经脊髓传到肌肉，几乎瞬间让手缩回，这种反射能在大脑感到痛之前保护你。
Touching something hot, sensory nerves send a signal via the spinal cord to muscles, pulling the hand back almost instantly; this reflex protects you before the brain feels pain.

为什么摔跤或转圈后会感到头晕？

Why do we feel dizzy after spinning?

快速转圈后停下，耳内负责平衡的前庭系统还在"转"，传给大脑的信息和眼睛看到的不一致，就会感到头晕。
After spinning, the inner-ear balance system still signals motion, conflicting with what the eyes see, making you feel dizzy.

为什么会感到疼痛？

Why do we feel pain?

疼痛是身体发出的"警报"，提醒我们哪里受伤或生病了，好让我们去保护它。
Pain is the body's alarm that tells you where you are hurt or sick, so you can protect that part.

大脑一天消耗多少能量？

How much energy does the brain consume per day?

大脑虽然只占体重约2%，却要消耗全身约20%的能量，主要用来维持神经活动和思考。
Though only about 2% of body weight, the brain uses about 20% of the body's energy, mainly to keep nerves active and support thinking.
