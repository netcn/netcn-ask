姑息理念只用在临终阶段吗？

Is palliative care only for the end of life?

不是。现代姑息强调从确诊重病起就同步管理症状与心理负担，贯穿治疗全程，而不只是最后阶段。
No. Modern palliative care starts managing symptoms and distress from diagnosis of serious illness onward, throughout treatment - not only at the end.

什么是“安宁疗护”（姑息 care）？

What is palliative (hospice) care?

它不为治愈疾病，而是缓解严重病痛、尊重患者意愿并支持家属，提升生命末期的生活质量，与积极治疗可并行。
It doesn't aim to cure but relieves serious suffering, honors the patient's wishes and supports families - improving quality of life at life's end, and can run alongside active treatment.

疼痛管理在姑息疗护中为什么重要？

Why is pain management central to palliative care?

持续疼痛会剥夺睡眠、食欲与尊严，规范镇痛能让人更安详地与家人相处，是照护的核心之一。
Constant pain steals sleep, appetite and dignity; proper pain relief lets people spend calmer time with family - a core part of care.
