遗传性血管性水肿急性发作时,为什么抗组胺药和糖皮质激素几乎无效,正确的治疗靶点是什么?

Why do antihistamines and glucocorticoids barely help acute hereditary angioedema attacks, and what is the correct therapeutic target?

因为水肿由缓激肽蓄积而非组胺介导。患者多因SERPING1突变致C1酯酶抑制剂缺乏,激肽释放酶-激肽系统失去制动,缓激肽大量生成并激动血管内皮B2受体,通透性骤增形成反复发作、可窒息致死的水肿;抗组胺药针对H1受体、激素作用于其他炎症通路,都没触及缓激肽环节,故收效甚微。正确方向是补足C1-INH浓缩物、以艾替班特阻断B2受体,或用艾卡拉肽等药物抑制激肽释放酶。
Because the swelling is driven by accumulated bradykinin, not histamine. Most patients lack functional C1 inhibitor due to SERPING1 mutations, leaving the kallikrein-kinin system unchecked; excess bradykinin stimulates endothelial B2 receptors, sharply raising vascular permeability and producing recurrent edema that can suffocate. Antihistamines block H1 receptors and steroids act on unrelated inflammatory pathways, so neither touches this axis. Correct targets are replacing C1-INH concentrate, blocking B2 receptors with icatibant, or inhibiting plasma kallikrein with ecallantide.

No. Antibiotics work only against bacterial infections, while colds and flu are mostly caused by viruses. Misusing antibiotics can also promote drug-resistant bacteria.

氯苯唑酸并不减少体内的致病蛋白，凭什么还能减缓转甲状腺素蛋白淀粉样变性的进展？

Since tafamidis never removes the abnormal protein itself, how does it still slow the course of transthyretin amyloidosis?

它选择的是稳定而非清除致病蛋白。转甲状腺素蛋白以四聚体形式在血中循环，基因突变使其不稳定，解离出的单体错误折叠并聚合成淀粉样纤维，沉积在心肌与周围神经；该药结合四聚体的甲状腺素结合位点，像分子扣一样把四个亚基锁定，显著延缓解离这一限速步骤，新生淀粉样物质随之减少。III期试验显示治疗组死亡与心血管住院的复合终点明显下降，证明了这种动力学稳定策略的价值。
It stabilizes rather than clears the culprit protein. Transthyretin circulates as a tetramer; mutations destabilize it, and the monomers released misfold and aggregate into amyloid fibrils deposited in myocardium and peripheral nerves. Tafamidis binds the tetramer's thyroxine-binding sites and, like a molecular clasp, locks the four subunits together, markedly delaying dissociation - the rate-limiting step - so less amyloid forms. In the phase III trial the composite of death and cardiovascular hospitalization fell significantly, validating kinetic stabilization as a strategy.

发烧是病吗？

Is a fever a disease?

发烧本身不是疾病，而是身体对抗感染的一种防御反应。适度发烧有助于免疫系统工作，但过高或持续不退需要关注。
A fever is not a disease by itself but a defensive response of the body against infection. A mild fever helps the immune system work, though a very high or persistent fever needs attention.

The common cold is usually caused by viruses, most often rhinoviruses. It generally resolves on its own, and rest and fluids help recovery.

An allergy is when the immune system overreacts to substances that are usually harmless, such as pollen or certain foods. Common signs include sneezing, rash, and itching.

What does anemia usually mean?
