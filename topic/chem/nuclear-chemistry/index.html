碳-14 测年法为什么能确定古物年代？

Why can carbon-14 dating determine the age of ancient objects?

活体生物不断与环境交换碳，体内碳-14 与稳定碳比例恒定；死亡后交换停止，碳-14 按已知半衰期衰变减少。测出现存碳-14 比例即可反推死亡至今的时间，有效范围约数万年。该方法由利比创立并获诺贝尔化学奖。
Living organisms constantly exchange carbon, keeping a constant C-14 to stable-carbon ratio; after death exchange stops and C-14 decays at its known half-life. Measuring the remaining C-14 fraction back-calculates the time since death, valid up to tens of thousands of years. Libby developed it and won the Nobel Prize in Chemistry.

什么是放射性？哪些原子核不稳定？

What is radioactivity and which nuclei are unstable?

放射性是某些原子核自发放出 α、β 或 γ 射线并转变为其他核素的现象。中子数与质子数比例失衡（过多或过少）或质量数过大的核往往不稳定，如铀-238、碳-14。衰变使核趋向更稳定的中子/质子比。
Radioactivity is the spontaneous emission of α, β, or γ radiation as a nucleus changes into another nuclide. Nuclei with imbalanced neutron-to-proton ratios or very large mass numbers are often unstable, like uranium-238 and carbon-14. Decay drives the nucleus toward a more stable ratio.

核裂变和核聚变有什么不同？

How do nuclear fission and fusion differ?

核裂变是重核（如铀-235）分裂成两个中等质量核并释放能量，是现有核电站的基础。核聚变是轻核（如氢的同位素）结合成较重核并释放更多能量，是太阳和氢弹的能量来源，目前受控聚变仍在攻关。两者都依质量亏损转化为能。
Nuclear fission splits a heavy nucleus (e.g., uranium-235) into medium ones, releasing energy and underpinning today's reactors. Fusion joins light nuclei (e.g., hydrogen isotopes) into heavier ones, releasing even more energy—the source of the Sun and hydrogen bombs; controlled fusion is still being developed. Both convert mass defect into energy.

放射性同位素的半衰期是什么？

What is the half-life of a radioactive isotope?

半衰期是放射性核素衰变掉一半所需的时间，是该核素的固有常数，不受温度、压力或化学状态影响。碳-14 半衰期约 5730 年，常用于考古测年；碘-131 半衰期约 8 天，用于甲状腺治疗。半衰期越长，放射性越持久。
Half-life is the time for half of a radioactive nuclide to decay; it is an intrinsic constant unaffected by temperature, pressure, or chemical state. Carbon-14's half-life is about 5730 years, used in archaeological dating; iodine-131's is about 8 days, used for thyroid treatment. Longer half-life means more persistent radioactivity.
