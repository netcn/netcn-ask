为什么蜡烛熄灭后的白烟能复燃？

Why can the white smoke relight a candle?

白烟是可燃气体(蜡蒸气)，遇火会重新燃烧。
The white smoke is flammable wax vapor that reignites with a flame.

为什么蜡烛外焰发黄内焰发蓝，蓝的反而更热？

Why is the blue inner flame of a candle hotter than the yellow outer one?

内焰燃烧较充分温度高，火焰本身偏蓝；外焰混有未燃尽的碳粒，受热发光显黄，温度反而较低。
The inner flame burns more completely and is hotter (bluish); the outer flame contains glowing unburned carbon particles (yellow) and is actually cooler.

为什么白磷在空气中会自己着火？

Why does white phosphorus ignite by itself in air?

白磷燃点约30°C，极易氧化放热而自燃，必须泡在水中保存；红磷结构稳定得多，燃点高。
White phosphorus ignites around 30 C by spontaneous oxidation and must be stored underwater; red phosphorus is far more stable.

本生灯为什么调大风门火焰变蓝、调小变黄？

Why does a Bunsen burner turn blue with more air and yellow with less?

风门开大进空气多，燃气充分燃烧呈高温蓝焰；空气不足则燃烧不完全，出现发黄光的碳粒。
More air gives complete, hot blue combustion; too little air yields incomplete combustion with glowing yellow soot particles.

为什么二氧化碳能灭火？

Why does CO2 put out fires?

二氧化碳不助燃且比空气重，能隔绝氧气闷灭火焰。
CO2 doesn't support burning and is heavier than air, smothering flames.

为什么扇风会让篝火更旺？

Why does fanning feed a campfire?

扇风送来更多氧气，让燃烧更充分更旺。
Fanning supplies more oxygen, making combustion more complete.

为什么火需要氧气？

Why does fire need oxygen?

燃烧是可燃物与氧气的剧烈氧化反应，缺氧就烧不起来。
Burning is rapid oxidation with oxygen; without it fire stops.
