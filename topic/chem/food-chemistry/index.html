为什么冰糖葫芦的糖壳又脆又亮？

Why is the sugar shell on candied hawthorn crisp and shiny?

熬糖时水分被熬干，糖浆达到高浓度的过饱和状态，快速冷却后形成无定形的玻璃态糖壳，所以透明、硬脆、有光泽。如果回潮吸水，糖会转成晶体变得发黏不脆。控制熬糖温度和冷却速度是关键。
Boiling drives off water until the syrup is highly supersaturated, and quick cooling forms an amorphous glass sugar shell that is transparent, hard, crisp, and glossy. If it reabsorbs moisture the sugar recrystallizes and turns sticky and dull. Controlling boil temperature and cooling speed is the key.

为什么熬骨头汤加一点醋更容易溶出钙？

Why does a little vinegar help calcium leach into bone broth?

骨头里的钙多以难溶的磷酸钙形式存在，加醋提供酸性环境，把部分钙转化为可溶的醋酸钙，更易进入汤里。不过溶出量有限，汤里的钙未必比牛奶高。想补钙仍应以奶制品、豆制品为主。
Bone calcium is mostly insoluble calcium phosphate; vinegar's acidity converts some into soluble calcium acetate that enters the broth. The amount is limited, though, and the soup's calcium may still fall short of milk. Dairy and soy remain better calcium sources.

为什么玉米能爆成爆米花？

Why does corn pop into popcorn?

爆裂玉米粒内部有少量水和坚硬的种皮，加热时水变成蒸汽、压力在粒内急剧升高，到种皮承受不住就瞬间爆开，淀粉膨胀成蓬松白花。水分含量（约14%）合适才能爆得好。
Popping corn holds a little water inside a tough hull; heating turns the water to steam and pressure builds until the hull bursts, and the starch explodes into a fluffy white puff. A right moisture level (about 14%) is needed for good popping.

为什么咸鸭蛋煮熟会出油、蛋黄沙糯？

Why does a salted duck egg ooze oil with a sandy yolk?

腌渍时盐使蛋白脱水、水分向蛋黄转移，蛋黄中的脂肪球聚集并部分从蛋白质网络中释放。煮熟后脂肪熔化析出，就是看到的红油，同时蛋白变咸、蛋黄起沙。盐浓度和腌制时间决定出油多少。
During curing, salt dehydrates the white and drives water toward the yolk, where fat globules gather and partly escape the protein network. On cooking the fat melts and seeps out as the visible red oil, while the white turns salty and the yolk sandy. Salt level and curing time set how much oil appears.

为什么薯片袋鼓鼓的还充氮气？

Why are chip bags puffy and filled with nitrogen?

薯片袋充入氮气这种惰性气体，既把袋子撑起缓冲防压碎，又隔绝氧气延缓油脂氧化变哈喇。氮气安全无毒、不参加反应，所以能保鲜又保形。打开后气体跑掉，袋子就瘪了。
Chip bags are flushed with nitrogen, an inert gas that both puffs the bag to cushion and prevent crushing and excludes oxygen to slow the oil from going rancid. Nitrogen is safe, non-toxic, and unreactive, so it preserves both freshness and shape. Once opened the gas escapes and the bag goes flat.
