为什么镁条能在水上烧起来？

Why can a magnesium strip burn on water?

镁相当活泼，高温下能夺取水中的氧生成氧化镁并放出氢气，反应放热使燃烧持续，所以金属并非都怕火。
Magnesium is reactive enough to take oxygen from water at high temperature, forming MgO and H2; the exothermic reaction sustains burning—metals aren't all fireproof.

拉瓦锡为什么被称为近代化学之父？他最重要的实验是什么？

Why is Lavoisier called the father of modern chemistry, and what was his most important experiment?

拉瓦锡最重要的贡献是用精确的天平做定量实验，推翻了燃素说。他在密闭容器中加热汞，发现汞与一部分空气化合成红色粉末（氧化汞），容器中空气减少约五分之一；再把粉末加热，得到汞并放出一种气体，这种气体恰好支持燃烧和呼吸，这就是氧气。他由此确立了燃烧是物质与氧的反应，以及质量守恒定律：化学反应前后物质的总质量不变。他还规范了化学命名法，把化学从定性的猜测变成定量的科学。
Lavoisier's greatest contribution was replacing the phlogiston theory with precise, quantitative weighing. In a sealed vessel he heated mercury and found it combined with about a fifth of the air into a red powder (mercury oxide); heating that powder released a gas which supported burning and breathing - oxygen. He concluded that combustion is a reaction with oxygen and established the law of conservation of mass: the total mass of substances is unchanged by a chemical reaction. His systematic naming rules also turned chemistry from qualitative guesswork into a quantitative science.

How did Lavoisier overthrow the phlogiston theory?

为什么充分预混的烃火焰呈蓝色，而扩散燃烧或富燃料火焰发黄光？这两种颜色对应的发光过程有何本质不同？

Why does a well-premixed hydrocarbon flame look blue while a diffusion or fuel-rich flame glows yellow, and what fundamentally different processes produce these two colours?

蓝光来自化学发光：预混火焰反应区内，激发态自由基CH*、C₂*跃回基态时发出位于蓝绿区的特征谱带。黄光来自炽热烟黑：富燃料缺氧区中，烃经乙炔与多环芳烃途径聚成纳米级烟黑颗粒，被加热到约2000 K后发射近似黑体的连续谱。前者是分子尺度的电子跃迁，后者是凝相颗粒的热辐射，故黄色火焰通常意味着不完全燃烧并伴随烟黑生成。
Blue light is chemiluminescence: in the reaction zone of a premixed flame, electronically excited radicals such as CH* and C2* emit characteristic band radiation in the blue-green region as they fall back to the ground state. Yellow light comes from glowing soot: in oxygen-starved, fuel-rich pockets, hydrocarbons grow through acetylene and polycyclic-aromatic pathways into nanometre soot particles that radiate a near-blackbody continuum at about 2000 K. Molecular-scale electronic transitions versus thermal radiation from condensed particles is why a yellow flame usually signals soot formation and incomplete combustion.

同样点燃氢氧混合气，有时猛烈爆炸，有时却只缓慢氧化，由此形成的爆炸极限该如何用链支化机理来解释？

Igniting a hydrogen-oxygen mixture sometimes triggers violent explosion and sometimes only slow oxidation; how can the resulting explosion limits be explained by chain-branching kinetics?

关键在于自由基增殖与销毁之间的竞争。H• + O2 -> OH• + O•这类支化步骤一次性翻倍活性粒子；低压时自由基易扩散到器壁复合，高压时三体碰撞（H + O2 + M）又将它们消灭，只有在中间的压力窗口内支化胜出，自由基雪崩式增长引发爆炸。温度与压力会移动这一平衡，于是出现下限与上限。
The decisive factor is the race between radical multiplication and radical removal. Branching steps such as H• + O2 -> OH• + O• double the active population at a stroke; at low pressure radicals diffuse to the walls and recombine, while at high pressure three-body collisions (H + O2 + M) destroy them. Only in an intermediate pressure window does branching win, producing an explosive radical avalanche; shifts of this balance with temperature and pressure define the lower and upper limits.

Why does black powder (saltpeter, sulfur, charcoal) explode?

硝酸钾供氧，木炭作燃料，硫降低燃点并增加产气；三者混合点燃瞬间生成大量二氧化碳和氮气，体积骤然膨胀而爆。
Potassium nitrate supplies oxygen, charcoal is fuel, sulfur lowers ignition temperature and adds gas; rapid combustion yields huge volumes of CO2 and N2, causing explosion.

什么是燃烧焓？

What is enthalpy of combustion?

标准燃烧焓指 1 mol 物质在标准状态下完全燃烧生成稳定产物（如 CO₂(g)、H₂O(l)）时的焓变，通常为负值（放热）。它是评价燃料能量含量的指标。
Standard enthalpy of combustion is the enthalpy change when 1 mol of a substance burns completely to stable products (e.g., CO₂(g), H₂O(l)) under standard conditions; it is usually negative (exothermic) and measures fuel energy content.
