聚合物的玻璃化转变温度（Tg）是什么？它如何影响材料使用？

What is the glass-transition temperature (Tg) of a polymer, and how does it affect material use?

Tg 是高分子链段运动从冻结（玻璃态）到解冻（高弹态）的可逆转变温度，不是热力学相变而是动力学松弛。高于 Tg 材料变软富弹性，低于 Tg 硬而脆。Tg 受链柔性、侧基、交联与增塑剂调控，决定塑料的服役温度窗口（如 PVC 的 Tg 约 80 ℃）。
Tg is the reversible temperature at which polymer segmental motion unfreezes, switching from a glassy to a rubbery state; it is a kinetic relaxation rather than a true phase transition. Above Tg the material is soft and elastic, below it hard and brittle. Tg is tuned by chain flexibility, side groups, cross-linking, and plasticizers and sets the service-temperature window (e.g., PVC's Tg is about 80 ℃).

导电聚合物（如聚乙炔、聚苯胺）为什么能导电？掺杂起什么作用？

Why can conducting polymers such as polyacetylene and polyaniline conduct electricity, and what role does doping play?

导电聚合物主链为共轭 π 体系，π 电子离域形成可移动的载流子通道。本征态导电性差，经氧化（p 型）或还原（n 型）掺杂后，在能隙中引入极化子/双极化子能级并产生载流子，电导率可提升数个数量级达到金属范围。掺杂是非化学计量的电荷转移过程。
Conducting polymers have a conjugated π backbone with delocalized π electrons that form channels for mobile carriers. In the pristine state they conduct poorly; oxidative (p-type) or reductive (n-type) doping introduces polaron/bipolaron levels within the gap and creates carriers, raising conductivity by orders of magnitude into the metallic range. Doping is a non-stoichiometric charge-transfer process.

两亲性分子是怎样自组装成胶束和囊泡的？驱动力是什么？

How do amphiphilic molecules self-assemble into micelles and vesicles, and what drives it?

两亲分子含亲水头与疏水尾，在水中为降低疏水尾与水的不利接触，自发排列成疏水核、亲水壳的结构。浓度超过临界胶束浓度（CMC）后形成胶束，双链两亲体可进一步封闭成双层囊泡。驱动力是疏水效应与水合熵增，几何填充参数决定最终形貌。
Amphiphiles have hydrophilic heads and hydrophobic tails; to minimize unfavorable contact of the tails with water they spontaneously arrange into structures with a hydrophobic core and hydrophilic shell. Above the critical micelle concentration (CMC) they form micelles, and double-tailed amphiphiles can close into bilayer vesicles. The driving force is the hydrophobic effect and associated water entropy gain, while the packing parameter sets the final morphology.

橡胶为什么要硫化？硫化改变了什么性能？

Why is rubber vulcanized, and what properties does vulcanization change?

天然橡胶是线性的聚异戊二烯，室温下发黏、易冷流且强度低。硫化用硫在分子链间形成共价交联（二硫/多硫桥），限制链滑移，使橡胶获得弹性、强度、耐热与耐溶剂性。适度交联是橡胶从黏流态变为可用弹性体的关键。
Natural rubber is linear polyisoprene that is sticky, cold-flowing, and weak at room temperature. Vulcanization uses sulfur to form covalent cross-links (disulfide/polysulfide bridges) between chains, restricting chain slippage and giving rubber elasticity, strength, heat, and solvent resistance. Proper cross-linking is what turns sticky gum into a useful elastomer.

聚乳酸（PLA）为什么被认为是一种可降解塑料？它在什么条件下能降解？

Why is polylactic acid (PLA) considered a degradable plastic, and under what conditions does it degrade?

PLA 是由乳酸缩聚或丙交酯开环聚合得到的可再生资源基聚酯，主链含易水解的酯键。在工业堆肥的高温高湿与微生物条件下，酯键被水解与酶解断链，最终生成乳酸并矿化。但在自然海洋或填埋环境中降解极慢，因此仍需配套堆肥设施。
PLA is a renewable-resource polyester made by polycondensation of lactic acid or ring-opening polymerization of lactide, with hydrolyzable ester linkages in the backbone. Under industrial composting—high temperature, humidity, and microbes—the esters are cleaved by hydrolysis and enzymes, eventually yielding lactic acid that mineralizes. In natural marine or landfill settings it degrades very slowly, so composting infrastructure is still required.

聚乙烯是怎样通过聚合反应制得的？高密度与低密度聚乙烯有何区别？

How is polyethylene made by polymerization, and how do HDPE and LDPE differ?

乙烯在催化剂（如 Ziegler–Natta 或茂金属）作用下发生加聚，打开双键连接成—CH₂—CH₂—长链。低密度聚乙烯（LDPE）由高压自由基聚合得到支化结构、较软透明；高密度聚乙烯（HDPE）在低压配位催化下链规整、支化少、强度高。二者密度与结晶度差异决定用途。
Ethylene undergoes addition polymerization under catalysts such as Ziegler–Natta or metallocene, opening its double bond to link into long —CH₂—CH₂— chains. Low-density polyethylene (LDPE) comes from high-pressure radical polymerization, giving branched, softer, transparent material; high-density polyethylene (HDPE) uses low-pressure coordination catalysis for more linear, less branched, stronger chains. Their density and crystallinity differences dictate applications.

尼龙等聚酰胺是通过什么反应合成的？这类反应有什么特点？

By what reaction are polyamides such as nylon synthesized, and what are its features?

尼龙等聚酰胺通常由二胺与二羧酸（或其酰氯）的缩聚反应制得，每形成一个酰胺键脱去一分子水（或 HCl）。该逐步聚合的产物分子量随反应程度提高而增长，需通过除去小分子、提高转化率来获得高分子量。己二胺与己二酸缩聚得到尼龙-66。
Polyamides such as nylon are made by step-growth polycondensation of a diamine with a diacid (or its diacid chloride), eliminating one water (or HCl) molecule per amide bond. In this step-growth process molecular weight grows as conversion increases, so high molecular weight requires removing the small molecule to drive the reaction. Hexamethylenediamine with adipic acid gives nylon-66.
