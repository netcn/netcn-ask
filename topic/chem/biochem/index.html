Why does organophosphate poisoning cause convulsions?

血液为什么是红色的？

Why is blood red?

血液的红来自血红素：亚铁离子坐在卟啉四个吡咯氮围成的平面中心，这套共轭体系把强吸收带压在蓝紫到蓝绿光区，蓝光被吸走，透出的便是红光。氧结合到铁上会改变配位场与卟啉的电子结构，于是氧合血亮红、缺氧血暗红——动脉与静脉的色差由此而来。至于静脉发蓝是错觉：血只是变暗，青紫来自皮肤对光的滤过；血浆本身近乎淡黄。
Blood is red because of heme: an iron(II) ion sits in the flat centre held by the four pyrrole nitrogens of the porphyrin, and this conjugated system absorbs strongly from the blue-violet into the blue-green, so the blue is taken out and what comes through is red. Adding oxygen to the iron changes its ligand field and the electronic structure of the porphyrin, which is why oxygenated blood is bright red and oxygen-poor blood dark red; that is the difference between arterial and venous colour. That veins "look blue" is an illusion - the blood has only darkened, and the bluish cast comes from light filtering through skin; plasma itself is nearly pale yellow.

咖啡因为什么能让人提神？

Why does caffeine keep you awake?

腺苷是清醒时在大脑里不断累积的信号，一坐上腺苷受体就压低觉醒神经元、催促入睡。咖啡因与腺苷同属甲基黄嘌呤、形状相近，于是抢先占住受体的位子却不按下开关，把腺苷挡在门外，困意信号传不进去。它是拮抗剂而不是燃料，并不减少腺苷，只是让受体收不到。这占位可逆，咖啡因被代谢后积压的腺苷照样生效，人就突然困倒；长期摄入还让受体数目上调，带来耐受与停喝后的头痛。
Adenosine is a signal that piles up in the brain through the waking hours, and once it sits on an adenosine receptor it damps the arousal neurons and pushes you toward sleep. Caffeine is a methylxanthine like adenosine and close enough in shape to take the seat on the receptor first without switching it on, shutting adenosine out so the drowsiness signal never lands. It is an antagonist, not a fuel: it does not reduce the adenosine, it only keeps the receptor from receiving it. That occupation is reversible, so the adenosine that has built up lands as soon as the caffeine is metabolized and the sleepiness crashes in; with regular intake the number of receptors is also upregulated, which is where tolerance and the headache after stopping come from.

为什么 ATP 被称为能量货币？

Why is ATP called energy currency?

ATP 末端的三磷酸链上负电荷彼此排斥；水解成 ADP 与磷酸根后排斥解除、产物溶剂化更好，自由能下降，这才是细胞支取的能量。它叫货币是因为既不存能也不干活，只居中介：氧化燃料供能把 ADP 再磷酸化，ATP 再交出磷酸基团推动运输、运动和合成。但要注意断键只吸热，「高能键放能」是简写；它也不是储备，只够几秒剧烈活动。
The negative charges on ATP's triphosphate tail repel one another; hydrolyzing it to ADP and phosphate relieves that repulsion and leaves the products better solvated, so free energy drops, and that drop is what a cell spends. It is a currency because it neither stores energy nor does work, sitting in the middle instead: energy from oxidizing fuel re-phosphorylates ADP, and ATP hands a phosphate group onward to drive transport, motion and synthesis. Breaking any bond only absorbs heat, so "the high-energy bond releases energy" is shorthand for more stable products, and ATP is no reserve—it covers seconds of hard work.

萤火虫发光的化学原理是什么？

What is the chemical basis of firefly luminescence?

萤火虫的光来自化学发光：荧光素酶催化荧光素与氧气反应，生成处于激发态的氧化荧光素，这个分子回到基态时放出约550到580纳米的黄绿色光。反应几乎不产生热量，被称为冷光，能量转换效率非常高。
Firefly light comes from chemiluminescence: the enzyme luciferase catalyzes luciferin reacting with oxygen to form excited oxyluciferin, which emits yellow-green light around 550-580 nanometers as it returns to the ground state. The reaction produces almost no heat - it is cold light - and converts chemical energy into light with remarkable efficiency.

肽键是怎样形成的？为什么有平面性？

How does a peptide bond form, and why is it planar?

氨基酸的羧基与另一氨基酸的氨基脱水缩合形成酰胺键（—CO—NH—）。由于共振，C—N 具部分双键特征，使肽单元呈刚性平面、不能自由旋转，这是蛋白质二级结构的基础。
Amino acid carboxyl and amino groups condense with loss of water to form an amide linkage (—CO—NH—). Resonance gives the C—N bond partial double-bond character, making the peptide unit a rigid planar group that cannot rotate freely—the basis of protein secondary structure.

How do enzyme catalysts differ from inorganic catalysts?

Enzymes are highly specific biomacromolecules that, under mild conditions, greatly accelerate reactions by lowering activation energy and are regulated by substrate structure, pH, temperature, and inhibitors; inorganic catalysts are generally broader, harsher, and less prone to denaturation.

蛋白质变性是什么意思？

What does protein denaturation mean?

变性是蛋白质的三维空间结构（二级及以上）被热、酸、酒精或机械作用破坏，失去生物活性，但一级肽链通常不断裂。例如煮蛋时蛋清由透明变白凝固就是变性。
Denaturation is the disruption of a protein's 3D structure (secondary and above) by heat, acid, alcohol, or agitation, losing biological activity while the primary peptide chain usually stays intact. Boiling an egg, where clear albumin turns white and solid, is denaturation.
