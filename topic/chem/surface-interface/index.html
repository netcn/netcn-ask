肥皂为什么能洗掉油污？

Why does soap remove grease?

肥皂是脂肪酸盐，分子一端亲水（羧酸根），另一端是长链疏水烃基。在水中，疏水端插入油滴、亲水端留在水里，形成胶束把油污包在内部，使油污被水冲走。胶束外壳亲水、内核疏水，油污因此能稳定地悬浮在水中。
Soap is a fatty-acid salt with a hydrophilic carboxylate head and a long hydrophobic hydrocarbon tail. In water, the tails embed in grease while the heads stay in the water, building micelles that encapsulate the oil so it can be rinsed away. Because the micelle has a hydrophilic shell and hydrophobic interior, the grease stays suspended in water.

乳化剂为什么能让油和水混合？

Why can emulsifiers mix oil and water?

乳化剂是两性分子，亲水头伸入水相、亲油尾伸入油相，降低油水界面张力并在液滴表面形成膜，阻止其聚并，从而稳定乳液（如蛋黄中的卵磷脂稳定蛋黄酱）。
Emulsifiers are amphiphilic: hydrophilic heads face water and lipophilic tails face oil, lowering interfacial tension and forming a film around droplets that prevents coalescence, stabilizing emulsions (e.g., lecithin in egg yolk stabilizes mayonnaise).

接触角滞后现象是如何产生的,前进角和后退角之差反映了界面的什么性质?

What causes contact angle hysteresis, and what does the difference between advancing and receding angles reflect about an interface?

当液体在三相线上移动时,表面化学不均匀性和微米/纳米级粗糙度会钉扎三相线,使润湿存在多个亚稳态。缓慢加液测得的前进角对应能量势垒较高的一侧,缓慢抽液测得的后退角对应另一侧,两者之差即滞后。滞后越大说明表面越"脏"或粗糙度梯度越大,因此它常被用作表面均匀性与洁净度的敏感判据。
As the three-phase contact line moves, chemical heterogeneity and micro/nanoscale roughness pin it, creating many metastable wetting states. Slowly adding liquid yields the advancing angle on the high-barrier side; slowly withdrawing gives the receding angle on the opposite side, and their gap is the hysteresis. Larger hysteresis signals greater contamination or roughness gradients, so it serves as a sensitive probe of surface uniformity and cleanliness.

水滴在荷叶上滚动时,前缘和后缘的接触角并不相同,这背后是什么界面现象?

When a water droplet rolls across a lotus leaf, its leading and trailing edges meet the surface at different angles — what interfacial phenomenon lies behind this?

前进角大于后退角,两者之差称为接触角滞后,来源于表面化学组成的不均一与微纳粗糙结构对三相接触线的钉扎。在Cassie-Baxter复合润湿态下,截留的空气进一步放大这种前后不对称。滞后足够小时液滴便能轻易滚离并携带尘粒,这就是自清洁的界面学基础。
The advancing angle exceeds the receding angle, and their gap is the contact angle hysteresis, rooted in chemical heterogeneity plus pinning of the three-phase contact line by micro/nano roughness. In the Cassie-Baxter composite state the trapped air amplifies this fore-aft asymmetry further. Once hysteresis is small enough, drops roll off readily and carry dust away — the interfacial basis of self-cleaning.

往水里加一点点十二烷基硫酸钠,表面张力就骤降,可继续加却几乎不再变化,这条曲线的转折点意味着什么?

A pinch of sodium dodecyl sulfate slashes the surface tension of water, yet adding more barely moves it afterward — what does the turning point on that curve signify?

两亲分子优先占据气液界面:硫酸根基团留在水中,十二条碳的长尾探向空气,替换掉一部分表面能很高的裸露水面,Gibbs吸附等温式定量描述了这一界面富集过程。当浓度抵达临界胶束浓度(约8 mmol/L),界面已经饱和,新增分子只能转入体相聚集成胶束,表面张力随之走平。胶束同时充当油污的容器,这正是洗涤剂增溶作用的出发点。
Amphiphiles crowd preferentially onto the air-water interface: sulfate heads stay submerged while twelve-carbon tails reach for air, displacing bare high-energy water surface, a process Gibbs adsorption isotherms quantify. Once concentration reaches the critical micelle concentration near 8 millimolar the interface saturates, newcomers can only aggregate into bulk micelles, and surface tension flattens out. Those micelles double as containers for oily soil — the starting point of detergent solubilization.

铝比铁活泼得多，为什么日常生活中铝制品却几乎不生锈？

Since aluminum is far more reactive than iron, why does it hardly ever corrode in daily life?

因为铝有很强的钝化本领。新暴露的铝一接触空气便立即与氧化合，生成仅几纳米厚的致密氧化铝膜，它结合牢固、无缝隙地盖住表面，切断氧气和水汽进入内部的通道，使腐蚀停留在表层；即使被划伤，膜也会在空气中迅速重建。不锈钢抗锈同理，靠铬形成富铬钝化膜。而铁的锈层疏松多孔，无法隔离内部，腐蚀才不断向深处推进。
Aluminum protects itself with a passive film. The moment freshly exposed metal meets air it oxidizes into an alumina layer only a few nanometers thick, which clings tightly and covers the surface completely, denying oxygen and moisture any path to the metal underneath; scratched spots repair themselves almost at once. Stainless steel works the same way through a chromium-rich passive film. Ordinary iron, by contrast, grows loose porous rust that shields nothing, so the attack digs ever deeper.

肥皂泡为什么是圆的,还会飘在天上不掉下来?

Why are soap bubbles round and floating in the air?

泡泡皮想让自己最省力,而圆球形用的「皮」最少、装得气最多,所以泡泡总是圆圆的。它那么轻,风一吹就飘起来了。对着它轻轻看,还能看见彩虹的颜色在上面流动呢!
Bubble skin wants the laziest shape: a sphere uses least skin for most air, so bubbles stay round. They weigh almost nothing so breezes carry them up. Look closely and rainbow colors swim across the surface!
