二氧化钛光催化为什么通常要掺杂或复合改性才能利用可见光?

Why does TiO2 photocatalysis usually need doping or coupling with other materials to use visible light?

锐钛矿 TiO2 的带隙约 3.2 eV,只能吸收波长小于 387 nm 的紫外光,而这部分只占太阳光谱的很小份额。掺氮、沉积贵金属或与窄带隙半导体异质结后,可以在带隙中引入杂质能级或构建 Z 型/II 型电荷转移通道,把响应边推到可见区。代价往往是引入的缺陷成为复合中心,所以活性和稳定性的平衡需要精细调控。
Anatase TiO2 has a band gap near 3.2 eV, absorbing only UV light below about 387 nm — a small slice of the solar spectrum. Nitrogen doping, noble-metal deposition, or heterojunctions with narrow-gap semiconductors introduce impurity levels or Z-scheme/Type-II charge-transfer pathways that push absorption into the visible range. The trade-off is that such defects can act as recombination centers, so activity and stability must be balanced carefully.

光催化全分解水为什么必须同时具备产氢产氧位点,单一助催化剂负载为何常常失败?

Why must overall water-splitting photocatalysts provide both hydrogen- and oxygen-evolution sites, and why does loading a single co-catalyst often fail?

全分解水要求四个空穴氧化水放氧、两个电子还原质子放氢在同一颗粒上同步进行,否则积累的电荷会通过复合或副反应耗散。仅负载 Pt 只加速放氢,空穴端缺乏有效抽取位点,表面会积聚过氧化物并发生逆向反应,氧气难以释放且效率骤降。采用 RhCrOx、CoOx 等双组分或选择性分别担载两种助催化剂,可以抑制逆反应并打通两个半反应,这正是 Domen 组 SrTiO3:Al 体系实现接近 100% 表观量子效率的关键设计。
Overall splitting demands four holes oxidize water to O2 while two electrons reduce protons to H2 concurrently on the same particle; otherwise accumulated charge dissipates via recombination or side reactions. Depositing Pt alone accelerates HER but leaves no extraction site for holes, peroxide species accumulate, back reactions consume the gases, and efficiency collapses. Dual components such as RhCrOx or CoOx, or site-selective loading of two cocatalysts, suppress reverse reactions and complete both half-reactions — the key design behind Domen's SrTiO3:Al system reaching nearly 100% apparent quantum yield.

#全分解水 #助催化剂

二氧化钛悬浮液在光照下明明产生了大量电子-空穴对,降解污染物的效率却常常很低,卡点在哪里?

Irradiated TiO2 slurries generate plenty of electron-hole pairs, yet pollutant degradation stays sluggish — where is the bottleneck?

光生载流子在皮秒到纳秒量级就完成体内复合,把能量以热或辐射形式白白耗散,而它们迁移到表面参与反应需要的时间窗口要长得多。只有少数空穴成功氧化水和氢氧根生成羟基自由基等活性氧物种,才真正启动矿化。沉积Pt捕集电子、掺杂引入缺陷能级、构筑异质结形成内建电场,都是延长载流子寿命的经典手段。
Photogenerated carriers recombine in the bulk within picoseconds to nanoseconds, dissipating their energy as heat or emission, while reaching the surface to react demands a far longer window. Only the minority of holes that oxidize water and hydroxide into hydroxyl radicals and related reactive oxygen species actually start mineralization. Depositing Pt to trap electrons, doping to introduce defect levels, and building heterojunctions with internal fields are the classic ways to stretch carrier lifetimes.
