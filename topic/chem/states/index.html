为什么有时热水比冷水结冰更快？

Why can hot water sometimes freeze faster than cold?

姆潘巴效应指特定条件下热水可能先结冰，可能与蒸发失水、对流、溶解气体差异有关，机制仍有争议，依赖具体条件。
The Mpemba effect: under some conditions hot water freezes faster, possibly via evaporation, convection, or dissolved-gas differences; the mechanism is debated and condition-dependent.

为什么氦在常压下再冷也不结成固体？

Why won't helium solidify no matter how cold at 1 atm?

量子零点能很大，让氦原子在1大气压下无法排成晶格，故不固化，必须加压才可能成固；液氦还能呈现超流动性。
Large quantum zero-point energy keeps helium atoms from locking into a lattice at 1 atm, so it won't solidify without pressure; liquid helium also shows superfluidity.

杠杆规则怎么读二元相图？

How does the lever rule read a binary phase diagram?

在两相区的某温度下，两相的比例由连接线按“杠杆”反比分割，靠近哪一端则哪一相更多。
At a temperature in the two-phase region, phase fractions split inversely along the tie-line like a lever; the nearer end gives the larger phase amount.

为什么微波加热纯水可能突然炸开？

Why might microwaved pure water suddenly burst?

水可过热到沸点以上却无气泡（无凝结核），受到扰动时剧烈暴沸喷出，放一根木勺提供气泡核可防。
Water can superheat above boiling with no bubbles (no nuclei); disturbance causes violent bumping. A wooden spoon provides nucleation sites.

水的相图有什么特别之处？

What is special about water's phase diagram?

固-液线向左倾斜，因为冰比水轻（密度反常），加压反而使冰熔化；三相点在约 0.01°C、611 Pa。
The solid-liquid line slopes left because ice is less dense than water (density anomaly), so pressure melts ice; the triple point is ~0.01°C, 611 Pa.

为什么很纯的水在冰箱可低于0°C仍不结冰？

Why can very pure water stay liquid below 0 C in a freezer?

缺少凝结核时，液态水能过冷到冰点以下仍保持液态；一旦投入冰晶或震动，瞬间大量结晶。
Without nucleation sites, pure water can supercool below 0 C and stay liquid; a seed crystal or shake triggers sudden freezing.

为什么干冰不化成水而是冒白烟消失？

Why does dry ice vanish with white smoke instead of melting to water?

干冰是固体二氧化碳，常压下直接升华成CO2气体，周围冷气使空气中水汽凝结成白雾，看起来像冒烟，其实那白雾是水不是CO2。
Dry ice is solid CO2; at room pressure it sublimes to gas, and the cold makes ambient water vapor condense into white fog—the smoke is water, not CO2.

为什么樟脑丸放着会变小却不留水渍？

Why do mothballs shrink without leaving liquid?

樟脑（或萘）在室温下会升华，直接从固体变成气体防蛀，所以变小却不见液体；但萘有一定毒性，现多用替代物。
Camphor (or naphthalene) sublimes at room temperature—solid to gas—so it shrinks without liquid; naphthalene is somewhat toxic, now often replaced.

水在0度结冰、100度沸腾,这个数字是永远不变的吗?

Does water always freeze at zero and boil at one hundred — are those numbers fixed forever?

只在标准大气压下成立。在青藏高原,气压低,水八九十度就沸腾了,所以高原煮饭要用高压锅强行加压;高压锅里气压可达两个大气压,水温能到120度,炖肉更快。冰点也随压力微变。化学课本上的常数都附带标准状况这个小字条款——条件变了,数字跟着变。
Only at standard atmospheric pressure. On the Tibetan Plateau low pressure boils water at eighty or ninety degrees, hence pressure cookers forcing higher pressure so water reaches 120 degrees for faster stews. Freezing shifts slightly with pressure too. Textbook constants carry fine print called standard conditions — change conditions, numbers move.
