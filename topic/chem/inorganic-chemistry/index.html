二茂铁为什么在当年是革命性的分子？

Why was ferrocene a revolutionary molecule in its day?

二茂铁由两个平行环戊二烯负离子夹一个 Fe²⁺，是首个被确认的“夹心”金属有机配合物，颠覆了人们对成键的认识。Kealy 与 Pauson 1951 年合成，Wilkinson 随后阐明其结构并因此获 1973 年诺奖。
Ferrocene sandwiches an Fe²⁺ between two parallel cyclopentadienyl anions, the first confirmed 'sandwich' metallocene, overturning ideas about bonding. Kealy and Pauson synthesized it in 1951; Wilkinson clarified its structure and won the 1973 Nobel Prize.

什么是硼烷的“缺电子”特性？它们靠什么特殊的化学键成键？

What does it mean that boranes are 'electron-deficient,' and what special type of chemical bond holds them together?

硼烷（如乙硼烷 B2H6）中价电子总数少于形成经典二中心二电子键（2c-2e）所需的数目，因此称为缺电子化合物。它们通过三中心二电子键（3c-2e，如桥氢键）成键，其多面体结构可由韦德规则（Wade's rules）预测。
In boranes such as diborane (B2H6), the total number of valence electrons is insufficient to form ordinary two-center two-electron bonds, making them electron-deficient. They are stabilized by three-center two-electron bonds (3c-2e, e.g. bridging B-H-B hydrogens), and their polyhedral structures are predicted by Wade's rules.

稀有气体真的完全不反应吗？

Are noble gases truly completely unreactive?

稀有气体外层电子全满，化学性质极不活泼，但并非绝对不反应。1962 年巴特利特首次合成六氟合铂酸氙，此后人们制得了 XeF₂、XeF₄、XeO₃ 等多种氙化合物。较重稀有气体（如氙、氪）在强氧化条件下可形成化合物。
Noble gases have full valence shells and are extremely inert, but not absolutely unreactive. In 1962 Bartlett first synthesized xenon hexafluoroplatinate, and later compounds such as XeF₂, XeF₄, and XeO₃ were made. Heavier noble gases like xenon and krypton can form compounds under strong oxidizing conditions.

过渡金属化合物为什么常有颜色？

Why are transition-metal compounds often colored?

过渡金属离子未充满的 d 轨道在配体场中分裂，电子吸收可见光在不同 d 能级间跃迁，于是透射或反射出特定颜色。Cotton 与 Wilkinson 在《高等无机化学》中系统论述了这一 d-d 跃迁。
In transition-metal ions the partially filled d orbitals split in a ligand field, and electrons absorb visible light jumping between these d levels, giving the compound its color. Cotton and Wilkinson treat this d-d transition in 'Advanced Inorganic Chemistry'.

什么是两性氧化物？举一例。

What is an amphoteric oxide? Give an example.

两性氧化物既能与酸反应又能与碱反应生成盐和水，典型例子是氧化铝（Al₂O₃）。它与盐酸生成氯化铝，与氢氧化钠生成偏铝酸钠。这类性质在工业提纯金属和陶瓷中很有用。
An amphoteric oxide reacts with both acids and bases to form salts and water; a classic example is aluminum oxide (Al₂O₃). It forms aluminum chloride with HCl and sodium aluminate with NaOH. Such behavior is useful in metal refining and ceramics.

为什么氢氟酸（HF）这么弱，尽管氟电负性最强？

Why is hydrofluoric acid (HF) so weak despite fluorine's high electronegativity?

HF 的 H-F 键极短极强，在水中难以解离，且解离出的 F⁻ 与未解离 HF 形成强氢键二聚体 HF₂⁻，抑制进一步电离。因此它是弱酸，而 HCl、HBr 等反而强得多。
The H-F bond is very short and strong, so HF barely dissociates in water, and the F⁻ formed hydrogen-bonds with undissociated HF into HF₂⁻, suppressing further ionization. Thus HF is a weak acid while HCl and HBr are far stronger.

硅酸盐矿物最基本的结构单元是什么？石英、云母和石棉在结构上有何联系？

What is the fundamental building unit of silicate minerals, and how are quartz, mica, and asbestos structurally related?

硅酸盐的基本结构单元是硅氧四面体（SiO4），其中硅位于四面体中心、氧位于顶点。这些四面体可通过共用氧原子连接成孤立岛状、链状、片状或三维网络结构，云母为层状、石棉为链状、石英为三维骨架，差异源于四面体的聚合方式。
The fundamental building block of silicates is the silicon-oxygen tetrahedron (SiO4), with silicon at the center and oxygen at the corners. These tetrahedra share oxygen atoms to form isolated, chain, sheet, or three-dimensional framework structures; mica is sheet-like, asbestos is chain-like, and quartz is a 3D network, differing in how the tetrahedra polymerize.

水分子为什么是弯曲的而不是直线形？

Why is a water molecule bent rather than linear?

氧原子有两对孤对电子，按价层电子对互斥理论（VSEPR），孤对-成键对之间的排斥大于成键对之间，把两个 O-H 键压成约 104.5° 的夹角。Gillespie 与 Nyholm 在 1957 年系统提出 VSEPR 模型。
Oxygen carries two lone pairs; by VSEPR theory lone-pair/bond-pair repulsion exceeds bond-pair/bond-pair repulsion, pushing the two O-H bonds to about 104.5°. Gillespie and Nyholm set out the VSEPR model in 1957.

硼砂是什么？它有哪些主要用途？

What is borax and what are its main uses?

硼砂是四硼酸钠的十水合物（Na₂B₄O₇·10H₂O），是一种天然硼酸盐矿物。它熔点低、能助熔，传统上用于陶瓷釉料、玻璃和清洁剂，并在分析化学中作为基准物质配制缓冲溶液。过量摄入对人体有害，因此食品用途受限。
Borax is the decahydrate of sodium tetraborate (Na₂B₄O₇·10H₂O), a natural borate mineral. With a low melting point and fluxing ability, it is used in ceramics glazes, glass, and detergents, and as a primary standard in analytical chemistry. Excessive ingestion is harmful, so its food uses are restricted.

为什么1962年之前化学家认为稀有气体（又称惰性气体）几乎不形成化合物？人类合成的第一种稀有气体化合物是什么？

Why did chemists before 1962 believe the noble gases (then called inert gases) formed almost no compounds, and what was the first noble-gas compound synthesized by humans?

稀有气体原子的最外层具有稳定的八电子构型，电离能极高，传统上被认为化学惰性、难以成键。1962年，尼尔·巴特利特（Neil Bartlett）用强氧化剂六氟化铂（PtF6）与氙反应，首次制得了六氟合铂酸氙（XePtF6），开创了稀有气体化学。
Noble-gas atoms have a stable octet in their outer shell and extremely high ionization energies, so they were long considered chemically inert and unable to bond. In 1962 Neil Bartlett reacted xenon with the powerful oxidizer platinum hexafluoride (PtF6) and produced xenon hexafluoroplatinate (XePtF6), the first synthesized noble-gas compound.

哈伯—博施法（Haber-Bosch process）是如何将空气中的氮转化为氨的？它对人类有何重大意义？

How does the Haber-Bosch process convert nitrogen from the air into ammonia, and why is it historically significant?

哈伯—博施法在高温高压（约400–500℃、150–300 atm）及铁基催化剂下，使氮气与氢气直接化合生成氨（N2 + 3H2 ⇌ 2NH3）。该方法使大规模合成氮肥成为可能，极大提高了粮食产量，弗里茨·哈伯因此获1918年诺贝尔化学奖。
The Haber-Bosch process combines nitrogen and hydrogen directly into ammonia (N2 + 3H2 ⇌ 2NH3) at high temperature and pressure (about 400–500 ℃, 150–300 atm) over an iron-based catalyst. It enabled the mass production of nitrogen fertilizers and dramatically raised food output; Fritz Haber received the 1918 Nobel Prize in Chemistry for it.

现代炼钢（碱性氧气转炉）为什么要加石灰(CaO)？

Why is lime (CaO) added in modern steelmaking (basic oxygen furnace)?

生铁含磷、硅、硫等杂质。石灰与它们反应生成炉渣浮在钢水表面被除去，从而把生铁炼成性能合格的钢。
Hot metal contains P, Si, S impurities. Lime reacts with them to form slag that floats off, turning pig iron into specification steel.

平流层臭氧是如何形成的？它有什么作用？

How does stratospheric ozone form and what does it do?

平流层中紫外线把氧气分子（O₂）光解为氧原子，氧原子再与 O₂ 结合生成臭氧（O₃）。臭氧层吸收大部分有害的紫外-B 和紫外-C 辐射，保护地表生命免受 DNA 损伤。人类排放的氟氯烃曾严重破坏该层，后被《蒙特利尔议定书》管控。
In the stratosphere, UV light splits O₂ into oxygen atoms, which then combine with O₂ to form ozone (O₃). The ozone layer absorbs most harmful UV-B and UV-C radiation, protecting surface life from DNA damage. Human-emitted CFCs once severely depleted it and were curbed by the Montreal Protocol.

碳元素有哪些常见的同素异形体？它们的性质为什么差别那么大？

What are the common allotropes of carbon, and why do their properties differ so greatly?

碳的常见同素异形体包括金刚石、石墨、富勒烯、碳纳米管和石墨烯等。它们都由碳原子构成，但因原子连接方式（sp³、sp²杂化及成键网络）不同，导致硬度、导电性等性质天差地别——金刚石极硬且不导电，石墨质软且能导电。
Common allotropes of carbon include diamond, graphite, fullerenes, carbon nanotubes, and graphene. Although all are made of carbon atoms, their vastly different connectivities (sp³ vs sp² hybridization and bonding networks) give them very different properties—diamond is extremely hard and insulating, while graphite is soft and conductive.

什么是金属活动性顺序？它有什么用途？

What is the metal activity series and what is it used for?

金属活动性顺序是按金属失去电子被氧化的难易程度排列的序列，钾、钠、钙等在最前，金、铂在最后。它可预测置换反应能否发生，例如活泼金属能把较不活泼金属的离子从溶液中置换出来。这也是电镀和防腐设计的依据。
The metal activity (reactivity) series ranks metals by how readily they lose electrons and are oxidized, with potassium, sodium, and calcium at the top and gold and platinum at the bottom. It predicts displacement reactions, e.g., a more active metal can displace a less active one's ions from solution. It also guides electroplating and corrosion protection.
