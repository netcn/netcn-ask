什么是放热反应和吸热反应？

What are exothermic and endothermic reactions?

放热反应在过程中向外界释放热量，如燃烧和大多数中和反应。吸热反应则需要从外界吸收热量才能进行，如光合作用。
Exothermic reactions release heat to the surroundings, such as combustion and most neutralization reactions. Endothermic reactions absorb heat from the surroundings, such as photosynthesis.

什么是质量守恒定律？

What is the law of conservation of mass?

质量守恒定律指出，在化学反应中参加反应的各物质总质量等于反应后生成物的总质量。这是因为反应只是原子重新组合，原子本身不增不减。
The law of conservation of mass states that in a chemical reaction the total mass of reactants equals the total mass of products. This is because the reaction only rearranges atoms without creating or destroying them.

什么是化学反应？

What is a chemical reaction?

化学反应是物质发生变化、生成新物质的过程，反应前后原子的种类和数目不变。例如氢气燃烧生成水就是一种化学反应。
A chemical reaction is a process in which substances change and new substances are formed, while the types and numbers of atoms stay the same. For example, hydrogen burning to form water is a chemical reaction.

燃烧需要什么条件？

What conditions are needed for burning?

燃烧通常需要可燃物、氧气（或助燃物）和足够高的温度三者同时存在。缺少了其中任何一个，火就会熄灭。
Burning usually requires three things at once: a fuel, oxygen or another supporter of combustion, and enough heat. Remove any one of them and the fire goes out.

化学反应通常有哪些类型？

What are the common types of chemical reactions?

常见的反应类型有化合、分解、置换和复分解反应。化合是多种物质生成一种，分解是一种物质分成多种，置换是单质替换化合物中的元素，复分解是两种化合物互相交换成分。
Common reaction types include combination, decomposition, single replacement, and double replacement. Combination joins substances into one, decomposition splits one into many, single replacement swaps an element in a compound, and double replacement exchanges parts between two compounds.

蜡烛燃烧是化学反应吗？

Is burning a candle a chemical reaction?

是的。蜡烛的蜡与空气中的氧气反应，生成二氧化碳和水，同时放出光和热。因为产生了与原来不同的新物质，所以属于化学反应。
Yes. The wax of a candle reacts with oxygen in the air to form carbon dioxide and water while releasing light and heat. Because new substances different from the original are produced, it is a chemical reaction.
