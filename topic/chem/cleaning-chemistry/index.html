为什么含氯漂白剂能让衣服变白？

Why do chlorine bleaches whiten clothes?

次氯酸钠在水中生成次氯酸，能氧化破坏色素分子的发色基团，使有色污渍褪色。它也能杀灭细菌和病毒。但会损伤丝绸、羊毛等蛋白质纤维，并可能使某些染料掉色。
Sodium hypochlorite forms hypochlorous acid in water, which oxidizes and destroys the chromophores—the color-bearing groups—of pigment molecules, fading stains. It also kills bacteria and viruses. But it damages protein fibers like silk and wool and can strip some dyes.

为什么银器发黑能用铝箔加盐和热水加小苏打复原？

Why can you restore tarnished silver with aluminum foil, salt, baking soda, and hot water?

银表面黑斑是硫化银，把它和铝箔一起泡在加盐和小苏打的热水中，构成原电池：铝比银活泼作负极被氧化，硫化银在正极被还原回银，黑色消失。这比打磨更保护银器，不伤表面。
The black on silver is silver sulfide; soaking it with aluminum foil in hot salted baking-soda water sets up a galvanic cell: aluminum, more reactive, oxidizes at the negative side while silver sulfide is reduced back to metallic silver at the positive side, removing the black. It protects the silver better than abrasive polishing.

为什么洗衣液比肥皂更适合机洗？

Why is laundry detergent better than soap for machines?

洗衣液是高浓度液体表面活性剂，在硬水和冷水中依然稳定、起泡少、易漂洗。肥皂在硬水中易与钙镁形成不溶皂垢，附着在衣物上发灰。洗衣液还常含酶，能分解蛋白、淀粉污渍。
Laundry liquid is a concentrated surfactant that stays stable and low-sudsing in hard or cold water and rinses easily. Soap forms insoluble scum with calcium and magnesium in hard water, leaving a gray film on clothes. Detergents also often contain enzymes that break down protein and starch stains.

为什么84消毒液不能和洁厕灵混用？

Why must you never mix 84 disinfectant with toilet cleaner?

84消毒液含次氯酸钠，洁厕灵含盐酸，两者混合会生成有毒的氯气。氯气刺激呼吸道，高浓度可致人中毒甚至危险。任何含氯消毒剂都不要与酸性清洁剂混用。
84 disinfectant contains sodium hypochlorite and toilet cleaner contains hydrochloric acid; together they release toxic chlorine gas. Chlorine irritates the airways and at high concentration can be dangerous. Never combine any chlorine bleach with acidic cleaners.

为什么在硬水里用肥皂不容易起泡，还会留下一层"皂垢"？

Why does soap lather poorly in hard water and leave a film of "scum"?

硬水中富含钙、镁离子，它们会与肥皂的脂肪酸钠反应生成不溶于水的钙/镁皂（皂垢），既消耗了肥皂又浮在表面。这些沉淀物无法起泡，还会附着在衣物和器皿上形成灰白色的垢渍。
Hard water is rich in calcium and magnesium ions, which react with soap's sodium fatty acids to form insoluble calcium/magnesium soaps (scum), wasting the soap and floating on the surface. These precipitates can't lather and coat fabrics and dishes with a grayish film.

为什么厨房重油污要用碱性清洁剂？

Why use alkaline cleaner for heavy kitchen grease?

油脂在碱性条件下会发生皂化反应，被水解成可溶于水的肥皂和甘油，从而容易被冲走。火碱（氢氧化钠）类去油力强但腐蚀性强，需戴手套稀释使用。日常可用小苏打、碳酸钠等较温和的碱。
Fats undergo saponification in alkaline conditions, hydrolyzing into water-soluble soap and glycerol that rinse away easily. Caustic soda (sodium hydroxide) is powerful but corrosive, so dilute it and wear gloves. Milder alkalis like baking soda or washing soda work for everyday grease.

为什么衣物柔顺剂能让衣服柔软、防静电？

Why does fabric softener make clothes soft and anti-static?

柔顺剂主要是阳离子表面活性剂，会附着在纤维表面、中和负电荷，减少纤维间摩擦和静电。它还在纤维上形成薄润滑层，使触感柔软、易熨烫。但过量会堵塞吸水性纤维（如毛巾）。
Softener is mainly cationic surfactant that coats fibers and neutralizes negative charges, reducing friction and static between them. It also lays down a thin lubricating layer for a softer feel and easier ironing. Too much can clog absorbent fibers like towels.

为什么防水防晒霜要用卸妆产品才洗得掉？

Why do waterproof sunscreens need makeup remover to come off?

防水防晒霜常含油溶性成膜剂和二氧化钛等，靠普通洗面奶的表面活性难以完全乳化。卸妆油以油溶油，能先把防晒膜溶解再乳化冲走。不卸干净易堵塞毛孔、引发粉刺。
Waterproof sunscreen contains oil-soluble film formers and particles like titanium dioxide that ordinary face wash cannot fully emulsify. Cleansing oil dissolves the film by like-dissolves-like and then emulsifies it away. Incomplete removal can clog pores and cause breakouts.

为什么洗洁精一搅就能冒出那么多泡泡？

Why does dish soap produce so many bubbles when you agitate it?

洗洁精里的表面活性剂分子一头亲水、一头亲油，会包在空气外面形成薄薄的水膜把空气包起来，就是泡泡。搅动把空气打进溶液，大量这样的小气泡聚在一起，就成了丰富的泡沫。
Surfactant molecules in dish soap have a water-loving head and an oil-loving tail, and they wrap around air to form a thin water film—a bubble. Agitating traps air in the solution, and many such tiny bubbles together make abundant foam.

为什么肥皂能洗掉油污？

Why can soap remove grease?

肥皂分子一头亲水、一头亲油（两亲性），亲油端插入油污、亲水端朝外，把油滴包成小乳粒随水冲走。这个过程叫乳化。硬水里的钙镁离子会让肥皂结垢降低效果。
A soap molecule has a water-loving head and an oil-loving tail, so the tail inserts into grease while the head faces outward, wrapping the oil into tiny droplets that rinse away—a process called emulsification. Calcium and magnesium in hard water can form scum and reduce its effectiveness.

为什么小苏打能去除冰箱异味？

Why does baking soda remove fridge odors?

小苏打（碳酸氢钠）是弱碱性，能中和空气中的酸性臭味分子，也能轻微吸附气味。它安全无毒，适合放在冰箱、鞋柜里除味。定期更换或烘干可恢复效果。
Baking soda (sodium bicarbonate) is mildly alkaline and neutralizes acidic odor molecules in the air, and it also mildly adsorbs smells. It is safe and non-toxic, good for fridges and shoe cabinets. Replacing or drying it periodically restores the effect.

为什么75%的酒精消毒效果最好？

Why is 75% alcohol the best for disinfection?

酒精使细菌蛋白质变性失活。浓度太高会瞬间让表面蛋白凝固形成壳，反而阻止酒精渗入；太低则渗透慢、杀菌弱。75%左右能在渗入和变性之间取得平衡，杀菌最彻底。
Alcohol denatures bacterial proteins. Too high a concentration coagulates surface proteins into a shell that blocks penetration; too low penetrates slowly and kills weakly. Around 75% balances penetration and denaturation for the most thorough kill.

为什么深浅色衣服要分开洗？

Why wash dark and light clothes separately?

新衣服或易掉色的染料在水和摩擦下会溶出，沾染到浅色衣物上造成串色。水温越高、洗涤越久，掉色越明显。分开洗并反面向外、冷水快洗能减少染色。
Loose or bleeding dyes dissolve in water and with friction can stain light garments, causing color transfer. Higher temperature and longer washing worsen bleeding. Wash separately, inside-out, in cold water and quickly to minimize it.

为什么洗洁精要冲洗干净？

Why should you rinse dish soap thoroughly?

洗洁精多为表面活性剂，残留在餐具上被吃进肚子可能刺激肠胃，长期也不利健康。虽然多数家用洗洁精低毒，但残留会影响食物口感和气味。用流动水多冲几遍最稳妥。
Dish soap is mostly surfactants, and residues eaten from dishes can irritate the stomach and are best avoided long term. Although household soap is low-toxicity, leftovers alter the taste and smell of food. Rinsing well under running water is the safe choice.
