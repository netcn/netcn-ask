什么是ATP，为什么叫能量货币？

What is ATP and why is it called energy currency?

ATP即三磷酸腺苷，是细胞内的直接能量载体，水解末端的磷酸键会释放能量供生命活动使用。细胞靠它统一收付能量，故称能量货币。
ATP, or adenosine triphosphate, is the cell's direct energy carrier; hydrolyzing its terminal phosphate bond releases energy for life processes. Cells use it as a common energy currency, hence the name.

一氧化碳中毒的分子机制是什么？

What is the molecular mechanism of carbon monoxide poisoning?

CO以直线型配位到血红素Fe2+上，结合能力比O2强两百多倍，与氧竞争同一配位位点。CO结合后还改变血红素构象，使整个血红蛋白向高亲和态转变，剩余亚基释放氧更困难。结果是氧被血红蛋白锁住、组织供氧中断，严重时可致死；治疗用高压氧舱将CO竞争置换下来。
CO coordinates linearly to the heme Fe2+ with an affinity more than 200 times that of O2, competing for the same binding site. CO binding also changes the heme conformation, shifting the whole hemoglobin toward the high-affinity state so remaining subunits release oxygen even less readily. Oxygen is thus locked up and tissues are starved, which can be fatal; hyperbaric oxygen therapy displaces CO competitively.

血红蛋白与氧的结合为什么呈现“协同效应”？其氧合曲线为何是S形？

Why does hemoglobin show 'cooperative' oxygen binding, and why is its oxygen-binding curve S-shaped?

血红蛋白由四个亚基组成，结合一个氧分子后会引起构象由紧张态（T态）向松弛态（R态）转变，使其余亚基更易结合氧，即正协同效应。这种协同性使氧合饱和度对氧分压呈S形（sigmoid）曲线，有利于在肺部高效摄取并在组织中释放氧气。
Hemoglobin has four subunits; binding one O2 triggers a conformational shift from the tense (T) state to the relaxed (R) state, making the remaining subunits bind oxygen more readily—positive cooperativity. This produces a sigmoidal oxygen-saturation curve versus partial pressure, enabling efficient oxygen uptake in the lungs and release in tissues.

什么是"玻尔效应"？它如何帮助血红蛋白在组织中释放氧气？

What is the Bohr effect, and how does it help hemoglobin release oxygen in tissues?

玻尔效应指血红蛋白的氧亲和力随 pH 降低（CO₂ 升高）而下降。代谢活跃的组织产生 CO₂ 和乳酸使局部偏酸，血红蛋白构象改变、更容易把结合的氧释放给组织，从而精准匹配供氧需求。
The Bohr effect is the decrease in hemoglobin's oxygen affinity as pH falls (and CO₂ rises). Active tissues produce CO₂ and lactic acid, lowering local pH; hemoglobin then shifts conformation and more readily releases its bound oxygen, matching supply to demand.

为什么底物浓度足够高时，酶促反应速率不再随底物增加而上升？

Why does enzyme reaction rate stop rising with more substrate once the substrate is plentiful?

酶催化必须先生成酶-底物复合物 ES：E+S 可逆生成 ES，ES 再生成 E+P，反应速率取决于 ES 的浓度。底物浓度低时，ES 浓度与[S]成正比，速率随底物线性上升；底物浓度高时，几乎所有酶分子都被底物占据，ES 达到饱和，速率逼近最大值 Vmax，不再随[S]增加。这一关系用米氏方程描述：v=Vmax[S]/(Km+[S])，其中 Km 是速率达到一半 Vmax 时的底物浓度，反映酶对底物的亲和力，Km 越小亲和力越大。许多药物就是通过占据活性中心、使 Km 增大而 Vmax 不变（竞争性抑制）来起作用的。
Enzyme catalysis must first form the enzyme-substrate complex ES: E + S ⇌ ES → E + P, and the rate depends on how much ES exists. At low substrate, ES grows linearly with [S] and so does the rate; at high substrate nearly every enzyme molecule is occupied, ES saturates, and the rate approaches its maximum Vmax no matter how much more substrate you add. This relationship is the Michaelis-Menten equation, v = Vmax[S]/(Km + [S]), where Km is the substrate concentration giving half of Vmax and reflects the enzyme's affinity for its substrate (smaller Km, tighter binding). Many drugs work by occupying the active site as competitive inhibitors, raising the apparent Km while leaving Vmax unchanged.

蛋白质的氨基酸序列为什么就能决定它最终的三维结构？

Why does a protein's amino-acid sequence determine its final three-dimensional structure?

这由蛋白质折叠的能量学决定。疏水氨基酸残基倾向于包埋进内部以避开水分，亲水残基暴露在表面；主链肽键之间形成氢键，侧链之间的离子键和二硫键进一步锁定构象。这些相互作用共同使折叠后的自由能最小化，而氨基酸序列正是这些相互作用的唯一来源，因此一级结构决定三维结构，这就是 Anfinsen 定律（1972年诺贝尔化学奖）。不过折叠通常需要分子伴侣帮助绕开错误折叠的能垒陷阱；折叠一旦异常，会形成聚集体和淀粉样纤维，与阿尔茨海默病、帕金森病等神经退行性疾病有关。
The energetics decide it. Hydrophobic residues pack into the core to escape water while hydrophilic ones stay on the surface; backbone peptide groups hydrogen-bond, and side-chain ionic bonds and disulfide bonds lock the conformation further. Together these interactions minimize the free energy of the folded state, and the amino-acid sequence is their only source - so sequence dictates structure, the Anfinsen principle (Nobel Prize in Chemistry, 1972). Folding usually needs molecular chaperones to dodge the energy traps of misfolding; when it goes wrong, aggregates and amyloid fibrils form, linked to neurodegenerative diseases such as Alzheimer's and Parkinson's.

米氏方程（Michaelis-Menten equation）描述了什么？米氏常数Km有什么物理意义？

What does the Michaelis-Menten equation describe, and what is the physical meaning of the Michaelis constant Km?

米氏方程 v = Vmax·[S]/(Km + [S]) 描述了酶促反应速率v随底物浓度[S]变化的饱和关系。Km等于反应速率达到最大速率一半时的底物浓度，反映了酶与底物的亲和力——Km越小，亲和力通常越高。
The Michaelis-Menten equation, v = Vmax·[S]/(Km + [S]), describes the saturating relationship between enzyme-catalyzed rate v and substrate concentration [S]. Km is the substrate concentration at which the rate is half of Vmax, and it reflects enzyme-substrate affinity—a smaller Km generally indicates higher affinity.

血红蛋白怎样实现协同结合氧？

How does hemoglobin achieve cooperative oxygen binding?

血红蛋白四个亚基在结合氧后发生构象变化（T 态转 R 态），使后续亚基更易结合氧，呈 S 形氧合曲线。Perutz 用 X 射线晶体学阐明了这一变构结构基础，协同效应的分子机制在 1970 年的《自然》论文中被系统提出。
Hemoglobin's four subunits change conformation (T to R state) as oxygen binds, making later sites bind more easily and giving a sigmoidal curve. Perutz elucidated this allosteric structural basis by X-ray crystallography, and the molecular mechanism of cooperativity was set out in his 1970 Nature paper.

酶的专一性是什么意思？为什么一种酶通常只催化一种反应？

What does enzyme specificity mean, and why does one enzyme usually catalyze only one reaction?

酶的活性中心是与底物结合并发生催化的特殊三维区域，其形状、电荷分布和疏水环境与特定底物互补，就像锁和钥匙。底物进入后还会诱发酶构象微调，使两者贴合得更紧（诱导契合），催化基团正好对准要断裂的化学键。因此酶的专一性来自底物与活性中心的精确匹配：形状差一点、电荷不对，就结合不牢或无法正确催化。正因如此，淀粉酶只切淀粉、蛋白酶只切蛋白质，细胞才能有条不紊地运行数千个化学反应。
The active site is the special three-dimensional region where an enzyme binds its substrate and performs catalysis; its shape, charge distribution and hydrophobic character complement the specific substrate like a lock and key. Binding also nudges the enzyme into a slightly new conformation that fits the substrate even better (induced fit), lining up catalytic groups against the very bonds to be broken. Specificity therefore comes from precise matching between substrate and active site: slightly wrong shape or wrong charges, and binding fails or catalysis misfires. That is why amylase only cuts starch and proteases only cut proteins, letting the cell run thousands of reactions in orderly fashion.

铅中毒为什么要用螯合剂来治疗？

Why are chelating agents used to treat lead poisoning?

铅离子会与蛋白质中的巯基等结合，干扰酶的功能。螯合剂（如EDTA、二巯基丙醇）分子上有多个配位原子，能像钳子一样同时抓住铅离子，形成稳定的环状配合物，把铅从体内的结合部位抢出来，随尿液排出，从而降低血铅水平。
Lead ions bind to thiol groups in proteins and disrupt enzyme function. Chelating agents such as EDTA and British anti-lewisite have several donor atoms that grip the lead ion like a claw, forming stable ring complexes that pull lead off its binding sites and excrete it in urine, lowering blood lead levels.

Which metal does vitamin B12 contain, and what happens when it is deficient?

Vitamin B12 (cobalamin) has a cobalt ion at its center, enclosed by a corrin macrocycle similar to porphyrin. The body depends on vitamin B12 for blood formation and a healthy nervous system; deficiency leads to megaloblastic anemia and nerve damage. Humans cannot synthesize vitamin B12 and must obtain it mainly from animal foods such as meat, eggs and dairy.

“锁钥”与“诱导契合”模型有何不同？

How do the 'lock-and-key' and 'induced fit' models differ?

锁钥模型认为酶与底物形状预先完全契合；Koshland 1958 年提出诱导契合，强调结合时酶构象主动调整以更好地包裹底物。后者更好解释了催化中的构象变化与专一性。
The lock-and-key model holds enzyme and substrate pre-fit like a key in a lock; Koshland's 1958 induced-fit says the enzyme actively reshapes on binding to cradle the substrate. The latter better explains conformational change and specificity in catalysis.

维生素 C 在人体内起什么作用？

What role does vitamin C play in the human body?

维生素 C（抗坏血酸）是水溶性抗氧化剂，能清除自由基并参与胶原蛋白的合成。它还是某些羟化酶的辅因子，缺乏会导致坏血病。人体不能自行合成，须从果蔬中摄取。
Vitamin C (ascorbic acid) is a water-soluble antioxidant that scavenges free radicals and aids collagen synthesis. It also serves as a cofactor for certain hydroxylases; deficiency causes scurvy. Humans cannot synthesize it and must obtain it from fruits and vegetables.

为什么我们的身体既需要蛋白质也需要糖类（碳水化合物）？

Why does our body need both proteins and carbohydrates?

糖类是身体的"快能源"，分解后能迅速供能；蛋白质由氨基酸组成，用来建造和修复肌肉、酶、皮肤等身体结构。两者功能不同、缺一不可：缺糖会没力气，缺蛋白会长不好、修不了组织。
Carbohydrates are the body's quick energy source, breaking down to fuel activity fast; proteins, built from amino acids, construct and repair muscles, enzymes, and skin. They serve different roles—without carbs you lack energy, without protein you can't grow or repair tissue.

为什么血液是红色的？

Why is blood red?

血液红色来自红细胞中的血红蛋白，它含有与氧结合的铁原子。含氧时呈鲜红，缺氧时偏暗红。
Blood is red because of hemoglobin in red blood cells, which contains iron atoms that bind oxygen. It appears bright red when oxygenated and darker red when not.

我们吃下的食物在体内变成了什么？

What does the food we eat become inside the body?

食物在消化道里被分解成糖、氨基酸和脂肪酸等小分子，再进入细胞通过化学反应释放能量或建造身体。未被吸收的部分变成废物排出。
In the digestive tract, food is broken into small molecules like sugars, amino acids, and fatty acids, which enter cells and release energy or build the body through chemical reactions. What is not absorbed becomes waste.

光合作用的总反应式是什么？

What is the overall equation of photosynthesis?

绿色植物在光照下把二氧化碳和水转化为葡萄糖并释放氧气：6CO₂ + 6H₂O → C₆H₁₂O₆ + 6O₂。该过程在叶绿体中进行，把光能转化为储存在糖中的化学能。它是地球碳氧循环的核心。
In light, green plants convert carbon dioxide and water into glucose while releasing oxygen: 6CO₂ + 6H₂O → C₆H₁₂O₆ + 6O₂. Occurring in chloroplasts, it turns light energy into chemical energy stored in sugar. It is central to Earth's carbon and oxygen cycles.
