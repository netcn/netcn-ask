为什么感冒鼻塞时吃东西觉得没味道？

Why does food taste flavorless when you have a blocked nose?

我们感受到的风味大部分来自嗅觉；鼻子不通时食物挥发的气味分子到不了嗅觉受体，于是只剩下甜咸酸苦等基础味觉。
Most of flavor is actually smell; with a blocked nose, odor molecules can't reach olfactory receptors, leaving only basic tastes like sweet, salt, sour and bitter.

为什么有人觉得某苦味纸片极苦有人却无感？

Why do some people find a bitter paper strip intensely bitter while others taste nothing?

对PTC、PROP苦味的感知由TAS2R38基因决定，基因型不同，有人极敏感，有人尝不出，体现人类味觉的遗传差异。
Sensitivity to PTC/PROP bitterness is set by the TAS2R38 gene; variants make some people highly sensitive and others unable to taste it—a genetic taste difference.

味精的鲜味到底是什么物质？

What compound gives MSG its umami taste?

主要是谷氨酸钠，它能激活舌头上的鲜味受体，和番茄、海带、奶酪里的天然谷氨酸是同一类物质。
It is sodium glutamate, which activates umami receptors—the same family as natural glutamate in tomatoes, kelp and cheese.

舌头不同区域分别只尝甜咸酸苦，这是真的吗？

Is it true that tongue regions taste only sweet, salt, sour or bitter?

这是过时说法；实际上所有味蕾都能感知各种基本味觉，只是灵敏度略有差异，并没有严格分区。
That is outdated; all taste buds sense every basic taste with slight sensitivity differences, not strict zones.

为什么酱油又咸又鲜？

Why is soy sauce both salty and savory?

米曲霉等分泌蛋白酶把大豆蛋白分解成氨基酸（谷氨酸带来鲜味），加盐既调味又抑制杂菌，长期晒酿而成。
Aspergillus oryzae releases proteases that break soy protein into amino acids (glutamate = umami); salt seasons and prevents spoilage during long brewing.

辣椒的辣味是真的味道吗，为什么喝水不解辣？

Is chili heat a real taste, and why doesn't water relieve it?

辣来自辣椒素激活痛觉受体（TRPV1），不是酸甜苦咸鲜那种味觉；辣椒素不溶于水，所以喝水没用，喝牛奶（脂肪）更有效。
Capsaicin activates pain receptors (TRPV1), not the tastes; it is oil-soluble, so water doesn't help—milk fat does.

薄荷为什么让人觉得凉？

Why does mint feel cool?

薄荷醇能激活皮肤里的冷觉受体（TRPM8），大脑误以为温度下降，于是产生清凉感。
Menthol activates cold receptors (TRPM8) in the skin, so the brain perceives a cooling sensation without actual cooling.

为什么辣椒吃起来会辣辣的？

Why does chili pepper taste hot and spicy?

辣椒里有一种叫辣椒素的成分，它会骗我们的舌头，让大脑以为嘴巴被烫到了。辣椒素会抓住舌头上的热觉受体，所以越吃越觉得烧，喝水也压不住那种辣。
Chili has a chemical called capsaicin that tricks our tongue and makes the brain think the mouth is being burned. Capsaicin grabs the heat-sensing receptors on the tongue, so the burning feeling grows and water does not wash it away.

为什么薄荷糖吃起来凉凉的？

Why does mint taste cool and fresh?

薄荷里有一种叫薄荷醇的成分，会打开嘴巴里感受凉意的传感器，让我们觉得凉。它骗了舌头，就像冷风一样，其实薄荷本身并不冷。
Mint contains a substance called menthol that switches on the cool-sensing receptors in our mouth, so we feel cold. It tricks the tongue the way cold air would, even though mint is not actually cold.

为什么臭奶酪闻起来那么臭？

Why does stinky cheese smell so strong?

做奶酪时加的小细菌会慢慢分解里面的脂肪和蛋白质，产生一种味道很冲的气体。这种气体里含有硫，闻起来像脚臭或臭袜子，但正是它让奶酪风味独特。
Tiny bacteria added to make cheese break down fats and proteins and release a gas with a very strong smell. That gas contains sulfur, which smells like stinky socks, but it is exactly what gives the cheese its strong flavor.

为什么姜吃起来辣辣的还有点麻？

Why does ginger taste spicy and a little tingling?

姜里有特殊的油状物质会刺激舌头和嗓子，让我们觉得辣和麻。这种成分叫姜辣素，它和辣椒素不一样，除了辣还会带来一阵麻麻的刺痛感。
Ginger has special oily compounds that tingle the tongue and throat, feeling spicy. The compound is called gingerol, and unlike capsaicin it adds a numbing, prickly sensation on top of the heat.

为什么香草冰淇淋有香香的味道？

Why does vanilla ice cream smell so good?

香草里有一种香香的小分子，叫香兰素，它钻进鼻子里，我们就觉得香。人的鼻子对香兰素特别敏感，只有很少一点就能闻到，所以香味闻起来很浓。
Vanilla has a sweet-smelling molecule called vanillin that floats into our nose and smells lovely. Our noses are extremely sensitive to vanillin, so only a tiny amount is needed to smell it, which is why vanilla scent is so strong.
