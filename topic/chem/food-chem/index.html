美拉德反应与焦糖化在化学本质上有何区别,为什么前者能在较低温度下发生?

How do the Maillard reaction and caramelization differ chemically, and why does the former proceed at lower temperatures?

焦糖化是糖类在高温下(蔗糖约需 160 °C 以上)经脱水、烯醇化和聚合生成深色风味物的过程,不需要其他组分参与。美拉德反应则是还原糖的羰基与氨基酸/蛋白质的氨基缩合形成 Schiff 碱,再经 Amadori 重排、Strecker 降解等串联路径生成类黑素与吡嗪类香气物。氨基作为亲核催化剂降低了羰基活化门槛,加上 Schiff 碱形成放热,使其在 100 °C 左右甚至更低即可显著进行,这也是烤肉着色快于熬糖的原因之一。
Caramelization is sugar alone undergoing dehydration, enolization, and polymerization at high temperature (sucrose needs roughly >160 °C). The Maillard reaction instead condenses carbonyls of reducing sugars with amino groups of amino acids or proteins into Schiff bases, followed by Amadori rearrangement and Strecker degradation cascades yielding melanoidins and pyrazines. Amines act as nucleophilic catalysts that lower the carbonyl activation barrier, so browning proceeds readily near 100 °C or below — one reason roasted meat browns faster than boiling sugar.

焦糖化与美拉德反应有何不同？

How does caramelization differ from the Maillard reaction?

焦糖化是纯糖类在 160°C 以上无水加热时的热分解与聚合，产生焦糖色与苦甜风味，不涉及氨基酸。美拉德则需要糖与氨基化合物共同作用，两者都属非酶促褐变但机理不同。
Caramelization is the anhydrous thermal decomposition and polymerization of sugars above ~160°C, yielding caramel color and bittersweet flavor without amino compounds. The Maillard reaction needs sugars plus amines; both are non-enzymatic browning but mechanistically distinct.

为什么柠檬汁写的字看不见，烘一下却显棕字？

Why do lemon-juice letters appear only when heated?

柠檬汁里除了水和柠檬酸，还有糖和多种有机物，水分蒸发后留下的薄痕几乎无色。受热时这些残留先脱水，再焦糖化、氧化成棕色的大分子，笔迹就现形。关键是门槛不同：纸要相当高的温度才焦化，果汁残留低得多，同样的热量下只有写过字的部分先变棕。牛奶、洋葱汁是同一道理；可火太近、温度太高，纸先焦黑，字反而被盖住。
Lemon juice holds water, citric acid and sugars plus other organic compounds; once the water evaporates the thin residue left behind is almost colorless. Heating drives off water and then caramelizes and oxidizes that residue into larger brown molecules, so the writing shows up. The point is the different thresholds: paper needs a fairly high temperature to char, whereas the juice residue browns at a much lower one, so under the same heat only the strokes darken. Milk and onion juice work on the same principle; but too close a flame and the paper blackens first, swallowing the message.

食品中的抗氧化剂如何防止变质？

How do antioxidants in food prevent spoilage?

脂类氧化会产生自由基链式反应使食品酸败。抗氧化剂（如维生素 C、E、酚类）作为氢供体中断自由基链，自身较稳定，从而延缓氧化、保持色泽风味并延长保质期。
Lipid oxidation proceeds by a radical chain reaction causing rancidity. Antioxidants (e.g., vitamins C and E, phenolics) donate hydrogen to break the chain and are relatively stable themselves, thus delaying oxidation, preserving color/flavor, and extending shelf life.

面包焙烤时发生的非酶褐变中,为什么偏偏是赖氨酸最先遭受损失?

In the nonenzymatic browning that occurs while bread bakes, why does lysine take the hit before any other amino acid?

赖氨酸侧链末端的ε-氨基亲核性突出,率先进攻还原糖的羰基生成Schiff碱,再经Amadori重排进入后续阶段,该残基由此丧失营养价值。同一反应链还派生出吡嗪类香气成分与大分子类黑精色素。把水分活度控制在0.6至0.7、适度调低炉温,可在风味生成与必需氨基酸保全之间取得折衷。
The epsilon-amino group capping the lysine side chain is unusually nucleophilic: it attacks carbonyls of reducing sugars first, giving Schiff bases that undergo Amadori rearrangement into later stages, and that residue thereby loses nutritional value. Along the way the same cascade releases pyrazine aroma compounds and macromolecular melanoidin pigments. Holding water activity at 0.6 to 0.7 and trimming oven temperature buys a compromise between flavor generation and preservation of the essential amino acid.

苹果切开后为什么很快变褐,滴上柠檬汁却能延缓变色?

Why does a cut apple turn brown quickly, and why does a squeeze of lemon juice slow the change?

切开后细胞破损,多酚氧化酶以氧气为电子受体把酚类底物氧化成醌,醌再聚合成褐色黑色素,这就是酶促褐变。柠檬汁里的抗坏血酸把醌还原回酚类,柠檬酸降低pH抑制酶活性,两者共同延缓变色;加热钝化酶或隔绝氧气也同样有效。
Cutting the fruit breaks cells open, and polyphenol oxidase then uses oxygen to oxidize phenolic substrates into quinones, which polymerize into the brown melanin pigments; this is enzymatic browning. Lemon juice helps twice over, because its ascorbic acid reduces the quinones back to phenols while citric acid lowers the pH and suppresses the enzyme. Blanching to denature the enzyme or keeping oxygen away works just as well.

蜂蜜为什么几乎不会变质?考古发现的千年蜂蜜还能吃吗?

Why does honey almost never spoil — can thousand-year-old honey from tombs still be eaten?

蜂蜜有三重防线:含水量极低,细菌会被高浓度糖反渗透脱水;糖浓度高本身渗透压惊人;还带弱酸性和微量过氧化氢。古埃及墓里放的蜂蜜数千年后仍可食用确有报道。但家庭储存要注意密封防潮——一旦吸水稀释,防线就破了。一岁以下婴儿不能吃蜂蜜,因为可能含肉毒杆菌芽孢。
Honey holds three defenses: tiny water content dehydrating germs by osmosis; fierce sugar pressure; plus mild acidity and traces of hydrogen peroxide. Honey in Egyptian tombs reportedly stayed edible millennia later. Home storage must stay sealed — absorbed moisture breaks the wall. Never feed honey to infants under one for botulism spore risk.

烤面包的表皮为什么会变成棕褐色,还散发出特有的烤香?

Why does a bread crust turn brown and give off its characteristic toasted aroma?

这主要是美拉德反应:还原糖的羰基与氨基酸或蛋白质侧链的氨基在约140℃以上缩合成糖胺,经Amadori重排进入中间阶段,再经Strecker降解产生吡嗪、醛类等上百种香气分子,末期缩聚成棕褐色的类黑素。面包表皮高温且水分少,恰好满足反应条件,所以上色与香气同时出现。
The browning is mainly the Maillard reaction. Above roughly 140 °C, the carbonyl group of a reducing sugar condenses with the amino group of an amino acid or protein side chain to form a glycosylamine, which rearranges through the Amadori pathway; later Strecker degradation liberates pyrazines, aldehydes and hundreds of other aroma molecules, while the final stage condenses into brown melanoidins. The crust, being hot and dry, offers ideal conditions, so color and aroma develop together.

酸奶和泡菜为什么远比鲜奶、鲜菜耐放——乳酸菌发酵到底是怎样起到防腐作用的？

Yogurt and pickled vegetables keep far longer than fresh milk or fresh greens — how does lactic-acid fermentation actually preserve food?

核心是乳酸把体系变酸。乳酸菌把糖类发酵成乳酸，pH降到4左右，绝大多数腐败菌和致病菌就此停止生长：未解离的有机酸穿过菌体细胞膜，在胞内解离出质子，破坏跨膜质子梯度使能量代谢瘫痪；发酵菌还占据生态位、耗尽养分，并产生过氧化氢和细菌素等抑菌物。多重屏障叠加，杂菌无从繁殖，酸菜、泡菜与酸奶因此得以长期保存。
The key is acidity. Lactic acid bacteria ferment sugars into lactic acid and push the pH down to around 4, where nearly all spoilage and pathogenic microbes stop growing: undissociated organic acid slips through microbial membranes and dissociates inside the cell, wrecking the proton gradient that powers their metabolism. The fermenters meanwhile occupy the niche, exhaust the nutrients, and release hydrogen peroxide and bacteriocins; with these barriers stacked, contaminants cannot multiply, which is why sauerkraut, kimchi and yogurt keep so well.

明知可能生成亚硝胺，为什么火腿、培根里仍离不开亚硝酸钠这种添加剂？

Given its potential to form nitrosamines, why does sodium nitrite remain irreplaceable in hams and bacon?

亚硝酸钠同时承担抑制肉毒梭菌与腌肉发色两大功能，迄今没有单一替代物能兼顾两者。机理：酸性腌肉体系中NO₂⁻转变为HNO₂并缓释出NO，NO与肌红蛋白络合生成亚硝酰肌红蛋白，受热转为稳定的粉红色亚硝基血色原，造就腌腊制品特有色泽；NO还能与细菌铁硫蛋白结合，阻断其电子传递与能量代谢，有效防止肉毒毒素形成。搭配抗坏血酸钠可加速发色并压低残余亚硝酸盐，显著减少亚硝胺风险。
Sodium nitrite performs two jobs at once - suppressing Clostridium botulinum and coloring cured meat - and no single substitute covers both. In the acidic curing matrix NO2- becomes HNO2 and slowly releases nitric oxide; NO binds myoglobin to give nitrosomyoglobin, which heating converts into the stable pink nitrosohemochrome behind the characteristic hue. NO likewise ties up bacterial iron-sulfur proteins, cutting electron transport and energy metabolism so botulinum toxin never forms. Paired with sodium ascorbate, color develops faster while residual nitrite drops, sharply reducing the nitrosamine risk.
