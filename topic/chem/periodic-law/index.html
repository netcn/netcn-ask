什么是"惰性电子对效应"？为什么铅常显 +2 价，而同族的碳、硅却以 +4 价为主？

What is the "inert-pair effect," and why does lead commonly show +2 while carbon and silicon in the same group favor +4?

在重 p 区元素（如铅、铊）中，ns² 电子对因相对论效应和较差的屏蔽而难以参与成键，倾向于保留下来，于是低价态（+2）更稳定。轻的同族元素（C、Si）相对论效应可忽略，ns² 电子正常参与成键，故以 +4 为主。
In heavy p-block elements such as lead, the ns² electron pair is stabilized by relativistic effects and poor shielding, so it tends to remain unshared and the lower oxidation state (+2) becomes favored. Lighter group members like C and Si show negligible relativistic effects, so their ns² electrons bond normally and +4 dominates.

什么是镧系收缩？

What is the lanthanide contraction?

镧系收缩指从镧到镥，因4f电子屏蔽效应差，有效核电荷增加使原子和离子半径逐渐减小的现象。它导致同副族中第五、六周期元素半径相近（如锆与铪难分离），并影响过渡金属的性质递变。这是周期表中重要的结构效应。
Lanthanide contraction is the gradual decrease in atomic and ionic radii from lanthanum to lutetium, caused by poor shielding of 4f electrons raising the effective nuclear charge. It makes elements of the 5th and 6th periods in the same subgroup nearly equal in size (e.g. zirconium and hafnium are hard to separate) and affects transition-metal trends. It is a key structural effect in the table.

碳有多少种同位素？

How many isotopes does carbon have?

碳的常见同位素包括稳定的碳-12、碳-13和放射性的碳-14，此外在实验室还能合成碳-10、碳-11等短寿命同位素。碳-12被定义为原子量标准，碳-14用于年代测定。不同同位素在质量上不同但化学性质几乎一致。
Common carbon isotopes include the stable carbon-12 and carbon-13 and the radioactive carbon-14; short-lived isotopes like carbon-10 and carbon-11 can also be made in the lab. Carbon-12 defines the atomic mass standard and carbon-14 is used for dating. They differ in mass but have nearly identical chemistry.

同周期元素的第一电离能整体升高，为什么在硼（B）和氧（O）处会出现"反常"下降？

First ionization energy generally rises across a period, so why do boron (B) and oxygen (O) show unexpected drops?

从铍到硼，电子进入更高的 2p 轨道且受内层屏蔽更强，更容易失去，故 B 比 Be 低。从氮到氧，氧的 2p 轨道出现成对电子，电子间排斥增大使其中一个更易被移走，故 O 比 N 低。这两处"反常"正体现了轨道能级与电子排斥的影响。
From Be to B the electron enters the higher-energy 2p orbital with more shielding, so it is easier to remove, dropping below Be. From N to O the added electron pairs in a 2p orbital, and increased electron–electron repulsion makes one easier to remove, dropping below N. These "dips" reflect orbital energy and repulsion effects.

为什么氟是电负性最强的元素？

Why is fluorine the most electronegative element?

氟原子半径很小且核电荷相对较大，外层电子离核近、被强烈吸引；同时它只需再得到一个电子即达稳定八隅体。这使得氟对成键电子的吸引力最强，鲍林电负性约为4.0，居所有元素之首。
Fluorine has a very small radius and relatively large nuclear charge, so its outer electrons are close to the nucleus and strongly attracted, and it needs only one more electron to complete its octet. This makes its pull on bonding electrons the strongest of all elements, with a Pauling electronegativity of about 4.0, the highest.

卤素的氧化性变化规律是什么？

What is the trend in oxidizing power of the halogens?

卤素自上而下（氟、氯、溴、碘）原子半径增大、得电子能力减弱，氧化性依次减弱。因此氟能置换氯、溴、碘的化合物，而碘几乎不能置换其他卤素。氧化性强的卤素单质常用作氧化剂。
From top to bottom among halogens (fluorine, chlorine, bromine, iodine), the atomic radius grows and the ability to gain electrons weakens, so oxidizing power decreases. Fluorine can displace the compounds of chlorine, bromine and iodine, while iodine can hardly displace any other halogen. The stronger oxidizers are often used as oxidizing agents.

稀有气体为什么以前叫惰性气体？

Why were noble gases once called inert gases?

稀有气体最外层电子已达稳定结构（氦为2电子，其余为8电子），极难得失电子，长期被认为是完全不反应的。直到1962年巴特利特合成出氙的化合物，人们才确认它们也能参与反应。因此它们更准确地被称为稀有气体。
Noble gases have complete outer shells (helium with 2 electrons, the rest with 8), making them extremely reluctant to gain or lose electrons, so they were long thought to be completely unreactive. Only after Bartlett synthesized a xenon compound in 1962 was it confirmed they can participate in reactions. The more accurate name is noble (rare) gases.

为什么碱金属遇水反应剧烈？

Why do alkali metals react violently with water?

碱金属（锂、钠、钾等）最外层只有一个电子，且原子半径大、电离能低，极易失去该电子。遇水时它们迅速将电子转移给水分子，生成氢气和强碱，并放出大量热。钾、铷、铯反应更为剧烈，甚至引发燃烧或爆炸。
Alkali metals (lithium, sodium, potassium and so on) have a single outer electron with large atomic radius and low ionization energy, so they lose it very easily. In water they rapidly transfer electrons to water molecules, producing hydrogen gas and a strong base while releasing much heat. Potassium, rubidium and cesium react even more violently, sometimes igniting or exploding.

元素的电负性是什么意思？

What does electronegativity of an element mean?

电负性衡量原子在化学键中吸引电子能力的强弱。数值越大，吸引共用电子的能力越强。鲍林标度上氟的电负性最高，约为4.0，而碱金属的电负性最低。
Electronegativity measures an atom's ability to attract electrons in a chemical bond. A higher value means a stronger pull on shared electrons. On the Pauling scale, fluorine has the highest electronegativity at about 4.0, while the alkali metals have the lowest.

什么是元素周期律？

What is the periodic law?

元素周期律指元素的性质随原子序数的递增而呈周期性变化的规律。门捷列夫最初按原子质量排列，现代周期表则按原子序数排列。该规律是预测元素及其化合物性质的基础。
The periodic law states that the properties of elements vary periodically with increasing atomic number. Mendeleev originally arranged them by atomic mass, while the modern table uses atomic number. This law is the basis for predicting the properties of elements and their compounds.

为什么同一纵列（族）的元素化学性质很像，比如锂、钠、钾都爱和水剧烈反应？

Why do elements in the same group (column) behave similarly—for example, lithium, sodium, and potassium all react vigorously with water?

同一族的元素最外层电子数相同，而化学性质主要由最外层（价）电子决定。碱金属最外层都只有 1 个电子，很容易失去它形成 +1 离子，所以都表现出相似的活泼性。
Elements in the same group share the same number of outermost (valence) electrons, and chemical behavior is governed mainly by those. Alkali metals all have a single outer electron that they readily lose to form +1 ions, so they all show similar reactivity.

金属性在周期表中如何变化？

How does metallic character vary in the periodic table?

金属性（易失电子）同周期向左增强、同族向下增强，因此左下角元素（如铯、钫）金属性最强，右上角（如氟）非金属性最强。这与电离能趋势相反。
Metallic character (ease of losing electrons) strengthens leftward across a period and downward in a group, so the lower-left elements (e.g., Cs, Fr) are most metallic and the upper-right (e.g., F) most nonmetallic. This is opposite to ionization-energy trends.

什么是同位素？

What are isotopes?

同位素是同一元素中质子数相同、中子数不同的原子。它们化学性质几乎相同，但物理性质（如质量、放射性）可能差异很大。例如碳-12稳定，碳-14具有放射性，常用于考古测年。
Isotopes are atoms of the same element that have the same number of protons but different numbers of neutrons. Their chemical properties are nearly identical, but physical properties such as mass and radioactivity can differ greatly. For example, carbon-12 is stable while carbon-14 is radioactive and is used in archaeological dating.

同一主族自上而下原子半径如何变化？

How does atomic radius change down a group of the periodic table?

在同一主族中，从上到下电子层数逐渐增加，原子半径随之增大。原子半径增大使最外层电子离核更远、受核吸引力减弱，因此金属性通常增强、非金属性减弱。例如碱金属从锂到铯，反应活性越来越高。
Within the same main group, the number of electron shells increases from top to bottom, so the atomic radius grows. A larger radius places the outer electrons farther from the nucleus and weakens nuclear attraction, so metallic character generally increases and nonmetallic character decreases. For example, among alkali metals from lithium to cesium, reactivity rises.

什么是稀有气体？

What are the noble gases?

稀有气体又称惰性气体，包括氦、氖、氩、氪、氙、氡，位于周期表最右侧的第18族。它们的外层电子均达到稳定结构，化学性质极不活泼。早期认为它们不与任何物质反应，后来才合成出少数稀有气体化合物。
Noble gases, also called inert gases, include helium, neon, argon, krypton, xenon and radon, occupying group 18 on the far right of the periodic table. Their outer electron shells are complete, making them extremely unreactive. They were once thought not to react at all, until a few compounds were later synthesized.

同周期元素从左到右金属性如何变化？

How does metallic character change from left to right across a period?

在同一周期中，从左到右核电荷数增加、原子半径减小，原子吸引电子能力增强。因此金属性逐渐减弱、非金属性逐渐增强。例如第三周期从钠到氯，活泼性由强金属过渡到强非金属。
Across a period from left to right, nuclear charge increases and atomic radius shrinks, so the atom's ability to attract electrons grows. Metallic character therefore weakens and nonmetallic character strengthens. In period 3, for instance, reactivity shifts from the strong metal sodium to the strong nonmetal chlorine.

什么是过渡金属？

What are transition metals?

过渡金属是周期表中d区元素，包括第3至12族的多种金属，如铁、铜、锌。它们常有可变的化合价、能形成有色离子和配合物，并多具备良好的导电性和催化性能。许多重要工业催化剂都由过渡金属构成。
Transition metals are the d-block elements in groups 3 to 12, such as iron, copper and zinc. They often show variable oxidation states, form colored ions and complexes, and have good conductivity and catalytic ability. Many important industrial catalysts are made of transition metals.

谁创造了元素周期表？

Who created the periodic table of elements?

元素周期表由俄国化学家门捷列夫于1869年提出。他将当时已知的63种元素按相对原子质量排列，并发现元素性质随原子量呈周期性变化。他还成功预测了若干尚未发现元素的性质。
The periodic table was proposed by the Russian chemist Dmitri Mendeleev in 1869. He arranged the then-known 63 elements by atomic mass and found that their properties varied periodically. He also successfully predicted the properties of several elements not yet discovered.

什么是金属性与非金属性？

What are metallic and nonmetallic character?

金属性指元素原子失去电子成为阳离子的倾向，非金属性指得到电子成为阴离子的倾向。周期表左侧元素金属性强，右侧非金属性强。金属性强的元素形成的氧化物多为碱性，非金属性强的多为酸性。
Metallic character is the tendency of an atom to lose electrons and form cations, while nonmetallic character is the tendency to gain electrons and form anions. Elements on the left of the table are metallic and those on the right are nonmetallic. Their oxides tend to be basic or acidic respectively.
