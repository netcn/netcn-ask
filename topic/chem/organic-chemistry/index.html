什么是聚合反应中的加聚和缩聚？

What are addition and condensation polymerization?

加聚是含不饱和键的单体直接相连成大分子，不放出小分子，如聚乙烯。缩聚是单体间脱去水或其他小分子形成聚合物，如尼龙和聚酯。
Addition polymerization links monomers with unsaturated bonds into large molecules without releasing small molecules, as in polyethylene. Condensation polymerization joins monomers while shedding water or another small molecule, as in nylon and polyester.

E1和E2消除反应在机理上有什么区别？

What mechanistic difference distinguishes E1 and E2 elimination?

E2是一步双分子消除：碱同时夺取beta-氢并促使离去基团离开，两者必须处于反式共平面位置，产物是反式消除的烯烃，速率与底物和碱的浓度都成正比。E1先解离成碳正离子，再由碱夺取beta-氢，分两步经过碳正离子中间体，速率只与底物有关，常与SN1竞争，并可能发生碳正离子重排。
E2 is a one-step bimolecular elimination: the base abstracts a beta-hydrogen while the leaving group departs simultaneously, and the two must be anti-periplanar, giving anti elimination; the rate depends on both substrate and base. E1 first ionizes to a carbocation and then a base removes a beta-hydrogen, proceeding in two steps through a carbocation intermediate; its rate depends only on the substrate, it often competes with SN1, and the cation may undergo rearrangement.

什么是逆合成分析？它的基本思路是什么？

What is retrosynthetic analysis, and what is its basic idea?

逆合成分析由诺贝尔化学奖得主科里提出：从目标分子出发，通过识别官能团转化、断键和重排，反向拆解成更简单的前体，逐步简化直到得到市售的廉价原料。逆向推导出的合成路线还要正向逐一验证每步反应的可行性、区域和立体选择性以及产率。
Retrosynthetic analysis, introduced by Nobel laureate E. J. Corey, works backward from the target molecule: by recognizing functional-group interconversions, bond disconnections and rearrangements, the molecule is progressively simplified into easier precursors until cheap commercial starting materials are reached. The reverse route is then validated forward, checking each step for feasibility, regio- and stereoselectivity, and yield.

苯环为什么特别稳定？

Why is the benzene ring especially stable?

苯（C₆H₆）的六个碳原子形成平面环，六个 π 电子离域在整个环上，满足休克尔 4n+2 规则（n=1）。这种离域降低了分子能量，使苯不易发生加成而倾向于取代反应。芳香性正是这种特殊稳定性的来源。
In benzene (C₆H₆) the six carbons form a planar ring with six π electrons delocalized over the whole ring, satisfying Hückel's 4n+2 rule (n=1). This delocalization lowers the energy, so benzene resists addition and favors substitution. Aromaticity is the source of this special stability.

加聚反应和缩聚反应有什么本质区别？为什么尼龙和涤纶是缩聚产物？

What is the fundamental difference between addition and condensation polymerization, and why are nylon and polyester condensation products?

加聚是单体打开双键直接串联，产物只有高分子，原子利用率百分之百，如聚乙烯、聚丙烯；缩聚则是带两个官能团的单体之间脱去小分子（如水）而连接，产物是高分子加小分子。尼龙66由己二酸与己二胺缩水成酰胺键生成，涤纶由对苯二甲酸与乙二醇酯化缩聚而成，每个连接都脱去一分子水，所以它们都是缩聚产物。由于缩聚要求单体官能团接近等摩尔且反应程度很高，工业化控制比加聚更讲究。
In addition polymerization monomers simply open their double bonds and link up, so the only product is the polymer, with 100% atom economy - as in polyethylene or polypropylene. In condensation polymerization monomers carrying two functional groups join by splitting off a small molecule such as water, so the products are the polymer plus small molecules. Nylon-66 forms amide bonds when hexanedioic acid and hexamethylenediamine condense, and polyester forms by esterifying terephthalic acid with ethylene glycol - every link releases one water molecule, so both are condensation polymers. Because condensation needs near-equal stoichiometry of the two monomers and near-complete conversion, industrial control is trickier than for addition polymerization.

什么是“芳香性”？休克尔规则（Hückel's rule）如何判断一个环状分子是否具有芳香性？

What is 'aromaticity,' and how does Hückel's rule determine whether a cyclic molecule is aromatic?

芳香性指平面、环状、完全共轭且含有4n+2个π电子的分子所表现出的特殊稳定性。根据休克尔规则，满足“平面+环状+连续共轭+π电子数为4n+2（n=0,1,2…）”的分子具有芳香性，例如苯有6个π电子（n=1）而稳定。
Aromaticity is the special stability shown by planar, cyclic, fully conjugated molecules that contain 4n+2 π electrons. By Hückel's rule, a molecule is aromatic if it is planar, cyclic, continuously conjugated, and has a 4n+2 count of π electrons (n = 0,1,2…); benzene, with 6 π electrons (n=1), is the classic example.

Why is the Diels-Alder reaction called a '[4+2] cycloaddition'?

A conjugated diene (4 π electrons) and a dienophile (2 π electrons) join in one concerted step to a six-membered ring with no intermediate—a classic pericyclic reaction. Diels and Alder discovered it in 1928 (Nobel 1950).

维蒂希（Wittig）反应有什么用？为什么它在有机合成中很重要？

What is the Wittig reaction used for, and why is it important in organic synthesis?

Wittig 反应用磷叶立德与醛/酮反应，专一地把羰基（C=O）转化为烯烃（C=C），并可精确控制双键位置。它避免了传统方法产生的位置异构副产物，是合成维生素 A、天然产物中烯键的常用手段，相关概念获 1979 年诺贝尔化学奖。
The Wittig reaction uses a phosphonium ylide to convert a carbonyl (C=O) into an alkene (C=C) with precise control of the double-bond position. It avoids the positional-isomer byproducts of older methods and is widely used to make alkene linkages in vitamin A and natural products; the underlying chemistry earned the 1979 Nobel Prize.

阿司匹林（乙酰水杨酸）分子中同时含酯基和羧基，这两个官能团如何解释它的疗效与副作用？

Aspirin (acetylsalicylic acid) contains both an ester group and a carboxyl group. How do these functional groups explain how it works and its side effects?

阿司匹林分子中，酯基由水杨酸的酚羟基被乙酸乙酰化而成，这一修饰降低了游离水杨酸对胃的刺激性并改善了药代性质；进入体内后，酯基被酯酶水解，逐步释放出水杨酸。真正不可逆抑制环氧化酶（COX）的是阿司匹林本身：它把乙酰基共价转移到COX活性中心的丝氨酸残基上，使酶永久失活，从而阻断前列腺素合成，产生退热、镇痛和抗炎作用；游离水杨酸对COX只有较弱且可逆的抑制。但前列腺素减少会削弱胃黏膜的保护层，这正是长期服用易伤胃的原因。羧基使分子呈弱酸性，能与碱成盐（如阿司匹林钠盐）改善水溶性。
In aspirin, the ester group forms when the phenolic hydroxyl of salicylic acid is acetylated by acetic acid; this modification reduces the stomach irritation of free salicylic acid and improves the drug's pharmacokinetics. Once ingested, esterases hydrolyse the ester and release salicylic acid gradually. It is aspirin itself that irreversibly inhibits cyclooxygenase (COX): it transfers its acetyl group covalently to a serine residue in the enzyme's active site, permanently disabling it and blocking prostaglandin synthesis, which brings antipyresis, analgesia and anti-inflammation; free salicylic acid inhibits COX only weakly and reversibly. Fewer prostaglandins, however, weaken the stomach's protective lining - the reason long-term use irritates it. The carboxyl group makes the molecule weakly acidic and lets it form water-soluble salts such as sodium acetylsalicylate.

SN1和SN2反应在机理、立体化学和速率上有什么不同？

How do SN1 and SN2 differ in mechanism, stereochemistry and rate?

SN2是一步双分子反应：亲核试剂从背面进攻，离去基团从另一侧离开，构型发生翻转（瓦尔登翻转），速率与底物和亲核试剂浓度都成正比，偏爱位阻小的甲基和伯碳底物。SN1分两步：先解离生成平面形碳正离子，再被亲核试剂从两侧进攻，产物发生外消旋化，速率只与底物浓度有关，能形成稳定碳正离子的叔碳底物最易发生。极性溶剂对两者影响也不同。
SN2 is a one-step bimolecular reaction: the nucleophile attacks from the back while the leaving group departs from the other side, inverting the configuration (Walden inversion); the rate depends on both the substrate and the nucleophile, and unhindered methyl and primary substrates react best. SN1 proceeds in two steps: dissociation to a planar carbocation, then nucleophile attack from either face, racemizing the product; the rate depends only on the substrate, and tertiary substrates that form stable carbocations react most readily. Solvent polarity affects the two differently as well.

碳正离子的稳定性顺序是什么？为什么是这个顺序？

What is the stability order of carbocations, and why does that order hold?

稳定性顺序为：叔碳正离子大于仲碳正离子大于伯碳正离子大于甲基正离子。烷基通过给电子诱导效应和超共轭效应，把正电荷分散到相邻的C-H或C-C sigma键上，烷基越多、正电荷越分散，越稳定。与双键或苯环共轭也能显著稳定正离子，所以烯丙基和苄基碳正离子格外稳定。
Stability follows: tertiary carbocation > secondary > primary > methyl. Alkyl groups disperse the positive charge through electron-donating inductive effects and hyperconjugation with adjacent C-H or C-C sigma bonds; more alkyl groups mean more charge dispersal and greater stability. Conjugation with double bonds or a benzene ring also stabilizes the cation strongly, so allylic and benzylic carbocations are especially stable.

Where does gasoline come from?

汽油来自石油，石油是远古生物形成的混合物，主要含碳氢化合物。炼油厂把原油分馏和加工，得到适合汽车的汽油。
Gasoline comes from petroleum, a mixture formed from ancient organisms and mainly containing hydrocarbons. Refineries distill and process crude oil to obtain gasoline suitable for cars.
