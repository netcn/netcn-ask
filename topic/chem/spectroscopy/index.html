核磁共振中化学位移受哪些因素影响？

What factors influence chemical shift in NMR?

化学位移 δ 反映核所处电子屏蔽程度：电负性取代基去屏蔽使 δ 增大，氢键与芳香环电流也显著位移。通过与标准物（TMS）比较 δ 值可识别官能团与分子环境。
Chemical shift δ reflects how shielded a nucleus is: electronegative substituents deshield and increase δ, while hydrogen bonding and aromatic ring currents also shift peaks. Comparing δ to a standard (TMS) identifies functional groups and molecular environment.

比尔-朗伯定律的适用前提与局限是什么？

What are the assumptions and limits of the Beer-Lambert law?

A = ε·c·l 假定单色光、稀溶液、无化学相互作用，吸光度与浓度线性。高浓度、缔合/解离、散射或偏离单色光时线性关系失效（偏离比尔定律），需稀释或换波长。
A = ε·c·l assumes monochromatic light, dilute solution, and no chemical interactions, giving linear absorbance vs concentration. At high concentration, association/dissociation, scattering, or non-monochromatic light break linearity (Beer's law deviation), requiring dilution or wavelength change.

核磁共振（NMR）为什么能解析分子结构？

Why can nuclear magnetic resonance (NMR) elucidate molecular structure?

某些原子核（如 ¹H、¹³C）具有自旋和核磁矩，在外磁场中分裂出不同能级。射频脉冲激发后，不同化学环境的核因屏蔽效应产生不同共振频率（化学位移），并提供耦合裂分信息。NMR 因此能确定原子的连接方式和空间关系。
Some nuclei (e.g., ¹H, ¹³C) have spin and a magnetic moment, splitting into levels in an external field. After radiofrequency excitation, nuclei in different chemical environments resonate at different frequencies (chemical shift) due to shielding and show coupling splittings. NMR thus reveals connectivity and spatial relations.

质谱仪如何测定分子质量？

How does a mass spectrometer determine molecular mass?

质谱先把样品分子电离成带电离子，再按质荷比（m/z）在电磁场中分离并检测。分子离子峰给出近似分子量，碎片离子峰反映结构信息。它与色谱联用（如 GC-MS）是复杂混合物分析的主力工具。
Mass spectrometry ionizes sample molecules into charged ions, then separates and detects them by mass-to-charge ratio (m/z) in electric and magnetic fields. The molecular ion peak gives the approximate molecular weight, and fragment peaks reveal structure. Coupled with chromatography (e.g., GC-MS), it is a workhorse for complex mixtures.

质谱中的 m/z 与分子离子峰如何用于定结构？

How are m/z and the molecular ion peak used to determine structure in mass spectrometry?

分子离子峰 M⁺· 的 m/z 近似分子质量，结合氮规则（含奇数 N 则分子量为奇数）与同位素峰（如 Cl、Br 的特征峰比）可推断分子式。碎片离子峰反映断键方式，用于解析结构。
The molecular ion peak M⁺· gives an approximate molecular mass; with the nitrogen rule (odd mass implies odd count of N) and isotope patterns (e.g., characteristic Cl/Br peak ratios) you infer a formula. Fragment ions reveal cleavage patterns for structure elucidation.

红外光谱中羰基伸缩振动频率为何随环张力增大而升高?请用环丁酮与普通脂肪酮的数据说明。

Why does the carbonyl stretching frequency rise as ring strain increases in cyclic ketones? Illustrate with cyclobutanone versus an ordinary aliphatic ketone.

小环酮的C–CO–C键角被压缩至远小于正常sp^2杂化的120°,按Bent规则,羰基碳伸向环内的两个σ单键被迫采用更多p成分,C=O键因此获得更多s成分、力常数增大,伸缩频率随之升高。丙酮的ν(C=O)约1715 cm^-1,而环丁酮升至约1780 cm^-1,环丙酮更高。该规律可用于推断未知物中羰基所处环系的大小。
Small rings compress the C–CO–C angle well below the normal sp2 value of 120°; relieving this angular strain pushes the carbonyl carbon toward more p-like character in its single bonds, increasing s character and force constant of the C=O bond itself, which raises the stretching frequency. Acetone absorbs near 1715 cm−1 whereas cyclobutanone appears around 1780 cm−1 and cyclopropanone higher still. This trend lets one infer ring size around a carbonyl in unknown structures.

朗伯-比尔定律是什么？

What is the Beer–Lambert law?

朗伯-比尔定律指出，溶液对单色光的吸光度 A 与吸光物质浓度 c 和光程 l 成正比：A = εcl，其中 ε 为摩尔吸光系数。它使分光光度法能通过测吸光度来定量未知浓度。该定律只在稀溶液和特定条件下严格成立。
The Beer–Lambert law states that a solution's absorbance A of monochromatic light is proportional to the concentration c and path length l: A = εcl, with ε the molar absorptivity. It lets spectrophotometry quantify unknown concentrations from absorbance. The law holds strictly only for dilute solutions under defined conditions.

紫外可见光谱基于什么原理？

On what principle does UV-Vis spectroscopy rest?

紫外可见光谱记录分子在 200–800 nm 范围内对光的吸收，源于价电子从基态到激发态的跃迁。含共轭双键或发色团的分子在该区有强吸收，吸收强度遵守朗伯-比尔定律 A = εcl。它广泛用于定量测定溶液浓度。
UV-Vis spectroscopy records a molecule's light absorption from 200–800 nm, arising from electronic transitions to excited states. Molecules with conjugated double bonds or chromophores absorb strongly there, and intensity follows the Beer–Lambert law A = εcl. It is widely used to quantify solution concentrations.

Why does NMR mainly look at ¹H and ¹³C?

在1H NMR谱中,N,N-二甲基甲酰胺(DMF)的两个甲基为什么表现为两个单峰而不是一个?

In the 1H NMR spectrum of N,N-dimethylformamide (DMF), why do the two methyl groups appear as two separate singlets rather than one singlet?

因为DMF分子中的C–N部分双键具有部分双键性质,阻碍了两个甲基的快速旋转交换。旋转受阻使两个甲基处于不同电子环境,化学位移不同,故各给一个单峰。升温会加速旋转,两峰逐渐合并为一个信号。
The C–N bond of DMF has partial double-bond character due to amide resonance, which restricts rotation about that bond. The two methyl groups therefore sit in different electronic environments and resonate at distinct chemical shifts, each giving its own singlet. Heating speeds up the restricted rotation until the two signals coalesce into one.

三种二甲苯异构体的1H NMR谱在脂肪区都只有一个甲基单峰,应当如何依据芳香区信号的组数与裂分形态快速区分邻、间、对三种异构体?

All three xylene isomers show just one methyl singlet in the aliphatic region of their 1H NMR spectra. How can you rapidly distinguish the ortho, meta and para isomers from the number and splitting patterns of the aromatic-region signals?

对二甲苯具有高度对称性,四个芳氢全部磁等价,在δ约7.05处表现为4H单峰;邻二甲苯构成AA'BB'体系,芳氢呈两组积分各2H的精细多重峰;间二甲苯对称性最低,H2、H4/6与H5给出三组信号,积分比约为1:2:1并带有间位远程偶合的小裂分。数清芳区信号组数再结合积分比即可完成指认,这也是分子对称性决定磁等价氢数目的直接例证。
Highly symmetric p-xylene makes all four aromatic protons magnetically equivalent, giving a 4H singlet near delta 7.05. o-Xylene forms an AA'BB' system whose aromatic protons appear as two fine multiplets integrating to 2H each, while m-xylene has the lowest symmetry: H2, H4/6 and H5 give three signals with roughly a 1:2:1 integral ratio plus small meta long-range couplings. Counting the aromatic signals together with their integrations assigns each isomer, a direct illustration of how molecular symmetry fixes the number of magnetic-equivalent protons.

分子为什么会有特征吸收光谱？

Why do molecules have characteristic absorption spectra?

分子中电子、振动、转动能级都是量子化的，只有当入射光子的能量恰好等于两个能级差时才会被吸收。不同分子能级结构不同，吸收的频率（波长）就不同，形成特征指纹。这正是红外、紫外可见等光谱定性分析的基础。
Electronic, vibrational, and rotational levels in molecules are quantized, so a photon is absorbed only when its energy exactly matches a level difference. Different molecules have different level structures and thus absorb different frequencies, forming characteristic fingerprints. This is the basis of IR, UV-Vis, and other qualitative spectral analyses.

红外光谱能告诉我们什么？

What can infrared (IR) spectroscopy tell us?

红外光谱测量分子对红外光的吸收，对应化学键的振动和转动能级跃迁。不同官能团在特定波数出现特征吸收峰，例如羰基约 1700 cm⁻¹、O–H 约 3300 cm⁻¹。它常用于鉴定有机物中的官能团。
Infrared spectroscopy measures a molecule's absorption of infrared light, corresponding to vibrational and rotational transitions. Different functional groups show characteristic peaks at specific wavenumbers, e.g., carbonyl near 1700 cm⁻¹ and O–H near 3300 cm⁻¹. It is commonly used to identify functional groups in organics.
