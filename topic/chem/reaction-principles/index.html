阿伦尼乌斯公式是什么？

What is the Arrhenius equation?

阿伦尼乌斯公式表达为k = A·e^(-Ea/RT)，其中k是速率常数，A是指前因子，Ea是活化能，R是气体常数，T是绝对温度。它说明速率常数随温度升高呈指数增长。对该式取对数可线性化处理并求得活化能。
The Arrhenius equation is k = A·e^(-Ea/RT), where k is the rate constant, A the pre-exponential factor, Ea the activation energy, R the gas constant and T the absolute temperature. It shows the rate constant grows exponentially with temperature. Taking logarithms linearizes it and allows determination of activation energy.

为什么碳酸钙在常温下很稳定，在水泥窑高温下却会分解成氧化钙和二氧化碳？

Why is calcium carbonate stable at room temperature yet decomposes into quicklime and carbon dioxide at kiln temperatures?

碳酸钙分解是吸热且熵增的反应：ΔH>0 不利，ΔS>0 有利，判断取决于 ΔG=ΔH-TΔS 的符号。常温下 TΔS 项很小，ΔG>0，反应不自发；高温时 TΔS 项增大到超过 ΔH，ΔG<0，分解才得以进行。因为 ΔH 和 ΔS 本身随温度变化不大，这类分解反应普遍具有明显的起始温度，石灰窑必须维持约900摄氏度才能连续产出生石灰。
The decomposition is endothermic and entropy-increasing: ΔH > 0 opposes it, ΔS > 0 favours it, and the balance is set by the sign of ΔG = ΔH - TΔS. At room temperature the TΔS term is small, so ΔG > 0 and nothing happens; at kiln temperatures TΔS grows until it exceeds ΔH, ΔG turns negative and decomposition proceeds. Since ΔH and ΔS barely change with temperature, such decompositions show a well-defined onset temperature, and lime kilns must be kept near 900 °C to make quicklime continuously.

什么是缓冲溶液？

What is a buffer solution?

缓冲溶液由弱酸及其共轭碱（或弱碱及其共轭酸）组成，能抵抗少量外加酸、碱或稀释而保持pH基本不变。其原理是共轭对通过消耗加入的H+或OH-来维持平衡。人体血液就依靠碳酸-碳酸氢盐缓冲体系维持酸碱稳定。
A buffer solution contains a weak acid and its conjugate base (or a weak base and its conjugate acid) and resists changes in pH upon adding small amounts of acid, base or dilution. The conjugate pair consumes added H+ or OH- to maintain equilibrium. Human blood relies on the carbonic acid-bicarbonate buffer to stay stable.

什么是熵？

What is entropy?

熵是体系混乱度或无序程度的度量，符号为S。在自发过程中孤立体系的熵总趋于增大（热力学第二定律）。气体比液体、液体比固体熵更大，因为微粒运动自由度更高。熵变与焓变共同决定过程是否自发。
Entropy, symbol S, measures the disorder or randomness of a system. In spontaneous processes the entropy of an isolated system tends to increase (the second law of thermodynamics). Gases have higher entropy than liquids and liquids than solids because of greater freedom of motion. Entropy change and enthalpy change together decide spontaneity.

过渡态理论（Eyring 方程）如何把反应速率和活化自由能联系起来？

How does transition-state (Eyring) theory link reaction rate to activation free energy?

Eyring 方程 k = (k_B T / h)·e^(−ΔG‡/RT) 把速率常数与反应物到过渡态的活化吉布斯自由能 ΔG‡ 直接挂钩。温度升高使指数项增大、速率加快；在室温附近，ΔG‡ 每升高约 5.7 kJ/mol，速率大约降低一个数量级。
The Eyring equation, k = (k_B T / h)·exp(−ΔG‡/RT), relates the rate constant directly to the activation Gibbs free energy ΔG‡ between reactants and the transition state. Raising temperature increases the exponential term, and at room temperature each ~5.7 kJ/mol rise in ΔG‡ slows the reaction by roughly a factor of ten.

温度对化学平衡的影响方向如何判断？

How do you determine the direction of temperature's effect on equilibrium?

升温总是使平衡向吸热方向移动，降温则向放热方向移动，与勒夏特列原理一致。判断需先确定正反应是吸热还是放热（由焓变ΔH符号决定）。对于放热反应，升温会使平衡常数K减小、平衡左移。
Heating always shifts equilibrium toward the endothermic direction and cooling toward the exothermic direction, consistent with Le Chatelier's principle. First determine whether the forward reaction is endothermic or exothermic (from the sign of ΔH). For an exothermic reaction, heating lowers K and shifts equilibrium left.

合成氨反应是放热且气体分子数减少的反应，为什么工业上偏偏采用高压而不是过高的温度？

The Haber process is exothermic and reduces the number of gas molecules. Why does industry use high pressure but not extremely high temperature?

勒夏特列原理指出：改变条件时，平衡会向抵消这种改变的方向移动。N2+3H2 可逆生成 2NH3 是放热反应，升温会推动平衡逆向移动、降低产率，所以工业上只用约450摄氏度作为催化剂活性的折中，并不追求更高温度。而高压使气体总体积减小，平衡向生成2分子氨的方向移动，直接提高转化率，因此采用200到300个大气压。实际温度的选择是反应速率与平衡产率之间的平衡：太低反应太慢，太高催化剂易失效。
Le Chatelier's principle says a system at equilibrium shifts to counteract a change in conditions. The reaction N2 + 3H2 ⇌ 2NH3 is exothermic, so higher temperature pushes the equilibrium backward and cuts the yield; industry therefore uses about 450 °C as a compromise for catalyst activity but avoids going hotter. High pressure shrinks the total gas volume, so the equilibrium shifts toward two molecules of ammonia and raises conversion - hence pressures of 200-300 atmospheres. The temperature is really a trade-off between reaction speed and equilibrium yield: too low and the reaction crawls, too high and both yield and catalyst suffer.

什么是吉布斯自由能？

What is Gibbs free energy?

吉布斯自由能G = H - TS，综合了体系的焓、温度和熵。在恒温恒压下，ΔG < 0 的反应自发进行，ΔG = 0 时达平衡，ΔG > 0 则非自发。它是判断化学反应方向与限度的核心判据。
Gibbs free energy G = H - TS combines a system's enthalpy, temperature and entropy. At constant temperature and pressure, a reaction with ΔG < 0 is spontaneous, ΔG = 0 means equilibrium and ΔG > 0 means non-spontaneous. It is the central criterion for reaction direction and extent.

为什么有的过程明明是吸热的，却依然能自发进行？

Why can some processes proceed spontaneously even though they absorb heat?

自发性不能只看焓变（吸热还是放热），还要看熵变。冰在0摄氏度以上融化、硝酸铵在水中溶解都是吸热的，却会自发发生，因为固体变成液体或溶解后分子排列更混乱，体系熵增大，提供了驱动力。真正的判据是吉布斯自由能 ΔG=ΔH-TΔS：放热（ΔH<0）或熵增（ΔS>0）都有利于ΔG<0。吸热而能自发，靠的正是熵增那一项在常温或高温下压过了吸热的代价。
Spontaneity is not decided by enthalpy alone - whether heat is released or absorbed - but also by entropy. Ice melting above 0 °C and ammonium nitrate dissolving in water are endothermic yet spontaneous, because a solid turning into a liquid or a salt dissolving makes the molecular arrangement more disordered, increasing entropy and supplying the driving force. The true criterion is the Gibbs free energy ΔG = ΔH - TΔS: both releasing heat (ΔH < 0) and gaining entropy (ΔS > 0) favour ΔG < 0. An endothermic process stays spontaneous when the +TΔS term outweighs the heat it must absorb.

什么是速率常数？

What is the rate constant?

速率常数k是速率方程中比例系数，反映反应在本征条件下的快慢，只与温度、催化剂和溶剂有关，与浓度无关。k越大反应越快。通过实验测定不同温度下的k可求取活化能。
The rate constant k is the proportionality coefficient in the rate law, reflecting how fast a reaction proceeds under intrinsic conditions; it depends only on temperature, catalyst and solvent, not on concentration. A larger k means a faster reaction. Measuring k at different temperatures lets one determine the activation energy.

洛杉矶型光化学烟雾中,臭氧峰值为何出现在午后而不是早晨的交通高峰?

In Los Angeles-type photochemical smog, why does the ozone peak come in the afternoon rather than during the morning rush hour?

臭氧的净生成由阳光驱动,车流只是提供原料。清晨尾气排出大量NO与VOC,NO先把夜间残留的臭氧滴定消耗;随着太阳升高,NO2在波长小于420nm的光照下光解出氧原子,与O2结合生成O3,同时OH自由基氧化VOC产生的过氧自由基把NO转回NO2却不消耗臭氧,净生成速率午后最强,臭氧遂累积至峰值,傍晚光照减弱后回落。
Net ozone production is driven by sunlight; traffic only supplies the ingredients. The morning rush emits large amounts of NO and VOCs, and that NO first titrates away the ozone left over from the night. As the sun climbs, NO2 photolyzes under light shorter than 420 nm to release oxygen atoms that combine with O2 into O3, while peroxy radicals from the OH oxidation of VOCs recycle NO back to NO2 without touching ozone. Net production is strongest in the early afternoon, so ozone accumulates to a peak and then falls as the light fades toward evening.

什么是溶度积？

What is the solubility product?

溶度积Ksp是难溶电解质饱和溶液中离子浓度幂之积的平衡常数，反映物质的溶解能力。当溶液中离子积大于Ksp时会产生沉淀，小于Ksp时沉淀会溶解。利用Ksp可进行沉淀分离和离子积判断。
The solubility product Ksp is the equilibrium constant equal to the product of ion concentrations (each to its power) in a saturated solution of a sparingly soluble electrolyte, reflecting its dissolution ability. When the ion product exceeds Ksp, a precipitate forms; below it, the precipitate dissolves. Ksp is used for precipitation separation and ion-product judgments.

为什么很多化学反应看起来反应不完，反应物总剩下一部分？

Why do many chemical reactions never seem to finish, always leaving some reactant behind?

这类反应是可逆的，正反应和逆反应同时在发生，当两者速率相等时就达到了化学平衡，反应物和产物的量都不再变化。平衡点由平衡常数K决定，K等于产物浓度幂的乘积与反应物浓度幂的乘积之比；K越大，反应进行得越完全。改变温度等条件能移动平衡，但不能删掉逆反应，所以产物中总会残留一些反应物。
Such reactions are reversible: the forward and reverse reactions proceed simultaneously, and equilibrium is reached when their rates are equal, so reactant and product amounts stop changing. The equilibrium position is set by the equilibrium constant K, the ratio of product concentrations raised to their coefficients over the same for reactants; the larger K, the more complete the reaction. Changing conditions like temperature can shift the equilibrium, but it cannot delete the reverse reaction, so some reactant always remains.

为什么升高温度能让大多数化学反应变快？

Why do higher temperatures speed up most chemical reactions?

反应要发生，分子必须带着足够的能量碰撞，撞断旧键、形成新键，这个能量门槛叫活化能。升温时分子的平均动能变大，更重要的是能量分布的高能尾部变厚，超过活化能的分子比例急剧增加，有效碰撞明显变多。温度每升高10摄氏度，许多反应速率约翻倍，正是这个原因。
For a reaction to happen, molecules must collide with enough energy to break old bonds and form new ones - the activation-energy barrier. Raising the temperature increases average kinetic energy, but the key is that the high-energy tail of the energy distribution swells, so the fraction of molecules above the barrier rises sharply and effective collisions multiply. That is why many reaction rates roughly double for every 10 °C rise.

什么是电离平衡？

What is ionization equilibrium?

弱电解质在水中只部分电离，已电离的离子与未电离的分子之间存在动态平衡，称为电离平衡。以醋酸为例，其电离程度用电离常数Ka衡量。稀释溶液通常会使弱电解质的电离度增大。
A weak electrolyte ionizes only partially in water, and a dynamic equilibrium exists between the ions formed and the un-ionized molecules; this is ionization equilibrium. For acetic acid, the extent is described by the ionization constant Ka. Dilution usually increases the degree of ionization of a weak electrolyte.

强酸和弱酸的区别是什么？

What is the difference between a strong acid and a weak acid?

强酸在水溶液中几乎完全电离，如盐酸、硫酸；弱酸只部分电离，存在电离平衡，如醋酸。因此相同浓度下强酸的氢离子浓度更高、酸性更强。弱酸的酸性强弱可用电离常数比较。
A strong acid ionizes almost completely in water, such as hydrochloric and sulfuric acid, while a weak acid ionizes only partially and establishes an ionization equilibrium, like acetic acid. So at the same concentration a strong acid yields a higher hydrogen ion concentration and is more acidic. The strength of weak acids is compared using ionization constants.

温度如何影响反应速率？

How does temperature affect reaction rate?

一般情况下，温度升高会加快反应速率，因为更多分子获得超过活化能的能量。温度每升高约10摄氏度，许多反应的反应速率可翻倍。这一规律在食品保鲜和工业控制中非常重要。
Generally, raising the temperature speeds up a reaction because more molecules acquire energy above the activation energy. For many reactions, a rise of about 10 degrees Celsius can double the rate. This principle matters in food preservation and industrial process control.

什么是电解质和非电解质？

What are electrolytes and non-electrolytes?

电解质是在水溶液中或熔融状态下能导电的化合物，如食盐、盐酸，因为它们能产生自由移动的离子。非电解质如蔗糖、乙醇，溶于水后仍以分子形式存在，不导电。电解质的强弱决定其导电能力。
An electrolyte is a compound that conducts electricity in aqueous solution or when molten, such as table salt and hydrochloric acid, because it produces freely moving ions. A non-electrolyte like sucrose or ethanol remains molecular in water and does not conduct. The strength of an electrolyte determines its conductivity.

什么是可逆反应？

What is a reversible reaction?

可逆反应是指在同一条件下既能向正方向进行、又能向逆方向进行的反应，常用可逆符号表示。绝大多数化学反应都具有一定的可逆性。可逆反应是建立化学平衡的前提。
A reversible reaction is one that can proceed in both the forward and reverse directions under the same conditions, often shown with a reversible arrow. Most chemical reactions have some reversibility. Reversible reactions are the basis for establishing chemical equilibrium.
