什么是“螯合效应”（chelate effect）？为什么多齿配体形成的配合物通常比单齿配体更稳定？

What is the 'chelate effect,' and why are complexes of polydentate ligands usually more stable than those of monodentate ligands?

螯合效应指多齿配体（如EDTA）以两个及以上配位原子同时与同一中心离子成环，形成的螯合物比相应单齿配体配合物更稳定。其主要热力学根源是熵增：一个多齿配体取代多个单齿配体，使溶液中粒子数增加、混乱度上升，从而ΔG更负、稳定性更高。
The chelate effect is the extra stability of complexes in which a polydentate ligand (such as EDTA) binds the same central ion through two or more donor atoms, forming rings. The main thermodynamic origin is an entropy gain: one polydentate ligand replaces several monodentate ones, increasing the number of particles and disorder in solution, making ΔG more negative and the complex more stable.

配位数与配合物的空间构型有什么关系？

How is coordination number related to a complex's geometry?

配位数指直接与中心金属结合的配位原子数，常见为 4 和 6。配位数 6 多呈八面体（如 [Co(NH₃)₆]³⁺），配位数 4 可为四面体或平面正方形（如 [Ni(CN)₄]²⁻）。配位数由金属离子大小和配体性质决定。
The coordination number is the count of donor atoms directly bound to the metal; 4 and 6 are common. Six-coordinate complexes are usually octahedral (e.g., [Co(NH₃)₆]³⁺), while four-coordinate ones can be tetrahedral or square planar (e.g., [Ni(CN)₄]²⁻). It depends on metal size and ligand nature.

配合物有哪些常见的异构现象？

What common isomerisms occur in coordination complexes?

配合物可有几何异构（如顺式/反式二氯二氨合铂）和旋光异构（手性配合物），还有电离异构、水合异构等。顺铂与反铂因结构不同药效迥异，说明构型对性质影响巨大。立体化学在配合物中同样重要。
Complexes show geometric isomerism (e.g., cis/trans-dichlorodiammineplatinum) and optical isomerism (chiral complexes), plus ionization and hydration isomers. Cisplatin and transplatin differ sharply in efficacy, showing how configuration matters. Stereochemistry is as important in complexes as in organics.

晶体场理论如何解释过渡金属配合物中心离子d轨道的分裂？这与配合物颜色有何关系？

How does crystal field theory explain the splitting of a transition-metal complex's d orbitals, and how is this related to the complex's color?

在配体电场作用下，原本能量简并的五个d轨道会按对称性分裂成不同能级（如八面体场中分裂为t2g和eg两组）。当电子在分裂后的d轨道间跃迁（d-d跃迁）并吸收特定波长的可见光时，配合物便呈现其补色，因此许多过渡金属配合物带有颜色。
Under the ligand electric field, the five degenerate d orbitals split into different energy levels according to symmetry (e.g., into t2g and eg sets in an octahedral field). When electrons transition between these split d levels (d-d transitions) and absorb specific visible wavelengths, the complex shows the complementary color—so many transition-metal complexes are colored.

什么是配位化合物？中心离子与配体是什么？

What is a coordination compound? What are the central ion and ligands?

配位化合物由中心金属离子（或原子）与围绕它的若干配体通过配位键结合而成。配体是提供孤对电子的分子或离子（如 NH₃、Cl⁻、H₂O），中心离子接受电子对。经典例子是六氨合钴(III)离子 [Co(NH₃)₆]³⁺。
A coordination compound consists of a central metal ion (or atom) bonded to surrounding ligands via coordinate bonds. Ligands are molecules or ions donating lone pairs (e.g., NH₃, Cl⁻, H₂O), and the central ion accepts them. A classic example is the hexaamminecobalt(III) ion [Co(NH₃)₆]³⁺.

顺铂（cisplatin）为什么能用于抗癌治疗？它的作用靶点是哪里？

Why can cisplatin be used as an anticancer drug, and what is its molecular target?

顺铂的化学名为顺式二氯二氨合铂(II)，进入细胞后氯离子被水分子取代，活化的铂配合物与DNA两条链上的相邻鸟嘌呤形成链内交联。这种DNA损伤阻断了复制与转录，从而抑制癌细胞增殖，是经典的金属类化疗药物。
Cisplatin (cis-diamminedichloroplatinum(II)) enters cells where chloride is replaced by water, and the activated platinum complex forms intrastrand cross-links between adjacent guanines on DNA. This DNA damage blocks replication and transcription, inhibiting cancer-cell proliferation, making it a classic metal-based chemotherapy drug.

配位化合物中的“配位数”指什么？常见的配位数有哪些？

What does the 'coordination number' mean in a coordination compound, and what values are common?

配位数是指直接与中心原子（或离子）成键的配位原子总数。配合物中常见的配位数有2、4、6，其中六配位（如[Co(NH3)6]³⁺的八面体）和四配位（四面体或平面正方形）最为普遍，具体数值取决于中心离子大小、电荷及配体性质。
The coordination number is the total number of donor atoms directly bonded to the central atom (or ion). Common coordination numbers are 2, 4, and 6, with six-coordinate (e.g., the octahedral [Co(NH3)6]³⁺) and four-coordinate (tetrahedral or square-planar) being most frequent; the value depends on the central ion's size and charge and on the ligands.
