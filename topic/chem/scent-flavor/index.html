为什么香水有前调、中调、后调之分？

Why do perfumes have top, middle, and base notes?

香水由不同挥发速度的香料组成：分子量小、易挥发的做前调（几分钟内闻到），中等做中调（heart），大分子或缓释的做后调（数小时后）。随着时间推移，易挥发成分先散尽，留下持久的底香。这是香精配方的金字塔结构。
A perfume blends fragrant molecules of different volatility: small, volatile ones form the top note you smell in minutes, medium ones the heart, and large or slow-releasing ones the base note hours later. Over time the volatile parts evaporate first, leaving the lasting base. This is the pyramid structure of fragrance formulation.

为什么刚进房间能闻到味道，待一会儿就闻不到了？

Why can you smell an odor when you enter a room but not after a while?

刚进房间时，气味分子持续刺激嗅觉受体产生神经信号，大脑便“闻到”味道；但受体和神经元会对恒定强度的刺激迅速产生适应性（敏感度下降），信号减弱，大脑遂不再将其标记为“有气味”。这是感觉系统的普遍机制，帮助我们把注意力留给变化和新异的刺激。离开一会儿后受体恢复，重新进入又能闻到。
On entering a room, odor molecules stimulate olfactory receptors and the brain registers the smell; but receptors and neurons quickly adapt to a steady stimulus, lowering their sensitivity so the signal fades and the brain stops flagging it as an odor. This sensory adaptation is common across senses and lets us save attention for changes and novel cues. Step out for a while and the receptors recover, so re-entering the room you smell it again.

为什么咖啡豆研磨冲泡后有浓郁香气？

Why does ground and brewed coffee smell so rich?

咖啡豆在烘焙时发生美拉德反应和焦糖化，生成数百种挥发性香气分子，如吡嗪、呋喃类。研磨打破细胞释放这些分子，热水冲泡把它们萃取到杯中。新鲜度和烘焙度都显著影响香味。
During roasting, coffee beans undergo Maillard reactions and caramelization that create hundreds of volatile aroma molecules such as pyrazines and furans. Grinding breaks cells and releases them, and hot water extracts them into the cup. Freshness and roast level strongly shape the scent.

为什么麝香的气味特别持久？

Why does musk scent last so long?

麝香的主要香成分是麝香酮等大环酮类分子，分子量较大、挥发性低，蒸发慢。它们还能与皮肤蛋白结合缓慢释放，所以留香很久。这也是很多香水用麝香作基香的原因。
Musk's main odorants are large macrocyclic ketones like muscone, which are heavy and evaporate slowly. They also bind to skin proteins and release gradually, giving very long persistence. That is why musk is used as a base note in many perfumes.

决定一个分子"闻起来是什么味道"的到底是什么——是化学结构还是分子形状？

What actually determines how a molecule smells—its chemical structure or its molecular shape?

目前主流的"形状理论"认为：气味分子必须契合嗅觉受体特定的结合口袋，形状、大小和官能团共同决定信号。同一种气味（如麝香）可由结构迥异的分子产生，只要它们的空间轮廓匹配同类受体；近年也有"振动理论"尝试用分子振动解释，但仍有争议。
The prevailing "shape theory" holds that an odorant must fit a specific olfactory receptor pocket, with shape, size, and functional groups together determining the signal. The same smell (e.g., musky) can come from structurally diverse molecules if their spatial contours match the same receptor class; a competing "vibrational theory" invoking molecular vibrations remains debated.

为什么丁香能局部镇痛、消毒？

Why can clove relieve pain and disinfect locally?

丁香富含丁香酚，它能作用于牙齿神经的痛觉通道产生局部麻醉感，并有一定抗菌作用。牙医用丁香油临时安抚蛀牙疼痛正是这个原理。但它是刺激性精油，不可内服过量。
Cloves are rich in eugenol, which acts on pain channels in dental nerves to give local numbing and also has antimicrobial effects. Dentists use clove oil to soothe a decayed tooth temporarily for this reason. But it is an irritant essential oil and must not be ingested in excess.

为什么我们能在很低浓度下闻到花香？

Why can we smell floral scents at very low concentrations?

人类嗅觉受体对许多花香分子（如芳樟醇、香叶醇）极其敏感，阈值可低至十亿分之一。嗅觉系统通过大量受体组合识别气味，无需很高浓度即可触发信号。这也是香料只需微量就能调香的原因。
Human olfactory receptors are extremely sensitive to many floral molecules such as linalool and geraniol, with thresholds as low as parts per billion. The olfactory system recognizes odors through many receptor combinations, so it triggers even at tiny concentrations. That is why perfumes need only trace amounts.

为什么某种气味一下就能唤起很久以前的记忆？

Why can a smell instantly bring back old memories?

嗅觉通路直接连到处理情绪和记忆的脑区（杏仁核、海马），绕过了其他感觉的中转，所以气味和记忆绑定得特别紧。
The olfactory pathway links directly to the amygdala and hippocampus, bypassing other relays, so smells bind tightly to emotion and memory.

为什么大蒜有强烈的刺激性气味？

Why does garlic have such a pungent smell?

大蒜含蒜氨酸，被捣碎时蒜氨酸酶将其转化为大蒜素等含硫化合物，这些物质挥发性强、辛辣刺激。大蒜素不稳定，很快分解成多种含硫香味物，所以生蒜味冲、熟蒜味香。
Garlic contains alliin, and when crushed the enzyme alliinase converts it into sulfur compounds like allicin, which are volatile and sharply pungent. Allicin is unstable and quickly breaks down into various sulfur aroma compounds, so raw garlic is harsh while cooked garlic is fragrant.

为什么柑橘类精油（柠檬、橙皮）容易挥发、香气清爽？

Why do citrus essential oils (lemon, orange peel) evaporate easily and smell fresh?

柑橘精油富含柠檬烯等单萜类，分子量小、蒸气压高，所以容易挥发，留下清新嗅感。但也因此易氧化变质、怕光热。它们是很多清洁剂和香水的前调原料。
Citrus oils are rich in monoterpenes like limonene, which are small and have high vapor pressure, so they evaporate readily and leave a fresh scent. For the same reason they oxidize and spoil easily and dislike light and heat. They are common top notes in cleaners and perfumes.

为什么腐烂的鸡蛋、肉有臭鸡蛋味？

Why do rotten eggs and meat smell like rotten eggs?

蛋白质腐败时含硫氨基酸被细菌分解，释放出硫化氢等含硫气体，就是典型的臭鸡蛋味。同时还会产生氨、吲哚等刺鼻物。闻到这种气味说明已变质，不应食用。
As proteins spoil, sulfur-containing amino acids are broken down by bacteria, releasing gases like hydrogen sulfide—the classic rotten-egg smell. Ammonia and indoles also form pungent odors. Such a smell means the food has spoiled and should not be eaten.

为什么樟脑丸能防虫？

Why do mothballs repel insects?

樟脑丸主要成分（樟脑或对二氯苯）会缓慢升华成气体，这种气味对衣蛾等害虫有驱避或毒杀作用。它直接作用在虫子的神经系统。注意要密封衣柜使用，且避免儿童和宠物接触。
The main components of mothballs (camphor or p-dichlorobenzene) slowly sublime into a gas whose odor repels or poisons pests like clothes moths. It acts on the insects' nervous system. Use them sealed in a closet and keep them away from children and pets.

天然气本来没味，为什么家里闻到的燃气有臭味？

Natural gas is odorless—why does household gas smell foul?

燃气公司特意加入微量的硫醇（如乙硫醇），利用其强烈的臭味帮助人们及时发现泄漏。
Suppliers add trace odorants like ethanethiol (a mercaptan) so leaks are detectable by their strong sulfur smell.

香水的“定香”和微胶囊是什么？

What are fixatives and microcapsules in perfume?

定香剂（如麝香）降低挥发、延长留香；微胶囊把香精包住，摩擦时缓慢释放。
Fixatives like musk slow evaporation to prolong scent; microcapsules trap fragrance and release it slowly when rubbed.

为什么香草荚有独特香味？

Why does the vanilla pod have its distinctive scent?

香草荚的香气来自香兰素，这是一种天然酚类香料分子。成熟和发酵过程促使香兰素前体转化积累。它甜润的奶香广泛用于冰淇淋、甜点。
Vanilla's aroma comes from vanillin, a natural phenolic fragrance molecule. Ripening and curing convert and accumulate its precursors. Its sweet, creamy note is widely used in ice cream and desserts.

为什么薄荷让人感觉凉？

Why does mint feel cool?

薄荷中的薄荷醇能激活皮肤上的冷觉感受器（TRPM8通道），大脑误以为接触了低温。其实温度没变，只是神经信号被骗了。所以薄荷糖、薄荷膏让人觉得清凉。
Menthol in mint activates the skin's cold receptors (the TRPM8 channel), so the brain thinks it touched something cold. The actual temperature does not change; the nerve signal is simply tricked. That is why mint candies and balms feel cooling.

为什么臭豆腐闻着臭、吃着香？

Why does stinky tofu smell bad but taste good?

发酵产生的含硫化合物（如硫化氢）和吲哚类气味刺鼻，所以闻着臭。但油炸和调味后，这些分子被部分分解，同时产生酯类、吡嗪等香气物，吃起来反而香。嗅觉和味觉在口中协同放大了香的体验。
Fermentation produces sulfur compounds like hydrogen sulfide and indoles that smell foul. But after frying and seasoning, those molecules partly break down while esters and pyrazines form, creating aroma, so it tastes good. Smell and taste together in the mouth amplify the delicious impression.

为什么有些人觉得香菜（芫荽）有一股"肥皂味"？

Why do some people think cilantro (coriander) tastes like soap?

香菜富含醛类香气分子（如 (E)-2-癸烯醛），它们恰好与肥皂、洗洁精中某些香味成分相似。部分人携带特定的嗅觉受体基因（如 OR6A2），对这些醛格外敏感，于是把清香解读成了"肥皂味"。
Cilantro is rich in aldehyde aroma molecules such as (E)-2-decenal, which resemble certain scent compounds in soaps and detergents. Some people carry olfactory-receptor gene variants (e.g., OR6A2) that make them especially sensitive to these aldehydes, so they perceive the herb as soapy.
