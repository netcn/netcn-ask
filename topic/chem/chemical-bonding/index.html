为什么许多过渡金属配合物（如硫酸铜溶液、血红素）带有鲜艳的颜色？

Why are so many transition-metal complexes, such as copper sulfate solution or heme, brightly coloured?

过渡金属离子的 d 轨道在配体电场作用下发生能级分裂，分裂能的大小恰好落在可见光光子能量范围。电子在分裂后的 d 轨道之间跃迁（d-d 跃迁）时吸收特定波长的可见光，剩下的光被我们看到，配合物因此呈现补色。配体不同、分裂能不同，吸收波长就不同，所以六水合铜离子呈蓝色，而四氨合铜离子呈深蓝。有些颜色还来自配体到金属的电荷转移跃迁，如高锰酸钾的紫色，血红蛋白的颜色则来自铁-卟啉体系对可见光的选择性吸收。
In the electric field of ligands, the d orbitals of a transition-metal ion split into different energies, and the splitting gap falls right in the energy range of visible light. When an electron jumps between split d orbitals (a d-d transition), it absorbs light of a specific wavelength, and we see the complementary colour of what remains. Different ligands give different splittings, hence different absorbed wavelengths - so the hexaaqua copper ion is blue while the tetraammine copper ion is deep blue. Some colours come from ligand-to-metal charge transfer, as in the purple of permanganate, and the colour of hemoglobin arises from the selective visible absorption of the iron-porphyrin system.

稀有气体为什么也能液化？色散力是怎样产生的？

Why can noble gases liquefy, and how does the dispersion force arise?

惰性气体原子虽无永久偶极，但电子云的瞬时涨落会产生瞬时偶极，诱导邻近原子产生诱导偶极，两者相互吸引，这就是色散力（伦敦力）。色散力随电子数增多、极化率增大而增强，所以从氦到氙沸点逐步升高。它存在于一切分子之间，是非极性分子间唯一的吸引来源。
Although noble-gas atoms have no permanent dipole, instantaneous fluctuations of their electron clouds create transient dipoles that induce dipoles in neighbors, which attract each other - this is the dispersion (London) force. It strengthens with electron count and polarizability, so boiling points rise steadily from helium to xenon. It operates between all molecules and is the only attraction among nonpolar ones.

乙烯分子中的pi键是怎样形成的？

How is the pi bond in ethene formed?

乙烯的两个碳各用三个sp2杂化轨道，在平面内以约120度角形成C-C sigma键和C-H sigma键；每个碳剩下的一个未杂化p轨道垂直于分子平面，两个p轨道侧面重叠形成pi键。pi电子云分布在平面上下，结合较弱，容易被试剂进攻，所以烯烃容易发生亲电加成反应。
Each carbon of ethene uses three sp2 hybrids to form the C-C sigma bond and C-H sigma bonds, arranged at about 120 degrees in a plane; each carbon's remaining unhybridized p orbital stands perpendicular to the plane, and the two p orbitals overlap side-on to form the pi bond. The pi electrons sit above and below the plane, are weakly held, and are easily attacked, which is why alkenes undergo electrophilic addition.

氟化氢的沸点为什么反常地高于氯化氢？

Why is the boiling point of HF anomalously higher than that of HCl?

HF分子间能形成相当强的F-H...F氢键，液态HF中还存在链状的氢键网络，打断这些氢键需要大量能量，所以其沸点约19.5摄氏度，反常地高。HCl分子间只有色散力，分子量虽更大，沸点却仅约零下85摄氏度。氢键使HF成为卤化氢中沸点最高者。
HF molecules form quite strong F-H...F hydrogen bonds, and liquid HF has chain-like hydrogen-bonded networks; breaking them requires substantial energy, so HF boils at about 19.5 degrees Celsius, anomalously high. HCl molecules interact only through dispersion forces, so despite a larger molar mass it boils at about -85 degrees Celsius. Hydrogen bonding makes HF the highest-boiling hydrogen halide.

一氧化碳（CO）和氮气（N₂）电子数相同，为什么 CO 有偶极矩而 N₂ 没有？

Carbon monoxide (CO) and nitrogen (N₂) are isoelectronic, so why does CO have a dipole moment while N₂ does not?

N₂ 完全对称、两端电负性相同，偶极矩为零。CO 中氧虽电负性更大，但氧的一个孤对电子以配位键形式提供给碳，且分子的主要共振结构为 ⁻C≡O⁺（碳端带部分负电荷、氧端带部分正电荷），这一电子分布压倒了电负性效应，使净偶极约 0.11 D、方向指向碳端。
N₂ is symmetric with identical end atoms, so its dipole is zero. In CO the more electronegative oxygen would pull electron density toward itself, but one of oxygen's lone pairs is donated to carbon as a dative bond and the dominant resonance form is −C≡O+ (partial negative on carbon, partial positive on oxygen). This distribution outweighs the electronegativity effect, giving CO a net dipole of about 0.11 D pointing toward the carbon end.

什么是电负性？它如何影响化学键的极性？

What is electronegativity and how does it affect bond polarity?

电负性是原子在分子中吸引成键电子能力的量度，鲍林标度中最强的是氟（约4.0）。当两个原子电负性差很大时形成离子键，差较小时形成极性共价键，相等时形成非极性共价键。电负性差越大，键的极性越强。
Electronegativity measures an atom's ability to attract bonding electrons; on the Pauling scale fluorine is the strongest at about 4.0. A large electronegativity difference yields ionic character, a small one gives polar covalent bonds, and zero gives nonpolar bonds. The greater the difference, the more polar the bond.

氨分子为什么是三角锥形而不是平面三角形？

Why is the ammonia molecule trigonal-pyramidal rather than planar trigonal?

氮有5个价电子，与三个氢成键后还剩一对孤对电子。四对电子对按四面体方向排斥，孤对电子占据一个位置，把三个N-H键压缩到约107度，因此氨呈三角锥形。孤对电子还能结合质子，这正是氨气呈碱性的原因。
Nitrogen has five valence electrons and, after bonding to three hydrogens, still has one lone pair. The four electron pairs repel toward tetrahedral directions; the lone pair occupies one position and compresses the N-H bonds to about 107 degrees, giving ammonia a trigonal-pyramidal shape. The lone pair can also accept a proton, which is why ammonia is basic.

为什么冰比水轻？

Why is ice less dense than water?

水结冰时，分子间氢键形成规则的六方晶格，使分子间距增大、结构疏松。因此冰的密度比液态水小，能浮在水面上。这一性质对水生生物的越冬保护非常重要。
When water freezes, intermolecular hydrogen bonds form a regular hexagonal lattice that spreads the molecules apart and makes the structure loose. Ice is therefore less dense than liquid water and floats on it. This property is crucial for protecting aquatic life in winter.

血红蛋白为什么能抓住氧气，又能在组织里把它放出来？

Why can hemoglobin capture oxygen and then release it in the tissues?

血红蛋白分子中心的血红素是一个卟啉环配合物，铁离子位于环中央，通过配位键与四个氮原子结合，还保留着一个空位。肺部氧分压高时，氧分子以配位键挂到铁离子上，形成氧合血红蛋白；到组织处氧分压降低，配位键变弱，氧就被释放。这本质上是金属配合物的可逆配位平衡：氧充足时结合，氧浓度下降时解离，铁离子就是氧的装卸站。
In the center of hemoglobin sits heme, a porphyrin-ring complex with an iron ion held at the middle by coordinate bonds to four nitrogen atoms, leaving one site free. In the lungs, where oxygen partial pressure is high, an O2 molecule coordinates to the iron, forming oxyhemoglobin; in the tissues the pressure is low, the coordinate bond weakens, and oxygen is released. This is essentially a reversible coordination equilibrium of a metal complex: binding when oxygen is plentiful, dissociation when it is scarce - the iron ion is the loading dock for oxygen.

为什么石墨能导电而金刚石不能？

Why can graphite conduct electricity but diamond cannot?

石墨中碳原子呈层状排列，每层内有离域的π电子可在层内自由移动，因此能导电。金刚石中每个碳都以sp3杂化形成四个定域共价键，没有自由电子，故不导电。两者都是碳的同素异形体，性质却截然不同。
In graphite, carbon atoms form layers with delocalized pi electrons that can move freely within a layer, so it conducts. In diamond, each carbon is sp3 hybridized into four localized covalent bonds with no free electrons, so it does not conduct. Both are allotropes of carbon yet behave very differently.

σ 键和 π 键在形成方式和性质上有什么不同？

How do sigma (σ) and pi (π) bonds differ in how they form and in their properties?

σ 键由原子轨道沿键轴"头碰头"重叠形成，电子云绕轴呈圆柱形对称，可绕轴自由旋转、键较强。π 键由 p 轨道"肩并肩"侧向重叠形成，电子云分布在键轴上下，不能绕轴自由旋转、键较弱且较易断裂。
A sigma bond forms by head-on overlap of orbitals along the bond axis, giving cylindrically symmetric electron density that allows free rotation and is relatively strong. A pi bond forms by side-on overlap of p orbitals above and below the axis, blocks free rotation, and is weaker and more easily broken.

什么是极性键和非极性键？

What are polar and nonpolar covalent bonds?

当两个相同原子成键时，电子对均分，形成非极性键，如氧气中的氧氧键。当两个电负性不同的原子成键时，电子对偏向电负性大的一方，形成极性键，如水中的氧氢键。键的极性影响分子极性和溶解性。
When two identical atoms bond, the electron pair is shared equally, forming a nonpolar bond, such as the O-O bond in oxygen. When atoms of different electronegativity bond, the pair shifts toward the more electronegative atom, forming a polar bond, such as the O-H bond in water. Bond polarity affects molecular polarity and solubility.

为什么水的沸点远高于分子量相近的硫化氢和氨？

Why is water's boiling point far higher than that of H2S or NH3 despite similar molar masses?

氢键的强度通常高于范德华力。水分子间形成O-H...O氢键，需要更多热能才能挣脱，所以沸点高达100摄氏度；硫化氢几乎无氢键，氨的氢键又弱于水，因此沸点都低得多。氢键还使水的比热容、熔化热异常偏大，形成许多反常性质。
Hydrogen bonds are typically stronger than van der Waals forces. Water molecules form O-H...O hydrogen bonds that take more thermal energy to break, so water boils at 100 degrees Celsius; H2S has almost no hydrogen bonding and NH3 forms weaker bonds, so both boil much lower. Hydrogen bonding also gives water its unusually large heat capacity and enthalpy of fusion.

什么是杂化轨道？以甲烷为例说明。

What is orbital hybridization? Explain using methane.

杂化是原子在成键时把能量相近的原子轨道混合，重新组合成数目相等、方向更合理的新轨道。甲烷中碳的一个 2s 与三个 2p 轨道杂化为四个等价的 sp³ 轨道，指向正四面体四个顶点，键角约109.5°。
Hybridization mixes atomic orbitals of similar energy to form new equivalent orbitals oriented for bonding. In methane, carbon's one 2s and three 2p orbitals hybridize into four equivalent sp³ orbitals pointing to a tetrahedron's corners, with bond angles near 109.5°.

甲烷为什么是正四面体形？碳原子怎样形成四个完全等价的C-H键？

Why is methane tetrahedral, and how does carbon form four identical C-H bonds?

碳原子把2s轨道和三个2p轨道杂化成四个能量相同的sp3杂化轨道，每个轨道与一个氢的1s轨道沿轴向重叠成键。四个sp3杂化轨道指向正四面体的四个顶点，夹角约109.5°，所以甲烷是正四面体形，四个C-H键完全等价。
Carbon hybridizes its 2s and three 2p orbitals into four equivalent sp3 hybrid orbitals, each overlapping a hydrogen 1s orbital along its axis to form a bond. The four sp3 hybrids point to the corners of a regular tetrahedron with angles of about 109.5°, so methane is tetrahedral with four fully equivalent C-H bonds.

水分子为什么是弯曲的（V形）而不是直线形的？

Why is the water molecule bent rather than linear?

根据VSEPR（价层电子对互斥）理论，氧原子有6个价电子，与两个氢成键后还剩两对孤对电子。四对电子对按四面体方向互相排斥，两对孤对电子排斥力更强，把O-H键压缩到约104.5°，所以水分子呈弯曲状而不是直线形。孤对电子不参与成键，却决定了分子的整体形状。
By VSEPR (valence-shell electron-pair repulsion) theory, oxygen has six valence electrons and, after bonding to two hydrogens, keeps two lone pairs. The four electron pairs repel toward tetrahedral directions, and the two lone pairs repel more strongly, squeezing the O-H bonds to about 104.5°, so the molecule is bent rather than linear. Lone pairs do not bond but still fix the overall shape.

为什么CO2是直线形而SO2是V形？
