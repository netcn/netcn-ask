SN2反应中亲核试剂为什么必须从离去基团的背面进攻？这导致产物构型发生什么变化？

Why must the nucleophile in an SN2 reaction attack from the backside of the leaving group, and what change in configuration results?

在SN2反应中，亲核试剂从离去基团相反的一侧进攻中心碳，因为这样能与部分形成的键轨道最大重叠、同时把离去基团推开，形成五配位的过渡态。背面进攻导致中心碳的构型完全翻转，即瓦尔登翻转（Walden inversion）。
In an SN2 reaction the nucleophile attacks the central carbon from the side opposite the leaving group, because this maximizes orbital overlap for bond formation while displacing the leaving group, giving a five-coordinate transition state. Backside attack inverts the configuration at the carbon completely—the Walden inversion.

消除反应 E2 是什么？

What is an E2 elimination reaction?

E2 是双分子消除：碱同时夺取 β-氢、离去基团从同一碳的邻位离去，一步形成碳碳双键，反应呈反式共平面最有利。它与 SN2 竞争，常在强碱、高位阻条件下占优。扎伊采夫规则常预测主要烯烃产物。
E2 is a bimolecular elimination where a base removes a β-hydrogen as the leaving group departs from the adjacent carbon in one step, forming a C=C double bond; anti-periplanar geometry is most favorable. It competes with SN2 and dominates under strong base and steric conditions. Zaitsev's rule often predicts the major alkene.

均相催化与多相催化有何不同？

How does homogeneous differ from heterogeneous catalysis?

均相催化中催化剂与反应物同处一相（常为液相），活性中心均匀、选择性高但难分离回收。多相催化中催化剂为另一相（多为固体），反应物在其表面吸附活化，易与产物分离、适合连续工业过程。两者机理都涉及降低活化能。
In homogeneous catalysis the catalyst and reactants share one phase (often liquid), giving uniform active sites and high selectivity but difficult recovery. In heterogeneous catalysis the catalyst is a separate phase (often solid); reactants adsorb and activate on its surface, easing separation for continuous industry. Both lower activation energy.

苯的亲电取代反应（如硝化）一般经历怎样的机理？为什么苯环需要先被活化才能发生反应？

What is the general mechanism of electrophilic aromatic substitution of benzene (e.g., nitration), and why must the ring be activated to react?

亲电取代通常分两步：亲电试剂（如硝酰阳离子NO2⁺）进攻苯环形成带正电的σ络合物（芳正离子）中间体，随后失去质子恢复芳香性。由于苯环电子云密度有限且芳香性稳定，反应需强亲电试剂或缺电子条件推动，故常常需要催化剂（如硝化中的浓硫酸）活化亲电体。
Electrophilic substitution generally proceeds in two steps: the electrophile (e.g., the nitronium ion NO2⁺) attacks the ring to form a positively charged σ-complex (arenium ion) intermediate, which then loses a proton to restore aromaticity. Because the benzene ring has limited electron density and its aromatic stabilization resists attack, a strong electrophile or catalyst (such as concentrated sulfuric acid in nitration) is needed to activate the electrophile.

酯的水解机理是怎样的？

What is the mechanism of ester hydrolysis?

酸性条件下羰基先质子化，水亲核进攻形成四面体中间体再脱醇；碱性条件下氢氧根直接进攻，生成羧酸盐并使平衡彻底向右。March 的《高等有机化学》给出两种路径的详细步骤与速率规律。
Under acid the carbonyl is protonated, water attacks to a tetrahedral intermediate, then alcohol leaves; under base hydroxide attacks directly to give a carboxylate, driving the equilibrium fully right. March's 'Advanced Organic Chemistry' details both pathways and kinetics.

SN1 与 SN2 亲核取代反应有何本质区别？

What is the essential difference between SN1 and SN2 substitution?

SN2 是一步协同过程，亲核试剂从离去基团背面进攻，伴随构型翻转；SN1 先离解成平面碳正离子，再被进攻，常导致外消旋化。Hughes 与 Ingold 在 1935 年用动力学与立体化学建立了这一分类。
SN2 is a one-step concerted attack from the backside of the leaving group, inverting configuration; SN1 first ionizes to a planar carbocation then is attacked, often giving racemization. Hughes and Ingold established this classification by kinetics and stereochemistry in 1935.

亲核取代或消除反应中为什么会发生碳正离子重排？举一个常见例子。

Why do carbocation rearrangements occur in nucleophilic substitution or elimination reactions, and what is a common example?

当反应经由碳正离子中间体时，若邻近基团迁移能使正电荷转移到更稳定的位置（如从仲碳正离子变为更稳定的叔碳正离子），就会发生重排。典型例子是新戊基体系中1,2-氢迁移或烷基迁移，生成更稳定的碳正离子，从而改变最终产物的碳骨架。
When a reaction proceeds through a carbocation intermediate, a neighboring group migrates if doing so places the positive charge on a more stable site (e.g., from a secondary to a more stable tertiary carbocation), causing rearrangement. A classic case is a 1,2-hydride or alkyl shift in neopentyl-type systems, yielding a more stable carbocation and altering the carbon skeleton of the final product.

烷烃的自由基卤代反应一般经历哪三个阶段？为什么该反应的选择性较差？

What three stages does the free-radical halogenation of alkanes generally go through, and why is the reaction poorly selective?

自由基卤代反应经历链引发（卤素分子均裂产生自由基）、链增长（夺取氢生成烷基自由基并继续传播）和链终止（自由基相互结合）三个阶段。由于不同C-H键的自由基取代速率相差不大，且反应放热量大，常生成多种一卤代物混合物，选择性较差（氯代尤甚，溴代稍好）。
Free-radical halogenation proceeds through initiation (homolysis of the halogen to form radicals), propagation (hydrogen abstraction to give an alkyl radical that continues the chain), and termination (radicals combining). Because various C-H bonds are abstracted at similar rates and the reaction is highly exothermic, multiple monohalogenated products form, giving poor selectivity—especially for chlorination, with bromination somewhat better.

苯的亲电取代反应有哪些例子？

What are examples of electrophilic substitution on benzene?

苯环富电子，易受带正电的亲电试剂进攻，发生取代而非加成。典型反应包括硝化（与混酸生成硝基苯）、卤代（FeX₃ 催化下上卤素）和傅-克烷基化/酰基化。这些反应保留了芳香环的稳定结构。
Benzene's electron-rich ring is attacked by electrophiles, undergoing substitution rather than addition. Typical reactions include nitration (with mixed acid to nitrobenzene), halogenation (halogen under FeX₃), and Friedel–Crafts alkylation/acylation. These preserve the stable aromatic ring.

中和反应的实质是什么？

What is the essence of a neutralization reaction?

强酸强碱中和的实质是 H⁺ 与 OH⁻ 结合生成水：H⁺ + OH⁻ → H₂O。反应放热，等当量点时溶液趋近于中性（pH≈7）。盐是酸碱中和的副产物，滴定常利用此原理。
The essence of strong acid–base neutralization is H⁺ combining with OH⁻ to form water: H⁺ + OH⁻ → H₂O. It is exothermic, and at equivalence the solution approaches neutrality (pH≈7). Salt is the byproduct, and titrations exploit this principle.

勒夏特列原理说什么？

What does Le Chatelier's principle state?

勒夏特列原理指出，处于平衡的体系若受到浓度、温度或压强等外界扰动，会自动调整以减弱这种扰动。例如增大反应物浓度会使平衡向生成物方向移动。它是预测和调控化学平衡的重要工具。
Le Chatelier's principle states that a system at equilibrium, when disturbed by changes in concentration, temperature, or pressure, shifts to counteract the disturbance. For example, adding reactant drives the equilibrium toward products. It is a key tool for predicting and controlling chemical equilibrium.

氧化还原反应的本质是什么？

What is the essence of a redox reaction?

氧化还原反应的本质是电子的转移：失去电子的物质被氧化，得到电子的物质被还原。化合价升高对应氧化，降低对应还原，两者总是同时发生。它在电池、燃烧、呼吸和冶金中无处不在。
A redox reaction is fundamentally an electron transfer: the species losing electrons is oxidized, the one gaining them is reduced. Rising oxidation state means oxidation, falling means reduction, and the two always occur together. It appears everywhere—in batteries, combustion, respiration, and metallurgy.
