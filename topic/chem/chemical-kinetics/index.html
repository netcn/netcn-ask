过渡态理论的核心思想是什么？

What is the core idea of transition state theory?

过渡态理论认为反应物与高能的活化络合物（过渡态）快速达成平衡，其转化为产物的速率由 k_BT/h 乘以平衡常数决定，并引入活化焓与活化熵。它比阿伦尼乌斯更细致地解释温度与结构对速率的影响。
Transition state theory assumes reactants equilibrate rapidly with a high-energy activated complex (the transition state), whose conversion to products proceeds at k_BT/h times an equilibrium constant, introducing activation enthalpy and entropy. It explains temperature and structural effects more deeply than Arrhenius.

别洛乌索夫-扎鲍京斯基(BZ)反应为什么能在成分均匀的溶液中自发产生颜色的周期性振荡？

Why can the Belousov-Zhabotinsky reaction spontaneously produce periodic color oscillations in a compositionally uniform solution?

因为总包的溴酸盐氧化丙二酸反应内部藏着自催化正反馈与Br⁻抑制负反馈的耦合，远离平衡态时形成浓度极限环。机理：FKN模型中HBrO₂自催化生成迅速把Ce³⁺氧化为Ce⁴⁺，指示剂随之变色；Br⁻积累到临界浓度便关闭自催化通道并被缓慢消耗，同时Ce⁴⁺氧化丙二酸后被还原回Ce³⁺，体系复位进入下一轮。HBrO₂、Br⁻与Ce⁴⁺/Ce³⁺三个变量经延迟反馈交替升降，颜色即周期性变化。
The overall bromate oxidation of malonic acid conceals coupled feedback: autocatalytic HBrO2 growth paired with bromide inhibition forms a concentration limit cycle far from equilibrium. In the FKN model, autocatalytic HBrO2 rapidly oxidizes Ce3+ to Ce4+, flipping the indicator color; when Br- reaches a critical level it shuts down the autocatalytic channel and is slowly consumed while Ce4+ oxidizes malonic acid and is reduced back to Ce3+, resetting the cycle. The delayed rise-and-fall of HBrO2, Br-, and the cerium couple drives the periodic color change.

气相单分子反应为何在高压下表现为一级、低压下却变成二级？Lindemann机理怎样统一解释这一反转？

Why does a gas-phase unimolecular reaction look first-order at high pressure yet second-order at low pressure, and how does the Lindemann mechanism reconcile the reversal?

因为真正分解的不是普通分子A，而是经碰撞获得的富能分子A*，压力决定A*的去活化与分解谁更快。机理：碰撞把A活化为A*，A*或被淬灭回A，或越过能垒分解成产物；对A*取稳态近似可得速率=k₁k₂[A]²/(k₋₁[A]+k₂)。高压下碰撞频繁，k₋₁[A]≫k₂，速率正比于[A]，表观为一级；低压下碰撞稀少，k₂≫k₋₁[A]，速率正比于[A]²，转为二级。这一时间滞后思想日后发展为RRKM理论。
What decomposes is not ordinary molecule A but an energized molecule A* built by collisions, and pressure decides whether quenching or decomposition consumes A* faster. Collisions activate A into A*, which either sheds its excess energy in another collision or clears the barrier to products; a steady-state treatment of A* gives a rate of k1 times k2 times [A] squared divided by k-1[A] plus k2. At high pressure deactivation dominates, the denominator is ruled by k-1[A], and the rate is first-order in A; at low pressure collisions grow scarce, decomposition wins, and the rate turns second-order. This time-lag picture later matured into RRKM theory.

阿伦尼乌斯方程描述了什么关系？

What relationship does the Arrhenius equation describe?

k = A·e^(−Ea/RT) 把速率常数 k 与温度 T 联系，Ea 为活化能，A 为指前因子。温度升高或 Ea 降低都会使 k 指数增大，从而加快反应。
k = A·e^(−Ea/RT) links the rate constant k to temperature T, with Ea the activation energy and A the pre-exponential factor. Higher T or lower Ea exponentially increases k, speeding the reaction.

碰撞理论如何解释反应速率？

How does collision theory explain reaction rates?

碰撞理论认为，反应发生的前提是分子相互碰撞，但只有能量足够高且取向合适的有效碰撞才能引发反应。升高温度增加了高能分子比例，因此加快反应。该理论说明了浓度、温度和催化剂的作用。
Collision theory holds that reactions require molecules to collide, but only effective collisions with enough energy and proper orientation lead to reaction. Raising temperature increases the fraction of high-energy molecules, speeding the reaction. The theory explains the roles of concentration, temperature, and catalysts.

什么是反应速率方程和反应级数？

What are the rate law and reaction order?

速率方程是把反应速率与反应物浓度联系的经验式，如 v = k[A]^m[B]^n。各浓度指数之和 m+n 为总反应级数，k 为速率常数。级数由实验测定，不一定等于化学计量数。
A rate law is an empirical relation linking reaction rate to reactant concentrations, e.g., v = k[A]^m[B]^n. The sum m+n is the overall reaction order, and k is the rate constant. Order is found experimentally and need not match stoichiometric coefficients.

什么是一级反应的半衰期？

What is the half-life of a first-order reaction?

半衰期是反应物浓度降到初始值一半所需的时间。一级反应的半衰期 t½ = ln2 / k，只与速率常数 k 有关，与初始浓度无关。放射性衰变和许多药物代谢都遵循一级动力学。
Half-life is the time for a reactant's concentration to fall to half its initial value. For a first-order reaction t½ = ln2 / k, depending only on the rate constant k, not the starting concentration. Radioactive decay and many drug metabolisms follow first-order kinetics.

什么是活化能？催化剂如何降低它？

What is activation energy and how do catalysts lower it?

活化能是反应物分子转变为产物必须越过的最低能量壁垒。催化剂提供一条能量更低的反应路径（新机理），从而降低活化能、加快反应，但不改变平衡位置。例如酶把生化反应的活化能大幅降低。
Activation energy is the minimum energy barrier reactants must overcome to become products. A catalyst offers a lower-energy pathway (a new mechanism), lowering the activation energy and speeding the reaction without shifting equilibrium. Enzymes, for example, greatly lower the activation energy of biochemical reactions.

活化能是什么？

What is activation energy?

活化能是反应物分子达到能发生反应的过渡态所需的最低能量壁垒。壁垒越低，达到它的分子比例越高，反应速率越快；催化剂的作用正是降低这一壁垒。
Activation energy is the minimum energy barrier reactants must reach (the transition state) to react. A lower barrier means more molecules can cross it, so the reaction is faster; catalysts work by lowering this barrier.

用Arrhenius方程解释：升高温度为什么几乎总能加快化学反应的速率？

In terms of the Arrhenius equation, why does heating almost always make a chemical reaction go faster?

因为温度升高使速率常数k按指数规律增大，有效碰撞随之增多。机理：Arrhenius方程k=A·exp(-Ea/RT)中，活化能Ea基本不随温度改变，T升高时玻尔兹曼分布中能量越过Ea能垒的碰撞分数呈指数增长。Ea越大升温效果越显著，例如Ea约50 kJ/mol的反应从298 K升到308 K，k大约翻倍；例外只有少数表观活化能为负或含预平衡的复杂体系。
Heating grows the rate constant exponentially, multiplying the collisions that actually react. In k = A·exp(-Ea/RT) the activation energy Ea hardly varies with temperature, so a higher T steeply increases the Boltzmann fraction of collisions able to clear the Ea barrier. The effect scales with Ea: a reaction with Ea around 50 kJ/mol roughly doubles its rate constant between 298 K and 308 K. Only rare systems with negative apparent activation energies or pre-equilibria break the rule.

同一个反应中C–H键断裂为什么总比对应的C–D键快得多？这一速率差还能告诉我们什么？

In one and the same reaction, why does breaking a C-H bond run much faster than breaking its C-D counterpart, and what else does that rate gap reveal?

因为氘更重，C–D键的振动频率与零点能都比C–H键低，翻越同一势垒时C–H路线的有效能垒更矮。机理：同位素替换几乎不改变势能面，改变的是振子零点能；C–H伸缩振动约3000 cm⁻¹而C–D约2200 cm⁻¹，反应物一侧C–H零点能更高，当速控步骤拉断该键时过渡态上这一振动消失，两侧零点能差收窄，净效果是C–H通道能垒更低，室温下kH/kD典型达6至8，数值远超此限往往提示氢经隧道效应穿垒。
Deuterium is heavier, so a C-D bond vibrates more slowly and carries less zero-point energy than its C-H twin, leaving the C-H route the lower effective barrier across the same electronic landscape. Isotopic substitution barely moves the potential energy surface; what changes is zero-point energy. A C-H stretch near 3000 wavenumbers drops to roughly 2200 for C-D, giving C-H the larger reactant-side advantage, and when the rate-determining step breaks that bond the vibration vanishes at the transition state, narrowing the gap into a primary kinetic isotope effect - typical room-temperature kH/kD values of six to eight, while far larger ones hint that hydrogen tunnels through rather than climbs the barrier.

碰撞理论怎样解释反应速率？

How does collision theory explain reaction rates?

反应发生的前提是粒子发生有效碰撞：既要达到足够能量（超过活化能）又要有合适取向。温度升高使更多碰撞具备能量，浓度增大则碰撞更频繁，二者都加快反应。
A reaction requires effective collisions: enough energy (above activation energy) and correct orientation. Higher temperature gives more energetic collisions; higher concentration gives more frequent collisions—both speed the reaction.
