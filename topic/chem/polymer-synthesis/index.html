Resin and hardener stay stable apart; mixed, they crosslink into a 3D network. Ratio and thorough mixing decide the final strength.

可控自由基聚合(ATRP/RAFT)与普通自由基聚合相比,分子量分布为什么明显更窄?

Compared with conventional free-radical polymerization, why do controlled radical polymerizations such as ATRP and RAFT give much narrower molecular weight distributions?

普通自由基聚合中链增长与链终止竞争剧烈,链一旦引发便不可逆地增长至死亡,各链寿命差异大,故分布接近高斯且PDI常在1.5以上。ATRP通过卤原子可逆钝化使活性种与休眠种之间快速交换,RAFT则通过硫代羰基硫化合物可逆加成断裂链转移实现同样的休眠-活化平衡。每条链经历多次活化-钝化循环,增长机会被均摊,所有链近似同步生长,数均分子量随转化率线性上升,PDI可降到1.1左右甚至更低。
In conventional radical polymerization, propagation competes irreversibly with termination: once initiated, a chain grows until it dies, chain lifetimes differ widely, and the distribution approaches Gaussian with PDI typically above 1.5. ATRP establishes a rapid equilibrium between active and dormant species via reversible halogen atom transfer, while RAFT achieves the same dormancy-activation balance through reversible addition-fragmentation chain transfer with thiocarbonylthio compounds. Every chain undergoes many activation-deactivation cycles, growth opportunities are equalized, all chains grow nearly synchronously, Mn rises linearly with conversion, and PDI drops to about 1.1 or lower.

Why is PVC made from vinyl chloride monomer?

为什么阴离子聚合能得到窄分子量分布的聚苯乙烯,而传统自由基聚合很难做到?

Why does anionic polymerization give narrow-molecular-weight-distribution polystyrene while conventional free-radical polymerization hardly ever can?

阴离子聚合的活性链端是碳负离子,在严格除水除氧的体系中既不双基终止也难发生链转移,所有链几乎同步引发、同步增长。分子量分布因此接近泊松分布,重均与数均之比可低至1.05左右。自由基聚合中引发剂缓慢分解、链转移和随机双基终止叠加在一起,分散度通常落在1.5到2.0以上。
In anionic polymerization the growing ends are carbanions that neither terminate bimolecularly nor readily transfer in rigorously dried, oxygen-free systems, so essentially every chain starts and grows in step. The distribution therefore approaches a Poisson limit, with weight-average over number-average ratios as low as about 1.05. Slow initiator decomposition, chain transfer, and random radical termination combine to keep radical dispersities above roughly 1.5 to 2.0.

加聚与缩聚的主要区别是什么？

What is the main difference between addition and condensation polymerization?

加聚是烯烃等不饱和单体通过双键打开反复连接，不释放小分子，如聚乙烯。缩聚是带双官能团单体缩合，每步脱去水、醇等小分子，如聚酯、聚酰胺；前者产物与单体元素组成相同，后者不同。
Addition polymerization opens double bonds of unsaturated monomers (e.g., ethylene) and links them without byproduct, as in polyethylene. Condensation polymerization joins bifunctional monomers while eliminating small molecules like water or alcohol, as in polyesters and polyamides; the latter changes elemental composition.

同样是把小分子连成长链,加聚和缩聚的本质区别在哪里?

Both build long chains from small molecules — what truly separates addition from condensation polymerization?

区别在于是否脱去小分子以及链如何生长。加聚(如乙烯变聚乙烯)打开碳碳双键彼此相连,原子全部进入长链,一经引发便快速长成高分子量;缩聚(如己二酸与己二胺缩成尼龙66)每形成一根酰胺键就脱去一分子水,靠官能团两两碰撞逐步延长,反应程度须达99%以上才有足够长的链——这是卡罗瑟斯方程的硬约束。工程后果也不同:加聚物主链耐水解而化学回收难,缩聚物含极性键更耐温却怕水解。
Whether small molecules drop out and how chains grow. Addition polymerization (ethylene to polyethylene) opens C=C bonds with one hundred percent of atoms entering the chain; once initiated, chains race to high molecular weight. Condensation (adipic acid plus hexamethylenediamine to nylon-6,6) ejects a water molecule per amide bond and extends only by pairwise functional-group collisions, demanding extreme conversion above ninety-nine percent for useful chain lengths — Carothers equation sets the hard rule. Consequences differ too: addition polymers resist hydrolysis but are harder to reprocess chemically; condensation polymers carry polar bonds that love strength yet fear hydrolysis.
