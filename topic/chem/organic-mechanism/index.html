E1 与 E2 消除反应如何区分？

How are E1 and E2 elimination reactions distinguished?

E2 是单步反式共平面消除，碱夺取 β-H 同时离去基离开，速率与底物和碱都有关，遵循扎伊采夫或霍夫曼规则取决于碱。E1 先离解成碳正离子再失去 β-H，速率只与底物有关，常伴重排，有利于能稳定碳正离子的叔底物与弱碱条件。
E2 is a one-step anti-periplanar elimination where base removes β-H as the leaving group leaves, rate depends on substrate and base, and follows Zaitsev or Hofmann rules by base strength. E1 first forms a carbocation then loses β-H, rate depends only on substrate, often rearranges, and favors tertiary substrates with weak base.

芳香族亲电取代（EAS）的机理步骤是什么？

What are the mechanistic steps of electrophilic aromatic substitution (EAS)?

先由 Lewis 酸（如 AlCl₃）生成强亲电试剂，芳环 π 电子进攻形成带正电的 σ 络合物（Wheland 中间体）破坏芳香性，随后碱夺去质子恢复芳香性得到取代产物。取代基通过给/吸电子效应与定位效应调控速率与区位。
A Lewis acid (e.g., AlCl₃) first generates a strong electrophile; the aromatic π system attacks it to form a positively charged σ-complex (Wheland intermediate) that loses aromaticity, then a base removes a proton to restore aromaticity and yield the substituted product. Substituents control rate and position via electronic and directing effects.

SN1 与 SN2 反应机理的关键区别是什么？

What are the key differences between SN1 and SN2 mechanisms?

SN2 是一步协同过程，亲核试剂从背面进攻，速率与底物和亲核试剂均有关，易发生构型翻转，利于位阻小的伯卤代烃。SN1 先离解成碳正离子再被进攻，速率只与底物有关，常伴重排与外消旋化，利于能稳定碳正离子的叔卤代烃。
SN2 is a one-step concerted backside attack whose rate depends on both substrate and nucleophile, giving inversion and favoring unhindered primary halides. SN1 first ionizes to a carbocation then is attacked, rate depends only on substrate, often with rearrangement and racemization, favoring tertiary halides that stabilize carbocations.

双分子亲核取代（SN2）反应为何总是伴随着构型翻转？请从亲核试剂的进攻方向和过渡态结构解释Walden翻转，并说明叔卤代烃为何难以发生SN2反应。

Why does the bimolecular nucleophilic substitution (SN2) reaction always proceed with inversion of configuration? Explain the Walden inversion through the direction of nucleophilic attack and the transition-state geometry, and account for why tertiary alkyl halides resist SN2.

亲核试剂须沿C-X键轴从离去基团背面进攻，使其填满电子的轨道与σ*(C-X)反键轨道有效重叠，正面进攻既受排斥又无重叠。反应经五配位三角双锥过渡态：C-Nu部分成键、C-X部分断裂，中心碳上三个基团如雨伞翻折般摆动，产物因而构型翻转。背面路径对位阻敏感，叔碳被三个烷基包围，活化能过高，故叔卤代烃几乎不发生SN2。
The nucleophile must attack along the axis of the C-X bond from the face opposite the leaving group, so that its filled orbital overlaps the σ*(C-X) antibonding orbital effectively; front-side approach meets repulsion and yields no useful overlap. The reaction runs through a five-coordinate, trigonal-bipyramidal transition state in which the C-Nu bond is partly formed while C-X is breaking, and the three substituents swing over like an inverting umbrella, so the product emerges with inverted configuration. Because backside access is sterically sensitive, a tertiary center surrounded by three alkyl groups raises the barrier so high that SN2 essentially cannot occur.

马氏规则在不对称烯烃加成中如何应用？

How is Markovnikov's rule applied to addition to unsymmetrical alkenes?

在 HX 等极性加成中，氢加在含氢较多的双键碳上，卤素加在含氢较少的碳上，因形成更稳定的碳正离子中间体。反马氏加成（如过氧化物存在下的 HBr）则经自由基机理实现。
In polar addition of HX to an unsymmetrical alkene, H adds to the carbon with more hydrogens and X to the other, because the more stable carbocation intermediate forms. Anti-Markovnikov addition (e.g., HBr with peroxides) proceeds via a radical mechanism.
