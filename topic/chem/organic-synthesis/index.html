Heck 反应和 Sonogashira 反应在成键类型和催化剂上有何区别？

How do the Heck reaction and the Sonogashira reaction differ in bond type formed and catalysts used?

Heck 反应是钯催化下卤代芳烃与烯烃偶联生成取代烯烃，关键步骤为氧化加成、烯烃迁移插入和 β-氢消除；Sonogashira 反应由钯/铜协同催化，卤代烃与末端炔烃经氧化加成、铜炔化物转金属化、还原消除偶联，生成芳基取代的内炔。成键类型上前者构筑 C(sp2)-C(sp2) 键，后者构筑 C(sp2)-C(sp) 键。
The Heck reaction couples aryl halides with alkenes under palladium catalysis to give substituted alkenes, proceeding through oxidative addition, migratory insertion of the alkene, and beta-hydride elimination; the Sonogashira reaction uses Pd/Cu cocatalysis to couple aryl halides with terminal alkynes through oxidative addition, transmetalation of a copper acetylide, and reductive elimination to give aryl-substituted internal alkynes. The former forms a C(sp2)-C(sp2) bond and the latter a C(sp2)-C(sp) bond.

点击化学（click chemistry）的核心思想是什么？CuAAC 反应为什么被称为「点击」反应？

What is the core idea of click chemistry, and why is the CuAAC reaction called a click reaction?

点击化学由 Sharpless 提出，主张用少数高选择性、高产率、条件温和的反应快速连接分子模块。CuAAC（铜催化叠氮-炔环加成）几乎定量生成 1,4-三唑且无副产物，符合点击反应的标准，因此成为典型代表。
Click chemistry, proposed by Sharpless, advocates using a small set of highly selective, high-yield, mild reactions to join molecular building blocks rapidly. CuAAC (copper-catalyzed azide-alkyne cycloaddition) gives near-quantitative 1,4-triazoles with no byproducts and thus epitomizes a click reaction.

不对称催化合成中，手性催化剂是如何实现对映体过量（ee）控制的？以 Sharpless 环氧化为例说明。

In asymmetric catalysis, how does a chiral catalyst achieve enantiomeric excess (ee) control? Explain using the Sharpless epoxidation.

手性催化剂通过过渡态中非对映异构相互作用的能差，使一种对映体路径的活化能更低，从而主导产物。Sharpless 环氧化中，钛-酒石酸酯配合物与过氧叔丁醇配位，使烯丙醇的烯键只能从某一面进攻，得到高 ee 值的环氧产物。
A chiral catalyst lowers the activation energy of one enantiomeric pathway via diastereomeric transition-state interactions, making that path dominant. In Sharpless epoxidation, a titanium-tartrate complex coordinates tert-butyl hydroperoxide so the allylic alcohol's alkene is attacked preferentially from one face, giving epoxides with high ee.

过渡金属催化偶联反应中，氧化加成、迁移插入和还原消除三个基元步骤分别起什么作用？

In transition-metal-catalyzed coupling, what roles do oxidative addition, migratory insertion, and reductive elimination play?

氧化加成使催化剂（如 Pd(0)）插入底物卤键，生成高价金属中间体；迁移插入将配位基团转移到金属上的另一个配体，延长碳链或成环；还原消除则把金属上两个配体接合为产物并再生低价催化剂。这一循环决定了反应的选择性与催化周转。
Oxidative addition inserts the catalyst (e.g., Pd(0)) into a substrate's halide bond to give a higher-valent metal complex; migratory insertion transfers a coordinated group onto another ligand, extending a chain or closing a ring; reductive elimination joins two ligands into the product and regenerates the low-valent catalyst. This cycle governs selectivity and catalytic turnover.

为什么许多有机合成需要无水无氧条件？常用哪些手段实现？

Why do many organic syntheses require anhydrous and oxygen-free conditions, and how are they achieved?

有机金属试剂（如格氏试剂、有机锂）和低价过渡金属催化剂易被水和氧破坏，导致失活或副反应。常用手段包括 Schlenk 技术、惰性气体保护、分子筛除水和使用高纯溶剂。
Organometallic reagents (e.g., Grignard and organolithium reagents) and low-valent transition-metal catalysts are readily destroyed by water and oxygen, causing deactivation or side reactions. Common practices include Schlenk techniques, inert-gas blanketing, molecular sieves, and rigorously dried solvents.

什么是 Wittig 反应？它主要用于合成哪类化合物？

What is the Wittig reaction and what class of compounds is it mainly used to make?

Wittig 反应是磷叶立德（Wittig 试剂）与醛或酮反应，将羰基转化为烯烃的反应。它是构筑碳-碳双键最经典的方法之一，常用于合成共轭烯烃和萜类骨架。
The Wittig reaction uses a phosphonium ylide (Wittig reagent) to convert the carbonyl group of an aldehyde or ketone into an alkene. It is one of the classic methods for constructing C=C double bonds and is often used for conjugated alkenes and terpene frameworks.

不少文献声称微波辐射带来无法用温度解释的“非热效应”，这类说法应当如何用严格的对照实验加以检验？

Many papers claim microwaves deliver 'non-thermal effects' that temperature cannot explain; how should such claims be tested with rigorous control experiments?

微波能经偶极极化与离子传导在介电损耗高的物料内部直接耗散，加热自本体而非器壁开始，温度梯度方向与常规相反，多数报道的加速皆源于此。要检验非热效应，必须使对照与样品温度曲线一致、用光纤探头实测内部温度，并用吸波而不含极性溶剂的碳化硅反应瓶做对照；迄今所有严格对照实验中，独立于温度的非热效应仍缺乏可靠证据。
Microwave energy dissipates directly inside lossy materials through dipolar polarization and ionic conduction, so heating starts in the bulk rather than at the vessel wall and the temperature gradient runs opposite to convention; most reported accelerations follow from this fast bulk heating. Testing non-thermal claims demands matched temperature profiles, internal readings by fiber-optic probes, and control runs in silicon-carbide vials that absorb microwaves yet hold no polar solvent - and in every rigorous comparison to date, genuinely non-thermal effects lack reliable evidence.

有机合成中为什么常常需要保护和脱保护官能团？举一个例子。

Why are protecting groups and deprotection commonly needed in organic synthesis? Give an example.

在多步合成中，某些活泼官能团会干扰后续反应，需要暂时遮蔽后再释放。例如把醇羟基转化为硅醚（TMS 或 TBS 保护），待目标反应完成后，再用氟离子试剂（如 TBAF、HF·吡啶）或稀酸/含水乙酸等条件选择性脱除。这样既保证了中间步骤的化学选择性，又不破坏其他敏感基团。
In multistep synthesis, reactive functional groups can interfere with later steps and must be temporarily masked and released later. For example, an alcohol is converted to a silyl ether (TMS or TBS protection) and then removed selectively with a fluoride source such as TBAF or HF-pyridine, or under dilute aqueous acid. This preserves chemoselectivity in the intervening steps without damaging other sensitive groups.

什么是 Suzuki-Miyaura 偶联反应？它在有机合成中有什么用途？

What is the Suzuki-Miyaura coupling reaction and what is it used for in organic synthesis?

Suzuki-Miyaura 偶联是在钯催化下，有机硼试剂与卤代烃发生交叉偶联生成碳-碳键的反应。它条件温和、底物兼容性好，广泛用于药物、材料和天然产物的芳环连接。
The Suzuki-Miyaura coupling is a palladium-catalyzed cross-coupling between organoboron reagents and haloarenes to form carbon-carbon bonds. It proceeds under mild conditions with broad functional-group tolerance and is widely used to join aromatic rings in pharmaceuticals, materials, and natural products.
