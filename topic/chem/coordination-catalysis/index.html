均相催化与多相催化在催化中心和分离方式上有何主要区别？

What are the main differences between homogeneous and heterogeneous catalysis in terms of active site and separation?

均相催化的活性中心与反应物处于同一相（通常为溶液），活性物种明确、选择性高，但产物分离困难；多相催化的催化剂为不同相（如固体负载金属），易与产物分离，但活性位点不均一。两者常通过配体或载体设计来调控性能。
In homogeneous catalysis the active site and reactants share one phase (usually solution), giving well-defined species and high selectivity but difficult product separation; heterogeneous catalysis uses a different phase (e.g., supported solid metal), allowing easy separation but less uniform sites. Performance is tuned via ligand or support design.

晶体场理论中，为什么八面体场中 d 轨道会分裂为 t2g 和 eg 两组？这种分裂如何影响配合物的颜色？

In crystal field theory, why do d orbitals in an octahedral field split into t2g and eg sets, and how does this splitting produce color?

八面体配体从六个方向靠近中心金属，使直接指向配体的 dz2、dx2-y2 轨道能量升高（eg），而夹在轴间的 dxy、dxz、dyz 能量较低（t2g），形成分裂能 Δo。当可见光中能量与 Δo 匹配的光子被吸收，电子从 t2g 跃迁到 eg，配合物便呈现其补色。
In an octahedral field the ligands approach along the axes, raising the energy of the dz2 and dx2-y2 orbitals (eg) while the inter-axial dxy, dxz, dyz lie lower (t2g), giving the splitting energy Δo. A visible photon whose energy matches Δo promotes an electron from t2g to eg, and the complex shows the complementary color.

什么是配位催化中的「配体效应」？膦配体如何通过电子和位阻性质调节催化活性？

What is the ligand effect in coordination catalysis, and how do phosphine ligands tune activity through electronic and steric properties?

配体效应指配体的电子给受能力和空间位阻改变金属中心的电子密度与空位，从而影响氧化加成、迁移插入和还原消除的速率。富电子膦配体促进氧化加成，大位阻膦配体则限制副反应、提高选择性，二者是设计催化剂的核心旋钮。
The ligand effect means a ligand's electron-donating/withdrawing ability and steric bulk change the metal's electron density and open coordination sites, affecting rates of oxidative addition, insertion, and reductive elimination. Electron-rich phosphines promote oxidative addition while bulky phosphines suppress side reactions and raise selectivity, both key design knobs.

什么是 18 电子规则？它在判断有机金属配合物稳定性时有什么局限？

What is the 18-electron rule, and what are its limitations in judging organometallic stability?

18 电子规则认为，过渡金属配合物若价层电子数达到 18（达到稀有气体构型）则较稳定，常用于预测有机金属物种的合理性。但它主要适用于低氧化态、强场配体的配合物，对高氧化态、早期过渡金属及具有金属-金属键或自由基的体系常常失效。
The 18-electron rule holds that a transition-metal complex is stable when its valence shell reaches 18 electrons (a noble-gas configuration) and is widely used to assess organometallic plausibility. It mainly applies to low-oxidation-state, strong-field complexes and often fails for high-oxidation-state, early-transition-metal, or metal-metal-bonded and radical species.

血红蛋白中的铁是如何与氧可逆结合的？这利用了配位化学的什么原理？

How does iron in hemoglobin bind oxygen reversibly, and what coordination principle does this illustrate?

血红蛋白的血红素中心是 Fe(II) 卟啉配合物，氧分子作为配体可逆地配位到 Fe 上而不被氧化为 Fe(III)，结合后引起构象变化增强后续结合。这体现了金属-配体可逆配位及协同效应在生物体系中的运用。
The heme center of hemoglobin is an Fe(II) porphyrin complex to which O2 binds reversibly as a ligand without oxidizing Fe to Fe(III); binding induces a conformational change that enhances further uptake. This illustrates reversible metal-ligand coordination and cooperativity in biology.

什么是螯合效应？为什么螯合配体形成的配合物往往更稳定？

What is the chelate effect, and why are complexes with chelating ligands often more stable?

螯合效应指多齿配体通过两个或多个配位原子与同一金属结合形成环状结构，使配合物比单齿配体类似物更稳定。从熵角度看，一个多齿配体取代多个单齿配体时体系粒子数增加，熵增有利于螯合物形成。
The chelate effect refers to the extra stability when a polydentate ligand binds a metal through two or more donor atoms to form a ring. Entropically, replacing several monodentate ligands by one multidentate ligand increases the number of free particles, and the entropy gain favors the chelate complex.

配位数为 6 的配合物最常见的几何构型是什么？举一例。

What is the most common geometry for a coordination number of 6, and give an example.

配位数 6 的配合物最常见为八面体构型，六个配体占据正八面体的顶点。典型例子是六水合钴(II)离子 [Co(H2O)6]2+ 或六氨合钴(III)离子 [Co(NH3)6]3+。
Coordination number 6 most commonly gives an octahedral geometry, with six ligands at the vertices of a regular octahedron. Typical examples are the hexaaquacobalt(II) ion [Co(H2O)6]2+ and the hexaamminecobalt(III) ion [Co(NH3)6]3+.

什么是配位键？配合物通常由哪两部分组成？

What is a coordinate bond, and what two parts typically make up a coordination compound?

配位键是由一个原子提供孤对电子、另一个原子提供空轨道形成的共价键。配合物通常由中心金属离子（或原子）和围绕它的配体组成，配体通过孤对电子与中心金属配位。
A coordinate bond is a covalent bond in which one atom supplies a lone pair and the other supplies an empty orbital. A coordination compound typically consists of a central metal ion (or atom) surrounded by ligands that donate lone pairs to the metal.
