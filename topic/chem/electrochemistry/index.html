什么是标准电极电势？

What is the standard electrode potential?

标准电极电势是在标准状态（25℃、1 atm或1 bar、离子活度为1）下，某半反应相对标准氢电极的电势差，单位为伏特。电势越正，氧化型越易得电子、氧化性越强。它用于判断氧化还原自发方向和计算电池电动势。
The standard electrode potential is the potential difference of a half-reaction relative to the standard hydrogen electrode under standard conditions (25 C, 1 atm or bar, unit activity), measured in volts. The more positive it is, the more easily the oxidized form gains electrons and the stronger its oxidizing power. It is used to judge the spontaneity of redox reactions and compute cell voltage.

什么是双电层？

What is the electric double layer?

金属浸入电解质溶液时，表面因离子溶解或吸附形成电荷，溶液侧出现等量反号离子层，构成紧密层与扩散层结合的界面结构，称为双电层。双电层电势差正是电极电势的微观来源，也是电容和腐蚀的基础。
When a metal is immersed in an electrolyte, its surface gains charge by ion dissolution or adsorption, and an equal opposite-charged ion layer forms on the solution side, creating an interfacial structure of a compact and a diffuse layer called the electric double layer. Its potential difference is the microscopic origin of electrode potential and the basis of capacitance and corrosion.

什么是浓差电池？

What is a concentration cell?

浓差电池是由两个相同电极但电解质浓度不同的半电池组成的原电池，其电动势来源于浓度差而非电极种类差异。浓度高的一侧发生还原、低的一侧发生氧化，直至浓度趋于平衡。它能说明扩散驱动的电能产生方式。
A concentration cell is a galvanic cell made of two identical electrodes but half-cells with different electrolyte concentrations; its voltage comes from the concentration difference rather than different electrode materials. Reduction occurs on the more concentrated side and oxidation on the dilute side until concentrations equalize. It illustrates electricity generated by diffusion driving force.

电镀时金属为什么能均匀地长到工件表面？镀层是怎么来的？

How does electroplating make metal grow evenly onto a workpiece, and where does the coating come from?

电镀属于电解：把工件（如铁件）接在阴极，要镀的金属（如铜、镍）作阳极，放入含该金属离子的电解液中。通电后阳极金属溶解（M-ne- 生成 Mn+），补充溶液中消耗的金属离子；同时 Mn+ 在阴极获得电子还原沉积成金属原子，逐层析出在工件表面。控制电流密度、温度和添加剂，可以使镀层晶粒细小、结合牢固且厚度均匀。镀层既美观又能防腐蚀，是典型的金属覆盖层保护方式。
Electroplating is electrolysis: the workpiece (say iron) is the cathode, the coating metal (copper or nickel) is the anode, and both sit in an electrolyte containing that metal's ions. On current flow the anode dissolves (M - ne⁻ → Mⁿ⁺), replenishing the ions the solution uses up, while at the cathode Mⁿ⁺ gains electrons and is reduced to metal atoms that deposit layer by layer on the workpiece. Controlling current density, temperature and additives yields fine grains, strong adhesion and uniform thickness. Besides looks, the coat shields the base metal from corrosion.

什么是钠硫电池？

What is a sodium-sulfur battery?

钠硫电池是一种高温熔盐电池，以液态钠为负极、硫为正极、β-氧化铝陶瓷为固态电解质，工作温度约300至350摄氏度。放电时钠失去电子生成钠离子穿过电解质与硫形成硫化钠。它具有高能量密度，常用于电网储能。
A sodium-sulfur battery is a high-temperature molten-salt cell with liquid sodium as the negative electrode, sulfur as the positive electrode and a beta-alumina ceramic as the solid electrolyte, operating around 300 to 350 degrees Celsius. During discharge, sodium loses electrons to form Na+ that crosses the electrolyte and reacts with sulfur to form sodium sulfide. It has high energy density and is used for grid storage.

为什么锂电池不能过度充电？

Why must lithium batteries not be overcharged?

过度充电会使正极材料结构破坏、负极锂金属析出形成枝晶，可能刺穿隔膜造成内部短路，甚至引发热失控燃烧。此外过充会加速电解液分解、损害循环寿命。因此电池管理系统必须限制充电上限电压。
Overcharging destroys the positive electrode structure and causes metallic lithium to plate as dendrites on the negative electrode, which can pierce the separator and short the cell internally, even triggering thermal runaway and fire. It also accelerates electrolyte decomposition and harms cycle life. A battery management system must therefore cap the charging voltage.

能斯特方程在电化学里起什么作用？

What role does the Nernst equation play in electrochemistry?

能斯特方程 E = E° − (RT/nF)lnQ 把电池电动势与温度、反应物活度联系起来，说明浓度与 pH 如何改变实际电极电势。Nernst 在 1888 年导出它，是理解电池、神经电位与 pH 计的基础。
The Nernst equation E = E° − (RT/nF)lnQ links cell voltage to temperature and reactant activities, showing how concentration and pH shift real electrode potentials. Nernst derived it in 1888; it underlies batteries, nerve potentials and pH meters.

为什么铝比铁更活泼却不容易继续被腐蚀，而铁锈会越锈越深？

Why does aluminum, though more reactive than iron, resist ongoing corrosion while iron rust keeps penetrating?

铝的标准电极电位比铁更负，热力学上更易被氧化；但氧化生成的 Al₂O₃ 膜致密且与基体结合牢固，立刻隔绝氧气和水，使腐蚀"自钝化"而停止。铁锈 Fe₂O₃·xH₂O 疏松多孔、容易剥落，无法阻隔环境，基体便持续被侵蚀。
Aluminum has a more negative standard potential than iron and is thermodynamically easier to oxidize, but the Al₂O₃ film it forms is dense and adheres tightly, instantly sealing off oxygen and water so corrosion self-passivates. Rust (Fe₂O₃·xH₂O) is porous and flakes off, leaving the underlying metal exposed to continued attack.

What is a galvanic cell?

The Volta pile was the earliest galvanic cell.

镀层保护利用了什么原理？

What principle does coating protection use?

镀层保护通过电镀或热镀在金属表面覆盖一层致密、耐腐蚀的金属（如锌、锡、铬），隔绝水与氧气。若镀层完整，主体金属被机械隔离而不被腐蚀。即使镀层破损，锌等更活泼金属还能提供牺牲阳极保护。
Coating protection covers a metal surface with a dense, corrosion-resistant layer such as zinc, tin or chromium by electroplating or hot-dipping, isolating it from water and oxygen. With an intact coating the base metal is mechanically shielded. Even if scratched, a more active metal like zinc still offers sacrificial protection.

什么是牺牲阳极法？

What is the sacrificial anode method?

牺牲阳极法是一种阴极保护技术：将更活泼的金属（如镁、锌）连接在被保护金属上，前者优先被氧化腐蚀，从而保护后者。它常用于船舶、地下管道和热水器的防锈。这种方法无需外加电源。
The sacrificial anode method is a cathodic protection technique: a more active metal such as magnesium or zinc is connected to the protected metal and oxidizes first, protecting the latter. It is used for ships, buried pipelines and water heaters. No external power supply is needed.

锌片和铜片插入稀硫酸并用导线相连，为什么导线中会有电流？

A zinc plate and a copper plate are dipped in dilute sulfuric acid and connected by a wire. Why does current flow in the wire?

锌和铜的活泼性不同，在稀硫酸中就构成了原电池：锌比铜活泼，锌失电子被氧化（Zn-2e- 生成 Zn2+）成为负极，电子沿导线流到铜电极；氢离子在铜表面获得电子被还原成氢气，铜是正极并冒出气泡。电子在导线中的定向移动形成电流，化学能转化为电能。若两极直接接触短路，电子就直接传给氢离子，外电路就没有电流。
Because the two metals differ in activity, they form a galvanic (voltaic) cell in dilute sulfuric acid. Zinc is the more active metal, so it loses electrons and is oxidized (Zn - 2e⁻ → Zn²⁺) as the anode; electrons travel through the wire to the copper electrode, where H⁺ gains electrons and is reduced to hydrogen gas, so copper is the cathode and bubbles gas. The directed flow of electrons in the wire is the electric current, and chemical energy becomes electrical energy. If the two metals touch directly, electrons pass straight to the ions and no current flows through an external circuit.

电解和原电池有什么区别？

What is the difference between electrolysis and a galvanic cell?

原电池利用自发的氧化还原反应对外供电，是化学能转电能；电解则需要外加电能驱动非自发反应，是电能转化学能。原电池的两极分别叫正负极，电解池的两极按连接电源的正负叫阴阳极。两者电子流动方向和能量转化正好相反。
A galvanic cell uses a spontaneous redox reaction to supply power, converting chemical to electrical energy; electrolysis needs external electrical energy to drive a non-spontaneous reaction, converting electrical to chemical energy. A galvanic cell's electrodes are called positive and negative, while an electrolytic cell's are cathode and anode by their connection to the power supply. Their electron flow and energy conversion are opposite.

Because the ions simply commute while the electrode frameworks stay intact, the reaction can repeat over and over. Each cycle does lose a little capacity, so after thousands of cycles the battery is visibly weaker and needs replacing.

法拉第第一定律：电解时在电极上析出物质的质量与通过的电量成正比；第二定律：相同电量下析出不同物质的量与它们的化学当量（摩尔质量/电子数）成正比。据此可由电量精确计算产物量，是电解、电镀等工业定量控制的理论基础。
Faraday's first law states that the mass of substance deposited at an electrode is proportional to the charge passed; the second law states that for the same charge, the amount of different substances deposited is proportional to their chemical equivalent (molar mass divided by electrons transferred). This lets industry calculate product amounts precisely from charge, underpinning electrolysis and electroplating.
