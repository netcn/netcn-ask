为什么古代骨螺紫染料极其珍贵？

Why was ancient Tyrian purple so precious?

骨螺紫提取自地中海骨螺的腺体分泌物，要经过光照氧化才显色，且上万只螺才能得几克染料。它色牢度极好、不易褪色，是古罗马皇室专用的高贵紫。高昂的收集成本使它成为古代最贵的染料之一。
Tyrian purple came from the gland secretion of Mediterranean mollusks and required sunlight oxidation to develop color; thousands of snails yielded only a few grams. Its exceptional fastness made it the royal purple of Rome. The huge gathering cost made it among the most expensive dyes of antiquity.

为什么传统蓝染要把靛蓝先发酵还原？

Why must traditional indigo dye be fermented and reduced first?

天然靛蓝本身不溶于水，必须先在碱性发酵液中用微生物或还原剂把它还原成可溶的无色靛白。布料浸入后氧化，靛白重新变回蓝色固着在纤维上，这就是先染后氧化的还原染色。
Natural indigo is insoluble, so it must first be reduced in an alkaline ferment by microbes or reducing agents into soluble, colorless leuco-indigo. The cloth is dipped and the leuco form oxidizes back to blue, fixing on the fiber—a dye-then-oxidize vat process.

为什么紫草能给布料染出紫色？

Why can gromwell (purple grass) dye cloth purple?

紫草根部含紫草素等萘醌类色素，可溶于油脂或酒精后上染纤维。它属于天然植物染料，颜色随pH和媒染剂变化，从紫红到蓝紫。古人用它做昂贵的紫色染材。
The roots of gromwell contain naphthoquinone pigments like shikonin that dissolve in oil or alcohol and bind to fibers. As a natural plant dye its hue shifts from purplish-red to blue-violet with pH and mordant. Ancient dyers valued it as a costly purple source.

为什么泡过的茶叶能染出褐色布料？

Why can used tea leaves dye cloth brown?

茶叶含茶多酚和色素（如茶黄素、茶红素），热水萃取后能附着在天然纤维上，呈现褐黄到棕褐色。经明矾或铁媒染后颜色更深更牢。它是一种随手可得的天然染料，常用于草木染体验。
Tea contains polyphenols and pigments like theaflavins and thearubigins that extract in hot water and attach to natural fibers, giving yellow-brown to brown shades. Alum or iron mordant deepens and fixes the color. It is a handy natural dye for plant-dyeing projects.

为什么天然染色常常要加媒染剂（如明矾、铁锈水）？

Why do natural dyes often need a mordant like alum or iron water?

许多天然色素与纤维结合力弱、容易掉色，媒染剂（金属离子如铝、铁、铜）能在染料和纤维间架桥，形成更牢的络合物。不同金属还会改变颜色，如铁媒染让颜色变暗发灰。这是草木染固色的关键。
Many natural pigments bind weakly to fibers and wash out, so a mordant—metal ions like aluminum, iron, or copper—bridges dye and fiber into a more stable complex. Different metals also shift the color, e.g., iron darkens and grays it. Mordanting is the key to fixing plant dyes.

为什么新牛仔裤第一次洗会掉蓝色？

Why does a new pair of jeans bleed blue on the first wash?

牛仔裤多用靛蓝染色，而靛蓝是靠物理吸附和表面附着固色的，没有深入到棉纤维内部，所以摩擦和水洗会带走表面浮色。随着穿着，颜色从深蓝逐渐磨白发白。冷水反面洗可减少掉色。
Jeans are dyed with indigo, which fixes by surface adsorption rather than penetrating deep into cotton, so friction and washing carry away surface color. With wear the deep blue gradually fades to lighter tones. Washing inside-out in cold water reduces bleeding.

为什么紫甘蓝汁能当酸碱指示剂？

Why can red cabbage juice act as a pH indicator?

紫甘蓝含花青素，这种色素在不同pH下分子结构改变，反射的光波长变化，于是呈现红、紫、蓝、绿等不同颜色。用它泡水滴到待测液里就能粗略判断酸碱性。这是天然的pH指示剂。
Red cabbage holds anthocyanins whose molecular structure changes with pH, shifting the wavelength of reflected light so the liquid shows red, purple, blue, or green. Dropping the juice into a test solution gives a rough read of acidity or alkalinity. It is a natural pH indicator.

为什么洋葱皮可以煮水染出黄色布料？

Why can onion skins dye cloth yellow?

洋葱外皮富含槲皮素等黄酮类色素，热水能把它们煮出来，直接染棉麻或经明矾媒染后更牢固。这是一种常见又便宜的厨房染料。颜色从浅黄到橙黄不等。
Onion skins are rich in flavonoid pigments like quercetin that boil out into water and directly color cotton or linen, or bind better after alum mordanting. It is a common, cheap kitchen dye. The shade ranges from pale to orange-yellow.

为什么蓝莓汁能把布料染成蓝紫色？

Why does blueberry juice dye cloth blue-purple?

蓝莓含花青素，这是一种水溶性植物色素，能附着到天然纤维上。花青素颜色随pH变化，酸性偏红、碱性偏蓝绿。它容易掉色，多用于趣味染色而非耐洗布料。
Blueberries contain anthocyanins, water-soluble plant pigments that attach to natural fibers. Their color shifts with pH—reddish in acid, blue-green in base. They bleed easily, so they suit playful dyeing more than wash-fast fabric.
