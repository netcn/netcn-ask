为什么电解水常要加酸或碱？

Why is acid or base usually added for water electrolysis?

纯水几乎不导电，加少量硫酸或氢氧化钾提供离子、增大电导，而这些添加剂本身基本不被消耗。
Pure water barely conducts; a little H2SO4 or KOH supplies ions to boost conductivity, and the additive is essentially not consumed.

为什么要考虑耦合平衡（如沉淀与配位同时发生）？

Why must coupled equilibria (e.g., precipitation with complexation) be considered together?

多个平衡共享同一离子时彼此牵连：例如 AgCl 沉淀可被 NH₃ 通过生成 [Ag(NH₃)₂]⁺ 而溶解，总过程由 Ksp 与稳定常数共同决定。分别单独计算会得出错误结论，必须联立求解。
When equilibria share an ion they are linked: AgCl precipitate can dissolve in NH₃ by forming [Ag(NH₃)₂]⁺, governed jointly by Ksp and the stability constant. Treating them separately gives wrong results; they must be solved together.

为什么金属钠溶在液氨里变成蓝色溶液？

Why does sodium dissolved in liquid ammonia give a blue solution?

钠把电子溶进液氨，形成被氨分子溶剂化的电子，这种溶剂化电子吸收红光，溶液显蓝，浓时转青铜色并能导电。
Sodium releases electrons solvated by ammonia; solvated electrons absorb red light, giving a blue solution that turns bronze and conductive at high concentration.

做冰淇淋时为什么要在冰块里加盐？

Why is salt added to the ice when making ice cream?

这利用的是凝固点降低这一依数性质。盐溶于水形成溶液，使冰的凝固点降到0摄氏度以下，冰在零下仍会融化，而融化需要吸收大量热量，于是周围温度骤降，足以把冰淇淋料冻结。盐浓度合适时温度最低可达约零下21摄氏度（冰盐共晶点），所以加盐的冰比纯冰更冷。
This uses freezing-point depression, a colligative property. Salt dissolving in water lowers the freezing point of the ice below 0 degrees Celsius, so the ice keeps melting below zero; melting absorbs a large amount of heat, rapidly cooling the surroundings enough to freeze the ice-cream mixture. At the right salt concentration the temperature can fall to about -21 degrees Celsius (the ice-salt eutectic), which is why salted ice is colder than plain ice.

溶度积 Ksp 与溶解度有什么关系？

How is Ksp related to solubility?

Ksp 是难溶盐饱和溶液中离子浓度幂的乘积常数。对 AB 型盐，溶解度 s 满足 Ksp = s²；对 A₂B 型则 Ksp = 4s³。但同离子效应或配位会显著改变实际溶解度。
Ksp is the ion-concentration product constant for a saturated sparingly soluble salt. For an AB salt, s satisfies Ksp = s²; for A₂B, Ksp = 4s³. Common-ion effects or complexation can markedly change actual solubility.

配制准确浓度的溶液为什么要用容量瓶而不是烧杯？

Why use a volumetric flask instead of a beaker for exact concentration?

容量瓶在指定温度下只有一个精确刻度，定容后体积很准；烧杯只有粗略刻度，误差大。
A volumetric flask has one calibrated mark at a set temperature for precise volume; a beaker's marks are only rough.

为什么有色溶液会呈现它“互补”的颜色？

Why does a colored solution show its ‘complementary’ color?

溶液吸收了白光中某些波长的光，剩下没被吸收的光混合起来就是我们看到的物质颜色，比如吸蓝的看起来偏黄。
The solution absorbs certain wavelengths from white light; the unabsorbed remainder is what we see—e.g., a blue-absorbing solution looks yellow.

为什么撒盐既能化雪又让汤更难烧开？

Why does salt both melt snow and make soup harder to boil?

都是依数性：盐降低水的凝固点（化雪）同时升高沸点（汤更难开），同一原理的两面，并不矛盾。
Both are colligative properties: salt lowers the freezing point (melts snow) and raises the boiling point (harder to boil soup)—two sides of one effect.

Why does sugar dissolve faster in hot water?

温度升高让水分子运动更剧烈，撞击糖粒更频繁，所以溶解得更快。
Higher temperature speeds molecular motion, so water hits sugar crystals more often and dissolves them faster.

为什么粗盐在潮湿天会结块，精盐却不会？

Why does crude salt cake in humid weather but refined salt does not?

粗盐含氯化镁、氯化钙等杂质，易吸湿潮解结块；精盐主要成分氯化钠本身不潮解，常加微量抗结剂。
Impure salt contains MgCl2/CaCl2 that absorb moisture and cake; pure NaCl is not hygroscopic and often has anti-caking agents added.

为什么冬天往结冰的路面上撒盐，冰就会融化？

Why does spreading salt on icy roads make the ice melt?

盐（氯化钠）溶解后产生的离子干扰了水分子结成冰晶，使溶液的凝固点低于 0°C，这叫凝固点降低。只要环境温度不太低（对 NaCl 通常高于约 −9°C），冰就会吸热融化成盐水。
Dissolved salt ions disrupt water's ability to form ice crystals, lowering the freezing point below 0°C—freezing-point depression. As long as the air is not too cold (roughly above −9°C for NaCl), the ice absorbs heat and melts into brine.

盐放进汤里就不见了,它去哪了?能把盐再变回来吗?

Salt vanishes into soup — where did it go? Can I get it back?

盐没有消失,而是化成肉眼看不见的微小粒子,均匀地钻进水分子之间,这叫溶解。想让它变回来?把汤熬干,盐就会析出晶体——海水晒盐用的正是这个办法。不过熬干的汤可就不能喝啦。
Salt never vanished — it breaks into tiny particles too small to see, tucked evenly between water molecules: that is dissolving. Want it back? Boil the soup dry and salt crystals return; drying seawater in the sun is exactly how sea salt is made. But do not drink that soup.
