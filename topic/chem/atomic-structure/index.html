什么是泡利不相容原理？

What is the Pauli exclusion principle?

泡利不相容原理指出，在一个原子中不能有两个电子拥有完全相同的四个量子数。这意味着每个原子轨道最多只能容纳两个自旋相反的电子。
The Pauli exclusion principle states that no two electrons in an atom can have the same set of four quantum numbers. This means each atomic orbital can hold at most two electrons with opposite spins.

卢瑟福用金箔实验得出了什么颠覆性结论？

What revolutionary conclusion came from Rutherford's gold-foil experiment?

绝大多数α粒子直穿金箔，少数大角度偏转，说明原子内部绝大部分是空旷空间，正电荷和几乎全部质量集中在极小的原子核。
Most alpha particles passed through while a few deflected sharply, proving atoms are mostly empty space with mass and positive charge concentrated in a tiny nucleus.

什么是电子云和原子轨道？

What are electron clouds and atomic orbitals?

在量子力学中，电子没有确定轨道，而是以概率分布出现在核外空间，称为电子云。原子轨道是描述电子出现概率的数学函数，按形状分为s、p、d、f等类型。
In quantum mechanics, electrons do not follow fixed paths but appear in space around the nucleus as probability distributions called electron clouds. Atomic orbitals are mathematical functions describing these probabilities and are classified by shape as s, p, d, and f types.

洪特规则说的是什么？

What does Hund's rule state?

等价轨道在半满前，电子先分占且自旋平行，使交换能最低、总能量最小。
Before pairing, electrons occupy equivalent orbitals singly with parallel spins, minimizing exchange energy and total energy.

什么是原子序数？

What is the atomic number?

原子序数是一个元素原子核中质子的数量，它决定了元素在周期表中的位置。原子序数相同的一类原子属于同一种元素。
The atomic number is the number of protons in the nucleus of an element's atoms, and it determines the element's position in the periodic table. Atoms with the same atomic number belong to the same element.

什么是相对原子质量？

What is relative atomic mass?

相对原子质量是某元素原子的平均质量与碳-12原子质量的十二分之一的比值，是一个没有单位的数。由于存在同位素，多数元素的相对原子质量不是整数。
Relative atomic mass is the ratio of the average mass of an element's atoms to one-twelfth of the mass of a carbon-12 atom, and it has no unit. Because of isotopes, most elements have non-integer relative atomic masses.

周期表中的 s、p、d、f 区是怎么划分的？

How are the s, p, d, and f blocks of the periodic table defined?

按元素基态最后填入的电子亚层划分：s 区（ns¹⁻²，碱金属与碱土）、p 区（np¹⁻⁶，主族其余）、d 区（(n−1)d¹⁻¹⁰，过渡金属）、f 区（(n−2)f，镧系锕系）。这反映价电子构型与化学性质。
Blocks are defined by the subshell receiving the last electron in the ground state: s-block (ns¹⁻², alkali/alkaline earth), p-block (np¹⁻⁶, rest of main groups), d-block ((n−1)d¹⁻¹⁰, transition metals), f-block ((n−2)f, lanthanides/actinides). This mirrors valence configuration and chemistry.

电子在原子中是怎样排布的？

How are electrons arranged in an atom?

电子分层排布在称为电子层的壳层中，离核越远能量越高。每层最多容纳的电子数遵循2n平方的规律，其中n是层序数。
Electrons are arranged in shells called energy levels, with higher levels farther from the nucleus having more energy. The maximum number of electrons in each shell follows the rule 2n^2, where n is the shell number.

原子还能再分吗？

Can an atom be divided further?

能。原子可分为原子核与电子，原子核还能分成质子和中子，质子和中子又由更小的夸克组成。不过在一般的化学反应中，原子是最小的参与者。
Yes. An atom splits into a nucleus and electrons, the nucleus into protons and neutrons, and protons and neutrons into smaller quarks. But in ordinary chemical reactions, the atom is the smallest participant.

原子是由什么组成的？

What is an atom made of?

原子由位于中心的原子核和围绕原子核运动的电子组成。原子核又由带正电的质子和不带电的中子构成。
An atom consists of a central nucleus surrounded by electrons moving around it. The nucleus is made up of positively charged protons and neutral neutrons.

为什么原子整体不带电？

Why is an atom electrically neutral overall?

原子核里的每个质子带一个正电荷，而核外电子带等量负电荷。在中性原子里，质子数等于电子数，正负电荷相互抵消。
Each proton in the nucleus carries one positive charge, while the surrounding electrons carry an equal negative charge. In a neutral atom, the number of protons equals the number of electrons, so the charges cancel out.

分子和原子有什么不同？

How is a molecule different from an atom?

原子是化学反应中的最小粒子，而分子是由两个或更多原子通过化学键结合而成的粒子。例如水分子由两个氢原子和一个氧原子构成。
An atom is the smallest particle in a chemical reaction, while a molecule is formed when two or more atoms join by chemical bonds. For example, a water molecule is made of two hydrogen atoms and one oxygen atom.
