为什么镧系元素的化学性质如此相似，分离它们格外困难？

Why are the lanthanides chemically so similar that separating them is notoriously hard?

原因是镧系收缩：4f 电子对核电荷的屏蔽能力差，随原子序数增加，核对最外层电子的引力增强，离子半径缓慢而持续地缩小。镧系元素的最外层电子结构几乎相同，化学性质主要由最外层电子决定，所以它们与同一配体的反应行为差别极小，半径的微小差异又不足以造成明显的化学选择性。工业上只能利用半径差异进行成千上万次离子交换或溶剂萃取循环，才能获得高纯的单一稀土，这也是稀土精炼成本高昂的重要原因。
The cause is the lanthanide contraction: 4f electrons shield the nucleus poorly, so as atomic number rises the nucleus pulls the outer electrons harder and ionic radii shrink slowly and steadily. Because the outermost electron configurations of the lanthanides are nearly identical and chemistry is governed largely by those outer electrons, their reactions with a given ligand are almost indistinguishable, while the small radius differences cannot provide enough chemical selectivity. Industry must run thousands of ion-exchange or solvent-extraction cycles exploiting those tiny radius variations to obtain one high-purity element - a big reason refined rare earths are so expensive.

反渗透净水器是怎么把盐和水分开的？

How does reverse osmosis separate salt from water?

渗透是水穿半透膜流向浓侧，直到两边化学势持平，撑住的压差便是渗透压，稀溶液中近似正比于浓度与热力学温度。反渗透是在浓侧加一道高于渗透压的外压，逼溶剂反向穿膜，盐分留在浓水侧排走：膜过的是溶剂而非溶液。两点常被略过：进料越咸所需压差越大，家用机与海水淡化不同量级；机理也不是把离子筛在孔外，而是致密膜内溶解扩散的快慢差别，二氧化碳等小分子照样透过。
Osmosis is water crossing a semipermeable membrane toward the concentrated side until the chemical potentials match, and the pressure it can hold up is the osmotic pressure, roughly proportional to concentration and absolute temperature in a dilute solution. Reverse osmosis adds a pressure on the concentrated side above that osmotic pressure, forcing the solvent backwards through the membrane while the salt is left in a concentrate that is drained off: what crosses is solvent, not solution. Two points often get skipped: the saltier the feed, the larger the pressure needed, so a household unit and a seawater plant are not on the same scale; and the mechanism is not screening ions out of pores but the difference in dissolution and diffusion through a dense membrane, so small molecules like carbon dioxide still cross.

反相高效液相色谱中,流动相甲醇比例提高对保留时间和选择性有什么影响?

In reversed-phase HPLC, how does increasing the methanol fraction of the mobile phase affect retention time and selectivity?

反相色谱固定相为疏水烷基链,溶质的保留主要靠疏水分配,流动相有机相比例升高会增强溶质在流动相中的溶解度,洗脱强度增大,保留时间按指数规律缩短。甲醇比例变化对不同官能团溶质的保留影响幅度不一,因此相邻色谱峰的相对距离即选择性因子也会改变,有时甚至出现峰序翻转。实际分离常通过梯度洗脱或二元/三元溶剂体系同时优化保留与选择性。
Reversed-phase stationary phases are hydrophobic alkyl chains and retention arises mainly from hydrophobic partitioning; raising the organic fraction increases solute solubility in the mobile phase, raises elution strength, and shortens retention times exponentially. The magnitude of this effect differs among functional groups, so the relative spacing of neighboring peaks - the selectivity factor - also changes, occasionally reversing elution order. Practical separations tune both retention and selectivity through gradient elution or binary/ternary solvent systems.

范第姆特方程中的涡流扩散、纵向扩散与传质阻力三项如何共同解释塔板高度随流速变化的典型曲线形状？

How do the eddy-diffusion, longitudinal-diffusion, and mass-transfer terms of the van Deemter equation together explain the typical shape of the plate-height-versus-flow-rate curve?

H = A + B/u + Cu：A 由填料粒径分布与流道不均匀性决定，与流速无关；B/u 在低流速下占优，溶质沿柱轴的纵向扩散把谱带拉宽；Cu 在高流速下占优，固定相内外传质跟不上流动相导致峰展宽。三项叠加使 H-u 曲线呈开口向上的双曲形，最低点对应最佳线速度，而实际方法常选稍快流速换取更短的分析时间。亚 2 微米填料同时缩小 A 与 C 项，这正是 UHPLC 能在高线速下保持柱效的原因。
H = A + B/u + Cu sums three band-broadening channels: A reflects eddy diffusion set by particle-size spread and bed irregularity and ignores flow rate; B/u dominates at low velocity where solutes diffuse longitudinally along the column; Cu dominates at high velocity because equilibration between mobile and stationary phases cannot keep pace. Their superposition bends the H-u plot into an upward-open hyperbola whose minimum marks the optimum linear velocity, although practical methods often run slightly faster to trade a little efficiency for shorter runs. Sub-2-micron packings shrink both the A and C terms, which is precisely why UHPLC holds its efficiency at high linear velocities.

色谱分离的基本原理是什么？

What is the basic principle of chromatographic separation?

混合物各组分在固定相与流动相之间的分配（吸附、溶解度或亲和力）不同，迁移速度有别而得以分离。例如气相/液相色谱按保留时间区分组分，薄层色谱按比移值 Rf 区分。
Components of a mixture partition differently between stationary and mobile phases (by adsorption, solubility, or affinity), so they migrate at different rates and separate. GC/HPLC resolve by retention time; TLC by the Rf value.
