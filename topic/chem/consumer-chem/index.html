为什么有人担心塑料里的双酚A（BPA）？

Why do people worry about BPA in plastics?

BPA结构类似雌激素，可能从容器迁入食物，干扰人体内分泌，所以婴幼儿用品多改用BPA-free材料。
BPA mimics estrogen and can leach into food, potentially disrupting hormones, so baby products use BPA-free materials.

防晒霜里的化学防晒剂是怎么挡紫外线的？

How do chemical UV filters in sunscreen block ultraviolet light?

化学防晒剂是共轭有机分子，能级差对上紫外光子，吸光后电子跳到激发态。关键在下一步：它靠内转换极快地回到基态，把能量交给分子振动、散成热，光既没深入皮肤，也没把分子拆坏。UVB 波长短、主要伤表皮，UVA 波长长、钻得深，而单个分子的吸收带很窄，得几种接力才盖得住。它不像物理颗粒只靠散射反射，且吸光会衰减，所以要补涂。
Chemical UV filters are conjugated organic molecules whose energy gap matches an ultraviolet photon, so absorbing the light lifts an electron into an excited state. The decisive step is the next one: they return to the ground state by internal conversion extremely fast, handing the energy to molecular vibration, that is, to heat. The light neither penetrates far into skin nor lingers long enough to break the molecule apart. UVB is the shorter wavelength that mainly scorches the surface, UVA the longer one that reaches deeper, and since a single molecule absorbs only a narrow band, several filters must be lined up to cover it. Unlike physical particles, which work mainly by scattering and reflection, these convert the energy, and their absorbing power decays, which is why they must be reapplied.

Why does bleach (sodium hypochlorite) whiten and kill germs?

次氯酸钠在水中放出强氧化性的次氯酸，能破坏色素分子和微生物的蛋白质，从而漂白并消毒。
Sodium hypochlorite releases oxidizing hypochlorous acid that destroys pigment molecules and microbial proteins, bleaching and disinfecting.

醒神用的嗅盐为什么一闻就让人清醒？

Why do smelling salts jolt someone awake?

嗅盐含碳酸铵，挥发出刺鼻的氨，强烈刺激呼吸道黏膜和三叉神经，触发吸气反射让人猛吸一口气。
Smelling salts release pungent ammonia from ammonium carbonate, irritating airways and triggering an inhalation reflex that snaps someone alert.

保湿霜里的甘油为什么能保湿？

Why does glycerol in moisturizer keep skin hydrated?

甘油是一种吸湿性保湿剂，分子上有三个羟基，能与水形成氢键，从空气中吸收水分并减缓皮肤水分蒸发。保湿霜通常把甘油、透明质酸等保湿剂与凡士林等封闭剂搭配：前者吸水锁水，后者在皮肤表面成膜，阻止水分流失。
Glycerol is a hygroscopic humectant: its three hydroxyl groups form hydrogen bonds with water, drawing moisture from the air and slowing evaporation from the skin. Moisturizers combine humectants such as glycerol or hyaluronic acid with occlusives like petroleum jelly - the former bind water, while the latter form a film on the skin to stop water loss.

物理防晒（氧化锌、二氧化钛）和化学防晒有什么不同？

How do physical sunscreens (ZnO, TiO₂) differ from chemical ones?

物理防晒靠颗粒散射反射紫外线，温和、广谱、不易致敏；化学防晒靠分子吸收紫外线转化为热。
Physical screens scatter/reflect UV via particles—gentle and broad-spectrum; chemical screens absorb UV and convert it to heat.

它靠封闭剂（如凡士林）在表面挡住水分蒸发，靠保湿剂（如甘油）从空气或深层吸水，两者配合柔润皮肤。
Occlusives like petrolatum block water loss, while humectants like glycerin draw water from air or deeper skin—together they soften skin.

洗发水中硫酸盐表活的清洁力强但刺激性也大,工业上如何通过分子结构设计来缓解这一矛盾?

Sulfate surfactants in shampoo clean strongly but irritate readily; how does molecular design ease this trade-off?

十二烷基硫酸钠的疏水尾短且直,临界胶束浓度高、脱脂力强,同时破坏皮脂膜并干扰蛋白结构引起刺激。改用月桂醇聚醚硫酸酯钠(AES)在尾链与硫酸基之间插入数个环氧乙烷单元,头基变大、CMC 降低,胶束更温和,对皮肤的蛋白变性作用减弱。进一步复配两性表活如椰油酰胺丙基甜菜碱可形成混合胶束,降低单一阴离子表活的有效电荷密度,从而兼顾泡沫、清洁与温和性。
Sodium lauryl sulfate has a short straight hydrophobic tail, high CMC, and strong degreasing power, but it strips sebum films and denatures skin proteins, causing irritation. Inserting several ethylene oxide units to give sodium laureth sulfate enlarges the headgroup, lowers the CMC, softens micelles, and reduces protein interaction. Blending amphoteric surfactants like cocamidopropyl betaine forms mixed micelles that dilute effective anionic charge density, balancing foam, cleansing, and mildness.

涂上薄荷膏皮肤并没有真的降温,清凉感究竟是怎么被制造出来的?

A dab of menthol balm cools your skin even though the temperature never actually drops — how is that chill manufactured?

(-)-薄荷醇是皮肤冷觉通道TRPM8的激动剂,该离子通道平时靠低温开启,被分子结合后同样向神经系统发出“变冷”指令。羟基与受体极性残基成氢键、环己基嵌入疏水口袋,这一构效关系决定了凉感的强弱与时长。与之互补的是辣椒素激活TRPV1带来的灼热感,两者共同说明温度感知可以被特定小分子劫持。
(-)-Menthol is an agonist of the skin's cold channel TRPM8, an ion pore normally gated by low temperature; once bound, it instructs the nervous system that cooling is under way. A hydroxyl group hydrogen-bonds to polar receptor residues while the cyclohexyl ring sits in a hydrophobic pocket — a structure-activity relationship that sets both strength and duration of the chill. Capsaicin activating TRPV1 supplies the complementary burn, showing together that temperature perception can be hijacked by specific small molecules.

为什么香水的味道过一会儿就变淡了？

Why does perfume smell fade after a while?

香水的香味其实是一群很小很小的香分子，它们会慢慢飞到空气里飘走，味道就变淡了。这些香分子从液体里跑出来飞走的过程，让香水在暖和或通风的地方消失得更快。
Perfume is made of tiny scent molecules that slowly float away into the air, so the smell gets weaker. This drifting-away of scent molecules is why a perfume fades faster in warm or breezy places.

透明质酸分子能抓住自身重量数百倍的水，在皮肤表面形成水膜锁住水分。
Hyaluronic acid can bind hundreds of times its weight in water, forming a film on skin that locks in moisture.
