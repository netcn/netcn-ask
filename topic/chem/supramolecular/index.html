What drives supramolecular self-assembly?

靠氢键、π-π 堆积、疏水作用、静电等非共价弱作用协同，自发形成有序结构，是材料与生命组装的基础。
It is driven by weak non-covalent forces—hydrogen bonds, π-π stacking, hydrophobic effect, electrostatics—acting together to form ordered structures, the basis of materials and life assembly.

环糊精作为药物载体时,其疏水内腔包合客体分子的驱动力有哪些?取代度如何影响增溶效果?

For cyclodextrins acting as drug carriers, what forces drive guest inclusion into the hydrophobic cavity, and how does degree of substitution affect solubilization?

主要驱动力包括疏水效应——水分子从非极性内腔释放回本体水相引起的熵增益,以及客体与腔壁范德华接触、可能的氢键与去溶剂化焓贡献。天然β-环糊精水溶性有限且有一定肾毒性,经羟丙基或磺丁基醚随机取代后溶解度大幅提高、结晶性下降,增溶能力随平均取代度先升后趋于平台。过高取代度反而可能因空间位阻削弱包合常数,故药用级衍生物需优化取代度范围。
Key driving forces include the hydrophobic effect — entropy gained when cavity-bound water returns to bulk solvent — plus van der Waals contact with the cavity wall and sometimes hydrogen bonding and desolvation enthalpy. Native β-cyclodextrin has limited water solubility and some nephrotoxicity; random hydroxypropyl or sulfobutyl ether substitution raises solubility markedly and disrupts crystallinity, and solubilizing power climbs with average substitution before plateauing. Excessive substitution can sterically weaken binding constants, so pharmaceutical derivatives optimize rather than maximize the degree of substitution.

18-冠-6对碱金属阳离子的结合选择性呈K+>Na+、Cs+的顺序,请用主客体尺寸匹配与大环效应解释这种识别行为。

18-crown-6 complexes alkali metal cations with the selectivity order K+ > Na+, Cs+. Explain this recognition behavior in terms of host-cavity/guest-ion size matching and the macrocyclic effect.

18-冠-6的六个醚氧原子朝向内侧空穴,空穴直径约2.6至3.2埃,与K+约2.8埃的直径最为匹配,六个给电子原子几乎同时包裹阳离子,焓与熵均得到补偿。Na+偏小使氧原子偏离最佳配位距离,Cs+过大只能位于环口之上,结合常数因此显著下降。相对于开链类似物,预组织的大环骨架还避免了结合时的构象熵损失,这一大环效应是冠醚化学获得1987年诺贝尔化学奖的核心概念之一。
The six ether oxygens of 18-crown-6 point into an internal cavity roughly 2.6-3.2 angstroms across, which best matches the approximately 2.8 angstrom diameter of K+, so six donor atoms wrap the cation simultaneously with favorable enthalpy and entropy. Smaller Na+ pushes the oxygens away from ideal coordination distances, while larger Cs+ can only sit atop the ring mouth, markedly lowering the binding constant. Compared with open-chain analogues, the preorganized macrocycle also avoids a conformational entropy penalty upon binding - the macrocyclic effect - one of the key concepts behind the 1987 Nobel Prize in Chemistry awarded for crown ether chemistry.

环糊精为什么能在水里把油性物质包起来？

How can cyclodextrins capture oily substances even in watery surroundings?

因为它外亲水、内亲油。环糊精由若干葡萄糖单元连成环状，外形像开口的截锥，外缘布满羟基、与水十分亲近，而内腔以碳氢键和糖苷氧为主，构成一个非极性的空间。滞留在腔内的水分子相对不安稳，油性客体分子便在疏水效应驱动下把水挤出空腔并安顿其中，形成包合配合物。药物借此提高溶解度，香精借此缓慢释放，除臭剂也利用这一原理吸附异味分子。
Their cavities are oil-friendly while their outsides love water. Cyclodextrins are rings of glucose units shaped like open truncated cones: hydroxyl groups stud the rims and blend readily with water, but the bore is lined with carbon-hydrogen bonds and glycosidic oxygens, forming a nonpolar chamber. Water parked in that chamber is energetically uneasy, so an oily guest expels it and settles inside — a hydrophobic-effect-driven inclusion complex. Pharmaceuticals exploit this to dissolve better, flavorings use it for slow release, and deodorizers trap odor molecules the same way.
