为什么青花瓷的蓝色来自一种矿石？

Why does the blue of blue-and-white porcelain come from an ore?

青花用含钴的矿物（如进口苏麻离青）画坯，罩透明釉高温烧，钴离子在釉下呈稳定蓝；钴料产地不同，呈色浓淡有别。
Blue-and-white uses cobalt-bearing ore (e.g. imported Sumali cobalt) painted under a clear glaze; cobalt ions give a stable blue after high-temperature firing, with hue varying by source.

白衣服和白纸放久了为什么会发黄？

Why do white clothes and paper turn yellow with age?

纸张和棉纤维里的木质素等成分被光和氧慢慢氧化成发黄的物质，漂白剂残留也会催化这一过程。
Lignin and similar components in paper and cotton slowly oxidize into yellow compounds under light and oxygen, and bleach residues can catalyze it.

蝴蝶翅膀和孔雀羽毛的蓝绿色，为什么不是颜料染出来的？

Why are the blues and greens of butterfly wings and peacock feathers not from pigment?

蝴蝶翅膀和孔雀羽毛的蓝绿色来自“结构色”：其表面有周期性纳米结构（如多层薄膜、光栅），通过薄膜干涉或衍射选择性反射特定波长，而非靠色素吸收。因此颜色随观察角度变化、不会像染料那样褪色，且常带金属光泽。把这类结构研磨破坏后颜色即消失，正说明它源于物理结构而非化学颜料。
The blues and greens of butterfly wings and peacock feathers come from structural color: nanoscale periodic structures (multilayers, gratings) interfere with or diffract light to selectively reflect particular wavelengths, instead of pigments absorbing others. The color therefore shifts with viewing angle, does not fade like a dye, and often has a metallic sheen. Grind the structure apart and the color disappears—proof it is physical, not chemical.

天空为什么是蓝色的？

Why is the sky blue?

阳光里的短波蓝光比长波红光更容易被空气分子散射（瑞利散射），所以朝各个方向散开成蓝天。
Air molecules scatter short-wavelength blue light more than red (Rayleigh scattering), spreading blue across the sky.

为什么叶子是绿色的？

Why are leaves green?

叶绿素主要吸收红光和蓝紫光，几乎不吸收绿光，把绿光反射回来，所以叶子显绿；秋天叶绿素分解，类胡萝卜素的黄红才露出。
Chlorophyll absorbs red and blue-violet light but reflects green, so leaves look green; in autumn chlorophyll breaks down, revealing carotenoid yellows/reds.

为什么古典油画的白颜料曾害苦画家？

Why did classic white paint harm old painters?

铅白（碱式碳酸铅）遮盖力好却含铅，研磨吸入或皮肤接触致慢性铅中毒；现代多用无毒的钛白替代。
Lead white (basic lead carbonate) covered well but is lead-based; chronic poisoning afflicted painters. Titanium white is the safe modern substitute.

为什么活虾青灰煮熟却变红？

Why do live grayish shrimp turn red when cooked?

虾蟹体内的虾青素原本与蛋白质结合显青灰色，加热使蛋白变性，游离出红色的虾青素，故熟后变红。
Astaxanthin in shellfish is bound to protein (grayish); heating denatures the protein, freeing red astaxanthin, so cooked shrimp turn red.

为什么烟花有不同颜色？

Why do fireworks have different colors?

不同金属盐在高温中发出特定颜色的光：火焰中的金属原子受热激发，电子跃迁到高能级后回落到低能级时放出特定波长的光。例如钠呈黄色、锶呈红色、钡呈绿色、铜呈蓝色。
Different metal salts emit characteristic colors when hot: heat excites the metal atoms' electrons, which then fall back to lower levels and emit light of specific wavelengths — for example, sodium glows yellow, strontium red, barium green, and copper blue.

秋天树叶为什么变红变黄？

Why do leaves turn red and yellow in autumn?

天冷后叶绿素分解，原本被盖住的黄色类胡萝卜素露出来；有些叶子还会合成红色花青素来护叶。
As chlorophyll breaks down in cold weather, hidden yellow carotenoids show; some leaves also make red anthocyanins to protect themselves.

为什么铋的结晶像彩虹金属？

Why do bismuth crystals look like rainbow metal?

铋凝固时表面形成厚薄不一的氧化膜，光干涉产生彩虹色；铋还具强反磁性，且凝固时微膨胀。
Bismuth's surface oxide film varies in thickness, causing interference colors (rainbow); it's also strongly diamagnetic and expands slightly on freezing.

为什么布能染上颜色洗也洗不掉？

Why does fabric keep its dye color even after washing?

染料的小分子钻进布纤维里，和纤维手拉手绑住，水就冲不掉了。有些染料还能和纤维发生化学反应，变成纤维的一部分，所以洗很多次也不掉色。
Dye molecules slip into the cloth fibers and latch on tightly, so water cannot wash them out. Some dyes even react chemically with the fiber and become part of it, staying bright through many washes.

为什么碘酒滴进米汤变成蓝黑色？

Why does iodine tincture turn rice soup blue-black?

碘（以I3-形式）钻进淀粉的螺旋空腔形成络合物，吸收特定波长的光而显蓝黑；加热蓝色消退，冷却又恢复。
Iodine (as I3-) slips into starch's helical cavity forming a complex that absorbs light and turns blue-black; heating fades it, cooling restores it.

为什么加一点钴就能让玻璃变成蓝色？

Why does a little cobalt turn glass blue?

钴离子(Co2+)在玻璃里吸收橙红光、透射蓝光，所以极少量就显出明显的蓝色，常用于玻璃与陶瓷着色。
Cobalt ions (Co2+) absorb orange-red light and transmit blue, so trace amounts give a strong blue—widely used to color glass and ceramics.

为什么糖放进水里再晾干会长出小晶体？

Why do sugar crystals grow when sugar water dries?

水蒸发后，糖的小颗粒没地方去，就一个挨一个排整齐变成亮晶晶的晶体。如果慢慢晾干，糖分子有空排队，就能长出更大更漂亮的晶体。
When water leaves, sugar particles have nowhere to go and line up neatly into shiny crystals. If the water leaves slowly, the sugar molecules get time to arrange into bigger, prettier crystals.

为什么柠檬汁滴到苹果上,苹果就不变黄了?

Why does lemon juice stop apples turning brown?

苹果切开碰到空气会慢慢变黄,那是氧化在捣乱。柠檬汁酸酸的,像给苹果穿上防护衣,让氧化反应慢下来。所以妈妈切苹果总是挤几滴柠檬汁——这是厨房里的小化学魔法。
Cut apples brown slowly as oxygen teases their flesh. Sour lemon juice dresses the fruit in armor that slows oxidation down. Moms squeeze lemon drops — kitchen chemistry magic at work.

把红黄蓝三种颜料混在一起,为什么会变成黑乎乎的颜色?

Why do red yellow and blue paints mix into muddy dark colors?

颜料混合是减法游戏:红色颜料只反射红光、吸收其他色光,混得越多,被吸收的光越多,最后几乎什么都不反射,就成了黑褐色。而舞台灯光是加法游戏,红绿蓝光叠加反而变白光。颜料做减法,光做加法。
Paint mixing is subtraction: red paint reflects only red light and swallows the rest; mix more pigments and more light gets eaten until nearly none reflects — muddy dark. Stage lights play addition: red green blue beams combine into white. Paint subtracts; light adds.
