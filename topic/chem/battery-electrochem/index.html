法拉第定律如何定量电解产物量？

How does Faraday's law quantify electrolytic product amounts?

电解沉积或消耗的物质质量 m = (Q·M)/(n·F)，其中 Q 为通过电量（库仑），M 为摩尔质量，n 为电子转移数，F 为法拉第常数（96485 C/mol）。它说明产物量与电量成正比、与 n 成反比。
The mass produced or consumed is m = (Q·M)/(n·F), where Q is charge in coulombs, M molar mass, n electrons transferred, and F Faraday's constant (96485 C/mol). It shows product amount is proportional to charge and inversely proportional to n.

锂离子电池石墨负极充电时为何必须控制截止电压下限以避免锂金属析出?

Why must the lower cutoff potential of a graphitic anode be controlled during charging of a lithium-ion cell to avoid lithium metal plating?

石墨嵌锂后电位随嵌锂量下降,在高倍率或低温大电流充电时,负极表面极化会使局部电位降至0 V(vs Li/Li+)以下,锂离子来不及扩散进入石墨层间而在表面还原为金属锂。析出的锂一部分失去电活性成为死锂并消耗电解液,还会促进枝晶生长刺穿隔膜造成内短路。因此BMS需根据温度与倍率动态限制充电电流,把负极电位维持在析锂阈值之上。
As lithium intercalates, the graphite potential falls, and under high-rate or low-temperature charging the surface overpotential can push the local anode potential below 0 V vs Li/Li+, so Li+ reduces to metallic lithium on the surface instead of intercalating. Deposited lithium partially becomes electrochemically inert dead lithium while consuming electrolyte, and it promotes dendrite growth that can pierce the separator and cause internal short circuits. Battery management systems therefore limit charge current dynamically according to temperature and rate to keep the anode potential above the plating threshold.

Graphite anodes in lithium-ion batteries typically reach only about 80-90% first-cycle coulombic efficiency owing to SEI formation. Explain why and how the SEI forms, and how vinylene carbonate functions as a film-forming additive.

石墨负极嵌锂电位约0.1 V(相对于Li/Li+),低于常规碳酸酯溶剂的还原分解电位,首次充电时溶剂与锂盐在石墨表面被还原,生成的碳酸锂、烷基碳酸锂与聚合物沉积成一层离子导电而电子绝缘的钝化层,即SEI膜。VC分子中的碳碳双键降低了LUMO能级,使其更易还原,能在溶剂共嵌入石墨之前于更高电位优先分解,形成致密且富有机锂组分的薄界面膜。该膜阻止溶剂分子嵌入导致石墨层剥离,并减少活性锂的不可逆消耗,从而提升首次效率和循环寿命。
Lithium intercalates into graphite at about 0.1 V versus Li/Li+, below the reduction potentials of common carbonate solvents, so during the first charge the solvent and lithium salt are reduced on graphite; Li2CO3, lithium alkyl carbonates and polymers deposit into a passivation layer that conducts ions but blocks electrons - the SEI. The carbon-carbon double bond in VC lowers its LUMO, letting it reduce preferentially at higher potential before solvent co-intercalation starts and form a dense, organic-lithium-rich thin film. This film prevents graphite exfoliation driven by co-intercalated solvent and curbs irreversible lithium consumption, improving first-cycle efficiency and cycling stability.

原电池如何把化学能转化为电能？

How does a galvanic cell convert chemical energy to electrical energy?

自发氧化还原反应被分隔在两电极：阳极发生氧化释放电子，经外电路流向阴极发生还原。电子流动形成电流，离子通过盐桥迁移维持电荷平衡，电池电势来自两半反应电势差。
A spontaneous redox reaction is split across two electrodes: oxidation at the anode releases electrons that flow through an external circuit to the cathode where reduction occurs. Electron flow is current, and a salt bridge moves ions to maintain charge balance; cell potential comes from the half-cell potential difference.

During charging of a lithium-ion battery, how do lithium ions travel from the cathode material (e.g. LiFePO4) and intercalate into the graphite layers at the anode?

充电时外接电源迫使正极发生氧化,锂离子从磷酸铁锂晶格中脱出,经电解液和隔膜迁移到负极;同时电子经外电路到达负极以维持电中性。在石墨表面,锂离子与电子结合并嵌入石墨层间,形成LiC6等嵌入化合物,电能以化学能形式储存。
The applied voltage drives oxidation at the cathode, de-intercalating lithium ions from the LiFePO4 lattice; they migrate through the electrolyte and separator toward the anode while electrons travel via the external circuit to keep the cell neutral. At the graphite surface, ions combine with electrons and intercalate between graphene layers forming compounds such as LiC6, storing energy chemically.

How do lithium ions move during charge and discharge of a Li-ion battery?

放电时 Li⁺ 从负极石墨脱出，经电解质嵌入正极金属氧化物（如 LiCoO₂），电子经外电路做功；充电时外加电压使过程逆转。其高电压与能量密度源于锂的轻量与高负还原电势。
During discharge Li⁺ de-intercalates from the graphite anode, moves through the electrolyte, and inserts into the cathode metal oxide (e.g., LiCoO₂) while electrons do work externally; charging reverses this with applied voltage. High voltage and energy density come from lithium's low mass and very negative reduction potential.

What reactions occur at the anode and cathode?
