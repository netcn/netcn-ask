为什么把铁片丢进硫酸铜溶液会自己长出铜？

Why does copper 'grow' on an iron sheet in copper sulfate solution?

铁比铜活泼，发生置换：Fe + CuSO4 → FeSO4 + Cu，铜析出附在铁上；宋代《梦溪笔谈》记载胆水浸铜，是世界最早的湿法炼铜之一。
Iron is more reactive than copper, displacing it: Fe + CuSO4 → FeSO4 + Cu, copper deposits on iron. The Song-era 'dan-water copper' method was among the world's earliest hydrometallurgy.

如何判断氧化剂和还原剂？

How do you identify oxidizing and reducing agents?

在氧化还原反应中，所含元素化合价降低、得到电子的物质是氧化剂；化合价升高、失去电子的物质是还原剂。氧化剂自身被还原，还原剂自身被氧化。常见氧化剂有高锰酸钾、氧气，常见还原剂有氢气、金属单质。
In a redox reaction, the substance whose element's oxidation state decreases and gains electrons is the oxidizing agent; the one whose state increases and loses electrons is the reducing agent. The oxidizer is itself reduced and the reducer is oxidized. Common oxidizers include potassium permanganate and oxygen; common reducers include hydrogen and metals.

为什么氢既可以算非金属也可以单独分类？

Why is hydrogen sometimes classified separately rather than as a nonmetal?

氢只有一个电子，既能失去电子显金属性（如形成H+），也能得到一个电子显非金属性（如形成H-）。它位于周期表左上角，既不像碱金属也不像卤素，性质独特。因此在周期表中常被单独列出。
Hydrogen has a single electron, so it can lose it and behave metallically (forming H+) or gain one and behave nonmetallically (forming H-). Located at the top left, it resembles neither alkali metals nor halogens and is chemically unique. It is therefore often listed separately in the periodic table.

什么是氧化还原反应？

What is a redox reaction?

氧化还原反应是反应中有电子转移、元素化合价发生变化的反应。失去电子的过程叫氧化，得到电子的过程叫还原，二者总是同时发生。燃烧、呼吸、金属腐蚀都属于氧化还原反应。
A redox reaction is one in which electrons are transferred and the oxidation states of elements change. Losing electrons is oxidation and gaining them is reduction; the two always occur together. Combustion, respiration and metal corrosion are all redox reactions.

为什么铁生锈是氧化还原反应？

Why is rusting of iron a redox reaction?

铁在潮湿空气中与氧气和水反应生成氧化铁（铁锈），铁失去电子被氧化，氧气得到电子被还原。这是一种缓慢的氧化还原反应，称为电化学腐蚀。涂漆或镀锌可以隔绝空气和水从而防止生锈。
In moist air, iron reacts with oxygen and water to form iron oxide (rust); iron loses electrons and is oxidized while oxygen gains electrons and is reduced. This is a slow redox reaction known as electrochemical corrosion. Painting or galvanizing blocks air and water to prevent rusting.
