为什么泡打粉遇水又遇热都会产气？

Why does baking powder release gas both with water and with heat?

泡打粉是碳酸氢钠加两种酸性盐（如焦磷酸二氢钠、磷酸铝钠等）的混合物：遇水时一部分酸先反应放出二氧化碳，加热时另一部分酸再反应继续产气。这种双重作用让面团在搅拌和烘烤两阶段都膨松。
Baking powder mixes sodium bicarbonate with two kinds of acid salts (e.g., sodium acid pyrophosphate, sodium aluminum phosphate): one reacts with water to release CO2 during mixing, and another reacts on heating to release more during baking. This double-acting leavening lifts the dough in both stages.

什么是强酸和弱酸？

What are strong and weak acids?

强酸在水中几乎完全电离，如盐酸、硫酸和硝酸。弱酸只部分电离，如醋酸和碳酸，因此相同浓度下酸性较弱。
Strong acids ionize almost completely in water, such as hydrochloric, sulfuric, and nitric acids. Weak acids only partially ionize, such as acetic and carbonic acids, so they are less acidic at the same concentration.

为什么雨水有时候是弱酸性的（酸雨）？

Why is rain sometimes weakly acidic (acid rain)?

Even normal rain is slightly acidic (pH about 5.6) from dissolved carbon dioxide. Industrial sulfur dioxide and nitrogen oxides oxidize in air to sulfuric and nitric acids that fall with rain, lowering pH into acid rain, which corrodes buildings and harms plants. Cutting emissions is the key fix.

为什么叫碱性电池，它和普通电池有何不同？

Why is it called an alkaline battery and how does it differ?

碱性电池以氢氧化钾等强碱为电解液，而传统锌锰干电池用氯化铵等中性或弱酸电解质。碱性环境让锌负极反应更高效、内阻更低、容量和放电更优。但它不能充电，且漏液有腐蚀性碱。
An alkaline battery uses a strong base like potassium hydroxide as electrolyte, whereas traditional zinc-carbon cells use neutral or weakly acidic ammonium chloride. The alkaline medium lets the zinc anode react more efficiently with lower internal resistance, giving better capacity and discharge. It is not rechargeable, and leaks are corrosive alkali.

路易斯酸碱理论与布朗斯特酸碱理论有什么不同？为什么说 BF₃ 是酸却不含氢？

How does Lewis acid–base theory differ from Brønsted–Lowry theory, and why is BF₃ called an acid despite having no hydrogen?

布朗斯特酸碱以"质子（H⁺）的给受"来定义，而路易斯酸碱以"电子对的给受"来定义：路易斯酸是电子对受体。BF₃ 中的硼有空的 p 轨道可接受电子对，所以是路易斯酸，但它本身既不能给出也不含可被给出的质子。
Brønsted–Lowry theory defines acids and bases by proton donation and acceptance, whereas Lewis theory uses electron-pair donation and acceptance—a Lewis acid is an electron-pair acceptor. BF₃ has an empty orbital on boron that accepts an electron pair, making it a Lewis acid even though it contains no proton to donate.

缓冲溶液为什么能抵抗 pH 变化？它的缓冲能力为什么是有限的？

Why can a buffer solution resist pH change, and why is its capacity limited?

缓冲对（如醋酸/醋酸根）中，加入少量酸时被其共轭碱中和，加入少量碱时被弱酸中和，使 pH 基本不变。但缓冲组分的总量是有限的，一旦被外加酸碱大量消耗或比例失衡（通常超出 1:10–10:1 范围），缓冲能力就会急剧下降。
In a buffer pair (e.g., acetic acid/acetate), added acid is consumed by the conjugate base and added base by the weak acid, keeping pH nearly constant. The total amount of buffer components is finite, however, so once they are largely consumed or the ratio leaves the ~1:10–10:1 range, buffering collapses.

为什么小苏打能缓解胃酸过多？

Why does baking soda relieve excess stomach acid?

pH 是什么？它如何表示酸碱强弱？

What is pH and how does it express acidity or basicity?

pH 是溶液中氢离子活度负对数的度量：pH = –log a(H⁺)。pH<7 为酸性、=7 为中性、>7 为碱性（25℃）。它用对数压缩了巨大的浓度跨度，使强酸弱酸的差别一目了然。pH 计和试纸是常用测量工具。
pH is the negative logarithm of hydrogen-ion activity: pH = –log a(H⁺). Below 7 is acidic, 7 neutral, above 7 basic (at 25°C). The logarithm compresses a huge concentration range so strong and weak acids are easy to compare. pH meters and papers are common tools.

为什么纯水的中性点在 pH=7，而且这个"7"只有在常温（约25°C）下才算中性？

Why is pH = 7 considered neutral only at room temperature, not under all conditions?

中性指 [H⁺] = [OH⁻]，而水的离子积 Kw 随温度升高而增大。25°C 时 Kw≈10⁻¹⁴，中性 pH=7；当升温到 100°C 时 Kw≈10⁻¹²，中性 pH 降到约 6，此时 pH=7 反而偏碱。
Neutrality means [H⁺] = [OH⁻], but water's ion product Kw grows with temperature. At 25°C, Kw ≈ 10⁻¹⁴ so neutral pH is 7; at 100°C, Kw ≈ 10⁻¹² and neutral pH falls to about 6, so pH 7 would actually be basic.

为什么醋是酸性的？

Why is vinegar acidic?

醋是粮食或水果发酵产生的，主要酸性成分是乙酸（约3%至5%）。乙酸电离出氢离子，所以pH偏低、尝起来酸。不同醋还含乳酸、苹果酸等，带来各自风味。
Vinegar comes from fermenting grains or fruit, and its main acid is acetic acid at about 3%–5%. Acetic acid releases hydrogen ions, so the pH is low and it tastes sour. Other acids like lactic or malic add distinctive flavors.

What happens when an acid and a base are mixed?

酸和碱混合会发生中和反应，生成盐和水，溶液的酸碱性会减弱。比如小苏打（碱）可以用来减轻胃酸过多带来的不适。
When an acid and a base are mixed they undergo neutralization, forming a salt and water, and the solution becomes less acidic or basic. For example, baking soda, a base, can relieve discomfort from excess stomach acid.

酸和碱有什么不同？

What is the difference between an acid and a base?

酸是尝起来发酸、能使蓝色石蕊变红的物质，如醋里的醋酸。碱多有滑腻感、能使红色石蕊变蓝，如肥皂里的成分。
An acid tastes sour and turns blue litmus red, such as acetic acid in vinegar. A base feels slippery and turns red litmus blue, such as the ingredients in soap.

用石蕊试纸怎么分辨酸和碱？

How do you use litmus paper to tell acids from bases?

把试纸浸入溶液，若变红说明是酸性，若变蓝说明是碱性，颜色不变则接近中性。石蕊是一种常用的酸碱指示剂。
Dip the paper into the solution: red means acidic, blue means basic, and no change means nearly neutral. Litmus is a common acid-base indicator.
