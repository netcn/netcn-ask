什么是自催化反应？

What is an autocatalytic reaction?

自催化反应中，某一生成物本身充当后续反应的催化剂，使反应速率随产物积累而加快。典型例子是高锰酸钾与草酸反应初期慢、随后因生成物催化而加速。自催化常表现为S形浓度-时间曲线。
In an autocatalytic reaction, a product itself acts as the catalyst for later steps, so the rate accelerates as product accumulates. A classic example is permanganate with oxalic acid, slow at first then speeding up via product catalysis. Autocatalysis often gives an S-shaped concentration-time curve.

什么是催化剂中毒？

What is catalyst poisoning?

催化剂中毒指某些杂质（如硫、磷、重金属离子）强烈吸附在活性中心上，占据或破坏活性位点，使催化活性显著下降甚至永久失活。中毒可分为可逆与不可逆两类。工业上需对原料进行深度净化以防止中毒。
Catalyst poisoning occurs when impurities such as sulfur, phosphorus or heavy-metal ions strongly adsorb onto active sites, occupying or destroying them and sharply lowering activity, sometimes permanently. Poisoning can be reversible or irreversible. Industry must deeply purify feedstocks to prevent it.

什么是光催化？

What is photocatalysis?

光催化利用半导体（如二氧化钛）在光照下激发产生电子-空穴对，参与表面氧化还原反应，从而分解污染物或分解水制氢。它属于非均相催化，可在常温常压下进行。光催化在环境净化和清洁能源中前景广阔。
Photocatalysis uses a semiconductor such as titanium dioxide that, under light, generates electron-hole pairs participating in surface redox reactions to degrade pollutants or split water for hydrogen. It is heterogeneous catalysis operating at room conditions. It holds promise for environmental cleanup and clean energy.

什么是哈伯法合成氨的催化剂？

What catalyst is used in the Haber process for ammonia?

哈伯法在铁基催化剂作用下，把氮气和氢气合成氨，是化肥工业的基础。该反应需要高温高压，催化剂显著降低活化能。
The Haber process uses an iron-based catalyst to synthesize ammonia from nitrogen and hydrogen, forming the basis of the fertilizer industry. It needs high temperature and pressure, and the catalyst greatly lowers the activation energy.

催化剂为什么不能改变化学平衡的位置？

Why can’t a catalyst change the position of equilibrium?

催化剂同等降低正、逆反应的活化能，只加快到达平衡的速度，但不改变平衡常数和最终产率。
A catalyst lowers the activation energy of both forward and reverse reactions equally—it speeds reaching equilibrium but changes neither K nor the final yield.

沸石为什么能作为催化剂载体？

Why can zeolites serve as catalyst supports?

沸石是具有规整微孔结构的铝硅酸盐，比表面积巨大且孔径均一，可均匀分散活性组分并限域反应物分子。其酸性位点本身也能催化反应，并凭借择形效应提高选择性。因此沸石广泛用于裂化、异构化等过程。
Zeolites are aluminosilicates with regular microporous structures, huge specific surface area and uniform pore sizes that disperse active species and confine reactant molecules. Their acidic sites can catalyze directly and, via shape selectivity, raise selectivity. Hence zeolites are widely used in cracking and isomerization.

什么是催化循环？

What is a catalytic cycle?

催化循环描述催化剂在反应中经历一系列中间态后再生、可再次参与反应的过程。每一步通常有明确的基元反应，催化剂在循环首尾状态不变。理解催化循环有助于设计更高效的催化剂与反应路径。
A catalytic cycle describes how a catalyst passes through a series of intermediates and is regenerated to participate again, with each step a defined elementary reaction and the catalyst unchanged from start to end. Understanding the cycle helps design more efficient catalysts and pathways.

什么是催化活性中心？

What is a catalytic active site?

活性中心是催化剂表面或内部能够吸附反应物并促成化学键断裂与形成的特定原子或原子团。反应物在活性中心上被活化，经历吸附、反应、脱附过程。活性中心的数量与性质决定催化剂的活性和选择性。
An active site is a specific atom or group on a catalyst's surface or interior that adsorbs reactants and promotes bond breaking and formation. Reactants are activated there through adsorption, reaction and desorption. The number and nature of active sites determine a catalyst's activity and selectivity.

不对称（手性）催化为什么能几乎只生成一种对映体？

How can asymmetric (chiral) catalysis produce essentially only one enantiomer?

手性催化剂本身具有不对称的立体环境，反应物在其活性中心有两种对映面的结合方式，其中能量更低的一种占优势，从而只加速生成某一种对映体。2001 年诺贝尔化学奖即授予 Knowles、Noyori、Sharpless 在不对称催化领域的贡献。
A chiral catalyst presents an asymmetric environment at its active site, so the reactant can bind in two mirror-image orientations with different energies; the lower-energy pathway dominates and selectively accelerates formation of one enantiomer. The 2001 Nobel Prize in Chemistry honored Knowles, Noyori, and Sharpless for asymmetric catalysis.

纳米催化为什么活性更高？

Why is nanocatalysis more active?

纳米尺度催化剂表面原子比例剧增，暴露在外的活性位点多，比表面积大，从而显著提升活性。同时量子尺寸效应和表面配位不饱和也改变电子结构、增强吸附活化能力。但纳米颗粒易团聚失活，需要稳定分散。
At the nanoscale, the fraction of surface atoms surges, exposing many active sites and a huge specific surface area, markedly raising activity. Quantum-size effects and unsaturated surface coordination also alter the electronic structure and strengthen adsorption-activation. But nanoparticles tend to agglomerate and deactivate, so stable dispersion is needed.

催化剂为什么能加快反应，却不会出现在最终产物里？它是怎么做到的？

How can a catalyst speed up a reaction without appearing in the final products?

催化剂提供了新的反应路径，显著降低反应的活化能，使更多分子能翻过能量障碍，反应因此变快。催化剂会先和反应物结合，再在后续步骤中重新释放出来，所以自身在反应前后的化学组成和数量不变，不会写进总反应方程式。但催化剂不能改变反应热和平衡位置，只能让体系更快到达平衡。
A catalyst opens a new reaction pathway with a much lower activation energy, so far more molecules can surmount the energy barrier and the reaction accelerates. It binds the reactants in an early step and is regenerated in a later one, so its own composition and amount are unchanged overall and it never appears in the balanced equation. A catalyst cannot change the heat of reaction or the equilibrium position; it only lets the system get there faster.

为什么催化剂在反应前后质量不变？

Why does a catalyst keep its mass before and after a reaction?

催化剂只改变反应途径而不被消耗，它在反应中被暂时结合，又在产物生成后释放出来。因此反应结束时的催化剂与开始时一样多。
A catalyst only changes the reaction pathway and is not consumed; it is temporarily bound during the reaction and released when products form. So the amount of catalyst at the end equals that at the start.

二氧化钛（TiO₂）光催化分解污染物时，紫外光究竟起了什么作用？

In TiO₂ photocatalysis for breaking down pollutants, what role does ultraviolet light play?

紫外光子的能量大于 TiO₂ 的带隙（约 3.2 eV），把电子从价带激发到导带，留下带正电的空穴。电子把氧分子还原成超氧自由基，空穴把水氧化成羟基自由基，这些高活性物种再去氧化分解有机物污染物。
UV photons exceed TiO₂'s band gap (~3.2 eV), exciting electrons from the valence band to the conduction band and leaving holes behind. Electrons reduce O₂ to superoxide radicals and holes oxidize water to hydroxyl radicals, and these reactive species decompose organic pollutants.

什么是均相催化和非均相催化？

What are homogeneous and heterogeneous catalysis?

均相催化中催化剂与反应物处于同一相（如都在溶液或气相），接触充分、效率高，但分离回收难。非均相催化中催化剂与反应物处于不同相（如固体催化剂作用于气体或液体），易于分离和重复使用。工业上多使用非均相催化。
In homogeneous catalysis the catalyst and reactants are in the same phase (both in solution or gas), giving thorough contact and high efficiency but difficult separation. In heterogeneous catalysis they are in different phases (e.g. a solid catalyst acting on gas or liquid), which is easy to separate and reuse. Industry mostly uses heterogeneous catalysis.

双氧水（过氧化氢）在室温下分解很慢，为什么加入二氧化锰后立即大量冒泡？

Hydrogen peroxide decomposes slowly at room temperature; why does adding manganese dioxide make it bubble vigorously at once?

过氧化氢分解 2H2O2 生成 2H2O+O2 的活化能较高，室温下能越过能量障碍的分子很少。二氧化锰作为催化剂吸附并分解过氧化氢分子，提供一条活化能低得多的路径，反应速率骤增，放出的氧气就成了大量气泡。反应结束后二氧化锰仍保持原状，可反复使用，这正是催化剂参加反应又回归原貌的典型例子。
The decomposition 2H2O2 → 2H2O + O2 has a fairly high activation energy, so at room temperature few molecules can clear the barrier. Manganese dioxide acts as a catalyst, adsorbing and breaking down H2O2 molecules along a much lower-energy path, so the rate jumps and the oxygen released forms a burst of bubbles. The manganese dioxide is left unchanged and reusable - a classic example of a catalyst taking part and then regenerating.

铂为什么常用作催化剂？

Why is platinum often used as a catalyst?

铂属于贵金属，化学性质稳定且表面易吸附反应物分子，能显著改变反应路径降低活化能。它常用于汽车尾气催化转化、氨氧化制硝酸等。但铂成本高，且易被硫化物等毒化失活。
Platinum is a noble metal that is chemically stable and readily adsorbs reactant molecules on its surface, markedly changing the pathway and lowering activation energy. 为什么铂、钯、镍等过渡金属常常被用作催化剂？这和它们的 d 轨道有什么关系？

均相催化与多相催化差别在哪？

What distinguishes homogeneous from heterogeneous catalysis?

均相催化剂与反应物同相（如溶液中的金属配合物），活性位点均一、选择性高但难回收；多相催化剂为另一相（如固体表面），易分离却可能传质受限。二者机理分别由有机金属与表面科学阐述。
A homogeneous catalyst shares a phase with reactants (e.g., a metal complex in solution), giving uniform sites and high selectivity but hard recovery; a heterogeneous one is a separate phase (solid surface), easy to separate yet mass-transport limited. Their mechanisms belong to organometallic and surface science respectively.

南极臭氧洞为什么总在南半球早春达到最深,而不是出现在极夜期间?

Why does the Antarctic ozone hole reach its deepest in early spring rather than during the polar night?

臭氧销毁需要阳光,而活性氯在极夜期间就已积累。极夜低温使极涡内形成极地平流层云,云粒表面把氯储库物种(HCl、ClONO2)转化为Cl2等活性氯;早春太阳回归后光解释放氯原子,经ClO二聚体催化循环,每个氯原子可销毁数万个臭氧分子,故空洞此时最深,并随气温回升、云层消散而减弱。
Ozone destruction needs sunlight, but the active chlorine is stockpiled during the polar night. Intense cold lets polar stratospheric clouds form inside the winter vortex, and on their surfaces reservoir species such as HCl and ClONO2 are converted into photolabile chlorine. When the sun returns in early spring, chlorine atoms are released at once and, through the ClO dimer catalytic cycle, each atom can destroy tens of thousands of ozone molecules, so the hole peaks then and fades as temperatures rise and the clouds vanish.

What is a catalyst?
