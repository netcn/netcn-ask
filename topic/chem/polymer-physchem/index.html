橡胶拉伸时为什么会发热，这和化学键有什么关系？

Why does stretched rubber warm up, and what does entropy have to do with it?

橡胶由交联的长链高分子组成，未拉伸时分子链卷曲、可用构象数（熵）大；拉伸时被强迫拉直，构象数减少、熵下降。根据热力学，橡胶的弹性主要源于熵变而非键能：快速拉伸时熵减使内能小幅上升而发热，松手回弹则熵增、吸热而变凉。所以橡胶的弹性是“熵弹性”，与金属弹簧的“能弹性”机制相反。
Rubber is a network of cross-linked long polymer chains. Unstretched, the chains are coiled and have many possible conformations (high entropy); stretching forces them straight, reducing the number of conformations and lowering entropy. Thermodynamically, rubber's elasticity comes mainly from this entropy change rather than bond energy: quick stretching lowers entropy, raises internal energy a bit, and warms the rubber, while releasing it lets entropy rise and the rubber cools. So rubber shows entropy elasticity, the opposite of a metal spring's energy elasticity.

Flory-Huggins 相互作用参数 chi 随温度升高而减小时,聚合物溶液会出现哪种相分离行为,临界点如何确定?

When the Flory-Huggins interaction parameter chi decreases with increasing temperature, what phase-separation behavior appears in a polymer solution and how is the critical point determined?

chi 随温度降低(即升温使 chi 减小)时体系表现出上临界共溶温度(UCST)行为:高温均相,降温进入双结线以内发生液-液相分离。对高分子量链,混合熵项被链长 N 压低,临界点满足 chi_c ≈ (1/2)(1+1/√N)^2 并趋于 0.5,临界体积分数 φ_c ≈ 1/√N 且偏向溶剂一侧。这解释了为何高分子溶液的互溶窗口远比小分子混合物窄。
Such a system shows upper critical solution temperature (UCST) behavior: miscible at high temperature, it enters the binodal on cooling and undergoes liquid-liquid demixing. For long chains the entropy term is suppressed by the degree of polymerization N, giving chi_c ≈ (1/2)(1+1/√N)^2 → 0.5 and a critical polymer volume fraction φ_c ≈ 1/√N skewed toward solvent-rich compositions. This is why polymer solutions have far narrower miscibility windows than small-molecule mixtures.

#Flory-Huggins理论 #相分离

多数聚合物溶液降温才浑浊,聚N-异丙基丙烯酰胺的水溶液却在加热到32摄氏度附近突然分相,这是为什么?

Most polymer solutions cloud on cooling, yet aqueous poly(N-isopropylacrylamide) suddenly phase-separates when warmed past about 32 degrees Celsius — why?

Flory-Huggins相互作用参数并不是常数,它同时包含依赖温度的焓贡献与过量熵贡献;酰胺基团与水之间的氢键随升温削弱,熵项又不利于混合,χ随之越过临界值。混合自由能曲线因此失去稳定性,体系在下临界共溶温度处自发脱混。这一温敏特性让PNIPAM成为智能水凝胶与温控分离膜的骨架材料。
The Flory-Huggins interaction parameter is anything but constant: it carries both a temperature-dependent enthalpic part and an excess entropic part. Hydrogen bonds between amide groups and water weaken as heat is applied while the entropic term already disfavors mixing, so chi climbs past its critical value. The mixed free-energy curve then loses stability and demixes spontaneously at the lower critical solution temperature — the property that makes PNIPAM a scaffold for smart hydrogels and thermally gated membranes.

邻苯二甲酸二辛酯这类小分子增塑剂,为什么能让硬质PVC的玻璃化转变温度大幅下降?

How do small-molecule plasticizers such as dioctyl phthalate drive the glass transition temperature of rigid PVC so far downward?

增塑剂分子插入高分子链之间,既扩大了体系的自由体积,又屏蔽了链段间的偶极-偶极吸引,使链段在更低的温度下就能开始协同运动。Tg的降幅可按增塑剂体积分数用Kelley-Bueche方程估算。用量过头则因相分离而迁移渗出,制品会重新发脆。
Plasticizer molecules slip between the chains, enlarging the free volume while screening dipole-dipole attraction between segments, so cooperative segmental motion begins at a much lower temperature. The drop in Tg can be estimated from the plasticizer volume fraction with the Kelley-Bueche equation. Beyond an optimum dose the additive phase-separates and migrates out, leaving the product brittle again.

为什么塑料那么轻？

Why is plastic so light?

塑料是碳氢长链高分子，本身密度接近水甚至更小，所以同样体积比金属轻得多。
Plastics are long carbon-hydrogen polymer chains with densities near or below water, so they are far lighter than metals by volume.
