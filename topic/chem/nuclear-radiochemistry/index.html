正电子发射断层扫描（PET）利用了什么核化学原理实现体内成像？

What nuclear-chemistry principle does positron emission tomography (PET) exploit for in vivo imaging?

PET 使用发射正电子的放射性核素（如 ¹⁸F、¹¹C）标记生物分子注入体内。正电子行进极短距离后与电子发生湮灭，产生一对方向相反的 511 keV γ 光子；符合探测这两个光子的共线事件即可重建示踪剂在体内的三维分布，反映代谢与受体活动。
PET uses positron-emitting radionuclides such as ¹⁸F or ¹¹C to label biomolecules injected into the body. The positron travels a very short distance before annihilating with an electron, producing a pair of 511 keV gamma photons emitted in opposite directions; coincidence detection of these collinear photons reconstructs the tracer's 3D distribution, reflecting metabolism and receptor activity.

碳-14 测年法的原理是什么？它的适用年代范围和局限有哪些？

What is the principle of carbon-14 dating, and what are its applicable range and limitations?

宇宙射线产生的大气 ¹⁴C 被生物通过碳循环固定，活着时体内 ¹⁴C/¹²C 比保持恒定；死亡后 ¹⁴C 按 5730 年半衰期衰变而不再补充。测残留 ¹⁴C 比例即可反推死亡年代，常规适用范围约 5 万年以内。局限包括需校正大气 ¹⁴C 波动、样品须含足够碳且未受现代碳污染。
Cosmic rays produce atmospheric ¹⁴C, which living organisms fix via the carbon cycle so their ¹⁴C/¹²C ratio stays constant; after death ¹⁴C decays with a 5730-year half-life and is no longer replenished. Measuring the remaining ¹⁴C fraction back-calculates the death date, typically up to about 50,000 years. Limits include calibrating atmospheric ¹⁴C fluctuations and ensuring samples contain enough carbon and no modern contamination.

锝-99m为什么是医学影像中最重要的放射性同位素？

Why is technetium-99m the most important radioisotope in medical imaging?

99mTc半衰期仅约6小时，发射140千电子伏的伽马射线，穿透力适中、组织吸收少，适合SPECT相机成像；它衰变后很快变成半衰期很长、只发软β射线的99Tc，对患者的剂量负担低。99mTc由钼-99衰变产生，可标记到多种配体上靶向不同器官，用于心脏灌注显像、骨扫描等。
99mTc has a half-life of only about 6 hours and emits 140-keV gamma rays that penetrate well with little tissue absorption, ideal for SPECT imaging; after decay it quickly becomes 99Tc, which has a long half-life and emits only soft beta radiation, keeping the patient dose low. Produced from molybdenum-99 decay, 99mTc can be tagged onto many ligands to target different organs, such as cardiac perfusion imaging and bone scans.

核裂变释放能量的物理化学本质是什么？链式反应如何维持？

What is the physico-chemical essence of energy release in nuclear fission, and how is a chain reaction sustained?

重核（如 ²³⁵U）吸收中子裂变为两个中等质量碎片，由于中等核每个核子平均结合能更高，质量亏损按 E=mc² 转化为巨大能量。裂变同时放出 2–3 个中子，若其中至少一个引发新的裂变并使增殖系数 k ≥ 1，链式反应即可自持。临界质量与几何、反射层共同决定能否达到临界。
A heavy nucleus such as ²³⁵U captures a neutron and splits into two medium-mass fragments; because medium nuclei have higher binding energy per nucleon, the mass defect converts to huge energy via E=mc². Fission also emits 2–3 neutrons, and if at least one induces another fission so the multiplication factor k ≥ 1, the chain reaction sustains itself. Critical mass and geometry, with reflectors, determine whether criticality is reached.

同位素示踪技术在化学与生物医学研究中有哪些典型应用？

What are typical applications of isotope tracing in chemical and biomedical research?

同位素示踪利用同一元素不同核素（如 ²H、¹³C、¹⁵N、¹⁸O）化学行为相同但可被检测的特性，追踪原子在反应或代谢中的去向。典型应用包括用 ¹³C 标记底物研究代谢通路、用 ¹⁵N 追踪肥料在土壤-植物中的迁移、以及用 ¹⁸F 标记示踪剂进行 PET 成像。
Isotope tracing exploits that different nuclides of an element (e.g., ²H, ¹³C, ¹⁵N, ¹⁸O) behave chemically identically yet are detectable, letting researchers follow atoms through reactions or metabolism. Typical uses include ¹³C-labeled substrates to map metabolic pathways, ¹⁵N to track fertilizer movement in soil–plant systems, and ¹⁸F tracers for PET imaging.

α、β、γ 三种射线各有什么特性？它们的穿透能力有何差异？

What are the characteristics of alpha, beta, and gamma radiation, and how do their penetrating powers differ?

α 粒子是氦核（⁴₂He），带正电、质量大，电离强但一张纸即可阻挡；β 粒子是高速电子，穿透力中等，需几毫米铝板屏蔽；γ 是高频电磁波（光子），电离弱但穿透极强，需厚铅或混凝土防护。三者电离与穿透能力呈反向关系。
Alpha particles are helium nuclei (⁴₂He), positively charged and massive, with strong ionization but stopped by paper; beta particles are fast electrons of moderate penetration needing a few millimeters of aluminum; gamma rays are high-frequency photons, weakly ionizing but highly penetrating, requiring thick lead or concrete. Their ionizing and penetrating powers are inversely related.

放射性衰变主要有哪几种类型？各伴随什么粒子或辐射？

What are the main types of radioactive decay, and what particles or radiation accompany each?

主要衰变类型包括 α 衰变（放出氦核，原子序数减 2）、β⁻ 衰变（中子转质子放出电子与反中微子）、β⁺ 衰变（质子转中子放出正电子与中微子）以及 γ 衰变（激发核退激放出光子）。此外还有电子俘获等过程。衰变类型由核的中子-质子比与能量状态决定。
Main decay types are alpha decay (emits a helium nucleus, atomic number drops by 2), beta-minus decay (a neutron becomes a proton, emitting an electron and antineutrino), beta-plus decay (a proton becomes a neutron, emitting a positron and neutrino), and gamma decay (an excited nucleus de-excites, emitting a photon). Electron capture also occurs. The type depends on the neutron–proton ratio and the nucleus's energy state.

放射性衰变的半衰期是什么意思？它能用来做什么？

What does the half-life of radioactive decay mean, and what is it used for?

半衰期是放射性核素数量衰变到原来一半所需的时间，由核种本身决定、不受温度压力等外界条件影响。衰变遵循一级动力学 N = N₀·(1/2)^(t/T₁/₂)，因此可用剩余活度推算样品年代或计算辐照剂量衰减。常见如 ¹⁴C 半衰期约 5730 年、¹³¹I 约 8 天。
The half-life is the time for a radioactive nuclide's amount to fall to half its initial value; it is intrinsic to the nuclide and independent of temperature, pressure, and chemical state. Decay follows first-order kinetics N = N₀·(1/2)^(t/T₁/₂), so remaining activity can date samples or compute dose decay. Examples include ¹⁴C at about 5730 years and ¹³¹I at about 8 days.

商品化的锝[99mTc]药盒都预装了氯化亚锡，注入淋洗液后室温摇匀即可完成标记。亚锡离子在其中起什么关键作用？

Commercial 99mTc radiopharmaceutical kits come preloaded with stannous chloride, so adding the generator eluate and shaking finishes labeling at room temperature. What key role does Sn(II) play here?

从发生器淋洗出的高锝酸钠中锝为+7价，对配体几乎呈惰性。亚锡离子把Tc(VII)还原到+4/+5等较低氧化态，形成能被DTPA、MDP或HMPAO等螯合剂迅速捕获的活性物种；过量锡还可清除溶解氧并维持合适pH，从而保障标记率和制剂稳定性。
Pertechnetate eluted from the generator carries Tc(VII), which is almost inert toward ligands. Stannous ion reduces Tc(VII) to lower oxidation states such as +4/+5, forming reactive species promptly captured by chelators like DTPA, MDP or HMPAO; surplus tin also scavenges dissolved oxygen and keeps the pH suitable, safeguarding labeling yield and formulation stability.
