啤酒发酵产生的双乙酰带来馊饭样(黄油样)异味:请说明它经由缬氨酸生物合成途径中α-乙酰乳酸非酶氧化脱羧的形成机理,以及成熟期酵母将其还原脱毒的途径与相应工艺控制手段。

Diacetyl imparts a buttery (spoiled-rice-like) off-flavor in beer. Explain how it forms via non-enzymatic oxidative decarboxylation of alpha-acetolactate leaked from the valine biosynthesis pathway, and how yeast removes it during maturation along with the process controls involved.

发酵期间酵母由乙酰羟酸合酶(ILV2编码)把丙酮酸转化为α-乙酰乳酸供缬氨酸合成,部分前体泄漏到胞外后经非酶氧化脱羧生成双乙酰。成熟期酵母的双乙酰还原酶先把它还原为乙偶姻、再还原为2,3-丁二醇,两者的感官阈值比双乙酰高出数千倍,异味随之消除。生产上在主酵末期回温进行双乙酰还原期(diacetyl rest)、维持足量悬浮酵母并避免过早降温,可以加速这一还原过程。
During fermentation yeast converts pyruvate into alpha-acetolactate via ILV2-encoded acetohydroxyacid synthase for valine synthesis; part of this precursor leaks out of the cell and undergoes non-enzymatic oxidative decarboxylation to diacetyl. In maturation, yeast diacetyl reductase steps it down to acetoin and then 2,3-butanediol, whose sensory thresholds are thousands of times higher, so the off-flavor fades away. Brewers accelerate the reduction with a diacetyl rest near the end of primary fermentation, keeping enough yeast in suspension and avoiding premature chilling.

发酵的化学本质是什么？

What is the chemical nature of fermentation?

发酵是无氧条件下微生物或酶把糖转化为乙醇、乳酸等并释放少量能量的代谢过程。如酵母将葡萄糖经糖酵解生成乙醇与 CO₂（酿酒、发面），乳酸菌生成乳酸（酸奶、泡菜）。
Fermentation is an anaerobic metabolic process where microbes or enzymes convert sugars into ethanol, lactate, etc., releasing little energy. Yeast turns glucose via glycolysis into ethanol and CO₂ (brewing, bread), while lactic-acid bacteria make lactate (yogurt, kimchi).

为什么绿茶清汤红茶红浓？

Why is green tea clear but black tea reddish and strong?

红茶经全发酵，多酚氧化酶把儿茶素氧化成茶黄素、茶红素，汤色红；绿茶杀青钝化酶，保留绿叶绿汤。
Black tea is fully oxidized: polyphenol oxidase turns catechins into theaflavins/thearubigins (red). Green tea is pan-fired to kill the enzyme, keeping it green.

为什么纳豆又黏又臭？

Why is natto sticky and smelly?

纳豆由纳豆菌（枯草芽孢杆菌）发酵大豆，产生γ-多聚谷氨酸等多糖而拉丝，并分解蛋白产生特殊风味，是东亚传统发酵食品。
Natto is fermented by Bacillus subtilis, producing γ-polyglutamate (stringy polysaccharide) and broken-down proteins (distinctive smell)—a traditional East Asian ferment.

啤酒酿造中的双乙酰是什么物质产生的风味缺陷?现代工艺如何将其控制在阈值以下?

Diacetyl is a flavor defect in beer. Which metabolic route produces it, and how do modern brewing processes keep it below threshold?

双乙酰由酵母缬氨酸合成支路中间体α-乙酰乳酸在发酵液中外溢并自发氧化脱羧生成,赋予啤酒馊饭样甜味,阈值仅约0.1 mg/L。控制手段包括提高发酵后期温度进行双乙酰还原、添加足量酵母利用α-乙酰乳酸脱羧酶加速转化,以及保证麦汁中足量可同化氮减少α-乙酰乳酸积累。成品检测双乙酰含量达标后方可过滤包装。
Diacetyl arises when α-acetolactate, an intermediate of yeast valine biosynthesis, leaks into wort and spontaneously oxidatively decarboxylates; it gives a buttery, spoiled-milk note with a threshold near 0.1 mg/L. Brewers control it with a warm diacetyl rest late in fermentation, sufficient pitching yeast (sometimes engineered or supplemented with α-acetolactate decarboxylase) to convert the precursor quickly, and adequate assimilable nitrogen in wort to limit precursor buildup. Packaging proceeds only after diacetyl analysis confirms the batch meets spec.

为什么中国白酒和馒头都靠酒曲而不是只加酵母？

Why do Chinese liquor and steamed buns rely on 'qu' (starter) rather than just yeast?

酒曲是发霉谷物，含霉菌（如根霉）和酵母；霉菌先把淀粉糖化成葡萄糖，酵母再把糖发酵成酒精，这种边糖化边发酵是中国酿造的特色。
Qu is steamed grain cultured with mold (e.g. Rhizopus) and yeast; the mold first saccharifies starch to sugar, then yeast ferments it to alcohol—a simultaneous saccharification-fermentation unique to East Asian brewing.

为什么发面蒸出来是松软的？

Why is steamed fermented dough soft and spongy?

酵母或老面中的微生物产二氧化碳，被面筋网络包裹，蒸时气体受热膨胀，蛋白质凝固定型，形成松软孔洞。
Yeast or old-dough microbes produce CO2 trapped by gluten; steaming expands the gas while proteins set, giving a spongy crumb.
