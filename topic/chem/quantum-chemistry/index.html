如何通过分子轨道图计算键级？以 N₂ 为例。

How do you compute bond order from a MO diagram, using N₂ as an example?

键级 = (成键电子数 − 反键电子数)/2。N₂ 有 10 个价电子，其中 8 个在成键轨道、2 个在反键轨道，键级 = (8−2)/2 = 3，对应三键且极稳定。
Bond order = (bonding − antibonding electrons)/2. N₂ has 10 valence electrons: 8 bonding and 2 antibonding, so bond order = (8−2)/2 = 3, matching a very stable triple bond.

波函数和薛定谔方程的作用是什么？

What is the role of the wavefunction and Schrödinger equation?

波函数 ψ 描述微观粒子的量子态，其平方 |ψ|² 给出在空间某处找到粒子的概率密度。薛定谔方程是一个描述体系能量与波函数关系的波动方程，求解它可得到原子和分子的能级与轨道。它是现代量子化学的基础。
The wavefunction ψ describes a particle's quantum state; its square |ψ|² gives the probability density of finding the particle at a point. The Schrödinger equation relates a system's energy to its wavefunction, and solving it yields the energy levels and orbitals of atoms and molecules. It is the foundation of modern quantum chemistry.

玻尔模型如何解释氢原子光谱？

How does the Bohr model explain the hydrogen spectrum?

玻尔模型假设氢原子的电子只能处在一系列分立的能级上，跃迁时吸收或发射特定频率的光子，能量差为 ΔE = hν。这解释了氢原子线状光谱的存在和里德伯公式。虽对多电子原子不完善，却是量子化的经典开端。
The Bohr model assumes a hydrogen electron occupies only discrete energy levels and emits or absorbs photons of specific frequency on transitions, with ΔE = hν. This explains the line spectrum of hydrogen and the Rydberg formula. Though incomplete for multi-electron atoms, it was a classic step toward quantization.

分子轨道理论如何解释 O₂ 的顺磁性？

How does molecular orbital theory explain the paramagnetism of O₂?

在 O₂ 的 MO 图中，两个最高占据轨道（π*2p）各容纳一个自旋平行电子，产生净磁矩，使其被磁场吸引。价键理论无法预测这一点，因为它只画了双键而没有未成对电子。
In O₂'s MO diagram the two highest occupied π*2p orbitals each hold one unpaired electron with parallel spins, giving a net magnetic moment that attracts it to a magnetic field. Valence bond theory fails here because it depicts only a double bond with no unpaired electrons.

洪特规则是什么？

What is Hund's rule?

洪特规则说，在能量相同的简并轨道上，电子会先以相同自旋分别占据不同轨道，再配对。这样排布使总自旋最大、电子间排斥最小，体系能量最低。例如碳的 2p 轨道先各占一个电子。
Hund's rule says that electrons first occupy degenerate orbitals singly with parallel spins before pairing. This maximizes total spin and minimizes electron repulsion, giving the lowest energy. For example, carbon's 2p electrons first occupy separate p orbitals.

什么是原子轨道？量子数有什么作用？

What is an atomic orbital and what do quantum numbers do?

原子轨道是描述原子中电子概率分布的空间区域，由主量子数 n、角量子数 l、磁量子数 m 和自旋量子数 ms 共同确定。n 决定能级与大小，l 决定形状（s 球形、p 哑铃形等），m 决定空间取向。它们限定了电子可能的量子态。
An atomic orbital is the spatial region describing an electron's probability distribution in an atom, specified by the principal n, azimuthal l, magnetic m, and spin ms quantum numbers. n sets energy and size, l sets shape (s spherical, p dumbbell, etc.), and m sets orientation. Together they define the allowed quantum states.
