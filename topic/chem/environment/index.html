Why are dioxins extremely toxic and persistent?

多氯二苯并二噁英脂溶、稳定，并沿食物链生物放大；它结合 Ah 受体扰乱基因表达。
Polychlorinated dibenzo-dioxins are lipophilic and stable, biomagnifying up the food chain; they bind the Ah receptor and disrupt gene expression.

What is the chemistry of ocean acidification?

大气 CO₂ 溶入海水生成 H₂CO₃，降低 pH 并减少 CO₃²⁻，使珊瑚与贝类更难形成 CaCO₃ 外壳。
Atmospheric CO₂ dissolves to form H₂CO₃, lowering pH and depleting CO₃²⁻, making it harder for corals and shellfish to build CaCO₃ shells.

Is 'speciation' the key to arsenic toxicity in groundwater?

无机砷（As(III)、As(V)）远毒于有机砷；As(III) 更易吸收且难沉淀，除砷需先氧化再加吸附或共沉淀。
Inorganic As(III)/As(V) is far more toxic than organic arsenic; As(III) is more absorbable and less precipitable, so removal needs oxidation then adsorption or co-precipitation.

为什么光化学烟雾会形成？

Why does photochemical smog form?

起点是 NO₂ 见光分解：波长小于约 420 nm 的光把 NO₂ 打成 NO 和一个氧原子，氧原子与 O₂ 结合成臭氧。问题是城里有一堆挥发性有机物，它们在阳光下降解出的自由基把 NO 又氧化回 NO₂，却不消耗臭氧——于是臭氧只积累不减少，到下午达到峰值。同时生成的过氧乙酰硝酸酯(PAN)和气溶胶一起，构成那种刺激眼睛、让能见度下降的光化学烟雾。这也是它和「煤烟型」烟雾相反的地方: 太阳越烈，污染越重。
It starts with photolysis of NO2: light shorter than about 420 nm splits NO2 into NO plus an oxygen atom, and that atom adds to O2 to make ozone. The trouble is the volatile organic compounds in a city: radicals from their breakdown re-oxidise NO back to NO2 without consuming the ozone, so ozone only accumulates, peaking in the afternoon. Peroxyacetyl nitrate (PAN) and aerosols formed alongside it make up the eye-stinging, visibility-reducing smog. This is the opposite of coal smoke type smog: the stronger the sun, the worse the pollution.

Why do some lakes resist acid rain while others acidify quickly?

含碳酸盐岩石的湖泊有碱度缓冲，能中和酸；缺乏缓冲的湖泊酸度积累，鱼和微生物会受害。
Lakes with carbonate rocks have alkalinity to neutralize acid; poorly buffered lakes accumulate acidity, harming life.

Alkanolamines like MEA reversibly form carbamates with CO₂ and are regenerated by heating; it is the leading power-plant capture method but energy-intensive.

Pb²⁺ mimics Ca²⁺ or Zn²⁺, disrupting enzymes and neurotransmitters and damaging the developing brain and intelligence.
