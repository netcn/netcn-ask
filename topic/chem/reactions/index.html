零级反应有什么特征？举一个实例。

What characterizes a zero-order reaction? Give an example.

零级反应中速率与反应物浓度无关（rate = k），浓度随时间线性下降，半衰期随初始浓度变化。典型实例是酶被底物饱和时的反应，或某些表面催化（如金属表面的氨分解）受活性位点数量限制。
In zero-order kinetics the rate is independent of reactant concentration (rate = k), so concentration falls linearly and the half-life depends on initial concentration. Examples include enzyme reactions saturated with substrate and surface-catalyzed processes (e.g., NH₃ decomposition on metal) limited by active-site count.

自由基反应为什么常常是"连锁"的？引发剂（如过氧化物）起了什么作用？

Why are radical reactions often "chain" reactions, and what role does an initiator like a peroxide play?

自由基带单电子、极活泼，一旦生成就会夺取别处的电子并生成新的自由基，使反应像链条一样自我延续（链传递）。引发剂（如过氧化二苯甲酰）受热或光照均裂产生初始自由基，启动这条链；没有引发剂，室温下很难自发生成自由基。
Radicals carry a single, highly reactive electron; once formed they snatch electrons from neighbors and create new radicals, letting the reaction perpetuate like a chain (chain propagation). An initiator such as benzoyl peroxide thermally or photochemically homolyzes to produce the first radicals, starting the chain—without it, radicals rarely form spontaneously at room temperature.

为什么一杯溶液能自己反复变红又变蓝？

Why can a solution repeatedly turn red then blue by itself?

这是Belousov-Zhabotinsky反应，溴酸盐氧化有机物过程中中间物浓度周期性振荡，使指示剂颜色来回变化，像一台化学钟。
The Belousov–Zhabotinsky reaction: bromate oxidizes an organic substrate with periodic concentration oscillations, flipping an indicator's color back and forth—a chemical clock.

蓝瓶子实验里溶液为什么会反复变蓝又褪色？

Why does the blue-bottle solution repeatedly turn blue then colorless?

蓝瓶子实验中，葡萄糖（还原剂）与亚甲基蓝（氧化还原指示剂）溶于水并接触空气。静置时空气中的氧把无色的还原态亚甲基蓝氧化成蓝色（氧化态）；同时葡萄糖缓慢把蓝色氧化态还原回无色。如此反复氧化还原循环，溶液便周期性地变蓝又褪色，振荡快慢受温度、氧气和糖浓度影响。
In the blue-bottle experiment, glucose (a reducing agent) and methylene blue (a redox indicator) are dissolved in water exposed to air. At rest, dissolved oxygen oxidizes the colorless reduced methylene blue to its blue oxidized form; meanwhile glucose slowly reduces the blue form back to colorless. This redox cycle repeats, so the solution periodically turns blue and then fades. The oscillation speed depends on temperature, oxygen, and sugar concentration.

反应商 Q 与 K 的比较如何判断反应方向？

How does comparing Q with K predict reaction direction?

Q 与 K 形式相同但用任意时刻浓度/分压。若 Q < K，反应正向进行以增大产物；Q > K 则逆向进行；Q = K 时达平衡。这一判据比勒夏特列更定量且普适。
Q has the same form as K but uses instantaneous concentrations/partial pressures. If Q < K, the reaction proceeds forward to make more product; if Q > K, it goes reverse; Q = K means equilibrium. This criterion is more quantitative and general than Le Chatelier.

合成氨的哈伯法为什么在约400–500°C而不是更高温度下进行？

Why is the Haber–Bosch ammonia process run at about 400–500 °C rather than higher?

合成氨是放热反应，温度太高会让平衡向左移、氨产率下降；但温度低反应又太慢。400–500°C配合铁催化剂，是速率与产率的折中。
Ammonia synthesis is exothermic, so high temperature shifts equilibrium away from NH3; but low temperature is too slow. Operating at 400-500 °C, typically near 450 °C, with an iron catalyst balances rate and yield.

反应速率方程中的反应级数如何确定？

How is the reaction order in a rate law determined?

反应级数不能从化学方程式猜测，必须由实验测定：保持其他反应物浓度不变，改变某一反应物浓度，观察速率变化倍数，从而得出该物质的级数；总级数为各级数之和。
Reaction order cannot be guessed from the balanced equation; it is found experimentally by varying one reactant's concentration while holding others constant and observing the rate change, giving that species' order; the overall order is their sum.

Kc 与 Kp 在什么情况下相等？

When are Kc and Kp equal?

Kp = Kc(RT)^(Δn)，其中 Δn 是气体生成物与反应物化学计量数之差。仅当 Δn = 0（气体摩尔数不变）时 Kp = Kc；否则两者数值随温度变化。
Kp = Kc(RT)^(Δn), where Δn is the change in moles of gas (products minus reactants). Kp equals Kc only when Δn = 0 (no net change in gas moles); otherwise they differ with temperature.

为什么木头、纸张平时不自己着火，非要先用火苗或电火花"点"一下？

Why don't wood or paper ignite by themselves at room temperature and need a flame or spark to start?

燃烧是放热的，但反应必须先跨过"活化能"这道门槛——常温下反应物分子的能量不足以启动链式氧化。火苗或火花提供初始能量把少数分子激发到能反应的状态，反应放出的热再维持连锁反应，于是继续烧下去。
Combustion is exothermic, but the reaction must first clear an "activation energy" barrier—at room temperature reactant molecules lack the energy to start the oxidation chain. A flame or spark supplies that initial energy to excite a few molecules; the heat they release then sustains the chain reaction.

ICE 表在平衡计算中有什么用途？

What is the use of an ICE table in equilibrium calculations?

ICE 表（Initial-Change-Equilibrium）列出各物质初态、变化量与平衡浓度，代入平衡常数表达式求解未知量。它能系统化处理弱酸解离、溶解与气体平衡等问题。
An ICE table (Initial-Change-Equilibrium) lists each species' starting amount, the change, and the equilibrium amount, then substitutes into the K expression to solve unknowns. It systematically handles weak-acid dissociation, solubility, and gas equilibria.

为什么木头烧完剩下的灰，比原来的木头轻那么多？

Why is the ash left after burning wood so much lighter than the original wood?

木头燃烧是碳、氢等元素与空气中的氧结合，生成二氧化碳和水蒸气跑掉，只留下不能燃烧的灰分（钾、钙、硅等的氧化物）。质量并没有消失，只是大部分产物以气体形式离开了，所以灰比木头轻。
Burning wood means carbon, hydrogen, and other elements combine with oxygen from the air to form carbon dioxide and water vapor that escape, leaving only incombustible ash (oxides of potassium, calcium, silicon, etc.). No mass is lost—most products simply leave as gas—so the ash is much lighter.
