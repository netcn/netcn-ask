What is international law?

国际法是国家之间自愿达成的规则体系，规范战争、贸易、人权等领域，主要靠条约和习惯维系，由各国合作执行。
International law is the body of rules agreed among states covering war, trade, human rights, and more, maintained mainly through treaties and custom and enforced by cooperation.

What is the International Court of Justice?

The International Court of Justice (ICJ) is the UN’s principal judicial organ in The Hague, settling legal disputes between states and giving advisory opinions. It differs from the ICC, which tries individuals.

什么是法理学中的自然法与实证法？

What are natural law and legal positivism in jurisprudence?

自然法主张存在超越实定法的、基于理性或道德的普遍法则，恶法可能不具真正法律效力；法律实证主义则认为法的有效性来自其制定程序与渊源，与道德对错相分离。两者的争论围绕“法律与道德是否必然相连”展开。
Natural law holds there are universal principles based on reason or morality above enacted law, and unjust laws may lack true legal validity; legal positivism holds a law's validity comes from its enactment process and sources, separate from moral rightness. Their debate centers on whether law and morality are necessarily linked.

Positivism treats law as commands of the sovereign, separate from morality; natural law holds that valid law must meet universal moral standards.

哈特在 1961 年主张法律是一套由“初级规则”（设定义务）与“次级规则”（规定如何制定、修改和裁判）结合的社会规则体系，并强调法律的“内在观点”。他以此批评奥斯丁的“命令说”过于简单。
In 1961 Hart argued that law is a system of social rules combining “primary rules” (imposing duties) and “secondary rules” (governing how rules are made, changed, and adjudicated), emphasizing the “internal point of view.” He thereby critiqued Austin’s simplistic “command” theory.

什么是程序正义？

What is procedural justice?

程序正义关注裁判过程是否公平，而不只看结果对错。它包括中立的裁判者、双方充分陈述、理由公开等；即便结果不利，公正的程序也更让人信服。
Procedural justice concerns whether the process is fair, not just the outcome. It includes a neutral decision-maker, the chance for both sides to be heard, and public reasons—people accept unfavorable results more when procedures are fair.

什么是普通法系与大陆法系？

What are common law and civil law systems?

普通法系（英美法系）以判例为主要法律渊源，强调法官通过先例发展法律；大陆法系（民法法系）以成文法典为核心，强调立法机关的系统性编纂。两者在司法推理和法律教育上差异显著。不少国家是混合或受两者影响的体系。
The common law system relies mainly on judicial precedent, with judges developing law through case law, while the civil law system centers on codified statutes enacted by legislatures. They differ markedly in legal reasoning and legal education. Many countries are hybrid or influenced by both.

Presumption of innocence means a person is considered innocent until proven guilty in court. The burden of proof lies with the prosecution, preventing arbitrary conviction.

什么是司法独立？

What is judicial independence?

司法独立指法院和法官在审理案件时不受立法、行政或其他外部力量不当干预，仅依据法律与事实裁判。它是实现公正审判和权力制衡的重要保障。制度上常通过法官任期、薪酬保障和管辖安排来维护独立。
Judicial independence means courts and judges decide cases free from improper interference by the legislature, executive, or other outside forces, basing rulings only on law and facts. It is vital for fair trials and checks and balances. It is typically protected through tenure, salary safeguards, and jurisdictional design.

The rule of law means everyone and every institution, including government, is bound by open, fair laws rather than arbitrary decisions by those in power. It protects fairness and liberty.

What is the main difference between the “common law” and “civil law” legal families?

Common law (exemplified by the UK and US) centers on case law and judge-made precedent, emphasizing stare decisis; civil law (exemplified by France and Germany) is built on codified statutes enacted by legislatures. They differ markedly in sources of law and reasoning.

什么是证据规则中的举证责任？

What is the burden of proof?

举证责任指由哪一方负责提供证据以证明其主张的规则。在刑事案件中通常由控方承担，且需达到“排除合理怀疑”的高标准；民事案件多为“谁主张、谁举证”。合理分配举证责任对公正裁判至关重要。
The burden of proof determines which party must produce evidence to support its claim. In criminal cases it usually rests on the prosecution and must meet a high 'beyond reasonable doubt' standard; in civil cases it is often 'he who asserts must prove.' Its fair allocation is crucial to just adjudication.

什么是正当程序？

What is due process?

正当程序指政府剥夺个人生命、自由或财产前，必须遵循公平、预先设定的法律程序，如告知、听证与上诉。它源于英国普通法，被多国宪法与《世界人权宣言》所确认。
Due process means the state must follow fair, established legal procedure—notice, hearing, appeal—before depriving anyone of life, liberty, or property. Rooted in English common law, it is affirmed in many constitutions and the Universal Declaration of Human Rights.

What is 'plea bargaining,' and why do many criminal cases settle before trial?

辩诉交易指被告承认较轻指控或更少罪名，以换取检察官较轻的刑罚建议，从而免去漫长审判。美国司法部的数据显示，绝大多数联邦刑事案件通过这种方式了结，既节省司法资源，也引发「透明度」与公平的争议。
Plea bargaining lets a defendant plead to a lesser or fewer charges in exchange for a lighter recommended sentence, avoiding a long trial. U.S. Department of Justice data show most federal criminal cases end this way—saving resources yet raising debates over transparency and fairness.

Contract law governs voluntary agreements between parties—offer, acceptance, consideration, performance—and remedies for breach such as damages. It is a foundational pillar of market economies.

刑法里的「犯意」（mens rea）与「犯罪行为」（actus reus）分别指什么？

In criminal law, what do 'mens rea' and 'actus reus' each refer to?

「犯意」指实施犯罪时的「明知或故意」等主观心理状态，「犯罪行为」指客观发生的不法举动或疏忽。多数犯罪须两者同时具备——既有坏心思、又做了坏事，才构成完整罪行。
'Mens rea' is the guilty mind—the intentional or knowing mental state when committing a crime—while 'actus reus' is the guilty act, the outward unlawful conduct or omission. Most crimes require both: a wrongful mind joined to a wrongful act.

What is the difference between civil law and criminal law?

民法调整平等主体之间的财产和人身关系，如合同与婚姻；刑法规定哪些行为构成犯罪并施加刑罚，以维护社会秩序。民事案件通常由受害方起诉，刑事案件多由国家机关追诉。两者的目的、程序和举证责任都有明显不同。
Civil law governs property and personal relations among equal parties, such as contracts and marriage; criminal law defines acts as crimes and imposes punishment to protect public order. Civil cases are usually brought by the injured party, while criminal cases are prosecuted by the state. Their purposes, procedures, and burdens of proof differ markedly.

Why does 'stare decisis' (following precedent) make rulings more stable in common law?

「遵循先例」要求法院在类似案件里沿用上级或本院此前作出的判决，保证「同样情形同样对待」。它让法律可预期、减少任意裁量，却也允许在旧判例明显错误时「推翻」先例。
'Stare decisis' requires courts to follow earlier decisions by higher or same courts in similar cases, ensuring 'like cases are treated alike.' It makes law predictable and curbs arbitrary discretion, while still allowing precedent to be overruled when clearly wrong.
