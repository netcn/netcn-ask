吉登斯的结构化理论如何试图超越“结构”与“能动性”的二元对立？他以“时空延伸”解释现代制度时有何核心主张？

How did Giddens's structuration theory try to transcend the structure/agency dualism, and what is his core claim about 'time-space distanciation' in modern institutions?

吉登斯认为结构既是行动的中介也是其结果，规则与资源在行动中“二重性”地循环再生产。他借“时空延伸”说明现代制度（如民族国家、货币）能把跨越广阔时空的互动脱域化，重塑信任与风险。
Giddens argued that structures are both medium and outcome of action, recursively reproduced through rules and resources (the duality of structure). His 'time-space distanciation' explains how modern institutions like the nation-state and money 'disembed' interactions across vast distances, reshaping trust and risk.

滕尼斯在《共同体与社会》中以“共同体”与“社会”这对概念刻画了何种社会变迁？它如何影响了后来的社会类型学？

What social transformation did Tönnies capture with the Gemeinschaft/Gesellschaft distinction in Community and Society, and how did it influence later typologies?

滕尼斯区分了基于血缘、邻里与友谊的“共同体”（有机、情感纽带）与基于契约、算计的“社会”（机械、理性关联）。这一从传统到现代的类型学为涂尔干、韦伯的现代性论述提供了概念工具。
Tönnies contrasted Gemeinschaft (organic bonds of kinship, neighborhood, friendship) with Gesellschaft (contractual, calculating association). His traditional-to-modern typology supplied conceptual tools for Durkheim and Weber's accounts of modernity.

西美尔在《大都市与精神生活》中指出都市居民为何发展出“冷漠”与“理智至上”的应对机制？这一论断与后来的城市社会学有何关联？

Why did Simmel argue that metropolitan residents develop 'blasé' detachment and an intellect-oriented stance in The Metropolis and Mental Life, and how did this shape later urban sociology?

西美尔认为城市密集的感官刺激与金钱交易的算计性，迫使个体用理智而非情感来过滤过量信息，从而表现为表面冷漠。这一“刺激屏障”概念成为芝加哥学派与城市互动理论的先声。
Simmel held that the overload of metropolitan stimuli and the calculative logic of money lead individuals to shield themselves with intellect rather than emotion, appearing detached. His notion of a 'protective organ' prefigured Chicago-school and interactionist urban theory.

福柯在《规训与惩罚》中用“全景敞视”说明现代权力如何运作？这种权力观与传统的镇压式权力有何不同？

How does Foucault use the 'panopticon' in Discipline and Punish to describe modern power, and how does this differ from repressive models of power?

福柯描述从公开酷刑转向以监视、规范化裁决与检查实现的“规训”权力，全景敞视监狱使被监视者自我规训。权力被视为生产性、弥散于日常机制，而非仅由国家自上而下压制。
Foucault traces a shift from spectacular torture to 'disciplinary' power via surveillance, normalizing judgment, and examination; the panopticon makes the watched internalize control. Power is productive and capillary, not merely top-down repression.

哈贝马斯的“交往行动”理论为何强调“生活世界”与“系统”的殖民化？他提出的理想言谈情境有何规范含义？

Why did Habermas emphasize the 'colonization of the lifeworld' by 'systems', and what normative weight does his 'ideal speech situation' carry?

哈贝马斯担心金钱与行政权力（系统）侵入原本靠沟通共识维系的生活世界，导致意义与团结的流失。理想言谈情境要求无强制、平等、真诚的论证，为民主商议提供规范性基准。
Habermas feared that money and administrative power (systems) invade the lifeworld sustained by communicative consensus, eroding meaning and solidarity. The ideal speech situation—coercion-free, equal, sincere argument—sets a normative standard for democratic deliberation.

涂尔干在《自杀论》中如何用“社会整合”与“道德规范”解释自杀率的差异？他采用的实证方法对社会学的学科化有何意义？

How did Durkheim use 'social integration' and 'moral regulation' to explain variations in suicide rates in Suicide, and why was his method pivotal for the discipline?

涂尔干将自杀分为利己型、利他型、失范型与宿命型，主张社会纽带强弱与规范清晰度决定个体脆弱性。他用官方统计控制变量来论证“社会事实”独立于个人心理，奠定了用经验数据检验社会因果的范式。
Durkheim classified egoistic, altruistic, anomic, and fatalistic suicide, arguing that the strength of social ties and clarity of norms shape individual vulnerability. By analyzing official statistics with controls, he demonstrated that 'social facts' exist independently of psychology, founding empirical sociology.

What is the core mechanism behind Max Weber's 'Protestant ethic' thesis in The Protestant Ethic and the Spirit of Capitalism, and why does it remain a flashpoint in methodological debates?

韦伯认为加尔文宗的预定论催生了“天职”观念与克制消费、勤勉积累的行为取向，为现代理性资本主义提供了精神动力。该命题以“理想类型”而非因果定律展开，因此常被争论其可证伪性与欧洲中心主义倾向。

什么是罗萨的“社会加速”理论？为什么省时技术越多，现代人反而越缺时间？

What is Hartmut Rosa’s theory of “social acceleration,” and why do time-saving technologies leave people feeling more pressed for time?

罗萨将现代性诊断为三种加速的自我强化循环：技术加速缩短运输、通信与生产时间；社会变迁加速令经验与预期迅速失效；生活步调加速造成普遍时间匮乏。三者互为因果——省下的时间被更多任务填满，变迁加快迫使人人提速以免落伍。当加速不再服务目的而自我推进，社会进入动态稳定：人们越跑越快却越发没时间，形成新的时间异化。
Rosa diagnoses modernity as a self-reinforcing cycle of three accelerations: technological acceleration shortens transport, communication, and production times; accelerating social change makes experience and expectations rapidly obsolete; and an accelerated pace of life yields pervasive time scarcity. The three cause one another—time saved is refilled with more tasks, while faster change compels everyone to speed up just to stay in place. When acceleration detaches from goals and propels itself, society enters dynamic stabilization: people run ever faster yet have ever less time, a new temporal alienation.

米德的符号互动论如何解释“自我”的形成？他区分的“主我”与“客我”分别对应什么？

How does Mead's symbolic interactionism explain the formation of the 'self', and what do 'I' and 'Me' distinguish?

米德主张自我通过扮演他人角色、内化社会态度而在社会互动中生成。'客我'是被内化的社会期望，'主我'是对这些期望的自发创造性回应，二者张力推动行为。
Mead held that the self emerges through taking the role of the other and internalizing social attitudes. The 'Me' is the internalized social expectation; the 'I' is the spontaneous, creative response to it, and their tension drives conduct.

戈夫曼的拟剧理论把日常互动比作舞台表演，他用“前台/后台”与“印象管理”说明了什么？

What does Goffman's dramaturgical theory reveal through the 'front stage/back stage' and 'impression management'?

戈夫曼认为人在互动中像演员一样管理他人对自己的印象，前台是按规范展演的公开区域，后台是放松与准备的真实区域。这一视角把自我视为情境性产物，挑战了固定人格观。
Goffman argued people manage the impressions others form of them; the front stage is the normative public performance, the back stage the relaxed preparation area. This frames the self as situationally produced rather than fixed.

贝克在《风险社会》中如何论证工业化在制造财富的同时也制造出系统性、不可归责的风险？

In Risk Society, how did Beck argue that industrialization produces systemic, unassignable risks alongside wealth?

贝克认为现代化的副作用（核、化学、气候风险）超越阶级边界且难以追责，传统的因果与赔偿框架失效。风险社会要求以“有组织的不负责任”视角重构政治与科学。
Beck argued modern hazards (nuclear, chemical, climate) cross class lines and resist attribution, defeating traditional causality and compensation. The risk society demands rethinking politics and science via 'organized irresponsibility'.

工业化如何改变普通人体验时间的方式？钟表时间为何会取代前工业社会的任务节奏？

How did industrialization change the way ordinary people experience time, and why did clock time displace task-oriented rhythms?

汤普森考察了工业资本主义对时间纪律的重塑：前工业社会依循任务节奏劳作，忙闲交替，昼夜与季节支配安排；工厂制度要求劳动同步化，钟表与汽笛便把一天切分为可计量、可出售的单位。“时间就是金钱”的观念经学校、教会与车间的纪律反复灌输，迟到旷工遂成为道德问题。今日的守时习惯与日程文化，正是这场转型留下的遗产。
E. P. Thompson traced how industrial capitalism rebuilt time discipline. Pre-industrial labor followed the rhythm of tasks, alternating intense effort with idleness under the sway of daylight and seasons; factories demanded synchronized work, and clocks and whistles cut the day into measurable, sellable units. Schooling, churches, and shop-floor discipline drilled in the idea that time is money until lateness became a moral failing; modern punctuality and scheduling culture descend from that transformation.
