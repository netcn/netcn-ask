为什么随机对照实验（RCT）被视为政策评估的“金标准”？它有哪些局限？

Why are randomized controlled trials (RCTs) considered the “gold standard” of policy evaluation, and what are their limits?

RCT通过随机分配“接受政策”与“对照组”，使两组在平均意义上除政策外无系统差异，从而干净识别因果效应，避免选择偏差。班纳吉、迪弗洛与克雷默因把RCT大规模引入扶贫评估而获诺贝尔经济学奖。但RCT有局限：外部效度问题（此地结果未必适用于他处）、样本规模与时间跨度有限、伦理约束（不能随机拒绝帮助），以及把复杂政策简化为“小项目”而忽略系统改革。因此近年强调对RCT结果做机制分析与外部效度检验，并与其他证据（自然实验、结构模型）互补。
RCTs randomly assign treatment and control groups so that, on average, groups differ only by the policy, cleanly identifying causal effects and avoiding selection bias. Banerjee, Duflo, and Kremer won the Nobel Prize partly for scaling RCTs into poverty-alleviation evaluation. But RCTs have limits: external validity (results may not travel), limited sample sizes and time horizons, ethical constraints (can't randomly deny help), and a tendency to compress complex reforms into small projects. Recent practice therefore adds mechanism analysis and external-validity checks, complementing RCTs with natural experiments and structural models.

What is an exchange rate?

An exchange rate is the price of one currency in terms of another, under fixed or floating exchange rate regimes. It affects import-export prices, capital flows, and inflation. The rate is shaped by market supply and demand, interest-rate differentials, and expectations, among others.

赫伯特·西蒙的「有限理性」如何修正了「经济人」假设？

How does Herbert Simon's 'bounded rationality' revise the 'economic man' assumption?

西蒙在 1957 年指出，人受信息、时间与认知能力限制，无法算出「最优」解，只能追求「满意」的选择。有限理性推翻了完全理性、无限计算力的古典假设，成为行为经济学与组织决策的理论基石。
Simon argued in 1957 that people are constrained by information, time, and cognitive limits, so they cannot compute the optimal choice and instead 'satisfice.' Bounded rationality overthrew the classical assumption of fully rational, infinitely calculating agents and became a cornerstone of behavioral economics and organizational decision-making.

In economics, what is a 'liquidity trap,' and why do interest-rate cuts stop working in it?

流动性陷阱指利率已降到近乎零、人们仍宁愿持有现金也不愿投资消费，此时央行再「放水」也难以刺激经济。凯恩斯在 1936 年提出这一概念，后来被用于解释日本「失去的十年」与 2008 年后的低利率困境。
A liquidity trap occurs when rates are near zero yet people still hoard cash instead of spending or investing, so the central bank's easing no longer stimulates the economy. Keynes introduced it in 1936; it later explained Japan's 'lost decade' and post-2008 low-rate stagnation.

为什么针对青年失业的“培训项目”效果常不如预期？哪些政策组合被证明更有效？

Why do training programs for youth unemployment often underperform, and which policy mixes have proven more effective?

随机评估发现，单纯的短期课堂式培训对青年就业效果微弱，原因在于青年失业多为“需求侧”问题（岗位不足、经验门槛）与匹配问题，而非单纯技能供给不足；且存在“替代效应”（受训者挤掉未受训者的岗位）与“锁定效应”（参与者减少求职努力）。效果较好的干预包括：工资补贴（直接降低企业雇佣青年的成本）、在职培训与学徒制（雇主参与、学用结合）、信息与求职辅导，以及针对NEET风险群体的早期密集干预。政策研究者据此主张用需求侧加工作现场培训的组合替代单纯的课堂供给。
Randomized evaluations find that short classroom-based training has weak effects on youth employment, because youth joblessness is largely a demand-side (too few jobs, experience thresholds) and matching problem rather than a pure skills-supply one; substitution effects (trainees displacing untrained workers) and lock-in effects (participants searching less) also blunt results. Interventions that fare better include wage subsidies (directly lowering employer costs of hiring youth), on-the-job training and apprenticeships (employer involvement, learn-by-doing), information and job-search counseling, and early intensive support for those at NEET risk. The policy lesson is to replace pure classroom supply with demand-side plus workplace-based packages.

医疗保险市场为何天然存在“逆向选择”与“道德风险”？保险设计如何回应？

Why do health-insurance markets inherently suffer from adverse selection and moral hazard, and how do designs respond?

逆向选择发生在签约前：投保人更了解自身健康，高危者更愿投保，若按平均风险定价，低危者感到吃亏而退出，保费螺旋上升，市场可能萎缩甚至崩溃（“死亡螺旋”）。道德风险发生在签约后：有了保险，患者倾向多用医疗、医生倾向多开检查，推高费用。回应手段包括强制参保（扩大风险池）、社区费率（禁止按个体风险定价）、共付与免赔额（让患者承担部分成本以抑制过度消费），但共付过高又伤害穷人并减少必要诊疗，凸显公平与效率的张力。
Adverse selection occurs before purchase: applicants know their health better, the high-risk are more eager to enroll, and if priced at the average risk, the low-risk drop out, premiums spiral, and the market can shrink or collapse—a “death spiral.” Moral hazard occurs after purchase: with insurance, patients use more care and doctors order more tests, inflating costs. Responses include mandatory enrollment (broadening the risk pool), community rating (banning individual risk pricing), and copays/deductibles (sharing costs to curb overuse)—but high copays hurt the poor and reduce necessary care, highlighting the fairness-efficiency tension.

What does the Gini coefficient measure, and what do 0 and 1 represent?

The Gini coefficient measures the inequality of income distribution in a population, introduced by Italian statistician Corrado Gini in 1912. A value of 0 means perfect equality (everyone has the same income) and 1 means perfect inequality (one person has all the income).

失业保险是否会使失业者更“挑剔”、延长失业期？如何设计才能兼顾保障与激励？

Does unemployment insurance make the unemployed pickier and prolong joblessness? How should it be designed to balance protection and incentives?

理论上，失业保险降低了求职的机会成本，会提高保留工资、延长搜索时间，这是道德风险；但搜索经济学也指出，更长时间搜索能匹配到更合适的岗位，保障收入使人不必接受糟糕的工作。实证（如利用美国各州待遇差异的研究）显示给付越长或越慷慨，失业期越长，但幅度适中；对“给付到期前求职率骤升”的研究证实了激励效应。设计上常用递减给付、严格求职义务、资格审查与在职培训来平衡；“工作共享”政策则通过缩短工时而非裁员应对冲击。
In theory, UI lowers the opportunity cost of job search, raising reservation wages and prolonging search—moral hazard; but search theory notes longer search can yield better matches, and income support keeps people from taking bad jobs. Evidence (e.g., studies exploiting state-to-state differences in the US) shows longer or more generous benefits extend unemployment duration, but moderately; research showing job-finding jumps as benefits near expiration confirms the incentive channel. Designs commonly balance protection and incentives via declining benefits, strict job-search requirements, eligibility checks, and training; “work-sharing” policies respond to shocks by reducing hours rather than layoffs.

卡德与克鲁格关于最低工资的著名研究为什么颠覆了教科书预期？后续研究如何回应？

Why did Card and Krueger's famous minimum-wage study upend conventional predictions, and how did later research respond?

1992年两人利用新泽西州上调最低工资、宾夕法尼亚州不变的“自然实验”，对快餐店做双重差分比较，发现就业并未下降，与标准竞争模型预测相悖，他们归因于企业买方垄断力。该研究引发方法革命，但后续验证并存：有的重复研究支持其发现，批评者则认为其数据采集窗口过短、样本噪声大；近年美国研究（如Cengiz等）发现适度提高最低工资对整体就业影响很小，而大幅度上调会显著减少就业。共识趋于“存在阈值与条件”，说明自然实验方法严谨但结论需多重验证。
In 1992, Card and Krueger used a natural experiment—New Jersey raising its minimum wage while neighboring Pennsylvania did not—and a difference-in-differences comparison of fast-food restaurants found no employment decline, contradicting the standard competitive model; they attributed this to monopsony. The study sparked a methodological revolution, but replications split: some confirmed the findings; critics argued the sampling window was short and noisy. Recent US research (e.g., Cengiz et al.) finds modest increases have little aggregate employment effect while large increases reduce employment substantially. The consensus is now conditional and threshold-based, showing that natural experiments are rigorous but conclusions need multiple verification.

GDP is the total market value of all final goods and services produced within a country or region over a period, and is a core measure of economic size. It can be calculated by the production, income, or expenditure approach. While GDP growth is used as a gauge of economic activity, it does not capture income distribution or environmental quality.

Introduced by Max Lorenz in 1905, the Lorenz curve plots cumulative population share against cumulative income share; the more it bows from the diagonal, the more unequal the distribution. The Gini coefficient is the area between that curve and the diagonal as a share of the whole triangle, where 0 means perfect equality and 1 means total concentration.

摩擦性失业与结构性失业有何区别？

How do frictional and structural unemployment differ?

摩擦性失业是工人转换工作或初次进入劳动力市场时的短期过渡性失业，反映信息搜索与匹配需要时间，属于正常现象。结构性失业则源于技能、行业或地理位置与岗位需求的长期错配，例如自动化和产业转移使部分工人技能过时。前者常可通过改进信息匹配缓解，后者需要再培训与劳动力再配置。
Frictional unemployment is short-term unemployment from job transitions and labor-market entry, reflecting the time needed to search and match—a normal feature of a dynamic economy. Structural unemployment arises from long-term mismatches between workers' skills, industries, or locations and available jobs, such as when automation or industrial shifts make skills obsolete. The former can be eased by better information and matching, the latter requires retraining and reallocation.
