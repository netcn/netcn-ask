奥尔森在《集体行动的逻辑》中为何认为大群体难以自发提供公共物品？他提出的“选择性激励”如何破解搭便车？

Why did Olson argue in The Logic of Collective Action that large groups struggle to provide public goods spontaneously, and how do 'selective incentives' solve free-riding?

奥尔森指出个人从公共物品中分得的收益相对于行动成本微不足道，理性个体倾向搭便车，导致大集团失灵。他主张用私人的奖励或惩罚（选择性激励）来驱动参与。
Olson noted that an individual's share of a public good is negligible versus the cost of acting, so rational members free-ride, causing large-group failure. He proposed private rewards or punishments—selective incentives—to motivate participation.

奥斯特罗姆为何反驳“公地悲剧”必然导致私有化或国家管制？她提出的自主治理原则有哪些？

Why did Ostrom reject that 'the tragedy of the commons' inevitably requires privatization or state control, and what principles of self-governance did she propose?

奥斯特罗姆通过多地案例证明社区能在明确边界、本地规则、监督与渐进制裁下长期可持续管理公共资源。她归纳出八项设计原则，说明去中心化治理同样有效。
Ostrom showed through field cases that communities sustain common-pool resources via clear boundaries, local rules, monitoring, and graduated sanctions. Her eight design principles demonstrate that decentralized governance can work.

帕特南在《独自打保龄》中用什么证据说明美国社会资本在下降？他区分的“黏合型”与“桥接型”社会资本有何不同？

What evidence did Putnam use in Bowling Alone to argue American social capital declined, and how do 'bonding' and 'bridging' social capital differ?

帕特南以社团参与、信任与互助频率的下降（如保龄球联赛式微）说明公民参与萎缩。黏合型资本强化群体内纽带，桥接型资本连接不同群体，后者更利于跨群体合作。
Putnam cited falling membership, trust, and reciprocity (e.g., declining bowling leagues) as evidence of eroding civic engagement. Bonding capital tightens in-group ties; bridging capital links diverse groups and aids cross-group cooperation.

阿克塞尔罗德的“重复囚徒困境”计算机竞赛为何证明“一报还一报”是稳健的合作策略？它对理解社会合作有何启示？

Why did Axelrod's repeated prisoner's dilemma tournament show 'tit for tat' as a robust cooperation strategy, and what does it imply for social cooperation?

阿克塞尔罗德发现善良、报复、宽容且清晰的“一报还一报”在重复博弈中最具适应性，因为它既避免被剥削又鼓励对方合作。该研究表明合作可由互惠规则自发涌现，无需中央权威。
Axelrod found that 'tit for tat'—nice, retaliating, forgiving, and clear—was most adaptive in iterated play, avoiding exploitation while inviting cooperation. It shows cooperation can emerge from reciprocity without central authority.
