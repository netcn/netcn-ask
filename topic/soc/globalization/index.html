沃勒斯坦的“世界体系”理论如何用核心—半边缘—边缘的分工解释长期不平等？

How does Wallerstein's 'world-systems' theory explain long-run inequality via a core-semiperiphery-periphery division of labor?

沃勒斯坦认为资本主义世界经济由不平等交换维系：核心抽取边缘的剩余，半边缘起缓冲与中介作用。国家地位由其在分工中的功能位置决定，而非单纯文化或制度差异。
Wallerstein held that the capitalist world-economy is sustained by unequal exchange: the core extracts surplus from the periphery, with the semiperiphery buffering. A state's position in the division of labor, not mere culture, fixes its status.

卡斯特在《网络社会的崛起》中为何把权力与财富重组为围绕“流动空间”的网络逻辑？

In The Rise of the Network Society, why did Castells argue power and wealth are reorganized around the 'space of flows' as network logic?

卡斯特认为信息技术使资本、信息与精英得以脱离地方空间，沿全球网络即时流动，形成流动空间；被排除在外的群体则困于地方空间。这重塑了不平等与政治动员的形态。
Castells argued information technology lets capital, information, and elites detach from locale, flowing instantly along global networks—the space of flows—while excluded groups remain in the space of places. This reshapes inequality and political mobilization.

阿帕杜莱用“scape（景观）”概念如何描述全球化中族群、媒体、金融等流动的去中心化？

How did Appadurai's 'scapes' concept depict the decentralized flows of ethnos, media, and finance in globalization?

阿帕杜莱提出族群景观、媒体景观、技术景观、金融景观与意识形态景观五种可拼装、非同步的流动，强调全球化是断裂而非单一同质化。这解释了本土抵抗与混杂身份的生成。
Appadurai proposed five disjunctured flows—ethnoscapes, mediascapes, technoscapes, financescapes, ideoscapes—emphasizing globalization as fractured, not homogenized. This explains local resistance and hybrid identities.
