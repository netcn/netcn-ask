宗教市场论如何解释美国与西欧在宗教参与上的巨大反差?

How does religious economies theory explain the sharp contrast in religious participation between the United States and Western Europe?

斯塔克与芬凯把宗教组织视为竞争市场中的供给方:在管制少、教派林立的环境里,各团体必须提升讲道质量、社群纽带与服务才能争取信众,总体参与率随之走高;国教垄断则滋生惰怠供给,参与趋于低迷。两人据此主张美国的高度虔诚与欧洲的冷清源于制度环境差异,而非现代性本身侵蚀信仰。批评者反驳称,北欧同样允许竞争却依旧世俗,可见需求侧因素不可省略。
Stark and Finke treat religious bodies as suppliers in a competitive market: where regulation is light and denominations abound, groups must improve preaching, community bonds, and services to attract adherents, lifting aggregate participation; monopoly churches breed complacent supply and low attendance. On this view American devoutness and European apathy reflect institutional environments rather than modernity eroding belief. Critics counter that Scandinavia allows competition yet stays secular, so demand-side factors cannot be omitted.

贝拉如何在总统就职演说中辨认出一套美国公民宗教?它同教派信仰和个人虔诚的分界在哪里?

How did Bellah discern an American civil religion within presidential inaugurals, and where does its boundary with denominational faith and personal piety run?

贝拉细读肯尼迪就职演说等公共文本,发现上帝、天命、牺牲与末世审判等圣经意象脱离具体教派反复出现,为国家的政治抉择提供超越性参照。这套符号不属于任何教会的信纲,也不同于私人内心的敬虔,而是在典礼与演说构成的公共舞台上运转,授予政治权威神圣合法性。文章刊出后引发长久论战,焦点包括这种宗教是否助长民族自义以及政教分离的边界所在。
Reading Kennedy's inaugural alongside other public texts, Bellah found biblical imagery of God, destiny, sacrifice, and final judgment recurring apart from any denomination, supplying transcendental reference points for political choice. These symbols belong to no church creed and differ from private devotion, operating instead on the civic stage of ceremony and oratory, where they confer sacred legitimacy on authority. The essay ignited lasting debate over whether such religion feeds national self-righteousness and where church-state separation should run.

#公民宗教 #公共仪式

贝拉笔下的美国公民宗教指什么?它与各教派的教会宗教有哪些实质差别?

What does Bellah designate as America's civil religion, and in what substantive ways does it differ from denominational church religion?

贝拉识别出一套以建国文献为经典、以独立与内战等事件为神圣叙事的公共信仰体系。它不从属任何宗派,而是在就职典礼、阵亡将士纪念等国族仪式中表述共同体的集体自我理解。二者差别在于:公民宗教的成员资格就是公民身份,其殿堂是公共领域而非礼拜堂。
Bellah discerned a public creed whose canon consists of founding documents and whose sacred narrative moves from independence through civil war. Attached to no denomination, it voices the community's collective self-understanding during inaugurations, war memorials, and similar national rites. The contrast is sharp: membership in civil religion coincides with citizenship, and its sanctuary is the public sphere rather than the chapel.

科学越来越发达,宗教为什么没有像十九世纪学者预言的那样消失?

Science keeps advancing — why hasnt religion vanished as nineteenth-century thinkers predicted?

世俗化理论曾断言现代化必然稀释宗教,但二十世纪末的数据打了脸:全球信教人口比例不降反升,美国保持高度虔信,而欧洲却显著世俗化。诺里斯与英格哈特的解释是存在性安全假说:宗教满足的是不确定与匮乏中的意义与保障需求,凡福利国家健全、贫富差距小、人身安全高的社会,宗教热情自然降温;反之在动荡与生存焦虑浓重的社会,无论科技多进步,宗教依旧繁荣。宗教因此不是被科学打败的过时品,而是随生存条件涨落的深层需求,其形态还在不断翻新。
Secularization theory predicted modernity would dilute religion, but late-twentieth-century data embarrassed it: global religiosity rose rather than fell, America stayed devout while Europe secularized sharply. Norris and Ingleharts existential security thesis explains this: religion supplies meaning and reassurance against uncertainty and want, so societies with strong welfare states, small gaps and personal safety cool off naturally, whereas wherever survival anxiety runs high, faith thrives regardless of technology. Religion is thus not an obsolete artifact defeated by science but a deep demand rising and falling with existential conditions — and it keeps reinventing its forms.
