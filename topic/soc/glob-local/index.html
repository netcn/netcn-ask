为什么“支持本地”的消费和全球贸易可以同时存在？

Why can 'buy local' sentiment and global trade coexist?

消费者拥有多层身份：为新鲜、认同与社区支持而买本地，又因品种、反季与低价而依赖全球贸易。二者服务于不同需求与尺度，因此互补并存，而非互相排斥。
Consumers hold layered identities: they may buy local for freshness, identity and community support while relying on global trade for variety, off-season goods and lower prices. The two serve different needs and scales, so they coexist as complements rather than opposites.

为什么“本地品牌”常常借全球潮流来包装自己？

Why do local brands often wrap themselves in global trends?

本地品牌借国际流行元素显得“现代”和“高级”，既迎合年轻人认同，又保留本地口味与渠道。全球化与在地化因此互相借用。
Local brands borrow international trends to seem modern and premium, pleasing youth identity while keeping local taste and channels. Globalization and localization thus borrow from each other.

为什么一个地方发生的灾害会影响全球供应链？

Why can a disaster in one place hit global supply chains?

现代生产把零件分散到多地：一部手机可能来自十几个国家。某地工厂停工或港口堵塞，下游组装就会缺料，价格与交付随之波动。
Modern production spreads parts across many places: a phone may draw from a dozen countries. A shutdown or port jam upstream means missing inputs downstream, swinging prices and delivery.

为什么本地的小吃会发展成全球连锁？

Why do local snacks grow into global chains?

当一种味道被标准化、能被快速复制，它就从地方习惯变成可买卖的“品牌”。本地口味借资本与物流走向世界，却也常被简化以迎合多数人口味。
Once a taste is standardized and quickly copied, it becomes a sellable 'brand'. Local flavor rides capital and logistics worldwide, yet is often simplified for the average palate.

为什么我们能在超市买到很远地方的水果？

Why can we buy fruit from far away at the supermarket?

因为冷藏船和飞机把水果快速运到各地，贸易规则让跨境买卖更方便。你冬天吃到的芒果，可能来自几千公里外的果园。
Reefer ships and planes move fruit fast, and trade rules ease cross-border buying. The mango you eat in winter may come from an orchard thousands of kilometers away.
