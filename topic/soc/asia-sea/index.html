种水稻的耕作方式如何影响了东南亚的社群？

How did rice farming shape communities in Southeast Asia?

水稻要集体修渠、育秧、抢收，催生紧密协作的村社与“互助换工”。水的共享也带来共同管理，使社群强调合作而非个人单干。
Rice needs collective canals, seedlings and rushed harvests, spawning tight village cooperation and labor exchange. Shared water meant shared management, so communities prized cooperation over lone effort.

什么是“东盟”式的社会合作？

What is 'ASEAN-style' social cooperation?

东盟强调协商一致、不干涉内政与渐进合作，各国先在经贸与反恐等低敏感领域协作，再慢慢深化。它重面子与默契，区别于西式投票多数决。
ASEAN stresses consensus, non-interference and step-by-step cooperation, starting with low-sensitive areas like trade and counter-terrorism before deepening. It values face and tacit agreement over Western majority voting.

为什么东南亚很多地方的人每天吃米饭？

Why do many Southeast Asians eat rice every day?

因为那里天气热、雨水多，特别适合种水稻；米饭便宜、顶饱又能配很多菜，久而久之成了每天的主食。
The hot, rainy climate suits rice; it is cheap, filling and pairs with many dishes, so it became the daily staple.

为什么热带地区多见露天集市，而温带多见超市？

Why are open-air markets common in the tropics but supermarkets in temperate zones?

热带生鲜易腐、小农多，集市便于当天卖鲜货、灵活议价；温带冷链与规模化零售更发达，超市效率高。气候、农业规模共同塑造了购买习惯。
In the tropics produce spoils fast and small farms abound, so markets suit same-day fresh sales and bargaining; temperate zones have more cold chains and scale retail, making supermarkets efficient. Climate and farm size shape habits.
