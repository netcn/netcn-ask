公共品供给中的“林达尔均衡”指什么？

What is the "Lindahl equilibrium" in public-goods provision?

它描述一种理想状态：每个消费者按其从该公共品中获得的边际收益来分担成本，使总量恰好满足集体偏好；难点在于个人往往隐瞒真实偏好以少付费。
It describes an ideal where each consumer pays according to their marginal benefit from the public good, matching supply to collective preference; the difficulty is that people tend to understate their true willingness to pay.

为什么国防通常被视为“纯公共品”？

Why is national defense usually treated as a "pure" public good?

国防一旦提供，难以排除任何公民受保护（非排他），且一人受保护不会减少他人受保护（非竞争），因此私人市场很难靠收费来供给。
Once provided, defense is hard to deny to any citizen (non-excludable) and one person's protection doesn't reduce another's (non-rival), so private markets struggle to supply it by charging fees.

什么是“公共品”？

What are "public goods"?

公共品是大家都能用、且一个人用了不会减少别人使用的东西，比如路灯和天气预报。
Public goods are things everyone can use and one person's use doesn't reduce another's, such as streetlights and weather forecasts.
