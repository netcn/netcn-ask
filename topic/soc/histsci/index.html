How did the Enlightenment shape modern politics?

李约瑟难题探讨什么？

What does the Needham Question explore?

它追问为何近代科学革命未在中国发生，尽管古代中国科技长期领先，涉及制度、经济与社会结构等因素。
It asks why the Scientific Revolution did not arise in China despite its earlier technological lead, implicating institutions, economy, and social structure.

Where did the Industrial Revolution begin and why does it matter?

18世纪晚期始于英国，以蒸汽机与工厂制度取代手工生产，引发了持续的经济增长与城市化。
Beginning in late-18th-century Britain, steam power and factories replaced hand production, triggering sustained growth and urbanization.

科学革命发生在何时、以什么为特征？

When did the Scientific Revolution occur and what characterized it?

约16至17世纪，以哥白尼、伽利略、牛顿等为代表，强调观察、数学与实验，取代了亚里士多德式的自然哲学。
Roughly the 16th–17th centuries, led by Copernicus, Galileo, and Newton, it emphasized observation, mathematics, and experiment over Aristotelian philosophy.

In the 15th–17th centuries European powers explored new sea routes, linking continents and launching global exchange of colonies, species, and trade.
