“低生育率陷阱”假说是什么？

What is the "low-fertility trap" hypothesis?

该假说认为一旦生育率跌破约1.5并持续，低生育的规范、经济约束与人口结构会相互强化，使回升越来越难，需要强力且及早的政策干预才可能逆转。
The hypothesis holds that once fertility falls below about 1.5 and stays there, low-fertility norms, economic constraints, and demographics reinforce each other, making recovery harder—needing strong, early policy action to reverse.

生育率为何会下降？

Why does fertility decline?

常见原因包括女性教育与就业提升、育儿成本上升、婚育年龄推迟、城市化与社会保障替代了“养儿防老”，以及观念变化；往往是多因素叠加。
Common drivers are women's education and work, rising child-rearing costs, later marriage/childbirth, urbanization and pensions replacing “old-age security from children,” and value change—usually combined.

什么是“总和生育率”？

What is the "total fertility rate" (TFR)?

总和生育率估算一名女性一生平均生育的子女数；人口长期稳定的更替水平约为每名女性2.1个孩子。
The total fertility rate estimates the average children a woman would have in her life; the replacement level for stable population is about 2.1 per woman.
