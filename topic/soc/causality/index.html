“工具变量”如何帮助识别因果关系？

How does an "instrumental variable" help identify causation?

当原因与结果互相影响或受遗漏变量干扰时，工具变量找一个只通过原因、不直接影响结果的外生因素，借此剥离出干净的因果效应；工具需满足相关且外生，实践中较难满足。
When cause and effect intertwine or are confounded, an instrumental variable uses a factor that affects only the cause and not the outcome directly, isolating clean effects; it must be relevant and exogenous—hard to satisfy in practice.

什么是研究中的“自然实验”？

What is a "natural experiment" in research?

自然实验利用现实中偶然发生的外生冲击（如政策突变、灾害）近似随机分配，比较受影响与未受影响群体，从而更接近地识别因果。
A natural experiment exploits real-world exogenous shocks (policy shifts, disasters) that mimic random assignment, comparing affected and unaffected groups to better identify causation.
