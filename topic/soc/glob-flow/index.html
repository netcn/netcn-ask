为什么移民寄回家的钱（汇款）能支撑整个家乡经济？

Why can migrants' remittances sustain a whole hometown economy?

汇款是一笔稳定的外汇流入，金额常超过当地普通收入，用于日常消费、建房、教育与小微生意。它直接到达家庭而非依赖波动的政府项目，因而能可靠地稳定并激活地方经济。
Remittances are a steady inflow of foreign currency, often exceeding local incomes, that funds daily consumption, housing, education and small businesses. Because they reach households directly rather than through volatile state projects, they reliably stabilize and stimulate local economies.

为什么文化的“出口”往往也伴随“进口”？

Why does cultural 'export' often come with 'import'?

文化交流很少是单向的：输出文化的同时，社会也会因接触外界而受到反向影响，而全球媒体平台本身就是双向通道。贸易、旅游与移民网络决定了，只要敞开大门输出，外来文化也必然随之进入。
Cultural exchange is rarely one-way; sending culture out also exposes a society to foreign influence in return, and global media platforms are themselves two-way channels. Trade, tourism and diaspora networks ensure that openness to export culture also lets outside culture in.

为什么流行文化（音乐、影视）会跨国流动？

Why does pop culture (music, film) flow across borders?

好的旋律与故事跨越语言；平台与明星把内容推到全球，年轻人借它表达身份。文化产品轻、复制成本低，比货物更容易“旅行”。
Good melodies and stories cross language; platforms and stars push content globally, and youth use it to express identity. Cultural goods are light and cheap to copy, traveling easier than cargo.

为什么世界上有那么多人说同一种语言（如英语）？

Why do so many people speak one shared language like English?

因为大英帝国的扩张、美国的崛起和全球贸易让英语成了通用语；科学、网络与航空也用它沟通。会英语便于跨国读书、做生意和交朋友。
Britain's expansion, America's rise and global trade made English a lingua franca; science, the internet and aviation use it too. English eases studying, business and friendship across borders.
