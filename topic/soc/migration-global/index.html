跨国主义分析在哪些方面动摇了移民一次迁出便彻底断根的经典同化假设?

In what ways does transnationalist analysis unsettle the classical assimilation assumption that migration means leaving home once and for all?

跨国主义研究显示,当代移民长期维系横跨母国与移居地的多重联系:汇款、返乡探视、远程育儿与祖籍地政治参与同时展开。移民由此栖身于跨越两个国家边界的社会场域,单向融入接收社会不再被视为唯一轨迹。这一转向也推动政策讨论从防止人才流失重新聚焦于人才环流的可能性。
Transnationalist work shows that today's migrants sustain dense ties spanning origin and destination: remittances, return visits, long-distance parenting, and homeland politics unfold together. Migrants come to inhabit social fields stretched across two states, and one-way absorption into the host society loses its status as the sole trajectory. This shift has also redirected policy debates from curbing brain drain toward cultivating potential brain circulation.

#跨国主义 #社会场域

移民网络如何让墨西哥至美国的流动在工资差距收窄之后依然延续?

Why do Mexico-to-US migration flows persist even as wage gaps narrow, according to migrant network theory?

先行移民依托亲缘与乡籍纽带,为后来者提供落脚处、岗位线索与路费周转,使每次迁移的成本和风险随网络扩张而递减。当迁徙沉淀为社区内部约定俗成的人生阶段,流动便获得自我维持的动能,不再单纯听命于两地收入差。这种滞后性既解释了限制政策为何见效迟缓,也说明切断既有网络比调整工资信号艰难得多。
Earlier migrants pass lodging leads, job contacts, and travel funds to successors through kin and hometown ties, so each trip costs less and risks less as the web widens. Once movement settles into a routine life stage within sending communities, it acquires self-sustaining momentum no longer governed purely by income differentials. This hysteresis explains why restrictionist policies bite slowly and why severing existing networks proves far harder than tweaking wage signals.

#移民网络 #迁移惯性

移民汇款为什么被称为最稳定的资金流?比外国投资还可靠吗?

Why are remittances called the steadiest capital flow — more reliable than foreign investment?

跨国比较给出三个理由。逆周期性:母国经济危机货币贬值时,外币汇款购买力反而上升,移民倾向多寄;而外资与旅游在危机中最先撤离。微观直达性:侨汇绕开政府与金融中介直接进入家庭预算,转化为学费、医疗与小型创业,减贫效率高。成本低争议小:不附带政治条件、不形成主权债务。代价面同样清楚——接收地可能产生依赖与劳动力退出,输出地承受人才流失。政策方向因此是降低汇款手续费(全球平均仍超百分之六)而非限制流动。
Cross-country evidence gives three reasons. Counter-cyclicality: when the home economy slumps and its currency falls, foreign-currency remittances gain purchasing power and migrants send more — while investment and tourism flee first. Micro-directness: money bypasses states and intermediaries straight into household budgets for school fees, healthcare and microbusinesses, with high poverty-reduction efficiency. Low-contestation: no political strings, no sovereign debt. Costs remain real — dependence, labor withdrawal abroad, brain drain. Policy therefore targets cutting transfer fees (still above six percent globally) rather than restricting flows.
