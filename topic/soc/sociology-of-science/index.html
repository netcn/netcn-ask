默顿所称的科学界马太效应揭示了承认分配的什么规律?它与普遍主义规范之间存在何种张力?

What pattern in allocating scientific recognition did Merton label the Matthew effect, and how does it strain the norm of universalism?

默顿观察到,声名显赫的科学家在合作成果中往往独揽注意力,其姓名更易被记诵、引用与授奖,而无名者的同等贡献遭到低估,承认遂沿既有声望层层累积。这与普遍主义规范——按工作内在价值而非作者身份施予承认——构成紧张,说明奖励体系内嵌着累积优势的结构性偏差。该概念后来被延伸到署名排序、经费评审与期刊审稿中的不平等研究。
Merton noticed that eminent scientists monopolize attention on joint work: their names are more readily recalled, cited, and rewarded, while equally strong contributions from unknown peers are undervalued, so recognition compounds along existing prestige. This sits uneasily with universalism, which demands credit by intrinsic merit regardless of authorship, revealing an embedded structural bias toward cumulative advantage. The concept was later extended to authorship order, grant review, and peer review inequalities.

#科学奖励 #累积优势

默顿提出的公有性规范要求科学家如何处置自己做出的发现?

What does Merton's norm of communalism demand that scientists do with their discoveries?

公有性规范要求研究者及时公开发表成果,并放弃对知识本身的所有权,以此换取科学共同体的承认。发现一经发表便进入公共知识库,他人可以自由地在之上继续建构。命名权和引用量成为主要的奖励形式,默顿称这种以承认为报酬的安排是科学的货币体系。
The norm of communalism requires researchers to publish promptly and surrender ownership claims over the knowledge itself in exchange for recognition by the scientific community. Once published, a finding joins the common stock on which anyone may freely build. Eponymy and citation serve as the chief rewards, and Merton described this arrangement as a system in which recognition functions as scientific currency.

#科学规范 #公有性

为什么诺贝尔奖得主越来越容易再得大奖?默顿发现的马太效应是什么?

Why do Nobel laureates keep winning more prizes? What Matthew effect did Merton spot in science?

默顿1968年观察到一个复利现象:荣誉倾向于流向已有声望者。同样的贡献,署名在诺奖得主名下会被高估,无名者则被忽视;资源分配随之倾斜——基金、实验室与学生都涌向头部,优势滚雪球。他用马太福音的一句经文命名:凡有的,还要加给他。这一机制不否认个体差异,而是揭示制度性放大器的存在;后续计量研究证实了早期优势对科学生涯的持续影响。对策也在演化:双盲评审、青年基金专项、对团队贡献的显性署名规范,都是在给马太效应装减速器。
Merton observed compounding in 1968: recognition flows toward the already renowned. Identical contributions get overcredited under Nobel laureates names and ignored under unknown ones; funding, labs and students pile onto heads, snowballing advantage. He borrowed Matthews verse: to those who have, more shall be given. The mechanism does not deny individual differences — it exposes institutional amplifiers; scientometric work confirms early boosts echo through careers. Remedies evolve: double-blind review, young-investigator grants, explicit team-authorship norms — all brakes on the Matthew engine.
