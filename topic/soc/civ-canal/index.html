漕运如何把“南方的粮食”与“北方的权力”绑在一起？

How did grain transport tie 'southern food' to 'northern power'?

中国古代政治中心（如长安、北京）多在北方，而粮食主产区在南方。通过运河把南方的税粮运到都城，养活了皇室、军队和官员，使中央政权得以稳定运转。谁掌握了漕运，谁就掌握了都城的命脉；漕运一旦中断，北方权力就动摇。
In imperial China the political center (Chang'an, Beijing) lay in the north while the grain heartland was in the south. Shipping tax grain north by canal fed the court, the army, and the bureaucracy, keeping the central state running. Whoever controlled the grain transport held the capital's lifeline; if the canal failed, northern power wobbled.

为什么一条运河能塑造出一个统一的经济圈？

Why can a canal help shape a unified economic sphere?

水路比陆路便宜得多，运河把产销地连成网络，使粮价、盐价在广大区域趋同，也促进人口与技术的流动，从而把分散的地方市场整合成一个大市场。
Water transport is far cheaper than land; a canal links producers and consumers into a network, equalizing grain and salt prices over a vast area and moving people and know-how, knitting local markets into one big market.

修建大运河的社会代价与收益为何常被争论？

Why is the social cost and benefit of building the Grand Canal often debated?

征调民夫、劳役繁重曾造成大量伤亡与社会痛苦（高代价）；但它长期保障了都城供给、平抑粮价、促进南北交流（高收益）。代价由底层承担、收益由政权与商人共享，因此评价分歧。
Mass conscription caused huge hardship and death (high cost), yet it long secured the capital's supply, stabilized grain prices and linked north and south (high benefit). Costs fell on the poor while benefits went to the state and merchants, so views differ.

为什么古代要把南方的米运到北方去？

Why did people in ancient times move rice from the south to the north?

因为南方雨水多、产米多，而北方是皇帝和很多士兵、官员住的地方，自己种的粮不够吃，所以要用水路把米运过去。
The south had more rain and rice; the north held the emperor, soldiers and officials whose local harvest fell short, so rice was shipped north by water.

大运河主要是用来做什么的？

What was the Grand Canal mainly used for?

大运河把南方的粮食（尤其是稻米）经水路运到北方政治中心（如长安、洛阳、北京），也运送盐和物资。它是一条国家命脉式的运输干线。
The Grand Canal moved southern grain (especially rice) by water to northern political centers (e.g., Chang'an, Luoyang, Beijing) and carried salt and goods. It was a lifeline transport artery for the state.
