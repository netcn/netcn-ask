斯维德勒在《文化在行动中》为何反对把文化看作价值观直接驱动行为，而提出“文化工具箱”隐喻？

In 'Culture in Action', why did Swidler reject viewing culture as values directly driving behavior and propose the 'toolkit' metaphor?

斯维德勒认为在稳定时期文化更像一套可调用的话语与技能，人们按情形拼装出行为策略，而非被固定价值观驱动。这使文化解释转向“能力”与“策略”而非“动机”。
Swidler argued that in settled periods culture is a repertoire of symbols and skills people assemble into strategies of action, not fixed values driving conduct. This shifts cultural explanation to 'capabilities' and 'strategies' rather than 'motives'.

格尔茨用“深描”方法研究文化，他关于巴厘岛斗鸡的经典分析揭示了符号如何承载社会意义？

Using 'thick description', how did Geertz's classic analysis of the Balinese cockfight reveal how symbols carry social meaning?

格尔茨认为斗鸡是巴厘社会的文本，下注与羽色隐喻地位、男性气概与宗族张力。深描要求还原行动者的意义脉络，而非仅记录行为频率。
Geertz treated the cockfight as a text of Balinese society where wagers and plumage encode status, masculinity, and kin tension. Thick description recovers actors' meaning contexts rather than merely counting behavior.

塔杰菲尔的社会认同理论如何解释人们仅因被随机分组就产生内群体偏袒？最小群体范式说明了什么？

How does Tajfel's social identity theory explain in-group favoritism even from random grouping, and what does the minimal group paradigm show?

社会认同理论主张个体通过所属群体获得自尊，因而倾向于偏袒内群体。最小群体范式证明仅凭任意分类即可诱发偏袒，说明认同与偏见可在无真实利益冲突时产生。
Social identity theory holds that self-esteem is tied to group membership, prompting in-group favoritism. The minimal group paradigm showed arbitrary categorization alone suffices to induce bias, implying identity and prejudice arise without real conflict.
