路易·沃斯为何认为“城市生活方式”是一种独特的生活形态？哪些因素塑造了它？

Why did Louis Wirth consider urbanism a distinctive way of life, and which factors shape it?

沃斯在1938年提出，人口规模、密度和异质性三者结合，塑造出独特的都市生存方式：人多使交往多为次级的、限于特定角色的接触；高密度带来竞争与摩擦，催生防御性的冷漠；异质性则瓦解亲属和邻里纽带，加剧分化。结果，都市人置身人群却彼此疏离，社会控制也从熟人舆论转向法律等正式制度，形成典型的“都市人格”。
In his 1938 essay Louis Wirth held that large size, high density, and heterogeneity together produce a distinctive urban way of life. Sheer numbers make most contacts secondary and confined to specific roles; density breeds competition, friction, and defensive reserve; heterogeneity weakens kinship and neighborhood ties and sharpens differentiation. City dwellers are therefore surrounded by crowds yet remain relatively detached, and social control shifts from acquaintance opinion to formal institutions such as law—the classic “urban personality.”
