平台反垄断的核心关切是什么？

What are the core concerns of platform antitrust?

关切包括平台利用数据和网络效应形成进入壁垒、自我优待（偏袒自有业务）、以及“赢家通吃”压制竞争与创新；监管倾向于促进互操作与数据可携带。
Concerns are barriers from data and network effects, self-preferencing (favoring own services), and “winner-takes-all” suppressing competition and innovation; regulation leans toward interoperability and data portability.

平台应承担怎样的内容审核责任？

What content-moderation responsibility should platforms bear?

介于“完全不干预”和“全面审查”之间，平台通常需对违法内容（如仇恨、暴力、欺诈）采取删除与标识，并公开规则与申诉渠道；尺度平衡言论自由与安全防护。
Between “no intervention” and “full censorship,” platforms typically must remove or flag illegal content (hate, violence, fraud) and publish rules and appeal channels; the balance is free expression versus safety.

什么是“平台治理”？

What is "platform governance"?

平台治理指对电商、社交、出行等数字平台在规则、内容、竞争和数据上的管理与监督，涉及平台、用户、政府与社会的多方责任。
Platform governance is the management and oversight of digital platforms (e-commerce, social, ride-hailing) on rules, content, competition, and data, involving platform, users, government, and society.
