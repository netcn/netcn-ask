巴特勒在《性别麻烦》中如何论证性别是“操演”而非内在身份？她的“异性恋矩阵”概念指什么？

How did Butler argue in Gender Trouble that gender is 'performed' rather than an inner identity, and what is the 'heterosexual matrix'?

巴特勒认为性别不是前因于行动的本质，而是通过反复的身体与言语仪式“操演”出来的稳定假象。异性恋矩阵指把生理性别、社会性别与欲望强制绑定为异性恋规范的话语框架。
Butler argued gender is not a prior essence but a repeated stylized performance producing the illusion of stability. The 'heterosexual matrix' is the discursive frame that coercively binds sex, gender, and desire into heterosexual normativity.

霍克希尔德在《被管理的心》中提出的“情绪劳动”如何揭示服务业中情感被制度化规训？她后来用“第二轮班”描述什么现象？

How does Hochschild's 'emotional labor' in The Managed Heart reveal the institutional regulation of feeling in service work, and what did her later 'second shift' describe?

情绪劳动指员工被要求按组织脚本管理自己的情绪表达（如微笑服务），造成表层或深层扮演。霍克希尔德在《第二轮班》中指出职业女性回家后仍承担多数家务，形成双重负担。
Emotional labor means workers must manage feeling displays to fit organizational scripts (e.g., smiling service), entailing surface or deep acting. In The Second Shift, Hochschild showed employed women still do most housework, a double burden.

韦斯特与齐默尔曼提出“doing gender”想说明性别如何在日常互动中被持续“做”出来？

What did West and Zimmerman mean by 'doing gender', and how is gender continually 'done' in everyday interaction?

他们认为性别不是属性而是互动中随时问责、可被观察的成就：人们按情境规范展示符合男/女的举止并被他人评价。此举把性别再生产成本定位到微观互动层面。
They argued gender is not an attribute but an emergent achievement accountable in interaction: people display norm-appropriate conduct and are judged for it. This locates the reproduction of gender in micro-interaction.
