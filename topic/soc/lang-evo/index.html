什么是“语言谱系”与“原始母语”重建？

What are “language families” and “proto-language” reconstruction?

比较法通过系统对应关系将语言归入谱系（如印欧语系），并逆向重建未被文字记录的“原始母语”，揭示人群迁徙史。
The comparative method groups languages into families (e.g., Indo-European) and reconstructs unrecorded “proto-languages,” illuminating migration history.

语言为什么会随时间变化？

Why does language change over time?

发音省力、词汇借用、社会分层与代际差异推动语言演变；变化通常缓慢且不可逆，通过口语传播在自然语言中持续发生。
Ease of articulation, borrowing, social stratification, and generational difference drive language change; it is slow, largely irreversible, and ongoing in speech.

什么是“通用语”（lingua franca）？

What is a “lingua franca”?

通用语是不同母语群体间用于交流的共有语言（如历史上的阿拉伯语、今日的英语），常随贸易与霸权兴衰而更替。
A lingua franca is a shared language for communication across mother tongues (Arabic historically, English today), shifting with trade and hegemony.

为什么一些方言正在消失？

Why are some dialects disappearing?

通用语、大众媒体与人口流动让年轻人更少使用方言，许多方言使用人数下降、面临消失；语言保护努力试图记录与传承它们。
A common language, mass media, and migration lead younger people to use dialects less, so many are declining and at risk of disappearing; preservation efforts try to document and sustain them.
