教育扩张一定会提升社会流动吗？

Does educational expansion necessarily increase social mobility?

不一定。扩招提高整体学历，但当高学历变普遍，其“信号”贬值，优势阶层更能把新文凭转化为岗位，弱势者未必获益，甚至加剧“学历通胀”下的相对劣势。
Not necessarily. Expansion raises average credentials, but as degrees become common their signal weakens; advantaged groups convert new credentials into jobs more easily, while the disadvantaged may face worsening relative standing via “credential inflation.”

什么是“玻璃天花板”？

What is the "glass ceiling"?

玻璃天花板指某些群体（常指女性或少数族裔）在职业晋升到高层时遇到的隐形障碍，看似可破却难以逾越，反映结构性的机会不均。
The glass ceiling is the invisible barrier some groups (often women or minorities) hit near top jobs—seemingly breakable yet hard to cross—reflecting structural inequality of opportunity.

“代内流动”和“代际流动”有何区别？

How do "intra-generational" and "intergenerational" mobility differ?

代内流动是一个人一生中地位的变化（如跳槽升职）；代际流动是子女相对父母地位的变化（如是否比父辈更富裕）。两者衡量流动的不同维度。
Intra-generational mobility is a person's change over their own life (e.g., promotion); intergenerational mobility is children's status versus parents' (e.g., richer than one's parents). They measure different dimensions.

什么是“社会流动”？

What is "social mobility"?

社会流动指人在社会地位、收入或职业层级上的上升或下降，比如从工人家庭的孩子成为医生。
Social mobility is movement up or down in social status, income, or occupation—e.g., a worker's child becoming a doctor.
