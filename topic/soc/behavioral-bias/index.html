“框架效应”如何改变人的选择？

How does the “framing effect” change people's choices?

同样的事实用不同方式表述（如“存活90%”对比“死亡10%”）会引导出不同决定，说明偏好并非固定，而受表述语境影响；这对医疗、营销和政策设计都有意义。
The same facts presented differently (e.g., “90% survive” vs. “10% die”) lead to different choices, showing preferences aren't fixed but context-dependent—relevant to medicine, marketing, and policy design.

什么是“从众效应”（羊群行为）？

What is the "bandwagon effect" (herd behavior)?

从众效应指人们因看到别人怎么做而跟随，哪怕自己并不确定，常见于抢购、投资追涨和舆论扩散；它放大波动，也可能引发泡沫或恐慌。
The bandwagon effect is following what others do despite uncertainty, seen in panic buying, momentum investing, and rumor spread; it amplifies swings and can fuel bubbles or panics.

什么是“禀赋效应”？

What is the "endowment effect"?

禀赋效应指人一旦拥有某物，就比未拥有时更看重它，导致要价高于买价；它解释了为何人们不愿割舍、市场成交偏少。
The endowment effect means people value something more once they own it, demanding more to sell than they'd pay to buy; it explains reluctance to part with items and thin markets.
