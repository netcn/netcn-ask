什么是碳十四测年？

What is carbon-14 dating?

碳十四测年利用生物死亡后体内碳-14放射性同位素按固定速率衰变的原理，测定有机遗物（骨骼、木炭、种子）的年代，有效范围约五万年以内。
Carbon-14 dating measures the decay of the radioactive isotope carbon-14 in once-living material—bone, charcoal, seeds—to date organic remains, reliable up to about 50,000 years.

什么是考古地层学？

What is stratigraphy in archaeology?

地层学借用地质学原理，通过土层堆积的上下顺序判断遗址年代——下层通常更早、上层较晚。它是考古断代与重建古代人类活动序列的基础方法。
Borrowing from geology, archaeological stratigraphy dates a site by the order of soil layers—lower deposits are usually older. It is the basic method for dating and reconstructing ancient activity sequences.

什么是石器时代？

What is the Stone Age?

石器时代是人类主要使用石器的最早历史阶段，按技术分为旧石器、中石器和新石器三个阶段，跨越数百万年，直至金属冶炼出现。
The Stone Age is the earliest period when humans mainly used stone tools, divided into Paleolithic, Mesolithic, and Neolithic stages spanning millions of years until metalworking emerged.
