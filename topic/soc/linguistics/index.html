什么是音素？

What is a phoneme?

音素是某种语言中能区分词义的最小语音单位。例如汉语“b”和“p”是不同音素（“八/趴”），但在某些语言里这种差别不改变词义，因此不构成音素对立。
A phoneme is the smallest sound unit that distinguishes meaning in a language. In Mandarin, /b/ and /p/ are separate phonemes (“ba” vs “pa”), but in some languages that difference does not change meaning and so is not a phonemic contrast.

国际音标（IPA）由哪个机构制定，主要用途是什么？

Which body created the International Phonetic Alphabet (IPA), and what is its main use?

国际音标由国际语音协会（IPA）于 1888 年首次制定，用一套与声音一一对应的符号精确记录人类语言的各种发音，不受具体文字拼写干扰。它是语言学和词典编纂标注发音的标准工具。
The International Phonetic Alphabet was first published by the International Phonetic Association in 1888, using one symbol per sound to record speech precisely regardless of spelling. It is the standard tool for transcription in linguistics and lexicography.

乔姆斯基的“普遍语法”假说主张什么？

What does Chomsky’s “universal grammar” hypothesis claim?

乔姆斯基在 1957 年《句法结构》及之后提出，人类天生具备一套内嵌于大脑的语言习得机制，即普遍语法，使儿童能在有限输入下快速掌握复杂语法。这一假说引发了语言学与认知科学的长久争论。
In “Syntactic Structures” (1957) and later, Chomsky proposed that humans are born with an innate language-acquisition device—universal grammar—enabling children to rapidly master complex grammar from limited input. The hypothesis sparked lasting debates in linguistics and cognitive science.

什么是句法？

What is syntax?

句法研究词语如何组合成合乎语法的句子、以及句子结构背后的规则。不同语言语序不同（如汉语主谓宾、日语主宾谓），但都受普遍语法原则约束。
Syntax studies how words combine into grammatical sentences and the rules behind sentence structure. Languages differ in word order (Mandarin SVO, Japanese SOV) yet follow universal grammatical principles.

什么是声调语言？

What is a tonal language?

声调语言用音高变化区分词义，如汉语普通话有四个声调，“妈、麻、马、骂”音高不同意义各异。世界上过半语言具有声调特征，在非洲和东南亚尤为常见。
Tonal languages use pitch to distinguish meaning—Mandarin has four tones, so “mā, má, mǎ, mà” mean different things. Over half the world’s languages are tonal, especially across Africa and Southeast Asia.

什么是语系？

What is a language family?

语系指由共同原始语言分化而来的一组语言，如汉藏语系、印欧语系。语言学家通过比较词汇和语法复原“母语”，据此把全球数千种语言归类成若干语系。
A language family is a group of languages descended from a common ancestral language, such as Sino-Tibetan or Indo-European. Comparing vocabulary and grammar lets linguists reconstruct the parent tongue and classify thousands of languages.

什么是表意文字与拼音文字？

What are logographic and alphabetic writing systems?

表意文字用字符直接表示词或语素（如汉字），拼音文字用字母表示语音（如拉丁字母、西里尔字母）。两者各有优劣，许多现代文字系统其实是混合形态。
Logographic systems use characters to represent words or morphemes (e.g., Chinese characters), while alphabetic systems use letters for sounds (e.g., Latin, Cyrillic). Each has trade-offs, and many modern scripts are mixed.

萨丕尔-沃尔夫假说（语言相对论）说的是什么？

What does the Sapir-Whorf hypothesis (linguistic relativity) say?

萨丕尔与沃尔夫提出，人们所使用的语言结构会影响其认知与看待世界的方式，即“语言塑造思维”。强版本（语言决定思维）已被大多数学者否定，但弱版本（语言影响认知倾向）仍有研究支持。
Sapir and Whorf proposed that the structure of one’s language influences cognition and how the world is perceived—“language shapes thought.” The strong version (language determines thought) is largely rejected, while the weak version (language influences cognitive tendencies) retains some support.

拉波夫的城市社会语言学研究发现语言变体与社会阶层有何关系？

What did Labov’s urban sociolinguistics reveal about language variation and social class?

拉波夫在 1966 年《纽约市英语的社会分层》中通过“随机应变的 (r)”等实验发现，即便无意识，说话者的发音变体也随社会阶层和教育程度系统性变化，并存在向高声望变体靠拢的倾向。
In 1966’s “The Social Stratification of English in New York City,” Labov showed via experiments like the variable (r) that speakers’ pronunciation variants shift systematically with social class and education even unconsciously, with a tendency toward prestige variants.

什么是借词？

What is a loanword?

借词是一种语言从另一种语言吸收来的现成词汇，如汉语的“咖啡”（源自阿拉伯语）、“雷达”（源自英语 radar）。借词反映文化交流与接触历史。
A loanword is a word borrowed ready-made from another language, such as Mandarin “kāfēi” (coffee, from Arabic) or “léidá” (radar, from English). Loanwords reveal cultural contact and exchange.
