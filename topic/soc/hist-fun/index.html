什么是“哥伦布大交换”？

What was the “Columbian Exchange”?

指1492年后新旧大陆间的生物、作物、人口与疾病的大规模交流：玉米、马铃薯传入旧大陆，天花等传入新大陆。它深刻改变了全球人口与饮食。
After 1492, it was the massive exchange of biota, crops, people, and disease between the Old and New Worlds: maize and potatoes reached the Old World, smallpox the New. It profoundly changed global population and diet.

什么是“丝绸之路”？它主要交换了什么？

What was the “Silk Road” and what did it mainly exchange?

丝绸之路是古代连接欧亚的陆上与海上商路，不仅贸易丝绸、瓷器、香料，也传播宗教、技术与作物。它是早期全球化的纽带。
The Silk Road was ancient overland and maritime trade routes linking Eurasia, carrying not just silk, porcelain, and spices but also religions, technologies, and crops. It was an early globalization link.

为什么“工业革命”被认为是历史分水岭？

Why is the “Industrial Revolution” seen as a historical watershed?

18世纪起机器与蒸汽动力取代手工，生产力暴涨、城市兴起、社会结构重塑。它开启了现代经济增长模式，也带来环境与劳资新问题。
From the 18th century, machines and steam replaced handwork, exploding productivity, raising cities, and reshaping society. It launched modern economic growth but also new environmental and labor problems.

什么是“大航海时代”对全球格局的影响？

What impact did the “Age of Exploration” have on global order?

15—17世纪欧洲航海拓展了跨洋贸易与殖民，连接了原本隔绝的洲陆，催生世界市场。它也开启了物种、疾病与文化的全球交换（哥伦布大交换）。
In the 15th–17th centuries, European voyages expanded ocean trade and colonization, linking isolated continents and birthing a world market. They also opened global exchange of species, diseases, and cultures (the Columbian Exchange).

为什么“邮票”曾经是通信的象征，现在很少见？

Why was the “stamp” a symbol of communication but is now rare?

邮票是预付邮资的凭证，伴随纸质信函普及；电子邮件与即时通讯让寄信锐减，邮票退为收藏与纪念。技术更替改变日常媒介。
Stamps prepaid postage and spread with paper letters; email and instant messaging crashed letter volume, leaving stamps as collectibles. Tech shifts change everyday media.

纸币最早是哪个文明发明的？

Which civilization invented paper money first?

世界上最早使用的纸币是中国北宋时期的“交子”，出现在四川，便于大宗交易携带。它比欧洲纸币早约六百年。
The world’s first paper money was China’s “Jiaozi” of the Northern Song dynasty in Sichuan, easing bulk-trade carriage. It predated European paper notes by about 600 years.

为什么一周有七天，而不是五天或十天？

Why is a week seven days, not five or ten?

七天周制源自古巴比伦，与月相周期（约28天分四段）和宗教传统有关，后被多文明沿用。它并非自然节律，而是人造时间单位。
The seven-day week came from Babylon, tied to lunar phases (about 28 days in four) and religious tradition, later adopted by many civilizations. It is not a natural rhythm but a human-made time unit.

为什么现代人普遍使用阿拉伯数字而不是汉字数字算账？

Why do we use Arabic numerals instead of Chinese numerals for calculation?

阿拉伯数字（实际源自印度）符号简洁、进位清晰、便于书写与运算，全球通用。它降低了跨语言数学交流成本。
Arabic numerals (actually from India) are concise, have clear place value, and are easy to write and compute, used worldwide. They cut cross-language math communication cost.
