为什么“现金为王”在经济不确定时经常被提起？

Why is “cash is king” often cited in uncertain times?

不确定时资产价格可能大跌、变现困难，现金流动性最强、能随时支付并抄底。但它也受通胀侵蚀，所以是权衡而非绝对。
In uncertainty, asset prices can crash and become hard to sell; cash is the most liquid, ready to pay and to buy the dip. But it is eroded by inflation, so it is a trade-off, not absolute.

什么是“通胀税”？它和普通税有什么不同？

What is “inflation tax” and how does it differ from a normal tax?

通胀税并非政府直接征收，而是货币超发使持有现金者购买力被悄悄稀释，相当于对现金持有人的隐性征税。它不体现在税单上，却真实减少财富。
An inflation tax is not levied directly; it is the silent erosion of cash holders’ purchasing power from money over-issue—an implicit tax on cash. It never appears on a tax bill yet genuinely cuts wealth.

什么是“名义利率”和“实际利率”的区别？

What is the difference between “nominal” and “real” interest rates?

名义利率是账面上标明的利率，实际利率要扣除通胀：实际≈名义−通胀。当通胀高于名义利率时，实际利率为负，存款实际在缩水。
Nominal rate is the stated rate; real rate subtracts inflation: real ≈ nominal − inflation. When inflation exceeds the nominal rate, real rate is negative and deposits shrink in real terms.

我们交的税大致都去了哪里？

Where does the tax we pay roughly go?

税收主要用于公共服务：教育、医疗、基础设施、国防、社会保障与行政运转等。不同国家结构不同，但多数用于全民共享的公共品。
Taxes mainly fund public services: education, health, infrastructure, defense, social security, and administration. Structures differ by country, but most go to shared public goods.

什么是“信用卡免息期”？

What is a credit card “interest-free period”?

从消费日到还款日之间，若全额还清通常不收利息，这段时间为免息期。利用好它可短期周转，但只还最低额会产生利息。
Between purchase and due date, if you pay in full you usually pay no interest—that gap is the grace period. Used well it aids short-term cash flow, but paying only the minimum incurs interest.

人民币升值或贬值对普通人的出国游有什么影响？

How does RMB appreciation or depreciation affect ordinary travelers?

本币升值意味着换外币更多，出国旅游、留学更便宜；贬值则相反，海外消费更贵。汇率波动主要影响跨境消费，对国内日常物价影响较间接。
A stronger currency buys more foreign money, making travel and study abroad cheaper; a weaker one does the opposite. Exchange moves mainly affect cross-border spending, less directly domestic prices.

把钱放银行活期，为什么利息几乎感觉不到？

Why is interest on a checking account barely noticeable?

活期利率通常远低于通胀率，实际购买力可能还在下降，所以“利息”感知很弱。它主要提供流动性而非增值。
Checking-account rates are usually far below inflation, so real purchasing power may even fall; the interest feels tiny. Its job is liquidity, not growth.

汇率牌价上的“买入价”和“卖出价”为什么不一样？

Why do exchange “buy” and “sell” rates differ?

银行或兑换机构靠买卖价差（点差）赚取服务费，所以同一时刻买入外币价低、卖出价高。这是金融机构的正常盈利方式。
Banks and exchanges earn a service fee from the spread, so at any moment they buy foreign currency low and sell it high. This spread is their normal profit.

什么是“信用”，为什么按时还款很重要？

What is “credit” and why does paying on time matter?

信用是个人履约还债的记录与能力，按时还款积累良好信用，能降低未来借贷成本。逾期会留下不良记录，影响贷款、租房甚至求职。
Credit is your record and ability to repay debts. Paying on time builds a good score that lowers future borrowing costs; defaults leave a bad record affecting loans, rentals, even jobs.

为什么小票、发票要妥善保管？

Why keep receipts and invoices properly?

发票是消费与纳税凭证，可用于保修、退换、报销和维权。电子发票普及后更易保存，但仍需防丢失与重复报销。
Invoices are proof of purchase and tax, useful for warranty, returns, reimbursement, and rights claims. E-invoices are easier to keep, but still guard against loss and double claims.

为什么存钱罐和“先存后花”被反复提倡？

Why is saving-first-before-spending repeatedly encouraged?

先储蓄再消费能建立应急缓冲，应对失业或意外开支，减少陷入债务。它是个人财务稳健的基础习惯。
Saving before spending builds an emergency buffer for job loss or surprises and reduces debt risk. It is a basic habit of personal financial health.
