克里斯塔勒的“中心地理论”解释了什么空间规律？

What spatial pattern does Christaller’s “central place theory” explain?

克里斯塔勒在 1933 年提出中心地理论，用“六边形市场区”模型解释城镇作为提供商品与服务的“中心地”如何按等级和服务范围在平原上均匀分布。它是人文地理学和城市体系规划的经典理论。
Christaller’s 1933 central place theory uses a hexagonal market-area model to explain how towns, as “central places” providing goods and services, are distributed evenly across a plain in a hierarchy by range and threshold. It is a classic in human geography and urban planning.

什么是核心-边缘理论？

What is core-periphery theory?

核心-边缘理论描述经济活动中，少数“核心”地区（如大城市、沿海）因集聚效应而繁荣，广大“边缘”地区则相对滞后并被抽取资源。它常用于解释区域不平衡发展。
Core-periphery theory describes how a few “core” areas—big cities, coasts—thrive through agglomeration while “peripheral” regions lag and supply them resources. It explains uneven regional development.

什么是地图投影变形？

What is map projection distortion?

把地球曲面展成平面地图必然产生变形，没有一种投影能同时保真面积、形状和距离。墨卡托投影保角却夸大高纬度面积，而等积投影会扭曲形状。
Flattening the globe onto a map inevitably distorts it—no projection preserves area, shape, and distance at once. Mercator keeps angles but inflates high-latitude areas; equal-area projections distort shapes.

什么是流域？

What is a river basin?

流域是地表水与地下水汇入同一条河流及其支流所覆盖的集水区域，以分水岭为界。上下游共享同一水系，使水污染、灌溉与洪水管理必须跨越行政边界协作。
A river basin is the land area where surface and groundwater drain into one river and its tributaries, bounded by watersheds. Up- and downstream share one system, so pollution, irrigation, and flood control need cross-boundary cooperation.

什么是“碳中和”，IPCC 在其中扮演什么角色？

What is “carbon neutrality,” and what role does the IPCC play?

碳中和指一定时期内人为温室气体排放与被吸收或抵消的量相等，实现净零排放。政府间气候变化专门委员会（IPCC）由联合国环境署与 WMO 于 1988 年设立，负责评估气候科学并为《巴黎协定》等提供权威依据。
Carbon neutrality means that over a period, human-caused greenhouse gas emissions equal the amount absorbed or offset, achieving net zero. The IPCC, established by UNEP and WMO in 1988, assesses climate science and provides the authoritative basis for agreements like the Paris Agreement.

“丝绸之路”这一名称是谁、在何时提出的？

Who coined the name “Silk Road,” and when?

“丝绸之路”一词由德国地理学家费迪南·冯·李希霍芬在 1877 年的著作中首次使用，用来概括汉代中国与地中海世界之间通过中亚的古老贸易通道。这一名称后被广泛用于指代横跨欧亚的古代商路网络。
The term “Silk Road” was first used by German geographer Ferdinand von Richthofen in his 1877 work to describe the ancient trade routes linking Han China with the Mediterranean via Central Asia. The name later came to denote the broader Eurasian trade network.

什么是可再生资源？

What are renewable resources?

可再生资源是能在合理时间内自然恢复或循环利用的资源，如太阳能、风能、森林与淡水。若开采速度超过再生速度，它们也会枯竭，因此仍需可持续管理。
Renewable resources regenerate or recycle within a reasonable time—solar, wind, forests, fresh water. If extraction outpaces renewal they can still be depleted, so sustainable management matters.

什么是“人口密度”？

What is “population density”?

人口密度指单位土地面积上居住的人口数量，通常用“人/平方公里”表示。它帮助人们比较不同地区人类分布的疏密程度，是地理学和城市规划的基本指标。
Population density is the number of people living per unit of land area, usually expressed as persons per square kilometer. It helps compare how densely people are distributed across regions and is a basic indicator in geography and urban planning.

什么是时区？

What is a time zone?

时区是为统一计时把地球按经度划分的区段，理论上每15°相差一小时。多数国家采用整小时区，也有半小时或45分钟的特例（如印度、尼泊尔）。
Time zones divide the Earth by longitude so clocks stay consistent—in theory one hour per 15°. Most countries use whole-hour zones, but some use half- or 45-minute offsets (e.g., India, Nepal).
