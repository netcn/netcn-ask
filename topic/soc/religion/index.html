什么是世俗化？

What is secularization?

世俗化指宗教在社会公共生活与制度中的影响逐渐减弱、宗教活动转向私人的精神趋势，常见于现代化与科学普及程度较高的社会。
Secularization is the declining public and institutional role of religion as faith becomes a more private matter, common in modernized, scientifically advanced societies.

Beyond the Protestant ethic, how did Weber use 'disenchantment' in his sociology of religion to describe the rationalization of the modern world?

韦伯把祛魅定义为神秘与神圣体验被科学解释与科层计算取代，世界变得可计算、可预测。理性化提升了效率却也带来“铁笼”式的意义空洞。
Weber defined disenchantment as the replacement of mystery and the sacred by scientific explanation and bureaucratic calculation, making the world predictable. Rationalization boosts efficiency yet risks a meaning-empty 'iron cage'.

什么是政教分离？

What is the separation of church and state?

政教分离指宗教机构与国家政权在制度上相互独立，政府不确立国教、不干预信仰，信仰也不直接支配立法。它旨在保障宗教自由并维持公共领域的中立。
Separation of church and state keeps religious institutions and government independent: the state establishes no official religion and does not dictate belief, nor does faith directly rule legislation. It protects religious freedom and public neutrality.

Why did Stark and Finke's religious economy theory model religion as a competitive 'spiritual market', and how does it explain varying secularization?

他们主张供给侧（多元教派竞争）决定宗教活力，国家垄断信仰反令需求萎缩。该模型挑战了世俗化必然论，解释为何美国高度信教而欧洲低落。
They argued supply—competition among denominations—drives religious vitality, while state monopolies stifle demand. The model challenges secularization as inevitable, explaining high U.S. religiosity versus European decline.

什么是世界宗教？

What are the world religions?

世界宗教指在多个地区拥有大量信众、具跨民族传播力的几大宗教，通常指基督教、伊斯兰教、印度教、佛教和犹太教等。它们塑造了全球文化与伦理图景。
World religions are those with large, cross-ethnic followings across regions—typically Christianity, Islam, Hinduism, Buddhism, and Judaism. They have shaped global culture and ethics.
