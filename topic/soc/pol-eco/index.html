生态现代化理论如何解释发展与环保的相容？

How does ecological modernization reconcile development with environmental protection?

该理论认为技术创新、市场激励与制度改革的结合可使经济增长与生态改善脱钩（decoupling），主张环保可以成为竞争力来源。
The theory holds that tech innovation, market incentives, and reform can decouple growth from environmental harm, making greenness a source of competitiveness.

环境不平等（environmental justice）如何与阶层和种族交织？

How does environmental inequality intertwine with class and race?

污染设施与气候风险常不成比例地落在低收入与少数族裔社区，环境不平等与阶层、种族叠加，催生环境正义运动要求公平负担。
Polluting facilities and climate risk fall disproportionately on low-income and minority communities, entwining environmental inequality with class and race, fueling environmental-justice demands.

什么是“政治生态学”？

What is “political ecology”?

政治生态学研究环境问题的政治与经济根源，强调资源争夺、权力关系如何塑造生态退化与气候脆弱性，而不只是自然过程。
Political ecology studies the political-economic roots of environmental problems, emphasizing how power and resource struggles shape degradation and climate vulnerability, not just nature.

什么是“公地悲剧”？它如何应用于气候？

What is the “tragedy of the commons,” and how does it apply to climate?

个体理性地使用共享资源（如大气、渔场）会过度消耗、损害全体。气候变化正是全球公地悲剧，需集体约束与配额安排。
Rational individuals over-exploit shared resources (atmosphere, fisheries), harming all—a tragedy of the commons; climate change needs collective limits and quota regimes.
