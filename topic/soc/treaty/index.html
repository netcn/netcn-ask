条约的“保留”制度指什么？

What is the treaty "reservation" system?

保留是当事国在加入条约时声明部分条款对自己不适用；它让更多国家能参与，但过多保留会削弱条约一致性，且通常不得违背条约核心目的。
A reservation is a state's declaration that some clauses won't apply to it upon joining; it lets more states participate but excessive reservations weaken consistency and may not contradict the treaty's core object.

《巴黎协定》的主要目标是什么？

What is the main goal of the Paris Agreement?

《巴黎协定》旨在把全球升温控制在远低于工业化前2摄氏度、并努力限制在1.5摄氏度内，由各缔约方自主提出并定期更新减排贡献。
The Paris Agreement aims to keep global warming well below 2°C above pre-industrial levels and pursue 1.5°C, with each party submitting and regularly updating its own emission targets.

什么是“国际条约”？

What is an "international treaty"?

国际条约是国家之间书面约定的协议，一旦批准就对当事国产生法律义务，涵盖贸易、人权、环境等广泛领域。
An international treaty is a written agreement between states that, once ratified, creates legal obligations, covering trade, human rights, environment, and more.
