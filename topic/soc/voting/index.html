选区制度如何影响政党格局？

How do district systems shape party systems?

单一选区多数制倾向放大两大党、不利于小党（杜瓦杰定律）；比例代表则降低门槛、容纳更多政党，但可能使组阁更分散；混合制介于两者之间。
Single-seat plurality tends to favor two big parties and penalize small ones (Duverger's law); proportional representation lowers barriers and admits more parties but can fragment coalitions; mixed systems sit in between.

什么是“阿罗不可能定理”？

What is "Arrow's impossibility theorem"?

它证明在满足几项合理条件（如尊重个人偏好、不独裁）时，不存在一种投票规则能始终把个人偏好汇成一致的集体排序，说明集体决策总有难以兼顾的权衡。
It proves no voting rule can always turn individual preferences into a consistent social ordering while meeting sensible conditions (respecting preferences, no dictator), showing collective choice always involves trade-offs.

“多数决”和“比例代表制”有什么不同？

How do "majority rule" and "proportional representation" differ?

多数决（如单选区相对多数）按得票最多者胜，易形成两党；比例代表按得票比例分配席位，更能反映多元诉求，但政党可能较多。
Majority rule (e.g., single-seat plurality) lets the top vote-getter win, tending toward two parties; proportional representation allocates seats by vote share, better reflecting diversity but often yielding many parties.
