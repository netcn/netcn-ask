科学知识的社会建构论主张什么？

What does the social construction of scientific knowledge claim?

社会建构论认为科学事实的确立受社会、文化与制度语境影响，并非纯粹客观发现的展开，引发与科学实在论的争论。
Social constructivism holds that established scientific facts are shaped by social, cultural, and institutional contexts, not pure objective discovery—debated against scientific realism.

同行评审（peer review）有哪些局限？

What are the limits of peer review?

同行评审虽过滤低质研究，但存在偏见、缓慢、难以发现造假与对新颖性的排斥；可重复性危机推动开放评审与预印本改革。
Peer review filters poor work but shows bias, slowness, limited fraud detection, and resistance to novelty; the reproducibility crisis drives open review and preprints.

什么是“马太效应”在科学界的表现？

What is the “Matthew effect” in science?

知名科学家更易获得荣誉、经费与引用，无名者贡献易被低估，优势累积放大不平等，即科学界的马太效应。
Famous scientists more easily gain credit, funding, and citations while lesser-known contributors are overlooked—advantage accumulates, the “Matthew effect” in science.

什么是“利益相关者”在科技决策中的角色？

What role do “stakeholders” play in science and technology policy?

科技政策影响公众、企业、研究者与政府等多方，利益相关者通过咨询、听证与游说参与，以求在风险与收益间达成可接受方案。
Tech policy affects publics, firms, researchers, and states; stakeholders engage via consultation, hearings, and lobbying to balance risks and benefits acceptably.
