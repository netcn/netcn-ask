科斯定理认为在什么条件下外部性可由私人协商解决？

Under what conditions does the Coase theorem say externalities can be solved by private bargaining?

当产权界定清晰、交易成本足够低、且相关方能够自由协商时，无论初始权利归谁，当事人都能达成有效率的结果；现实中交易成本和集体行动问题常使其难以实现。
When property rights are clear, transaction costs are low, and parties can bargain freely, an efficient outcome is reached regardless of who holds the initial right; in practice, transaction costs and collective-action problems often block this.

正外部性和负外部性有什么不同？

How do positive and negative externalities differ?

负外部性是行为给旁人带来成本（如污染），正外部性是带来收益（如接种疫苗保护他人）；市场往往生产过多负外部性、过少正外部性。
Negative externalities impose costs on others (e.g., pollution); positive ones create benefits (e.g., vaccination protecting others). Markets tend to produce too much of the former and too little of the latter.

污染税（庇古税）是如何“内化”外部性的？

How do pollution taxes (Pigouvian taxes) "internalize" externalities?

通过对污染等负外部性征税，把社会成本计入生产者价格，使其减少过量排放；税额通常应等于边际外部损害。
By taxing pollution and similar negative externalities, the social cost is added to the producer's price, discouraging excess; the tax ideally equals marginal external damage.

什么是“外部性”？

What is an "externality"?

外部性指一个人的行为给不相关的旁人带来好处或坏处，而双方并没有为此付钱或收钱。
An externality is when someone's action creates benefits or harms for uninvolved bystanders, without money changing hands.
