什么是“举证责任”？为什么“谁主张谁举证”？

What is the “burden of proof” and why “he who asserts must prove”?

举证责任指由哪一方提供证据证明主张，通常“谁主张谁举证”以避免空口指控。但在部分侵权案件中，法律会倒置举证以保护法益弱势方。
The burden of proof is who must supply evidence; usually “he who asserts must prove” to avoid bare accusations. But some tort cases reverse it to protect the weaker party’s interests.

什么是“格式条款”？它为何受法律特别规制？

What is a “standard-form clause” and why is it specially regulated?

格式条款是商家预先拟定、对方不能协商的条款（如免责声明）。为防止强者欺弱，法律要求显著提示并排除不合理免责，否则可能无效。
A standard-form clause is pre-drafted by one party with no negotiation (e.g., disclaimers). To prevent the strong exploiting the weak, law requires prominent notice and voids unreasonable exemptions.

购物“七天无理由退货”有哪些例外？

What are exceptions to the “7-day no-reason return” rule?

定作、鲜活易腐、拆封音像/软件、报纸期刊及个人卫生用品等通常不适用无理由退货。它平衡消费者反悔权与商家防滥用。
Custom-made, perishable, opened audio/video or software, newspapers, and personal hygiene items usually are excluded. The rule balances buyers’ regret rights with sellers’ abuse prevention.

什么是“知识产权”？它保护什么？

What is “intellectual property” and what does it protect?

知识产权保护人类智力成果，如著作权、专利、商标，赋予创作者一定期限独占权以激励创新。盗版与抄袭即侵犯此类权利。
Intellectual property protects human creations—copyright, patents, trademarks—granting creators time-limited exclusivity to spur innovation. Piracy and plagiarism violate it.

签合同前为什么要仔细看条款？

Why read contract terms carefully before signing?

合同一旦签署即具法律效力，模糊或不利条款可能让你承担额外义务。重点看价格、期限、违约责任与解除条件。
Once signed, a contract is legally binding; vague or unfair clauses may add obligations. Focus on price, term, breach liability, and exit conditions.

什么是“隐私权”？它在手机时代为何更重要？

What is the “right to privacy” and why does it matter more in the phone era?

隐私权保护个人生活安宁与不愿公开的信息不被非法收集散布。手机App频繁索取位置、通讯录，使数据与隐私保护成为日常议题。
Privacy protects personal tranquility and information one does not wish exposed from unlawful collection or disclosure. Apps constantly request location and contacts, making data privacy an everyday issue.

什么是“正当防卫”？它有什么边界？

What is “justifiable defense” and what are its limits?

正当防卫指对正在进行的不法侵害采取制止行为，不负刑事责任，但需针对不法侵害且不过度。明显超过必要限度造成重大损害属防卫过当。
Justifiable defense is stopping an ongoing unlawful attack; it bears no criminal liability but must target the attack and not exceed necessity. Clearly excessive force causing grave harm is over-defense.

未成年人打赏主播或大额充值，钱能退吗？

Can money be refunded if a minor tips streamers or makes large in-app purchases?

不满八周岁为无民事行为能力人，其充值原则上无效可退；八周岁以上限制民事行为能力人，超出年龄智力的消费需监护人追认，否则可主张返还。实践中需举证。
Children under 8 lack civil capacity, so their top-ups are void and refundable; those 8+ are limited, and spending beyond their age and intellect needs guardian ratification or can be reclaimed. Proof is needed in practice.

为什么拍照发别人照片到网上要先征得同意？

Why ask permission before posting others’ photos online?

未经同意公开他人肖像可能侵犯肖像权与隐私权，尤其涉及孩童或私密场景。征得同意是基本的数字礼仪与法律常识。
Posting someone’s portrait without consent may violate portrait and privacy rights, especially for children or private scenes. Asking first is basic digital etiquette and legal common sense.
