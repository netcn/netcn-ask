全球税收协调（如全球最低企业税）要解决什么？

What problem does global tax coordination (e.g., a global minimum tax) address?

为遏制跨国企业向低税地转移利润（税基侵蚀），OECD推动15%全球最低企业税，减少“逐底竞争”，但仍面临执行与主权分歧。
To curb profit-shifting to low-tax jurisdictions (BEPS), the OECD pushed a 15% global minimum corporate tax, reducing the “race to the bottom,” though enforcement and sovereignty disputes remain.

为什么全球气候合作面临“集体行动困境”？

Why does global climate cooperation face a “collective action dilemma”?

减排具全球公共品属性：各国都想“搭便车”享受他人减排成果而不愿承担成本，导致整体努力不足；需通过可核查的协议与惩罚机制缓解。
Emissions cuts are a global public good; each state free-rides on others’ efforts, yielding under-provision. Verifiable agreements and enforcement help overcome it.

什么是跨境数据流动治理？

What is cross-border data-flow governance?

数据跨越国界带来隐私、安全与主权争议。各国通过数据本地化、充分性认定与协议（如跨境隐私规则）来平衡流通与监管。
Cross-border data raises privacy, security, and sovereignty disputes. States balance flow and control via data localization, adequacy decisions, and frameworks like the CBPR.

联合国安理会常任理事国否决权如何塑造国际行动？

How does the UN Security Council veto shape international action?

五常的一票否决使安理会在涉及大国核心利益时易陷于瘫痪，难以对强权冲突采取强制行动，推动在人道干预等议题上寻找替代机制。
The P5 veto lets any permanent member block action, often paralyzing the Council on conflicts touching great-power interests and prompting alternative mechanisms for humanitarian intervention.

什么是“软实力”与“多边主义”？

What are “soft power” and “multilateralism”?

软实力指通过文化、价值观与制度吸引力而非强制来影响他国；多边主义指多个国家依共同规则协调，常见于联合国与WTO框架。
Soft power influences others via culture, values, and institutions rather than coercion; multilateralism coordinates multiple states by shared rules, as in the UN and WTO.

什么是全球治理？

What is global governance?

全球治理指国家、国际组织、企业与非政府主体为应对跨国问题（气候、疫情、网络）而协调规则与行动的过程，并不依赖单一世界政府。
Global governance is the coordination of rules and actions by states, international organizations, firms, and NGOs to address transnational issues (climate, pandemics, cyberspace)—without a single world government.
