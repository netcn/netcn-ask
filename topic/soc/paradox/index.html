为什么在集体里“多劳”未必“多得”？

Why needn't 'more work' mean 'more reward' in a group?

在集体中，个人产出难以精确计量，回报又常按共享或身份分配，于是努力与所得并不严格对应。搭便车者能坐享他人劳动，而贡献者得到的回报被稀释——这是典型的集体行动困境。
In groups, individual output is hard to measure and rewards are often shared or assigned by status, so effort is not strictly matched to return. Free-riders can benefit from others' work while contributors see diluted rewards—a classic collective-action problem.

为什么把信息全公开，有时反而会降低信任？

Why can full transparency sometimes lower trust?

未经筛选的全透明会把复杂细节与琐碎瑕疵一并摊开，让人误以为机构比实际更混乱或无能。信任来自连贯的叙事与被感知的能力，而不只是公开信息的数量。
Raw, unfiltered transparency can overwhelm people with complexity and expose every minor flaw, making institutions look more chaotic or incompetent than they are. Trust rests on a coherent narrative and perceived competence, not merely on the volume of information disclosed.

为什么选择越多，人有时反而越不快乐？

Why can more choices make people less happy?

心理学家发现“选择过载”：选项太多会让人犹豫、担心选错，事后也更容易后悔。少而精的选择常比多而杂更让人踏实。
Psychologists found 'choice overload': too many options cause hesitation, fear of wrong choice and later regret. Fewer, better choices often feel safer than many messy ones.

为什么惩罚变重，犯罪却未必减少？

Why needn't crime fall just because punishment gets harsher?

威慑取决于“被抓的概率×代价”。若破案率低，再重的刑罚也难吓住人；何况贫困、成瘾等根因不解决，惩罚只是事后补救。
Deterrence depends on caught-probability times cost. With low clearance rates, harsher penalties scare few; and without fixing poverty or addiction, punishment is only after-the-fact.
