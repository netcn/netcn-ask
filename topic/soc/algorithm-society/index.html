算法偏见通常从哪里来？

Where does algorithmic bias usually come from?

偏见多源于有偏的训练数据、被放大的历史歧视、目标函数只优化点击等窄指标，以及设计者的隐含假设；它并不“中立”，需审计与多样数据来纠正。
Bias often comes from skewed training data, amplified historical discrimination, narrow goals like click optimization, and designers' hidden assumptions; it isn't “neutral” and needs auditing and diverse data to correct.

算法推荐如何塑造人们看到的信息？

How does algorithmic recommendation shape the information people see?

推荐系统按用户过往行为推送相似内容以提升停留，可能形成“信息茧房”与极端化；它既满足兴趣，也限制了偶遇不同观点的机会。
Recommenders push similar content by past behavior to boost engagement, possibly creating “filter bubbles” and polarization; they satisfy interests but limit chance encounters with differing views.

什么是“算法社会”？

What is an "algorithmic society"?

算法社会指日常决策——如新闻推送、贷款审批、招聘筛选——越来越多由算法自动完成，技术逻辑深度嵌入社会运行。
An algorithmic society is one where daily decisions—news feeds, loan approvals, hiring screens—are increasingly automated, with technical logic woven into social life.
