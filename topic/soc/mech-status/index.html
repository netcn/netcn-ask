为什么“炫耀性消费”在阶层快速上升时往往更明显？

Why is conspicuous consumption often sharper during rapid class rise?

新晋阶层缺乏传统的身份标识，便通过可见的消费——奢侈品、品牌、排场——来宣示地位。当流动性快、旧有地位信号薄弱时，炫耀性消费就成了快速确认与彰显阶层的最直接方式。
Newly rising groups lack traditional status markers, so they signal their new position through visible consumption—luxury goods, brands, displays. When mobility is fast and old status cues are weak, conspicuous spending becomes the quickest way to claim and confirm rank.

为什么“低调炫耀”在精英群体里更流行？

Why is 'quiet bragging' more common among elites?

成熟精英的地位本已稳固且被公认，过分张扬反而显得俗气或招致审视；于是他们用低调信号——内敛的品牌、圈内人才懂的暗语——向能读懂的人彰显格调。低调炫耀既确认了身份，又规避了炫耀带来的社交代价。
Established elites already hold secure, recognized status, so overt display can look vulgar or draw scrutiny; instead they use subtle signals—understated brands, insider references—to convey distinction to those who can read them. Quiet signaling affirms rank while avoiding the social cost of flaunting it.

为什么人会用消费来“显示”自己的身份地位？

Why do people use consumption to 'signal' their status?

社会学家凡勃伦称其为“炫耀性消费”：昂贵物品向他人发出“我有资源”的信号，帮助获得尊重、伴侣或机会。消费因此不只是实用，也是地位沟通。
Sociologist Veblen called it 'conspicuous consumption': costly goods signal 'I have resources' to others, aiding respect, mates or opportunities. Consumption thus communicates status, not just utility.

为什么“送礼”在人际关系中很重要？

Why is 'gift-giving' important in relationships?

礼物是关系的“证据”：它制造人情与亏欠，绑定双方未来互帮。人类学家莫斯指出，礼物往往附带“必须回礼”的社会义务。
A gift is proof of a tie: it creates favor and obligation, binding future mutual help. Anthropologist Mauss noted gifts carry a social duty to reciprocate.

为什么比赛要发奖牌、证书？

Why do competitions give medals and certificates?

奖牌证书把“你很棒”这件事变成看得见、可展示的符号，满足成就感，也向别人发出能力与努力的信号，激励人继续参与。
Medals and certificates turn 'you did well' into a visible, shareable symbol, satisfying achievement and signaling ability and effort to others, motivating continued participation.
