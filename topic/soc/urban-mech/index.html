城市规模分布遵循“齐普夫定律”吗？

Does city-size distribution follow “Zipf’s law”?

齐普夫定律指出城市人口排序与规模近似成反比（第2大城市约为第1大的一半）。许多国家大致符合，反映集聚与迁移的均衡机制。
Zipf’s law: a city’s rank times its size is roughly constant (2nd city ≈ half the 1st). Many countries roughly fit it, reflecting equilibrium in agglomeration and migration.

城市更新中的“绅士化”如何导致 displacement？

How does “gentrification” cause displacement in urban renewal?

中产迁入改善老旧街区会推高房价与租金，原低收居民被迫迁出（绅士化），带来空间不平等与文化置换，需以可负担住房政策缓冲。
Inflow of middle-class residents upgrades old neighborhoods and raises housing costs, displacing low-income residents (gentrification), demanding affordable-housing policies.

什么是“城市蔓延”与“紧凑城市”？

What are “urban sprawl” and the “compact city”?

城市蔓延指低密度、依赖汽车的无序扩张，增加通勤与能耗；紧凑城市通过高密度混合用地提升公共交通效率与可持续性。
Sprawl is low-density, car-dependent expansion raising commuting and energy use; compact cities use dense mixed-use land to improve transit efficiency and sustainability.

城市热岛效应会带来哪些健康与能源影响？

What health and energy impacts does the urban heat island have?

城市因混凝土、沥青吸热且绿地少，气温常高于周边郊区（热岛效应）。它加剧中暑与心脑血管风险、推高空调能耗，可通过增加绿地、反光屋顶与通风廊道缓解。
Cities are warmer than suburbs (the urban heat island) due to heat-absorbing surfaces and less greenery. It raises heat-illness and cooling energy use, mitigated by green space, reflective roofs, and ventilation corridors.

什么是“15分钟城市”理念？

What is the “15-minute city” idea?

该理念规划居民在步行或骑行15分钟内可达工作、购物、教育与绿地，以减少通勤、提升生活品质与减排，已在巴黎等地试点。
The 15-minute city plans daily needs (work, shops, school, green space) within a 15-minute walk or bike ride, cutting commutes and emissions; piloted in Paris.

什么是“集聚经济”？它为何吸引人口进城？

What are “agglomeration economies,” and why do they attract people to cities?

企业和人才在城市集中可共享供应链、知识与劳动力池，降低成本和提升创新，形成集聚经济，是城市吸引人口的核心动力。
Firms and talent cluster in cities to share supply chains, knowledge, and labor pools, cutting costs and boosting innovation—agglomeration economies draw population to cities.
