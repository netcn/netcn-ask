分权如何与地方“政府竞争”相互作用？

How does decentralization interact with inter-local "government competition"?

分权后地方为吸引资本与人口，会在税收、服务和制度上相互竞争，可能提升效率，也可能压低环保与劳工标准；竞争效果取决于规则与转移支付等上层设计。
After decentralization, localities compete via taxes, services, and institutions to attract capital and people—potentially raising efficiency but also lowering environmental and labor standards; the outcome depends on upper-level rules and transfers.

财政分权有哪些潜在好处与风险？

What are the potential benefits and risks of fiscal decentralization?

好处是地方政府更了解本地、便于试验与竞争（“用脚投票”）；风险是地区间财力不均、为招商而放松监管或陷入“逐底竞争”，以及宏观协调变难。
Benefits: local governments know local needs and can experiment and compete (“voting with feet”). Risks: uneven regional wealth, regulatory races to attract business, and harder macro coordination.

什么是“分权”？

What is "decentralization"?

分权指把决策和财政权力从中央下放到地方或基层政府，使更接近民众、回应本地需求。
Decentralization shifts decision and fiscal power from central to local or grassroots government, bringing it closer to people and local needs.
