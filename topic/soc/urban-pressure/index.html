城市的“集聚经济”与“集聚不经济”如何权衡？

How are urban "agglomeration economies" and "diseconomies" balanced?

集聚带来分工、知识外溢与低成本匹配，提升生产率；过度集聚则推高地价、通勤与污染，降低宜居度。城市规模的最优在于两者边际相等处。
Agglomeration brings division of labor, knowledge spillovers, and cheap matching that boost productivity; excess raises land prices, commuting, and pollution, hurting livability. The optimal city size is where their margins equalize.

为什么城市中心的气温往往高于周边郊区？

Why are city centers usually warmer than surrounding suburbs?

城市因混凝土、少植被和人为散热，气温常高于周边乡村，这称为“城市热岛效应”，夏季更明显；它加剧用电高峰与健康风险，尤其影响弱势社区。
Cities run hotter than surrounding countryside due to concrete, less vegetation, and human heat—the “urban heat island” effect, peaking in summer; it spikes power demand and health risks, hitting vulnerable neighborhoods hardest.

快速城市化会带来哪些压力？

What pressures does rapid urbanization bring?

城市扩张常超出住房、交通、供水和垃圾处理等设施的承载能力，导致拥挤、贫民窟、污染与公共服务紧张。
Rapid urban growth often outpaces housing, transport, water, and waste systems, causing congestion, slums, pollution, and strained public services.
