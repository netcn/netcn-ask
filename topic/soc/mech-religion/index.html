为什么“来世”信念有时能降低现实中的冲突成本？

Why can a belief in the 'afterlife' sometimes lower real-world conflict costs?

来世信念把现世的苦难重新定义为暂时的、并将正义留待日后奖赏，从而提高信徒对困境的忍耐，降低为眼前得失立刻抗争的冲动。这能在现实中缓解冲突的紧迫性，降低社会摩擦的治理成本。
A belief in the afterlife reframes present suffering as temporary and reserves justice for later reward, raising believers' tolerance for hardship and reducing the urge to fight for immediate redress. This can ease the urgency of conflict and lower the cost of managing social friction.

为什么在越来越世俗的社会里，宗教仪式仍然被保留？

Why are religious rituals kept even in increasingly secular societies?

仪式之所以延续，是因为它们标记人生转折、凝聚社群归属感，并提供世俗制度难以替代的意义感。即便不信教的人，也常把它们当作文化认同、家族传统或社会黏合剂来保留。
Rituals persist because they mark life transitions, build a sense of community, and supply meaning that secular institutions do not fully replace. Even non-believers keep them as cultural identity, family tradition or social glue rather than purely religious practice.

为什么很多节日都和宗教或农时有关？

Why are many festivals tied to religion or the farming calendar?

农时决定播种收割的节点，古人借节日感谢自然、祈求丰收；宗教则把固定日期变成集体礼拜。节日因此既安排生产，也凝聚社群。
The farming calendar set sowing and harvest nodes; ancients used festivals to thank nature and pray for yield, while religion fixed dates for collective worship. Festivals thus scheduled work and bonded communities.

宗教在较早的社会里有什么实际功能？

What practical functions did religion serve in early societies?

宗教用共同神话与仪式凝聚群体、解释未知、规范行为（如禁忌），并在灾荒时提供心理安慰与互助。它把“为何活、如何共处”变成共享答案。
Religion bonded groups with shared myth and ritual, explained the unknown, guided behavior (taboos) and offered comfort and mutual aid in hardship. It turned 'why live, how coexist' into shared answers.

为什么人们喜欢聚在一起祈祷或参加庆典？

Why do people like to pray or celebrate together?

一起行动会让人感到“我们是一伙的”，大脑也会释放让人愉快的化学物质。集体仪式强化归属，孤独感随之降低。
Acting together makes people feel 'we are one group', and the brain releases feel-good chemicals. Collective ritual strengthens belonging and lowers loneliness.
