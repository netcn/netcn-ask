什么是“议程设置”？媒体如何影响我们“想什么”？

What is “agenda-setting” and how does media shape what we think about?

议程设置理论指媒体虽难决定你怎么想，却能通过报道量决定你想什么——高频议题会被公众视为重要。它揭示注意力也是一种被分配的资源。
Agenda-setting theory holds media rarely tell you how to think, but through coverage volume tell you what to think about—frequent topics seem important. It reveals attention as an allocated resource.

什么是“沉默的螺旋”？它如何影响公开表达？

What is the “spiral of silence” and how does it affect public expression?

该理论指当人感知自己的观点属少数时，会因怕被孤立而选择沉默，使多数意见显得更强、少数更沉默。社交媒体既能放大也能抑制此效应。
The theory says when people feel their view is minority, fear of isolation makes them stay silent, making the majority seem stronger and the minority quieter. Social media can amplify or dampen this effect.

为什么“反转新闻”越来越多？

Why are “plot-twist news” (rapid reversals) more common?

抢时效与流量压力下，片面信息先被放大，后续调查才还原全貌，造成“反转”。慢求证、等官方通报能减少被反复打脸。
Under pressure for speed and traffic, one-sided info is amplified first, then later investigation reveals the full story, causing “reversals.” Slow verification and waiting for official notice reduce whiplash.

什么是“深伪（deepfake）”技术的风险？

What are the risks of “deepfake” technology?

深度伪造用AI生成以假乱真的音视频，可被用于诈骗、造谣与操纵舆论。辨别需看破绽、查证原始出处，平台与法律也在加强治理。
Deepfakes use AI to fabricate convincing audio/video, enabling scams, rumors, and opinion manipulation. Spot flaws and verify the original source; platforms and laws are tightening governance.

什么是“谣言”？如何初步辨别？

What is a “rumor” and how to spot one at an early stage?

谣言是未经证实却广泛传播的信息。辨别可看来源是否权威、是否煽动情绪、是否缺乏具体时间与出处，必要时查证官方渠道。
A rumor is unverified yet widely spread information. Check whether the source is authoritative, whether it stirs emotion, and whether it lacks specific time and origin; verify via official channels if needed.

什么是框架理论？

What is framing theory?

框架理论关注媒体如何通过选取、强调与编排信息的角度，来塑造受众对事件的理解。同一事件用“事故”或“悲剧”框架呈现，会引发不同的公众反应。
Framing theory examines how the media shapes understanding by selecting, emphasizing, and organizing angles. The same event framed as an “accident” versus a “tragedy” evokes different public reactions.

什么是“标题党”？它为何有害？

What is “clickbait” and why is it harmful?

标题党用夸张、隐瞒或误导的标题吸引点击，内容常名不副实。它消耗注意力、放大焦虑，长期损害媒体公信力。
Clickbait uses exaggerated, withholding, or misleading headlines to draw clicks while the content under-delivers. It wastes attention, amplifies anxiety, and erodes media credibility.

什么是回音室效应？

What is the echo chamber effect?

回音室效应指人们只接触与自己观点一致的信息，使原有立场不断被强化、异见被隔绝的现象，常由算法推荐与同温层社交放大，加剧极化。
The echo chamber effect is when people encounter only like-minded information, reinforcing their views and shutting out dissent—amplified by recommendation algorithms and homogeneous social circles, deepening polarization.

为什么同一件事，不同App推送的说法常不一样？

Why do different apps report the same event differently?

各平台选题、立场与算法不同，会放大不同侧面；加之自媒体二次加工，信息出现偏差。多看几个信源有助于拼出更全的图景。
Different platforms choose angles, stances, and algorithms differently, amplifying distinct facets; self-media rework content, causing skew. Checking several sources helps piece together a fuller picture.

为什么手机上的新闻“越来越像我想看的”？

Why does news on my phone seem “more and more like what I want to see”?

平台用算法根据用户点击与停留推送相似内容，形成“信息茧房”。它提升黏度却也收窄视野，让人难见不同观点。
Platforms push similar content by your clicks and dwell time, forming a “filter bubble.” It boosts engagement but narrows the view and hides differing views.
