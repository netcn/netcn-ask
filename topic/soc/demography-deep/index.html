人口负增长会触发哪些经济反馈？

What economic feedbacks does population decline trigger?

劳动力收缩推高单位人力成本、养老负担加重并可能压低住房与消费，但也可能提升人均资本与自动化替代；净效应取决于生产率与政策。
Shrinking labor raises per-worker costs and pension burdens and may lower housing/consumption, yet can raise capital per head and automation; net effect depends on productivity and policy.

什么是“第二次人口转型”？

What is the “second demographic transition”?

指20世纪后期起避孕普及、同居与非婚生育上升、晚育与低生育成为常态、家庭形式多元化，反映价值个体化与性别角色变化。
Since the late 20th c., contraception, cohabitation, nonmarital births, delayed and low fertility, and diverse families rose—reflecting individualization and shifting gender roles.

移民的“选择性”如何影响来源国与接收国？

How does migrant “selectivity” affect origin and destination countries?

移民往往更年轻、教育更高（正向选择）或特定群体集中，对来源国造成人才外流、对接收国补充技能，重塑两地人力资本结构。
Migrants tend to be younger and better-educated (positive selection) or clustered, causing brain drain at origin and skill supply at destination, reshaping human capital.

为什么有些国家人越来越多、有些越来越少？

Why do some countries grow in population while others shrink?

出生多、去世少的地方人口会增加；相反，生育少又寿命长的地方可能变少。迁移也会让一些地方人数变化。
Where births exceed deaths, population grows; where births are few and lives long, it may shrink. Migration also shifts numbers.
