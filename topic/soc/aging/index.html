“长寿风险”是什么意思？

What does "longevity risk" mean?

长寿风险指人比预期活得更久，导致退休储蓄和年金不足以支撑的风险；对家庭是耗尽积蓄，对保险公司与社保则是给付超预期，需更谨慎的精算与储备。
Longevity risk is outliving expected lifespan so savings and annuities fall short—depleting household funds and forcing insurers and social security to pay longer, calling for prudent actuarial reserves.

老龄化对养老金体系有什么压力？

What pressure does aging put on pension systems?

退休者增多、缴费者相对减少，使现收现付式养老金的抚养比恶化，收支缺口扩大，可能需要提高缴费、延迟退休或增加财政补贴。
More retirees and fewer contributors worsen the support ratio of pay-as-you-go pensions, widening deficits—possibly requiring higher contributions, later retirement, or more fiscal subsidy.

什么是“人口老龄化”？

What is "population aging"?

人口老龄化指老年人口比重上升、整体年龄变大的过程，主要由生育率下降和寿命延长共同推动。
Population aging is the rising share of older people and a graying population, driven mainly by falling fertility and longer lifespans.
