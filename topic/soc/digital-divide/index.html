算法会如何加剧数字鸿沟？

How can algorithms worsen the digital divide?

推荐与风控算法若以多数群体数据训练，可能对弱势群体服务更差、信息更窄或授信更少；加上数字技能落差，弱势者更难从数字红利中获益，差距被放大。
Recommendation and scoring algorithms trained on majority data can serve disadvantaged groups worse, narrow their information, or deny them credit; combined with skill gaps, the vulnerable gain less from digital dividends, widening the divide.

在网络接入渐趋普及之后，研究者为何宣称数字鸿沟并未消失、只是下沉到了第二个层次？

As internet access became widespread, why did researchers insist the digital divide had not disappeared but merely sunk to a second level?

因为不平等的重心从能否接入转到接入之后的技能与用途之别。机理：哈吉泰伊测量青少年网民后发现，上网时长之外，家庭的教育与文化资源左右着检索、浏览、甄别信息等技能高下，构成第二级鸿沟；范迪克的占有模型再把接入拆成动机、物质、技能、使用四道依次过滤的门槛，社会经济地位沿门槛层层折算为在线获益能力的差距，线上所得又回流线下教育与求职，鸿沟于是换位延续。
Because the center of gravity moved from whether one connects to differences in skill and use after connecting. Measuring teenage users, Hargittai found that beyond time online, family educational and cultural resources govern skills such as searching, browsing, and sifting information—a second-level divide. Van Dijk's appropriation model then splits access itself into four sequential thresholds—motivation, material access, skills, usage—along which socioeconomic status converts stepwise into unequal capacity to profit online, and online gains feed back into offline schooling and job seeking, so the divide persists in a new place.

#数字鸿沟 #互联网技能 #不平等再生产

当接入与技能层面的差距逐步缩小时，研究者为何还要提出存在第三个层次的数字鸿沟？这一层次衡量的又是什么？

As gaps in access and skills narrowed, why did researchers still posit a third level of the digital divide—and what does that level measure?

因为真正的分野已移到结果端：它衡量的是同样会上网的人在线下兑现收益的差距。机理：范德森与赫尔斯珀发现，接入普及、技能趋同之后，社会经济地位仍在折算为数字红利的落差——优势群体更善于把网络用于增进资本的用途，如检索健康信息、办理电子政务、经营职业网络，弱势者则更多停留于娱乐消遣；线下资源决定谁能把点击兑换成现实收益，互联网由此更像放大既有不平等的杠杆而非均衡器。
Because the decisive cleavage moved to outcomes: this level measures how unequally equally connected users convert going online into real-world gains. Van Deursen and Helsper showed that once access is widespread and skills converge, socioeconomic position still translates into unequal digital dividends—advantaged groups excel at capital-enhancing uses such as finding health information, dealing with e-government, and cultivating professional networks, while disadvantaged users cluster around entertainment. Offline resources decide who turns clicks into tangible benefits, making the internet less a leveler than a lever amplifying existing inequality.

数字鸿沟包含哪些层面？

What dimensions does the digital divide include?

一是“接入鸿沟”（有无网络与设备），二是“技能鸿沟”（能否有效使用），三是“使用鸿沟”（用网络做什么、收益多少）；后两层在接入普及后更显关键。
First an “access gap” (having network and devices), then a “skills gap” (using them effectively), and a “usage gap” (what and how much one gains); the latter two matter more once access spreads.

什么是“数字鸿沟”？

What is the "digital divide"?

数字鸿沟指不同群体在接触互联网、使用数字设备和获取数字技能上的差距，常按收入、地区、年龄和性别分布。
The digital divide is the gap between groups in accessing the internet, using devices, and gaining digital skills, often split by income, region, age, and gender.
