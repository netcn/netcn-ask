科举真的“公平”吗？它筛选出了什么、又漏掉了什么？

Was the imperial exam truly 'fair'? What did it select for, and what did it miss?

它相对世袭更公平，但偏向有财力长期读书的男性家庭，且以诗赋经义取士，偏好文学记忆而非技术、商业与实操能力。它塑造了文官精英，却也弱化了工商与技术阶层的上升通道。
It was fairer than heredity but favored male families who could afford years of study, and tested classical essays over technical, commercial or practical skill. It built a literary elite while narrowing paths for merchants, engineers and artisans.

为什么朝鲜、越南等东亚国家也采用了类似科举的制度？

Why did countries like Korea and Vietnam also adopt exam-like systems?

受中国科举与儒家文化圈影响，它们用考试选官以集中皇权、减少门阀，并形成“读书取仕”的共同价值。考试成了东亚官僚社会的标志性机制。
Influenced by China's exam system and the Confucian cultural sphere, they used exams to centralize royal power and curb hereditary clans, sharing the value that study leads to office. Exams became a hallmark of East Asian bureaucracy.

#东亚 #儒家圈 #制度扩散

什么是科举制度？

What was the imperial examination system?

科举是中国古代通过统一考试选拔官员的制度，约从隋唐延续到清末（1300多年）。它让普通人（理论上）可以靠读书考试进入官场，是早期以考试选才的制度。
The imperial examination was a system in imperial China that selected officials through standardized exams, lasting from the Sui-Tang era to the late Qing (over 1,300 years). In theory it let ordinary people enter government by studying and passing exams—an early merit-based selection system.

为什么科举制度能持续一千三百多年？

Why did the imperial examination system last for more than 1,300 years?

它把国家与读书人绑定：皇帝用考试换取精英的忠诚，读书人用功名换取地位，减少了地方豪强世袭。低成本的人才选拔加上文化认同，使其长期稳定。
It tied the state to scholars: the ruler bought elites' loyalty with degrees, and scholars gained status through study, weakening hereditary local strongmen. Cheap talent selection plus shared culture made it durable.

科举如何削弱了世袭贵族对官职的垄断？

How did the imperial exam weaken hereditary nobles' monopoly on office?

考试以文章而非出身取人，使寒门子弟也能入仕，稀释了世家大族的政治特权，官僚来源变得更分散，中央对地方的控制也更强。
Exams selected by writing, not birth, letting commoners enter office and diluting great clans' political privilege. The bureaucracy became more diverse, strengthening central control over localities.
