国际刑事法院（ICC）与主权豁免有何张力？

What is the tension between the ICC and sovereign immunity?

ICC可起诉个人战争罪，但常被指责侵蚀国家主权与豁免；美、中、俄等非缔约国不承认其管辖，限制其执行力与普遍性。
The ICC prosecutes individuals for war crimes but is criticized for eroding sovereignty and immunity; major non-parties (US, China, Russia) reject its jurisdiction, limiting its reach.

海洋法中的“领海”“专属经济区”与“公海”如何划分？

How are “territorial sea,” “EEZ,” and “high seas” delimited in the law of the sea?

沿海国领海至多12海里享有主权；专属经济区至200海里拥有资源主权但允许航行自由；200海里外为公海，属人类共同利益，由《联合国海洋法公约》规范。
Territorial sea (≤12 nm) is sovereign; the EEZ (≤200 nm) grants resource rights with freedom of navigation; beyond lies the high seas as common heritage, under UNCLOS.

什么是《日内瓦公约》？

What are the Geneva Conventions?

《日内瓦公约》是规范武装冲突中对待战俘、伤病员与平民的国际条约体系，奠定现代国际人道法，要求区分战斗员与平民。
The Geneva Conventions are treaties governing the treatment of prisoners, wounded, and civilians in war, founding modern international humanitarian law and the combatant/civilian distinction.

什么是“条约必须遵守”（pacta sunt servanda）？

What is “pacta sunt servanda” (treaties must be honored)?

这是国际法基本原则：合法缔结的条约对当事国具有约束力，须善意履行，构成国际秩序可预期性的基础。
A core principle: treaties in force bind parties and must be performed in good faith, underpinning the predictability of the international order.

国际法靠什么约束国家？

What enforces international law among states?

国际法缺乏世界政府，主要依靠国家同意、互惠、声誉、争端机制与有限制裁来执行；违反会损害信用并招致反制。
International law lacks a world government; it relies on state consent, reciprocity, reputation, dispute mechanisms, and limited sanctions; violations cost credibility and invite countermeasures.

何为“普遍管辖权”？它在实践中受限在哪？

What is “universal jurisdiction,” and where is it limited in practice?

普遍管辖权允许任何国家对严重危害人类罪等提起诉讼，不论发生地。实践中受政治意愿、证据获取与外交阻力限制，且常与主权原则冲突。
Universal jurisdiction lets any state prosecute grave crimes like genocide regardless of where committed; limited by political will, evidence, and sovereignty tensions.
