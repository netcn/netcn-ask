议题投票与政党认同投票在经验研究中有何消长？

What is the empirical shift between issue-based and party-identification voting?

坎贝尔等的「密歇根模型」强调稳定的政党认同主导投票；而后期议题投票研究（如 Nie、Verba 与 Petrocik 的《变化的美国选民》）显示随着政党极化，政策立场对投票的影响上升、认同黏性下降。
Campbell et al.'s Michigan model stressed stable party identification, while later issue-voting research (e.g., Nie, Verba and Petrocik's The Changing American Voter) shows that as parties polarise, policy positions weigh more and identification becomes less sticky.

为什么李普塞特认为经济发展与民主政体的存续密切相关？他给出了什么机制解释，后续研究又如何修正了这一论断？

Why did Seymour Martin Lipset tie the survival of democracy to economic development, what mechanisms did he propose, and how has later scholarship revised his argument?

李普塞特于1959年提出，富裕社会更易维持民主：经济增长扩大中产阶层，缓和阶级两极对立；教育普及滋养宽容与妥协；有效绩效为政府换来合法性，冲突得以纳入制度轨道。但普沃斯基等人的跨国研究发现，发展对民主的“催生”作用有限，更强的效应是“稳定”：民主一旦确立，便极少在高收入国家崩溃。现代化命题由此从单向因果改为条件性关联。
Lipset argued in 1959 that affluent societies sustain democracy more readily: growth enlarges the middle class and softens class polarization; mass schooling nurtures tolerance and habits of compromise; effective performance earns governments legitimacy, channeling conflict into institutions. Yet Przeworski and colleagues later showed, with worldwide data, that development rarely triggers democratization; its stronger effect is stabilization, since once established democracy almost never collapses in rich countries. The modernization thesis was thus recast from one-way causation into a conditional relationship.

理性选择视角下「投票悖论」（投票成本高于预期收益却仍投票）如何被解释？

How is the paradox of voting (costs exceed expected benefits yet people vote) explained in rational choice?

投票的预期个人收益极低（单票难定结果），理性人似不应投票。解释包括「表达性投票」、履行公民义务的心理收益，以及把投票视为公共品供给的集体行动修正。
The expected personal benefit of one vote is negligible, so a rational actor seemingly should not vote. Explanations include expressive voting, the psychological payoff of civic duty, and revised collective-action models treating voting as public-good provision.

中位选民定理的核心预测是什么？

What is the core prediction of the median voter theorem?

在单一议题、选民偏好单峰且两党竞争下，两大党会向中位数选民立场收敛以争取多数。唐斯将其纳入理性选择框架，说明政策趋同的来源。
Under single-issue, single-peaked preferences and two-party competition, both parties converge toward the median voter to win a majority. Downs embedded this in a rational-choice framework, explaining policy convergence.

什么是政治效能感？它与投票率的关系如何？

What is political efficacy, and how is it related to turnout?

政治效能感是个人相信政治体系会回应自己、自己行动有意义的感受。内在效能感越高，越倾向投票与参与，经验研究显示它是投票率的稳健预测因子。
Political efficacy is the feeling that the political system responds to you and that your action matters. Higher internal efficacy predicts greater voting and participation; empirical studies treat it as a robust predictor of turnout.

选票结构中的比例代表制与多数制对政党数量有何影响？

How do proportional and majoritarian electoral systems affect the number of parties?

杜瓦杰定律指出，单一选区多数制倾向于形成两党制，而比例代表制有利于多党并存。制度设计通过「机械」与「心理」效应塑造政党体系。
Duverger's law holds that single-member plurality systems tend toward two parties, while proportional representation favours multiparty systems. Electoral design shapes party systems through mechanical and psychological effects.

罗肯的「社会裂痕」（cleavage）理论如何解释政党体系的稳定？

How does Rokkan's social cleavage theory explain party-system stability?

罗肯认为民族、宗教、阶层与城乡等历史裂痕固化了政党阵营，使政党体系长期稳定。新建政党难以跨越既有裂痕重组，解释了欧洲多数政党格局的持久性。
Rokkan argued that historical cleavages—centre-periphery, church-state, class and rural-urban—froze party alignments, lending party systems long-run stability. New parties struggle to reconfigure across entrenched cleavages, explaining the durability of European party maps.

社会资本如何影响政治参与？

How does social capital affect political participation?

普特南指出，社区中的信任、网络与规范降低集体行动成本，提升投票与公民参与。社团成员更可能参与公共生活，体现为横向联系促进政治效能感。
Putnam argues that trust, networks and norms in communities lower the cost of collective action and raise voting and civic engagement. Associational members tend to participate more, as horizontal ties foster a sense of political efficacy.

马克斯·韦伯把合法权威分为哪几种类型？每种类型靠什么让人们服从？

How did Max Weber classify legitimate authority, and on what does each type rely to secure obedience?

韦伯区分了三种理想类型的合法权威。传统型依赖世代相传的习俗和对“历来如此”秩序的忠诚，如家长制和世袭王权；魅力型建立在追随者相信领袖具有超凡品质之上，通常需在领袖身后“例行化”为固定职务和规则；法理型以非人格规则为基础，服从由制度授权的职位而非个人，现代科层制是其典型。统治能否稳固，关键在于人们是否相信其正当性。
Weber distinguished three ideal types of legitimate authority. Traditional authority rests on inherited custom and loyalty to an order that has “always existed,” as in patriarchal rule and hereditary kingship. Charismatic authority depends on followers’ belief in a leader’s extraordinary gifts and must usually be routinized into stable offices and rules after the leader’s departure. Legal-rational authority rests on impersonal rules: obedience goes to institutionally authorized offices rather than persons, with modern bureaucracy as its paradigm.
