社会学的“因果推断”为何需要超越相关，借助自然实验与工具变量来识别效应？

Why must sociological causal inference go beyond correlation, using natural experiments and instrumental variables to identify effects?

观察数据常因混杂与选择偏差使相关不等于因果；自然实验利用外生政策冲击、工具变量借随机变异隔离因果通道。这些方法把社会学解释从联想推向机制验证。
Observational data confound cause with selection, so correlation is not causation; natural experiments exploit exogenous shocks and instruments isolate causal channels via random variation. These methods move sociology from association to mechanism testing.

社会调查中“抽样”为何要从概率抽样入手以保证代表性？非概率抽样在什么情形下仍可接受？

Why should social surveys start with probability sampling for representativeness, and when is non-probability sampling still acceptable?

概率抽样使每个总体单元有已知非零入选概率，从而可用统计推断到总体并量化误差。在探索性研究或难以框定总体时，方便或滚雪球抽样可作为补充，但结论不能外推。
Probability sampling gives each population unit a known nonzero chance, enabling statistical inference and error quantification. In exploratory work or when no sampling frame exists, convenience or snowball sampling is acceptable but cannot generalize.

“大数据”社会学在利用数字痕迹时面临哪些方法论陷阱，如选择性、情境缺失与算法偏见？

What methodological traps—such as selection, context loss, and algorithmic bias—does 'big data' sociology face when using digital traces?

数字痕迹并非随机样本，偏向特定平台与活跃用户；脱离访谈情境易误读行为；平台算法又塑造可见数据。研究者须把大数据与传统方法三角验证，避免把相关性当普遍规律。
Digital traces are not random samples and skew toward certain platforms and active users; stripped of interview context they mislead; platform algorithms shape what is visible. Researchers should triangulate big data with traditional methods rather than treat patterns as universal law.

民族志为何强调长期参与观察与“异文化”式的陌生化视角？它和问卷调查的互补价值在哪？

Why does ethnography emphasize long-term participant observation and a defamiliarizing 'stranger' perspective, and how does it complement surveys?

民族志通过沉浸式观察捕捉行动者自身的意义与日常实践，能发现问卷预设之外的机制。它与大规模量化互为补充：一个提供深度机制，一个提供广度代表性。
Ethnography captures actors' meanings and everyday practice through immersion, surfacing mechanisms surveys miss. It complements large-scale quantification: one supplies depth of mechanism, the other breadth of representativeness.
