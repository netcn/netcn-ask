格兰诺维特的“弱关系优势”命题为何主张找工作时弱关系比强关系更有效？它如何改变了我们对社会资本的看法？

Why did Granovetter argue 'the strength of weak ties' makes weak ties better than strong ones for job-seeking, and how did this reshape views of social capital?

弱关系连接不同社交圈，能带来强关系无法提供的非冗余新信息，因此更利于求职。该命题说明桥梁式联系比亲密纽带更具信息价值，是网络分析的奠基发现之一。
Weak ties bridge otherwise separate circles and carry non-redundant information strong ties lack, aiding job search. The claim shows bridging ties, not intimacy, carry informational value—a founding insight of network analysis.

伯特的“结构洞”概念如何解释占据网络间隙位置的人能获得信息与控制优势？

How does Burt's 'structural holes' concept explain why those spanning gaps in a network gain information and control advantages?

结构洞指网络中两群互不联结的人之间的空缺；占据其间的人充当经纪人，能优先获取异质信息并控制资源流动。伯特据此把创业与晋升优势归因于网络位置而非个人特质。
A structural hole is a gap between unconnected groups; the person spanning it acts as broker, gaining early heterogeneous information and control over resource flow. Burt attributes entrepreneurial and promotion advantages to network position, not individual traits.

森托拉关于“复杂传播”的研究为何发现强关系反而更利于扩散需要说服的行为（如健康行动）？

Why did Centola's work on 'complex contagion' find that strong ties are better than weak ones for spreading behaviors requiring persuasion, such as health actions?

复杂传播需要多次、可信的社会确认才会被采纳；强关系提供反复互动与信任，足以克服采纳门槛。与之相对，弱关系擅长扩散简单信息却难以推动需要改变习惯的行为。
Complex contagions require multiple, credible social reinforcements to be adopted; strong ties supply repeated interaction and trust to clear adoption thresholds. Weak ties spread simple information but fail at behaviors needing habit change.
