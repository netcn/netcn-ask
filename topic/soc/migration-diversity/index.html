奥米与维南特的“种族形成”理论如何把种族视为由政治斗争不断建构的社会项目，而非固定生物类别？

How does Omi and Winant's 'racial formation' theory treat race as an ongoing political project rather than a fixed biological category?

他们认为种族是借由“种族化”把历史、政治利益编织进身体分类的过程，由国家政策与反抗运动共同塑造。种族因此随权力格局变动而被持续再建构。
They argue race is produced through 'racialization' that encodes historical and political interests into bodily classifications, shaped by state policy and resistance. Race is thus continually remade as power relations shift.

社会融合的「分段同化」（segmented assimilation）理论如何解释移民后代的不同轨迹？

How does segmented assimilation theory explain divergent trajectories of immigrants' children?

波特的 segmented assimilation 认为二代移民并非单一同化，而是分化为向上同化、向下同化（落入贫困族群社区）与选择性适应（保留原生文化并向上流动）三条路径，取决于族裔资本与居住隔离。
Portes' segmented assimilation holds that the second generation does not assimilate uniformly but diverges into upward assimilation, downward assimilation (into poor co-ethnic enclaves) and selective acculturation (retaining origin culture while moving up), depending on ethnic capital and residential segregation.

移民对接收国本土工资的「因果效应」经验证据如何？

What is the empirical causal evidence on immigrants' effect on native wages?

卡德利用 1980 年「马里尔小船」自然实验发现迈阿密低技能本土工资未受显著冲击。多数准实验研究显示移民对本土工资的短期影响很小，长期取决于互补性。
Card used the 1980 Mariel boatlift natural experiment and found no significant hit to Miami's low-skilled native wages. Most quasi-experimental studies find immigrants' short-run wage impact on natives is small, with the long run depending on complementarity.

第二代移民的「融入悖论」指什么？

What is the integration paradox of the second generation?

融入悖论指第二代移民在出生地长大、语言文化更贴近主流，却可能因更高期望与现实落差、歧视感知而比父辈更强烈表达族群认同或不满。这挑战了线性同化假设。
The integration paradox is that second-generation immigrants, raised in the host country with mainstream language and culture, may express stronger ethnic identity or grievance than parents due to higher expectations and perceived discrimination. It challenges linear assimilation assumptions.

杜波依斯在《费城黑人》与“双重意识”概念中，如何既做实证研究又揭示种族化的内在体验？

How did Du Bois combine empirical research with the lived experience of racialization through The Philadelphia Negro and the concept of 'double consciousness'?

杜波依斯以系统入户调查写就美国首部城市社区社会学专著，量化就业、教育与居住隔离。他同时用“双重意识”刻画黑人在“美国人”与“黑人”两种自我间的撕裂体验。
Du Bois produced the first urban community sociology monograph via systematic household surveys, quantifying employment, education, and residential segregation. His 'double consciousness' captures the divided self of Black Americans between 'American' and 'Negro' identities.

海克特的内部殖民主义理论与现代化理论的族群趋同预期有何根本分歧?

What is the fundamental disagreement between Hechter's internal colonialism theory and the modernization expectation of ethnic convergence?

现代化理论预期工业化会以共同公民身份稀释族群界线,冲突只是发展阶段的残留物。海克特基于不列颠凯尔特边缘的历史指出,核心区对边缘族群的系统性剥削反而固化族群分工、强化认同并催生分离诉求。分歧因此在于:族群冲突会随现代化自然消解,还是被权力结构持续再生产。
Modernization theory expected industrialization to dilute ethnic boundaries through shared citizenship, treating conflict as a residue of development stages. Drawing on the Celtic fringe of Britain, Hechter showed that systematic exploitation of peripheral groups by the core hardens ethnic divisions of labor, thickens identity, and fuels separatist demands. The dispute is therefore whether ethnic conflict dissolves naturally with modernization or is continually reproduced by power structures.

国际移民的主要推力与拉力因素有哪些？

What are the main push and pull factors of international migration?

推力包括来源国的冲突、贫困与就业机会短缺；拉力包括目的地的工资差异、安全与家庭团聚网络。世界银行指出收入差距与迁移网络是持续迁移的结构性驱动力。
Push factors include conflict, poverty and job scarcity at origin; pull factors include wage gaps, safety and family networks at destination. The World Bank notes income gaps and migrant networks are structural drivers of sustained migration.

移民对接收国财政是净负担还是净贡献？

Are immigrants a net fiscal burden or contribution to host countries?

OECD 研究表明，移民总体对接收国财政多为小幅净贡献或中性，取决于年龄结构与技能构成；年轻、高技能移民长期更可能净贡献，老龄低技能短期或增加福利支出。
OECD research shows immigrants are mostly a small net fiscal contribution or neutral, depending on age structure and skills; young, high-skilled migrants are more likely to contribute net long run, while older low-skilled ones may raise welfare outlays short term.

侨汇（remittances）对来源国经济的主要作用是什么？

What is the main economic role of remittances for origin countries?

世界银行数据显示侨汇是许多发展中国家最大的外部资金流之一，直接改善家庭收入与减贫，但也可能削弱本地劳动激励并推高本地物价。
World Bank data show remittances are among the largest external financial flows to many developing countries, directly raising household income and reducing poverty, though they may also weaken local work incentives and raise local prices.

多元文化政策的主要目标是什么？

What are the main goals of multiculturalism policy?

多元文化政策旨在承认并支持少数族裔保持语言与文化，同时保障平等公民权，以缓解排斥、促进融入。但批评者担心它可能固化族群边界、削弱共同认同。
Multiculturalism policy aims to recognise and support ethnic minorities' languages and cultures while guaranteeing equal citizenship, easing exclusion and aiding integration. Critics worry it may entrench group boundaries and weaken common identity.

帕克的“族群关系周期”(race relations cycle)把移民与当地社会的互动划分为哪几个阶段?后来的学者如何批评这个理论?

Into which stages did Robert Park's 'race relations cycle' divide interaction between migrants and the host society, and how did later scholars criticize the theory?

帕克主张族群关系依次经历接触、竞争、适应与同化四个阶段,并将其视为基本不可逆的自然进程。格拉泽与莫伊尼汉基于纽约的研究反驳道,族裔认同并不随代际更替而线性消退,各群体反而通过新的政治动员主动维系自身边界。这类批评促使研究者转向关注权力不平等与制度性排斥,而不是把同化当作必然结局。
Robert Park proposed that ethnic relations move successively through contact, competition, accommodation, and assimilation, treating the sequence as a broadly irreversible natural process. Working on New York City, Nathan Glazer and Daniel Patrick Moynihan replied that ethnic identification does not fade linearly across generations, because groups actively sustain their boundaries through new forms of political mobilization. Such criticism pushed research toward power asymmetries and institutional exclusion instead of treating assimilation as the inevitable endpoint.
