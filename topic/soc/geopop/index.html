人口转变理论描述什么过程？

What does the demographic transition theory describe?

它描述社会从高低出生、高低死亡，经死亡率先降、出生率后降，最终走向低出生、低死亡的阶段演变。
It describes the shift from high birth and death rates through falling mortality then fertility, to a low-birth-low-death equilibrium.

板块构造如何塑造地形？

How does plate tectonics shape terrain?

岩石圈分成若干刚性板块，漂在软流圈上，靠俯冲下拉与洋脊前推带动，速度约每年几厘米，与指甲生长同量级；但千万年累积就是上千公里，够开合一个大洋。地貌随边界类型而定：碰撞带地壳缩短增厚成高山与高原；张裂带成洋中脊与裂谷；俯冲带留下海沟与火山弧。易混的是地形不只由内力刻成，抬升与侵蚀同时在跑，海拔是两者拉锯的结果；火山地震也大多贴着边界，板内与热点是例外。
The lithosphere is split into a handful of rigid plates riding the flowing asthenosphere, pulled down by subducting slabs and pushed along by ridges, at roughly a few centimetres a year, about the growth rate of a fingernail. But tens of millions of years add up to over a thousand kilometres, enough to open and shut an ocean whole. Landforms follow boundary type: collision shortens and thickens the crust into mountains and plateaus, rifting builds mid-ocean ridges and rift valleys, subduction leaves a trench and a volcanic arc. What gets mixed up is that interior forces do not carve the map alone — uplift and erosion run at the same time, so elevation is their tug of war, and volcanoes and quakes mostly hug the plate boundaries, with intra-plate and hotspot cases as exceptions.

为什么一些地区人口更密集？

Why are some regions more densely populated?

宜居气候、肥沃土地、河流与就业机会吸引人口，而极端地形与干旱则限制聚居。
Temperate climate, fertile land, rivers, and jobs attract people; harsh terrain and aridity limit settlement.

什么是粮食安全？

What is food security?

粮食安全指所有人随时随地都能获得充足、安全、营养的食物，以满足健康生活的需要。
Food security means all people have reliable access to sufficient, safe, nutritious food for a healthy life.

什么是人口迁徙？

What is migration?

迁徙指人跨越一定距离改变常住地的移动，可分为国内迁徙与国际迁徙。
Migration is the movement of people changing residence across a distance, either within or across countries.
