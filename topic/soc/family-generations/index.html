贝弗里奇式的福利国家在家庭照护上有何制度性盲点？

What institutional blind spot do Beveridge-style welfare states have regarding family care?

贝弗里奇模式以「男性养家」为隐性前提，将照护视为家庭私务，公共供给不足。女性主义福利研究指出这使女性被锁定于无酬照护，限制其劳动参与。
The Beveridge model implicitly assumes a male breadwinner and treats care as a private family matter, under-providing public care. Feminist welfare scholarship shows this locks women into unpaid care and limits their labour-force participation.

贝克尔的「数量-质量权衡」理论如何解释生育率随收入下降？

How does Becker's quantity-quality tradeoff explain falling fertility as incomes rise?

贝克尔认为父母在子女数量与对每孩的人力资本投入间权衡。收入上升时，投资质量的边际回报更高，家庭倾向少生优育，从而生育率下降。该框架是人口转型的经济学基础之一。
Becker argues parents trade off the number of children against investment in each child's human capital. As income rises, the returns to quality investment dominate, so families have fewer children but invest more, lowering fertility. This is a cornerstone of the economic theory of the demographic transition.

性别角色规范如何通过「性别分工」影响家务时间分配？

How do gender-role norms shape housework time allocation through the gender division of labour?

传统性别规范将无偿家务与照护默认为女性责任，使女性劳动时间呈「第二班」叠加。OECD 时间利用调查显示即便双职工家庭，女性家务时数仍显著更高。
Traditional gender norms default unpaid domestic and care work to women, creating a 「second shift」 on top of paid work. OECD time-use surveys show that even in dual-earner households, women's housework hours remain significantly higher.

吉登斯所说的“纯粹关系”如何概括当代亲密关系的走向,又带来哪些新的张力?

How does Giddens' notion of the pure relationship characterize the trajectory of contemporary intimacy, and what new tensions does it bring?

吉登斯以“纯粹关系”指代仅因双方彼此满足而延续、可随时协商退出的亲密关系,婚姻的核心由制度约束转向情感承诺。贝克夫妇进一步指出,个体化进程让人既追求自我实现又须维系承诺,由此出现“爱的正常混乱”。这一转向抬升了对关系质量的期待,却同时削弱了稳定性,家庭形态随之趋于多样,离婚与再婚同步增多。
Giddens coined the pure relationship to describe intimacy sustained only through each partner's satisfaction and open to negotiated exit, shifting marriage's core from institutional constraint to emotional commitment. Ulrich Beck and Elisabeth Beck-Gernsheim add that individualization pushes people to seek self-realization while holding on to commitments, producing the normal chaos of love. This shift raises expectations for relationship quality yet undercuts stability, diversifying family forms as divorce and remarriage climb together.

什么是代际支持？它在老龄化社会中的作用是什么？

What is intergenerational support, and what role does it play in ageing societies?

代际支持指家庭内不同世代间的经济、照料与情感互助，如子女赡养父母、祖辈协助育儿。在养老金不足的老化社会中，它构成非正式社会保障的重要补充。
Intergenerational support is the economic, caregiving and emotional mutual aid across generations within families, such as children supporting parents or grandparents helping with childcare. Where pensions are thin, it supplements informal social security in ageing societies.

总和生育率（TFR）达到更替水平是多少？低于它意味着什么？

What total fertility rate (TFR) constitutes replacement level, and what does falling below it mean?

更替水平总和生育率约为 2.1（考虑死亡后的每对夫妇平均生育数）。长期低于该值会导致人口负增长与老龄化，联合国人口展望显示多国已持续低于此线。
The replacement-level TFR is about 2.1 (average births per woman net of mortality). Sustained below this leads to population decline and ageing; the UN World Population Prospects shows many countries persist below it.

人口老龄化对社会养老体系的主要压力是什么？

What is the main pressure of population ageing on pension systems?

老龄化提高老年抚养比，即每名劳动者需供养更多退休者，压缩缴费基数并扩大支出。联合国人口老化报告指出这迫使多国提高退休年龄或调整参数。
Ageing raises the old-age dependency ratio, meaning each worker supports more retirees, shrinking the contribution base while expanding outlays. The UN ageing report notes this forces many countries to raise retirement ages or adjust parameters.

什么是「家庭结构变迁」中的核心家庭化趋势？

What is the trend toward the nuclear family in family-structure change?

核心家庭化指家庭规模缩小、以夫妻加未婚子女为主，传统扩大家庭减少。城市化、流动与观念变化推动该趋势，并改变代际同住与赡养模式。
Nuclear-family formation means smaller households centred on a couple and unmarried children, with traditional extended families declining. Urbanisation, mobility and changing norms drive this, altering co-residence and care patterns.

家庭代际转移中「利他模型」与「交换模型」有何分歧？

What divides the altruistic and exchange models of intergenerational transfers?

利他模型（贝克尔）认为父母转移出于对子女效用的关切，会填补子女收入缺口；交换模型认为转移是对子女照料等服务的对价。两种逻辑对遗产税与赡养政策的预测截然不同。
The altruistic model (Becker) holds parents transfer out of concern for children's utility, filling income gaps; the exchange model sees transfers as payment for services like care. The two imply very different predictions for estate taxes and eldercare policy.

帕森斯关于核心家庭“结构—功能”分析为何认为现代家庭主要承担社会化与紧张舒缓两类功能？

In Parsons's structural-functional analysis, why did he argue the modern nuclear family mainly performs socialization and tension-management functions?

帕森斯认为工业化使家庭丧失生产功能，核心家庭集中于抚育子女（社会化）与为成年成员提供情感慰藉（紧张舒缓）。该观点后被批评忽视了家庭内部的权力与冲突。
Parsons held that industrialization stripped the family of productive functions, leaving the nuclear family to socialize children and provide emotional relief (tension management). Critics note it ignored intra-family power and conflict.

哈特曼的“家庭工资”概念如何揭示工资制度把性别分工嵌入资本主义再生产？

How does Hartmann's 'family wage' concept reveal how wage systems embedded gender division into capitalist reproduction?

哈特曼指出男性“家庭工资”制度以单一养家者为前提，把女性固定在无酬家务劳动上，使资本得以低薪再利用劳动力。她把父权制与资本主义视为相互支撑的体系。
Hartmann argued the male 'family wage' presumed a single breadwinner, fixing women in unpaid domestic labor and letting capital reuse cheap labor. She viewed patriarchy and capitalism as mutually reinforcing systems.

切尔林提出的婚姻“去制度化”是什么意思？它怎样改变了人们对亲密关系的期待？

What does Andrew Cherlin mean by the 'deinstitutionalization' of marriage, and how has it changed expectations about intimate relationships?

去制度化指婚姻作为现成规范脚本的力量衰弱：同居、离婚、婚外生育等过去被视为越轨的安排如今成了可接受的个人选项。伴侣关系的正当性不再来自角色义务，而取决于关系本身能否带来成长、亲密与自我实现，因而需要双方持续协商来维系。其结果是，婚姻从成年的必经门槛变成一种需要论证的“成就”，其稳定性也更依赖于关系质量而非外部规范。
Deinstitutionalization refers to the weakening of marriage as a ready-made normative script: cohabitation, divorce, and out-of-wedlock childbearing have shifted from deviant arrangements to acceptable personal choices. A couple's bond now derives legitimacy less from role obligations than from whether the relationship delivers growth, intimacy, and self-fulfillment, so it must be sustained through ongoing negotiation. Marriage consequently becomes an achievement to be justified rather than a compulsory rite of adulthood, and its stability rests more on relationship quality than on external norms.

老龄化社会中,照护劳动为何主要由家庭尤其是女性承担?这种安排带来哪些结构性风险?

In aging societies, why does care work fall mainly on families and especially on women, and what structural risks does this arrangement create?

比较福利研究显示,多数国家把家庭当作长期照护的默认单位,而性别分工又把无偿照护推给女性亲属。生育率下降和人口流动使可参与照护的家庭成员减少,照护缺口随之扩大。其后果包括女性被迫中断就业、养老金积累受损,以及公共照护服务供给不足的问题被持续掩盖。
Comparative welfare research shows that most countries treat the family as the default unit of long-term care, and gendered divisions push unpaid caring onto female kin. Falling fertility and population mobility shrink the pool of relatives able to give care, so the care gap keeps widening. Consequences include interrupted careers and weakened pension accrual for women, alongside the persistent masking of shortfalls in public care provision.

照护赤字为什么会从家庭难题上升为老龄化社会的公共议题?

Why does the care deficit rise from a family difficulty to a public issue in aging societies?

当老年人口增速超过家庭照护供给时,长期由女性无偿承担的照护出现结构性缺口,霍克希尔德以全球照护链描述富裕国家如何经由移民家务劳工跨国填补缺口。缺口同时迫使国家介入,推动长期照护政策与福利安排的重构。照护由此从私人家务转变为牵动性别平等、移民与福利国家的公共政治议题。
When older populations grow faster than family care supply, a structural gap opens in caregiving long provided unpaid by women, which Hochschild described through global care chains linking affluent households to migrant domestic workers across borders. The gap also forces states to step in, reshaping long-term care policy and welfare arrangements. Care thereby moves from private housework into public politics, entangling gender equality, migration, and the welfare state.
