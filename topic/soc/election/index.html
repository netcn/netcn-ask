什么是“选区划分”（gerrymandering）？

What is “gerrymandering”?

执政者通过刻意绘制选区边界使本党占优，称为杰利蝾螈。它可扭曲民意与代表性，部分国家以独立委员会划界以减少偏见。
Gerrymandering draws district boundaries to favor a party, distorting representation; some countries use independent commissions to reduce bias.

排序复选（ranked-choice）与两轮决选如何改变策略投票？

How do ranked-choice voting and runoffs change strategic voting?

排序复选允许按偏好排序、逐步剔除末位并转移选票，减少“弃保”策略；两轮决选则通过 runoff 迫使联盟形成，均影响选民与政党的策略行为。
Ranked-choice voting lets voters rank candidates and transfers eliminate last-place votes, reducing tactical “spoiler” voting; runoffs force coalition-building—both reshape strategy.

比例代表制与多数制有何区别？

What is the difference between proportional and majoritarian electoral systems?

多数制（如单一选区相对多数）易形成两党与稳定政府但代表性弱；比例代表制按得票比例分配席位，更反映多元诉求但易碎片化、组阁复杂。
Majoritarian systems (plurality) favor two parties and stable government but weak representation; PR allocates seats by vote share, mirroring diversity yet fragmenting and complicating coalitions.

什么是选举门槛？它如何影响小党？

What is an electoral threshold, and how does it affect small parties?

比例代表制常设得票门槛（如5%），未达标政党无法进入议会，旨在防止碎片化，但也可能排斥新兴小党与少数群体代表。
PR systems often set a vote threshold (e.g., 5%); parties below it are excluded, curbing fragmentation but possibly sidelining new or minority parties.

为什么投票重要？

Why does voting matter?

投票是公民选择谁来制定规则与分配资源的方式。每个人的票都微小，但汇总起来决定社区和国家的方向。
Voting lets citizens choose who makes rules and allocates resources. Each vote is small, but together they steer a community and country.
