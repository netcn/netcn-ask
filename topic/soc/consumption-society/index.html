计划行为理论（TPB）如何解释绿色消费意愿到行为的落差？

How does the Theory of Planned Behavior (TPB) explain the gap between green purchase intention and behavior?

TPB 认为行为由态度、主观规范与感知行为控制共同决定。绿色消费常因价格高、可得性差等「行为控制」不足而停滞，使高意愿难以转化为实际购买。
TPB holds behaviour is jointly determined by attitude, subjective norms and perceived behavioural control. Green consumption often stalls because weak control (high price, low availability) prevents high intention from becoming actual purchase.

「地位商品」（positional goods）为何会造成社会低效？

Why do positional goods cause social inefficiency?

弗兰克指出地位商品的价值取决于相对排名而非绝对数量，个体为领先而过度投入（如炫耀性教育开支），形成零和军备竞赛，社会总福利反而下降。
Frank argues the value of positional goods depends on relative rank, not absolute amount, so individuals over-invest (e.g., conspicuous schooling) to get ahead, creating a zero-sum arms race that lowers aggregate welfare.

鲍德里亚如何用「符号价值」重构消费社会学？

How did Baudrillard reconstruct the sociology of consumption with sign value?

鲍德里亚认为在后现代消费中，物品的价值主要来自其符号差异而非使用功能，消费是对「能指」的系统性操控。这把消费从需求满足转为身份编码的社会游戏。
Baudrillard argues that in postmodern consumption, objects derive value mainly from sign difference rather than use, and consumption is systematic manipulation of signifiers. This shifts consumption from need-satisfaction to a social game of identity coding.

什么是「消费降级」现象？它反映何种社会心态？

What is the consumption downgrading phenomenon, and what mindset does it reflect?

消费降级指在经济不确定下，消费者转向更务实、低价的替代品，弱化符号性炫耀。它反映风险预期上升时的防御性储蓄与去虚荣化倾向。
Consumption downgrading means consumers shift to pragmatic, cheaper substitutes and shed symbolic showiness amid economic uncertainty. It reflects defensive saving and de-vanity tendencies when risk expectations rise.

绿色消费的主要动机有哪些？

What are the main motivations behind green consumption?

绿色消费动机包括利他环保价值、自我形象提升与制度激励（如补贴、标签）。但「漂绿」使消费者难以辨别真实环境效益，常需第三方认证支撑信任。
Green-consumption motives include altruistic environmental values, self-image uplift and institutional incentives like subsidies and labels. But 「greenwashing」 makes real environmental benefit hard to judge, often requiring third-party certification to sustain trust.

凡勃伦的「炫耀性消费」指什么？

What does Veblen's conspicuous consumption refer to?

凡勃伦在《有闲阶级论》中指出，炫耀性消费是通过公开挥霍商品来彰显社会地位而非满足效用。它揭示消费的符号维度：商品成为阶层区隔的符号。
In The Theory of the Leisure Class, Veblen describes conspicuous consumption as publicly wasting goods to signal social status rather than satisfy utility. It reveals the symbolic dimension: goods become markers of class distinction.

消费主义如何与身份认同相互建构？

How do consumerism and identity co-construct each other?

消费文化提供用以表达「我是谁」的风格符号，个体通过品牌与品味选择建构身份。社会学研究指出这既赋予表达自由，也加深了以消费划分的阶层边界。
Consumer culture supplies style-signs for expressing who I am, and individuals build identity through brand and taste choices. Sociology notes this grants expressive freedom yet deepens class boundaries drawn by consumption.

布迪厄凭什么断言审美判断背后站着阶级位置?《区分》的调查证据勾勒出怎样的趣味层级?

On what evidence does Bourdieu rest the claim that aesthetic judgment tracks class position, and what hierarchy of tastes does Distinction sketch?

依据1960年代针对法国人的摄影、音乐偏好与博物馆参观调查,布迪厄发现趣味沿经济资本与文化资本的总量及构成整齐分布:文化资本雄厚的阶层偏爱克制而形式化的欣赏姿态,大众趣味系于必需品与直接快感,小资产阶级则在效仿正统中流露焦虑。趣味经由幼年家庭熏陶习得,却被当事人体验为天赋,于是把社会出身伪装成个人禀赋之别。这正是符号暴力嵌入日常生活品位的运作方式。
Drawing on 1960s surveys of French photographic, musical, and museum-going preferences, Bourdieu showed tastes distributing neatly along the volume and composition of economic and cultural capital: capital-rich classes favor detached formal appreciation, popular taste clings to necessity and immediate pleasure, and the petty bourgeoisie betrays anxiety while mimicking orthodoxy. Taste is absorbed through early upbringing yet lived as innate gift, disguising social origin as personal endowment, which is how symbolic violence settles into everyday consumption.
