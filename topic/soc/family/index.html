什么是“人口红利”？它为什么会消退？

What is the “demographic dividend” and why does it fade?

人口红利指劳动年龄人口占比高、抚养负担轻时，经济增长获额外推力。随着生育率下降与老龄化，红利窗口会关闭，转为“老龄化负担”。
The dividend is extra growth when the working-age share is high and dependency low. As fertility falls and aging rises, the window closes and turns into an aging burden.

为什么“随母姓”或孩子跟谁姓近年被更多讨论？

Why is “taking the mother’s surname” more discussed lately?

传统上子女多随父姓，反映父系宗法；随母姓的讨论体现性别平等与家庭协商意识增强。法律允许子女可随父或母姓，属家庭自主。
Traditionally children take the father’s surname, reflecting patriarchy; debate on maternal surnames shows rising gender equality and family negotiation. Law allows either parent’s surname as a family choice.

为什么许多国家鼓励生育却效果有限？

Why do many countries’ pro-birth policies have limited effect?

生育决策受育儿成本、女性就业、住房与教育压力共同影响，补贴难以抵消机会成本。观念从“多子多福”转向“少而精养”也降低了意愿。
Birth choices are shaped by child-rearing cost, women’s employment, housing, and education pressure; subsidies rarely offset opportunity cost. The ideal shifted from “more children, more blessing” to “fewer, better cared-for.”

什么是“赡养”与“扶养”义务？

What are “support” and “maintenance” obligations within family?

赡养指成年子女对父母的供养义务，扶养泛指家庭成员间（如夫妻、长辈对未成年晚辈）的经济与生活扶助。它们是法定义务，也是伦理基础。
Support (赡养) is adult children’s duty to parents; maintenance (扶养) covers mutual aid among family members, e.g., spouses or elders to minors. Both are legal duties and ethical foundations.

什么是“空巢家庭”？它带来哪些变化？

What is an “empty-nest family” and what changes does it bring?

指子女离家后只剩父母（尤其老人）同住的家庭形态，随城镇化与少子化增多。它带来老人照料、情感陪伴与独居安全的新挑战。
It is a household where children have left, leaving parents (often elderly) alone; it grows with urbanization and low fertility. It raises new challenges in eldercare, companionship, and solo-living safety.

为什么现在“核心家庭”（爸妈孩子）比“大家庭”更常见？

Why are “nuclear families” (parents and children) now more common than extended families?

城镇化、人口流动与个人主义兴起使年轻人独立居住，三代同堂减少。核心家庭更灵活但也弱化家族互助网络。
Urbanization, mobility, and individualism lead young people to live independently, reducing three-generation households. Nuclear families are flexible but weaken family mutual-aid networks.

什么是“老龄化社会”？怎么判断？

What is an “aging society” and how is it judged?

当一个国家或地区65岁及以上人口占比达到7%即为老龄化社会（联合国标准），达到14%为深度老龄化。它带来劳动力减少与养老压力。
By UN standard, a society is aging when those 65+ reach 7% of population, and deeply aging at 14%. It brings shrinking labor and pension pressure.

为什么老人带孙辈在不少家庭很普遍？

Why is grandparent childcare common in many families?

双职工家庭托育成本高、托育供给不足，祖辈协助既省钱又基于亲情。但也可能引发代际育儿观念冲突与老人负担。
Dual-income families face high childcare cost and short supply, so grandparents help cheaply and out of kinship. But it can spark intergenerational clashes and burden the elderly.
