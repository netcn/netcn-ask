法治指数通常测量哪些维度？

What dimensions do rule-of-law indices usually measure?

常见维度包括政府对权力的约束、腐败控制、秩序与安全保障、基本权利保护、监管与司法有效等；不同机构加权不同，因此排名仅供横向参考。
Common dimensions are constraints on government power, control of corruption, order and security, protection of rights, and regulatory/judicial effectiveness; weightings differ across providers, so rankings are only rough comparisons.

“法治”与“以法治国”有什么区别？

How does "rule of law" differ from "rule by law"?

法治强调法律约束政府、保障权利；以法治国只是把法律当作统治工具，政府自身可不受同等约束。前者限制权力，后者可能只是便利管理。
Rule of law binds government and protects rights; rule by law merely uses law as a tool of rule, with government possibly unbound. The former limits power; the latter may just ease administration.

为什么“程序正义”很重要？

Why is "procedural justice" important?

程序正义关注过程是否公平、透明、可参与，而不只看结果；即使结果有利，若过程不公正，公众也会质疑其正当性，削弱对制度的信任。
Procedural justice focuses on whether the process is fair, transparent, and participatory, not just the outcome; even favorable results lose legitimacy if the process is unjust, eroding trust.

什么是“法治”？

What is the "rule of law"?

法治指法律至上，政府和个人都要守法，法律公平对待所有人、保护基本权利，而不是由掌权者随意决定。
The rule of law means law is supreme: both government and citizens obey it, it treats everyone fairly and protects basic rights, rather than leaving decisions to rulers' whims.
