什么是“阶层固化”？

What is "class rigidity" (class reproduction)?

阶层固化指代际之间位置难以改变、优势与劣势在家族内传递的现象，常表现为教育、人脉和财富代际累积；它削弱社会流动，引发公平忧虑。
Class rigidity means positions hardly change across generations, with advantage or disadvantage passed within families via education, networks, and wealth—weakening mobility and raising equity concerns.

什么是“中产阶层”？

What is the "middle class"?

中产阶层一般指收入与教育处于社会中段、以专业或管理职业为主、有一定资产和稳定性的群体；不同国家界定标准差异很大。
The middle class generally sits in the income and education middle, working in professional or managerial roles with some assets and stability; definitions vary widely by country.

“客观阶层”和“主观阶层”有什么不同？

How do “objective” and “subjective” class differ?

客观阶层按收入、职业、学历等可测量的标准划分；主观阶层则是个人自认为属于哪一层。两者常不一致：有人收入高却自认“普通”，也有人认同高于自身条件。
Objective class uses measurable criteria like income, occupation, and education; subjective class is what people feel they belong to. They often diverge—some high earners feel “ordinary,” others identify above their means.

社会学里说的“阶层”指什么？

What does sociology mean by "social class"?

阶层是社会按财富、职业、教育等把人分成的不同等级，影响收入、生活方式和机会。
Class is society's ranking of people by wealth, occupation, and education, shaping income, lifestyle, and opportunity.
