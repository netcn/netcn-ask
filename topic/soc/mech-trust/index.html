为什么“高信任”社会做交易的成本更低？

Why do 'high-trust' societies have lower transaction costs?

当人们普遍相信他人会守约，就无需在监督、签约、法律执行和核验上投入过多。交易成本下降，更多交易得以更快更便宜地完成，从而提升整体经济效率。
When people generally trust others to keep promises, less effort is spent on monitoring, contracting, legal enforcement and verification. Lower transaction costs let more deals happen faster and cheaper, raising overall economic efficiency.

为什么“陌生人社会”更依赖制度信任，而不是熟人信任？

Why do 'stranger societies' rely more on institutional trust than acquaintance trust?

在庞大、流动、匿名的社会里，你无法认识大多数人，于是信任被委托给非人格化的制度——合同、法院、认证、货币。正是这些正式机制让与陌生人大规模合作成为可能。
In large, mobile, anonymous societies you cannot know most people personally, so trust is delegated to impersonal institutions—contracts, courts, certifications, money. These formal mechanisms are what make large-scale cooperation with strangers possible.

为什么“闲话”（八卦）对社会其实有用？

Why is 'gossip' actually useful for society?

闲话传播谁可信、谁不可信的信息，让不守规矩的人有声誉代价，从而无形中维持合作。它是最早期的“社会信用”机制之一。
Gossip spreads who is trustworthy, giving rule-breakers a reputation cost and quietly sustaining cooperation. It is one of the earliest 'social credit' mechanisms.

为什么在小社区里，口碑往往比广告更能影响消费？

Why does word of mouth beat advertising in small communities?

小社区里人们反复打交道，谁坑过人大家都知道，口碑是长期验证过的信号；广告只是一次信息，可信度低。信任来自重复互动而非宣传。
In small communities people meet repeatedly; everyone knows who cheated, so reputation is a long-verified signal, while ads are one-shot and less credible. Trust comes from repeated interaction, not promotion.

为什么人会在意“别人怎么看自己”？

Why do people care what others think of them?

因为人类靠群体生存，被群体接纳才安全。在意评价能帮我们遵守规则、维持关系，所以“在乎名声”是进化出来的生存本能。
Humans survived in groups, and acceptance meant safety. Caring about judgment helps us follow rules and keep ties, so 'caring about reputation' is an evolved survival instinct.
