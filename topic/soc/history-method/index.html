为什么历史有时会被重写？

Why is history sometimes rewritten?

历史会被重写，一方面因为新档案、考古或研究方法出现，推翻了旧结论；另一方面，历史叙述从来离不开“谁在写、为谁写”。掌握话语权的一方会强调某些事实、淡化另一些，以服务于国家认同或政治需要。所以历史既是过去的事实，也是不断被重新解释的叙事。
History gets rewritten for two reasons. New archives, archaeology, or methods overturn old conclusions; and historical narrative always depends on who writes it and for whom. Those holding the narrative spotlight certain facts and downplay others to serve national identity or political needs. So history is both the record of the past and a continually reinterpreted story.

什么是考古断代？

What is archaeological dating?

考古断代用碳-14测年、地层叠压关系等方法确定遗迹或器物的年代，帮助把发现物放进正确的时间顺序。
Archaeological dating uses methods like carbon-14 radiocarbon dating and stratigraphy to determine the age of finds, placing them in the correct chronological order.

历史学家如何判断史料真伪？

How do historians verify sources?

历史学家会核对不同来源是否一致、考察作者动机与时代背景、检验物质证据（如碳测年），并交叉验证以排除伪造或讹传。
Historians cross-check sources for consistency, examine the author's motive and context, test physical evidence such as carbon dating, and corroborate to rule out forgery or error.

什么是历史相对主义？

What is historical relativism?

历史相对主义强调：评判过去的人和事，应当放在当时的社会、文化与价值背景下，而不是用今天的标准简单否定。它提醒我们历史叙述受立场和时代限制，没有绝对中立的视角。但相对主义不等于“怎么解释都对”——证据和逻辑仍是检验说法的底线。
Historical relativism holds that past people and events should be judged in their own social, cultural, and moral context rather than by today's standards alone. It reminds us that every narrative is shaped by standpoint and era, with no perfectly neutral viewpoint. But relativism is not 'anything goes'—evidence and logic remain the baseline for testing any account.

什么是口述历史？

What is oral history?

口述历史是采访亲历者、记录他们回忆的史料方法，能保存没有被文字记载的普通人经历和集体记忆。
Oral history records interviews with witnesses to capture their memories, preserving ordinary people's experiences and collective memory absent from written records.

什么是第一手史料？

What is a primary source?

第一手史料是事件发生时或接近当时由亲历者留下的材料，如日记、信件、照片和官方档案，最能直接反映历史。
A primary source is material created during or near an event by those who experienced it, such as diaries, letters, photos, and official records, giving the most direct evidence.
