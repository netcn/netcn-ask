算法偏见如何在信贷审批中产生系统性歧视？其机制与缓解路径是什么？

How does algorithmic bias produce systemic discrimination in credit scoring, and what are its mechanisms and mitigation paths?

当训练数据本身反映历史上的歧视（如少数族裔获批率偏低），模型会把相关性误当作审批依据，并通过代理变量（如邮编）间接编码受保护特征。缓解需做偏差审计、引入公平约束，并在关键决策保留人工复核。
When training data reflect historical discrimination, say lower approval rates for minorities, the model learns proxies such as zip code that encode protected attributes and mistakes correlation for approval criteria. Mitigation requires bias audits, fairness constraints, and human review at critical decisions.

平台反垄断中的「守门人」规则如何运作？它与传统反垄断有何不同？

How do platform-antitrust 'gatekeeper' rules work, and how do they differ from traditional antitrust?

欧盟《数字市场法》（DMA）将具市场支配力的核心平台列为「守门人」，事前施加互操作、数据可携等义务，而不必等待事后证明垄断损害。它把监管重心从行为救济前移到结构性预防。
The EU Digital Markets Act designates dominant core platforms as 'gatekeepers' and imposes ex ante duties such as interoperability and data portability, without waiting to prove harm after the fact. It shifts regulation from behavioral remedies to structural prevention.

在平台经济中,算法如何重构零工劳动者的劳动过程与自主性?

How do algorithms restructure the labor process and autonomy of gig workers in the platform economy?

算法通过派单、评分与动态定价把管理权从科层组织转移到系统本身,劳动者名义上可以自由接单,实际上处处受制于不透明的规则。平台刻意维持信息不对称,例如不完全公开计价与派单逻辑,以此削弱劳动者的议价能力并加剧相互竞争。游戏化的等级、奖励与冲单任务则进一步把提高劳动强度转化为劳动者自我剥削的动力。
Algorithms shift managerial authority from bureaucratic organizations into dispatching, rating, and dynamic-pricing systems, so workers who nominally choose their own hours remain governed by opaque rules. Platforms deliberately preserve information asymmetry, for instance by withholding pricing and dispatch logic, to weaken bargaining power and intensify competition. Gamified tiers, bonuses, and order-streak tasks turn higher work intensity into self-driven exploitation.

什么是「回音室效应」（echo chamber）？算法如何强化它？

What is the 'echo chamber' effect, and how do algorithms reinforce it?

回音室效应指人们被封闭在重复自身观点的信息环境中，难以接触异见。个性化推送为追求互动而持续供给同质内容，使观点极化与错误信念更易固化。
The echo chamber effect describes being enclosed in information that merely echoes one's own views, with little exposure to dissent. Personalization feeds homogeneous content to maximize engagement, entrenching polarization and misbelief.

零工经济（gig economy）劳动者的社会保障缺口通常如何形成？

How does the social-protection gap for gig-economy workers typically arise?

平台以「独立承包」身份归类劳动者，使其脱离雇主缴费的社保与工伤体系，失去稳定缴费基础。算法派单的不透明与收入波动又使自愿参保能力下降，保障进一步弱化。
Platforms classify workers as independent contractors, excluding them from employer-funded social insurance and injury schemes and removing a stable contribution base. Opaque algorithmic dispatch and income volatility also lower their ability to self-insure, weakening protection further.

什么是数字鸿沟？它如何加剧已有的社会不平等？

What is the digital divide, and how does it deepen existing social inequality?

数字鸿沟指不同群体在接入、使用和从数字技术中获益方面的差距，可分为接入鸿沟、技能鸿沟和结果鸿沟。当教育、就业与公共服务日益线上化，缺乏数字能力的群体在机会获取上进一步落后。
The digital divide is the gap between groups in access to, use of, and benefits from digital technology, spanning access, skills, and outcomes. As education, employment, and public services move online, those lacking digital capability fall further behind in opportunity.

数字平台上的「知情同意」为何难以真正实现？

Why is informed consent on digital platforms so hard to achieve in practice?

隐私政策冗长、默认勾选与「接受或退出」的二选一，使同意常流于形式而非真实授权。当用户别无替代选项时，同意缺乏自愿性，难以构成有效的权利行使。
Lengthy privacy policies, pre-ticked boxes, and take-it-or-leave-it choices turn consent into a formality rather than real authorization. When users have no viable alternative, consent lacks voluntariness and fails as an exercise of rights.

什么是算法推荐？数字平台通常如何利用它影响用户的信息获取？

What is algorithmic recommendation, and how do digital platforms typically use it to shape users' information access?

算法推荐是利用用户行为数据（点击、停留、点赞）训练模型，预测并推送最可能吸引注意力的内容。平台借此提升用户黏性与广告收益，但也可能让个体越来越局限于符合其偏好的信息流。
Algorithmic recommendation uses behavioral data such as clicks, dwell time, and likes to train models that predict and surface the content most likely to capture attention. Platforms use it to increase engagement and ad revenue, but it can also trap individuals within information streams that confirm their existing preferences.

什么是数据权利？个人对自己的数字数据通常享有哪些权利？

What are data rights, and what entitlements do individuals typically hold over their digital data?

数据权利指个人对其被采集、存储和处理的数据所享有的控制与受益权，通常包括知情权、访问权、更正权和被遗忘权（删除权）。欧盟《通用数据保护条例》（GDPR）将这些权利制度化，要求数据处理以同意为基础。
Data rights refer to individuals' entitlements to control and benefit from data collected, stored, and processed about them, typically including the right to be informed, to access, to rectify, and to be forgotten. The EU's General Data Protection Regulation codifies these rights and makes consent the basis for processing.

平台的算法管理如何改变零工劳动者的工作体验与议价能力?

How does algorithmic management change gig workers' experience of work and their bargaining power?

网约车与外卖平台借助算法派单、动态定价和评分机制对劳动过程实施实时控制,学界称之为算法管理。这种安排把管理成本转移给劳动者,他们名义上时间自由,实则暴露于收入波动、超时接单与账号被单方面停用的风险之中。由于零工彼此分散、多与平台构成承揽而非雇佣关系,传统工会模式难以直接移植,劳动者更多依靠线上社群互助与集体法律行动维护权益。
Ride-hailing and delivery platforms exert real-time control over the labor process through algorithmic dispatch, dynamic pricing, and rating systems, which scholars call algorithmic management. This design transfers managerial costs onto workers who enjoy nominal schedule freedom yet face volatile earnings, extended hours, and unilateral account deactivation. Because gig workers are dispersed and usually classified as contractors rather than employees, standard union models transfer poorly, and workers lean on online communities and collective legal action instead.
