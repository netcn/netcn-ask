什么是“零工经济”？它带来哪些保障难题？

What is the “gig economy” and what protection challenges does it bring?

零工经济指通过平台接短单、灵活就业（如外卖、网约车），自由度高但常缺社保与劳动合同保护。它重新定义了雇佣关系，催生权益保障争议。
The gig economy means short, platform-mediated flexible jobs (delivery, ride-hailing): high freedom but often lacking social insurance and labor contracts. It redefines employment and sparks protection disputes.

什么是“同工同酬”？现实中为何难完全实现？

What is “equal pay for equal work” and why is it hard to fully achieve?

同工同酬指相同或同等价值工作应获相同报酬，旨在消除性别、户籍等歧视。但岗位评估难、议价能力差异与隐性偏见使其执行打折扣。
Equal pay for equal work means same or equivalent work gets same pay, aiming to remove gender, residency, and other discrimination. But hard job evaluation, unequal bargaining, and hidden bias blunt its execution.

什么是“试用期”，劳动者在试用期有哪些权利？

What is a “probation period” and what rights do workers have during it?

试用期是劳资双方相互考察的阶段，但劳动者仍受劳动法保护：合同、工资不得低于约定标准，解雇需有合法理由。不得随意“白干”或零薪酬。
Probation is a mutual evaluation phase, but workers are still protected by labor law: pay and contract must meet agreed standards, and dismissal needs lawful cause. Free or zero-pay work is not allowed.

为什么“加班”在不少行业被视为常态？

Why is overtime seen as normal in some industries?

项目制、考核导向与“在场文化”使加班被默认为努力象征，有时也因人手不足。但长期加班损害健康与效率，多地已立法限制工时。
Project-based work, metrics, and “presenteeism” make overtime a default symbol of effort, sometimes from understaffing. But chronic overtime harms health and efficiency; many places now cap hours by law.

什么是“远程办公”？它和在家办公一样吗？

What is “remote work”? Is it the same as working from home?

远程办公指不在公司固定场所完成工作，可在家、咖啡馆或共享空间。它依赖网络与协作工具，能省通勤但也可能模糊工作与生活界限。
Remote work means doing the job away from a fixed office—home, café, or co-space. It relies on network and collaboration tools: it saves commuting but can blur work-life boundaries.

为什么说“团队合作”不等于“人越多越好”？

Why is teamwork not “the more people the better”?

团队规模过大易出现沟通成本上升、责任分散（“社会惰性”）。研究认为小团队更灵活高效，规模需与任务匹配。
Oversized teams raise communication cost and diffuse responsibility (“social loafing”). Research suggests smaller teams are nimbler; size should match the task.

什么是“职业倦怠”？有哪些常见信号？

What is “burnout” and what are common signs?

职业倦怠是长期工作压力导致的身心耗竭，表现为情绪枯竭、效率下降与对工作的疏离感。它与单纯疲劳不同，需要休息与角色调整。
Burnout is exhaustion from chronic work stress, showing emotional depletion, falling efficiency, and detachment from the job. Unlike mere fatigue, it needs rest and role adjustment.

为什么很多公司强调“打卡”与考勤？

Why do many companies emphasize clocking in and attendance?

考勤是管理出勤与工时的最直接方式，便于核算工资与纪律。但它只衡量“在场”，未必反映产出，近年被弹性考核挑战。
Attendance is the most direct way to manage presence and work hours for payroll and discipline. But it only measures “being there,” not output, and faces challenges from flexible assessment.

为什么开会太多常被员工抱怨？

Why do too many meetings get complained about?

会议占用专注时间、打断工作流，若缺乏明确议题与结论就变成低效消耗。好的会议应有目标、纪要和后续行动。
Meetings steal focus time and break flow; without clear agenda and conclusions they become low-efficiency drains. Good meetings have goals, notes, and follow-ups.

面包师叔叔为什么天不亮就起床工作?

Why does the baker start working before sunrise?

因为面团发酵和烤面包需要好几个小时,要让人们在早餐时间买到热乎乎的面包,面包师就得在星星还在天上的时候开始干活。每个职业都在用自己的方式帮助别人。
Dough needs hours to rise and bake, so bakers start while stars still shine to have warm bread ready for breakfast time. Every job helps people in its own way.
