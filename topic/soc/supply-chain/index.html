近岸外包（nearshoring）为什么兴起？

Why has "nearshoring" been rising?

为降低远链运输风险、缩短交期并应对关税与地缘不确定性，企业把生产从远端转移到邻近地区；它提升韧性却常伴随成本上升与产能重新布局。
To cut long-haul risk, shorten lead times, and hedge tariffs and geopolitical uncertainty, firms move production closer to home markets; it raises resilience but often at higher cost and the need to rebuild capacity.

“准时制”（just-in-time）供应有什么风险？

What are the risks of "just-in-time" (JIT) supply?

JIT 通过减少库存提高效率，但也使生产高度依赖即时交付；一旦港口拥堵、灾害或地缘冲突打断运输，整条线会迅速停摆，韧性较弱。
JIT boosts efficiency by cutting inventory but makes production rely on on-time delivery; once ports clog, disasters strike, or conflicts interrupt transport, the line stalls quickly, with weak resilience.

什么是“全球供应链”？

What is a "global supply chain"?

全球供应链是一件产品从原料、零件到组装、销售分散在多国完成，各环节靠贸易与物流连接，以降低成本和贴近市场。
A global supply chain spreads a product's raw materials, parts, assembly, and sales across countries, linked by trade and logistics to cut costs and reach markets.
