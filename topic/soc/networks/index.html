网络中的“幂律分布”与“无标度网络”指什么？

What are power-law distributions and “scale-free” networks?

许多真实网络（互联网、引文网）中少数节点拥有极多连接（枢纽），连接数服从幂律而非正态分布，使网络既健壮又对枢纽攻击脆弱。
In many real networks (web, citations), few hubs hold most links and degrees follow a power law, not a normal distribution, making networks robust yet vulnerable to hub attacks.

什么是六度分隔理论？

What is the six degrees of separation theory?

该理论认为任意两人平均通过约六层熟人关系即可相连，反映社交网络的“小世界”特性：高度聚类却又路径很短。
The theory holds any two people are connected by about six acquaintance links, reflecting social networks’ “small world” property: high clustering yet short paths.

什么是“信息茧房”与“回音室效应”？

What are “information cocoons” and the “echo chamber” effect?

算法与社交圈层让用户反复接触相似观点，强化既有立场、削弱异质信息接触，被称为回音室；它可能加剧社会极化。
Algorithms and homogeneous circles repeatedly expose users to similar views, reinforcing priors and reducing dissent—an “echo chamber” that can deepen societal polarization.

社交媒体如何改变集体行动与动员？

How does social media change collective action and mobilization?

社交媒体降低组织成本、加速信息传播，使去中心化动员更容易（如#MeToo），但也带来虚假动员、注意力碎片化与“点赞式行动”（slacktivism）。
Social media lowers organizing costs and speeds diffusion, enabling decentralized mobilization (e.g., #MeToo), but also fosters fake mobilization, attention fragmentation, and “slacktivism.”
