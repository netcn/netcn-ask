萨瑟兰的“差异交往论”与“白领犯罪”概念如何挑战了当时把犯罪归于底层阶级的常识？

How did Sutherland's 'differential association' and 'white-collar crime' challenge the then-common assumption that crime is concentrated in lower classes?

差异交往论认为犯罪行为通过学习与越轨者频繁接触而习得，与阶级无关。萨瑟兰提出“白领犯罪”指体面职业者职务内的违法，反驳了犯罪仅属贫困群体的偏见。
Differential association holds that criminal behavior is learned through frequent contact with deviant definitions, independent of class. Sutherland coined 'white-collar crime' for offenses by respectable professionals, refuting the idea that crime is a lower-class phenomenon.

默顿的失范—张力理论如何解释为什么社会规定的成功目标与可达手段之间的错位会催生越轨？他区分了哪几种适应模式？

How did Merton's anomie-strain theory explain why the gap between culturally prescribed success goals and available means generates deviance, and what adaptation modes did he distinguish?

默顿认为美国文化高度强调成功却未平等提供手段，造成结构性张力。个体可能以遵从、创新、仪式主义、退隐或反抗五种模式应对，其中“创新”即用非法手段追求主流目标。
Merton argued American culture overemphasizes success while under-providing means, producing structural strain. Individuals adapt via conformity, innovation, ritualism, retreatism, or rebellion; 'innovation' uses illicit means for mainstream goals.

贝克尔的标签理论主张“越轨是被界定的”，他在《局外人》中如何用“道德企业家”解释规则与越轨的共构？

Becker's labeling theory claims 'deviance is created by definition'; in Outsiders, how do 'moral entrepreneurs' explain the co-production of rules and deviance?

贝克尔认为并非行为本身而是被有权者贴上标签才成为越轨，道德企业家通过推动规则把某些群体标记为越轨者。标签一旦内化会引发“二次越轨”，凸显权力在定义秩序中的作用。
Becker argued deviance is not intrinsic to acts but results from being labeled by those in power; moral entrepreneurs lobby to create rules that mark groups as deviant. Internalizing the label can trigger secondary deviance, highlighting power in defining order.

赫希的社会纽带理论把越轨解释为与社会的联结薄弱，他提出的四种纽带是什么？

Hirschi's social bond theory explains deviance as weak ties to society; what are the four bonds he proposed?

赫希提出依恋、承诺、参与与信念四种纽带：对他人的情感依恋、对传统目标的投入、对常规活动的参与以及对共同价值的信念。任一纽带薄弱都会提高越轨概率。
Hirschi proposed four bonds—attachment, commitment, involvement, and belief: emotional ties to others, investment in conventional goals, engagement in routine activities, and adherence to shared values. Weakness in any raises deviance risk.
