工具变量（IV）如何帮助估计因果？

How does an instrumental variable (IV) help estimate causation?

当自变量受混杂或反向因果干扰时，IV提供只经自变量影响结果的外生变量，借由两阶段估计剥离因果成分，但依赖强相关与排他性假设。
When an endogenous variable is confounded, an IV affects the outcome only via that variable; two-stage estimation recovers causal effects, relying on relevance and exclusion assumptions.

随机对照试验（RCT）如何识别因果效应？

How does a randomized controlled trial (RCT) identify causal effects?

RCT通过随机分配处理组与对照组，使混杂因素平均平衡，从而将组间差异归因于干预。其外部效度（可推广性）常受样本与情境限制。
RCTs randomly assign treatment and control, balancing confounders on average so group differences attribute to the intervention; external validity is often limited.

相关不等于因果，为什么？

Why does correlation not imply causation?

两变量相关可能由共同第三因素、反向因果或巧合造成；要确定因果需实验或准实验设计（如随机对照、工具变量）控制混杂。
Correlation may reflect a confounder, reverse causality, or chance; establishing causation needs experiments or quasi-experiments (RCTs, instruments) controlling confounders.

什么是“幸存者偏差”？

What is “survivorship bias”?

只关注“幸存”样本而忽略已退出者会得出偏误结论，如仅研究现存企业而忽视倒闭者，会高估成功率。它提醒注意缺失数据。
Focusing only on “survivors” and ignoring dropouts biases conclusions—e.g., studying only living firms overstates success; mind missing data.
