艾斯平-安德森依据什么标准，把当代资本主义福利国家划分为自由主义、保守主义和社会民主三种体制？

On what criterion did Esping-Andersen sort contemporary capitalist welfare states into liberal, conservative, and social-democratic regimes?

标准是去商品化程度，即个人不必依赖在市场上出卖劳动力也能维持体面生活的程度。机理：自由主义体制靠家计审查与低额统一给付，把多数人留在市场内，去商品化最弱；保守主义体制按职业身份缴费给付，福利固化既有市场地位并倚重家庭供养；社会民主体制推行普惠且对标中产标准的给付，社会权利不因退出就业而丧失，去商品化最强。三型背后是工人阶级动员水平与阶级联盟政治的差异。
The criterion is decommodification: how far a person can keep up a decent life without selling labor-power on the market. Liberal regimes rely on means-tests and low flat grants, leaving most people inside the market, and decommodify least. Conservative regimes pay benefits tied to occupational status, cementing existing market positions while leaning on the family for support. Social-democratic regimes grant universal benefits pitched to middle-class standards, so social rights survive exiting employment—decommodification at its strongest. Behind the three types stand different levels of working-class mobilization and different politics of class coalition.

皮尔逊注意到战后各国政府扩充福利轻而易举，削减福利却屡战屡败，他用怎样的政治逻辑解释这种不对称？

Postwar governments expanded welfare programs with ease yet failed again and again to cut them back—what political logic did Paul Pierson offer for this asymmetry?

答案是责备规避的政治：福利扩张造就感恩的受益者，削减则造就愤怒的损失者。机理：花钱是给选民送礼，政客竞相邀功；紧缩却要收回既得利益，而损失带来的痛感远超同额收益的快感，受损者会组织起来惩罚推动者。于是高明的削减总被刻意遮蔽——化整为零、推迟生效、把成本转嫁给未来或非选举机构，令选民无从归罪。这正是福利国家的刚性所在：一经建立便自我巩固。
Blame-avoidance politics: expanding welfare creates grateful beneficiaries, cutting it creates furious losers. Spending is a gift voters reward, so politicians scramble for credit; retrenchment claws back vested gains, and because losses bite harder than equivalent gains please, the harmed organize to punish whoever moved against them. Clever reformers therefore bury the pain—splitting cuts into fragments, delaying their onset, offloading costs onto the future or non-electoral agencies—so blame finds no address. Therein lies the welfare state's rigidity: once built, it entrenches itself.

什么是全民医疗保险？

What is universal health coverage?

全民医疗保险指所有居民都能获得所需医疗服务而不致陷入经济困境。世界卫生组织将其列为可持续发展目标，实现方式包括单一支付方、社会医疗保险等。
Universal health coverage means all residents can access needed health services without financial hardship. The WHO marks it as a Sustainable Development Goal, achieved via single-payer, social insurance, and other models.

什么是社会保障体系？

What is a social security system?

社会保障体系是国家通过立法为公民在年老、失业、疾病、工伤等风险时提供基本经济保障的制度集合，包括养老金、医疗保险与失业救济等。
A social security system is the set of state-backed programs giving citizens basic economic protection against old age, unemployment, illness, and injury—pensions, health insurance, unemployment benefits, and more.

什么是最低工资？

What is a minimum wage?

最低工资是政府依法规定雇主必须支付的最低时薪或月薪，旨在保障劳动者基本生活。支持者认为它能减贫，反对者担忧可能减少低技能岗位。
A minimum wage is the legally set lowest pay employers may offer, meant to secure workers’ basic living. Supporters cite poverty reduction; critics worry it may cut low-skill jobs.
