“信号传递”如何缓解信息不对称？

How does "signaling" help reduce information asymmetry?

信息少的一方可主动发出成本高、难伪造的信号来表明真实质量，如求职者用学历、企业用保修承诺；斯宾塞的信号理论说明这类信号能部分恢复市场效率。
The less-informed side can send costly, hard-to-fake signals of true quality—e.g., degrees for job seekers, warranties for firms; Spence's signaling theory shows such signals partly restore market efficiency.

什么是“逆向选择”？以二手车市场为例。

What is “adverse selection”? Use the used-car market as an example.

逆向选择发生在交易前，掌握信息多的一方利用优势获益。二手车市场里卖家比买家更懂车况，劣质车把优质车挤出市场，买家整体只愿出低价，形成“劣币驱逐良币”。
Adverse selection happens before a deal, when the better-informed side exploits its edge. In used cars, sellers know condition better; lemons drive out good cars, so buyers only offer low prices—“bad drives out good.”

“道德风险”指什么？

What does "moral hazard" mean?

道德风险发生在交易或投保之后：因为有人替自己承担部分后果，一方变得更冒险，比如买了保险后疏于防范。它源于行为与责任的不匹配。
Moral hazard arises after a deal or insurance: shielded from some consequences, one behaves more recklessly—e.g., taking fewer precautions once insured. It stems from a mismatch between action and accountability.

什么是“信息不对称”？

What is "information asymmetry"?

信息不对称指交易双方掌握的信息不一样多，比如卖家比买家更清楚商品质量，可能造成不公平或低效。
Information asymmetry means the two sides of a deal hold unequal information—e.g., a seller knows a product's quality better than the buyer—which can cause unfairness or inefficiency.
