古代的户籍与“编户齐民”如何把人固定在土地上？

How did ancient household registration and 'registered commoners' tie people to the land?

“编户齐民”指国家把百姓按户登记、编入户籍，作为收税和征役的单位。户籍与土地、赋税绑定，迁移要获批，否则就是“逃户”要受罚。这样一来，国家能稳定掌握人口与税源，百姓也被牢牢固定在特定土地上，难以自由流动。
Registered commoners meant the state enrolled households in registers as the units for tax and labor service. Household status was tied to land and tribute, and moving required permission—those who fled were 'runaway households' punished by law. This let the state steadily count people and collect revenue, while binding commoners to specific plots of land and limiting free movement.

为什么传统中国家庭特别重视“大家庭”和祭祖？

Why did traditional Chinese families prize the 'big family' and ancestor worship?

农业社会以家庭为生产单位，多代同堂能分摊劳力与风险；祭祖强化代际联系与家族认同，也起到道德约束作用。家族是福利与秩序的基本单元。
Farming made the household the unit of production; multi-generation homes spread labor and risk. Ancestor worship strengthened ties and identity and acted as moral restraint. The family was the basic unit of welfare and order.

为什么现代家庭普遍从“大家庭”变成“核心家庭”？

Why did modern families shift from extended to nuclear?

工业化与城市让年轻人外出工作、独立居住；教育与健康改善降低了“多子防老”的需要；法律与福利部分替代了家族的养老与保障功能。家庭变小但更平等。
Industrialization and cities pulled youth out to work and live independently; better education and health cut the need for 'many children for old age'; law and welfare partly replaced the clan's care functions. Families shrank but became more equal.

什么是宗族？它在传统社会里起什么作用？

What is a clan, and what role did it play in traditional society?

宗族是由同一祖先繁衍的父系家族群体，有祠堂、族谱和族规。它管救济、调解、办学与祭祀，在国家和家庭之间填补了治理空白。
A clan is a patrilineal group descended from one ancestor, with ancestral halls, genealogies and rules. It provided relief, mediation, schooling and ritual, filling governance gaps between state and household.
