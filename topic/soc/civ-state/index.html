为什么大规模农业帝国往往走向中央集权，而不是长期分裂？

Why do large agrarian empires tend toward centralization rather than lasting fragmentation?

大规模农业帝国依赖治水、储粮和大型公共工程，这些只有集中组织才能办成。中央把税收、军队和官僚体系统一到手中，既能调配资源防灾，也能维持秩序、防止地方割据。地理上的连片平原和一致的农业节奏，也天然利于一个统一政权来管理。
Large agrarian empires depended on flood control, grain storage, and big public works that only centralized organization could manage. A central authority that unified taxation, the military, and bureaucracy could allocate resources against disasters and prevent regional warlords from breaking away. Connected plains and a shared agricultural cycle also naturally favored a single unifying state.

为什么“书同文、车同轨”对统一社会的维系如此关键？

Why were 'uniform writing and uniform axle widths' so key to holding a unified society together?

“书同文”让各地的人能跨越方言障碍读写交流，“车同轨”让道路和车辆互通、物资人员顺畅往来。统一的标准大幅降低治理与贸易的成本，也催生共同的文化认同。没有这些标准，庞大帝国很容易被语言、距离和地方习惯割裂。
'Uniform writing' let people across regions communicate in writing despite different dialects, and 'uniform axle widths' made roads and vehicles compatible so goods and people moved freely. Common standards sharply cut the cost of governance and trade and nurtured a shared cultural identity. Without them, a vast empire easily fragments along language, distance, and local custom.

什么是郡县制，它为什么取代分封制？

What was the commandery-county system, and why did it replace feudal enfeoffment?

郡县制由中央直接任命地方官、收税调兵，官员不世袭；分封制则把土地与权力交给诸侯世袭。郡县让中央更强、地方更难割据，适合大一统帝国治理。
The commandery-county system had centrally appointed, non-hereditary local officials who collected tax and troops; enfeoffment gave land and power to hereditary lords. Commanderies strengthened the center and reduced warlordism, suiting a unified empire.

长城仅仅是“墙”吗？它有哪些超出防御的社会与经济功能？

Was the Great Wall merely a 'wall'? What social and economic functions lay beyond defense?

长城不只是军事屏障。它也是一条“边境线”：设有关卡管理贸易、收税、控制人员往来，烽燧系统兼具通信功能。修墙与驻军带动了沿线屯田、物资运输和人口聚集，催生了边贸城镇。它还是国家权力与疆域的象征。
The Great Wall was more than a military barrier. It also served as a border line: its passes regulated trade, collected taxes, and controlled movement, while its beacon towers formed a communication network. Building and garrisoning it spurred farming, supply transport, and settlement along the frontier, giving rise to border trading towns. It was also a symbol of state power and territory.

为什么古代中国长期重视“统一”？

Why did imperial China long prize 'unity'?

大河流域需要统一治水与调粮，战乱让百姓渴望安定，儒家也强调“天下归一”。统一便于修渠、平准物价、抵御外患，逐渐成为强大的政治文化传统。
River civilizations needed unified flood control and grain distribution; wars made people crave stability, and Confucianism stressed 'all-under-heaven as one'. Unity eased canal-building, price stabilization and defense, becoming a strong political tradition.
