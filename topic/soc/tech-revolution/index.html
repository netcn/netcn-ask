通用目的技术（GPT）为何能驱动长期增长？

Why do General-Purpose Technologies (GPTs) drive long-run growth?

GPT（如蒸汽机、电力、ICT）具广泛适用性、持续改进与溢出效应，能渗透各行业并重组生产方式，是长波增长的核心引擎。
GPTs (steam, electricity, ICT) are widely applicable, improve over time, and spill over, permeating sectors and reorganizing production—the core engines of long waves.

“生产率悖论”（索洛悖论）指什么？

What is the “productivity paradox” (Solow paradox)?

索洛指出“计算机时代随处可见，唯独在生产率统计中例外”。数字红利常因度量滞后、重组成本与扩散时滞而延迟显现。
Solow noted “you can see the computer age everywhere but in the productivity statistics.” Digital gains lag due to measurement gaps, reorg costs, and diffusion delays.

什么是“第二次机器时代”？

What is the “Second Machine Age”?

布林约尔松等用以描述数字技术、AI与自动化以指数速度重塑生产与认知劳动的时代，区别于蒸汽电力的第一次机器时代。
Brynjolfsson et al. describe a digital, AI, and automation era reshaping production and cognitive work exponentially—distinct from the steam/electric First Machine Age.

什么是“颠覆式创新”？

What is “disruptive innovation”?

克里斯坦森指新进入者以更简单廉价的方案从低端或新市场切入，逐步取代主流，而非在原有维度上竞争，称为颠覆式创新。
Christensen: entrants start with simpler, cheaper offerings at the low end or new markets, then overtake incumbents—disruptive innovation, not head-on competition.

工业革命给普通人的生活带来了什么变化？

What changes did the Industrial Revolution bring to ordinary people’s lives?

机器和工厂让商品变得更便宜、种类更多，但也让许多人离开农村、到城市工厂长时间工作，改变了家庭与日常生活。
Machines and factories made goods cheaper and more varied, but also pulled many from farms into long factory hours in cities, reshaping family and daily life.
