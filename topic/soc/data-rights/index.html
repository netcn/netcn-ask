“被遗忘权”指什么？

What is the "right to be forgotten"?

它指个人可要求删除不再必要、已过时或损害声誉的个人信息链接，尤其在搜索引擎等场合；它与公众知情权和言论自由之间存在持续张力。
It lets individuals request removal of links to information that is no longer necessary, outdated, or harmful to reputation, especially in search engines; it tensions with the public's right to know and free expression.

个人数据保护有哪些核心原则？

What are the core principles of personal-data protection?

常见原则包括目的限制（只用于约定用途）、最少收集、知情同意、安全保存与可更正删除，以在利用与隐私之间取得平衡。
Core principles include purpose limitation, data minimization, informed consent, secure storage, and the right to correct or delete—balancing use against privacy.

什么是“数据权属”？

What is "data ownership"?

数据权属讨论谁对个人数据、平台数据与公共数据拥有控制、收益和许可的权利，是数字时代新型财产权争议的核心。
Data ownership asks who holds control, benefit, and permission rights over personal, platform, and public data—a core dispute over new property-like rights in the digital age.
