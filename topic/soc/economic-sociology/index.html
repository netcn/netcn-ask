格兰诺维特的“经济行动嵌入社会结构”论文如何反驳新古典经济学把行动者视为孤立个体的假设？

How did Granovetter's 'Economic Action and Social Structure' rebut the neoclassical assumption of the atomized actor?

格兰诺维特主张经济行为受具体人际关系与网络结构制约，过度社会化与低度社会化同样失真。他用信任网络解释为什么交易往往依赖私人关系而非纯粹合约。
Granovetter argued economic behavior is constrained by concrete relations and network structure; both over- and under-socialized views mislead. He used trust networks to explain why transactions rely on personal ties, not pure contract.

波兰尼在《大转型》中如何论证市场并非自然存在，而是被“嵌入”社会关系并由国家制度保护？

In The Great Transformation, how did Polanyi argue that markets are not natural but 'embedded' in social relations and protected by state institutions?

波兰尼提出“双向运动”：自由市场扩张会摧毁土地、劳动与货币等虚构商品，引发社会自我保护（如福利与管制）。他主张经济始终嵌入社会制度，纯自发市场是乌托邦。
Polanyi described a 'double movement': free-market expansion destroys fictitious commodities (land, labor, money), provoking societal self-protection via welfare and regulation. He held that economy is always embedded in institutions; pure self-regulating markets are utopian.

经济学假定货币完全同质、可以任意替代，为什么现实中的家庭却坚持把收入分成工资、彩礼、学费等彼此不可混用的几份？

Economics assumes money is perfectly fungible, so why do real households insist on splitting income into separate pots—wages, betrothal gifts, school fees—that cannot be mixed?

因为货币从来不只是抽象购买力，人们还要用它标记具体的社会关系与道德义务。机理：泽利泽研究20世纪初的美国工人家庭，发现他们用信封分装、单独开户等办法把同一种现金改造成多种货币——丈夫的工资归家用，妻子的收入只算贴补，孩子的储蓄轻易动不得；专款专用划清了家庭内部的权威与情感边界，抵挡市场逻辑对亲密生活的侵蚀，预算因而是一种道德秩序而不只是理财手段。
Money is never bare purchasing power; people deploy it to mark concrete social bonds and moral obligations. Studying working-class American families around 1900, Zelizer documented how envelopes, separate bank accounts, and mental partitions converted one uniform cash into many monies—a husband's wage belonged to the household, a wife's earnings counted only as a supplement, a child's savings were untouchable. Earmarking thus drew lines of authority and affection inside the family and held market logic away from intimate life, making the household budget a moral order rather than mere accounting.

泽利泽研究“亲密关系中的金钱”如何挑战了把金钱视为单一、去人格化力量的经济学假设？

How did Zelizer's work on 'money in intimate relations' challenge the economic assumption that money is a single, impersonal force?

泽利泽表明人们会把钱标记为“礼物钱”“津贴”“丧葬费”等不同意义，用以维持而非腐蚀社会关系。她证明货币的社会意义随关系情境而分化。
Zelizer showed people mark money as 'gift money', 'allowance', or 'condolence money' to sustain rather than corrupt relationships. She demonstrated that the social meaning of money varies by relational context.

为什么找工作时那些关系一般的“点头之交”，往往比亲密好友更能带来有用的招聘信息？

Why are casual acquaintances often more useful than close friends when it comes to hearing about job openings?

因为弱关系连接着不同的社交圈，能传递好友圈内没有的新信息。机理：强关系双方彼此相识，各自掌握的信息高度重叠，你知道的职位空缺对方多半也知道；泛泛之交则嵌入另一张网络，充当连通两个群体的桥梁，其消息对你才是非冗余的增量。格兰诺维特对波士顿地区白领的调查显示，多数人经由偶尔见面的人际渠道获知工作线索，而职业流动性越强、职位越高的人越依赖弱关系获取新工作。
Weak ties reach into circles that close friends never touch, so they carry genuinely new information. Strong ties know one another and overlap heavily in what everyone has heard—an opening your best friend knows about, you have heard of too—whereas an acquaintance is embedded in another network and acts as a bridge between groups, delivering non-redundant news. Surveying white-collar workers around Boston, Granovetter found most people learned of their jobs through contacts seen only occasionally, and the more mobile (higher-status) the workers, the more they relied on such weak ties for new jobs.

凡勃伦观察到有些商品价格越贵反而越好卖，这种炫耀性消费背后的社会逻辑是什么？

Veblen observed that some goods sell better the dearer they become—what social logic lies behind such conspicuous consumption?

顾客买的其实不是使用价值，而是昂贵本身所传递的财力与地位信号。机理：凡勃伦在《有闲阶级论》里指出，财产占有成为荣誉的标尺，人们借助浪费性消费与炫耀性休闲证明自己无须劳动、余财充裕；价格越高，公开展示财力的资格越显赫，在财富攀比中赢得的优越感越强，降价反而会吓跑主顾，于是出现需求曲线局部上斜的凡勃伦商品。
Buyers are paying less for usefulness than for the status signal that expense itself sends. In The Theory of the Leisure Class, Veblen argued that property holding had become the yardstick of honor: wasteful consumption and conspicuous leisure proved a person need not labor and had money to spare. The higher the price, the grander the license to display wealth openly and the stronger the superiority won in pecuniary rivalry—so a price cut drives customers away, yielding Veblen goods whose demand curves locally slope upward.
