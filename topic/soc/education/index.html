“教育公平”面临哪些结构性障碍？

What structural barriers does “educational equity” face?

教育公平追求不同背景者享有均等机会，但城乡资源差距、家庭资本与户籍限制构成结构性障碍。早期劣势会通过课外投入与社交网络被放大，形成代际传递。补偿性拨款与公平招生被视为重要对策。
Educational equity seeks equal opportunity regardless of background, but urban–rural resource gaps, family capital, and household registration create structural barriers. Early disadvantage is amplified through extracurricular spending and networks, passing across generations. Compensatory funding and fair admissions are seen as key remedies.

“教育回报率”如何衡量？

How is the “return to education” measured?

教育回报率通常用多受一年教育带来的收入增幅表示，可用明瑟收入方程等计量方法估计。它帮助个人判断是否值得继续求学，也供政府评估教育投资效益。但回报受性别、地区与专业影响，差异很大。
The return to education is usually expressed as the income gain from one extra year of schooling, estimated with models like the Mincer earnings equation. It helps individuals decide whether more study pays off and lets governments assess education investment. But returns vary widely by gender, region, and field.

什么是教育回报率？

What is the return on education?

教育回报率指多受一年教育带来的收入增加比例，是衡量教育经济价值的核心指标。大量研究显示，基础教育与高等教育的回报率在多数国家均为正且显著。
The return on education is the percentage increase in earnings from one extra year of schooling—a key measure of education’s economic value. Studies broadly find positive, significant returns at both basic and higher levels.

为什么教育能减少贫困？

Why does education reduce poverty?

教育能减少贫困，是因为它提升人的生产力和就业能力，使人获得收入更高的工作。受教育程度高的人通常更健康、更会做财务与育儿决策，也更容易参与社会和公共事务。教育还能打破“贫困代际传递”，让下一代起点更高。
Education reduces poverty by raising people's productivity and employability, opening access to better-paid work. Better-educated people tend to be healthier, make sounder financial and parenting choices, and engage more in civic life. Education also breaks the intergenerational transmission of poverty, giving the next generation a higher starting point.

How does Becker's 'human capital' theory treat education as an economic investment?

Becker argued in 1964 that spending on education, training, and health raises a person's productivity and earning capacity, much like a firm buying machines. The theory frames schooling as rational investment—costing time and money now for higher lifetime returns later.

蒙台梭利教育法的核心是什么？

What is core to Montessori education?

蒙台梭利把儿童当作自身发展的主体，成人的工作从讲授移到环境：教具按难度成系列、自身能显示错误，孩子自选自己做，从物件而非分数发现规律，教师只观察示范。混龄让年幼的看示范、年长的靠重做巩固，社会性学习在组里发生。她的依据是「吸收性心智」与敏感期，内在动机比奖惩靠得住。但要注意自由不等于放任：教具有限度，环境准备最吃设计。
Montessori treats the child as the agent of his or her own development, so the adult's work moves from lecturing to the environment: materials in graded series that display their own errors, letting a child choose and handle them, finding the rule in the object rather than in a score, while the teacher watches and demonstrates. Mixed-age groups let the younger watch and the older consolidate by doing it again, so social learning arises inside the group. Her warrant is the "absorbing mind" and sensitive periods, which make intrinsic motivation sounder than praise and punishment. Freedom here is not permissiveness: the materials come with limits, and a quiet teacher presupposes a painstakingly prepared setting.

“义务教育”是什么意思？

What does “compulsory education” mean?

义务教育指由国家法律规定、适龄儿童和少年必须接受且政府免费提供的基础教育，通常为小学到初中阶段。它旨在保障基本文化素质与平等起点。多数国家把九年左右作为义务教育的年限。
Compulsory education is basic education that the state legally requires school-age children to attend and provides free of charge, usually from primary through junior secondary. It aims to guarantee basic literacy and an equal starting point. Most countries set about nine years as the compulsory span.

“双语教育”有哪些常见形式？

What common forms does “bilingual education” take?

双语教育可指用两种语言授课，常见形式包括过渡型（先用母语再转主流语）、维持型（同时保留母语）和沉浸式（以少数语言为主环境）。选择哪种常涉及文化认同与平等议题。研究表明早期双语多有益于认知灵活性。
Bilingual education teaches through two languages; common forms include transitional (mother tongue then shift to majority language), maintenance (keep mother tongue), and immersion (minority language as the main environment). The choice involves cultural identity and equality. Research suggests early bilingualism aids cognitive flexibility.

Why do children tend to 'forget' some of what they learned after a long school break?

Studies find that after long breaks like summer vacation, students' reading and math scores tend to drop—and more so for low-income children—a pattern called 'summer learning loss.' It shows learning needs steady practice; without use, knowledge slowly fades.

普鲁士教育模式有什么特点？

What are the features of the Prussian education model?

19世纪普鲁士建立分年级、按课程表和统一教材运作的国民学校，强调纪律、读写算与爱国忠诚。这种模式后被多国效仿，奠定了现代公立学校体系。其标准化也常被批评压抑个性。
In the 19th century Prussia built graded public schools run by timetable and uniform textbooks, stressing discipline, the three Rs, and patriotic loyalty. Many countries later copied it, founding the modern public-school system. Its standardization is also criticized for suppressing individuality.

什么是识字率？

What is the literacy rate?

识字率指能读写简单句子的人口占适龄人口的比例，常用来衡量基础教育水平。近百年全球识字率大幅提升，但地区间仍有差距。提高识字率是减贫与发展的重要前提。
The literacy rate is the share of the population able to read and write a simple sentence, often used to gauge basic education. Global literacy rose sharply over the last century, though gaps remain between regions. Raising it is key to poverty reduction and development.

义务教育是什么？

What is compulsory education?

义务教育是国家依法要求适龄儿童必须接受的基础教育，通常免费。
Compulsory education is the state-mandated, usually free basic schooling for children of certain ages.

为什么要有义务教育？

Why is compulsory education needed?

义务教育保证每个适龄儿童都能免费上学，掌握读写和基础知识，既帮助个人发展，也让整个社会更有能力。
Compulsory education ensures every child can attend school free of charge to learn literacy and basics, helping both personal development and society's capacity.

科举制度考的是什么？

What did the imperial examination test?

科举是隋唐到清末通过考试选拔官员的制度，主要考儒家经典、诗赋与策论，强调读写与治国理政知识。它让平民可凭才学入仕，打破了世家垄断。其公平竞争理念影响深远，甚至启发了现代文官考试。
The imperial examination, from the Sui–Tang through the Qing, selected officials by testing Confucian classics, poetry, and policy essays, emphasizing literacy and governance knowledge. It let commoners enter office by merit, breaking aristocratic monopoly. Its ideal of fair competition influenced later civil-service exams.

什么是终身学习？

What is lifelong learning?

终身学习指人离开学校后仍然持续学习新知识和技能，以适应变化的社会和工作，像一直给大脑充电。
Lifelong learning means continuing to gain knowledge and skills after school to adapt to a changing society and work, like constantly recharging the brain.
