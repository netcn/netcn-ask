锚定效应如何在谈判与定价中起作用？

How does the anchoring effect operate in negotiation and pricing?

锚定指人在估计时过度依赖最先接触的信息（“锚”），后续调整往往不足。商家先标高价再打折、谈判中先开价，都利用了锚定。
Anchoring: people rely too heavily on the first piece of information (“the anchor”) when estimating, and adjustments are typically insufficient. Retail markdowns and first offers in negotiation exploit this bias.

什么是“双曲贴现”？它如何解释拖延？

What is hyperbolic discounting, and how does it explain procrastination?

双曲贴现指人们极度偏好即时小奖励而低估未来大奖励，导致拖延与储蓄不足；与指数贴现不同，偏好会随延迟缩短而反转。
Hyperbolic discounting means people heavily prefer a smaller immediate reward over a larger later one, causing procrastination and under-saving; unlike exponential discounting, preferences reverse as the delay shrinks.

什么是损失厌恶？为什么人们害怕亏钱多于喜欢赚钱？

What is loss aversion, and why do people fear losses more than they enjoy gains?

损失厌恶指人们对损失的痛苦约为同等收益带来快乐的两倍，导致回避风险、过早卖出或固守现状。它在投资、谈判与公共选择中广泛存在。
Loss aversion means losses hurt about twice as much as equivalent gains feel good, leading to risk avoidance, premature selling, and status-quo bias. It appears across investing, negotiation, and public choice.

什么是行为经济学？

What is behavioral economics?

行为经济学结合心理学研究人们在真实决策中如何偏离“完全理性”假设，例如受偏见、情绪和社会因素影响。它补充了传统经济学对理性人模型的不足。
Behavioral economics combines psychology with economics to study how people deviate from the “fully rational” assumption in real decisions—e.g., under bias, emotion, and social influence. It complements the traditional rational-agent model.
