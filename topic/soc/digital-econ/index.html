什么是“赢家通吃”与平台垄断？

What are “winner-take-all” dynamics and platform monopoly?

数字平台因网络效应、数据壁垒和极低的边际成本，容易形成少数巨头主导（赢家通吃）。这引发对反垄断、数据可携带与互操作性的监管讨论。
Digital platforms tend toward winner-take-all dominance due to network effects, data moats, and near-zero marginal cost, raising antitrust debates over data portability and interoperability.

区块链与分布式账本如何可能改变信任机制？

How might blockchain and distributed ledgers change mechanisms of trust?

区块链以去中心化、不可篡改的账本记录交易，可在缺乏中央权威时建立可信协作（如跨境支付、供应链溯源），但存在能耗、吞吐量与监管不确定性。
Blockchain uses a decentralized, tamper-resistant ledger to enable trust without central authority (cross-border payments, supply-chain traceability), yet faces energy, throughput, and regulatory uncertainty.

数据作为生产要素，其产权与收益分配有何难题？

As a factor of production, what are the property-rights and distribution problems of data?

数据由用户行为产生却由平台采集控制，存在确权困难、外部性强和议价能力不对称等问题，催生数据确权、隐私保护与数据税等政策讨论。
Data is generated by users but captured by platforms, creating ambiguous ownership, strong externalities, and asymmetric bargaining power, prompting debates on data rights, privacy, and data taxes.

什么是零工经济？它对劳动关系意味着什么？

What is the gig economy, and what does it mean for labor relations?

零工经济通过平台匹配短期、灵活的任务（如外卖、网约车），降低用工成本但弱化了传统雇佣关系，带来社保覆盖与劳动权益的新挑战。
The gig economy matches short, flexible tasks via platforms (delivery, ride-hailing), lowering labor costs but weakening traditional employment and raising challenges for social insurance and worker rights.

什么是算法推荐？它如何塑造信息消费？

What is algorithmic recommendation, and how does it shape information consumption?

算法推荐根据用户行为预测偏好并推送内容，提升匹配效率，但也可能形成“信息茧房”和过滤气泡，收窄视野。
Algorithmic recommendation predicts preferences from behavior to push content, improving matching efficiency but potentially creating “information cocoons” and filter bubbles that narrow exposure.

平台经济的“双边市场”特征是什么？

What is the “two-sided market” feature of platform economies?

平台同时服务两类相互吸引的用户（如买家与卖家），一边用户增多会提升对另一边的价值，形成网络效应；平台常对一边补贴、另一边收费。
Platforms serve two interdependent user groups (e.g., buyers and sellers); more users on one side raise value for the other via network effects. Platforms often subsidize one side and charge the other.

什么是数字经济？

What is the digital economy?

数字经济指以数字知识和信息为关键生产要素、以信息网络为载体的经济活动，包括电商、数字金融、平台服务和数据产业。
The digital economy refers to economic activity built on digital data and information as key factors of production and networks as infrastructure—including e-commerce, fintech, platforms, and data industries.
