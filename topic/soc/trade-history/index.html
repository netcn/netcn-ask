荷兰东印度公司为何被视为第一家现代股份公司？

Why is the Dutch East India Company seen as the first modern joint-stock company?

1602年成立的荷兰东印度公司通过公开发行股票募集资本，股东承担有限责任，并由董事会统一管理远洋贸易。它建立了可转让的股权与持续的法人结构，被视为现代股份公司与资本市场的雏形。
Founded in 1602, the Dutch East India Company raised capital by publicly issuing shares, gave shareholders limited liability, and had a board manage long-distance trade. It created transferable equity and a lasting corporate structure, seen as the prototype of the modern joint-stock company and capital market.

汉萨同盟是一个国家吗？

Was the Hanseatic League a country?

汉萨同盟不是国家，而是13到17世纪北欧约200个城市组成的商业与防御联盟。它控制波罗的海贸易、制定共同规则并保护成员商人的利益。随着海运技术和民族国家的兴起，同盟影响力逐渐衰落。
The Hanseatic League was not a state but a commercial and defensive alliance of about 200 North European cities from the 13th to 17th centuries. It controlled Baltic trade, set common rules, and protected member merchants. Its influence declined as shipping technology and nation-states rose.

朝贡贸易和自由买卖有什么不同？

How did tributary trade differ from free buying and selling?

在东亚朝贡体系下，周边国家以“进贡”名义携带商品来华，明朝与清朝以厚礼回赐，贸易服务于政治臣属关系而非纯利润。形式上官方管控严格，民间私贩则常被限制。它体现了贸易与外交的结合。
Under East Asia's tributary system, neighboring states brought goods to China as “tribute” and the Ming and Qing courts rewarded them with generous gifts; trade served political subordination rather than pure profit. It was tightly controlled by the state, while private trade was often restricted. It shows the blend of trade and diplomacy.

「荷兰病」如何解释资源暴富反而拖垮了其他产业？

How does 'Dutch disease' explain why a resource boom can wreck other industries?

科登与尼里在 1982 年用荷兰天然气致富后制造业衰退的案例命名该现象：出口资源推高本币汇率，使制造业与农业「变贵、失去竞争力」。它说明「横财」也会通过汇率扭曲引发「去工业化」。
Corden and Neary named the effect in 1982 after the Netherlands' manufacturing decline following a gas windfall: resource exports raise the currency, making manufacturing and farming too expensive to compete. It shows a windfall can also trigger de-industrialization through exchange-rate distortion.

“重商主义”看重贸易的什么？

What did “mercantilism” value in trade?

重商主义是16到18世纪欧洲盛行的政策观念，认为国家富强在于积累金银、鼓励出口并限制进口以减少逆差。政府常通过关税、特许公司和殖民地垄断来干预贸易。它推动了早期殖民竞争，也遭到自由贸易思想的批评。
Mercantilism, dominant in 16th–18th century Europe, held that national power lay in accumulating gold and silver by encouraging exports and limiting imports to shrink deficits. Governments intervened through tariffs, chartered companies, and colonial monopolies. It drove early colonial rivalry and was later criticized by free-trade ideas.

茶叶如何影响了近代世界贸易格局？

How did tea reshape modern world trade?

18世纪欧洲对中国茶叶需求暴涨，英国人用白银购买，后来以鸦片抵偿逆差，直接酿成鸦片战争与贸易体系重塑。茶叶贸易也推动了中国与全球市场的深度连接。它说明单一商品能牵动国际政治。
In the 18th century European demand for Chinese tea surged; the British paid in silver and later offset the deficit with opium, directly precipitating the Opium Wars and a reshaping of the trade system. Tea also pulled China into deeper connection with global markets. It shows how a single commodity can move international politics.

京杭大运河在古代贸易中起什么作用？

What role did the Grand Canal play in ancient trade?

京杭大运河贯通南北，把南方的粮食、丝绸和盐运往北方政治中心，是维系王朝运转的“漕运”命脉。它降低了大宗物资的陆运成本，也催生了沿线商业城市。直到铁路兴起，运河才开始衰落。
The Grand Canal linked north and south, carrying southern grain, silk, and salt to the northern political centers as the lifeline of “caoyun” grain transport that sustained the dynasties. It cut the cost of moving bulk goods over land and spawned commercial cities along its route. It declined only after railways rose.

How did the East India Company differ from an ordinary trading firm?

东印度公司由本国政府特许，不仅做买卖，还拥有铸币、建军、宣战和殖民治理等准国家权力。荷兰与英国的东印度公司都曾统治大片海外领土。它们既是贸易工具，也是早期殖民扩张的载体。
Chartered by their governments, the East India Companies did more than trade: they could mint coins, raise armies, wage war, and govern colonies as quasi-states. Both the Dutch and British companies ruled large overseas territories. They were both commercial tools and vehicles of early colonial expansion.

贸易顺差和逆差是什么意思？

What do trade surplus and deficit mean?

贸易顺差指一国出口额大于进口额，逆差则相反。顺差常被视为赚入外汇，但长期逆差也可能反映强劲的内需与消费能力。单看顺差逆差并不能简单判断经济好坏。
A trade surplus means a country's exports exceed its imports, while a deficit is the opposite. A surplus is often seen as earning foreign exchange, but a persistent deficit may also reflect strong domestic demand and consumption. Surplus or deficit alone does not simply tell whether an economy is doing well.

1947 年的「关税与贸易总协定」（GATT）如何一步步演变成今天的 WTO？

How did the 1947 General Agreement on Tariffs and Trade (GATT) evolve step by step into today's WTO?

GATT 在 1947 年成立后，通过多轮「减税谈判」大幅降低了成员国关税，成为事实上的国际贸易规则。1995 年它正式升级为「世界贸易组织」（WTO），新增了争端解决机制与服务贸易规则。
After its founding in 1947, GATT ran repeated rounds of tariff-cutting negotiations that sharply lowered members' duties and became the de facto rules of world trade. In 1995 it was formally upgraded into the World Trade Organization (WTO), adding a dispute-settlement system and rules on services.

丝绸之路主要交换了哪些东西？

What were the main goods exchanged along the Silk Road?

丝绸之路连接中国与中亚、欧洲，中国输出丝绸、瓷器和茶叶，换回毛皮、香料、宝石和马匹。它不只是商品通道，也促成了宗教、技术与艺术的传播。骆驼商队是这条路上最主要的运输方式。
The Silk Road linked China with Central Asia and Europe; China exported silk, porcelain, and tea in return for furs, spices, gems, and horses. It was not only a trade route but also a channel for religion, technology, and art. Camel caravans were the main means of transport along it.

“三角贸易”指的是什么？

What does the “triangular trade” refer to?

三角贸易是16到19世纪跨大西洋的贸易循环：欧洲把工业品运到非洲，非洲奴隶被运往美洲，美洲的糖、棉花、烟草再运回欧洲。它给欧洲带来巨额财富，却建立在奴役与苦难之上。这是历史上规模最大的强迫迁移之一。
The triangular trade was a transatlantic cycle from the 16th to 19th centuries: Europe shipped manufactured goods to Africa, enslaved Africans were carried to the Americas, and American sugar, cotton, and tobacco returned to Europe. It generated vast wealth for Europe but rested on enslavement and suffering, and was among the largest forced migrations in history.

古代市集和今天的超市有什么相似处？

What do ancient markets share with today's supermarkets?

古代市集是固定时间地点聚集买卖、讨价还价的场所，和超市一样都是人们获取商品的中介节点。不同在于市集多靠口碑与当面交易，而超市依赖标准化标价与供应链。基本功能都是连接生产者和消费者。
Ancient markets were places where people gathered at set times and spots to haggle and trade, serving like supermarkets as intermediary nodes for obtaining goods. The difference is that markets relied on reputation and face-to-face dealing, while supermarkets depend on standardized pricing and supply chains. Both basically connect producers and consumers.

集装箱如何改变了世界贸易？

How did the container change world trade?

1950年代标准化集装箱普及后，货物可整箱吊装、跨运输方式无缝转运，装卸时间从数天缩短到几小时。这大幅降低了海运成本，推动了全球供应链与产业分工。如今绝大部分国际贸易货物都靠集装箱运输。
After standardized containers spread in the 1950s, cargo could be lifted as whole boxes and transferred seamlessly across transport modes, cutting loading time from days to hours. This sharply lowered shipping costs and fueled global supply chains and the division of labor. Today most international trade moves in containers.

Centuries ago, why did Britain pass 'Navigation Acts' requiring goods to be carried in its own ships?

17 世纪的英国为了把海运和殖民贸易「牢牢抓在自己手里」，立下航海条例，规定许多货物只能由英国或殖民地的船只运输。这保护了本国船主，却也惹恼了荷兰等对手，多次引发战争。
In the 1600s Britain wanted to keep sea trade and colonial commerce firmly in its own hands, so it passed Navigation Acts saying many goods could be carried only on British or colonial ships. It protected English shipowners but angered rivals like the Dutch and sparked wars.

香料贸易为什么曾如此重要？

Why was the spice trade once so important?

在中世纪，胡椒、丁香等香料用于保存食物、调味和医药，欧洲本地无法生产，只能从亚洲长途进口。香料利润极高，直接推动了欧洲航海探险和新航路的开辟。它也是早期全球化的重要动力。
In the Middle Ages, spices like pepper and clove were used to preserve food, flavor dishes, and treat illness, but Europe could not produce them and had to import them from Asia over long distances. Their huge profits directly drove European maritime exploration and new sea routes. They were also a key engine of early globalization.

什么是关税？

What is a tariff?

关税是政府对进出口商品征收的税，通常在边境口岸收取。它可以增加财政收入，也被用来保护本国产业免受外来竞争。过高的关税会推高物价并可能引发贸易摩擦。
A tariff is a tax a government levies on imported or exported goods, usually collected at the border. It can raise revenue and also protect domestic industries from foreign competition. Excessive tariffs raise prices and can trigger trade disputes.
