NGO 在全球治理中扮演什么角色？

What role do NGOs play in global governance?

NGO 提供本地信息、监督政府与企业的行为、在谈判中代表弱势声音，并填补公共服务空白；它们也常因代表性不足或受资助方影响而受到质疑。
NGOs supply local knowledge, monitor states and firms, voice marginalized groups in negotiations, and fill service gaps—though they're also questioned for weak representativeness or funder influence.

资源依赖会怎样影响 NGO 的独立性？

How does resource dependence affect an NGO's independence?

当 NGO 主要依赖政府或少数大额捐助时，其议题与立场可能向资助方倾斜，出现“倡导靠捐款人”的张力；多元筹资与透明披露有助于保持独立。
When an NGO relies heavily on government or a few big donors, its agenda and stance may tilt toward funders—a tension between advocacy and money; diverse funding and disclosure help preserve independence.

什么是“非政府组织”（NGO）？

What is a "non-governmental organization" (NGO)?

NGO 是不以营利为目的、独立于政府的民间组织，常从事扶贫、环保、人权与救灾等工作，依靠捐赠或会费运转。
An NGO is a non-profit, non-governmental civil group often working on poverty, environment, human rights, and disaster relief, funded by donations or membership.
