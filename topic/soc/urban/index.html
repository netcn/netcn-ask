贫民窟形成的原因是什么？

What causes slums?

贫民窟主要是城市增长快过制度供给的结果，不是个人选择的产物。人口集中的速度快过住房和管网铺开的速度，正规房源跟不上，只能自建或在他人土地上落脚。产权不清、审批登记门槛高，加盖与出租难合法化，居民缺少改善住所的动力；岗位集中在低薪部门，服务常按户籍或产权分配。拆迁不等于消除贫民窟，只要涌入的动力还在，新的聚居还会形成。
Slums come mainly from urban growth outrunning the institutions meant to house it, not from individual choices. People concentrate in cities faster than housing and pipe networks can be laid out, and when the formal market cannot supply enough homes, they build their own or settle on land belonging to others. Unclear title and demanding approval and registration rules keep extensions and rentals informal, which also leaves residents little reason to upgrade a dwelling, while work clusters in low-paid jobs and services are often allocated by household registration or property rights. Clearing a shantytown is not the same as ending a slum: while the forces driving the inflow remain, new informal settlements form again.

什么是城市蔓延？

What is urban sprawl?

城市蔓延指城市用地以低密度、摊大饼的方式向外扩张：郊区地块大、路网稀疏、功能单一，同样多的人口要摊上更长的道路、管线和通勤距离，人均的基建与能耗因此高于紧凑城区，被占的多是城市边缘的农田。它不等于城市化或郊区化：后两者说人口与岗位迁往何处，蔓延说占地的形态，所以总人口不增长、只要密度继续摊薄，也照样在蔓延。
Urban sprawl is the outward expansion of urban land at low density, spreading like a pancake: suburbs have large blocks, sparse roads and single uses, so the same population is spread over longer streets, pipelines and commutes, which makes infrastructure and energy use per person higher than in a compact city, while the land taken is usually farmland at the urban edge. It is not the same as urbanization or suburbanization: those say where people and jobs go, sprawl describes the form of land occupation, so a city with a flat population can keep sprawling as long as density keeps thinning.

A compact city promotes high density, mixed use, and transit-oriented development to cut commuting, energy, and preserve green space.
