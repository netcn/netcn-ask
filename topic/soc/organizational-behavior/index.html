“群体思维”为什么会导致糟糕决策？

Why does “groupthink” lead to bad decisions?

群体思维指高度凝聚的团队为维持一致而压制异议，导致风险评估失真与方案单一，常见于封闭、领导力强的小组。詹尼斯以古巴导弹危机等案例说明其危害。设立“唱反调者”与鼓励外部意见可缓解它。
Groupthink occurs when a cohesive team suppresses dissent to preserve harmony, distorting risk assessment and narrowing options, common in closed groups with strong leaders. Janis illustrated its harm using cases like the Cuban Missile Crisis. Appointing a “devil's advocate” and inviting outside views can mitigate it.

“代理问题”在组织里指什么？

What does the “principal-agent problem” mean in organizations?

代理问题指所有者（委托人）与经营者（代理人）目标不一致，且委托人难以完全监督代理人行为，导致后者可能损公肥私。现代企业用薪酬挂钩、信息披露与董事会监督来缓解。它也是公司治理的核心议题。
The principal-agent problem arises when owners (principals) and managers (agents) have misaligned goals and principals cannot fully monitor agents, who may then act for self-interest. Firms use pay linkage, disclosure, and board oversight to ease it. It is also central to corporate governance.

内在激励和外在激励有何不同？

How do intrinsic and extrinsic motivation differ?

外在激励来自奖金、批评等外部回报，内在激励则来自兴趣、成就感等内部满足。研究提示过度依赖外在奖励有时反而削弱内在动力。有效管理常把两者结合，匹配任务与个人价值。
Extrinsic motivation comes from external rewards like bonuses or criticism, while intrinsic motivation comes from internal satisfaction such as interest or achievement. Research suggests over-relying on external rewards can sometimes weaken intrinsic drive. Effective management blends both and matches tasks to personal values.

马斯洛需求层次讲了什么？

What does Maslow's hierarchy of needs describe?

马斯洛把人的需求由低到高分为生理、安全、归属、尊重与自我实现五层，认为低层满足后才会追求高层。它常用于理解员工激励，但后来被批评过于线性、忽视文化差异。它仍是管理学的经典框架。
Maslow ranked needs from low to high as physiological, safety, belonging, esteem, and self-actualization, arguing people pursue higher needs only after lower ones are met. It is often used for employee motivation but later criticized as too linear and culturally blind. It remains a classic management framework.

“领导力”和“管理”是一回事吗？

Are “leadership” and “management” the same?

管理侧重计划、组织与控制以维持秩序，领导侧重设定方向、激励与变革以带动人心。优秀组织往往需要两者结合：既要稳健运营，也要有人描绘愿景。二者互补而非互相替代。
Management focuses on planning, organizing, and controlling to keep order, while leadership sets direction, motivates, and drives change. Good organizations need both: steady operation and someone painting the vision. They complement rather than replace each other.

什么是组织文化？

What is organizational culture?

组织文化是团队共享的价值观、信念与行为习惯，常体现在仪式、语言和做事方式中，非正式却深刻影响绩效。强文化能凝聚人心，也可能阻碍变革。它不像规章那样写明白，却“无处不在”。
Organizational culture is the shared values, beliefs, and habits of a team, shown in rituals, language, and ways of working; informal yet deeply affecting performance. A strong culture unites people but may resist change. It is not written like rules yet is “everywhere.”

组织社会学所说的“制度同构”(institutional isomorphism)是什么意思?它包含哪三种机制?

What does organizational sociology mean by 'institutional isomorphism', and which three mechanisms produce it?

迪马乔与鲍威尔发现,处于同一组织场域中的组织会随时间变得越来越相似,驱动力常常不是效率而是对合法性的追求。他们区分了强制同构(法律法规与监管要求)、模仿同构(在高度不确定时效仿被视为成功的同行)与规范同构(专业教育和行业准则的扩散)。这解释了韦伯所言的科层制“铁笼”为何在现代组织中反复重现。
Paul DiMaggio and Walter Powell observed that organizations within the same organizational field grow increasingly alike over time, driven less by efficiency than by the pursuit of legitimacy. They distinguished coercive isomorphism imposed by law and regulation, mimetic isomorphism under uncertainty when firms copy apparently successful peers, and normative isomorphism spread through professional training and industry codes. This explains why Weber's iron cage of bureaucracy keeps reappearing across modern organizations.

什么是组织中的目标置换?米歇尔的寡头铁律如何解释这一现象?

What is goal displacement in organizations, and how does Michels' iron law of oligarchy explain it?

米歇尔在研究德国社会民主党时发现,大规模组织必然催生专业化领导层,科层程序与领导者的既得利益会把组织拉离初衷,这就是寡头铁律。组织目标由此从原始宗旨转向自我延续,例如工会将资源用于维系会员而非推进原有诉求。默顿进一步指出,对规则的僵化服从本身也可能取代规则所要服务的目的。
Studying the German Social Democratic Party, Robert Michels found that large organizations inevitably produce professionalized leaderships whose bureaucratic routines and vested interests pull the body away from its founding purpose—the iron law of oligarchy. Goals then drift from mission toward self-perpetuation, as when unions devote resources to keeping members rather than advancing their original demands. Merton added that rigid rule-following itself can come to displace the ends the rules were meant to serve.

霍桑实验得出了什么结论？

What conclusion did the Hawthorne experiments draw?

1920年代在西部电气公司的实验发现，工人产量不仅受工时与报酬影响，更受关注、群体氛围等社会心理因素驱动，即“霍桑效应”。它开创了关注人而非仅效率的管理视角。行为科学由此进入组织研究。
Experiments in the 1920s at Western Electric found workers' output was driven not just by hours and pay but by attention and group atmosphere—the “Hawthorne effect.” It opened a management view focused on people, not just efficiency. Behavioral science thus entered organization studies.

为什么几乎所有公司都长成层级分明的金字塔?韦伯一百年前就给出了答案

Why do almost all firms grow into rigid pyramids? Max Weber answered a century ago.

韦伯把科层制(bureaucracy)定义为现代组织的理性化形态:明确的职权等级、成文规则、按专业资格录用晋升、档案化记录。对比传统型(听命于君主个人)与魅力型(追随领袖感召)权威,法理型权威的优势是可预期与可复制——大公司能在全球开设分部并保持标准一致,靠的正是这套非人格化机器。科层也自带病理:目标置换(守规矩忘了办事)、彼得原理(胜任者被提拔到不胜任层)、繁文缛节。组织社会学百年研究都在回答同一问题:如何在保留科层效率的同时注入弹性与创新——矩阵制、扁平化都是这场拉锯的当代回合。
Weber defined bureaucracy as modern organizations rationalized form: explicit hierarchy, written rules, hiring and promotion by credentials, filed records. Against traditional authority (loyalty to persons) and charismatic authority (devotion to leaders), legal-rational authority wins on predictability and replicability — multinationals open branches worldwide with consistent standards precisely via this impersonal machine. Bureaucracy carries its own pathologies: goal displacement (rules over results), the Peter principle (promoted to incompetence), red tape. A century of organizational sociology asks one question: how to keep bureaucratic efficiency while injecting flexibility and innovation — matrix structures and flat organizations are just its latest rounds.
