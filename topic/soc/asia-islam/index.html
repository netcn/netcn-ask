为什么宗教法（sharia）能与世俗法律在许多社会并存？

Why can religious law (sharia) coexist with secular law in many societies?

许多社会采用“属人法”的分域模式：世俗法管公共与商业事务，宗教法管各社群的婚姻、家庭与继承。这种务实的区隔让多元法律体系并行，不必以某一方完全取代另一方。
Many societies adopt a 'personal-status' division: secular law governs public and commercial matters while religious law handles marriage, family and inheritance for each community. This pragmatic compartmentalization lets plural legal orders coexist without one fully replacing the other.

为什么中世纪伊斯兰世界曾在科学与贸易上长期领先？

Why was the medieval Islamic world long ahead in science and trade?

中世纪伊斯兰世界处在欧亚大陆的十字路口，掌控着利润丰厚的贸易路线，并把财富投入学术。其相对开放、重视翻译的文化保存并发展了希腊、波斯与印度的知识，在数学、医学和光学等领域取得突破。
The medieval Islamic world sat at the crossroads of Eurasia, controlled lucrative trade routes, and channeled wealth into scholarship. Its relatively open, translation-friendly culture preserved and advanced Greek, Persian and Indian knowledge, yielding breakthroughs in mathematics, medicine and optics.

什么是“乌玛”（ummah），它和一般的“国家”有什么不同？

What is the 'ummah', and how does it differ from a ordinary state?

乌玛指全球穆斯林的信仰共同体，以宗教认同相连，超越民族与国界；它更像“跨国的精神—社会网络”，而非一块固定领土上的政府。
Ummah means the global community of Muslims, bound by faith beyond nation and border; it is more a transnational spiritual-social network than a government over fixed territory.

伊斯兰的“天课”（zakat）是如何组织社会的？

How does Islamic almsgiving (zakat) organize society?

天课是信徒按财产比例缴纳的施舍，集中用于救济贫者、债务人与公共需要。它把宗教义务变成再分配机制，强化共同体内部的互助与凝聚。
Zakat is a proportional charity from wealth, pooled for the poor, debtors and public needs. It turns religious duty into redistribution, strengthening mutual aid and cohesion within the community.

为什么麦加是穆斯林朝圣的中心？

Why is Mecca the center of Muslim pilgrimage?

麦加是伊斯兰教发源地，有禁寺和克尔白，先知穆罕默德在此传教。每位有能力的穆斯林一生应朝觐一次，使麦加成为全球穆斯林的精神焦点。
Mecca is Islam's birthplace, home to the Grand Mosque and the Kaaba where the Prophet preached. Every able Muslim should pilgrimage once, making Mecca the faith's spiritual focus worldwide.
