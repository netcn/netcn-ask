什么是“慕课（MOOC）”？它改变了什么？

What is a MOOC and what did it change?

慕课是大规模在线开放课程，让名校课程免费或低价向全球开放，降低学习门槛。但它完成率低，难以完全替代学历与面对面培养。
A MOOC is a massive open online course bringing elite classes to the world cheaply or free, lowering learning barriers. But completion is low, and it hardly replaces degrees or face-to-face training.

什么是“教育代际流动”？它如何衡量？

What is “intergenerational educational mobility” and how is it measured?

它衡量子女教育水平相较父母是上升、持平还是下降，常用代际相关系数表示。系数越低说明教育机会越平等、阶层固化越弱。
It measures whether children’s education rises, stays, or falls versus parents’, often via an intergenerational correlation coefficient. Lower coefficients mean more equal opportunity and weaker class lock-in.

为什么“学区房”价格常高于同地段其他房？

Why are “school-district homes” often pricier than nearby ones?

因绑定优质公立学位，购房被视为买入学资格，需求被附加价值推高。它折射教育资源分布不均与家长对好学校的竞争。
Tied to quality public seats, buying such homes is seen as buying admission, pushing demand and price up. It reflects uneven education resources and parental competition for good schools.

什么是“就近入学”政策？

What is the “nearby schooling” policy?

指义务教育阶段按户籍或居住地划片，优先入读附近公办学校，旨在均衡资源、减轻择校焦虑。但它也引发学区房涨价等副作用。
In compulsory education, students are assigned to nearby public schools by household or residence zoning, to balance resources and ease school-choice anxiety. It also fuels school-district housing prices.

为什么“双减”政策针对校外培训？

Why did the “double reduction” policy target after-school tutoring?

政策旨在减轻学生作业与校外培训负担，缓解内卷与家庭焦虑，并降低教育成本。它引发培训行业收缩，也带来托管与素质教育新需求。
The policy aims to cut homework and after-school tutoring burdens, easing competition and family anxiety and lowering education cost. It shrank the tutoring industry and raised demand for childcare and well-rounded education.

为什么幼儿园和小学重视“养成教育”？

Why do kindergarten and primary school stress “habit formation”?

幼年阶段是规则意识、生活习惯与社交能力建立的关键期，比知识灌输更影响长远发展。好习惯能降低日后学习与行为成本。
Early childhood is a key window for rules, habits, and social skills—more decisive for long-term growth than rote knowledge. Good habits lower later learning and behavior costs.

什么是“终身学习”？为什么它越来越重要？

What is “lifelong learning” and why is it more important?

终身学习指离开学校后持续获取知识与技能，以适应职业变化与技术迭代。在AI与自动化时代，单一学历难以覆盖整个职业生涯。
Lifelong learning means continuously gaining knowledge and skills after school to adapt to job change and tech churn. In the AI age, a single degree rarely covers an entire career.

为什么“高考”在中国社会如此受重视？

Why is the “gaokao” (college entrance exam) so important in Chinese society?

高考是大规模、相对公平的选拔通道，成绩直接影响升学与早期职业机会。它承载阶层流动期待，也被批评“一考定终身”压力大。
The gaokao is a large-scale, relatively fair selection channel whose scores directly shape college and early career chances. It carries hopes of mobility but is criticized for high “one exam decides all” pressure.
