卡德与克鲁格 1994 年的新泽西快餐业研究如何挑战了最低工资提高必损就业的常识？

How did Card and Krueger's 1994 New Jersey fast-food study challenge the conventional view that raising the minimum wage always reduces employment?

两人利用新泽西提高最低工资、相邻的宾夕法尼亚未变的「自然实验」，对比了两地快餐店雇佣变化。结果发现新泽西就业不降反略升，质疑了简单供需模型，并推动了差分中的差分（DID）方法在劳动经济学中的普及。
Using the natural experiment of New Jersey raising its minimum wage while neighbouring Pennsylvania did not, they compared hiring at fast-food restaurants. Employment in New Jersey rose slightly rather than falling, contesting the simple supply-demand model and popularising difference-in-differences (DID) methods in labour economics.

性别工资差距的「未解释部分」通常用什么方法分解？

What method is commonly used to decompose the unexplained part of the gender pay gap?

奥克斯卡-布林德分解将工资差距拆为可观测特征（教育、经验）贡献与残差（常归因于歧视或不可观测因素）。OECD 就业展望据此报告多数成员国差距中相当部分仍属未解释。
The Oaxaca-Blinder decomposition splits the wage gap into a part explained by observable characteristics (education, experience) and a residual often attributed to discrimination or unobservables. OECD Employment Outlook reports that a substantial share remains unexplained across member countries.

自动化与人工智能对就业总量是中性的吗？奥托尔如何解释「任务模型」？

Is automation and AI neutral for total employment? How does Autor explain the task model?

奥托尔的任务模型认为技术既替代常规任务、又互补非常规认知任务，因此长期就业总量未必下降，但岗位结构极化。世界银行 2019 年发展报告进一步指出工作性质变化大于工作消失。
Autor's task model holds that technology both substitutes routine tasks and complements non-routine cognitive ones, so total employment need not fall long term but jobs polarise. The World Bank's 2019 report argues that the nature of work changes more than work disappears.

波兰尼的「双向运动」如何解释劳动力市场的制度嵌入？

How does Polanyi's double movement explain the institutional embeddedness of labour markets?

波兰尼在《大转型》中指出，劳动力等商品是「虚拟商品」，完全市场化会破坏社会，因此社会会以保护性立法反向运动。这解释了为何劳动力市场始终被最低工资、工会等制度所嵌入，而非纯粹自由交换。
In The Great Transformation, Polanyi argues that labour is a 「fictitious commodity」 whose full commodification would destroy society, prompting protective counter-movements. This explains why labour markets are always embedded in institutions like minimum wages and unions rather than pure free exchange.

哈特1973年关于阿克拉城市低收入者的研究如何催生“非正规部门”这一概念？它改变了发展研究中对城市贫困的哪些假设？

How did Keith Hart's 1973 study of low-income urban Accra generate the concept of the 'informal sector', and which assumptions about urban poverty did it overturn in development studies?

哈特发现阿克拉的移民并未真正“失业”，而是通过摆摊、搬运、中介、打零工等未被官方登记的活动谋取收入，据此区分了“正规”与“非正规”收入机会。这一概念打破了把城市贫民视为被动剩余人口的图景，表明他们嵌入在真实运作的市场与关系网络中。它也促使学界追问：非正规性究竟是前现代的残留，还是国家规制、执照制度与发展战略共同塑造甚至有意生产的产物。
Hart found that migrants in Accra were not truly unemployed but earned through unregistered activities—market stalls, porterage, brokerage, casual jobs—and he therefore distinguished formal from informal income opportunities. The concept displaced the image of the urban poor as a passive residual population by showing them embedded in working markets and networks. It also pushed scholars to ask whether informality is a pre-modern residue or something jointly shaped, even deliberately produced, by state regulation, licensing, and development strategy.

布洛威在《制造同意》中追问：为什么工人不只服从管理，还会主动卖力生产？赶工游戏在其中扮演什么角色？

In Manufacturing Consent, Burawoy asks why workers not only submit to management but actively strive to produce. What role does the game of making out play in this?

布洛威对美国一家机械厂十个月的参与观察显示，工人靠超额完成定额的赶工游戏排解流水线的单调，并在同伴中赢得地位。管理层通过计件工资、内部劳动力市场等规则组织这场游戏，把注意力从报酬是否公正转向怎样才能赢。工人在游戏中尝到自主与成就感，于是自愿配合生产，剥削在日常同意中被掩盖并延续。
Based on ten months of participant observation in an American machinery plant, Burawoy showed that workers beat quotas in a game of making out that relieves assembly-line monotony and earns standing among peers. Management organizes the game through rules such as piece rates and internal labor markets, shifting attention from whether pay is fair to how to win. Experiencing autonomy and achievement inside the game, workers cooperate willingly, so exploitation persists masked by everyday consent.

什么是劳动参与率？它为何会随人口老龄而下降？

What is the labour force participation rate, and why does it fall as populations age?

劳动参与率是就业与积极求职人口占劳动年龄人口的比例。人口老龄化使退休者占比上升、适龄劳动人口下降，从而压低整体参与率，OECD 多国已观察到这一趋势。
The labour force participation rate is the share of the working-age population that is employed or actively seeking work. Population ageing raises the retired share and lowers the working-age share, dragging the rate down, a trend OECD observes across many countries.

零工经济的主要特征是什么？

What are the main features of the gig economy?

零工经济以平台中介、短期任务化用工和劳动者缺乏传统雇佣保障为特征。劳动者多为独立承包商，工作时间灵活但缺少社保与集体谈判权，OECD 将其视为非标准就业的重要组成。
The gig economy is characterised by platform mediation, short task-based jobs and the absence of traditional employment protections. Workers are mostly independent contractors with flexible hours but little social insurance or collective bargaining power, which OECD counts as a key form of non-standard employment.

什么是自然失业率？它为什么不会为零？

What is the natural rate of unemployment, and why does it never fall to zero?

自然失业率是指经济在充分就业状态下仍存在的失业率，主要由摩擦性失业和结构性失业构成。弗里德曼指出，由于信息不对称、劳动力流动和技能错配，市场总有岗位空缺与求职并存，因此自然失业率不会为零。
The natural rate of unemployment is the level that persists even when the economy is at full employment, driven by frictional and structural unemployment. Friedman argued that information asymmetry, labour mobility and skill mismatches mean vacancies and job seekers always coexist, so the natural rate is never zero.

霍克希尔德提出的“情绪劳动”(emotional labor)指什么?它与私人生活中的情感工作有什么区别?

What did Arlie Hochschild mean by 'emotional labor', and how does it differ from emotion work in private life?

情绪劳动指劳动者按照雇主设定的表达规则管理自身感受,以便向顾客展示规定情绪并以此换取工资,典型如空乘人员必须始终保持微笑。它与私人生活中的情感管理不同:后者是霍克希尔德所称的“情感工作”(emotion work),服务于家庭与亲密关系,既不出售也不受组织监督,而情绪劳动则把感受本身变成出售的商品。她进一步区分仅调整外显表情的“表层扮演”与真正改动内心体验的“深层扮演”,并指出当感受被商品化时,从业者容易出现自我疏离、角色混淆与职业倦怠。
Emotional labor denotes workers managing their feelings according to employer-set display rules so as to show customers prescribed emotions in exchange for wages, as when flight attendants must keep smiling. It differs from the parallel managing of feeling in private life, which Hochschild calls 'emotion work': the latter serves family and intimate ties and is neither sold nor organizationally supervised, whereas emotional labor turns feelings themselves into commodities. She further distinguished 'surface acting', which adjusts only outward expression, from 'deep acting', which genuinely reshapes inner experience, warning that commodified feeling tends toward self-alienation, role confusion, and burnout.

空乘人员的微笑为什么被霍克希尔德视为一种劳动,而不只是个人礼貌?

Why did Hochschild treat flight attendants' smiles as labor rather than mere personal politeness?

霍克希尔德在《心灵的整饰》中提出情绪劳动概念:服务业雇员必须按雇主的脚本管理自己的感受,把内心状态整饰成职业表情。这种劳动持续消耗自我,容易产生表层扮演带来的情感疏离。它揭示情感不只是私人体验,更是被组织征用、监督并定价的生产要素。
In The Managed Heart, Arlie Hochschild introduced emotional labor: service employees must manage their feelings to an employer's script, polishing inner states into professional expressions. This work steadily drains the self and breeds the estrangement known as surface acting. It shows emotion is not merely private experience but a productive resource that organizations requisition, monitor, and price.
