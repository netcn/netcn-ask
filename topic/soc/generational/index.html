人口转型如何影响代际赡养负担？

How does demographic transition affect the intergenerational dependency burden?

随着生育率下降与寿命延长，劳动年龄人口相对减少、老年人口增多，每位工作者需供养的退休者比例上升，养老金与医疗压力加大，代际转移负担加重。
As fertility falls and lifespans rise, the working-age share shrinks while elders grow, so each worker supports more retirees—raising pension and health costs and the intergenerational transfer burden.

公共政策中的“代际公平”指什么？

What does "intergenerational equity" mean in public policy?

代际公平要求今天的政策不把成本转嫁给未来世代，如控制债务、保护环境、维持养老金可持续，使各代人负担相对合理。
Intergenerational equity demands today's policies not shift costs to future generations—e.g., controlling debt, protecting the environment, and keeping pensions sustainable—so burdens are fairly shared across eras.

什么是“代际差异”？

What are "generational differences"?

代际差异指不同出生年代的人在价值观、消费习惯和技术使用上呈现系统性不同，常由各自成长期的经济、社会与科技环境塑造。
Generational differences are systematic variations in values, consumption, and tech use across birth cohorts, shaped by the economic, social, and technological conditions of their formative years.
