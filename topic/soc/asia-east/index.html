为什么东亚“高语境”文化会影响人们的沟通方式？

Why does East Asia's 'high-context' culture shape how people communicate?

高语境文化里，含义很大程度由语境、关系和潜台词承载，而非直白的语言本身。因此沟通更含蓄，依赖彼此默契，常避免正面冲突以维护面子与关系和谐。
In high-context cultures, meaning is carried heavily by context, relationships and unspoken cues rather than by explicit words. Communication therefore tends to be indirect, relies on shared understanding, and avoids open confrontation to preserve face and relational harmony.

越南的社会文化在哪些方面受到儒家与科举影响？

In what ways did Vietnam's society absorb Confucian and exam influences?

越南历史上长期仿行科举、尊孔设学，重视家庭孝道、敬祖与读书取仕。虽然语言属南亚语系，社会结构却带有明显的东亚儒家痕迹。
Vietnam long ran its own exams and Confucian schools, valuing filial piety, ancestor respect and study-for-office. Though its language is Austroasiatic, its social structure shows clear East Asian Confucian marks.

为什么韩国社会特别看重学历和考试？

Why does South Korean society prize credentials and exams so much?

受科举遗风与战后资源有限的影响，考试被视为最公平的上升通道；高学历直接挂钩好工作与社会评价，于是补习与升学竞争异常激烈。
Shaped by exam traditions and postwar scarcity, tests were seen as the fairest ladder up; degrees linked directly to good jobs and status, making cram schools and admission competition intense.

日本的传统“村落社会”有什么特点？

What are the features of Japan's traditional 'village society'?

日本村落强调“共同体”与相互扶助（如结、讲、组），讲究和睦与不出头；个人行为受邻里眼光约束。这种“纵向社会”与强集体规范影响至今。
Japanese villages stressed community and mutual aid (such as kumi groups), valuing harmony and not standing out; neighbors' eyes constrained behavior. This 'vertical society' with strong group norms still echoes today.

为什么日本还保留很多传统仪式（如成人式、茶道）？

Why does Japan still keep many traditional rituals like coming-of-age day and tea ceremony?

仪式把个人成长、群体归属和文化记忆连在一起；现代生活节奏快，人们反而更想借仪式获得安定与认同，所以传统被刻意保留并定期举行。
Rituals tie personal growth, group belonging and cultural memory together; in fast modern life people seek stability and identity through them, so traditions are deliberately kept and repeated.
