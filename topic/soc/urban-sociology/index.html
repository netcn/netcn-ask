沃思在《作为一种生活方式的城市性》中如何用规模、密度与异质性定义城市性，并预言其社会后果？

In 'Urbanism as a Way of Life', how did Wirth define urbanism via size, density, and heterogeneity, and what social consequences did he predict?

沃思把城市性视为由人口规模大、密度高、异质性强共同塑造的生活模式，导致次级关系、匿名与正式控制取代亲密共同体。该生态学视角奠定了城市社会学的经典命题。
Wirth defined urbanism by large size, high density, and heterogeneity, yielding secondary relationships, anonymity, and formal control replacing intimate community. This ecological framing set classic urban-sociology propositions.

雅各布斯在《美国大城市的死与生》中为何反对以高速公路和清除贫民窟为导向的现代城市规划？她提倡什么原则？

Why did Jacobs oppose modernist planning built on highways and slum clearance in The Death and Life of Great American Cities, and what principles did she champion?

雅各布斯批评功能分区割裂街道生活、削弱“街道眼”式的自然监视，反而制造犯罪与衰败。她主张短街块、混合用途与老旧建筑共存以提升城市活力与安全。
Jacobs criticized zoning and clearance for severing street life and weakening 'eyes on the street' natural surveillance, breeding crime and decay. She advocated short blocks, mixed use, and old-building diversity for urban vitality and safety.

格莱泽在《城市的胜利》中用集聚经济解释城市为何仍具吸引力，他与雅各布斯的分歧在哪？

In Triumph of the City, how did Glaeser use agglomeration economies to explain cities' enduring appeal, and where does he differ from Jacobs?

格莱泽强调面对面交流、技能匹配与知识溢出带来的生产率提升，主张城市是高密度人力资本网络。他比雅各布斯更强调市场集聚收益，对怀旧式街区保护较不浪漫。
Glaeser stresses productivity gains from face-to-face exchange, skill matching, and knowledge spillovers, framing cities as dense human-capital networks. He leans more on market agglomeration than Jacobs and is less romantic about preservation.
