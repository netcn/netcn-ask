新闻里说的事都是真的吗?怎么判断一条消息可不可信?

Is everything on the news true? How can I tell if a message is trustworthy?

三招查证法:一看来源——正规媒体有记者署名和机构;二找证据——有没有照片、数据和当事人;三比多家——不同的可靠渠道说的是否一致。标题特别吓人、只让你转发的,多半要打个问号。会查证的人,谣言骗不到。
Three checks: source — real media name reporters and organizations; evidence — photos, data, named witnesses; cross-check — do reliable channels agree? Scary headlines begging for shares deserve question marks. Verify like a detective and rumors cannot fool you.

#媒介素养 #查证 #谣言

标题党为什么屡禁不止?夸张标题背后的流量逻辑是什么?

Why do clickbait headlines survive? What traffic logic hides behind exaggeration?

因为平台的推荐算法奖励点击:标题越耸动,打开率越高,广告收入越多。创作者被逼着在真实与流量之间做选择,劣币驱逐良币。破解之道在两端:平台把完读率、举报率纳入排序而非只看点击;读者用不点进来投票。当你不再为标题买单,标题党就失去了生存土壤。
Recommendation algorithms reward clicks: the more sensational the headline, the higher the opens and ad revenue, forcing creators to choose truth versus traffic as bad coins drive out good. Fixes work both ends: platforms ranking by read-through and reports rather than clicks alone, readers voting by not clicking. Starve clickbait of attention and it starves.

回音室效应是真的吗?算法推荐让我们更极化了吗?

Is the echo chamber real? Do recommendation algorithms actually polarize us?

经验证据比直觉复杂。一方面,选择暴露确实存在:人们偏好立场一致的信源,社交网络的好友同质性放大了这一点。另一方面,大规模研究发现普通用户的线上新闻饮食其实比自我感觉更多元,纯粹的回音室集中在少数重度党派用户;算法的责任常被高估——人际传递与主动搜索的贡献不小于排序模型。真正确定的机制是情绪经济学:愤怒与道德谴责内容获得更高互动,激励创作者生产对立标签化内容。所以解药不在打破气泡,而在平台激励机制与媒介素养双管齐下。
Evidence is messier than intuition. Selective exposure is real: people prefer congenial sources and homogeneous friend networks amplify it. Yet large-scale audits find ordinary users encounter more diverse news online than they believe, pure echo chambers concentrate among small highly partisan minorities, and algorithms take blame that human sharing and active search share equally. The solid mechanism is emotional economics: outrage and moral condemnation earn engagement, rewarding creators who produce adversarial labels. So the cure is not bursting bubbles but fixing platform incentives alongside media literacy.
