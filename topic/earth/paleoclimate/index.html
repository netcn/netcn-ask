雪球地球假说认为远古地球曾整体被冰封，证据是什么？

The snowball-Earth hypothesis holds that the ancient Earth was entirely ice-covered; what is the evidence?

雪球地球假说认为约7亿年前的成冰纪，冰川曾推进到赤道，全球被冰壳包裹。支持证据包括赤道地区发现的冰川沉积、后因二氧化碳积累而出现的盖帽碳酸盐岩。霍夫曼等人在1998年发表奠基性论文，基施文克则提供了早期的机制解释。
The snowball-Earth hypothesis argues that during the Cryogenian around 700 million years ago, glaciers reached the equator and ice sheathed the globe. Evidence includes glacial deposits found at equatorial latitudes and the cap carbonates that formed once carbon dioxide built up afterward. Hoffman and colleagues published the foundational paper in 1998, and Kirschvink provided an early mechanism.

How do Milankovitch cycles drive glacial-interglacial rhythms?

米兰科维奇循环指地球轨道偏心率、地轴倾角和岁差的长周期变化，改变各纬度接收到的太阳辐射季节分布。这些微弱变化叠加共振，触发并调节约十万年尺度的冰期与间冰期。
Milankovitch cycles are long-period changes in Earth's orbital eccentricity, axial tilt and precession that alter seasonal solar radiation by latitude. Their combined resonance triggers and paces the roughly 100,000-year glacial-interglacial rhythm.

黄土为什么是古气候档案？

Why is loess a paleoclimate archive?

风成黄土是风从干旱源区搬来、披覆在边缘的粉砂，连续、广布、易采样。层序中黄土层与古土壤层交替：干冷期风尘供强、成土弱，暖湿期堆积慢、土壤化强，气候摆动就成了层圈。粒度指示风力与源区远近，磁化率反映成土的水热状况。但这些都是替代指标：磁化率也受粒度与源区控制，须分区标定、对照有定年的记录，否则易把局部信号读成全球结论。
Aeolian loess is silt winnowed out of arid source areas and blanketed over their margins, and it makes a convenient archive because the sections are nearly continuous, spread over large areas and easy to sample. Loess layers alternate with buried soils: when it was cold and dry, dust supply was strong and soil formation weak; when warm and wet, accumulation slowed and pedogenesis intensified, so the climate swings are written as couplets. Grain size speaks to wind strength and distance from the source, magnetic susceptibility to the heat and moisture available during soil formation. Both are proxies, though - susceptibility also responds to grain size and source changes - so each region needs its own calibration and must be tied to records that carry a dating framework, otherwise a local signal gets read as a global conclusion.

氧同位素比率为什么能当作古温度计来重建过去的海温？

Why can oxygen-isotope ratios serve as a paleothermometer to reconstruct past sea temperatures?

海水中的氧有轻重两种同位素，温度较低时海洋生物壳体更倾向于吸收较重的氧-18，使壳体同位素比值随水温变化。通过测量有孔虫等化石壳体的氧-18与氧-16之比，可反推沉积时的温度。埃米利亚尼与沙克尔顿在1950至1970年代奠定了这一方法。
Seawater oxygen comes in light and heavy isotopes, and at lower temperatures marine shells preferentially take up the heavier oxygen-18, so the shell's isotope ratio tracks water temperature. Measuring the O-18 to O-16 ratio in fossils like foraminifera reconstructs the temperature at deposition. Emiliani and Shackleton established the method in the 1950s to 1970s.

什么是孢粉分析？

What is pollen analysis?

孢粉分析靠的是花粉外壁含极耐腐蚀的孢粉素，能在地层里长期保存，产量大、随风远扬，形态又常可辨到属甚至种。镜下计数、算出各植物类群的百分比就复原了古植被，再借植物分布受温湿度支配这层关系转译成气候。这是间接信号：风媒花粉产量占绝对优势，虫媒植物被系统性低估，远距搬运又混入外地花粉，所以它记的是区域植被而非脚下那片林。
Pollen analysis works because the pollen wall is built from sporopollenin, which is extremely resistant to decay, so grains survive once buried in sediment; they are also produced in enormous quantities, carried far by the wind, and often distinguishable to genus or even species by shape. Counting them under a microscope and expressing each plant group as a percentage reconstructs the vegetation of the time, and climate comes in at the second step, since plant distributions are governed by temperature and moisture. Note that this is an indirect signal. Wind-pollinated plants flood the pollen rain and insect-pollinated ones are systematically under-represented, and long-distance transport mixes in pollen from far away, so a diagram records regional vegetation rather than the woods outside the site.

黄土—古土壤序列如何记录冰期旋回？

How do loess-paleosol sequences record glacial cycles?

In the Loess Plateau, loess layers correspond to dry-cold wind-deposition glacial stages, while paleosol layers mark warm-humid weathering interglacials. Their alternating stacking acts like natural climate rings recording Quaternary glacial cycles.

底栖有孔虫壳体的δ18O同时包含冰量与深水温度两种信号,古海洋学家靠什么手段把二者解耦?

Benthic foraminiferal δ18O mixes ice-volume and deep-water temperature signals—how do paleoceanographers disentangle the two?

δ18O单指标无法区分冰量与温度贡献,需引入独立温度计:壳体Mg/Ca比值随钙化温度呈指数变化,可先行定量古水温。将Mg/Ca推算的温度效应从实测δ18O中扣除,剩余项即代表海水δ18O,据此反演大陆冰体积,该联合方法奠定了新生代冰盖演化曲线的基础。
A single δ18O record cannot separate ice-volume from temperature contributions, so an independent thermometer is required: shell Mg/Ca rises exponentially with calcification temperature and quantifies past water temperature first. Subtracting the Mg/Ca-derived effect from measured δ18O leaves the seawater component, from which continental ice volume is inverted—together these metrics underpin the Cenozoic ice-sheet evolution curve.

树木年轮为什么能反映气候？

Why can tree rings reflect climate?

温暖湿润年份树木长得快，年轮较宽；干旱寒冷年份年轮窄。通过测量系列树木年轮宽度并交叉定年，可重建数百上千年的温度与降水变化。
In warm wet years trees grow faster and rings are wider; in dry cold years rings are narrow. Measuring and cross-dating ring widths reconstructs centuries to millennia of temperature and precipitation change.

Why can corals record sea temperature?

Corals secrete calcium carbonate skeletons whose oxygen isotopes and trace elements shift with the seawater temperature at growth. Annual growth bands also date them, reconstructing tropical sea-surface temperature change.

What is the oxygen isotope thermometer?

水中较重的氧-18和较轻的氧-16在蒸发和降水中的比例随温度改变。海洋有孔虫壳或冰芯中两者的比值，可反推当时海温和全球冰量，是研究古温度的关键工具。
