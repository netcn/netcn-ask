海底的"磁条带"是怎样证明海底扩张的？

How do magnetic stripes on the seafloor prove seafloor spreading?

洋中脊喷出的玄武岩含磁性矿物，冷却到居里点以下时按当时地磁场方向被磁化。地磁场历史上多次倒转，新洋壳持续记录每次倒转，于是洋中脊两侧形成对称的、正反相间的磁条带，像磁带一样。海底磁异常图显示条带年龄随离脊距离增大而变老，与海底扩张的预测完全吻合，成为板块构造最有力的证据之一，也间接证实了古地磁学预言的地磁倒转史。
Basalt erupting at mid-ocean ridges contains magnetic minerals that lock in the direction of Earth's field when they cool below the Curie point. Because the geomagnetic field has reversed many times, newly formed crust records each flip, leaving symmetric stripes of normal and reversed polarity on both flanks of the ridge, like a tape recording. Maps of seafloor magnetic anomalies show stripes getting older away from the ridge, exactly as seafloor spreading predicts—one of the strongest confirmations of plate tectonics and independent proof of the reversal history paleomagnetism had predicted.

什么是板块回卷？

What is slab rollback?

俯冲板片比周围地幔冷而重，负浮力把它往深处拖。当下拖快过汇聚供给的速率，海沟与随之迁移的岩浆弧便朝洋侧后退，这就是板片回卷。回卷在上覆板块后侧造出空间：弧被拉伸减薄、地幔上升减压熔融，于是形成弧后盆地，俯冲角与震源分布随之调整。但回卷并非弧后扩张的唯一原因，地幔对流与沿走向拖曳也能拉开弧后；两速消长时海沟会转为推进。
A subducting slab is colder and denser than the mantle around it, so its negative buoyancy drags it downward. When that pull outruns the rate at which convergence feeds plate into the trench, the trench and the magma arc attached to it migrate oceanward - that is slab rollback. Rollback opens space behind the upper plate: the arc is stretched and thinned, mantle rises and melts by decompression, a back-arc basin forms, and the dip of the slab and the distribution of its earthquakes adjust accordingly. Rollback is not the only way to open a back-arc basin, however, since mantle convection and trench-parallel drag can stretch it too; and when the two rates change balance, the trench turns from retreating to advancing.

什么是洋中脊？

What is a mid-ocean ridge?

洋中脊是沿张裂边界延伸的全球海底山系：地幔上涌减压熔融，玄武岩在两侧添成新洋壳，两翼年龄与地形对称，磁异常条带向外排开。它是世界最长的山链，足以把地球绕一圈还有余，却几乎全埋在深海之下，冰岛是少数露出海面的地段。两处易混：中脊未必在海盆正中，东太平洋海隆就偏在洋盆一侧；脊轴被横向转换断层错成一段段，断层本身不扩张。
A mid-ocean ridge is the global chain of submarine mountains built along divergent plate boundaries: as mantle rises it melts by decompression, and the basalt is added to both sides as new oceanic crust, so the flanks mirror each other in age and shape and the magnetic stripes spread symmetrically outward. It is the longest mountain chain on the planet, long enough to wrap the Earth more than once, yet nearly all of its axis lies deep beneath the sea, with Iceland one of the rare places where it breaks the surface. Two confusions to avoid: a mid-ocean ridge need not sit in the middle of its basin, since the East Pacific Rise lies off-centre in the Pacific; and its axis is stepped into segments by transverse transform faults, which do not spread themselves.

魏格纳提出大陆漂移时，有哪些证据支持他？

What evidence did Alfred Wegener marshal for continental drift?

魏格纳1912年提出大陆漂移说，依据包括：大西洋两岸海岸线形态吻合；巴西与西非的岩带和地层连续；舌羊齿植物群以及中龙、水龙兽等淡水生物化石跨洋分布；古冰川遗迹指向统一的冈瓦纳大陆冰盖；煤田、蒸发盐等古气候标志与现代纬度不符。这些几何、地层、生物、冰川和气候方面的证据，都指向各大陆曾连为一体。当时学界普遍反对，因为魏格纳没能提出令人信服的驱动力，直到20世纪60年代板块构造理论补上了机制，大陆漂移说才被重新接受。
In 1912 Wegener proposed continental drift, citing the jigsaw fit of Atlantic shorelines; continuous rock belts and strata across Brazil and West Africa; identical fossils on now-separated continents, including the Glossopteris flora and the freshwater reptiles Mesosaurus and Lystrosaurus; glacial striations implying a single Gondwana ice sheet; and paleoclimate markers such as coalfields and evaporites inconsistent with present latitudes. These geometric, stratigraphic, biologic, glacial, and climatic lines of evidence all pointed to formerly connected continents. Critics objected mainly because Wegener lacked a convincing driving force; only in the 1960s did plate tectonics supply the mechanism, and continental drift was finally vindicated.

夏威夷火山链的年龄为什么从西北到东南越来越新？

Why are the Hawaiian volcanoes progressively younger toward the southeast?

夏威夷群岛上方的地幔热点固定在太平洋板块之下，来自地幔深处的热柱不断上涌提供岩浆，板块则向西北漂移，热点不断在新的位置"点燃"火山。于是西北端的火山最老、侵蚀最深，东南端的基拉韦厄火山最年轻、仍在喷发。热点火山链的方向和年龄梯度为板块运动的方向与速率提供了直接证据：考艾岛约500万年前喷发，而新火山活动延续至今。
A mantle hot spot sits fixed beneath the Pacific plate; a deep mantle plume feeds magma while the plate drifts northwest, so the plume lights volcanoes at new positions. The northwestern islands are therefore the oldest and most eroded, while Kilauea in the southeast is youngest and still erupting. The direction and age gradient of the chain give direct evidence of plate motion direction and speed: Kauai last erupted about 5 million years ago while the newest volcano is active today.

像圣安德烈亚斯断层这样的转换边界是如何调节板块运动的？

How do transform boundaries such as the San Andreas Fault accommodate plate motion?

转换边界上两块板块水平错动、既不生成也不消亡地壳，圣安德烈亚斯断层便是太平洋板块与北美板块的右旋滑动边界。应力沿断层积累并周期性释放，造成浅源走滑型地震。
At a transform boundary two plates slide horizontally past each other without creating or destroying crust; the San Andreas Fault is the right-lateral boundary between the Pacific and North American plates. Stress builds along the fault and releases periodically in shallow strike-slip earthquakes.

板块运动怎样反过来改变全球气候？

How does plate tectonics feed back on global climate?

板块运动通过多种途径影响气候：造山带抬升加速硅酸盐风化，风化过程消耗大气二氧化碳并把碳埋入海洋，是长时间尺度的降温机制；巴拿马地峡的隆升切断了大西洋与太平洋的赤道洋流，可能促成北半球冰期；青藏高原与喜马拉雅的隆起强化亚洲季风，改变全球大气环流。这些板块尺度变化以千万年计，是驱动地质历史时期冷暖交替的重要因素。
Plate tectonics alters climate through several paths: mountain building accelerates silicate weathering, which consumes atmospheric CO2 and buries carbon in the ocean—a long-term cooling mechanism; the rise of the Isthmus of Panama cut off equatorial flow between the Atlantic and Pacific and likely helped trigger Northern Hemisphere glaciation; and uplift of the Tibetan Plateau and Himalaya intensified the Asian monsoon, reshaping global circulation. Operating on tens of millions of years, these tectonic changes are major drivers of deep-time climate swings.

大洋板块为什么会在海沟处沉入地幔？俯冲带能产生哪些现象？

Why does an oceanic plate sink into the mantle at a trench, and what happens along a subduction zone?

大洋板块比下面软流层的岩石更冷、更重、密度更大，于是会在海沟处像传送带一样弯折下沉进入地幔。俯冲板片在深处释放水分并诱发熔融，岩浆上升形成岛弧火山链，同时板片间的摩擦会引发频繁地震。著名的环太平洋火山地震带正是众多俯冲带拼接而成的。
Oceanic plates are colder, denser, and heavier than the asthenosphere below, so they bend and sink into the mantle at trenches like a conveyor belt. As the slab descends it releases water, triggering melting that feeds island-arc volcano chains, while friction between plates generates frequent earthquakes. The famous Pacific Ring of Fire is exactly a chain of such subduction zones.

What is a transform fault?

转换断层是两盘沿其走向只做水平剪切错动的板块边界，它常把洋中脊错断成一截一截，沿线地震浅、垂直升降小，既不造山也无深源地震。它容易被误认成普通走滑断层：真错动只发生在相邻两段洋中脊之间，脊外侧两盘其实同向移动，图上巨大的错距只是假象，并非实际位移量。圣安德烈斯断层就是把洋中脊的转换段延伸到大陆上的例子。
A transform fault is a plate boundary whose two sides only slide horizontally along its strike. It commonly cuts mid-ocean ridges into offset segments, and quakes along it are shallow with little vertical movement, so it builds no mountains and has no deep-focus earthquakes. It is easily mistaken for an ordinary strike-slip fault: real displacement happens only between two adjacent ridge segments, because outside them both sides move in the same direction, so the huge offset on a map is an appearance, not the actual amount of slip. The San Andreas fault is a ridge transform segment extended onto a continent.

板块运动的主要驱动力是什么？

What drives plate motion?

板块运动的背景是地幔对流，但推拽力多来自板块自身：冷而密的板片在海沟下沉，拖住后面的板块，即板片拖拽；洋中脊抬高，新洋壳沿重力向两侧滑出，即脊推。一般认为板片拖拽最强，故有长俯冲带的板块最快，每年数厘米，与指甲生长同量级。易混的是把对流当传送带直接拖着岩石圈：对流只管输运热量，直接做功的是重力，板底拖曳有时反成阻力。
Mantle convection sets the background, but the forces that move plates come mostly from the plates themselves: a cold, dense slab sinks at a trench and drags the plate behind it, which is slab pull, while the mid-ocean ridge stands high and the young crust slides gravitationally outward, which is ridge push. Slab pull is generally held to be the strongest, which is why plates with a long subduction zone at their edge move fastest, a few centimetres a year, the same order as fingernail growth. The confusion to avoid is treating convection as a conveyor belt that drags the lithosphere along: convection only carries heat, gravity does the direct work, and basal drag can even act as resistance.

地球表面主要有几大板块？

How many major tectonic plates does Earth have?

通常分为六大主要板块：太平洋、欧亚、印度洋、非洲、美洲和南极洲板块，还有许多小板块。
Usually six major plates: Pacific, Eurasian, Indo-Australian, African, American and Antarctic, plus many smaller ones.
