河流纵剖面为何常呈现上凹的“幂律”形态？其与气候和岩性的耦合机制是什么？

Why do river longitudinal profiles often show a concave-up power-law shape, and how is this coupled to climate and lithology?

在均衡状态下，河流比降随汇水面积增大而减小，满足 S ∝ A^(-θ)（θ 为凹度），由物质守恒与水流功率平衡导出。岩性坚硬段形成陡坎，强降水区下切更快，使剖面形态记录区域气候与构造的相互作用。
At equilibrium, channel slope decreases with drainage area as S ∝ A^(−θ) (θ the concavity), derived from mass conservation and stream-power balance. Resistant lithology creates knickpoints, and high precipitation accelerates incision, so the profile encodes the interplay of climate and tectonics.

河口羽流（river plume）的营养盐输出如何影响近岸生态系统？

How does nutrient export from a river plume affect nearshore ecosystems?

大河河口形成的低密度羽流将淡水与营养盐（氮、磷、硅）输送到陆架，常促进浮游植物勃发与渔业生产力；但过量营养盐也可引发低氧“死亡区”，如密西西比河羽流下游的墨西哥湾缺氧区。
Low-density plumes from large rivers deliver freshwater and nutrients (N, P, Si) onto the shelf, often boosting phytoplankton blooms and fisheries productivity; but excess nutrients can trigger hypoxia dead zones, such as the Gulf of Mexico hypoxia linked to the Mississippi plume.

河流的“侵蚀基准面”概念是什么？海平面变化如何影响河流下切？

What is the concept of a river's base level, and how do sea-level changes affect river incision?

侵蚀基准面是河流下切所能达到的最低高程，通常以海平面为终极基准面。海平面下降会增大河流坡降，促进下切与峡谷形成；海平面上升则减弱下切、增强堆积，河口转为加积。
Base level is the lowest elevation a river can erode down to, with sea level as the ultimate base. Falling sea level steepens the river gradient, promoting incision and canyon cutting; rising sea level reduces incision and enhances deposition, shifting estuaries to aggradation.

河流注入相对静水盆地后,三角洲是如何通过分流河道摆动、前积层推进与决口改道逐步向海进积的?其内部地层格架为何常表现为倾斜的斜层反射?

Once a river enters a standing water body, how does its delta prograde seaward through distributary-channel swing, foreset advance, and crevasse avulsion, and why is the internal stratigraphic architecture often expressed as inclined clinoform reflections?

三角洲进积的动力来自河流输沙在河口的快速卸载:粗颗粒先在河口坝堆积构成三角洲前缘相,细粒物质则被搬运至更远的底积层。随着分流河道反复摆动、决口扇不断叠置,前积层持续向盆地推进,形成向上变浅、以倾斜斜层理为标志的进积序列。密西西比河三角洲的鸟足状形态正是多次决口改道导致朵叶体迁移叠合的直接结果。
Delta progradation starts with rapid sediment dumping at the river mouth: coarse grains build mouth-bar deposits of the delta front while fines are carried farther out onto the prodelta. As distributary channels swing repeatedly and crevasse splays stack, foresets keep advancing basinward, producing a shallowing-upward regressive succession marked by inclined clinoforms. The birdfoot outline of the Mississippi Delta records exactly this lobe switching under repeated avulsion.

什么是河流的“截弯取直”？对地貌有何影响？

What is river cutoff, and what are its geomorphic effects?

当曲流颈部被洪水冲决或渐进颈缩使河道直接连通，便发生截弯取直。旧河道废弃成牛轭湖，主河流程缩短、坡降变陡，下游输沙与侵蚀格局随之改变。
When a meander neck is breached by flood or narrows until the channel reconnects directly, a cutoff occurs. The abandoned loop becomes an oxbow lake, the main channel shortens and steepens, and downstream sediment and erosion patterns shift.

什么是河流的搬运能力？它与流速有何关系？

What is a river's transporting capacity, and how does it relate to flow velocity?

搬运能力指河流能够搬运的泥沙粒径与总量。经典水力学经验表明，推移质输运率约与流速的 6 次方成正比，因此流速稍有增加，河流搬运沙石的能力便急剧上升，洪水期输沙量由此剧增。
Transporting capacity is the size and total mass of sediment a river can carry. Classic hydraulic experience holds that bedload transport scales with roughly the sixth power of flow velocity, so even a modest speed increase sharply raises the river's ability to move sand and gravel, which is why flood-stage sediment loads surge.

什么是河流阶地？它记录了怎样的地貌演化？

What is a river terrace, and what geomorphic evolution does it record?

河流阶地是昔日河床或泛滥平原被抬升或基准面下降后留下的阶梯状地貌。多级阶地反映区域地壳抬升、气候变化或海平面变动的序列，是恢复古环境与构造活动的重要证据。
A river terrace is a step-like landform left when a former channel or floodplain is raised by uplift or lowered base level. Multiple terrace levels record sequences of crustal uplift, climate change, or sea-level shifts, key evidence for reconstructing paleoenvironments and tectonics.

三角洲是如何形成的？为何许多大型三角洲面临下沉风险？

How do deltas form, and why are many large deltas subsiding?

河流入海（湖）时流速骤减，泥沙在河口堆积形成三角洲。大量三角洲因上游水库拦截泥沙、地下水与油气超采导致地面沉降，加之海平面上升，正面临淹没与侵蚀加剧的风险。
Where a river meets the sea or lake, its velocity drops and sediment accumulates to build a delta. Many deltas now subside because upstream dams trap sediment and groundwater or hydrocarbon extraction causes land subsidence, compounded by sea-level rise.

什么是河流的曲流（meander）？它是如何形成的？

What is a river meander, and how does it form?

曲流是河流在低坡度平原上呈弯曲蜿蜒的河道。水流惯性使凹岸侵蚀、凸岸沉积，弯曲不断加剧并向侧向迁移，最终可能截弯取直形成牛轭湖。
A meander is a sinuous bend in a river on low-gradient plains. Flow inertia drives erosion on the outer (concave) bank and deposition on the inner (convex) bank, deepening the curve and migrating it laterally until cutoff forms an oxbow lake.

都是河流携泥沙入海堆积而成,为什么有的三角洲呈鸟足状,有的却是圆滑弧形?

All deltas form where rivers drop their sediment into the sea, yet some spread out bird-foot shaped while others bulge in smooth arcs — why?

关键在于河流输沙与波浪、潮汐三种动力的强弱对比。河流作用占优时,泥沙沿分流河口持续向海推进,形成密西西比河式的鸟足状外形;波浪能量强则把河口泥沙沿岸重新分配,堆出平滑的弧形轮廓;潮差大的区域,往复潮流冲刷出多条喇叭状汊道。这套三端元框架由Galloway于1975年提出,至今仍是三角洲分类的基础。
The controlling factor is the balance between river-borne sediment supply and marine wave versus tidal energy. Where river processes dominate, sediment keeps prograding seaward from distributary mouths into a Mississippi-style bird foot; strong waves rework the mouth deposits alongshore into smooth arcuate outlines; large tidal ranges scour several funnel-shaped channels instead. This three-end-member framework was proposed by Galloway in 1975 and still anchors delta classification.
