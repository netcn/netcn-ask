高光谱遥感相比多光谱在物质识别上有何优势？

What advantage does hyperspectral remote sensing have over multispectral for material identification?

高光谱传感器在数百个连续窄波段记录地物连续光谱，可捕捉矿物和植被的精细吸收特征（光谱指纹），从而区分成分相近的物质。它在地质填图、作物胁迫和污染识别中优于仅数个宽波段的多光谱数据。
Hyperspectral sensors record contiguous narrow bands across hundreds of channels, capturing fine absorption features, spectral fingerprints, that distinguish materials with similar composition. This outperforms multispectral data with only a few broad bands for geological mapping, crop stress, and pollution detection.

合成孔径雷达干涉测量凭什么能分辨毫米量级的地表位移?相位信息在其中扮演什么角色?

What allows InSAR to resolve surface displacement down to a few millimeters, and what work does the phase of the radar signal do in the method?

雷达回波的相位正比于传感器到地面的距离,把同一地区两次成像的相位相减,便得到两次成像期间距离的变化。一个整周期条纹约对应半个波长,C波段约为2.8厘米,经相位解缠后测量精度可推进到毫米级。扣除地形、大气延迟与轨道误差之后,干涉图即可刻画地震、火山活动或地面沉降造成的细微形变场。
The phase of a radar echo scales with the sensor-to-ground distance, so differencing phases from two passes over one scene yields the range change between those epochs. A single fringe cycle spans roughly half a wavelength—about 2.8 cm at C-band—and phase unwrapping pushes precision to the millimeter level. After removing topographic, atmospheric, and orbital terms, the interferogram maps the fine deformation fields produced by earthquakes, volcanic unrest, or subsiding ground.

环境DNA（eDNA）如何与遥感结合来监测生态系统？

How is environmental DNA (eDNA) combined with remote sensing to monitor ecosystems?

eDNA技术从水体和土壤样本中测序生物脱落的DNA以识别物种组成，遥感则提供大尺度栖息地结构（如水温、浊度、植被）。两者融合可在景观尺度上关联物种分布与环境变量，提升生物多样性监测效率。
eDNA sequencing identifies species from DNA shed into water and soil, while remote sensing supplies large-scale habitat structure such as water temperature, turbidity, and vegetation. Fusing the two links species distributions to environmental variables across landscapes, improving biodiversity monitoring.

归一化植被指数（NDVI）为什么能反映植被生长状况？

Why does the Normalized Difference Vegetation Index (NDVI) reflect vegetation condition?

健康叶片强烈吸收红光用于光合作用、反射近红外光，NDVI利用这两种波段之差归一化，数值越高代表植被越茂密健康。它被广泛用于监测农作物长势、森林覆盖与荒漠化。
Healthy leaves strongly absorb red light for photosynthesis and reflect near-infrared; NDVI normalizes the difference between these bands, with higher values indicating denser, healthier vegetation. It is widely used to monitor crops, forest cover, and desertification.

雷达干涉（InSAR）怎样测量地表形变？

How does radar interferometry (InSAR) measure ground deformation?

卫星两次经过同一地点拍摄雷达，对比相位差就能测出毫米级的抬升或下沉，用于监测火山、断层和沉降。
A satellite images the same spot twice; comparing radar phase reveals millimeter uplift or subsidence, monitoring volcanoes, faults and sinking land.

卫星测高如何持续监测全球海平面变化？

How does satellite altimetry continuously monitor global sea level change?

测高卫星向海面发射微波脉冲并测量往返时间，结合轨道与校正模型得到海面高度，精度可达厘米级。长期观测显示全球平均海平面以约3至4毫米/年的速率上升，主要由海水增温膨胀与冰川融化造成。
Altimetry satellites emit microwave pulses to the sea surface and time the return, combining orbit and correction models to derive sea-surface height with centimeter precision. Long records show global mean sea level rising about 3 to 4 mm per year, mainly from thermal expansion and ice melt.

星载或机载激光雷达（LiDAR）怎样获取高精度地形？

How do spaceborne or airborne LiDAR systems acquire high-precision terrain?

LiDAR向地面发射激光脉冲并精确测量往返时间，计算出密集的三维点位，能穿透林冠获取地表与植被结构。它被广泛用于绘制数字高程模型、洪水淹没范围与考古地貌。
LiDAR fires laser pulses at the ground and measures round-trip time precisely to compute dense 3-D points, penetrating forest canopy to capture both surface and vegetation structure. It is used for digital elevation models, flood mapping, and archaeological terrain.

卫星热红外遥感如何揭示城市热岛效应？

How does satellite thermal-infrared remote sensing reveal the urban heat island effect?

热红外传感器接收地表发射的长波辐射并反演地表温度，城市建成区因混凝土吸热、绿化少而显著高于周边农田与林地。长时间序列温度图可量化热岛强度及其随城市扩张的变化。
Thermal-infrared sensors record emitted longwave radiation and retrieve land surface temperature; urban built-up areas run markedly hotter than surrounding fields and forests because concrete stores heat and greenery is scarce. Long time series map heat-island intensity and its change with urban expansion.

为什么雷达遥感能在多云多雨地区正常成像？

Why can radar remote sensing image normally in cloudy and rainy regions?

主动雷达发射微波并接收回波，微波波长比云雨粒子大得多，可穿透云、雾和雨幕，因此不受天气与昼夜限制。这使它在热带雨林和极夜地区成为不可替代的观测手段。
Active radar transmits microwaves and records the return echo; microwave wavelengths are far larger than cloud and rain droplets, so they penetrate cloud, fog, and rain, operating regardless of weather or daylight. This makes radar indispensable over tropical rainforests and polar nights.

GRACE重力卫星怎样测算地下水储量？

How does the GRACE gravity satellite estimate groundwater storage?

双星测量地球重力场的微小变化，水多水少会改变重力，从而反推出区域地下水、冰川和土壤水的盈亏。
Twin satellites track tiny gravity-field changes; more or less water shifts gravity, revealing gains and losses in groundwater, ice and soil moisture.

在云层常年覆盖或极夜条件下,合成孔径雷达凭什么仍能源源不断地监测地表毫米级形变?

Under persistent cloud cover or polar night, how does synthetic aperture radar keep tracking millimeter-scale surface deformation without interruption?

SAR主动发射微波并接收回波,微波既能穿透云层又不依赖太阳光照,白天黑夜均可成像。干涉处理将两次过境的相位逐像素比对,把相位差换算为视线向位移,精度可达厘米乃至毫米级;这一原理使InSAR成为监测断层蠕滑、火山隆起和城市沉降的主力工具。
A SAR actively transmits microwaves and records the returns; the signal penetrates cloud and needs no sunlight, so imaging continues day and night. Interferometry compares the phase of repeat passes pixel by pixel, converting phase differences into line-of-sight displacements accurate to centimeters or even millimeters, which has made InSAR a workhorse for monitoring fault creep, volcanic inflation, and urban subsidence.

什么是遥感？

What is remote sensing?

遥感是用卫星、飞机或无人机从远处感知地表，通过拍摄不同波段的影像来观察土地、海洋和大气。
Remote sensing uses satellites, planes or drones to observe the surface from afar, imaging land, ocean and atmosphere in different wavelengths.
