有的年份江南“空梅”，一滴梅雨都没有，是怎么回事？

Some years the Yangtze delta has a dry plum season with no plum rain—why?

当副热带高压位置偏北或冷空气太弱，雨带直接跳到华北，长江中下游就跳过梅雨，容易出现夏旱。
If the subtropical high sits too far north or cold air is too weak, the rain band jumps to North China, skipping the Yangtze and causing summer drought.

#空梅 #副高

丝绸之路为什么大多走在季风区的边缘？

Why did the Silk Road mostly run along the edge of the monsoon region?

季风区内部雨水多、沼泽林密难行，边缘地带既有绿洲水源又较干旱易通行，于是商队沿河西走廊等半干旱带往来。
The monsoon core is rainy and overgrown; its arid edge offers oasis water yet easy travel, so caravans used semi-arid corridors like Hexi.

#丝绸之路 #绿洲廊道

季风如果“太强”或“太弱”，会带来什么麻烦？

What trouble comes when the monsoon is too strong or too weak?

太强会暴雨成灾、江河泛滥；太弱则伏旱少雨、田地开裂，所以季风不稳是东亚旱涝交替的主因。
Too strong brings floods; too weak brings summer drought and cracked fields—monsoon variability drives East Asia's alternating floods and droughts.

#季风异常 #旱涝

为什么夏季风总从海洋吹向陆地，而不是相反？

Why does the summer monsoon blow from sea to land rather than the reverse?

夏天陆地比海洋升温快，空气受热膨胀上升，近地面形成低压，海洋相对高压，风便从高压的海吹向低压的陆，把水汽带进去。
Land warms faster than the sea in summer; rising air creates low pressure over land while the sea stays high, so wind flows sea to land, carrying moisture.

#夏季风 #气压梯度

中国的季风为什么深受青藏高原影响？

Why is China's monsoon so influenced by the Qinghai–Tibet Plateau?

巨大高原夏天像一块巨型“加热器”抬升气流、吸引西南季风深入内陆，冬天又像“冷源”加强偏北风，从而放大了我国的季风强弱。
The huge plateau acts as a summer heat source that pulls the southwest monsoon inland and a winter cold source that strengthens northerly winds, amplifying China's monsoon.

#高原季风 #热力作用

江南初夏为什么总是一连几十天的阴雨绵绵？

Why does the Yangtze delta get weeks of drizzle every early summer?

这时南方暖湿气流和北方冷空气在长江中下游“僵持”，谁也赶不走谁，雨带停留形成连绵的梅雨。
Warm moist air from the south meets cold air from the north and stalls over the middle-lower Yangtze, leaving a stationary rain band—the plum-rain season.

#梅雨 #锋面

冬季风为什么又干又冷，和夏季风正好相反？

Why is the winter monsoon dry and cold, the opposite of summer?

冬天陆地比海冷，空气在陆地上方下沉形成高压，风从干冷的陆地吹向海洋，所以冬季风又干又冷、少雨。
In winter the land is colder than the sea, air sinks over it and creates high pressure, so wind blows from the dry cold land toward the ocean—dry and cold with little rain.

#冬季风 #高压

为什么中国南方比北方雨水多？

Why does southern China get more rain than the north?

南方更靠近海洋、又常受夏季风和台风影响，暖湿气流抬升容易成云下雨；北方离海远、干冷空气更强，降水就少。
The south is closer to the ocean and often hit by monsoons and typhoons, so moist air rises and rains; the north is farther from the sea with drier air.

#降水 #南北差异

季风区的主导风向为什么会随整个季节翻转,冬夏看起来像两套完全不同的系统?

Why do prevailing winds across monsoon regions reverse with the seasons, as if summer and winter ran two entirely separate systems?

根源是海陆热力差异的季节反转:夏季大陆升温远快于海洋,陆地上空形成热低压,把湿润气流从海上吸入内陆并带来集中降水;冬季大陆迅速冷却转为冷高压,干冷空气调头流向海洋,降水骤减。此外,青藏高原夏季强烈的抬升加热如同高空调热泵,显著放大了亚洲季风的强度,也造成雨季来临时的爆发式突变。
The root cause is the seasonal flip of the land-sea thermal contrast: in summer the continent heats far faster than the ocean, a thermal low forms over land, and moist air is drawn from the sea inland, delivering concentrated rainfall; in winter the land cools quickly into a cold anticyclone and dry air flows back toward the sea, collapsing the rains. On top of this, intense elevated heating of the Tibetan Plateau acts like an upper-level heat pump in summer, amplifying the Asian monsoon and sharpening its abrupt onset.

#季风环流 #海陆热力差异

为什么厄尔尼诺年份印度夏季风常常明显偏弱,甚至酿成大旱?

Why does the Indian summer monsoon often turn weak, occasionally bringing severe drought, in El Niño years?

直答:厄尔尼诺抬升赤道中东太平洋海温,使沃克环流上升支离开印度上空,季风对流受到压制。机理:常年暖池位于印度—西太平洋,旺盛对流维持季风槽;厄尔尼诺期间对流中心东移,印度上空转为异常下沉,水汽辐合与季风降水减弱,雨季中断延长;统计上多数严重旱年与厄尔尼诺对应,但印度洋海温和大气内部变率仍会调制结果。
El Niño warms the central-eastern equatorial Pacific and shifts the rising branch of the Walker circulation away from India. Convection normally anchored over the Indo-Pacific warm pool sustains the monsoon trough; when it migrates eastward, anomalous subsidence settles over the subcontinent, weakening moisture convergence, monsoon rainfall, and the rhythm of the rains. Most severe Indian droughts cluster in El Niño years, though Indian Ocean conditions and internal variability modulate each case.

#印度季风 #厄尔尼诺 #沃克环流

为什么夏天季风会带来那么多雨？

Why does the summer monsoon bring so much rain?

夏天陆地比大海热得快，空气上升形成低气压，潮湿的海风就吹过来，遇热上升变成云和雨，所以季风季总是湿漉漉。
In summer the land heats faster than the sea, air rises and makes low pressure, so moist sea breezes blow in and rise into clouds and rain—that's why monsoon season is so wet.

#季风 #海陆温差

什么是季风？为什么中国夏天多雨、冬天干冷？

What is the monsoon, and why is China rainy in summer but dry and cold in winter?

季风就是随季节反转的大风。夏天陆地热得快、空气上升，海上的湿润空气吹来下雨；冬天陆地冷、风从干冷的内陆吹向海。
A monsoon is a wind that reverses with seasons. In summer the land heats up, drawing moist sea air that brings rain; in winter cold dry inland air blows toward the sea.

#季风 #海陆差异

东亚夏季风的建立主要受什么机制驱动?

What mechanism mainly drives the onset of the East Asian summer monsoon?

直答:根本驱动力是海陆热力差异的季节性反转。机理:夏季陆地比海洋增温快,大陆低空形成热低压,青藏高原的强烈地表加热进一步增强低压并把空气抬升至高空;印度洋和南海的暖湿气流随西南季风向北推进,雨带沿副热带高压边缘依次带来华南前汛期、江淮梅雨与华北雨季;冬季热力差异反转,盛行干冷的偏北风。
The engine is the seasonal reversal of land-sea thermal contrast. In summer the continent heats faster than the ocean, setting up surface low pressure over Asia that plateau heating intensifies while pumping air aloft. Warm moist flow from the Indian Ocean and the South China Sea pushes north along the subtropical high margin, marching the rain belt through south China rains, Meiyu over the Yangtze valley, and North China rains. In winter the contrast flips and dry northerlies take over.
