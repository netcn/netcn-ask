地球磁场是怎么产生的？为什么会发生“地磁倒转”？

How is Earth's magnetic field generated, and why does it reverse?

地球磁场主要由液态外核中导电铁镍流体的对流（地磁发电机效应）产生，近似一个巨大的偶极磁场。地质记录显示磁场会不定期整体倒转（南北极互换），最近一次主要的布容—松山倒转约发生在78万年前，其机制与外核流体的复杂动力学有关。
Earth's magnetic field is mainly generated by convection of electrically conducting iron-nickel fluid in the liquid outer core—the geodynamo effect—approximating a giant dipole. Geological records show the field periodically reverses (north and south poles swap); the most recent major Brunhes–Matuyama reversal occurred about 780,000 years ago, tied to the complex dynamics of the outer-core flow.

地磁极性倒转是如何被记录在岩石中的？

How are geomagnetic reversals recorded in rocks?

当玄武岩冷却或沉积物沉积时，其中含铁矿物会沿当时地磁场方向排列，把磁场极性冻结下来。通过逐层测量这种原生剩磁，地质学家重建出倒转的时间序列，成为海底扩张的重要证据。梅里尔与麦克法登在《地球磁场》中系统论述了地磁倒转的机理。
As basalt cools or sediment settles, iron-bearing minerals align with the ambient field and lock in its polarity. Measuring this primary remanence layer by layer lets geologists reconstruct reversal sequences, a key proof of seafloor spreading. Merrill and McFadden's The Magnetic Field of the Earth details the reversal mechanism.

范艾伦辐射带是什么，它们如何保护地表生命？

What are the Van Allen radiation belts, and how do they shield life at the surface?

范艾伦带是地球磁层中被磁场俘获的高能带电粒子区域，分为内外两层环绕地球。它们把太阳风与宇宙射线中大部分粒子束缚在高空，减少到达地表的辐射。范艾伦在1958年通过早期卫星探测首次确认了这些带的存在，NASA的范艾伦探测器后续做了精细测量。
The Van Allen belts are regions of the magnetosphere where energetic charged particles are trapped by Earth's field, arranged in inner and outer layers. They confine most solar-wind and cosmic-ray particles at high altitude, reducing radiation reaching the surface. Van Allen confirmed them with early satellites in 1958, and NASA's Van Allen Probes later measured them in detail.
