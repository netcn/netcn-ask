米兰科维奇旋回用哪三种轨道变化解释冰期循环？

What three orbital variations does the Milankovitch cycle use to explain ice-age cycles?

米兰科维奇旋回包含地球轨道偏心率约10万年的变化、地轴倾角约4万年的摆动，以及岁差约2万年的进动。它们共同调制各纬度接收到的夏季日照量，从而触发或结束冰期。海斯、英布里与沙克尔顿在1976年用深海岩芯首次验证了这一理论。
The Milankovitch cycle combines changes in orbital eccentricity on about 100,000-year periods, axial tilt oscillation near 41,000 years, and precession of about 21,000 years. Together they modulate summer insolation by latitude, triggering or ending glaciations. Hays, Imbrie, and Shackleton verified the theory with deep-sea cores in 1976.

冰雪反照率反馈如何放大气候变化？

How does the ice-albedo feedback amplify climate change?

冰雪反射大部分入射阳光，使地表吸收的热量减少；当气候变暖使冰雪退缩，暴露出的暗色海岩或土壤吸收更多热量，进一步升温。这一正反馈会放大初始的温度扰动，使冰期和间冰期的变化更剧烈。布迪科在1969年的能量平衡模型最早量化了这一机制。
Snow and ice reflect most incoming sunlight, so less heat is absorbed; when warming retreats the ice, darker ocean or soil is exposed and absorbs more heat, raising temperatures further. This positive feedback amplifies the initial disturbance, sharpening glacial-interglacial swings. Budyko first quantified the mechanism with an energy-balance model in 1969.

冰芯记录如何重建数十万年前的气候与大气成分？

How do ice cores reconstruct climate and atmospheric composition from hundreds of thousands of years ago?

南极与格陵兰的冰层逐年积雪压实，把当时的大气气体与粉尘封存在气泡和层中。通过分析冰芯中的氧同位素、甲烷与二氧化碳浓度，可还原温度与温室气体历史。佩蒂等在1999年发表的东方站冰芯覆盖了过去42万年的完整记录。
Yearly snow in Antarctica and Greenland compacts into ice that traps the era's atmospheric gases and dust in bubbles and layers. Analyzing oxygen isotopes, methane, and carbon dioxide in the core reconstructs temperature and greenhouse-gas history. Petit and colleagues published the Vostok core in 1999, covering 420,000 years of record.
