大气重力波是如何把动量从对流层传递到中间层的？

How do atmospheric gravity waves transport momentum from the troposphere to the mesosphere?

重力波由山地气流、对流等激发，在稳定层结大气中向上传播并随密度减小而增幅，破碎后把动量沉积到高层，驱动中间层环流与准两年振荡。这种波-平均流相互作用是数值模式必须参数化的关键过程。
Gravity waves are generated by mountain flow and convection and propagate upward through stably stratified air, growing in amplitude as density drops; when they break they deposit momentum into higher layers, driving mesospheric circulation and the quasi-biennial oscillation. This wave-mean-flow interaction is a key process that climate models must parameterize.

科里奥利力怎样影响风向？

How does the Coriolis force affect winds?

地球自转让北半球运动的空气持续右偏、南半球左偏，而偏转方向总与运动方向垂直，所以它只改变风向，不给空气加速。纬度越高、风速越大，偏转越明显，赤道附近几乎不偏。真正推动空气的是气压梯度力，科里奥利力要等空气动起来才起作用；两者大致平衡时，风便沿等压线吹而不是直穿等压线，信风带、西风带和气旋的旋转都由此而来。
Earth's rotation makes moving air swing steadily right in the Northern Hemisphere and left in the Southern, and since the deflection always acts across the direction of travel, it turns the wind aside without speeding the air up. The swing grows with latitude and with wind speed, and near the equator it all but disappears. The pressure-gradient force is what actually drives the air, and Coriolis only takes hold once the air is already moving; when that deflection and the pressure gradient roughly balance, wind runs along the isobars instead of straight across them, which is where the trade winds, the westerlies and the spin of cyclones come from.

什么是露点？

What is the dew point?

前提是气压与水汽含量不变：空气降温，饱和水汽压下降，实际水汽压一到饱和值、相对湿度成 100%，水汽就在草叶上凝出，这温度即露点。它衡量水汽的实际多少，比相对湿度更可比：同为 80% 的相对湿度，暖空气含水汽远多于冷空气，沿海闷夜露点几乎贴着气温，内陆干夜则低得多。表面本身已低于 0°C 时，水汽越过液态直接凝华为霜。
The premise is constant pressure and unchanged vapor content: as air cools, saturation vapor pressure falls, and once the actual vapor pressure reaches it, when relative humidity becomes 100%, vapor condenses on a grass blade, and that temperature is the dew point. It measures how much vapor is really there, so it is more comparable than relative humidity: at the same 80% relative humidity warm air holds far more vapor than cold air, and on a muggy coastal night the dew point sits almost against the temperature but on a dry inland night far below. Where the surface itself is already below 0°C, vapor skips the liquid and deposits as frost.

什么是大气急流？

What is the jet stream?

急流是对流层顶附近一条窄而长的强西风带，位于冷暖空气之间的锋区上方：温差越大，热成风平衡要求的高空西风越强，所以冬季比夏季更强、位置也更偏南。它像导轨一样引导高空槽脊和地面风暴东移，也让跨洋航班顺风省时、逆风误时。它并非固定的一条风带，会波动、分裂和南北摆动，一摆动就让某地持续偏旱或偏涝；它也在高空，地面风弱不代表急流消失。
The jet stream is a narrow, elongated ribbon of strong westerly wind near the tropopause, sitting above the front between cold and warm air: the greater the temperature contrast, the stronger the upper westerly that thermal-wind balance requires, so it is stronger and lies farther south in winter than in summer. It works like a track steering upper-level troughs and ridges and their surface storms eastward, and it makes transoceanic flights faster with a tailwind and slower into a headwind. It is not one fixed belt but waves, splits and shifts north and south, and each shift leaves one region persistently dry and another wet; and it sits aloft, so light surface wind does not mean the jet has gone.

什么是风切变？

What is wind shear?

风切变是风速或风向在短距离内的明显差异：垂直切变发生在上下层之间，水平切变在同一高度上。近地面风被摩擦和地形转向，高空风由气压场主导，两层常对不上方向。它的强弱看差值而非风速本身，两层都不算快而方向相反，照样是强切变。强垂直切变会把发展中的雷暴上部吹离底部、切断暖湿入流，使对流难以为继；低空风切变则在起降瞬间改变空速与升力，格外危险。
Wind shear is a marked difference of wind speed or direction over a short distance: vertical shear between layers at different heights, horizontal shear along a single level. Friction and terrain turn the wind near the ground while the upper wind follows the pressure field, so the two layers often fail to line up. Its strength is that difference rather than the wind speed itself, so two sluggish layers blowing opposite ways still give strong shear. Strong vertical shear blows the top of a developing thunderstorm off its base and cuts off the warm moist inflow, so convection struggles to persist; low-level shear, changing airspeed and lift together during takeoff and landing, is what makes it so hazardous for aircraft.

什么是副热带高压？

What is the subtropical high?

赤道受热上升的空气在高空向两极流动，到南北纬约30°上空堆积下沉，构成哈得来环流的下沉支。下沉中空气被压缩变暖，水汽难凝结，所以云少雨稀，地面形成副热带高压带。它随太阳直射点移动，夏季偏北、冬季偏南，中国东部的梅雨与伏旱都跟它的进退有关。易混的是，同一纬度上大陆西岸多沙漠而东岸湿润，因为高压西侧把海上湿空气送上了岸。
Air heated at the equator rises and flows poleward aloft, piling up to sink near 30° latitude as the descending limb of the Hadley cell. Sinking, it is compressed and warms, water vapour can no longer condense, so cloud and rain are scarce and a subtropical high builds at the surface. The belt drifts with the sun, poleward in summer and back toward the equator in winter, and the plum rain front and summer droughts of eastern China follow its advance and retreat. What misleads people is that at this same latitude western continental margins are mostly desert while eastern coasts stay humid, because the western flank of the high pushes moist ocean air ashore.

雷声为什么总是“轰隆隆”持续很久？

Why does thunder rumble on and on?

什么是焚风？

What is a foehn wind?

Moist air forced up the windward slope expands and cools until its water condenses out as rain or snow, so by the time it crosses the ridge it has turned dry. Sinking down the lee slope it is squeezed by increasing pressure and warms dry-adiabatically, the further it drops the warmer it gets, arriving as a hot, parched wind. In an Alpine foehn, at the same height the lee side is warmer and drier than the windward side, which is why it melts snow fast and ripens crops early. It reaches only a narrow strip below the ridge and dies the moment the wind reverses; the heat is not carried in from somewhere else, it comes from compression during descent.

什么是飑线？

What is a squall line?

飑线是一条狭长的强雷暴带，多排在冷锋或冷空气外流边界前缘：雷暴的冷下沉把低层空气铺开成冷空气堤，暖湿空气撞上堤被强烈抬升，前方不断点起新单体、后方老单体消亡，整条线像传送带般前推。它比单个雷暴大而持久，一个单体只活几十分钟，整条能撑好几个小时。易混的是锋面雨带：锋面降水通常缓和成片，飑线的标志是风力突增、风向急转，气压与温度几分钟内跳变，还常伴冰雹。
A squall line is a long, narrow band of severe thunderstorms, usually riding just ahead of a cold front or a cold-air outflow boundary: the cold downdraft of each storm spreads low-level air into a dam of cold air, warm moist air hitting that dam is lifted hard, new cells keep igniting at the front while old ones die at the rear, and the whole line advances like a conveyor belt. It is larger and longer-lived than a single storm, which lasts only tens of minutes, whereas the line can hold together for several hours. The confusion is with a frontal rain band: frontal precipitation is gentle and widespread, while the signature of a squall line is wind rising suddenly, the direction swinging, pressure and temperature jumping within minutes, and often hail with it.
