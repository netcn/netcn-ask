流星雨和彗星有什么亲戚关系？

How are meteor showers related to comets?

彗星靠近太阳时撒下尘埃和碎块，沿轨道铺成“碎片带”；地球每年穿过时，这些碎屑冲进大气燃烧，就成了流星雨。
Comets shed dust and debris that spread along their orbit; when Earth passes through, the bits burn up as a meteor shower.

流星为什么会在天上划出一道光？

Why does a meteor streak a line of light across the sky?

流星体高速闯进大气层，和空气剧烈摩擦生热燃烧，拖出亮亮的光尾，没烧完落地的就叫陨石。
A meteoroid plunges into the atmosphere at high speed, friction heats and burns it into a glowing trail; what survives to land is a meteorite.

陨石坑是怎么形成的？

How do impact craters form?

小行星或彗星高速撞上地表，巨大的冲击力把岩石炸飞、挖出一个碗状凹坑，就形成了陨石坑。
An asteroid or comet slamming into the ground at high speed blasts rock away and digs a bowl-shaped pit called an impact crater.
