为什么“夏至”不是一年最热、“冬至”也不是最冷？

Why isn't the summer solstice the hottest day, nor the winter solstice the coldest?

因为地表像大海一样有“热惯性”：夏至后地面吸收的热量仍多于散失，气温继续升；冬至后放热仍多于吸热，气温继续降，最值会滞后。
The surface has thermal inertia: after the solstice the ground still gains more heat than it loses, so temperatures keep rising (or falling) before peaking later.

“瑞雪兆丰年”有什么科学道理？

What is the science behind timely snow promises a good harvest?

冬雪像被子盖住麦苗保温，冻死部分害虫和病菌；化雪又补水，所以来年春田墒情好、收成有望。
Snow insulates wheat, kills pests and pathogens by freezing, then melts to water the fields—boosting the spring harvest.

#积雪 #农谚

为什么说“清明前后，种瓜点豆”？

Why around Qingming, sow melons and beans?

清明时气温回升、雨水增多、地温合适，正是春播好时机，种下的瓜豆容易发芽生长。
By Qingming the soil has warmed and rains increased—ideal for spring sowing, so melons and beans germinate well.

#清明 #春播

二十四节气为什么能指导农民种地？

Why can the 24 solar terms guide farming?

节气按太阳在天上的位置划分，标出气温、光照的变化节点，农民据此知道何时播种、插秧、收割最合适。
The terms mark the sun's position and thus shifts in temperature and daylight, telling farmers when to sow, transplant or harvest.
