锂电池里的“锂”主要来自哪里？

Where does the lithium in batteries mainly come from?

锂主要来自两类地方：澳大利亚等地的硬岩矿（锂辉石），以及南美“锂三角”盐湖里晒卤水提取的锂。
Lithium comes mainly from hard-rock mines (spodumene) in places like Australia, and from brine pumped out of salt lakes in South America's “lithium triangle.”

什么是稀土元素？为什么它们虽名为稀土却具关键地缘价值？

What are rare earth elements and why are they geopolitically critical despite the name?

稀土是17种性质相近的镧系加钪、钇元素，在地壳中并不算极稀少，但可经济开采的富集成矿极少、分离提纯困难。它们是永磁体、风机与电子设备不可替代的原料，因而成为战略资源。
Rare earths are 17 similar lanthanide plus scandium and yttrium elements; they are not extremely scarce in crust but rarely occur in economic concentrations and are hard to separate. They are irreplaceable in magnets, wind turbines, and electronics, making them strategic resources.

What is shale gas and why does its extraction use “hydraulic fracturing”?

Why do most wind turbines have just three blades?

Three blades balance efficiency and stability: more add weight and drag, fewer spin unsteadily and noisily, so three is economical and quiet.

海底"黑烟囱"为什么能生成金属矿？

Why do deep-sea black smokers form metal deposits?

海水沿洋中脊裂隙下渗，被下方岩浆加热到几百度，成为高温热液并溶解大量硫和金属离子，再从海底喷出遇冷海水，金属硫化物迅速沉淀，堆成"黑烟囱"。这类热液系统形成的块状硫化物矿床富含铜、锌、金、银等，是重要的潜在矿产来源。陆地上许多层状金属矿也记载着古代海底热液活动的历史。
Seawater seeps down through fissures at mid-ocean ridges, is heated to hundreds of degrees by magma below, and dissolves sulfur and metal ions into a hot hydrothermal fluid. When this fluid vents into cold seawater, metal sulfides precipitate instantly and build black-smoker chimneys. Massive sulfide deposits created by these systems are rich in copper, zinc, gold, and silver and are an important potential mineral resource; many layered metal ores on land likewise record ancient seafloor hydrothermal activity.

地下水是如何补充的？含水层为什么会被抽干？

How is groundwater replenished and why can aquifers be depleted?

雨水与河水渗入地下、储存在砂砾含水层中，这个过程叫补给，通常很缓慢。若抽水速度长期超过补给速度，水位下降、含水层压缩，便形成枯竭与地面沉降。
Rain and river water seep into the ground and store in sandy or gravelly aquifers, a slow process called recharge. If pumping long exceeds recharge, water levels drop and the aquifer compacts, leading to depletion and land subsidence.

煤和石油是怎样形成的？为什么被称为化石燃料？

How do coal and oil form, and why are they called fossil fuels?

煤来自数亿年前沼泽中堆积的植物残体，被泥沙掩埋后在高压高温下经泥炭、褐煤、烟煤到无烟煤逐步碳化。石油和天然气主要来自海洋浮游生物，它们死亡沉入海底，在缺氧的沉积物里被埋藏、加热，有机质成熟生成烃类，再运移到孔隙岩层中聚集。因为它们都是古代生物遗体转化而来，所以叫化石燃料，形成需要数百万年而消耗极快。
Coal comes from plant remains piled in swamps hundreds of millions of years ago; buried and squeezed, they pass through peat, lignite, bituminous, and anthracite stages as carbon concentrates. Oil and natural gas come mainly from marine plankton that sank to the seafloor, were buried in oxygen-poor sediment, and were cooked so their organic matter matured into hydrocarbons that migrated into porous reservoir rock. Because all derive from ancient life, they are called fossil fuels—forming over millions of years but consumed in centuries.

深海底为什么铺满土豆?锰结核里藏着哪些未来金属?

The deep seabed is paved with potatoes? Which future metals hide inside manganese nodules?

锰结核是数百万年缓慢沉淀的杰作:铁锰氧化物从海水与沉积物孔隙水中层层吸附镍、钴、铜与稀有金属,以每百万年几毫米的速度包覆核石生长,克拉里昂-克利珀顿区的储量据估超过陆地同类矿藏总和。电动车电池需求让它成为焦点——多金属结核恰好富含电池所需的镍钴铜锰。争议同样巨大:深海生态脆弱而未知,采集搅起的沉积物羽流可能摧毁海底生物群落;国际海底管理局仍在制定开采规章。它是一张未来资源牌,也是一场科学未知数与商业时钟的赛跑。
Manganese nodules are million-year masterworks: iron-manganese oxides adsorb nickel, cobalt, copper and rare metals layer by layer from seawater and pore waters, growing millimeters per million years around cores; the Clarion-Clipperton Zone likely holds more of these metals than all known land deposits combined. EV batteries put nodules center stage since they concentrate battery nickel-cobalt-copper-manganese exactly. The fight is equally large: abyssal ecosystems fragile and unmapped, sediment plumes from collectors could bury communities, and the International Seabed Authority still writes mining rules — a future resource card racing commercial clocks against unknown science.

什么是蓝碳？

What is blue carbon?

蓝碳是红树林、海草床和盐沼等海岸植物固定的碳，它们把二氧化碳存在沉积物和根系里。
Blue carbon is carbon captured by coastal plants like mangroves, seagrass and salt marshes, stored in sediments and roots.

为什么中东地区盛产石油？

Why is the Middle East so rich in oil?

亿万年前这里曾是温暖浅海，大量浮游生物沉积成富含有机质的岩层，后来被埋藏并转化成石油，储层又好又厚。
Millions of years ago it was a warm shallow sea whose plankton formed organic-rich rock that was buried and turned to oil, with excellent thick reservoirs.
