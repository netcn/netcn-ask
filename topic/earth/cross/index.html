什么是“逆温层”，为什么它会让雾霾散不掉？

What is a temperature inversion, and why does it trap smog?

正常情况下高处更冷、空气上升;逆温时反而高处更暖，像盖子压着，污染物没法向上扩散，雾霾就积在城市里。
Usually it is cooler aloft and air rises; in an inversion warmer air sits above, acting like a lid so pollutants can't rise and smog builds up below.

人类能“人工增雨”吗，原理是什么？

Can humans “make rain” artificially, and how?

可以。向冷云播撒碘化银或盐粉当凝结核，让过冷水滴结冰或变大，等条件合适时就能多下一点雨（人工增雨）。
Yes. Seeding cold clouds with silver iodide or salt gives nuclei so supercooled droplets freeze or grow, squeezing out a bit more rain when conditions allow.

天气预报说的“蝴蝶效应”是怎么回事？

What is the “butterfly effect” in weather forecasting?

大气对初始条件极其敏感，一点点误差会被放大，所以超过约两周的预报就不可靠了——这就是“蝴蝶扇翅可能引发远方风暴”。
The atmosphere is exquisitely sensitive to initial conditions, so tiny errors balloon and forecasts beyond about two weeks lose reliability—the butterfly's wing idea.

#蝴蝶效应 #预报

同样纬度，为什么沿海和内陆气候差那么多？

At the same latitude, why do coast and inland climates differ so much?

海水升温降温都慢（比热大），像“恒温器”缓和沿海气温；内陆离海远，夏天更热冬天更冷，温差大。
The sea warms and cools slowly (high heat capacity), buffering coastal temperatures like a thermostat; far inland, summers are hotter and winters colder with bigger swings.

为什么高楼在风里会轻轻摇摆，会不会危险？

Why do tall buildings sway slightly in wind—is it dangerous?

高楼设计成有一定柔韧性，风一吹会小幅摆动来卸掉风力，像芦苇弯腰。只要在限度内，反而更安全。
Tall buildings are designed to be flexible; they sway a little to shed wind force, like reeds bending. Within limits this makes them safer, not riskier.

#高楼 #风

为什么许多古都（如长安、罗马）都靠近大河？

Why were many ancient capitals (like Chang'an, Rome) built near big rivers?

大河提供饮用和灌溉水、方便乘船运粮运货，还能界河防御。有水有路，城市才容易长大。
Big rivers gave drinking and irrigation water, easy boat transport of grain and goods, and a defensive border. With water and routes, cities could grow.

#古都 #河流

高大的山脉为什么会“挡住”季风和降雨？

How can a tall mountain range block monsoons and rain?

暖湿气流爬山脉时被抬升、变冷、下雨（迎风坡多雨），翻过去后空气变干，背风坡就少雨，形成“雨影区”。
Moist air is lifted by the range, cools and rains on the windward side; past the crest the air is dry, leaving the lee side arid—a rain shadow.
