什么是海洋酸化？自工业革命以来表层海水 pH 大约下降了多少？

What is ocean acidification and by roughly how much has surface ocean pH fallen since preindustrial times?

Ocean acidification is the drop in seawater pH as the ocean absorbs atmospheric CO2 and forms carbonic acid. Since the preindustrial era surface pH has fallen from about 8.2 to about 8.1, a roughly 30% increase in hydrogen ion concentration, threatening corals and shell-forming organisms.

深海"全球输送带"是怎么转起来的？

How does the deep ocean's global conveyor belt operate?

北大西洋高纬海面强烈冷却并蒸发，海水变冷变咸、密度增大而下沉，形成北大西洋深层水，向南流经大西洋底，绕南极进入印度洋与太平洋，在深层缓慢上涌，再随表层洋流回到北大西洋，构成全球温盐环流。温度与盐度共同决定密度，故称热盐环流。它把热量、氧和碳输送到全球深海，若格陵兰冰盖大量融水注入，淡化海水可能减弱甚至关闭输送带，对全球气候影响深远。
In the high-latitude North Atlantic, strong cooling and evaporation make surface water cold and salty, dense enough to sink as North Atlantic Deep Water; it flows south along the Atlantic bottom, rounds Antarctica into the Indian and Pacific Oceans, slowly upwells, and returns via surface currents—the global thermohaline circulation, named because temperature and salinity jointly set density. It delivers heat, oxygen, and carbon to the deep ocean worldwide; if large meltwater pulses from Greenland freshen the surface, the sinking could weaken or even shut down, with far-reaching climate consequences.

月球为什么一年比一年远离地球？潮汐摩擦如何影响地球自转？

Why does the Moon recede from Earth every year, and how does tidal friction affect Earth's spin?

月球的潮汐鼓包并不正对月球，而是因地球自转比月球公转快而被"带"在前面，鼓包的引力反过来拉月球向前加速、拉地球自转减速。结果是角动量从地球自转向月球轨道转移，月球以每年约3.8厘米的速度远离地球（激光测月反射镜测得），同时地球自转每世纪约减慢近2毫秒。反过来，月球曾被潮汐锁定、永远同一面朝向地球，也是同类机制的结果。数十亿年前地球自转快得多，一天只有约20小时甚至更短。
The Moon's tidal bulge does not point exactly at the Moon: because Earth spins faster than the Moon orbits, the bulge is dragged ahead of it, and its gravity pulls the Moon forward while braking Earth's rotation. Angular momentum transfers from Earth's spin to the lunar orbit, so the Moon recedes about 3.8 cm per year (measured by lunar laser ranging) while Earth's day lengthens by nearly 2 milliseconds per century. The Moon's own tidal lock, always showing the same face, arises from the same mechanism. Billions of years ago Earth spun much faster, with days of only about 20 hours or less.

Thermohaline circulation is driven by density differences from ocean temperature and salinity, slowly looping through the global ocean as a 'global conveyor belt.' It carries surface heat into the deep sea and is vital to climate regulation.

上升流海域为什么总是鱼群聚集？

Why do upwelling zones attract so many fish?

沿岸风吹走表层海水，深层冷水涌上来填补，把海底沉积物里的硝酸盐、磷酸盐等营养盐带到透光层。浮游植物大量繁殖，浮游动物、小鱼到大鱼的食物链随之兴旺，形成著名渔场，如秘鲁沿岸和加利福尼亚海域。上升流海水温度低，还能让沿岸气候偏凉、雾气弥漫。
When coastal winds blow surface water offshore, cold deep water rises to replace it, carrying nitrates, phosphates, and other nutrients from the seafloor up into the sunlit zone. Phytoplankton bloom, and the food chain from zooplankton to small then large fish flourishes, creating famous fisheries off Peru and California. The rising cold water also keeps coastal climates cool and foggy.

海面洋流为什么在大洋里绕成圆圈？

Why do surface currents circle around in the oceans?

信风在西侧、西风带在东侧持续推动表层海水，加上科里奥利效应使水流在北半球右偏、南半球左偏，海水就在各大洋绕出巨大的顺时针或逆时针漩涡，叫大洋环流。环流把热量从低纬送到高纬，调节全球气候，例如西边界流（湾流、黑潮）流速最快、最暖。环流圈还收集漂浮物，形成大洋"垃圾带"。
Steady trade winds push surface water westward while the westerlies push it eastward, and the Coriolis effect deflects flow right in the Northern Hemisphere and left in the Southern, so water swirls into giant clockwise or counterclockwise loops called gyres. Gyres carry heat from low to high latitudes and regulate climate; the warm, fast western boundary currents such as the Gulf Stream and Kuroshio are their most striking parts. Gyres also concentrate floating debris into ocean garbage patches.

为什么农历初一、十五的潮水特别大？

Why are tides especially large around the new and full moon?

潮汐主要由月球引力造成，太阳也贡献一部分。农历初一和十五，太阳、月球与地球大致排成一线，太阳和月球的引力叠加，涨落幅度最大，叫大潮。上弦月和下弦月时，太阳与月球的方向互相垂直，两个引力部分抵消，潮差最小，叫小潮。潮差大小还受海岸形状的影响。
Tides are driven mainly by the Moon's gravity, with the Sun adding a smaller contribution. Around the new and full moon the Sun, Moon, and Earth line up, so the two pulls combine into the largest tides, called spring tides. At the first and third quarters the Sun and Moon act at right angles and partly cancel, producing the smallest tides, called neap tides. Coastal shape also strongly affects how large the range becomes.

大西洋经向翻转环流（AMOC）对气候有什么作用？

What role does the Atlantic Meridional Overturning Circulation (AMOC) play in climate?

AMOC把表层暖咸水向北输送，在高纬冷却下沉后以深层水向南回流，是“全球海洋传送带”的关键环节。它向北大西洋和欧洲输送热量，调节海洋储碳并为深层水供氧。IPCC评估它在21世纪很可能减弱，极端情景下甚至可能在百年尺度上崩溃，深刻改变区域气候。
AMOC carries warm salty surface water northward, which cools and sinks at high latitudes and returns south as deep water—a key link in the global ocean conveyor belt. It delivers heat to the North Atlantic and Europe, regulates ocean carbon storage, and oxygenates the deep ocean. IPCC assesses it will very likely weaken this century, and in extreme scenarios could collapse on centennial timescales, profoundly altering regional climate.

为什么大多数海边一天有两次涨潮？

Why do most coasts see two high tides a day?

海水为什么从上到下分成好几层？

Why is seawater layered from top to bottom?

阳光加热表层海水，风浪混合使上层温度均匀，往下温度骤降的界面叫温跃层。密度随温度和盐度变化，冷水、咸水更重，于是形成稳定分层：表层的混合层、中间的温跃层和深层的冷水。这种分层阻挡上下物质交换，深层水的氧和营养主要靠洋流和下沉补充。海洋还按深度划分为透光层、弱光层和无光层等生态带。
Sunlight warms the surface, and wind mixes the upper layer to a uniform temperature, below which a sharp drop called the thermocline occurs. Density depends on temperature and salinity—cold, salty water is heavier—so the ocean stabilizes into a mixed surface layer, the thermocline, and deep cold water. The layering blocks vertical exchange, so oxygen and nutrients in deep water arrive mainly through currents and sinking. Ecologically, the ocean is also zoned into sunlit, twilight, and midnight layers.

海洋为什么会出现分层？

Why does the ocean stratify?

海水密度由温度和盐度决定，分层本质是密度分层。太阳加热表层，风浪搅出温度均匀的混合层；其下温度骤降成温跃层、盐度骤变成盐跃层，合称密度跃层。轻上重下，翻动须克服浮力，垂向交换很弱，深层的氧与营养盐难以上返。它并非处处存在：高纬冬季表层水又冷又咸而下沉，对流击穿跃层，深层水由此补充；河口有大量淡水时，分层改由盐度主导。
Seawater density is set by temperature and salinity, so stratification is really density layering. The sun warms the surface and wind stirs a mixed layer of nearly uniform temperature; below it temperature drops sharply as the thermocline and salinity as the halocline, and together these make the pycnocline. With light water over dense water, anything that mixes vertically has to work against buoyancy, so exchange is weak and the oxygen and nutrients of the deep water struggle to return upward. The structure is not the same everywhere: in winter at high latitudes the surface becomes cold and salty and sinks, and convection punches through the thermocline, which is where the deep water is renewed; and where a river mouth delivers a lot of fresh water, salinity takes over as the control on the layering.

海水的盐度大约是多少？

About how salty is seawater?

开阔大洋海水的平均盐度约为千分之35，也就是约3.5%的溶解盐分，以氯化钠为主。不同海域因蒸发、降水和径流差异，盐度会有所不同。
Open ocean seawater averages about 35 parts per thousand, roughly 3.5% dissolved salts, mostly sodium chloride. Salinity varies by region because of differences in evaporation, rainfall, and river input.
