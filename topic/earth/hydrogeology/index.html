承压含水层大规模抽水形成的「降落漏斗」为什么波及范围广且多年难以消除?

Why does heavy pumping from a confined aquifer build a far-reaching cone of depression that lingers for decades?

直答:承压井抽取的主要是弹性储量,表现为水头整体下降而非含水层疏干,压降信号传播远且消退慢。机理:承压含水层储水系数通常仅10⁻⁵至10⁻⁴量级,单位降深释放的水量极小,满足大规模取水必须压降巨大范围,漏斗半径可达数十公里;加之弱透水层越流补给有限、侧向径流缓慢,华北平原深层水漏斗历经数十年方趋稳定。
A confined aquifer gives up mostly elastic storage: pumping lowers hydraulic head rather than draining pores, and the pressure deficit spreads widely and fades slowly. Storativity is tiny, typically 10^-5 to 10^-4, so meeting a large demand requires depressurizing a huge area, letting the cone stretch tens of kilometers. Limited leakage through confining beds and sluggish lateral flow mean recovery trails pumping cuts for decades, as the deep-water funnels of the North China Plain show.

为什么超采地下水造成的地面沉降几乎不可能复原?

Why is land subsidence caused by excessive groundwater pumping nearly impossible to reverse?

直答:承压水位下降使黏土层发生塑性压密,孔隙结构无法弹回。机理:砂砾层释水近似弹性变形,水位回升尚可小幅回弹;黏土压缩性强而渗透性低,排水固结缓慢,有效应力超过先期固结压力后颗粒重排、孔隙坍缩即成永久变形,即便停采使水位恢复,地面也难以抬升,墨西哥城与圣华金河谷的沉降因此长期固化。
Falling confined-aquifer heads permanently squeeze buried clay beds. Sands and gravels deform elastically, so levels bounce back modestly when pumping stops. Clays combine low permeability with high compressibility: once effective stress exceeds their preconsolidation pressure, grains repack and pores collapse for good. Even full recovery of water levels cannot raise the ground again, which is why subsidence in Mexico City and the San Joaquin Valley endures.

#地面沉降 #黏土压密 #有效应力

长期超采地下水为什么会让整片平原的地面一年比一年更低？

Why does long-term groundwater over-pumping sink the surface of an entire plain a little more every year?

抽水使含水层水头下降，孔隙水压力减小，上覆地层的有效应力随之增大，土体骨架被压密，地表便整体下沉。砂砾层近似弹性，水位回升尚可部分回弹；黏土隔水层的压密是塑性且基本不可逆的，因此停采后大部分沉降也无法恢复。华北平原局部累计沉降已逾两米，沿海还伴生海水入侵，控采、人工回灌与外调水源由此成为治理核心。
Pumping lowers hydraulic heads, pore-water pressure drops, effective stress on the grain skeleton rises, and the aquifer system compacts until the land surface sinks. Sandy layers behave nearly elastically and rebound somewhat when levels recover, but clays compact plastically and irreversibly, so most subsidence persists even after pumping stops. Parts of the North China Plain have subsided more than two meters, with seawater intrusion along the coast, making abstraction limits, artificial recharge and imported water the core remedies.
