什么是地下水补给区和排泄区？

What are recharge and discharge areas of groundwater?

补给区是地表水渗入、补充含水层的高地或透水区；排泄区是地下水自然流出地表的地方，如泉、河流或湿地。理解二者对保护水源和管理污染至关重要。
A recharge area is where surface water infiltrates and replenishes an aquifer, often high permeable ground; a discharge area is where groundwater naturally surfaces, such as springs, rivers or wetlands. Understanding both is key to protecting water and managing pollution.

什么是地下水漏斗？

What is a groundwater cone of depression?

长期集中抽水会使井周围地下水位下降，形成向下凹陷的水位漏斗。多个漏斗叠加可造成区域水位持续下降、河流断流和地面沉降等连锁问题。
Sustained concentrated pumping lowers the water table around a well, forming a downward depression shaped like a cone. Overlapping cones cause regional water-level decline, dried rivers and subsidence as chained problems.

什么是「承压含水层」？它为何会自流？

What is a 'confined aquifer', and why does it flow on its own?

承压含水层被上下隔水层夹住，内部充满水并承受压力。当井孔穿透上覆隔水层且水位高于地表时，水会自行上涌，成为自流井，如澳大利亚大自流盆地。
A confined aquifer is bounded above and below by aquitards and filled under pressure. When a well pierces the cap and the pressure head rises above ground, water flows up by itself as an artesian well, like Australia's Great Artesian Basin.

什么是泉水？它为什么会从地下冒出来？

What is a spring, and why does water emerge from the ground?

泉水是地下水自然涌出地表的地方。当含水层被隔水层切断或地形切穿含水层时，承压水便沿裂隙流出，形成泉。温泉则多因深处地热加热。
A spring is where groundwater naturally surfaces. When an aquifer is cut off by an aquitard or exposed by terrain, confined water flows out along fractures, forming a spring. Hot springs are usually heated by deep geothermal energy.

为什么有些地方会「地面沉降」？

Why does land subside in some places?

过度抽取地下水会使含水层孔隙水压下降、颗粒被压密，导致地表缓慢下沉。沿海城市如上海、墨西哥城都曾因超采地下水发生明显沉降。
Over-pumping groundwater lowers pore pressure and compacts aquifer grains, slowly sinking the surface. Coastal cities like Shanghai and Mexico City have seen notable subsidence from excessive extraction.

为什么沿海打井太深会打出咸水？

Why does deep coastal well drilling yield salty water?

沿海含水层下方常有海水入侵形成的咸淡水过渡带。过量抽取淡水会使水位下降，海水向内陆和向上补给，导致井水变咸，称为咸水入侵。
Beneath coastal aquifers lies a saltwater-freshwater transition zone from seawater intrusion. Over-pumping lowers freshwater levels, letting seawater move inland and upward, salinizing wells, a process called saltwater intrusion.

井水冬天为什么比较温暖、夏天比较凉？

Why is well water warmer in winter and cooler in summer?

地下水温受地温影响，随深度变化很小，常年稳定在十几摄氏度。相比地表气温的季节大幅波动，抽取上来的井水就显得冬暖夏凉。
Groundwater temperature tracks the stable geothermal gradient and varies little with depth, staying around a steady teens of degrees Celsius. Compared with large seasonal swings at the surface, pumped well water feels warm in winter and cool in summer.

地下水是从哪里来的？

Where does groundwater come from?

地下水主要来自降水渗入土壤和岩层空隙，在重力作用下向下汇集。它储存在含水层里，是重要的淡水资源，全球可用的淡水很大一部分是地下水。
Groundwater mainly comes from precipitation seeping into soil and rock voids, collecting downward under gravity. Stored in aquifers, it is a vital freshwater resource, making up a large share of usable global freshwater.

地下水会被污染吗？常见污染源有哪些？

Can groundwater be polluted, and what are common sources?

会。化肥农药下渗、生活污水和工业废水渗漏、垃圾填埋场渗滤液都可污染含水层。地下水流动慢、自净弱，一旦污染往往很难恢复。
Yes. Fertilizer and pesticide infiltration, leaking sewage and industrial wastewater, and landfill leachate can all pollute aquifers. Groundwater flows slowly and self-purifies poorly, so once contaminated it is hard to restore.

什么是含水层？

What is an aquifer?

含水层是能储存并让水顺畅流动的岩层或土层，如砂砾层、裂隙发育的石灰岩。相对地，隔水层（如黏土）几乎不让水通过，可保护或限制地下水。
An aquifer is a rock or soil layer that stores and transmits water readily, like sand-gravel beds or fractured limestone. In contrast, an aquitard such as clay barely lets water pass, protecting or confining groundwater.
