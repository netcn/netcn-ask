火山碎屑流为什么破坏力极强？

Why are pyroclastic flows so destructive?

火山碎屑流是高温气体与碎屑混合的高速密度流，温度可达数百摄氏度、速度超每小时百公里。它沿山坡倾泻、所到之处焚毁一切，1902年培雷火山即以此摧毁圣皮埃尔城。
A pyroclastic flow is a high-speed density current of hot gas and debris, reaching hundreds of degrees and over 100 km/h. It pours down slopes destroying everything; in 1902 Mount Pelée's flow obliterated Saint-Pierre city.

什么是破火山口？

What is a caldera?

破火山口是塌陷造成的负地形：岩浆房被喷发抽空，顶板失去支撑，沿环形断层整体下沉，留下远比喷发口宽大的碗状洼地。它和普通火山口是两回事：火山口在喷发口上，由喷发物堆筑或爆炸挖成，宽以百米计；破火山口切穿锥体，跨以公里计。塌陷后常见环状断块、火口湖与复活穹隆；也别把它等同于超级火山：岩浆房浅、顶板易塌时，中等喷发也能造出。
A caldera is a collapse landform rather than a built-up one: an eruption drains the magma chamber, the roof above it loses support and drops wholesale along ring faults, leaving a bowl far wider than the vent that erupted. It is not the same thing as an ordinary crater, which sits on the vent, is built of ejecta or blasted out, and measures hundreds of metres across, whereas a caldera cuts across the cone that stood there before and spans kilometres. Ring-faulted blocks, a crater lake and a resurgent dome are the usual aftermath; and a caldera should not be equated with a supervolcano either, since a shallow chamber under a fragile roof can collapse in a moderate eruption.

什么是火山爆发指数（VEI）？

What is the Volcanic Explosivity Index (VEI)?

VEI 是用对数尺度衡量火山喷发规模的指数，从0到8级，每升一级喷出的物质体积约增10倍。它综合喷发柱高度和喷出物体积来反映喷发的强烈程度。
The VEI is a logarithmic scale from 0 to 8 ranking eruption size, with each step up about ten times more ejected material. It combines plume height and ejected volume to express how violent an eruption is.

什么是火山碎屑流（pyroclastic flow）？它为什么极其致命？

What is a pyroclastic flow, and why is it so deadly?

火山碎屑流是火山喷发时沿山坡高速奔涌的炽热气体、灰烬和岩石碎块的混合物，温度可超700°C、速度可达每小时数百公里。它密度大、蔓延快且难以躲避，是历史上造成最多火山伤亡的灾害之一（如1902年培雷火山）。
A pyroclastic flow is a fast-moving mixture of hot gas, ash, and rock fragments that races down a volcano's flanks, reaching temperatures above 700°C and speeds of hundreds of km/h. Dense, swift, and nearly impossible to outrun, it has caused some of history's deadliest volcanic disasters (e.g., Mont Pelée, 1902).

为什么有的火山平缓如盾、有的陡峭如锥？

Why are some volcanoes flat shields and others steep cones?

火山形态取决于岩浆黏度。夏威夷的岩浆是低硅、低黏度的玄武岩浆，气体易逸出，呈平静溢流，冷却后铺成坡度很小的盾状火山，如冒纳罗亚。环太平洋的层状火山喷发的则是高硅、高黏度的安山岩-流纹岩岩浆，黏稠塞住火山通道，气体积累到高压后爆炸性喷发，碎屑堆积成陡峭的层状锥，如富士山和维苏威。黏度由温度、SiO2含量和挥发分共同控制。
A volcano's shape reflects magma viscosity. Hawaii erupts low-silica, low-viscosity basalt that degasses easily and flows quietly, cooling into broad shield volcanoes like Mauna Loa. Around the Pacific rim, stratovolcanoes erupt high-silica, viscous andesite-rhyolite that plugs the conduit; gas builds up pressure until explosive eruptions pile steep cones of debris, as at Fuji and Vesuvius. Viscosity is set by temperature, silica content, and volatiles acting together.

岩浆的黏度与它所含的二氧化硅（SiO₂）有什么关系？这如何影响喷发方式？

How does magma viscosity relate to its silica (SiO₂) content, and how does this affect eruption style?

岩浆中二氧化硅越多（长英质/酸性），黏度越高、气体越难逸出，易积累压力造成猛烈爆发（如流纹岩）。二氧化硅少（镁铁质/基性，如玄武岩）则黏度低、气体易释放，多为平静的溢流式喷发。因此成分差异决定了火山的“脾气”。
The more silica a magma contains (felsic/acidic compositions), the higher its viscosity and the harder it is for gases to escape, so pressure builds and produces explosive eruptions (e.g., rhyolite). Low-silica (mafic/basaltic) magmas are fluid, letting gas escape easily and causing gentle effusive eruptions. Composition thus dictates a volcano's 'temperament.'

What is pillow lava, and what does it indicate?

枕状熔岩是熔岩在水下喷发时因快速冷却而形成的一堆形似枕头的圆丘状岩体。它的存在是某地曾发生过水下火山活动、或古老岩石原属洋壳的重要证据。
Pillow lava is a pile of pillow-shaped mounds formed when lava erupts underwater and cools rapidly. Its presence is strong evidence that an area once had submarine volcanism or that ancient rocks originally belonged to oceanic crust.

热点（hotspot）火山与板块边界火山有何本质不同？

How do hotspot volcanoes fundamentally differ from plate-boundary volcanoes?

热点火山由地幔深处相对固定的地幔柱提供岩浆，不依赖板块边界，如夏威夷群岛随板块移动形成一串火山。板块边界火山则直接由板块相互作用驱动，位置随边界变化。
Hotspot volcanoes are fed by relatively fixed mantle plumes from deep in the mantle and do not depend on plate boundaries, as seen in the Hawaiian chain formed as the plate moved over the plume. Plate-boundary volcanoes are driven directly by plate interactions and shift with the boundary.

什么是「超级火山」？其喷发规模如何量化？

What is a 'supervolcano', and how is its eruption scale quantified?

超级火山指能发生8级（VEI-8）喷发、喷发物体积超过1000立方千米的火山，如黄石和托巴。喷发规模常用火山爆发指数（VEI）按对数分级，从0到8，每升一级喷发物体积约增10倍。
A supervolcano is one capable of a VEI-8 eruption ejecting over 1,000 cubic kilometers of material, such as Yellowstone or Toba. Eruption size is quantified by the Volcanic Explosivity Index (VEI), a logarithmic 0–8 scale where each step up means roughly ten times more ejected volume.

超级火山喷发为什么比普通火山猛烈得多？

Why are supervolcano eruptions so much more violent than ordinary ones?

超级火山源自浅层巨大的岩浆房，其中高硅、富含挥发分的岩浆在持续压力下蓄势，一旦喷发往往以破火山口形式塌陷，喷出数千立方公里的火山灰与碎屑流。黄石热点区的三次超级喷发形成三个破火山口，最近一次约64万年前。这类喷发会向平流层注入大量硫酸盐气溶胶，遮蔽阳光、使全球气温在数年内显著下降，被称为"火山冬天"，其强度用VEI（火山爆发指数）8级衡量，比普通喷发高好几个量级。
Supervolcanoes draw on huge shallow magma chambers of silica-rich, gas-charged magma under sustained pressure; when they erupt, the ground collapses into a caldera while thousands of cubic kilometers of ash and pyroclastic flows are expelled. Yellowstone's hot-spot system has produced three such calderas, the last about 640,000 years ago. These eruptions inject massive sulfate aerosols into the stratosphere, dimming sunlight and cooling global temperature for years in a volcanic winter; on the Volcanic Explosivity Index they rate 8, orders of magnitude above ordinary eruptions.

什么是破火山口？它与普通圆锥状火山的喷发形成方式有何不同？

What is a caldera and how does its formation differ from a cone-building eruption?

破火山口是岩浆大规模喷空后，上方岩层因失去支撑而塌陷形成的巨大碗状洼地，如黄石与托巴。它不靠喷发物堆叠成锥，而是塌陷，规模常远超普通火山锥。
A caldera is a large bowl-shaped depression formed when a magma chamber empties in a massive eruption and the overlying roof collapses, as at Yellowstone and Toba. Rather than building a cone from ejected material, it collapses, often on a far larger scale than ordinary volcanic cones.

科学家是怎么提前发现火山要喷发的？

How do scientists detect that a volcano is about to erupt?

岩浆上升会压裂岩石并移动地下流体，监测主要靠三大信号：地震仪捕捉岩浆涌动引起的小地震群和火山震颤；GPS与卫星雷达（InSAR）测出山体膨胀变形；气体站检测二氧化硫等火山气体异常增加。把这些信号结合起来，就能判断岩浆是否在上升并发布预警。不过目前仍无法精确预测喷发时刻，只能给出概率性的警报。
Rising magma fractures rock and shifts underground fluids, so monitoring relies on three main signals: seismometers pick up swarms of small quakes and volcanic tremor; GPS and satellite radar (InSAR) measure swelling of the edifice; and gas stations detect surges of sulfur dioxide and other volcanic gases. Combining these signals reveals whether magma is ascending and allows warnings to be issued. Still, the exact moment of eruption cannot yet be predicted, only probabilistic alerts.

火山灰有哪些危害？

What are the hazards of volcanic ash?

火山灰不是尘土，而是炸碎的玻璃质岩屑，有棱角、多孔，能被喷发云带到很远。干时轻飘，受潮结块增重、压塌屋顶；堵塞进气与过滤系统，被喷气发动机吸入后在涡轮处熔化再凝固，推力可骤失；细粒入肺、锐粒伤眼与气道；在地表污染水源、遇水成泥流。灰的影响范围远大于熔岩流；气候变冷主要靠伴生二氧化硫生成的平流层气溶胶，灰本身沉降很快。
Volcanic ash is not dust but shattered glassy fragments, angular and full of pores, carried far by the eruption cloud. Dry it drifts lightly; once damp it cakes, gains weight and collapses roofs. It chokes air intakes and filters, and after being sucked into a jet engine it melts onto the turbine and re-solidifies, so thrust can vanish in an instant. Fine grains hurt the lungs, sharp ones the eyes and airways; on the ground it fouls water and turns into mudflows with rain. Ash reaches far beyond any lava flow, and what actually cools the climate is the stratospheric aerosol made from accompanying sulfur dioxide, since ash itself settles out quickly.

What are the common products of a volcanic eruption?

火山为什么会喷发？

Why do volcanoes erupt?

地球内部温度很高，岩石部分熔化形成岩浆。当岩浆中的气体和压力在地下越积越多，就会沿着地壳的薄弱裂缝向上冲出地表，形成喷发。喷发的剧烈程度取决于岩浆黏度和气体含量。
Deep inside the Earth, high temperatures partly melt rock into magma. As gas and pressure build up underground, the magma forces its way up through weak cracks in the crust and bursts onto the surface, producing an eruption. How violent the eruption is depends on the magma's viscosity and gas content.

火山附近的土地为什么往往很肥沃？

Why is land near volcanoes often very fertile?

火山灰和风化后的火山岩富含钾、磷等植物养分，且质地疏松利于保水透气。经过漫长风化，这些矿物质释放到土壤里，使火山周边成为天然的高产农业区。
Volcanic ash and weathered volcanic rock are rich in plant nutrients like potassium and phosphorus and are loose, helping retain water and air. Over long weathering, these minerals enter the soil, making volcanic regions naturally productive farmland.
