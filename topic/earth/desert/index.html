为什么沙漠会扩大？

Why do deserts expand?

过度放牧和干旱使植被消失，土壤被风沙带走。
Overgrazing and drought strip vegetation, letting sand take over.

地球上为什么会有沙漠？

Why are there deserts on Earth?

在副热带高压带或大山背风处，空气下沉、少雨，加上蒸发强，长期下来就变成了干旱的沙漠。
Under subtropical highs or behind mountains, air sinks and rains little while evaporation is high, gradually creating arid deserts.

沙尘暴里的沙子、尘土都是从哪里来的？

Where does the sand and dust in a sandstorm come from?

主要来自干旱区裸露的戈壁、荒漠和干涸湖床，强风把疏松的沙尘卷上高空，一路吹到很远的地方。
Mainly from bare gobi, desert and dried lake beds; strong wind lofts loose dust high and carries it far.
