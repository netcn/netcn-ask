驱动板块运动的力里，哪一种被认为最重要？

Among the forces driving plate motion, which is thought most important?

多数研究认为“板片拖曳”（slab pull）最重要：俯冲带密度大的板片沉入地幔，像拉窗帘一样把后面整块板块往下拽；其次还有洋中脊的“脊推”和地幔对流的拖带。
Most studies rank slab pull as the top force: the dense subducting slab sinks into the mantle and drags the rest of the plate along, like pulling a curtain; ridge push and mantle drag also contribute.

板块一年大概移动多少，和指甲长速比呢？

About how far do plates move in a year, compared with fingernail growth?

板块每年大约移动几厘米，和人的指甲生长速度差不多，慢得平时根本感觉不到。
Plates move only a few centimeters per year—about the rate your fingernails grow—so slowly we never feel it.
