雪花为什么都是六个角的?世界上有两片一样的雪花吗?

Why do snowflakes have six arms — are any two flakes identical?

雪花是小冰晶排队伍,水分子天生喜欢六边形队形,所以每片都长六个瓣。可下落途中温度湿度千变万化,长出的花纹各不相同——找两片完全一样的雪花,比找两片一样的树叶还难!
Snowflakes are tiny ice crystals, and water molecules love lining up in six-sided teams, so every flake grows six arms. Each falls through different temperatures and humidity, growing unique patterns — finding two identical flakes is harder than finding two identical leaves.
