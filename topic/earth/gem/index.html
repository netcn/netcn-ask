红宝石和蓝宝石其实是同一种东西，为什么颜色却不同？

Rubies and sapphires are the same mineral—why are their colors different?

它们都是刚玉（氧化铝），本身无色；只因混入了极少的微量元素——红宝石靠铬显红，蓝宝石靠铁和钛显蓝，所以“同矿不同色”。
Both are corundum (aluminum oxide), colorless by itself; trace elements tint them—chromium makes ruby red, iron and titanium make sapphire blue—so it's the same mineral in different colors.

钻石其实是碳做的，它和铅笔里的石墨有什么不同？

Diamond is made of carbon—what makes it different from graphite in a pencil?

钻石和石墨都是纯碳，但原子排列方式不同：钻石在高温高压下原子紧锁成坚硬透明晶体，石墨的碳原子层松散易滑，所以一个最硬、一个能写字。
Both are pure carbon but arranged differently: diamond's atoms lock tightly under high pressure into a hard clear crystal, while graphite's carbon layers slide easily, so one is hardest and the other writes.

普通人想挑出真宝石，最实用的几招是什么？

What are the most practical tricks for spotting a real gem?

可以看硬度（真宝石通常能在玻璃上留痕却不易被划伤）、观察折射带来的火彩、以及用放大镜找天然包裹体；最可靠还是送检，因为仿品越来越像。
Check hardness (real gems scratch glass yet resist scratches), look for refractive 'fire', and use a loupe for natural inclusions; the surest way is a lab test, since fakes keep improving.

祖母绿那抹绿色是从哪儿来的？

Where does emerald's green color come from?

祖母绿是绿柱石家族的一员，原本接近无色，因为晶体里混进了一点点铬或钒，就染出了鲜艳的绿色。
Emerald is a member of the beryl family, nearly colorless on its own; a tiny bit of chromium or vanadium tints it the vivid green.
