大陆地壳为何比大洋地壳更厚且密度更低？其地球动力学意义是什么？

Why is continental crust thicker yet less dense than oceanic crust, and what is its geodynamic significance?

大陆地壳以富硅的花岗质岩石为主，平均厚度约 35–40 公里，密度约 2.7 g/cm³；大洋地壳为玄武质，厚约 7 公里，密度约 3.0 g/cm³。较低密度使大陆长期漂浮于软流圈之上不易俯冲消亡，从而保存了古老的地质记录。
Continental crust is dominated by silica-rich granitic rocks, averaging 35–40 km thick with a density near 2.7 g/cm³, whereas oceanic crust is basaltic, about 7 km thick and ~3.0 g/cm³. Its lower density lets continents persist atop the asthenosphere without readily subducting, preserving ancient geological records.

为什么某些岩浆喷发呈爆炸式、而另一些呈溢流式？关键控制因素是什么？

Why are some eruptions explosive while others are effusive, and what are the key controlling factors?

决定性因素是岩浆的挥发分（主要是水和二氧化碳）含量与黏度。玄武质岩浆黏度低、气体易逸出，多形成溢流喷发；流纹质岩浆黏度高、富硅，气体被困后压力骤增，引发爆炸式喷发。岩浆上升速率与围岩约束也会影响喷发样式。
The decisive factors are the volatile content—mainly water and carbon dioxide—and the viscosity of the magma. Low-viscosity basaltic magma lets gas escape easily, producing effusive eruptions, while high-silica, viscous rhyolitic magma traps gases until pressure builds explosively. Ascent rate and country-rock confinement also shape the style.

地震层析成像如何揭示地幔柱和俯冲板片的存在？

How does seismic tomography reveal the existence of mantle plumes and subducting slabs?

地震波在温度较低、密度较高的物质中速度更快。通过记录全球地震波的走时异常，层析成像重建地幔三维速度结构：高速异常对应冷的下沉俯冲板片，低速异常对应炽热的上涌地幔柱。这一方法为地幔对流提供了直接成像证据。
Seismic waves travel faster through cooler, denser material. By measuring travel-time anomalies of global earthquakes, tomography reconstructs the mantle's 3D velocity structure: fast anomalies mark cold descending slabs, slow anomalies mark hot upwelling plumes. This provides direct imaging evidence of mantle convection.

地幔柱除了形成火山岛链，还可能引发何种规模的岩浆活动？

Besides volcanic island chains, what scale of magmatism can mantle plumes trigger?

当巨型地幔柱头部上涌至岩石圈底部，可造成大规模溢流玄武岩喷发，形成大火成岩省（LIP），面积可达百万平方公里，如印度的德干高原与西伯利亚暗色岩。这类事件常伴随快速的气候与生物灭绝事件。
When a giant mantle-plume head reaches the base of the lithosphere it can drive massive flood-basalt eruptions forming large igneous provinces (LIPs) up to about a million square kilometers, such as the Deccan Traps and Siberian Traps. Such events are often linked to rapid climate shifts and mass extinctions.

什么是岩浆房？它通常位于地壳多深的位置？

What is a magma chamber, and at what depth beneath the surface do they typically form?

岩浆房是地壳或上地幔中聚集熔融岩石的空腔或裂隙网络。多数浅成岩浆房位于地下约 5 至 15 公里处，深成岩浆房可下延至 30 公里以上。它们并非巨大的单一空腔，而是由充满岩浆的裂隙和孔隙组成的储集系统。
A magma chamber is a reservoir of molten rock accumulated in cavities or fracture networks within the crust or upper mantle. Most shallow chambers sit roughly 5 to 15 km below the surface, while deeper ones extend beyond 30 km. Rather than a single giant void, they are storage systems of magma-filled fractures and pores.

什么是破火山口（caldera）？它是如何形成的？

What is a caldera, and how does it form?

破火山口是岩浆大规模喷发后，地下空腔排空、上方地表塌陷形成的近圆形洼地。喷发量可达数十至上百立方公里，塌陷直径常达数公里至数十公里，如黄石和托巴破火山口。
A caldera is a roughly circular depression formed when the ground above a magma reservoir collapses after a massive eruption empties the chamber. Eruptions can eject tens to over a hundred cubic kilometers of material, and collapse diameters range from a few to tens of kilometers, as seen at Yellowstone and Toba.

火山灰与火山气体对气候有何短期影响？

What short-term effect do volcanic ash and gases have on climate?

大型喷发将二氧化硫注入平流层，转化为硫酸盐气溶胶，反射阳光并使全球地表在数月到数年内降温。1991 年皮纳图博火山喷发使全球平均气温暂时下降约 0.5℃。火山灰本身则很快沉降，对气候影响有限。
Major eruptions inject sulfur dioxide into the stratosphere, where it becomes sulfate aerosols that reflect sunlight and cool the global surface for months to a few years. The 1991 Pinatubo eruption lowered global mean temperature by about 0.5℃ temporarily. Ash falls out quickly and has limited climatic effect.

火山预警中常监测的“火山形变”指什么？它说明了什么？

What is volcanic deformation monitored in eruption warnings, and what does it indicate?

火山形变指火山地表因岩浆侵入或撤离而发生隆起或沉降。GPS 与卫星雷达（InSAR）可测出毫米级变化。持续隆起往往意味着岩浆正在补给，是预测喷发的重要指标之一。
Volcanic deformation refers to uplift or subsidence of a volcano's surface caused by magma intruding or withdrawing. GPS and satellite radar (InSAR) detect millimeter-scale changes. Sustained uplift often signals magma recharge and is a key indicator for eruption forecasting.
