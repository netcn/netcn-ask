沃克环流是什么，它平时怎样在热带太平洋上空转圈？

What is the Walker circulation, and how does it normally loop over the tropical Pacific?

沃克环流是沿赤道太平洋的东西向大气环流：低层信风把暖水往西推，在西太平洋空气上升成云降雨，到东太平洋下沉，再沿低层吹回西边，形成一个闭环。
The Walker circulation is an east-west loop over the tropical Pacific: low-level trade winds push warm water west, air rises and rains in the west, sinks in the east, and returns westward near the surface, closing the loop.

热带太平洋两边，一边常下雨一边常干旱，这是为什么？

Why is one side of the tropical Pacific rainy and the other dry?

因为信风把暖水和湿热空气堆在西边，西边空气上升常下雨；东边空气下沉，又干又晴，所以两边天气很不一样。
Trade winds pile warm moist air on the west, where it rises and rains; the east has sinking dry air, so it stays clear—making the two sides very different.
