黑碳（煤烟）在气候系统中扮演怎样的正负角色？

What positive and negative roles does black carbon (soot) play in the climate system?

黑碳强烈吸收可见光与红外辐射，既加热大气又沉降在雪冰上降低反照率加速融化（正反馈），同时若悬浮在对流层也可通过吸收抑制云的形成。它寿命短但增温潜势高，是仅次于CO2的重要短期气候强迫。
Black carbon strongly absorbs visible and infrared radiation, heating the atmosphere and, when deposited on snow and ice, lowering albedo to accelerate melt (a positive feedback), while absorbing in the troposphere can also suppress cloud formation. Short-lived but with high warming potential, it is a major short-term climate forcing after CO2.

气溶胶通过哪些途径影响气候并可能产生反馈？

Through what pathways do aerosols affect climate and possibly generate feedbacks?

气溶胶既能直接散射或吸收太阳辐射，又能作为云凝结核改变云的反照率与寿命（间接效应），还可改变地表反照率（如黑碳沉降雪面）。这些效应与温度、降水和水循环耦合，形成复杂的气候反馈。
Aerosols both scatter or absorb solar radiation directly and act as cloud condensation nuclei that alter cloud albedo and lifetime (indirect effect), and they can change surface albedo (e.g., black carbon on snow). These effects couple with temperature, precipitation, and the water cycle into complex climate feedbacks.

为什么氟氯碳化物（CFC）会破坏平流层臭氧层？

Why do chlorofluorocarbons (CFCs) destroy stratospheric ozone?

CFC在对流层稳定，进入平流层后被紫外线分解释放出活性氯原子，氯催化分解臭氧分子并可在数月至数年内反复作用，导致臭氧层变薄甚至出现臭氧洞。蒙特利尔议定书限制其生产后臭氧已开始恢复。
CFCs are stable in the troposphere but, once in the stratosphere, ultraviolet light releases reactive chlorine atoms that catalytically destroy ozone molecules and act repeatedly over months to years, thinning the layer and opening ozone holes. After the Montreal Protocol limited production, ozone has begun to recover.

地表附近的臭氧污染是怎样生成的？

How does near-surface ozone pollution form?

地面臭氧并非直接排放，而是氮氧化物与挥发性有机物在阳光照射下发生光化学反应二次生成，常见于夏季晴热、车流与工业排放高的城市。它是一种危害呼吸系统的关键空气污染物。
Ground-level ozone is not emitted directly; it forms secondarily when nitrogen oxides and volatile organic compounds undergo sunlight-driven photochemistry, common on hot, sunny days in cities with heavy traffic and industry. It is a key air pollutant harmful to the respiratory system.

什么是PM2.5，为什么它对健康危害尤其大？

What is PM2.5, and why is it especially hazardous to health?

PM2.5指空气动力学直径不大于2.5微米的细颗粒物，可深入肺泡甚至进入血液，携带硫酸盐、重金属与有机污染物。长期暴露与心肺疾病、卒中风险上升相关，是城市空气质量的核心指标。
PM2.5 denotes fine particles with aerodynamic diameter no larger than 2.5 micrometers; they penetrate deep into alveoli and even the bloodstream, carrying sulfates, heavy metals, and organics. Long-term exposure raises risks of heart and lung disease and stroke, making it a core urban air-quality metric.

火山喷入平流层的硫酸盐气溶胶为何能持续影响气候一两年？

Why can stratospheric sulfate aerosols from volcanoes affect climate for a year or two?

平流层空气垂直混合很慢、降水无法清除其中颗粒，硫酸盐气溶胶可随环流全球扩散并停留1至2年，持续反射阳光使地表降温。这与对流层气溶胶数日即被雨水清除形成对比。
The stratosphere mixes vertically very slowly and has no rain to wash out particles, so sulfate aerosols spread globally and persist for one to two years, continuously reflecting sunlight and cooling the surface. This contrasts with tropospheric aerosols cleared by rain within days.

气溶胶怎样通过改变云的反照率来影响地球能量平衡？

How do aerosols alter Earth's energy balance by changing cloud albedo?

更多气溶胶作为云凝结核使云滴更细小密集，云对阳光的反射增强（Twomey效应），冷却地表；但若云滴过小抑制降水，云寿命延长也会改变水循环。这一间接效应是气候模拟中不确定性的主要来源之一。
More aerosols as cloud condensation nuclei make cloud droplets smaller and more numerous, increasing the cloud's reflection of sunlight (the Twomey effect) and cooling the surface; if droplets are too small to rain, longer cloud lifetime also alters the water cycle. This indirect effect is a leading source of uncertainty in climate models.
