张掖七彩丹霞为什么同时有红、黄、绿、白多种颜色？

Why does Zhangye's Rainbow Danxia show red, yellow, green and white at once?

不同地质时期沉积的岩层含铁、锰等矿物种类和多少不同，氧化环境也不一样，层层叠叠就显出多彩条纹。
Layers deposited in different eras contain varying iron, manganese and other minerals and formed under different oxidation conditions, producing multicolored bands.

#七彩丹霞 #矿物色

丹霞地貌为什么一片红色，像火烧过的山？

Why are Danxia landforms red, like fire-scorched mountains?

这里的砂砾岩里含铁，岩石被氧化后呈红褐色；经流水切割，露出层层红崖，于是有了“色如渥丹”的奇观。
Iron in the sandstones oxidizes to red-brown; erosion by water exposes red cliffs, giving the red-as-cinnabar scenery.

#丹霞 #红层

丹霞和雅丹看起来都奇形怪状，到底哪里不同？

Both Danxia and Yardang look bizarre—what is the difference?

丹霞是流水把红色砂砾岩切出来的，多在我国南方湿润区；雅丹是干旱区大风把湖相土丘吹蚀出来的，颜色偏灰黄。
Danxia is carved by water from red sandstone in wetter south China; Yardang is shaped by wind from lake-bed mounds in dry northwest, gray-yellow in color.
