什么是急流？

What is a jet stream?

急流是高空西风带中狭窄、强而快的西风气流带，风速可超过每小时300公里。它位于温度梯度大的锋区上方，对地面气旋路径和航空飞行都有重要影响。
A jet stream is a narrow, fast ribbon of westerly wind high in the atmosphere, with speeds exceeding 300 km/h. It lies above sharp temperature gradients at frontal zones and strongly influences surface storm tracks and aviation routes.

极锋理论如何解释中纬度气旋的生命周期？

How does polar front theory explain the life cycle of mid-latitude cyclones?

极锋理论由比耶克尼斯在1919年提出，把中纬度气旋看作极地冷气团与热带暖气团交界面上发展的波动。气旋沿锋面成长、产生暖锋与冷锋，并在锢囚阶段达到最强后逐渐填塞。这一理论奠定了现代天气图分析与预报的基础。
Bjerknes proposed polar front theory in 1919, viewing mid-latitude cyclones as waves developing along the boundary between polar cold and tropical warm air masses. A cyclone grows along the front, spawning warm and cold fronts, peaks at occlusion, then fills. The theory founded modern weather-map analysis and forecasting.

急流为什么出现在大约十公里高的天空？

Why do jet streams occur around 10 km up in the sky?

急流位于不同温度气团交界（如极地冷气团与副热带暖气团）附近，温度梯度大导致强气压梯度，再加上高空摩擦小，于是形成狭窄的高速西风带。
Jet streams sit near boundaries of very different temperatures (polar vs subtropical air); the large temperature gradient drives a strong pressure gradient, and with little friction aloft, a narrow fast westerly wind forms.

什么是“厄尔尼诺—南方涛动”（ENSO）？它怎样影响全球气候？

What is the El Niño–Southern Oscillation (ENSO), and how does it affect global climate?

ENSO是热带中东太平洋海表温度与大气气压的周期性异常：厄尔尼诺时该区海温异常偏暖、信风减弱，拉尼娜时则偏冷、信风增强。“南方涛动”一名由吉尔伯特·沃克（Gilbert Walker）在1920年代提出，该现象会通过大气遥相关改变全球的降雨与干旱格局。
ENSO is the periodic fluctuation of sea-surface temperature and atmospheric pressure in the tropical central-eastern Pacific: El Niño brings unusually warm waters and weakened trade winds, while La Niña brings the opposite. The term 'Southern Oscillation' was coined by Gilbert Walker in the 1920s, and the phenomenon shifts global rainfall and drought patterns through atmospheric teleconnections.

为什么最常见的日晕半径约为22°,而且内侧偏红、外侧偏蓝紫?

Why does the commonest solar halo have a radius of about 22°, and why is its inner edge reddish while its outer rim is bluish?

22°晕是阳光穿过大气中随机取向的六方柱状冰晶发生折射形成的。光线经过夹角为60°的两个晶面时,能量集中在最小偏向角附近——红光约21.8°,蓝紫光约22.4°,于是围绕太阳出现半径约22°的明亮光环。短波长的光偏向角更大而被排到环的外侧,红光留在内侧,晕因此呈现内红外蓝的色彩顺序。
The 22° halo forms when sunlight refracts through randomly oriented hexagonal columnar ice crystals in the air. Rays crossing two crystal faces that meet at 60° concentrate near the minimum-deviation angle, about 21.8° for red light and 22.4° for violet, so a bright ring of roughly 22° radius encircles the Sun. Shorter wavelengths deviate more strongly and are displaced outward, leaving red on the inside and giving the halo its characteristic colour order.

闪电放电为什么能在数毫秒内把通道里的空气加热到约3万K,比太阳表面还要热?

How can a lightning discharge heat the air in its channel to about 30,000 K within milliseconds, hotter than the surface of the Sun?

雷暴云内的电荷分离可建立上亿伏的电位差,击穿后先导通道开辟出直径仅几厘米的高导电通路,紧随其后的回击电流峰值通常达20至30千安。如此巨大的电流挤在极小的截面里,靠焦耳热在微秒量级内把稀薄空气加热到约30000 K,通道气压骤增并向外推出冲击波。这道冲击波传到人耳,就是我们听到的雷声。
Charge separation inside a thundercloud builds potential differences of hundreds of megavolts; once breakdown occurs, the leader carves a conductive channel only centimeters wide, and the following return stroke carries peak currents of roughly 20-30 kA. Forcing that current through such a tiny cross section deposits Joule heat fast enough to raise the thin air to about 30,000 K in microseconds, and the sudden pressure rise drives a shock wave outward. That shock is what reaches our ears as thunder.

什么是露点温度？

What is the dew point temperature?

露点是在恒定气压下空气冷却到饱和、开始凝结出水汽所对应的温度，反映空气中实际的水汽含量。露点越高说明空气越潮湿，当气温降至露点以下时常出现雾、露或云。
The dew point is the temperature to which air must cool at constant pressure to become saturated and begin condensing moisture, reflecting its actual water vapor content. A higher dew point means more humid air, and when air temperature drops to the dew point, fog, dew, or clouds form.

飓风（台风）是如何形成的？

How do hurricanes (typhoons) form?

飓风在温暖（约26℃以上）的洋面上形成，海面水汽上升释放潜热，使中心气压降低并旋转增强，最终发展为具风眼的风暴。它们在东亚称台风，在美洲附近称飓风。
Hurricanes form over warm ocean water above about 26°C, where rising moisture releases latent heat, lowering central pressure and spinning the system up into a storm with an eye. They are called typhoons in East Asia and hurricanes near the Americas.

什么是“科里奥利效应”？它如何影响风和洋流？

What is the Coriolis effect, and how does it affect winds and ocean currents?

科里奥利效应源于地球自转：在旋转的地球上，运动的物体（如风、洋流）在北半球向右偏、南半球向左偏。它使全球风带和洋流呈旋转状分布，也是热带气旋旋转方向（北半球逆时针、南半球顺时针）的原因。
The Coriolis effect arises from Earth's rotation: moving objects such as winds and currents are deflected to the right in the Northern Hemisphere and to the left in the Southern Hemisphere. It shapes global wind belts and ocean gyres and determines the spin direction of tropical cyclones (counterclockwise north, clockwise south).

季风是什么？为什么亚洲季风显著？

What is a monsoon, and why is Asia's monsoon so strong?

季风是随季节反转的大范围风向变化，主要由海陆热力差异驱动。亚洲拥有世界最大陆地（欧亚大陆）和最大海洋（太平洋），夏季陆地升温快形成低压吸引海洋湿润气流，故季风特别强盛。
A monsoon is a large-scale seasonal reversal of wind direction, mainly driven by land-sea thermal contrasts. Asia sits between the huge Eurasian landmass and the Pacific Ocean; in summer the land heats fast, forming low pressure that draws in moist ocean air, making its monsoon especially strong.

What is a tornado?

风眼是飓风中心一片相对平静、少云无风的圆形区域，被周围高耸的眼墙所包围。眼墙内风雨最猛，穿过风眼后会遭遇另一侧同样强烈的天气。
The eye is a relatively calm, clear, low-wind circle at a hurricane's center, ringed by the towering eyewall. The eyewall holds the storm's fiercest winds and rain, and the other side brings equally violent weather after the eye passes.

Why does a wavy jet stream lead to extreme weather?

季风是什么，亚洲季风为何如此强大？

What is a monsoon, and why is the Asian monsoon so powerful?

季风是随季节反转的大尺度风系，根源是海陆间巨大的热力差异造成气压季节性反转。亚洲拥有最高耸的青藏高原与最广袤的陆地，夏季大陆剧烈增温形成强低压，把印度洋湿空气吸向内陆。韦伯斯特在2006年对亚洲季风的机制与变率做了综合论述。
A monsoon is a seasonal reversal of large-scale winds rooted in the huge thermal contrast between land and ocean that flips pressure patterns. Asia combines the towering Tibetan Plateau with the largest landmass, so summer heating creates a strong low that draws moist Indian Ocean air inland. Webster synthesized the Asian monsoon's mechanics and variability in 2006.

气旋和反气旋有什么不同？

What is the difference between a cyclone and an anticyclone?

气旋是中心气压低、空气向内辐合并上升的天气系统，北半球逆时针旋转，常带来阴雨。反气旋中心气压高、空气向外辐散下沉，北半球顺时针旋转，多晴朗干燥天气。
A cyclone is a low-pressure system where air converges inward and rises, rotating counterclockwise in the Northern Hemisphere and often bringing clouds and rain. An anticyclone is a high-pressure system with diverging sinking air, rotating clockwise northward and bringing clear, dry weather.

How does a tornado form, and why are mid-latitudes its hotspot?

龙卷风源于强雷暴中的中气旋，当上升气流与风切变使暖湿与干冷空气剧烈旋转，便可能下伸成漏斗。中纬度常是冷干极地气团与暖湿热带气团交锋之地，提供了最易产生超级单体的环境条件。马科夫斯基与理查森在2009年综述了龙卷形成的物理机制。
A tornado develops from a mesocyclone within a strong thunderstorm, where updrafts and wind shear make warm-moist and dry-cold air rotate violently and stretch into a funnel. Mid-latitudes are where cold polar and warm tropical air masses collide, yielding the classic environment for supercells. Markowski and Richardson reviewed tornado-genesis physics in 2009.

What produces the ring of light at 22 degrees around the Sun or Moon, and why is it often taken as a sign of approaching rain?

高云中的六棱柱状冰晶在随机翻滚取向下,阳光经晶体相邻两个侧面发生折射,透射光集中在最小偏向角约22°处最亮,形成内缘锐利的光环。由于折射色散,晕的内侧偏红、外侧渐变为蓝紫色。卷云往往是一整套锋面雨带最先抵达的高云,因此民间日晕主雨的经验说法具备一定的天气学依据。
Hexagonal prism ice crystals tumbling randomly in high cloud refract sunlight through two adjacent side faces, and the transmitted light peaks near the minimum deviation angle of about 22 degrees, drawing a bright ring with a sharp inner edge. Because refraction disperses wavelengths, the halo's inner rim appears reddish and shades outward through blue and violet. Cirrostratus sheets usually arrive ahead of an approaching frontal system, which gives folk sayings linking halos to imminent rain a real meteorological footing.

闪电的梯级先导是如何一步步打通云地之间的放电通道的?

How does a stepped lightning leader open a discharge channel between cloud and ground step by step?

对流云中霰粒与冰晶碰撞起电,可在云底与地面之间积累出每米数百千伏的强电场。当局部场强逼近空气击穿阈值,先导顶端发射的流光汇聚成直径厘米级、温度上万开尔文的高温通道,以约50米的步长间歇式向下推进。先导一旦接地便触发回击,瞬时电流平均可达30千安左右,通道骤然增亮并激起冲击波,这就是我们听到的雷声。
Collisional charging between graupel and ice crystals inside convective clouds accumulates electric fields reaching hundreds of kilovolts per meter between cloud base and ground. Once the local field nears air breakdown, streamers launched at the leader tip merge into a hot channel centimeters wide at tens of thousands of kelvin, probing downward in intermittent steps of roughly 50 meters. When the leader touches ground it triggers the return stroke, carrying instantaneous currents averaging about 30 kiloamperes; the channel flares brilliantly and drives the shock wave we hear as thunder.

How does wind form?

风是空气因温度差异造成的气压差而发生的水平流动。当某地空气受热上升、气压降低，周围高压空气就会流过来填补，于是形成了风。
Wind is the horizontal movement of air caused by pressure differences that arise from temperature variations. When air heats and rises, lowering pressure in one place, surrounding higher-pressure air flows in to fill the gap, creating wind.

什么是大气压？

What is atmospheric pressure?

大气压是地球大气因重力作用而对地表单位面积施加的压力，海平面平均约为1013百帕。气压的高低变化与天气系统密切相关，低压常带来阴雨，高压多对应晴朗。
Atmospheric pressure is the force exerted by the weight of the air on a unit area of the surface, averaging about 1013 hPa at sea level. Pressure variations link closely to weather: low pressure often brings clouds and rain, while high pressure usually means fair weather.

What is the difference between weather and climate?

What causes lightning and thunder?

雷暴云内电荷分离，当电位差足够大时空气被击穿放电形成闪电，瞬间加热空气使其急剧膨胀发出轰鸣，这就是雷。因为光比声快，我们总是先见闪电后闻雷声。
