关中平原为什么叫“八百里秦川”，又怎么成了帝王之都？

Why is the Guanzhong Plain called Eight Hundred Li of Qin and a seat of capitals?

渭河冲积出平坦肥沃的八百里沃野，四面有关山屏障、易守难攻，又有水利之便，所以周秦汉唐多定都于此。
The Wei River built a flat fertile plain, shielded by passes and easy to defend with good water, so many dynasties made it their capital.

河西走廊为什么是古代丝绸之路的咽喉？

Why was the Hexi Corridor the throat of the ancient Silk Road?

南有祁连雪山供水、北有沙漠戈壁相隔，中间这条狭长绿洲带是中原通往西域唯一易行的通道，所以商旅必走。
With the Qilian snows to the south and desert to the north, this narrow green belt was the only practical path from China to the west, so caravans had to use it.

四川盆地为什么被称为“天府之国”？

Why is the Sichuan Basin called the Land of Abundance?

都江堰引水灌田，加上盆地周山挡寒、内部温暖湿润，物产丰足，自古就粮仓殷实，故称天府。
Dujiangyan irrigates the fields while surrounding mountains block cold and keep it warm-humid, yielding abundant crops—hence the name.

看似坚硬的石头也会“风化”慢慢碎掉吗？

Can seemingly hard rock weather and crumble over time?

会。日晒冻融让岩石胀缩开裂，水和二氧化碳溶蚀矿物，树根也能撑裂石缝，物理和化学风化终把大山削平。
Yes. Heating-freezing, dissolution by water and CO2, and plant roots all crack rock; physical and chemical weathering slowly flatten mountains.

云南为什么被称为“植物王国”？

Why is Yunnan called a kingdom of plants?

它从热带到高山气候齐全，地形起伏大，给成千上万种植物留出各自的小环境，所以物种特别多。
From tropics to alpine climates across rugged terrain, Yunnan offers countless micro-habitats, so plant species abound.

长江中下游为什么被称为“鱼米之乡”？

Why is the middle-lower Yangtze called a land of fish and rice?

这里河网密布、湖泊众多，水田连片、热量雨水充足，既产稻米又盛鱼虾，物产格外丰富。
Dense rivers and lakes, vast paddies and ample heat and rain make it rich in both rice and fish.
