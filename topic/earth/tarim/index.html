曾经的大湖罗布泊为什么消失了？

Why did the once-great Lop Nur lake disappear?

上游孔雀河、塔里木河的水被大量引去灌溉，入湖水量锐减，加上气候变干，湖面最终干涸，只留下盐壳戈壁。
Upstream water was diverted for irrigation and the climate dried, so inflow plummeted and the lake dried to a salt-crusted wasteland.

塔克拉玛干沙漠为什么被称为“死亡之海”？

Why is the Taklamakan called the Sea of Death?

它是我国最大沙漠，极度干旱、沙丘移动、寸草难生，古代商队迷路其中九死一生，因而得名。
China's largest desert is hyper-arid with shifting dunes and almost no life; lost caravans rarely survived, earning the grim name.

Why is the Tarim Basin so dry, with almost no rain?

它被高山四面围住，又离海洋几千公里，湿润海风翻不过山也走不到这里，于是成了我国最干旱的地方之一。
Surrounded by high mountains and thousands of km from the sea, moist winds can't reach it, making it one of China's driest places.
