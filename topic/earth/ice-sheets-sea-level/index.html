末次盛冰期（LGM）以来冰盖退缩对地壳均衡调整（GIA）有何表现？

How does post-Last Glacial Maximum ice-sheet retreat manifest in glacio-isostatic adjustment (GIA)?

厚冰盖压迫地壳使其下沉、地幔物质外移；冰消融后地壳缓慢回弹，周边因物质回流而下沉，形成“跷跷板”式均衡调整。今日北欧与加拿大仍以上升为主，而部分海岸则相对下沉，影响区域相对海平面。
Heavy ice depressed the crust and displaced mantle; after melt the crust slowly rebounds while peripheral areas subside as mantle flows back—a see-saw isostatic response. Today Fennoscandia and Canada still rise, while some coasts subside, shaping regional relative sea level.

南极“底层水”的形成与冰架融化有何联系？对全球洋流意味着什么？

How is Antarctic Bottom Water formation linked to ice-shelf melt, and what does it mean for global ocean circulation?

南极底层水由沿岸高密度冷水下沉形成，是经向翻转环流的重要组成部分。冰架融化的淡水输入降低海水密度、阻碍下沉，削弱底层水形成，可能减缓全球深层洋流并影响热量与碳的输送。
Antarctic Bottom Water forms where dense coastal water sinks, a key part of the meridional overturning circulation. Freshwater from ice-shelf melt lowers density and hinders sinking, weakening bottom-water formation, which could slow global deep currents and disrupt heat and carbon transport.

什么是冰盖的“动力不稳定性”（如海洋冰盖失稳 MISI 与海洋冰崖失稳 MICI）？

What is the dynamic instability of ice sheets, such as marine ice-sheet instability (MISI) and marine ice-cliff instability (MICI)?

海洋性冰盖若基底低于海平面，当暖水侵蚀或冰架消失使接地线后退，冰流加速，形成海洋冰盖失稳（MISI）。若冰崖过高还会因重力崩塌产生海洋冰崖失稳（MICI），使冰量在短时间内大量入海，远超单纯融化估算。
Marine-based ice sheets grounded below sea level can destabilize as warm water or shelf loss retreats the grounding line, speeding ice flow—marine ice-sheet instability (MISI). Very tall ice cliffs may also collapse under gravity, marine ice-cliff instability (MICI), dumping ice to the ocean far faster than melt alone predicts.

格陵兰与南极冰盖的质量变化目前由哪些卫星监测？

Which satellites currently monitor mass changes of the Greenland and Antarctic ice sheets?

NASA 与德国航太中心的 GRACE 及后继 GRACE-FO 卫星通过探测地球重力场变化估算冰盖质量盈亏；NASA 的 ICESat 与 ICESat-2 激光测高则直接测量冰面高程变化，两者互补。
NASA and DLR's GRACE and successor GRACE-FO satellites infer ice-sheet mass change from Earth's gravity field, while NASA's ICESat and ICESat-2 laser altimetry directly measure surface elevation—the two approaches complement each other.

全球海平面上升的两大主要原因是什么？

What are the two main causes of global sea-level rise?

一是海水受热膨胀（热膨胀），二是陆地冰（山岳冰川、格陵兰与南极冰盖）融化汇入海洋。IPCC AR6 指出二者贡献相当，20 世纪以来全球平均海平面已上升约 20 厘米。
One is thermal expansion of warming seawater, the other is land ice—mountain glaciers and the Greenland and Antarctic ice sheets—melting into the ocean. IPCC AR6 notes both contribute comparably, with global mean sea level already up about 20 cm since 1900.

冰架是什么？它为何对海平面上升有“缓冲”作用？

What is an ice shelf, and why does it act as a buffer against sea-level rise?

冰架是陆地冰盖向海洋延伸、漂浮在海上的厚冰体。它已浮于海水之上，融化不直接影响海平面；但其阻拦作用减缓了后方陆地冰流入海，一旦崩解，内陆冰流失加速，反而推高海平面。
An ice shelf is a thick platform of glacial ice extending from the land and floating on the sea. Because it already displaces seawater, its melt doesn't directly raise sea level, but by buttressing land ice it slows its flow; once it collapses, inland ice loss accelerates and sea level rises.

为什么北极海冰减少对全球气候有“放大”效应？

Why does Arctic sea-ice loss have an amplifying effect on global climate?

海冰反照率高，能反射大量阳光；海冰消融露出深色海面，吸收更多热量，进一步升温并加速消融，形成反照率正反馈。这种“北极放大”使北极增温速率约为全球平均的 2–3 倍。
Sea ice has high albedo, reflecting sunlight; its loss exposes dark ocean that absorbs more heat, warming further and melting more—an albedo positive feedback. This Arctic amplification makes Arctic warming roughly 2–3 times the global average.

冰芯中困住气体的“气体年龄”为何往往比包裹它的冰层更年轻？

Why is the gas age of trapped air in an ice core often younger than the age of the ice enclosing it?

雪层层层压实到完全封闭气泡需要一段深度（封闭深度），此前的孔隙仍与大气连通。因此气泡封存的是稍晚时期的大气，形成数百年的“气体—冰年龄差”。在南极冰芯中这一差值可达数百年，解读记录时必须校正。
Snow must compact to a sealing depth before bubbles close off, while pores above remain open to the atmosphere. Thus the trapped air records slightly later atmospheric composition, creating a gas–ice age difference of up to several centuries in Antarctic cores that must be corrected when interpreting records.

什么是冰芯？

What is an ice core?

冰芯是从冰川或冰盖中钻出的长圆柱冰样，一层层冰里封着古代的雪、气泡和灰尘，像地球的日记。
An ice core is a long cylinder drilled from a glacier or ice sheet; each layer traps old snow, air bubbles and dust like a diary of Earth.
