铅同位素怎样追踪污染的来源？

How do lead isotopes trace pollution sources?

不同矿床和含铅汽油的铅同位素组成不同，比对土壤、冰芯和人体样本就能锁定污染来自哪里。
Lead from different ores and gasoline has distinct isotope signatures; matching soils, ice cores and human samples pins down the pollution source.

大氧化事件怎样改变了地球？

How did the Great Oxidation Event change Earth?

约24亿年前光合作用释放的氧气首次在大气中累积，消灭了厌氧生物、形成红层并奠定需氧生命的基础。
About 2.4 billion years ago oxygen from photosynthesis built up in air, killing anaerobes, forming red beds and enabling aerobic life.

硼同位素如何重建古海洋的酸度？

How do boron isotopes reconstruct ancient ocean acidity?

硼两种同位素在海水中的比例随pH变化，并会被珊瑚和有孔虫壳层记录，据此可反推地质时期的海洋酸碱度。
The boron isotope ratio in seawater tracks pH and is recorded in coral and foraminifera shells, letting us infer past ocean acidity.

为什么智利有世界最大的铜矿带?

Why does Chile host the world largest copper belt?

直答:智利纵贯安第斯俯冲带上斑岩铜矿最有利的地段,兼具矿床生成与保存的双重优势,故拥有世界最大铜矿带。机理:纳斯卡板片俯冲脱水使地幔楔部分熔融,富金属岩浆上升到浅部定位冷凝,晚期岩浆热液把铜沉淀在斑岩体顶部,聚成巨型斑岩铜矿;北部极端干旱剥蚀微弱,矿床得以完好保存,加上百余年规模化开采,使智利铜产量约占全球四分之一。
Chile straddles the most favorable stretch of Andean subduction for porphyry copper, combining ideal ore generation with preservation. Dehydration of the subducting Nazca plate partially melts the mantle wedge; metal-rich magmas rise and crystallize at shallow levels, and late magmatic-hydrothermal fluids deposit copper near the porphyry apices into giant deposits. Extreme aridity in northern Chile limits erosion and preserves them, while a century of scaled-up mining makes the country supply roughly a quarter of world copper.

条带状铁建造为什么几乎只形成于约25亿年前的早前寒武纪，其后仅在雪球地球等极端时期短暂再现？

Why did banded iron formations form almost exclusively before about 2.5 billion years ago, briefly returning only during extreme episodes like Snowball Earth?

早前寒武纪海洋溶有大量二价铁而整体缺氧，浅海底栖蓝细菌释放的氧一经产生便把铁氧化，沉淀出铁氧化物与燧石互层的条带状铁建造。约24亿年前的大氧化事件令大气充氧并逐渐波及深海，残余的铁转而被硫化物束缚成黄铁矿，BIF随之断绝了原料来源；直到新元古代雪球地球使海洋长期封冻、底层水重新缺氧，这类矿床才昙花一现地复发。
Early Precambrian oceans carried abundant dissolved ferrous iron under an essentially anoxic atmosphere; whatever oxygen benthic cyanobacteria released in shallow water immediately oxidized that iron, which settled as alternating iron-oxide and chert bands. The Great Oxidation Event around 2.4 billion years ago oxygenated the air and eventually the deep ocean, locking residual iron into pyrite and starving BIF of feedstock; only when Snowball Earth froze the seas and re-created anoxic bottom waters did such ores briefly return.

蛇纹石化与生命起源有什么联系？

How is serpentinization linked to the origin of life?

海水与地幔橄榄岩反应产生富氢流体和甲烷，为早期生命提供能量与有机物，类似深海热液生态的化学基础。
Seawater reacting with mantle rock yields hydrogen-rich fluids and methane, supplying energy and organics for early life like hydrothermal ecosystems.

什么是氮沉降，它有什么影响？

What is nitrogen deposition and why does it matter?

工业和农业排放的氮化物随雨雪落到陆地海洋，过量会酸化土壤水体、扰乱植被并污染水体。
Nitrogen compounds from industry and farming fall with rain and snow; excess acidifies soils and waters and disrupts ecosystems.

铁施肥如何影响海洋生产力？

How does iron fertilization affect ocean productivity?

铁是远洋浮游植物稀缺的养分，少量补铁可促其大量繁殖、吸收二氧化碳，但也会改变生态并存在争议。
Iron is a scarce nutrient for open-ocean phytoplankton; adding a little can spur blooms that absorb CO2, but it alters ecosystems and is debated.

俯冲带里的水循环是怎样的？

What is the water cycle in subduction zones?

海洋板块携带含水矿物下沉，深处受热脱水，水汽上升降低熔点触发火山，水就这样在地球内部循环。
A sinking plate carries water-rich minerals; deep heat drives it off, the vapor rises, lowers melting point and fuels volcanoes, cycling water through the Earth.

How are rare-earth elements used to trace a material's source?

不同岩石的稀土元素配分模式像指纹，比较沉积物或尘埃的稀土特征就能判断它们来自哪片母岩。
Rare-earth patterns act like fingerprints of rocks; matching sediments or dust to them reveals which parent rock they came from.
