侵入岩和喷出岩的矿物粒度为何不同？

Why do intrusive and extrusive rocks differ in mineral grain size?

侵入岩在地下缓慢冷却，矿物有充足时间结晶，故颗粒粗大（如花岗岩）。喷出岩在地表快速冷却，结晶时间短，颗粒细甚至形成玻璃质（如黑曜石）。冷却速率直接决定结晶程度。
Intrusive rocks cool slowly underground, giving minerals time to grow large crystals, as in granite. Extrusive rocks cool fast at the surface, leaving little time to crystallize, yielding fine grains or even glassy texture, as in obsidian. Cooling rate directly controls crystallinity.

如何通过砾岩判断古环境？

How can conglomerate reveal past environments?

砾岩由磨圆砾石胶结而成，砾石大小、磨圆度和分选反映搬运距离与水动力：远源强水动力磨圆好、分选佳，常指示河流或滨浅海环境；棱角状近源砾岩则多为山前冲积扇。
Conglomerate is cemented rounded gravel; clast size, roundness and sorting reflect transport distance and energy. Well-rounded well-sorted far-traveled gravel indicates rivers or shallow marine settings, while angular near-source clasts point to alluvial fans.

板岩、千枚岩、片岩代表怎样的变质程度？

What metamorphic grades do slate, phyllite and schist represent?

它们显示递进变质：板岩由泥岩轻度变质、具板状劈理；千枚岩进一步重结晶、现丝绢光泽；片岩结晶更明显、片状矿物定向排列。反映温度压力逐步升高。
They show progressive metamorphism: slate is low-grade from mudrock with slaty cleavage; phyllite is further recrystallized with silky sheen; schist has clearer crystals and aligned flaky minerals. They reflect rising temperature and pressure.

砂岩和页岩是如何形成的？

How do sandstone and shale form?

砂岩由砂粒经水流或风搬运后压实胶结而成，颗粒较粗；页岩由极细的黏土颗粒在静水环境缓慢沉积、压实而成，层理薄且易裂成片。
Sandstone forms when sand grains transported by water or wind are compacted and cemented, with coarser particles. Shale forms from very fine clay particles slowly deposited and compacted in quiet water, with thin layering that splits easily.

大理岩和石灰岩有什么关系？

What is the relationship between marble and limestone?

大理岩是石灰岩或白云岩在高温高压下重结晶形成的变质岩。原岩中的方解石颗粒长大并重新联结，使大理岩更致密、具晶粒结构，并常呈现美丽纹理。
Marble is a metamorphic rock formed by recrystallization of limestone or dolomite under heat and pressure. Calcite grains in the parent rock grow and rejoin, making marble denser with a crystalline texture and often beautiful veining.

玄武岩为什么常呈柱状节理？

Why does basalt often show columnar joints?

厚层玄武岩岩浆缓慢冷却时均匀收缩，产生垂直于冷却面的六边形或多边形裂隙，风化后显出规则的石柱，如爱尔兰巨人堤。这是物理收缩破裂的典型结果。
Thick basalt cools slowly and contracts evenly, forming hexagonal or polygonal cracks perpendicular to the cooling surface; weathering reveals regular columns like the Giant's Causeway in Ireland. It is a classic contraction-fracture result.

Rocks are made from different materials that form in volcanoes, rivers, or the sea, so they come in many colors and shapes.

宝石为什么有不同颜色？

Why are gemstones colorful?

宝石的颜色来自石头里极少量的金属杂质，比如铁让石头发红、铬让它发绿。晶体结构也会改变光的方式，让宝石闪闪发亮。
A gem's color comes from trace metals in the stone, such as iron making it red or chromium making it green. The crystal structure also bends light so the gem sparkles.

地壳为什么会慢慢移动？

Why does the Earth's crust move?

地壳分成几块大板块，底下软软的岩流带着它们慢慢漂移，所以地壳一直在动。板块移动也造成地震和火山。
Earth's crust is split into big plates that float on soft rock below and drift slowly, so the crust is always moving. Their motion also causes earthquakes and volcanoes.

山顶的空气为什么稀薄？

Why is mountain air thin?

越往高处空气越稀薄，因为大部分空气压在低处，山顶能呼吸的氧气就变少。爬山的人常要慢慢走。
Higher up the air gets thinner because most of it is pressed down low, leaving less oxygen on the peak. Climbers often move slowly to cope.

煤是岩石吗？它是怎么来的？

Is coal a rock, and how did it form?

煤是一种可燃有机岩，由远古植物遗体在沼泽中堆积，经长期压实、缺氧和煤化作用形成。它属沉积岩的特殊类型，记录了古植被和古气候信息。
Coal is a combustible organic rock formed from ancient plant remains accumulating in swamps and undergoing long compaction, oxygen-poor conditions and coalification. It is a special type of sedimentary rock recording past vegetation and climate.

Rainwater with a weak acid seeps into cracks in limestone and slowly dissolves the rock, hollowing out big caves over a long time.

火成岩、沉积岩和变质岩是怎么区分的？

How are igneous, sedimentary and metamorphic rocks distinguished?

火成岩由岩浆冷却凝固形成；沉积岩由碎屑或化学沉淀经压实胶结而成；变质岩则是已有岩石在高温高压下改变结构矿物形成。三类岩石可经地质循环相互转化。
Igneous rocks form by cooling and solidifying magma; sedimentary rocks by compaction and cementation of fragments or chemical precipitates; metamorphic rocks by alteration of existing rock under heat and pressure. The three transform into each other through the rock cycle.

间歇泉为什么会喷热水？

Why do geysers erupt?

地下通道里的水被岩浆烤热，底部的水太烫却因压力煮不沸，一旦压力松动就猛地炸成蒸汽喷出来。间歇泉就是这样定时喷热水。
Water in underground channels is heated by magma; deep water gets very hot but cannot boil under pressure, then suddenly bursts as steam when pressure drops. That is why a geyser erupts on schedule.

地球内部为什么分好几层？

Why is the Earth made of layers?

地球从外到内是地壳、地幔和地核，那是按温度和密度自然分层的。沉重的铁镍沉到中心，较轻岩石浮在外层。
Earth is layered from outside in as crust, mantle, and core, sorted by temperature and density. Heavy iron and nickel sank to the center while lighter rock floated outward.

化石到底是什么？

What are fossils?

很久以前动植物死后被泥沙盖住，经过很长很长时间变成了石头里的印子，就是化石。
Long ago, plants and animals were buried by mud and sand and slowly turned into stone prints over a very long time; those are fossils.

沙子是从哪里来的？

Where does sand come from?

大石头被风、水和冰块一点点磨碎，最后变成细细的沙粒。
Big rocks are slowly ground down by wind, water, and ice until they become tiny grains of sand.
