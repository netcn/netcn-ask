区域变质和接触变质有什么不同？

What distinguishes regional from contact metamorphism?

接触变质发生在岩浆侵入体周围，热量使围岩在狭窄的接触晕内重结晶，温度高而压力低，形成角岩等，不含定向构造。区域变质是造山运动中大范围深埋加压加温的结果，分布面积广大，常伴随板岩、片岩、片麻岩等，特征是矿物定向排列的片理构造。两种作用都靠矿物重结晶实现，但温压组合和空间尺度截然不同，变质程度还可以用温压计定量估计。
Contact metamorphism occurs in a narrow halo around an igneous intrusion, where heat alone recrystallizes the host rock under low pressure into hornfels and other rocks without foliation. Regional metamorphism accompanies mountain building, where rocks are deeply buried, squeezed, and heated over vast areas, producing slate, schist, and gneiss with a characteristic foliation from aligned minerals. Both rely on recrystallization, but their temperature-pressure conditions and spatial scales differ sharply, and metamorphic grade can be quantified with thermobarometers.

三角洲的垂向沉积序列说明什么？

What does a delta's vertical sequence tell us?

三角洲垂向序列把水深变化翻译成岩性。同一地点向上变粗，说明离河口渐近、水变浅，三角洲向海推进，即空间增长赶不上来沙，或相对海平面在降，属海退序列；向上变细则相反，是海水侵入的海进序列。岩芯配上水深指示的化石与波痕，能重建古海岸线与升降方向。谨慎在于：来沙增多与海平面下降能造出同样的向上变粗，序列本身分不开。
A delta's vertical sequence translates changes in water depth into rock type. Coarsening upward at one place means it was shallowing and nearing the river mouth, the delta prograding seaward because space for sediment grew more slowly than the sand supply or relative sea level was falling, a regressive sequence; fining upward is the opposite, a transgressive one in which the sea floods in. Cores plus depth-indicating fossils and ripples let you restore the ancient shoreline and the sense of uplift. The caution is that more sand and a falling sea level make the same coarsening-up, and the sequence alone cannot tell them apart.

为什么外核的对流能维持地球磁场？固体内核在其中起什么作用？

Why does outer-core convection sustain Earth's magnetic field, and what role does the solid inner core play?

外核是导电流体，其湍流对流在科里奥利力作用下形成螺旋运动，通过自激发电机过程把机械能转化为磁能。内核凝固释放潜热并排出轻元素（如氧、硫），为外核对流提供热—组成双层驱动：轻元素上升与热量耗散共同维持强烈的对流。内核还起稳定作用，使极性倒转更有规律；若外核停止对流，磁场会在数万年内衰减消失。地震层析与数值模拟还表明，内核的不对称生长可能使对流呈现非对称形态，影响磁场的长期行为。
The outer core is a conducting fluid whose turbulent, Coriolis-driven helical convection generates magnetic energy from mechanical energy through a self-exciting dynamo. Inner-core crystallization releases latent heat and expels light elements (oxygen, sulfur), providing both thermal and compositional buoyancy that drives convection. The solid core also stabilizes the system, making reversals more regular; without outer-core convection, the field would decay away within tens of thousands of years. Seismic tomography and numerical dynamo simulations further suggest that asymmetric growth of the inner core shapes the convection pattern and influences the field's long-term behavior.

片麻岩中为什么常呈现黑白相间的条带状构造？它与板岩的板理有何不同？

Why do gneisses commonly show alternating light and dark bands, and how does this differ from slaty cleavage?

在高温高压条件下，浅色长英质矿物（长石、石英）与暗色镁铁质矿物（黑云母、角闪石）发生分离并分别集中成条带，形成片麻状构造。这种分带是变质分异的结果：高温塑性变形下矿物经压溶、扩散与重结晶，在应力方向分别富集，而非仅靠片状矿物的机械旋转定向。与板理相比，片麻状构造对应更高的变质级别，温度继续升高时岩石还可局部熔融形成混合岩。
Under high-grade conditions, light felsic minerals (feldspar, quartz) and dark mafic minerals (biotite, hornblende) segregate into distinct bands, producing gneissic banding. The banding results from metamorphic differentiation: under high-temperature plastic deformation, pressure solution, diffusion, and recrystallization concentrate minerals separately along the stress direction, not merely by mechanical rotation of platy minerals. Compared with slaty cleavage, gneissic banding marks a higher metamorphic grade, and with further heating the rock partially melts to form migmatite.

What are stratigraphy and index fossils?

冰川是如何塑造地形的？

How do glaciers shape the landscape?

冰川不是静止的冰，而是靠自重流动、底部融水润滑的固体河流。它啃噬基岩有两个办法：冻在冰底的砾石像砂纸磨过冰床；渗进裂隙的水反复融冻，把岩块撬起拔出并带走，这叫刨蚀。冰运的碎屑不分选不分层，黏土与巨石混在一起，巨石能被推到远离母岩之处，流水做不到。认地貌要把挖与堆分开：U形谷、冰斗、角峰是侵蚀产物，碛垄与鼓丘是堆积产物。
A glacier is not standing ice but a solid river that moves under its own weight, lubricated by meltwater at its bed. It attacks bedrock two ways: gravel frozen into the base scours the floor like sandpaper, and water refreezing in cracks pries blocks loose and rips them out, which is plucking. Its load is unsorted and unlayered, clay and boulders mixed together, and a boulder can be pushed far from the rock it came from, something running water cannot do. When reading the landforms, separate digging from dumping: U-shaped valleys, cirques and horns are eroded, while moraine ridges and drumlins are piled up.

磁性地层学如何给岩石定年？

How does magnetostratigraphy date rocks?

沉积落底或岩浆冷却时，岩石里的磁性矿物顺着当时地磁场取向并留住方向；地磁场极性还会正反交替，连续地层里就留下正、逆相间的磁化带。测出剖面磁化方向的变化，与标准极性年表比对，地层便有了年代框架。它自己不产出绝对年龄，年表靠海底磁异常条带加同位素定年标定；前提是层序连续、未倒转、无缺失，构造翻转和沉积间断都会让对比串位。
As sediment settles or magma cools, the magnetic minerals in the rock align with the field of the moment and lock its direction in; because the geomagnetic field reverses polarity, an unbroken sequence of strata becomes alternating bands of normal and reversed magnetization. Measure how the direction changes up a section and match that pattern to the standard polarity timescale, and the beds gain an age framework. The method yields no absolute age of its own—that timescale must be calibrated from seafloor magnetic anomalies plus radiometric dates. It also assumes continuous, unoverturned strata: folding or a sedimentary hiatus puts the correlation out of place.

What is the difference between intrusive and extrusive rock?

Magma cooling slowly underground forms intrusive rock (e.g. granite) with coarse crystals; erupting and cooling fast forms extrusive rock (e.g. basalt) with fine or glassy texture.

三叶虫的眼睛由方解石构成，这对它们的化石保存与研究有何意义？

Trilobite eyes were made of calcite. What does this mean for their fossilization and study?

三叶虫复眼的晶状体由方解石单晶构成，方解石的双折射等晶体光学性质本会使光线分离成像，但三叶虫将晶状体的光轴精确控制在垂直于透镜表面的方向，从而避免了重影，实现了能聚焦成像的矫正镜片。因为晶状体本身是矿物，比软组织更容易石化，数亿年前的晶状体结构与光学设计至今仍可用显微技术研究。这一发现成为自然光学工程的经典案例，也说明复杂的光学系统早在寒武纪就已演化出现。
The lenses of trilobite compound eyes are single calcite crystals; calcite's birefringence would normally split images, but trilobites oriented the lens optic axis precisely perpendicular to the lens surface, avoiding double images and producing corrective lenses that focus light. Because the lenses are themselves minerals, they fossilize far more readily than soft tissue, so lens structure and optical design from hundreds of millions of years ago can still be studied microscopically. This makes them a classic case of natural optical engineering and shows that sophisticated optics evolved already in the Cambrian.

从孤立岛状到三维骨架状硅酸盐，矿物硬度和解理为何呈现系统性变化？

From isolated island silicates to three-dimensional framework silicates, why do hardness and cleavage change systematically?

聚合程度主要决定内部键合方式、解理与定向硬度，但硬度并不随聚合程度单调增加：架状硅酸盐（石英）在所有方向都硬，片状硅酸盐（云母）因层间键弱而在垂直片层方向极软。橄榄石虽是岛状硅酸盐，但因晶格致密堆积，硬度（约6.5–7）反而高于链状的辉石和角闪石（约5–6）。解理方向完全受四面体链或面取向控制——辉石沿单链呈约90°的两组解理，角闪石沿双链呈约120°解理，云母沿片层呈完全解理。
Polymerization chiefly controls bonding, cleavage, and directional hardness, but hardness does not rise monotonically with it: framework silicates (quartz) are hard in all directions, while sheet silicates (mica) are very soft normal to the sheets because interlayer bonds are weak. Olivine, though an island silicate, is actually harder (about 6.5–7) than chain silicates such as pyroxene and amphibole (about 5–6) owing to its densely packed lattice. Cleavage direction is set by the orientation of tetrahedral chains or sheets—pyroxene cleaves in two directions at about 90 degrees along single chains, amphibole at about 120 degrees along double chains, and mica perfectly parallel to its sheets.

支持“小行星撞击导致恐龙灭绝”的主要证据有哪些？

What are the main lines of evidence supporting the asteroid-impact theory for dinosaur extinction?

核心证据包括：希克苏鲁伯撞击坑的年代（约6600万年前）与K-Pg界线重合；全球K-Pg界线上富含铱的薄黏土层（铱在地壳中稀少但富集于陨石）；冲击石英、玻璃陨石和撞击球粒等撞击产物。铱的全球富集量与希克苏鲁伯撞击体的陨石成分相符，而冲击石英只产生于超高速撞击，排除了单纯的火山成因。界线处花粉组合突变以及反映全球野火（烟灰层）和海啸的沉积记录，共同表明撞击引发了全球性大火、遮天蔽日与生态崩溃，形成完整的证据链。
Key evidence includes: the Chicxulub impact crater dated to about 66 million years ago, matching the K-Pg boundary; a thin iridium-rich clay layer at the global boundary (iridium is rare in the crust but abundant in meteorites); and impact products such as shocked quartz, tektites, and impact spherules. The globally estimated iridium inventory matches the meteoritic composition of the Chicxulub impactor, and shocked quartz is produced only by hypervelocity impacts, ruling out a purely volcanic origin. Abrupt pollen changes at the boundary together with soot layers recording global wildfires and deposits recording tsunamis show that the impact triggered worldwide fire, dust-induced darkness, and ecological collapse, completing a coherent chain of evidence.

铀-铅测年为什么能测定最古老矿物的年龄？

Why can uranium-lead dating determine the age of the oldest minerals?

铀-238和铀-235分别以约45亿年和7亿年的半衰期衰变成铅-206和铅-207，两组独立衰变互为校验。锆石晶体在形成时几乎不含铅，却牢固锁住铀，且极耐风化侵蚀，是理想的测年矿物。测出样品中铀铅比并代入衰变方程即可算出结晶年龄；若两组数据不一致，则提示锆石曾发生铅丢失或体系扰动。西澳大利亚杰克山发现的约44亿年锆石就是用这种方法测出的，是目前已知最古老的地球物质。
Uranium-238 and uranium-235 decay to lead-206 and lead-207 with half-lives of about 4.5 and 0.7 billion years, giving two independent clocks that cross-check each other. Zircon crystals incorporate almost no lead when they form yet firmly retain uranium and survive weathering, making them ideal for dating. Measuring the U/Pb ratios and plugging them into the decay equations yields the crystallization age; disagreement between the two systems signals lead loss or disturbance. Zircons from Jack Hills, Western Australia, dated this way at about 4.4 billion years, are among the oldest known Earth materials.

月球高地的斜长岩与月海的玄武岩分别记录了月球早期演化的哪些阶段？

What stages of early lunar evolution do the highland anorthosites and mare basalts record, respectively?

斜长岩高地记录月球岩浆洋阶段：约44亿年前月球外部大部分熔融，密度较小的斜长石浮到表面结晶成白色高地，暗色矿物沉入月幔。其后内部放射性生热引发部分熔融，基性岩浆沿撞击盆地裂隙涌出，填充低洼处形成玄武岩月海，其绝对年龄（约39–31亿年前）与撞击盆地的形成时间吻合，为内太阳系晚大轰炸假说提供了时间锚点。两类岩石在成分与分布上的差异是月球岩浆洋假说的直接证据，并由阿波罗与嫦娥任务返回的样本得到验证。
Highland anorthosites record the lunar magma-ocean stage: about 4.4 billion years ago much of the Moon's exterior was molten, and less-dense plagioclase floated to the surface, crystallizing the bright highlands while dense minerals sank into the mantle. Later radiogenic heating caused partial melting; mafic magma erupted along impact-basin fractures and filled lowlands as mare basalts, whose absolute ages (about 3.1–3.9 billion years) match the formation of the basins they fill, anchoring the inner solar system's late heavy bombardment hypothesis. The compositional and distributional differences between the two rock types are direct evidence for the lunar magma-ocean hypothesis, confirmed by samples returned by Apollo and Chang'e missions.

为什么火星失去了大部分大气？这与它的内部演化有什么关系？

Why did Mars lose most of its atmosphere, and how does this relate to its internal evolution?

火星早期就失去了全球偶极磁场，推测约40亿年前其发电机停转，原因是内核冷却、对流减弱。失去磁屏蔽后，太阳风和高能粒子直接剥离大气（主要是离子逃逸），带走大部分CO₂和水；MAVEN任务证实这一过程至今仍在进行。氩同位素测量显示火星大气中⁴⁰Ar丰度远低于地幔脱气应产生的量，说明早期大气大部分已经逃逸。大气变薄使液态水无法稳定存在，火星最终演变成寒冷荒漠，其气候演化与内部动力学深度耦合。
Mars lost its global dipole field early—the dynamo is thought to have stalled about 4 billion years ago as its core cooled and convection weakened. Without the magnetic shield, the solar wind and energetic particles stripped the atmosphere (mainly ion escape), carrying away most CO₂ and water; the MAVEN mission confirms the process continues today. Argon-isotope measurements show far less ⁴⁰Ar in the Martian atmosphere than mantle degassing should have produced, indicating that most of the early atmosphere is already gone. The thinning atmosphere made liquid water unstable, turning Mars into a cold desert, with its climate evolution tightly coupled to its internal dynamics.

What is the basic principle of radiometric dating?

Radiometric dating uses the fact that certain isotopes, such as uranium-238, decay to daughter isotopes like lead-206 at a fixed half-life. By measuring the parent-to-daughter ratio and applying the known decay constant, scientists calculate how long ago the sample formed.

蓝晶石、红柱石和矽线石的化学成分相同（Al₂SiO₅），为什么它们出现在不同变质级别的岩石中？

Kyanite, andalusite, and sillimanite share the same composition (Al₂SiO₅), yet occur in rocks of different metamorphic grade. Why?

它们是同质多象变体（多形），各自只在特定的温度—压力区间内稳定：蓝晶石是低温高压相，红柱石是低压相，矽线石是高温相。三种矿物的稳定域在温压图上交汇于一个三重点，因此它们被用作变质指示矿物：地质学家根据岩石中出现的是蓝晶石、红柱石还是矽线石，即可推断岩石经历的温压条件。例如巴罗式区域变质带中，从低绿片岩相到角闪岩相依次出现绿泥石、黑云母、蓝晶石，最后出现矽线石，指示变质级别逐步升高。
They are polymorphs, each stable only within a specific pressure–temperature field: kyanite is the high-pressure low-temperature phase, andalusite the low-pressure phase, and sillimanite the high-temperature phase. Their stability fields meet at a triple point on the P-T diagram, which is why they serve as metamorphic index minerals: identifying whether kyanite, andalusite, or sillimanite occurs lets geologists infer the P-T conditions the rock experienced. In Barrovian metamorphic zones, for example, the sequence chlorite→biotite→kyanite→sillimanite marks steadily rising metamorphic grade.

什么是自由空气重力异常和布格重力异常？它们分别揭示什么？

What are free-air and Bouguer gravity anomalies, and what does each reveal?

自由空气异常是实测重力与理想椭球体参考值之差且不扣除地形质量，主要反映地形与深部质量的综合影响，如山区出现正异常可能暗示深部质量补偿不足；布格异常先扣除地形质量，因而主要反映地下密度的横向变化——沉积盆地下方呈负异常，致密基岩和俯冲板片呈正异常。解释时需两者配合：在均衡补偿良好的地区，山区自由空气异常近于零，而布格异常显著为负（地壳厚根）。因此布格异常直接用于刻画莫霍面起伏、盆地充填与俯冲板片等深部结构，这一方法广泛用于找矿和地壳结构研究。
Free-air anomalies are observed gravity minus the ellipsoid reference without terrain correction, capturing the combined effect of topography and deep mass—positive values in mountains may indicate incomplete isostatic compensation; Bouguer anomalies subtract the terrain mass first, so they mainly image lateral density variations—sedimentary basins show negative anomalies, dense basement and subducted slabs positive ones. The two must be interpreted together: where isostatic compensation is complete, mountain free-air anomalies are near zero while Bouguer anomalies are strongly negative over the crustal root. Bouguer anomalies therefore map Moho depth, basin fill, and subducted slabs directly, and both are widely used in mineral exploration and crustal studies.

为什么热带土壤常呈红色且富含铁铝氧化物？

Why are tropical soils commonly red and rich in iron and aluminum oxides?

高温多雨条件下硅酸盐矿物被强烈水解，盐基离子（K、Na、Ca、Mg）和硅被大量淋失，难溶的铁铝氧化物（赤铁矿、三水铝石）相对富集，赤铁矿使土壤呈红色。这类高度风化的砖红壤因盐基被淋失而呈酸性、肥力较低，植被主要依赖表层的有机质与养分循环。深部的铁铝氧化物还能吸附固定磷和重金属，影响养分有效性，同时这些土壤封存着大量稳定的有机质，是理解热带碳库与成土过程的关键。
Under high temperature and rainfall, silicate minerals hydrolyze intensely; base cations (K, Na, Ca, Mg) and silica are largely leached away, while insoluble iron and aluminum oxides (hematite, gibbsite) concentrate, hematite giving the soil its red color. These highly weathered lateritic soils are acidic and low in fertility because bases have been stripped out, so vegetation depends mainly on surface organic matter and nutrient cycling. Deep iron-aluminum oxides also adsorb and fix phosphorus and heavy metals, controlling nutrient availability, while these soils store large amounts of stabilized organic matter—key to understanding tropical carbon pools and pedogenesis.

为什么液态石油主要生成并保存在大约60–120 °C的“生油窗”内？

Why is liquid oil generated and preserved mainly inside the roughly 60–120 °C oil window?

烃源岩中的干酪根靠温度驱动的热裂解生成油气，反应速率按阿伦尼乌斯定律随温度指数增长：低于约60 °C时，地质时间尺度内降解太慢，几乎不生油；高于约150 °C时，已生成的液态烃会进一步裂解为湿气与干气并残留碳质残渣。只有中间这段温压条件既能大量产出液态烃又不致将其破坏，故称生油窗，对应的埋深大致为一两千米到四五千米。
Oil forms as kerogen in source rocks cracks under heat, with reaction rates rising exponentially as temperature climbs. Below roughly 60 °C the kerogen degrades too slowly over geologic time to yield much oil; above about 150 °C liquid hydrocarbons already formed crack onward into wet and dry gas, leaving carbonaceous residue. Only this intermediate temperature range both produces abundant liquids and preserves them—the oil window, reached at depths of roughly one to five kilometers.

地下石油并不是现成的“油湖”，它要经历哪些关键环节才能最终聚集成可供开采的油气藏？

Crude oil underground is not some ready-made subterranean lake—through which key stages does it finally gather into a producible oil and gas reservoir?

富含有机质的细粒沉积物深埋后，其中干酪根在约60–120℃的生油窗内热裂解生成石油与天然气；压实作用和烃类自身的浮力将其排出烃源岩，沿多孔渗透的储层或输导层向上倾方向运移，遇连续致密封盖层受阻后，在背斜、断层遮挡或地层尖灭等圈闭中聚集成藏。烃源岩、储层、盖层、圈闭与适时的运移配置，共同构成完整的含油气系统。
Once organic-rich fine sediments are buried deeply enough, their kerogen cracks thermally into oil and gas inside the roughly 60–120 °C oil window. Compaction and the buoyancy of the hydrocarbons themselves expel them from the source rock, and they migrate updip along porous, permeable carrier beds until a continuous tight seal stops them, letting them pool in anticlinal, fault-blocked, or stratigraphic pinch-out traps. Source rock, reservoir, seal, trap, and well-timed migration together constitute a complete petroleum system.
