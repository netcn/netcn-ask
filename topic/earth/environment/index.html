为什么绿洲大多出现在沙漠边缘、大山大脚下？

Why do oases mostly lie at desert edges, at the foot of big mountains?

山上的冰雪融水和山区降雨形成河流，出山口进入平原后下渗或溢出，正好在沙漠边养出一片片绿洲。
Mountain snowmelt and rainfall form rivers that emerge at the piedmont and seep or surface at the desert edge, nurturing oases there.

什么是“咸潮”，为什么枯水期海水会倒灌进河里？

What is “salt tide” and why does seawater intrude up rivers in dry season?

枯水期河流水位低、推力弱，涨潮时海水沿河口上溯，把河口的水变咸，会影响自来水取水。
In dry season the river is low and weak, so at high tide seawater pushes upstream and makes the estuary salty, troubling water intakes.

#咸潮 #倒灌

海平面上升会先淹没哪些地方？

Which places will sea-level rise flood first?

最先遭殃的是低洼海岸、河流三角洲（如长江、珠江口）、岛国（如马尔代夫、图瓦卢）和孟加拉国这样的平坦低地。
First hit are low coasts, river deltas (e.g. Yangtze and Pearl River mouths), island nations (e.g. Maldives, Tuvalu) and flat lowlands like Bangladesh.

为什么城市里的雨有时反而比乡下多（雨岛效应）？

Why can a city get more rain than the countryside (rain-island effect)?

城市热岛让空气上升更猛，高楼和粗糙表面扰乱气流，加上污染微粒当凝结核，容易在城区及下风方向多下阵雨。
The urban heat island boosts rising air, buildings roughen the flow, and pollution particles act as nuclei, so cities and downwind areas get extra showers.

为什么全球变暖，北极熊受影响最大？

Why are polar bears hit hardest by global warming?

北极升温比全球平均快，海冰早早融化，北极熊捕猎海豹的“平台”变小，觅食越来越难。
The Arctic warms faster than the global average; earlier sea-ice loss shrinks the platform polar bears use to hunt seals, making food scarce.

植树造林为什么能减轻沙尘暴？

How does planting trees reduce sandstorms?

树木和草能固定土壤、降低风速，像“挡风墙”和“地毯”一样，让沙子不容易被吹起来。
Trees and grass hold the soil and slow the wind, acting like a wall and a carpet so sand is harder to lift.

荒漠化为什么会加重沙尘暴？

How does desertification worsen sandstorms?

植被被破坏、土壤变松后，大风一吹就把干土和沙卷上天；地表越光秃，能飞的沙尘就越多。
When plants vanish and soil loosens, wind lifts dry dust and sand; the barer the land, the more dust becomes airborne.

沙漠里为什么还会冒出片片绿洲？

Why do patches of oasis appear in the desert?

高山冰雪融水渗到地下，在沙漠低处冒出泉或汇聚成河，有水的地方草木生长，就成了绿洲。
Meltwater from mountains seeps underground and surfaces as springs or rivers in low spots; where water appears, plants grow—an oasis.

什么是“碳足迹”，普通人怎么减少它？

What is a “carbon footprint” and how can ordinary people shrink it?

碳足迹是你日常活动排放的温室气体总量。少开车多公交、节约用电、减少浪费，就能降低自己的碳足迹。
Your carbon footprint is the total greenhouse gas from your daily life. Less driving, saving electricity and reducing waste all shrink it.
