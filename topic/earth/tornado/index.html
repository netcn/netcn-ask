为什么美国中部被称为“龙卷 alley（走廊）”，特别多龙卷？

Why is the central US called 'Tornado Alley' with so many tornadoes?

那里是冷干的加拿大气团、暖湿的墨西哥湾气流和干热的西南气流交汇处，强烈的风切变和不稳定空气极易催生超级单体，从而频繁产生龙卷。
There, dry Canadian air, moist Gulf air, and hot dry southwestern air collide; strong wind shear and instability readily spawn supercells, making tornadoes frequent.

龙卷风是怎么从雷暴云里“拧”出来的？

How does a tornado get 'twisted' out of a thunderstorm cloud?

在超级单体风暴里，上升气流被风切变拧成旋转的涡管（中气旋），当旋转向下延伸到地面，就形成了漏斗状的龙卷。
In a supercell, updrafts are twisted by wind shear into a rotating column (mesocyclone); when that rotation stretches down to the ground, it becomes a funnel-shaped tornado.

#龙卷 #中气旋

龙卷风为什么破坏力那么吓人？

Why is a tornado's damage so frightening?

龙卷风是把空气拧成的高速旋转柱子，中心气压很低，能把屋顶掀飞、把树拔起，虽然范围小但风力极强。
A tornado is a fast-spinning column of air with very low central pressure that can rip off roofs and uproot trees; small in size but extremely violent.
