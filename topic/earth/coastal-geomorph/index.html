为什么有的海岸线在“后退”，有的却在向前“长”？

Why do some coastlines retreat while others advance?

河口泥沙多、淤积快的海岸（如黄河口）向海推进；受海浪侵蚀或海平面上升、又少泥沙补充的海岸则会后退。
Sediment-rich deltas (like the Yellow River mouth) grow seaward, while wave-eroded or sea-level-rise coasts with little sediment retreat.

#海岸 #侵蚀堆积

本该平直的沙滩上,为什么会规律地排布出一列新月形凹坑——滩角?

Along an otherwise straight sandy beach, why does a regularly spaced row of crescent-shaped hollows — beach cusps — appear?

主流解释是冲流带内的自组织正反馈:上冲水流在某处加速汇聚并掏蚀出小凹地,凹地把后续冲流导向两侧,使凹陷加深、两侧滩肩加宽,最终稳定在与波能尺度匹配的间距上;另一种假说则归因于入射波与边缘波的相互作用。野外观测显示滩角间距随冲流爬高和入射波能系统增大,风暴期形成的粗颗粒陡滩上最为发育,涌浪季节又被重塑。因此滩角被视为海滩泥沙对波候变化快速响应的直接记录。
The leading explanation is self-organized positive feedback within the swash zone: where uprush accelerates and converges it scours a small embayment, which deflects later swash toward the flanks, deepening the hollow and widening its horn until a stable spacing emerges that scales with wave energy; a competing hypothesis invokes interaction between incident waves and edge waves. Field surveys show cusp spacing growing systematically with swash run-up and incident wave energy, and cusps develop best on the coarse, steep beaches built by storms before fair-weather waves rebuild the profile. They are read as a direct record of how beach sediment responds rapidly to changing wave climate.

#滩角 #自组织地形

波浪、潮流与河流来沙三者共同作用下,海岸泥沙如何重新分配,进而塑造出障壁岛—潟湖、潮汐汊道与喇叭形河口湾等不同地貌组合?

Under the joint action of waves, tidal currents, and fluvial sediment supply, how is coastal sand redistributed to build contrasting assemblages such as barrier island-lagoon systems, tidal inlets, and funnel-shaped estuaries?

波能与潮能的相对强弱决定海岸类型:浪控岸段沿岸流纵向搬运泥沙,塑造障壁岛与潟湖体系;潮控岸段则以宽阔潮坪和潮流砂脊占主导。当河流来沙不足以抵消海洋动力的再改造时,河口被淹没并展宽为漏斗状河口湾。钱塘江口与密西西比口的对比清楚展示了这两个端元之间的连续过渡谱系。
The relative strength of wave versus tidal energy sets the coastal type: on wave-dominated sectors longshore drift moves sand alongshore to form barrier islands backed by lagoons, whereas tide-dominated sectors favor broad tidal flats and tidal sand ridges. Where riverine supply cannot keep pace with marine reworking, the river mouth drowns and widens into a funnel-shaped estuary. Contrasting the Qiantang estuary with the Mississippi mouth illustrates the continuous spectrum between these two end members.

#海岸地貌 #潮汐动力学

为什么有些海岸线每年后退几米？海平面上升如何加速这一过程？

Why do some coastlines retreat several meters every year, and how does sea-level rise speed up the process?

海岸侵蚀由波浪冲击、泥沙供应不足和海平面上升三重因素叠加驱动。当海平面上升时，波浪基面抬高，原本稳定的沙滩被冲刷后无法自然补充。全球约四分之一沙质海岸在上世纪已净退缩，加勒比和西非部分地区尤为严重。防护手段从硬式海堤到基于自然的牡蛎礁修复不等。
Coastal erosion is driven jointly by wave attack, insufficient sediment supply and rising sea level. As sea level climbs, the wave base rises and nearshore water deepens, so waves strike farther inland and once-stable beaches are flushed away faster than sand can be replenished; where rivers bring less material, retreat runs to several meters a year. About a quarter of the world sandy coasts retreated net over the last century, worst around the Caribbean and West Africa. Responses range from hard seawalls to nature-based oyster reef restoration.
