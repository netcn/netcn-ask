撒哈拉沙尘跨大西洋传输的主要大气环流机制是什么？

What is the main atmospheric circulation mechanism behind Saharan dust transport across the Atlantic?

东北信风将撒哈拉粉尘向西输送，并常由非洲东风波与热带东风急流（African Easterly Jet）组织成高浓度的沙尘层。这些粉尘可跨越大西洋抵达加勒比与美洲，每年有大量粉尘参与跨洋传输。
The northeast trade winds carry Saharan dust westward, organized by African easterly waves and the African Easterly Jet into concentrated dust layers. They can cross the Atlantic to the Caribbean and the Americas, with large amounts transported overseas each year.

沙尘作为云凝结核（CCN）如何影响云的性质与降水？

How does dust acting as cloud condensation nuclei affect cloud properties and precipitation?

沙尘颗粒可作为云凝结核改变云滴数浓度与粒径分布：更多核使云滴更小更密，云反照率增高并可能抑制暖云降水（Twomey 效应）；但在深对流中也可能通过冰核作用改变降水效率，结果因条件而异。
Dust particles serve as cloud condensation nuclei, altering droplet concentration and size: more nuclei yield smaller, denser droplets, raising cloud albedo and possibly suppressing warm-rain precipitation (Twomey effect); in deep convection they may also act as ice nuclei, changing rain efficiency—outcomes vary with conditions.

什么是大气气溶胶？沙尘在其中属于哪一类？

What is an atmospheric aerosol, and into which category does dust fall?

气溶胶是悬浮在大气中的固态或液态微粒。按来源分为自然与人为两类，沙尘属于自然源矿物气溶胶，由风蚀地表松散沉积物扬起，是质量最大的自然气溶胶成分之一。
An aerosol is a suspension of solid or liquid particles in the air. By source they split into natural and anthropogenic; dust is a natural mineral aerosol lifted by wind erosion of loose sediment, among the most massive natural aerosol components.

什么是生物土壤结皮？其破坏为何会显著加剧沙尘释放？

What is a biological soil crust, and why does its destruction markedly increase dust emission?

生物土壤结皮是由苔藓、地衣与蓝藻等形成的地表覆盖，能固沙并减少风蚀。过度放牧、踩踏与车辆碾压会破坏结皮，使下层疏松粉尘直接暴露，起沙阈值降低，沙尘释放随之大幅上升。
Biological soil crusts are surface covers of mosses, lichens, and cyanobacteria that stabilize sand and reduce wind erosion. Overgrazing, trampling, and vehicle traffic destroy them, exposing loose dust and lowering the threshold for entrainment, so dust emission rises sharply.

沙尘暴中的矿物粉尘被远距离传输后，对海洋生态有何益处？

What benefit does mineral dust transported far by dust storms bring to ocean ecosystems?

沙漠粉尘含铁、磷等营养元素，跨洋沉降到贫营养海域后可促进浮游植物生长，如撒哈拉粉尘滋养了大西洋与亚马孙流域。这是陆地向海洋输送生命元素的重要通道。
Desert dust carries iron, phosphorus, and other nutrients; deposited across oceans into nutrient-poor waters it fuels phytoplankton growth, as Saharan dust nourishes the Atlantic and the Amazon. It is a key land-to-ocean conduit of life-essential elements.

亚洲沙尘（如中国北方春季沙尘暴）的主要源区在哪里？

Where are the main source regions of Asian dust, such as spring dust storms in northern China?

主要源区为蒙古高原南部、中国西北的戈壁与塔克拉玛干等干旱半干旱区。春季强冷空气大风将表层粉砂吹起，沿西风带向东传输，可影响华北、朝鲜半岛与日本，甚至越洋至北美。
The main sources are the southern Mongolian Plateau and arid Gobi and Taklamakan regions of northwest China. Spring cold-front gales lift silt that travels eastward along the westerlies, affecting North China, the Korean Peninsula, Japan, and occasionally crossing to North America.

沙尘如何同时影响地球的能量收支（冷却与加热）？

How can dust simultaneously affect Earth's energy budget through both cooling and heating?

沙尘在大气顶部反射入射阳光使地表降温，但在大气中吸收并向下再辐射长波辐射，对地表又有增温作用。其净效应取决于粉尘高度、光学厚度及下垫面反照率，区域差异显著。
Dust reflects incoming sunlight at the top of atmosphere, cooling the surface, yet absorbs and re-emits longwave radiation downward, warming it. The net effect depends on dust altitude, optical depth, and surface albedo, varying strongly by region.
