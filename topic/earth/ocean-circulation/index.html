如果北大西洋温盐环流明显减弱,西欧的气候会受到什么影响?

What would happen to Western Europe's climate if the North Atlantic thermohaline circulation weakened markedly?

温盐环流如同一条横贯大洋的传送带,把低纬海区的热量经湾流及其延伸源源不断地输往北大西洋高纬海域。环流一旦减弱,向北的热量输送随之减少,西欧冬季气温可能显著偏低,海冰也会向南扩张。新仙女木期等古气候事件表明,这类环流的骤然变化可在几十年尺度上重塑区域气候格局。
The thermohaline circulation works like a conveyor belt spanning the oceans, steadily carrying low-latitude heat northward into the high-latitude North Atlantic through the Gulf Stream and its extension. A marked weakening would cut this poleward heat transport, likely making Western European winters substantially colder and allowing sea ice to expand southward. Palaeoclimate episodes such as the Younger Dryas show that abrupt shifts of this overturning can reshape regional climate within decades.

#海洋环流 #温盐环流

大西洋经向翻转环流(AMOC)依靠什么维持运转?格陵兰冰盖融水会如何干扰它?

What keeps the Atlantic Meridional Overturning Circulation running, and how could meltwater from the Greenland Ice Sheet disturb it?

AMOC如一条海洋传送带:湾流把温暖偏咸的海水送往北大西洋高纬,水体冷却增密后在拉布拉多海和北欧海发生深对流下沉,再沿深层向南折返,构成全球大洋输送带的核心环节。格陵兰冰盖融水与周边径流注入淡水,稀释表层海水并降低其密度,从而削弱驱动翻转的下沉支。IPCC AR6的综合评估认为,AMOC在本世纪很可能减弱,但在本世纪内完全崩溃的可能性不大。
The AMOC works like an ocean conveyor: the Gulf Stream carries warm, salty water northward, where cooling raises its density enough to drive deep convection in the Labrador and Nordic Seas, and the dense water returns southward at depth as the core limb of the global overturning. Meltwater runoff from Greenland freshens the surface North Atlantic, lowering its density and weakening the sinking branch that powers the overturning. The IPCC AR6 assessment concludes the AMOC is very likely to weaken over the 21st century, though a complete collapse within the century remains unlikely.

#热盐环流 #北大西洋深水形成

冬季的西欧远比同纬度的北美东岸温暖,这真的全是墨西哥湾流的功劳吗?

Winters in western Europe are much milder than on the east coast of North America at the same latitudes — is that really all down to the Gulf Stream?

湾流连同其延伸的北大西洋暖流向高纬输送了大量热量,但定量研究表明这只是部分原因。更关键的因子是常年盛行的偏西风,它们把海洋释放的热量与水汽源源送上欧洲大陆;北美一侧则因落基山地形使冷空气更容易倾泻到东岸。所以欧洲冬季温和是洋流、大气环流与地形共同作用的结果,而非湾流单独所为。
The Gulf Stream together with its North Atlantic extension carries a great deal of heat poleward, but quantitative studies show this accounts for only part of the difference. The decisive factor is the prevailing westerly winds, which deliver the heat and moisture released by the ocean onto the European continent, while on the American side the Rocky Mountains help cold air spill readily onto the eastern seaboard. Europe's mild winter thus reflects ocean currents, atmospheric circulation, and topography acting together.
