台风为什么常常绕开广东、直奔福建浙江？

Why do typhoons often bypass Guangdong and head for Fujian–Zhejiang?

这和副高位置、引导气流以及台湾岛地形有关：当副高呈东西向摆布，台风沿其南缘西北行，常登陆浙闽；台湾中央山脉也会让路径偏折。
It depends on the subtropical high and steering flow plus Taiwan's terrain; when the high bridges east-west, storms ride its southern edge NW toward Zhejiang–Fujian, and Taiwan's mountains deflect them.

台风中心“台风眼”为什么反而风平浪静？

Why is the typhoon eye calm and clear?

台风眼是风暴中心下沉气流的区域，周围狂风暴雨绕圈跑，唯独中间空气下沉、云散风小，像风暴里的“安静小屋”。
The eye is the center where air sinks; the violent winds rotate around it, leaving the middle strangely calm and clear.

台风一登陆为什么很快就变弱了？

Why does a typhoon weaken quickly after landfall?

台风靠温暖海面“吃饭”，登陆后断了水汽和热量来源，又受地形摩擦，旋转力量迅速减小，最后散架成普通雨带。
Typhoons feed on warm seas; on land they lose heat and moisture and meet friction, so they spin down into an ordinary rain band.

台风为什么总在夏秋季节出现？

Why do typhoons mostly appear in late summer and autumn?

台风需要又暖又湿的洋面提供能量，夏秋西北太平洋海温最高，水蒸气充足，最容易“生”出台风。
Typhoons need warm, moist ocean energy; in late summer-autumn the NW Pacific is warmest with plenty of vapor, ideal for formation.
