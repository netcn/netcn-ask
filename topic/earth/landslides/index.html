什么是冻土滑坡蠕变？

What is frozen-slope creep?

冻土坡上的蠕变是重力与冻融的慢账：暖季活动层向下融化，下面仍是不透水的冻层，融水排不走，表层泡成软泥，抗剪强度大跌，土体便沿冻融界面顺坡缓流。冬季冰晶顶起颗粒，化冻后垂直回落，每次都把位移往坡下偏一点。它一年只走毫米到厘米，与有滑面、瞬时失稳的滑坡相反，线索是歪斜的醉汉林与弯弓的树根；活动层过湿时蠕移会升级成滑塌。
Creep on a frozen slope is a slow account kept by gravity and frost together. In the warm season the active layer thaws downward while the ground beneath stays frozen and impermeable, so meltwater cannot drain; the surface turns to soft mud whose shear strength collapses, and the layer flows downhill along that frozen interface. Winter ice crystals lift the particles and thaw drops them straight back down, each cycle nudging the displacement a little further downslope. It moves only millimeters to centimeters a year, the opposite of a landslide, which has a slip surface and fails at an instant; its tells are drunken forests of tilted trunks and roots bent into bows. Let the active layer get too wet and creep escalates into a real slide.

什么是滑坡？

What is a landslide?

山坡上的土石沿着一个滑动面整体向下滑，就叫滑坡，常由暴雨或地震触发。
A landslide is rock and soil sliding downslope on a slip surface, often after rain or quakes.

泥石流是什么？

What is a debris flow?

泥石流是暴雨或融雪把山坡上的土石、泥沙和水混成一股洪流,迅猛冲下山谷,破坏力很强。
A debris flow is a fast river of mud, rock and water set loose by heavy rain or melt, rushing down valleys with great force.
