海洋混合层的深度由什么控制？为什么它对气候很重要？

What controls the depth of the oceanic mixed layer and why does it matter for climate?

混合层深度由风搅拌、浮力通量（冷却与蒸发使表层变重）和海浪破碎共同决定，季节上冬季加深、夏季变浅。它调控海气间热量与碳的交换速率，是气候模式中的关键变量。
Mixed-layer depth is controlled by wind stirring, buoyancy flux (cooling and evaporation that make surface water heavier), and wave breaking, deepening in winter and shoaling in summer. It regulates the rate of heat and carbon exchange between ocean and atmosphere, a key variable in climate models.

什么是海洋锋面？

What is an ocean front?

海洋锋面是两种性质不同的水体交汇、存在明显温盐梯度的狭窄过渡带。它常伴随强烈的垂直运动和丰富的生物聚集，是渔场和天气系统活跃的地带。海湾流与 Labrador 寒流交汇处就是著名例证。
An ocean front is a narrow transition zone where two water masses of different properties meet, showing a sharp temperature and salinity gradient. It often brings strong vertical motion and rich biological aggregation, making productive fishing grounds and active weather zones. The meeting of the Gulf Stream and the Labrador Current is a classic example.

温盐环流存在中断的风险吗？

Is there a risk that the thermohaline circulation could shut down?

气候模拟显示，若全球变暖使格陵兰冰盖大量融化，淡水注入北大西洋会降低海水盐度和密度，削弱下沉驱动力。这可能影响温盐环流甚至使其局部减缓，进而改变欧洲气候和全球热量分配。不过目前主流观点认为短期内完全中断的可能性较低。
Climate models suggest that if global warming melts the Greenland ice sheet massively, freshwater input to the North Atlantic would lower salinity and density and weaken the sinking drive. This could slow the thermohaline circulation and alter European climate and global heat distribution. Most studies, however, see a complete shutdown in the near term as unlikely.

厄尔尼诺如何影响西北太平洋台风的生成频率？

How does El Nino affect the frequency of typhoons in the western North Pacific?

厄尔尼诺年西太平洋暖池东移，使对流中心偏离西北太平洋，该海域的生成频数常略减但路径更偏东、影响范围变化。同时垂直风切变和海温分布改变，使个别台风更强。研究仍在细化这种年际关系。
In El Nino years the western Pacific warm pool shifts east, moving the convection center away from the western North Pacific, so genesis frequency there often dips slightly while tracks shift eastward. Changed vertical wind shear and sea temperature patterns can also make individual storms stronger. Research continues to refine this year-to-year relationship.

验潮站是如何测量海平面的？

How do tide gauges measure sea level?

验潮站通过固定在岸边的标尺、浮筒或压力传感器持续记录相对于陆地的海平面高度。现代验潮站常与 GPS 联用，以扣除陆地升降的影响。长期序列还能揭示海平面趋势、潮汐和风暴增水的变化。
Tide gauges measure sea level relative to the land using fixed staffs, floats, or pressure sensors that record water height continuously. Modern gauges are often combined with GPS to remove the effect of land uplift or subsidence. Long records also reveal trends as well as changes in tides and storm surges.

为什么海洋中的微生物不是食物链的“终点”，而是把溶解有机物送回食物网的关键？

Why are marine microbes not a "dead end" in the food chain but rather the key that returns dissolved organic matter to the food web?

浮游植物分泌大量溶解有机碳（DOC），传统观点认为它被细菌利用后就短路了；而微生物环机制表明，异养细菌摄食DOC后自身又被微型浮游动物摄食，把碳和营养重新送回主食物链。微型浮游动物对细菌的摄食是这一环节的核心，它使细菌固定的碳进入浮游动物→鱼类的能量流；病毒裂解细菌与浮游植物时还会再次释放DOC，进一步驱动循环。这一环节还影响碳向深海的输出效率，因为细菌呼吸使部分碳滞留在表层水中，减少了可沉降到深海的有机碳比例。
Phytoplankton exude large amounts of dissolved organic carbon (DOC); the classic view held that its uptake by bacteria short-circuited the chain. The microbial loop, however, shows that heterotrophic bacteria are themselves grazed by microzooplankton, returning carbon and nutrients to the main food web; grazing by microzooplankton is the core step that funnels bacterially fixed carbon into the zooplankton→fish energy flow, and viral lysis of bacteria and phytoplankton releases more DOC to drive the loop. The loop also modulates carbon export efficiency, because bacterial respiration keeps some carbon in surface waters, reducing the fraction of organic carbon that can sink to depth.

为什么向海洋投放微量铁会促进浮游植物生长？作为气候工程手段它为何存在争议？

Why does adding trace iron to the ocean stimulate phytoplankton growth, and why is it controversial as a climate-engineering approach?

在南大洋等高营养低叶绿素（HNLC）海区，表层营养盐充足但缺铁，浮游植物无法充分利用；铁是光合作用与固氮酶系的关键辅因子，补铁可大幅提高初级生产力，IronEx II和SOFeX等试验均观测到叶绿素爆发。争议在于：碳沉降的净量与长期固碳效果难以准确评估，试验显示大部分新固定的碳在表层再矿化，向深海输出的比例远低于预期。还可能引发物种组成改变、有毒藻类爆发、深水缺氧等生态后果，而且公海投放难以监管，故未被接受为气候工程手段。
In high-nutrient, low-chlorophyll (HNLC) regions such as the Southern Ocean, surface nutrients are abundant but iron is scarce, so phytoplankton cannot use them; iron is a key cofactor in photosynthesis and nitrogen-fixation enzyme systems, and iron addition dramatically raises primary production—experiments such as IronEx II and SOFeX observed chlorophyll blooms. Controversy stems from the difficulty of quantifying net carbon drawdown and long-term sequestration: experiments show much of the newly fixed carbon is remineralized in the surface layer, with far less exported to depth than hoped. Possible ecological consequences (shifted species composition, toxic blooms, deep-water hypoxia) and weak regulatory oversight over open-ocean releases mean iron fertilization is not accepted as a climate-engineering tool.

南极绕极流为什么独一无二？

Why is the Antarctic Circumpolar Current unique?

南极绕极流是地球上唯一环绕整个大陆、没有陆地阻挡的洋流。它沿南极洲外围自西向东流动，连接了大西洋、太平洋和印度洋。它把三大洋深层水混合，并对全球热量和碳的分配起关键作用。
The Antarctic Circumpolar Current is the only current on Earth that flows all the way around a continent without being blocked by land. It circulates eastward around Antarctica and connects the Atlantic, Pacific, and Indian Oceans. By mixing the deep waters of the three oceans it plays a key role in global heat and carbon distribution.

疯狗浪（异常巨浪）是什么？

What is a rogue wave (freak wave)?

疯狗浪指波高明显超过周围波浪、出现概率远低于常规统计预期的孤立巨浪。它可由多个波列叠加、海流与地形作用或风暴中的非线性效应产生。历史上曾造成船只突然断裂，因此是航海安全的重要研究课题。
A rogue wave is an isolated giant wave whose height greatly exceeds the surrounding waves and whose occurrence is far rarer than conventional statistics predict. It can arise from the superposition of several wave trains, the effect of currents and topography, or nonlinear processes during storms. It has historically caused ships to break apart suddenly and is an important topic in maritime safety.

什么是温盐环流（大洋传送带）？

What is the thermohaline circulation (the ocean conveyor belt)?

温盐环流是由海水的温度和盐度差异驱动的深海环流，常被称为大洋传送带。在高纬度海域，冷水因密度大而下沉，再沿深层缓慢流向低纬度并上升。它把热量和碳在全球海洋中循环，对长期气候有重要影响。
The thermohaline circulation is a deep-ocean current driven by differences in seawater temperature and salinity, often called the ocean conveyor belt. In high-latitude regions dense cold water sinks and slowly flows toward lower latitudes at depth before rising again. It cycles heat and carbon through the global ocean and has a major influence on long-term climate.

海洋酸化具体是怎么回事？为什么伤害贝类与珊瑚？

What exactly is ocean acidification, and why does it hurt shellfish and corals?

大气二氧化碳约三成被海洋吸收，溶解后生成碳酸，电离出氢离子使海水pH下降，同时碳酸根浓度降低，这一过程叫海洋酸化。造壳生物和珊瑚靠碳酸钙构筑外壳与骨骼，碳酸根减少使钙化难度增大、甚至开始溶解；实验室与野外研究都显示高CO2环境下的钙化速率下降。工业革命以来海水pH已下降约0.1个单位，相当于酸度升高约30%，对未来海洋生态系统构成长期压力。
The ocean absorbs about a third of human CO2 emissions; the gas dissolves and forms carbonic acid, which releases hydrogen ions and lowers pH while consuming carbonate ions—that is ocean acidification. Shell-forming organisms and corals build their shells and skeletons from calcium carbonate; with fewer carbonate ions, calcification becomes harder and existing structures may even dissolve. Lab and field studies both show slower calcification under high CO2. Since the Industrial Revolution, surface pH has fallen by about 0.1 units, roughly a 30% rise in acidity, placing long-term stress on marine ecosystems.

什么是厄尔尼诺南方涛动（ENSO）？

What is the El Nino-Southern Oscillation (ENSO)?

ENSO是热带太平洋海气耦合系统中最重要的年际气候信号，包含暖位的厄尔尼诺和冷位的拉尼娜。南方涛动指赤道太平洋东西两侧海平面气压的反向跷跷板变化。ENSO通过遥相关影响全球许多地区的降水和温度。
ENSO is the most important year-to-year climate signal in the tropical Pacific ocean-atmosphere coupled system, comprising the warm El Nino phase and the cold La Nina phase. The Southern Oscillation refers to the seesaw in sea-level air pressure between the eastern and western equatorial Pacific. Through teleconnections ENSO influences rainfall and temperature in many regions worldwide.

什么是海洋分层与温跃层？

What is ocean stratification and the thermocline?

海洋因温度、盐度差异在垂直方向上分成表层混合层、温跃层和深层水，称为海洋分层。温跃层是温度随深度急剧下降的薄层，像盖子一样阻碍上下层交换。分层强弱影响营养盐供给和碳的垂直输送。
The ocean is vertically divided by temperature and salinity into a surface mixed layer, a thermocline, and deep water, a structure called stratification. The thermocline is a thin layer where temperature drops sharply with depth and acts like a lid limiting exchange between layers. Stratification strength affects nutrient supply and vertical carbon transport.

Why do corals bleach, and can bleached corals recover?

Zooxanthellae inside coral tissues supply most of the coral's energy through photosynthesis and give corals their vivid colors; when seawater stays unusually warm (about 1 C above the summer average for weeks) or under pollution and acidification, the algae are expelled or die and the white skeleton shows through—bleaching. If the stress ends quickly, corals can take up new zooxanthellae and recover; if it persists, they starve. Global marine heatwaves have repeatedly caused mass bleaching in recent years.

什么是海洋热膨胀？

What is ocean thermal expansion?

海洋热膨胀指海水温度升高时体积增大、密度减小的现象。由于海洋吸收了大部分多余的地球热量，热膨胀已成为海平面上升的主要贡献者之一。它使相同质量的水占据更大体积，从而抬高海平面。
Ocean thermal expansion is the increase in seawater volume and decrease in density as its temperature rises. Because the ocean has absorbed most of the excess heat on Earth, thermal expansion has become one of the main contributors to sea level rise. It lets the same mass of water occupy a larger volume and thus raises sea level.

赤潮是怎么发生的，为什么会毒到海鲜？

How do red tides form, and why can they poison seafood?

当海水富营养化又遇到温暖稳定的天气，某些藻类会爆发性繁殖形成赤潮；部分藻类释放毒素，被贝类富集后就可能毒害吃海鲜的人。
When nutrient-rich, warm, calm water lets some algae explode in number, a red tide forms; certain algae release toxins that accumulate in shellfish and can poison people who eat them.

什么是墨西哥湾流？

What is the Gulf Stream?

墨西哥湾流是北大西洋一条强大的暖流，从墨西哥湾沿北美东岸流向北大西洋。它把热带的热量输送到高纬度地区，显著影响北欧的气候。它是全球大洋环流的重要组成部分。
The Gulf Stream is a powerful warm current in the North Atlantic that flows from the Gulf of Mexico along the east coast of North America into the North Atlantic. It carries tropical heat toward high latitudes and strongly influences the climate of Northern Europe. It is a major component of the global ocean circulation.

海水为什么是咸的，什么是盐度？

Why is seawater salty and what is salinity?

海水中的盐分主要来自陆地岩石风化后被河流带入海洋，以及海底热液活动。盐度指每千克海水中溶解盐类的总克数，全球平均约35。海水蒸发只带走淡水，盐分长期积累，使海洋保持咸味。
The salt in seawater mainly comes from rock weathering on land carried by rivers and from seafloor hydrothermal activity. Salinity is the total grams of dissolved salts per kilogram of seawater, averaging about 35 globally. Evaporation removes only fresh water, so salt accumulates and the ocean stays salty.

北大西洋暖流对欧洲气候有什么影响？

How does the North Atlantic Current affect Europe's climate?

北大西洋暖流把墨西哥湾流带来的暖水继续输往欧洲西北岸。它使同纬度的西北欧比加拿大东部温暖得多，冬季港口不易结冰。没有它，欧洲气候会接近严寒的大陆性气候。
The North Atlantic Current carries the warm water of the Gulf Stream onward to the northwestern shores of Europe. It makes northwestern Europe much warmer than eastern Canada at the same latitude, and keeps its winter ports largely ice-free. Without it, Europe's climate would be far closer to a harsh continental one.

海冰和冰山有什么区别？

What is the difference between sea ice and an iceberg?

海冰是海水直接在低温下冻结形成的冰，漂浮在海面并随季节消长。冰山则是陆地冰川或冰盖断裂后滑入海中漂浮的淡水冰块。冰山体积可非常巨大，是航海中的潜在危险。
Sea ice forms by the direct freezing of seawater and floats on the surface, growing and melting with the seasons. An iceberg is a chunk of fresh water broken off a land glacier or ice sheet that floats in the ocean. Icebergs can be enormous and pose a hazard to navigation.

什么是潮汐？它主要由什么引起？

What are tides and what mainly causes them?

潮汐是海面周期性的升降，主要由月球引力对地球两侧海水的拉伸造成，太阳引力也有贡献。农历初一、十五的大潮便是日月引力叠加的结果。
Tides are the regular rise and fall of the sea surface, caused mainly by the Moon's gravitational pull stretching Earth's oceans, with the Sun adding a smaller effect. Spring tides near new and full moon occur when the Sun and Moon align and reinforce each other.

海平面上升对沿海城市有什么影响？

How does sea level rise affect coastal cities?

海平面上升会加剧风暴潮和海岸侵蚀，使低洼城区更易被淹没。它还会让咸水侵入地下淡水和农田，影响饮水与农业。许多沿海大城市因此被迫投资海堤和排水系统。
Sea level rise worsens storm surges and coastal erosion, making low-lying districts more prone to flooding. It also lets salt water intrude into underground fresh water and farmland, affecting drinking water and agriculture. Many coastal megacities are therefore investing in seawalls and drainage systems.

全球海平面为什么在上升？

Why is global sea level rising?

全球海平面上升主要有两个原因：海水因变暖而膨胀，以及陆地冰川和冰盖融化把更多水注入海洋。工业化以来人类活动导致气候变暖，使这两方面都在加剧。海平面上升会威胁沿海城市和岛国。
Global sea level rise has two main causes: seawater expands as it warms, and melting land ice and ice sheets add more water to the ocean. Since industrialization, human activity has driven warming that worsens both effects. Rising seas threaten coastal cities and island nations.
