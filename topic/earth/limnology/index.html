湖水夏天明明晒得很透,为什么湖底却凉得刺骨?秋天翻个身又发生了什么?

Summer sun bakes the lake surface — why is the bottom icy, and what happens when the lake turns over in autumn?

水在4摄氏度密度最大,这一反常性质使深湖夏季自然分层:晒暖的低密度表水层(epilimnion)浮在冷重的底水层(hypolimnion)之上,中间温度骤降的斜温层像隐形盖板阻止混合,底层溶解氧只出不进,渐被分解耗尽。秋季表层变冷变重,大风一搅,密度差消失,整湖翻转:氧气送回湖底,营养盐翻上表层,奠定来春藻类格局。温带深湖每年混合一至两次;不少浅水热带湖全年频繁混合,少数深湖则近乎永久分层。
Water is densest at four degrees Celsius, and that anomaly stratifies deep lakes in summer: warm low-density epilimnion floats over cold hypolimnion, with the thermocline as an invisible lid blocking mixing, so bottom oxygen is consumed faster than replenished. Autumn cools the surface until wind erases the density contrast and the whole lake turns over, sending oxygen down and nutrients up — setting the next spring's algae stage. Deep temperate lakes mix once or twice yearly; many shallow tropical lakes mix constantly, while a few deep ones stay near-permanently stratified.

湖泊为什么会「死」?蓝绿藻一开花,鱼去哪儿了?

How does a lake die during an algae bloom — and where did the fish go?

富营养化是一部三幕剧。第一幕:农田化肥与生活污水带来过量氮磷,蓝藻等浮游植物获得无限自助餐,水面铺成绿漆。第二幕:大量藻类死亡下沉,底层微生物分解它们时消耗溶解氧,而斜温层阻止氧气补充,湖底沦为无氧区。第三幕:鱼类与底栖动物窒息逃离或死亡;更阴险的是某些蓝藻释放微囊藻毒素,直接威胁饮用水安全。治理的核心是切断营养盐源头而非杀死藻类本身——控源截污后,沉水植物重建与食物链修复需要数年到数十年,太湖与伊利湖的经验都证明这是场持久战。
Eutrophication runs in three acts. First: fertilizer and sewage deliver excess nitrogen and phosphorus; cyanobacteria feast and paint the surface green. Second: massive algal die-offs sink, microbes decomposing them strip dissolved oxygen while the thermocline blocks resupply — bottom waters go anoxic. Third: fish and benthos suffocate or flee, and some cyanobacteria add microcystin poisons threatening drinking water. The cure is cutting nutrient sources, not killing algae; restoring submerged plants and food webs takes years to decades, as Taihu and Erie taught.
