南大西洋异常是什么，为何受关注？

What is the South Atlantic Anomaly and why does it matter?

那里地磁场异常偏弱，对卫星和空间站辐射更强、易出故障，也被视为地磁可能正在重组的信号。
There the field is unusually weak, exposing satellites and stations to stronger radiation and glitches, and is seen as a sign the field may be reorganizing.

卫星测高如何监测海平面变化？

How does satellite altimetry monitor sea level?

测高卫星向海面发射微波并接收回波，精确算出卫星到海面的距离，长期记录就能看出海面是升是降。
Altimetry satellites bounce microwaves off the sea and time the return, giving precise height; long records show whether seas rise or fall.

地磁北极为什么在加速移动？

Why is the magnetic north pole accelerating?

地核外核液态铁镍流动改变，使磁极从加拿大向北西伯利亚漂移，近年速度明显加快。
Changes in the swirling iron-nickel outer core are shifting the pole from Canada toward Siberia, and recently at a faster pace.

什么是地磁极？

What is a geomagnetic pole?

地磁极是地球磁场近似像一根大磁棒时，磁感线垂直出入地表的两点，和地理南北极并不重合。
Geomagnetic poles are where Earth's field lines enter and leave the surface vertically, like a giant bar magnet, and they differ from the geographic poles.
