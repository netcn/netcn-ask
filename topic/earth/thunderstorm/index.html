闪电的电是怎么在云里分出来正负两极的？

How does lightning's positive and negative charge separate inside a cloud?

云内冰晶和霰粒（软雹）碰撞时，较轻的冰晶带上正电被上升气流带到云顶，较重的霰粒带负电沉到云底，形成上下电场，积累到足够强就放电成闪电。
Inside clouds, collisions between ice crystals and graupel (soft hail) separate charge: lighter crystals gain positive charge and rise to the top, heavier graupel gains negative and sinks, building a field that discharges as lightning.

#电荷分离 #闪电

一场雷暴要“点火”，一般得凑齐哪三样条件？

What three ingredients does a thunderstorm generally need to ignite?

要够潮湿的空气、够不稳定的大气（下面热上面冷，容易上冲），以及能把空气抬起来触发对流的力量（如地形或锋面），三者齐了就容易打雷下雨。
It needs moist air, an unstable atmosphere (warm below, cold above, so air rushes up), and a lift to trigger convection (like terrain or a front); with all three, thunderstorms form.

#雷暴 #对流条件

先看见闪电还是先听见雷，为什么？

Do we see lightning or hear thunder first, and why?

先看到光。因为光跑得比声音快得多，闪电和打雷其实同时发生，但光瞬间到达，声音要过一会儿才传来。
We see light first. Light travels far faster than sound; the flash and the boom happen together, but light arrives instantly while sound takes a moment.
