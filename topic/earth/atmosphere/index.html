What is the stratosphere and its features?

位于对流层之上约10至50公里，气温随高度上升，气流平稳，商用飞机和臭氧层都在这一层。
Above the troposphere from about 10 to 50 km, temperature rises with height and air is calm; airliners and the ozone layer are found here.

什么是对流层顶？为什么它限制了绝大多数天气的高度？

What is the tropopause and why does it cap the height of most weather?

对流层顶是对流层与平流层之间的过渡层，此处温度随高度不再下降、形成稳定逆温，抑制了空气的垂直对流。因此云和风暴基本被限制在其下方，商用飞机也多在其附近巡航以避开颠簸。
The tropopause is the boundary where the troposphere meets the stratosphere; temperature stops falling with height there, creating a stable inversion that suppresses vertical convection. Clouds and storms are thus largely confined below it, and airliners often cruise near it to avoid turbulence.

Why do jets often fly in the stratosphere above the troposphere?

极光（北极光/南极光）是怎么形成的？

How do the auroras (Northern and Southern Lights) form?

太阳风带来的带电粒子被地球磁场引导聚集到南北磁极附近，与高层大气中的氧原子和氮分子碰撞激发，以光的形式释放能量。氧常发出绿光或红光，氮则偏蓝紫色，于是形成夜空中舞动的极光。
Charged particles from the solar wind are funneled by Earth's magnetic field toward the magnetic poles, where they collide with oxygen and nitrogen in the upper atmosphere and emit light. Oxygen often glows green or red and nitrogen blue-purple, producing the dancing auroras.

什么是“温室效应”？哪些气体是主要贡献者？

What is the 'greenhouse effect,' and which gases are the main contributors?

温室气体允许太阳短波辐射进入、却阻挡地表长波（红外）辐射逃逸，使热量滞留近地面，从而温暖地球。自然温室效应让地表平均温度维持在约15°C（若无则约-18°C）；主要温室气体包括水汽、二氧化碳、甲烷和氧化亚氮。
Greenhouse gases let incoming solar shortwave radiation through but trap outgoing longwave (infrared) radiation from the surface, warming the lower atmosphere. The natural greenhouse effect keeps Earth's average surface near about 15°C (versus roughly -18°C without it); the main gases are water vapor, carbon dioxide, methane, and nitrous oxide.

什么是臭氧层？为什么它曾受氟利昂(CFCs)威胁？

What is the ozone layer and why was it threatened by CFCs?

臭氧层位于平流层，能吸收有害紫外线保护生命。氟利昂等氯氟烃释放的氯原子会催化分解臭氧分子，造成南极臭氧洞，促使1987年《蒙特利尔议定书》限制其生产。
The ozone layer in the stratosphere absorbs harmful ultraviolet radiation, protecting life. Chlorine from chlorofluorocarbons (CFCs) catalytically destroys ozone molecules, creating the Antarctic ozone hole and prompting the 1987 Montreal Protocol to phase out these chemicals.

什么是臭氧层？

What is the ozone layer?

臭氧层位于平流层中，由臭氧分子组成，能吸收大部分有害的太阳紫外线。它的损耗曾因氟利昂等人造物质而加剧，后经国际协议得到保护。
The ozone layer sits in the stratosphere and absorbs most harmful solar ultraviolet radiation. Its depletion was worsened by human-made substances like CFCs but is now recovering under international agreements.

什么是信风？

What are trade winds?

赤道两侧常年稳定吹向赤道的东风，古代帆船靠它跨洋贸易，所以叫信风。
Steady easterly winds near the equator that helped old trading ships cross oceans.

臭氧层在哪里？它对我们为什么重要？

Where is the ozone layer, and why is it important to us?

臭氧层位于平流层中（约15–35公里高），能吸收大部分有害的太阳紫外线（UV-B）。它像一把“防晒伞”保护地面生物免受晒伤、皮肤癌和生态系统损害；1987年《蒙特利尔议定书》正是为了限制消耗臭氧的物质。
The ozone layer lies in the stratosphere (about 15–35 km up) and absorbs most of the Sun's harmful ultraviolet (UV-B) radiation. It acts like a 'sunscreen' protecting life on the surface from sunburn, skin cancer, and ecosystem damage; the 1987 Montreal Protocol was created to limit ozone-depleting substances.
