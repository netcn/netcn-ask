极地涡旋是什么，它为何与北半球的寒潮有关？

What is the polar vortex, and why is it linked to cold spells in the Northern Hemisphere?

极地涡旋是冬季平流层与对流层中围绕极地的持续性冷性气旋环流，由极夜与温度梯度维持。当涡旋减弱或分裂，冷空气便会向南爆发，造成中纬度异常寒潮。沃与波利亚科夫的研究描述了涡旋突然增温与地表冷事件之间的联系。
The polar vortex is a persistent cyclonic circulation around the pole in the winter stratosphere and troposphere, sustained by polar night and temperature gradients. When it weakens or splits, cold air spills southward and produces anomalous mid-latitude cold snaps. Waugh and Polyakov described the link between sudden stratospheric warming and surface cold events.

急流是什么，它如何影响中纬度天气系统？

What is a jet stream, and how does it influence mid-latitude weather?

急流是高空狭窄的强风带，由极地与热带之间的温度梯度驱动，沿冷暖空气交界处蜿蜒。其波状路径决定冷暖气团的南北进退，并引导气旋与锋面移动。美国海洋大气管理局指出急流位置偏移会显著改变区域的干旱与降水格局。
A jet stream is a narrow, fast upper-level wind band driven by the temperature contrast between polar and tropical air, meandering along the boundary of warm and cold masses. Its wavy path governs the north-south excursions of air masses and steers cyclones and fronts. NOAA notes that shifts in the jet's position markedly change regional drought and precipitation patterns.

哈得来环流如何把热量从赤道向副热带输送？

How does the Hadley cell transport heat from the equator toward the subtropics?

低纬空气在赤道受热上升，在高空向两极流动并在副热带下沉，形成闭合的经向环流。下沉气流造成副热带高压与沙漠带，而上升支带来多雨的赤道辐合带。霍尔顿在《动力气象学导论》中给出这一环流的定量描述。
Air near the equator is heated and rises, then flows poleward aloft and sinks around the subtropics, closing a meridional circulation. The sinking branch produces subtropical high pressure and desert belts, while the rising branch feeds the rainy intertropical convergence zone. Holton's An Introduction to Dynamic Meteorology gives a quantitative account of this cell.
