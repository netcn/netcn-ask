为什么火山附近的土壤特别肥沃？

Why is soil near volcanoes especially fertile?

火山灰和熔岩风化成富含钾、磷等矿物的土壤，疏松透气又养分足，所以火山脚下常是良田。
Volcanic ash and lava weather into loose soil rich in potassium, phosphorus and more, making fertile farmland on volcanic flanks.

为什么会有温泉和火山？

Why are there hot springs and volcanoes?

地球内部很热：地下水沿地壳薄弱带被加热上涌形成温泉，岩浆被挤到地表则形成火山。
Earth's interior is very hot: where groundwater is heated and rises along weak crustal zones it forms hot springs, and where magma is pushed to the surface it forms volcanoes.
