同位素氧-18与氧-16的比值如何被用来定量重建古温度？

How is the ratio of oxygen-18 to oxygen-16 used to quantitatively reconstruct past temperatures?

海水蒸发时较轻的氧-16优先进入大气，导致冰期时海洋相对富集氧-18；有孔虫等海洋生物壳体的氧-18/氧-16比值因此记录下温度与冰量信号。通过校准的氧同位素—温度关系，可将壳体比值转化为古海水温度或大陆冰量指标。
During evaporation lighter oxygen-16 preferentially enters the atmosphere, enriching the ocean in oxygen-18 during glacials; the oxygen-18/oxygen-16 ratio in foraminifera shells therefore records both temperature and ice volume. Calibrated isotope-temperature relations convert shell ratios into paleo-sea-temperature or continental-ice-volume proxies.

末次冰期内的快速气候事件（如Dansgaard-Oeschger事件）有什么特征？

What characterizes the abrupt climate events of the last ice age, such as Dansgaard-Oeschger events?

Dansgaard-Oeschger事件表现为北大西洋地区在数十年内气温骤升8至16℃、随后缓慢回落的准周期性摆动，与海洋环流（特别是大西洋经向翻转环流）的减弱和恢复相关。它们揭示气候系统存在阈值式、非线性的快速响应。
Dansgaard-Oeschger events show quasi-periodic jumps of 8 to 16°C in North Atlantic temperatures within decades followed by slow cooling, linked to weakening and recovery of ocean circulation, especially the Atlantic Meridional Overturning Circulation. They reveal threshold-like, nonlinear, abrupt climate responses.

米兰科维奇轨道参数变化如何驱动冰期-间冰期旋回？

How do Milankovitch orbital variations drive glacial-interglacial cycles?

地球轨道的偏心率（约10万年）、地轴倾角（约4.1万年）和岁差（约2.3万年）周期性改变各纬度的夏季日照量，进而调制北半球冰盖的消长。这些轨道强迫与冰芯和海洋沉积中的同位素记录高度吻合，是第四纪冰期旋回的主控因素。
Periodic changes in Earth's orbital eccentricity (about 100 kyr), axial tilt (about 41 kyr), and precession (about 23 kyr) alter summer insolation by latitude and thereby modulate Northern Hemisphere ice sheets. These orbital forcings match isotopic records in ice and ocean sediments and are the main driver of Quaternary glacial cycles.

地质记录能告诉我们关于厄尔尼诺-南方涛动（ENSO）的哪些历史变化？

What historical changes in ENSO can geologic records reveal?

珊瑚、树木年轮和湖泊沉积中的氧同位素与元素比值保留了ENSO的季节性信号，显示其强度与频率在千年尺度上并非恒定，例如中全新世与中世纪暖期曾出现不同于今的ENSO态。这些记录帮助检验气候模式对ENSO的模拟能力。
Oxygen-isotope and elemental ratios in corals, tree rings, and lake sediments preserve ENSO's seasonal signal, showing its intensity and frequency varied over millennia, e.g., distinct ENSO states in the mid-Holocene and Medieval Warm Period. Such records test climate models' ability to simulate ENSO.

科学家如何确定深冰芯中每一层的大致年代？

How do scientists estimate the age of layers in a deep ice core?

冰芯定年结合年度层计数（浅层可见季节纹层）、火山灰层等已知事件标志，以及基于冰流模型的年龄—深度尺度。深层常用气体与冰的「年龄差」校正，因气泡封闭滞后于降雪堆积。
Ice-core dating combines annual layer counting (visible seasonal bands in shallow ice), markers such as volcanic ash horizons, and ice-flow-model age-depth scales. In deep ice, a gas-age versus ice-age offset is corrected because bubbles close off after snow accumulates.

末次盛冰期地球的气候与今天有何显著不同？

How did Earth's climate during the Last Glacial Maximum differ markedly from today?

约2.1万年前的末次盛冰期，全球气温比今低约4至6℃，大规模冰盖覆盖北美与北欧，海平面比今低约120米，盛行风与干旱带位置明显不同。这一状态由冰芯、海洋与湖泊沉积物等多指标重建。
At the Last Glacial Maximum about 21,000 years ago, global temperatures were roughly 4 to 6°C lower, massive ice sheets covered North America and northern Europe, sea level was about 120 m lower, and prevailing winds and dry belts sat in different positions. This state is reconstructed from ice cores, marine, and lake sediments.

冰芯中的气泡如何帮助重建古代大气二氧化碳浓度？

How do bubbles in ice cores help reconstruct ancient atmospheric CO2 concentrations?

降雪压实成冰时封存了当时的大气气泡，钻取冰芯后可在洁净实验室中释放气泡并测定其气体成分，从而直接得到过去80万年的大气CO2记录。南极冰芯显示冰期CO2约180 ppm、间冰期约280 ppm。
As snow compacts into ice it traps tiny bubbles of the contemporaneous atmosphere; ice cores are drilled and their gases extracted in clean labs to measure composition, giving a direct record of atmospheric CO2 over the past 800,000 years. Antarctic cores show roughly 180 ppm during glacials and 280 ppm during interglacials.

冰芯中的甲烷浓度记录揭示了冰期气候的什么规律？

What pattern of glacial climate does the methane record in ice cores reveal?

冰芯气泡的甲烷浓度在冰期低、间冰期高，并与南北半球温度变化同步起伏，反映湿地排放对温度的敏感响应。Dansgaard-Oeschger事件中甲烷快速上升常被用作北半球突然增温与全球碳循环联动的证据。
Methane in ice-core bubbles is low during glacials and high during interglacials, rising and falling in step with hemispheric temperatures, reflecting the sensitivity of wetland emissions to warming. Rapid methane rises during D-O events are taken as evidence of abrupt Northern Hemisphere warming coupling to the global carbon cycle.
