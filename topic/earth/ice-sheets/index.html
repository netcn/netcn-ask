格陵兰冰盖如果全部融化,海平面会上升多少?为什么科学家担心它越过「临界点」?

How much would sea level rise if the Greenland Ice Sheet melted entirely — and why do scientists worry about a tipping point?

格陵兰冰盖平均厚约1.6公里、覆盖约170万平方公里,全部融化将使全球海平面升高约7.4米,足以重塑绝大多数沿海城市。更麻烦的是它的正反馈机制:融水在冰面形成暗色池塘,吸收更多阳光加速消融;边缘冰川变薄后暖海水侵蚀接地线,冰流加快把内陆冰体更快送往海洋。冰盖对气候的响应存在滞后与不可逆性——一旦质量损失越过某条阈值,即使气温回落也可能持续亏损,这正是IPCC评估中最受关注的海平面临界系统之一。
The Greenland Ice Sheet averages 1.6 kilometers thick over roughly 1.7 million square kilometers; melting it entirely would raise global sea level by about 7.4 meters, redrawing most coastlines. Feedbacks make it worse: meltwater pools darken the surface and soak up more sunshine, while thinning marine-terminating glaciers retreat and speed up, shipping interior ice seaward faster. Because ice sheets respond with lag and irreversibility, past a threshold of mass loss they may keep shrinking even if temperatures stabilize — making Greenland one of the most-watched tipping systems in IPCC assessments.

南极的冰有的几万年才压成,科学家怎么用冰芯读出远古气候?

Antarctic ice took tens of millennia to form — how do scientists read ancient climate from ice cores?

雪在极地逐层堆积,压实成冰时把当时的空气封存成气泡,冰芯因此是一部双向档案:测水中氧18与氘之比可重建降雪时的温度;抽取气泡能直接测定八十万年前大气的二氧化碳与甲烷浓度;硫酸盐峰对应远古火山喷发。冰穹C的EPICA冰芯已回溯80万年,显示其间二氧化碳从未超过300ppm,而今天已突破420ppm——冰芯由此成为检验人类活动影响最硬的证据之一。
Snow piles up layer by layer in polar regions, sealing each era's air as bubbles when it compacts to ice. A core is therefore a two-sided archive: oxygen-18 and deuterium ratios in the water reconstruct past temperatures, the trapped bubbles give direct readings of atmospheric carbon dioxide and methane up to eight hundred thousand years back, and sulfate spikes mark great eruptions. The EPICA Dome C core spans 800 millennia, showing CO2 never exceeded 300 ppm — today it exceeds 420 ppm, making ice cores among the hardest evidence of human influence on climate.
