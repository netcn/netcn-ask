全球水是如何分布的？其中淡水且可利用的部分占多少？

How is Earth's water distributed globally, and what fraction is fresh and accessible?

地球约97%的水是海洋咸水，淡水中绝大部分又被封存在冰川与深层地下水，真正可供人类直接利用的河湖淡水不足总量的1%。这种高度不均凸显了淡水资源的珍贵。
About 97% of Earth's water is salty ocean water, and most freshwater is locked in glaciers and deep groundwater, leaving rivers and lakes accessible to people at well under 1% of the total. This extreme imbalance underlines how precious usable freshwater is.

Calcium-carbonate deposits on the lake beds act like a natural filter, and with algae and light scattering the water shows a dreamy blue-green.

自流井为什么会自己往外冒水？

Why does an artesian well flow on its own?

当地下水被夹在不透水的上下两层之间的承压含水层里，含水层像一根被堵住两端的水管。若补给区地势较高，水压会把水抬到高于含水层顶部的位置；打井穿透上部隔水层后，水就沿井管自动涌出甚至喷成喷泉。这类井叫自流井，能自流的关键是承压水头高于井口。
When groundwater is trapped between two impermeable layers in a confined aquifer, the layer behaves like a sealed pipe. If the recharge area sits at higher elevation, pressure lifts the water above the top of the aquifer; once a well pierces the upper seal, water rises on its own and may even spray like a fountain. Such artesian wells flow because the pressure head stands higher than the well opening.

A waterfall's hard ledge sits above softer rock that erodes and collapses, so the cliff edge moves slowly upstream, as with Niagara Falls.

冰川是怎么从雪花变成几十米厚的冰块的？

How does snow turn into a glacier hundreds of meters thick?

高寒地区积雪多年不化，新雪把旧雪压实，排走空气变成粒雪，再在更大压力下重结晶成致密的冰川冰。冰川冰达到足够厚度后，底部在重压下会像软糖一样缓慢变形，并沿山坡向下流动，一年只移动几米到几百米。冰川既是水体也是缓慢流动的侵蚀工具，磨出U形谷等地貌。
In cold highlands snow persists year after year; fresh snow compresses the old layers, expels air to form granular firn, and then recrystallizes under great pressure into dense glacier ice. Once thick enough, the ice deforms like taffy under its own weight and creeps downhill, advancing only meters to hundreds of meters per year. A glacier is both a water reservoir and a slow-moving erosional tool that carves U-shaped valleys.

A sponge city uses permeable pavements, sunken green spaces and storage ponds so rain can soak in, be stored and drain slowly, cutting street flooding in storms.

一场雨落到地面上，它的河水最终流向哪片海，由什么决定？

When rain hits the ground, what decides which ocean the river water eventually flows to?

分水岭是相邻流域之间的高地形边界，如分水岭山脊，落到两侧的降水会进入不同的河流系统。整个被分水岭围住、河水都汇入同一干流的区域叫流域或集水区，例如长江流域面积约180万平方公里。流域边界也决定污染物去向、洪水风险与水资源管理范围，所以治水必须先看懂流域。
A divide is the high terrain, like a ridge line, separating neighboring drainage basins; precipitation falling on its two flanks enters different river systems. The whole region drained into one main river is its watershed or drainage basin—the Yangtze drains about 1.8 million square kilometers. Basin boundaries determine where pollutants go, flood risk, and how water is managed, which is why water governance begins with reading the watershed.

地下水都藏在哪些地方？地下水位为什么会变化？

Where is groundwater stored, and why does the water table change?

地下水储存在土壤和岩石的空隙中，能大量蓄水的透水层叫含水层，如砂砾层和砂岩；黏土等几乎不透水的叫隔水层。地下水位是饱水带的顶面，受降雨补给和抽水影响，旱季或大量抽水时会下降。地下水不断向河流和泉补给，是许多地区饮用水与农业的主要来源，过度抽取会造成水位持续下降和地面沉降。
Groundwater fills pores and cracks in soil and rock; layers that store and transmit it freely, like gravel beds and sandstone, are aquifers, while clay and other nearly impermeable layers are aquitards. The water table is the top of the saturated zone; it falls in droughts or under heavy pumping and rises with recharge. Groundwater continuously feeds rivers and springs, supplies much of the world's drinking and irrigation water, and over-pumping causes long-term declines and land subsidence.

水为什么被称为万能溶剂？

What makes water a 'universal solvent'?

水分子一端带正电、一端带负电，能拉开许多离子化合物和极性分子，把它们均匀分散。这种极性使水可溶解盐、糖等大量物质，因而有万能溶剂之称。
A water molecule has a positive end and a negative end, so it can pry apart many ionic and polar substances and disperse them evenly. This polarity lets water dissolve salts, sugars, and many other materials, earning it the name universal solvent.

Why does water evaporate?

The Sun warms the water and its molecules rise as invisible vapor; that is evaporation. It is also why wet clothes dry.

为什么水结冰时反而膨胀？

Why does water expand when it freezes?

大多数东西冷了会缩小，但水在结成冰时分子排成疏松的六角结构，占据更大空间，于是冰比水轻、能浮在水面。这保护了水下生物安全过冬。
Most things shrink when cold, but when water freezes its molecules arrange into an open hexagonal pattern that takes up more space, so ice is lighter than water and floats. This protects the life below from freezing solid in winter.

Why does the ocean have currents?

风、地球自转和海水冷热不同，推动海水在大洋里循环流动，形成洋流。洋流像海洋里的河流。
Wind, Earth's spin, and differences in water temperature push the sea into looping flows called currents. Currents are like rivers inside the ocean.

Why do tsunamis form?

海底地震或火山把大块海床猛地抬起，立刻推动上面整层海水形成长长的高浪。浪到浅海时堆得很高，冲上岸就很危险。
An undersea quake or volcano jerks the seafloor upward and shoves the whole water layer above it into a long, fast wave. As it reaches shallow water the wave piles up and becomes dangerous onshore.

Why are polar ice sheets important?

南北极的巨厚冰盖把大量太阳光反射回太空，帮地球保持凉爽，还存着全球大部分淡水。冰一旦融化，海平面就会上升。
The thick polar ice sheets reflect sunlight back to space and help keep Earth cool, and they hold most of the planet's fresh water. If they melt, sea level rises.

潮汐为什么跟着月亮走？

Why do tides follow the Moon?

月球引力把正对和背对它的两边海水微微拉高，地球自转让各地依次经过这两处高水位。太阳也会帮忙，所以潮水跟着月亮走。
The Moon's gravity lifts the ocean slightly on the near and far sides, and Earth's spin carries each coast past those bulges. The Sun adds a smaller pull, so tides follow the Moon.
