如果地球没有大气和温室气体，地表平均温度会是多少？

If Earth had no atmosphere or greenhouse gases, what would its average surface temperature be?

把地球当作反射约 30% 阳光的黑球，仅靠辐射平衡算出的有效温度约 -18°C；而实际因为有温室效应，平均约 15°C，相差约 33 度。
Treating Earth as a blackbody reflecting about 30% of sunlight gives an effective temperature near -18°C; the actual ~15°C average is about 33 degrees warmer because of the greenhouse effect.

地球把吸收到的阳光又怎么送回太空？

How does Earth send the sunlight it absorbs back to space?

地球把热量以红外线的形式向外辐射；大气中的温室气体会拦住一部分红外辐射再放回地面，于是热量收支达到平衡，温度保持稳定。
Earth radiates its heat as infrared light; greenhouse gases intercept part of that infrared and re-radiate it back down, so the energy budget balances and temperature stays steady.

#红外辐射 #能量收支

地球表面大约把多少阳光直接反射回天上？

About how much sunlight does Earth's surface and clouds reflect back to space?

云、冰、雪和地面加起来大约把照到地球的阳光反射掉三成，剩下的才被吸收用来加热地球。
Clouds, ice, snow, and the ground together reflect about 30% of incoming sunlight; the rest is absorbed and warms the planet.
