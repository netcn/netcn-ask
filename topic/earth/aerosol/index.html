大气中的气溶胶是怎么影响气候的？

How do atmospheric aerosols affect climate?

气溶胶既能直接散射或吸收阳光（多数散射型让地球变冷），也能作为凝结核改变云的颜色和寿命，间接增强云的反射，产生降温效应；但黑碳等吸光型反而会加热。
Aerosols directly scatter or absorb sunlight—most scattering types cool Earth—and indirectly brighten and lengthen clouds by providing nuclei; but light-absorbing black carbon warms instead.

为什么大火山喷发后，全球气温常常短暂下降？

Why does global temperature often dip briefly after a big volcanic eruption?

火山把大量硫酸盐气溶胶送入高空，这些微粒会反射阳光，使到达地面的太阳辐射减少，从而让全球平均温度暂时降低。
Eruptions inject sulfate aerosols high into the atmosphere; these particles reflect sunlight, reducing solar radiation at the surface and temporarily lowering global average temperature.

工厂和汽车排放的微粒，对气候有什么矛盾的作用？

What contradictory effect do factory and vehicle particles have on climate?

它们中的硫酸盐和粉尘会散射阳光、给云增白而短期降温，但其中的黑碳吸光又会加热大气；总体上污染气溶胶掩盖了一部分温室增暖，却带来健康与污染问题。
Sulfates and dust scatter sunlight and brighten clouds, cooling short-term, while black carbon absorbs light and warms the air; pollution aerosols mask some greenhouse warming but cause health and pollution harm.
