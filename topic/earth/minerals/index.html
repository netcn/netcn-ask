How do orogenic gold deposits form?

造山带深处含金流体沿断层上升，在温压骤降时把金和石英一起沉淀在裂隙中，形成脉状金矿。
Deep gold-rich fluids rise along faults in orogens and precipitate gold with quartz in fractures when temperature and pressure drop.

什么是晶体结构？

What is crystal structure?

晶体结构是矿物内部原子、离子或分子在三维空间中有规律重复排列的方式，决定了矿物的对称性。同一种化学成分若排列方式不同（同质多象），可形成性质迥异的矿物，如金刚石和石墨。
Crystal structure is the regular, repeating three-dimensional arrangement of atoms, ions, or molecules inside a mineral, which determines its symmetry. The same composition arranged differently (polymorphism) can form very different minerals, like diamond and graphite.

硅酸盐矿物的晶体结构基本单元是什么？

What is the basic structural unit of silicate minerals?

硅酸盐以硅氧四面体（一个硅原子被四个氧包围）为基本单元。这些四面体通过共用氧原子，连接成岛状、链状、层状或架状等不同结构，决定了矿物的物理性质。
Silicates are built from the silica-oxygen tetrahedron, one silicon surrounded by four oxygens. These tetrahedra share oxygen atoms to form isolated, chain, sheet or framework structures, which determine the mineral's physical properties.

#矿物 #定年

地壳中含量最多的元素是什么？

What are the most abundant elements in the crust?

地壳是硅酸盐外壳，含量排序由氧、硅、铝、铁打头：氧与硅结成硅氧四面体，连成长石、云母、石英等造岩矿物，两者合占绝大部分地壳质量，铝与铁再顶替其间的金属位置。按质量与按原子个数计，排序略有差别；也要分清地壳与整个地球或海水：铁在地球整体里更靠前，因为分化时大量铁沉入地核，而海水里占优的是氧与氢。
The crust is a silicate shell, which is why the abundance ranking opens with oxygen, silicon, aluminium and iron: oxygen bonds with silicon into the silicon-oxygen tetrahedron, and these link up into the rock-forming minerals feldspar, mica and quartz, so those two elements alone make up most of the crust by mass, while aluminium and iron fill the metallic positions among them. Ranking by mass and by number of atoms gives slightly different orders, and the crust is not the same as the whole Earth or the sea: iron ranks higher for the planet as a whole because differentiation sent much of it into the core, whereas seawater is dominated by oxygen and hydrogen.

为什么大多数矿物属于硅酸盐类？

Why do most minerals belong to the silicate group?

硅酸盐矿物以硅氧四面体为基本结构单元，而硅和氧是地壳中含量最丰富的两种元素。它们通过共享氧原子连接成链、层或三维骨架，因此占据了已命名矿物的绝大多数。
Silicate minerals are built from silicon-oxygen tetrahedra, and silicon and oxygen are the two most abundant elements in the crust. These tetrahedra link by sharing oxygen into chains, sheets, or 3-D frameworks, so silicates make up the vast majority of known minerals.

为什么有些矿石有金属光泽？

Why do some minerals have a metallic luster?

有金属光泽的矿物里价电子离域成半满能带，可见光电场一推，这批电子就整体振荡并重新辐射出同频率的光，反射率高、光穿不进多深，于是既明亮又不透明。非金属矿物没有这批自由电子，靠折射与内部散射反光，所以只有玻璃或金刚光泽。别把光泽当成分：黄铁矿、方铅矿乃至石墨都不含金属单质却一样闪亮；而光泽随观察角度与抛光强弱变化，鉴别时条痕往往比反光可靠。
In minerals with a metallic luster the valence electrons are delocalized into half-filled bands, so the electric field of visible light sets this whole population oscillating and they re-radiate light of the same frequency: reflectivity is high and light penetrates only a shallow way in, which makes them both bright and opaque. Non-metallic minerals lack that free-electron population and shine instead by refraction and internal scattering, so they show only a vitreous or adamantine luster. Do not read luster as composition: pyrite, galena and even graphite contain no native metal yet are just as shiny, and luster varies with viewing angle and how well the face is polished, so the streak is usually more reliable than the shine.

Why does calcite fizz when it meets acid?

Calcite is calcium carbonate that reacts with dilute hydrochloric acid to release carbon dioxide bubbles, a characteristic of carbonate minerals. Geologists use dilute acid to quickly identify limestone and calcite.

云母为什么能一层层撕开？

Why can mica be peeled into sheets?

云母晶体内部是层状硅酸盐结构，层内为强共价键，层间仅为弱范德华力。因此容易沿层间裂开成薄片，这种性质称为极完全解理。
Mica has a layered silicate structure with strong bonds inside sheets but weak van der Waals forces between them. It thus splits easily along layers into thin sheets, a property called perfect cleavage.

矿物的「硬度」用什么标准衡量？

What standard is used to measure a mineral's 'hardness'?

常用摩氏硬度计，把矿物按能否互相划伤分为1到10级，滑石最软为1，金刚石最硬为10。它反映的是相对抗划伤能力，而非绝对硬度。
The Mohs scale ranks minerals from 1 to 10 by which can scratch which; talc is softest at 1 and diamond hardest at 10. It reflects relative scratch resistance, not absolute hardness.

What do diamond and common coal have in common and differ?

Both are mainly carbon, but in diamond the atoms are neatly arranged making it hard and clear, while coal is a loose impure carbon mass.

金矿通常是以什么形式存在的？

In what forms does gold ore usually occur?

自然金常以细小颗粒或块状散布在石英脉或河流砂砾（砂金）中。因为金化学性质稳定、不易与其他元素结合，所以多以单质形式被开采。
Native gold often occurs as tiny grains or nuggets dispersed in quartz veins or in river gravel as placer gold. Because gold is chemically stable and rarely bonds with other elements, it is mostly mined in its pure metallic form.

钻石是在哪里和怎样形成的？

Where and how do diamonds form?

钻石是碳在高温高压下结晶而成的矿物，通常形成于地下150公里以上的地幔深处。它们随火山喷发被带到地表，藏在一种叫金伯利岩的特殊岩石中。
Diamonds are minerals of carbon crystallized under high temperature and pressure, usually deep in the mantle more than 150 km down. They are brought to the surface by volcanic eruptions and hosted in a special rock called kimberlite.

如何根据条痕判断矿物？

How is a mineral identified by its streak?

条痕是矿物在无釉瓷板上划出的粉末颜色，往往比表面颜色更稳定。例如黄铁矿表面金黄，但条痕呈绿黑色，可借此与真金区分。
Streak is the color of a mineral's powder when scratched on unglazed porcelain, often more consistent than its surface color. Pyrite looks golden but leaves a greenish-black streak, helping distinguish it from real gold.

矿物的颜色和条痕分别由什么决定？

What determines a mineral's color and its streak?

颜色常受微量杂质影响、易变，不如条痕可靠；条痕是矿物粉末在素瓷板上的颜色，由成分与晶体结构决定，更稳定。鉴定时条痕往往比外表颜色更具判别价值。
Color is often altered by trace impurities and can be misleading, whereas streak, the color of a mineral's powder rubbed on a porcelain plate, is set by composition and structure and stays consistent. For identification, streak is usually more diagnostic than surface color.

What determines the color of gemstones?

A gemstone's color mainly comes from trace transition-metal impurities or structural defects in the crystal. For example, a little chromium turns corundum red into ruby, and colors beryl green into emerald.

什么是矿床？

What is an ore deposit?

矿床是地质作用把有用组分在地壳里堆到能在当前技术与价格下开采的程度，判据是品位、储量与开采条件，不是含量高于平均值就行。这种集中靠岩浆分异、热液搬运沉淀、沉积与风化，因为多数元素在普通岩石中稀薄分散，要再富集好几个数量级才谈得上采矿。矿床因此是个经济概念：低品位矿与老尾矿今天不算矿，涨价或选冶过关后就可能变成矿，伴生组分也要看有没有回收途径。
An ore deposit is material that geological processes have concentrated in the crust to a degree that can be mined profitably under current technology and prices; the test is grade, tonnage and mining conditions, not merely content above average. That concentration comes from magmatic differentiation, the transport and precipitation of hydrothermal fluids, sedimentation and weathering, because most elements sit thinly dispersed in ordinary rock and must be gathered by several more orders of magnitude before mining is worth discussing. So a deposit is an economic idea: low-grade ore and old tailings do not count as a mine today, yet a higher price or a new beneficiation route can turn them into one, and a by-product element only counts if there is a way to recover it.

为什么金刚石比石墨硬得多，尽管二者都是纯碳？

Why is diamond so much harder than graphite even though both are pure carbon?

二者同素异形体，差异全在原子排列：金刚石中每个碳原子与四个邻居形成强三维共价键网络；石墨是层状结构，层内强、层间仅靠弱范德华力结合。于是金刚石极硬、石墨却柔软易剥层。
Both are allotropes of pure carbon, but their atom arrangement differs: in diamond each carbon bonds strongly to four neighbors in a 3-D covalent network, while graphite stacks in sheets held together only by weak van der Waals forces between layers. Hence diamond is extremely hard, whereas graphite is soft and flakes easily.

为什么石盐（食盐的矿物）能沿三个相互垂直的平面劈成小立方体，而石英只会裂出不规则的弯曲断面？

Why does halite (the mineral of table salt) split into little cubes along three mutually perpendicular planes, while quartz breaks into irregular curved surfaces?

解理是晶体沿结合力最弱的原子面劈开的习性。石盐为离子键矿物，钠、氯离子堆成的立方格子在三组相互垂直的方向上键强一致，故发育完全立方体解理；石英是硅氧四面体连成的架状硅酸盐，各方向都被牢固的Si–O键锁定，没有薄弱面，受力时只能形成贝壳状不规则断口。解理方位因此直接反映晶体内部结构。
Cleavage is a crystal’s tendency to split along atomic planes where bonding is weakest. Halite is an ionic mineral: Na⁺ and Cl⁻ ions pack into a cubic lattice with identical bond strengths along three mutually perpendicular directions, giving perfect cubic cleavage. Quartz is a framework silicate locked by strong Si–O bonds in every direction, leaving no weak plane, so it fails irregularly with conchoidal curved fractures. Cleavage orientation thus mirrors the internal crystal structure.

金刚石与石墨都是碳元素构成的单质，为什么两者的硬度和导电性差别悬殊？

Diamond and graphite are both elemental carbon—why do their hardness and electrical conductivity differ so dramatically?

二者互为碳的同质多象变体，晶体结构决定宏观性质：金刚石中每个碳原子以sp³杂化与四个碳原子成键，形成牢固的三维共价网络，因而硬度居天然矿物之首且不导电；石墨由sp²键构成的六方原子层堆叠而成，层间仅靠微弱的范德华力维系，层片易于滑动，故质地柔软可作润滑剂，而层内离域的π电子能沿层面自由移动，使石墨成为良好的导体。
They are polymorphs of carbon, and crystal structure dictates behavior. In diamond each atom bonds to four neighbors through strong directional sp³ bonds, building a rigid three-dimensional framework—hence the hardest known mineral and an electrical insulator. Graphite stacks flat sp²-bonded hexagonal sheets held together only by weak van der Waals forces: the sheets slip easily, making it soft enough to serve as a lubricant, while delocalized electrons roam within each sheet, so graphite conducts along the layers.

怎样区分矿物和岩石？

How can you tell a mineral from a rock?

矿物是天然形成、成分固定、有规则晶体结构的单质或化合物，如石英；岩石则是由一种或多种矿物组成的集合体，如花岗岩。简单说，矿物是零件，岩石是由零件拼成的整体。
A mineral is a naturally formed substance with a fixed composition and ordered crystal structure, like quartz; a rock is an aggregate of one or more minerals, like granite. Simply put, minerals are the parts and rocks are the assemblies built from them.
