德干暗色岩等大陆洪水玄武岩与生物大灭绝有何关联？

How are continental flood basalts like the Deccan Traps linked to mass extinctions?

大陆洪水玄武岩是短时间内喷出的巨量玄武岩，德干暗色岩的喷发恰与白垩纪末大灭绝部分重叠。它们释放的二氧化碳与气溶胶可造成长期温室变暖与酸雨，扰动全球碳循环。库尔蒂约等人指出这类喷发的时间常与生物危机吻合，但撞击事件的作用仍需区分。
Continental flood basalts are enormous volumes of basalt erupted over short intervals, and the Deccan Traps overlap in part with the end-Cretaceous mass extinction. The carbon dioxide and aerosols they release can drive long-term greenhouse warming and acid rain, disturbing global carbon cycling. Courtillot and colleagues noted such eruptions often coincide with biotic crises, though impact effects must be disentangled.

黄石火山为何被归类为超级火山，它的喷发历史说明了什么？

Why is Yellowstone classified as a supervolcano, and what does its eruption history show?

黄石的破火山口源于大规模岩浆房塌陷，过去数百万年发生过数次喷发量超过一千立方公里的超级喷发。克里斯滕森等人对黄石的岩浆管道系统做了详细重建，显示其喷发间隔漫长但不规律。这类喷发足以改变全球气候，因此被列为重点监测对象。
Yellowstone's caldera formed from collapse of a large magma chamber and has produced several supereruptions exceeding 1,000 cubic kilometers over the past millions of years. Christiansen and colleagues reconstructed its magmatic plumbing in detail, showing long but irregular intervals between eruptions. Such events can alter global climate, so the system is closely monitored.

破火山口是如何形成的，它与盾状火山有何不同？

How does a caldera form, and how does it differ from a shield volcano?

当巨型岩浆房排空后，上方岩石失去支撑而塌陷，形成碗状的大型破火山口，而非典型的锥形山峰。与之不同，盾状火山由流动性玄武岩层层溢流堆成平缓穹丘，并不发生塌陷。利普曼在1984年系统总结了破火山口塌陷的动力学。
When a huge magma chamber empties, the overlying rock loses support and collapses into a basin-shaped caldera rather than building a conical peak. A shield volcano, by contrast, is a gentle dome built by fluid basalt flows and does not collapse. Lipman summarized caldera-collapse dynamics in 1984.

超级火山爆发真的能让地球进入数年寒冬?黄石之下到底埋着什么?

Could a supervolcano really freeze the planet for years — what actually lies beneath Yellowstone?

黄石地下是一个反复苏醒的岩浆系统:浅层储存着以流纹岩浆为主、混入玄武岩补给的晶粥体,约64万年前一次喷发抛出1000立方公里级物质,火山灰远达堪萨斯。如此规模的普林尼式喷发会把二氧化硫注入平流层,气溶胶反射阳光使全球均温下降数度并持续多年,农业减产才是真正的全球风险——1991年皮纳图博仅注入约2000万吨二氧化硫就压低了全球均温0.5度。监测显示黄石头顶目前以抬升与地震群著称但岩浆房大部分仍是可缓慢挤压的晶体糊,近期喷发概率极低;科学界更担心的是无人实时监测的发展中国家活火山。
Yellowstone hosts a repeatedly reawakening magmatic system: a shallow crystal-rich mush of rhyolite fed by basalt, whose caldera-forming eruption 640,000 years ago lofted on the order of a thousand cubic kilometers of material, ash reaching Kansas. A Plinian event at that scale injects sulfur dioxide into the stratosphere; sulfate aerosols reflect sunlight and cool global mean temperature by several degrees for years — agriculture, not lava, is the planetary hazard. Pinatubo alone injected about twenty megatonnes and cooled Earth half a degree. Monitoring shows uplift and earthquake swarms, but the reservoir remains mostly squishable crystal mush with negligible near-term eruption odds; scientists worry more about unmonitored volcanoes in poorer nations.

热点火山作用如何解释夏威夷群岛的线性排列？

How does hotspot volcanism explain the linear arrangement of the Hawaiian Islands?

热点是地幔柱把熔融物质从深部输送到岩石圈的近乎固定位置，板块在其上方移动时留下一串火山。夏威夷从西北端的古老死火山到东南端的活跃基拉韦厄，显示了板块向西北漂移的轨迹。摩根在1971年用热点理论统一解释了这类链状火山。
A hotspot is a near-stationary source where a mantle plume delivers melt to the lithosphere, and a plate drifting above it leaves a chain of volcanoes. Hawaii's chain, from ancient extinct volcanoes in the northwest to active Kilauea in the southeast, records the plate's northwestward motion. Morgan used hotspot theory in 1971 to explain such linear volcanic chains.

火山碎屑流为何如此致命，它由哪些物质组成？

Why are pyroclastic flows so lethal, and what are they made of?

火山碎屑流是沿火山坡高速奔涌的炽热气体、灰与岩块的混合物，温度可达数百摄氏度且速度超过每小时百公里。由于其密度大且贴地流动，它能越过地形障碍并瞬间吞没大片区域，如摧毁庞贝的维苏威喷发。费舍尔与海肯在1982年系统总结了这类沉积的成因与危害。
A pyroclastic flow is a hot mixture of gas, ash, and rock fragments racing down a volcano's flanks, reaching hundreds of degrees and speeds over 100 km/h. Dense and ground-hugging, it overruns terrain and buries vast areas within moments, as when Vesuvius destroyed Pompeii. Fisher and Heiken summarized their origin and hazards in 1982.

内瓦多·德尔·鲁伊斯的悲剧表明,火山没有大规模喷发也可能夺走数万人的生命——这是为什么?

The tragedy of Nevado del Ruiz shows that a volcano can kill tens of thousands without a large eruption — how is that possible?

1985年该火山仅发生一次规模不大的火山碎屑喷发,喷发柱的热量足以融化山顶冰帽,融水裹挟新鲜火山灰沿河谷加速下泄,形成时速数十公里的火山泥流,掩埋了四十余公里外的阿尔梅罗镇,逾两万三千人罹难。灾后复盘指出,若当时的警戒建议得到落实,伤亡本可大幅避免,这也促使各国把泥流模拟纳入火山风险评估。
The 1985 eruption was modest, yet its heat melted the summit ice cap and the floodwater mixed with fresh ash, racing down valleys as lahars moving tens of kilometers per hour that buried Armero more than forty kilometers away, killing over twenty-three thousand people. Post-event reviews concluded that acting on earlier warnings would have saved most lives, a lesson that pushed lahar modeling into standard volcanic risk assessment.

#火山泥流 #次生灾害

夏威夷式的玄武岩喷发多以熔岩流缓缓外溢,而富硅岩浆的喷发却动辄剧烈爆炸,差别从何而来?

Hawaiian basaltic eruptions mostly ooze out as lava flows, yet silica-rich eruptions tend to blow apart violently—where does the difference come from?

岩浆黏度随二氧化硅含量升高而增大:玄武岩浆稀薄炽热,气泡得以顺利逃逸,气体平和释放,熔岩便以流动方式铺开。富硅岩浆黏稠难散,挥发分被封闭在通道内不断聚集,直到压力撕碎岩浆,气体骤然膨胀驱动碎屑高速喷出。可见黏度与挥发分含量共同决定了喷发是溢流还是爆炸。
Magma viscosity climbs with silica content: runny, hot basalt lets bubbles escape so gas vents gently and lava spreads as flows. Sticky silica-rich magma holds volatiles trapped until pressure shatters it apart and expanding gas blasts the fragments skyward at high speed. Viscosity acting together with volatile content thus decides whether an eruption pours or explodes.

#岩浆黏度 #爆炸性喷发

同样是火山,为什么夏威夷的岩浆温柔流淌,而维苏威会猛烈爆炸?

Why do Hawaiian volcanoes ooze gently while Vesuvius explodes catastrophically?

决定性因素是岩浆的二氧化硅含量与气体逃逸难度。夏威夷的玄武岩浆含二氧化硅仅约50%,黏度像蜂蜜,气泡能从容上浮逃逸,熔岩湖与火帘喷泉虽壮观却少致命爆发;维苏威式的俯冲带岩浆含二氧化硅60%以上,黏稠如沥青,还携带大量水汽与二氧化碳——气体被高压憋在岩浆房里,直到裂隙开启的瞬间才急速膨胀,把岩浆撕成数亿颗灰粒,以音速冲出地表,形成普林尼式喷发柱。一句话:黏的岩浆等于摇晃过的香槟。
The deciding factors are silica content and how hard it is for gas to escape. Hawaiian basalt holds only about fifty percent silica; it flows like honey and bubbles escape gently, producing lava lakes and fire fountains rather than deadly blasts. Subduction-zone magma like Vesuvius carries over sixty percent silica — sticky as tar — plus abundant water vapor and carbon dioxide. Gas stays pressurized until a fracture opens, then expands explosively, shredding magma into hundreds of millions of ash particles at sonic speed: a Plinian column. Viscous magma is simply shaken champagne.

火山灰为什么是喷气发动机的噩梦?它和篝火的灰烬是一回事吗?

Why is volcanic ash a nightmare for jet engines — and is it the same stuff as campfire ash?

不是一回事。火山灰是被气体猛烈喷射撕碎的微小玻璃质岩石与矿物颗粒,硬度高、熔点低(约800–1200摄氏度即软化)。喷气发动机涡轮前温度恰好落在这个区间:灰粒吸入后被部分熔成黏稠玻璃体,黏附在涡轮叶片上冷却结垢,轻则推力骤降喘振,重则四台发动机空中全停——1982年英航9号航班与1989年KLM867都因此险坠。所以航路一旦发现火山灰云,管制部门宁可大面积绕飞。
Not the same thing at all. Volcanic ash consists of jagged glassy rock and mineral fragments shattered by expanding gas, hard and low-melting (softening near 800–1200 °C). Turbine inlet temperatures sit right in that range: ingested ash partially melts into sticky glass that coats turbine blades and solidifies, causing surges, thrust loss — even all four engines flaming out in flight, as in the 1982 British Airways Flight 9 and 1989 KLM 867 incidents. That is why air traffic reroutes widely around any ash cloud.
