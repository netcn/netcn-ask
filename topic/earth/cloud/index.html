为什么云对气候的反馈是科学家最头疼的不确定性之一？

Why is cloud feedback one of the trickiest uncertainties for climate scientists?

云既能反射阳光又能保温，且小到公里尺度难以被气候模型直接算清；变暖后云量、高度、厚度的变化方向不完全确定，会放大或减弱增暖。
Clouds both reflect and trap radiation, yet they occur at scales too small for models to resolve directly; how cloud amount, height, and thickness change as it warms is uncertain, so they may amplify or damp warming.

#云反馈 #不确定性

云到底是在给地球降温还是升温？

Do clouds cool or warm the Earth?

两种都有：云把阳光反射回太空会降温，但云也会像毯子一样拦住地面红外辐射而升温；低厚云多半净降温，高空薄云多半净升温。
Both: clouds reflect sunlight (cooling) but also trap outgoing infrared like a blanket (warming). Low thick clouds usually net-cool, while high thin clouds usually net-warm.

#云 #反照率

白云在晴天为什么会帮我们挡住一点热？

How do white clouds help shade us on a sunny day?

白云亮亮的，会把一部分太阳光直接弹回天上，地面接收到的阳光变少，所以云底下会凉快一些。
Bright white clouds bounce part of the sunlight back to space, so the ground gets less sun and feels cooler underneath.
