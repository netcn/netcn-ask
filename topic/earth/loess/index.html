黄土高原的黄土与古土壤交替层序记录了怎样的冰期—间冰期气候信息？为什么地层磁化率可作为古夏季风强度的代用指标？

What glacial-interglacial climatic information does the loess-paleosol alternation on the Loess Plateau record, and why can stratigraphic magnetic susceptibility serve as a proxy for palaeo-summer-monsoon intensity?

冰期冬季风强盛时，西风带与近地面偏北风把亚洲内陆荒漠粉尘搬至高原堆积成黄土层；间冰期夏季风增强带来降水，地表发育古土壤。成土过程中铁氧化物经历还原—再氧化，生成大量超顺磁性细粒磁铁矿与磁赤铁矿，使古土壤磁化率显著升高。磁化率曲线因此追踪夏季风强弱，可与深海氧同位素阶段直接对比，重建约260万年以来的季风演化史。
When glacial winter monsoons strengthened, westerlies and northerly near-surface winds carried dust from Central Asian deserts onto the plateau to form loess horizons; intensified interglacial summer monsoons brought rainfall and soil development. During pedogenesis, iron oxides were reduced and reoxidized into abundant ultrafine superparamagnetic magnetite and maghemite, markedly raising paleosol susceptibility. Susceptibility curves therefore track summer-monsoon strength and correlate directly with marine oxygen-isotope stages, reconstructing monsoon history over roughly the past 2.6 million years.

在中国黄土研究中，为什么沉积物粒度可作为东亚冬季风强度的代用指标？洛川剖面揭示了什么？

Why does sediment grain size serve as a proxy for East Asian winter monsoon intensity in Chinese loess research, and what has the Luochuan section revealed?

黄土高原的风尘堆积主要靠冬季风从北方荒漠搬运：风力越强，能携带并输送到更远处的粗颗粒越多，因此粒度变粗指示冬季风增强。洛川剖面保存约250万年以来的黄土—古土壤互层，冰期黄土层粒度偏粗、间冰期古土壤层偏细，由此可定量重建第四纪冬季风的旋回式演变，并与深海氧同位素记录对比。
Loess dust reaching the Chinese Loess Plateau is transported mainly by the winter monsoon from northern deserts: stronger winds carry coarser particles farther, so coarsening grain size signals a stronger monsoon. The Luochuan section preserves some 2.5 million years of alternating loess and palaeosol layers — coarse-grained loess laid down in glacials, finer palaeosols in interglacials — enabling quantitative reconstruction of cyclic Quaternary winter-monsoon evolution that can be compared with deep-sea oxygen-isotope records.

黄土高原为什么特别容易发生水土流失？

Why is the Loess Plateau so prone to soil erosion?

黄土本身疏松、垂直节理发育，加上降水集中、坡度大、植被少，雨水一冲就容易带走表层沃土。
Loose, cracked loess plus concentrated rainfall, steep slopes and sparse vegetation lets rain wash away topsoil easily.

黄土高原为什么千沟万壑，像被刀切过一样？

Why is the Loess Plateau cut into countless gullies like it was sliced?

黄土疏松怕水，夏季暴雨顺着坡面冲刷，把松软的黄土一点点冲走，天长日久就刻出了密密麻麻的沟壑。
Loose loess erodes easily; summer rainstorms wash the soft soil downhill, carving dense gullies over time.

黄土高原的窑洞为什么冬暖夏凉？

Why are cave dwellings on the Loess Plateau warm in winter and cool in summer?

黄土像厚厚的棉被，传热很慢，地下窑洞温度一年四季变化很小，所以冬天不冷、夏天不热。
Loess is a poor conductor of heat, so cave homes stay at a steady temperature year-round—cool in summer, warm in winter.

黄土明明是黄色的，为什么还能长出庄稼？

Loess is yellow, so why can crops still grow in it?

黄土里有很多细小的矿物质养分，而且疏松透气，存得住水和肥，所以很适合种小米和麦子。
Loess is rich in fine minerals, loose and airy, and holds water and nutrients well, so it suits millet and wheat.

黄土高原上厚厚的黄土是从哪里吹来的?

Where did the thick loess on the Loess Plateau blow in from?

风是主要搬运工：冰期时西北戈壁沙漠中的细粉砂被冬季季风卷起搬运数千公里后沉降堆积形成厚50至80米的黄土覆盖层。黄土中古土壤标记了温暖间冰期使剖面成为古气候档案。
Wind lofted glacial silt from inland deserts settling layer by layer building fifty-to-eighty-meter profiles over two million years. Buried paleosols mark warm intervals making the profile a continuous archive.
