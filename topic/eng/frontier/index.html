What inspires biomimetic materials?

它们模仿自然界结构，如莲叶的自清洁、壁虎脚趾的粘附、贝壳的层状增韧，用微观结构设计出疏水、强韧或自修复的新材料。
They mimic nature, lotus self-cleaning, gecko adhesion, shell layering, using micro-structure for hydrophobic, tough or self-healing materials.

建筑3D打印如何工作？

How does construction 3D printing work?

它用机械臂或龙门架把混凝土或特种砂浆按数字模型逐层挤出堆叠，像写毛笔字一样打印墙体，可节约模板与人力、实现异形结构。
A robotic arm or gantry extrudes concrete layer by layer per a digital model, printing walls like brush strokes, saving formwork and enabling organic shapes.

电动车电池为什么要热管理？

Why do EV batteries need thermal management?

锂电池在过高或过低温度下容量、寿命和安全都会恶化，热管理系统把电芯温度维持在适宜区间，并均衡单体间温差以防热失控。
Li-ion cells lose capacity, life and safety outside a narrow temperature band, so thermal management keeps cells in range and balances cell temperatures to prevent thermal runaway.

绿氢是怎么制取的？

How is green hydrogen produced?

绿氢用可再生能源发电，再通过电解槽把水电解成氢气和氧气，全过程零碳排放，被视为难减排行业的清洁燃料。
Green hydrogen uses renewable electricity to electrolyze water into H2 and O2 with zero emissions, seen as a clean fuel for hard-to-abate sectors.

风力发电机怎样把风变成电？

How does a wind turbine turn wind into electricity?

叶片受风推动旋转，经主轴和齿轮箱（或直驱）带动发电机转子切割磁感线发电，控制系统随风速调节桨距和偏航以保安全高效。
Blades spin from wind, driving a generator, via gearbox or direct-drive, to produce electricity; controls adjust pitch and yaw for safe, efficient operation.
