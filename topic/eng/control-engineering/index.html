什么是『自适应控制』？

What is 'adaptive control'?

自适应控制在运行中在线估计系统参数并自动调整控制器，使对象特性随时间或工况变化时仍保持良好表现，常用于飞行器、机器人和工况多变的工业过程。
Adaptive control estimates parameters online and retunes itself during operation, keeping performance as the plant changes over time or conditions—used in flight, robotics, and varying industrial processes.

PID控制器中的比例、积分、微分各解决什么问题？

What do the proportional, integral, and derivative terms in a PID controller each address?

比例项按当前误差大小给出纠正力度；积分项累计历史误差以消除稳态余差；微分项依据误差变化率提前抑制超调。三者配合可在多数过程控制中取得平稳响应。
The proportional term reacts to current error; the integral accumulates past error to wipe steady-state offset; the derivative anticipates via error rate to curb overshoot. Together they give smooth control in many processes.

控制中的『鲁棒性』是什么？

What is 'robustness' in control?

鲁棒性指控制器在模型不准、参数漂移或存在外界扰动时仍能保持良好性能的能力。设计鲁棒控制器就是要让系统在不确定性下也不易失稳或失效。
Robustness is a controller's ability to keep performing well despite model error, parameter drift, or disturbances. Robust design keeps the system stable and effective under uncertainty.

What is robust control and what problem does it solve?

奈奎斯特稳定判据是怎样只用开环数据判断闭环稳定性的？

How does the Nyquist stability criterion judge closed-loop stability from open-loop data alone?

奈奎斯特判据看开环频率响应的复平面轨迹如何绕过点 (-1,0)，结合右半平面开环极点数判断闭环是否稳定，无需先求出闭环特征方程。它在存在时延和不确定时尤其有用。Nyquist 在 1932 年提出这一思想。
The Nyquist criterion looks at how the open-loop frequency-response locus encircles the point (-1,0) and, with the count of right-half-plane open-loop poles, decides closed-loop stability without solving the characteristic equation. It is valuable when delays and uncertainty are present. Nyquist proposed it in 1932.

什么是“负反馈”？它为何能提升系统的稳定性与精度？

What is negative feedback, and why does it improve a system's stability and accuracy?

负反馈把输出的一部分减去后送回输入端，从而减小对参数变化的敏感度、拓宽带宽并降低失真。但若相位滞后过大则可能引发振荡，需用奈奎斯特/伯德判据分析稳定性。
Negative feedback subtracts a portion of the output from the input, reducing sensitivity to parameter variations, widening bandwidth, and lowering distortion. But excessive phase lag can cause oscillation, so stability is analyzed with Nyquist or Bode criteria.

What is the state-space method and what systems is it suited for?

状态空间法用一组一阶微分方程描述系统内部状态，并写成矩阵形式进行设计和分析。它能方便地处理多输入多输出、时变和非线性系统，是现代控制理论的核心工具。
The state-space method describes a system's internal states with first-order differential equations in matrix form for design and analysis. It readily handles multiple-input multiple-output, time-varying, and nonlinear systems and is central to modern control theory.

卡尔曼滤波在工程中如何估计无法直接测量的状态？

How does the Kalman filter estimate states that cannot be measured directly?

它以系统状态方程和带噪声的量测为输入，每步先由模型预测、再用新量测做最优加权修正，递推得到均值与协方差最小均方误差估计。广泛用于导航、制导和信号去噪。
Given a state model and noisy measurements, it predicts forward each step then optimally blends the new measurement, yielding a minimum-variance estimate of the mean and covariance recursively. It is widely used in navigation, guidance, and denoising.

什么是“状态空间”模型？它与传递函数有何不同？

What is a state-space model, and how does it differ from a transfer function?

状态空间用一组一阶微分方程 x' = Ax + Bu、y = Cx + Du 描述系统，可处理多输入多输出、非线性与任意初始条件。传递函数是线性时不变系统的频域输入输出比值，通常只适用于单输入单输出。
State space describes a system by first-order equations x' = Ax + Bu and y = Cx + Du, handling MIMO, nonlinear, and arbitrary initial conditions. A transfer function is the frequency-domain input-output ratio of a linear time-invariant system and is usually limited to SISO.

什么是比例-积分-微分（PID）控制的三个作用？

What are the three actions of PID control?

比例项按当前误差大小快速响应，积分项消除长期稳态误差，微分项根据误差变化速度提前抑制超调。三者配合能在速度和稳定之间取得良好平衡。
The proportional term responds quickly to the current error, the integral term removes long-term steady error, and the derivative term anticipates overshoot by the error's rate of change. Together they balance speed and stability well.

什么是反馈，它为什么能让系统稳定？

What is feedback and why does it stabilize a system?

反馈是把系统输出的一部分送回输入端，与设定目标比较产生纠正动作。负反馈能抵消偏离，使温度、速度等保持在目标附近，是大多数稳定控制的基础。
Feedback sends part of a system's output back to its input to compare with the target and produce correction. Negative feedback counteracts deviation, holding temperature, speed, and the like near the goal, the basis of most stable control.

控制里说的系统『稳定』是什么意思？

What does 'stability' of a system mean in control?

稳定指系统受扰动偏离平衡后，能在有限时间内回到或接近原状态；不稳定则会持续发散或振荡。稳定性是控制器设计首先要保证的性质。
Stability means a system perturbed from equilibrium returns or stays near it in finite time; instability means it diverges or oscillates without bound. It is the first property a controller must guarantee.

自动调温器里的『反馈控制』是怎么工作的？

How does 'feedback control' work in a thermostat?

反馈控制不断测量实际温度并与设定值比较，用差值去调节加热器或空调，使温度回到目标附近。因为依据结果修正，所以能抵消干扰、保持稳态。
Feedback control keeps measuring the actual temperature against the setpoint and uses the error to adjust the heater or cooler, holding the target. Correcting by result rejects disturbances.

What is an actuator in control systems?

执行机构接收控制器的指令，直接对受控对象施加动作，比如电动机、阀门和液压缸。它把微弱的控制信号变成实际的力或运动，是控制的“手脚”。
An actuator receives commands from the controller and acts directly on the controlled object, such as motors, valves, and hydraulic cylinders. It turns weak control signals into real force or motion, the hands and feet of control.

What are open-loop and closed-loop control?

Open-loop control acts by a fixed program without checking the actual result, like a timer-switch streetlight. Closed-loop control keeps measuring the output and compares it with the setpoint to correct error, like an air conditioner adjusting to room temperature.
