什么是交通流的三相理论，它如何解释高速公路上的幽灵堵车？

What is the three-phase traffic theory and how does it explain phantom jams?

传统模型只有自由流和拥堵两态，三相理论加入同步流作为中间态，车速相近、密度中等但不稳定。微小扰动在同步流中会被放大成局部停走波并向上游传播，于是没有明显原因也会出现堵车。
Classic models have only free and jammed flow; three-phase theory adds an unstable synchronized flow where speeds are similar at medium density. Tiny disturbances amplify into stop-and-go waves propagating upstream, explaining jams with no obvious cause.

How does cooperative adaptive cruise control under V2X differ from solo radar cruise, and how much fuel can platooning save?

单车巡航只看前车雷达，协同巡航还接收前车加速度广播，反应更快更平顺。多车近距离编队能共享“破风”尾流，卡车编队风阻大降，油耗可省一成到两成。
Solo cruise only sees the front radar; cooperative cruise also gets the leader's acceleration broadcast, reacting faster and smoother. Close platooning shares the slipstream, cutting truck aerodynamic drag so fuel use drops by roughly 10 to 20 percent.

In rail transit, why does regenerative braking recover kinetic energy as electricity?

When slowing, the traction motors switch to generator mode, turning wheel rotation into electricity fed back to the overhead line for other trains or storage. Unlike friction brakes that waste heat, this saves energy and cuts wear.

自动驾驶为什么需要融合多种传感器？融合通常在什么层级进行？

Why must autonomous driving fuse multiple sensors, and at which levels is fusion typically performed?

单一传感器都有盲区：摄像头对光线敏感、测距不精确，毫米波雷达分辨率低、难以分辨静止物体，激光雷达昂贵且受雨雾影响。多传感器融合利用各自的互补性，在时间同步和空间对齐（外参标定）的前提下，把检测结果或原始数据组合成统一的环境模型。融合可分为数据级、特征级和目标级三类，目标级融合把各传感器识别出的目标关联匹配后合成航迹，鲁棒性高、计算量可控，是主流方案。
Every sensor has blind spots: cameras are light-sensitive and poor at ranging, mmWave radar has low resolution and trouble with stationary objects, and LiDAR is costly and degraded by rain and fog. Sensor fusion exploits their complementarity, combining detections or raw data into one environmental model after time synchronisation and spatial calibration. Fusion happens at data, feature or object level; object-level fusion matches and merges the targets each sensor detects into unified tracks, offering high robustness at manageable compute cost and is the mainstream approach.

整车控制器（VCU）在电动汽车中承担什么角色？如何根据踏板信号决定扭矩请求？

What role does the vehicle control unit (VCU) play in an EV, and how does it determine torque demand from pedal signals?

VCU是整车动力系统的决策中枢，采集加速和制动踏板、挡位、车速、电池及电机状态等信息，根据驾驶员意图和车辆安全边界计算电机扭矩指令并下发给电机控制器。扭矩请求综合考虑踏板开度与速率、当前车速和电池允许功率：低速起步大扭矩、高速适度降扭，同时必须受电机峰值扭矩特性和电池放电能力约束，并协调能量回收制动与液压制动的分配。
The VCU is the decision centre of the EV powertrain: it reads the accelerator and brake pedals, gear, vehicle speed, battery and motor states, computes a motor torque command from driver intent and safety limits, and sends it to the motor controller. Torque demand weighs pedal position and rate, current speed, and the battery's allowable power, high torque for launch at low speed and reduced torque at high speed, while always respecting the motor's torque envelope and the battery's discharge capability, and it also arbitrates between regenerative and hydraulic braking.

什么是轮胎滑移率？为什么制动力在滑移率约15%至20%时达到最大？

What is the slip ratio of a tyre, and why does braking force peak at a slip ratio of about 15 to 20 percent?

滑移率指车轮在制动或驱动时实际车速与轮速的相对差，纯滚动为0，完全抱死为100%。轮胎与地面的纵向附着力随滑移率先上升后下降，在约15%至20%时出现峰值，之后进入滑动区，附着系数反而降低。ABS把滑移率控制在峰值附近，就是希望始终利用最大附着力，同时保留侧向力能力以维持转向稳定，避免车轮抱死。
Slip ratio is the relative difference between vehicle speed and wheel speed under braking or driving, 0 percent in pure rolling and 100 percent when locked. Longitudinal adhesion between tyre and road first rises with slip, peaks around 15 to 20 percent, then falls as the contact patch slides. ABS holds slip near this peak to use maximum adhesion while retaining lateral force for steering stability, precisely so that the wheels never lock.

为什么汽油机的热效率很难突破45%？主要能量损失在哪里？

Why is it so hard for a gasoline engine to exceed about 45 percent thermal efficiency, and where does the lost energy go?

奥托循环的理论效率由压缩比决定，但压缩比受爆震限制，加上汽油机空燃比控制在化学计量附近、泵气损失和部分负荷时节气门节流损失大，实际效率远低于理论值。能量去向包括排气带走的热、冷却系统散失的热、摩擦与泵气损失以及不完全燃烧损失。提高效率的路线包括稀薄燃烧、高压缩比加米勒循环、直喷和可变压缩比等。
The theoretical efficiency of the Otto cycle is set by the compression ratio, but knock limits how high the ratio can go, and stoichiometric air-fuel control, pumping losses and throttle losses at partial load push real efficiency far below the ideal. Energy is lost to exhaust heat, coolant heat, friction and pumping, and incomplete combustion. Efficiency routes include lean burn, high compression with the Miller cycle, direct injection and variable compression ratio.

磁悬浮列车为什么能"浮"起来跑，而且没有轮子？

Why can a maglev train float and run with no wheels?

磁悬浮利用电磁力对抗重力。常导型（EMS）靠车底电磁铁与轨道铁磁轨之间的吸引力把车体吸起来，悬浮间隙仅约1厘米，必须实时控制防止"吸死"；超导型（EDS）如日本L0列车，则用超导磁体在轨道线圈中感应出强大的排斥力，悬浮间隙更大且天然稳定。没有轮轨接触摩擦后，阻力只剩空气阻力，所以时速可达500公里以上，噪声和磨损也大幅下降。
Maglev uses electromagnetic force to beat gravity. Electromagnetic suspension (EMS) levitates by the attractive force between onboard magnets and ferromagnetic rails, holding a gap of only about 1 cm under real-time control to avoid contact. Superconducting EDS, as in Japan's L0, induces strong repulsion in track coils and floats with a larger, inherently stable gap. With no wheel-rail contact, the only drag left is air resistance, so speeds exceed 500 km/h with far less noise and wear.

电动汽车电池管理系统（BMS）的主要功能有哪些？为什么必须均衡管理？

What are the main functions of a battery management system (BMS) in an electric vehicle, and why is cell balancing necessary?

BMS负责采集每节电芯的电压、电流和温度，据此估算荷电状态（SOC）与健康状态（SOH），并在过充、过放、过温或过流时切断回路保护电池。由于制造差异和使用环境不同，电芯容量会逐渐离散，若不均衡，短板电芯会过早到达充放电边界而限制整个电池包，所以BMS通过被动或主动均衡使各电芯趋于一致，延长电池寿命。
A BMS measures per-cell voltage, current and temperature, estimates state of charge (SOC) and state of health (SOH), and disconnects the pack on overcharge, over-discharge, over-temperature or over-current. Because of manufacturing variance and uneven operating conditions, cell capacities drift apart over time; without balancing the weakest cell hits its charge or discharge limit early and limits the whole pack, so the BMS uses passive or active balancing to keep cells matched and extend battery life.

那就是绿波带：工程师把主干道上相邻交叉口的绿灯启亮时间按设计车速错开，让车队以建议时速行驶时正好一路绿灯。更先进的信号控制还能根据路口埋设的检测线圈或视频识别出的车流量，实时调整各相位的绿灯时长，这叫感应控制或自适应控制，可用来优先放行公交车、疏导拥堵。配时还要兼顾行人过街时间与路口间距，绿波不是"永远绿灯"，而是把等待集中在少数路口。
That is a green wave: engineers offset the green-start times of adjacent intersections along an arterial by the travel time at a design speed, so a platoon rolling at that speed hits green after green. Smarter systems use detection loops or video to measure traffic and re-time phase durations in real time — actuated or adaptive control — which can also prioritize buses and dissolve congestion. Timing must respect pedestrian crossing times and intersection spacing; a green wave does not mean always green, just that delay concentrates at a few intersections.

为什么电动汽车普遍采用永磁同步电机作为驱动电机？

Why do electric vehicles predominantly use permanent-magnet synchronous motors (PMSM) as traction motors?

永磁同步电机的转子由永磁体提供磁场，无需从定子吸取励磁电流，因而效率更高、功率密度更大，同样的体积和重量能输出更强的扭矩，直接有助于增加续航里程。它体积小、低速扭矩大，配合磁场定向控制能实现精准的转速与扭矩调节。主要缺点是稀土永磁材料（如钕铁硼）价格波动大、高温下有退磁风险。
An EV traction PMSM generates rotor flux from permanent magnets, so it draws no excitation current from the stator and offers higher efficiency and power density, more torque for a given size and weight, which directly benefits driving range. It is compact, delivers strong low-speed torque, and pairs with field-oriented control for precise torque and speed regulation. Its main drawbacks are the cost volatility of rare-earth magnet materials such as NdFeB and the risk of demagnetisation at high temperature.

汽车悬架的软硬调节为什么要在舒适性和操控性之间权衡？

Why must suspension tuning trade off ride comfort against handling?

悬架通过弹簧和减振器吸收路面冲击，软悬架能把振动过滤得更彻底，乘坐舒适但转弯时车身侧倾大、动态响应慢。硬悬架支撑好、转向反应快，但路面冲击直接传到车身，舒适性下降。减振器阻尼也需要平衡：阻尼过小车身余振不断，阻尼过大则冲击刚性传递，可变阻尼悬架正是为了在不同工况下动态切换这种权衡。
Suspension uses springs and dampers to absorb road impacts: soft springs filter vibration thoroughly for comfort but allow large body roll and slow responses in corners, while stiff springs support the body and give quick steering response at the cost of transmitting road shocks. Damper tuning is a similar balance, too little damping leaves the body oscillating and too much makes impacts feel harsh. Adaptive dampers exist precisely to shift this trade-off dynamically with driving conditions.

自动驾驶汽车是如何"看见"周围世界的？

How does a self-driving car "see" the world around it?

自动驾驶融合多种传感器：激光雷达发射激光束测距，得到三维点云；毫米波雷达利用多普勒效应在雨雾中测目标的距离与速度；摄像头提供车道线、交通标识、信号灯等语义信息。各路数据先经过时间同步和空间标定对齐，再交给感知算法做目标检测与跟踪。多传感器冗余是关键：摄像头怕逆光黑夜，毫米波雷达分辨不出低矮障碍，彼此互补才能在各种天气下可靠感知。
Self-driving cars fuse multiple sensors: lidar fires laser pulses to build 3-D point clouds, millimeter-wave radar uses the Doppler effect to measure distance and speed through rain and fog, and cameras supply semantic details like lane lines, signs, and traffic lights. The data streams are synchronized in time and aligned in space through calibration, then fed to perception algorithms for detection and tracking. Redundancy is key: cameras struggle in glare and darkness, radar misses low obstacles, so the sensors cover each other's weaknesses.

激光雷达和毫米波雷达在车载感知上各有什么优劣？

What are the strengths and weaknesses of automotive LiDAR versus millimetre-wave radar?

激光雷达通过发射激光脉冲测距并扫描得到高分辨率三维点云，能精确识别物体轮廓和位置，但成本高、在雨雪雾天气性能衰减明显。毫米波雷达直接测量距离和速度（利用多普勒效应），对雨雾穿透力强、测速准、成本低，但角度分辨率低，难以分辨行人等小目标。因此两者常与摄像头互补使用，各取所长。
LiDAR emits laser pulses and scans to build high-resolution 3D point clouds, accurately resolving object shapes and positions, but it is expensive and degrades badly in rain, snow and fog. Millimetre-wave radar directly measures range and velocity via the Doppler effect, penetrates rain and fog well, is accurate for speed and cheap, but offers low angular resolution and struggles to distinguish small objects such as pedestrians. The two are therefore usually fused with cameras so each covers the others' weaknesses.

为什么电动汽车需要电池热管理系统？它如何保证电池在低温下也能正常工作？

Why do electric vehicles need a battery thermal management system, and how does it keep the battery working in cold weather?

锂离子电池的最佳工作温度通常在20至40摄氏度，高温会加速老化甚至引发热失控，低温则使内阻增大、可用容量和充电功率大幅下降。热管理系统通过液冷或风冷回路、加热膜或热泵实现加热与冷却：高温时把热量带出电池包，低温时先加热再允许大电流充电，使电池始终处于适宜温度区间，既保障安全也维持性能。
Lithium-ion batteries work best around 20 to 40 degrees Celsius: heat accelerates ageing and can trigger thermal runaway, while cold raises internal resistance and sharply cuts usable capacity and charge power. The thermal management system heats and cools through liquid or air loops, heating pads or heat pumps: it rejects heat from the pack in summer, and preheats before fast charging in winter, keeping cells in the optimal window for both safety and performance.

天空中那么多飞机，为什么不会相撞？

With so many planes in the sky, why do they never collide?

民航客机大多在仪表飞行规则下由空中交通管制（ATC）统一指挥。飞机沿指定的航路飞行，航路在水平方向和垂直方向都互相隔开（垂直间隔通常约300米，雷达管制下还可缩小），管制员用雷达监视并下达指令保证间隔。飞机还装有应答机广播自己的高度和呼号，机载防撞系统（TCAS）在间隔过小时会自动给出爬升或下降指令。起飞降落阶段则由塔台按顺序和间隔放行，跑道时刻精确到秒级。
Commercial flights mostly fly under instrument flight rules controlled by air traffic control (ATC). Aircraft follow defined airways that are separated both laterally and vertically — vertical separation is typically about 300 m, reduced under radar control — and controllers monitor radar and issue instructions to keep spacing. Aircraft also broadcast their altitude and identity via transponders, and the TCAS collision-avoidance system automatically commands climb or descend when separation gets too tight. At takeoff and landing, tower controllers sequence departures and arrivals to intervals timed in seconds.

为什么高铁车轮不是平的，而是带一点"锥度"？

Why are train wheels slightly cone-shaped instead of flat?

车轮踏面做成锥形是为了让列车自动对中转弯。轮对是左右两轮固定在一根轴上的，过弯时外轮走的长轨、内轮走短轨，若两轮滚动半径相同就会打滑。锥形踏面让车体在弯道向一侧偏移时，外侧车轮的滚动半径变大、内侧变小，两轮转速自动匹配内外轨长度，实现自导向。这个几何特性也正是高铁"蛇行运动"的来源，需要靠踏面磨耗管理和主动稳定来控制。
Conical wheel treads let a trainset self-center on curves. A wheelset has two wheels fixed on one axle; in a curve the outer wheel travels a longer rail than the inner, and equal rolling radii would cause slipping. With a coned profile, when the set shifts sideways the outer wheel rides a larger rolling radius and the inner a smaller one, so the wheels automatically match the two rail lengths and steer themselves. The same geometry is what produces high-speed "hunting" oscillation, controlled through tread wear management and active stability.

为什么高速公路的弯道要向外倾斜？

Why are highway curves banked outward?

弯道路面倾斜后，车辆转弯时路面的支撑力有一部分指向圆心，帮忙提供转弯所需的向心力，减少靠轮胎摩擦力硬扛，于是过弯更稳、更不容易侧滑。
Banking tilts the road so part of the normal force points toward the center, helping supply the centripetal force for turning and reducing reliance on tire friction, making cornering steadier and less prone to sliding.
