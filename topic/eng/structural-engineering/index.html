什么是有限元在结构分析中的作用？

What is the role of finite elements in structural analysis?

有限元法把复杂结构划分为网格单元，逐单元求解位移和应力后再拼合成整体响应。它能处理不规则几何、非均匀材料和复杂边界条件，是现代结构分析软件的核心。
The finite element method divides a complex structure into a mesh of elements, solves displacement and stress per element, then assembles the global response. It handles irregular geometry, non-uniform materials, and complex boundaries, forming the core of modern structural analysis software.

什么是屈曲，细长柱为什么容易失稳？

What is buckling and why do slender columns fail by instability?

屈曲是受压细长构件在远未达到材料强度时，因侧向挠曲而突然丧失承载能力的现象。欧拉临界荷载公式表明，柱的临界力与长度平方成反比、与刚度成正比，所以越细长越容易失稳。
Buckling is the sudden loss of load capacity of a compressed slender member through lateral bowing, long before material strength is reached. Euler's critical-load formula shows buckling load is inversely proportional to length squared and proportional to stiffness, so the slimmer the column, the easier it buckles.

为什么固定式导管架平台的基本自振周期必须明显低于风暴海况中波浪能量的主要周期范围？

Why must the fundamental period of a fixed steel-jacket platform lie well below the period range where storm-wave energy is concentrated?

风暴与涌浪的能量集中在周期约6–15秒的波段。若导管架自振周期落入其中，接续到来的波峰波谷会激起共振，动力放大系数陡增，构件应力与疲劳损伤都难以控制。因此设计上通过增强撑杆刚度、限制甲板组块质量，把基本周期压缩到约3–5秒，令波浪载荷接近准静态作用；动力效应减小后，疲劳谱分析与极端波强度校核才有可靠依据。
Storm-sea energy concentrates at wave periods of roughly 6–15 s. If the jacket's natural period fell inside that band, arriving crests and troughs would excite resonance, multiplying dynamic stresses and fatigue damage beyond control. Designers therefore stiffen the bracing and cap topside mass to push the fundamental period down to about 3–5 s, so wave loads act almost quasi-statically. With dynamic amplification suppressed, spectral fatigue analysis and extreme-wave strength checks rest on predictable behaviour.

张力腿平台依靠什么原理把垂荡、纵摇和横摇约束到极小幅度？系索张力与平台的重量和浮力之间是什么关系？

By what principle does a tension leg platform restrain heave, pitch, and roll to very small amplitudes, and how are tendon tensions related to the platform's weight and buoyancy?

张力腿平台的浮力大于其总重，多余净浮力由竖直系索承受，使各系索恒处于张紧状态。拉紧的系索把平台锚定于海面下：垂荡、纵摇、横摇的固有周期被压到约2-4秒，避开海浪能量集中周期而不共振；纵荡、横荡由系索水平分力提供恢复力，运动平缓。单根系索失效会引发张力重分配，设计须校核冗余与极限海况。
A tension leg platform is designed with buoyancy exceeding its total weight; the excess buoyancy is carried by vertical tendons, keeping every tendon permanently in tension. The taut tendons anchor the platform below the sea surface like stiff springs: the natural periods of heave, pitch, and roll are pushed down to roughly 2-4 s, below the band where most wave energy lies, so resonance is avoided. Surge and sway are restored by horizontal components of tendon tension and remain slow and moderate. Losing one tendon redistributes the loads, so designs must verify redundancy under extreme sea states.

什么是隔震技术？

What is seismic isolation technology?

隔震是在建筑基础和上部结构之间设置橡胶支座等柔性层，延长结构周期、削弱地震能量向上传递。它能显著降低地震时楼内的晃动，保护人员和设备。
Seismic isolation places flexible layers such as rubber bearings between a building's foundation and its superstructure, lengthening its period and reducing upward transmission of quake energy. It markedly cuts interior shaking and protects people and equipment.

什么是结构的自振频率和共振？

What are natural frequency and resonance of a structure?

自振频率是结构在无外力时自由振动的固有频率，由质量和刚度决定。当外部激励频率接近自振频率时会发生共振，振幅急剧增大，可能导致破坏，因此桥梁和高楼要避开共振。
Natural frequency is the rate at which a structure vibrates freely on its own, set by its mass and stiffness. When external excitation nears this frequency, resonance occurs and amplitude grows sharply, risking damage, so bridges and towers are designed to avoid it.

什么是弯矩和剪力？

What are bending moment and shear force?

弯矩是使构件弯曲的内力矩，剪力是使截面错动的内力。工程师绘制弯矩图和剪力图来判断梁在何处受力最大，从而决定截面尺寸。
Bending moment is the internal moment that bends a member, while shear force is the internal force that slides one section past another. Engineers draw moment and shear diagrams to find where a beam is most stressed and size its cross-section.

什么是桁架结构？

What is a truss structure?

桁架由许多细长杆件在节点铰接组成，荷载主要通过杆件轴向受拉或受压来传递。它重量轻、刚度大，常用于屋顶、塔架和大跨桥梁。
A truss is made of slender members pinned at joints, carrying loads mainly through axial tension or compression in the bars. Light and stiff, it is used in roofs, towers, and long-span bridges.

什么是悬索桥？

What is a suspension bridge?

悬索桥用主缆悬挂在塔架上，再由吊索把桥面吊起，主缆的两端锚固在地面。它能以较轻的结构跨越很大的距离，是世界上跨度最大的桥型之一。
A suspension bridge hangs its deck from main cables draped over towers, with the cable ends anchored to the ground. It spans very long distances with a relatively light structure and is among the longest bridge types in the world.

为什么很多桥是拱形的？

Why are many bridges arched?

拱形能把桥面受到的重力沿着弧线传向两端的桥墩，主要承受压力。石头和砖这类抗压强的材料做拱桥特别合适，所以拱桥既稳固又省料。
An arch directs the weight on the deck along its curve to the supports at both ends, mainly under compression. Stone and brick resist compression well, so arch bridges are both stable and material-efficient.

什么是梁，它在建筑中起什么作用？

What is a beam and what role does it play in buildings?

梁是承受横向荷载、主要发生弯曲的细长结构构件，常见于楼层和桥梁。它把屋顶和楼板的重量传递到柱子和墙体，是建筑骨架的重要部分。
A beam is a slender structural member that carries transverse loads and bends, common in floors and bridges. It transfers the weight of roofs and slabs to columns and walls and is a key part of a building's skeleton.

什么是剪力墙？

What is a shear wall?

剪力墙是建筑中抵抗水平力（如风力和地震）的竖向墙体，常用钢筋混凝土做成。它像一道坚固的挡板，能防止高楼在侧向力下过度晃动和倒塌。
A shear wall is a vertical wall, usually reinforced concrete, that resists horizontal forces like wind and earthquakes. Acting like a stiff shield, it stops tall buildings from swaying or collapsing under lateral loads.
