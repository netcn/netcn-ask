城市供水管网爆管停水，抢修时为什么要先关一大片阀门？

When a water main bursts, why close a whole district of valves to fix it?

为隔离漏点必须把上下游阀门关掉形成可控区段，避免整网失压；关阀范围越大影响越多，所以要靠分区计量精准定位。
Crews isolate the break by closing valves to a district to avoid system-wide pressure loss, balancing repair access against outages.

What is an urban utility tunnel and why bundle pipes in one big tunnel?

把电力、通信、给水、热力等管线集中放进可通行的隧道，便于检修和扩容、减少反复开膛破路；造价高但寿命长。
It bundles utilities in a walkable tunnel for easy maintenance and expansion, cutting repeated road digging despite high cost.

Why do tall buildings need secondary water tanks or pumps?

City pressure only reaches lower floors; tall buildings need tanks and variable-frequency pumps to boost, with cleaning vital to avoid contamination.

Why are footbridges usually steel rather than concrete?
