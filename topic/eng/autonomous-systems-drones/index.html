把强化学习用于机器人控制时，仿真到现实的域差距为何会成为部署瓶颈？

In applying reinforcement learning to robot control, why does the simulation-to-reality domain gap become a deployment bottleneck?

强化策略多在仿真中训练，而仿真与真实在动力学、摩擦、传感器噪声上存在差异，学到的策略一旦上机容易失效或失稳。常用域随机化（在仿真中加随机扰动）、系统辨识与真实数据微调来弥合差距，但这些方法仍难完全覆盖现实多样性。
RL policies are mostly trained in simulation, yet sim and reality differ in dynamics, friction, and sensor noise, so learned policies often fail or destabilize on real hardware. Domain randomization, system identification, and real-data fine-tuning bridge the gap, but they still struggle to cover the full diversity of the real world.

在室内或城市峡谷等 GPS 拒止环境中，无人机靠什么实现自主定位？

In GPS-denied environments such as indoors or urban canyons, what does a drone rely on for autonomous localization?

此时依赖自身传感器：视觉惯性里程计用相机与 IMU 估计相对位姿，激光雷达或 UWB 信标提供绝对参照，配合地图匹配修正漂移。多传感器紧耦合融合可在无卫星信号时维持连续定位，但对计算资源与场景特征丰富度有要求。
It then relies on onboard sensing: visual-inertial odometry fuses camera and IMU for relative pose, while LiDAR or UWB beacons supply absolute references, with map matching correcting drift. Tightly coupled multi-sensor fusion maintains continuous localization without satellites but demands compute and feature-rich scenes.

同时定位与建图（SLAM）中的闭环检测为什么对长期定位精度至关重要？

Why is loop closure detection critical to long-term accuracy in simultaneous localization and mapping (SLAM)?

SLAM 通过递推估计机器人位姿，误差会随路径累积漂移；闭环检测在重访已建区域时识别出重复场景，用约束把累积误差全局修正回零点。没有闭环，长期运行的地图会严重失真，因此它是实现一致、可复用地图的关键。
SLAM estimates pose recursively, so error drifts cumulatively along the path; loop closure recognizes a revisited area and applies a constraint that corrects accumulated error globally back to zero. Without it, long runs yield severely distorted maps, so it is key to consistent, reusable mapping.

垂直起降（VTOL）飞行器相比固定翼在任务适应性上有什么优势与代价？

What are the advantages and costs of vertical take-off and landing (VTOL) aircraft versus fixed-wing in mission flexibility?

VTOL 无需跑道即可起降，适合狭小平台与城市空运；但悬停时升力完全由旋翼提供、气动效率远低于巡航，导致能耗高、航时航程受限。近年电动垂直起降（eVTOL）通过分布式推进试图在续航与便捷间折中。
VTOL needs no runway, suiting confined sites and urban air mobility, but during hover all lift comes from rotors at far lower aerodynamic efficiency than cruise, raising energy use and limiting endurance and range. Electric VTOL (eVTOL) with distributed propulsion seeks a compromise between range and convenience.

自动驾驶中激光雷达（LiDAR）和摄像头在环境感知上如何互补？

How do LiDAR and cameras complement each other in autonomous-driving perception?

摄像头提供丰富的纹理与颜色信息，擅长识别车道、标志与分类，但在暗光和距离估计上较弱；激光雷达直接给出高精度三维点云与距离，对光照不敏感，却缺乏语义细节。两者融合才能获得既测距准确又语义完整的场景理解。
Cameras give rich texture and color, excelling at lane, sign, and object classification but weak in low light and ranging; LiDAR yields high-precision 3D point clouds and distances independent of lighting but lacks semantic detail. Fusing both yields scene understanding that is both metrically accurate and semantically complete.

无人机集群通常用什么通信与决策方式实现协同？

What communication and decision-making approaches do drone swarms typically use to coordinate?

集群多采用去中心化的相对定位与局部通信，每个单元依据邻居状态与简单规则（如分离、对齐、聚合）自行决策，整体涌现出编队、覆盖或围捕等行为。相比集中控制，这种方式对单点失效更鲁棒，也更易扩展规模。
Swarms typically use decentralized relative positioning and local communication, where each unit decides from neighbors' states via simple rules such as separation, alignment, and cohesion, and the group emerges behaviors like formation, coverage, or encirclement. Versus centralized control, this is more robust to single-point failure and easier to scale.

A* 路径规划算法相比简单的广度优先搜索，靠什么机制更高效？

Compared with plain breadth-first search, what mechanism makes the A* path-planning algorithm more efficient?

A* 在扩展节点时除记录已走代价 g 外，还用启发函数 h 估计到目标的剩余代价，优先扩展 f=g+h 最小的节点。借助合理的启发（如欧氏距离），它能在保证找到最优路径的同时大幅减少搜索范围，比盲目搜索更高效。
A* tracks the cost-so-far g for each node and adds a heuristic h estimating remaining cost to the goal, expanding nodes with the smallest f=g+h first. With a suitable heuristic such as Euclidean distance, it finds the optimal path while pruning the search space far more than blind search.

自主系统中传感器融合主要解决什么问题？

What main problem does sensor fusion solve in autonomous systems?

单一传感器各有盲区与噪声，融合把多个异源传感器的观测按统计最优方式结合，得到比任一单独传感器更可靠、更完整的状态估计。卡尔曼滤波及其扩展是经典工具，能在噪声与缺失下持续给出位置、速度等关键信息。
Each single sensor has blind spots and noise; fusion combines heterogeneous observations in a statistically optimal way to yield more reliable and complete state estimates than any sensor alone. The Kalman filter and its variants are classic tools, continuously providing position, velocity, and other key states under noise and dropouts.
