火箭喷管为什么做成拉瓦尔喷管（先收窄后扩张）的形状？

Why is a rocket nozzle shaped like a converging-diverging (de Laval) nozzle?

燃气在喉部被加速到音速，之后在扩张段继续膨胀加速到超音速，把热压高效转化为向前喷出的高速气流，从而获得更大推力。
Gas reaches the speed of sound at the throat and then expands in the diverging section to supersonic speed, efficiently converting pressure into a fast forward jet for greater thrust.

火箭上的 POGO 振动是怎么产生的，为什么它能危及整枚箭？

What causes rocket pogo oscillation, and why can it endanger an entire vehicle?

POGO 是结构与推进系统构成的闭环耦合：箭体纵向弹性振动使推进剂管路入口压力波动，泵入口压力变化引起推力脉动，推力脉动又反过来激励箭体纵向模态，若相位形成正反馈，振幅可在几秒内急剧增长。土星五号第一级和双子星-泰坦飞行中都出现过明显纵向振荡，严重时会损坏结构、破坏发动机或让乘员无法操作。典型抑制手段是在氧化剂或燃料管路上加装充气蓄压器（POGO 抑制器），改变管路的等效柔度与谐振频率，把推进系统响应与结构模态解耦。
Pogo is a closed loop between structure and propulsion: longitudinal elastic vibration modulates propellant pressure at the pump inlet, that inlet variation modulates thrust, and the thrust pulsation re-excites the longitudinal mode, so amplitude can grow explosively within seconds when the phase relationship is regenerative. Saturn V first-stage and Gemini-Titan flights both showed pronounced longitudinal oscillation, which at high amplitude can damage structure, wreck engines or leave the crew unable to operate controls. The standard fix is a gas-filled accumulator in the oxidizer or fuel feedline that retunes the line's effective compliance and resonance, decoupling propulsion response from the structural mode.

飞船着陆时，为什么有时用降落伞、有时用反推火箭？

Why do spacecraft use parachutes for landing in some cases but retro-rockets in others?

降落伞靠大气阻力减速，只适用于有稠密大气的行星（如地球、火星）；在没有大气或需要精准软着陆的地方（如月球），只能用反推火箭向下喷气来减速。
Parachutes rely on atmospheric drag, so they work only where there is thick air like Earth or Mars; with no atmosphere or for precise soft landings such as on the Moon, retro-rockets firing downward are required.

什么是超音速激波，它对飞机有何影响？

What is a supersonic shock wave and how does it affect aircraft?

当物体速度超过音速时，前方的压力扰动无法及时散开，会叠加形成突然的压强、温度和密度跳变的激波。激波带来强烈的波阻和气动加热，并要求机体结构承受额外载荷。
When an object exceeds the speed of sound, pressure disturbances ahead cannot disperse in time and pile up into a shock wave with abrupt jumps in pressure, temperature, and density. Shock waves cause strong wave drag and aerodynamic heating and demand extra structural strength.

什么是气动加热，高超音速飞行时为何机身前缘温度急剧上升？

What is aerodynamic heating and why do leading edges get extremely hot at hypersonic speed?

高超音速下，飞行器前方空气被强烈压缩且粘性摩擦把动能转化为热能，边界层温度随马赫数平方量级升高。最尖锐的前缘驻点处气流几乎停滞，动能全部转为热能，因此温度最高。
At hypersonic speed, compressed and viscous air converts kinetic energy to heat, with boundary-layer temperature rising roughly with Mach squared. At the sharp leading-edge stagnation point the flow nearly stops, dumping all kinetic energy into heat, so it gets hottest there.

火箭发动机的“分级燃烧循环”为什么比简单燃气发生器循环效率更高，代价是什么？

Why is a staged-combustion rocket cycle more efficient than a simple gas-generator cycle, and what is the cost?

燃气发生器把部分推进剂烧完就排掉，白白损失能量；分级燃烧把所有富燃燃气都送回主燃烧室充分燃烧，比冲更高。代价是极高温高压的预燃室和涡轮，结构复杂、制造苛刻。
A gas generator burns some propellant just to dump it, wasting energy; staged combustion sends all fuel-rich gas back to the main chamber to burn fully, raising specific impulse. The cost is very hot, high-pressure preburners and turbines—complex and demanding to build.

探测器去火星时为什么要先"绕远路"而不是直线飞过去？

Why do Mars probes take a long elliptical route instead of flying straight there?

关闭发动机后的探测器只在太阳引力下飞行，轨迹是以太阳为焦点的椭圆轨道。霍曼转移取一个近日点在地球轨道、远日点在火星轨道的椭圆：第一次点火在近地轨道加速进入转移椭圆，抵达远日点后再点火把轨道圆化成火星轨道。虽然是绕远路，但这两次点火所需的速度增量总和最小，能省下大量燃料。代价是飞行时间长达6到9个月，而且发射窗口每26个月才出现一次，因为地球与火星的相对位置必须合适。
Once its engines shut off, a probe flies only under solar gravity along an ellipse with the Sun at one focus. A Hohmann transfer picks an ellipse whose perihelion touches Earth's orbit and aphelion touches Mars': the first burn accelerates the probe onto the ellipse, and at aphelion a second burn circularizes it into Mars orbit. It is the long way around, but the sum of the two burns' velocity changes (delta-v) is the minimum possible, saving enormous fuel. The cost is 6-9 months of flight time, and launch windows recur only every 26 months because Earth and Mars must be in the right relative positions.

飞船以每秒7.6公里的速度再入大气层，为什么没有被烧成灰烬？

Re-entering at 7.6 km/s, why doesn't a spacecraft burn to ash?

再入时的极端热量主要不是来自摩擦，而是激波压缩：飞行器以高超音速挤压前方空气，弓形激波后的空气被绝热压缩到几千摄氏度，高温气体再对飞船表面加热。航天飞机的陶瓷隔热瓦靠极低的热导率把热量挡在外面；载人飞船则用烧蚀热防护——酚醛树脂等材料表面熔化、气化时吸热并带走热量，烧蚀层自身不断"牺牲"以保护内部。从水星、阿波罗到神舟都采用烧蚀方案，它简单可靠，只是不能重复使用。
The extreme heat of re-entry comes mainly not from friction but from shock compression: at hypersonic speed the vehicle shoves air ahead of it, and behind the bow shock the air is adiabatically compressed to thousands of degrees, then heats the surface. The Space Shuttle's silica tiles block heat with very low thermal conductivity; crew capsules instead use ablative shielding — phenolic resin chars, melts, and vaporizes, absorbing and carrying heat away while the ablator sacrifices itself to protect the interior. From Mercury and Apollo to Shenzhou, capsules chose ablation: simple and reliable, though not reusable.

什么是“气动弹性”，为什么飞机机翼在高速时既要够硬又不能太硬？

What is aeroelasticity, and why must a wing at high speed be stiff yet not too stiff?

气动弹性研究气动力与结构柔度的耦合：太软会颤振发散，太硬则重且把载荷硬传给机身。设计师在重量、刚度和气动效率间找平衡，避开危险的共振频率。
Aeroelasticity studies coupling of aerodynamic force and structural flexibility: too soft invites flutter divergence, too stiff adds weight and harsh load transfer. Designers balance weight, stiffness and efficiency while avoiding dangerous resonant frequencies.

喷气式发动机是怎样产生推力的？

How does a jet engine produce thrust?

发动机吸入空气，压缩后与燃料燃烧膨胀，从尾部高速喷出；根据反作用力，向后喷出的气体把飞机向前推，这和火箭、划船是一个道理。
It sucks in air, compresses it, burns fuel to expand the gas, and blasts it out the back at high speed; the reaction pushes the plane forward, the same principle as a rocket or rowing.

旅行者号飞了二百多亿公里，信号为什么还能传回地球？

How can Voyager's signal still reach Earth from over 20 billion kilometers away?

深空通信靠窄波束射电和极高的接收灵敏度。旅行者号以约8千兆赫的X波段发射约20瓦信号，信号按距离平方衰减，到达地球时功率已低至约10的负19次方瓦，但NASA直径70米的大天线把零星光子一点点收集起来，再靠信道编码纠错把信噪比极低的信号"救"回来。由于单程信号要飞二十多个小时，地面还必须用深空测控网（DSN）分布在三大洲的天线接力跟踪。
Deep-space links use narrow radio beams and extreme receiver sensitivity. Voyager transmits about 20 watts at X-band near 8 GHz; the signal fades as the square of distance and arrives at Earth at roughly 1e-19 watts, yet NASA's 70-meter antennas accumulate those few photons, and channel coding and error correction recover data from a signal far below the noise floor. With a one-way light time of over twenty hours, ground stations must also hand the spacecraft over across the three Deep Space Network (DSN) sites as Earth rotates.

离子推进器的推力只有几十毫牛，为什么深空任务还偏爱它？

Ion thrusters produce only tens of millinewtons of thrust, so why do deep-space missions favour them?

电推进用电场把氙等工质加速到 20–40 km/s 的排气速度，比化学火箭高一个数量级，按齐奥尔科夫斯基公式，获得同样速度增量所需的推进剂质量因此大幅减少，比冲可达 3000 s 以上。推力虽小，却能连续工作数千小时乃至数年，把微小加速度累积成几公里每秒的速度增量，非常适合无阻力环境下的深空巡航与卫星位置保持。代价是必须携带大功率太阳能电池阵和电源处理单元，加速缓慢、无法用于快速逃逸机动，栅极溅射侵蚀还限制寿命。
Electric propulsion accelerates xenon or similar propellant electrostatically to exhaust speeds of 20–40 km/s, an order of magnitude above chemical rockets, so by the Tsiolkovsky equation the propellant mass for a given velocity change drops sharply and specific impulse exceeds 3000 s. Thrust is tiny but can run for thousands of hours or years, accumulating small accelerations into several km/s of delta-v, which suits drag-free deep-space cruise and satellite station keeping. The costs are large solar arrays and power processing units, slow acceleration unusable for quick escape manoeuvres, and grid erosion by ion sputtering limiting operating life.

喷气发动机和火箭发动机有什么不同？

What is the difference between a jet engine and a rocket engine?

喷气发动机从大气中吸入空气作为氧化剂来燃烧燃料，因此只能在有空气处工作。火箭发动机自带氧化剂，不依赖空气，所以可用于太空飞行。
A jet engine draws air from the atmosphere as oxidizer to burn fuel, so it only works where air exists. A rocket carries its own oxidizer and does not depend on air, enabling it to operate in space.

How do satellites 'call' each other (inter-satellite links), and why lets them form a global constellation without ground stations?

卫星用激光或微波直接互相对接通信，数据在太空里一跳跳转发，不必每颗都连地面。这样即使地面站少、极地海洋也能联网，延时更低、覆盖更稳。
Satellites link directly by laser or microwave and relay data hop-by-hop in space without each touching ground. Even with few ground stations, polar oceans get connected, with lower latency and steadier coverage.

火箭在真空中没有空气可"推"，为什么还能加速？

With no air to push against in space, how can a rocket still accelerate?

火箭靠动量守恒获得推力，不依赖任何外部介质。燃料在燃烧室内反应生成高温高压燃气，经喷管加速后高速喷出，喷出的气体带走向后的动量；根据动量守恒，火箭必然获得等大反向的动量增量，于是向前加速。推力等于每秒喷出的质量乘以排气速度，喷管做成先收敛后扩张的形状，正是为了把燃气加速到超音速、最大化排气速度。
A rocket accelerates by conserving momentum, needing no external medium. Fuel reacts in the chamber into hot high-pressure gas, which the nozzle accelerates and ejects rearward; the expelled gas carries backward momentum, and momentum conservation forces the rocket to gain an equal and opposite forward momentum. Thrust equals the mass flow rate times the exhaust velocity, and the convergent-divergent nozzle exists precisely to push the gas past the speed of sound and maximize that velocity.

Where do oxygen and water on a space station come from?

飞机为什么能飞起来？

Why can an airplane fly?

飞机机翼上表面弯曲、下表面较平，气流经过时机翼上方流速更快、压强更低，从而产生向上的升力。更准确地说，机翼的迎角与环量使气流被向下偏转，根据牛顿第三定律产生反作用升力；当升力超过飞机重力时，飞机就能离地升空。
An airplane wing is curved on top and flatter below, so air moves faster over the top and creates lower pressure there, producing upward lift. More precisely, the wing's angle of attack and the circulation around it deflect the airflow downward; by Newton's third law this produces a reaction force. When lift exceeds the plane's weight, the aircraft can leave the ground.

火箭靠什么推进？

What propels a rocket?

火箭依靠向后高速喷出气体，根据作用力与反作用力原理获得向前的推力。它自带燃料和氧化剂，因此不需要空气也能在太空中工作。
A rocket is propelled by ejecting gas backward at high speed, gaining forward thrust by Newton's action-reaction principle. It carries both fuel and oxidizer, so it works in space where there is no air.

火箭整流罩是干什么的？

What is a rocket fairing for?

整流罩在发射初期包裹并保护卫星等有效载荷，使其免受气动加热与声振损伤，飞出稠密大气层后抛掉以减轻重量。
The fairing encloses and protects the payload from aerodynamic heating and vibration during ascent, then is jettisoned after leaving the dense atmosphere.

卫星主要靠什么供电？

What mainly powers a satellite?

大多数卫星展开太阳能帆板将太阳光转为电能，并配蓄电池在阴影期（如地影）继续供电，少数深空探测器用放射性同位素电源。
Most satellites deploy solar arrays and store energy in batteries for eclipse periods; a few deep-space probes use radioisotope power instead.
