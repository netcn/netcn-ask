变频空调为什么比定频空调省电，它凭什么不用频繁开停？

Why does an inverter air conditioner use less energy than a fixed-speed one that keeps cycling?

定频压缩机只能全速运行或停机，靠反复开停维持温度，每次重启都要重新建立油膜与压差、承受启动电流冲击，停机期间制冷剂迁移还带来额外损失，室温也在设定值上下摆动。变频机通过改变供给压缩机的电源频率连续调节转速，长期在部分负荷下低速运行——此时换热面积相对过剩，蒸发温度升高、冷凝温度降低，压缩比减小，能效比反而高于满负荷。因此季节能效指标（如 APF、SEER）明显优于定频机，代价是电子器件更多，变频器本身也有几个百分点的损耗。
A fixed-speed compressor can only run flat out or stop, so it cycles to hold temperature, and every restart must re-establish oil films and pressure difference, endure inrush current and recover refrigerant migration losses while room temperature swings around the setpoint. An inverter varies the frequency fed to the compressor and hence its speed, letting it run slowly at part load where the heat exchangers are effectively oversized, evaporating temperature rises, condensing temperature falls, pressure ratio drops and the coefficient of performance actually exceeds the full-load value. Seasonal metrics such as APF or SEER therefore favour inverter units clearly, at the price of more electronics and a few percent of loss in the drive itself.

风冷『无霜』冰箱其实也会结霜，它是怎么把霜化掉又不让食物变热的？

Frost-free refrigerators do form frost — how do they melt it off without warming the food?

风冷冰箱把蒸发器藏在箱体后部的风道里，风机吹动箱内空气经过蒸发器降温除湿，水汽全部凝结在这块看不见的翅片上，所以食品间看不到霜。控制器按压缩机累计运行时间或化霜传感器判断结霜量，定期停止制冷并接通蒸发器上的电加热丝几分钟，融化的水沿排水槽流到压缩机上方的接水盘，靠压缩机余热蒸发掉。化霜期间压缩机与风机停转，箱体保温使内部只升温几度且时间很短，随后立即恢复制冷；代价是电加热与循环风机带来额外能耗，箱内空气偏干使不密封的食物更容易失水。
A frost-free unit hides its evaporator in an air duct behind the cabinet, and a fan draws cabinet air across those cold fins so all moisture condenses there instead of on food shelves. A controller decides from accumulated compressor run time or a defrost sensor when to pause cooling and switch on an electric heater at the evaporator for a few minutes; meltwater drains to a pan above the compressor and evaporates using its waste heat. During defrost the compressor and fan stop while the insulated cabinet limits warming to a few degrees for a short time before cooling resumes, the costs being extra energy for heater and circulating fan and drier air that desiccates unwrapped food.

电风扇吹着好凉快，可房间里的温度并没有降下来，这是为什么？

A fan feels wonderfully cool, yet the room temperature does not drop — why?

风扇只是把空气搅动起来，它自己的电动机还在把电变成热，房间温度其实会略微上升。人觉得凉，是因为流动的空气把皮肤旁边被体温烘热的空气层吹走，并加快汗液蒸发，而蒸发要带走大量热量，皮肤的散热效率就提高了。所以风扇在闷热、汗不容易蒸发的时候最管用；空调则是真的把屋里的热搬到室外去，温度计上的数字才会往下走。
A fan only stirs the air and its motor even adds a little heat, so room temperature edges up rather than down. People feel cooler because moving air sweeps away the warm film of air hugging the skin and speeds up sweat evaporation, and evaporation carries off a lot of heat, raising the skin's cooling rate. That is why a fan helps most in muggy stillness, whereas an air conditioner genuinely moves heat out of the room, which is what makes the thermometer fall.

Why do LEDs save power and last longer than old bulbs?

白炽灯靠灯丝发热发光，约九成能量变热；LED用电致发光直接出光、发热少，同样亮度用电更少、寿命更长。
Incandescents waste about 90% as heat; LEDs emit light directly with little heat, so they use less power and last longer.

What makes lamps light up when we flip the switch?

Tiny electric helpers live inside wires. Flip the switch and the road opens: they race through the bulb's thin filament, which glows hot with light. Flip again, the door closes and the lamp sleeps. Never put fingers in sockets!

When a washer spins, why don't clothes fly out while water is thrown away?
