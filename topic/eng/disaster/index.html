切尔诺贝利事故的根本原因是什么？反应堆设计和操作上存在哪些致命缺陷？

What was the root cause of the Chernobyl disaster, and what fatal flaws existed in both the reactor design and its operation?

切尔诺贝利用的是苏联RBMK型反应堆，它有一个致命设计缺陷，即正空泡系数：冷却水汽化形成气泡反而会增加反应性，功率越高这种正反馈越强。事故当晚操作员在计划外的低功率、低水流量状态下试验，把安全棒大部分抽出，加上操纵员干预跳闸保护，导致反应堆进入功率快速攀升的正反馈状态，瞬间功率飙升引发蒸汽爆炸，摧毁堆芯并掀翻堆顶。大量放射性物质泄漏，成为人类历史上最严重的核事故，事故分析和IAEA的报告深刻改变了核安全理念。
Chernobyl used a Soviet RBMK reactor with a fatal design flaw, a positive void coefficient: when coolant water boils into steam bubbles, reactivity increases, and the feedback strengthens with power. On the night of the accident operators ran an unapproved test at low power and low water flow, withdrawing most of the control rods and overriding trip protection, pushing the reactor into a rapidly escalating positive-feedback state. Power surged out of control, causing a steam explosion that destroyed the core and blew the lid off the reactor building, releasing huge amounts of radioactivity. The accident and IAEA analyses fundamentally reshaped nuclear safety philosophy.

大坝、核电站的设计地震动为什么要用概率地震危险性分析（PSHA）来确定？

Why is the design ground motion for dams and nuclear plants derived from probabilistic seismic hazard analysis?

确定性做法只取某条断层的最大可信地震，无法回答这种地震动多久遇到一次，也难以综合多条潜在震源和场地条件的联合影响。PSHA 把震源几何、震级—频度关系、地震动衰减模型及其不确定性组合起来积分，给出场地在给定时间内超越某一加速度水平的年超越概率，即危险性曲线，Cornell 于 1968 年提出了这一框架。工程上据此按后果严重程度选取重现期——一般建筑常用 475 年，重大设施可到数千甚至上万年——并通过危险性解耦找出主控震级与距离，用于挑选输入地震时程。
A deterministic maximum credible earthquake on one fault cannot say how often such motion occurs, nor combine the joint influence of many potential sources and site conditions. PSHA integrates source geometry, magnitude-frequency relations, ground-motion attenuation models and their uncertainties to yield the annual rate at which a site exceeds a given acceleration — the hazard curve — a framework introduced by Cornell in 1968. Engineers then choose a return period according to consequence, typically 475 years for ordinary buildings and up to thousands or tens of thousands of years for critical facilities, and use hazard deaggregation to identify the controlling magnitude and distance for selecting input time histories.

深水地平线（墨西哥湾）漏油事故中，防喷器（BOP）为什么没能阻止井喷？

Why did the blowout preventer (BOP) fail to stop the blowout in the Deepwater Horizon accident in the Gulf of Mexico?

2010年深水地平线事故的链条很长：固井质量差且水泥胶结测试被判读失误，负压测试未识别流体侵入风险，井口被大量流体突袭时，防喷器虽有闸板但未能在剪切头切断钻杆，且切断后仍未形成密封，BOP控制系统的电池耗尽也使其无法应急启动。多项机械与程序缺陷叠加导致井喷失控，最终平台爆炸沉没、约490万桶原油泄漏。事故调查促成了行业对井控、BOP冗余设计和应急响应能力的全面改革。
The 2010 Deepwater Horizon disaster was a long chain of failures: poor cementing with a wrongly interpreted cement bond test, a negative-pressure test that failed to flag the risk of influx, and a blowout preventer that, though equipped with rams, did not shear the drill pipe and seal, with the BOP's emergency-control battery also depleted. The compounding mechanical and procedural failures left the well out of control, the platform exploded and sank, and roughly 4.9 million barrels of oil spilled. The investigation drove industry-wide reform of well control, BOP redundancy and emergency response.

为什么塔科马海峡大桥会被一阵不算大的风"吹塌"？

Why did the Tacoma Narrows Bridge collapse in wind that was not even that strong?

1940年塔科马大桥的倒塌是空气动力颤振造成的，而不是被强风直接吹垮或强度不足。桥面在风中开始扭转振动，振动又改变了气流分离的方式，气动力反过来给振动"加能量"，振幅越来越大，这就是气动弹性自激振动，直到中间桥面被扭断、大桥解体。这个教训让工程师认识到：大跨柔性桥梁必须做风洞试验和颤振分析才能动工。
The 1940 Tacoma Narrows Bridge fell to aeroelastic flutter, not to strong wind or insufficient strength. The deck began torsional oscillation, and the motion altered how airflow separated, feeding energy back into the vibration — a self-excited oscillation that grew until the mid-span section tore apart and the bridge broke up. The disaster taught engineers that flexible long-span bridges must pass wind-tunnel testing and flutter analysis before they are built.

大风天塔式起重机为什么要松开回转刹车，让吊臂随风自由转动？

Why are tower cranes left free to slew in high winds instead of having the slewing brake locked?

塔机吊臂在侧风下承受很大的风压力矩，如果锁死回转机构，这个力矩就全部由塔身承担，可能使标准节、附墙件或基础超载。松开回转刹车后，吊臂像风向标一样自动转到风阻最小的位置（通常起重臂顺风、平衡臂迎风），风载对塔身的扭矩和倾覆弯矩显著减小。因此非工作状态的风载校核就是按自由回转工况进行的，规范同时规定停止作业与撤离的风速阈值、锚固方式以及吊钩和小车的收起状态。
A crane's jib picks up a large wind moment in a crosswind, and locking the slewing mechanism forces the tower, mast sections, tie-ins and foundation to carry all of it, which can overload them. Releasing the slew brake lets the jib weathervane into the position of least resistance — typically jib downwind and counter-jib into the wind — greatly reducing torsion and overturning moment on the tower. Out-of-service wind loads are therefore assessed for the free-slewing condition, and standards also set wind-speed thresholds for stopping work and evacuating, tie-down arrangements and the required stowed position of hook and trolley.

抗震'摇摆墙'凭什么比刚性墙更安全？

Why can a 'rocking wall' be safer than a rigid wall?

摇摆墙在墙底留一条可控水平缝，震时整墙绕墙脚如钟摆转动、一侧抬离基础，把输入能量转为刚体摆动，缝处阻尼器与预应力筋耗能并把它拉回原位。变形由转动承担，墙身不必靠开裂与钢筋屈服耗能，震后几乎无残余位移。刚性墙则靠墙脚塑性铰开裂与钢材屈服耗能，构造简单却把不可逆损伤留在主体上。更安全指震后还能用、好修复，不是位移一定更小。
A rocking wall leaves a controlled horizontal joint at its base, so in a quake the whole wall turns about its toe like a pendulum and one side lifts off the foundation, turning the incoming energy into rigid-body rocking, while dampers and tendons at the joint both dissipate that energy and pull it back. Because the deformation comes from rotation, the wall body need not crack or yield its steel to absorb energy, and almost no residual displacement is left. A rigid wall instead burns energy through a plastic hinge cracking at its toe and yielding steel, which is simple to build but keeps irreversible damage in the structure. Safer here means still usable and easy to repair after the quake, not necessarily smaller drift.

举高消防车最多只能伸到几十米，更高楼层起火靠什么扑救？

Aerial fire trucks reach only a few tens of metres, so what fights a fire on much higher floors?

主流举高消防车的工作高度多在五十到七十米，大约十几到二十几层，再高就受车辆稳定性、臂架强度和场地条件限制，所以高层建筑必须自带消防能力。规范要求设置室内消火栓系统、自动喷水灭火系统、消防水泵、高位消防水箱和转输水泵，消防员可在着火层下一层的消火栓接口取水并携带水带上楼扑救。同时用防烟楼梯间、消防电梯、避难层和防火分区限制火势与烟气蔓延，室外还设水泵接合器，让消防车向室内管网加压补水。
Most aerial appliances work to about 50–70 m, roughly the teens to low twenties of storeys, beyond which vehicle stability, boom strength and site access take over, so tall buildings must carry their own firefighting capability. Codes require interior standpipes with hose outlets, automatic sprinklers, fire pumps, a high-level fire water tank and transfer pumps so crews can connect hoses at the standpipe one floor below the fire. Smoke-proof stairs, firefighter lifts, refuge floors and compartmentation limit fire and smoke spread, while fire department connections outside let engines boost pressure and water into the building's own piping.

商场里绿色的『安全出口』灯，为什么停电了还一直亮着？

Why do the green exit signs in a shopping mall stay lit even when the power goes out?

这类标志灯内部带有自备蓄电池，平时由市电充电，一旦外部供电中断，控制电路立刻切换到电池供电，让灯继续亮够规定的时间（常要求三十分钟以上）。火灾里浓烟遮挡视线、走廊一片漆黑，人往往只能沿着贴近地面的持续光带和箭头找到最近的出口，所以规范要求这些标志常亮、配备用电源并定期做断电试验。这也是为什么它们不能被货物遮挡，更不能随便把电源关掉。
Such signs contain their own rechargeable battery kept topped up by the mains, and when external supply fails the control circuit switches instantly to battery power so the sign stays lit for a required period, commonly at least thirty minutes. In a fire, smoke blocks vision and corridors go dark, so people can often only follow the continuous glow and arrows near floor level to the nearest exit, which is why codes require these signs to remain illuminated, have standby power and be tested by simulated power failure. It is also why they must never be blocked by goods or switched off casually.
