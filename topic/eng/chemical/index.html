在精馏塔中，为什么回流比增大能提高分离纯度却降低产能？

In a distillation column, why does a larger reflux ratio raise purity but lower capacity?

回流把部分塔顶冷凝液送回塔内，提供更多气液接触次数，使轻重组分分得更干净，纯度上升。但回流占用塔内上升蒸气的能力，可用于采出产品的蒸气减少，因此单位时间产量下降、能耗增加。
Reflux returns some condensed overhead to the column, giving more vapor-liquid contacts so components separate cleaner and purity rises. But reflux consumes the column's vapor-handling capacity, leaving less vapor for product draw, so throughput and energy cost worsen.

什么是传质单元高度 HTU，它如何量化填料塔的分离效率？

What is the height of a transfer unit (HTU) and how does it quantify packing efficiency?

HTU 表示完成一个传质单元所需的填料高度，等于塔高除以理论传质单元数 NTU。HTU 越小，说明单位高度的填料传质越充分、分离效率越高，受气液流量、填料类型和分布均匀性影响。
HTU is the packing height needed for one transfer unit, equal to column height divided by the number of transfer units (NTU). A smaller HTU means each meter of packing transfers more mass and the column is more efficient, depending on flow rates, packing type, and distribution.

在聚合反应中，为什么转化率和分子量分布需要分开控制？

In polymerization, why must conversion and molecular-weight distribution be controlled separately?

转化率主要反映反应进行程度与产量，而分子量分布决定材料的强度、粘度和加工性能。两者受温度、引发剂浓度和链转移速率的不同影响，需分别调节工艺参数才能得到既高产又性能稳定的聚合物。
Conversion reflects how far and how much the reaction proceeded, while molecular-weight distribution sets strength, viscosity, and processability. They respond differently to temperature, initiator, and chain-transfer rates, so parameters must be tuned separately for both high yield and stable properties.

超临界流体（如超临界CO2）为什么既是“气体”又是“液体”，萃取咖啡脱咖啡因靠它什么特性？

Why is a supercritical fluid (like CO2) both 'gas' and 'liquid', and what property decaffeinates coffee?

超过临界温度和压力后，流体密度接近液体（溶解力强）又像气体一样易扩散穿透，状态独特。超临界CO2 能钻进咖啡豆带走咖啡因，再降压就变回气体分离，无溶剂残留。
Above critical temperature and pressure the fluid has liquid-like density (strong dissolving) yet gas-like diffusivity and penetration—a unique state. Supercritical CO2 infiltrates coffee beans to pull out caffeine, then drops pressure to revert to gas and separate, leaving no solvent.

什么是催化裂化，炼油厂为什么要把重油分解成汽油？

What is catalytic cracking and why do refineries break heavy oil into gasoline?

催化裂化用催化剂在高温下把大分子重油断裂成较小的烃分子，提高汽油和柴油等轻质馏分的产率。这样既增产高价值燃油，又消化掉需求较低的重质残渣。
Catalytic cracking uses a catalyst at high temperature to break large heavy-oil molecules into smaller hydrocarbons, raising yields of valuable light fractions like gasoline and diesel while consuming low-demand heavy residue.

为什么化工厂常用“膜”而不是只靠蒸馏来分离物质，膜分离省在哪里？

Why do chemical plants often use membranes rather than only distillation, and where does membrane separation save?

蒸馏要靠加热汽化再冷凝，耗能巨大；膜靠压力让小分子透过、大分子留下，通常不需相变。对盐水脱盐、气体分离尤其节能，且设备紧凑易放大。
Distillation boils then condenses, costing huge energy; membranes let small molecules pass under pressure while holding larger ones, usually without phase change. For desalination and gas separation it saves energy and packs compactly.

为什么化工厂的管道常常包着厚厚的保温层？

Why are chemical plant pipes often wrapped in thick insulation?

许多物料需要保持特定温度才能流动或反应，保温层减少热量散失、节约能源并防止管内结晶或凝固。它也保护人员不被烫到，并避免外壁结露腐蚀。
Many streams must stay at a set temperature to flow or react; insulation cuts heat loss, saves energy, and prevents freezing or solidifying inside. It also protects people from burns and stops condensation corrosion.

流化床反应器里固体颗粒为什么像“沸腾的液体”一样翻滚，这对反应有什么好处？

Why do solid particles in a fluidized bed churn like boiling liquid, and what benefits does that bring?

从底部吹入的气体把颗粒托起并使其悬浮翻滚，形成似流体的状态。气固接触极充分、传热均匀，温度好控，广泛用于催化裂化和燃煤燃烧。
Gas blown from the bottom suspends and tosses the particles into a fluid-like state. Gas-solid contact is excellent and heat transfer uniform and controllable, widely used in catalytic cracking and coal combustion.

什么是放热反应，反应器为什么要控制温度防止失控？

What is an exothermic reaction and why control reactor temperature to avoid runaway?

放热反应在反应时释放热量，若热量积累使温度升高，而升温又加快反应，就会形成正反馈导致温度和压力骤升、甚至爆炸。冷却系统和联锁能在过热前移走热量，保证安全。
Exothermic reactions release heat; if it builds up and raises temperature, the faster reaction feeds back more heat, spiking temperature and pressure toward explosion. Cooling and interlocks remove heat before it runs away.

肥皂为什么能把油污洗掉？

Why does soap remove greasy dirt?

肥皂分子一头亲水、一头亲油，亲油端钻进油污，亲水端朝外，把油滴包成小团带到水里冲走。这样油就不粘在手上，能被水冲掉了。
Soap molecules have a water-loving end and an oil-loving end; the oil-loving end dives into grease while the other faces out, wrapping oil into tiny blobs carried away by water, so it rinses off your hands.

为什么煮盐水时，盐不会跟着水蒸气一起跑掉？

Why doesn't salt escape with the steam when boiling saltwater?

水加热到一百度变成蒸汽跑掉，而食盐的沸点远高于水，常温下是固体颗粒，不会汽化。所以蒸汽冷凝后基本是纯水，盐留在锅里。
Water turns to vapor at 100 degrees C and leaves, but salt's boiling point is far higher and it stays solid at room temperature, so it doesn't vaporize. The condensed steam is nearly pure water; the salt remains in the pot.
