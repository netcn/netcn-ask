固态电池为什么被认为比液态锂电池更安全，主要技术难点“固-固界面阻抗”指什么？

Why is the solid-state battery seen as safer than liquid lithium batteries, and what does the key challenge of solid-solid interfacial impedance mean?

它用不可燃的固体电解质替代易燃有机液，从根源上避免漏液和燃爆。但固-固接触是“点接触”，界面缝隙让锂离子难以通过，电阻（阻抗）剧增，容量和快充性能因此受限。
It replaces flammable liquid electrolyte with a non-flammable solid, removing the root cause of leakage and fire. But solid-solid contact is point-like, and gaps block lithium-ion transport, spiking interfacial resistance and limiting capacity and fast charging.

硫化物固态电解质离子电导率很高，但为什么对水和空气极其敏感，量产面临什么挑战？

Sulfide solid electrolytes have very high ionic conductivity, so why are they extremely sensitive to water and air, and what challenges does mass production face?

硫化物遇水会释放有毒的硫化氢，遇空气也易分解，因此全工艺必须在干燥惰性气氛里完成，设备成本飙升。同时它与高电压正极的界面仍不稳定，封装和规模化都很难。
Sulfides release toxic H2S on contact with water and degrade in air, so the whole process needs dry inert atmosphere, raising equipment cost. Their interface with high-voltage cathodes is also unstable, complicating packaging and scale-up.

储能并网对新能源有什么意义？

Why does energy storage matter for renewable integration?

风电光伏出力随天气波动，储能可在富电时充电、缺电时放电，平抑波动、提供调频备用，让电网更稳定地接纳清洁能源。
Wind and solar vary with weather; storage charges when surplus and discharges when short, smoothing output and providing frequency reserve for stable clean integration.

什么是锂枝晶，为什么它既威胁液态锂电池安全也困扰固态电池研发？

What is a lithium dendrite, and why does it threaten liquid lithium batteries yet also plague solid-state research?

充电时锂若沉积不均会长成针状枝晶，在液态电池里可能刺穿隔膜造成短路起火；在固态电池里它同样会沿缝隙钻长、撑裂脆弱的固界面。抑制枝晶是两类电池共同的难题。
Uneven plating grows needle-like lithium dendrites that can pierce the separator and short-circuit a liquid cell, and in solid cells they creep through gaps and crack the brittle interface. Suppressing dendrites is a shared challenge.

柔性电子设备的电池为什么要用多孔集流体和可拉伸结构，而不是普通硬电池？

Why do flexible electronics need porous current collectors and stretchable structures rather than ordinary rigid batteries?

弯折时刚性电极会开裂断路，柔性电池用蛇形导线、海绵状电极和可延展封装，让内部导电通路在拉伸弯曲中仍保持连通。这样手表手环才能随意弯折而不掉电。
Rigid electrodes crack and break circuit when bent; flexible cells use serpentine traces, spongy electrodes and stretchable packaging so conductive paths survive stretching. That lets watches and bands flex without losing power.

钠离子电池为什么被视为锂电池的补充，它在哪些性能指标上处于劣势？

Why is the sodium-ion battery seen as a complement to lithium batteries, and in which metrics is it at a disadvantage?

钠资源丰富、便宜且分布广，适合大规模储能，但钠离子更重、半径更大，导致能量密度和体积效率明显低于锂电。它更适合对重量不敏感的电网储能，而非长续航手机。
Sodium is abundant, cheap and widely available, suiting large-scale storage, but its heavier, larger ion gives lower energy and volumetric density than lithium. It fits weight-insensitive grid storage more than long-range phones.

逆变器在新能源系统里做什么？

What does an inverter do in a renewable system?

光伏板和电池输出的是直流电，逆变器把直流变成与电网同频同相的交流电才能并网或供家电使用。
Solar panels and batteries output DC; the inverter converts it to grid-synchronous AC so it can feed the grid or power appliances.
