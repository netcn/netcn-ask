建筑的抗震设防烈度是怎么定的，是不是越硬越抗震？

How is seismic fortification level set, is stiffer always better?

按场地可能遭遇的地震动概率划分设防等级；太刚易放大某些频率、太柔会晃坏，靠延性和耗能而非单纯硬来抗倒塌。
Seismic intensity reflects expected ground motion; neither too stiff nor too flexible is best, ductility and energy dissipation prevent collapse.

威布尔寿命分析中，形状参数β小于1、等于1和大于1分别对应什么失效机理？各自指向何种维修策略？

In Weibull life analysis, what failure mechanisms correspond to shape parameters β below 1, equal to 1, and above 1, and what maintenance strategy does each suggest?

β<1表示失效率随时间递减，属早期失效，多源于制造缺陷或磨合不良，宜用筛选与老炼试验剔除薄弱个体；β=1退化为指数分布，故障随机出现，定时更换不能降低风险，应以状态监测为主；β>1表示失效率随时间上升，属耗损型失效，存在明确的特征寿命，此时按役龄定期更换或翻修才能真正控制风险。β值还能外推不同可靠度下的安全寿命。
When β < 1 the failure rate falls with time: these are early-life failures driven by manufacturing defects, so screening and burn-in remove weak units. β = 1 reduces the model to the exponential distribution, where failures occur randomly; scheduled replacement cannot reduce risk, and condition monitoring is the rational choice. When β > 1 the failure rate rises, marking wear-out with a characteristic life, so age-based replacement or overhaul genuinely controls risk. The fitted β also supports extrapolation of safe lives at required reliability levels.

设备故障率的浴盆曲线为什么两头高、中间低？这条规律怎样指导维修策略？

Why does an equipment failure-rate bathtub curve rise at both ends and dip in the middle, and how does it shape maintenance policy?

寿命初期，材料缺陷与装配误差陆续暴露，故障率高但随磨合迅速下降；随后进入偶然故障期，故障仅由随机过载等因素引发，故障率近似恒定，体现设计固有的可靠水平；进入耗损期后，磨损、腐蚀与老化不断累积，故障率再次攀升。维修上宜分段应对：早期靠筛选与磨合剔除缺陷，平稳期用状态监测代替频繁解体检修，临近耗损期则按寿命定期更换。
Early in life, material flaws and assembly errors surface quickly, so failure rate starts high and falls as units burn in. Failures then become random events at a nearly constant rate—a useful-life plateau reflecting the design's inherent reliability. Finally wear, corrosion and ageing accumulate and the rate climbs again. Policy should follow the phases: screen and run in new units, use condition monitoring instead of intrusive overhauls on the plateau, and move to age-based replacement as wear-out nears.
