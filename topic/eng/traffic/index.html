Why do interchanges spiral or loop instead of crossing plainly?

立体交叉让不同方向车流分层通过、互不抢道，螺旋或环道在有限用地里用高度换平面空间，减少红绿灯停车。
Interchanges separate flows by level so they don't conflict; spirals or loops trade height for land and cut signal stops.

火车车轮的踏面为什么做成锥形，这和高速时的『蛇行』运动有什么关系？

Why are railway wheel treads coned, and how does that relate to hunting oscillation at speed?

轮对的两个车轮固接在同一车轴上，踏面带约 1:20 到 1:40 的锥度，当轮对偏离轨道中心时，一侧滚动半径变大、另一侧变小，同一转速下两轮行程不同，轮对便自动向中心回摆，这就是不需要转向机构的自导向原理。但这种回复运动与轮轨蠕滑力构成的系统在高速下阻尼不足，轮对会绕垂轴周期性偏摆并沿线路蜿蜒前进，即蛇行运动，超过临界速度后振幅剧增，加剧轮轨磨耗甚至危及行车安全。因此设计要在踏面锥度、一次悬挂纵横向刚度和抗蛇行减振器之间取得平衡，让自导向能力与蛇行临界速度同时满足要求。
Both wheels of a wheelset are rigid on one axle and their treads are coned at roughly 1:20 to 1:40, so when the set shifts off centre one wheel rolls on a larger radius and the other on a smaller one, making the set steer itself back toward the track centre without any steering linkage. That restoring action combined with wheel-rail creep forces forms a lightly damped system, and above a critical speed the wheelset yaws periodically and snakes along the track — hunting — with amplitude growing sharply, causing heavy wheel and rail wear and eventually threatening safety. Vehicle design therefore balances tread conicity, primary suspension longitudinal and lateral stiffness and yaw dampers so self-steering is retained while the hunting critical speed stays above operating speed.

公路的波形梁护栏为什么做成波浪断面，端头还常向下弯或装一个大装置？

Why do highway W-beam guardrails use a corrugated profile, and why are their ends turned down or fitted with a large terminal?

波形断面在纵向提供高抗弯刚度、在横向允许护栏板连同立柱一起变形，车辆被接住后沿护栏滑行并逐渐减速，能量由护栏板拉伸、立柱屈曲和土体变形分散吸收，而不是把车硬弹回车道。若护栏端头直接暴露，正面撞击时端部可能像矛一样撕入车厢，因此端部要么埋入路侧土坡、向下弯曲，要么安装能沿轴向溃缩吸能的端头装置。设计还要校核护栏的工作宽度与路侧净区，保证变形空间足够，否则车辆仍会撞上被护栏保护的障碍物。
The corrugated section gives high bending stiffness along the rail while letting rail and posts deflect laterally, so a vehicle is captured and redirected along the barrier, decelerating as rail tension, post bending and soil deformation absorb energy instead of bouncing it back into traffic. An exposed rail end can spear into the passenger compartment in a head-on hit, so ends are either buried in a backslope and turned down or fitted with an energy-absorbing crash terminal that telescopes along the rail. Design must also check working width and roadside clear zone so deflection space is adequate, otherwise the vehicle still strikes the very hazard being shielded.

自行车头盔摔过一次，外观没裂也建议换掉，为什么？

Why should a bicycle helmet be replaced after a single crash even if it looks undamaged?

头盔的核心是发泡聚苯乙烯内衬，它靠自身被压碎、永久变形来削减撞击的峰值加速度，这个吸能过程是一次性的，压溃后的泡沫不会弹回原状。外壳只是薄薄一层塑料，用来分散局部压力并保护泡沫，所以泡沫内部的压实和微裂往往被外壳挡住，肉眼很难判断。头盔认证测试正是用规定高度的落锤冲击并限制头模的加速度峰值，一旦泡沫的吸能能力已被消耗，再次撞击时能提供的缓冲明显下降，因此摔过就该更换。
A helmet's protection comes from its expanded polystyrene liner, which crushes and deforms permanently to cut the peak acceleration of an impact, and that energy absorption is one-shot because crushed foam does not spring back. The shell is only a thin plastic skin that spreads local pressure and protects the foam, so compacted or micro-cracked foam underneath is easily hidden from view. Certification tests drop the helmet from a specified height and cap the headform's peak acceleration, so once the liner has been used up the cushioning available in a second impact is much reduced and the helmet should be retired.

The retractor's ratchet locks under sudden pull but lets you move normally; pretensioners snug it in a crash.

马路上的斑马线为什么是白色，还好像会反光？

Why are crosswalks white and seem to glow at night?

白漆掺了玻璃微珠等逆反射材料，车灯照上去把光原路返给司机，夜间更醒目；白对比强也易辨识。
Glass beads make the paint retroreflective so headlights bounce back to drivers at night; white also contrasts strongly.
