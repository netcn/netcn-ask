AGV调度系统要解决什么问题？

What does an AGV scheduling system solve?

它要协调多台小车在仓库或车间中的任务分配、路径规划和交通避让，避免拥堵与碰撞，提升整体物流效率。
It coordinates task assignment, routing and traffic avoidance for many vehicles to prevent jams and collisions and raise logistics efficiency.

AGV和AMR有什么区别？

What is the difference between AGV and AMR?

AGV（自动导引车）沿固定路径（磁条、导轨、二维码）行驶，需改造地面；AMR（自主移动机器人）靠激光或视觉自主导航，能绕障并动态规划路线。
AGVs follow fixed paths (magnetic tape, rails, QR codes) and often need floor changes, while AMRs navigate autonomously with lidar or vision and reroute around obstacles.

自动驾驶的感知融合是什么？

What is perception fusion in autonomous driving?

它把摄像头、激光雷达、毫米波雷达和GPS或IMU的数据融合，互补各自优劣（视觉辨色、雷达测距测速），形成统一可靠的环境模型。
It fuses camera, lidar, radar and GPS or IMU data, each compensating the others, vision for color, radar for range and speed, into one reliable environment model.

PLC在工业中做什么？

What does a PLC do in industry?

可编程逻辑控制器（PLC）是工业现场的实时控制计算机，读取传感器与按钮信号，按梯形图等程序驱动电机、阀门，抗干扰、稳定可靠。
A programmable logic controller reads sensors and buttons and drives motors and valves per a program (e.g., ladder logic), rugged and reliable for real-time control.
