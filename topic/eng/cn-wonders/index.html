三峡船闸为什么像'楼梯'一级一级？

Why is the Three Gorges ship lock like stairs?

水头高达百米以上，无法一次升船，只能靠分级闸室逐段升降船舶。
The head exceeds 100 m, impossible to lift at once, so stepped chambers raise and lower ships in stages.

中国盾构机为什么能挖穿山河？

Why can Chinese TBMs bore through mountains and rivers?

盾构把破岩、出渣、支护、衬砌接成连续流水线：滚刀压裂岩体，螺旋机或皮带出渣，盾尾拼装管片成永久衬砌，钢壳此前顶住围岩；掌子面靠渣土或泥浆平衡水土压力，河底才稳得住。相比钻爆法炸一茬、支护一轮的断续循环，它振动小、工序不停，长隧道才推得动。边界不在功率而在地质：软硬交替、破碎带、高水压与刀具磨损定成败，机型按地层定制。
A shield machine strings breaking rock, removing muck, supporting the ground and building the lining into one continuous production line: disc cutters fracture the rock, a screw conveyor or belt clears the muck, the shield tail assembles segments into the permanent lining, and the steel shell holds the ground until that lining takes load; the face is steadied by soil or slurry balancing groundwater and earth pressure, which is what keeps a river crossing from collapsing. Against drill-and-blast, which cycles a charge then a round of support, it runs on with little vibration, so a long tunnel can advance at all. The limit is geology, not horsepower: alternating soft and hard rock, fault zones, high water pressure and cutter wear decide the outcome, and machines are configured for the ground.

Why does the South-to-North Water Diversion have three routes?

By terrain and hydrology: the east pumps via the old canal, the central flows from Danjiangkou, the west feeds the upper Yellow River—splitting eases the work.

万里长城为什么要修在山脊上而不是平地上？

Why were the Great Walls built along mountain ridges instead of on flat land?

山体本身是一道天然屏障，城墙只需补上缺口；高处视野开阔便于燃烽传讯，且敌人仰攻更费力、难架云梯。
Mountain ridges are natural barriers, so the wall only needs to fill the gaps. High ground gives a wide view for beacon signals, and attackers must climb upward, making scaling harder.

都江堰为什么两千多年还在发挥作用？

Why has Dujiangyan still worked for over 2,000 years?

因为它'无坝引水、乘势利导'，顺着岷江的水文来分水排沙，而不是硬去堵水，所以很少被冲毁。
It uses dam-free diversion that follows the river's natural flow to split water and expel sand, rather than blocking it—so it is rarely destroyed.

都江堰没有大坝，怎么做到自动分水又防洪？

Dujiangyan has no dam—how does it split water and control floods automatically?

鱼嘴分水堤利用弯道环流：枯水走内江灌溉，洪水走外江排走；飞沙堰溢洪排沙，宝瓶口控制进流量，全靠'因势利导'。
The fish-mouth levee uses bend circulation—low water goes to the inner river for irrigation, flood water to the outer river. The Feishayan spillway dumps sediment and the Bottle-Neck controls inflow, all by working with nature.

青藏铁路怎么让铁轨不被冻土'拱'起来？

How does the Qinghai–Tibet Railway keep tracks from being heaved by permafrost?

线路用通风管路基、热棒（重力式虹吸）、碎石护坡和主动冷却，把热量带走、保持冻土终年冻结稳定。
The line uses ventilated embankments, thermosyphons (gravity-fed heat pipes), crushed-stone slopes and active cooling to remove heat and keep the permafrost frozen and stable year-round.

港珠澳大桥的海底隧道为什么用'沉管'而不是钻挖隧道？

Why does the HK-Zhuhai-Macao Bridge use immersed tubes rather than a bored tunnel?

沉管在岸上预制、浮运到位再沉放对接，对繁忙航道和海底起伏适应好，接缝与抗震可控，且工期更短。
Tunnel elements are prefabricated on land, floated out and sunk into place. This suits busy shipping lanes and uneven seabeds, with controllable joints and seismic behavior and a shorter schedule.

长城的城砖为什么能历经千年不散？

Why do the Great Wall's bricks survive a thousand years?

明代常用糯米灰浆（糯米汁混合石灰），粘结力强、抗裂又耐水；再加上选址排水得当，寿命大大延长。
Ming bricks often used lime mortar tempered with sticky-rice juice—strong, crack-resistant and water-durable. Good siting and drainage also extended their life.

京杭大运河怎么翻越山东高地这样的分水岭？

How did the Grand Canal cross watersheds like the Shandong highland?

靠'闸河'——逐级船闸与引水工程把水位一段段抬升，在分水岭设'水脊'调控南北双向水流，船随闸室升降而过。
It used lock canals: a series of ship locks and diversion works lifted water step by step, with a watershed 'ridge' dividing north- and south-flowing water so boats rose and fell between chambers.

北盘江大桥为什么能架在离谷底约565米的高空？

Why can the Beipanjiang Bridge stand about 565 m above the valley floor?

它采用斜拉桥加高桥墩，一跨跨越深V形峡谷，既缩短路线又避免盘山展线；钢桁梁和高墩减轻自重、抗风抗震。
A cable-stayed design with very tall piers spans the deep V-shaped gorge in one leap, shortening the route and avoiding mountain switchbacks. Steel trusses and high piers cut self-weight while resisting wind and quakes.

Is generating power really the top job of the Three Gorges Dam?

防洪才是首要效益，发电和航运次之；汛期它拦蓄洪峰、削减下游威胁，才是最重要的设计目标。
Flood control is the primary purpose, with power and navigation secondary. In flood season it stores peak flows to protect the middle and lower reaches—that is the key design goal.
