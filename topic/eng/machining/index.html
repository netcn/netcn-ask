为什么铣削时只换一个主轴转速，原本平稳的切削会突然出现剧烈颤振？

Why can changing just one spindle speed setting turn a smooth milling cut into violent chatter?

这是再生型自激振动：刀齿总在前一齿留下的波纹表面上切入，瞬时切削厚度取决于相邻两齿波纹的相位差。当主轴转速使该相位差落入不稳定区间，切削过程注入机床结构的能量超过阻尼耗散，振幅便自发增长。稳定性叶瓣图给出每个转速对应的临界切深，把转速选进高阶叶瓣或减小轴向切深即可恢复稳定切削。
Milling chatter is a regenerative self-excited vibration: each tooth cuts into the wavy surface left by its predecessor, so the instantaneous chip thickness depends on the phase shift between successive tooth paths. When spindle speed places that phase shift in an unstable range, the cutting process pumps more energy into the machine structure than damping dissipates, and vibration grows spontaneously. A stability lobe diagram gives the critical depth of cut at each speed; choosing a speed inside a higher-order lobe or reducing axial depth restores stable cutting.

低速切削塑性金属时为什么会形成积屑瘤？它对加工质量有什么影响，又该如何抑制？

Why does a built-up edge form when cutting ductile metals at low speed, how does it affect machining quality, and how can it be suppressed?

低速切削塑性金属时，前刀面与切屑之间的压力和摩擦极大，紧贴前刀面的切屑底层在高温高压下剧烈应变硬化并粘附其上，层层堆积便长成积屑瘤。它会间歇性脱落并划伤已加工表面，使粗糙度和尺寸精度恶化；但它也在一定程度上保护刀刃并增大实际前角。把切削速度提高到粘结温度区以上、使用润滑性好的切削液或涂层刀具，即可有效抑制积屑瘤。
At low cutting speeds the pressure and friction between chip and rake face are extreme; the chip's underlayer work-hardens severely under this heat and pressure and bonds to the rake face, growing layer by layer into a built-up edge. It tears off intermittently and scratches the machined surface, worsening roughness and dimensional accuracy, although it partly protects the cutting edge and enlarges the effective rake angle. Raising the cutting speed beyond the bonding regime, applying lubricating cutting fluid, or using coated tools effectively suppresses it.

数控机床用的是什么指令语言？

What instruction language do CNC machines use?

数控机床主要用G代码（G-code）控制刀具的运动轨迹、转速与进给，由CAM软件根据零件模型自动生成。
CNC machines are mainly driven by G-code, which sets toolpath, spindle speed and feed, usually generated by CAM software from a part model.
