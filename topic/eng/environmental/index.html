生命周期评价（LCA）怎么算一件产品从生到死的碳排放，为什么“碳足迹”常被争论？

How does life-cycle assessment (LCA) compute a product's cradle-to-grave carbon, and why is 'carbon footprint' often disputed?

LCA 把原料开采、制造、运输、使用到报废的全链条清单化，再乘排放因子求和。争论多因系统边界、数据来源和分摊方法不同，同一产品结论可差很多。
LCA inventories the whole chain—extraction, manufacturing, transport, use, disposal—then sums emissions by factors. Disputes arise because boundaries, data and allocation methods differ, so the same product gets very different numbers.

什么是厌氧消化，它如何把有机废物变成沼气并发电？

What is anaerobic digestion and how does it turn organic waste into biogas and power?

厌氧消化在无氧条件下由产酸菌和产甲烷菌分阶段把有机物分解成甲烷和二氧化碳混合的沼气。沼气驱动内燃机或燃料电池发电，残余消化液还可作肥料，实现废物能源化。
Anaerobic digestion uses acidogenic and methanogenic bacteria in oxygen-free tanks to break organics into biogas, a methane-CO2 mix. The gas fuels engines or fuel cells for power, and the digestate becomes fertilizer—waste to energy.

在大气治理中，湿法脱硫利用什么化学反应去除燃煤烟气中的二氧化硫？

In air pollution control, what reaction does wet flue-gas desulfurization use to remove SO2?

湿法脱硫用石灰石浆液在吸收塔里与烟气中的二氧化硫反应，先生成亚硫酸钙，再鼓入空气氧化成稳定的硫酸钙（石膏）。这样把气态污染物固定成固体副产物，便于后续处置或利用。
Wet desulfurization sprays limestone slurry that reacts with SO2 to form calcium sulfite, then air oxidizes it to stable calcium sulfate (gypsum). The gaseous pollutant is fixed into a solid byproduct for disposal or reuse.

在垃圾填埋场，为什么要用防渗膜和渗滤液收集系统双重防护？

At landfills, why use both a geomembrane and a leachate collection system?

垃圾降解会产生含重金属和有机物的渗滤液，一旦渗入土壤和地下水将长期污染。防渗膜像不透水的底衬阻断下渗，其上的排水层把渗滤液导排到收集池处理，双保险降低环境风险。
Decomposing waste yields leachate rich in heavy metals and organics that would pollute soil and groundwater if it seeped. The geomembrane acts as an impermeable liner blocking downward flow, while the drainage layer above routes leachate to a collection tank for treatment—double protection.

电催化还原CO2为什么被寄予厚望，它和工厂捕集的碳怎么变成燃料？

Why is electrochemical CO2 reduction promising, and how does captured carbon become fuel?

用可再生电力在催化剂上把CO2加水还原成一氧化碳、甲酸、乙烯甚至乙醇，等于把多余绿电存进化学键。捕集的碳因此从废物变成可循环的合成原料。
Renewable electricity reduces captured CO2 with water on a catalyst into CO, formate, ethylene or even ethanol—storing surplus green power in chemical bonds. Captured carbon thus becomes recyclable feedstock instead of waste.

什么是活性污泥法，里面的微生物靠吃什么净化污水？

What is the activated sludge process and what do its microbes eat to clean water?

活性污泥法是把含大量微生物的絮体回流到曝气池，微生物以污水中的有机物和氮磷为食，把它们转化成自身细胞和二氧化碳等。之后在二沉池里污泥沉降分离，清水排出。
Activated sludge recirculates microbe-rich flocs into an aeration tank where organisms consume organics and nutrients like nitrogen and phosphorus, turning them into biomass and CO2. The sludge then settles in a clarifier and clear water flows out.

为什么城市下雨时有时下水道会反臭甚至溢流？

Why do city sewers sometimes smell or overflow when it rains?

老城区的合流制管道把雨水和污水混在一起，暴雨时水量远超管道容量，检查井就会冒水溢流。管内沉积的有机物在厌氧条件下产生硫化氢等臭气，随气流从井口冒出。
Older combined sewers mix stormwater and sewage; heavy rain overwhelms capacity and manholes overflow. Anaerobic deposits release hydrogen sulfide and other foul gases that vent through the openings.

人工湿地怎么用“芦苇+微生物+砂石”把脏水变清，它比传统污水厂省在哪？

How do constructed wetlands use reeds, microbes and gravel to clean water, and where do they save versus traditional plants?

污水慢慢渗过砂石和芦苇根系，根系供氧养活微生物降解有机物，砂石和植物还能吸附氮磷。它靠自然过程运行，几乎不耗电、好维护，适合农村和小社区。
Water seeps through gravel and reed roots; the roots supply oxygen for microbes that degrade organics, while gravel and plants adsorb nitrogen and phosphorus. Relying on nature, it uses almost no power and is easy to maintain—good for villages.

什么是生物可降解塑料，它在自然中靠什么分解？

What are biodegradable plastics and what breaks them down in nature?

这类塑料的分子链含有能被微生物或水水解切断的键，在堆肥或土壤里由细菌、真菌分泌酶逐步拆成小分子，最终变成水、二氧化碳和生物质。但要注意许多需要工业堆肥条件才能真正降解。
These plastics have bonds microbes or water can cleave; in compost or soil, bacteria and fungi secrete enzymes that chop the chains into small molecules ending as water, CO2, and biomass. Many still need industrial composting to truly degrade.

污水处理厂为什么要往脏水里吹气？

Why do treatment plants bubble air into dirty water?

吹入的空气把氧气送进水里，让好氧微生物活起来，它们靠吃掉污水中的有机物来净化水质。氧气不足时这些细菌会死亡，处理效果就变差。
The bubbled air dissolves oxygen so aerobic microbes stay alive and eat the organic pollutants, cleaning the water. Without enough oxygen those bacteria die and treatment worsens.

为什么海边风电或工厂旁有时会种大片红树林或芦苇，植物也能“吃”污染吗？

Why are mangroves or reeds sometimes planted by coasts and factories—can plants 'eat' pollution?

植物和根上微生物能吸收、降解或固定重金属和营养盐，像天然滤网把脏水变干净，同时护岸防风。它们不是真“吃”污染，而是把污染物拦下、转化或锁住。
Plants and their root microbes take up, degrade or trap heavy metals and nutrients, acting like a natural filter that cleans water and protects shores. They do not truly 'eat' pollution but intercept, transform or lock it away.

为什么有的杯子能放进微波炉，有的不能？

Why can some cups go in the microwave and others cannot?

微波炉用微波让食物中的水分子快速抖动发热。金属杯子会把微波反射甚至打火，所以不行；玻璃和标明可微波的塑料能让微波穿过、自己不怎么吸热，因此安全。
Microwaves make water molecules in food vibrate and heat up. Metal cups reflect microwaves and can spark, so they're unsafe; glass and microwave-safe plastics let waves pass through without heating much, so they're fine.
