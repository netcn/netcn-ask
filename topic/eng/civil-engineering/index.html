What is structural reliability and how do engineers ensure safety?

结构可靠度指结构在规定时间内、规定条件下完成预定功能的概率。工程上采用分项系数设计法，考虑荷载和抗力的不确定性，使失效概率控制在可接受范围内。
Structural reliability is the probability that a structure performs its intended function over a given time under stated conditions. Engineers use partial-factor design methods that account for uncertainty in loads and resistance, keeping failure probability within acceptable limits.

What is soil consolidation and what does Terzaghi's theory explain?

Consolidation is the slow process in which saturated soft clay expels pore water and compresses under load, so settlement can last a long time. Terzaghi's one-dimensional consolidation theory uses a differential equation to describe pore-pressure dissipation over time and underlies soft-soil settlement prediction.

重力坝和拱坝在受力原理上有何本质区别？为什么拱坝对地形地质条件要求更高？

What is the fundamental difference in load-carrying mechanism between gravity dams and arch dams, and why do arch dams demand more favourable topography and geology?

重力坝依靠自身的巨大自重抵抗水压力，坝体与地基接触面的抗滑摩擦是稳定的关键，要求地基有足够的抗剪强度，因而坝体断面大、混凝土用量多。拱坝则主要把水荷载通过坝体拱圈传递到两岸山体，属于空间壳体受力，坝体可以做得薄得多。但拱坝要求河谷狭窄、两岸岩体完整坚硬且无软弱结构面，否则拱座一旦失稳就会导致整体破坏，因此其对地形地质条件的依赖远高于重力坝。
A gravity dam resists water pressure through its own enormous dead weight, so sliding resistance at the foundation interface is critical and the section must be massive. An arch dam instead transfers most of the water load through arching action into the abutments on both banks, acting as a thin spatial shell, which allows a much thinner section. However, arch dams require a narrow valley with strong, intact abutment rock free of weak structural planes; if the abutments yield, the whole structure fails, so their dependence on topography and geology is far greater than that of gravity dams.

深基坑施工的『逆作法』为什么反而先做地面以上结构再往下挖？

Why does top-down construction of a deep basement build floors first and excavate afterwards?

逆作法先施作围护墙和竖向支承桩柱，再自上而下逐层浇筑楼板，把已完成的楼板当作围护结构的水平内支撑，然后在其下方掏挖土方。与顺作法的临时钢支撑相比，永久楼板刚度大、变形小，能显著减小围护墙位移和邻近建筑、管线的沉降，特别适合城市密集区以及需要早期恢复地面交通的场地。代价是地下作业空间受限、出土与通风效率低，竖向支承柱的定位与沉降控制要求极高，新旧混凝土和柱板节点构造复杂，工期与造价往往高于顺作。
Top-down construction first installs the retaining wall and vertical support columns, then casts each floor slab from the top downward and uses those completed slabs as stiff permanent props for the wall while soil beneath is mined out. Compared with temporary steel struts in bottom-up work, permanent slabs are far stiffer, so wall movement and settlement of adjacent buildings and utilities shrink markedly, which suits dense urban sites and cases where the street surface must reopen early. The trade-offs are cramped underground working space, slow muck removal and ventilation, very tight tolerances on column position and settlement, complex joints between old and new concrete, and schedule and cost that often exceed bottom-up methods.

坝基渗流控制的核心是什么？为什么上游铺盖、齿墙和灌浆帷幕能减小渗流危害？

What is the core of seepage control in dam foundations, and why do upstream blankets, cutoffs and grouting curtains reduce seepage hazards?

渗流控制的核心是延长渗径、减小水力坡降和渗透压力，使渗流既不带走地基颗粒（管涌）也不产生过高的扬压力。上游不透水铺盖加长水平渗径，齿墙和灌浆帷幕形成垂直防渗体切断渗流通道，降低坝基扬压力并防止管涌。排水减压设施（减压井、反滤层）则把已渗入的水安全排出，两者配合才能保证大坝稳定。
The essence of seepage control is lengthening the seepage path to reduce hydraulic gradient and uplift pressure, so that flow neither carries away foundation particles (piping) nor builds excessive uplift under the dam. An impervious upstream blanket lengthens the horizontal path, while a cutoff wall or grouting curtain forms a vertical barrier that interrupts flow paths, lowering uplift and preventing piping. Relief drains, such as relief wells and filters, then discharge the infiltrated water safely, and the two systems together ensure dam stability.

自动喷水灭火系统为什么能在火灾初期有效控火？喷头的启动原理是什么？

Why can an automatic sprinkler system control a fire effectively in its early stage, and what triggers a sprinkler head?

自动喷淋由管网、水源、报警阀和喷头组成，火灾初期热烟气上升，喷头处的玻璃球或易熔合金受热达到额定动作温度（如68摄氏度）时破裂或熔化，喷头开启喷水，同时水流启动报警装置。水直接冷却火焰和周围表面、抑制烟气蔓延，系统持续供水直到火灾被控制。关键在于喷头只在局部高温区域开启、响应快，能在火势蔓延前压制火灾，并配合疏散和排烟降低伤亡。
An automatic sprinkler system comprises piping, a water supply, alarm valves and sprinkler heads. As a fire builds, hot smoke rises; when a head's glass bulb or fusible link reaches its rated activation temperature (e.g. 68 degrees Celsius) it bursts or melts, water discharges from that head, and flow triggers the alarm. The water cools the flames and surrounding surfaces and suppresses smoke spread, and the system keeps supplying water until the fire is controlled. The key is that only heads near the fire open, responding in seconds to suppress the fire before it grows, working alongside evacuation and smoke control to reduce casualties.

什么是预应力混凝土？先张法与后张法在工艺上有何根本区别？

What is prestressed concrete, and what is the fundamental difference between the pretensioning and post-tensioning methods?

预应力混凝土是在承受外荷载前预先对混凝土施加压应力，以抵消服役期的拉应力、延缓开裂。先张法是先张拉钢筋再浇筑混凝土，待混凝土硬化后放张，靠粘结力传递预压应力；后张法是先浇筑并预留孔道，混凝土硬化后再穿筋张拉并用锚具锁定，预压应力靠锚具直接传递。
Prestressed concrete is pre-compressed before service loads to counteract tensile stresses and delay cracking. In pretensioning, tendons are tensioned before the concrete is cast and the stress is transferred by bond after hardening; in post-tensioning, tendons are tensioned after the concrete hardens inside ducts and anchored with end fittings, transmitting the stress directly through the anchors.

为什么又细又长的柱子不是被"压碎"，而是突然被"压弯"？

Why does a slender column suddenly buckle sideways instead of being crushed?

受压细长杆在载荷达到欧拉临界力时，直线平衡状态失稳：轴向压力"耗尽"了杆的侧向弯曲刚度，杆会突然横向弯出，即使此时杆内应力还远低于材料强度。临界力与长度的平方成反比、与截面惯性矩成正比，所以长度增加危险性急剧上升，加大截面（如宽翼缘工字钢、钢管混凝土）则能有效抗屈曲。与压碎不同，屈曲本质是几何稳定问题，失效表现为整体弯折或扭转，是钢结构和高层建筑必须验算的内容。
When a slender column's axial load reaches the Euler critical load, its straight equilibrium loses stability: the axial force "spends" the lateral bending stiffness, and the column suddenly bows sideways even though the internal stress is still far below the material strength. Critical load falls with the square of length and rises with the section's moment of inertia, so extra length is dramatically dangerous while a larger section (wide-flange I-beam, concrete-filled steel tube) resists buckling well. Unlike crushing, buckling is a problem of geometric stability, failing as a sudden bend or twist — a required check in steel and tall-building design.

钢筋为什么能与混凝土很好地协同工作，共同承受荷载？

Steel and concrete have similar coefficients of thermal expansion and concrete bonds to the roughened steel surface, so they deform together and share load. Concrete carries compression while steel carries tension, so reinforced concrete overcomes concrete's weakness in tension.

工地取样的混凝土试件为什么要养护到 28 天才压强度？

Why are concrete specimens cured for 28 days before being crushed for strength?

水泥水化是持续进行的化学反应，强度随龄期增长但速率递减，普通硅酸盐水泥在标准养护条件下约 28 天已发展到长期强度的大部分（常在八成以上），此后增长明显放缓。28 天因此被选为兼顾『接近稳定』与『工期可接受』的验收龄期，标准据此规定试件尺寸、养护温湿度和加载速率，使不同工地的数据可以互相比较。工期紧张时会同时留 3 天、7 天试件推算发展趋势；掺矿渣或粉煤灰的混凝土早期强度偏低而后期继续增长，必要时按 56 天或成熟度法评定。
Cement hydration is an ongoing reaction whose strength gain decelerates with age, and ordinary Portland cement concrete under standard curing typically reaches most of its long-term strength — often over eighty percent — by 28 days, after which gains slow markedly. That age was adopted as an acceptance compromise between near-stable strength and tolerable construction schedules, and standards fix specimen size, curing temperature and humidity and loading rate so results are comparable between sites. When schedules are tight, 3-day and 7-day specimens project the trend, and slag or fly-ash concretes that keep gaining later may be assessed at 56 days or by maturity methods.
