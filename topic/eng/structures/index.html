抗连续倒塌设计里的『备用荷载路径』是什么意思？

What does the alternate load path approach mean in progressive-collapse design?

连续倒塌指局部构件（例如底层一根柱）意外失效后，荷载重分配使相邻构件相继失效，破坏范围远超初始损伤，典型案例是伦敦 Ronan Point 公寓和俄克拉何马城 Murrah 联邦大楼。备用荷载路径法要求假想拆除某个关键竖向构件，验算剩余结构能否通过梁的连续性、悬链作用和楼板双向传力把荷载绕过缺口传到基础，并配置足够的水平与竖向拉结件。与之互补的思路是对关键构件做局部加强；由于这属于极端偶然作用，验算允许材料进入大变形与塑性并采用较低的荷载系数。
Progressive collapse is the chain of failures that follows when a local element such as a ground-floor column is lost and redistributed load overwhelms its neighbours, spreading damage far beyond the initial harm, as at Ronan Point and the Murrah Federal Building. The alternate-path method notionally removes a key vertical element and checks whether the remaining structure can bridge the gap through beam continuity, catenary action and two-way slab behaviour, backed by adequate horizontal and vertical ties. The complementary route is specific local strengthening of key elements, and because this is an extreme accidental scenario the check permits large deformation and plastic response with reduced load factors.

伦敦千禧桥开放当天为什么会剧烈横向晃动，行人只是正常走路？

Why did the London Millennium Bridge sway violently on opening day when pedestrians were merely walking normally?

行人步行除竖向力外还有约 1 Hz 的横向摆动分力，而桥的几阶侧向模态频率恰好落在这个范围。桥面一旦轻微横移，行人会本能调整步伐维持平衡，结果步频与桥的摆动同步，持续沿摆动方向做功，形成同步侧向激励的正反馈；这是人与结构相互作用而非简单共振，响应在人数超过临界值后突然放大。整改方案不是增加刚度，而是安装三十七个粘性阻尼器和多个调谐质量阻尼器大幅提高侧向阻尼比，使临界人数远高于实际通行量。
Walking produces not only vertical force but a lateral component near 1 Hz, close to several of the bridge's lateral mode frequencies. Once the deck moved sideways slightly, pedestrians instinctively adjusted their gait to keep balance, synchronized with the motion and kept feeding energy into it — a pedestrian-structure interaction with positive feedback rather than simple resonance, amplifying abruptly once crowd size passed a critical value. The retrofit did not stiffen the bridge but added thirty-seven viscous dampers plus tuned mass dampers to raise lateral damping so the critical crowd size far exceeds real traffic.

在悬索桥中，主缆的垂度如何影响桥的刚度和用钢量？

In a suspension bridge, how does main-cable sag affect stiffness and steel use?

垂度小则主缆更平、水平拉力更大，需要更强锚碇和更多钢材，但桥面竖向刚度好、变形小；垂度大则缆力缓和、省钢，却刚度下降、容易在活载下挠曲。设计要在经济性与刚度间权衡。
Low sag flattens the cable, raising horizontal tension so stronger anchors and more steel are needed, but deck stiffness and small deflection improve. High sag eases cable force and saves steel yet lowers stiffness and allows more live-load sag—a trade-off between economy and rigidity.

在软土地基上建高层建筑时，为什么有时采用桩筏基础而不是独立基础？

On soft soil, why use a pile-raft foundation instead of isolated footings for tall buildings?

软土承载能力低且压缩性高，独立基础会产生过大沉降甚至差异沉降。桩筏基础把筏板与深入硬土层的桩结合，既由桩承担大部分荷载，又靠筏板协调各桩沉降，整体刚度与稳定性更好。
Soft soil has low bearing capacity and high compressibility, so isolated footings cause excessive or differential settlement. A pile-raft combines a slab with piles reaching stiff strata: piles carry most load while the raft equalizes settlements, giving better overall stiffness and stability.

什么是空气动力颤振，设计师如何避免长跨桥梁发生扭转失稳？

What is aeroelastic flutter and how do designers avoid torsional instability in long bridges?

颤振是风致振动与结构弹性耦合后的自激发散现象，当风速超过临界值时扭转模态被持续放大，可能导致桥梁破坏。设计通过风洞试验优化断面形状、增加气动措施和扭转刚度，把临界风速提高到远超实际风速。
Flutter is a self-excited divergence where wind-induced motion couples with elasticity; above a critical wind speed the torsional mode grows until failure. Designers use wind-tunnel tests to shape the deck, add aerodynamic devices and torsional stiffness, raising the critical speed well above reality.

在斜拉桥中，为什么拉索振动需要加装阻尼器来抑制？

In cable-stayed bridges, why are dampers needed to suppress cable vibration?

长拉索质量轻、阻尼小，容易在风或雨激振下发生大幅横振甚至风雨振，长期会导致锚具疲劳。在索上或近锚处加装粘滞阻尼器能提高系统阻尼，快速衰减振动、保护锚固。
Long stays are light with little damping, so wind or rain excites large vibrations, even rain-wind induced, risking anchor fatigue. Adding viscous dampers near the cable raises system damping, quickly decaying motion and protecting anchors.

How does base isolation make buildings safer during earthquakes?

Flexible rubber or sliding bearings between the building and ground lengthen its period and absorb seismic energy, so the upper structure moves far less than the shaking ground.

为什么金属在远低于强度的应力下反复受力也会突然断裂？

Why does metal suddenly break under stress far below its strength when the load repeats?

这是疲劳失效。金属在循环应力下，即使峰值远低于屈服强度，微裂纹也会在应力集中处萌生；每一次循环都让裂纹尖端的塑性区反复滑移，裂纹以每循环若干纳米的速率扩展。当裂纹长到临界长度，裂尖应力强度因子K超过材料的断裂韧性Kc时，剩余截面瞬间失稳断裂，宏观上毫无预兆。断口上的贝壳状疲劳辉纹可以反推裂纹的扩展历史。飞机、桥梁、涡轮叶片因此必须按"无限寿命"或"损伤容限"设计并定期检修。
This is fatigue failure. Under cyclic stress — even when the peak is far below yield — microcracks nucleate at stress raisers, and each cycle drives repeated slip in the plastic zone at the crack tip, advancing the crack a few nanometers per cycle. Once the crack reaches a critical length where the stress-intensity factor K exceeds the material's fracture toughness Kc, the remaining section snaps instantly with no macroscopic warning. Clam-shell fatigue striations on the fracture surface can reveal the crack's growth history. Aircraft, bridges, and turbine blades are therefore designed for infinite life or damage tolerance and are inspected on strict schedules.

受压细长杆为什么会“屈曲”而不是被压碎，欧拉临界载荷公式抓住了什么物理本质？

Why does a slender compressed column buckle rather than crush, and what physics does Euler's critical load capture?

当杆足够细长和柔，侧向弯一点所需的弯曲刚度，会小于继续正直受压的趋势，于是它走“弯着更省力”的捷径失稳。欧拉公式说明临界力反比于长度平方、正比于弯曲刚度。
When a member is slender, the stiffness to bend slightly is less than the urge to keep compressing straight, so it takes the 'easier' bowed path and buckles. Euler's formula shows critical load scales with bending stiffness and inversely with length squared.

断裂力学里的“应力强度因子”是干嘛的，它怎么判断一条裂纹会不会继续长大？

What is the stress intensity factor in fracture mechanics, and how does it tell whether a crack will keep growing?

它描述裂纹尖端附近应力场的强弱，随外载和裂纹长度变化。把算出的K和材料的断裂韧性K_IC比较，K超过临界值裂纹就失稳扩展，否则安全。
It characterizes the stress field near a crack tip and grows with load and crack length. Comparing computed K to the material's fracture toughness K_IC tells you: exceed the critical value and the crack runs; otherwise it is safe.

为什么许多超高层大楼顶部挂着几百吨重的"大铁球"？

Why do many supertall towers carry a huge steel ball near the top?

那是调谐质量阻尼器（TMD）。风或地震让大楼摇晃时，悬挂的重球以相反相位摆动，其惯性力把大楼的振动能量"吸收"掉。球的质量、吊杆长度须与建筑固有频率精确匹配（调谐）：楼晃得越大，球摆得越凶，抵消得越多。台北101的阻尼器重约660吨，能让楼顶位移减小三到四成。
That is a tuned mass damper (TMD). When wind or a quake makes the tower sway, the suspended ball swings in opposite phase, and its inertia force absorbs the building's vibrational energy. The ball's mass and suspension length must be precisely tuned to the structure's natural frequency: the harder the building sways, the harder the ball swings back, canceling more motion. Taipei 101's damper weighs about 660 tonnes and cuts roof displacement by 30-40%.

Why are suspension bridge main cables shaped like a catenary curve?

Under its own weight a flexible cable naturally hangs in a catenary, a shape where the tension is nearly uniform and most efficient; deck load shifts it toward a parabola.

什么是地基的承载力，工程师如何判断一块土地能承受多大重量？

The main cable of a suspension bridge is made of thousands of steel wires twisted together, hanging from tower tops in a catenary (approximated as a parabola in design). Deck loads pass through vertical hangers into the cable, which then carries the force to the towers and the anchorages at both ends: tension flows along the curve, the towers take compression, and the anchorages resist the horizontal pull. The whole system works like a drawn bow, and the cable's strength sets how far the bridge can span.

斜拉桥和悬索桥都是"吊"起来的桥，有什么不一样？

错缝让每块砖跨在下层两块砖之上，局部荷载可以沿约四十五度向外扩散到更大面积，整片墙像一个整体协同受力。若竖缝上下对齐，砂浆缝就形成一条贯通的薄弱面，砂浆的抗拉抗剪远低于砖块，裂缝会顺着这条通缝直接开裂，墙体也失去砖块之间的咬合抗剪能力。因此传统砌法和规范都要求丁顺搭接、错缝长度不小于砖长的四分之一左右，转角与交接处还要用整砖咬砌来保证整体性。
Staggered bonding makes each brick bridge two bricks below, so a local load spreads outward at roughly 45 degrees over a wider area and the wall acts as one composite element. Aligned vertical joints instead create a continuous plane of mortar, which is far weaker than the units in tension and shear, so cracks run straight down that line and the wall loses the interlock that transfers shear between bricks. Masonry practice and codes therefore require overlapping patterns with laps typically at least about a quarter of a brick length, and corners and intersections are toothed with whole bricks to preserve continuity.

The triangle is one of the most stable shapes. A truss uses many triangles to spread the loads from traffic and self-weight and transfer them to the piers, preventing the beams from bending or twisting.
