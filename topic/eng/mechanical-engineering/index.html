普朗特(Prandtl)的边界层概念解决了什么流体难题？

What fluid problem did Prandtl's boundary-layer concept solve?

早先用理想无黏流理论算不出物面附近的阻力，普朗特在 1904 年提出边界层：贴近壁面很薄一层黏性主导、速度从零渐增到主流。这把黏性影响局限在薄层内，使外流可用势流求解，奠定了现代空气动力学。
Ideal inviscid theory could not predict drag near surfaces; Prandtl proposed in 1904 a thin boundary layer where viscosity dominates and velocity rises from zero to the freestream. Confining viscosity to that layer lets the outer flow be treated as potential flow, founding modern aerodynamics.

什么是疲劳破坏，工程上如何预防？

What is fatigue failure and how is it prevented in engineering?

疲劳破坏是指材料在远低于其静强度的反复交变应力下，经过许多循环后突然断裂的现象。工程上通过优化几何形状避免应力集中、表面强化处理、限制工作应力以及定期无损检测来预防疲劳失效。
Fatigue failure is the sudden fracture of a material under repeated cyclic stresses far below its static strength, after many load cycles. Engineers prevent it by avoiding stress concentrations through geometry, surface hardening, limiting operating stress, and performing periodic non-destructive inspection.

什么是“屈服强度”和“极限抗拉强度”？如何从应力—应变曲线得到它们？

What are yield strength and ultimate tensile strength, and how are they obtained from a stress-strain curve?

屈服强度是材料开始发生塑性变形时的应力，对无明显屈服平台的金属常取0.2%残余应变偏移法确定。极限抗拉强度是拉伸曲线上最大工程应力，出现在颈缩之前；二者均由单轴拉伸试验得到。
Yield strength is the stress at which a material begins plastic deformation; for metals without a clear yield point it is found by the 0.2% offset method. Ultimate tensile strength is the maximum engineering stress on the tensile curve before necking; both come from a uniaxial tensile test.

直列四缸发动机为什么天生存在二阶往复惯性力，需要平衡轴来抵消？

Why does an inline four-cylinder engine inherently produce second-order reciprocating forces that need balance shafts?

活塞运动不是纯简谐：由于连杆长度有限，其加速度可展开为频率等于曲轴转速的一阶项，加上频率为两倍转速的二阶项，二阶项幅值大致按曲柄半径与连杆长度之比缩放。直列四缸相邻曲拐相位相差一百八十度，一阶惯性力互相抵消，但四个活塞的二阶分量同相叠加，形成沿气缸轴线、频率为转速两倍的合力，曲轴配重无法消除。因此排量较大的四缸机常加两根以两倍转速反向旋转的 Lanchester 平衡轴，用偏心质量抵消这一激励，代价是增加零件、摩擦与成本；直列六缸和水平对置布局则从相位上天然抵消该项。
Piston motion is not purely sinusoidal: because the connecting rod has finite length, acceleration expands into a first-order term at crank speed plus a second-order term at twice crank speed whose amplitude scales roughly with the ratio of crank radius to rod length. In an inline four the crank throws are 180 degrees apart so first-order forces cancel, but all four second-order components act in phase and sum into a net force along the cylinder axis at twice engine speed, which crankshaft counterweights cannot remove. Larger four-cylinder engines therefore add two Lanchester balance shafts counter-rotating at twice crank speed whose eccentric masses oppose that excitation, at the cost of extra parts, friction and money, while inline sixes and flat layouts cancel the term by phasing alone.

What is finite element analysis (FEA) and how is it applied in mechanical design?

有限元分析是一种把复杂结构离散成许多小单元、再用数值方法求解应力应变分布的工程计算方法。设计者用它来预测零件在载荷下的变形、强度和疲劳寿命，从而在没有实物样机前就优化结构。
Finite element analysis is a computational method that discretizes a complex structure into many small elements and solves for stress and strain distributions numerically. Designers use it to predict deformation, strength, and fatigue life under loads, optimizing structures before building physical prototypes.

几何公差（GD&T）与普通尺寸公差有何区别？位置度和最大实体原则有什么用？

How does geometric dimensioning and tolerancing (GD&T) differ from ordinary size tolerances, and what are position tolerance and the maximum material principle used for?

尺寸公差只约束孔轴等要素的大小，几何公差则用公差框格控制形状、方向、位置和跳动，比如平面度、垂直度、位置度，能更明确地表达装配功能要求。位置度以理论正确尺寸定位基准，允许公差带为一个圆柱区，轴或孔在该圆柱内即合格。最大实体原则（MMC）在要素处于最大实体状态时给最小公差，尺寸偏离时公差随之放大，既保证装配不干涉，又放宽加工成本。
Size tolerances only bound features such as hole or shaft diameter, whereas GD&T uses feature control frames to limit form, orientation, location and runout, for example flatness, perpendicularity and position, expressing assembly requirements precisely. Position tolerance locates a feature with basic dimensions and allows a cylindrical tolerance zone the axis must lie in. The maximum material condition (MMC) gives the tightest tolerance when the feature is at its largest material size and enlarges the zone as size departs, guaranteeing assembly without interference while relaxing machining cost.

什么是“应力集中”？为什么在孔、缺口和台阶处特别容易发生？

What is stress concentration, and why does it especially occur at holes, notches, and steps?

应力集中是指构件几何不连续处（如孔、缺口、台阶、圆角）局部应力显著高于名义应力，因为力线在此拥挤。其程度用理论应力集中系数 K_t 量化，疲劳裂纹往往从这些高应力点萌生。
Stress concentration is the local elevation of stress around geometric discontinuities such as holes, notches, fillets, and steps, where stress flow lines crowd together. It is quantified by the theoretical stress concentration factor K_t, and fatigue cracks often initiate at these high-stress points.

什么是基孔制与基轴制？配合分为哪几类？为什么轴比孔易加工时优先采用基孔制？

What are hole-basis and shaft-basis systems, what classes of fit exist, and why is hole-basis preferred when the shaft is easier to machine?

基孔制以基准孔H为基准，改变轴的公差带位置得到不同配合；基轴制以基准轴h为基准，改变孔的公差带。配合按间隙分为三类：间隙配合（恒有间隙，可相对运动）、过盈配合（恒有过盈，靠变形传递力）、过渡配合（可能间隙可能过盈，用于定位）。孔一般用铰刀、镗刀等定尺寸刀具加工，改变孔径要换刀，而轴由车刀调整即可，所以优先固定孔、调整轴，这就是基孔制更常用的原因。
In the hole-basis system the basic hole H is fixed and shaft tolerance zones are varied to create different fits; the shaft-basis system fixes the basic shaft h and varies the hole. Fits divide into three classes by clearance: clearance fits (always a gap, allowing relative motion), interference fits (always tight, transmitting load by deformation) and transition fits (either, used for location). Holes are usually made with fixed-size tools such as reamers or boring bars, changing hole size means changing tooling, while the shaft is sized simply by adjusting the lathe tool, so it is cheaper to hold the hole and vary the shaft, which is why the hole-basis system dominates.

焊缝中的气孔和夹渣是如何形成的？焊接热裂纹又是什么机制？

How do porosity and slag inclusions form in welds, and what mechanism causes hot cracking?

气孔源于熔池中溶解的气体（氢、氮或一氧化碳）在快速凝固时来不及逸出而被困在焊缝中，氢的主要来源是水分和油污，因此焊前清理和干燥极为重要。夹渣是熔渣未充分浮出或前道焊渣未清净被夹在焊道之间，多因焊接速度过快、电流过小或坡口清理不彻底。热裂纹发生在凝固末期，因晶界存在低熔点共晶（如硫磷偏析物），在收缩应力下沿晶界开裂，与硫磷含量和接头拘束度密切相关。
Porosity forms when dissolved gases in the pool (hydrogen, nitrogen or carbon monoxide) are trapped as the weld metal solidifies quickly; hydrogen comes mainly from moisture and oil, so pre-cleaning and drying matter greatly. Slag inclusions result when slag fails to float out or previous weld slag is not fully removed and gets trapped between passes, typically from excessive speed, low current or poor groove cleaning. Hot cracking occurs at the end of solidification when low-melting-point eutectics (e.g. sulphur and phosphorus segregates) along grain boundaries tear under shrinkage stress; it is closely tied to S and P content and joint restraint.

精密主轴的滚动轴承为什么要施加预紧，把游隙『消掉』？

Why are the rolling bearings of a precision spindle preloaded to remove internal clearance?

存在游隙时只有承载区的少数滚动体接触，轴在载荷方向会先发生一段几乎无阻力的自由位移，刚度低且载荷方向反转时产生冲击与噪声。轴向预紧（成对角接触球轴承对顶安装、加预紧弹簧或磨配隔套）让所有滚动体始终受载，接触刚度提高、轴心位置确定，从而抑制振动、提高回转精度和系统固有频率。预紧过大则接触应力与摩擦发热上升，温升又因内外圈热膨胀差进一步加大预紧，形成恶性循环并缩短寿命，所以高速主轴多用定压弹簧预紧，并按转速、润滑方式与允许温升折中取值。
With clearance present only a few rolling elements carry load, the shaft can move almost freely for a short distance in the load direction, stiffness is low, and reversals cause impact and noise. Axial preload — back-to-back angular contact ball bearings, springs or ground spacers — keeps every rolling element loaded, raising contact stiffness, fixing the shaft centre and lifting both running accuracy and system natural frequency. Excessive preload raises contact stress and frictional heat, and the temperature difference between inner and outer rings then increases preload further in a runaway loop that shortens life, so high-speed spindles favour constant-pressure spring preload set as a compromise among speed, lubrication and allowable temperature rise.

Inside is a hydraulic cylinder: as the piston strokes up and down it forces oil through small orifices and valve discs, and viscous drag converts mechanical energy into heat. The spring stores energy and softens the impact; the damper bleeds that stored energy away, and together they settle the body quickly. Damping can differ between extension and compression strokes to balance comfort and handling.

机油桶上的『5W-30』是什么意思，冬天为什么要选前面数字小的？

What does 5W-30 on a bottle of engine oil mean, and why pick a lower first number for winter?

这是多级粘度标号：W 前的数字对应低温性能，用规定低温下的启动与泵送粘度上限分级，数字越小表示低温流动性越好；后面的数字对应 100 ℃ 时的运动粘度区间以及高温高剪切粘度要求。冷启动时机油若太稠，油泵来不及把油送到轴瓦和凸轮，发动机磨损主要就发生在这几十秒里，所以严寒地区选 0W 或 5W。高温档也不能随意降低，否则油膜太薄承载不了轴承比压；随着行驶里程增加，添加剂耗尽和剪切变稀会让油品跌出等级，这正是需要按里程换油的原因。
It is a multigrade designation: the number before W is a low-temperature class defined by limits on cranking and pumping viscosity at specified cold temperatures, so a smaller number means better cold flow, while the second number sets the kinematic viscosity band at 100 °C plus high-temperature high-shear requirements. On a cold start, oil that is too thick keeps the pump from reaching bearings and cams, and most engine wear happens in those first tens of seconds, which is why 0W or 5W grades suit severe winters. The high-temperature grade cannot simply be lowered either, since a thinner film may not carry bearing pressure, and additive depletion plus shear thinning eventually push used oil out of grade, which is what oil-change intervals address.
