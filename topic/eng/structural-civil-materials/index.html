金属疲劳破坏有何特征，S-N 曲线在设计中如何应用？

What characterizes metal fatigue failure, and how is the S-N curve used in design?

疲劳破坏在低于静强度的循环应力下萌生微裂纹并逐步扩展，通常无明显塑性预兆、断口可见疲劳辉纹。S-N 曲线给出应力幅与失效循环数的关系，设计取低于疲劳极限或规定寿命对应应力以确保安全。
Fatigue failure initiates and grows microcracks under cyclic stress below static strength, often with little plastic warning and fatigue striations on the fracture. The S-N curve relates stress amplitude to failure cycles; design uses stress below the fatigue limit or the value for a target life.

什么是碱-硅酸反应（ASR）？为什么掺粉煤灰能抑制它引起的混凝土膨胀开裂？

What is the alkali-silica reaction (ASR), and why does adding fly ash suppress the expansive cracking it causes in concrete?

水泥水化释放的钾、钠离子使孔隙液pH高达13～14，足以溶解骨料中的活性二氧化硅，生成吸水的碱硅凝胶；凝胶肿胀内压一旦超过抗拉强度，就形成网状“地图裂缝”。粉煤灰玻璃相早期消耗氢氧化钙、拉低孔隙液碱度，其低钙C-S-H产物又能固结碱离子并细化孔道、阻碍水分迁移，因而显著减小膨胀。
Alkalis released by cement hydration push pore-solution pH to about 13-14, dissolving reactive silica from aggregates into a hygroscopic alkali-silica gel; once swelling pressure exceeds tensile strength, map-shaped network cracking appears. Fly ash glass consumes calcium hydroxide and lowers pore alkalinity, and its low-calcium C-S-H binds alkalis while refining pores and blocking moisture paths, sharply reducing expansion.

什么是混凝土的碳化，它对结构耐久性有什么影响？

What is concrete carbonation, and how does it affect structural durability?

空气中二氧化碳渗入混凝土孔隙，与氢氧化钙反应生成碳酸钙，使孔溶液碱度下降。当碳化前沿到达钢筋表面时，钝化膜破坏、钢筋开始锈蚀，进而胀裂混凝土、削弱截面。
Atmospheric CO2 penetrates concrete pores and reacts with calcium hydroxide to form calcium carbonate, lowering pore solution alkalinity. When the carbonation front reaches the rebar, the passive film breaks down and corrosion starts, cracking concrete and reducing section.

什么是纤维增强复合材料的层间剪切强度，它对结构失效有何意义？

What is the interlaminar shear strength of fiber-reinforced composites, and why does it matter for failure?

层间剪切强度衡量相邻铺层界面抵抗沿厚度方向剪切分离的能力。它通常远低于纤维方向强度，是层合板最薄弱的环节，易在弯曲或冲击下发生分层，限制结构承载。
Interlaminar shear strength measures a laminate's resistance to shear separation between plies along the thickness. It is usually far lower than in-fiber strength, making delamination under bending or impact the weakest failure mode and limiting load capacity.

什么是结构的冗余度，它对建筑安全有何意义？

What is structural redundancy, and why does it matter for building safety?

冗余度指结构在局部构件失效后仍能依靠其他路径继续承重的能力。冗余度高的结构对偶然荷载或损伤不敏感，能防止连续倒塌，是稳健设计的重要原则。
Redundancy is a structure's ability to keep carrying load through alternate paths after a local member fails. Highly redundant structures resist progressive collapse under accidental loads or damage, a key principle of robust design.

高性能混凝土中矿物掺合料（如粉煤灰、矿渣）改善耐久性的机理是什么？

By what mechanism do mineral admixtures like fly ash and slag improve durability of high-performance concrete?

粉煤灰、矿渣等具有火山灰活性，与水泥水化产生的氢氧化钙反应生成额外水化硅酸钙，细化孔隙、降低渗透性。同时降低水化热与碱骨料反应风险，提升抗侵蚀与长期体积稳定性。
Fly ash and slag are pozzolanic: they react with calcium hydroxide from cement hydration to form additional C-S-H, refining pores and lowering permeability. They also reduce heat of hydration and alkali-silica reaction risk, improving resistance and long-term stability.

预应力混凝土通过什么方式提升构件的承载能力？

How does prestressed concrete improve a member's load capacity?

在荷载作用前对混凝土施加预压应力，使受拉区在外荷载下先抵消部分拉应力，从而推迟开裂、减小挠度并提高抗裂与刚度。常用先张法或后张法建立预应力。
Before service loads, concrete is pre-compressed so that tension under load is partly offset, delaying cracking, reducing deflection, and raising crack resistance and stiffness. Prestress is typically introduced by pretensioning or post-tensioning.

往混凝土里故意引入亿万个微小气泡，为什么反而能提高它的抗冻融能力？

Why does deliberately stirring billions of tiny bubbles into concrete improve its resistance to freezing-and-thawing damage?

冻害源于毛细孔水结冰约9%的体积膨胀带来的静水压，及孔隙液盐浓度差形成的渗透压。引气剂靠表面活性作用，在拌合中形成并稳定大量20–200μm的封闭气泡。硬化后气泡密布，间距系数一般不超过250μm，冰晶旁多余水分即可就近挤入气泡泄压，切断结冰应力的累积路径，抑制微裂缝扩展与表层剥落；代价是含气量每增1%强度约降5%。
Frost damage is driven by hydrostatic pressure from the roughly 9% volume expansion of pore water freezing, plus osmotic pressure from salt concentration gradients in the pores. Air-entraining agents use surface activity to create and stabilise vast numbers of closed 20–200 μm bubbles during mixing; once hardened, the bubbles lie close together (spacing factor normally kept below about 250 μm), letting excess water near growing ice squeeze into a nearby void to relieve pressure and cut off the build-up of freezing stress behind microcracking and surface scaling. The price is about 5% compressive-strength loss per 1% of extra air.

钢筋混凝土中钢筋和混凝土为何能协同工作？

Why can steel rebar and concrete work together in reinforced concrete?

钢筋与混凝土两者线膨胀系数接近，温度变化下变形协调；混凝土硬化后与钢筋粘结良好，并把钢筋包裹保护免受锈蚀。钢筋抗拉、混凝土抗压，二者互补形成高效复合材料。
Rebar and concrete have similar thermal expansion coefficients, so they deform together under temperature changes, and hardened concrete bonds well to steel and shields it from corrosion. Steel resists tension while concrete resists compression, forming an efficient composite.

隔震支座在抗震设计中起什么作用？

What role do seismic isolation bearings play in earthquake-resistant design?

隔震支座安装在基础与上部结构之间，延长结构基本周期、避开场地震动主频，并消耗地震输入能量。它使上部结构加速度反应大幅降低，从而保护构件与内部设备。
Isolation bearings sit between foundation and superstructure, lengthening the fundamental period to avoid the dominant site frequency and dissipating input energy. They greatly reduce superstructure acceleration, protecting members and equipment.
