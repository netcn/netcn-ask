测温度时热电偶和热电阻该怎么选？

When measuring temperature, how do you choose between a thermocouple and an RTD?

热电偶利用两种金属接点的温差电动势，量程宽可达上千摄氏度，响应快、结构结实、成本低，但灵敏度低、需要冷端补偿，精度一般。热电阻如铂电阻靠金属阻值随温度变化测温，在零下二百到六百摄氏度范围内精度高、稳定性好、线性优良，但价格较高、响应较慢且需要考虑引线电阻。高温炉窑用热电偶，实验室和过程精密控温多用铂电阻。
A thermocouple uses the thermoelectric voltage of a junction between two metals, offering a wide range up to thousands of degrees Celsius, fast response, rugged construction and low cost, but low sensitivity, a need for cold-junction compensation and modest accuracy. A resistance thermometer such as a platinum RTD infers temperature from metal resistance, giving high accuracy, good stability and excellent linearity from about minus two hundred to six hundred degrees Celsius, at higher cost, slower response and with lead resistance to consider. Furnaces use thermocouples, while laboratories and precise process control usually use platinum RTDs.

差压式流量计是怎么用压力算出流量的？

How does a differential-pressure flow meter compute flow from pressure?

在管道中放置孔板或文丘里管，流体经过缩口时流速增大、静压下降，前后压差与流速平方近似成正比。测出压差再开平方并乘以标定系数，就得到流量。这种方法结构简单、无活动部件、适应高温高压，但压损较大，且小流量时压差很小导致相对误差增加。
An orifice plate or Venturi tube is placed in the pipe; as fluid passes the constriction its velocity rises and static pressure falls, and the pressure difference across it is roughly proportional to the square of velocity. Taking the square root of the measured differential and multiplying by a calibration coefficient gives the flow rate. The method is simple, has no moving parts and tolerates high temperature and pressure, but it causes appreciable pressure loss and its relative error grows at low flow where the differential is tiny.

传感器的量程、精度和分辨率分别指什么？

What do a sensor's range, accuracy and resolution each mean?

量程是传感器能正常测量的最小到最大范围，超出会饱和甚至损坏。精度描述读数与真值的接近程度，通常以满量程的百分比表示，包含线性度、重复性和温漂等误差。分辨率是它能分辨的最小变化量。分辨率高不等于精度高，比如一台仪表可以显示到零点零一度，但整体误差仍有半度。
Range is the span from minimum to maximum that a sensor can measure properly, beyond which it saturates or is damaged. Accuracy describes how close the reading is to the true value, usually expressed as a percentage of full scale and covering linearity, repeatability and thermal drift. Resolution is the smallest change it can distinguish. High resolution does not imply high accuracy: an instrument may display hundredths of a degree while its overall error is still half a degree.
