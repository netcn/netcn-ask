金属3D打印常用哪种技术？

What technique is common for metal 3D printing?

选区激光熔融（SLM）用高功率激光逐层熔化金属粉末并凝固成形，可制造复杂内腔与轻量化结构，常用于航空航天零件。
Selective Laser Melting (SLM) fuses metal powder layer by layer with a laser, building complex internal channels and lightweight parts for aerospace.

预测性维护靠什么信号判断故障？

What signals does predictive maintenance use?

常用振动、温度、电流和声学信号，例如轴承早期磨损会使振动频谱出现特定频率峰值，据此提前报警。
It uses vibration, temperature, current and acoustic signals; early bearing wear shows characteristic peaks in the vibration spectrum, enabling early alarms.

OPC UA在工厂里起什么作用？

What role does OPC UA play in a factory?

OPC UA是跨平台、安全的工业通信标准，让不同厂商的设备与上位系统（如MES、SCADA）互通数据，是信息互联互通的关键协议。
OPC UA is a secure, cross-platform industrial standard letting devices from different vendors exchange data with MES or SCADA, key to interoperability.

MES系统管什么？

What does a MES system manage?

制造执行系统（MES）位于车间层，负责排产、工序跟踪、物料与质量数据采集，连接上层ERP与底层设备，让生产透明可控。
Manufacturing Execution Systems sit on the shop floor, handling scheduling, tracking, material and quality data, bridging ERP above and machines below.

工业4.0的核心思想是什么？

What is the core idea of Industry 4.0?

它把物联网、信息物理系统（CPS）与智能工厂结合，让设备、产品和系统互联并自主决策，实现定制化、柔性化的高效生产。
It links IoT, cyber-physical systems and smart factories so machines, products and systems interconnect and self-decide, enabling flexible, customized production.

机器人焊接比人工好在哪？

What are the advantages of robotic welding?

机器人重复定位精度高、可连续工作且参数稳定，焊缝质量一致，适合大批量、长焊缝和有害环境下的作业。
Robots weld with high repeatability and stable parameters, giving consistent seams and suiting high-volume or hazardous work.

It offers higher bandwidth and faster real-time performance over one network carrying control, video and IT data, simplifying factory architecture.

SCADA系统做什么？

What does a SCADA system do?

数据采集与监控系统（SCADA）从远端设备采集数据并下发控制指令，广泛用于电力、水务、油气等基础设施的集中监控。
Supervisory Control and Data Acquisition gathers data from remote devices and sends commands, used to centrally monitor power, water and oil infrastructure.
