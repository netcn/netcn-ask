混流式机组在部分负荷下运行时,尾水管内的涡带为什么会诱发压力脉动?顶盖补气又是如何抑制它的?

Why does the vortex rope in a Francis turbine's draft tube induce pressure pulsation at part load, and how does head-cover air admission suppress it?

部分负荷时转轮出口环量增大,水流在尾水管内卷成螺旋形涡带,其偏心进动使管壁压力周期性波动,并可上传至机组引发功率摆动和厂房振动。从顶盖或主轴中心孔向尾水管入口补入适量空气,气体的弹性缓冲吸收了涡带低压核的能量,削平压力峰值并瓦解其刚性旋转。补气量须经模型试验标定,补得过多反而拉低水轮机效率。
At part load the swirl leaving the runner intensifies and the flow winds into a helical vortex rope inside the draft tube; the rope's eccentric precession makes wall pressure oscillate periodically, feeding power swings into the unit and vibration into the powerhouse. Admitting a metered amount of air through the head cover or the shaft center hole surrounds the rope's low-pressure core with an elastic gas volume, flattening pressure peaks and disrupting its rigid rotation. The correct admission rate is calibrated by model tests, since excessive air drags down turbine efficiency.

#尾水管涡带 #压力脉动

混凝土重力坝沿建基面的抗滑稳定验算为什么普遍采用抗剪断强度公式而非纯摩擦公式?

Why do stability checks of concrete gravity dams along the foundation plane generally apply the shear-friction formula instead of pure friction?

纯摩擦公式只计法向压力乘摩擦系数,忽略岩体与混凝土胶结面的黏聚力,既偏保守也无法体现帷幕灌浆与排水的加固效益。抗剪断公式同时计入黏聚力c′与摩擦系数f′,与SL 319和美国垦务局准则一致,更贴合剪摩破坏机制。设计还需对最不利深层块体做抗滑复核,不足时设置抗力齿墙或预应力锚索。
The pure-friction formula counts only normal pressure times a friction coefficient and ignores the cohesion of the bonded rock-concrete interface, making it overly conservative and blind to the benefit of grout curtains and drainage. The shear-friction formula includes both cohesion c′ and friction coefficient f′, consistent with SL 319 and US Bureau of Reclamation criteria, matching the shear-slip failure mechanism far better. Designs additionally verify deep-seated critical blocks, adding shear keys or prestressed anchors where margin is insufficient.

#重力坝 #抗滑稳定

混凝土重力坝靠什么顶住上游的水推力?坝底的扬压力为什么会成为抗滑稳定设计的控制因素?

What holds a concrete gravity dam against upstream water thrust, and why does base uplift become the controlling factor in sliding stability design?

重力坝以自身重量在坝基接触面上产生的摩阻力抵抗水压力、泥沙压力等水平推力,抗滑稳定安全系数必须满足规范限值。库水沿坝基裂隙渗入会在坝底形成竖直向上的扬压力,大面积抵消坝体有效重量,因而成为抗滑稳定的控制性不利作用;帷幕灌浆配合排水孔幕一般能把扬压力削减三成以上,从而明显收窄坝体断面、减少混凝土用量。
A gravity dam resists horizontal thrusts such as water and silt pressure through friction mobilized by its own weight on the foundation contact plane, and the sliding safety factor must satisfy code limits. Reservoir water percolating along foundation fissures builds a vertical uplift pressure beneath the base that cancels a large share of the effective dam weight, making it the governing adverse action for stability; a grout curtain backed by drain holes typically cuts uplift by more than thirty percent, noticeably slimming the section and saving concrete.
