铣削时突然出现刺耳啸叫和振纹，为什么调整主轴转速常常比减小进给更有效？

When milling suddenly screams and leaves chatter marks, why does changing spindle speed usually help more than cutting feed?

这是切削中的再生颤振：刀齿在上一转留下的波纹表面上切削，切厚随之波动，切削力波动又激起机床—刀具系统的模态振动，若相邻两转之间振动相位不利，振幅就自激放大。稳定性取决于主轴转速与结构模态频率之比，因此在转速—切深平面上存在交替的稳定与不稳定区（稳定性叶瓣图），转速落在叶瓣峰值处可用的极限切深比谷底大出数倍。工程上通过锤击试验测出频响函数算出叶瓣图来选转速，或采用不等齿距刀具和变转速调制打破再生反馈，而单纯减小进给对颤振门限影响很小。
This is regenerative chatter in cutting: each tooth machines a surface already waved by the previous pass, so chip thickness oscillates, the fluctuating force excites the machine-tool structure's modes, and an unfavourable phase between successive revolutions makes the vibration self-amplify. Stability depends on the ratio of spindle speed to modal frequency, producing alternating stable and unstable regions in the speed-depth plane — the stability lobe diagram — where sitting on a lobe peak allows several times the limiting depth of cut available in a valley. Practice measures the frequency response by tap testing to compute those lobes and select speed, or breaks the regenerative feedback with variable-pitch cutters and spindle-speed modulation, whereas simply reducing feed barely moves the chatter threshold.

In injection molding, why is the holding phase crucial against sink marks and dimensional error?

After the cavity fills, the melt cools and shrinks; without hold pressure the interior vacates, causing sink marks and undersize parts. The holding phase keeps pushing extra melt in to compensate shrinkage, ensuring flat surfaces and stable dimensions.

生鲜果蔬气调包装为什么必须让薄膜透气率与内容物的呼吸速率相匹配，而不是透气性越高越好？

Why must film permeability in modified-atmosphere packaging of fresh produce match the contents' respiration rate rather than simply being as high as possible?

直答：薄膜是果蔬与外界之间的气体交换阀，只有氧气与二氧化碳透过量分别等于呼吸耗氧和产二氧化碳的速率，袋内才能稳定保持低氧高二氧化碳的目标气氛。机理：呼吸速率随温度近乎指数上升，膜的渗透遵循溶解扩散规律且温敏性弱；透气太快则袋内接近空气组分，抑菌与降呼吸作用落空，透气太慢则氧气耗尽转入无氧发酵，乙醇乙醛累积导致异味与腐败，故须按呼吸强度与贮运温度核算膜厚与面积。
The film works as a gas-exchange valve between produce and the outside world: only when oxygen ingress and carbon dioxide efflux equal the rates consumed and released by respiration will the headspace settle stably at the target low-oxygen, high-carbon-dioxide mix. Respiration climbs almost exponentially with temperature while film permeation follows solution-diffusion behavior and is far less temperature-sensitive. A film too open leaves the pack near ordinary air and forfeits antimicrobial and respiration-suppressing effects; one too tight exhausts the oxygen into anaerobic fermentation, where ethanol and acetaldehyde build up into off-flavors and rot — so thickness and area must be computed from respiration intensity and storage temperature.

运输包装设计里，为什么缓冲衬垫并不是越厚越软就越安全，反而要按产品脆值和预计跌落高度精确匹配选材？

In transport packaging design, why is thicker, softer cushioning not automatically safer — why must pad material be matched precisely against the product's fragility and the expected drop height?

直答：缓冲垫靠自身变形吸收跌落动能，把传递给产品的最大加速度压到脆值以内，而每种材料只在特定静应力区间才具有最高吸能效率。机理：材料常用缓冲系数衡量，即所受应力除以单位体积吸收的应变能，其曲线存在低谷；衬垫过软会在一次冲击中被压到底再硬性回弹，加速度反而陡增，过硬则初始刚度过大致峰值超标，过厚徒增成本与体积，故须按产品重量、承压面积与跌落高度对照缓冲曲线确定尺寸。
A cushion's job is to deform and absorb the kinetic energy of the fall so the peak acceleration reaching the product stays below its fragility limit, yet every material delivers peak energy absorption only across a narrow static-stress band. Materials are rated by the cushion factor — stress divided by strain energy absorbed per unit volume — whose curve dips to a minimum. A pad too soft bottoms out and rebounds harshly, spiking acceleration; one too stiff starts rigid and overshoots the limit; extra thickness just inflates cost and volume, so dimensions come from weight, bearing area, and drop height read off cushion curves.

金属激光选区熔化的零件为什么要连着基板做去应力处理，切下来就会变形？

Why must laser powder-bed metal parts be stress relieved while still on the build plate, or they warp when cut free?

激光熔池局部升温到熔点以上又在毫秒级冷却，熔化区收缩受周围已固化材料约束，逐层累积形成很大的残余应力，通常表层为拉应力、内部为压应力，量级可接近材料屈服强度。零件与基板和支撑连在一起时这些应力被机械约束住，一旦线切割分离，约束解除后应力重分布就表现为翘曲、扭曲甚至开裂。因此工艺上先带板进行去应力退火或热等静压，再切离基板；同时用分区（棋盘）扫描策略、基板预热、优化支撑布局和留出机加余量共同控制变形。
A laser melt pool is heated above melting and cooled within milliseconds, and the shrinking fused region is restrained by already-solid material, so layer after layer builds large residual stresses, typically tensile near the surface and compressive inside, approaching the material's yield strength. While the part stays fixed to the build plate and supports those stresses are mechanically restrained, but wire-cutting it free removes the restraint and the redistributing stress appears as warping, distortion or cracking. Practice therefore anneals for stress relief with the part still on the plate, or applies hot isostatic pressing, before separation, complemented by island or chequerboard scan strategies, plate preheating, optimized supports and machining allowance.

生产线上说『Cpk 只有 1.0 不达标』，Cp 和 Cpk 到底衡量什么？

When a production line is told its Cpk is only 1.0, what exactly do Cp and Cpk measure?

Cp 用公差带宽度除以六倍过程标准差，只衡量过程的离散程度能否装进公差，完全不管分布是否居中；Cpk 取规格上下限到均值距离与三倍标准差之比中的较小者，同时反映离散与偏移，因此 Cpk 永远不大于 Cp。Cpk 等于 1.0 意味着均值到最近规格限刚好三个标准差，对应约每百万件一千三百多件超差，过程稍有漂移就会大量报废，所以汽车行业普遍要求量产 Cpk 不低于 1.33、关键特性不低于 1.67。使用前提是过程处于统计受控状态且分布近似正态，若控制图显示存在异常波动，必须先消除特殊原因，能力指数才有意义。
Cp divides the tolerance width by six process standard deviations, judging only whether the spread fits inside the specification and ignoring centring, while Cpk takes the smaller of the distances from the mean to each specification limit divided by three sigma, capturing both spread and offset so it can never exceed Cp. A Cpk of 1.0 means the mean sits exactly three sigma from the nearest limit, about 1350 parts per million beyond it, and any drift produces heavy scrap, which is why automotive practice generally requires 1.33 in production and 1.67 for critical characteristics. Both indices assume a stable, roughly normal process, so if control charts show special-cause variation those causes must be removed before a capability number means anything.

塑料件表面为什么常有几个圆形浅印，侧壁还都带一点斜度？

Why do injection-moulded parts often show a few round shallow marks, and why are their side walls slightly tapered?

圆印是顶针留下的：制品冷却收缩后会抱紧模芯，开模时需要顶针从背面把它推出，接触处压力集中便留下顶针印，所以顶针位置通常安排在非外观面，直径也要与壁厚匹配以免顶白或顶穿。侧壁的斜度叫脱模斜度，一般每侧半度到两度，用来让制品脱模时侧壁迅速与钢件分离、减小摩擦和拉伤，没有斜度可能顶不出来或表面被拉花。分型线（两半模具的合缝痕）和浇口痕同样是工艺留下的必然特征，设计时只能安排它们的位置而无法完全消除。
The round marks come from ejector pins: as the part cools it shrinks onto the core, so pins must push it off from behind at mould opening, and the concentrated contact pressure leaves a witness mark, which is why pins go on non-cosmetic faces and are sized to the wall to avoid blush or push-through. The wall taper is draft, usually half a degree to two degrees per side, letting the wall break away from the steel quickly and reducing friction and drag marks; without it the part may refuse to eject or come out scuffed. Parting lines from the two mould halves and gate vestiges are similarly unavoidable process signatures, so designers choose where they fall rather than eliminating them.
