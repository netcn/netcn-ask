为什么机器人能认出前面的东西？

Why can a robot recognize what is in front of it?

机器人身上装着摄像头、超声波等传感器，像眼睛和耳朵一样收集信息。电脑把这些信息比对后，就知道前面是墙、是人还是球。
A robot carries sensors like cameras and ultrasonic beacons that act as eyes and ears. Its computer compares the data and tells whether the object ahead is a wall, a person, or a ball.

为什么洗衣机能把衣服洗干净？

Why can a washing machine clean clothes?

洗衣机把衣服和水、洗衣粉一起翻来翻去。水和肥皂把脏东西松开来，再被水流冲走。
A washing machine tumbles clothes with water and soap. The soap loosens the dirt and the flowing water carries it away.

为什么自动售货机知道该掉出哪样零食？

Why does a vending machine know which snack to drop?

你按的按钮变成电信号，告诉机器要第几排第几个格子。机器转动小马达推开那格的弹簧，零食就掉下来了。
Your button press becomes an electric signal telling the machine which row and slot you chose. A small motor turns a coil that pushes the snack out of its spot.

为什么电磁炉不用火也能烧热锅？

Why can an induction cooktop heat a pan without fire?

电磁炉通电流后产生看不见的磁场，让铁锅自己里面产生电流发热。锅热了菜就熟了，炉面本身却不太烫。
An induction cooktop sends current that makes an invisible magnetic field, which drives current inside an iron pan so the pan heats itself. The food cooks while the surface stays fairly cool.

为什么电动车不用汽油也能跑？

Why can an electric car move without petrol?

电动车靠大电池供电，电让马达转起来带动车轮。没有排气管，跑起来也安安静静。
An electric car runs on a big battery; the electricity spins a motor that turns the wheels. With no exhaust pipe, it moves along quietly.

为什么踩刹车车就会停？

Why does a car stop when you press the brake?

刹车片紧紧夹住车轮上的圆盘，制造摩擦。摩擦把车子跑的力气变成热，车就慢下来停下。
Brake pads squeeze a disc on the wheel and create friction. The friction turns the car's motion into heat, so the car slows and stops.

为什么吸尘器能把灰尘吸走？

Why can a vacuum cleaner suck up dust?

吸尘器里面有个小风扇，转起来把空气往外抽。里面的空气变少，外面的空气带着灰尘被吸了进去。
A vacuum has a small fan that pushes air out. With less air inside, outside air rushes in and carries dust along with it.

Why do escalator steps keep looping around?

Why does a TV respond when you press the remote?

When you press it, the remote sends invisible infrared light that a small sensor on the TV receives. The TV reads the command and changes channel or volume.

Why can a microwave heat food so fast?

为什么老式蒸汽火车能靠烧水前进？

Why could old steam trains move by boiling water?

火车把水烧成蒸汽，蒸汽力气很大往筒里冲，推动活塞来回动。活塞带动车轮，火车就开起来了。
The train boils water into steam, and the forceful steam pushes a piston back and forth. The piston turns the wheels, and the train rolls along.
