什么是车路协同系统（V2X）？

What is a vehicle-to-everything (V2X) system?

车路协同让车辆与车辆、道路、行人和网络实时交换信息，提前感知盲区风险和交通状态。它是自动驾驶和主动安全的重要支撑，可显著降低事故率。
V2X lets vehicles exchange information in real time with other vehicles, roads, pedestrians, and networks, sensing blind-spot risks and traffic states early. It underpins autonomous driving and active safety, markedly cutting crash rates.

什么是BRT快速公交系统？

What is a Bus Rapid Transit (BRT) system?

BRT是用专用车道、水平登乘、优先信号和封闭站台模拟地铁服务的公交系统。它以远少于地铁的造价提供大容量、准点、快速的公共交通。
BRT uses dedicated lanes, level boarding, signal priority, and enclosed stations to mimic metro service by bus. It delivers high-capacity, punctual, fast transit at a fraction of metro cost.

高速公路入口匝道信号明明在限制车辆进入主线，为什么这种限流措施反而能减少所有出行者的总延误？

Freeway ramp meters deliberately restrict vehicles from entering the mainline — why can such rationing actually reduce the total delay borne by all travelers?

直答：交通流存在临界密度，主线密度一旦越过临界值，车流失稳且通行能力还会额外跌落约百分之五到十，匝道控制的实质就是把主线状态锁定在临界密度以下的最佳流率附近。机理：密度低于临界点时流量随密度上升，突破瓶颈能力后激波回荡、排队向上游蔓延，形成越堵越少的恶性循环；匝道信号以每次放行一两辆车的节奏调节汇入率，用匝道上数十秒的有序等待换取主线数千辆车的稳定通行，系统总延误因此下降。
Traffic flow has a critical density: once mainline density crosses it, flow destabilizes and capacity drops an extra five to ten percent, so metering essentially pins the mainline near its best flow rate below that point. Below critical, throughput rises with density; beyond it, shockwaves roll back and queues march upstream, producing the perverse regime where more cars mean fewer vehicles through. Releasing cars one or two at a time paces the merge, so dozens of seconds of orderly waiting on the ramp buy steady movement for thousands on the mainline and total system delay falls.

什么是道路的视距，为什么重要？

What is sight distance on a road and why is it important?

视距是驾驶员能看清前方路面障碍并安全停车或避让的最短距离。弯道和纵坡设计必须保证足够视距，否则容易引发追尾和冲出道路的事故。
Sight distance is the shortest length over which a driver can see an obstacle ahead and stop or avoid it safely. Curve and grade design must guarantee enough sight distance, or rear-end and run-off-road crashes become likely.

什么是立交桥，它解决什么问题？

What is a grade-separated interchange and what problem does it solve?

立交桥让不同方向的车流在不同高度交叉通过，彼此不发生平面冲突。它消除了红绿灯等待，提高了主干道的通行能力和安全性。
A grade-separated interchange lets conflicting traffic streams cross at different heights without meeting at one level. It removes signal waits and raises the capacity and safety of main roads.

什么是交通流的三要素？

What are the three fundamental elements of traffic flow?

交通流三要素是流量（单位时间通过的车辆数）、速度（车辆行驶快慢）和密度（单位长度内的车辆数）。它们满足流量等于密度乘以速度的基本关系，是分析拥堵的基础。
The three fundamentals of traffic flow are flow (vehicles per unit time), speed (how fast they move), and density (vehicles per unit length). They obey the basic relation flow equals density times speed and form the basis of congestion analysis.

在高速公路入口匝道设信号灯让车辆排队等待，为什么反而能让整条高速跑得更快？

Why does holding cars in queues behind ramp signals at freeway entrances actually let the whole freeway run faster?

直答：匝道控制并非削减出行需求，而是错开汇入时刻，防止主线车流密度越过临界点后崩溃。机理：高速公路流量存在对应临界密度的最大值，一旦超限，车流从稳定态跃入拥堵态，瓶颈断面流量骤降约百分之五到十并产生向上游传播的走停激波，通行能力不升反降;控制系统依据下游检测器实测占有率实时调节红灯时长，把主线密度维持在临界值附近，富余车辆以匝道排队形式有序蓄存，主线吞吐量与车速同步提升。
Metering does not suppress travel demand; it staggers merge instants so mainline density never crosses the point where flow breaks down. Freeway throughput peaks at a critical density, and past it traffic flips into a congested regime: bottleneck discharge falls some five to ten percent below capacity while stop-and-go shockwaves march upstream. The controller tunes red time against live occupancy readings from downstream detectors, holding mainline density near the critical value and storing the surplus as orderly ramp queues, so both throughput and speed rise together.

城市主干道实施绿波协调控制时，为什么沿线各交叉口必须采用统一的公共周期，并按行车速度设定相邻路口之间的相位差？

When an urban arterial runs coordinated green-wave signaling, why must every intersection along it share one common cycle length, with offsets between neighboring junctions set from the running speed?

直答：统一公共周期使全线绿灯启闭节拍一致，相位差按相邻路口间距除以车队平均行驶速度整定，车队到达下游时恰好赶上绿灯放行。机理：各路口周期若不统一，信号间的时间对应关系每循环漂移一次，绿波带无法稳定存在；按行程时间设定相位差后，头车抵达下游停车线的时刻落入绿波带，后车鱼贯通行，停车次数与平均延误同步下降；对向车流则用镜像相位差另设一条反向波带。
One common cycle keeps every intersection ticking in step, and the offset equals the spacing between junctions divided by the platoon's average running speed, so cars arrive downstream just as the light turns green. With differing cycles the timing alignment drifts every cycle and no stable band can exist; once offsets match travel time, the lead car reaches the downstream stop line inside the green band and followers roll through, cutting both stops and average delay, while opposing traffic gets its own band through mirrored offsets.

什么是自行车的刹车原理？

What is the braking principle of a bicycle?

自行车刹车靠刹车块压紧车轮边缘或碟盘，借助摩擦力把滚动的动能转化为热能耗散。捏紧把手力越大，摩擦力越大，减速越快。
A bicycle brake presses pads against the wheel rim or disc, using friction to turn rolling kinetic energy into heat. Squeezing the lever harder raises friction and slows the bike faster.

为什么高速公路上没有红绿灯？

Why are there no traffic lights on highways?

高速公路通过全封闭、出入口控制和立体交叉，让车流各行其道、互不干扰。车辆不必在路口停下等待，因此能保持高速、连续地行驶。
Highways keep traffic separate through full access control, limited entries, and grade-separated interchanges, so flows do not conflict. Vehicles need not stop at intersections and can keep moving at high, continuous speed.

什么是轨道交通？

What is rail transit?

轨道交通是车辆在固定钢轨上运行的公共运输方式，包括地铁、轻轨和火车。它运量大、准点、能耗低，是城市和大区域骨干交通的重要形式。
Rail transit is public transport with vehicles running on fixed steel rails, including metro, light rail, and trains. With high capacity, punctuality, and low energy use, it is a backbone of urban and regional transport.

城市路口红绿灯的时长是怎么定出来的？什么是一个信号交叉口的最佳周期？

How are the green-light durations at urban intersections determined, and what counts as the optimal cycle for a signalized junction?

直答：并非凭经验估定，经典设计用Webster公式求延误最小的最优周期C0=(1.5L+5)/(1-Y)，其中L为一周期内各相位损失时间之和，Y为各相位关键流量比之和。机理：周期过短时损失时间占比过高，交叉口很快饱和;周期过长则红灯等候被整体拉长，平均延误反而上升。Webster以交叉口平均延误最小为目标推导出上式，再按各相位流量比分配有效绿灯时间，这一框架至今仍是定时配时设计的起点。
Not trial and error: the classic Webster formula yields the delay-minimizing cycle C0=(1.5L+5)/(1-Y), where L totals the lost time of all phases per cycle and Y sums the critical flow ratios. A too-short cycle lets lost time dominate and saturates the junction, while a too-long cycle stretches every red wait and pushes average delay back up. Deriving from minimal mean intersection delay, Webster then splits effective green in proportion to phase flow ratios — a framework that still anchors fixed-time signal design today.
