石墨烯是什么，为什么被称为神奇材料？

What is graphene and why is it called a wonder material?

石墨烯是仅一层碳原子组成的二维蜂窝状薄片，导电导热极快、强度极高又几乎透明。它有望用于更快的芯片、柔性屏和高效电池，但大面积廉价制造仍难。
Graphene is a one-atom-thick honeycomb of carbon with superb conductivity, heat transport and strength yet transparency; it promises faster chips, flexible screens and better batteries, though cheap large-scale making remains hard.

How is tempered glass made safer?

为什么大多数金属冷却凝固时会收缩，而水结冰反而膨胀？

Why do most metals shrink on cooling but water expands when freezing?

金属原子在固态晶格中排列比液态更紧密，所以凝固时体积缩小。水的冰晶因氢键形成开放的六方结构，固态反而比液态占据更大空间，因此结冰膨胀、密度变小。
Metal atoms pack more tightly in the solid lattice than in the liquid, so freezing shrinks volume. Water's ice crystals form an open hexagonal structure via hydrogen bonds, occupying more space solid than liquid, so it expands and gets less dense.

不锈钢为什么不像普通钢那样生锈？

Why doesn't stainless steel rust like ordinary steel?

铬对氧的亲和力高于铁，钢见空气便自发生成一层几个纳米厚、连续致密的氧化铬钝化膜，隔断氧和水、挡住离子穿膜，腐蚀速率因此下降几个数量级；划伤后只要有氧，铬重新氧化便把膜补上。普通钢的铁锈疏松含水、比基体大又易剥落，腐蚀层层向内推进。不锈钢不是不腐蚀而是靠钝化：缺氧缝隙、氯离子或酸性环境会破膜起点蚀，铬含量得先过一道门槛。
Chromium's affinity for oxygen beats iron's, so on contact with air the steel grows a continuous, dense chromium-oxide passive film only a few nanometers thick, shutting out oxygen and water and blocking ions across it, which cuts the corrosion rate by orders of magnitude; where it is scratched, chromium re-oxidizes and reseals the film as long as oxygen is present. Rust on ordinary steel is porous hydrated iron oxide, bulkier than the metal beneath, poorly bonded and flaking, so corrosion presses inward layer by layer. Stainless is not immune but passive: oxygen-starved crevices, chloride or acid service break the film and start pitting, and chromium content must first pass a threshold.

奥氏体不锈钢明明很耐蚀，为什么在含氯离子的热环境里会突然开裂？

Austenitic stainless steel resists corrosion well, so why does it crack suddenly in hot chloride environments?

这是氯离子引起的应力腐蚀开裂：拉应力（包括焊接残余应力）、敏感材料与氯离子介质同时存在时，氯离子在局部击穿钝化膜，裂尖金属持续溶解而侧壁重新钝化，裂纹便以极窄的分支形态穿晶扩展。宏观上几乎没有失重和塑性变形，外观看不出腐蚀，却可能在远低于屈服强度的应力下穿透管壁，典型场景是保温层下的 304/316 管道和泳池上方的不锈钢吊挂件。对策包括改用高钼、双相或超级奥氏体钢，焊后消除残余应力，控制保温层浸水与氯含量，并避免不锈钢长期停留在 50–150 ℃ 的含氯冷凝环境。
This is chloride stress-corrosion cracking, which needs tensile stress (including welding residual stress), a susceptible alloy and chloride ions together: chlorides locally break down the passive film, the crack tip keeps dissolving while flanks repassivate, and a very narrow branching transgranular crack advances. There is almost no weight loss or visible deformation, yet a wall can be penetrated at stresses far below yield, the classic cases being 304/316 piping under wet insulation and stainless suspension parts above swimming pools. Countermeasures are higher-molybdenum, duplex or super-austenitic grades, post-weld stress relief, keeping insulation dry and low in chloride, and avoiding prolonged service in chloride condensate around 50–150 °C.

在合金设计中，固溶强化和析出强化分别靠什么机制提高强度？

In alloy design, how do solid-solution and precipitation strengthening raise strength?

固溶强化靠把异类原子溶入基体晶格，引起晶格畸变从而阻碍位错运动。析出强化则让细小第二相颗粒从过饱和固溶体中析出，位错必须切过或绕过这些颗粒，需要额外应力，因此显著提高强度。
Solid-solution strengthening dissolves alien atoms into the lattice, distorting it and hindering dislocation motion. Precipitation strengthening lets fine second-phase particles precipitate from a supersaturated solution; dislocations must cut through or bypass them, requiring extra stress and raising strength.

金属增材制造为什么要充惰性气体、打印完还要热处理去应力？

Why is metal additive manufacturing done in inert gas, with heat treatment after printing?

这是金属增材制造中的粉末床熔融工艺：激光或电子束按切片数据逐层扫描金属粉末，每层熔化凝固后铺新粉再熔，层层堆积成零件。激光工艺必须在氩气或氮气等惰性气氛中进行，防止高温熔池被氧化——氧化会让材料变脆并产生气孔；电子束工艺则在真空室中完成，真空同样隔绝了氧。但逐层快速熔凝带来两大工程难题：一是残余应力大，热应力会让薄壁件翘曲开裂，所以需要支撑结构和打印后的去应力热处理；二是凝固组织方向性强（柱状晶），力学性能存在各向异性，常需热等静压消除内部孔隙。它擅长制造复杂内腔和拓扑优化结构，但成本和速度仍高于传统加工。
This is the powder-bed fusion process of metal additive manufacturing: a laser or electron beam scans each sliced layer over metal powder, melting and solidifying it, then fresh powder is spread and melted again, building the part up layer by layer. Laser-based processes run in an inert atmosphere (argon or nitrogen) to keep the molten pool from oxidizing — oxidation embrittles the material and creates pores; electron-beam melting instead works in a vacuum chamber, which excludes oxygen just as effectively. Rapid layer-wise melting brings two hard problems: large residual stress, which warps and cracks thin features (hence supports and stress-relief heat treatment), and strongly directional columnar solidification giving anisotropic mechanical properties, usually remedied by hot isostatic pressing to close internal pores. It excels at complex internal channels and topology-optimized parts, but remains slower and costlier than conventional machining.

为什么往纯金属里掺一点点其他元素，性能就大不一样？

Why does adding a small amount of another element drastically change a metal's properties?

纯金属的原子排列规则，位错很容易滑移，所以纯铝、纯铁又软又弱。加入合金元素后，原子尺寸差异引起晶格畸变，阻碍位错运动，这叫固溶强化；还能析出细小的第二相颗粒把位错"钉住"，即沉淀强化。钢就是铁中加入约0.8%的碳，再通过控制冷却让碳化物析出，强度成倍提升；而奥氏体到马氏体的相变则是钢热处理强化的核心机制。
In pure metals atoms line up regularly and dislocations glide easily, so pure aluminum or iron is soft and weak. Adding alloying elements distorts the lattice with their different atomic sizes, blocking dislocation motion — solid-solution strengthening — and tiny second-phase particles can also pin dislocations, which is precipitation hardening. Steel is iron with about 0.8% carbon; controlling the cooling makes carbides precipitate and multiplies strength. 为什么工程陶瓷的强度要用韦布尔分布来描述，而金属常给一个确定的屈服强度？

陶瓷几乎没有塑性变形能力，裂纹尖端的应力无法通过屈服被削平，构件强度由体内或表面最严重的那个缺陷决定，是典型的最弱链环问题。缺陷尺寸随机分布，同批试样的断裂强度离散很大，需要用韦布尔分布的特征强度与形状参数 m 来表征，m 越小说明缺陷分散越严重。由此还产生尺寸效应：体积越大越可能含有大缺陷，平均强度反而下降，所以陶瓷按允许失效概率取许用应力，并靠热压致密化和精细研磨减小缺陷，而不是去『提高屈服强度』。
Ceramics have almost no plasticity to blunt crack-tip stresses, so component strength is set by the single most severe flaw present, a classic weakest-link problem. Flaw sizes are randomly distributed, so measured fracture strengths scatter widely and are characterized by a Weibull characteristic strength plus a modulus m, where a small m means highly variable flaws. That also produces a size effect: larger volumes more likely contain a large flaw, so mean strength falls with size, and ceramic design assigns allowable stress from an accepted failure probability while hot pressing and fine polishing shrink flaws, rather than trying to raise a yield point.

Stainless steel contains chromium, which forms a thin, dense chromium-oxide film in air that seals the metal from water and oxygen, stopping further corrosion.

同一块木头，顺着纹路很难拉断，横着掰却容易开，这是为什么？

Why is a piece of wood very hard to pull apart along the grain yet easy to split across it?

木材由沿树干方向排列的管状细胞组成，细胞壁里的纤维素微纤丝也大致顺着这个方向，顺纹受拉时力沿着强度很高的纤维传递。横纹方向靠的是细胞之间较弱的木质素粘结和细胞壁的横向支撑，抗拉强度往往只有顺纹的几十分之一，裂缝顺着细胞界面很容易延伸。木工因此让承重梁的纹路顺着跨度方向，胶合板则把奇数层薄板交错九十度叠放，用互相垂直的纹理弥补横纹的弱点。
Wood is built from tube-like cells aligned with the trunk, and the cellulose microfibrils in their walls run roughly the same way, so pulling along the grain loads very strong fibres directly. Across the grain the load must pass through weaker lignin bonding between cells, so tensile strength perpendicular to the grain is often a small fraction of the parallel value and cracks run easily along cell boundaries. Carpenters therefore align beam grain with the span, and plywood glues an odd number of veneers at ninety degrees to each other so crossed grain compensates for the weak direction.

Iron reacts with water and oxygen to form flaky rust that keeps spreading; painting, galvanizing with zinc, or oiling blocks contact with air and moisture to prevent it.

Bulletproof glass bonds many hard glass layers with soft plastic interlayers; the hard layers spread the force and the soft layers absorb energy and catch shards. Ordinary glass just shatters into sharp pieces and cannot stop a bullet.

同样是用短纤维纺纱，为什么环锭纱的强力普遍高于转杯纱？

Why do ring-spun yarns come out consistently stronger than rotor-spun yarns made from the same staple fibers?

直答：环锭纱内纤维沿近似圆锥螺旋线排列，伸直平行度高，受拉时绝大多数纤维共同承载；转杯纱结构分层且含大量缠绕纤维，强力先天受限。机理：环锭加捻依靠钢丝圈张力使纤维内外转移，两端握持、张力连续，拉断前多数纤维先后断裂；转杯纱纤维在凝聚槽内呈单侧握持状态，外层缠包纤维仅起捆扎作用、芯层捻度不足，拉伸时滑移纤维占比高，成纱强力通常低一到两成。
Ring-spun fibers lie along near-conical helixes, straightened and parallel, so under tension nearly all of them carry load together; rotor yarns are layered structures laced with wrapper fibers that cap strength from the outset. Ring twisting uses traveler tension to migrate fibers inward and outward with both ends gripped under continuous tension, so most fibers must break before the yarn fails. Rotor fibers are gripped from one side only in the grooved channel: outer wrappers merely lash the bundle, the core carries little twist, and slipping fibers dominate under load, leaving strength some ten to twenty percent lower.
