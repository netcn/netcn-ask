被动式房屋为什么几乎不用空调也能冬暖夏凉？

Why does a passive house stay comfortable with almost no AC?

靠超级保温、无热桥、气密性和热回收新风，把冷热需求压到极低，主要靠环境得热和人体设备散热维持舒适。
Extreme insulation, airtightness, and heat-recovery ventilation shrink demand so comfort comes from passive gains, not active HVAC.

地源热泵为什么能在冬天从地下取热、夏天又把热存回去？

How does a ground-source heat pump take heat from the earth in winter and return it in summer?

地下恒温约十几度，埋管循环介质与土壤换热，冬天取热、夏天排热，效率比直接电热高数倍。
The ground stays about 15°C; buried loops exchange heat with soil, heating in winter and cooling in summer far more efficiently than resistance heat.

How do rain gardens and bioswales make stormwater slow down?

凹绿地和植被浅沟滞蓄、下渗并净化径流，延迟峰值、补充地下水，比直接排管更能防内涝和污染。
Bioswales and rain gardens detain, infiltrate, and clean runoff, delaying peaks and recharging groundwater better than pipes alone.

What is greywater reuse, can bath water really flush toilets?

把洗浴洗衣等较干净灰水经简单处理（过滤消毒）后用于冲厕、绿化，省下饮用水；需独立管道防混用。
Greywater from baths and laundry is filtered and disinfected for toilets and irrigation via separate pipes, saving potable water.

碳捕集与封存（CCS）做什么？

What does carbon capture and storage do?

它在电厂或工厂把烟气中的二氧化碳分离出来，压缩后注入地下岩层封存，减少温室气体进入大气，常配合提高采油率。
CCS separates CO2 from flue gas, compresses it and injects it into underground rock for permanent storage, cutting emissions, sometimes with enhanced oil recovery.

Why doesn't permeable pavement pool water like concrete?

透水砖和透水基层让雨水直接下渗补给地下水、减少径流和内涝；孔隙堵了需维护，否则也会失效。
Permeable bricks and base let rain infiltrate, recharging groundwater and cutting runoff; clogged pores must be maintained.
