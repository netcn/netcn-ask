核反应堆靠什么让链式反应"持续但可控"？

What keeps a nuclear reactor's chain reaction going but under control?

铀-235吸收一个中子裂变时平均放出2到3个快中子，如果每次裂变恰好留下一个中子引发下一次裂变，链式反应就以恒定功率自持，这对应有效增殖因子k等于1的临界状态。控制棒用硼或镉制成，强烈吸收中子，插入越深中子损失越多；慢化剂（轻水、重水或石墨）把快中子减速成热中子，因为铀-235对热中子的裂变截面大得多。反应堆通过升降控制棒、调节冷却剂中硼浓度和控制慢化剂量来微调k，再依靠功率负反馈（多普勒展宽使吸收增强）天然稳定，防止功率失控。
When uranium-235 absorbs a neutron it fissions and releases 2-3 fast neutrons on average; if exactly one neutron per fission triggers the next fission, the chain sustains itself at constant power, corresponding to an effective multiplication factor k of 1 — criticality. Control rods made of boron or cadmium absorb neutrons strongly, so deeper insertion means more neutron loss; moderators (light water, heavy water, or graphite) slow fast neutrons to thermal energies, where U-235's fission cross section is far larger. Reactors tune k by moving rods, adjusting dissolved boron concentration, and controlling moderator volume, then rely on negative power feedback — Doppler broadening that increases absorption as temperature rises — for inherent stability against runaway power.

钙钛矿/硅叠层电池为什么能突破单结硅电池的 Shockley–Queisser 效率上限？

How do perovskite/silicon tandem cells exceed the Shockley–Queisser limit that caps single-junction silicon?

单结电池的极限主要来自光谱失配：能量低于带隙的光子完全不被吸收，高于带隙的光子多余能量以热载流子弛豫成热，两项损失把 1.1 eV 硅的理论效率压在约 33% 以内。叠层把带隙约 1.6–1.7 eV 的钙钛矿放在顶层吸收蓝绿光并输出更高电压，让红外光透射到底部硅电池吸收，热化损失大幅减少，双结理论极限提高到 40% 以上。工程上两个子电池串联，需要电流匹配、透明复合（隧穿）层以及钙钛矿在湿热与紫外下的稳定性，目前实验室两端叠层认证效率已超过 30%。
The single-junction ceiling comes mainly from spectral mismatch: photons below the band gap are not absorbed at all, while excess energy of higher-energy photons thermalizes as heat, capping 1.1 eV silicon near 33 percent. A tandem puts a wide-gap perovskite of about 1.6–1.7 eV on top to harvest blue-green light at higher voltage and transmits infrared light to the silicon bottom cell, cutting thermalization losses and lifting the two-junction theoretical limit above 40 percent. In practice the sub-cells are series connected, so current matching, a transparent recombination or tunnel layer, and perovskite stability against humidity, heat and UV govern real devices; certified two-terminal tandems have now passed 30 percent.

为什么"碳中和"不只是少开车那么简单，难在哪儿？

Why is carbon neutrality far harder than simply driving less?

实现碳中和要在能源全链条发力：把电力系统的化石燃料换成风、光、水、核等零碳电源，再让交通、供暖、工业"电气化"——电动车、热泵、电炉，这又依赖大规模储能和特高压电网来支撑波动的新能源。钢铁、水泥、航空、化工等难减排行业需要氢冶金、碳捕集利用与封存（CCUS）等尚在商业化早期的技术。还要计算全生命周期排放，最后剩下的少量排放用生态碳汇或碳移除抵消。难点不只是技术，还有电网稳定性、成本以及数十年期能源基础设施的更新换代。
Reaching carbon neutrality demands action across the whole energy chain: replace fossil-fuel generation with zero-carbon wind, solar, hydro, and nuclear, then electrify transport, heating, and industry — EVs, heat pumps, electric furnaces — which in turn relies on massive storage and UHV grids to balance intermittent renewables. Hard-to-abate sectors like steel, cement, aviation, and chemicals need hydrogen metallurgy and carbon capture, utilization and storage (CCUS), still early in commercialization. Life-cycle emissions count too, and the small remainder is offset by carbon sinks or removal. The difficulty is not just technology but grid stability, cost, and replacing decades of energy infrastructure.

一片风电场里，为什么下风向排的风机发电量常明显低于前排？

Inside a wind farm, why do downwind turbines usually generate noticeably less than the front row?

上游风机从气流中取走动能后，后方形成风速降低、湍流增强的尾流区，尾流要靠与周围空气掺混才逐渐恢复，恢复距离常达叶轮直径的若干倍到十几倍。由于风功率与风速三次方成正比，尾流里风速降低百分之十几就可能让发电量下降三成，整场累计的阵列损失常在百分之几到二十左右。因此布机时要沿主导风向拉大行距并错列排布，还可用尾流偏航控制让前排略微偏航，牺牲自身一点出力换取全场总发电量提升。

风力发电机的叶片长达上百米,它为什么是柔韧的?退役叶片去哪了?

Wind turbine blades stretch a hundred meters yet stay flexible — and where do retired ones go?

现代叶片是玻璃纤维或碳纤维复合材料制成的空心翼型,设计上刻意允许叶尖在大风中挠动数米:柔性卸载阵风弯矩,减轻根部载荷与重量。变桨系统实时转动全叶片改变攻角,在超过额定风速时主动泄功保护机组。麻烦在后半生:热固性复合材料无法像金属一样重熔,早期叶片多被切碎填埋,欧洲部分国家已经立法禁填埋。新方案包括机械回收做水泥添加料、热解回收纤维,以及新一代可回收环氧树脂——让叶片在服役二十五年后能整体拆解回炉。
Modern blades are hollow airfoils of glass or carbon fiber composites, deliberately flexible: tips bend meters in gusts to shed bending moments, slimming roots and mass. Pitch systems rotate blades to change angle of attack, spilling power above rated wind to protect the drivetrain. Retirement is the headache: thermoset composites cannot remelt like metal, early blades were shredded into landfill, and several European states now ban that. New routes grind blades into cement kiln feedstock, pyrolyze fibers back out, or build with recyclable epoxy so twenty-five-year-old blades can finally come apart cleanly.

Dams generate power from held water — why does pumped storage pump at night and generate by day?

水力发电利用水位落差推动水轮机。抽水蓄能电站更进一步:夜里电网用电少、电价低,就用多余电力把下库的水抽上高库存着;白天用电高峰放水下坡发电,像给城市配了一块巨型充电宝。它的转换效率约75%,看似亏本,实则解决了风、光发电看天吃饭的波动问题——储能是清洁能源的搭档。
Hydropower rides water falling through turbines. Pumped storage goes further: nights with cheap surplus power lift water uphill into reservoirs; daytime peaks release it down through generators — a giant power bank for cities. Efficiency near seventy-five percent sounds lossy but tames the on-again-off-again nature of wind and solar. Storage partners clean energy.

屋顶光伏为什么要按当地纬度选一个倾角，朝向也不能随便定？

Why is a rooftop PV array tilted to a particular angle and aimed in a particular direction instead of laid flat?

组件发电量与阳光入射方向和板面法线夹角的余弦成正比，倾角大致取当地纬度时，全年正午前后的入射角最小、年发电量最高。北半球朝正南（南半球朝正北）能让上午与下午的出力曲线对称、峰值最大；朝东或朝西会削掉一部分峰值，但可以把发电挪到早晚用电高峰。若更看重冬季供电，可把倾角调大以迎合冬日太阳高度低的特点，平屋顶还要在倾角与阵列前后遮挡、积灰冲洗和抗风载荷之间折中。
Output scales with the cosine of the angle between sunlight and the module normal, so a tilt roughly equal to site latitude minimizes that angle around solar noon and maximizes annual yield. Facing true south in the northern hemisphere (true north in the southern) makes the morning and afternoon curve symmetric with the highest peak, while east or west orientations trade peak energy for output shifted toward morning or evening demand. A steeper tilt favours winter generation when the sun is low, and on flat roofs the chosen tilt must also balance inter-row shading, soiling washoff and wind loading.
