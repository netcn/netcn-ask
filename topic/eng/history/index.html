世界上第一座全铁桥为什么敢用铁代替石头木头？

Why did the first all-iron bridge dare replace stone and wood with iron?

1779年英国科尔布鲁克代尔铁桥用铸铁首次造出大跨度拱，证明工业铁件可做承重结构，开启金属桥梁时代。
The 1779 Iron Bridge in Britain used cast iron for a large arch, proving metal could carry loads and starting the metal-bridge era.

交流电和直流电之争，为什么最后家里用的是交流？

In the AC vs DC war, why did homes end up with AC?

交流电可用变压器轻松升压远距离输电、损耗小，战胜直流；家用电器的直流再由适配器变换。
AC won because transformers enable efficient long-distance high-voltage transmission; devices' DC comes from adapters.

电梯最早为什么让人敢坐，之前大家怕掉下去？

Why did people dare ride early elevators when they feared falling?

1853年奥的斯在博览会上演示砍断缆绳平台不坠的安全钳，证明有保险，才让公众接受电梯上高楼。
In 1853 Otis demonstrated a safety brake that held the platform after cutting the rope, convincing the public elevators were safe.

谁最早给房子配上钢筋和混凝土，让楼能又高又结实？

Who first put steel and concrete together so buildings could be tall and strong?

19世纪园艺师莫尼耶先以水泥裹钢筋做花盆，后推广到楼板管道；现代钢筋混凝土由此起步。
19th-century gardener Monier first reinforced concrete with iron for planters, then slabs and pipes, launching modern reinforced concrete.
