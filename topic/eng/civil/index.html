盾构机（TBM）怎么在地下“边挖边撑”，土压平衡和泥水平衡两种模式怎么防塌？

How does a TBM dig and support at once underground, and how do earth-pressure and slurry modes prevent collapse?

刀盘切削土体，密封舱里用削出的土屑或泥浆顶住开挖面，压力刚好抵消地下水与土压，防止坍塌。土压平衡靠螺旋机排土控压，泥水平衡靠循环泥浆护壁，各适合不同地层。
The cutterhead excavates while a sealed chamber uses muck or slurry to press the face, balancing groundwater and soil pressure to stop collapse. Earth-pressure mode controls pressure via a screw conveyor; slurry mode circulates mud to support the wall—each fits different ground.

围堰在水里施工时有什么用？

What is a cofferdam used for in construction over water?

围堰是用钢板桩或土石在水里围出一道临时挡水墙，把中间的水抽干，工人才能在干涸的坑里浇筑桥墩或地基，完工后再拆除。
A cofferdam is a temporary watertight wall of sheet piles or earth that pumps out the enclosed water so workers can build piers or foundations in the dry, then it is removed.

沉井法施工适合什么场景？

When is the caisson method suitable?

沉井是一个在地面预制、靠自重或压重逐层下沉的井筒结构，常用于桥梁墩台、泵站和深基坑，能在水下或软土中形成坚固井壁。
A caisson is a prefabricated shaft that sinks under its own weight, used for bridge piers, pump stations and deep pits, forming strong walls in water or soft soil.

堤坝（防洪堤）是怎样挡住洪水的？

How does a levee (flood bank) hold back floodwater?

堤坝是用土石堆成的高墙，把河水限制在河道里；内部常铺防渗层和排水，防止水渗过去把堤身泡软冲垮。
A levee is an earthen wall keeping river water inside its channel; an impermeable core and drainage stop seepage from softening and breaching the bank.

在松软的地基上，桩基础是怎样撑住房子的？

On soft ground, how do pile foundations support a building?

把长桩打到深处坚硬的土层或岩层，让建筑物的重量通过桩传到深处可靠的地层，避免软土下沉导致房屋开裂。
Long piles are driven deep to firm soil or rock, carrying the building's weight down to reliable layers so soft ground cannot sink and crack the structure.

城市的下水道系统是怎样保持干净的？

How does a city sewage system keep the city clean?

千家万户的废水和雨水通过埋在地下的管道靠坡度自流汇集，送到处理厂净化后再排放，避免脏水横流传播疾病。
Waste and rainwater flow by gravity through buried pipes to treatment plants that clean it before release, preventing disease from open sewage.

“海绵城市”是什么，为什么让城市像海绵一样能吸能放雨水能减少内涝？

What is a 'sponge city', and why letting the city absorb and release rainwater like a sponge reduces flooding?

它用透水铺装、下沉绿地、湿地和蓄水池代替硬质地面，让雨水就地渗入、滞蓄和净化，而不是全冲进管道。暴雨时削峰、晴时回补地下水，内涝和污染都减轻。
It replaces hard surfaces with permeable pavement, sunken green spaces, wetlands and storage to let rain infiltrate, detain and purify on site instead of rushing to pipes. Storms are peak-shaved and groundwater recharged, easing floods and pollution.

悬索桥靠什么承重？

What carries the load in a suspension bridge?

桥面荷载经吊索传到两根主缆，主缆再通过锚锭和桥塔把巨大拉力分散到地基，主缆受拉、桥塔受压，适合大跨度跨越。
Deck load travels via hangers to two main cables, which spread the tension to anchorages and towers; cables carry tension and towers compression, suiting long spans.

地铁或管廊的“管片”是什么，隧道挖出来后靠什么拼成坚固的圆筒？

What are segments in subways or utility tunnels, and what assembles the dug tunnel into a strong cylinder?

管片是工厂预制的弧形混凝土块，盾构每前进一环就把数块拼成圆圈，环环相扣并施加预应力螺栓，形成永久衬砌。它既是模板也是结构，扛住周围土水压。
Segments are factory-made curved concrete blocks; as the shield advances a ring, several are assembled into a circle and bolted with prestress, forming permanent lining. It is both formwork and structure, resisting surrounding soil and water pressure.

自来水厂是怎样把河水变成干净饮用水的？

How does a water treatment plant turn river water into clean drinking water?

先加药让泥沙和杂质凝结沉淀，再经过沙滤去掉细小颗粒，最后用氯或紫外线杀菌，达标后才通过水管送到各家各户。
Chemicals make suspended dirt clump and settle, sand filters catch fine particles, then chlorine or UV kills germs before the safe water flows to homes.

船闸是怎样让船在高低不同的水面之间通行的？

How do locks let boats pass between water levels of different height?

船闸像带门的水箱：关上门把船关进来，再注水或放水把水位升到或降到与另一段河面齐平，开门后船就能平顺通过。
A lock is a gated tank: it encloses the boat, then fills or empties to match the next water level, and opens so the boat passes smoothly between heights.

有些房子为什么用柱子架在水面上，像踩高跷？

Why are some houses built on stilts over the water?

把房子架在高高的柱子上，可以让风吹水流通、房子不被潮水泡坏，也方便在底下停小船，就像给房子穿了一双不怕水的高跷鞋。
Lifting the house on tall posts lets wind and water flow underneath, keeps it dry from tides, and gives room for boats, like waterproof stilt shoes for the home.

为什么修地铁要先把工地围起来挖很深的坑，而不是直接在地上打洞？

Why must subway construction wall off a site and dig a deep pit instead of just boring a hole from the surface?

城市地下管线密、土层松，明挖或先做竖井再暗挖能先支护好四周再往下走，避免塌方影响地面楼和路。深坑像给隧道先做个安全的“盒子”。
Urban underground is full of pipes and loose soil; cut-and-cover or shaft mining lets workers brace the sides before going down, avoiding collapses that would hurt surface buildings and roads. The deep pit is a safe 'box' for the tunnel.

马路为什么是一层一层铺起来的？

Why are roads built in layers?

最底下压实土基，往上铺碎石排水承重，再铺沥青或混凝土面层。分层让雨水排走、把重量均匀分散，路才不容易坑洼和塌陷。
A compacted soil base, then a stone layer for drainage and support, then asphalt or concrete on top; layers shed water and spread weight so the road resists potholes and sinking.
