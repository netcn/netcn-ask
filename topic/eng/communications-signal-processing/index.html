香农信道容量公式揭示了什么工程上的基本限制？

What fundamental engineering limit does Shannon's channel capacity formula reveal?

公式 C = B log2(1+S/N) 表明，在给定带宽 B 与信噪比 S/N 下，无差错传输速率存在上限。它指导工程师在带宽、功率与可靠性之间权衡，且仅靠增加带宽在噪声受限时收益递减。
C = B log2(1+S/N) shows that with given bandwidth B and SNR S/N there is a hard upper bound on error-free rate. It guides trade-offs among bandwidth, power, and reliability, and reveals diminishing returns from bandwidth alone when noise-limited.

5G 采用大规模 MIMO 相比传统 MIMO 带来哪些性能增益？

What performance gains does massive MIMO bring over conventional MIMO in 5G?

大规模 MIMO 在基站部署数十至数百天线，利用信道空间维度同时服务多用户（空分复用），成倍提升频谱效率。波束赋形增益提高信干噪比，并能用信道硬化抑制小尺度衰落。
Massive MIMO deploys tens to hundreds of base-station antennas, exploiting spatial dimensions to serve multiple users simultaneously (spatial multiplexing) and multiply spectral efficiency. Beamforming raises SINR and channel hardening mitigates small-scale fading.

正交频分复用（OFDM）如何应对多径衰落？

How does orthogonal frequency-division multiplexing (OFDM) cope with multipath fading?

OFDM 把宽带信道划分为大量正交窄带子载波，将频率选择性衰落转化为若干平坦子信道。配合循环前缀吸收多径时延，并在各子载波独立编码与均衡，显著降低均衡复杂度。
OFDM splits a wideband channel into many orthogonal narrow subcarriers, turning frequency-selective fading into several flat subchannels. A cyclic prefix absorbs multipath delay, and per-subcarrier coding and equalization greatly reduce equalizer complexity.

6G 候选技术中太赫兹通信面临的主要工程挑战是什么？

What are the main engineering challenges of terahertz communications among 6G candidate technologies?

太赫兹频段带宽极大但路径损耗与大气吸收随频率急剧上升，传播距离极短且易被障碍物阻断。器件方面高效功放与低噪声接收尚不成熟，波束管理与时频同步也更为苛刻。
Terahertz bands offer huge bandwidth but path loss and atmospheric absorption rise steeply with frequency, giving very short range and easy blockage. Efficient power amplifiers and low-noise receivers are immature, and beam management and synchronization are far stricter.

阵列天线的切比雪夫幅度加权为什么被称为给定副瓣电平下的最优综合?工程上采用它需要付出哪些代价?

Why is Chebyshev amplitude weighting called the optimum synthesis for a given sidelobe level in array antennas, and what price does a design pay for adopting it?

在指定副瓣电平约束下,切比雪夫分布给出最窄的主瓣宽度,即等纹波意义下的最优解。代价主要有三点:波束展宽使增益和孔径效率下降;两端单元激励幅度最大,对馈电网络精度和功率容量要求苛刻;被压低的副瓣呈等纹波分布,远离主瓣方向也不快速衰减。实际设计常取−30 dB左右作折中。
Under a prescribed sidelobe level, the Chebyshev distribution yields the narrowest attainable main beam—the optimum in the equal-ripple sense. The cost comes threefold: the broadened beam erodes gain and aperture efficiency; end elements require the largest excitation amplitudes, stressing feed-network accuracy and power handling; and the suppressed sidelobes ripple rather than decaying quickly far off boresight. Practical designs typically settle near −30 dB as a compromise.

什么是采样定理，为什么采样频率要高于信号最高频率的两倍？

What is the sampling theorem, and why must the sampling rate exceed twice the highest signal frequency?

采样定理指出，要无失真地由离散样本重建模拟信号，采样频率须大于信号最高频率的两倍（奈奎斯特率）。否则相邻频谱会混叠，使高频成分错误折叠进低频，无法恢复。
The sampling theorem states that to reconstruct an analog signal from samples without distortion, the sampling rate must exceed twice the highest frequency (Nyquist rate). Otherwise spectral copies alias, folding high frequencies into low ones irrecoverably.

傅里叶变换和拉普拉斯变换各解决哪类问题，为什么工程上常配合使用？

What kinds of problems do Fourier and Laplace transforms each solve, and why are they often used together in engineering?

傅里叶把信号拆成稳态的正弦分量，适合看频率和频谱；拉普拉斯多了指数衰减项，能处理暂态、增长和不稳定系统。工程上常先用拉普拉斯解响应，再借傅里叶看频率特性。
Fourier decomposes signals into steady sinusoids, good for frequency and spectrum; Laplace adds exponential terms to handle transients, growth and unstable systems. Engineers often solve response with Laplace, then read frequency behavior via Fourier.

什么是调制，为什么无线电通信需要对载波进行调制？

What is modulation, and why must radio communication modulate a carrier?

调制是用基带信息改变高频载波的幅度、频率或相位。调制的目的是把信号搬移到适合天线辐射与频道分配的频带，并支持多路复用与抗干扰传输。
Modulation varies a high-frequency carrier's amplitude, frequency, or phase with baseband information. It shifts the signal to a band suited for antenna radiation and channel allocation, and enables multiplexing and interference resistance.

什么是码分多址（CDMA），它如何实现多用户共享同一频段？

What is code-division multiple access (CDMA), and how does it let users share one frequency band?

CDMA 给每个用户分配互不正交但近似正交的扩频码，把窄带信号扩展到宽频带。接收端用相同码相关解扩，不同用户的信号因码间低相关性而相互区分，实现同频同时多址。
CDMA assigns each user a spreading code that is approximately orthogonal, spreading a narrowband signal over a wide band. The receiver despreads with the same code; other users' signals are separated by their low cross-correlation, enabling same-frequency same-time access.

相控阵雷达为什么不用转动天线？

Why doesn't a phased-array radar need to rotate?

它通过控制各单元发射信号的相位差，使合成波束电子扫描指向不同方向，转向快、可同时跟踪多目标，无需机械转动。
By controlling phase differences across elements it steers the beam electronically, scanning fast and tracking many targets without moving parts.

低通滤波器的作用是什么，一阶 RC 滤波器的截止频率如何确定？

What does a low-pass filter do, and how is a first-order RC filter's cutoff frequency determined?

低通滤波器允许低于截止频率的信号通过、衰减高频成分，用于去噪与抗混叠。一阶 RC 滤波器的截止频率由 f_c = 1/(2πRC) 给出，R 与 C 越大截止频率越低。
A low-pass filter passes signals below its cutoff and attenuates higher frequencies, used for denoising and anti-aliasing. A first-order RC filter's cutoff is f_c = 1/(2πRC); larger R or C lowers the cutoff.

为什么微带贴片天线的阻抗带宽通常只有百分之几,有哪些展宽带宽的工程手段?

Why does a microstrip patch antenna typically offer only a few percent of impedance bandwidth, and what engineering techniques widen it?

贴片可视为四周开缝的薄谐振腔,品质因数高、储存能量大而辐射损耗占比小,10 dB回波损耗相对带宽通常仅为百分之几。常用手段包括加厚基板或改用低介电常数材料、采用E形槽或L形探针馈电引入容性补偿抵消感性,以及层叠多谐振贴片构成耦合双谐振。工程上U形槽贴片配厚泡沫基板可将相对带宽做到20%以上。
A patch acts as a thin resonant cavity bounded by radiating slots; its high Q stores considerable energy while radiated loss stays small, so the 10-dB return-loss bandwidth is only a few percent. Common remedies include thicker substrates or low-permittivity materials, E-shaped slots and L-shaped probes whose capacitive loading cancels feed inductance, and stacked patches forming coupled dual resonances. In practice a U-slot patch over thick foam reaches relative bandwidths above 20%.

天线增益高代表什么？

What does high antenna gain mean?

增益高并不意味着凭空放大功率，而是把辐射能量更集中地投向某个方向（方向性更强），等效于在该方向看得更远。
High gain doesn't create power; it focuses radiation into a narrower direction, effectively reaching farther in that beam.
