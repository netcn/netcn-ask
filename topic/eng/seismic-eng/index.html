抗震钢筋混凝土框架为什么刻意遵循「强柱弱梁」，宁可让梁先破坏也不希望柱出铰？

Why do seismic reinforced-concrete frames deliberately follow the strong-column weak-beam rule, accepting beam damage rather than hinging in the columns?

直答：有意让柱强于梁是为了把塑性铰赶到梁端、避免柱端出铰，结构才能以整体延性的梁铰机制耗能而不倒塌。机理：柱是唯一的竖向传力通道，某层柱上下端同时出铰即形成危险的层侧移机构，可能引发整层乃至连续垮塌;分散于众多梁端的塑性铰各自转动耗能，楼层仍具承重能力。设计上要求同一节点柱端抗弯承载力之和乘以增大系数后不低于梁端之和，并对角柱从严加强。
The intent is to push plastic hinges into beam ends and keep them out of columns, so the frame dissipates energy through a ductile beam-hinge mechanism instead of collapsing. Columns are the sole gravity-load path; hinges at both ends of one story's columns form a dangerous soft-story mechanism that can drop that story and trigger progressive collapse. Hinges distributed over many beam ends rotate harmlessly while the story keeps carrying load. Codes therefore require the sum of column flexural capacities at a joint, times an amplification factor, to exceed the sum of beam capacities, with stricter rules for corner columns.

建筑隔震支座怎么减小地震破坏？

How do seismic isolation bearings reduce quake damage?

在建筑物与基础间设橡胶隔震支座，延长结构自振周期、避开地震主频，使上部结构运动大幅减弱，类似把楼浮在柔性层上。
Rubber bearings between building and ground lengthen the natural period away from the quake's dominant frequency, greatly reducing motion, like floating the structure on a soft layer.

为什么在建筑底部安装铅芯橡胶隔震支座能显著减轻地震对上部结构的破坏？

Why do lead rubber bearings installed at a building's base markedly reduce earthquake damage to the superstructure?

铅芯橡胶支座由橡胶层与薄钢板交替叠合、中央灌入铅芯制成。橡胶层水平柔韧，把结构基本周期从约0.5–1秒延长到3秒以上，避开地震动能量集中的频段，传入上部结构的加速度随之锐减；铅芯剪切屈服后消耗振动能量并限制支座位移，同时在风载与小震下保持足够刚度，使结构近乎整体平动，避免层间集中变形。
A lead rubber bearing stacks rubber layers bonded to thin steel shims around a central lead core. The flexible rubber lengthens the building's fundamental period from about 0.5–1 s to over 3 s, away from the band where earthquake energy concentrates, so the acceleration reaching the superstructure falls sharply. The lead core yields in shear, dissipating energy and capping displacement, yet stays stiff under wind and minor tremors, letting the building sway almost rigidly instead of deforming storey by storey.
