心室辅助装置（人工心脏泵）起什么作用？

What does a ventricular assist device do?

它用机械泵部分或完全代替衰弱心脏泵血，维持全身循环，常作为心脏移植前的过渡或终末期心衰的长期支持。
It uses a mechanical pump to partially or fully replace a failing heart's pumping, maintaining circulation as a bridge to transplant or long-term support.

胰岛素泵如何工作？

How does an insulin pump work?

它用微型泵按算法持续皮下输注基础量胰岛素，并在餐前追加大剂量，模拟健康胰腺的分泌节律，帮助糖尿病人控糖。
A micro-pump delivers a continuous basal insulin dose and meal-time boluses, mimicking healthy pancreatic rhythm to control blood sugar in diabetics.

人工肾（透析）依靠什么原理净化血液？

What principle does an artificial kidney (dialysis) use to clean blood?

透析利用半透膜两侧的浓度差，让小分子代谢废物（如尿素）和多余水分扩散到透析液中，而血细胞和大分子蛋白保留在血液里。
Dialysis relies on a semipermeable membrane: small wastes like urea and excess water diffuse into the dialysate while blood cells and large proteins stay behind.

人工关节常用什么材料？

What materials are artificial joints usually made of?

常用生物相容性好的钛合金、钴铬钼合金以及超高分子量聚乙烯作摩擦面，既耐磨又不易被人体排斥。
They use biocompatible titanium or cobalt-chrome alloys with ultra-high-molecular-weight polyethylene bearing surfaces, wear-resistant and well tolerated.

除颤器抢救心脏的原理是什么？

How does a defibrillator rescue a heart?

当发生室颤时心肌乱跳，除颤器释放一次高能电脉冲使全部心肌同步除极，争取让窦房结重新主导正常节律。
In ventricular fibrillation the muscle quivers; a defibrillator delivers a high-energy shock to depolarize all of it at once, letting the natural pacemaker regain control.

数字助听器比老式有什么进步？

How are digital hearing aids better than old ones?

数字助听器用麦克风采集声音后经数字信号处理器降噪、分频放大，可按听损曲线个性化补偿，还能抑制啸叫和突发噪声。
Digital aids use a DSP to denoise and frequency-shape sound to the user's loss profile, suppressing feedback and sudden noise, far more personalized than analog.

呼吸机怎样帮人呼吸？

How does a ventilator help breathing?

它向气道施加正压把气体送入肺部（正压通气），并按设定频率和潮气量维持氧合与二氧化碳排出，用于麻醉或呼吸衰竭。
It pushes gas into the lungs with positive pressure at set rates and volumes, maintaining oxygenation and CO2 removal during anesthesia or respiratory failure.
