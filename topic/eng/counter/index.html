鸡蛋壳那么薄，为什么用手捏不碎？

Why is a thin eggshell hard to crush by squeezing?

拱形薄壳把压力沿曲面分散，均匀受压时强度很高；只在一点集中受力时才易破。
The arched shell spreads pressure along its curve, so even squeeze load is strong; only a point load breaks it.

Why should you worry more once a non-stick pan is scratched?

涂层剥落会失去不粘、还可能随食物吃下；干烧还会释放有害气体，并非锅本身有毒。
Flaking loses the non-stick and may be ingested; overheating releases fumes—not that the pan is poisonous, but misuse is risky.

薄薄的羽绒服为什么那么保暖？

Why is a thin down jacket so warm?

绒朵锁住大量静止空气，空气导热差形成隔热层；保暖靠蓬松度而非厚度。
Down traps still air; air is a poor conductor, so the loft—not thickness—makes the insulation.

薄纸做成蜂窝或瓦楞为什么能扛大弯？

Why can thin paper as honeycomb or corrugation bear big loads?

弯曲时中性面附近几乎不受力，材料离中性面越远越出力。瓦楞和蜂窝把两张薄皮撑开，让皮承担拉压，几乎全是空气的芯只传剪力并防皮起皱。薄皮的抗弯贡献随它到中性面的距离平方增长：皮间距拉远到三倍，同样多的纸抗弯约涨九倍，重量增得很少。但要注意省的是同重量，同体积实心板仍更硬；它只在垂直于波纹的方向强，横向压、压痕与受潮先失效。
Bending leaves the middle of a section nearly unloaded and puts the work on material far from that neutral surface. Corrugation and honeycomb stand two thin skins apart so the skins take tension and compression while the near-air core only carries shear and props them against wrinkling. A skin's contribution grows with the square of its distance from the neutral surface: skins three times farther apart make the same paper roughly nine times as bend-resistant, with little added weight. The saving is per unit weight—a solid slab of the same volume is stiffer—and the panel is strong only across its corrugations, since sideways compression, dents and damp fail first.

走钢丝的人为什么拿一根长杆？

Why do tightrope walkers carry a long pole?

长杆增大转动惯量、降低重心晃动的频率，给人更多调整平衡的时间。
The long pole raises rotational inertia and slows the sway frequency, giving more time to correct balance.

水结冰膨胀为什么能撑裂石头和水管？

Why does freezing water crack rock and pipes?

水结冰体积胀大约 9%，孔隙和管腔里的水被围住，膨胀无处可去只能变成压力：冰一胀孔壁就受拉，岩石、铸铁抗拉远低于抗压，裂纹尖端一楔就开。压力升高还压低冰的熔点，未冻水被挤向冻结前沿，冰透镜体越积越厚，冻胀因此是吸水再楔入的累积过程，反复冻融逐轮拆散岩体。边界在约束：膨胀有处可去就不致胀裂，水管冻裂多出在冰塞封住的一段。
When water freezes its volume grows by roughly 9%, and water in rock pores or a pipe cavity is confined, so expansion with nowhere to go becomes pressure: as ice swells the wall is loaded in tension, yet rock and cast iron are far weaker in tension than in compression, so a crack tip is driven open with little force. Rising pressure also depresses the melting point of ice, so unfrozen water is squeezed toward the freezing front and ice lenses keep thickening; frost heave is a cumulative process of drawing water in and wedging, worked through cycle by cycle. The boundary is confinement: expansion that has somewhere to go bursts nothing, and pipes usually fail in the stretch sealed by an ice blockage.

竹子比同重量的钢铁还'结实'吗？

Is bamboo 'stronger' than steel of the same weight?

竹子的比强度（强度除以密度）接近某些钢材，中空结构很高效，但绝对强度仍低于钢。
Bamboo's specific strength (strength/density) rivals some steels thanks to its hollow efficient form, but its absolute strength is still below steel.

摩天楼在风里'摆动'为什么反而是安全的？

Why is a skyscraper swaying in wind actually safe?

柔性结构把振动能量耗散掉，限制幅度下的晃动比硬扛更安全，还能避开共振破坏。
A flexible structure dissipates vibration energy; bounded sway is safer than fighting it and avoids resonant failure.

比萨斜塔为什么歪了那么久还不倒？

Why has the Leaning Tower of Pisa stayed up while leaning?

它的重心仍落在底面范围内，软土让沉降可控，塔身对称、风载小，处于临界稳定状态。
Its center of mass still falls inside the base; soft soil made settling gradual, and the symmetric tower with low wind sits in marginal stability.

强度高的'硬'材料为什么有时反而更脆？

Why can a strong, hard material be brittle?

高强常伴随低塑性，裂纹一旦萌生就迅速扩展；韧性材料会先变形耗能再断。
High strength often means low ductility, so a crack runs fast once started; tough materials deform and absorb energy first.
