高铁时速三百公里,车轮和钢轨之间真的只有指甲盖宽的接触面吗?

At 300 km/h, does the wheel really touch the rail on a patch the size of a fingernail?

是真的。轮轨接触斑大约一平方厘米,却要传递牵引、制动与导向的全部力。秘密在锥形踏面与钢轨廓形的配合:轮对左右两轮刚性同轴,滚动半径差让它过弯时自动向内偏移实现自导向,减少轮缘摩擦;蠕滑理论描述了接触面间微小相对滑动如何传递切向力。高速化的代价是对平顺性极端敏感——毫米级的轨道不平顺经车厢弹簧放大成不适甚至脱轨风险,所以无砟轨道把误差控制在毫米内,检测车用激光每几天扫描一遍全线路基。
True. The wheel-rail contact patch spans about one square centimeter yet transmits all traction, braking and steering forces. The trick lies in coned treads matched to rail profiles: rigidly coupled wheelsets steer themselves through curves by rolling-radius difference, sparing flange friction; creep theory describes how micro-slips in that patch transmit tangential forces. High speed makes everything unforgiving — millimeter track irregularities amplify through suspension into discomfort or derailment risk, so slab track holds tolerances to millimeters and laser inspection trains scan the whole line every few days.

高铁为什么不用轮子转得最快的柴油机车,而要用电?轨道上的电网怎么供电?

Why do high-speed trains run on electricity rather than fast diesel engines — how does the trackside grid power them?

高速下阻力几乎全来自空气(功率随速度三次方增长),牵引需求集中在持续大功率,电动机功率密度与全速域扭矩远胜内燃机,且再生制动能把制动能量回送电网三成上下。供电靠架空接触网:变电所每几十公里一站,把27.5千伏单相电经受电弓喂给车顶;回流经钢轨与回流线闭合。长编组高速动车组峰值功率在一两万千瓦量级,相当于一个小城镇的用电负荷。
At speed, drag dominates — power grows with velocity cubed — so traction needs sustained megawatts where electric motors outclass diesels in power density and full-range torque, plus regenerative braking returns about thirty percent of braking energy to the grid. Power arrives via overhead catenary: substations every few tens of kilometers feed single-phase 27.5 kV through the pantograph, returning through rails and return conductors. A long-formation high-speed EMU peaks in the ten-to-twenty megawatt class — the electric load of a small town.

Why do high-speed drivers keep tapping a pedal while driving?

那是警醒装置(死人开关):司机必须在设定时间内周期性踩踏或操作,证明自己清醒。一旦超时无动作,系统判定司机失能,自动触发紧急制动让列车停下。航空、高铁、货运列车的驾驶室都有类似设计——再可靠的机器也要防备最不可控的人为失误。
It is a dead-man switch vigilance device: drivers must press within set intervals proving alertness. No action past timeout means the system judges the driver incapacitated and slams emergency brakes. Cockpits and freight cabs share such designs — even reliable machines must guard against human lapses.
