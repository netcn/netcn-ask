氢气储运为什么比天然气麻烦得多，液态储氢、氨载体和有机液体储氢各解决什么问题？

Why is hydrogen storage and transport far trickier than natural gas, and what do liquid hydrogen, ammonia carriers and liquid organic carriers each solve?

氢气极轻、常压体积巨大，液化要深冷到零下253℃且耗能高。液氢适合航天等高频场景，氨和有机液体（LOHC）则在常温常压下以化合物形式安全储运，用时再释放氢气。
Hydrogen is very light and bulky at ambient pressure; liquefaction needs minus 253°C and high energy. Liquid H2 suits spaceflight; ammonia and liquid organic carriers (LOHC) store and ship it safely at room conditions, releasing H2 on demand.

什么是“氢脆”，为什么它让输氢管道和储氢罐的材料选择格外困难？

What is hydrogen embrittlement, and why does it make material choice for hydrogen pipelines and tanks especially difficult?

氢原子会渗入金属晶界，使材料变脆、在远低于设计应力下开裂。普通高强度钢和某些焊缝容易中招，因此输氢设备往往要改用低强度钢、奥氏体不锈钢或特殊涂层。
Hydrogen atoms seep into metal grain boundaries, making it brittle and cracking below design stress. Common high-strength steels and some welds are vulnerable, so hydrogen equipment often uses low-strength steel, austenitic stainless or special coatings.

质子交换膜燃料电池的铂催化剂为什么昂贵，非铂催化剂研究卡在哪里？

Why is the platinum catalyst in PEM fuel cells expensive, and where does platinum-free catalyst research get stuck?

铂能高效催化阴极的氧还原反应，但稀缺昂贵，占电堆成本大头。铁氮碳等非铂材料活性已接近，却在长期稳定性和抗一氧化碳中毒上仍不及铂，难以撑过数千小时寿命。
Platinum efficiently catalyzes cathodic oxygen reduction but is scarce and costly, dominating stack price. Platinum-free Fe-N-C materials approach its activity yet lag in long-term stability and CO tolerance, struggling to last thousands of hours.

“绿氢”“蓝氢”“灰氢”是按什么来区分的，为什么绿氢最被推崇？

How are green, blue and gray hydrogen distinguished, and why is green hydrogen most favored?

它们按制氢过程的碳排放划分：灰氢来自化石能源且无捕集，蓝氢有碳捕集，绿氢用可再生电力电解水几乎零碳。绿氢最清洁，但当前成本也最高。
They are classified by carbon emissions: gray from fossil fuels without capture, blue with capture, and green from renewable-powered electrolysis near zero carbon. Green is cleanest but currently costliest.

氢燃料电池车和氢内燃机车的区别是什么，为什么前者效率更高？

What is the difference between a hydrogen fuel-cell car and a hydrogen combustion car, and why is the former more efficient?

燃料电池车在电池里让氢和氧直接发生电化学反应发电驱动，不受热机效率上限束缚；氢内燃机只是把氢当汽油烧，仍受卡诺效率限制，能量浪费更多。
A fuel-cell car makes electricity by electrochemically combining hydrogen and oxygen, free of the heat-engine efficiency ceiling, while a hydrogen engine just burns hydrogen and is still limited by Carnot efficiency, wasting more energy.
