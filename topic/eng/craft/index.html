钣金折弯时，为什么回弹让尺寸总差一点？

Why does springback make bent sheet metal slightly off?

金属弹性部分在卸载后会部分恢复，使折角小于模具角；要靠补偿角度、加压矫形或选材来控制回弹误差。
Elastic recovery after unloading springs the bend back, so dies must over-bend or coining and material choice control the error.

装配线为什么常把节拍卡得很死，慢一点会怎样？

Why is line takt so tight, what if one station is slow?

节拍由客户需求定，任一工位超时会在线旁堆积半成品、拖垮整线产出；平衡工位和防错是关键。
Takt is set by demand; a slow station backs up WIP and drags output, so line balancing and mistake-proofing matter.

金属件表面做阳极氧化是什么，和喷漆是一回事吗？

What is anodizing, is it the same as painting?

不是。阳极氧化在铝等表面用电化学长出致密氧化层，既着色又耐磨防腐；喷漆只是外层覆盖，附着力与寿命不同。
Anodizing grows a dense oxide layer electrochemically for color, wear, and corrosion resistance; paint merely coats the surface.

焊接时为什么常围着一团保护气，是怕焊点氧化吗？

Why is there often shielding gas around a weld?

是的。氩气等保护气把空气隔开，防止熔池氧化、气孔和脆化，焊缝才牢固美观。
Shielding gas keeps air away from the molten pool to prevent oxidation, porosity, and brittleness for sound welds.

数控机床怎么知道刀该走到哪儿、转多快？

How does a CNC machine know where to move the tool and how fast?

工程师把图纸编成G代码，控制系统按代码驱动机床三轴并读取编码器反馈实时纠偏，精度和重复定位都很高。
A program drives the axes while encoders feed position back for correction, giving high precision and repeatability.

数控机床喷的那层冷却液有什么用，不用行不行？

What does cutting fluid do, can you skip it?

冷却液降温、润滑并冲走切屑，保护刀具和工件、提升表面质量；干切虽可行但刀具磨损快、精度难保。
Coolant cools, lubricates, and flushes chips to protect tool and finish; dry cutting wears tools fast and hurts accuracy.

激光切割为什么能隔空把钢板切开，还不碰到工件？

How does laser cutting slice steel without touching it?

聚焦的激光在极小光斑释放极高能量熔化或气化金属，同时喷辅助气体吹走熔渣；非接触故无刀具磨损。
A focused beam melts and vaporizes metal while assist gas blows away slag; being non-contact, it avoids tool wear.

金属砂型铸造为什么能做出发动机缸体这样复杂的大件？

How can sand casting make complex big parts like engine blocks?

用型砂按模样做出空腔，浇入铁水冷却后破砂取件，几乎不受形状复杂度限制；精度不如切削但适合大件小批。
Sand molding forms a cavity from a pattern; poured metal cools and the sand is broken away, shapes can be complex, good for big low-volume parts.

How is a plastic shell made, not cut out?

为什么绝大多数螺丝是向右（顺时针）拧紧的？

Why do most screws tighten clockwise?

主要是约定俗成，右旋螺纹配右手发力更顺；统一方向避免装反，少数左扣用于防松脱的特殊轴。
Right-hand threads became standard because they suit right-handed tightening; uniformity prevents mistakes, with left-hand used rarely to avoid loosening.
