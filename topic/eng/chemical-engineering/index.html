增大精馏回流比为什么既能提高分离效果又会增加能耗？

Why does increasing the reflux ratio improve separation performance but also raise energy consumption?

回流比是回流液量与塔顶产品量的比值，增大回流比意味着塔内液气流量都增加，塔板间传质推动力更大、所需理论塔板数减少，产品纯度提高。但回流液由塔顶蒸气冷凝而来，要汽化更多上升蒸气就需在再沸器消耗更多热量，冷凝器负荷也相应增加，因此操作费用随回流比近似线性上升。设计常取最小回流比的1.2至2倍作为经济回流比。
The reflux ratio is the ratio of reflux returned to the column over the distillate product. Raising it increases internal liquid and vapour flows, giving stronger stage-to-stage mass-transfer driving force, fewer required theoretical stages, and purer products. But the reflux is condensed from overhead vapour, so producing more vapour requires more heat at the reboiler and more duty at the condenser, making operating cost rise roughly linearly. Designs typically use 1.2 to 2 times the minimum reflux ratio as the economical choice.

化工中的『传质』指的是什么过程？

What process does 'mass transfer' refer to in chemical engineering?

传质是物质因浓度差从一处迁移到另一处，如吸收、萃取、干燥中的扩散与对流。它常与传热耦合，是分离、反应和环保设备设计的核心。
Mass transfer is the movement of species driven by concentration differences—diffusion and convection in absorption, extraction, drying. Coupled with heat transfer, it underpins separation and reactor design.

连续搅拌釜反应器（CSTR）中的反应物浓度为什么低于进料浓度？

Why is the reactant concentration inside a CSTR lower than that of its feed?

CSTR依靠搅拌使釜内组成均匀，出口组成与釜内相同，这意味着整个反应都在出口的低浓度下进行。反应物一进入就被釜内大量产物稀释，所以推动反应的浓度水平始终较低；同等转化率下CSTR需要的反应体积往往大于活塞流反应器（PFR）。但均匀的浓度和温度使其易于控制，特别适合强放热反应，可避免局部过热。
Because the impeller keeps the tank contents uniform, the outlet composition equals that inside, so all the reaction proceeds at the low outlet concentration. Fresh feed is instantly diluted by the large mass of product already in the tank, so the driving concentration stays low; for the same conversion a CSTR therefore usually needs a larger volume than a plug-flow reactor (PFR). Its uniform concentration and temperature, however, make it easy to control, which is advantageous for strongly exothermic reactions where local overheating must be avoided.

活塞流反应器（PFR）的浓度沿管长如何变化？它比CSTR有哪些优势？

How does concentration vary along a plug-flow reactor (PFR), and what advantages does it have over a CSTR?

PFR中物料以活塞式流动通过管段，没有轴向混合，反应物浓度沿管长连续下降，反应速率也随之下降，理论上每一微分段都保持较高的推动力。因此在相同转化率下PFR所需体积小于CSTR，且不存在返混造成的转化率损失。缺点是温度控制较困难，强放热反应易出现热点，需要分段冷却或外部换热。
In a PFR the fluid moves like a plug along the tube with no axial mixing, so reactant concentration falls continuously along the length and the reaction rate follows suit; each differential section retains a high driving force. At equal conversion a PFR therefore needs a smaller volume than a CSTR and loses none of the conversion to back-mixing. Its drawback is harder temperature control, strongly exothermic reactions can create hot spots, so staged cooling or external heat exchange is required.

分离混合物的“最小分离功”是多少？精馏实际能耗由什么决定？

What is the minimum work of separation for a mixture, and what determines the actual energy demand of distillation?

理论最小分离功等于可逆分离该混合物所需的自由能，不低于混合过程的吉布斯自由能增量。实际精馏需远高于此的再沸器与冷凝器热负荷，主要由组分相对挥发度和回流比决定。
The theoretical minimum separation work is the reversible work needed to separate a mixture, no less than the Gibbs free energy of mixing. Real distillation needs far more reboiler and condenser duty, set mainly by the relative volatility of components and the reflux ratio.

HAZOP（危险与可操作性分析）是如何系统识别化工装置风险的？

How does a HAZOP (Hazard and Operability) study systematically identify risks in a chemical plant?

HAZOP由多专业团队按节点（如管路、容器、工序）逐段分析，把设计参数（流量、压力、温度、液位等）与引导词（多、少、无、反向等）组合，推演偏离设计意图的情况及其原因和后果，再评估现有保护措施是否充分。引导词法保证审查全面而不遗漏偏差，最终给出需降低风险的整改建议，是流程工业安全审查的标准方法。
A HAZOP study is carried out by a multi-disciplinary team node by node (lines, vessels, operations), combining process parameters (flow, pressure, temperature, level) with guide words (more, less, none, reverse) to reason out deviations from design intent, their causes and consequences, and to judge whether existing safeguards are adequate. The guide-word method ensures the review is exhaustive and misses no deviation, and the outcome is a set of recommendations for reducing risk; it is the standard hazard-review methodology of the process industries.

精馏分离液体混合物的基本原理是什么？为什么需要回流？

What is the basic principle of distillation for separating liquid mixtures, and why is reflux needed?

精馏利用混合物各组分挥发度不同，通过气液两相在塔板或填料上的逆流接触反复部分汽化和冷凝，使易挥发组分富集于塔顶、难挥发组分富集于塔底。回流把塔顶冷凝的一部分液体送回塔内，为塔提供持续下行的液相，保证各级气液接触和组分分离连续进行，是精馏高效分离的关键。
Distillation exploits differences in volatility: vapour and liquid contact countercurrently on trays or packing, repeatedly partially vaporising and condensing, so the more volatile component concentrates at the top and the less volatile one at the bottom. Reflux returns part of the condensed overhead liquid to the column, providing a continuous downward liquid flow so that staged vapour-liquid contact and separation proceed steadily; it is essential for efficient separation.

管壳式换热器中为什么设置折流板？它如何提高传热效率？

Why are baffles placed inside a shell-and-tube heat exchanger, and how do they improve heat transfer?

折流板安装在壳程中，使壳侧流体在管束间反复横向折流流动，从而强化湍流、增加流体与管壁的接触路径。相比直线流动，这种流动提高了对流传热系数，同时支撑管束、抑制管子振动并减小管子的跨距变形。折流板间距过小会增大压降，设计时要在传热与压降之间权衡。
Baffles in the shell side force the shell-side fluid to flow back and forth across the tube bundle, promoting turbulence and lengthening the contact path with the tube surfaces. This raises the convective heat-transfer coefficient compared with straight-through flow, while also supporting the tubes, suppressing vibration, and limiting tube span deflection. Too small a baffle spacing raises pressure drop, so design balances heat transfer against pumping cost.

什么是热交换器的“对数平均温差”（LMTD）？

What is the log-mean temperature difference (LMTD) of a heat exchanger?

LMTD = (ΔT1 − ΔT2) / ln(ΔT1/ΔT2)，是沿换热器两端温差的有效平均值，用来驱动传热。设计公式 Q = U A LMTD 中，U 为总传热系数、A 为传热面积。
The LMTD = (ΔT1 − ΔT2) / ln(ΔT1/ΔT2) is the effective mean temperature difference between the two ends of a heat exchanger that drives heat transfer. In the design equation Q = U A LMTD, U is the overall heat-transfer coefficient and A the area.

连续搅拌釜式反应器（CSTR）与平推流反应器（PFR）的核心区别是什么？

What is the core difference between a continuous stirred-tank reactor (CSTR) and a plug-flow reactor (PFR)?

CSTR 内完全混合，器内浓度处处等于出口浓度，单容积转化率低但换热易控。PFR 内无轴向返混，浓度沿轴向递减，对一级反应在相同容积下转化率更高。二者选择取决于反应动力学与热管理。
A CSTR is perfectly mixed so its internal concentration equals the outlet concentration; it gives low conversion per volume but easy heat control. A PFR has no axial backmixing and concentration falls along its length, giving higher conversion per volume for first-order reactions. Choice depends on kinetics and heat management.

What does 'unit operation' mean in chemical engineering?

精馏（蒸馏）分离混合物的原理是什么？什么是“理论板”？

What is the principle of distillation separation, and what is a theoretical plate?

精馏利用组分挥发度差异分离：上升蒸气富含易挥发组分、下降液体富含难挥发组分，在塔板上多次接触趋于平衡。理论板是指气液两相达到平衡的假定级，所需板数可用 McCabe-Thiele 图解法估算。
Distillation exploits differences in volatility: rising vapor is enriched in the more volatile component and descending liquid in the less volatile one, contacting repeatedly on trays toward equilibrium. A theoretical plate is an ideal stage where vapor and liquid reach equilibrium; the number of stages is estimated by the McCabe-Thiele method.

气体吸收操作的目的是什么？为什么逆流吸收比并流吸收效果好？

What is the purpose of gas absorption, and why is countercurrent absorption more effective than co-current?

吸收是用液体溶剂从气体混合物中脱除可溶组分的过程，例如用水洗去烟气中的二氧化硫。逆流时液体的溶质浓度从塔顶到塔底递增，气液两相始终存在较大的浓度差即传质推动力，同一塔高能得到更高的吸收率；并流时推动力沿塔迅速下降，效果明显变差。
Absorption removes a soluble component from a gas mixture using a liquid solvent, for example scrubbing sulfur dioxide from flue gas with water. In countercurrent operation the liquid solute concentration rises from top to bottom, so the gas-liquid concentration difference (mass-transfer driving force) stays high along the column, and a given column height achieves a higher removal efficiency; in co-current flow the driving force falls off quickly and performance degrades markedly.

How do membrane separation technologies like reverse osmosis work?

液液萃取适用于什么场合？为什么萃取溶剂要选择与料液互不相溶的物质？

When is liquid-liquid extraction used, and why must the extraction solvent be nearly immiscible with the feed?

当混合物沸点接近、易形成共沸物或组分热敏性高时，蒸馏难以或不宜实现分离，此时可以用萃取：选择对目标组分有较强亲和力的溶剂，使溶质在两液相间分配，从而从原溶液中转移出去。溶剂与料液必须高度互不相溶，才能在混合后分层形成两相，否则无法实现相分离和有效传质。
Extraction is chosen when components have close boiling points, form azeotropes, or are heat-sensitive and cannot be distilled economically. A solvent with strong affinity for the target solute is chosen so the solute distributes between two liquid phases and is removed from the feed. The solvent must be highly immiscible with the feed so the mixture separates into two clear phases, which is essential for mass transfer and phase separation.

石油和酒精工业里的『精馏』是怎样分离液体的？

How does 'distillation' separate liquids in oil and alcohol industries?

精馏利用混合物中各组分沸点不同：加热使低沸点组分先汽化，经塔内多层塔板反复部分冷凝与蒸发，逐步提浓，最终在塔顶和塔底得到不同产品。
Distillation exploits different boiling points: heating vaporizes the lighter component, and repeated partial condensation and evaporation across tray stages enrich it, yielding products at top and bottom.

超高温(UHT)处理为何只用几秒钟的热处理就能实现牛乳的商业无菌,同时较好地保留风味与维生素?

How can ultra-high-temperature treatment render milk commercially sterile within seconds while still preserving flavor and vitamins comparatively well?

微生物致死的温度敏感性远高于美拉德褐变和维生素分解,因此把温度提升到135–150 °C并将保温时间压缩到数秒,可在杀灭细菌芽孢的同时把风味与营养损失控制在很小范围。蒸汽喷射等直接加热方式升降温极快,蒸煮味明显轻于间接板式换热。后续无菌灌装用双氧水处理包材,防止成品二次污染。
Microbial death responds to temperature far more steeply than Maillard browning or vitamin degradation, so raising the temperature to 135–150 °C while squeezing the holding time down to seconds destroys spores before flavor and nutrient losses mount. Direct steam injection heats and cools almost instantly, producing a far lighter cooked note than indirect plate heat exchangers. Downstream aseptic fillers treat packaging material with hydrogen peroxide to prevent recontamination of the finished product.

#UHT杀菌 #商业无菌

乳品灭菌为什么普遍改用约140℃只维持数秒的超高温瞬时（UHT）工艺，而不是长时间低温加热，营养损失反而更小？

Why has dairy sterilization shifted to ultra-high-temperature (UHT) treatment holding milk for mere seconds near 140 degrees C instead of long low-temperature heating, and why does that spare nutrients better?

直答：芽孢致死速率对温度升高的敏感度远高于营养成分热降解的敏感度，用升温换缩短时间对品质极为划算。机理：以z值衡量，芽孢致死曲线z值约10℃，温度每升高10℃灭菌效率提高十倍；硫胺素等营养素z值达25至30℃，同等升温损失仅增至两三倍。因此140℃级超高温数秒即可实现商业无菌，美拉德褐变与风味劣变被压到最低，这是长时间低温灭菌难以兼顾的。
Spore death rates respond to rising temperature far more sharply than nutrient degradation rates do, so trading time for temperature strongly favors quality. Measured by z-values, spore lethality curves sit near 10 degrees C — every ten-degree rise multiplies killing efficiency tenfold — while nutrients such as thiamine have z-values of 25 to 30 and lose only a few times more over the same rise. Seconds at ultra-high temperature therefore reach commercial sterility while Maillard browning and flavor damage are squeezed to a minimum, which long low-temperature sterilization cannot manage.

催化剂为什么能加快反应速率而自身不被消耗？载体在工业催化剂中起什么作用？

Why does a catalyst speed up a reaction without being consumed, and what role does a support play in industrial catalysts?

催化剂通过提供新的低活化能反应路径降低反应的能垒，使更多分子能达到反应所需的能量，因而速率提高，但催化剂在反应前后化学性质不变。工业催化剂多为负载型，活性组分（如铂、钯）以微小颗粒分散在氧化铝、沸石等高比表面积载体上，载体既提高活性组分的分散度以增大有效面积，又提供机械强度并帮助散热。
A catalyst offers an alternative reaction pathway with a lower activation energy, lowering the energy barrier so far more molecules can react, while its own chemical identity is unchanged before and after the reaction. Industrial catalysts are usually supported: active components such as platinum or palladium are dispersed as fine particles on high-surface-area carriers like alumina or zeolites, which maximise dispersion of the active phase, provide mechanical strength, and help dissipate heat.

什么是“传质”与“菲克定律”？

What are mass transfer and Fick's law?

传质是物质因浓度差而发生的迁移过程。菲克第一定律指出扩散通量 J 与浓度梯度成正比，即 J = −D∇c，其中 D 为扩散系数。
Mass transfer is the movement of species driven by a concentration difference. Fick's first law states that the diffusion flux J is proportional to the concentration gradient: J = −D∇c, where D is the diffusion coefficient.

什么是pH值，它衡量什么？

What is pH, and what does it measure?

pH衡量溶液的酸碱性，数值等于氢离子浓度的负对数。pH为7是中性，小于7偏酸，大于7偏碱。它影响化学反应、土壤和人体消化。
pH measures acidity or alkalinity as the negative log of hydrogen ion concentration. Seven is neutral, below is acidic, above is basic, affecting reactions, soil, and digestion.

冷冻干燥为什么能让食品中的冰不经过液态就变成水汽抽走，从而比热风干燥更完整地保留营养与风味？

Why can freeze-drying turn the ice inside food straight into vapor without any liquid phase, preserving nutrition and flavor far more completely than hot-air drying?

直答：冻干在水的三相点以下运行，降压使冻结的冰直接升华为水汽被真空系统抽走，物料全程保持低温冻结状态。机理：热风干燥中水分经液相蒸发，高温加长时间会破坏维生素、色素与挥发性香气成分，蛋白质还易变性；冻干同时避开液态水和高温这两个损伤来源，冰晶原位留下的孔隙使干燥层疏松多孔，复水后组织接近新鲜状态，代价则是真空与长周期操作带来的高能耗。
Freeze-drying operates below water's triple point, where reduced pressure sends frozen ice directly off as vapor carried away by the vacuum pumps while the product stays cold and solid throughout. Hot-air drying evaporates liquid water, and its pairing of heat with long exposure wrecks vitamins, pigments, volatile aroma compounds, and often denatures proteins besides. Freeze-drying dodges both damage sources — liquid water and high temperature — and pores left where ice crystals once stood give the dried layer an open sponge-like structure that rehydrates close to fresh, at the price of energy-hungry vacuum operation.

化学反应中的『放热』和『吸热』是什么意思？

What do 'exothermic' and 'endothermic' mean for chemical reactions?

放热反应是产物总能量低于反应物、向环境释放热量的反应，如燃烧；吸热反应则需要吸收热量才能进行，如石灰石煅烧。理解热效应对安全和能效很重要。
Exothermic reactions release heat because products hold less energy than reactants, like combustion; endothermic ones absorb heat, like limestone calcination. The heat effect matters for safety and efficiency.
