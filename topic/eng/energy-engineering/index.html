什么是核聚变，它与核裂变有何不同？

What is nuclear fusion and how does it differ from fission?

核聚变是轻原子核结合成较重原子核并释放能量的过程，正是太阳发光的方式。它与裂变相反，燃料丰富、不产生长寿命高放废料，但需要上亿度高温和强约束，目前尚未实现商用发电。
Nuclear fusion is the joining of light nuclei into heavier ones with energy release, the process that powers the Sun. Opposite to fission, its fuel is abundant and it produces no long-lived high-level waste, but it needs temperatures of hundreds of millions of degrees and strong confinement, so commercial power is not yet achieved.

What is hydrogen energy and why does green hydrogen matter?

Hydrogen energy uses hydrogen as fuel or storage medium, burning to water only, a potentially zero-carbon source. Green hydrogen is made by electrolyzing water with renewable electricity, producing no carbon emissions, and can serve industry and transport that are hard to electrify.

How does a nuclear fission reactor turn atomic energy into electricity?

In the reactor, uranium-235 fissions in a chain reaction when struck by neutrons; the released heat is carried by coolant to a steam generator that drives a turbine. Fermi built the first controlled chain reactor in 1942; control rods and absorbers regulate power and safe shutdown.

风力机为什么存在理论最大效率的『贝兹极限』？

Why is there a theoretical maximum—the Betz limit—for wind turbine efficiency?

风轮机要减速气流才能提取动能，但若把风完全拦停，下游空气无法流出、反而无风可吸；Betz 在 1919 年算出能提取的最大功率约为来流动能的 59.3%。实际机组因损耗只能接近这一极限。
A turbine must slow the air to extract kinetic energy, but if it stopped the air completely, downstream flow would stall and no wind would follow; Betz calculated in 1919 that at most about 59.3% of the incoming kinetic power can be captured. Real machines only approach it due to losses.

在窄密度窗口地层中，常规钻井停泵接单根时为什么容易发生井涌？控压钻井的恒定井底压力模式如何解决这一难题？

In formations with a narrow mud-weight window, why is conventional drilling prone to kicks when pumps are shut off for a connection, and how does the constant-bottomhole-pressure mode of managed pressure drilling solve this problem?

常规钻井靠钻井液静液柱压力平衡地层孔隙压力，循环时环空摩阻压耗叠加其上；停泵接单根时压耗消失，井底压力骤降，一旦低于地层压力便发生井涌。控压钻井的恒定井底压力模式在井口设置节流管汇与自动控制系统：停泵时施加井口回压补偿损失的摩阻，使井底压力始终保持在溢流压力与漏失压力之间的窄窗内，从而安全钻穿高压易漏互层段。
In conventional drilling the mud column alone balances pore pressure, and annular frictional losses exist only while circulating; when the pumps stop for a connection, bottomhole pressure drops abruptly and can fall below formation pressure, letting reservoir fluids flow into the wellbore. The constant-bottomhole-pressure mode of managed pressure drilling adds a choke manifold with automated control at surface: as flow ceases, applied backpressure replaces the lost friction, holding bottomhole pressure inside the narrow band between pore pressure and fracture pressure so that interbedded, high-pressure, loss-prone sections can be drilled safely.

How does a lithium-ion battery work, and how do lithium ions move during charge and discharge?

什么是能量转换效率？

What is energy conversion efficiency?

能量转换效率是输出的有用能量与输入总能量的比值，永远小于100%。例如燃煤电厂把约40%的燃料热转化为电，其余以废热形式损失。
Energy conversion efficiency is the ratio of useful output energy to total input energy and is always below 100%. A coal plant, for example, converts only about 40% of fuel heat into electricity, losing the rest as waste heat.

什么是“抽水蓄能”？为何储能对电网很重要？

What is pumped-storage hydropower, and why is energy storage important for the grid?

抽水蓄能是在电力富余时把水抽到上游水库、用电高峰时放水发电，往返效率约70%–80%。储能可平抑风电光伏的波动、削峰填谷并参与调频，是高比例可再生能源电网的关键。
Pumped-storage hydropower pumps water uphill when power is abundant and generates by releasing it at peak demand, with round-trip efficiency of about 70%–80%. Storage balances intermittent wind and solar, shifts peak load, and provides frequency regulation, making it key to renewable-heavy grids.

什么是“热电联产”（CHP）？为何它的能源利用率高？

What is combined heat and power (CHP), and why is its energy utilization high?

热电联产在发电的同时回收排热用于供热或工艺蒸汽，使燃料总利用率可达70%–90%，远高于单独电厂约40%的水平，因而显著降低能耗与排放。典型应用包括工业园区自备电站以及医院、数据中心的分布式能源系统。
Combined heat and power generates electricity while recovering waste heat for heating or process steam, raising total fuel utilization to 70%–90%, far above the roughly 40% of separate plants, thus cutting energy use and emissions. Typical uses include industrial-park cogeneration plants and distributed energy systems for hospitals and data centers.

What is 'energy density' often compared among energy sources?

Energy density is energy per mass or per volume, split into gravimetric and volumetric. It decides how far a fuel goes or how light a battery is—key when choosing a power source.

旋转钻井中钻井液（泥浆）要一刻不停地循环，它究竟承担哪些关键任务？

During rotary drilling, drilling fluid (mud) must circulate nonstop—what critical jobs does it actually perform?

钻井液经钻杆泵送到井底，从钻头喷嘴高速喷出后沿环空返出地面，同时完成三件事：把钻头破碎的岩屑携带出井，冷却并润滑钻头与钻具，以及在裸眼段维持液柱压力。液柱压力略高于地层孔隙压力时，油气水就被“顶住”进不了井筒；一旦停泵，岩屑下沉易卡钻，压力失衡还可能诱发井涌乃至井喷，因此循环不能中断。
Mud is pumped down the drill pipe, jets out of the bit nozzles at high speed, and returns up the annulus, doing three jobs at once: carrying rock cuttings out of the hole, cooling and lubricating the bit and string, and holding hydrostatic pressure across the openhole interval. As long as this column pressure slightly exceeds formation pore pressure, oil, gas, and water are held back from entering the wellbore; stop circulation and cuttings settle to stick the pipe, while any pressure imbalance can trigger a kick or even a blowout—so circulation never pauses.

地热能是怎样被用来发电或供暖的？

How is geothermal energy used for power or heating?

地球内部的热量加热地下水和岩石，可通过钻井抽取高温蒸汽或热水直接供暖，或推动汽轮机发电。它稳定可靠、不受天气影响，但受地质条件限制。
Earth's internal heat warms underground water and rock; wells draw hot steam or water for direct heating or to spin turbines for power. It is steady and weather-independent but site-limited.

什么是可再生能源？

What is renewable energy?

可再生能源是来自自然界能不断补充的能源，如太阳能、风能、水能和生物质能。它们取用后不会枯竭，且通常比化石燃料更清洁。
Renewable energy comes from natural sources that replenish continuously, such as solar, wind, hydro, and biomass. They are not depleted by use and are generally cleaner than fossil fuels.

什么是“能量转换效率”？以光伏电池为例说明。

What is energy conversion efficiency, and explain it using a photovoltaic cell?

能量转换效率 = 有用输出能量 / 输入能量。光伏电池把太阳光转为电能，商用晶硅电池约20%–23%，受肖克利—奎伊瑟单结极限（约33%）制约。
Energy conversion efficiency equals useful output energy divided by input energy. A solar PV cell converts sunlight to electricity; commercial silicon cells reach about 20%–23%, limited by the Shockley-Queisser single-junction limit of about 33%.
