什么是材料的蠕变，在什么条件下最危险？

What is creep in materials, and when is it most dangerous?

蠕变是材料在恒定应力下随时间的缓慢塑性变形，通常在高温（对金属）或长期荷载（对混凝土、塑料）下显著。它会导致涡轮叶片、管道和预应力构件逐渐失效。
Creep is slow time-dependent deformation under constant stress, significant at high temperature for metals or under long loads for concrete and plastics. It can gradually fail turbine blades, pipes, and prestressed members.

What are crystal defects and how do they affect material properties?

晶体缺陷是材料原子排列中偏离理想周期性的地方，包括空位、位错和晶界等。位错的运动决定了金属的塑性变形能力，而控制缺陷数量和类型可以调控材料的强度与韧性。
Crystal defects are departures from the ideal periodic atomic arrangement, including vacancies, dislocations, and grain boundaries. Dislocation motion governs a metal's plastic deformation, and controlling defect type and density tunes strength and toughness.

PET 熔体纺丝过程中，提高牵伸倍数如何改变分子取向、结晶度以及纤维的最终力学响应？

How does raising the draw ratio during PET melt spinning reshape molecular orientation, crystallinity, and the mechanical response of the finished filament?

牵伸把大分子链段拉向纤维轴向，诱导规整片晶生成，结晶度与晶区取向同步上升，强度和初始模量随之提高。非晶区可伸展的连接链变少，断裂伸长率相应下降，过度牵伸还会萌生微孔和原纤化损伤。工业上常把总牵伸倍数控制在约三点五至五倍，并配合热定型松弛内应力以平衡强伸指标。
Drawing stretches chain segments along the fibre axis and induces ordered lamellae, so crystallinity and crystal orientation climb together with tenacity and initial modulus. Stretchable tie chains in the amorphous phase grow scarce, dropping elongation at break, while over-drawing seeds voids and fibrillation. Mills therefore hold total draw ratios near 3.5 to 5 and heat-set the yarn to relax residual stress and balance strength against extensibility.

纸张受潮后为什么沿横向（CD）的伸长明显大于纵向（MD）？这种尺寸各向异性从何而来？

Why does paper lengthen much more in the cross direction (CD) than in the machine direction (MD) when it takes up moisture, and where does this dimensional anisotropy come from?

根源在纤维形态与网上排列。单根纤维素纤维吸湿时直径可膨胀20%以上，而长度几乎不变（约1%）。纸机上纤维多数沿运行方向（MD）取向，横向（CD）因此叠加大量纤维直径方向的溶胀，湿胀幅度远超MD；干燥时受约束冻结的内应力遇高湿又部分释放，进一步放大CD变形。这种各向异性会造成套印失准与卷曲，需靠浆料处理、加填与涂布控制。
The origin lies in fibre geometry and its alignment on the wire. A single cellulose fibre can swell more than 20% in diameter upon moisture uptake yet barely changes in length (about 1%). Because most fibres orient along the run direction (MD), the cross direction (CD) sums many diametral swellings and shows far larger hygroexpansion and shrinkage than MD; internal stresses frozen in during restrained drying are partly released at high humidity, amplifying CD distortion further. This anisotropy causes print misregister and curl, controlled through furnish treatment, filling and coating.

A hundred tonnes of molten iron becomes steel in half an hour — how does basic oxygen steelmaking move so fast?

诀窍是把纯氧直接吹进铁水当氧化剂。氧枪从炉口插入,高压氧气射流穿入熔池,硅、锰、碳与磷的氧化都是强放热反应,不仅不需外热反而升温到1600度以上;碳氧化成一氧化碳气泡搅动熔池形成金属雨滴喷雾,反应面积暴增,精炼速度比平炉时代快十倍以上。终点用副枪测温取样自动判定碳含量,一键出钢。副产品物尽其用:炉渣铺路造水泥,高炉煤气与转炉煤气回收发电。现代钢铁联合企业里,矿石进门到钢卷出厂可以压缩到数小时。
The trick is blowing pure oxygen straight into the melt as oxidizer. An oxygen lance pierces the furnace mouth; the jet drives oxidation of silicon, manganese, carbon and phosphorus — all fiercely exothermic, heating the bath past sixteen hundred degrees with no external fuel. Carbon monoxide bubbles churn the pool into a spray of droplets, multiplying reaction area and making refining more than ten times faster than the open hearth. A sublance samples temperature and carbon automatically to call the endpoint. Slag paves roads and feeds cement; converter gas returns as fuel — ore in, coil out within hours at integrated mills.

Why are ceramics both hard and brittle?

Ceramics bond by strong ionic or covalent bonds in a dense lattice, giving high hardness, heat, and corrosion resistance. But the rigid bonds lack slip systems, so cracks spread fast under impact or tension—brittle failure.

涤纶织物出厂前为什么要经过高于玻璃化转变温度的热定形处理?

Why are polyester fabrics heat-set above their glass transition temperature before leaving the mill?

纺丝拉伸后的纤维内部冻结着取向应力,遇热便以收缩和起皱的形式释放。在玻璃化温度以上保温让链段重排并生成稳定结晶,冷却后新形态被固定,织物获得尺寸稳定性、持久折皱回复力和染色过程中的保形性。
As-spun drawn fibres carry frozen-in orientation stresses that release later as shrinkage and creasing when heated. Holding the fabric above the glass transition lets chain segments rearrange into stable crystals, and on cooling the new geometry is locked in, yielding dimensional stability, durable crease recovery and shape retention through dyeing and washing.

瓦楞纸箱的抗压强度为什么能用纸板边压强度、箱体周长和纸板厚度估算出来？

Why can a corrugated box's compression strength be estimated from board edge crush strength, box perimeter, and board thickness?

受载的纸箱像一根薄壁短柱：失效由四块侧壁板的屈曲控制。纸板边压强度越高，箱体承压越强；周长加大或纸板变厚会提高壁板的抗弯刚度，故整箱承载力近似随二者乘积的平方根增长。McKee 公式 BCT≈5.87×ECT×√(Z×d)（Z 为周长、d 为板厚）据此用简单的材料试验数据预测堆码破坏载荷，大幅减少实物试压次数，是运输包装设计的经典依据。
A loaded box behaves like a thin-walled short column whose failure is governed by buckling of its four side panels. Higher edge crush strength lets the board carry more load, while larger perimeter or thicker board raises panel bending stiffness, so box strength grows roughly with the square root of their product. The McKee formula BCT ≈ 5.87 × ECT × √(Z×d), with Z the perimeter and d the board thickness, predicts stacking failure loads from simple board tests, greatly reducing physical compression trials—a classic basis of transport packaging design.

纸为什么干的时候挺结实，一泡水就变软发弱？

Why does paper feel fairly strong when dry but turn soft and weak when soaked in water?

纸的强度主要来自相邻纤维素纤维之间羟基形成的氢键：压榨和干燥使纤维收紧贴近，生成大量氢键把纤维“粘”成整体；水分子进入后会占据这些羟基位点、拆散氢键，纤维间结合力骤降，纸随之变软变弱。此外，经历过去干燥的纤维会发生“角质化”，再次润湿后结合能力下降，这正是再生纸浆强度通常低于原生纸浆的原因。
Dry strength comes mainly from hydrogen bonds between hydroxyl groups on neighboring cellulose fibers: pressing and drying pull the fibers close together, forming extensive bonds that knit them into a coherent sheet. When water penetrates, its molecules occupy those hydroxyl sites and break the bonds, so inter-fiber cohesion collapses and the sheet weakens. Once-dried fibers also “hornify”, bonding less effectively after rewetting—which is why recycled pulp usually yields weaker paper than virgin pulp.
