在液压系统中，为什么帕斯卡原理能让小力气顶起大重物？

In hydraulics, why does Pascal's principle let a small force lift a big load?

帕斯卡原理说密闭液体各点压强相等，若在面积小的活塞上施加力产生压强，大活塞上相同压强会乘上更大面积得到更大的力。能量守恒保证小活塞要移动更长的距离来补偿大力程的短位移。
Pascal's principle says pressure is equal everywhere in a confined liquid; force on a small piston creates pressure that, on a larger piston, multiplies by the bigger area into a larger force. Energy conservation means the small piston moves farther to compensate the large force's short stroke.

涡轮机和活塞发动机有什么不同？

What is the difference between a turbine engine and a piston engine?

活塞发动机靠气缸里来回运动的活塞把燃烧变成旋转；涡轮则让燃气持续冲击叶片连续旋转，没有往复部件，适合高转速、大功率和稳定气流的场合。
A piston engine converts combustion to rotation via reciprocating pistons in cylinders, while a turbine has gas continuously spinning blades with no back-and-forth parts, suiting high-speed, high-power, steady-flow use.

In the Brayton and Rankine cycles, where do they heat and do work, and why is combined cycle more efficient?

在压缩机中，为什么往复式和离心式适用于完全不同的压力流量工况？

In compressors, why do reciprocating and centrifugal types suit very different pressure-flow duties?

往复式靠活塞间歇压缩，每级压比高、流量小但排气压力可达很高，适合小流量高压。离心式靠叶轮连续加速气体再用扩压器转成压力，流量大、压比单级有限，适合大流量中低压的连续工况。
Reciprocating compressors use pistons for intermittent compression with high per-stage ratio and small flow but very high discharge pressure—good for low-flow high-pressure. Centrifugal types accelerate gas continuously with impellers and diffusers: large flow, limited single-stage ratio, suited to high-flow medium-low pressure duty.

为什么热机的效率永远达不到100%？

Why can a heat engine never reach 100% efficiency?

热机靠高温热源与低温冷源之间的温差做功，热力学第二定律规定：一部分热量必须作为废热排给冷源。理想卡诺热机的效率等于1减去冷源温度与热源温度之比，是任何实际热机的上限。热源温度越高、冷源温度越低，效率越高——这就是为什么燃气轮机、超临界锅炉都在追求更高的初温，冷源则尽量利用低温海水。实际热机还要扣除各种不可逆损失：汽油机约25%到30%，现代联合循环燃气轮机可达60%左右。
A heat engine works from the temperature difference between a hot reservoir and a cold one, and the second law of thermodynamics dictates that some heat must be dumped to the cold reservoir as waste. The ideal Carnot efficiency, 1 minus the cold-to-hot temperature ratio, is the upper bound no real engine can exceed. Higher hot-source temperature and lower cold-source temperature raise efficiency — which is why gas turbines and supercritical boilers chase ever-higher inlet temperatures while cold sinks use cold seawater. Real engines lose more to irreversibility: gasoline engines manage about 25-30%, while modern combined-cycle gas turbines reach around 60%.

为什么齿轮的齿形几乎都是"渐开线"而不是直线或圆弧？

Why are gear teeth almost always involute curves rather than straight or circular?

渐开线是"线绕圆无滑动展开时端点留下的轨迹"，它有一个独特性质：两个渐开线齿廓啮合时，公法线始终是两基圆的公共内公切线，啮合线是一条固定直线。这意味着瞬时传动比恒定，传动平稳无冲击；而且即使中心距因轴承磨损或装配误差略有改变，传动比仍然不变，这叫传动的可分性。此外啮合过程以纯滚动为主、兼有少量滑动，便于油膜润滑。直线或圆弧齿形会在节圆附近产生冲击和干涉，因此很少用于动力传动。
An involute is the curve traced by the end of a string unwinding without slipping from a circle, and it has a unique property: as two involute profiles mesh, their common normal is always the internal common tangent of the two base circles, so the line of action is a fixed straight line. That means the instantaneous velocity ratio is constant, giving smooth shock-free transmission; and the ratio stays constant even if the center distance shifts slightly due to bearing wear or assembly error — the property of separability. Meshing is mostly pure rolling with a little sliding, which helps oil-film lubrication. Straight or circular teeth would impact and interfere near the pitch circle, so they are rarely used for power transmission.

齿轮失效里的点蚀和胶合是两种完全不同的机理吗？

Are gear pitting and scuffing two fundamentally different failure mechanisms?

点蚀属于接触疲劳：齿面在赫兹接触应力下反复受载，次表层最大剪应力处萌生裂纹并扩展剥落成麻点，是随循环次数累积的疲劳过程，取决于接触应力水平、材料硬度与钢材洁净度。胶合（擦伤）则是润滑失效引起的瞬时黏着：滑动速度与载荷使油膜过薄、闪温过高，微凸体金属直接黏焊后又被撕开，几分钟内就能毁掉齿面。因此对策方向不同：抗点蚀靠渗碳淬火提高硬度、改善表面粗糙度和降低接触应力；抗胶合靠极压添加剂与合适黏度、降低齿面滑动率、齿形修形和加强冷却。
Pitting is contact fatigue: repeated Hertzian loading nucleates cracks near the depth of maximum shear stress, which grow and spall into small craters, an accumulating process governed by contact stress, hardness and steel cleanliness. Scuffing is instantaneous adhesive failure of the lubricant film: high sliding speed and load thin the film and raise flash temperature until asperities weld and tear, ruining a flank within minutes. The remedies diverge accordingly — pitting resistance comes from case carburizing, higher hardness, smoother finish and lower contact stress, while scuffing resistance comes from extreme-pressure additives and viscosity, lower sliding ratio, profile modification and better cooling.

什么是临界转速，为什么旋转轴超过该转速会发生剧烈振动？

What is critical speed and why does a rotating shaft vibrate violently past it?

临界转速是转轴系统的固有弯曲振动频率对应的转速。当转速接近该频率时，不平衡质量激发的离心力与轴的变形相互加强，发生共振，振幅急剧放大，可能损坏设备。
Critical speed is the rotation rate matching the shaft's natural lateral vibration frequency. Near it, centrifugal force from unbalance reinforces shaft deflection, causing resonance with sharply growing amplitude that can destroy the machine.

在热力学中，卡诺效率为什么是热机效率的理论上限？

In thermodynamics, why is Carnot efficiency the theoretical upper limit for heat engines?

卡诺效率只取决于高温热源与低温热源的温度比值，表示在理想可逆循环中热量转化为功的最大可能比例。任何实际循环都有摩擦、不可逆传热等损失，因此其效率必然低于这个由温度决定的上限。
Carnot efficiency depends only on the hot and cold reservoir temperatures and represents the maximum fraction of heat convertible to work in an ideal reversible cycle. Real cycles have friction and irreversible heat transfer, so they can never exceed this temperature-limited bound.

Why does a fridge get hot at the back but stay cold inside?

The price is more weight and material, which is why modern design increasingly uses probabilistic reliability methods to balance cost and safety.

抽水马桶按下按钮后水是怎么冲走的？

After pressing the toilet button, how does the water flush away?

按下按钮会打开水箱里的阀门，存水快速冲进马桶，借落差和虹吸把脏水连同废物从弯管吸走排到下水道，然后水箱再慢慢蓄满水。
Pressing the button opens a valve in the tank; stored water rushes in and, using the drop and siphon action, sucks the waste through the curved trap into the sewer, then the tank slowly refills.
