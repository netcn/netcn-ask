基于性能的抗震设计（PBSD）与传统按规定设计在目标设定上有何本质不同？

How does performance-based seismic design (PBSD) fundamentally differ from traditional prescriptive design in its objectives?

传统设计按规范公式保证不倒的最低安全；PBSD 则明确不同地震水准下的性能目标（如正常使用、可修复、防倒塌），并通过非线性时程分析与损伤控制反向优化结构。这使投资与风险可量化权衡，但对模型与不确定性要求更高。
Traditional design uses code formulas for a minimum no-collapse safety; PBSD explicitly sets performance goals under different seismic levels (e.g., operational, repairable, collapse-prevention) and optimizes the structure via nonlinear time-history analysis and damage control. It makes cost-risk trade-offs quantifiable but demands better models and uncertainty handling.

结构健康监测（SHM）中基于振动的损伤识别，为何难以区分真实损伤与环境噪声？

In vibration-based structural health monitoring (SHM), why is it hard to separate real damage from environmental noise?

温度、风速、荷载等环境因素会显著改变结构模态频率，其量级常与早期损伤引起的变化相当甚至更大，造成误报。解决依赖长期基线建模、机器学习区分环境效应，或用应变、声发射等多源信息交叉验证损伤。
Temperature, wind, and loading shift modal frequencies markedly, often as much as or more than early damage does, causing false alarms. Solutions rely on long-term baseline modeling, machine learning to separate environmental effects, or cross-validating with strain and acoustic emission from multiple sources.

基础隔震技术如何让建筑物在地震中受到的力大幅减小？

How does base isolation greatly reduce the forces a building experiences during an earthquake?

隔震层在建筑物与地基之间布置橡胶或滑动支座，把结构自振周期拉长到远离地震主能量频段，使上部结构随地面缓慢平动而非剧烈摇晃。这样输入结构的加速度被显著削弱，从而保护主体与内部设备，但需合理设计位移余量。
An isolation layer of rubber or sliding bearings between building and foundation lengthens the structure's natural period away from the earthquake's main energy band, so the superstructure moves slowly with the ground rather than shaking violently. Input acceleration is greatly reduced, protecting the structure and its contents, though displacement capacity must be well designed.

韧性城市概念关注的韧性主要指系统具备什么能力？

In the resilient-city concept, what capability does resilience primarily refer to?

韧性指城市系统在遭受冲击（自然灾害、疫情、断供）时维持基本功能，并在冲击后快速恢复甚至变得更强的能力，涵盖冗余、多样性与自适应。它与单纯抗灾强度不同，更强调吸收扰动与学习演进，而非把风险完全消除。
Resilience refers to a city's ability to maintain essential functions under shocks (disasters, pandemics, supply cuts) and recover quickly or even adapt stronger afterward, covering redundancy, diversity, and self-organization. Unlike mere resistance, it emphasizes absorbing disturbance and learning rather than eliminating risk entirely.

建筑信息模型（BIM）相比传统二维图纸为工程管理带来什么改变？

What change does building information modeling (BIM) bring to engineering management versus traditional 2D drawings?

BIM 用包含几何、材料与工序等属性的三维数字模型作为单一信息源，使设计冲突、工程量与进度可在施工前被模拟与协调。它支持碰撞检查、成本估算与运维管理，减少返工并提升多方协作效率。
BIM uses a 3D digital model carrying geometry, material, and schedule attributes as a single source of truth, letting design clashes, quantities, and timelines be simulated and coordinated before construction. It supports clash detection, cost estimation, and facility management, cutting rework and improving collaboration.

城市数字孪生在防灾减灾中可以发挥什么作用？

What role can an urban digital twin play in disaster prevention and mitigation?

城市数字孪生把建筑、管网、交通与人口等要素实时映射到虚拟模型，可模拟洪水、地震或拥堵的演化并预演应急方案。管理者借此在灾害发生前识别薄弱点、优化疏散与资源调度，把被动响应转为主动预防。
An urban digital twin mirrors buildings, utilities, traffic, and population in real time, letting planners simulate floods, earthquakes, or congestion and rehearse response. It helps identify vulnerabilities, optimize evacuation and resource dispatch before disasters, turning passive response into active prevention.

装配式建筑相比现场浇筑在施工效率与质量上有什么优势？

What advantages does prefabricated construction have over cast-in-place building in efficiency and quality?

装配式在工厂把构件标准化预制，现场仅吊装连接，受天气影响小、工期可缩短数成，且工厂环境更利于质量与精度控制。代价是运输尺寸受限、节点连接设计要求高，且需配套的吊装与施工组织。
Prefabrication standardizes components in a factory and only assembles them on site, less weather-dependent, often cutting schedule by tens of percent, with better quality and dimensional control in the shop. The cost is transport-size limits, demanding connection design, and matching lift and logistics planning.
