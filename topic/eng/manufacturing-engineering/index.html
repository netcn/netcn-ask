什么是“六西格玛”？它如何用统计方法控制质量？

What is Six Sigma, and how does it use statistics to control quality?

六西格玛是一套以数据驱动的质量管理方法，目标是在考虑1.5σ偏移时每百万机会缺陷数不超过3.4。它用 DMAIC 流程与统计过程控制（SPC）持续降低过程变异。
Six Sigma is a data-driven quality methodology aiming for no more than 3.4 defects per million opportunities (with a 1.5σ shift). It uses the DMAIC roadmap and statistical process control (SPC) to continuously reduce process variation.

什么是“增材制造”（3D 打印）？相比减材制造有何优势？

What is additive manufacturing (3D printing), and how does it compare with subtractive manufacturing?

增材制造按 CAD 模型逐层堆积材料成形，无需模具。优势是可做复杂几何、材料浪费少、适合快速原型；相对切削加工，速度较慢且性能可能呈各向异性。
Additive manufacturing builds parts layer by layer from a CAD model without tooling. Advantages are complex geometry, low material waste, and rapid prototyping; compared with machining it is slower and properties can be anisotropic.

什么是“公差”与“配合”？为什么零件要标注公差？

What are tolerance and fit, and why are tolerances specified on parts?

公差是允许的尺寸变动量，配合描述相配零件间的松紧关系（间隙配合、过渡配合、过盈配合）。标注公差可实现互换性、保证功能，又避免不必要的过高加工精度带来的成本。
Tolerance is the permissible variation in a dimension, and fit describes the clearance or interference between mating parts (clearance, transition, or interference fit). Specifying tolerances ensures interchangeability and function without the cost of unnecessarily high precision.
