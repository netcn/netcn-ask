汉明码是如何发现并纠正单个比特错误的？校验位的个数怎么确定？

How does a Hamming code detect and correct a single-bit error, and how is the number of parity bits determined?

汉明码把校验位放在2的幂次位置（1、2、4、8等），第i个校验位负责所有编号的二进制第i位为1的位置，接收端重新计算各组校验并对照收到的校验位，得到出错位置的二进制编号，称为校正子。n个校验位可以定位2的n次方减1个位置，因此要满足2的n次方大于等于m加n加1，其中m是数据位数，例如m为11时n等于4，构成(15,11)汉明码。校正子为0表示无错，非零值直接指出错误位，翻转即可纠正，这就是它纠正单比特错误的原理。
Hamming codes place parity bits at power-of-two positions (1, 2, 4, 8 and so on), and parity bit i covers all positions whose binary index has bit i set; the receiver recomputes the parity groups and compares them with the received parity bits, and the pattern of mismatches, called the syndrome, gives the binary index of the erroneous bit. With n parity bits, 2^n minus 1 positions can be located, so the requirement is 2^n greater than or equal to m plus n plus 1 for m data bits; for m equal to 11, n equals 4, giving the (15,11) code. A zero syndrome means no error, and a non-zero syndrome directly names the flipped bit, which is then inverted, which is how single-bit errors are corrected.

In CMOS ICs, why does static power come mainly from leakage rather than switching?

In ideal CMOS one network is on and the other off in steady state, so there's no DC path and switching power occurs only during transitions. As processes shrink, subthreshold and gate leakage grow, making standby leakage the dominant static power.

为什么"异步"电动机的转子转速永远比旋转磁场慢一点？

Why does an induction motor's rotor always run slightly slower than the rotating field?

三相定子绕组产生以同步转速旋转的磁场，转子导条相对磁场运动时感应出电流，电流在磁场中受力产生转矩。如果转子转速追平同步转速，转子与磁场的相对运动为零，感应电流消失、转矩归零，所以转子永远以一定的"滑差"落后，实际转速等于同步转速乘以一减滑差率，滑差率一般在1%到5%。负载越重，需要的转矩越大，滑差也越大。正是这个"跟不上"才让电机能带负载运行，也是"异步"名称的由来。
The three-phase stator windings create a field rotating at synchronous speed; the rotor bars move relative to it and have currents induced, and those currents experience force in the field to produce torque. If the rotor ever matched synchronous speed, relative motion would vanish, induced current would die, and torque would go to zero — so the rotor always lags by a "slip", with actual speed equal to synchronous speed times one minus the slip fraction, typically 1-5%. Heavier load needs more torque, hence more slip. That very "lagging behind" is what lets the motor drive loads, and where the name induction/asynchronous comes from.

What is impedance matching and why does a mismatched RF line reflect signals?

When a line's characteristic impedance differs from the load, the traveling wave isn't fully absorbed at the load and the excess reflects back, forming standing waves that waste power and distort signals. Matching minimizes reflection and maximizes transfer.

为什么全世界电力系统都选三相交流电，而不是两相或四相？

Why does the whole world standardize on three-phase AC, not two or four phases?

三相制的三个相位相差120度，各相功率的脉动互相抵消，合成瞬时功率恒定，电机输出转矩没有波动，这是单相或两相做不到的。用同样三根导线，在三相对称时零线电流为零，输送的功率可达单相系统的约1.732倍，导线材料利用率最高。三相绕组还能天然产生旋转磁场，让三相异步电动机无需启动装置就能自行启动。相数再多（如六相）对功率品质的提升微乎其微，却要多花导线成本，所以三相是综合最优的选择。
Three phases spaced 120 degrees apart cancel each other's power pulsations, giving constant instantaneous total power and ripple-free motor torque — something single-phase or two-phase cannot achieve. With the same three conductors, and zero neutral current when the system is balanced, you can deliver about 1.732 times the power of single-phase, the best utilization of conductor material. Three-phase windings also naturally produce a rotating magnetic field, so three-phase induction motors start by themselves with no starting hardware. More phases (say six) improve power quality only marginally while requiring extra conductors, which is why three-phase is the optimum compromise.

用变频器驱动的电机，为什么轴承会出现电蚀损坏？

Why do motors fed by variable-frequency drives suffer electrical bearing damage?

PWM 逆变器输出的三相电压之和不为零，产生高频共模电压，它通过定子绕组与转子、机壳之间的寄生电容耦合到转轴上形成轴电压。当轴电压超过轴承润滑油膜的击穿强度时发生放电，在滚道和滚珠表面熔出微坑，长期积累成搓板状条纹，伴随振动噪声上升并很快导致轴承失效。抑制措施包括安装导电碳刷或轴接地环旁路电流、采用绝缘或陶瓷球轴承切断回路，以及使用对称屏蔽电缆和共模电抗器降低共模电压与 dV/dt。
A PWM inverter's three output voltages do not sum to zero, producing a high-frequency common-mode voltage that couples through parasitic capacitances between stator winding, rotor and frame to appear as shaft voltage. When it exceeds the dielectric strength of the bearing's lubricant film, a discharge melts micro-craters in raceways and balls, which accumulate into washboard fluting with rising noise and vibration and rapid bearing failure. Countermeasures include conductive brushes or shaft grounding rings to bypass the current, insulated or ceramic-ball bearings to break the loop, and symmetrical shielded cable plus common-mode chokes to cut common-mode voltage and dV/dt.

卫星通信链路预算中的关键损耗有哪些？为什么雨衰对Ku/Ka频段影响很大？

What are the key losses in a satellite link budget, and why does rain fade matter so much in the Ku/Ka bands?

链路预算把发射功率、天线增益、自由空间损耗、大气吸收、雨衰和接收机灵敏度等叠加起来计算链路余量。自由空间损耗随频率的平方和距离的平方增大，静止轨道卫星距离远，单跳损耗巨大。雨衰源于雨滴对电磁波的吸收和散射，频率越高波长越短，雨滴尺寸越接近波长，衰减越严重，因此Ku/Ka频段大雨时可能损失数dB甚至十几dB。工程上用衰减余量、自适应编码调制和上行功率控制来对抗。
A link budget sums transmit power, antenna gains, free-space loss, atmospheric absorption, rain fade and receiver sensitivity to yield the link margin. Free-space loss rises with the square of both frequency and distance, and geostationary satellites are so far away that one hop loses tens of dB. Rain fade comes from absorption and scattering by raindrops; as frequency rises, drop size approaches the wavelength, so attenuation grows, and in the Ku/Ka bands heavy rain can cost several to over ten dB. Fade margins, adaptive coding and modulation, and uplink power control are used to counter it.

什么是波分复用（WDM）？它如何成倍提升光纤的传输容量？

What is wavelength division multiplexing (WDM), and how does it multiply the capacity of a single fibre?

WDM把多路不同波长的光信号在发送端通过合波器合成一束光注入同一根光纤传输，接收端再用分波器把各波长分离出来，实现一根光纤同时承载多路信号。由于每路波长互相独立、互不干扰，光纤容量就按波长数成倍增长，再加上每路采用高速调制和相干接收，单纤容量可达几十Tbps。它与时分复用不同，是在光域维度上的复用。
WDM combines multiple signals on different optical wavelengths into one beam at the transmit end via a multiplexer, sends them down the same fibre, and at the receive end a demultiplexer separates the wavelengths again, so a single fibre carries many channels at once. Because the wavelengths are independent and do not interfere, capacity grows in proportion to the number of wavelengths, and with high-speed modulation and coherent detection each channel is very fast, pushing single-fibre capacity to tens of Tbps. Unlike time division multiplexing, it multiplies capacity in the optical domain.

超高压输电线路为什么把一相做成好几根并排的分裂导线？

Why is one phase of an extra-high-voltage line built as several bundled sub-conductors?

单根导线的表面电场强度随电压升高而增大，一旦超过空气击穿场强就产生电晕，带来功率损耗、无线电与可听噪声以及臭氧腐蚀。把一相拆成二到八根间隔布置的子导线后等效半径大幅增大，表面场强显著降低，于是不必使用不切实际的超粗导线就能抑制电晕。分裂还减小线路电抗、提高输送能力，代价是需要间隔棒与更多金具、铁塔风载增大，并要防范子导线间的次跨距振荡与覆冰舞动。
Surface field strength on a single conductor grows with voltage, and once it exceeds air's breakdown strength corona appears, bringing power loss, radio and audible noise and ozone attack. Splitting a phase into two to eight spaced sub-conductors greatly increases the equivalent radius and lowers the surface gradient, suppressing corona without impractically thick conductors. Bundling also reduces series reactance and raises transmission capacity, at the cost of spacers and extra hardware, larger tower wind loads, and the need to control subspan oscillation and ice galloping between sub-conductors.

But denser constellations mean smaller point spacing and higher sensitivity to noise and phase noise, so high-order QAM demands high SNR and is paired with channel coding and equalisation.

为什么电动机通上电就会转起来？

Why does an electric motor spin when current flows?

电动机靠磁场相互作用转动：定子线圈通电产生旋转磁场，转子（永磁体或感应电流）处在这个磁场中，载流导体受到的电磁力让转子追逐旋转磁场，感应电机的转速略低于同步转速，同步电机则与之完全同步。直流电机靠换向器在转子转到合适角度时切换电枢电流方向，使转矩始终朝同一方向，转子才能持续旋转；交流异步电机则靠转子与旋转磁场之间的速度差（滑差）维持感应电流，同步电机靠转子磁极锁定旋转磁场。输出力矩的大小由电流与磁场强度的乘积决定。
A motor spins through interacting magnetic fields: current in the stator windings creates a rotating field, and the rotor (permanent magnets or induced currents) sits inside it and feels electromagnetic force on its conductors, which makes the rotor chase the rotating field — an induction rotor runs slightly below synchronous speed, while a synchronous rotor locks to it exactly. In a DC motor the commutator switches the armature current direction as the rotor reaches the right angular position, so torque keeps acting in one rotational direction and the rotor keeps spinning; an induction motor relies on the speed difference (slip) between rotor and field to sustain the induced currents, while a synchronous motor locks its poles to the rotating field. Output torque is set by the product of current and field strength.

发电机是怎样把运动变成电的？

How does a generator turn motion into electricity?

让线圈在磁场中转动（或让磁场相对线圈动），磁通量变化就会在线圈里感应出电流，这就是法拉第电磁感应，水轮、风轮都是先转再发电。
Rotating a coil in a magnetic field changes the magnetic flux and induces a current by Faraday's law; water and wind turbines spin first, then generate.

电池里的电是怎么存起来的？

How is electricity stored inside a battery?

电池里有两种不同的材料泡在电解液里，化学反应会把电子推到一侧积存起来。用电器接通时，电子从一边流向另一边形成电流，反应慢慢消耗完电就没了。
A battery has two different materials in an electrolyte; chemical reactions pile electrons on one side. When you connect a device, electrons flow from one side to the other as current, and the reaction runs down until empty.

为什么手机充电器要把交流电变成直流电？

Why does a phone charger convert AC to DC?

电网送来的是方向和大小周期性变化的交流电，而手机电池和内部电路需要方向恒定的直流电才能稳定充放电。充电器里的整流电路先把交流变直流，再降压到合适的电压。
The grid supplies alternating current that reverses direction, but a phone battery and its circuits need steady direct current. The charger rectifies AC to DC, then steps it down to the right voltage.

电池是怎样储存和放出电能的？

How does a battery store and release electrical energy?

电池内部发生化学反应，把化学能存起来；接通电路时反应反向进行，电子从一端流到另一端形成电流，电就用掉了。
A battery stores energy in chemical reactions; when connected, the reaction runs the other way and electrons flow from one terminal to the other as current, delivering power.

避雷针是怎样保护建筑物的？

How does a lightning rod protect a building?

金属避雷针立在最高处，通过粗导线把雷电引到地下，让巨大电流安全地导入大地，而不是击穿房顶或引发火灾。
A metal rod at the highest point channels lightning through a thick wire into the ground, safely dumping the huge current into Earth instead of through the roof.
