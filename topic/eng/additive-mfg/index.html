激光粉末床熔融(LPBF)成形Ti-6Al-4V时,为什么把相邻层的扫描方向旋转约67°能明显降低残余应力并减少缺陷?

When laser powder bed fusion builds Ti-6Al-4V, why does rotating the scan direction by roughly 67° between layers noticeably reduce residual stress and defects?

若每层都沿同一方向扫描,热量沿该方向反复累积,温度梯度与收缩呈强各向异性,裂纹和翘曲随之加重。逐层转过67°这类非整数角后,新熔道不再持续压在前一层的熔道间隙上,孔隙难以连通成贯穿通道,整体热历史也更均匀。该策略配合基板预热已成为钛合金增材制造的常规做法。
If every layer is scanned along the same direction, heat accumulates anisotropically, steepening temperature gradients and shrinkage along that axis and aggravating cracking and warping. Rotating by a non-divisor angle such as 67° keeps new tracks from sitting persistently on the gaps left by previous ones, so pores struggle to link into continuous channels and the thermal history evens out. Combined with build-plate preheating, this strategy has become routine practice for titanium alloys.

#扫描策略 #残余应力

金属激光选区熔化(SLM/LPBF)打印Ti-6Al-4V时，为何默认工艺参数下零件往往呈现明显的各向异性力学性能？后处理热处理如何改善各向异性与残余应力？

When Ti-6Al-4V is printed by laser powder bed fusion (SLM/LPBF) with default parameters, why does the part often show markedly anisotropic mechanical behavior, and how does post-process heat treatment mitigate anisotropy and residual stress?

逐层扫描使熔池经历极高温度梯度（10^6–10^7 K/m），柱状β晶粒沿建筑方向外延生长，粗大原始β晶界贯穿多层，导致Z向延伸率与疲劳寿命低于X/Y向；层间未再熔合区也是裂纹萌生位点。920°C左右固溶+时效可使α′马氏体分解为α+β层片组织并部分再结晶打断柱状晶列，HIP（920°C/100MPa）进一步闭合内部孔隙，显著恢复疲劳性能。残余应力则主要靠去应力退火（650°C附近）缓解，否则薄壁件易翘曲开裂。
Layer-wise scanning imposes extreme thermal gradients (10^6–10^7 K/m); columnar prior-β grains grow epitaxially along the build direction across layers, so elongation and fatigue life in Z fall short of X/Y values, and lack-of-fusion boundaries act as crack initiation sites. Solution treatment near 920 °C plus aging decomposes α′ martensite into α+β lamellae and partially recrystallizes to break up columnar colonies, while HIP (920 °C / 100 MPa) closes internal porosity and substantially restores fatigue behavior. Residual stress is mainly relieved by stress-relief annealing around 650 °C; otherwise thin walls distort or crack.

#增材制造与3D打印 #LPBF钛合金各向异性与热处理

FDM打印件沿Z向的强度为什么通常比XY面低?有哪些工艺参数能减轻这种各向异性?

Why are FDM parts usually weaker along the Z direction than across the XY plane, and which process parameters ease this anisotropy?

熔丝逐层堆积时,层与层之间仅靠材料局部重熔形成的界面粘接,界面处孔隙率高、浸润不完全,受拉时裂纹优先沿层间扩展,所以Z向拉伸强度与层间剪切强度明显低于面内性能。提高热床与腔体温度、减小层厚、加大挤出宽度与走线重叠率都能增强层间融合;把主承载方向布置到面内同样是有效的对策。
Layer-by-layer deposition bonds layers only through partial local remelting, so interfaces carry higher porosity and incomplete wetting; under tension cracks preferentially run between layers, leaving Z-direction tensile and interlayer shear strength clearly below in-plane properties. Higher bed and chamber temperatures, thinner layers, wider extrusion, and greater raster overlap all strengthen interlayer fusion, and orienting the main load path into the plane works just as well.
