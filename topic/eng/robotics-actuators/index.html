伺服系统的三环控制（位置、速度、电流）各自作用是什么？

What are the respective roles of the three control loops (position, speed, current) in a servo system?

电流环最内，快速调节转矩、抑制电流波动；速度环居中，跟踪指令速度并改善动态；位置环最外，保证末端按指令定位。由内到外带宽递减，形成级联以保证精度与刚度。
The innermost current loop quickly regulates torque and suppresses current ripple; the middle speed loop tracks commanded velocity and improves dynamics; the outer position loop ensures the end effector follows position commands. Bandwidth decreases outward, forming a cascade for precision and stiffness.

在移动机器人路径规划中，A* 算法相比 Dijkstra 的优势来自哪里？

In mobile robot path planning, where does A* gain its advantage over Dijkstra?

Dijkstra 对所有节点盲目扩展，A* 引入启发函数估计到目标的代价，仅优先扩展有希望的方向，显著减少搜索节点数。在可采纳启发下 A* 仍能保证最优，效率远高于 Dijkstra。
Dijkstra expands nodes blindly, while A* adds a heuristic estimating cost to the goal and prioritizes promising directions, greatly reducing explored nodes. With an admissible heuristic A* still guarantees optimality but is far more efficient.

什么是执行器的死区，它对控制系统有什么影响？

What is actuator dead zone, and how does it affect a control system?

死区指输入控制信号小于某阈值时执行器无输出或不动的区域，源于摩擦、间隙或阀口开启压力。它会造成小信号下稳态误差、极限环振荡，需在控制律中补偿。
Dead zone is the input range below a threshold where an actuator produces no motion, due to friction, backlash, or valve cracking pressure. It causes steady-state error and limit-cycle oscillations under small signals, requiring compensation in the controller.

编码器在机器人中用来测量什么，增量式与绝对式有何不同？

What does an encoder measure in a robot, and how do incremental and absolute encoders differ?

编码器把转轴角位移转为电信号，用于位置与速度反馈。增量式输出相对脉冲、断电后需回零；绝对式直接给出唯一绝对角度编码，上电即知位置，抗掉电但成本较高。
An encoder converts shaft angular displacement to electrical signals for position and speed feedback. Incremental encoders output relative pulses and need homing after power loss; absolute encoders give a unique absolute angle, known at power-up, but cost more.

直流有刷电机和无刷电机的主要区别是什么？

What is the main difference between brushed and brushless DC motors?

有刷电机靠机械电刷与换向器切换电流，结构简单但电刷磨损需维护；无刷电机用电子换向驱动永磁转子，效率高、寿命长、噪声低，但需配套控制器。
Brushed motors switch current mechanically via brushes and a commutator, simple but wearing and maintenance-prone. Brushless motors use electronic commutation driving a permanent-magnet rotor, giving higher efficiency, longer life, and lower noise but needing a controller.

谐波减速器凭什么实现高减速比与高精度，其柔轮为何易疲劳？

How does a harmonic drive achieve high reduction and precision, and why is its flexspline prone to fatigue?

谐波减速器利用柔轮（薄壁弹性齿轮）在波发生器作用下的可控弹性变形与刚轮啮合，单级即可达到数十到数百减速比、几乎零背隙。柔轮每转承受交变弯曲应变，长期在应力集中处萌生疲劳裂纹。
A harmonic drive uses a thin-wall flexible spline whose controlled elastic deformation under the wave generator meshes with a rigid spline, giving single-stage ratios of tens to hundreds with near-zero backlash. The flexspline endures cyclic bending strain each turn, so fatigue cracks initiate at stress concentrations.

并联机器人（如 Stewart 平台）相比串联机械臂在刚度上有何优势？

What stiffness advantage do parallel robots like the Stewart platform have over serial manipulators?

并联机构由多条独立支链共同约束动平台，负载被分配到多个闭环，误差不逐级累积。因此刚度高、末端精度好、动态响应快，适合精密定位与运动模拟，但工作空间受限。
A parallel mechanism constrains the platform through several independent limbs, distributing load across closed loops so errors do not accumulate link by link. This yields high stiffness, good end accuracy, and fast dynamics, suiting precision positioning and motion simulation, though workspace is limited.

六轴工业机器人常用的运动学问题有哪两类？

What two classes of kinematic problems are common for six-axis industrial robots?

正向运动学由关节角求末端位姿，通过关节变换链直接计算；逆运动学由期望末端位姿反求各关节角，通常多解或无解，是轨迹规划的核心难点。
Forward kinematics computes end-effector pose from joint angles via the link transformation chain; inverse kinematics finds joint angles for a desired pose, often with multiple or no solutions, and is the core difficulty in trajectory planning.
