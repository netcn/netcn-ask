高速转向架为什么要采用带卸荷特性的抗蛇行减振器来抑制蛇行失稳?

Why do high-speed bogies fit anti-hunting yaw dampers whose damper force saturates through a relief valve?

锥形踏面使轮对在高速下可能发生自激蛇行运动,一旦振幅发散即为蛇行失稳,对应的线性临界速度须明显高于运营速度并留裕量。减振器串联于车体与构架之间,小幅振动时提供大阻尼保证直线运行稳定性,大幅运动时卸荷阀使阻尼力饱和,避免曲线通过时轮轨横向力过大和连接件疲劳。
Conical wheel treads make hunting a self-excited risk at speed, and the linear critical speed must sit well above operating speed with margin. Mounted between carbody and frame, the yaw damper supplies large damping for small oscillations to stabilise straight running, while its relief valve saturates the force during large motions so curve negotiation neither overloads the wheel-rail interface nor fatigues the mounting.

#蛇行运动 #临界速度

铁道车辆的蛇行失稳是如何从小幅摆动演变为发散运动的？工程上采取哪些措施推迟其临界速度？

How does railway vehicle hunting evolve from small self-steering sway into divergent motion, and which engineering measures postpone its critical speed?

锥形踏面的重力刚度使轮对在直线上自发横移并与摇头耦合，轮轨蠕滑向系统输入负阻尼；当这部分能量超过一系与二系悬挂的耗散能力时，摆动振幅持续放大直至轮缘贴靠钢轨。工程上通过提高一系定位刚度、加装抗蛇行油压减振器、优化踏面等效锥度和转向架回转阻力，把线性临界速度抬到最高运营速度之上并留足裕量。
Conical treads give wheelsets gravity stiffness that couples lateral shift with yaw on tangent track, and wheel-rail creep feeds negative damping into the system; once that input outgrows dissipation in primary and secondary suspensions, sway amplitude keeps swelling until flanges strike the rails. Engineers stiffen primary positioning, fit yaw hydraulic dampers, tune tread equivalent conicity and bolster rotation resistance, lifting the linear critical speed above top service speed with ample margin.

#蛇行运动 #走行部稳定性

高速动车组为什么普遍采用动力分散编组，而不是由一台大功率机车集中牵引全部车厢？

Why do high-speed EMUs spread traction motors across many cars instead of relying on one powerful locomotive to haul the entire consist?

动力分散把驱动轴布置在多节车厢之下，单根动轴所需的黏着重量大幅下降，起动加速和持续爬坡不再逼近轮轨黏着的极限。全列车轴重分布均匀，对轨道的动作用力和磨耗随之降低，检修时还能按转向架模块整组更换，运用可用度明显提高。
Distributed traction places driven axles under many cars, so each axle needs far less adhesive weight and starting acceleration or long-grade climbing never approaches the wheel-rail adhesion ceiling. The uniform axle-load spread cuts dynamic track loading and rail wear, and modular bogie swaps at depots raise fleet availability.
