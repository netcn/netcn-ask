水下机器人为什么把推进器斜着装成矢量布局？

Why are the thrusters of an underwater robot mounted at angles in a vectored layout?

水下机器人要在六个自由度上机动，而单个推进器只能沿自身轴线产生推力。把若干推进器按不同倾角布置，各自推力的矢量合成就能同时产生前进、侧移和转艏等运动。控制器通过求解推力分配矩阵，把期望的合力与合力矩分解到每台推进器。
An underwater vehicle must manoeuvre in six degrees of freedom, yet a single thruster only pushes along its own axis. Mounting several thrusters at different angles lets the vector sum of their thrusts produce surge, sway and yaw at the same time. The controller solves a thrust-allocation matrix to split the desired net force and moment among the individual units.

为什么PID控制能成为工业控制里最常用的算法？

Why is PID the most widely used control algorithm in industry?

PID控制按偏差工作：比例项的输出与当前误差成正比，负责快速响应；积分项累积历史误差，用来消除稳态偏差，否则系统会始终存在静差；微分项预测误差的变化趋势，抑制超调。只要把三个参数按被控对象调好，温度、转速、位置就能快速稳定。它不需要知道系统的精确数学模型，用一个经验整定法则就能工作，所以电机调速、恒温器、自动驾驶仪都用它。它的局限在于高度非线性或大滞后的对象，这类场合需要模型预测控制等更高级的方法。
PID acts on the error: the proportional term is proportional to the current error for fast response; the integral term accumulates past error to eliminate steady-state offset, without which a persistent static error remains; the derivative term anticipates the error's slope to damp overshoot. With just three tuned gains it can settle temperature, speed, or position quickly. It needs no precise model of the plant — an empirical tuning rule suffices — which is why motor drives, thermostats, and autopilots all use it. Its limits appear in strongly nonlinear or long-delay plants, which call for advanced methods like model predictive control.

机械臂建模为什么普遍采用 Denavit–Hartenberg 参数，四个参数就够描述一个连杆？

Why is Denavit–Hartenberg notation the standard way to model a robot arm, and why do four parameters suffice per link?

DH 约定规定每个连杆坐标系的 z 轴取在关节轴线上、x 轴取在相邻两轴线的公垂线方向，这样一般需要六个参数的刚体变换被约束到只剩连杆长度 a、连杆扭角 α、关节偏置 d 和关节角 θ 四个。相邻坐标系间的齐次变换于是写成两次旋转加两次平移的固定乘积，整条手臂的正运动学变成矩阵连乘，便于统一推导雅可比与动力学。代价是坐标系放置不自由，遇到平行或相交的轴线会出现参数不唯一与表示奇异，所以实际标定常改用带完整六参数的修正模型来吸收制造误差。
The DH convention forces each link frame's z axis onto the joint axis and its x axis along the common normal between consecutive axes, which reduces a general six-parameter rigid transform to four: link length a, twist α, offset d and joint angle θ. Consecutive frames are then related by a fixed product of two rotations and two translations, so forward kinematics becomes a chain of matrix multiplications from which Jacobians and dynamics follow systematically. The price is that frame placement is constrained and parallel or intersecting axes give non-unique parameterizations, so calibration often switches to a full six-parameter model to absorb manufacturing errors.

视觉伺服中基于图像的 IBVS 与基于位姿的 PBVS，在控制律上有何本质差别？

What is the fundamental control-law difference between image-based and position-based visual servoing?

IBVS 直接在图像平面上定义误差（如特征点像素坐标之差），用交互矩阵（图像雅可比）把像素误差映射成相机速度，因此对相机标定和目标模型误差不敏感，但相机在三维空间的轨迹不可控，大角度旋转时可能退化甚至让特征跑出视野。PBVS 先从图像重建目标相对位姿，再在笛卡尔空间做位姿控制，轨迹平滑直观，但依赖精确的内参和目标三维模型，标定误差会直接变成定位误差。工程上常用混合（2.5D）视觉伺服，把平移误差留在图像域、旋转误差放到位姿域，兼顾鲁棒性与轨迹品质。
IBVS defines the error directly in the image plane and maps pixel errors to camera velocity through the interaction matrix, so it tolerates calibration and model errors well but leaves the camera's 3-D path uncontrolled and can degenerate or drive features out of the field of view during large rotations. PBVS first reconstructs the target pose from the image and regulates pose in Cartesian space, giving predictable motion but inheriting every error in intrinsic calibration and in the target's 3-D model. Hybrid or 2.5-D visual servoing combines the two, controlling translation in the image and rotation in pose space to keep robustness while making the trajectory well behaved.

为什么机械臂要把"想去的目标位置"换算成"每个关节转多少度"？

Why must a robot arm convert a target position into joint angles?

机械臂的伺服电机只认关节角度，所以控制末端姿态必须解逆运动学：已知末端的位置和姿态，求各关节变量。正运动学是由关节角求末端位姿，相对简单；逆解则是非线性方程，可能有多解、无解或奇异位形——比如手臂完全伸直时就失去了某些方向的运动能力。6自由度机械臂利用腕部结构解耦可得到解析解，冗余机械臂则要用雅可比求逆等数值迭代方法，在无穷多个解里选出最优的一个。逆运动学是轨迹规划与操作控制的基础。
A robot arm's servo motors only understand joint angles, so commanding the end-effector requires solving inverse kinematics: given the desired position and orientation, find the joint variables. Forward kinematics — joint angles to pose — is straightforward; the inverse is a set of nonlinear equations with possible multiple solutions, no solutions, or singular configurations, such as a fully extended arm losing the ability to move in certain directions. Six-axis arms get closed-form solutions by decoupling at the wrist, while redundant arms iterate numerically (e.g., Jacobian inversion) to pick the best among infinitely many solutions. Inverse kinematics underpins trajectory planning and manipulation control.

助力外骨骼怎么知道穿戴者想抬腿还是想停下？

How does a powered exoskeleton know whether the wearer wants to lift a leg or stop?

外骨骼通过多种信号推测意图：关节角与角速度反映当前步态相位，足底压力判断支撑腿与摆动腿切换，表面肌电或人机接触力可在动作发生前几十毫秒捕捉发力趋势。控制器把这些信号送入状态机或学习模型，判定意图后再输出助力力矩。误判会让人对抗设备，因此意图识别的可靠性比助力大小更关键。
An exoskeleton infers intent from several signals: joint angles and rates indicate the current gait phase, insole pressure reveals the switch between stance and swing leg, and surface electromyography or human-machine interaction forces can catch muscle effort tens of milliseconds before motion. A state machine or learned model combines these signals to classify intent before commanding assistive torque. Misclassification makes the wearer fight the device, so reliable intent recognition matters more than raw assistance power.

惯性测量单元为什么会越用越偏，怎么办？

Why does an inertial measurement unit drift over time, and what can be done?

惯导要把角速度和加速度积分成姿态和位置，传感器的零偏和噪声也一起被积分，误差随时间累积成漂移。工程上用温度补偿和标定压低零偏，再融合磁力计、GPS、轮式里程计或视觉进行修正。行人和机器人还常用静止时刻的零速修正来周期性归零。
Inertial navigation integrates angular rate and acceleration into attitude and position, and the sensor's bias and noise get integrated too, so error accumulates as drift. Engineers reduce bias by temperature compensation and calibration, then fuse magnetometer, GPS, wheel odometry or vision data to correct it. Pedestrian and robot systems also apply zero-velocity updates during stationary moments to reset the error periodically.

为什么很多机器人手采用欠驱动手指？

Why do many robot hands use underactuated fingers?

为什么大多数工业机械臂刚好有6个关节（6自由度）？

A rigid body in 3-D space has 6 independent motions: translation along x, y, and z plus rotation about three axes. To place the end-effector at any position with any orientation, you need exactly 6 DOF to match those 6 parameters. With fewer than 6 you can only reach some orientations; with more you gain agility and obstacle avoidance but far more complex control. A 6-DOF arm's inverse kinematics has closed-form solutions, which is why it became the industrial standard; each joint is driven by a servo motor plus gearbox.

Vacuum suction grippers lift cardboard boxes and glass panels easily, so why do they fail on porous foam parts or rough castings?

吸盘靠内外压差把工件压在密封唇上，可提供的力约等于真空度乘以有效吸附面积，因此必须先建立并维持一个密闭腔。多孔材料和粗糙、弯曲的表面会持续漏气，真空泵的抽气量跟不上泄漏量，压差建立不起来，抓取力迅速衰减。对这类工件通常改用磁力吸盘、机械夹指或针刺式夹具，或者先贴一层柔性密封垫，人为造出可密封的接触面。
A suction cup holds a part through the pressure difference across its sealing lip, and the available force is roughly the vacuum level times the effective sealed area, so a closed cavity must exist first. Porous foams and rough or curved surfaces leak continuously; once leakage exceeds the pump's flow capacity the pressure difference never builds up and holding force collapses. Such parts are handled with magnetic, mechanical-finger or needle grippers instead, or by adding a compliant sealing pad that creates a sealable contact face.

机器人的自由度是什么意思，六个自由度够用吗？

What does degrees of freedom mean for a robot, and are six enough?

自由度指机器人可以独立运动的方向数目。要让末端工具在空间中到达任意位置并保持任意姿态，需要三个位置量加三个姿态量，共六个自由度。六轴机械臂正好满足这一需求，因此成为工业标配。七轴机器人多出的一个自由度用来绕开障碍或避免奇异位形，代价是求解和标定更复杂。
Degrees of freedom means the number of independent directions in which a robot can move. Placing a tool at an arbitrary position in space with an arbitrary orientation requires three position values plus three orientation values, six in total. A six-axis arm meets exactly that need, which is why it is the industrial standard. A seven-axis robot uses the extra freedom to dodge obstacles or avoid singularities, at the cost of harder inverse kinematics and calibration.

The surgeon moves a master handle and the robot scales the motion down by a set ratio, so a one-centimetre hand movement may become two millimetres at the instrument, shrinking errors along with it. The control system also filters out the high-frequency components of physiological tremor and can enforce a remote centre of motion so the instrument pivots about the incision. The result is finer, less tiring suturing and dissection inside confined cavities.
