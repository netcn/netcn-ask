什么是数字主线？

What is a digital thread?

数字主线是贯穿产品从设计、制造到运维全生命周期的连续性数据流，让各环节基于同一份可追溯数据协同，区别于单点的数字孪生。
A digital thread is the continuous data flow across a product's whole life, design, build and operate, so all stages share one traceable source, unlike a point digital twin.

在工业预测性维护中，数字孪生如何结合传感器数据和物理模型提前发现设备失效？

In industrial predictive maintenance, how does a digital twin combine sensor data with physical models to spot failures early?

孪生体一边按物理方程推算部件应有的温度、振动和应力，一边比对实测值，二者之差（残差）异常放大就是早期故障信号。再结合寿命模型，就能在损坏前提早安排维修。
The twin computes expected temperature, vibration and stress from physics while comparing with measurements; an abnormally growing mismatch (residual) signals early faults. Coupled with life models, maintenance is scheduled before breakdown.

数字孪生城市能做什么？

What can a digital twin city do?

它把城市的建筑、管网、交通和能耗建成可计算的虚拟模型，用于洪水推演、交通优化和应急演练，在虚拟中先试错再落地。
It builds a computable virtual model of buildings, pipes, traffic and energy for flood simulation, traffic optimization and drills, testing ideas virtually before real action.

什么是数字孪生，它和普通计算机仿真模型的根本区别是什么？

What is a digital twin, and what fundamentally distinguishes it from an ordinary computer simulation model?

数字孪生是物理对象在电脑里的实时动态镜像，靠传感器不断喂入真实数据来校准和更新，而传统仿真多是一次性的理想化计算。孪生体因而能反映设备当前的真实健康状态，而不只是理论表现。
A digital twin is a live, dynamic mirror of a physical object, continuously calibrated by sensor data, whereas a traditional simulation is usually a one-off idealized calculation. The twin thus reflects the asset's real current state, not just theory.

给一座大桥建“数字孪生”，工程师能从电脑里看到什么平时看不到的东西？

If a bridge gets a digital twin, what hidden things can engineers see on the computer that they normally cannot?

它能实时显示桥里每根钢索的拉力、桥面的微小变形和哪里正在慢慢疲劳，像给桥装了会说话的体检表。哪里快撑不住了，电脑会提前亮红灯提醒。
It shows in real time the tension in each cable, tiny deck deflections and where fatigue is creeping in—like a talking health report for the bridge. When something nears its limit, the screen warns you early.
