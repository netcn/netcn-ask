高铁为什么几乎不会追尾？

Why do high-speed trains almost never rear-end?

列控系统（如CTCS）实时测距、自动限速并保持闭塞分区，车地通信确保最小安全间隔。
The train-control system (e.g. CTCS) measures distances, auto-limits speed and keeps block sections, with cab–ground links enforcing minimum safe gaps.

磁悬浮列车为什么能'飘'在轨道上方？

How can a maglev train float above the track?

电磁力（吸引或排斥）抵消重力使车体与轨道无接触，没有机械摩擦，因而极快且安静。
Electromagnetic force (attraction or repulsion) cancels gravity so the train never touches the rail—no friction means very fast and quiet.

高铁车站为什么常常建在城外而不是市中心？

Why are high-speed stations often built outside the city center?

需要长直线进出站和大量用地，城外征地成本低、能带动新区，也避免穿越密集城区。
They need long straight approaches and land; suburbs cost less, spark new districts and avoid cutting through dense city areas.

为什么高铁车厢是'鼓形'而不是方方正正？

Why are train cars barrel-shaped rather than boxy?

鼓形增大内部空间与车体刚度，又降低气动阻力，是外形和结构的双重优化。
The barrel shape adds interior room and stiffness while cutting aerodynamic drag—a structural and aerodynamic win.

高铁车轮为什么是整体钢轮而不是橡胶轮胎？

Why solid steel wheels instead of rubber tyres on high-speed trains?

钢轮与钢轨接触阻力小、承载力大、耐高速磨损，能耗低；橡胶既不耐高温也不耐磨于这种速度。
Steel-on-steel has low resistance, high load capacity and wears well at speed with low energy use; rubber can't take the heat or wear at such speeds.

高铁为什么用无砟轨道而不是普通石子道床？

Why does high-speed rail use ballastless track instead of gravel ballast?

无砟轨道平顺性好、沉降小、几乎免维护，才能满足350公里时速对平顺与稳定的严苛要求。
Ballastless track is smoother, settles less and needs almost no maintenance—essential for the strict smoothness and stability at 350 km/h.

'复兴号'车头为什么是尖长像子弹？

Why is the Fuxing train's nose long and pointed like a bullet?

细长流线型降低空气阻力，并削弱进出隧道产生的压缩波（微压波噪声），更安静也更省电。
The long streamlined nose cuts drag and tames the compression wave (micro-pressure wave) at tunnels—quieter and more efficient.

高铁过弯道为什么不减速，人也不觉得晕？

Why can high-speed trains take curves without slowing or discomfort?

线路用大半径曲线并设'超高'（外轨抬高），让离心力被重力的分量抵消，乘客几乎感觉不到侧倾。
Gentle curves with 'superelevation' (outer rail raised) let gravity's component balance centrifugal force, so riders feel little sway.

Why does the pantograph slide along the overhead wire in a bow shape?

碳滑板持续接触接触网取电，弹性悬挂吸收振动，保证高速下稳定供电而不拉弧断流。
A carbon strip keeps contact with the catenary to draw power; elastic suspension absorbs vibration so supply stays stable at speed without arcing.
