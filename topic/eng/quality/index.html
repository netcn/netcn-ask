机器视觉为何要做相机标定？

Why must machine vision calibrate the camera?

标定得到相机的内参（焦距、畸变）和外参（位姿），把图像像素映射到真实世界坐标，才能保证尺寸测量与定位准确。
Calibration yields intrinsic (focal length, distortion) and extrinsic (pose) parameters, mapping pixels to real-world coordinates for accurate measurement and localization.

六西格玛想解决什么问题？

What problem does Six Sigma address?

它是一套以数据驱动的质量改进方法，目标是把缺陷率降到每百万次机会不超过3.4次，通过DMAIC流程减少过程波动。
Six Sigma is a data-driven quality method aiming for fewer than 3.4 defects per million opportunities, reducing process variation via the DMAIC cycle.

在线质量检测相比人工抽检有什么优势？

What are the advantages of inline inspection over manual sampling?

机器视觉可100%全检、速度快、标准一致，能发现人眼易漏的微小缺陷，并实时反馈给产线调整工艺。
Machine vision inspects 100 percent of parts at speed with consistent criteria, catches tiny defects humans miss, and feeds results back to tune the process.
