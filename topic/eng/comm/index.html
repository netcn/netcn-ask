Massive MIMO为什么能提升5G容量？

Why does Massive MIMO boost 5G capacity?

基站在同一频段布设大量天线，用波束赋形把信号精确指向各用户并复用空间维度，从而在相同频谱下服务更多用户、提升吞吐。
The base station uses many antennas to beamform signals to each user and reuse space, serving more users and raising throughput on the same spectrum.

调制在通信里起什么作用？

What does modulation do in communications?

调制把要传的信息（语音、数据）加载到高频载波上，便于远距离辐射和频分复用，常见方式有调幅、调频和调相。
Modulation puts information onto a high-frequency carrier for efficient radiation and channel sharing, via amplitude, frequency or phase modulation.

光纤为什么能传那么远还不丢失信号？

Why can fiber carry signals so far without loss?

光在玻璃纤芯中靠全反射约束前进，优质石英光纤损耗极低（约0.2 dB/km），且不受电磁干扰，因此适合大容量长距离通信。
Light is guided by total internal reflection in the glass core; quality silica fiber loses only about 0.2 dB/km and is immune to electromagnetic interference, ideal for long-haul, high-capacity links.
