自行车轮为什么是细辐条而不是实心圆盘？

Why spoked wheels instead of solid discs on bikes?

细辐条主要受拉、效率高又轻，迎风面积小阻力低；实心盘重且兜风。
Thin spokes work in tension—light and efficient with low drag; a solid disc is heavy and catches wind.

手机盖板玻璃的『离子交换强化』为什么能让薄玻璃更耐摔？

How does ion-exchange chemical strengthening make a thin cover glass more break resistant?

玻璃几乎总是从表面微裂纹在拉应力作用下扩展而断裂。把含钠的铝硅酸盐玻璃浸入约 400 ℃ 的熔融硝酸钾盐浴，较大的钾离子扩散进去置换较小的钠离子，表层想膨胀却被内部约束，于是形成几十微米深、数百兆帕的压应力层，内部则以较小的拉应力平衡。外来弯曲或划伤产生的拉应力必须先抵消这层压应力才能让裂纹扩展，因此强度和跌落抗划伤性能大幅提升。代价是一旦裂纹穿过压应力层进入内部拉应力区，储存的能量会让裂纹迅速蔓延、碎得更彻底，而且切割和打孔必须在强化之前完成。
Glass almost always fails by growth of surface flaws under tension. Immersing a sodium-bearing aluminosilicate glass in molten potassium nitrate near 400 °C lets larger potassium ions diffuse in and replace smaller sodium ions, so the surface layer wants to expand but is restrained by the interior, creating a compressive layer tens of micrometres deep at hundreds of megapascals balanced by mild interior tension. Any tension from bending or a scratch must first cancel that compression before a crack can run, which is why strength and drop resistance improve dramatically. The trade-off is that once a flaw penetrates past the compressive layer into the tensile core the stored energy drives rapid fragmentation, and cutting or drilling must be finished before strengthening.

帐篷和雨伞的撑杆为什么交叉成X形？

Why do tent and umbrella poles cross into X shapes?

四杆铰接成方框是机构而非结构：边长不变也能被推扁成平行四边形，侧风一推就塌。加一道斜撑、或让两杆交叉并固定交点，方框就拆成三角形，三边一定形状唯一。载荷于是转为沿杆轴的拉压，不必逼细杆硬受弯，同粗细能担大得多的力；伞的交叉还把沿杆推转成对称张开。但要注意交叉杆主要受压，太细会失稳弯折；交点若只滑不固定，约束依旧不足。
Four bars pinned into a frame make a mechanism, not a structure: the lengths can stay the same while the shape shears into a parallelogram, so a sideways gust folds it. Add a diagonal, or cross two bars and fix the crossing, and the frame becomes triangles, whose three sides allow only one shape. Load turns into tension and compression along the bars instead of forced bending, so rods of the same thickness carry far more, and an umbrella's crossings also change a push along the shaft into a symmetric opening. Crossed bars work mostly in compression, though, so a slender one buckles, and a crossing that slides instead of being fixed leaves the constraint short.

为什么很多门用长条形把手往下压就能开，而不用圆圆的球形门把？

Why do so many doors use a long lever handle you push down instead of a round knob?

长条把手本身就是一根杠杆，手掌、手肘甚至手臂随便压一下就能产生足够的转动力矩；圆球门把必须用手指抓住再靠摩擦扭转，手上有水、戴着手套或握力不足时很容易打滑。把手的形状还直观地告诉人应该往下压，这种一看就知道怎么用的特征在设计学里叫示能，能减少试错。因此无障碍设计规范普遍要求公共通道的门采用可用握拳或手肘操作、不需要抓握和转腕的把手形式。
A lever handle is literally a lever, so a palm, elbow or even forearm can generate ample turning moment, whereas a round knob must be gripped by the fingers and twisted through friction, which slips when hands are wet or gloved or grip strength is low. The handle's shape also visibly suggests pushing down, a self-explanatory cue designers call an affordance that spares people trial and error. Accessibility codes accordingly require door hardware on public routes to be operable with a closed fist or elbow, without tight grasping or twisting of the wrist.

人行道过街口的斜坡上，为什么要压出一片凸起的小圆点？

Why is a patch of raised domes built into the sloped part of a sidewalk curb ramp?

那是触感警示，由规定直径、高度和间距的截头圆锥凸点组成，作用是在坡道与车行道交界处给视障者一个脚下和盲杖都能感知的明确信号，提示前方即将进入车行道。缘石坡道本身是为轮椅、婴儿车和拉杆箱消除高差的，纵坡通常限制在约 1:12，坡顶还要留出平台、两侧做过渡；但坡道抹平了原本靠缘石高差提供的边界线索，所以必须用凸点把这个提示补回来。规范还要求凸点区域与周围铺装有明显的明暗对比，让低视力者也能看清。
Those are detectable warnings, truncated domes of specified diameter, height and spacing that give blind and low-vision pedestrians an unmistakable underfoot and cane-detectable signal where the ramp meets the roadway. The ramp itself removes the level difference for wheelchairs, strollers and rolling luggage, with running slope generally limited to about 1:12 plus a level landing at the top and graded flares at the sides. Because flattening the curb erases the tactile edge cue the curb used to provide, the domes restore it, and standards additionally require visual contrast with the surrounding paving so people with low vision can see it.
