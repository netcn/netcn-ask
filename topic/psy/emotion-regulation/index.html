什么是情绪粒度？

What is emotional granularity?

情绪粒度指人们精确区分和命名不同情绪状态的能力。粒度越高，越能准确识别自己是失望还是羞耻，从而选择更合适的调节方式。它是情绪能力的重要指标。
Emotional granularity is the ability to precisely distinguish and label different emotional states. Higher granularity lets people tell disappointment from shame and choose more fitting regulation strategies. It is a key indicator of emotional skill.

矛盾情绪有什么适应意义？

What is the adaptive value of mixed emotions?

同时体验两种相反情绪（如喜悦又担忧）称为矛盾情绪，它在复杂决策中能促使更谨慎的信息加工。适度矛盾情绪与更灵活的思维相关。完全单一的情绪未必最优。
Experiencing two opposite emotions at once, such as joy mixed with worry, is called mixed or ambivalent emotion, and it can prompt more careful information processing in complex decisions. Moderate ambivalence relates to more flexible thinking. Uniform single emotions are not always optimal.

情绪传染是怎样发生的？

How does emotional contagion happen?

情绪传染指个体在无意识中模仿他人表情和姿态，从而“感染”相似情绪，常通过镜像机制实现。它在亲密关系和服务场景中尤其明显。理解它有助于管理群体氛围。
Emotional contagion is when people unconsciously mimic others' expressions and postures and thereby 'catch' similar feelings, often via mirroring. It is especially noticeable in close relationships and service settings. Understanding it helps manage group moods.

情绪的扩展—建构理论讲了什么？

What does the broaden-and-build theory of positive emotions say?

弗雷德里克森提出，积极情绪能短暂拓展人的注意、思维和行动范围，并长期建构身体、认知和社会资源。这与消极情绪收缩注意的模型相反。该理论解释了积极情绪的累积价值。
Frederickson proposed that positive emotions briefly broaden a person's attention, thinking, and action repertoire, and over time build lasting physical, cognitive, and social resources. This contrasts with models where negative emotions narrow attention. The theory explains the cumulative value of positive emotion.

长期压抑情绪会有什么后果？

What are the consequences of chronically suppressing emotion?

持续压抑情绪会增加慢性压力、损害人际关系，并与焦虑和躯体化症状相关。适度表达而非压抑，更有利于心理健康。表达方式可以温和而有边界。
Continually suppressing emotion raises chronic stress, harms relationships, and is linked to anxiety and bodily symptoms. Moderate expression rather than suppression supports mental health. Expression can be gentle and boundary-respecting.

为什么越反复想“我怎么这么惨”，心情反而越差、越走不出来？

Why does repeatedly chewing on why am I so miserable make mood worse and recovery slower?

诺伦-霍克西玛的“反刍”研究（Nolen-Hoeksema, 1991）指，被动反复聚焦自身痛苦而不采取行动，会延长并加深抑郁。反刍像在情绪伤口上打转，夺走解决问题的精力；相比之下，分散注意或意义重评更能打断这趟下行电梯。
Nolen-Hoeksema's rumination research (1991) shows passively brooding on one's distress without action prolongs and deepens depression. Rumination circles the emotional wound, stealing energy from problem-solving; distraction or meaning reappraisal better breaks the downward elevator.

表达性书写有什么心理好处？

What are the psychological benefits of expressive writing?

把创伤或压力事件写下来，有助于理清思绪、降低情绪强度，并改善身心健康状况。研究表明它能减少就医次数和提升情绪调节力。关键在于坦诚、持续地书写。
Writing down traumatic or stressful events helps organize thoughts, lower emotional intensity, and improve mental and physical health. Studies link it to fewer doctor visits and better emotion regulation. The key is honest, sustained writing.

感恩练习对情绪有什么影响？

How does gratitude practice affect emotion?

定期记录值得感激的事，能提升积极情绪、降低抑郁并改善睡眠。它把注意从缺失转向拥有，重塑日常体验。坚持数周即可见到变化。
Regularly noting things to be grateful for raises positive emotion, lowers depression, and improves sleep. It shifts attention from what is lacking to what is present, reshaping daily experience. Changes can appear within a few weeks.

什么是情绪劳动？

What is emotional labor?

情绪劳动指工作中需按规则管理和展露特定情绪，如服务员保持微笑。长期伪装情绪会带来疲惫和疏离感。它在服务业和护理行业中十分常见。
Emotional labor means managing and displaying prescribed emotions at work, such as a server maintaining a smile. Long-term faking of feelings brings fatigue and detachment. It is common in service and caregiving jobs.

给情绪命名为什么能减轻痛苦？

Why does naming an emotion reduce distress?

把模糊的不适具体命名为“焦虑”或“委屈”，能降低情绪唤起，这被称为影响标签。语言化让大脑的情绪反应更易被调节。它是对自己说话的简单技巧。
Pinpointing vague discomfort as 'anxiety' or 'grief' lowers emotional arousal, a process called affect labeling. Putting feelings into words makes the brain's emotional response easier to regulate. It is a simple talk-to-yourself skill.

为什么用“他/她”第三人称回想尴尬事，会比反复想“我怎么这么蠢”更快平静？

Why does replaying an embarrassing moment in the third person calm us faster than brooding why was I so stupid?

克罗斯的“自我距离”研究发现，用旁观者视角、以第三人称重述情绪事件，能降低心率与负性反刍（Kross 等, 2009; 2011）。拉开心理距离让大脑从沉浸的“热”状态切换到可分析的“冷”状态，于是情绪更快降温而不被反复咀嚼。
Kross's self-distancing work shows that recounting an emotional event from a third-person, observer view lowers heart rate and negative rumination (Kross et al., 2009, 2011). Creating psychological distance flips the brain from immersed hot to analytical cool processing, so feelings cool without being chewed over.

什么是认知重评？

What is cognitive reappraisal?

认知重评是指改变对事件的看法从而改变情绪反应，比如把失败看作学习机会。它比压抑情绪更健康和持久。大量研究把它列为最有效的情绪调节策略之一。
Cognitive reappraisal means changing how you interpret an event to change your emotional response, such as seeing failure as a learning opportunity. It is healthier and more lasting than suppressing emotion. Research ranks it among the most effective regulation strategies.

什么是正念情绪调节？

What is mindfulness-based emotion regulation?

正念调节强调不加评判地觉察当下情绪，与情绪保持距离而不被卷入。它降低反应性，让人更从容地回应。冥想练习是常见培养方式。
Mindfulness regulation emphasizes noticing present emotions without judgment and keeping distance from them rather than being swept along. It reduces reactivity and lets people respond more calmly. Meditation is a common way to build it.

笑真的能让人更开心吗？

Does laughing really make you happier?

微笑和笑声会向大脑传递积极信号，轻度提升情绪并缓解紧张。即使刻意微笑，也可能产生微弱的愉悦效应。当然笑不能替代对真实困扰的处理。
Smiling and laughing send positive signals to the brain, mildly lifting mood and easing tension. Even forced smiles can produce a small pleasant effect. Laughter, of course, cannot replace addressing real distress.

深呼吸为什么能缓解紧张？

Why does deep breathing ease nervousness?

缓慢的深呼吸能激活副交感神经系统，降低心率和血压，让身体从应激状态放松下来。它是一种随时可用的简单调节方法。配合数拍呼吸效果更好。
Slow deep breathing activates the parasympathetic nervous system, lowering heart rate and blood pressure and shifting the body out of a stressed state. It is a simple technique available anytime. Pairing it with counted breaths works even better.

听音乐能调节情绪吗？

Can listening to music regulate emotion?

听喜欢的音乐可以降低压力激素、提升愉悦感，帮助放松或集中注意力。不同节奏的音乐对不同情绪状态有不同效果。它是一种便捷的日常调节工具。
Listening to favored music can lower stress hormones, boost pleasure, and help relaxation or focus. Different tempos affect different mood states in different ways. It is a convenient everyday regulation tool.

什么是情绪调节？

What is emotion regulation?

情绪调节是人们管理自己情绪强度和持续时间的过程，比如让愤怒平息或让悲伤缓解。它既可以是无意识的，也可以是有意识练习的。良好的调节能力有助于人际关系和心理健康。
Emotion regulation is the process of managing the intensity and duration of one's emotions, such as calming anger or easing sadness. It can be automatic or a consciously practiced skill. Good regulation supports relationships and mental health.

哭出来真的会让心情变好吗？

Does crying actually make you feel better?

对许多人来说，哭泣能释放压力并引发安慰感，尤其在有支持的陪伴下。但它并非对所有人、所有情境都有效。哭本身是正常的情绪表达。
For many people, crying releases tension and brings a sense of relief, especially with supportive company. But it does not help everyone or every situation. Crying itself is a normal emotional expression.
