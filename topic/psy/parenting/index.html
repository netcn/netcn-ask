屏幕时间一定有害吗？怎样区分“被动看”和“一起互动学”？

Is screen time always harmful, and how do we tell passive watching from interactive learning?

质量比时长更关键：被动刷短视频与亲子共同看、边看边聊、随后现实里延伸游戏，效果完全不同。后者保留互动与迁移，更接近学习而非消磨。
Quality matters more than hours: passive short videos differ greatly from watching together, talking about it and extending into real play. The latter keeps interaction and transfer, closer to learning than killing time.

为什么说“父母的情绪稳定”是孩子最重要的安全基地？

Why is a parent's emotional stability the child's most important safe base?

依恋理论指出，照顾者是可预测、可回应的“安全基地”时，孩子才敢向外探索并学会调节自己。父母先稳住，孩子才有样板去处理情绪。
Attachment theory says when the caregiver is a predictable, responsive safe base, the child dares to explore and learns to self-regulate. Parents steady first, giving the child a model for handling emotion.

为什么让孩子“自己先试一试”，比马上帮忙更好？

Why is letting a child try first better than jumping in to help?

在“脚手架”式支持里，孩子先摸索、大人只在卡住时给一点提示，能长出真正的胜任感。什么都代劳，反而夺走了练习解决问题机会。
In scaffolded support the child explores first and the adult gives a small hint only when stuck, building real competence. Doing it all robs the chance to practice solving.

真正的“挫折教育”是故意给孩子制造困难吗？

Is real adversity education about deliberately creating hardships for a child?

不是。挫折教育的重点是当孩子自然遇到困难时，陪他面对而非替他扫平，并在事后一起复盘。人为制造苦难只会带来无助，而非韧性。
No. The point is to stand with the child through natural setbacks rather than clearing them, and review together afterward. Invented hardship breeds helplessness, not resilience.

孩子说谎，一定是品德问题吗？

Is a child lying always a moral problem?

很多时候说谎是发展阶段：幼儿分不清想象与现实，大些的孩子才懂“说谎能避免麻烦”。把它当信号去了解原因，比贴标签更有帮助。
Often lying is developmental: toddlers mix imagination with reality, older children learn lying avoids trouble. Treat it as a signal to understand the cause rather than a label.

孩子摔倒后，为什么先看大人的脸色再决定哭不哭？

After a fall, why does a child check the adult's face before deciding to cry?

这叫“社会参照”：婴幼儿还不会判断危险程度，会借用照顾者的表情来决定自己该怕还是该放松。大人镇定，孩子往往也更快平静。
Called social referencing, infants cannot yet judge how dangerous something is and borrow the caregiver's expression to decide whether to fear or relax. A calm adult helps the child settle faster.

为什么固定作息能让孩子情绪更稳？

Why does a regular routine make a child's mood steadier?

可预测的生活降低“接下来会怎样”的不确定感，孩子更有安全感，也更容易在固定时间入睡。规律不是束缚，而是给成长的稳定底座。
A predictable day lowers uncertainty about what comes next, giving the child safety and easier sleep at set times. Routine is not a cage but a stable base for growth.

孩子闹脾气时，为什么“先共情”比“先讲道理”更管用？

When a child melts down, why does empathizing first work better than reasoning first?

情绪上头时孩子听不进道理，先说出他的感受（“你很生气因为……”）能帮他感到被懂，情绪降下来才听得进话。先连接，再引导。
In a meltdown a child cannot hear reason; naming the feeling (you are angry because) helps him feel understood, and once calm he can listen. Connect first, then guide.

为什么表扬“你真努力”比“你真聪明”更能培养孩子的抗挫力？

Why does praising your effort help resilience more than praising smart?

夸聪明把孩子绑在“证明自己聪明”上，一遇困难就怕露怯；夸努力则把成功归因为可控制的行为，失败只是“还没学会”。这更接近成长型思维。
Praising smart ties a child to proving intelligence and fearing failure; praising effort credits controllable behavior, so failure is just not learned yet. This fits a growth mindset.
