正念减压（MBSR）用于临床干预，目前有哪些实证证据支持？

What empirical evidence supports mindfulness-based stress reduction (MBSR) as a clinical intervention?

随机对照试验显示 MBSR 可显著降低焦虑、抑郁与慢性疼痛患者的症状严重程度，并伴随前额叶与默认网络活动重塑。但效应量多为中等，且长期效果与师资质量高度相关，需避免夸大其适用范围。
Randomized trials report MBSR reduces symptoms of anxiety, depression, and chronic pain, accompanied by shifts in prefrontal and default-mode network activity. Effects are typically moderate, long-term outcomes depend on teacher quality, and claims about its scope should not be overstated.

双相情感障碍的情绪波动在神经与行为层面有何特征？

What are the neurobiological and behavioral features of mood swings in bipolar disorder?

双相障碍以反复发作的躁狂（或轻躁狂）与抑郁交替为特征，伴随昼夜节律失调、奖赏系统过度敏感与睡眠需求减少。神经影像显示杏仁核与纹状体反应性异常，遗传度约 60%–80%，但环境触发同样关键。
Bipolar disorder features recurrent manic or hypomanic episodes alternating with depression, along with circadian disruption, hypersensitive reward systems, and reduced sleep need. Neuroimaging shows atypical amygdala and striatal reactivity; heritability is about 60%–80%, yet environmental triggers matter.

抑郁症在生物学层面有哪些可观测的标记，它们与心理社会因素如何交互？

What biologically observable markers are linked to depression, and how do they interact with psychosocial factors?

研究发现抑郁与海马体积缩小、前额叶-边缘系统功能连接异常，以及下丘脑-垂体-肾上腺轴过度激活相关。这些神经内分泌与结构改变并非孤立，而是与早年创伤、慢性压力等心理社会因素通过表观遗传机制相互塑造。
Depression is associated with reduced hippocampal volume, altered prefrontal-limbic connectivity, and hyperactivity of the hypothalamic-pituitary-adrenal axis. These neurobiological changes interact with psychosocial factors such as early trauma and chronic stress through epigenetic mechanisms rather than acting in isolation.

心理咨询中的「治疗联盟」为什么会影响治疗效果？

Why does the therapeutic alliance influence the outcome of psychotherapy?

治疗联盟指来访者与咨询师之间就目标、任务达成的一致以及情感联结。大量元分析表明，联盟质量是跨流派预测疗效最稳定的因素之一，甚至超过具体技术手段，因为它影响来访者的投入与依从。
The therapeutic alliance is the agreement on goals and tasks plus the emotional bond between client and therapist. Meta-analyses rank alliance quality among the most consistent predictors of outcome across schools of therapy, often outweighing specific techniques by strengthening engagement.

焦虑障碍主要包括哪些类型，认知行为疗法又是如何治疗焦虑的？

What are the main types of anxiety disorders, and how does cognitive behavioral therapy treat anxiety?

焦虑障碍涵盖广泛性焦虑、惊恐障碍、社交焦虑与特定恐惧等类型。认知行为疗法通过识别并修正导致焦虑的扭曲认知，并结合系统性暴露练习来降低回避行为，其疗效已被大量随机对照试验证实。
Anxiety disorders include generalized anxiety, panic disorder, social anxiety, and specific phobias. Cognitive behavioral therapy reduces avoidance by identifying and restructuring distorted thoughts and pairing them with systematic exposure, an approach validated by numerous randomized controlled trials.

什么是「习得性无助」，它与抑郁有什么关系？

What is learned helplessness, and how is it related to depression?

习得性无助指个体在反复经历不可控的负面事件后，即使后来出现可改变的机会也放弃尝试。塞利格曼的实验表明，这种无助感与抑郁中的被动与无望感在机制上相似，是理解抑郁的重要模型。
Learned helplessness describes how organisms exposed to uncontrollable aversive events later fail to act even when change is possible. Seligman's experiments showed this helplessness resembles the passivity and hopelessness seen in depression, offering a key model for the disorder.

心理咨询中的「暴露疗法」是基于什么原理工作的？

On what principle does exposure therapy in psychotherapy work?

暴露疗法让来访者在安全环境下反复接触引发恐惧的刺激，利用习惯化与消退学习使恐惧反应逐渐降低，并打破通过回避维持焦虑的循环。它常用于恐惧症、PTSD 与强迫症。
Exposure therapy repeatedly presents fear-eliciting cues in a safe setting, relying on habituation and extinction learning to reduce fear responses and break the avoidance cycle that maintains anxiety. It is widely used for phobias, PTSD, and OCD.
