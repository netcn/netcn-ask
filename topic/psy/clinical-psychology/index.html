记忆再固化理论如何为创伤治疗提供新思路？

How does memory reconsolidation theory open new approaches to trauma treatment?

动物实验发现，记忆被提取后会短暂进入不稳定状态，需要再次合成蛋白才能重新稳定，这个窗口期内的干预可以修改记忆的情绪强度。基于此，研究者尝试在唤起创伤记忆后给予普萘洛尔等阻断肾上腺素作用的药物，试图削弱记忆的情绪成分而保留事实内容，部分随机试验报告了症状改善。此外，行为学范式如提取后立即进行消退训练也被用于降低恐惧复发。目前的争议在于人类研究结果不够一致，边界条件如记忆年龄与提取强度仍需明确。
Animal work showed that a retrieved memory briefly becomes labile and requires new protein synthesis to restabilise, so intervening within that window can alter the memory's emotional intensity. Building on this, researchers have given propranolol and other adrenergic blockers after deliberately reactivating a traumatic memory, aiming to weaken its emotional component while preserving factual content, and several randomised trials report symptom improvement. Behavioural variants such as retrieval followed immediately by extinction training have also been used to reduce return of fear. The open controversy is that human findings are inconsistent and boundary conditions such as memory age and strength of reactivation remain to be pinned down.

Rosenhan 的“精神病房里的正常人”实验得出了什么结论？

What conclusion did Rosenhan's “On Being Sane in Insane Places” experiment draw?

Rosenhan 于 1973 年安排 8 名假病人（自诉幻听）进入精神病院，入院后表现正常，却无人被识破，且出院诊断多为“精神分裂症缓解期”。实验揭示精神诊断标签的顽固性与诊断标准的不可靠，推动了诊断体系的改革。
In 1973 Rosenhan had eight confederates (complaining only of a hallucinated voice) admitted to psychiatric hospitals; once inside they acted normally yet none were detected, and discharges carried diagnoses like “schizophrenia in remission.” The study exposed the stickiness of psychiatric labels and unreliability of diagnosis, spurring reforms.

抑郁症的血清素失衡说法为什么受到系统性质疑？

Why has the serotonin imbalance account of depression been systematically challenged?

血清素假说源于早期药理观察：提升突触间血清素水平的药物能缓解症状，因此推断病因是血清素不足。一项覆盖多类证据的伞形综述汇总了血清素代谢物浓度、受体与转运体影像、色氨酸耗竭实验及基因关联研究，未能找到支持血清素水平或活性总体降低的一致证据。这并不意味着抗抑郁药无效，随机对照试验显示中重度抑郁患者可获得优于安慰剂的收益，但作用机制可能涉及神经可塑性与情绪加工偏向的改变，简单的化学失衡叙事已不足以描述病理。
The serotonin hypothesis grew from early pharmacology: drugs that raise synaptic serotonin relieve symptoms, so a serotonin deficit was inferred as the cause. An umbrella review pooling several evidence streams — serotonin metabolite levels, receptor and transporter imaging, tryptophan depletion studies, and genetic association work — found no consistent support for globally reduced serotonin levels or activity. This does not mean antidepressants are ineffective, since randomised trials show benefit over placebo in moderate to severe depression, but their mechanism likely involves neuroplasticity and shifts in emotional processing bias, and the simple chemical imbalance narrative no longer describes the pathology.

焦虑到底是大脑的哪个部分在“拉警报”？

Which part of the brain pulls the anxiety alarm?

焦虑的核心是杏仁核——大脑的威胁检测器，它能抢在前额叶皮层理性评估之前迅速启动警报（心跳加快、呼吸变浅、想逃跑）。前额叶负责“刹车”和重新评估，但它在警报下的工作效率会下降。如果杏仁核长期过度警觉（高估威胁、低估应对能力），就构成焦虑障碍的神经基础。好消息是，认知行为疗法和暴露训练能逐步降低这种过度警觉，这是可塑的。
The core of anxiety is the amygdala — the brain's threat detector — which fires the alarm (racing heart, shallow breathing, urge to flee) before the prefrontal cortex can rationally evaluate. The prefrontal cortex brakes and reappraises, but works worse under alarm. Chronic amygdala hypervigilance (overestimating threat, underestimating coping) underlies anxiety disorders. The hopeful part: CBT and exposure training measurably lower that hypervigilance — it is plastic.

精神分裂症的正负症状是如何区分的？

How are positive and negative symptoms of schizophrenia distinguished?

精神分裂症的正性症状是正常功能的“过度或扭曲”，如幻觉、妄想和思维紊乱；负性症状则是正常功能的“缺失或减退”，如情感淡漠、意志缺乏和社会退缩。这一区分由 Crow 等人提出，对预后判断与药物研发具有重要意义。
Positive symptoms are excesses or distortions of normal function—hallucinations, delusions, disorganized thinking—while negative symptoms are deficits—flat affect, avolition, social withdrawal. Crow and others proposed this distinction, which matters for prognosis and drug development.

暴露疗法起效的机制是消除恐惧记忆，还是建立新的抑制性学习？

Does exposure therapy work by erasing fear memories or by building new inhibitory learning?

早期习惯化解释认为反复暴露会逐渐削弱恐惧反应本身，但复发现象与情境依赖性提示原有记忆并未消失。克拉斯克等人提出抑制性学习模型：暴露过程建立了新的安全联结，与旧的威胁联结在提取时竞争，因此治疗效果取决于新记忆能否在多样情境中被优先提取。由此推出的优化策略包括制造预期违背而非单纯降低焦虑、跨情境与多线索练习、变化强度而非固定递增，以及减少安全行为对新学习的削弱。
Early habituation accounts assumed repeated exposure gradually weakens the fear response itself, but relapse and context-dependence indicate the original memory persists. Craske and colleagues proposed an inhibitory learning model in which exposure creates a new safety association that competes with the old threat association at retrieval, so outcomes depend on whether the new memory is retrieved preferentially across contexts. Practical implications follow: engineer violations of expectancy rather than merely lowering anxiety, practise across varied contexts and cues, vary intensity instead of strictly grading it upward, and curtail safety behaviours that undermine the new learning.

认知疗法中的“认知重构”是什么？

What is 'cognitive restructuring' in therapy?

认知重构是CBT中的技术，帮助患者识别自动化负性思维，并用更客观、均衡的想法替代它们，从而改变情绪与行为。它体现“想法影响感受”这一核心原理。
Cognitive restructuring is a CBT technique that helps clients identify automatic negative thoughts and replace them with more balanced, realistic ones, thereby changing emotion and behavior. It rests on the core idea that thoughts influence feelings.

What is borderline personality disorder?

边缘型人格障碍以情绪不稳定、强烈而不稳定的人际关系、自我形象混乱以及冲动和自伤行为为特征。它常伴随对被抛弃的极度恐惧，可通过辩证行为疗法等改善。
Borderline personality disorder features unstable mood, intense and unstable relationships, a disturbed self-image, and impulsivity or self-harm. It often involves intense fear of abandonment and can improve with dialectical behavior therapy.

为什么成瘾“戒断”那么难？

Why is addiction so hard to break?

成瘾与中脑边缘多巴胺系统密切相关：成瘾物质和过度行为让多巴胺异常大量释放，制造强烈的“想要”，并逐渐让奖赏系统对正常愉悦变得麻木，于是只有更强刺激才够“爽”。同时，线索（地点、人、画面）会唤醒条件化的渴求，而前额叶的抑制控制被削弱，形成恶性循环。成瘾是慢性脑疾病而非道德缺陷，科学的戒断需要药物、行为治疗和社会支持的组合，复发也是过程的一部分，不等于失败。
Addiction involves the mesolimbic dopamine system: substances and compulsive behaviors trigger abnormally large dopamine surges that create intense “wanting,” gradually numbing the reward system to ordinary pleasure — so stronger stimuli are needed to feel good. Meanwhile cues (places, people, images) evoke conditioned craving while prefrontal inhibitory control weakens — a vicious cycle. Addiction is a chronic brain disorder, not a moral failing; recovery combines medication, behavioral therapy, and social support, and relapse is part of the process, not failure.

不同流派的心理治疗效果为何常常相差不大？

Why do different schools of psychotherapy often show similar outcomes?

大量比较研究发现，规范实施的主流疗法在多数常见障碍上的疗效差异较小，这一现象被称为渡渡鸟裁决。共同因素模型认为，治疗联盟、来访者的期待与投入、治疗师提供的解释框架和情绪表达机会，解释了相当部分的疗效变异，其中治疗师之间的差异往往大于疗法之间的差异。反对意见指出，某些障碍确有特异性优势疗法，例如强迫症的暴露与反应阻断、恐惧症的暴露治疗，因此更稳妥的结论是共同因素设定了基线，特定技术在特定诊断上提供增量。
Large comparative literatures find that competently delivered mainstream therapies differ modestly in outcome for most common disorders, a pattern known as the dodo bird verdict. Common factors models attribute much of the variance to the therapeutic alliance, client expectations and engagement, and the provision of a coherent explanatory framework plus space for emotional expression, with differences between therapists frequently exceeding differences between treatments. Critics note genuine specificity for some conditions — exposure with response prevention for obsessive-compulsive disorder, exposure for phobias — so the safer conclusion is that common factors set the baseline while specific techniques add increments for particular diagnoses.

为什么抑郁时连记忆都偏向“坏事情”？

Why does depression tilt even memory toward the negative?

贝克提出抑郁的认知三角：对自己、世界和未来都持消极看法。抑郁时大脑对负性信息的注意、记忆和解读都会增强，正性信息反而被过滤，形成恶性循环——心情差更注意坏事，注意坏事心情更差。认知行为疗法就是通过识别、检验和改写这些自动负性思维来打断循环。抑郁不是“想开点”就能解决的意志问题，它是可以被科学理解的脑与认知状态。
Beck's cognitive triad: depression involves negative views of self, world, and future. Depressed brains show enhanced attention, memory, and interpretation of negative information while filtering positives — a vicious cycle where low mood amplifies noticing bad things and noticing bad things deepens low mood. CBT breaks this by identifying, testing, and revising automatic negative thoughts. 认知行为疗法的核心模型是什么？为什么它强调可检验的作业？

What is the core model of cognitive behavioural therapy, and why does it stress testable homework?

贝克提出的认知模型认为，情绪困扰并非直接由事件引起，而是经由自动化的负性想法与更深层的信念图式中介，例如把一次失败解读为我一无是处。治疗因此聚焦识别自动想法、检验其证据、发展更符合事实的替代解释，并配合行为激活、暴露与技能训练。强调家庭作业与行为实验的原因是，仅在会谈中的言语说服难以撼动长期形成的图式，只有让来访者在真实情境中收集反证，新的认知才会获得体验层面的支持。
Beck's cognitive model holds that distress is not produced directly by events but mediated by automatic negative thoughts and deeper belief schemas, as when one failure is read as proof of being worthless. Therapy therefore focuses on identifying automatic thoughts, examining the evidence for them, and developing more accurate alternatives, combined with behavioural activation, exposure, and skills training. Homework and behavioural experiments are central because verbal persuasion within sessions rarely shifts long-standing schemas; only gathering disconfirming evidence in real situations gives new appraisals experiential support.

为什么“直面恐惧”能减轻恐惧？

Why does facing fear directly reduce it?

这叫暴露疗法。恐惧常靠逃避维持：逃开一次，就少了一次“原来也没事”的学习机会。在安全的、循序渐进的暴露中，大脑慢慢把原本的恐惧记忆与新经验联系起来，恐惧反应随之降低。这个过程叫“消退”，是恐惧症、创伤后应激障碍等治疗的核心成分之一。
That's exposure therapy. Fear is maintained by avoidance: every escape skips a chance to learn “nothing bad happened.” Through safe, graded exposure, the brain links the old fear memory with new experience, and the fear response weakens. This process, called extinction, is a core component in treating phobias and PTSD.

为什么有人说“突然戒断”比“慢慢减量”更容易成功？

Why do some say quitting “cold turkey” succeeds more than cutting down gradually?

不少戒烟研究（包括系统综述）发现，“即刻全面停止”配合支持，成功率常高于慢慢减量——因为减量期间持续的烟草暴露让渴求和“再来一根”的机会一直存在。当然，结合替代疗法、动机访谈和防复发计划，成功率会进一步提升。哪种方式适合自己因人而异，关键是选一条并坚持，必要时请专业人士一起制定计划。
Many smoking-cessation studies (including systematic reviews) find abrupt quitting with support often beats gradual reduction — because tapering keeps exposure and craving alive, along with the chance for “just one more.” Adding nicotine replacement, motivational interviewing, and relapse-prevention plans raises success further. The best method varies by person; the key is choosing one, sticking with it, and building a plan with help if needed.

“认知行为疗法（CBT）”的基本原理是什么？

What is the basic principle of "cognitive behavioral therapy (CBT)"?

认知行为疗法由 Aaron Beck 在 1960 年代发展，认为情绪困扰源于扭曲的认知（如灾难化、非黑即白），通过识别和重构这些自动思维来改变情绪与行为。大量随机对照试验支持其对抑郁、焦虑等障碍的疗效。
Cognitive behavioral therapy, developed by Aaron Beck in the 1960s, holds that emotional distress stems from distorted thinking (e.g., catastrophizing, all-or-nothing), and that identifying and restructuring automatic thoughts changes emotion and behavior. Numerous RCTs support its efficacy for depression and anxiety.

What does psychological 'resilience' refer to?

心理韧性指个体在逆境、创伤或压力中恢复并适应的能力，并非不会受伤，而是能反弹与成长。它受社会支持、自我效能和应对方式影响，且可通过练习提升。

“正念”练习到底在练什么？

What exactly does mindfulness practice train?

正念是把注意力温和地带回当下，对此刻的念头和感受保持觉察而不评判。研究发现，长期练习可以降低压力反应、改善注意力和情绪调节，并作为抑郁复发预防的有效方法。它不需要“放空大脑”——注意到走神再轻轻拉回来，本身就是练习。
Mindfulness means gently returning attention to the present, noticing thoughts and feelings without judging them. Research shows regular practice lowers stress reactivity, improves attention and emotion regulation, and works as effective relapse prevention for depression. You don't need an empty mind — noticing you've wandered and gently returning is itself the practice.

为什么刷短视频、打游戏时会“停不下来”？

Why is it so hard to stop scrolling short videos or gaming?

这类行为会刺激多巴胺系统，但关键不是“快乐”而是“期待”——大脑在“可能得到奖励”时释放更多多巴胺，让人不断想“再看一个”“再来一局”。内容不可预测（下一条是什么不知道）时最抓人。这是正常的大脑机制，意识到“期待陷阱”，用定时提醒、物理隔离手机等边界方法，就能重新掌握主动。
These behaviors stimulate the dopamine system, but the driver isn't pleasure — it's anticipation. The brain releases more dopamine when reward might be coming, pushing “one more video,” “one more round.” Unpredictable content (you don't know what's next) is the most gripping. This is a normal brain mechanism; seeing the “anticipation trap” and setting boundaries — timers, physically separate the phone — restores your agency.

What is exposure therapy?

创伤后应激障碍（PTSD）的核心症状有哪些？

What are the core symptoms of post-traumatic stress disorder (PTSD)?

根据 DSM-5，PTSD 的核心症状包括侵入性再体验（闪回、噩梦）、对创伤线索的回避、认知与情绪的负面改变，以及过度警觉与易激惹。该病须由经历或目睹实际死亡、重伤或性暴力等创伤事件引发，症状持续超过一个月。
Per DSM-5, PTSD core symptoms include intrusive re-experiencing (flashbacks, nightmares), avoidance of trauma cues, negative alterations in cognition and mood, and hyperarousal/irritability. It requires exposure to a traumatic event (e.g., death, serious injury, sexual violence) with symptoms lasting over a month.
