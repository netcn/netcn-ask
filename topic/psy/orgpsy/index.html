领导风格的“变革型”与“交易型”有何不同？

How do transformational and transactional leadership differ?

交易型领导用奖惩换绩效（管当下），变革型领导以愿景与赋能激励成长（管方向）。后者常关联更高投入与创新，但需真诚而非表演式魅力。
Transactional leaders exchange rewards for performance (manage now); transformational leaders inspire growth via vision and empowerment (manage direction). The latter links to higher engagement and innovation, but needs sincerity, not showy charisma.

根据工作要求-资源模型，为什么高强度的工作量并不必然导致职业倦怠？

According to the job demands-resources model, why does a heavy workload not necessarily lead to burnout?

该模型提出两条相对独立的路径：持续的高工作要求（如超负荷、时间压力）会耗竭身心能量，经由健康损耗过程催生倦怠；自主性、反馈、上级支持等工作资源则经动机激发过程提升投入，并能缓冲高要求的伤害。倦怠与否取决于要求与资源的动态平衡，资源充足时同样的高负荷未必致倦。
The model posits two independent processes. Sustained demands such as overload and time pressure erode energy through a health-impairment pathway, producing exhaustion. Resources like autonomy, feedback, and supervisor support fuel engagement via a motivational pathway and buffer the impact of high demands. Whether heavy workload breeds burnout therefore depends on the dynamic balance of demands and resources, not on workload alone.

什么是“心理安全感”——团队为何敢说“我错了”？

What is psychological safety—why can teams say 'I was wrong'?

团队心理安全感指成员相信冒险发言、认错不会受罚。它预测学习、创新与安全感，由领导示范谦逊与对失误的良性回应所塑造。
Team psychological safety is the shared belief that speaking up or admitting error is safe. It predicts learning, innovation and safety, built when leaders model humility and respond well to failure.

“工作满意度”的主要来源有哪些？钱是唯一吗？

What drives job satisfaction—is money the only thing?

研究显示自主、胜任、关系与发展空间常比薪水更能持久提升满意度（自我决定理论）。钱到基本线后边际效应递减，意义与掌控更关键。
Research shows autonomy, competence, relatedness and growth often sustain satisfaction more than pay (self-determination theory). Beyond a baseline, money's marginal return drops; meaning and control matter more.

什么是“目标设定理论”——好目标长什么样？

What is goal-setting theory—what makes a good goal?

明确且有挑战的目标（SMART 类）比“尽力而为”更能提升绩效，因聚焦与反馈。但目标过难或唯一量化会诱发作弊与忽视未量化的重要事。
Specific, challenging goals (SMART-like) beat 'do your best' via focus and feedback. Yet overly hard or solely quantified goals invite cheating and neglect of unmeasured essentials.

什么是“职业倦怠”（burnout）的三维度？

What are the three dimensions of burnout?

倦怠含情绪耗竭（累）、去人格化（对人对事冷漠）、个人成就感降低。它来自长期资源（控制、支持）被过度索取，非单纯“懒”，需调结构而非只劝坚强。
Burnout has three parts: emotional exhaustion, cynicism/depersonalization, and reduced personal accomplishment. It stems from chronically drained resources (control, support), not mere laziness—fix structure, not just willpower.

根据工作特征模型，一份工作为什么能让人干劲十足？哪些特征决定它的激励潜能？

According to the job characteristics model, which features of a job determine its motivational potential?

Hackman 与 Oldham 提出，技能多样性、任务完整性、任务重要性、自主性与反馈五项核心特征共同决定工作的激励潜能：前三项让员工体验到工作的意义，自主性带来责任感，反馈帮助了解绩效结果。对成长需求较强的员工，这些特征经由关键心理状态提升内在动机、工作满意度与绩效表现；将五项特征加权合成即得到激励潜能分数。
Hackman and Oldham proposed five core dimensions—skill variety, task identity, task significance, autonomy, and feedback—that jointly determine a job's motivational potential. The first three let workers experience the work as meaningful, autonomy creates a sense of responsibility, and feedback tells them how well they are doing. For employees with strong growth needs, these dimensions raise intrinsic motivation, job satisfaction, and performance through critical psychological states; weighting and combining the five features yields a motivating potential score.

为什么“固定工位 vs 开放办公”会影响心理？

Why does fixed vs open office affect the mind?

开放办公促协作却增噪音、降低隐私与专注，研究常报告满意度与专注下降；可预测的控制权（能否选择安静处）是关键调节。
Open offices aid collaboration but raise noise and cut privacy and focus; studies often report lower satisfaction and concentration. The key moderator is perceived control (choice of quiet space).
