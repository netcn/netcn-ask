激活—整合假说如何解释梦的产生过程?它与威胁模拟理论的根本分歧是什么?

How does the activation-synthesis hypothesis account for the generation of dreams, and where does it fundamentally diverge from the threat simulation theory?

Hobson与McCarley在1977年提出,梦起源于REM睡眠期间脑桥的自发放电,这些信号上行激活皮层后由前脑加以整合,编织出看似连贯的叙事,因此梦本身不承载专门设计的功能意义。Revonsuo在2000年提出的威胁模拟理论则主张,梦在进化中承担了模拟危险情境的训练功能,让人能在无真实风险的环境中演练觉察与逃避反应。二者的根本分歧在于梦究竟只是神经活动的副产品,还是具有适应价值的认知功能。
Hobson and McCarley proposed in 1977 that dreams originate in spontaneous pontine firing during REM sleep; these signals ascend to activate the cortex and are then synthesized by the forebrain into seemingly coherent narratives, so dreams carry no purpose-built functional meaning. The threat simulation theory advanced by Revonsuo in 2000 instead claims that dreaming evolved to rehearse dangerous situations, allowing individuals to practice detection and avoidance responses without real risk. Their fundamental disagreement is whether dreams are merely by-products of neural activity or cognitive functions with adaptive value.

为什么人会做噩梦？

Why do people have nightmares?

白天积累的压力与焦虑会在睡眠中被激活，尤其在快速眼动（REM）睡眠期，形成恐惧梦境；创伤经历（如PTSD）、某些药物与饮酒也可能诱发噩梦。
Daytime stress and anxiety can resurface during sleep, especially in REM sleep, as frightening dreams; trauma (such as PTSD), certain medications, and alcohol can also trigger nightmares.

为什么我们在梦里能清楚地“看见”画面，醒来后却几乎记不住梦的内容？

Why do we vividly "see" images in our dreams, yet can barely recall them after waking?

梦境的视觉体验来自快速眼动睡眠期间视觉皮层被内部信号自上而下激活，此时外界输入已被脑干阻断，大脑便用内生信号生成画面。醒来后记不住，是因为该阶段背外侧前额叶活动受到抑制，工作记忆与情景编码能力大幅下降；同时较低的去甲肾上腺素水平也不利于把梦固化成长期记忆。所以梦通常在醒后几分钟内迅速消退。
Dream imagery arises when the visual cortex is activated top-down by internal signals during REM sleep, after external input has been gated off by the brainstem. Recall fails because dorsolateral prefrontal activity is suppressed at this stage, crippling working memory and episodic encoding, while low noradrenaline levels hinder consolidating dreams into long-term memory. That is why dreams typically dissolve within minutes of waking.
