什么是“镜像自我”与自我概念发展？

What are the 'looking-glass self' and the development of self-concept?

库利提出“镜像自我”，认为我们通过想象别人如何看待自己来形成自我概念。儿童的自我概念从具体外在（年龄、喜好）逐渐发展到抽象内在（品格、价值观）。同伴反馈在青春期对自我概念影响尤其大。
Cooley proposed the 'looking-glass self', that we form self-concept by imagining how others see us. Children's self-concept moves from concrete external traits (age, likes) to abstract internal ones (character, values). Peer feedback strongly shapes self-concept during adolescence.

What levels are in Kohlberg's theory of moral development?

什么是依恋理论？

What is attachment theory?

鲍尔比提出，婴儿与主要照料者形成的情感联结叫依恋，安斯沃思通过“陌生情境”实验区分出安全型、焦虑-矛盾型和回避型等。安全的依恋有助于儿童探索世界并建立健康的人际关系。依恋模式在成年亲密关系中也会体现。
Bowlby proposed that the emotional bond an infant forms with a primary caregiver is called attachment; Ainsworth's 'Strange Situation' identified secure, anxious-ambivalent, and avoidant types. Secure attachment helps children explore the world and build healthy relationships. Attachment patterns also appear in adult intimacy.

Kohlberg proposed three levels and six stages of moral reasoning—preconventional (avoid punishment, self-interest), conventional (please others, uphold rules), and postconventional (social contract, universal principles). It traces moral reasoning from external sanctions to internal principles, though Gilligan criticized its male-biased samples.

How does Marcia chart divergent paths of adolescent identity development along exploration and commitment, and which transition trajectory does longitudinal evidence favor?

以是否经历主动探索、是否建立稳定承诺交叉分类,可得到四种状态:兼具两者者为同一性获得,有危机而无定论者为延缓,未加探索就接受外界安排者为早闭,两者皆无者为弥散。Meeus 等人的多年追踪显示,多数转变经由延缓走向获得,说明探索性危机更像建设性阶段而非单纯动荡。
Crossing active exploration with firm commitment yields four statuses: identity achievement pairs both, moratorium carries crisis without resolution, foreclosure accepts external arrangements without searching, and diffusion shows neither. Multi-year tracking by Meeus and colleagues finds most transitions running from diffusion or foreclosure through moratorium into achievement, casting exploratory crisis as a constructive phase rather than mere turbulence.

紊乱型依恋(D 型)依据哪些行为特征来识别?它为何与令人恐惧或受惊的养育方式紧密相连?

Which behavioral markers identify disorganized (Type D) attachment, and why is it closely tied to frightened-or-frightening caregiving?

梅因与所罗门借助逐帧分析发现,紊乱型婴儿呈现策略崩解的迹象:接近照料者时突然转身、僵住,或同时出现趋近与回避的矛盾动作序列。元分析证实其最强的预测因素是父母对孩子构成恐吓,或父母自身显露出受惊状态——当本应充当安全港湾的人同时也是威胁来源时,婴儿无法组织出一致的应对策略。这类儿童日后出现解离症状与外化行为问题的风险显著升高。
Through freeze-frame analysis, Main and Solomon found that disorganized infants show signs of strategic breakdown: approaching the caregiver while suddenly turning away, freezing, or emitting contradictory sequences of simultaneous approach and avoidance. Meta-analyses confirm the strongest predictor is caregiving that frightens the child or reveals the parent herself alarmed—when the presumed safe haven is also the source of threat, no coherent strategy can be organized. These children face markedly elevated later risks of dissociation and externalizing problems.

什么是发展心理学中的“关键期”？

What is a critical period in development?

关键期是某种能力或行为最容易习得、且错过便难以补偿的特定发育窗口，例如视觉和语言发展的早期阶段。但许多能力也存在可塑性较强的“敏感期”。
A critical period is a specific developmental window when a capacity or behavior is most easily acquired and missed opportunities are hard to compensate, such as early vision and language development. Many abilities instead have a more plastic 'sensitive period.'

Harlow 的恒河猴实验对理解“依恋”有什么关键发现？

What key finding did Harlow's rhesus monkey experiments contribute to attachment?

Harlow 于 1958 年让幼猴在“铁丝母亲”（带奶瓶）与“绒布母亲”（无奶瓶）间选择，发现幼猴更依恋柔软的绒布母亲，说明依恋的基础是“接触安慰”而非单纯的食物满足。这一结果反驳了当时以喂养解释依恋的行为主义观点。
In 1958 Harlow offered infant monkeys a wire “mother” with a bottle versus a soft cloth “mother” without one, and found they clung to the cloth mother, showing that attachment rests on “contact comfort” rather than mere feeding. This refuted the behaviorist view that attachment derives from food provision.

埃里克森的心理社会发展有哪八个阶段？

What are Erikson's eight stages of psychosocial development?

埃里克森认为人一生要经历八个危机阶段，从婴儿期的“信任对不信任”到老年的“自我整合对绝望”。每个阶段都要平衡一对矛盾，顺利解决才能形成健康人格。
Erikson held that people pass through eight crisis stages, from 'trust vs. mistrust' in infancy to 'integrity vs. despair' in old age. Each stage balances a pair of opposites, and resolving it well fosters a healthy personality.

什么是“最近发展区”？

What is the 'zone of proximal development'?

维果茨基提出，最近发展区是孩子“在帮助下能做到、单独还做不到”的任务区间。成人和同伴的恰当引导（脚手架）能帮孩子跨越这个区。这提示教学应略高于孩子当前水平。
Vygotsky described the zone of proximal development as tasks a child can do with help but not yet alone. Proper guidance (scaffolding) from adults or peers helps the child cross this zone. It suggests teaching should be slightly above the child's current level.

皮亚杰的认知发展理论包含哪几个阶段？

What stages does Piaget's cognitive development theory include?

皮亚杰提出儿童认知发展经历四个阶段：感知运动期（0–2 岁，获客体恒常性）、前运算期（2–7 岁，自我中心）、具体运算期（7–11 岁，掌握守恒）和形式运算期（11 岁以上，抽象推理）。该理论奠定了发展心理学的建构主义基础。
Piaget proposed four stages of cognitive development: sensorimotor (0–2, object permanence), preoperational (2–7, egocentrism), concrete operational (7–11, conservation), and formal operational (11+, abstract reasoning). The theory founded the constructivist basis of developmental psychology.

青春期的脑发生了什么变化？

What changes happen in the brain during adolescence?

青春期时，负责情绪和奖励的边缘系统先成熟，而负责自控的前额叶皮层要到二十多岁才完全发育。这种“不平衡”解释了青少年为何更容易冲动、爱冒险。充足的睡眼和稳定支持能帮助平稳度过。
In adolescence, the emotion- and reward-related limbic system matures first, while the self-control prefrontal cortex is not fully developed until the twenties. This 'imbalance' explains why teens are more impulsive and risk-taking. Enough sleep and steady support help them through it.

发展心理学中的“心理理论（theory of mind）”指什么？

What is "theory of mind" in developmental psychology?

心理理论是个体理解他人拥有与自己不同的信念、愿望和意图的能力。Wimmer 与 Perner 于 1983 年用“错误信念任务”发现，儿童约在 4 岁左右才具备此能力，这被认为是社会认知发展的关键里程碑。
Theory of mind is the ability to attribute to others beliefs, desires, and intentions different from one's own. Wimmer and Perner's 1983 false-belief task showed children typically acquire this around age 4, a key milestone in social-cognitive development.

Is there a critical period for language acquisition?

什么是“关键期”？

What is a 'critical period'?

关键期是指个体在某段时间里特别容易习得某种能力（如语言），一旦错过就难以达到最佳水平。例如幼年缺乏语言刺激可能影响终身语言发展。但许多能力也有“敏感期”，只是没那么绝对。
A critical period is a window when an individual is especially ready to acquire a skill, such as language, and missing it makes reaching the best level difficult. For example, lack of language exposure in early childhood can affect lifelong language development. Many skills have 'sensitive periods' that are less absolute.

Bowlby 与 Ainsworth 的“依恋理论”如何描述婴儿与照料者的关系？

How do Bowlby and Ainsworth's “attachment theory” describe infant–caregiver bonds?

依恋理论由 Bowlby 提出、Ainsworth 用“陌生情境”实验发展，认为婴儿与主要照料者形成的安全或不安全的情感联结影响其后社会关系。Ainsworth 区分了安全型、回避型与矛盾/抗拒型，后来 Main 又补充了紊乱型。
Attachment theory, proposed by Bowlby and extended by Ainsworth's “Strange Situation” experiments, holds that the emotional bond infants form with a primary caregiver shapes later social relationships. Ainsworth identified secure, avoidant, and ambivalent/resistant patterns, later supplemented by Main's disorganized type.

不少青少年独自一人时能冷静权衡风险,同伴一到场就转而做出鲁莽决定——神经发展模型如何解释这种落差?

斯坦伯格的双系统模型指出,青春期早中期负责奖赏加工的社会情绪系统会在同伴情境下被强烈激活,而前额叶的认知控制系统要等到二十岁出头才发育成熟,两个系统的不同步造成了“油门先于刹车”的失衡。驾驶模拟等实验显示,同伴在场会放大奖赏敏感性,而独处时这种失衡相当轻微,因此冒险高峰集中于同伴在场的高唤起情境。该模型把青少年冒险从“无知或纯粹冲动”重构为特定情境下的调节失灵。
Steinberg's dual systems model holds that the socioemotional system processing reward is strongly activated by peer contexts in early-to-mid adolescence, whereas the prefrontal cognitive control system matures only into the early twenties, producing an imbalance in which the accelerator arrives before the brakes. Driving-simulation experiments show that peer presence amplifies reward sensitivity while the imbalance stays slight when adolescents are alone, so risk-taking peaks in arousing peer-present settings. The model thereby reframes adolescent risk-taking as context-specific regulatory failure rather than sheer ignorance or impulsivity.

加工速度理论如何解释成年期认知能力出现的年龄差异？

How does the processing-speed theory explain age-related differences in cognition across adulthood?

该理论认为，随年龄增长的中枢神经系统传导减慢是认知老化的基础机制。加工速度下降会限制单位时间内可完成的认知操作数量，并削弱同时保持多份信息的能力，从而间接拉低推理、工作记忆等复杂任务的成绩。大量研究发现速度指标能中介大部分年龄差异，提示干预应注重效率训练与健康生活方式，而非仅针对单一任务反复练习。
Processing-speed theory holds that age-related slowing in the central nervous system is a foundational mechanism of cognitive aging. Slower processing limits how many mental operations can be completed in a given time and weakens the capacity to keep several pieces of information concurrently available, indirectly lowering performance on complex tasks such as reasoning and working memory. Because speed measures mediate much of the age variance in large studies, interventions should target processing efficiency and healthy lifestyles rather than rote repetition of single tasks.
