什么是变应性负荷（allostatic load）？它如何量化长期压力伤害？

What is allostatic load and how does it quantify long-term stress damage?

McEwen 提出，反复或慢性应激导致身体调节系统（HPA、心血管、代谢、免疫）反复失衡，磨损累积成多系统病理风险。它用血压、皮质醇、炎症等生物指标综合打分，把压力伤身变成可测量概念。
McEwen coined allostatic load: repeated/chronic stress makes regulatory systems (HPA, cardiovascular, metabolic, immune) repeatedly imbalance, accumulating wear into multisystem disease risk. It scores biomarkers like blood pressure, cortisol, and inflammation—making stress damages the body measurable.

什么是创伤后成长？痛苦之后为何有人反而更强大？

What is post-traumatic growth and why do some grow stronger after pain?

Tedeschi 与 Calhoun 提出，部分人在经历危机后报告人际关系更紧密、人生优先级更清晰、个人力量感增强等正向变化。成长不与痛苦抵消，而是通过对意义的重新建构与主动应对，在裂痕中长出新视角。
Tedeschi and Calhoun found some report positive change after crisis—closer relationships, clearer priorities, stronger personal strength. Growth does not cancel pain; through meaning reconstruction and active coping, new perspectives grow from the rupture.

Lazarus 的认知评价理论如何定义压力？

How does Lazarus's cognitive appraisal theory define stress?

Lazarus 认为压力不在事件本身，而在一手的初级评价（这事对我有威胁吗？）与次级评价（我能应付吗？）。同一事件因评价不同可为挑战或威胁，说明认知重构是减压关键。
Lazarus argued stress is not in the event but in appraisal: primary (is this a threat?) and secondary (can I cope?). The same event becomes challenge or threat by appraisal—so cognitive reappraisal is key to reducing stress.

什么是应对策略与情境可控性的“匹配”？它为何意味着问题焦点应对并不总是最有效？

What is the 'goodness of fit' between a coping strategy and situational controllability, and why does problem-focused coping not always win?

匹配假说主张，应对成效取决于策略与情境可控性的契合度。Folkman 与 Lazarus 发现：压力源可控时，问题焦点应对预示更好的适应；压力源不可控时，执意解决问题容易受挫，接纳、认知重评等情绪焦点方式反而伴随更少症状。Vitaliano 等的实证检验同样显示，应对与可控性错配者抑郁症状显著更多——关键不在哪种应对最好，而在何时用哪种应对。
The goodness-of-fit hypothesis holds that a strategy succeeds when it matches how controllable the stressor is. Folkman and Lazarus found that problem-focused coping predicted better adjustment under controllable stressors, whereas with uncontrollable ones stubborn problem-solving bred frustration while acceptance and cognitive reappraisal went with fewer symptoms. Empirical tests by Vitaliano and colleagues likewise showed markedly more depressive symptoms when coping and controllability mismatched—the question shifts from which coping is best to which coping fits when.

压力对记忆到底是好是坏？

Is stress good or bad for memory?

取决于时序与强度：急性适度压力可增强与生存相关事件的编码（肾上腺素助记忆）；但慢性或回忆时高压会损害海马依赖的精确回忆，并偏向记住负面情绪。即急性助、慢性伤。
It depends on timing and intensity: acute moderate stress can enhance encoding of survival-relevant events (adrenaline aids memory); but chronic stress or high stress at recall impairs hippocampus-dependent precise recall and biases toward negative material. Acute helps, chronic hurts.

Selye 的一般适应综合征分哪几个阶段？

What stages make up Selye's general adaptation syndrome?

Selye 将应激反应分为三阶段：警觉期（快速动员）、抵抗期（身体适应并维持高警备）、耗竭期（资源耗尽、机能崩溃）。它说明压力若持续超过承受，最终会损害健康。
Selye's GAS has three stages: alarm (rapid mobilization), resistance (adapting and staying on high alert), and exhaustion (resources depleted, breakdown). It shows that unrelenting stress beyond capacity eventually harms health.

什么是心理韧性？它可以被培养吗？

What is psychological resilience and can it be built?

韧性不是不受伤，而是在逆境中恢复并适应的能力，受基因、支持系统与应对方式共同影响。它可通过建立社会连接、认知重评、规律睡眠运动等被训练提升，并非固定天赋。
Resilience is not never hurt but recovering and adapting after adversity, shaped by genes, support, and coping. It is trainable via social connection, reappraisal, and regular sleep/exercise—not a fixed gift.

为什么长期压力比短期压力更伤身？

Why is chronic stress more harmful than short-term stress?

短期压力是适应性动员；长期压力使皮质醇持续偏高，抑制免疫、损害海马记忆与心血管，并扰乱代谢。身体本为偶尔应激设计，持续高水平会进入耗损状态。
Short-term stress is adaptive mobilization; chronic stress keeps cortisol high, suppressing immunity, harming hippocampal memory and the cardiovascular system, and disrupting metabolism. Bodies evolved for occasional stress; sustained high levels lead to wear.

在拉扎勒斯的应激交互作用模型中，“初级评价”和“次级评价”分别指什么？

In Lazarus's transactional model of stress, what do “primary appraisal” and “secondary appraisal” respectively refer to?

该模型认为应激不是事件固有的属性，而是源于个体对环境要求与自身资源匹配关系的动态评估。初级评价判断某事件是否构成伤害、威胁或挑战；次级评价则盘点自己可用于应对的资源与行动选项。当要求被判定超出可用资源时，人才体验到应激，因此同一事件会在不同个体身上引发不同反应。这一框架也奠定了问题焦点应对与情绪焦点应对的经典区分。
In this model, stress is not an inherent property of events; it emerges from a person's dynamic evaluation of the fit between environmental demands and personal resources. Primary appraisal judges whether an event threatens harm or instead presents a challenge; secondary appraisal takes stock of the resources and options available for coping. Stress is experienced when demands are judged to exceed available resources, which is why the same event can provoke different reactions in different people. This framework also grounded the classic distinction between problem-focused and emotion-focused coping.

为什么考试前会紧张？紧张一定有害吗？

Why do we get nervous before exams, and is nervousness always bad?

紧张是身体为重要任务动员能量的正常反应（交感激活）。适度紧张提升专注与表现（耶克斯-多德森定律）；只有过度时才拖累发挥，所以关键是把紧张控制在最佳唤醒区。
Nervousness is the body mobilizing energy for an important task (sympathetic activation). Moderate nerves boost focus and performance (Yerkes-Dodson law); only excess hurts. The key is keeping arousal in the optimal zone.

为什么深呼吸能让人平静下来？

Why does deep breathing calm us down?

缓慢深呼吸激活副交感神经系统，降低心率与皮质醇，打断交感过度唤醒的循环。它是少数能直接自上而下调节自主神经的反应之一，见效快且无需工具。
Slow deep breathing activates the parasympathetic system, lowering heart rate and cortisol and breaking the sympathetic over-arousal loop. It is one of the few ways to directly, top-down regulate the autonomic nervous system—fast and tool-free.

什么是战斗或逃跑反应？

What is the fight-or-flight response?

面对威胁时，交感神经系统与肾上腺素让心跳加快、肌肉供血增加、注意力聚焦，为应对或逃离做准备。这是进化的生存机制，短期有用，长期频繁激活则伤身。
Facing threat, the sympathetic nervous system and adrenaline speed the heart, send blood to muscles, and narrow attention to prepare fight or flight. An evolutionary survival mechanism—useful short-term, harmful if chronically activated.
