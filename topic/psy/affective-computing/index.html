“微表情识别”真的能测谎吗？

Can 'micro-expression recognition' really detect lies?

微表情是极短的不随意面部抽动，被一些训练宣称能暴露谎言，但严格研究未发现可靠测谎效果。表情与内在情绪并非一一对应，依赖微表情判断诚实既不科学也易误伤。
Micro-expressions are very brief involuntary facial movements touted to expose lies, but rigorous studies find no reliable lie-detection effect. Expressions do not map one-to-one to inner emotion, so judging honesty by them is unscientific and error-prone.

“共情型 AI”真的能理解你的感受吗？

Can 'empathetic AI' truly understand how you feel?

共情型 AI 能基于语言与语调匹配恰当回应、提供陪伴与情绪支持，但它没有真实感受，只是对线索的模式拟合。它可辅助疏导，却不能替代人际共情与专业帮助。
Empathetic AI can match apt responses from language and tone, offering companionship and support, but it has no real feelings—only pattern fitting to cues. It can assist soothing yet cannot replace human empathy or professional help.

计算机如何“识别”人的情绪？

How do computers 'recognize' human emotion?

情感计算综合面部动作编码、语音韵律、生理信号与文本语气，用机器学习把特征映射到情绪类别或维度（效价-唤醒）。它常用于教育、客服与健康监测，但跨文化与个体校准仍是难点。
Affective computing fuses facial action coding, vocal prosody, physiological signals and text tone, using machine learning to map features to emotion categories or dimensions (valence-arousal). Used in education, service and health monitoring, though cross-cultural and individual calibration remain hard.

语音里的情绪是怎么被机器听出来的？

How do machines hear emotion in voice?

机器从音高、语速、能量、颤动等声学特征提取情绪线索，再用分类器判断愤怒、快乐、中性等。背景噪声、语种与个体声线会显著影响准确率，因此常需结合文本与语境。
Machines extract emotional cues from pitch, rate, energy and jitter, then classify anger, happiness, neutrality and so on. Background noise, language and individual voice strongly affect accuracy, so text and context are often added.
