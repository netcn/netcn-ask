荣格提出的“集体无意识”是什么？

What is Jung's 'collective unconscious'?

荣格认为除个人无意识外，人类还共享一套遗传而来的“集体无意识”，内含原型等普遍意象。它试图解释跨文化神话与梦境中的相似主题。
Jung argued that beyond the personal unconscious, humans share an inherited 'collective unconscious' containing universal images called archetypes. It aims to explain similar themes across cultures' myths and dreams.

自我决定论（Self-Determination Theory）认为哪些基本心理需求驱动动机？

Which basic psychological needs does Self-Determination Theory say drive motivation?

自我决定论由 Deci 与 Ryan 提出，认为自主性、胜任感与关系感是人类三种普遍的基本心理需求，满足它们能促进内在动机与心理健康。该理论自 1985 年提出后广泛应用于教育、运动与组织领域。
Self-Determination Theory, proposed by Deci and Ryan, holds that autonomy, competence, and relatedness are three universal basic psychological needs whose satisfaction fosters intrinsic motivation and well-being. Since 1985 it has been widely applied in education, sport, and organizations.

人格心理学中的“大五人格（Big Five）”包含哪些维度？

What dimensions make up the "Big Five" personality traits?

大五人格模型用五个广泛维度描述人格：开放性、尽责性、外向性、宜人性与神经质（OCEAN）。Costa 与 McCrae 在 1980 年代通过 NEO-PI 系列量表系统确立该模型，其修订版 NEO-PI-R 于 1992 年发布，该模型在跨文化研究中表现出良好的稳定性。
The Big Five model describes personality along five broad dimensions: Openness, Conscientiousness, Extraversion, Agreeableness, and Neuroticism (OCEAN). Costa and McCrae established the model through the NEO-PI series of inventories in the 1980s, with the revised NEO-PI-R published in 1992, and it shows robust cross-cultural stability.

Rotter 的“控制点（locus of control）”指什么？

What does Rotter's "locus of control" refer to?

控制点指个体对自己生活事件成败原因的归因倾向：内控者认为结果取决于自身努力与能力，外控者则归因于运气、命运或他人。Rotter 于 1966 年提出该概念，并编制量表加以测量，是人格与动机研究的重要变量。
Locus of control is the extent to which people attribute life outcomes to themselves versus external forces: internals credit their own effort and ability, externals credit luck, fate, or others. Rotter introduced it in 1966 with a measuring scale, a key variable in personality and motivation research.

什么是班杜拉的“自我效能感”？

What is Bandura's self-efficacy?

自我效能感是个体对自己能否完成某任务的信念。高自我效能的人更愿挑战困难、坚持更久，它受成败经验、榜样、言语劝说和情绪状态影响。
Self-efficacy is a person's belief in their ability to accomplish a task. Those with high self-efficacy take on harder challenges and persist longer; it is shaped by mastery experiences, models, persuasion, and emotional states.

什么是“A型人格”？

What is Type A personality?

A型人格由弗里德曼和罗森曼提出，特征是竞争性强、急迫、敌意和时间紧迫感。早期研究将其与冠心病风险相联系，但后来的证据更谨慎。
Type A personality, described by Friedman and Rosenman, features competitiveness, urgency, hostility, and time pressure. Early research linked it to coronary heart disease risk, though later evidence is more cautious.

弗洛伊德精神分析中的“心理防御机制”有哪些常见类型？

What are common types of "defense mechanisms" in Freudian psychoanalysis?

心理防御机制是自我用来无意识地减少焦虑的方式，常见类型包括压抑、投射、合理化、否认与升华等。弗洛伊德在 19 世纪末提出这一概念，其女儿 Anna Freud 于 1936 年系统整理成《自我与防御机制》。
Defense mechanisms are unconscious strategies the ego uses to reduce anxiety, including repression, projection, rationalization, denial, and sublimation. Sigmund Freud introduced the concept in the late 19th century, and Anna Freud systematically cataloged them in The Ego and the Mechanisms of Defence (1936).

罗杰斯的人本主义人格观强调什么？

What does Rogers's humanistic view of personality emphasize?

罗杰斯强调“自我概念”与“无条件积极关注”，认为人天生有向善和自我实现的倾向。心理困扰常源于真实自我与理想自我之间的差距过大。
Rogers emphasized the 'self-concept' and 'unconditional positive regard,' believing people are naturally inclined toward growth and self-actualization. Distress often stems from too large a gap between the real and ideal self.

人格研究中“特质（trait）”与“状态（state）”有何区别？

What is the difference between "trait" and "state" in personality research?

特质是相对稳定、跨情境一致的人格倾向（如一个人通常是否焦虑），而状态是短暂、随情境波动的心理体验（如某次考试前的紧张）。这一区分由 Cattell 等人在因素分析法研究人格结构时确立，是人格测量的基础。
A trait is a relatively stable, cross-situational disposition (e.g., whether someone is generally anxious), whereas a state is a transient, situation-bound experience (e.g., nervousness before one exam). Cattell and others established this distinction through factor-analytic studies of personality structure.

描述人的词语成千上万,为什么最后只剩五个维度说了算?

Thousands of trait words exist in dictionaries — why do just five dimensions end up doing the work?

思路源于词汇学假设:凡重要到被反复用来形容人的差异,都值得收进科学目录。研究者从英语词典近两万个人格描述词出发层层筛选,再经互评与因素分析压缩,不同团队反复得到同样五个因子:开放性、尽责性、外向性、宜人性、神经质(OCEAN);跨语言重复验证使其成为人格测量的事实标准。大五是维度而非类型——每个人都是五个刻度上的连续坐标;它与绩效、健康行为的关联稳定,但相关不等于命运,测量预测的是倾向而非剧本。
The trick was lexical: any individual difference important enough gets encoded as words — the lexical hypothesis. Researchers distilled over ten thousand trait adjectives from dictionaries, had peers rate each other, and factor-analyzed; different labs kept landing on five factors — openness, conscientiousness, extraversion, agreeableness, neuroticism (OCEAN). Cross-language replication made Big Five the de facto measurement standard. They are dimensions, not types: everyone is continuous coordinates on five scales; statistical ties to job performance and health are robust, but correlation is not destiny — traits predict tendencies, not scripts.

弗洛伊德说的“本我、自我、超我”分别是什么？

What are the id, ego, and superego in Freud's theory?

本我追求本能快乐、遵循即时满足，超我代表道德理想，自我在两者间协调并面对现实。健康的人格需要三者相对平衡。
The id seeks instinctual pleasure on the principle of immediate gratification, the superego represents moral ideals, and the ego mediates between them while dealing with reality. A healthy personality needs relative balance among the three.

马斯洛需求层次金字塔从下到上是什么？

What is Maslow's hierarchy of needs from bottom to top?

从底层到顶层依次是：生理需求、安全需求、归属与爱的需求、尊重需求、自我实现需求。低层满足后，人会更追求高层的精神成长。
From bottom to top: physiological, safety, love and belonging, esteem, and self-actualization needs. Once lower needs are met, people pursue higher psychological growth.
