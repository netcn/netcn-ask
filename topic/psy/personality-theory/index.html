荣格的“原型”与“集体无意识”指什么？

What do Jung's archetypes and collective unconscious mean?

荣格认为除个人无意识外，人类共享一层“集体无意识”，其中沉淀着跨文化的普遍意象，即原型，如母亲、英雄、阴影。原型通过梦、神话和象征显现，影响性格与意义感。该概念偏向人文解释，缺乏可证伪的实证检验。
Jung held that beyond personal unconscious, humans share a collective unconscious holding universal cross-cultural images called archetypes, such as mother, hero, and shadow. Archetypes appear in dreams, myths, and symbols, shaping character and meaning. The idea is humanistic and lacks easily falsifiable tests.

弗洛伊德的本我、自我、超我分别是什么？

What are Freud's id, ego, and superego?

弗洛伊德的人格结构分三层：本我追求本能快乐（快乐原则），超我代表道德理想，自我在现实约束下调和二者。健康在于三者动态平衡，冲突过强会产生焦虑与防御。该模型是精神分析的核心比喻。
Freud's structure has three layers: the id seeks instinctual pleasure (pleasure principle), the superego represents moral ideals, and the ego mediates under reality. Health is their dynamic balance; strong conflict brings anxiety and defense. The model is core to psychoanalysis.
