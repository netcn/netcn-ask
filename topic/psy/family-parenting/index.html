隔代抚养对儿童发展有什么影响？

What effects does grandparental rearing have on child development?

隔代抚养指祖辈承担主要养育角色，其影响取决于质量而非形式：温暖稳定的隔代照料可成为支持资源，但代际教养观念冲突或资源不足可能增加风险。研究多显示与依恋和学业结果无必然好坏，关键在关系质量与亲子联结是否维持。它是特定社会文化下的多样安排。
Grandparental rearing means grandparents take the main caregiving role; its effect depends on quality, not the form. Warm stable care can be a support, yet conflicts in parenting beliefs or scarce resources raise risk. Research shows no fixed good or bad link to attachment or school outcomes; what matters is relationship quality and kept parent bonds. It is a varied arrangement across cultures.

什么是家庭中的“代际传递”？

What is intergenerational transmission in families?

代际传递指教养方式、依恋模式或应对风格从上一代延续到下一代，比如曾被严厉对待的父母更可能延续严厉教养。它既可通过模仿学习，也通过内化工作模型实现。意识到传递链条有助于主动打断不利循环。
Intergenerational transmission is that parenting styles, attachment patterns, or coping styles carry from one generation to the next, such as harshly raised parents tending to repeat harsh parenting. It happens through modeling and internalized working models. Seeing the chain helps deliberately break harmful cycles.

权威型教养方式的特点是什么？

What are the features of authoritative parenting?

权威型教养既高要求又高回应：设定清晰规则与期望，同时温暖、倾听并解释理由。研究普遍把它与儿童更好的自律、学业和社交结果相联系。它与专断型（高要求低回应）和放任型（低要求高回应）不同。
Authoritative parenting is both demanding and responsive: clear rules and expectations plus warmth, listening, and explaining reasons. Research links it to better self-discipline, school, and social outcomes. It differs from authoritarian (demanding, cold) and permissive (undemanding, warm) styles.
