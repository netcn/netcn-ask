视交叉上核如何通过与褪黑素的互动调控昼夜节律？

How does the suprachiasmatic nucleus regulate circadian rhythm through melatonin?

下丘脑视交叉上核是主生物钟，接收视网膜光照信号并协调外周钟。夜间黑暗促使松果体分泌褪黑素、反馈抑制视交叉上核并诱发睡意，光照则抑制其分泌并重置相位。
The suprachiasmatic nucleus in the hypothalamus is the master clock, receiving retinal light signals and coordinating peripheral clocks. Darkness at night prompts the pineal gland to secrete melatonin, which feeds back to inhibit the SCN and induce sleepiness, while light suppresses melatonin and resets phase.

完全睡眠剥夺会首先损害哪些认知功能？

Which cognitive functions does total sleep deprivation impair first?

睡眠剥夺最先损害注意维持、工作记忆与高阶执行功能，而简单反射相对保留。迪明与丁格斯的研究显示，清醒 17 到 19 小时的认知损伤相当于约 0.05% 的血醇水平。
Sleep deprivation first impairs sustained attention, working memory, and higher executive functions, while simple reflexes are relatively spared. Durmer and Dinges showed that 17 to 19 hours awake produces cognitive impairment comparable to a blood alcohol level of about 0.05%.

为什么针对失眠的认知行为疗法被推荐为一线治疗？

Why is cognitive behavioral therapy for insomnia recommended as first-line treatment?

该疗法通过刺激控制、睡眠限制与认知重构打破焦虑失眠循环，疗效可媲美药物且无依赖。美国睡眠医学会推荐其作为慢性失眠首选，随访中效果更持久。
The therapy breaks the anxiety-insomnia loop through stimulus control, sleep restriction, and cognitive restructuring, matching drug efficacy without dependence. The American Academy of Sleep Medicine recommends it as the first-line treatment for chronic insomnia, with more durable effects at follow-up.

什么是社会时差，它为何影响健康？

What is social jet lag and why does it affect health?

社会时差指个体内在生物钟与社会作息之间的相位差（如夜猫子被迫早起）。伦内贝格研究发现它与代谢综合征、肥胖及情绪问题风险升高相关，因长期睡眠不规律打乱节律。
Social jet lag is the mismatch between a person's internal biological clock and social schedules (e.g., a night owl forced to rise early). Roenneberg found it correlates with higher risk of metabolic syndrome, obesity, and mood problems because irregular sleep chronically disrupts rhythm.

一个完整的夜间睡眠大约由多少个 90 分钟的周期构成？

How many roughly 90-minute cycles make up a full night of sleep?

成人每晚通常经历 4 到 6 个睡眠周期，每周期约 90 分钟，交替出现非快速眼动睡眠与快速眼动睡眠。前半夜以深睡为主，后半夜快速眼动比例增加。
Adults typically cycle through four to six sleep cycles per night, each about 90 minutes, alternating non-rapid-eye-movement and rapid-eye-movement sleep. Deep sleep dominates the first half of the night and REM proportion rises toward morning.

睡眠为什么有助于学习与记忆巩固？

Why does sleep help learning and memory consolidation?

睡眠中海马向新皮层回放当日信息，慢波睡眠巩固陈述性记忆，快速眼动巩固程序性与情绪记忆。斯蒂克戈德等发现学习后睡眠者的保持显著优于睡眠剥夺者。
During sleep the hippocampus replays the day's information to the neocortex; slow-wave sleep consolidates declarative memory and REM consolidates procedural and emotional memory. Stickgold and colleagues found that sleepers retain learned material far better than sleep-deprived people.

为什么研究者认为快速眼动睡眠与生动梦境关系最密切？

Why do researchers believe REM sleep is most closely tied to vivid dreams?

在快速眼动期脑电接近清醒状态而身体瘫痪，约 80% 被唤醒者报告生动梦境。霍布森的激活整合模型认为脑干随机放电被皮层整合为梦境叙事。
During REM, brain activity approaches waking levels while the body is paralyzed, and about 80% of awakenings yield vivid dream reports. Hobson's activation-synthesis model holds that random brainstem firing is integrated by the cortex into dream narratives.
