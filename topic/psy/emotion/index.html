詹姆斯-兰格理论如何解释情绪？

How does the James-Lange theory explain emotion?

詹姆斯-兰格理论认为，我们并非先感到害怕再逃跑，而是先对身体变化（如心跳加快）产生知觉，然后才体验到情绪。也就是说“因为我们发抖，所以感到害怕”。这挑战了“情绪在前、身体反应在后”的常识。
The James-Lange theory holds that we do not feel fear and then run; rather, we perceive bodily changes (such as a racing heart) first and then experience the emotion. In other words, 'we feel afraid because we tremble'. This challenges the common-sense view that emotion comes before the bodily response.

为什么看到壮丽的星空或大山，会感到“自己很渺小”却心情舒畅？

Why does a vast starry sky or mountain make us feel small — yet deeply good?

敬畏是在面对宏大、超出日常理解的事物时产生的情绪，核心体验是“自我变小、与世界相连”。研究显示，敬畏会降低自我关注，让人更慷慨、更愿意帮助他人，同时伴随着迷走神经活动增强带来的平静感。看自然风景、凝视星空、欣赏艺术都可能唤起敬畏，是低成本的心理健康资源。
Awe arises when we confront something vast that transcends everyday understanding; its core is a sense of a smaller self connected to the world. Studies find awe reduces self-focus, increases generosity and prosocial behavior, and is accompanied by calming vagal activity. Nature views, a starry sky, or great art can trigger it — a low-cost psychological resource.

Gross 的“情绪调节过程模型”包含哪些策略？

What strategies does Gross's "process model of emotion regulation" include?

Gross 于 1998 年提出情绪调节过程模型，将策略按情绪产生的时间进程排列：情境选择、情境修正、注意分配、认知重评与反应抑制。其中“认知重评”（改变对事件的解读）通常比“表达抑制”更有利于情绪健康与社交。
Gross's 1998 process model orders regulation strategies along the timeline of emotion generation: situation selection, situation modification, attentional deployment, cognitive reappraisal, and response inhibition. Cognitive reappraisal (changing how one interprets an event) generally benefits well-being and social functioning more than expressive suppression.

为什么运动员会自言自语“稳住”“加油”？这真的有用吗？

Why do athletes talk to themselves — “stay calm,” “come on”? Does it help?

这是“自我对话”，运动心理训练的标准工具。研究表明，功能性自我对话（指导动作、给自己打气）能改善注意分配和自信，而消极的自我批判（“我真没用”）会损害表现。语言会占用注意力资源，所以“说什么”和“怎么说”影响很大。把内心的批评换成温和、具体、教练式的指令，考试和面试同样受益。
That's self-talk, a standard tool in sport psychology training. Research shows functional self-talk (instructional cues, encouragement) improves attention and confidence, while harsh self-criticism (“I'm useless”) hurts performance. Language consumes attentional resources, so both content and tone matter. Replacing inner criticism with calm, specific, coach-like instructions helps in exams and interviews too.

Lisa Feldman Barrett 的情绪建构理论挑战了什么传统观点？

What traditional view does Barrett's theory of constructed emotion challenge?

传统观点认为情绪是进化固定的、由特定脑区触发的普遍反应。建构理论主张情绪是大脑用身体感受（内感）、概念和情境实时预测并构建的产物，不同文化会构建出不同情绪类别，情绪并非被动触发而是主动生成。
The traditional view sees emotions as evolved, universally triggered reactions in fixed brain circuits. The construction view holds that emotions are predictions the brain builds in real time from bodily sensations, concepts, and context; cultures construct different emotion categories—emotions are generated, not triggered.

什么是情绪的两因素理论？

What is the two-factor theory of emotion?

沙赫特和辛格提出，情绪由生理唤醒和对唤醒的“认知标签”共同决定：先有身体激动，再由情境解释成具体情绪。例如同样的心跳加快，在游乐场被解释为兴奋，在黑暗小巷被解释为恐惧。这说明认知会塑造情绪体验。
Schachter and Singer proposed that emotion results from both physiological arousal and a cognitive 'label' for that arousal: the body is stirred first, then the situation defines the emotion. The same racing heart can be labeled excitement at a fair or fear in a dark alley. This shows cognition shapes emotional experience.

情绪传染是如何通过无意识模仿发生的？

How does emotional contagion happen through unconscious mimicry?

情绪传染指人们无意识地模仿他人的表情、声音和姿势，触发自身相应情绪（具身模仿）。Hatfield 等指出，这种模仿-反馈环路经镜像系统运作，使情绪在群体中会像病毒般扩散，影响团队氛围与决策。
Emotional contagion is unconsciously mimicking others' expressions, voice, and posture, which triggers matching feelings (embodied mimicry). Hatfield et al. show a mimicry-feedback loop via mirror systems spreads emotion through groups, shaping team mood and decisions.

情绪调节中重新评估为何比压抑更健康？

Why is reappraisal healthier than suppression in emotion regulation?

重新评估是在情绪产生前改写对事件的解读（如把威胁看成挑战），从源头降低负性反应；压抑则是强压表达，虽表面平静却增加生理负担和记忆成本。Gross 的过程模型显示，早期干预（重新评估）比晚期（压抑）更有效且更少代价。
Reappraisal reframes the event's meaning early (threat to challenge), reducing negative response at the source; suppression forces down expression, staying calm outwardly but raising physiological and memory costs. Gross's process model favors early reappraisal over late suppression.

为什么“忍住不哭”“憋住怒火”，时间久了反而更累？

Why does holding in tears or anger leave us more drained over time?

情绪调节研究区分两种常见策略：认知重评是“改变对事件的解释”，表达抑制是“压下情绪的外在表现”。长期使用抑制，大脑要持续调用资源维持表面平静——这会升高生理唤醒、消耗精力，并与更高的压力水平和更低的幸福感相关。重评需要练习，但长期更省力、更可持续。
Emotion regulation research contrasts two common strategies: cognitive reappraisal changes how we interpret an event, while expressive suppression pushes down the outward display. Chronic suppression keeps the brain working to maintain a calm surface — raising physiological arousal, draining energy, and linking to more stress and less well-being. Reappraisal takes practice but is more effort-efficient and sustainable.

沙赫特-辛格的“情绪二因素理论”如何解释情绪？

How does the Schachter–Singer two-factor theory explain emotion?

沙赫特与辛格于 1962 年提出，情绪由两部分构成：生理唤醒加上个体对唤醒的“认知标签”。他们用肾上腺素实验证明，当人感到莫名唤醒却无合理解释时，会依据环境线索为情绪“命名”，说明认知在情绪体验中起关键作用。
Schachter and Singer proposed in 1962 that emotion comprises physiological arousal plus a cognitive label for that arousal. Their epinephrine experiment showed that when people feel unexplained arousal, they name the emotion from situational cues, indicating cognition's key role in emotional experience.

为什么新手机带来的快乐很快会“褪色”？

Why does the thrill of a new phone quickly fade?

这叫享乐适应：人会对持续不变的好事快速“习惯化”，情绪基线随之回落。中彩票者的幸福感在数月后基本回到原来水平，就是这个现象。但研究也发现，某些快乐反而“耐适应”——比如与人的连接、做有意义的事、不断有新体验的旅行。把钱花在体验而不是物品上，往往能买到更持久的幸福。
Hedonic adaptation: people habituate to steady good fortune quickly and the emotional baseline drifts back down. Lottery winners largely return to baseline happiness within months. But research finds some joys are more “adaptation-proof” — connection with people, meaningful activity, and new experiences like travel. Spending on experiences rather than objects tends to buy longer-lasting happiness.

人类有哪些基本情绪？

What are the basic human emotions?

保罗·艾克曼通过跨文化研究提出六种基本情绪：快乐、悲伤、愤怒、恐惧、厌恶和惊讶，它们的面部表情在不同文化中高度相似。近年研究也常把“轻蔑”列为第七种。基本情绪被认为具有进化意义。
Paul Ekman's cross-cultural research identified six basic emotions—happiness, sadness, anger, fear, disgust, and surprise—whose facial expressions are similar across cultures. Some recent work also lists contempt as a seventh. Basic emotions are thought to have evolutionary significance.

为什么强制微笑可能让人心情变好？

Why might forcing a smile actually lift your mood?

面部反馈假说认为，做出表情会向大脑回送信号，轻微影响情绪。研究发现，用嘴含笔（模拟微笑肌肉）的人看卡通更觉得好笑，说明身体姿势能反向塑造感受。
The facial feedback hypothesis: making an expression sends signals back to the brain, subtly shaping emotion. People holding a pen with their teeth (mimicking a smile) found cartoons funnier—bodily pose can reverse-shape feeling.

为什么心情差的时候，脑子里冒出来的全是倒霉事？

Why does a bad mood seem to flood the mind with other unhappy memories?

“情绪一致性记忆”指人在某种情绪状态下更容易提取带有相同情绪色彩的记忆（Bower, 1981）。一种机制是编码时情绪为记忆“打了标签”，回忆时相似情绪像钥匙一样打开了同一类材料，于是低落时消极往事格外容易被激活，形成情绪越陷越深的循环。
Mood-congruent memory means we retrieve material that matches our current emotional state more easily (Bower, 1981). One mechanism is that emotion tags memories at encoding, and the same mood later acts like a key that opens that category - so sadness preferentially activates negative recollections, deepening the dip.

为什么吃一次某样东西后呕吐，以后好多年都再也吃不下它？

Why does one bout of sickness after eating something make us unable to touch it for years?

“味觉厌恶学习”是生物进化出的快速防御：只要一次进食与恶心配对，动物甚至人就会长期回避该味道（Garcia & Koelling, 1966）。它违反了“多次邻近才能学会”的常规规律，说明味觉与呕吐回路在进化上被预先“接线”，用来以最小代价躲开有毒食物。
Taste-aversion learning is an evolved fast defense: a single pairing of a flavor with nausea produces long-lasting avoidance in animals and people (Garcia & Koelling, 1966). It breaks the usual rule that learning needs repeated contiguity, showing that taste and vomit circuits are pre-wired to dodge poison at minimal cost.

情绪是什么？

What is an emotion?

情绪是人对事物产生的短暂而强烈的心理和生理反应，比如高兴、害怕、生气。它通常包含感受、身体变化和想做的行为三部分。情绪帮助我们快速判断环境是否安全、是否值得接近。
An emotion is a brief but intense psychological and physiological response to something, such as happiness, fear, or anger. It usually includes a feeling, bodily changes, and an action urge. Emotions help us quickly judge whether a situation is safe or worth approaching.

为什么人会脸红？

Why do people blush?

脸红是情绪（如害羞、尴尬）引起的血管扩张，让面部血流增加而出现红晕。它是一种不受意志控制的生理反应，常出现在被关注或做错事时。脸红其实传递了“我知道自己失礼了”的社交信号。
Blushing is a vasodilation triggered by emotions like shyness or embarrassment, increasing blood flow to the face. It is an involuntary physiological response, often appearing when we are the center of attention or have done something wrong. Blushing actually signals 'I know I was impolite' socially.
