什么是“心盛”（flourishing）？它和“没病”有什么区别？

What is flourishing, and how does it differ from not being ill?

积极心理学用 PERMA 描述心盛：积极情绪、投入、关系、意义、成就共同饱满的状态。它不只是“没有心理疾病”，而是人在多个维度上真正蓬勃发展。
Positive psychology uses PERMA for flourishing: positive emotion, engagement, relationships, meaning and achievement all full. It is not merely no disorder but a person genuinely thriving across dimensions.

心理韧性是“孤军奋战扛过来”，还是来自支持的关系？

Is resilience about soldiering on alone, or about supportive relationships?

研究更支持后者：韧性常在安全的连接中长出来——有人可依靠、被理解，人才更敢面对挫折。它不是硬撑，而是“我知道不是一个人”。
Research favors the latter: resilience grows in safe connection, with someone to lean on and be understood, so people dare face setbacks. It is not gritting alone but knowing I am not by myself.

为什么“乐观”其实是一种可以练习的解释风格？

Why is optimism actually a learnable explanatory style?

Seligman 发现，人对坏事的解释落在“暂时/特定/外在”还是“永久/普遍/内在”，会影响后续状态。把失败归因为具体可控的因素，是可以训练出来的习惯。
Seligman found how we explain bad events, as temporary, specific and external versus permanent, broad and internal, shapes what follows. Blaming failure on specific controllable factors is a trainable habit.

为什么“有意义”常比“舒服”更能持久地让人满足？

Why does meaningful often satisfy more lastingly than comfortable?

舒服多是即时消耗的快感，意义来自与目标、他人和价值的连接，能支撑人在困难里也感到值得。研究把“意义”列为幸福感的核心维度之一。
Comfort is mostly consumed-on-the-spot pleasure, while meaning comes from links to goals, others and values, sustaining a sense of worth through hardship. Research lists meaning as a core dimension of well-being.

为什么中大奖或买新手机后，快乐没几天就回去了？

Why does happiness after a jackpot or a new phone fade within days?

这叫“快乐水车”（享乐适应）：人对好事的感受会很快回到基线。真正可持续的满足更多来自关系、成长和意义，而非不断加码的外在获得。
Called the hedonic treadmill, our feelings about good events quickly return to baseline. Lasting satisfaction comes more from relationships, growth and meaning than from ever-bigger external gains.

为什么“发挥优势”比只补缺点更让人成长？

Why does building on strengths grow people more than only fixing weaknesses?

优势视角认为，从已有的特质与能力出发，投入产出比更高、也更易进入心流；缺点只需补到“不拖后腿”。扬长带短，比死磕短板更可持续。
The strengths view holds that starting from existing traits yields higher return and easier flow, while weaknesses need only reach not holding you back. Building on strength beats grinding the weak spot.

为什么把目标写成“成为谁”，不如写成“每天做一点什么”？

Why does framing a goal as who to become work worse than what to do each day?

“成为谁”太抽象、难检验，容易停在想象；“每天做一点”是可执行、可反馈的具体行为，更容易积累成改变。身份是行动的结果，不是起点。
Who to become is too abstract and hard to check, stalling in imagination; what to do each day is actionable and feedback-rich, easier to accumulate into change. Identity is the result of action, not its start.

为什么“投入当下”比“等以后就好了”更让人幸福？

Why does being in the present beat waiting until later for happiness?

把幸福一直推到“等将来”，等于把生活悬空；而投入手边的事能生出心流与满足。未来值得规划，但此刻才是唯一能体验的地方。
Pushing happiness to someday suspends life; engaging the task at hand grows flow and satisfaction. The future is worth planning, but now is the only place experience happens.

为什么“小确幸”比一年一次的大惊喜更能累积幸福感？

Why do small sure joys build well-being more than one big yearly surprise?

频率比强度更影响长期感受：日常里可期待的小好事不断刷新积极体验，而罕见的盛大时刻很快被适应掉。幸福多是“常常有点好”，不是“偶尔极好”。
Frequency shapes long-term feeling more than intensity: daily small joys keep refreshing positive experience, while rare grand moments are quickly adapted away. Well-being is often a little good often, not extreme good rarely.

为什么“感恩拜访”（当面感谢一位重要的人）能明显提升情绪？

Why does a gratitude visit to thank someone important lift mood noticeably?

它把抽象的感激变成具体、真实的连接时刻，双方都被触动，记忆还会反复回放带来余温。一次真诚的致谢，效益常持续数周。
It turns vague gratitude into a concrete, real moment of connection that moves both people, and the memory replays as afterglow. One sincere thank-you often pays off for weeks.

为什么“给予”常常比“获取”更带来意义感？

Why does giving often bring more sense of meaning than getting?

给予让人感到与他人的连接和自己的价值，这种“被需要”的体验很难靠消费获得。一次小的分享或帮忙，就足以提醒自己“我对别人有用”。
Giving creates a sense of connection and personal value that consumption rarely matches. A small share or help reminds you that I am useful to others.

为什么帮了别人，自己也会跟着开心？

Why do we feel happier after helping someone?

助人会激活与奖赏相关的脑区，并强化“我被需要、我有能力”的感受，也常带来一段真实连接。所以善意对双方都是滋养。
Helping activates reward-related brain regions and strengthens feeling needed and capable, often adding a real connection. So kindness nourishes both sides.

为什么“和亲近的人共度质量时间”是幸福感最强的预测之一？

Why is quality time with close ones among the strongest predictors of happiness?

大量研究把亲密关系排在幸福感因素的前列：被理解和陪伴能满足人最根本的连接需要。比起更多物质，一段专注相处的时光往往更值钱。
Many studies rank close relationships near the top of happiness factors: feeling understood and accompanied meets the core need to connect. Over more stuff, a stretch of focused time together is often worth more.
