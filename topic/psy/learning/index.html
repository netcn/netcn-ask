什么是迁移学习？

What is transfer of learning?

迁移学习指在一个情境中学到的知识或技能应用到另一个情境，分为正迁移（促进）和负迁移（干扰）。例如会骑自行车有助于学电动车，但母语发音可能干扰外语。理解迁移能帮我们设计更通用的教学。
Transfer of learning is applying knowledge or skill from one context to another, as positive transfer (help) or negative transfer (interference). Riding a bike helps with an e-scooter, but native pronunciation can interfere with a foreign language. Understanding transfer helps design more general teaching.

什么是学习迁移？为什么学会了 A 有时帮不了 B？

What is transfer of learning and why does mastering A sometimes fail to help B?

学习迁移指在一个情境习得的知识/技能应用于另一情境。近迁移（相似情境）容易，远迁移（差异大）困难，受元素相似性与抽象原理掌握程度影响。过度情境绑定的练习会阻碍迁移，而强调底层原理促进迁移。
Transfer is applying learned knowledge/skill to a new context. Near transfer (similar contexts) is easy; far transfer is hard, shaped by surface similarity and grasp of underlying principles. Over context-bound practice blocks transfer; emphasizing abstract principles promotes it.

认知负荷理论对如何设计有效学习材料有何启示？

What does cognitive load theory imply for designing effective learning?

该理论认为工作记忆容量有限，学习受内在（材料难度）、外在（呈现方式冗余）和相关（促进图式）三类负荷影响。良好设计应减少外在负荷、管理内在负荷、最大化相关负荷，例如拆分步骤、去除无关信息、用样例引导。
Cognitive load theory says working memory is limited; learning is shaped by intrinsic (material difficulty), extraneous (poor presentation), and germane (schema-building) load. Good design cuts extraneous load, manages intrinsic, maximizes germane—e.g., chunk steps, remove clutter, use worked examples.

什么是间隔重复与闪卡？

What are spaced repetition and flashcards?

间隔重复是根据遗忘曲线，在即将忘记前复习，使记忆保持最久；闪卡把问题写一面、答案写另一面，便于自测。现代软件（如 Anki）用算法自动安排复习时间。这是被实证支持的高效记忆法。
Spaced repetition reviews material just before forgetting, based on the forgetting curve, to keep memory longest; flashcards put a question on one side and answer on the other for self-testing. Modern apps like Anki schedule reviews automatically. It is an evidence-backed efficient method.

元认知是什么？为什么知道自己不知道比聪明更重要？

What is metacognition and why does knowing what you don't know matter more than being smart?

元认知是对自己认知过程的监控与调节（计划、监控、评估）。高元认知者能准确判断我会/不会，从而合理分配复习；元认知错觉（如流畅性误判）会让人高估掌握度。校准判断与表现，是高效学习的核心。
Metacognition is monitoring and regulating your own cognition (planning, monitoring, evaluating). High metacognition means accurately judging I know/don't know to allocate study well; the fluency illusion makes people overrate mastery. Calibrating judgment to performance is central to effective learning.

为什么“检索练习”(做题、自测)比反复重读更能促进长期记忆保持?

Why does retrieval practice (self-testing) promote long-term retention better than repeated rereading?

检索练习迫使学习者主动重建记忆痕迹,这一过程本身会修改并巩固记忆表征,而重读只是提升当下的流畅感。Roediger 与 Karpicke 的实验发现,学习后一周,检索组的遗忘量约为重复学习组的一半,尽管重读组当时自我感觉更好。这类“合意困难”说明主观流畅性与真实保持之间存在系统性分离。
Retrieval practice forces learners to actively reconstruct memory traces, a process that itself modifies and consolidates the representation, whereas rereading only boosts momentary fluency. In Roediger and Karpicke's experiments, one week after study the retrieval conditions forgot roughly half as much as restudying conditions, even though rereaders felt more confident at first. Such desirable difficulties show that subjective fluency and actual retention systematically dissociate.

“间隔效应（spacing effect）”对学习和记忆有什么启示？

What does the "spacing effect" suggest for learning and memory?

间隔效应指将学习分散在多个时段（间隔重复）比集中时间一次性学习（填塞）能取得更好的长期保持。这一规律最早由 Ebbinghaus 在 19 世纪研究遗忘曲线时发现，并得到大量现代教育心理学实验的支持。
The spacing effect is that distributing study across sessions (spaced repetition) yields better long-term retention than massing it in one session (cramming). Ebbinghaus first observed it in the 19th century while studying the forgetting curve, and it is strongly supported by modern educational psychology.

交错练习为什么比集中练一种效果更好？

Why does interleaving beat blocking one type at a time?

交错练习指混合不同题型/技能轮换训练，而非长时间只练一种（集中练习）。它增加提取难度与辨别训练，提升长期保留和迁移，虽当下更费劲、成绩看起来更差。
Interleaving mixes different problem types/skills in rotation rather than massing one (blocking). It adds retrieval difficulty and discrimination training, boosting long-term retention and transfer—though it feels harder and looks worse immediately.

什么是“交错学习”？

What is 'interleaving' in learning?

交错学习是把不同科目或题型混合练习，而不是长时间只做同一种。相比“集中练习”，交错能提升辨别和迁移能力，虽然当下感觉更难。比如学数学时把几种题型混着做效果更好。
Interleaving mixes different subjects or problem types in practice instead of doing one kind for a long time. Compared with 'blocked practice', it improves discrimination and transfer, though it feels harder at the moment. For example, mixing math problem types works better.

什么是观察学习？

What is observational learning?

班杜拉指出，人可以通过观察别人的行为及其后果来学习，而不必自己试错，这叫观察学习或模仿。他的“波波娃娃实验”显示儿童会模仿成人对玩偶的攻击。榜样和媒体内容因此影响很大。
Bandura showed that people learn by observing others' behavior and its consequences without trial and error, called observational learning or modeling. His 'Bobo doll experiment' showed children imitate adults' aggression toward a doll. Role models and media therefore have great influence.

什么是经典条件反射？

What is classical conditioning?

巴甫洛夫发现，本来无关的刺激（如铃声）如果反复伴随会带来反应的事物（如食物），之后单独出现也会引发反应（流口水），这叫条件反射。它解释了恐惧、偏好等许多情绪性学习。广告也常利用这种联想。
Pavlov found that a neutral stimulus (a bell) paired repeatedly with a meaningful one (food) later triggers the response (salivation) alone—a conditioned reflex. It explains many emotional learnings like fears and preferences. Advertising also exploits such associations.

学习科学中的“测试效应（testing effect）”是什么？

What is the "testing effect" in the science of learning?

测试效应指主动提取信息（如自测、做测验）比被动重读更能促进长期记忆的巩固。Roediger 与 Karpicke 于 2006 年在实验中证实，反复提取练习能显著提高日后回忆，被称为“提取练习效应”。
The testing effect is the finding that actively retrieving information (e.g., self-quizzing) strengthens long-term retention more than passive rereading. Roediger and Karpicke demonstrated in 2006 that repeated retrieval practice greatly improves later recall, also called the retrieval practice effect.

斯金纳的“操作性条件反射”中，不同强化程序如何影响行为？

In Skinner's operant conditioning, how do different reinforcement schedules affect behavior?

操作性条件反射由 Skinner 提出，行为后果（奖励或惩罚）决定其再发生的概率。Ferster 与 Skinner 于 1957 年系统比较了连续强化与间歇强化，发现可变比率强化（如赌博）最能维持稳定而持久的行为。
Operant conditioning, developed by Skinner, holds that behavior is shaped by its consequences (reinforcement or punishment). Ferster and Skinner systematically compared continuous and intermittent schedules in 1957, finding that variable-ratio schedules (as in gambling) produce the most persistent behavior.

What is the generation effect and why do self-generated answers stick?

生成效应指相比被动阅读，自己生成内容（填空、造句、推导）能形成更深、更独特的记忆痕迹。主动构建迫使大脑建立更多检索路径，因此回忆率更高。
The generation effect: producing content yourself (fill-ins, sentences, derivations) creates deeper, more distinctive memory traces than reading. Active construction forces more retrieval routes, raising recall.

What is operant conditioning?

斯金纳提出，行为的结果会影响它再次出现的概率：奖励增加行为（正强化），惩罚或减少后果则降低行为。训练宠物、课堂管理都用这一原理。关键是“后果”要及时且一致。
Skinner proposed that the consequence of a behavior affects its future probability: rewards increase it (positive reinforcement), while punishment or removing a reward decreases it. Training pets and classroom management use this principle. The consequence must be timely and consistent.

儿童看到榜样因打人受罚后几乎不再模仿,可事后一宣布模仿有奖,他们做出的攻击动作立刻与其他孩子一样多。这一经典结果揭示了学习与表现之间的什么关系?

Children who saw a model punished for hitting hardly imitated at all, yet the moment imitation was later promised a reward, they produced aggressive acts just as readily as everyone else. What does this classic result reveal about the relation between learning and performance?

直答:榜样受罚并没有抹掉习得,只是压制了表现。机理:Bandura在1965年让不同组儿童看到攻击榜样的三种结局,受罚组当场模仿确实骤减,但改用奖品激励后,他们对动作的再现与其他组不相上下,可见观察早已完成学习,替代惩罚改变的是行为被表达的概率。是否表现出学到的行为取决于预期后果,这正是社会学习理论区分习得与表现的核心依据。
Punishment never erased what the children had learned - it only suppressed performance. In Bandura's 1965 experiment different groups watched an aggressive model come to three fates; the punished group imitated far less on the spot, yet once rewards were promised for reproducing the acts they matched the others almost move for move, showing observation had already done the teaching. Vicarious consequences shift how likely an acquired act is expressed, which is why social learning theory keeps acquisition and performance apart.

巴甫洛夫的“经典性条件反射”是怎样形成的？

How does Pavlov's "classical conditioning" form?

经典性条件反射中，一个原本中性的刺激（如铃声）反复与能引发反应的无条件刺激（如食物）配对后，单独呈现该中性刺激也能引发类似反应（如狗分泌唾液）。巴甫洛夫在 20 世纪初通过狗的实验系统揭示了这一学习机制。
In classical conditioning, a neutral stimulus (e.g., a bell) repeatedly paired with an unconditioned stimulus (e.g., food) comes to elicit a similar response (e.g., salivation) on its own. Pavlov systematically demonstrated this learning mechanism in dogs in the early 20th century.

为什么做错题反而记得更牢？

Why do we remember mistakes better?

当我们答错并被纠正时，大脑会因为“意外”而更专注地处理正确答案，这叫“测试效应”和“提取练习”。主动回忆比反复阅读效果更好。所以错题本其实是高效的学习工具。
When we answer wrong and get corrected, the brain pays more attention to the right answer because of 'surprise'—this is the 'testing effect' and retrieval practice. Active recall works better than rereading. So an error notebook is actually an efficient learning tool.

什么是主动回忆？为什么比反复阅读更有效？

What is active recall and why beats rereading?

主动回忆指在学完後合上材料，主动从脑中提取信息（自测、做题）。提取本身会强化记忆路径，比被动重读更能暴露薄弱点，且让记忆更持久。
Active recall means closing the material and retrieving info from memory (self-testing, practice problems). Retrieval itself strengthens memory pathways, exposes weak spots better than passive rereading, and makes memory last.

面对同一个行为示范,为什么儿童更倾向于模仿自己喜欢的或与自己相似的榜样?

Confronted with the very same demonstration, why do children lean toward imitating models they like or perceive as similar to themselves?

直答:因为榜样的吸引力决定了观察学习第一步的成败。机理:Bandura认为模仿须经注意、保持、再现和动机四个环节,地位高、讨人喜欢或与自己相似的榜样最能抓住儿童的注意,其行为也更容易被编码保持;对相似榜样的认同还会唤起「他能做到我也能」的自我效能预期,进一步强化模仿动机,于是同样的示范产生了强弱不同的学习效果。
A model's appeal decides whether the first step of observational learning succeeds at all. Bandura held that imitation passes through attention, retention, reproduction and motivation; high-status, likable, or self-similar models grab children's attention most readily, so their actions get encoded and retained better. Identifying with a similar model also raises the expectation that 'if they can do it, so can I,' which strengthens the motivation to copy - hence identical demonstrations yield different amounts of learning.
