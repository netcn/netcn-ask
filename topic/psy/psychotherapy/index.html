接纳承诺疗法（ACT）和传统 CBT 有什么不同？

How does Acceptance and Commitment Therapy (ACT) differ from traditional CBT?

CBT 多聚焦改变扭曲想法的内容；ACT 则主张不与念头争辩，而是接纳不适、明确价值并承诺行动（认知解离+正念+价值）。它把目标从“消除症状”转为“带着症状过有意义的生活”。
CBT often changes the content of distorted thoughts; ACT instead advises not wrestling with thoughts but accepting discomfort, clarifying values and committing to action (defusion + mindfulness + values). Its goal shifts from 'remove symptoms' to 'live meaningfully with them'.

EMDR（眼动脱敏与再加工）为什么有争议？

Why is EMDR (eye movement desensitization and reprocessing) controversial?

EMDR 让患者在回想创伤时跟随左右眼动，声称能减轻痛苦，对 PTSD 确有疗效证据。但眼动本身是否关键存疑——对照试验显示其效果可能来自暴露与再加工，而非眼球运动，故机制仍争议。
EMDR has patients recall trauma while following side-to-side eye movements, claiming relief, with evidence for PTSD. But whether the eye movement is essential is doubtful—controlled trials suggest benefits may come from exposure and reprocessing, not the saccades, so the mechanism is contested.

辩证行为疗法（DBT）是为解决什么问题设计的？

What problem was dialectical behavior therapy (DBT) designed to solve?

DBT 最初为反复自伤、情绪极端不稳的边际人格者设计，融合认知行为技术与正念、痛苦耐受、情绪调节等技能。它强调在“接纳”与“改变”间取得辩证平衡。
DBT was built for people with self-injury and extreme emotional instability (borderline personality), blending CBT with mindfulness, distress tolerance and emotion-regulation skills. It balances 'acceptance' and 'change' dialectically.

各种心理疗法有效，靠的是“共同因素”吗？

Do all psychotherapies work via 'common factors'?

研究发现不同疗法疗效差异不大，真正起作用的是共同因素：治疗关系、共情、期望、卷入与给予希望。具体技术重要，但温暖可信的结盟往往是改变的核心。
Studies find modest differences between therapies; what matters are common factors: the alliance, empathy, expectation, engagement and instilled hope. Specific techniques help, but a warm, trustworthy bond is often the core of change.

精神分析在今天还有科学地位吗？

Does psychoanalysis still have scientific standing today?

经典精神分析的可证伪性与疗效证据较弱，主流循证医学评价不高；但其对潜意识、移情与早期经验的洞见影响了现代动力治疗。今天多被视为文化思潮而非首选项实证疗法。
Classic psychoanalysis scores poorly on falsifiability and efficacy evidence and is little favored by evidence-based medicine; yet its insights on the unconscious, transference and early experience shaped modern dynamic therapy. Today it is more cultural legacy than first-line empirical treatment.

不同流派的谈话治疗中，治疗关系本身在多大程度上能预示疗效？研究给出了怎样的量化证据？

Across different schools of talk therapy, how far does the therapeutic relationship itself predict outcome, and what quantitative evidence exists?

治疗联盟指来访者与治疗师之间的情感信任，以及对目标和任务的一致认同。跨流派元分析显示，联盟质量与结局的相关约 0.28，早期联盟尤其关键，控制早期症状改善后关联仍然存在。但相关不等于因果，联盟好坏也可能部分源于症状变化本身。总体看，技术之外，联盟的建立与修复是可训练、可评估的核心成分。
The therapeutic alliance denotes the bond of trust between client and therapist plus shared agreement on goals and tasks. Cross-school meta-analyses find alliance quality correlates with outcome around .28, the early alliance being especially pivotal, and the association persists after controlling for early symptom change. Correlation is not causation, however, since alliance quality may partly stem from symptom change itself. Beyond technique, building and repairing alliance is a trainable, assessable core ingredient.

什么是“暴露疗法”？为什么面对恐惧反而能治好恐惧？

What is exposure therapy, and why does facing fear cure fear?

暴露疗法让人在安全环境下逐步接触害怕的对象或记忆，反复经验后大脑学会“没危险”，恐惧反应自然消退（习惯化与消退学习）。它是治疗恐惧症、创伤的有效方法之一。
Exposure therapy gradually and safely contacts the feared object or memory; repeated experience teaches the brain 'no danger', and fear responses fade via habituation and extinction learning. It is an effective treatment for phobias and trauma.
