遇到心理危机时，除了热线，还可以有哪些求助思路？

In a mental health crisis, besides a hotline, what other help pathways exist?

思路包括联系信任的人陪在身边、前往就近的急诊或社区心理服务、让家人协助保管可能用到的危险物。关键是别独自硬扛，把“此刻安全”放在第一位。
Pathways include reaching a trusted person to stay with you, going to nearby emergency or community mental health services, and having family secure items that could be used for harm. The key is not coping alone and putting safety now first.

为什么“病耻感”会拖住本该求助的人？

Why does stigma hold back people who should seek help?

病耻感来自“求助=软弱或有缺陷”的误解，让人害怕被贴标签、被另眼相看。把它重新看作“和感冒一样去处理健康问题”，能降低这道心理门槛。
Stigma grows from the false link of help-seeking with weakness or flaw, fearing labels and sideways looks. Reframing it as handling a health issue like a cold lowers that mental threshold.

为什么心理状态的“恢复”常常是波动的，而不是直线变好？

Why is recovery from distress often up and down rather than a straight line up?

情绪像波浪，进步中夹着反复是常态，不代表“没用”或“复发”。把起伏看作康复的一部分，反而能减少因“又变差”而来的挫败。
Mood moves in waves, and progress with setbacks is normal, not proof of uselessness or relapse. Taking the swings as part of recovery reduces the defeat of feeling worse again.

安慰低谷中的人，为什么“陪着”比“劝他想开”更重要？

Comforting someone low, why does just being there beat telling them to look on the bright side?

“想开点”常被听成否定痛苦，而稳定的陪伴传递“你不是一个人”。先接住情绪，等对方有力气了，建议才可能被听进去。
Look on the bright side is often heard as denying pain, while steady presence says you are not alone. Hold the feeling first; advice lands only once the person has strength.

网上心理健康信息那么多，怎么判断靠不靠谱？

With so much mental health info online, how do we judge if it is trustworthy?

看来源：是否来自专业机构、是否注明依据、是否把个例当普遍结论。警惕“包好”“秒愈”的承诺，可靠信息通常谨慎、可核查。
Check the source: is it from a professional body, does it cite basis, does it generalize from one case. Beware promises of instant cure; reliable info is cautious and verifiable.

心理自助书有用，但为什么替代不了专业治疗？

Self-help books help, so why cannot they replace professional therapy?

书提供知识与视角，却无法针对你的具体情况反馈、陪你度过情绪难关。中重度困扰或危机时，仍需有训练的人和系统方案。
Books give knowledge and perspective but cannot tailor feedback to your case or sit with you through distress. For moderate to severe struggles or crisis, trained people and structured plans are still needed.

为什么“先照顾好自己”不是自私？

Why is taking care of yourself first not selfish?

照顾自己是维持助人能力的底座：耗干了的自己既帮不了人，也更容易崩溃。把自己当需要维护的“设备”，反而能更久地支持别人。
Self-care is the base that keeps your ability to help; a drained self helps no one and breaks easier. Treat yourself as equipment that needs upkeep to support others longer.

“心理热线”是做什么的？拨通后一般会发生什么？

What does a mental health hotline do, and what usually happens when you call?

热线提供即时、匿名、不评判的倾听与初步支持，帮你在危机或孤单时稳住，并转介进一步资源。它不诊断、不开药，而是先接住你这个人。
A hotline offers immediate, anonymous, non-judging listening and first support, helping you steady in crisis or loneliness and pointing to further resources. It does not diagnose or prescribe, but first holds the person.

找心理咨询师聊天，和找朋友倾诉有什么不同？

How is talking to a counselor different from talking to a friend?

朋友给陪伴与安慰，咨询师受过系统训练、保持中立与保密，用循证方法帮你理清模式而非只听故事。两者互补，专业帮助不等于“朋友不够好”。
Friends give company and comfort; a counselor is trained, neutral and bound by confidentiality, using evidence-based methods to map patterns not just hear stories. They complement each other, and professional help is not a verdict on friendship.

什么情况下，情绪困扰该考虑找专业帮助？

When should emotional distress prompt considering professional help?

一个简单的信号是“功能受损”：睡眠、工作、学习或关系持续受影响超过两周，或出现自伤念头。越早求助，恢复通常越快，求助是照顾自己的表现。
A simple signal is impaired function: sleep, work, study or relationships are hit for over two weeks, or there are self-harm thoughts. Earlier help usually means faster recovery; seeking it is self-care.

被老师批评了,是不是说明我很差劲?

Does getting criticized by my teacher mean I am no good?

批评针对的是那件事的做法,不是你这个人。老师说字写得潦草,意思是这一页可以更工整,而不是你不好。把批评当成地图上的红圈:圈出需要加油的位置,改掉它你就升级了。会接受批评的人进步最快。
Criticism targets the work, not the person. Messy handwriting feedback means that page can improve, not that you are bad. Treat criticism as red circles on a map marking where to train — fix it and level up. Those who accept feedback improve fastest.
