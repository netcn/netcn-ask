最小群体范式为什么能证明内群偏好不需要任何利益冲突？

Why does the minimal group paradigm show in-group favouritism needs no conflict of interest?

塔杰费尔用抛硬币或对抽象画的偏好等毫无意义的标准把被试分成两组，成员彼此匿名、没有互动也没有实际利益关联，随后请他们分配点数。结果被试系统性地偏向本组成员，甚至在有更高绝对收益的方案面前，仍宁愿选择让本组相对领先的分配。这说明单纯的分类就足以引发群际区分，社会认同理论据此提出人们通过让内群优于外群来获得积极的自我评价。这一发现把偏见的来源从资源竞争扩展到认同需求本身。
Tajfel divided participants by meaningless criteria such as a coin toss or a preference between abstract paintings, keeping members anonymous with no interaction and no material stake, then asked them to allocate points. Participants systematically favoured their own group and often preferred allocations that maximised the in-group's relative advantage over ones giving their group more in absolute terms. Mere categorisation is therefore enough to generate intergroup differentiation, and social identity theory accounts for it by arguing people derive positive self-evaluation from seeing their in-group as superior. The finding extended the roots of prejudice from resource competition to identity needs themselves.

米尔格拉姆服从实验的档案研究带来了哪些新的解读？

What new interpretations have archival studies brought to Milgram's obedience experiments?

原始报告强调普通人在权威指令下会施加看似危险的电击，服从率之高震动了学界。研究者后来查阅耶鲁大学档案中的录音与被试事后问卷，发现相当比例的被试怀疑电击是假的，而怀疑程度与服从行为相关；实验者的说服话术也远比程序说明中的四句标准提示灵活多变。这些发现并未否认情境压力的作用，但把解释重心从盲目服从转向了对情境定义的接受、对科学事业的认同以及被试与实验者之间的互动过程。
The original reports emphasised that ordinary people would deliver apparently dangerous shocks under an authority's instruction, and the high obedience rates shocked the field. Researchers later examined recordings and post-experiment questionnaires in the Yale archives and found a substantial share of participants doubted the shocks were real, with that scepticism correlating with compliance, while the experimenter's prods were far more improvised than the four standard lines described in the procedure. These findings do not deny the power of the situation, but they shift the explanation from blind obedience toward acceptance of the situation's definition, identification with the scientific enterprise, and the interactive dynamics between participant and experimenter.

接触假说要满足哪些条件才能真正减少偏见？

What conditions must contact satisfy to genuinely reduce prejudice?

奥尔波特提出四个促进条件：群体间地位平等、共同追求的目标、合作而非竞争的互动结构，以及权威或制度的明确支持。佩蒂格鲁与特罗普的大规模元分析纳入五百多项研究，确认接触与偏见降低存在稳定的负相关，满足奥尔波特条件时效应更强，但即使不完全满足也有一定效果。后续研究还发现，跨群体友谊、共情与减少焦虑是主要中介机制，而通过媒体或想象的间接接触也能产生较弱的类似效果。需要注意的是因果方向存在选择效应，偏见较低的人更可能主动寻求接触。
Allport specified four facilitating conditions: equal status between groups, common goals, cooperative rather than competitive interaction, and explicit support from authorities or institutions. Pettigrew and Tropp's meta-analysis of more than five hundred studies confirmed a robust negative association between contact and prejudice, stronger when Allport's conditions are met yet still present when they are not fully satisfied. Later work identified cross-group friendship, empathy, and reduced anxiety as the main mediators, and found weaker but real effects for indirect contact through media or imagined encounters. A caveat is selection: less prejudiced people are more likely to seek contact in the first place, complicating causal inference.

Can the Implicit Association Test predict real-world discriminatory behaviour?

该测验通过比较相容与不相容配对条件下的反应时差异，测量概念与评价之间的自动联结强度，被广泛用于研究内隐偏见。元分析发现，测验分数与外显行为指标的相关通常较弱，重测信度也低于常规人格量表，因此不适合用于评估或筛选个体。但在群体与区域层面，汇总的测验分数与一些社会结果指标存在关联。较为稳妥的定位是把它当作研究自动化社会认知的实验工具，而不是判定某人是否会歧视他人的诊断手段。
The test infers the strength of automatic associations between concepts and evaluations from response-time differences between congruent and incongruent pairings, and it is widely used to study implicit bias. Meta-analyses find its scores correlate only weakly with overt behavioural measures and that test-retest reliability is lower than for conventional personality scales, so it is unsuitable for evaluating or screening individuals. At group or regional level, aggregated scores do track some social outcomes. The defensible position is to treat it as a laboratory tool for studying automatic social cognition rather than a diagnostic of whether a given person will discriminate.

Is moral judgment fixed from birth?

刻板印象威胁的研究结论为何在重复检验中变得更加谨慎？

Why have conclusions about stereotype threat become more cautious under replication scrutiny?

斯蒂尔与阿伦森最初发现，当测验被描述为诊断能力且提示了群体身份时，处于负面刻板印象下的被试成绩下降，机制被解释为担忧证实刻板印象所引发的额外认知负荷与焦虑。近年来的多实验室重复研究与考虑发表偏差的元分析显示，效应量比早期报告小得多，在部分场景中不显著，而干预效果在实地随机试验中也不稳定。目前较稳妥的表述是：情境线索确实能影响表现，但效应依赖具体人群、任务难度与身份显著性，不能简单套用于解释群体间的整体成绩差距。
Steele and Aronson originally found that when a test was framed as diagnostic of ability and group identity was made salient, members of negatively stereotyped groups performed worse, explained by the extra cognitive load and anxiety of fearing one would confirm the stereotype. Multi-lab replications and meta-analyses adjusting for publication bias now report much smaller effects, non-significant in some settings, and field randomised trials of interventions show inconsistent benefits. The defensible summary is that situational cues can influence performance, but the effect depends on the specific population, task difficulty, and identity salience, and cannot be applied wholesale to explain group-level achievement gaps.

对应偏差为什么这么难克服？即使知道对方没有选择余地也一样？

Why is correspondence bias so hard to overcome, even when we know the person had no choice?

琼斯与哈里斯让被试阅读支持或反对某位领导人的短文，并明确告知作者是被指定立场的，被试仍推断文章观点反映了作者的真实态度。这种把行为直接对应到内在特质、低估情境约束的倾向被称为对应偏差或基本归因错误。原因包括行为在知觉上比情境更显著、对情境力量的估计需要额外认知努力，以及人们默认他人的行为出自选择。研究还发现，强调情境信息、要求被试解释他人为何不得不如此，或让其亲身处于同一角色，能部分削弱该偏差。
Jones and Harris had participants read essays for or against a leader while being told explicitly that the writer's position had been assigned, yet participants still inferred the essay reflected the writer's true attitude. This tendency to map behaviour directly onto inner traits while discounting situational constraints is called correspondence bias or the fundamental attribution error. Contributing factors include the perceptual salience of the actor relative to the situation, the extra cognitive effort needed to weigh situational force, and a default assumption that behaviour is chosen. Highlighting situational information, asking participants to explain why the person had no alternative, or placing them in the same role can partly reduce the bias.

为什么人们在艰难地做出选择之后,常常会更确信自己选中的那个选项本来就是最好的?

Post-decision cognitive dissonance is behind this: the flaws of the chosen option and the merits of the forgone one clash with the belief that you decided wisely, and the clash feels unpleasant. Because the decision cannot be undone, the cheapest fix is to shift the attitude rather than the behavior - rating the picked option higher and the rejected one lower, exactly what Brehm saw when participants re-rated appliances after choosing.
