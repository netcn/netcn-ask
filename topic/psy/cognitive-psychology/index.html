沃森的四卡问题为什么大多数人做错，换成社会情境却轻松通过？

Why do most people fail Wason's four-card task yet solve it easily in a social context?

抽象版任务要求检验若一面是元音则另一面是偶数这类条件规则，多数被试只翻验证性的卡片而忽略最关键的否定后件，反映出确认倾向和对逻辑蕴含的直觉误用。但如果把同一逻辑结构换成检查饮酒年龄这类社会契约情境，正确率会大幅上升。对此有两种主要解释：一是内容效应与熟悉度提供了可用的语义线索，二是进化心理学提出人类具备专门检测欺骗与违规者的推理机制。实验之所以重要，在于它显示推理表现高度依赖问题的表征方式，而非单纯的一般逻辑能力。
In the abstract version participants must test a conditional rule such as if one side shows a vowel then the other shows an even number, and most turn only the confirming card while neglecting the crucial potential falsifier, revealing a confirmation tendency and misuse of logical implication. Recast with the same logical structure as a social contract, such as checking a drinking-age rule, accuracy rises sharply. Two accounts compete: content effects and familiarity supply usable semantic cues, and evolutionary psychologists propose a specialised mechanism for detecting cheaters and rule violators. The task matters because it shows reasoning performance depends heavily on how a problem is represented rather than on general logical competence alone.

双语者真的具有执行功能优势吗？这一主张为何存在争议？

Do bilinguals really have an executive function advantage, and why is the claim contested?

早期研究提出，双语者需要持续抑制非目标语言，这种日常训练会迁移到一般性的冲突监控与切换能力，并可能延缓痴呆症状出现。质疑来自多方面：大样本研究与预注册重复常未发现优势，早期文献存在显著结果更易发表的偏差，双语群体往往在移民背景、社会经济地位与教育经历上与单语群体不可比。目前较为审慎的看法是，双语在语言控制的特定任务上确有可测差异，但泛化到一般执行功能的强主张证据不足，认知储备方面的效应仍需更严格的纵向设计检验。
Early studies proposed that bilinguals must continuously suppress the non-target language, and that this daily practice transfers to general conflict monitoring and switching ability, perhaps delaying the onset of dementia symptoms. Doubts arose from several directions: large-sample studies and preregistered replications often find no advantage, the early literature shows bias toward publishing significant results, and bilingual samples typically differ from monolingual ones in immigration background, socioeconomic status, and schooling. The cautious current view is that measurable differences exist on specific language-control tasks, evidence for the strong generalisation to executive function is weak, and cognitive reserve effects need more rigorous longitudinal designs.

认知负荷理论（Cognitive Load Theory）对教学设计有何启示？

What implications does Cognitive Load Theory have for instructional design?

认知负荷理论由 Sweller 于 1988 年提出，认为工作记忆容量有限，学习任务若占用过多“内在”或“外在”认知负荷会阻碍图式建构。有效教学应通过减少无关负荷、促进相关负荷来帮助学生形成长期记忆中的知识结构。
Proposed by Sweller in 1988, Cognitive Load Theory holds that working memory is limited, and tasks imposing excessive intrinsic or extraneous load hinder schema construction. Effective instruction reduces extraneous load and supports germane load to build knowledge structures in long-term memory.

面孔识别是否依赖专门的脑区？先天性面孔失认症提供了什么证据？

Does face recognition depend on a dedicated brain region, and what does developmental prosopagnosia show?

功能磁共振研究在颞下皮层发现纺锤状面孔区，它对面孔的反应显著强于其他物体，支持面孔加工具有专门机制的观点。反对者认为该区域反映的是对高度熟练类别的精细区分能力，例如鸟类专家看鸟、汽车迷看车时也会激活。先天性面孔失认症患者在没有明显脑损伤和智力问题的情况下终身难以识别面孔，却能正常辨认物体，而获得性病例则往往伴随该区域损伤；这类双向分离支持面孔加工在功能上相对独立，但专门化程度仍在争论。
Functional MRI identified a fusiform face area in inferior temporal cortex that responds far more strongly to faces than to other objects, supporting a dedicated face-processing mechanism. Critics argue the region reflects fine-grained discrimination within any highly practised category, since it also activates when bird experts view birds or car enthusiasts view cars. People with developmental prosopagnosia struggle lifelong to recognise faces despite no evident brain injury or intellectual impairment while identifying objects normally, and acquired cases typically involve damage to this region; such double dissociations support relative functional independence for face processing, though the degree of specialisation remains debated.

认知负荷理论如何指导教学材料设计？

How does cognitive load theory guide the design of instructional materials?

该理论假定工作记忆容量有限，把负荷分为内在负荷、外在负荷与相关负荷：内在负荷取决于内容本身的复杂度，外在负荷来自糟糕的呈现方式，相关负荷则用于建构图式。设计原则由此推出，例如把图与对应文字放在一起以避免分散注意效应，删除装饰性插图以减少冗余，为新手提供完整的样例讲解而不是纯粹的问题求解。值得注意的是专长逆转效应：对已有图式的学习者，详尽指导反而成为多余负荷，此时应转向独立解题。
The theory assumes working memory is limited and distinguishes intrinsic load set by content complexity, extraneous load created by poor presentation, and germane load devoted to building schemas. Design rules follow: place diagrams next to their explanatory text to avoid the split-attention effect, strip decorative illustrations that add redundancy, and give novices fully worked examples rather than unguided problem solving. Notably, the expertise reversal effect means that for learners who already possess the relevant schema, detailed guidance becomes redundant load and practice should shift toward independent problem solving.

认知心理学中的“双过程理论”如何区分两类思维？

How do “dual-process theories” in cognitive psychology distinguish two kinds of thinking?

双过程理论将思维分为快速、自动、不费力且常凭直觉的“系统一（System 1）”与缓慢、需要努力和逻辑分析的“系统二（System 2）”。Stanovich 与 West 于 2000 年系统提出这一区分，Kahneman 在 2011 年的著作中将其普及并用于解释判断偏差。
Dual-process theories divide thinking into “System 1,” which is fast, automatic, effortless, and intuitive, and “System 2,” which is slow, effortful, and analytic. Stanovich and West systematically proposed the distinction in 2000, and Kahneman popularized it in 2011 to explain judgment biases.

为什么人会对没有实际因果关系的事件产生控制错觉？

Why do people develop an illusion of control over events they cannot actually influence?

兰格的实验显示，当彩票情境中加入选择号码、熟悉度、竞争对手或练习机会等本与结果无关的技能线索时，被试对中奖概率的主观估计明显上升。机制在于人们用技能情境的启发式来解读随机事件，同时倾向把成功归因于自身操作、把失败归因于外部干扰，从而维持了控制感。这一偏差在赌博行为、股票交易与迷信仪式中都有体现，适度的控制错觉与心理健康正相关，但在需要准确风险评估的场景中会造成系统性误判。
Langer's experiments showed that adding skill cues irrelevant to the outcome — choosing one's own lottery numbers, familiarity with the tickets, a visibly incompetent competitor, or practice trials — raised participants' subjective estimates of winning. The mechanism is that people apply heuristics from skill situations to interpret random events, while attributing successes to their own actions and failures to external interference, which preserves a sense of control. The bias appears in gambling, stock trading, and superstitious rituals; a moderate illusion of control correlates with psychological wellbeing, but it produces systematic misjudgement wherever accurate risk assessment matters.

什么是“前瞻性记忆”？

What is prospective memory?

前瞻性记忆是记得在未来某个时刻执行计划好的行为，比如按时服药或赴约，与回想过去事件的回溯性记忆不同。它依赖大脑的执行功能与线索触发。
Prospective memory is remembering to perform an intended action at a future time, such as taking medicine or keeping an appointment, unlike retrospective memory which recalls past events. It depends on executive function and on cues that trigger the intention.

斯特鲁普效应说明了什么样的加工冲突？为什么练习会削弱它？

What kind of processing conflict does the Stroop effect reveal, and why does practice weaken it?

当颜色词的字义与墨色不一致时，说出墨色的反应时明显变长、错误增多，因为熟练成人的阅读高度自动化，字义在无意图的情况下被激活，与需要努力控制的颜色命名任务竞争同一反应通道。这说明自动化加工难以随意关闭，冲突解决依赖前扣带皮层与前额叶的控制机制。若让被试反复练习颜色命名，或反过来削弱阅读的自动化程度，干扰量就会减小，这也是自动化程度可训练的证据。
When a colour word's meaning conflicts with its ink colour, naming the ink takes longer and produces more errors, because reading is highly automatic in skilled adults so word meaning is activated unintentionally and competes for the same response channel as the effortful colour-naming task. This shows automatic processing cannot simply be switched off, and that conflict resolution recruits control mechanisms in the anterior cingulate and prefrontal cortex. Extensive practice at colour naming — or conditions that reduce reading automaticity — shrinks the interference, evidence that automaticity itself is trainable.

变化盲视实验为什么能颠覆我们对视觉经验的直觉？

Why do change blindness experiments overturn our intuitions about visual experience?

在西蒙斯与莱文的实地实验中，实验者向路人问路，途中两人抬着门板穿过，趁遮挡把对话者换成另一个人，约半数路人没有察觉。类似地，在图片渐变或闪烁遮挡范式中，画面里相当大的物体消失也常被忽略。这说明视觉系统并不维护一份完整的场景副本，而是按需把注意投向局部并即时提取信息，所谓丰富连续的视觉体验很大程度上是主观建构，而不是逐点存储的结果。
In Simons and Levin's field study an experimenter asked pedestrians for directions and, while two people carried a door between them, was swapped for a different person mid-conversation; roughly half the pedestrians never noticed. Similar failures occur in gradual-change and flicker paradigms, where sizeable objects vanish from a scene unremarked. The lesson is that vision does not maintain a complete internal copy of the world but directs attention locally and extracts information on demand, so our sense of a rich continuous visual experience is largely a construction rather than point-by-point storage.

为什么同样治好9成和“死1成”的药，大家接受度差那么多？

Why is a treatment described as 90 percent survive accepted far more than the same one called 10 percent die?

特沃斯基与卡尼曼的“框架效应”（Tversky & Kahneman, 1981）表明，决策随信息是“收益框架”还是“损失框架”而反转，哪怕客观结果相同。面对收益人们偏保守、面对损失偏冒险，这揭示偏好并不稳定，而被表述方式左右。
Tversky and Kahneman's framing effect (1981) shows choices flip depending on whether information is cast as gains or losses, even with identical outcomes. People turn risk-averse for gains and risk-seeking for losses, revealing that preferences are unstable and shaped by wording.

为什么专家在自己领域内的直觉判断有时非常可靠，有时又完全失灵？

Why is expert intuition sometimes highly reliable and sometimes completely unreliable?

克莱因研究消防指挥官等实务专家，发现他们能在数秒内识别情境模式并选出可行方案，依靠的是长期积累的模式库。卡尼曼则指出许多领域的专家预测并不优于简单统计规则。两人后来达成共识：直觉可靠需要两个条件，一是环境具有足够的规律性与可预测结构，二是从业者能通过长期实践获得及时且明确的反馈。棋类、消防与麻醉满足这些条件，而长期政治预测和股票选择则不满足，因此在低效度环境中应更多依赖形式化模型与检查清单。
Klein studied practitioners such as fireground commanders and found they recognise situational patterns within seconds and select a workable option, drawing on a large repertoire built over years. Kahneman countered that in many fields expert forecasts do not beat simple statistical rules. The two later converged on two conditions for trustworthy intuition: an environment with sufficient regularity and predictable structure, and an opportunity to learn it through prolonged practice with prompt, unambiguous feedback. Chess, firefighting, and anaesthesia meet these criteria while long-range political forecasting and stock picking do not, so in low-validity environments formal models and checklists should carry more weight.
