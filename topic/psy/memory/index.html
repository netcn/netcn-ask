What is the capacity of working memory?

The classic view is that adults hold about 7±2 separate chunks in working memory; more recent estimates suggest around 4±1. 'Chunking' can expand effective capacity.

什么是前瞻性记忆？

What is prospective memory?

前瞻性记忆是指记得在将来某个时刻去做某件事，比如“下班顺路买牛奶”或“三小时后吃药”。它依赖执行功能和情境线索的触发，比起回忆过去更难被意识监控。随着年龄增长，前瞻性记忆容易下降。
Prospective memory is remembering to do something in the future, such as 'buy milk on the way home' or 'take medicine in three hours'. It relies on executive function and contextual cues, and is harder to monitor consciously than recalling the past. It tends to decline with age.

什么是情景记忆与语义记忆？

What are episodic and semantic memory?

情景记忆是个人亲身经历的具体事件（如昨天生日聚会发生了什么），带有时空背景。语义记忆是关于世界的事实和概念知识（如“巴黎是法国首都”），不依赖具体经历。两者都属于长时记忆中的陈述性记忆。
Episodic memory is memory of specific personal events with a time and place, like what happened at yesterday's birthday party. Semantic memory is factual and conceptual knowledge, like 'Paris is the capital of France', independent of personal experience. Both are types of declarative memory in long-term storage.

为什么老人记得几十年前的往事，却忘了刚放的钥匙？

Why do older adults recall decades-old memories but forget where they put the keys?

久远而反复讲述、带有强烈情感的事件，经过多年反复加工，记忆非常牢固；而“钥匙放哪”这类情境记忆依赖编码时的注意力，一走神就存不进去。年龄相关的记忆变化主要是“提取变慢、易受干扰”，而不是记忆消失。固定放钥匙的位置、使用提醒清单，就能很好地弥补。
Distant, often-recounted, emotionally rich events are consolidated for years and stay vivid, while “where did I put the keys” is a context memory that depends on attention at encoding — a moment of distraction and it never lands. Age-related memory change is mostly slower retrieval and more interference, not memory loss. A fixed spot for keys and reminder lists compensate beautifully.

What is prospective memory and why do we more often forget intended actions with age?

前瞻记忆是记住在未来某个时刻执行某个意图（如服药、赴约），分基于时间和基于事件两类。它依赖执行功能和注意监控，老年人在无明显提醒时尤其容易失败，因默认模式网络与额叶功能减弱。
Prospective memory is remembering to perform an intention in the future (e.g., taking medication), split into time- and event-based types. It relies on executive control and monitoring; older adults especially fail without explicit cues due to weaker frontal and default-mode function.

心理学家 Schacter 提出的记忆的七宗罪指什么？

What are the seven sins of memory proposed by Schacter?

Schacter 将记忆失误归为七类：易逝、分心（遗忘相关），以及错认、暗示、偏差、纠缠、持久（扭曲相关）。它们不是缺陷，而是适应性系统的副产物——例如遗忘让大脑高效，但代价是失真。
Schacter groups memory failures into seven sins: transience and absent-mindedness (forgetting), plus misattribution, suggestibility, bias, persistence, and intrusions (distortion). They are side effects of an adaptive system—forgetting keeps the brain efficient at the cost of accuracy.

什么是提取诱发遗忘？为什么反复练习回忆一部分材料会妨碍同类其余材料的回忆？

What is retrieval-induced forgetting, and why does repeated retrieval practice on some material hinder recall of the remaining material from the same category?

该效应指：练习提取某类别的部分项目后，同类未练习项目变得更难回忆。机制在于竞争与抑制：提取时，共享线索的相关项目作为竞争者被激活，须受抑制才能选中目标，抑制并留下持久的提取代价；Anderson 等的提取练习范式为此提供了系统证据。它提醒复习应覆盖全部要点，也解释了目击者访谈中谈及一个细节可能妨碍相邻细节的回忆。
This effect refers to unpracticed items of a category becoming harder to retrieve after some of its members receive retrieval practice. The mechanism is competition and inhibition: while retrieving a target, associates sharing its cue are activated as competitors and must be suppressed for the target to win, leaving a lasting retrieval cost, as shown in Anderson and colleagues' retrieval-practice paradigm. It counsels covering every point when reviewing, and helps explain why asking a witness about one detail can momentarily block adjacent details.

什么是编码特异性原则？潜水员水下记忆实验是如何支持它的？

What is the encoding specificity principle, and how did the diver memory experiments support it?

该原则主张：提取成功取决于测验情境提供的线索是否与编码时形成的线索相匹配。潜水员分别在岸上或水下学习单词表，随后在相同或不同环境中回忆；学习与测试环境一致时自由回忆显著更佳，错位则明显下滑，提示许多遗忘源于线索失配而非记忆痕迹消失。此效应在自由回忆中最强，在有强线索支持的再认中很弱。
The principle states that retrieval succeeds when cues available at test match those established during encoding. Divers studied word lists either on land or underwater and later recalled them in the same or the other environment; free recall was markedly better when learning and testing contexts matched and fell sharply when they mismatched. Much forgetting thus reflects cue mismatch rather than trace loss—an effect strongest in free recall and weak in recognition, where strong cues are supplied.

已经牢固储存多年的恐惧记忆,为什么在被提取唤起后的短时间内会重新变得不稳定,甚至可能被削弱或改写?

Why does a fear memory stored stably for years briefly become unstable again - open to weakening or even rewriting - right after it is retrieved?

直答:每次提取都会迫使旧记忆再走一遍巩固流程,即再巩固。机理:线索唤起把已稳定的记忆痕迹打回分子层面的不稳定态,须靠新的蛋白质合成才能重新固定;Nader等2000年在Nature发表的大鼠杏仁核实验证明,再激活后阻断蛋白合成可抹除条件性恐惧的行为表达。这个数小时的可塑窗口,正是暴露疗法等干预强调在记忆唤起后及时介入的原因。
Every retrieval forces an old memory through consolidation once more - reconsolidation. A retrieval cue knocks the stable trace back into a molecularly unstable state that must restabilize through new protein synthesis. Nader and colleagues showed in Nature in 2000 that blocking protein synthesis in the rat amygdala after reactivation erased the behavioral expression of conditioned fear. That plastic window of a few hours is why exposure-based interventions stress acting soon after a memory is evoked.

什么是位置记忆法？

What is the method of loci?

位置记忆法是一种古老的记忆技巧，把要记的内容和熟悉的地点（如家里的房间顺序）一一对应，靠在脑中“走一遍”来回忆。古罗马演说家就常用它记住长篇演讲。把抽象信息变成空间画面能提升记忆效果。
The method of loci is an ancient memory technique that links items to be remembered with familiar locations, such as rooms in your home, and recalls them by mentally 'walking' through the space. Roman orators used it to remember long speeches. Turning abstract information into spatial images improves recall.

Why does quizzing yourself beat rereading ten times — what makes the testing effect counterintuitive?

Fluency deceives. Rereading breeds familiarity, and the brain mistakes easy recognition for mastery; retrieval practice — closed-book recall, self-quizzing — forces reconstruction of search pathways, each effortful successful pull strengthening the trace. Roediger and Karpicke showed in 2006 that after one week self-testers retained about fifty percent more than rereaders. Even failed attempts help, paving paths for later learning. The recipe is short: spaced repetition, self-questioning, teaching others; rereading plus highlighters remains the most popular yet least effective pairing.

为什么背完材料后美美地睡上一觉,常常比通宵硬撑记得更牢?

Why does a good night's sleep right after memorizing often lock the material in better than an all-nighter?

直答:睡眠不是学习的暂停键,而是巩固记忆的加工车间。机理:入睡后海马把白天新编码的信息以快速重放的方式转写进新皮层,慢波与睡眠纺锤波的耦合正是这场离线回放的标志;睡眠还切断清醒时源源不断的新干扰,保护脆弱的短期痕迹不被覆盖。通宵硬撑既错过回放窗口又任由干扰累积,所以复习量相同时,睡过一夜的人提取更快也更准。
Sleep is not a pause button for learning but the workshop where memories get consolidated. After falling asleep, the hippocampus replays freshly encoded material into neocortex, with coupling of slow waves and sleep spindles marking this offline rehearsal; sleep also cuts off the constant stream of new waking interference that would overwrite fragile traces. Pulling an all-nighter misses the replay window while interference keeps piling up, so with equal study time those who slept retrieve faster and more accurately.
