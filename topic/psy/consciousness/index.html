“现象意识”和“取用意识”有什么不同？

How do phenomenal and access consciousness differ?

现象意识是“有感受”的主观状态本身；取用意识是该信息能否被报告、推理与控制行为。两者可分离——有些状态你隐隐感到却说不出，也有信息可用却无感受，区分它们有助于厘清意识研究。
Phenomenal consciousness is the subjective state of 'having a feel'; access consciousness is whether that info can be reported, reasoned with and used to act. They can dissociate—some feelings are ineffable, some info is usable without feel—a key distinction in consciousness research.

意识的“困难问题”与“简单问题”有何区别？

How do the 'hard' and 'easy' problems of consciousness differ?

简单问题是如何实现感知、记忆、报告等功能的机制，可用神经科学逐步解答；困难问题是为何这些处理会伴随主观感受（ qualia）。功能可解释，感受却仍无公认答案。
Easy problems are mechanisms for perception, memory and reporting, answerable by neuroscience step by step; the hard problem is why such processing is accompanied by subjective feel (qualia). Function is explainable, feeling is not yet.

整合信息理论（IIT）给了意识什么量化思路？

What quantitative idea does integrated information theory (IIT) offer consciousness?

IIT 用 Φ 度量系统“不可还原的整合程度”，认为意识正比于一个系统整合信息的能力。它预言某些复杂网络有意识、简单并联装置没有，但如何与脑实测对应仍争议巨大。
IIT quantifies consciousness with Φ, the irreducible degree of integration, holding that consciousness scales with a system's capacity to integrate information. It predicts some complex networks are conscious while simple parallel devices are not, but linking Φ to the brain remains hotly debated.

在双眼竞争里,两只眼接收的图像始终不变,而知觉却在两者之间来回切换,为什么这个现象被视为定位意识神经相关物(NCC)的关键杠杆?

In binocular rivalry the two retinal images never change, yet perception alternates between them. Why is this phenomenon regarded as a crucial lever for localizing the neural correlates of consciousness (NCC)?

因为它把恒定的物理输入与变化的主观体验解耦了:凡是放电随知觉而非随刺激翻转的神经元,就是 NCC 的候选者。Logothetis 团队在清醒猕猴上的单细胞记录显示,颞下皮层等高级视觉区的神经元跟随知觉切换发放,而初级视皮层多数细胞仍在追踪刺激本身,说明意识相关活动更多集中在高级表征区。该范式由此成为区分前意识加工与意识通达的标准实验手段。
Rivalry decouples constant physical input from fluctuating experience: any neuron whose firing flips with perception instead of with the stimulus is an NCC candidate. Single-unit recordings in awake macaques by Logothetis's group revealed that inferior-temporal neurons fired in step with perceptual switches while most V1 cells kept tracking the stimulus, placing consciousness-related activity preferentially in higher representational areas. The paradigm thereby became a standard tool for separating preconscious processing from conscious access.

“盲视”现象揭示了意识的什么？

What does blindsight reveal about consciousness?

盲视患者声称看不见视野缺损区的刺激，却能较准地猜出位置或运动方向。说明视觉加工可在无主观视觉体验下进行，把“有信息加工”与“有意识看见”分了家。
Blindsight patients claim not to see stimuli in a blind field yet can accurately guess location or motion. It shows visual processing can occur without conscious visual experience, separating 'information processed' from 'consciously seen'.

全局工作空间理论如何解释意识在信息加工中的角色?

How does global workspace theory explain the role of consciousness in information processing?

该理论由伯纳德·巴尔斯提出,认为脑内存在一个容量有限的全局工作区,进入其中的信息会被广播到众多专门化加工模块。无意识加工是并行且模块化的,而意识通达的标志正是这种串行、面向全脑的广播过程。德阿纳等人随后用fMRI与颅内记录提供支持:意识知觉伴随着顶额网络呈'点燃'式的激活放大。
Proposed by Bernard Baars, the theory holds that the brain contains a capacity-limited global workspace whose contents are broadcast widely to many specialized modules. Unconscious processing runs in parallel and modularly; what marks conscious access is this serial, brain-wide broadcast. Dehaene and colleagues later supported it with fMRI and intracranial recordings showing an 'ignition'-like amplification across frontoparietal networks during conscious perception.
