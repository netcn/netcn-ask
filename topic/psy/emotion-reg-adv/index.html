情绪调节也有“代价”吗——控制情绪会耗资源？

Does emotion regulation have a cost—does controlling feelings drain resources?

情绪调节需认知控制资源；强行压抑负面情绪会占用工作记忆、损害随后任务表现（情绪调节耗损）。这解释了压力大时更难专心、更易出错。
Regulating emotion draws on cognitive-control resources; forcing down negative feeling consumes working memory and impairs later tasks (regulation depletion). It explains why under stress we concentrate worse and err more.

焦虑的“认知模型”怎么解释——为什么越回避越焦虑？

How does the cognitive model of anxiety explain why avoidance fuels anxiety?

认知模型认为焦虑来自对威胁的灾难化解读与“无法应对”的信念；回避虽短期减压，却剥夺了“其实没出事”的校正经验，使恐惧泛化。暴露与重评打破此循环。
The cognitive model sees anxiety as catastrophizing threat plus 'I can't cope' beliefs; avoidance eases fear briefly but denies the corrective lesson that nothing happened, generalizing fear. Exposure and reappraisal break the loop.

反刍（rumination）为什么让低落情绪持续更久？

Why does rumination prolong low mood?

反刍是反复咀嚼“我为什么这么糟/事情多糟”却不求解，会延长负面心境、强化消极自我图式并阻碍问题解决。打断它的办法常是具体行为（运动、做事）而非继续想。
Rumination is chewing 'why am I so bad / why is it so bad' without seeking solutions; it prolongs low mood, strengthens negative self-schemas and blocks problem-solving. Breaking it needs concrete action (exercise, tasks), not more thinking.

什么是“共情的陷阱”——过度共情会耗竭吗？

What is the empathy trap—can too much empathy exhaust you?

共情耗竭发生在长期替他人承压而缺乏边界，常见于照护者。保护做法包括区分“理解”与“承担”、设置情绪界限、用同情（compassion）替代沉浸式共情。
Empathy fatigue arises when someone chronically carries others' distress without boundaries, common in caregivers. Protect by separating understanding from taking on, setting emotional limits, and replacing immersed empathy with compassion.

“情绪劳动”是什么——为什么微笑服务也会累？

What is emotional labor—why is smiling on the job tiring?

情绪劳动指工作中必须按规则表现特定情绪（如客服保持热情），不管真实感受。表层扮演需持续压抑与伪装，易致疏离与耗竭；深层扮演（真调整心态）代价较小。
Emotional labor is displaying required emotions at work (e.g., upbeat service) regardless of true feeling. Surface acting needs constant suppression and faking, breeding detachment and burnout; deep acting (genuinely shifting) costs less.

羞耻（shame）和内疚（guilt）是同一种感受吗？

Are shame and guilt the same feeling?

不同。内疚聚焦“我做了一件坏事”（可修复、促补偿），羞耻聚焦“我这个人很糟”（指向自我、易逃避或攻击）。研究认为羞耻更伤关系与自尊，内疚更能促亲社会修复。
No. Guilt targets 'I did a bad thing' (repairable, prompts repair); shame targets 'I am bad' (self-focused, breeds hiding or aggression). Shame tends to harm relationships and self-worth more, while guilt better drives prosocial repair.

什么是“自我关怀”（self-compassion）？它和自怜、自大有何不同？

What is self-compassion, and how does it differ from self-pity or ego?

自我关怀是在痛苦时以善意对待自己、承认人人都会失败、保持觉察而不淹没。它不同于沉溺的自怜，也不同于自我膨胀，研究关联更低焦虑与更强改变动力。
Self-compassion is treating yourself kindly in pain, seeing failure as shared, and staying mindful without drowning. Unlike self-pity or ego-inflation, it links to lower anxiety and stronger motivation to change.

重新评估（reappraisal）和表达抑制，哪种情绪调节更健康？

Between reappraisal and expressive suppression, which regulation is healthier?

重评是改变对事件的解读（如“这不是威胁而是挑战”），通常减负且不伤社交；表达抑制是压住外显情绪，短期有效却增加生理唤醒、损害记忆与关系。长期看重评更可持续。
Reappraisal changes how you interpret an event ('a challenge, not a threat'), usually lowering distress without social cost; expressive suppression hides outward emotion, works briefly yet raises physiological arousal and harms memory and relationships. Reappraisal is more sustainable.

愤怒来袭时，除了“数到十”还能怎么做？

When anger hits, what else helps besides counting to ten?

愤怒管理先从生理降温（离开现场、慢呼吸、冷水敷脸）再处理认知；也可练习“我语句”表达需求而非指责。关键是给前额叶时间重新上线，而非当场争输赢。
Anger management first cools physiology (leave, slow breathing, cold water on face) then addresses thoughts; use 'I-statements' to state needs, not blame. The point is to let the prefrontal cortex come back online rather than win on the spot.

只会用“好/坏/烦”描述情绪，会带来什么麻烦？

What goes wrong if you only describe feelings as good/bad/annoyed?

情绪粒度低的人难以精确命名感受，更难匹配调节策略，也更易患焦虑、抑郁与冲动发泄。能细分“失望、委屈、嫉妒”更易找到对症的应对。
People with low emotional granularity struggle to name feelings precisely, match regulation strategies, and are more prone to anxiety, depression and impulsive outbursts. Distinguishing disappointment, grievance or envy helps pick the right response.

什么是“积极情绪扩建”理论——好心情不只是结果？

What is the broaden-and-build theory—is good mood only an outcome?

弗雷德里克森提出，积极情绪不只让人舒服，还会“扩建”注意力、思维与社交资源（如玩、探索、联结），长期累积成韧性。所以快乐也是成长的养料，而非奢侈品。
Fredrickson's broaden-and-build theory says positive emotion does more than feel good—it broadens attention, thought and social resources (play, explore, connect), building resilience over time. Happiness is fuel for growth, not a luxury.
