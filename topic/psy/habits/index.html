什么是实施意图（if-then 计划）？为什么比我要努力更有效？

What are implementation intentions (if-then plans) and why beat I'll try hard?

Gollwitzer 提出把目标写成如果出现 X 情境，我就做 Y 行为的具体计划，把触发与行动预先绑定，减少执行时的决策消耗与拖延。研究显示它能显著提升目标达成，尤其在习惯自动化前作为桥梁。
Gollwitzer's if X situation arises, then I do Y pre-binds trigger to action, cutting deliberation and procrastination at execution. Studies show it markedly lifts goal attainment, bridging the gap before habits automate.

自我损耗理论现在还站得住脚吗？

Does the ego depletion theory still hold up?

自我损耗认为意志力的心理能量会被消耗、后续自控力下降。但大规模复现失败与开放科学质疑使其地位动摇；更稳妥的解释是动机与信念（如意志力有限的信念本身）调节努力，而非存在单一能量池。
Ego depletion claimed a limited mental energy for self-control depletes with use. Large failed replications and open-science scrutiny have weakened it; a safer view is that motivation and beliefs (e.g., believing willpower is limited) modulate effort, not a single energy tank.

内在动机和外在动机哪个更好？

Is intrinsic or extrinsic motivation better?

内在动机（因兴趣/意义而为）通常更持久、带来更深投入；外在动机（为奖赏/避免惩罚）短期有效但易依赖。不过外在动机可通过内化转化为自主驱动，关键是让人感到行为与自身价值相连。
Intrinsic motivation (for interest/meaning) is usually more durable and deeply engaging; extrinsic (rewards/punishment) works short-term but breeds dependence. Yet extrinsic can be internalized into autonomous drive—when behavior feels linked to one's values.

自我决定理论认为什么才是真正持久的动力？

What does self-determination theory say is truly lasting motivation?

Deci 与 Ryan 提出，满足自主（autonomy）、胜任（competence）、关系（relatedness）三种基本心理需求时，内在动机最强且最持久；靠外部奖惩驱动的行为一旦奖励撤走就易停止。
Deci and Ryan argue that satisfying autonomy, competence, and relatedness yields the strongest, most lasting intrinsic motivation; externally rewarded behavior often stops once rewards vanish.

目标设定理论为什么强调具体且略有挑战的目标？

Why does goal-setting theory stress specific, moderately challenging goals?

Locke 发现模糊目标（如尽力）效果差，而具体且稍有难度的目标最能提升表现，因为清晰标准指引努力与反馈。但目标过难会挫败，需配合进展反馈与能力匹配。
Locke found vague goals (do your best) underperform; specific, moderately hard goals boost performance most by directing effort and feedback. Too-hard goals frustrate—pair with progress feedback and ability fit.

为什么小奖励能帮我们养成习惯？

Why do small rewards help build habits?

奖励在提示-例行-奖励回路中标记行为值得重复，大脑释放多巴胺强化连接。及时、稳定的小奖励比遥远大奖励更有效，因为大脑更看重当下的正向反馈。
Rewards mark a behavior as worth repeating in the cue-routine-reward loop, with dopamine reinforcing the link. Timely, consistent small rewards beat distant big ones—the brain values immediate positive feedback.

什么是习惯叠加？怎么用它建立新习惯？

What is habit stacking and how do you use it to build new habits?

习惯叠加是把新行为绑在已有稳定习惯之后，例如刷完牙后立刻冥想 1 分钟。借助既有提示降低启动成本，让新例行更容易被触发，是行为设计中的实用技巧。
Habit stacking attaches a new behavior to an existing stable habit—e.g., after brushing teeth, meditate 1 minute. Leveraging the old cue lowers startup cost and makes the new routine easier to trigger—a practical behavior-design trick.

习惯真的 21 天就能养成吗？

Can a habit really form in 21 days?

21 天来自整形外科观察的误传，并非普适规律。Lally 等研究发现，自动化的平均天数约 66 天，且因行为与个体差异从 18 到 254 天不等。耐心与重复比追求天数更重要。
21 days came from a misread of plastic-surgery observations, not a universal rule. Lally et al. found habit automation averaged ~66 days, ranging 18-254 by behavior and person. Patience and repetition matter more than a number.

习惯是怎么形成的？提示-例行-奖励是什么？

How do habits form, and what is cue-routine-reward?

习惯由神经回路在提示（触发）到例行（行为）到奖励（满足）的重复中固化。大脑为省能会把频繁行为自动化，所以改习惯要替换例行或改变提示，而非只靠意志力。
Habits solidify via repeated cue to routine to reward loops. The brain automates frequent behavior to save energy, so changing habits means swapping the routine or cue, not just willpower.

为什么坏习惯那么难改?好习惯怎么才能养成?

Why are bad habits hard to break — how do good habits grow?

习惯是大脑走熟的近路:做得越多,神经通路越宽,自动跑得越欢。改掉它不能靠硬憋,要用新路线替换:想少看电视,就把书放在遥控器旁边。好习惯同理——每天固定时间做一点点,坚持得越久,新路就修得越牢。
Habits are brain shortcuts: the more you repeat, the wider the path and the faster it auto-runs. Breaking them needs replacement, not willpower wrestling — want less TV, park books beside the remote. Good habits work the same: fixed time, tiny dose, and the longer you stick, the stronger the road grows.
