心理治疗中的安慰剂效应是什么？

What is the placebo effect in therapy?

即使“治疗”本身无效，患者的期望、治疗关系与仪式感也能带来真实症状改善，说明心理背景本身就是疗效的一部分。
Even with an inert 'treatment,' a patient's expectation, the therapeutic relationship, and ritual can produce real symptom relief, showing context itself is part of the cure.

什么是认知行为疗法（CBT）？

What is cognitive behavioral therapy (CBT)?

CBT 是一种结构化心理治疗，帮助识别并修正导致痛苦的“自动负面思维”，用更现实的想法改变情绪与行为，对抑郁焦虑证据充分。
CBT is a structured therapy that identifies and rewrites automatic negative thoughts, using more realistic thinking to change mood and behavior; well-evidenced for depression and anxiety.

什么是“心理自助”与何时该求助？

What is 'self-help' and when should one seek help?

心理自助包括规律运动、睡眠、写日记、正念等维护情绪的方法，对轻度困扰有帮助。但当情绪持续两周以上、影响学习工作或人际，或出现自伤念头时，应尽早寻求心理咨询师或医生。求助是勇敢而非软弱的表现。
Self-help includes exercise, sleep, journaling, and mindfulness to maintain mood and helps with mild distress. But when mood lasts over two weeks, disrupts work or relationships, or includes self-harm thoughts, seek a counselor or doctor early. Seeking help is brave, not weak.

什么是抑郁症？

What is depression?

抑郁症是一种常见心境障碍，表现为持续低落、兴趣减退、精力下降，并影响睡眠、食欲和注意力，持续两周以上需重视。它不是“想不开”，而是与脑内化学物质、基因和处境都有关。及时寻求医生或心理咨询非常重要。
Depression is a common mood disorder with persistent low mood, loss of interest, and reduced energy, affecting sleep, appetite, and concentration; lasting over two weeks warrants attention. It is not 'weak will', but linked to brain chemistry, genes, and circumstances. Seeking a doctor or counselor promptly is important.

惊恐发作是怎么回事？

Why do panic attacks happen?

惊恐发作是突然的强烈恐惧伴心悸、窒息感，常由身体感觉被误读为“危险”引发，形成“恐惧恐惧”的循环。
A panic attack is sudden intense fear with racing heart and breathlessness, often triggered when bodily sensations are misread as danger, creating a 'fear of fear' loop.

什么是焦虑？它和正常担心有什么区别？

What is anxiety, and how is it different from normal worry?

焦虑是对未来威胁的过度、持续担忧，常伴心跳加快、坐立不安等身体反应；正常担心通常针对具体事情且会随解决而消失。当焦虑严重影响生活时，可能发展为焦虑障碍，应寻求专业帮助。适度焦虑其实也能提醒我们做好准备。
Anxiety is excessive, persistent worry about future threats, often with a racing heart and restlessness; normal worry is usually about a specific thing and fades when resolved. When anxiety severely disrupts life, it may be an anxiety disorder needing professional help. Moderate anxiety can actually prompt preparation.

什么是正念（mindfulness）？

What is mindfulness?

正念是有意识、不评判地关注当下体验的练习，能降低压力、提升专注，常见形式包括呼吸觉察与身体扫描。
Mindfulness is purposefully paying attention to the present without judgment, reducing stress and improving focus via practices like breath awareness and body scans.

为什么和朋友玩能让人开心？

Why does playing with friends make us happy?

和朋友相处会促使大脑释放催产素和多巴胺等“快乐激素”，让人感到安全和愉悦。社交联系是人类的基本需求，缺位时会让人孤单难过。所以交朋友、多交流对心理健康很重要。
Spending time with friends releases 'happy hormones' like oxytocin and dopamine, bringing safety and joy. Social connection is a basic human need, and its absence brings loneliness. So making friends and talking more is important for mental health.

悲伤和抑郁症有什么区别？

What is the difference between sadness and depression?

悲伤是对具体事件的短暂情绪，会随时间缓解；抑郁是持续两周以上、影响功能的综合状态，常无缘由且伴精力与兴趣丧失。
Sadness is a temporary reaction to a specific event that eases; depression is a sustained (2+ weeks), functioning-impairing state, often without clear cause, with lost energy and interest.

该怎么帮助情绪低落的朋友？

How can we help a friend who feels down?

最重要的是耐心倾听、不急于说教或否定对方的感受，让他感到被理解。可以鼓励对方保持作息、运动和联系专业人士，但不要替他做决定。如果对方有自伤念头，务必尽快联系可信赖的成人或心理援助热线。
The most important thing is to listen patiently without lecturing or dismissing their feelings, so they feel understood. Encourage routines, exercise, and contacting professionals, but don't make decisions for them. If there are thoughts of self-harm, reach a trusted adult or a mental health hotline promptly.

什么是心理韧性（resilience）？

What is psychological resilience?

韧性是从逆境中恢复并适应的能力，受支持性关系、可控感和应对技能影响，且可通过练习提升。
Resilience is the ability to recover and adapt after adversity, shaped by supportive relationships, sense of control, and coping skills—and it can be trained.

什么是心理健康？

What is mental health?

心理健康不只是没有心理疾病，还包括能良好应对压力、与人相处、发挥自身能力并享受生活。它和身体健康一样重要，会随生活变化而波动。保持规律作息、运动和社交都有助于心理健康。
Mental health is not just the absence of illness, but the ability to cope with stress, relate to others, realize one's abilities, and enjoy life. Like physical health, it fluctuates with life changes. Regular sleep, exercise, and social contact support mental health.
