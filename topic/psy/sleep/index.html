默认模式网络与做梦和走神有什么联系？

How is the default mode network linked to dreaming and mind-wandering?

默认模式网络（DMN）在静息、走神、自传式思考时活跃，在 REM 梦中也高度参与。它串联过去经验以构建自我叙事，梦与白日梦可能都是 DMN 主导的离线和自我相关的模拟，有助于整合身份与规划未来。
The default mode network (DMN) activates during rest, mind-wandering, and autobiographical thought, and is heavily engaged in REM dreams. It stitches past experience into a self-narrative; dreams and daydreams may both be DMN-led offline, self-related simulations aiding identity integration and future planning.

为什么有人每天只睡四五小时还精神抖擞，你却做不到？

Why do some people thrive on four or five hours of sleep while others cannot?

极少数“天然短睡者”携带 DEC2 等基因的突变，让他们用更短睡眠获得同等恢复（Fu 等, 2009, Science）。这类变异影响了生物钟与睡眠深度的调控，并非靠意志硬撑；对绝大多数人而言，长期少睡仍会损害记忆与代谢，所以不应盲目模仿。
A tiny fraction of natural short sleepers carry mutations such as DEC2 that let them recover fully on far less sleep (Fu et al., 2009, Science). These variants reshape clock and sleep-depth regulation and are not willpower; for most people chronic short sleep still harms memory and metabolism, so copying them is unwise.

What cognitive ability does lucid dreaming (knowing you are dreaming) reflect?

睡眠与情绪之间是单向还是双向关系？

Is the sleep-emotion link one-way or bidirectional?

二者是双向的：缺觉会放大杏仁核反应、削弱前额叶调控，使负面情绪更难管理；反过来，焦虑抑郁也会破坏睡眠结构（尤其 REM 与深睡）。形成恶性循环，因此改善任一侧都能缓解另一侧。
It is bidirectional: sleep loss amplifies amygdala reactivity and weakens prefrontal control, worsening mood regulation; conversely, anxiety/depression disrupt sleep architecture (especially REM and deep sleep). A vicious cycle—improving either side eases the other.

睡眠如何参与记忆巩固？慢波睡眠与快眼动睡眠分工有何不同？

How does sleep contribute to memory consolidation, and how do slow-wave and REM sleep differ in role?

在慢波睡眠期，海马内白天形成的神经元放电序列会以压缩速度重放，并与皮层慢振荡、丘脑纺锤波耦合，把记忆逐步转移到新皮层实现系统性巩固，这一过程对陈述性记忆尤为关键。快眼动睡眠则与情绪记忆的调节、程序性技能的稳定和记忆间的联结整合关系更密切，有助于提取规则和产生新颖联想。实验上，午睡或整夜睡眠后记忆保持优于等长的清醒时段，而在慢波睡眠中播放学习时的气味或声音线索可以定向增强特定记忆。
During slow-wave sleep, hippocampal firing sequences formed during the day are replayed at compressed speed and coupled with cortical slow oscillations and thalamic spindles, gradually transferring memories to neocortex in what is called systems consolidation, a process especially important for declarative material. REM sleep is more closely tied to regulating emotional memory, stabilising procedural skills, and integrating associations across memories, which supports rule extraction and novel connections. Experimentally, retention after a nap or a night of sleep exceeds an equal period of wakefulness, and replaying an odour or sound cue from learning during slow-wave sleep can selectively strengthen the associated memory.

什么是睡眠惯性？为什么刚醒时昏沉沉？

What is sleep inertia and why are we groggy right after waking?

睡眠惯性指从深睡中被唤醒后持续数分钟到半小时的认知与运动功能低下，因前额叶与警觉系统尚未完全上线。它尤其常见于从慢波睡眠中醒来，可用光照和轻度活动加速消退。
Sleep inertia is impaired cognition and mobility for minutes to half an hour after waking from deep sleep, as prefrontal and alerting systems are not fully online. It is worst when roused from slow-wave sleep; light and light activity speed recovery.

为什么熬夜复习反而考不好？

Why does all-night cramming actually hurt exam performance?

睡眠不足时，注意力、工作记忆和情绪调节都会打折，新知识难以稳定存储，第二天的提取也变差。与其牺牲睡眠硬撑，不如保证充足睡眠再高效复习。规律睡眠本身就是学习计划的一部分。
Sleep deprivation degrades attention, working memory, and emotion regulation; new knowledge doesn't consolidate well and recall suffers the next day. Rather than sacrificing sleep, protect it and study efficiently. Consistent sleep is part of the study plan itself.

Sleepwalking occurs in deep (non-REM) sleep when parts of the brain partially awaken and the motor system activates abnormally, linked to genetics, deprivation, or stress.

什么是睡眠纺锤波？它和记忆有什么关系？

What are sleep spindles and how do they relate to memory?

睡眠纺锤波是 N2 期出现的短暂高频脑电爆发，被认为在丘脑与新皮层间搬运记忆，参与将陈述性记忆从海马转移到长期存储，也与抗干扰和智力相关。
Sleep spindles are brief high-frequency EEG bursts in N2 sleep, thought to shuttle memory between thalamus and cortex, helping transfer declarative memory from hippocampus to long-term store; they also relate to resilience to interference and intelligence.

为什么清醒越久越困，午睡一小会儿却又能“满血”？

Why do we get sleepier the longer we stay awake, yet a short nap can restore us?

波贝利的两过程模型（Borbély, 1982）把睡眠驱动拆成“睡眠稳态压力”和“昼夜节律”两条线。清醒时脑细胞消耗 ATP 释放腺苷，腺苷在脑中累积就像“睡眠压力”的时钟，咖啡因正是堵住腺苷受体让人暂时不困；小睡则部分清掉了这股压力。
Borbély's two-process model (1982) splits sleep drive into a sleep homeostatic pressure and the circadian rhythm. While awake, brain cells burn ATP and release adenosine, which accumulates as a rising sleep-pressure clock; caffeine blocks adenosine receptors to mask it, and a nap partly clears that pressure.

为什么会“倒时差”？

Why does jet lag happen?

跨时区飞行打乱了体内生物钟（昼夜节律）与目的地光暗周期的同步，导致失眠、疲惫和注意力下降，通常需要数天重新校准。
Crossing time zones desynchronizes the body clock (circadian rhythm) from local light-dark cycles, causing insomnia and fatigue that take days to recalibrate.

失眠的首选治疗为什么是认知行为疗法而不是安眠药？

Why is cognitive behavioural therapy, not sleeping pills, the first-line treatment for insomnia?

失眠的认知行为疗法包含睡眠限制、刺激控制、认知重构与睡眠卫生教育，核心是打破在床上清醒焦虑的条件化联结，并重建稳定的睡眠驱动。随机对照试验显示其疗效与镇静催眠药短期相当，但停止治疗后收益能维持数月以上，而药物停用后常出现反弹性失眠与耐受问题。基于此，美国内科医师学会等机构的指南把它列为成年慢性失眠的首选治疗，数字化交付形式也使可及性显著提高。
Cognitive behavioural therapy for insomnia combines sleep restriction, stimulus control, cognitive restructuring, and sleep hygiene education, aiming to break the conditioned association between bed and anxious wakefulness while rebuilding a strong sleep drive. Randomised trials show short-term efficacy comparable to sedative hypnotics, but its benefits persist for months after treatment ends, whereas stopping medication often brings rebound insomnia and tolerance. On that basis guidelines from bodies such as the American College of Physicians name it the first-line treatment for chronic insomnia in adults, and digital delivery has substantially improved access.

为什么青少年晚上睡不着、早上起不来？

Why can't teenagers fall asleep at night and get up in the morning?

进入青春期，人体的生物钟（昼夜节律）会整体后移：褪黑素的分泌高峰来得更晚，所以到点不困。这不是懒，而是发育中的正常变化。很多国家因此呼吁推迟中学上课时间，研究表明学生睡眠多了，出勤、情绪和成绩反而变好。
In adolescence the body clock (circadian rhythm) shifts later: melatonin peaks later, so teens aren't sleepy on schedule. That's normal development, not laziness. Many places now debate later school start times because research shows more sleep improves attendance, mood, and grades.

什么是睡眠瘫痪（鬼压床）？

What is sleep paralysis?

在入睡或醒来时，意识已清醒但身体仍处于 REM 的麻痹状态，短暂无法动弹并常伴压迫感或幻觉，通常几分钟内自行解除。
At sleep onset or waking, consciousness is alert but the body remains REM-paralyzed, causing brief immobility with pressure or hallucinations, usually resolving within minutes.

Why do we dream? What are the main scientific hypotheses?

什么是昼夜节律？它如何控制我们的困倦与清醒？

What is the circadian rhythm and how does it control sleepiness and wakefulness?

昼夜节律是约 24 小时的内源生物钟（下丘脑视交叉上核主导），受光照调节，驱动褪黑素夜间升高、皮质醇晨间升高。它与睡眠压力（腺苷累积）共同决定你何时困、何时清醒。
The circadian rhythm is a ~24-hour internal clock (hypothalamic suprachiasmatic nucleus), light-entrained, driving melatonin up at night and cortisol in the morning. Together with sleep pressure (adenosine buildup), it decides when you are sleepy or alert.

睡眠由哪些阶段组成？REM 和 NREM 有什么不同？

What stages make up sleep, and how do REM and NREM differ?

睡眠在 NREM（1-3 期，深度递增）与 REM 之间循环，每夜约 4-6 个周期。NREM 深睡助身体修复与记忆巩固，REM 伴做梦、脑活跃而肌麻痹，与情绪加工和学习有关。
Sleep cycles between NREM (stages 1-3, deepening) and REM, about 4-6 cycles a night. Deep NREM aids physical repair and consolidation; REM brings vivid dreams, active brain, muscle paralysis, and links to emotion and learning.

为什么周末补觉反而让你周一更困、更暴躁？

Why does catching up on sleep at weekends leave you more tired and irritable on Monday?

“社交时差”指工作日的起床时间与周末自然醒的时间相差太大，使内在生物钟和外界时钟错位（Wittmann 等, 2006）。这种慢性错位像轻度持续倒时差，会打乱褪黑素与皮质醇节律，与注意力下降、情绪变差甚至代谢问题相关。
Social jetlag is the gap between your weekday alarm clock and your natural weekend wake time, misaligning the internal clock with the external one (Wittmann et al., 2006). This chronic mismatch acts like mild persistent travel fatigue, disrupting melatonin and cortisol rhythms and linking to poorer attention, mood and metabolism.

褪黑素是什么？它真的能帮你入睡吗？

What is melatonin and does it really help you sleep?

褪黑素是大脑松果体在黑暗中分泌、标示夜晚到来的激素，主要作用是调节睡眠时机（生物钟），而非直接催眠。对时差和作息紊乱有帮助，但对一般失眠效果有限。
Melatonin is a hormone from the pineal gland released in darkness, signaling night is here. It mainly tunes sleep timing (the clock), not direct sedation. It helps jet lag and shifted schedules more than ordinary insomnia.

睡眠不足为什么让我们难以集中注意力？

Why does sleep loss make concentration so hard?

缺觉会削弱前额叶的执行功能，并让大脑出现短暂微睡眠式注意力中断。反应变慢、易分心、错误率上升，相当于轻度醉酒对认知的影响。
Sleep loss weakens prefrontal executive function and triggers brief microsleep-like attention lapses. Reactions slow, distractibility rises, error rates climb—cognitively akin to mild intoxication.
