吉尔福特的发散思维概念为何被视为创造力研究的基石?

Why is Guilford's concept of divergent thinking considered a cornerstone of creativity research?

在1950年美国心理学会主席就职演讲中,吉尔福特指出传统智力测验几乎不触及创造潜能,由此开启了系统化的创造力实证研究。他将发散思维定义为沿多个方向生成多样答案的加工,并以流畅性、变通性、独创性与精致性作为核心指标。这一框架直接催生了托兰斯创造性思维测验等标准化工具,至今仍是领域测量的主干。
In his 1950 APA presidential address, J. P. Guilford observed that conventional intelligence tests barely touched creative potential, launching systematic empirical study of creativity. He defined divergent thinking as generating varied answers along many directions, indexed by fluency, flexibility, originality, and elaboration. The framework directly inspired standardized tools such as the Torrance Tests of Creative Thinking, still central to measurement in the field.

#创造力 #发散思维

远距离联想测验给出三个词、要求找到一个能同时联系它们的第四个词,这一设计如何体现 Mednick 把创造力视为远距离联结的理论?

The Remote Associates Test gives three words and asks for a fourth connecting them all. How does this design embody Mednick's view of creativity as the forming of remote associations?

Mednick 主张有创造力的人其语义网络的层级组织更扁平,远隔概念更容易被同时激活,RAT 正是把这种能力操作化:例如由“茅屋、瑞士、蛋糕”想到“奶酪”。RAT 得分与顿悟型问题解决及部分现实创造性成就相关,但它测的是朝向唯一答案的聚合式提取,与发散思维测验所测的观念流畅性并不相同。因此它是联想机制的有效指标,却不足以覆盖创造力的全部成分。
Mednick argued that creative individuals organize semantic hierarchies flatter, letting distant concepts co-activate more readily, and the RAT operationalizes exactly this ability, as when cottage, Swiss, and cake yield cheese. RAT scores correlate with insight problem solving and some real-world creative achievement, yet the test taps convergent retrieval toward a single answer, distinct from the ideational fluency measured by divergent-thinking tests. It is therefore a valid index of associative mechanisms but cannot cover every component of creativity.

#远距离联想测验 #聚合思维

发散思维测验测出的表现,为什么常常预测不了现实中的创造性成就?

Why does performance on divergent-thinking tests often fail to predict real-world creative achievement?

这类测验(如列举砖头的多种用途)只捕捉想法生成的流畅性、灵活性与独创性,却无法评估想法的可行性与社会价值,而后者才是创造性成就的关键。贝尔等人的综述指出,测验分数与真实创作成果的相关通常较弱,且高度依赖领域知识,因此它更适合作为能力侧写的一个成分,而不是创造力的单一指标。
Such tasks (like listing alternative uses for a brick) capture only the fluency, flexibility, and originality of idea generation, not the feasibility and social value of ideas, which is what creative achievement actually turns on. Reviews by Baer and colleagues show these scores correlate weakly with real creative products and depend heavily on domain knowledge, so they are better treated as one component of an ability profile than as a single measure of creativity.
