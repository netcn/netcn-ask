边缘型人格障碍（BPD）的主要特征是什么？

What are the main features of borderline personality disorder (BPD)?

BPD 以情绪不稳、强烈而不稳定的人际关系、害怕被抛弃以及冲动自伤行为为特征。患者常伴随不稳定的自我形象和强烈的被遗弃恐惧。辩证行为疗法（DBT）等专门针对情绪调节的干预效果较好。
BPD features unstable mood, intense unstable relationships, fear of abandonment, and impulsive self-harm. Sufferers often have a shifting self-image and strong abandonment fear. Dialectical behavior therapy (DBT) targeting emotion regulation works relatively well.

人格障碍与正常人格差异在哪里？

How does a personality disorder differ from normal personality?

人格障碍是长期、僵化且跨情境的模式，明显偏离文化期望，并导致显著痛苦或功能损害，如边缘型或反社会型。正常人格差异虽多样却不致严重失能。诊断强调“适应不良”与“持久稳定”，而非单纯的性格特别。
A personality disorder is a long, rigid, cross-situational pattern markedly deviating from cultural expectations and causing distress or impairment, such as borderline or antisocial types. Normal personality variation is diverse but not severely disabling. Diagnosis stresses maladaptiveness and stability, not mere uniqueness.
