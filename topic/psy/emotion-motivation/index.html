自我决定理论提出的三种基本心理需求是什么？

What three basic psychological needs does self-determination theory propose?

自我决定理论认为自主性、胜任感与关系感是人的三种基本心理需求。当环境支持这三种需求时，外在动机更易内化，个体的幸福感与持久投入更高。
Self-determination theory holds that autonomy, competence, and relatedness are three basic psychological needs. When the environment supports these needs, extrinsic motivation is more readily internalized and well-being and sustained engagement rise.

基本情绪理论与情绪建构论的核心分歧在哪里？

What is the core disagreement between basic emotion theory and the theory of constructed emotion?

埃克曼等人主张存在若干普遍的基本情绪，各自具有特定的面部表情、生理模式与神经回路，跨文化面部识别研究是主要证据。巴雷特则提出，情绪并非天生的离散类别，而是大脑基于内感受信号、情境与习得的情绪概念实时建构的类别，元分析显示所谓专属的自主神经模式与脑区定位并不稳定。分歧还延伸到方法论：强制选择标签的识别任务可能人为制造了普遍性，而自由标注与跨文化田野研究得到的结果更为多样。
Ekman and colleagues argue for a small set of universal basic emotions, each with a characteristic facial expression, physiological signature, and neural circuitry, with cross-cultural facial recognition studies as the main evidence. Barrett counters that emotions are not innate discrete categories but categories the brain constructs in real time from interoceptive signals, context, and learned emotion concepts, noting that meta-analyses fail to find stable emotion-specific autonomic patterns or dedicated brain regions. The dispute extends to method: forced-choice labelling tasks may manufacture apparent universality, whereas free labelling and cross-cultural field studies yield considerably more variability.

条件性恐惧的习得与消退在杏仁核中分别对应什么过程？

What processes in the amygdala correspond to acquisition and extinction of conditioned fear?

恐惧习得依赖基底外侧杏仁核对条件刺激与无条件刺激联结的编码；消退并非抹除原记忆，而是在前额叶与杏仁核回路中形成竞争性抑制记忆。勒杜用神经解剖研究证实了这两条可分离的通路。
Fear acquisition depends on the basolateral amygdala encoding the link between conditioned and unconditioned stimuli; extinction does not erase the original memory but forms a competing inhibitory memory within prefrontal-amygdala circuits. LeDoux's neuroanatomical work confirmed these separable pathways.

习得性无助的解释在提出五十年后为何被作者自己修正？

Why did the authors themselves revise the account of learned helplessness fifty years on?

最初的动物实验表明，经历无法逃避的电击后，动物在可以逃避的情境中也放弃尝试，被解释为学到了反应与结果无关这一预期。神经生物学研究后来发现，被动本身是面对长期厌恶刺激的默认反应，而背侧中脑背侧被盖与前额叶通路在检测到可控性时会主动抑制这种被动，因此真正被学到的是可控性而不是无助。迈尔与塞利格曼据此重述理论：习得的是控制感，无助是缺乏该学习时的默认状态，这一修正也改变了对抑郁与心理弹性干预的理解。
The original animal experiments showed that after inescapable shock, animals stopped trying to escape even when escape became possible, interpreted as learning the expectation that responses and outcomes are independent. Later neurobiology found passivity is instead the default response to prolonged aversive stimulation, and that circuits involving the dorsal raphe and prefrontal cortex actively suppress it once controllability is detected — so what is learned is control, not helplessness. Maier and Seligman restated the theory accordingly: control is learned and helplessness is the default when that learning is absent, a revision that also reshaped thinking about interventions for depression and resilience.

正念训练的疗效证据到底有多强？

How strong is the evidence for the benefits of mindfulness training?

系统综述显示，正念冥想类项目对焦虑、抑郁与疼痛有小到中等的改善，证据强度在与主动对照条件比较时明显下降，而对注意、物质使用、睡眠与体重等结果的证据不足或不一致。方法学问题包括缺少积极对照、参与者难以盲化、依赖自评量表以及不良反应报告不足。较有说服力的结论是，八周正念减压课程等结构化项目对情绪症状有可重复的适度收益，但把冥想当作广谱疗法或替代循证治疗缺乏根据。
Systematic reviews report small to moderate improvements in anxiety, depression, and pain from mindfulness meditation programmes, with evidence strength dropping noticeably when active control conditions are used, while findings for attention, substance use, sleep, and weight are insufficient or inconsistent. Methodological problems include scarce active controls, the impossibility of blinding participants, reliance on self-report scales, and under-reporting of adverse effects. The defensible conclusion is that structured programmes such as eight-week mindfulness-based stress reduction yield replicable modest benefits for mood symptoms, but treating meditation as a broad-spectrum remedy or a substitute for evidence-based treatment is unsupported.

外部奖励为什么有时会削弱内在动机？

Why can external rewards sometimes undermine intrinsic motivation?

过度理由效应的经典演示是，让本来喜欢画画的幼儿在获得奖状奖励后，自由活动时主动画画的时间反而减少，因为孩子把行为归因于外部奖赏而非兴趣本身。自我决定理论用自主感、胜任感与归属感三种基本需要加以解释：可预期的、以控制为目的的物质奖励削弱自主感，因而降低内在动机；而信息性的正面反馈提升胜任感，通常不产生这种损害。因此实践含义并非取消奖励，而是避免把奖励设计成对行为的控制条件。
The classic demonstration of the overjustification effect had preschoolers who already enjoyed drawing receive certificates for it, after which they spent less free-choice time drawing, because they attributed the activity to the reward rather than to their own interest. Self-determination theory explains this through three basic needs — autonomy, competence, and relatedness: expected tangible rewards used to control behaviour undercut autonomy and thus intrinsic motivation, whereas informational positive feedback enhances competence and generally does no such damage. The practical implication is not to abolish rewards but to avoid designing them as controlling contingencies on behaviour.

詹姆斯–兰格情绪理论的核心观点是什么？

What is the core claim of the James-Lange theory of emotion?

该理论提出我们因哭泣而悲伤、因逃跑而害怕，即身体反应先于情绪体验，情绪是对生理变化的知觉。它把外周生理反馈置于情绪起因的核心，区别于后来的认知理论。
The theory proposes that we feel sad because we cry and afraid because we flee, meaning bodily responses precede emotional experience and emotion is the perception of physiological change. It places peripheral feedback at the core of emotion causation, unlike later cognitive theories.

什么是情绪粒度，它对心理健康有何影响？

What is emotional granularity and how does it affect mental health?

情绪粒度指个体区分和标记精细情绪（如沮丧、懊恼而非笼统的不好）的能力。高情绪粒度者能更精准地选配调节策略，巴瑞特研究发现其与更少的冲动行为和更高的调节效能相关。
Emotional granularity is the ability to discriminate and label fine-grained emotions (such as dejected versus annoyed, rather than a vague bad) . High granularity lets people pick more precise regulation strategies, and Barrett found it correlates with fewer impulsive acts and better regulation.

重新解读情境和压抑表情，哪种情绪调节策略代价更小？

Which emotion regulation strategy costs less: reappraisal or expressive suppression?

格罗斯的过程模型按时间顺序区分调节策略，认知重评发生在情绪反应完全展开之前，通过改变对情境意义的解释来调整情绪；表达抑制发生在反应之后，只压住外显行为。实验与日常记录显示，重评能同时降低主观负性体验与生理唤醒，且不损害记忆和社交互动质量；抑制虽然减少了外部表现，但主观感受未减轻，交感激活反而升高，还会削弱对话中的融洽感与事后记忆。长期使用抑制与较差的心理健康指标相关，因此临床训练通常优先培养重评能力。
Gross's process model orders regulation strategies in time: cognitive reappraisal occurs before an emotional response has fully unfolded and works by changing how the situation is construed, whereas expressive suppression comes afterwards and only restrains outward behaviour. Experiments and daily diary studies show reappraisal lowers both subjective negative experience and physiological arousal without impairing memory or the quality of social interaction, while suppression reduces visible expression yet leaves felt emotion intact, raises sympathetic activation, and degrades rapport and later recall of the conversation. Habitual suppression is associated with poorer mental health indicators, so clinical training generally prioritises building reappraisal skills.

耶克斯–多德森定律如何描述唤醒水平与表现的关系？

How does the Yerkes-Dodson law describe the relation between arousal and performance?

该定律指出表现与生理或心理唤醒呈倒 U 形关系：适度唤醒最佳，过低导致松懈、过高引发焦虑。任务越复杂，达到峰值表现所需的最佳唤醒水平越低。
The law states that performance and arousal follow an inverted-U relationship: moderate arousal is optimal, too little brings lethargy and too much brings anxiety. The more complex the task, the lower the optimal arousal level for peak performance.

内在动机和外在动机分别指什么，过度奖励会有什么后果？

What are intrinsic and extrinsic motivation, and what happens when rewards are overused?

内在动机来自活动本身的乐趣，外在动机来自奖赏或压力。德西发现，对本来有趣的任务提供外部金钱奖励，反而会降低内在动机，即过度正当化效应。
Intrinsic motivation comes from the enjoyment of the activity itself, while extrinsic motivation comes from rewards or pressure. Deci found that offering external monetary rewards for already enjoyable tasks can lower intrinsic motivation, an effect called overjustification.
