什么是“健康素养”？看不懂说明书为何危险？

What is health literacy—why is misreading instructions dangerous?

健康素养是获取、理解并运用健康信息的能力。低素养者更易误用药物、漏诊、住院，是健康不平等的重要中介；清晰沟通与可视化比责怪个人更有效。
Health literacy is accessing, understanding and using health information. Low literacy links to misuse, missed diagnosis and hospitalization—a key mediator of health inequality; clear communication and visuals beat blaming individuals.

什么是“改变阶段模型”（跨理论模型）？

What is the stages-of-change (transtheoretical) model?

它把行为改变分为前意向、意向、准备、行动、维持五阶段。同一人不同行为处于不同阶段，干预要匹配阶段（如对“不想改”者先提意识，而非直接给计划）。
The model stages change as precontemplation, contemplation, preparation, action, maintenance. A person sits in different stages for different habits; match the nudge to the stage—raise awareness before plans for the unwilling.

什么是“健康信念模型”？人为何迟迟不体检？

What is the health belief model—why do people delay check-ups?

该模型说行为取决于对威胁的感知（易感性+严重度）与行动收益/障碍。人们常低估风险、高估麻烦，所以科普要同时降障碍、增易感与效能感。
The model says behavior hinges on perceived threat (susceptibility+severity) vs. action benefits/barriers. People under-rate risk and over-rate hassle, so messaging must cut barriers and raise susceptibility and self-efficacy.

什么是“社会支持”缓冲压力的机制？

How does social support buffer stress?

社会支持从情感（被理解）、信息（建议）、工具（实际帮助）三面缓冲压力，降低皮质醇与心血管反应。质量比数量重要，单向索取反而增负。
Social support buffers stress via emotional (being understood), informational (advice) and tangible (help) routes, lowering cortisol and cardiovascular response. Quality beats quantity; one-way taking backfires.

什么是“实施意向”——“if-then”计划为何管用？

What is an implementation intention—why do if-then plans work?

实施意向把意图写成“当 X 情境，我就做 Y”，把行为预绑到线索，减少临场靠意志。研究显示它能显著提升接种、运动等依从，因把决策提前自动化。
Implementation intentions state 'when X, I'll do Y,' pre-binding behavior to a cue and sparing willpower. They reliably lift adherence (vaccination, exercise) by automating the decision beforehand.

相信“我能做到”本身会影响健康行为吗？——自我效能

Does believing 'I can' affect health behavior—self-efficacy?

班杜拉提出，自我效能是对“我能完成某行为”的信心，常比能力更预测是否行动（如戒烟、运动）。高效能者在挫折中坚持，低效能者易放弃；小胜积累可提升它。
Bandura's self-efficacy is confidence 'I can do this behavior,' often predicting action (quitting, exercising) more than ability. High efficacy persists through setbacks, low gives up; small wins build it.

压力一定有害吗？什么是“压力的有益面”？

Is stress always harmful—what is the beneficial side of stress?

适度压力（良性应激）提升唤醒与专注、促成长；只有长期失控的压力才伤身。把压力当“资源”的人，生理反应更健康、表现更好。
Moderate stress (eustress) boosts arousal and focus and aids growth; only chronic uncontrollable stress harms. Those who view stress as a resource show healthier physiology and better performance.
