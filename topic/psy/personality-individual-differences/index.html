行为遗传学的双生子研究如何估计基因与环境对特质的贡献？

How do behavioral-genetics twin studies estimate genetic and environmental contributions to traits?

通过比较同卵（遗传 100%）与异卵（约 50%）双胞胎在分开或共同抚养下的相似度，可分解遗传率、共享与非共享环境。普洛明等研究显示多数人格特质的遗传率约 40% 到 50%，非共享环境影响更大。
By comparing similarity of monozygotic (100% genetic) and dizygotic (about 50%) twins raised together or apart, researchers decompose heritability, shared environment, and non-shared environment. Plomin and colleagues found most personality traits are about 40 to 50% heritable, with non-shared environment mattering more.

斯皮尔曼的一般智力 g 因素是如何被推断出来的？

How was Spearman's general intelligence factor g inferred?

斯皮尔曼发现不同智力测验得分彼此正相关，于是用因素分析提取出一个共同因子 g，代表一般认知能力。各测验还含特定因子 s，但 g 最能预测学业与职业成就。
Spearman observed that scores on different intelligence tests correlated positively and used factor analysis to extract a common factor g representing general cognitive ability. Each test also has a specific factor s, but g best predicts academic and occupational achievement.

米歇尔的情境与人格之争对人格心理学提出了什么挑战？

What challenge did Mischel's person-situation debate pose to personality psychology?

米歇尔指出跨情境行为一致性远低于预期，强调具体情境与认知情感单元的作用。此后学界转向如果……那么……一致性及特征激活观，调和了特质与情境之争。
Mischel argued that cross-situational behavioral consistency was far lower than assumed, emphasizing specific situations and cognitive-affective units. Later work shifted to if-then consistency and trait-activation views, reconciling the trait-versus-situation debate.

心理学如何区分自恋的两种表现形态？

How does psychology distinguish the two manifestations of narcissism?

坎贝尔与米勒区分了浮夸型自恋（外向、好表现、寻求钦佩）与脆弱型自恋（防御、敏感、易羞耻）。两者都含自我中心，但情绪调节与人际模式不同，且分别与不同风险行为相关。
Campbell and Miller distinguish grandiose narcissism (outgoing, exhibitionistic, admiration-seeking) from vulnerable narcissism (defensive, hypersensitive, easily shamed). Both involve self-centeredness but differ in emotion regulation and interpersonal patterns and link to different risk behaviors.

人格心理学中的大五因素分别指哪五个维度？

What are the five dimensions of the Big Five model in personality psychology?

大五（OCEAN）包括开放性、尽责性、外倾性、宜人性与神经质。麦克雷与科斯塔通过词汇学与问卷研究确立了这一跨文化稳定结构，常用于预测工作与关系结果。
The Big Five (OCEAN) are Openness, Conscientiousness, Extraversion, Agreeableness, and Neuroticism. McCrae and Costa established this cross-culturally stable structure through lexical and questionnaire research and it is widely used to predict work and relationship outcomes.

加德纳的多元智力理论列举了哪些类型的智力？

What kinds of intelligence does Gardner's multiple intelligences theory list?

加德纳提出智力是相对独立的多种能力，最初包括语言、逻辑数学、空间、音乐、身体动觉、人际与内省等。该理论强调单一智商无法概括个体优势，常用于教育差异化。
Gardner proposed that intelligence consists of relatively independent abilities, initially including linguistic, logical-mathematical, spatial, musical, bodily-kinesthetic, interpersonal, and intrapersonal intelligences. The theory stresses that a single IQ cannot capture individual strengths and is used in differentiated education.

为什么尽责性被认为与更长的寿命相关？

Why is conscientiousness thought to be associated with longer life?

高尽责者更规律地作息、就医、规避风险行为。弗里德曼等人的长期追踪显示，尽责性可预测更低的全因死亡率，部分通过健康行为与慢性病管理中介。
Highly conscientious people keep more regular routines, seek medical care, and avoid risky behaviors. Friedman and colleagues' long-term follow-up showed conscientiousness predicts lower all-cause mortality, partly mediated by health behaviors and chronic-disease management.

儿童气质与成人人格有何联系？

How is childhood temperament linked to adult personality?

气质是先天的、生物基础的情绪与活动反应倾向（如行为抑制性），人格是其受经验塑造后的发展结果。卡根研究发现婴儿期抑制性气质可部分预测儿童期的内向与焦虑倾向。
Temperament is the inborn, biologically based tendency to react emotionally and behaviorally (such as behavioral inhibition), while personality is its experience-shaped developmental outcome. Kagan found that infant inhibited temperament partly predicts later childhood introversion and anxiety.
