“掌握目标”与“表现目标”有何不同？动机取向如何影响学习？

How do mastery and performance goals differ—how does orientation affect learning?

掌握目标者关注“学会没”，敢犯错、用深层策略；表现目标者关注“显得聪明”，避挑战、遇挫易弃。课堂氛围与评价方式会引导其中一种取向。
Mastery-goal students care 'did I learn,' risk errors and use deep strategies; performance-goal students care 'look smart,' avoid challenge and quit on failure. Classroom climate and grading steer which orientation forms.

什么是教育加速（educational acceleration），“数学早慧青年研究”（SMPY）数十年的追踪数据如何评价它对高能力学生的效果？

What is educational acceleration, and how have decades of longitudinal data from the Study of Mathematically Precocious Youth (SMPY) evaluated its effects on highly able students?

SMPY由Julian Stanley于1971年在约翰斯·霍普金斯大学创立，1990年代初起由Lubinski与Benbow在范德堡大学接续，已追踪数千名能力居前1%的青少年。数据显示，跳级、提前入学等加速措施与更高的博士学位获得率、更多的高影响力论文和专利相关，且未发现社交情绪适应受损的可靠证据。这挑战了“加速会妨碍儿童社会化”的传统顾虑，支持按认知准备度而非年龄安排课程。
Founded by Julian Stanley at Johns Hopkins University in 1971 and continued from the early 1990s by Lubinski and Benbow at Vanderbilt, SMPY has followed thousands of adolescents scoring in the top 1% of ability. Its data link acceleration—grade skipping, early college entrance—to higher rates of doctorates, influential publications and patents, with no reliable evidence of impaired social-emotional adjustment. This challenges the traditional worry that acceleration harms socialization and supports matching curricula to cognitive readiness rather than age.

为什么“先测验再学”有时更好——前测效应？

Why can testing before learning help—the pre-testing effect?

在正式学之前先来一轮困难但无害的预测试，会激活相关知识、标示空白，使后续学习更有针对性。即使答错，提取尝试本身已铺路。
A hard but harmless pre-test before learning activates prior knowledge and flags gaps, making later study targeted (pre-testing effect). Even wrong answers lay groundwork via the retrieval attempt.

什么是“最近发展区”？好老师如何“搭脚手架”？

What is the zone of proximal development—how do scaffolds help?

维果茨基提出，孩子能在他人帮助下做到、却还不能独立做到的部分叫最近发展区。脚手架是逐步撤除的提示与支持，让学习者从依赖走向独立。
Vygotsky's zone of proximal development is what a learner can do with help but not alone. Scaffolding is prompts and support withdrawn step by step, moving from dependence to independence.

高效学习的三大“必要难度”怎么搭配使用？

How do the three desirable difficulties combine for efficient learning?

研究推荐的配方是：学后尽快自测（检索）、拉开复习间隔（间隔）、把相关题型混练（交错）。三者叠加比单用任一种更接近长期掌握。
The evidence-backed recipe: self-test soon after learning (retrieval), space the reviews (spacing), and mix related problem types (interleaving). Stacking all three beats any alone for lasting mastery.

什么是“测试焦虑”？它怎样拖垮表现？

What is test anxiety—how does it sabotage performance?

高度考试焦虑占用工作记忆、引发灾难化思维与生理唤醒，使已会的知识提取不出。降低法含充分模拟、放松训练与把焦虑重评为“准备信号”。
High test anxiety hijacks working memory and sparks catastrophic thoughts and arousal, blocking retrieval of known material. Relief includes realistic practice, relaxation, and reappraising anxiety as a readiness signal.

小孩“假装玩”只是玩吗？它对认知有什么用？

Is pretend play just play—what does it do for cognition?

假装游戏（过家家）锻炼符号思维、换位思考与执行功能：孩子把积木当蛋糕，练习“代表”与“抑制真实”。它是早期抽象与社会的训练场。
Pretend play (house) trains symbolic thought, perspective-taking and executive function: a block becomes a cake, practicing 'represent' and 'inhibit real.' It is early drill for abstraction and social skill.

表扬“聪明”和表扬“努力”，长期影响有何不同？

Does praising smart vs effort differ in the long run?

夸“聪明”易让孩子怕失败、回避挑战（固定型）；夸“努力/策略”鼓励坚持与投入（成长型）。关键是表扬具体过程，而非天赋或结果。
Praising 'smart' makes kids fear failure and avoid challenge (fixed); praising effort/strategy encourages persistence (growth). Key is to praise the specific process, not talent or outcome.
