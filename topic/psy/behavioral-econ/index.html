“双曲线贴现”如何解释我们明知道却还是拖延？

How does hyperbolic discounting explain why we procrastinate despite knowing better?

人对近期奖励过度偏好、对远期奖励迅速贬值，其贴现曲线是双曲线而非指数。于是当下轻松总是赢过未来收益，导致存钱难、健身荒——解决靠缩短反馈距离（小目标、即时反馈）。
People over-prefer near rewards and steeply devalue distant ones along a hyperbolic curve, not exponential. So immediate ease beats future gain, causing poor saving and skipped workouts; fixes shorten feedback distance via small goals and instant cues.

#双曲线贴现 #拖延 #跨期

一项被广泛引用的研究发现,以色列法官的有利假释裁定比例在餐歇后跳升、随庭审推进一路下滑,作者将其归因于决策疲劳。该研究的核心主张是什么,其证据又有哪些方法学软肋?

A widely cited study found Israeli judges' favorable parole rulings spiked after meal breaks and slid steadily as sessions wore on, which the authors attributed to decision fatigue. What does the study claim, and what are the methodological weak points of its evidence?

主张:裁决依赖有限的认知资源,连续权衡使法官疲惫,便退回最省力的默认选项——驳回申请、维持现状。证据:Danziger等分析千余次庭审发现,有利裁定占比在早晚餐歇后骤升、其后逐案递减。软肋:案件排布并非随机,案型轻重、律师水平与相邻案件的相关都可能伪造出这种节律;后续大样本重分析大多未能复现,效应更可能来自序列混杂而非纯粹的资源耗竭,故宜当作警示线索而非定论。
Claim: rulings run on a finite cognitive resource, so hours of weighing cases exhaust judges into the least-effort default of denying parole. Evidence: analyzing over a thousand hearings, Danziger and colleagues found favorable rulings surged after morning and lunch breaks and declined case by case. Weak points: dockets were not randomized, so case severity, counsel quality, and correlation between neighboring files could forge the rhythm; later large-scale reanalyses mostly failed to replicate it. The effect more likely reflects ordering artifacts than pure depletion, so it stands as a cautionary lead rather than settled fact.

为什么曾被广泛引用的自我损耗效应,在横跨二十三个实验室的预注册重复中几乎降到零点?

Why did the once widely cited ego-depletion effect collapse to nearly zero in a preregistered replication spanning twenty-three laboratories?

直答:早期效应量很可能被系统性高估,真实效应即使存在也远小于最初报告。机理:Hagger团队协调二十多个实验室、两千余名被试,用统一的经典自控任务范式做预注册重复,结果效应接近零,置信区间排除了文献中的典型大小;小样本、分析灵活性与发表偏倚被视为高估来源。动机与信念等调节因素尚存争议,但把自控力当作会耗尽的单一燃料池,已经缺乏稳健的证据支持。
Early effect sizes were likely inflated systematically, and any real effect is far smaller than first reported. Hagger's team coordinated over twenty laboratories and more than two thousand participants in a preregistered repetition of a unified classic self-control paradigm; the effect landed near zero, with confidence intervals excluding typical published magnitudes. Small samples, analytic flexibility, and publication bias are blamed for the inflation. Moderators like motivation and belief remain debated, yet treating willpower as one depletable fuel tank no longer rests on robust evidence.

金融市场里的“羊群效应”有什么心理基础？

What psychological basis underlies herding in financial markets?

羊群效应来自社会证明（别人买必有理）、损失厌恶与对不确定性的回避，个体会放弃独立判断跟随群体。它放大泡沫与踩踏，说明市场并非总是理性有效。
Herding stems from social proof (others must know), loss aversion and aversion to uncertainty, leading individuals to abandon independent judgment. It amplifies bubbles and crashes, showing markets are not always rationally efficient.

最后通牒博弈说明了人有“公平感”还是只图利？

Does the ultimatum game show we value fairness or just profit?

在最后通牒博弈中，接受方常拒绝明显不公的分配，哪怕自己也会损失——说明人愿意花钱惩罚不公平。这表明经济行为深受公平规范与情绪驱动，而非纯自利。
In the ultimatum game, responders often reject unfair splits even at personal cost, showing willingness to pay to punish unfairness. Economic behavior is driven by fairness norms and emotion, not pure self-interest.

为什么选择太多反而让人更不快乐？

Why can too many choices make us less happy?

“选择过载”指选项过多会增加决策疲劳、提高预期并放大后悔，反而降低满意度与行动力。适度限制选项、提供推荐往往比海量选择更让人满意。
Choice overload means too many options raise decision fatigue, inflate expectations and amplify regret, lowering satisfaction and action. Limiting options or offering guidance often pleases more than sheer abundance.

#选择过载 #决策疲劳 #满意度

在商场里精心比较挑选了一堆商品的人,出门后反而更难抵挡巧克力曲奇的诱惑。请结合实验证据说明,做选择本身会如何影响随后的自控?

Someone who has spent a shopping trip carefully comparing products comes out less able to resist chocolate-chip cookies. Drawing on experimental evidence, what does the very act of choosing do to the self-control available afterward?

直答:做选择本身就会消耗自控所依赖的资源。机理:Vohs等2008年的系列实验发现,先连续在多个商品间取舍的被试,随后把手浸入冰水的坚持时间更短、更容易向甜食屈服;对真实商场顾客的追踪也呈现同样模式。原因在于每一次取舍都要压住落选方案的吸引力并承担选错的风险,几轮下来资源见底,再遇诱惑时能用来踩刹车的储备已所剩无几。
Choosing itself drains the resource that self-control runs on. Across a 2008 series of experiments, Vohs and colleagues found that people first made to trade off among many products kept their hands in ice water for less time and caved to sweets more easily, and shoppers tracked in a real mall fitted the same pattern. Every trade-off means suppressing the pull of rejected options while shouldering the risk of a bad pick, so after a few rounds little reserve remains for braking against the next temptation.

#决策疲劳 #自我损耗 #消费者行为

为什么人在连着做了一长串决定之后,明明没打算购物,也更容易一时冲动把钱花出去?

After making a long string of decisions, why do people become more likely to spend impulsively even when shopping was never the plan?

直答:先前的连续选择消耗了自控资源,抑制购买冲动的力量随之下降。机理:Vohs与Faber让部分被试先完成耗资源的自控任务,随后他们在模拟购买情境中报告更强的购买欲、愿出更高的价、买得更多;决策疲劳削弱了对占有冲动的抑制,也让注意更容易被诱人商品捕获,价格权衡让位于即时欲望,冲动消费因此上升。
Self-control resources drained by earlier choices weaken the brake on buying impulses. Vohs and Faber had some participants first perform effortful self-control tasks; afterward, in simulated purchasing situations they reported stronger urges to buy, offered higher prices, and bought more items. Decision fatigue erodes inhibition of acquisition impulses and lets attention latch onto tempting products, so price evaluation gives way to immediate desire and impulsive spending climbs.

什么是“心理账户”？为什么钱不是一律等价？

What is mental accounting, and why isn't money fungible to us?

人们把钱按来源或用途分进不同“账户”，对不同账户的花法心态不同（奖金敢花、工资舍不得）。这种非理性分账会扭曲消费与储蓄决策，是行为经济的核心发现之一。
People split money into mental accounts by source or purpose and treat them differently (freely spend a bonus, hoard salary). This irrational partitioning distorts spending and saving—a core behavioral-economics finding.

什么是“禀赋效应”——为什么拥有后就舍不得卖？

What is the endowment effect—why do we overvalue what we own?

一旦拥有了某物，我们给它的估价往往高于未拥有时，源于损失厌恶与所有权依恋。它解释了为何清仓、退换货和谈判中人们难以接受公平价。
Once we own something, we value it above what we would pay to acquire it, driven by loss aversion and ownership attachment. It explains why clearing clutter, returns and negotiations feel so hard at fair prices.

谈判里随便给个数字，为什么会影响最终结果？

Why does any opening number in negotiation skew the final outcome?

这就是锚定效应：先出现的不相关数字会成为后续判断的参照点，人们调整不足。即便明知数字随意，报价仍会把对方拉向自己一侧，所以谈判开局要慎给锚。
This is anchoring: an initially presented irrelevant number becomes a reference point and people adjust insufficiently. Even knowing the number was random, offers pull the other side toward it, so openings should set anchors carefully.

什么是“现状偏差”？为什么我们懒得改变？

What is status quo bias, and why do we resist change?

人倾向于维持当前选项，哪怕替代方案更优，因为改变带来决策成本、损失厌恶与禀赋感。它体现在默认选项被大量选中的现象，也常被用于器官捐献等公共政策设计。
People favor the current option even when alternatives are better, due to decision costs, loss aversion and a sense of ownership. It appears as massive uptake of default choices and is used in policy design like organ-donation enrollment.
