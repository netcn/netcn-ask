计划行为理论如何预测并改变健康行为？

How does the theory of planned behavior predict and change health behavior?

该理论以行为态度、主观规范与感知行为控制共同决定行为意向，再由意向预测实际行为。干预可通过改变这些前因信念来推动锻炼、戒烟等健康行为，其中感知控制常是瓶颈。
The theory of planned behavior holds that attitudes, subjective norms, and perceived behavioral control shape behavioral intention, which predicts action. Interventions shift these antecedent beliefs to promote exercise or smoking cessation, with perceived control often the bottleneck.

习惯形成的神经机制，特别是「习惯回路」是怎样的？

What are the neural mechanisms of habit formation, especially the habit loop?

习惯形成涉及从目标导向的背内侧纹状体向习惯化的背外侧纹状体的控制转移，伴随基底节中「提示-行为-奖赏」回路的固化。一旦自动化，行为由情境线索直接触发，不再依赖结果评估。
Habit formation involves a shift from goal-directed dorsomedial to habit-based dorsolateral striatum control, consolidating a cue-behavior-reward loop in the basal ganglia. Once automated, context cues trigger behavior without outcome evaluation.

心理「复原力」包含哪些保护性因素？

What protective factors does psychological resilience involve?

复原力指个体在逆境中维持或恢复心理功能的能力，其保护因素包括稳定的社会支持、有效的情绪调节策略、自我效能感与意义感。它并非固定特质，而可在关系中培养与发展。
Resilience is the capacity to maintain or regain functioning under adversity, protected by stable social support, effective emotion regulation, self-efficacy, and sense of meaning. It is not a fixed trait but can be built through relationships.

慢性压力如何通过 HPA 轴影响身体健康？

How does chronic stress affect physical health through the HPA axis?

长期压力持续激活下丘脑-垂体-肾上腺轴，使皮质醇长期偏高，进而削弱免疫、扰乱睡眠并损伤海马。这种「劳损」效应将心理压力转化为心血管与代谢疾病的生理风险。
Chronic stress keeps the hypothalamic-pituitary-adrenal axis activated, sustaining high cortisol that suppresses immunity, disrupts sleep, and damages the hippocampus. This allostatic load translates psychological stress into cardiovascular and metabolic risk.

睡眠与心理健康之间存在怎样的双向关系？

What is the bidirectional relationship between sleep and mental health?

睡眠不足会放大情绪反应、削弱前额叶调控并升高焦虑与抑郁风险；反过来，抑郁与焦虑也常伴失眠。改善睡眠常能缓解情绪症状，使其成为心理干预的重要切入点。
Poor sleep amplifies emotional reactivity, weakens prefrontal control, and raises anxiety and depression risk, while those disorders also commonly cause insomnia. Improving sleep often eases mood symptoms, making it a key intervention target.

社会支持与身体健康之间有哪些可验证的关联？

What verifiable links exist between social support and physical health?

纵向研究显示，较强的社会联结与更低的全因死亡率、更好的心血管恢复及更低的炎症水平相关。社会隔离则被比作与吸烟相当的死亡风险因素，提示关系本身就是健康资源。
Longitudinal studies link stronger social ties to lower all-cause mortality, better cardiovascular recovery, and reduced inflammation. Social isolation rivals smoking as a mortality risk factor, suggesting relationships are themselves health resources.

自我效能感如何影响个体的行为改变？

How does self-efficacy influence behavior change?

班杜拉提出自我效能感指对能否完成特定行为的信念，高自我效能者更愿发起并坚持困难目标，在挫折中更快恢复。它是健康行为改变模型中的关键中介变量，可通过掌握性体验提升。
Bandura's self-efficacy is belief in one's ability to perform a behavior; higher efficacy predicts initiating and persisting at hard goals and faster recovery from setbacks. It is a central mediator in behavior-change models, raised through mastery experiences.

健康信念模型包含哪些核心构成，如何解释健康行为？

What are the core components of the health belief model, and how does it explain health behavior?

健康信念模型认为人们采取预防行为取决于对疾病严重性与易感性的感知、对行动收益与障碍的权衡，以及提示行动线索与自我效能。它常用于设计疫苗接种与筛查的促进行为。
The health belief model explains preventive action through perceived severity and susceptibility, benefits versus barriers, cues to action, and self-efficacy. It is widely used to design vaccination and screening campaigns.
