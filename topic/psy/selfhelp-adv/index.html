什么是“认知行为三角”——思维、情绪、行为怎么互转？

What is the cognitive triangle, and how do thought, emotion and behavior feed each other?

CBT 模型里三者互相影响：灾难化想法导致焦虑导致回避，而改任一边都会牵动其余。自助进阶可从记录自动思维、检验、换行动的小循环练起，比空想更有效。
In the CBT model the three loop: catastrophic thought brings anxiety brings avoidance, and changing any one moves the others. Advanced self-help practices the small loop of logging automatic thoughts, testing and changing actions, better than blank thinking.

“自我同情”包含哪三个部分？它和“可怜自己”有何不同？

What three parts make up self-compassion, and how is it different from pitying yourself?

Neff 提出三成分：正念（不淹没情绪）、共通人性（承认人人会失败）、自我友善（像对好友般温和）。它不等于自怜或放任，而是用善意面对失误，从而腾出空间去调整行为。
Neff's three parts: mindfulness (not drowning in emotion), common humanity (everyone fails), self-kindness (gentle as to a friend). It is not self-pity or indulgence but facing mistakes with kindness, freeing space to adjust behavior.

什么是“元认知”——知道自己在“怎么想”的能力？

What is metacognition, the ability to know how you are thinking?

元认知是对思考的思考（如我现在的念头只是担心，不是事实）。提升元认知能让你不被念头带着跑；正念与认知去融合都训练这一点，是自助进阶的核心肌肉。
Metacognition is thinking about thinking, like this thought is just worry, not fact. Raising it stops you being dragged by thoughts; mindfulness and cognitive defusion train it, the core muscle of advanced self-help.

什么是“接纳承诺疗法”（ACT）？它和“解决问题”矛盾吗？

What is Acceptance and Commitment Therapy, and does it conflict with problem-solving?

ACT 先接纳无法立刻改变的感受，再据价值采取行动，而非先消灭情绪再生活。它与解决问题不矛盾：对可控的解决，对不可控的接纳，双轨并行反而减少内耗。
ACT first accepts feelings that cannot change at once, then acts on values rather than waiting to kill the emotion. It does not conflict with solving problems: address what is controllable, accept what is not, in parallel, which cuts inner struggle.

“成长型思维”被误用成“只要努力就能”吗？真正的做法是什么？

Is growth mindset misused as just try hard and you can, and what is the real practice?

成长型思维（德韦克提出）是说能力可以通过练习提升，但它常被误读成“只要拼命努力就一定能成功”。真正做法是：相信能力可发展，同时讲究方法和策略，从失败和反馈中学习，并把努力用在更聪明的地方。努力重要，但方向、资源和现实限制同样关键。
Growth mindset (Dweck) means abilities can be developed through practice, but it is often misread as 'try hard and you'll surely succeed.' The real practice is to believe skills can grow while also using good strategies, learning from failure and feedback, and directing effort wisely. Effort matters, but direction, resources, and real constraints matter too.

什么是“心流”的进阶用法——如何把它变成日常状态？

What is the advanced use of flow, and how to make it a daily state?

心流是全身投入、忘记时间的精神状态，前提是挑战难度和自身技能相匹配。想把它变成日常，可以：把任务拆成清晰的小目标、设定即时反馈、主动调节难度、减少干扰，并建立固定的进入状态的小仪式（如固定时间地点）。关键在于让挑战略高于当前能力，又不太高而让人挫败。
Flow is a state of total absorption where you lose track of time, occurring when challenge and skill are matched. To make it a daily habit, break tasks into clear small goals, seek immediate feedback, tune the difficulty, cut distractions, and build a ritual that signals 'start' (same time, same place). The key is keeping challenge just above your current ability—without tipping into frustration.

为什么“行为激活”有时比“先想通再动”更管用？

Why does behavioral activation sometimes beat thinking it through before acting?

抑郁时常卡在没动力、不动、更低落的循环；行为激活主张先安排小行动（散步、回消息），用行动撬动情绪，而非等感觉好了才动。动起来本身就会反馈给大脑我能。
Depression traps in no-motivation, no-action, lower-mood; behavioral activation schedules small acts first (a walk, a reply) to move emotion via action rather than waiting to feel ready. Acting itself tells the brain I can.

为什么“暴露疗法”能治恐惧？原理是什么？

Why does exposure therapy cure fear, and what is the principle?

恐惧靠回避维持；渐进、安全地接触害怕情境且不发生可怕后果，会让大脑消退旧的条件反应。关键在不逃以让学习发生，需在安全节奏下进行。
Fear is maintained by avoidance; gradual safe contact with the feared situation, without the dreaded outcome, lets the brain extinguish the old conditioned response. The key is not fleeing so learning occurs, at a safe pace.

进阶自助里，怎么用“找证据”来松动一个钻牛角尖的念头？

In advanced self-help, how do you use evidence to loosen a stuck thought?

认知重构的实操是：把自动念头写下，问支持与反对的证据是什么、有没有更平衡的解读。例如我全搞砸了经检验常变成这件事没做好，但其他还行，情绪随之松动。
Cognitive restructuring in practice: write the automatic thought, ask what evidence supports or opposes it and what a balanced reading is. I ruined everything often becomes this went wrong but other things are fine, and the feeling loosens.

为什么“价值澄清”比“设目标”更能长期坚持？

Why does values clarification sustain effort better than just setting goals?

目标是做什么，价值是为什么重要；当行动连回我想成为怎样的人，意志耗尽时仍有内在锚点。写下这对我意味着什么能把外在任务变内在动力。
Goals are what to do, values why it matters; when action ties to who I want to be, an inner anchor remains after willpower fades. Writing what this means to me turns external tasks into internal drive.

自助卡住时，什么时候该找专业人士而不是自己扛？

When self-help stalls, when should you see a professional instead of coping alone?

当情绪持续数周、影响睡眠、工作、关系，或出现自伤念头，说明超出自助范围，应寻求心理咨询或医疗评估。求助是能力而非软弱，越早越好。
When mood lasts weeks and hits sleep, work or relationships, or self-harm thoughts appear, it exceeds self-help; seek counseling or medical assessment. Asking for help is capacity not weakness, and earlier is better.
