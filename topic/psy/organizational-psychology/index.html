为什么同样加班，有人顶得住，有人很快垮掉？

Why can some people handle the same overtime while others break quickly?

职业压力研究（如Karasek的“要求—控制模型”）发现，真正有害的不是工作量本身，而是“高要求＋低控制”的组合：任务多但几乎没有决策权时，健康风险最大。社会支持（同事和上级的理解）能显著缓冲压力。所以“扛得住”的人往往不是更拼命，而是拥有更多资源：能调整节奏、有人分担、知道自己有选择。
Job-strain research (e.g., Karasek's demand–control model) finds the real hazard isn't workload itself but “high demand plus low control”: heavy demands with little decision latitude carry the greatest health risk. Social support from colleagues and supervisors buffers much of this. So people who “hold up” usually aren't tougher — they have more resources: adjustable pace, people to share with, and a felt sense of choice.

为什么一个看起来很聪明的团队，会集体做出愚蠢的决定？

Why do smart teams sometimes make collectively foolish decisions?

这叫“群体思维”：团队为了维持表面一致而压抑异议。当凝聚力强、领导强势、外部压力大时，成员会自我审查、随声附和，把“一团和气”误当成“达成共识”，还不自觉地低估风险和对手。破解方法包括指定“魔鬼代言人”故意唱反调、领导最后发言、用匿名方式收集意见，让不同声音有制度性的出口。
That's groupthink: a team suppresses dissent to preserve harmony. With high cohesion, a dominant leader, and external pressure, members self-censor and go along, mistaking politeness for consensus while underestimating risks and rivals. Remedies include appointing a devil's advocate, having the leader speak last, and gathering anonymous input so dissenting voices have an institutional outlet.

为什么有的领导让人心甘情愿追随，有的只靠命令？

Why do some leaders inspire willing followers while others rely on orders?

领导力研究区分交易型领导（用奖惩交换）与变革型领导（描绘愿景、以身作则、关注成员成长）。变革型领导与更高的投入和满意度相关；“魅力”更像结果而不是原因。真正让人追随的，常常是言行一致、值得信任、把团队利益放在心上的行为，而不是头衔本身。
Leadership research contrasts transactional leaders (exchanging rewards and punishments) with transformational ones (articulating vision, modeling values, developing people). Transformational leadership predicts higher engagement and satisfaction; “charisma” looks more like an outcome than a cause. What earns willing followers is consistent, trustworthy, team-serving behavior — not the title itself.

为什么有些工作越做越有劲，有些越做越没意思？

Why do some jobs get more engaging while others drain all meaning?

根据自我决定理论，当工作满足三种基本需求时，内在动力最旺盛：自主（有选择空间）、胜任（有成长和掌握感）、归属（有好的人际关系）。工资很重要，但长期来看，能掌控节奏、学到东西、和同事处得来，才让人真正投入。这就是为什么“有意思的工作”不等于“钱多的工作”。
According to self-determination theory, intrinsic motivation thrives when three basic needs are met: autonomy (having choice), competence (growing and mastering), and relatedness (good connections). Pay matters, but long-term engagement comes from controlling your pace, learning new things, and getting along with colleagues. That's why “meaningful work” isn't the same as “high-paying work.”

为什么团队里总有人“摸鱼”，干活儿的总是那几个人？

Why do some team members slack off while others do the heavy lifting?

这叫“社会懈怠”：当个人贡献难以被区分时，个体在群体中会不自觉地减少付出。好消息是，当任务重要、成员关系紧密、彼此能看到贡献时，懈怠会明显减少。把大目标拆成可辨认的个人任务、及时反馈进度，是提升团队效率的实用办法。
That's social loafing: when individual contributions are hard to distinguish, people unconsciously reduce effort in a group. The good news: loafing drops when the task matters, members are close, and contributions are visible. Breaking big goals into identifiable individual tasks with regular feedback is a practical fix.

为什么工作久了，会从“热爱”变成“麻木”？

Why does a loved job turn into numbness over time?

职业倦怠不是“懒”或“不上进”，而是长期工作压力下的综合反应：情绪耗竭（累到心空）、去人格化（对服务对象越来越冷漠）、成就感下降。常见压力源有过载、缺乏控制、不公平和缺少支持。恢复需要调整负荷、找回自主感、保证睡眠和休息；若持续严重影响生活，寻求专业帮助是明智的选择。
Burnout isn't laziness — it's the accumulated result of chronic work stress: emotional exhaustion (running on empty), depersonalization (growing distant from the people you serve), and reduced accomplishment. Typical drivers include overload, low control, unfairness, and scarce support. Recovery involves adjusting load, restoring autonomy, and protecting sleep and rest; if it persistently disrupts life, seeking professional help is wise.
