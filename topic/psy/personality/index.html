为什么一想到“自己会死”，人就容易更捍卫自己的文化和攻击异见？

Why does being reminded of our own death make people cling harder to their culture and lash out at dissent?

“恐惧管理理论”（Greenberg 等, 1990）认为对死亡的觉察会触发存在性焦虑，于是人靠坚守文化价值观与自尊来对抗它。实验里被提示死亡的人，会更褒奖符合自身价值观者、贬低异见者，说明对不朽符号的追求是行为的隐形引擎。
Terror Management Theory (Greenberg et al., 1990) holds that awareness of death stirs existential anxiety, so people defend cultural values and self-esteem to counter it. In experiments, those reminded of death reward value-conforming others and punish dissenters, revealing a hidden engine behind our quest for symbolic immortality.

人格在成年后是固定不变，还是可以慢慢改变？

Does personality stay fixed in adulthood or can it slowly change?

研究表明人格具中等稳定性：随年龄有可预测的平均变化（如尽责性、宜人性上升，神经质下降，所谓成熟原则），且重大生活事件与刻意干预可推动个体改变。遗传设定范围，但环境与努力在范围内塑形。
Research shows rank-order stability is moderate: mean levels shift predictably with age (conscientiousness/agreeableness rise, neuroticism falls—the maturity principle), and major life events or deliberate intervention can shift individuals. Genes set a range; environment and effort shape within it.

人格在多大程度上由基因决定？遗传率意味着什么？

How much is personality determined by genes, and what does heritability mean?

行为遗传学（双生子研究）估计大五特质遗传率约 40-50%，即群体差异中约一半与基因相关，但非注定。遗传率是群体统计量，不决定个人，且基因通过环境与表观遗传持续互动。
Behavior genetics (twin studies) puts Big Five heritability around 40-50%—about half the population variation links to genes, but not destiny. Heritability is a population statistic, not a personal verdict, and genes interact with environment via epigenetics.

什么是自我效能感？

What is self-efficacy?

班杜拉提出，自我效能感是个体对自己能否完成某特定任务的信念。高自我效能的人更敢于挑战、坚持更久，并在困难前更少焦虑。它可通过成功经验、榜样和积极反馈来提升。
Bandura defined self-efficacy as a person's belief in their ability to accomplish a specific task. Those with high self-efficacy take on challenges, persist longer, and feel less anxiety before difficulties. It can be raised through success experiences, models, and positive feedback.

什么是特质与状态的区分？

What is the difference between trait and state?

特质是相对稳定、跨时间的人格倾向（如“容易焦虑”的性格），状态是暂时的、由情境引发的心理体验（如“此刻很紧张”）。同一人在不同情境下状态会变，但特质相对稳定。区分二者能避免把一时情绪误判为性格。
A trait is a relatively stable, cross-time personality tendency (e.g., an 'anxious' disposition), while a state is a temporary, situation-triggered experience (e.g., 'feeling nervous right now'). A person's states change with situations, but traits stay fairly stable. Distinguishing them avoids mistaking a momentary mood for character.

双生子设计依据什么逻辑拆分人格特质中遗传与环境的贡献?其相等环境假定又为何屡遭批评?

On what logic does the twin design partition genetic and environmental contributions to personality, and why does its equal-environments assumption draw repeated criticism?

设计通过对比同卵与异卵双胞胎在某特质上的组内相关,把同卵相似度超出异卵的部分换算成遗传度估计。整套推理默认两类双胞胎共享环境的程度一致,即相等环境假定;批评者指出父母往往给同卵双胞胎更一致的穿着与教养,从而人为抬高其相似性。扩展家系设计与收养数据正是为校正这一偏差而引入。
The design contrasts within-pair correlations of monozygotic and dizygotic twins on a trait, converting whatever extra resemblance identical pairs show into a heritability estimate. The whole inference rests on the equal-environments assumption that both twin types share settings equally; critics counter that parents tend to dress and rear identical twins alike, inflating their similarity artificially. Extended-family designs and adoption data were introduced precisely to correct this distortion.

心理学家怎么描述一个人的性格？

How do psychologists describe personality?

现代人格研究最常用“大五”模型，把性格归为五个维度：开放性、尽责性、外向性、宜人性和神经质（情绪稳定性）。每个人的分数都落在连续的区间里，没有绝对的好与坏。大五被大量跨文化研究验证，比星座和血型更能稳定预测行为。
Modern personality research relies on the “Big Five”: openness, conscientiousness, extraversion, agreeableness, and neuroticism (emotional stability). Everyone falls somewhere along each continuum — no absolute good or bad. Validated across cultures, the Big Five predicts behavior far better than astrology or blood type.

什么是 locus of control（控制点）？

What is locus of control?

控制点指人们认为事情结果由谁掌控：内控者觉得靠自己努力，外控者觉得靠运气或他人。内控倾向通常与更高的主动性和学业成就相关。它可以通过经历和引导慢慢调整。
Locus of control is whether people believe outcomes are controlled by themselves or by external forces: internals credit their effort, externals credit luck or others. An internal orientation often links to more initiative and academic achievement. It can shift gradually through experience and guidance.

特质和状态在心理学上有什么区别？

What is the difference between trait and state in psychology?

特质是相对稳定、跨情境的人格倾向（如容易焦虑）；状态是特定时刻的暂时心理生理状况（如此刻很紧张）。同一个人特质高，但在不同状态下表现波动，测量时需区分基线与即时。
A trait is a stable, cross-situational disposition (e.g., anxious-prone); a state is a temporary here-and-now condition (e.g., tense right now). A high-trait person still fluctuates by state; assessment must separate baseline from momentary.

什么是人格的黑暗三联征？

What is the Dark Triad of personality?

黑暗三联征指自恋（膨胀自我加渴求赞赏）、马基雅维利主义（操纵、冷算）、精神病态（缺乏共情加冲动）。三者都涉及冷酷自我中心，但与负面人际与职场行为相关，并非精神疾病诊断。
The Dark Triad: narcissism (inflated self plus need for admiration), Machiavellianism (manipulation, cynical calculation), and psychopathy (low empathy plus impulsivity). All involve cold self-centeredness and link to negative interpersonal/work behavior—but are not clinical diagnoses.

内向和外向有什么不同？

What is the difference between introversion and extraversion?

外向者从社交和外界刺激中获得能量，内向者则在安静独处中“充电”。这并非害羞与否，而是能量来源不同。大多数人都处在两者之间，叫中间性格。
Extraverts gain energy from socializing and external stimulation, while introverts 'recharge' in quiet solitude. This is not about shyness but about where energy comes from. Most people fall in between, called ambiversion.

情境主义如何挑战人格特质决定行为的观点？

How does situationism challenge traits determine behavior?

情境主义（如 Milgram、Ross）指出，行为更多受具体情境与角色压力驱动，而非稳定特质。经典实验显示普通人在权威或角色下会做出惊人行为，提示我们常高估性格、低估环境的解释力。
Situationism (Milgram, Ross) argues behavior is driven more by context and role pressure than stable traits. Classic experiments show ordinary people do startling things under authority or roles, warning we overestimate character and underestimate environment.

什么是自恋型人格？

What is narcissistic personality?

自恋型人格表现为夸大自我重要性、需要过度赞赏且缺乏共情，但并非所有自信的人都是自恋。严重时会成为人格障碍，影响亲密关系。心理学用专业的量表来谨慎评估，而非随意贴标签。
Narcissistic personality involves an inflated sense of importance, need for excessive admiration, and lack of empathy, but not everyone confident is narcissistic. In severe form it becomes a personality disorder affecting relationships. Psychology uses careful scales rather than casual labeling.

为什么同一个人会在不同场合表现得判若两人？

Why can the same person act like a different person in different settings?

因为人格是特质乘以情境的交互：你有跨情境的平均倾向，但具体行为随角色、规则和当下状态调整。人有多面性很正常，并非虚伪，而是适应性地匹配环境。
Personality is trait times situation: you have average tendencies, but behavior shifts with role, rules, and current state. Having different sides is normal adaptation, not dishonesty.

高自尊和自恋有什么不同？

How do high self-esteem and narcissism differ?

健康高自尊是对自我价值的稳定、真实认可，不依赖贬低他人；自恋则是脆弱、需外部赞赏维持的膨胀自我，常伴随敌意与比较。研究发现自恋比真正高自尊更易引发攻击与关系冲突。
Healthy high self-esteem is stable, genuine self-worth not needing to demean others; narcissism is a fragile, praise-dependent inflated self with hostility and comparison. Narcissism predicts more aggression and conflict than true high self-esteem.

同样是碰壁，为什么有人很快爬起来，有人却一蹶不振？

After the same setback, why does one person bounce back fast while another stays down?

“气质性乐观”是一种稳定倾向：乐观者习惯把坏事解释成暂时、具体、外部的，把好事归为自己的能力（Scheier & Carver, 1985 生活取向测验）。这种解释风格缓冲压力、促进求助与坚持，而悲观的“永久-普遍-个人”归因则更易滑向抑郁（Seligman, 1991）。
Dispositional optimism is a stable trait: optimists explain bad events as temporary, specific and external and credit good events to themselves (Scheier & Carver, 1985, Life Orientation Test). This explanatory style buffers stress and sustains effort, whereas the pessimistic permanent-pervasive-personal style slides more easily into depression (Seligman, 1991).

People high in neuroticism have a lower threshold for negative stimuli, so minor setbacks trigger anxiety and irritability that are intense and slow to fade. They lean on emotion-focused strategies such as rumination and worry instead of tackling problems directly. The trait is continuously distributed, and a high score by itself does not amount to clinical disorder.

米歇尔1968年的批评发现,人格量表分数对单个情境行为的预测相关很难超过0.30——这场人格-情境之争最终靠什么思路得到缓解?

后来的研究意识到,低相关有一部分来自效标聚合不足:爱泼斯坦证明把多个情境下的行为加以平均后,特质与行为的相关可以翻倍以上。真正的出路则是“如果-那么”式行为签名——个体拥有稳定的情境-行为对应模式,却不等于在一切场合都以相同方式行事。人格研究由此从追问“特质能否预测行为”转向刻画“谁在何种情境下如何起伏”。
Later work realized that part of the low correlation stemmed from insufficient aggregation of criteria: Epstein demonstrated that averaging behavior across occasions more than doubled trait-behavior correlations. The genuine resolution lay in if-then behavioral signatures—individuals carry stable situation-behavior contingencies without behaving identically everywhere. Research accordingly turned from asking whether traits predict behavior to charting who fluctuates in what way across which contexts.

Snyder's self-monitoring personality (1974) describes how readily people adjust public behavior: high self-monitors read the room and adapt, while low self-monitors stay true to inner attitudes and change little. It is not fakery but two stable social strategies, each superior in different settings.

人的性格会完全改变吗？

Can a person's personality change completely?

人格在成年后相对稳定，但并非一成不变；重大生活事件、长期努力和心理咨询都可能带来缓慢改变。比如随着年龄增长，多数人变得更尽责、更情绪平稳。所以“江山易改”只是夸张，性格其实可以成长。
Personality is fairly stable after adulthood but not fixed; major life events, sustained effort, and counseling can bring slow change. For example, with age most people become more conscientious and emotionally stable. So 'character never changes' is an exaggeration—personality can grow.

What is personality?

人格是一个人相对稳定、独特的思维、情绪和行为模式，让我们在不同情境下都表现出“自己”。它受到基因和成长环境的共同影响。了解人格有助于理解和接纳彼此的不同。
