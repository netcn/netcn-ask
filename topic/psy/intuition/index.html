医生靠“直觉”诊断安全吗？

Is it safe for doctors to diagnose by 'intuition'?

经验丰富者在典型病例上凭模式直觉又快又准，但罕见与不典型情况直觉易错，需系统性核对清单与数据。最佳实践是直觉触发假设、证据再做确认，而非单凭感觉下结论。
Experienced clinicians are fast and accurate via pattern intuition in typical cases, but rare or atypical ones trip intuition, needing checklists and data. Best practice: intuition generates hypotheses, evidence confirms—not gut alone.

“肠胃直觉”——身体会比脑子先知道吗？

'Gut intuition'—can the body know before the mind?

内脏感觉（interoception）把心跳、呼吸等信号送进大脑，形成“说不出的预感”。研究表明人常在意识理由出现前，身体已对风险或信任对象作出反应，即身体先于言语直觉。
Interoception feeds heart-rate, breathing and other signals to the brain, forming 'wordless hunches'. Studies show the body often reacts to risk or trust before conscious reasons appear—the body intuiting ahead of words.

“直觉”和“理性”是两套系统吗？（双过程理论）

Are 'intuition' and 'reason' two systems? (dual-process theory)

双过程理论把思维分为快速、自动、省力的系统1（直觉）与缓慢、可控、费力的系统2（理性）。我们多数日常判断靠系统1，省力但易偏；系统2负责核查与深思。
Dual-process theory splits thinking into fast, automatic, effortless System 1 (intuition) and slow, controlled, effortful System 2 (reason). Most daily judgments use System 1—efficient but biased; System 2 checks and deliberates.
