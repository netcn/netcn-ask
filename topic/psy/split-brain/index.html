切断胼胝体后，人会拥有“两个意识”吗？

After severing the corpus callosum, does a person have 'two consciousnesses'?

部分裂脑病人在特殊测试中表现出两套可分离的反应与意图，引发“一个脑两个心智”的讨论。但日常中仍是一个统一主体，左右更多在信息隔离下各管一段，而非真正分裂成两个人。
Some split-brain patients show separable responses and intentions in special tasks, prompting talk of 'two minds in one brain'. But in daily life there remains one unified agent; the hemispheres mostly handle separate channels under info isolation, not two persons.

裂脑人实验里左右脑是怎么“分工”的？

In split-brain experiments, how do the left and right hemispheres 'divide labor'?

为治严重癫痫切断胼胝体后，左右脑信息不互通。经典结果发现左脑擅长语言与序列分析，右脑擅长空间与面孔，但这是倾向而非绝对。它让我们看到大脑功能有偏侧化，却非神话般的“左逻辑右创意”。
After the corpus callosum is cut to treat severe epilepsy, the hemispheres cannot share info. Classic findings show left-hemisphere strength in language and sequential analysis, right in space and faces—but tendencies, not absolutes. It reveals lateralization, not the myth of 'left logic, right creativity'.

“左脑理性、右脑创意”的说法科学吗？

Is 'left brain rational, right brain creative' scientifically true?

不科学。多数高级功能（语言、创造、逻辑）都需要两半球协作，偏侧化只是相对优势。把人分成“左脑型/右脑型”是流行误解，没有神经科学依据。
No. Most higher functions (language, creativity, logic) need both hemispheres working together; lateralization is only relative advantage. Typing people as 'left-brained' or 'right-brained' is a popular myth with no neural basis.
