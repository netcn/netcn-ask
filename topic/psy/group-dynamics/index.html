群体极化实验是怎样证明自由讨论会把群体结论推得比成员初始立场的平均值更极端的?

How did group polarization experiments demonstrate that free discussion drives group verdicts further from the mean of members’ initial positions?

Moscovici 与 Zavalloni 先让被试匿名记录各自立场,再经过集体讨论后重新评定,结果群体判断系统性地向初始倾向的极端一端移动。社会比较机制解释为成员为了显得高于平均水平而加码表态,说服性论据机制则强调讨论暴露了更多同方向理由。该效应在冒险与保守两个方向上均得到重复验证。
Moscovici and Zavalloni first recorded participants’ stances privately, then had them re-rate after collective discussion, and group judgments shifted systematically toward the extreme end of the initial tendency. Under social comparison, members stake out stronger positions to appear above average; under persuasive-arguments theory, discussion simply surfaces more same-direction reasons. Replications have captured the effect in both risky and cautious directions.

#群体极化 #社会比较

贾尼斯复盘猪湾事件等决策惨败后提出群体思维概念——哪些症状指标可以在事前提示某个决策团体正在滑向群体思维?

After dissecting fiascoes such as the Bay of Pigs, Janis introduced the concept of groupthink—which symptom indicators can signal in advance that a decision-making body is sliding toward it?

贾尼斯开出的症状清单包括:刀枪不入的错觉、对外群体的刻板化、成员对异议的自我审查、全体一致的幻觉,以及自封的“思想卫士”压制不利信息;这些症状合流后造成备选方案排查不全、风险评估缺位。操作层面,“成员开会前已自我沉默”“领导者在讨论早期就亮明偏好”可作为可核查的预警信号。他的处方是领导者暂缓表态、引入外部专家质询并将团队分组独立审议,以恢复议事规范的开放性。
Janis's symptom checklist includes illusions of invulnerability, stereotyping of outgroups, members' self-censorship of dissent, illusions of unanimity, and self-appointed mindguards suppressing adverse information; flowing together, these produce an incomplete survey of alternatives and absent risk appraisal. Operationally, members silencing themselves before meetings and leaders stating preferences early in discussion serve as verifiable warning signs. His prescription was leaders withholding their positions, summoning outside experts for challenge, and splitting the team into independent subgroups to restore open deliberative norms.

#群体思维 #共识错觉

拉绳实验中,参与人数越多,人均出力反而越少。这种“社会惰化”现象背后的心理机制是什么?

In rope-pulling studies, the more people involved, the less effort each individual exerts on average. What psychological mechanisms underlie this so-called social loafing?

拉塔内等人将社会惰化归因于个体贡献不可辨识所引发的责任分散:当产出以群体合计呈现时,个人努力与结果之间的联系被稀释,搭便车便成了低成本选择。后续实验表明,只要让每个人的成绩可被单独识别,或任务本身具有挑战性与个人意义,惰化幅度就会显著缩小。
Latané and colleagues attributed social loafing to diffusion of responsibility arising when individual contributions cannot be identified: once output is pooled at the group level, the link between personal effort and outcome is diluted, making free riding a low-cost option. Follow-up experiments showed that loafing shrinks markedly when each person's performance is individually identifiable, or when the task itself is challenging and personally meaningful.
