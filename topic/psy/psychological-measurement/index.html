MMPI（明尼苏达多相人格测验）有什么特点？

What are the features of the MMPI (Minnesota Multiphasic Personality Inventory)?

MMPI 是应用最广的客观人格与临床量表之一，包含多个效度量表以检测作答是否认真或掩饰。它用实证标准（而非理论）区分不同临床群体，常用于评估与筛查。
The MMPI is among the most widely used objective personality and clinical inventories, with validity scales detecting careless or defensive responding. It uses empirical criteria rather than theory to distinguish clinical groups and is used for assessment and screening.

什么是心理测验的“常模”？

What is a norm in psychological testing?

常模是某一代表性群体在测验上的得分分布，用来解释个体分数的相对位置。没有常模，单个原始分几乎没有可比意义，跨文化使用尤需注意常模的适用性。
A norm is the score distribution of a representative reference group, used to interpret an individual's relative standing. Without norms, a raw score is barely meaningful; cross-cultural use especially requires attention to norm appropriateness.

什么是“效标关联效度”？

What is criterion-related validity?

效标关联效度指测验分数与外部效标（如实际工作表现、后续成绩）之间的相关程度，分同时效度与预测效度。它是选拔类测验最看重的证据之一。
Criterion-related validity is the correlation between test scores and an external criterion such as job performance or later achievement, split into concurrent and predictive validity. It is among the most valued evidence for selection tests.

心理测验中的“信度”与“效度”分别是什么？

What are reliability and validity in psychological testing?

信度指测验结果的一致性与稳定性，效度指测验是否真正测到了它声称要测的东西。效度比信度更根本：不可靠的测验一定无效，可靠却不一定有效。
Reliability is the consistency and stability of test results; validity is whether the test truly measures what it claims. Validity is more fundamental: an unreliable test cannot be valid, but a reliable one is not automatically valid.

智商（IQ）是怎么测的？

How is intelligence quotient (IQ) measured?

常用的韦氏量表等分测验测言语、推理、记忆等能力，再把个人得分与同龄常模比较换算成离差智商，平均值设为100。IQ测的是特定认知能力，并非人格或全部潜能。
Common tools like the Wechsler scales test verbal, reasoning, and memory abilities, then compare a person's score to age-based norms to yield a deviation IQ with a mean of 100. IQ measures specific cognitive capacities, not personality or full potential.

罗夏墨迹测验是什么？

What is the Rorschach inkblot test?

罗夏测验呈现十张对称墨迹图，让被试自由描述看到什么，借以探查人格与思维特征。它属于投射测验，但信效度与解释标准在学界一直存在争议。
The Rorschach presents ten symmetrical inkblots for the person to describe freely, probing personality and thought patterns. It is a projective test whose reliability, validity, and scoring standards remain debated.
