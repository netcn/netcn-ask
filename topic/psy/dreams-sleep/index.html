梦的激活—整合假说是什么？

What is the activation-synthesis hypothesis of dreams?

霍布森提出，梦是脑干在睡眠中随机激活产生的神经信号，大脑皮质再把这些信号整合编成看似合理的故事。它强调梦源于生理信号而非隐藏含义。这一假说挑战了梦的“象征解析”。
Hobson proposed that dreams arise from random neural signals activated by the brainstem during sleep, which the cortex then integrates into seemingly coherent stories. It stresses dreams come from physiological signals rather than hidden meanings. This challenges symbolic dream interpretation.

梦的内容能预测未来吗？

Can dream content predict the future?

没有科学证据支持梦能预言未来。梦多是对记忆、情绪和随机神经活动的重组，偶尔“应验”多因巧合或自我实现。把梦当警示可以，但当作预言并不可靠。
There is no scientific evidence that dreams predict the future. Dreams mostly recombine memory, emotion, and random neural activity, and occasional 'hits' are coincidence or self-fulfillment. Treating dreams as warnings is fine, but as prophecies they are unreliable.

什么是睡眠瘫痪？

What is sleep paralysis?

睡眠瘫痪发生在入睡或醒来时，意识已清醒但身体暂时无法动弹，常伴随压迫感和幻觉。它因快速眼动期的肌肉麻痹与清醒不同步所致，通常短暂且无害。保持冷静多能自行缓解。
Sleep paralysis occurs when falling asleep or waking, with consciousness alert but the body temporarily unable to move, often with pressure sensations and hallucinations. It comes from REM muscle paralysis being out of sync with wakefulness and is usually brief and harmless. Staying calm lets it pass.

什么是清醒梦？

What is a lucid dream?

清醒梦是做梦时意识到自己在做梦，有些人还能在梦中主动操控情节。它可通过现实检验等练习诱发。研究用它探索意识与睡眠的关系。
A lucid dream is one in which the dreamer knows they are dreaming and some can deliberately steer the plot. It can be induced through reality-testing practices. Research uses it to explore consciousness and sleep.

什么是快速眼动睡眠？

What is REM sleep?

快速眼动睡眠是睡眠中眼球快速转动、梦境生动、身体近乎瘫痪的阶段，对情绪和记忆整合很重要。它通常占总睡眠的20%左右，整夜中循环出现。缺乏它会让人情绪不稳。
REM sleep is the stage with rapid eye movements, vivid dreams, and near-paralysis of the body, important for emotion and memory integration. It is about 20% of total sleep and recurs in cycles through the night. Lacking it makes mood unstable.

为什么有时会在梦里“复习”白天的事？

Why do dreams sometimes replay the day's events?

睡眠尤其是快速眼动期会回放和巩固白天的经历，梦中出现相关片段是记忆整理的表现。这被称为记忆的离线重演。它与学习效果密切相关。
Sleep, especially REM, replays and consolidates the day's experiences, and dream fragments about them reflect memory sorting, called offline replay. It is closely tied to learning.

一个完整的睡眠周期是怎样的？

What does a full sleep cycle look like?

睡眠由多个约90分钟的周期组成，每个周期在浅睡、深睡和快速眼动之间循环。前半夜深睡较多以恢复身体，后半夜快速眼动增多以整合记忆。了解周期有助于安排起床时间。
Sleep is made of several roughly 90-minute cycles, each cycling through light, deep, and REM sleep. Early night has more deep sleep for physical recovery; later night has more REM for memory integration. Knowing cycles helps time waking.

经常失眠该怎么办？

What helps with frequent insomnia?

改善失眠可从规律作息、睡前远离屏幕、营造安静黑暗环境入手，并减少床上清醒时的焦虑。认知行为疗法对慢性失眠效果优于药物。若长期严重应咨询医生。
Improving insomnia starts with regular schedules, avoiding screens before bed, a quiet dark room, and reducing anxiety while awake in bed. Cognitive behavioral therapy outperforms medication for chronic insomnia. For long severe cases, consult a doctor.

频繁做噩梦是病吗？

Are frequent nightmares a disorder?

偶尔噩梦正常，多由压力、创伤或药物引起；若频繁到影响睡眠和白天状态，可能是噩梦障碍。记录梦境和减压有帮助，严重时需专业干预。儿童做噩梦也较常见。
Occasional nightmares are normal, often from stress, trauma, or medication; if frequent enough to impair sleep and daytime function, it may be nightmare disorder. Logging dreams and reducing stress help, and severe cases need professional care. Nightmares are also common in children.

睡前看手机会影响睡眠吗？

Does using a phone before bed affect sleep?

手机屏幕的蓝光会抑制褪黑素分泌，推迟睡意，加上内容刺激让大脑兴奋。建议睡前一小时放下屏幕。换成阅读纸质书更有助入睡。
Blue light from phone screens suppresses melatonin and delays sleepiness, and engaging content keeps the brain alert. It is best to put screens away an hour before bed. Reading a paper book helps more.

熬夜会对情绪产生什么影响？

How does staying up late affect mood?

睡眠不足会削弱情绪调节能力，让人更容易烦躁、焦虑和情绪低落。连续熬夜还会降低注意力和判断力。保证规律睡眠是情绪稳定的基础。
Lack of sleep weakens emotion regulation, making people more irritable, anxious, and low. Consecutive late nights also lower attention and judgment. Regular sleep is the base of stable mood.

打鼾说明睡得香吗？

Does snoring mean sleeping soundly?

打鼾不一定代表睡得好，它常是气道不畅的信号，严重时可能是睡眠呼吸暂停，反而导致缺氧和白天困倦。若鼾声很大并伴有憋气，应就医检查。
Snoring does not necessarily mean good sleep; it often signals airway obstruction and, when severe, may be sleep apnea that causes oxygen loss and daytime drowsiness. Loud snoring with breath-holding warrants a medical check.

午睡有必要吗？

Is napping necessary?

短暂的午睡（20分钟左右）能恢复精力、提升下午的注意力和情绪。过长或过晚的午睡可能影响夜间睡眠。是否午睡因人而异。
A short nap of about 20 minutes restores energy and boosts afternoon attention and mood. Too long or too late a nap can hurt nighttime sleep. Whether to nap varies by person.
