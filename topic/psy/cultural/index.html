什么是“文化适应”（acculturation）？移民会失去原有自我吗？

What is acculturation, and do immigrants lose their original self?

文化适应是个体接触新文化后在行为、价值与身份上的调整，可有同化、分离、融合等不同策略。健康适应常在保持原文化与接纳新文化间取得平衡（融合），而非简单替换。
Acculturation is the adjustment of behavior, values and identity upon contact with a new culture, with strategies like assimilation, separation or integration. Healthy adaptation often balances keeping the heritage culture and embracing the new, rather than replacing one.

个人主义与集体主义文化，会塑造不同的“自我”吗？

Do individualist vs collectivist cultures shape different 'selves'?

个人主义文化更强调独立自我（我是独特个体），集体主义文化更强调互依自我（我由关系定义）。这影响记忆、动机与冲突处理：前者重个人成就，后者重和谐与面子。
Individualist cultures emphasize an independent self (a unique individual); collectivist cultures emphasize an interdependent self (defined by relationships). This shapes memory, motivation and conflict handling—personal achievement versus harmony and face.

东西方在“归因”上有什么系统差异？

What systematic attribution differences exist between East and West?

西方观察者更倾向性格归因（他性子急），东亚观察者更倾向情境归因（当时太挤）。这种差异来自独立 versus 互依自我，也会影响对责任、教育与管理的理解。
Western observers lean toward dispositional attribution (he is impatient); East Asian observers lean toward situational (it was crowded). This follows independent versus interdependent selves and affects views of responsibility, education and management.

文化会怎样影响“自传体记忆”？

How does culture shape autobiographical memory?

西方被试的记忆更突出个人独特事件与自我，东亚被试更常回忆与他人的关系情境并更谦抑。记忆并非纯个人存档，而受文化叙事脚本塑造，被称为“集体记忆”的延伸。
Western respondents recall more unique, self-centered events; East Asians more often recall relational contexts and self-efface. Memory is not a purely personal archive but shaped by cultural narrative scripts—an extension of 'collective memory'.

情绪该不该外露，不同文化规则一样吗？

Should emotions be shown? Do display rules differ across cultures?

“情绪表达规则”规定何时、对谁、以何种强度表达情绪。比如东亚文化更鼓励抑制负性表达以维持和谐，欧美文化更接受外露。同一情绪内在相似，表达却被文化训练塑形。
Display rules dictate when, to whom and how strongly to show emotion. East Asian cultures often encourage suppressing negative expression for harmony; Western cultures accept more openness. The inner feeling may be similar, but expression is culturally trained.
