What higher functions does the prefrontal cortex support?

前额叶皮层负责执行功能，包括计划、决策、冲动控制和工作记忆。它发育较晚，直到二十多岁才完全成熟，因此青少年易冲动。
The prefrontal cortex supports executive functions such as planning, decision making, impulse control, and working memory. It matures late, not fully until the twenties, which partly explains adolescent impulsivity.

裂脑研究如何揭示两半球的功能分工？

How did split-brain research reveal the division of labour between hemispheres?

为控制严重癫痫而切断胼胼体的患者，两半球之间的直接信息交流被阻断。斯佩里与加扎尼加设计的实验把刺激只呈现给一侧视野：呈现给右半球的物体患者无法用言语说出名称，却能用左手从遮挡的物品中挑出对应物；呈现给左半球的则可以正常报告。这确立了语言输出主要依赖左半球，而空间与整体性加工偏向右半球。研究还发现左半球倾向为不明原因的行为编造解释，这一虚构倾向对理解自我叙事的构建有深远意义。
In patients whose corpus callosum was severed to control severe epilepsy, direct communication between the hemispheres is interrupted. Sperry and Gazzaniga presented stimuli to a single visual field: an object shown to the right hemisphere could not be named aloud, yet the left hand could pick out the matching item from hidden objects, while stimuli shown to the left hemisphere were reported normally. This established that speech output depends mainly on the left hemisphere while spatial and holistic processing leans right. The work also revealed a left-hemisphere tendency to confabulate explanations for behaviour it did not initiate, with lasting implications for how self-narratives are constructed.

什么是镜像神经元？

What are mirror neurons?

镜像神经元在个体自己执行某动作或观察他人执行同一动作时都会放电，最早由里佐拉蒂在猕猴脑中发现。它被认为是模仿、共情和语言演化的神经基础之一。
Mirror neurons fire both when an individual performs an action and when observing another do the same, first discovered by Rizzolatti in monkey brains. They are considered a neural basis for imitation, empathy, and language evolution.

什么是突触可塑性与“长时程增强”（LTP）？

What are synaptic plasticity and long-term potentiation (LTP)?

突触可塑性指神经元之间连接强度可随经验改变，LTP是其中反复激活使突触变强、信号传递更高效的过程。它被广泛视为学习与记忆在细胞层面的基础。
Synaptic plasticity means the strength of connections between neurons can change with experience; LTP is the process where repeated activation strengthens a synapse and makes signaling more efficient. It is widely viewed as the cellular basis of learning and memory.

EEG 与 fMRI 在心理学研究中各测什么？

What do EEG and fMRI each measure in psychology research?

EEG 通过头皮电极记录大脑电活动，时间分辨率高但空间定位粗；fMRI 通过血氧水平间接反映神经活动，空间分辨率高但时间分辨率较低。两者常互补使用。
EEG records the brain's electrical activity via scalp electrodes, with high time but coarse spatial resolution; fMRI indirectly maps neural activity via blood oxygen, with high spatial but lower time resolution. The two are often used complementarily.

疼痛的闸门控制理论为什么能解释揉搓伤处会减轻疼痛？

Why does the gate control theory of pain explain why rubbing a sore spot helps?

梅尔扎克与沃尔提出，脊髓背角存在类似闸门的中间神经元网络，来自粗大 A-beta 触压纤维的输入会抑制细小 C 纤维与 A-delta 纤维携带的痛信号传递，因此揉搓、振动或经皮电刺激能关小闸门。理论还引入下行调控通路，说明注意、情绪与预期可以经由脑干和皮质影响疼痛体验，为疼痛不等于组织损伤程度提供了机制框架。它直接催生了经皮神经电刺激等临床技术，也是现代生物心理社会疼痛模型的起点。
Melzack and Wall proposed a gate-like network of interneurons in the dorsal horn of the spinal cord, where input from large A-beta touch and pressure fibres inhibits transmission of pain signals carried by small C and A-delta fibres, so rubbing, vibration, or transcutaneous electrical stimulation partly closes the gate. The theory also introduced descending control pathways, explaining how attention, emotion, and expectation act through brainstem and cortex to shape the pain experience and providing a mechanism for why pain does not simply mirror tissue damage. It directly inspired clinical techniques such as transcutaneous electrical nerve stimulation and launched the modern biopsychosocial model of pain.

为什么什么都不做发呆时，大脑其实一点都没“关机”？

Why is the brain far from off when we do nothing and just zone out?

雷克尔等的“默认模式网络”（DMN, Raichle 等, 2001）发现，休息、走神、回想自我时，一组脑区反而更活跃。DMN 负责自我参照、自传体记忆与社交推演，过度活跃又与抑郁反刍相关，说明“放空”是大脑最耗能的后台之一。
Raichle and colleagues' default mode network (DMN, 2001) shows a set of regions fire up precisely when we rest, mind-wander or think about ourselves. The DMN handles self-reference, autobiographical memory and social simulation, and its overactivity links to depressive rumination - so zoning out is among the brain's costliest background modes.

病人 H.M. 的案例如何改变了记忆系统的理论？

How did the case of patient H.M. transform theories of memory systems?

为治疗顽固性癫痫，H.M. 接受了双侧内侧颞叶包括大部分海马的切除，术后智力与语言保持正常，短时记忆完好，却无法形成新的情景与语义记忆，出现严重的顺行性遗忘。关键发现是他在镜像描画等运动技能任务上能逐日进步，尽管每次都声称从未做过，这证明程序性学习不依赖海马。因此记忆不再被视为单一能力，而被划分为陈述性与非陈述性两大系统，各有不同的神经基础。
To treat intractable epilepsy H.M. underwent bilateral removal of the medial temporal lobes including most of the hippocampus; afterwards his intelligence, language, and short-term memory were preserved but he could no longer form new episodic or semantic memories, leaving profound anterograde amnesia. The decisive observation was that he improved day by day on motor skills such as mirror drawing while insisting he had never attempted the task, proving procedural learning does not depend on the hippocampus. Memory was consequently reconceived not as a single faculty but as declarative and non-declarative systems with distinct neural substrates.

镜像神经元的功能常被过度解读，学界的谨慎意见是什么？

Mirror neurons are often over-interpreted — what is the cautious scientific view?

猕猴前运动皮层中的一类神经元在猴子自己抓握和观看他人抓握时都会放电，这一发现被推广用于解释共情、语言起源与孤独症，形成了流行叙事。批评者指出，人类中直接的单细胞证据有限，动作理解在失用症或运动系统受损的患者中并未全面崩塌，且这类反应可能来自感觉运动联结的学习而非先天的理解机制。目前较稳妥的表述是，镜像性质的反应参与动作预测与模仿，但把它当作社会认知的统一解释缺乏充分证据。
A class of neurons in macaque premotor cortex fires both when the monkey grasps an object and when it watches another grasp, and the finding was extended to explain empathy, the origins of language, and autism, generating a popular narrative. Critics point out that direct single-cell evidence in humans is sparse, that action understanding does not collapse wholesale in patients with apraxia or damaged motor systems, and that such responses may arise from learned sensorimotor associations rather than an innate comprehension mechanism. The prudent formulation is that mirror-like responses contribute to action prediction and imitation, but treating them as a unified explanation of social cognition is not adequately supported.

How is serotonin linked to mood?

Serotonin is a key neurotransmitter regulating mood, sleep, and appetite; low levels are linked to depression and anxiety. Many antidepressants, such as SSRIs, work by increasing its availability.

What is the circadian rhythm, and what does melatonin do?

The circadian rhythm is an internal roughly 24-hour clock regulating sleep, body temperature, and hormones; the pineal gland secretes melatonin in darkness, signaling the body to sleep. Light suppresses its release.

什么是“神经可塑性（neuroplasticity）”？

What is "neuroplasticity"?

神经可塑性指神经系统在经验、学习或损伤后改变结构与功能连接的能力，包括突触强化、皮层重组与神经发生。这一机制贯穿一生，是康复、学习与记忆的生理基础，由 Ramón y Cajal 等早期提出并由当代影像研究证实。
Neuroplasticity is the nervous system's capacity to change its structure and functional connections after experience, learning, or injury, including synaptic strengthening, cortical reorganization, and neurogenesis. It operates across the lifespan and underlies recovery, learning, and memory, proposed early by Ramón y Cajal and confirmed by modern neuroimaging.

Do the brain's left and right hemispheres really have separate 'jobs'?

The hemispheres do show lateralization: the left is typically stronger in language and logic, the right in spatial and face processing. But most tasks engage both cooperatively, so 'left brain rational, right brain creative' is an oversimplification.

What did Sperry's “split-brain” research reveal about the cerebral hemispheres?

Sperry and colleagues studied “split-brain” patients whose corpus callosum was cut to treat epilepsy, finding functional specialization: the left hemisphere excels at language and logic, the right at spatial and face processing. Sperry received the 1981 Nobel Prize in Physiology or Medicine.

“盖奇（Phineas Gage）”病例对脑功能定位有什么意义？

What is the significance of the Phineas Gage case for brain localization?

1848 年，铁路工人盖奇被一根铁棍贯通前额叶，虽幸存但性格从温和变为冲动、缺乏计划，表明前额叶对人格、决策与社会行为至关重要。这一经典病例是脑功能定位与额叶执行功能研究的重要起点。
In 1848 railroad worker Phineas Gage survived a tamping iron piercing his prefrontal cortex, after which his personality shifted from temperate to impulsive and disinhibited, showing the prefrontal lobe's role in personality, decision-making, and social behavior. The case is a landmark for brain localization and executive function.

伦敦出租车司机的海马研究说明成年大脑有多大可塑性？

What does research on London taxi drivers reveal about adult brain plasticity?

麦圭尔团队发现，通过极其严苛的伦敦路线知识考试的出租车司机，其海马后部灰质体积大于对照组，且体积与从业年限相关。为排除天生差异的解释，研究者又对受训者进行纵向追踪，发现只有最终通过考试的人出现海马后部体积增长，未通过者与对照组则没有变化。这为成年人复杂空间学习可以引起结构性可塑性提供了较强证据，同时也提示这种适应可能伴随代价，例如某些新的空间信息学习任务上的表现相对较弱。
Maguire's group found that taxi drivers who had passed London's demanding street-knowledge examination had larger posterior hippocampal grey matter volume than controls, with volume related to years on the job. To rule out pre-existing differences, the researchers followed trainees longitudinally and found posterior hippocampal growth only in those who eventually qualified, with no change in those who failed or in controls. This provides strong evidence that complex spatial learning can drive structural plasticity in adults, while also suggesting the adaptation carries trade-offs, since qualified drivers performed relatively worse on some tasks requiring new spatial learning.

为什么抗抑郁药大多瞄准“快乐信使”去甲肾上腺素、多巴胺和血清素？

Why do most antidepressants target the happy messengers noradrenaline, dopamine and serotonin?

“单胺假说”（Schildkraut, 1965）提出，抑郁与脑内去甲肾上腺素、血清素等单胺类神经递质不足有关，这奠定了 SSRI 等药物的思路。它虽能解释部分药效，却过于简化——真实机制还涉及神经可塑、炎症与神经发生，新药正朝这些方向拓展。
The monoamine hypothesis (Schildkraut, 1965) proposes depression involves deficient noradrenaline, serotonin and other monoamines, grounding drugs like SSRIs. Useful yet oversimplified, it misses plasticity, inflammation and neurogenesis, which newer treatments now target.

布罗卡区与韦尼克区在语言加工中各起什么作用？

What roles do Broca's and Wernicke's areas play in language processing?

布罗卡区（左额下回）损伤导致表达性失语，患者理解尚可但说话费力、语法缺失；韦尼克区（左颞上回）损伤导致感受性失语，患者流利却语无伦次、理解困难。二者由 Broca（1861）与 Wernicke（1874）先后发现，奠定了语言脑定位的基础。
Damage to Broca's area (left inferior frontal gyrus) causes expressive aphasia—impaired, nonfluent speech with preserved comprehension; damage to Wernicke's area (left superior temporal gyrus) causes receptive aphasia—fluent but meaningless speech and poor comprehension. Broca (1861) and Wernicke (1874) founded the cortical localization of language.

海马体与记忆有什么关系？

How is the hippocampus related to memory?

海马体位于颞叶，是把新经验巩固为长期陈述性记忆的关键结构。著名的病人H.M.在海马体被切除后无法形成新长期记忆，证实了它的作用。
The hippocampus, in the temporal lobe, is crucial for consolidating new experiences into long-term declarative memory. The famous patient H.M., after hippocampal removal, could not form new long-term memories, confirming its role.

为什么海马严重受损的遗忘症患者仍能学会新的动作技能?

Why can amnesic patients with severe hippocampal damage still acquire new motor skills?

动作技能依赖以基底节和小脑为核心的程序性记忆系统,其运转并不需要内侧颞叶参与。经典病例H.M.完全记不住每天的练习经历,却在镜画任务上一天天画得更好,证明陈述性记忆与技能学习由不同的脑系统分别支持。
Motor skills rely on the procedural memory system centered on the basal ganglia and cerebellum, which operates without the medial temporal lobes. The classic patient H.M. retained no memory of his daily practice sessions yet drew better and better on the mirror-drawing task, demonstrating that declarative memory and skill learning are supported by distinct brain systems.

病人H.M.的病例为什么被视为记忆神经心理学研究的分水岭?他的手术带来了哪些关键发现?

Why is the case of patient H.M. regarded as a watershed in the neuropsychology of memory, and what key findings followed from his surgery?

为控制难治性癫痫,H.M.于1953年接受双侧内侧颞叶切除手术,术后出现严重的顺行性遗忘,无法形成新的陈述性记忆,但短时记忆与手术前的远期记忆基本保留。Scoville与Milner在1957年的病例报告中指出,海马结构受损使新记忆难以巩固,而镜像描画等程序性学习任务仍可正常进步。这种双重分离促使研究者把记忆拆分为可分离的系统,并确立了海马在陈述性记忆形成中的核心地位。
To control intractable epilepsy, H.M. underwent bilateral medial temporal lobe resection in 1953, which left him with profound anterograde amnesia: he could form no new declarative memories, while short-term memory and remote memories from before the surgery remained largely intact. Scoville and Milner's 1957 case report showed that damage to the hippocampal region impaired consolidation of new memories even as procedural learning on tasks like mirror-drawing continued to improve normally. This double dissociation led researchers to split memory into separable systems and fixed the hippocampus at the center of declarative memory formation.
