点击化学（click chemistry）在化学生物学里有什么用？

What is click chemistry used for in chemical biology?

它提供像搭扣一样快速、特异的连接反应，能在活细胞内给蛋白或糖装上荧光标签来追踪其行踪。
It provides fast, specific snap-like reactions to label proteins or sugars with reporters inside living cells.

小分子探针如何帮助研究细胞信号？

How do small-molecule probes help study cell signaling?

设计能特异性结合某蛋白或阻断某通路的小分子，可在不敲除基因的情况下即时扰动信号，定位关键节点。
Selective small molecules perturb a pathway on demand, pinpointing key nodes without gene knockout.

光遗传学的核心思想是什么？

What is the core idea of optogenetics?

把光敏蛋白（如 Channelrhodopsin）表达在神经元上，用光精确开关特定细胞，以毫秒级控制神经活动。
Light-sensitive proteins expressed in neurons let researchers switch specific cells on or off with millisecond precision using light.
