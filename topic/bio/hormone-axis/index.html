瘦素（leptin）和饥饿素（ghrelin）如何对话调控食欲？

How do leptin and ghrelin talk to regulate appetite?

脂肪细胞分泌瘦素告知能量充足以抑制食欲，胃在空腹时分泌饥饿素刺激进食，二者构成能量平衡回路。
Adipose leptin signals energy sufficiency to suppress appetite, while gastric ghrelin stimulates eating when empty, forming an energy-balance loop.

甲状腺激素是怎样影响全身代谢速度的？

How do thyroid hormones influence whole-body metabolic rate?

它们提高基础代谢、促进产热和蛋白质合成；过多会让人消瘦心悸，过少则令人乏力怕冷。
They raise basal metabolism and heat production; excess causes weight loss and a fast heartbeat, deficiency fatigue and cold sensitivity.

为什么运动后心情常常变好？

Why does mood often improve after exercise?

运动促使大脑释放内啡肽等快乐相关物质，并降低压力激素，让人感到轻松和愉悦。
Exercise prompts the brain to release endorphin-like feel-good molecules and lowers stress hormones, bringing relief and pleasure.
