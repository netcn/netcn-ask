冷冻电镜分辨率革命依靠哪些关键技术突破,让TRPV1这样难结晶的膜蛋白也达到近原子分辨率?

Which key technical breakthroughs powered the cryo-EM resolution revolution, bringing hard-to-crystallize membrane proteins such as TRPV1 to near-atomic resolution?

关键是直接电子探测相机与统计三维重构算法的结合。直接探测器逐帧记录单电子事件,信噪比远超胶片与CCD,低剂量成像保住高频信息;软件用最大似然法对数十万颗粒做二维分类与三维精修,校正辐射损伤导致的各向异性衰减,把玻璃态冰中的样品收敛为近原子级密度图。2013年Liao等据此解出3.4埃的TRPV1通道结构,无需结晶即阐明其离子选择性与门控基础,宣告冷冻电镜黄金时代的到来。
Direct electron detection cameras combined with statistical three-dimensional reconstruction algorithms. Direct detectors record individual electron events frame by frame, lifting signal-to-noise far beyond film and CCDs, while low-dose imaging preserves high-frequency information. Maximum-likelihood software sorts hundreds of thousands of particles into 2D classes and refines the 3D map, correcting the anisotropic falloff caused by radiation damage, until specimens held in vitreous ice yield near-atomic density maps. In 2013 Liao and colleagues solved the 3.4-angstrom TRPV1 channel without crystals, explaining its ion selectivity and gating and opening the cryo-EM golden age.

#结构生物学 #冷冻电镜 #膜蛋白结构

新生肽链还没折叠好就开始转运,细胞怎么防止它提前聚集或误折叠?

How does the cell keep a nascent polypeptide from aggregating or misfolding before it finishes folding?

分子伴侣如Hsp70家族结合暴露的疏水片段,把未折叠肽链罩住,防止其在水环境中互相粘连。许多伴侣依赖ATP循环反复结合与释放底物,给肽链多次尝试正确构象的机会。若反复尝试失败,错误折叠蛋白会被送去泛素-蛋白酶体系统降解。
Chaperones such as the Hsp70 family bind exposed hydrophobic segments, shielding unfolded chains so they do not stick together in an aqueous environment. ATP-driven binding-and-release cycles give each chain repeated chances to reach its native conformation. When attempts repeatedly fail, the misfolded protein is routed to the ubiquitin-proteasome system for degradation.

#蛋白质折叠 #分子伴侣

蛋白酶体如何判断一个蛋白该不该被销毁?

How does the proteasome decide whether a protein should be destroyed?

多数情况下判定依据是多聚泛素链:E1活化、E2接载、E3连接酶把泛素逐个接到目标蛋白的赖氨酸残基上,E3的种类决定了底物的选择性。带上特定构型多聚泛素链的蛋白会被26S蛋白酶体的调节颗粒捕获,去折叠后送入核心筒状腔降解成短肽。N端规则途径和内质网关联降解等分支说明这套系统的判据远不止一种标签。
The verdict usually rests on polyubiquitin chains: E1 activates ubiquitin, E2 carries it, and E3 ligases attach it to lysine residues on the target, with the identity of the E3 setting substrate selectivity. Proteins bearing the right chain topology are captured by the regulatory particle of the 26S proteasome, unfolded, and threaded into the barrel-shaped core to be cut into short peptides. Branches such as the N-end rule pathway and ER-associated degradation show the system recognizes more than one kind of tag.

#蛋白质降解 #泛素-蛋白酶体系统

既然氨基酸序列本身已决定蛋白质的最终形状，细胞为什么还需要分子伴侣帮忙？

If an amino acid sequence already specifies a protein's final shape, why do cells bother with molecular chaperones?

安芬森的核糖核酸酶实验证明序列本身包含折叠成天然构象的热力学信息，但该结论建立在稀溶液的理想条件下。在拥挤的细胞质里，新生肽链暴露出的疏水片段可能在折叠完成前相互碰撞并聚沉。Hsp70家族伴侣蛋白会遮蔽这些疏水片段，借助ATP驱动的循环将其释放以便重试；伴侣蛋白复合体则为难折叠底物提供隔离腔室。
Anfinsen's ribonuclease experiments showed that the sequence carries the thermodynamic information for the native fold, but that conclusion assumed dilute, ideal conditions. Inside a crowded cytosol, nascent chains expose hydrophobic patches that can collide and aggregate before folding completes. Hsp70-family chaperones shield those patches, ATP-driven cycles release them for another attempt, and chaperonins offer an isolated chamber for stubborn substrates.
