蘑菇是怎么长出来的？

How do mushrooms grow?

蘑菇是真菌，不是植物。它从地下的细丝里冒出来，喜欢在潮湿阴暗的地方长大。
A mushroom is a fungus, not a plant. It pops up from tiny threads underground and likes to grow in damp, shady places.

蘑菇和植物有什么不一样？

How are mushrooms different from plants?

蘑菇属于真菌，不会用阳光自己做食物，而是分解腐烂的木头和叶子来吸收营养。植物能光合作用，真菌不能。
Mushrooms are fungi that cannot make their own food from sunlight; instead they break down dead wood and leaves to absorb nutrients. Plants photosynthesize, but fungi do not.

做面包用的酵母是什么？

What is the yeast used in bread?

酵母是一种微小的真菌，吃糖后会放出二氧化碳气泡。这些小气泡让面团鼓起来，面包才又松又软。
Yeast is a tiny fungus that releases carbon dioxide bubbles after eating sugar. The bubbles make dough rise, giving bread its soft, airy texture.

细菌都是坏家伙吗？

Are all bacteria bad guys?

不是，很多细菌对我们很有帮助。比如肠道里的小细菌帮我们消化食物，还能保护身体。
No, many bacteria are very helpful. Tiny bacteria in your gut help digest food and even protect your body.
