吃辣椒的“辣”到底是不是一种味道，舌头是怎么区分辣和酸咸甜的？

Is the “spiciness” of chili a taste at all, and how does the tongue tell hot from sour, salty, sweet?

辣不是味觉，而是一种痛觉/灼热感：辣椒素激活舌头上的痛觉受体（TRPV1），模拟“烫”。酸咸甜鲜由味蕾不同受体识别，归为味觉；辣是神经把化学刺激当成了伤害信号。
Spiciness isn't taste but a pain/burning feel: capsaicin activates pain receptors (TRPV1), mimicking “heat”. Sour, salty, sweet, umami are detected by taste buds' receptors—real tastes; “hot” is the nerve reading a chemical as harm.

#辣 #辣椒素 #痛觉

我们眼里的红花绿叶，蜜蜂看到的却不一样，它们看见了什么我们看不见的？

Red flowers and green leaves look different to bees—what do they see that we don't?

蜜蜂看不见红色，却能看到紫外线。许多花在紫外下有“蜜导”花纹，像跑道指示花蜜位置；它们还靠偏振光和紫外定位，所以我们觉得平淡的花，在蜂眼里可能是亮闪的“招牌”。
Bees can't see red but see ultraviolet. Many flowers show UV “nectar guides” like runways to nectar; bees also use polarized UV light to navigate. Plain-to-us blooms may glow as bright “signboards” to a bee.

#蜜蜂 #紫外线 #蜜导

吃辣椒后嘴里的灼烧感与真实温度无关——舌头上的哪类分子感受器被辣椒素“冒名顶替”了?

The burn after eating a chili pepper has nothing to do with actual temperature—which receptor molecule does capsaicin effectively impersonate?

辣椒素直接激活TRPV1阳离子通道,而该通道的本职工作是探测43摄氏度以上的伤害性热度。通道开放令钠钙离子涌入痛觉神经末梢并触发动作电位,大脑便将信号解读为灼烧,即使组织并未升温。薄荷醇则反向操作,激活冷觉受体TRPM8制造虚假凉意,两者共用同一套“感受器借用”逻辑。
Capsaicin directly opens TRPV1 cation channels whose day job is detecting noxious heat above roughly 43 degrees Celsius. Channel opening floods nociceptor endings with sodium and calcium ions, firing action potentials that the brain reads as burning even though no tissue has warmed. Menthol runs the same trick in reverse, activating the cold receptor TRPM8 to produce a phantom chill.

#感觉生物学 #温度感受受体

耳蜗怎样把不同频率的声音指派给不同部位的毛细胞,让一张仅约35毫米长的膜铺满人类听力横跨的约十一个八度?

How does the cochlea assign distinct sound frequencies to hair cells at distinct positions, letting a membrane barely 35 millimeters long cover the roughly eleven octaves of human hearing?

基底膜的宽度与刚度沿蜗轴连续变化:底圈窄而坚硬,顶圈宽而柔软,行波因此在不同位置抵达各自的峰值,形成音调拓扑图。高频振动的峰落在靠近卵圆窗处,低频的峰推向蜗顶,每个部位的毛细胞由此获得专属调谐。外毛细胞随电位主动伸缩,放大行波峰并锐化选频,使这台纯机械滤波器兼具灵敏与分辨力。
Basilar-membrane width and stiffness change continuously along the spiral: narrow and stiff at the base, wide and compliant at the apex, so traveling waves peak at position-specific points and lay down a tonotopic map. High-frequency peaks land near the oval window while low-frequency peaks travel toward the apex, granting each stretch of hair cells its own tuning. Outer hair cells elongate and shorten with membrane potential, amplifying the wave peak and sharpening frequency selectivity, so this purely mechanical filter achieves both sensitivity and resolution.

#听觉机制 #音调拓扑定位

人类视网膜中的视杆细胞和视锥细胞在感光特性与功能分工上有什么本质区别?

What are the fundamental differences between rod and cone photoreceptors in light sensitivity and functional roles within the human retina?

视杆细胞对光极其敏感,单个光子即可触发可检测的电响应,但只含视紫红质一种色素,无法分辨颜色,主要承担暗视觉;视锥细胞需更强的光照才被激活,却拥有分别对长、中、短波敏感的三种视蛋白,负责明视觉和色觉。两者在视网膜上分布也不对称:视杆集中于周边,视锥密集于中央凹,这解释了为何夜间用余光看暗弱星体反而更清晰。暗适应时视杆系统约需30分钟才能达到最大灵敏度。
Rods are extraordinarily sensitive—a single photon can trigger a detectable electrical response—but they carry only one pigment (rhodopsin) and cannot discriminate color, so they mediate dim-light vision. Cones need brighter light yet come in long-, medium-, and short-wavelength classes, supporting daylight vision and color discrimination. Their retinal distributions are complementary: rods dominate the periphery while cones pack the fovea, which is why faint stars are easier to see slightly off-center at night. Full dark adaptation of the rod system takes roughly thirty minutes.
