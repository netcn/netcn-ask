植物能通过地下真菌网络互相“通讯”或分享养分吗？

Can plants communicate or share nutrients through underground fungal networks?

许多陆地植物根系与丛枝菌根真菌形成共生，真菌菌丝像网络连接不同植株，能传递糖分、氮磷甚至警报信号。被啃食的植株可通过菌丝向邻居发出化学警示，使其提前启动防御。这类“木维网”现象在森林中研究较多，但共享规模和意义仍有争论。
Many land plants form symbioses with arbuscular mycorrhizal fungi whose hyphae link different individuals, transporting sugars, nitrogen, phosphorus, and even warning signals. A grazed plant can send chemical alerts through the hyphae so neighbors preactivate defenses. The 'wood wide web' is well studied in forests, though the scale and importance of sharing remain debated.

捕蝇草怎么知道虫子真的来了，而不是被雨滴骗到？

How does the Venus flytrap know a real insect has arrived rather than being tricked by a raindrop?

捕蝇草的夹子内表面有三根细毛，虫子必须在一二十秒内连续触碰其中两根，才触发闭合。这种“两次触碰”机制能滤掉无意义刺激，避免白白消耗能量。闭合后若虫子挣扎会继续分泌消化液。
The Venus flytrap has three tiny trigger hairs inside each lobe; an insect must touch two of them within about ten to twenty seconds to set off closure. This 'two-touch' rule filters out meaningless stimuli like raindrops and avoids wasting energy. Once shut, the struggling prey prompts the plant to secrete digestive fluid.

种子是怎么知道自己该什么时候发芽的？

How does a seed know when it should germinate?

种子通过感知光照、温度、水分和休眠激素来判断时机。很多种子需要经历低温层积或火烤才能打破休眠，避免在不合适的季节萌发。有些种子甚至能在土里沉睡数十年，遇到适宜条件才苏醒。
Seeds judge timing by sensing light, temperature, moisture, and dormancy hormones. Many need cold stratification or even fire to break dormancy, avoiding sprouting in the wrong season. Some can lie dormant in soil for decades and awaken only under favorable conditions.

有些花为什么在晚上才开放、白天闭合？

Why do some flowers open at night and close by day?

夜来香、昙花等靠蛾类或蝙蝠传粉，这些传粉者在夜间活动，所以花朵选择夜间开放并释放浓香。白天闭合可减少水分流失和避免日晒伤。这是植物与传粉者协同演化的结果。
Flowers like night jasmine or epiphyllum are pollinated by moths or bats active at night, so they open after dark and release strong scent. Closing by day cuts water loss and sun damage. This is coevolution between plant and pollinator.

红树林怎么在咸水里活下来？

How do mangroves survive in salty water?

红树林有特殊的盐分泌腺体把多余盐分排出，还用密集的呼吸根伸出泥滩呼吸空气。它们还能过滤根部吸收的盐分，并把少量盐分锁在老叶里随落叶带走。这些本领让它们成为海岸防线的关键物种。
Mangroves have special salt-excreting glands that push out excess salt and dense breathing roots that poke above the mud to take in air. They also filter salt at the roots and dump small amounts into old leaves that drop off. These traits make them key coastal-protecting species.

含羞草、捕蝇草会“动”，它们算动物吗？

Mimosa and Venus flytrap 'move'; are they animals?

它们仍是植物。植物没有肌肉和神经，靠细胞快速失水（膨压变化）或生长性运动来动，速度远慢于动物肌肉，但确实能响应刺激。能运动是植物的特性之一，并非动物的专利。
They are still plants. Without muscle or nerve, they move by rapid water loss (turgor change) or growth movements, far slower than animal muscle but clearly responsive. Movement is one plant trait, not an animal monopoly.

瓶子草、猪笼草怎样用“陷阱”抓虫？

How do pitcher plants trap insects with 'pitfalls'?

猪笼草等的捕虫笼内壁光滑并带向下倒毛，昆虫滑入后被笼底的消化液淹死分解。笼口分泌的蜜露引诱猎物，笼盖防止雨水稀释消化液。它们多生长在贫氮环境，靠补虫获取氮素。
Pitcher plants have slippery inner walls with downward hairs; insects slip in and are drowned and digested by fluid at the bottom. Nectar at the rim lures prey, and a lid keeps rain from diluting the fluid. They grow in nitrogen-poor soils and gain nitrogen from bugs.

为什么切开的苹果放一会儿会变褐？

Why does a cut apple turn brown after sitting for a while?

苹果细胞破损后，多酚氧化酶接触到酚类物质和氧气，把它们氧化成褐色的醌类，这是酶促褐变。柠檬汁里的维生素C能减慢这个过程，低温也能延缓。这种反应本身无害，只是卖相变差。
When apple cells are broken, the enzyme polyphenol oxidase meets phenolic compounds and oxygen, oxidizing them into brown quinones—enzymatic browning. Vitamin C in lemon juice slows it, and cold temperature also helps. The reaction is harmless, just less appealing.

含羞草为什么会“害羞”地合上叶子？

Why does the mimosa plant close its leaves as if it were shy?

含羞草叶子受到触碰时，叶枕细胞会迅速失水使叶片下垂闭合，这是一种快速的运动反应。它能吓退啃食的昆虫或减轻风雨伤害，属于植物的防御行为。几分钟后水分重新分布，叶子又会慢慢张开。
When a mimosa leaf is touched, cells in its pulvinus rapidly lose water, causing the leaflet to droop and close in a fast movement response. This startles leaf-eating insects or reduces damage from wind and rain, acting as a defensive behavior. After a few minutes the water redistributes and the leaf reopens.

为什么秋天树叶会变黄变红？

Why do leaves turn yellow and red in autumn?

入秋后日照变短、气温下降，树木回收叶子里的叶绿素等养分，绿色褪去后原本被掩盖的黄色类胡萝卜素显现。有些树还会合成红色花青素保护叶片、辅助养分回流。这是落叶前的“告别表演”。
As days shorten and cool in autumn, trees reclaim nutrients like chlorophyll from leaves; the green fades to reveal hidden yellow carotenoids. Some trees also make red anthocyanins to protect leaves and aid nutrient recovery. It is a farewell show before dropping leaves.

向日葵真的会一直跟着太阳转吗？

Does the sunflower really keep turning to follow the sun?

未成熟的花盘在白天确实会随太阳从东转到西，夜晚再悄悄转回东边，这叫向光性。开花成熟后花盘基本固定朝东，不再转动。朝东能让花在清晨更快回暖，吸引更多传粉昆虫。
Before the flower head matures it does track the sun from east to west during the day and reorients east overnight, a behavior called heliotropism. Once mature, the head usually locks facing east and stops moving. Facing east lets it warm up faster in the morning and attract more pollinators.
