端粒和端粒酶有什么作用？

What do telomeres and telomerase do?

端粒是染色体末端的重复序列“保护帽”，每次复制会变短；端粒酶能重建端粒，干细胞和多数癌细胞借此维持长度、延长寿命。
Telomeres are protective repeat 'caps' at chromosome ends that shorten with each replication; telomerase rebuilds them, letting stem cells and most cancer cells stay long-lived.

DNA聚合酶为什么需要引物？冈崎片段是什么？

Why does DNA polymerase need a primer, and what are Okazaki fragments?

DNA聚合酶只能从已有核苷酸的3′端延伸，需RNA引物起始；后随链不连续合成，形成许多短的冈崎片段，再被连接酶连成完整链。
DNA polymerase can only extend from an existing 3′ end, so an RNA primer is needed to start; the lagging strand is made in short Okazaki fragments that are later joined by ligase.

DNA复制为什么叫“半保留”？

Why is DNA replication called 'semi-conservative'?

复制时双链解开，每条旧链作为模板合成一条新链，因此每个子代DNA分子都含一条旧链和一条新链，各保留一半亲代信息。
The two strands separate, each serving as a template for a new strand, so every daughter DNA molecule keeps one old and one new strand—conserving half the parental information.
