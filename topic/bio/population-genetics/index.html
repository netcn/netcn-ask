为什么芬兰人群富集了多种在世界其他地区很罕见的隐性遗传病？奠基者效应在其中起了什么作用？

Why does the Finnish population carry an unusually high burden of recessive disorders that are rare elsewhere, and what role did the founder effect play?

芬兰约两千年前由来自西南海岸的少数移民奠基，此后经历瘟疫、饥荒与战争造成的多次瓶颈，并长期内婚隔离。奠基者携带的个别稀有等位基因随种群扩张被漂变随机放大，使三十余种隐性病在当地异常高发（如芬兰型先天性肾病综合征），而囊性纤维化、苯丙酮尿症等别处常见病反而少见；共享的单倍型片段还显著便利了致病基因的定位克隆。
Finland was founded roughly two millennia ago by a small band of settlers from the southwestern coast; epidemics, famines and wars later squeezed the population through repeated bottlenecks, and marriage stayed largely local. Random drift amplified a few rare alleles carried by these founders, so more than thirty recessive disorders are unusually common (e.g., Finnish-type congenital nephrotic syndrome), while cystic fibrosis and phenylketonuria stay rare. Shared ancestral haplotypes also greatly eased positional cloning of the culprit genes.

什么是遗传漂变？为什么它在小种群中的作用尤其强烈？

What is genetic drift, and why does it act especially strongly in small populations?

遗传漂变指等位基因频率因有限种群的抽样偶然性而在世代间随机波动。小种群每代参与繁殖的个体少，抽样误差大：杂合度每代约按1/(2Ne)衰减，等位基因更易被随机固定或彻底丢失。布里的果蝇bw品系实验中，数十个平行小群体的基因频率方差逐代扩大，许多品系最终完全固定，直观证明漂变强度随有效种群缩小而急剧上升。
Genetic drift is the random fluctuation of allele frequencies across generations caused by sampling chance in finite populations. When few individuals breed each generation, sampling error grows: heterozygosity declines by about 1/(2Ne) per generation, and alleles are readily fixed or lost at random. In Buri's classic bw experiment with Drosophila, allele-frequency variance among dozens of replicate small lines widened every generation until many were completely fixed, showing that drift strengthens sharply as effective population size falls.

#遗传漂变 #有效种群大小 #固定与丢失

佛罗里达美洲狮因长期孤立出现弯尾、心脏缺陷等问题，1995年引入得州近缘美洲狮后种群迅速恢复。基因流在其中起了什么作用？

Florida panthers, long isolated, developed kinked tails, heart defects and other problems; after closely related cougars from Texas were introduced in 1995, the population rebounded. What role did gene flow play?

小而孤立的种群容易近交，隐性有害变异纯合导致弯尾、隐睾、心脏缺损等缺陷。1995年引入8头雌性得州美洲狮带来新等位基因，杂交后代杂合度上升，缺陷比例大幅下降，幼崽存活率提高，种群从约30头增长到上百头。这一“遗传拯救”表明适度的基因流能快速恢复适合度，已成为保护遗传学的重要工具。
Small, isolated populations easily become inbred, and homozygosity for harmful recessive variants produces kinked tails, cryptorchidism and heart defects. In 1995 managers released eight female Texas cougars, adding fresh alleles: hybrid offspring carried higher heterozygosity, defect rates dropped sharply, kitten survival improved, and numbers climbed from roughly thirty to well over a hundred. This genetic rescue shows that modest gene flow rapidly restores fitness and is now a key conservation tool.
