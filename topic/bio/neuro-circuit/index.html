海马体里的位置细胞和网格细胞如何构建认知地图？

How do place cells and grid cells in the hippocampus build a cognitive map?

位置细胞在动物到达特定地点时放电，网格细胞提供六边形的空间坐标，二者合作编码位置与导航。
Place cells fire at specific locations while grid cells provide hexagonal coordinates, together encoding space and navigation.

突触可塑性（如 LTP）为什么被认为是记忆的基础？

Why is synaptic plasticity like LTP considered the basis of memory?

长时程增强让反复激活的突触变强，把一起放电的神经元连在一起，是学习与记忆的细胞机制。
Long-term potentiation strengthens repeatedly active synapses, neurons that fire together wire together, underlying learning and memory.

人打哈欠为什么会“传染”，明明自己并不困也会被带得打哈欠？

Why is yawning “contagious”—why do we yawn when not even tired, just seeing someone else?

这与大脑里的“镜像神经元”和共情有关：看到别人打哈欠，我们大脑会自动模仿，可能用于同步群体状态、提高警觉。黑猩猩和狗也有类似反应，是人类社交脑的体现。
It involves mirror neurons and empathy: seeing a yawn makes our brain mimic it, possibly to sync a group's state and boost alertness. Chimps and dogs do it too—a sign of our social brain.

多巴胺在奖赏回路里真的是快乐分子吗？

Is dopamine in the reward circuit really the happiness molecule?

更准确地说，多巴胺编码预测误差与动机——当结果好于预期时放电，驱动学习与渴求，而非单纯的快乐感受。
More precisely it encodes prediction error and motivation, firing when outcomes beat expectations to drive learning and craving, not just pleasure.

什么是神经振荡（脑电波），它和认知有什么关系？

What are neural oscillations (brain waves) and how do they relate to cognition?

神经元群体同步放电产生不同频段的节律：γ 波关联注意、θ 波关联记忆检索，跨区同步支撑信息整合。
Population rhythms vary by frequency, gamma tracks attention and theta memory retrieval, with cross-area synchrony integrating information.
