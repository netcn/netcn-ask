蝴蝶翅膀的斑斓色彩，是颜料还是结构造成的？

Are butterfly wing colors from pigment or structure?

两者都有：部分来自色素，更多来自鳞片微观结构对光的干涉与衍射（结构色），所以某些角度会变色且颜色鲜亮不褪色。
Both: some from pigment, but more from microscopic scale structures causing light interference and diffraction (structural color), so colors shift with angle and stay vivid without fading.

达·芬奇的人体解剖素描对生物学有什么意义？

What significance did da Vinci's anatomical drawings have for biology?

他亲自解剖尸体并精确描绘肌肉、骨骼与器官，把艺术观察与解剖科学结合，在印刷术尚未普及时就推动了对人体结构的准确认知。
He dissected bodies himself and drew muscles, bones and organs precisely, merging art with anatomy and advancing accurate understanding of human structure before widespread printing.

为什么自然界里充满了对称美，比如花瓣和贝壳？

Why is nature full of symmetrical beauty, like petals and shells?

双侧对称和辐射对称有利于发育稳定、运动平衡和吸引传粉者；对称常被人类视为美，是进化规律与审美直觉的交汇。
Bilateral and radial symmetry aid developmental stability, movement balance and pollinator attraction; symmetry is widely perceived as beautiful, where evolution meets aesthetic intuition.

为什么莲花、樱花等植物常被不同文化当作艺术与精神符号？

Why are plants like lotus and cherry blossom used as artistic and spiritual symbols across cultures?

它们鲜明的形态与生命周期（出淤泥而不染、花期短暂）契合人们对纯洁、无常与重生的联想，因而频繁出现在绘画、纹样与文学中。
Their striking forms and life cycles (rising clean from mud, brief blooming) resonate with ideas of purity, impermanence and rebirth, so they recur in painting, patterns and literature.

为什么许多画家喜欢用植物、花卉和昆虫作静物题材？

Why have many painters favored plants, flowers and insects in still life?

植物与昆虫既有丰富的形态与色彩，又常承载象征意义（如荷兰静物寓意生命短暂），体现了自然观察与艺术表达的紧密结合。
Plants and insects offer rich form and color and often carry symbolism (e.g. Dutch still lifes as vanitas, hinting at life's brevity), blending natural observation with artistic expression.
