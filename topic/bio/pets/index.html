猫从高处摔下有时存活率反而更高，这是真的吗？

Is it true cats sometimes survive high falls better than mid-height ones?

猫有翻正反射能在空中调整姿态四脚着地，且体态较轻、终端速度较低、落地时肌肉松弛可分散冲击；研究确实发现伤情在一定高度内随高度上升而加重，超过某临界后反而略缓，但高楼坠落仍极危险。
Cats have a righting reflex to land feet-first, are light with a lower terminal velocity, and relax muscles to spread impact; studies even show injury risk rises with height then plateaus above a threshold, yet high-rise falls remain very dangerous.

狗摇尾巴就一定代表开心吗？

Does a wagging tail always mean a dog is happy?

尾巴是狗重要的情绪与社交信号：研究指出向右偏摇多与积极情绪相关、向左偏多伴随不安，摇动的速度和高度也有含义，因此摇尾并不都表示高兴。
The tail is a key emotional and social signal: studies link right-biased wags to positive states and left-biased to anxiety, with speed and height also meaningful, so wagging is not always happiness.

猫为什么要用爪子把自己排出的粪便埋起来？

Why do cats bury their feces with their paws?

野生猫科动物掩埋排泄物以减少气味，避免被捕食者和竞争者发现；家猫多保留这一本能，但群体中地位高的猫有时并不掩埋。
Wild felids bury waste to reduce scent and avoid detection by predators and rivals; house cats retain this instinct, though dominant individuals may skip it.

猫的呼噜声（purr）是怎么发出来的？

How does a cat produce its purr?

猫通过喉部肌肉快速节律性振动，配合吸气与呼气产生约 25 赫兹的持续声响；它既在满足时出现，也会在疼痛或紧张时自我安抚，频率还可能有助于组织修复。
Cats produce purring by rapid rhythmic contraction of laryngeal muscles during both inhalation and exhalation at about 25 Hz; it appears in contentment but also self-soothing during pain, and the frequency may aid tissue repair.

猫为什么总在黎明和黄昏最活跃？

Why are cats most active at dawn and dusk?

猫的祖先是晨昏性猎手，在黎明黄昏捕猎效率最高；家猫保留了这一内在节律，所以常在人睡觉的清晨和深夜跑酷。
Cats descend from crepuscular hunters most active at dawn and dusk; house cats retain this internal rhythm, so they often zoom around when people are asleep.

兔子为什么会吃自己排出的一种软便？

Why do rabbits eat a special kind of soft droppings they produce?

兔子是食粪动物：盲肠发酵产生富含 B 族维生素和蛋白的软粪（盲肠便），夜间直接吞食以二次吸收营养，这是它们高效利用纤维素的关键。
Rabbits are coprophagic: the cecum ferments a nutrient-rich soft pellet (cecotrope) full of B vitamins and protein, re-ingested at night for a second pass of nutrition—key to using cellulose efficiently.

仓鼠为什么把食物塞进腮帮子里？

Why do hamsters stuff food into their cheeks?

仓鼠的颊囊像临时储物袋，能把找到的食物一次性搬回安全的巢穴再慢慢吃，野外用来应对食物不稳定的环境。
A hamster's cheek pouches are temporary storage bags that carry found food back to a safe nest for later, helping wild hamsters cope with unreliable food.

金鱼真的只有几秒记忆吗？

Do goldfish really have only a few seconds of memory?

不会。实验表明金鱼记忆可维持数月，能学会辨认喂食信号、避开电击和走迷宫，几秒记忆只是流传已久的误解。
No. Experiments show goldfish memory lasts months; they can learn to recognize feeding cues, avoid shocks and navigate mazes, so the few-seconds claim is a myth.

小狗为什么爱追着自己的尾巴转圈？

Why do puppies chase their own tails?

幼犬多为玩耍和探索行为，尾巴一动就激发追逐本能；成年犬若频繁追尾，则可能出于无聊、焦虑或皮肤寄生虫问题，需要区分看待。
In puppies it is mostly play and exploration sparked by a moving tail; in adults frequent tail-chasing may signal boredom, anxiety or skin parasites and should be evaluated.
