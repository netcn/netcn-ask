疫苗里的“佐剂”起什么作用？

What do adjuvants in vaccines do?

佐剂本身不是抗原，而是刺激免疫系统、延长抗原暴露并增强炎症反应，从而让疫苗产生更强更持久的抗体应答。
An adjuvant is not the antigen; it stimulates the immune system, prolongs antigen exposure, and boosts inflammation, yielding a stronger, longer-lasting antibody response.

mRNA疫苗是怎样让细胞生产抗原的？

How do mRNA vaccines make cells produce antigens?

疫苗把编码病毒抗原的mRNA送入细胞，细胞据此合成抗原蛋白，免疫系统识别后产生抗体和记忆，且mRNA很快被降解不留体内。
The vaccine delivers mRNA encoding a viral antigen; cells make the antigen protein, the immune system responds with antibodies and memory, and the mRNA is quickly degraded.

减毒活疫苗和灭活疫苗有何区别？

How do live-attenuated and inactivated vaccines differ?

减毒活疫苗用弱化但仍活的病原，免疫强且持久但免疫缺陷者慎用；灭活疫苗用杀死的病原，更安全但常需加强针。
Live-attenuated vaccines use weakened live pathogens—strong, lasting immunity but cautious in the immunocompromised; inactivated ones use killed pathogens—safer but often need boosters.
