Hox基因如何充当身体的“位置密码”？

How do Hox genes act as the body's 'position code'?

Hox基因沿胚胎前后轴按顺序排列，决定各体节发育成何种结构，从蝇到人都高度保守，是动物身体蓝图的核心调控基因。
Hox genes are arrayed along the embryo's head-to-tail axis and specify what each segment becomes; conserved from flies to humans, they are core to the animal body plan.

内共生学说怎样解释线粒体的来历？

How does endosymbiotic theory explain the origin of mitochondria?

该学说认为线粒体源自被早期真核细胞吞入并共生的细菌，保留了自己的环形DNA和双层膜，证据包括其分裂方式与细菌相似。
The theory holds that mitochondria came from bacteria engulfed and kept as symbionts by early eukaryotes, retaining circular DNA and double membranes, and dividing like bacteria.

所有真核生物的共同祖先是谁？

Who is the common ancestor of all eukaryotes?

真核生物（动物、植物、真菌、原生生物）都源自一个拥有细胞核和线粒体的共同祖先，约在十几亿年前出现，之后分化出各大类群。
All eukaryotes (animals, plants, fungi, protists) descend from a common ancestor with a nucleus and mitochondria that appeared over a billion years ago and then diversified.

猛犸象基因组研究有哪些意义？

What is the significance of woolly mammoth genome research?

测序猛犸象基因组帮助重建其适应寒冷的特征，也为探讨“去灭绝”与冷冻标本的遗传保护提供模型和伦理讨论。
Sequencing the mammoth genome helps reconstruct its cold adaptations and fuels debate on 'de-extinction' and genetic conservation of frozen specimens.

古DNA为何难以保存？科学家如何克服？

Why is ancient DNA hard to preserve, and how do scientists cope?

古DNA会因水解和氧化高度降解、且易被现代DNA污染，科学家用无菌发掘、超净实验室和短片段测序来提取并验证古老序列。
Ancient DNA degrades badly via hydrolysis and oxidation and is easily contaminated; scientists use sterile digging, clean labs, and short-fragment sequencing to recover and verify old sequences.

同源器官和同功器官有什么不同？

How do homologous and analogous structures differ?

同源器官源自共同祖先、结构相似（如蝙蝠翼与人臂）；同功器官功能相似却来源不同（如昆虫翅与鸟翼），分别揭示亲缘与趋同演化。
Homologous structures share ancestry and layout (bat wing vs human arm); analogous ones share function but not origin (insect vs bird wing), revealing kinship versus convergent evolution.

同源器官为什么能揭示亲缘关系？

Why do homologous structures reveal relatedness?

同源器官（如人臂、蝙蝠翼、鲸鳍的骨骼布局）源自共同祖先的同一结构，虽功能不同但骨骼排列相似，是演化亲缘的化石级证据。
Homologous structures (human arm, bat wing, whale flipper) derive from the same ancestral blueprint; despite different functions, similar bone layout reveals shared ancestry.
