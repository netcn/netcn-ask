什么是营养级联（trophic cascade）？

What is a trophic cascade?

顶级捕食者数量的变化会层层向下影响食草动物和植物，例如狼的回归让河流两岸的植被得以恢复。
Predator changes ripple down to herbivores and plants, as when wolves returned and restored riverside vegetation.

食物网的连通性为什么重要？

Why does the connectivity of a food web matter?

连接越多的物种网络越复杂，扰动可能沿多条路径传播；适度的冗余能缓冲单一物种丧失带来的冲击。
Highly connected webs propagate perturbations along many paths, while redundancy buffers the shock of losing one species.

为什么食物网中很少出现很长的食物链（超过五个营养级）？

Why are long food chains (over five trophic levels) rare?

能量沿营养级大约只传递 10%，越往上可支撑的生物量越少，热力学限制了食物链的长度。
Only about 10% of energy passes each level, so biomass and support dwindle, limiting chain length by thermodynamics.

保护关键种为什么常常比保护数量多的物种更划算？

Why is protecting a keystone species often more cost-effective than protecting abundant ones?

关键种一旦被移除会引发连锁崩溃，保护它能顺带保住依赖它的整个食物网，投入产出比很高。
Removing a keystone collapses the web, so protecting it safeguards the dependent food web efficiently.
