酸奶包装上写的活性菌，和常说的益生菌是一回事吗？

Are the live cultures on yogurt labels the same as probiotics?

酸奶常用的发酵菌（如保加利亚乳杆菌）多在肠道中过路而不长期定植；益生菌则强调某株菌在宿主内能定植或明确发挥健康功能，二者定义和证据标准不同。
Common yogurt starters like Lactobacillus bulgaricus are mostly transient in the gut; probiotics are defined by a specific strain that colonizes or clearly benefits the host, with different evidentiary standards.

为什么煮熟的米饭放久了会产生蜡样芽孢杆菌的风险？

Why can cooked rice left out risk Bacillus cereus poisoning?

蜡样芽孢杆菌的耐热芽孢在米饭蒸煮后依然存活，米饭在室温下存放会大量繁殖并产呕吐毒素，之后即使再加热也难以完全破坏毒素。
Heat-resistant Bacillus cereus spores survive cooking; rice left at room temperature lets them multiply and make an emetic toxin that reheating often fails to fully destroy.

为什么有些疫苗要打好几针（加强针）？

Why do some vaccines need several doses (boosters)?

初次接种建立基础免疫，但抗体水平会随时间下降；后续加强针再次刺激免疫系统，使记忆细胞扩增、抗体滴度回升，从而维持长期保护。
The first dose builds a baseline response but antibody levels wane; later boosters re-stimulate the immune system, expanding memory cells and raising antibody titers for lasting protection.

酱油、纳豆等发酵豆制品为什么风味那么独特？

Why do fermented soy foods like soy sauce and natto taste so distinctive?

米曲霉等霉菌先分解大豆蛋白为氨基酸和肽，酵母菌和细菌再产生酯类、有机酸等风味物，多菌种协作造就了鲜香复杂的味道。
Molds like Aspergillus oryzae first break soy protein into amino acids and peptides, then yeasts and bacteria add esters and organic acids—multi-microbe teamwork yields the complex savory flavor.

人体表面和肠道里为什么住着那么多微生物？

Why do so many microbes live on our skin and in our gut?

这些常驻微生物与人体形成共生：帮助消化、合成维生素、训练免疫系统，并通过占位效应抵御病原菌，是健康的重要部分。
These resident microbes form a symbiosis: they aid digestion, synthesize vitamins, train the immune system and block pathogens by colonization resistance—central to health.

为什么受伤后用酒精或碘伏消毒？

Why do we disinfect wounds with alcohol or iodine?

它们能使微生物的蛋白质变性或被氧化灭活，减少伤口表面的细菌数量；但对已经侵入组织深部的感染作用有限，深部感染需就医。
They denature or oxidize microbial proteins to inactivate them and cut surface bacteria; they are limited against infection already deep in tissue, which needs medical care.

发霉的面包不能吃，但青霉素也是霉变来的，两者为何不同？

Moldy bread is inedible, yet penicillin comes from mold—why the difference?

面包上的霉常产生黄曲霉毒素等有毒霉菌毒素；青霉素来自特定青霉菌株，经培养、提纯和严格质控得到，来源与剂量都完全不同。
Bread molds often make toxic mycotoxins like aflatoxin, whereas penicillin comes from a specific, purified Penicillium strain under strict quality control—different source and dose entirely.

益生菌到底是什么，吃了真的有用吗？

What exactly are probiotics, and do they really help?

益生菌是足量摄入可对宿主健康产生益处的活微生物，某些株能改善腹泻、过敏和肠道不适；但效果高度依赖菌株和个体，并非包治百病。
Probiotics are live microbes that benefit host health when taken in adequate amounts; some strains help diarrhea, allergies and gut discomfort, but effects are strain- and person-specific, not a cure-all.

为什么普通感冒吃抗生素通常没用？

Why are antibiotics usually useless against the common cold?

感冒大多由病毒引起，而抗生素只针对细菌；滥用抗生素还会破坏正常菌群并催生耐药菌，所以感冒一般靠休息和症状缓解。
Colds are mostly viral while antibiotics target bacteria; misuse also disrupts normal flora and breeds resistance, so colds are managed by rest and symptom relief.

食物放进冰箱是不是就永远不会坏了？

Does refrigeration mean food never spoils?

低温只减缓微生物生长和酶反应，并不能杀死它们；部分嗜冷菌在冰箱里仍会缓慢繁殖，所以久放依然会变质甚至产毒。
Cold only slows microbial growth and enzyme reactions, it does not kill them; some psychrotrophs still grow slowly in the fridge, so food eventually spoils or even becomes toxic.

脚气（足癣）是细菌感染引起的吗？

Is athlete's foot (tinea) caused by bacteria?

不是。脚气是皮肤癣菌这类真菌感染引起的，温暖潮湿的环境容易发作，需要用抗真菌药而非抗生素。
No. Athlete's foot is a fungal infection by dermatophytes, thriving in warm moist conditions and needing antifungal, not antibiotic, treatment.

用肥皂洗手为什么能有效预防生病？

Why does washing hands with soap prevent illness so well?

肥皂能破坏许多病菌的脂质包膜并靠搓洗机械带走污垢，显著减少手上的病原体，是最经济有效的防病方法之一。
Soap disrupts the lipid envelopes of many germs and mechanically lifts dirt away, sharply cutting hand pathogens—one of the cheapest, most effective disease preventions.
