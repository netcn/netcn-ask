神经科学中「神经调质」与经典神经递质在作用方式上有何本质区别？

What is the essential difference in action between neuromodulators and classical neurotransmitters in neuroscience?

经典递质在明确的突触间隙快速、点对点地传递信号；神经调质（如多巴胺、血清素）则由弥散释放，经代谢型受体缓慢而广泛地改变大群神经元的兴奋性与可塑性状态。调质因此更像是「调节背景」，而非传递具体消息。
Classical transmitters act rapidly and point-to-point across defined synapses, whereas neuromodulators such as dopamine and serotonin are released diffusely and act slowly through metabotropic receptors to broadly shift the excitability and plasticity of neuronal populations. Modulators thus set a regulatory background rather than conveying specific messages.

NMDA 受体依赖的 LTP 诱导为什么需要「双重门控」，其分子逻辑是什么？

Why does NMDA-receptor-dependent LTP induction require coincidence detection, and what is its molecular logic?

NMDA 受体既是递质门控又是电压门控：只有当谷氨酸结合且突触后膜因已去极化而解除镁离子阻塞时，钙离子才流入。这种双重条件使 LTP 仅在「突触前释放递质且突触后已活跃」同时发生时被诱导，从而把时序相关性编码为突触强化。
The NMDA receptor is both ligand- and voltage-gated: calcium enters only when glutamate is bound and the postsynaptic membrane is already depolarized enough to relieve magnesium block. This dual condition means LTP is induced only when presynaptic release coincides with postsynaptic activity, encoding temporal correlation as synaptic strengthening.

神经元如何通过动作电位的「速率编码」与「时间编码」两种方式表示信息？

How do neurons represent information through rate coding versus temporal coding of action potentials?

速率编码以单位时间内放电频率的高低反映刺激强度，是最早被确认的编码方式。时间编码则强调放电相对于特定时间基准（如刺激起点或脑振荡相位）的精确时刻，可在毫秒尺度携带额外信息，常见于听觉与嗅觉系统。
Rate coding uses the average firing frequency over time to reflect stimulus intensity and was the first identified scheme. Temporal coding emphasizes the precise timing of spikes relative to a reference such as stimulus onset or ongoing oscillations, carrying extra information on the millisecond scale, as seen in auditory and olfactory systems.

闭环脑机接口相比开环系统有何优势，举例说明其应用场景？

What advantages do closed-loop brain-computer interfaces have over open-loop systems, and what is an example application?

闭环系统实时读取神经状态并反馈刺激或提示，能根据表现动态调整，提升控制稳定性与学习效果。例如运动皮层闭环刺激可在中风康复中依据尝试性运动意图给予同步电刺激，促进功能重建。
Closed-loop systems read neural state in real time and deliver feedback stimulation or cues, dynamically adjusting to performance to improve stability and learning. For example, closed-loop motor-cortex stimulation can synchronize electrical input with intended movement attempts during stroke rehabilitation to promote functional recovery.

侵入式脑机接口通常记录哪类神经信号，其空间分辨率和风险有何特点？

What neural signals do invasive brain-computer interfaces typically record, and what are their spatial resolution and risk trade-offs?

侵入式接口常通过皮层电极阵列记录单个神经元或局部场电位的放电活动，空间分辨率可达单个细胞级别，信号质量高。代价是需要开颅手术植入，存在感染、瘢痕组织包裹电极导致信号衰减等风险。
Invasive interfaces often use cortical electrode arrays to record single-unit or local-field-potential activity, reaching single-cell spatial resolution with high signal quality. The cost is craniotomy for implantation and risks such as infection and glial scarring that degrade signals over time.

大脑皮层中「柱状结构」反映了神经组织哪项组织原则？

What organizational principle of neural tissue does the columnar structure of the cerebral cortex reflect?

柱状结构指皮层内垂直于表面的细胞以相似感受野或功能纵向排列成柱，体现功能模块化与局部回路重复的思想。这种组织方式使相近的处理单元被紧凑复用，有利于并行加工与层级化信息流。
Columnar structure refers to vertically arranged cells within the cortex sharing similar receptive fields or functions, reflecting functional modularity and repeated local circuits. This organization compactly reuses similar processing units, supporting parallel processing and hierarchical information flow.

长期增强效应（LTP）与长期抑制效应（LTD）在突触可塑性中分别起什么作用？

What roles do long-term potentiation (LTP) and long-term depression (LTD) play in synaptic plasticity?

LTP 指高频刺激后突触传递效率持续增强，被认为是学习与记忆的细胞基础；LTD 则相反，指突触强度持续减弱。两者通过调节 AMPA 受体数量与磷酸化状态，双向微调神经元间的连接权重。
LTP is a persistent strengthening of synaptic transmission after high-frequency stimulation and is viewed as a cellular basis of learning and memory; LTD is its counterpart, a lasting weakening. Both bidirectionally tune connection weights between neurons by regulating the number and phosphorylation of AMPA receptors.

什么是脑机接口中的「解码器」，它如何把神经活动转化为控制指令？

What is a decoder in a brain-computer interface, and how does it turn neural activity into control commands?

解码器是一组统计模型（如线性回归、卡尔曼滤波或神经网络），先把记录的神经信号提取为特征，再映射到期望的运动或输出变量。系统通常通过用户反馈进行在线训练，使模型逐步适应用户的皮层活动模式。
A decoder is a set of statistical models such as linear regression, Kalman filtering or neural networks that extract features from recorded neural signals and map them to desired movement or output variables. The system is usually trained online with user feedback so the model gradually adapts to the individual's cortical patterns.
