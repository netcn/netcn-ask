昙花为什么只在夜间短暂开放？

Why does the night-blooming cereus open only briefly at night?

昙花原产热带干旱环境，夜间开放可避开烈日减少水分流失，并依赖蛾类等夜间传粉者，因此演化出短促的夜间开花习性。
Native to hot dry habitats, it blooms at night to avoid water loss in harsh sun and to attract moth pollinators, evolving a brief nocturnal flowering habit.

捕蝇草会动、含羞草会合，植物有神经吗？

Venus flytraps move and Mimosa folds—do plants have nerves?

植物没有神经系统，它们靠细胞膨压的快速变化和动作电位（离子流）实现运动，与动物的神经机制完全不同，却同样能感知和响应刺激。
Plants have no nervous system; they move via rapid turgor changes and action potentials (ion flows), a mechanism entirely different from animal nerves yet still able to sense and respond.

含羞草一碰叶子就合拢，它是怎么做到的？

How does Mimosa pudica fold its leaves when touched?

触碰刺激使叶枕细胞迅速流失钾离子和水分、膨压下降，叶片因此下垂；膨压变化还能以电信号方式在植株内传导，引起相邻小叶接连闭合。
Touch triggers rapid loss of potassium and water from pulvinus cells, dropping turgor so leaves fold; the turgor change spreads as electrical signals, closing neighboring leaflets in turn.

竹子为什么常常开花之后就整片枯死？

Why do bamboos often die after flowering?

多数竹子是一次性开花植物：多年积累后同步开花结实，耗尽体内储藏资源而枯死；这种同步大量结实在进化上可能是一种用数量淹没种子捕食者的策略。
Most bamboos are monocarpic: after years of storage they flower and seed synchronously, exhausting reserves and dying; the mass seeding may be an evolutionary predator-saturation strategy.

枫叶为什么到了秋天会变红？

Why do maple leaves turn red in autumn?

入秋后叶绿素分解，原本被遮盖的类胡萝卜素显露出黄色；同时低温和强光照促使叶片合成花青素，呈现红色，花青素还能保护叶片延迟凋落。
In autumn chlorophyll breaks down, revealing yellow carotenoids, while cold and bright light spur anthocyanin synthesis for red; anthocyanins also protect leaves and delay drop.

大蒜切开后的刺激性气味是从哪里来的？

Where does the pungent smell of cut garlic come from?

大蒜破损后，蒜氨酸酶把无味的蒜素前体转化为大蒜素等含硫化合物，这些物质气味刺鼻，并具有广谱抗菌和驱虫作用。
When crushed, alliinase converts odorless precursors into allicin and other sulfur compounds, which are pungent and have broad antimicrobial and insect-repellent effects.

为什么有些植物会咬人，比如荨麻？

Why do some plants sting, like nettles?

荨麻的刺毛像微型注射器，触碰时注入甲酸、组胺等混合物引发刺痛红肿，这是防御食草动物的物理化学武器。
Nettle stinging hairs act like tiny syringes, injecting formic acid and histamine on contact to cause pain and swelling—a physical-chemical defense against herbivores.

秋天有些树叶变黄、有些变红，机制一样吗？

In autumn some leaves turn yellow and some red—same mechanism?

变黄主要是叶绿素降解、露出原本就有的类胡萝卜素；变红则是叶片临时新合成花青素，两者机制并不相同。
Yellowing is mainly chlorophyll breakdown revealing preexisting carotenoids, while red is newly synthesized anthocyanin—different mechanisms.

芦荟为什么常被用来处理烫伤和晒伤？

Why is aloe vera used for burns and sunburn?

芦荟凝胶含多糖和多种化合物，能保湿、舒缓并轻度抗炎，对轻微晒伤和烫伤有帮助；但深度烧伤仍需及时就医。
Aloe gel holds polysaccharides and compounds that moisturize, soothe and mildly reduce inflammation, helping minor sunburns and burns—but deep burns still need a doctor.

切洋葱前先放冰箱冷藏一下，为什么没那么辣眼睛？

Why does chilling onions before cutting make them less eye-stinging?

低温会降低蒜氨酸酶活性，也减缓催泪因子的挥发扩散，所以冷藏后再切时刺激明显减轻。
Low temperature lowers alliinase activity and slows the volatile lachrymatory factor's diffusion, so chilled onions sting far less when cut.

多肉植物为什么那么耐旱？

Why are succulents so drought-tolerant?

它们的茎叶有发达的储水组织，表皮角质层厚、气孔少且在夜间开放（CAM 途径），能最大限度地减少水分流失。
Their stems and leaves store water, have thick cuticles, few stomata and open them at night (CAM pathway), minimizing water loss.

薄荷为什么让人觉得清凉？

Why does mint feel cooling?

薄荷醇能激活皮肤上的冷觉感受器（TRPM8），向大脑传递冷的信号，实际上皮肤温度并没有降低。
Menthol activates cold-sensing receptors (TRPM8) in the skin, signaling coolness to the brain even though actual temperature does not drop.
