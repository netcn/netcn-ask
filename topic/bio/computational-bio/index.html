蛋白质结构预测（如 AlphaFold）为什么被称为里程碑？

Why is protein structure prediction such as AlphaFold called a milestone?

从氨基酸序列直接预测三维结构长期是难题，深度学习已能以接近实验的精度给出结构，极大加速了药物和功能研究。
Predicting 3D structure from sequence was long unsolved; deep learning now achieves near-experimental accuracy, accelerating drug and function studies.

宏基因组组装中的分箱（binning）是在做什么？

What does binning do in metagenome assembly?

面对混合样本里大量碎片化的 DNA，分箱按序列组成和覆盖度把片段归到各自的微生物基因组，重建无需培养的单菌基因组。
Binning groups fragments by composition and coverage to reconstruct individual genome bins without culturing each microbe.

比较基因组学如何帮助发现新基因的功能？

How does comparative genomics help discover gene functions?

跨物种比对保守序列，能在亲缘关系远的生物间找出功能相似的基因，并定位非编码的调控区域。
Cross-species comparison flags conserved sequences that hint at function and pinpoints regulatory non-coding regions.

什么是系统生物学，它和传统拆零件的思路有何不同？

What is systems biology and how does it differ from the traditional reductionist approach?

还原论研究单个零件，系统生物学则用模型把基因、蛋白和代谢网络整体建模，以理解涌现出的整体行为。
Reductionism studies parts alone; systems biology models gene-protein-metabolite networks as a whole to understand emergent behavior.

什么是系统发育树，它是怎样画出来的？

What is a phylogenetic tree and how is it built?

系统发育树用 DNA 或蛋白序列的相似程度推断物种亲缘关系，分支越靠近代表共同祖先越近。
It infers relatedness from sequence similarity, with closer branches indicating more recent common ancestors.
