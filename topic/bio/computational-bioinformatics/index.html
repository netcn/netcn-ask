蛋白质语言模型（如 ESM）为何能不依赖同源模板直接推断结构与功能？

Why can protein language models (such as ESM) infer structure and function without relying on homology templates?

蛋白质语言模型在海量未标注序列上做自监督学习，把残基共现规律编码为「语言」表征，使模型隐式捕捉进化约束与结构偏好。这种表征可零样本预测突变效应与远程同源，弥补传统比对在孤儿基因上的不足。
Protein language models are self-supervised on vast unlabeled sequences, encoding residue co-occurrence patterns as linguistic representations that implicitly capture evolutionary constraints and structural preferences. Such representations enable zero-shot prediction of mutation effects and remote homology, compensating for traditional alignment gaps on orphan genes.

二代测序（NGS）的边合成边测序原理如何兼顾通量与准确性？

How does the sequencing-by-synthesis principle of next-generation sequencing (NGS) balance throughput and accuracy?

NGS 将 DNA 打断为海量片段并并行扩增，测序时每掺入一个荧光标记核苷酸就成像一次，数百万簇同时读取。借助高覆盖度与质量值过滤，单碱基错误可被统计校正，在低成本高通量下保持可用准确性。
NGS fragments DNA into massive parallel amplicons and images each incorporated fluorescent nucleotide, reading millions of clusters simultaneously. High coverage combined with quality-score filtering statistically corrects per-base errors, preserving usable accuracy at low cost and high throughput.

单细胞 RNA 测序（scRNA-seq）相比 bulk 测序能解答哪些独特的生物学问题？

What unique biological questions can single-cell RNA sequencing (scRNA-seq) answer compared with bulk sequencing?

bulk 测序给出细胞群体平均表达，掩盖异质性；scRNA-seq 解析单个细胞的转录组，可识别稀有细胞类型、细胞状态连续谱与发育轨迹。它已广泛用于绘制组织细胞图谱与解析肿瘤微环境。
Bulk sequencing reports average expression of cell populations and masks heterogeneity; scRNA-seq resolves individual cell transcriptomes, revealing rare cell types, continuous cell-state spectra and developmental trajectories. It is widely used to map tissue cell atlases and dissect tumor microenvironments.

什么是序列比对中的「空位罚分」，它对同源检测有何影响？

What is gap penalty in sequence alignment, and how does it affect homology detection?

空位罚分是比对中引入插入或缺失（gap）所付出的得分代价，用于权衡序列中插入大段与切割成多段的取舍。罚分过低会产生不连续的假比对，过高则漏掉含真实结构重排的同源关系，需结合生物学先验设定。
A gap penalty is the score cost of introducing insertions or deletions in an alignment, trading off between long indels and fragmented matches. Too low a penalty yields spurious discontinuous alignments; too high misses homology involving real rearrangements, so it must be set with biological priors.

BLAST 搜索结果里的 E 值到底衡量什么?E 值等于 0.001 意味着什么?

What does the E-value in a BLAST search actually measure, and what does an E-value of 0.001 imply?

E 值是在给定数据库规模和打分系统下,纯粹因随机比对而期望出现的、得分不低于该命中的匹配条数。E=0.001 表示平均每一千次这样的搜索才会随机产生一条同等高分的结果。它并不是同源的直接概率,E 值越小证据越强,但解读仍要结合比对覆盖度与生物学背景。
The E-value is the expected number of matches scoring at least as well as this hit that would arise purely by chance, given the database size and scoring system. An E-value of 0.001 means that on average only about one hit of such quality would appear at random per thousand searches. It is not the direct probability of homology; smaller values mean stronger evidence, but interpretation still requires alignment coverage and biological context.

面对数十亿碱基对的公共数据库,BLAST 凭什么思路做到秒级检索同源序列?

Against databases of billions of residues, how does BLAST manage near-instant homology searches?

它把查询序列拆成短词,先用打分矩阵筛出可能的高分词,再只在数据库中定位这些种子并向两侧延伸成局部比对。绝大多数无关区域从不进入动态规划运算,耗时由序列长度的乘积降到近似线性。这一启发式策略只牺牲极小的灵敏度,换来可扩展的大规模检索能力。
BLAST splits the query into short words, pre-filters for likely high-scoring ones with a scoring matrix, locates those seeds in the database, and extends each hit sideways into a local alignment. Nearly all unrelated regions never enter dynamic programming, cutting cost from the product of sequence lengths to roughly linear time. The heuristic trades a sliver of sensitivity for retrieval power that scales.

AlphaFold 等深度学习方法预测蛋白质结构的核心突破是什么？

What is the core breakthrough of deep-learning methods like AlphaFold in protein structure prediction?

AlphaFold 利用进化同源序列隐含的共进化约束，结合注意力网络从多重序列比对推断残基间空间关系，再折叠为三维结构。它在 CASP14 上达到接近实验精度的水平，使结构预测从瓶颈变为可规模化的常规工具。
AlphaFold exploits co-evolutionary constraints hidden in homologous sequences, using attention networks to infer residue-residue spatial relations from multiple-sequence alignments and then folds them into 3D structures. At CASP14 it reached near-experimental accuracy, turning structure prediction from a bottleneck into a scalable routine tool.
