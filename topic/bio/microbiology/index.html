细菌的鞭毛是如何驱动运动的？

How do bacterial flagella drive movement?

细菌鞭毛是由鞭毛蛋白构成的旋转螺旋丝，基部有类似马达的基体和质子通道，靠质子动势驱动其像螺旋桨一样旋转，推动菌体前进或翻滚转向。其结构与真核细胞的鞭毛（微管构成、波浪摆动）完全不同。
A bacterial flagellum is a helical filament of flagellin with a motor-like basal body and proton channel at its base; proton motive force spins it like a propeller to push the cell forward or tumble to change direction. Its design is entirely different from eukaryotic flagella, which are microtubule-based and beat in waves.

补体系统通过哪些途径被激活，又如何清除病原体？

What pathways activate the complement system, and how does it eliminate pathogens?

补体是血浆中的一组酶原蛋白，经三条途径激活：经典途径（IgG/IgM与病原结合后C1q识别）、凝集素途径（甘露糖结合凝集素MBL识别病原表面糖类）、替代途径（病原表面自发低水平激活并被正反馈放大），三者汇合于C3转化酶，形成C5转化酶后启动膜攻击复合物。效应包括：C3b沉积在病原表面调理促进吞噬；C5b-C9膜攻击复合物在革兰阴性菌膜上打孔使其裂解；C3a、C5a等过敏毒素招募并激活吞噬细胞。宿主自身细胞表面有衰变加速因子（DAF）、CD59等调节蛋白防止补体误伤。
Complement is a group of plasma zymogens activated via three routes: the classical pathway (C1q recognizes IgG/IgM bound to pathogens), the lectin pathway (mannose-binding lectin recognizes microbial sugars), and the alternative pathway (spontaneous low-level activation on pathogen surfaces amplified by positive feedback). They converge on C3 convertase, then C5 convertase, and finally the membrane attack complex. Effector functions include: C3b opsonizing pathogens for phagocytosis; C5b-C9 forming membrane attack complexes that lyse Gram-negative bacteria; and the anaphylatoxins C3a/C5a recruiting and activating phagocytes. Host cells carry regulators such as decay-accelerating factor (DAF) and CD59 to prevent self-damage.

B细胞为什么能产生种类极其多样的抗体？

How can B cells generate an enormous diversity of antibodies?

抗体的多样性主要由V(D)J基因重组产生。B细胞在骨髓发育过程中，免疫球蛋白重链基因的V、D、J基因片段和轻链的V、J片段被RAG1/RAG2重组酶随机剪切并连接，产生数百万到上亿种组合，构成初级抗体库。不同片段的随机组合，加上连接处的多样性（插入/删除核苷酸），使每个B细胞拥有独特的抗体基因。抗原激活后，体细胞高频突变（SHM）和类别转换（从IgM/IgD转为IgG、IgA、IgE）进一步优化抗体的亲和力和效应功能。
Antibody diversity is generated chiefly by V(D)J recombination. As B cells develop in the bone marrow, V, D, and J gene segments of the immunoglobulin heavy chain (and V, J segments of the light chain) are randomly cut and joined by the recombinases RAG1 and RAG2, generating millions to hundreds of millions of combinations, the primary antibody repertoire. Random segment combination plus junctional diversity (nucleotide insertion or deletion) gives each B cell a unique antibody gene. After antigen activation, somatic hypermutation (SHM) and class switching (from IgM/IgD to IgG, IgA, or IgE) further refine affinity and effector function.

逆转录病毒（如HIV）是如何把自己的遗传信息插入宿主基因组的？

How do retroviruses such as HIV insert their genetic information into the host genome?

逆转录病毒携带逆转录酶，进入宿主细胞后以自身RNA为模板合成DNA，再由整合酶把这段DNA插入宿主染色体，随细胞分裂永久保留。正因为病毒DNA成了宿主基因组的一部分，艾滋病才如此难以根治。
Retroviruses carry reverse transcriptase that copies their RNA genome into DNA inside the host cell, and an integrase then inserts that DNA into the host chromosome, where it persists permanently through cell division. Because the viral DNA becomes part of the host genome, HIV is especially hard to cure.

细菌和古菌有什么重要区别？

What are the key differences between bacteria and archaea?

细菌和古菌都是原核生物，但古菌的细胞膜脂类、细胞壁成分和核糖体 RNA 与细菌差异显著，在演化上更接近于真核生物。许多古菌生活在极端环境（如高温热泉、高盐湖），而细菌分布更为广泛。

自身免疫病是如何发生的？主要的耐受失效机制有哪些？

How do autoimmune diseases arise, and what are the main mechanisms of tolerance breakdown?

机体通过中枢耐受（胸腺中T细胞阴性选择、骨髓中B细胞克隆清除）和外周耐受（调节性T细胞Treg、克隆无能、免疫赦免部位）淘汰或抑制对自身抗原反应的淋巴细胞。自身免疫病源于这些机制失效：如分子模拟（A组链球菌蛋白与心肌抗原相似，引发风湿性心脏病）、隐蔽抗原暴露（眼晶状体、精子等免疫赦免部位抗原因创伤释出）、Treg数量或功能缺陷、以及MHC易感基因（如HLA-DR4与类风湿关节炎）。例：1型糖尿病（自身反应性T细胞破坏胰岛β细胞）、Graves病（自身抗体刺激TSH受体）、重症肌无力（抗乙酰胆碱受体抗体阻断神经肌肉接头）。
The body eliminates or suppresses self-reactive lymphocytes through central tolerance (negative selection of T cells in the thymus, clonal deletion of B cells in the marrow) and peripheral tolerance (regulatory T cells, anergy, immune-privileged sites). Autoimmune disease arises when these fail, through mechanisms such as molecular mimicry (group A streptococcal proteins resemble cardiac antigens, causing rheumatic heart disease), release of sequestered antigens (immune-privileged antigens like lens and sperm exposed after injury), defects in Treg number or function, and susceptibility MHC alleles (e.g., HLA-DR4 in rheumatoid arthritis). Examples include type 1 diabetes (autoreactive T cells destroy pancreatic beta cells), Graves' disease (autoantibodies stimulate the TSH receptor), and myasthenia gravis (anti-acetylcholine-receptor antibodies block the neuromuscular junction).

抗体是怎么“抓住”病原体的？为什么得过一次天花就不会再得？

How do antibodies "grab" pathogens, and why does one bout of smallpox protect you for life?

抗体是 Y 字形的蛋白质，两臂顶端的可变区能精确匹配特定病原体表面的抗原表位，像钥匙配锁一样结合上去，从而中和病毒、标记细菌让吞噬细胞清除。感染过后，专一识别该病原体的记忆 B 细胞会长期存活，再次遇到时能快速增殖分化成大量抗体工厂。天花的病原体不会大变异，也没有动物宿主，所以一次感染或接种带来的终身免疫让人群再无新病例，世界卫生组织在1980年宣布天花被消灭。
Antibodies are Y-shaped proteins whose variable tips precisely match specific antigen epitopes on a pathogen, like a key in a lock, neutralizing viruses and tagging bacteria for phagocytes to destroy. After an infection, memory B cells that recognize that pathogen survive for years; upon re-exposure they rapidly multiply into antibody factories. Smallpox's virus barely mutates and has no animal reservoir, so lifelong immunity from one infection or vaccination left no new cases anywhere, and the WHO declared smallpox eradicated in 1980.

什么是水平基因转移？

What is horizontal gene transfer?

水平基因转移指生物把基因直接传给非后代的其他个体，常见于细菌之间。方式包括接合、转化和转导，能让有益基因（如抗药性）快速扩散。它在微生物进化和基因工程中都很重要。
Horizontal gene transfer is when an organism passes genes directly to another individual that is not its descendant, common among bacteria. Mechanisms include conjugation, transformation, and transduction and let useful genes like drug resistance spread fast. It matters in both microbial evolution and genetic engineering.

抗生素为什么越来越不灵？“超级细菌”是怎么来的？

Why do antibiotics keep losing their power, and where do "superbugs" come from?

耐药性是自然选择加水平基因转移的结果。细菌繁殖快、突变不断，只要种群中有一个个体出现耐药突变，抗生素就会把它筛选出来：敏感菌被杀死，耐药菌繁殖扩散。更麻烦的是细菌之间通过质粒等可移动元件交换耐药基因，一种细菌学会的耐药性可以“批发”给其他菌种。滥用和用药不足（如给病毒感染开抗生素、自行停药）都加剧筛选。多重耐药的 MRSA 和碳青霉烯耐药肠杆菌就是典型的超级细菌，能对抗几乎全部常用抗生素。
Resistance is natural selection combined with horizontal gene transfer. Bacteria reproduce quickly and mutate constantly; if even one individual carries a resistance mutation, antibiotics select for it, as sensitive cells die while resistant ones multiply and spread.
