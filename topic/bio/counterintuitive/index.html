有些鱼（如小丑鱼、石斑鱼）能改变自己的性别，是谁决定的，环境怎么“下令”的？

Some fish (clownfish, groupers) can change sex—who decides, and how does the environment “order” it?

这叫性别逆转，由社会结构和激素调控。例如小丑鱼群里只有最大雌鱼和一只雄鱼繁殖，雌鱼死后，最大的雄鱼会转变为雌鱼；环境信号通过激素重写生殖腺，而非改DNA。
It's sex reversal controlled by social structure and hormones. In clownfish, only the largest female and one male breed; if she dies, the biggest male turns female. Environmental cues rewrite the gonad via hormones, not DNA.

北极熊看起来雪白，可它的毛其实是透明的，这是怎么回事？

Polar bears look snow-white, yet their hairs are actually transparent—how?

北极熊的毛是中空透明的，只是把可见光散射成白色；皮肤其实是黑的，能吸收阳光保暖。毛管还能导光到皮肤，像天然“暖气管”。
Polar bear hairs are hollow and transparent, merely scattering visible light to look white; the skin underneath is black to absorb sun warmth. The hollow hairs even pipe light to the skin like natural “radiators”.

#北极熊 #透明毛 #保暖

蜜蜂蜇人之后自己会死，明明对自己没好处，为什么进化还保留这招？

A honeybee dies after stinging—no good for it, so why keep such a trait?

因为蜜蜂的螫针带倒钩，蜇哺乳动物后会连内脏拔出导致死亡；但这能保护整个蜂巢——用一只工蜂的命换群体安全，对“家族”更有利，所以被自然选择保留。
A bee's barbed stinger rips out its guts when stuck in mammal skin, killing it. But it defends the whole hive; sacrificing one worker for colony safety benefits the “family”, so evolution kept it.
