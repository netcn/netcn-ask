毛毛虫变蝴蝶的时候,蛹里面到底发生了什么?

What happens inside a chrysalis while a caterpillar becomes a butterfly?

毛毛虫在蛹里会分泌酶把自己大部分身体化成营养汤,只保留一些叫「成虫盘」的小口袋;这些小口袋用汤里的材料长出翅膀、长腿和复眼。更神奇的是,有些蛾子能记住幼虫时期学会的躲避训练——化蝶并没有完全抹去记忆。
Inside the chrysalis enzymes dissolve most caterpillar into soup, keeping tiny pockets called imaginal discs; they drink the soup and build wings, legs and compound eyes. Stranger still, some moths remember dangers learned as caterpillars — becoming a butterfly does not erase everything.

蚂蚁排队搬东西,它们是怎么商量好的?

How do ants agree to march in lines carrying things together?

蚂蚁会留下看不见的气味小路!走在前面的蚂蚁边走边洒气味,后面的伙伴闻着味道跟上来,队伍越排越长。这就像在地上画了一条只有蚂蚁能看见的隐形箭头。
Ants leave invisible smell trails! Scouts drip scent as they walk and friends follow the fragrance, forming long lines. It is like drawing arrows only ants can see.

萤火虫的光为什么不发热?冷光是什么原理?

Why is firefly light cold? How does bioluminescence work?

灯泡发光靠把金属烧到白热,九成能量变成热浪费掉;萤火虫走的是化学路线:荧光素在荧光素酶催化下与氧气反应,能量几乎全部转化为光子,效率接近百分之百,所以叫冷光。不同种类的萤火虫用闪光频率当身份证,雄雌对上暗号才能约会——每种光的节奏都是专属密码。
Bulbs glow by heating metal white-hot, wasting ninety percent as heat. Fireflies go chemical: luciferin reacts with oxygen under luciferase enzyme, converting nearly all energy into photons — cold light near perfect efficiency. Different species flash their own rhythm as ID code; males and females must match signals before dates.
