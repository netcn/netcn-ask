什么是"真社会性"，裸鼹鼠为何是哺乳动物中罕见的真社会案例？

What is eusociality, and why are naked mole-rats a rare mammalian example of it?

真社会性指同一物种中繁殖被少数个体垄断、多数成员不育并协作育幼与防御的高度社会组织。裸鼹鼠像蜜蜂一样只有一只"女王"和少数雄鼠繁殖，其余工鼠终生不育、分工挖掘地道，是哺乳动物中少见的真社会案例。
Eusociality is an advanced social system where reproduction is restricted to a few individuals while most members are non-reproductive and cooperate in raising offspring and defense. Like honeybees, naked mole-rats have a single breeding "queen" and a few males; the rest are sterile workers that dig tunnels—a rare case among mammals.

什么是“亲缘选择”，为什么工蜂愿意不生育去帮女王？

What is 'kin selection', and why do worker bees forgo breeding to help the queen?

亲缘选择理论说，动物会牺牲自身繁殖去帮助近亲传递共同基因。蜜蜂姐妹间基因相似度高于它们与后代的相似度，因此工蜂帮母亲（女王）养育妹妹，比自己产卵更能“传播基因”。这就是“广义适合度”的核心逻辑。
Kin selection says animals may sacrifice their own reproduction to help relatives pass shared genes. Bee sisters are more genetically similar to each other than to their own offspring, so workers help the mother queen raise sisters, spreading genes better than breeding themselves. This is the core of inclusive fitness.

What is meme-like animal song culture; do orcas have dialects?

不同虎鲸族群拥有各自独特的呼叫声和捕猎歌诀，并通过母子代际相传，构成类似“文化”的非基因传统。这种声学方言能帮助族群相互识别、维持社会纽带。研究把它视为动物社会学习的典型案例。
Different orca clans have distinct calls and hunting songs passed from mother to calf, forming non-genetic traditions akin to culture. These acoustic dialects help clans recognize each other and maintain social bonds. Researchers treat it as a flagship case of animal social learning.

Some shore crabs swarm ashore to spawn at full moon or spring tide—how do they “see” the moon?

它们体内有随月相和潮汐波动的生殖节律，能感知月光强度和海水流量的变化；大潮时水位高、滩涂暴露少、卵被冲走的风险低，于是同步产卵提高后代存活。
They carry lunar/tidal reproductive rhythms, sensing moonlight intensity and water flow. Spring tides bring higher water and less exposed shore, lowering the risk of eggs washing away—so synchronized spawning boosts offspring survival.

贝氏地松鼠的雌鼠会发出招引天敌的报警叫声，这类自我冒险的行为为何能用广义适合度解释？

Female Belding's ground squirrels emit alarm calls that attract predators to themselves—how does inclusive fitness explain such self-endangering behaviour?

报警叫声提高了鸣叫者被捕食的风险，却能提醒附近亲属钻洞避险。汉密尔顿法则指出：当亲缘系数r与收益B的乘积大于成本C（rB>C）时，利他行为会被自然选择保留。谢尔曼的野外观测发现，留守出生地的雌鼠身边多是姐妹和后代，遇险时频繁报警；散居远方、缺乏亲属的雄鼠极少出声，鸣叫率随在场亲属数量上升，与广义适合度预测一致。
An alarm call raises the caller's own risk of predation yet prompts nearby kin to retreat into burrows. Hamilton's rule states that altruism persists when relatedness r times benefit B exceeds cost C (rB>C). Paul Sherman's field observations found that females staying at their birth site, surrounded by sisters and offspring, call frequently during danger, while dispersing males with few relatives nearby rarely call—and calling rates climb with the number of kin present, matching inclusive-fitness predictions.

候鸟秋天往南、春天往北，飞几千公里不迷路，它们靠什么导航？

Migratory birds fly thousands of km south in autumn, north in spring—what navigates for them?

它们综合多种线索：白天看太阳、夜晚看星辰，还能感知地球磁场（喙部和眼里有磁受体），并记住海岸、山脉等地标。幼鸟还继承“天生路线”，边飞边学。
They combine cues: sun by day, stars by night, Earth's magnetic field (magnetoreceptors in beak and eye), and landmarks like coasts and ranges. Young birds inherit an innate route and learn en route.

狐獴群体觅食时，为什么总有一只站在高处"放哨"？

Why does a meerkat stand guard on high ground while the group forages?

狐獴群觅食时，会有一只个体爬上高处当哨兵，监视鹰、蛇等天敌并发出警报提醒同伴躲避。放哨者自己少吃东西却提高了全群存活率，这种行为可用亲缘选择和互惠利他来解释。
While a meerkat group forages, one individual climbs to a vantage point as a sentinel, scanning for predators such as hawks and snakes and giving alarm calls that send the group to cover. The sentinel eats less but raises the whole group's survival, a behavior explained by kin selection and reciprocal altruism.

Cleaner fish with big fish, crocodiles with plovers, rhinos with oxpeckers are classic pairs: one gets cleaning or alarm, the other gets food or delousing. Gobies and shrimp team up—one stands guard, the other digs—depending on each other. Such ties reveal nature's intricate cooperation.

企鹅爸爸为什么要把蛋放在脚背上？

Why do father penguins keep the egg on top of their feet?

帝企鹅在南极寒冬由雄鸟孵蛋：它把蛋放在脚背并用腹部的育儿袋盖住，整整两月不吃不喝抵御风雪。这样能让蛋离开冰面保暖，等雌鸟归来再交接。是动物界少见的“超级奶爸”行为。
Emperor penguin fathers incubate in the Antarctic winter: they balance the egg on their feet and cover it with a belly brood pouch, fasting for about two months against the storm. This keeps the egg off the ice and warm until the mother returns. It is a rare 'super-dad' behavior.

吸血蝠有时整夜觅食无获，为什么成功捕食的个体会把血液反吐给没有亲缘关系的同栖伙伴？

Vampire bats sometimes return from a night's foraging empty-handed—why do successful individuals regurgitate blood to roostmates they are unrelated to?

吸血蝠若连续两三夜未吸到血便会饿死，而猎获成功的个体常有富余。同栖成员经长期互相理毛彼此熟识，能记住谁曾反吐过血给自己，并在对方挨饿时优先回赠。捐出少量血液代价很小，却能救回一条命并换来未来的保障。这种建立在个体识别之上的直接互惠，是特里弗斯互惠利他理论的经典野外例证，亲缘关系仅起辅助作用。
A vampire bat starves to death after only two or three nights without blood, while successful hunters often come home with a surplus. Roostmates know one another through constant social grooming and remember who has fed them before, so hungry bats receive priority donations from partners they helped earlier. Donating a little blood costs little yet saves a life and secures future help. This individually addressed reciprocity is a classic field demonstration of Trivers's reciprocal altruism, with kinship playing only a supporting role.

Why is the octopus considered very intelligent?

章鱼能开罐子、走迷宫、用椰子壳当盾牌，还能通过观察和试错学习。它三分之二的神经元在腕足里，每条腕可独立“思考”。作为无脊椎动物，它的认知能力相当惊人。
Octopuses can open jars, solve mazes, use coconut shells as shields, and learn by observation and trial. About two-thirds of their neurons are in the arms, each capable of semi-independent 'thinking.' As an invertebrate, their cognition is remarkable.

How do migratory birds know where and how far to fly?

候鸟靠多种线索导航：白天的太阳、夜晚的星辰、地球的磁场，以及地标和气味。许多路线是本能编码在基因里，幼鸟首次迁徙便能循着亲代留下的“传统”路线前进。距离感则来自对日照长度和体内生物钟的综合判断。
Migrants navigate by several cues: the sun by day, stars by night, Earth's magnetic field, plus landmarks and smell. Many routes are instinctively encoded, so young birds can follow traditional paths on their first trip. Distance sense comes from day length and an internal clock combined.

大象怎样“打电话”远距离联络？

How do elephants 'make phone calls' over long distances?

大象能发出人耳听不到的次声波（低于20赫兹），可传数公里，用来寻找同伴或警告危险。它们也用跺脚让地面产生振动，远处的象用脚感知。配合气味和姿态，构成一套远距离通讯系统。
Elephants produce infrasound below 20 Hz that travels several kilometers, used to locate companions or warn of danger. They also stamp to send ground vibrations sensed by distant elephants' feet. Together with smell and posture, this forms a long-distance communication system.

What are whales doing when they sing?

Male humpback whales produce complex songs mainly for courtship and territory display, possibly also for group coordination. Songs travel tens of kilometers, and different populations have their own 'dialects.' The songs appear to change gradually from year to year.

猫为什么喜欢蹭人的腿？

Why do cats like to rub against people's legs?

猫脸部、尾巴根有气味腺，蹭你是把它的气味标记在你身上，宣示“这是我的家人”。同时它也在收集你的气味、表达亲近。在猫的语言里，蹭是一种社交问候而非单纯撒娇。
Cats have scent glands on the face and tail base; rubbing you marks you with their smell, claiming 'this is my family.' They also pick up your scent and show affection. In cat language, rubbing is a social greeting, not just coyness.

萤火虫的光是用来聊天的吗？

Is a firefly's glow for chatting?

萤火虫发光主要靠腹部荧光素酶反应，多数用于求偶：雄虫闪特定节奏，雌虫以同样节奏回应。不同种类有不同闪光密码，避免找错对象。少数种类幼虫发光则是警告“我有毒别吃我”。
Fireflies glow via a luciferase reaction in the abdomen, mostly for courtship: males flash a specific rhythm and females answer in kind. Different species have different flash codes to avoid mismating. A few larvae glow to warn predators they are toxic.

蚂蚁排队走路是在“聊天”吗？

When ants walk in lines, are they 'talking'?

蚂蚁主要靠气味交流：找到食物的工蚁会沿途留下信息素，同伴顺着气味跟上形成蚁路。遇到危险则释放告警信息素召集同伴。它们没有语言，却能用化学信号高效协作。
Ants mostly communicate by smell: a worker that finds food lays a pheromone trail, and nestmates follow the scent to form a line. Alarm pheromones summon others to danger. They have no language yet cooperate efficiently through chemical signals.

壁虎为什么能在墙上倒挂着不掉下来？

Why can a gecko hang upside down on a wall without falling?

壁虎脚趾上有数百万根极细的刚毛，刚毛末端又分叉成更细的铲状结构，靠范德华力与墙面分子相吸。这种吸附不需要胶水也不留痕迹，且能快速重复粘脱，让它们飞檐走壁。
Gecko toes bear millions of tiny setae that branch into spatula tips, adhering to wall molecules via van der Waals forces. The grip needs no glue, leaves no residue, and can stick and release rapidly, letting them run up walls and across ceilings.

狗摇尾巴是什么意思？

What does it mean when a dog wags its tail?

摇尾巴是狗表达情绪和身体状态的方式：开心或兴奋时快速摇，紧张时夹着尾巴或僵硬小幅摆动。狗还会根据尾巴左右偏摆传达不同信息，尾巴是它们重要的"社交语言"。
Tail wagging is how dogs express emotion and state: a fast wag often means happy or excited, while a tucked or stiff wag can signal nervousness. Dogs even show left-right bias that conveys different messages, making the tail an important part of their social language.

海豚睡觉时会不会淹死？

Can dolphins drown while sleeping?

海豚和鲸类采用“单半球睡眠”：大脑一半休息、另一半保持清醒控制呼吸和游动，每隔一段时间两半球轮换。所以它们能边睡边浮出水面换气，不会淹死。幼崽出生后相当长一段时间都和妈妈一起半睡半醒。
Dolphins and whales use 'unihemispheric sleep': one brain half rests while the other stays awake to control breathing and swimming, switching sides periodically. So they surface to breathe while dozing and won't drown. Calves stay half-awake with mothers for a long while.
