含羞草为什么一碰就害羞地合拢叶子?它有感觉吗?

Why do mimosa leaves snap shut when touched — does the plant feel?

含羞草没有神经,却有一套液压机关:叶柄根部有一个叫叶枕的水垫,触碰信号以电化学波形式在细胞间传递,叶枕瞬间排水,叶片失去支撑便垂下合拢。这是防御策略——装死让草食动物失去兴趣,或在大雨时减少受雨面积。几分钟后水回位,叶片重开。植物没有感情,但确实会用电和液压快速反应。
Mimosa has no nerves but runs hydraulics: touch triggers electrochemical waves to pulvini — water cushions at leaf joints — which dump water instantly so leaves fold. Playing dead discourages grazers and sheds storm rain. Water returns minutes later and leaves reopen. Plants lack feelings yet command electricity and pressure fast.

小种子埋在土里,怎么知道往上长出芽、往下长出根?

How does a tiny seed know to grow roots down and sprouts up?

种子有两位隐形向导:重力告诉它根要往深处钻,阳光召唤芽往亮处伸。就算你把种子翻个身种,它也会拐个弯重新找对方向——植物的根和芽天生就认得路!
Seeds carry two invisible guides: gravity pulls roots downward while sunlight calls sprouts upward. Plant a seed upside down and it curves back on course. Roots and sprouts are born pathfinders.

向日葵真的会一直跟着太阳转头吗?晚上它怎么办?

Do sunflowers really follow the sun all day — what about nighttime?

年轻的向日葵确实白天从东往西追太阳,靠茎两边生长速度不同实现转头;晚上再慢慢转回东边等日出。但花盘成熟后就不追了,固定朝向东方——这样早晨能更快晒暖,吸引更多蜜蜂来做客。
Young sunflowers really track the sun east to west as stem sides grow at different speeds, swinging back overnight for sunrise. But mature heads stop tracking and face east permanently — warming faster at dawn to welcome more bees.
