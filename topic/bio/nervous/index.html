突触如何通过化学方式传递信息？

How does a synapse transmit information chemically?

动作电位抵达突触前膜，触发钙离子内流、释放神经递质到突触间隙，递质结合后膜受体，把信号转为电信号继续传播。
An action potential reaches the presynaptic terminal, triggering calcium influx and release of neurotransmitters into the gap; they bind postsynaptic receptors, converting the signal back to electrical flow.

血脑屏障有什么作用？

What is the function of the blood-brain barrier?

脑毛细血管内皮紧密连接形成屏障，阻挡大多数大分子与病原体进入脑组织，保护大脑稳态，但也让许多药物难以进入。
Tight junctions in brain capillaries form a barrier blocking most large molecules and pathogens from the brain, protecting its environment—but also keeping many drugs out.

髓鞘怎样让神经信号传得更快？

How does myelin make nerve signals travel faster?

髓鞘是包裹轴突的脂质绝缘层，迫使动作电位在郎飞结间“跳跃”传导（跳跃传导），速度远快于无髓纤维，也节能。
Myelin is a fatty insulation around axons that forces the action potential to 'jump' between nodes of Ranvier (saltatory conduction), far faster and more efficient than unmyelinated conduction.

神经元是怎样传递信号的？

How do neurons transmit signals?

神经元在静息时膜内外有电位差；受刺激时钠离子内流产生动作电位，沿轴突传导，到末梢释放神经递质传给下一个细胞。
At rest a neuron has a voltage across its membrane; a stimulus lets sodium rush in, creating an action potential that travels the axon and releases neurotransmitters to the next cell.
