离子通道是怎样实现选择性过滤的？

How do ion channels achieve selective filtering?

钾通道用精确排布的羰基氧原子模拟水合钾离子的大小，只放行钾而排斥较小的钠，靠尺寸与配位实现选择。
K+ channels mimic the hydration shell with precisely placed oxygens, selecting K+ over smaller Na+ by size and coordination.

单分子技术（如光镊、FRET）能测到什么？

What can single-molecule techniques like optical tweezers and FRET measure?

它们能在单个分子水平观察酶折叠、DNA 解旋和蛋白构象变化，揭示群体平均信号所掩盖的动力学细节。
They watch enzyme folding, DNA unwinding, and conformational changes one molecule at a time, exposing kinetics hidden by population averages.

贝壳里那层彩虹色的珍珠层,凭什么比普通石灰石坚韧几千倍?

What makes iridescent nacre thousands of times tougher than plain limestone?

成分几乎相同(都是文石型碳酸钙加少量有机质),差别全在排布:珍珠层把几百纳米厚的六边形文石小板像砖块一样层层码放,砖缝间填着几纳米厚的蛋白质-几丁质砂浆。裂纹想穿过时,必须在每层砖间反复拐弯、桥连、摩擦拔出,能量被一层层吞掉——断裂功比纯方解石高出三到四个数量级,同时保持轻质。仿生学界称其为天然复合材料之王,层压装甲、抗冲击陶瓷与人工骨材料的论文里处处是它的影子;而软体动物用一套酸碱蛋白模板在常温水中就完成了工厂级精密制造。
Same chemistry — aragonitic calcium carbonate with traces of organics — radically different architecture: nacre stacks polygonal aragonite tablets a few hundred nanometers thick like bricks, mortared by protein-chitin layers nanometers thin. A crack must zigzag, bridge and friction-pull across every layer, dissipating energy layer by layer; work of fracture outruns pure calcite by three to four orders of magnitude while staying light. Biomimetics crowns it the king of natural composites — laminated armor, shock-resistant ceramics and bone implants all borrow its blueprint — and the mollusk runs this precision factory in seawater at room temperature using acidic protein templates.

牙釉质是人体最硬的组织,可为什么磕掉就永远长不回来?

Tooth enamel is the hardest tissue in your body — why can it never grow back?

因为建造它的工厂在你出生前就拆除了。牙釉质由成釉细胞分泌的釉原蛋白模板引导磷酸钙晶体定向生长,成熟期晶体排成致密的棱柱阵列,矿物质含量高达96%——硬度来自牺牲:为了塞满晶体,成釉细胞在釉质完成时整体凋亡,而牙齿一旦萌出便再无这些细胞,也没有血管供应修复材料。唾液只能带来微量的钙磷再矿化浅表脱矿。补牙材料的挑战正在于复刻这种各向异性棱柱结构;仿生矿化研究试图重启模板化结晶,目前仍停留在实验室阶段。所以护牙的唯一真法是不让它坏。
Because the factory is demolished before you are born. Ameloblasts secrete amelogenin templates guiding calcium-phosphate crystals into dense prismatic arrays reaching ninety-six percent mineral — hardness bought by sacrifice: once enamel matures, ameloblasts die en masse, and erupted teeth carry neither those cells nor blood vessels delivering repair materials. Saliva offers only trace remineralization of shallow lesions. Filling materials struggle to copy the anisotropic prismatic architecture; biomimetic remineralization tries restarting template crystallization, still lab-bound. The only true dentistry is prevention.

蛋白质折叠的能量地形是什么意思？

What is the energy landscape of protein folding?

蛋白质折叠被想象成在起伏的能量地形上滚向最低谷（天然态），漏斗形状解释了它为何能快速且可重复地折叠。
Folding is like rolling down a funnel-shaped energy landscape to the native state, explaining fast, reproducible folding.

为什么膜电位可以用能斯特方程来估算？

Why can membrane potential be estimated with the Nernst equation?

该方程把某种离子在膜两侧的浓度差换算成平衡电位，解释了静息膜电位和动作电位的离子基础。
The equation converts an ion's membrane concentration gradient into an equilibrium potential, underlying resting and action potentials.

为什么细胞膜像一块智能选择性滤网？

Why is the cell membrane like a smart selective filter?

磷脂双层天然挡住大多数带电和水溶性分子，膜上的通道与载体蛋白则按需放行特定物质，维持细胞内外差异。
The lipid bilayer blocks most charged and watery molecules, while channels and carriers selectively let specific substances through, maintaining inside-outside differences.
