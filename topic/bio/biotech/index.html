基因驱动为何能打破孟德尔遗传的 50% 传递规律？

Why can a gene drive break Mendel's 50% inheritance rule?

基因驱动借助 CRISPR 在杂合个体中把目标等位基因复制到同源染色体上，使子代几乎全部携带该基因，从而让性状在野生群体中快速扩散，也带来生态伦理风险。
A gene drive uses CRISPR to copy the target allele onto the homologous chromosome in heterozygotes, so nearly all offspring inherit it, spreading a trait rapidly through wild populations and raising ecological-ethics concerns.

CRISPR-Cas9 实现基因编辑的基本步骤是什么？

What are the basic steps by which CRISPR-Cas9 performs gene editing?

向导 RNA 引导 Cas9 蛋白识别互补的 DNA 序列，Cas9 像分子剪刀在特定位点切开双链，细胞修复时引入插入、缺失或外源片段，从而实现敲除或定点修改。
A guide RNA directs Cas9 to a complementary DNA site where Cas9 cuts the double strand like molecular scissors; cellular repair then introduces insertions, deletions or donor sequences, achieving knockout or precise edits.

重组胰岛素是如何利用转基因技术生产的？

How is recombinant insulin produced using transgenic technology?

把人胰岛素基因克隆到质粒并导入大肠杆菌或酵母，让工程菌大量表达人胰岛素，再纯化制成药物，避免了从动物胰脏提取的供应与过敏问题。
The human insulin gene is cloned into a plasmid and introduced into E. coli or yeast, which mass-produce human insulin that is purified into medicine, avoiding supply and allergy issues of animal-derived insulin.
