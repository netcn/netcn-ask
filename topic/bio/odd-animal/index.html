鸭嘴兽有哪些“拼凑”而来的特征？

What 'patchwork' traits does the platypus have?

鸭嘴兽产卵却哺乳、有喙与蹼足、雄性后肢带毒刺，还用电磁感应捕食，基因组混合了爬行、鸟与哺乳的特征，是演化上的活化石。
The platypus lays eggs yet nurses, has a bill and webbed feet, a venomous spur in males, and senses electric fields; its genome mixes reptile, bird, and mammal traits—a living evolutionary patchwork.

章鱼为什么被认为很“聪明”？

Why is the octopus considered intelligent?

章鱼有集中与分散结合的三套“脑”、可解决迷宫与开罐，并能改变颜色伪装；其神经元约三分之二在腕足，触手能独立“思考”。
Octopuses have a combined central and distributed 'brain', solve mazes and open jars, and change color to camouflage; two-thirds of their neurons are in the arms, which can act semi-independently.

裸鼹鼠为什么几乎不得癌症且很长寿？

Why do naked mole-rats rarely get cancer and live long?

裸鼹鼠细胞分泌透明质酸使组织紧密、抑制肿瘤扩散，并有特殊的蛋白质稳态与DNA修复机制，使其抗癌且寿命远长于同体型啮齿类。
Naked mole-rat cells secrete hyaluronan that keeps tissue tight and blocks tumor spread, plus unusual protein stability and DNA repair, making them cancer-resistant and long-lived for their size.

电鳗是怎样放电的？

How does an electric eel produce shocks?

电鳗体内有成排的发电细胞，像串联电池一样协同放电，可瞬间产生高压电流用于捕食、自卫和感知，是活的“生物电池”。
Electric eels have rows of electrocytes that act like stacked batteries discharging together, producing high-voltage pulses for hunting, defense, and sensing—living biological batteries.

水熊虫为什么能在极端环境中存活？

Why can tardigrades survive extreme environments?

水熊虫在脱水、极寒或辐射下进入“隐生”状态，代谢近乎停止、DNA被特殊蛋白保护，遇水复活，是已知最顽强的动物之一。
Under drying, extreme cold, or radiation, tardigrades enter 'cryptobiosis' with near-zero metabolism and DNA protected by special proteins, reviving with water—among the toughest animals known.

捕蝇草是怎样“知道”有昆虫落下的？

How does a Venus flytrap 'know' an insect has landed?

捕蝇草的夹子内表面有感觉毛，昆虫触碰两次才会触发动作电位，确认是活猎物后才在秒级内合拢，避免误耗能。
The flytrap's inner surface has trigger hairs; two touches spark action potentials that confirm a live prey before it snaps shut within seconds, avoiding wasted energy.
