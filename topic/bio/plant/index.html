秋天银杏叶变黄飘落前，树会把叶子里的养分“回收”回树干，它怎么做到？

Before yellow ginkgo leaves fall in autumn, the tree “reclaims” their nutrients—how does it do that?

入秋日照变短，叶片启动衰老程序：把叶绿素和氮、镁等可移动养分分解、经叶柄运回枝干储存，只留纤维素等“骨架”；养分回收后叶子变黄脱落，来年再复用。
Shorter autumn days trigger leaf aging: mobile nutrients like chlorophyll, nitrogen and magnesium are broken down and shipped back via the petiole to branches for storage, leaving only the cellulose “skeleton”. Reclaimed leaves yellow and drop, reused next year.

#落叶 #养分回收 #衰老

同一棵树朝光面和背光面的叶子形状、厚薄常不一样，这是树的“记忆”吗？

Leaves on a tree's sunny versus shady side differ in shape and thickness—is that the tree's “memory”?

这叫表型可塑性：叶子根据光照、风、湿度实时调整结构（向光面更厚、叶绿体更多），是同一套基因在不同环境下的表达，并非像动物记忆那样“记住”过去，而是随时适应环境。
It's phenotypic plasticity: leaves adjust structure (thicker, more chloroplasts on the lit side) to light, wind and humidity—the same genes expressed differently by environment. Not “memory” like animals, but continuous adaptation.

#表型可塑性 #叶子 #环境

树干为什么是圆的而不是方的，这和它往树冠运水、往下运糖有什么关系？

Why are trunks round, not square, and how does that relate to moving water up and sugar down?

圆形受力最均匀，能抗风抗重；树干里的导管和筛管从根到冠连成管道，圆形截面让水分靠蒸腾拉力快速上升、糖分靠压力均匀下送，方形的棱角反而会削弱运输和强度。
A round shape spreads force evenly, resisting wind and weight. Inside, vessels and sieve tubes form pipelines from root to crown; the round cross-section lets water rise fast by transpiration pull and sugars move down evenly. Square corners would weaken both.

#树干 #运输 #结构

有些树被划破会流出红色像血的汁液（如龙血树、鸡血藤），那真的是“血”吗？

Some trees bleed red “blood-like” sap (dragon tree, spatholobus)—is it really blood?

不是血，是植物的树脂或乳汁。龙血树的红汁含黄酮和鞣质，遇空气氧化变深红，古人当作药材；它其实是植物的“创可贴”，能封住伤口、防菌防虫。
Not blood—it's resin or latex. The dragon tree's red sap holds flavonoids and tannins that darken in air; people used it as medicine. It's the plant's “band-aid”, sealing wounds against microbes and pests.

#龙血树 #树脂 #防御

为什么秋天树叶会变红？

Why do leaves turn red in autumn?

叶绿素分解后，类胡萝卜素和花青素显现出红黄色。
As chlorophyll fades, carotenoids and anthocyanins show red and yellow.

#花青素 #叶绿素 #季节

苔藓能在光秃秃的石头上长出来，它又没有真正的根，靠什么“喝水”？

Moss grows on bare rock with no true roots—how does it “drink”?

苔藓全身都能吸水：叶片薄、表面直接吸收雨水和空气中的水汽，假根只起固定作用。它先在石头上分泌酸性物质慢慢风化石面，为后来者铺路，是“拓荒者”。
Moss drinks through its whole body: thin leaves absorb rain and humidity directly, while rhizoids only anchor. It secretes acids that slowly weather rock, paving the way for later plants—a pioneer.

Why do plants grow toward light?

植物激素在背光侧分布更多，使那侧长得快而弯向光。
Hormones on the shaded side speed growth, bending the plant to light.

#向光性 #激素 #生长

竹子为什么能一夜长高差不多一米，它到底算草还是算树？

Why can bamboo shoot up nearly a meter in a night, and is it grass or tree?

竹子其实是巨大的草！它用地下竹鞭快速窜笋，秆在出土前就长好了节，只是吸水撑开拉长。因为它是禾本科（和稻麦一家），所以再高也是“草”不是树。
Bamboo is actually a giant grass! Underground rhizomes push up shoots whose segments were pre-formed; they just fill with water and elongate. Being in the grass family (with rice and wheat), it's a grass, not a tree.

#竹子 #禾本科 #快速生长

植物不会走路，可含羞草一碰就合叶、捕蝇草会“咬”虫子，它们真的会动吗？

Plants can't walk, yet touch-me-not folds its leaves and Venus flytrap “bites” insects—do they really move?

会！含羞草叶枕细胞失水变软让叶子合拢；捕蝇草靠叶片上的毛感应、细胞快速改变水分让两瓣合上。这是植物用“胀水运动”做出的快速反应，不用肌肉也能动。
Yes! Mimosa leaves close when water leaves cushion cells; the flytrap senses hairs and snaps shut via rapid water shifts in cells. Plants move by “turgor changes”—no muscles needed.

#含羞草 #捕蝇草 #运动

老人说“霜打的青菜分外甜”，为什么低温反而让蔬菜变甜？

“Cabbage sweetened by frost”—why does cold make vegetables taste sweeter?

低温下植物为防冻会把淀粉水解成葡萄糖、蔗糖等可溶性糖，降低细胞液冰点；这些糖既是“防冻剂”也带来甜味。所以经霜的青菜、萝卜确实更甜。
In cold, plants hydrolyze starch into soluble sugars (glucose, sucrose) to lower the freezing point of cell fluid; the sugars act as antifreeze and taste sweet. Frost-touched greens and radishes really do get sweeter.

#霜 #可溶性糖 #抗冻

树叶正面总是更绿，背面常常浅一些，上下两面干的活不一样吗？

Leaf tops are greener, bottoms paler—do the two sides do different jobs?

是的。正面朝上、光照强，排列着更多叶绿体来拼命光合；背面气孔多，专管呼吸和水分蒸发（蒸腾）。两面分工，叶子才既产糖又通风排水。
Yes. The sunlit top packs more chloroplasts for photosynthesis; the underside has more stomata for gas exchange and water loss (transpiration). The two sides divide labor—make sugar up top, breathe and vent below.
