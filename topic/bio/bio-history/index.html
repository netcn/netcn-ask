施莱登和施旺提出的细胞学说核心是什么？

What is the core of the cell theory by Schleiden and Schwann?

他们在 1838–1839 年提出一切动植物都由细胞构成、细胞是生命的基本单位，后来补入所有细胞来自已有细胞，统一了人们对生物结构的认识。
In 1838–1839 they proposed all plants and animals are made of cells and the cell is the unit of life; later amended that all cells come from existing cells, unifying biological structure.

居里夫人的放射性研究对生物学有何深远影响？

How did Curie's radioactivity research influence biology?

放射性同位素示踪技术（如用碳-14 标记）后来成为研究代谢通路、DNA 复制和医学影像的基石，而射线本身也发展出放疗等医学手段，源头都来自放射化学。
Radioisotope tracing (e.g. carbon-14 labeling) became foundational for studying metabolism, DNA replication and medical imaging, and radiation spawned radiotherapy—roots in radiochemistry.

达尔文是怎样提出自然选择学说的？

How did Darwin arrive at the theory of natural selection?

他随小猎犬号环球考察，观察到物种的地理分布、化石与变异；受马尔萨斯人口论启发，认识到生存竞争下有利变异被保留，于 1859 年发表《物种起源》。
On the Beagle voyage he observed geographic distribution, fossils and variation; inspired by Malthus, he saw favorable variants preserved under struggle for existence, publishing On the Origin of Species in 1859.

孟德尔的豌豆实验为什么被埋没了几十年？

Why was Mendel's pea experiment overlooked for decades?

他在 1866 年把论文发表在地方性刊物，用当时不被理解的遗传因子和数学统计解释性状，直到 1900 年才被三位植物学家重新发现并确立其地位。
His 1866 paper appeared in a local journal, using then-unfamiliar hereditary factors and statistics; it was rediscovered and established only in 1900 by three botanists.

巴斯德怎样用实验反驳了自然发生说？

How did Pasteur disprove spontaneous generation?

他用鹅颈瓶把煮沸的培养液与空气灰尘隔开：若瓶颈完好液体久放不腐败，一旦接入灰尘便很快变质，证明微生物来自已有微生物而非自发产生。
With swan-neck flasks he separated boiled broth from airborne dust: intact necks kept it sterile, but adding dust spoiled it, proving microbes come from other microbes, not spontaneous generation.

沃森和克里克是如何发现 DNA 双螺旋结构的？

How did Watson and Crick discover the DNA double helix?

他们综合了富兰克林的 X 射线衍射照片、威尔金斯的资料以及查哥夫碱基比例规律，于 1953 年构建出双螺旋模型，并由此揭示了碱基配对与半保留复制的机制。
Combining Franklin's X-ray diffraction photos, Wilkins' data and Chargaff's base-ratio rules, they built the double-helix model in 1953, revealing base pairing and semi-conservative replication.

Which plant is linked to Tu Youyou's discovery of artemisinin?

她从黄花蒿中提取出青蒿素用于治疗疟疾，显著降低了重症死亡率，挽救了数百万生命，并因此获得 2015 年诺贝尔生理学或医学奖。
She extracted artemisinin from Artemisia annua to treat malaria, sharply cutting severe-case deaths and saving millions, earning the 2015 Nobel Prize in Physiology or Medicine.

1918 年大流感常被称为西班牙流感，它真的源自西班牙吗？

The 1918 flu is called Spanish flu—did it really start in Spain?

并不。一战参战国普遍审查疫情以稳住民心，中立国西班牙自由报道使其显得疫情最重，名字源于媒体偏见而非真实起源地。
No. Wartime combatants censored epidemics to keep morale, while neutral Spain reported freely and looked worst; the name comes from media bias, not the true origin.

李时珍的《本草纲目》在生物史上是怎样的著作？

What kind of work is Li Shizhen's Compendium of Materia Medica in biological history?

这部 16 世纪的巨著系统记录了上千种动植物和矿物的形态、习性与药性，是中国传统生物认知和药物学的重要文献，也影响了东亚自然史。
This 16th-century compendium systematically recorded over a thousand plants, animals and minerals—their form, habits and uses—a key text of traditional Chinese biological and pharmacological knowledge.

林奈为什么用属名加种名的双名法给生物命名？

Why did Linnaeus use the genus-plus-species binomial naming?

他在 1753 年提出用拉丁文的属名加种加词为每个物种定一个全球通用的名字，避免了各地俗名混乱，奠定了现代分类学的基础。
In 1753 he proposed a Latin genus plus species epithet as one globally shared name per organism, ending local-name confusion and founding modern taxonomy.
