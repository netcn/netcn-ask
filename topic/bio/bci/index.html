神经信号解码为什么离不开机器学习？

Why does neural signal decoding rely on machine learning?

神经元放电模式复杂且个体差异很大，深度学习能从海量神经活动中拟合出思维到动作的映射关系。
Firing patterns are complex and highly individual, so deep learning fits the mind-to-action mapping from massive neural datasets.

脑机接口（BCI）是如何工作的？

How does a brain-computer interface (BCI) work?

BCI 通过电极或头戴设备读取大脑神经信号，经算法解码为指令，让瘫痪者用意念操控光标、机械臂或语音合成器。
A BCI reads neural signals via electrodes or headsets and decodes them into commands, letting paralyzed users move cursors, robotic arms, or speech synthesizers by thought.

侵入式和非侵入式脑机接口各有什么权衡？

What are the trade-offs between invasive and non-invasive BCIs?

侵入式电极植入大脑皮层，信号清晰但需手术且有感染风险；非侵入式（如脑电图）安全但分辨率和信噪比较低。
Implanted electrodes give high-resolution signals at surgical risk, whereas EEG is safe but lower resolution and noisier.
