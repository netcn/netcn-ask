酒是怎么酿出来的？

How is alcoholic drinks brewed?

酿酒依赖酵母把糖类在无氧下转化为乙醇和二氧化碳；原料（葡萄、粮食、蜂蜜）和工艺决定风味，进一步蒸馏可得更高酒精度的烈酒。
Brewing relies on yeast converting sugars to ethanol and CO2 under anaerobic conditions; the raw material and process shape flavor, and distillation yields higher-alcohol spirits.

味精（谷氨酸钠）是什么，为什么有鲜味？

What is MSG (monosodium glutamate), and why does it taste umami?

谷氨酸是天然存在的氨基酸，游离谷氨酸能激活舌头上的鲜味受体；海带、番茄、奶酪中本就富含，味精只是把这种鲜味浓缩提纯，并非有害物质。
Glutamate is a natural amino acid; free glutamate activates umami receptors on the tongue. It is naturally rich in kombu, tomatoes and cheese, and MSG simply concentrates that taste, not a harmful additive.

茶叶按发酵程度分为哪几类，风味为何不同？

How are teas classified by fermentation, and why do flavors differ?

绿茶不发酵、白茶微发酵、黄茶轻发酵、乌龙茶半发酵、红茶全发酵、黑茶后发酵；所谓发酵实为茶多酚的酶促氧化，氧化程度决定转化产物的构成，从而带来颜色、涩感和香气的系统差异。
Green is unoxidized, white barely oxidized, yellow lightly so, oolong partially, black fully, and dark tea post-fermented by microbes; 'fermentation' in tea is really enzymatic oxidation of polyphenols, and its degree sets the product mix behind the systematic differences in color, astringency and aroma.

奶酪是怎么从牛奶变成一块块的？

How does milk become a block of cheese?

用凝乳酶或酸使牛奶中的酪蛋白凝固，分离出液体乳清后加盐、接种特定菌种并熟成，不同的微生物与工艺造就了千百种风味。
Rennet or acid coagulates milk casein; the liquid whey is drained, then salt, specific microbes and aging create thousands of flavor varieties.

豆浆是怎么变成豆腐的？

How does soy milk become tofu?

向煮沸的豆浆加入盐卤或石膏（含钙、镁离子）作为凝固剂，使大豆球蛋白聚集沉淀成豆花，再压去部分水分便成豆腐。
Adding a coagulant such as bittern or gypsum (calcium/magnesium ions) to boiled soy milk makes soy globulins aggregate into curds, which are pressed to remove water and become tofu.

中医讲“药食同源”，像泡菜、豆豉、酸奶这类发酵食物，到底算药还是算食物？

Traditional medicine says “medicine and food share a source”—are fermented foods like kimchi, douchi and yogurt medicine or just food?

它们首先是食物：靠乳酸菌、霉菌等把原料转化，产生风味、更易消化，并带入活菌和有益代谢物，常吃有助肠道健康。称“药”是取其调理之意，不能替代药物治疗疾病。
They are mainly food: microbes like lactobacteria and molds transform ingredients into tastier, more digestible forms with live bacteria and helpful metabolites that support gut health. Calling them “medicine” means dietary regulation, not a cure.

泡菜、酸菜为什么能比新鲜蔬菜保存更久？

Why do kimchi and sauerkraut keep longer than fresh vegetables?

盐和厌氧环境抑制腐败菌，乳酸菌发酵产生的乳酸降低 pH，酸性条件进一步抑制有害微生物，从而延长保存。
Salt and anaerobic conditions suppress spoilers, while lactic acid from fermentation lowers pH and further inhibits harmful microbes, extending shelf life.

面包为什么能发起来变得松软？

Why does bread rise and become soft?

酵母在无氧条件下把面团里的糖分解成二氧化碳和酒精，二氧化碳被面筋网络锁在面团中形成气孔，烘烤后定形为松软结构。
Yeast ferments dough sugars into carbon dioxide and ethanol; gluten traps the CO2 as bubbles that set during baking into a light, porous crumb.

蜂蜜为什么几乎放不坏？

Why does honey hardly ever spoil?

蜂蜜含糖量极高、水分活度很低，本身又呈酸性并含过氧化氢与抗菌酶，微生物难以生长；考古曾在古墓中发现仍可食用的蜂蜜。
Honey is very high in sugar with low water activity, acidic, and contains hydrogen peroxide and antimicrobial enzymes, so microbes scarcely grow; edible honey has even been found in ancient tombs.

腊肉、咸鱼为什么能放很久不坏？

Why do cured meats and salted fish keep so long?

高盐、高糖或干燥大幅降低水分活度，使细菌难以繁殖；烟熏还会带来酚类等抗菌物质，从而延长保存。
High salt, sugar or drying sharply lower water activity so bacteria struggle to grow; smoking adds antimicrobial phenolics, extending preservation.

咖啡为什么能让人提神？

Why does coffee make us alert?

咖啡中的咖啡因会占据大脑里腺苷的受体，阻止疲劳信号传递，从而暂时提升警觉，但它并不能替代真正的睡眠。
Caffeine occupies adenosine receptors in the brain, blocking fatigue signals to temporarily boost alertness, but it does not replace real sleep.

酸奶是怎么做出来的，和牛奶有什么不同？

How is yogurt made, and how does it differ from milk?

乳酸菌发酵牛奶中的乳糖产生乳酸，使蛋白质凝固变酸稠，部分乳糖被分解更易消化，并保留大量活菌。
Lactic-acid bacteria ferment milk lactose into lactic acid, curdling and acidifying the protein; some lactose is broken down for easier digestion and live cultures remain.

切开的苹果为什么会变褐色？

Why do cut apples turn brown?

果肉中的多酚氧化酶接触空气，把酚类物质氧化成褐色的醌类；滴柠檬汁（维C和酸）可抑制该反应，延缓变色。
Polyphenol oxidase in the flesh uses oxygen to oxidize phenolics into brown quinones; lemon juice (vitamin C and acid) inhibits the reaction and slows browning.

茶明明含咖啡因能提神，为什么很多人喝完又觉得放松平静？

Tea contains caffeine that wakes you up—so why do many feel calm after drinking it?

茶叶里的茶氨酸能促进α脑波、舒缓神经，抵消部分咖啡因的紧张感；L-茶氨酸与少量咖啡因搭配，常让人清醒而不焦躁。不同茶类咖啡因和茶氨酸比例不同，感受也有别。
Tea's L-theanine promotes calming alpha brain waves, offsetting caffeine's jitter. The combo of theanine with modest caffeine leaves people alert but not anxious. Ratios vary by tea type, changing the feel.

超市卖的鸡蛋为什么孵不出小鸡？

Why can't supermarket eggs hatch into chicks?

未受精的鸡蛋里没有胚胎，无论如何保温都不会发育；只有种蛋（受精且处于适宜温度）才能孵化出小鸡。
Unfertilized eggs contain no embryo and will not develop no matter the warmth; only fertile eggs kept at the right temperature can hatch.
