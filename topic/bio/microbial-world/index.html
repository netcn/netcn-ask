什么是CRISPR，细菌为什么要留着它？

What is CRISPR, and why do bacteria keep it?

CRISPR是细菌对抗病毒的“免疫记忆”系统：它们把入侵病毒的DNA片段存进自身基因组，再次遇到时由Cas蛋白按片段识别并剪断病毒DNA。人类后来把它改造成精准的基因编辑工具。它本质是细菌与噬菌体长期军备竞赛的产物。
CRISPR is a bacterial 'immune memory' against viruses: cells store snippets of invading viral DNA in their own genome and, on reencounter, use Cas proteins to recognize and cut the viral DNA by matching those snippets. Humans later turned it into a precise gene-editing tool. It is essentially the product of a long bacterial-virus arms race.

什么是“病毒是否算生命”的争议？

What is the debate over whether viruses count as alive?

病毒具备遗传信息和演化能力，但不能独立代谢、必须依赖宿主复制，因此处于生命与非生命的边界。支持者认为它们是“寄生性遗传实体”，反对者强调缺少细胞与代谢不符合生命定义。这一争议影响了生命起源和分类研究。
Viruses carry genetic information and evolve, yet cannot metabolize independently and must replicate inside hosts, sitting on the boundary of life. Supporters call them 'parasitic genetic entities'; opponents stress the lack of cells and metabolism disqualifies them. The debate shapes origin-of-life and classification research.

什么是“微生物暗物质”，为什么它重要？

What is 'microbial dark matter', and why does it matter?

微生物暗物质指环境中大量存在却无法在实验室培养、也难以归类的微生物类群，多靠宏基因组测序才被发现。它们可能占据生命之树的许多未知分支，参与碳氮循环却功能成谜。弄清它们有助于理解地球生化循环和新型药物来源。
Microbial dark matter refers to abundant environmental microbes we cannot culture in labs or easily classify, found mainly via metagenomic sequencing. They may occupy many unknown branches of the tree of life and drive carbon and nitrogen cycles while their functions stay mysterious. Unraveling them helps explain Earth's biochemistry and new drug sources.

产甲烷古菌是什么，它们为何与众不同？

What are methanogenic archaea, and what makes them distinctive?

产甲烷古菌在缺氧环境（湿地、反刍动物胃、填埋场）中以二氧化碳、氢气或乙酸为底物产生甲烷，是地球甲烷的主要生物来源。它们拥有独特的辅酶F420和甲基辅酶M还原酶，这一代谢使它们明显区别于细菌。
Methanogenic archaea in oxygen-free settings (wetlands, ruminant stomachs, landfills) produce methane from CO2, H2, or acetate, making them Earth's main biological methane source. They rely on unique coenzyme F420 and methyl-coenzyme M reductase, a metabolism that clearly sets them apart from bacteria.

什么是“水平基因转移”，它为什么让细菌很危险？

What is horizontal gene transfer, and why does it make bacteria dangerous?

水平基因转移指细菌之间不靠繁殖而直接交换基因，如通过质粒传递耐药基因。这让抗生素耐药性能在不同菌种间快速扩散，甚至出现“超级细菌”。它是微生物演化快于多细胞生物的重要原因。
Horizontal gene transfer is the direct exchange of genes between bacteria without reproduction, such as passing drug-resistance genes on plasmids. It lets antibiotic resistance spread quickly across species, producing 'superbugs.' It is a major reason microbes evolve faster than multicellular organisms.

为什么抗生素用多了会“失效”？

Why do antibiotics 'stop working' when overused?

细菌繁殖极快，群体中偶发耐药突变的个体在药物环境下被筛选存活并扩散，加上水平基因转移把耐药基因传来传去，最终出现大量耐药菌。滥用和未完成疗程都会加速这一过程，所以抗生素要按需、足量、遵医嘱使用。
Bacteria reproduce fast; individuals with random resistance mutations survive drug pressure and spread, while horizontal gene transfer passes resistance around, producing many resistant strains. Overuse and unfinished courses accelerate this, so antibiotics need careful, full, doctor-guided use.

什么是"极端微生物"，它们为什么能在沸腾的酸或冰里生存？

What are extremophiles, and why can they survive boiling acid or ice?

极端微生物能在常人难以生存的环境（深海热泉、盐湖、强酸）中存活，靠特殊的膜脂、耐热蛋白和相容溶质保护细胞结构。例如嗜热菌的酶在接近沸点仍稳定，已被用于PCR等生物技术。
Extremophiles thrive where most life cannot—deep-sea vents, salt lakes, strong acid—using special membrane lipids, heat-stable proteins, and compatible solutes to protect their structures. For example, heat-loving bacteria have enzymes stable near boiling and are now used in PCR and other biotechnologies.

酸奶里的“益生菌”和酸奶菌是一回事吗？

Are the 'probiotics' in yogurt the same as yogurt bacteria?

普通酸奶靠保加利亚乳杆菌和嗜热链球菌发酵，它们多数过胃时被胃酸消灭，未必能定植肠道。商家宣称的“益生菌”常指双歧杆菌等能耐受胃酸、可能对肠道有益的种类。两者都属乳酸菌大家族，但功能侧重不同。
Regular yogurt is fermented by Lactobacillus bulgaricus and Streptococcus thermophilus, which mostly die in stomach acid and may not colonize the gut. Marketed 'probiotics' often mean strains like Bifidobacterium that survive acid and may benefit the gut. Both are lactic-acid bacteria but with different roles.

为什么洗手要用肥皂而不是只用清水？

Why should we use soap rather than just water to wash hands?

许多病菌外面裹着一层油脂膜，清水冲不掉。肥皂分子一端亲水、一端亲油，能把油脂和附着在上面的病菌一起包裹成小团冲走。正确搓洗20秒以上才有效。
Many germs are coated in a greasy layer that plain water cannot remove. Soap molecules have a water-loving end and an oil-loving end, so they wrap the grease and the attached germs into tiny clusters that rinse away. Scrubbing for at least twenty seconds makes it effective.

什么是“硝化作用”，和鱼缸换水有什么关系？

What is nitrification, and what does it have to do with changing fish-tank water?

鱼缸里鱼的排泄物变成有毒的氨，硝化细菌把它先氧化成亚硝酸盐、再氧化成毒性较低的硝酸盐，这就是硝化作用。成熟过滤系统靠这层“菌膜”净化水质，所以新缸要“养水”建立菌群，不能完全不换水也不能一次换太猛。
Fish waste in a tank turns into toxic ammonia; nitrifying bacteria oxidize it first to nitrite then to less toxic nitrate—nitrification. A mature filter relies on this bacterial film to clean water, so a new tank must 'cycle' to build the colony, and you should neither skip water changes nor swap too much at once.

酸奶和泡菜是怎么靠细菌做出来的？

How are yogurt and kimchi made with the help of bacteria?

乳酸菌把牛奶或蔬菜里的糖分发酵成乳酸，使环境变酸并抑制坏菌生长。酸奶靠保加利亚乳杆菌等把牛奶变稠变酸，泡菜靠多种乳酸菌让蔬菜入味且不腐坏。这种发酵是人类最古老的保藏食物方式之一。
Lactic acid bacteria ferment sugars in milk or vegetables into lactic acid, acidifying the environment and suppressing spoilage microbes. Yogurt relies on species like Lactobacillus bulgaricus to thicken and sour milk, while kimchi uses mixed lactic bacteria to flavor and preserve vegetables. Such fermentation is among humanity's oldest food-preservation methods.

为什么伤口化脓是身体在“战斗”？

Why is pus at a wound a sign the body is 'fighting'?

化脓是免疫细胞和入侵细菌厮杀后的“战场残骸”：大量死去的白细胞、细菌和组织液混在一起形成黄色脓液。它在提醒你身体正在抗感染，但如果红肿热痛加重或发烧应及时就医。
Pus is the debris of a battle between immune cells and invading bacteria: dead white cells, bacteria, and tissue fluid mix into yellowish matter. It signals the body is fighting infection, but if redness, swelling, pain, or fever worsen, you should see a doctor.

肠道里的小细菌是怎样帮我们消化食物的？

How do the tiny bacteria in our gut help digest food?

我们肠道里住着数不清的细菌，它们能分解人体自己消化不了的膳食纤维，发酵产生短链脂肪酸，既为肠道细胞供能，也帮助调节免疫。没有这些"房客"，很多营养和抵抗力都会打折扣。
Countless bacteria live in our gut and break down dietary fiber that human enzymes cannot digest, fermenting it into short-chain fatty acids that feed colon cells and help regulate immunity. Without these "tenants," much of our nutrition and resistance would suffer.

为什么伤口用碘酒或酒精擦会杀细菌？

Why do iodine or alcohol kill bacteria on a wound?

碘酒和酒精能破坏细菌蛋白质的外层和酶，使微生物变性失活，属于广谱消毒剂。但它们也会刺激人体细胞，所以只能用于皮肤表面，不能往深伤口里灌。日常小伤口清洁足够。
Iodine and alcohol denature bacterial proteins and enzymes, inactivating microbes as broad-spectrum disinfectants. They also irritate human cells, so use only on skin surface, not deep wounds. They suffice for cleaning minor cuts.

为什么牛奶放久了会酸坏？

Why does milk go sour and spoil if left too long?

牛奶营养丰富，空气中的乳酸菌等趁机制造乳酸，使蛋白质凝结、味道变酸，这就是自然发酵变酸。若污染了致病菌还可能腐臭。冷藏能延缓但无法永远阻止微生物活动。
Milk is nutritious, so airborne lactic bacteria and others make lactic acid, curdling proteins and souring the taste—natural fermentation. If pathogens contaminate it, it can also rot and smell foul. Refrigeration slows but cannot stop microbes forever.

细菌和病毒哪个更小，它们有什么不同？

Which is smaller, bacteria or viruses, and how do they differ?

病毒通常比细菌小得多，细菌是完整的单细胞生物，能自己代谢繁殖；病毒没有细胞结构，必须钻进宿主细胞才能复制。抗生素能杀死细菌但一般对病毒无效，所以感冒多为病毒引起时吃药用处有限。
Viruses are usually far smaller than bacteria. Bacteria are complete single-celled organisms that metabolize and reproduce on their own, while viruses have no cell structure and must enter a host cell to replicate. Antibiotics kill bacteria but generally do nothing against viruses, so they are little help for most colds.
