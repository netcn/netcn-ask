基因表达噪声来自哪里，为什么同基因型细胞会走向不同命运？

Where does gene expression noise come from, and why can genetically identical cells adopt different fates?

噪声可分为内在与外在两部分：内在噪声源于转录起始、mRNA 降解等在低拷贝数下的随机事件，尤其是启动子的爆发式转录使产物数目服从远离泊松的分布；外在噪声来自细胞间在核糖体、聚合酶数量、细胞周期阶段和体积上的差异，会同时影响多个基因。当下游存在正反馈或阈值型开关时，这些随机差异可被放大并锁定，如枯草杆菌的感受态与芽孢化选择、噬菌体的裂解-溶原决策。噪声因此既是干扰，也是无遗传差异下实现群体分化和风险分摊的机制。
Noise splits into intrinsic and extrinsic components: intrinsic noise arises from the stochastic nature of transcription initiation and mRNA decay at low copy number, and bursty promoters in particular give distributions far from Poisson, while extrinsic noise reflects cell-to-cell differences in ribosome and polymerase abundance, cell-cycle stage and volume that affect many genes together. When downstream positive feedback or a threshold switch exists, such random differences are amplified and locked in, as in competence and sporulation choices in Bacillus subtilis or the lysis-lysogeny decision of phage. Noise is thus both a nuisance and a mechanism for diversifying an isogenic population and hedging risk.

负反馈与正反馈在生物网络中分别产生什么动态行为？

What dynamic behaviours do negative and positive feedback generate in biological networks?

负反馈使输出反向抑制自身上游，效果是压缩稳态波动、提高对参数变化的鲁棒性并加快响应，若在反馈回路中引入足够的时间延迟或多步中介，还会产生持续振荡，如 p53-MDM2 振荡与生物钟。正反馈和双负反馈则放大微小差异，在合适参数下形成双稳态与滞后现象，使系统在两个稳定状态间做全或无切换，并对撤除刺激具有记忆。真实网络常把两者耦合：正反馈负责决策的不可逆性，负反馈负责限定幅度与恢复，这种组合是可靠细胞决策的通用设计。
Negative feedback lets output inhibit its own upstream step, which compresses steady-state fluctuations, buys robustness to parameter variation and speeds response, and with sufficient delay or multiple intermediates it produces sustained oscillation, as in p53-MDM2 cycles and circadian clocks. Positive or double-negative feedback amplifies small differences and, in the right parameter range, creates bistability and hysteresis so the system flips all-or-none between states and remembers a stimulus after it is withdrawn. Real networks usually couple both: positive feedback makes a decision irreversible while negative feedback bounds amplitude and permits reset, a generic design for reliable cellular decisions.

基因调控网络中的前馈环等网络模块有什么功能意义？

What functional meaning do network motifs such as the feed-forward loop carry in gene regulatory networks?

网络模块是在真实调控网络中出现频率显著高于随机网络的小型连接模式，说明它们被选择保留。相干型前馈环中调控因子既直接激活靶基因又经中间因子间接激活，能过滤短暂噪声脉冲、只对持续信号做出响应；非相干型前馈环则产生脉冲式响应并加速达到稳态。此外负自调控可缩短响应时间并降低表达噪声，单输入模块可实现基因的时序表达。识别这些模块使研究者能从静态连线图推断动态功能。
Network motifs are small wiring patterns that appear far more often in real regulatory networks than in randomized ones, implying they were selected. In a coherent feed-forward loop a regulator activates its target both directly and through an intermediate, which filters brief noise pulses and responds only to persistent input, while the incoherent version generates a pulse-like response and speeds approach to steady state. Negative autoregulation shortens response time and reduces expression noise, and single-input modules impose temporal ordering of gene expression. Identifying motifs lets researchers infer dynamic function from a static wiring diagram.

为什么蛋白互作网络常呈无标度特征，这对细胞的鲁棒性意味着什么？

Why do protein interaction networks often look scale-free, and what does that imply for cellular robustness?

在许多物种的蛋白互作和代谢网络中，节点连接度分布近似幂律，多数蛋白只有少量伙伴，而少数枢纽蛋白连接极多，这与基因复制加优先连接的演化过程相符。这样的拓扑对随机移除节点相当耐受，因为随机命中的多为低度节点，网络连通性几乎不变。但若定向破坏枢纽节点，网络会迅速瓦解，这解释了枢纽基因敲除更常致死，也提示以枢纽为靶点的药物策略与其潜在毒性。
In protein interaction and metabolic networks of many species the degree distribution approximates a power law: most proteins have few partners while a few hubs have very many, consistent with an evolutionary process of gene duplication plus preferential attachment. Such topology tolerates random node removal well, because a random hit usually lands on a low-degree node and connectivity barely changes. Targeted removal of hubs, however, fragments the network quickly, which explains why deleting hub genes is more often lethal and why hub-directed drugs can be both potent and toxic.

系统生物学关注什么，与传统的单基因单分子研究如何互补？

What does systems biology focus on, and how is it complementary to traditional single-gene studies?

系统生物学把细胞或生物体视为由基因、蛋白和代谢物相互作用构成的网络，关注在扰动下网络整体的动态行为，而不只是单个组分的功能。它依赖高通量组学测量、定量数学建模和迭代的实验验证，去解释稳态维持、开关式决策和振荡等系统层面性质。传统还原论研究提供必需的分子机制细节，系统方法则回答这些组件为何组合成如此结构，二者互为前提。
Systems biology treats a cell or organism as a network of interacting genes, proteins and metabolites and asks how that network behaves dynamically under perturbation, not merely what one component does. It combines high-throughput omics measurement, quantitative mathematical modelling and iterative experimental testing to explain system-level properties such as homeostasis, switch-like decisions and oscillation. Reductionist work supplies the indispensable molecular detail while the systems view explains why those parts are wired as they are, so the two approaches depend on each other.

通量平衡分析如何在不知道酶动力学参数的情况下预测细胞的生长表型？

How can flux balance analysis predict growth phenotypes without knowing enzyme kinetic parameters?

通量平衡分析假设代谢处于稳态，即每种胞内代谢物的生成与消耗相等，于是化学计量矩阵与通量向量的乘积为零，构成一组线性约束。再加上营养摄取上限、反应可逆性和能量维持等边界条件，可行通量空间被限定为一个凸多面体；在其中以生物量合成通量最大化为目标做线性规划，即可预测生长速率和副产物分泌。该方法只需化学计量信息而不需动力学常数，因此能用于全基因组尺度模型，并常用来预测基因敲除是否致死。
Flux balance analysis assumes metabolism is at steady state, so production equals consumption for every internal metabolite and the stoichiometric matrix times the flux vector equals zero, giving a set of linear constraints. Adding bounds such as nutrient uptake limits, reaction reversibility and maintenance energy confines feasible fluxes to a convex polytope, and linear programming that maximizes biomass synthesis within it predicts growth rate and by-product secretion. Because only stoichiometry is needed rather than kinetic constants, the method scales to genome-wide models and is widely used to predict whether a gene deletion is lethal.

把基因组、转录组、蛋白组和代谢组数据整合起来，主要难点在哪里？

What are the main difficulties in integrating genomic, transcriptomic, proteomic and metabolomic data?

各层数据的测量尺度、动态范围、噪声结构和缺失模式差别很大，蛋白与代谢物的覆盖度远低于测序，直接拼接容易被技术批次效应主导。层与层之间还存在时间延迟和非线性关系，转录水平变化并不能线性预测蛋白丰度或代谢通量，需要引入调控与化学计量的先验知识而非单纯相关分析。此外样本数远小于特征数带来严重过拟合风险，因此整合分析通常依赖降维、多视图因子模型和网络先验，并要求独立队列验证。
The layers differ sharply in measurement scale, dynamic range, noise structure and missingness, and coverage of proteins and metabolites is far lower than that of sequencing, so naive concatenation is easily dominated by technical batch effects. Relations between layers are delayed and nonlinear, since transcript changes do not linearly predict protein abundance or metabolic flux, which calls for regulatory and stoichiometric prior knowledge rather than correlation alone. Sample numbers are also far smaller than feature numbers, creating severe overfitting risk, so integration usually relies on dimensionality reduction, multi-view factor models and network priors, with validation in independent cohorts.
