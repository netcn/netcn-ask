保护遗传学如何评估种群的近交衰退风险？

How does conservation genetics assess inbreeding depression risk?

通过杂合度、近交系数和有效种群大小来评估；近交会让有害的隐性等位基因暴露，降低幼体存活率。
Heterozygosity, inbreeding coefficient, and effective population size reveal how inbreeding exposes deleterious recessives and lowers survival.

迁徙廊道的设计为什么要考虑基因流？

Why should wildlife corridor design consider gene flow?

连通的廊道让隔离的种群交换个体与基因，避免近交并提升整体适应潜力，是保护遗传多样性的关键。
Connected corridors let isolated populations exchange genes, avoiding inbreeding and boosting adaptive potential.

什么是遗传瓶颈，它对濒危物种意味着什么？

What is a genetic bottleneck and what does it mean for endangered species?

种群骤减会使基因多样性大幅丢失，之后即使数量回升也缺乏变异，难以适应环境变化。
A sharp population crash loses diversity; even recovery leaves low variation, hampering adaptation.

为什么要保护物种的遗传多样性？

Why protect the genetic diversity of species?

遗传多样性越高，种群越能适应环境变化、抵抗疾病和避免近交，是物种长期存活的保险。
Higher genetic diversity lets populations adapt to change, resist disease, and avoid inbreeding, an insurance for long-term survival.
