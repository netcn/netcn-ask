CAR-T疗法怎样让免疫细胞攻击肿瘤？

How does CAR-T therapy make immune cells attack tumors?

抽取患者T细胞，基因改造使其表达识别肿瘤抗原的嵌合抗原受体（CAR），扩增后回输，使其精准杀伤癌细胞，对部分血液癌效果显著。
A patient's T cells are engineered to express a chimeric antigen receptor (CAR) that recognizes tumor antigens, expanded, and reinfused to precisely kill cancer cells—effective in some blood cancers.

“液态活检”为什么只需一管血就能查癌症？

Why can a 'liquid biopsy' detect cancer from a blood tube?

肿瘤会向血液释放游离DNA（ctDNA）和循环肿瘤细胞，测序这些片段可发现致癌突变并监测复发，比组织活检更微创、可重复。
Tumors shed cell-free DNA (ctDNA) and circulating tumor cells into blood; sequencing these fragments reveals cancer mutations and monitors relapse—less invasive and repeatable than tissue biopsy.

单克隆抗体药物是怎样工作的？

How do monoclonal antibody drugs work?

把识别特定靶蛋白（如癌细胞表面标志物）的抗体大量制备，它们像精准制导导弹结合目标，阻断信号或引导免疫系统清除病变细胞。
Monoclonal antibodies targeting a specific protein (e.g. a cancer marker) are mass-produced; like guided missiles they bind the target, blocking signals or flagging bad cells for removal.
