酶联级联（如凝血、补体）为何能放大信号？

Why can enzyme cascades (e.g. clotting, complement) amplify a signal?

级联中每个活化的酶能激活许多下游分子，信号被逐级放大，使微弱的起始信号产生巨大的细胞反应。这也让机体能快速响应损伤或感染。
In a cascade each activated enzyme activates many downstream molecules, so the signal is amplified at every step, turning a tiny trigger into a large response—letting the body react fast to injury or infection.

什么是酶的别构调节？

What is allosteric regulation of an enzyme?

别构调节指小分子结合到酶的非活性部位，改变其形状，从而增强或抑制酶的活力。许多代谢通路靠别构效应实现反馈调控。
Allosteric regulation occurs when a small molecule binds a site other than the active site, changing the enzyme's shape to boost or inhibit its activity. Many metabolic pathways use it for feedback control.

竞争性抑制剂和非竞争性抑制剂有何区别？

How do competitive and non-competitive inhibitors differ?

竞争性抑制剂与底物争夺酶的活性部位，可被高底物浓度冲淡；非竞争性抑制剂结合别处、改变酶构象，增加底物浓度也无法恢复活性。
A competitive inhibitor competes with the substrate for the active site and can be outcompeted by more substrate; a non-competitive inhibitor binds elsewhere and changes the shape, so adding substrate cannot restore activity.

辅酶和辅基有什么作用？

What do cofactors and coenzymes do?

许多酶需要非蛋白的辅助因子才能工作：金属离子（如Mg²⁺、Zn²⁺）或有机辅酶（如NAD⁺、辅酶A）参与传递电子、基团，是酶催化不可或缺的一部分。
Many enzymes need non-protein helpers: metal ions (Mg²⁺, Zn²⁺) or organic coenzymes (NAD⁺, coenzyme A) that carry electrons or groups, and are essential for catalysis.

酶是什么？为什么需要适宜的温度？

What is an enzyme, and why does it need a suitable temperature?

酶是加速化学反应的蛋白质催化剂，本身不被消耗。温度过高会让酶变性失活，温度过低则反应变慢，所以酶在适温下效率最高。
Enzymes are protein catalysts that speed up reactions without being consumed. Too much heat denatures them, while cold slows them down, so each works best at its optimal temperature.
