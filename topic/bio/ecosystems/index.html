水葫芦（凤眼莲）当年被当成好东西引进，为什么后来在一些水域成了“灾害”？

Water hyacinth was once welcomed—why did it become a “disaster” in some waters?

它繁殖极快、又无天敌制约，铺满水面挡住阳光和氧气，导致水下植物和鱼虾死亡、水质变臭、堵塞河道。外来物种在新环境失去平衡，就易泛滥成灾。
It reproduces explosively with no natural enemy, blanketing surfaces, blocking light and oxygen, killing underwater life and clogging rivers. An alien species without balance in its new home easily turns invasive.

落叶“化作春泥”后，为什么能让整片森林越来越肥沃，腐殖质是谁做出来的？

Fallen leaves “become spring mud”—why does that enrich the whole forest, and who makes the humus?

枯叶被真菌、细菌等分解者慢慢降解，混合动植物残体形成黑亮的腐殖质，它能保水保肥、疏松土壤，像海绵一样储存养分供树根吸收，所以森林越落叶越肥沃。
Decomposers like fungi and bacteria break leaves into dark humus, which holds water and nutrients and loosens soil like a sponge for roots. More leaf fall, richer forest—nature recycles everything.

蜜蜂对大自然为什么这么重要？

Why are bees so important to nature?

蜜蜂传粉让果树和作物结出果实，人类吃的三分之二的食物都和传粉有关。没有蜜蜂，很多植物和食物都会变少。
Bees pollinate fruit trees and crops so they bear fruit, and about two-thirds of our food relies on pollination. Without bees, many plants and foods would disappear.

森林对地球有什么用？

What do forests do for the Earth?

森林像地球的绿肺，吸收二氧化碳并放出氧气。它们还给无数动物当作家，并帮土壤留住水分。
Forests act like the Earth's green lungs, taking in carbon dioxide and releasing oxygen. They also give countless animals a home and help soil hold water.

海洋为什么对地球很重要？

Why are oceans important to the Earth?

海洋覆盖了地球七成多的面积，产生我们呼吸的大部分氧气。它调节气候，也是无数生物的家园。
Oceans cover more than 70 percent of Earth and produce most of the oxygen we breathe. They also regulate the climate and are home to countless creatures.
