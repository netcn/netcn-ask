人一紧张、考试前就老想上厕所，肠道和大脑到底是怎么连在一起的？

Why do nerves or pre-exam jitters send us to the toilet—how are gut and brain linked?

肠和脑由“肠—脑轴”双向连通：紧张时大脑通过交感神经让肠道蠕动加快、分泌增多，于是想排便。这原本是准备“战斗或逃跑”的古老反应。
Gut and brain talk via the gut–brain axis; under stress the brain's sympathetic nerves speed intestinal motion and secretion, creating the urge—an ancient “fight or flight” prep response.

肠道菌群会不会影响我们的心情，甚至让我们更想吃某类食物？

Can gut bacteria affect our mood, even make us crave certain foods?

会。菌群通过“肠—脑轴”：产生神经递质（如血清素前体、GABA）、调节炎症并影响迷走神经。不同菌偏好不同养分，会微妙地改变宿主食欲；压力也会反过来改变菌群，形成循环。
Yes. The microbiome uses the gut–brain axis—making neurotransmitters (serotonin precursors, GABA), shaping inflammation and signaling via the vagus nerve. Different bacteria prefer different nutrients and subtly steer appetite; stress flips back and reshapes the flora.

无菌动物（gnotobiotic）模型如何帮助研究者确立肠道菌群与疾病的因果关系？

How do gnotobiotic (germ-free) animal models help researchers establish causal links between the gut microbiome and disease?

无菌动物在无菌环境中繁育、体内无微生物，可作为空白背景接受单一或已定义的菌群定植。通过比较定植与未定植个体的表型差异，并做菌群移植，研究者能将特定菌株与代谢或免疫表型直接挂钩，排除混杂。
Gnotobiotic animals are bred in sterile conditions with no microbes and can be colonized with a single or defined community, providing a blank background. By comparing phenotypically colonized versus uncolonized individuals and performing transplants, researchers directly link specific strains to metabolic or immune traits while controlling confounders.

肠道微生物产生的短链脂肪酸如何影响远端器官的免疫与代谢？

How do short-chain fatty acids produced by gut microbes affect immunity and metabolism in distal organs?

短链脂肪酸（乙酸、丙酸、丁酸）可被吸收进入循环，丁酸是结肠细胞主要能源并能增强肠道屏障。它们作为受体配体（如 GPR43）和组蛋白去乙酰化酶抑制剂，调节全身炎症与胰岛素敏感性，连接肠道与肝脏、脂肪等远端组织。
Short-chain fatty acids (acetate, propionate, butyrate) are absorbed into circulation; butyrate fuels colonocytes and strengthens the barrier. Acting as ligands for receptors such as GPR43 and as histone deacetylase inhibitors, they modulate systemic inflammation and insulin sensitivity, linking the gut to distal tissues like liver and fat.

菌群-肠-脑轴通过哪些神经与内分泌通路双向沟通？

Through which neural and endocrine pathways does the microbiota-gut-brain axis communicate bidirectionally?

该轴包括迷走神经传入、肠嗜铬细胞释放的血清素、微生物代谢物（短链脂肪酸、胆汁酸）以及下丘脑-垂体-肾上腺轴激素。菌群可经这些通路影响情绪与认知，而应激激素反过来改变菌群组成，形成闭环。
The axis includes vagal afferents, serotonin released by enterochromaffin cells, microbial metabolites (short-chain fatty acids, bile acids) and hypothalamic-pituitary-adrenal axis hormones. Microbes can shape mood and cognition through these routes, while stress hormones in turn alter microbial composition, forming a feedback loop.

粪便菌群移植（FMT）用于治疗哪种疾病最成熟，其原理是什么？

For which disease is fecal microbiota transplantation (FMT) most established, and what is its rationale?

FMT 最成熟的适应症是复发性艰难梭菌感染，通过把健康供体的粪便菌群导入患者肠道以重建多样菌群、抑制病原菌。相比抗生素，它能恢复生态抵抗并显著降低复发率。
FMT's most established indication is recurrent Clostridioides difficile infection, where stool microbiota from a healthy donor is introduced to rebuild a diverse community and suppress the pathogen. Compared with antibiotics, it restores ecological resistance and markedly cuts recurrence.

肠道菌群主要通过哪些途径影响宿主的代谢健康？

Through which main pathways does the gut microbiome influence host metabolic health?

菌群发酵膳食纤维产生短链脂肪酸（如丁酸），调节能量平衡与肠道屏障；还能影响胆汁酸代谢与肠脑信号。失调与肥胖、2 型糖尿病和炎症性肠病风险上升相关。
Gut microbes ferment dietary fiber into short-chain fatty acids such as butyrate, which tune energy balance and the intestinal barrier, and they also shape bile-acid metabolism and gut-brain signaling. Dysbiosis is linked to higher risks of obesity, type 2 diabetes and inflammatory bowel disease.

皮肤微生物组在健康皮肤屏障中扮演什么角色？

What role does the skin microbiome play in a healthy skin barrier?

皮肤表面的常驻菌（如葡萄球菌、丙酸杆菌）通过竞争营养与分泌抗菌物质抑制病原菌定植。它们还参与调节局部免疫与皮脂分解，维持弱酸环境，失调与痤疮、特应性皮炎等相关。
Resident skin microbes such as staphylococci and propionibacteria inhibit pathogen colonization by competing for nutrients and secreting antimicrobials. They also help regulate local immunity and lipid breakdown to maintain an acidic environment; dysbiosis is linked to acne and atopic dermatitis.

为什么抗生素使用会同时带来治疗收益与对微生物组的长期扰动？

Why does antibiotic use bring therapeutic benefit yet cause long-term perturbation of the microbiome?

抗生素杀灭病原菌缓解感染，但也会无差别削减共生菌，降低多样性并留下生态空位。部分菌群可能在数周至数月恢复，但某些分类群与功能基因或长期缺失，增加继发感染与代谢风险。
Antibiotics kill pathogens to resolve infection but also broadly deplete commensals, lowering diversity and leaving ecological vacancies. Some communities recover within weeks to months, yet certain taxa and functional genes may remain absent long-term, raising risks of secondary infection and metabolic disruption.
