人身体里好像有个“生物钟”，天天差不多时候困、饿、精神，熬夜为什么会把它打乱？

The body seems to have a “clock”—same times for sleep, hunger, alertness. Why does staying up late disrupt it?

下丘脑视交叉上核是主钟，受光线（尤其晨光）校准，指挥全身激素（如皮质醇、褪黑素）起伏。熬夜、蓝光和三餐不定时会给它错误信号，让钟“走慢或走乱”，表现为失眠、白天困。
The suprachiasmatic nucleus is the master clock, set by light (especially morning) and driving hormones like cortisol and melatonin. Late nights, blue light and irregular meals send wrong signals, desyncing the clock—insomnia, daytime drowsiness.

#生物钟 #视交叉上核 #熬夜

跨时区飞行后的时差感从何而来,身体靠什么信号重新对表?

What causes jet lag after crossing time zones, and what cues reset the body's clock?

视交叉上核按原有节律继续发布睡眠、体温和激素的时间表,与目的地光照周期错位便产生了时差感。重置主要依靠视网膜接收的光信息经视网膜下丘脑束传至视交叉上核,调整其神经元振荡相位。褪黑素分泌时机和进食时间也会参与校准,因此规律的光照与进餐安排有助于更快适应新时区。
The suprachiasmatic nucleus keeps issuing schedules for sleep, body temperature, and hormones according to the old rhythm, and the mismatch with the destination light cycle is felt as jet lag. Resetting relies chiefly on light detected by the retina, relayed via the retinohypothalamic tract to shift the phase of those oscillating neurons. Melatonin timing and meal schedules also contribute, so regular exposure to daylight and meals helps travelers adapt sooner.

#时间生物学 #昼夜节律

为什么傍晚的强光会把生物钟推后，而清晨的光照却能把它提前？

Why does bright evening light delay your internal clock while morning light shifts it earlier?

视网膜中含黑视素的神经节细胞把光信号传给视交叉上核——这一主起搏点进而同步全身各组织的外周时钟。生物钟对光的响应呈相位反应曲线：在生理傍晚照光会使周期推迟，而在体温最低点之后照光则会提前。临床上正利用这条曲线，通过定时强光照射来纠正睡眠时相延迟障碍和跨时区飞行造成的节律紊乱。
Melanopsin-containing retinal ganglion cells relay light signals to the suprachiasmatic nucleus, the master pacemaker that synchronizes peripheral clocks across tissues. The clock answers light with a phase response curve: exposure during the biological evening delays the cycle, while exposure after the body temperature minimum advances it. Clinicians exploit this curve with timed bright-light sessions to correct delayed sleep-wake phase disorder and jet lag.

#昼夜节律 #相位反应曲线

为什么有的花只在白天开、有的偏要夜里开，它们看得懂时间吗？

Why do some flowers open by day and others only at night—can they tell time?

能！植物体内有“生物钟”，会根据光照和温度安排开花，好让授粉者（蜜蜂白天、飞蛾夜晚）刚好在场。夜里开的如昙花、月见草，就是等夜间昆虫来传粉。
They can! Plants have internal clocks that time flowering to light and temperature so pollinators show up—bees by day, moths by night. Night bloomers like epiphyllum and evening primrose wait for nocturnal insects.
