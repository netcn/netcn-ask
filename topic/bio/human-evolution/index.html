直立人“走出非洲”意味着什么？

What does Homo erectus 'out of Africa' mean?

直立人是较早扩散出非洲的人属成员，约180万年前进入欧亚，使用手斧、可能控火，是后续各人群迁徙的重要先声。
Homo erectus was an early Homo that spread out of Africa about 1.8 million years ago, using handaxes and possibly fire—paving the way for later migrations.

科学家如何从化石中提取古人类DNA？

How do scientists extract ancient human DNA from fossils?

从化石致密骨或牙齿中提取极少量、高度降解的DNA，用测序与污染控制比对，重建古人类基因组，揭示亲缘与迁徙史。
Tiny, heavily degraded DNA is pulled from dense bone or teeth, sequenced under strict contamination control, and compared to reconstruct ancient genomes and reveal kinship and migration.

尼安德特人和现代人有过交集吗？

Did Neanderthals and modern humans ever meet?

尼安德特人与走出非洲的智人曾在欧亚共存并发生过杂交，今天许多非非洲人基因组中仍保留约1%–4%的尼安德特人基因。
Neanderthals coexisted with migrating Homo sapiens in Eurasia and interbred; today many non-Africans carry about 1–4% Neanderthal DNA.
