亲缘关系接近的物种是如何通过生态位分化共存的？

How do closely related species coexist through niche partitioning?

生态位分化指共存的物种在资源利用上产生系统差异：空间分化（同片森林中不同鸟在树冠、树干、地面等不同高度取食）、时间分化（昼夜节律或季节错开活动）、食物分化（取食种子大小、猎物种类不同）、形态分化（达尔文雀喙的大小和形状对应不同食物，加勒比安乐蜥按树干位置、直径和植被类型细分栖息地）。此外，竞争性释放和性状替换（同域分布物种间差异增大）是生态位分化的进化证据。这些分化降低了种间竞争，是解释高多样性共存（如热带雨林）的核心机制。
Niche partitioning means coexisting species systematically differ in resource use: spatial partitioning (birds in the same forest feed at different heights, canopy, trunk, ground), temporal partitioning (activity shifted in time of day or season), food partitioning (different seed sizes or prey types), and morphological partitioning (Darwin's finches differ in beak size and shape for different seeds; Caribbean Anolis lizards subdivide habitats by perch height, diameter, and vegetation type). Competitive release and character displacement, greater divergence where species co-occur, provide evolutionary evidence. Reduced competition through such partitioning is the core mechanism explaining highly diverse coexistence such as in tropical rainforests.

什么是生态演替？

What is ecological succession?

生态演替是群落随时间逐步更替的过程，可分为原生和次生演替。例如火山岩上从无生命到长出地衣、苔藓，再到草本和森林，是原生演替。森林火灾后的恢复则属于次生演替。
Ecological succession is the gradual replacement of communities over time, divided into primary and secondary types. On bare volcanic rock, life begins with lichens and mosses, then grasses and eventually forest, an example of primary succession. Recovery after a forest fire is secondary succession.

高斯的草履虫实验和竞争排斥原理说明了什么？

What do Gause's Paramecium experiments and the competitive exclusion principle demonstrate?

高斯将大草履虫和双小核草履虫混合培养在单一有限资源的培养基中，结果竞争力较弱的大草履虫趋于灭绝，而单独培养时两者都能正常存活，由此提出竞争排斥原理：生态位完全重叠的两个物种不能在同一环境中长期共存。Lotka-Volterra竞争模型给出共存条件：当每个物种的种内竞争强度大于其对另一物种的种间竞争时，两物种可稳定共存。实际群落中物种通过生态位分化（资源分割）弱化竞争，使多样性得以维持，如潮间带藤壶按潮位分层分布。
When Gause cultured Paramecium aurelia and P. caudatum together on a single limited resource, the poorer competitor (P. caudatum) tended to go extinct, though either species alone thrived, leading to the competitive exclusion principle: two species with identical niches cannot coexist indefinitely in the same environment. The Lotka-Volterra competition model shows that coexistence is stable when each species' intraspecific competition exceeds its interspecific effect on the other. In real communities, species reduce competition through niche differentiation (resource partitioning), e.g., barnacles on the shore stratify by tidal level, sustaining diversity.

What is the spatial structure of a community?

群落的空间结构指生物在垂直和水平方向上的分层与分布格局。例如森林分乔木层、灌木层和草本层，提高了对光和空间的利用。水平分布则常受土壤、湿度等环境梯度影响。
The spatial structure of a community is how organisms are layered and distributed vertically and horizontally. A forest, for example, has tree, shrub, and herb layers that improve use of light and space. Horizontal patterns are often shaped by gradients like soil and moisture.

什么是群落演替？

What is ecological succession?

群落演替是生态系统在干扰或裸地上随时间有序更替的过程，先锋物种先定居，逐步改善环境后被更复杂的物种取代。可分为初生演替（无土壤裸地）和次生演替（原有土壤保留），最终可能趋向相对稳定的顶极群落。
Succession is the orderly, time-ordered replacement of communities after disturbance or on bare ground: pioneer species settle first and modify the environment until more complex species replace them. It ranges from primary (no soil) to secondary (soil retained) succession and may approach a relatively stable climax community.

"中度干扰假说"如何解释为什么中等频率的干扰反而物种最多？

How does the intermediate disturbance hypothesis explain why moderate disturbance yields the most species?

该假说认为，扰动（如风暴、火灾）过于频繁会不断清除物种，过于稀少则少数竞争强者独占资源，二者都降低多样性。中等频率的干扰为早期与晚期物种都留出空间，防止优势种垄断，从而使物种多样性达到最高。
The hypothesis holds that very frequent disturbance wipes species out and very rare disturbance lets competitive dominants monopolize resources—both lower diversity. Moderate disturbance leaves room for both early- and late-successional species, preventing any single strong competitor from excluding the rest and maximizing diversity.

什么是生物富集（生物放大）？

What is biomagnification?

能否放大看物质性质：难降解、脂溶又排不出的污染物（有机氯农药、甲基汞）会长期留在体内。消费者吃下的不只是食物，还有猎物攒的存货，一生摄入远超自身体重，于是浓度沿食物链逐级抬升，顶级捕食者可比周围水体高出成千上万倍。对照来看：能量每级只剩约十分之一，污染物却每级都加。两处易混：生物积累指同一个体对环境的富集，放大指跨营养级上升；水溶性强、易排出的不走这条路。
Whether a substance magnifies depends on its chemistry: pollutants that resist breakdown, dissolve in fat and are hard to excrete (organochlorine pesticides, methylmercury) stay put in an organism's tissues. A consumer eats not just food but everything its prey had stored, and over a lifetime it ingests far more than its own body weight, so concentrations climb rung by rung until top predators carry levels thousands of times above the surrounding water. Set that against energy in a food chain, which drops to roughly a tenth at each step while the pollutant rises at each step. Two confusions to separate: bioaccumulation is one organism enriching against its environment, biomagnification is the rise across trophic levels; and substances that dissolve in water and are readily excreted do not take this route.

碳在光合作用、呼吸作用、分解和燃烧之间循环。植物通过光合作用把空气中的二氧化碳固定进有机物（糖、纤维素），成为木头、粮食的骨架；动物吃植物又把碳带进动物身体，呼吸时把碳以二氧化碳形式还给大气。生物死亡后，分解者把有机碳矿化回二氧化碳。还有一部分碳被沉积物封存，经地质作用变成煤炭、石油，燃烧又把它们释放出来。海洋也是巨大的碳库，吸收大气二氧化碳；人类燃烧化石燃料加速了这个过程，导致大气二氧化碳浓度上升。
Carbon cycles through photosynthesis, respiration, decomposition and combustion. Plants fix atmospheric CO2 into organic molecules, sugars and cellulose, which form the skeleton of wood and grain; animals eat plants and take carbon into their bodies, then return it to the air as CO2 when they breathe. When organisms die, decomposers mineralize the organic carbon back to CO2. Some carbon gets buried in sediments and, over geological time, becomes coal and oil; burning releases it again. The ocean is also a huge carbon reservoir that absorbs CO2 from the atmosphere, and burning fossil fuels has accelerated the flow, raising atmospheric CO2 levels.

The Arctic food web is short and steep: ice algae on sea ice are the primary producers, eaten by small crustaceans such as krill, passing up through fish and seals to the top predator, the polar bear. The ice edge that forms each spring when sea ice melts is where ice algae bloom and zooplankton gather; as sea ice declines, light and water-column structure change, algal production drops, and the whole chain goes short of food from the bottom up, while polar bears lose their hunting platform, swim farther and lose condition. 为什么地衣常被用作监测空气质量的生物指示物？

Why are lichens commonly used as bioindicators of air quality?

地衣是真菌与藻类（或蓝细菌）的共生体，没有根和角质层，营养和水分几乎全部来自空气和降水，因此对空气中的污染物（特别是SO2和氮氧化物）极其敏感。污染物会破坏藻类光合作用，使地衣生长受阻、枯死或仅耐受性强的种类存活。因此通过调查地衣的种类组成、覆盖度和生长状况，可以间接评估区域大气硫氧化物等污染水平，是廉价有效的环境监测方法。
Lichens are symbiotic associations of a fungus with algae or cyanobacteria. Lacking roots and a cuticle, they obtain nearly all their water and nutrients from air and rain, making them extremely sensitive to airborne pollutants, especially SO2 and nitrogen oxides, which damage the algal partner's photosynthesis. Pollutant exposure stunts or kills sensitive species, leaving only tolerant ones. Surveying lichen species composition, cover, and health provides an inexpensive, effective proxy for regional sulfur dioxide and other air pollution.

食物链里的能量为什么会越往上越少？

Why does energy decrease at every step up a food chain?

每一营养级的生物都要进行呼吸作用，把摄入能量的一部分变成维持生命的热量散失掉，剩下的才用于生长、繁殖并被下一级吃掉。平均每个营养级只有约10%的能量能传递给下一级，这就是“十分之一定律”。能量一路递减，所以食物链通常只有四五级就再也养不起了，营养级越高，需要的底层生物量越大。
Organisms at every trophic level respire, burning a large share of ingested energy into heat just to stay alive; only what goes into growth and reproduction can be eaten by the next level. On average only about ten percent of energy passes from one level to the next, the “10 percent rule.” Because energy dwindles at each step, food chains rarely stretch beyond four or five links, and higher levels require enormous biomass underneath.

种群增长的J型曲线和S型曲线有什么不同？

How do J-shaped and S-shaped population growth curves differ?

在无资源限制的理想环境中，种群按指数方程dN/dt=rN增长，增长率恒定、曲线呈J形，但现实中难以长期持续。受食物、空间、天敌等环境因素限制，种群增长随数量接近环境容纳量（K值）而减慢，符合逻辑斯蒂方程dN/dt=rN(1-N/K)，曲线呈S形并稳定在K附近波动。大多数自然种群呈现S型动态，密度制约因素（竞争、疾病、捕食）是其核心调节力量。
In an ideal environment without resource limits, a population grows exponentially (dN/dt = rN) with a constant growth rate, producing a J-shaped curve that cannot persist long in reality. Limited by food, space, and predators, growth slows as numbers approach the carrying capacity (K), fitting the logistic equation dN/dt = rN(1 - N/K), giving an S-shaped curve that stabilizes around K. Most natural populations show S-shaped dynamics, regulated chiefly by density-dependent factors such as competition, disease, and predation.

食物链描述能量和养分在生物之间依次传递的顺序，通常从植物开始。食草动物吃植物，食肉动物再吃食草动物。它展示了自然界谁吃谁的简单关系。
A food chain describes the sequence by which energy and nutrients pass from one organism to the next, usually starting with plants. Herbivores eat plants, and carnivores eat the herbivores. It shows the simple who-eats-whom relationships in nature.

为什么珊瑚礁被称为海洋中的雨林？

Why are coral reefs called the rainforests of the sea?

珊瑚礁虽然只占海洋面积很小一部分，却养育了约四分之一的海洋物种，极为丰富。它们像雨林一样结构复杂、物种密集。但珊瑚礁对水温升高非常敏感，容易白化死亡。
Coral reefs cover only a tiny fraction of the ocean but shelter about a quarter of all marine species, making them extraordinarily rich. Like rainforests, they are structurally complex and densely packed with life. Yet reefs are very sensitive to warming and easily bleach and die.

为什么蜜蜂对植物很重要？

Why are bees important to plants?

蜜蜂在采蜜时把花粉从一朵花带到另一朵，帮助植物完成授粉并结出果实和种子。许多农作物依赖蜜蜂等昆虫传粉才能丰收。蜜蜂数量下降会威胁粮食安全。
As bees gather nectar, they carry pollen from one flower to another, helping plants pollinate and produce fruit and seeds. Many crops depend on bees and other insects for pollination to yield a harvest. Declining bee numbers threaten food security.

大自然里的生产者、消费者和分解者分别做什么？

What do producers, consumers, and decomposers each do in nature?

生产者（如绿色植物）用阳光制造食物，消费者（如动物）吃植物或其他动物来获取能量，分解者（如蘑菇和细菌）把死掉的动植物分解成养分归还土壤。三者合作，让大自然的物质能循环往复。
Producers like green plants make food from sunlight, consumers like animals eat plants or other animals for energy, and decomposers like mushrooms and bacteria break dead plants and animals back into nutrients for the soil. Working together, they keep nature's materials cycling.

食物链和食物网在生态系统中起什么作用？

What role do food chains and food webs play in ecosystems?

食物链描述生物之间“谁吃谁”的线性能量传递，而食物网由多条交错的食物链组成，更真实地反映物种间复杂的营养关系。它们揭示了能量如何从生产者经由各级消费者流动，以及某一物种消失可能引发的连锁效应。
A food chain describes a linear “who eats whom” transfer of energy, while a food web interlinks many chains to reflect the complex feeding relationships among species. They show how energy flows from producers through consumers and how the loss of one species can ripple through the system.

生态位和栖息地有什么不同？

What is the difference between a niche and a habitat?

栖息地是生物生活的物理场所，而生态位描述其在环境中所扮演的功能角色，包括食物、活动时间和与其他物种的关系。
A habitat is the physical place where an organism lives, whereas a niche describes its functional role—its food, activity timing and relationships with other species.
