基因组印迹为何只表达来自单一亲本的那个拷贝,这种沉默如何维持?

Why does genomic imprinting silence one parental copy so only the other is expressed, and how is that silencing maintained?

印迹基因在精子或卵子形成时被打上亲本特异的DNA甲基化标记,受精后该标记保留而双亲等位基因携带不同的表观遗传状态。沉默通常由差异甲基化区招募CTCF或Polycomb复合体实现,阻断增强子与启动子的沟通。体细胞分裂时DNMT1将甲基化模式照抄到新链,使印迹在个体一生中保持稳定。
Imprinted genes acquire parent-specific DNA methylation marks during sperm or egg formation, and after fertilization the two alleles carry different epigenetic states. Silencing is usually enforced when a differentially methylated region recruits CTCF or Polycomb complexes, blocking enhancer-promoter communication. During cell division DNMT1 copies the methylation pattern onto the new strand, keeping the imprint stable across the lifespan.

雌性哺乳动物如何在每个细胞里整条沉默一条X染色体？她又为何因此成为嵌合体？

How does a female mammal silence one entire X chromosome in each of her cells, and why does she end up a mosaic?

早期胚胎会对X染色体进行计数，并在每个细胞中随机选定一条使之失活，浓缩成巴氏小体。长链非编码RNA Xist以顺式方式包被被选中的那条染色体，招募Polycomb复合体沉积抑制性的H3K27me3修饰，随后又在启动子CpG岛上加上DNA甲基化。由于沉默状态随细胞分裂被克隆式继承，相邻组织可能表达不同的等位基因，三花猫的毛色便是这种嵌合体的著名例证。
Early embryos count their X chromosomes and randomly select one per cell for inactivation, condensing it into the Barr body. The long noncoding RNA Xist coats the chosen chromosome in cis, recruiting Polycomb complexes that deposit repressive H3K27me3 marks followed by DNA methylation at promoter CpG islands. Because descendants inherit the silenced state clonally, neighboring tissues can express different alleles, famously visible as calico cat coat patterns.

为什么小鼠胚胎必须既有爸爸的又有妈妈的基因?基因组印记揭示的亲本博弈

Why do mouse embryos need genes from both parents? Genomic imprinting reveals parental tug-of-war.

大多数基因父母双方版本等价,但约百分之一的基因带着来源标签(印记),只表达单一亲本副本——在动物界为哺乳类所特有。进化解释是亲本利益的拉锯:父亲一方希望胚胎尽量汲取母体资源(他的基因可能在别的后代身上),母亲一方要平衡本次与未来生育。证据极端干脆:只用父本基因构建的小鼠胚胎长出大胎盘却发育停滞,只用母本基因则胚胎相对大而胎盘发育不良——两套基因组像谈判双方,缺一不可。印记基因出错会导致贝威氏综合征等罕见病,也解释了克隆哺乳动物的诸多困难。
Most genes treat both parental copies equally, but about one percent carry parent-of-origin marks (imprints) expressing only one side — unique to mammals. The evolutionary reading is parental tug-of-war: paternal genes push embryos to extract maximum maternal resources (his genes may ride other offspring), maternal genes balance this litter against future ones. The evidence is stark: mouse embryos with only paternal genomes grow huge placentas but stall, maternally-only ones build big embryos on skimpy placentas — two genomes negotiating, neither optional. Imprint errors cause syndromes like Beckwith-Wiedemann and much of cloning difficulty.

DNA 甲基化等表观遗传修饰如何在不改变序列的情况下影响基因表达？

How do epigenetic modifications like DNA methylation affect gene expression without changing the sequence?

DNA 甲基化多在 CpG 岛的胞嘧啶上加甲基，招募抑制性蛋白并阻碍转录因子结合，使基因沉默；这类修饰可在细胞分裂中稳定遗传，并受发育和环境因素调控，但不改变碱基序列。
DNA methylation typically adds a methyl group to cytosine in CpG islands, recruiting repressive proteins and blocking transcription-factor binding to silence genes; such marks are stably inherited through cell division and shaped by development and environment without altering the base sequence.

染色质重塑复合物如何调控基因的可及性？

How do chromatin-remodeling complexes regulate gene accessibility?

ATP 依赖的染色质重塑复合物通过滑动、弹出或重组核小体改变 DNA 在组蛋白上的包裹方式，使转录因子更易或更难结合启动子，从而在不改变序列的前提下调控转录。
ATP-dependent remodeling complexes slide, evict or restructure nucleosomes to alter how DNA is wrapped around histones, making promoters more or less accessible to transcription factors and thereby regulating transcription without sequence change.

表观遗传是什么，为什么一个人的经历可以不改DNA序列也传给后代？

What is epigenetics, and how can a person's experience pass to offspring without changing DNA?

DNA序列像字母，表观遗传像“批注”：甲基化、组蛋白修饰等能开关基因而不改字母顺序。饥荒、压力等环境可改变这些标记，并可能延续一两代，但大多不永久改写代码本身。
The DNA sequence is the letters; epigenetics are the “annotations”—methylation and histone marks switch genes on/off without altering letters. Famine or stress can shift these marks and possibly persist a generation or two, yet rarely rewrite the code permanently.

为什么两个细胞携带完全相同的DNA序列，基因活性却可能相差悬殊？

Why can two cells carrying identical DNA sequences show vastly different levels of gene activity?

DNA甲基化是在胞嘧啶上添加甲基基团（多发生于CpG二核苷酸），启动子区域的高甲基化通常会抑制该基因的转录。由于碱基本身的序列未被改动而基因输出却发生了改变，这类修饰属于表观遗传范畴。DNMT家族作为书写酶建立这一模式，并在每轮DNA复制后通过维持性甲基化将其拷贝到子链上。
DNA methylation adds methyl groups to cytosines, mostly at CpG dinucleotides, and dense methylation in a promoter generally suppresses that gene's transcription. Because the underlying base sequence stays untouched while gene output changes, the mark qualifies as epigenetic. DNMT enzymes install the pattern, and maintenance methylation copies it onto daughter strands after each round of DNA replication.

同卵双胞胎基因几乎一样,为什么长大后的性格与健康差异不小?

Identical twins share almost the same DNA — why do their personalities and health diverge with age?

表观遗传给出了关键一环:DNA序列之外,甲基化标记与组蛋白修饰决定着哪些基因被打开或沉默,而这些标记会随生活经历持续改写。研究者比较不同年龄的同卵双胞胎发现,年轻时两人的表观图谱几乎重合,年长者却在数百个基因上出现明显差异——饮食、压力、吸烟、运动都在其中留下印记。基因相同只说明乐谱一致,演奏强弱由表观修饰实时调整,于是同一对双胞胎走向了不完全相同的人生。
Epigenetics supplies a key part of the answer: beyond the DNA sequence, methylation marks and histone modifications decide which genes are on or off, and those marks keep changing with life experience. Comparing identical twins, researchers found the epigenetic patterns nearly overlap in the young but diverge across hundreds of genes in older pairs — diet, stress, smoking and exercise all leave traces. Identical genes mean an identical score; epigenetic marks act as the conductor adjusting every performance, so twin lives diverge.
