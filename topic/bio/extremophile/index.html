有些细菌能休眠上千年，遇水又“复活”，它们是怎么熬过这么久的？

Some bacteria sleep for millennia then “revive” with water—how do they survive so long?

它们形成坚固的内生孢子：把DNA和少量必要物质紧紧包裹、脱水、代谢几乎停止，像按了暂停键。孢子抗热、抗辐射、抗干燥，环境合适时重新吸水苏醒分裂。
They form tough endospores: DNA and essentials are packed tight, dehydrated, metabolism nearly halted—like pressing pause. Spores resist heat, radiation and dryness, reviving and dividing when conditions return.

#内生孢子 #休眠 #复苏

嗜酸原核生物在细胞质pH接近中性的情况下,依靠哪些机制维持跨膜pH稳态并避免酸性外环境对胞内酶系的破坏?

How do acidophilic prokaryotes maintain transmembrane pH homeostasis—keeping cytoplasmic pH near neutral—despite growth in strongly acidic environments that would denature most cytosolic enzymes?

耐酸菌主要通过三种协同机制应对极端酸性:反向转运蛋白以K+/H+和Na+/H+逆向运输将流入的质子排出胞外;细胞膜富含饱和脂肪酸并带有疏水基团修饰,显著降低质子通透性;外膜蛋白和表面糖链形成缓冲层中和局部酸度。此外,胞内伴侣蛋白可修复少量已发生部分变性的蛋白质。这些机制使 Acidihalobacter prosperus 等菌株能在pH 1–2的酸性矿坑水中完成铁硫氧化代谢。
Acidophiles counter extreme acidity through three cooperating mechanisms: K+/H+ and Na+/H+ antiporters expel the protons that leak inward; membranes enriched in saturated fatty acids with hydrophobic modifications sharply reduce proton permeability; and outer-membrane proteins plus surface polysaccharides form a buffering layer that neutralizes local acidity. Cytoplasmic chaperones additionally refold the small fraction of proteins that partially unfold. Together these systems let organisms such as Acidihalobacter prosperus run iron- and sulfur-oxidizing metabolism in pH 1–2 mine water.

嗜盐菌为什么需要高盐环境？

Why do halophiles require high-salt environments?

它们靠在细胞内累积高浓度的钾离子或兼容溶质来平衡外界渗透压，并拥有适应高盐的酶和膜。
They balance external osmotic pressure with intracellular K+ or compatible solutes and possess salt-adapted enzymes and membranes.

水熊虫为什么能扛住极端干燥和强辐射？

Why can tardigrades survive extreme dehydration and radiation?

脱水时它们进入隐生状态、合成无水保护蛋白并折叠 DNA，复苏后再修复损伤，可承受接近真空与强辐射。
In cryptobiosis they make protective proteins and compact DNA, then repair damage after rehydration, enduring near-vacuum and radiation.

#水熊虫 #隐生

耐辐射奇球菌能承受把自身基因组打成碎片的数千戈瑞伽马射线——它的看家本领却不是保护DNA免于断裂,而是另辟蹊径,妙在何处?

Deinococcus radiodurans endures gamma doses of several kilograys that shatter its own genome—yet its signature skill is not keeping DNA intact. Where does its ingenuity actually lie?

锰基抗氧化络合物优先保护蛋白质不受辐射氧化,于是DNA虽已碎裂数百段,修复酶体系本身仍运转如常。该菌借ESDSA同源重组途径,以完整拷贝为模板延长单链尾端,几十小时内把散乱片段逐段拼回环状染色体。“先保蛋白、后修DNA”的取舍策略,同样解释了它对干旱环境的出色耐受。
Manganese-based antioxidant complexes shield proteins from radiation-driven oxidation, so although the DNA breaks into hundreds of fragments, the repair machinery itself keeps working. Through ESDSA homologous recombination the bacterium extends long single-stranded tails templated on intact copies and stitches the scattered pieces back into a circular chromosome over tens of hours. Protecting proteins first and repairing DNA second equally explains its remarkable tolerance of desiccation.

什么是嗜极生物？

What are extremophiles?

它们是一类在极端温度、盐度、酸碱或辐射下也能繁盛的微生物，挑战了我们对生命宜居的传统认识。
They are microbes that thrive in extreme heat, salt, pH, or radiation, challenging traditional ideas of where life can live.
