身体如何调节血压？

How does the body regulate blood pressure?

肾脏释放肾素启动肾素-血管紧张素系统，使血管收缩、保钠保水升压；压力感受器与神经、激素共同维持血压稳态。
The kidneys release renin to start the renin-angiotensin system, constricting vessels and retaining salt and water to raise pressure; baroreceptors and hormones together maintain blood-pressure balance.

毛细血管为什么是物质交换的场所？

Why are capillaries the site of exchange?

毛细血管壁仅一层内皮细胞、管径极细且分布密集，血流慢、总面积大，便于氧气、营养和废物在血液与组织间扩散交换。
Capillary walls are a single endothelial cell thick, narrow and dense, with slow flow and huge surface area—ideal for diffusing oxygen, nutrients, and wastes between blood and tissue.

心脏是怎样把血液送到全身的？

How does the heart send blood around the body?

心脏分左右两边：右边把缺氧血送到肺换氧（肺循环），左边把含氧血泵到全身（体循环），瓣膜保证血液单向流动。
The heart has right and left sides: the right pumps oxygen-poor blood to the lungs (pulmonary circuit), the left pumps oxygen-rich blood to the body (systemic circuit), with valves keeping flow one-way.

红细胞靠什么把氧气运到全身？

How do red blood cells carry oxygen throughout the body?

红细胞含血红蛋白，其铁原子能可逆结合氧气，在肺里装载、在组织里释放，是高效运氧的关键分子。
Red blood cells contain hemoglobin whose iron atoms bind oxygen reversibly—loading in the lungs and releasing in tissues—making oxygen transport efficient.
