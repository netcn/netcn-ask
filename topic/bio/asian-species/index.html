藏羚羊每年穿越青藏高原长途迁徙，它们没有地图，靠什么找到方向和路线？

Tibetan antelopes migrate across the plateau without maps—what guides their direction and route?

它们主要靠继承自亲代的“种群记忆”和嗅觉、地磁、日月星辰及地形线索导航，迁徙路线世代相传；产羔地的高原草甸食物丰富、天敌较少，是长期自然选择固定的行为。
They navigate using inherited “population memory” plus smell, geomagnetism, celestial cues and terrain; the route passes down generations. The lambing grounds offer rich food and fewer predators—a behavior fixed by natural selection.

大熊猫几乎只吃竹子，可它的消化系统明明是食肉动物的样子，为什么会这样？

Giant pandas eat almost nothing but bamboo, yet their digestive system looks built for a carnivore—why?

大熊猫的祖先其实是吃肉的，约200万年前为了避开竞争、利用丰富的竹子才逐渐转为以竹为主食。它们的肠道仍短而简单、缺纤维素酶，靠大量进食和肠道微生物帮忙，只能吸收约17%的能量，所以一天要啃十几公斤竹子。
Panda ancestors were carnivorous; about 2 million years ago they shifted to abundant bamboo to avoid competition. Their gut is still short and simple, lacking cellulase, so they rely on huge intake and gut microbes, absorbing only ~17% of energy—hence eating over ten kg of bamboo daily.

银杏被称为“活化石”，地球上和它同类的植物几乎都灭绝了，为什么只有它留了下来？

Ginkgo is called a “living fossil”—nearly all its relatives went extinct. Why did only it survive?

银杏是最古老种子植物类群之一，曾广布全球，冰期后野生种群仅存于中国。它能在恶劣环境休眠、耐火、抗虫、寿命极长（可达千年），又因人类长期栽培才没彻底消失。
Ginkgo is among the oldest seed-plant lineages, once widespread. After ice ages wild populations survived only in China. It tolerates harsh conditions, fire and pests, lives thousands of years, and was kept alive by long human cultivation.

川金丝猴长着一张“阿凡达”般的蓝脸，这种颜色是怎么来的，有什么用？

Sichuan golden snub-nosed monkeys have eerie blue faces—where does the color come from and what is it for?

它们的蓝色并非来自色素，而是皮肤胶原纤维呈规则排列，把蓝光散射出来（结构色）。鲜艳的蓝脸在群体中有助于辨识个体、显示健康，并在求偶和等级竞争中起作用。
The blue comes not from pigment but from regularly arranged dermal collagen fibers that scatter blue light (structural color). It helps individuals recognize each other, signal health, and play a role in mating and rank.

海南长臂猿为什么被称为世界上最稀有的灵长类，它的情况有多危急？

Why is the Hainan gibbon called the world's rarest primate, and how dire is it?

它仅分布在海南霸王岭一小片原始雨林，曾因砍伐和狩猎只剩不到10只。经严格保护和栖息地恢复，现已回升到约30多只，但仍比大熊猫更稀少，是极危物种。
It lives only in a tiny patch of old-growth forest in Hainan's Bawangling. Logging and hunting once reduced it to under 10; protected habitat recovery has lifted it to about 30+, still rarer than the panda and critically endangered.

扬子鳄和电视里那种大鳄鱼有什么不一样，为什么好像只有中国有？

How is the Chinese alligator different from the big crocodiles on TV, and why is it only in China?

扬子鳄体型小、吻部短圆、性情温顺，是中国特有的淡水鳄，生活在长江下游的河湖沼泽；它比湾鳄等热带鳄更耐寒，但数量稀少，是国家一级保护动物。
The Chinese alligator is small, with a short rounded snout and gentle temperament—a freshwater crocodile endemic to China's lower Yangtze lakes and marshes. It tolerates cold better than tropical crocs but is rare and highly protected.

亚洲象和非洲象除了个头，还有什么一眼能看出来的不同？

Besides size, what's an easy way to tell Asian and African elephants apart?

亚洲象耳朵较小、头顶有两个鼓包，只有公象有象牙；非洲象耳朵大得像地图，雌雄都长牙，背脊中间凹陷。亚洲象主要分布在南亚和东南亚，中国云南也有少量。
Asian elephants have smaller ears, two domes on the head, and tusks only in males. African elephants have huge map-shaped ears, tusks in both sexes, and a sway-backed profile. Asian elephants live in South/Southeast Asia, with a small population in Yunnan, China.
