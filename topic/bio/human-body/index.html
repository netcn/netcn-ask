伤口愈合时为什么会发痒？

Why does a healing wound itch?

愈合早期新血管和肉芽组织生长会释放组胺等介质刺激神经末梢，同时干燥结痂牵拉周围皮肤，二者共同造成瘙痒；过度抓挠可能撕裂新生组织。
Early healing grows new vessels and granulation tissue that release histamine and other mediators stimulating nerve endings, while a drying scab tugs the skin—both cause itch; scratching can tear new tissue.

肺泡表面活性物质有什么生理功能？缺乏它会导致什么问题？

What is the physiological function of pulmonary surfactant, and what happens when it is deficient?

表面活性物质由Ⅱ型肺泡细胞分泌，主要成分是二棕榈酰磷脂酰胆碱（DPPC）等磷脂和表面活性蛋白，覆盖在肺泡内表面的液膜上，降低液-气界面的表面张力。呼气时肺泡缩小、表面活性物质相对浓度升高，表面张力进一步降低，防止肺泡塌陷（稳定小肺泡）；吸气时张力升高防止过度扩张，并减少呼吸做功。早产儿肺Ⅱ型细胞尚未成熟、缺乏表面活性物质，导致肺泡塌陷和新生儿呼吸窘迫综合征，可通过气管内补充外源表面活性物质治疗。
Pulmonary surfactant, secreted by type II alveolar cells, consists mainly of phospholipids such as dipalmitoylphosphatidylcholine (DPPC) and surfactant proteins. It lines the liquid film on the alveolar surface and lowers surface tension at the air-liquid interface. During expiration the alveoli shrink, the relative surfactant concentration rises, and surface tension falls further, preventing alveolar collapse and stabilizing small alveoli; during inspiration surface tension rises, preventing over-distension, and the work of breathing is reduced. In premature infants whose type II cells are immature, surfactant deficiency causes alveolar collapse and neonatal respiratory distress syndrome, treated by tracheal instillation of exogenous surfactant.

肾素-血管紧张素-醛固酮系统是如何调节血压和水盐平衡的？

How does the renin-angiotensin-aldosterone system regulate blood pressure and salt-water balance?

血压下降或肾血流减少时，入球小动脉的球旁细胞释放肾素，把肝脏合成的血管紧张素原剪切成血管紧张素I，再由血管紧张素转换酶（ACE）转化为血管紧张素II。血管紧张素II一方面强烈收缩全身小动脉迅速升压，另一方面刺激肾上腺皮质球状带分泌醛固酮，促进远曲小管和集合管重吸收Na+，水随之被重吸收，血容量增加，血压持久升高；它还促进抗利尿激素（ADH）释放，并增强渴觉。该负反馈系统是长期血压调控的核心。
When blood pressure or renal blood flow falls, juxtaglomerular cells of the afferent arteriole release renin, which cleaves liver-derived angiotensinogen into angiotensin I; angiotensin-converting enzyme (ACE) then converts it to angiotensin II. Angiotensin II strongly constricts systemic arterioles, quickly raising pressure, and stimulates the zona glomerulosa of the adrenal cortex to secrete aldosterone, which promotes Na+ reabsorption in the distal tubule and collecting duct; water follows, so blood volume and pressure rise persistently. Angiotensin II also promotes antidiuretic hormone (ADH) release and enhances thirst. This negative-feedback system is central to long-term blood pressure regulation.

什么是表型与基因型？

What are phenotype and genotype?

基因型是一个人的遗传信息本身，即DNA上携带的基因组合。表型是可观察到的特征，如身高、血型，由基因型与环境共同决定。同卵双胞胎基因型相同，但表型仍可能因环境而略有差异。
Genotype is a person's actual genetic information, the combination of genes carried in DNA. Phenotype is the observable trait, such as height or blood type, shaped by both genotype and environment. Identical twins share a genotype yet may differ slightly in phenotype due to environment.

为什么生病时体温会升高？发烧到底是帮身体还是害身体？

Why does body temperature rise during illness, and does fever help or harm the body?

病原体感染后，免疫细胞（如巨噬细胞）释放白细胞介素-1 等致热因子，它们作用于下丘脑的体温调节中枢，把“设定温度”调高，身体于是通过肌肉颤抖产热、皮肤血管收缩减少散热，体温升到新标准。中度发热能抑制部分病原体繁殖、加快免疫细胞活化和抗体生成，总体上利大于弊。但体温过高（如超过39℃）会损伤细胞和器官，对幼儿还可能导致热性惊厥，所以高热仍需积极降温。
After infection, immune cells such as macrophages release pyrogens like interleukin-1, which act on the hypothalamus's thermostat and raise the set point. The body then shivers to produce heat and constricts skin vessels to save it, climbing to the new target. A moderate fever slows some pathogens and speeds up immune activation and antibody production, so on balance it helps; but very high temperatures, above about 39 degrees Celsius, damage cells and organs and can trigger febrile seizures in young children, which is why high fever still needs active treatment.

精子发生和卵子发生过程有哪些主要差别？

What are the main differences between spermatogenesis and oogenesis?

精子发生在睾丸生精小管中进行，一个精原细胞经减数分裂形成4个等大、带鞭毛的精子，且从青春期起终生不断进行。卵子发生在卵巢中，一个卵原细胞经减数分裂只形成1个富含细胞质和营养的大卵细胞，其余细胞质被分配到3个极体中并退化；卵子的形成在胎儿期即已启动，出生后暂停于减数分裂I，排卵时卵母细胞停留在减数分裂II中期，受精时才完成第二次减数分裂。二者在时间、数量和产物大小上差异显著。
Spermatogenesis occurs in the seminiferous tubules of the testis: one spermatogonium undergoes meiosis to produce four equal-sized, flagellated sperm, and it continues continuously from puberty throughout life. Oogenesis occurs in the ovary: one oogonium yields a single large, cytoplasm-rich ovum, with the rest of the cytoplasm parceled into three polar bodies that degenerate. Oocyte formation begins in fetal life, arrests in prophase of meiosis I, resumes at puberty, and the secondary oocyte is again arrested at metaphase of meiosis II at ovulation; meiosis II is completed only after fertilization. The two processes differ greatly in timing, yield, and cell size.

为什么蚊子叮过的地方会鼓起一个又红又痒的小包？

Why does a mosquito bite leave a red, itchy lump?

痒和肿不是蚊子本身造成，而是你的免疫系统在“迎战”。蚊子吸血时注入的唾液含有防止血液凝固的物质，对大多数人来说这些蛋白是过敏原。免疫细胞识别后释放组胺，组胺让局部血管扩张、毛细血管通透性增加，液体渗出到组织里，就形成红、肿、痒的丘疹。再次被叮时反应常更快更重，说明免疫系统已经记住了它。
The itch and swelling are not caused by the mosquito itself but by your immune system fighting back. When it feeds, the mosquito injects saliva containing anticoagulants, and for most people those proteins act as allergens. Immune cells release histamine, which dilates local blood vessels and makes capillaries leaky; fluid seeps into the tissue, producing the red, swollen, itchy wheal. Subsequent bites usually react faster and harder, a sign the immune system remembers the intruder.

淋巴系统在维持体液平衡中起什么作用？

What role does the lymphatic system play in maintaining fluid balance?

血液流经毛细血管时，动脉端滤出的液体大部分在静脉端被重吸收，剩余约10%进入毛细淋巴管成为淋巴。淋巴沿淋巴管单向回流，最终经胸导管和右淋巴导管汇入锁骨下静脉回到血液循环，从而维持组织液生成与回流的动态平衡，防止水肿。淋巴管还负责运输肠道吸收的脂肪（乳糜）和免疫细胞。
When blood passes through capillaries, most of the fluid filtered at the arterial end is reabsorbed at the venous end, while the remaining ~10% enters lymphatic capillaries to become lymph. Lymph flows one way through lymphatic vessels and finally returns to the bloodstream via the thoracic duct and right lymphatic duct into the subclavian veins, maintaining a dynamic balance between tissue-fluid production and reabsorption and preventing edema. Lymphatics also transport dietary fats (chyle) from the gut and circulate immune cells.
