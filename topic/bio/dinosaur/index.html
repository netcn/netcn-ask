非鸟恐龙是如何灭绝的？

How did non-avian dinosaurs go extinct?

约6600万年前一颗小行星撞击墨西哥尤卡坦，引发全球降温、火灾与黑暗（白垩纪—古近纪界线），导致非鸟恐龙及许多物种灭绝，为哺乳动物腾出生态位。
About 66 million years ago an asteroid hit the Yucatán, causing global cooling, fires, and darkness (the K-Pg boundary), wiping out non-avian dinosaurs and many species, opening niches for mammals.

恐龙是冷血动物还是温血动物？

Were dinosaurs cold-blooded or warm-blooded?

越来越多证据表明许多恐龙（尤其兽脚类）有较高代谢率、羽毛和快速生长线，更像温血动物；但不同类群可能介于两者之间的中间状态。
Growing evidence—high metabolism, feathers, fast growth rings—suggests many dinosaurs, especially theropods, were more warm-blooded; different groups may have spanned a middle range.

霸王龙主要依靠什么感官捕猎？

What senses did Tyrannosaurus rex mainly rely on to hunt?

化石显示霸王龙有巨大眼眶与发达内耳，嗅觉脑区也很大，说明它兼具敏锐视觉、听觉与嗅觉，是综合型掠食者。
Fossils show T. rex had large eye sockets, a developed inner ear, and a big olfactory brain region, implying keen vision, hearing, and smell—an all-around predator.

为什么说鸟类是恐龙的后代？

Why are birds said to be descendants of dinosaurs?

化石和DNA分析显示鸟类属于兽脚类恐龙一支，与迅猛龙等近缘，保留空心骨、叉骨等特征，可视为“活着的恐龙”。
Fossils and DNA show birds belong to the theropod dinosaur branch, close to Velociraptor, retaining hollow bones and a furcula—living descendants of dinosaurs.

羽毛化石为恐龙与鸟类的联系提供了什么证据？

What evidence do feathered fossils give for the dinosaur-bird link?

中国等地发现的带羽恐龙化石显示羽毛先用于保温或展示，后才用于飞行，说明鸟类飞行结构是从恐龙已有羽毛逐步演化而来。
Feathered dinosaur fossils from places like China show feathers first for insulation or display, later for flight, indicating avian flight structures evolved gradually from dinosaur feathers.
