细胞为什么常用“磷酸化”来开关信号？

Why do cells commonly use phosphorylation to switch signals on and off?

激酶把磷酸基加到目标蛋白上，可改变其形状与活性，从而快速、可逆地开关信号；磷酸酶则去掉磷酸基使其关闭。这种可逆修饰让信号既灵敏又可控。
Kinases add phosphate groups to target proteins, changing their shape and activity to switch signals on; phosphatases remove the phosphate to switch them off. This reversible modification makes signaling both fast and controllable.

G蛋白偶联受体（GPCR）如何把信号传进细胞？

How do G-protein-coupled receptors (GPCRs) relay signals into the cell?

GPCR被配体结合后，会激活细胞膜内侧的G蛋白，G蛋白再激活或抑制下游效应酶（如腺苷酸环化酶），从而放大并传递信号。约三分之一的西药以GPCR为靶点。
When bound by a ligand, a GPCR activates an intracellular G protein, which then turns downstream effector enzymes (like adenylyl cyclase) on or off, amplifying and passing the signal. Roughly a third of medicines target GPCRs.

受体酪氨酸激酶（RTK）和生长因子有什么关系？

What is the link between receptor tyrosine kinases (RTKs) and growth factors?

生长因子（如EGF）结合RTK后，会使受体二聚并自身磷酸化，招募下游信号分子，促进细胞增殖。RTK过度激活与多种癌症有关。
Growth factors such as EGF bind RTKs, causing them to dimerize and autophosphorylate, which recruits downstream molecules and drives cell proliferation. Overactive RTKs are linked to many cancers.

细胞内的“第二信使”是什么？请以cAMP为例说明。

What are intracellular 'second messengers,' and what is cAMP an example of?

第二信使是细胞内的小分子，负责把细胞膜收到的信号放大并扩散到细胞内部。cAMP就是典型的一种，它由ATP在腺苷酸环化酶作用下生成，进而激活蛋白激酶A。
Second messengers are small intracellular molecules that amplify and spread a signal received at the membrane. cAMP is a classic one, made from ATP by adenylyl cyclase, and it then activates protein kinase A.

什么是细胞信号通路？

What is a cell signaling pathway?

细胞信号通路是细胞接收外界信号（如激素、生长因子）后，通过一系列蛋白质依次激活，把信息从细胞膜传到细胞核、引发特定反应的链条。
A signaling pathway is a chain in which a cell receives an external signal (e.g. a hormone or growth factor) and relays it through a series of proteins to the nucleus, triggering a specific response.
