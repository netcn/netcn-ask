什么是冬眠？

What is hibernation?

冬眠不是普通的睡觉，而是被主动调低的生理状态：体温设定点下调，心率、呼吸和代谢大幅减速，体温能落到接近环境温度，烧的是入秋前囤下的脂肪。它也不是一觉到春天，中间会周期性短暂醒转，每次醒转都要花掉可观的能量。容易混的是那些每天只降代谢一小段的小型动物，机制相同但持续时间短，一般不叫冬眠。
Hibernation is not ordinary sleep but a deliberately lowered physiological state: the body's temperature set point drops, heart rate, breathing and metabolism slow sharply, body temperature can fall close to ambient, and the fuel is fat stored before autumn. Nor is it one unbroken sleep until spring—animals briefly arouse now and then, and each arousal burns a substantial amount of energy. The confusing neighbor is the small animal that cuts its metabolism for a short spell each day: same mechanism, much shorter bout, so it is not usually called hibernation.

候鸟迁徙来回上万公里，它们靠什么认路？

Migratory birds travel tens of thousands of kilometers round trip. How do they navigate?

候鸟是“多传感器导航系统”。白天它们用太阳的位置和体内生物钟推算方向，夜晚用星座（尤其是北极星附近）导航，还能感知地磁场：眼睛中的隐花色素蛋白可能通过自由基对机制把磁场方向转化为视觉信号，头部、鼻腔也有磁感应结构。此外河流、山脉、海岸线等地理标志和自身记忆也会参与。不同物种侧重不同线索，幼鸟第一年常靠遗传的本能方向飞行，随后在经验中逐步完善路线。
Migratory birds are multi-sensor navigation systems. By day they steer by the sun's position and their internal clock; by night they use star patterns, especially around Polaris; and they sense Earth's magnetic field, with cryptochrome proteins in the eye possibly converting field direction into a visual signal via a radical-pair mechanism, while structures in the head and nasal area also respond to magnetism. Landmarks such as rivers, mountains and coastlines plus learned memory join in. Different species rely on different cues; first-year birds often fly an inherited compass bearing and refine the route through experience.

章鱼、鱿鱼等头足类动物是如何在不到一秒内改变体色伪装的？

How do cephalopods such as octopus and squid change color and camouflage in under a second?

头足类皮肤布满色素细胞、虹彩细胞和乳头状突起，由神经系统直接控制，可在不到一秒内扩张或收缩色素囊、改变反光与纹理以匹配背景。它们甚至能透过皮肤感光，实现极快的伪装。
Cephalopod skin is packed with chromatophores, iridophores, and papillae controlled directly by the nervous system, letting them expand or contract pigment sacs and alter reflection and texture in under a second to match the background. They can even sense light through the skin, enabling remarkably fast camouflage.

什么是共生关系？举例说明。

What is symbiosis? Give an example.

共生指两个不同物种长期而密切地生活在一起。小丑鱼与海葵是典型：海葵触手的刺细胞一般蜇不到它，通常归因于鱼体表黏液的保护，鱼因此躲开天敌，它的残渣与排泄物又为海葵补上氮素。要分清共生并不等于互利，按双方利弊它分为互利、共栖与寄生，寄生同样算共生；一对关系的性质还会随环境在互利与寄生之间滑动，判断要看双方存活与繁殖是否真的都受益。
Symbiosis is a long-term, close association between members of two different species. Clownfish and sea anemones are typical: the stinging cells of the anemone's tentacles generally fail to fire on the clownfish, usually credited to the protective mucus on its skin, so the fish escapes predators, while its scraps and wastes supply the anemone with nitrogen. Note that symbiosis is not the same as mutual benefit - sorted by who gains and who loses it covers mutualism, commensalism and parasitism, and parasitism is still symbiosis; and a given relationship can slide between mutualism and parasitism as conditions change, so the test is whether survival and reproduction really improve for both.

帝王蝶如何跨越数千公里、准确飞到同一片越冬地？

How do monarch butterflies migrate thousands of kilometers to the same overwintering site?

帝王蝶凭借"太阳罗盘"（按太阳位置结合体内生物钟校正方向）和感光的磁罗盘（隐花色素感知地磁场）双重导航，跨代接力飞行数千公里，仍准确抵达墨西哥同一片冷杉林越冬。这是昆虫导航的奇迹。
Monarch butterflies navigate with a time-compensated sun compass plus a light-sensitive magnetic compass (based on cryptochrome) that senses Earth's field, letting them migrate thousands of kilometers across generations to the very same fir forests in Mexico. It is a marvel of insect navigation.

节肢动物的外骨骼限制了生长，它们是如何通过激素调控完成蜕皮的？

The arthropod exoskeleton constrains growth, so how is molting controlled by hormones?

节肢动物必须周期性蜕皮才能长大：脑神经分泌细胞分泌促前胸腺激素（PTTH），刺激前胸腺合成并释放蜕皮激素（蜕皮酮ecdysone），启动旧皮溶解、新皮形成和蜕出。蜕皮结果由保幼激素（JH）决定：JH水平高时蜕皮后仍保持幼虫形态，JH下降后蜕皮进入蛹期，最终在JH近乎消失时羽化为成虫。
Arthropods must molt periodically to grow. Neurosecretory cells of the brain release prothoracicotropic hormone (PTTH), which stimulates the prothoracic gland to produce and release the molting hormone ecdysone, triggering dissolution of the old cuticle and formation of a new one. The outcome of each molt is set by juvenile hormone (JH): high JH keeps the animal in a larval form after molting, declining JH allows molting into the pupa, and when JH is nearly absent the adult emerges.

沙丁鱼群为什么会像“一个整体”一样突然集体转向？

Why does a school of sardines suddenly turn all at once, moving like a single organism?

鱼群没有指挥官，转向是“自组织”涌现出的集体行为。每条鱼只遵守三条局部规则：向身边最近的伙伴靠拢、与太近的鱼保持距离、向身边多数同伴的方向对齐，并通过侧线感知邻居的水流，把信息以约每30毫秒一次的速度在鱼群中传递。任何一条鱼察觉危险先转向，动作沿“信息波”传播，整个鱼群就能在不到一秒内完成集体转向。这种同步让捕食者面对一片模糊的“超级个体”，难以锁定单个目标。
The school has no leader; the turn is an emergent, self-organized behavior. Each fish follows three local rules: stay near neighbors, keep a minimum distance, and align with the heading of nearby fish, while its lateral line senses neighbors' water movements, passing information through the group about every 30 milliseconds. When any fish detects danger and turns, the movement spreads as an information wave and the whole school completes a synchronized turn in under a second. This synchrony makes predators face a blurred superorganism and makes it hard to single out any individual.

帝企鹅爸爸在漫长极夜里孵蛋，是怎么做到几个月不吃东西的？

How do male emperor penguins survive months without food while incubating eggs through the polar night?

雌企鹅产蛋后就回到海里捕食，孵蛋任务全部落在雄企鹅身上。雄企鹅在极夜中把蛋放在脚背上、用育儿袋盖住，两个多月不吃不喝，靠夏秋两季积累的厚厚脂肪维持生命。它们上千只挤在一起轮流换到群体中心，利用同伴的体温挡住零下几十摄氏度的寒风，并把代谢率降到最低来省能量。雌企鹅归来时，雄企鹅常常已瘦掉近一半体重。
After the female lays, she heads back to the sea to feed, leaving the male to incubate. Through the polar night he balances the egg on his feet under a brood pouch, fasting for more than two months on the fat stored during summer and autumn. Thousands huddle together, taking turns at the warm center, sheltering from winds of tens of degrees below zero, and lowering metabolism to save energy. By the time the female returns, the male has often lost nearly half his body weight.

鱼类体侧的侧线系统有什么功能？

What is the function of the lateral line running along a fish's body?

侧线是鱼类和水生两栖类特有的感觉器官，由沿体侧分布的机械感觉单元（神经丘）组成，能感受水流方向、流速、水压变化和低频振动，相当于远距离触觉。它帮助鱼类躲避障碍和捕食者、发现猎物、感知同伴动向，并辅助群体游动。
The lateral line is a sensory organ unique to fish and aquatic amphibians, composed of mechanosensory units called neuromasts arranged along the body. It detects water currents, pressure changes, and low-frequency vibrations, functioning like touch at a distance. It helps fish avoid obstacles and predators, locate prey, sense companions, and coordinate schooling.

为什么猫的眼睛在夜里会发亮？

Why do cats' eyes glow in the dark?

Young and weak birds often sit in the most favorable spots, letting the whole flock fly farther.

Why do squirrels hide nuts in autumn?

松鼠在秋天把果子埋起来存着，因为冬天找不到食物。等饿了再挖出来吃，就能安稳过冬。
Squirrels bury nuts in autumn because food is hard to find in winter. They dig them up later when hungry, which helps them survive the cold season.

电鳗是怎么发出电击的？

How does an electric eel produce its electric shock?

电鳗尾巴里有特化的发电细胞（电细胞，由肌肉演化而来），像电池一样排列；受到神经触发时它们同步翻转离子差，三块电器官串联可产生超过600伏的电击。电鳗用它来捕食、防御和在水中"定位"。
An electric eel has specialized electrocytes (derived from muscle) in its tail arranged like batteries; when nerves trigger them they simultaneously flip ion gradients, and three electric organs in series can produce shocks over 600 volts. The eel uses this for hunting, defense, and electrolocation.

树懒为什么动作那么慢？

Why do sloths move so slowly?

树懒吃树叶，树叶没什么营养，所以它动作慢来省力气。慢吞吞的它还能躲开爱吃肉的动物。
Sloths eat leaves that give little energy, so they move slowly to save strength. Moving slowly also helps them hide from animals that might eat them.

鲸鱼为什么会“唱歌”？

Why do whales 'sing'?

鲸鱼发出又低又长的声音，可以在很远的海里互相打招呼。科学家认为它们用歌声找朋友和告诉同伴自己在哪里。
Whales make low, long sounds that travel far underwater to greet each other. Scientists think the songs help them find friends and tell companions where they are.

蓝鲸到底有多大？

Just how big is a blue whale?

蓝鲸是地球上最大的动物，体长可超过30米，心脏就有一辆小汽车那么大。它虽然庞大，却只吃小小的磷虾。
The blue whale is the largest animal on Earth, reaching over 30 meters long, with a heart the size of a small car. Even so, it eats only tiny shrimp called krill.

熊冬眠时身体会发生什么变化？

What happens to a bear's body during hibernation?

冬眠时熊的心跳和体温下降，几乎不吃喝也不上厕所，靠夏天攒下的脂肪过活。开春后它醒来，已经瘦了一大圈。
During hibernation a bear's heartbeat and body temperature drop, and it barely eats, drinks, or moves, living on fat stored in summer. When spring comes it wakes up much thinner.

蜘蛛是昆虫吗？

Is a spider an insect?

不是。蜘蛛有8条腿、身体分两段，属于蛛形纲；昆虫有6条腿、身体分三段。
No. Spiders have 8 legs and two body parts (arachnids); insects have 6 legs and three body parts.
