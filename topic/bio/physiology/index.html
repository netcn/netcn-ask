肾素-血管紧张素系统怎样调节血压？

How does the renin-angiotensin system regulate blood pressure?

肾灌注下降时肾脏释放肾素，把血管紧张素原切成血管紧张素 I，再经血管紧张素转化酶（ACE）生成强效缩血管的角 II，升高血压并保钠保水。Guyton 与 Hall 把它列为长期血压调控的核心回路。
Low renal perfusion makes the kidney release renin, cleaving angiotensinogen to angiotensin I, then ACE forms vasoconstrictor angiotensin II, raising pressure and conserving salt and water. Guyton & Hall rank this as the core long-term pressure loop.

人类如何仅凭约400种嗅觉受体识别成千上万种气味？

How can humans discriminate thousands of odors with only about 400 olfactory receptors?

嗅觉受体由人类基因组中最大的基因家族之一编码（约400个功能基因），每个嗅觉感觉神经元只表达一种受体，气味分子与受体结合后通过G蛋白激活cAMP信号通路，使神经元发放动作电位。表达同一种受体的神经元把轴突汇聚到嗅球中特定的嗅小球，形成一受体一小球的拓扑图谱。不同气味激活不同受体组合，大脑以组合编码的方式识别海量气味。此外嗅觉神经元是少数能终身再生的感觉神经元，并具有敏锐的浓度分辨能力。
Olfactory receptors are encoded by one of the largest gene families in the human genome (about 400 functional genes). Each olfactory sensory neuron expresses only one receptor; odorant binding activates the G-protein-cAMP pathway and generates action potentials. Neurons expressing the same receptor project their axons to a specific glomerulus in the olfactory bulb, producing a one-receptor-one-glomerulus topographic map. Different odorants activate different combinations of receptors, and the brain decodes countless odors by combinatorial coding. Olfactory neurons are among the few sensory neurons that regenerate throughout life, and the system can resolve small concentration differences.

激素是怎么“传递命令”的？为什么胰岛素出了问题会得糖尿病？

How do hormones "deliver orders," and why does a problem with insulin cause diabetes?

激素是由内分泌腺分泌、经血液运输到全身的化学信使，只有带对应受体的靶细胞才能响应。胰岛素属于肽类激素，与细胞膜受体结合后触发细胞内信号链，促使肌肉和脂肪细胞把葡萄糖转运入胞，从而降低血糖。1型糖尿病是免疫系统破坏胰岛B细胞，胰岛素几乎不能分泌；2型糖尿病则是受体对胰岛素不敏感，细胞“听不见命令”。两者都让葡萄糖滞留在血液里，长期高血糖会损伤血管、神经和肾脏。
Hormones are chemical messengers secreted by endocrine glands and carried through the blood; only target cells bearing the matching receptor can respond. Insulin is a peptide hormone: it binds a membrane receptor and triggers an intracellular signaling chain that makes muscle and fat cells transport glucose inside, lowering blood glucose. Type 1 diabetes arises when the immune system destroys the pancreatic beta cells, leaving almost no insulin; type 2 diabetes is insulin resistance, where cells fail to hear the order. In both cases glucose stays in the blood, and chronic high glucose damages blood vessels, nerves and kidneys.

How does the baroreceptor reflex rapidly stabilize blood pressure?

Baroreceptors in the carotid sinus and aortic arch sense pressure changes and signal the medulla; rising pressure slows heart rate and dilates vessels, while falling pressure does the reverse, restoring pressure within seconds.

肾脏是怎么把血液里的“垃圾”滤出来，又把有用的东西留下来的？

How does the kidney filter waste out of the blood while keeping the useful molecules?

肾脏的基本单位是肾单位，分三步工作。第一，肾小球滤过：血压把血液里的水、小分子（葡萄糖、氨基酸、无机盐、尿素）连同废物压过滤过膜，血细胞和大分子蛋白质被挡住留在血管里。第二，肾小管重吸收：管壁把几乎所有葡萄糖和氨基酸主动运回血液，按身体需要回收大部分水分和无机盐。第三，肾小管分泌：多余的离子、氢离子、药物被主动排进管腔。抗利尿激素和醛固酮根据身体水平衡调整水的重吸收量，最终形成的尿液就是浓缩后的多余水和废物。
The kidney's working unit is the nephron, which does three jobs. First, glomerular filtration: blood pressure pushes water and small molecules, including glucose, amino acids, salts and urea, through the filter membrane, while blood cells and large proteins are held back. Second, tubular reabsorption: the tubule wall actively hauls almost all glucose and amino acids back into the blood and reclaims most water and salts according to the body's needs. Third, tubular secretion: excess ions, hydrogen ions and drugs are actively pumped into the tubule. The hormones ADH and aldosterone tune how much water is reabsorbed, and the final urine is simply the surplus water and waste concentrated together.

什么是内环境稳态？

What is homeostasis?

内环境稳态指机体通过调节机制把体温、血糖、pH等内部条件维持在相对稳定范围的能力。例如出汗降温、胰岛素调节血糖都是稳态表现。稳态失衡往往意味着疾病。
Homeostasis is the body's ability to keep internal conditions such as temperature, blood sugar, and pH within a stable range through regulatory mechanisms. Sweating to cool down and insulin adjusting blood sugar are examples. Loss of homeostasis often signals disease.

什么是血液循环的双循环？

What is double circulation in the body?

人类等哺乳动物拥有肺循环和体循环两套回路：肺循环把缺氧血送到肺换气，体循环把含氧血送往全身。心脏的四个腔室把这两套血流完全分开，提高了供氧效率。这是温血动物维持高代谢的基础。
Mammals like humans have two circuits: pulmonary circulation sends oxygen-poor blood to the lungs, and systemic circulation delivers oxygen-rich blood to the whole body. The heart's four chambers keep these two flows separate, raising oxygen-delivery efficiency. This underpins the high metabolism of warm-blooded animals.

肾脏髓质里的"逆流倍增"是如何把尿液浓缩的？

How does the countercurrent multiplier in the kidney medulla concentrate urine?

肾髓质中，亨勒袢与集合管平行逆向流动形成"逆流倍增"：髓质间质由外向内盐浓度递增，集合管在抗利尿激素作用下对水通透，水被高渗间质抽走，从而浓缩尿液、减少水分丢失。
In the renal medulla, the loop of Henle and collecting ducts run in parallel counterflow to create a "countercurrent multiplier": the interstitial salt gradient rises from outer to inner medulla, and under antidiuretic hormone the collecting duct becomes water-permeable, letting water be pulled out to concentrate urine and conserve water.

How does hormonal feedback regulation maintain homeostasis?

多数内分泌轴采用负反馈：当某激素水平过高，会抑制上游腺体（如下丘脑、垂体）继续分泌，使其回落。例如甲状腺激素过多即抑制促甲状腺激素释放，从而把各项指标稳定在正常范围。
Most endocrine axes use negative feedback: when a hormone rises too high, it inhibits upstream glands such as the hypothalamus or pituitary, bringing levels back down. Excess thyroid hormone, for example, suppresses releasing signals so values stay within a normal range.

长时程增强（LTP）为什么被认为是学习记忆的细胞基础？

Why is long-term potentiation (LTP) considered a cellular basis of learning and memory?

长时程增强指高频刺激突触后，该突触的传递效率持续数小时至数天的增强，最先在海马被Bliss和Lømo（1973）发现。其机制：突触前释放谷氨酸，激活突触后AMPA受体使膜去极化，Mg2+脱离NMDA受体，Ca2+大量流入突触后细胞，激活CaMKII等激酶，磷酸化并插入更多AMPA受体（早期LTP），并通过激活CREB等转录因子促进新蛋白合成、形成新突触（晚期LTP），实现持久增强。LTP的诱导和维持依赖海马，与空间记忆（如莫里斯水迷宫）和行为记忆高度相关，是记忆存储于突触连接改变这一假说的核心证据。
LTP is a long-lasting (hours to days) increase in synaptic strength following high-frequency stimulation, first described in the hippocampus by Bliss and Lømo (1973). Mechanistically, presynaptic glutamate activates postsynaptic AMPA receptors, depolarizing the membrane; Mg2+ leaves the NMDA receptor, allowing Ca2+ to flood the postsynaptic cell and activate kinases such as CaMKII, which phosphorylates and inserts more AMPA receptors (early LTP). Late LTP involves CREB-driven gene transcription, protein synthesis, and formation of new synapses, making the potentiation persistent. LTP induction requires the hippocampus and correlates with spatial memory (e.g., the Morris water maze) and behavioral memory, providing key evidence that memories are stored in changes of synaptic connections.

钠钾泵对神经细胞有什么重要性？

Why is the sodium-potassium pump important for nerve cells?

钠钾泵每消耗一个 ATP，就把三个钠离子泵出细胞、两个钾离子泵入，维持细胞膜内外约 -70 mV 的静息电位。这种离子梯度是神经元产生动作电位、传递信号的前提，也是许多物质继发性主动运输的能量来源。
