蚂蚁为什么总是排成一条线行走?它们是怎么认路的?

Why do ants always march in single file, and how do they find their way back home?

许多蚁类会沿路分泌信息素留下气味轨迹,同伴循着这条化学路径往返于巢与食物之间,走的人越多痕迹越浓,队伍也就越直越整齐。此外它们还能利用太阳位置和步数计数来估计距离。一旦信息素被雨水冲散或被其他气味覆盖,队列就会乱成一团。
Many ant species lay pheromone trails as they walk; nestmates follow this chemical path between nest and food, and the more ants use it, the stronger the trail and the straighter the column. They also navigate by the sun's position and count steps to gauge distance. When rain washes the trail away or other odors mask it, the orderly line collapses.

昆虫靠马氏管排泄,为什么这套系统让它们能在极端干旱的环境里活下来?

Insects excrete via Malpighian tubules; why does this system let them survive extremely arid environments?

马氏管把含氮废物主要以尿酸的形式排入后肠,尿酸难溶于水且毒性低,随废物带走的水分极少。后肠的直肠垫还能主动回收离子和水,使最终排出的粪便几乎呈干燥状态。再加上不透水表皮和气门开闭的精细调控,昆虫把失水量压到了陆地动物的最低水平,这是它们成功占领陆地生境的关键适应之一。
Malpighian tubules pass nitrogenous waste into the hindgut mainly as uric acid, which is poorly soluble, minimally toxic, and carries away almost no water. Rectal pads in the hindgut then actively reclaim ions and water, leaving the final feces nearly dry. Combined with a water-proof cuticle and fine control of spiracle opening, insects push water loss down to among the lowest of any land animal, a key adaptation behind their conquest of terrestrial habitats.

蚁群里没有指挥官,成千上万只蚂蚁凭什么协作修筑结构精巧的巢穴?

With no commander in charge, how do thousands of ants coordinate to build intricately structured nests?

蚁群依赖被称为共识主动性(stigmergy)的自组织机制:每只工蚁只执行局部规则,比如沿信息素浓度梯度行动、把挖出的土粒堆放在同伴触碰频率最高的位置。个体留下的痕迹反过来引导其他个体,构成不断放大的正反馈回路。大量简单规则叠加之后,通风良好、温湿度稳定的巢穴结构自然涌现,任何一只蚂蚁都不需要了解整体设计图。
Ant colonies rely on a self-organizing mechanism called stigmergy: each worker obeys purely local rules, such as moving along pheromone gradients and dropping excavated soil where encounters with nestmates are most frequent. Traces left by individuals steer the behavior of others, forming self-amplifying positive-feedback loops. Out of many simple rules emerges a nest with effective ventilation and stable temperature and humidity, even though no single ant ever holds the overall blueprint.
