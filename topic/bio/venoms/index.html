为什么说芋螺毒素是药物开发的宝库？请从芋螺肽的结构与靶点特异性角度解释。

Why are cone snail toxins described as a treasure trove for drug development? Answer in terms of conotoxin structure and target specificity.

芋螺毒液含数十至上百种小肽，各组分分子小、二硫键密集、构象刚性强，能以极高的亲和力专一识别某种离子通道或受体亚型，例如ω-芋螺毒素MVIIA可选择性阻断N型钙通道。这种精确性使它们既能作神经研究的分子探针，也是理想的药物先导物——源自纹芋螺的镇痛药齐考诺肽已于2004年获批上市。
Cone snail venom holds dozens to over a hundred small peptides. Each conotoxin is tiny, densely cross-linked by disulfide bridges and conformationally rigid, letting it bind one particular ion-channel or receptor subtype with very high affinity: omega-conotoxin MVIIA, for instance, selectively blocks N-type calcium channels. Such precision makes them valuable molecular probes and ideal drug leads; the painkiller ziconotide, derived from Conus magus venom, was approved in 2004.

毒蛇毒液如何催生经典的降压药？请以巴西具窍蝮蛇（Bothrops jararaca）毒液中的缓激肽增强肽为例，说明卡托普利作为首个口服血管紧张素转化酶（ACE）抑制剂的设计由来及其降压机制。

How did viper venom give rise to a landmark antihypertensive? Using the bradykinin-potentiating peptides from the venom of Bothrops jararaca as an example, explain how captopril was developed as the first oral angiotensin-converting enzyme (ACE) inhibitor and describe its mechanism of lowering blood pressure.

巴西具窍蝮蛇毒液中的缓激肽增强肽可抑制血管紧张素转化酶，延缓扩血管的缓激肽降解。施贵宝的Cushman与Ondetti据此设计出首个口服ACE抑制剂卡托普利（1981年获批）：它阻断血管紧张素Ⅰ向强缩血管的Ⅱ转变，并减少醛固酮释放而降压。毒液为制伏猎物演化出的高选择性酶抑制分子，与人体同源通路契合，是理想的药物先导物。
Bradykinin-potentiating peptides in Bothrops jararaca venom inhibit angiotensin-converting enzyme, prolonging the action of the vasodilator bradykinin. Building on this, Cushman and Ondetti at Squibb designed captopril, approved in 1981 as the first oral ACE inhibitor: it blocks the conversion of angiotensin I into strongly vasoconstricting angiotensin II and reduces aldosterone release, lowering blood pressure. Venoms evolve highly selective enzyme inhibitors to subdue prey; these often fit homologous human regulatory pathways, making them ideal drug leads.

被银环蛇这类眼镜蛇科毒蛇咬伤后，伤者为何会出现弛缓性瘫痪乃至呼吸衰竭？

After a bite from elapid snakes like the many-banded krait, why do victims suffer flaccid paralysis and even respiratory failure?

眼镜蛇科毒液富含三指折叠的α-神经毒素，如α-银环蛇毒素。它高亲和力占据神经肌肉接头烟碱型乙酰胆碱受体上的乙酰胆碱位点，却不开放离子通道，运动兴奋无法传给肌肉，出现弛缓性麻痹；膈肌瘫痪即致呼吸衰竭。许多种类还携突触前β-毒素（磷脂酶A2类）破坏神经末梢、阻断递质释放，前后膜双重夹击构成致死机制。
Elapid venoms are rich in three-finger alpha-neurotoxins such as alpha-bungarotoxin. With very high affinity these occupy the acetylcholine site of nicotinic receptors at the neuromuscular junction without opening the ion channel, so motor excitation never reaches the muscle and flaccid paralysis sets in; paralysis of the diaphragm causes respiratory failure. Many elapids also carry presynaptic beta-toxins of the phospholipase A2 family that damage nerve terminals and block transmitter release, attacking both sides of the synapse.
