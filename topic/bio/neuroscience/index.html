动作电位是如何产生和传导的？

How is an action potential generated and conducted?

当神经元受到足够刺激，电压门控钠通道开放使 Na+ 内流、膜快速去极化；随后钾通道开放使 K+ 外流复极，形成一次尖峰。动作电位沿轴突不衰减地传导，其「全或无」特性保证信号可靠传递。
When a neuron is sufficiently stimulated, voltage-gated sodium channels open and Na+ influx rapidly depolarizes the membrane; then potassium channels open and K+ efflux repolarizes it, producing a spike. The action potential travels along the axon without decay, and its all-or-none nature ensures reliable signaling.

Do the left and right brain really have different jobs?

左右脑确有大致侧重：左脑多管语言逻辑，右脑多管空间与面孔识别，但多数任务由两侧协同完成。
The hemispheres do lean differently—left often handles language and logic, right handles space and faces—but most tasks use both together.

血脑屏障有什么作用，它是如何构成的？

What does the blood-brain barrier do and how is it built?

血脑屏障由脑毛细血管内皮细胞间紧密连接及周细胞、星形胶质细胞共同构成，限制大多数大分子、病原体和药物进入脑组织。它保护神经元免受血液波动和毒素干扰，但也给中枢神经药物递送带来挑战。
The blood-brain barrier is formed by tight junctions between brain capillary endothelial cells together with pericytes and astrocyte end-feet, restricting most large molecules, pathogens, and drugs from entering brain tissue. It shields neurons from blood-borne fluctuations and toxins but challenges drug delivery to the CNS.

什么是脑机接口？

What is a brain-computer interface?

脑机接口是在大脑与外部设备之间建立直接通信的技术，可读取神经信号或向脑回送刺激。它帮助瘫痪者用意念控制机械臂，也为治疗神经疾病提供新途径。该领域正快速从实验室走向临床。
A brain-computer interface builds a direct communication link between the brain and an external device, reading neural signals or sending stimulation back. It lets paralyzed people control a robotic arm by thought and offers new routes to treat neurological disease. The field is moving quickly from lab to clinic.

为什么说中脑多巴胺神经元发放的是奖赏预测误差而非奖赏本身,它如何充当学习的教学信号?

Why do midbrain dopamine neurons fire for reward prediction error rather than reward itself, and how does that serve as a teaching signal for learning?

多巴胺放电量反映实际奖赏减去预期的差值,而非愉悦本身。Schultz在清醒猴的黑质致密部与腹侧被盖区记录:意外获得果汁时多巴胺成簇发放;反复训练后放电前移到预测线索出现的一刻,奖赏如期而至反而不再响应;预期落空时放电跌破基线。三种模式正好实现Rescorla-Wagner学习规则,多巴胺由此作为教学信号驱动突触可塑性与策略更新。
Dopamine firing scales with actual reward minus expectation, not with pleasure itself. Recording in the substantia nigra pars compacta and ventral tegmental area of alert monkeys, Schultz saw bursts to unpredicted juice; after training, activity shifted to the predictive cue and vanished when reward arrived as expected, while omitted rewards drove firing below baseline. These three patterns implement the Rescorla-Wagner rule, letting dopamine act as a teaching signal that drives synaptic plasticity and policy updating.

长时程增强与记忆有什么关系？

What is the link between long-term potentiation and memory?

长时程增强指反复协同的电活动使突触传递持续增强，被认为是学习与记忆的细胞基础。Bliss 与 Lømo 在 1973 年于海马首次记录到这一现象，Kandel 后续用海兔证明其可塑性机制。
Long-term potentiation is a lasting strengthening of synaptic transmission after repeated coincident activity, taken as the cellular basis of learning and memory. Bliss and Lømo first recorded it in the hippocampus in 1973; Kandel later proved the plasticity mechanism in Aplysia.

中枢神经系统和周围神经系统有什么区别？

What is the difference between the central and peripheral nervous systems?

中枢神经系统由脑和脊髓组成，是处理和决策的中心；周围神经系统包括脑神经、脊神经及自主神经，负责把信息在身体与中枢之间来回传递。二者配合，让机体既能感知环境又能作出反应。
The central nervous system consists of the brain and spinal cord and is the center for processing and decision-making; the peripheral nervous system, cranial and spinal nerves plus the autonomic nerves, carries information between the body and the center. Together they let the body sense and respond to its environment.

视网膜怎样把光变成神经信号？

How does the retina convert light into nerve signals?

视杆与视锥中的视色素吸收光子后触发光转导级联，关闭 Na⁺ 通道、使感光细胞超极化，再经双极与神经节细胞编码成动作电位传向大脑。Wald 在 1935 年阐明视黄醛循环并因此获 1967 年诺奖。
Rod and cone pigments absorb photons and trigger a transduction cascade that closes Na⁺ channels, hyperpolarizing the photoreceptor, then bipolar and ganglion cells encode spikes to the brain. Wald clarified the retinal cycle in 1935 (Nobel 1967).

什么是突触可塑性？

What is synaptic plasticity?

突触可塑性指神经元之间连接强度可随活动和经验而改变的能力。它被认为是学习和记忆的生理基础，常用长时程增强来描述。反复练习会让相关神经回路更高效。
Synaptic plasticity is the ability of connections between neurons to change strength with activity and experience. It is considered the physiological basis of learning and memory, often described as long-term potentiation. Repeated practice makes the relevant circuits more efficient.

What role do neurotransmitters play in the brain?

什么是反射，它为什么快？

What is a reflex, and why is it fast?

反射是机体对刺激做出的快速、不随意的固定反应，如手碰到烫物立刻缩回。它走「反射弧」：感受器→传入神经→脊髓中枢→传出神经→效应器，常在脊髓就完成，不必先经大脑，所以又快又省。
A reflex is a rapid, involuntary, fixed response to a stimulus, like pulling a hand from something hot. It follows a reflex arc, receptor, sensory neuron, spinal cord center, motor neuron, effector, often completed in the spinal cord without first involving the brain, which makes it fast and efficient.

神经元和突触是如何传递信息的？

How do neurons and synapses transmit information?

神经元通过电信号（动作电位）沿轴突传导，到达神经末梢时释放化学神经递质越过突触间隙，作用于下一个神经元。这种“电—化学—电”的转换使神经信号在脑中快速传递。
A neuron carries signals as electrical action potentials along its axon; at the terminal it releases chemical neurotransmitters across the synaptic gap to act on the next neuron. This “electrical–chemical–electrical” handoff lets signals race through the brain.

Where is memory stored?

包裹在轴突外的髓鞘为什么能让神经冲动的传导速度大大加快？

Why does the myelin sheath wrapped around an axon greatly speed up nerve impulse conduction?

髓鞘由施万细胞或少突胶质细胞反复缠绕轴突构成，是高脂低电容的绝缘层，可大幅减少跨膜漏电与离子流动。动作电位只在两段髓鞘间裸露的郎飞结重新触发，因为电压门控钠通道集中分布于结区；局部电流遂沿轴突内部快速跃迁至下一结点。跳跃式传导将速度提高数十倍，并节省重建离子梯度的能量。多发性硬化的传导缺陷正源于髓鞘受损。
Myelin, wound around axons by Schwann cells or oligodendrocytes, is a fatty, low-capacitance insulator that sharply reduces transmembrane current leakage. Action potentials are re-triggered only at the bare nodes of Ranvier between myelin segments, where voltage-gated sodium channels cluster, so local currents leap rapidly down the axon interior to the next node. This saltatory conduction raises speed tens of times over and conserves the energy spent rebuilding ion gradients. The conduction defects of multiple sclerosis stem from damaged myelin.

神经冲动到达突触末端后，信号是怎样跨过两个神经元之间约20纳米的间隙传给下一个细胞的？

When a nerve impulse reaches a synaptic terminal, how does the signal cross the roughly 20-nanometer gap to the next cell?

冲动到达末梢引起去极化，电压门控钙通道开放，钙离子内流成为触发信号。囊泡上的SNARE复合体在钙感应蛋白协助下把突触囊泡锚定并融入突触前膜，以胞吐释放神经递质；递质扩散过间隙，与突触后受体结合并开启离子通道，产生分级电位。只有突触前末梢能释放递质，传递天然单向，并带来约半毫秒的突触延搁。这是神经系统信息传递的基本机制。
The arriving impulse depolarizes the terminal, opening voltage-gated calcium channels so that calcium influx acts as the trigger. With help from calcium sensors, SNARE complexes on vesicles dock and fuse the synaptic vesicle with the presynaptic membrane, releasing neurotransmitter by exocytosis; the transmitter diffuses across the cleft and binds postsynaptic receptors, opening ion channels that generate graded potentials. Because only the presynaptic terminal releases transmitter, transmission is inherently one-way and carries a delay of about half a millisecond—the universal mechanism of communication in the nervous system.

突触传递为什么是量子式的,神经末梢怎样以突触囊泡为单位释放乙酰胆碱?

Why is synaptic transmission quantal, and how does a nerve terminal release acetylcholine one synaptic vesicle at a time?

因为递质以突触囊泡为最小包装单位整份释放。Katz等在蛙神经肌肉接头记录到自发的微终板电位,其幅度恰是最小单位的整数倍,表明单个囊泡一次倾出数千个乙酰胆碱分子;当动作电位抵达末梢,钙通道开放,钙离子结合突触结合蛋白解除对SNARE复合体的钳制,囊泡便在活性区与前膜融合,以全或无的胞吐完成一次传递。
Because transmitter is packaged in synaptic vesicles and released whole. At the frog neuromuscular junction Katz and colleagues recorded spontaneous miniature end-plate potentials whose amplitudes came in integer multiples of a smallest unit, showing that one vesicle dumps several thousand acetylcholine molecules at once. When an impulse invades the terminal, calcium channels open, calcium binds synaptotagmin to loosen the SNARE clamp, and vesicles fuse with the presynaptic membrane at active zones in an all-or-none exocytotic event.
