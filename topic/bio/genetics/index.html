实时荧光定量PCR（qPCR）是如何定量检测DNA的？

How does real-time quantitative PCR (qPCR) quantify DNA?

qPCR在每个扩增循环中同步检测荧光，通过Ct值定量：使用SYBR Green荧光染料（结合双链DNA发光）或TaqMan探针（利用FRET，探针被Taq酶5'到3'外切活性水解后释放荧光）。荧光信号随产物指数增长而增强，达到设定荧光阈值所需的循环数（Ct值）与初始模板拷贝数成反比，模板越多Ct越小。通过与已知浓度标准品作出的标准曲线比较，即可精确定量。若先以RNA为模板用逆转录酶合成cDNA（RT-qPCR），则可检测基因表达水平。
qPCR detects fluorescence at each amplification cycle and quantifies via the cycle threshold: SYBR Green dye, which fluoresces when bound to double-stranded DNA, or a TaqMan probe, a FRET-based probe cleaved by Taq's 5'-to-3' exonuclease activity, reports product accumulation. The number of cycles needed to reach a set fluorescence threshold (Ct value) is inversely proportional to the starting template copy number; more template means a smaller Ct. Comparing against a standard curve from known concentrations gives absolute quantification; using reverse transcriptase to make cDNA from RNA first (RT-qPCR) allows measurement of gene expression.

什么是遗传漂变？

What is genetic drift?

Genetic drift is the random change in gene frequencies in a small population due to chance events, unrelated to natural selection. For example, when a few individuals found a new group, the founder effect can make some genes unexpectedly rise or fall. Its evolutionary impact is especially strong in small populations.

Sanger（链终止）测序法的基本原理是什么？

What is the basic principle of Sanger (chain-termination) sequencing?

Sanger法利用2',3'-双脱氧核苷三磷酸（ddNTP）终止DNA链合成：ddNTP缺少3'-羟基，被DNA聚合酶掺入后下一个核苷酸无法连接，合成随即终止。测序反应中按比例混入少量标记的ddATP/ddTTP/ddCTP/ddGTP，与正常dNTP竞争，产生末端分别在A/T/C/G处终止、长度各异的片段库。现代自动化版本用四种不同荧光标记的ddNTP在单管反应中完成，经毛细管电泳按长度分离，激光检测器逐个读出荧光颜色，即得到碱基序列。
Sanger sequencing exploits 2',3'-dideoxynucleoside triphosphates (ddNTPs), which lack a 3'-hydroxyl group: once incorporated by DNA polymerase, no further nucleotide can be added and synthesis stops. A small proportion of labeled ddATP, ddTTP, ddCTP, and ddGTP is included with normal dNTPs, producing a library of fragments terminated randomly at each A, T, C, or G. Modern automated versions use four fluorescently labeled ddNTPs in a single reaction, separate the fragments by capillary electrophoresis by length, and a laser detector reads the color at each position to reveal the sequence.

Beneficial mutations can give organisms new abilities and serve as the raw material of evolution, while disease-causing mutations are often behind inherited disorders.
