用米氏方程的语言说明:他汀类竞争性抑制剂占据活性中心后,肝脏中HMG-CoA还原酶的表观Km与Vmax各自发生什么变化,这又如何决定给药剂量的收益上限?

In Michaelis–Menten terms, what happens to the apparent Km and Vmax of hepatic HMG-CoA reductase once a statin-type competitive inhibitor occupies the active site, and why does this set a ceiling on dose escalation?

竞争性抑制剂与底物争夺同一活性中心,底物需更高浓度才能达到半速,故表观Km增大;但只要底物足够过量,酶仍可被底物全部占据,Vmax维持不变。双倒数图中表现为多条直线交于纵轴同一点。正因Vmax封顶,他汀加量只能逼近而不能突破该酶的最大抑制程度,剂量收益随之递减。
A competitive inhibitor vies for the same active site, so a higher substrate concentration is needed to reach half-maximal velocity—the apparent Km rises while Vmax stays unchanged, since saturating substrate can still occupy every enzyme molecule. On a Lineweaver–Burk plot the lines pivot through a shared y-intercept. Because Vmax is capped, escalating statin doses approach but never exceed maximal suppression of the enzyme, so returns diminish with each increment.

#酶动力学 #竞争性抑制

为什么竞争性抑制剂会提高酶的表观 Km 却不改变 Vmax?在 Lineweaver-Burk 图上如何识别这种模式?

Why does a competitive inhibitor raise the apparent Km of an enzyme without changing Vmax, and how is this pattern recognized on a Lineweaver-Burk plot?

竞争性抑制剂与底物结构相似,可逆地占据活性中心,使一部分酶以 EI 复合物形式被扣留。提高底物浓度可以把抑制剂竞争性地挤走,因此在饱和底物下最大速率得以恢复,Vmax 不变;但达到半速反应所需的底物浓度升高,表观 Km 变为 Km(1+[I]/Ki)。在双倒数图上表现为各条直线相交于同一纵截距 1/Vmax,而斜率随抑制剂浓度按 (1+[I]/Ki) 倍增大。
A competitive inhibitor resembles the substrate and binds reversibly in the active site, sequestering part of the enzyme as an EI complex. Raising substrate concentration outcompetes the inhibitor, so at saturating S the full catalytic rate is restored and Vmax is unchanged; more S is simply needed to reach half-maximal velocity, giving Km(app) = Km(1+[I]/Ki). On the Lineweaver-Burk plot all lines intersect at the same y-intercept (1/Vmax), while slopes increase by the factor (1+[I]/Ki).

米氏常数Km的物理含义是什么?Km值的大小能否直接用来比较不同酶对其底物的亲和力?

What does the Michaelis constant Km physically represent, and can its value be used directly to compare substrate affinity across different enzymes?

Km在数值上等于反应速率达到最大速度一半时的底物浓度,其严格定义为(k-1+kcat)/k1,即ES复合物解离速率之和与其生成速率之比。只有当k-1远大于kcat(即复合物更倾向于解离而非发生催化)时,Km才近似等于解离常数Kd,此时低Km才意味着高底物亲和力。若kcat占比较大,Km会明显偏离Kd,直接跨酶比较Km可能得出误导性结论;严谨的说法是仅在同一酶-底物体系内或满足快速平衡假设时才能这样解读。
Numerically, Km equals the substrate concentration at which velocity reaches half of Vmax; rigorously it is defined as (k-1+kcat)/k1, the sum of ES breakdown rates divided by its formation rate. Only when k-1 greatly exceeds kcat—that is, the complex is more likely to dissociate before catalysis—does Km approximate the dissociation constant Kd, and only then does a lower Km indicate tighter binding. When kcat contributes substantially, Km deviates from Kd, so comparing Km values across different enzymes can mislead; the safe reading applies within one enzyme-substrate system or when the rapid-equilibrium assumption holds.
