为什么高山和深海也会造成生物地理隔离？

Why do mountains and deep seas create biogeographic isolation too?

这些屏障把种群隔开、限制基因流，长期累积差异后形成特有种，如同被隔离出来的演化实验室。
Such barriers limit gene flow, and accumulated differences produce endemic species like enclosed evolutionary laboratories.

岛屿生物地理学理论的主要预测是什么？

What does the theory of island biogeography predict?

岛屿物种数由迁入与灭绝的平衡决定：面积越大、离大陆越近，能容纳的物种就越多。
Species richness balances immigration against extinction: larger islands and those closer to the mainland hold more species.

华莱士线说明了什么生物地理现象？

What biogeographic phenomenon does Wallace's Line illustrate?

这条划分东南亚与澳洲动物区的界线，显示地理隔离如何让两侧演化出截然不同的哺乳动物区系。
The line separates distinct Asian and Australian faunas, showing how isolation shaped separate mammalian assemblages.
