雪豹生活在高山峭壁，为什么很难见到，它在生态系统里扮演什么角色？

Snow leopards live on high cliffs and are rarely seen—what role do they play in the ecosystem?

雪豹是高山食物链的顶端捕食者，控制岩羊、北山羊等食草动物数量，间接保护高山植被不被过度啃食。它行踪隐秘、数量稀少，是高山生态健康的“指示物种”。
The snow leopard is an apex predator of alpine chains, controlling herbivores like blue sheep so vegetation isn't overgrazed. Elusive and rare, it's an “indicator species” of alpine ecosystem health.

三北防护林种了大量树，为什么生态学家说单一树林不如天然林“好用”？

The Three-North Shelterbelt planted vast trees—why do ecologists say single-species forests are less “useful” than natural ones?

单一人工林物种少、食物链短、易爆发病虫害、保土保水能力有限；天然林多物种搭配、层次丰富，更能抗旱、固碳和供养动物。所以造林要讲“混交”和近自然恢复。
Monoculture plantations have few species, short food chains, pest outbreaks and limited soil/water holding; natural forests mix species across layers, better resisting drought, storing carbon and feeding wildlife. Afforestation should favor mixed, near-natural restoration.

福建武夷山为什么能被列入“世界生物圈保护区”，它到底特别在哪？

Why is Mount Wuyi listed as a “World Biosphere Reserve”—what makes it special?

武夷山处在中亚热带，地形垂直变化大，自成一统的湿润山地孕育了极高特有性：大量昆虫、两栖爬行和孑遗植物（如南方铁杉）在此栖息，是东亚生物多样性关键节点。
Wuyi sits in mid-subtropical China with strong vertical terrain, fostering a humid mountain world of high endemism: numerous insects, amphibians, reptiles and relic plants (like southern hemlock). It's a key biodiversity node in East Asia.

中华穿山甲几乎从野外消失，到底是偷猎更致命，还是失去栖息地更致命？

The Chinese pangolin has nearly vanished—is poaching or habitat loss the bigger killer?

两者叠加，但偷猎（为鳞片和肉）曾是直接主因，使其在中国大陆几近野外灭绝；同时森林砍伐抹去栖息地。如今保护要靠严打盗猎加恢复连通的森林。
Both combine, but poaching (for scales and meat) was the direct main cause, near-extincting it on the mainland; deforestation erased habitat too. Saving it needs anti-poaching plus reconnecting forests.
