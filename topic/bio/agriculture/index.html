为什么市面上绝大多数香蕉都是同一个品种（华蕉），而且靠无性繁殖？

Why are most bananas the same Cavendish variety and propagated asexually?

华蕉是三倍体，染色体不成套导致不育、不能靠种子繁殖，只能靠吸芽或组培克隆；遗传高度单一使它对抗黄叶病（香蕉枯萎病）等病害极为脆弱。
The Cavendish is triploid and sterile, so it cannot seed and is cloned via suckers or tissue culture; its genetic uniformity makes it extremely vulnerable to diseases like Panama wilt.

为什么保护农作物的地方品种（地方种）很重要？

Why is conserving local crop landraces important?

地方品种是在特定气候土壤中长期驯化形成的，携带适应本地病虫害和气候的独特基因；现代化推广少数高产品种会使这些遗传资源流失，削弱未来育种的缓冲能力。
Landraces are locally adapted over centuries, carrying unique genes for regional pests and climate; promoting a few modern varieties erodes this genetic resource and weakens future breeding resilience.

抗除草剂转基因作物会不会催生出难以对付的超级杂草？

Can herbicide-tolerant GM crops create hard-to-control superweeds?

长期单一使用同种除草剂会对杂草形成选择压，携带抗性基因的个体被保留并扩散，甚至通过近缘种间的基因漂移获得抗性，因此需配合轮作和多种除草策略管理。
Long-term use of one herbicide selects for resistant weeds that survive and spread, sometimes gaining resistance via gene flow to relatives, so rotation and multiple tactics are needed for management.

转基因作物和传统的杂交作物，本质区别在哪里？

What is the essential difference between transgenic and traditional hybrid crops?

杂交是在同一或近缘物种内重新组合已有的基因；转基因则可跨物种引入外源基因（如苏云金杆菌的 Bt 抗虫基因），精准度更高但也带来更严格的监管与伦理讨论。
Hybridization recombines existing genes within or between related species, whereas transgenics insert foreign genes across species (e.g. Bt insecticidal genes from Bacillus thuringiensis), offering precision but tighter regulation and ethical debate.

温室里为什么常放一箱蜜蜂或熊蜂？

Why are bees or bumblebees often placed in greenhouses?

许多果蔬（番茄、草莓等）需要昆虫传粉才能结好果，温室密闭缺少野生传粉者，人工引入蜂类可完成授粉、显著提高坐果率和产量。
Many fruits and vegetables need insect pollination for good set; sealed greenhouses lack wild pollinators, so introducing bees boosts fruit set and yield.

豆类作物（如大豆、花生）为什么能肥田？

Why do legume crops like soybean and peanut enrich the soil?

豆科根部的根瘤里共生着根瘤菌，能把空气中植物不能直接利用的氮气转化为氨态氮供植物吸收，相当于天然施肥，减少化肥需求。
Legume roots host rhizobia in nodules that convert atmospheric nitrogen into ammonia plants can use, acting like natural fertilization and cutting fertilizer needs.

水稻养活了几十亿亚洲人，野生稻和碗里香喷喷的栽培稻差别到底在哪？

Rice feeds billions in Asia—what really differs between wild rice and the tasty cultivated grain?

野生稻种子小、易脱落、产量低；先民长期选育让栽培稻谷粒变大、不易掉粒、口感更好，并分化出籼、粳等类型。中国是世界上最早种植水稻的地区之一，长江流域的稻作历史逾万年。
Wild rice has tiny, easily shattered, low-yield grains; millennia of selection gave cultivated rice larger, non-shattering, tastier grains and types like indica and japonica. China is among the earliest rice-cultivating regions, with Yangtze farming over 10,000 years old.

为什么有些水果采摘后还要人工催熟（比如用乙烯）？

Why are some fruits artificially ripened after harvest, e.g. with ethylene?

乙烯是植物的成熟激素，能启动淀粉转糖、果肉变软和香气形成；青果采后人工用乙烯或在密闭环境存放，便于长途运输并在到店时恰好成熟。
Ethylene is the plant's ripening hormone, triggering starch-to-sugar conversion, softening and aroma; post-harvest ethylene or sealed storage lets fruit ship unripe and ripen on arrival.

畜牧中为什么要给公畜去势（阉割）？

Why are male livestock castrated in animal husbandry?

去势后动物性情更温顺、便于管理，脂肪沉积改善肉质，并防止近交和过早配种；这是传统畜牧中兼顾效率与安全的手段。
Castration makes animals calmer and easier to manage, improves meat fattening, and prevents inbreeding and premature mating—a traditional husbandry trade-off of efficiency and safety.

#去势 #畜牧 #肉质

古人把秸秆、粪肥堆在一起“沤肥”，庄稼就长得好，这其实是在利用什么微生物过程？

Ancients piled straw and manure to “rot fertilizer” and crops grew well—what microbial process were they using?

这是堆肥：细菌、真菌等分解者把有机物逐步降解，温度升高杀灭病菌杂草籽，最终变成腐殖质，释放氮磷钾等养分供植物吸收。所谓“沤肥”就是让微生物替我们造肥。
That's composting: decomposers like bacteria and fungi break organic matter down, heat kills pathogens and weed seeds, and the result—humus—releases nitrogen, phosphorus and potassium for plants. “Rotting fertilizer” lets microbes make the fertilizer for us.

种子库（种质资源库）为什么要保存作物的野生近缘种？

Why do seed banks preserve wild relatives of crops?

野生近缘种携带栽培品种已丢失的抗旱、抗病、耐盐等基因，是育种的基因宝库；保存它们相当于为未来气候变化和病害准备备份。
Wild relatives carry drought-, disease- and salt-tolerance genes lost from cultivars, a gene reservoir for breeding; banking them backs up against future climate and disease threats.

玉米为什么既是粮食、又是饲料还能做工业原料？

Why is maize at once food, feed and industrial raw material?

玉米籽粒富含淀粉，可直接食用或作牲畜饲料；深加工又能提取淀粉制成糖浆、发酵产乙醇，或转化为可降解塑料的前体，是少有的多用途作物。
Maize is starch-rich: eaten directly or fed to livestock, yet processed into syrup, fermented to ethanol, or converted to bioplastic precursors—a rare multipurpose crop.

杂交水稻为什么能比普通水稻增产？

Why can hybrid rice outyield ordinary rice?

杂交利用杂种优势：不同亲本杂交得到的子一代在生长势、抗逆性和产量上常优于双亲，源于显性效应和基因的互补。
Hybrids exploit heterosis: the F1 of distinct parents often surpasses both in vigor, stress tolerance and yield, due to dominance effects and gene complementation.

为什么农民常轮作而不是连年种同一种作物？

Why do farmers rotate crops instead of growing the same one year after year?

连作会耗竭特定养分并积累土传病害和害虫；轮作不同科作物可恢复地力、打断病虫害循环，还能利用豆科固氮减少化肥。
Continuous cropping depletes specific nutrients and builds up soil-borne pests and diseases; rotating different plant families restores soil, breaks pest cycles and can use legumes to fix nitrogen.

老人说“保护蚯蚓就是保护庄稼”，蚯蚓对土地到底做了什么好事？

“Protect earthworms and you protect the crops”—what good do earthworms do for the soil?

蚯蚓在土里钻洞，让空气和水能进到根部；它们吃进枯叶泥土、排出肥沃的粪粒，等于免费松土又施肥。有蚯蚓的土壤更疏松、庄稼长得更旺。
Earthworms tunnel through soil so air and water reach roots; they eat leaf litter and soil and excrete rich castings—free tilling and fertilizing. Wormy soil is looser and crops grow better.

二十四节气里“清明前后，种瓜点豆”，这和科学种田有什么关系？

The solar term “around Qingming, sow melons and beans”—what's the science in this?

节气是古人按太阳位置和物候总结的农时。清明前后气温回升、霜期已过、土壤变暖，正适合种子发芽，所以这时播种出苗又快又齐，是长期观察自然的科学经验。
Solar terms summarize farming timing from the sun's position and nature's signs. Around Qingming, warmth returns, frost ends and soil warms—ideal for germination, so sowing then gives fast, even sprouts: science from long observation.

农民常说“瑞雪兆丰年”，一场雪和来年的收成有什么生物学关系？

Farmers say “a timely snow promises a good harvest”—what's the biology linking snow to next year's crop?

冬雪像被子盖住麦苗，保温防冻；雪水融化慢、渗入土壤补水；低温还能冻死不少越冬害虫和病菌，等于给田地做了一次自然消毒，所以开春庄稼长得好。
Snow blankets wheat like a quilt, insulating it; meltwater slowly soaks the soil; cold kills many overwintering pests and pathogens—a natural disinfection—so crops thrive in spring.
