雷帕霉素靶蛋白（mTOR）通路为什么成为抗衰老研究的热点？

Why is the mTOR pathway a focus of anti-aging research?

mTOR 感知营养并调控生长，抑制它可在多种动物中延长寿命并改善健康跨度，但需权衡免疫抑制等副作用。
mTOR senses nutrients and controls growth; inhibiting it extends lifespan in many animals by shifting metabolism toward maintenance, though with side effects like immunosuppression.

细胞衰老（senescence）为什么会加速组织老化？

How does cellular senescence accelerate tissue aging?

衰老细胞停止分裂却分泌促炎因子（SASP），招来慢性炎症并干扰周围健康细胞；在老年小鼠中清除它们可改善健康。
Senescent cells stop dividing yet secrete inflammatory factors (SASP) that drive chronic inflammation and harm neighbors; clearing them improves health in aged mice.

什么是炎症衰老（inflammaging）？

What is inflammaging?

随着年龄增长，体内慢性炎症水平缓慢上升，与衰老细胞和肠道菌群变化有关，被认为是多种老年病的重要推手。
Chronic low-grade inflammation rises with age, linked to senescent cells and microbiota shifts, and is a major driver of many age-related diseases.

为什么少吃一点可能让人活得更长？

Why might eating a bit less help you live longer?

在营养充足但不过量的情况下，动物会调低生长信号、加强细胞修复，于是从酵母到猴子的许多物种都出现了寿命延长。
With enough but not excess food, animals dial down growth signaling and boost repair, lengthening life across species from yeast to monkeys.
