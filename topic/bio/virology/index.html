流感每隔数十年换上一副全新面孔,疫苗却须年年重配——“抗原漂移”与“抗原转变”背后分别是哪两种尺度迥异的进化事件?

Influenza reappears with a fresh face every few decades while vaccines need annual reformulation—what two very different scales of evolutionary event lie behind antigenic drift versus antigenic shift?

抗原漂移是血凝素与神经氨酸酶编码区点突变的逐年累积,免疫压力筛出逃逸株,属于渐进的线性微进化。抗原转变则依托分节段基因组的跨种重配:人流感株与禽流感株共感染同一细胞时,八段基因组随机包装,一步即可产出表面抗原焕然一新的重组毒株。漂移解释季节性流行,转变解释大流行的零星引爆,两者共同迫使疫苗定期换代。
Antigenic drift is the year-on-year accretion of point mutations in the hemagglutinin and neuraminidase genes, with immune pressure selecting escape variants—a gradual, incremental process. Antigenic shift instead rides on cross-species reassortment of the eight-segmented genome: when human and avian strains co-infect one cell, random segment packaging can deliver, in a single step, a recombinant wearing wholly novel surface antigens. Drift accounts for seasonal epidemics, shift for sporadic pandemics, and together they force periodic vaccine renewal.

#病毒学 #流感病毒进化

为什么RNA病毒在感染者体内往往不是一群完全相同的颗粒,而是一片不断互争互补的“突变云”?

Why do RNA viruses circulating in an infected host form a shifting cloud of competing yet complementary mutants rather than a population of identical particles?

因为RNA依赖的RNA聚合酶缺少DNA聚合酶那样的校对外切核酸酶,单次复制的碱基错配率约为10^-4到10^-5。高错误率让每个基因组都携带若干新突变,群体因此呈现为准种——由主导序列及其近缘变体构成的动态分布。这种多样性使病毒能快速响应抗体或抗病毒药物施加的选择压力。
Because their RNA-dependent RNA polymerase lacks the proofreading exonuclease found in DNA polymerases, each replication cycle introduces mismatches at roughly 10^-4 to 10^-5 per nucleotide. This high error rate seeds every genome with new mutations, so the population behaves as a quasispecies—a dynamic distribution centered on a master sequence plus its close variants. Such diversity lets the virus respond rapidly to selection imposed by antibodies or antiviral drugs.

#病毒学 #准种多样性

为什么RNA病毒比DNA病毒的突变率高得多?这种高突变率对病毒本身是纯粹的优势吗?

Why do RNA viruses mutate far more rapidly than DNA viruses, and is this high mutation rate purely advantageous to the virus?

RNA依赖的RNA聚合酶缺乏DNA聚合酶所具备的3'到5'校对活性,且RNA化学稳定性低于DNA,因此RNA病毒的每位点突变率通常比DNA病毒高出几个数量级。高突变率让病毒种群呈准种分布,能快速逃逸宿主免疫和抗病毒药物;但突变率存在上限——超过错误阈值后基因组信息会因错误累积而崩溃。这正是利巴韦林等致突变化合物对抗丙型肝炎病毒等RNA病毒的理论基础。
RNA-dependent RNA polymerases lack the 3-prime-to-5-prime proofreading activity of DNA polymerases, and RNA is chemically less stable than DNA, so RNA viruses mutate at rates orders of magnitude higher per site. The resulting mutant swarm, or quasispecies, lets these viruses quickly evade host immunity and antiviral drugs. Yet the mutation rate has an upper bound: past the error threshold, genomic information collapses under accumulated mistakes—which is exactly how mutagenic drugs such as ribavirin push hepatitis C virus into lethal mutagenesis.
