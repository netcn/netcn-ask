为什么演替后期常出现竞争与散播的权衡？

Why do trade-offs between competition and dispersal shape late succession?

早期物种靠快速散播占领空地，后期物种靠竞争优势立足，资源水平和干扰频率决定谁占主导。
Early species win by fast dispersal, later ones by competitive ability, with resources and disturbance frequency deciding dominance.

火灾在一些生态系统中为什么是必要的过程？

Why is fire a necessary process in some ecosystems?

在松林和稀树草原，火能打破种子休眠、清除竞争者并回补养分；一味抑制火反而会让系统退化。
In pine forests and savannas, fire breaks seed dormancy, clears competitors, and recycles nutrients; suppressing it degrades the system.

生态恢复（例如废弃矿场复绿）和天然演替有什么关系？

How is ecological restoration related to natural succession?

恢复生态学常模仿或加速次生演替：先种先锋植物改良土壤，再引入后期物种重建群落。
Restoration ecology mimics or accelerates secondary succession, using pioneer plants to improve soil before later species return.
