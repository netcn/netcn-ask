肝脏在代谢和解毒中承担哪些工作？

What metabolic and detox roles does the liver play?

肝脏把葡萄糖转为糖原储存、分解毒素与药物、合成血浆蛋白和胆汁，并通过尿素循环处理氨，是全身代谢的中枢工厂。
The liver stores glucose as glycogen, breaks down toxins and drugs, makes plasma proteins and bile, and processes ammonia via the urea cycle—a central metabolic factory.

小肠为什么是吸收营养的主要场所？

Why is the small intestine the main site for nutrient absorption?

小肠内壁有密布的绒毛和微绒毛，极大增加表面积，并富含血液和淋巴管，能高效地吸收糖、氨基酸、脂肪等营养物质。
The small intestine's wall is covered with villi and microvilli, vastly increasing surface area and richly supplied with blood and lymph, efficiently absorbing sugars, amino acids, and fats.

胃是如何帮助消化食物的？

How does the stomach help digest food?

胃分泌胃酸和蛋白酶，酸性环境既杀灭部分细菌又激活酶，把食物搅成半流质的食糜，再送入小肠继续消化。
The stomach secretes acid and protease; the acidic environment kills some bacteria and activates enzymes, churning food into semi-liquid chyme before passing it to the small intestine.
