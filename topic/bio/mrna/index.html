自扩增 mRNA 与传统 mRNA 疫苗有什么不同？

How does self-amplifying mRNA differ from conventional mRNA vaccines?

传统 mRNA 疫苗只递送编码抗原的读框，用完即降解；自扩增 mRNA 用正链 RNA 病毒的复制子，另带复制酶基因，进胞后先造出复制酶，由它大量复制模板，抗原因此表达更久、剂量更低。代价是转录本长得多、纯化更麻烦，复制必经的双链 RNA 也更易触发先天免疫。两者都不进细胞核、不碰基因组，这是它与 DNA 疫苗的分界。
A conventional mRNA vaccine delivers only the reading frame encoding the antigen, and that transcript is used once before it degrades. Self-amplifying mRNA uses the replicon of a positive-strand RNA virus and carries a replicase gene as well: the cell builds the replicase first, which then copies the template many times, so the antigen lasts longer and a smaller dose suffices. The price is a far longer transcript, fussier purification, and double-stranded RNA intermediates that more easily trigger innate immunity. Neither kind enters the nucleus or touches the genome, and that is where they part company with DNA vaccines.

mRNA 疫苗为什么要用脂质纳米颗粒（LNP）包裹？

Why are mRNA vaccines packaged in lipid nanoparticles (LNPs)?

裸露的 mRNA 易被酶降解且难以进入细胞，LNP 既保护它又帮助其进入细胞质释放，是递送的关键技术。
Naked mRNA is fragile and entry-poor; LNPs protect it from enzymes and ferry it into the cytoplasm, solving delivery.

#脂质纳米颗粒 #递送

除了疫苗，mRNA 技术还能做什么？

Beyond vaccines, what else can mRNA technology do?

它可被编程表达几乎任何蛋白，用于癌症个性化抗原、缺失酶的替代治疗以及体内基因编辑工具的递送。
It can be programmed to express nearly any protein for personalized cancer antigens, enzyme replacement, or delivering gene editors in vivo.

mRNA 疫苗是怎么让人体产生抗体的？

How do mRNA vaccines make the body produce antibodies?

疫苗把编码病毒刺突蛋白的 mRNA 送进细胞，细胞按图纸造出蛋白，免疫系统借此识别并储备防御能力。
The mRNA instructs cells to make a viral protein, which the immune system learns to recognize and defend against.

#mRNA疫苗 #刺突蛋白

mRNA 疫苗和传统疫苗最大的不同是什么？

What is the biggest difference between mRNA and traditional vaccines?

传统疫苗多直接递送弱化或灭活的病原体，而 mRNA 疫苗只递送制造一种病毒蛋白的说明书，让细胞自己生产抗原。
Traditional vaccines often deliver weakened or killed pathogens, while mRNA vaccines deliver only the instructions to make one viral protein, letting cells produce the antigen.
