浙江青田的“稻鱼共生”系统被定为农业遗产，古人这套智慧妙在哪？

Qingtian's rice–fish system is a heritage farm—what's smart about this ancient wisdom?

稻田养鱼让鱼吃虫除草、鱼粪肥田，少施农药化肥；稻为鱼遮阴供氧、鱼为稻松土除害，一水两用、一田双收，是循环农业的典范，已被联合国粮农组织列为全球重要农业文化遗产。
Raising fish in paddies lets fish eat pests and weeds while their droppings fertilize; rice shades and oxygenates water, fish loosen soil and remove harm—one field, two harvests. A model of circular farming, listed by FAO as agricultural heritage.

#稻鱼共生 #循环农业 #遗产

很多中药要“炮制”，比如炒、蒸、煅、酒炙，古人是在改变化学成分吗？

Many herbs must be “processed”—fried, steamed, calcined, wine-soaked. Were ancients changing chemistry?

是的。炮制能通过加热、水飞、加辅料降低毒性（如附子减毒）、改变药性、便于保存或增强某类成分溶出。这是经验化学：在不懂分子结构时，靠反复实践调控药效与安全性。
Yes. Processing with heat, water, or adjuvants lowers toxicity (e.g. prepared aconite), shifts properties, aids storage or boosts extraction of active compounds. It was empirical chemistry—tuning efficacy and safety by practice long before molecular science.

#炮制 #毒性 #化学成分

《本草纲目》把成千上万种动植物都当成药，古人没有化学仪器，是怎么知道哪种有用的？

The Compendium of Materia Medica lists thousands of plants and animals as medicine—without labs, how did ancients know what worked?

古人靠长期观察、试错和口耳相传积累经验：看动物生病自疗、记民间的“吃什么补什么”、用身体反应验证。其中部分被现代研究证实（如青蒿治疟、麻黄平喘），但也有大量缺乏证据，不能盲信。
Ancients built knowledge by long observation, trial and error, and oral tradition—watching sick animals self-medicate, noting folk “like cures like”, testing on themselves. Some (artemisinin for malaria, ephedra for asthma) are validated; many lack evidence and shouldn't be trusted blindly.

#本草纲目 #传统 #经验

传统稻田里养鱼、养鸭，为什么比只种水稻反而更少长虫、更少施肥？

Traditional rice fields raise fish and ducks alongside—why fewer pests and less fertilizer than rice alone?

鸭吃害虫和杂草、翻动泥土，鱼吃蚊虫幼虫和落叶碎屑；它们的排泄物又是天然肥料，形成“稻—鱼—鸭”小循环，减少农药化肥，还提高产量和品质。
Ducks eat pests and weeds and stir the mud; fish consume mosquito larvae and debris; their droppings fertilize naturally. The rice–fish–duck loop cuts pesticides and fertilizer while raising yield and quality.

傣族的“龙山林”、藏族的“神山”不许乱砍，这种传统信仰真的能保护生物多样性吗？

Dai “holy hills” and Tibetan “sacred mountains” forbid felling—can such traditional beliefs really protect biodiversity?

能。把山林当作神山、龙山禁忌砍伐和狩猎，等于立了一道文化“保护区”，让原生植被和动物得以保留。许多传统信仰客观上成了最早的就地保护，和现代保护理念不谋而合。
Yes. Treating hills or mountains as sacred and banning cutting or hunting acts like a cultural “reserve”, preserving native vegetation and wildlife. Many such beliefs were humanity's earliest in-situ conservation, matching modern ideas.

#神山 #龙山 #传统保护

端午节挂艾草、菖蒲，老人说能“驱虫辟邪”，这有科学道理吗？

At Dragon Boat Festival people hang mugwort and calamus to “repel insects and evil”—is there science in it?

艾草和菖蒲含挥发性芳香油（如桉叶素、黄酮），能驱赶蚊蝇、抑制部分细菌，初夏湿热蚊虫多，挂它们确有实际驱虫效果，“辟邪”其实是古人对防病的说法。
Mugwort and calamus release volatile oils (eucalyptol, flavonoids) that repel mosquitoes and inhibit some bacteria. In the muggy early summer this genuinely deters pests; “warding off evil” was the ancient way of saying disease prevention.

#艾草 #菖蒲 #驱虫

古人看“七九河开、八九雁来”安排种地，这叫物候，物候到底是什么？

Ancients planted by signs like “rivers thaw at 7×9 days, geese return at 8×9”—that's phenology. What is it?

物候是生物随季节节律出现的现象，如开花、候鸟来去、虫鸣。古人把动植物的“时间表”和农事对应，就成了不用仪器的自然日历，用来判断播种、收获的时机。
Phenology is the timing of biological events with the seasons—flowering, bird migration, insect song. Ancients matched nature's “schedule” to farming, creating a natural calendar for sowing and harvest without instruments.
