睡前刷手机为什么特别影响入睡?蓝光到底做了什么?

Why does late-night screen time hurt sleep so much — what does blue light actually do?

视网膜上有一种对约480纳米蓝光格外敏感的感光细胞(含黑视蛋白),它们不负责成像,却直连视交叉上核,向大脑报告「现在还是白天」。夜间长时间接收这类蓝光会抑制褪黑素的分泌、推迟昼夜钟的相位,让人该困的时候不困;实验显示睡前使用发光屏幕的人褪黑素分泌起点显著推迟,次日早晨也更困倦。暖色暗光、夜间模式或提前一小时放下手机,都是在给生物钟留出日落缓冲。
A class of retinal cells containing melanopsin is exquisitely sensitive to blue light near 480 nm. They do not form images; they wire straight to the suprachiasmatic nucleus and tell the brain it is still daytime. Sustained evening exposure suppresses melatonin and delays the circadian phase, so you feel sleepy later than you should; experiments show evening screen use markedly delays melatonin rise and leaves people groggier next morning. Warm dim light, night modes, or putting the phone away an hour earlier all give the clock its sunset buffer.

倒时差为什么难受?我们身体里真的有一座「钟」吗?

Why is jet lag so miserable — is there really a clock inside our bodies?

真有。下丘脑里约两万个神经元组成的视交叉上核是中枢时钟,它通过昼夜光照校准,再指挥肝脏、心脏等外周器官的节律基因,让体温、激素、代谢在24小时周期里有序起落。飞机快速跨越时区后,中枢钟还停在新城市的「旧时间」上,各器官的重校速度又不一样,睡眠、消化与清醒信号彼此错位,便产生疲惫与肠胃不适。通常每跨一个时区需要约一天才能重新对齐,向东飞行因需拨快往往更慢适应。
There really is one. The suprachiasmatic nucleus — roughly twenty thousand neurons in the hypothalamus — acts as the central clock, calibrated daily by light and in turn synchronizing clock genes in the liver, heart and other peripheral organs, so body temperature, hormones and metabolism rise and fall on a 24-hour cycle. After a fast flight across time zones the central clock still keeps the old city's time while tissues resynchronize at different speeds, so sleep, digestion and alertness fall out of phase — hence fatigue and stomach upset. Alignment takes about a day per time zone, and eastward travel adapts more slowly.
