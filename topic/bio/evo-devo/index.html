身体蓝图中的深层同源说明了什么？

What does deep homology of body plans suggest?

昆虫的眼与脊椎动物的眼睛虽外观不同，却共享 Pax6 等关键调控基因，说明复杂器官可源于同一个古老的遗传模块。
Fly and vertebrate eyes differ in form yet share core regulators like Pax6, suggesting complex organs arose from an ancient genetic module.

为什么 Hox 基因突变会让果蝇长出错位的腿或多余翅膀？

Why do Hox gene mutations make flies grow misplaced legs or extra wings?

Hox 基因负责指定体节的身份，突变会错误地开启别处的发育程序，把触角位置变成腿、把平衡棒变成翅膀。
Hox genes assign segment identity; mutations misactivate programs so legs replace antennae and halteres become wings.

演化发育生物学为什么说改动开关比改动零件更容易产生新形态？

Why does Evo-Devo say changing switches is easier than changing parts to make new forms?

许多形态差异来自基因调控元件（如增强子）的变化而非蛋白本身改变；相同零件在不同时间地点被开启，就造出不同的结构。
Many form differences come from regulatory elements like enhancers rather than the proteins; the same parts switched on differently build different structures.
