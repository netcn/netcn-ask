仙人掌生活在干旱沙漠里，为什么还偏偏在晚上张开气孔进行气体交换？

Cacti live in dry deserts, so why do they open their stomata at night to exchange gases?

这是 CAM 途径的巧妙设计。白天沙漠高温，气孔若张开会流失大量水分，所以仙人掌白天关闭气孔；到了凉爽的夜晚，气孔张开吸收二氧化碳，并把它固定成苹果酸储存在液泡里。白天气孔关闭时，苹果酸再脱羧释放出二氧化碳，正好供应卡尔文循环合成糖。二氧化碳的“取”与“用”被时间隔开，植物几乎不蒸腾也能完成光合作用，代价是固定同样多的碳需要消耗更多能量。
This is the elegance of CAM photosynthesis. By day the desert is hot, and open stomata would lose huge amounts of water, so cacti close them. On cool nights the stomata open and take in carbon dioxide, which is fixed into malic acid and stored in the vacuole. By day, with stomata shut, the malic acid decarboxylates and releases CO2, feeding the Calvin cycle. Carbon dioxide uptake and use are separated in time, so the plant photosynthesizes with almost no water loss, at the cost of needing more energy to fix the same amount of carbon.

What is the biological significance of plant secondary metabolites?

Secondary metabolites such as alkaloids, flavonoids, and terpenes are not required for basic growth yet play key roles in defense against herbivores, attracting pollinators, and stress tolerance. Many are drug sources, artemisinin, morphine, serving as plants' chemical tools in environmental interactions.

光合作用怎样把水分解放氧？

How does photosynthesis split water to release oxygen?

光系统 II 利用光能从水中夺取电子，经 Kok 循环（放氧复合体）每四次周转放出一分子 O₂，并把质子泵入类囊体腔建立跨膜梯度。Barber 在 2009 年综述了放氧中心的结构机理，Taiz 与 Zeiger 的《植物生理学》作系统讲解。
Photosystem II uses light to pull electrons from water; the Kok cycle (oxygen-evolving complex) releases one O₂ every four turnovers and pumps protons into the thylakoid lumen. Barber reviewed the OEC structure in 2009; Taiz & Zeiger explain it in 'Plant Physiology'.

C4 植物相比 C3 植物有什么适应优势？

What adaptive advantage do C4 plants have over C3 plants?

C4 植物在炎热干旱环境中先用 PEP 羧化酶在叶肉细胞固定 CO2 为四碳化合物，再运至维管束鞘细胞释放供卡尔文循环，从而减少光呼吸损失。这种「CO2 浓缩」机制使玉米、甘蔗等在强光高温下比 C3 植物更高效。
In hot, dry conditions, C4 plants first fix CO2 into a four-carbon compound in mesophyll cells via PEP carboxylase, then release it in bundle-sheath cells for the Calvin cycle, cutting photorespiration losses. This CO2-concentrating mechanism lets corn and sugarcane outperform C3 plants under strong light and heat.

在植物的系统性获得抗性（SAR）中，水杨酸甲酯如何充当长距离移动信号？

How does methyl salicylate serve as the long-distance mobile signal in systemic acquired resistance (SAR)?

局部病原侵染后，感病叶中的水杨酸被SAMT酶甲基化为水杨酸甲酯（MeSA）。MeSA脂溶性较强，经韧皮部运抵远端健康组织，由酯酶SABP2水解还原为水杨酸；积累的水杨酸经受体NPR1诱导PR病程相关基因表达并建立预激发状态，使全株在二次侵染时启动更快更广的防御，构成植物系统性免疫的核心长距离信号通路。
After localized infection, salicylic acid in the diseased leaf is methylated by SAMT into methyl salicylate (MeSA). The lipophilic MeSA moves through the phloem to distant healthy tissue, where the esterase SABP2 hydrolyzes it back to salicylic acid; accumulated SA then acts via receptor NPR1 to induce pathogenesis-related genes and establish a primed state, letting the whole plant mount faster and broader defense upon secondary attack — the core long-distance circuit of plant systemic immunity.

许多植物的种子被鸟类吞下、随粪便排出后反而更容易萌发。这段消化道经历究竟改变了种子的什么？这对森林更新有何意义？

Many seeds germinate better after being swallowed by birds and passed in their droppings. What exactly does the journey through the gut change, and why does it matter for forest regeneration?

果肉常含萌发抑制物并维持高渗透压，黏附的果皮还阻碍水分进入；鸟类的消化去除果肉并轻度磨损种皮，从而打破休眠。同时种子被运离母树，避开亲株附近密集的专一天敌与幼苗竞争（Janzen–Connell效应），萌发率与建群机会同步提高，深刻影响森林更新的空间格局。
Fruit pulp often carries germination inhibitors and keeps seeds in an osmotically stressful environment, while clinging pericarp blocks water uptake. Gut transit strips the pulp and lightly abrades the coat, breaking dormancy; birds also carry seeds away from the parent tree, escaping dense natural enemies and seedling competition near adults (the Janzen–Connell effect). Germination and colonization chances rise together, shaping the spatial pattern of forest regeneration.

为什么环割一圈果树的树皮，果实反而结得更大？

Why does cutting a ring of bark off a fruit tree make its fruit bigger?

树皮内侧的韧皮部承担“向下送粮”的运输：光合产物（糖）从叶片经筛管运往根部等“库”。环割切断了韧皮部，糖无法下运，就大量积存在切口以上的枝条和果实里，果实得到的糖更多，自然更大更甜；而负责向上输水的木质部在树皮内侧更深层，未被损伤，根系仍能靠储存物质存活。若环割过宽且不愈合，根部长期断粮最终会让整棵树枯死，所以生产中只做轻度环割或螺旋割。
The phloem inside the bark ships sugar downward: photosynthate moves from leaves through sieve tubes to sinks like the roots. Girdling severs the phloem, so sugar can no longer travel down and piles up in the branches and fruits above the cut, giving fruits extra sugar and making them bigger and sweeter. The water-carrying xylem lies deeper, beyond the cut, so it keeps working and the roots survive on stored reserves; if the cut ring is too wide and fails to heal, the roots are starved and the whole tree may die, which is why growers use only a light or spiral girdle.

什么是菌根？丛枝菌根真菌如何为植物提供好处？

What are mycorrhizae, and how do arbuscular mycorrhizal fungi benefit plants?

菌根是植物根系与真菌形成的互利共生结构，约90%的陆生植物都形成菌根。丛枝菌根真菌的菌丝侵入皮层细胞，形成树状丛枝结构，大幅扩大根系吸收面积，为植物提供磷、氮等矿质养分和水分；作为回报，植物把约20%的光合产物（糖类）提供给真菌。菌根还增强植物抗干旱、抗病原菌的能力，是陆地生态系统碳循环的重要环节。
A mycorrhiza is a mutualistic association between plant roots and fungi; about 90% of land plants form mycorrhizae. Arbuscular mycorrhizal fungi grow hyphae into cortical cells, forming tree-like arbuscules that vastly expand the absorbing surface, supplying the plant with phosphorus, nitrogen, and water. In return, the plant transfers roughly 20% of its photosynthates (sugars) to the fungus. Mycorrhizae also improve drought and pathogen resistance and play a major role in terrestrial carbon cycling.

花的演化与传粉者之间存在怎样的相互作用？

How have flowers and their pollinators co-evolved?

花的结构与传粉者形成了高度特异的共演化：虫媒花（如兰科植物）颜色鲜艳、有香味或花蜜，花瓣形态适应特定昆虫，如蜂兰花模拟雌蜂吸引雄蜂为其授粉；鸟媒花多为红色、无香而蜜量丰富；风媒花则花粉量大、柱头羽毛状而花被退化。这种传粉综合征使传粉更精确高效，也推动了开花植物的辐射演化。
Flower structure and pollinators show highly specific co-evolution: insect-pollinated flowers (e.g., orchids) are colorful, scented, or nectar-producing, with petal shapes fitted to particular insects; bee orchids mimic female bees to attract males for pollination. Bird-pollinated flowers tend to be red, scentless, and nectar-rich; wind-pollinated flowers produce abundant pollen, have feathery stigmas, and reduced perianths. These pollination syndromes make pollination more precise and efficient and drove the adaptive radiation of angiosperms.

光周期现象如何影响植物的开花？

How does photoperiodism affect plant flowering?

许多植物通过感知昼夜长短（光周期）来决定开花时机：长日照植物在白昼超过临界值才开花，短日照植物则相反，日中性植物不受明显影响。这由叶片中的光敏色素接收信号、经激素传达到顶端分生组织调控。
Many plants time flowering by sensing day length: long-day plants bloom once days exceed a critical length, short-day plants do the opposite, and day-neutral plants are little affected. Leaf phytochromes receive the signal and relay it via hormones to the shoot apical meristem to regulate flowering.

苔藓植物（如葫芦藓）为什么被认为是高等植物中比较原始的类型？

Why are bryophytes such as mosses regarded as a relatively primitive group among land plants?

苔藓植物已具有多细胞结构和颈卵器、孢子囊，但还没有真正的根（只有假根），体内没有维管组织，无法远距离输导水分。它们的配子体（绿色植株）占优势且自养，孢子体寄生在配子体上。由于无维管束且受精依赖水，苔藓体型矮小，多生于阴湿处。
Bryophytes have multicellular structures, archegonia, and sporangia, but they lack true roots (only rhizoids) and vascular tissue, so they cannot transport water over long distances. Their dominant, photosynthetic generation is the gametophyte, and the sporophyte lives attached to it. In short, the light reactions charge the battery and the Calvin cycle builds the product, energy-rich organic matter plus oxygen.

What substances do xylem and phloem each transport?

木质部主要把根吸收的水分和无机盐向上运输到地上部分，韧皮部则把叶片光合作用制造的糖类等有机物运送到全身各器官。
Xylem mainly carries water and minerals upward from roots, while phloem distributes sugars and other organic products made by photosynthesis to organs throughout the plant.
