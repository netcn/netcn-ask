01人为什么能长高，青春期的身高突增又是怎么来的？Why do humans grow taller, and where does the adolescent height spurt come from?

长骨靠骨骺软骨板增殖骨化而延长，生长激素经 IGF-1 驱动，性激素促成突增并最终闭合骨骺。Long bones lengthen as epiphyseal cartilage proliferates and ossifies, driven by growth hormone via IGF-1; sex hormones add the spurt and later close the plates.

人能长高，直接原因是四肢和躯干的长骨在两端不断变长，而长骨变长靠的是骨骺软骨板持续增殖再骨化；生长激素是这条链条的主要内分泌信号，青春期性激素又叠加出一段快速突增。等骨骺软骨板被骨组织替代、骨骺闭合，长骨就不再变长，身高也随之定型。这一章把身高从儿童期到成年的增长链条拆开讲清楚。Human growth in height comes directly from the lengthening of long bones in the limbs and trunk. That lengthening depends on continual proliferation and ossification at the epiphyseal growth plates near each end of a bone. Growth hormone is the main endocrine signal in this chain, and sex hormones add a rapid adolescent growth spurt on top of it. Once the growth plate is replaced by bone and the epiphysis closes, the long bone can no longer grow longer, and adult height is fixed.[1][2]

长骨两端的骨骺与骨干之间有一层透明软骨构成的骨骺板。板内软骨细胞不断分裂、增大，靠近骨干的一侧逐步被羟基磷灰石类的骨组织替代，骨头于是沿纵轴延长。生长激素由垂体前叶分泌，属于促合成代谢激素，它直接促进骨骼与肌肉的蛋白质合成，并通过肝脏生成胰岛素样生长因子 1（IGF-1）放大对软骨细胞的刺激。OpenStax 生理学教材把生长激素列为调控生长的核心垂体激素，下丘脑则以生长激素释放激素和生长抑素一促一抑地调节它的分泌节奏。Between the epiphysis and the shaft of a long bone lies a layer of hyaline cartilage called the epiphyseal plate. Chondrocytes in the plate keep dividing and enlarging, while the side nearer the shaft is gradually replaced by mineralized bone tissue, so the bone lengthens along its long axis. Growth hormone, secreted by the anterior pituitary, is an anabolic hormone that promotes protein synthesis in bone and muscle. Through liver-derived insulin-like growth factor 1, or IGF-1, it amplifies the stimulus on cartilage cells. OpenStax physiology textbooks list growth hormone as the key pituitary hormone for growth, with the hypothalamus balancing it through growth hormone-releasing hormone and somatostatin.[1][2]

生长激素的分泌并非全天均匀。它在夜间深睡眠时分泌脉冲更明显，运动、空腹和低强度应激也会促进释放；儿童和青少年时期分泌量高于成年后。进入青春期，睾丸和卵巢分泌的性激素在前期加速骨骼生长，形成身高突增，男孩女孩都能在青春期的两三年内获得成年身高的相当一部分；OpenStax 骨组织章节指出，性激素同时促进成骨细胞活动和骨基质合成，并在生长后期推动骨骺板闭合。Growth hormone is not released evenly through the day. Its pulses are larger during deep sleep at night, and exercise, fasting and mild stress also promote release; secretion stays higher in children and adolescents than in adults. At puberty, sex hormones from the testes and ovaries first accelerate bone growth and produce the height spurt, and both boys and girls gain a substantial part of their adult height within a couple of years. The OpenStax bone tissue chapter notes that sex hormones also support osteoblast activity and matrix production, and later drive closure of the epiphyseal plates.[2][3]

可核对的临床线索也支持这条链条。儿童期生长激素分泌不足会导致身材明显矮小；骨骺是否闭合、骨龄处于什么阶段，是儿科判断还能否长高的依据。成年后的身高变化主要来自椎间盘厚度、姿势和脊柱曲度，而不是长骨继续延长。遗传决定身高的大部分个体差异，营养、睡眠和慢性疾病则决定这条遗传轨道能走多远。Clinical evidence supports this chain. Childhood growth hormone deficiency leads to markedly short stature, and whether the growth plates have closed, together with skeletal age, is how pediatricians judge whether further height gain is possible. Height changes after adulthood come mainly from disc thickness, posture and spinal curvature, not from continued long-bone lengthening. Genetics accounts for most individual differences in height, while nutrition, sleep and chronic disease decide how far that genetic trajectory is realized.[3]

边界要写清：骨骺闭合之后，补钙、增高补品或额外补充生长激素都不能再把长骨拉长，滥用激素反而带来代谢风险。生长激素调控的是组织合成与骨代谢，并不是“睡得越高就长得越高”的单向开关；成年后它的作用转向维持肌肉量、骨密度和脂肪分布。另外，这里讨论的是人类长骨的纵向生长，鱼类、两栖类的骨骼终身持续生长，机制并不相同。The boundary matters: after the plates close, calcium supplements, height products or extra growth hormone cannot lengthen the long bones again, and misusing hormones carries metabolic risks. Growth hormone regulates tissue synthesis and bone metabolism; it is not a one-way switch in which more sleep automatically means more height. In adults its role shifts toward maintaining muscle mass, bone density and fat distribution. This chapter describes longitudinal growth of the human long bone; fish and amphibians keep growing throughout life, under a different mechanism.[3]

02人体为什么会衰老？细胞层面到底发生了什么？Why does the body age, and what happens at the cellular level?

分子损伤累积使部分细胞进入衰老停滞状态，堆积后释放炎症因子，逐步拉低组织储备。Accumulated damage pushes some cells into senescent arrest; they pile up and release inflammatory factors that gradually lower tissue reserve.

衰老不是某个器官单纯“用旧了”，而是分子损伤持续累积、细胞修复能力下降和衰老细胞在组织中堆积共同造成的结果。从细胞层面看，随着年龄增长，一部分细胞停止分裂却没有按程序死亡，它们留在组织里释放炎症信号，周围健康细胞随之受到影响。理解衰老，需要把损伤来源、细胞状态和组织后果分层看。Aging is not simply one organ wearing out. It results from the steady accumulation of molecular damage, declining repair capacity and the buildup of senescent cells in tissues. At the cellular level, some cells stop dividing with age but do not die as programmed. They remain in place and release inflammatory signals that affect neighboring healthy cells. To understand aging, damage sources, cell state and tissue consequences have to be read as separate layers.[4]

DNA 复制差错、活性氧造成的氧化损伤、端粒随分裂逐渐缩短，以及蛋白质和线粒体功能的退化，都会随时间叠加。当损伤超过修复系统的承受范围，细胞可以进入一种称为细胞衰老的状态：周期停滞、不再增殖，但仍然存活。美国国家老龄化研究所（NIA）把这类细胞形容为“像一只发霉的果子弄脏整碗水果”，数量不多却能通过释放炎症相关的化学物质波及邻近细胞。衰老细胞分泌的这一组因子常被称为衰老相关分泌表型，简称 SASP。Copying errors in DNA, oxidative damage from reactive oxygen species, telomere shortening with each division, and the decline of protein and mitochondrial function all pile up over time. When damage outruns repair capacity, a cell can enter a state called cellular senescence: its cycle stalls, it no longer proliferates, but it stays alive. The U.S. National Institute on Aging compares these cells to one moldy piece of fruit corrupting the whole bowl: a small number can spread inflammation through neighboring cells. The factors they secrete are collectively called the senescence-associated secretory phenotype, or SASP.[4]

不同组织的表现可以互相印证。皮肤成纤维细胞和免疫细胞的衰老标志随年龄增多，组织干细胞的自我更新能力下降；伤口愈合变慢、肌肉力量衰退、器官储备缩小，都与细胞层面的停滞和炎症有关。2018 年发表于《自然·医学》的小鼠研究由 NIA 资助：用选择性清除衰老细胞的化合物处理自然衰老小鼠，其健康寿命和总寿命都得到延长。这说明衰老细胞的累积与功能衰退之间存在可检验的因果线索，但证据目前主要来自动物模型。Tissue-level observations reinforce the picture. Senescence markers in skin fibroblasts and immune cells rise with age, and the self-renewal of tissue stem cells falls. Slower wound healing, weaker muscle and shrinking organ reserves all relate to this stall and its inflammation. A 2018 Nature Medicine study supported by NIA treated naturally aging mice with compounds that selectively clear senescent cells, extending both health span and lifespan. This links senescent-cell accumulation to functional decline, but the evidence so far comes mainly from animal models.[4][5]

组织层面的后果是储备普遍下降。心脏、肺、肾脏和骨骼肌在静息状态下仍能维持日常需求，但应对高强度运动、感染或外伤的余量变小；神经组织中不可完全替换的神经元减少，认知和反应速度随之改变。皮肤变薄、弹性下降、毛发色素减少，也是同一批衰老过程在体表的可见表现。The tissue-level consequence is a general drop in reserve. The heart, lungs, kidneys and skeletal muscle still meet daily demands at rest, but their margin for intense exercise, infection or injury shrinks. In neural tissue, irreplaceable neurons are lost, and cognition and reaction time change accordingly. Thinner skin, reduced elasticity and loss of hair pigment are the visible signs of the same aging process on the body surface.[3][4]

边界要写清：衰老速度个体差异很大，遗传、饮食、运动和睡眠都会改变这条曲线，但目前没有证据表明任何补品能逆转细胞衰老。小鼠中清除衰老细胞的结论不能直接搬到人身上，相关药物仍在临床试验阶段。还要区分“衰老”和“老年疾病”：退行性疾病有各自的病理过程，不能把所有老年症状都归因于细胞衰老这一条机制。The boundary matters: the pace of aging varies widely between individuals, and genetics, diet, exercise and sleep shift the curve, but no supplement has been shown to reverse cellular aging. Results from clearing senescent cells in mice cannot be transferred directly to people, and the relevant drugs remain in clinical trials. Aging also differs from age-related disease: degenerative illnesses have their own pathology, and not every symptom of old age can be blamed on cellular senescence alone.[4][5]

03为什么没有能活上千年的动物？寿命上限由什么决定？Why is there no animal that lives for a thousand years, and what sets the lifespan ceiling?

寿命受繁殖策略与修复投入约束，损伤累积追上修复能力后个体走向衰老；哺乳动物上限多在百年量级。Lifespan is constrained by reproductive strategy and repair investment; once damage outruns repair the individual ages, and mammalian ceilings sit around a century.

地球上没有能活上千年的哺乳动物，现存物种的寿命大多落在几年到两百多年这个区间。寿命上限不是“活得小心就能无限延长”，而是由繁殖策略、身体修复能力和代谢模式共同约束的：一个物种在进化中分配给生长、繁殖和损伤修复的资源有限，修复跟不上损伤累积，个体就会走向衰老和死亡。No mammal on Earth lives for a thousand years; existing species mostly range from a few years to a little over two centuries. The lifespan ceiling is not something care alone can lift indefinitely. It is constrained jointly by reproductive strategy, body repair capacity and metabolic pattern. A species has limited resources to split among growth, reproduction and damage repair, and when repair falls behind damage accumulation, the individual moves toward aging and death.[6]

按一次性体细胞理论，生物体的能量需要在繁殖和维护之间分配。晚熟、繁殖慢的大型动物通常投入更多资源维持体细胞，寿命更长；小型啮齿类繁殖早、代谢快，把资源优先传给后代，自身维护投入较少。体型、代谢速率、捕食压力和世代时间都参与塑造物种的寿命范围，而不是某一个器官决定“能活多久”。Under the disposable soma theory, an organism allocates energy between reproduction and maintenance. Large, late-maturing animals usually invest more in keeping somatic cells alive and live longer; small rodents breed early and metabolize fast, put resources into offspring and spend less on self-maintenance. Body size, metabolic rate, predation pressure and generation time all shape a species' lifespan range; no single organ decides how long an individual lives.[6]

数据库里的物种寿命可以核对。人类有记录的最长寿者约 122 岁，多数国家的预期寿命在七十到八十岁区间；家鼠平均寿命约两到三年；家犬约十到十三年；加拉帕戈斯象龟记录到百年以上；弓头鲸有超过 200 岁的个体。人类衰老基因组资源数据库（AnAge）汇总各物种的最大寿命记录，可以按物种逐项查证。这些数字是物种内的观测上限，不是每个个体都能达到的目标。Species lifespans in databases can be checked. The longest recorded human lived about 122 years, while life expectancy in most countries lies in the seventies to eighties. A house mouse lives roughly two to three years, a dog about ten to thirteen years, a Galápagos tortoise over a hundred, and some bowhead whales beyond 200 years. The Human Ageing Genomic Resources database, AnAge, compiles maximum longevity records by species. These are observed upper bounds within a species, not targets every individual reaches.[6]

为什么修复不能一直维持身体？体细胞每一次分裂都有误差累积，端粒和干细胞储备有限；心肌和神经等组织缺少可大量替换的细胞，一旦衰退难以整体翻新。损伤分子随时间堆积，免疫系统清除异常细胞的能力下降，心血管和神经系统往往先出现功能约束。也就是说，寿命上限是多个组织系统先后到达各自修复极限的综合结果。Why can repair not keep the body going forever? Each somatic cell division carries accumulated error, and telomere and stem-cell reserves are finite. Tissues such as heart muscle and nerve lack cells that can be replaced in bulk, so once they decline they cannot be renewed wholesale. Damaged molecules pile up over time, immune clearance of abnormal cells weakens, and the cardiovascular and nervous systems often reach functional limits first. The lifespan ceiling is therefore the combined result of several systems arriving at their own repair limits in sequence.[4][5]

边界要写清：物种最大寿命不等于平均预期寿命，多数个体活不到记录上限。缓步动物、某些深海生物和长寿树木的特殊策略并不推翻哺乳动物的框架；讨论人类寿命时，还要把“延长寿命”和“延长健康寿命”分开——减少带病存活的年份，比单纯推迟死亡更接近现有研究的目标。The boundary: maximum species lifespan is not average life expectancy, and most individuals never reach the recorded upper bound. Special strategies of tardigrades, certain deep-sea organisms and long-lived trees do not overturn the mammalian framework. In humans, extending lifespan and extending health span are different goals; cutting years lived with illness matters more than merely postponing death in the current research record.[5][6]
