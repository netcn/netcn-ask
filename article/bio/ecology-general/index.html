01发霉的面包上那些绿毛是什么？What are the green fuzz patches on moldy bread?

可见霉斑是霉菌菌丝体，真菌以几丁质菌丝分解有机物，靠空气传播孢子繁殖。Visible patches are fungal mycelium; molds decompose matter through chitinous hyphae and spread by airborne spores.

面包、水果或潮湿墙面长出的绿毛、黑毛，肉眼可见的部分是霉菌的菌丝集合体，也就是菌丝体；霉菌属于真菌，既不是植物，也不是细菌。它没有叶绿素，不能靠光合作用制造有机物，而是分泌酶分解基质中的有机物，再吸收养分生长。日常见到的霉斑，只是真菌在食物或墙面这一小片基质上长出的可见结构。The green or black fuzz on bread, fruit or damp walls is a visible mycelium, the mass of fungal hyphae; mold belongs to the fungi, neither plant nor bacterium. It has no chlorophyll and cannot photosynthesize, so it secretes enzymes to break down organic matter in its substrate and absorb the nutrients. The visible patch on food or wall is only the structure grown on that small substrate.[1]

结构上，霉菌由大量管状菌丝组成，菌丝有分隔或无分隔，细胞壁主要成分为几丁质。单根菌丝直径通常仅数微米，肉眼不可见，只有大量菌丝交织成束、形成菌丝体后才呈现为毛状斑块。菌丝在基质表面和内部蔓延交织，形成肉眼可见的霉斑；生长后期在直立菌丝顶端形成孢子囊或分生孢子，呈现绿、蓝、灰黑等颜色。孢子轻而干燥，对干燥和温度变化耐受较强，可在环境中长期悬浮，随空气传播，落到新的基质上萌发成新的菌丝网络。Structurally, molds are made of tubular hyphae, septate or non-septate, with chitin as the main cell-wall material. Hyphae spread over and inside the substrate to form visible patches; later, upright hyphae bear sporangia or conidia in green, blue or grey-black. Spores are light and dry, travel on air, and germinate into a new hyphal network on a fresh substrate.[1]

在生态系统中，霉菌和其他真菌是重要分解者，能分解纤维素、木质素等其他生物难利用的有机物，把元素归还环境。真菌不能进行光合作用，营养方式为吸收型：菌丝分泌胞外酶把大分子切成可溶小分子，再透过细胞壁吸收。常见类群包括青霉和曲霉：青霉是青霉素的来源，部分曲霉则产生黄曲霉毒素等霉菌毒素。真菌与细菌共同承担残体分解，使碳、氮等元素重新进入循环。In ecosystems, molds and other fungi are key decomposers, breaking down cellulose and lignin that few organisms can use and returning elements to the environment. Common groups include Penicillium, source of penicillin, and Aspergillus, some species of which produce mycotoxins such as aflatoxin. Fungi and bacteria together decompose remains and return carbon, nitrogen and other elements to circulation.[2][5]

霉菌生长需要有机物、适宜温度和较高水分活度；干燥、冷藏和密封降低水分活度可以抑制生长。普通加热能杀灭菌丝营养体，但孢子和部分耐热毒素可能残留，因此明显发霉的食物不建议切除霉斑后继续食用，菌丝和毒素可能已经扩散到肉眼看不见的部分。Mold growth needs organic matter, suitable temperature and high water activity; drying, refrigeration and sealing lower water activity and inhibit it. Ordinary heating kills vegetative hyphae, but spores and some heat-stable toxins may remain, so visibly moldy food should not be trimmed and eaten; hyphae and toxins may have spread beyond what the eye can see.[1][2]

边界要写清：霉菌不等于全部真菌：酵母菌是单细胞真菌，蘑菇、木耳是大型真菌，结构与生活史不同。这里讲霉菌的结构、营养方式与生态角色；发霉引起的过敏、呼吸道刺激和食物中毒属于临床与食品安全话题，不在此展开。Boundary: mold is not the whole fungus kingdom: yeasts are single-celled fungi, and mushrooms and wood ears are large fungi with different structure and life history. This chapter covers mold structure, nutrition and ecological role; allergy, respiratory irritation and food poisoning are clinical and food-safety topics outside it.[1]

02细菌是什么？它和真菌、病毒、古菌怎样区分？What is a bacterium, and how does it differ from fungi, viruses and archaea?

细菌是无成形细胞核的原核单细胞，肽聚糖壁；按细胞结构与真菌、病毒、古菌区分。Bacteria are prokaryotic single cells without a true nucleus and with a peptidoglycan wall, distinguished from fungi, viruses and archaea by cell structure.

细菌是原核单细胞生物：细胞内没有成形的细胞核，遗传物质以环状 DNA 集中在拟核区域，也没有线粒体、叶绿体等膜包细胞器。典型细菌长约 1–10 µm，光学显微镜染色后可见三种基本形态：球形的球菌、杆状的杆菌和弯曲或螺旋状的螺旋菌。细菌分布极广，土壤、水体、空气和动植物体表体内都有定植，多数种类需在适宜温度和水分条件下才有活跃代谢。Bacteria are prokaryotic single-celled organisms: there is no true nucleus, genetic material lies as circular DNA in a nucleoid region, and membrane-bound organelles such as mitochondria or chloroplasts are absent. Typical cells are 1–10 µm, stained and viewed under the light microscope as spherical cocci, rod-shaped bacilli or curved spirilla.[3]

基本结构包括细胞壁、细胞膜、细胞质和核糖体；多数细菌细胞壁含肽聚糖，革兰氏染色据此分为阳性与阴性。部分细菌还有鞭毛用于运动、菌毛用于附着、荚膜抵抗吞噬；小型环状 DNA 为质粒，常携带抗生素抗性基因，可在细菌间转移。Basic structures include cell wall, membrane, cytoplasm and ribosomes; most walls contain peptidoglycan, and Gram staining separates Gram-positive from Gram-negative types. Some bacteria add flagella for movement, pili for attachment and a capsule against phagocytosis; small circular plasmids often carry antibiotic-resistance genes that can transfer between bacteria.[3]

与其他微生物区分要看细胞层次。真菌是真核生物，有细胞核和几丁质细胞壁，个体通常更大；病毒没有细胞结构，只由核酸和蛋白外壳组成，必须进入宿主细胞才能复制；古菌外形与细菌相似，但细胞壁成分、膜脂和代谢通路不同，多生活在高温、高盐等极端环境。分类学上细菌与古菌同属原核生物，但 16S rRNA 序列和细胞膜结构差异足以把它们分为两个独立的域。Distinguishing from other microbes depends on cellular level. Fungi are eukaryotes with a nucleus and chitin wall, usually larger; viruses have no cell structure, only nucleic acid and a protein coat, and replicate only inside host cells; archaea resemble bacteria externally but differ in wall composition, membrane lipids and metabolism, often living in hot or salty extreme environments.[1][3]

细菌通过二分裂繁殖，速度快，条件适宜时数量可在短时间内倍增；分裂时 DNA 复制后均分到两个子细胞，群体增长呈指数形式，受营养供应和代谢废物积累限制。在生态与人体中，多数细菌是分解者或共生者：土壤细菌参与固氮和物质循环，人体肠道定植大量共生菌协助消化和合成维生素；只有少数种类是病原菌，是否致病还取决于入侵部位和宿主状态。Bacteria are widespread in soil, water, air and on and inside animals and plants, most active under suitable temperature and moisture. They reproduce by binary fission and multiply fast, doubling numbers quickly under favorable conditions. In ecosystems and the body, most are decomposers or symbionts: soil bacteria fix nitrogen and cycle matter, and gut microbiota aid digestion and vitamin synthesis; only a few species are pathogenic, and disease also depends on entry site and host state.[4]

边界要写清：70% 左右的酒精主要使蛋白质变性并破坏细胞膜，对细菌繁殖体有效，对细菌芽孢和无包膜病毒作用有限；酒精浓度过高会使表面蛋白快速凝固、阻碍渗透，效果反而下降。细菌并非都有害，评估微生物时要区分种类、定植部位和致病条件，不能把显微镜下见到的细菌一概视为感染。Boundary: around 70% alcohol denatures proteins and disrupts membranes, effective against vegetative bacteria but limited against spores and non-enveloped viruses; overly high concentration coagulates surface protein fast and blocks penetration, working worse. Bacteria are not all harmful; judging a microbe means separating species, colonization site and conditions of pathogenicity, and bacteria seen under a microscope should not be treated automatically as infection.[3]

03什么是生态系统？生物与环境怎样组成整体？What is an ecosystem, and how do organisms and environment form a whole?

生态系统是生物群落与非生物环境相互作用的整体；能量沿食物链单向递减，物质在生物与环境间循环。An ecosystem is a community interacting with its environment; energy declines one way along food chains while matter cycles between organisms and environment.

生态系统是一定空间内生物群落与非生物环境相互作用构成的整体。生物成分包括生产者（绿色植物、藻类等自养生物）、消费者（动物等异养生物）和分解者（真菌、细菌等）；非生物成分包括水、空气、光、温度、土壤和矿质元素。系统内各成分通过营养关系、气体交换和物质循环彼此联系，单独抽出某一部分就不再是完整生态系统。划分生态系统范围是为了研究方便，一个池塘、一片森林都可以作为独立系统分析。An ecosystem is the whole of a biological community interacting with its nonliving environment within a defined space. Biotic components include producers (autotrophs such as plants and algae), consumers (heterotrophs such as animals) and decomposers (fungi, bacteria); abiotic components include water, air, light, temperature, soil and mineral elements. Its boundary is drawn for research convenience: a pond or a forest can each be analyzed as a separate system.[5]

能量流动从生产者固定太阳能开始，经光合作用变成化学能，再沿食物链和食物网传给各级消费者。照射到地表的太阳能只有约 1%–2% 被生产者固定为有机物，其余以热反射或散失。能量在相邻营养级之间的传递效率大约只有 10% 上下，其余以呼吸热散失；因此食物链通常不超过四五个环节，顶级消费者数量有限，体型也往往更大。Energy flow begins when producers fix solar energy into chemical matter by photosynthesis, then pass it along food chains and webs to consumers at each level. Transfer efficiency between adjacent trophic levels is only around 10%, with the rest lost as respiratory heat; food chains therefore rarely exceed four or five links, and top consumers stay few in number, often with larger body size.[6]

物质循环与能量流动并行：碳、氮、水等元素在生物与无机环境之间循环往复，分解者把动植物残体中的有机物分解回无机态，生产者再吸收利用。碳以二氧化碳、有机物和碳酸盐等形态在大气、水体与生物间转换；氮经固氮、硝化、反硝化完成循环。物质不会沿食物链单向流失，而是在系统内反复周转，从环境进入生物，再回到环境。Matter cycles alongside energy flow: carbon, nitrogen and water circulate between organisms and the inorganic environment, and decomposers break down remains back to inorganic forms that producers reuse. Matter does not drain away one-way along the chain but turns over within the system, moving from environment into organisms and back.[4][5]

生态系统依靠物种多样性和反馈调节缓冲外界扰动：食物网越复杂，一条食物链受破坏后其他路径越容易替代；环境容量限制种群规模，出生率与死亡率、迁入迁出共同决定种群数量。外来物种入侵或关键种消失可能打破原有反馈，使食物网结构发生不可逆改变。物种组成改变后，系统会在新的结构上重新达到动态平衡。Ecosystems buffer disturbance through species diversity and feedback regulation: the more complex the food web, the easier other paths substitute when one chain breaks. Carrying capacity limits population size, and birth, death, immigration and emigration together determine population numbers. After species composition changes, the system reaches a new dynamic balance on a different structure.[5]

边界要写清：生态系统的边界按研究需要划分，小到一潭积水、大到整个生物圈。能量单向流动、逐级递减，不能循环；物质守恒、反复循环。人类活动可以改变输入输出和物种组成，但不改变能量递减与物质循环的基本规律。Boundary: ecosystem boundaries are drawn for the question at hand, from a small puddle to the whole biosphere. Energy flows one way and declines stepwise; it cannot recycle. Matter is conserved and cycles repeatedly. Human activity can alter inputs, outputs and species composition, but does not change the basic rules of energy decline and matter cycling.[6]
