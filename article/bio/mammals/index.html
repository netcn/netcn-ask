01斑马为什么长满条纹？条纹是伪装、驱虫还是干扰捕食者判断？Why do zebras have stripes, and do they serve camouflage, insect defense or predator confusion?

条纹主要干扰吸血昆虫定位并产生运动眩目，静态隐蔽作用有限。Stripes mainly disrupt biting-insect targeting and motion dazzle; static concealment is limited.

斑马的黑白条纹不是捉迷藏式的伪装，而是多项功能叠加的性状：干扰叮咬昆虫的定位、破坏运动中的轮廓识别，并在社群中辅助个体识别。条纹在绿色草原背景上并不提供传统意义上的隐蔽，它的主要作用指向昆虫防御和视觉混淆，而不是与环境融为一体。把条纹理解为静态保护色，忽略了条纹在昆虫视觉和运动视觉中的特殊效果。A zebra's black-and-white stripes are not hide-and-seek camouflage; they are a trait with several overlapping functions: disrupting the targeting of biting insects, breaking up the outline during motion, and helping individuals recognize one another in the herd. Against green savanna the stripes do not offer conventional concealment, so their main roles point toward insect defense and visual confusion rather than blending into the surroundings.[1][3]

采采蝇和牛虻等吸血昆虫需要在偏振光和运动目标上定位落点；深色与浅色交替的条纹会干扰昆虫对连续表面的判断，降低落虫率。Larison 等人 2015 年在 PLoS ONE 发表的比较研究检查了条纹长度、密度与蝇类骚扰之间的关系，支持条纹与驱虫相关的假说，条纹越明显的个体受蝇类骚扰的模式存在差异。实验中纯色马匹比条纹标本更容易吸引蝇类落停，这一差别在近距离和低速运动时尤其明显。Biting flies such as tsetse flies and horseflies locate landing targets using polarized light and moving surfaces. Alternating dark and light stripes interfere with the insects' judgment of a continuous surface and reduce landing rates. A 2015 comparative study by Larison and colleagues in PLoS ONE examined how stripe length and density relate to fly harassment, supporting the fly-repulsion hypothesis and showing that harassment patterns differ where striping is more marked.[3]

斑马奔跑时，交替条纹在观察者眼中产生运动眩目效果，使捕食者难以判断个体边界、速度和方向；多只斑马同时移动时，轮廓互相重叠，追踪单个目标更困难。这一作用依赖群体运动，静止单只斑马的条纹混淆效果有限，条纹图案的动态效果要在移动中才充分显现。捕食者在高速追逐中需要快速锁定目标，条纹造成的轮廓抖动会延长判断时间。When zebras run, alternating stripes produce a motion-dazzle effect that makes it hard for predators to read an individual's boundary, speed and direction. As several zebras move together their outlines overlap, making a single target harder to track. This effect relies on group motion; a single standing zebra shows little confusion, and the dynamic effect of the stripes becomes apparent mainly while moving.[1][6]

每只斑马的条纹图案类似指纹，具有个体特异性，同类可以据此辨认彼此；在高密度社群中，条纹也可能辅助母幼识别和等级交流。条纹的形成与黑色素沉积的发育调控有关，胚胎期条纹模式逐步定型，不同部位条纹粗细随身体轮廓调整。幼斑马出生后条纹随身体生长按比例延展，图案基本保持不变。Each zebra's stripe pattern is as individual as a fingerprint, so conspecifics can recognize one another; in dense herds stripes may also help mothers and young identify each other and signal social status. Stripe formation is tied to developmental regulation of melanin deposition, the pattern setting up gradually in the embryo, and stripe width adjusts along the body contour.[1]

条纹各假说的权重仍有争议，驱虫证据较强，运动混淆和社交识别多为合理推断；不同斑马物种的条纹粗细、疏密与栖息地和牲畜压力相关，不能用单一功能解释全部变异。把条纹说成草原保护色，混淆了静态隐蔽与动态视觉干扰两种不同机制。未来还需要在不同地区和物种间继续比较落虫率、捕食成功率和社群识别数据。The weight of evidence for each hypothesis remains debated: insect defense has the stronger support, while motion confusion and social recognition are reasonable inferences. Stripe width and spacing vary across zebra species with habitat and livestock pressure, so no single function explains all variation. Calling stripes savanna camouflage conflates static concealment with dynamic visual disruption.[3][6]

02大象的耳朵为什么那么大？长鼻由什么构成，承担哪些功能？Why are elephant ears so large, what is the trunk made of, and what does it do?

大耳散热并收集低频声；无骨长鼻由约 4 万块肌肉完成嗅探、抓取与吸水。Large ears dissipate heat and pick up low sound; the boneless trunk uses about 40,000 muscles to smell, grasp and draw water.

非洲象的大耳朵首先是散热器官，其次承担声音收集和视觉交流功能；长鼻则是由肌肉构成的灵活取食与感知器官。耳朵尺寸与体型、气候相关，热带大型象靠增大耳廓面积扩大散热表面，大耳是对大型体型热负荷的补偿结构。象长期生活在高温开阔生境中，体温调节对器官大小的选择压力很直接。The African elephant's large ears are first a heat-dissipation organ, and second a sound collector and visual signaler; the trunk is a flexible feeding and sensing organ built from muscle. Ear size relates to body size and climate: large tropical elephants enlarge their ear surface to dump heat, so big ears compensate for the heat load of a large body.[2][4]

耳廓皮肤薄、血管表浅，血液流经时把热量散到空气中；象可以扇动耳朵加速空气流过皮肤，进一步降温。非洲象耳廓可长达约 1 米以上，背面皮肤褶皱增加表面积；象耳还能接收低频声波，并通过耳朵姿态表达情绪，耳朵既是散热器也是感觉和交流工具。扇耳动作形成的气流同时吹走体表昆虫，减少叮咬干扰。The pinna skin is thin with superficial blood vessels, so blood flowing through releases heat to the air; elephants flap their ears to drive air across the skin and cool further. African elephant ears can exceed about one meter in length, and folds on the back increase the surface area. The ears also receive low-frequency sound, and ear posture expresses mood, so they serve as radiator, sense organ and communication tool at once.[2][4]

象鼻没有骨骼，由约 4 万块肌肉和结缔组织构成，兼具嗅探、抓取、吸水和喷水功能。鼻尖的指状突起可以捡起细小物体；鼻腔内的嗅觉感受器数量多，使象能远距离定位水源和同类气味，鼻子同时是四肢、手和鼻子的合并器官。吸水时鼻腔作为储水腔，再喷入口中或洒在身上降温，动作由精细肌肉序列完成。The trunk has no bone and is built from about 40,000 muscles and connective tissues, serving smelling, grasping, sucking up water and spraying. A finger-like projection at the tip can pick up small objects; many olfactory receptors let elephants locate water and conspecific scent over long distances, making the trunk a combined limb, hand and nose.[4]

ADW 对非洲象的记录把大耳、长鼻和厚皮列为关键识别特征；象群通过低频鸣声和触觉保持联系。体型大的动物相对表面积小、散热困难，大耳和扇耳动作帮助把体热带走；厚皮则在干燥环境中减少水分流失，皮肤褶皱还能留住泥沙防晒。象群活动范围广，远距离通讯依赖可传播数公里的低频声；象还会用鼻端拾取石块和枝条改造活动区域。The Animal Diversity Web lists big ears, long trunk and thick skin as key identifying features of the African elephant, and herds keep in touch through low-frequency calls and touch. Large animals have less surface area relative to volume and lose heat with difficulty, so big ears and ear flapping carry body heat away; thick skin reduces water loss in dry conditions, and skin folds hold mud for sun protection.[1][2]

耳朵大小同时受遗传、性别和温度影响，不能只按气候判断；象鼻的精细操作依赖多组肌肉协调，不是单一器官完成所有动作。非洲象与亚洲象在耳廓大小、鼻端突起数量上有差别，比较耳朵和鼻子功能时要先区分物种，避免把一类象的特征套到另一类上。体型、年龄和饮食状况也会改变耳朵和鼻子的使用方式与活动节奏。Ear size is shaped by genetics, sex and temperature together, so climate alone cannot explain it; fine trunk manipulation relies on coordinated muscle groups rather than one organ doing everything. African and Asian elephants differ in ear size and the number of trunk-tip projections, so any comparison of ear and trunk function should start by separating the species.[1][4]

03长臂猿为什么是“空中飞人”？鼯鼠真的会飞吗，高原和半水生哺乳动物怎样适应环境？Why are gibbons brachiators, do flying squirrels really fly, and how do plateau and semi-aquatic mammals adapt?

臂行、皮膜滑翔、高原氧适应和头顶五官各是一套结构与生理组合。Brachiation, membrane gliding, plateau oxygen adaptation and top-set senses each form a structural-physiological package.

哺乳动物在不同生境中发展出臂行、滑翔、高海拔耐受和半水生等运动与生存方式。长臂猿用手臂在树间摆荡，鼯鼠靠皮膜滑翔，牦牛适应高原低温低氧，河马把眼耳鼻孔移到头顶，各对应一套骨骼、肌肉和生理改造，运动方式与栖息地资源长期配合。同一纲内部在不同环境压力下走出差别很大的适应路径。Mammals in different habitats evolved arm-swinging, gliding, high-altitude tolerance and semi-aquatic ways of living. Gibbons brachiate between branches, flying squirrels glide on skin membranes, yaks cope with cold low-oxygen plateaus, and hippos carry eyes, ears and nostrils on top of the head; each matches a package of skeletal, muscular and physiological remodeling, with locomotion tuned to habitat resources over time.[1][6]

长臂猿是典型臂行动物，上肢比下肢长，腕部灵活，能够一手接一手地在树枝间摆荡前进；肩部和胸部肌肉为悬挂发力提供附着点，这种方式在树冠层节省下到地面再重新上树的移动成本，长臂猿由此成为林冠中的高效移动者。摆荡时身体借助重力加速，长上肢扩大每次抓握的跨度，减少中途换枝的停顿，手腕旋转以适应不同粗细的枝条。Gibbons are classic brachiators: the arms are longer than the legs, the wrist is flexible, and they swing hand over hand through the branches. Shoulder and chest muscles anchor the hanging forces, and this mode saves the cost of dropping to the ground and climbing back up through the canopy, making gibbons efficient movers in the treetops.[1]

鼯鼠前后肢间连接皮膜，从高处展开滑翔，不是靠翅膀主动飞行；它靠空气升力延长跳跃距离，落地前调整皮膜角度和姿态。牦牛生活在海拔约 4000 到 6000 米的高原，血红蛋白对氧亲和力较高，心肺功能和厚毛适应低温低氧环境，食物稀缺时靠粗饲草维持能量。滑翔过程中皮膜同时起到稳定舵的作用，落地后四肢缓冲冲击。A flying squirrel stretches a membrane between fore and hind limbs and glides from height rather than flying with wings; aerodynamic lift extends the jump, and the membrane angle and posture are adjusted before landing. Yaks live on plateaus around 4,000 to 6,000 meters where hemoglobin has a relatively high oxygen affinity; heart, lungs and thick fur adapt to cold and low oxygen, and coarse forage supplies energy when food is scarce.[1][2]

河马眼、耳、鼻孔都位于头顶，身体浸在水中时仍能观察、呼吸和听声；鼻孔可在潜水时关闭，眼睛和耳朵上移减少出水暴露。半水生生活同时兼顾降温、防晒伤和陆地取食，河马白天待在水中，夜间上岸觅食，水陆之间的节律固定。皮肤缺少厚毛，长时间离水容易干裂，浸水同时保护皮肤。A hippo's eyes, ears and nostrils sit on top of the head, so it can watch, breathe and hear while the body is submerged. The nostrils close underwater, and the raised eyes and ears minimize exposure above the surface. The semi-aquatic life balances cooling, sun protection and land feeding: hippos stay in water by day and come ashore at night to graze on a fixed water-land rhythm.[1][6]

这些适应是结构、生理与行为的组合，不能只凭一项外形判断运动方式。滑翔不产生主动升力，和蝙蝠、鸟类的拍翼飞行不同；高原耐受涉及基因、呼吸和代谢多个层面，厚毛只是其中一环。把皮膜滑翔称作飞翔，或把厚毛等同于高原适应，都遗漏了配套的骨骼、肌肉和生理改造。比较不同物种时，还需结合食性、天敌和活动时间综合判断。These adaptations combine structure, physiology and behavior, so one external feature alone cannot identify a locomotor mode. Gliding produces no active lift and differs from the flapping flight of bats and birds; high-altitude tolerance involves genes, breathing and metabolism together, with thick fur only one part. Calling membrane gliding flight, or equating thick fur alone with plateau adaptation, misses the supporting skeletal, muscular and physiological changes.[2][6]

04海洋哺乳动物为什么长水平尾鳍？蓝鲸能长到多大，是否接近体型上限？Why do marine mammals have horizontal flukes, how big do blue whales get, and are they near a size limit?

水平尾鳍配合上下摆动；蓝鲸可超 30 米、150–190 吨，体型仍受食物与骨骼约束。Horizontal flukes drive up-and-down strokes; blue whales exceed 30 m and 150–190 t, still bounded by food and bone.

鲸豚等海洋哺乳动物由陆地祖先返回水中，尾部演化为水平向尾鳍，与鱼类的垂直尾鳍方向不同；蓝鲸是已知体型最大的动物，成年个体可超过 30 米、体重约 150 到 190 吨，是哺乳类二次入水后大型化的极端案例。水平与垂直方向的差别，来自两类祖先躯干和肌肉发力方式的不同。Cetaceans and other marine mammals returned to water from land ancestors, and their tails evolved into horizontal flukes, oriented differently from the vertical tails of fish. The blue whale is the largest animal known, with adults over 30 meters and about 150 to 190 tonnes; it marks an extreme of enlargement after mammals secondarily entered the water.[1][5]

水平尾鳍配合上下摆动推进，适合哺乳动物水平躯干的肌肉发力方式；后肢退化，前肢变成鳍状，身体流线化，鼻孔移到头顶形成喷气孔。返回水中的演化在不同哺乳类群中多次独立发生，属于趋同适应，各群体保留的陆地祖先痕迹程度不同。海豹、海牛和鲸类分别在不同时期入水，改造路径并不完全一致，皮下脂肪层在冷水中减少热量散失。The horizontal fluke drives propulsion through up-and-down strokes, matching the muscle forces of a mammal's horizontal trunk; hind limbs are reduced, forelimbs become flippers, the body streamlines, and the nostrils move to the top as a blowhole. The return to water happened independently in several mammal groups, a case of convergent adaptation, with varying traces retained from land ancestors.[1][6]

水生环境提供浮力，支持骨骼和器官承受更大体重；冷水中大型动物相对表面积小、散热较慢，有利于维持体温。NOAA 渔业记录的蓝鲸体型数据显示，滤食磷虾的生活方式支撑了超大能量摄入，但体型仍受食物供给、骨骼强度和浮力限制，大型化并不是无限度的。磷虾群的季节分布和密度，直接约束蓝鲸能够积累的能量储备。Water buoyancy supports bones and organs under greater body weight; in cold water large animals have less surface area relative to volume and lose heat more slowly, which helps maintain body temperature. NOAA Fisheries records of blue whale size show that filtering krill supplies the huge energy intake, but body size remains bounded by food supply, bone strength and buoyancy; enlargement is not unlimited.[2][5]

化石序列显示鲸类祖先接近约 5000 万年前的巴基斯坦鲸一类陆生偶蹄动物，踝骨、牙齿和内耳结构把鲸类与偶蹄目联系起来；后代逐步过渡到浅水环境，再进入开阔海洋，四肢、骨盆和鼻腔位置随入水程度持续改造。化石连续记录了耳骨位置、牙齿形态和四肢负重方式的逐步变化。The fossil sequence points to land artiodactyl relatives close to Pakicetus around 50 million years ago; ankle bones, teeth and inner-ear structures link cetaceans to the artiodactyl order. Descendants moved gradually into shallow water and then the open ocean, with limbs, pelvis and nasal position remodeling further as aquatic life intensified.[6]

水平尾鳍是哺乳类返回水后的运动改造，不能反向推断所有鱼类都用同一推进方式；蓝鲸是否接近理论体型上限仍受食物和代谢约束，现有数据支持其处于大型化的极端位置，而非无限增大的终点。不同海洋哺乳动物的入水时间和适应路径不同，尾鳍方向和体型不能互相套用。体型上限还随海洋生产力和猎物分布的长期变化而缓慢调整。The horizontal fluke is a locomotor remodel after mammals entered water, and it does not imply that all fish use the same propulsion. Whether blue whales near a theoretical size ceiling still depends on food and metabolism; existing data place them at an extreme of enlargement rather than the end point of unlimited growth. Different marine mammals entered water at different times along different paths, so fluke orientation and body size cannot be copied across groups.[5][6]

05狮群里雌雄怎样分工？为什么人类婴儿比其他哺乳动物幼崽无助得多？How do lionesses and males divide labor, and why are human babies so helpless compared with other mammals?

雌狮合作捕猎育幼、雄狮守卫领地；人类晚熟受骨盆限制与大脑扩展共同塑造。Females hunt and rear cubs cooperatively, males guard territory; human altriciality follows pelvic limits and brain expansion.

狮群中雌狮主要承担合作捕猎和育幼，雄狮负责领地防御和群体守卫；人类婴儿相比其他哺乳动物高度晚熟，出生时脑和运动系统尚未发育成熟，这与直立行走的骨盆限制和大脑扩展有关。社群分工与幼崽发育是哺乳动物社会生活的两端，分别关系到群体生存和后代投入。In lion prides, lionesses mainly do cooperative hunting and cub rearing, while males defend the territory and guard the group. Human infants are highly altricial compared with other mammals: brain and motor systems are immature at birth, tied to the pelvic constraint of bipedal walking and the expansion of the brain. Social division of labor and offspring development are two ends of mammalian social life, bearing on group survival and parental investment respectively.[1][6]

雌狮常以群体协作围捕猎物，合作提高大型猎物的捕猎成功率；幼崽由群内母兽共同照料。雄狮巡逻标记领地，驱赶入侵者，鬃毛和体型用于威慑，捕猎并非其主要职责。狮群的分工随猎物类型、栖息地和群体规模调整，雌狮之间也存在轮流捕猎和哺乳的配合。合作捕猎还减少个体在大型猎物面前受伤的风险。Lionesses often hunt cooperatively in groups, and teamwork raises success on large prey; cubs are raised communally by the females. Males patrol and mark territory, drive off intruders, and use mane and size for deterrence, so hunting is not their main duty. Pride division shifts with prey type, habitat and group size, and lionesses coordinate around alternating hunts and nursing.[1]

人类骨盆受双足行走限制，产道宽度有限，而新生儿脑容量持续增长，婴儿必须在大脑尚未完全成熟时出生，随后经历漫长依赖期。漫长童年为语言、社会学习和复杂技能训练留出时间，幼年期大脑在互动环境中逐步建立神经连接，这一阶段是人类文化传递的基础。晚熟使后代需要多年照护，也强化了家庭和社群的合作抚育。The human pelvis is constrained by bipedal walking, limiting birth-canal width, while neonatal brain volume keeps growing; infants must be born before the brain fully matures and then go through a long dependence period. A long childhood leaves time for language, social learning and complex skill training, and the juvenile brain builds neural connections through interaction, which underlies cultural transmission.[2][6]

哺乳动物基础代谢率随体重按约 0.75 次幂增长，体型越大单位体重代谢越慢；恒温、毛发、乳腺和三块听小骨是哺乳类的共同特征。幼崽发育速度与孕期、断奶时间和营养供给相互配合，不同物种的孕期长短和新生幼崽成熟度对应各自的觅食和被捕食压力。早成兽类幼崽出生后较快活动，依赖的是母体孕期内的发育积累。Mammalian basal metabolic rate rises with body mass to roughly the 0.75 power: the larger the animal, the slower the per-unit-weight metabolism. Warm blood, hair, mammary glands and three middle-ear bones are shared mammalian traits. Developmental pace matches gestation length, weaning time and nutrition, and pregnancy and newborn maturity in different species respond to their own foraging and predation pressures.[1][2]

狮群分工是观察到的群体模式，具体捕猎参与者随环境变化；人类晚熟是解剖、脑发育和文化教育共同作用的结果，不能简化为单一骨盆因素。不同哺乳动物的幼崽早熟程度差异很大，草食动物幼崽出生后较快站立奔跑是另一套应对被捕食的适应路线，不能用人类婴儿的发育节奏衡量所有哺乳类。社会结构、觅食方式和天敌压力共同塑造了每一种哺乳动物的后代投入策略。Pride division is an observed group pattern, and who hunts shifts with environment; human altriciality results from anatomy, brain development and cultural education together and cannot be reduced to the pelvis alone. Newborn maturity varies widely across mammals: prey-animal young that stand and run soon after birth follow a different anti-predator route, and human infant timing should not be used as the measure for all mammals.[1][6]

06熊猫为什么主要吃竹子？它不是食肉动物吗？Why do giant pandas eat bamboo, given they are carnivoran mammals?

伪拇指与齿颌结构适配取竹，肠道仍是食肉型，靠每天长时间进食补偿低效率。Pseudo-thumb and skull-teeth suit bamboo, but the gut stays carnivore-like; long feeding bouts compensate low efficiency.

大熊猫属于熊科、食肉目演化支，分类史上曾在熊科与浣熊科之间有过争议，现归入熊科；它的肠道配置仍是食肉动物类型：消化道短而简单，没有反刍动物那样的盲肠发酵室，基因组也缺少降解纤维素所需酶系的同源基因；但现存食谱中竹子占比超过 99%。这种食肉动物的消化管搭配取食竹子的食性，是理解熊猫的核心矛盾，它并不是在演化中变成了完整的草食动物。The giant panda belongs to the bear family within the carnivoran lineage, and its digestive tract retains a carnivore layout: a short, simple gut, no ruminant-style fermentation chamber, and a genome lacking the homologs needed for cellulose-digesting enzymes. Yet bamboo makes up more than 99 percent of its present diet. This mismatch between a carnivore gut and a bamboo diet is the core puzzle: pandas did not evolve into full herbivores.[7][8]

取食结构上，熊猫腕部的桡侧籽骨异常增大，形成能对握的伪拇指，配合掌部握住竹竿；头骨大、颧弓向两侧展开、矢状嵴发达，咬肌附着面宽，颊齿平宽、齿冠纹路复杂，适合压碎纤维；胃壁肌肉厚，肠壁覆盖黏液层，减少竹刺划伤。伪拇指在大熊猫和小熊猫身上趋同出现，相关肢体发育基因受到平行选择，两种熊猫在没有最近共同祖先的情况下独立形成了握竹结构。For feeding, the radial sesamoid bone at the wrist is enlarged into an opposable pseudo-thumb that grips stalks alongside the palm. The skull is large, the zygomatic arches flare sideways, the sagittal crest is prominent, and the cheek teeth are broad with complex crown ridges suited to crushing fiber. The stomach wall is muscular and the gut lining carries a thick mucus layer that limits bamboo-splinter injury. The pseudo-thumb appears convergently in giant and red pandas, with parallel selection on limb-development genes.[7][8]

消化效率仍然偏低：研究估计熊猫只能提取竹叶和竹茎中约 17% 的养分，只能利用半纤维素而难以降解纤维素；消化道通过速度快，竹子在体内停留时间短，进一步限制养分提取。肠道菌群偏向蛋白质与半纤维素代谢，发酵产物以乳酸、乙酸和乙醇为主，组成随季节变化。低效率靠长时间进食补偿：每天进食 10 小时以上，部分记录达 12–16 小时，活动范围小、能量预算严格，很少长距离移动。Digestive efficiency stays low. Studies estimate pandas extract only about 17 percent of nutrients from leaves and stalks, and they use hemicellulose more readily than cellulose. The gut microbiome leans toward protein and hemicellulose metabolism, fermenting mainly lactate, acetate and ethanol, and its composition shifts with the seasons. Low efficiency is compensated by long feeding bouts: more than 10 hours a day, with some records reaching 12 to 16 hours, a small home range and a strict energy budget with little long-distance travel.[8][9]

演化上，化石与基因组证据显示熊猫的祖先为杂食性熊类，在数百万年前转向以竹子为生境核心：竹类在山地森林中全年可获得、竞争者少，作为稳定食源支撑了这一支系的存续。但它仍保留食肉目的味觉与消化配置，野外偶食小型哺乳动物、鱼类和昆虫；食谱转型是结构、生理与行为长期妥协的结果，并非一夜之间改吃竹子。Over millions of years pandas shifted to bamboo as the core of their habitat: bamboo is available year-round in montane forests and faces few competitors, providing a stable food base for the lineage. They still retain carnivore taste and digestive wiring, occasionally eating small mammals, fish and insects in the wild. The dietary switch is a long compromise among structure, physiology and behavior, not an overnight change to bamboo.[7][9]

边界：熊猫吃竹子是一套不彻底的适应组合，营养效率低、依赖竹林面积与连续性，栖息地碎片化是保护重点。竹子本身的生长速度、开花周期属于植物生理问题，不在本章展开；熊猫仅分布于中国西南山地的地理格局，涉及地质历史与扩散范围，需另作考察。Boundary: bamboo eating is an incomplete adaptation package with low nutritional efficiency, dependent on the extent and continuity of bamboo forest; habitat fragmentation is the central conservation concern. How fast bamboo itself grows and how it flowers are plant-physiology topics outside this chapter, and the geographic pattern of pandas confined to southwest Chinese mountains involves geological history and dispersal that needs separate treatment.[7][8]

07狮子为什么杀死鬣狗却很少吃掉它？Why do lions kill hyenas but rarely eat them?

同域竞争清除对手；围攻反击风险高、鬣狗肉回报低，食腐机会成本也不划算。Intraguild removal of a rival; mobbing and injury risk are high, hyena meat returns little, and scavenging trade-offs favor other prey.

狮子和斑鬣狗是非洲同域分布的顶级捕食者，共享有蹄类猎物，也互相偷食；相遇时狮子会驱赶甚至杀死鬣狗，但鬣狗尸体通常被留下不吃。这一行为属于同域类群内的竞争，主要目的是减少竞争对手，而不是获取一顿食物。把咬死鬣狗理解为捕食行为，混淆了竞争清除与取食两种不同的选择压力，二者在演化上面对的收益和风险并不相同。Lions and spotted hyenas are sympatric top predators in Africa, sharing ungulate prey and stealing food from each other. On encounters lions drive off or even kill hyenas, but the hyena carcass is usually left uneaten. This behavior is intraguild competition whose main payoff is removing a rival rather than obtaining a meal. Treating the killing of hyenas as predation conflates competitor removal with food acquisition, two different selective pressures.[1][10]

制服一只成年鬣狗的成本不低。成年鬣狗体型接近小型狮，皮厚、颌部咬合力强，且成群活动，会以群体围攻方式逼近狮子，反击能造成咬伤；追踪、围堵并制服一只成年鬣狗要付出时间、能量和受伤风险，而鬣狗肉在长期食性中并非狮的偏好猎物。狮子的常规猎物是斑马、角马等有蹄类，单位捕猎投入换来的肉量和脂肪回报更稳定，捕猎大型有蹄类也更符合狮群的协作方式。Subduing an adult hyena carries real cost. Hyenas live in groups and mob lions by approaching side by side; their powerful jaws can deliver biting injuries. Tracking, surrounding and restraining an adult hyena spends time and energy and carries injury risk, while hyena meat is not a preferred prey in the lion's long-standing diet. Lions normally hunt ungulates such as zebras and wildebeest, which return a steadier amount of meat and fat per hunting effort.[10][11]

从食腐机会成本看，狮子本身也会捡食其他动物的尸体；处理鬣狗尸体往往意味着继续与一整群鬣狗纠缠，沾染对方的取食来源，回报却有限。狮群进食时，鬣狗常在周围等待捡剩，双方在尸体旁保持距离、反复试探。两物种更多通过活动时间错开、栖息地细分配和局部回避行为降低冲突，而不是把对方列为常规猎物。研究在埃托沙和乔贝等地记录到两者在夜间活动节奏和家域利用上的细微分化。From the scavenging opportunity-cost side, lions themselves feed on other animals' carcasses. Processing a hyena carcass often means remaining entangled with a whole hyena clan and sharing their food source, with limited return. The two species reduce conflict mainly through shifted activity times, fine-scale habitat separation and localized avoidance, rather than listing each other as regular prey. Studies in Etosha and Chobe record subtle differences in nocturnal activity rhythm and home-range use between the two predators.[10][11]

竞争是双向的。鬣狗群体可围攻落单狮子、偷食狮群的猎获物，也会袭击没有成年狮保护的幼狮，幼狮失去群体看护时风险最高；狮子杀死的鬣狗多为入侵领地或争夺食物的竞争个体。在纳米比亚的一项研究中，相当比例的鬣狗死亡与狮子有关，死亡归因于竞争清除而非被食用。The competition runs both ways. Hyena groups can mob lone lions, steal kills from prides, and attack cubs without adult protection; hyenas killed by lions are mostly intruders or food competitors. In one Namibian study a substantial share of hyena mortality was attributed to lions, and the deaths reflect competitor removal rather than consumption.[10][11]

边界：狮子不吃鬣狗是统计上的常见模式，不是绝对禁忌，野外偶尔也会记录到取食尸体的情况。狮与鬣狗的关系是竞争加机会性利用，不能简化为有仇或单纯挑食。本章比较的是陆地大型食肉动物的种间关系，海豚等水生哺乳类的群体协作与社会行为，属于海洋哺乳动物相关章节的内容，不在本章比较范围。Boundary: lions do not eat hyenas as a common statistical pattern, not an absolute taboo, and carcass consumption is recorded occasionally in the field. The lion-hyena relationship combines competition with opportunistic use and should not be reduced to grudge or simple pickiness. Cooperative group behavior in aquatic mammals such as dolphins belongs to the marine mammal chapter and is not compared here.[1][10]
