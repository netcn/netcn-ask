听觉：声波、毛细胞与编码Hearing: Sound Waves, Hair Cells and Coding

01人耳能听到什么样的声音？频率和响度怎样描述？What sounds can the human ear hear, and how are frequency and loudness described?

声音是机械压力波；频率（Hz）决定音高，振幅与分贝决定响度，青年可听约 20 Hz–20 kHz。Sound is a pressure wave; frequency in Hz sets pitch, amplitude and decibels set loudness, with a young-adult range near 20 Hz–20 kHz.

声音是在空气或其他介质中传播的机械压力波：声源振动推动相邻介质分子，形成疏密相间的纵波向外扩散。描述声音有两个基本物理量：频率，即每秒振动次数，单位赫兹（Hz），对应主观音高；振幅，即压力波动的大小，对应主观响度。声音本身是物理扰动，“听见”则是感受器与大脑后续完成的感知过程。Sound is a mechanical pressure wave traveling through air or another medium: the source vibration pushes neighboring molecules, and a compression-rarefaction longitudinal wave spreads outward. Two physical quantities describe it: frequency, vibrations per second in hertz (Hz), corresponding to perceived pitch; and amplitude, the size of the pressure fluctuation, corresponding to loudness. The wave itself is a physical disturbance, while hearing is perception completed by receptors and the brain.[1]

健康青年人耳的可听频率范围约为 20 Hz 到 20 kHz；日常语音主要落在 1–4 kHz 这一最灵敏的区间。频率低于 20 Hz 为次声，高于 20 kHz 为超声。听力图通常以 125–8000 Hz 为常规检测频段，言语清晰度最依赖 2–4 kHz 的听觉完整性。随年龄增长，高频听力先下降，这就是老年性听力减退最常见的表现；长期强声暴露也会优先损伤高频区域。A healthy young adult hears roughly 20 Hz to 20 kHz; everyday speech sits mainly in the most sensitive 1–4 kHz band. Below 20 Hz is infrasound, above 20 kHz ultrasound. With age, high frequencies decline first, the common pattern of presbycusis; sustained loud exposure also damages high-frequency regions first.[1][2]

波的传播遵守简单关系：波长等于声速除以频率。20°C 空气中声速约 343 m/s，1 kHz 声波波长约 34 cm；频率越高波长越短，绕开障碍物的能力越弱。声音需要介质传播，真空中不能传声；在水和固体中声速高于空气，传播速度随介质弹性和密度变化。Propagation follows a simple relation: wavelength equals wave speed divided by frequency. At 20 °C sound travels about 343 m/s in air, so a 1 kHz wave has a wavelength near 34 cm; higher frequencies have shorter wavelengths and bend less around obstacles. Sound needs a medium and cannot travel through vacuum; speed is higher in water and solids than in air, varying with elasticity and density.[1]

响度用分贝（dB）按对数刻度表示：分贝是功率比的对数，每增加 10 dB 对应声功率约 10 倍，声压幅度约 10 倍对应 20 dB，而不是线性叠加。0 dB SPL 是青年人的平均听阈参考；日常对话约 60 dB；持续暴露在 85 dB 以上可造成听力损伤；120 dB 附近接近人耳痛阈。频率相同的两个声音，响度差异来自振幅和声压级，而不是频率本身。Loudness uses the decibel (dB) logarithmic scale: a decibel is the logarithm of a power ratio, and each 10 dB rise means about ten times the sound power rather than a linear addition. 0 dB SPL is the average young-adult hearing threshold; ordinary speech is about 60 dB; sustained exposure above 85 dB can damage hearing; near 120 dB approaches the pain threshold. At equal frequency, loudness differences come from amplitude and sound-pressure level, not from frequency itself.[1][3]

边界要写清：声波是物理刺激，主观音高和响度是神经系统加工后的结果，不能把分贝高低直接等同于音量感受。可听范围因人、因年龄而异，也因物种而异：蝙蝠靠超声回声定位，大象能感知次声。这里只讲声音的物理属性与人类可听范围；噪声性损伤、耳鸣与平衡觉在其他章节处理。Boundary: a sound wave is a physical stimulus, while subjective pitch and loudness result from neural processing; decibel level cannot be equated directly with perceived volume. The audible range varies with age and person, and across species: bats use ultrasound for echolocation, elephants sense infrasound. This chapter covers physical properties and the human range; noise injury, tinnitus and balance are treated elsewhere.[2][3]

02耳蜗里的毛细胞怎样把振动变成神经信号？How do cochlear hair cells turn vibration into neural signals?

基底膜行波推动静纤毛偏转，tip link 拉开机械门控通道，K+/Ca2+ 内流去极化，递质激活听神经。Membrane traveling waves bend stereocilia, tip links open gated channels, K+/Ca2+ influx depolarizes the cell, and transmitter activates auditory fibers.

声波经外耳道推动鼓膜振动，三块听小骨把振动放大并传入耳蜗外淋巴。鼓膜面积与听小骨杠杆把空气中的声压转化为耳蜗淋巴内更高的压强，弥补气—液界面的能量损失。耳蜗是盘绕的管状结构，基底膜横贯其中；淋巴波动使基底膜上下起伏，附着其上的 Corti 器随膜运动，盖膜与毛细胞静纤毛之间产生相对剪切运动。声音的机械振动在这一步开始被转化为细胞层面的电活动。Sound waves push the eardrum through the ear canal, and three middle-ear ossicles amplify and transmit the vibration into the cochlear perilymph. The cochlea is a coiled tube with the basilar membrane running through it; fluid waves make the membrane rise and fall, the organ of Corti riding on it, and a shearing motion develops between the tectorial membrane and the hair-cell stereocilia. At this step mechanical vibration begins to turn into cellular electrical activity.[2][4]

人类耳蜗约有内毛细胞 3500 个、外毛细胞 12000 个。静纤毛成排排列，相邻纤毛顶部由连接丝（tip link）相连。当纤毛朝一侧偏转，连接丝拉开机械门控阳离子通道，钾离子和钙离子快速内流，毛细胞去极化，产生受体电位；反向偏转时通道关闭，细胞回到静息电位。The human cochlea holds about 3,500 inner hair cells and 12,000 outer hair cells. Stereocilia stand in rows, linked at their tips by tip links. When the bundle deflects one way, the links pull open mechanically gated cation channels; potassium and calcium flow in rapidly, depolarizing the hair cell and producing a receptor potential; deflection the other way closes the channels and returns the cell to resting potential.[4][5]

换能链条随后接通听觉神经：去极化的毛细胞在底部释放谷氨酸类递质，递质以量子化小包释放，释放量与去极化程度相关，刺激听神经纤维发放动作电位，信号经蜗神经传到脑干和听觉皮层。外毛细胞还具有电动性：膜蛋白 prestin 随电位变化改变细胞长度，把基底膜振动放大，提高整体灵敏度并锐化频率分辨。内毛细胞是主要的传入信息来源，外毛细胞更多承担调节与放大角色。The transduction chain then connects to the auditory nerve: depolarized hair cells release glutamate-like transmitter at their base, triggering action potentials in auditory nerve fibers that travel via the cochlear nerve to the brainstem and auditory cortex. Outer hair cells also show electromotility: the membrane protein prestin changes cell length with voltage, amplifying basilar-membrane vibration to raise sensitivity and sharpen frequency tuning. Inner hair cells are the main afferent source, while outer hair cells mainly regulate and amplify.[4][6]

这一过程的时间尺度在微秒级，足以跟随高频声波周期。毛细胞属于终末分化细胞，人类成年后不再分裂补充；机械损伤、强声或耳毒性药物造成的毛细胞丢失通常不可自行再生。NIDCD 的听力说明与耳蜗生理教材都把毛细胞列为听觉换能的关键结构，机械门控通道是整条链条的分子入口。The process operates on a microsecond timescale, fast enough to follow high-frequency sound cycles. Hair cells are terminally differentiated and do not divide to replace themselves after maturity; loss from mechanical injury, loud sound or ototoxic drugs usually cannot regenerate on its own. NIDCD hearing materials and cochlear physiology references identify hair cells as the key auditory transduction structure, with the mechanically gated channel as the molecular entry point.[2][5]

边界要写清：毛细胞只完成机械振动到电信号的转换；音色、语音分辨和声源方向由后续通路和大脑皮层加工。内耳中负责平衡觉的毛细胞位于半规管和耳石器，感受头部位置与加速度，是另一套感觉系统，不参与听觉换能。这里不展开中耳病变、助听器或人工耳蜗等临床话题。Boundary: hair cells only convert mechanical vibration into electrical signals; timbre, speech discrimination and source direction are processed along later pathways and the cortex. Balance hair cells in the semicircular canals and otolith organs sense head position and acceleration as a separate sensory system and do not participate in auditory transduction. Middle-ear disease, hearing aids and cochlear implants are clinical topics outside this chapter.[4]

03大脑怎样区分音高、响度和声音方向？How does the brain distinguish pitch, loudness and sound direction?

位置与频率/发放率编码音高和响度，双耳时间差与强度差及耳廓滤波判断方向。Place and rate/firing cues encode pitch and loudness; binaural time and intensity differences plus pinna filtering localize sound.

大脑区分音高主要靠两条互补编码机制。位置理论认为，基底膜不同位置对不同频率共振：靠近基底的部分短而硬，响应高频；靠近蜗顶的部分长而软，响应低频。频率理论则认为，对低频声音，听神经纤维的发放频率可以跟随声波周期，把频率信息直接编码在放电节奏里。两条机制分别覆盖不同频段：位置理论解释高频分辨，频率理论在低频更适用，中频由两者共同参与。Pitch is encoded through two complementary mechanisms. Place theory holds that different basilar-membrane positions resonate with different frequencies: the short, stiff base responds to high frequencies, the long, floppy apex to low ones. Frequency theory adds that for low frequencies auditory nerve fibers can fire in step with the sound cycle, encoding frequency in the firing rhythm itself.[4][6]

位置编码的具体过程是：声波在行波传播到基底膜的某一位置时振幅最大，该处毛细胞被优先激活；兴奋沿拓扑有序的听神经通路传到听觉皮层，不同频率对应皮层上不同位置。复合声音可以在基底膜上同时激起多个峰值，分别对应其频率成分，听觉系统再把这些位置信号组合起来。In place coding, the traveling wave reaches its maximum amplitude at one membrane position, where hair cells are preferentially activated; excitation travels along a topographic auditory pathway to the cortex, with each frequency mapped to a distinct cortical location. A complex sound can raise several peaks at once, matching its separate frequency components, and the auditory system combines these place signals.[4]

响度编码由三条线索共同决定：被激活的毛细胞数量、听神经纤维的放电率，以及参与的纤维类型。声音越响，激活范围越大、发放频率越高，粗大有髓纤维也被募集进来；弱刺激只调动少量阈值低的纤维。位置与发放率两条线索叠加，使大脑能在几十分贝的范围内区分强弱。同一声音在不同频率上的响度感受并不相等，等响度曲线显示人耳对中频最敏感，响度判断还受既往听觉经验影响。Loudness coding rests on three cues: the number of activated hair cells, the firing rate of auditory fibers, and the fiber types recruited. Louder sounds activate a wider range, raise firing rates and recruit large myelinated fibers; weak stimuli engage only a few low-threshold fibers. Place and rate cues combine, letting the brain distinguish strength across a range of tens of decibels.[3][6]

声源方向靠双耳线索判断：两耳之间的时间差用于低频定位，强度差（头部遮挡造成的双耳声级差）主要用于高频；耳廓的凹凸形状还对不同频率产生滤波，提供前后和高低位置线索。头部转动时双耳线索动态变化，进一步辅助定位。单耳听力会明显削弱方向判断，尤其是低频和前后辨别。Source direction uses binaural cues: interaural time differences serve low-frequency localization, while intensity differences from head shadow mainly serve high frequencies; the pinna's ridges filter frequencies to give front-back and vertical cues. Monoaural hearing weakens direction judgment markedly, especially for low frequencies and front-back separation.[3][4]

边界要写清：编码是神经层面的映射，不等于主观音高或音色体验；和弦、语音语调和音乐旋律需要听觉皮层对多组信号做组合加工。频率感知与响度感知在病理下可以分离受损，因此不能用一条编码机制解释全部听觉现象；这里也不讨论声源识别与语音理解的皮层模型。Boundary: coding is a neural mapping, not the subjective experience of pitch or timbre; chords, speech intonation and melody require the auditory cortex to combine multiple signals. Frequency and loudness perception can be impaired separately in disease, so no single coding mechanism explains all hearing phenomena; cortical models of sound identification and speech understanding are outside this chapter.[3]
