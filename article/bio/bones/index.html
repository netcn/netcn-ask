骨骼、肌肉与运动Bone, Skeletal Muscle and Movement

从骨皮质与骨小梁怎样承重、成骨与破骨怎样终身重塑，到骨骼肌为何必须成对拮抗才能完成屈伸，再到训练增肌、停训萎缩与再训练更快背后的结构与神经适应：三章给出可核对的量级、机制环节与适用边界。How cortical bone and trabeculae carry load, lifelong remodeling by osteoblasts and osteoclasts, why muscle acts in antagonist pairs, and the basis of hypertrophy, atrophy and faster retraining.

01骨头内部是什么结构，为什么又硬又韧？What is the internal structure of bone, and why is it both hard and tough?

皮质骨承重、松质骨小梁分散应力，胶原与钙盐配合成刚柔基质；成骨与破骨细胞终身重塑。Cortical bone carries load, trabeculae disperse stress, collagen and mineral form a flexible-yet-hard matrix; osteoblasts and osteoclasts remodel lifelong.

骨是活的器官，并不是石头。外层是致密的皮质骨，负责承重与保护；内部是松质骨，由骨小梁交织成网格，间隙中填充骨髓。骨基质约三分之一为胶原纤维，提供柔韧性；约三分之二为羟基磷灰石等钙盐结晶，提供硬度。刚柔两种成分按比例配合，使骨骼既能承受垂直压力，又能抵抗弯折与扭转。Bone is a living organ, not stone. A dense cortical shell on the outside carries load and protection, while spongy bone inside forms a trabecular lattice whose spaces hold marrow. About one third of the matrix is collagen fibers for flexibility, and about two thirds calcium-salt crystals such as hydroxyapatite for hardness. The two components together let bone resist vertical compression as well as bending and twisting.[1]

骨组织里有三类主要细胞在终身协作。成骨细胞分泌骨基质并促进矿化，被基质包埋后成为骨细胞；破骨细胞在骨表面吸收陈旧骨组织；骨细胞则感知力学负荷并调节代谢。皮质骨由一层层同心圆排列的骨单位构成，松质骨的骨小梁沿着主要受力方向排列，把力量引导到承重轴线上。OpenStax 骨组织章节把这套形成与吸收并存的过程称为骨重塑。Three main cell types cooperate lifelong. Osteoblasts secrete and mineralize matrix, becoming osteocytes once trapped; osteoclasts resorb old bone at the surface; osteocytes sense mechanical load and regulate metabolism. Compact bone is built from concentric osteons, and trabeculae align along the main lines of stress, channeling force toward the weight-bearing axis. The OpenStax bone chapter calls this ongoing balance of formation and resorption remodeling.[1]

可核对的量级是：人体骨量通常在 30 岁前后达到峰值，此后骨形成与骨吸收逐渐失衡。钙、磷、维生素 D 的摄入以及负重运动都会影响骨密度；生长激素、性激素和机械应力是骨代谢的主要调节因素。儿童青少年期的骨量积累决定成年后的骨储备，峰值越高，老年后离骨质疏松的距离越远。成年后骨重塑仍在持续，只是形成速度逐渐追不上吸收；女性绝经后雌激素下降，骨量流失会明显加快。A checkable benchmark: peak bone mass is usually reached around age 30, after which formation and resorption gradually drift out of balance. Calcium, phosphorus, vitamin D intake and weight-bearing exercise all affect density; growth hormone, sex hormones and mechanical stress are the main regulators of bone metabolism. Bone accumulated in childhood and adolescence sets adult reserve, and a higher peak leaves more distance from osteoporosis in old age.[2][3]

当骨吸收持续超过骨形成，骨小梁变细、皮质骨变薄，骨强度下降，轻微外力就可能骨折，这就是骨质疏松。骨折愈合也遵循重塑逻辑：先形成血肿与纤维软骨骨痂，再由成骨细胞铺设新骨、破骨细胞修整外形，最终恢复骨的连续结构。When resorption persistently exceeds formation, trabeculae thin and the cortex weakens, so ordinary force can fracture the bone; this is osteoporosis. Fracture healing follows the same remodeling logic: a hematoma and fibrocartilage callus form first, then osteoblasts lay new bone, osteoclasts trim the outline, and continuity is restored.[3]

边界要写清：化石能在地下留存百万年，是有机质降解后残留下来的矿物骨架，不能据此推断活体骨的韧性与代谢状态。牙齿不属于骨骼：最外层的釉质是矿化的上皮产物，硬度与形成方式和骨不同，也没有骨膜与重塑细胞群。这里讨论人类骨骼与骨骼肌，鱼、两栖类的骨骼终身持续生长是另一套机制。The boundary: a fossil lasting millions of years is the mineral skeleton left after organic material decayed, not evidence for the toughness or metabolism of living bone. Teeth are not bones: the outer enamel is a mineralized epithelial product with a different hardness, origin and repair path, and it lacks periosteum and remodeling cells. This chapter covers human bone and skeletal muscle; lifelong bone growth in fish and amphibians follows a different mechanism.[1]

02肌肉只能收缩，身体怎样完成相反方向的动作？If muscle can only contract, how does the body perform movements in the opposite direction?

骨骼肌借肌腱牵拉骨骼，屈肌与伸肌成对拮抗：一侧收缩缩短，另一侧放松拉长，反向时互换角色。Skeletal muscle pulls bone through tendons; flexors and extensors act in pairs, one contracting while the other relaxes, then swapping roles.

骨骼肌纤维只能主动缩短，不能主动把自己拉长。关节之所以能朝两个方向活动，靠的是成对的拮抗肌：一侧肌肉收缩缩短，另一侧放松并被拉长，需要反向动作时两侧角色互换。没有拮抗配合，单块肌肉只能把关节拉向一个方向，无法复位。Skeletal muscle fibers can only actively shorten; they cannot actively pull themselves longer. A joint moves in two directions because opposing muscle pairs work together: one side contracts and shortens while the other relaxes and is stretched, then the roles reverse. Without this pairing, a single muscle could only pull the joint one way and could not return it.[4]

从结构上看，肌肉借肌腱附着在骨面上。肌小节是收缩的基本单位：粗丝为肌球蛋白，细丝为肌动蛋白，收缩时细丝在横桥带动下滑行，肌小节缩短，整个肌纤维随之变短；横桥循环由 ATP 供能。运动神经在神经肌肉接头释放乙酰胆碱，触发肌浆网释放钙离子，暴露肌动蛋白上的结合位点。横小管把动作电位传向肌纤维深部，钙离子结合肌钙蛋白后，原肌球蛋白移开位点，横桥才能与细丝结合。Structurally, muscle attaches to bone through tendons. The sarcomere is the basic contractile unit: thick myosin and thin actin filaments slide as cross-bridges pull, shortening the sarcomere and the whole fiber; cross-bridge cycles run on ATP. A motor neuron releases acetylcholine at the neuromuscular junction, triggering calcium release from the sarcoplasmic reticulum and exposing binding sites on actin.[5]

日常动作都能对应到这对配合。屈肘时肱二头肌收缩、肱三头肌放松并被拉长；伸肘时肱三头肌收缩、肱二头肌放松。眼睑开闭也是成对肌肉配合：眼轮匝肌收缩使眼睑闭合，提上睑肌收缩把上眼睑抬起，两侧张力的平衡决定眼睑停留在什么位置。眼球转向则由六条眼外肌按拮抗与协同方式分组拉动。Everyday movements map onto this pairing. In elbow flexion the biceps contracts and the triceps relaxes and stretches; extension reverses the roles. The eyelid works the same way: the orbicularis oculi closes the lid, the levator palpebrae lifts the upper lid, and the balance of the two sets its position. Eye rotation uses six extraocular muscles grouped into antagonist and synergist pairs.[4][5]

神经层面，一个运动神经元连同它所支配的全部肌纤维构成一个运动单位；同时激活的单位越多，肌肉张力越大。单位按大小原则募集：小单位先参与精细动作，大单位在需要大力时加入。拮抗肌之间的协同收缩让动作平滑可控，避免关节被猛烈拉扯。肌肉在静息状态下仍保留低水平的肌张力，用于维持姿势和关节稳定。At the neural level, one motor neuron plus all fibers it innervates forms a motor unit; recruiting more units raises muscle tension. Co-contraction between antagonists keeps movement smooth and protects the joint from sudden pulls. At rest, muscle keeps a low level of tone to maintain posture and joint stability.[7]

边界要写清：心肌和平滑肌不按骨骼肌这种拮抗配对的方式工作。肌肉被拉长时属于被动弹性变形，不是肌肉主动向外发力；所谓放松指主动张力下调，而不是产生一个反向拉力。肌腱在肌肉与骨之间传递拉力，本身由致密胶原结缔组织构成，弹性有限；肌腱只传递力，不能缩短，讨论肌肉动作时不要把肌腱的弹性回弹混进肌肉收缩的机制里。The boundary: cardiac and smooth muscle do not follow the skeletal antagonist-pair model. A stretched muscle deforms passively rather than pulling outward actively; relaxation means lower active tone, not a reverse pulling force. Tendons only transmit force and cannot shorten themselves; their elastic recoil should not be counted as part of muscle contraction.[4]

03练过的肌肉停下来后，重新练为什么会更快？Why does previously trained muscle return faster after a break?

训练使肌纤维增粗、神经募集提高；停训后萎缩，再训练时神经通路与保留的肌核缩短重建周期。Training enlarges fibers and improves recruitment; after atrophy, retained neural pathways and myonuclei speed the rebuild.

抗阻训练使肌纤维增粗，也就是肌肥大。力量增长来自三个方面：肌纤维横截面积增大、神经募集运动单位的效率提高，以及肌腱和筋膜等结缔组织的适应。肌肥大不仅是肌纤维变粗，快肌与慢肌纤维的比例也会随训练类型发生适应性偏移。长期不活动时，肌肉蛋白分解超过合成，肌纤维变细，力量和耐力随之下降。Resistance training enlarges muscle fibers, or hypertrophy. Strength gains come from three sources: a bigger fiber cross-section, more efficient motor-unit recruitment, and adaptation of connective tissue such as tendon and fascia. During long inactivity, protein breakdown outpaces synthesis, fibers thin, and strength and endurance fall.[6]

训练刺激启动的合成链条是：机械负荷经感受器传入信号，肌纤维内肌原纤维蛋白合成增加，肌节数量与纤维横截面积上升；重复训练后肌肉变得更粗、能输出更大张力。制动、卧床或长期久坐会使合成信号下调，分解代谢占优，肌纤维逐渐萎缩，神经对肌肉的支配效率也一起下降。The training stimulus starts a synthesis chain: mechanical load signals through receptors, myofibrillar protein synthesis rises inside fibers, and sarcomere number and fiber area increase; repeated training makes the muscle thicker and able to output more tension. Immobilization, bed rest or long sedentary sitting lowers the synthesis signal, breakdown dominates, fibers atrophy, and neural drive to the muscle drops as well.[5][6]

停训后重新训练，力量恢复通常快于初次增肌。早期进步主要来自神经适应：运动单位重新被高效募集，协同肌与拮抗肌的配合恢复。已经训练过的肌纤维还保留着较多肌核和结构基础，缩短了蛋白重建周期；停训期间肌纤维并未完全回到初始状态，已分化的结构蛋白网络保留了一部分合成模板。这就是常说的“肌肉记忆”，它更接近神经通路与肌纤维结构的残留，而不是大脑像记动作要领那样把训练内容存了下来。Retraining after a break usually restores strength faster than first-time muscle gain. Early progress comes mainly from neural adaptation: motor units are efficiently recruited again and antagonist-synergist coordination returns. Previously trained fibers also retain more myonuclei and structural bases, shortening the protein rebuild cycle. This "muscle memory" is closer to residual neural pathways and fiber structure than to the brain storing a movement like a memorized recipe.[6][7]

可核对的证据来自制动与恢复研究：卧床数周即可观察到明显肌力下降；规律训练者停训一段时间后，仍保留部分肌核与神经通路，复健速度快于零基础人群。恢复速度受年龄、营养和睡眠影响，老年人的肌肉合成响应较慢，儿童与青少年的适应轨迹也不同；蛋白质摄入不足会压低合成速率，睡眠不足也会削弱训练后的恢复适应。Checkable evidence comes from immobilization and recovery studies: several weeks of bed rest produce measurable strength loss; trained people keep some myonuclei and neural pathways after stopping, and regain muscle faster than beginners. Recovery speed varies with age, nutrition and sleep: older adults respond more slowly in muscle protein synthesis, and the adaptation trajectory differs in children and adolescents.[6]

边界要写清：肌肉记忆并不是永久保留。长期停用后肌核与结构优势仍会逐步流失，肌肉持续萎缩。基因决定一个人增肌的上限，训练量、蛋白质摄入和休息决定接近上限的程度；不存在只靠回忆动作就能保住的肌肉，也没有局部减脂式的“想练哪里就只长哪里”。The boundary: muscle memory is not permanent. After long inactivity the myonuclear and structural advantage still leaks away and muscle atrophies steadily. Genetics set the hypertrophy ceiling, while training volume, protein intake and rest decide how close one gets to it. Muscle cannot be kept by recall alone, and there is no local fat-loss or spot-growth rule that builds one muscle in isolation.[6]
