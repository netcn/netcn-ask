01植物的叶子为什么通常是绿色的？秋天叶片又为什么会变色？Why are plant leaves usually green, and why do they change color in autumn?

叶绿素吸收红蓝光、反射绿光；秋季叶绿素降解快于类胡萝卜素，黄橙色显现，部分后期合成花青素。Chlorophyll absorbs red and blue light and reflects green; in autumn chlorophyll degrades faster than carotenoids, revealing yellow and orange, with anthocyanins in some species.

叶子通常呈绿色，是因为叶绿素主要吸收红光和蓝光，绿光大部分被反射或透射，进入眼睛的光以绿色为主。叶绿素位于叶绿体的类囊体膜上，是光合作用捕获光能的主要色素；叶片看起来的颜色，正是没有被色素吸收、剩余下来的那部分光。叶肉细胞排列和角质层厚度也影响光线反射，厚叶多层细胞对光的吸收更充分。Leaves usually look green because chlorophyll absorbs mainly red and blue light, while most green light is reflected or transmitted, so the light reaching the eye is dominated by green. Chlorophyll sits on the thylakoid membranes of chloroplasts and is the main pigment that captures light for photosynthesis. The apparent leaf color is the portion of light not absorbed by pigments.[1]

叶绿素的吸收有明确的波长区间。叶绿素 a 在约 430 nm 的蓝光和 660 nm 的红光处有吸收峰，叶绿素 b 偏向 453 nm 和 642 nm；类胡萝卜素包括胡萝卜素和叶黄素，吸收蓝紫光，把捕获的能量传递给叶绿素，同时在强光下清除多余激发能，起到光保护作用。不同色素的吸收光谱互补，使叶片在可见光范围内利用更多波段；多种色素的吸收叠加，决定了最终反射光的色调。Chlorophyll absorption has defined wavelength ranges. Chlorophyll a peaks around 430 nm in blue and 660 nm in red, while chlorophyll b leans toward 453 nm and 642 nm. Carotenoids absorb blue-violet light and pass the captured energy to chlorophyll; under strong light they also clear excess excitation energy for photoprotection. The absorption spectra of several leaf pigments overlap to set the final reflected hue.[1]

植物没有进化成黑色，与早期光合生物所处的光环境、能量平衡和强光保护有关。叶绿素在红光和蓝光区的吸收效率高，反射出的绿光并非完全浪费：部分绿光仍可被叶片深层组织利用，厚叶和阴生叶对绿光的利用比例更高。强光下叶绿素若吸收过量，会产生活性氧损伤膜系统，类胡萝卜素参与清除多余激发能。色素组成还会随光照强度调整，避免吸收过多光能造成氧化损伤。Plants did not evolve to be black, a result tied to the light environment of early photosynthetic organisms, energy balance and protection against strong light. Chlorophyll is efficient in red and blue bands, and the reflected green light is not entirely wasted: some green light is still used by deeper leaf tissues, and thick or shade leaves use a higher proportion of it. Pigment composition also adjusts with light intensity to avoid oxidative damage from absorbing too much energy.[1]

入秋后气温下降、日照缩短，叶绿素降解速度快于类胡萝卜素，原本被绿色掩盖的黄色和橙色显现出来；部分植物在后期合成花青素，花青素由糖代谢产物在液泡中合成，颜色随 pH 偏红或偏蓝，使叶片呈现红色。落叶前叶绿素分解，养分从叶片回收转移到枝干，叶色变化是衰老和养分回流的外在表现。In autumn, falling temperature and shorter daylight make chlorophyll degrade faster than carotenoids, revealing the yellow and orange previously masked by green; some plants later produce anthocyanins and turn red. Before leaf fall chlorophyll breaks down and nutrients are recycled back into branches, so color change is an external sign of senescence and nutrient withdrawal.[1][7]

边界上，并非所有叶子都是绿色：斑叶品种、幼嫩红叶和缺少叶绿素的寄生植物不呈典型绿色；阴生植物会调整叶绿素比例以适应弱光。叶色还受光照强度、光谱和营养状态影响，秋季颜色深浅不能简单等同于植物健康状况。The boundary: not every leaf is green. Variegated varieties, young red leaves and chlorophyll-lacking parasitic plants do not show the typical green; shade plants adjust chlorophyll ratios to weak light. Leaf color also depends on light intensity, spectrum and nutrition, and autumn color intensity cannot be read simply as plant health.[7]

02光合作用是怎样把二氧化碳和水变成糖类的？为什么有些植物可以在弱光下生存？How does photosynthesis turn carbon dioxide and water into sugars, and why do some plants survive in weak light?

光反应裂解水生成 ATP、NADPH，卡尔文循环固定 CO₂ 成糖；阴生植物调色素，寄生植物异养。Light reactions split water to make ATP and NADPH; the Calvin cycle fixes CO₂ into sugar. Shade plants adjust pigment ratios, while parasites give up autotrophy.

光合作用把二氧化碳和水在光下合成糖类并释放氧气，总反应可写为 6CO₂ + 6H₂O + 光能 → C₆H₁₂O₆ + 6O₂。叶绿体主要分布在叶片栅栏组织和海绵组织细胞中，类囊体堆叠成基粒以扩大膜面积。整个过程分为光反应和卡尔文循环两段，分别在类囊体膜和基质中进行；两段通过 ATP 和 NADPH 衔接，缺一不可。Photosynthesis builds sugars from carbon dioxide and water under light and releases oxygen; the overall reaction is 6CO₂ + 6H₂O + light → C₆H₁₂O₆ + 6O₂. The process splits into light-dependent reactions and the Calvin cycle, carried out on thylakoid membranes and in the stroma respectively; the two are linked by ATP and NADPH, and neither works alone.[1]

光反应在类囊体膜上进行。光系统吸收光能后驱动电子传递，水在光下裂解，释放电子、质子和氧气；电子在光系统 II 和光系统 I 之间传递，质子梯度驱动 ATP 合酶合成 ATP，能量最终存入 ATP 和 NADPH。氧气来自水的裂解，而不是来自二氧化碳，这一点是理解光合产物来源的关键。The light reactions run on the thylakoid membrane. Photosystems absorb light and drive electron transport; water splits under light, releasing electrons, protons and oxygen. Electrons pass along the chain to store energy in ATP and NADPH. The oxygen comes from water splitting, not from carbon dioxide, which is key to understanding where photosynthetic products originate.[2]

卡尔文循环在叶绿体基质中进行。循环先固定二氧化碳，把它接入五碳受体分子，再利用光反应提供的 ATP 和 NADPH 把碳还原成三碳糖；三碳糖一部分离开叶绿体合成蔗糖，一部分留在叶绿体合成淀粉备用，供植物生长和储存。光反应提供能量载体，卡尔文循环完成碳的固定和还原，两者在白天同步运转。The Calvin cycle runs in the chloroplast stroma. It first fixes carbon dioxide onto a five-carbon acceptor, then uses ATP and NADPH from the light reactions to reduce carbon into three-carbon sugars. These sugars are further assembled into sucrose and starch for growth and storage. The light reactions supply energy carriers, the Calvin cycle fixes and reduces carbon, and the two run together in daylight.[3]

光合作用需要光，但不同植物对光强的适应范围不同。阴生植物在弱光下提高叶绿素比例，仍能维持光合；完全不含叶绿素的寄生植物和菌异养植物不能自营光合，直接从宿主或共生真菌获取有机物，菌异养植物借助真菌菌丝从腐殖质中取得碳源。光照不足时净光合可能低于呼吸消耗，植物需要储存的有机物维持生长。这些例外改变的是碳源获取方式，并不推翻光合反应的基本链条。Photosynthesis needs light, but species differ in their tolerated light range. Shade plants raise chlorophyll ratios under weak light and still maintain photosynthesis; fully chlorophyll-lacking parasites and mycoheterotrophs cannot photosynthesize on their own and obtain organic matter from hosts or symbiotic fungi. These exceptions change the carbon source but do not overturn the basic photosynthetic chain.[1]

边界上，呼吸作用昼夜进行，消耗糖类并释放二氧化碳，净光合速率要同时考虑光合与呼吸；夜间没有光反应，卡尔文循环也随之减弱。C4 和 CAM 植物有额外的固碳路径，在高温干旱条件下减少水分损失；光合产物分配到生长、储存和防御的比例，还受温度、水分和养分条件影响。The boundary: respiration runs day and night, consuming sugars and releasing carbon dioxide, so net photosynthesis must account for both. C4 and CAM plants add extra carbon-fixation pathways that reduce water loss in heat and drought. How photosynthate is split among growth, storage and defense also depends on temperature, water and nutrient conditions.[3]

03含羞草为什么会“害羞”地合上叶子？Why does the sensitive plant fold its leaves when touched?

触碰触发动作电位，叶枕运动细胞的钾氯外流、水分渗出，膨压下降使小叶合拢，离子回流后重新张开。Touch triggers an action potential; potassium and chloride leave pulvinar motor cells, water follows, turgor drops and leaflets fold, reopening as ions return.

含羞草受到触碰后小叶合拢、叶柄下垂，是叶枕运动细胞膨压快速变化造成的感震性运动；叶枕位于小叶基部和叶柄连接处，两侧各有一组可调节膨压的运动细胞。整个过程不需要肌肉，也不依赖生长，是细胞内水分和离子重新分布的结果。反应在数秒内完成，刺激消失后叶片再逐步张开。When touched, Mimosa pudica folds its leaflets and droops its petiole through a rapid turgor change in pulvinus motor cells. This seismonastic movement needs no muscle and no growth; it results from redistribution of water and ions inside cells. The response completes within seconds, and the leaf reopens gradually after the stimulus stops.[5]

机械刺激先转为电信号。触碰产生的动作电位沿维管束传到叶枕，电位变化伴随细胞膜去极化，钙离子先进入细胞，再触发钾离子和氯离子外流；离子外渗使细胞内溶质浓度下降，水分随之按渗透梯度离开细胞。电位传导速度与温度和植株含水量有关，干燥条件下反应变迟钝。信号在植株内可以从一片叶传到相邻叶片，引发连锁反应。Mechanical stimulation first becomes an electrical signal. The touch-triggered action potential travels through vascular tissue to the pulvinus; the potential change drives potassium and chloride ions out of motor cells. As ions leave, intracellular solute concentration drops, and water follows down the osmotic gradient. The signal can spread from one leaf to neighboring leaves and trigger a chain reaction.[5]

叶枕两侧的运动细胞失水后膨压下降，小叶和叶柄失去支撑而闭合。叶枕相当于关节位置的可充放液小囊，一侧细胞收缩、另一侧维持膨压，压差把叶片拉向闭合方向。刺激消失后，离子泵重新建立渗透梯度，运动细胞重新积累溶质，离子和水分逐步回流，数分钟到数十分钟后叶片重新张开，细胞结构本身没有改变。After losing water, motor cells on both sides of the pulvinus drop in turgor, and leaflets and petiole lose support and close. The pulvinus acts like a small fluid-filled sac at a joint; one side contracts while the other keeps turgor, pulling the leaf toward closure. Once the stimulus ends, ions and water flow back into motor cells, and the leaf reopens over minutes to tens of minutes without changing its structure.[5]

关于这种运动的功能，一般认为快速闭合可以惊吓植食动物、减少被取食的概率，同时降低雨水和风力对叶片的损伤；闭合还减少叶面暴露面积，降低夜间热量和水分散失。机制研究把电信号、离子流动和膨压变化串联起来：动作电位是触发信号，离子跨膜移动改变渗透势，膨压变化表现为可见的叶片运动，三者依次衔接构成完整反应链。On function, rapid folding is thought to startle herbivores and lower the chance of being eaten, while also reducing rain and wind damage to the leaf and cutting water loss. Mechanistic studies link electrical signaling, ion flow and turgor change: the action potential is the trigger, transmembrane ion movement shifts osmotic potential, and the turgor change appears as visible leaf movement.[5]

边界上，这是可逆的膨压运动，不是生长过程，反复触碰后叶片不会变长或变短，运动主要由膨压驱动，不涉及细胞分裂或组织伸长。含羞草与捕蝇草都有快速运动，但捕虫夹靠触发毛和叶片形态完成捕虫，机制并不相同。持续或过强刺激会让运动细胞反应灵敏度下降，需要一段时间恢复；环境温度过低时反应也会变慢。The boundary: this is reversible turgor movement, not growth; repeated touching does not make the leaf longer or shorter. Mimosa and the Venus flytrap both move fast, but the trap uses trigger hairs and blade morphology to catch prey, a different mechanism. Continuous or strong stimulation lowers motor-cell sensitivity and needs time to recover; response also slows at low temperature.[5]

04植物怎样把根部吸收的水分运到叶片？附生植物又怎样获得水分？How do plants move water from roots to leaves, and how do epiphytes obtain water?

根毛渗透吸水进入木质部，连续水柱靠蒸腾拉力向上，气孔调节失水；附生植物用气生根或叶鳞吸收水分。Root hairs take up water into the xylem; the continuous column rises by transpiration pull, stomata regulate loss; epiphytes use aerial roots or leaf scales.

植物根系通过根毛吸收土壤水分，经木质部向上运输到茎叶，叶片蒸腾失水产生的拉力把水柱持续拉上去；这条运输链由渗透作用、毛细附着和蒸腾张力共同驱动，不需要心脏那样的泵。水分运输同时伴随矿质养分向上分配，叶片是蒸腾和光合的主要场所；叶片失水后若得不到补充，会出现萎蔫。水分运输的方向和速率，由根部吸水与叶片失水之间的平衡决定。Root hairs take up soil water, which travels upward through the xylem to leaves and stems; transpiration water loss from leaves pulls the continuous water column upward. This transport chain is driven by osmosis, capillary adhesion and transpiration tension, with no pump like a heart. The direction and rate of water transport are set by the balance between root uptake and leaf water loss.[4]

根毛显著扩大根部吸收面积。土壤水分沿渗透梯度进入根部，经质外体或共质体途径进入木质部；矿质离子被主动吸收后维持根部与土壤之间的水势差。根压来自根部主动吸收离子后水势下降，水分被推入木质部，通常只提供有限推力。内皮层的凯氏带迫使水分和离子经过细胞膜选择进入，避免溶质自由回流。Root hairs greatly expand the absorbing surface. Soil water enters the root along an osmotic gradient through apoplast or symplast pathways into the xylem; mineral ions are actively absorbed to maintain the water-potential difference between root and soil. The Casparian strip in the endodermis forces water and ions through cell membranes for selective entry, preventing solutes from flowing back freely.[4]

木质部由中空的死细胞构成导管和管胞，导管分子成熟后原生质解体形成中空管道，管胞壁上的纹孔允许水分横向通过。水分在其中形成连续水柱，水柱连续是运输正常进行的前提，水分子之间的内聚力和水与管壁的附着力使水柱保持不断，叶片蒸腾产生的张力把整段水柱向上拉，这就是内聚力学说对高大植物水分上升的解释。The xylem is built from hollow dead cells forming vessels and tracheids, in which water forms a continuous column. Cohesion between water molecules and adhesion to vessel walls keep the column unbroken; tension from leaf transpiration pulls the whole column upward, which is the cohesion-tension explanation for water rising in tall plants.[6]

气孔开放时，水分以水蒸气形式从叶内间隙散失到空气中，蒸腾拉动水柱上升；气孔开闭同时调节二氧化碳进入叶片和水分流失。附生植物没有接地的根系，气生根常具海绵状根被，叶片鳞片可在降雨时快速吸收和储存水分，运输方式与陆生植物有所不同。When stomata open, water escapes as vapor from leaf intercellular spaces, and transpiration pulls the column upward; stomatal opening and closing regulate both carbon dioxide entry and water loss. Epiphytes lack ground roots and absorb and store water through aerial roots or leaf scales, so their transport differs from land plants.[7]

边界上，水分并不是靠根压单独泵上去的，根压只在低矮植物和夜间发挥有限作用。夜间蒸腾停止时，水分可在叶尖以吐水形式排出；水柱中若出现气泡形成气栓，会阻断局部运输，植物可通过旁路导管绕过。干旱条件下气孔关闭、蒸腾减弱，木质部运输随之调整。运输速率还受空气湿度、温度和风力影响，高温干燥时失水加快，植物需要关闭气孔保水。The boundary: water is not pumped upward by root pressure alone; root pressure plays only a limited role in low plants and at night. When transpiration stops at night, water may exit at leaf tips as guttation. Under drought, stomata close, transpiration weakens and xylem transport adjusts accordingly. Transport rate also depends on humidity, temperature and wind; in hot dry air water loss speeds up and plants must close stomata to retain water.[4][7]

05植物为什么向上生长？叶子为什么是绿色的，为什么需要阳光？Why do plants grow upward, why are leaves green, and why do they need sunlight?

生长素背光积累使茎弯向光源，淀粉体感知重力决定根向下茎向上；叶绿素吸红蓝光，光能供光合固碳。Auxin accumulates on the shaded side to bend the shoot toward light; statoliths set gravity direction; chlorophyll captures red and blue light for photosynthesis.

向光性由茎尖的光信号感知启动。茎尖最幼嫩部位的受体感知单侧光的方向与光质，生长素在薄壁组织中侧向再分配到背光侧；背光侧细胞伸长更多，茎因此弯向光源。切除茎尖后向光反应消失，说明感知部位就在尖端。弯曲在照光后约十分钟级即可观测到起始，属于生长方向调节，不是肌肉式的快速运动；叶片和枝条还通过调整叶角来获取光照。Phototropism begins when receptors in the shoot tip sense light direction and quality. Auxin is transported laterally to the shaded side under unilateral light; shaded cells elongate more, and the stem bends toward the source. The first bending can be observed within about ten minutes of illumination, and this is growth-direction regulation rather than muscle-like movement. Leaves and shoots also adjust leaf angle to capture light.[8]

向重力性由根冠中的淀粉体感知。含淀粉颗粒的淀粉体随重力方向沉降，带动信号使根正向地、茎负向地；根与茎对生长素浓度的敏感性不同，同一浓度在根中抑制伸长、在茎中促进伸长，因此同向重力信号产生相反的生长方向。两种向性配合，芽向上争取光照，根向下扎入土壤取水取养分。植物没有神经，信号传递靠激素、化学信号和维管组织中的电信号，响应以生长和代谢调节为主，速度远慢于动物的肢体运动。Gravitropism uses statoliths in the root cap. These starch-filled plastids settle in the gravity direction, and the signal drives roots downward and shoots upward; together the two tropisms put the shoot where light reaches and the root where water and minerals lie. Plants have no nerves; signalling runs through hormones, chemical signals and electrical impulses in vascular tissue, with responses expressed as growth and metabolism, far slower than animal movement.[8]

叶片呈绿色与光合吸收的波段有关。叶绿素位于叶绿体类囊体膜上，叶绿素 a 在约 430 nm 的蓝光和 660 nm 的红光处有吸收峰，类胡萝卜素等辅助色素吸收部分蓝紫光并把能量转给叶绿素；绿光大部分被反射或透射，进入眼睛的光以绿色为主。光反应裂解水生成 ATP 和 NADPH，卡尔文循环固定二氧化碳合成糖；缺氮或衰老时叶绿素分解，才会显现类胡萝卜素的黄橙色。植物需要阳光，是因为光能是把二氧化碳和水固定成有机物的能量来源。Photosynthesis and green colour rest on chlorophyll in thylakoid membranes. Chlorophyll a peaks near 430 nm in blue light and 660 nm in red light, while most green light is reflected or transmitted, which is why leaves look green. Light reactions split water to make ATP and NADPH, and the Calvin cycle fixes carbon dioxide into sugar. Plants need sunlight because light energy is the power source for fixing CO2 and water into organic matter.[1][7]

不同植物对光强的适应范围不同。阴生植物在弱光下提高叶绿素比例，把更多能量分配给捕光色素，仍能维持光合；阳生叶厚而色深，栅栏组织多层，靠蒸腾散热，阴生叶薄而舒展。光照不足时净光合可能低于呼吸消耗，需要储存的有机物维持生长；光不仅供能，还作为信号调节种子萌发、分枝和开花，叶片色素组成也会随光照强度调整。Species adapt to different light levels. Shade plants raise their chlorophyll ratio under weak light and still maintain photosynthesis; sun leaves are thick and dark, shade leaves thin and spread. Below a light threshold net photosynthesis can fall below respiration, so stored organic matter sustains growth. Light acts not only as energy but also as a signal regulating germination, branching and flowering, and pigment composition adjusts with light intensity.[1][7]

边界：向性是激素介导的生长反应，研究多以幼苗和茎尖为对象，成株树冠调整还涉及结构因素；叶色深浅不能单独代表光合效率，还要看叶龄、营养和温度条件。完全不含叶绿素的寄生植物和菌异养植物不能自营光合，直接从宿主或共生真菌获取有机物，是无光环境下的例外。Boundary: tropism is a hormone-mediated growth response, mostly studied in seedlings and shoot tips, while mature canopy adjustment involves structural factors. Leaf greenness alone does not set photosynthetic efficiency, which also depends on leaf age, nutrition and temperature. Chlorophyll-lacking parasites and mycoheterotrophs cannot photosynthesise on their own and obtain organic matter from hosts or symbiotic fungi, the exceptions that live without light.[1][8]

06植物细胞为什么有细胞壁？为什么植物细胞没有中心粒？Why do plant cells have a cell wall, and why do plant cells lack centrioles?

纤维素微纤丝构成壁骨架，膨压与壁约束平衡维持硬挺；被子植物无中心粒，纺锤体自行组装。Cellulose microfibrils form the wall skeleton; turgor balances wall restraint for firmness; angiosperms lack centrioles and assemble the spindle without them.

植物细胞外层有一层细胞壁，位于细胞膜之外。壁的骨架由纤维素微纤丝交织而成，再与基质多糖、果胶等填充分层；幼嫩细胞先形成较薄的初生壁，可随生长扩展，停止生长后部分细胞在膜与初生壁之间沉积次生壁，木质部和纤维组织成熟后还沉积木质素加厚。细胞壁维持细胞形状，把细胞约束在固定轮廓内，并防止细胞在低渗环境中吸水后胀破，是植物细胞区别于动物细胞的外层结构之一。A plant cell carries a cell wall outside its plasma membrane. The wall skeleton is a woven network of cellulose microfibrils embedded in matrix polysaccharides and pectins; xylem and fibre tissues later deposit lignin to thicken. The wall holds the cell in a fixed outline and prevents it from bursting when water enters under hypotonic conditions, and it is one of the outer structures that separate plant from animal cells.[6][9]

支撑靠壁与膨压的平衡。成熟植物细胞有大液泡，吸水后细胞内膨压约在 0.1–1 MPa 量级，对壁产生向外的压力；壁的约束与膨压相互平衡，使幼嫩茎、叶保持硬挺，也让细胞质紧贴壁面维持紧张度。植物没有骨骼，草本植物幼茎无木质骨架，地上部分的形态主要靠膨压和厚壁组织共同维持；失水时膨压下降，组织失去支撑，就表现为萎蔫。Support comes from the balance of wall and turgor. A mature plant cell has a large vacuole; when it takes up water, internal turgor reaches roughly 0.1 to 1 MPa and presses outward on the wall. Wall restraint balances this pressure, keeping young stems and leaves firm. Plants have no skeleton, so above-ground form rests on turgor and thick-walled tissue together; when water is lost, turgor drops, support is lost and the tissue wilts.[9]

壁随生长重塑并按组织特化。细胞伸长时壁先松弛、局部重塑，允许体积扩大；成熟后部分细胞次生壁加厚并木质化。表皮壁外覆角质层减少失水，纤维壁厚而坚韧起机械加固，导管壁加厚后木质化以承受水分运输中的负压；导管分子、厚壁纤维和表皮细胞的壁结构各不相同，分别承担水分运输、机械支持和保护功能。纹孔和胞间连丝允许相邻细胞之间交换水分与物质，维管组织借此把水和养分分配到各处。The wall remodels with growth and specialises by tissue. During elongation the wall loosens and remodels locally to allow volume to expand; after maturity some cells add a thick secondary wall and lignify. Vessel elements, sclerenchyma fibres and epidermal cells have different wall structures for water transport, mechanical support and protection; pits and plasmodesmata let neighbouring cells exchange water and materials, and vascular tissue distributes water and nutrients throughout.[6][7]

高等植物细胞分裂时没有中心粒。动物细胞的中心粒组成中心体，组织纺锤体微管；被子植物细胞中没有中心粒结构，纺锤体由核周微管组织区自行组装，微管从两极发出并附着在染色体着丝粒上牵引分离，染色体分离照常完成。中心粒常见于动物细胞和多数低等植物、藻类，在被子植物演化过程中丢失；它只是微管组织的一种辅助结构，缺少中心粒并不影响有丝分裂进行。Higher plant cells divide without centrioles. In animal cells centrioles form the centrosome that organises spindle microtubules; angiosperm cells lack centrioles, and the spindle assembles from perinuclear microtubule organising regions, so chromosome separation still completes. Centrioles occur in animal cells and many lower plants and algae, but were lost in the angiosperm lineage; their absence does not block mitosis.[9]

边界：不同生物的壁成分不同，真菌壁为几丁质、细菌壁为肽聚糖，与植物纤维素壁区分开；细胞壁在幼嫩细胞是可生长的活层，成熟木质化后才成为死的机械层。萎蔫通常是水分不足、膨压下降，而不是壁本身破裂；细胞能否保持硬挺，还受水分供应、组织年龄和土壤养分影响。Boundary: wall composition differs across groups; fungal walls are chitin and bacterial walls peptidoglycan, unlike the cellulose wall of plants. The wall is a growable living layer in young cells and becomes a dead mechanical layer only after lignification. Wilting usually means water shortage and low turgor rather than a ruptured wall, and firmness also depends on water supply, tissue age and soil nutrients.[7][9]

07植物为什么没有像动物那样的器官系统？Why do plants lack organ systems like those in animals?

植物模块化生长，分生组织持续补充器官；细胞壁液泡储能，结构层级到器官为止，无神经循环系统。Plants grow modularly with meristems adding organs; cell walls and vacuoles do the work, and the hierarchy stops at organs, with no nervous or circulatory system.

动物身体在胚胎发育完成后，器官数目和位置基本固定，再由神经、循环、消化等器官系统分工协同；植物的结构层级到器官为止，没有“器官系统”这一层。植物身体由细胞、组织、根、茎、叶等器官构成，再多器官也直接挂在植物体上，不组成类似动物心、肝、肺那样协同运作的系统层级。这一体制差异来自固着生活方式，而不是构造上的残缺。After embryonic development, an animal has a roughly fixed number and position of organs, coordinated by organ systems such as the nervous, circulatory and digestive systems. Plants stop at the organ level and have no organ-system tier. Their body is built from cells, tissues and organs such as roots, stems and leaves, which attach directly to the plant body rather than forming systems that work together like an animal heart, liver and lungs. This body-plan difference follows a sessile lifestyle, not a structural defect.[10]

模块化生长是植物没有器官系统的关键原因。根尖、茎尖和侧生形成层保留分生组织，整个生命周期持续分裂，产生新的根、叶和侧枝；成熟植株有多少叶片、多少分枝并不固定，水分养分充足时多长，环境差时脱落或暂停。动物需要在发育早期就把各系统装配到位，植物则靠分生组织不断添补模块，身体结构随环境调整。成熟植物组织分为保护组织、基本组织和维管组织 3 类，营养器官包括根、茎、叶 3 类，花、果实、种子属于生殖器官，分类数量随生长持续增减。Modular growth is the key reason plants lack organ systems. Root tips, shoot tips and lateral cambium keep meristematic tissue that divides through the life cycle to produce new roots, leaves and branches; an adult plant has no fixed number of leaves or branches, growing more when water and nutrients are ample and shedding or pausing under poor conditions. Animals must assemble their systems early in development, while plants keep adding modules from meristems and tune body structure to the environment. Mature plant tissues fall into three types: dermal, ground and vascular; vegetative organs are roots, stems and leaves, three classes to which flowers, fruits and seeds are added as reproductive organs, and these numbers keep changing with growth.[6][10]

细胞层面的结构替代了部分动物系统的功能。细胞壁提供机械支撑，大液泡储水并维持膨压，让草本个体保持挺立；木质部和韧皮部组成维管组织，承担水、矿质和光合产物的长距离运输。植物没有肌肉、心脏和血管，主动运动和循环运输由膨压变化、蒸腾拉力以及激素与化学信号完成，结构上不需要一套专门的循环器官。Cell-level structures take over some animal-system functions. Cell walls give mechanical support, and a large vacuole stores water and maintains turgor so herbaceous plants stay upright; xylem and phloem form vascular tissue that transports water, minerals and photosynthate over long distances. Plants have no muscles, heart or blood vessels, and movement and circulation are handled by turgor changes, transpiration pull, hormones and chemical signals, so no dedicated circulatory organ is needed.[6][7]

信号传递方式也不同。植物没有神经元和大脑，感受光、重力、水分和触碰后，靠激素、化学物质以及维管组织中的电信号传递信息，响应以生长、开花、休眠和膨压变化等慢过程为主。这套系统能调整方向和节奏，但处理速度和通讯方式与动物神经系统不在同一层级；把植物的化学信号交流理解成语言或意识，属于越界解释。Signal transmission differs as well. Plants have no neurons or brain; after sensing light, gravity, water or touch, they pass information through hormones, chemicals and electrical signals in vascular tissue, and respond mainly through slow processes such as growth, flowering, dormancy and turgor changes. This system adjusts direction and pace, but its speed and communication mode are not on the same tier as an animal nervous system; reading plant chemical signalling as language or consciousness goes beyond the evidence.[10]

边界：没有器官系统不等于低等，而是对固着生活的适应。模块化生长和再生能力让植物耐修剪、耐动物取食，受损后可以从分生组织重新长出器官。不同植物类群的结构复杂度差别很大：苔藓等早期陆生植物维管组织简单，没有真正的根、茎、叶，不能用种子植物那套器官标准去衡量；讨论体制差异时，要把生活方式、结构层级和发育模式分开看。The boundary: lacking organ systems is not a mark of lower status but an adaptation to sessile life. Modular growth and regeneration let plants survive pruning and herbivory, regrowing organs from meristems after damage. Structural complexity varies greatly among plant groups: mosses and other early land plants have simple vascular tissue and no true roots, stems or leaves, and cannot be measured by seed-plant organ standards. When comparing body plans, lifestyle, structural tier and developmental mode should be kept separate.[7][10]

08向日葵为什么会跟着太阳转？Why does a sunflower follow the Sun across the sky?

幼嫩花盘靠茎两侧不均等伸长随日转动，夜间回转朝东；成熟开花后固定朝东。Young heads track the Sun through unequal stem growth, reorient east at night, and stay facing east once mature.

会追着太阳转的主要是还没开花的幼嫩向日葵。清晨花盘朝东迎接日出，白天慢慢转向西方日落，夜里又转回来朝东等第二天早晨。这种随太阳方位转动的现象叫向日性，是植物向光反应的一种特殊形式。等花苞开放、茎秆停止伸长以后，花盘就不再逐日转动，而是固定朝向东方；所以成熟向日葵并非全天都在扭头。It is mainly the young, unopened sunflower head that follows the Sun. In the morning the head faces east to greet sunrise, slowly turns toward the western sunset during the day, and turns back east at night to wait for the next dawn. This turning with the Sun's position is called heliotropism, a special form of phototropic response. Once the flower opens and the stem stops elongating, the head no longer tracks daily; it stays fixed facing east, so a mature sunflower is not turning its head all day.[8]

转动来自茎两侧生长速度的差异。向日葵茎的背光一侧生长更快，把茎推向光源方向；光受体感知蓝光方位，生长激素（如生长素 IAA）在两侧重新分配，调节细胞伸长速率。白天东侧和西侧生长节奏交替变化，使茎顶端和花盘缓缓转向太阳。近年研究也发现，向光侧可能产生抑制生长的物质，两侧伸长不均等由促进与抑制共同决定，不只是单一激素梯度。The turning comes from different growth rates on the two sides of the stem. The shaded side of the sunflower stem grows faster, pushing the stem toward the light; photoreceptors sense the direction of blue light, and growth hormones such as auxin (IAA) redistribute between the sides to regulate cell elongation. During the day the growth rhythm alternates between east and west sides, so the stem tip and head turn gradually toward the Sun. Recent work also finds that the lit side may produce growth-inhibiting substances; unequal elongation is set by both promotion and inhibition, not just a single hormone gradient.[8][11]

夜间的回转由内在节律驱动。没有光照方向参考时，茎按约 24 小时的昼夜节律反向调整生长，花盘从西侧转回东方，在日出前就已经就位。这种内源节律与光信号配合，使转动不会被阴天打乱。研究向日葵茎段基因表达发现，向日性调用的光信号通路与普通向光弯曲有重叠，也有自己独特的一组基因表达模式，说明它不是简单地把向光性放大。The night-time reorientation is driven by an internal rhythm. Without a directional light reference, the stem adjusts growth in reverse according to a circadian rhythm, moving the head from west back to east so it is already in place before sunrise. This endogenous rhythm works together with light signals so the turning is not disrupted by cloudy days. Gene-expression studies of sunflower stems find that heliotropism shares some light-signalling pathways with ordinary phototropic bending but also has its own distinct expression pattern, so it is not simply an amplified phototropism.[11]

成熟后固定朝东有生态意义。早晨朝东的花盘在阳光下升温更快，温暖的花盘更吸引蜜蜂等传粉昆虫，有观测记录朝东花盘吸引的传粉者可比朝西多出数倍；固定方向也避免成熟茎秆反复弯曲消耗能量。至于普通盆栽花草向窗光歪头，是持续单向光下的向光生长，与向日葵这种逐日来回转动的向日性并不完全相同。Staying fixed east after maturity has an ecological purpose. An east-facing head warms faster in the morning sun, and a warmer head attracts more pollinators such as bees; observations record east-facing heads drawing several times more pollinators than west-facing ones. A fixed direction also saves the energy that a mature stem would spend bending back and forth. When an ordinary houseplant leans toward a window, that is phototropic growth under steady one-sided light, not quite the same as the daily back-and-forth heliotropism of a sunflower.[6][11]

边界：向日性只发生在仍能伸长的幼嫩茎段，木质化老茎无法靠伸长弯曲；转动幅度受品种、株龄、光照和温度影响，阴天或室内弱光下不明显。把向日葵追日说成有眼睛跟着太阳看是拟人化，实际是光受体、激素和生长速率差异共同造成的机械弯曲。本章描述生长机制，不涉及品种选育或种植施肥建议。The boundary: heliotropism occurs only in young stem sections that can still elongate; a woody old stem cannot bend by elongation. The amount of turning depends on variety, age, light and temperature, and is weak on cloudy days or in dim indoor light. Saying the sunflower has eyes that watch the Sun is anthropomorphic; the bending is a mechanical result of photoreceptors, hormones and differing growth rates. This chapter describes growth mechanisms only, and gives no advice on breeding, planting or fertilizing.[8]
