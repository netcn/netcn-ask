01猫的呼噜是怎么发出来的？猫薄荷对猫起什么作用？How does a cat purr, and what does catnip do to cats?

喉部结缔组织垫配合肌肉节律产生 20–150 Hz 振动；荆芥内酯触发可遗传的反应。Laryngeal pads and rhythmic muscles produce 20–150 Hz vibration; nepetalactone triggers a heritable response.

猫的呼噜是喉部肌肉周期性收缩产生的低频振动，常出现在放松或需要自我安抚的情境；猫薄荷等植物通过挥发油触发嗅觉与行为反应，反应强度受遗传影响。呼噜频率多在约 20 到 150 赫兹区间，常见录音集中在低频段，振动可以通过骨骼和软组织传到体表，人把手放在猫喉部或躯干上常能感到轻微震颤，频率分布受个体和当下情境影响。A cat's purr is a low-frequency vibration produced by periodic contraction of the laryngeal muscles, occurring in relaxed or self-soothing situations; catnip and related plants trigger olfactory and behavioral responses through volatile oils, with intensity shaped by genetics. Purr frequencies fall mostly in the 20 to 150 hertz range, commonly recorded in the low band, and vibration reaches the body surface through bone and soft tissue.[1][2]

Herbst 等人 2023 年 Current Biology 的解剖研究发现，猫喉部声带附近存在结缔组织垫，配合喉部肌肉节律性开闭，让气流产生振动而不必逐次由脑发出神经脉冲。呼噜可在吸气和呼气时持续，发声时嘴部外形变化不大，这与通常靠声带主动发声的方式有区别，研究把这种发声机制称为声带加组织垫的被动振动。A 2023 Current Biology anatomical study by Herbst and colleagues found connective-tissue pads near the cat's vocal folds; together with rhythmic opening and closing of laryngeal muscles, they let airflow vibrate without separate neural pulses for each cycle. Purring continues through both inhalation and exhalation, with little change in mouth shape, which differs from ordinary active vocalization through the vocal folds.[2]

猫在被抚摸、休息和幼猫哺乳时呼噜，受伤或紧张时也可能呼噜作为自我安抚；呼噜不等于单纯的快乐信号，需要结合耳朵、尾巴和身体姿态判断。幼猫在哺乳期就开始用呼噜与母猫交流，成年后把这一信号扩展到与人类的互动，呼噜的含义随情境改变，安静环境和惊扰环境中的同一种振动要分开解读。Cats purr when petted, resting and nursing as kittens, and may also purr when injured or stressed as self-soothing; purring alone is not a happiness signal and must be read with ears, tail and body posture. Kittens begin purring to communicate with the mother during nursing, and adults extend this signal to interactions with people, so its meaning shifts with context.[1][2]

猫薄荷中的荆芥内酯挥发油结合鼻腔嗅觉受体，引发嗅闻、舔舐、翻滚和蹭脸等反应；多数猫对该类植物有反应，无反应个体与遗传背景有关。反应在幼猫一定月龄后出现，持续数分钟后消退，随后进入一段不应期，反复接触同一植物不会立刻再次触发，木天蓼等其他植物也会引发类似但不完全相同的反应。The nepetalactone volatile oil in catnip binds nasal olfactory receptors and triggers sniffing, licking, rolling and face rubbing. Most cats respond to such plants, while non-responders track genetic background. The response appears after kittens reach a certain age, lasts a few minutes, then fades into a refractory period during which the same plant will not trigger it again at once.[3]

呼噜频率与骨修复、镇痛的关联仍是假说，现有证据不足以把呼噜当作治疗手段；猫薄荷反应个体差异大，绝育、年龄和接触经历都会影响表现。不能仅凭呼噜判断猫是否疼痛或高兴，综合耳朵、尾巴、瞳孔和身体姿态等行为线索才更可靠，长期行为变化还应结合饮食、排便和兽医检查，观察周期应持续数天，而不是凭单一场合下结论。Links between purr frequency and bone repair or pain relief remain hypotheses, and current evidence does not support treating purring as a therapy. Catnip response varies widely between individuals, altered by neutering, age and prior exposure. Purring alone cannot tell whether a cat is in pain or content, and combined cues of ears, tail, pupils and posture are more reliable.[2][3]

02狗为什么摇尾巴？吐舌喘气是在出汗吗，舔舐又表达什么？Why do dogs wag their tails, is panting sweating, and what does licking mean?

尾巴位置与偏向反映唤醒状态；喘气靠黏膜蒸发散热，舔舐混合梳理与社交信息。Tail position and bias show arousal; panting cools by mucosal evaporation; licking mixes grooming and social cues.

狗摇尾巴是情绪与注意力的外露信号，尾巴位置、摆动幅度和摆动偏向对应不同唤醒程度；吐舌喘气主要是蒸发散热，狗的汗腺分布有限，不能像人一样靠全身出汗降温。肢体信号和呼吸调节都需要结合环境温度、运动状态和身体姿态一起解读，单看一个动作容易误判，同一动作在不同犬种间也存在品种特有的幅度差别。Tail wagging in dogs is an outward signal of emotion and attention: tail position, amplitude and lateral bias correspond to different arousal levels. Panting with the tongue out is mainly evaporative cooling, because dogs have limited sweat glands and cannot cool themselves by whole-body sweating like humans. Both body signals and breathing regulation must be read together with ambient temperature, exercise state and posture.[1][6]

尾巴高举表示警觉和自信，低垂或夹在腿间表示顺从或紧张，快速小幅摆动对应高唤醒状态。近年研究还观察到摆动偏向与情绪侧化有关，摇尾本身不等于友好，需结合耳朵方向、身体紧张度和面部表情判断，避免把所有摇尾都读成欢迎。尾巴还参与平衡和转向，运动中的摆动不能只当作情绪表达。A raised tail signals alertness and confidence, a lowered or tucked tail signals submission or tension, and fast small wagging reflects high arousal. Recent work also links wagging side bias to emotional lateralization; wagging itself does not mean friendliness, and ear direction, body tension and facial expression are needed to avoid reading every wag as welcome.[1]

狗舌头和呼吸道黏膜血管扩张，快速呼吸使唾液和黏膜水分蒸发，带走热量；汗腺主要集中在脚垫，皮肤表面分泌有限。环境温度高、运动后或兴奋时喘气加快，若喘气持续、舌色发紫或行为呆滞，则可能是过热信号，需要及时降温并观察。脚垫出汗主要在紧张和防滑时出现，并不承担主要体温调节，湿冷天气下唾液蒸发慢，散热效果相应减弱。Dogs dilate blood vessels in the tongue and respiratory mucosa, and rapid breathing evaporates moisture from saliva and mucosal surfaces to carry away heat; sweat glands are concentrated mainly in the paw pads, with limited skin secretion. Panting quickens in heat, after exercise or when excited; persistent panting, a purple tongue or dull behavior may signal overheating and require prompt cooling and observation.[6]

狗舔人结合了梳理行为记忆、味觉探测和社会联系；舔舐可以收集皮肤上的盐分和气味信息，也是幼年哺乳行为的延伸。猫舔舐兼具梳理、降温信息素和社交标记作用，舌面倒刺负责除去杂毛和异物，舔舐频率在放松和求安抚两种情境下都可能出现。互相舔舐在同群动物中还承担缓和冲突和维持等级的作用，幼畜向成畜舔舐行为的过渡与断奶阶段相关。Dog licking of people combines grooming memory, taste exploration and social bonding; licking gathers salt and odor information from skin and extends infant nursing behavior. Cat licking combines grooming, scent marking and social signals, with tongue papillae removing loose hair and debris; licking frequency appears in both relaxation and comfort-seeking contexts.[1][5]

肢体信号要在完整情境中解读，同一摇尾在不同背景下含义不同；喘气散热效率受湿度影响，高湿环境蒸发变慢，散热能力下降。不能把所有舔舐都理解为表达爱意，取食气味和缓解焦虑也是常见动机，过度舔舐持续不缓解时还可能反映皮肤不适或行为问题，需要结合皮肤状态和环境变化判断。Body signals must be read in full context, since the same wag means different things in different settings; panting cooling efficiency depends on humidity, and evaporation slows in moist air. Not all licking expresses affection: sampling food smells and relieving anxiety are common motives, and persistent excessive licking can also indicate skin discomfort or a behavioral problem.[1][5]

03犬猫怎样使用嗅觉？它们能认出主人的照片吗？How do dogs and cats use smell, and can they recognize pictures of their owners?

狗有约 1.5 亿–3 亿受体细胞；认人同时依赖声音、气味与整体情境。Dogs have 150–300 million receptor cells; person recognition mixes sound, odor and overall context.

犬猫主要靠嗅觉获取环境信息，狗的嗅觉受体数量和嗅球相对比例远高于人类，能够分辨混合气味中的不同成分；对主人形象的识别则是视觉、听觉和嗅觉线索的综合判断，并非单独靠图片完成。嗅觉在定位、社交和情绪探测中承担主要角色，视觉更多用于近距离辨认姿态和表情。Dogs and cats gather most environmental information through smell, and dogs have far more olfactory receptors and a larger relative olfactory bulb than humans, allowing them to separate components of mixed odors. Recognition of an owner's image, by contrast, combines visual, auditory and olfactory cues rather than relying on pictures alone; smell carries the main load in locating, socializing and detecting mood.[1][6]

狗的嗅觉受体细胞估计约 1.5 亿到 3 亿个，人类约 500 万个；鼻腔内褶皱增大受体面积，嗅叶在脑中占比更高。狗可以追踪随时间扩散的气味层，区分个体、情绪状态和经过路径，经过训练后承担搜救、缉私和医疗探测等任务，不同工作方向利用的是嗅觉分辨与追踪能力，受训犬还能识别特定疾病相关的挥发性气味，不同品种的嗅闻偏好随选育方向出现差别。A dog's olfactory receptor cells are estimated at 150 to 300 million, versus about 5 million in humans; nasal folds enlarge the receptor area, and the olfactory lobe occupies a larger share of the brain. Dogs can track odor trails that diffuse over time, distinguish individuals and emotional states, and after training perform search-and-rescue, detection and medical screening, with different tasks using discrimination versus tracking ability.[6]

嗅上皮结合气味分子后产生神经信号，脑内嗅球先做初步分离，不同气味成分被并行处理；犬类呼气侧孔帮助旧气味排出，保持吸气通道新鲜。猫嗅觉同样灵敏，但行为上更多用于领地标记和近距离辨识，气味在猫的社群交流中以粪便和腺体标记为主，猫对同类和环境气味的反应常通过蹭擦和抓挠表达，爪垫和面部腺体也参与留下可被同类读取的气味标记。After the olfactory epithelium binds odor molecules, signals reach the olfactory bulb for initial separation, and different odor components are processed in parallel. Side nostrils help expel stale air and keep the inhalation channel fresh. Cats are similarly sensitive, but smell serves mainly territory marking and close recognition, with social odor communication carried by feces and gland marks.[1][6]

狗对主人照片或面容的识别实验显示，狗能在熟悉与陌生面孔间做区分，但成绩依赖同时呈现的声音和气味线索；脱离其他线索时，纯视觉辨别的稳定性低于人类。狗识别人主要靠整体熟悉情境，而不是像人那样把面部特征拆成独立单元比较，熟悉的步态、衣着和声音都会参与判断。Experiments on dog recognition of owner photos or faces show dogs can distinguish familiar from unfamiliar faces, but performance depends on sound and odor cues presented together; without those cues, pure visual discrimination is less stable than in humans. Dogs identify people through overall familiar context rather than breaking facial features into separate units the way humans do.[1]

嗅觉灵敏度的数字随测量方法和品种不同而变化，不能用单一受体数排名所有犬种；对图片的理解与人类识别人脸的机制不同，狗更多依赖整体情境和熟悉线索。嗅探能力还受训练、年龄和健康状态影响，老年或呼吸道疾病会降低气味分辨表现，疲劳和注意力分散也会缩短有效嗅探时间，长期训练可以保持嗅探专注，但不能改变先天受体量级。Olfactory sensitivity figures vary with measurement method and breed, so a single receptor count cannot rank all dog breeds; picture recognition differs from human face processing, and dogs lean on overall context and familiar cues. Sniffing ability also depends on training, age and health, and old age or respiratory disease reduces odor discrimination.[4][6]

04狗被驯化了多久？犬种体型为什么差异这么大？How long have dogs been domesticated, and why is dog size so diverse?

驯化至少两万多年；IGF1 等少数位点配合选育形成从玩赏犬到工作犬的跨度。Domesticated at least 20,000 years; IGF1 and other loci plus selection span toy to working dogs.

狗由灰狼在数万年前被逐步驯化，长期伴随人类生活；犬种体型差异之大，从小型玩赏犬到大型工作犬的跨度远超其他野生犬科。体型多样性既有选育历史，也有少数主要基因位点控制，驯化与人工选育共同塑造了今天的犬种外形，野生祖先的体型范围远小于现代犬种之间的差距。Dogs were domesticated gradually from gray wolves tens of thousands of years ago and have long lived alongside people; size diversity across breeds, from tiny companion dogs to large working dogs, far exceeds that of other wild canids. This diversity reflects both a selection history and a few major gene loci, with domestication and artificial selection together shaping modern breed appearance.[1][4]

遗传学和考古证据支持狗在距今至少两万多年前与人类分道，早期驯化围绕营地警戒、协作狩猎和食物分享展开。现代犬种的外形分化主要来自近几百年的人工选育，不同地区按功能选择护卫、放牧、寻回和陪伴类型，品种标准在相对短的时间内固定了毛色、耳型和骨架差异，很多外形特征在野生状态下并不具备适应优势，早期犬只外形更接近灰狼，体型和毛色分化较晚才出现。Genetic and archaeological evidence places the split between dogs and humans at least more than 20,000 years ago, and early domestication centered on camp guarding, cooperative hunting and food sharing. The modern divergence in breed appearance comes mainly from artificial selection over the past few centuries, with regions choosing dogs for guarding, herding, retrieving or companionship; breed standards fixed coat color, ear shape and skeleton over a relatively short period.[1][4]

研究定位到 IGF1 等少数位点对小型体型有显著影响，相关等位基因在小型犬中频率高；大型犬则保留更多生长促进等位基因。毛色、耳型和骨架长度由多个位点共同决定，品种选育把这些差异逐代加强，最终出现同一物种内罕见的体型跨度，体重差距可以从几公斤到上百公斤，除 IGF1 外，骨架长度和肌肉量还受其他生长位点调节。Studies identify a few loci such as IGF1 with a strong effect on small size, with the relevant allele frequent in small dogs; large dogs retain more growth-promoting alleles. Coat color, ear shape and skeleton length are jointly determined by several loci, and selection reinforced these differences generation after generation, producing a size range rare within a single species.[4]

伴随驯化，犬类在毛色、头骨比例、下颌和行为温顺程度上出现一系列变化，与人类长期选择温顺反应有关。猫的驯化程度较浅，更多保留独立觅食和领地行为，体型分化也小于犬；猫更多是半共生式地进入人类营地，选育方向偏近于控鼠和陪伴，外形变化远不如犬种剧烈。Alongside domestication, dogs changed in coat color, skull proportion, jaw and behavioral tameness, tied to long-term selection for docile reactions. Cats remain less domesticated, retaining more independent foraging and territorial behavior, with less body-size divergence; they entered human camps partly as a semi-commensal relationship, selected mainly for rodent control and companionship.[1][6]

驯化时间的具体年代仍有考古与遗传证据的争论，不同研究给出的区间不一致；体型差异不能简单对应智力或服从性，品种选育目标不同。跨品种杂交会重新组合等位基因，后代体型并不固定继承父母某一方，极端小型或大型特征在杂交后代中可能重新分离，性格与工作倾向也会随选配组合变化，绝育时间也会影响部分生长板闭合节奏，进而影响体型。The exact timing of domestication remains debated between archaeological and genetic evidence, and study intervals differ. Size differences cannot be equated with intelligence or obedience, since breeds were selected for different tasks. Cross-breeding recombines alleles, so offspring size does not simply follow one parent, and extreme small or large traits can re-segregate in hybrid offspring.[1][4]

05为什么大型犬比小型犬寿命短？犬猫一般能活多久？Why do large dogs live shorter than small ones, and how long do dogs and cats live?

巨型犬约 7–10 年，小型犬约 12–13 年，室内猫约 12–15 年；体型与健康管理共同决定寿命。Giant dogs 7–10 years, small dogs 12–13, indoor cats 12–15; size and health care jointly set longevity.

大型犬平均寿命短于小型犬，体型越大的犬种中年以后衰老相关疾病出现越早；猫在室内饲养条件下普遍活到十几岁。犬猫寿命受体型、品种、绝育、饮食和医疗条件共同影响，群体平均趋势不能直接套到单个个体，同一品种内部也会因血缘和饲养条件拉开差距。Large dogs live shorter on average than small dogs, and the larger the breed, the earlier age-related disease appears after midlife; cats commonly reach their teens when kept indoors. Dog and cat longevity is shaped by body size, breed, neutering, diet and medical care together, and group averages cannot be applied directly to any individual.[5][6]

英格兰犬群调查中，犬只中位寿命约十岁出头，小型玩赏犬常达十二到十三年，巨型犬种多在七到十年；室内猫平均寿命约十二到十五年。大型犬生长周期长，细胞增殖和骨骼关节负担更大，中年后肿瘤和骨科问题更常见，健康寿命与存活年限需要分开统计，能保持活动能力的年限往往比总存活年限更值得关注，猫的品种间体型差距小于犬种，寿命差距也相对小。In an English dog-population survey, median dog longevity was just over ten years: small toy breeds often reached twelve to thirteen, while giant breeds usually lived seven to ten; indoor cats averaged twelve to fifteen years. Large dogs have longer growth periods and heavier burden on cell proliferation and bones and joints, so tumors and orthopedic problems appear more often after midlife; healthy lifespan and survival years should be counted separately.[5]

体型与寿命的关系在犬种内部比跨物种更明显；快速生长可能伴随细胞修复和衰老进程的差异。大型犬心脏负荷和关节磨损更高，部分品种存在遗传易感疾病，缩短健康年限。体重超标、运动不足和品种高发疾病都会叠加影响实际寿命，长期定量喂养和控制体重可以减轻关节与心脏负担，慢性炎症和口腔问题同样会削弱老年犬的生活质量。The size-longevity relationship is clearer within dog breeds than across species; rapid growth may accompany differences in cell repair and aging. Large dogs carry higher cardiac load and joint wear, and some breeds carry genetic predispositions that shorten healthy life. Excess weight, insufficient exercise and breed-specific diseases stack on top of these effects.[5][6]

猫寿命受室内外活动影响，室外猫遭遇交通事故、传染病和打斗的风险更高；绝育、疫苗和定期体检能延长健康年限。犬猫衰老速度不能按人类年龄倍数简单换算，不同体型换算比例不同，把七岁狗直接等同于七十岁老人并不准确，牙齿、关节和心肺状态才是更可靠的衰老指标，室内环境减少了外界暴露，但运动量不足会反向影响心肺和体重。Cat longevity depends on indoor versus outdoor life: outdoor cats face higher traffic, infectious-disease and fight risks; neutering, vaccination and regular checkups extend healthy years. Aging speed cannot be converted by a simple human-age multiple, and the ratio differs with body size, so equating a seven-year-old dog with a seventy-year-old person is inaccurate.[1][6]

寿命统计来自特定地区和饲养群体，不能直接套到其他国家或流浪种群；品种平均寿命掩盖个体差异，饮食、运动和遗传背景都会改变实际年限。大型犬短寿是群体趋势，不代表每只大型犬都必然早逝，个体健康管理可以在一定程度上延长其健康生活时间，定期体检和早发现疾病对大型犬尤为重要。Longevity statistics come from particular regions and kept populations and should not be transferred directly to other countries or feral groups; breed averages mask individual differences, and diet, exercise and genetic background all alter actual years. Short lifespan in large dogs is a group trend, not a guarantee that every large dog dies young, and good individual care can extend healthy life to some degree.[1][5]

06狗为什么不能吃巧克力？可可碱为什么在犬体内排得那么慢？Why can't dogs eat chocolate, and why is theobromine cleared so slowly in dogs?

可可碱半衰期约 17.5 小时，犬肝酶代谢慢，剂量按体重累积到中毒量级。Theobromine half-life is about 17.5 h in dogs; slow liver clearance lets the dose accumulate.

巧克力对犬有毒，关键成分是可可碱（theobromine），另含少量咖啡因等甲基黄嘌呤类物质。人吃巧克力后，肝脏能较快把这类物质代谢掉；犬的相关肝酶代谢速度慢，可可碱在体内停留时间长，容易累积到引起反应的剂量。甲基黄嘌呤进入体内后会阻断腺苷受体，刺激中枢神经系统、心肌和平滑肌，这是中毒表现的生化基础。不同犬种、年龄和体重对剂量的耐受差别很大，小型犬按体重计算风险更高。Chocolate is toxic to dogs chiefly because of theobromine, with smaller amounts of caffeine and other methylxanthines. Humans metabolize these compounds fairly quickly in the liver, while dogs clear the related methylxanthines more slowly, so theobromine stays in circulation long enough to accumulate to an active dose. Methylxanthines block adenosine receptors and stimulate the central nervous system, heart muscle and smooth muscle, which is the biochemical basis of poisoning. Tolerance varies with breed, age and body weight, and small dogs carry a higher risk per kilogram.[7][8]

剂量按每公斤体重估算：文献中轻症（烦躁、多动）约在 20 mg/kg 附近，心血管症状多出现在 40 mg/kg 以上，震颤或抽搐常在 60 mg/kg 以上，致死剂量量级约 100–200 mg/kg，不同研究给出的区间在 100–500 mg/kg，也有 115 mg/kg 即致死的记录。巧克力类型决定可可碱浓度：烘焙巧克力和黑巧克力浓度高，约 5–20 mg/g，牛奶巧克力约 2 mg/g，白巧克力含量极低。同样一块巧克力，对 5 公斤小型犬和 30 公斤大型犬的意义完全不同。Doses are estimated per kilogram of body weight. Literature places mild signs such as agitation around 20 mg/kg, cardiovascular signs mostly above 40 mg/kg, tremor or seizures above 60 mg/kg, and a lethal dose in the rough order of 100 to 200 mg/kg; studies report a wider range of 100 to 500 mg/kg, with deaths recorded after 115 mg/kg. Concentration depends on the chocolate type: baking and dark chocolate are high, roughly 5 to 20 mg/g, milk chocolate about 2 mg/g, and white chocolate very low. The same bar means something very different for a 5-kilogram toy dog than for a 30-kilogram working dog.[8]

中毒表现随剂量上升：早期可见呕吐、腹泻、烦躁不安、多动和多饮；随后心率加快、心律不齐、肌肉震颤，严重时出现抽搐、虚脱。可可碱吸收后在体内缓慢清除，症状可能在进食后数小时才明显，持续时间也比一般食物不耐受长。犬的可可碱半衰期约 17.5 小时，人类代谢只需 2–3 小时量级，长半衰期意味着一次摄入后浓度下降慢，重复暴露会叠加。Signs escalate with dose: early vomiting, diarrhea, restlessness, hyperactivity and increased drinking, then faster heart rate, arrhythmia and muscle tremor, and in severe cases seizures or collapse. Because theobromine is absorbed and cleared slowly, signs may not appear for several hours after ingestion and last longer than ordinary food intolerance. Its half-life in dogs is about 17.5 hours, compared with roughly 2 to 3 hours in humans; this long clearance means concentration drops slowly after a single intake, and repeated exposures stack up.[7][8]

犬比人敏感，根源是代谢路径差异：甲基黄嘌呤在肝脏经去甲基化和结合反应清除，犬相关酶活性低、排泄慢；猫同样对这类物质敏感，但很少主动采食巧克力。葡萄、洋葱、木糖醇等对犬的风险机制各不相同，不能用同一张禁食清单概括，本章只解释巧克力这一类。Dogs are more sensitive because of metabolic differences: methylxanthines are cleared in the liver through demethylation and conjugation, and dogs have lower enzyme activity and slower excretion. Cats are similarly sensitive to these compounds but rarely seek out chocolate. Grapes, onions and xylitol each act through different mechanisms, so they cannot be collapsed into one banned-food list; this chapter covers chocolate only.[6]

边界：本章只讲机制与剂量量级，不是兽医诊疗建议，也不提供催吐、灌服或解毒操作。发现犬只误食巧克力，应立即联系兽医或动物中毒热线，告知巧克力类型、大概数量和犬只体重，由专业人员评估处理；不要自行喂药或等待症状出现再行动。把可可粉、烘焙巧克力和含可可的甜点放在犬只接触不到的地方，是环境层面的主要预防。Boundary: this chapter explains mechanisms and dose ranges, not veterinary treatment, and it gives no instructions for inducing vomiting, dosing medicines or decontamination. If a dog is known to have eaten chocolate, contact a veterinarian or a pet poison hotline immediately, reporting the chocolate type, estimated amount and body weight, and let professionals decide management. Do not self-treat or wait for signs to appear. Keeping cocoa powder, baking chocolate and cocoa-containing desserts out of reach is the main preventive measure at home.[7][8]

07狗为什么不能吃葡萄和葡萄干？Why are grapes and raisins toxic to dogs?

部分犬只食后出现急性肾损伤，酒石酸假说在验证中，个体与果实差异大。Some dogs develop acute kidney injury; the tartaric-acid hypothesis is still tested, with wide individual variation.

葡萄、葡萄干、黑加仑样的 Vitis 属果实以及罗望子，在部分犬只身上可诱发急性肾损伤；风险的不可预测性是它最突出的特点：有的犬只吃少量即发病，有的吃下更多却没有明显异常，不能凭上次吃了没事判断下次安全。中毒个案在犬只中报道最多，猫和雪貂仅有零星传闻，缺乏公开病例报告。Grapes, raisins, Vitis-relative currants and tamarind have been linked to acute kidney injury in some dogs. The most striking feature is unpredictability: some dogs fall ill after a small amount, while others eat more without obvious signs, so a previous harmless meal cannot be read as proof that the next one is safe. Case reports are most numerous in dogs; cats and ferrets have only anecdotal reports without published case series.[7]

肾毒性的具体机制至今没有完全定论。早年提出的假说包括果实表面的霉菌毒素、重金属或农药残留、维生素 D 过量、单宁不耐受、单糖过量摄入，以及低血容量或肾脏缺血。近年研究把目光投向酒石酸（tartaric acid）：犬肾近端小管经有机阴离子转运体 OAT1 把酒石酸摄入细胞，缺少类似人 OAT4 的向管腔排泄转运体，酸性物质在近端小管细胞内累积。这一假说有细胞与个案证据支持，但仍在积累之中，不能当作已经确定的结论。The exact nephrotoxic mechanism is not fully settled. Earlier hypotheses included mycotoxin contamination, heavy metals or pesticide residue, excess vitamin D, tannin intolerance, excessive simple sugars, hypovolemic shock and renal ischemia. Recent work points toward tartaric acid: canine proximal tubules take it up through the organic anion transporter OAT1 and lack an OAT4-like transporter that secretes it into tubular fluid in people, so the acid accumulates inside proximal tubular cells. This hypothesis has cellular and case evidence behind it, but it is still accumulating support and should not be treated as a settled conclusion.[9][10]

已报告的最低致病剂量差异很大：葡萄约 19.6 g/kg 体重，葡萄干约 2.8 g/kg；兽医手册提示一般超过每 4.5 公斤体重一颗葡萄或葡萄干，就可能含有足够酒石酸造成肾脏影响。临床表现通常在误食后 6–12 小时内出现呕吐、腹泻、嗜睡、厌食、多饮和脱水，血肌酐与尿素氮升高，部分犬只在 24–72 小时内进展为少尿或无尿性肾衰竭，一旦无尿，预后较差。Reported lowest toxic doses vary widely: about 19.6 g/kg body weight for grapes and 2.8 g/kg for raisins. Veterinary manuals suggest that more than one grape or raisin per 4.5 kilograms of body weight may contain enough tartaric acid to pose a renal risk. Clinical signs usually appear within 6 to 12 hours: vomiting, diarrhea, lethargy, anorexia, increased drinking and dehydration, with rising creatinine and BUN. Some dogs progress to oliguric or anuric renal failure within 24 to 72 hours, and prognosis is poor once anuria develops.[9][10]

个体与果实差异可以解释一部分不可预测性：酒石酸含量随品种、产地、季节和加工方式变化，浓缩的葡萄干单位重量风险更高；犬只年龄、肾功能状态、空腹与否和同时进食的其他食物也会影响结果。剂量阈值不能由少量个案简单外推，一颗葡萄对不同体重犬只的相对负荷也不同。Individual and fruit differences explain part of the unpredictability. Tartaric content varies with variety, origin, season and processing, and concentrated raisins carry a higher risk per unit weight; age, prior kidney status, fasting state and co-ingested food also affect the outcome. Thresholds cannot be extrapolated from a few cases, and one grape means a different relative load for dogs of different weights.[9]

边界：机制未定不等于可以试探性喂食。发现犬只误食葡萄、葡萄干或相关制品，应立即联系兽医或动物中毒热线，带上果实包装或估算数量；本章只讲机制与已知表现，不写催吐、补液剂量或其他处理操作。日常喂食以犬粮和兽医认可的食物为主，不要用葡萄、葡萄干喂犬，也避免掉落的果实被捡食。Boundary: an unsettled mechanism does not mean the fruit can be offered experimentally. If a dog has eaten grapes, raisins or related products, contact a veterinarian or pet poison hotline at once, bringing the packaging or an estimate of the amount. This chapter covers mechanism and known signs only, with no instructions for induced vomiting, fluid doses or other treatment. Keep a dog on a recognized diet and veterinarian-approved foods, do not feed grapes or raisins, and clean up fallen fruit before it is scavenged.[7][10]

08猫为什么会踩奶？猫为什么普遍怕水？Why do cats knead, and why are most cats afraid of water?

踩奶是哺乳期安抚行为残留；怕水来自被毛不防水、耳道结构与负强化记忆。Kneading is a nursing comfort remnant; water avoidance follows coat limits, ear structure and negative memory.

踩奶是猫幼年期哺乳行为的残留动作：幼猫吸吮母乳时，两只前爪交替按压母猫乳房周围，刺激泌乳并让自己保持放松；成年猫在安心、依恋的情境下重现这套动作，爪子一前一后交替推按软垫、毛毯或人的身体，力度通常轻柔，常伴随呼噜和缓慢眨眼，多出现在刚睡醒、被抚摸或准备休息时。它本质上是安抚行为的延伸，而不是成年猫还想喝奶。Kneading is a remnant of kitten nursing behavior. While suckling, kittens press their two forepaws alternately around the mother's mammary area, stimulating milk flow and staying relaxed. Adult cats replay this sequence in secure, attached situations, pushing a soft blanket, cushion or the owner's body in alternating strokes, often with purring and slow blinking. It is an extension of a comfort behavior, not a sign that the adult cat still wants milk.[1]

按压动作本身伴随局部血流增加和爪垫气味留下：猫在熟悉的表面上留下自身气味标记，把环境确认为安全。个体表达差异很大，是否踩奶与早期断奶时间、同窝经历、与主人的依恋程度有关；有的猫成年后很少踩奶，有的则在每次休息前重复这套动作。成年猫突然出现过度踩奶、伴随后腿蹬踏或自残式舔毛，需要结合皮肤状态和焦虑表现另行评估。The pressing also increases local blood flow and leaves scent from the paw pads: on familiar surfaces the cat marks the environment as its own and safe. Expression varies widely between individuals, and whether a cat kneads tracks early weaning time, litter experience and attachment to the owner. Sudden excessive kneading in adults, especially with hind-leg treading or self-directed over-grooming, should be assessed alongside skin condition and anxiety signs.[1][3]

猫普遍怕水，首先和被毛结构有关。猫毛分表层护毛与底层绒毛，护毛的防水能力有限，毛发一旦湿透，保暖与隔热层被破坏，水分蒸发还带走体表油脂，皮肤屏障变干；湿毛重量增加、吹干很慢，改变被毛隔热厚度，让猫在气温变化时更难维持体温，对短毛猫和老年猫都是额外热负荷。猫的嗅觉敏感，潮湿泥土、清洁剂和水温也会放大不适感。Most cats dislike water first because of their coat structure. Cat fur has guard hairs over an undercoat, and the guard hairs offer limited waterproofing. Once soaked, the insulating layer breaks down; evaporation strips surface oils and dries the skin barrier. Wet fur adds weight and dries slowly, an extra heat load especially for short-coated and old cats. Sensitive smell amplifies discomfort from damp soil, cleaning products and water temperature.[6]

耳道结构和负强化记忆加深了回避。猫耳道短而弯曲，进水后不易自然排出，干燥慢，微生物容易滋生；被按在水里、脚下打滑、洗澡时无法逃脱的经历，会让猫把全身浸湿与不可控的威胁情境联系起来，形成条件性回避，下次靠近水时先出现耳朵后压、尾巴绷紧等预备反应。需要区分的是，很多猫并不排斥流动的水，愿意喝自来水或舔舐水龙头，它们排斥的是全身被浸湿。Ear structure and negative reinforcement deepen the avoidance. The feline ear canal is short and curved, so water does not drain easily, dries slowly and lets microbes take hold. Experiences of being held in water, slipping on a wet surface or being unable to escape during a bath link full immersion to an uncontrollable threat and build conditioned avoidance. It matters that many cats do not dislike moving water: they drink from taps or lick running water, and reject being soaked all over.[1][6]

怕水程度因品种和早期社会化而异：土耳其梵猫等一些品种喜欢下水，幼年接触水温和游戏化洗漱的猫成年后更易接受；强行按入水中只会加深回避，需要接触水时应从小水量、浅水和奖励开始。行为判断要结合耳朵方向、尾巴姿态、瞳孔放大和身体紧张度，不能单凭躲水或踩奶一个动作下结论；持续的回避、恐惧反应或皮肤问题，应由兽医和行为评估综合判断。Water tolerance varies by breed and early socialization. Some breeds such as the Turkish Van enjoy entering water, and kittens exposed to gentle, play-like washing early are easier to manage as adults. Behavior should be read with ear direction, tail posture, pupil dilation and body tension rather than a single action of avoiding water or kneading. Persistent avoidance, fear reactions or skin problems call for a combined veterinary and behavioral assessment.[1][3]

09狗为什么吃屎？食粪行为是生病吗？Why do dogs eat feces, and is coprophagia a sign of illness?

多为祖先巢穴卫生行为残留，叠加消化、饥饿、无聊与关注强化；疾病需单独排查。Mostly a den-hygiene remnant stacked with digestion, hunger, boredom and attention reinforcement; illness needs separate screening.

食粪（coprophagia）指犬只摄食同类或其他动物的粪便；调查中约一成多到两成犬只出现过这种行为，多数属于行为性表现，而不是器质性疾病。已有综述把解释分为两类：一类认为食粪是异常行为，源于厌恶感缺失、营养不良或应激；另一类认为它是从野生犬科祖先继承下来的适应行为。现有证据更倾向于后者，但单一原因并不能解释所有个体。Coprophagia means a dog eating feces, either of its own kind or other animals. Surveys place the behavior at roughly ten to twenty percent of dogs, and most cases are behavioral rather than organic disease. A published review splits explanations into two classes: abnormal behavior rooted in absent aversion, poor nutrition or stress, and an adaptive behavior inherited from wild canid ancestors. Evidence leans toward the latter, though no single cause explains every individual.[1][11]

祖传适应假说强调巢穴卫生与寄生虫防御。野生犬科在巢穴附近清除粪便，减少感染性幼虫在休息区积累，也隐藏气味、降低吸引捕食者的概率；哺乳母犬会舔舐刺激幼犬排便，并把粪便吃掉以保持巢穴清洁。幼犬早期在这一环境中成长，相关行为模式可能残留到成年家犬身上，尤其在母犬产后阶段。The ancestral adaptation hypothesis emphasizes den hygiene and parasite defense. Wild canids remove feces from around the den, reducing the build-up of infective larvae in resting areas and hiding scent that might attract predators. Nursing mothers lick to stimulate puppies to eliminate and then consume the waste to keep the den clean. Puppies grow up in this environment, and the pattern can persist into adult pet dogs, especially around postpartum females.[11]

其他诱因在个案中叠加出现：食物消化不全、膳食纤维或消化酶不足、长期饥饿、无聊和分离焦虑都可能把犬只引向粪便；粪便中残留的酶类、B 族维生素和气味对犬只具有吸引力。被主人呵斥、追逐有时反而形成正反馈：从犬只视角，抢粪便变成一场获得关注和跑动的游戏，行为被反复强化。Other triggers stack up case by case: incomplete digestion, insufficient fiber or digestive enzymes, chronic hunger, boredom and separation anxiety can all draw a dog to feces, whose residual enzymes, B vitamins and smells are attractive to canids. Scolding and chasing can instead reinforce the behavior: from the dog's viewpoint, grabbing stool becomes a game that earns attention and running, so the action repeats.[1][11]

与疾病的关系需要分开看。综述未发现食粪犬在家如厕训练和粪便厌恶感上与普通犬有明显差异；但胰腺外分泌不足、肠道寄生虫、消化吸收障碍等疾病会改变粪便气味和食欲，胰腺功能不足时粪便中仍有未消化的脂肪与蛋白，气味对犬只更有吸引力。频繁食粪若伴随消瘦、腹泻、呕吐或被毛变差，应先由兽医排查身体原因。食粪本身不等于缺微量元素，不建议自行补充维生素或矿物质。The medical side needs to be kept separate. The review found no clear difference in housetraining or fecal aversion between coprophagic and ordinary dogs. Conditions such as exocrine pancreatic insufficiency, intestinal parasites or malabsorption do alter fecal odor and appetite; frequent coprophagia with weight loss, diarrhea, vomiting or poor coat should first be checked by a veterinarian. Eating feces does not by itself mean a trace-element deficiency, and self-supplementing vitamins or minerals is not advised.[6][11]

边界：本章讲行为机制，不提供训练方案或处方。减少粪便可及性、出门即时清理是环境层面最基本的做法；持续的食粪行为由兽医或动物行为师评估诱因。粪便可能携带寄生虫卵、细菌和病原，犬只接触后应注意手部和环境清洁，家中有老人、婴幼儿或免疫低下成员时更要控制接触。Boundary: this chapter covers behavioral mechanisms, not a training plan or prescription. Removing access and cleaning up immediately on walks are the basic environmental measures; persistent coprophagia should be assessed by a veterinarian or animal behaviorist. Feces can carry parasite eggs, bacteria and pathogens, so hands and surroundings should be cleaned after contact, and contact should be controlled where elderly people, infants or immunocompromised members live.[1][6]

10仓鼠为什么一直咬笼子，又为什么喜欢跑滚轮？Why do hamsters keep biting the cage bars, and why do they like running on a wheel?

啃栏是掘洞觅食动机未满足的刻板行为；跑轮释放夜行长距离活动驱力，滚轮直径关系脊柱福利。Bar-chewing is stereotypic behaviour when burrowing and foraging drives are unmet; the wheel releases nocturnal locomotion, and wheel size matters for spinal welfare.

仓鼠反复啃咬笼子栏杆，常被误以为是在磨牙，但门齿生长只是背景条件，不是啃栏的主因。仓鼠门齿属于终生生长的齿型，每周约生长 1–2 mm，需要持续磨损维持长度；可野外仓鼠磨损门齿靠的是啃掘根茎、硬壳种子和洞穴土质，而不是金属栏杆。圈养环境里掘洞、觅食和巡视领域的动机缺少出口，动作便转向手边唯一可啃的硬物，栏杆于是成为刻板行为的载体。Repeated cage-bar chewing is often mistaken for tooth trimming, but continuous tooth growth is only background, not the main cause. Hamster incisors are ever-growing teeth that add about 1–2 mm per week and need steady wear to stay at length; in the wild, wear comes from gnawing roots, hard-shelled seeds and burrow soil, not metal bars. When burrowing, foraging and ranging drives have no outlet in captivity, the action turns to the only hard object at hand, and the bars become the carrier of a stereotypic behaviour.[1][12]

刻板行为指反复出现、形式固定、当下看不出直接功能的动作。对雌性叙利亚仓鼠的四档笼舍实验（1,800、2,500、5,000、10,000 cm²）记录到，啃栏在所有笼舍中都有出现，但小笼中的持续时间和发生频次显著更高；垫料过浅、缺少隐蔽处时，仓鼠还会更多利用屋顶平台。近因是空间贫瘠和可探索路径不足，远因则是夜行性活动、掘洞和贮食本能在狭小环境中无法完整表达。Stereotypic behaviour means repeated, fixed actions with no obvious immediate function. In a four-cage study of female golden hamsters (1,800, 2,500, 5,000 and 10,000 cm²), wire-gnawing appeared in every size, but lasted significantly longer and occurred more often in the smaller cages; with shallow bedding and few hiding places, hamsters also used the shelter roof as an extra platform more often. The proximate cause is barren space and too few exploration paths, while the ultimate cause is nocturnal activity, burrowing and food-caching instincts that cannot be fully expressed in a small enclosure.[12]

跑滚轮表达的是同一套活动驱力的另一面。仓鼠多在夜间或晨昏活动，野外一晚出洞活动数公里搜寻食物并多处贮食；圈养下活动范围被限制，跑轮成为少数可以把长距离跑动释放出来的装置。对两种侏儒仓鼠的野外与实验室对照显示，跑轮使用与光周期挂钩，坎贝尔仓鼠常在天黑前开始跑动，峰值跑速记录约每小时 5,200–5,400 转；活动节律由内在生物钟和环境光照共同设定。The wheel releases the other side of the same locomotor drive. Hamsters are mostly nocturnal or crepuscular, and in the wild leave the burrow for several kilometres each night to search for food and cache it in several places; in captivity the range is restricted, and the wheel is one of the few devices that lets long-distance running come out. Field and laboratory comparison of two dwarf hamster species links wheel use to photoperiod: Campbell's hamsters often start running before lights-off, and peak wheel activity was recorded around 5,200–5,400 revolutions per hour; the rhythm is set by an internal clock together with ambient light.[13][14]

自愿跑动本身带有奖赏属性。跑动激活与探索、奖赏相关的神经通路，过程中释放的强化信号让跑轮行为被重复；使用时长受年龄、性别、物种和环境丰富度影响，个体差异明显。需要区分的是，跑轮满足的是运动驱力，不能替代掘洞、觅食探索和垫料挖掘；野外观察还显示，金色仓鼠在自然栖息地的活动时段与实验室记录并不完全一致，笼内节律不能直接当作野外习性。Voluntary running itself carries reward properties. Running activates pathways linked to exploration and reward, and the reinforcing signals released in the process make the wheel behaviour repeat. Usage varies with age, sex, species and environmental enrichment, with clear individual differences. The wheel satisfies locomotor drive only; it cannot replace burrowing, foraging exploration and digging in bedding. Field observations also show that the activity timing of golden hamsters in natural habitat does not fully match laboratory records, so captive rhythms cannot be read directly as wild habits.[13][14]

福利边界要写清楚：持续、高频的啃栏和长期不活动是环境不足的信号，而不是值得展示的可爱习惯。滚轮直径不足会迫使背部弓起，长期可造成脊柱弯曲，短尾侏儒类直径宜在 20 cm 以上、叙利亚仓鼠在 30 cm 以上；本文只解释行为机制，不提供饲养配置清单。若仓鼠同时出现食欲下降、被毛粗乱或口腔异常，应交给异宠兽医评估，不能凭啃栏动作自行判断健康状况。The welfare boundary deserves to be stated plainly. Persistent, high-frequency bar chewing and long-term inactivity are signals of an impoverished environment, not cute habits to display. A wheel that is too small forces an arched back and can cause spinal curvature over time; welfare guidance puts minimum diameters above 20 cm for dwarf species and above 30 cm for Syrian hamsters. This chapter explains mechanisms only and gives no housing checklist. If a hamster also loses appetite, develops a rough coat or shows oral abnormalities, it should be assessed by an exotic-pet veterinarian; health cannot be judged from bar chewing alone.[12][13]
