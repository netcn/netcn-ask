01植物从水生到陆生经历了哪些结构改造？水分怎样运输到树冠，树干年轮怎样形成？What structural changes moved plants from water to land, how does water reach the canopy, and how do trunk rings form?

维管组织、角质层与气孔支撑登陆；蒸腾拉力拉动水柱，形成层次生生长形成年轮。Vascular tissue, cuticle and stomata support land life; transpiration pull lifts the column, cambium secondary growth makes rings.

陆生植物由水生绿藻祖先登上陆地。早期登陆面临支撑、保水、获取空气和配子传播等问题；苔藓等早期陆生植物没有真正维管组织，依赖湿润环境；蕨类和种子植物发展出维管组织、角质层和气孔，逐步适应干旱陆地。OpenStax 在绿藻与陆生植物关系一节比较藻类和陆生植物的共同特征。藻类没有真正的根、茎、叶分化，登陆后首先要解决固定植株和从土壤取水的问题。Land plants arose from aquatic green-algal ancestors. The early move onto land faced support, water retention, air access and gamete dispersal. Bryophyte-like early land plants lacked true vascular tissue and depended on moist conditions; ferns and seed plants developed vascular tissue, cuticle and stomata and gradually adapted to dry land. OpenStax compares shared features of algae and land plants in its green-algal precursors section.[1]

身体结构分根、茎、叶。根扎入土壤吸收水分和矿质，茎支撑并运输，叶进行光合；表皮角质层减少失水，气孔调节气体交换和蒸腾。维管组织中木质部把水和矿质向上输送，韧皮部把光合产物运到生长部位；组织分化让植物体能够离开水面挺立生长，不再依赖水体浮力支撑。表皮毛和角质层共同减少蒸发，幼嫩组织保留薄表皮便于气体交换。The body divides into root, stem and leaf. Roots anchor in soil and absorb water and minerals, the stem supports and transports, and leaves photosynthesise. An epidermal cuticle reduces water loss, and stomata regulate gas exchange and transpiration. In vascular tissue, xylem carries water and minerals upward, phloem moves photosynthate to growing parts; tissue differentiation lets the plant stand without buoyancy in water.[2]

水分上升到树冠靠内聚-拉力机制。叶片蒸腾失水产生负压，水柱在木质部导管中被向上拉动；水分子之间内聚、与管壁附着，连续水柱可维持到数十米高。根压只在夜间和矮植株上辅助推动，高大乔木主要靠蒸腾拉力，而不是从下往上泵水；导管连续、气泡阻断水柱时会影响水分供应。木质部导管或管胞内壁加厚，支持水柱承受负压；水分经根毛进入皮层后到达木质部。Water reaches the canopy by cohesion-tension. Transpiration pulls water from leaves, generating negative pressure that drags the continuous column up xylem vessels; cohesion between water molecules and adhesion to the wall sustain the column to tens of metres. Root pressure helps mainly at night and in small plants; tall trees rely mostly on transpiration pull, not a bottom-up pump, and air bubbles that break the column disrupt supply.[3]

次生生长形成树干年轮。形成层细胞向外产生韧皮部、向内产生木质部；春季水分充足时导管大而壁薄，夏末秋初导管小而壁厚，早晚材颜色和密度不同，横切面形成同心年轮。年轮宽度受降水、温度和光照影响，可用于反推历史气候；树木年龄按年轮计数，伤裂和偏心生长会干扰判读。Secondary growth forms trunk rings. Cambium produces phloem outward and xylem inward; spring vessels are large and thin-walled under good moisture, while late-season vessels are small and thick-walled, so early- and latewood differ in colour and density and form concentric rings in cross-section. Ring width responds to rainfall, temperature and light and can reconstruct past climate; age is counted from rings, though scars and eccentric growth interfere.[2][3]

边界：登陆适应是分阶段出现的，不同植物类群解决方式不同；苔藓与维管植物不能按同一套结构比较。水分运输机制以被子植物和裸子植物的木质部为主要依据，矮小草本和附生植物有不同辅助方式；年轮记录受火灾、虫害和局部立地条件影响，不能把每一圈宽度都直接对应年降水量。The boundary: land adaptations appeared in stages, and groups solved problems differently; bryophytes and vascular plants cannot be compared on one structural plan. Water-transport mechanisms rest mainly on seed-plant xylem, while small herbs and epiphytes use different aids. Ring records are affected by fire, insects and local site conditions, so each ring width cannot be read directly as annual rainfall.[1][3]

02植物怎样进行光合作用？C3、C4 与 CAM 路径在用水效率上有什么差别？How do plants photosynthesise, and how do C3, C4 and CAM pathways differ in water-use efficiency?

C3 直接固定碳，C4 在鞘细胞富集 CO₂ 减弱光呼吸，CAM 夜间开门储酸白天闭孔。C3 fixes carbon directly; C4 enriches CO2 in sheath cells to cut photorespiration; CAM stores acid by night, closes stomata by day.

光合作用在叶绿体中把光能转化为化学能。光反应在类囊体膜上分解水、产生 ATP 和 NADPH，释放氧气；卡尔文循环在基质中固定二氧化碳，合成三碳糖，再生成淀粉和蔗糖。植物用这些产物建造细胞结构，并为生长和繁殖供能。光合产物一部分就近供叶片呼吸，一部分运输到根、果实和种子储存。OpenStax 光合一节描述光反应与碳反应的衔接。光合产物还供给快速生长：毛竹等竹类靠每个节间基部的居间分生组织同时分裂伸长，春梢旺季日高生长可超过 1 m，居间分生组织活跃 45–60 天；长得快首先是分生组织与细胞伸长策略，再由光合产物和地下根茎储备供能。Photosynthesis converts light energy into chemical energy in chloroplasts. Light reactions on thylakoid membranes split water, produce ATP and NADPH and release oxygen; the Calvin cycle in the stroma fixes CO2 into three-carbon sugars that become starch and sucrose. Plants use these products to build cells and supply growth and reproduction. OpenStax links light and carbon reactions in its photosynthesis section. Photosynthate also feeds fast growth: Moso bamboo uses intercalary meristems at each internode base that divide and elongate together, spring shoots can exceed 1 m per day, and the meristem stays active for 45–60 days; speed rests first on meristem and elongation strategy, powered by photosynthate and rhizome reserves.[4][11]

固定二氧化碳的酶 Rubisco 既结合二氧化碳也可能结合氧气，后者引起光呼吸，消耗已固定的碳。C3 植物直接在叶肉细胞中进行卡尔文循环，是多数温带植物的路径；高温干燥时气孔关闭，叶内二氧化碳下降，光呼吸增强，用水和固碳效率受到压力。光呼吸回收部分代谢中间物，但净损失已固定的碳，在高温强光下尤其明显。The CO2-fixing enzyme Rubisco can bind both CO2 and oxygen; the oxygen reaction is photorespiration, which consumes fixed carbon. C3 plants run the Calvin cycle directly in mesophyll cells, the path of most temperate plants; in heat and drought stomata close, internal CO2 falls, photorespiration rises, and water-use and carbon-fixation efficiency come under pressure.[5]

C4 植物在叶肉细胞先把二氧化碳固定成四碳酸，再运到维管束鞘细胞释放二氧化碳供卡尔文循环；鞘细胞内二氧化碳浓度高，Rubisco 少接触氧气，光呼吸弱。玉米、甘蔗和狗尾草等属于 C4，在炎热强光下固碳和用水效率较高，但额外合成四碳化合物需要能量投入。C4 叶片具有花环式解剖结构，叶肉与鞘细胞分工，常见于禾本科和部分阔叶草本。C4 plants first fix CO2 into four-carbon acids in mesophyll cells, then move them to bundle-sheath cells where CO2 is released for the Calvin cycle; high sheath CO2 keeps Rubisco away from oxygen and weakens photorespiration. Maize, sugarcane and foxtail grasses are C4, efficient at carbon fixation and water use in hot strong light, but synthesising four-carbon compounds costs extra energy.[5]

CAM 植物走夜间开门路线。景天酸代谢植物夜间气孔开放吸收二氧化碳，以苹果酸形式储存在液泡，白天气孔关闭减少失水，再把储存的二氧化碳放出供卡尔文循环。仙人掌、菠萝和许多多肉植物用这套路径适应干旱，白天低蒸腾换取用水效率，生长速度通常较慢。夜间吸收的二氧化碳以苹果酸储存在液泡，白天分解释放后再由 Rubisco 固定。CAM plants open their stomata at night. Crassulacean acid metabolism takes up CO2 at night, stores it as malic acid in vacuoles, closes stomata by day to limit water loss, and releases stored CO2 for the Calvin cycle. Cacti, pineapples and many succulents use this path for drought survival, trading low daytime transpiration for water efficiency and usually slower growth.[2][5]

边界：三条路径是对温度、水分和光照的适应分化，不是优劣等级；同一植株在不同环境下气孔开闭也会调整。C4 和 CAM 的节水以额外代谢成本和慢生长为代价，在阴湿环境中得不到优势。固定碳的总量还要看叶片面积、光照时间和养分供应，路径类型不能单独决定产量。The boundary: the three paths are adaptive divergence to temperature, water and light, not a ranking of better and worse; stomata open and close within one plant by condition. C4 and CAM save water at the cost of extra metabolism and slow growth, with no advantage in moist shade. Total fixed carbon also depends on leaf area, light hours and nutrient supply; pathway alone cannot determine yield.[4][5]

03落叶为什么变黄变红？常绿植物怎样越冬，叶的向光性来自什么机制？Why do leaves turn yellow or red in autumn, how do evergreens overwinter, and what drives leaf phototropism?

叶绿素分解显露类胡萝卜素、花青素新合成；离层促成落叶，生长素背光积累使茎弯向光源。Chlorophyll loss reveals carotenoids, anthocyanins are newly made; abscission drops leaves, auxin on the shaded side bends the shoot toward light.

叶片是光合和蒸腾的主要器官。叶肉细胞含叶绿体，叶脉提供水分运输和支撑，上下表皮气孔调节气体；叶形、厚度和角质层按生境分化，阳生叶厚而色深，阴生叶薄而舒展。叶片寿命随植物策略不同，落叶植物与常绿植物对应不同的越冬方式。气孔多分布在下表皮，蒸腾同时带动水分和矿质在木质部上升；叶序排列减少相互遮阴。Leaves are the main organs of photosynthesis and transpiration. Mesophyll cells carry chloroplasts, veins transport water and support, and epidermis stomata regulate gas exchange; shape, thickness and cuticle diverge by habitat, sun leaves thick and dark, shade leaves thin and spread. Leaf lifespan follows plant strategy, with deciduous and evergreen plants matching different wintering modes.[2][6]

秋天变色来自色素变化。叶绿素在低温和短日照下分解较快，原本被绿色掩盖的类胡萝卜素显现出黄色和橙色；部分叶片在衰老前合成花青素，给出红色和紫色。变黄和变红机制并不完全相同：黄是原有色素显露，红是新合成色素，颜色还受糖分积累、温度和光照影响。花青素在酸性细胞液中偏红，深浅随糖分和 pH 变化，同一片叶在不同天气下显色不同。Autumn colour comes from pigment change. Chlorophyll breaks down faster under low temperature and short daylength, revealing carotenoids masked by green, giving yellow and orange; some leaves synthesise anthocyanins before senescence, producing red and purple. Yellowing and reddening are not identical: yellow reveals existing pigment, red forms new pigment, and colour also depends on sugar accumulation, temperature and light.[6]

落叶前形成离层。叶柄基部细胞分裂形成离层，切断水分和养分通道，叶片脱落，伤痕被保护层封闭；落叶减少冬季失水和积雪压枝，让植物把养分收回枝干。常绿植物并非不落叶，而是老叶分批替换，新叶陆续长出，终年保留部分叶片；针叶树厚角质层和松脂帮助越冬。离层形成后叶柄机械连接减弱，风或自重使叶脱落，保护层防止病菌和水分从伤口进入。An abscission layer forms before leaf fall. Cells at the petiole base divide into a separation layer that cuts water and nutrient channels; the leaf drops and the wound seals with a protective layer. Leaf fall reduces winter water loss and snow load and lets the plant withdraw nutrients to branches. Evergreens do not keep every leaf forever: old leaves are replaced in batches while new ones grow, leaving some foliage year-round; thick cuticle and resin conifers help overwinter.[6]

向光性来自生长方向调节。茎尖生长素在背光侧积累，使背光侧细胞伸长更多，茎弯向光源；叶片和枝条还通过气孔开闭、叶角调整获取光照。植物没有神经，向性是激素和细胞伸长的生长反应，速度远慢于动物运动，但能在数小时到数天内改变朝向。除向光性外，根表现向地性、茎背地生长，触碰信号让卷须和缠绕茎沿支撑物盘旋。Phototropism works through growth-direction regulation. Auxin accumulates on the shaded side of the shoot tip, so shaded cells elongate more and the stem bends toward light; leaves and shoots also adjust stomata and leaf angle for light. Plants have no nerves; tropism is a growth response of hormones and cell elongation, far slower than animal movement, but orientation can shift over hours to days.[8]

边界：叶色年际差异受天气和立地影响，不能只凭颜色判断树种；离层形成受光周期和温度共同触发，各地秋季时间不同。常绿与落叶是连续策略，某些树种在不同气候下表现不同；向光性研究多以幼苗和茎尖为对象，成株树冠调整涉及更多结构因素。The boundary: year-to-year leaf colour depends on weather and site, so species cannot be judged by colour alone; abscission is triggered by both photoperiod and temperature, and autumn timing varies by region. Evergreen and deciduous form a continuum, and some species behave differently across climates. Phototropism studies mostly use seedlings and shoot tips; mature canopy adjustment involves more structural factors.[6][8]

04植物怎样授粉和传播种子？人类作物是否反过来被植物“驯化”？How do plants pollinate and disperse seeds, and have crop plants in a sense domesticated humans?

虫媒风媒结构对应不同传粉者，花粉管完成受精；冠毛风力扩散与动物携带离开母株。Insect and wind structures match vectors, pollen tubes complete fertilisation; pappus wind drift and animal carrying leave the mother plant.

被子植物靠花完成繁殖。雄蕊产生花粉，雌蕊子房内含胚珠；花粉落到柱头上萌发花粉管，把精子送到胚珠完成受精，胚珠发育成种子，子房常发育成果实。传粉媒介包括昆虫、鸟类、风、水和哺乳动物，花的颜色、气味、花蜜和结构对应不同媒介。胚珠受精后发育出种皮，胚胎和储存养分形成种子，果皮帮助保护、传播或吸引取食者。Angiosperms reproduce through flowers. Stamens produce pollen, and the ovary contains ovules; pollen lands on the stigma, grows a pollen tube and delivers sperm to the ovule for fertilisation. Ovules become seeds and the ovary often develops into fruit. Pollinators include insects, birds, wind, water and mammals, while flower colour, scent, nectar and structure match different vectors.[7]

虫媒与风媒各有结构安排。虫媒花通常醒目、有气味和花蜜，花粉较大有黏性；风媒花不依赖吸引，花粉量大、轻而干燥，靠气流带到柱头上。传粉者与植物在长期相互作用中协同变化，花形限制能进入取食的昆虫种类，访花行为同时帮助植物结实。花的开放时间与访花者活动时段配合，夜间花多发白、气味浓，吸引蛾类。Insect and wind pollination have separate arrangements. Insect-pollinated flowers are usually conspicuous, scented and nectarous with larger sticky pollen; wind-pollinated flowers do not rely on attraction, producing abundant light dry pollen carried by air. Pollinators and plants co-vary over long interaction; flower shape limits which insects can feed, and visits help plants set seed.[7]

种子传播离开母株竞争区域。蒲公英瘦果顶端有冠毛，成熟后借助风力飘移；果实可附在动物皮毛、被取食后随粪便排出，或靠果实开裂弹射。传播距离和方式决定幼苗占据的生境，种子休眠帮助等待适宜温度和水分条件。冠毛增大浮力，无风时也可短距离扩散；水媒果实靠浮力漂浮，动物传播常带硬壳抵抗消化。Seed dispersal leaves the area under the mother plant's competition. A dandelion achene carries a pappus that drifts on wind when ripe; fruits cling to fur, pass through digestive tracts and emerge in dung, or split open to eject seeds. Distance and vector determine where seedlings establish, and seed dormancy waits for suitable temperature and moisture.[2][7]

作物与人类的关系常被说成植物驯化人类。禾本科谷物种子大而富含淀粉，人类保护、播种和扩散这些作物；从植物视角看，与人类共生使作物分布范围和繁殖成功率大增。但驯化是双向选择：人类按产量和口味选育，作物依赖农业条件，野生祖先并不具备同样的传播能力。人类选择使谷物种子不易自然脱落，传播反而依赖收割播种；这种性状在野生状态下不利于自身扩散。The crop-human relationship is often framed as plants domesticating humans. Grass grains with large starchy seeds were protected, sown and spread by people; from the plant's view, symbiosis with humans widened range and reproductive success. But domestication is two-way selection: humans bred for yield and taste, crops depend on farming conditions, and wild ancestors lack the same dispersal ability.[2][5]

边界：协同演化是相互影响，不等于双方意图明确；花与访花者的配对可能被外来物种干扰。种子传播方式在近缘种间有混合，不能只靠果实外形断定媒介。作物与人类的驯化比喻强调繁殖成功，不应理解为植物具有策略意识；品种性状受人工选择强烈塑造，脱离农田后生存能力下降。The boundary: coevolution is mutual influence, not explicit intent on either side, and flower-visitor pairing can be disrupted by alien species. Dispersal modes mix among close relatives, so fruit shape alone cannot settle the vector. The domestication metaphor highlights reproductive success, not plant strategic awareness; cultivar traits are strongly shaped by artificial selection and decline outside farmland.[2][7]

05植物怎样感知环境？食虫植物、仙人掌刺、土壤养分和寿命边界如何体现适应？How do plants sense their environment, and how do carnivory, cactus spines, soil nutrients and lifespan show adaptation?

受体经激素调节生长；食虫补氮磷，刺是退化叶配合储水茎，菌根扩大养分吸收。Receptors adjust growth through hormones; carnivory supplements N and P, spines are reduced leaves with succulent stems, mycorrhizae enlarge uptake.

植物能感知多种环境信号。光强、光质、重力、水分、触碰和养分浓度都被受体接收，通过激素调整生长、开花和休眠；根向水和养分生长，茎向光，卷须缠绕支撑。植物没有神经系统，信号传递靠激素、化学信号和维管组织中的电信号，响应以生长和代谢调节为主。叶片能感知红光和远红光比例，判断遮阴环境，据此调整茎伸长和分枝。Plants sense many environmental signals. Light intensity and quality, gravity, moisture, touch and nutrient concentration are received by receptors that use hormones to adjust growth, flowering and dormancy; roots grow toward water and nutrients, shoots toward light, and tendrils coil around supports. Plants have no nervous system; signalling runs through hormones, chemical signals and electrical signals in vascular tissue, with responses mainly in growth and metabolism.[8]

食虫植物适应贫瘠土壤。捕蝇草触发毛被触碰后闭合叶片，狸藻在水中捕虫；这类植物仍靠光合作用制造有机物，动物补充的是土壤中不足的氮和磷。消化腺分泌酶分解猎物，吸收养分，结构上保留叶片功能，只是特化出捕捉器官。捕获后分解含氮有机物，吸收的养分补充贫瘠土壤中的缺口。猪笼草的捕虫笼靠笼口蜜腺引诱昆虫，滑口让虫失足落入消化液，笼内分泌的酶把猎物分解，氮磷养分再被笼壁吸收。Carnivorous plants adapt to poor soil. Venus flytrap traps close when trigger hairs are touched, and bladderworts catch prey underwater; these plants still make organic matter by photosynthesis, while animals supplement nitrogen and phosphorus lacking in soil. Digestive glands secrete enzymes to break down prey and absorb nutrients, retaining leaf function while specialising a capture organ.A pitcher plant lures insects with nectar on the rim, a slippery lip lets them fall into the digestive fluid, and enzymes there break the prey down before the wall absorbs nitrogen and phosphorus.[6][8]

仙人掌刺是退化的叶。叶片缩小成刺，减少蒸腾面积并防御动物；绿色茎进行光合，表皮角质层厚，储水组织膨大，根系浅而广，在雨季快速吸水。刺不是单纯的武器，而是旱生结构改造的一部分，与多肉茎和浅根配合度过干旱期。肉质茎储存水分和叶绿体，刺同时减少动物取食，这类植物生长季短，靠储水度过长期干旱。Cactus spines are modified leaves. Leaves are reduced to spines that cut transpiration area and deter animals; the green stem photosynthesises, with a thick cuticle, swollen water-storing tissue and shallow wide roots that take up rain quickly. Spines are not mere weapons but part of a xeric remodelling that works with succulent stems and shallow roots to survive drought.[2][6]

土壤养分与菌根配合。植物根与真菌形成菌根，菌丝扩大吸收面积，帮助获取磷和水，植物供给真菌光合产物；根瘤在豆科植物中固氮。土壤结构、pH 和有机质影响可利用养分，植物通过根形调整和分泌物改善局部吸收条件。根系范围与菌根菌丝共同扩大搜寻面积，土壤微生物活动也参与有机质分解和养分释放。Soil nutrients work together with mycorrhizae. Plant roots form mycorrhizae with fungi whose hyphae enlarge uptake area for phosphorus and water, while the plant supplies photosynthate; nodules fix nitrogen in legumes. Soil structure, pH and organic matter affect available nutrients, and plants adjust root shape and exudates to improve local uptake.[2]

边界：植物说话或通讯多为化学信号和菌根网络介导，不等于有语言或意识；信号强度和方向受环境限制。食虫植物的捕虫是固定机制触发，不是主动追捕；旱生结构在水分充足时也有代价，比如生长慢。寿命边界受气候、火灾和病害影响，高龄树木也不能无限生长，年轮和分生组织能力决定存活上限；不同植物的繁殖世代长短差异很大。The boundary: plant talk or communication is mostly chemical signals and mycorrhizal networks, not language or consciousness; signal strength and direction are environment-limited. Carnivorous trapping is triggered by fixed mechanisms, not active pursuit; xeric traits carry costs even with water, such as slow growth. Lifespan is bounded by climate, fire and disease, and even very old trees cannot grow indefinitely; rings and meristem capacity set the survival ceiling.[6][8]

06仙人掌为什么浑身是刺？刺和储水茎怎样配合度过干旱？Why are cacti covered in spines, and how do spines and the water-storing stem work together?

叶退化成刺缩小蒸腾面，绿茎储水并走 CAM 夜间固碳，浅根雨季快速吸水。Leaves reduced to spines cut transpiration; the green stem stores water and runs CAM, shallow roots take rain quickly.

仙人掌浑身是刺，首先是因为它的叶子已经退化成了刺。仙人掌科植物的叶原基在刺座上发育成硬化的刺状变态叶，叶片的光合面积被压缩到极小，蒸腾表面随之大幅缩小；原来由叶片承担的光合作用，改由绿色肉质茎完成。茎表皮角质层厚、气孔数量少且位置较深，茎内薄壁储水组织膨大，把旱生植物最容易失水的器官换成了最耐储水的器官。刺座上同时长出毛状腺体，在茎表形成一层细密的覆盖结构。A cactus is covered in spines because its leaves have been reduced to them. In cacti, leaf primordia develop at areoles into hardened spine-like modified leaves, so the photosynthetic surface and transpiring area are cut to a tiny fraction of an ordinary broad leaf. The green succulent stem takes over photosynthesis. Its epidermis has a thick cuticle and few deeply placed stomata, while the inner water-storage parenchyma swells to hold reserves; the organ most prone to water loss is replaced by one built for storage.[2][6]

储水组织靠黏液多糖结合水分。肉质茎的薄壁细胞内含有大量胶质和多糖物质，水分被结合在组织中，不像普通叶片那样一遇干热就快速散失；雨季吸饱水后，茎可以在无雨期缓慢动用库存。光合路径上，仙人掌走景天酸代谢路线：夜间气孔开放吸收二氧化碳，以苹果酸形式储存在液泡中，白天气孔关闭，再把储存的二氧化碳释放出来交给卡尔文循环。夜间固碳、白天闭孔的节奏，把白天高温时段的蒸腾失水压到很低。The storage tissue binds water with mucilage and polysaccharides. Cells in the fleshy stem hold abundant gums and polysaccharides that retain water, so tissue moisture does not evaporate as quickly as in a thin leaf; after a rainy spell the stem draws on this reserve through dry periods. For carbon fixation, cacti use CAM: stomata open at night to take up CO2, store it as malic acid in vacuoles, close by day, and release the stored CO2 for the Calvin cycle. Nighttime fixation with daytime closure keeps transpiration low through the hot hours.[5]

刺还有防御和微环境作用。硬化的刺能阻挡植食动物取食多汁的茎；密集刺丛在茎周围托起一层相对静止的空气，削弱直射阳光和过境气流，降低茎表温度和蒸腾。根系则浅而分布广，不往深处扎，而是在表层大范围铺开，降雨后几天内就快速吸收渗入土壤的水分。茎、刺、根和气孔节奏组成一套配合，而不是某一个器官单独耐旱。Spines also defend and form a microclimate. Hard spines deter herbivores from eating the juicy stem, and a dense spine cluster holds a layer of still air around it, weakening direct radiation and airflow to lower stem surface temperature and transpiration. Roots are shallow and wide rather than deep, spreading over the surface to absorb rain within days of a shower. Stem, spines, roots and stomatal rhythm work as one drought package rather than a single drought-proof organ.[2][6]

仙人掌这个名称，来自扁圆或掌状肉质茎的外形联想；这类植物多原产美洲干旱、半干旱地区，叶退化、茎肉质化和 CAM 代谢是长期适应少雨环境的协同改造。外形相似的多肉植物不止仙人掌一科，大戟科等也有肉质茎和刺，但刺的来源和内部结构并不相同，属于趋同演化。The familiar cactus shape comes from these fleshy, often flattened stems. Most cacti originate from arid and semi-arid regions of the Americas; reduced leaves, succulent stems and CAM metabolism are coordinated adaptations to low rainfall. Other succulent groups, such as many euphorbias, look similar with fleshy stems and spines, but the spines derive from different structures, a case of convergent evolution.[2]

边界：刺不是单纯的武器，它首先是叶的变态结构；旱生结构在水分充足时也有代价，比如生长慢、怕积涝，CAM 代谢只在夜间开门也限制了白天可固定的碳量。储水和节水不能消除干旱的极限，长期缺水仍会耗尽组织水分。不同仙人掌类群对高温、干旱的耐受范围差别很大，不能把沙漠中存活的仙人掌理解为不需要水。Boundary: spines are first modified leaves, not merely weapons. Xerophytic traits carry a cost even when water is ample, including slow growth and intolerance of waterlogging; CAM opening only at night also limits how much carbon can be fixed by day. Storage and water saving cannot erase drought limits, and prolonged dryness still depletes tissue water. Tolerance ranges differ widely among cacti, and a desert survivor is not a plant that needs no water.[2][5]

07树为什么会落叶？树干为什么刷白漆，年轮为什么南疏北密？Why do trees drop their leaves, why are trunks whitewashed, and why are rings wider on the south side?

短日照触发离层落叶并回收养分；涂白反射阳光防日灼；南向形成层活动强使年轮南疏北密。Short days trigger an abscission layer and nutrient withdrawal; whitewash reflects sun to prevent sunscald; rings widen on the sunny side.

落叶由光周期和温度信号触发。入秋后日照变短，叶片中的光敏色素感知红光与远红光比例变化，衰老程序启动，低温在其后叠加促进进程；生长素向叶柄基部的运输减弱，脱落酸水平上升，促使叶柄基部形成离层带。离层带分为离区与保护层两段，离区细胞分解胞间中层，叶柄与枝条之间的机械连接逐渐变弱，风或自重便把叶片带下，伤口随后被保护层封闭。Leaf fall is triggered by photoperiod and temperature signals. As days shorten in autumn, leaves sense the shifting red-to-far-red ratio and enter a senescence programme; auxin transport to the petiole base weakens, abscisic acid rises, and an abscission zone forms at the petiole base. Cells in this zone break down the middle lamella, the mechanical link between leaf and branch loosens, and wind or the leaf's own weight drops it; a protective layer then seals the wound.[6]

落叶前植物先把养分收回来。叶片衰老过程中，约 50%–80% 的氮、磷等矿质养分被分解运回枝干皮层和根系中储存，来年芽萌动时再运往新叶；叶绿素分解后类胡萝卜素显露黄橙色，部分叶片后期合成花青素呈红紫色。落叶本身还减少了冬季叶面的水分散失和积雪、大风压枝的负荷，使树冠在冬季风荷载下减重，是落叶乔木越冬的一套策略。The plant recycles nutrients before shedding. During senescence, roughly 50 to 80 percent of nitrogen and phosphorus are broken down and moved back into branches and roots for storage, ready for next spring's new leaves. Chlorophyll breaks down to reveal carotenoid yellows and oranges, while some leaves synthesise anthocyanins late in the process for red and purple tones. Dropping leaves also cuts winter water loss and the snow load on branches.[2][6]

树干刷白漆是人工保护，不是植物自身结构。白涂剂多由石灰、硫黄和水配成，涂在树干基部；白色涂层反射冬季午后阳光，减小树干昼夜温差，避免皮层因反复冻融开裂，同时阻碍害虫在树皮缝隙产卵、减少真菌和地衣附着。涂白主要起反射和屏障作用，不向树干供给养分。Whitewashing trunks is human protection, not a plant structure. The white coating, usually lime mixed with sulphur and water, is painted on the lower trunk; its white surface reflects winter afternoon sun, narrowing the day-night temperature swing and preventing frost cracks from repeated freeze-thaw. It also blocks insects from laying eggs in bark crevices and limits fungi and lichen attachment. The paint reflects and forms a barrier; it does not feed the trunk.[2]

年轮南疏北密，是向阳面形成层活动更强的结果。春季回暖后形成层先恢复活动，在水分充足条件下产生的导管大而壁薄，夏末产生的导管小而壁厚，早晚材密度不同形成同心环；北半球南侧光照充足、温度较高，形成层在南侧活动更旺盛，早材带更宽，年轮在南侧显得疏松，北侧背阴则偏密。偏心生长、坡向和局部遮阴都会干扰这种对称。Rings wider on the south side come from stronger cambial activity on the sunny side. Spring vessels are large and thin-walled, late-season vessels small and thick-walled, so early- and latewood differ in density and form concentric rings. In the Northern Hemisphere the south side gets more light and warmth, the cambium there stays more active, earlywood bands are wider, and rings appear looser on the south and tighter on the north. Eccentric growth, slope and local shade disturb this symmetry.[2][3]

边界：落叶是对短日照和低温的季节性适应，常绿植物只是分批替换老叶；涂白是栽培措施，不同地区涂刷时间和高度按树种与虫害调整。年轮宽窄受降水、温度和光照共同影响，单侧偏宽不能简单等同于单侧光照的直接记录；判读年龄和气候时还要排除伤裂、偏心和局部立地的干扰。Boundary: leaf fall is a seasonal response to short days and cold; evergreens simply replace old leaves in batches. Whitewashing is a cultivation practice, timed and applied by species and pest pressure. Ring width responds to rainfall, temperature and light together, so a wider ring on one side cannot be read as a direct record of that side's sunlight alone. Dating and climate reading must exclude scars, eccentricity and local site effects.[3][6]

08植物怎样把种子传播出去？蒲公英的种子为什么能随风飞很远？How do plants disperse their seeds, and why can dandelion seeds drift far on the wind?

冠毛张开成多孔伞，气流穿过形成分离涡环提供升力，轻小瘦果随风缓慢飘落扩散。A porous pappus opens into a parachute; air through the hairs forms a detached vortex ring that lifts the light achene for slow drift.

种子离开母株主要靠四类媒介。风力传播借冠毛或翅果把种子带上气流；动物传播靠果实附着皮毛，或被取食后随粪便排出；水媒果实靠浮力漂浮；另有一些果实成熟后开裂，把种子弹射出去。不同方式对应种子大小和果实形态：大粒种子多靠动物或重力落地，轻小种子才适合风力扩散。传播的意义是避开母株附近的竞争、病虫害和荫蔽，让幼苗到新的生境定居；种子休眠则等待合适的温度与水分条件。Seeds leave the mother plant through four main vectors. Wind dispersal uses pappus hairs or winged fruits to lift seeds on air currents; animal dispersal relies on clinging to fur or passing through a gut and emerging in dung; water dispersal uses buoyant fruits; some fruits split open and eject seeds. Dispersal matters because it escapes the competition, pests and shade close to the parent, letting seedlings establish elsewhere; dormancy waits for suitable temperature and moisture.[2][7]

蒲公英的瘦果顶端长有一簇冠毛，成熟后像一把张开的小伞。冠毛由约上百根细丝组成，按轮排列在果柄顶端，张开后形成多孔的伞状结构；2018 年一项流体力学研究在风洞中用示踪气流观测到，气流穿过冠毛时，冠毛下方会形成一个与种子分离、不贴附丝丛的回流涡环，涡环稳定地提供升力，把沉降速度压得很低。单枚瘦果质量在约 0.1 mg 量级，很容易被微弱气流带走。A dandelion achene carries a pappus on its tip that opens like a small umbrella when ripe. The pappus is made of roughly a hundred fine bristles that form a porous parachute. A 2018 fluid-dynamics study in wind tunnels visualised the flow and found a vortex ring that stays detached below the pappus; air passing through the bristles maintains this recirculating ring, which provides lift and lowers the settling speed. One achene weighs about 0.1 mg, so it is easy for a weak breeze to carry.[9]

冠毛并不是实心伞面。丝与丝之间留有孔隙，气流可以穿过；低雷诺数下丝周围空气黏性占主导，相邻细丝相互作用，维持涡环不被吹散。冠毛增大了拖曳面积，使种子缓慢飘落，而不是像小石子那样直落地面，张开角度也会影响阻力和升力大小。风速达到起飞阈值后，种子脱离果球，随气流水平漂移，无风时只能短距离滚落。The pappus is not a solid canopy. Gaps remain between bristles, air can pass through, and neighbouring hairs interact at a low Reynolds number to keep the vortex ring stable. The pappus enlarges drag area so the seed drifts down slowly instead of dropping like a pebble. Once wind reaches a take-off threshold, the seed leaves the head and drifts horizontally; in still air it only rolls a short distance.[2][9]

其他风媒结构走另一条路线。槭树翅果带薄翅，下落时螺旋旋转，靠翼面滑翔减速；动物传播的果实常带硬壳或黏液，抵抗消化或粘在皮毛上；水媒果皮疏松多孔、比重小；弹射传播常见于豆科、凤仙花等，果皮失水卷曲把种子弹出数十厘米。不同结构对应不同媒介，近缘种之间也可能出现混合方式。Other wind-dispersed structures take a different route. Maple samaras carry a thin wing and spin as they fall, gliding to slow descent; animal-dispersed fruits often have hard shells or mucus to resist digestion or stick to fur; water-dispersed skins are porous and low in density. Structures match their vectors, and close relatives may mix modes.[7]

边界：传播距离受地形、风速、湿度和果球成熟度影响，冠毛飞行研究多以蒲公英等草本为模型，木本风媒种子的细节有差异。被风吹到远处不等于定居成功，种子落地后还要面对土壤、光照和竞争；弹射、动物和水力传播各有自己的适用范围，不能只凭果实外形断定媒介。Boundary: dispersal distance depends on terrain, wind speed, humidity and ripeness; pappus flight work is mostly modelled on herbaceous dandelion-type seeds, and woody wind-dispersed seeds differ in detail. Being blown far does not guarantee establishment: after landing the seed still faces soil, light and competition. Explosive, animal and water dispersal each have their own range, and fruit shape alone cannot settle the vector.[2][7]

09花为什么颜色鲜艳？蘑菇为什么长在阴暗处？Why are flowers brightly coloured, and why do mushrooms grow in the shade?

花色由花青素等色素配合传粉者视觉；真菌无叶绿素，靠分解腐殖质在阴湿处生长。Flower colours signal pollinators through pigments and UV guides; fungi have no chlorophyll, feed on decomposing matter in damp shade.

花的颜色主要是给传粉者看的视觉信号。花瓣色素以液泡中的花青素和细胞内的类胡萝卜素为主：类胡萝卜素给出黄、橙色调，花青素随细胞液 pH 改变颜色，酸性偏红、碱性偏蓝，深浅还受糖苷结构、糖分积累和温度影响；花瓣表皮细胞的细微纹理也会改变反光。花色与花蜜、气味、花形配合，把访花者引到雄蕊和雌蕊附近，提高传粉效率。Flower colour is mainly a visual signal to pollinators. Petal pigments are mostly anthocyanins held in the vacuole and carotenoids inside cells; anthocyanins shift with vacuolar pH, red in acid and blue in alkaline, with depth also affected by sugar accumulation and temperature. Colour works together with nectar, scent and shape to guide visitors to stamens and stigma and raise pollination success.[6][7]

昆虫的视觉范围和人眼不同。蜜蜂等传粉昆虫可见紫外波段，约 300–400 nm 的紫外光也在其感知范围内；蜂类复眼对蓝紫和紫外敏感、对红光感知较弱，因此虫媒花多以蓝紫色调为主，鸟媒花则常偏红。许多花瓣上有人眼看不见的紫外导蜜纹，从边缘指向花心，标记花蜜和花粉位置。花的开放时段、颜色和气味对应不同访花者，夜间花多偏白、气味浓，吸引蛾类。Insect vision differs from human vision. Bees and other pollinators see into the ultraviolet, around the 300 to 400 nm band; many petals carry ultraviolet nectar guides that are invisible to us, leading from the edge toward the centre where nectar and pollen sit. Opening time, colour and scent match particular visitors; night flowers tend to be pale and strongly scented for moths.[7]

蘑菇并不是植物。真菌不含叶绿素，不能进行光合作用，属于异养生物；菌丝在土壤或朽木中蔓延成网状，分泌胞外酶把落叶、朽木中的纤维素和木质素分解成可吸收的小分子，再由菌丝直接吸收现成有机物作为碳源和能量，在生态系统中承担分解者角色。真菌不形成根、茎、叶的分化结构，我们看到的伞盖只是繁殖子实体，大部分菌体以菌丝藏在基质内部。A mushroom is not a plant. Fungi lack chlorophyll and cannot photosynthesise, so they are heterotrophic. Hyphae in the substrate secrete extracellular enzymes that break down cellulose and lignin in leaves and rotting wood into absorbable small molecules, then take up ready-made organic matter for carbon and energy, acting as decomposers. The cap we see is only the reproductive fruiting body; most of the organism lives as hidden hyphae inside the substrate.[10]

蘑菇多长在阴暗处，与养分来源和湿度有关。林下阴湿处积累了枯落物和腐殖质，正是腐生真菌的食物来源；阴凉环境湿度高，子实体不容易被阳光快速晒干。子实体多在雨后湿度较高时出现，成熟后释放粉状孢子，孢子随风落到合适基质再萌发成新菌丝。朽木、腐殖土和林下地被层是真菌常见的生长位置，光照本身并不被真菌用作能量。Mushrooms favour shade because of their food source and moisture. Damp forest floors accumulate litter and humus, the food supply of saprotrophic fungi; the cool humid air keeps the fruiting body from drying fast in sunlight, and spores disperse and germinate more readily in moist air. Rotting wood, humus soil and forest ground cover are typical sites, and light itself is not used by the fungus as energy.[10]

边界：花色还受土壤 pH、温度和营养状态影响，同一植株不同花可有色差；真菌细胞壁由几丁质构成，与植物的纤维素壁不同，分类上更接近动物。野生蘑菇是否可食不能凭外形或颜色判断，本章只讲结构与生境机制，不提供采食依据。Boundary: flower colour also shifts with soil pH, temperature and nutrition, and flowers on the same plant can differ. Fungal cell walls are made of chitin, unlike the cellulose walls of plants, and fungi are closer to animals in classification. Whether a wild mushroom is edible cannot be judged by shape or colour alone; this chapter covers structure and habitat, not any foraging guidance.[6][10]
