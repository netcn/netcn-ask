01人为什么会打哈欠？Why do we yawn?

打哈欠不是“缺氧”，更像大脑散热与状态切换的信号；传染性与共情、社交认知相关，但不是诊断依据。Yawning is less about oxygen than a brain-cooling and state-switching signal; contagion is linked to empathy and social cognition, but is not a diagnostic test.

会议室里一个人打哈欠，几秒后对面的人也张开了嘴。我们常把打哈欠解释成“大脑缺氧”，但实验证据并不支持这个直觉。一次完整的哈欠大约持续 6 秒：张大嘴、深吸气、短暂停顿，再缓慢呼气；它更容易出现在困倦、刚醒来或注意力切换的时候，更像身体发出的状态信号，而不是一次紧急补氧。One person yawns in a meeting; seconds later, someone across the table does too. The familiar explanation — "the brain needs oxygen" — is not what the evidence supports. A full yawn lasts about six seconds: the mouth opens wide, a deep inhalation follows, then a brief pause and a slow exhalation. It appears most often during drowsiness, just after waking, or when attention shifts — closer to a state signal than an emergency oxygen top-up.[10]

呼吸假说最早也最流行：血液氧含量低或二氧化碳升高，人就会打哈欠。可实验并不配合。让受试者吸入高浓度氧，或改变二氧化碳浓度，并没有稳定增加哈欠；过度换气造成的低碳酸血症，有时反而伴随哈欠。心衰、肺病患者长期缺氧，也不见得比普通人更爱打哈欠。更关键的是，哈欠的深吸气后还跟着一段短暂屏气，用它来补氧，效率远不如几次正常深呼吸。所谓“打哈欠=缺氧”，更像一个流传很广的直觉，而不是被证实的机制。The respiratory hypothesis was the first and most popular: low blood oxygen or high carbon dioxide makes us yawn. Experiments do not cooperate. Breathing high concentrations of oxygen, or changing carbon dioxide, does not reliably increase yawning; hyperventilation-induced hypocapnia is sometimes accompanied by yawns. Patients with chronic hypoxia from heart or lung disease do not necessarily yawn more than healthy people. And the deep inhalation of a yawn is followed by a brief apnea — an inefficient way to top up oxygen compared with a few normal deep breaths. "Yawning equals oxygen shortage" is a widespread intuition, not a demonstrated mechanism.[10]

另一条更有竞争力的解释是脑温调节。哈欠时下颌、面颈和咽喉部肌肉强力收缩，局部血流加快；深吸气把较凉的空气带入口鼻腔，呼气又带走热量。动物实验里，大鼠在哈欠前常出现小幅脑温上升，哈欠后脑温下降；在人身上，给额头冷敷或用鼻腔呼吸，会降低“传染性哈欠”的发生率；环境温度接近体温时，哈欠也会变少。这些结果拼起来，像是身体在给大脑做一次短暂的散热。A stronger alternative is brain-temperature regulation. During a yawn, the jaw, facial, neck, and throat muscles contract forcefully, increasing local blood flow; a deep inhalation brings cooler air into the nose and mouth, and exhalation carries heat away. In animal studies, rats often show a small rise in brain temperature before a yawn and a fall afterward. In humans, forehead cooling or nasal breathing reduces the incidence of contagious yawning, and yawning becomes less frequent when ambient temperature approaches body temperature. Together, these results look like a brief radiator cycle for the brain.[11][12]

但冷却说并不是定论。反对者指出，冷敷额头也会让人清醒，哈欠减少可能只是睡意被打断；用鼻腔呼吸同样改变了呼吸方式，很难把温度和睡意分开。另有综述认为，目前缺少直接证据证明哈欠能显著降低人脑温度，生理性假说整体证据不足，哈欠更可能是一种社会信号。换句话说，冷却说解释了一部分数据，却没有把所有反例收进来。But the cooling account is not settled. Critics note that a cold forehead pack also wakes people up, so fewer yawns may simply mean less sleepiness; nasal breathing changes breathing itself, making temperature and drowsiness hard to separate. Another review argues that direct evidence that yawning significantly lowers human brain temperature is still missing, that physiological hypotheses are weakly supported overall, and that yawning may be primarily a social signal. In other words, cooling explains part of the data without absorbing all the counterexamples.[10][11]

社会/沟通假说抓住了另一个事实：哈欠会传染。看到、听到甚至想到别人打哈欠，约 40%–60% 的成年人会在实验条件下跟着打；这种传染在 4 岁左右明显出现，孤独症谱系儿童的传染性哈欠则偏少。研究者因此猜测，哈欠可能在同步群体状态、传递困倦或压力信号。不过，传染性强不等于“读懂了对方”，更不能用一个人打不打哈欠来判断共情能力或健康状况。比较稳妥的结论是：哈欠可能同时承担散热、状态切换和社交信号几种角色，具体权重取决于情境，科学界还没有唯一答案。The social/communication hypothesis grabs another fact: yawning is contagious. Seeing, hearing, or even thinking about someone else's yawn makes roughly 40–60% of adults yawn under experimental conditions. Contagion becomes clearly established around age four, and children with autism spectrum disorder show less of it. Researchers therefore suspect that yawning may synchronize group states or transmit drowsiness or stress. But contagion is not proof that someone "reads" another person's mind, and you cannot use whether a person yawns to judge empathy or health. The safer conclusion is that yawning may serve several roles at once — cooling, state switching, and social signalling — with the balance depending on context; science has no single answer yet.[10][13]

02为什么会流泪？哭的时候为什么也会流鼻涕？Why do we cry tears, and why does crying also make your nose run?

泪液分基础、反射、情感三类；泪管通往鼻腔，所以哭会“流鼻涕”；情感性哭泣后的副交感回升让人平静。Tears come in basal, reflex, and emotional kinds; the tear ducts drain into the nose, so crying makes it run; the parasympathetic rebound after emotional crying calms us.

眼泪不是只有“哭”才有。基础泪液持续润滑角膜、冲刷灰尘；反射泪液在风、洋葱、烟雾或异物刺激时大量分泌；情感泪液在情绪波动时出现，人类研究得最多，其他动物是否有类似状态仍有争议。泪液类似血浆的超滤液，含盐、蛋白质、溶菌酶和免疫球蛋白，所以是咸的；它不是“排毒水”，也不能把体内毒素冲走。Tears are not only for crying. Basal tears lubricate the cornea and flush debris; reflex tears surge with wind, onions, smoke, or a foreign body; emotional tears appear with strong feeling and are best studied in humans, while whether other animals have a comparable state is debated. Tears resemble an ultrafiltrate of plasma, containing salt, proteins, lysozyme, and immunoglobulins, which is why they taste salty. They are not a detox fluid and cannot flush toxins out of the body.[1][2]

哭时流鼻涕，主要是泪液改道。泪腺分泌的泪液经鼻泪管流入鼻腔；平静时分泌量小，鼻腔黏膜能处理；情绪性哭泣时泪液量可在短时间内明显增加，鼻腔来不及引流，液体就从鼻孔流出。看起来像“鼻涕”，其实是眼泪，不是鼻窦发炎。鼻泪管堵塞时，平时也可能溢泪。鼻泪管很细，婴儿尤其容易因为狭窄而溢泪。A runny nose during crying is mostly tears taking another route. The lacrimal gland drains through the nasolacrimal duct into the nose; at rest the volume is small enough for the nasal mucosa to handle, but emotional crying can raise tear flow sharply, overwhelming drainage so fluid exits the nostril. It looks like mucus but is tear fluid, not sinusitis. A blocked duct can also cause tearing at other times.[1][3]

情感泪液的成分与基础泪液不完全相同，研究检测到促肾上腺皮质激素、脑啡肽等与应激和情绪相关的物质，但浓度很低，不能证明“哭能排毒”。哭泣时心率、呼吸和肌肉张力先升高，随后副交感活动增强，有些人会感到放松；这种“哭后平静”受情境、陪伴和文化影响，不是每个人都能获得。这也解释了为什么有人哭完头痛、眼睛肿：眼睑和鼻黏膜的血管扩张、组织液增多。Emotional tears differ somewhat from basal tears; studies have detected ACTH, enkephalins, and other stress- or emotion-related substances, but at low concentrations, which does not prove that crying removes toxins. Heart rate, breathing, and muscle tension often rise first, followed by increased parasympathetic activity and a sense of calm in some people. This after-cry calm depends on context, company, and culture, and not everyone gets it.[1][3]

哭泣也有社会功能：它向他人发出需要帮助的信号，可能换来安慰和支持；独自哭、被责备后哭，效果可能相反。文化规范允许或禁止表达情绪，会改变哭泣的发生率和主观感受。把哭当成“软弱”或“万能解药”，都不符合证据。旁观者的反应会改变哭泣的走向：被接纳时更容易平复，被羞辱时可能更难受。不是所有哭都有效，眼泪不是万能药。Crying also has a social function: it signals a need for help and may bring comfort and support. Crying alone, or after being blamed, can have the opposite effect. Cultural rules about expressing emotion change how often people cry and how it feels afterward. Treating crying as weakness or as a universal cure is not supported by the evidence.[1][2]

边界：持续流泪、眼痛、畏光、视力变化，或单侧流泪伴鼻塞，可能提示干眼、结膜炎、鼻泪管阻塞等问题，应看眼科；婴儿哭时流泪少或完全没有眼泪，也需要评估。情绪性哭泣若长期影响生活，可寻求心理支持，而不是硬憋或强迫自己哭。Boundaries: persistent tearing, eye pain, light sensitivity, vision change, or unilateral tearing with nasal blockage may point to dry eye, conjunctivitis, or a blocked nasolacrimal duct and deserves an eye examination. An infant who cries without tears also needs assessment. If emotional crying is persistently disruptive, psychological support is more useful than forcing or suppressing it.[1][3]

03鼻子有什么用？为什么会打喷嚏？为什么香味闻久了就不香了？What is the nose for? Why do we sneeze, and why does a scent fade after a while?

鼻子是空气空调加气味传感器；喷嚏是神经反射；闻久不香是嗅觉适应，不是鼻子坏了。The nose is an air conditioner plus an odour sensor; a sneeze is a neural reflex; a fading scent is olfactory adaptation, not a broken nose.

鼻子不是一根通气管。它把吸入的空气加温、加湿、过滤，还把气味分子送到鼻腔顶部一小片嗅上皮；那里的嗅觉神经元把化学信号变成电信号，再送到嗅球。鼻腔里的鼻甲像折流板，让空气走更长的路，也给嗅上皮更多接触气味的机会。嗅上皮只有约 5 平方厘米，却容纳数百万个嗅觉神经元；每个神经元末端伸出纤毛，气味分子先溶进黏液，再与受体结合。The nose is not just a tube. It warms, humidifies, and filters inhaled air, and delivers odour molecules to a small patch of olfactory epithelium high in the nasal cavity; olfactory neurons there turn chemical signals into electrical ones and send them to the olfactory bulb. The nasal conchae act like baffles, lengthening the air path and giving odour molecules more contact with the epithelium. The olfactory epithelium is only about 5 square centimetres, yet it holds millions of olfactory neurons; each neuron ends in cilia, where odour molecules dissolve in mucus before binding receptors.[1][8][9]

打喷嚏是保护性反射。灰尘、病毒、冷空气或辣椒素刺激鼻腔黏膜，触发三叉神经末梢；信号传到脑干，先深吸气，再关闭声门、让胸腔增压，最后高速把空气从鼻和口喷出。2021 年 Li 等追踪到一条从鼻腔到脑干的肽能通路：TRPV1+ 感觉神经元释放神经调节素 B（NMB），激活脑干 NMBR+ 神经元，再驱动呼吸肌。喷嚏不是“把刺激物直接吹走”这么简单，它需要呼吸肌、声门和软腭的精确配合；腹肌、肋间肌和膈肌一起发力，声门在最后才打开。A sneeze is a protective reflex. Dust, viruses, cold air, or capsaicin irritate the nasal lining and trigger trigeminal nerve endings; the signal reaches the brainstem, which first deepens the breath, then closes the glottis, pressurises the chest, and finally blasts air out through nose and mouth. In 2021, Li and colleagues traced a peptidergic pathway from nose to brainstem: TRPV1+ sensory neurons release neuromedin B (NMB), which activates NMBR+ brainstem neurons and drives the respiratory muscles. A sneeze is not simply “blowing the irritant away”; it needs precise coordination of respiratory muscles, glottis, and soft palate—abdominal, intercostal, and diaphragm muscles fire together, and the glottis opens only at the end.[6][7]

香味闻久了不香，不是鼻子坏了，而是嗅觉适应。气味分子持续结合受体，细胞内钙信号和受体脱敏让反应下降；同时大脑会把注意力从恒定背景移开。适应发生在几秒到几分钟，恢复也很快，所以离开一会儿再回来又能闻到。A scent fading is not a broken nose; it is olfactory adaptation. Odour molecules keep binding receptors, calcium signalling and receptor desensitisation reduce the response, and the brain shifts attention away from a constant background. Adaptation develops over seconds to minutes and recovers quickly, so leaving the room and returning restores the scent.[9][10]

它通常只针对特定气味，不会让所有气味一起变淡；这也是为什么调香师能在混合气味中继续分辨。适应的时间尺度取决于气味浓度和持续时间，强气味可能让适应更快、更深。It is usually specific to one odour, not a blanket dulling of all smells—which is why a perfumer can keep resolving notes in a mixture. The timescale depends on concentration and duration; strong odours can adapt faster and more deeply.[10]

边界：“喷嚏速度上百公里/小时”“能喷几米远”多是夸张；真实喷射距离通常在几米内，速度也因测量方法而异。“挖鼻孔会挖到大脑”是都市传说，但手指确实可能把细菌带入黏膜，增加局部感染风险。感冒时闻不到味道，多是鼻腔黏膜和嗅上皮的暂时损伤，通常可逆；若长期嗅觉丧失，或伴随鼻塞、头痛、嗅觉倒错，就不只是“适应”，应就医。Boundary: “sneezes travel at over 100 km/h” and “spray several metres” are mostly exaggeration; real distances are usually within a few metres, and speed depends on measurement. “Picking your nose reaches the brain” is an urban legend, but fingers can carry bacteria into the mucosa and raise local infection risk. Smell loss during a cold usually reflects temporary damage to the nasal lining and olfactory epithelium and is often reversible; persistent loss, or loss with congestion, headache, or distorted smell, is more than adaptation and deserves medical advice.[7][8]

04舌头为什么能尝出味道？吃辣椒为什么“辣”？唾液是干什么的？How does the tongue taste? Why does chilli burn, and what is saliva for?

舌头尝味靠的不是整片舌面均匀分布的“味觉地图”——那个分区图是误传。真正的味觉单位是味蕾：几千个味蕾主要住在舌乳头里，每个含数十个受体细胞，五种基本味道在同一个味蕾上都能被检测。甜、苦、鲜由 G 蛋白偶联受体识别，咸与酸直接走离子通道；味蕾只是前端，嗅觉和质地也在“味道”里。味蕾的分布并不按“甜在前、苦在后”排列。Taste is not a uniform map across the tongue; that regional map is a myth. The functional unit is the taste bud: thousands of buds sit mainly in lingual papillae, each with dozens of receptor cells, and all five basic tastes can be detected in the same bud. Sweet, bitter, and umami use G-protein-coupled receptors, while salty and sour use ion channels. The bud is only the front end; smell and texture also shape what we call flavour.[1][2]

辣的真相是：它不是味道。辣椒素结合的是热痛受体 TRPV1——平时负责感受 43℃ 以上烫伤的那个通道。受体被辣椒素激活后，大脑收到的是“烫”的信号，加上痛觉纤维参与，于是形成“火辣辣”。所以辣度不是味觉强度，而是痛温觉被劫持的程度；牛奶、油脂能带走辣椒素，水反而效果差。TRPV1 也存在于皮肤和内脏，所以辣有时会带来全身发热、出汗。所谓“吃辣上瘾”，更接近痛觉被安抚后的奖赏循环：辣椒素激活痛觉纤维后，身体释放内啡肽镇痛并带来轻松感；反复接触会让 TRPV1 对刺激逐步脱敏，耐受量慢慢提高，于是需要更辣才能得到同等体验，这并非真正的物质成瘾。The truth about spiciness is that it is not a taste. Capsaicin binds TRPV1, the heat-pain channel normally activated above about 43°C. Once capsaicin opens it, the brain receives a burn signal, and pain fibres add to the sensation. Heat is therefore hijacked thermo-pain, not taste intensity. Milk and fat help remove capsaicin; water is less effective. The so-called craving for spice is closer to a reward loop after pain relief: once capsaicin activates pain fibres, the body releases endorphins that blunt discomfort and produce a light euphoria; repeated exposure gradually desensitizes TRPV1, so tolerance rises and more heat is needed for the same effect. This is not a true substance addiction.[1][3][19]

唾液远不只是“口水”。它每天分泌约 1–1.5 升：淀粉酶启动淀粉消化；黏蛋白把食物和口腔黏膜润滑起来，帮助吞咽和说话；它还是缓冲液，中和产酸细菌造成的酸蚀，并携带溶菌酶和免疫球蛋白做初级抗菌。唾液减少时，龋齿和口腔感染风险上升，所以口干不是小事。唾液里的黏蛋白还能包裹病毒，减少附着，但不能替代疫苗。Saliva is far more than water. Humans produce roughly 1–1.5 litres a day: amylase starts starch digestion; mucin lubricates food and oral mucosa for swallowing and speech; bicarbonate buffers acid from plaque bacteria; lysozyme and immunoglobulins provide initial antimicrobial defence. When saliva falls, caries and oral infection risk rise, so dry mouth is not a trivial complaint.[1][3]

味觉差异有很大遗传成分。苦味受体 TAS2R38 的变异让一些人对苦味特别敏感；香菜里的醛类气味对部分人像“肥皂”，与嗅觉受体差异有关。年龄、药物、鼻塞和吸烟也会改变味觉。把“挑食”全归因于性格，会忽略这些生理差异。嗅觉丧失时，人会误以为“味道变了”。Taste differences have a strong genetic component. Variants in the bitter receptor TAS2R38 make some people especially sensitive to bitterness; aldehydes in cilantro taste soapy to some people, linked to olfactory receptor differences. Age, medicines, nasal blockage, and smoking also change taste. Blaming all picky eating on character ignores these physiological differences.[1][3]

边界：味觉和嗅觉是两套系统，尝不出味道有时是嗅觉问题；突然失去味觉或嗅觉、持续口苦、口腔灼痛，应就医。唾液不是消毒水，不能靠“多咽口水”杀灭病原；辣也不是“锻炼”出来的痛觉耐受，适量即可。如果味觉改变伴随体重下降或发热，应尽快就医。Boundaries: taste and smell are separate systems, and loss of flavour can be an olfactory problem. Sudden loss of taste or smell, persistent bitter taste, or burning mouth deserves medical assessment. Saliva is not a disinfectant, and swallowing more saliva will not kill pathogens; chilli tolerance is not a measure of toughness, so moderation is reasonable.[1][2]

05牙齿为什么形状不同？为什么会烂？为什么要早晚刷牙？食物在哪儿变褐色？Why do teeth have different shapes? Why do they decay? Why brush twice daily, and where does food turn brown?

牙形各司其职（切、撕、磨）；龋齿=细菌产酸+釉质脱矿；刷牙打断生物膜；食物的褐色主要在结肠交给肠道菌群。Tooth shapes match jobs (cutting, tearing, grinding); decay equals bacteria-making-acid plus enamel demineralisation; brushing breaks the biofilm; food turns brown mainly in the colon

牙齿形状是分工的结果：门齿薄而锋利负责切割，犬齿尖长负责撕扯，前臼齿和臼齿宽大带窝沟负责研磨。这套“杂食配方”既能咬水果也能磨谷物，灵长类的牙齿形态本身就是一部食谱史；不同动物的牙列差异，反映它们吃什么、怎么处理食物。牙根和牙周膜把咀嚼力分散到颌骨，牙齿不是直接“钉”在骨头里。Tooth shape reflects division of labour: incisors are thin and sharp for cutting, canines are pointed for tearing, and premolars and molars are broad with pits and grooves for grinding. This omnivore kit can bite fruit and mill grain; primate dentition is itself a record of diet. Differences in tooth rows across animals reflect what they eat and how they process it.[1][2]

龋齿（“牙齿烂了”）的因果链很清楚：口腔里的变形链球菌等细菌附着在牙面，把食物残糖发酵成酸；局部 pH 降到 5.5 以下时牙釉质开始脱矿——釉质是人体最硬的物质，但没有细胞、不能自我修复，脱矿持续就形成龋洞。糖的“次数”比总量更关键，频繁吃甜食让酸蚀反复发生。唾液中的钙和磷能让早期脱矿再矿化，但前提是酸蚀停止。The causal chain of caries is clear. Bacteria such as Streptococcus mutans attach to the tooth surface and ferment dietary sugar into acid. When local pH falls below about 5.5, enamel begins to demineralise. Enamel is the hardest tissue in the body but has no cells and cannot repair itself, so continued demineralisation creates a cavity. Frequency matters more than total sugar: repeated sweets keep acid attacks coming.[1][2]

刷牙的价值在于打断生物膜（牙菌斑）。细菌附着几小时就会形成有结构的膜，一旦钙化成牙石，刷牙就刷不掉了。每天至少两次机械清除，加上氟化物强化釉质，能把脱矿—再矿化的天平拨回健康一侧；牙缝要用牙线或牙缝刷，漱口水和口香糖只能做补充。刷牙后不要立刻大量漱口，让氟化物在牙面多停留一会儿。Brushing matters because it disrupts the biofilm. Bacteria form a structured film within hours, and once it calcifies into tartar, brushing cannot remove it. Mechanical cleaning at least twice a day, plus fluoride to strengthen enamel, tips the balance from demineralisation toward repair. Clean between teeth with floss or interdental brushes; mouthwash and sugar-free gum are supplements, not substitutes.[1][3]

食物变褐的地方不在胃。胃里是强酸环境，褐变反应很难发生；真正的“上色”发生在结肠——肠道菌群发酵残余物，胆汁色素被还原成棕色的粪胆素，便便因此是棕色。这也是“食物在哪儿变褐色”的答案在大肠而非胃的原因；消化道出血会让便色变黑，但那是另一回事。便便颜色还受食物色素、铁剂和胆汁排泄影响。Food does not turn brown in the stomach. The stomach is strongly acidic and browning reactions are unlikely there; the colour is added in the colon, where gut bacteria ferment residues and bile pigments are reduced to brown stercobilin. That is why the answer to where food turns brown is the large intestine, not the stomach. Bleeding in the gut can make stool black, but that is a different process.[1][3]

边界：牙痛、牙龈出血、牙齿敏感不是“上火”，可能已经是龋齿或牙周病，应看牙医；美白牙膏不能替代洁牙，过度用力刷牙会磨损釉质。儿童用含氟牙膏要控制用量，吞咽控制不好的幼儿尤其要注意。定期洁牙和检查能发现自己看不到的邻面龋，牙线初次使用出血，正确使用后通常会改善。Boundaries: toothache, bleeding gums, and sensitivity are not 'heatiness'; they may already be caries or periodontal disease and need a dentist. Whitening toothpaste does not replace professional cleaning, and hard brushing wears enamel. Children using fluoride toothpaste need controlled amounts, especially before they can reliably spit.[1][2]

06眼皮为什么会跳？嘴唇发紫是怎么回事？皮肤真的能分辨冷热吗？冷了为什么会打颤起鸡皮疙瘩？Why do eyelids twitch? Why do lips turn purple? Does skin really sense hot and cold, and why do we shiver and get goosebumps?

眼皮跳=肌纤维束自发放电，多为疲劳咖啡因；紫唇=缺氧或受冷；冷热各有专职受体；打颤与立毛是产热和保温动作。Eyelid twitching is spontaneous firing of muscle-fibre bundles, usually fatigue or caffeine; purple lips mean low oxygen or cold; hot and cold each have dedicated receptors; shivering

“眼皮跳”医学上叫眼睑肌纤维颤搐：眼轮匝肌的一小束肌纤维自己随机放电，牵动皮肤反复抽动。它多数是良性的，疲劳、压力、咖啡因、干眼是常见诱因；持续数周不缓解、牵连半张脸、伴随闭眼困难或面部无力，才需要看神经科。 “左眼跳财右眼跳灾”没有证据支持。多数人在休息、减少咖啡因后几天内自行缓解。打嗝（呃逆）是另一类不随意的反射性收缩：迷走神经与膈神经受到刺激后传入延髓呃逆中枢，膈肌和肋间肌突然痉挛收缩，同时声门快速关闭，气流受阻发出短促的“嗝”声；饱胀、冷热刺激和情绪波动常诱发，多数数分钟内自行停止。Eyelid twitching is usually myokymia: a small bundle of orbicularis oculi fibres fires spontaneously and pulls the skin. It is mostly benign, with fatigue, stress, caffeine, and dry eye as common triggers. Twitching that persists for weeks, involves half the face, or comes with difficulty closing the eye or facial weakness deserves neurological assessment. The folk saying about left and right eyelids predicts nothing. Most settle within days with rest and less caffeine. Hiccups are another involuntary reflex burst: vagal and phrenic afferents carry irritation to the medullary hiccup center, the diaphragm and intercostal muscles contract suddenly, and the glottis closes, giving the short “hic” sound; gastric distension, hot or cold stimuli, and emotion commonly trigger it, and most episodes stop within minutes.[1][4][9]

嘴唇发紫叫发绀，要看是“冷出来的”还是“缺氧出来的”。寒冷时外周血管收缩、局部血流变慢，唇色会偏暗，保暖后恢复；安静状态下持续发绀，或伴随呼吸困难、胸痛、意识改变，则可能提示心肺问题，需要急诊。发绀的深浅受血红蛋白总量和光线影响，不能单凭颜色判断血氧。中心性发绀常累及舌和口腔黏膜，外周性多限于肢端和唇。Blue lips are cyanosis, and the cause matters. Cold causes peripheral vasoconstriction and slower local flow, so the lips look darker and recover with warming. Persistent cyanosis at rest, or cyanosis with breathlessness, chest pain, or altered consciousness, may indicate a heart or lung problem and needs urgent care. Colour alone cannot measure oxygen saturation; haemoglobin level and lighting also matter. Central cyanosis often involves the tongue and oral mucosa; peripheral cyanosis is mostly limited to the extremities and lips.[1][3]

冷热不是痛觉的“副产品”。皮肤里有专职的温度感受器：TRPV1 负责灼热，TRPV3/TRPV4 参与温和热觉，TRPM8 被低温和薄荷醇激活；冷、热各有传导通路，所以薄荷只凉不烫。痛觉与温度觉共享部分通道，极热也会痛，但“冷热只是痛”的说法已被否证。温度感受器也受局部血流和皮肤状态影响。Temperature is not a by-product of pain. Skin has dedicated thermoreceptors: TRPV1 responds to burning heat, TRPV3/TRPV4 contribute to warm sensation, and TRPM8 is activated by cold and menthol. Cold and warm have separate pathways, which is why menthol feels cool rather than hot. Pain and temperature share some channels at extremes, but the idea that temperature is only pain has been disproved. Thermoreceptors are also influenced by local blood flow and skin condition.[1][3]

冷了打颤，是运动神经元让肌肉做小幅度快速收缩，机械做功直接产热；这是急性寒冷中最主要的产热方式。鸡皮疙瘩（立毛反射）是竖毛肌收缩把毛发立起来，理论上能留住一层隔热空气；人类毛发稀疏，保温效果很有限，但反射仍保留，成了寒冷和情绪共用的古老反应。寒战产热能让代谢率在短时间内升高数倍。Shivering is the main acute heat-producing response in cold: motor neurons drive rapid small muscle contractions, and mechanical work releases heat. Goosebumps come from piloerection, in which arrector pili muscles raise hairs to trap an insulating layer of air. Humans have sparse hair, so the warming effect is small, but the reflex remains, shared by cold and emotional states. Shivering can raise metabolic rate several-fold for a short time.[1][4]

边界：眼皮跳大多不用处理，但持续、加重或伴面部抽动要排查；发绀要结合呼吸、血氧和病史，不能只看嘴唇；寒冷时先保暖，若出现反应迟钝、言语含糊、剧烈寒战后的停止，要警惕低体温。夏天大汗后只喝白水、不补电解质，也可能出现低钠，不能把“多喝水”当成无条件正确。持续低体温会抑制酶活性，复温要循序渐进。Boundaries: most eyelid twitches need no treatment, but persistent or progressive twitching with facial movement needs review. Cyanosis should be judged with breathing, oxygen saturation, and history, not lip colour alone. Warm first in cold exposure, but confusion, slurred speech, or shivering that stops can signal hypothermia. After heavy sweating, replacing water without electrolytes can dilute sodium, so 'drink more water' is not unconditional. Prolonged hypothermia inhibits enzyme activity, and rewarming should be gradual.[1][3]

07为什么憋尿太久对身体不好？为什么切掉一个肾也能活？Why is holding urine too long bad for you, and why can you live with one kidney?

膀胱过度充盈让残余尿滋生细菌、削弱膀胱肌；肾脏有强大储备，一个肾就能代偿全部滤过任务。An overstretched bladder breeds bacteria with residual urine and strains the bladder wall; kidneys hold huge reserve, and one can cover the entire filtration job.

膀胱不是气球。它平时以低压储尿，逼尿肌在容量增加时保持柔软；当容量被强行撑到远超平时的水平，肌纤维会被过度拉长，反复如此可能削弱收缩力，导致之后“排不干净”。残余尿是细菌的培养基，上行感染可引起膀胱炎，甚至肾盂肾炎。膀胱壁的牵张感受器在容量达到一定程度时才发出尿意。The bladder is not a balloon. It stores urine at low pressure, and the detrusor stays soft as volume rises. Forcing it far beyond normal capacity stretches muscle fibres; repeated overdistension can weaken contraction and leave residual urine. Residual urine is a culture medium for bacteria, and ascending infection can cause cystitis or pyelonephritis. Stretch receptors in the bladder wall trigger the urge only after a certain volume.[1][2]

肾脏的储备远超日常所需。两个肾每天滤过约 180 升原尿，只留下 1.5–2 升尿；单个肾通常就足以维持血肌酐和电解质。捐肾后，剩下那个肾会代偿性增大，单肾滤过率能恢复到正常总和的七成以上；这也是活体捐肾在严格筛选下能够开展的基础。肾小球滤过率在捐肾后数月内逐步稳定。The kidneys have far more reserve than daily needs. Two kidneys filter about 180 litres of primary urine a day but produce only 1.5–2 litres of final urine; one kidney is usually enough to maintain creatinine and electrolytes. After donation, the remaining kidney undergoes compensatory enlargement, and single-kidney filtration can recover to more than seventy percent of the original total, which is why living donation is possible under strict screening. GFR stabilises over months after donation.[1][2]

长期随访显示，单肾捐献者终末期肾病的相对风险略高于常人，但绝对风险仍低，总体寿命与对照人群没有明显差异。前提是本身没有高血压、糖尿病、蛋白尿或肾结构异常；这些情况会让单肾负担显著加重，因此捐肾前要评估，而不是“少一个肾也能活”就人人适合。单肾者要控制血压、避免肾毒性药物。Long-term follow-up shows that kidney donors have a slightly higher relative risk of end-stage renal disease, but the absolute risk remains low, and overall survival is not clearly different from controls. The premise is no hypertension, diabetes, proteinuria, or structural kidney disease; those conditions shift more burden to the single kidney. 'You can live with one kidney' is therefore not a reason to skip evaluation. Single-kidney patients should control blood pressure and avoid nephrotoxic drugs.[1][2]

憋尿的风险不在“一次”，而在“长期、反复、极限”。短时间憋尿（比如开会两小时）通常无害；有结石、前列腺增生、神经源性膀胱的人，排尿不畅会让残余尿更多，应把及时排尿当成医嘱级习惯。尿色深多数是浓缩，不代表“肾在排毒”；尿量骤减、泡沫持续、血尿才是需要检查的信号。尿液浓缩能力也会随年龄下降。The risk of holding urine lies in long, repeated, extreme retention rather than one short episode. Holding for a two-hour meeting is usually harmless; people with stones, prostate enlargement, or a neurogenic bladder empty poorly and should treat timely voiding as medical advice. Dark urine is usually concentrated urine, not evidence of 'kidney detox'; a sudden drop in output, persistent foam, or blood in urine needs investigation. Urine-concentrating ability also declines with age.[1][3]

边界：尿痛、尿频、发热、腰侧痛可能提示尿路感染，应就医；突然完全尿不出来、下腹剧痛是急性尿潴留，需要急诊。女性尿道短，感染风险更高，但男性前列腺问题同样不能忽视。少憋尿、适量饮水、不滥用抗生素，比任何“养肾偏方”都可靠。每日尿量少于 400 毫升属于少尿，少于 100 毫升属于无尿；发热伴腰痛不要只吃止痛药；不要硬撑，及时排空。Boundaries: dysuria, frequency, fever, and flank pain may indicate urinary infection and need care. Sudden inability to urinate with severe lower abdominal pain is acute retention and an emergency. Women have a shorter urethra and higher infection risk, but prostate problems in men matter too. Avoiding prolonged retention, drinking sensibly, and not abusing antibiotics are more reliable than any 'kidney tonic'. Urine output below 400 mL/day is oliguria and below 100 mL/day is anuria; fever with flank pain should not be treated with painkillers alone.[1][2]

08身体怎么知道该喝水了？人体吸收热量有上限吗？人能靠自身脂肪活多久？How does the body know when to drink? Is there a limit to calorie absorption, and how long can a human live on stored fat?

渴感由渗透压和血容量共同触发；吸收有物理上限；脂肪每千克约 7700 千卡，是身体的“能量仓库”。Thirst is triggered by osmolality and blood volume together; absorption has physical limits; fat stores about 7,700 kcal per kilogram — the body's energy warehouse.

“渴”是一种精确的生理报警。下丘脑的渗透压感受器监测血浆浓度，失水达到体重的 1%–2% 就能触发渴感；血容量或血压下降则通过压力感受器和肾素—血管紧张素系统走另一条通路，促进血管升压素释放、减少尿量并加强渴的信号。两条通路都指向同一件事：身体需要水。抗利尿激素作用于集合管，让水重吸收增加。Thirst is a precise physiological alarm. Hypothalamic osmoreceptors monitor plasma concentration, and a water deficit of about 1–2% of body weight can trigger thirst. A fall in blood volume or blood pressure acts through baroreceptors and the renin-angiotensin system, promoting vasopressin release, reducing urine output, and reinforcing thirst. Both routes point to the same need for water. Vasopressin acts on the collecting duct to increase water reabsorption.[1][2]

“等渴了再喝”要分人分场景。健康人短时间靠渴感调节通常够用；高温、运动、发热、腹泻，以及老年人和部分服药者，渴感可能滞后，需要按计划补水。反过来，短时间内灌下大量白水也可能稀释血钠，引起头痛、恶心甚至低钠血症；补水要少量多次，而不是一次“冲”。运动饮料不是人人需要，糖和电解质要看运动强度和环境。The advice to drink before thirst depends on the person and situation. For healthy people, thirst usually regulates short-term intake adequately; in heat, exercise, fever, diarrhoea, and in older adults or some medication users, thirst may lag, so planned intake helps. Conversely, drinking large volumes of plain water quickly can dilute sodium and cause headache, nausea, or hyponatraemia. Small, repeated drinks are safer than one large surge. Sports drinks are not for everyone; sugar and electrolytes depend on exercise intensity and environment.[1][2]

吸收有上限，但不是一个固定克数。胃排空速度、肠道转运体的饱和、食物渗透压都会影响单位时间的吸收；短时间大量糖或脂肪涌入，可能因渗透负荷和转运不及引起腹胀、腹泻。脂肪的吸收更慢，胃排空也更慢，所以一顿高脂大餐的“饱”会拖得更久。胃排空还受食物温度、体积和成分影响。Absorption has limits, but not a single fixed number of grams. Gastric emptying, saturation of intestinal transporters, and the osmotic load of food all shape how much is absorbed per unit time. A sudden large load of sugar or fat can exceed transport and cause bloating or diarrhoea. Fat empties more slowly and is absorbed more slowly, so a high-fat meal keeps satiety around longer. Gastric emptying also depends on food temperature, volume, and composition.[1][3]

脂肪是身体的正规仓库：每千克脂肪组织约储存 7700 千卡。一个体脂 15 千克的成年人，仓库里约有 11.5 万千卡，理论上能在不进食的情况下支撑数周。但只靠脂肪不够：必需氨基酸、必需脂肪酸、维生素和电解质无法由脂肪提供；长期禁食后恢复进食，还要警惕再喂养综合征。脂肪组织同时是内分泌器官，会分泌瘦素等激素。Fat is a legitimate energy store: each kilogram of adipose tissue holds roughly 7,700 kcal. An adult with 15 kg of body fat therefore carries about 115,500 kcal, enough in theory to survive weeks without food. But fat alone is not enough: essential amino acids, essential fatty acids, vitamins, and electrolytes cannot come from adipose tissue, and refeeding after prolonged starvation can trigger refeeding syndrome. Adipose tissue is also an endocrine organ, secreting leptin and other hormones.[1][2]

边界：热量“吸收上限”和“存储上限”是两回事。单餐吸收不完的部分会进入结肠发酵，但长期过量摄入仍会以脂肪储存，体重增加没有“吃到某个数就自动停止”的开关。持续口渴、多尿、体重下降可能提示糖尿病；水肿、乏力、心悸则要查电解质和心肾功能，不要用“多喝水”掩盖。短期体重波动多为水分，不是脂肪增减。Boundaries: absorptive capacity and storage capacity are different. What is not absorbed in one meal may ferment in the colon, but chronic excess intake still becomes stored fat; there is no automatic stop at a particular calorie count. Persistent thirst, frequent urination, and weight loss may suggest diabetes; oedema, fatigue, and palpitations call for electrolyte and heart/kidney assessment. Do not use 'drink more water' to explain away those signs. Short-term weight swings are mostly water, not fat.[1][3]

09痒到底是什么？自己挠自己不痒怎么回事？舔伤口、血凝固有用吗？What actually is an itch? Why can't you tickle yourself? Do licking wounds and blood clotting really help?

痒是“想抓挠”的特异性感觉；挠痒痒的抑制来自小脑预测；唾液抗菌有限但可能带菌；凝血靠瀑布式级联只在破损处启动。An itch is the specific urge-to-scratch sensation; self-tickling fails because the cerebellum predicts it; saliva's antimicrobial effect is real but limited, and clotting works via a

痒不是轻微的痛，而是一种独立的感觉。特化的感觉神经元、脊髓通路和脑内网络专门传递“想抓挠”的冲动；组胺只是其中一条通路，神经性瘙痒、胆汁淤积性瘙痒、尿毒症性瘙痒并不都靠组胺。这也是为什么抗组胺药对一部分瘙痒有效，对另一部分却几乎无效。慢性痒还常伴随睡眠障碍和情绪负担。Itch is not mild pain but a distinct sensation. Specialised sensory neurons, spinal pathways, and brain networks carry the urge to scratch. Histamine is only one route; neuropathic itch, cholestatic itch, and uraemic itch do not all depend on histamine. That is why antihistamines work for some itches and do little for others. Chronic itch often brings sleep disturbance and emotional burden.[1][4]

抓挠能短暂止痒，是因为“轻微痛+压力”的输入覆盖了痒的信号，类似闸门控制；但抓破皮肤会启动炎症，炎症又加重痒，形成“越抓越痒”的循环。止痒的第一步是降温、保湿、减少刺激，而不是抓到破皮；持续瘙痒要找出原因，不能只靠意志力忍。Scratching gives brief relief because light pain and pressure input override the itch signal, similar to gate control. But breaking the skin starts inflammation, and inflammation worsens itch, creating an itch-scratch cycle. The first response should be cooling, moisturising, and reducing irritation, not scratching until the skin bleeds; persistent itch needs a cause, not just willpower.[1][4]

“自己挠自己为什么不痒”与大脑的预测有关：当你自己伸手去挠，脑内运动系统提前生成“即将有什么感觉”的预测，并把这部分自产感觉从体验中减弱；别人挠你没有这个预测，感觉更完整。它不是“自己挠就不痒”的绝对规律，痒的强度和部位仍会改变结果。Why does self-scratching feel less itchy? The brain predicts self-generated touch: when you reach to scratch, motor systems generate an expectation of the coming sensation and attenuate it. Another person scratching you lacks that prediction, so the sensation arrives more fully. It is not an absolute rule; itch intensity and location still change the result.[1][2]

舔伤口有真实成分，也有风险。唾液里的溶菌酶、乳过氧化物酶和富组氨酸蛋白（histatins）能轻度抑菌，血小板因子也能参与局部凝血；但口腔里有数百种细菌，深伤口、动物咬伤或免疫力低下的人被舔，可能引入感染。应急处理是清水冲洗、干净纱布加压止血，而不是用嘴去“消毒”。Licking a wound has real components and real risks. Saliva contains lysozyme, lactoperoxidase, and histatins with mild antimicrobial action, and platelet factors can participate in local clotting. But the mouth carries hundreds of bacterial species; a deep wound, animal bite, or immunocompromised person may be exposed to infection. Emergency care is clean water irrigation and firm pressure with clean gauze, not licking as disinfection.[1][2]

凝血是身体版的“哪里破损哪里启动”：血管受损暴露胶原后，血小板黏附聚集；凝血因子按级联放大，最终把可溶的纤维蛋白原切成不溶的纤维蛋白网，兜住血细胞形成血栓。健康人几分钟到十几分钟就能初步止血；血友病或抗凝药物使用者则可能持续出血。边界：痒超过 6 周、伴体重下降、夜间盗汗、黄疸或皮疹扩散，应就医；伤口深、脏、被动物咬伤，或出现红肿热痛、脓液、发热，要清创并评估破伤风/狂犬病；不要用酒精、牙膏、酱油涂伤口，也别把“舔一舔”当成可靠的消毒手段。磕碰后皮下漏出的红细胞破裂，血红蛋白依次分解为胆绿素、胆红素和含铁血黄素，淤青因此由紫青转黄绿，再慢慢消退。Clotting is the body's 'start where the damage is' system: exposed collagen lets platelets adhere and aggregate; coagulation factors amplify in a cascade and convert soluble fibrinogen into insoluble fibrin, trapping blood cells in a clot. Healthy people stop bleeding within minutes to a quarter of an hour; people with haemophilia or on anticoagulants may bleed persistently. Boundaries: itch lasting more than six weeks, with weight loss, night sweats, jaundice, or spreading rash, needs medical review. Deep, dirty, or animal-bite wounds, or wounds with spreading redness, heat, pain, pus, or fever, need cleaning and assessment for tetanus and rabies. Do not apply alcohol, toothpaste, or soy sauce to a wound, and do not treat licking as reliable disinfection.In a bruise, red blood cells leak under the skin and break apart; haemoglobin is then decomposed into biliverdin, bilirubin and haemosiderin, so the mark shifts from purple-blue to green-yellow before fading.[1][2]

10为什么有的器官成双、有的只有一个？人为什么没有尾巴？小孩为什么先爬后走？Why are some organs paired and others single? Why did we lose our tail, and why do babies crawl first?

成双源于左右对称，单发源于发育时的信号偏侧化；尾巴在胚胎期出现又退化；爬是四足步态的发育遗产。Pairing comes from bilateral symmetry; single organs arise from developmental signals that fuse or bias one side; the tail appears in embryos then regresses; crawling is a four-limbed

人体的“成双”来自左右对称这一基本身体计划：肺、肾、眼睛、输尿管都是镜像复制的备份件。心脏、肝脏、胃看起来“只有一个”，其实是胚胎期成对原基在发育中被信号融合或偏向一侧的结果——左右心管在中线融合成单心，成对的器官原基再逐步整合。成双提供备份，成单则集中功能，两者并不矛盾。这种备份逻辑也解释了为什么单肾、单肺叶缺失有时仍能维持生活。The body's paired organs come from a basic left-right symmetry: lungs, kidneys, eyes, and ureters are mirrored backups. The heart, liver, and stomach look single, but they arise from paired primordia that fuse or become biased to one side during development; the paired heart tubes, for example, merge at the midline. Duplication provides reserve, while singleness concentrates function; the two are not contradictory. This backup logic explains why a single kidney or a missing lung lobe can sometimes still suffice.[1][18]

左右不对称不是小细节。胚胎早期就建立前后、背腹和左右三个轴，Nodal、Pitx2 等通路把“左”和“右”写成不同的发育指令，心脏偏左、肝脏偏右、胃偏左都由此而来。极少数人内脏位置完全镜像（内脏反位），多数功能正常，但手术和就医时要提前说明。Left-right asymmetry is not a detail. Early in development the embryo establishes anterior-posterior, dorsal-ventral, and left-right axes. Nodal, Pitx2, and related pathways write different instructions for left and right, placing the heart left, the liver right, and the stomach left. In rare situs inversus the organs are mirrored; most functions remain normal, but clinicians should be told before surgery.[1][4][18]

为什么大多数人右利手？语言和精细运动中枢多在左半球，加上文化训练，形成约九成右利的分布；但手性不是单一基因决定，左撇子也不是“异常”。把左撇子强行改成右利手，历史上被证明会带来书写和协调困扰，现代教育更强调顺其自然。Why are most people right-handed? Language and fine-motor centres are often left-hemisphere dominant, and cultural training adds to that, producing a roughly ninety-percent right-handed distribution. Handedness is not decided by one gene, and left-handedness is not abnormal. Forcing left-handers to switch was historically linked to writing and coordination difficulties; modern education generally accepts the natural preference.[1][3]

人没有尾巴写在发育史里：胚胎第三到第五周会长出尾部芽，随后基因调控让尾部程序性死亡，退化成尾骨；黑猩猩胚胎同样有尾。尾骨不是“废骨”，它是盆底肌和部分韧带的附着点，也是坐姿时的骨性支点；尾骨骨折在跌倒时并不少见，疼起来别硬坐。尾巴的消失与直立行走、骨盆改造有关，但具体演化路径仍有争论。The absence of a tail is written into development. A tail bud appears in the third to fifth embryonic week, then genetic programs drive its regression into the coccyx; chimpanzee embryos also have a tail. The coccyx is not useless: pelvic-floor muscles and some ligaments attach to it, and it provides a bony support in sitting. Tail loss is linked to upright walking and pelvic remodelling, but the exact evolutionary path remains debated; coccyx fractures after falls are not rare and make sitting painful.[3][18]

“先爬后走”是常见顺序，不是硬性考勤。婴儿可能先腹爬、手膝爬、屁股挪，甚至直接扶站；只要其他运动里程碑在合理范围内，跳过爬行本身不等于发育障碍。若同时出现明显肌张力异常、双侧不对称、18 个月仍不能独走，才需要发育评估。边界：单肾约每千人一例，内脏反位更少见；尾骨痛、久坐痛要查原因，不要归因于“没尾巴”。内脏反位者阑尾炎的表现可能左右颠倒，容易延误诊断。Crawling before walking is a common sequence, not a compulsory checklist. Infants may belly-crawl, hand-and-knee crawl, scoot, or pull to stand directly. If other motor milestones are within range, skipping crawling alone does not mean a developmental disorder. Assessment is warranted if there is abnormal tone, marked asymmetry, or no independent walking by about eighteen months. Boundaries: single kidney occurs in roughly one in a thousand people, situs inversus is rarer, and coccyx pain needs a cause rather than a 'no tail' explanation. In situs inversus, appendicitis can present on the opposite side, which can delay diagnosis.[1][3]

11手指为什么不一样长？为什么是五根手指？手为什么是人体最敏感的部位？肚脐眼有用吗？Why are our fingers unequal? Why five? Why is the hand the most sensitive body part, and does the navel have a function?

指长受产前激素与功能分工塑造；五指源自五趾型附肢祖征；手因触觉小体和高皮层配额而最敏感；成人肚脐只剩脐带遗迹。Finger lengths are shaped by prenatal hormones and functional division; five digits inherit the five-toe ancestral limb; the hand wins sensitivity on receptor density and cortical quota

手指不一样长是功能与发育共同写下的方案：拇指短而能对掌负责抓握，中指最长平衡力臂，食指灵活支撑书写与触控。无名指（第四指）与食指（第二指）的比例（2D:4D）受胎儿期雄激素暴露影响，成年后与某些运动能力有统计相关——但相关不等于因果，不能拿来预测个人天赋。拇指的对掌让手能捏、握、拧，这是工具使用的关键。Finger length is a joint product of function and development: the thumb is short for opposition and grip, the middle finger is longest for leverage, and the index is mobile for writing and touch. The ratio of the fourth to second digit (2D:4D) is influenced by prenatal androgen exposure and shows statistical correlations with some athletic traits, but correlation is not causation and cannot predict individual talent.[3][4]

为什么偏偏是五根？哺乳动物的前肢继承自五趾型附肢的早期四足动物祖先；发育中一个沿前后轴的抑制梯度限制趾数，猴类在长期演化后仍维持五指。所以“五指”不是最优解，而是祖先蓝图加上对掌拇指改良后的历史产物——三指或九指的方案在演化史上并非不可想象，只是没有出现在我们的支系。多指畸形在人类中仍会出现，说明发育程序可以被扰动。Why five digits? The mammalian forelimb inherited the five-toed limb of early tetrapods; during development, a gradient along the anterior-posterior axis constrains digit number, and primates retained five. Five is therefore not an optimum but a historical product of the ancestral blueprint plus a refined opposable thumb. Three or nine digits are imaginable in evolution; they simply did not arise in our branch.[1][3]

手是全身最敏感的部位，有硬件和软件两重原因。硬件上，指尖的触觉小体（Meissner）、Merkel 盘和帕西尼小体密度冠绝全身，能分辨两毫米内的差异；软件上，大脑体感皮层给手的配额极不成比例——在著名的“皮层侏儒”图中，两只手几乎占了半条“身体”。工具使用又反过来训练这套系统。触觉还参与本体感觉，手被冻麻时动作会明显变笨。The hand is the body's most sensitive region for both hardware and software reasons. Mechanoreceptors such as Meissner corpuscles, Merkel discs, and Pacinian corpuscles are densely packed in the fingertips, supporting discrimination of a couple of millimetres. The somatosensory cortex allocates a wildly disproportionate share to the hand; in the classic cortical homunculus, the two hands occupy a large part of the body map. Tool use then trains the system further.[3][4]

肚脐在成人身上基本没有功能。它是胎儿期脐血管与尿管遗迹的疤痕组织，不再连接任何脏器；“有用”的现代残留只剩皮肤褶皱可能藏污，需要清洗。肚脐的褶皱里住着独特的微生物群落，但“排毒口”“吸收营养”的说法没有解剖基础。肚脐的形状因人而异，和脐带结扎方式、愈合过程有关。The adult navel has essentially no function. It is scar tissue left by the umbilical vessels and urachus, no longer connected to any organ; its modern residual claim to usefulness is a skin fold that can collect debris and needs cleaning. The fold hosts a distinctive microbial community, but 'detox port' and 'nutrient absorption' claims have no anatomical basis.[1][3]

边界：手指长度和激素暴露的统计关系很弱，不能据此判断运动或性格；手麻、无力、持续疼痛可能提示神经压迫或关节炎，应就医。肚脐出现分泌物、红肿、异味或疼痛，也不是“上火”，要排除感染；不要用尖锐物掏肚脐。手部保暖和适度活动能减少寒冷引起的僵硬。Boundaries: the statistical link between finger ratio and hormones is weak, so it cannot judge athletic ability or personality. Numbness, weakness, or persistent hand pain may indicate nerve compression or arthritis and deserves care. Discharge, redness, odour, or pain at the navel is not 'heatiness' but may be infection; do not probe it with sharp objects.[1][3]

洗澡的必要性来自皮肤生态：汗液、皮脂、脱落的角质和常驻菌群堆在一起，细菌分解汗液中的有机物产生短链脂肪酸和含硫化合物，形成体味；毛囊口被角质和油脂堵住，可能引发毛囊炎。皮肤表面并不是无菌的，它有一套稳定的微生物群落，清洗是“管理生态”，不是“消灭细菌”。Washing matters because of skin ecology: sweat, sebum, shed keratin, and resident microbes accumulate; bacteria ferment organic compounds in sweat into short-chain fatty acids and sulfur-containing molecules that cause odour, and blocked follicles can become folliculitis. The skin surface is not sterile; it hosts a stable microbial community. Cleaning is ecosystem management, not sterilization.[1][16]

频率因人而异。每天温和清洁、隔几天一次，甚至运动后只冲洗，都可能是正常的；关键看皮肤类型、出汗量、环境和使用什么清洁剂。过度用强皂、热水长时间搓洗，会破坏角质层脂质和酸性膜，让皮肤更干、更敏感。洗完趁湿涂保湿剂，比追求“搓出泥”更有意义。Frequency varies. Daily gentle cleansing, every few days, or simply rinsing after exercise can all be normal; skin type, sweat, environment, and the cleanser matter. Harsh soap, hot water, and long scrubbing damage the keratin-layer lipids and acid mantle, making skin drier and more reactive. Applying moisturiser while skin is damp is more useful than chasing visible 'dirt'.[1][3]

“饭后不能马上洗澡”有半个真实内核：热水让皮肤血管扩张，部分血流从内脏分流去散热，理论上会轻微影响消化；但健康人一次温水澡远不足以造成症状。真正要避免的是饭后立刻剧烈运动、酒后洗澡、长时间高温泡澡，以及老年人独自在浴室久待，因为体位性低血压和跌倒风险更实际。The advice not to bathe immediately after eating has a partial basis: hot water dilates skin vessels and diverts some blood flow from the gut to heat loss, which could mildly affect digestion. But a warm bath after a meal is far from enough to cause symptoms in a healthy person. The real risks are strenuous exercise right after eating, bathing after alcohol, long hot baths, and older people bathing alone, where orthostatic hypotension and falls matter more.[1][16]

传统人群痤疮较少见，不能用“他们洗得少”解释。痤疮的核心是雄激素驱动皮脂分泌、毛囊角化异常和 Cutibacterium acnes 定植，遗传、饮食（高血糖负荷、部分乳制品）和 IGF-1 通路都会影响。清洁能减少表面油脂，但不能根治痤疮；过度搓洗反而刺激炎症。Acne is less common in some traditional populations, but that is not because they wash less. Acne centres on androgen-driven sebum production, abnormal follicular keratinization, and Cutibacterium acnes colonization; genetics, high glycaemic-load diets, some dairy, and IGF-1 signalling all contribute. Cleaning reduces surface oil but does not cure acne, and scrubbing can worsen inflammation.[1][16]

婴儿指纹在妊娠 16–18 周左右定型：真皮乳头层向上顶起表皮，形成脊纹；只要真皮乳头结构不被深层瘢痕破坏，纹路会按原样生长。浅割伤愈合后指纹通常恢复，深部烧伤或某些疾病才可能改变。指纹识别依赖的正是这套“发育定型、后期稳定”的解剖结构，但“终身绝对不变”仍应留一点边界。边界：突然出现持续体味、腋下或腹股沟红斑脱屑，可能是真菌或细菌感染；痤疮成片化脓、留下瘢痕，应看皮肤科，不要只换洗浴用品。湿疹和玫瑰痤疮患者要减少热水、香精和强皂；抗菌皂并不比普通温和清洁更有效，长期使用还可能扰乱菌群。Infant fingerprints are laid down around sixteen to eighteen weeks of gestation: dermal papillae push up the epidermis and create ridges. As long as the dermal papilla is not destroyed by deep scarring, the pattern grows with the skin. Superficial cuts usually heal with the same pattern; deep burns or some diseases can change it. Fingerprint identification relies on this developmental fixation and later stability, though 'absolutely unchanging for life' still deserves a small caveat. Boundaries: sudden persistent body odour, red scaly patches in the axillae or groin, or folliculitis may indicate fungal or bacterial infection. Widespread pustular acne or scarring needs dermatology, not just a different body wash. Eczema and rosacea benefit from less hot water, fragrance, and harsh soap; antibacterial soap is not clearly better than gentle cleansing and long-term use can disturb the microbiome.[1][3]

13为什么有人打呼噜？人能憋气憋到什么时候？Why do some people snore, and how long can you hold your breath?

鼾=睡眠中咽壁软组织振动；憋气醒来的真正触发器是二氧化碳不是缺氧；极限值由训练、水温和个体决定。Snoring is vibration of throat soft tissue in sleep; the real trigger to breathe is rising carbon dioxide, not low oxygen; limits set by training, water temperature, and individuals.

打呼噜是上气道的物理声学：入睡后咽部肌肉张力下降，软腭、舌根和咽侧壁更容易塌陷，气流通过变窄的通道时让软组织周期性振动。肥胖、饮酒、镇静药、仰卧位和鼻中隔偏曲都会加重；如果气道反复完全或部分堵塞，就不只是“吵”，而是阻塞性睡眠呼吸暂停（OSA）。鼻腔、咽腔和喉腔任何一段变窄，都可能把呼吸变成哨音，打鼾的音量并不代表堵塞程度。Snoring is the acoustics of the upper airway. After sleep onset, pharyngeal muscle tone falls, the soft palate, tongue base, and pharyngeal walls collapse more easily, and airflow through a narrowed channel makes soft tissues vibrate. Obesity, alcohol, sedatives, supine posture, and septal deviation worsen it. If the airway repeatedly closes completely or partly, the problem is not just noise but obstructive sleep apnoea (OSA). Narrowing anywhere from nose to pharynx to larynx can turn breathing into a whistle; snore volume does not measure obstruction.[1][17]

OSA 的麻烦在于反复缺氧和微觉醒：夜间呼吸暂停几十秒，血氧下降，大脑短暂醒来，再入睡，周而复始。人可能不记得醒来，却出现白天嗜睡、晨起头痛、注意力下降和血压难控。诊断不能靠录音软件或床伴描述，标准方法是睡眠监测；持续气道正压（CPAP）是常见有效治疗，减重、避免酒精和镇静药也很关键。OSA matters because of repeated hypoxia and micro-arousals: breathing pauses for tens of seconds, oxygen falls, the brain briefly wakes, sleep resumes, and the cycle repeats. A person may not remember waking but has daytime sleepiness, morning headache, reduced attention, and difficult blood pressure. Diagnosis should not rely on a recording app or a bed partner's description; sleep monitoring is the standard, and continuous positive airway pressure (CPAP) is a common effective treatment, along with weight loss and avoiding alcohol and sedatives.[1][17]

憋气的极限常被误解为“大脑缺氧报警”。真正逼人呼吸的信号主要是二氧化碳分压升高，化学感受器对 CO₂ 比缺氧更敏感；自由潜水员通过过度换气先压低 CO₂，再加上哺乳动物潜水反射（心率下降、外周血管收缩），能把闭气时间推长。危险也在这里：低碳酸血症延迟了“该呼吸”的信号，缺氧到临界前可能没有痛苦感，水中突然失去意识，就是浅水昏迷。The breath-hold limit is often misunderstood as an oxygen alarm. The main drive to breathe is rising carbon dioxide, and chemoreceptors are more sensitive to CO2 than to hypoxia. Freedivers hyperventilate to lower CO2 first, then add the mammalian dive reflex—slower heart rate and peripheral vasoconstriction—to prolong breath-holding. The danger lies there: hypocapnia delays the signal to breathe, and hypoxia can arrive without distress; loss of consciousness in water is shallow-water blackout.[1][2]

健康人清醒安静时憋气通常几十秒到一两分钟，训练差异很大，不能拿憋气时长当健康指标。任何闭气训练都不应在无人监护的水中进行；过度换气后再下水，是拿生理机制赌命。若清醒憋气就出现发绀、心悸、头晕，或静息血氧偏低，应查心肺，而不是“练肺活量”。A healthy person at rest can usually hold the breath for tens of seconds to a couple of minutes, with large training variation; breath-hold duration is not a health metric. Breath-hold training should never be done in water without supervision; hyperventilating before a dive is gambling with physiology. Cyanosis, palpitations, dizziness, or low resting oxygen saturation during a breath hold calls for cardiopulmonary assessment, not 'lung training'.[1][2]

边界：响亮的鼾声本身不等于有病，但“鼾声+被目击的呼吸暂停+白天嗜睡”是去做睡眠监测的明确理由。儿童打鼾常与扁桃体、腺样体肥大有关，长期张口呼吸可能影响颌面发育，应看耳鼻喉科。打鼾不是“睡得香”，也不是靠止鼾贴就能解决；先分清单纯打鼾和 OSA，再谈治疗。Boundaries: loud snoring alone is not a disease, but snoring plus witnessed pauses plus daytime sleepiness is a clear reason for sleep testing. Snoring in children often involves enlarged tonsils or adenoids; chronic mouth breathing may affect facial growth and should be assessed by an ENT specialist. Snoring is not 'deep sleep', and anti-snoring patches are not a diagnosis; distinguish simple snoring from OSA first.[1][17]

药物不会“导航”，它们被血流送到全身，能否在目标处起作用，取决于浓度、亲和力和组织分布。止痛药结合疼痛通路上的受体，抗生素结合细菌特有的结构；所谓“知道去哪”，其实是“哪里结合得牢、哪里就留下”。副作用也来自同一逻辑：药物在非目标组织上结合了不该结合的受体；同一剂量在不同人身上的血药浓度差异很大。Drugs do not navigate. They are carried by blood throughout the body, and whether they act at a target depends on concentration, affinity, and tissue distribution. An analgesic binds receptors in pain pathways; an antibiotic binds structures specific to bacteria. 'Knowing where to go' really means 'binding tightly where it stays'. Side effects follow the same logic: the drug binds receptors in the wrong place.[1][2]

全身麻醉不是“睡一觉”，而是对中枢神经的可逆性抑制：静脉或吸入麻醉药增强抑制性受体（如 GABA-A）或抑制兴奋性受体（如 NMDA），让丘脑—皮层通讯被切断，意识、记忆和痛觉分级关闭。麻醉深度要同时看意识、镇痛和肌松，单靠“睡得沉不沉”判断不安全；麻醉药也会抑制呼吸和循环，所以术中监测不可少。General anaesthesia is not sleep. It is reversible suppression of the central nervous system: intravenous or inhaled agents enhance inhibitory receptors such as GABA-A or suppress excitatory ones such as NMDA, cutting thalamo-cortical communication and shutting down consciousness, memory, and pain. Depth is judged by consciousness, analgesia, and muscle relaxation together; 'sleeping deeply' is not a safe measure.[1][4]

术后几天注意力下降、记性变慢并不少见，尤其在高龄、大手术或原有认知下降的人；多数会逐步恢复，但持续认知下降需要评估，不能全归因于“麻醉伤了脑子”。麻醉风险主要来自心肺基础病、气道问题和血压波动，术前评估比“麻醉药毒性”更决定安全。Attention and memory may be off for days after surgery, especially in older adults, major surgery, or people with pre-existing cognitive decline. Most recover gradually, but persistent decline needs assessment and should not be blamed entirely on 'anaesthesia damaging the brain'. Anaesthetic risk is driven more by heart/lung disease, airway problems, and blood-pressure swings than by 'drug toxicity'; pre-operative assessment matters most.[1][2]

纹身的颜色留在真皮，是因为墨水颗粒太大，无法被淋巴直接引流；驻留的巨噬细胞把颗粒吞了，但无法消化，细胞死后颗粒被下一个巨噬细胞接住——“吞噬—死亡—再接住”的循环让墨色长期留在真皮；纹身墨水并非无菌，正规机构会控制污染和交叉感染。部分墨水成分可进入淋巴结，长期安全性仍在研究，不能简单说“身体完全没有反应”；红色等某些颜料更容易引起过敏或肉芽肿。Tattoo colour stays in the dermis because ink particles are too large for direct lymphatic drainage. Resident macrophages engulf them but cannot digest them; when a cell dies, the next macrophage picks up the particles. This engulf-die-re-engulf cycle keeps pigment in the dermis for a long time. Some ink components can reach lymph nodes, and long-term safety is still being studied; 'the body has no reaction at all' is too simple.[1][3]

边界：药物相互作用、肝肾功能和基因差异都会改变血药浓度，不要把“别人吃了有效”当成自己也能吃；麻醉前要如实说清药物、过敏和打鼾/呼吸暂停史。纹身可能感染、过敏、形成肉芽肿，或在极少数情况下干扰 MRI；激光是把颗粒打碎让淋巴带走，不是“把墨水变没”。选择正规机构、看清墨水成分，比事后补救更重要。Boundaries: drug interactions, liver/kidney function, and genetic variation all change blood levels, so 'it worked for someone else' is not a prescription. Before anaesthesia, disclose medicines, allergies, and snoring/sleep-apnoea history. Tattoos can become infected, allergic, form granulomas, or rarely interfere with MRI; laser breaks particles up for lymphatic clearance, it does not 'erase' the ink. A regulated studio and known ink composition matter more than after-the-fact rescue.[1][2]

器官再生的控制权在细胞身份的可塑性。蝾螈断肢后，伤口处的成熟细胞能“去分化”形成芽基，再长出一只完整的腿；哺乳动物大部分细胞被锁死在分化身份上，受损后倾向启动瘢痕修复而非再生。肝脏是例外：部分肝切除后，剩余肝叶会通过代偿性增生恢复接近原体积，但这是“增大”，不是重新长出被切掉的解剖分段；肝脏的代偿能力很强，不等于其他器官也能再生。Organ regeneration depends on cellular plasticity. The liver is an exception: after partial hepatectomy, the remaining lobes undergo compensatory hyperplasia and recover close to the original volume, but this is enlargement, not regrowth of the exact resected anatomical segments.[1][3]

“器官不能二次捐赠”要分器官和组织。常规实体器官移植通常只移植一次：器官已经经历供者年龄、缺血再灌注损伤和免疫排斥，再次移植的风险和排异负担很高；但在极少数情况下，存在多米诺肝移植或再次移植的安排，不能把“绝对不可能”当成生物学定律。角膜、骨、肌腱、皮肤和心脏瓣膜等组织经过处理，可以再次作为供体。'Organs cannot be donated twice' needs a split between organs and tissues. Routine solid-organ transplants are usually single: the organ has already endured donor age, ischaemia-reperfusion injury, and immune rejection, and re-transplantation carries high risk. In rare cases, domino liver transplantation or re-transplantation is arranged, so 'absolutely impossible' is not a biological law. Corneas, bone, tendon, skin, and heart valves can be processed and used again.[1][2]

只吃肉和蛋会出问题，不是“蛋白质不够”这么简单：缺乏膳食纤维使肠道菌群多样性下降、短链脂肪酸生成减少，结肠黏膜少了一层能量来源；维生素 C、叶酸和钾不足会带来坏血病样风险；饱和脂肪与血红素铁过量则与心血管风险相关。全蛋是优质蛋白来源，但“只吃肉蛋”是把饮食变成单腿站立。Eating only meat and eggs fails for more than 'not enough protein'. Lack of dietary fibre lowers gut microbial diversity and short-chain fatty acid production, depriving colonocytes of an energy source; insufficient vitamin C, folate, and potassium creates scurvy-like risks; excess saturated fat and haem iron is linked to cardiovascular risk. Whole eggs are a good protein source, but a meat-and-egg-only diet is standing on one leg.[1][2]

“全身细胞每隔几年换新”并不精确：肠上皮几天一换，红细胞约 120 天，皮肤数月，骨骼以十年计，而神经元和心肌细胞更新很慢。“我还是我”的答案不在原子的同一性：大脑靠模式、记忆和连续叙事维持自我，物质在流动，信息结构不断被复制。身份更像一首不断演奏的曲子，而不是一块不变的金子。'Every cell is replaced every few years' is imprecise: intestinal epithelium turns over in days, red blood cells in about 120 days, skin in months, bone over a decade, while neurons and cardiomyocytes renew very slowly. The answer to 'am I still me' is not atomic identity: the brain maintains the self through patterns, memory, and a continuous narrative; matter flows while information structure is copied. Identity is more like a piece being played than a fixed ingot.[1][2]

边界：再生医学能修复部分组织，但不能让成年人重新长出一条肢体；器官移植的瓶颈是供体、排斥和感染，不是“技术不够先进”。只吃肉蛋不是“原始饮食”的通行证，肾病患者、孕妇和儿童尤其不能照搬。细胞更新率也不等于“旧细胞一定有害”，更不意味着换得越快越健康。Boundaries: regenerative medicine can repair some tissues but cannot make an adult regrow a limb; transplantation is limited by donors, rejection, and infection, not by 'not enough technology'. A meat-and-egg diet is not a passport to 'ancestral health'; people with kidney disease, pregnant women, and children should not copy it. Cell turnover does not mean old cells are harmful, and faster replacement is not healthier.[1][3]

16运动后第二天肌肉又酸又疼是怎么回事？Why are your muscles sore and stiff the day after a hard workout?

那是延迟性肌肉酸痛（DOMS）：肌纤维与结缔组织微损伤引发的炎症修复反应，与“乳酸堆积”无关。That is delayed-onset muscle soreness (DOMS): an inflammatory repair response to micro-tears in fibres and connective tissue — nothing to do with lactic acid.

第二天的酸痛叫延迟性肌肉酸痛（DOMS）：多在运动后 12–24 小时出现，24–72 小时最重，之后逐步缓解。它的成因不是乳酸——乳酸在运动后一小时内就基本被代谢清除。真正的触发器是不习惯的离心收缩，比如下坡跑、慢放下哑铃，让肌纤维和结缔组织承受“拉长同时发力”的负荷；下坡比上坡更容易酸痛，原因就在这里。The ache of the next day is delayed-onset muscle soreness (DOMS): it usually appears 12–24 hours after exercise, peaks at 24–72 hours, and then resolves. The cause is not lactate; lactate is largely cleared within an hour after exercise. The trigger is unaccustomed eccentric contraction, such as downhill running or slowly lowering a dumbbell, which loads muscle fibres and connective tissue while they lengthen; downhill running is more soreness-provoking than uphill for this reason.[1][15]

身体随即启动修复：受损处释放钾离子、酶类和炎症介质刺激神经末梢，免疫细胞进入清除碎片，卫星细胞（肌肉干细胞）被激活、增殖并与既有纤维融合，补充收缩蛋白。这个过程让肌肉在“修理—适应”中变强，但不是越痛越好；过大的负荷会超出修复能力，变成拉伤或横纹肌溶解；睡眠不足和突然加量会放大损伤。Repair follows: damaged areas release potassium, enzymes, and inflammatory mediators that stimulate nerve endings; immune cells clear debris; satellite cells (muscle stem cells) activate, proliferate, and fuse with existing fibres to restore contractile protein. This 'repair and adapt' process can make muscle stronger, but more pain is not better; excessive load exceeds repair capacity and becomes a strain or rhabdomyolysis. Sleep loss and sudden increases in volume amplify the damage.[1][15]

酸痛程度与训练效果关系很弱。规律训练几周后，同样强度几乎不再酸痛，这叫“重复效应”，但力量和有氧能力仍在提高。缓解手段里，低强度活动（散步、轻松骑行）靠血流促进恢复；按摩、泡沫轴、冷热敷主观有效，但证据有限；拉伸对预防 DOMS 几乎无作用；循序渐进地增加离心训练，能让重复效应提前出现。Soreness has little to do with training effect. After a few weeks of regular training, the same intensity causes little soreness—the repeated-bout effect—while strength and aerobic capacity still improve. Among relief measures, low-intensity activity such as walking or easy cycling promotes blood flow; massage, foam rolling, and hot/cold packs feel good but have limited evidence; stretching does almost nothing to prevent DOMS. Progressive eccentric training brings on the repeated-bout effect sooner.[1][15]

边界信号要分清：DOMS 是双侧、弥漫、按压酸胀，活动后逐渐缓解；锐痛、单侧痛、肿胀淤青、关节活动受限更像拉伤或撕裂。若出现酱油色/可乐色尿、明显无力、肿胀和恶心，要警惕横纹肌溶解，必须就医，不能当成“练到位”。Boundaries: DOMS is bilateral, diffuse, tender, and eases with movement; sharp pain, one-sided pain, swelling, bruising, or restricted joint motion suggests a strain or tear. Dark brown urine, marked weakness, swelling, and nausea may indicate rhabdomyolysis and require urgent care, not a 'good workout' label.[1][15]

边界：循序渐进增加负荷、保证睡眠和蛋白质摄入，比追求“每次练到走不动”更可靠。补水不能预防所有酸痛，但脱水会加重不适；止痛药能压住疼痛，不等于组织修好。没有酸痛也不代表没效果，持续疼痛或反复同一部位受伤，应找教练或医生评估动作和负荷；疼痛超过一周仍不缓解，要排查应力性损伤，不要硬练；恢复也是训练的一部分，计划里要留出恢复窗口。Boundaries: progressive loading, sleep, and adequate protein are more reliable than chasing the inability to walk after every session. Hydration does not prevent all soreness, but dehydration worsens it; painkillers can mask pain without repairing tissue. No soreness does not mean no effect. Persistent pain or repeated injury in the same area should prompt a coach or clinician to review technique and load; pain lasting more than a week needs a stress-injury check, not more training. Recovery is part of training, so the plan needs recovery windows.[1][3]

17智齿为什么会长横？阑尾为什么会发炎？Why do wisdom teeth come in sideways, and why does the appendix get inflamed?

颌骨缩短使第三磨牙空间不足而阻生；阑尾是富含淋巴组织的免疫器官，腔道梗阻后细菌入侵引发炎症。A shortened jaw leaves the third molar impacted; the appendix is a lymphoid immune organ, and luminal obstruction lets bacteria invade and inflame it.

智齿就是第三磨牙，通常在 17–25 岁之间萌出，因为萌出时已接近成年、智力发育趋于成熟，民间俗称智齿。现代人的颌骨相比狩猎采集阶段有所缩短，多项体质测量研究支持颌骨尺寸随饮食变软而下降的趋势，具体幅度随样本和测量部位不同；牙量没有同步减少，最后萌出的第三磨牙空间不足，于是出现阻生、前倾，甚至水平横长，顶推前面的第二磨牙。在颌骨发育充分、牙列宽松的个体中，智齿也可能正常萌出并参与研磨，并非每个人都必须拔除。Wisdom teeth are the third molars, usually erupting between ages 17 and 25; the common name reflects that they appear near adulthood, when mental development has largely matured. Modern human jaws are shorter than those of hunter-gatherer populations, and several anthropometric studies support a downward trend in jaw size as diet became softer, though the magnitude varies by sample and measurement site. Tooth size did not shrink in step, so the last molars run out of space and become impacted, tilted, or even horizontally impacted, pushing against the second molar in front.[2][18]

阻生智齿不一定立刻疼痛，但部分萌出时牙龈瓣覆盖牙面，食物残渣和细菌藏在瓣下，容易引起冠周炎，表现为肿痛、张口受限、吞咽不适；清洁困难也会增加邻牙龋坏和牙周炎症的风险。是否需要拔除，要结合牙片上的位置、萌出方向和反复发炎史由口腔科判断。An impacted wisdom tooth does not always hurt at once. When it partially erupts, a gum flap covers the tooth surface, trapping food debris and bacteria underneath, which can trigger pericoronitis with swelling, pain, limited mouth opening and discomfort on swallowing. Poor cleaning also raises the risk of caries and periodontal inflammation in the neighboring molar. Whether extraction is needed depends on position, eruption angle and repeated inflammation seen on dental imaging, judged by a dentist.[2][3]

阑尾是盲肠下端伸出的细长盲管，成人长度约 5–10 cm。它长期被当作退化残留器官，现代解剖与发育生物学的看法已经修正：阑尾壁富含淋巴组织，参与肠道免疫应答，也被认为是肠道菌群的一个储存处，在腹泻等菌群紊乱后帮助菌群恢复。它不是无用的痕迹，只是在人体中的功能权重不如肝、肠等主要脏器。The appendix is a narrow blind tube extending from the cecum, about 5 to 10 cm long in adults. It was long regarded as a vestigial leftover, but modern anatomy and developmental biology have revised that view: its wall is rich in lymphoid tissue, contributes to gut immune responses, and is thought to serve as a reservoir for gut microbiota, helping the flora recover after diarrheal disturbance. It is not a useless trace, though its functional weight is smaller than that of major organs such as the liver or intestine.[3][18]

阑尾炎的直接机制是管腔梗阻。阑尾腔窄、开口小，粪石、增生的淋巴滤泡或食物残渣都可能堵住开口；梗阻后分泌物继续积聚，腔内压力升高，血供受压，细菌繁殖并侵入壁层，引发炎症。典型表现起初是上腹或脐周隐痛，数小时后转移并固定到右下腹，可伴压痛、发热和恶心；位置异常的阑尾疼痛部位可能不典型。Appendicitis starts with luminal obstruction. The appendix has a narrow cavity and small opening; a fecalith, enlarged lymphoid follicle or food residue can block it. Secretions keep accumulating behind the blockage, pressure rises, blood supply is compressed, and bacteria multiply and invade the wall, producing inflammation. The classic pattern begins with dull pain in the upper abdomen or around the navel, which after hours shifts and settles in the right lower quadrant, with tenderness, fever and nausea; an abnormally positioned appendix may cause atypical pain.[2][3]

边界：反复肿痛、萌出位置不正的阻生智齿由口腔科评估处理；持续右下腹疼痛、发热、呕吐或疼痛加重需要急诊外科判断，不能按上火或消化不良自行观察。本章讲演化背景、解剖结构与发炎机制，不展开拔牙手术指征、切口方式或阑尾炎的具体治疗方案。Boundary: repeatedly swollen or malpositioned impacted wisdom teeth are assessed by a dentist; persistent right lower abdominal pain, fever, vomiting or worsening pain needs emergency surgical evaluation and should not be watched at home as heatiness or indigestion. This chapter covers evolutionary background, anatomy and inflammation mechanisms, not extraction indications, surgical approach or appendicitis treatment details.[3]

18为什么人老了会变矮？Why do people get shorter as they age?

椎间盘失水变薄、骨量丢失致椎体压缩、背部肌肉减弱姿势前倾，三者叠加使中老年身高下降。Discs lose water and thin, bone loss compresses vertebrae, and weak back muscles tilt posture; together they lower height in later life.

成年以后身高会随年龄缓慢下降，多在中老年阶段逐渐显现，缩短主要来自脊柱和椎间盘，而不是腿骨本身变短。椎间盘是相邻椎体之间的纤维软骨垫，中央髓核富含水分，外围纤维环把它束缚在原位；它承担缓冲震荡、允许脊柱屈伸和侧屈的作用，年轻时含水量高、弹性好，是脊柱长度的重要组成部分。脊柱由 20 多节椎体叠成，椎间盘在腰段占的长度比例不小，所以椎间盘厚度变化对总身高的影响很直接。Adult height declines slowly with age, mostly becoming visible in middle and older age. The shortening comes mainly from the spine and intervertebral discs, not from shortening of the leg bones. A disc is a fibrocartilage pad between adjacent vertebrae: the central nucleus pulposus is rich in water, and the outer annulus fibrosus holds it in place. Discs cushion impact and allow flexion, extension and lateral bending; when young, they hold much water and elasticity, and contribute substantially to spinal length.[2][3]

椎间盘退变是变矮的第一块基石。随年龄增长，髓核含水量下降，椎间盘厚度降低、弹性减弱，缓冲能力变差。椎间盘本身存在昼夜波动：白天站立、负重和活动把椎间盘压缩，身高可减少约 1–2 cm，夜间平卧、解除负重后吸水回弹；长期退变使这种压缩不能完全恢复，节段高度逐年累积丢失。Disc degeneration is the first building block of height loss. With age the nucleus loses water content, disc thickness drops, elasticity weakens and cushioning worsens. Discs also show a daily cycle: during daytime standing, loading and activity the disc is compressed and height can fall by about 1 to 2 cm, while lying flat at night removes the load and lets the disc reabsorb water. Long-term degeneration prevents full rebound, so segmental height accumulates loss year after year.[1][2]

骨密度变化是第二条线。绝经后女性和老年男性都会出现骨量丢失，椎体骨小梁变细、数量减少，骨骼在日常负荷下可能出现微小压缩变形；多个椎体轻度压缩叠加起来，脊柱长度缩短，身高明显下降。骨质疏松相关的椎体压缩往往伴随背痛，也可能在没有明显外伤的情况下发生。女性绝经后雌激素水平下降，骨量丢失速度常快于同龄男性，这也是老年女性身高下降更明显的常见背景。Bone density is the second line. Postmenopausal women and older men both lose bone mass; vertebral trabeculae become thinner and fewer, and vertebrae may develop minor compression deformities under daily loading. Several mildly compressed vertebrae add up to a shorter spine and measurable height loss. Osteoporosis-related vertebral compression often accompanies back pain and can occur without obvious trauma.[1][3]

姿势和肌肉进一步改变外形。背部伸肌力量下降后，胸廓容易前倾、圆背加重，脊柱胸段曲度变大，人在站立位上显得更矮；这种姿势性缩短和椎间盘、骨骼的变化叠加在一起，不是某一种组织单独萎缩。体重、肌肉量和日常活动习惯也会影响老年阶段的体态。Posture and muscle reshape the outline further. As back extensor strength declines, the chest tends to lean forward, round back worsens and the thoracic spine curves more, making the standing posture look shorter. This postural shortening stacks on top of disc and bone changes; it is not one tissue shrinking alone. Body weight, muscle mass and daily activity habits also shape later-life posture.[1][3]

边界：短期内身高明显下降、持续背痛或身高骤降，需要排查骨质疏松和椎体压缩；本章讲机制与量级，不展开骨密度检查流程、诊断标准或补钙、抗骨质疏松方案。日常可维持适度负重活动和蛋白质、钙、维生素 D 摄入，但具体措施应结合体检与医生建议。Boundary: a rapid drop in height, persistent back pain or sudden height loss warrants evaluation for osteoporosis and vertebral compression. This chapter covers mechanisms and magnitudes, not bone-density testing, diagnostic criteria or calcium and anti-osteoporosis regimens. Moderate weight-bearing activity and adequate protein, calcium and vitamin D are reasonable supports, but specific measures should follow checkups and clinician advice.[2]

19为什么人老了会长皱纹？Why do people get wrinkles as they age?

真皮胶原与弹性纤维退变、表情反复收缩、日晒光老化及脂肪垫移位，共同形成皱纹与松弛。Dermal collagen and elastic-fiber degeneration, repeated expression movement, photoaging and fat-pad shift together produce wrinkles and looseness.

皱纹是皮肤老化在表皮、真皮和皮下组织层面的可见结果。面部皮肤由表层表皮、厚层真皮和皮下脂肪构成，真皮里的胶原纤维提供张力和支撑，弹性纤维负责回弹，皮下脂肪垫填充轮廓；三层共同维持皮肤的平整与饱满，任何一层退变都会反映为纹路或松弛。皮肤还分布血管、神经和毛囊皮脂腺等附属结构，老化时这些结构的功能也会同步减退。Wrinkles are the visible result of skin aging across the epidermis, dermis and subcutaneous tissue. Facial skin consists of a surface epidermis, a thick dermis and subcutaneous fat. Collagen fibers in the dermis provide tension and support, elastic fibers provide recoil, and fat pads fill the contour; the three layers together keep skin smooth and full, and degeneration in any layer shows as lines or looseness.[2][3]

真皮退变是皱纹的主要结构基础。随年龄增长，成纤维细胞活性下降，胶原合成减少、原有胶原交联增多，弹性纤维变性、断裂，真皮厚度和含水量下降；皮肤支撑力减弱，被反复折叠后回弹不到原来的位置，先出现细浅的干纹，再逐渐加深为静态皱纹。成纤维细胞是合成胶原和弹性纤维的主要细胞，其活性随年龄、日照和局部微循环状态变化；交联增多让胶原束更僵硬，柔韧性下降。Dermal degeneration is the main structural basis of wrinkles. With age, fibroblast activity declines, collagen synthesis drops and existing collagen cross-links increase; elastic fibers degenerate and break, while dermal thickness and water content fall. Support weakens, so after repeated folding the skin cannot return to its original position: fine dry lines appear first and gradually deepen into static wrinkles.[1][2]

表情运动在纹路形成中起叠加作用。额肌、眼轮匝肌和口周肌肉反复收缩，在额部、眼角和口周形成动态表情纹；长期重复使皮肤在肌肉放松后也部分保留折痕。日晒造成的光老化是最重要的外部累积因素，紫外线加速真皮弹力纤维变性和胶原破坏，使面部皱纹出现更早、更深。Facial movement adds on top. Frontalis, orbicularis oculi and perioral muscles contract repeatedly, forming dynamic expression lines on the forehead, around the eyes and around the mouth; long repetition leaves some crease even when the muscle relaxes. Sunlight-driven photoaging is the most important external cumulative factor: ultraviolet light accelerates dermal elastic-fiber degeneration and collagen damage, making facial wrinkles appear earlier and deeper.[1][3]

表皮和皮下的变化继续加深纹路。表皮更新变慢、变薄，屏障保水能力下降，皮肤显得干燥粗糙；皮下脂肪垫逐渐移位、萎缩，对皮肤的填充支撑减少，法令纹、嘴角纹和面部松弛随之明显。面部不同部位脂肪垫的萎缩速度不一致，有的先下垂、有的先变薄，轮廓改变常不对称，纹路位置也因人而异。遗传、性别、吸烟、日照年限和皮肤基础类型，都会影响皱纹出现的早晚和深浅。Epidermal and subcutaneous changes deepen the lines further. Epidermal renewal slows and thins, barrier water retention drops, and skin looks dry and rough; fat pads gradually shift and atrophy, reducing contour support, so nasolabial folds, mouth-corner lines and facial looseness become noticeable. Genetics, sex, smoking, cumulative sun exposure and basic skin type all affect when wrinkles appear and how deep they go.[2][3]

边界：本章讲自然老化与光老化的机制，不评测护肤品成分、注射填充、激光或拉皮手术；短期内异常松弛、皮疹、瘙痒或不明原因的皮肤改变，需要皮肤科评估。皱纹是多组织长期变化的结果，单一保养手段只能影响其中一部分因素，不存在逆转全部老化的办法。Boundary: this chapter explains natural aging and photoaging mechanisms, not skincare ingredients, injectable fillers, laser treatments or facelift surgery. Abnormal looseness, rash, itching or unexplained skin change over a short period needs dermatological evaluation. Wrinkles result from long-term changes across several tissues; a single care product can affect only part of the factors, and no method reverses all aging.[1]

20为什么蛋白质很重要？Why are proteins important?

蛋白质来自希腊语 proteios（首要），由氨基酸折叠成链；9 种必需氨基酸须由食物提供，承担多种功能。Protein comes from Greek proteios (first in importance), folded from amino acids; nine essential amino acids must come from food.

蛋白质这个名称来自希腊语 proteios，意思是第一位的、首要的；19 世纪科学家用它命名这类被认为居于生命首位的物质。蛋白质是细胞内含量最丰富的有机物之一，承担结构、催化、运输、免疫和信号传递等多种功能，理解它要从分子结构入手，而不是把它当成笼统的营养品标签。The word protein comes from the Greek proteios, meaning first or of primary importance; in the 19th century scientists named this class after the substance regarded as foremost in life. Proteins are among the most abundant organic molecules in cells and carry out structural, catalytic, transport, immune and signaling roles. Understanding them starts from molecular structure, not from a vague supplement label.[2][5]

蛋白质由氨基酸连接而成。一个氨基酸的氨基与另一个氨基酸的羧基脱水缩合，形成肽键，多个氨基酸连成多肽链；链再盘绕、折叠成特定的空间构象，构象决定它能结合什么、执行什么功能。人体使用约 20 种标准氨基酸，排列顺序不同，折叠出的蛋白质就不同；一条链可以包含数十到数百个氨基酸残基。氨基酸的侧链各不相同，决定折叠后表面的电荷、形状和亲疏水性，这是酶识别底物、抗体结合抗原的结构基础。Proteins are built from amino acids. The amino group of one amino acid and the carboxyl group of another lose water and form a peptide bond; many amino acids link into a polypeptide chain, which then coils and folds into a specific three-dimensional shape. That conformation decides what the protein can bind and what function it performs. The body uses about 20 standard amino acids: different sequences fold into different proteins, and one chain may hold tens to hundreds of residues.[1][2]

这些氨基酸并非都能由身体自行合成。其中 9 种称为必需氨基酸，人体不能合成或合成速度不足以满足需要，必须由食物提供；其余氨基酸可由糖、脂肪代谢的中间产物转化而来。食物蛋白质按氨基酸组成被分为完全蛋白和不完全蛋白，不同来源的植物蛋白搭配食用，可以在氨基酸谱上互相补充。Not all these amino acids can be made by the body. Nine are called essential amino acids: the body cannot synthesize them, or cannot make them fast enough, so they must come from food. The rest can be derived from intermediates of sugar and fat metabolism. Food proteins are grouped by amino-acid profile into complete and incomplete proteins, and combining plant proteins from different sources can complement each other's amino acid patterns.[2][5]

蛋白质在体内处于不断合成与降解的动态周转。酶是蛋白质，催化几乎所有代谢反应；胶原蛋白和角蛋白构成结缔组织、皮肤和毛发；抗体参与免疫防御，血红蛋白运输氧气，膜上的受体和通道传递信号、控制物质进出。膳食提供的氨基酸是合成这些新蛋白的原料，旧蛋白降解后的氨基酸也有一部分被回收再利用。Proteins are in constant turnover, synthesized and degraded all the time. Enzymes are proteins that catalyze nearly all metabolic reactions; collagen and keratin form connective tissue, skin and hair; antibodies defend immunity, hemoglobin carries oxygen, and receptors and channels in membranes relay signals and control what enters and leaves cells. Dietary amino acids are the raw material for new proteins, and some amino acids released when old proteins break down are recycled.[1][5]

边界：本章讲词源、分子结构和生理功能，不做营养推荐或食谱搭配；蛋白质需要量随年龄、体重、活动量和健康状况变化，是否需要补充以及补充多少，应由专业评估决定。把蛋白质理解为一条由氨基酸折叠而成、承担多种工作的分子链，比记住某一种补品名称更接近它的本来含义。Boundary: this chapter covers etymology, molecular structure and physiological function, not nutritional advice or meal planning. Protein needs vary with age, body weight, activity level and health status; whether supplementation is needed and how much should be decided by professional assessment. Understanding protein as a folded chain of amino acids doing many jobs is closer to its real meaning than memorizing the name of any supplement.[2]

21为什么喝酒会脸红？Why does alcohol make the face flush?

ALDH2*2 变异使乙醛分解变慢，乙醛蓄积扩张血管；东亚约 30%–40% 携带，脸红不是酒量。The ALDH2*2 variant slows acetaldehyde breakdown; accumulated acetaldehyde dilates vessels. About 30–40% of East Asians carry it, and flush is not tolerance.

酒精进入人体后要分两步代谢：乙醇经胃肠吸收进入血液，先在细胞质由乙醇脱氢酶（ADH）氧化成乙醛，再由线粒体里的乙醛脱氢酶 2（ALDH2）把乙醛分解成乙酸，乙酸最终进入三羧酸循环，被氧化成二氧化碳和水。脸红的关键在第二步：一部分人携带 ALDH2*2 变异，酶活性很低，乙醛来不及分解就蓄积在血液里，皮肤小血管被扩张，面、颈和耳郭先出现潮红。Alcohol is metabolized in two steps. Ethanol is absorbed from the gut into blood, first oxidized in the cytosol by alcohol dehydrogenase (ADH) into acetaldehyde; then mitochondrial aldehyde dehydrogenase 2 (ALDH2) breaks acetaldehyde down into acetate, which enters the Krebs cycle for energy. Flushing comes from the second step: some people carry the ALDH2*2 variant with very low enzyme activity, so acetaldehyde accumulates instead of being cleared, dilating small skin vessels and producing redness first on the face, neck and ears.[1][20]

乙醛不是无害中间产物。它会刺激血管扩张，并与组胺等介质共同参与心率加快、皮肤发热的反应；同样喝一杯酒，酶活性低的人蓄积更多，脸红、心悸、头胀也更明显，有时连上身皮肤都会发烫。这种反应在东亚人群中比例较高：约 30%–40% 的中国人、日本人、韩国人携带至少一个失活等位基因，欧洲和非洲人群中则很少见。Acetaldehyde is not a harmless intermediate. It dilates blood vessels and, together with histamine and related mediators, contributes to faster heart rate and a hot skin sensation. With the same drink, low-activity carriers accumulate more, so flushing, palpitations and head fullness are more marked, and sometimes the upper body feels hot as well. The reaction is common in East Asian populations: about 30 to 40 percent of Chinese, Japanese and Korean people carry at least one inactive allele, while it is rare in European and African populations.[5][20]

差异来自一个核苷酸位点。ALDH2*2 是第 12 外显子上的单碱基替换，改变了酶二聚界面上的氨基酸，使四聚体活性下降；杂合子通常只保留约 10%–45% 的酶活性，纯合子只有约 1%–5%。脸红是酶活性不足的外在表型，并不是“练出来的酒量”；反复饮酒只能让人对不适变得习惯，并不能把失活的酶补回来，也不会提高分解乙醛的能力。The difference comes from a single nucleotide site. ALDH2*2 is a base substitution in exon 12 that changes an amino acid at the enzyme dimer interface and lowers tetramer activity. Heterozygotes usually retain about 10 to 45 percent of enzyme activity, and homozygotes only about 1 to 5 percent. Flushing is an outward sign of low enzyme activity, not a tolerance that can be trained; repeated drinking can only make the discomfort feel familiar, not restore the inactive enzyme or raise acetaldehyde clearance.[1][20]

蓄积的乙醛还会伴随其他表现：心跳加快、搏动性头痛、恶心、胃部不适，少数人出现皮疹样潮红。长期饮酒时，乙醛暴露与上消化道肿瘤风险升高相关；携带变异的人往往饮酒量更小，部分原因正是这种即时不适形成了回避，但脸红并不等于身体自带保护；面、耳郭和上身潮红的范围可以粗略反映乙醛蓄积程度，却不能用来判断酒量。Accumulated acetaldehyde also accompanies other symptoms: fast heart rate, throbbing headache, nausea and stomach discomfort, and in some people a rash-like flush. With long-term drinking, acetaldehyde exposure is associated with a higher risk of upper digestive tract cancers; carriers often drink less partly because the immediate discomfort leads them to avoid alcohol, but flushing does not mean the body has built-in protection.[5][20]

边界：喝酒脸红不等同于酒精过敏，也不能单凭脸红判断是否适合饮酒；若出现喉头紧、喘憋、全身风团等急性过敏表现，需要紧急就医。本章只讲酒精代谢与潮红的机制，不提供戒酒方案、用药或肝病诊疗建议。Boundaries: facial flush after alcohol is not the same as alcohol allergy, and flushing alone cannot decide whether someone can drink safely. Tight throat, breathlessness or generalized hives are acute allergy signs needing urgent care. This chapter covers alcohol metabolism and flushing only; it does not give abstinence plans, medication advice or liver-disease treatment.[1]

22为什么人会疲劳？Why do people get tired?

疲劳分外周与中枢两条线：肌肉供能下降、代谢产物积累，神经驱动与主观精力同步减弱；休息后多可逆。Fatigue has peripheral and central routes: muscle energy falls and metabolites accumulate, while neural drive and subjective energy weaken; most acute fatigue is reversible with rest.

疲劳是身体持续或重复活动后，做功能力下降、主观精力减退的状态，它不是某一种“毒素堆满了肌肉”。日常困倦、运动后乏力和疾病带来的疲惫可以互相重叠，但机制并不完全相同；研究通常把疲劳分成两条线：外周疲劳发生在肌肉及其神经肌肉接头，中枢疲劳发生在脑和脊髓的驱动环节，两者可以先后出现，也可以同时叠加。长时间清醒、反复决策带来的主观困倦，与肌肉出力下降并不是同一条通路。Fatigue is the decline in work capacity and subjective energy after sustained or repeated activity; it is not one single toxin piled up in muscle. Ordinary tiredness after a long day, post-exercise weakness and illness-related exhaustion overlap, but their mechanisms are not identical. Research usually divides fatigue into two routes: peripheral fatigue at the muscle and its neuromuscular junction, and central fatigue at the drive side of brain and spinal cord. The two can appear in sequence or overlap.[1][21]

外周疲劳的核心是供能与代谢。收缩靠 ATP，短时间由磷酸肌酸缓冲，持续运动再靠糖原和血糖；长时间或高强度活动后，ATP、磷酸肌酸和糖原储备下降；磷酸肌酸在几秒到十几秒内补足 ATP，糖原储备决定中等以上强度活动能维持多久；无机磷酸、氢离子、氨等代谢产物积累，钠钾与钙离子梯度受扰，钙释放和回收变慢，肌球蛋白横桥循环减速，肌肉出力和放松都变得吃力。Peripheral fatigue turns on energy supply and metabolism. Contraction relies on ATP, buffered briefly by phosphocreatine, then by glycogen and blood glucose during sustained effort. After long or intense activity, ATP, phosphocreatine and glycogen stores fall; inorganic phosphate, hydrogen ions and ammonia accumulate; sodium, potassium and calcium gradients are disturbed; calcium release and reuptake slow; and myosin cross-bridge cycling slows, so both force and relaxation become labored.[1][21]

中枢疲劳表现为神经驱动下降：运动皮层和运动神经元发放频率、同步化程度降低，随意发力的意愿和肌肉募集一起减弱。主观感受还受睡眠、情绪、动机和环境影响；脑内把糖原、心率等信号当作“该放慢”的提醒，参与控制节奏和配速，而不是等肌肉完全衰竭才停。主观用力程度上升、动作变形，常常先于力量明显下降出现。Central fatigue shows up as reduced neural drive: motor cortex and motor neuron firing frequency and synchronization fall, and voluntary intent and muscle recruitment weaken together. The subjective feeling is also shaped by sleep, mood, motivation and environment. The brain uses signals such as glycogen and heart rate as reminders to slow down, pacing the body before muscle failure rather than waiting for exhaustion.[5][21]

疲劳是否快速恢复，取决于储备回补和休息：碳水补充帮助糖原恢复，水分和电解质纠正离子平衡，睡眠让神经和肌肉完成修复、补充蛋白结构。普通急性疲劳在进食、补水和睡眠后多可逆；但恢复速度受训练水平、营养和基础健康状态影响，个体差异不小。How fast fatigue recovers depends on replenishment and rest: carbohydrates restore glycogen, fluids and electrolytes correct ion balance, and sleep repairs nerve and muscle and replaces protein structure. Ordinary acute fatigue is mostly reversible after food, hydration and sleep, though recovery speed varies with training, nutrition and baseline health.[1][5]

边界：本章解释急性疲劳的生理机制，不制定训练处方，也不推荐补剂或药物。若疲劳持续数周不缓解、伴随胸闷、黑朦、明显体重下降、情绪低落或运动能力无故下降，应就医评估；把持续疲劳当作“练得不够”硬扛，可能漏掉疾病因素。Boundaries: this chapter explains the physiology of acute fatigue and gives no training prescription, supplement or medication advice. Fatigue lasting weeks, with chest tightness, blurred vision, marked weight loss, low mood or unexplained drop in performance, warrants medical assessment; treating persistent fatigue as not training enough can miss a disease factor.[1]
