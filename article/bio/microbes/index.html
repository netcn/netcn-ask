01细菌、古菌、病毒、真菌和朊病毒在结构与大小上有什么差别？How do bacteria, archaea, viruses, fungi and prions differ in structure and size?

原核细胞、真核细胞、病毒颗粒与蛋白分子分属不同结构层次，尺寸跨越微米到纳米。Prokaryotes, eukaryotes, virions and protein molecules sit at different structural levels, from micrometres to nanometres.

微生物是一个按大小和生存方式组织的集合，并不是单一分类群。细菌和古菌是原核生物，细胞没有核膜包被的细胞核；真菌是真核生物，细胞有核膜和细胞器；病毒不具备完整细胞结构，只能在宿主细胞内复制；类病毒、拟病毒和朊病毒更小，分别对应裸露核酸、核酸片段和错误折叠蛋白。OpenStax 微生物学把这些类型按结构和复制方式分列在微生物类型一节。Microorganisms are grouped by size and way of life, not as a single taxonomic group. Bacteria and archaea are prokaryotes with no nucleus enclosed by a nuclear membrane; fungi are eukaryotes with nuclei and organelles; viruses lack a complete cell structure and replicate only inside host cells; viroids, virusoids and prions are smaller still, corresponding to naked nucleic acid, a nucleic-acid fragment and a misfolded protein. OpenStax Microbiology lists these types by structure and replication in its section on microbial types.[1]

尺寸差距决定观察手段。多数细菌细胞直径约 1 µm 上下，需要光学显微镜油镜观察；病毒颗粒通常在 20–300 nm 范围，必须借助电子显微镜成像；朊病毒是单个错误折叠的蛋白质分子，不携带核酸基因组。数量级差别意味着过滤除菌、抗菌靶点和感染途径都要按对象选择，不能用同一套方法处理细菌、病毒和蛋白颗粒。Size determines observation method. Most bacterial cells are around 1 µm across and need an oil-immersion light microscope; virions usually fall in the 20–300 nm range and require electron microscopy; a prion is a single misfolded protein molecule with no nucleic-acid genome. The order-of-magnitude difference means filtration, antibacterial targets and infection routes must be chosen by agent, and one method cannot handle bacteria, viruses and protein particles alike.[1][2]

细菌细胞具细胞壁（多数含肽聚糖）、细胞膜、细胞质和环状 DNA，部分类群有鞭毛、菌毛和芽孢；古菌细胞壁成分不同，多适应高温、高盐等极端环境。两者外形相似，但核糖体 RNA 和膜脂等分子特征彼此分开，遗传距离接近真核生物与细菌之间的差距，单凭杆状或球状外形不能判定亲缘。Bacterial cells have a wall, in many cases peptidoglycan, a membrane, cytoplasm and circular DNA; some groups carry flagella, pili and endospores. Archaeal wall chemistry differs, and many archaea tolerate high heat or salt. The two look alike, but ribosomal RNA and membrane lipids separate them, with a genetic distance comparable to that between eukaryotes and bacteria; rod or sphere shape alone cannot assign relationship.[1][3]

真菌以酵母单细胞或菌丝多细胞形式存在，可形成孢子；病毒由核酸芯粒外包蛋白衣壳，部分再有脂包膜，进入宿主细胞后劫持复制、转录和翻译机器。朊病毒通过诱导正常蛋白错误折叠传播，不携带基因信息，这一机制区别于病毒感染；类病毒多感染植物，是环状裸露 RNA，不编码蛋白质。Fungi exist as single-celled yeasts or multicellular hyphae and form spores. A virus consists of a nucleic-acid core wrapped in a protein capsid, sometimes with a lipid envelope, and hijacks host replication, transcription and translation after entry. Prions spread by inducing normal proteins to misfold and carry no genetic information, which distinguishes them from viral infection; viroids are circular naked RNAs that mostly infect plants and encode no protein.[2][3]

边界：微生物不是一个单系演化群，大小和生活环境只是分组线索；古菌与细菌外观相近，不能凭形状判断亲缘。病毒是否算生命存在定义争议，朊病毒更只是蛋白分子；把显微镜下看到的颗粒都归为细菌，会误判结构、传播途径和应对方式。教材中的大小区间是常见范围，具体物种的尺寸仍需以测量为准。The boundary: microorganisms are not a monophyletic group, and size or habitat are grouping clues only. Archaea and bacteria look alike, so shape alone cannot show relationship. Whether viruses count as living is definitional, and prions are protein molecules; calling every microscopic particle a bacterium misjudges structure, transmission and response. Textbook size ranges are common intervals, and individual species still need measurement.[1][3]

02微生物有哪些代谢类型？它们在氮循环和碳循环中处于什么位置？What metabolic types do microbes have, and where do they sit in the nitrogen and carbon cycles?

自养异养、光能化能、好氧厌氧与发酵交叉分类；固氮、硝化、反硝化和分解由群落协作完成。Cross-classified by carbon, energy and oxygen; fixation, nitrification, denitrification and decomposition proceed as community work.

原核生物的代谢类型高度多样。按碳源分自养和异养：自养者利用二氧化碳合成有机物，异养者分解现成有机物；按能量来源分光能和化能；按对氧气的需求分好氧、兼性厌氧、专性厌氧和微需氧。发酵是不依赖电子传递链末端氧的产能方式，产物包括酸、醇和气体。OpenStax 在原核生物代谢一节把这些类型按碳源、能源和电子受体交叉分类。Prokaryotes show a wide range of metabolic types. By carbon source they split into autotrophs and heterotrophs: autotrophs build organic matter from carbon dioxide, heterotrophs break down existing organic matter. By energy source they are phototrophs or chemotrophs, and by oxygen requirement aerobic, facultative anaerobic, obligate anaerobic or microaerophilic. Fermentation makes ATP without oxygen as the terminal electron acceptor, yielding acids, alcohols and gases. OpenStax cross-classifies these types by carbon source, energy source and electron acceptor in its prokaryotic metabolism section.[5]

呼吸路径对应不同终端电子受体。好氧呼吸以氧为末端受体，产能效率高；厌氧呼吸用硝酸盐、硫酸盐、碳酸盐等作受体，产能较低；发酵把电子交给有机中间产物。氧气需求影响微生物在水体、肠道和沉积物中的分布，专性厌氧菌暴露于有氧环境会受损，而兼性厌氧菌可以在两种条件下切换产能路径。Respiratory paths match different terminal electron acceptors. Aerobic respiration uses oxygen and yields energy efficiently; anaerobic respiration uses nitrate, sulfate or carbonate with lower energy yield; fermentation passes electrons to organic intermediates. Oxygen requirement shapes distribution in water, gut and sediment: obligate anaerobes are damaged by exposure to air, while facultative anaerobes switch paths between conditions.[4][5]

在氮循环中，固氮菌把大气氮气还原为氨，进入植物和食物链；硝化细菌把氨逐步氧化为硝酸盐；反硝化细菌把硝酸盐还原为氮气回到大气。这些步骤由不同代谢类群完成，缺少任一环都会打断氮素在土壤、水体和生物群落之间的流动；氨化过程则把含氮有机物分解为氨，连接尸体和排泄物与下一阶段。In the nitrogen cycle, nitrogen fixers reduce atmospheric N2 to ammonia, which enters plants and food chains; nitrifiers oxidise ammonia stepwise to nitrate; denitrifiers reduce nitrate back to N2 gas. These steps are carried out by different metabolic groups, and losing any one breaks nitrogen flow among soil, water and biota; ammonification decomposes nitrogen-rich organic matter to ammonia, linking dead bodies and waste to the next stage.[4]

在碳循环中，分解者真菌和细菌分解动植物残体与排泄物，把有机碳矿化为二氧化碳，部分以甲烷形式释放；自养微生物固定二氧化碳，参与海洋和土壤的碳汇过程。微生物还参与硫、铁等元素的氧化还原，连接生物群落与无机环境，土壤和沉积物中的微生物量是有机质周转的关键环节。In the carbon cycle, decomposer fungi and bacteria break down plant and animal remains and waste, mineralising organic carbon to CO2, partly released as methane; autotrophic microbes fix CO2 and contribute to ocean and soil carbon sinks. Microbes also drive sulfur and iron redox reactions, linking biotic communities to the inorganic environment; microbial biomass in soil and sediment is a key part of organic-matter turnover.[1][4]

边界：代谢分类是功能归类，同一物种可在不同条件下切换部分路径；循环步骤由群落协作完成，不能归因于单一微生物。实验室培养条件与自然环境不同，测得的产能方式不能直接外推到野外群落。固氮和分解速率受温度、水分和氧分压影响，存在明显季节差异；野外循环通量需要结合土壤、水体实测数据判断。土壤微生物群落还与植物根系形成共生关系，协助根系吸收矿物质养分。The boundary: metabolic categories are functional labels, and one species can switch parts of its path under different conditions; cycle steps are accomplished by community cooperation, not a single microbe. Lab culture conditions differ from the wild, so measured energy paths cannot be extended directly to field communities. Fixation and decomposition rates depend on temperature, moisture and oxygen partial pressure, with clear seasonal variation; field flux must be judged with soil and water measurements.[4][5]

03病原微生物怎样传染致病？为什么抗生素不能抗病毒，细菌耐药怎样产生？How do pathogens spread and cause disease, why do antibiotics not act on viruses, and how does bacterial resistance arise?

附着侵入与毒素造成损伤；抗生素攻击细菌特有结构，耐药靠突变和基因转移积累选择。Attachment, invasion and toxins cause damage; antibiotics target bacterial structures; resistance accumulates by mutation and gene transfer.

病原微生物通过不同途径进入宿主。呼吸道飞沫、消化道污染食物和水、皮肤伤口、昆虫叮咬和接触传播是常见路径；进入后依附组织、侵入细胞或产生毒素，造成局部或全身反应。感染剂量、宿主免疫状态和病原毒力共同决定是否发病，OpenStax 在病原致病一节区分附着、侵入、免疫逃逸和组织损伤几个阶段。Pathogens enter hosts through different routes. Respiratory droplets, contaminated food and water, skin wounds, insect bites and contact transmission are common paths; once inside, they attach to tissues, invade cells or produce toxins, causing local or systemic reactions. Infectious dose, host immune state and pathogen virulence jointly determine illness, and OpenStax separates attachment, invasion, immune evasion and tissue damage in its disease section.[6]

致病机制分直接损伤和毒素作用。部分细菌在组织中繁殖破坏细胞，部分分泌外毒素或形成内毒素；病毒在细胞内复制导致细胞裂解或功能改变。症状与病原体增殖和宿主炎症反应都有关，发热、腹泻等表现是双方共同作用的结果，不能把症状直接等同于病原体本身，需要结合培养、抗原或核酸检测确认。Disease mechanisms split into direct damage and toxin action. Some bacteria multiply in tissue and destroy cells; others secrete exotoxins or form endotoxins; viruses replicate inside cells, causing lysis or functional change. Symptoms reflect both pathogen growth and the host inflammatory response, so fever or diarrhoea cannot be equated with the pathogen itself; confirmation relies on culture, antigen or nucleic-acid tests.[2][6]

抗生素针对细菌特有的结构和过程。青霉素类抑制细胞壁肽聚糖合成，部分药物作用于核糖体蛋白合成或代谢酶；人体细胞没有细胞壁，因此这类靶点对人细胞影响较小。病毒在宿主细胞内利用人的复制机器，缺乏细菌那样独特的细胞壁或核糖体靶点，所以抗生素不能抗病毒；针对病毒的是疫苗预防或专门设计的抗病毒药物。Antibiotics target bacterial-specific structures and processes. Penicillins block peptidoglycan cell-wall synthesis, and other drugs act on ribosomal protein synthesis or metabolic enzymes; human cells lack a wall, so these targets affect host cells less. Viruses use the human replication machine inside cells and lack such unique bacterial targets as walls or ribosomes, so antibiotics do not act on viruses; protection comes from vaccines or designed antiviral drugs.[6][7]

耐药通过突变和水平基因转移积累。细菌分裂快，随机突变产生耐药个体，质粒等载体可把耐药基因在菌株甚至种间传递；不规范用药、剂量不足和疗程中断选择出耐药菌群。这并不意味着抗生素永久失效：靶点仍敏感的菌株仍可被抑制，新的药物组合和药敏试验指导选药，公共卫生层面通过处方规范和感染控制延缓耐药扩散。Resistance accumulates through mutation and horizontal gene transfer. Bacteria divide rapidly, random mutations produce resistant individuals, and plasmids can carry resistance genes between strains and even species; irregular use, underdosing and interrupted courses select resistant populations. This does not mean antibiotics fail forever: strains still sensitive at the target remain suppressible, new combinations and susceptibility tests guide choice, and prescribing practice plus infection control slow spread at population level.[7]

边界：致病力是病原与宿主相互作用的结果，同样的细菌在不同免疫状态下表现不同；耐药不等于对所有抗生素都耐药，要按药敏结果判断。抗生素不治疗病毒感染，误用还会筛选耐药和扰乱正常菌群；症状原因需要实验室检出或影像等证据，不能凭经验直接归类。环境中存在的正常菌群与致病菌角色不同，分离到的微生物是否致病要结合病灶和症状判断。The boundary: virulence results from pathogen-host interaction, and the same bacterium behaves differently across immune states; resistance does not mean resistance to every antibiotic, and susceptibility results decide. Antibiotics do not treat viral infection, and misuse selects resistance and disrupts normal flora. The cause of symptoms needs laboratory detection or imaging, not empirical classification. Normal flora in the environment differ from pathogens, and whether an isolated microbe causes disease depends on lesion and symptoms.[6][7]

04先天免疫和适应免疫怎样协作？疫苗和加强针为什么能预防感染？How do innate and adaptive immunity cooperate, and why do vaccines and boosters prevent infection?

先天屏障快速响应，适应免疫形成抗体与记忆细胞；疫苗诱导记忆，加强针把抗体拉回有效区间。Innate barriers respond fast; adaptive immunity makes antibodies and memory; vaccines induce memory, boosters restore effective antibody levels.

免疫防御分先天和适应两层。先天免疫是第一道防线：皮肤黏膜屏障、吞噬细胞、炎症反应和补体等在遇到异物时快速响应，识别范围较广但没有长期记忆；适应免疫由淋巴细胞承担，针对特定抗原分化出抗体和记忆细胞，反应较慢但可重复激发。两层在感染中配合工作，屏障和炎症先控制扩散，适应免疫随后形成针对性反应。Defence splits into innate and adaptive layers. Innate immunity is the first barrier: skin and mucous membranes, phagocytes, inflammation and complement respond quickly to foreign material, with broad recognition but no long-term memory. Adaptive immunity uses lymphocytes, which differentiate antibodies and memory cells against a specific antigen; it responds more slowly but can be re-triggered. The two cooperate: barriers and inflammation first limit spread, and adaptive immunity then forms a targeted response.[2][8]

适应免疫经历识别、扩增和记忆过程。抗原被呈递后，相应 T 细胞和 B 细胞克隆增殖，B 细胞分化为浆细胞产生抗体；一部分细胞成为记忆细胞长期存留。再次遇到同一抗原时，记忆细胞快速扩增，抗体上升更快更高，感染被压在潜伏期或轻症范围，这是疫苗预防的基本原理。Adaptive immunity goes through recognition, expansion and memory. After antigen presentation, matching T and B cell clones proliferate; B cells differentiate into plasma cells that make antibodies, and some become long-lived memory cells. On re-exposure to the same antigen, memory cells expand quickly, antibody levels rise faster and higher, and infection stays latent or mild; this is the basic principle of vaccine protection.[8]

疫苗把灭活病原体、亚单位抗原、载体或核酸序列引入人体，在不造成重病的前提下诱导记忆细胞和抗体。不同疫苗的抗原形式、接种路线和加强节奏不同；初次接种后抗体水平随时间下降，加强针再次激发记忆反应，把防护水平拉回有效区间。OpenStax 疫苗一节按抗原类型、免疫策略和接种程序分类，候选疫苗还要经过安全性和有效性试验。Vaccines introduce killed pathogens, subunit antigens, vectors or nucleic acid sequences to induce memory cells and antibodies without causing serious disease. Antigen form, route and booster schedule differ by product; antibody levels fall after the primary series, and a booster re-stimulates memory to pull protection back into the effective range. OpenStax classifies vaccines by antigen type, immune strategy and schedule, and candidates still pass safety and efficacy trials.[7][8]

群体防护来自个体免疫覆盖。足够比例的人获得免疫后，病原在人际间传播链被打断，未免疫者也得到间接保护；覆盖率下降时易感人群积累，传染病容易重新扩散。疫苗效果随时间、病原体变异和人群年龄结构变化，需要监测抗体水平、发病率和住院数据，不能只凭一次接种判断长期保护。Population protection rests on individual immune coverage. When enough people are immune, transmission chains between people are broken and the unimmunised gain indirect protection; as coverage falls, susceptible people accumulate and outbreaks resurface. Vaccine effectiveness changes with time, pathogen variation and age structure, so antibody levels, incidence and hospitalisation must be monitored; one dose cannot be assumed to give lifelong protection.[8]

边界：疫苗诱导的免疫不保证终身不感染，作用是降低重症和传播概率；加强针是否需要取决于抗体衰减、流行株和高危人群，不是人人同一节奏。先天与适应免疫相互依赖，屏障受损时适应免疫也难以发挥；个体免疫缺陷、用药和基础病会改变响应，不能用健康人的反应推断所有人。接种禁忌和程序要结合个人病史由专业意见判断。The boundary: vaccine-induced immunity does not guarantee lifelong non-infection; its role is to lower severe disease and transmission. Whether a booster is needed depends on antibody decay, circulating variants and high-risk groups, not a single schedule for everyone. Innate and adaptive immunity depend on each other; with a damaged barrier, adaptive responses work poorly. Immunodeficiency, medication and chronic disease change response, so healthy-volunteer results cannot be generalised, and contraindications follow professional advice with personal history.[7][8]

05病毒怎样变异形成株系？突变与流行演化怎样影响疫苗研发和癌症疫苗前景？How do viruses mutate into lineages, and how does epidemic evolution affect vaccine development and cancer-vaccine prospects?

RNA 病毒错配积累，漂移与重配改变表面蛋白；疫苗按流行株更新，癌疫苗仍处临床试验阶段。RNA mismatches accumulate; drift and reassortment change surface proteins; vaccines track strains, cancer vaccines remain trial-stage.

病毒复制过程中容易积累突变。RNA 病毒的聚合酶纠错能力弱，复制时碱基错配较多，种群内同时存在多种序列；宿主免疫压力和传播条件选择出适应度更高的变异株。突变本身是随机的，环境压力决定哪些变异被保留，OpenStax 在病毒形态与分类一节讨论表面蛋白变化与株系分化的关系。Viruses accumulate mutations readily during replication. RNA-virus polymerases have weak proofreading, so base mismatches are frequent and multiple sequences coexist in a population; host immune pressure and transmission conditions select higher-fitness variants. Mutations are random, and environmental pressure decides which are retained; OpenStax links surface-protein change to strain divergence in its virus morphology and classification section.[2]

变异累积到一定程度形成不同株系。抗原漂移是逐步替换表面蛋白氨基酸位点，改变识别结合；抗原转换是较大幅度的重组或重配，表面蛋白性质明显改变。一个变体是否被单独命名，取决于序列差异、传播能力、免疫逃逸和致病表现的综合标准，而不是单一突变数；监测序列和流行病学数据共同支撑株系判断。Variation accumulates to form separate lineages. Antigenic drift gradually replaces amino-acid sites on surface proteins and alters recognition; antigenic shift is a larger recombination or reassortment that changes surface-protein character markedly. Whether a variant is named separately depends on sequence difference, transmissibility, immune escape and disease behaviour together, not a single mutation count; sequence surveillance and epidemiological data support strain judgement.[2][7]

流行演化影响疫苗设计。疫苗成分依据流行株抗原序列更新，类似流感疫苗按季节株系调整；表面蛋白变化使原有抗体结合力下降时，需要更新候选毒株。病毒在人群中传播越广，复制和选择机会越多，出现逃逸变异的概率越高；疫苗接种率高、传播被压制时，选择压力和变异出现的机会也随之改变。监测网络持续采样测序，才能判断变异是否脱离原有株系范围。Epidemic evolution shapes vaccine design. Vaccine components are updated to circulating strain antigen sequences, much as flu vaccines are adjusted by seasonal strain; when surface-protein change lowers original antibody binding, candidate strains must be refreshed. The wider a virus circulates, the more replication and selection opportunities and the higher the chance of escape variants; high vaccination and suppressed transmission also change selection pressure and mutation opportunity.[2][8]

癌疫苗是另一类探索方向。肿瘤相关抗原可以被免疫系统识别，研究尝试用疫苗激发对癌细胞特定蛋白的反应；与预防传染病的疫苗不同，癌疫苗多为治疗性候选，仍处于临床试验阶段，个体肿瘤抗原差异大，标准化疫苗难度高。核酸平台技术缩短了序列设计到制备的周期，但疗效、剂量和适用人群仍需按试验结果确定。Cancer vaccines are a different line of exploration. Tumour-associated antigens can be recognised by the immune system, and research tries to raise responses against particular cancer proteins; unlike infectious-disease vaccines, cancer vaccines are mostly therapeutic candidates still in clinical trials, with large individual antigen differences and difficulty in standardisation. Nucleic-acid platforms shorten the sequence-to-product cycle, but efficacy, dose and eligible groups follow trial results.[7][8]

边界：病毒突变率不等于致病率上升，多数变异不改变传播或重症表现；株系命名受监测和分类标准影响，不同地区通报节奏不同。疫苗更新需要时间走完研发、试验和生产流程；癌疫苗前景建立在抗原识别证据上，疗效和适用人群需按试验结果判断，不能由动物或早期实验直接外推到临床常规使用。The boundary: mutation rate does not equal rising disease severity, and most variants do not change transmission or severe disease; strain naming depends on surveillance and classification standards, and reporting cadence differs by region. Vaccine update takes time through development, trials and production. Cancer-vaccine prospects rest on antigen-recognition evidence, and efficacy and eligibility are judged by trial results, not extrapolated from animal or early studies into routine clinical use.[2][8]

06抗生素为什么对病毒无效，它们分别攻击细菌的什么结构？Why do antibiotics not work on viruses, and what bacterial structures do they target?

抗生素靶向细菌细胞壁、核糖体等特有结构；病毒无独立细胞装置，找不到结合位点。Antibiotics target bacterial walls and ribosomes; viruses lack independent cellular machinery and offer no binding site.

抗生素不是笼统地“杀菌”，而是在细菌独有的结构和生理过程上找靶点。β-内酰胺类（青霉素、头孢菌素）抑制肽聚糖细胞壁的交联合成，细菌失去完整壁后在渗透压下破裂；大环内酯类、四环素类进入菌体，结合细菌核糖体亚基，阻断细菌自己的蛋白质合成；喹诺酮类干扰 DNA 旋转酶，影响细菌核酸复制。人体细胞没有细胞壁，核糖体结构与细菌不同，这类靶点对宿主细胞影响相对较小，这就是选择性化疗的基础。Antibiotics do not kill bacteria indiscriminately; each class acts on a structure or process unique to bacteria. Beta-lactam drugs such as penicillins and cephalosporins block cross-linking of peptidoglycan in the cell wall, so the bacterium ruptures under osmotic pressure. Macrolides and tetracyclines enter the cell and bind bacterial ribosomal subunits, stopping bacterial protein synthesis. Quinolones interfere with DNA gyrase and block nucleic-acid replication. Human cells lack a cell wall and their ribosomes differ from bacterial ones, which is the basis of selective toxicity.[9]

不同药物的靶点和覆盖范围并不相同。细胞壁合成抑制剂主要作用于繁殖期细菌；核糖体和代谢酶抑制剂各有自己的抗菌谱；另有药物破坏细胞膜或干扰叶酸代谢。药敏试验在体外测定菌株对各药的抑制圈，用来指导选药；同一类药对不同菌种、不同耐药状态的效果差别很大，不能凭“消炎药”的俗称互换使用。选择性不是绝对的，药物也会影响人体正常菌群和个别宿主结构，因此存在相应副作用。Drug classes differ in target and coverage. Cell-wall inhibitors act mainly on dividing bacteria, while ribosome- and metabolism-targeting groups each carry their own spectrum. Some agents damage the membrane or interfere with folate metabolism. Susceptibility testing measures inhibition zones in vitro and guides choice; the same class performs differently across species and resistance states, so drugs cannot be swapped by the loose label of anti-inflammatory. Selectivity is not absolute: antibiotics also affect normal flora and occasional host structures, which accounts for their side effects.[7][9]

病毒找不到对应靶点，因为它根本不是独立细胞。病毒颗粒只由核酸芯粒外包蛋白衣壳构成，部分再有一层脂包膜；没有细胞壁、核糖体、代谢酶系和独立产能通路，进入人体后完全借用宿主细胞的复制、转录和翻译机器。青霉素作用的肽聚糖结构、大环内酯作用的细菌核糖体，在病毒身上都不存在，所以抗生素不能抑制病毒；普通感冒、流感等病毒性感染用抗生素无效。Viruses offer no matching target because they are not independent cells. A virion consists of a nucleic-acid core wrapped in a protein capsid, sometimes with a lipid envelope; it has no wall, no ribosomes, no metabolic enzymes and no independent energy production. Once inside a human cell it hijacks the host replication, transcription and translation machinery. The peptidoglycan wall attacked by penicillin and the bacterial ribosome bound by macrolides simply do not exist in viruses, which is why antibiotics cannot suppress a viral infection.[6][9]

误用抗生素还会付出额外代价。不针对病毒使用抗生素，既压不住病原体，又会扰乱肠道和体表的正常菌群，削弱菌群对致病菌的占位保护；存活下来的细菌更可能携带耐药基因，长期反复用药就在群体层面筛选出耐药菌群。病毒性感染的支持处理以休息、补液和对症缓解为主，预防主要靠疫苗和防护措施。Misuse carries an extra cost. Used against a virus, an antibiotic neither stops the pathogen nor spares the commensal flora of the gut and skin, weakening the protective occupancy of normal microbes. Surviving bacteria are more likely to carry resistance genes, and repeated use selects resistant populations at the community level. Viral illness is managed mainly with rest, fluids and symptomatic relief, while prevention rests on vaccination and protective measures.[6][7]

边界：抗生素是抗细菌药物，不治疗病毒感染，也不缓解所有类型的炎症；症状由哪种病原引起，需要结合培养、抗原或核酸检测判断。本章只讲作用机制与用药原则，不展开处方选择、剂量疗程和具体药物配伍。Boundary: antibiotics treat bacteria, not viruses, and they do not resolve every kind of inflammation. Which pathogen causes a symptom requires culture, antigen or nucleic-acid testing. This chapter covers mechanisms and principles, not prescription selection, dosing course or drug combinations.[6][7]

07细菌耐药是怎样产生的，为什么抗生素要吃满疗程？How does bacterial resistance arise, and why should an antibiotic course be completed?

药物选择压保留已有耐药突变，质粒可跨菌转移；足疗程把菌群压到免疫可清除的范围。Drug selection preserves pre-existing resistance mutants, plasmids transfer genes across bacteria; a full course pushes load below immune clearance.

耐药不是药物直接“教”细菌产生的，而是药物环境把种群里已经存在的突变筛选保留下来。细菌分裂快，单个个体繁殖一代可短至 20 到 30 分钟，种群内偶然出现随机突变；多数突变无用甚至有害，只有少数恰好改变了药物靶点、降解药物或增强外排，让携带个体在含药环境中存活。这些存活菌大量繁殖，敏感菌被清除后空出的空间和营养由耐药个体占据。Resistance is not taught to bacteria by the drug; the medication selects mutants that already exist in the population. Bacteria divide rapidly, with a generation as short as 20 to 30 minutes, and random mutations arise by chance. Most are useless or harmful, but a few alter the drug target, degrade the drug or strengthen efflux pumps, letting carriers survive in a drug-containing environment. These survivors multiply, while cleared susceptible cells leave space and nutrients that resistant individuals then occupy.[7]

耐药基因还能在菌株甚至种间转移。质粒、转座子等载体把编码钝化酶、改变靶点或外排泵的基因携带给其他细菌；一次获得的抗药性可以跨物种扩散，这也是同一医院、同一环境里不同菌种出现相似耐药谱的原因。细菌表面的获得性屏障、生物膜包裹和菌株密度，都会进一步降低药物到达作用位点的浓度。Resistance genes also move between strains and even species. Plasmids and transposons carry genes coding for inactivating enzymes, target changes or efflux pumps, passing resistance to other bacteria. Once acquired, the trait can spread across species, which explains why different organisms in the same hospital or environment share similar resistance profiles. Acquired surface barriers, biofilm coating and high cell density further reduce the concentration of drug that reaches its site of action.[7][9]

选择压力来自用药方式本身。剂量不足、疗程中断、症状一缓解就停药，会把菌群压到半清半不清的状态：敏感者被杀掉，耐药者留档，下一轮面对药物时存活的正是这部分群体。不必要的预防性用药、反复自行购药和农业生产中的抗菌药使用，都在持续提供选择压，让耐药突变在人群和环境中积累扩散。Selection pressure comes from how the drug is used. Underdosing, interrupted courses and stopping as soon as symptoms ease leave the population half-cleared: susceptible cells die, resistant ones remain, and the next round selects exactly that survivors' group. Unnecessary prophylaxis, repeated self-medication and agricultural antimicrobial use keep supplying selection pressure so resistant mutants accumulate across people and the environment.[7]

“疗程”的机制意义就在这里。按方案用够时间和剂量，目标是把细菌负荷压到宿主免疫能最终清除的范围，而不是只压到症状消失；残留少量耐药菌时，症状可能已经减轻，但停药后它们会重新增殖。足疗程并不是鼓励多吃药，而是避免在最利于耐药者存活的强度上反复暴露药物。耐药也不等于对所有抗生素都耐药，换用作用于不同靶点的药物仍可能有效，具体需依据药敏试验。The mechanistic purpose of a full course is therefore clear. Following the prescribed dose and duration aims to push bacterial load below what host immunity can finish off, rather than only below the level at which symptoms disappear. If a few resistant cells remain, symptoms may already have eased, yet after stopping they regrow. A full course is not an invitation to take more medicine; it avoids repeated exposure at the strength that best lets resistant cells survive. Resistance also does not mean resistance to every antibiotic, and a drug acting on a different target may still work, as susceptibility testing decides.[7][9]

边界：本章描述耐药产生与选择的机制，不评价具体药物的临床地位。个人不应凭经验延长、缩短或更换抗菌治疗；是否存在耐药、属于哪种机制，需要培养和药敏结果支持。公共卫生层面延缓扩散靠处方规范、感染控制和新药与替代方案研发，这些措施超出了单章机制的范围。Boundary: this chapter describes how resistance arises and is selected, not the clinical standing of any particular drug. Individuals should not lengthen, shorten or switch antibacterial treatment by experience alone; whether resistance exists and by which mechanism requires culture and susceptibility results. Slowing spread at public-health level rests on prescribing practice, infection control and new drug development, beyond the scope of this chapter.[6][7]

08为什么癌症会转移，血管生成和免疫逃逸在其中起什么作用？Why do cancers metastasize, and what role do angiogenesis and immune evasion play?

癌细胞降解基底膜入血，VEGF 诱导新生血管供养并提供通道，逃避免疫监视后在远处定植。Cancer cells breach the basement membrane into blood, VEGF drives nourishing leaky vessels, and immune escape permits distant colonization.

转移指肿瘤细胞从原发部位扩散到远处器官并形成继发灶，它是一组连续步骤，而不是肿瘤“跑过去”这么简单。第一步是局部侵袭：肿瘤细胞改变黏附能力，分泌基质金属蛋白酶等酶类，降解基底膜和细胞外基质，从原有组织边界向外渗出。上皮—间质转换让细胞获得更强的迁移和抗凋亡能力，形状从规整上皮样变为可移动的间充质样。Metastasis means tumor cells spread from the primary site to a distant organ and establish a secondary growth; it is a sequence of continuous steps, not a simple running away. The first step is local invasion. Tumor cells alter adhesion and secrete matrix metalloproteinases and other enzymes that degrade the basement membrane and extracellular matrix, moving out past the original tissue boundary. An epithelial-to-mesenchymal transition gives cells stronger migration and apoptosis resistance, changing them from orderly epithelial shapes into motile mesenchymal-like forms.[11]

突破组织边界后，癌细胞需要进入循环。它们先穿过血管或淋巴管的内皮基底膜，这一步受趋化因子和黏附分子引导；随后成为循环肿瘤细胞。在血流中，血小板常包裹这些细胞，为其提供保护信号并帮助躲避机械冲刷；存活细胞有时与血小板、白细胞聚集成团。大部分循环细胞会在血液或淋巴途中死亡，只有少数能在远处存活下来。After crossing the tissue boundary, cancer cells must enter the circulation. They invade nearby small blood vessels or lymphatics and become circulating tumor cells. In the bloodstream, platelets often cloak these cells, providing survival signals and shielding against mechanical shear. Most circulating cells die along the way, and only a few survive in a distant site.[10][11]

血管生成是转移的配套条件。实体瘤长到一定体积后内部缺氧，缺氧诱导因子启动，肿瘤和间质细胞分泌 VEGF 等生长因子，刺激邻近内皮细胞长出新生血管。这些血管为肿瘤输送养分和氧气，结构紊乱、管壁渗漏，同时也给癌细胞提供了进入血流的通道。抗血管生成药物阻断 VEGF 通路，正是从切断供养和扩散通道入手，但肿瘤可激活其他旁路适应。Angiogenesis supplies the infrastructure for spread. Once a solid tumor reaches a certain size its interior becomes hypoxic; hypoxia-inducible factors kick in, and tumor and stromal cells secrete growth factors such as VEGF that stimulate nearby endothelial cells to grow new vessels. These vessels deliver nutrients and oxygen, but they are disorganized and leaky, and they also give cancer cells a route into the bloodstream. Anti-angiogenic drugs block the VEGF pathway to cut supply and exit routes, though tumors can adapt through alternative pathways.[11]

免疫逃逸决定脱落细胞能否不被清除。肿瘤微环境里，肿瘤相关巨噬细胞、髓系抑制细胞等会压制抗肿瘤免疫；肿瘤细胞表达 PD-L1 等分子，结合 T 细胞上的检查点受体，把本应识别异常细胞的免疫反应关掉。逃过监视的癌细胞在靶器官穿出血管，遇到能支持其生长的预转移微环境，才定植形成继发瘤；器官选择有一定偏好，但并非随机。Immune evasion decides whether shed cells are cleared. In the tumor microenvironment, tumor-associated macrophages and myeloid suppressor cells dampen antitumor immunity. Tumor cells express molecules such as PD-L1 that bind checkpoint receptors on T cells and switch off the response that would otherwise recognize abnormal cells. Cells escaping surveillance then extravasate in a target organ and, when they meet a supportive pre-metastatic niche, colonize into secondary tumors. Organ preference is biased rather than random.[6][11]

边界：本章只讲转移的机制链条，不讨论分期诊断、影像判断或治疗方案。不同癌种的远处好发部位不同，常见靶器官包括肺、肝、骨和脑，具体规律需按癌种判断。转移能力高低因肿瘤类型和个体差异很大，体外和动物实验结论不能直接外推到临床；是否发生远处转移，需要影像、病理和随访证据综合判断。Boundary: this chapter explains the mechanistic chain of metastasis, not staging, imaging or treatment. metastatic capacity varies widely by tumor type and individual; in vitro and animal findings cannot be extrapolated directly to clinical practice. Whether distant spread has occurred rests on imaging, pathology and follow-up evidence taken together.[10][11]

09为什么癌症会让人消瘦，恶病质只是吃得少造成的吗？Why does cancer cause wasting, and is cachexia just a matter of eating little?

炎症因子驱动肌肉脂肪分解，摄食中枢被调整、能耗升高；单纯进补不能完全逆转恶病质。Inflammatory factors drive muscle and fat breakdown, appetite centres reset and energy use rises; feeding alone cannot fully reverse cachexia.

癌症相关消瘦在医学上称为恶病质，它不是单纯“吃得少、饿瘦了”。这是一种多因子综合征，特征是持续的骨骼肌量丢失，可同时伴有脂肪量减少，常规营养支持只能部分而不能完全逆转。综述统计，恶病质在不同癌种中的比例可达较高水平，常见于消化道、胰腺和晚期肿瘤，是预后差、治疗耐受下降的重要因素。它和单纯节食减重不同：节食减少的主要是脂肪，肌肉通常还能保留一部分，而恶病质以骨骼肌持续丢失为标志。Cancer-related wasting is called cachexia, and it is not simply eating too little and becoming thin. It is a multifactorial syndrome marked by ongoing loss of skeletal muscle mass, with or without fat loss, that conventional nutritional support can only partly reverse. Reviews report high proportions across cancer types, especially in gastrointestinal, pancreatic and advanced disease, and cachexia is a major factor in poorer prognosis and lower treatment tolerance.[12]

炎症因子是驱动分解的核心。肿瘤及宿主炎症细胞产生 TNF-α、IL-6、IL-1β 等介质，进入循环后激活 NF-κB 等通路，促进肌肉蛋白降解；TNF-α 早期曾被称为恶病质素，能同时促进肌肉和脂肪组织分解。肿瘤还可产生蛋白水解诱导因子、脂动因子等，直接作用于肌肉和脂肪，肌生成调节因子 MyoD 等再生信号被下调，肌肉既被分解又难以修补。Inflammatory mediators drive the catabolism. Tumor and host inflammatory cells produce TNF-alpha, IL-6 and IL-1 beta, which enter circulation and activate pathways such as NF-kB that promote muscle protein breakdown. TNF-alpha was once called cachectin because it drives breakdown of both muscle and fat. Tumors may also produce proteolysis-inducing factor and lipid-mobilizing factor that act directly on muscle and fat, while muscle regeneration signals such as MyoD are downregulated, so muscle is broken down and poorly repaired.[11][12]

脂肪和肌肉消耗相互叠加。白色脂肪被大量动员，游离脂肪酸在肌肉中过度氧化；骨骼肌通过泛素—蛋白酶体和自噬通路加速降解，肌量、肌力和活动能力一起下降。患者常伴随静息能量消耗升高、胰岛素抵抗和食欲下降，形成“分解大于合成”的负平衡。单纯增加进食，往往补不回被炎症通路持续分解的肌肉。Fat and muscle loss stack on each other. White adipose tissue is mobilized heavily and free fatty acids are over-oxidized in muscle; skeletal muscle is degraded faster through the ubiquitin-proteasome and autophagy pathways, so mass, strength and activity decline together. Resting energy expenditure often rises, insulin resistance appears and appetite falls, producing a negative balance in which breakdown exceeds synthesis. Adding food alone usually cannot restore muscle being continuously decomposed by inflammatory pathways.[12]

食欲和摄食中枢也被炎症信号调整。炎症介质作用于下丘脑，促进抑制摄食的神经元、抑制促进摄食的神经元，同时影响瘦素、胃饥饿素和 GDF-15 等食欲相关信号，于是患者早饱、无味、进食意愿低。情绪低落、乏力和治疗副作用进一步减少活动和摄入，与身体分解形成相互加重的循环。Appetite and feeding centres are also reset by inflammatory signals. Mediators act on the hypothalamus, promoting anorexigenic neurons and inhibiting orexigenic ones, while leptin, ghrelin and GDF-15 signals are altered, so patients feel early fullness, dull taste and reduced willingness to eat. Low mood, fatigue and treatment side effects further reduce activity and intake, forming a self-reinforcing cycle with bodily breakdown.[10][12]

边界：评估恶病质看半年内非主动减重、BMI、骨骼肌量和炎症指标，需由临床结合化验、影像和基础病判断；本章不给出营养处方或治疗方案。消瘦也可见于甲亢、糖尿病、胃肠疾病等非肿瘤情况，不能凭体重下降直接归因于癌症，持续或报警性症状应及时就医排查。Boundary: cachexia assessment looks at unintentional weight loss over six months, BMI, skeletal muscle mass and inflammatory markers, judged clinically with labs, imaging and comorbidity; this chapter gives no nutrition prescription or treatment plan. Wasting also occurs in thyroid disease, diabetes and gastrointestinal illness, so weight loss cannot be attributed to cancer alone; persistent or alarming symptoms should be evaluated promptly.[10][11]

10病毒为什么不算生物，它脱离宿主后能自己繁殖吗？Why are viruses not counted as living, and can they reproduce without a host?

病毒只有核酸与衣壳、无独立代谢，必须借用宿主机器装配；是否算生命取决于定义争议。Virions have nucleic acid and capsid without independent metabolism, they hijack host machinery; life status rests on definitional debate.

病毒算不算生物，取决于用哪一套生命定义。按照常见的细胞生命标准——有细胞结构、能独立代谢、能自主繁殖——病毒不满足：它没有细胞膜、核糖体和代谢酶系，不能自己从底物获取能量，也不能分裂产生新个体。离开宿主细胞，病毒颗粒只是一个相对稳定的化学颗粒，不表现生长、代谢或应激反应。Whether viruses count as living depends on which definition of life is used. Under the common cellular standard—cell structure, independent metabolism and autonomous reproduction—they do not qualify: no membrane, no ribosomes, no metabolic enzymes, no energy drawn from substrates, and no division into new individuals. Outside a host cell, a virion is a relatively stable chemical particle that shows no growth, metabolism or stress response.[2]

病毒的结构比细胞简单得多。核心是一段核酸（DNA 或 RNA），外包蛋白衣壳，有些病毒在衣壳外还有一层来自宿主细胞膜的脂包膜，包膜上嵌着识别宿主的糖蛋白。颗粒直径多在 20 到 300 纳米，比多数细菌小一到两个数量级，光学显微镜下看不到，需要电子显微镜成像。有无包膜影响它对脂溶剂和环境干燥的抵抗力，也影响入侵方式。A virus is far simpler than a cell. Its core is a nucleic-acid strand, DNA or RNA, wrapped in a protein capsid; some carry an outer lipid envelope stolen from the host membrane, studded with glycoproteins that recognise host cells. Most virions are 20 to 300 nanometres across, one to two orders of magnitude smaller than typical bacteria, invisible under a light microscope and seen only by electron microscopy. Presence or absence of an envelope changes resistance to solvents and drying, as well as the entry route.[1][2]

病毒的繁殖完全依赖宿主。吸附到易感细胞后，病毒把核酸注入或以整体进入方式脱壳；随后借用宿主的核糖体、氨基酸、核苷酸和能量系统，先复制核酸、合成衣壳蛋白，再装配成新颗粒，最后释放到细胞外。这一复制周期不经过细胞分裂，而是像“接管工厂”批量组装；新病毒再去感染下一个细胞。缺少宿主机器时，这一过程无法启动。Replication depends entirely on the host. After adsorbing to a susceptible cell, the virus injects its nucleic acid or enters and uncoats; it then borrows host ribosomes, amino acids, nucleotides and energy to copy nucleic acid, make capsid proteins and assemble new particles, which are eventually released. This cycle does not use cell division but operates like taking over a factory for batch assembly, and new virions infect the next cell. Without host machinery the process cannot start.[2][3]

与其他非细胞因子相比，病毒仍有自身特征。类病毒是环状裸露 RNA，只感染植物、不编码蛋白质；朊病毒是错误折叠的蛋白质分子，通过诱导正常蛋白错误折叠传播，不携带核酸基因组。把这些都称为“微生物”是按研究对象集合分类，不代表它们都走同一条繁殖路线。生命定义在学界仍有争议：有人把能复制和演化纳入生命特征，也有人认为没有独立代谢就不能算作生命。Virions still differ from other non-cellular agents. Viroids are circular naked RNAs that infect plants and encode no protein; prions are misfolded protein molecules that propagate by inducing normal proteins to misfold, with no nucleic-acid genome. Calling all of them microbes groups research objects, not shared reproductive routes. The definition of life remains debated: some include replication and evolution as life traits, while others hold that without independent metabolism something cannot count as alive.[1][3]

边界：本章区分结构、复制方式和定义争议，不评价具体病毒的致病性或传播风险。病毒分类、包膜和大小是常见范围，具体物种以检测和电镜观测为准；是否感染、属于哪一类病原，需要实验室证据，不能凭发热或皮疹自行判断。Boundary: this chapter separates structure, replication route and definitional debate, without grading the pathogenicity or transmission risk of any specific virus. Classification, envelope and size ranges are common intervals, and individual species are settled by testing and electron microscopy; whether an infection exists and which agent it belongs to needs laboratory evidence, not self-judgement from fever or rash.[2][3]
