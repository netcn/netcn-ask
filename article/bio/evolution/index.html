01什么是演化？自然选择通过什么机制改变种群？What is evolution, and by what mechanism does natural selection change populations?

可遗传变异经差异繁殖被筛选，有利等位基因频率逐代上升。Heritable variation is filtered by differential reproduction, raising favorable allele frequencies over generations.

演化是种群内等位基因频率逐代变化的过程，自然选择是其中一种机制：个体在可遗传变异上存在差异，存活和繁殖机会不同，有利性状在下一代中比例上升。演化改变的是种群，不是单个个体在一生中定向改变性状，个体的后天锻炼不会直接变成可遗传的序列改变；后天获得的性状只有在改变生殖细胞遗传物质时才可能被传递，普通锻炼不能改写序列。Evolution is the change in allele frequencies in a population over generations, and natural selection is one mechanism: individuals differ in heritable variation, survive and reproduce at different rates, and favorable traits rise in the next generation. Evolution changes populations, not a single individual that deliberately transforms its traits during its lifetime; acquired exercise does not become a direct sequence change passed on.[1][2]

第一步是变异，由突变、重组和中性变化产生；第二步是遗传，变异通过繁殖传递；第三步是差异繁殖，环境和配偶选择使某些个体留下更多后代。可遗传且提高存活繁殖的性状被保留，不利性状被淘汰，这一过程不需要预先设定方向，也不保证产生更复杂的后代；变异中既有提高适合度的，也有中性甚至有害的，选择只作用于表型差异。The first step is variation from mutation, recombination and neutral change; the second is heredity, by which variation is passed through reproduction; the third is differential reproduction, as environment and mate choice leave some individuals with more offspring. Heritable traits that improve survival and reproduction are retained, unfavorable ones removed, and this process has no preset direction nor a guarantee of more complex descendants.[2]

加拉帕戈斯地雀的喙形与取食种子、昆虫的类型对应，长期记录显示干旱年份厚喙个体比例上升；抗生素耐药、昆虫抗药性和人工选育的品种分化，都是可观察到的选择过程。化石、比较解剖和 DNA 序列的同源关系在不同尺度上印证共同祖先，独立证据之间互相吻合，这些观察不需要等待地质年代尺度，短世代生物和人工选育就能看到响应。Galápagos finch beaks match seed- and insect-eating types, and long records show thick-beaked individuals rise in dry years; antibiotic resistance, insecticide resistance and breed differentiation under artificial selection are observable selection processes. Fossils, comparative anatomy and DNA sequence homology corroborate common ancestry at different scales, with independent evidence fitting together.[1][4]

自然选择不产生完美设计，只在现有变异和历史约束下筛选；性状可能先为某一功能出现，再被另一种用途借用。选择没有朝向人类的目标，环境变化后曾经有利的性状可能不再适应，残留结构和次功能改造都是演化历史的痕迹；环境变迁后，原本服务于某一生境的器官可能转为其他用途，结构改造常滞后于功能变化。Natural selection does not produce perfect design; it filters under existing variation and historical constraints. A trait may first arise for one function and later be borrowed for another. Selection has no human-directed goal, and a trait once favorable may cease to fit as the environment changes; vestigial structures and repurposed functions are traces of evolutionary history.[1][2]

适应性解释要区分选择、漂变和基因流的贡献；小种群中随机漂变也会改变频率。演化证据支持种群随时间改变，但具体谱系关系和性状起源仍会随化石与基因组数据更新，不能把简化谱系当作固定结论。区分可观察的事实与待检验的假说，是讨论演化时需要保留的边界；基因流可以把邻近种群的变异带入，漂变在小种群中甚至压过选择方向。Adaptive explanations must separate the contributions of selection, drift and gene flow; in small populations random drift also shifts frequencies. Evidence supports that populations change over time, but specific lineage relationships and trait origins keep updating with fossil and genomic data, so a simplified tree should not be treated as fixed. Distinguishing observed fact from testable hypothesis is the boundary to keep when discussing evolution.[1][2]

02恐龙真的没有灭绝吗？鸟类为什么被看作恐龙的后代？Did dinosaurs not go extinct, and why are birds considered dinosaur descendants?

鸟类是兽脚恐龙一支；始祖鸟约 1.5 亿年前过渡化石记录羽毛先于飞行出现。Birds are a theropod branch; the 150 Ma Archaeopteryx records feathers before flight.

现生鸟类是兽脚类恐龙的一支后裔，羽毛、骨骼中空和三指足等特征把鸟类与小型兽脚恐龙联系起来；白垩纪末灭绝事件中，非鸟恐龙消失，鸟类祖先幸存并辐射出今天的鸟类。鸟类没有脱离恐龙谱系，而是恐龙中延续到现在的一个分支；现生鸟类在分类上归入兽脚恐龙中的鸟翼类，与霸王龙等共享若干骨骼细节。Living birds are descendants of one branch of theropod dinosaurs; feathers, hollow bones and three-toed feet link birds to small theropods. At the end-Cretaceous extinction, non-avian dinosaurs disappeared while bird ancestors survived and radiated into today's birds. Birds did not leave the dinosaur lineage; they are the branch of dinosaurs that persists to the present.[4][5]

始祖鸟生活在约 1.5 亿年前，同时具备爬行动物的牙齿、长尾和鸟类的羽毛、翼结构，是恐龙向鸟类过渡的代表化石。中国发现的带羽毛恐龙化石显示，羽毛先于飞行出现，早期功能可能是保温、展示或伪装，飞行是后来在骨骼、肌肉和呼吸系统配合下逐步改造出来的；化石羽毛印痕保存了羽轴和分支结构，可与现代羽毛的微观排列对照。Archaeopteryx lived about 150 million years ago and combined reptilian teeth and a long tail with birdlike feathers and wing structure, a representative transitional fossil. Feathered dinosaur fossils from China show feathers arose before flight, their early functions possibly insulation, display or camouflage; flight was remodeled later through cooperation of bone, muscle and respiratory system.[5]

鸟类骨骼薄而中空、愈合程度高，前肢变成翼，胸骨有龙骨突附着飞行肌；恒温、高代谢和呼吸系统的气囊结构支持持续飞行。这些特征在恐龙祖先身上已有部分雏形，逐步叠加而成，不是从无到有一次成型。羽毛的微观结构在化石中可与鳞片和毛发区分；气囊延伸到骨骼内部，在吸气和呼气时都有含氧空气流过肺部，满足飞行高耗氧。巨型化还受生长模式支持：蜥脚类骨骼组织学显示纤维板层骨快速持续生长，鸟式气囊肺提高供氧效率，长颈取食配合高代谢组合，让部分类群保守估计体重超过 50 吨；体型上限仍受骨骼承重、食物网和资源可得性约束。Bird bones are thin, hollow and highly fused, the forelimb became a wing, and the sternum carries a keel for flight muscles; warm blood, high metabolism and an air-sac respiratory system sustain continuous flight. Parts of these traits already appeared in dinosaur ancestors and were layered on gradually rather than forming all at once. Microscopic feather structure can be distinguished from scale and hair in fossils. Gigantism was also supported by growth mode: sauropod bone histology shows fibrolamellar bone for fast, sustained growth, and an air-sac birdlike lung raised oxygen supply; the long neck, feeding and high-metabolism combination let some lineages exceed 50 tonnes by conservative estimates, while size ceilings still track skeletal load, food webs and resource availability.[1][5][8]

约 6600 万年前白垩纪末，小行星撞击叠加火山活动改变气候，非鸟恐龙和许多海洋、陆生类群灭绝。体型大、食物网受冲击的类群消失，小型、恒温、食性灵活的鸟类祖先存活，随后在新生代占领空缺生态位，发展出树栖、涉禽和猛禽等多种类型；灭绝后腾空的天空和陆地生态位由幸存鸟类、哺乳类和爬行类逐步填补。About 66 million years ago at the end of the Cretaceous, an asteroid impact combined with volcanic activity altered climate, and non-avian dinosaurs plus many marine and terrestrial groups went extinct. Large animals dependent on impacted food webs vanished, while small, warm-blooded, flexible-eating bird ancestors survived and occupied empty niches in the Cenozoic, giving rise to perching, wading and raptor types.[4][5]

鸟类与恐龙的亲缘关系有骨骼、化石和分子证据支持，但具体过渡节点和羽毛功能的早期解释仍在研究；不能把所有恐龙都想象成会飞的鸟类，多数兽脚恐龙没有飞行能力。灭绝原因的权重在撞击与火山活动之间仍有讨论，不同地层记录对事件节奏的判断也存在差异；羽毛是否最初用于保温、展示或其他功能，仍需更多带羽毛化石的生长结构证据。The bird-dinosaur relationship is supported by skeletal, fossil and molecular evidence, but specific transition nodes and early interpretations of feather function remain under study; not all dinosaurs should be imagined as flying birds, and most theropods could not fly. The relative weight of impact versus volcanism in the extinction remains debated, and stratigraphic records differ on the event's pace.[4][5]

03人类演化的主要谱系顺序是怎样的？脑容量与直立行走哪个先出现？What is the main order of human evolution, and which came first, bipedalism or brain expansion?

直立行走先于脑扩展；从南方古猿到直立人再到智人，谱系多次分叉并存。Bipedalism preceded brain expansion; from Australopithecus through H. erectus to sapiens, the tree branches and overlaps.

人类演化是一条多次分叉的谱系树，而不是直线从猴到现代人。南方古猿一类先获得双足直立行走，人属再扩展脑容量、制造工具；现代人属智人约在 30 万年前于非洲出现，之后扩散到其他大陆。谱系上同时存在过多个已灭绝支系，现代人只是其中延续至今的一支；不同支系在非洲和欧亚大陆先后出现、扩散，部分之间发生过接触和交流。Human evolution is a repeatedly branching tree rather than a straight line from ape to modern human. Australopithecus-like forms first acquired bipedal walking, and the genus Homo later expanded brain size and made tools; Homo sapiens appeared in Africa about 300,000 years ago and subsequently spread to other continents. Several extinct branches coexisted along the tree, and modern people are only the lineage that persists today.[1][6]

撒海尔人等约 700 万年前已显示早期直立相关迹象；南方古猿生活在约 400 万到 200 万年前，骨盆和足弓适应行走，脑容量仍接近猿类；直立人约 190 万年前出现，长距离行走、使用火和阿舍利工具，脑容量逐步增大。各时间节点来自化石测年，不同标本的归属和年代仍可能修订；化石年代通过火山灰测年和古地磁层位标定，标本破碎时常只能给出区间。Sahelanthropus and related forms show early bipedal-related signs about seven million years ago; Australopithecus lived roughly four to two million years ago, with pelvis and foot arches adapted to walking while brain size remained ape-like. Homo erectus appeared about 1.9 million years ago, with long-distance walking, fire use and Acheulean tools, and brain size grew gradually. Timeline nodes come from fossil dating, and the assignment and age of specimens may be revised.[6]

脑容量从早期人属约 350 毫升级别的猿脑，增长到现代人约 1350 毫升左右；工具制作、合作狩猎和食物加工与脑扩展互相促进。直立行走先于大脑扩展，骨盆受双足行走限制，与晚熟婴儿的解剖背景相关。脑容量之外，脑结构重组和神经连接方式同样影响认知，不能只按体积判断能力；工具类型从简单砍砸到标准化手斧，再到骨器和精细石器，与脑和手的配合同步演进。Brain volume grew from an early-Homo ape-like brain around 350 cc to the modern roughly 1,350 cc; tool making, cooperative hunting and food processing reinforced brain expansion. Bipedal walking preceded brain enlargement, and the pelvis constrained by two-legged walking relates to the anatomical background of altricial infants. Beyond volume, brain reorganization and neural connections affect cognition, so ability cannot be judged by size alone.[1][6]

人属历史上并存过多个物种或支系，如直立人、尼安德特人和早期智人，部分支系之间有基因交流；现代非洲以外人群基因组中保留少量古人类基因片段。智人不是从古猿直线直接变成，而是多分支演化后的一支幸存延续，杂交交流使不同支系的部分遗传成分并入现代人群；古 DNA 提取受保存条件限制，只在较晚近且环境适宜的标本中获得完整序列。Several species or branches coexisted in the genus Homo, such as H. erectus, Neanderthals and early sapiens, with genetic exchange between some branches; genomes of modern non-African populations retain small ancient-human segments. Sapiens did not arise in a straight line from apes but survived as one branch after multi-branch evolution, and admixture incorporated parts of other lineages into modern people.[4][6]

化石稀少且标本不完整，物种划分和亲缘关系随新发现调整；脑容量不能单独衡量智力，工具和文化证据需要与骨骼形态结合。不同地区化石的年代和归属存在争论，谱系图会随古 DNA 和新化石证据继续修改。把某一段化石直接当作现代人直系祖先，往往忽略了同期并存的其他支系；语言、艺术和工具文化的证据分散在考古遗存中，不能仅凭脑容量大小推断其出现时间。Fossils are scarce and incomplete, so species division and relationships adjust with new finds; brain size alone does not measure intelligence, and tool and cultural evidence must combine with skeletal anatomy. Dates and assignment of regional fossils remain debated, and the tree keeps changing with ancient DNA and new fossils. Treating any single fossil as the direct ancestor of modern humans often overlooks coexisting branches.[1][6]

04成对的眼睛独立演化过多少次？翅膀和蓝鲸体型为什么是趋同演化？How many times did paired eyes evolve independently, and why are wings and blue-whale size convergent?

眼与翅在远缘类群中多次独立改造；同源看共同祖先，同功看独立相似功能。Eyes and wings remodeled independently in distant groups; homology traces common ancestry, analogy independent similar function.

复杂结构在演化中可以多次独立起源，亲缘关系很远的类群面对相似环境压力时，会长出功能相近的结构，这是趋同演化；同源结构来自共同祖先，同功结构则是独立演化出的相似功能。判断依据是发育路径、解剖细节和基因组证据，而不是外形像不像；分子同源性可以追溯更深层的共同祖先，外形相似则可能只是后期适应的结果。Complex structures can originate independently many times in evolution; distantly related groups facing similar environmental pressures grow functionally similar structures, which is convergent evolution. Homologous structures come from a common ancestor, while analogous structures are similar functions evolved independently. The judgment rests on developmental path, anatomical detail and genomic evidence, not on superficial resemblance.[3][4]

脊椎动物和头足类的照相机眼都有透镜、视网膜和成像功能，但胚胎发育路径和神经排布不同，属于独立改造的相似方案。两侧对称的身体配合成对眼睛提供深度判断，眼点到透镜眼的复杂程度在不同谱系中多次升级，感光细胞的基础分子机制则有共同起源成分；眼点只感受明暗，透镜眼把光线聚焦到受体阵列，成像能力随神经处理同步升级。Vertebrate and cephalopod camera eyes both have a lens, retina and imaging function, but their embryonic development and nerve layout differ, making separately remodeled similar solutions. Bilateral bodies with paired eyes provide depth perception, and complexity from eyespots to lens eyes upgraded repeatedly across lineages, while the basic molecular machinery of photoreceptors carries a shared-origin component.[3]

昆虫翼、翼龙翼、鸟类翼和蝙蝠翼分别起源，骨骼来源和膜结构不同，都服务于升空与滑翔；趋同只要求功能相似，不要求结构细节相同。飞行在不同类群中利用空气动力学的同一套约束，却走出不同解剖路径，翅膀在昆虫是表皮外突，在脊椎动物是前肢改造；蝙蝠翼由指骨间皮膜展开，鸟翼由掌骨愈合覆羽构成，二者承重和气动面不同。Insect wings, pterosaur wings, bird wings and bat wings arose separately, with different skeletal sources and membrane structures, all serving lift and gliding; convergence requires similar function, not identical structural detail. Flight exploits the same aerodynamic constraints across groups but follows different anatomical paths: insect wings are epidermal outgrowths, vertebrate wings are forelimb remodelings.[3][5]

蓝鲸超过 30 米、上百吨，超过任何恐龙的体型；水生浮力支持体重，冷水中大型个体散热慢，滤食磷虾提供高密度能量。大型化在海洋哺乳动物中受食物网和代谢约束，与陆地恐龙的重量限制机制不同，陆地骨骼承重受重力直接约束，海洋环境把部分体重负担转嫁给浮力；须鲸的滤食板和巨大食量支撑体型，幼年生长速率直接受猎物密度季节变化影响。Blue whales exceed 30 meters and 100 tonnes, larger than any dinosaur; aquatic buoyancy supports body weight, large animals lose heat slowly in cold water, and krill filtering supplies high-density energy. Enlargement in marine mammals is bounded by food webs and metabolism, differently from the weight constraints of land dinosaurs, where bones bear gravity directly; water shifts part of the weight burden to buoyancy.[4][5]

判断同源还是同功需要比较解剖、发育和基因组证据，不能只按外形相似下结论；独立起源的结构在细节上保留各自祖先的痕迹。趋同演化不意味着朝向同一终点，相似功能可以由不同材料和路径实现，比较时还要区分基础分子机制的古老同源与后期结构的独立改造；比较结构时区分祖先遗留、功能改造和环境约束，才能避免把趋同误读为直系继承。Distinguishing homology from analogy requires comparative anatomy, development and genomic evidence, not resemblance alone; independently originated structures keep traces of their ancestors in detail. Convergence does not mean toward one endpoint, and similar functions can be achieved by different materials and paths. Comparisons must also separate ancient homology of basic molecular machinery from later independent remodeling of structures.[3][4]

05地球生命在何处产生、如何产生？最早的生命证据距今多久？Where and how did life arise on Earth, and how old is the earliest evidence?

最早化石约 35–38 亿年前；RNA 世界假说与膜区室逐步耦合代谢和复制。Earliest fossils about 3.5–3.8 Ga; RNA-world and membrane compartments couple metabolism and replication.

地球生命起源研究回答的是从无机分子到第一个能自我复制、代谢系统的过渡；目前最早的可靠生命化石证据约在 35 亿到 38 亿年前，而实验室尚未完整重演从原始地球条件到活细胞的全过程。起源研究面对的是无法直接重复的历史事件，结论需要地质、化学和实验证据互相支撑；已知生命都以细胞为基本单位，起源研究首先解释从分子集合到细胞系统的跨越。Research on life's origin addresses the transition from inorganic molecules to the first self-replicating, metabolizing system; the earliest reliable fossil evidence of life dates to about 3.5 to 3.8 billion years ago, and no lab has yet replayed the full path from early-Earth conditions to a living cell. Origin research deals with a historical event that cannot be directly repeated, so conclusions must rest on mutually supporting geological, chemical and experimental evidence.[1][7]

地球形成于约 45 亿年前，早期大气、海洋和火山活动提供碳、氮、水和能量；液态水是已知生命的必要条件。海洋表层、潮间带和深海热液喷口都被提出作为可能起始场所，热液喷口持续提供化学能和矿物催化表面，反应物在矿物孔隙中被局部浓缩；早期太阳光度较弱，火山排气和陨击补给物质，海洋在晚期重轰炸前后逐渐稳定。Earth formed about 4.5 billion years ago, and the early atmosphere, ocean and volcanic activity supplied carbon, nitrogen, water and energy; liquid water is a necessary condition for known life. Ocean surface, tidal flats and deep-sea hydrothermal vents have all been proposed as possible starting sites; vents continuously supply chemical energy and catalytic mineral surfaces, concentrating reactants in mineral pores.[7]

主流假说先形成有机单体，再聚合为能复制的分子；RNA 世界假说提出 RNA 同时承担遗传储存和催化功能，后来 DNA 承担储存、蛋白质承担催化。膜结构包裹反应体系，形成原始细胞区室，代谢与复制逐步耦合，自催化循环一旦形成，就能在边界内维持并放大反应；RNA 既能储存序列又具核酶活性，是连接遗传与催化的候选中间物。A mainstream hypothesis forms organic monomers first, then polymerizes them into replicating molecules; the RNA-world hypothesis proposes RNA carrying both genetic storage and catalysis, later replaced by DNA for storage and proteins for catalysis. Membranes enclose reaction systems into protocell compartments, and metabolism and replication gradually couple; once an autocatalytic cycle forms, it can persist and amplify reactions within a boundary.[7]

叠层石和古老沉积岩中的生物标志把生命存在推到太古代；实验室模拟原始大气可产生氨基酸等单体，矿物表面有助于寡聚反应。这些实验支持各步骤可行，但串联成完整细胞仍缺关键环节，从分子集合到可遗传、可代谢的生命体之间还有未解释的跳跃；古老沉积岩的碳同位素和矿物形态可以作为间接线索，但单独一项不足以定论。Stromatolites and biomarkers in ancient sedimentary rocks push life into the Archean; lab simulations of the early atmosphere produce amino-acid monomers, and mineral surfaces aid oligomerization. These experiments show individual steps are feasible, but the chain into a complete cell still lacks key links, and the jump from molecular assembly to a heritable, metabolizing organism remains unexplained.[4][7]

生命起源是历史事件，无法像现代实验那样重复观察；不同假说在起始场所、分子先后和能量来源上仍有竞争。科学解释限定在自然过程范围，证据不足时保留不确定性，新的地质和实验发现会继续修正模型。把假说中的某一步当成已经被实验完全证明的结论，会超出现有证据的支持范围；对地外环境的搜寻也以液态水、有机分子和能量梯度为参照，不把地球生命形式当作唯一可能。Life's origin is a historical event and cannot be observed repeatedly like a modern experiment; hypotheses still compete on starting site, molecular order and energy source. Scientific explanation stays within natural processes and retains uncertainty where evidence is insufficient, and new geological and experimental findings will revise models. Treating any hypothesized step as fully proven by experiment would overreach the support current evidence provides.[1][7]
