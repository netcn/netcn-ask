01人的心脏每天大约跳动多少次？泵血量由什么决定？About how many times does the human heart beat per day, and what sets pumping output?

静息心率约 60–100 次/分、每天约 10 万次；心输出量由心率与每搏输出量共同决定。Resting heart rate is about 60–100 bpm, near 100,000 beats a day; cardiac output is heart rate times stroke volume.

心脏是循环系统的动力泵，壁主要由心肌构成，通过节律性收缩把血液推送到全身。心脏位于胸腔纵隔内、两肺之间，大小约与本人拳头相近，心壁由心内膜、心肌层和心外膜构成，心肌层是收缩做功的主体。成人静息心率通常为每分钟 60–100 次，按此估算一天跳动约 10 万次；心率随年龄、运动状态和自主神经张力变化，儿童静息心率高于成人，长期训练者可能低于 60 次/分。The heart is the pump of the circulatory system, its wall mostly cardiac muscle, contracting rhythmically to push blood around the body. A resting adult heart rate is usually 60–100 beats per minute, roughly 100,000 beats per day; rate varies with age, exercise state and autonomic tone, and children rest at a higher rate than adults, while trained athletes may rest below 60 beats per minute.[6]

心脏泵血的输出由心率和每搏输出量共同决定：心输出量等于心率乘以每搏输出量。每搏输出量指一次心室收缩射出的血量，静息时约 60–80 mL；静息状态下成人每分心输出量约 5 L，运动时静脉回流增加，心肌初长度变化使每搏量上升，心率也加快，心输出量可升至数倍，以满足肌肉供氧和代谢废物排出的需要。Pump output is set by heart rate and stroke volume together: cardiac output equals heart rate times stroke volume. At rest an adult pumps about 5 L per minute; during exercise rate and stroke volume rise, and output can increase several-fold to supply working muscle and clear metabolic waste.[1][7]

结构上分工明确：心房接收回流血液并送入心室，心室收缩把血泵出；房室瓣和半月瓣防止血液倒流。瓣膜只能单向开启：房室瓣在心室舒张时开放、收缩时关闭，半月瓣在心室收缩时开放、舒张时关闭。右心把缺氧血送入肺循环充氧，左心把含氧血送入体循环，两条回路串联在同一颗心脏上，瓣膜开合保证血液只朝一个方向流动。Structure divides the work: atria receive returning blood and pass it to ventricles, which contract to eject it; atrioventricular and semilunar valves prevent backflow. The right heart sends deoxygenated blood through the pulmonary circuit for oxygenation, the left heart sends oxygenated blood through the systemic circuit, and the two loops are series-connected in one heart; valve opening and closing keep blood flowing one way.[6]

心率受自主神经和激素调节：交感神经兴奋加快心率、增强收缩，副交感（迷走）神经减慢心率；静息时迷走张力占优，心率低于内在起搏频率，应激时交感占优。肾上腺素、甲状腺素和体温变化也会改变心率；血压、血氧和体温信号经延髓心血管中枢整合后传出。运动、情绪、疼痛和体位变化都通过反射回路即时调整泵血节奏，使输出量与当下代谢需求匹配。Rate is regulated by autonomic nerves and hormones: sympathetic excitation speeds the heart and strengthens contraction, while parasympathetic vagal input slows it; adrenaline, thyroid hormone and temperature also shift rate. Exercise, emotion, pain and posture adjust pump rhythm through reflex loops in real time, matching output to current metabolic demand.[1][7]

边界要写清：心率快慢不等于心脏强弱。长期训练者静息心率偏低，主要是迷走张力高、每搏量较大，而不是心跳变慢就是虚弱。这里讲泵血节律与输出量，心电图诊断、瓣膜病变和心力衰竭属于临床话题，不在此展开。Boundary: heart rate alone does not measure cardiac strength. Trained athletes rest at a lower rate mainly because of high vagal tone and larger stroke volume; a slow resting heart is not weakness. This chapter covers pump rhythm and output; ECG diagnosis, valve disease and heart failure are clinical topics outside it. That is why a resting rate is always read together with stroke volume.[6]

02人身体里的血有什么用？全身有多少血液？What does blood do, and how much blood is in the body?

血量约占体重 7%–8%（约 5 L）；血浆运输缓冲，红细胞携氧，白细胞防御，血小板止血。Volume is 7–8% of body weight (about 5 L); plasma transports and buffers, red cells carry oxygen, white cells defend, platelets stop bleeding.

成人全身血量约占体重的 7%–8%，按 70 kg 体重估算约 5 L。血液在心血管系统中循环一周约需一分钟左右；动脉血与静脉血的成分差异主要在氧合程度，并不是两种不同液体。血液离心后分为两层：上层血浆约占 55%，下层血细胞约占 45%；血细胞包括红细胞、白细胞和血小板，各自承担不同任务。血液在封闭血管中循环，把各组织的需求与供给连接起来。Total adult blood volume is about 7–8% of body weight, roughly 5 L for a 70 kg person. Centrifuged blood separates into two layers: plasma about 55% on top and blood cells about 45% below; the cells are red blood cells, white blood cells and platelets, each with a different task. Blood circulates in closed vessels, connecting the supply and demand of every tissue.[1]

血浆以水为主体，含白蛋白等血浆蛋白、电解质、营养物质、激素、凝血因子和代谢废物。血浆中白蛋白含量最高，负责维持胶体渗透压和运输脂肪酸、激素等物质；球蛋白参与免疫，纤维蛋白原参与凝血。白蛋白维持胶体渗透压，凝血因子参与止血，血浆同时是运输与酸碱缓冲的载体；血浆本身为淡黄色，不是红色。Plasma is mostly water, with albumin and other plasma proteins, electrolytes, nutrients, hormones, clotting factors and waste products. Albumin maintains colloid osmotic pressure, clotting factors participate in hemostasis, and plasma acts as both transport vehicle and acid-base buffer; plasma itself is pale yellow, not red.[1][3]

血液的功能可以分成四类：运输氧气、二氧化碳、营养和激素，覆盖从肺部摄取氧气、肠道吸收营养，到把组织产生的二氧化碳、尿素等废物送往肺、肾排泄；白细胞与抗体参与免疫防御；血小板和凝血因子完成止血，在血管破损时优先启动；还通过循环分配热量、缓冲酸碱，维持内环境稳定。任何一类成分不足或异常，都会在对应功能上表现出来。Blood functions fall into four groups: transporting oxygen, carbon dioxide, nutrients and hormones; white cells and antibodies run immune defense; platelets and clotting factors stop bleeding; circulation also distributes heat and buffers acid-base balance to keep the internal environment stable. Deficiency or abnormality in any component shows up in its corresponding function.[1][3]

可核对的参考量级：成年男性红细胞约 4.8–5.4 百万/µL，女性略低；白细胞约 4–10 千/µL；血小板约 15–40 万/µL，具体范围随实验室标准而异。红细胞呈双凹圆盘状、无核，寿命约 120 天，衰老后在脾脏和肝脏被清除，骨髓持续生成新红细胞补充。红细胞数量最多，是血液呈红色的主要原因；白细胞和血小板在常规血涂片中所占比例很小。Checkable reference ranges: red cells run about 4.8–5.4 million per µL in adult men, slightly lower in women; white cells about 4–10 thousand per µL; platelets about 150,000–400,000 per µL, varying by laboratory standard. Red cells are by far the most numerous and are the main reason blood looks red; white cells and platelets occupy a small fraction of a routine smear.[1][2]

边界要写清：血液不是单纯的红色液体。血型由红细胞表面抗原决定，输血必须配型；这里不展开贫血、白血病等血液病分型，也不讨论献血适应证与血液成分分离。血液各项指标的临床解读需要结合检测参考范围和整体病情。Boundary: blood is not simply a red liquid. Blood type is set by antigens on red-cell surfaces, and transfusion requires matching; this chapter does not cover anemia or leukemia classification, donation eligibility or component separation. Clinical interpretation of blood counts needs the laboratory reference range and the overall condition. Reference ranges also shift with age, sex and altitude, so one number rarely settles a diagnosis.[1]

03血红蛋白怎样携带氧气？解离曲线为什么呈 S 形？血为什么是红色？How does hemoglobin carry oxygen, why is the dissociation curve S-shaped, and why is blood red?

四个血红素基团协同结合氧气形成 S 形曲线；波尔效应调节组织释氧，血红色来自含铁血红素的吸收光谱。Four heme groups bind oxygen cooperatively in an S-shaped curve; the Bohr effect adjusts tissue unloading, and color comes from iron heme absorption.

红细胞的主要功能分子是血红蛋白。每个血红蛋白分子由四个亚基组成四聚体，每个亚基结合一个血红素基团，基团中心的亚铁离子可以结合一分子氧气。血红蛋白在红细胞内高浓度存在，每克血红蛋白约可结合 1.34 mL 氧气；亚铁状态是结合氧的前提，铁被氧化为三价后携氧能力下降。成熟红细胞没有细胞核，内部空间几乎留给血红蛋白，以提高携氧容量。The main functional molecule of the red cell is hemoglobin. Each molecule is a four-subunit tetramer, and each subunit carries one heme group whose central ferrous ion binds one oxygen molecule. Each gram of hemoglobin carries about 1.34 mL of oxygen, and the ferrous state is required for binding; oxidized ferric heme loses this capacity. Mature red cells lack a nucleus, leaving almost all internal space for hemoglobin to raise oxygen capacity.[2][3]

氧结合具有协同效应：第一个氧气分子结合后，血红蛋白构象变化，其余位点对氧的亲和力提高。因此氧合—解离曲线呈 S 形：曲线下段对应低氧分压区域，上段接近平台；在肺部氧分压高处血红蛋白接近饱和，在组织氧分压低处逐步释放氧气，曲线中段对分压变化最敏感，少量分压下降就能释放较多氧气，这种形状使充氧和释氧都留有缓冲余地。Oxygen binding shows cooperativity: after the first oxygen binds, a conformational change raises affinity at the remaining sites. The oxygen-hemoglobin dissociation curve is therefore S-shaped: hemoglobin nears saturation at the high partial pressure in the lungs and releases oxygen stepwise at the low pressure in tissues, with the mid-curve most sensitive to pressure changes, so a small pressure drop releases a substantial amount of oxygen.[2][4]

波尔效应调节释放：组织代谢产生的低 pH、高二氧化碳和升温使曲线右移，血红蛋白在该处释放更多氧气；组织越活跃，CO₂ 越多、温度越高、pH 越低，右移越明显，释氧越充分，安静组织则曲线偏左、保留更多氧气。二氧化碳大部分以碳酸氢盐形式在血浆中运输，小部分与血红蛋白结合。氧气与二氧化碳的结合位点并不完全相同，两条运输路径互相配合。The Bohr effect adjusts release: low pH, high carbon dioxide and warmth in metabolizing tissue shift the curve right, so hemoglobin unloads more oxygen there. Most carbon dioxide travels in plasma as bicarbonate, with a smaller fraction bound to hemoglobin; oxygen and carbon dioxide do not share identical binding sites, and the two transport paths work together.[4]

血液颜色来自血红蛋白：亚铁血红素结合氧后吸收光谱改变，含氧血呈鲜红色，脱氧血呈暗红色；含氧与脱氧血红蛋白对红光和红外光的吸收差异，也是脉搏血氧仪通过透光率估算饱和度的物理基础。静脉血管在皮肤下看似蓝色，只是皮肤和血管壁对光线的散射效果，抽出体外的脱氧血实际为暗红色。Blood color comes from hemoglobin: oxygenated ferrous heme changes its absorption spectrum, so oxygenated blood is bright red and deoxygenated blood dark red. Veins look blue under the skin only because skin and vessel walls scatter light; deoxygenated blood drawn from a vein is actually dark red.[2]

边界要写清：一氧化碳与血红蛋白的亲和力远高于氧气，可置换结合位点造成组织缺氧；血红蛋白携氧能力受铁营养、海拔适应和红细胞数量影响。这里只讲携氧机制与曲线形状，贫血分型、血氧监测指标和一氧化碳中毒救治不在此展开。Boundary: carbon monoxide binds hemoglobin far more tightly than oxygen and can displace it, causing tissue hypoxia. Oxygen-carrying capacity depends on iron nutrition, altitude adaptation and red-cell count. This chapter covers the carrying mechanism and curve shape only; anemia classification, blood-oxygen metrics and carbon-monoxide poisoning treatment are outside it.[2][4]

04心脏为什么能一直跳？它的节律从哪里来？Why does the heart keep beating, and where does its rhythm come from?

窦房结起搏细胞自发产生动作电位，经房室结、希氏束、浦肯野纤维传遍心肌；长不应期保证完整收缩放松。SA-node pacemakers fire spontaneously through AV node, His bundle and Purkinje fibers to the myocardium; a long refractory period ensures full contraction and relaxation.

心脏能持续跳动，首先因为心肌具有自律性：窦房结中的起搏细胞不需要神经指令，就能自发产生动作电位，形成心跳的天然节律。起搏细胞的膜电位存在自发去极化：舒张期缓慢钠内流使电位升到阈电位，再触发动作电位，这一慢反应电位是自律性的离子基础。神经和激素只调节快慢强弱，并不替心脏发起每一次搏动；这也是离体心脏在合适条件下仍能维持一段时间节律的原因。The heart keeps beating because cardiac muscle is autorhythmic: pacemaker cells in the sinoatrial node generate action potentials spontaneously, without a nerve command, forming the natural heartbeat rhythm. Pacemaker cells show spontaneous diastolic depolarization: a slow sodium inward current raises the membrane potential to threshold before the next action potential. Nerves and hormones only adjust rate and strength; they do not initiate each beat, which is why an isolated heart can keep a rhythm for a time under suitable conditions.[5]

兴奋沿固定传导通路扩散：窦房结发出冲动，经结间束传到房室结，短暂延迟后经希氏束、左右束支传到浦肯野纤维，兴奋传遍整个心房和心室。房室结的延迟约 0.1 秒，让心房有时间把血液排空进入心室；束支沿室间隔下行，浦肯野纤维在心内膜下快速铺开兴奋，心肌按顺序收缩，先心房后心室，保证血液依次充盈和射出。Excitation spreads along a fixed conduction pathway: the sinoatrial node fires through internodal pathways to the AV node, after a brief delay down the bundle of His, bundle branches and Purkinje fibers, reaching the whole atria and ventricles so the myocardium contracts in sequence, atria before ventricles, ensuring filling and ejection in order.[5]

心肌细胞为横纹肌，细胞间通过闰盘电耦合，可同步收缩；闰盘含缝隙连接，离子可直接在细胞间流动，使心房肌和心室肌分别成为功能合胞体。心肌动作电位有一个长平台期，由钙离子内流与钾外流平衡维持，有效不应期也长，保证心肌完成一次完整收缩和放松后才接受下一次刺激，不会像骨骼肌那样发生强直收缩，从而留出充盈时间。Cardiac cells are striated and electrically coupled through intercalated discs, so they contract synchronously; gap junctions in the discs let ions pass directly between cells, making atrial and ventricular muscle each a functional syncytium. The cardiac action potential has a long plateau phase and a long effective refractory period, ensuring a full contraction and relaxation before the next stimulus; cardiac muscle cannot tetanize like skeletal muscle, which leaves time for filling.[5]

心肌之所以能长期节律工作，是因为细胞内线粒体密度高、以有氧代谢为主，能量供应持续；心肌细胞不能大量再生，成年后主要靠肥大适应，持续节律工作依赖冠状动脉供血，供氧中断会迅速影响起搏与收缩。胚胎发育第 21–22 天左右心脏就开始跳动并泵血。自主神经与激素在自律节律基础上微调心率和收缩强度，使泵血与身体需求匹配。The myocardium sustains this long-term rhythm because its cells are dense with mitochondria and rely mainly on aerobic metabolism, keeping energy supply continuous. The heart begins beating and pumping around embryonic day 21–22; autonomic nerves and hormones fine-tune rate and contraction strength on top of the intrinsic rhythm, matching pumping to body demand.[5][6]

边界要写清：自律性不等于不受调节。窦房结起搏异常或传导通路阻滞可导致心律失常，需要临床评估。这里讲心肌电活动与节律起源，不展开起搏器植入、心肌病治疗或心电图波形诊断。Boundary: autorhythmicity does not mean unregulated. Abnormal SA-node pacing or conduction block causes arrhythmias requiring clinical evaluation. This chapter covers cardiac electrical activity and rhythm origin; pacemaker implantation, cardiomyopathy treatment and ECG waveform diagnosis are outside it.[5][7]
