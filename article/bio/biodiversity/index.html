01生物多样性包含哪几个层次，它们之间是什么关系？What levels make up biodiversity, and how do they relate?

基因、物种、生态系统三层嵌套支撑；遗传变异是适应原材料，生境提供共存基础。Genetic, species and ecosystem levels nest and support one another; genetic variation adapts, habitat provides the coexistence base.

生物多样性不是单一数字，而是嵌套在三个尺度上的变异总和。基因多样性指同一物种内种群间和个体间的遗传变异，包括等位基因差异、种群结构和育种潜力；物种多样性指一个区域内物种的种类与数量；生态系统多样性指生境、群落类型和生态过程的多样。《生物多样性公约》把生物多样性定义为来自所有来源的生物体的变异性，包括陆地、海洋和其他水生生态系统及其构成的生态复合体。Biodiversity is not a single number but variation nested across three scales. Genetic diversity is the variation between populations and individuals within a species, including allelic differences, population structure and breeding potential. Species diversity is the variety and number of species in an area; ecosystem diversity covers habitat types, community types and ecological processes. The Convention on Biological Diversity defines biodiversity as the variability among living organisms from all sources, including terrestrial, marine and other aquatic ecosystems and the ecological complexes they form.[5]

三层尺度相互支撑。种内基因多样性是物种适应环境变化的原材料，种群遗传多样度低时，面对病害、气候波动的缓冲能力较弱；物种多样性构成群落的成分，不同物种占据不同功能位；生态系统多样性则提供物种共存的生境和物质循环过程。例如作物近缘野生种携带的抗病基因只能在物种内的遗传变异中找到，而这些物种又依赖完整的传粉者和水热条件才能存活。保护往往需要同时考虑三层，单一物种保护不能替代对生境和种群结构的维护。The three scales support one another. Within-species genetic diversity is the raw material for adaptation to environmental change; when a population is genetically depauperate, its buffer against disease and climate fluctuation is weaker. Species diversity forms the composition of a community, with different species occupying different functional roles, while ecosystem diversity provides the habitats and material cycles in which species coexist. Conservation usually has to address all three; protecting a single species cannot substitute for maintaining habitat and population structure.[1][5]

估计尺度要给区间。OpenStax 教材记载已描述命名的物种约在百万量级，估计现存物种总数在数百万到上千万之间，大量无脊椎动物、真菌和微生物仍未被分类。不同类群的认知深度差异很大：脊椎动物记录较完整，昆虫、线虫和微生物的描述比例低，因此已知物种数不等于现存总数。Estimates are given as ranges. OpenStax records that described, named species number in the millions, while estimates of the total living species run from several million to tens of millions, with many invertebrates, fungi and microbes still undescribed. Knowledge differs sharply by group: vertebrates are reasonably well documented, while insects, nematodes and microbes remain poorly described, so the count of known species is not the total that exists.[1]

多样性与人类收益的联系发生在不同层次。基因多样性支撑作物和家畜的育种资源；物种多样性提供授粉、捕食调节等生态功能；生态系统提供水分调节、土壤形成和碳储存。授粉、天敌害虫调节和水源涵养常由多个物种共同完成，单个关键种缺失可能影响整条功能链。OpenStax 47.2 把这些归为生物多样性对人类生活的价值，但价值评估本身受选择和度量方法影响。Links to human benefit operate at each level. Genetic diversity supports crop and livestock breeding resources; species diversity provides pollination and predation regulation; ecosystems supply water regulation, soil formation and carbon storage. OpenStax 47.2 groups these as the value of biodiversity to human life, though valuation itself depends on what is selected and how it is measured.[2]

边界：三层划分是分析框架，边界可以重叠；区域物种数受取样强度影响，不能把一次调查的清单当成完整名录。遗传多样性和生态系统功能的关系在不同群落中强度不同，不能用单一物种数量推断生态系统是否健康。The boundary: the three-level scheme is an analytical framework whose edges overlap. A regional species count depends on sampling effort, so one survey list cannot be treated as a complete catalog. The strength of the link between genetic diversity and ecosystem function varies across communities, and a single species count cannot indicate whether an ecosystem is healthy.[1][2]

02一个群落的物种多样性怎样定量度量？How is species diversity in a community measured quantitatively?

丰富度数物种，多度看个体分布，均匀度补足均衡程度；香农与辛普森指数侧重不同。Richness counts species, abundance tracks individuals, evenness balances the distribution; Shannon and Simpson indexes emphasize different aspects.

群落的物种多样性由两个维度构成：丰富度，即出现的物种数目；多度，即每个物种的个体数量或生物量。只数物种数会忽略均匀度——一个物种占绝大多数、其余寥寥的群落，与各物种数量相近的群落，丰富度可能相同但多样性不同。生物量度量多见于植物群落，动物群落常用个体数或捕获量，不同度量方式得到的均匀度不能直接互换。度量时需要把种类数和个体分布结合起来。Community species diversity has two dimensions: richness, the number of species present, and abundance, the number of individuals or biomass of each species. Counting only species misses evenness: a community dominated by one species with a few others can match another with similar numbers distributed evenly, yet differ in diversity. Measurement has to combine species count with the distribution of individuals.[4]

常用指数各有侧重。香农指数 H′ 基于各物种比例 p_i，H′ = −Σ p_i ln p_i，同时反映丰富度和均匀度，比例越均匀指数越高；辛普森指数衡量随机取两个个体属于不同物种的概率，对优势种更敏感。OpenStax 群落生态学部分用多度和丰富度描述群落结构，指数选择取决于研究问题。Common indexes emphasize different things. The Shannon index H′ uses each species proportion p_i: H′ = −Σ p_i ln p_i, reflecting both richness and evenness, and rising as proportions become more even. The Simpson index measures the probability that two randomly drawn individuals belong to different species and is more sensitive to dominant species. OpenStax community ecology describes community structure with abundance and richness, and the index chosen depends on the research question.[2][4]

调查方法决定数据可比。陆生植物常用样方固定面积计数，动物用样线、陷阱、网捕或影像记录；不同取样面积、时长和方法得到的物种数不能直接比较。样方面积要根据植物体型调整：森林用更大样方，草本用小样方；重复样方数量不足时，稀有类群容易被遗漏。稀有种检出概率低，常以累积物种曲线评估取样是否充分：继续增加调查努力，新物种增速放缓才说明名录接近完整。Survey methods decide whether data are comparable. Terrestrial plants are often counted in fixed-area quadrats, while animals use transects, traps, nets or image records; species counts from different areas, durations and methods cannot be compared directly. Rare species are less likely to be detected, so a species accumulation curve assesses sampling adequacy: as effort increases, the rate of new species slows when the catalog approaches completeness.[4]

分子和古生物资料补充度量。环境 DNA 可检出难以直接观察的物种，补充传统调查；化石记录用于比较地质时间尺度上的群落更替。这些方法各有偏差：eDNA 浓度受降解和扩散影响，只能指示存在与否的相对信号，不能给出绝对个体数；化石保存偏向外矿化程度高的类群，比较时还要注意地层年代和沉积环境一致，解释时要标注方法限制。Molecular and fossil evidence supplement measurement. Environmental DNA detects species hard to observe directly and complements traditional surveys; fossil records compare community turnover on geological timescales. Each method has bias: eDNA cannot give absolute individual counts, and fossil preservation favors groups with mineralized shells or skeletons, so method limits must be stated in interpretation.[1]

边界：指数是相对比较工具，跨生境比较时取样标准必须一致；高丰富度不等于高生态功能，均匀度和优势种角色也要看。指数数值受分类分辨率影响，把种下遗传变异并入物种多样性或反之，都会改变结论。The boundary: indexes are relative comparison tools, and cross-habitat comparison requires consistent sampling standards. High richness does not equal high ecological function; evenness and the role of dominants also matter. Index values depend on taxonomic resolution, and folding within-species genetic variation into species diversity, or the reverse, changes the conclusion.[2][4]

03多样性怎样随面积与纬度变化？物种-面积关系说明什么？How does diversity change with area and latitude, and what does the species-area relation show?

多样性从极地到赤道升高；更大面积记录更多物种，S = cA^z 可用于估算生境丧失后果，但属模型外推。Diversity rises from poles to equator; larger areas hold more species, S = cA^z, used to estimate habitat-loss consequences as model extrapolation.

多样性在空间上并非均匀分布。最突出的格局是纬度梯度：从极地到赤道，多数类群的物种数增加，热带地区的物种丰富度显著高于温带和寒带。鸟类、乔木等多数类群沿纬度递增，深海和某些寄生类群则不遵循这一趋势；热带既保留古老类群，也有较高的新近种化。解释包括能量与水分供给、生境复杂性、演化时间和种化速率等假说，但格局的成因仍是多个因素共同作用，单一假说难以解释所有类群。Diversity is not spread evenly across space. The clearest pattern is the latitudinal gradient: from poles to equator, species counts rise for most groups, and tropical richness is markedly higher than temperate and boreal levels. Explanations include energy and water supply, habitat complexity, evolutionary time and speciation rate, but the pattern arises from several factors together; no single hypothesis fits every group.[1]

面积是另一个关键变量。物种-面积关系描述在更大区域内记录到更多物种，常见形式为 S = cA^z，S 为物种数、A 为面积、c 和 z 为常数，双对数坐标近似直线。岛屿大小、生境斑块面积与物种数的关系被用来推断栖息地丧失的后果：OpenStax 教材记载，按物种-面积关系估算约 90% 生境丧失可能对应约 50% 物种走向灭绝，这是模型估计而非直接观测。Area is the other key variable. The species-area relationship records that larger areas hold more species, commonly written S = cA^z, where S is species count, A is area, and c and z are constants; on log-log axes the relation approximates a straight line. The link between island or patch size and species count is used to infer consequences of habitat loss: OpenStax notes that species-area estimates suggest about 90 percent habitat loss could correspond to roughly half of species heading toward extinction. This is a model estimate, not a direct observation.[1][2]

热点区域是保护优先级概念。生物多样性热点指物种丰富度高且特有比例大、同时栖息地受威胁严重的区域；特有种只分布在限定区域，一旦当地生境破坏，其全球种群即受影响。热点方法侧重集中保护单位，但也有批评指出它可能低估广阔但物种中等丰富的生态系统价值。Hotspots are a conservation-priority concept. Biodiversity hotspots are areas of high richness and high endemic proportion where habitat is also severely threatened. Endemic species occur only in a restricted region, so destroying that local habitat affects their global population. The hotspot approach concentrates protection units, though critics note it can undervalue broad ecosystems of moderate richness.[1]

地形、隔离和演替阶段叠加在梯度之上。山地海拔梯度、岛屿隔离、生境连续性都会改变局部多样性；岛屿上物种数同时受面积和隔离距离影响，大陆物种库通过扩散决定岛屿能定居哪些类群。演替早期物种少、以先锋种为主，成熟群落种类增多。OpenStax 群落生态学把这些格局与竞争、扩散和环境过滤联系起来，局部观察需要放在区域物种库的背景下解释。Topography, isolation and successional stage overlay the gradient. Elevation gradients on mountains, island isolation and habitat continuity all alter local diversity; early succession has few species dominated by pioneers, while mature communities hold more species. OpenStax community ecology ties these patterns to competition, dispersal and environmental filtering, and local observations must be read against the regional species pool.[4]

边界：纬度趋势在多数类群成立，但存在海洋、寄生生物等例外；物种-面积曲线的 z 值随类群和空间尺度变化，外推到未来灭绝速率有不确定性。热点划分是人为优先级框架，不等同于其他区域没有保护价值。The boundary: the latitudinal trend holds for most groups, with exceptions in marine and parasitic taxa. The z value of the species-area curve varies by group and spatial scale, and extrapolation to future extinction rates carries uncertainty. Hotspot designation is a human priority framework, not a statement that other areas lack conservation value.[1][2]

04当前灭绝速率怎样估计，哪些因素驱动多样性丧失？How are current extinction rates estimated, and what drives diversity loss?

生境破碎、入侵种、过度利用、污染与气候变化驱动丧失；速率以化石背景、名录评估和模型三类证据分别估计。Fragmentation, invasives, overexploitation, pollution and climate drive loss; rates are estimated from fossil background, Red List assessment and models separately.

多样性丧失由若干可识别的威胁驱动。栖息地丧失和破碎化居首：森林砍伐、湿地填埋、城市和农田扩张把连续生境切成隔离斑块，斑块边缘比例升高，小种群近交和随机波动风险加大；外来入侵种常随贸易和旅行被带入缺乏天敌的群落，改变本地群落，过度利用、污染和气候变化叠加影响。OpenStax 47.3 把生境丧失、入侵种、过度采集、污染和新兴疾病列为主要威胁。Diversity loss is driven by identifiable threats. Habitat loss and fragmentation come first: deforestation, wetland filling, urban and agricultural expansion split continuous habitat into isolated patches. Invasive species alter local communities, and overexploitation, pollution and climate change compound the pressure. OpenStax 47.3 lists habitat loss, invasive species, overharvesting, pollution and emerging disease as the main threats.[3]

灭绝速率用不同基准估计。背景灭绝速率来自化石记录，量级约为每百万物种年 1 次上下；现代估计常以物种-面积关系、IUCN 红色名录受威胁比例和模型推算，给出的当前速率显著高于背景值，但具体数值区间因方法而异，不同估计给出的数字相差数倍。IPBES 评估强调趋势方向和受威胁类群，而非单一精确速率；具体速率引用时要标注估计方法。Extinction rates are estimated against different baselines. The background extinction rate comes from the fossil record, on the order of about one extinction per million species-years; modern estimates use species-area relations, the threatened share of the IUCN Red List and models, and place the current rate well above background, though the exact range varies with method. IPBES assessments report declining trends in natural ecosystems and species worldwide, and any quoted rate should carry its estimation method.[6][7]

IUCN 红色名录按评估标准把物种分为无危、近危、易危、濒危、极危、野外灭绝和灭绝等等级，依据种群规模、下降速率、分布范围和碎片化程度。名录是状态评估工具，不等同于法律保护名单；一个物种被列为受威胁，说明评估的风险水平，而非必然即将灭绝。The IUCN Red List classifies species through criteria into least concern, near threatened, vulnerable, endangered, critically endangered, extinct in the wild and extinct, based on population size, decline rate, range and fragmentation. The list is a status assessment tool, not a legal protection roster; a threatened listing means assessed risk level, not that extinction is imminent.[7]

保护在多个层级展开。就地保护保护栖息地和种群，迁地保护如动物园、种子库作为补充；迁地种群通常只保存部分遗传多样性，重新引入还需要合适的栖息地和种群规模，廊道能提高斑块间个体交流。保护区面积、廊道连通性和管理有效性决定保护成效。OpenStax 记载全球约一成多陆地以某种形式受保护，但单纯面积数字不代表生境质量和连通性。Conservation unfolds at several levels. In situ conservation protects habitats and populations, while ex situ measures such as zoos and seed banks supplement it. Reserve area, corridor connectivity and management effectiveness determine outcome. OpenStax records that a little over ten percent of land is protected in some form globally, but area alone says nothing about habitat quality or connectivity.[3][5]

边界：灭绝确认需要在长期调查后判定，未观测到不等于存活；受威胁比例不能直接换算成未来灭绝时间。模型估计依赖假设，不同研究给出的速率差异较大，引用时应区分化石背景、名录评估和模型推算三类证据。The boundary: extinction is confirmed only after long surveys, and absence of observation is not survival. A threatened share cannot be converted directly into a future extinction date. Model estimates depend on assumptions, and studies differ widely, so citations should separate three kinds of evidence: fossil background, Red List assessment and model projection.[6][7]
