01DNA 是什么？基因和基因组在结构上怎样分工，人类基因组有多大？What is DNA, how do genes and the genome divide structural roles, and how large is the human genome?

核苷酸配对成双螺旋，23 对染色体承载约 32 亿碱基对和约 2 万个编码基因。Nucleotides pair into a double helix; 23 chromosome pairs carry about 3.2 billion base pairs and 20,000 coding genes.

DNA 是携带遗传信息的大分子，基因是 DNA 上编码蛋白质或功能 RNA 的片段；一个物种的整套遗传物质称为基因组。人类基因组约 32 亿个碱基对，分布在 23 对染色体上，其中蛋白质编码基因约 2 万个。OpenStax 把核苷酸描述为 DNA 的基本构件：一分子脱氧核糖、一分子磷酸，以及四种含氮碱基之一，即 A、T、C、G。基因可以编码蛋白质，也可以只产生功能 RNA，而基因组包含全部染色体上的 DNA 序列。DNA is the large molecule that carries genetic information, and a gene is a segment of DNA that codes for a protein or a functional RNA; the complete set of an organism's hereditary material is its genome. The human genome contains about 3.2 billion base pairs arranged on 23 pairs of chromosomes, with roughly 20,000 protein-coding genes. OpenStax describes the nucleotide as the basic building block: one deoxyribose sugar, one phosphate group, and one of four nitrogenous bases, A, T, C and G.[1]

双链由碱基互补配对维系，A 配 T、C 配 G，两条链方向相反并盘绕成双螺旋。复制时双链解开，每条链按互补规则合成新链，因此两个子代 DNA 各保留一条母链。转录把基因信息抄成 RNA，再翻译为蛋白质。遗传信息的基本方向是 DNA 到 RNA 再到蛋白质，碱基排列顺序就是编码所用的字母表，碱基的增减或替换会改变对应的遗传指令。The two strands are held together by complementary base pairing: A pairs with T and C pairs with G, and the strands run in opposite directions in a double helix. During replication the strands separate, and each serves as a template for a new complementary strand. Transcription copies a gene into RNA, which is then translated into protein. The basic flow of information runs from DNA to RNA to protein, and the order of bases forms the coding alphabet.[1][2]

人体每个体细胞都携带同一套基因组，尽管不同细胞开启的基因并不相同。同一套序列通过选择性表达分化出肌肉、神经、上皮和结缔组织等类型。测序数据显示，人类蛋白质编码序列只占基因组很小一部分，绝大部分 DNA 不直接编码蛋白质；NHGRI 的基因组词条把染色体、基因与非编码序列作为整体加以说明，而不是把基因等同于整条染色体。Nearly every somatic cell in the body carries the same genome, although different cells switch on different subsets of genes. Sequencing shows that protein-coding sequences occupy only a small fraction of the genome, while most DNA does not code directly for protein. The NHGRI genome glossary treats chromosomes, genes and noncoding sequences as one system rather than equating a gene with an entire chromosome.[2][3]

DNA 负责稳定储存信息，RNA 承担信使、转运和催化等中间角色。基因先转录为信使 RNA，再由核糖体按密码子装配氨基酸；信使 RNA 携带密码子，转运 RNA 搬运氨基酸，核糖体 RNA 参与催化肽键形成。少数基因的产物本身就是 RNA，不翻译成蛋白质。DNA 与 RNA 在糖基、碱基和单双链上的差别，对应储存与表达两种不同分工。DNA stores information stably, while RNA handles messenger, transfer and catalytic intermediate roles. A gene is first transcribed into messenger RNA, and the ribosome then assembles amino acids according to the codons; a minority of genes produce RNA as their final product and are never translated. The differences in sugar, bases and strand structure between DNA and RNA match their separate jobs of storage and expression.[1]

碱基对数和基因数都是随注释版本调整的近似口径，测序技术和研究进展更新后，基因边界与数量会有小幅调整。基因数量少并不代表信息量低，非编码序列、调控区域和剪接方式共同扩展了可读信息。也不能把 DNA 直接等同于性格或智力：表型由基因、发育环境与随机因素共同决定，序列只是其中一个环节。Base-pair and gene counts are approximate figures that shift with annotation versions. A small gene count does not mean low information content, because noncoding sequence, regulatory regions and splicing all expand what can be read out. DNA cannot be equated directly with personality or intelligence: phenotype arises from genes, developmental environment and random factors acting together, and the sequence is only one part of that process.[2][3]

02为什么人类基因只有约两万个？编码基因为什么只占基因组约 2%？Why do humans have only about 20,000 genes, and why do coding genes make up only about 2% of the genome?

可变剪接让一个基因产出多种蛋白，调控网络补偿了有限的基因数量。Alternative splicing yields multiple proteins per gene, and regulatory networks compensate for the limited gene count.

人类只有约 2 万个蛋白质编码基因，与线虫等结构简单的生物相差不大，而编码序列只占基因组约 1 到 2%。基因数量少并不代表生物复杂度低，关键在于剪接、调控和非编码 RNA 把有限的基因读出多种产物，复杂度主要体现在调控层级和产物多样性上，而不是基因条数本身。Humans have only about 20,000 protein-coding genes, not far more than simple animals such as nematodes, and coding sequence makes up only about 1 to 2 percent of the genome. A small number of genes does not mean low biological complexity. Splicing, regulation and noncoding RNA read many products out of a limited gene set, so complexity sits mainly in regulatory layers and product diversity rather than in gene count.[2][3]

多数真核基因由外显子和内含子相间组成；转录后内含子被剪接去除，外显子按不同组合连接。可变剪接使同一基因产生多种信使 RNA，翻译出结构不同的蛋白质，这些亚型在组织分布、结合对象和活性上可以有明显差别。综述文献估计，人类多外显子基因中超过九成发生可变剪接，一个基因对应多个蛋白亚型是常态，而不是例外。Most eukaryotic genes alternate exons with introns. After transcription the introns are spliced out, and the exons are joined in different combinations. Alternative splicing lets one gene produce several messenger RNAs that are translated into structurally different proteins. Review literature estimates that more than 90 percent of human multi-exon genes undergo alternative splicing, so one gene yielding multiple protein isoforms is the norm rather than an exception.[4]

基因组测序显示，约 98% 的人类 DNA 不编码蛋白质，其中包括内含子、重复序列、转座子残留和调控片段。增强子、启动子和非编码 RNA 决定基因在何时、何地、以多大强度表达，这套调控网络的复杂度在很大程度上补偿了基因数的有限，使有限的基因可以支撑高度分化的组织，也让同一套基因组在不同细胞中读出不同程序；不同组织开启的增强子组合不同，因而分化出多种器官类型。Genome sequencing shows that about 98 percent of human DNA does not code for protein, including introns, repeats, transposon remnants and regulatory segments. Enhancers, promoters and noncoding RNA determine when, where and how strongly a gene is expressed; this regulatory network largely compensates for the limited number of genes, allowing a small set of genes to support highly differentiated tissues.[3][4]

早期按基因数量推断生物复杂度的做法，忽略了剪接多样性和调控层级。一个基因并不对应一个性状，多数复杂表型由多个基因协同影响；非编码区也不是全部无效的垃圾，其中相当一部分参与调控或维持染色体结构，只是具体功能需要逐项实验验证，不能凭序列长短下结论。Early attempts to infer complexity from gene count overlooked splicing diversity and regulatory layers. One gene does not map to one trait, and most complex phenotypes are shaped by several genes acting together. Nor is the noncoding region all useless junk: a substantial part participates in regulation or maintains chromosome structure, although each function still needs experimental confirmation.[2][4]

可变剪接的比例和功能重要性在不同组织与物种之间存在差别，部分剪接产物可能是随机噪声，而不是稳定功能产物。编码区占比是基因组注释口径下的估计值，不能据此给每一段非编码序列逐一判定功能；哪些片段具备选择保留下来的作用，仍要靠进化比较和实验证据来确认，把非编码区一概称为垃圾基因并不准确。The proportion and functional importance of alternative splicing differ across tissues and species, and some splice products may be random noise rather than stable functional outputs. The coding fraction is an estimate under a particular genome annotation scheme, so it cannot assign a function to every noncoding segment. Which fragments have been retained by selection still rests on comparative evolutionary analysis and experimental evidence.[3][4]

03遗传变异怎样影响健康和表型？同卵双胞胎 DNA 相同，为什么并不完全一样？How does genetic variation affect health and phenotype, and why are identical twins not perfectly identical despite shared DNA?

突变与重组产生变异，表观修饰调节表达；同卵双胞胎仍受环境与体细胞差异影响。Mutation and recombination generate variation, epigenetics tunes expression; twins still differ through environment and somatic divergence.

遗传变异是种群内个体之间 DNA 序列的差异，来源包括复制错误产生的突变、有性生殖中的重组，以及染色体水平的结构变化。任何两个无关人类个体之间，约每 1000 个碱基对有 1 处差异，全基因组累计约数百万个单核苷酸变异；NHGRI 把变异定义为种群内序列差异的统称，而不限于致病突变，既包括罕见的新突变，也包括群体中常见的多态位点。Genetic variation is the difference in DNA sequence among individuals in a population, arising from mutation during replication, recombination in sexual reproduction and structural changes at the chromosome level. Between any two unrelated humans, about one base pair in a thousand differs, amounting to several million single-nucleotide variants across the genome. NHGRI defines variation broadly as sequence differences within a population, not only disease-causing mutations.[5]

变异经遗传传递，在基因型与表型之间形成多层映射。单个基因的不同等位基因可能影响酶活性、受体结构或代谢通路；多数性状由多个基因加上环境因素共同塑造。DNA 甲基化等表观修饰不改变碱基序列，却可以改变基因表达强度，使相同的序列在不同状态下读出不同的活性，这类标记还可以在细胞分裂中部分传递。Variation is inherited and forms a multilayer mapping between genotype and phenotype. Different alleles of a single gene may alter enzyme activity, receptor structure or a metabolic pathway, while most traits are shaped by several genes together with environmental factors. Epigenetic marks such as DNA methylation do not change the base sequence but can change expression level, so the same sequence can read out different activity under different states.[1][5]

同卵双胞胎来自同一受精卵，初始基因组几乎一致，但出生后积累的体细胞突变、不同的发育环境和表观修饰，会使他们在性状上出现可观察到的差异。DNA 相同并不保证表型完全相同，序列是发育的起点，而不是决定一切的答案；随年龄增长，个体细胞之间的体细胞差异也会逐步累积，外界饮食、活动和疾病经历同样参与塑造最终状态。Identical twins come from one fertilized egg and begin with nearly the same genome, but somatic mutations accumulated after birth, differing developmental environments and epigenetic marks produce observable differences in their traits. Shared DNA does not guarantee an identical phenotype; the sequence is the starting point of development rather than a complete answer, and somatic differences keep accumulating between cells as people age.[1][5]

长期近交提高了有害隐性基因纯合的概率，使遗传负荷更容易表现为健康问题；停止近交可以通过引入新的等位基因降低纯合风险，但不能清除已经存在于种群中的变异。近交改变的是基因型组合的频率，而不是删除变异本身；已有的隐性有害位点会在种群基因库中长期保留，需要多代选择才可能逐步降低其频率。Long-term inbreeding raises the chance that harmful recessive alleles become homozygous, making genetic load easier to express as health problems. Stopping inbreeding lowers homozygous risk by introducing new alleles, but it cannot remove variation already present in the population. It changes the frequency of genotype combinations rather than deleting the variants themselves, and existing recessive harmful sites remain in the gene pool.[3][5]

变异与健康的关联要区分相关与因果，许多常见变异只小幅提高风险，并不直接导致疾病。表观遗传标记在不同组织之间并不稳定，不能把个体差异全部归因于基因。表型是基因型、环境与随机发育过程共同作用的结果，单一变异的效应通常需要在群体数据和实验验证中反复核对，单凭一项关联研究不足以判定因果。When linking variation to health, correlation must be separated from causation; many common variants raise risk only slightly and do not directly cause disease. Epigenetic marks are not stable across tissues, so individual differences cannot be blamed on genes alone. Phenotype emerges from genotype, environment and random developmental processes together, and the effect of any single variant needs repeated checking against population data and experimental validation.[1][5]

04单个植物体细胞怎样长成整株植物？动物克隆和体细胞核移植是怎样实现的？How can a single plant somatic cell grow into a whole plant, and how does animal cloning or somatic cell nuclear transfer work?

植物细胞保留全能性；动物核移植需卵母细胞重编程，端粒与重编程不完全构成边界。Plant cells retain totipotency; animal transfer needs oocyte reprogramming, with telomeres and incomplete reprogramming as limits.

植物的已分化体细胞在适宜条件下可以重新发育为完整植株，这体现细胞的全能性；动物的体细胞核则需要移入去核卵母细胞，经过重编程才可能启动胚胎发育，即体细胞核移植。NHGRI 的克隆词条区分了分子克隆、细胞克隆与个体克隆，三者对象不同，不能混为一谈；日常所说的动物克隆，指的是由体细胞核发育出遗传上基本一致的个体。A differentiated plant cell can, under suitable conditions, develop again into a whole plant, which shows cellular totipotency. An animal somatic nucleus, by contrast, must be transferred into an enucleated oocyte and reprogrammed before it can initiate embryonic development; this is somatic cell nuclear transfer. The NHGRI cloning glossary distinguishes molecular cloning, cell cloning and organism cloning, which should not be conflated.[6]

植物细胞保留整套基因组，在激素和培养条件下脱分化，再分化出根、茎和叶；动物体细胞核携带全套遗传信息，但分化状态由表观标记维持，卵细胞胞质可以部分抹除这些标记，使细胞核重回胚胎程序。重编程是否彻底，直接影响后续胚胎能否正常发育，已经分化的细胞记忆不会被完全擦除。Plant cells retain the full genome and, under hormone and culture conditions, dedifferentiate before redifferentiating into roots, stems and leaves. An animal somatic nucleus carries the full genetic information, but its differentiated state is maintained by epigenetic marks; the egg cytoplasm can partly erase those marks and return the nucleus to an embryonic program. How completely reprogramming proceeds directly affects whether the embryo develops normally.[1][6]

多莉羊是成年乳腺细胞核移植的著名案例；克隆胚胎的妊娠成功率低，许多个体在胚胎期或出生后夭折，流产、体重异常和器官发育问题都有报道。供体年龄与端粒长度相关：端粒随细胞分裂逐步缩短，老年供体的核可能携带较短的端粒，但克隆个体的端粒长度并非简单复制供体状态，胚胎期的端粒重建会部分补偿这一差异。Dolly the sheep is the famous case of nuclear transfer from an adult mammary-gland cell. Pregnancy success in cloning is low, and many embryos fail before birth or die shortly after. Donor age relates to telomere length: telomeres shorten with each cell division, so an older donor nucleus may carry shorter telomeres, but a clone's telomere length does not simply copy the donor; telomere rebuilding in the embryo partly compensates for this difference.[6][7]

接受器官移植后，受体自身细胞的 DNA 不变，移植器官的细胞携带供体基因型；在血液等样本中可能检测到供体来源的细胞或游离 DNA，这属于嵌合状态，并不改变受体生殖细胞的遗传组成。器官移植带来的供体细胞主要定居在移植器官及其局部循环中，长期免疫抑制下也可能有少量供体细胞迁移到其他组织。After an organ transplant, the recipient's own cells keep their original DNA, while the transplanted organ's cells carry the donor genotype. Donor-derived cells or cell-free DNA may be detected in blood samples, a state of chimerism that does not alter the recipient's germline genetic makeup. Donor cells introduced by transplantation settle mainly in the graft and its local circulation.[1][6]

植物全能性不等于动物体细胞都能直接长成个体；核移植成功率受供体细胞类型、卵母细胞质量与培养条件影响。克隆涉及表观重编程不完全、发育异常等风险，不能把克隆理解为对供体的精确复制。同一套基因组在不同细胞质和发育环境中，可以走出并不完全相同的发育路径，表型仍受环境和随机因素调节。Plant totipotency does not mean every animal somatic cell can grow directly into an individual; nuclear-transfer success depends on donor cell type, oocyte quality and culture conditions. Cloning carries risks of incomplete epigenetic reprogramming and developmental abnormalities, so it cannot be treated as an exact copy of the donor. The same genome, in a different cytoplasm and developmental environment, can follow a somewhat different developmental path.[6][7]

05人类的遗传多样性在动物界算高还是低？哪些基因改变造就了独特的人类性状？Is human genetic diversity high or low compared with other animals, and which genetic changes underlie distinctive human traits?

人类种内多样性偏低，外貌差异位点有限；人属性状由少数基因与文化共同塑造。Within-species diversity is low, visible traits map to few loci; human traits arise from a few genes plus culture.

与黑猩猩、大猩猩等近缘物种相比，人类种群内的遗传多样性偏低。当前所有人类属于同一物种，任意两个人的遗传相似度约 99.9%；种群分化时间较短，加上历史上的种群扩张与瓶颈效应，使群体内变异总量有限，个体之间的序列差异集中在少数位点上，整体基因库仍然连续。Compared with close relatives such as chimpanzees and gorillas, humans show relatively low genetic diversity within the species. All living humans belong to one species, and any two people are about 99.9 percent genetically similar. A short time since population divergence, together with past expansion and bottleneck effects, has kept within-group variation limited, with sequence differences concentrated at a minority of sites.[2][5]

遗传多样性由突变累积、种群大小、迁移和选择共同决定。小种群中遗传漂变更强，稀有等位基因更容易随机丢失；人类走出非洲后的奠基效应和历次瓶颈，降低了部分地区种群的等位基因丰富度。大种群中选择和重组的作用更充分，稀有变异也更容易被保留下来，长期地理交流则使不同地区种群之间的差异被持续抹平。Genetic diversity is shaped by mutation accumulation, population size, migration and selection acting together. In small populations genetic drift is stronger, and rare alleles are more likely to be lost at random; the out-of-Africa founder effect and later bottlenecks reduced allelic richness in some regional populations. In large populations selection and recombination act more fully, and rare variants are easier to retain.[5]

使人类区别于近缘猿类的性状，如直立行走、脑容量扩展和语言相关能力，对应少数基因在调控与编码上的改变，但不存在单一基因定义人属。FOXP2 等基因与发声运动相关，相关性状仍是多基因、发育与文化共同作用的结果，不能把某项能力简化为某个基因的产物，文化学习和社会交流同样塑造了这些能力的表达，相关基因的作用通常是调节发育过程，而不是单独决定某项行为。Traits that distinguish humans from close apes, such as bipedal walking, expanded brain size and language-related abilities, correspond to changes in a small number of genes at both regulatory and coding levels, but no single gene defines the human genus. Genes such as FOXP2 relate to vocal-motor control, yet the traits remain products of many genes, development and culture together; no ability can be reduced to one gene.[2][3]

多样性比较要看比较对象：种内多样性与种间差异不能混为一谈。人类外观差异，如肤色和身高，对应的遗传位点有限，远小于物种内整体变异；表面差异并不对应深层的种群遗传划分，地理上相邻的人群之间基因交流连续，连续渐变的分布比离散分组更符合实际数据。Diversity comparisons depend on the comparison being made: within-species diversity must not be mixed up with between-species differences. Visible human differences such as skin color and height correspond to a limited number of loci, far smaller than overall variation within the species. Surface differences do not map onto deep genetic divisions between populations, and gene flow between neighboring groups has been continuous.[3][5]

低多样性是相对其他灵长类的量级比较，不能据此否认个体之间存在的真实差异。基因研究支持人类连续的地理渐变分布，不支持把人群划分为本质不同的遗传类别。任何社会文化差异都不能由遗传多样性数据直接推出；遗传相似性描述的是生物学历史，不构成对人群价值或能力的判断依据，这一边界在引用基因数据时必须保留。Low diversity is a magnitude comparison against other primates and does not erase individual differences. Genetic research supports a continuous geographic gradient in humans rather than essentially distinct genetic categories. No social or cultural difference can be inferred directly from genetic diversity figures; genetic similarity describes biological history and carries no judgment about the value or ability of any group.[2][5]
