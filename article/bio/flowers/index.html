01花为什么有颜色和香味？低浓度的花香为什么也能被闻到？Why do flowers have colors and scent, and why can faint floral odor be detected at low concentration?

花被色素决定可见色彩，挥发性萜类与苯丙素提供嗅觉定向；两类信号与传粉者偏好协同演化。Perianth pigments provide visible color, while volatile terpenes and phenylpropanoids provide scent; both signals coevolve with pollinator preferences.

花的颜色和香气是一套给传粉者的信号系统：花被色素决定远距离可见的色彩，挥发性有机物在近距离和夜间补充嗅觉定向，两者共同提高传粉者找到花朵、携带花粉离开的概率。颜色主要由花瓣表皮细胞中的色素决定，香气则由花瓣和花托等部位释放的小分子挥发物构成，二者来源不同但功能上配合。Flower color and scent form a signal system aimed at pollinators: perianth pigments provide visible color at distance, while volatile organic compounds add olfactory orientation up close and at night. Together they raise the chance that a pollinator finds the flower and carries pollen away. Color comes mainly from pigments in petal epidermal cells, and scent from small volatile molecules released by petals and floral tissues; the two have different sources but work together.[1]

花瓣色素主要分两类。花青素是溶于细胞液泡的黄酮类色素，随液泡 pH 不同呈现红、紫、蓝等色调；类胡萝卜素是脂溶性色素，呈现黄、橙、红色。花青素还常与辅助色素、金属离子形成复合色素，使蓝色花的色调更稳定。花瓣表皮细胞的形状和蜡质层还会改变光线反射，部分花在昆虫可见的紫外波段形成纹路，引导传粉者落到花蜜位置。Petal pigments fall into two main groups. Anthocyanins are water-soluble flavonoids in the cell vacuole and shift between red, purple and blue with vacuolar pH; carotenoids are lipid-soluble pigments giving yellow, orange and red. Epidermal cell shape and the waxy cuticle also alter light reflection, and some flowers form patterns in the ultraviolet band visible to insects that guide visitors toward nectar.[1]

花香来自挥发性有机化合物。常见类别包括单萜类（如芳樟醇）和苯丙素、苯环类化合物；这些分子分子量小、容易从花瓣扩散到空气中。挥发物释放还受温度和昼夜节律调节，夜间开花的种类在傍晚到夜间释放量最高。昆虫和哺乳动物的嗅觉受体在很低浓度下就能识别特定气味分子，因此人也能在花香物质浓度不高时闻到气味；不同花卉的挥发物组成不同，形成各自的香气特征。Floral scent comes from volatile organic compounds. Common classes include monoterpenes such as linalool and phenylpropanoid and benzenoid compounds; these small molecules diffuse easily from petals into air. Insect and mammal olfactory receptors recognize specific odor molecules at very low concentrations, which is why people can smell floral scent even when its concentration is modest. Different flowers carry different volatile blends and their own aroma profiles.[4]

花色、香气与传粉者的视觉和嗅觉偏好协同演化。蜂类对蓝、紫和紫外纹路敏感，蝶类偏好红色花，蛾类在夜间活动，更多依赖白色花和较浓的香气；鸟媒花常呈红色、管状并提供花蜜，与蜂媒花的形态形成对照。传粉综合征把花部特征与主要访客类型对应起来。已有研究记录花色在不同地区随传粉者偏好发生地理变异，说明颜色信号受传粉选择压力塑造。Color and scent coevolve with pollinator vision and smell. Bees are sensitive to blue, purple and ultraviolet patterns; butterflies favor red flowers; moths active at night rely more on white flowers and stronger scent. Pollination syndromes match floral traits to main visitor types, and studies document geographic variation in flower color tracking local pollinator preferences, showing that color signals are shaped by pollinator selection.[3][4]

边界上，风媒花通常花被小、颜色淡、香气弱，不依赖视觉和嗅觉信号吸引访客。人对气味和颜色的感知与昆虫、鸟类并不相同，人类觉得香的气味对特定传粉者未必最有效。花朵授粉之后，部分种类的花色变暗、香气减弱，以减少重复访问；把花色和香气一概理解为给人观赏，会忽略其传粉功能。The boundary: wind-pollinated flowers usually have small, pale perianths and weak scent and do not rely on visual or olfactory signals. Human perception of color and odor differs from that of insects and birds, and a scent pleasant to people may not be most effective for a particular pollinator. After pollination, some flowers darken or lose scent to discourage repeat visits. Reading color and scent as decoration for people alone misses their pollination function.[3][5]

02自花传粉的花怎样完成授粉？植物又有哪些避免自交的机制？How does a self-pollinating flower set seed, and what mechanisms avoid selfing?

花粉经生物或非生物媒介到达柱头，自交保证结实但多样性低；异交还靠异株、不亲和、异熟与空间分离实现。Pollen reaches the stigma by biotic or abiotic vectors; selfing secures seed but lowers diversity, while outcrossing uses dioecy, incompatibility, dichogamy and separation.

传粉是花粉从花药转移到柱头的过程，媒介分为生物媒介和风、水等非生物媒介。花粉壁含外壁蛋白，落到柱头后先经历识别过程，亲和的花粉才能完成水合和萌发；随后花粉管沿花柱把精子送入胚囊。传粉只解决花粉到达雌蕊的问题，真正的受精发生在花粉管进入胚囊之后。风媒花产生大量轻小花粉，虫媒花则用花部结构把花粉附着到访客身上。Pollination is the transfer of pollen from anther to stigma, carried by animal vectors or by abiotic agents such as wind and water. Once pollen lands on the stigma it germinates a pollen tube that grows through the style and delivers sperm into the embryo sac. Pollination only gets pollen to the pistil; actual fertilization occurs after the tube enters the embryo sac. Wind-pollinated flowers make abundant lightweight pollen, while animal-pollinated flowers place pollen on visitors through floral structure.[2]

自花传粉在同一朵花内完成，花粉直接落到同一朵花的柱头上；同一植株不同花之间的传粉称为同株传粉。闭花受精是自花传粉的极端形式，花在未开放时即完成授粉，完全不依赖外部媒介。自交在缺少传粉者时仍能结实，保证繁殖成功，但后代只携带同一亲本的基因组合，遗传多样性较低，对环境变化的适应余地较小。Self-pollination completes within one flower, with pollen landing on its own stigma; transfer between flowers on the same plant is called geitonogamy. Selfing still sets seed when pollinators are scarce and secures reproduction, but the offspring carry only one parent's gene combination, with lower genetic diversity and less room to adapt to environmental change.[2]

植物演化出多种机制避免长期自交。雌雄异株让植株只产生雄蕊或雌蕊；自交不亲和通过 S 位点识别自身花粉，阻止其在柱头上萌发；雌雄蕊异熟使雄蕊和雌蕊在不同时间成熟；空间分离则把花药和柱头放在不同位置。这些机制在不同物种中组合出现，提高异花传粉比例的同时保留一定自交繁殖保障，让后代获得双亲的基因组合。Plants have evolved several mechanisms to avoid prolonged selfing. Dioecy makes separate staminate and pistillate plants; self-incompatibility recognizes self pollen through the S-locus and blocks its germination on the stigma; dichogamy matures stamens and pistils at different times; herkogamy places anthers and stigma apart. These raise outcrossing rates and give offspring genes from two parents.[1][2]

异花传粉完成后进入双受精。一个精子与卵细胞结合形成二倍体合子，发育成胚；另一个精子与中央细胞结合形成三倍体胚乳，为胚胎发育储存养分；胚乳可为淀粉型、油质型或蛋白型，依作物种类储存不同养分。花粉管还会引导胚珠发育方向，使种子和果实结构与受精过程对应起来。After outcrossing comes double fertilization. One sperm fuses with the egg to form a diploid zygote that develops into the embryo; the other fuses with the central cell to form a triploid endosperm that stores nutrients for the embryo. The pollen tube also guides ovule development, so seed and fruit structures correspond to the fertilization process.[2]

边界上，自交产生的种子通常仍然可育，只是遗传多样性低；无融合生殖可以不经过受精、以种子形式完成无性繁殖，属于传粉受精框架之外的特例。传粉综合征只是趋势对应，同一朵花常常吸引多种访客，花粉也可能在不同物种间混杂。判断某朵花的传粉方式，需要结合花部结构、媒介观察和花粉来源，不能只看花色或气味。The boundary: selfed seed is usually fertile, though genetically less diverse. Apomixis produces seed asexually without fertilization and sits outside the pollination-fertilization framework. Pollination syndromes are trends, not absolute matches; one flower often attracts several visitors, and pollen can mix across species. Judging a flower's pollination mode requires floral structure, visitor observations and pollen source, not color or scent alone.[2]

03植物的种子是从哪里来的？果实又怎样把种子传播出去？Where do plant seeds come from, and how does the fruit disperse them?

受精后胚珠发育成种子、子房发育成果实；冠毛、翅果、可食果肉和钩刺分别对应风、动物等传播方式。After fertilization the ovule becomes a seed and the ovary a fruit; pappus, samara wings, edible pulp and burrs match wind and animal dispersal.

受精完成以后，胚珠发育成种子，子房发育成果实。种子由胚、胚乳和种皮构成，果实是包裹或伴随种子完成传播的结构；日常说的水果只是果实中可食用的一部分，植物学上的果实范围更广。果实类型与种子传播策略一一对应，是植物繁殖策略的外在表现。从花到种子和果实的对应关系，是理解植物繁殖结构的主线。After fertilization, the ovule develops into a seed and the ovary into a fruit. A seed consists of embryo, endosperm and seed coat, while the fruit is the structure that encloses or accompanies the seed during dispersal. Everyday fruit names cover only the edible portion; the botanical definition of fruit is broader. The correspondence from flower to seed and fruit is the main thread for understanding plant reproductive structure.[2]

胚珠内部，受精卵经过分裂和分化发育成胚，也就是新一代植物的雏形；胚乳积累淀粉、脂肪或蛋白质，为萌发提供养分；珠被发育成种皮，在种子休眠期保护胚。子房壁随发育分化为果皮，外果皮、中果皮和内果皮的厚度与质地因种类不同；成熟过程中果皮颜色、质地和糖分随叶绿素降解、色素合成与淀粉转化逐步变化。有些食用部分来自花托等附属组织，称为假果。Inside the ovule, the fertilized egg divides and differentiates into the embryo, the next-generation plant in miniature; the endosperm accumulates starch, fat or protein to feed germination; the integument becomes the seed coat protecting the embryo during dormancy. The ovary wall differentiates into pericarp, whose layers vary in thickness and texture across species; some edible flesh comes from accessory tissues such as the receptacle and is called an accessory fruit.[1]

果实和种子的结构对应不同传播方式。风力传播借助冠毛、翅果等轻小结构；动物传播靠可食果肉吸引取食者，或靠钩刺挂在皮毛上；另有重力落地、果实弹力炸裂和水流携带等方式；水传播的果实常具疏松果皮或气室，能浮在水面随水流漂移。传播距离和方向由果实结构、媒介活动与环境共同决定。Fruit and seed structures match different dispersal modes. Wind dispersal uses pappus hairs or winged samaras; animal dispersal relies on edible pulp attracting feeders or burrs clinging to fur; gravity, explosive dehiscence and water transport are other routes. Dispersal distance and direction depend on fruit structure, vector activity and environment.[2]

蒲公英的瘦果顶端带冠毛，成熟后冠毛张开成伞状，被风带走飘散；槭树的翅果带翅，旋转着下落以延长飞行距离。可食果实被动物取食后，坚硬种子随粪便排出并获得传播；钩刺类果实粘附在经过的动物体表。弹射类果实成熟后果皮干燥收缩，突然开裂把种子弹出数厘米到数十厘米。这些结构都是为把胚送到远离母株的位置，减少与亲代竞争。A dandelion achene carries a pappus on top; when ripe the hairs open into a parachute and are carried off by wind. A maple samara has a wing and spins as it falls to extend flight. Edible fruits are eaten by animals, and hard seeds pass out with feces to be dispersed; burr fruits stick to passing animal coats. These structures all serve to move the embryo away from the parent and reduce competition with it.[2]

边界上，植物学意义上的果实不等于日常水果：番茄、黄瓜、豆荚都是果实，而很多坚果在结构上属于种子。传播距离受风力强弱、动物活动范围和地形影响，并非每次都能成功扩散。部分种子需要低温层积或种皮破损后才萌发，果实成熟与种子萌发之间还有休眠条件需要满足。The boundary: botanical fruit is not the same as everyday fruit; tomato, cucumber and pods are fruits, while many nuts are seeds structurally. Dispersal distance varies with wind strength, animal range and terrain, and not every attempt succeeds. Some seeds need cold stratification or seed-coat scarification before germination, so dormancy conditions sit between fruit ripening and seed sprouting.[1][2]

04花为什么有时开有时不开？梅花为什么在冬末早春开放？Why do flowers open at some times and not others, and why do plum blossoms open in late winter?

光周期与低温春化信号经成花素传到分生组织，年龄和营养决定成花阈值；梅花先累积低温再随回暖开放。Photoperiod and vernalization signals reach the meristem via florigen, while age and nutrition set the threshold; plums accumulate cold first, then open as warmth returns.

开花时间由植物对环境信号的感知和内部发育状态共同决定，主要环境信号是日照长度、低温经历和温度，年龄与资源积累决定植物是否已经具备成花能力。幼年期植物即使环境合适也不会开花，需经过营养生长阶段积累足够体积和养分。植物不会随机开花，而是在信号组合满足条件后，把顶端分生组织从营养生长转向花原基分化。Flowering time is set jointly by the plant's perception of environmental signals and its internal developmental state. Main external signals are daylength, cold experience and temperature, while age and accumulated resources decide whether the plant is competent to flower. Plants do not flower at random; when the signal combination meets the threshold, the shoot apical meristem switches from vegetative growth to floral primordium formation.[6]

光周期是重要的成花信号。叶片是感知日照长度的主要器官，摘除叶片后植株不能正常响应日照长度信号；信号经光受体和昼夜节律传导，产生成花素（FT 类蛋白），通过韧皮部运到顶端分生组织，启动花原基发育。按日照反应植物分为长日植物、短日植物和日中性植物；临界日照长度因物种和温度条件不同而变化。Photoperiod is a key flowering signal. Leaves sense daylength and, through photoreceptors and circadian signaling, produce florigen (FT-type proteins) that travel via the phloem to the apical meristem and start floral development. By light response, plants are long-day, short-day or day-neutral; the critical daylength varies with species and temperature.[6]

二年生和冬性植物需要经历一段低温才能开花，这一过程称为春化。低温解除对成花抑制基因的封锁，使植物在之后回暖时具备开花能力；春化效应在当代植株上保持，花芽分化通常在春化之后的生长季完成。梅花在冬末早春开放，是先满足低温累积、再随春季回暖启动花芽发育的结果，并不是主动选择在冬天开花；花芽在前一年夏秋已经形成，冬季只是等待温度信号。Biennial and winter annual plants need a period of cold before flowering, a process called vernalization. Cold lifts the repression on flowering genes, so the plant can flower when warmth returns. Plum blossoms opening in late winter and early spring first accumulate sufficient cold, then start bud development as spring warms; they do not choose to flower in winter. The flower buds formed in the previous summer and autumn wait through winter for the temperature signal.[6]

赤霉素等激素与年龄、碳氮营养状态共同调节成花阈值。碳氮比和储存的碳水化合物水平影响成花，养分不足时花芽退化或脱落；内部资源不足、植株年龄太小时，即使环境信号合适也会推迟开花。环境信号通过改变分生组织中的成花基因表达，把外部季节信息转成发育决策。Gibberellins and other hormones, together with age and carbon-nitrogen nutrition, regulate the flowering threshold. When internal resources are insufficient or the plant is too young, flowering is delayed even if external signals are suitable. Environmental signals change flowering-gene expression in the meristem, converting seasonal information into a developmental decision.[6]

边界上，具体物候受局部气候、品种和栽培条件影响，室内盆栽开花还受修剪、水肥和盆器大小影响。光周期反应是遗传倾向，实际花期是多因素叠加的结果，不能仅凭日照长短预测某一株植物的开花日期。温度异常波动、病虫害和资源胁迫都会改变成花时间，本章描述的是信号调控的一般框架。The boundary: actual phenology depends on local climate, cultivar and cultivation conditions, and indoor potted plants are also affected by pruning, water, fertilizer and pot size. Photoperiod response is a genetic tendency; the real flowering date combines several factors and cannot be predicted from daylength alone. Unusual temperature swings, pests, disease and resource stress all shift flowering time, and this chapter describes the general signaling framework.[6]

05花为什么会枯萎？切花插在水里为什么还是会蔫？Why do flowers wilt, and why do cut flowers droop even in water?

萎蔫是失水超过吸水后膨压下降；气栓堵塞、乙烯促衰老和碳水耗尽共同缩短瓶插寿命。Wilting follows turgor loss when water loss outruns uptake; emboli, blockage, ethylene senescence and depleted carbohydrates shorten vase life.

花枯萎的直接机制是细胞膨压下降。植物细胞靠液泡吸水把细胞壁撑紧，花瓣、叶片因此挺立；当失水速度超过吸水速度，细胞内水分减少，膨压回落，组织失去支撑而软塌下垂。切花被剪断后根系不再供水，茎秆只能靠切口处的维管束吸水，水分平衡从一开始就比整株植物脆弱，花瓣薄、表面积大，失水后最先表现出蔫软。The direct mechanism of wilting is a fall in cell turgor. Plant cells stay firm because the vacuole takes up water and presses the cell wall tight, which holds petals and leaves upright. When water loss outruns uptake, intracellular water falls, turgor drops, and the tissue loses support and droops. After a flower is cut, the roots no longer supply water, and the stem can only draw through the cut vascular tissue; water balance is more fragile than in an intact plant from the start, and thin, large-surface petals wilt first.[1][7]

离体后水分平衡沿几条路径被打破。叶片和花瓣的蒸腾持续向外散失水汽，根部吸收的水分约有 90% 通过蒸腾散失，空气干燥、高温和强光会加快失水；切口暴露在空气中时，气泡进入导管形成气栓，打断连续水柱，切口在空气中停留越久，被吸入导管的气泡越多，瓶中微生物繁殖以及树胶、黏液沉积也会堵塞导管内腔。吸水下降而蒸腾不停，花瓣和叶片的水分被优先调用，于是先从花头和叶缘开始发软下垂。After cutting, water balance breaks along several routes. Transpiration from leaves and petals keeps losing water vapour, and about 90% of root uptake is lost through transpiration, and dry air, heat and bright light speed the loss. When the cut end sits in air, bubbles enter vessels as emboli that interrupt the continuous water column, and the longer the cut stays exposed, the more bubbles are drawn in; microbes growing in the vase, plus gum and mucilage deposits, also block vessel lumens. Uptake falls while transpiration continues, and water is drawn preferentially from petals and leaves, so the flower head and leaf edges soften first.[7][8]

衰老程序并不只等缺水才启动。切花采收后呼吸作用仍然旺盛，组织储存的碳水化合物逐渐耗尽；花朵和叶片释放乙烯，对乙烯敏感的花种在很低浓度下就会出现花瓣衰老、变色和脱落。水分胁迫又反过来促进乙烯产生，失水与衰老互相加强，萎蔫之后往往紧接着花瓣褐变和脱落，而不是单纯的暂时缺水。Senescence does not wait for water shortage to start. After harvest the cut flower still respires actively, and stored carbohydrates are gradually depleted; flowers and leaves release ethylene, and ethylene-sensitive species show petal ageing, discoloration and abscission at very low concentrations. Water stress in turn promotes ethylene production, so dehydration and senescence reinforce each other, and wilting is often followed by browning and drop rather than a temporary dry spell.[8]

不同花种的主导原因并不相同。以香石竹为例，常规切花瓶插寿命约 5–10 天，乙烯敏感品种经乙烯处理后瓶插期可缩短到约 4 天；香石竹、部分兰科等乙烯敏感型花朵，气体信号对寿命影响更大；另一些花的萎蔫主要由水分失衡和导管堵塞决定。花瓣寿命由发育程序、水分状况、微生物侵染和温度共同决定，同一瓶花中不同花朵也会先后进入衰老阶段，不能把所有蔫花都归为同一种原因。The dominant cause differs among species. In carnations, conventional cut flowers last about 5–10 days in the vase, and ethylene-sensitive varieties can drop to around 4 days after ethylene treatment; Carnations and some orchids are ethylene-sensitive, where the gas signal weighs more on vase life; in others wilting is driven mainly by water imbalance and vessel blockage. Petal longevity is set jointly by developmental programme, water status, microbial infection and temperature, and flowers in one vase senesce one after another, so not every drooping bloom has the same cause.[8][9]

边界：萎蔫不等于单纯“没浇水”，把蔫花直接归因于缺水会漏掉气栓、堵塞和乙烯衰老。瓶插寿命还受采收成熟度、贮藏温度、水质和切口处理影响；本文只讲生理机制，不对保鲜效果作承诺。整株植物持续萎蔫、浇水后仍不恢复，或叶片大面积发软、根部发黑，可能提示根系或维管束受损，需要结合栽培条件进一步判断。The boundary: wilting is not simply a matter of not enough water, and blaming every drooping flower on dehydration misses emboli, blockage and ethylene senescence. Vase life also depends on harvest maturity, storage temperature, water quality and cut treatment; this chapter covers physiology only and makes no preservation promise. If an intact plant stays wilted after watering, or large areas of leaves soften with dark roots, root or vascular damage may be involved and should be judged against the growing conditions.[7][8]

06种子为什么会进入休眠？Why do seeds enter dormancy?

成熟种子脱水并由脱落酸维持低代谢状态，等待温度、水分等环境信号合适后再萌发。A mature seed dries out and ABA keeps metabolism low, waiting until temperature and moisture cues are suitable before germinating.

休眠是种子成熟后主动保持的一种暂停状态。果实成熟时，种子逐步脱水，含水量降到较低水平，细胞内水分减少，代谢速率随之放慢；胚并不死亡，只是分裂和生长暂时停止。这种状态让种子能在干燥、低温或不适宜的季节里保存活力，等到环境有利时再发芽，而不是一沾到水就立刻萌发。种子内部以胚和储存养料的子叶或胚乳为主，干燥状态下细胞分裂停滞，酶仍保持很低活性，可在土壤中存活数月到数年。Dormancy is a paused state maintained deliberately after the seed matures. As the fruit ripens, the seed gradually dehydrates to a low moisture content; less water inside the cells slows metabolism. The embryo does not die, it merely suspends division and growth. This state keeps the seed viable through dry, cold or unfavorable seasons, so it can sprout when conditions become favorable rather than germinating as soon as it touches water.[1][10]

激素平衡控制休眠与萌发的转换。脱落酸（ABA）在种子发育过程中积累，促进并维持休眠，抑制胚过早生长；赤霉素（GA）则在吸水后促进萌发，解除对生长的抑制。种子内部 ABA 与 GA 的相对比例决定休眠深浅：ABA 占优时保持休眠，GA 信号增强、ABA 被降解后，萌发程序才启动。其他激素和环境信号也参与调节这条平衡通路。A hormone balance controls the switch between dormancy and germination. Abscisic acid (ABA) accumulates during seed development, promotes and maintains dormancy, and stops the embryo from growing too early; gibberellin (GA) promotes germination after water uptake and releases that growth inhibition. The relative ABA-to-GA ratio sets dormancy depth: dormancy holds while ABA dominates, and the germination program starts only when GA signaling rises and ABA is degraded. Other hormones and environmental cues take part in this same balance pathway.[1][10]

休眠避免在错误的时间发芽。若种子在秋季成熟后立即萌发，幼苗会经历寒冬或干旱，成活概率低；休眠把萌发推迟到温度、水分和日照都合适的春季。不同植物的休眠由不同线索解除：有的需要一段低温层积，有的需要光照打破遮蔽，有的种皮坚硬，要经过磨损或微生物作用透水透气，这些条件分别对应原产地的季节规律。休眠类型也有区别：有的属于生理休眠，必须经历低温或后熟；有的属于物理休眠，种皮不透水、不透气，需要磨损打破。Dormancy prevents germination at the wrong time. If a seed sprouted immediately after ripening in autumn, the seedling would face winter cold or drought with a low survival chance; dormancy delays emergence to a spring when temperature, moisture and daylight are suitable. Different plants release dormancy through different cues: some need a period of cold stratification, some need light to break shade, and some hard coats must be worn or rotted to let water and air through. These conditions match the seasonal patterns of their native ranges.[2][10]

打破休眠需要吸水和信号配合。干燥种子吸胀后水分进入细胞，酶开始活动，ABA 代谢下降，胚根突破种皮；冷处理、光照或刻伤种皮可以缩短等待时间。休眠深度因物种和收获时成熟度而异，同一批种子也可能先后萌发，这种分散出苗降低了整批失败的风险，还能避开短时恶劣天气和病虫害集中爆发，是植物对不确定环境的风险分摊策略。Breaking dormancy needs water uptake together with signaling. A dry seed imbibes water, enzymes become active, ABA metabolism falls, and the radicle pushes through the coat; cold treatment, light or scarification can shorten the wait. Dormancy depth varies with species and harvest maturity, and even one batch may germinate at different times; this staggered emergence reduces the risk of the whole batch failing.[2][10]

边界：种子休眠不是动物式的睡眠，也不等同于单纯因缺水造成的静止；休眠种子即使补水，在休眠未解除时也可能不发芽。本章讲种子激素与环境信号的一般机制，不提供催芽药剂、浸种浓度或处理时长等农业操作建议。Boundary: seed dormancy is not animal-style sleep, nor is it the same as mere quiescence caused by lack of water; a dormant seed may not sprout after watering until dormancy is released. This chapter explains the general mechanism of seed hormones and environmental cues, and it gives no germination chemicals, soaking concentrations or treatment schedules for farming.[1][2]

07果实成熟后为什么会变甜？Why does fruit become sweet when it ripens?

成熟过程中淀粉被酶分解成糖，酸度下降、果胶变软，乙烯协调呼吸跃变和色泽香气变化。During ripening enzymes break starch into sugars, acidity drops and pectin softens, while ethylene coordinates the respiratory climacteric, color and aroma.

未熟果实偏酸偏涩，主要成分还不是糖。青果细胞里储存着淀粉，同时含有较多有机酸、单宁和较硬的果胶；叶绿素让果实呈绿色，香气物质很少。此时果肉紧实，口感酸涩，保护尚未发育完成的种子不被过早取食。未熟果实细胞膨压高、果胶层完整，咬起来脆硬；种子未成熟时，果实靠酸涩和硬质地降低被取食概率。随着果实接近成熟，一套协调的生理过程开始把这些储存和防御成分逐步转化。An unripe fruit tastes sour and astringent because its main components are not yet sugars. Green fruit cells store starch and hold relatively large amounts of organic acids, tannins and firm pectin; chlorophyll keeps the fruit green and aroma compounds are scarce. The flesh is compact and tart, protecting the not-yet-developed seeds from being eaten too early. As the fruit approaches maturity, a coordinated physiological process gradually converts these storage and defense materials.[1][11]

变甜的核心是淀粉转化为糖。成熟信号启动后，淀粉酶等把不甜的淀粉分解为葡萄糖、果糖和蔗糖，可溶性糖含量上升；苹果、香蕉等果实采收后仍可继续完成这一转化，所以放几天后会更甜。不同果实积累的糖种类不同，香蕉以淀粉快速转化为主，葡萄则在成熟期逐步积累糖分，甜度变化节奏并不相同。糖分来自果实自身储存物的水解，不是从外界运输进来的新糖。The key to sweetness is starch turning into sugar. When the ripening signal starts, amylase and other enzymes break the non-sweet starch into glucose, fructose and sucrose, raising soluble sugar content. Fruits such as apples and bananas can finish this conversion after picking, which is why they taste sweeter after a few days. The sugar comes from hydrolysis of the fruit's own stored materials, not from new sugar transported in from outside.[1][11]

成熟还伴随酸度下降和质地变软。有机酸被呼吸消耗或转化，酸甜比例改变；果胶酶分解细胞壁间的果胶，果肉由硬脆变得软嫩；叶绿素分解，类胡萝卜素或花青素显现出成熟色泽，同时合成酯类等香气物质。香气酯类和色泽物质在成熟后期合成，熟果更容易被动物识别取食，帮助种子散布。淀粉糖化、脱酸、软化、上色和出香，都在同一套成熟程序下先后发生。Ripening also lowers acidity and softens texture. Organic acids are consumed by respiration or converted, changing the sweet-sour balance; pectin enzymes break down the pectin between cell walls, so the flesh shifts from crisp to tender. Chlorophyll decomposes, carotenoids or anthocyanins show the ripe color, and esters and other aroma compounds are synthesized. Sugar release, acid decline, softening, coloring and aroma all unfold in sequence under the same ripening program.[2][11]

乙烯是协调成熟的信号分子。跃变型果实（如香蕉、苹果、番茄）在成熟前后出现一次呼吸速率上升，同时乙烯释放增加，乙烯再诱导成熟相关基因表达；果实释放的乙烯可以催熟旁边的果实，这也是把生果和成熟苹果放在一起能加快变软的原因。乙烯由果实细胞合成，气体可在组织间扩散，也能通过空气传到邻近果实。非跃变型果实成熟过程较平缓，呼吸高峰不明显。Ethylene is the signal molecule coordinating ripening. Climacteric fruits such as bananas, apples and tomatoes show a rise in respiration rate around ripening, together with increased ethylene release; ethylene then induces ripening-related genes. Ethylene given off by one fruit can ripen neighboring fruit, which is why placing raw fruit with a ripe apple speeds softening. Non-climacteric fruits ripen more smoothly without a clear respiratory peak.[2][11]

边界：果实变甜是储存碳水化合物水解和激素协调的结果，过度放熟后糖分也会被呼吸消耗；本章讲成熟的一般生化机制，不提供保鲜贮藏条件、催熟剂量或品种选育建议。腐烂是微生物侵染，和正常成熟是两个过程，不能凭甜度判断果实是否安全可食。Boundary: fruit sweetness results from hydrolysis of stored carbohydrates and hormone coordination; left too long, sugar is also consumed by respiration. This chapter explains the general biochemistry of ripening, not storage conditions, ripening-agent doses or breeding advice. Rotting is microbial infection and a separate process from normal ripening; sweetness alone cannot judge whether a fruit is safe to eat.[1][2]
