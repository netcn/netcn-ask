01人头发变白的原因是什么？What causes human hair to turn white?

毛囊黑素细胞衰退、黑素供应减少，发干先灰后白；遗传、氧化损伤与信号通路影响早晚。Follicle melanocyte decline cuts melanin supply, shafts go gray then white; genetics and oxidative stress affect timing.

头发变白，主要是毛囊里的黑素细胞随年龄增长减少或功能衰退，新长出的发干缺少黑素颗粒，颜色由深转灰再变白；白发不是色素被洗掉，而是毛囊停止向发干供应色素。人全身约有数百万个毛囊，头皮毛囊密度最高，每根头发的色素状态由对应毛囊决定，所以白发可以一根一根出现。遗传、氧化损伤和局部信号共同决定白发出现的早晚。Hair turns gray mainly because melanocytes in the follicle decline in number or function with age, so newly growing shafts lack melanin granules and shift from dark to gray to white; graying is not pigment washed away, but the follicle stopping its pigment supply. The scalp carries the highest follicle density, and each strand's pigment state is set by its own follicle, so white hairs appear one by one. Genetics, oxidative damage and local signaling together decide when graying begins.[5]

色素生产发生在毛囊底部的毛球。黑素细胞合成黑素，经树突把含色素的黑素体转移给周围正在角化的毛干细胞；黑素体在细胞内合成后沿细胞骨架转运到树突末端，再由毛干细胞吞噬进入毛干。黑素分真黑素与褐黑素两类，真黑素偏棕黑，褐黑素偏红黄，二者比例决定头发、眉毛和体毛的颜色深浅与色调；毛干从内到外为髓质、皮质与毛小皮，皮质是黑素颗粒主要停留的部位。Pigment production takes place in the hair bulb at the follicle base. Melanocytes synthesize melanin and transfer pigment-containing melanosomes through dendrites to surrounding keratinizing shaft cells; melanosomes travel along the cytoskeleton to dendrite tips before being taken up by shaft cells. Eumelanin is brown-black, pheomelanin is red-yellow, and their ratio sets the depth and tone of scalp hair, eyebrows and body hair. The shaft has medulla, cortex and cuticle layers, and the cortex holds most pigment granules.[1][2]

白发形成是一个渐进过程。毛囊隆突部的黑素干细胞是黑素细胞的储备库，随年龄增长和氧化损伤累积，干细胞数量减少、分化补充不足，毛球处黑素细胞衰退，新生发干的黑素含量逐步下降；先出现灰白发丝，即发干中仍含少量黑素，继续发展才变成全白。早期白发常先出现在两鬓与胡须，再蔓延到头顶；同一个人不同部位毛囊的衰退速度并不一致，这也是发色斑驳的原因。Graying progresses step by step. Melanocyte stem cells in the follicle bulge are the reserve pool; with aging and accumulated oxidative stress this pool shrinks and differentiates less, bulb melanocytes decline, and melanin in new shafts drops gradually. A strand first becomes gray, still carrying a little pigment, before turning fully white. Early graying often starts at the temples and beard before spreading to the crown, and follicles in different regions decline at different rates.[1][5]

同行评议综述《International Journal of Biological Sciences》2023 年一篇关于毛发色素与色素恢复的研究指出，毛发色素受毛囊周期、氧化应激和多条信号通路调控；遗传是早生白发的主要背景因素，吸烟、营养状态和长期压力被列为相关影响因素。年龄上，高加索人群常在 30 岁前后出现少量白发，亚洲人群略晚，个体差异可达十余年；早于二十多岁的成片白发需结合家族史与营养状况评估。白发毛囊中有时仍保留部分干细胞，相关研究仍在探索再色素化的可能。A 2023 review in the International Journal of Biological Sciences on hair pigmentation and repigmentation notes that hair color is governed by the follicle cycle, oxidative stress and several signaling pathways. Genetics is the main background factor for early graying, while smoking, nutrition and chronic stress are listed as associated factors. Caucasian populations usually show a few gray hairs around age 30, Asians somewhat later, with individual differences of more than a decade. Some gray follicles still retain stem cells, and research on repigmentation remains ongoing.[5]

自然老化形成的白发不能靠普通洗发水或偏方逆转；局部白斑、短期内成片变白或伴随皮肤异常，需要皮肤科评估。这里只讲毛发色素来源与衰退过程，不展开染发化学原理、脱发疾病分型或美容遮盖方案。Naturally aged white hair cannot be reversed by ordinary shampoo or folk remedies; localized white patches, sudden diffuse whitening or skin abnormalities need dermatological evaluation. This chapter covers melanin origin and decline only, not hair-dye chemistry, hair-loss diagnosis or cosmetic concealment.[2][5]

02为什么人的皮肤有的白，有的黑？Why are some people's skin lighter and others darker?

肤色由基底层黑素细胞产生的黑素种类与数量决定，真黑素多则深，黑素吸收散射紫外线。Skin tone depends on melanocyte melanin type and amount in the basal layer; more eumelanin darkens skin and absorbs UV.

人与人之间的肤色差异，主要由表皮基底层黑素细胞合成的黑素种类与数量决定，而不是皮肤厚薄或血液颜色；真黑素多则肤色深、偏棕黑，褐黑素比例高则肤色偏红偏黄。除黑素外，血红蛋白带来的血色、胡萝卜素类物质也会参与肤色观感，但主导长期肤色差异的仍是黑素含量。肤色是一条连续谱，不同人群之间是程度差异，不是截然二分。Differences in skin color come mainly from the type and amount of melanin produced by melanocytes in the basal epidermis, not from skin thickness or blood color. More eumelanin gives a deeper brown-black skin tone, while more pheomelanin leans red-yellow. Blood color from hemoglobin and carotenoids also affect appearance, but melanin content dominates long-term skin-tone differences. Skin color is a continuous spectrum: populations differ in degree, not in two opposite categories.[1]

黑素细胞位于表皮基底层，树突把黑素体送入周围角质形成细胞；黑素颗粒常在细胞核上方聚成一层“色素帽”，吸收和散射紫外线，减少紫外线对 DNA 的直接损伤。黑素体大小、分布和降解速度在不同细胞中也有差异；深色皮肤的黑素体更大、降解更慢，在角质形成细胞中保留更久。日晒会刺激黑素合成，角质形成细胞与黑素细胞之间的信号反馈使色素加深，这就是晒黑的过程。Melanocytes sit in the basal layer and send melanosomes through dendrites into neighboring keratinocytes. Pigment granules often gather into a cap over the nucleus, absorbing and scattering ultraviolet light and reducing direct DNA damage. Melanosome size, distribution and degradation speed vary between cells; in darker skin they are larger, degrade more slowly and persist longer in keratinocytes. Sunlight stimulates melanin synthesis; signaling between keratinocytes and melanocytes deepens pigment, which is how tanning works.[1][3]

深浅肤色带有环境适应背景。高日照地区人群通常真黑素更多，皮肤防护紫外线的能力更强；低日照地区肤色较浅，以便保留足够紫外线参与维生素 D 合成。维生素 D 在皮肤中经紫外线触发合成，肤色过深在低日照环境可能限制其合成，肤色过浅在强日照下则更易晒伤；这一权衡也是肤色纬度梯度的演化解释之一。紫外线既参与维生素 D 代谢，过量又损伤皮肤，两类压力在长期演化中形成平衡。Light and dark skin tones carry environmental adaptation backgrounds. People in high-sunlight regions usually carry more eumelanin and stronger UV protection, while lower-sunlight regions tend toward lighter skin so enough UV can still drive vitamin D synthesis. Vitamin D is triggered in skin by UV; very dark skin may limit synthesis in low sunlight, while very light skin burns more easily in strong sun, a tradeoff behind the latitudinal gradient. UV both contributes to vitamin D metabolism and damages skin in excess, a balance shaped over long-term evolution.[1][3]

世界卫生组织紫外线实况报道把紫外线分为 UVA 与 UVB：UVA 穿透较深，促进光老化，UVB 主要引起晒伤；按波长 UVA 为 320–400 nm、UVB 为 280–320 nm，短波 UVC 被臭氧层阻挡，到达地面的主要是 UVA 与部分 UVB。OpenStax 解剖生理教材把屏障保护、感觉功能和维生素 D 合成列为皮肤的主要功能。长期过度日晒增加光老化和皮肤癌风险，防护手段包括衣物、遮阳和防晒霜。A WHO ultraviolet radiation fact sheet divides UV into UVA and UVB: UVA penetrates deeper and drives photoaging, while UVB mainly causes sunburn. By wavelength UVA is 320–400 nm and UVB 280–320 nm; shortwave UVC is blocked by the ozone layer, so ground-level UV is mostly UVA with some UVB. OpenStax anatomy and physiology lists barrier protection, sensation and vitamin D synthesis as the main skin functions. Chronic excessive sun exposure raises photoaging and skin-cancer risk, and protection includes clothing, shade and sunscreen.[3][6]

黑素颗粒不是免疫细胞，肤色深浅也不直接等于皮肤健康程度；这里不展开黄褐斑、白癜风等色素性疾病分型，也不评测具体防晒产品成分。需要区分：肤色由遗传与环境共同塑造，不能用单一指标推断一个人的健康状态。Melanin granules are not immune cells, and skin tone does not directly measure skin health; this chapter does not cover melasma, vitiligo or other pigment-disorder classification, nor evaluate specific sunscreen ingredients. Skin color is shaped by both genetics and environment, and one single metric cannot infer a person's health.[1]

03皮肤细胞每隔几周就更新一次，为什么瘢痕不会随之消失？Skin cells renew every few weeks, why do scars persist?

表皮约 28–40 天更新一轮；深伤口由真皮胶原瘢痕修复，缺少皮嵴与附属器，不随表皮脱落。Epidermis renews in 28–40 days; deep wounds repair with dermal collagen scar lacking ridges and appendages, so it does not shed.

表皮大约每 28–40 天更新一轮：基底层的角质形成细胞分裂，细胞向上迁移、分化、角化，最终在角质层脱落。表皮没有血管，营养由真皮组织液扩散供应；细胞向上迁移的同时逐步脱离真皮环境，最终角化脱落。表皮细胞不断更替，瘢痕却能长期存在，原因在于浅表皮损伤由表皮再生修补，深达真皮的损伤主要由胶原纤维构成的瘢痕组织修复，两者修复方式不同。The epidermis renews roughly every 28–40 days: keratinocytes divide in the basal layer, migrate upward, differentiate and keratinize, and finally shed from the stratum corneum. The epidermis has no blood vessels; nutrients diffuse from dermal tissue fluid, and cells detach from the dermal environment as they move upward. Epidermal cells keep turning over, yet scars persist, because superficial epidermal injury is repaired by epidermal regeneration while deep dermal injury is repaired mainly by collagenous scar tissue; the two repair routes differ.[1][4]

更新链条可以分成几步。基底层干细胞分裂产生新细胞，细胞经棘层、颗粒层向上移动，逐步合成角蛋白、脱水变扁，到达角质层时已是无核的角化死细胞，成片脱落。基底层细胞分裂周期约数天到一周，新生细胞推挤上层细胞上移；从分裂到脱落的完整旅程约 28–40 天，随年龄增长会变慢。毛囊隆突部的干细胞也会参与表皮损伤后的补充修复。The renewal chain has several steps. Stem cells in the basal layer divide to make new cells, which move up through the spinosum and granulosum layers, accumulate keratin, lose water and flatten, and reach the stratum corneum as anucleate dead keratinocytes that shed in sheets. Basal cells divide every few days to a week, pushing upper cells upward; the full journey from division to shedding takes 28–40 days and slows with age. Bulge stem cells of hair follicles also help replenish epidermis after injury.[1][3]

皮肤破损后的修复分阶段进行。破损处先止血、结痂，炎症细胞清除碎片，真皮成纤维细胞沉积胶原、重建支架；愈合早期伤口处先形成血凝块与纤维蛋白网，随后肉芽组织长入，新生毛细血管和成纤维细胞逐步替代血凝块，上皮细胞从伤口边缘和毛囊上皮向中心爬行覆盖创面。如果伤口深达真皮，新生组织以纤维结缔组织为主，缺少正常皮嵴、毛囊和汗腺等附属结构。Wound repair proceeds in stages. A break first clots and forms a scab; inflammatory cells clear debris, and dermal fibroblasts deposit collagen to rebuild the scaffold. Early repair forms a clot and fibrin mesh, then granulation tissue grows in with new capillaries and fibroblasts replacing the clot, while epithelial cells crawl from wound edges and follicle epithelium toward the center. When a wound reaches the dermis, the new tissue is mostly fibrous connective tissue and lacks normal rete ridges, hair follicles, sweat glands and other appendages.[1][4]

瘢痕组织的胶原排列与正常皮肤不同，颜色、弹性和纹理都有差异；成熟瘢痕中胶原以粗大束状平行排列，缺少正常真皮的波浪状编织，因此弹性与延展性较差，瘢痕颜色初期偏红，随血管减少逐渐变浅。表皮虽然持续更新，瘢痕位于真皮层，并不随表皮脱落而消失。愈合速度受年龄、伤口部位、营养状态和伤口深度影响，血供丰富、伤口整齐的部位恢复更快。Scar collagen is arranged differently from normal skin, so color, elasticity and texture all differ. Mature scar collagen lies in thick parallel bundles rather than the wavy weave of normal dermis, so elasticity and stretch are poorer; scars start red and pale as vessels regress. Although the epidermis keeps renewing, the scar sits in the dermis and does not disappear with epidermal shedding. Healing speed depends on age, wound site, nutrition and wound depth; well-perfused, neatly edged sites recover faster.[1][4]

浅表擦伤通常只伤及表皮，一般不留明显瘢痕；深伤口、感染或反复牵拉容易形成增生性瘢痕。儿童愈合能力较强，老年人表皮更新慢、伤口愈合时间更长；反复牵拉和感染会加重纤维沉积，使瘢痕更明显。这里不讨论瘢痕疙瘩治疗、植皮手术或伤口感染处置；出现红肿渗液、愈合缓慢的伤口应就医处理。Superficial abrasions usually stay in the epidermis and leave little or no scar; deep wounds, infection or repeated pulling tend to form hypertrophic scars. Children heal more strongly, while older adults renew epidermis slowly and heal longer; repeated tension and infection worsen fibrous deposition. This chapter does not cover keloid treatment, skin grafting or wound infection management; wounds with redness, discharge or slow healing should be seen by a clinician.[4]

04为什么眉毛不会像头发那样长长？Why do eyebrows not grow as long as scalp hair?

眉毛与体毛生长期以周、月计，头皮生长期约 2–7 年；生长期长度决定毛发最终长度。Eyebrow and body hair anagen lasts weeks to months, scalp anagen 2–7 years; growth-phase length sets final hair length.

头发、眉毛和体毛长度不同，主要由各自毛囊的生长期长短决定。毛囊是皮肤的附属器官，由表皮下陷到真皮形成；每根毛发的长度上限由它自己的周期决定，而不是被剪短后停止生长。头皮毛囊的生长期可以持续数年，头发得以持续长长；眉毛和躯干体毛的生长期短得多，长到一定长度就进入休止、脱落，因此不会像头发那样一直变长。Scalp hair, eyebrows and body hair differ in length mainly because their follicles spend different amounts of time in the growth phase. The follicle is an appendage formed by epidermis dipping into the dermis; each strand's length ceiling is set by its own cycle, not by being cut short. Scalp follicles grow for years, so hair keeps elongating, while eyebrow and trunk follicles have much shorter growth phases and rest or shed after reaching a limited length.[2]

毛发生长周期分三个主要阶段。生长期毛囊毛球活跃分裂，毛干不断延伸；退行期约持续 2–3 周，毛囊下段缩短、毛乳头上移，细胞进入凋亡；休止期约持续 3 个月左右，发干与毛囊连接松动，之后脱落，新的毛囊周期再启动，由同一区域陆续轮换。The hair cycle has three main stages. In anagen the bulb divides actively and the shaft extends; in catagen, lasting about 2–3 weeks, the lower follicle shortens, the papilla moves up and cells undergo apoptosis; in telogen, about 3 months, the shaft loosens and sheds before a new cycle starts, with follicles in one region rotating through stages.[2][5]

毛囊位于真皮内，毛根包裹在毛囊鞘中，底部毛乳头提供血供与信号；毛干从内到外为髓质、皮质和毛小皮，皮质由角蛋白束构成并含黑素颗粒；毛根下端膨大形成毛球，包绕毛乳头，毛乳头的信号决定毛囊是否进入生长。露出皮肤的毛干是角化的死细胞束，颜色由毛球黑素决定。毛小皮反复受梳理摩擦、烫染化学处理和高温损伤后翘起、缺损，失去保护的皮质角蛋白束就沿纵向裂开形成分叉；毛干没有活细胞去把裂口重新接上，护发产品只能临时抚平毛糙，真正的分叉只能剪掉。同一区域的毛囊并不同步，有的生长、有的休止，所以日常脱落与新生同时发生。Follicles sit in the dermis, the root wrapped in follicle sheaths, and the dermal papilla at the base supplies blood and signals. The shaft has medulla, cortex and cuticle; the cortex is keratin bundles holding melanin, and the root base swells into the bulb wrapping the papilla, whose signals decide whether the follicle enters growth. The shaft above the skin is a bundle of keratinized dead cells whose color is set by bulb melanin. Once the cuticle chips or lifts from combing friction, chemical treatments and heat, the unprotected cortex frays along its length and splits into ends. The shaft has no living cells to fuse the crack back; products can smooth it temporarily, but a true split can only be cut off. Follicles in the same region are not synchronized; some grow while others rest, so shedding and replacement happen together.[1][2][7]

量级上，头皮约 80%–90% 的毛囊处于生长期，头皮生长期约 2–7 年，头发可长到数十厘米甚至更长，每天脱落约 50–100 根属于正常替换；眉毛生长期约 1–2 个月，躯干毛更短，长度因此受限。每日脱落的头发中多数来自休止期毛囊，属正常轮换。遗传、激素和毛囊局部信号共同决定每个毛囊的周期长度。Quantitatively, about 80–90% of scalp follicles are in anagen, which lasts 2–7 years, so scalp hair can reach tens of centimeters, and shedding about 50–100 hairs per day is normal replacement. Eyebrow anagen lasts about 1–2 months and trunk hair even less, which limits their length; most daily shed hairs come from telogen follicles. Genetics, hormones and local follicle signals together set each cycle length.[2]

体毛分布与长度受性激素影响，但多毛症、雄激素性脱发和脱毛手术属于临床话题。毛囊周期受局部生长因子、激素和神经信号调控；剃刮只切断露出皮肤的毛干，不改变毛囊周期，新长出的毛发断面较粗硬只是视觉感受，也不会让毛发永久变粗。这里只讲毛囊周期与长度限制，不展开脱发诊断或激光脱毛原理。Body-hair distribution and length are influenced by sex hormones, but hirsutism, androgenetic alopecia and hair-removal surgery are clinical topics. The cycle is tuned by local growth factors, hormones and nerve signals; shaving only cuts the exposed shaft and does not change cycle length, and the coarser feel of regrowth is a visual effect, not permanent thickening. This chapter covers the follicle cycle and length limits only, not hair-loss diagnosis or laser hair-removal principles.[2][5]

05为什么头发掉得厉害？Why does hair fall out so much?

毛囊按生长期、退行期、休止期轮换；DHT 使敏感毛囊微型化，营养与全身状态也影响脱落量。Follicles rotate through anagen, catagen and telogen; DHT miniaturizes sensitive follicles, and nutrition and systemic state affect shedding.

头皮大约有 10 万个毛囊，健康成人每天脱落约 50–100 根头发，属于毛囊轮换的正常替换，并不是头发在集体死掉。每根头发都走自己的周期：生长期毛球活跃分裂、毛干持续延伸，头皮毛囊的生长期约 2–6 年；退行期约 2–3 周，毛囊下段缩短、毛乳头上移，细胞逐步凋亡；休止期约 3 个月，发干与毛囊连接松动，随后脱落，同一位置再由新的周期接替。不同毛囊并不同步，所以脱落和新生在同一天发生。The scalp carries about 100,000 follicles, and shedding roughly 50 to 100 hairs a day is normal turnover rather than mass death. Each strand follows its own cycle. In anagen the bulb divides actively and the shaft keeps elongating; scalp anagen lasts about 2 to 6 years. Catagen lasts about 2 to 3 weeks, the lower follicle shortens, the papilla moves up and cells undergo apoptosis. Telogen lasts about 3 months, the shaft loosens and sheds before a new cycle takes over the same spot. Follicles in one region are not synchronized, so shedding and regrowth happen on the same day.[2]

判断掉发是否异常，要看数量、速度和分布。短期内脱落明显增多、一把一把地掉，或发缝逐渐变宽、发际线后退，就超出了日常轮换的范围。一类常见情况是休止期脱发：高热、手术、重大压力、快速减重、产后等事件让大批毛囊提前离开生长期、进入休止期，经过 2–3 个月的潜伏期后集中脱落；诱因去除后，多数毛囊会重新进入生长期，头发逐步恢复，过程常以月计算。Whether shedding is abnormal depends on amount, speed and pattern. A sharp rise in loss, handfuls of hair coming out, a widening parting or a receding hairline goes beyond daily turnover. One common route is telogen effluvium: fever, surgery, major stress, rapid weight loss or childbirth push a large batch of follicles out of anagen early into telogen, and after a two to three month latent period they shed together. Once the trigger is removed, most follicles re-enter anagen and hair recovers over months rather than days.[2][5]

雄激素性脱发是另一条机制线。睾酮在 5α-还原酶作用下生成双氢睾酮（DHT），DHT 结合毛乳头细胞上的受体，使毛囊生长期缩短、毛囊微型化：新生发干一代比一代细、软，终毛逐渐变成浅色细软的毳毛，头皮看起来更露白。遗传决定了毛囊对 DHT 的敏感程度，男性多表现为发际线后移与头顶稀疏，女性多表现为发缝变宽；它不是发根被油脂堵死，而是毛囊本身的生长能力被激素信号改写。Androgenetic alopecia runs a separate mechanism. Testosterone is converted by 5-alpha-reductase into dihydrotestosterone, or DHT, which binds receptors on papilla cells and shortens anagen while miniaturizing the follicle: each new shaft grows thinner and softer, terminal hairs gradually become fine vellus hairs, and more scalp shows. Genetics sets how sensitive a follicle is to DHT. Men often show a receding hairline and crown thinning, women a widening parting. The cause is not oil blocking roots but hormonal signals rewriting the follicle's growth capacity.[4][5]

营养和全身状态也会反映在头发上。毛球分裂速度快，对蛋白质、铁、锌、维生素 D 等供应敏感；缺铁性贫血、甲状腺功能异常、明显营养不良时，常伴随弥漫性脱落。这类缺乏通常表现为整体变稀，而不是孤立的圆形秃斑；恢复供应后，毛囊代谢需要时间恢复，不会在几天内见效。Nutrition and general state also reach the hair. The dividing bulb is sensitive to protein, iron, zinc and vitamin D supply; iron-deficiency anemia, thyroid disorders and marked malnutrition can accompany diffuse shedding. Such deficiency usually thins the whole scalp rather than producing isolated round patches, and after supply is restored follicle metabolism takes time to recover, so results do not appear within days.[2][5]

边界：斑片状脱发、头皮红斑鳞屑、牵拉后成束脱落，或短期内大量掉发，需要皮肤科评估；本章只讲毛囊周期与激素、营养相关的机制，不展开脱发分型诊断、用药方案、植发或偏方。日常区分的关键是：每日 50–100 根左右、整体密度不变属于正常轮换，数量持续上升或可见稀疏才需要进一步检查。Boundary: patchy hair loss, red scaly scalp, shedding in pulled bundles or a sudden large increase needs dermatological evaluation. This chapter covers follicle cycles and hormone or nutrition mechanisms only, not diagnosis of hair-loss types, medication courses, hair transplantation or folk remedies. The practical dividing line is that about 50 to 100 hairs a day with unchanged overall density is normal turnover; a persistent rise or visible thinning warrants further assessment.[4]

06为什么头发容易出油？Why does the hair and scalp get oily easily?

皮脂腺受雄激素与遗传调控分泌皮脂，沿毛干铺展成膜；出油多不等于脱发，过度清洁可代偿性出油。Androgens and genetics set sebum output; sebum spreads along shafts as a lipid film. Oiliness does not mean hair loss, and over-cleaning can raise oil.

头皮和面部皮肤一样密布皮脂腺。皮脂腺贴附在毛囊旁，导管开口于毛囊漏斗部，分泌的皮脂主要由甘油三酯、蜡酯、角鲨烷和游离脂肪酸组成，经毛干铺到皮肤表面，与汗液乳化形成一层皮脂膜。这层膜润滑毛发、减少角质层水分散失，并在表面抑制一部分微生物过度繁殖；皮脂中的脂质也参与维持头皮屏障的完整。头皮毛囊密度高，皮脂腺数量随之增多，所以头部是全身出油最明显的区域之一，额头、鼻周与头皮常连成一片皮脂腺丰富的区域。The scalp, like facial skin, is densely packed with sebaceous glands. Each gland sits beside a follicle and drains through a duct into the follicle funnel. Its sebum, mainly triglycerides, wax esters, squalene and fatty acids, spreads along the shaft to the surface, where it emulsifies with sweat into a lipid film that lubricates hair, limits water loss and holds back some microbes. High follicle density also means many glands, so the head is one of the oiliest areas of the body.[2][3]

皮脂分泌量主要由雄激素背景和遗传决定。皮脂腺对雄激素敏感，青春期前后随着性激素水平上升，分泌明显变旺，这也是少年以前头皮通常偏干爽的原因之一。温度高、湿度大、情绪紧张和出汗都会让观感更油；同一个人头皮不同部位的出油量也不均匀，头顶和前额通常更明显，枕部相对少一些。Secretion volume is set mainly by androgen background and genetics. Sebaceous glands are sensitive to androgens, and output rises noticeably around puberty. Heat, humidity, stress and sweating all make the oily appearance stronger, and oiliness is uneven across the scalp, usually heaviest at the crown and forehead.[2]

头发显油，是因为皮脂沿毛干向外铺展；头发越短越细，皮脂越容易在发干上铺开成可见的油亮层，长发则常把油脂集中在发根位置。头皮上的马拉色菌等菌群以皮脂为营养，分解后产生游离脂肪酸，可能刺激头皮、加重头屑和瘙痒。出油多并不等于脱发：两者常共享雄激素这一背景因素，但油脂本身并不把毛囊堵死，也不直接让头发变细；把脱发归因于没洗干净，是把两件相关但不同的事混在了一起。Hair looks oily because sebum spreads outward along the shaft; short, fine hair lets the lipid layer spread into a visible sheen. Malassezia and other scalp flora break sebum down into free fatty acids, which can irritate the scalp and worsen dandruff and itching. Oily scalp does not equal hair loss: the two often share androgen background, but oil itself does not block follicles or directly thin the hair.[2][4]

清洁的作用是用表面活性剂带走表面皮脂、灰尘和已脱落的角质。洗发频率按出油量调整即可，出油多的人可以适当增加次数，干燥头皮则应减少。过度清洁会破坏皮脂膜，头皮可能干燥紧绷、脱屑，随后出现代偿性出油；护发素和发膜主要涂发干，避开头皮可以减少残留黏腻，也避免给微生物留下多余脂质。Cleaning removes surface sebum, dust and shed keratin. Washing frequency should follow oiliness; over-cleaning damages the lipid film, leaving the scalp tight and dry, followed by compensatory oil production. Conditioners and masks are applied to the shaft, and keeping them off the scalp reduces sticky residue.[2][3]

边界：头皮出现红斑、厚屑、明显瘙痒、脓疱或成块脱发，需要皮肤科评估；本章讲皮脂分泌与头皮菌群的机制，不评测具体洗发水成分、控油产品或治疗方案。头发容易出油是常见生理差异，不等于头皮不健康，也不能单凭出油量判断激素水平或推断脱发风险。Boundary: red patches, thick scales, marked itching, pustules or patchy shedding need dermatological evaluation. This chapter explains sebum secretion and scalp flora, not shampoo ingredients, oil-control products or treatment plans. An oily scalp is a common physiological variation, not by itself a sign of poor scalp health, and oiliness alone cannot judge hormone levels.[4]
