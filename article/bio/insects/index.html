01昆虫为什么能飞？膜质翅、外骨骼和蜕皮怎样构成它的身体方案？How do insects fly, and how do the membrane wing, exoskeleton and moulting form their body plan?

三段体躯六足两对翅，几丁质外骨骼保护并限制生长，蜕皮完成体积增长。Three-body-segment plan with six legs and wings; chitin exoskeleton protects and caps growth, moulting adds size.

昆虫飞行与脊椎动物飞行是两套独立方案。鸟翼、蝙蝠翼和昆虫翅膀并非同源结构：脊椎动物翅膀由前肢改造，昆虫翅是胸背板向外延伸的膜质结构，翅脉支撑膜面。鸟、蝙蝠和昆虫都能飞，属于趋同演化，不代表亲缘接近。OpenStax 在节肢动物一节把附肢结构与脊椎动物肢体的区分作为比较基础。Insect flight and vertebrate flight are two independent solutions. Bird wings, bat wings and insect wings are not homologous: vertebrate wings are modified forelimbs, while an insect wing is a membrane outgrowth from the thoracic tergite, braced by veins. Birds, bats and insects all fly through convergence, not because they are close relatives. OpenStax uses the distinction between arthropod appendages and vertebrate limbs as its comparison basis in the arthropod section.[3][5]

昆虫身体分头、胸、腹三段，胸部具六足，成虫多数具两对膜质翅。翅薄而轻，翅脉既支撑膜面又输送血淋巴；翅拍动频率高，小型昆虫依靠翅根肌肉直接或间接驱动飞行。苍蝇后翅退化为平衡棒，在飞行中感知姿态，所以能迅速闪避拍击；翅的开合与胸部骨片弹性配合，不需要逐步长大的前肢骨骼。翅根肌肉拉动胸壁变形，间接带动翅面上下拍动，飞行中腹部气管搏动辅助空气交换。The insect body has three regions: head, thorax and abdomen; the thorax bears six legs, and most adults carry two pairs of membranous wings. The wings are thin and light, with veins that both brace the membrane and carry haemolymph. Beat frequency is high, and small insects drive flight through direct or indirect muscles at the wing root. Flies have hind wings reduced to halteres that sense attitude in flight, which lets them dodge a swat quickly; wing motion works with thoracic plate elasticity rather than a growing forelimb skeleton.[1][5]

外骨骼是几丁质构成的硬质外壳，包裹身体，防止失水和机械损伤，肌肉附着在内壁。外壳不能随身体长大，昆虫必须周期性蜕皮：旧外骨骼裂开退出，新壳在硬化前短暂伸展，再鞣化变硬。每次蜕皮之间称为龄期，蜕皮次数因类群而异，刚蜕皮的个体颜色浅、壳软，需要一段时间恢复。The exoskeleton is a hard chitinous shell that wraps the body, prevents water loss and mechanical injury, and provides inner walls for muscle attachment. Because the shell cannot grow with the animal, insects moult periodically: the old cuticle splits and the animal exits, the new shell stretches briefly before it sclerotises and hardens. The interval between moults is an instar, and the number of moults varies by group; newly moulted animals are pale and soft for a while.[1]

体壁与内部结构配合出小型体型。分节附肢、气管系统直接把空气送到组织，血淋巴在体腔中循环；空气经体节两侧的气门进入气管，气管逐级分支成微气管贴近组织细胞，气体以扩散为主，腹部搏动辅助通风，氧气不经血淋巴运输，这与脊椎动物靠循环系统运氧不同；小型体型下气管扩散足以供氧，这也限制了昆虫不能按线性比例无限放大。翅和外骨骼的组合让昆虫在陆地和空中占住微小生态位，许多类群能利用花、枯枝落叶和小型寄主体内等狭小空间。体型差异帮助昆虫避开被捕食者和竞争者，微小体型让同一个树冠层就能容纳取食不同部位的多种种类。Cuticle and internal structure suit a small body. Segmented appendages and a tracheal system carry air directly to tissues, while haemolymph circulates in the body cavity. Air enters through spiracles on the body segments, branches through tracheae into tracheoles close to tissue cells, and moves mainly by diffusion with abdominal pumping aiding ventilation; oxygen is not carried by haemolymph, unlike vertebrate circulatory oxygen transport. At small size, tracheal diffusion supplies enough oxygen, which also limits how far insects can scale up linearly. The combination of wing and exoskeleton lets insects occupy tiny terrestrial and aerial niches, including flowers, litter and the narrow space inside small host bodies.[1]

边界：趋同只说明功能相似，不能据此推断同源；翅的具体来源在不同昆虫类群中有差异。蜕皮受激素调控，并非单纯长大换壳；外骨骼既保护也限制生长和体型上限。把昆虫翅膀当成缩小的鸟翼，会误解其膜质结构、翅脉支撑和胸部肌肉驱动方式。The boundary: convergence shows functional similarity only, not homology; wing origins differ among insect groups. Moulting is hormone-regulated rather than a simple shell swap, and the exoskeleton both protects and caps growth and body size. Treating an insect wing as a scaled-down bird wing misunderstands its membrane, vein bracing and thoracic-muscle drive.[3][5]

02毛毛虫变成蝴蝶时身体怎样重构？完全变态和不完全变态差在哪里？How is the body rebuilt when a caterpillar becomes a butterfly, and how do complete and incomplete metamorphosis differ?

卵-幼虫-蛹-成虫四阶段重构身体；渐变态无蛹期，若虫渐次蜕皮长大。Egg-larva-pupa-adult remodels the body; gradual metamorphosis lacks a pupa, nymphs grow through moults.

完全变态经过卵、幼虫、蛹、成虫四个阶段。蝴蝶幼虫也就是毛毛虫，取食植物茎叶，身体以取食生长为主；成熟后化蛹，蛹内幼虫组织被解体重构，成虫盘发育出翅、足和口器。以帝王蝶为例，蛹期约十天左右羽化为成虫；这一过程不是简单长大，而是两套身体方案之间的切换。成虫盘在胚胎后期就已决定未来翅和口器的位置，蛹期并非从头长出全部器官。Holometabolous development passes through egg, larva, pupa and adult. The butterfly larva, the caterpillar, feeds on leaves and grows, then pupates; inside the pupa larval tissue is disassembled and imaginal discs grow into wings, legs and mouthparts. In monarchs the pupal stage lasts about ten days before adult emergence; this is not simple growth but a switch between two body plans.[1]

幼虫和成虫占据不同食物与生境，减少同种竞争。毛虫咀嚼叶片，成虫吸食花蜜；口器、足和消化系统分别适应各自阶段。完全变态在昆虫中占比很高，是甲虫、蜂、蝶、蝇等大类的共同发育模式，幼虫与成虫甚至可以生活在水中和空中两类环境里。Larva and adult occupy different foods and habitats, reducing competition within the species. Caterpillars chew leaves while adults sip nectar; mouthparts, legs and digestive tract each match their own stage. Complete metamorphosis is widespread and shared by beetles, bees, butterflies and flies; larva and adult may even live in water and air respectively.[1][3]

不完全变态也就是渐变态，没有蛹期，卵孵化出若虫，形态与成虫相近，翅膀以翅芽形式逐步在外骨骼外生长，多次蜕皮后达到成虫体型。蝗虫、螳螂、蝽类属于此类。若虫每蜕一次皮，翅芽长大一龄，外骨骼硬化前身体可略微伸展。两类变态方式对应不同生态策略，不能用都要蜕皮来概括全部昆虫发育，若虫与成虫共享食性，竞争方式也不同。Hemimetabolous, or gradual, development has no pupal stage. The egg hatches a nymph similar in shape to the adult, with wings growing outward as wing buds through successive moults until adult size. Locusts, mantises and true bugs follow this route. The two metamorphosis strategies suit different ecologies and cannot be reduced to the fact that both moult: nymphs and adults share food, so competition differs too.[1][5]

蝉代表特殊的世代安排。若虫在地下吸食树根汁液多年，北美周期蝉以 13 年或 17 年周期集中出土，羽化后成虫主要完成繁殖；地下长发育期与地上短暂繁殖期分工。多年地下生活靠的是若虫阶段持续取食和蜕皮，而不是成虫延长寿命；集中出土还可以通过数量压过捕食者取食压力。固定年份周期本身降低与捕食者种群数量同步的概率，大批出现时被捕食个体的比例被摊薄。Cicadas represent a special life schedule. Nymphs feed on root sap underground for years, and North American periodical cicadas emerge in concentrated 13- or 17-year cycles, after which adults mainly breed. The long underground phase and brief above-ground reproductive phase are divided between stages; the years underground come from continuous nymphal feeding and moulting, not from an extended adult life. Massed emergence also overwhelms predator feeding pressure by sheer numbers.[1]

边界：蛹期重构程度因类群不同，部分成虫结构在幼虫期已有成虫盘雏形；发育时间受温度和食物影响，不能把某一物种的天数当成通用值。变态是适应分化，但并非所有昆虫都走同一套路径；环境压力也会改变发育进度，滞育可以让个体等待适宜季节再继续发育。The boundary: degree of pupal remodelling varies by group, and some adult structures already appear as imaginal discs in the larva. Development time depends on temperature and food, so one species' days are not a universal value. Metamorphosis is adaptive divergence, but not all insects follow the same path; environmental pressure can alter progress, and diapause lets an individual wait for a favourable season.[3][5]

03蚁群和蜂群怎样分工，靠什么信息组织出行、筑巢和防御？How do ant and bee colonies divide labour, and what signals organise travel, nest building and defence?

繁殖品级与工作品级分工，追踪和报警信息素协调局部行为，汇总成群体决策。Reproductive and working castes divide labour; trail and alarm pheromones coordinate local behaviour into group decisions.

蚁群和蜂群由繁殖品级和工作品级组成。蜂群中有蜂王、工蜂和雄蜂，蚁群有蚁后、工蚁，兵蚁常是特化工蚁；品级不是个体随意选择，而是由营养、信息素和发育条件分化出的分工群体。整群在繁殖、觅食和防御上协作，常被比作超个体，但个体仍有各自的生理限制，不能把群体当成一个统一意识。蜂王由受精卵发育并经特殊饲喂，体型更大、负责持续产卵；工蜂是不育的雌性个体，承担巢内大部分劳作。Ant and bee colonies consist of reproductive and working castes. A honeybee hive has a queen, workers and drones; an ant colony has a queen and workers, with soldiers often a specialised worker form. Castes are not chosen at will but develop through nutrition, pheromones and developmental conditions into a division of labour. The group cooperates in reproduction, foraging and defence and is often likened to a superorganism, yet each individual has its own physiological limits; the colony is not a single unified mind.[1][4]

信息素是群体协调的主要信号。工蚁沿食物路径留下追踪信息素，同伴跟随气味形成蚁路；巢内信息素维持品级识别和秩序。蚂蚁搬家常与巢址条件变化、食物源转移或群体扩张有关，并非预知天气；路径选择通过个体往返反馈，较强气味被更多同伴重复标记，逐渐压倒较弱路径。Pheromones are the main coordination signal. Workers lay trail pheromones along food paths, and nestmates follow the scent to form ant lines; nest pheromones maintain caste recognition and order. Ant nest moves usually follow changes in nest-site conditions, food-source shifts or colony expansion, not weather prediction. Route choice feeds back through individual trips: stronger scent is repeated by more nestmates and gradually overwhelms weaker trails.[1][6]

筑巢结构体现集体建造。蜂巢巢室多为六边形，相同周长下围出的面积最大、蜂蜡材料最省；工蜂分泌蜡质筑造，巢室同时用于育幼和储食。蜂群防守时集体驱赶入侵者，蜂刺和报警信息素联动，个体防御触发群体响应。蚁巢通道和巢室也按温度、湿度与育幼需求分区挖掘。巢室角度和尺寸由工蜂依据巢址温度和群体规模调整，并非单只蜜蜂预先设计好图纸。Nest building shows collective construction. Honeycomb cells are mostly hexagonal: for a given perimeter, this shape encloses the largest area with the least wax; workers secrete wax and the cells serve both rearing and food storage. When defending the hive, bees collectively drive off intruders, with stings and alarm pheromones linked so individual defence triggers a group response. Ant tunnels and chambers are also dug according to temperature, humidity and brood needs.[1]

群体决策来自局部规则汇总。没有中央指挥者；个体依据信息素、触须交流和食物收益调整行动，路径优劣由往返次数筛选。群体规模扩大时，分工和通讯范围同步变化；缺乏采集者时巢群扩张受限，不会无限变大。新群通过婚飞交配、筑新巢建立，原有巢穴承载能力存在上限。Group decisions arise from aggregating local rules. There is no central commander; individuals adjust action using pheromones, antenna contact and food payoff, and route quality is filtered by trip frequency. As colony size grows, division of labour and communication range change together; without foragers the colony cannot expand indefinitely. New colonies are founded through nuptial flight, mating and a fresh nest, and the original nest has a carrying limit.[1][4]

边界：社会性程度在昆虫中差别很大，独居蜂和独居蚁不按品级分工；信息素信号需要结合实验观察解释，不能把蚂蚁行为直接拟人化。超个体是类比，不等于群体具有统一意识；品级协作的演化路径在不同膜翅目类群中独立出现过，不能据此推断所有社会昆虫同源。The boundary: sociality varies widely among insects; solitary bees and ants have no castes. Pheromone signals need experimental observation and should not be anthropomorphised. Superorganism is an analogy, not a unified mind, and caste cooperation evolved independently in different hymenopteran groups, so social insects cannot be assumed homologous.[3][6]

04昆虫怎样取食、防御和发光？口器、跳跃与发光机制有何差别？How do insects feed, defend themselves and glow? How do mouthparts, jumping and light production differ?

口器匹配食性，弹性后腿与警告色化学防御自保，荧光素酶反应产生近冷光。Mouthparts match diet; elastic hind legs, warning colour and chemical defence protect; luciferase reaction yields near-cold light.

昆虫口器对应不同取食方式。蝶蛾虹吸式口器吸取花蜜，蚊类刺吸式口器刺入皮肤吸取血液，甲虫咀嚼式口器啃食固体食物；瓢虫成虫和幼虫捕食蚜虫，是常见的捕食性益虫。口器形态与消化道酶系配合，决定一种昆虫能利用哪些食物资源，植食、捕食、寄生和腐食各有对应的结构安排。寄生类口器和产卵器进入寄主体内，利用寄主组织完成发育，成体与幼体的取食位置也可能不同。Insect mouthparts match different feeding modes. Butterflies and moths use a siphoning proboscis for nectar, mosquitoes piercing-sucking mouthparts for blood, and beetles chewing mandibles for solid food; ladybird adults and larvae prey on aphids and are common predatory beneficial insects. Mouthpart shape works with digestive enzymes to determine which food resources an insect can use, with separate arrangements for herbivory, predation, parasitism and scavenging.[1][5][6]

雌蚊吸血与繁殖有关。雄蚊和多数蚊类取食植物汁液，雌蚊需要血液中的蛋白质发育卵粒；被叮咬偏好与体味、二氧化碳和体温等线索有关，不同个体招引程度不同。蚊虫传播疾病的环节是病原随吸血过程进入或离开宿主，并非叮咬本身制造疾病；口器刺入时还会分泌唾液，引起局部瘙痒反应。Female mosquito blood feeding ties to reproduction. Males and most other mosquitoes take plant juices, while females need blood protein to develop eggs; biting preference responds to body odour, carbon dioxide and temperature, and individuals differ in attractiveness. Disease transmission occurs when a pathogen enters or leaves the host during blood feeding; the bite itself does not create the disease. Saliva injected during piercing causes local itching.[1]

跳跃和防御是另一套适应。跳蚤后腿特化，依靠弹性蛋白储存能量后释放，跳跃距离可超过自身体长数十倍；甲虫鞘翅保护后翅，瓢虫鲜艳色斑警告天敌；部分昆虫拟态环境或积累化学防御物质，把毒素或异味贮存在体内，受惊时释放。体色、体型和行为拟态可以配合，降低被发现或被取食的概率。跳蚤跳跃前肌肉缓慢压缩弹性垫，释放时靠储能组织回弹，后腿本身并不直接发力蹬地。Jumping and defence are another set of adaptations. Fleas have specialised hind legs that store energy in elastic protein and release it, jumping distances tens of times their body length; beetle elytra protect the hind wings, and ladybird warning colouration advertises to predators. Some insects mimic the environment or accumulate chemical defence, storing toxins or unpleasant compounds for release when disturbed. Colour, shape and behavioural mimicry can combine to lower detection or consumption.[1][6]

萤火虫发光是生物发光机制。腹部发光器官含荧光素和荧光素酶，氧气参与反应把化学能直接转为光能，几乎不产生热，所以称为冷光；闪光节奏用于同类识别和求偶通讯。发光效率远高于白炽灯泡的热辐射方式，但不同发光昆虫的信号模式差别很大，部分种类的雌虫还会模仿其他种类闪光来捕食。Firefly light is a bioluminescent mechanism. The abdominal light organ holds luciferin and luciferase; oxygen participates in a reaction that converts chemical energy almost directly to light with little heat, hence cold light. Flash rhythm serves species recognition and mating communication. Efficiency far exceeds the heat radiation of an incandescent bulb, but signal patterns differ among glowing insects; some female fireflies mimic other species' flashes to prey on them.[1]

边界：取食和防御策略按类群和生境分化，不能把一种昆虫的食性推广到整个目；益虫、害虫是按人类农业和健康利益划分，生态功能上各有位置。发光机制不等于所有荧光生物都用同一套酶系；防御化学物质的积累依赖寄主食物来源，脱离食物条件，毒性和警告色都不能稳定维持。The boundary: feeding and defence strategies vary by group and habitat, so one insect's diet cannot be extended to a whole order. Beneficial or pest labels follow human agricultural and health interests, while ecological roles vary. Bioluminescence does not mean every glowing organism uses the same enzyme system; defensive chemical accumulation depends on host food, and without those food conditions toxicity and warning colouration cannot be maintained.[1][6]

05为什么昆虫物种这么多？体型放大后力量还能按比例变大吗，其他节肢动物怎样对照？Why are there so many insect species? Does strength scale up proportionally, and how do other arthropods compare?

约一百万已描述种，小体型、翅、变态与寄主分化叠加；平方-立方与气管供氧限制放大。About a million described species from small size, wings, metamorphosis and host split; square-cube and tracheal supply cap enlargement.

昆虫是动物界物种最丰富的类群。已描述昆虫约一百万种，占已描述动物种的大部分；估计现存总数可能在数百万到上千万之间，大量热带昆虫尚未分类。物种繁盛与体型小、翅的出现、变态发育和寄主分化等因素叠加有关，OpenStax 在生物多样性一节给出描述种与估计种的区分口径，不能把估计区间当成清点结果。Insects are the most species-rich animal group. Roughly one million insect species have been described, accounting for most described animal species; estimated living totals range from several million to ten million, with many tropical insects still undescribed. Richness comes from small size, the origin of wings, metamorphic development and host differentiation stacked together. OpenStax distinguishes described species from estimated totals in its biodiversity section, so an estimate range cannot be read as a census.[1][4][7]

小体型带来利用微小生境的优势，但也受物理约束。按平方-立方关系，体长放大时体重按立方增长，肌肉和骨骼截面按平方增长；等比例放大后相对力量下降，外骨骼和气管系统也难以支撑巨型昆虫。蚂蚁按比例放大到人那么大，肢体承重、外骨骼鞣化和气管供氧都无法维持原有的相对表现，这是材料与扩散约束，而不是单纯力气问题。外骨骼在体积放大后厚度增长不足，气管末端的扩散距离也会成为供氧瓶颈，无法支撑巨型陆生昆虫。Small size brings access to tiny niches, but physical constraints apply. Under the square-cube relationship, body mass grows with the cube of length while muscle and bone cross-section grow with the square; scaled up isometrically, relative strength falls, and the exoskeleton and tracheal system cannot support giant insects. An ant enlarged to human size could not maintain its relative performance in limb load, cuticle sclerotisation or tracheal oxygen supply; this is a material and diffusion constraint, not a raw-strength issue.[1]

昆虫与其他节肢动物同属一门但各有支系：蜘蛛、蜱螨等蛛形纲四对足、无翅；虾、蟹等甲壳纲多水生、具鳃；蜈蚣、马陆等多足纲足数多、步行为主。比较这些类群能看清昆虫六足、两对翅和陆上气管的独特组合，也能看到附肢在不同支系中改造为螯、触角和游泳足。Insects share the arthropod phylum with other branches: arachnids such as spiders and mites have four pairs of legs and no wings; crustaceans such as shrimp and crabs are mostly aquatic with gills; myriapods such as centipedes and millipedes have many walking legs. Comparing these groups clarifies the unique combination of six legs, two wing pairs and terrestrial tracheae in insects, and shows appendages modified into chelae, antennae and swimming legs in other branches.[1][2]

非节肢动物的无脊椎动物对照：蜗牛等软体动物具外套膜和贝壳，靠肌肉足爬行，与昆虫外骨骼和分节附肢路线不同。螃蟹的螯是附肢特化用于取食和防御，与昆虫口器来源不同；这些对照说明无脊椎不是单一身体方案，胚胎发育、体腔和附肢结构才是分类依据。Non-arthropod invertebrates provide a contrast: molluscs such as snails have a mantle and shell and crawl on a muscular foot, a different route from the arthropod exoskeleton and segmented appendages. The crab chela is an appendage specialised for feeding and defence, not homologous to the insect mouthpart. These contrasts show that invertebrate is not one body plan; classification rests on embryonic development, body cavity and appendage structure.[1][2]

边界：物种估计区间受取样和分类影响，描述种数不等于现存总数；体型比例问题必须结合材料强度、呼吸和循环方式一起看，不能只比较腿的粗细。节肢动物和软体动物的划分依据胚胎发育、体腔和附肢结构，表面相似的爬行或取食方式不代表亲缘接近；翅和小体型是繁盛因素之一，但不能单独解释全部物种多样性。The boundary: species estimate ranges depend on sampling and taxonomy, and described counts are not living totals. Scale questions must consider material strength, respiration and circulation together rather than leg thickness alone. Arthropod and mollusc divisions rest on development, body cavity and appendage structure; superficially similar crawling or feeding does not imply close relationship. Wings and small size contribute to richness but cannot alone explain all species diversity.[2][7]

06蜜蜂为什么能飞，又为什么要采蜜、怕烟，蜇人后为什么会死？How do bees fly, why do they collect nectar, fear smoke and die after stinging?

高频拍翅加前缘涡产生动态升力；花蜜酿成蜂蜜储粮，烟扰嗅觉，倒刺蜇针一次性防御。High-frequency beats and leading-edge vortex give dynamic lift; nectar ripens to honey, smoke masks alarm, barbed stingers are fatal.

蜜蜂能飞，靠的不是一把小翅膀在空气里划动，而是胸部肌肉驱动的高频拍翅加非定常气动力。蜜蜂没有连到翅尖的大块胸肌，靠胸腔内的间接飞行肌：背纵肌和背腹肌交替收缩，把胸壁拉长或压扁，胸壁变形通过翅基关节带动翅膀上下拍动。工蜂拍翅频率大约每秒 200 到 230 次，翅薄而小，前后翅由翅钩连成整体翼面。早年用固定翼升力公式估算蜜蜂翅膀，会得出飞不起来的结论；该公式假设稳定气流和平板翼型，漏掉了扑翼的动态过程。Bees fly not by paddling a small wing through air but by thorax-driven high-frequency beats plus unsteady aerodynamics. They have no large muscle attached to the wing tip; instead indirect flight muscles inside the thorax alternate contraction. Dorsolongitudinal and dorsoventral muscles squeeze or flatten the thorax, and thoracic deformation drives the wing up and down through the wing-base joint. A worker beats its wing roughly 200 to 230 times per second; the wings are thin and small, fore and hind wings locked into one surface by hamuli. Early fixed-wing lift calculations suggested bees should not fly, but they assume steady flow and a flat plate, missing flapping dynamics.[1][8]

真正提供升力的是扑翼过程中的涡流和转动效应。翅在下拍前缘形成前缘涡，把更多空气卷向翼面，产生延迟失稳的额外升力；上拍时翅面翻转，转动和尾部涡旋再补一部分力。翅膀每一拍都在扰动空气并重新利用前一拍留下的气流，属于非定常气动机制。胸部弹性与肌肉共振配合，使高频拍翅以较低能耗维持悬停和直线飞行，匹配蜂类小体型、高翅载荷的尺度。Lift comes from vortices and rotation during the stroke. On the downstroke a stable leading-edge vortex forms at the wing front, rolling more air over the surface and adding delayed-stall lift; on the upstroke the wing flips, and wing rotation and wake vortices contribute part of the force again. Each beat disturbs the air and reuses airflow left by the previous beat, an unsteady mechanism. Thorax elasticity and muscle resonance keep the high-frequency beat hovering and cruising at modest extra energy cost. Small body size and high wing loading match this dynamic lift scheme.[1][8]

采蜜和储粮是蜂群的另一套分工。外出工蜂采集蔗糖为主的花蜜，吸入蜜囊带回蜂巢；巢内工蜂反复反吐，在蜜囊和口器之间搬运加工，同时扇动翅膀加速通风，把花蜜中多余水分蒸发掉。蔗糖经酶转化为葡萄糖和果糖，浓缩成蜂蜜长期储存，作为越冬和缺花期食物；花粉由后足花粉篮成团携带回巢，作为幼虫发育所需的蛋白来源。花蜜供碳水能量，花粉供蛋白营养，支撑工蜂、蜂王、雄蜂和育幼。Foraging and storage are a separate colony division. Field workers collect nectar, mainly sucrose, in the honey stomach and bring it back; hive workers regurgitate it repeatedly, processing between honey stomach and mouthparts while fanning wings to ventilate and evaporate excess water. Enzymes convert sucrose into glucose and fructose, and the concentrated honey is stored long term as food through winter and dearth. Pollen is carried back in pellets on the hind-leg pollen baskets, supplying protein for larval development. Nectar and pollen supply carbohydrate and protein, supporting workers, queen, drones and brood.[1][6]

防御靠信息素和蜇针联动。受威胁时守卫蜂释放报警信息素，主要成分是乙酸异戊酯，召唤同伴加入防御；蜂群靠近时，烟雾能遮蔽这种化学信号并抑制蜜蜂嗅觉，群体警报难以扩散，蜜蜂趋于安静，同时出现吸蜜备迁的反应。需要区分：烟不是把蜜蜂熏晕。工蜂蜇针末端带倒刺，刺入人的厚皮后倒钩挂住皮肤无法拔出；蜜蜂挣脱时撕裂腹部和部分内脏，毒囊留在伤口继续泵注毒液，工蜂不久死亡。胡蜂、熊蜂的蜇针光滑，没有同类倒刺，可以反复蜇刺。Defence links pheromones and the stinger. On threat, guard bees release alarm pheromone, one major component being isoamyl acetate; the scent calls nestmates to join defence. Near the colony, smoke masks this chemical signal and depresses bee olfaction, so the alarm cannot spread and bees settle into quiet, with an engorging response resembling preparation for relocation. Smoke does not asphyxiate the bees. The worker stinger carries barbs at its tip; once lodged in thick mammalian skin the hooks hold fast and cannot be withdrawn. Pulling away tears the abdomen and internal organs, the venom sac stays in the wound pumping venom, and the worker dies soon after. Wasps and bumblebees have smooth stingers without these barbs and can sting repeatedly.[1][6]

边界：蜂类飞行参数因物种、体重和负载变化，200 到 230 赫兹是膜翅目飞行肌收缩率的测量区间，不能当作每只蜜蜂的固定值。采蜜、酿蜜和防御行为是群体分工，个体工蜂不完成全部环节；报警反应和蜇针致死性也随蜂种、温度和受威胁程度不同。蜜蜂不符合空气动力学的说法沿用早期固定翼估算；现代高速影像与力学测量支持涡流加拍翼的动态方案。The boundary: flight parameters vary with species, body weight and load; 200 to 230 Hz is a measured range for hymenopteran flight muscle, not a fixed value for every bee. Foraging, ripening and defence are colony tasks, and one worker does not complete every step; alarm response and stinger fatality vary by species, temperature and threat. The claim that bees violate aerodynamics came from early fixed-wing estimates; high-speed imaging now supports vortex-based flapping flight.[1][8]

07昆虫为什么能在墙上爬，又为什么会被灯光吸引？Why can insects climb walls, and why are they attracted to lights?

爪间垫靠脂质润湿毛细黏附；趋光源于天体导航被近距离点光源打乱成盘旋轨迹。Arolia grip by wet capillary adhesion; light attraction comes from celestial navigation disrupted by a nearby point source.

昆虫能在光滑墙面和天花板上爬行，靠的是足端两套结构配合：爪和爪间垫。足末端是一对弯曲的爪，遇到粗糙表面时钩住纹理和缝隙提供抓力；两爪之间还有一个柔软的囊状垫，称为爪间垫（arolium），表面是细密的表皮纹理。在玻璃、叶片蜡质层这类光滑表面上，爪找不到着力点，爪间垫展开贴上去，成为主要附着结构。垫可以随每一步主动展开、放松后弹性回缩剥离，所以昆虫贴得上也拔得下，不会被粘住。Insects climb smooth walls and ceilings with two structures at the foot: claws and an adhesive pad. The foot tip bears a pair of curved claws that grip texture and crevices on rough surfaces. Between them sits a soft sac-like pad, the arolium, with a finely textured cuticle. On smooth surfaces such as glass or waxy leaf cuticle, the claws have nothing to hold, and the arolium unfolds to become the main attachment. The pad actively extends with each step and peels off on elastic recoil, so the insect can both attach and detach rather than staying stuck.[1][9]

爪间垫的黏附属于润湿黏附。垫面会分泌一层表皮脂质液体，液体填充垫与表面之间的微小缝隙，靠毛细力和表面张力形成黏着；接触面越平整，液体膜铺得越匀，黏附越稳。这和壁虎靠刚毛末端范德华力的干黏附是两套机制，昆虫垫子上有液体参与。观察和力学测量显示，垫的展开角度、接触面积和受力方向都随步态调节：拉向身体时垫贴紧，推离时先从边缘剥离再抬足，减少撕下的力气。Arolium adhesion is wet adhesion. The pad surface secretes a cuticular lipid film that fills the micro-gaps between pad and surface; capillary force and surface tension then create the grip. The flatter the contact, the more evenly the film spreads and the stronger the adhesion. This differs from a gecko's dry adhesion by van der Waals forces on setae, because the insect pad involves liquid. Observations and mechanical measurements show pad angle, contact area and force direction adjust with gait: pulled toward the body the pad seals tight, pushed away it peels from the edge before the foot lifts, reducing the force needed to detach.[1][9]

这套方案受体重约束。按平方-立方关系，体长放大时体重按立方增长，附着垫面积只按平方增长；体型越大，单位体重需要的垫面积越大，黏附越难维持。这也是为什么靠足部黏附爬墙的多是小型昆虫，大型节肢动物更多依赖爪和步行姿态。不同类群垫子形态差别明显：蚁和蜂的爪间垫可主动转动，蝇类用垫状爪垫，甲虫和螳螂另有各自的足端结构；黏附能力同时受表面粗糙度、湿度和脂质成分影响。This scheme is constrained by body weight. Under the square-cube relationship, mass grows with the cube of length while pad area grows with the square; the larger the body, the more pad area per unit weight is required and the harder adhesion becomes. This is why wall-climbing by pad adhesion belongs mostly to small insects, while large arthropods rely more on claws and walking posture. Pad form differs across groups: ant and bee arolia rotate actively, flies use pulvillar pads, and beetles and mantises have their own foot structures. Adhesion also depends on surface roughness, humidity and lipid composition.[1][9]

夜间围着灯光转的趋光行为，是另一套与导航有关的机制。许多夜行昆虫以月光这类遥远平行光作参考，保持光线与身体固定夹角来直线飞行。人工点光源距离很近，光线呈辐射状散开，昆虫维持固定夹角时轨迹就变成绕光源盘旋，越飞越近。不同昆虫对光谱的偏好不同，有些更趋紫外和短波光线；灯光还会干扰交配、产卵和活动节律，城市夜间照明对夜行昆虫种群有可观测的影响。Night-time circling around lights comes from a separate navigation mechanism. Many nocturnal insects use distant parallel light such as moonlight as a reference, holding a fixed angle between light and body to fly straight. A nearby artificial point source emits radial light; keeping that fixed angle turns the flight path into a spiral around the lamp, drawing the insect closer. Spectral preference differs by species, some favouring ultraviolet and short wavelengths. Light also interferes with mating, oviposition and activity rhythms, and night-time urban lighting has measurable effects on nocturnal insect populations.[1][6]

边界：爬墙黏附和趋光不能混为一谈，一个是足端力学结构，一个是导航线索反应。垫黏附的测量多来自蚁、蜂、蝇等模式类群，不能直接套到所有昆虫；趋光的具体神经机制和物种差异仍在研究中。表面干净度、温湿度和光源波长都会改变观察结果，把昆虫扑灯简单解释成喜欢亮，会忽略天体导航被近距离点光源打乱这一过程。The boundary: pad adhesion and phototaxis should not be conflated; one is a foot-end mechanical structure, the other a response to navigational cues. Adhesion measurements come mostly from ant, bee and fly model groups and cannot be transferred directly to every insect. The neural mechanisms and species differences behind light attraction are still under study. Surface cleanliness, temperature, humidity and lamp wavelength all change the observation; reducing insects' lamp attraction to liking brightness misses how celestial navigation is disrupted by a nearby point source.[1][9]

08蚊子为什么要吸血，蜘蛛为什么不是昆虫？Why do mosquitoes suck blood, and why are spiders not insects?

雌蚊吸血补蛋白铁质育卵；蜘蛛八足两体段无触角，与六足三段的昆虫分属不同纲。Females blood-feed for egg protein and iron; spiders have eight legs, two segments and no antennae, unlike six-legged insects.

蚊子叮人吸血，主要是雌蚊为卵巢发育补充营养。雄蚊和多数未产卵的雌蚊取食花蜜、植物汁液这类糖源；只有交配后的雌蚊寻找血液，血液里的蛋白质和铁用于卵黄合成和卵子成熟。一只雌蚊饱吸一次血后卵巢发育一批卵，产卵后再寻找下一次血餐；不同蚊种偏好的宿主和吸血时段不同。被叮咬后的红肿瘙痒，来自蚊子刺入时注入的唾液蛋白，而不是血液本身；病原微生物正是随吸血和唾液交换在宿主之间传播。Mosquitoes bite and suck blood mainly because females need nutrients for ovarian development. Males and most unfed females take sugar sources such as nectar and plant juices; only mated females seek blood, and its protein and iron support yolk synthesis and egg maturation. One blood meal lets a female develop a batch of eggs, after which she seeks the next meal; species differ in host preference and biting time. The red swelling and itch after a bite come from saliva proteins injected during piercing, not from the blood itself; pathogens pass between hosts through this blood-and-saliva exchange.[1][10]

雌蚊的口器是一套刺吸式结构。外表的下唇像鞘一样包住一束六根口针：两根上颚、两根下颚、上唇和舌，刺入皮肤时下唇向后弯折成弓，口针束钻进毛细血管附近。舌部唾液道把抗凝和麻醉性质的唾液注入，上唇把血液吸进食道。这套结构与蝶蛾虹吸花蜜、甲虫咀嚼固体食物的口器对应不同食性。雌蚊靠体味、呼出的二氧化碳、体温和湿度线索定位宿主，个体之间的招引程度差别明显。The female mouthpart is a piercing-sucking bundle. The outer labium sheathes six stylets: two mandibles, two maxillae, the labrum and the hypopharynx. On piercing, the labium bends back in a bow while the stylet bundle enters near a capillary. A salivary channel in the hypopharynx injects anticoagulant and mildly anaesthetic saliva, and the labrum draws blood into the oesophagus. This contrasts with the siphoning proboscis of butterflies and moths or the chewing mandibles of beetles, matching different diets. Females locate hosts through body odour, exhaled carbon dioxide, temperature and humidity, and individual attractiveness varies widely.[1][10]

蜘蛛常被当成大虫子，但它不是昆虫。昆虫纲的身体方案是头、胸、腹三段，胸部生三对共六条足，成虫多数有一对触角和两对翅；蛛形纲的蜘蛛只有头胸部和腹部两段，生四对共八条足，没有触角，也没有翅。两类动物都属节肢动物门，体表有外骨骼、分节附肢，但体段划分和附肢数量从幼体发育阶段就分开，分类依据是胚胎发育、体腔和附肢结构，而不是会不会爬、有没有毒性这类表面印象。Spiders are often called big bugs, but they are not insects. The insect plan has three regions, head, thorax and abdomen, with three pairs, six legs, on the thorax, and most adults carry one pair of antennae and two wing pairs. Arachnid spiders have only cephalothorax and abdomen, four pairs, eight legs, no antennae and no wings. Both groups are arthropods with an exoskeleton and segmented appendages, but body division and appendage count separate them from development onward. Classification rests on embryonic development, body cavity and appendage structure, not surface impressions such as whether they crawl or are venomous.[1][2]

呼吸和取食结构也对不上。昆虫靠气管系统把空气直接送到组织，气门在体侧开合；蜘蛛以书肺为主、气管为辅，书页状薄片扩大气体交换面，没有昆虫式的翅和触角。蜘蛛腹部纺器分泌丝质结网或拖丝，口器为螯肢和须肢，用于抓捕和注射毒液；昆虫口器则分化出咀嚼、虹吸、刺吸等多种类型。比较这些结构可以看出，蜘蛛与昆虫的亲缘距离，比不同昆虫类群之间还远。Respiratory and feeding structures do not match either. Insects use a tracheal system that carries air directly to tissues, with spiracles opening along the flanks; spiders rely mainly on book lungs plus tracheae, with page-like lamellae expanding gas exchange, and lack insect-style wings and antennae. Spider abdominal spinnerets produce silk for webs or draglines, and chelicerae plus pedipalps catch prey and inject venom; insect mouthparts instead diverge into chewing, siphoning and piercing-sucking forms. Comparing these structures shows spiders are farther from insects than different insect groups are from one another.[1][2]

边界：吸血行为只涉及雌蚊的繁殖阶段，不能说所有蚊子都叮人；疾病传播是病原随吸血环节发生，叮咬本身不制造疾病。蜘蛛与昆虫的划分基于形态和发育证据，个别外形或生态位相似不代表同纲。把蜘蛛、蜈蚣、甲壳类一概叫虫子是日常说法，分类上它们分属蛛形纲、多足纲和甲壳纲；判断身体方案要数足数、看体段和触角，而不是看大小或是否居家可见。The boundary: blood feeding belongs only to the female reproductive stage, so not every mosquito bites; disease transmission occurs when pathogens ride the blood meal, and the bite itself does not create the disease. The spider-insect division rests on morphology and developmental evidence, and a similar shape or niche does not mean the same class. Calling spiders, centipedes and crustaceans all bugs is everyday usage; taxonomically they belong to Arachnida, Myriapoda and Crustacea. Judging a body plan means counting legs and checking body segments and antennae, not size or whether the animal is seen indoors.[1][10]

09蜻蜓点水是在做什么？What is a dragonfly doing when it dips its tail in the water?

点水是雌虫把卵产入水中的繁殖行为，卵和稚虫都在水里发育，羽化后才离水飞行。Dipping the water is the female's egg-laying behaviour; eggs and nymphs develop in water and only the adult leaves the surface to fly.

蜻蜓点水不是喝水，也不是玩耍，而是在产卵。夏天在池塘、河滨和水田边，常见到蜻蜓用腹部末端反复轻点水面，溅起一圈圈细小水花；这只雌蜻蜓正把卵产进水体或贴近水面的水生植物组织里。蜻蜓目昆虫的繁殖依赖水体，成虫在空中交配后，雌虫要把后代安排到能生长发育的水环境中，点水就是这套繁殖动作的最后一环。Dipping the water is not drinking or playing; it is egg laying. In summer a dragonfly by a pond or stream often taps the water surface repeatedly with the tip of its abdomen, raising small splashes. This female is placing her eggs into the water or into the tissue of aquatic plants near the surface. Dragonflies depend on water for reproduction: after mating in flight, the female must place her offspring in an aquatic environment where they can grow, and the water-tapping is the last step of this breeding sequence.[1]

交配在飞行或停歇时完成。雌雄蜻蜓腹部连接成串，精子由雄虫传递到雌虫体内完成受精。不同种类产卵方式有别：有的用产卵器把卵插进水面下的植物茎或叶，有的直接把卵产在水面，还有的把卵产进水边腐木或泥里。一只雌虫一生可产卵数十到数百粒，分几天甚至几批产下；卵呈长椭圆形，通常一周左右孵化。Mating happens in flight or at rest. Male and female join their abdomens in tandem, and the male transfers sperm to the female for internal fertilization. Species differ in how they lay eggs: some use an ovipositor to insert eggs into submerged stems or leaves, some drop eggs directly onto the surface, and others place them in rotten wood or mud near the water. A female may lay tens to hundreds of eggs over several days or batches; the eggs are elongated and usually hatch within about a week.[11]

孵化出的稚虫叫水虿，完全在水中生活。水虿没有翅膀，靠直肠鳃在水中呼吸，是凶猛的水生捕食者，用可迅速伸出的下唇（面罩）捕捉孑孓、小蝌蚪、小鱼和其他小型无脊椎动物。它在水下经历约 9–14 次蜕皮，体型逐次增大，稚虫期可持续数月到数年，取决于种类和水温；这段时间占了蜻蜓一生的大部分。The hatched nymph, called a naiad, lives entirely in water. It has no wings and breathes through rectal gills; it is an aggressive aquatic predator that uses a rapidly extendable lower lip, the mask, to catch mosquito larvae, tadpoles, small fish and other small invertebrates. Underwater it moults about 9 to 14 times, growing larger each time; the nymph stage can last months to years depending on species and water temperature, and it occupies most of the dragonfly's life.[10][11]

发育成熟后，稚虫爬出水面羽化。它攀附到挺水植物茎秆或石头上，背部裂开，成虫钻出，把皱缩的翅膀展开、体液充入翅脉，等待翅膜变硬后第一次飞行。刚羽化的成虫颜色较浅，称嫩成虫，随后逐渐成熟，再回到水边交配产卵，完成世代循环。蜻蜓和豆娘都属于蜻蜓目，稚虫水生这一点相同，成虫停落时翅膀张开还是合拢是两类常见区分。When mature, the nymph climbs out of the water to emerge. It clings to an emergent plant stem or stone, its back splits open, the adult dragonfly crawls out, spreads its crumpled wings, pumps fluid into the wing veins, and waits for the wing membrane to harden before its first flight. The newly emerged adult is pale and called a teneral adult; it then matures and returns to the water to mate and lay eggs, completing the cycle. Dragonflies and damselflies are both Odonata: their aquatic nymphs are alike, while whether wings are spread or folded at rest is a common way to tell the two groups apart.[1]

边界：点水特指产卵行为，不能理解为蜻蜓口渴喝水；成虫主要在空中捕食小飞虫，不取食水体。蜻蜓对水质较敏感，稚虫存活要求水体有一定溶氧量，因此水边蜻蜓多少可以反映水环境状况，但具体种类识别和水体评价需要结合更多观测。本章只描述繁殖与生活史机制，不涉及捕捞或放养建议。The boundary: water-tapping specifically means egg laying, not a thirsty dragonfly drinking; adults mainly catch small flying insects in the air and do not feed on the water. Dragonflies are somewhat sensitive to water quality, and nymphs need a certain dissolved oxygen level, so their presence near water can hint at the state of the environment, though species identification and water assessment need more observation. This chapter describes reproduction and life history only, and gives no advice on collecting or releasing animals.[11]

10蜘蛛怎样结网？How do spiders weave their webs?

腹部丝腺储存可溶性丝蛋白，经纺管 pH 梯度固化成纤维，纺器拉出不同丝型，先搭框架径向丝再绕黏性螺旋丝。Abdominal glands store soluble silk protein; a pH gradient in the duct solidifies it into fiber, spinnerets draw silk types, and frame radial threads precede the sticky spiral.

蜘蛛的丝产自腹部而不是口器。腹部有多组丝腺，丝蛋白（spidroin）以可溶状态储存在腺体内；丝液沿狭窄纺管向前输送，管壁存在由尾部到导管的酸碱度梯度，pH 可从约 7.6 降到 5.7 左右。蛋白两端的结构域在这一环境变化下发生重排，可溶蛋白形成β-折叠纤维，在导管内快速固化，出丝速度可超过每秒 1 米。丝腺与导管相连，蛋白在体内保持可溶，离开导管后才形成稳定纤维，避免在体内提前凝固。Spider silk is produced in the abdomen, not the mouth. Several abdominal glands store silk protein (spidroin) in soluble form; the liquid travels along a narrow spinning duct, and a pH gradient runs from tail to duct, dropping from about 7.6 to around 5.7. Terminal domains at both ends of the protein rearrange under this change, and the soluble protein forms β-sheet fibers that solidify rapidly in the duct, with output speed above 1 meter per second.[1][12]

纺器负责拉出和布置丝。腹部末端的纺器像一组可转动的小喷嘴，能控制丝的粗细、贴合和牵引；蜘蛛可以根据用途调用不同丝腺产生不同丝型。牵引丝用于框架和径向支撑，强度高、延展性好；捕捉丝带黏性液滴，用来粘住猎物；包裹卵囊和猎物的丝又是另一类。纺器还能在拉丝时调节牵伸程度，纤维取向和结晶度受牵伸影响，这也是蛛丝兼具强度和韧性的原因之一。不同丝型配合，才构成一张分工明确的网。Spinnerets draw out and arrange the silk. The spinnerets at the abdomen tip act like a set of rotatable small nozzles, controlling thickness, adhesion and traction. The spider can call on different glands for different silk types. Dragline silk forms the frame and radial supports, strong and extensible; capture silk carries sticky droplets to hold prey; wrapping silk for egg cases and prey is another type. These silk types work together to form a web with clear division of labor.[1][12]

结圆网有大致固定的顺序。蜘蛛先放出牵引丝随风飘到对面，搭出外框和桥丝；再从中心向外拉出多条径向辐丝，构成放射骨架；然后由外向内绕出螺旋状捕捉丝，丝上布有黏性球粒。径向丝不粘人、主要承重，螺旋丝负责拦截昆虫，两种丝交替布置。圆网直径可以长到数米级，具体大小因蛛种和环境而异。网的几何结构让重量和张力沿辐丝分散到固定点，螺旋间距还会随猎物捕获情况调整。Orb webs follow a roughly fixed sequence. The spider first releases dragline silk that drifts on the wind to the opposite side, building the outer frame and bridge threads; then it pulls radial spokes outward from the center to form the radial skeleton; finally it winds a spiral capture thread from outside in, dotted with sticky droplets. Radial threads are non-sticky and mainly bear load, while the spiral intercepts insects, and the two are arranged alternately. An orb web can reach several meters across, varying by species and environment.[2][12]

网是捕食和感知工具。昆虫撞上螺旋丝后挣扎，振动沿丝传到中心，蜘蛛通过腿上感受器感知丝线振动方向和强弱，判断猎物位置；之后用丝把猎物捆住。蜘蛛并不把网当织物来回编织，而是按丝型和几何顺序铺设；不同类群结网方式差别很大，有的结圆网，有的结漏斗网、片网，也有的游猎不结网。蜘蛛常在夜间结网或补网，旧网损耗后重新铺设，部分种类会回收旧丝蛋白。The web is a predation and sensing tool. When an insect hits the spiral and struggles, vibration travels along the threads to the center; receptors on the legs read direction and strength, and the spider locates the prey before wrapping it in silk. The spider does not weave the web like cloth; it lays threads by silk type and geometric order. Groups differ widely: orb webs, funnel webs, sheet webs, and wandering spiders that build no web at all.[1][12]

边界：蜘蛛属于蛛形纲，不是昆虫；本章讲丝蛋白固化与结网行为的一般机制，不评价捕蛛饲养、毒性处理或仿生纤维产业化方案。被蜘蛛叮咬后的皮肤反应属于医疗问题，需要结合具体情况由专业人员判断。本章只描述自然结网过程，不涉及具体蛛种识别或蛛丝利用方案。Boundary: spiders are arachnids, not insects. This chapter explains general silk-protein solidification and web-building behavior, not spider keeping, bite treatment or biomimetic fiber commercialization. Skin reactions after a spider bite are a medical matter to be assessed professionally for the specific case.[1][2]
