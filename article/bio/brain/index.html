01记忆在大脑里怎样形成、巩固和提取？How is memory formed, consolidated and retrieved in the brain?

海马参与新记忆形成并逐步转移到皮层存储；提取是可重构过程，事后信息可通过错误信息效应修改记忆。The hippocampus forms new memories that transfer to cortex; retrieval is reconstructive, and post-event information can alter memory through the misinformation effect.

记忆不是存在单一格子里，而是经过编码、巩固和提取三个阶段。外界信息先在短时记忆中保持，经过注意和复述进入长时记忆；海马与内侧颞叶参与事件记忆的形成，随后记忆逐步转移到新皮层存储，这一过程称为系统巩固。感觉记忆在毫秒到秒级保持原始感官信息，工作记忆用于暂时操作和加工，长时记忆的容量与保持时间相对更长。OpenStax 心理学把记忆分为感觉记忆、短时或工作记忆和长时记忆，长时记忆再分陈述性记忆与程序性记忆。Memory is not stored in a single drawer but passes through encoding, consolidation and retrieval. Information is first held in short-term memory and, through attention and rehearsal, enters long-term memory. The hippocampus and medial temporal lobe participate in episodic memory formation, after which memories gradually transfer to the neocortex for storage; this is called systems consolidation. OpenStax psychology divides memory into sensory memory, short-term or working memory and long-term memory, the latter split into declarative and procedural forms.[3]

环路证据来自病人研究与成像实验。著名病人 H.M. 切除海马后无法形成新的陈述性记忆，说明海马对新记忆形成是必要的；这一案例同时显示陈述性记忆与程序性记忆可以分离，他能学会新动作技能，却不记得练习过。fMRI 研究显示海马在编码和提取阶段激活，皮层在长期存储阶段参与。不同类型记忆依赖不同环路：情景与语义记忆偏内侧颞叶，技能和习惯依赖基底神经节与小脑。Circuit evidence comes from patient studies and imaging. The famous patient H.M., after hippocampal removal, could not form new declarative memories, showing the hippocampus is necessary for new memory formation. fMRI studies show hippocampal activation during encoding and retrieval, with cortex involved in long-term storage. Different memory types rely on different circuits: episodic and semantic memory lean on the medial temporal lobe, while skills and habits depend on the basal ganglia and cerebellum.[2][4]

记忆每次提取都可能被重构，而不是原样回放。错误信息效应指事后接触误导信息后，人对原事件的记忆被修改：Loftus 等人的实验让被试在提问措辞或事后描述影响下，回忆出并未发生的颜色、速度或事件细节，有时对虚构细节的信心与真实细节接近。这说明记忆是可更新的重构过程，主观信心高低并不等于记忆准确性。Each retrieval may reconstruct memory rather than replay it verbatim. The misinformation effect describes how exposure to misleading information afterward modifies the memory of an event: in Loftus and colleagues' experiments, participants recalled details that never happened under the influence of question wording or post-event description. Memory is thus an updatable reconstruction, and subjective confidence does not equal accuracy.[3]

巩固在重复和睡眠中加强。重复提取、精细复述以及与已有知识建立联系都能提高保持率，睡眠期海马回放白天活动模式，帮助把临时存储转为皮层长期记忆；遗忘既可由痕迹消退导致，也可由提取线索缺失或后续干扰造成。Consolidation strengthens through repetition and sleep. Repeated retrieval, elaborate rehearsal and linking to prior knowledge all improve retention, and sleep stages participate in memory stabilization. Forgetting can result from trace decay, missing retrieval cues, or later interference.[3]

边界：成像激活显示相关脑区，不等于该区域单独存储记忆；错误信息效应证明记忆可以被篡改，但不能推出所有日常记忆都不可靠。个体记忆表现受注意、情绪和材料影响，实验室结论外推到法庭证言等场景需要谨慎。The boundary: imaging activation shows correlated regions, not a single storage site; the misinformation effect proves memory can be altered, but does not mean every everyday memory is unreliable. Individual performance depends on attention, emotion and material, and lab results must be extrapolated cautiously to settings such as courtroom testimony.[2][3]

02大脑发育的关键期与可塑性是怎样的？成年后还能改变吗？What are critical periods and plasticity in brain development, and can the brain still change in adulthood?

早期经验依赖关键期塑造环路，随后突触修剪；成年可塑性下降但保留，训练可调整代表区，体细胞可重编程为神经元。Early critical periods shape circuits before pruning; adult plasticity declines but remains, training adjusts maps, and cells can be reprogrammed to neurons.

大脑发育从胚胎期开始，神经发生、迁移和分化先搭建基本环路；神经元增殖后沿放射状胶质纤维迁移到皮层各层，轴突延伸建立跨脑区连接，髓鞘化从脑后部向前部推进。出生后脑体积继续增大，突触数量在儿童期达到高峰，随后通过突触修剪，保留常用连接、减弱不用连接。OpenStax 与 NINDS 资料都指出，发育早期存在经验依赖的关键期，特定功能在相应阶段最容易被塑造。Brain development begins in the embryo, where neurogenesis, migration and differentiation first build the basic circuits. After birth the brain keeps growing, synapse counts peak in childhood, and pruning then retains frequently used connections while weakening unused ones. OpenStax and NINDS materials note an experience-dependent critical period early in development, during which particular functions are most readily shaped.[1][2]

关键期过后可塑性下降但不为零。视觉、语言等系统在早期对输入最敏感，早期经验缺失会造成相应皮层代表区发育不足，而丰富环境能增加突触密度；成年后仍可通过训练改变手指或运动皮层代表区，只是速度和程度受限。前额叶负责计划、抑制和冲动控制，成熟较晚，结构上要到二十多岁才逐步完成髓鞘化和修剪，所谓大脑约 25 岁发育成熟，指的是前额叶功能轨迹，而不是全脑在某一天突然长好。Plasticity declines after the critical period but does not reach zero. Visual and language systems are most sensitive to input early; adults can still alter cortical representations through training, though more slowly and to a limited degree. The prefrontal cortex, responsible for planning, inhibition and impulse control, matures late, with myelination and pruning completing gradually into the twenties. The phrase that the brain finishes developing around 25 describes this prefrontal trajectory, not a whole brain completing on a single day.[2]

体细胞可以重编程为神经细胞。诱导多能干细胞技术把皮肤等体细胞回退到类似胚胎干细胞的状态，再定向分化为神经元，绕过胚胎材料的伦理限制，可来自患者自身细胞，用于疾病建模和药物筛选研究。NINDS 资料说明神经元由胞体、树突和轴突组成，胶质细胞提供支持和绝缘。Somatic cells can be reprogrammed into neurons. Induced pluripotent stem cell technology reverts cells such as skin cells to an embryonic-stem-like state, then directs differentiation into neurons, providing material for developmental models and disease cells. NINDS explains that a neuron consists of a cell body, dendrites and an axon, while glial cells provide support and insulation.[1][5]

环境输入塑造环路。丰富刺激、语言互动和练习影响突触保留，突触修剪受活动依赖性机制调控，反复使用的连接被保留；语言习得、视觉矫正等窗口在童年更开放，早期严重剥夺会阻碍对应功能发育，错过后补偿难度增大。可塑性也有代价：修剪使环路更高效，但早期形成的异常连接纠正起来更难。Environmental input shapes circuits. Enriched stimulation, language interaction and practice affect which synapses are retained; severe early deprivation hinders the corresponding functions. Plasticity also has a cost: pruning makes circuits more efficient, but abnormal connections formed early are harder to correct later.[2]

边界：关键期是相对窗口而非绝对截止线；25 岁成熟是群体发育趋势的通俗说法，个体差异很大。重编程得到的神经元是研究工具，不等于可以替代活体受损脑区；发育受遗传和环境共同影响，不能用单一时间点判定成熟与否。The boundary: critical periods are relative windows, not absolute cutoffs; the age-25 maturity statement is a popular summary of a group trend with wide individual variation. Reprogrammed neurons are a research tool, not a replacement for damaged living brain tissue. Development depends on genes and environment together, and maturity cannot be judged from a single time point.[1][5]

03感觉信息沿什么通路进入并在皮层加工？成像证据是什么？What pathways carry sensory input into the cortex, and what imaging evidence supports them?

感受器经传入通路和丘脑中继投射到对应初级皮层，保留拓扑映射；fMRI 定位激活代表区，阈下感知效应有限。Receptors project via afferent pathways and thalamic relay to primary cortex, preserving topographic maps; fMRI localizes activated representations, and subliminal effects are limited.

感觉信息沿特异通路进入中枢。感受器把刺激转为神经冲动，经传入神经上行；多数感觉在丘脑中继后投射到对应初级皮层：视觉到枕叶纹状皮层，听觉到颞叶，体感到顶叶。嗅觉经嗅球直接投射边缘系统，是少数不经过丘脑中继的主要感觉，味觉则经脑干和丘脑到达皮层；各通路在投射前保持相对分离，避免不同感觉通道混淆。OpenStax 生物学 35.3 描述了中枢神经系统的传入投射与功能分区。Sensory information enters the central nervous system along specialized pathways. Receptors convert stimuli into nerve impulses that travel up afferent nerves; most senses relay in the thalamus before projecting to the corresponding primary cortex: vision to the occipital striate cortex, hearing to the temporal lobe, and somatic sensation to the parietal lobe. OpenStax Biology 35.3 describes afferent projections and functional divisions of the central nervous system.[4]

皮层存在拓扑映射。视觉皮层保留视野的空间排列，体感皮层按身体部位形成代表区；代表区大小与感受器密度和使用程度相关，而不是身体面积本身。体感皮层代表图中唇、手等部位面积大，躯干代表区小；长期练习乐器或动作可以扩大对应代表区范围。fMRI 测量血氧依赖信号，反映局部血流和代谢变化，而非直接记录神经元放电；它和皮层电位研究可以定位特定刺激激活的代表区，观察到任务训练后代表区发生调整。The cortex preserves topographic maps. Visual cortex keeps the spatial arrangement of the visual field, and somatosensory cortex forms representations by body part. Representation size relates to receptor density and use rather than body area itself: lips and hands occupy large areas in the somatosensory map while the trunk is smaller, and long practice with an instrument or movement can expand the corresponding representation. fMRI and cortical potential studies locate the representation activated by a given stimulus and observe adjustment after task training.[1][4]

下意识感知有实验证据，但边界清楚。掩蔽实验中，图像快速呈现并被后续刺激遮蔽，被试报告看不到，仍可能出现皮层激活或情绪反应偏向；掩蔽效应取决于呈现时长、刺激强度和任务难度，多数偏好在简单判断任务中测得。这属于阈下加工，强度有限，不等于可以不经意识影响复杂决策。Subliminal perception has experimental evidence, but its boundary is clear. In masking experiments an image appears briefly and is covered by a later stimulus; participants report not seeing it yet may still show cortical activation or an emotional response bias. The effect depends on presentation duration, stimulus strength and task difficulty, and most biases are measured in simple judgment tasks. This is limited-strength subliminal processing, not an ability to influence complex decisions without awareness.[2]

通路之间存在整合。初级皮层加工基本特征，联合皮层把多种感觉信息与已有知识和预期结合，不同感觉通道在联合皮层交互，形成统一的物体与场景知觉；自上而下的预期会调制初级皮层反应，错觉正是感觉输入与先验假设共同作用的结果，说明感知是建构过程，而不是对外界的被动复印。Pathways integrate with one another. Primary cortex processes basic features, and association cortex combines sensory input with prior knowledge and expectation; top-down expectation modulates early cortical responses, and illusions arise from the joint action of sensory input and prior assumptions. Perception is construction rather than a passive copy of the world.[4]

边界：成像激活是群体相关模式，受 fMRI 时间和空间分辨率限制；阈下刺激效应多在简单任务中测得，不能推出潜意识广告或复杂说服。感觉代表区可随训练改变，但变化幅度和长期稳定性因人而异。The boundary: imaging activation is a group-level correlated pattern limited by the temporal and spatial resolution of fMRI. Subliminal effects are mostly measured in simple tasks and cannot support claims of subconscious advertising or complex persuasion. Representations can change with training, but the magnitude and long-term stability vary between people.[1][2]

04脑有哪些主要结构，成年后还会产生新神经元吗？What are the main brain structures, and are new neurons produced in adulthood?

皮层、小脑、脑干、脊髓分工协作，颅骨、脑膜和脑脊液缓冲外力；成年神经发生证据有限而集中，不等于全脑再生。Cortex, cerebellum, brainstem and spinal cord divide labor, with skull, meninges and CSF cushioning force; adult neurogenesis is limited and localized, not whole-brain regeneration.

人脑分主要结构：大脑皮层分额叶、顶叶、颞叶、枕叶，左右半球通过胼胝体交换信息，额叶参与计划与行为控制，枕叶处理视觉输入；小脑位于后脑，协调运动与技能习惯，也参与部分运动学习；脑干连接脊髓并调控呼吸、心跳等基础功能，其中网状结构参与维持觉醒；脊髓在椎管内传导上下行信号。NINDS 脑基础资料以神经元为基本功能单位，神经元由胞体、树突、轴突构成，胶质细胞提供支持和绝缘。The human brain has major divisions: the cerebral cortex splits into frontal, parietal, temporal and occipital lobes; the cerebellum sits at the rear; the brainstem connects to the spinal cord and regulates breathing, heart rate and other basic functions; the spinal cord conducts ascending and descending signals within the vertebral canal. NINDS brain basics treat the neuron as the basic functional unit, composed of a cell body, dendrites and an axon, with glial cells providing support and insulation.[2][4]

成年神经发生的证据有限而集中。传统观点认为成年后不再产生新神经元；后来在海马齿状回等脑区观察到成年新生神经元参与模式分离和记忆分辨，嗅球在部分哺乳动物中也有更新；新生神经元需要迁移、成熟并整合进既有环路后才可能参与功能。人类研究样本有限，方法差异导致结论尚未统一，成年神经发生的范围和速率仍存在争议。NINDS 资料记录了神经元的产生、迁移和存活过程。Evidence for adult neurogenesis is limited and localized. The traditional view held that no new neurons form after adulthood; later studies observed newborn adult neurons participating in function in regions such as the hippocampal dentate gyrus, and the olfactory bulb renews in some mammals. The extent and rate of adult neurogenesis in humans remain debated. NINDS materials document neuron birth, migration and survival.[5]

保护结构缓冲外力。颅骨形成硬壳，脑膜分层包裹脑和脊髓，脑脊液在脑室和蛛网膜下腔循环，浮力和缓冲减轻震动，蛛网膜下腔的液体还缓冲颅骨与脑表面之间的摩擦，同时带走代谢产物，颅内压由脑脊液产生和吸收的平衡维持；奔跑或跳跃时头部晃动被颈肌、脑脊液和颅骨分散。但这些结构不能防止强烈撞击造成的脑震荡或更重损伤。Protective structures cushion force. The skull forms a hard shell, meninges wrap the brain and spinal cord in layers, and cerebrospinal fluid circulates in ventricles and the subarachnoid space, with buoyancy and damping reducing shock. During running or jumping, head movement is spread by neck muscles, cerebrospinal fluid and skull. These structures cannot prevent concussion or worse injury from a strong impact.[2][4]

神经元数量与连接规模。人脑神经元数量在千亿量级，突触连接以万亿计、远多于神经元；具体估算因计数方法、年龄和脑区而异，方法包括立体学抽样和全脑推算，引用时应给区间。胶质细胞与神经元的比例在不同脑区并不固定，不能笼统说成某一固定倍数。Neuron counts and connection scale. The human brain holds on the order of one hundred billion neurons, with far more synaptic connections; exact estimates vary with counting method and region, so a range should be quoted. The glia-to-neuron ratio is not fixed across brain regions and cannot be stated as one constant multiple.[2]

边界：脑区功能分工有重叠和代偿，损伤表现取决于位置、范围和病程；成年神经发生不等于全脑可以再生修复。保护结构降低日常震荡风险，不能替代头盔等防撞措施；数量估算受方法限制，不宜当作精确常数。The boundary: functional division overlaps and compensates, and deficit after injury depends on location, extent and course. Adult neurogenesis does not mean the whole brain can regenerate. Protective structures lower everyday shaking risk but cannot replace helmet impact protection; count estimates are method-limited and should not be treated as precise constants.[2][5]
