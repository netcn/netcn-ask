01心脏泵血和肺换气怎样配合？一次血液循环怎样经过肺和全身？How do the heart and lungs work together, and how does one circulation pass through lungs and body?

心肺串联：右心室泵血入肺换气，含氧血回左心室送往全身；静息心输出量约 5 L/min。Heart and lung are series: the right ventricle pumps blood to the lungs, oxygenated blood returns to the left ventricle and the body; resting cardiac output is about 5 L/min.

心脏和肺在循环通路上是串联耦合的：右心室把缺氧血泵入肺动脉，血液在肺泡处排出二氧化碳、结合氧气，经肺静脉回到左心房，再由左心室泵向全身；全身组织用完氧气后，缺氧血经静脉回到右心房，完成一次完整循环。心脏是推动血液的泵，肺是气体交换的场所，两者通过同一套血管网络连接，任何一侧效率下降都会影响另一侧的供氧能力。Heart and lung are coupled in series along the circulation: the right ventricle pumps deoxygenated blood into the pulmonary artery, blood releases carbon dioxide and picks up oxygen at the alveoli, returns through pulmonary veins to the left atrium, and the left ventricle then pumps it to the body; after tissues use the oxygen, deoxygenated blood returns through veins to the right atrium and closes the loop. The heart is the pump, the lungs are the gas-exchange site, and the two are joined by one vascular network, so a drop in either side weakens oxygen supply on the other.[1][2]

一次心跳中，心室收缩把血液射出，舒张时充盈血液。成年人静息心率约 60–100 次/分，每搏输出量约 60–80 毫升，心输出量等于心率乘以每搏输出量，静息状态下约 5 升/分；运动时心率和每搏量同时增加，心输出量可上升数倍。心脏自身的营养由冠状动脉供应，冠状动脉狭窄会影响心肌供血，进而削弱泵血能力。Each heartbeat fills the ventricles in diastole and ejects blood in systole. A resting adult heart rate is about 60–100 beats per minute, stroke volume about 60–80 mL, and cardiac output equals heart rate times stroke volume, roughly 5 L/min at rest; during exercise both rise and output can multiply several times. The heart muscle itself is fed by coronary arteries, and coronary narrowing cuts myocardial supply and weakens pumping.[1][2]

肺循环和体循环分工明确但连续不断。右心室压力较低，只把血液送到邻近的肺部；左心室肌肉更厚，需要把血液推到全身各处。房室瓣和半月瓣保证血液单向流动，防止心室收缩时反流回心房；心肌自身耗氧量大，安静时也持续依赖冠脉供血。血液在肺里只停留很短时间，却要完成氧气和二氧化碳的交换；通气量和心输出量相互匹配，运动时呼吸加深加快，肺血流也随之增加，保证气体供应跟上组织需求。Pulmonary and systemic circuits are distinct but continuous. The right ventricle works at lower pressure, sending blood only to the nearby lungs; the left ventricle has a thicker wall and must push blood everywhere in the body. Blood spends little time in the lungs yet completes oxygen and carbon dioxide exchange there; ventilation and cardiac output match each other, and exercise deepens and speeds breathing while pulmonary flow rises to keep gas supply ahead of tissue demand.[1][2]

NHLBI 的心脏与肺部科普资料分别说明：心脏通过四个腔室和瓣膜推动血液在体循环与肺循环之间流动，肺则通过气道和肺泡把氧气送入血液、把二氧化碳排出。OpenStax 解剖生理学教材把心脏解剖、心动周期和气体交换放在同一循环框架下描述，强调静脉血—肺—动脉血—组织—静脉血的连续路径。NHLBI heart and lung materials explain that the heart moves blood between systemic and pulmonary circuits through four chambers and valves, while the lungs pass oxygen into blood and remove carbon dioxide through airways and alveoli. The OpenStax anatomy text places heart anatomy, the cardiac cycle and gas exchange in one circulation framework, following venous blood to the lungs, arterial blood to tissues and back to veins.[1][2][3]

边界：本章讲心肺耦合的基本循环与量级，不展开具体心脏病诊断、心律失常机制或肺功能检查指标。心输出量、每搏输出量是群体参考范围，个体数值受体位、年龄、训练水平和疾病影响；出现胸痛、气短等症状应就医评估，不能凭循环常识自我判断。Boundary: this chapter covers basic cardiopulmonary coupling and reference magnitudes, not specific cardiac diagnosis, arrhythmia mechanisms or pulmonary-function test indices. Cardiac output and stroke volume are population reference ranges; individual values vary with posture, age, training and disease. Chest pain or shortness of breath needs medical evaluation, not self-judgment from circulation basics.[1][2]

02肺里的气体怎样在肺泡和血液之间交换？How do gases exchange between alveoli and blood in the lungs?

呼吸肌造成压力差完成通气；氧气二氧化碳按分压跨过薄层肺泡膜扩散，血红蛋白在血液中运输。Muscles drive ventilation by pressure gradient; gases diffuse across the thin alveolar membrane by partial pressure, and hemoglobin carries them in blood.

呼吸的第一阶段是肺通气：膈肌和肋间肌收缩扩大胸腔，肺内压低于大气压，空气经鼻、咽、气管进入肺泡；呼气时肌肉放松，胸腔缩小，气体排出。静息潮气量约 500 毫升，每次呼吸只交换一部分肺内气体；运动时呼吸加深加快，潮气量和呼吸频率同时上升。气道沿途有加温、加湿和过滤作用，到达肺泡的空气接近体温并被水蒸气饱和。The first stage of breathing is pulmonary ventilation: the diaphragm and intercostal muscles expand the chest, pressure inside the lungs drops below atmospheric, and air moves through nose, pharynx and trachea into alveoli; on exhalation muscles relax, the chest shrinks and gas leaves. Resting tidal volume is about 500 mL, and each breath exchanges only part of the lung gas; exercise raises both tidal volume and frequency. Along the airways, air is warmed, humidified and filtered, reaching alveoli near body temperature and saturated with water vapor.[2][3]

气体交换发生在肺泡与毛细血管之间。肺泡总数约数亿个，展开后的气体交换表面积约 70–80 平方米，肺泡壁与毛细血管壁合起来只有约半微米厚，氧气和二氧化碳靠分压差扩散：肺泡内氧气分压高于血液，氧气进入血液；血液中二氧化碳分压高于肺泡，二氧化碳进入肺泡排出。交换面积越大、膜越薄，扩散效率越高。Gas exchange occurs between alveoli and capillaries. There are hundreds of millions of alveoli, giving a gas-exchange surface of about 70–80 m²; the alveolar and capillary walls together are only about half a micrometre thick, and oxygen and carbon dioxide diffuse along partial-pressure gradients: alveolar oxygen pressure exceeds blood, so oxygen enters blood, while blood carbon dioxide exceeds alveolar air and leaves. Larger area and thinner membrane raise diffusion efficiency.[3][8]

氧气进入血液后主要与红细胞内的血红蛋白结合，随血流输送到组织；二氧化碳则以碳酸氢根、结合在血红蛋白上和溶解在血浆中的多种形式运输。血红蛋白在肺部结合氧气，在组织处按分压差释放；血氧饱和度反映血红蛋白结合氧气的比例，是临床判断换气和运输状态的常用指标。通气、扩散和运输三个环节任一受阻，都会造成组织供氧不足。Oxygen entering blood binds mainly to hemoglobin inside red cells and travels to tissues; carbon dioxide moves as bicarbonate, bound to hemoglobin and dissolved in plasma. Hemoglobin picks up oxygen in the lungs and releases it by pressure gradient in tissues; oxygen saturation measures the share of hemoglobin carrying oxygen, a common clinical index of ventilation and transport. A blockage in ventilation, diffusion or transport can all starve tissues of oxygen.[3][8]

NHLBI 肺部资料说明气道分支、肺泡结构和呼吸肌如何完成通气；OpenStax 解剖生理学的气体交换章节按分压梯度解释氧气和二氧化碳跨膜扩散，气体运输章节描述血红蛋白结合与二氧化碳的多种携带形式。教材数据与临床常用指标一致：静息潮气量、肺泡表面积和血氧饱和度都是可核对的量级与测量口径。NHLBI lung materials explain how airway branching, alveolar structure and respiratory muscles complete ventilation; the OpenStax gas-exchange chapter explains oxygen and carbon dioxide crossing membranes along pressure gradients, and the transport chapter describes hemoglobin binding and the multiple forms of carbon dioxide carriage. Textbook figures match common clinical measures: resting tidal volume, alveolar surface area and oxygen saturation are verifiable magnitudes and measurement definitions.[2][3][8]

边界：本章讲通气、扩散和气体运输的基本过程，不展开呼吸力学的压力—容积曲线、弥散障碍的病因诊断或呼吸机参数设置。肺泡表面积和潮气量是平均参考值，个体差异受肺容量、体位和疾病影响；长期咳嗽、活动后气短等情况需要结合检查判断，不能凭换气常识推断病因。Boundary: this chapter covers ventilation, diffusion and gas transport, not pressure-volume curves of respiratory mechanics, diagnostic causes of diffusion impairment or ventilator settings. Alveolar area and tidal volume are average reference values varying with lung capacity, posture and disease; chronic cough or exertional shortness of breath needs examination rather than cause inference from exchange basics.[3][8]

03为什么血液是红色的？血型 A、B、O、AB 是怎么决定的？Why is blood red, and how are A, B, O and AB blood types determined?

红色来自红细胞内含铁血红蛋白；ABO 由红细胞表面 A/B 抗原决定，输血需避免抗原抗体凝集。Redness comes from iron-containing hemoglobin in red cells; ABO follows A/B surface antigens, and transfusion must avoid antigen-antibody agglutination.

血液呈红色，来自红细胞内的血红蛋白。血红蛋白是含铁的蛋白质，四个亚基各结合一个血红素基团，铁离子与氧气结合前后颜色发生变化：含氧血红蛋白鲜红，脱氧血红蛋白偏暗红，静脉血因此看起来较深。红细胞约占血液体积的四成多，血浆本身是淡黄色液体；血液的红色主要由红细胞密度和血红蛋白含量决定，贫血时红细胞或血红蛋白减少，面色和血色会偏淡。Blood looks red because of hemoglobin inside red blood cells. Hemoglobin is an iron-containing protein with four subunits, each carrying a heme group; the iron changes color before and after binding oxygen: oxygenated hemoglobin is bright red, deoxygenated hemoglobin darker red, which is why venous blood looks deep. Red cells make up about two fifths of blood volume, while plasma itself is pale yellow; blood redness follows red-cell density and hemoglobin content, and anemia with fewer cells or less hemoglobin leaves the complexion and blood paler.[3][4]

ABO 血型由红细胞表面的糖蛋白抗原决定。红细胞膜上可以有 A 抗原、B 抗原，或两者都有、两者都无：A型有 A 抗原，B型有 B 抗原，AB型两者都有，O型两者都无。编码 ABO 抗原的基因位于 9 号染色体，父母各传一个等位基因，组合决定子女血型；血浆中还存在针对自身所缺抗原的天然抗体，A型血浆含抗B，B型含抗A，O型两者都有，AB型两者都无。ABO blood type is set by glycoprotein antigens on the red-cell surface. The membrane may carry A antigen, B antigen, both, or neither: type A has A, type B has B, AB has both, and O has neither. The ABO gene sits on chromosome 9, and each parent passes one allele whose combination determines the child's type. Plasma also holds natural antibodies against antigens the body lacks: type A plasma has anti-B, type B anti-A, O has both, and AB has neither.[4][5]

输血配型要避免抗原与对应抗体相遇。供者红细胞上的抗原若被受者血浆抗体识别，会发生凝集和溶血反应，因此常规输血优先选择同型血；紧急情况下 O 型红细胞可作为通用供血的说法只在红细胞制品、严格交叉配血和医疗监护下成立，血浆制品的适配方向则相反。血型鉴定通过红细胞与标准抗血清的凝集反应判定，Rh 等其他血型系统另有抗原规则。Transfusion matching keeps donor antigens apart from recipient antibodies. If recipient plasma antibodies recognize donor red-cell antigens, agglutination and hemolysis follow, so same-type blood is preferred; the idea of universal O-type red cells holds only for red-cell products under strict cross-matching and medical supervision, while plasma products match in the opposite direction. Blood typing uses agglutination against standard antisera, and other systems such as Rh follow their own antigen rules.[3][5]

OpenStax 解剖生理学的红细胞章节描述血红蛋白的结构、含铁基团和氧气结合；血型章节说明 ABO 抗原、天然抗体与凝集反应。血液颜色和血型是两个不同问题：颜色由血红蛋白与氧气状态决定，血型由红细胞表面抗原种类决定，二者不能互相推断。The OpenStax erythrocyte chapter describes hemoglobin structure, iron-containing groups and oxygen binding; the blood-typing chapter explains ABO antigens, natural antibodies and agglutination. Blood color and blood type are separate questions: color follows hemoglobin and oxygen state, type follows surface antigen kinds, and one cannot be inferred from the other.[4][5]

边界：本章讲血液颜色来源与 ABO 分型的基本规则，不展开 Rh 系统细节、新生儿溶血机制或罕见血型鉴定。血型不能作为性格、体质或亲缘关系的判断依据；输血和献血应在医疗机构按规范配型进行，不能凭血型常识自行判断。Boundary: this chapter covers blood color and basic ABO rules, not Rh-system details, hemolytic disease of the newborn or rare typing. Blood type cannot judge personality, physique or kinship; transfusion and donation follow medical cross-matching standards, not casual type assumptions.[4][5]

04为什么感冒时鼻子会流鼻涕、鼻塞？鼻腔怎样防御吸入空气？Why does a cold cause a runny or blocked nose, and how does the nose defend inhaled air?

黏液黏附颗粒、纤毛后推清除，黏膜血管加温加湿；炎症放大分泌与水肿，造成鼻塞流涕。Mucus traps particles and cilia sweep them back, mucosal vessels warm and humidify; inflammation amplifies secretion and swelling into congestion and discharge.

鼻腔是呼吸道的第一道防线。吸入空气先经过鼻毛和鼻黏膜：黏液黏附灰尘、花粉和微生物，纤毛像小刷子一样把黏液连同颗粒向后推向咽部，再被吞咽或咳出。感冒时鼻黏膜受炎症刺激，杯状细胞和腺体分泌增加，黏膜下血管渗出的液体混入鼻涕，加上血管扩张和组织水肿，鼻腔变窄、鼻涕由清水样逐渐变稠，鼻塞和流涕就是这层防御被炎症放大后的表现。The nasal cavity is the first line of the respiratory defense. Inhaled air passes nose hairs and mucosa: mucus traps dust, pollen and microbes, and cilia brush the mucus and particles backward toward the throat, where they are swallowed or coughed out. During a cold the mucosa is inflamed, mucus secretion rises, vessels dilate and tissue swells, narrowing the cavity and increasing discharge; congestion and runny nose are this defense amplified by inflammation.[2][8]

鼻腔同时负责加温加湿。黏膜下血管网把吸入空气加热到接近体温，黏液层补充水分，使到达气管和肺泡的空气温暖湿润，避免干燥冷空气直接刺激下呼吸道。鼻泪管把眼睛分泌的泪液引流到鼻腔，所以哭泣时鼻涕增多；感冒时鼻黏膜肿胀也可能堵塞鼻泪管开口，出现流泪或眼屎增多。The nose also warms and humidifies air. A submucosal vascular plexus heats inhaled air toward body temperature, and the mucus layer adds moisture so air reaching the trachea and alveoli is warm and damp, avoiding direct irritation from cold dry air. The nasolacrimal duct drains tear fluid from the eye into the nasal cavity, which is why crying increases nasal discharge; inflamed swollen mucosa can block the duct opening and bring tearing or eye discharge.[2][8]

用鼻呼吸比用口呼吸更符合生理设计。鼻腔的过滤、加温加湿和纤毛清除减少了颗粒物直接进入肺部；张口呼吸绕开这层处理，干燥空气和颗粒更容易刺激咽喉和气道。气味分子溶解在黏液层中被嗅上皮感知，鼻腔同时承担部分嗅觉功能；长期张口呼吸还可能影响睡眠质量和口腔状态。长期鼻塞、张口呼吸可能与鼻中隔偏曲、鼻炎或腺样体问题有关，需要检查原因，而不是单纯忍耐。Nasal breathing fits the body's design better than mouth breathing. Filtration, warming, humidification and mucociliary clearance in the nose keep particles from reaching the lungs; mouth breathing bypasses this treatment, letting dry air and particles irritate the throat and airways. Chronic congestion and mouth breathing may relate to septal deviation, rhinitis or adenoid problems and deserve examination rather than endurance.[2][8]

OpenStax 解剖生理学的呼吸系统章节描述鼻腔、鼻甲、黏液纤毛装置和鼻泪管的结构与功能；NHLBI 肺部资料把上呼吸道过滤加温作为整体呼吸防御的起点。鼻腔、鼻窦和鼻咽在解剖上与中耳、泪器相通，黏膜炎症可沿这些通道扩散，引起耳闷或眼部不适。鼻涕、鼻塞和流泪看似分散，都与鼻黏膜的分泌、血管和引流结构直接相关。The OpenStax respiratory-system chapter describes the nasal cavity, turbinates, mucociliary apparatus and nasolacrimal duct; NHLBI lung materials place upper-airway filtering and warming at the start of overall respiratory defense. Runny nose, congestion and tearing look separate but all tie to the mucosa's secretion, vessel and drainage structures.[2][3][8]

边界：本章讲鼻黏膜防御与常见症状的机制，不展开过敏性鼻炎的药物治疗、鼻窦解剖手术或反复鼻塞的病因诊断。流涕鼻塞多数与炎症或刺激有关，但持续脓涕、发热、面部胀痛等需要就医判断；不要把所有鼻部症状都归因于感冒。Boundary: this chapter covers nasal defense and common symptom mechanisms, not rhinitis drug treatment, sinus surgery or diagnosis of chronic congestion. Discharge and congestion mostly follow inflammation or irritation, but persistent purulent mucus, fever or facial pain needs medical assessment; not every nasal symptom is a cold.[2][8]

05食物过敏和食物不耐受有什么区别？What is the difference between food allergy and food intolerance?

过敏是 IgE 免疫介导、可快速全身发作；不耐受多为酶缺乏或消化问题，与摄入量相关。Allergy is IgE-mediated and can strike systemically and fast; intolerance is often enzyme deficiency or digestion trouble, related to intake dose.

食物过敏和食物不耐受是两类机制不同的问题。食物过敏由免疫系统介导：过敏原进入身体后诱发特异性 IgE 抗体，再次接触时抗体结合肥大细胞并释放组胺等介质，出现皮疹、口唇肿胀、胃肠症状，严重时喉头水肿、血压下降，即严重过敏反应。发作通常较快，可由少量食物触发，花生、坚果、牛奶、鸡蛋、小麦、大豆、鱼类和甲壳类是常见过敏原。Food allergy and food intolerance are two problems with different mechanisms. Food allergy is immune-mediated: an allergen induces specific IgE antibodies, and on re-exposure antibodies bind mast cells and release histamine and other mediators, causing rash, lip swelling and gut symptoms, or severe airway swelling and low blood pressure in anaphylaxis. Reactions are usually rapid and can be triggered by small amounts; peanut, tree nuts, milk, eggs, wheat, soy, fish and shellfish are common allergens.[4][6]

食物不耐受多不是免疫反应。以乳糖不耐受为例，小肠乳糖酶不足，牛奶中的乳糖不能被充分分解，进入大肠后被细菌发酵，产生气体和渗透性腹泻，表现为腹胀、肠鸣、腹痛；它通常与摄入剂量相关，少量尚可耐受，不一定出现皮疹或全身反应。类似的还有其他消化酶缺乏、消化吸收不良或食物刺激引起的不适，鉴别关键是有无免疫机制和全身过敏表现。Food intolerance is usually not an immune reaction. In lactose intolerance, small-intestine lactase is insufficient, milk lactose cannot be fully broken down and reaches the large intestine, where bacteria ferment it into gas and osmotic diarrhea with bloating, borborygmi and pain. It usually tracks intake dose: small amounts may be tolerated, without rash or systemic symptoms. Enzyme deficiency, malabsorption or food irritation cause similar discomfort; the key distinction is whether an immune mechanism and systemic allergic signs are present.[6][7]

两类问题的处理方向不同。确诊的食物过敏需要严格回避过敏原，随身携带医生开具的急救药物，识别严重过敏征兆；不耐受则可通过减量、分次食用、选择低乳糖制品或补充相应酶制剂改善。饮食记录和诱因日记有助于医生判断；长期回避不当也可能造成营养不足，应在指导下进行。仅凭一次饭后不适不能断定是过敏，也不能把所有消化不适都归为不耐受；反复或严重发作应就医，通过病史、皮肤点刺或血液抗体检测等帮助判断。Management differs. A confirmed food allergy requires strict allergen avoidance, prescribed emergency medication carried at all times and recognition of anaphylaxis signs. Intolerance can improve by smaller portions, spaced intake, low-lactose products or enzyme supplements. A single post-meal discomfort does not prove allergy, nor should every digestive upset be labeled intolerance; repeated or severe episodes should be evaluated through history, skin-prick or antibody blood testing.[6][7]

MedlinePlus 食物过敏条目说明 IgE 介导的反应、常见过敏原和严重过敏风险；乳糖不耐受条目描述酶缺乏、发酵产气和剂量相关的胃肠症状。OpenStax 免疫学章节则把过敏归入免疫系统对无害物质的过度反应，区分先天与适应性免疫参与的不同环节。MedlinePlus food-allergy entry explains IgE-mediated reactions, common allergens and anaphylaxis risk; its lactose-intolerance entry describes enzyme deficiency, fermented gas and dose-related gut symptoms. The OpenStax immunity chapter places allergy among immune overreactions to harmless substances, separating innate and adaptive immune involvement.[6][7]

边界：本章讲过敏与不耐受的鉴别框架，不展开具体过敏原检测流程、脱敏治疗方案或个人饮食处方。严重过敏、呼吸困难或面唇肿胀属于急症，需要立即就医；食物是否安全应结合个人病史和检查结果判断，不能套用通用结论。Boundary: this chapter gives the allergy–intolerance distinction framework, not allergen testing workflows, desensitization protocols or personal diet prescriptions. Anaphylaxis, breathing difficulty or face-lip swelling is an emergency needing immediate care; whether a food is safe depends on individual history and test results, not generic conclusions.[6][7]

06胸痛有哪几类？为什么胸痛要当心？What types of chest pain are there, and why should chest pain be taken seriously?

胸膜性随吸气加重、胸壁性按压可复现、心源性与劳力相关；突发压榨痛伴出汗立即就医。Pleuritic pain worsens on inspiration, chest-wall pain is reproducible by pressure, cardiac pain tracks exertion; crushing pain with sweating needs emergency care.

胸痛的来源并不只有心脏。胸壁皮肤、肌肉、肋骨、肋软骨、胸膜、肺、食管和心脏大血管都可以产生痛觉信号；其中皮肤、肌肉、骨膜和壁层胸膜痛觉神经丰富，定位较清楚，而肺实质和脏层胸膜本身对切割、挤压并不敏感，痛感多来自胸膜受牵拉或炎症刺激。同样是胸口疼，来自哪一层组织，决定了疼痛的性质、诱发方式和危险程度，不能用一句“胸口疼”笼统概括。Chest pain does not come only from the heart. The chest wall skin, muscles, ribs, costal cartilage, pleura, lungs, esophagus and great vessels can all send pain signals. Skin, muscle, periosteum and the parietal pleura carry dense sensory nerves and localize pain clearly, while lung tissue and the visceral pleura are largely insensitive to cutting or pressure; their pain usually follows pleural stretch or inflammation. Which layer produces the pain shapes its character, trigger and risk, so one generic label of chest pain cannot cover all cases.[1][2]

第一类是胸膜性胸痛。壁层胸膜分布着感觉神经，当胸膜发生炎症、受到积液刺激或被牵拉时，深呼吸、咳嗽、打喷嚏会让两层胸膜摩擦幅度增大，疼痛随之加重，屏住呼吸时减轻。这类疼痛常定位在胸壁一侧，可随呼吸节奏出现，常见于胸膜炎、肺炎累及胸膜或气胸等情况；判断线索是疼痛与吸气动作明显绑定。The first group is pleuritic chest pain. The parietal pleura carries sensory nerves; when it is inflamed, irritated by fluid or stretched, deeper breathing, coughing or sneezing increases friction between the two pleural layers and worsens the pain, which eases when breath is held. The pain is often localized to one side of the chest and tracks the breathing rhythm, as seen in pleurisy, pneumonia reaching the pleura or pneumothorax. The key clue is that the pain is clearly tied to the inspiratory movement.[2][8]

第二类是肋间肌和胸壁源性疼痛。肌肉劳损、肋软骨或胸壁筋膜受刺激时，疼痛位置表浅，用手指按压同一部位可以复现相似痛感，转头、弯腰、抬手臂或扭转躯干时加重，安静坐着、呼吸平稳时减轻。它与动作和体位相关，通常不伴随呼吸困难、出汗等全身表现；久坐姿势不良、剧烈咳嗽后肌肉拉伤都可能出现这类不适。The second group comes from intercostal muscles and the chest wall. With muscle strain or irritation of costal cartilage and fascia, the pain is superficial; pressing the same spot with a finger reproduces a similar ache, and turning the head, bending, raising an arm or twisting the trunk worsens it, while sitting still with steady breathing eases it. It tracks posture and movement and usually does not come with breathlessness or sweating. Poor seated posture or a strained muscle after heavy coughing can both produce this kind of discomfort.[2][8]

第三类是心源性胸痛。心肌缺血引起的不适多在胸骨后，呈压榨、紧缩或憋闷感，可放射到左肩、后背、下颌或手臂，常由体力活动、快走、上楼或情绪激动诱发，休息后多可逐渐缓解，发作时可伴出汗、气短、恶心或乏力。有些人描述为心脏“突然刺痛”，但几秒即过的尖锐刺痛更常见于胸壁或肋间神经敏感；单凭刺痛几秒不能自我排除心脏问题，也不能据此断定心脏没事。The third group is cardiac chest pain. Discomfort from myocardial ischemia usually sits behind the sternum as pressure, tightness or heaviness, and may radiate to the left shoulder, back, jaw or arm. It is often triggered by exertion, brisk walking, climbing stairs or emotional stress, and tends to ease after rest, with sweating, shortness of breath, nausea or fatigue during the episode. Some people describe a sudden stabbing pain in the heart, but a sharp twinge lasting only seconds more often comes from the chest wall or an intercostal nerve; a few seconds of stabbing cannot rule out a heart problem, nor prove the heart is fine.[1][2]

边界：本章只讲三类胸痛的鉴别线索，不展开诊断和治疗。突发压榨性胸痛、持续不缓解、伴大汗、呼吸困难、放射至下颌或左肩、眼前发黑或晕厥，属于需要立即拨打急救电话、急诊评估的情况；反复出现、原因不明或活动后加重的胸痛，也应就医结合心电图等检查判断。胸壁痛按压可复现、胸膜痛吸气加重、心源性痛与劳力相关，是初步区分的三条线索，但不能替代医生评估。Boundary: this chapter covers only the clues that separate these three groups, not diagnosis or treatment. Crushing chest pain that starts suddenly, does not ease, comes with heavy sweating, breathlessness, radiation to the jaw or left shoulder, blurred vision or fainting, requires an immediate emergency call and hospital evaluation. Recurrent, unexplained or exertion-related chest pain should also be assessed with an ECG and other tests. Chest-wall pain reproducible by pressure, pleuritic pain worsening on inspiration, and exertion-related cardiac pain are three preliminary clues, not a substitute for medical evaluation.[1][2][8]

07为什么会呼吸困难？呼吸时胸口发闷是怎么回事？Why does breathing become difficult, and what causes chest tightness during breathing?

通气、扩散、运输与化学调节任一环节跟不上需氧，呼吸肌用力、频率加快，主观感到气短胸闷。Ventilation, diffusion, transport or chemical regulation failing demand raises muscle effort and rate and causes subjective breathlessness and tightness.

呼吸困难是身体对供氧与需氧失衡的主观感受。当肺通气、气体扩散或血液运输跟不上组织对氧气的需求，呼吸肌会更用力地收缩，呼吸频率加快、幅度加深，人主观感到气不够用、喘不上气，活动耐量下降，严重时平卧也感到憋闷，需要垫高上半身。它是一个症状而不是单一疾病，背后可能是通气、扩散、运输或呼吸调节中的任一环节出了问题，需要结合发作诱因、持续时间和伴随表现判断。Dyspnea is the subjective feeling that oxygen supply cannot keep up with tissue demand. When ventilation, gas diffusion or blood transport fails to meet oxygen needs, respiratory muscles contract harder, breathing speeds up and deepens, and the person feels short of breath or unable to draw enough air. It is a symptom rather than one disease, and the problem may sit in ventilation, diffusion, transport or respiratory regulation.[2][3]

通气环节出问题时，空气进出肺泡的阻力或动力不足。气道狭窄、痉挛或分泌物增多会提高气道阻力；呼吸肌疲劳、胸廓活动受限则减少每次吸入的气量，肺在每次呼吸中只能交换一部分气体。静息状态下成人潮气量约 500 毫升，呼吸频率约 12–20 次/分；运动时需氧量上升，潮气量和频率同时增加，若通气能力跟不上，就会出现活动后气短，气道梗阻明显时还可听到喘息声。In the ventilation stage, air moves into and out of alveoli against resistance or with limited power. Narrowed or spastic airways and extra secretions raise airway resistance; fatigued respiratory muscles or restricted chest movement reduce the volume drawn in each breath. At rest, an adult tidal volume is about 500 mL and respiratory rate roughly 12 to 20 breaths per minute; exercise raises oxygen demand and increases both tidal volume and frequency, so insufficient ventilation causes exertional breathlessness.[2][8]

扩散与运输环节决定氧气能否进入血液并送达组织。肺泡膜增厚、交换面积下降或通气—血流不匹配，会降低氧气跨膜扩散；红细胞和血红蛋白不足、血氧饱和度下降，则使血液携氧能力减弱，即使通气正常也可能出现组织供氧不足。血氧分压和饱和度是临床判断换气与运输状态的常用指标，扩散面积、膜厚度和血液携氧能力任一受限，都会让组织供氧不足。Diffusion and transport decide whether oxygen enters blood and reaches tissue. A thickened alveolar membrane, reduced exchange surface or ventilation-perfusion mismatch lowers oxygen diffusion; fewer red cells or lower hemoglobin and oxygen saturation weaken the blood's carrying capacity. Blood oxygen partial pressure and saturation are common clinical indices of ventilation and transport, and a blockage at any point leaves tissue under-supplied.[3][8]

呼吸感受还受化学调节驱动。颈动脉体和延髓感受器感知血氧、二氧化碳分压和血液酸碱度；二氧化碳潴留、酸中毒或运动产生的乳酸都会刺激呼吸中枢，加深加快呼吸。情绪紧张、过度通气也会改变呼吸节奏，出现胸闷、气短、口周发麻的主观感受，但机制与器质性换气障碍不同；主观胸闷还可能与心率、心律变化有关，需要检查区分。Breathing drive is also shaped by chemical regulation. Carotid body and medullary sensors track blood oxygen, carbon dioxide pressure and pH; retained carbon dioxide, acidosis or lactate from exercise stimulate the respiratory center to deepen and speed breathing. Emotional tension and hyperventilation can alter breathing rhythm and produce chest tightness or breathlessness, but their mechanism differs from organic ventilation failure.[2][3]

边界：本章讲呼吸困难的环节框架，不展开病因诊断、肺功能指标或用药。安静状态下持续气短、突发喘憋、伴胸痛、嘴唇发紫、不能平卧或意识改变，需要立即就医；活动后短暂气短多与体能水平有关，但反复出现或逐渐加重时应结合检查判断，不能凭换气常识自我归因。Boundary: this chapter gives the framework of dyspnea stages, not diagnostic causes, pulmonary-function indices or medication. Persistent breathlessness at rest, sudden severe shortness of breath, chest pain, bluish lips, inability to lie flat or altered consciousness require immediate medical care. Brief breathlessness after activity often tracks fitness level, but recurrent or worsening episodes deserve examination rather than self-attribution from ventilation basics.[2][3][8]

08血压为什么会升高？血压忽高忽低是什么原因？Why does blood pressure rise, and why does it fluctuate up and down?

血压等于心输出量乘外周阻力，受血管弹性、血容量与交感-RAAS 调节；波动来自体位情绪运动等短期因素。Pressure equals cardiac output times peripheral resistance, shaped by vessel elasticity, volume and sympathetic-RAAS regulation; fluctuation follows posture, emotion and exercise.

血压是血液在血管内流动时对动脉壁产生的侧压力，心室收缩射血时的峰值为收缩压，心室舒张、动脉弹性回缩时的最低值为舒张压，临床记录为收缩压/舒张压，单位毫米汞柱（mmHg）。成人诊室血压常用参考：理想水平低于 120/80 mmHg，达到或超过 140/90 mmHg 通常作为高血压的诊断阈值，不同指南的分级切点略有差异，单次测量偏高不能直接下结论。Blood pressure is the lateral force blood exerts on arterial walls as it flows. The peak during ventricular contraction is systolic pressure, and the minimum during diastole, when elastic arteries recoil, is diastolic pressure; readings are recorded as systolic over diastolic in millimeters of mercury, mmHg. Common clinic reference levels place ideal blood pressure below 120/80 mmHg, while 140/90 mmHg or above is generally used as the diagnostic threshold for hypertension, with minor guideline differences in grading cut points; one high reading alone does not justify a diagnosis.[9][10]

从循环关系看，血压等于心输出量乘以外周阻力，而不是简单由“血量多少”决定。心输出量取决于心率和每搏输出量；外周阻力主要来自小动脉的收缩状态；大动脉弹性好时可缓冲收缩压、维持舒张压，血管硬化、顺应性下降后收缩压更容易升高。血容量通过影响回心血量和每搏量参与血压形成，但容量潴留与血管张力共同作用，不能把高血压理解成“血太多”。In circulatory terms blood pressure equals cardiac output times peripheral resistance, rather than following blood volume alone. Cardiac output depends on heart rate and stroke volume; peripheral resistance follows the contractile state of small arteries. Elastic large arteries buffer systolic pressure and sustain diastolic pressure, so stiffened, less compliant vessels tend to raise systolic pressure. Blood volume contributes through venous return and stroke volume, but volume retention acts together with vascular tone; hypertension should not be read as simply having too much blood.[9][10]

血压受两套系统短时和长期调节。交感神经兴奋使心率加快、心肌收缩增强、小动脉收缩，快速升高血压；颈动脉窦和主动脉弓压力感受器感知血压变化，通过反射调整交感与副交感张力。长期调节主要靠肾素-血管紧张素-醛固酮系统：肾素启动血管紧张素生成，既收缩血管又促进醛固酮保钠保水，调整阻力和容量；抗利尿激素和肾脏排钠也参与长期平衡。Blood pressure is regulated over short and long horizons by two systems. Sympathetic activation speeds the heart, strengthens contraction and constricts small arteries, raising pressure quickly; carotid sinus and aortic arch baroreceptors sense pressure changes and adjust sympathetic and parasympathetic tone by reflex. Long-term regulation relies on the renin-angiotensin-aldosterone system: renin starts angiotensin production, which both constricts vessels and prompts aldosterone to retain sodium and water, tuning resistance and volume; antidiuretic hormone and renal sodium excretion also participate in the long-term balance.[10][11]

血压本来就会随状态波动。体位变化、情绪紧张、运动、饱餐、睡眠不足、钠盐摄入和测量姿势都会让读数上下变动；正常人血压还有昼夜节律，清晨偏高、夜间偏低。血压忽高忽低可以是上述短期因素叠加，也可能与测量不规范有关；需要在安静休息后规范复测、连续记录，才能判断是否存在持续性高血压，不能凭一两次偶测值下定论。Blood pressure fluctuates by state. Posture, emotional tension, exercise, meals, poor sleep, sodium intake and measurement posture all move readings up or down; normal pressure also follows a diurnal rhythm, higher in the morning and lower at night. Wide swings can reflect these short-term factors together or non-standard measurement; after resting quietly, repeated standardized measurements and a continuous record are needed before deciding whether sustained hypertension exists, rather than judging from one or two casual readings.[9][11]

边界：本章讲血压形成、调节与波动的基本机制，不展开降压药物、靶器官损害评估或个人治疗方案。长期血压偏高、波动明显，或伴头痛、视物模糊、胸闷等表现，应就医规范测量和评估；血压管理涉及生活方式与医疗干预，应在专业指导下进行，不能套用通用结论。Boundary: this chapter covers how pressure forms, regulates and fluctuates, not antihypertensive drugs, target-organ damage assessment or individual treatment plans. Persistently high or markedly fluctuating pressure, especially with headache, blurred vision or chest tightness, should be measured and evaluated in medical care; blood-pressure management combines lifestyle and clinical guidance and cannot follow generic conclusions.[9][10][11]

09为什么血压会偏低？血压低时身体怎样代偿？Why does blood pressure run low, and how does the body compensate?

低心输出量、低阻力或低容量使灌注不足；压力感受器启动交感兴奋与 RAAS 保水，体位变化可致头晕黑朦。Low output, low resistance or low volume reduces perfusion; baroreceptors trigger sympathetic activation and RAAS water retention, and posture change may cause dizziness and blackout.

低血压指血压低于维持组织灌注所需水平的状态。不少年轻人体型偏瘦、长期血压基础值偏低，没有头晕、乏力等不适，多属于体质性差异，并不等于疾病；部分人平时血压就偏低，运动后心率和血压仍能正常上升。判断血压是否偏低，不能只看一个数值，还要结合体位变化、伴随症状和平时基线水平综合看待，无症状的基础偏低与伴症状的低血压意义不同。Hypotension means pressure below the level needed to maintain tissue perfusion. Many young, lean adults run a persistently low baseline pressure without dizziness or fatigue, which mostly reflects constitutional variation rather than disease. Whether pressure is too low cannot be judged from one number; posture change, accompanying symptoms and the person's usual baseline all need to be weighed together.[9][10]

血压下降通常来自三个方向：心输出量不足、外周阻力不足或血容量不足。心率偏慢、心肌收缩力偏弱会降低每搏输出量；小动脉张力不足、血管扩张使阻力下降；脱水、出汗过多、进食饮水少、腹泻或失血则减少循环血量。三条路径任一突出，都可能让血压降到不足以保证脑和肌肉灌注的水平，不同原因对应的补液、休息或就医方向也不同。Pressure falls through three routes: low cardiac output, low peripheral resistance or low blood volume. Slow heart rate or weak contraction reduces stroke volume; insufficient small-artery tone and vasodilation lower resistance; dehydration, heavy sweating, poor fluid or food intake, diarrhea or blood loss reduce circulating volume. Any one of these standing out can drop pressure below what the brain and muscles need.[9][10]

身体有一套快速代偿反应。颈动脉窦和主动脉弓压力感受器感知灌注下降后，反射性提高交感张力、减弱副交感作用，心率加快、心肌收缩增强、小动脉收缩，试图把血压拉回；中长期则由肾素-血管紧张素-醛固酮系统和抗利尿激素参与，促进保钠保水、维持血容量。代偿及时时人可能只感到轻微乏力，代偿不足才出现明显症状；老年人、长期卧床者和服用相关药物者的代偿反应往往偏弱。The body has a fast compensation loop. Carotid sinus and aortic arch baroreceptors sense reduced perfusion, raise sympathetic tone and dampen parasympathetic action by reflex, speeding the heart, strengthening contraction and constricting small arteries to pull pressure back up. Over the medium and long term, the renin-angiotensin-aldosterone system and antidiuretic hormone retain sodium and water to maintain volume. When compensation is timely there may be only mild fatigue; clear symptoms appear when it falls short.[10][11]

低血压常见表现是脑和肌肉供血不足：起身时头晕、眼前发黑、乏力、站立不稳，严重时短暂晕厥。体位性低血压指从卧位或坐位站起后，收缩压下降达到 20 mmHg 或舒张压下降达到 10 mmHg 以上；久站、闷热环境、饱餐之后、长时间卧床后起身也可能诱发类似不适。脱水、发热和部分药物会放大这种反应，反复发生时应记录发作时的体位、诱因和伴随表现。Common low-pressure symptoms follow poor blood supply to brain and muscle: dizziness on standing, blurred or blacked-out vision, fatigue and unsteadiness. Orthostatic hypotension is defined as a systolic drop of 20 mmHg or a diastolic drop of 10 mmHg or more after rising from lying or sitting. Prolonged standing, hot environments, after meals and getting up after bed rest can trigger similar episodes; dehydration, fever and some medications amplify the reaction.[9][11]

边界：本章讲低血压的形成与代偿机制，不展开升压药物或病因诊断。若伴晕厥、胸痛、黑便或便血、持续脱水、面色苍白，或在服药后明显低血压，应及时就医评估；长期无症状的基础偏低血压通常不需要特殊处理。体位变化时动作放缓、保证饮水和进食是一般注意事项，具体原因需结合病史和检查判断。Boundary: this chapter covers how low pressure forms and how compensation works, not pressor drugs or diagnostic causes. Fainting, chest pain, black or bloody stools, persistent dehydration, pallor, or marked hypotension after medication warrant medical evaluation. A long-standing, asymptomatic low baseline usually needs no special treatment. Rising slowly, keeping fluids and meals regular are general precautions; the specific cause depends on history and examination.[9][10][11]
