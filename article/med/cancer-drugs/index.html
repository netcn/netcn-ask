癌症药物：靶点、化疗与耐药机制Cancer Drugs: Targets, Chemotherapy and Resistance

从驱动突变怎样成为可成药靶点，到细胞毒化疗的三类作用环节，再到小分子 TKI 与单克隆抗体的分工、耐药机制与临床试验验证：讲清分子靶点、作用通路、证据等级和现有能力边界，不提供个体化用药建议。How driver mutations become druggable targets, the three chemotherapy classes, TKIs and antibodies, resistance and trial validation: targets, pathways, evidence strength and the boundary of what can be concluded.

01癌症药物是怎样找到靶点的？驱动突变如何决定用哪一类药？How do cancer drugs identify targets, and how do driver mutations decide which drug class to use?

驱动突变产生可被药物结合的异常蛋白；分子检测匹配靶点，再决定用药类别。Driver mutations produce abnormal proteins a drug can bind; molecular testing matches the target before a drug class is chosen.

肿瘤能成为药物的作用对象，是因为癌细胞携带改变生长与存活程序的分子异常。正常细胞的增殖、分裂和凋亡受检查点控制；体细胞分裂中累积突变后，某些基因活性被改写，细胞脱离控制、持续生长形成肿瘤。能被药物特异性识别并结合的异常蛋白、酶或受体，就是药物靶点；靶向治疗把药物作用到这一异常分子上，而不是笼统攻击所有分裂细胞。A cancer drug can target a tumor because malignant cells carry molecular abnormalities that override growth and survival controls. In a normal cell, proliferation, division and apoptosis are held in place by checkpoints; as somatic mutations accumulate, some genes change activity, the cell escapes that control, and it keeps growing into a tumor. A target is an abnormal protein, enzyme or receptor that a drug can recognize and bind specifically. Targeted therapy aims the drug at that abnormal molecule rather than attacking every dividing cell indiscriminately.[6]

从突变到靶点，通常分两类方向。一类是原癌基因被激活：正常基因发生点突变、扩增或融合后，编码的蛋白激酶持续处于开启状态，把分裂信号不断放大。肺腺癌常见的 EGFR 突变、乳腺癌的 HER2 基因扩增、慢性粒细胞白血病中的 BCR-ABL 融合基因，都属于这一类；激酶一旦持续自磷酸化，下游通路就不受配体控制。另一类是抑癌基因失活，例如 p53 这类检查点蛋白失去功能，损伤的 DNA 得不到修复或清除。抑癌失活本身较难直接用小分子阻断，药物更多针对被激活的下游通路。Mutations map to targets in two main directions. One is activation of a proto-oncogene: a normal gene gains a point mutation, amplification or fusion, and its encoded kinase stays permanently on, amplifying the division signal. EGFR mutations in lung adenocarcinoma, HER2 amplification in breast cancer and the BCR-ABL fusion in chronic myeloid leukemia belong here; once the kinase is constitutively autophosphorylated, downstream signaling runs without a ligand. The other direction is loss of a tumor suppressor: a checkpoint protein such as p53 loses function, so damaged DNA is neither repaired nor cleared. A suppressor defect is harder to block directly with a small molecule, and drugs usually act on the activated downstream pathway instead.[1][6]

临床上寻找靶点，依赖分子检测而不是影像。肿瘤组织或外周血样本经测序、免疫组化等手段，判断是否存在已知的驱动突变或蛋白过表达；检测结果与获批药物的适应症匹配后，才决定用药类别。美国国家癌症研究所（NCI）的靶向治疗事实表把这类药物定义为干扰参与肿瘤生长、扩散的特定分子，OpenStax 生物学教材则把 p53、Rb 等检查点与细胞周期失控联系起来理解。同一类肿瘤在不同患者身上的驱动突变可能不同，这也是需要分型用药的原因。Clinically, targets are found through molecular testing rather than imaging. Tumor tissue or a blood sample is sequenced and examined by immunohistochemistry to identify a known driver mutation or protein overexpression; only when the result matches an approved drug's indication is a drug class chosen. The NCI targeted therapies fact sheet defines these drugs as interfering with specific molecules involved in tumor growth and spread, while OpenStax biology links checkpoints such as p53 and Rb to loss of cell-cycle control. Patients with the same tumor can carry different drivers, which is why treatment is stratified by subtype.[1][6]

致癌物与药物靶点要分开看。以石棉为例，细长纤维长期滞留肺组织，引起慢性炎症、反复组织修复和 DNA 氧化损伤，逐步提高突变概率；它是诱发因素，而不是某个可被药物结合的分子靶点。烟草烟雾中的化学致癌物、紫外线和辐射的作用路径类似：先造成损伤和突变，再选择出具有优势的克隆。理解这一区分，有助于判断一项证据是在讲致癌原因，还是在讲治疗目标。Carcinogens are distinct from drug targets. Asbestos fibers lodge in lung tissue, cause chronic inflammation, repeated repair and oxidative DNA damage, and gradually raise mutation probability; they are a causative factor, not a bindable molecular target. Chemical carcinogens in tobacco smoke, ultraviolet radiation and ionizing radiation act along similar pathways: injury and mutation first, selection of a clone later. Keeping this distinction straight matters when judging whether evidence concerns a cause of cancer or a therapeutic target.[5]

边界必须写清。并非所有突变都有对应药物，肿瘤存在异质性，一次检测只反映取样部位；找到匹配靶点不等于疗效确定，应答率和缓解持续时间仍需临床试验在对应人群中验证。目前只能把相同分子异常的患者分层比较；把群体缓解率套到个人，或把体外、动物实验结果当成临床获益，都超出证据强度。The boundary must be stated plainly. Not every mutation has a matching drug, tumors are heterogeneous, and one assay reflects only the sampled region. Finding a match does not guarantee benefit; response rate and duration still have to be verified in trials within the corresponding patient group. What is feasible today is stratifying patients by shared molecular abnormality. Extrapolating a population remission rate to one individual, or treating in vitro and animal results as clinical benefit, exceeds the strength of the evidence.[1][6]

02传统化疗药为什么能杀死癌细胞，又为什么会损伤健康组织？Why do cytotoxic chemotherapy drugs kill cancer cells, and why do they also damage healthy tissue?

化疗干扰 DNA 合成、交联与纺锤体，优先打击分裂细胞；快速更新的正常组织同样受波及。Chemotherapy disrupts DNA synthesis, cross-linking and the spindle, preferentially hitting dividing cells; rapidly renewed normal tissue is affected too.

传统化疗的基本思路是干扰 DNA 复制或细胞分裂，优先杀伤分裂活跃的细胞。与靶向药物针对特定分子异常不同，细胞毒药物作用于增殖过程中普遍存在的环节：DNA 合成、DNA 链连接、纺锤体组装。肿瘤通常分裂频繁，因此受到的打击较大；但骨髓、毛囊、消化道黏膜等同样快速更新的组织也会被波及，这是化疗不良反应的主要来源。Conventional chemotherapy works by disrupting DNA replication or cell division, preferentially killing actively dividing cells. Unlike targeted drugs that address a specific molecular abnormality, cytotoxic drugs act on steps shared by proliferation: DNA synthesis, DNA strand linkage and spindle assembly. Tumors usually divide frequently and take the larger hit, but rapidly renewed tissues such as bone marrow, hair follicles and the gut lining are also affected, which is the main source of chemotherapy side effects.[2]

按作用环节可分为三类。抗代谢药模拟叶酸或核苷酸的结构，干扰核酸合成：甲氨蝶呤抑制叶酸代谢相关酶，5-氟尿嘧啶掺入并阻断 DNA 与 RNA 合成，主要作用于 DNA 合成的 S 期。烷化剂与铂类直接在 DNA 链之间形成交联，例如环磷酰胺、顺铂，使双链无法正常解链和复制，损伤积累后触发细胞凋亡。微管抑制剂干扰纺锤体组装：紫杉醇稳定微管、阻止其解聚，长春新碱结合微管蛋白、抑制聚合，把细胞阻滞在有丝分裂的 M 期。The drugs fall into three mechanistic classes. Antimetabolites mimic folate or nucleotide structure and interfere with nucleic acid synthesis: methotrexate inhibits a folate-metabolism enzyme, and 5-fluorouracil incorporates itself and blocks DNA and RNA synthesis, acting mainly in S phase of DNA synthesis. Alkylating agents and platinum compounds form cross-links directly between DNA strands; cyclophosphamide and cisplatin prevent normal strand separation and replication, and accumulated damage triggers apoptosis. Microtubule inhibitors disturb spindle assembly: paclitaxel stabilizes microtubules and prevents depolymerization, while vincristine binds tubulin and blocks polymerization, arresting the cell in M phase of mitosis.[2][7]

化疗对细胞周期的依赖解释了副作用谱。S 期和 M 期是敏感窗口，快速分裂的造血细胞首先受影响，表现为白细胞、血小板下降；毛囊细胞分裂快，出现脱发；胃肠道黏膜更新快，出现口腔黏膜炎和腹泻。这些组织在疗程结束后多可逐步恢复，因为损伤主要针对分裂过程，而不是永久改变基因组。The dependence on cell cycle explains the side-effect profile. S phase and M phase are the sensitive windows; rapidly dividing blood-forming cells are affected first, showing as lower white cells and platelets. Hair follicles divide fast and produce hair loss, and the rapidly renewed gut lining causes oral mucositis and diarrhea. Many of these tissues recover after treatment because the injury targets the division process rather than permanently rewriting the genome.[2]

方案和剂量由证据和经验共同决定。NCI 的化疗资料说明这类药物常以联合方案使用，不同作用环节搭配可以提高杀伤并减少单药耐药；OpenStax 教材记载甲氨蝶呤是最早进入临床的化疗药物之一，其剂量和给药顺序在早期实践中逐步建立。现代方案多按体表面积计算剂量，并结合血象、肝肾功能调整，以在肿瘤控制和正常组织耐受之间取得平衡。Regimens and doses rest on evidence and accumulated practice. NCI chemotherapy materials note that such drugs are often combined, so drugs hitting different steps can improve kill and reduce single-drug resistance; OpenStax records methotrexate as one of the earliest chemotherapy drugs to enter clinical use, with dose and sequencing worked out in early practice. Modern regimens commonly calculate dose by body surface area and adjust against blood counts and liver or kidney function, balancing tumor control against normal-tissue tolerance.[6][7]

边界要写清楚。化疗不区分癌细胞与正常细胞，缓解不等于治愈；不同患者对同一方案的耐受和反应差异很大，个体的血象、合并用药都会影响结果。细胞毒机理不能外推到靶向药或抗体药物：后两者的选择性来自分子靶点，副作用谱和用药判断方式都不同。化疗是否适合，需要结合病理分型、分期和身体状况由临床团队评估，不能凭药名判断。The boundary is plain. Chemotherapy does not distinguish cancer from healthy cells, and remission is not a cure. Tolerance and response to the same regimen vary widely between patients, with blood counts and concurrent medication affecting outcomes. Cytotoxic mechanisms cannot be extended to targeted drugs or antibodies: the latter gain selectivity from molecular targets, and their side-effect profile and treatment judgment differ. Whether chemotherapy fits a case depends on pathology subtype, stage and overall condition, assessed by the clinical team rather than judged from a drug name alone.[2]

03靶向药和单克隆抗体分别通过什么方式阻断肿瘤？How do small-molecule targeted drugs and monoclonal antibodies block tumors?

小分子穿透细胞膜结合胞内激酶；抗体结合膜表面抗原并招募免疫效应，两类机制与副作用谱不同。Small molecules cross the membrane to bind intracellular kinases; antibodies bind surface antigens and recruit immune effects, with different mechanisms and side-effect profiles.

靶向药物分两大形态：可进入细胞的小分子和留在胞外的单克隆抗体。小分子酪氨酸激酶抑制剂体积小，能穿透细胞膜，结合胞内激酶的 ATP 结合位点，阻止磷酸化反应；单克隆抗体是大分子蛋白，通常无法自由穿过细胞膜，结合的是细胞膜表面受体或抗原的胞外区域。两类药物阻断信号的位置不同，适用的分子异常和给药方式也不同。Targeted drugs come in two forms: small molecules that enter the cell and monoclonal antibodies that stay outside. Small-molecule tyrosine kinase inhibitors are compact enough to cross the membrane and bind the ATP pocket of an intracellular kinase, blocking phosphorylation. Monoclonal antibodies are large proteins that generally cannot cross the membrane freely; they bind the extracellular domain of a membrane receptor or antigen. The two classes block signaling at different positions, and they suit different molecular abnormalities and delivery routes.[1][3][6]

典型实例可以对照着看。伊马替尼结合 BCR-ABL 融合激酶的 ATP 位点，使慢性粒细胞白血病中的异常激酶失去活性，是小分子 TKI 的代表；吉非替尼、厄洛替尼针对 EGFR 突变的胞内激酶区。抗体方面，曲妥珠单抗结合 HER2 蛋白的胞外域，西妥昔单抗结合 EGFR 的胞外域；抗体结合后可以促进受体内化，阻断配体结合，还能通过抗体依赖的细胞介导毒性和补体依赖反应招募免疫系统参与杀伤。The classic examples line up side by side. Imatinib binds the ATP pocket of the BCR-ABL fusion kinase and silences the abnormal kinase in chronic myeloid leukemia, representing the small-molecule TKI; gefitinib and erlotinib target the intracellular kinase region of EGFR mutations. Among antibodies, trastuzumab binds the extracellular domain of HER2, and cetuximab binds the extracellular domain of EGFR. After binding, antibodies can promote receptor internalization, block ligand attachment, and recruit immune killing through antibody-dependent cellular cytotoxicity and complement-dependent reactions.[1][3]

抗体药物除了直接阻断受体信号，还携带免疫效应。NCI 的单克隆抗体资料列出几种作用方式：附着到肿瘤细胞表面的抗原上，标记其供免疫系统清除；阻断受体与配体结合；或促使特定细胞表面蛋白下调。部分抗体还与化疗、放射性核素或细胞毒载荷偶联，由抗体把载荷带到肿瘤部位。这些机制都依赖靶点抗原在肿瘤细胞上的表达，检测不匹配时疗效缺乏依据。Beyond directly blocking receptor signaling, antibodies carry immune effects. NCI monoclonal-antibody materials list several modes: attaching to an antigen on the tumor cell surface and marking it for immune clearance; blocking receptor-ligand binding; or driving down-regulation of a surface protein. Some antibodies are conjugated to chemotherapy, a radionuclide or a cytotoxic payload, with the antibody carrying the payload to the tumor. All these mechanisms depend on antigen expression on the tumor; without a matching target, efficacy has no basis.[3]

选择性带来不同的副作用谱。靶向小分子和抗体针对特定分子通路，不良反应多与该通路在正常组织中的生理功能有关，例如皮肤反应、心脏功能影响或输液相关反应，而不是像化疗那样普遍攻击快速分裂组织。但"靶向"不等于无毒：靶点在正常细胞上同样存在，长期抑制可能影响正常生理，耐受性需要在疗程中持续观察。Selectivity produces a different side-effect profile. Targeted small molecules and antibodies act on a specific molecular pathway, so adverse effects often relate to that pathway's normal physiological role, such as skin reactions, cardiac effects or infusion-related reactions, rather than attacking rapidly dividing tissue across the board. But "targeted" does not mean harmless: the target also exists on normal cells, and long-term inhibition can affect normal physiology, so tolerance must be watched throughout treatment.[1][3]

边界要说明。抗体分子量大，在需要穿过血脑屏障或深部组织的场景中受到限制；靶点表达会随治疗和克隆变化，初始有效后仍可能出现耐药。一种抗体或 TKI 是否适用，取决于分子检测结果、已获批的适应症和临床试验证据，不能仅凭肿瘤名称推断。跨适应症用药属于研究性使用，应在临床试验或规范指导下进行。The boundary deserves note. Antibodies are large and are limited where crossing the blood-brain barrier or deep tissue is required. Target expression changes with treatment and across clones, so an initial response can still give way to resistance. Whether an antibody or TKI fits depends on molecular testing, approved indications and trial evidence; it cannot be inferred from the tumor name alone. Use outside an approved indication is investigational and belongs in a clinical trial or formal guidance.[1]

04肿瘤为什么会产生耐药，药物疗效又通过什么试验验证？Why do tumors develop resistance, and how is drug efficacy tested?

二次突变、旁路激活、外排泵和克隆选择导致耐药；疗效经随机对照试验和终点评估，安慰剂在癌症试验中很少单用。Secondary mutations, bypass activation, efflux pumps and clonal selection drive resistance; efficacy is tested in randomized trials, and placebo is rarely used alone.

肿瘤耐药在分子和群体两个层面都能发生。分子层面，药物结合位点出现二次突变，使药物亲和力下降；原本被抑制的通路通过旁路激酶或受体重新激活；细胞膜上的外排泵把药物转运出细胞，降低胞内浓度。群体层面，肿瘤内部存在异质性，治疗先杀灭敏感克隆，少数携带耐药变异的克隆被选择出来并扩增，这一过程类似抗生素选择耐药菌。Resistance arises at both the molecular and the population level. At the molecular level, a secondary mutation at the drug binding site lowers affinity; the suppressed pathway is reactivated through a bypass kinase or receptor; and efflux pumps on the membrane move the drug out of the cell, lowering intracellular concentration. At the population level, tumors are heterogeneous: treatment kills sensitive clones first, and a few clones carrying resistant variants are selected and expand, in a way analogous to antibiotic selection of resistant bacteria.[1][2]

临床应对围绕耐药机制展开。针对二次突变，可以开发结合方式不同的新一代抑制剂；针对旁路激活，采用联合用药同时阻断主通路和代偿通路；序贯治疗在初始药物失效后换用不同机制的药物。液体活检通过监测血液中的循环肿瘤 DNA，追踪残余病灶和新出现的突变，帮助判断耐药信号出现的时间，而不必每次都依赖再次组织活检。Clinical responses to resistance follow those mechanisms. Against secondary mutations, next-generation inhibitors with a different binding mode are developed; against bypass activation, combination therapy blocks both the main and compensatory pathways; sequencing switches to a drug with a different mechanism after the first drug fails. Liquid biopsy monitors circulating tumor DNA in blood, tracking residual disease and emerging mutations to detect resistance earlier, without relying on repeat tissue biopsy every time.[1]

药物疗效要通过临床试验验证。试验按阶段推进，先评估安全性和剂量，再比较新方案与标准治疗的应答率、无进展生存或总生存。随机分组和盲法用于减少选择偏倚和评估偏倚；NCI 的临床试验资料说明，研究人员会比较两组治疗，判断新方案能否成为新标准。试验终点的选择直接影响结论：影像学缩小不等于长期生存获益，替代终点需要在后续研究中验证。Drug efficacy is validated through clinical trials. Trials advance in stages, first assessing safety and dose, then comparing response rate, progression-free survival or overall survival between the new regimen and standard care. Randomization and blinding reduce selection and assessment bias; NCI trial materials explain that researchers compare the two treatment groups to decide whether the new regimen can become the standard. The chosen endpoint shapes the conclusion: imaging shrinkage does not equal long-term survival benefit, and surrogate endpoints must be verified in later studies.[4]

安慰剂在癌症治疗试验中很少单独使用。NCI 资料指出，安慰剂仅在没有标准治疗时才可能单独设置，更多见的是标准治疗加安慰剂对照标准治疗加新药；安慰剂外观与受试药一致，用于掩盖分组、减少偏差。受试者在入组前会被告知是否使用安慰剂，知情同意文件中写明安排；伦理上，已有有效治疗时不会让患者仅接受安慰剂。Placebos are rarely used alone in cancer treatment trials. NCI notes that a placebo alone is possible only when no standard treatment exists; far more often the trial compares standard treatment plus placebo against standard treatment plus the new drug. The placebo matches the study drug in appearance to conceal group assignment and reduce bias. Participants are told before enrollment whether a placebo is used, and the consent form states the arrangement; ethically, a patient will not receive placebo alone when an effective treatment exists.[4]

证据边界必须保守表述。体外细胞和动物模型中的抑制效果，不等于人类患者的临床获益；耐药倾向可以预测概率，但不能确定某个克隆何时出现。个体反应受合并疾病、肝肾功能、药物相互作用影响，群体试验的中位数据不能直接套到个人。把早期研究的初步信号说成确定疗效，或把一次缓解当作长期控制，都超出了当前证据能支持的结论。The evidence boundary must be stated conservatively. Inhibition in cell culture and animal models does not equal clinical benefit in human patients; resistance trends predict probabilities, not the time when a given clone appears. Individual response is shaped by co-morbidities, liver and kidney function, and drug interactions, so group medians cannot be applied directly to one person. Describing an early signal as definite efficacy, or a single remission as long-term control, goes beyond what the current evidence supports.[1][4]
