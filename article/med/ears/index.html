01声音怎样走完外耳、中耳和内耳，变成神经信号？How does sound travel through the outer, middle and inner ear and become neural signals?

耳廓收集声波，鼓膜与听小骨传力，耳蜗毛细胞把振动变成电信号。The pinna collects sound; the eardrum and ossicles transmit force; cochlear hair cells convert vibration into electrical signals.

把耳朵贴近桌面，能听见远处火车经过的低鸣；用手指堵住耳道，声音立刻变闷。耳廓像一只略向前弯的漏斗，把来自不同方向的声波收进耳道。声波本质上是空气压力的微小起伏，耳道把它引到鼓膜；鼓膜是一层紧绷的薄膜，压力变化使它前后振动。振动再通过中耳的三块听小骨——锤骨、砧骨和镫骨——传到内耳。三块小骨不是简单的接力棒，它们把鼓膜上较大的面积、较小的力，转换成卵圆窗上较小的面积、较大的压强，让空气中的振动更容易进入充满液体的耳蜗。Put your ear against a table and you can hear a distant train rumble; plug the canal and everything sounds muffled. The pinna is a slightly curved funnel that collects sound from different directions and sends it into the ear canal. Sound is a tiny fluctuation in air pressure; the canal guides it to the eardrum, a taut membrane that moves back and forth. The vibration then passes through the three middle-ear ossicles—malleus, incus and stapes—into the inner ear. The ossicles do more than relay: they convert a larger area and smaller force at the eardrum into a smaller area and larger pressure at the oval window, helping air-borne sound enter the fluid-filled cochlea.[1][2]

耳蜗像一枚约两圈半的蜗牛壳，里面排列着成千上万的毛细胞。声波使耳蜗内的液体和基底膜振动，不同频率在基底膜的不同位置形成峰值：高频靠近入口，低频靠近顶端。毛细胞顶端的纤毛随振动弯曲，把机械位移转成电信号，再沿听神经传向脑干和大脑。人耳能听到约 20 赫兹到 20 千赫兹的声音，但随年龄增长，高频通常先下降。The cochlea is a coiled tube about two and a half turns long, lined with thousands of hair cells. Sound vibrates the fluid and the basilar membrane, and different frequencies peak at different places: high frequencies near the base, low frequencies near the apex. Hair bundles bend with the vibration, turning mechanical displacement into electrical signals that travel along the auditory nerve to the brainstem and brain. Humans hear roughly 20 Hz to 20 kHz, but high frequencies usually decline first with age.[1][2]

耳道并不是一根空管。它外段有毛囊和耵聍腺，分泌的耳屎能粘住灰尘和小虫，弱酸性环境也抑制部分细菌。耳屎通常会随着咀嚼、说话和下颌运动慢慢向外移动，不需要用棉签深挖。棉签会把耳屎推得更深，甚至压到鼓膜上；掏耳朵还可能划伤耳道皮肤，引起感染。只有耳屎堵塞、听力下降或耳道疼痛时，才应请医生在直视下清理。The ear canal is not an empty pipe. Its outer part has hair follicles and cerumen glands; earwax traps dust and small insects, and its slightly acidic environment suppresses some bacteria. Wax usually migrates outward with chewing, speaking and jaw movement, so deep cotton-swab cleaning is unnecessary. Swabs push wax deeper, sometimes against the eardrum, and can scratch the skin and cause infection. Cleaning under direct vision is for blockage, hearing loss or pain.[1][4][5]

听到声音并不等于听清。外耳和中耳负责传声，内耳和听神经负责把声音变成信号；任何一环出问题，表现都可能是“听不见”。传导性听力损失常与耳屎堵塞、中耳炎、鼓膜穿孔有关；感音神经性听力损失多与毛细胞或听神经损伤有关。纯音测听、声导抗和言语识别测试能把两者分开，不能只凭“我觉得小声”判断。Hearing sound is not the same as understanding speech. The outer and middle ear conduct sound; the inner ear and auditory nerve turn it into signals. Problems at any point can present as “not hearing”. Conductive loss is often linked to wax, middle-ear infection or a perforated eardrum; sensorineural loss usually involves hair cells or the auditory nerve. Pure-tone audiometry, tympanometry and speech testing separate the two; “it sounds quiet” is not enough.[2][3]

边界在于：同样的声压，不同人对“吵”的感受不同；同样的听力图，不同人对言语的理解也不同。耳朵是一个连续的通路，不是单独的鼓膜或耳蜗。判断听力问题，要看气导与骨导的差值、言语识别得分和病程，而不是只盯一个频率。The boundary: the same sound pressure feels different to different people, and the same audiogram can support different speech understanding. The ear is a continuous pathway, not a lone eardrum or cochlea. Assessment looks at air–bone gaps, speech scores and time course, not a single frequency.[1][3]

02噪声多大才会伤耳？听力损失为什么常常不可逆？How loud is too loud, and why is noise-induced hearing loss often permanent?

85 分贝连续 8 小时已是风险线；毛细胞死后不能再生，噪声性听力损失通常不可逆。Eighty-five decibels for eight hours is already a risk line; hair cells do not regenerate, so noise-induced hearing loss is usually permanent.

噪声伤耳不是“声音大一点”这么简单。声压每增加 10 分贝，能量增加 10 倍；85 分贝大约相当于繁忙城市道路或嘈杂餐厅，连续暴露 8 小时已接近风险线；100 分贝的演唱会或电锯，可能几十分钟就造成损伤。脉冲噪声更危险：枪声、爆竹、爆炸的峰值可超过 140 分贝，瞬间就能震伤毛细胞。85 分贝是职业暴露的 8 小时加权平均值，不是瞬时峰值；峰值要用 C 计权或峰值声压级单独衡量。Noise damage is not simply “a loud sound”. Every 10 dB increase raises energy tenfold. At 85 dB—roughly a busy road or a noisy restaurant—eight hours of exposure is already near the risk line; at 100 dB—a concert or a chainsaw—minutes to tens of minutes can cause damage. Impulse noise is worse: gunshots, firecrackers and explosions can peak above 140 dB and injure hair cells instantly.[3][4]

毛细胞是耳蜗里不可再生的“传感器”。强烈噪声会让纤毛机械性断裂，也会产生过量活性氧，损伤细胞和螺旋神经节。暂时性阈移——听完演唱会耳朵发闷、耳鸣——说明毛细胞已经受累；如果反复发生，暂时性阈移会变成永久性阈移。噪声性听力损失通常先丢高频，患者常觉得“听得到但听不清”，尤其在嘈杂环境。耳蜗外毛细胞负责放大弱信号，内毛细胞负责把机械振动传给听神经；两类细胞都受损时，言语识别下降更明显。Hair cells are the cochlea’s non-regenerating sensors. Intense noise can mechanically break hair bundles and generate excess reactive oxygen species, damaging cells and spiral ganglion neurons. A temporary threshold shift—muffled hearing or tinnitus after a concert—shows that hair cells have been stressed; repeated episodes can turn it into a permanent shift. Noise-induced loss usually starts in the high frequencies, so people often report “I can hear, but I cannot understand speech in noise”.[2][3]

保护不是“忍一忍”。离开噪声源、降低音量、缩短暴露时间，比事后吃药有效。耳机遵循 60/60 原则：音量不超过最大音量的 60%，连续使用不超过 60 分钟；在 85 分贝以上的环境，应佩戴合适的耳塞或耳罩。耳塞要选降噪量足够、佩戴密封的型号；棉花团几乎不起作用。耳塞的降噪值（NRR/SNR）不是戴上就全额生效，实际佩戴密封性差会打折。Protection is not endurance. Leaving the noise source, lowering the volume and shortening exposure work better than any pill afterwards. For headphones, follow 60/60: no more than 60% of maximum volume for no more than 60 minutes at a time. Above 85 dB, wear properly fitted earplugs or earmuffs with adequate attenuation; cotton wool is nearly useless.[4]

噪声的影响不止听力。长期暴露可升高血压、增加心血管风险、干扰睡眠和注意力；突然的强脉冲还可能造成鼓膜穿孔。已经出现持续耳鸣、耳闷或言语辨识下降，应尽快做听力学检查。噪声性听力损失不可逆，但可以预防；助听器和听力保护能减少进一步损伤。儿童耳道短、鼓膜薄，对同样声压更敏感；家长要特别留意玩具、爆竹和耳机。Noise affects more than hearing. Chronic exposure can raise blood pressure, increase cardiovascular risk and disturb sleep and attention; a sudden impulse can perforate the eardrum. Persistent tinnitus, fullness or reduced speech recognition warrants an audiology assessment. Noise-induced hearing loss is permanent but preventable; hearing protection and hearing aids can limit further damage.[3][4]

边界：同样的噪声，有人敏感、有人耐受，个体差异真实存在；年龄、药物、疾病也会叠加。不要用“我习惯了”判断安全，习惯只是主观适应，毛细胞不会因为习惯而免疫。判断风险要看声压级、暴露时长和是否出现暂时性阈移。The boundary: people differ in susceptibility, and age, drugs and illness add up. “I am used to it” is subjective adaptation, not immunity—hair cells do not adapt. Judge risk by sound level, exposure time and whether a temporary threshold shift occurs.[3]

03耳鸣不是耳朵在响：声音从哪里来？Tinnitus is not the ear ringing: where does the sound come from?

耳鸣多是耳蜗损伤后大脑中枢增益上调的结果，不是外界真有声音。Tinnitus often reflects increased central gain after cochlear damage; it is not a real external sound.

耳鸣是“耳朵里一直有声音”，但检查时外界并没有这个声音。它可能是蝉鸣、嗡嗡、呼呼或脉搏样的节律。耳鸣不是一种独立疾病，而是听觉系统发出的症状：耳蜗毛细胞、听神经或中枢听觉通路受到损伤后，大脑会提高增益来“寻找”缺失的信号，安静时尤其明显。它可以是持续音，也可以是间歇音；音量会随疲劳、睡眠和情绪波动。Tinnitus is a sound heard only by the patient; there is no corresponding external source. It may be a cicada-like ring, buzzing, whooshing or a pulse-synchronous beat. Tinnitus is not a disease in itself but a symptom of the auditory system: after hair cells, the auditory nerve or central pathways are damaged, the brain turns up its gain to “look” for the missing signal, especially in quiet.[2][7]

最常见的原因是噪声性损伤和年龄相关听力下降，耳屎堵塞、中耳炎、耳毒性药物、头部外伤、颞下颌关节问题也可能参与。搏动性耳鸣与心跳同步，要警惕血管性原因；单侧持续耳鸣伴听力下降，需要排除听神经瘤等结构问题。耳鸣本身很少直接导致耳聋，但它会干扰睡眠、注意力和情绪。一些常用药（如高剂量阿司匹林、某些利尿剂和氨基糖苷类抗生素）可能诱发或加重耳鸣，但需由医生评估，不能自行停药。The commonest causes are noise injury and age-related hearing loss; wax impaction, middle-ear disease, ototoxic drugs, head injury and temporomandibular joint problems can also contribute. Pulse-synchronous tinnitus raises concern for vascular causes; unilateral persistent tinnitus with hearing loss needs evaluation for structural problems such as a vestibular schwannoma. Tinnitus rarely causes deafness directly, but it can disrupt sleep, attention and mood.[3][7]

目前没有一种“吃掉耳鸣”的药。证据较充分的是认知行为治疗、耳鸣习服治疗和声音治疗：目标不是让声音消失，而是降低它对生活的控制。助听器对伴有听力下降的人常有帮助，因为放大外界声音能减少大脑的过度补偿。安静环境会让耳鸣更突出，完全躲进无声房间反而可能加重。声音治疗使用宽频噪声或自然声，音量通常低于耳鸣声，目的是让听觉系统重新适应，而不是盖住耳鸣。No drug simply “removes” tinnitus. The better-supported approaches are cognitive behavioural therapy, tinnitus retraining and sound therapy: the goal is not silence but reducing how much tinnitus controls daily life. Hearing aids often help people with coexisting hearing loss, because amplifying external sound reduces central overcompensation. Quiet makes tinnitus more prominent; hiding in a silent room can make it worse.[7]

应对耳鸣，先做听力学和耳科检查，排除可治疗的原因；再记录出现时间、音量、伴随症状和对睡眠的影响。减少咖啡因和酒精、保证睡眠、避免突然强声，有助于降低波动。不要反复用棉签掏耳，也不要把“补肾”“偏方”当成主要方案。睡眠不足和焦虑会把耳鸣的注意力放大，形成越关注越响的循环；规律作息和放松训练是基础。A practical approach starts with audiology and ear examination to rule out treatable causes, then a diary of timing, loudness, associated symptoms and sleep impact. Reducing caffeine and alcohol, protecting sleep and avoiding sudden loud sounds can reduce fluctuations. Do not keep digging at wax with cotton swabs, and do not treat “kidney tonics” or folk remedies as the main plan.[7]

边界：耳鸣的主观感受和客观测量并不完全对应，别人听不到不代表不存在；同样强度的耳鸣，有人能忽略，有人被折磨。治疗目标是功能改善，不是追求绝对安静。若耳鸣突然发生、单侧加重或伴眩晕/面瘫，应尽快就医。The boundary: subjective tinnitus and objective measurements do not map neatly; others not hearing it does not mean it is unreal. The same loudness can be ignorable for one person and disabling for another. The goal is functional improvement, not absolute silence. Sudden tinnitus, unilateral worsening, vertigo or facial weakness needs prompt medical review.[3][7]

04晕车、眩晕和平衡：前庭系统怎样判断你在动？Motion sickness, vertigo and balance: how does the vestibular system know you are moving?

前庭、视觉和本体感觉信号冲突时，人会出现晕车或眩晕。When vestibular, visual and proprioceptive signals conflict, motion sickness or vertigo can result.

闭眼原地转几圈，停下来会觉得天旋地转；坐车看手机，车在动、眼睛却盯着静止屏幕，胃里很快翻腾。这些体验都和前庭系统有关。内耳里有三条互相垂直的半规管，负责感受旋转和角加速度；还有椭圆囊和球囊，负责感受直线加速和重力方向。它们把头部运动变成神经信号，送到脑干和小脑。半规管内充满淋巴液，头部转动时液体因惯性推动毛细胞，身体静止时它也会慢慢回位。Spin around with your eyes closed, then stop: the room seems to keep turning. Read a phone in a moving car and the stomach turns over. Both experiences involve the vestibular system. The inner ear has three mutually perpendicular semicircular canals that sense rotation and angular acceleration, plus the utricle and saccule that sense linear acceleration and the direction of gravity. They convert head motion into neural signals for the brainstem and cerebellum.[1][7]

人判断“我在动”不只靠前庭，还靠眼睛和肌肉关节的本体感觉。三套信号一致时，平衡感稳定；信号冲突时，大脑会拼命整合，结果可能是眩晕、恶心、出汗。晕车就是典型冲突：前庭感到车在颠簸，眼睛却看到车内静止，脑干收到矛盾信息后触发保护性反应。大脑还会参考以往经验；长期在船上生活的人，上岸后仍会觉得地面在晃，这叫“上岸病”。We judge “I am moving” not only by the vestibular system but also by vision and proprioception from muscles and joints. When the three agree, balance feels stable; when they conflict, the brain struggles to integrate them and may produce vertigo, nausea and sweating. Motion sickness is a classic conflict: the vestibular system feels the car bumping while the eyes see a still interior; the brainstem receives contradictory information and triggers a protective response.[7]

眩晕和头晕不是一回事。眩晕是“明明没转，却觉得自己在转”的运动错觉，常来自前庭；头晕则更模糊，可能是低血压、贫血、焦虑或药物。良性阵发性位置性眩晕（耳石症）在翻身、抬头时诱发短暂旋转；前庭神经炎常有持续数天的剧烈眩晕；梅尼埃病还会伴波动性听力下降和耳闷。眼震的方向和类型能帮助判断是哪一侧前庭出了问题，医生会用视频眼震图记录。Vertigo is not the same as dizziness. Vertigo is the false sense that “I am spinning” when nothing is; dizziness is vaguer and may come from low blood pressure, anaemia, anxiety or drugs. Benign paroxysmal positional vertigo (BPPV) causes brief spinning when turning in bed or looking up; vestibular neuritis causes severe vertigo lasting days; Ménière’s disease also brings fluctuating hearing loss and fullness.[1][7]

处理取决于原因。耳石症靠复位手法，不是靠长期吃药；前庭神经炎急性期需要止晕和补液，之后尽早做前庭康复训练；梅尼埃病要限制盐分、利尿和倍他司汀等，按专科方案。晕车可坐前排、看远处地平线、避免低头看手机；必要时提前用晕车药，但不要自行叠加。复位后可能短期仍有头晕，通常几天到两周逐渐缓解；反复发作要复查。Treatment depends on the cause. BPPV is treated with repositioning manoeuvres, not long-term medication; acute vestibular neuritis needs symptom control and fluids, followed by vestibular rehabilitation; Ménière’s disease is managed with salt restriction, diuretics and betahistine under specialist care. For motion sickness, sit in front, look at the distant horizon and avoid looking down at a phone; prophylactic medication can help, but do not stack several products.[3][7]

边界：不是所有头晕都是耳朵的问题。突发眩晕伴说话不清、肢体无力、视物重影，可能是中枢卒中，要立即就医；持续耳鸣伴单侧听力下降也要查。前庭康复不是“多转圈”，而是按眼动、头动和平衡任务分级训练。判断来源，要靠病史、眼震检查和听力学，而不是一句“晕车体质”。The boundary: not all dizziness comes from the ear. Sudden vertigo with slurred speech, limb weakness or double vision may be a central stroke and needs emergency care; persistent tinnitus with unilateral hearing loss also needs investigation. Vestibular rehabilitation is not “spinning more”, but graded eye, head and balance training. Source diagnosis rests on history, nystagmus and audiology, not the label “car-sick constitution”.[1][7]

05助听器和人工耳蜗：怎样把声音重新送回大脑？Hearing aids and cochlear implants: how can sound be sent back to the brain?

助听器放大声音，人工耳蜗绕过毛细胞直接刺激听神经。Hearing aids amplify sound; cochlear implants bypass hair cells and stimulate the auditory nerve directly.

助听器和人工耳蜗解决的是不同环节的问题。助听器把外界声音放大、压缩和降噪，再送到耳道，适合外耳、中耳或轻中度感音神经性听力损失；人工耳蜗绕过受损的毛细胞，把声音变成电脉冲，直接刺激耳蜗内的听神经，适合重度到极重度感音神经性聋、助听器帮助有限的人。助听器适合的听力损失范围通常到重度，但也要看言语识别；人工耳蜗则用于助听器无法提供足够言语信息的重度以上患者。Hearing aids and cochlear implants solve problems at different points in the pathway. A hearing aid amplifies, compresses and denoises external sound and sends it into the ear canal; it suits outer- or middle-ear loss and mild-to-moderate sensorineural loss. A cochlear implant bypasses damaged hair cells, converts sound into electrical pulses and stimulates the auditory nerve directly; it suits severe-to-profound sensorineural loss when hearing aids provide limited benefit.[2][5]

助听器不是“随便买个小喇叭”。要先做听力图和言语识别测试，选配时要匹配耳道形状、增益曲线和最大输出；双耳佩戴能改善声源定位和嘈杂环境下的言语理解。数字助听器能做方向性麦克风、反馈抑制和蓝牙连接，但仍需要验配和随访，不是开箱即用。验配师会做真耳耦合腔测量，确认实际鼓膜处的增益；儿童需要更频繁复查，因为耳道在长大。A hearing aid is not “a small loudspeaker you buy off the shelf”. Audiometry and speech testing come first; fitting must match the ear canal, gain curve and maximum output. Bilateral fitting improves localization and speech understanding in noise. Digital aids offer directional microphones, feedback suppression and Bluetooth, but they still need fitting and follow-up; they are not plug-and-play.[5]

人工耳蜗由体外和体内两部分组成：麦克风、言语处理器和发射线圈在体外，接收器、刺激器和电极阵列在体内。电极阵列植入耳蜗，沿基底膜分布，按不同位置给予不同频率的电刺激。术后听到的声音不是立刻“正常”，需要开机调试和听觉康复训练，把电信号重新映射成语言。电极数目不是越多越好，覆盖范围和刺激策略更关键；术后调机要平衡响度、舒适度和言语清晰度。A cochlear implant has external and internal parts: microphone, speech processor and transmitter coil outside; receiver, stimulator and electrode array inside. The array is placed in the cochlea along the basilar membrane, and different electrodes stimulate different frequency regions. The sound after surgery is not immediately “normal”; switch-on, mapping and auditory rehabilitation are needed to turn electrical patterns into speech.[6]

人工耳蜗有严格的适应证：重度以上感音神经性聋、助听器效果很差、言语识别得分低，还要评估听神经和影像结构。不是所有耳聋都适合，也不是越贵越好。术后效果受植入年龄、耳聋时长、残余听力和康复训练影响，儿童越早干预通常语言发育越好。成人语后聋患者通常比先天聋成人更容易适应，因为大脑已有语言经验；儿童则需要家庭参与康复。Candidacy is strict: severe-to-profound sensorineural loss, poor benefit from hearing aids, low speech-recognition scores, and assessment of the auditory nerve and temporal bone anatomy. Not every deafness is suitable, and the most expensive device is not automatically the best. Outcomes depend on age at implantation, duration of deafness, residual hearing and rehabilitation; early intervention in children usually supports better language development.[2][6]

边界：助听器不能修复毛细胞，人工耳蜗也不能恢复自然听觉；两者都是“补偿”，不是“治愈”。保护残余听力、避免强噪声、定期调试和康复训练，决定最终效果。对突发性耳聋，尽快就医比先买助听器更重要。人工耳蜗不能替代保护残余听力；中耳炎、影像异常或听神经缺失会影响适应证。The boundary: hearing aids cannot repair hair cells, and cochlear implants do not restore natural hearing; both are compensation, not cure. Preserving residual hearing, avoiding loud noise, regular mapping and rehabilitation determine the final result. For sudden hearing loss, prompt medical care matters more than buying a hearing aid first.[5][6]

06耳朵为什么越掏越痒？Why does cleaning your ears make them itch more?

掏挖破坏耳道皮肤屏障，致痒介质释放，形成痒—抓—更痒的循环。Digging breaks the canal barrier, pruritogens are released, and a scratch-itch cycle takes over.

耳道痒起来，棉签伸进去转两圈，舒服一两分钟，随后更痒——这是门诊里最常见的一类“自造痒”。外耳道只有一层很薄的皮肤，厚度大约 0.2 毫米，直接贴在软骨和骨面上，皮下几乎没有脂肪缓冲，却密布着毛囊、耵聍腺与皮脂腺，也密布着对触压、温度和化学刺激敏感的感觉神经末梢。任何伸进耳道的硬物，都会同时刺激这几套结构，痒感因此很容易被“点着”。An itchy ear canal invites a cotton swab, and the relief lasts about a minute before the itch returns stronger. The outer canal is lined by skin only about 0.2 mm thick, lying directly on cartilage and bone with almost no fat cushion, yet it is packed with hair follicles, cerumen glands, sebaceous glands and dense sensory nerve endings sensitive to pressure, temperature and chemicals. Anything pushed into the canal stimulates all of them at once, which is why the itch is so easy to trigger.[1]

正常情况下，耳道有一套不需要人插手的自洁机制。耵聍由耵聍腺分泌，混着皮脂、脱落的上皮细胞和灰尘，铺成一层弱酸性薄膜，pH 大约在 5.0 到 5.7 之间，能防水、抑菌，也能粘住小虫和碎屑。颞下颌关节的咀嚼、说话动作会让耳道皮肤缓慢向外迁移，速度大约每天十分之一毫米，像一条很慢的传送带，把耵聍送到耳道口自然脱落。多数人一辈子都不需要主动清理耳道。Left alone, the canal cleans itself. Cerumen from the cerumen glands mixes with sebum, shed keratinocytes and dust into a weakly acidic film, roughly pH 5.0 to 5.7, that repels water, suppresses bacteria and traps debris. Chewing and speaking move the jaw and shift the canal skin slowly outward, about 0.1 mm per day, like a very slow conveyor belt that carries cerumen to the opening, where it flakes away. Most people never need to clean their ears at all.[1][5]

越掏越痒，是因为掏挖本身变成新的刺激源。棉签或耳勺反复摩擦，先造成角质层的微小破损；屏障一破，感觉神经末梢就暴露在空气、汗液和洗护用品里，肥大细胞与角质细胞释放组胺、IL-31 等致痒介质，痒感被放大；抓挠又带来新的机械损伤，于是形成“痒—抓—更痒”的循环。长期掏挖还会诱发外耳道皮炎；如果耳道经常进水、湿度高，曲霉、念珠菌等真菌容易定植，形成耳道真菌病，表现为持续发痒、闷堵和少量分泌物。Scratching makes the itch worse because the cleaning itself becomes the irritant. A cotton swab or metal pick abrades the stratum corneum; once the barrier is broken, nerve endings are exposed to air, sweat and toiletries, and mast cells and keratinocytes release histamine, IL-31 and other pruritogens. The itch signal is amplified, scratching adds fresh damage, and a scratch-itch cycle establishes itself. Repeated digging can also produce canal dermatitis, and in a damp canal Aspergillus or Candida may colonise, causing otomycosis with persistent itching, fullness and light discharge.[1][5]

风险不在痒，而在“掏到什么”。棉签的直径接近耳道，会把耵聍向深处推挤压实，形成耵聍栓塞，传导性听力下降可达 20 到 30 分贝；更糟的情况是戳破鼓膜、脱位听小骨，或把棉絮留在耳道里。耳道皮肤一旦破损感染，疼痛通常比原来的痒更难忍。儿童耳道更窄、更怕突然转头，风险更高。The real risk is what the swab reaches. Its diameter is close to that of the canal, so it pushes cerumen deeper and packs it into an impaction that can cost 20 to 30 dB of conductive hearing; worse, it can perforate the eardrum, dislocate the ossicles or leave cotton fibres behind. Once the skin is broken and infected, the pain is usually far worse than the original itch. Children have narrower canals and are more likely to jerk their heads, so the risk is higher.[3][5]

边界在于：痒是症状，处理要看清原因。只在耵聍栓塞、耳道疼痛、听力下降或进水后持续闷堵时就医，由医生在耳内镜直视下用吸引器、冲洗或专用器械取出；不要用棉签、耳烛、发夹、钥匙。若只是轻度干燥发痒，减少清洁频率、避免刺激性洗护，必要时由医生短期使用保湿或弱效抗炎滴耳液。干性耵聍与湿性耵聍由基因决定，清理需求差别很大，别人的经验不能照搬。The boundary is that itch is a symptom, and treatment depends on its cause. Seek care when cerumen is impacted or there is pain, hearing loss or persistent fullness after water exposure; a clinician removes it under otoscopy with suction, irrigation or dedicated instruments. Avoid swabs, ear candles, hairpins and keys. For mild dryness, reduce cleaning and irritating products, and let a clinician decide about short courses of moisturising or mildly anti-inflammatory drops. Dry and wet cerumen types are genetically determined, so one person's cleaning routine is not a recommendation for another.[3][5]
