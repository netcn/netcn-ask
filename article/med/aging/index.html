皱纹：皮肤、果实与大地Wrinkles: Skin, Fruit, and Earth

皮肤失弹、果实失水、岩层受压：三种尺度上的“皱纹”看似无关，却都是薄层在支撑变弱后失稳屈曲。这个 Hub 用皮肤老化、苹果贮藏和地质褶皱三条线索，拆开 4 个问题，也说明类比在哪里成立、在哪里失效。Lost elasticity, lost water and compressed rock: wrinkles at three scales look unrelated, yet each is a thin layer buckling when its support weakens. This Hub follows skin aging, apple storage and geological folding through four questions, and marks where the analogy holds and where it fails.

01人老了为什么会长皱纹？Why do people develop wrinkles as they age?

皮肤变薄、胶原与弹性纤维流失、脂肪垫萎缩，表皮在表情肌反复折叠处塌陷；防晒最能减缓。Thinner skin, lost collagen and elastin, and shrinking fat pads let the surface collapse along expression lines; sun protection slows it most.

先给结论：皱纹不是皮肤表面多了几条线，而是皮肤的三层结构一起变了。最外层表皮变薄，中间真皮里的胶原蛋白和弹性纤维减少，最下面的皮下脂肪垫也在缩小；支撑变弱，表皮就在表情肌反复折叠的地方塌下去，形成抬头纹、鱼尾纹和法令纹。日晒、吸烟、体重快速下降和长期缺水会加速这套变化，但基因和个体差异同样重要。Start with the result: a wrinkle is not just a line drawn on the surface. All three layers change. The epidermis thins; the dermis loses collagen and elastin; the subcutaneous fat pad shrinks. When support weakens, the surface collapses along lines where expression muscles repeatedly fold it—forehead, crow's feet, nasolabial folds. Sun, smoking, rapid weight loss and dehydration speed this up, but genes and individual variation matter.[1][3]

皮肤像一张绷在软垫上的薄毯：真皮提供厚度和回弹，皮下脂肪提供体积，表皮只是最外面的覆盖层。年轻时胶原与弹性纤维让真皮像新床垫一样撑得住；随着年龄增长，胶原合成减少、弹性纤维断裂，真皮变薄，脂肪垫萎缩，表面那层“薄毯”就更容易在眉间、眼角和嘴角留下折痕。手背的深纹还多一层原因：这里的皮下脂肪本来就少，肌腱和静脉之间的空隙大，失去填充后纹路显得更深。Think of skin as a thin blanket stretched over a soft mattress: the dermis supplies thickness and recoil, fat supplies volume, and the epidermis is the cover. Young dermis holds collagen and elastin like a new mattress; with age collagen synthesis falls, elastin fragments, the dermis thins and fat shrinks, so the cover creases more easily. The back of the hand has less fat to begin with; gaps between tendons and veins make the lines look deeper.[1][3]

外因里最厉害的是紫外线。UVA 能穿进真皮，反复照射会让胶原纤维排列紊乱、弹性纤维变性，皮肤变得粗糙、松弛，这种“光老化”可能比自然年龄更早出现。吸烟会让皮肤微循环变差，尼古丁使血管收缩，长期下来肤色暗沉、皱纹加深。频繁皱眉、眯眼、抿嘴也不是“练出皱纹”，而是同一处皮肤被表情肌反复折叠，年深日久形成固定折痕。还有一个常见误会：泡澡后手指发皱是角质层吸水膨胀的暂时现象，和衰老性皱纹不是一回事。UV is the strongest external driver. UVA penetrates the dermis; repeated exposure disorganizes collagen and damages elastin, producing roughness, sagging and photoaging earlier than chronological age. Smoking narrows blood vessels and impairs microcirculation, leaving skin dull and lined. Repeated squinting, frowning or pursing is not 'exercise'—it folds the same skin thousands of times until the crease becomes fixed. One common confusion: pruney fingers after a bath are swollen stratum corneum, transient and unrelated to age wrinkles.[1][2]

能做的是什么？防晒是性价比最高的一步：广谱防晒、帽子和遮阳能减少新的光损伤；保湿剂能暂时把表皮“撑”起来，让细纹看起来浅一些，但不能重建真皮；维 A 醇/维 A 酸类和肽类可能帮助胶原生成，需要在医生指导下使用，也可能刺激皮肤。激光、化学剥脱和填充属于医疗美容，效果与风险都要个体评估。没有任何面霜能把已经断裂的弹性纤维“抹回去”。What helps? Sun protection is the best value: broad-spectrum sunscreen, hats and shade reduce new photodamage. Moisturizer temporarily plumps the epidermis and makes fine lines look shallower, but it cannot rebuild the dermis. Retinoids and peptides may support collagen and need medical guidance because they can irritate. Lasers, peels and fillers are medical procedures with individual benefits and risks. No cream can 'iron back' broken elastin.[2][3]

边界：皱纹本身不是病，但突然出现、只在一侧、伴随皮肤发红脱屑或体重下降的皮肤变化，值得看皮肤科。每个人的胶原流失速度、日晒史、表情习惯和减重经历都不同，同样的年龄可以有很不一样的皮肤；把皱纹简单归因于“老了”会漏掉可防可控的部分，把全部希望押在一瓶“抗皱霜”上也不现实。Boundary: wrinkles are not a disease, but a sudden, one-sided or rapidly changing skin lesion with redness, scaling or weight loss deserves a dermatologist. Collagen loss, sun history, expression habits and weight change differ from person to person, so people of the same age can look very different. Blaming everything on 'old age' misses what can be prevented; pinning everything on a jar of cream is equally unrealistic.[1][2]

02苹果放了一段时间后为什么会起皱纹Why does an apple wrinkle after being stored for a while?

苹果采后仍在呼吸、蒸腾失水，膨压下降使果皮起褶；低温高湿、气调与包装可减缓。Harvested apples keep respiring and losing water; falling turgor lets the skin fold. Cold, humid storage, controlled atmosphere and packaging slow it.

先给结论：苹果起皱纹，主要是它还在“活着”，而且一直在失水。采摘后的苹果并没有停止呼吸和蒸腾：果皮、果柄和萼洼都在向空气散失水分，细胞里的水分少了，膨压下降，原本绷紧的果皮就像泄了气的气球一样塌出褶皱。失水越多，皱纹越明显；这不是腐烂的同义词，也不等于苹果“老了”。Start with the result: an apple wrinkles mainly because it is still alive and still losing water. After harvest it continues to respire and transpire. Water leaves through the skin, stem and calyx; cell water drops, turgor falls, and the taut skin collapses into folds like a deflating balloon. More water loss means deeper wrinkles. This is not the same as rot, and it does not simply mean the apple is 'old'.[4]

苹果表面有一层蜡质角质层，能减慢水分散失，但不是完全密封。果皮上的气孔和皮孔、采果时的微小划伤、以及果柄和花萼处的开口，都是水汽的通道；温度越高、空气越干、风速越大，果内外的水汽压差越大，失水就越快。与此同时，苹果仍在呼吸，消耗糖和有机酸，释放二氧化碳、水和乙烯；乙烯会推动软化与成熟，让果肉细胞壁更容易失去结构。皱纹的直接原因是失水，软化则更多来自细胞壁酶和成熟过程，两者常同时发生，但不是同一件事。The skin has a waxy cuticle that slows water loss but does not seal it. Lenticels, stomata, tiny harvest scratches and openings at the stem and calyx are channels for water vapour. Warmer, drier, windier air increases the vapour-pressure difference and speeds loss. The apple also respires, burning sugars and acids and releasing carbon dioxide, water and ethylene; ethylene drives softening and ripening and makes cell walls lose structure. Wrinkling is mainly water loss; softening is mainly cell-wall enzymes and ripening—often together, but not the same process.[4][5]

商业贮藏就是围绕这条水路做文章：低温降低呼吸，90%–95% 的相对湿度减小水汽压差，控制气氛（低氧、较高二氧化碳）再放慢代谢；气调库、包装和涂膜都是为了把失水压到最低。研究里，苹果失重达到 4%–7.5% 时就可能出现可见皱缩，具体阈值因品种而异；在 20℃、30% 相对湿度的货架环境里，失水会比 95% 湿度快得多。家里把苹果裸放在干燥冰箱，等于给它一个“加速失水”的小环境。Commercial storage works on this water path: low temperature slows respiration; 90–95% relative humidity reduces the vapour-pressure gap; controlled atmosphere (low oxygen, higher carbon dioxide) slows metabolism. CA rooms, packaging and coatings all minimize water loss. In studies, visible shrivelling can appear at 4–7.5% mass loss, depending on cultivar; at 20°C and 30% RH, water loss is far faster than at 95% RH. A bare apple in a dry fridge is in a water-loss accelerator.[4][6]

已经起皱的苹果还能不能吃？如果只是失水，没有霉斑、没有酒味、没有软烂渗液，通常仍然可以吃，只是口感偏绵、甜味和香气可能变化。泡水或喷水不能让细胞“重新鼓起来”：失去的水分补不回去，细胞壁和膜的结构已经改变，泡久了还容易给霉菌创造机会。想延缓皱纹，买回来别洗、用透气纸袋或保鲜袋松松地包住，放冰箱冷藏；切开后则要密封并尽快吃完。Can a wrinkled apple be eaten? If it is only water loss—no mould, no alcoholic smell, no soft leaking—it is usually still edible, though mealier and less aromatic. Soaking or misting cannot 'reinflate' the cells: the water is gone and cell walls and membranes have changed; prolonged moisture also invites mould. To slow wrinkling, do not wash before storage; keep apples in a perforated plastic bag or loosely covered container in the fridge; once cut, seal and eat soon.[5][6]

边界：不是所有苹果皱纹都只是失水。冷害、二氧化碳伤害、真菌腐烂、虫伤和机械碰伤也会让果皮起皱或凹陷；如果皱缩伴随褐色斑块、霉层或异味，就不要吃。不同品种的耐贮性差别很大，同一批苹果的失水速度也不一样。把“皱纹”当成唯一的新鲜度指标会误导：有的苹果先皱后坏，有的已经变质却仍然光滑。Boundary: not every apple wrinkle is simple water loss. Chilling injury, carbon-dioxide injury, fungal rot, insect damage and bruising can also pucker or dent the skin. If wrinkling comes with brown patches, mould or off-odours, discard it. Cultivars differ greatly in storability, and even one batch loses water at different rates. Wrinkles alone are a poor freshness test: some apples wrinkle before they rot, and some spoiled apples still look smooth.[4][5]

03地球的皱纹是什么What are the Earth's wrinkles?

岩层在挤压下塑性弯曲成背斜与向斜；山脉集中在板块边界，不是地球冷却收缩的随机皱纹。Rock layers bend into anticlines and synclines under compression; mountains concentrate at plate boundaries, not random wrinkles from a cooling Earth.

先给结论：“地球的皱纹”是岩层的褶皱，不是地球像苹果一样冷却缩水留下的表皮褶子。岩层在漫长的地质时间里受到挤压，温度、围压和岩石性质合适时，会像面团一样缓慢弯曲，形成背斜和向斜；如果岩石太脆或挤压太快，则更容易断裂成断层。今天看到的山脉，多数是板块碰撞或俯冲的产物，皱纹只是这场大挤压留在岩石里的形状。Start with the result: 'the Earth's wrinkles' are folds in rock layers, not the skin of a cooling, shrinking planet. Under compression over geological time, and at temperatures and pressures where rock can bend rather than snap, layers fold into anticlines and synclines. If the rock is too brittle or the strain too fast, it faults instead. Most mountain ranges we see are products of plate collision or subduction; the wrinkles are the shape left in the rock by that squeeze.[7]

褶皱的几何可以很简单：岩层向上拱起叫背斜，向下凹陷叫向斜，两侧倾斜的部分叫翼部；背斜的核心通常是最老的岩层，向斜的核心则相反。把一层层沉积岩想象成地毯：从两头往中间推，地毯会在中间拱起和凹陷，形成一组连续的褶皱。区别在于，地毯几秒钟就能折出来，岩层要在数千万年到数亿年里、在地下几公里到几十公里的高温高压中慢慢变形；等抬升和剥蚀把盖子掀掉，我们才在崖壁上看到这些弯曲。The geometry is simple: an upward arch is an anticline, a downward trough a syncline, and the sloping sides are limbs; the core of an anticline usually holds the oldest layers, the core of a syncline the youngest. Picture a carpet pushed from both ends: it arches and dips in a series of folds. The difference is time: a carpet folds in seconds, while rock folds over millions to hundreds of millions of years, kilometres down, and is later uplifted and stripped by erosion so we can see the bends in a cliff.[7]

实例并不神秘。喜马拉雅山脉来自印度板块与欧亚大陆的碰撞，两者至今仍以约 40–50 毫米/年的相对速度汇聚，北向的挤压让地壳增厚、山脉抬升；阿尔卑斯、扎格罗斯和阿巴拉契亚也各有自己的碰撞或俯冲历史。挪威 Lahko 国家公园的褶皱岩层、英国 Cornwall 的 chevron 褶皱，则是海西造山带挤压留下的更小尺度样本。褶皱可以在手标本上，也可以在几十公里尺度上；尺度不同，物理机制相同：层状材料在挤压下失稳、弯曲。Examples are not mysterious. The Himalaya comes from the collision of India with Eurasia; the two still converge at about 40–50 mm/yr, thickening the crust and lifting the range. The Alps, Zagros and Appalachians have their own collision or subduction histories. The folded layers of Lahko National Park in Norway and the chevron folds of Cornwall are smaller-scale samples left by ancient compression. Folds range from hand specimens to tens of kilometres, but the physics is the same: layered material becomes unstable under compression and buckles.[7][8]

十九世纪七十年代，James Nasmyth 曾把一只皱手和一只干苹果并置，作为“地球冷却收缩、表皮褶皱成山”的类比。这张图今天仍有教学价值，但它的假说已经被板块构造取代：山脉不是全球均匀收缩挤出来的随机皱纹，而是集中在板块边界，伴随俯冲、碰撞、地壳增厚和火山作用；大洋中脊还在不断生成新洋壳，地球并没有整体冷缩到能把地表挤成这种褶皱。用苹果类比可以启发直觉，但不能当成证据。In the 1870s James Nasmyth placed a wrinkled hand beside a shrivelled apple as an analogy for mountain ranges produced by shrinkage of the globe. The image is still useful for teaching, but the hypothesis has been replaced by plate tectonics: mountains are not random wrinkles from uniform global cooling. They concentrate at plate boundaries, with subduction, collision, crustal thickening and volcanism; mid-ocean ridges keep making new oceanic crust, and Earth has not contracted enough to squeeze the surface into these folds. The apple analogy can train intuition, not prove a mechanism.[9][10]

边界：并非所有褶皱都由水平挤压造成。重力滑动、盐丘上拱、火山塌陷和撞击坑也会让岩层弯曲；有些褶皱是早期构造被后期叠加改造的结果，读起来像一团被揉过又展开的纸。把“地球的皱纹”说成和皮肤皱纹同一种东西，是修辞，不是同一套机制：皮肤靠失水、失弹和基底支撑变化，岩层靠应力、温度和时间的塑性变形。类比能帮我们看见尺度，但不能替我们做地质结论。Boundary: not every fold is caused by horizontal compression. Gravity sliding, salt domes, volcanic collapse and impact craters can bend layers too; some folds are earlier structures overprinted by later deformation, like paper crumpled and partly reopened. Calling Earth's folds 'the same' as skin wrinkles is rhetoric, not identity: skin wrinkles involve water loss, lost elasticity and support; rock folds involve stress, temperature and time. The analogy can show scale, but it cannot make the geological conclusion for us.[7]
