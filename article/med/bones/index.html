01运动后为什么会酸痛？DOMS 与肌肉拉伤不是一回事Why do muscles ache after exercise—and when is it a strain rather than DOMS?

DOMS 多在运动后 12–24 小时出现，24–72 小时最重，5–7 天消退；拉伤是即刻撕裂，恢复以周计。DOMS usually starts 12–24 hours after exercise, peaks at 24–72 hours and fades by 5–7 days; a strain is an immediate tear and takes weeks to heal.

运动后第二天酸得下不了楼，常被归咎于“乳酸堆积”，但乳酸在运动结束后几小时内就被代谢掉，不会隔夜才痛。这种延迟出现的酸痛叫延迟性肌肉酸痛（DOMS），多在从未习惯的离心运动后 12–24 小时出现，24–72 小时达到高峰，5–7 天逐渐消退。它的强度与肌肉损伤指标并不完全同步，因此“越酸说明练得越有效”也不成立。The ache two days after exercise is often blamed on “lactic acid”, but lactate is cleared by the liver, heart and muscle within tens of minutes to a few hours; it does not wait overnight to hurt. The delayed ache is delayed-onset muscle soreness (DOMS): it usually appears 12–24 hours after unaccustomed eccentric exercise, peaks at 24–72 hours, and fades over 5–7 days. Its intensity correlates poorly with markers of muscle damage, so “the more it hurts, the better the session” is not a rule.[1][2]

离心收缩是肌肉在受力同时被拉长，例如下坡跑、深蹲下蹲、冲刺后减速。此时肌纤维和细胞外基质承受较高机械张力，较弱的肌节先发生微撕裂，钙离子释放并激活蛋白酶，进一步降解结构蛋白。随后中性粒细胞在 4–6 小时内进入，巨噬细胞在 1–14 天内跟进，释放 IL-1β、IL-6、TNF-α 等细胞因子，清除碎片并启动修复。这是无菌性炎症，不是感染，也不等于“把肌肉练坏了”。Eccentric contraction means the muscle is lengthened while producing force—downhill running, the lowering phase of a squat, decelerating after a sprint. The fibres and extracellular matrix carry high mechanical tension; weaker sarcomeres micro-tear first, calcium leaks from disrupted sarcoplasmic reticulum and stretch-sensitive channels, and proteases degrade structural proteins. Neutrophils arrive within 4–6 hours; macrophages follow over 1–14 days, releasing IL-1β, IL-6 and TNF-α, clearing debris and starting repair. This is aseptic inflammation, not infection, and not proof that the muscle has been “broken”.[1][2]

酸痛的时间表相当一致：12–24 小时起，24–72 小时最重，5–7 天退；但力量恢复常比酸痛更慢，酸痛消失并不代表可以立刻回到最大强度。一个有用的现象是“重复效应”：第一次离心运动后，数周甚至数月内再做相似动作，酸痛、肌酸激酶升高和力量下降都会减轻。渐进增加离心负荷，比一次“练到废”更能建立这种保护。恢复期做轻量活动、保证睡眠和蛋白质摄入，通常比完全不动更舒服；止痛药能缓解感受，但不能证明组织已修复。The timeline is fairly consistent: onset at 12–24 hours, worst at 24–72 hours, gone by 5–7 days. Strength often recovers more slowly than soreness, so feeling better does not mean being ready for a maximal session. The repeated-bout effect is useful: after a first eccentric bout, a similar bout within weeks or months produces less soreness, a smaller rise in creatine kinase and less strength loss. Progressing eccentric load builds this protection better than one “destroy myself” session. Light activity, sleep and adequate protein usually feel better than complete rest; analgesics can ease the feeling, but “no pain” is not proof of repaired tissue.[1]

肌肉拉伤则是另一条线：肌肉在过度拉伸或突然发力时被拉伤甚至撕裂，常发生在腘绳肌、小腿和腰背。它通常是即刻的尖锐痛，可伴“啪”的一声、肿胀、瘀斑和明显无力；按严重程度分三度，轻者少量肌纤维受累，重者完全断裂。多数拉伤在 2–6 周内好转，愈合过程常需 6–8 周，严重者数月。前 2–3 天按 PRICE 保护、休息、冰敷、加压、抬高，之后逐步负重活动。A muscle strain is a different event: the muscle is overstretched or torn during a sudden effort, most often in the hamstrings, calf or lower back. It usually causes immediate sharp pain, sometimes a “pop”, swelling, bruising and clear weakness; strains are graded 1–3, from a few fibres to a complete tear. Most improve within 2–6 weeks, healing commonly takes 6–8 weeks, and severe cases take months. For the first 2–3 days use PRICE—protection, rest, ice, compression, elevation—then load gradually within a pain-free range; prolonged complete rest is usually not helpful.[8][9][10]

两者最关键的区别在起始和外观：DOMS 是延迟的钝痛、按压痛，多数没有明显肿胀瘀斑，力量只轻度下降；拉伤是即刻的尖锐痛，常有肿胀、瘀斑和功能性无力。若出现畸形、麻木、完全不能负重或疼痛加重，应按急症处理。DOMS 也会在极端情况下伴随酱油色尿、剧烈肿胀和明显无力，这同样需要就医。把酸痛当“勋章”、带痛上强度，或把拉伤当酸痛硬扛，都会把一次可恢复的负荷变成更长的停训。The practical distinction is onset and appearance: DOMS is a delayed, dull, pressure-sensitive ache, usually without major swelling or bruising, and strength drops only a little. A strain is immediate, sharp, often with swelling, bruising and functional weakness. Deformity, numbness, inability to bear weight, worsening pain or rapidly expanding swelling or bruising needs urgent assessment. Rarely, DOMS can come with dark urine, extreme swelling and marked weakness, which also needs medical care. Treating soreness as a badge and training through it, or treating a strain as ordinary soreness, turns a recoverable load into a much longer layoff.[1][8][10]

02慢性疼痛里的「中枢敏化」：不是想出来的痛How does central sensitization produce chronic pain?

持续伤害信号让脊髓背角阈值下降、抑制失灵、胶质与下行易化放大疼痛；轻触也可能痛。Sustained nociceptive signals lower dorsal-horn thresholds, weaken inhibition and let glia and descending facilitation amplify pain; even light touch can hurt.

中枢敏化不是“想出来的痛”，而是中枢神经系统的一种可塑性改变。IASP 的定义是：中枢内伤害感受神经元对正常或阈下的传入反应增强。同样的刺激会被放大成明显疼痛；轻触、温度变化甚至轻轻按压也可能触发疼痛。它常见于纤维肌痛、慢性广泛性疼痛、部分神经病理性疼痛和慢性下背痛，是慢性疼痛“从症状变成疾病”的关键机制之一。Central sensitization is not “pain invented by the mind”; it is a plastic change in the central nervous system. The IASP definition is increased responsiveness of nociceptive neurons in the CNS to their normal or subthreshold afferent input. In practice, a stimulus that once caused mild discomfort is amplified into clear pain; light touch, temperature change or gentle pressure can trigger pain. It appears in fibromyalgia, chronic widespread pain, some neuropathic pain and chronic low back pain, and is one of the mechanisms by which chronic pain becomes a disease in its own right.[4][11]

起点常在外周：持续、反复或强烈的伤害信号让 C 纤维在脊髓背角释放过量谷氨酸、物质 P 和 CGRP。谷氨酸结合 NMDA 受体后，镁离子阻断被移开，钙离子大量内流，激活 PKC、CaMKII 和 MAPK 等激酶。结果是 AMPA 受体更多上膜、NMDA 受体更敏感，二级神经元的激活阈值下降、感受野扩大；本来只传导触觉的低阈值 Aβ 纤维，也可能“挤进”疼痛回路。临床上就表现为痛觉过敏和触诱发痛。It often starts in the periphery: sustained, repeated or intense nociceptive signals make C fibres release excessive glutamate, substance P and CGRP in the dorsal horn. Glutamate opens NMDA receptors after the magnesium block is removed; calcium floods in and activates PKC, CaMKII and MAPK. More AMPA receptors reach the membrane, NMDA receptors become more responsive, the threshold of second-order neurons falls and their receptive fields expand. Low-threshold Aβ fibres, which normally carry touch, can now drive pain circuits. Clinically this shows up as hyperalgesia and allodynia.[3][4]

放大器不止一个。持续兴奋使 KCC2 下调，GABA/甘氨酸抑制减弱，相当于“刹车”失灵；小胶质细胞和星形胶质细胞被激活后释放 IL-1β、TNF-α、BDNF 等，进一步增强 NMDA 受体、促进突触长时程增强，形成自持闭环。脑干下行调控偏向易化：去甲肾上腺素和 5-HT 抑制减弱，CCK 等易化信号增强。疼痛因此不再需要外周伤害持续存在，也能自己维持。There is more than one amplifier. Sustained excitation downregulates KCC2 and weakens GABAergic and glycinergic inhibition—the “brakes” fail. Activated microglia and astrocytes release IL-1β, TNF-α and BDNF, further enhancing NMDA receptors and long-term potentiation, creating a loop in which neural activity drives glia and glia amplify neural activity. Descending control also shifts toward facilitation: inhibitory noradrenergic and serotonergic pathways lose efficacy, while facilitatory signals such as CCK increase and can oppose opioid-like analgesia. Pain can then persist without a continuing peripheral injury.[3][4]

临床上，中枢敏化的痛常常“范围比损伤大、程度比损伤重”：弥散性疼痛、烧灼感、怕风怕碰、睡眠差、疲劳和情绪困扰常一起出现。它不是单一疾病，也不能靠某个化验确诊；医生通常结合病史、分布、诱发因素和神经系统检查判断。治疗不靠“忍”或单纯加止痛药，而是处理可纠正的外周驱动、渐进活动与力量训练、睡眠和压力管理，必要时用神经病理性疼痛药物或神经调控。心理因素会调节疼痛，但调节不等于虚构。Clinically, central sensitization often produces pain that is wider and more intense than the tissue injury would suggest: widespread pain, burning, sensitivity to wind or touch, poor sleep, fatigue and distress cluster together. It is not a single disease or a label that one blood test confirms; clinicians use history, distribution, triggers and neurological examination. Treatment is not “just endure it” or simply more analgesics. It addresses reversible peripheral drivers, graded activity and strength training, sleep and stress, and when appropriate medications for neuropathic pain or neuromodulation. Psychological factors modulate pain, but modulation is not fabrication.[3][4][11]

边界要划清：中枢敏化是机制，不是“所有慢性疼痛都是它”，也不是“疼痛全在脑子里”。伤害性、神经病理性和伤害可塑性疼痛可以并存，肿瘤、感染、骨折、风湿免疫病等仍要排除。把中枢敏化讲清楚，是为了减少“检查没大问题就是你太敏感”的指责，也避免把真实的结构病变漏掉。对患者最有用的一句话是：疼痛真实存在，但疼痛信号可以被重新训练；目标不是立刻归零，而是逐步恢复功能。The boundary matters: central sensitization is a mechanism, not “all chronic pain is central”, and not “pain is all in the brain”. Nociceptive, neuropathic and nociplastic pain can coexist; tumours, infection, fractures and rheumatic disease still need to be excluded. Explaining central sensitization should reduce the accusation that “your tests are normal, so you are just sensitive”, and also prevent missing real structural disease. The useful message for patients is: the pain is real, but pain signalling can be retrained; the goal is not immediate zero, but gradual return of function.[3][4]

03睡前剧烈运动不好吗？脂肪又去了哪里？Does vigorous exercise before bed harm sleep—and where does fat go when you lose weight?

晚近高强度可能推迟入睡；脂肪主要氧化成 CO₂ 和水，84% 经肺呼出，16% 成水。Late high-intensity exercise can delay sleep; fat is oxidised to CO₂ and water, with about 84% exhaled by the lungs and 16% becoming water.

“睡前运动不好”不是一条绝对规则，关键在强度、结束时间和个体差异。Oda 等让 12 名活跃年轻男性在 21:20–22:00 运动 40 分钟，高强度组（80% 心率储备）在睡前出现明显生理兴奋：入睡潜伏期延长 14.0 分钟，心率比对照高 25.7 次/分，心率变异性高频功率下降；中等强度组没有同样效应。也就是说，问题更常出在“太晚、太猛”，而不是“晚上动了动”。“Vigorous exercise before bed is bad” is not an absolute rule; intensity, timing and the individual matter. Oda and Shirakawa had 12 active young men exercise for 40 minutes from 21:20 to 22:00. The high-intensity group (80% heart-rate reserve) was physiologically aroused at bedtime: sleep onset was delayed by 14.0 minutes, heart rate was 25.7 bpm above the control condition, and high-frequency heart-rate variability fell. The moderate-intensity group did not show the same effect. The problem is usually “too late and too hard”, not “moving in the evening” per se.[6]

Leota 等 2025 年的可穿戴研究纳入 14,689 名身体活动者、4,084,354 个夜晚，运动越晚、负荷越高，越容易出现入睡延迟、睡眠缩短、睡眠质量下降、夜间静息心率升高和心率变异性降低。研究也给出一个实用分界：无论负荷高低，运动在入睡前至少 4 小时结束，与睡眠变化没有显著关联。这是观察性数据，不能证明因果，个体差异也很大；容易失眠的人可把高强度训练提前，结束后留出降温放松时间。Larger data come from Leota et al. (2025), using wearables from 14,689 physically active people over 4,084,354 person-nights. Later exercise and higher strain were associated with delayed sleep onset, shorter sleep, lower sleep quality, higher nocturnal resting heart rate and lower heart-rate variability. The study also offers a practical boundary: regardless of strain, bouts ending at least 4 hours before sleep onset were not associated with sleep changes. This is observational and cannot prove causation, and individual variation is large. For people prone to insomnia, moving hard training earlier and leaving time to cool down and relax is more sensible than banning evening exercise altogether.[7]

脂肪去向是另一套常被误解的账。脂肪以甘油三酯储存在脂肪细胞里；能量缺口出现时，脂肪被分解成脂肪酸，进入线粒体氧化。碳原子最终变成二氧化碳，氢原子变成水，能量以 ATP 和热的形式释放。按平均甘油三酯 C55H104O6 计算，完全氧化需要 78 个 O2，生成 55 个 CO2 和 52 个 H2O；按质量算，约 84% 的脂肪质量经肺以 CO2 呼出，16% 变成水，肺才是脂肪的主要排泄器官。Fat loss is another ledger full of misconceptions. Fat is stored as triglyceride in fat cells; when an energy deficit occurs, it is broken down into fatty acids and oxidised in mitochondria. Carbon atoms leave as carbon dioxide, hydrogen becomes water, and energy is released as ATP and heat. For an average triglyceride, C55H104O6, complete oxidation requires 78 O2 and yields 55 CO2 and 52 H2O. By mass, about 84% of the fat leaves the body through the lungs as CO2 and 16% becomes water; the lungs are the primary excretory organ.[5]

以 10 kg 脂肪为例：完全氧化需要约 29 kg 氧气，产生约 28 kg 二氧化碳和 11 kg 水；其中 8.4 kg 是来自脂肪的 CO2，1.6 kg 成为水。这也说明“出汗排毒”“脂肪变肌肉”“脂肪随粪便排掉”都不准确：汗液主要调节体温，脂肪细胞缩小而非变成肌肉，粪便主要是未吸收的食物残渣。真正决定减脂的是持续的能量缺口，运动增加消耗并帮助保留肌肉，但不能指定只减某个部位。Take 10 kg of fat: complete oxidation needs about 29 kg of oxygen and produces about 28 kg of carbon dioxide and 11 kg of water; 8.4 kg is exhaled as CO2 and 1.6 kg becomes water. This is why “sweat out toxins”, “fat turns into muscle” and “fat leaves in stools” are inaccurate: sweat mainly regulates temperature, fat cells shrink rather than become muscle, and stool is mainly unabsorbed food residue and metabolites, not the bulk of fat. A sustained energy deficit drives fat loss; exercise adds expenditure and helps preserve muscle, but it cannot choose a specific spot.[5]

睡前运动与减脂的关系也更清楚：运动时间影响睡眠，睡眠又影响食欲、胰岛素敏感性和第二天愿不愿意动；但一次晚练不会毁掉减脂，一顿“补偿性加餐”却可能抵消缺口。高强度训练尽量安排在睡前 4 小时以上；只能晚练时降低强度、缩短时长、结束后降温并调暗灯光；减脂以温和缺口、足够蛋白质和力量训练为主。若出现不明原因体重下降、明显水肿或持续疲劳，应先就医，而不是继续加码。Putting the two together: exercise timing affects sleep, and sleep affects appetite, insulin sensitivity and whether you move the next day. One late session will not ruin fat loss, but a “compensatory” extra meal can erase the deficit. Practical steps: schedule high-intensity training at least 4 hours before bed; if evening is the only option, reduce intensity and duration, take a warm shower and dim the lights afterwards; aim for a moderate deficit, adequate protein and strength training. Unexplained weight loss, marked oedema or persistent fatigue should be medically assessed rather than met with more training.[5][6][7]

04为什么肌肉会自己跳动？Why do muscles twitch on their own?

良性肌束颤动是一个运动单位自发放电带动一束肌纤维抽动；疲劳、咖啡因、压力、缺睡和电解质紊乱常见。A benign fasciculation is one motor unit firing spontaneously and pulling a fibre bundle; fatigue, caffeine, stress, poor sleep and electrolyte disturbance are common triggers.

偶尔看到皮肤下某一小块肌肉自己跳几下，医学上叫肌束颤动（fasciculation）。它是一个运动神经元所支配的那一束肌纤维自发、短暂地收缩，牵动皮肤出现细小动作；范围通常局限在一束肌纤维，不是整块肌肉持续强直抽搐，不是癫痫样发作，也不是关节在动。它和抽筋不同：抽筋是整组肌肉持续强直收缩并伴明显疼痛，束颤只是短暂的局部跳动。A small patch of muscle jumping under the skin now and then is called a fasciculation. It is the spontaneous, brief contraction of the bundle of fibres served by one motor neuron, pulling the skin into a fine movement. The range is usually one fibre bundle, not a sustained tetanic spasm of a whole muscle, not an epileptic seizure, and it is not a joint moving.[1][12]

颤动发生在神经肌肉接头之前的发放环节。前角运动神经元或其轴突兴奋性增高，出现一次自发放电，冲动沿轴突传到其所支配的肌纤维束，整束一起短暂收缩；同一运动单位的肌纤维成组活动，所以肉眼看到的是一小片跳动，而不是单根肌纤维在皮肤表面抽动；运动单位越大、支配的肌纤维越多，跳动范围也越容易被肉眼看到。The twitch arises at the firing side before the neuromuscular junction. When an anterior horn motor neuron or its axon becomes hyperexcitable, it discharges spontaneously; the impulse travels down the axon to its fibre bundle, which contracts briefly as a group. Because fibres of one motor unit fire together, the eye sees a small patch twitch rather than a single fibre moving at the skin surface.[12]

健康人出现良性束颤很常见，诱因多为疲劳、剧烈运动、咖啡因或尼古丁、压力与焦虑、睡眠不足、脱水以及钠、钾、钙、镁等电解质紊乱。综述估计约 70% 的健康人一生中至少经历过一次良性肌束颤动；这类跳动常游走在眼皮、小腿、大腿或手臂，时有时无，单次持续时间短；这类生理性跳动不影响肌肉力量，查体和化验通常没有异常发现，休息、补水和改善睡眠后，多数人在几小时到一两天内减轻。Benign fasciculations are common in healthy people, most often triggered by fatigue, strenuous exercise, caffeine or nicotine, stress and anxiety, poor sleep, dehydration, and electrolyte disturbance of sodium, potassium, calcium or magnesium. Reviews estimate that about 70 percent of healthy people experience a benign fasciculation at least once in life. Such twitches often wander around the eyelid, calf, thigh or arm, come and go, last only a short time each, and ease with rest, hydration and better sleep.[12][13]

需要区分的是伴随征象。良性束颤通常短暂、游走，没有肌肉无力或萎缩；如果束颤持续固定在同一部位，并伴真无力、肌肉变细、吞咽或言语困难、反射异常，就需要神经科结合查体和电生理评估。本章只描述常见自发跳动的机制，不展开神经肌肉疾病的诊断流程，也不据此判断预后。The accompanying signs matter for distinction. Benign twitches are usually brief and wandering, without muscle weakness or wasting. A twitch fixed in one place, together with true weakness, thinning muscle, swallowing or speech difficulty, or abnormal reflexes, needs neurological examination and electrophysiological assessment. This chapter describes the mechanism of common spontaneous twitches only, not the diagnostic workup or prognosis of neuromuscular disease.[12][13]

边界：本章不建议自行补钾、补镁或服用药物，也不把束颤等同于某种疾病。偶发、孤立、无伴随症状的跳动多属生理波动；持续加重或出现上述无力、萎缩表现时，应交给医生判断，而不是按网络信息对号入座。Boundaries: this chapter does not recommend self-supplementing potassium or magnesium, or any medication, and a fasciculation is not equated with a particular disease. Occasional, isolated twitches without accompanying symptoms are mostly physiological fluctuation; persistent worsening or the weakness and wasting above should be assessed by a clinician rather than matched to online information.[13]

05为什么骨骼会响？Why do joints crack?

掰关节的弹响多来自滑液内气体空腔的形成与塌陷振动；气体约 15–20 分钟回溶，另一类咔哒声来自肌腱滑动。Joint cracking mostly comes from gas cavities forming and collapsing in synovial fluid; gases redissolve in about 15 to 20 minutes, while another click comes from tendon sliding.

掰手指或活动关节时发出的“咔哒”弹响，最常见的来源不是骨头互相摩擦，也不是关节滑液被用光。关节面覆盖软骨，外包关节囊，囊内充满滑液润滑软骨、减少摩擦；软骨本身没有血管，营养靠滑液渗透，滑液润滑是关节顺畅活动的前提。快速牵拉或屈伸把关节面分开，关节腔内压力变化，滑液内溶解的气体析出形成空腔，空腔形成和塌陷时的振动就发出声音。The cracking sound when knuckles are pulled or joints moved most often comes neither from bones grinding together nor from depleted joint fluid. Joint surfaces are covered by cartilage, wrapped in a capsule filled with lubricating synovial fluid that reduces friction. Rapid distraction or bending separates the surfaces, pressure inside the capsule changes, gases dissolved in the fluid come out of solution as a cavity, and the vibration of its formation and collapse produces the sound.[14]

这一过程在生物力学上称为空化（cavitation）或 tribonucleation。关节表面先被黏液层贴住，牵拉到临界距离后表面迅速分离，局部压力骤降，滑液中溶解的氮、氧、二氧化碳等气体离开溶液形成可见空腔；高速摄影和实时影像研究都观察到，弹响伴随空腔出现，声音来自空腔形成与随后塌陷的流体振动，而不是软骨被磨掉；空腔在弹响后并不会立刻消失，而是停留片刻再慢慢塌陷。Biomechanically this is called cavitation, or tribonucleation. The surfaces first stick through a thin mucus layer; once traction passes a critical distance they separate quickly, local pressure drops sharply, and dissolved nitrogen, oxygen and carbon dioxide leave the fluid to form a visible cavity. High-speed photography and real-time imaging both observe the cavity appearing with the crack, and the sound comes from fluid vibration as the cavity forms and later collapses, not from cartilage being worn away.[14][15]

弹响之后有一段回静期。气体需要约 15–20 分钟才重新溶解回滑液，这段时间里同一个关节很难再次掰响；这也解释了为什么掰完一次要等一会儿。目前研究没有支持“反复掰手指导致关节炎”的说法，弹响本身不直接磨薄软骨，关节退变另有磨损、炎症和年龄等因素。There is a refractory period after cracking. The gases take about 15 to 20 minutes to redissolve into the synovial fluid, during which the same joint is hard to crack again, which explains the wait between two cracks. Existing evidence does not support repeated knuckle cracking causing arthritis, and the sound itself does not directly wear cartilage thin; joint degeneration also involves wear, inflammation and age.[14][15]

还有另一类弹响来源：肌腱或韧带在活动时划过骨突、关节隆起或腱鞘边缘，滑动复位时发出一过性“咔哒”，常见于肩、髋、膝和手指。这类声音来自组织滑过骨性标志，与关节腔内气体空腔不是同一条机制线；两者多为无痛、偶发，活动到某个角度时才出现；肌腱弹响常在屈伸到同一角度时重复出现，一般不伴疼痛或肿胀。Another source of cracking is tendons or ligaments sliding over bony prominences, joint ridges or tendon sheath edges during movement, giving a momentary click as they slip back into place; this is common at the shoulder, hip, knee and fingers. That sound comes from tissue passing a bony landmark, a different route from the intra-articular gas cavity, and both types are usually painless, occasional and appear at a certain angle.[1][14]

边界：本章只解释关节弹响的常见机制，不提供关节复位手法或治疗方案。如果弹响伴随疼痛、肿胀、关节卡住、交锁、无力，或发生在外伤之后，应到骨科或运动医学科评估；把无痛弹响当成关节磨损而过度保护，也没有必要。Boundaries: this chapter explains common cracking mechanisms only, and gives no joint-manipulation technique or treatment plan. Cracking with pain, swelling, locking, catching, weakness, or appearing after trauma should be assessed by orthopedics or sports medicine. Treating a painless crack as joint wear and over-protecting the joint is likewise unnecessary.[14]
