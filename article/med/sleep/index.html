睡眠：脑电阶段、困意、昼夜节律与梦境Sleep: Stages, Sleep Pressure, Circadian Rhythm and Dreams

从 NREM/REM 的脑电划分，到腺苷怎样积累困意、生物钟怎样被光照校准，再到 REM 梦境与睡眠剥夺证据：讲清睡眠的生理机制、可核对的频段与时长范围，以及功能假说和证据强度的边界。From NREM/REM EEG staging, adenosine sleep pressure and light-set clocks to REM dreams and deprivation: physiology, checkable ranges, and the boundary between hypotheses and evidence.

01睡眠由哪些脑电阶段构成，人为什么需要睡眠？What EEG stages make up sleep, and why do we need to sleep?

睡眠按 N1、N2、N3 与 REM 划分，脑电频率递减、振幅升高；功能假说互补，单一答案尚未定论。Sleep divides into N1, N2, N3 and REM, with falling frequency and rising amplitude; functional hypotheses complement each other and no single answer is settled.

睡眠不是简单关机，而是一段被脑电特征划分的主动状态。人在清醒警觉时脑电以低幅高频的 β 波（约 13–30 Hz）为主；放松闭眼时出现 α 波（8–12 Hz）。入睡后依次进入非快速眼动（NREM）的 N1、N2、N3 阶段，再进入快速眼动（REM）睡眠；约 90 分钟一个周期，一夜重复 4–5 轮，慢波睡眠集中在前半夜，REM 在后半夜增多。多导睡眠记录按约 30 秒一页把这些阶段连成整夜的睡眠结构图。Sleep is not a simple shutdown but an active state partitioned by EEG features. While awake and alert, the EEG is dominated by low-amplitude, high-frequency beta waves (about 13–30 Hz); relaxed closed eyes show alpha waves (8–12 Hz). Falling asleep moves through N1, N2 and N3 of non-rapid-eye-movement (NREM) sleep, then into rapid-eye-movement (REM) sleep. One cycle runs about 90 minutes, repeating four or five times a night; slow-wave sleep clusters in the first half of the night, and REM increases toward the morning.[2]

各阶段由频率和振幅区分。N1 是过渡期，脑电从 α 转向 θ 波（4–7 Hz），心率、肌张力和体温下降；N2 以 θ 波为主，出现睡眠纺锤波和 K 复合波，是整夜占比最长的阶段，纺锤波被认为与感觉门控有关，K 复合波是对刺激的一过性大慢波反应；N3 为慢波睡眠，δ 波（低于 4 Hz）占优势，振幅升高、频率降低。REM 脑电形态接近清醒的低幅高频，但骨骼肌张力几乎消失，眼球快速转动。Stages are distinguished by frequency and amplitude. N1 is a transition in which EEG moves from alpha to theta waves (4–7 Hz), while heart rate, muscle tone and body temperature drop. N2 is dominated by theta waves with sleep spindles and K-complexes, and it occupies the largest share of the night. N3 is slow-wave sleep, where delta waves (below 4 Hz) prevail: higher amplitude, lower frequency. REM EEG looks close to waking low-amplitude high-frequency activity, but skeletal muscle tone nearly disappears and the eyes move rapidly.[1][2]

睡眠的功能假说覆盖多个方向。OpenStax 心理学教材归纳：生长激素等物质在睡眠中分泌，学习与记忆巩固与 NREM 和 REM 都有关；突触稳态假说提出睡眠帮助下调白天过度增强的突触连接，代谢废物也可能通过类淋巴系统在睡眠期清除。记忆巩固的证据多来自学习任务前后的睡眠对照实验，突触稳态与废物清除仍属继续验证的机制假说。需要说明，人为什么要睡目前仍没有单一被完全证实的答案，不同机制提供的是互补证据，而不是互相替代。Functional hypotheses cover several directions. The OpenStax psychology textbook notes that growth hormone and related substances are secreted during sleep, and that memory consolidation is linked to both NREM and REM. The synaptic homeostasis hypothesis proposes that sleep down-scales connections strengthened during the day, while waste clearance may occur through the glymphatic system during sleep. It should be noted that no single explanation for why we sleep is fully confirmed yet; the mechanisms offer complementary evidence rather than replacements for one another.[1][3]

睡眠结构随年龄变化。婴儿睡眠占比高、REM 比例大；成人夜间睡眠约 7–9 小时，N3 慢波随年龄减少，夜间觉醒增多；老年人睡眠片段化，节律提前。成人推荐时长来自人群调查，个体可在区间内存在差异，轮班、光环境和合并疾病都会改变睡眠结构。多导睡眠图通过脑电、眼电、肌电和心率记录划分阶段，是判断睡眠结构的客观手段。Sleep structure changes with age. Infants sleep a large share of the day with a high REM proportion; adults sleep about seven to nine hours, with N3 slow-wave declining and night awakenings increasing with age; older sleep becomes fragmented and the rhythm advances. Polysomnography records EEG, eye movement, muscle tone and heart rate to classify stages objectively.[3]

边界：阶段划分基于脑电和行为指标，个体差异存在；不要把某一阶段的功能假说当成已定论的生理刚需清单。睡眠时长建议是群体范围，不是对每个人的精确处方；长期睡眠不足的影响有流行病学和实验证据，但具体到某一晚缺觉的后果，不能简单按小时线性外推。The boundary: stage classification rests on EEG and behavioral markers, and individual variation exists; functional hypotheses for any stage should not be treated as settled physiological requirements. Sleep-duration guidance is a population range, not an exact prescription for each person. Long-term sleep loss has epidemiological and experimental support, but the effect of one missed night cannot be extrapolated linearly by the hour.[1][3]

02困意是怎么积累起来的，咖啡因为什么能提神？How does sleep pressure build, and why does caffeine keep us alert?

清醒时腺苷积累推动睡眠压力，睡眠中清除；咖啡因占位腺苷受体、暂时阻断信号。Adenosine accumulates during wakefulness to raise pressure and clears during sleep; caffeine occupies adenosine receptors and blocks the signal temporarily.

困意来自稳态压力，而不只是到点了。清醒期间，大脑代谢物腺苷在突触周围逐步积累：神经元活动消耗 ATP 并脱磷酸产生腺苷，作用于 A1、A2A 受体推动睡眠压力上升；睡眠过程中腺苷被清除，压力回落。这就是双过程模型中的 S 过程：压力随清醒时长上升、随睡眠下降，与昼夜节律的 C 过程相互配合，共同决定睡意何时到来。Sleepiness comes from homeostatic pressure, not only from the clock of the hour. During wakefulness, the metabolite adenosine accumulates around synapses and acts on A1 and A2A receptors to raise sleep pressure; during sleep adenosine is cleared and pressure falls. This is Process S in the two-process model: pressure rises with time awake and falls during sleep, working together with the circadian Process C to determine when drowsiness arrives.[3][6]

咖啡因的提神作用是受体层面的占位。咖啡因分子结构与腺苷相似，结合腺苷受体却不产生相同效应，把受体堵住，使积累的腺苷信号传不进去；它并不清除腺苷，只是暂时阻断信号，药效过后积压的困意可能更明显。咖啡因经肝脏代谢，半衰期个体差异较大，阻断作用可持续数小时，累积摄入会推迟入睡并降低深睡比例，这也解释了为什么下午摄入咖啡因会延迟入睡。The alerting effect of caffeine is receptor-level occupancy. Caffeine resembles adenosine in structure, binds adenosine receptors without producing the same effect, and blocks them so the accumulated adenosine signal cannot get through. It does not remove adenosine, only temporarily interrupts the signal; once the drug wears off, the backed-up sleepiness may be stronger. This also explains why afternoon caffeine delays sleep onset.[3]

除腺苷外，其他稳态信号参与调节。前列腺素 D2 促进睡眠倾向，体温下降和糖原储备恢复参与压力回落，胶质细胞通过代谢支持调节神经元活动；睡眠剥夺后慢波睡眠增多，被看作稳态补偿的表现。NHLBI 的睡眠资料把睡眠-觉醒看成受多重内部信号调节的状态，而不是单一开关。Other homeostatic signals participate alongside adenosine. Prostaglandin D2, body-temperature change, glycogen depletion and glial activity all relate to sleep pressure; after deprivation, slow-wave sleep increases as a sign of homeostatic compensation. NHLBI sleep materials treat wake and sleep as a state regulated by multiple internal signals rather than a single switch.[3][6]

稳态过程与节律过程相互配合：S 过程像睡眠负债的积分，决定缺了多少觉，C 过程像定时闹钟，决定几点困、几点醒。两条曲线在一天中叠加，白天两者反向抵消时人保持清醒，傍晚 C 过程下行、S 过程上行，睡意合并出现；清晨警觉恢复。跨时区或轮班时，稳态压力和节律相位错位，就会出现白天犯困、夜间清醒。The homeostatic and circadian processes cooperate: Process S determines how much sleep is owed, while Process C sets when one becomes sleepy and alert. The two curves combine over the day: sleepiness rises in the evening and alertness returns in the morning. After time-zone travel or shift work, homeostatic pressure and circadian phase are misaligned, producing daytime drowsiness and nighttime wakefulness.[4]

边界：腺苷模型是解释困意的主流框架之一，但睡眠驱动还受遗传、激素和疾病影响；咖啡因阻断受体的剂量和个体代谢差异很大，代谢快慢与相关肝酶有关，不能据此判断喝多少咖啡都不影响睡眠。睡眠债务的恢复程度因人而异，补觉只能部分偿还既往欠账，长期节律紊乱需要临床评估。The boundary: the adenosine model is a leading framework, but sleep drive also depends on genetics, hormones and disease. Caffeine dose and individual metabolism vary widely, so one cannot conclude that any amount of coffee leaves sleep unaffected. Recovery from sleep debt differs between people; hours of catch-up sleep cannot simply cancel an accumulated debt.[3][6]

03昼夜节律由什么生物钟控制，光照怎样校准它？What clock controls circadian rhythm, and how does light calibrate it?

下丘脑 SCN 接收光照校准相位，时钟基因反馈环以约 24 小时同步外周组织；光照是主要授时因子。The hypothalamic SCN calibrates phase from light, a clock-gene feedback loop synchronizes peripheral tissues on about 24 hours; light is the main timekeeper.

昼夜节律是体内大约 24 小时的生理周期，由中枢生物钟授时。视交叉上核（SCN）位于下丘脑，是一对很小的核团，每侧约含一万个神经元，自身能维持约 24 小时的振荡；它接收视网膜传来的光照信号，校准自身相位，并通过神经和激素通路把节律传给外周组织。NIGMS 资料指出，Clock、Period 等节律基因编码的蛋白入核抑制自身转录，以转录-翻译负反馈循环在约 24 小时内升降；Hall、Rosbash 和 Young 因在果蝇中鉴定出这类时钟基因获得 2017 年诺贝尔奖。The circadian rhythm is an approximately 24-hour physiological cycle set by a central biological clock. The suprachiasmatic nucleus (SCN) sits in the hypothalamus as a pair of tiny nuclei, each side containing about ten thousand neurons. It receives light signals from the retina, calibrates its own phase, and relays the rhythm to peripheral tissues through neural and hormonal pathways. NIGMS explains that clock-gene proteins rise and fall in a transcription-translation negative-feedback loop over about 24 hours; the 2017 Nobel Prize recognized the clock genes identified in fruit flies.[5]

光照是主要授时因子。视网膜内在光敏神经节细胞含视黑素，直接向 SCN 传递光强信息；清晨光照把生物钟提前，傍晚光照延后。黑暗中松果体分泌褪黑素，向身体传递夜间信号；光照抑制褪黑素合成与释放。除光照外，进食时间、体力活动和环境温度也能调整外周组织的相位。Light is the main timekeeper. Photosensitive retinal ganglion cells send light-level information directly to the SCN; morning light advances the clock, while evening light delays it. In darkness the pineal gland secretes melatonin, carrying a nighttime signal to the body, and light suppresses melatonin release. Beyond light, meal timing, activity and temperature can also reset peripheral clocks.[4][5]

节律贯穿多个系统。心率、血压、皮质醇、体温、警觉性和免疫功能都按昼夜相位波动；肝脏、胰腺等外周组织有自己的局部时钟，受 SCN 同步，例如肝脏的药物代谢酶、胰腺的胰岛素分泌都按相位调节，代谢酶和免疫因子的活性随相位变化。睡眠-觉醒相位、代谢和药物反应的时间差异，都与这套系统有关。The rhythm runs through many systems. Heart rate, blood pressure, cortisol, body temperature, alertness and immune function all fluctuate along circadian phases; peripheral tissues keep local clocks synchronized by the SCN. Timing differences in sleep-wake phase, metabolism and drug response all relate to this system.[5]

节律错位产生时差和轮班不适。跨时区后，SCN 仍按原相位运行，通常每天校准约 1–2 小时，需要数天逐步对齐；轮班工作让睡眠时间落在生物夜之外，长期与代谢综合征、心血管问题相关。NHLBI 睡眠-觉醒周期资料说明，内部时钟与环境线索不同步时会出现入睡困难和日间困倦。Rhythm misalignment produces jet lag and shift-work discomfort. After crossing time zones the SCN still runs on the old phase and needs several days to recalibrate. Shift work places sleep outside the biological night, and long-term exposure is linked to metabolic and cardiovascular risks. NHLBI sleep-wake materials note that when the internal clock and environmental cues are out of sync, sleep onset and daytime alertness suffer.[3][4]

边界：节律周期是约 24 小时，不同人相位偏好（早睡型、晚睡型）受基因和年龄影响；褪黑素补充剂的时相调整证据有限，不能当作通用助眠手段。个体的睡眠节律应由长期作息和专业评估判断，不能凭一次困倦推断生物钟紊乱。The boundary: the cycle is approximately 24 hours, and morning or evening preference is shaped by genes and age. Evidence for shifting phase with melatonin supplements is limited, so they are not a universal sleep aid. An individual's rhythm should be judged from long-term habits and professional assessment, not from a single episode of drowsiness.[4][5]

04做梦发生在什么阶段？睡眠缺失有什么后果？In which stage does dreaming occur, and what are the effects of sleep loss?

情节性梦境集中在 REM；剥夺先损警觉与注意，慢波和 REM 会反弹，长期不足关联代谢与心血管风险。Narrative dreaming concentrates in REM; deprivation first impairs alertness and attention, slow-wave and REM rebound, and chronic short sleep links to metabolic and cardiovascular risk.

做梦大多发生在 REM 睡眠。REM 阶段脑血流和脑代谢接近清醒，脑电接近清醒形态，眼球快速往返转动，呼吸和心率不规则；脑干通路抑制运动神经元，骨骼肌处于张力低下的弛缓状态，防止梦境动作付诸实施。从 REM 唤醒时报告情节性、情绪性梦境的比例最高；NREM 也可能有思维样内容，但情节性梦境以 REM 为主。梦境之所以显得真实，是因为 REM 中视觉加工和边缘系统等情绪相关脑区活跃，而负责逻辑核查与现实检验的前额叶活动相对降低；加上运动输出被抑制、意识缺少与外界对照的线索，梦境就被体验为正在发生的经历，醒来后前额叶恢复，才容易察觉情节不合常理。Most dreaming occurs during REM sleep. In REM the EEG approaches waking patterns, the eyes dart back and forth, breathing and heart rate are irregular, and skeletal muscle tone is deeply relaxed, confining dream movement to the brain. Dream reports are most common when a person is awakened from REM. NREM can carry thought-like content, but narrative dreaming is concentrated in REM. Dreams feel real because during REM, visual-processing and limbic emotion regions stay active while prefrontal areas for logic checking and reality testing are relatively suppressed; with motor output inhibited and no external comparison available, the dream is experienced as a happening event, and only after waking, when prefrontal activity returns, does its odd plot become obvious.[2][6]

梦境的功能仍有多种假说。一种观点把梦看作记忆巩固和情绪调节过程的副产品；另一种认为做梦参与情绪记忆加工和威胁模拟；OpenStax 教材也提到梦可能反映重要生活事件，或被看作意识早期形成的一种虚拟现实演练。各假说都有局部实验支持，目前没有单一结论被公认证实。Several hypotheses remain about dream function. One view treats dreams as a byproduct of memory consolidation and emotion regulation; another holds that dreaming participates in processing emotional memory. OpenStax also notes that dreams may reflect important personal events or be seen as a virtual-reality rehearsal in an early form of consciousness. No single hypothesis is universally confirmed.[1]

睡眠剥夺的后果有实验和观察证据。连续缺觉首先降低反应速度、注意维持和工作记忆，情绪冲动和易怒增加；在受控实验中，睡眠受限后的主观疲惫和客观注意力测试同步下降。长期睡眠不足与认知表现下降、血压和糖代谢异常、免疫指标变化相关。NHLBI 与 NCBI 睡眠生理学资料都把睡眠视为恢复性需求，剥夺后出现慢波和 REM 的反弹补偿，补觉后部分指标可以恢复，但长期累积的影响仍在持续观察。The consequences of sleep deprivation have experimental and observational support. Continuous loss of sleep first impairs alertness, attention and reaction speed, and worsens emotional control; chronic short sleep is associated with lower cognitive performance and metabolic and cardiovascular risks. NHLBI and NCBI sleep-physiology materials treat sleep as a restorative need, with slow-wave and REM rebound after deprivation.[3][6]

不同阶段被剥夺时补偿方式不同。选择性剥夺 N3 慢波睡眠，随后夜间慢波比例会优先回升；打断 REM 后 REM 占比反弹。学习任务在睡眠后回忆表现更好，支持睡眠参与巩固，睡眠纺锤波和慢波与陈述性记忆的关系在不同任务中有不同表现；但实验多在受控条件下完成，样本和任务各不相同，外推到日常作息需要谨慎。Compensation differs when stages are selectively deprived. If N3 slow-wave sleep is selectively removed, later nights prioritize slow-wave recovery; after REM is interrupted, the REM proportion rebounds. Memory tasks perform better after sleep, supporting a role in consolidation, but most experiments are controlled and extrapolation to daily schedules needs caution.[2][6]

边界：做梦等于清醒时排练是假说而非定论；睡眠剥夺研究的时长和任务各不相同，不能把极端实验结果等同于日常少睡一两小时。个体对缺觉的耐受差异很大，持续失眠、日间功能受损应通过临床评估，而不是靠固定补觉方案解决。The boundary: "dreaming equals waking rehearsal" is a hypothesis, not a settled fact. Deprivation studies vary in duration and tasks, so extreme experimental results cannot be equated with losing an hour or two of ordinary sleep. Tolerance to missing sleep varies widely; persistent insomnia or impaired daytime function should be assessed clinically rather than solved by a fixed catch-up schedule.[1][3]
