01人为什么要睡觉，能不能长期不睡？Why do we sleep, and can humans go without sleep long term?

睡眠按约 90 分钟周期在 NREM 与 REM 间交替；腺苷积累形成睡眠压力，咖啡因阻断受体只暂时屏蔽困倦。Sleep cycles through NREM and REM in about 90-minute periods; adenosine builds sleep pressure, and caffeine blocks receptors only to mask drowsiness temporarily.

人不能长期不睡。睡眠是周期性的生理状态，成人通常每 24 小时经历 4–6 个睡眠周期，每个约 90 分钟，在非快速眼动（NREM）和快速眼动（REM）睡眠间交替。NREM 由浅入深，深睡眠时脑电波变慢、生长激素分泌增加；REM 阶段脑电活跃、骨骼肌松弛，与梦境和记忆加工相关。持续睡眠剥夺会依次影响警觉、注意、反应时和情绪判断，长期缺睡与代谢、心血管和认知风险升高相关。Humans cannot go without sleep for long. Sleep is a cyclic physiological state: adults usually go through 4–6 cycles per 24 hours, each about 90 minutes, alternating between non-rapid-eye-movement (NREM) and rapid-eye-movement (REM) sleep. NREM deepens stage by stage, with slower brain waves and more growth hormone in deep sleep; REM shows active EEG, relaxed skeletal muscles, and relates to dreaming and memory processing. Sustained deprivation impairs alertness, attention, reaction time and emotional judgment in sequence, and chronic short sleep tracks higher metabolic, cardiovascular and cognitive risk.[1]

睡眠压力从哪来。清醒时间越长，脑内腺苷等促眠物质积累越多，睡眠压力随之上升；入睡后腺苷水平下降，压力得到释放。这一稳态过程与昼夜节律相互配合：视交叉上核按光照周期调节褪黑素等信号，决定困倦出现的时间窗。睡眠不足时，主观困倦感和客观任务表现可以分离，有人自以为适应了缺睡，测验成绩却仍在下降。Where sleep drive comes from. The longer one stays awake, the more adenosine and other sleep-promoting substances accumulate, raising sleep pressure; after falling asleep adenosine levels fall and pressure releases. This homeostatic process works with the circadian rhythm: the suprachiasmatic nucleus tunes melatonin and other signals to the light-dark cycle, setting the sleep window. With insufficient sleep, subjective sleepiness and objective task performance can diverge: people feel they have adapted while test scores keep dropping.[1][7]

咖啡因是提神还是还债。咖啡因是腺苷受体拮抗剂，主要阻断 A1 和 A2A 受体，让积累的腺苷信号传不进去，主观困倦被暂时屏蔽，但腺苷并没有被清除。相关综述指出，急性咖啡因主要经 A2A 受体促进清醒；傍晚摄入还会推迟入睡、缩短深睡眠。长期饮用后身体出现耐受，受体密度和代谢适应改变，停用可出现头痛、疲倦等戒断症状，这就是常说的“还债”。Caffeine: stimulation or paying debt? Caffeine is an adenosine receptor antagonist, blocking mainly A1 and A2A receptors so accumulated adenosine signals cannot pass through; subjective sleepiness is masked temporarily, but adenosine is not cleared. A review of adenosine and sleep-wake regulation notes acute caffeine promotes wakefulness chiefly through A2A, while evening intake delays sleep onset and shortens deep sleep. Habitual intake leads to tolerance as receptor density and metabolism adapt, and withdrawal can bring headache and fatigue—the “debt” people speak of.[7]

可核对的证据。NHLBI 睡眠资料把警觉、情绪和代谢风险列为缺睡后果；腺苷—咖啡因综述给出受体机制与时间窗。成人一般建议每晚 7 小时以上，具体需求有个体差异；打盹可以部分恢复警觉，但不能完全替代夜间睡眠。睡眠阶段比例随年龄变化，婴幼儿 REM 占比更高。Checkable evidence. NHLBI sleep materials list alertness, mood and metabolic risks as consequences of deprivation; the adenosine-caffeine review gives the receptor mechanism and timing. Adults are generally advised seven or more hours, with individual variation; naps restore part of alertness but cannot replace night sleep. Stage proportions shift with age, and infants spend a larger share in REM.[1][2][7]

边界：这里区分机制与相关性。腺苷积累是睡眠压力的机制之一，不能把所有困倦都归因于腺苷；缺睡与慢性病之间多为流行病学关联，受饮食、活动和基础疾病混杂。咖啡因不补充能量，只是掩盖疲劳信号；把提神当成恢复了睡眠，会低估累积债。睡眠呼吸暂停、失眠等睡眠障碍在后续章节处理。Boundary: mechanism and correlation are separated here. Adenosine accumulation is one mechanism of sleep pressure, not an explanation for every form of drowsiness; links between short sleep and chronic disease are mostly epidemiological associations mixed with diet, activity and baseline illness. Caffeine adds no energy, it masks the fatigue signal; treating stimulation as recovered sleep understates the accumulated debt. Sleep apnea and insomnia are treated in later chapters.[2][7]

02人睡觉时为什么会打鼾，打鼾和呼吸暂停有什么区别？Why do people snore in sleep, and how is it different from sleep apnea?

咽部软组织被气流振动形成鼾声；上气道反复塌陷导致 OSA，伴反复低氧与觉醒，CPAP 撑开气道。Airflow vibrates pharyngeal soft tissue to snore; repeated airway collapse causes OSA with hypoxia and arousals, and CPAP splints the airway open.

打鼾是气流振动咽部软组织发出的声音。清醒时咽部肌肉张力支撑气道保持开放；入睡后肌肉松弛，舌根、软腭和悬雍垂后移，气道变窄。呼吸气流通过狭窄断面时流速加快、软组织振动，产生鼾声。仰卧时舌根更容易后坠，饮酒和镇静药会进一步放松肌肉，肥胖使颈部脂肪堆积压缩气道，都让打鼾更响。Snoring is the sound of airflow vibrating soft tissue in the throat. While awake, pharyngeal muscle tone holds the airway open; during sleep the muscles relax, and the tongue base, soft palate and uvula shift backward, narrowing the airway. Air accelerates through the narrowed section and vibrates the soft tissue, producing the snore. Back sleeping lets the tongue fall farther back; alcohol and sedatives relax muscles further; obesity pads the neck with fat that compresses the airway, and all make snoring louder.[3]

从打鼾到呼吸暂停。单纯打鼾气流仍在，只是有振动；阻塞性睡眠呼吸暂停（OSA）则是上气道反复塌陷，气流部分或完全中断。每次憋气结束时，身体短暂觉醒恢复气道，一晚可发生数十到数百次。反复低氧和觉醒打断睡眠周期，白天出现嗜睡、晨起头痛、注意力下降；长期与高血压、心律失常和心血管风险相关。From snoring to apnea. In plain snoring airflow continues despite vibration; obstructive sleep apnea (OSA) is repeated upper-airway collapse that partly or fully interrupts airflow. Each episode ends with a brief arousal that restores the airway, and dozens to hundreds can occur in one night. Repeated hypoxia and arousals fragment sleep, leaving daytime sleepiness, morning headache and poor attention; over time the condition tracks hypertension, arrhythmia and cardiovascular risk.[3][4]

危险因素与检查。男性、肥胖、颈围粗、扁桃体肥大、鼻中隔偏曲、下颌后缩和年龄增长都增加风险；儿童打鼾常与扁桃体腺样体肥大有关。诊断靠多导睡眠监测，记录呼吸、血氧、脑电和体位；家用睡眠监测可在部分低风险人群中筛查。NHLBI 睡眠呼吸暂停资料把舌体后坠和软腭阻塞列为阻塞性通气的直接原因，综述则把咽部肌肉反应性和觉醒阈值列入非解剖因素。Risk factors and diagnosis. Male sex, obesity, large neck circumference, enlarged tonsils, deviated septum, receded jaw and older age raise risk; children's snoring often links to enlarged adenoids and tonsils. Diagnosis relies on polysomnography recording breathing, oxygen, EEG and body position; home sleep testing can screen selected lower-risk adults. NHLBI materials name tongue-base fall and soft-palate blockage as direct causes of obstructed breathing, and reviews list pharyngeal muscle responsiveness and arousal threshold among non-anatomical factors.[2][3][4]

睡姿与干预。侧睡减少舌根后坠，是简单的体位措施；减重、戒酒、避免睡前镇静药可减轻气道松弛。持续气道正压通气（CPAP）在睡眠中经面罩维持气道压力，像气动夹板撑开气道，是中重度 OSA 的常用治疗；口腔矫治器前移下颌，适用于部分轻中度患者。儿童腺样体扁桃体问题需专科评估。Position and treatment. Side sleeping reduces tongue-base fall as a simple positional measure; weight loss, cutting alcohol and avoiding bedtime sedatives lessen airway relaxation. Continuous positive airway pressure (CPAP) keeps airway pressure through a mask like a pneumatic splint and is standard treatment for moderate to severe OSA; mandibular advancement devices help selected mild to moderate patients. Pediatric adenoid and tonsil issues need specialist assessment.[3][4]

边界：打鼾不等于呼吸暂停，多数打鼾者没有反复憋气和低氧；但鼾声不规则、中途憋气、晨起嗜睡需要就医评估。OSA 与心血管疾病的关联有队列证据，因果链条涉及反复低氧、交感激活和睡眠破碎；不能凭打鼾响度判断严重程度，必须结合监测。这里不展开呼吸机参数选配和手术方案。Boundary: snoring is not apnea, and most snorers have no repeated choking or hypoxia; irregular snoring, pauses and morning sleepiness warrant evaluation. The OSA–cardiovascular association rests on cohort evidence, with a causal chain of repeated hypoxia, sympathetic activation and sleep fragmentation; loudness alone cannot grade severity, monitoring is required. Ventilator settings and surgical options are outside this chapter.[2][4]

03为什么有些人会长期失眠，致死性家族失眠症又是怎么回事？Why do some people have chronic insomnia, and what is fatal familial insomnia?

失眠是过度觉醒与条件化唤醒叠加；CBT-I 为一线行为治疗，FFI 则是 PRNP 突变致丘脑退化的罕见朊蛋白病。Insomnia combines hyperarousal with conditioned wakefulness; CBT-I is first-line behavioral treatment, while FFI is a rare prion disease from PRNP mutation and thalamic degeneration.

失眠是入睡困难、睡眠维持困难或早醒，且影响白天功能，每周出现至少数晚并持续一段时间。它不是单纯“睡得少”，而是大脑过度觉醒与睡眠环境条件化共同作用。失眠者常在床和卧室之间形成条件性唤醒：一上床就警觉、思维反刍，即使疲劳也难以入睡；这种习得性联系可以独立于初始诱因长期存在。Insomnia is difficulty falling asleep, staying asleep or waking too early, with daytime impairment, occurring several nights a week over a period of time. It is not simply sleeping little: it combines cortical hyperarousal with conditioned association to the sleep environment. Insomniacs often develop conditioned arousal to bed and bedroom—alertness and rumination set in the moment they lie down, and sleep does not come despite fatigue. This learned link can persist long after the original trigger is gone.[5]

机制模型。现代模型把失眠看作易感因素（高觉醒特质、焦虑倾向）、诱发因素（压力事件、疾病、作息改变）和维持因素（卧床时间过长、打盹补偿、对睡眠的灾难化关注）叠加的结果。神经层面可见皮层和交感系统过度兴奋，入睡潜伏期延长、深睡眠比例下降。失眠常与抑郁、焦虑相互加重，但两者诊断需要分开评估。Mechanistic model. Modern models frame insomnia as a stacking of predisposing factors (high-arousal traits, anxiety proneness), precipitating factors (stressful events, illness, schedule change) and perpetuating factors (time in bed too long, compensating naps, catastrophic worry about sleep). Neural levels show cortical and sympathetic overexcitation, longer sleep-onset latency and a lower share of deep sleep. Insomnia and depression or anxiety worsen each other, but the two diagnoses are assessed separately.[5][6]

治疗与证据。认知行为治疗（CBT-I）是慢性失眠的一线方案，包括睡眠限制、刺激控制、认知重构和睡眠卫生教育；随机对照研究显示它改善入睡潜伏期和睡眠效率，效果在停药后较稳定。助眠药物短期可缩短入睡时间，但依赖、耐受和次日残留镇静是限制；NHLBI 失眠资料强调先评估诱因和行为干预。一项针对共病抑郁失眠者的随机对照试验也检验了 CBT-I 对睡眠和情绪症状的作用。Treatment and evidence. Cognitive behavioral therapy for insomnia (CBT-I) is the first-line treatment for chronic insomnia, combining sleep restriction, stimulus control, cognitive restructuring and sleep-hygiene education. Randomized trials show it improves sleep-onset latency and sleep efficiency, with effects that hold after treatment ends. Hypnotic drugs can shorten onset in the short term, but dependence, tolerance and next-day residual sedation limit their use; NHLBI insomnia guidance stresses assessing triggers and behavioral intervention first. A randomized trial also tested CBT-I in people with comorbid insomnia and depression.[5][6]

边界病例：致死性家族失眠症。FFI 是罕见常染色体显性朊蛋白病，PRNP 基因 D178N 突变导致丘脑内侧核神经元进行性丢失，睡眠结构完全破坏，出现顽固失眠、自主神经紊乱、认知和运动恶化，起病后数月到数年死亡。它说明睡眠依赖特定脑结构，但属于极罕见遗传病，不能用它解释普通失眠。Boundary case: fatal familial insomnia. FFI is a rare autosomal dominant prion disease; the PRNP D178N mutation causes progressive loss of neurons in medial thalamic nuclei, destroying sleep structure and producing intractable insomnia, autonomic disturbance, cognitive and motor decline, with death months to years after onset. It shows sleep depends on specific brain structures, but as an extremely rare inherited disease it cannot explain ordinary insomnia.[10]

边界：普通失眠是可干预的睡眠—觉醒调节问题，FFI 是神经退行性朊蛋白病，二者机制不同。CBT-I 证据主要来自成人慢性失眠，青少年、孕期和共病人群需个体化；睡眠时长因人而异，不能用固定小时数判断是否失眠。这里不讨论具体处方药物选择和剂量。Boundary: common insomnia is a treatable sleep-wake regulation problem, while FFI is a neurodegenerative prion disease; their mechanisms differ. CBT-I evidence comes mainly from adults with chronic insomnia, and adolescents, pregnancy and comorbid groups need individualized care. Sleep need varies, so a fixed hour count cannot define insomnia. Specific prescription choices and doses are not discussed here.[5][10]

04抑郁会影响智商和记忆力吗，认知缺陷表现在哪些方面？Does depression affect IQ and memory, and where do cognitive deficits show up?

发作期执行功能、注意与言语学习测验常低于对照；IQ 反映当前表现，部分指标缓解后可改善，需与脑损伤定论分开。In episodes executive function, attention and verbal learning often score below controls; IQ reflects current state, some measures improve after remission.

抑郁会影响认知表现，但不等于智力永久下降。重性抑郁发作期，患者在执行功能、注意、言语学习和工作记忆测验上常比健康对照差；任务表现受情绪低落、动机、疲劳、睡眠紊乱和注意力反刍共同影响。IQ 测验测量的是当前测验表现，低分反映发作期状态，不能直接等同于先天智力永久受损。Depression affects cognitive performance, but this is not the same as permanently lowered intelligence. During a major depressive episode, patients often score below healthy controls on executive function, attention, verbal learning and working memory tasks; performance reflects low mood, motivation, fatigue, disrupted sleep and ruminative attention together. An IQ test measures current performance, and a low score reflects the state of an episode, not a fixed loss of innate ability.[8][9]

实验范式与效应方向。元分析纳入多项对照研究，抑郁组在认知灵活性、抑制控制和言语流畅性上缺陷较明显，有综述报告执行功能效应量 Cohen's d 达到 1.0 上下；注意和战略规划为中等程度损害。任务类型很关键：需要启动、转换和抑制的执行任务最容易暴露困难，简单再认任务受影响较小。效应方向是抑郁组低于对照，但幅度随病程、严重度和用药变化。Experimental paradigm and effect direction. Meta-analyses pooling controlled studies find clearer deficits in cognitive flexibility, inhibitory control and verbal fluency, with one review reporting executive-function effect sizes around Cohen's d = 1.0; attention and strategic planning show moderate impairment. Task type matters: executive tasks requiring initiation, switching and inhibition are most sensitive, while simple recognition is less affected. The direction is depressed groups below controls, but magnitude varies with illness course, severity and medication.[9]

记忆与脑结构。抑郁患者常主诉记性差，客观测验多体现为编码和提取效率下降，而非记忆存储整体损坏；结构影像元分析在脑岛、额下回、杏仁核和海马等区域观察到体积减小，这些关联受病程、复发次数和治疗影响。症状缓解后，部分认知和结构指标可以改善；长期、反复发作与更持久的改变相关，但“不可逆损伤”的说法需要证据分层。Memory and brain structure. Patients often complain of poor memory, while objective tests mostly show weaker encoding and retrieval efficiency rather than wholesale loss of storage. Structural MRI meta-analyses report reduced volumes in the insula, inferior frontal gyrus, amygdala and hippocampus; these associations depend on course, recurrence count and treatment. After symptom remission some cognitive and structural measures improve; long recurrent episodes track more persistent change, but claims of irreversible damage need layered evidence.[8][9]

临床与公共卫生。NIMH 抑郁资料把持续情绪低落、兴趣减退和功能下降列为核心表现；认知症状是功能恢复的一部分，缓解情绪后仍可能残留注意和执行困难。抑郁与认知下降可以互为因果：睡眠差、活动减少和社会隔离加重认知负担，认知困难又加重情绪低落。筛查和随访应覆盖功能恢复，而不只看情绪评分。Clinical and public health. NIMH depression materials list persistent low mood, anhedonia and functional decline as core features; cognitive symptoms are part of functional recovery, and attention and executive difficulties can linger after mood lifts. Depression and cognitive decline can reinforce each other: poor sleep, reduced activity and social isolation raise cognitive load, while cognitive difficulty worsens mood. Screening and follow-up should cover functional recovery, not mood scores alone.[5][8]

边界：抑郁认知研究以横断面和观察性队列为主，自我报告与客观测验不完全一致；不能把测验缺陷解释为大脑损伤定论。IQ 是稳定能力与当前状态的混合测量，发作期低分需要在缓解后复测。抑郁与痴呆、睡眠障碍和物质使用可能共病，混杂因素需要分开；这里不评估具体抗抑郁药物对认知的影响。Boundary: depression cognition research is mostly cross-sectional and observational, and self-report does not match objective tests exactly; test deficits cannot be read as definitive brain damage. IQ mixes stable ability with current state, and episode-phase low scores should be retested after remission. Depression can coexist with dementia, sleep disorders and substance use, and confounders must be separated; this chapter does not assess how specific antidepressants affect cognition.[8][9]

05人为什么会做梦，梦醒后为什么很快就记不住？Why do we dream, and why are dreams forgotten so quickly after waking?

REM 期情绪回路激活产生梦境；觉醒前后编码条件不足，梦痕迹难以转成可提取记忆。REM emotional circuits generate dreams; weak encoding around waking leaves traces hard to retrieve.

做梦主要出现在快速眼动（REM）睡眠，但并非只在这一阶段发生。成人睡眠约每 90 分钟一个周期，夜间经历 4 到 6 次，非快速眼动（NREM）由浅入深，REM 阶段脑电活跃、骨骼肌松弛、眼球快速转动。REM 期间边缘系统和情绪回路激活，前额叶部分控制减弱，于是出现画面跳跃、情绪鲜明的梦境；早期脑电观察中，超过七成在 REM 醒来的人能报告梦，NREM 醒来则多为思维样片段。Dreaming occurs mainly in rapid-eye-movement (REM) sleep, but it is not confined to that stage. Adults go through about four to six cycles a night, each around 90 minutes, alternating non-REM (NREM) deepening stages with REM. In REM the EEG is active, skeletal muscles relax and eyes move rapidly; the limbic and emotional circuits are activated while prefrontal control is partly dampened, producing vivid, emotionally charged imagery with abrupt scene shifts. Early EEG work found that over 70 percent of people awakened from REM reported a dream, while NREM awakenings yielded more thought-like fragments.[1][13]

梦之所以很快记不住，关键在编码和回忆条件。梦能否被记住，取决于觉醒前后的脑电状态：前额 theta 活动较强、局部慢波活动较低时，回忆更容易形成；深睡眠阶段慢波强、觉醒时多巴胺能记忆编码不足，梦境线索没有转成可提取的记忆痕迹。醒来后若不立即复述，注意力很快转向白天任务，短期梦意象随之消退。这不是大脑刻意删除梦境，而是巩固和提取的条件不足。Dreams fade quickly because encoding and recall conditions are weak. Whether a dream is remembered depends on the brain state just before waking: recall is more likely with stronger frontal theta activity and reduced local slow-wave activity. In deep sleep slow waves are strong and dopaminergic memory encoding at awakening is insufficient, so dream traces are not converted into retrievable memories. If the dream is not rehearsed immediately on waking, attention shifts to daytime tasks and short-lived imagery dissolves. This is not deliberate deletion but a failure of consolidation and retrieval conditions.[1][13]

睡眠中的记忆加工与做梦相互重叠。一个周期里，NREM 与 REM 交替参与记忆巩固：情节记忆和技能记忆在不同阶段被重新激活、整理并转存到长期存储；情绪材料也在相关睡眠阶段被再加工。梦的内容常延续白天经历、情绪和近期刺激，这一连续性假设得到不少观察支持；睡前看到、听到或感受到的信息，有时会被编入梦境场景。Memory processing during sleep overlaps with dreaming. Across a cycle, NREM and REM cooperate in consolidation: episodic and skill memories are reactivated, reorganised and stored in different stages, and emotional material is reprocessed in relevant sleep stages. Dream content often continues waking experience, mood and recent stimuli, a continuity supported by many observations; information seen, heard or felt before sleep is sometimes woven into dream scenes.[13]

回忆频率存在明显个体差异。有人几乎夜夜记得梦，有人很少回忆，这种稳定差异与脑结构和唤醒特质有关。睡眠片段化、夜间觉醒增多、醒后立刻记录，都能提高回忆率；NREM 与 REM 的界限也并非绝对，部分脑损伤和药物研究显示，做梦与 REM 生成并不由同一套脑机制单独控制。Recall frequency varies widely between individuals. Some people remember dreams nearly every night while others rarely do, a stable difference linked to brain structure and arousal traits. Sleep fragmentation, more night-time awakenings and writing down dreams immediately on waking all raise recall. The NREM-REM boundary is not absolute: lesion and medication studies suggest dreaming and REM generation are not controlled by a single shared mechanism.[1][2]

边界：梦的功能理论仍有争议，情绪加工、记忆巩固和激活合成等模型证据强度不同，不能把梦解释为预兆或固定象征。频繁噩梦、睡眠中受伤或白天严重嗜睡属于需要评估的情况；本章不讨论梦境解析式的心理治疗，也不据此判断心理状态。Boundary: theories of dream function remain debated, and models such as emotional processing, memory consolidation and activation-synthesis differ in evidence strength. Dreams should not be read as omens or fixed symbols. Frequent nightmares, injury during sleep or severe daytime sleepiness warrant evaluation; this chapter does not cover interpretive dream therapy or infer mental state from dream content.[2][13]

06为什么有人会梦游，梦游时人在做梦吗？Why do some people sleepwalk, and are they acting out a dream?

梦游是深 NREM 不完全觉醒，运动启动而意识离线，事后多遗忘；与 REM 梦境不是一回事。Sleepwalking is incomplete arousal from deep NREM with motor onset and offline awareness, usually amnesic; not REM dreaming.

梦游（睡行症）发生在深 NREM 慢波睡眠，而不是 REM 做梦阶段。它多在入睡后前半夜、夜间前三分之一出现，从深睡眠突然不完全觉醒：运动系统启动了，意识和对外界判断却仍处于离线状态，属于一种状态解离。儿童慢波睡眠占比更高，发作更常见，学龄期比例相对较高，多数人到青春期后逐渐减轻；成人新发则需要另外排查药物、神经和代谢方面的诱因。Sleepwalking, or somnambulism, occurs during deep NREM slow-wave sleep, not during REM dreaming. Episodes usually take place in the first third of the night, as an incomplete arousal from deep sleep: the motor system starts while consciousness and judgement stay offline, a state dissociation. Children spend more time in slow-wave sleep and have more episodes, most improving by adolescence; adult onset needs separate evaluation for triggers.[1][11]

发作表现以简单到复杂的自动行为为主。患者可坐起、下床走动、翻找或挪动物品，眼神茫然，对呼唤和指令反应迟钝，判断与规划能力受损；事后通常没有记忆，这与 REM 梦境的回忆不同。同夜可能伴梦话或夜惊，此时不要强行摇晃或大声叫醒，骤然唤醒反而加重意识模糊和定向困难。Episodes range from simple to complex automatic behaviours. The person may sit up, get out of bed, walk about, or search for and move objects, with a blank stare and dulled response to calls or commands; decision-making and planning are impaired. There is usually no memory afterwards, which differs from recall of REM dreams. Sleep talking or night terrors may occur the same night; do not shake or shout the person awake, because abrupt arousal worsens confusion and disorientation.[11]

倾向与触发因素有迹可循。脑血流研究观察到发作时额叶、顶叶局部灌注降低，与意识范围狭窄的表现吻合；遗传倾向明显，同卵双生的一致性高于异卵双生，部分家族呈常染色体显性遗传但外显不全。睡眠剥夺超过约 24 小时、发热、某些镇静催眠药和部分抗抑郁药、甲状腺功能异常，都可能诱发易感者发作。Predisposition and triggers are identifiable. Cerebral perfusion studies show reduced frontal and parietal local blood flow during episodes, matching the narrowed conscious field. There is a clear genetic tendency: monozygotic twins agree more often than dizygotic ones, and some families show autosomal dominant inheritance with incomplete penetrance. Sleep deprivation beyond roughly 24 hours, fever, certain sedative-hypnotics and some antidepressants, and thyroid dysfunction can trigger episodes in predisposed people.[1][11]

处理以安全防护和识别诱因为主。多数睡行症属良性，环境上应锁好门窗、移走易碎和危险物品、楼梯口加护栏；记录连续几晚的发作时间，可在常发时间前 15 到 30 分钟定时唤醒。诊断依靠病史和目击者描述，多导睡眠监测用于鉴别困难、涉及外伤或法律争议的情况；成人发作、伴神经系统症状或药物相关时，需评估药物、神经退行性疾病和甲状腺等问题。Management centres on safety and removing triggers. Most somnambulism is benign: lock doors and windows, remove breakable or dangerous objects and fit stair gates. Keep a diary of episode timing for several nights, and scheduled waking 15 to 30 minutes before the usual episode time can help. Diagnosis rests on history and witness reports; polysomnography is reserved for difficult differential diagnosis, injury or legal questions. Adult episodes, neurological symptoms or medication-linked onset call for assessment of drugs, neurodegenerative disease and thyroid problems.[2][11]

边界：本章区分 NREM 觉醒障碍与 REM 睡眠行为障碍、夜惊；梦游不是在表演梦境，事后遗忘也不是故意隐瞒。频繁走出室外、坠落、伴随攻击行为或造成危险时应就医评估，不自行加用或调整安眠药物；儿童偶发、环境安全时通常以观察和防护为主。Boundary: this chapter separates NREM arousal disorders from REM sleep behaviour disorder and night terrors. Sleepwalking is not acting out a dream, and amnesia afterwards is not concealment. Frequent outdoor episodes, falls, aggression or danger warrant clinical evaluation; do not start or adjust sleeping medication without guidance. Occasional childhood episodes in a safe environment are usually watched and protected against injury.[1][2]

07咖啡为什么能提神，为什么越喝越困、还会利尿？Why does coffee stimulate, why does tolerance build, and why is it diuretic?

咖啡因拮抗 A1/A2A 受体屏蔽腺苷信号；半寿期约 5 小时，长期饮用出现耐受，轻度利尿。Caffeine blocks A1/A2A adenosine signals; half-life about 5 hours, tolerance builds, mild diuresis.

咖啡因提神，靠的是拮抗腺苷。清醒时间越长，脑内腺苷积累越多，结合 A1 和 A2A 受体发出困意信号；咖啡因分子结构与腺苷相似，占据这些受体位点，让积累的腺苷信号传不进去，主观困倦被暂时屏蔽，但腺苷本身并没有被清除。相关综述指出，急性咖啡因主要经 A2A 受体促进清醒；傍晚摄入还会推迟入睡、缩短深睡眠比例。Caffeine keeps people awake by antagonising adenosine. The longer one stays awake, the more adenosine accumulates and binds A1 and A2A receptors to signal sleepiness. Caffeine has a similar shape and occupies those receptor sites, so accumulated adenosine cannot pass its signal through; subjective drowsiness is masked temporarily, but adenosine is not cleared. Reviews note acute caffeine promotes wakefulness chiefly through A2A, and evening intake delays sleep onset and shortens deep sleep.[1][7]

代谢速度决定一杯咖啡能管多久。咖啡因在肝脏代谢，成人血浆半寿期约 5 小时，个体差异很大：吸烟加快代谢，妊娠和部分药物减慢代谢。随着时间推移血药浓度下降，作用逐渐消退；不同饮品的咖啡因含量差异很大，豆种、冲泡时间和方式都会影响实际摄入。下午和晚上摄入，仍可能影响后半夜的睡眠结构。需要强调的是，咖啡因不补充能量，也没有清除睡眠压力，只是把疲劳信号暂时挡住。Metabolism sets how long a cup lasts. Caffeine is processed in the liver, with an adult plasma half-life of about five hours and wide individual variation: smoking speeds it up, pregnancy and some drugs slow it down. As blood concentration declines over time the effect fades, and afternoon or evening intake can still distort the later night's sleep architecture. Caffeine adds no energy and does not remove sleep pressure; it only blocks the fatigue signal for a while.[7]

长期饮用会出现耐受和戒断。规律摄入后，受体密度和代谢发生适应改变，同样剂量的提神效果下降，于是出现越喝越困的感受；停用后可出现头痛、疲倦、情绪低落等戒断症状，通常数天内缓解。这就是常说的"还债"：之前被屏蔽的腺苷压力，在停药后集中显现。耐受程度随饮用频率和个人代谢不同，不能用别人的饮用量衡量自己。Habitual use produces tolerance and withdrawal. With regular intake, receptor density and metabolism adapt, the same dose works less well, and more coffee feels necessary to stay awake. Stopping can bring headache, fatigue and low mood as withdrawal, usually resolving within days. This is the so-called debt: adenosine pressure that was masked reappears once the drug wears off. Tolerance varies with intake frequency and personal metabolism, so someone else's amount is not a measure for yourself.[7]

咖啡因还有轻度利尿等作用。它可增加肾血流和尿量，属于温和利尿，习惯饮用者耐受更明显；口干或脱水感更多来自个体状态，不必把一杯咖啡等同于大量排水。此外咖啡因会刺激胃酸分泌、加快心率，本身焦虑明显、有心律失常或孕期等情况，摄入量需要更谨慎，并结合基础病判断。Caffeine also has a mild diuretic effect. It can raise renal blood flow and urine output, a gentle diuresis to which regular users adapt; dry mouth or dehydration mostly reflects individual state, and one cup should not be equated with heavy fluid loss. It also stimulates gastric acid and speeds heart rate. Those with marked anxiety, arrhythmia or pregnancy should be more cautious and judge intake against their underlying condition.[2][7]

边界：本章讲作用机制和一般反应，不给出每日安全剂量或处方。咖啡因反应受基因代谢、基础疾病和合并用药影响；长期依靠大量咖啡维持清醒，往往提示睡眠不足或存在睡眠障碍，应回到规律作息和睡眠评估，而不是继续叠加提神饮品。若心悸、手抖或失眠持续，应就医排查。Boundary: this chapter explains mechanisms and general responses, not a daily safe dose or prescription. Caffeine response depends on metabolic genes, illness and other medications. Relying on large amounts of coffee to stay awake often signals insufficient sleep or a sleep disorder; the answer is regular schedules and sleep evaluation, not stacking more stimulants. Persistent palpitations, tremor or insomnia should be medically checked.[1][2]

08情绪为什么会影响肠胃，紧张时为什么会胃疼或拉肚子？Why does mood affect the gut, and why does stress cause stomach pain or diarrhoea?

脑肠轴经自主神经、迷走传入与内分泌免疫通路双向通信；交感兴奋改变蠕动分泌和敏感性。The gut-brain axis communicates bidirectionally via autonomic nerves, vagal afferents, endocrine and immune routes; sympathetic activation alters motility, secretion and sensitivity.

情绪和肠胃互相影响，连接两者的是脑肠轴这条双向通信网络。大脑边缘系统和下丘脑处理紧张与压力，经自主神经和内分泌通路下行到胃肠；肠管的运动、分泌和敏感性信号，又经迷走神经和脊髓传入上行回到脑干、岛叶和杏仁核，进一步参与情绪感受。双向的含义是：压力先改变肠胃状态，肠胃不适又会反过来加重焦虑和低落，形成来回强化的回路。Mood and the gut influence each other through the gut-brain axis, a bidirectional communication network. The brain's limbic system and hypothalamus process stress and send signals down through the autonomic nervous system and endocrine pathways to the gut; gut motility, secretion and sensitivity travel back up vagal and spinal afferents to the brainstem, insula and amygdala, feeding into emotional experience. Bidirectional means stress first alters the gut, and gut discomfort in turn worsens anxiety and low mood in a reinforcing loop.[12]

自主神经怎样调节胃肠，是最直接的下行通路。交感兴奋时抑制胃肠运动和分泌、收紧括约肌；副交感（迷走神经）则促进蠕动和消化液分泌。紧张焦虑时交感占优，蠕动变慢或出现痉挛，胃酸分泌和肠道敏感性发生改变，于是有胃部拧着、没胃口、反复便意或腹胀；放松状态下，消化活动通常更顺畅。The autonomic nervous system is the most direct downward route. Sympathetic activation suppresses gut motility and secretion and tightens sphincters; parasympathetic vagal input promotes peristalsis and digestive secretion. Under tension and anxiety the sympathetic side dominates, slowing or spasming motility and changing acid secretion and intestinal sensitivity, which produces a knotted stomach, poor appetite, repeated urge to defecate or bloating; digestion usually runs more smoothly when relaxed.[8][12]

肠壁内还有独立的肠神经系统，常被称为第二大脑，能完成局部反射。肠内分泌细胞感知腔内营养和微生物产物，释放 5-羟色胺、胆囊收缩素、GLP-1 等信号，激活迷走传入纤维；肠道菌群代谢物、短链脂肪酸和局部免疫状态也参与这条通信，影响大脑的情绪和应激反应。神经、内分泌、免疫和代谢几条通路并行，不只是一条情绪神经在起作用。The gut wall also holds its own enteric nervous system, often called the second brain, which completes local reflexes independently. Enteroendocrine cells sense luminal nutrients and microbial products and release serotonin, CCK, GLP-1 and other signals that activate vagal afferents. Microbial metabolites, short-chain fatty acids and local immune state also participate in this communication, influencing mood and stress responses. Neural, endocrine, immune and metabolic pathways run in parallel rather than a single emotion nerve.[12]

常见功能性表现就在这条轴上反复出现。紧张时胃疼或拉肚子，压力大时便秘，在功能性胃肠问题中很常见；情绪低落、睡眠差和活动减少会改变肠道环境，肠胃不适又降低生活质量，相互加重。需要注意，脑肠轴研究不少来自动物模型和观察性人群证据，个体身上的因果链条需要分开评估，不能把所有胃部不适都归为情绪问题。Common functional symptoms recur along this axis. Stomach pain or diarrhoea when tense, constipation under pressure, are frequent in functional gastrointestinal disorders. Low mood, poor sleep and reduced activity alter the gut environment, while gut discomfort lowers quality of life, each worsening the other. Much gut-brain evidence comes from animal models and observational human data, so causal chains in an individual must be assessed separately; not every stomach symptom should be attributed to mood.[1][12]

边界：情绪相关的胃肠不适不能替代器质性判断。持续腹痛、黑便、便血、不明原因消瘦、吞咽困难、呕血或症状进行性加重时，应就医排查溃疡、炎症、肿瘤等问题；本章不诊断功能性胃肠病，也不给出用药或调药方案。急性剧烈腹痛、发热伴呕吐等情况需要及时线下评估。Boundary: emotion-related gut discomfort cannot replace organic evaluation. Persistent abdominal pain, black or bloody stool, unexplained weight loss, swallowing difficulty, vomiting blood or progressive worsening warrant medical investigation for ulcers, inflammation or tumours. This chapter does not diagnose functional gastrointestinal disorders or prescribe medication. Acute severe abdominal pain with fever and vomiting needs prompt in-person assessment.[1][8]
