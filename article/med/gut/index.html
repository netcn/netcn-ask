胃与消化：自消化、排空和胃鸣The Stomach: Self-Digestion, Emptying, and Rumbles

How does it release food into the small intestine, and why does it rumble when we are hungry? This Hub uses the stomach wall barrier, acid rhythm and gastric emptying to answer five common questions and to separate fasting from fed, and liquid from solid.

01胃为什么不会消化自己？Why doesn't the stomach digest itself?

胃靠黏液—碳酸氢盐层、紧密连接和黏膜血流保护自己；屏障坏了，胃才会被酸和酶伤到。Mucus, bicarbonate, tight junctions and mucosal blood flow protect the stomach; injury begins when the barrier fails.

胃每天都在泡在自己分泌的强酸和蛋白酶里，却通常不会被消化。直接原因是胃壁并不是一层裸露的肉：表面覆盖着黏液和碳酸氢盐，上皮细胞之间由紧密连接封住，黏膜下的血流还会把少量反渗的氢离子带走。再加上胃酸分泌随进食起落，胃腔并不是一缸恒定不变的强酸。这套防线是动态的：黏液不断更新，上皮不断修复，血流持续供能；任何一环掉链子，保护就会打折。The stomach spends the day bathed in the acid and protease it secretes, yet it usually does not digest itself. The immediate reason is that the stomach wall is not a bare sheet of flesh: its surface is covered by mucus and bicarbonate, the epithelial cells are sealed to one another by tight junctions, and mucosal blood flow removes the small amount of hydrogen ion that leaks back. On top of that, acid secretion rises and falls with meals, so the lumen is not a constant vat of strong acid. This defence is dynamic: mucus is renewed, epithelium is repaired, and blood flow supplies energy; if any link fails, protection weakens.[1]

屏障的核心是“黏液—碳酸氢盐层”。表面上皮细胞分泌黏蛋白，形成一层黏稠的凝胶；上皮细胞同时把血液中的碳酸氢根转运到表面，让黏液层外侧仍受酸侵袭，靠近细胞的一侧却被中和到接近中性。胃蛋白酶在酸性环境中才有活性，但这层屏障把酶和活细胞隔开。紧密连接像细胞之间的密封条，黏膜血流则负责冲走已经穿过多余的氢离子。所以它不是一道死墙，而是一层持续更新的活组织。The core of this protection is the mucus–bicarbonate barrier. Surface epithelial cells secrete mucins that form a viscous gel; at the same time, they transport bicarbonate from the blood to the surface. The outer side of the gel still faces acid, but the side next to the cells is neutralised towards a pH close to 7. Pepsin only works in an acidic environment, and the barrier keeps the enzyme away from living cells. Tight junctions act like seals between cells, while mucosal blood flow washes away hydrogen ions that have slipped through. It is not a dead wall but a living, continually renewed layer.[1][2]

胃酸也不是一直满格。壁细胞分泌的盐酸由 H⁺/K⁺-ATPase 泵出，受胃泌素、组胺和乙酰胆碱调控；进食时分泌增加，食物进来后蛋白质和唾液、食物中的水分会把酸缓冲，胃内 pH 常升到 4–5；两餐之间，胃里没有食物，pH 才回到 1.5–3.5 左右。所谓“胃是一缸恒定强酸”，是把空腹和餐后两个状态混为一谈。Acid is not always at maximum either. Parietal cells pump hydrochloric acid out through H⁺/K⁺-ATPase, under the control of gastrin, histamine and acetylcholine. Secretion increases with a meal, but the food itself — especially protein, saliva and the water in food — buffers the acid, and intragastric pH often rises to about 4–5. Between meals, with an empty stomach, pH falls back to roughly 1.5–3.5. The idea of a “constant vat of strong acid” conflates the fasting and fed states.[1][3]

临床证据也支持“屏障第一”。幽门螺杆菌感染、长期使用非甾体抗炎药、严重缺血或应激，都会削弱黏液—碳酸氢盐层或上皮修复，让胃酸和胃蛋白酶有机会直接伤害黏膜，形成胃炎或溃疡。反过来，单有胃酸并不足以解释溃疡：有些人胃酸并不高，黏膜屏障却先坏了。Clinical evidence also puts the barrier first. Helicobacter pylori infection, long-term non-steroidal anti-inflammatory drugs, severe ischaemia or stress can weaken the mucus–bicarbonate layer or epithelial repair, giving acid and pepsin a chance to injure the mucosa and produce gastritis or an ulcer. Conversely, acid alone is not a sufficient explanation: some people with ulcers do not have high acid output, but their mucosal barrier has failed first.[2][4][5]

所以，更准确的说法是：胃通常能保住自己，但不是“绝对不会”被消化。屏障受损、血流不足或修复跟不上时，胃也会受伤。反复胃痛、黑便、呕血或体重下降，不应靠“胃酸多不多”自行判断，要及时就医。尤其要留意突然出现的贫血、吞咽困难或持续呕吐。A more accurate statement, then, is that the stomach usually protects itself, but not absolutely. When the barrier is damaged, blood flow is poor, or repair cannot keep up, the stomach can be injured too. Repeated stomach pain, black stools, vomiting blood or unexplained weight loss should not be self-diagnosed by guessing about acid levels; seek medical care. Anaemia, difficulty swallowing or persistent vomiting deserve particular attention.[1][4]

02胃是怎么排空的？饿了为什么会咕咕叫？How does the stomach empty, and why does it rumble when we're hungry?

胃排空是幽门调控的渐进过程：水约 15–30 分钟排空一半，混合餐 3–4 小时；胃鸣多由气体和液体随蠕动流动产生。Gastric emptying is a pylorus-controlled trickle: water is half-emptied in about 15–30 minutes, a mixed meal takes 3–4 hours, and rumbling mostly comes from gas and liquid moved by

胃不是把食物分成一批批倒出去，也不是一路连续不停地流。更准确地说，它先储存、搅拌、研磨，再由幽门控制，把食糜一小股一小股地送进十二指肠。液体最快，混合餐次之，固体最慢；所以“分批还是连续”本身是个伪二选一。The stomach does not dump food in batches, nor does it stream continuously. More accurately, it stores, mixes and grinds food first, then lets the pylorus release chyme into the duodenum in small pulses. Liquids go fastest, mixed meals next, solids last. “Batch or continuous” is therefore a false choice.[1][2]

清液体几乎没有研磨过程，主要靠胃和十二指肠之间的压力差排空。喝下 500 毫升等渗盐水，约 15–30 分钟就有一半离开胃；含糖、含脂肪的液体则会慢得多。固体食物先要在胃窦被研磨成小于 2 毫米的颗粒，这个“滞后相”可能持续半小时到一小时，之后才以接近线性的速度排空。一顿普通的混合餐，胃通常在 3–4 小时内恢复到接近空腹的状态，但脂肪多、热量高的餐会明显拖长。Clear liquids have almost no grinding phase; they empty mainly because of the pressure gradient between the stomach and the duodenum. After 500 mL of isotonic saline, about half has left the stomach within 15–30 minutes, whereas sugary or fatty liquids empty much more slowly. Solid food must first be triturated in the antrum to particles smaller than 2 mm. This lag phase can last about half an hour to an hour, after which solids empty in a roughly linear way. After an ordinary mixed meal, the stomach usually returns to a near-fasting state within 3–4 hours, but fatty, calorie-dense meals take longer.[1][5]

这套节律由近端胃、远端胃和幽门分工完成。近端胃像储袋，先接纳食物并缓慢收缩；远端胃的蠕动波把食物推向关闭的幽门，形成剪切和研磨；幽门则像阀门，只放行足够细的颗粒和液体。十二指肠也不是被动接收：脂肪、酸、渗透压和过多食糜会触发 CCK、GLP-1、PYY 等信号，抑制胃窦蠕动、收紧幽门，让排空慢下来。The rhythm is shared among the proximal stomach, distal stomach and pylorus. The proximal stomach acts as a reservoir, receiving food and contracting slowly; antral peristaltic waves push food against a closed pylorus, creating shear and grinding; the pylorus acts as a gate, letting through only sufficiently small particles and liquid. The duodenum is not a passive receiver either: fat, acid, osmolarity and excess chyme trigger CCK, GLP-1, PYY and other signals that inhibit antral motility, tighten the pylorus and slow emptying.[1][3]

饿的时候肚子“咕咕”叫，通常不是胃在消化自己，而是气体和液体被蠕动推着走的声音。空腹时，胃和小肠仍会周期性收缩，称为移行性复合运动；胃里残留的气体和少量液体在狭窄处流动，就会发出可听见的声响。吞下的空气、碳酸饮料、肠道产气或乳糖不耐受，也会让声音更明显。它多数时候只是正常运动的副产品，并不等于“胃空了”或“胃病了”。The “rumbling” stomach when you are hungry is usually not the stomach digesting itself. It is the sound of gas and liquid being pushed along by peristalsis. In the fasting state, the stomach and small intestine still contract periodically — the migrating motor complex — and residual gas and a little liquid flowing through narrow segments can produce audible borborygmi. Swallowed air, carbonated drinks, intestinal gas or lactose intolerance can make the sound more obvious. Most of the time it is a by-product of normal movement, not proof that the stomach is empty or diseased.[1][4]

不过，胃排空有明显个体差异，也会被疾病和手术改变。糖尿病胃轻瘫、幽门梗阻、胃部手术后，排空可能过慢或过快；反之，紧张、某些药物和体位也会影响速度。偶尔听到胃鸣不需要紧张；如果同时有持续恶心、呕吐、早饱、明显胀痛或体重下降，就应就医，而不是只凭声音判断。Gastric emptying varies widely between people and changes with illness and surgery. Diabetic gastroparesis, pyloric obstruction or stomach surgery can make emptying too slow or too fast; stress, some drugs and body position can also affect speed. Occasional rumbling is not a reason to worry. If it comes with persistent nausea, vomiting, early fullness, marked bloating or weight loss, seek medical care rather than judging by the sound alone.[2][5]

03胃是不是一缸恒定强酸？Is the stomach basically a constant vat of acid?

不是。胃酸随进食起落，餐后食物把 pH 缓冲到 4–5，空腹才回到 1.5–3.5；它是一个受调控的器官，不是恒定酸缸。No. Acid rises and falls with meals; food buffers pH to about 4–5 after eating, and fasting pH returns to 1.5–3.5. The stomach is regulated, not a constant vat.

不是。胃酸分泌有明确的昼夜和进食节律：吃饭时上升，两餐之间回落；胃里还有食物、唾液和黏液，胃腔并不是一缸纯盐酸。把胃想象成始终满格的酸缸，既不符合生理，也容易把“胃酸多”当成所有胃病的解释。胃酸的强弱还受到胃内食物、黏液和反流的影响，不能只凭“烧心”的感觉判断；这也是为什么不能用同一个“胃酸值”给所有人下结论。No. Acid secretion follows a daily and meal-related rhythm: it rises with eating and falls between meals, and the stomach also contains food, saliva and mucus, so the lumen is not a vat of pure hydrochloric acid. Imagining a permanently full acid tank fits neither physiology nor the way clinicians think about stomach symptoms. Acid strength is also shaped by food, mucus and reflux, so heartburn alone is not a measurement, and one acid value cannot characterise everyone.[1]

真正决定酸度的是壁细胞。壁细胞通过 H⁺/K⁺-ATPase 把氢离子泵进胃腔，胃泌素、组胺和乙酰胆碱是主要开关；进食、蛋白质和胃扩张会推动分泌。胃酸帮助激活胃蛋白酶、杀灭部分细菌，也促进铁和维生素 B12 的吸收，但分泌量有上限，不会无限变强；壁细胞数量、年龄、幽门螺杆菌感染和药物也都会改变分泌能力。Parietal cells set the acidity. They pump hydrogen ions into the lumen through H⁺/K⁺-ATPase, switched mainly by gastrin, histamine and acetylcholine; eating, protein and gastric distension drive secretion. Acid activates pepsin, kills some microbes and helps iron and vitamin B12 absorption, but output has limits; it does not become arbitrarily strong. Parietal-cell number, age, H. pylori infection and drugs also change secretory capacity.[1][3]

食物是最好的缓冲剂。一餐进来，蛋白质、唾液和水分把酸稀释、中和，胃内 pH 常升到 4–5；空腹时没有食物，pH 才回到 1.5–3.5。不同人、不同时间点差异很大，单次胃酸检测也不能代表全天。所谓“胃酸多”，有时只是反流、敏感性或动力问题；它和“胃内酸度高”并不是同一个概念，后者需要 pH 或分泌量数据；这些数值只是人群范围，不是个人诊断线。Food is the best buffer. During a meal, protein, saliva and water dilute and neutralise acid, and intragastric pH often rises to 4–5; when the stomach is empty, pH falls back to 1.5–3.5. Values vary widely between people and across the day, so a single acid measurement cannot represent the whole day. “High acid” is sometimes really reflux, sensitivity or a motility problem; it is not the same as a high intragastric hydrogen-ion concentration, which needs pH or output data; population ranges are not individual diagnostic cut-offs.[1][2]

临床也不把“酸多”当成唯一目标。质子泵抑制剂能强力抑酸，但长期使用要评估必要性；胃酸太少会影响消化和营养吸收，增加部分感染风险。幽门螺杆菌、NSAIDs、胆汁反流和动力异常，都可以在没有高胃酸的情况下造成胃部不适。因此治疗要看病因，而不是一味抑酸或补酸。Clinically, “more acid” is not the only target. Proton-pump inhibitors suppress acid strongly, but long-term use needs review; too little acid can impair digestion and nutrient absorption and increase some infection risks. H. pylori, NSAIDs, bile reflux and motor disorders can all cause stomach symptoms without high acid output. Treatment therefore targets the cause, not simply more or less acid.[3][5]

所以，胃不是一缸恒定强酸，而是一个受神经、激素和食物共同调节的器官。偶尔反酸、烧心不等于胃酸过多；反复症状、吞咽困难、黑便或体重下降，应就医检查，而不是自行长期吃抑酸药；如果正在长期服用抑酸药，也应定期和医生复盘。So the stomach is not a constant vat of strong acid; it is an organ regulated by nerves, hormones and food. Occasional reflux or heartburn does not prove acid excess; persistent symptoms, difficulty swallowing, black stools or weight loss call for medical assessment, not indefinite self-treatment with acid suppressants; long-term acid suppression should be reviewed with a clinician.[1][5]
