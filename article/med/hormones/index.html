01激素疗法为什么能让某些身体部位发育变化，停药后又回缩？Why does hormone therapy change some body parts, and why do some changes recede after stopping?

激素信号持续作用时组织增生或改变分布，停药后部分回退、部分保留。Sustained hormone signals make tissues grow or redistribute; after stopping, some changes recede and some persist.

先给结论：激素疗法不是把身体“重新编程”，而是持续给组织一个信号。乳房、脂肪、肌肉、皮肤和毛囊对雌激素或雄激素敏感，信号在时它们会增生、改变分布；停药后信号撤掉，一部分变化退回，另一部分已经被组织“记住”，不会完全复原。Start with the result: hormone therapy does not reprogram the body; it gives tissues a sustained signal. Breasts, fat, muscle, skin and hair follicles respond to estrogen or testosterone. While the signal is present, tissues proliferate or redistribute; after it stops, some changes recede, while others are “remembered” by the tissue and do not fully reverse.[1][2]

以雌激素为例：它和乳腺、脂肪、皮肤里的受体结合，促进乳腺腺体与结缔组织发育，让脂肪更多沉积在臀部和大腿，也让皮肤保水、变软。雄激素则促进肌肉蛋白合成、声带增厚、胡须和体毛生长。这里的关键不是“有激素就变”，而是组织有没有受体、敏感度多高、暴露多久；同样的剂量，不同人的反应可以差很多。Take estrogen: it binds receptors in the breast, fat and skin, promotes mammary gland and connective-tissue development, shifts fat toward the hips and thighs, and makes skin softer and more hydrated. Testosterone promotes muscle protein synthesis, vocal-fold thickening, beard and body-hair growth. What matters is not simply “hormone present” but whether the tissue has receptors, how sensitive it is and how long it is exposed; the same dose can produce very different responses.[1][2]

哪些会回退？Cornell Health 的患者手册写得很清楚：雌激素带来的乳房发育属于永久变化，停药后乳房可能缩小，但不会完全消失；脂肪分布、肌肉量、皮肤软硬和油脂分泌属于可逆变化，停药几周到几个月可能回到基线；声音、骨结构、喉结和已经长出的终毛通常不会因停药而逆转。青春期也是同一套逻辑：NICHD 指出女孩约 8–13 岁、男孩约 9–14 岁进入青春期，生长突增、痤疮和体毛出现，背后是性激素脉冲增强，而不是单一激素“开关”。What reverses? Cornell Health's patient guide is explicit: breast growth from estrogen is permanent; breasts may shrink after stopping but will not disappear completely. Fat distribution, muscle mass, skin softness and oiliness are reversible and may return toward baseline over weeks to months. Voice, bone structure, the Adam's apple and terminal hair that has already grown usually do not reverse after stopping. Puberty follows the same logic: NICHD notes that girls usually start around ages 8–13 and boys around 9–14, with growth spurts, acne and body hair driven by stronger pulsatile sex-hormone signals, not a single “switch.”[1][3][5]

那胸大就等于雌激素多吗？不是。OpenStax 的解剖学教材指出，乳房由乳腺腺体和脂肪组织构成，脂肪多少在很大程度上决定乳房大小，乳房大小也不影响产奶量；雌激素确实刺激乳房发育，但血清雌激素水平随月经周期波动，不能用一个罩杯反推。1997 年《American Journal of Epidemiology》对 65 名未孕年轻女性的研究也发现，乳房大小与体重、身高、BMI 相关，黄体期与雌二醇、孕酮有相关性，但关系不是简单的“越高越大”。So does a larger cup size mean more estrogen? No. OpenStax's anatomy text explains that the breast is made of glandular tissue and fat; fat largely determines breast size, and size does not affect milk production. Estrogen does stimulate breast development, but serum estrogen fluctuates across the menstrual cycle and cannot be inferred from a cup size. A 1997 American Journal of Epidemiology study of 65 nulligravid women aged 19–25 found breast size correlated with weight, height and BMI, and with estradiol and progesterone in the luteal phase, but the relationship was not a simple “higher hormone, larger breast.”[5][6]

睾酮与男孩行为的关系同样被过度简化。青春期男孩的睾酮产量约增加 30 倍，但 Duke 等的系统综述纳入 27 项研究，只有 1 项是纵向研究；横断面研究常看到睾酮与攻击性的相关，纵向证据却不足以确认“睾酮升高必然导致行为改变”。行为还受家庭、同伴、睡眠、情绪和社会期待影响。边界是：激素治疗有明确的医学监测和个体化方案，不能根据网络上的外观对比自行用药；如果出现声音、月经、生育或情绪方面的困扰，应找内分泌或生殖专科评估。Testosterone and boys' behavior are likewise oversimplified. During puberty, boys' testosterone production rises about 30-fold, but Duke et al.'s systematic review found 27 studies, only one longitudinal. Cross-sectional studies often report associations with aggression, but longitudinal evidence is insufficient to confirm that rising testosterone necessarily changes behavior. Family, peers, sleep, mood and social expectations also matter. The boundary: hormone therapy requires medical monitoring and individualized regimens; do not self-prescribe based on online body comparisons. Voice, menstrual, fertility or mood concerns deserve an endocrinology or reproductive-health assessment.[3][4]

02全民补碘以后，甲状腺肿大还常见吗？Is goiter still common after universal salt iodization?

补碘消除了碘缺乏性甲状腺肿带，但碘充足地区仍有桥本、结节等病因。Iodization removed the iodine-deficiency goiter belt, but Hashimoto, nodules and other causes remain in iodine-sufficient areas.

先给结论：全民补碘把“甲状腺肿带”压下去了，但没有让甲状腺肿从此消失。缺碘仍然是全球甲状腺肿最常见的原因，可是在碘充足的国家，桥本甲状腺炎、结节性甲状腺肿、Graves 病等自身免疫或结构性原因接棒，成为更常见的病因。换句话说，补碘解决的是“原料不够”，解决不了“甲状腺自己出问题”。Start with the result: universal salt iodization pushed the “goiter belt” down, but did not make goiter disappear. Iodine deficiency remains the most common cause of goiter worldwide, yet in iodine-sufficient countries Hashimoto thyroiditis, nodular goiter, Graves' disease and other autoimmune or structural causes take over. In other words, iodized salt fixes a missing raw material; it cannot fix a thyroid that has become the problem.[7][8]

碘是合成甲状腺激素的原料。摄入不足时，甲状腺激素合成下降，垂体分泌更多 TSH 来刺激甲状腺，甲状腺滤泡细胞增生、腺体变大，这就是代偿性甲状腺肿。StatPearls 的数据说，全球约 22 亿人受碘缺乏影响；轻度缺碘地区甲状腺肿患病率约 5%–20%，中度 20%–30%，重度可超过 30%。美国在 1920 年代以前，五大湖、阿巴拉契亚和西北部就是著名的“甲状腺肿带”，加碘盐推广后基本消失。Iodine is the raw material for thyroid hormone. When intake is low, hormone synthesis falls, the pituitary secretes more TSH to stimulate the gland, and follicular cells proliferate and enlarge the thyroid—compensatory goiter. StatPearls estimates about 2.2 billion people are affected by iodine deficiency; goiter prevalence is roughly 5–20% in mild deficiency, 20–30% in moderate deficiency and above 30% in severe deficiency. Before the 1920s, the Great Lakes, Appalachian and northwestern United States were a famous “goiter belt”; iodized salt largely eliminated it.[7][8][9]

但补碘不是一劳永逸。WHO 与 UNICEF 在 1993 年推荐 universal salt iodization，2014 年的指南估计全球 120 多个国家实施食盐加碘，约 71% 的家庭能获得合格加碘盐，但仍有约 18.8 亿人面临碘摄入不足的风险。食盐加碘的覆盖率在不同国家、不同加工食品之间差别很大；如果只给餐桌盐加碘、加工食品不用加碘盐，人群仍可能缺碘。But iodization is not a one-time fix. WHO and UNICEF recommended universal salt iodization in 1993; a 2014 guideline estimated that more than 120 countries fortify salt and about 71% of households have adequately iodized salt, yet about 1.88 billion people remain at risk of insufficient intake. Coverage varies by country and by processed food; if only table salt is iodized and processed foods use non-iodized salt, populations can still be short.[9][10]

那“胯宽好生养”呢？这句话把外在臀宽和骨盆内径混为一谈。StatPearls 的骨盆解剖条目指出，女性骨盆确实更宽、更浅，耻骨下角更大，骨盆出口横径约 8–11 cm、前后径约 9.5–12.5 cm；但分娩是否顺利，取决于骨盆入口、中骨盆和出口的内径与形状，以及胎儿大小、胎位、宫缩和产程管理，不是看臀部多宽。骨盆形状还分多种类型，混合型很常见。What about “wide hips mean easy childbirth”? That sentence conflates visible hip width with the internal dimensions of the pelvis. StatPearls' pelvic-outlet entry notes that the female pelvis is wider and shallower, with a more obtuse subpubic angle; the outlet's transverse diameter is about 8–11 cm and its anteroposterior diameter about 9.5–12.5 cm. But labor depends on the inlet, midpelvis and outlet—their dimensions and shape—plus fetal size, position, contractions and labor management, not on how wide the buttocks look. Pelvic shapes include gynaecoid, android, anthropoid and platypelloid, and mixed types are common.[10][11]

更关键的是，外在形态与生育力的证据很弱。2025 年 Cambridge 的系统综述纳入 19 篇文章、68 个效应量、125,062 人，结论是现有证据不足以支持“女性化的外貌特征=生育潜力”；腰臀比与生育的关系甚至常常相反，较高的腰臀比更可能反映已经生育过，而不是生育力更强；乳房大小与生育的研究结果互相矛盾。边界：胯宽可能来自骨骼、脂肪分布或姿势，既不能证明好生，也不能证明难产；真正需要评估时，产科医生看的是骨盆、胎儿和产程，而不是体型。More importantly, the evidence linking outer shape to fertility is weak. A 2025 Cambridge systematic review pooled 19 articles, 68 effect sizes and 125,062 people and concluded that the evidence is too weak to support “feminine morphological traits = reproductive potential.” Waist-to-hip ratio often relates in the opposite direction: a higher ratio may reflect having already given birth, not greater fertility; breast-size studies conflict. Boundary: wide hips may come from bone, fat distribution or posture, and neither prove easy nor difficult birth; when evaluation is needed, obstetricians assess the pelvis, the fetus and the course of labor—not body shape.[8][11]

03未成熟的卵子去了哪里？大熊猫和跨物种受精为何这么难？Where do immature eggs go, and why are panda breeding and cross-species fertilization so difficult?

卵子在卵巢内闭锁；大熊猫受孕窗口仅 24–72 小时；跨物种杂交多受阻于受精与胚胎发育。Eggs undergo atresia in the ovary; pandas have a 24–72 hour window; cross-species hybrids usually fail at fertilization or development.

先回答“未成熟的卵死了去哪”：它们大多在卵巢里被“闭锁”处理掉，而不是排到体外。女性胎儿期形成的卵泡，在一生中不断有卵母细胞凋亡、卵泡闭锁，残余组织被卵巢吸收、改建；只有排卵时排出的那一颗卵，才可能走到输卵管，遇精子受精。若未受精，排卵后形成的黄体退化，子宫内膜失去激素支持而脱落，形成月经。Start with the question “where do immature eggs go”: most are disposed of inside the ovary by atresia, not expelled from the body. Follicles formed during fetal life continually lose oocytes to apoptosis and follicular atresia; the remaining tissue is absorbed and remodeled by the ovary. Only the egg released at ovulation can travel into the fallopian tube and meet sperm. If it is not fertilized, the corpus luteum regresses and the endometrium loses hormonal support and sheds—menstruation.[5][12][13]

数量上很直观：StatPearls 说胎儿 20 周时卵巢里约 600–700 万个卵母细胞，出生后降到约 100–200 万，青春期约 30–40 万，一生只有 400–500 个真正排卵；ASRM 则给出出生时约 50 万–100 万个、闭锁与排卵共同消耗的数字。两组数字的起点和口径不同，但方向一致：绝大多数卵子从没机会排卵，就在卵巢里退化了。ASRM 还提醒，卵巢储备指标能预测取卵数，却不能单独预测自然怀孕的机会。The numbers are stark: StatPearls notes about 6–7 million oocytes at 20 weeks of fetal life, roughly 1–2 million at birth, 300,000–400,000 by reproductive age, and only 400–500 ovulated in a lifetime; ASRM gives about 500,000–1 million at birth, with atresia and ovulation both depleting the pool. The starting points and definitions differ, but the direction is the same: the vast majority of eggs never ovulate and degenerate in the ovary. ASRM also cautions that ovarian-reserve tests predict egg yield, not natural fertility on their own.[12][13]

大熊猫为什么不“直接人工授精”？其实人工授精一直在用，而且史密森尼国家动物园已有 4 次成功：2005 年 Tai Shan、2013 年 Bao Bao、2015 年 Bei Bei、2020 年 Xiao Qi Ji。难点不在“会不会做”，而在“什么时候做”：雌性大熊猫每年春季只排卵一次，可受孕窗口只有 24–72 小时，错过就要再等一年；它们还会出现假孕，激素和行为能提示发情，却不能保证怀上。Why not “just artificially inseminate” giant pandas? Artificial insemination is already used, and Smithsonian's National Zoo has had four successful births: Tai Shan in 2005, Bao Bao in 2013, Bei Bei in 2015 and Xiao Qi Ji in 2020. The difficulty is not whether to do it but when: females ovulate once a year in spring, the fertile window is only 24–72 hours, and missing it means waiting another year. Pandas can also show pseudopregnancy; hormones and behavior can signal estrus but cannot guarantee conception.[14]

就算受精成功，大熊猫的繁殖仍然慢：通常一胎生两个，但常常只有一个存活；幼崽出生时只有 3–5 盎司，约一根黄油大小，母亲要带 1.5–3 年，野生雌性至少隔一年才能再繁殖。人工授精能绕过自然交配失败，却不能把“每年一次、窗口极短、幼崽难养”变成“随时能生”。所以不是“不授精”，而是授精只是众多瓶颈中的一环。Even after fertilization, panda reproduction remains slow: litters usually contain two cubs but often only one survives; a newborn weighs 3–5 ounces, about the size of a stick of butter, and the mother invests one and a half to three years, so a wild female can reproduce at best every other year. Assisted insemination can bypass failed natural mating, but it cannot turn “once a year, a tiny window, difficult cubs” into “on demand.” The problem is not that insemination is unused; it is one bottleneck among many.[14]

不同物种的精子和卵子相遇，通常连受精都到不了：精子要识别卵子表面的分子、穿透卵丘和透明带，物种差异会让这些步骤失败。即使形成杂交受精卵，合子后隔离也可能让胚胎在早期死亡、发育异常，或让后代不育——马和驴的骡就是例子：马 64 条、驴 62 条染色体，骡有 63 条，减数分裂时难以正常配对，通常不能产生可育配子。边界：杂交不是“一定失败”，植物、鱼类和少数哺乳动物都有例外；但把不同物种的配子随意组合，既有生物学屏障，也有伦理和法律边界。When sperm and egg from different species meet, fertilization often fails before it starts: sperm must recognize egg-surface molecules and penetrate the cumulus and zona pellucida, and species differences block those steps. Even if a hybrid zygote forms, postzygotic isolation can cause early embryo death, abnormal development or sterile offspring—the mule is an example: horses have 64 chromosomes, donkeys 62, and mules 63, so meiotic pairing is abnormal and viable gametes usually fail. Boundary: hybridization is not guaranteed to fail; plants, fish and a few mammals have exceptions. But combining gametes across species at will faces biological barriers as well as ethical and legal limits.[15][16]

04为什么糖尿病会让人变瘦、还容易饿？Why does diabetes cause weight loss and persistent hunger?

胰岛素缺乏切断细胞摄糖，脂解与酮体生成占上风，脂肪和肌肉持续分解。Insulin deficiency blocks cellular glucose uptake; lipolysis and ketogenesis dominate and fat and muscle break down.

糖尿病让人变瘦，核心不是消耗变大，而是胰岛素不足把代谢从储存切换成了分解。胰岛素是合成代谢激素：正常进食后它促进肌肉和脂肪细胞摄取葡萄糖，把多余糖合成糖原、脂肪酸和蛋白质储存起来，同时抑制脂肪组织里的脂肪分解。胰岛素缺乏或作用不足时，这条储存通路运转不动，身体拿不到可利用的糖，只能动用自身储备。Losing weight in diabetes is not mainly about burning more calories; insulin deficiency switches metabolism from storage to breakdown. Insulin is an anabolic hormone: after a meal it promotes glucose uptake by muscle and fat cells, converts excess sugar into glycogen, fatty acids and protein for storage, and suppresses fat breakdown in adipose tissue. When insulin is absent or ineffective, this storage pathway stalls, cells cannot use available glucose, and the body has to draw on its own reserves.[17][23]

细胞摄糖受阻，血糖留在血里，脂肪组织的脂解却失去抑制。甘油三酯被分解成游离脂肪酸和甘油，脂肪酸进入肝脏被加工成酮体；酮体包括乙酰乙酸、β-羟丁酸和丙酮，可以作为部分组织的替代燃料。1 型糖尿病以胰岛 β 细胞自身免疫破坏、胰岛素绝对缺乏为特征，新发时典型表现就是多饮、多尿、多食、不明原因体重下降和乏力；2 型糖尿病早期常有胰岛素抵抗，体重下降通常不如 1 型典型。With cells blocked from taking up glucose, blood sugar stays high while adipose lipolysis loses its brake. Triglycerides split into free fatty acids and glycerol; fatty acids travel to the liver and are processed into ketone bodies, including acetoacetate, beta-hydroxybutyrate and acetone, which serve as an alternative fuel for some tissues. Type 1 diabetes results from autoimmune destruction of pancreatic beta cells with absolute insulin deficiency, and new cases typically present with polydipsia, polyuria, polyphagia, unintentional weight loss and fatigue. Type 2 diabetes often begins with insulin resistance, so weight loss is usually less striking than in type 1.[17][18]

为什么明明吃得多还是瘦？多食来自细胞层面的能量饥饿：血糖虽高，却进不了肌肉和脂肪细胞，能量信号仍在发出进食指令。与此同时，葡萄糖随尿液排出带走热量，脂肪和蛋白质持续被分解供能，肌肉量下降。HbA1c（糖化血红蛋白）反映过去约 2–3 个月的平均血糖，长期偏高提示分解代谢持续占上风；体重快速下降常出现在血糖控制很差的阶段。Why does appetite stay high while weight falls? Polyphagia reflects energy starvation at the cellular level: glucose remains high in blood but cannot enter muscle and fat, so energy signals still call for food. Meanwhile glucose carries calories away in urine, and fat and protein keep being catabolized for fuel, so muscle mass drops. HbA1c, glycated hemoglobin, reflects average blood glucose over about two to three months; persistently high values indicate that breakdown metabolism has been dominating. Rapid weight loss usually appears when glucose control is very poor.[18][23]

脂解失控如果继续加速，酮体产量超过肝脏和组织的利用、缓冲能力，就会出现酮症甚至糖尿病酮症酸中毒。酮体是酸性物质，堆积后血液 pH 下降，伴随脱水和电解质紊乱，表现为恶心、呕吐、呼吸深快、呼气有烂苹果味，严重时意识模糊。这是急症状态，需要及时医疗处理；普通消瘦本身不等于酮症，要看血糖、血酮或尿酮和酸碱状态。If uncontrolled lipolysis keeps accelerating, ketone production outpaces hepatic and tissue use and buffering capacity, leading to ketosis or diabetic ketoacidosis. Ketones are acidic; accumulation lowers blood pH and comes with dehydration and electrolyte disturbance, producing nausea, vomiting, deep rapid breathing and a fruity breath odor, and in severe cases confusion. This is an emergency requiring prompt medical care. Ordinary leanness alone does not equal ketosis, and the distinction depends on glucose, blood or urine ketones and acid-base status.[17][18]

边界需要写清：本章只解释变瘦的机制，不给出用药或饮食方案。体重下降也可见于甲状腺功能亢进、肠道吸收障碍、抑郁和刻意节食，不能单凭瘦就判断糖尿病；短期内不明原因体重明显下降、反复口渴多尿或乏力，应做血糖和 HbA1c 等评估。在胰岛素严重缺乏时，少吃并不能把体重压回去，因为问题是激素通路，而不只是摄入多少。The boundary: this chapter explains the mechanism of weight loss and gives no medication or diet plan. Weight loss also occurs in hyperthyroidism, malabsorption, depression and deliberate restriction, so thinness alone cannot identify diabetes. Unexplained marked weight loss with recurrent thirst, urination or fatigue warrants blood glucose and HbA1c testing. In severe insulin deficiency, eating less cannot force weight back down, because the problem lies in the hormone pathway, not only in how much food enters.[17][23]

05为什么血糖高会让人多尿、又不停口渴？Why does high blood glucose cause frequent urination and constant thirst?

血糖超过肾糖阈后糖随尿排出，渗透性利尿带走水分和电解质，脱水刺激口渴中枢。Glucose above the renal threshold spills into urine; osmotic diuresis loses water and electrolytes, and dehydration drives thirst.

多尿多饮是高血糖最经典的组合，机制按顺序发生：血糖先超过肾脏的重吸收能力，糖随尿排出并带走水分，身体脱水后再触发口渴。理解这条链条，关键是分清肾脏的处理阈值和尿液里的渗透效应，而不是把口渴当成单纯的喝水习惯问题。Polyuria and polydipsia are the classic high-glucose combination, and the mechanism unfolds in order: blood sugar first exceeds the kidney's reabsorption capacity, sugar leaves in urine and carries water with it, and dehydration then triggers thirst. Understanding this chain means distinguishing the renal threshold from the osmotic effect in urine, rather than treating thirst as a drinking habit issue.[17][23]

正常情况下，血液经肾小球滤过后，葡萄糖在近端小管被几乎全部重吸收回血液。当血糖升高超过肾糖阈，大约 10 mmol/L（约 180 mg/dL），小管液里的葡萄糖浓度超过转运上限，剩下的糖留在尿液中。小管液渗透压随之升高，水分跟随溶质留在管腔里，不能像平常那样被回吸收，这就是渗透性利尿。尿量因此明显增多，夜尿也可能变多。Under normal conditions, blood filtered by the glomerulus has its glucose almost entirely reabsorbed back into the blood in the proximal tubule. When glucose rises above the renal threshold, about 10 mmol/L or 180 mg/dL, luminal glucose exceeds transport capacity and the remainder stays in urine. Tubular fluid osmolarity then rises, keeping water in the lumen instead of reabsorbing it, and this is osmotic diuresis. Urine volume rises markedly, and nocturia may increase as well.[17][18]

水分大量丢失的同时，钠、钾等电解质随尿液一起排出。葡萄糖渗透性利尿还会干扰肾小管对离子的处理，血容量下降、血浆渗透压升高；如果补液不及时，人会出现口干、乏力、头晕等脱水表现。1 型糖尿病新发或血糖控制很差时，脱水可以与酮症酸中毒叠加出现，属于需要尽快处理的状态。As water is lost, sodium, potassium and other electrolytes are carried out with it. Glucose-driven diuresis also interferes with tubular ion handling; blood volume falls and plasma osmolarity rises. Without timely fluid replacement, dry mouth, fatigue and dizziness follow. In new-onset type 1 diabetes or very poor control, dehydration can overlap with ketoacidosis and demands prompt treatment.[17][23]

口渴是身体的补偿动作。血浆渗透压升高和血容量减少刺激下丘脑的口渴中枢，产生主动饮水的冲动；喝水补充后血容量回升，多尿造成的失水被暂时对冲。糖尿病患者的多饮多尿因此形成循环：高血糖排糖排水，脱水刺激喝水，喝水再稀释尿液。进食高糖饮食后短时的口渴多尿与慢性高血糖不是同一回事，后者持续存在且血糖检查异常。夜间平卧后肾脏灌注增加，夜尿增多也常成为早期被注意到的信号。Thirst is the body's compensatory move. Raised plasma osmolarity and reduced volume stimulate the hypothalamic thirst center and drive active drinking; water intake restores volume and temporarily offsets the fluid lost to urine. The polyuria-polydipsia loop therefore runs: high glucose excretes sugar and water, dehydration prompts drinking, and drinking dilutes the urine again. Brief thirst after a sugary meal differs from chronic hyperglycemia, which persists and shows abnormal glucose tests.[17][18]

边界需要写清：本章只讲水分和糖在肾脏的处理机制，不讨论降糖方案。多尿也可见于尿崩症、利尿剂使用、大量饮水习惯和肾脏疾病；口渴也可由干燥环境、发热或脱水引起。出现持续多饮多尿、夜尿增多、体重下降，或尿液异常伴乏力、意识改变时，应检测血糖、尿糖和电解质；本章描述的量级来自健康成人与糖尿病患者的典型测量，个体阈值和反应存在差异。The boundary: this chapter describes renal handling of sugar and water and discusses no treatment regimen. Polyuria also appears in diabetes insipidus, diuretic use, high fluid intake habits and renal disease, and thirst can follow a dry environment, fever or dehydration. Persistent drinking and urination, nocturia, weight loss, or abnormal urine with fatigue and altered consciousness warrant glucose, urine glucose and electrolyte testing. The figures cited are typical measurements in healthy adults and people with diabetes, and individual thresholds and responses vary.[17][23]

06糖尿病患者为什么会出现低血糖？Why do people with diabetes develop low blood glucose?

降糖药、进食、运动和饮酒失衡使血糖跌破 70 mg/dL，先出交感症状，再出现脑供糖不足。Imbalance among drugs, meals, exercise and alcohol pushes glucose below 70 mg/dL; sympathetic symptoms precede neuroglycopenia.

低血糖是糖尿病管理中最容易被患者直接感知的急性事件：血糖跌得太快或太低，身体先发出交感警报，大脑随后因为供糖不足而出现功能异常。它不是血糖越高越危险那么简单，药物、进食、运动和饮酒之间的平衡一旦被打破，低血糖就可能出现。Hypoglycemia is the acute event most directly felt in diabetes care: when glucose falls too fast or too low, the body first raises a sympathetic alarm, and the brain then malfunctions under insufficient sugar supply. Danger does not run one way with high glucose; once the balance among medication, meals, exercise and alcohol breaks down, low blood glucose can appear.[20][23]

定义上，血浆葡萄糖低于约 70 mg/dL（3.9 mmol/L）进入低血糖区间；症状往往要到更低水平才显现。低于约 55 mg/dL 时，肾上腺素能和交感兴奋症状出现：出汗、心慌、颤抖、饥饿、焦虑、面色苍白。血糖继续降到约 48–50 mg/dL 以下，脑供糖不足，出现头晕、注意力涣散、言语不清、视物模糊，严重时意识模糊甚至昏迷。反复发生低血糖的人，症状阈值可能被调低，早期感知变迟钝。By definition, plasma glucose below about 70 mg/dL, or 3.9 mmol/L, enters the hypoglycemic range, though symptoms often appear only at lower levels. Below roughly 55 mg/dL, adrenergic and sympathetic symptoms emerge: sweating, palpitations, tremor, hunger, anxiety and pallor. As glucose continues below about 48 to 50 mg/dL, the brain lacks fuel, producing dizziness, poor concentration, slurred speech and blurred vision, and in severe cases confusion or coma. People with recurrent hypoglycemia may have their symptom threshold lowered, so early perception blunts.[17][20]

常见诱因分几类。外源性胰岛素或磺脲类、格列奈类促泌剂直接推动血糖下降；进食不足、推迟进餐或运动量突然增大消耗增多；饮酒抑制肝脏糖异生；肝肾功能变化影响药物清除。1 型糖尿病和使用促泌剂的 2 型患者风险更高；空腹过夜后的夜间低血糖可能只表现为噩梦、出汗后浸湿衣物或晨起乏力。Common triggers fall into groups. Exogenous insulin or sulfonylurea and meglitinide secretagogues drive glucose down directly; missed or delayed meals and a sudden increase in exercise raise consumption; alcohol suppresses hepatic gluconeogenesis; changes in liver or kidney function alter drug clearance. Type 1 diabetes and type 2 patients on secretagogues carry higher risk. Nocturnal hypoglycemia overnight may show only as nightmares, sweat-soaked clothing or morning fatigue.[20][23]

判断低血糖要看 Whipple 三联征：有低血糖症状、当时测得低血糖、补充葡萄糖后症状缓解。只凭心慌出汗不能下结论，因为焦虑、贫血、心律失常也可有类似表现；糖尿病患者出现不明原因意识改变时，现场测血糖是第一步。长期反复低血糖会削弱神经感知和自我处理能力，频繁发作需要由专科评估药物与饮食运动安排。Diagnosis rests on the Whipple triad: compatible symptoms, a measured low glucose at the time, and relief after glucose is given. Palpitations and sweating alone cannot establish hypoglycemia, because anxiety, anemia and arrhythmia can resemble it; when a person with diabetes has unexplained altered consciousness, checking glucose on site is the first step. Repeated episodes erode perception and self-management ability, and frequent events warrant specialist review of medication, diet and exercise.[17][20]

边界需要写清：本章只解释低血糖的机制与症状分层，不给出调药指令。非糖尿病者长时间禁食、剧烈运动后也可出现一过性低血糖，但反复发作或空腹低血糖需要排查胰岛素分泌异常等其他病因。出现意识不清、抽搐或补糖后仍不缓解，属于需要紧急医疗处理的情况；本章数值为常见临床阈值，个体症状出现的血糖水平可以不同。The boundary: this chapter explains the mechanism and symptom layers of hypoglycemia and gives no medication adjustment. Non-diabetic people can have transient low glucose after prolonged fasting or heavy exercise, but recurrent or fasting hypoglycemia requires evaluation for insulin secretion disorders and other causes. Unconsciousness, seizure or failure to improve after sugar are emergencies needing urgent medical care. The values given are common clinical thresholds, and the glucose level at which an individual notices symptoms can differ.[20][23]

07食盐里为什么要加碘？Why is iodine added to table salt?

碘是甲状腺激素原料，缺碘时 TSH 升高刺激滤泡增生形成甲状腺肿；加碘盐补足原料。Iodine is the raw material for thyroid hormone; deficiency raises TSH, drives follicular proliferation and goiter, and iodized salt supplies it.

食盐加碘解决的是一个原料问题：碘是合成甲状腺激素的必需原料，原料长期不够，甲状腺会在 TSH 刺激下代偿性增大。全民食盐加碘的历史背景是缺碘地区广泛存在的地方性甲状腺肿；理解加碘盐，要先看清碘、激素合成和甲状腺代偿之间的因果，而不是把甲状腺肿简单归为盐的问题。Iodized salt addresses a raw-material problem: iodine is essential for synthesizing thyroid hormone, and a chronic shortage drives compensatory enlargement under TSH stimulation. Universal iodization arose against a backdrop of endemic goiter in iodine-deficient regions. To understand iodized salt, the causal chain of iodine, hormone synthesis and thyroid compensation must be clear first, rather than blaming salt for goiter.[7][8]

碘进入人体后被甲状腺滤泡摄取，用来合成甲状腺素 T4 和三碘甲腺原氨酸 T3。饮食碘长期不足时，激素合成原料短缺，血中 T3、T4 水平偏低；垂体通过促甲状腺激素（TSH）感知到这一反馈，分泌更多 TSH 持续刺激甲状腺。滤泡细胞反复增生、腺体变大，以在原料不足时尽量维持激素产量，形成代偿性甲状腺肿；严重缺碘地区还会影响胎儿和儿童的神经发育。Iodine is taken up by thyroid follicular cells and used to make thyroxine T4 and triiodothyronine T3. When dietary iodine stays low, raw material runs short and circulating T3 and T4 fall. The pituitary senses this feedback through thyroid-stimulating hormone, or TSH, and secretes more of it to keep stimulating the gland. Follicular cells proliferate repeatedly and the gland enlarges, trying to maintain hormone output despite limited material; this is compensatory goiter. Severe deficiency also affects fetal and childhood neurodevelopment.[7][8][9]

WHO 与 UNICEF 在 1993 年推荐 universal salt iodization，即在食盐中统一加碘。指南估计全球 120 多个国家实施了食盐加碘，约 71% 的家庭能获得合格加碘盐，但仍有约 18.8 亿人面临摄入不足风险。加碘盐把微量碘固定在日常咸味来源里，成本低、覆盖广，是人群层面纠正缺碘的主要手段；覆盖率在不同国家和加工食品之间差别很大。WHO and UNICEF recommended universal salt iodization in 1993, adding a fixed trace amount of iodine to table salt. A guideline estimated that more than 120 countries fortify salt, about 71% of households have adequately iodized salt, yet roughly 1.88 billion people remain at risk of insufficient intake. Iodized salt puts a small iodine dose into the daily seasoning that most people use, at low cost and broad coverage, making it the main population-level measure against deficiency. Coverage varies by country and by processed-food use.[8][9]

需要分清的是，碘充足以后甲状腺肿并没有从病因清单上消失。缺碘仍是全球甲状腺肿最常见的原因，但在碘充足国家，桥本甲状腺炎等自身免疫病因、结节性甲状腺肿和 Graves 病接棒成为更常见的原因。StatPearls 的资料显示，女性甲状腺肿患病率约为男性的四倍；轻度缺碘地区甲状腺肿患病率约 5%–20%，中度 20%–30%，重度可超过 30%。It is important to separate this from the fact that goiter did not disappear after iodine sufficiency. Iodine deficiency remains the most common global cause of goiter, but in iodine-sufficient countries Hashimoto thyroiditis and other autoimmune causes, nodular goiter and Graves disease take over as more common causes. StatPearls notes goiter is about four times more common in women than men; prevalence runs roughly 5%–20% in mild deficiency, 20%–30% in moderate deficiency and above 30% in severe deficiency.[8][9]

边界需要写清：本章只解释加碘盐的机制与背景，不评价具体补碘剂量。碘摄入过高或过低都可能影响甲状腺，已有甲状腺疾病者是否额外补碘应由内分泌评估。盐的其他成分、钾钠摄入和血压管理是另一组问题，不能与碘缺乏混在一起讨论。本章引用的患病率与覆盖率来自人群流行病学资料，个体甲状腺状态需要结合超声、甲功和尿碘等检查判断。The boundary: this chapter explains the mechanism and background of iodized salt and does not recommend an individual iodine dose. Both too little and too much iodine can affect the thyroid, and people with existing thyroid disease should ask endocrinology about extra iodine. Sodium and potassium intake and blood pressure are a separate set of issues and should not be mixed with iodine deficiency. The prevalence and coverage figures come from population epidemiology; an individual's thyroid status is judged with ultrasound, thyroid function tests and urinary iodine.[7][9]

08甲状腺结节为什么体检时越来越多？Why are thyroid nodules found more often on check-ups?

超声可检出 2 mm 病灶，检出率远高于触诊；多数结节良性，按影像特征分层评估。Ultrasound detects 2 mm lesions far better than palpation; most nodules are benign and assessed by imaging risk stratification.

体检报告里甲状腺结节越来越常见，主要原因之一是筛查手段变得更灵敏：触诊摸不到的小结节，高分辨超声一照就能发现。检出率上升不等于甲状腺癌在短时间内翻倍，结节和癌也不是一回事；多数甲状腺结节为良性，需要评估的是其中少数具有可疑特征的病灶。The growing number of thyroid nodules on check-up reports is partly a screening artifact: small nodules missed by palpation are readily detected by high-resolution ultrasound. Rising detection does not mean thyroid cancer doubled in a short time, and a nodule is not the same as cancer. Most nodules are benign; only a minority with suspicious features need evaluation.[8][19]

甲状腺结节是甲状腺内与周围组织不同的局灶性结构，可以是实性、囊性或混合性。单纯靠体格检查触诊，只能摸到较大、位置表浅的结节，成人触诊检出率大约 4%–7%；而高分辨超声可以探及小至约 2 mm 的病灶，在成年人群中的检出率约 20%–76%。过去体检普及触诊、后来普遍加做超声，同一批人群被发现的结节数量自然上升；年龄增长和女性也都是检出率更高的相关因素。A thyroid nodule is a focal structure differing from surrounding tissue, and it may be solid, cystic or mixed. Physical palpation alone reaches only large, superficial lesions, with an adult detection rate of about 4%–7%. High-resolution ultrasound, by contrast, can identify lesions as small as about 2 mm and detects nodules in roughly 20%–76% of adults. As check-ups moved from palpation toward routine ultrasound, the number found in the same population naturally rose. Older age and female sex are also associated with higher detection.[7][19]

超声评估看的是一组形态特征：结节大小、边界、回声、有无微钙化、纵横比和血流情况，而不是只看有没有结节。良性结节可以是囊性、边界清楚、回声均匀；可疑特征包括低回声、微钙化、边缘不规则、纵横比大于一和被膜侵犯等。影像分层后，医生再决定随访观察还是细针穿刺；穿刺活检取细胞做病理判断，是区分良恶性的关键步骤。Ultrasound assesses a set of morphologic features: size, margins, echogenicity, microcalcifications, taller-than-wide shape and vascularity, not merely whether a nodule exists. Benign nodules may be cystic, well-defined and homogeneous; suspicious features include hypoechoicity, microcalcification, irregular margins, taller-than-wide shape and capsular invasion. After imaging stratification, clinicians decide between follow-up observation and fine-needle aspiration, whose cytology is the key step in distinguishing benign from malignant disease.[8][19]

需要避免两种相反的误读。一种是把结节直接当成癌，反复焦虑或过度切除；另一种是完全忽略，把可疑特征也当作没事。甲状腺结节女性多于男性，随年龄增长检出增多；碘摄入状态、既往颈部辐射史和家族史也与风险有关。结节是否需要进一步处理，依据是影像风险分层和必要的病理结果，而不是大小本身或外观触感。Two opposite misreadings should be avoided. One treats every nodule as cancer, inviting anxiety or unnecessary surgery; the other ignores suspicious features altogether. Nodules are more frequent in women than men and rise with age; iodine status, prior neck radiation and family history also relate to risk. Whether a nodule needs further management depends on imaging risk stratification and, when indicated, pathology, not on size alone or how it feels.[7][19]

边界需要写清：本章只解释检出率变化和评估逻辑，不做个体诊断。发现结节后是否复查、间隔多久、是否穿刺，应由内分泌或甲状腺外科结合超声报告决定。颈部肿块快速增大、声音改变、吞咽或呼吸受影响等表现需要及时就诊。本章检出率数字来自不同人群的超声与触诊研究，研究设计和人群不同，区间本身较宽，不能套用到单个病灶上。The boundary: this chapter explains detection trends and assessment logic and makes no individual diagnosis. Whether a nodule needs follow-up, at what interval, or aspiration should be decided by endocrinology or thyroid surgery using the ultrasound report. Rapid neck enlargement, voice change, or trouble swallowing or breathing warrants timely evaluation. The detection figures come from palpation and ultrasound studies of different populations; designs and groups vary, so the ranges are wide and cannot be applied to a single lesion.[8][19]

09为什么怀孕后鼻子会变大、容易堵？Why does the nose enlarge and feel congested during pregnancy?

雌激素增加黏膜通透性与水肿，孕期血容量增加约四到五成，鼻甲肿胀使鼻道变窄。Estrogen raises mucosal permeability and edema, pregnancy volume rises about 40%–50%, and swollen turbinates narrow the nasal passage.

孕期出现鼻塞、鼻子变圆变大，主要是雌激素、孕激素和血容量变化作用于鼻黏膜的结果，并不是鼻子本身长了新的结构，也不是骨骼被撑宽。鼻黏膜血管丰富、组织疏松，对激素和容量变化敏感；孕期这些变化以充血水肿为主，多在孕晚期加重，产后激素回落以后逐步缓解。除了鼻子，孕期牙龈、鼻腔和下肢软组织也常出现类似的充血水肿倾向。Nasal congestion and a rounder, larger-looking nose during pregnancy result mainly from estrogen, progesterone and blood-volume changes acting on the nasal mucosa; no new nasal structure actually grows. The mucosa has rich vessels and loose tissue sensitive to hormones and volume shifts, and these changes are dominated by congestion and edema, worst late in pregnancy and easing after hormones fall back postpartum.[21][22]

雌激素在鼻黏膜血管上存在受体。孕期血清雌激素持续上升，提高局部血管通透性，引起黏膜下组织水肿，并上调组胺受体，即使没有炎症也会产生堵塞感；孕激素有舒张血管平滑肌的作用，使局部血管更容易充盈。鼻黏膜腺体分泌也增多，鼻甲肿胀、鼻道变窄，呼吸阻力上升，这就是常说的孕期鼻炎。激素水平在妊娠中晚期维持在较高区间，因此鼻塞往往随孕周推进而更明显。Estrogen receptors are present on nasal mucosal vessels. Rising serum estrogen through pregnancy raises local vascular permeability, causing submucosal edema and upregulating histamine receptors, so a blocked sensation arises even without inflammation. Progesterone relaxes vascular smooth muscle and lets local vessels fill more easily. Mucosal glands also secrete more; turbinates swell, the nasal passage narrows and breathing resistance rises, the picture often called pregnancy rhinitis.[21][22]

血容量增加是第二条机制。孕期血浆容量和总血容量大约增加四到五成，孕晚期最明显；增多的血液和向血管外间隙的液体转移，使黏膜血液淤积加重。外鼻由皮肤、软骨和疏松结缔组织构成，局部水肿会让鼻翼和鼻尖看起来更圆、更宽；这类外观改变通常与全身孕期水肿平行出现，产后随容量回落而减轻。面部和手部的轻度肿胀在孕期也较常见，属于容量变化的一部分。Blood-volume expansion is the second mechanism. Plasma and total blood volume rise about 40%–50% in pregnancy, most evident in the third trimester. The added blood and fluid shifting toward the extravascular space worsen mucosal pooling. The external nose consists of skin, cartilage and loose connective tissue; local edema makes the alae and tip look rounder and broader, usually tracking generalized pregnancy edema and improving postpartum as volume recedes.[21][22]

孕期鼻黏膜改变需要和其他鼻塞原因区分。普通感冒多伴咽痛、咳嗽、发热，短期可好转；过敏性鼻炎有明确过敏原接触史，喷嚏、清水涕更突出；孕期鼻炎则持续时间更长，常与孕周平行。严重鼻塞影响睡眠、张口呼吸，或伴脓涕、面部胀痛、发热时，应区分是单纯孕期充血还是鼻窦炎等合并问题。反复鼻出血或单侧持续堵塞也需要检查局部结构问题。These changes must be distinguished from other causes of congestion. A common cold usually brings sore throat, cough or fever and improves quickly; allergic rhinitis follows allergen exposure and stands out with sneezing and clear watery discharge; pregnancy rhinitis lasts longer and tracks gestational age. When severe congestion disrupts sleep or forces mouth breathing, or accompanies purulent discharge, facial pressure or fever, clinicians separate simple pregnancy congestion from sinusitis or other complications.[5][22]

边界需要写清：本章只解释孕期鼻部变化的激素与容量机制，不推荐滴鼻或口服药物。孕期用药需要兼顾妊娠阶段和安全性，应由产科或耳鼻喉科评估；鼻塞也可能由鼻中隔偏曲、鼻息肉等结构性问题引起，不能全部归因于怀孕。本章的血容量增幅和受体机制来自孕期生理与鼻炎综述资料，个体水肿程度和出现时间差异较大，外观变化一般在产后数周到数月内逐步恢复。The boundary: this chapter explains the hormonal and volume mechanisms and recommends no nasal or oral medication. Pregnancy medication must account for gestational stage and safety and be reviewed by obstetrics or ENT. Congestion can also arise from a deviated septum or nasal polyps and cannot all be attributed to pregnancy. The volume increase and receptor mechanisms cited come from pregnancy physiology and rhinitis reviews, and individual edema and timing vary considerably.[21][22]
