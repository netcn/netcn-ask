01瞳孔为什么遇到强光会缩小，眼睛又是怎样把目光锁定在物体上？Why does the pupil shrink in bright light, and how do our eyes lock onto a target?

对光反射经视网膜、视路到中脑再由副交感收缩瞳孔；调节、会聚与眼动配合锁定目标。The light reflex runs retina, optic pathway and midbrain before parasympathetic fibers constrict the pupil; accommodation, convergence and eye movements lock the gaze.

瞳孔遇强光缩小、遇暗处放大，是一条不需要意识参与的反射弧；与此同时，眼睛靠晶状体调节、双眼会聚和眼球转动，把目光持续对准感兴趣的物体。光线变强时，瞳孔从暗处约 6–8 mm 缩到亮光下约 2–3 mm，进光量随之减少；看近物时瞳孔还会伴随调节同步缩小，以减少像差、加深景深。这套配合让视网膜在不同亮度和距离下都能得到相对清晰的像。The pupil constricts in bright light and dilates in darkness through a reflex arc that does not require conscious effort. At the same time, accommodation, binocular convergence and eye movements keep the gaze locked on a target of interest. As light strengthens, the pupil shrinks from about 6–8 mm in the dark to roughly 2–3 mm in daylight, cutting the amount of light entering; during near focus it also constricts with accommodation to reduce aberration and deepen depth of field. This combination keeps the retinal image reasonably clear across brightness and distance.[1][4]

对光反射的通路分两段。光线照到视网膜，视杆、视锥细胞把光子信号转成神经冲动，经视神经传到中脑顶盖前区；交换神经元后，动眼神经副核发出副交感纤维，经睫状神经节换元，支配瞳孔括约肌收缩。一侧受光，两侧瞳孔同时缩小，称为互感性对光反射；医生用手电筒查瞳孔，检查的就是这条反射弧是否完整。交感神经则支配瞳孔开大肌，在紧张、惊恐或暗环境中把瞳孔拉开。The light reflex runs in two stages. Light reaches the retina, where rods and cones convert photons into nerve impulses that travel along the optic nerve to the pretectal nucleus in the midbrain. After synapsing there, parasympathetic fibers leave the accessory oculomotor nucleus, relay in the ciliary ganglion, and contract the sphincter pupillae. Light in one eye constricts both pupils, the consensual light reflex; a flashlight exam tests whether this arc is intact. The sympathetic nervous system drives the dilator pupillae, opening the pupil during stress, fear or darkness.[3][4][6]

看近物时的调节是另一套配合。睫状肌收缩、放松悬韧带，晶状体靠自身弹性变厚、曲率变大，把近处发散的光线重新会聚到视网膜上；同时双眼向内会聚，瞳孔缩小，三者合称近反射三联征。眼球追踪物体靠眼外肌的两套系统：快速跳动的扫视把视线带到目标上，平滑追随系统跟住移动的目标；中央凹只占视野很小一块，眼球不断微动，才能把不同区域交给高分辨率的中央凹处理。Near focus uses a separate coordinated response. The ciliary muscle contracts and slackens the suspensory ligaments, so the lens rounds up by its own elasticity, bending nearby diverging rays back onto the retina; both eyes converge inward and the pupil narrows, together called the near triad. Eye tracking relies on two extraocular systems: saccades jump the gaze onto a target, while smooth pursuit follows a moving object. The fovea covers only a small field, so continuous small eye movements deliver different regions to the high-acuity fovea.[1][4]

这些机制有可核对的临床对应。正常成人亮光下瞳孔直径约 2–4 mm，暗处可到 5–8 mm；对光反射消失或两侧不等大，提示反射弧、中脑或动眼神经可能受损。揉压眼球时看到的闪光样光斑，是机械刺激直接激活了视网膜感光细胞或神经节细胞，视觉皮层按视网膜拓扑把刺激解释成光点，并不来自外部光线。验光、眼底检查和神经查体，都建立在瞳孔、调节和眼动这套配合之上。These mechanisms map onto clinical findings that can be checked. In daylight an adult pupil measures about 2–4 mm, widening to 5–8 mm in the dark; an absent reflex or unequal pupils points to a problem in the arc, midbrain or oculomotor nerve. The flashes seen after rubbing the eye come from mechanical stimulation of retinal photoreceptors or ganglion cells; the visual cortex maps this stimulus to a phosphene according to retinal topography, not from external light. Refraction, fundus exams and neurological screening all build on this pupil, accommodation and eye-movement coordination.[4][6]

边界要写清：瞳孔大小不只由光线决定，情绪、警觉度、疼痛和某些药物都会改变它；对光反射是自主反射，不能靠意志随意控制。眼球能锁定目标，依赖眼外肌、小脑和视觉皮层协同，疲劳、醉酒或神经病变都会让追踪变飘。把瞳孔缩小说成眼睛在用力看，或把揉眼闪光当成视网膜真的接收到了光，都越过了机制本身。Boundary: pupil size is set not only by light but also by emotion, alertness, pain and certain drugs; the light reflex is autonomic and cannot be willed on or off. Target locking depends on extraocular muscles, the cerebellum and visual cortex working together, and fatigue, alcohol or neurological disease makes pursuit drift. Calling constriction a voluntary effort to see harder, or treating a pressure phosphene as light entering the eye, goes beyond what the mechanism shows.[1][6]

02人眼的分辨率能不能量化，相当于多少像素？Can the eye's resolution be measured, and how many pixels would it equal?

20/20 视力约对应 1 角分；中央凹视锥最密，周边采样稀疏，像素类比不能直接套相机规格。20/20 vision matches about one arcminute; foveal cones are densest while peripheral sampling is sparse, so the pixel analogy does not map onto a camera sensor.

人眼分辨率可以量化，但“相当于多少像素”是个容易误导的类比。标准 20/20 视力对应视角约 1 角分，即 1/60 度，在这个角度下两个点仍能被分辨；中央凹处视锥细胞最密，神经节细胞与视锥接近一对一连接，是分辨率最高的区域。把整只眼睛折算成像素的流行估算常在一亿像素上下，但这个数字把扫视拼接、周边低分辨率和神经压缩都混在了一起，不能直接和相机传感器规格比较。The eye's resolution can be measured, but the comparison to a camera pixel count is misleading. Standard 20/20 vision corresponds to about one arcminute, one sixtieth of a degree, the angle at which two points can still be told apart. Cones are densest in the fovea, where ganglion cells connect to cones nearly one-to-one, the highest-resolution region. Popular estimates put the whole eye near one hundred megapixels, but that figure mixes saccadic stitching, low peripheral resolution and neural compression, so it cannot be matched directly to a sensor specification.[3][4]

结构决定分辨率上限。人眼约有 600 万视锥和 1.2 亿视杆；视锥集中在中央凹，负责明视觉和颜色，视杆分布在周边，负责暗视觉。中央凹中心视锥密度可达每平方毫米约 18 万到 20 万个，细胞间距接近衍射极限：在良好光线下，瞳孔衍射和视锥间距对分辨率的限制大致相当。离开中央凹十几度，视锥密度骤降，周边视野只能分辨粗轮廓和运动。Structure sets the upper limit. The human retina holds about six million cones and 120 million rods. Cones cluster in the fovea for daylight and color, while rods spread through the periphery for dim light. Peak cone density at the foveal center reaches roughly 180,000 to 200,000 cells per square millimetre, with spacing near the diffraction limit: in good light, pupil diffraction and cone spacing impose comparable limits. A dozen degrees off the fovea, cone density drops sharply, and the peripheral field resolves only coarse outlines and motion.[3][4]

测量与等效像素。视力表按 1 角分设计视标；20/20 表示在 20 英尺（约 6 米）处看清标准视标。若按中央凹采样密度外推，高分辨率区域对应的像素量级确实很大，但周边视网膜采样稀疏，大脑通过扫视把多帧信息拼接成稳定画面。相机是一次性记录整幅画面，眼睛则是边看边采样，所谓总像素并不能直接换算成传感器的有效分辨率。Measurement and the pixel analogy. Eye charts design targets to one arcminute; 20/20 means reading the standard target at 20 feet, about six metres. Extrapolated from foveal sampling density, the high-resolution region does correspond to a large pixel count, but peripheral retina samples sparsely, and the brain stitches several frames together through saccades into a stable scene. A camera records one whole frame at once; the eye samples as it looks, so a total pixel figure does not convert into an equivalent effective sensor resolution.[4]

证据与应用。OpenStax 生物学教材区分了视杆与视锥的分布和功能；NCBI 眼科生理教材给出视网膜感光细胞数量与中央凹高敏度。验光时矫正的是屈光不正造成的模糊，而不是感光细胞本身的分辨极限。衍射在小瞳孔下更明显，大瞳孔像差增加，最佳清晰度通常出现在中等瞳孔；夜间对比敏感度下降，是视杆系统分辨率低于视锥的直接表现。Evidence and application. The OpenStax biology text separates rod and cone distribution and function, and the NCBI ophthalmic physiology reference gives photoreceptor counts and foveal sensitivity. An optometric correction fixes blur from refractive error, not the resolving limit of the receptors themselves. Diffraction matters more with a small pupil, aberration rises with a large one, and sharpest vision usually falls at an intermediate pupil. Lower contrast sensitivity at night directly reflects the lower resolution of the rod system.[3][4][6]

边界：分辨率随光照、对比度、年龄和神经状态变化。周边视力差不是“没看清”，而是采样密度本来就低；黄斑病变、屈光间质混浊会把可达到的分辨率压到受体极限以下。把一亿像素当成眼睛的静态规格，或把视力表分数等同于整体视觉能力，都忽略了神经系统在编码和解释中起的作用。Boundary: resolution varies with illumination, contrast, age and neural state. Poor peripheral vision is not a matter of failing to look carefully; the sampling density there is simply low. Macular disease or cloudy refractive media push attainable resolution below the receptor limit. Treating one hundred megapixels as a fixed eye specification, or equating an eye-chart score with overall visual ability, ignores the role the nervous system plays in coding and interpretation.[3][4]

03近视、远视和散光分别是怎么回事，眼镜和隐形眼镜怎样矫正？What are myopia, hyperopia and astigmatism, and how do glasses and contacts correct them?

像成在视网膜前为近视、在后为远视、曲率不均为散光；凹、凸透镜与柱面透镜把像移回视网膜。Focus in front of the retina is myopia, behind it hyperopia, uneven curvature astigmatism; concave, convex and toric lenses move the image back onto the retina.

近视、远视和散光都属于屈光不正：光线经过角膜和晶状体后，没有准确会聚在视网膜上。近视眼看远模糊，因为眼球偏长或角膜偏陡，像成在视网膜前方；远视眼看近更费力，因为眼球偏短，像落在视网膜后方；散光则是角膜或晶状体曲率不均匀，一个方向上形成两条焦线。眼镜和隐形眼镜用额外的透镜把像重新移到视网膜上，而不是改变眼球本身的形状。Myopia, hyperopia and astigmatism are all refractive errors: after passing the cornea and lens, light does not converge precisely on the retina. A myope sees distant blur because the eyeball is too long or the cornea too steep, and the image forms in front of the retina. A hyperope strains near vision because the eye is too short and focus falls behind the retina. Astigmatism comes from an uneven corneal or lens curvature, producing two focal lines in different directions. Glasses and contact lenses add power to move the image back onto the retina; they do not reshape the eyeball itself.[1][2]

矫正原理。近视配凹透镜，即发散透镜，先把光线略微散开，让焦点后移到视网膜；远视配凸透镜，即会聚透镜，提前会聚光线；散光用柱面透镜，只在曲率异常的方向上补偿度数。老花不是屈光位置错了，而是晶状体随年龄失去弹性、调节力下降，看近需要阅读镜。隐形眼镜直接贴在角膜表面，视野更大，但矫正散光需要定制 toric 镜片。The correction follows the optics. Myopia uses a concave, diverging lens that spreads rays slightly so the focus shifts back onto the retina; hyperopia uses a convex, converging lens that bends rays inward earlier; astigmatism uses a toric cylinder that compensates only in the meridian with abnormal curvature. Presbyopia is not a misplaced focus: the lens loses elasticity with age and accommodation weakens, so near work needs reading glasses. Contact lenses sit directly on the cornea for a wider field, but correcting astigmatism requires custom toric lenses.[2][4]

眼镜会不会加重近视？目前证据不支持“戴眼镜让度数变深”的说法。眼镜只是把像矫正到视网膜上，不改变眼轴长度；近视进展主要由眼轴增长驱动，与遗传、长时间近距离用眼和户外活动不足有关。儿童青少年近视的队列研究反复发现，每天户外活动时间较长与近视发生率较低相关；长时间近距离用眼与近视发展方向一致，但因果权重在不同研究中仍有争议。Do glasses make myopia worse? The evidence does not support that claim. Glasses move the corrected image onto the retina without changing axial length; myopia progression is driven mainly by axial elongation, linked to genetics, sustained near work and limited outdoor time. Cohort studies of children and adolescents repeatedly associate more daily outdoor time with lower myopia onset, while prolonged near work tracks in the same direction, though causal weight varies between studies.[2][4]

看电视太近会不会弄坏眼睛？距离过近本身不会把眼球看坏，但会增加调节负担，引起眼干、酸胀等视疲劳症状；对儿童而言，持续近距离用眼是近视发展的环境因素之一，而不是一次性损伤。婴幼儿若有斜视、高度屈光不正或屈光参差，需要尽早验光矫正，因为视觉发育有关键期，长期模糊成像可能导致弱视。成人视觉系统成熟后，短期近距离工作一般不造成永久性屈光改变。Does sitting too close to a television damage the eyes? Proximity itself does not permanently damage the eyeball, but it raises accommodation load and can cause dryness, soreness and other asthenopia symptoms. For children, sustained near work is one environmental factor in myopia development, not a one-off injury. Infants with strabismus, high refractive error or anisometropia need early optical correction because visual development has a critical period, and long-term blur can lead to amblyopia. Once the visual system matures, short spells of near work generally do not produce permanent refractive change.[2][4]

边界：矫正镜片不能缩短已经变长的眼轴，也不能治愈近视；角膜塑形镜、低浓度阿托品等近视控制手段有各自的适用年龄和证据强度，需由眼科评估。屈光不正的类型和度数要靠验光确定，不能凭看远模糊自行判断。把视疲劳等同于近视加深，或认为不戴眼镜可以锻炼眼睛，都与现有证据不符。Boundary: corrective lenses cannot shorten an elongated axis or cure myopia; orthokeratology and low-dose atropine carry their own age ranges and evidence strength, and require an ophthalmology assessment. Refractive type and power must be established by refraction, not guessed from distant blur alone. Equating eye strain with worsening myopia, or thinking that going without lenses trains the eye, does not match current evidence.[2][4]

04虹膜的颜色由什么决定，蓝眼睛的人是否有共同祖先？What determines iris color, and do blue-eyed people share a common ancestor?

虹膜颜色由黑色素多少与基质散射决定；OCA2 与 HERC2 是主要基因，蓝眼相关变异可追溯到欧洲人群的同一祖先。Iris color comes from melanin amount and stromal scattering; OCA2 and HERC2 are the main genes, and the blue-eye variant traces to one founder in European populations.

虹膜颜色不是由某种蓝色素决定的，而是虹膜组织中黑色素的多少与分布，加上基质对光线的散射共同造成。棕色虹膜在前层和基质中含大量黑色素；蓝、绿、灰色虹膜黑色素较少，短波长光被基质散射，长波长光透过，看起来偏冷色。同一虹膜上深浅不一的纹理和斑点，来自色素分布的局部差异。Iris color is not produced by a blue pigment. It results from the amount and distribution of melanin in the iris tissue, combined with how the stroma scatters light. Brown irises carry heavy melanin in the anterior layer and stroma; blue, green and grey irises have less melanin, so short wavelengths are scattered by the stroma while longer wavelengths pass through, giving a cool appearance. Uneven streaks and freckles within one iris come from local differences in pigment distribution.[3][4]

遗传基础。15 号染色体上的 OCA2 基因负责合成 P 蛋白，参与黑色素小体成熟，直接影响虹膜色素量；邻近的 HERC2 基因内含子 86 中的一段调控序列控制 OCA2 的表达强弱。MedlinePlus 遗传学资料指出，这一区域是虹膜颜色的主要决定因素，但还有其他基因共同影响绿色、褐色和淡褐色等中间色，所以虹膜颜色不是简单的单基因显隐性遗传。Genetic basis. On chromosome 15 the OCA2 gene makes the P protein, which helps melanosomes mature and directly affects iris pigment; the nearby HERC2 gene carries a regulatory segment in intron 86 that controls how strongly OCA2 is expressed. MedlinePlus Genetics identifies this region as the main determinant of iris color, but other genes jointly shape green, brown and hazel intermediates, so iris color is not a simple single-gene dominant-recessive trait.[5]

蓝眼共同祖先。对欧洲人群的遗传研究把蓝眼相关的关键变异追溯到 HERC2 调控区的同一个单核苷酸多态性；Eiberg 等人 2008 年在 Human Genetics 上的研究提出，这一变异可能来自一位生活在约 6,000 到 10,000 年前的共同祖先，随人群迁徙扩散到欧洲各地。需要强调，这是说相关调控变异有共同起源，不等于所有蓝眼人属于同一血统，也不排除其他地区独立出现浅色虹膜的可能。A common ancestor for blue eyes. Genetic studies of European populations trace the key blue-eye variant to a single nucleotide polymorphism in the HERC2 regulatory region. Eiberg and colleagues proposed in Human Genetics in 2008 that this variant may descend from a common ancestor living roughly 6,000 to 10,000 years ago, spreading across Europe with human migrations. The claim is that the regulatory variant shares one origin, not that all blue-eyed people share a single lineage, and it does not rule out light irises arising independently elsewhere.[5]

观察与应用。婴儿出生时虹膜常偏蓝灰，数月到数年内黑色素逐渐沉积，颜色才稳定；双眼颜色不一，即异色症，可由色素分布差异、发育异常或外伤引起。虹膜纹理高度个体化，被用于生物识别，但识别的是纹理而非颜色。临床上虹膜颜色一般不直接决定视力好坏；白化病因色素缺乏伴随畏光和视力问题，属于特殊病理情况。Observation and application. Newborn irises often look blue-grey; melanin deposits over months and years before the color stabilizes. Heterochromia, different colors in the two eyes, can come from pigment distribution differences, developmental anomalies or injury. Iris texture is highly individual and used in biometric identification, which reads the texture rather than the color. Clinically, iris color generally does not determine visual acuity; albinism, with its lack of pigment, brings photophobia and visual problems as a special pathological case.[4][5]

边界：虹膜颜色与性格、智力或健康倾向之间没有被可靠证据支持的对应关系。基因研究给出的是欧洲人群中的关联与可能起源时间，样本和人群范围决定结论不能外推到所有族群。颜色在成年后也可能因疾病、药物或外伤发生改变；把蓝眼来自单一祖先理解成蓝眼人群在遗传上更单一，是对研究结论的过度推广。Boundary: no reliable evidence links iris color to personality, intelligence or health predisposition. Genetic studies report associations and a probable origin time within European samples, and the sample and population scope keep the conclusion from extending to every ethnic group. Color can also change in adulthood through disease, medication or injury. Reading the single-ancestor finding as meaning blue-eyed people are genetically more uniform overstates what the study shows.[3][5]

05近视为什么不可逆，眼轴变长后还能缩回去吗？Why is myopia irreversible, and can an elongated eye shorten again?

眼轴增长是结构性拉长，每 +1 mm 约对应 -2.5 至 -3.0 D；矫正与手术都不缩回眼轴。Axial growth stretches the eye structurally; each +1 mm adds about 2.5–3.0 D, and neither lenses nor surgery shorten the axis.

近视不可逆，核心在于眼轴变长是结构性改变，而不是屈光状态的临时偏移。平行光线进入眼球后，经过角膜和晶状体折射，本应会聚在视网膜上；近视时眼球前后径（眼轴）过长，或角膜曲率过陡，焦点落在视网膜前方，看远模糊。成年人正常眼轴约 23.5–24.5 毫米；眼轴每增长约 1 毫米，对应的近视度数大约增加 2.5 到 3.0 屈光度（D）。这个长度变化发生在眼球壁的巩膜和视网膜上，一旦拉长，成年后不会自行缩回。Myopia is largely irreversible because axial elongation is a structural change, not a temporary shift in refraction. Parallel rays enter the eye and bend through the cornea and lens; in myopia the eye is too long, or the cornea too steep, so focus falls in front of the retina and distant objects blur. An adult axial length is roughly 23.5 to 24.5 mm; every 1 mm of additional axial length corresponds to about 2.5 to 3.0 dioptres of myopia. Once the sclera and retina have stretched, the axis does not shorten again in adulthood.[2]

为什么眼轴会变长。儿童青少年期，长时间近距离用眼、户外活动不足等环境因素与遗传背景叠加，眼球在发育中被拉长；巩膜胶原重塑、眼内压与脉络膜厚度变化都参与其中。近视进展主要由眼轴增长驱动，而不是角膜度数“用掉了”。验光单上的负度数只是把当前屈光状态记下来，度数加深反映的是眼轴继续变长，而不是眼球被镜片“压坏”。The axis grows during development. In children and adolescents, prolonged near work, limited outdoor time and genetic background overlap, and the eye elongates as scleral collagen remodels, with intraocular pressure and choroidal thickness contributing. Progression is driven mainly by axial growth, not by corneal power being used up. The minus number on a prescription records the current refractive state; deeper myopia reflects continued axial lengthening, not damage caused by lenses.[2][4]

矫正手段分别改了什么。框架眼镜和隐形眼镜是在眼球外加一块透镜，把前移的焦点重新移到视网膜上，不改变眼轴；角膜屈光手术（如 LASIK、全飞秒）切削或重塑角膜，改变角膜曲率这一屈光环节，让光线提前或延后会聚，同样不缩短已经变长的眼轴。高度近视者眼轴拉长后，视网膜被拉薄，周边视网膜变性、裂孔和脱离的风险随眼轴增加而上升，这也是手术不能“治愈”近视的原因：屈光可以矫正，眼球结构的改变仍在。Each correction changes a different link. Glasses and contacts add a lens outside the eye that moves the forward focus back onto the retina without altering the axis. Corneal refractive surgery such as LASIK or SMILE reshapes corneal curvature, one refractive step, and again does not shorten an elongated eye. In high myopia the elongated axis stretches the retina, raising the risk of peripheral degeneration, holes and detachment; this is why surgery corrects refraction but cannot cure myopia, and why the structural change remains.[1][2]

可核对的观察与证据。眼科 A 超或光学生物测量可以直接读出眼轴长度，近视随访中比较眼轴增长速度，比单纯看度数更能判断进展；散瞳验光排除调节痉挛后得到的是真实屈光状态。户外活动与儿童近视发生率较低相关，队列研究反复观察到这一关联，但因果权重在不同研究中仍有争议；低浓度阿托品、角膜塑形镜等控制手段有各自的适用年龄和证据强度，需由眼科评估。Measurements and evidence. Ocular A-scan or optical biometry reads axial length directly; tracking growth speed in follow-up is more informative than the prescription alone, and cycloplegic refraction removes accommodation spasm before recording true error. Cohort studies repeatedly associate more daily outdoor time with lower myopia onset, though causal weight varies between studies; low-dose atropine and orthokeratology carry their own age ranges and evidence strength and require ophthalmology assessment.[1][4]

边界。近视不可逆不等于不能控制：青少年期减缓眼轴增长速度是主要目标；成年后度数稳定，手术改善的是脱镜需求，不改变高度近视的眼底风险。出现飞蚊突然增多、闪光感、视野缺损或视力骤降时，需要尽快眼科检查，排查视网膜脱离等急症。把“近视可以恢复”的训练、按摩或仪器宣传当成逆转眼轴的手段，与现有解剖和证据不符。Boundary. Irreversibility does not mean uncontrollable: slowing axial growth in childhood and adolescence is the main target. Once the prescription stabilises in adulthood, surgery addresses spectacle dependence, not the retinal risks of high myopia. A sudden shower of floaters, flashes, visual-field loss or a drop in acuity needs prompt ophthalmology to rule out retinal detachment. Claims that training, massage or devices can shorten an elongated axis do not match anatomy and current evidence.[2][4]

06为什么近视会遗传，父母近视孩子就一定会近视吗？Why does myopia run in families, and must a myopic parent have a myopic child?

近视为多基因易感性叠加环境触发；遗传影响长眼轴倾向，不锁定固定度数。Myopia is polygenic susceptibility plus environmental trigger; genes bias axial growth tendency rather than fixing a prescription.

近视有明显的家族聚集倾向，但它不是简单的单基因显隐性遗传，而是遗传易感性与环境因素共同作用的多基因性状。父母双方都近视，子女近视的概率高于父母不近视的家庭；父母一方近视，风险居中；高度近视的遗传倾向通常比低度近视更强。这种相关性既包含基因差异，也包含同一家庭内近距离用眼、阅读环境和户外活动习惯的共享，不能直接读成“基因决定一切”。Myopia clusters in families, but it is not a simple single-gene dominant or recessive trait; it is a polygenic susceptibility interacting with environment. When both parents are myopic, children face a higher probability than in non-myopic families; one myopic parent gives an intermediate risk, and high myopia usually shows a stronger hereditary tendency than low myopia. This correlation mixes genetic differences with shared household near-work, reading habits and outdoor time, so it cannot be read as genes deciding everything.[2][5]

遗传基础研究。双胞胎研究显示，眼轴长度和屈光度数在同卵双生之间的一致性高于异卵双生，提示遗传贡献；全基因组关联研究已定位到上百个与近视相关的基因位点，部分参与眼球发育、巩膜生长和视网膜信号传导。与虹膜颜色由 OCA2、HERC2 等少数主效基因决定不同，近视相关位点单个效应都很小，需要多个位点叠加，再与环境互动才表现出度数差异。Genetic evidence. Twin studies show axial length and refractive error agree more between identical than fraternal twins, indicating a genetic contribution; genome-wide association studies have linked over a hundred loci to myopia, some involved in eye development, scleral growth and retinal signalling. Unlike iris color, driven by a few major genes such as OCA2 and HERC2, each myopia locus has a small individual effect, and several must stack up before interacting with the environment to produce a prescription.[4][5]

环境把易感性变成度数。同样携带易感背景，户外活动时间长、近距离用眼负荷低的孩子，近视发生更晚、进展更慢；长期伏案、屏幕和阅读距离过近的环境会放大遗传风险。也就是说，基因影响的是“在同样用眼条件下更容易长眼轴”的倾向，而不是出生时就锁定一个固定度数。儿童期到青春期是眼轴增长和近视进展的主要窗口，成年后眼轴一般趋于稳定。Environment turns susceptibility into power. Children with the same background develop myopia later and more slowly when they spend more time outdoors and carry a lower near-work load; long desk work, screens and close reading amplify genetic risk. Genes bias how easily the axis elongates under similar viewing conditions, rather than locking in a fixed degree at birth. Childhood through adolescence is the main window for axial growth and progression, after which the axis usually stabilises in adulthood.[2][5]

可核对的观察。家族史是近视筛查的参考因素之一；验光和眼轴测量可以记录实际屈光状态与长度变化。需要区分两种遗传：单纯性近视在学龄后出现、度数多在 -6 D 以内；病理性高度近视眼轴进行性拉长，伴随眼底改变，部分由特定基因变异导致，家族中多人高度近视时建议眼科遗传咨询。目前没有基因检测能准确预测某个孩子将来的近视度数。Observations and distinctions. Family history feeds myopia screening, while refraction and axial measurements record actual state and change. Two hereditary patterns differ: simple myopia appears after school age, usually within minus six dioptres; pathological high myopia elongates progressively with fundus changes, sometimes driven by specific variants, and families with several highly myopic members may benefit from ophthalmic genetic counselling. No gene test currently predicts a child's future myopia degree accurately.[2][4]

边界。“父母近视孩子一定近视”和“父母不近视孩子绝不会近视”都不成立：易感基因需要环境触发，而环境干预（增加户外时间、控制近距离用眼距离和时长、定期验光）对有家族史的孩子同样有效。把近视归因于遗传而放弃随访，或把环境改善说成可以逆转已形成的眼轴长度，都越过了证据边界。Boundary. Myopic parents guaranteeing a myopic child and non-myopic parents ruling it out are both false: susceptibility needs environmental triggers, and interventions that raise outdoor time, control near-work distance and duration, and schedule regular refraction benefit children with a family history too. Blaming genetics and skipping follow-up, or claiming environmental improvement can reverse an already formed axial length, goes beyond the evidence.[2][5]

07为什么眼睛会流泪，泪膜分哪几层？Why do our eyes tear, and what layers make up the tear film?

基础、反射、情绪三种流泪共用三层泪膜：脂质减蒸发、水样供氧冲洗、黏蛋白贴附上皮。Basal, reflex and emotional tears share a three-layer film: lipid limits evaporation, aqueous supplies oxygen and washing, mucin anchors to epithelium.

眼睛流泪分三种触发方式：基础泪液持续湿润眼表，反射泪液在异物、刺激或眼表干燥时涌出，情绪性泪液在强烈情绪下分泌。无论哪一种，泪液都先铺成一层泪膜，覆盖角膜和结膜表面；泪膜由外向内分三层：最外层是睑板腺分泌的脂质层，减少水分蒸发；中间是泪腺分泌的水样层，占泪膜体积的大部分，负责供氧、冲洗和抗菌；最内层是结膜杯状细胞分泌的黏蛋白层，把泪液黏附在亲水的角膜上皮上。Tears arise in three ways: basal secretion keeps the surface moist continuously, reflex tears flood the eye under irritation or dryness, and emotional tears flow during intense feeling. In every case the fluid spreads as a tear film over cornea and conjunctiva, in three layers. The outermost lipid layer, from meibomian glands, slows evaporation; the middle aqueous layer, from the lacrimal gland, carries most of the volume for oxygen supply, washing and antibacterial proteins; the inner mucin layer, from conjunctival goblet cells, anchors the film to the hydrophilic corneal epithelium.[1][4]

泪液从哪里来、到哪里去。基础分泌由副泪腺完成，量少而持续；反射性分泌由泪腺主导，三叉神经眼支把眼表刺激信号传到脑干，再由副交感纤维支配泪腺。泪液湿润眼表后，经内眼角的泪小点进入泪小管、泪囊和鼻泪管，排入鼻腔；所以哭的时候鼻涕增多，就是多余泪液从鼻腔流出的表现。泪膜每隔数秒随眨眼重新铺展一次，眨眼不充分时泪膜破裂加快，眼表就会发干、发涩。Where fluid comes from and where it goes. Basal secretion comes from accessory lacrimal glands in small, steady amounts; reflex secretion is dominated by the main lacrimal gland. The ophthalmic branch of the trigeminal nerve carries surface signals to the brainstem, and parasympathetic fibers drive the gland. After wetting the eye, tears enter the puncta at the inner corner, pass through canaliculi, lacrimal sac and nasolacrimal duct, and drain into the nose; this is why crying brings a runny nose. Blinking re-spreads the film every few seconds, and inadequate blinking shortens film break-up time.[1][4]

泪液成分与渗透压。水样层含水、电解质（钠、钾、氯等）、蛋白质、溶菌酶和免疫球蛋白；钠浓度决定了眼泪的咸味，泪液渗透压通常在 300 毫渗量每升上下，比血浆略高。脂质层薄、蒸发加快时，剩余泪液被浓缩，渗透压升高，会刺激眼表神经产生干涩和异物感；干燥、空调环境和长时间看屏幕（眨眼频率下降）都会放大这种蒸发。泪膜破裂或干燥直接刺激三叉神经眼支末梢，可产生刺痒和异物感；接触花粉、尘螨等过敏原时，眼表肥大细胞释放组胺等介质，也会刺激同一批神经末梢引起眼痒，常伴流泪和结膜充血。Composition and osmotic pressure. The aqueous layer holds water, electrolytes such as sodium, potassium and chloride, proteins, lysozyme and immunoglobulins; sodium sets the salty taste, and tear osmolarity runs near 300 milliosmoles per litre, slightly above plasma. A thin lipid layer speeds evaporation, concentrating the remaining fluid, raising osmolarity and stimulating surface nerves into dryness and foreign-body sensation. Dry air, air conditioning and screen watching, with its reduced blink rate, all amplify this evaporation. Tear-film break-up and dryness directly stimulate ophthalmic nerve endings, producing a stinging itch or foreign-body sensation; when allergens such as pollen or dust mites contact the surface, mast cells release histamine and other mediators that stimulate the same endings, causing itchy eyes with tearing and redness.[4][6]

情绪性流泪。人在悲伤、激动甚至强烈喜悦时也会流泪，这是自主神经系统参与的反应；有研究比较过情绪泪液与反射泪液的成分，报告两者在蛋白质、激素相关物质上存在差异，但证据并不一致，情绪泪液是否通过排出特定物质来缓解压力，仍有争议。能确认的是：情绪与自主神经状态可以影响泪腺分泌，而泪腺同时受副交感和交感神经调节。Emotional tears. People also cry under grief, excitement or strong joy through an autonomic reaction. Studies comparing emotional and reflex tears report differences in proteins and hormone-related substances, but the evidence is inconsistent, and whether emotional tears relieve stress by excreting specific substances remains debated. What is established is that emotion and autonomic state influence lacrimal secretion, and the gland receives both parasympathetic and sympathetic input.[1][6]

边界。流泪本身是保护机制，不写成护眼建议清单。持续流泪、眼红、疼痛、畏光、分泌物增多或泪溢（泪道排不出去）需要眼科检查，可能涉及干眼、结膜炎、睑板腺功能障碍或鼻泪管阻塞；偶尔的干涩多与环境和眨眼不足有关。把情绪流泪解释成排毒，或把所有流泪都归为缺水，都超出了现有证据。Boundary. Tearing is a protective mechanism, not a skincare checklist. Persistent tearing with redness, pain, photophobia, discharge or epiphora needs ophthalmology to check dry eye, conjunctivitis, meibomian dysfunction or nasolacrimal blockage; occasional dryness usually tracks environment and blinking. Treating emotional tears as detoxification, or blaming every tear on insufficient water, goes beyond current evidence.[1][4]

08为什么切洋葱会流泪，眼泪为什么是咸的？Why do onions make us cry, and why are tears salty?

含硫挥发物刺激三叉神经触发反射流泪；水样层钠氯决定咸味，渗透压约 300 mOsm/L。A sulfur irritant triggers reflex tearing via the trigeminal nerve; sodium and chloride in the aqueous layer make tears salty, near 300 mOsm/L.

切洋葱流泪是一条化学反射链。洋葱被切开、细胞破损后，内部的含硫氨基酸在酶（蒜氨酸酶）作用下生成挥发性的次磺酸类物质，进一步重排为含硫的催泪挥发物；气体上升到眼睛，刺激角膜和结膜上的三叉神经末梢，信号传到脑干后反射性地命令泪腺分泌泪液，试图把刺激物冲掉。这属于反射性流泪，和情绪无关。Onion tears follow a chemical reflex chain. Cutting breaks cells, and sulfur-containing amino acids react with alliinase enzymes to form volatile sulfenic compounds that rearrange into a tear-gas-like sulfur irritant. The gas rises to the eye, stimulates trigeminal endings on cornea and conjunctiva, the signal reaches the brainstem, and the lacrimal gland reflexively secretes fluid to wash the irritant away. This is reflex tearing, unrelated to emotion.[1][4]

眼泪为什么是咸的。泪液水样层含钠、氯等电解质，钠浓度与血浆接近但经泪腺调节，所以尝起来有咸味；泪液渗透压约 300 毫渗量每升，比纯水高。基础泪液量少、不断被眨眼重新铺展；反射性流泪量大时，盐分被大量水分稀释，咸味会变淡；长时间不眨眼、泪液蒸发浓缩后，咸味和刺激感会更明显。Why tears taste salty. The aqueous layer carries sodium and chloride; sodium concentration approaches plasma levels but is regulated by the gland, which gives tears their saltiness. Tear osmolarity is about 300 milliosmoles per litre, far above pure water. Basal tears are small in volume and re-spread with each blink; a large reflex flow dilutes the salts and tastes milder, while fluid left unblinked and evaporated becomes more concentrated, saltier and more irritating.[1][4]

泪膜三层与刺激后的反应。催泪物接触眼表后，除了反射流泪，还可能引起短暂刺痛、发红；正常泪膜的脂质层、水样层和黏蛋白层负责把异物和刺激物冲走、维持眼表湿润。黏液层由杯状细胞分泌，帮助泪液附着在角膜上皮；如果刺激持续，泪液来不及排走，就从内眼角溢出。切洋葱时先冷藏洋葱、用锋利刀具快速切开或在通风处操作，可减少挥发物到达眼睛的量，这是减少接触的环境调整，不是治疗。The tear film and the after-effect. After the irritant reaches the surface, reflex tears may bring brief stinging and redness. The lipid, aqueous and mucin layers wash away foreign material and keep the surface moist; goblet-cell mucin anchors tears to the epithelium. If stimulation continues and drainage cannot keep up, fluid spills from the inner corner. Chilling the onion, cutting quickly with a sharp knife or working in ventilated air reduces the volatile compounds reaching the eye; this is an exposure adjustment, not a treatment.[1][6]

可核对的机制细节。催泪物质在切破细胞后才生成，完整洋葱气味较轻；洋葱切开后放置，含硫挥发物继续释放。三叉神经眼支同时负责角膜的痛觉和触觉信号，所以化学刺激先被感觉成刺痛，再触发流泪；鼻泪管把多余泪液排入鼻腔，因此流泪时鼻腔也会发酸、流涕。Checkable details. The irritant forms only after cells are broken, so an intact onion smells milder; left after cutting, sulfur volatiles keep releasing. The ophthalmic trigeminal branch carries both corneal pain and touch, so chemical stimulation reads as stinging first and then triggers tears. The nasolacrimal duct drains excess fluid into the nose, which is why the nose also stings and runs during onion cutting.[4][6]

边界。切洋葱流泪是正常反射，不代表眼睛有病；如果不切洋葱也持续流泪、畏光、疼痛、视力模糊或有脓性分泌物，需要眼科排查干眼、结膜炎、角膜上皮损伤或泪道阻塞。把眼泪咸当成身体缺水或盐分失衡的指标并不准确，泪液成分受泪腺局部调节；把催泪挥发物说成单纯辣味，也忽略了它是含硫化合物刺激三叉神经的化学过程。Boundary. Onion tears are a normal reflex and do not signal eye disease. Persistent tearing without onions, photophobia, pain, blurred vision or purulent discharge needs ophthalmology to check dry eye, conjunctivitis, corneal epithelial injury or duct blockage. Salty tears are not a marker of dehydration or salt imbalance, because gland secretion is locally regulated; calling the irritant plain spice ignores that sulfur compounds stimulate the trigeminal nerve chemically.[1][4]

09瞳孔为什么会放大，光线和情绪分别怎样调节它？Why does the pupil dilate, and how do light and emotion regulate it?

副交感支配括约肌收缩，交感支配开大肌放大；亮光下 2–4 mm，暗处 5–8 mm，情绪与药物也参与。Parasympathetic drives the sphincter, sympathetic the dilator; 2–4 mm in daylight, 5–8 mm in dark, with emotion and drugs also involved.

瞳孔是虹膜中央的开孔，大小由两组平滑肌反向调节：瞳孔括约肌受副交感神经支配，收缩时瞳孔缩小；瞳孔开大肌受交感神经支配，收缩时瞳孔放大。正常成人亮光下瞳孔直径约 2–4 毫米，暗处可扩大到 5–8 毫米；放大时进光量增加，缩小时减少像差、加深景深。除了光线，情绪、警觉度、疼痛和某些药物也会改变瞳孔大小。The pupil is the opening in the iris, sized by two smooth muscles working in opposition. The sphincter pupillae, under parasympathetic control, constricts it; the dilator pupillae, under sympathetic control, enlarges it. In daylight an adult pupil measures about 2 to 4 mm, widening to 5 to 8 mm in the dark; dilation raises light intake, constriction reduces aberration and deepens depth of field. Beyond light, emotion, alertness, pain and certain drugs also alter pupil size.[1][4]

光照反射通路。光线照到视网膜，视杆、视锥把光子信号转成神经冲动，经视神经传到中脑顶盖前区；交换神经元后，动眼神经副核发出副交感纤维，经睫状神经节换元，支配括约肌收缩。一侧受光，两侧瞳孔同时缩小，即互感性对光反射；暗处交感通路占优，开大肌把瞳孔拉开。这条反射弧不经过意识皮层，不能靠意志随意控制。The light reflex pathway. Light hits the retina, where rods and cones transduce photons into nerve impulses that travel the optic nerve to the pretectal midbrain. After synapsing, parasympathetic fibers leave the accessory oculomotor nucleus, relay in the ciliary ganglion and contract the sphincter. Light in one eye constricts both pupils, the consensual light reflex; in the dark the sympathetic pathway dominates and the dilator opens the pupil. This arc bypasses conscious cortex and cannot be willed on or off.[4][6]

交感与情绪放大。交感神经在紧张、惊恐、兴奋或警觉状态下被激活，除了加快心率、升高血压，也支配开大肌使瞳孔扩大；这就是人在紧张或专注时瞳孔变大的神经基础。看近物时则相反：调节、会聚伴随瞳孔缩小（近反射三联征），减少近距成像的像差。瞳孔还随年龄变化，老年人瞳孔偏小、暗处扩大能力减弱。Sympathetic drive and emotion. In fear, alarm, excitement or alertness the sympathetic system activates; alongside faster heart rate and blood pressure it drives the dilator to widen the pupil, the neural basis for pupils enlarging under stress or focus. Near focus runs the opposite way: accommodation and convergence come with pupillary constriction, the near triad, which reduces near-image aberration. Pupil size also changes with age, shrinking and dilating less well in older adults.[1][4]

可核对的临床对应。医生用手电筒检查对光反射，看两侧是否等大、收缩是否对称；瞳孔不等大（瞳孔大小不一）、对光反射消失或扩大后不回缩，提示反射弧、中脑、动眼神经或交感通路可能受损。某些药物（如散瞳药、部分抗胆碱药）通过阻断副交感或增强交感作用放大瞳孔；中毒也会在瞳孔大小上留下线索。Clinical correspondences. A flashlight exam tests the reflex for symmetry and constriction; unequal pupils, an absent reflex or failure to constrict after dilation points to a problem in the arc, midbrain, oculomotor nerve or sympathetic pathway. Some drugs, such as mydriatics and anticholinergics, dilate the pupil by blocking parasympathetic drive or boosting sympathetic action, and intoxication also leaves its mark on pupil size.[4][6]

边界。瞳孔大小随光线和情绪波动是正常现象，单次观察不能下结论；需要结合两侧对比、对光反应和病史判断。把瞳孔放大解释成眼睛在用力看，或把紧张时的瞳孔变化当成性格、智力的指标，都没有证据支持。出现突发瞳孔不等大、复视、头痛或眼睑下垂时，应尽快就医排查神经急症。Boundary. Pupil fluctuation with light and emotion is normal, and a single observation cannot diagnose anything; comparison between the two sides, reflex response and history matter. Calling dilation an effort to see harder, or reading pupil change in stress as a marker of personality or intelligence, has no evidence support. Sudden unequal pupils, double vision, headache or drooping eyelid warrants prompt neurological evaluation.[1][6]
