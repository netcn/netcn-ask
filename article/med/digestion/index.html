01食物从口腔到胃，经历了哪些机械研磨和化学消化？What mechanical grinding and chemical digestion does food undergo from the mouth to the stomach?

咀嚼磨碎食物、唾液启动糖分解，胃靠高酸和胃蛋白酶把蛋白质拆开并混匀成食糜。Chewing grinds food and saliva starts carbohydrate breakdown; the stomach uses strong acid and pepsin to split proteins and mix chyme.

食物从口腔到胃，是一条先机械研磨、再化学分解的流水线。咀嚼把大块食物切成细小颗粒，增大食物与消化液接触的表面积；唾液先开始分解碳水化合物，进入胃后胃酸和胃蛋白酶再把蛋白质拆开，形成酸性食糜。吞咽反射和食管蠕动把食团安全推送过咽部与食管，胃则用三层肌肉把内容物搓揉混匀，按节奏分批送入十二指肠。From mouth to stomach, food follows an assembly line of mechanical grinding first and chemical breakdown second. Chewing cuts large pieces into fine particles and raises the surface exposed to digestive juices. Saliva starts carbohydrate digestion, while acid and pepsin in the stomach split proteins until acidic chyme forms. Swallowing and esophageal peristalsis move the bolus past pharynx and esophagus, and the stomach mixes everything with three muscle layers before releasing it in batches to the duodenum.[1][2][3]

口腔是机械消化的起点。牙齿切断研磨食物，舌把食物推向牙面、与唾液混匀；三对大唾液腺每天分泌约 1–1.5 L 唾液，九成以上是水，其余含黏液、唾液淀粉酶、舌脂酶、溶菌酶和免疫球蛋白 A。唾液淀粉酶在近中性环境下把淀粉切成较短糖链，舌脂酶在胃内被激活后承担少量脂肪分解；溶菌酶与免疫球蛋白限制微生物穿入上皮，缓冲成分维持唾液近中性。食物在口腔停留时间短，碳水化合物并不会在这里完全分解。The mouth is where mechanical digestion begins. Teeth cut and grind food, and the tongue presses it against the teeth while mixing it with saliva. The three major salivary glands plus smaller mucosal glands secrete about 1 to 1.5 liters of saliva daily, over ninety per cent water, along with mucus, salivary amylase, lingual lipase, lysozyme and secretory immunoglobulin A. Amylase cuts starch into shorter sugar chains near neutral, and lingual lipase activates later in the stomach for a little fat breakdown. Lysozyme and immunoglobulin A keep microbes from crossing the epithelium, while buffers hold saliva close to neutral. Food spends little time here, so carbohydrates are not fully digested in the mouth.[3]

吞咽分三期推进。口腔期是随意动作，舌把食团推到咽部；咽部期由延髓吞咽中枢控制，软腭上抬封闭鼻咽，咽缩肌依次收缩把食团挤进食管；食管期靠平滑肌交替收缩形成的蠕动波推送，上食管括约肌放松放行，下食管括约肌在食团通过后收缩，防止胃酸反流。食管黏膜只分泌黏液润滑，本身不进行化学消化；下括约肌闭合不全时，胃内容物倒流进入食管，就会出现烧心症状。Swallowing proceeds in three stages. The oral phase is voluntary: the tongue pushes the bolus to the back of the mouth. The pharyngeal phase is controlled by the swallowing center in the medulla: the soft palate closes the nasopharynx and constrictors drive the bolus into the esophagus. In the esophageal phase, alternating smooth-muscle waves of peristalsis push the bolus downward; the upper sphincter relaxes to let it pass, and the lower sphincter contracts afterward to keep acid from refluxing. The esophageal mucosa secretes only lubricating mucus and performs no chemical digestion; if the lower sphincter fails to close, reflux causes heartburn.[3]

食物进入胃后，胃壁额外的斜行肌层进行强有力搓揉，把颗粒磨细并与胃液混匀。空腹胃只有拳头大小，进食后可扩张到约 4 L；壁细胞分泌盐酸和内因子，主细胞分泌胃蛋白酶原，盐酸把胃蛋白酶原激活为胃蛋白酶，并使胃液维持 pH 1.5–3.5 的高酸环境，灭活随食物进入的细菌、使蛋白质变性以便酶解。混合波分批推送食糜，幽门部一次只放约 3 mL 进入十二指肠，其余退回胃体继续混匀；唾液淀粉酶在酸性食糜中失活，舌脂酶则继续工作。Once food reaches the stomach, an extra oblique muscle layer churns vigorously, grinding particles fine and mixing them with gastric juice. An empty stomach is fist-sized but can stretch to about four liters. Parietal cells secrete hydrochloric acid and intrinsic factor, and chief cells secrete pepsinogen; the acid activates pepsinogen into pepsin and keeps gastric juice at pH 1.5 to 3.5, killing swallowed bacteria and denaturing proteins for enzymatic attack. Mixing waves release chyme in batches: about three milliliters pass the pylorus into the duodenum at a time, the rest returning for further mixing. Salivary amylase is inactivated by the acidic chyme, while lingual lipase keeps working.[2][4]

边界需要写清：胃几乎不吸收营养物质，只吸收少量酒精、阿司匹林等可扩散药物；排空速度随食物成分变化，脂肪含量高的一餐在胃内可停留 6 小时以上，液体和碳水化合物排空更快。胃酸的强腐蚀能力由表面碳酸氢根黏液屏障对冲。上述量级来自健康成人的典型测量，个体之间存在差异；食管和胃的机械运动正常工作，前提是括约肌与平滑肌反射完整，只把消化理解成化学反应，会忽略咀嚼、蠕动和排空节奏等机械环节。The boundaries matter. The stomach absorbs almost no nutrients, only small amounts of diffusible drugs such as alcohol and aspirin. Emptying speed depends on meal composition: a fatty meal can stay more than six hours, while liquids and carbohydrates leave faster. Acid's corrosive power is countered by a bicarbonate-rich mucus barrier at the surface. These figures are typical for healthy adults and vary between people. Normal motility depends on intact sphincters and smooth-muscle reflexes; treating digestion as chemistry alone leaves out chewing, peristalsis and the rhythm of emptying.[3][4]

02小肠依靠哪些结构把消化后的营养物质吸收进血液和淋巴？Which structures of the small intestine absorb digested nutrients into blood and lymph?

皱襞、绒毛和微绒毛把吸收面放大到约 200 m²；水溶性营养入毛细血管，脂类入乳糜管。Folds, villi and microvilli expand the absorptive surface to about 200 square meters; water-soluble nutrients enter capillaries, lipids enter lacteals.

小肠是营养吸收的主力场所，靠长度、环形皱襞、绒毛和微绒毛四级放大，把有限的管腔变成约 200 m² 的吸收表面。消化产物穿过绒毛上皮后分两路离开：单糖和氨基酸进入毛细血管，经门静脉送往肝脏；脂肪分解产物进入淋巴管，再经胸导管汇入静脉。每天约 9 L 液体进入小肠，约九成水和八成电解质在这里被回收，剩下的残渣才进入大肠。The small intestine is the main site of nutrient absorption. Through length, circular folds, villi and microvilli, it enlarges a limited lumen into an absorptive surface of about 200 square meters. After crossing the villus epithelium, digestion products leave by two routes: sugars and amino acids enter capillaries and travel to the liver through the portal vein, while lipid products enter lymphatics and reach venous blood through the thoracic duct. About nine liters of fluid enter the small intestine daily, and roughly ninety per cent of its water and eighty per cent of its electrolytes are recovered before residue passes to the large intestine.[5][7]

小肠在活体中长约 2.5–3 m，依次分为十二指肠、空肠和回肠。黏膜和黏膜下层形成深陷的环形皱襞，皱襞上密布 0.5–1 mm 长的指状绒毛，每平方毫米约 20–40 根；绒毛上皮细胞游离面再长出约 1 µm 长的微绒毛，每平方毫米估计 2 亿根。这三级结构把上皮吸收面积扩大 600 倍以上，总面积约 200 m²，超过皮肤表面积一百倍。微绒毛膜上固定着刷状缘酶，在营养物进入细胞前完成碳水化合物和蛋白质的最后几步分解。In a living person the small intestine is about 2.5 to 3 meters long, divided into duodenum, jejunum and ileum. Mucosa and submucosa form deep circular folds carrying dense villi 0.5 to 1 millimeter long, about 20 to 40 per square millimeter. Each epithelial cell then bears microvilli about one micrometer long, an estimated 200 million per square millimeter. These three levels expand the absorptive area more than 600-fold, to roughly 200 square meters, over a hundred times the skin area. Brush-border enzymes on the microvillus membrane finish the last steps of carbohydrate and protein breakdown before nutrients enter the cell.[5]

每根绒毛内部都有一套毛细血管床和一根叫乳糜管的淋巴管。单糖和氨基酸经上皮主动转运进入毛细血管，随血液经门静脉到肝脏处理；长链脂肪酸和甘油一酯先被胆汁酸盐和卵磷脂包成胶束，贴近微绒毛后进入上皮细胞，再包装成乳糜微粒进入乳糜管，沿淋巴系统经胸导管汇入锁骨下静脉。短链脂肪酸水溶性较强，可直接扩散进入绒毛毛细血管。大多数碳水化合物和蛋白质在空肠吸收，铁和钙主要在十二指肠按身体需要量摄取。Inside each villus sit a capillary bed and a lacteal, a lymphatic capillary. Monosaccharides and amino acids cross the epithelium by active transport into capillaries and travel through the portal vein to the liver. Long-chain fatty acids and monoglycerides are first wrapped by bile salts and lecithin into micelles; after approaching the microvilli they enter epithelial cells, are repackaged as chylomicrons, and enter lacteals to reach the subclavian vein via the thoracic duct. Short-chain fatty acids diffuse directly into villus capillaries. Most carbohydrate and protein absorption occurs in the jejunum, while iron and calcium are taken up mainly in the duodenum as needed.[7]

小肠运动方式为吸收争取时间。十二指肠分节运动最快，约每分钟 12 次，回肠约每分钟 8 次；蠕动复合体把食糜缓慢向前推送，一餐的头一部分约 90–120 分钟抵达回肠末端。每天进入小肠的约 9 L 液体中，2–3 L 来自饮水和食物，其余来自消化道分泌；水分顺着溶质转运形成的浓度梯度以渗透方式进入上皮细胞。末端回肠还专门回收胆汁酸，并吸收与内因子结合的维生素 B12。Motility buys time for absorption. Duodenal segmentation runs fastest, about twelve contractions per minute, slowing to about eight in the ileum. Migrating complexes push chyme forward slowly, and the first part of a meal reaches the ileum in roughly 90 to 120 minutes. Of the nine liters entering daily, two to three liters come from drinks and food, the rest from secretions. Water follows the concentration gradient created by solute transport and enters cells by osmosis. The terminal ileum also reclaims bile salts and absorbs vitamin B12 bound to intrinsic factor.[2][5][7]

边界需要写清：吸收并非均匀铺满整段小肠，不同营养物各有主要区段；表面面积估计随测量方法和个体差异变化，200 m² 是教材常用量级。小肠因手术切除或疾病变短后，水分、电解质和营养物的回收都会受影响。水的吸收始终跟随溶质转运，不能脱离糖、氨基酸和离子的主动运输单独理解；把绒毛只看成一层光滑内壁，会漏掉放大面积和分路运输这两个关键设计。The boundaries are clear. Absorption is not spread evenly along the intestine, and each nutrient has its main segment. Surface-area estimates vary with method and individual differences; 200 square meters is the textbook order of magnitude. Surgical shortening or disease reduces absorptive area, and recovery of water, electrolytes and nutrients suffers. Water absorption always follows solute transport and cannot be understood apart from active transport of sugars, amino acids and ions. Treating the villus wall as a smooth lining would miss area amplification and split-route transport.[5][7]

03肝、胆囊和胰腺怎样向小肠输送消化液，胆汁酸为什么要回收再利用？How do the liver, gallbladder and pancreas deliver digestive juices to the small intestine, and why are bile salts recycled?

肝脏每日分泌约 1 L 胆汁，胆囊浓缩储存，胰腺分泌含碳酸氢盐和酶的胰液；胆汁酸经回肠肠肝循环回收。The liver secretes about a liter of bile daily, the gallbladder concentrates it, and the pancreas sends bicarbonate- and enzyme-rich juice; bile salts return via enterohepatic circulation.

肝脏、胆囊和胰腺是消化道的附属腺体：肝脏持续合成胆汁，胆囊在两餐之间浓缩储存胆汁，胰腺分泌含酶和碳酸氢盐的胰液，进食后按激素信号把消化液送入十二指肠。胆汁酸完成乳化后并不随粪便全部丢失，而是在回肠被重吸收、经门静脉回到肝脏再分泌，这条回路叫肠肝循环。三者分工明确：胆汁负责脂肪分散，胰液负责三大营养物酶解，碳酸氢盐负责把酸性食糜调回适合酶工作的弱碱性环境。The liver, gallbladder and pancreas are accessory digestive glands. The liver continuously makes bile, the gallbladder concentrates and stores it between meals, and the pancreas secretes enzyme- and bicarbonate-rich juice. After a meal, hormonal signals deliver these juices to the duodenum. Once bile salts finish emulsifying fat, they are reabsorbed in the ileum, return to the liver through the portal vein and are secreted again; this loop is the enterohepatic circulation. Their jobs are divided: bile disperses fat, pancreatic enzymes break down the three major nutrient classes, and bicarbonate buffers acidic chyme to a weakly alkaline range suited to enzymes.[6]

肝细胞每天分泌约 1 L 胆汁，是含水、胆汁酸盐、胆色素、卵磷脂等磷脂、电解质、胆固醇和甘油三酯的黄绿色碱性液体。胆汁酸盐和磷脂一端亲水、一端疏水，能像洗洁精一样把脂肪大滴拆成细小乳滴，增大脂肪与脂肪酶接触的表面积。胆汁本身不含消化酶，完成的是物理乳化；胆红素是衰老红细胞分解的废物，经胆汁排出。两餐之间壶腹括约肌关闭，胆汁转入胆囊，黏膜吸收水和离子，浓缩最多约 10 倍。Hepatocytes secrete about one liter of bile per day, a yellow-green alkaline fluid with water, bile salts, pigments, phospholipids such as lecithin, electrolytes, cholesterol and triglycerides. Bile salts and phospholipids have hydrophilic and hydrophobic ends, so like dish soap they break large fat globules into fine droplets, increasing the surface available to lipase. Bile contains no digestive enzyme; what it does is physical emulsification. Bilirubin, a waste product of old red blood cells, is excreted in bile and gives it color. Between meals the ampullary sphincter closes, bile diverts to the gallbladder, whose mucosa absorbs water and ions to concentrate it up to tenfold.[6]

胰腺每天分泌 1 L 以上清亮胰液，主要成分为水、钠盐、碳酸氢盐和多种消化酶。碳酸氢盐使胰液保持 pH 7.2–8.2 的弱碱性，中和酸性食糜、灭活胃蛋白酶，为小肠内对 pH 敏感的酶创造适宜环境。胰淀粉酶分解淀粉，胰脂肪酶分解脂肪，核酸酶分解核酸；蛋白酶以无活性酶原形式分泌，进入十二指肠后才激活，否则会消化胰腺自身。淀粉酶、脂肪酶和核酸酶则以活性形式分泌，因为它们不会攻击胰腺组织。The pancreas secretes more than one liter of clear juice daily, mostly water, sodium salts, bicarbonate and several enzymes. Bicarbonate keeps the juice weakly alkaline at pH 7.2 to 8.2, buffering acidic chyme, inactivating pepsin and suiting pH-sensitive enzymes in the small intestine. Amylase breaks down starch, lipase fat, nucleases nucleic acids. Proteases are secreted as inactive zymogens and activate only in the duodenum; otherwise they would digest the gland itself, as in pancreatitis. Amylase, lipase and nucleases are active from the start because they do not attack pancreatic tissue.[6]

这些外分泌活动由激素和副交感神经共同调度。酸性食糜进入十二指肠，刺激肠壁释放促胰液素，促使导管细胞分泌富含碳酸氢盐的胰液；蛋白质和脂肪刺激缩胆囊素，促使腺泡分泌富含酶的胰液，并让胆囊收缩、排出胆汁。进食头期和胃期，迷走神经的副交感兴奋也推动胰液分泌。胰腺分泌的碳酸氢盐通常与胃产生的盐酸量相匹配，两者在血液侧相互中和，维持流向肝脏的静脉血 pH 稳定。These outputs are coordinated by hormones and the parasympathetic nervous system. Acidic chyme in the duodenum triggers secretin, driving duct cells to secrete bicarbonate-rich juice. Protein and fat stimulate cholecystokinin, which prompts acinar cells to secrete enzyme-rich juice and contracts the gallbladder to release bile. During cephalic and gastric phases, vagal parasympathetic firing also promotes pancreatic secretion. Pancreatic bicarbonate usually matches the hydrochloric acid produced by the stomach, and the two neutralize each other on the blood side, keeping venous pH to the liver stable.[2][6]

边界需要写清：肠肝循环只在回肠完整时高效进行，末端回肠切除或病变会打断回收，迫使肝脏加快合成；胆囊切除后胆汁持续低浓度流入肠道，脂肪消化节奏随之改变。上述分泌量是健康成人的日均值，随进食量和成分波动。乳化不等于把脂肪变成可吸收分子，后者仍靠胰脂肪酶和上皮内包装；把肝脏只说成解毒器官，会漏掉它分泌胆汁、处理门静脉营养物的外分泌角色。The boundaries are clear. Enterohepatic circulation works efficiently only with an intact terminal ileum; resection or disease there breaks the loop and speeds liver synthesis. After gallbladder removal, bile flows continuously at lower concentration, changing the rhythm of fat digestion. The secretion volumes are adult averages that fluctuate with meal size and composition. Emulsification does not make fat absorbable on its own; that still needs pancreatic lipase and intracellular packaging. Calling the liver only a detox organ leaves out its steady bile secretion and processing of portal-borne nutrients.[6][7]

04大肠怎样处理食物残渣中的水分和电解质，肠道菌群发酵起什么作用？How does the large intestine handle water and electrolytes in food residue, and what role does gut bacterial fermentation play?

结肠回收剩余水和盐分、把 500 mL 残渣浓缩成约 150 mL 粪便；菌群发酵残余糖产短链脂肪酸与气体。The colon recovers remaining water and salts, concentrating 500 mL of residue into about 150 mL of stool; microbiota ferment residual sugars into short-chain fatty acids and gas.

大肠接收小肠送来的食物残渣，主要任务是回收剩余水分和电解质、储存并排出粪便，本身不再分泌消化酶。腔内菌群完成最后的化学分解：残余碳水化合物被发酵，产生短链脂肪酸和气体，部分维生素由细菌合成。每天约 500 mL 残渣进入盲肠，最终约 150 mL 形成粪便；残渣在结肠停留 12–24 小时，水分被逐步吸收，内容物从液态变成半固体。The large intestine receives residue from the small intestine, mainly recovering remaining water and electrolytes, storing and eliminating stool; it secretes no digestive enzymes. Luminal bacteria carry out the final chemical breakdown: residual carbohydrates ferment into short-chain fatty acids and gases, and some vitamins are microbial products. About 500 milliliters of residue enter the cecum daily, and roughly 150 milliliters become feces. Residue lingers 12 to 24 hours in the colon while water is absorbed, turning liquid into semisolid stool.[1][5]

结肠没有环形皱襞和绒毛，肠壁以吸收细胞和分泌黏液的杯状细胞为主，腺体不产酶。进入盲肠的残渣已几乎不含可直接吸收的营养，主要是水、离子、植物纤维等不可消化物和大量细菌。结肠通过袋状往返运动、较慢蠕动和集团蠕动混合并推动残渣，杯状细胞分泌的黏液润滑粪便，保护肠壁免受细菌产生的酸和气体刺激。回盲括约肌平时收缩，回肠蠕动增强时才放松，控制残渣进入结肠的节奏并阻止反流。The colon has no circular folds or villi. Its wall is mainly absorptive enterocytes and mucus-secreting goblet cells, and its glands produce no enzymes. Residue reaching the cecum holds almost no directly absorbable nutrients, mostly water, ions, indigestible material such as fiber and many bacteria. Haustral shuffling, slower peristalsis and mass movements mix and propel the residue, while goblet-cell mucus lubricates stool and protects the wall from bacterial acids and gases. The ileocecal sphincter stays contracted and relaxes when ileal motility rises, controlling entry and preventing backflow.[5][7]

结肠内生活着 700 种以上细菌，多数是留在腔内不致病的共生菌。上段肠道未能消化的碳水化合物由细菌糖解发酵，生成氢、二氧化碳和甲烷等气体；一天之内结肠产气最多可达 1500 mL，乳糖不耐受者未被吸收的乳糖在结肠发酵，呼气中可测出氢。水溶性较强的短链脂肪酸可直接被吸收细胞摄取进入血液，细菌还能合成部分维生素。菌群组成因人而异，影响能量提取和局部炎症，与肥胖和代谢性疾病存在关联，但相关不等于简单的因果开关。More than 700 bacterial species live in the colon, most harmless commensals confined to the lumen. Carbohydrates not digested higher up ferment by saccharolytic action, producing hydrogen, carbon dioxide and methane that make intestinal gas; up to 1,500 milliliters of flatus can form daily, and unabsorbed lactose in intolerance ferments to hydrogen detectable in breath. Water-soluble short-chain fatty acids enter enterocytes directly and reach the blood, and bacteria synthesize certain vitamins. Composition varies between people and affects energy harvest and local inflammation, linking microbiota to obesity and metabolic disease, but this association is not a simple causal switch.[5]

水分和电解质的回收决定粪便性状。残渣在结肠停留过久，额外水分被吸收，粪便变硬，可能便秘；通过过快，水分来不及吸收，就会腹泻。结肠保留了大量腺体和黏液分泌，即使整段切除，消化吸收功能也不会显著受损，粪便可由回肠造口袋收集。阑尾在腹泻等疾病后可能作为细菌储库，帮助菌群重新定植；上皮紧密连接、黏液层和黏膜相关淋巴组织共同维持屏障，把菌群限制在腔内。Water and electrolyte recovery determines stool character. If residue stays too long, extra water is absorbed and stool hardens, potentially causing constipation; if it passes too quickly, water cannot be recovered and diarrhea follows. The colon retains many glands and mucus secretions, so even complete removal does not significantly impair digestion, and waste collects in an ileostomy bag. The appendix may act as a bacterial reservoir after diarrheal illness, helping flora repopulate. Tight junctions, mucus layer and gut-associated lymphoid tissue together keep bacteria inside the lumen.[5][8]

边界需要写清：大肠不进行酶性消化，化学过程完全依赖腔内菌群；菌群种类和数量随饮食、年龄和用药变化，不能用单一好坏菌清单概括。上述气体量、物种数和停留时间是成人典型范围，个体差异很大。婴儿肠道菌群尚未成熟、胃酸较弱，对肉毒杆菌孢子抵抗有限，这是 1 岁以下不宜食用蜂蜜的背景。把腹胀或排气简单归因于肠胃虚弱，会忽略食物成分、发酵程度和结肠通过时间这些可测量环节。The boundaries are clear. The large intestine performs no enzymatic digestion; its chemistry depends entirely on luminal bacteria. Species and counts shift with diet, age and medication, so a simple good-or-bad list cannot describe the community. Gas volume, species count and transit time are typical adult ranges with wide variation. An infant microbiota is immature and stomach acid weaker, offering limited resistance to botulinum spores, the background to avoiding honey before age one. Blaming bloating on a weak stomach misses measurable food composition, fermentation and colonic transit time.[1][5]

05消化系统受哪些神经和激素信号调控，胃肠活动怎样随饥饿与饱胀调整？Which neural and hormonal signals regulate the digestive system, and how does gut activity change with hunger and fullness?

迷走神经与肠神经系统完成局部反射，胃泌素、促胰液素、缩胆囊素分段调度；空腹 MMC 收缩产生肠鸣。The vagus and enteric nervous system run local reflexes; gastrin, secretin and CCK schedule segments, and fasting MMC contractions produce bowel sounds.

消化系统受神经与内分泌信号双重调控，既接受迷走神经等外周输入，也靠肠神经系统在肠壁内完成局部反射。视、嗅、味觉触发时，头期反射先启动唾液和胃液分泌；食物入胃后，胃壁扩张和胃泌素维持胃期分泌；食糜进入十二指肠后，促胰液素和缩胆囊素调度胰液与胆汁，并通过肠-胃反射放慢胃排空。The digestive system is regulated by a dual neural and endocrine network. It receives input from peripheral nerves such as the vagus, while the enteric nervous system completes local reflexes within the gut wall. Signals lead at different meal stages: the cephalic reflex starts saliva and gastric secretion on sight, smell or thought of food; once food reaches the stomach, stretch and gastrin sustain the gastric phase; when chyme enters the duodenum, secretin and cholecystokinin schedule pancreatic juice and bile, and the enterogastric reflex slows emptying.[2][8]

调控网络分三层。最外层是自主神经系统：副交感纤维主要经迷走神经促进胃肠运动和分泌，交感兴奋总体抑制消化。中间层是肠神经系统，即埋在壁内的神经元丛，不经大脑就能感受局部扩张和化学刺激，协调蠕动、分节运动和腺体分泌，形成短反射。最内层是壁内散在的内分泌细胞，把信号分泌进血液。这些反射在食物入胃前就已准备。The control network has three layers. The outer autonomic layer: parasympathetic fibers, mainly vagal, promote motility and secretion, while sympathetic activation generally inhibits digestion. The middle enteric layer consists of neuron plexuses in the gut wall that sense local stretch and chemistry and coordinate peristalsis, segmentation and glands as short reflexes without going through the brain. The inner layer holds endocrine cells scattered in the wall that release signals into blood. These central reflexes prepare the gut before food arrives.[2][4]

胃液分泌分头期、胃期和肠期。头期发生在食物入胃之前，由食物气味、味道、视觉或念头触发迷走反射，时间较短；胃期持续约 3–4 小时，食物入胃后由局部神经和激素机制启动，胃壁牵张和蛋白质消化产物刺激胃泌素释放，胃泌素再促进壁细胞分泌胃酸；肠期同时有兴奋和抑制成分，十二指肠被食糜扩张后，肠-胃反射抑制胃分泌并关闭幽门括约肌，避免食糜一次涌入超出小肠处理能力。Gastric secretion runs through cephalic, gastric and intestinal phases. The cephalic phase occurs before food arrives, triggered by smell, taste, sight or thought through a vagal reflex and lasts briefly. The gastric phase lasts about three to four hours, set in motion after food arrives by local neural and hormonal mechanisms: stretch and protein products stimulate gastrin, which prompts parietal cells to secrete acid. The intestinal phase has excitatory and inhibitory elements; as the duodenum fills, the enterogastric reflex inhibits secretion and closes the pylorus, preventing one chyme flood from exceeding intestinal capacity.[2][6]

胃肠激素把一段肠道的状态通报给其他器官。胃泌素除刺激胃酸，还增强回肠运动、放松回盲括约肌；促胰液素受酸性食糜刺激，促使胰腺分泌富含碳酸氢盐的胰液；缩胆囊素受蛋白和脂肪刺激，促使分泌酶并收缩胆囊。局部反射连接胃与下游：胃扩张引起胃回肠反射增强回肠分节，胃刺激经胃结肠反射加快结肠集团蠕动。一次进食过量时，胃扩张经胃结肠反射进一步加快结肠蠕动；食糜量超过小肠消化吸收速率，未吸收的糖类和脂肪形成渗透负荷，把水分留在肠腔，结肠来不及回收水分，粪便就变稀、次数增多。空腹时移行性复合运动以清扫波把残渣和细菌推向下游，形成饥饿时的肠鸣。Gut hormones report one segment's state to other organs. Besides acid, gastrin enhances ileal motility and relaxes the ileocecal sphincter. Secretin, triggered by acidic chyme, drives bicarbonate-rich pancreatic juice and increases bile production. Cholecystokinin, stimulated by protein and fat, prompts enzyme-rich juice and gallbladder contraction. Local reflexes link stomach to bowel: distention provokes the gastroileal reflex, strengthening ileal segmentation, and stomach stimulation accelerates colonic mass movements via the gastrocolic reflex. After an oversized meal, gastric distension further accelerates colonic motility; when chyme exceeds the small intestine's digestive and absorptive capacity, unabsorbed sugars and fats create an osmotic load that keeps water in the lumen, and the colon cannot reclaim it fast enough, so stool becomes loose and more frequent. Between meals the migrating motor complex sweeps residue and bacteria downstream; these contractions are the main source of hungry growling.[2][5][8]

边界需要写清：消化调控是冗余网络，同一件事常由神经、局部反射和激素多条路径并行，不存在单一主管开关。心理因素会影响肠蠕动和酶分泌，紧张时口干、食欲下降就是交感与副交感切换的表现。这里描述健康成人的基础节律，饱胀感还涉及下丘脑与全身能量信号，不只由肠道决定。把肠鸣只理解为肚子空了，或把某一激素说成唯一控制者，都会忽略局部反射与自主神经配合。The boundaries are clear. Regulation is a redundant network in which nerves, local reflexes and hormones often run in parallel, so there is no single master switch. Psychological factors clearly affect motility, enzyme secretion and gut function; dry mouth and lost appetite under tension show the switch between autonomic tones. This chapter describes baseline rhythm in healthy adults; hunger and satiety also involve hypothalamic and whole-body energy signals, not the gut alone. Reducing a growl to an empty stomach, or naming one hormone sole controller, ignores local reflexes and autonomic teamwork.[2][8]

06口臭为什么自己通常闻不到？Why can't people usually smell their own bad breath?

厌氧菌分解含硫氨基酸生成 VSC，嗅觉受体对持续自身气味快速适应，本人察觉不到。Anaerobes make VSCs from sulfur amino acids, and olfactory receptors adapt to a constant self-odor, so the owner misses it.

口臭自己闻不到，通常有两层原因：气味分子由口腔细菌产生，而人的嗅觉对持续存在的自身气味会快速适应。口臭的主要成分是挥发性硫化物（VSC），不是食物残渣本身的味道；它们来自舌背、牙周和牙缝里厌氧菌对含硫氨基酸的分解。自己的呼吸长期经过同一套嗅觉受体，受体反应逐渐下调，于是习惯了这种气味。Bad breath goes unnoticed by its owner for two reasons: odor molecules are produced by oral bacteria, and the sense of smell adapts quickly to a constant self-odor. Halitosis is dominated by volatile sulfur compounds, or VSCs, rather than the smell of food debris itself. These compounds form when anaerobic bacteria in the tongue coating, periodontal pockets and gaps between teeth break down sulfur-containing amino acids. Because one's own breath constantly passes the same olfactory receptors, receptor responses gradually decline and the odor becomes familiar.[3][9]

口腔是细菌密集的环境。三对大唾液腺每天分泌约 1–1.5 L 唾液，含淀粉酶、溶菌酶和免疫球蛋白 A，唾液冲刷和缓冲作用限制细菌堆积；睡眠时唾液分泌减少，舌背和牙缝里的食物碎屑、脱落上皮被细菌分解，晨起口气就更明显。舌背有凹陷的乳头结构，容易积存舌苔，是厌氧菌最集中的部位之一；牙周袋、补牙缝隙和智齿周围也容易藏污纳垢。The mouth is a densely bacterial environment. The major salivary glands secrete about 1 to 1.5 liters of saliva daily, with amylase, lysozyme and secretory IgA; saliva's flushing and buffering limits bacterial buildup. Saliva flow drops during sleep, food particles and shed epithelial cells on the tongue and between teeth are left to bacterial action, which is why morning breath is stronger. The tongue dorsum has recessed papillae that trap coating and concentrate anaerobes; periodontal pockets, fillings and wisdom-tooth areas also collect debris.[3][9]

厌氧菌在低氧环境下分解半胱氨酸、蛋氨酸等含硫氨基酸，产生硫化氢（H2S）、甲硫醇（CH3SH）和二甲基硫醚等 VSC。这些物质浓度很低就能被嗅觉检出，气味阈值低；甲硫醇常被认为是臭味最突出的成分。除硫化物外，细菌代谢还可产生吲哚、粪臭素和有机酸，混合成人们感觉到的口气。舌苔厚、牙龈出血、口干和吸烟会加重 VSC 生成。In low-oxygen settings, anaerobes break down cysteine, methionine and other sulfur amino acids into hydrogen sulfide, methyl mercaptan and dimethyl sulfide. These VSCs are detected at very low concentrations because their odor thresholds are low, and methyl mercaptan is often the most malodorous component. Bacterial metabolism also yields indole, skatole and organic acids that mix into the perceived breath odor. Heavy tongue coating, bleeding gums, dry mouth and smoking increase VSC production.[9][10]

嗅觉适应解释了为什么本人察觉不到。感觉受体在持续刺激下动作电位发放频率下降，对恒定刺激逐渐变迟钝；嗅觉受体属于适应较快的类型，人对自己身上和自己呼出的气味尤其容易适应。别人刚靠近时闻到的是相对新鲜的气味分子，而自己的呼吸环境始终不变，受体已经下调反应。鼻腔还存在鼻后通路，口腔气味可经鼻咽部到达嗅区，但长期接触同样造成适应。Olfactory adaptation explains why the owner does not notice. Sensory receptors lower their action-potential firing rate under sustained stimulation and become less responsive to a constant stimulus; olfactory receptors adapt relatively fast, and people adapt especially well to smells on or coming from themselves. Another person nearby encounters relatively fresh odor molecules, while the self-breathing environment never changes, so receptors have already down-regulated. Oral odor can also reach the olfactory area through the retronasal route via the nasopharynx, but prolonged contact there produces the same adaptation.[3][10]

边界需要写清：本章只解释口气来源与适应机制，不推荐漱口水或用药方案。绝大多数口臭源自口腔局部，清洁舌背、牙龈和牙缝后仍持续，或伴牙龈出血、牙痛、鼻窦症状、反酸和全身气味时，应由口腔科或耳鼻喉科排查。少数口气来自鼻窦、胃食管或代谢性疾病，不能都按口腔问题处理；口干、真菌感染或假牙清洁不当也会改变口气。The boundary: this chapter explains the source of breath odor and adaptation, and recommends no mouthwash or medication. Most halitosis originates in the mouth; if it persists after cleaning the tongue, gums and gaps, or comes with bleeding gums, tooth pain, sinus symptoms, reflux or a systemic odor, dental or ENT evaluation is warranted. A minority of breath odor arises from sinus, gastroesophageal or metabolic disease and cannot all be treated as an oral problem; dry mouth, fungal infection and poorly cleaned dentures also alter breath odor.[9][10]

07为什么人会放屁，有的屁为什么很臭？Why do people pass gas, and why is some flatus so smelly?

气体来自吞咽空气与大肠发酵，臭味由少量挥发性硫化物决定，随饮食和菌群变化。Gas comes from swallowed air and colonic fermentation; odor is set by a little volatile sulfur compounds and shifts with diet and microbiota.

放屁是消化道正常的排气过程，气体来自两条路径：吞咽下去的空气，和大肠菌群发酵未消化食物产生的气体；气味则主要由少量含硫化合物决定，而不是气体总量多大。一天之内结肠产气最多可达约 1500 mL，多数人每天排气多次，量和气味随饮食、菌群和吞咽习惯变化。Passing gas is normal gastrointestinal exhaust, with gas from two routes: swallowed air and colonic fermentation of undigested food. The odor is mainly decided by small amounts of sulfur compounds rather than total gas volume. The colon can produce up to about 1,500 mL of gas per day, and most people pass gas several times; volume and odor vary with diet, microbiota and swallowing habits.[5][11]

第一条路径是吞咽空气。进食快、边吃边说话、嚼口香糖或喝碳酸饮料时，空气随唾液和食物进入胃，再经小肠到达结肠；这部分气体以氮气为主，本身基本无味。吞咽空气过多会让腹胀和排气次数增加，但它对臭味的贡献有限。第二条路径是大肠发酵：小肠没有完全消化的碳水化合物，如豆类纤维、部分淀粉、乳糖和某些果蔬糖，进入结肠后被 700 余种细菌分解，产生氢、二氧化碳和甲烷。The first route is swallowed air. Fast eating, talking while eating, gum chewing or carbonated drinks carry air with saliva and food into the stomach, then through the small intestine to the colon; this gas is mostly nitrogen and essentially odorless. Excess aerophagia raises bloating and passage frequency but contributes little to the smell. The second route is colonic fermentation: carbohydrates the small intestine does not fully digest, such as legume fiber, some starch, lactose and certain fruit and vegetable sugars, reach the colon and are broken down by over 700 bacterial species into hydrogen, carbon dioxide and methane.[5][11]

臭味来自发酵过程中少量生成的挥发性硫化物。当底物含硫或菌群分解含硫氨基酸时，可产生硫化氢、甲硫醇（甲基硫醇）和二甲基硫醚；这些物质嗅觉阈值极低，微量就有明显臭味。吲哚、粪臭素等挥发物也参与气味。同一个人的排气气味会随当天饮食改变，吃豆类、十字花科蔬菜或高脂餐之后，硫化物和发酵产物增多，气味往往更重。Odor comes from small amounts of volatile sulfur compounds formed during fermentation. When substrates contain sulfur or bacteria break down sulfur amino acids, hydrogen sulfide, methanethiol and dimethyl sulfide can appear; their olfactory thresholds are extremely low, so traces produce a noticeable smell. Indole, skatole and other volatiles also contribute. A person's flatus odor changes with the day's diet; after beans, cruciferous vegetables or high-fat meals, sulfur compounds and fermentation products rise and the odor often worsens.[5][11]

气体在结肠内并非全部排出。以氢为例，生成的氢气只有约一成经直肠排出，其余被其他结肠细菌消耗，或吸收入门静脉进入血液后经呼吸排出；甲烷由产甲烷菌生成，使排气更易燃、气味组成不同。菌群组成因人而异，影响发酵产物和气体比例，这也是有的人更容易腹胀、排气更臭的原因之一。排气增多也与食物不耐受有关，例如乳糖未被小肠吸收，进入结肠后大量发酵。Not all colonic gas is expelled. Of the hydrogen produced, only about ten percent leaves via the rectum; the rest is consumed by other colonic bacteria or absorbed into the portal blood and later exhaled. Methane is made by methanogens, making flatus more combustible and shifting its odor profile. Microbiota composition varies between people and affects fermentation products and gas ratios, which partly explains why some people bloat more and pass smellier gas. Increased flatus also relates to food intolerance: when lactose is not absorbed in the small intestine, it ferments in the colon in large amounts.[1][11]

边界需要写清：排气是正常生理现象，臭味不代表排出毒素。长期明显胀气、排气骤增并伴持续腹痛、排便习惯改变、便血、体重下降或发热时，需要消化科评估，排查吸收不良、炎症性肠病等问题。本章描述的气体量和成分来自成人典型测量，个体差异很大；把臭屁简单归为肠胃虚弱，会忽略食物成分、吞咽空气和结肠通过时间这些可测量环节。The boundary: passing gas is normal physiology, and odor does not mean toxins are being expelled. Persistent marked bloating, a sudden rise in gas with ongoing abdominal pain, changed bowel habits, blood in stool, weight loss or fever warrants gastroenterology evaluation to check for malabsorption or inflammatory bowel disease. The gas volumes and compositions cited are typical adult measurements with wide individual variation. Blaming smelly gas on a weak stomach ignores measurable factors such as food composition, swallowed air and colonic transit time.[5][11]

08为什么说肠道是第二大脑？Why is the gut called the second brain?

肠神经系统含数以亿计神经元，靠壁内短反射协调运动与分泌，与迷走神经和激素双向配合。The enteric nervous system has hundreds of millions of neurons, runs wall-level short reflexes for motility and secretion, and works bidirectionally with vagus and hormones.

肠道常被称为第二大脑，指的是肠壁内有一套可以独立完成局部反射的庞大神经网络，而不是说肠道会思考或有意识。这套肠神经系统由埋在胃肠壁内的神经元丛组成，能感受局部扩张和化学刺激，直接协调蠕动、分节运动和腺体分泌；中枢神经系统通过迷走神经等输入做整体调制，但很多局部动作不需要大脑下令。The gut is often called the second brain because its wall contains a large neural network that completes local reflexes on its own; this does not mean the gut thinks or has consciousness. This enteric nervous system consists of neuron plexuses embedded in the gastrointestinal wall, sensing local stretch and chemistry and directly coordinating peristalsis, segmentation and gland secretion. The central nervous system modulates the whole through vagal and other inputs, but many local actions do not require a brain order.[2][8]

肠神经系统分两组主要神经丛。肌间神经丛位于环行肌与纵行肌之间，主要调节胃肠运动；黏膜下神经丛位于黏膜下层，调节腺体分泌、局部血流和上皮功能。两套神经丛之间有纤维连接，接收机械感受器、化学感受器和肠道内分泌细胞的信号。整个网络包含数以亿计的神经元，数量级接近脊髓水平，这是第二大脑说法的解剖学来源；神经元之间还通过局部递质和中间神经元形成反射回路。The enteric nervous system has two main plexuses. The myenteric plexus lies between circular and longitudinal muscle and mainly regulates gut motility; the submucosal plexus sits in the submucosa and controls gland secretion, local blood flow and epithelial function. The two connect by fibers and receive signals from mechanoreceptors, chemoreceptors and gut endocrine cells. The whole network holds hundreds of millions of neurons, a count approaching spinal-cord scale; this anatomical scale is where the second-brain phrase comes from, and local transmitters and interneurons form reflex circuits.[1][8]

局部短反射在肠壁内闭环完成。食糜扩张肠段，感受器激活局部神经元，协调近端收缩、远端放松，把内容物向前推送；分节运动把食糜与消化液混匀。这些反射不经过大脑就能运转，所以即使切断外来神经联系，肠道仍能保留相当一部分运动和分泌节律。肠壁还散布内分泌细胞，把一段肠道的状态通过激素信号通报给胃、胰腺、胆囊和肝脏。Local short reflexes close the loop inside the gut wall. When chyme stretches a segment, receptors activate local neurons that coordinate proximal contraction and distal relaxation, pushing contents forward; segmentation mixes chyme with digestive juices. These reflexes run without passing through the brain, so even after external nerve connections are cut, the gut retains much of its motility and secretion rhythm. Endocrine cells scattered in the wall report one segment's state by hormonal signals to the stomach, pancreas, gallbladder and liver.[2][8]

中枢与外周配合而非各自为政。迷走神经的副交感纤维促进胃肠运动和分泌，交感兴奋总体抑制消化；胃扩张、酸性食糜和脂肪通过局部反射与胃肠激素，把上游状态传给下游，例如胃扩张增强回肠分节，胃刺激加快结肠集团蠕动。饥饿时的肠鸣来自空腹期间的移行性复合运动清扫波。情绪和紧张也会影响肠道，因为脑与肠之间存在双向信号通路，而不只是肠道单方面受大脑支配。Central and peripheral work together rather than in isolation. Vagal parasympathetic fibers promote gut motility and secretion, while sympathetic activation generally inhibits digestion. Gastric distention, acidic chyme and fat pass upstream state to downstream segments through local reflexes and gut hormones: stomach stretch strengthens ileal segmentation, and stomach stimulation accelerates colonic mass movements. Hunger growls come from the migrating motor complex sweeping waves between meals. Mood and tension also affect the gut because brain and gut communicate bidirectionally, rather than the intestine obeying the brain alone.[1][8]

边界需要写清：第二大脑是比喻，肠神经系统没有意识、记忆或决策能力，不能等同于人脑。本章描述健康成人的基础调控节律，饥饿、饱胀和情绪反应还涉及下丘脑和全身能量信号。持续腹痛、排便异常、腹胀或营养吸收障碍需要消化科评估；把胃肠不适简单说成肠神经系统乱掉，会忽略饮食、感染、炎症和结构病变这些可检查的原因。The boundary: second brain is a metaphor; the enteric nervous system has no consciousness, memory or decision-making and cannot be equated with the human brain. This chapter describes baseline regulatory rhythm in healthy adults, and hunger, satiety and emotional responses also involve the hypothalamus and whole-body energy signals. Ongoing abdominal pain, abnormal bowel habits, bloating or malabsorption warrant gastroenterology evaluation. Blaming gut discomfort on a misfiring enteric network ignores checkable causes such as diet, infection, inflammation and structural disease.[2][8]
