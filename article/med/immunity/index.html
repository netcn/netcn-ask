01免疫系统怎样识别病原体？疫苗为什么能预防疾病？How does the immune system recognize pathogens, and why do vaccines prevent disease?

先天免疫先拦截异物，T/B 细胞针对性扩增并产生抗体；疫苗诱导记忆细胞和抗体，再次暴露时快速应答。Innate defenses intercept invaders first; T/B cells expand specifically and make antibodies. Vaccines induce memory cells and antibodies for a fast response on re-exposure.

免疫系统通过“识别自我与非我”来保护身体。病原体表面有被免疫系统称为抗原的分子结构，先天免疫先靠皮肤、黏膜、纤毛、杀菌物质和吞噬细胞快速拦截异物；遇到入侵后，适应性免疫再由 T 淋巴细胞和 B 淋巴细胞针对性扩增。T 细胞直接杀伤被感染细胞并调控免疫反应，B 细胞分化为浆细胞产生抗体，抗体结合抗原以中和病原体、标记清除。The immune system protects the body by distinguishing self from non-self. Pathogens carry surface molecules called antigens; innate immunity first blocks invaders through skin, mucosa, cilia, antimicrobial substances and phagocytes, while adaptive immunity then expands specific T and B lymphocytes. T cells directly kill infected cells and regulate the response, and B cells mature into plasma cells that make antibodies, which bind antigens to neutralize pathogens and mark them for clearance.[1][2]

识别的关键是受体与抗原的匹配。T 细胞受体和 B 细胞表面的免疫球蛋白受体经过基因重排，形成能识别成千上万种抗原的受体库；每克隆只识别一种抗原，入侵后对应克隆被选择并大量增殖。抗体是 B 细胞分泌的 Y 形分子，按恒定区不同分为若干类，在血液、黏膜和组织中分别承担中和、调理和激活补体等功能；抗体滴度即血液中抗体的浓度水平，常用于衡量免疫应答强度。Recognition hinges on matching receptor to antigen. T-cell receptors and B-cell surface immunoglobulin receptors rearrange their genes to form a receptor library recognizing thousands of antigens; each clone recognizes one antigen, and the matching clone is selected and multiplies after invasion. Antibodies are Y-shaped molecules secreted by B cells, falling into classes by constant region and acting in blood, mucosa and tissue through neutralization, opsonization and complement activation; antibody titer is the blood concentration, commonly used to measure response strength.[2][3]

疫苗利用免疫记忆预防疾病。疫苗把灭活或减毒病原体、抗原蛋白或核酸片段送入身体，不引起完整发病，却诱导适应性免疫产生记忆 B 细胞、记忆 T 细胞和特异性抗体；真正病原体入侵时，记忆细胞迅速扩增、抗体快速升高，把感染压在早期。抗体滴度在接种后一段时间达到峰值，随后缓慢下降，记忆细胞则在体内存留更久；加强针用于把抗体水平和记忆细胞重新提升。Vaccines prevent disease through immunological memory. They deliver inactivated or attenuated pathogens, antigen proteins or nucleic fragments without causing full disease, yet induce memory B cells, memory T cells and specific antibodies; when the real pathogen invades, memory cells expand rapidly and antibodies rise fast, suppressing infection early. Titer peaks after vaccination and slowly declines, while memory cells persist longer; booster shots raise antibody levels and memory cells again.[2][3]

先天与适应性免疫分工配合。先天反应快但不针对特定抗原，提供第一道和第二道防线；适应性反应较慢但高度特异，并留下长期记忆。OpenStax 生物学教材把先天免疫的吞噬、炎症和补体，与适应性免疫的 T 细胞、B 细胞、抗体及记忆过程分别成节描述；抗原识别、克隆选择和免疫记忆是理解疫苗和感染恢复的共同基础。Innate and adaptive immunity cooperate. The innate response is fast but non-specific, giving the first and second lines of defense; the adaptive response is slower but highly specific and leaves long-term memory. The OpenStax biology text separates innate phagocytosis, inflammation and complement from adaptive T cells, B cells, antibodies and memory; antigen recognition, clonal selection and memory form the common basis for understanding vaccines and recovery from infection.[1][2][3]

边界：本章讲免疫识别、抗体与记忆的基本框架，不展开具体疫苗的临床试验数据、免疫缺陷病分型或免疫治疗方案。抗体滴度高低不能单独等同于保护力，保护还取决于记忆细胞、黏膜免疫和病原体类型；疫苗接种和免疫疾病诊断应遵循专业指南。Boundary: this chapter gives the basic framework of recognition, antibodies and memory, not vaccine trial data, immunodeficiency subtypes or immunotherapy protocols. Antibody titer alone does not equal protection, which also depends on memory cells, mucosal immunity and pathogen type; vaccination and immune-disease diagnosis follow professional guidelines.[2][3]

02蚊子咬后为什么又痒又起包？为什么蚊子专咬某些人？Why do mosquito bites itch and swell, and why do mosquitoes prefer some people?

唾液蛋白诱发组胺局部反应形成小包瘙痒；CO₂、乳酸、体味和体温叠加吸引，防蚊靠清积水、屏障和注册驱虫剂。Saliva proteins trigger a local histamine bump and itch; CO₂, lactate, odor and temperature combine to attract people, prevented by draining water, barriers and registered repellents.

蚊子叮咬后又痒又起包，是唾液引起的局部免疫反应。雌蚊吸血时把含抗凝蛋白和其他化学物质的唾液注入皮肤，防止血液凝固；人体把这些外来蛋白当作过敏原，肥大细胞释放组胺等介质，使局部血管扩张、组织液渗出，形成小包和红晕。随后组胺刺激皮肤神经末梢，产生瘙痒感；再次被同一类蚊子叮咬时，记忆反应让肿胀和发痒出现得更快、更明显。The itch and bump after a mosquito bite are a local immune reaction to saliva. Female mosquitoes inject anticoagulant proteins and other chemicals into the skin to keep blood flowing; the body treats these foreign proteins as allergens, mast cells release histamine and other mediators, local vessels dilate and fluid leaks into tissue, forming a bump and redness. Histamine then stimulates skin nerve endings and produces itching; with repeated bites by the same type, memory makes swelling and itching appear faster and more pronounced.[4][6]

蚊子怎样找到人，靠的是多种线索叠加。它们感知呼出的二氧化碳、皮肤散发的乳酸和体味、体温、汗液湿度以及衣物颜色等信号；二氧化碳气流帮助蚊子在较远处定位，近距离再凭气味和体温锁定。个体间体味组成、汗液成分、呼吸强度和体温不同，加上皮肤菌群代谢，使蚊子更容易偏向某些人；运动后出汗、饮酒和穿着深色衣物也会增加被吸引的概率。Mosquitoes find people through overlapping cues. They sense exhaled carbon dioxide, skin lactate and body odor, body temperature, sweat humidity and clothing color; carbon dioxide plumes help locate targets from afar, while scent and temperature lock on up close. People differ in odor composition, sweat, breathing and temperature, and skin bacterial metabolism adds variation, so mosquitoes lean toward some individuals; exercise sweating, alcohol and dark clothing also raise attraction.[6][7]

防蚊要从减少暴露和阻断叮咬入手。清除室内外积水、翻盆倒罐，减少幼虫孳生；穿长袖长裤、使用纱窗蚊帐减少接触；皮肤暴露部位按说明书使用 EPA 注册的驱蚊成分，如避蚊胺（DEET）和派卡瑞丁（picaridin），涂抹在衣物或皮肤上避免接触眼口。叮咬后可冷敷减轻痒肿，避免抓破继发感染；儿童被叮咬后更应看住双手，防止抓破留疤或合并感染；反复肿胀、水疱或全身反应应就医评估。Mosquito prevention starts with reducing exposure and blocking bites. Remove standing water indoors and outdoors to cut larval breeding; wear long sleeves and pants, use screens and bed nets to reduce contact; apply EPA-registered repellents such as DEET and picaridin to exposed skin as directed, away from eyes and mouth. Cold compresses ease itch and swelling after a bite, and scratching should be avoided to prevent secondary infection; repeated swelling, blisters or systemic reactions need medical review.[6][7]

WHO 虫媒疾病资料说明蚊子等节肢动物传播病原体的途径和控制重点；EPA 驱虫剂条目按有效成分、适用人群和使用方式说明防蚊产品。OpenStax 免疫学章节把叮咬后的红肿瘙痒归入肥大细胞和组胺参与的局部过敏样反应，与全身严重过敏相区别。WHO vector-borne disease material explains how mosquitoes and other arthropods carry pathogens and where control focuses; the EPA repellent entry covers active ingredients, eligible users and use methods. The OpenStax immunity chapter places bite redness and itching among local mast-cell and histamine reactions, distinct from systemic anaphylaxis.[4][6][7]

边界：本章讲叮咬反应机制、蚊子吸引因素和常用防蚊措施，不展开具体蚊种分布、虫媒传染病诊断或驱蚊成分的儿童用量细则。包块和瘙痒多为局部反应，但出现发热、持续红肿加重或全身皮疹需就医；驱蚊剂使用应核对标签禁忌和成分浓度。Boundary: this chapter covers bite mechanisms, attraction cues and common prevention, not mosquito-species distribution, vector-borne disease diagnosis or child-dose details of repellents. Bumps and itch are usually local, but fever, worsening swelling or generalized rash needs medical care; repellent use should check label contraindications and concentration.[6][7]

03为什么康复或接种后还会再次感染？Why can people be reinfected after recovery or vaccination?

记忆消退使抗体滴度下降，抗原变异造成部分逃逸；既往记忆仍可被唤醒，通常使再次发病更轻。Fading memory lowers titer and variant variation causes partial escape; prior memory still wakes up and usually makes repeat illness milder.

康复后或接种后仍可能再次感染，首先与免疫记忆的消退有关。免疫系统在初次感染或接种后产生的抗体和记忆细胞会随时间缓慢减少，血液抗体滴度下降后，中和入侵病原体的能力随之减弱；记忆 B 细胞和 T 细胞存留更久，但数量和应答速度也会降低。当病原体再次遇到尚未被记忆有效覆盖的人群，或间隔很久之后，免疫屏障就可能被突破。Reinfection after recovery or vaccination relates first to fading immunological memory. Antibodies and memory cells made after the first infection or dose slowly decline over time; as blood titer drops, the ability to neutralize the pathogen weakens. Memory B and T cells persist longer, but their numbers and response speed also fall. When a pathogen meets a population no longer well covered by memory, or after a long gap, the immune barrier can be breached.[2][3]

第二类原因是病原体发生变异。病毒在复制中积累突变，表面抗原结构改变，原有抗体识别和结合的位点受到影响，出现部分逃逸；变异株与当初诱导免疫的毒株差异越大，既往免疫的保护力下降越明显。不同变异株的传播能力和抗原差异并不相同，既往免疫对各变异株的保护程度也有差别。细菌也可通过抗原变异、荚膜或酶解等方式逃避免疫；宿主免疫状态、年龄和基础疾病同样影响再感染风险。A second cause is pathogen variation. Viruses accumulate mutations during replication, changing surface antigen structure and interfering with the sites original antibodies recognize and bind, producing partial escape; the more a variant differs from the strain that induced immunity, the more prior protection drops. Bacteria can also evade immunity through antigen variation, capsules or enzyme breakdown; host immune status, age and underlying disease add to reinfection risk.[2][4]

再感染并不意味着免疫系统失效。既往记忆在再次暴露时仍能被唤醒，通常使症状更轻、重症更少；真正需要区分的是保护性抗体消失、记忆细胞更新不足和抗原逃逸三类机制。免疫记忆的作用是降低重症概率，而不是保证完全不被感染。个体可以通过规范接种加强针、按指南更新疫苗株来维持抗体水平，但无法对所有病原体和未来变异保证永久免疫。Reinfection does not mean the immune system has failed. Prior memory can still be recalled on re-exposure, usually producing milder symptoms and less severe illness; the real distinctions are loss of protective antibody, insufficient memory renewal and antigen escape. People can maintain titer through scheduled boosters and updated vaccine strains, but permanent immunity to every pathogen and future variant cannot be promised.[2][3]

OpenStax 适应性免疫章节描述记忆细胞形成与再次应答的加速过程；免疫紊乱章节把免疫应答不足、记忆衰退和病原体逃逸列为感染复发的相关因素；抗体章节说明滴度随时间变化并受检测方法影响。教材框架与流行病学观察一致：再感染是记忆、变异和暴露共同作用的结果。The OpenStax adaptive-immunity chapter describes memory-cell formation and the accelerated secondary response; the immune-disorders chapter lists weak response, memory decay and pathogen escape as factors in recurrence; the antibody chapter notes titer changes over time and depends on assay method. The textbook framework matches epidemiological observation: reinfection results from memory, variation and exposure together.[2][3][4]

边界：本章讲再感染的机制分类，不预测具体变异株走向，也不替代疾病诊断。抗体检测阴性或滴度低不能单独判断是否会被感染；既往感染史、接种记录和当前症状应结合医生评估。免疫低下、老年和有基础病者的防护策略需个体化。Boundary: this chapter explains reinfection mechanism categories, not variant predictions or diagnosis. A negative antibody test or low titer alone cannot say whether infection will occur; prior infection history, vaccination record and current symptoms need medical evaluation. Protection strategy should be individualized for immunocompromised, elderly and medically fragile people.[2][4]

04传染病主要通过哪些途径传播？哪些措施能阻断？How do infectious diseases spread, and which measures block transmission?

呼吸道飞沫、接触饮食和虫媒三类途径各有对应阻断；通风距离、洗手清洁、防虫和疫苗共同切断传播链。Respiratory droplets, contact-food and vector routes each have matching blockage; ventilation, distance, handwashing, cleaning, insect control and vaccines cut the chain.

传染病通过不同途径在宿主之间传播。呼吸道病原体借咳嗽、喷嚏和说话产生的飞沫扩散，近距离吸入后感染；接触传播经手、物品和黏膜接触病原体；消化道污染经食物和饮水进入肠道；虫媒传播则由蚊子、蜱等节肢动物把病原体从一个宿主带到另一个宿主。传播途径不同，需要的阻断手段也不同，弄清途径是防控措施有效的前提。Infectious diseases spread between hosts by different routes. Respiratory pathogens travel in droplets from coughing, sneezing and talking and are inhaled at close range; contact transmission passes through hands, objects and mucosal contact; digestive contamination enters the gut through food and water; vector-borne transmission uses mosquitoes, ticks and other arthropods to carry the pathogen from one host to another. Different routes call for different blockage measures, and identifying the route is the premise of effective control.[1][6]

阻断传播针对链条上的环节。呼吸道传播通过通风、保持距离、佩戴口罩和咳嗽礼仪减少飞沫吸入；接触传播靠勤洗手、清洁消毒高频接触表面和避免手摸口鼻眼；食物饮水卫生靠煮熟、饮水安全和粪便处理；虫媒传播靠清除孳生地、防蚊防虫和驱虫剂。疫苗在个体水平诱导免疫，降低易感者比例，是切断传播链的另一类手段；不同措施的适用场景随病原体和环境条件调整。Transmission is interrupted at points along the chain. Respiratory spread is reduced by ventilation, distance, masks and cough etiquette; contact spread by handwashing, cleaning high-touch surfaces and avoiding hand-to-face contact; food and water safety through cooking, safe drinking water and waste disposal; vector spread by breeding-site removal, screening and repellents. Vaccines induce individual immunity and lower the share of susceptible people, another means of breaking the chain.[1][6][8]

公共卫生根除依赖长期阻断和监测。脊髓灰质炎通过疫苗免疫使野毒株在多数国家被阻断，全球仍在持续监测输入性病例；狂犬病通过犬只免疫、暴露后处置和管理传染源压低传播。这类案例说明，当传播环节被疫苗、环境卫生和监测持续切断，疾病可以在一个地区乃至全球范围内被消除；但病原体未完全消灭时，放松措施仍可能导致反弹。Public-health eradication relies on sustained blockage and surveillance. Polio vaccination has blocked wild strains in most countries, with ongoing surveillance for imported cases; rabies is held down through dog vaccination, post-exposure treatment and source management. These cases show that when vaccines, sanitation and surveillance keep cutting the chain, a disease can be eliminated regionally or globally; but if measures relax while the pathogen remains, rebound is possible.[6][8]

OpenStax 先天免疫章节把皮肤、黏膜和屏障结构作为阻止病原体进入的第一道防线；WHO 虫媒疾病资料说明媒介控制与暴露防护；免疫紊乱章节讨论群体免疫与易感者比例对传播的影响。不同措施常组合使用，环境改造、个人防护和免疫接种互为补充。不同病原体在途径、潜伏期和环境存活能力上差别很大，防控方案必须针对具体疾病。The OpenStax innate chapter treats skin, mucosa and barrier structures as the first defense against entry; WHO vector-borne material covers vector control and exposure protection; the immune-disorders chapter discusses herd immunity and the share of susceptibles in transmission. Pathogens differ widely in route, incubation and environmental survival, so control must target the specific disease.[1][6]

边界：本章讲传播途径与通用阻断原则，不展开具体传染病的防疫流程、隔离期限或消毒浓度标准。措施效果受病原体特性和执行依从性影响；出现可疑传染病症状应按当地指引就医并报告，不能仅凭通用防护知识自行处理。Boundary: this chapter covers transmission routes and general blocking principles, not disease-specific protocols, isolation periods or disinfection concentrations. Effectiveness depends on pathogen traits and adherence; suspicious infectious symptoms should be reported and treated under local guidance, not managed with general protection knowledge alone.[1][6][8]

05器官为什么会排异？哪些病原体特别难对付？Why are transplanted organs rejected, and which pathogens are especially difficult?

HLA 差异触发排异，免疫抑制剂平衡防排异与感染；超敏是对无害抗原过强反应，难治病原体靠隐匿、变异和细胞内藏匿逃逸。HLA mismatch triggers rejection; immunosuppression balances rejection and infection. Hypersensitivity overreacts to harmless antigens; hard pathogens hide, vary or reside inside cells.

器官移植后发生排异，是因为免疫系统识别供者组织为非我。每个人细胞表面的人类白细胞抗原（HLA）组合独特，供受者 HLA 差异越大，受者 T 细胞越容易把移植器官当成异物攻击；配型相合程度、供受者血缘关系和术后免疫抑制治疗都影响排异风险。免疫抑制剂压低排斥反应，同时也会削弱整体免疫监视，需要在防排异和防感染之间平衡。Rejection after organ transplantation occurs because the immune system recognizes donor tissue as non-self. Each person has a unique set of HLA molecules on cell surfaces; the greater the HLA mismatch, the more readily recipient T cells attack the graft as foreign. Matching degree, donor-recipient relation and postoperative immunosuppression all affect rejection risk. Immunosuppressants lower rejection but also blunt immune surveillance, balancing rejection against infection.[4][5]

免疫系统还可能对无害物质过度反应。花粉、食物、尘螨等刺激诱发的超敏反应，就是免疫把无害抗原当成威胁而产生过强应答；自身免疫病则是免疫系统错误攻击自身组织。OpenStax 教材把这些归入免疫反应失调：免疫过强造成过敏和自身损伤，免疫过弱则导致反复感染和清除病原体能力下降。The immune system can also overreact to harmless substances. Hypersensitivity triggered by pollen, food or dust mites is an overly strong response to a harmless antigen; autoimmune disease means the system mistakenly attacks self tissue. The OpenStax text groups these as dysregulation: excessive immunity causes allergy and self-injury, while weak immunity causes recurrent infection and poor pathogen clearance.[2][4]

部分病原体特别难对付，与它们的生存方式和逃逸策略有关。狂犬病毒沿神经移行、潜伏期长，发病后病情危重，暴露后疫苗和免疫球蛋白处置窗口关键；朊粒是异常折叠蛋白，不被常规免疫识别，能在组织中累积；HIV 攻击辅助 T 细胞，逐步破坏适应性免疫基础。这些病原体有的隐匿、有的变异快、有的缺乏有效免疫清除，防控依赖暴露阻断、暴露后干预和早期发现，日常难以靠自身免疫力清除。Some pathogens are especially difficult because of their survival and escape strategies. Rabies virus travels along nerves with a long incubation and becomes severe once symptomatic, so post-exposure vaccine and immunoglobulin timing are critical; prions are misfolded proteins not recognized routinely by immunity and accumulate in tissue; HIV attacks helper T cells and gradually dismantles adaptive immunity. These pathogens are hidden, fast-mutating or poorly cleared, so control relies on exposure blockage, post-exposure intervention and early detection.[2][4]

移植与感染难题的共同点是免疫识别和免疫记忆的边界。排异要在识别“非我”和保护移植器官之间取舍；难治病原体则利用免疫识别不到、记忆覆盖不足或细胞内藏匿等漏洞。OpenStax 移植与肿瘤免疫章节说明 HLA 配型、免疫抑制和肿瘤免疫监视；免疫紊乱章节总结过敏、自身免疫和免疫缺陷的表现。Transplantation and hard infections share the boundaries of immune recognition and memory. Rejection requires trade-offs between recognizing non-self and protecting the graft; difficult pathogens exploit poor recognition, thin memory coverage or intracellular hiding. The OpenStax transplantation chapter covers HLA matching, immunosuppression and tumor surveillance; the immune-disorders chapter summarizes allergy, autoimmunity and immunodeficiency.[2][4][5]

边界：本章讲排异、超敏和难治病原体的机制概览，不展开移植手术、免疫抑制用药方案或个体化诊疗。病原体接触史、暴露后处置和器官配型都应由专科医生判断；高风险暴露后应在规定时间内规范处理，不能依赖自身抵抗力。Boundary: this chapter overviews rejection, hypersensitivity and difficult pathogens, not transplant surgery, immunosuppressant regimens or individual care. Exposure history, post-exposure treatment and organ matching belong to specialist judgment; after high-risk exposure, act within the required window rather than relying on natural resistance.[4][5]

06为什么冬天更容易感冒？免疫力为什么会下降？Why do colds strike more often in winter, and why does immunity weaken?

干燥冷空气削弱黏膜防御，密闭聚集增加飞沫接触；睡眠疲劳营养压力等叠加使应答减弱。Dry cold air weakens mucosal defense, crowded closed rooms raise droplet contact; sleep, fatigue, nutrition and stress stack to blunt response.

普通感冒多由鼻病毒、普通冠状病毒等引起，冬春季高发并不是寒冷本身直接“冻出”感冒，而是病毒传播条件与上呼吸道防御在季节里共同变化的结果。感冒本质是病毒感染，是否发病取决于接触到的病毒量、黏膜防御状态和免疫记忆覆盖，气温低只是背景因素之一；受凉本身不会凭空产生病毒，只是可能让局部防御暂时变差。The common cold is mostly caused by rhinoviruses and common coronaviruses. Its winter and spring peak does not mean cold weather directly freezes a cold into being; rather, viral transmission conditions and upper-airway defense shift together across seasons. A cold is a viral infection, and whether illness develops depends on viral dose encountered, mucosal defense and immune-memory coverage, with low temperature only one background factor. Chilling itself cannot create a virus out of nowhere, but it may leave local defense temporarily weaker.[1][9]

鼻腔和气道黏膜是呼吸道的第一道屏障。黏液黏附颗粒，纤毛把黏液向后推送清除；干燥冷空气进入后，黏液变稠、纤毛运动效率下降，局部屏障暂时变弱。冬季室内供暖使空气更干燥，鼻腔和气道黏膜更容易受到刺激；黏膜状态下降时，吸入的病毒更容易在上皮停留并复制，局部免疫细胞的清除速度也可能受干燥环境影响。Nasal and airway mucosa form the first respiratory barrier. Mucus traps particles and cilia sweep mucus backward for clearance; dry cold air thickens mucus and slows ciliary motion, temporarily weakening the local barrier. Winter indoor heating dries the air further and irritates nasal and airway mucosa; once mucosal defense drops, inhaled viruses are more likely to lodge in epithelium and replicate, and local immune cells may clear them more slowly in dry conditions.[1][9]

季节还改变人群接触方式。冬天门窗紧闭、通风差，人们在室内近距离聚集，咳嗽、说话产生的飞沫和接触传播机会增多；研究提示低湿度环境有利于部分呼吸道病毒在体外存活，干燥空气也可能影响黏膜表面的防御颗粒。这些因素叠加，使冬春季家庭、学校和办公场所的感冒传播更容易发生，一户一人患病后常陆续传染给同住者。Season also changes how people contact one another. Winter keeps doors and windows closed, ventilation poor and people close together indoors, raising chances of droplet spread from coughing and talking and of contact transmission. Research suggests low humidity helps some respiratory viruses survive outside the body, and dry air may also affect defensive particles at the mucosal surface; together these factors make colds spread more easily in households, schools and offices in winter and spring, where one sick person often infects household members in turn.[4][9]

所谓“免疫力下降”通常是多个因素叠加，而不是免疫系统整体开关被关掉。长期睡眠不足、疲劳、营养不良、精神压力、年龄增长和基础疾病都会削弱先天屏障和适应性应答；既往感染或接种留下的记忆细胞能对熟悉病毒快速应答，但感冒病毒血清型多、又不断变异，记忆覆盖并不全面，仍可能再次感染。短期内反复着凉、作息紊乱，更多是通过疲劳和黏膜状态影响发病概率。So-called weakened immunity usually stacks several factors rather than flipping a global immune switch. Chronic poor sleep, fatigue, poor nutrition, stress, older age and underlying disease all blunt innate barriers and adaptive responses. Memory cells from past infection or vaccination respond fast to familiar viruses, but cold viruses have many serotypes and keep varying, so memory coverage is incomplete and reinfection remains possible. Repeated chills and disrupted routines in the short term affect illness probability mainly through fatigue and mucosal condition.[1][4]

边界：本章讲季节因素与免疫状态的基本机制，不展开具体感冒药、抗生素使用或并发症诊断。反复感冒、高热持续、脓涕、面部胀痛、耳闷或症状超过一周不缓解应就医；日常防护靠室内通风、勤洗手、避免用手摸口鼻、规律作息和均衡饮食，不能把怕冷、受凉直接等同于必然感冒。Boundary: this chapter covers seasonal factors and immune status, not cold medications, antibiotic use or complication diagnosis. Recurrent colds, persistent fever, purulent discharge, facial pain, ear fullness or symptoms lasting over a week deserve medical care. Daily protection rests on ventilation, handwashing, avoiding hand-to-nose and mouth contact, regular sleep and balanced diet; feeling cold or chilled cannot be equated with catching a cold for sure.[1][4][9]

07过敏为什么会反复发生？同一过敏原为什么年年发作？Why do allergies recur, and why does the same allergen trigger attacks year after year?

IgE 致敏长期留存，过敏原难以回避，反复暴露放大局部炎症；需区分速发型与迟发反应。IgE sensitization persists, allergens are hard to avoid, and repeated exposure amplifies local inflammation; immediate and delayed reactions differ.

过敏是免疫系统对原本无害的抗原产生的过度反应。以最常见的 IgE 介导速发型为例，初次接触过敏原后，身体诱导出特异性 IgE 抗体，这些抗体结合在肥大细胞和嗜碱性粒细胞表面受体上，使机体处于致敏状态；再次接触同一过敏原，过敏原与细胞表面 IgE 交联，细胞脱颗粒，释放组胺和其他介质，症状随即出现。致敏一旦建立，往往可以维持较长时间，不会因为几次没有接触就自动消失。Allergy is an overreaction of the immune system to an otherwise harmless antigen. In the common IgE-mediated immediate type, the first contact with an allergen induces specific IgE antibodies that bind receptors on mast cells and basophils, leaving the body sensitized. On re-exposure to the same allergen, it cross-links IgE on these cells, which degranulate and release histamine and other mediators, producing symptoms soon after. Once established, sensitization often lasts a long time and does not clear automatically after a few missed contacts.[4][10]

过敏容易反复，首先因为过敏原很难完全回避。花粉随空气飘散，尘螨长期存在于床垫、被褥和布艺中，食物可能存在交叉污染，接触途径又包括吸入、食入、皮肤接触甚至注射。只要环境中持续存在过敏原，致敏状态下的个体就会反复被触发；花粉季、换季时节同步反复暴露，表现为年年同期发作，离开暴露环境一段时间后症状才可能减轻。食入性过敏原中，虾、蟹等海鲜的主要变应原是肌肉蛋白原肌球蛋白，耐热且可诱导特异性 IgE；再次进食同一海鲜后，除皮肤风团外，还可出现胃肠道蠕动亢进、黏膜水肿等消化道反应。Allergies recur first because allergens are hard to avoid completely. Pollen drifts on air, dust mites persist in mattresses, bedding and upholstery, and foods may carry cross-contamination; exposure routes include inhalation, ingestion, skin contact and even injection. As long as the allergen remains in the environment, a sensitized individual can be triggered again; synchronized repeated exposure through pollen season and seasonal change shows up as annual attacks at the same time each year, easing only after a period away from exposure. Among ingested allergens, the major shellfish allergen is tropomyosin, a heat-stable muscle protein that induces specific IgE; after eating the same seafood again, besides skin wheals, gastrointestinal hypermotility and mucosal swelling can follow.[2][10][12]

反复暴露还会让反应逐步放大。每次发作的局部炎症会在黏膜和皮肤留下高反应状态，下一次接触的触发阈值可能更低；肥大细胞和嗜碱性粒细胞持续致敏，局部嗜酸粒细胞、细胞因子参与慢性炎症过程。需要区分的是，IgE 介导的速发型反应在接触后很快出现，而非 IgE 机制的迟发反应出现较慢、介质组合不同，不能混为一谈，反复暴露后的慢性炎症与单次速发反应也不是同一环节。Repeated exposure also amplifies the reaction step by step. Local inflammation after each episode leaves mucosa and skin in a hyper-responsive state, lowering the trigger threshold next time; mast cells and basophils stay sensitized, and local eosinophils and cytokines take part in chronic inflammation. It matters that IgE-mediated immediate reactions appear soon after contact, while non-IgE delayed reactions emerge more slowly with a different mediator mix; the two should not be lumped together, and chronic inflammation after repeated exposure is a different stage from a single immediate reaction.[2][4]

发作频率和严重程度还受暴露量、接触频率和环境浓度影响。家族特应性体质使个体更容易产生 IgE；过敏性鼻炎、哮喘和湿疹常在同一人身上先后或同时出现，称为共病。生活环境中过敏原浓度高、清理不彻底，会让反复暴露持续存在；单次症状缓解并不等于致敏状态解除，季节一到或再次接触就可能重新发作。Frequency and severity also follow exposure dose, contact frequency and environmental concentration. A family atopic constitution makes IgE production more likely; allergic rhinitis, asthma and eczema often appear in the same person sequentially or together, known as comorbidity. High allergen concentration and incomplete removal keep repeated exposure going, and easing one episode does not mean the sensitized state has ended; symptoms may return with the season or the next contact.[4][10]

边界：本章讲过敏反复的机制与影响因素，不展开过敏原检测流程、脱敏治疗方案或个人处方。反复发作、影响睡眠和日常活动，或出现呼吸不畅等表现，应就医评估并明确过敏原；回避措施应结合病史和检查结果制定，不能仅凭经验长期忌口或脱离环境。Boundary: this chapter covers recurrence mechanisms and contributing factors, not allergen testing workflows, desensitization protocols or personal prescriptions. Recurrent attacks disrupting sleep and daily life, or any breathing difficulty, should be evaluated medically with allergens identified; avoidance plans should follow history and test results rather than long-term self-imposed diets or environmental removal by guesswork.[2][4]

08为什么过敏会发痒？痒和皮疹是怎样产生的？Why does allergy cause itching, and how do itch and rash develop?

肥大细胞脱颗粒释放组胺，作用于微血管与神经末梢，血管渗出形成风团红斑，抓挠放大痒觉循环。Mast cells degranulate histamine acting on vessels and nerve endings, vascular leakage forms wheals and redness, and scratching amplifies the itch cycle.

过敏时发痒，起点在肥大细胞脱颗粒。过敏原与致敏肥大细胞表面 IgE 交联后，细胞释放组胺等介质；组胺一方面作用于皮肤和黏膜下微血管，另一方面刺激局部感觉神经末梢的 H1 受体，痒信号由此产生。除组胺外，白三烯、前列腺素和细胞因子也参与放大局部反应，不同介质组合决定了痒、肿和充血的程度。Itching in allergy starts when mast cells degranulate. After an allergen cross-links IgE on sensitized mast cells, the cells release histamine and other mediators. Histamine acts on small vessels under skin and mucosa and on local sensory nerve endings through H1 receptors, which is how the itch signal arises; leukotrienes, prostaglandins and cytokines also amplify the local reaction, and their combination sets the level of itch, swelling and redness.[4][10]

血管反应形成可见的皮疹。组胺使局部小血管扩张、通透性增加，血浆成分渗出到组织间隙，皮肤出现红斑和风团，组织肿胀又机械地刺激周围神经末梢。风团是真皮浅层水肿形成的隆起，通常边界清楚、瘙痒明显，位置和形状可随时间变化；鼻、眼黏膜的同类反应则表现为发痒、流泪和喷嚏，同一过敏过程可在多个部位同时出现。Vascular reactions produce the visible rash. Histamine dilates local small vessels and raises permeability, so plasma components leak into tissue, forming redness and wheals on skin; tissue swelling then mechanically stimulates nearby nerve endings. A wheal is a raised bump from superficial dermal edema, usually well demarcated and clearly itchy, with position and shape changing over time; the same reaction in nasal and eye mucosa shows as itching, tearing and sneezing, and one allergic process can appear in several sites at once.[4][10]

痒感信号经无髓鞘 C 纤维传向脊髓和脑。抓挠虽然短暂压制痒觉，却会损伤皮肤屏障、刺激肥大细胞释放更多介质，形成痒—抓—更痒的循环；反复抓挠还可能造成破损和继发感染。瘙痒的定位、范围和持续时间，有助于区分单纯局部过敏与更广泛的全身反应，皮肤抓破后出现渗液、结痂时需要注意感染征象。The itch signal travels to the spinal cord and brain through unmyelinated C fibers. Scratching suppresses itch briefly but damages the skin barrier and prompts mast cells to release more mediators, forming an itch-scratch cycle; repeated scratching can also cause breaks and secondary infection. The location, spread and duration of itch help distinguish a purely local allergy from a broader systemic reaction, and weeping or crusting after broken skin should be watched for infection signs.[3][4]

过敏原可经多条途径进入身体：吸入的花粉、尘螨，食入的食物，接触的植物、金属或化妆品成分，以及药物注射或虫咬。不同途径引起的反应分布不同，皮肤风团、鼻痒眼痒、气道不适可以同时出现。常见过敏原包括花粉、尘螨、霉菌、动物皮屑、牛奶、鸡蛋、花生和某些药物，个体差异很大，同一物质在不同人身上反应轻重不一；皮肤、呼吸道和消化道是过敏原接触的主要界面，家居清洁程度、季节变化和职业环境都会影响日常暴露浓度。Allergens enter by several routes: inhaled pollen and dust mites, ingested foods, contacted plants, metals or cosmetic ingredients, and injected drugs or insect bites. Different routes produce different reaction patterns, and skin wheals, nasal and eye itching and airway discomfort can appear together. Common allergens include pollen, mites, mold, animal dander, milk, eggs, peanuts and certain drugs, with wide individual variation; the same substance can cause reactions of very different severity between people.[3][10]

边界：本章讲过敏瘙痒与皮疹的产生机制，不展开止痒药物、激素使用或个体化诊疗。若伴面唇舌肿胀、喉头紧、喘憋、头晕或全身大片风团，属于严重过敏反应，需立即就医；反复出现的皮疹应记录诱因并就医明确，避免自行长期用药或反复接触可疑过敏原。Boundary: this chapter explains how allergic itch and rash arise, not antipruritic drugs, steroid use or individual diagnosis. Swelling of the face, lips or tongue, tight throat, breathlessness, dizziness or widespread wheals indicate anaphylaxis and need immediate care. Recurrent rash should be recorded by trigger and evaluated medically; avoid long-term self-medication or repeated contact with suspected allergens.[4][10]

09疫苗为什么打在胳膊上？为什么有些疫苗要打几针？Why are vaccines given in the arm, and why do some vaccines require several doses?

三角肌血流丰富、邻近腋窝淋巴结，抗原引流完成免疫激活；多剂次巩固抗体滴度与记忆细胞。The deltoid is well perfused near axillary nodes for antigen drainage and activation; multiple doses consolidate titer and memory cells.

绝大多数疫苗采用肌内注射，上臂三角肌是成人最常用的接种部位。肌肉组织血流丰富，注射后抗原被局部树突状细胞等免疫细胞摄取，再引流到附近淋巴结；肌肉空间也能容纳疫苗容量，吸收相对平稳，局部红肿等反应较易控制。选择上臂三角肌，正是兼顾了肌肉条件、淋巴结分布和操作便利，皮下注射则多用于另一部分疫苗。Most vaccines are given by intramuscular injection, and the deltoid muscle of the upper arm is the most common adult site. Muscle tissue has rich blood flow; after injection local immune cells such as dendritic cells take up the antigen and drain it to nearby lymph nodes. Muscle can also hold the vaccine volume, absorption stays relatively steady, and local redness or swelling is easier to manage. Choosing the upper deltoid balances muscle condition, lymph-node distribution and ease of administration, while subcutaneous injection fits another group of vaccines.[2][11]

免疫激活在局部淋巴结完成。被摄取的抗原经树突状细胞递呈给 T 细胞和 B 细胞，对应克隆选择性扩增，B 细胞分化为浆细胞产生抗体，并形成记忆 B 细胞和记忆 T 细胞。三角肌邻近腋窝淋巴结群，抗原引流路径短，有利于在淋巴结内完成识别、扩增和记忆形成；这也是接种部位与免疫效果直接相关的原因，淋巴结附近的抗原更容易被适应性免疫捕获。Immune activation completes in local lymph nodes. Antigen taken up is presented by dendritic cells to T and B cells; matching clones expand selectively, B cells mature into plasma cells that make antibodies, and memory B and T cells form. The deltoid sits near the axillary lymph-node group, giving a short drainage path that favors recognition, expansion and memory formation inside the node; this is why injection site relates directly to immune effect, and antigen near lymph nodes is more easily captured by adaptive immunity.[2][3]

不同疫苗有各自推荐的接种途径和部位。肌内注射、皮下注射各有适用疫苗；婴幼儿肌肉体积小，常选择大腿前外侧作为接种部位。接种部位的淋巴结分布、肌肉厚度和疫苗剂型共同决定部位选择，并不是随便挑一块肌肉注射即可。接种后手臂酸胀、局部硬结多为局部免疫反应，通常短期自行缓解，少数人可有低热、乏力等全身表现。Different vaccines have their own recommended route and site. Intramuscular and subcutaneous injection each fit certain vaccines; infants have small muscle mass and often receive injections in the anterolateral thigh. Lymph-node distribution, muscle thickness and vaccine formulation together determine the site, rather than picking any muscle at random. Sore arms and local nodules after vaccination are mostly local immune reactions that ease on their own in the short term, with a few people having low fever or fatigue as general reactions.[2][11]

有些疫苗需要接种多剂次，原因与初次应答和记忆巩固有关。单次接种诱导的抗体水平和记忆细胞数量有限，基础免疫的第二、第三剂次反复刺激免疫系统，把抗体滴度和记忆细胞提升到足以保护的水平；随时间推移抗体逐渐衰减，加强针再次唤醒记忆细胞。灭活、蛋白和核酸等不同类型疫苗免疫原性不同，所需剂次和间隔也因此不同，剂次安排是根据临床试验和群体免疫数据确定的。Some vaccines require multiple doses because of primary response and memory consolidation. A single dose produces limited antibody levels and memory cells; the second and third primary doses repeatedly stimulate the system to raise titer and memory numbers to a protective level. Antibodies decline over time, and booster doses wake memory cells again. Inactivated, protein and nucleic vaccines differ in immunogenicity, so their required doses and intervals differ as well; dose schedules are set from trial and population immunization data.[2][3]

边界：本章讲接种部位选择和多剂次原理，不展开具体疫苗临床试验数据、接种禁忌或个人接种程序。具体疫苗打几针、间隔多久，应按疫苗说明书和当地接种指南执行；接种后持续高热、呼吸困难、面唇肿胀等严重反应需及时就医，局部轻微红肿低热多为常见反应。Boundary: this chapter explains injection-site choice and multi-dose principles, not vaccine trial data, contraindications or individual schedules. How many doses a vaccine needs and their interval follow the product label and local immunization guidelines. Persistent high fever, breathing difficulty or face-lip swelling after vaccination needs prompt medical care; mild local redness, swelling and low fever are common reactions.[2][3][11]
