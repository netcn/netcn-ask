皮肤、毛发与指甲Skin, Hair, and Nails

01为什么皮肤会晒黑？Why does skin tan?

晒黑是 DNA 损伤后的防护反应；皮肤强在薄而韧的复合结构；完整皮肤很少吸收酒精，破损加封闭才危险。A tan is a protective response to DNA damage; skin is a thin, tough composite; intact skin absorbs little alcohol, and broken skin plus occlusion is the dangerous combination.

先给结论：晒黑不是“皮肤变健康”，而是紫外线造成 DNA 损伤后，黑素细胞加班合成黑色素，并把黑素小体转运到角质形成细胞，像给细胞核撑起一把伞。晒黑确实能提供防护，但相当于 SPF 约 3，不足以替代防晒；晒黑和晒伤可以同时发生。“先晒黑打底”并不安全：每次暴晒都在累积 DNA 损伤。Start with the conclusion: a tan is not evidence that skin has become healthier. Ultraviolet (UV) radiation damages DNA; in response, melanocytes increase melanin production and transfer melanin-containing melanosomes to keratinocytes, placing a dark umbrella over the cell nucleus. A tan offers only modest protection—roughly SPF 3—so it cannot replace sunscreen, and tanning can coexist with sunburn. "Pre-tanning" before a holiday is not safe: every exposure adds to the DNA-damage burden.[1][2][18]

紫外线主要分 UVA 和 UVB。UVB 能量高，主要伤表皮；UVA 穿透更深，可到真皮。UVB 会激活 p53–POMC–MC1R–酪氨酸酶通路，让黑素细胞合成新的黑色素；UVA 则让已有黑色素氧化、加速向表皮转运，所以有“即刻黑化”与“持久黑化”。迟发晒黑通常在日晒后 2–4 天出现，峰值约在 10 天到 4 周；新黑素排在角质形成细胞核上方，能减少后续 DNA 损伤，但它不是“无害”的标志。UVB is higher-energy and damages mainly the epidermis; UVA penetrates deeper into the dermis. UVB activates the p53–POMC–MC1R–tyrosinase pathway, prompting melanocytes to make new melanin. UVA oxidizes existing melanin and accelerates its transfer to the epidermis, producing immediate and persistent darkening. Delayed tanning usually appears 2–4 days after exposure, peaks around 10 days to 4 weeks, and the new melanin sits above keratinocyte nuclei, reducing later DNA damage—but it is not a sign of harmlessness.[1][3][4]

皮肤的强度不能只看厚度。表皮靠角蛋白丝和细胞间连接抗拉，真皮靠胶原纤维网络承担大部分拉伸负荷，弹性纤维负责回弹；角质层在最外面，含水量高时更柔，干燥时更脆。人表皮约 0.05–0.15 mm，真皮约 0.15–4 mm，胶原约占真皮干重的 70%，弹性纤维只占 2%–4%。所以人皮肤是“薄而韧”的复合结构，单位厚度并不弱，但不能直接和兽皮比厚、比抗撕咬。Skin strength is not just thickness. The epidermis resists tension through keratin filaments and cell–cell junctions; the dermis carries most of the tensile load through a collagen network, while elastic fibers provide recoil. The stratum corneum is the outer layer: it is softer when hydrated and more brittle when dry. Human epidermis is about 0.05–0.15 mm thick and the dermis about 0.15–4 mm; collagen makes up about 70% of the dermis by dry weight, and elastin only 2–4%. Human skin is therefore a thin but tough composite, strong per unit thickness, but not directly comparable to animal hide in thickness or bite resistance.[8][9]

和动物皮比，也不能只比一个数。猪真皮与人的胶原结构相似，常被用作实验模型；但厚度、胶原交联、含水量、毛发密度都不同。人皮肤的优势是感觉、散热和屏障，不是铠甲。真正决定“强不强”的，是胶原网络、含水量、年龄、日晒和是否受伤，而不是某个单一指标。Comparisons with animal skin cannot rest on a single number. Pig dermis resembles human dermis in collagen architecture and is often used as an experimental model, but thickness, collagen cross-linking, water content and hair density differ. Human skin excels at sensation, heat dissipation and barrier function, not at acting like armor. What actually determines strength is the collagen network, hydration, age, sun exposure and injury—not one isolated metric.[8][9]

完整皮肤是很好的屏障：角质层脂质双层挡住多数水溶性分子，乙醇经皮吸收通常只有涂抹量的 1%–2%，而且很快蒸发，所以用免洗洗手液不会“喝醉”。但破损皮肤是另一回事：擦伤、抓破、湿疹、大面积浸渍会显著增加吸收。已有病例报告，一名有精神病史的男子把含 62% 乙醇的洗手液涂满背部、用硬刷擦破皮肤，并在塑料袋中封闭 30 小时，出现意识不清、血乙醇 3.7 g/L、渗透压间隙和酸中毒。这不是说“皮肤会像胃一样喝酒”，而是“完整皮肤低、破损加封闭加大量加长时间”才可能。儿童、皮肤病患者、醉酒或意识不清者风险更高；接触后出现头晕、嗜睡、呕吐、呼吸抑制，应立即脱去污染衣物、冲洗皮肤并就医。Intact skin is an excellent barrier: the lipid layers of the stratum corneum block most water-soluble molecules, and ethanol absorbed through skin is usually only about 1–2% of the amount applied, with much of it evaporating; that is why hand sanitizer does not make you drunk. Broken skin is different: abrasions, scratches, eczema and large areas of maceration increase absorption. A case report described a man with a psychiatric history who applied 62% ethanol sanitizer to his back, scrubbed the skin with a hard brush and occluded it with a plastic bag for 30 hours; he became stuporous with a blood ethanol of 3.7 g/L, an osmolal gap and acidosis. The point is not that skin "drinks like a stomach", but that intact skin absorbs little and broken skin plus occlusion, large volume and long contact can matter. Children, people with skin disease and anyone drowsy or confused are at higher risk; after exposure, remove contaminated clothing, rinse the skin and seek medical care for drowsiness, vomiting or slow breathing.[10][11]

02头发怎么知道什么时候停止生长？How do our hairs know when to stop growing?

毛干长度由生长期决定；毛多不等于毛囊多；吹风机靠风扇和电阻丝加热，伤害主要来自高温和过度干燥。Shaft length is set by anagen duration; visible hairiness does not mean more follicles; a dryer uses a fan and a heating element, and damage comes from heat and over-drying.

“头发怎么知道停止？”毛囊其实不知道“长度”，它按周期运行。生长期（anagen）约 2–7 年，毛母质细胞不断分裂、向上推出毛干；进入退行期（catagen）约 2–4 周，毛囊下部萎缩；休止期（telogen）约 3 个月，旧发脱落，随后新周期开始。生长期越长，毛干越长；眉毛、睫毛的生长期只有几个月，所以长不长。How does hair "know" when to stop? The follicle does not measure length; it runs a cycle. Anagen lasts about 2–7 years, with matrix cells dividing and pushing the shaft upward. Catagen lasts about 2–4 weeks, and the lower follicle regresses. Telogen lasts about 3 months, the old hair falls out and a new cycle begins. The longer the anagen phase, the longer the shaft; eyebrows and eyelashes have short anagen phases, so they stay short.[5][6]

人类体毛变细变短，是进化取舍，不是“退化了所以没用”。主流解释是散热：浓密体毛会妨碍汗液蒸发，而人类靠大量外泌汗腺和直立行走时的对流散热；体毛保留在眉毛、睫毛、腋下、阴部等部位，仍有保护、气味扩散和触觉功能。另一个假说是减少体外寄生虫，但证据不如散热假说强。Human body hair became finer and shorter through evolutionary trade-offs, not because it became useless. The leading explanation is thermoregulation: dense fur interferes with sweat evaporation, whereas humans rely on abundant eccrine sweat glands and convective cooling while walking upright. Hair remains on the eyebrows, eyelashes, axillae and pubic area, where it still provides protection, scent dispersion and touch sensation. A parasite-reduction hypothesis also exists, but the evidence is weaker than for thermoregulation.[4][5]

“毛多的人毛囊更多吗？”多数情况下不是。人类毛囊总数在出生时基本确定，约 500 万个，头皮约 10 万个；成年后体表面积增大，密度被摊薄，所以看起来“毛发多”不等于“毛囊多”。不同部位密度差异很大：颊、额约 800 个/cm²，大腿、小腿只有约 50 个/cm²；头皮毛囊密度约 1.3–4.0 根/mm²。体毛粗细、颜色和密度受雄激素、遗传和毛囊类型影响；毳毛细软无色，终毛粗长有色。青春期雄激素让部分毳毛变成终毛，所以“毛变多”常是毛囊类型改变，不是新长出一批毛囊。Do hairy people have more follicles? Usually not. The total number of human follicles is largely set at birth—about 5 million, including about 100,000 on the scalp. As body surface area grows, density is diluted, so visible hairiness does not equal more follicles. Density varies greatly by site: the cheeks and forehead have about 800 follicles/cm², while the thighs and calves have only about 50/cm²; scalp density is about 1.3–4.0 hairs/mm². Hair thickness, colour and density depend on androgens, genetics and follicle type. Vellus hairs are fine and unpigmented; terminal hairs are thicker and pigmented. Puberty turns some vellus follicles into terminal follicles, so "more hair" is often a change in follicle type, not a new crop of follicles.[5][6][7]

吹风机不“吹干”头发，它做两件事：风扇把空气送过电阻丝加热，热空气提高水分蒸发速率，同时把湿空气带走。典型结构是电机带动离心风扇、镍铬合金电热丝、温控器和保险丝；冷风档只是断开加热丝。传统机型电机常见约 1–2 万转/分，高速无刷机型转速更高、风量更大。热量会伤害毛干：角蛋白在高温下变性、毛小皮翘起，头发变脆、失去光泽；湿发时毛小皮张开，更脆弱。把吹风机贴太近、长时间高温、先吹湿发再高温定型，都是常见错误。A hair dryer does not "dry" hair by magic; it does two things: a fan pushes air across a heating element, and the hot air accelerates evaporation while carrying moisture away. A typical dryer contains a motor-driven centrifugal fan, a nichrome heating element, a thermostat and a thermal fuse; the cool setting simply switches off the heater. Traditional motors often run at about 10,000–20,000 rpm, while high-speed brushless models run faster and move more air. Heat damages the shaft: keratin denatures at high temperature, the cuticle lifts, and hair becomes brittle and dull. Wet hair has an open cuticle and is more vulnerable. Holding the dryer too close, using high heat for too long, or blasting wet hair with hot air are common mistakes.[15][16]

边界：每天掉 25–100 根头发属于正常范围；突然大量掉发、片状脱发、头皮红斑或瘢痕，要看皮肤科。雄激素性脱发是毛囊逐渐微型化，不是“毛囊死光了”；米诺地尔、非那雄胺等有证据，但需医生评估。吹风机边界：低档、保持距离、半干时换冷风，比追求“高温快干”更护发；不要在被窝里或浴室潮湿处长时间使用，注意电气安全。Boundaries: losing 25–100 hairs a day is normal. Sudden shedding, patchy loss, scalp redness or scarring warrants a dermatology review. Androgenetic alopecia is gradual miniaturization of follicles, not "dead follicles"; minoxidil and finasteride have evidence but need medical assessment. For the dryer, use a lower setting, keep a distance and switch to cool air when hair is partly dry; do not use it for long periods under bedding or in a damp bathroom, and respect electrical safety.[6][16][17]

03指甲上的纵向隆起是怎么来的？What causes raised ridges in human fingernails?

纵向嵴多与年龄和甲母质生长不均有关；横向 Beau 线才更常提示系统性问题；不要过度打磨。Longitudinal ridges mostly reflect age and uneven matrix growth; transverse Beau lines are more often a systemic clue; do not over-polish.

先给结论：指甲上从根到尖的纵向隆起，多数是生理性老化。甲板由近端甲母质不断产生，像传送带一样把新甲推向指尖；甲母质生长速度、甲板含水量和局部微循环随年龄变化，甲板就会变得不平整，出现一条条纵向嵴。它通常对称、缓慢加重、不影响甲板强度，不是“缺钙”或“肾不好”的特异信号。Start with the conclusion: longitudinal ridges running from the nail root to the tip are usually a sign of physiological ageing. The nail plate is produced by the proximal nail matrix and pushed forward like a slow conveyor belt. As matrix growth rate, plate water content and local microcirculation change with age, the plate becomes less even and develops longitudinal ridges. They are usually symmetrical, progress slowly and do not reduce plate strength; they are not a specific sign of calcium deficiency or kidney disease.[12][14]

指甲是甲母质的产品：甲母质在近端，甲板沿纵向生长，手指甲约 1 mm/6–10 天，脚趾甲约 1.62 mm/月。纵向嵴反映甲母质局部生长速度不一致；横向凹沟则反映某一段时间甲母质生长被“暂停”，所以凹沟的位置能估算事件发生时间。甲板主要成分是角蛋白，含水量 5%–30%，还含脂质和微量元素；它硬而略弹。老化时甲板角化细胞变大、生长变慢、含水量和成分改变，纵向嵴更明显；频繁洗手、清洁剂、甲油卸除剂会让甲板更干、纹路更显。The nail is a product of the matrix. The matrix sits proximally, and the plate grows longitudinally; fingernails advance about 1 mm every 6–10 days, toenails about 1.62 mm per month. Longitudinal ridges reflect uneven local matrix growth, whereas transverse grooves reflect a temporary pause in matrix growth, so the position of a groove can help estimate when an event occurred. The plate is mainly keratin, with 5–30% water plus lipids and trace elements; it is hard but slightly elastic. With age, plate cells enlarge, growth slows and water content and composition change, making ridges more visible. Frequent handwashing, detergents and nail-polish remover dry the plate and make the texture more obvious.[12][13]

纵向嵴的“证据”要分两类。生理性纵向嵴：常见于中老年人，双手对称，从甲根到甲尖，粗细不一，甲板完整；不需要治疗，保湿、减少刺激即可。病理性原因：甲母质损伤、甲沟炎、银屑病、扁平苔藓、斑秃、外伤、局部肿瘤等，常伴随甲板变色、增厚、分离、甲周红肿或单指改变。系统性疾病的经典甲征多是横向的 Beau 线、Muehrcke 线、Terry 甲等，而不是单纯纵向嵴。The evidence falls into two groups. Physiological longitudinal ridges are common in older adults, symmetrical, run from root to tip, vary in thickness and leave the plate intact; no treatment is needed—moisturize and reduce irritation. Pathological causes include matrix injury, paronychia, psoriasis, lichen planus, alopecia areata, trauma and local tumours; they often come with colour change, thickening, separation, redness around the nail or a single affected digit. Classic systemic nail signs are more often transverse Beau lines, Muehrcke lines or Terry nails, not isolated longitudinal ridges.[12][14]

Beau 线是横向凹沟，不是纵向嵴。它由近端甲母质暂时停止生长引起，常见于高热、重症、化疗、严重营养不良、低体温、Raynaud 现象等；凹沟在事件后 2–3 周才随着甲板长出被看到，距离甲根远近可以粗略判断时间。COVID-19 后也有报道：一位 45 岁男性在感染 3.5 个月后，双侧大脚趾出现距甲根 5 mm 的横向凹沟。这个例子说明：横向凹沟更像“时间标记”，纵向嵴更像“长期纹路”，两者不能混为一谈。Beau lines are transverse grooves, not longitudinal ridges. They arise when the proximal matrix temporarily stops growing and are associated with high fever, severe illness, chemotherapy, severe malnutrition, hypothermia and Raynaud phenomenon. The groove becomes visible 2–3 weeks after the event as the plate grows out, and its distance from the cuticle gives a rough time estimate. After COVID-19, a report described a 45-year-old man who developed transverse grooves 5 mm from the nail root on both great toenails 3.5 months after infection. Transverse grooves act more like time markers; longitudinal ridges act more like long-term texture.[13][14]

边界：绝大多数纵向嵴是良性、与年龄相关；但如果出现单条明显凹沟、甲板突然变黑、变白、变红、甲板与甲床分离、甲周红肿疼痛、指甲变厚变脆，或伴发热、体重下降，应就医排查。不要用“补钙”或“排毒”处理指甲纹路；也不要过度打磨，打磨会削薄甲板，反而更容易断。保湿可以改善外观，但不能把已经变化的甲板“磨平重生”；新甲从甲根长出来，需要几个月。Boundaries: most longitudinal ridges are benign and age-related. Seek medical advice if a single prominent groove appears, the plate suddenly turns black, white or red, separates from the nail bed, or the surrounding skin becomes red, swollen and painful; also if nails thicken or become brittle, or if fever or weight loss accompanies the change. Do not treat nail texture with calcium supplements or "detox" remedies, and do not over-polish—polishing thins the plate and makes it more likely to break. Moisturizer improves appearance but cannot "regrow" a changed plate; new nail comes from the matrix and takes months.[12][14]

04防晒霜是怎样防晒的？How does sunscreen protect the skin?

防晒剂分物理与化学两类：无机微粒在皮肤表面反射散射 UVA/UVB，有机成分吸收紫外线光子能量后转化为微量热再释放。Filters are physical or chemical: inorganic particles reflect and scatter UVA/UVB at the surface, while organic agents absorb UV photons and release a little heat.

防晒霜的作用是减少到达皮肤表皮和真皮的紫外线。紫外线按波长分 UVA（约 320–400 nm）和 UVB（约 280–320 nm）两段，UVA 穿透较深、与皮肤光老化有关，UVB 主要作用于表皮、容易引起晒红晒伤。防晒产品里的有效成分，就是在皮肤表面布置一层能拦截这两段射线的介质。紫外线光子能量高于可见光，长期或过量照射会损伤表皮细胞遗传物质并促成光老化；防晒剂的目标就是在光子到达细胞之前把它拦截或转化。Sunscreen reduces ultraviolet light that reaches the epidermis and dermis. Ultraviolet splits into UVA, about 320 to 400 nm, and UVB, about 280 to 320 nm; UVA penetrates deeper and relates to photoaging, while UVB acts mainly on the epidermis and causes redness and sunburn. The active ingredients lay a medium on the skin surface that intercepts these two bands.[1][19]

物理防晒，也称无机防晒，常用氧化锌、二氧化钛等微粒。这些颗粒停留在皮肤表面，对紫外线起反射和散射作用，像一层细小的散光屏障，把部分 UVA 和 UVB 从皮肤方向弹开或散开，减少直接打到细胞上的光子。颗粒越细，涂开后白垩感越弱，但阻隔原理仍是表面反射散射。Physical, or inorganic, sunscreen commonly uses particles such as zinc oxide and titanium dioxide. These particles stay on the skin surface and reflect and scatter ultraviolet light, acting like a fine scattering barrier that bounces or spreads part of the UVA and UVB away from the skin and reduces photons hitting cells directly. Finer particles leave less white cast, but the blocking principle remains surface reflection and scattering.[19]

化学防晒，也称有机防晒，成分分子吸收紫外线光子的能量，把高能光子转化为微量热能再慢慢释放，避免光子直接作用于表皮细胞的 DNA。不同有机分子各有吸收波长范围，配方常把多种成分搭配起来，分别覆盖 UVA 和 UVB 段。被吸收的能量以热的形式散失，不会在皮肤里累积成新的射线。物理颗粒如果粒径过大，会在皮肤表面留下白色痕迹；现代配方把颗粒做得更细，兼顾阻隔效果和涂抹观感，但作用方式仍是表面反射散射。Chemical, or organic, sunscreen molecules absorb the energy of UV photons and convert it into a small amount of heat that is released gradually, so photons do not act directly on epidermal cell DNA. Different organic molecules absorb different wavelength ranges, and formulations often combine several ingredients to cover both UVA and UVB bands. The absorbed energy leaves as heat rather than building up as new radiation in the skin.[1][19]

标注上，SPF 主要衡量对 UVB 的防护倍数，数值越高表示引起晒红所需的紫外线剂量倍数越大；UVA 防护则由另外的标识或成分范围表示。实际防护效果还取决于涂抹用量、是否均匀、出汗和摩擦后是否补涂；用量不足或被汗水冲掉，标注的防护倍数就打折扣。On labels, SPF mainly measures protection against UVB; a higher number means a larger multiple of the UV dose needed to cause redness. UVA protection is shown by separate marks or ingredient ranges. Actual protection also depends on amount applied, even coverage, and reapplication after sweating or rubbing; under-application or sweat washout lowers the labeled factor.[1][18]

边界要写清：本章只讲防晒剂阻隔紫外线的机制和常见标注含义，不推荐具体品牌、肤质用药或治疗方案。已经出现晒伤、光敏反应或皮肤疾病时，应咨询皮肤科专业人员；防晒只是减少紫外线损伤的措施之一，配合衣物、遮阳和避开强日照时段，防护更稳妥。The boundary matters: this chapter explains only how filters block ultraviolet light and what common labels mean, not brand recommendations, skin-specific medication or treatment plans. For sunburn, photosensitivity reactions or skin disease, consult a dermatology professional; sunscreen is one measure among clothing, shade and avoiding strong sun, and combining them gives more reliable protection.[19]
