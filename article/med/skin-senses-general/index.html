01指纹有什么用，为什么每个人的指纹都不相同？What are fingerprints for, and why is everyone's fingerprint different?

摩擦嵴提高抓握与触感；遗传定大类，宫内微环境决定嵴线细节，因此人人不同。Friction ridges aid grip and touch; genetics sets the broad class while fetal micro-environment decides details, making prints unique.

指纹是手指腹面皮肤上排列成嵴纹的摩擦皮纹，主要作用是增加接触摩擦力、提高抓握和触感分辨能力；每个人的指纹排列不同，这种独特性来自遗传基础与胎儿期微环境的共同作用，因此可用于身份识别。指纹在胎儿发育中期形成，之后终身保持基本形态，纹路稳定不变，除非深度损伤或皮肤疾病破坏了真皮层。Fingerprints are friction ridges on the pads of the fingers: ridged skin that raises contact friction, improves grip and helps discriminate touch. Each person's ridge arrangement differs, a uniqueness built from a genetic basis combined with micro-environmental effects in the womb, which makes fingerprints usable for identification. The pattern forms mid-gestation and stays basically unchanged for life unless deep injury or skin disease damages the dermis.[1]

皮纹的嵴由表皮与下方真皮乳头相互嵌合形成：真皮乳头向上突起，带动表皮形成平行或分支的嵴线，汗管开口在嵴顶的汗孔。嵴线在指尖、指节和掌部分别形成弓、箕、斗等基本形态，不同区域的汗腺密度和皮肤厚度也有差异。胎儿在子宫内发育的第 10 到 24 周左右，皮纹模式受皮肤生长速度、局部血流、羊水压力和手部位置等微小差异影响；基因决定皮纹大类倾向，而这些随机微环境因素决定嵴线的具体分叉、端点和螺旋位置。Ridges arise as epidermis interlocks with underlying dermal papillae: papillae project upward and pull the epidermis into parallel or branching ridge lines, with sweat ducts opening at pores on the ridge crest. Around weeks 10 to 24 of fetal development, ridge pattern is shaped by local skin growth rate, blood flow, amniotic fluid pressure and hand position; genes set the broad tendency, while these random micro-environmental factors decide the exact bifurcations, endings and whorls.[5]

证据来自胚胎解剖、双生子比较和法医皮肤学记录：同卵双胎的指纹大类可能相近，但嵴线细节并不相同，说明遗传不能单独解释唯一性；嵴线在表皮再生后仍沿原有真皮乳头模式恢复。现场采集通常留取十指纹路，比对以嵴线端点、分叉等特征点的数量和位置为准，汗孔位置和嵴线细节共同构成可比对的标记。Evidence comes from embryological anatomy, twin comparisons and forensic dermatology: identical twins may share a broad ridge category but not matching fine details, showing genetics alone cannot explain uniqueness, and ridges regenerate along the original dermal-papilla pattern after epidermal repair. Fingerprint capture records sweat and oil residue along ridges; pore positions and ridge details together form comparable minutiae.[4]

边界：指纹识别依赖纹路未被破坏。烧伤、深度割伤、长期摩擦或湿疹、银屑病等皮肤病改变真皮乳头后，指纹可以变浅或暂时消失；人为损伤也可能干扰比对。皮纹还受染色体发育异常等因素影响，某些综合征伴有特殊皮纹模式，所以指纹是发育状态的记录，而不只是孤立的表皮图案。Identification depends on the pattern staying intact. Burns, deep cuts, chronic friction or diseases such as eczema and psoriasis alter dermal papillae and can soften or temporarily erase the print; deliberate injury can also interfere with matching. Dermatoglyphics also reflect developmental conditions: certain syndromes carry distinctive ridge patterns, so a fingerprint records developmental history rather than an isolated epidermal drawing.[1][4]

与其他皮肤特征相比，指纹的稳定性和个体差异使它成为长期身份标识；理解其形成机制，也有助于区分天生皮纹与后天皮肤损伤造成的纹路变化。下一章从皮肤屏障本身讲起，说明痤疮、脓疱和痱子这些常见皮疹分别由哪些机制引起。Compared with other skin features, fingerprint stability and individual variation make it a long-term identity marker, and understanding its formation helps distinguish innate ridges from changes caused by later skin damage. The next chapter starts with the skin barrier itself, explaining the mechanisms behind acne, pustules and prickly heat.[4][5]

02为什么会长青春痘，痘痘里挤出来的白色东西是什么，痱子和保湿又各是什么机制？Why does acne form, what is the white material in a pimple, and how do prickly heat and moisturizers work?

痤疮经皮脂、角化堵塞、细菌增殖到炎症脓疱四步；白脓以中性粒细胞为主，痱子是汗管闭塞。Acne follows sebum, plugging, bacterial growth and inflammatory pustules; white pus is mainly neutrophils, prickly heat is duct blockage.

皮肤最外层的角质层是主要屏障：角化细胞叠加形成致密板层，配合皮脂和神经酰胺等脂质锁住水分、阻挡外界刺激物。痤疮、脓疱和痱子虽然都表现为皮疹，但机制不同：痤疮涉及皮脂分泌、毛囊角化、细菌增殖和炎症四步叠加；脓疱是炎症部位中性粒细胞聚集形成的脓液；痱子则是汗管闭塞、汗液外渗到周围组织造成的浅表小疹。The stratum corneum is the main outer barrier: stacked keratinocytes form a dense lamella that, together with sebum and lipids such as ceramides, retains water and blocks irritants. Acne, pustules and prickly heat all look like rashes but differ in mechanism: acne stacks sebum overproduction, follicular keratinization, bacterial growth and inflammation; a pustule forms where neutrophils gather in an inflamed site; prickly heat is superficial rash from blocked sweat ducts leaking sweat into surrounding tissue.[4]

痤疮的起点通常是雄激素水平或皮脂腺敏感性提高，皮脂分泌增多；毛囊漏斗部角质细胞脱落异常、堆积成栓，把毛孔堵塞。毛囊内的痤疮丙酸杆菌在厌氧皮脂环境中增殖，分解皮脂产生刺激物，诱发毛囊壁炎症和周围红肿。摸起来有脓液的白头或脓疱，是白细胞（主要是中性粒细胞）聚集并与组织液、细菌残骸混合后的结果。Acne often starts when androgen levels or sebaceous-gland sensitivity rise and sebum output increases. Keratinocytes at the follicle funnel shed abnormally, pile into a plug and block the pore. Cutibacterium acnes multiplies in the anaerobic sebum, breaks down lipids into irritants, and triggers follicle-wall inflammation and surrounding redness. The white, pus-filled head or pustule is a mixture of white blood cells, mainly neutrophils, with tissue fluid and bacterial debris.[5]

痱子多发生在高温高湿环境：汗腺导管开口或上段被角质、汗液黏稠成分堵塞，汗液排不出去而渗入周围表皮，形成密集的小丘疹或小水疱，伴随刺痒。保湿霜改善屏障的思路则相反：在皮肤表面形成封闭或吸湿层，减少水分经表皮流失，补充神经酰胺等脂质，帮助角质层保持柔软；它不能直接溶解已经形成的粉刺或脓疱。Prickly heat appears in heat and humidity: sweat-duct openings or upper segments are blocked by keratin and thickened sweat, so sweat cannot escape and seeps into nearby epidermis, producing dense small papules or vesicles with prickling itch. Moisturizers work the other way around: a sealing or humectant layer reduces transepidermal water loss and replenishes ceramides to keep the stratum corneum soft; they cannot dissolve existing comedones or pustules.[1]

证据来自皮肤组织观察、皮疹分型和护肤成分研究：镜下可见堵塞的毛囊角栓、扩张的皮脂腺和炎性浸润；痱子在降温干燥后较快消退，提示汗管闭塞是可逆过程。不同人群对激素、油脂成分和汗液刺激的反应不同，成人痘与婴儿疹的诱因和处理重点也有差别。Tissue observation, rash classification and skincare ingredient studies support this: biopsies show follicular plugs, enlarged sebaceous glands and inflammatory infiltration, and prickly heat clears quickly after cooling and drying, indicating reversible duct blockage. Responses to hormones, oily ingredients and sweat irritation vary, and adult acne differs in triggers and management from infant rash.[1][4]

边界：痤疮是多因素慢性炎症，单纯清洁或挤压不能纠正角化和细菌因素，挤压还可能把炎症压向深层并留下瘢痕。脓疱的白色内容物以炎症细胞为主，不等于感染化脓都需要抗生素；痱子护理的核心是降温、干燥和透气。保湿产品只修复屏障，对已经形成的囊肿型痤疮作用有限，持续或严重皮疹需要皮肤科评估。下一章转向前庭系统，解释晕车晕船为什么会伴随恶心和出汗。Acne is a multifactorial chronic inflammation: washing or squeezing alone does not correct keratinization and bacterial factors, and squeezing can push inflammation deeper and leave scars. The white material in a pustule is mostly inflammatory cells, not every purulent lesion needs antibiotics; prickly heat care centers on cooling, drying and breathable clothing. Moisturizers repair the barrier only and do little for cystic acne, so persistent or severe rash needs dermatology evaluation. The next chapter turns to the vestibular system, explaining why motion sickness brings nausea and sweating.[1][5]

03为什么有的人会晕车、晕船，前庭和视觉信号不一致是怎样引起恶心出汗的？Why do some people get carsick or seasick, and how do mismatched vestibular and visual signals cause nausea and sweating?

半规管和耳石感知运动，视觉场景与前庭信号冲突，脑干整合后触发自主神经反应。Canals and otoliths sense motion; conflict with visual input integrates in brainstem and triggers autonomic nausea and sweating.

晕车、晕船和晕机的核心解释是感觉冲突理论：内耳前庭系统与视觉系统给出的运动信号不一致，大脑接收到相互矛盾的身体状态信息，触发自主神经反应，表现为恶心、出汗、面色苍白、头晕甚至呕吐。前庭负责感知头部转动、直线加速度和重力方向，视觉负责从周围场景判断身体是否在移动。The central explanation for carsickness, seasickness and airsickness is sensory conflict theory: the inner-ear vestibular system and the visual system send inconsistent motion signals, and the brain receives contradictory body-state information, triggering an autonomic reaction with nausea, sweating, pallor, dizziness and sometimes vomiting. The vestibular system senses head rotation, linear acceleration and gravity, while vision judges whether the body is moving from the surrounding scene.[2]

前庭器官包括半规管和耳石器官：三个互相垂直的半规管感受角加速度，内淋巴流动带动毛细胞纤毛偏转；耳石中的耳石膜随直线运动和重力牵拉毛细胞，传递倾斜和线性加速度信号。坐在车内看手机或看书时，眼睛看到的画面相对静止，而前庭感知到车身颠簸、转弯和加减速，两套信号不匹配；反过来在甲板上看远处地平线，视觉与前庭信息一致，晕动症状会减轻。The vestibular organs include semicircular canals and otolith organs: three perpendicular canals sense angular acceleration, as endolymph flow bends hair-cell cilia; the otolithic membrane pulls hair cells under linear motion and gravity, conveying tilt and linear acceleration. Reading a phone or book inside a car, the eyes see a nearly still scene while the vestibular system detects sway, turns and acceleration, and the two signals mismatch; looking at a distant horizon on deck aligns visual and vestibular information and eases symptoms.[1]

冲突信号经由前庭神经传入脑干前庭核，再与视觉、本体感觉信息整合；当大脑把矛盾解释为中毒或感知系统紊乱时，激活迷走神经和交感神经，引起恶心、唾液增多、出汗和胃部不适。重复暴露后，神经系统学会在该运动情境下重新校准，症状逐渐减轻，这就是适应性训练有效、反复乘坐后不容易晕的原因。Conflict signals travel through the vestibular nerve to brainstem vestibular nuclei, where they integrate with visual and proprioceptive input. When the brain interprets the mismatch as poisoning or sensory disruption, vagal and sympathetic activation produces nausea, salivation, sweating and stomach upset. After repeated exposure, the nervous system recalibrates for that motion context and symptoms fade, which is why adaptation training and repeated rides reduce motion sickness.[5]

常见缓解方式围绕减少冲突和降低敏感性展开：注视远处固定参照物可让视觉与前庭信号重新对齐，闭眼或平躺让视觉让位给前庭，保持通风；抗组胺类药物和东莨菪碱可降低前庭或脑干整合的敏感性，需在症状出现前按说明书或医嘱使用。儿童和敏感人群反应更强，疲劳、空腹或异味环境会加重症状。Relief focuses on reducing conflict and lowering sensitivity: watch a fixed distant reference, close the eyes or lie down to let vestibular input dominate, and keep air flowing. Antihistamines and scopolamine reduce vestibular or brainstem integration sensitivity and should be used before symptoms according to instructions or a clinician. Children and sensitive people react more strongly, and fatigue, an empty stomach or odors worsen symptoms.[2][5]

边界：感觉冲突不是晕动症的唯一解释，个体差异、既往经验和焦虑预期也会放大反应；如果停止乘车后仍长期头晕、步态不稳或出现听力下降，可能是前庭神经炎、耳石脱位或其他内耳疾病，需要专科检查。抗晕药物有嗜睡、口干等副作用，不能替代病因诊断。下一章回到皮肤本身，介绍触觉末梢的种类、密度和两点辨别阈值。Sensory conflict is not the only explanation: individual differences, prior experience and anxious expectation amplify the reaction. If dizziness, unsteady gait or hearing loss persists after the ride, it may reflect vestibular neuritis, displaced otoliths or another inner-ear condition requiring specialist evaluation. Anti-motion drugs carry drowsiness and dry-mouth side effects and do not replace diagnosis. The next chapter returns to the skin, covering touch receptor types, density and two-point discrimination thresholds.[1][5]

04皮肤触摸信号是怎样被感知的，不同触觉末梢的密度和辨别阈值有什么差别？How does the skin perceive touch, and how do receptor densities and discrimination thresholds differ?

四类机械感受器按深浅、快慢适应分工；指尖密度高、两点阈约 2–5 mm，躯干阈值更高。Four mechanoreceptors split by depth and adaptation; fingertips are dense with 2–5 mm two-point threshold, trunk thresholds higher.

皮肤触摸信号由不同类型的机械感受器接收，它们在皮肤深度、感受野大小和适应速度上分工：有的负责轻触和滑动，有的负责深压和振动，有的分辨纹理，有的感知皮肤牵拉。感受器把机械形变转化为神经冲动，经周围神经传到脊髓和大脑体感皮层，大脑再根据信号来源和强度判断接触位置、质地和压力。Touch signals are received by different mechanoreceptors that divide labor by skin depth, receptive-field size and adaptation speed: some handle light touch and sliding, others deep pressure and vibration, others texture and still others skin stretch. Receptors convert mechanical deformation into nerve impulses that travel through peripheral nerves to the spinal cord and somatosensory cortex, where location, texture and pressure are judged from source and intensity.[1]

四类主要机械感受器分工明确。迈斯纳小体位于真皮乳头层，感受轻触、滑动和低频刺激，适应快，在指尖和唇部分布密集；帕奇尼小体位于深层真皮，感受深压和高频振动，也是快适应；梅克尔细胞位于表皮基底层，慢适应，负责纹理和边缘细节；鲁菲尼小体慢适应，感知皮肤牵拉和持续张力。快适应感受器对变化敏感，慢适应感受器持续报告静态压力和形状。Four main mechanoreceptors have clear roles. Meissner corpuscles sit in dermal papillae, detect light touch, sliding and low-frequency stimuli, adapt quickly and are dense at fingertips and lips. Pacinian corpuscles lie in deep dermis, detect deep pressure and high-frequency vibration and also adapt fast. Merkel cells at the epidermal base adapt slowly and signal texture and edges; Ruffini endings adapt slowly and sense stretch and sustained tension. Fast-adapting receptors respond to change, slow-adapting ones report static pressure and shape continuously.[2]

感受器密度决定感觉分辨能力：指尖和唇缘的感受器密度高、感受野小，两点辨别阈可低到约 2 到 5 毫米，即两个相距很近的点能被分辨为两点；背部、大腿等区域感受器稀疏、感受野大，两点辨别阈可达十几毫米甚至更高。同一区域不同感受器叠加，并伴随温度、痛觉等其他末梢信号，使皮肤同时传递压力、纹理、振动和温度等混合触感。Receptor density sets discrimination ability: fingertips and lip margins have dense receptors and small fields, with two-point thresholds near 2 to 5 millimeters, meaning two close points are resolved as two. Back and thigh have sparse receptors and large fields, with thresholds over ten millimeters. Receptors overlap within a region so skin transmits pressure, texture, vibration and temperature as a mixed signal.[5]

证据来自神经生理学记录和两点辨别实验：在显微解剖和电生理记录中可区分不同感受器的刺激阈值与适应曲线；体表两点阈测量绘制出感觉敏感度地图，指尖、舌和唇最敏感，躯干和近端肢体较迟钝。触觉信号还会与视觉、本体感觉整合，例如物体滑出手指时，视觉和关节位置信息参与判断抓握是否稳固。Neurophysiological recording and two-point tests support this: microdissection and electrophysiology distinguish stimulus thresholds and adaptation curves, and surface threshold maps mark fingertips, tongue and lips most sensitive, trunk and proximal limbs less so. Touch signals integrate with vision and proprioception; when an object slips from the fingers, sight and joint-position input help judge grip stability.[1][5]

边界：触觉阈值受年龄、皮肤干燥程度、神经传导和情绪状态影响，测量结果因人而异。周围神经病变、糖尿病或外伤可降低触觉敏感度；感受器分工是典型模式，实际皮肤中不同类型常有重叠和协同。下一章讨论嗅觉：气味分子怎样被受体识别，组合编码又如何把成千上万种气味区分开来。Touch thresholds vary with age, dryness, nerve conduction and emotional state, and measurements differ between people. Peripheral neuropathy, diabetes or injury can reduce sensitivity; receptor division of labor is a typical pattern, and real skin shows overlap and cooperation. The final chapter turns to olfaction: how odor molecules are recognized and how combinatorial coding distinguishes thousands of smells.[2][5]

05气味能不能被复制和重建，嗅觉受体的组合编码是怎样分辨不同气味的？Can smells be copied and rebuilt, and how does combinatorial receptor coding distinguish odors?

约 400 个嗅觉受体按组合模式编码气味，大脑据此分辨上万种气味；复杂天然气味难以完全复刻。About 400 receptors encode odors in combinations the brain uses to distinguish thousands of smells; complex natural odors resist full replication.

嗅觉识别的起点是气味分子进入鼻腔，溶解在嗅上皮表面的黏液中，与嗅纤毛上的受体结合。人类约有 400 个功能性嗅觉受体基因，每个受体对一类分子结构有响应；一种气味分子可激活多个受体，一种受体也可响应多种分子，大脑根据被激活受体的组合模式识别气味，这就是组合编码。Olfaction begins when odor molecules enter the nose, dissolve in mucus over the olfactory epithelium and bind receptors on olfactory cilia. Humans have about 400 functional olfactory receptor genes, each responding to a class of molecular structures; one odorant can activate several receptors and one receptor can respond to several molecules, so the brain identifies a smell from the activated receptor combination, called combinatorial coding.[3]

结合信号经嗅感觉神经元传到嗅球，由僧帽细胞和丛状细胞进一步汇聚，再投射到梨状皮层等嗅觉皮层区域，形成气味性质、强度和熟悉度的表征。组合编码使人能分辨成千上万种气味，而不需要为每种气味配备一个专属受体；相似结构的分子会激活部分重叠的受体组合，因此气味接近，差异则体现在组合的增减和强度上。The binding signal travels through olfactory sensory neurons to the olfactory bulb and on to piriform cortex and other olfactory areas, where odor quality, intensity and familiarity are represented. Combinatorial coding lets people distinguish thousands of smells without a dedicated receptor per odorant. Structurally similar molecules activate partly overlapping receptor combinations and smell alike; differences appear as additions, omissions or intensity changes in the combination.[1]

气味可以被记录和部分复制：调配香精和合成分子可以模拟许多常见气味，但复杂天然气味通常由上百种挥发物组成，各成分比例、挥发速度和相互作用难以完全重建。嗅觉还与情绪、记忆和进食密切相关，嗅上皮受损或受体功能下降时，会出现嗅觉减退或缺失，并影响味觉体验，因为很多所谓味道其实来自鼻后嗅觉。Smells can be recorded and partly copied: flavor compounds and synthesized molecules mimic many common odors, but complex natural smells usually contain hundreds of volatiles whose ratios, evaporation rates and interactions are hard to rebuild fully. Olfaction ties closely to emotion, memory and eating; damaged epithelium or reduced receptor function causes hyposmia or anosmia and affects taste, since much of what is called flavor arrives via retronasal smell.[5]

鼻形和鼻腔结构存在气候相关的适应趋势：寒冷干燥环境中，较长、较窄的鼻腔有更多时间给吸入空气加温加湿；温暖湿润地区的鼻腔相对短宽。这种趋势是群体水平的演化适应，个体鼻形同时受遗传、发育和随机因素影响，不能单凭外形判断嗅觉能力或族群来源。Nasal shape shows climate-related adaptive trends: in cold dry climates a longer, narrower nose gives inhaled air more time to warm and humidify, while warm humid regions favor shorter, broader nostrils. This trend is a population-level evolutionary adaptation; individual nose shape also reflects genetics, development and random factors, so form alone cannot judge olfactory ability or ancestry.[3][5]

证据来自嗅觉心理物理实验、受体基因研究和疾病记录：嗅觉阈值和辨别测试可量化气味敏感度，受体克隆与结合实验验证分子-受体对应；感冒、鼻窦炎、头部外伤和神经退行性疾病都可能损伤嗅上皮或通路。边界在于：组合编码的具体受体映射仍在研究中，人类嗅觉长期被低估，且嗅觉测量受环境气味、注意力和鼻黏膜状态影响。下一章回到整体，把皮肤与感官机制放回日常健康判断的框架中。Psychophysical tests, receptor-gene research and disease records support this: threshold and discrimination tests quantify sensitivity, receptor cloning and binding assays verify molecule-receptor matches, and colds, sinusitis, head trauma and neurodegenerative disease can damage epithelium or pathways. The exact receptor mapping of combinatorial coding is still under study, human olfaction has long been underestimated, and measurements shift with ambient odor, attention and mucosal condition. Taken together, these skin and sensory mechanisms form the frame for everyday health judgment.[1][5]

06狐臭为什么会有，大汗腺、菌群和基因各起什么作用？What causes body odor, and what role do apocrine glands, bacteria and genes play?

大汗腺分泌物被棒状杆菌等分解成挥发气味；ABCC11 影响分泌物类型，小汗腺汗液本身不臭。Apocrine secretions are broken by Corynebacterium into volatile odor; ABCC11 affects secretion type, and eccrine sweat itself is odorless.

体味（狐臭、腋臭）来自大汗腺分泌物被皮肤菌群分解，而不是汗液本身有臭味。人体汗腺分两类：小汗腺遍布全身，分泌稀薄水样汗液，主要用于散热；大汗腺（顶泌腺）集中在腋窝、乳晕、腹股沟等部位，开口于毛囊，分泌物较黏稠，含脂质、蛋白质和脂肪酸类物质，本身气味不重。进入青春期后，雄激素水平上升使大汗腺活跃，这也是体味多在青春期后明显的原因。Body odor, including axillary odor, arises when apocrine secretions are broken down by skin bacteria; sweat itself is not the source. Eccrine glands cover the body and secrete dilute watery sweat for heat loss, while apocrine glands concentrate in the armpits, areola and groin, open into hair follicles, and release thicker fluid rich in lipids, proteins and fatty acids with little odor of their own. Androgen levels rise at puberty and activate the apocrine glands, which is why odor usually becomes noticeable after adolescence.[1][5]

菌群把分泌物变成气味。腋窝温暖潮湿，棒状杆菌等细菌在皮肤表面繁殖，分解大汗腺分泌物中的脂质和脂肪酸，产生短链脂肪酸、硫化物等挥发性气味分子；不同人的菌群组成和分泌物成分不同，气味强度和类型也有差别。小汗腺汗液主要是水和盐，被细菌分解后也会产生一般汗味，但腋下典型的刺鼻体味主要来自大汗腺—菌群这条路径。Bacteria convert secretion into scent. The warm, damp armpit supports Corynebacterium and other skin flora, which break down lipids and fatty acids in apocrine fluid into volatile short-chain fatty acids and sulfur compounds. Flora composition and secretion differ between people, so odor strength and type vary. Eccrine sweat, mostly water and salt, also picks up general body odor when colonised, but the sharp typical axillary odor follows the apocrine-bacteria pathway.[1][4]

遗传基础。ABCC11 基因影响大汗腺分泌物的类型和耳垢干湿状态：该基因的特定变异与耳垢干燥、体味较轻相关，在东亚人群中频率较高；携带另一型变异的人耳垢偏湿、腋下大汗腺分泌物更易被细菌分解，体味通常更明显。这是群体水平的遗传关联，单个基因不能决定全部气味，饮食、卫生习惯、菌群和出汗量都会叠加影响。Genetic basis. The ABCC11 gene influences apocrine secretion type and earwax consistency: one variant correlates with dry earwax and lighter body odor, common in East Asian populations, while the other wet earwax type tends to carry more decomposable apocrine material and stronger odor. This is a population-level association; one gene does not determine the whole smell, and diet, hygiene, flora and sweating add their own effects.[1][5]

可核对的观察。体味在出汗多、衣物不透气时加重；清洁、透气衣物和减少细菌负荷可以减轻气味，但不能消除大汗腺本身。持续强烈的体味、异常气味（如霉味、果味）或伴随皮肤红肿、渗液，需要皮肤科或内科评估；代谢性疾病、感染也可能改变体味。把体味简单归为不讲卫生，忽略大汗腺类型和遗传差异，并不客观。Observations. Odor worsens with heavy sweating and non-breathable clothing; cleaning and reducing bacterial load lighten it but cannot remove the glands themselves. Persistent strong odor, an unusual smell such as musty or fruity notes, or accompanying redness and discharge warrants dermatology or internal medicine evaluation, since metabolic disease or infection can alter odor. Reducing odor to poor hygiene ignores gland type and genetic differences.[4][5]

边界。本章只讲机制，不写治疗方案。除臭、止汗产品的选择和肉毒素、手术等干预需由皮肤科评估；儿童体味过早明显可结合发育和内分泌情况咨询医生。ABCC11 关联是群体统计结论，不能凭耳垢类型判断个人体味轻重；把遗传易感性说成注定有狐臭，或认为清洁可以完全改变腺体类型，都越过了证据边界。Boundary. This chapter explains mechanism only and gives no treatment plan. Deodorant and antiperspirant choice, botulinum toxin or surgical intervention should be assessed by dermatology; unusually early odor in children may warrant developmental and endocrine review. The ABCC11 link is a population statistic, and earwax type cannot predict personal odor; calling susceptibility destined to have body odor, or claiming hygiene can change gland type, exceeds the evidence.[1][5]

07皮肤为什么会痒，组胺、神经和屏障各起什么作用？Why does skin itch, and what role do histamine, nerves and the barrier play?

组胺与 IL-31 等介质激活痒觉末梢；屏障受损降低痒阈，抓挠形成痒—抓—更痒循环。Histamine and IL-31 activate itch endings; barrier damage lowers the threshold, and scratching forms an itch-scratch cycle.

皮肤痒是一套独立于痛觉的感觉信号，由致痒介质激活皮肤内的痒觉神经末梢，经周围神经传到脊髓和大脑体感皮层。最常见的致痒介质是组胺：肥大细胞在过敏、虫咬或皮肤受刺激时脱颗粒释放组胺，结合痒觉神经上的受体，产生瘙痒信号；除此之外，IL-31 等细胞因子、神经肽和皮肤屏障破损后的化学刺激也能引发痒感，不同介质对应的痒类型并不完全相同。Itch is a sensation separate from pain: pruritogens activate itch-specific endings in the skin, which travel through peripheral nerves to the spinal cord and somatosensory cortex. Histamine is the best-known mediator: mast cells degranulate under allergy, insect bites or irritation and release histamine, which binds receptors on itch nerves to produce the signal. Beyond histamine, cytokines such as IL-31, neuropeptides and chemical stimulation after barrier breakage can trigger itch, and these itch types do not respond identically.[1][5]

屏障为什么决定痒感门槛。最外层角质层和脂质构成皮肤屏障；屏障完整时，外界刺激物、过敏原和干燥因子被挡在外面，神经末梢受刺激较少。屏障受损（干燥、湿疹、搔抓、洗护过度）后，通透性升高，致痒介质更容易接触神经末梢，痒阈下降；抓挠又造成新的微小破损，屏障进一步松动，形成痒—抓—更痒的循环。The barrier sets the itch threshold. The stratum corneum and lipids form the outer barrier; when intact it keeps irritants, allergens and drying agents away from nerve endings. A damaged barrier, from dryness, eczema, scratching or over-washing, raises permeability, lets mediators reach endings more easily and lowers the itch threshold. Scratching adds fresh micro-damage, loosening the barrier further and establishing an itch-scratch-itch cycle.[1][4]

神经与编码。痒觉有相对特异的神经通路和受体分子，组胺能痒和非组胺能痒（如 IL-31 介导）在通路和反应上有区别：抗组胺药对组胺相关的痒（如荨麻疹）效果较明显，对部分慢性湿疹、干燥性痒作用有限。皮肤神经还与温度、触觉感受器叠加，所以摩擦、冷热变化都会改变痒感；反复搔抓会在中枢形成敏感化，使原来轻微的刺激也觉得痒。Nerves and coding. Itch has relatively specific pathways and receptor molecules; histaminergic itch differs from non-histaminergic itch such as IL-31 signalling. Antihistamines work well against histamine-driven itch like urticaria but are less effective for chronic eczema or dry itch. Skin nerves overlap with temperature and touch receptors, so friction and hot or cold changes alter the sensation; repeated scratching also sensitises central pathways, so mild stimuli start to feel itchy.[1][5]

可核对的观察。虫咬、荨麻疹的风团伴明显组胺性痒；湿疹、特应性皮炎以屏障受损和慢性痒为主，常伴干燥、红斑和抓痕；老年人皮肤干燥引起的干燥性痒，在秋冬和洗浴过勤后加重。鉴别痒的原因要看分布、持续时间、有无皮疹、全身症状和诱因，不能只凭痒一个字判断。Observations. Insect bites and urticarial wheals show marked histamine itch; eczema and atopic dermatitis center on barrier damage and chronic itch, often with dryness, redness and scratch marks; dry-skin itch in older adults worsens in winter and after over-washing. Cause assessment looks at distribution, duration, rash, systemic symptoms and triggers, not the single word itch.[4][5]

边界。本章只讲机制，不给用药指令。持续全身痒、夜间痒到影响睡眠、伴黄疸、皮疹、脱屑或体重下降，需要皮肤科或内科检查，排查过敏、皮肤病、肝胆或肾脏相关原因；局部短暂痒多与干燥、摩擦或虫咬有关。把所有痒都归为过敏、一律吃抗组胺药，或认为抓挠可以把痒抓出来，与现有机制和证据不符。Boundary. Mechanism only, no medication instructions. Persistent generalized itch, itch severe enough to disturb sleep, or itch with jaundice, rash, scaling or weight loss needs dermatology or internal medicine to check allergy, skin disease, or liver and kidney-related causes; brief local itch usually tracks dryness, friction or bites. Treating every itch as allergy and reaching for antihistamines, or thinking scratching draws the itch out, does not match current mechanism and evidence.[1][5]
