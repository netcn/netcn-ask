0112 岁以后仍尿床，在医学上如何划分遗尿？背后是哪些机制？If bedwetting continues past age 12, how is enuresis classified medically, and what mechanisms drive it?

按年龄、频率与病程分型，机制涉及夜间多尿、唤醒阈值偏高和膀胱容量不足三因素叠加。Classified by age, frequency and course; mechanisms combine nocturnal polyuria, high arousal threshold and small bladder capacity.

遗尿指儿童在已经具备控制排尿能力的年龄之后，仍反复在睡眠中尿湿床铺。国际儿童尿控学会把没有白天排尿症状的情况称为单症状性夜遗尿；诊断通常要求年龄达到 5 岁左右（按发育年龄评估）、每周至少发生 2 次、持续至少 3 个月。5 岁以前偶尔尿床仍属于排尿控制尚未成熟的常见过程，需要先与病理性遗尿区分开。Enuresis means repeated bedwetting during sleep after a child has reached the age at which bladder control is normally expected. The International Children's Continence Society calls the form without daytime symptoms monosymptomatic nocturnal enuresis. Diagnosis usually requires an age around five years (assessed by developmental age), at least two episodes per week, and persistence for at least three months. Occasional wetting before age five is still a common part of maturing bladder control and must be separated from clinical enuresis first.[1][2]

夜间多尿是第一条机制。正常情况下加压素（抗利尿激素）在夜间分泌升高，让肾脏减少夜间尿量；如果这一节律不足，肾脏夜间产生的尿液偏多，膀胱在睡前来不及容纳，充盈信号就会触发排尿。另一部分孩子膀胱功能性容量偏小，或逼尿肌在储尿期出现不稳定收缩，也会在夜间较早达到排尿阈值。Nocturnal polyuria is the first mechanism. Vasopressin, the antidiuretic hormone, normally rises at night so the kidneys produce less overnight urine; when this rhythm is insufficient, nighttime urine output stays high and the bladder cannot hold it before sleep. Some children have a smaller functional bladder capacity, or the detrusor contracts unstably during filling, reaching the voiding threshold earlier at night.[2]

第二条机制是唤醒阈值过高。膀胱充盈产生的神经信号本应在睡眠中把孩子唤起，但部分孩子的睡眠—唤醒回路对这类信号不敏感，排尿反射在深睡中完成而本人并未醒来。排尿信号传导和大脑唤醒中枢的阈值随发育成熟逐步下调，部分孩子这一进程偏慢。夜间多尿、膀胱容量不足和唤醒困难三个因素常常叠加出现，家族中常有类似病史，提示存在遗传倾向。The second mechanism is a high arousal threshold. Nerve signals from a full bladder should wake the child during sleep, but in some children the sleep-arousal circuit is insensitive to these signals, and the voiding reflex completes in deep sleep without waking them. Nocturnal polyuria, small bladder capacity and poor arousal often overlap, and a family history is common, which points to a genetic component.[2]

按病程可分为原发性与继发性：原发性遗尿指孩子从未连续干床半年以上；继发性遗尿指已经连续干床半年之后再次出现尿床。继发性遗尿需要排查便秘、尿路感染、糖尿病、睡眠呼吸暂停等诱因。ICCS 2020 年标准化文件与 NHS 儿童尿床资料给出了定义、分型和评估框架；这一领域的证据以观察性队列研究和专家共识为主，诊断标准本身来自临床共识。By course, enuresis is primary or secondary. Primary means the child has never stayed dry for six months; secondary means wetting returns after six months of dryness. Secondary enuresis calls for checking constipation, urinary tract infection, diabetes and sleep apnea. The ICCS 2020 standardization document and the NHS bedwetting guidance provide definitions, subtypes and an assessment framework; evidence in this field rests mainly on observational cohorts and expert consensus, and the diagnostic criteria themselves are clinical consensus.[1][2]

边界需要说清：本章只描述诊断标准与发生机制，不展开治疗建议。遗尿不等于孩子故意不听话，也不直接等同于泌尿系统畸形；5 岁以下的偶发尿床多数随发育改善。成人偶发或新近出现的尿失禁属于另一类问题，需要按成年泌尿外科或妇科评估路径处理，不能套用儿童遗尿的分型。The boundary: this chapter describes diagnostic criteria and mechanisms only, not treatment advice. Enuresis is not deliberate misbehavior, nor does it directly imply a urinary tract malformation. Occasional wetting under five usually improves with development. Occasional or new urinary incontinence in adults follows a separate evaluation path in adult urology or gynecology and cannot be classified using the childhood enuresis subtypes.[1][2]

02皮肤割伤后伤口经历哪几个愈合阶段？为什么不宜反复擦拭新长的组织？What stages does a cut wound go through, and why should new healing tissue not be wiped repeatedly?

愈合按止血、炎症、增殖、重塑四阶段推进；反复擦拭破坏血凝块与迁移中的上皮细胞。Healing runs through hemostasis, inflammation, proliferation and remodeling; repeated wiping disrupts the clot and migrating epithelium.

皮肤切割伤之后，愈合按止血、炎症、增殖、重塑四个阶段顺序推进，各阶段时间上有重叠但功能分工明确。反复擦拭创面主要破坏刚形成的血凝块和正在向中心迁移的上皮细胞，并不会加快愈合；新长出的肉芽组织和上皮非常脆弱，外力反复剥离会把愈合过程重新拉回炎症阶段。After a skin cut, healing proceeds in four overlapping but functionally distinct stages: hemostasis, inflammation, proliferation and remodeling. Repeated wiping mainly damages the freshly formed clot and the epithelial cells migrating toward the wound center; it does not speed healing. New granulation tissue and epithelium are fragile, and repeated stripping pulls the process back into the inflammatory stage.[3][4]

止血期最先发生。受损血管先短暂收缩，血小板在破损处聚集形成栓块，同时释放凝血因子启动凝血级联，随后纤维蛋白交织成网，把血细胞和血小板固定成血凝块覆盖创面；表面干燥后形成的结痂起到临时屏障作用，阻挡外界微生物和水分流失。这一阶段的目标是尽快把出血止住并封闭伤口。Hemostasis comes first. Damaged vessels constrict briefly, platelets aggregate at the break to form a plug, and fibrin weaves a mesh that traps blood cells and platelets into a clot covering the wound. As the surface dries, the scab acts as a temporary barrier against microbes and water loss. The goal of this stage is to stop bleeding and seal the wound quickly.[4]

炎症期紧随其后。中性粒细胞先到达创面清除细菌和异物，随后巨噬细胞清理坏死组织并分泌生长因子和信号分子，募集修复细胞、衔接血管与成纤维细胞活化；局部红、肿、热、痛与免疫细胞募集和血管通透性升高有关。如果伤口残留异物、坏死组织或发生感染，炎症期会被拉长，创面长期停留在清理阶段而无法进入修复。The inflammatory stage follows. Neutrophils arrive first to clear bacteria and foreign material, then macrophages remove dead tissue and secrete signals that recruit repair cells. Local redness, swelling, warmth and pain relate to immune-cell recruitment and increased vascular permeability. If foreign material, necrotic tissue or infection remains, the inflammatory stage stretches out and the wound cannot move into repair.[4]

增殖期以组织重建为核心。成纤维细胞合成胶原纤维并形成肉芽组织，新生血管长入缺血区域带来氧气和营养，上皮细胞从创缘向中心迁移，逐步覆盖裸露创面；这一阶段通常在伤后数天到三周内展开。进入重塑期后，沉积的胶原重新排列、交联并增强强度，瘢痕组织继续成熟，这一过程可以持续数月到数年，最终瘢痕颜色变浅、质地变硬。The proliferative stage centers on rebuilding tissue. Fibroblasts synthesize collagen and form granulation tissue, new vessels grow into the ischemic area, and epithelial cells migrate from the wound edges toward the center to cover the exposed surface; this stage usually unfolds over days to about three weeks. In remodeling, deposited collagen realigns, cross-links and gains strength, and the scar keeps maturing for months to years until it pales and firms.[4]

愈合速度受伤口深度、部位、年龄、营养状态和局部血液循环影响；伤口边缘对合越整齐，上皮迁移距离越短，愈合越快。糖尿病、外周血管病变等情况下，伤口容易停滞在炎症期，形成慢性难愈创面。本章描述的是自然愈合的一般过程，不提供具体处理方案；深部伤口、异物残留、持续渗液或红肿扩大等感染征象需要由临床人员评估，不能仅凭阶段判断自行处理。Healing speed depends on wound depth, location, age, nutrition and local circulation. In diabetes or peripheral vascular disease, wounds easily stall in inflammation and become chronic non-healing ulcers. This chapter describes the general natural healing process and gives no specific management plan. Deep wounds, retained foreign material, ongoing drainage or spreading redness need clinical assessment and cannot be judged from the staging alone.[3][4]

03胎记是什么，为什么很多人出生时就带有胎记？What is a birthmark, and why are so many people born with one?

胎记分血管性与色素性两类，分别来自胚胎期血管发育异常或黑素细胞迁移滞留。Birthmarks split into vascular and pigmented groups, from embryonic vessel anomalies or trapped migrating melanocytes.

胎记是出生时或出生后不久出现的皮肤颜色、质地或形态改变，主要分为血管性和色素性两大类。它们来自胚胎发育阶段局部血管或黑素细胞的发育异常：血管结构在真皮层形成异常扩张或增殖，黑素细胞在迁移路径中局部滞留或成团。胎记的形成与孕期饮食、活动或母亲情绪没有因果关系，不能归因于怀孕期间接触的普通事物。A birthmark is a skin change in color, texture or shape present at birth or appearing shortly afterward. The two main groups are vascular and pigmented birthmarks. They arise from developmental anomalies of local blood vessels or melanocytes during embryogenesis: vessels form abnormal dilation or proliferation in the dermis, or melanocytes stop migrating and cluster locally. Birthmarks have no causal link to the mother's diet, activity or mood during pregnancy and cannot be attributed to ordinary exposures.[5]

血管性胎记由皮肤血管发育差异造成。鲑鱼斑是真皮浅层毛细血管扩张形成的淡粉红斑，常见于眉间、眼睑和颈后，多数随年龄增长变淡；按压后褪色、松开复红是这类毛细血管扩张皮损的常见表现。婴幼儿血管瘤是血管内皮细胞增殖形成的红色隆起，出生后常先增大，随后在数年内逐步消退；鲜红斑痣属于毛细血管畸形，出生即有并持续存在，随皮肤生长逐渐加深、增厚。Vascular birthmarks come from developmental differences in skin vessels. Salmon patches are pale pink marks from dilated superficial dermal capillaries, common between the brows, on eyelids and the back of the neck, and most fade with age. Infantile hemangiomas are red raised lesions from proliferating endothelial cells; they often enlarge after birth then gradually involute over several years. Port-wine stains are capillary malformations present at birth that persist and darken and thicken as the skin grows.[5][6]

色素性胎记由黑素细胞的位置或数量异常造成。蒙古斑是胚胎期黑素细胞向表皮迁移时部分停留在真皮层，光线经过皮肤散射后呈现蓝灰色斑块，多位于腰骶部，常在儿童期逐渐消退；先天性黑素痣是真皮或表皮内的黑素细胞团块，出生时即存在，大小从斑点到大面积斑块不等。MedlinePlus 与 NHS 的胎记资料按外观、深度和自然病程对这些类型进行分类。Pigmented birthmarks come from abnormal location or number of melanocytes. Mongolian spots form when melanocytes migrating toward the epidermis during embryogenesis remain trapped in the dermis; light scattering through the skin makes them look blue-gray, usually on the lower back, and they often fade in childhood. Congenital melanocytic nevi are clusters of melanocytes in the dermis or epidermis present at birth, ranging from small spots to large plaques. MedlinePlus and NHS birthmark guidance classify these types by appearance, depth and natural course.[5][6]

多数胎记为良性，以观察其自然变化为主。面积很大的先天性黑素痣需要长期随访评估恶变风险；面部沿三叉神经分布的鲜红斑痣，有时需要排查相关的神经系统或眼部合并异常。分类依赖临床外观检查，必要时结合皮肤镜或影像学评估。Most birthmarks are benign and followed for natural change. Very large congenital melanocytic nevi need long-term surveillance for malignancy risk; port-wine stains on the face along trigeminal branches sometimes require checking associated neurological or eye abnormalities. Classification relies on clinical appearance, supplemented by dermoscopy or imaging when needed.[6]

胎记与后天获得的色素痣不是同一类：后者在出生后由局部黑素细胞增殖形成，出现时间和发展轨迹不同。仅凭外观不能替代诊断，如果胎记快速增大、破溃、出血、颜色不均或边界不对称，需要皮肤科评估。本章只解释分类与形成机制，不讨论具体治疗选择，也不对个体胎记的预后下结论。Birthmarks differ from acquired moles, which form later from local melanocyte proliferation and have a different onset and trajectory. Appearance alone cannot replace diagnosis; rapid enlargement, ulceration, bleeding, uneven color or asymmetric borders warrant dermatology assessment. This chapter explains classification and formation mechanisms only, does not discuss treatment choices, and makes no prognosis for an individual mark.[5][6]

04器官移植后为什么会排异？年轻供体器官能让受者变年轻吗？Why does organ transplant rejection occur, and can a young donor organ make the recipient younger?

排异由 HLA 差异驱动，分超急、急性与慢性；器官保留供者年龄轨迹，不传递经历记忆。Rejection follows HLA mismatch with hyperacute, acute and chronic forms; the organ keeps the donor's age trajectory and carries no experiential memory.

器官排异由供者与受者之间的 HLA 差异驱动。HLA（人类白细胞抗原）是细胞表面的主要组织相容性分子，受者的免疫系统把供者器官上的 HLA 识别为外来抗原并发起攻击；HLA 配型越接近、组织相容性越好，排异风险通常越低。除 HLA 外，ABO 血型匹配和预存抗体状态也会影响移植后早期风险。Organ rejection is driven by HLA differences between donor and recipient. HLA, the human leukocyte antigen, is the major histocompatibility molecule on cell surfaces; the recipient immune system recognizes the donor organ's HLA as foreign and attacks it. The closer the HLA match, the better the histocompatibility and the generally lower the rejection risk. ABO blood-group matching and preformed antibody status also affect early post-transplant risk.[7][8]

按发生时间和机制，排异分为几类。超急性排异由体内预存抗体识别供者组织引起，可在血管接通后数分钟到数小时内发生；急性排异主要由 T 细胞识别供者抗原启动，多在术后数天到数周出现；慢性排异涉及抗体介导的血管损伤和间质纤维化，在数月到数年中逐步影响器官功能。机制上又可分为细胞性排异和抗体介导排异两类，供者特异性抗体水平和移植活检结果用于判断排异类型与活动度。By timing and mechanism, rejection falls into several types. Hyperacute rejection is caused by preformed antibodies recognizing donor tissue and can appear within minutes to hours after vessels are connected. Acute rejection is initiated mainly by T cells recognizing donor antigens, usually days to weeks after surgery. Chronic rejection involves antibody-mediated vascular injury and interstitial fibrosis that gradually impair organ function over months to years. Mechanistically, rejection is divided into cellular and antibody-mediated types, corresponding on tests to T-cell infiltration and donor-specific antibody evidence.[8]

免疫抑制药物用来压制受者的免疫应答，降低对供者器官的攻击。这类药物需要长期规律服用，受者停药后，体内已有的记忆免疫细胞可以再次识别供者抗原并攻击移植器官，因此排异风险在停药后明显回升；免疫抑制同时削弱对感染和异常细胞的监控能力，药物剂量和组合由移植团队在排异与感染两类风险之间平衡调整。Immunosuppressive drugs suppress the recipient's immune response and reduce attack on the donor organ. These medications are taken long term and regularly; after stopping, existing memory immune cells can recognize donor antigens again and attack the graft, so rejection risk rises sharply. Dose and combination are balanced by the transplant team against rejection and infection risks.[7]

关于两个常见疑问：年轻供体的器官移植给年长者，并不会让受者整体变年轻。器官保留供者自身的年龄轨迹和获取、保存过程中的缺血损伤，在受者体内按自身结构继续衰老；受者其余组织仍按原有年龄进程变化。也没有可靠的对照证据表明移植器官保留了供者的经历或记忆；器官中的过客白细胞会逐渐被受者自己的免疫细胞替换。On two common questions: a young donor organ given to an older recipient does not make the recipient younger overall. The organ keeps its own age trajectory and the ischemic damage from recovery and preservation, and continues aging by its own structure inside the recipient; the recipient's other tissues keep their own aging course. There is no reliable controlled evidence that a transplanted organ retains the donor's experiences or memories; passenger leukocytes in the graft are gradually replaced by the recipient's own immune cells.[7][8]

本章描述的是免疫机制与排异分型，不给出用药、配型或手术建议。供器官的年龄、缺血保存时间、受者基础疾病和免疫状态共同决定远期预后；出现移植后不适或指标变化时，应由移植团队按随访路径评估，不能依据机制描述自行调整药物。This chapter describes immunological mechanisms and rejection subtypes, not medication, matching or surgical advice. Donor organ age, ischemic preservation time, recipient underlying disease and immune status together decide long-term outcomes. Symptoms or lab changes after transplant should be assessed by the transplant team through follow-up; medication should not be adjusted from a mechanism description alone.[7][8]

05发烧为什么先觉得冷、随后又觉得热？Why do people feel cold first and hot later when fever sets in?

下丘脑调定点上移，身体先寒战产热、收缩血管保温，体温升到新水平后才觉得热。The hypothalamic set point rises; shivering and vasoconstriction conserve heat until temperature reaches the new target.

发热时先怕冷、后觉得热，根源在下丘脑的体温调定点。正常核心体温约 36.5 到 37.2 ℃，下丘脑视前区像恒温器，把身体温度维持在这一范围附近。感染或炎症时，免疫细胞释放白细胞介素-1、肿瘤坏死因子等细胞因子，这些致热原经血流作用于下丘脑，使调定点上移 1 到 2 ℃；身体判断当前体温低于新设定值，于是启动产热和保温反应，人就先觉得冷。Fever starts with a cold feeling because the hypothalamic set point has been raised. Normal core body temperature sits around 36.5 to 37.2 degrees Celsius, and the preoptic area of the hypothalamus acts like a thermostat holding it there. During infection or inflammation, immune cells release cytokines such as IL-1 and TNF; these pyrogens travel in the blood and act on the hypothalamus, shifting the set point upward by 1 to 2 degrees. The body then reads its current temperature as below the new target, so heat production and heat conservation begin, which is why chills appear first.[9]

畏寒与寒战是上升期的典型表现。调定点上移后，皮肤血管收缩以减少体表散热，手脚因此偏凉；骨骼肌出现节律性不随意收缩，即寒战，肌肉快速交替收缩与放松，产热效率明显高于安静状态，人还会蜷缩身体、起鸡皮疙瘩。这一阶段体温仍在上升，主观感觉是冷，测量体温却可能已经偏高；畏寒越明显，往往提示体温上升速度越快。Chills and vasoconstriction follow during the rising phase. Once the set point rises, skin blood vessels constrict to reduce heat loss, which explains cool hands and feet. Skeletal muscles begin rhythmic involuntary contractions known as shivering, and muscle metabolism raises heat output; the person may curl up and develop goosebumps. During this phase the temperature is still climbing, so the person feels cold even though the thermometer may already read high. The more obvious the chills, the faster the temperature is usually rising.[9][11]

当核心体温升到新调定点附近，产热与散热重新平衡，进入高热持续期。皮肤血管转而舒张，面部发红发热，人就由怕冷转为觉得热。体温每升高约 1 ℃，心率通常增加 10 到 15 次/分，代谢率也随之上升。调定点上移是生理性的重新设定，并不等于身体被烧坏；多数发热由感染引起，持续时间因病因而异。When core temperature reaches the new set point, production and loss rebalance in the fever plateau. Skin vessels relax again, the face flushes and feels hot, and the person switches from feeling cold to feeling hot. Each 1 degree rise in temperature typically raises heart rate by about 10 to 15 beats per minute, and metabolic rate rises with it. This set-point reset is a physiological adjustment rather than the body simply overheating; most fevers follow infection, and their duration varies with the cause.[10][11]

不同人群的体温反应并不一致。老人、婴幼儿和免疫低下者的体温反应可能不典型，寒战也可见于严重感染、输液反应等情况。本章只解释体温调节机制，不给出用药方案。出现持续高热不退、精神萎靡、皮疹、呼吸困难、意识改变，或发热超过约定时间仍不缓解，应由临床人员评估，不能凭怕冷还是怕热自行判断病因。Reactions differ between groups. Older adults, infants and immunocompromised people may not show a typical fever response, and rigors can also occur with severe infection or infusion reactions. This chapter explains thermoregulation only and gives no medication plan. Persistent high fever, lethargy, rash, breathing difficulty, altered consciousness, or a fever that does not settle within the expected timeframe should be assessed clinically; feeling cold or hot alone cannot identify the cause.[9][10]

当致热原被清除，调定点逐步回落到正常范围，身体转而加强散热：皮肤血管扩张、出汗，体温缓慢下降。退热期的出汗与乏力感，来自散热增加和疾病本身的消耗。是否需要降温干预、采用什么方式，由医生结合年龄、基础疾病和病因决定，个人经验不能照搬。When pyrogens clear, the set point gradually returns to normal and heat loss is ramped up through skin vasodilation and sweating, and the temperature falls slowly. The sweating and tiredness of the defervescence phase come from increased heat loss and the general consumption of illness. Whether cooling is needed and how it is done is decided by a clinician based on age, underlying disease and cause, and home routines should not be copied from other people.[10][11]

06为什么退烧时会全身出汗？Why does the body sweat all over when a fever breaks?

调定点回落后皮肤血管扩张、汗腺分泌，汗液蒸发带走热量，退热期出汗并伴乏力。After the set point returns, skin vessels dilate and sweat glands secrete; evaporation removes heat during defervescence.

退热期出汗，是身体把多余热量排出去的主要途径。发热平台期体温维持在调高的调定点上；致热原减少后，下丘脑调定点回到 37 ℃ 附近，核心体温相对偏高，于是指令皮肤增加散热。皮肤血管舒张、血流量增加，体表温度升高；汗腺受交感胆碱能纤维支配而分泌汗液，汗液在皮肤表面蒸发带走汽化热，每蒸发约 1 升汗液可带走约 2.4 MJ 热量，约合 580 千卡。Sweating during defervescence is the body's main route for shedding excess heat. During the fever plateau the temperature is held at the raised set point; when pyrogens diminish, the hypothalamic set point returns to around 37 degrees, and core temperature reads relatively high, so the skin is told to increase heat loss. Skin vessels dilate and blood flow rises, warming the surface; sweat glands controlled by sympathetic cholinergic fibers secrete sweat, which evaporates from the skin and carries away latent heat. Evaporating about one liter of sweat removes roughly 2.4 MJ, or about 580 kcal, of heat.[9][11]

出汗为什么集中在退热阶段，与体温分期有关。上升期身体优先保温，皮肤血管收缩、出汗受到抑制；平台期散热与产热平衡，出汗不多；只有调定点回落后，散热反应才被全面开启，表现为全身出汗、衣物被褥潮湿，体温随之逐步下降。出汗程度因人而异，也受环境温度和湿度影响：湿度高时汗液不易蒸发，散热效率下降，人会觉得汗黏而热感不退。Sweating concentrates in the defervescence phase because of how fever stages work. During the rising phase the body prioritizes heat conservation, skin vessels constrict and sweating is suppressed; in the plateau heat production and loss balance, so little sweat appears. Only after the set point drops does the heat-loss response open fully, producing generalized sweating, damp clothes and bedding, and a slow temperature decline. How much people sweat varies individually and with ambient temperature and humidity; when humidity is high, sweat cannot evaporate easily, cooling efficiency drops, and the sweat feels sticky while heat persists.[10]

大量出汗同时丢失水分和少量电解质，可出现口干、乏力、心率偏快；退热过程中体温下降并不代表病因已经消除，只是体温调节回到平衡。发热期间代谢消耗增加，退热后一段时间仍可能疲倦。是否补充液体、补充何种液体，应结合个人情况由临床人员判断，本章不给出具体补液或用药方案。Heavy sweating also loses water and a small amount of electrolytes, which can cause dry mouth, fatigue and a faster heart rate. A falling temperature during defervescence does not mean the underlying cause has cleared; it only means thermoregulation has rebalanced. Metabolic consumption during fever leaves tiredness that may persist for a while afterward. Whether and how fluids are replaced, and with what fluid, should be decided clinically for the individual; this chapter gives no rehydration or medication plan.[9][11]

发热三个阶段的表现可以对照看。上升期畏寒、寒战、皮肤苍白；平台期皮肤潮红、自觉燥热、心率加快；退热期出汗、体温缓慢回落。不同部位测量的体温正常范围略有差异，腋温、口温、耳温不能直接画等号。老人、儿童和慢性病患者对体温变化的耐受不同，出汗过多时跌倒、脱水的风险需要留意。The three fever stages can be compared directly. The rising phase brings chills, rigors and pale skin; the plateau brings flushed skin, subjective heat and a faster pulse; the defervescence phase brings sweating and a gradual fall in temperature. Readings from armpit, mouth and ear thermometers have slightly different normal ranges and cannot be treated as identical. Older adults, children and people with chronic illness tolerate temperature changes differently, and heavy sweating raises fall and dehydration risks that deserve attention.[10][11]

少量出汗退热属于常见过程；若大汗淋漓伴头晕、晕厥、四肢湿冷，或发热反复超过数天，伴随胸痛、呼吸困难、意识模糊、持续呕吐等，应及时就医评估。发热原因从普通病毒感染到需要排查的严重感染都有可能，单凭出汗多少不能判断轻重。本章描述机制，不替代诊断，也不讨论具体处理药物。Modest sweating as a fever breaks is common, but profuse sweating with dizziness, fainting or cold clammy limbs, a fever recurring over several days, or chest pain, breathing difficulty, confusion and persistent vomiting warrants prompt clinical assessment. Causes range from ordinary viral infection to serious infection that must be ruled out, and the amount of sweat alone cannot judge severity. This chapter describes mechanism only, does not replace diagnosis, and does not discuss specific treatment drugs.[9][10]

07为什么蹲久了站起来会眼前发黑？Why does everything go dark in front of the eyes after standing up from a squat?

站立时下肢血液淤积，压力感受器反射代偿不足导致短暂脑灌注下降，出现黑朦。Gravity pools blood in the legs; when the baroreflex compensates poorly, brief cerebral hypoperfusion causes lightheadedness.

蹲久或久坐后突然站起，眼前发黑、头晕，多数是体位变化瞬间脑部灌注短暂下降。人体站立时，重力使约 300 到 800 毫升血液淤积在下肢和腹腔静脉，回心血量短暂减少。正常人通过压力感受器反射，交感神经兴奋、心率加快、下肢血管收缩，在数秒内把血压稳住；反射代偿不足时，收缩压下降过多，脑部供血短暂减少，视网膜和前庭系统最先感知，出现眼前发黑、发飘、站立不稳。After standing quickly from a long squat or sit, darkening vision and dizziness usually reflect a brief drop in brain perfusion at the moment of posture change. On standing, gravity pools about 300 to 800 milliliters of blood in the leg and abdominal veins, briefly reducing venous return. Healthy people compensate through the baroreflex: sympathetic activation raises heart rate and constricts leg vessels, stabilizing blood pressure within seconds. When this compensation falls short and systolic pressure drops too far, cerebral supply briefly declines; the retina and vestibular system notice first, producing dark vision, floating sensation and unsteadiness.[12]

体位性低血压有明确的判定标准。医学上把站立后 3 分钟内收缩压下降 ≥20 mmHg，或舒张压下降 ≥10 mmHg，定义为体位性低血压；测量需先卧位数分钟，再在站立后 1 分钟、3 分钟分别复测。常见诱因包括脱水、出汗过多、进食后血液分流、长时间卧床、高龄，以及某些降压或镇静药物的影响；机制上涉及自主神经反射、血容量和心血管功能三方面。Orthostatic hypotension has a defined diagnostic threshold. Clinically it is defined as a fall of at least 20 mmHg in systolic pressure or 10 mmHg in diastolic pressure within three minutes of standing. Measurements require lying down for several minutes first, then repeating one and three minutes after standing. Common triggers include dehydration, heavy sweating, postprandial blood diversion, prolonged bed rest, older age and the effects of certain blood-pressure or sedating medications; the mechanism spans autonomic reflexes, blood volume and cardiovascular function.[12][13]

眼前发黑而不是全身不适，与供血次序有关。眼动脉是颈内动脉的细小分支，血压下降时视网膜供血最先受影响，表现为一过性视物发暗；内耳前庭和小脑对灌注也敏感，可出现旋转感、失衡。多数人在站稳几秒后反射完成、血压回升，症状自行缓解；若代偿失败、脑灌注进一步下降，可能发生晕厥。MedlinePlus 与 NHS 的头晕资料都把起身时头晕列为体位变化相关的常见表现。Dark vision rather than generalized discomfort relates to the order of perfusion. The ophthalmic artery is a small branch of the internal carotid, so retinal supply is affected first when pressure falls, producing transient dim vision; the inner ear vestibule and cerebellum are also perfusion-sensitive, which can add spinning or imbalance. Most people recover within a few seconds after steadying up, as the reflex completes and pressure returns; if compensation fails and perfusion drops further, syncope may follow. MedlinePlus and NHS dizziness guidance both list dizziness on standing as a common posture-related symptom.[13][14]

它需要和前庭性头晕区分开。起身发黑属于血压与灌注问题，多在改变体位时出现、平卧后缓解；旋转性眩晕来自内耳前庭或中枢通路，常伴视物旋转、眼球震颤，可持续数分钟到数小时。两者都被患者统称为头晕，机制并不相同。反复发作、黑朦持续不缓解，或伴胸痛、心悸、言语不清、肢体无力，需要临床评估排查心血管和神经系统原因。It must be separated from vestibular dizziness. Standing blackout is a blood-pressure and perfusion problem, appearing with posture change and easing when lying flat; rotational vertigo arises from the inner ear vestibule or central pathways, often with spinning vision and nystagmus lasting minutes to hours. Patients call both dizziness, but their mechanisms differ. Recurrent episodes, blackout that does not clear, or associated chest pain, palpitations, slurred speech or limb weakness need clinical assessment to rule out cardiovascular or neurological causes.[12][14]

本章只解释体位变化时的血压调节机制，不给出用药或升压方案。脱水、长期卧床后起身、餐后头晕都有相应诱因；起身放慢动作属于一般物理常识，但反复发作必须就医查明原因，不能用体虚笼统解释。老年人跌倒风险高，反复直立性头晕应纳入系统评估。This chapter explains blood-pressure regulation during posture change only and gives no medication or pressure-raising plan. Dehydration, getting up after prolonged bed rest and postprandial dizziness each have their own triggers; rising slowly is ordinary physical common sense, but repeated episodes must be investigated clinically rather than dismissed as weakness. Older adults carry a high fall risk, and recurrent orthostatic dizziness deserves a systematic evaluation.[13][14]

08为什么头晕时会觉得恶心、想吐？Why does dizziness bring nausea and the urge to vomit?

前庭与视觉输入冲突激活前庭—迷走反射，呕吐中枢兴奋，产生恶心、出汗与呕吐。Conflict between vestibular and visual input activates the vestibulovagal reflex; the vomiting center drives nausea, sweating and vomiting.

眩晕伴恶心呕吐，是前庭系统与自主神经之间的反射通路被激活。内耳前庭包括半规管和耳石器，感知头部转动和体位变化，信号经前庭神经传到脑干前庭核；当前庭输入与视觉、本体感觉传入不一致，例如坐车时看车内而窗外景物快速后退，脑干把这种冲突解读为感觉紊乱，随即激活邻近的迷走神经和呕吐中枢，产生恶心、面色苍白、出冷汗、流涎，严重时呕吐。Vertigo with nausea and vomiting comes from activating the reflex pathway between the vestibular system and the autonomic nerves. The inner ear vestibule, including semicircular canals and otolith organs, senses head rotation and posture; its signal travels via the vestibular nerve to the vestibular nuclei in the brainstem. When vestibular input conflicts with visual and proprioceptive input, for example watching the car interior while scenery rushes backward outside, the brainstem reads the mismatch as sensory disturbance and activates nearby vagal fibers and the vomiting center, producing nausea, pallor, cold sweating and salivation, with vomiting in severe cases.[14]

冲突为什么会触发呕吐，通常用进化上的防御反应解释：感觉紊乱常被系统误判为中毒，于是启动呕吐以排除毒物。前庭—迷走反射直接连接脑干前庭核与延髓呕吐中枢，不经过高级皮层判断，所以眩晕来得突然，恶心难以靠意志压住。晕车、晕船，以及耳石脱落导致的良性阵发性位置性眩晕，都可沿这条通路引起恶心；症状持续时间与病因相关，位置性眩晕常持续数秒到数分钟。Why conflict triggers vomiting is usually explained as an evolved defense reaction: sensory mismatch is often misread as poisoning, so vomiting starts to expel the suspected toxin. The vestibulovagal reflex links the brainstem vestibular nuclei directly to the medullary vomiting center without cortical judgment, which is why vertigo arrives suddenly and nausea cannot be suppressed by willpower. Motion sickness, seasickness and benign paroxysmal positional vertigo from loose otoconia can all cause nausea along this pathway; duration depends on the cause, and positional vertigo often lasts seconds to minutes.[13][14]

伴随的自主神经表现不止恶心呕吐。还可能出现心率减慢、血压波动、出汗、面色苍白，这些都是迷走神经兴奋的表现。反复呕吐会带来脱水和电解质丢失，因此眩晕发作时的体液状态需要关注。MedlinePlus 头晕与眩晕资料、NHS 头晕指南均指出，旋转感伴恶心呕吐是前庭源性症状的典型组合，同时也需要与中枢性问题鉴别。The accompanying autonomic effects go beyond nausea and vomiting. Heart rate may slow, blood pressure fluctuate, sweating and pallor appear; all reflect vagal activation. Repeated vomiting causes dehydration and electrolyte loss, so fluid status during a vertigo episode deserves attention. Both MedlinePlus dizziness and vertigo guidance and NHS dizziness guidance note that spinning sensation with nausea and vomiting is the typical vestibular pattern, while central causes must also be distinguished.[13][14]

外周与中枢问题可以通过线索区分。外周前庭问题，即内耳或前庭神经病变，通常眩晕程度重，伴耳鸣或听力变化，发作与头位相关；中枢性问题累及脑干、小脑，可伴复视、言语含糊、肢体无力、走路偏斜。出现这些红旗信号时应尽快就医。本章不讨论止吐或前庭康复的具体方案，只解释恶心如何由前庭反射产生。Peripheral and central problems can be told apart by clues. Peripheral vestibular disease, affecting the inner ear or vestibular nerve, usually causes intense vertigo with tinnitus or hearing change, tied to head position; central disease affecting the brainstem or cerebellum may add double vision, slurred speech, limb weakness or veering gait. These red flags call for prompt medical care. This chapter does not discuss antiemetic drugs or vestibular rehabilitation plans; it only explains how nausea arises from the vestibular reflex.[12][14]

偶发、与明确头位或颠簸相关的短暂恶心眩晕，多为良性过程；若眩晕持续不缓解、反复发作、伴随听力下降或神经系统体征，或首次出现且年龄偏大，需要耳鼻喉科或神经内科评估。恶心本身是症状，病因要结合眩晕性质、持续时间和伴随表现判断，不能只凭想吐就下结论。Brief nausea and vertigo tied to a clear head position or bumpy ride are usually benign, but persistent or recurrent vertigo, hearing loss, neurological signs, or a first episode at an older age warrant ENT or neurology assessment. Nausea itself is a symptom; the cause must be judged from the character and duration of vertigo plus accompanying signs, and the urge to vomit alone cannot establish a diagnosis.[12][13]

09头痛有哪几条产生通路？为什么睡眠不足和感冒都会头痛？Through which pathways does headache arise, and why do sleep loss and colds both trigger it?

头痛沿血管、肌肉、神经三条通路产生，睡眠不足与感冒通过不同环节放大痛觉。Headache arises through vascular, muscular and neural pathways; sleep loss and a cold amplify pain at different links.

头痛不是单一来源。头皮、肌肉、血管和脑膜都有痛觉结构，而脑组织本身几乎没有痛觉神经。常见头痛可以沿三条通路理解：血管通路、肌肉通路和神经通路。睡眠不足、感冒、紧张、脱水等诱因通过不同通路使痛觉信号被放大，疼痛常集中在额、颞、枕部或全头，主观感受相似但机制不同。Headache does not come from a single source. Scalp, muscles, vessels and meninges all carry pain-sensitive structures, while brain tissue itself has almost no pain fibers. Common headache can be understood along three pathways: vascular, muscular and neural. Triggers such as sleep loss, a cold, tension and dehydration amplify pain signals through different routes; pain often concentrates in the forehead, temples, occiput or across the whole head, feeling similar subjectively despite different mechanisms.[15]

血管与炎症通路是第一条。脑膜血管和颅内外血管壁分布痛觉感受器；感冒、发热时炎症介质使血管扩张、通透性改变，牵拉和刺激周围结构，可产生搏动性胀痛。偏头痛属于原发性头痛，发作通常持续 4 到 72 小时，常伴畏光、畏声或恶心；其机制涉及三叉神经血管系统的激活，并不只是血管扩张这么简单。MedlinePlus 与 StatPearls 急性头痛资料按症状和持续时间区分不同头痛类型。The first pathway is vascular and inflammatory. Pain receptors line meningeal vessels and intracranial and extracranial vessel walls; during colds and fever, inflammatory mediators dilate vessels and increase permeability, stretching and stimulating surrounding structures to produce throbbing ache. Migraine is a primary headache usually lasting 4 to 72 hours, often with light or sound sensitivity and nausea; its mechanism involves activation of the trigeminovascular system and is not simply vessel dilation. MedlinePlus and StatPearls acute headache references classify headache types by symptoms and duration.[15][17]

肌肉紧张通路是第二条。长时间低头、伏案、肩颈姿势不良时，头皮、颈枕部和颞肌持续收缩，局部血流和代谢产物堆积，筋膜和肌肉内的伤害感受器被激活，形成紧箍样、压迫感疼痛；午后加重常见于一天姿势积累之后。睡眠不足降低痛觉阈值，使原有轻度不适更容易被感知为疼痛。这类紧张型头痛多为双侧压迫感，持续数十分钟到数天不等。The second pathway is muscular tension. During prolonged looking down, desk work and poor shoulder-neck posture, scalp, occipital and temporal muscles contract continuously; local blood flow and metabolites accumulate, and nociceptors in fascia and muscle are activated, producing a band-like pressure pain. Worsening in the afternoon is common after a day of posture accumulation. Sleep loss lowers the pain threshold, making mild discomfort easier to perceive as headache. Such tension-type headache is usually bilateral and pressure-like, lasting tens of minutes to days.[16][17]

神经通路负责痛觉传入与放大。三叉神经和颈神经把头面部痛觉传入脑干和丘脑，再到皮层；反复刺激可使痛觉通路敏化，痛阈下降，轻触也觉得痛。感冒时鼻窦黏膜肿胀、分泌物压力刺激，也可引起面部和前额的牵涉痛。NHS 头痛资料把突发剧烈头痛、伴随神经症状、发热伴颈强直等列为需要紧急评估的信号。The neural pathway carries and amplifies pain. The trigeminal and cervical nerves transmit head and face pain to the brainstem and thalamus, then to cortex; repeated stimulation sensitizes the pain pathway and lowers the threshold so light touch hurts. During a cold, swollen sinus mucosa and secretion pressure can also produce referred pain in the face and forehead. NHS headache guidance lists sudden severe headache, accompanying neurological symptoms, and fever with neck stiffness as signals needing urgent assessment.[15][16]

本章只梳理三条机制通路，不给出止痛用药方案，也不判断个体头痛属于哪一型。偶发、短期、与疲劳或感冒相关的头痛多为良性；若头痛首次剧烈发作、进行性加重、伴意识改变、肢体无力、视物不清、发热颈项强直，或外伤后出现，应尽快就医评估。头痛原因从肌肉紧张到颅内病变跨度很大，机制解释不能替代诊断。This chapter outlines three mechanism pathways only, gives no analgesic medication plan, and does not classify an individual headache. Occasional, short-lived headache tied to fatigue or a cold is usually benign, but a first severe attack, progressive worsening, altered consciousness, limb weakness, blurred vision, fever with stiff neck, or headache after trauma should be assessed promptly. Headache causes range from muscle tension to intracranial disease, and a mechanism account cannot replace diagnosis.[16][17]

10为什么感冒会流鼻涕，咳嗽为什么夜里更厉害？Why does a cold cause a runny nose, and why does cough worsen at night?

平卧后鼻后滴漏刺激气道，夜间迷走张力升高、黏液积聚，咳嗽反射被反复触发。Lying down lets postnasal drip irritate the airway; nocturnal vagal tone and mucus buildup repeatedly trigger cough.

感冒时流鼻涕和咳嗽，是上呼吸道黏膜受刺激后的防御反应。病毒感染后鼻黏膜充血、水肿，杯状细胞和腺体分泌增加，先流出清亮稀薄的分泌物，随后可含炎症细胞和脱落上皮，变得黏稠；鼻腔分泌物量多时向后流到咽喉，即鼻后滴漏，持续刺激咽部和气道，触发咳嗽。MedlinePlus 普通感冒资料把鼻塞流涕、咳嗽列为上呼吸道病毒感染的常见症状。A runny nose and cough during a cold are defense responses of irritated upper-airway mucosa. After viral infection the nasal mucosa congests and swells, and goblet cells and glands increase secretion; thin clear discharge appears first, later thickening with inflammatory cells and shed epithelium. When nasal discharge is heavy it flows backward into the throat, known as postnasal drip, which continuously irritates the pharynx and airway and triggers cough. MedlinePlus common cold guidance lists nasal congestion, discharge and cough as common upper-respiratory viral symptoms.[18]

咳嗽夜里更明显，与平卧姿势和夜间神经状态有关。平卧时头部与躯干同高，分泌物不再靠重力向前流出，而是沿后鼻道流到咽喉和气道，咳嗽反射被持续触发；夜间迷走神经张力偏高，气道平滑肌敏感性增高，咳嗽反射阈值下降；睡眠中气道黏液排出变慢，积聚物刺激感受器。这些因素叠加，使平卧后数小时和清晨起床时咳嗽更频繁。Cough becomes more obvious at night because of lying posture and nighttime nerve state. When lying flat the head and trunk are level, so discharge no longer drains forward by gravity; it runs along the posterior nasal passages into the throat and airway, keeping the cough reflex triggered. Nocturnal vagal tone is higher, airway smooth muscle is more sensitive, and the cough reflex threshold drops; mucus clearance slows during sleep, and pooled material stimulates receptors. Together these make cough more frequent in the hours after lying down and on waking in the morning.[18][20]

气道在病毒感染后会短暂处于高敏感状态。上皮短暂受损，感觉神经末梢暴露，对冷空气、干燥空气、烟雾和气味反应增强；感冒后咳嗽可持续数周，属于气道高反应期。咳嗽本身是清除分泌物和异物的反射：气流在声门关闭后猛地冲出，帮助把黏液排出。夜间咳嗽程度因人而异，也受室内湿度、尘螨和平卧姿势影响。The airway stays briefly hypersensitive after a viral infection. The epithelium is temporarily damaged, sensory nerve endings are exposed, and reactions to cold dry air, smoke and odors are amplified; post-viral cough can last weeks during this airway hyperreactivity phase. Cough itself is a reflex for clearing secretions and foreign material: after the glottis closes, a sharp burst of airflow helps expel mucus. Nighttime cough varies individually and is also affected by indoor humidity, dust mites and sleeping posture.[18][20]

鼻涕与咳嗽通过连续通道相连。鼻腔、咽喉、气道是连续的通道，鼻后滴漏是慢性夜间咳嗽的常见原因之一；分泌物从清稀到黏稠反映炎症阶段，但单凭鼻涕颜色不能确定是病毒还是细菌感染。NHS 咳嗽资料与 MedlinePlus 咳嗽条目都把咳嗽按持续时间和伴随症状分类，用于区分普通自限性过程与需要评估的情况。Nasal discharge and cough are linked through a continuous tract. Nose, pharynx and airway form one continuous channel, and postnasal drip is one common cause of chronic nighttime cough; the shift from thin to thick secretion reflects the inflammatory stage, but discharge color alone cannot distinguish viral from bacterial infection. NHS cough guidance and the MedlinePlus cough entry classify cough by duration and accompanying symptoms to separate ordinary self-limiting illness from cases needing assessment.[19][20]

本章只解释流涕、鼻后滴漏和夜间咳嗽的机制，不给出镇咳、通鼻或抗感染方案。普通感冒相关咳嗽通常在数周内逐步减轻；若咳嗽伴呼吸困难、胸痛、咯血、持续高热、喘息，或咳嗽超过数周不缓解、夜间憋醒，应就医评估。婴幼儿、孕妇和基础肺病患者的症状阈值更低，出现加重应尽早面诊。This chapter explains the mechanisms of discharge, postnasal drip and nighttime cough only, and gives no antitussive, decongestant or anti-infective plan. Cough related to an ordinary cold usually eases over weeks, but breathlessness, chest pain, hemoptysis, persistent high fever, wheezing, a cough lasting beyond several weeks, or waking with choking needs medical assessment. Infants, pregnant people and those with underlying lung disease have a lower symptom threshold and should be seen early if symptoms worsen.[18][19]
