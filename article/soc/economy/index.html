01什么是从众消费，它为什么会发生，他人选择在其中起什么作用？What is conformist consumption, why does it occur, and what role do others' choices play?

信息不足时他人选择被当作质量信号，信息性影响与归属需要共同驱动跟随。Others' choices act as quality cues under uncertainty; informational influence and belonging needs jointly drive following.

从众消费指个体在不确定商品好坏时，参照他人的选择来决定自己买什么：排队越长、评价越多、同款被推荐得越广，个体越倾向于认为商品值得购买。它不是单纯的虚荣心，而是一种社会信息加工：在信息不足时，他人行为被当作质量信号，跟随多数人可以降低判断成本，但也可能把信号放大成羊群式抢购。Conformist consumption means that when people are unsure whether a product is good, they use others' choices as a decision signal: longer queues, more reviews and broader recommendation all raise the perceived value. It is not simply vanity but social information processing: under uncertainty, others' behavior is read as a quality cue, and following the majority lowers judgment cost, though it can also amplify the cue into a herd-style rush.[3]

机制上，社会证明假设认为人们会观察周围人的行为来确定何为恰当选择；当个体缺乏直接经验时，他人的选择提供了关于产品、价格和风险的间接线索。信息性社会影响偏向修正自己的判断，规范性社会影响则出于归属和避免排斥而跟随群体，两者常同时作用。消费场景中的热销标签、销量排名和限时库存提示，都是在放大这种从众线索。The social proof hypothesis holds that people watch what others do to decide what seems appropriate. Lacking direct experience, others' choices provide indirect clues about product, price and risk. Informational social influence tends to revise one's own judgment, while normative influence follows the group for belonging and to avoid rejection; the two often act together. Best-seller tags, sales rankings and limited-stock alerts all amplify these conformity cues in consumption settings.[1]

经典实验提供了效应方向的证据。阿希线段判断实验中，多数被试在同伙一致给出错误答案时，有相当比例跟随说出错误线段长度；后续变式显示，只要有一个同伙给出正确答案，从众率就明显下降。市场实验与观测研究也发现，评分、下载量和推荐数量会提高后续选择概率，但效应随信息源可信度和时间跨度变化，并非所有人都会被带动。Classic experiments show the direction of the effect. In the Asch line-judgment study, a notable proportion of participants repeated an obviously wrong line length when confederates answered wrong unanimously; variants showed that a single correct dissenting answer sharply reduced conformity. Market studies likewise find that ratings, downloads and recommendations raise later choice probability, but the effect varies with source credibility and time span, so not everyone is pulled along.[5]

边界在于：从众不是盲目的统一行动。个体对产品的既有知识、价格敏感度和风险态度会调节跟随强度；当群体选择与自身偏好冲突时，部分人会坚持己见。社会证明在信息模糊、商品体验难以预先判断时最强，对耐用品、复杂服务的影响通常弱于快消和时尚商品。抢购情绪还可能由稀缺提示和情绪传染叠加造成，不能全部归因于理性判断。Conformity is not blind uniform action. Prior product knowledge, price sensitivity and risk attitude moderate how strongly someone follows; when group choice conflicts with personal preference, some people hold their own view. Social proof is strongest when information is vague and experience cannot be judged in advance, and it usually weighs less for durable goods and complex services than for fast-moving consumer goods and fashion. Rush buying can also stack scarcity cues and emotional contagion, so it cannot be credited entirely to rational judgment.[3][5]

把从众放在经济行为框架下看，它连接了个体决策与群体互动：购买决策不是孤立的偏好计算，而是在他人信号、归属需求和信息成本之间权衡。下一章讨论与从众相对的另一面——人类为什么能在陌生人之间形成合作，以及集体行动如何克服搭便车。Framed as economic behavior, conformity links individual choice to group interaction: a purchase is not an isolated preference calculation but a trade-off among others' signals, belonging needs and information costs. The next chapter turns to the counterpart of conformity: why cooperation emerges among strangers, and how collective action overcomes free riding.[1][5]

02人类的合作行为是如何演化出来的，普通人又怎样通过集体行动改变社会？How did human cooperation evolve, and how can ordinary people change society through collective action?

亲缘、互惠、声誉与规范惩罚互补维持合作；集体行动靠分工、激励和参与阈值放大。Kinship, reciprocity, reputation and norms-punishment sustain cooperation; collective action scales through division, incentives and participation thresholds.

人类合作之所以能在陌生人之间扩展，是因为演化和制度发展出了几套相互补充的机制：亲缘选择帮助亲属间互助，互惠规范让单次交换可以延迟结算，声誉机制把一个人的合作记录传递给未来的交易对象，群体层面的规范与惩罚则约束搭便车者。集体行动能否成规模，取决于这些机制能否降低协调成本、让合作者得到回报。Human cooperation expands among strangers because evolution and institutions built several complementary mechanisms: kin selection supports mutual aid among relatives, reciprocity lets exchanges settle later, reputation carries a person's cooperation record to future partners, and group norms and punishment constrain free riders. Whether collective action scales depends on whether these mechanisms lower coordination costs and reward cooperators.[5]

亲缘选择按遗传相关度解释利他：帮助亲属提高共同基因的传递概率；互惠则把合作建立在未来回报的预期上，直接互惠发生在重复相遇的双方之间，间接互惠借助第三方评价传播声誉。公共品博弈实验中，参与者向公共账户出资，收益按人均分；若没有惩罚或声誉反馈，出资通常随轮次下降，而引入同伴惩罚或公开声誉后，合作水平可以维持在较高区间。Kin selection explains altruism by genetic relatedness: helping relatives raises the chance shared genes are passed on. Reciprocity rests on expected future return: direct reciprocity between parties who meet repeatedly, indirect reciprocity spreading reputation through third-party evaluation. In public-goods experiments, participants contribute to a shared account whose payoff is divided equally; without punishment or reputation feedback, contributions usually fall over rounds, while peer punishment or open reputations keep cooperation higher.[3]

集体行动还面临搭便车问题：个体可以享受公共品收益而不承担成本，若多数人这样做，公共品就供给不足。现实中，组织化的分工、可识别的贡献、选择性激励和领导者协调会提高参与率；社会运动和公共倡议往往需要达到一定参与阈值，早期参与者打破沉默后，后续加入的心理成本下降。普通人改变社会的路径，通常是先在小群体中形成共识，再通过组织、媒体和制度渠道放大影响。Collective action also faces free riding: an individual can enjoy public-good benefits without bearing cost, and if most do so the good is underprovided. In practice, organized division of labor, identifiable contributions, selective incentives and leader coordination raise participation. Social movements and public initiatives often need a participation threshold; after early participants break silence, the psychological cost of joining later drops. Ordinary people typically change society first by building consensus in small groups, then amplifying through organizations, media and institutional channels.[1]

证据来自博弈实验、田野调查和组织研究：重复博弈提高合作率，惩罚违规者的制度在跨文化样本中普遍出现；社区供水、森林管理和志愿组织的案例显示，清晰规则、本地参与和可监督的贡献与持续合作相关。声誉评分、公开贡献记录和可追溯的合作历史，在在线平台和现实组织中都被用来维持互惠。Evidence comes from game experiments, field studies and organizational research: repeated play raises cooperation, and institutions punishing violators appear across cultural samples. Cases of community water supply, forest management and volunteer groups link clear rules, local participation and monitorable contributions to sustained cooperation. Reputation scores, public contribution records and traceable cooperation histories maintain reciprocity on online platforms and real organizations alike.[1][5]

边界：合作机制不是万能的。亲缘范围有限，陌生人之间的互惠依赖重复接触和信息透明；惩罚若不被视为公平，可能引发报复和对立。集体行动受群体规模、异质性和资源约束影响，规模越大协调成本越高；把成功合作简单归因于道德水平，会忽略制度设计、激励结构和沟通条件。下一章进入收入与财富差距，讨论用什么口径度量不平等，以及不同统计口径会怎样改变结论。Cooperation mechanisms are not universal. Kinship reaches only so far, and stranger reciprocity relies on repeated contact and transparent information; punishment perceived as unfair can trigger retaliation and opposition. Collective action is shaped by group size, heterogeneity and resource constraints: coordination cost rises with scale, and attributing successful cooperation simply to moral level ignores institutional design, incentive structure and communication conditions. The next chapter turns to income and wealth gaps, asking how inequality is measured and how statistical definitions change the conclusion.[1][3]

03为什么有的人穷、有的人富，基尼系数和购买力平价分别怎样度量差距？Why are some people poor and others rich, and how do Gini and PPP measure the gap?

基尼按洛伦兹曲线面积概括整体不平等；PPP 按当地物价换算实际购买力，口径不同结论会变。Gini summarizes overall inequality from Lorenz area; PPP converts real purchasing power by local prices, and definitions shift conclusions.

有的人收入高、有的人收入低，差距来自人力资本、技能需求、资本回报、行业结构和机会分配等多重因素；度量差距最常用的指标是基尼系数，它把洛伦兹曲线与绝对平等线之间的面积换算成 0 到 1 之间的数值，0 表示完全平等，1 表示集中在一人。比较国家或地区时，还必须区分收入口径、财富口径，以及名义汇率和购买力平价两种价格调整方式。Income and wealth gaps arise from human capital, skill demand, returns to capital, industry structure and opportunity allocation. The most common summary measure is the Gini index, which converts the area between the Lorenz curve and the absolute-equality line into a value from 0 to 1: 0 means full equality, 1 means concentration in one person. Comparing regions also requires separating income from wealth, and nominal exchange rates from purchasing-power adjusted figures.[1]

基尼系数按税后可支配收入或初次分配收入计算，结果会明显不同；财富差距通常大于收入差距，因为房产、企业股权和金融资产会在代际间累积。购买力平价（PPP）按一篮子商品和服务在当地的价格换算，消除不同国家物价水平差异；用名义汇率比较时，低收入国家的生活成本优势被低估，实际消费水平的差距看起来会更大。Gini computed on disposable post-tax income differs markedly from one computed on primary income; wealth inequality is usually larger than income inequality because housing, business equity and financial assets accumulate across generations. Purchasing power parity converts by a local basket of goods and services, removing cross-country price-level differences; under nominal exchange rates, low-income countries' cost-of-living advantage is understated, so the real consumption gap looks larger.[2]

世界银行等机构的面板数据显示，各国基尼系数分布在较宽区间：部分北欧国家可支配收入基尼在 0.25 至 0.30 附近，部分高不平等经济体在 0.45 以上。长期看，全球化提高了部分国家低收入群体的实际收入，同时扩大了技能和资本拥有者之间的差距；技术变革对高技能劳动和资本收益的回报更高，这一效应在不同制度下被税收、转移支付和教育政策部分抵消。World Bank panel data place national Gini values across a wide range: some Nordic countries sit near 0.25 to 0.30 on disposable income, while several high-inequality economies exceed 0.45. Over the long run, globalization raised real income for low-income groups in some countries while widening gaps between skilled workers and capital owners; technological change rewards high skills and capital more, an effect partly offset by taxes, transfers and education policy under different institutions.[3]

证据来自住户调查、税务记录和跨国统计：家庭收支调查给出收入分布，税务数据补充高收入尾部，资产负债调查反映财富累积。基尼系数可以在同一口径下跨年、跨地区比较，但它只概括整体不平等程度，不显示差距发生在哪个群体、是代内还是代际传递；中位数收入和最富/最贫分位份额需要与系数配合阅读。Evidence comes from household surveys, tax records and cross-national statistics: budget surveys give the income distribution, tax data fill the high-income tail, and balance-sheet surveys track wealth accumulation. Gini allows year-to-year and regional comparison within one definition, but it summarizes overall inequality only and does not show where the gap lies, whether within or across generations; median income and top-to-bottom decile shares must be read alongside the coefficient.[1][3]

边界：基尼系数上升或下降不直接说明福利变好或变坏，还要看绝对收入水平、流动性和公共服务。PPP 调整适合比较实际购买力，但对服务质量、公共品供给和非市场收入反映有限；财富差距受资产价格波动影响，单一年份数据可能放大短期变化。把贫富归因于单一因素，会忽略教育机会、制度环境、产业周期和运气的叠加作用。下一章讨论人口规模：地球能负担多少人，生育率为什么随收入和教育变化。A rising or falling Gini does not by itself show whether welfare improved; absolute income, mobility and public services matter as well. PPP suits real purchasing-power comparison but reflects service quality, public goods and non-market income only weakly; wealth gaps swing with asset prices, and a single year can exaggerate short-term change. Attributing wealth or poverty to one factor ignores the stacking of educational opportunity, institutions, industry cycles and luck. The next chapter turns to population: how many people the Earth can support and why fertility changes with income and education.[2][3]

04地球到底能负担多少人口，为什么许多社会的人选择少生孩子？How many people can the Earth support, and why do many societies choose fewer births?

承载能力随技术、消费与分配变化；人口转型中教育、就业和养育成本推动生育率下降。Carrying capacity moves with technology, consumption and distribution; transition pushes fertility down through education, employment and rearing cost.

地球能负担多少人口，没有一个固定数字：承载能力取决于粮食产量、水资源、能源供给、技术效率、消费结构和废弃物处理能力，也随生活水平和分配方式变化。人口增长模型通常结合生育率、死亡率、迁移和年龄结构做预测；联合国人口司的中位预测给出未来几十年的区间估计，而不是单一确定上限。How many people the Earth can support has no fixed number: carrying capacity depends on food output, water, energy, technical efficiency, consumption patterns and waste processing, as well as living standards and distribution. Population models combine fertility, mortality, migration and age structure for forecasts; the UN Population Division's medium variant gives a range estimate for coming decades, not one certain ceiling.[4]

人口转型描述生育率和死亡率随发展水平变化的典型路径：早期高出生、高死亡，人口增长缓慢；医疗和粮食改善后死亡率先下降，人口快速膨胀；随后教育、城市化、女性就业和养育成本上升使生育率下降，逐步进入低出生、低死亡阶段。转型速度在不同地区差异很大，政策、文化和经济预期都会影响生育决策的时间和幅度。Demographic transition describes the typical path of fertility and mortality with development: early high birth and high death with slow growth; after medical and food improvements mortality falls first and population expands quickly; later education, urbanization, female employment and child-rearing costs push fertility down toward low birth and low death. Transition speed varies widely across regions, and policy, culture and economic expectations shape its timing and scale.[5]

生育率随收入和教育变化的机制包括：子女从家庭劳动力转变为需要长期投入的人力资本，养育成本上升；儿童死亡率下降后，家庭不必靠多生育对冲夭折风险；女性受教育程度提高、劳动参与率上升，生育的机会成本提高；避孕手段可得性改善使生育时间可以自主安排。收入提高在短期可能降低子女的经济贡献预期，长期与较低生育率相伴出现。Fertility moves with income and education through several mechanisms: children shift from family labor to long-term human capital investment, raising rearing cost; as child mortality falls, families need fewer births to offset death risk; higher female education and labor participation raise the opportunity cost of childbearing; accessible contraception lets couples time births deliberately. Rising income may lower the expected economic contribution of children in the short run and associates with lower fertility in the long run.[3]

证据来自人口普查、抽样调查和跨国面板：总和生育率、预期寿命和城镇化率在转型过程中呈系统性联动；联合国估计与预测按年龄结构和生育假设逐年更新。历史上农业改良、灌溉和化肥提高了土地承载人口的能力，但同时改变了饮食结构、能耗和环境足迹，承载上限本身也随技术和消费模式移动。Censuses, surveys and cross-national panels support this: total fertility, life expectancy and urbanization move together through transition; UN estimates and projections update yearly with age structure and fertility assumptions. Historically, agricultural improvement, irrigation and fertilizer raised how many people land could feed, while also changing diets, energy use and environmental footprint, so the ceiling itself moves with technology and consumption.[3][4]

边界：承载能力不是静态常数，技术进步可以提高供给，奢侈消费和不平等分配则会压低有效承载；预测依赖生育、死亡和迁移假设，经济冲击和政策变化会使实际路径偏离中位估计。把人口压力简单等同于生育多少人，会忽略人均资源消耗、技术效率和分配制度；下一章讨论需要集体供给的公共品，以疫苗接种和群体免疫为例说明外部性如何影响个人选择。Carrying capacity is not a static constant: technology can raise supply, while lavish consumption and unequal distribution lower effective capacity. Forecasts rest on fertility, mortality and migration assumptions, and shocks or policy change can push the actual path away from the medium variant. Treating population pressure as a simple birth count ignores per-capita resource use, technical efficiency and distribution institutions. The final chapter turns to collective public goods, using vaccination and herd immunity to show how externalities shape individual choices.[3][5]

05疫苗接种率与群体免疫之间是什么关系，公共品外部性怎样影响个人选择？How does vaccination coverage relate to herd immunity, and how does the public-good externality shape personal choice?

接种产生外部性，免疫比例达到约 1−1/R0 后传播链难以维持；高覆盖保护无法接种的高危人群。Vaccination creates an externality; immune coverage near 1−1/R0 breaks transmission chains, and high coverage protects those who cannot be inoculated.

疫苗接种是典型的公共品问题：个人接种既保护自己，也降低病原体在社区内传播的机会，未接种者从他人接种中获得间接保护，这种收益就是接种的外部性。群体免疫指人群中足够多的人获得免疫后，传播链难以维持，易感者即使未接种也受到间接保护；所需接种阈值与病原体的基本传染数 R0 直接相关。Vaccination is a classic public-good problem: inoculation protects the individual while lowering the chance the pathogen spreads within the community, and non-vaccinated people gain indirect protection from others' uptake; that benefit is the vaccination externality. Herd immunity means that once enough people in a population are immune, transmission chains cannot sustain themselves, and susceptible people are indirectly protected even without being vaccinated. The required coverage threshold links directly to the basic reproduction number R0.[5]

阈值可由感染传播模型估算：R0 表示一个感染者在完全易感人群中平均传染的人数，阻断持续传播所需的免疫比例约为 1 - 1/R0。R0 越高，需要免疫覆盖的比例越高；例如麻疹 R0 较高，阈值通常在很高的接种覆盖率附近，流感等传播力较弱的病原体阈值相应较低。接种率下降时，易感者比例回升，聚集性疫情和局部暴发的风险随之增加。The threshold can be estimated from transmission models: R0 is the average number of people one infects in a fully susceptible population, and the immune fraction needed to stop sustained spread is about 1 minus 1 over R0. The higher R0, the higher the required immune coverage; measles, with high R0, needs coverage near a high threshold, while weaker-spreading agents such as influenza have a lower threshold. When vaccination falls, the susceptible fraction rises, and clusters and local outbreaks become more likely.[4]

公共品供给常面临搭便车：部分人希望享受群体免疫带来的安全，而自己不承担接种的成本和轻微不适。现实中，免疫规划通过免费或补贴接种、学校入园接种要求、医生建议和社区沟通提高覆盖率；对免疫受损、无法接种的人群，社区高接种率是他们的主要保护来源。接种记录、覆盖率监测和疫情预警让公共卫生部门可以及时识别覆盖率薄弱区域。Public-good supply often invites free riding: some people hope to enjoy herd safety without bearing the cost and mild discomfort of inoculation. In practice, immunization programs raise coverage through free or subsidized vaccination, school entry requirements, clinician advice and community communication. For immunocompromised people who cannot be vaccinated, high community coverage is the main source of protection; records, coverage monitoring and outbreak alerts let public health teams spot weak areas in time.[1]

证据来自流行病学观察和疫苗试验：接种覆盖率提高与相应疾病发病率下降在跨国和社区数据中反复出现；打破免疫屏障后，曾被控制的传染病在局部地区重新出现。模型对阈值的估计随传染力、接触模式和疫苗有效性调整，研究同时比较不同接种策略对重症和死亡的保护效果。Evidence comes from epidemiological observation and vaccine trials: higher coverage repeatedly accompanies lower disease incidence across countries and communities, and when immunity breaks down, previously controlled diseases reappear locally. Model thresholds adjust for transmissibility, contact patterns and vaccine effectiveness, and studies compare how different strategies protect against severe illness and death.[1][5]

边界：群体免疫阈值不是固定数字，疫苗有效性、接种及时性、人口密度和接触网络都会改变实际保护水平；接种不能替代对高危人群的其他防护。个人选择受健康禁忌、信息环境和信任度影响，公共政策需要在保护个体意愿与维持群体覆盖之间平衡。把公共健康问题简化为个人偏好，会忽略外部性、公平可及性和集体行动的制度条件。The herd-immunity threshold is not a fixed number: vaccine effectiveness, timing, population density and contact networks all alter actual protection, and vaccination does not replace other safeguards for high-risk groups. Personal choice is shaped by medical contraindications, information environment and trust, so policy must balance individual autonomy with maintaining group coverage. Reducing public health to personal preference ignores externalities, equitable access and the institutional conditions for collective action.[1][4]

06物价为什么会普遍上涨？通货膨胀是怎样由货币、供需和预期共同造成的？Why do prices rise broadly, and how do money, supply and demand, and expectations jointly produce inflation?

CPI 度量一篮子价格总水平；长期看货币扩张，短期分需求拉动与成本推动，预期脱锚会让通胀自我实现。CPI measures the basket price level; long-run inflation tracks money, short-run inflation splits into demand-pull and cost-push, and unanchored expectations make it self-fulfilling.

物价普遍持续上涨就是通货膨胀，衡量的不是单件商品变贵，而是一篮子商品和服务的价格总水平上行。常用口径是居民消费价格指数（CPI）：固定一篮子代表性消费品和服务，按支出权重汇总各分项价格同比变化；食品能源波动大时，再看剔除二者的核心 CPI。CPI 是加权平均价格变化，单看某种菜价或油价不能判断整体通胀；名义收入不变时，CPI 上行意味着同样的钱买到的商品减少。Inflation means a broad, sustained rise in prices, not one good becoming dearer but an upward movement in the overall basket price level. The standard measure is the consumer price index (CPI): statisticians fix a representative basket of consumer goods and services and aggregate year-on-year price changes by expenditure weights. When food and energy swing widely, analysts also watch core CPI excluding them. CPI is a weighted average, so a single vegetable or fuel price cannot establish overall inflation; with nominal income fixed, rising CPI means the same money buys fewer real goods.[6]

货币层面是长期通胀背景。货币数量与产出、价格的数量关系常写成 MV=PY：货币量 M、流通速度 V、实际产出 Y、价格水平 P。若货币扩张快于实际产出增长，在流通速度大体稳定时，价格水平 P 倾向于上行；反之货币收紧、需求不足时，价格上涨放缓甚至出现通缩。短期产出和速度都会波动，货币增长不会立刻一对一变成物价；长期看持续的普遍涨价通常伴随货币总量较快扩张。Money supply sets the long-run background for inflation. The quantity relation between money, output and prices is often written MV=PY: money stock M, velocity V, real output Y and price level P. If money expands faster than real output growth and velocity stays roughly stable, the price level tends to rise; if money tightens and demand weakens, price growth slows or turns to deflation. In the short run output and velocity both fluctuate, so money growth does not translate one-to-one into prices immediately, but over the long run sustained broad price rises usually accompany faster expansion of the money stock.[6][7]

短期通胀可分成需求拉动与成本推动两类。需求拉动指总需求超过潜在产出：消费、投资、政府支出或出口同时走强，订单追不上供给，厂商提价、招工加薪，价格水平被需求推高。成本推动来自供给侧：能源、原材料、工资或进口价格上涨，企业把成本转嫁到售价上；油价、粮价冲击属于典型的成本推动。两类机制可以叠加：需求过热抬高工资，工资上涨又推高成本，形成工资—物价的交替上行。Short-run inflation splits into demand-pull and cost-push mechanisms. Demand-pull occurs when aggregate demand outruns potential output: consumption, investment, government spending or exports strengthen together, orders outrun supply, firms raise prices and bid up wages, and the price level is pushed by demand. Cost-push comes from the supply side: energy, raw materials, wages or import prices rise, and firms pass the cost into selling prices; oil and food shocks are typical cost-push cases. The two can stack: overheated demand lifts wages, higher wages push costs up, and a wage-price alternation develops.[7][8]

预期会让通胀自我实现。若家庭和企业普遍预期明年物价继续上涨，工人谈薪要求提前加薪，厂商提前调价，零售商提前备货，预期变成当下定价行为，通胀被自己维持住。预期一旦脱锚，央行需要更高利率或更强需求收缩才能压回来；反之低且稳定的预期本身就是物价稳定的一部分。观察通胀要看环比与同比、核心与分项、预期调查和工资数据，单月波动可能来自季节或一次性冲击，不能直接当成趋势。Expectations make inflation self-fulfilling. If households and firms broadly expect prices to keep rising, workers ask for early raises, firms raise prices early, retailers stock up ahead, and the expectation becomes current pricing behavior, sustaining inflation. Once inflation expectations become unanchored, central banks need higher rates or stronger demand contraction to push expectations back down; conversely, low and stable expectations are themselves part of price stability. Reading inflation means looking at month-on-month and year-on-year rates, core and component breakdowns, expectation surveys and wage data; a single month may reflect seasonality or a one-off shock, not a trend.[7]

边界：CPI 口径随篮子构成、权重和抽样方法变化，跨国比较要先对齐口径；通胀不等于经济差，高增长期也可能有需求拉动通胀，经济走弱与物价上涨并存的滞胀则有特定供给冲击背景。各国货币制度、汇率和供给弹性不同，同样的货币扩张物价反应差别很大。通胀是价格总水平的统计现象，单件商品价格还受供需、品质和品牌影响；判断宏观通胀要回到篮子价格、货币条件和预期数据，不能用个别商品价格代替整体结论。Boundary: CPI definitions vary with basket composition, weights and sampling methods, so cross-country comparison must align definitions first. Inflation does not equal a weak economy: demand-pull inflation can appear during strong growth, while stagflation, weak growth alongside rising prices, has specific supply-shock backgrounds. Monetary regimes, exchange rates and supply elasticity differ across countries, so the same money expansion produces different price responses. Inflation concerns the overall price level; an individual good's price reflects its own supply, demand, quality and brand. Macro conclusions rest on basket prices, monetary conditions and expectations, not one product.[6][8]

07为什么经济会出现衰退？收缩是怎样被传导和放大的？Why does the economy slip into recession, and how is the downturn transmitted and amplified?

衰退是总体活动广泛下滑，经需求乘数、信用收缩与预期扩散连环传导放大。Recession is a broad decline in aggregate activity, transmitted and amplified through demand multipliers, credit contraction and spreading expectations.

衰退指总体经济活动在一段时间内显著、广泛地下滑。美国 NBER 经济周期日期委员会按深度、广度、持续 3 个标准综合判断，不只看单一指标；非正式的经验口径常用实际 GDP 连续 2 个季度负增长作为信号。衰退从顶峰之后开始，到谷底结束，之后进入扩张；周期可以被观察到，但每次的长度、深度和触发原因并不相同，不是固定间隔的机械摆动。A recession is a significant, broad decline in aggregate economic activity over a period. The U.S. NBER Business Cycle Dating Committee judges depth, diffusion and duration together rather than relying on one indicator; an informal rule of thumb uses two consecutive quarters of negative real GDP growth as a signal. A recession begins after the peak and ends at the trough, followed by expansion; cycles are observable, but their length, depth and trigger differ each time, so they are not mechanically spaced swings.[9][10]

需求侧是最常见的传导链条。消费、投资或出口任一部门走弱，企业订单下降，收入随之减少；收入减少又压低下一阶段的消费和投资，乘数效应让初始冲击被连环放大。企业预期转弱时会推迟设备投资和招聘，失业回升进一步压缩家庭支出，需求收缩在部门之间扩散，单一行业的放缓通过订单、价格和就业相互传导，局部放缓演变为总体下滑。The demand side is the most common transmission chain. When consumption, investment or exports weaken, corporate orders fall and incomes drop; lower incomes then cut the next round of consumption and investment, and the multiplier amplifies the initial shock through linked rounds. As expectations weaken, firms postpone equipment investment and hiring, rising unemployment further compresses household spending, and the demand contraction spreads across sectors until a local slowdown becomes an aggregate decline.[11]

信用与预期是放大器。信贷扩张期，企业和家庭加杠杆购买资产或扩产；信用收紧后，偿债支出挤占消费和投资，资产价格回落又削弱抵押能力。预期具有自我实现的特征：银行惜贷、企业推迟投资、家庭增加储蓄的判断彼此强化，悲观情绪在机构和家庭之间扩散，即使最初冲击不大，也可能被放大成一轮收缩。Credit and expectations act as amplifiers. During credit expansion, firms and households take on leverage to buy assets or expand production; once credit tightens, debt service crowds out consumption and investment, and falling asset prices weaken collateral. Expectations are self-fulfilling: cautious lending, postponed investment and higher household savings reinforce one another, and pessimism spreads between institutions and households, so even a modest initial shock can be magnified into a contraction.[10][11]

库存相位解释了周期中的节奏。企业按预期需求备货，实际需求不及预期时库存积压，被迫减产去库；减产带来收入和就业下降，需求进一步走弱，直到库存回到合意水平、预期重新稳定，经济才从谷底回升。扩张、过热、收缩、去库构成循环，但库存、信用和外部冲击的叠加使每一轮曲线的形状和幅度都有差别。The inventory phase explains the rhythm within the cycle. Firms stock to expected demand; when actual demand misses expectations, inventories pile up and production is cut to reduce stock. Production cuts lower income and employment, demand weakens further, and the economy returns from the trough only after inventories reach desired levels and expectations stabilize. Expansion, overheating, contraction and destocking form a loop, but the mix of inventory, credit and external shocks makes the shape and amplitude of each round different.[9][11]

边界说明。本章只解释衰退形成与传导的机制和统计口径，不提供任何投资建议；衰退成因是需求、信用、外部冲击与政策反应的叠加，不同历史案例的主因并不相同，不能用单一原因解释所有收缩。判断当前处于周期哪个相位，需要结合收入、就业、工业生产和零售等多组数据，而不是只看一个季度的 GDP 数字。The boundary: this chapter explains the mechanisms and statistical definitions of recession only and gives no investment advice. Recessions combine demand, credit, external shocks and policy responses, and the dominant cause differs across historical cases, so no single reason explains every downturn. Locating the current phase requires income, employment, industrial production and retail data together, not one quarter of GDP alone.[9][10]
