01为什么千斤顶能撬动汽车？杠杆和螺旋怎样放大力量？How does a jack lift a car, and how do levers and screws multiply force?

杠杆平衡 F₁L₁=F₂L₂，动力臂越长机械利益越大；螺旋把旋转行程换成短举升，省力不省功。Lever balance is F₁L₁ = F₂L₂; longer effort arm raises mechanical advantage, and a screw converts rotation into short lift, trading force for distance.

千斤顶能顶起汽车，靠的是杠杆和螺旋两类省力机械的组合。杠杆在支点两侧平衡时，动力乘以动力臂等于阻力乘以阻力臂，即 F₁L₁=F₂L₂；动力臂越长、阻力臂越短，机械利益越大，人用较小的力就能克服很大的阻力。修车用的剪式千斤顶和液压千斤顶把长手柄上的小力放大成举升整车的大力，代价是手柄要走很长的行程，重物只上升很短距离，省力不省功。A jack lifts a car through the combination of lever and screw mechanisms. A lever balances when effort times effort arm equals load times resistance arm, F₁L₁ = F₂L₂; the longer the effort arm and the shorter the resistance arm, the larger the mechanical advantage, so a modest force overcomes a heavy load. Scissor and hydraulic jacks amplify a small force on a long handle into a large lifting force, at the cost of a long handle travel for only a short rise; force is gained, not work.[1][2]

杠杆是最简单的机械之一。按支点、动力点和阻力点的相对位置，杠杆分成三类：支点在中间的跷跷板和撬棍，阻力在中间的开瓶器，动力在中间的镊子。不论哪一类，平衡条件都是两侧力矩相等；力矩是力臂与垂直于力臂方向分力的乘积，力沿力臂方向的分量不产生转动效果。轮轴可以看成绕同一轴线转动的杠杆，轮半径大于轴半径，摇轮就能在轴上得到更大的力。The lever is the simplest machine. Levers fall into three classes by the relative positions of fulcrum, effort and load: a seesaw and crowbar with the fulcrum in the middle, a bottle opener with the load in the middle, and tweezers with the effort in the middle. In every class the balance condition is equal moments on both sides; a moment is the arm times the force component perpendicular to the arm, while the force along the arm produces no turning effect. A wheel and axle acts as a lever rotating on one axis: the larger wheel radius gives a greater force at the smaller axle.[1][2]

螺旋千斤顶把旋转运动转换成直线举升。螺杆每转一周，重物沿螺距上升一个螺距，长手柄的转动行程被螺纹分解成很小的轴向位移，从而在小力下获得大举升力；螺纹接触面还提供自锁，松手后重物不会自行回落。液压千斤顶则用小面积柱塞泵把油压入大面积液压缸，按帕斯卡原理放大推力，举起卡车和工程车辆。A screw jack converts rotation into straight lifting. With every full turn the load rises by one pitch, and the long handle travel is broken into a small axial displacement, giving a large lifting force from a small effort; the thread contact also provides self-locking, so the load does not fall back when released. A hydraulic jack instead pumps oil from a small plunger into a large cylinder, amplifying force by Pascal's principle to lift trucks and vehicles.[2][8]

工程上没有“凭空省力”。机械利益来自力与距离的交换：省力的机械必然费距离，省距离的机械必然费力。实际机械还存在摩擦和机械效率损失，输入功总有一部分消耗在摩擦、形变和发热上，输出功小于输入功。OpenStax 大学物理把简单机械归纳为杠杆、轮轴、滑轮、斜面、螺旋和楔子，各自通过改变力臂、行程或接触面来改变力的大小方向。Engineering offers no free force. Mechanical advantage trades force against distance: a force-saving machine costs distance, and a distance-saving machine costs force. Real machines also lose energy to friction, deformation and heat, so output work stays below input work. The OpenStax college-physics text groups simple machines into lever, wheel and axle, pulley, inclined plane, screw and wedge, each changing force magnitude, direction or travel through arms, paths or contact surfaces.[1][2]

边界：杠杆平衡讨论的是静止或匀速状态下的力矩关系，加速举升时还要计入惯性和摩擦损失。机械利益高不代表可以超载，材料强度、螺纹自锁和液压密封才是承重安全的限制。本章讲简单机械的力—距离交换与效率概念，不展开具体千斤顶的额定载荷计算或工程起重方案选型。Boundary: lever balance describes moments at rest or in uniform motion; during accelerated lifting, inertia and friction losses must also be counted. A high mechanical advantage does not permit overloading, because material strength, thread self-locking and hydraulic seals set the safety limit. This chapter covers force-distance trade and efficiency concepts in simple machines, not rated-load calculations or lifting-system selection.[1][2]

02汽车刹车时人为什么会向前冲？电梯升降时为什么会不舒服？Why do passengers lurch forward when braking, and why do elevators feel uncomfortable?

惯性让身体保持原速，合力按 F=ma 减速；前倾是内部速度差，电梯不适来自加速度切换带来的超重失重。Inertia keeps body speed; net force decelerates by F=ma. The lean is an internal speed difference, and elevator discomfort comes from heavier/lighter acceleration switches.

汽车刹车时人向前冲，是惯性在起作用。物体保持原有运动状态的性质叫惯性：车减速时，人的下肢随车受到制动减速，躯干和头部仍按原来的速度向前运动，于是身体前倾；等安全带或座椅把人体整体减速，前倾才被拉住。车辆加速时身体则向后仰，道理相同——身体各部分改变速度的时间差，来自惯性和约束力分别作用在不同部位。A passenger lurches forward when the car brakes because of inertia. Inertia is the tendency of a body to keep its motion: as the vehicle decelerates, the lower body slows with it through braking, while the torso and head keep moving at the original speed and lean forward; only the seatbelt and seat slow the whole body down. On acceleration the body leans backward for the same reason: parts change speed at different times because inertia and restraining forces act on different locations.[3][4]

牛顿第一定律说明，不受外力或合力为零时，物体保持静止或匀速直线运动；要改变速度，必须有合力。牛顿第二定律把这个关系写成 F=ma，合力等于质量乘以加速度：减速越猛、质量越大，需要的制动力越大，人体前倾的趋势也越强。刹车距离与初速度平方相关，速度加倍，制动距离大致变成四倍，这也是高速跟车必须留出更长距离的原因。Newton's first law states that with no net force a body stays at rest or in uniform motion; changing speed requires a net force. The second law writes this as F = ma: net force equals mass times acceleration. The harder the deceleration and the larger the mass, the greater the braking force needed and the stronger the forward lean. Braking distance scales with the square of speed, so doubling speed roughly quadruples the stopping distance, which is why high-speed following demands a longer gap.[3][4][8]

电梯升降时的不适来自加速度，而不是匀速运动。电梯开始上行时先加速，人体感觉超重；到达前减速，又短暂失重。下行时先加速下降、再减速停止，同样经历超重和失重的切换。前庭器官和皮肤压力感受器把加速度变化传给大脑，耳内压力随体位调整，部分人会出现轻微心慌、发飘甚至恶心。匀速运行阶段加速度接近零，身体反而感觉平稳。Elevator discomfort comes from acceleration, not steady motion. When an elevator starts rising it accelerates first, and the body feels heavier; before stopping it decelerates and briefly feels weightless. Descending starts with downward acceleration and ends with deceleration, switching between heavier and lighter feelings again. The vestibular system and skin-pressure receptors pass these acceleration changes to the brain, middle-ear pressure adjusts with posture, and some people feel mild unease, lightness or nausea. At constant speed acceleration is near zero and the body feels steady.[3][4]

惯性在交通工具上处处可见：火车车厢连接处、货物捆绑和汽车安全带都用来防止物体因惯性相对车体移动；货运车辆急刹时未固定的货物会向前冲击。OpenStax 教材把惯性定义为物体对速度变化的抵抗，用第一定律和第二定律区分了“保持运动状态”和“在合力作用下改变运动状态”两种情形；摩擦力、空气阻力等外力共同决定减速过程。Inertia appears throughout transport: coupler connections, cargo tie-downs and seatbelts all keep objects from shifting relative to the vehicle; unsecured cargo surges forward under hard braking. The OpenStax text defines inertia as resistance to speed change, and separates, through the first and second laws, keeping a motion state from changing it under a net force; friction, air resistance and other forces jointly set the deceleration process.[3][4]

边界：前倾是人身体内部速度差造成的主观感受，不是有一股力量把人往前推；所谓惯性力是在非惯性参考系里的等效描述。电梯不适还可能与前庭敏感、空气压力和空腹等个人因素有关，不能全部归因于加速度。本章讲惯性、合力与加速度的基本关系，不展开碰撞力学、安全带碰撞测试或晕车的临床机制。Boundary: the forward lean is a speed difference inside the body, not a force pushing a person ahead; so-called inertial force is an equivalent description inside a non-inertial frame. Elevator discomfort also depends on vestibular sensitivity, air pressure and personal state such as an empty stomach, not acceleration alone. This chapter covers inertia, net force and acceleration, not collision mechanics, seatbelt crash tests or clinical motion sickness.[3][4]

03为什么汽车轮胎是黑色的？轮胎花纹起什么作用？Why are car tires black, and what do tread grooves do?

炭黑补强橡胶、耐磨抗老化；花纹排水防磨耗水滑，抓地靠胎面与路面的静摩擦，胎压影响接地面积。Carbon black reinforces rubber for wear and aging resistance; grooves drain water against hydroplaning, grip comes through static friction, and inflation pressure sets contact area.

汽车轮胎多为黑色，主要是因为掺入了炭黑。天然橡胶强度低、不耐磨，加入炭黑后橡胶分子与炭黑颗粒形成补强结构，抗拉、耐磨和抗撕裂性能明显提高；炭黑还吸收紫外线，减缓橡胶在日晒下的老化龟裂。早期浅色轮胎因缺少这种保护，更容易开裂和磨耗，黑色轮胎由此成为主流。轮胎配色如果不用炭黑，需要另加补强剂和抗老化体系，成本与耐久都不占优。Car tires are mostly black because carbon black is mixed into the rubber. Natural rubber is weak and wears poorly; combined with carbon black, rubber molecules form a reinforced structure around the particles, raising tensile strength, abrasion and tear resistance. Carbon black also absorbs ultraviolet light and slows cracking under sun exposure. Early light-colored tires lacked this protection, cracked and wore faster, and black became the mainstream choice. Colored tires without carbon black need other reinforcing and anti-aging systems, with cost and durability disadvantages.[2][5]

花纹沟槽的作用是排水和保持抓地。轮胎接地时，花纹把胎面与路面之间的水排向沟槽两侧，避免水膜把轮胎托离路面形成水滑；干地行驶时花纹块咬合路面，提供侧向附着。胎面磨损到限位标记后，沟槽变浅，排水和湿地抓地能力下降，制动距离在湿路上明显拉长。光滑胎面在干燥赛道上接地面积大、抓地强，但遇到积水会迅速失去附着，只适合干燥场地竞速。Tread grooves drain water and preserve grip. Under contact with the road, grooves push water away from between tread and surface, preventing a water film from lifting the tire into hydroplaning; on dry roads tread blocks bite the pavement and provide lateral adhesion. Once wear reaches the indicator marks, grooves are shallow, drainage and wet grip drop, and wet braking distance lengthens noticeably. Slick tread has a large contact patch and strong grip on dry racetracks but loses adhesion quickly in water and suits only dry racing.[5][8]

轮胎与路面之间的力主要靠静摩擦传递。驱动、制动和转向都通过接地面积上的摩擦把力传给地面；摩擦系数受路面材质、胎面橡胶配方、温度和积水影响。胎压过低时胎侧弯折生热、接地不均，胎压过高时接地面积变小，两者都会降低抓地并加快局部磨损。轮胎结构还包括帘布层、钢丝圈和气密层，分别承担抗拉、固定轮辋和保压的作用。Road forces pass mainly through static friction between tire and pavement. Drive, brake and steering forces reach the ground through friction on the contact patch, and the coefficient depends on surface material, rubber compound, temperature and water. Underinflation flexes the sidewall and heats unevenly; overinflation shrinks the contact patch; both cut grip and speed local wear. Tire construction also includes ply layers, bead wires and an inner liner, carrying tension, locking the rim and retaining pressure.[5][8]

OpenStax 物理教材把摩擦分为静摩擦、动摩擦和滚动摩擦，说明摩擦力与正压力、接触面性质相关；轮胎花纹与橡胶配方的工程目标，是在干湿不同路面上把可用摩擦尽量保持稳定。路面标线、积水和温度都会改变摩擦条件，同一套轮胎在高速湿滑路面上的附着余量远低于干燥路面。The OpenStax text divides friction into static, kinetic and rolling forms, and links friction to normal force and contact-surface properties. Tire tread and rubber compounds aim to keep usable friction steady across wet and dry surfaces. Road markings, standing water and temperature all change friction, and the grip margin at high speed on wet pavement is far lower than on dry road.[2][5]

边界：轮胎黑色是材料工程选择，不是橡胶本身的颜色；浅色橡胶轮胎可以存在，但需要额外补强和耐老化处理。花纹越多不等于抓地越好，过多细碎花纹会降低刚性、磨损加快。本章讲轮胎材料、花纹与摩擦的基本原理，不展开轮胎配方专利、轮胎等级测试标准或爆胎的应急处置。Boundary: black color is a materials-engineering choice, not the natural color of rubber; light tires can exist but need extra reinforcement and aging protection. More grooves do not mean better grip, and too many fine tread blocks lower rigidity and wear faster. This chapter covers tire materials, tread and friction basics, not compound patents, tire-rating test standards or blowout emergency handling.[5][8]

04为什么飞机可以在天上飞，汽车却不能？Why can an airplane fly in the sky while a car cannot?

机翼靠上下表面压力差和向下排空气获得升力；推力克服阻力、升力平衡重力，汽车靠路面支撑与摩擦。The wing gains lift from a pressure difference and downward air deflection; thrust beats drag and lift balances weight, while the car relies on road support and friction.

飞机能上天而汽车不能，关键在于飞机产生了足以平衡重力的升力。机翼上表面弯曲、下表面较平，气流绕机翼流动时上下表面速度不同，按伯努利原理形成压力差，下方压力高于上方，合力向上；与此同时机翼把空气向下偏转，空气给机翼一个向上的反作用力，两种描述都指向同一个向上的升力。升力与机翼面积、空气密度、速度平方和升力系数有关，速度越快，升力增长越快，飞机必须在跑道上加速到足够速度才能离地。A plane flies while a car cannot because the aircraft generates lift strong enough to balance weight. The wing arcs on top and sits flatter below; airflow around it differs in speed between the two surfaces, and Bernoulli's principle gives a pressure difference with higher pressure below and an upward resultant. At the same time the wing deflects air downward, and the air pushes the wing upward by reaction; both descriptions converge on the same upward lift. Lift scales with wing area, air density, speed squared and a lift coefficient: the faster the speed, the faster lift grows, so the aircraft must accelerate on the runway to enough speed to leave the ground.[6][7]

一架飞机受四个力支配：升力向上平衡重力，推力向前克服阻力，阻力来自空气对机身和机翼的阻碍。发动机提供推力，机翼提供升力，尾翼控制俯仰和方向；起飞阶段推力持续加速，升力随速度增大，达到离地速度后升力超过重力。汽车没有产生空气升力的机翼，也不依赖空气支撑：它靠路面的法向力托住车身，驱动力来自轮胎与地面的摩擦，离开路面后既没有支撑也没有附着，无法像飞机一样飞行。A plane is governed by four forces: lift upward balances weight, thrust forward overcomes drag, and drag comes from air resistance to fuselage and wings. Engines provide thrust, wings provide lift, and tail surfaces control pitch and direction. During takeoff thrust keeps accelerating, lift grows with speed, and once the takeoff speed is reached lift exceeds weight. A car has no lift-generating wing and does not rely on air for support: the road's normal force holds the body up, and drive force comes through tire-road friction; off the road it has neither support nor adhesion and cannot fly like an aircraft.[4][7]

气流绕机翼的规律可用流体力学理解。伯努利关系说明流速高处压力低、流速低处压力高，机翼截面据此设计；作用与反作用则说明机翼向下排开空气，空气把机翼向上推。低速大迎角时机翼上方气流可能分离，升力突然下降进入失速；襟翼和缝翼在起降阶段改变翼型弯度，低速时增加升力。OpenStax 流体章节用伯努利方程和连续性方程描述流速与压力关系，NASA 航空入门材料把升力、重力、推力、阻力四个力作为分析框架。Airflow around the wing follows fluid mechanics. Bernoulli's relation links high flow speed to low pressure and low speed to high pressure, which shapes the airfoil; action and reaction show the wing pushing air down and the air pushing the wing up. At high angle of attack at low speed, airflow may separate above the wing and lift drops suddenly into a stall; flaps and slots change camber during takeoff and landing to add low-speed lift. The OpenStax fluid chapter uses Bernoulli and continuity equations for speed-pressure relations, while NASA's beginner aeronautics material frames lift, weight, thrust and drag as the analysis framework.[6][7]

飞行边界由结构和动力共同决定。飞机机翼和机身要承受升力、机动过载和阵风，发动机持续输出推力克服阻力；高度升高后空气密度下降，相同速度下升力减小，飞机需要调整速度和迎角。汽车的设计目标是在地面承载与牵引，发动机功率主要转化为车轮摩擦牵引力，车身造型更多考虑降低风阻而不是产生升力。Flight limits follow both structure and propulsion. Wings and fuselage carry lift, maneuver g-loading and gusts, and engines keep producing thrust against drag. At altitude lower air density cuts lift at the same speed, so the plane must adjust speed and angle of attack. A car is built for ground support and traction: engine power mostly becomes wheel friction traction, and body shaping targets low drag rather than lift generation.[4][7]

边界：机翼升力不能简化成“上表面路程长所以空气必须跑得快”的单一说法，气流偏转和压力差是同一现象的不同侧面；真实机翼还受涡流、压缩性和翼尖涡影响。本章讲升力来源与四力平衡，不展开翼型设计计算、失速改出程序或具体机型的飞行性能包线。Boundary: wing lift cannot be reduced to a single claim that "the upper path is longer so the air must speed up"; airflow deflection and pressure difference are two sides of the same phenomenon, and real wings also involve vortices, compressibility and tip vortices. This chapter covers lift sources and four-force balance, not airfoil design calculations, stall recovery procedures or model-specific flight envelopes.[6][7]

05比萨斜塔为什么会倾斜？河堤为什么下宽上窄？Why does the Leaning Tower of Pisa tilt, and why are dams wider at the base?

斜塔源于软土地基不均匀沉降；河堤断面随静水压强 p=ρgh 下宽上窄，地基、材料与几何形状共同承重。The Pisa lean comes from uneven soft-soil settlement; dam sections widen downward with hydrostatic pressure p = ρgh, and foundation, materials and geometry carry load together.

比萨斜塔倾斜，是地基不均匀沉降造成的结构问题。塔身建在冲积土层上，一侧地基下沉更多，塔身随软弱土层逐步倾斜；倾斜本身是地基变形的表现，不是塔身材料被压弯。长期监测发现沉降与倾斜相互作用：倾斜一侧地基压力集中，土体被进一步压缩，倾斜加剧；后来通过地基加固、抽土纠偏和配重控制，把倾角拉回到相对安全的范围。结构稳定首先取决于地基承载力是否均匀，上部结构再坚固也无法替代可靠的持力层。The Leaning Tower of Pisa tilts because of uneven foundation settlement. It was built on alluvial soil, one side settled more than the other, and the tower tilted gradually with the soft ground; the lean shows foundation deformation rather than the masonry being bent in compression. Monitoring found settlement and lean interact: pressure concentrates under the tilted side, soil compresses further and the lean grows; later ground reinforcement, soil extraction and counterweights brought the angle back to a safer range. Structural stability first depends on even foundation bearing capacity, and a strong superstructure cannot replace a reliable bearing layer.[1][8]

河堤做成下宽上窄，是为了承受随深度增大的水压强。液体压强 p=ρgh，深度越深压强越大，坝底比坝顶承受更大的水平推力；断面下部加宽可以把力分散到更宽的地基面上，同时增大自重抵抗滑动。重力坝、海堤和挡水墙都遵循同一原则：迎水面一侧随深度加厚，基础嵌入不透水层，减小渗流和扬压力对坝体稳定的削弱。材料强度负责抵抗弯矩和剪切，断面形状则由压力分布和地基条件决定。River embankments widen toward the bottom to withstand water pressure that grows with depth. Liquid pressure p = ρgh rises with depth, so the base carries a larger horizontal push than the top; a wider lower section spreads force over a broader foundation and adds self-weight against sliding. Gravity dams, sea walls and retaining walls follow the same principle: the water-facing side thickens with depth, and the base embeds into an impermeable layer to cut seepage and uplift pressure that would weaken stability. Material strength resists bending and shear, while section shape follows pressure distribution and ground conditions.[1][8]

建筑物和构筑物依靠材料与几何形状共同承重。墙体、立柱把上部荷载传到基础，混凝土和石材抗压能力强，钢材抗拉能力好；拱券把竖向荷载分解成沿拱圈的压力，并向两侧拱脚推送，所以古代砖石结构能跨越较大空间。薄壳、桁架和索结构通过材料布置让构件主要受拉或受压，避免材料承受不利的弯曲。风、地震和地基不均匀沉降产生的附加力，需要在结构布置中预留冗余和变形能力。Buildings and structures carry load through materials and geometry together. Walls and columns pass upper loads to the foundation; concrete and stone are strong in compression, steel in tension. Arches redirect vertical loads into compression along the ring and push outward on the abutments, which is why ancient masonry could span large spaces. Shells, trusses and cable structures place materials so members are mainly in tension or compression, avoiding unfavorable bending. Wind, earthquake and uneven settlement add forces that design must accommodate through redundancy and deformation capacity.[1][8]

OpenStax 物理教材用压强定义压力在面积上的分布，用静力学平衡分析结构构件的受力和力矩；压力随深度的关系解释了堤坝和墙体的断面形状。不同地基的承载力差异很大，岩石、砂土和软弱压缩性土层对沉降的反应不同，设计前需要勘察持力层和地下水位。桥梁和高层建筑还需要考虑活载、温度变形、风振和长期蠕变，结构安全是荷载、材料和地基三方面共同匹配的结果。The OpenStax physics text defines pressure as force distributed over an area and uses static equilibrium to analyze member forces and moments; the depth-pressure relation explains embankment and wall section shapes. Bearing capacity varies widely between rock, sand and soft compressible soil, which respond differently to settlement, so surveys must identify the bearing layer and groundwater level before design. Bridges and tall buildings also account for live load, thermal deformation, wind vibration and long-term creep; structural safety matches loads, materials and foundations together.[1][5][8]

边界：斜塔倾斜是地基问题，不能把它当成“塔身被压弯”；现代纠偏措施改善的是地基平衡，而不是改变塔身砌体本身。堤坝下宽上窄由水压分布决定，不能照搬成所有建筑的通用断面。本章讲结构承载的力学概念与地基、材料分工，不展开结构抗震设计规范、地基处理施工方案或具体桥梁的荷载验算。Boundary: the Pisa lean is a foundation problem, not masonry bent in compression; modern correction improves foundation balance rather than changing the tower masonry itself. The widening dam section follows water-pressure distribution and cannot be copied as a universal building shape. This chapter covers structural-load concepts and the division between foundation and materials, not seismic code provisions, ground-treatment construction plans or bridge load verification.[1][8]

063D 打印机怎样一层层造出物体？How does a 3D printer build an object layer by layer?

三维模型先被切成一层层二维薄片，喷头或光源按切片路径逐层堆积、固化材料，层间结合后形成完整物体，属于增材制造。A 3D model is sliced into layers; a nozzle or light source deposits and cures material along each slice, and layers bond into a complete object.

3D 打印又称增材制造，和切削去除材料的传统加工方向相反。它不从整块料上削去多余部分，而是把材料一点点加上去堆出形状。整个过程从一个三维数字模型开始：先在电脑里建好物体的几何模型，再由切片软件把模型沿高度方向切成许多很薄的二维截面，每一层截面就是打印机一次要完成的轮廓。3D printing, or additive manufacturing, runs opposite to traditional machining that removes material. Instead of cutting away excess from a solid block, it builds shape by adding material a little at a time. The process starts from a three-dimensional digital model: the geometry is designed on computer, then slicing software cuts the model along its height into many thin two-dimensional sections, each one a contour the printer completes in one pass.[9]

切片之后，打印机按层加工。最常见的熔融沉积工艺（FDM）把丝材加热熔化，从喷嘴挤出，在成型平台上按当前层路径铺出材料，冷却后固化；光固化工艺用紫外光逐层照射液态树脂，被照到的位置聚合固化；粉末床工艺则用激光或粘结剂选择性固化一层粉末，再铺上新粉继续下一层。每层完成后平台下降或喷嘴上移，开始下一层。无论哪种工艺，共同思路都是把三维问题拆成一系列二维层：先确定每层截面轮廓，再在该层范围内堆积或固化材料，逐层叠加还原出立体形状。After slicing, the printer works layer by layer. The common fused deposition modeling, FDM, melts filament and extrudes it through a nozzle, laying material on the build platform along the current layer path, where it cools and solidifies. Photopolymerization cures liquid resin layer by layer where ultraviolet light strikes. Powder-bed processes selectively fuse a layer of powder with laser or binder, then spread fresh powder for the next layer. Once a layer finishes, the platform lowers or the nozzle rises for the next one.[1][9]

层厚决定精度和效率。常见层厚在 0.1–0.3 mm 之间，FDM 喷嘴直径常约 0.4 mm；层越薄，表面越细腻、尺寸越准，但打印时间越长。物体侧面会留下一层层叠加的台阶痕迹，曲面处尤其明显；层与层之间的结合方向往往也是力学上相对薄弱的方向，受力设计要考虑层间强度。Layer thickness sets accuracy and speed. Common layers are 0.1 to 0.3 mm thick, and FDM nozzle diameter is often about 0.4 mm; thinner layers give a finer surface and tighter accuracy but take longer. The sides show the stepped trace of stacked layers, especially on curves, and the bond direction between layers is often a relatively weak mechanical direction, so load design must account for interlayer strength.[9]

成型过程中常需要支撑结构。悬空或悬挑部分在没有材料依托时会下垂，打印时先加临时支撑托住，成型后再拆除；封闭内腔、薄壁和复杂镂空结构，正是增材制造相对切削加工更擅长的形状。模型切片路径、填充密度和材料选择，都会影响成品的强度、重量和表面质量。填充密度决定零件内部是实心还是网格状，影响重量和强度；悬空角度越小，需要的支撑越多，成型后清理支撑也会留下表面痕迹。Support structures are often needed during printing. Overhangs would sag without material beneath, so temporary supports are printed first to hold them, then removed after molding. Enclosed cavities, thin walls and complex lattices are exactly the shapes additive manufacturing handles better than machining. Slice paths, infill density and material choice all affect finished strength, weight and surface quality.[2][9]

边界要写清：增材制造适合定制、小批量和复杂结构；大批量同形零件用注塑、铸造等工艺往往更经济。本章只讲逐层堆积的基本流程和常见工艺分类，不展开设备选型、材料配方参数或具体工程设计规范；实际打印的精度和可靠性还要结合材料、设备和后处理评估。The boundary matters: additive manufacturing suits customization, small batches and complex structures; high-volume identical parts are often more economical by injection molding or casting. This chapter covers only the basic layer-by-layer flow and common process categories, not equipment selection, material formulation parameters or engineering design standards. Real printing accuracy and reliability must be assessed together with material, machine and post-processing.[2]
