01数码相机怎样把进入镜头的光变成一张数字照片？How does a digital camera turn the light entering its lens into a digital photo?

镜头把光线会聚到传感器上，光圈、快门和感光度共同控制曝光量，像素再把光转成电信号。The lens focuses light onto a sensor; aperture, shutter speed and ISO set the exposure, and pixels convert light into electrical signals.

数码相机成像分三步：镜头会聚光线，传感器记录亮度与颜色，电路把读数编码成数字文件。镜头相当于可变焦距的凸透镜，把场景中成倒立缩小的实像投到传感器表面；光圈控制通光孔径，快门控制曝光时间，感光度（ISO）放大传感器信号。一张照片的明暗由这三者共同决定，而不是某一个旋钮单独决定。A digital camera makes a picture in three stages. The lens gathers and focuses the light, the sensor records brightness and colour, and the electronics turn those readings into a digital file. The lens acts as a variable-focal-length converging lens that projects a small, inverted real image onto the sensor. The aperture sets the opening diameter, the shutter sets the exposure time, and the ISO gain amplifies the sensor signal. The brightness of a photo is set by all three together, not by any single dial alone.[1][4]

镜头由多片透镜组成，通过前后移动改变焦距；焦距越短视角越宽，焦距越长越能把远处物体拉近。光圈用 f 数表示，f 等于焦距除以孔径直径，f 数越小孔径越大、进光越多，背景虚化也越强。光线穿过镜头后到达图像传感器，常见全画幅传感器约 36 mm × 24 mm，上面排列数百万到数千万个光电二极管；每个像素只记录落在它上面的光强，彩色画面靠拜耳滤色片把红、绿、蓝分到相邻像素上。The lens is built from several glass elements that move to change the focal length. A shorter focal length gives a wider field of view, while a longer one brings distant subjects closer. The aperture is quoted as an f-number equal to focal length divided by aperture diameter: a smaller f-number means a wider opening, more light, and shallower depth of field. After the lens, light reaches the image sensor. A typical full-frame sensor measures about 36 mm by 24 mm and holds several million to tens of millions of photodiodes. Each pixel records only the light falling on it, and a Bayer filter splits red, green and blue across neighbouring pixels to form a colour image.[1][2]

曝光量是孔径面积、曝光时间与信号增益的乘积。快门速度从千分之一秒以上的高速档到几秒的长曝光不等；ISO 从 100 到 6400 以上，提高 ISO 等于放大电信号，同时也放大噪点。OpenStax 几何光学章节用薄透镜公式说明像距、物距与焦距的关系；人眼与相机的对照也在同一章：眼球的晶状体相当于镜头，视网膜相当于传感器。拍摄运动物体用高速快门凝固动作，拍夜景则靠小光圈加长时间曝光，或提高 ISO 换取亮度。Exposure is the product of aperture area, exposure time and signal gain. Shutter speeds range from fractions of a millisecond to several seconds for long exposures, and ISO runs from 100 to beyond 6400; raising the ISO amplifies the electrical signal but also the noise. The OpenStax geometric-optics chapter uses the thin-lens equation to relate image distance, object distance and focal length, and compares the eye with a camera: the eye's crystalline lens corresponds to the camera lens and the retina to the sensor. A fast shutter freezes moving subjects, while night scenes rely on a small aperture with a long exposure, or on a higher ISO for brightness.[1][2]

手机相机把这套结构压缩进几毫米厚的机身：镜头组更小，传感器尺寸受限，靠多镜头拼接、计算摄影和多帧合成补足景深与弱光表现。专业相机保留可换镜头和较大传感器，以换取浅景深、高动态范围和低噪点。镜头上的镀膜减少反射眩光，传感器前的低通滤波器抑制摩尔纹；这些部件都服务于同一件事：让到达每个像素的光尽量干净、可辨。A phone packs this structure into a body only a few millimetres thick. Its lens group is smaller and its sensor is limited in size, so multiple lenses, computational photography and multi-frame merging compensate for depth and low-light performance. A professional camera keeps interchangeable lenses and a larger sensor to obtain shallow depth of field, high dynamic range and low noise. Lens coatings reduce reflected glare, and an optical low-pass filter in front of the sensor suppresses moiré patterns; all of these parts keep the light reaching each pixel clean and readable.[1][2]

边界要写清：相机记录的是传感器接收到的光，不直接记录“真实颜色”；白平衡、曝光补偿和后期都会改变最终画面。镜头成像遵循几何光学，但景深、噪点和动态范围还受传感器尺寸与算法影响。把“大光圈一定更好”套用到手机小传感器上会失真，因为孔径、焦距和传感器尺寸必须放在一起比较。The boundary matters. A camera records the light reaching its sensor, not an objective truth about colour; white balance, exposure compensation and post-processing all change the final image. Lens imaging follows geometric optics, but depth of field, noise and dynamic range also depend on sensor size and software. Treating "a wide aperture is always better" as a rule for small phone sensors is misleading, because aperture, focal length and sensor size have to be compared together.[1]

02手机通话和上网靠什么把信号传到基站？射频辐射会伤害人体吗？How does a phone carry voice and data to a base station, and does its radio-frequency radiation harm the body?

手机把语音和数据调制到射频电磁波上，经蜂窝小区的基站转发；射频属于非电离辐射，现有证据未证实日常暴露会损伤 DNA。A phone modulates voice and data onto radio-frequency waves that a cellular base station relays; RF is non-ionizing, and routine exposure has not been shown to damage DNA.

手机通话不靠导线，而是把语音和数据编码后调制到射频电磁波上，发给附近的基站；基站再经固定网络接通对方。现代蜂窝网络把地面划分成若干小区，每个基站服务本小区，手机移动时在小区之间切换。手机与基站之间的频段多在 700 MHz 到 6 GHz 之间，属于微波与超短波范围，波长从几十厘米到几十米。A phone call travels without a wire. Voice and data are encoded, modulated onto radio-frequency waves, and sent to the nearest base station, which relays the call through a fixed network to the other handset. A modern cellular network divides the ground into small cells, each served by one base station, and the phone hands over between cells as it moves. Phone-to-base-station bands mostly run from 700 MHz to 6 GHz, in the microwave and very-high-frequency range, with wavelengths from tens of centimetres to tens of metres.[3]

电磁波由变化的电场和磁场相互激发向前传播，速度约 3.00×10⁸ m/s；OpenStax 电磁学章节把麦克斯韦方程与赫兹实验作为这一结论的依据。发射端把信号叠加到载波上，数字通信多用正交频分复用等方式，接收端再解调还原。频段越高可用带宽越大，但绕射能力越弱；因此低频段覆盖广、适合语音与偏远地区，高频段容量大、适合高密度城区的高速数据。An electromagnetic wave travels forward through mutually inducing electric and magnetic fields at about 3.00×10⁸ m/s; the OpenStax electromagnetism chapter traces this conclusion to Maxwell's equations and Hertz's experiments. The transmitter overlays the signal on a carrier wave, using schemes such as orthogonal frequency-division multiplexing in digital systems, and the receiver demodulates it back. Higher bands offer more bandwidth but weaker diffraction, so low frequencies cover wide areas for voice and remote regions, while high frequencies supply large capacity for fast data in dense cities.[3][4]

关于“危险辐射”，先要分清电离与非电离。射频光子能量远低于可见光，不足以打断化学键或直接损伤 DNA；美国国家癌症研究所（NCI）的电磁场资料指出，国际癌症研究机构（IARC）在 2011 年把射频场列为 2B 类“可能致癌”，依据是有限且不一致的流行病学证据，并不等于已证实致癌。各国对手机比吸收率（SAR）设有上限，美国标准为 1.6 W/kg，欧盟参考 ICNIRP 指南约 2 W/kg，手机设计需在限值内工作。Before asking about "dangerous radiation", it is essential to separate ionizing from non-ionizing radiation. Radio-frequency photons carry far less energy than visible light and cannot break chemical bonds or damage DNA directly. The U.S. National Cancer Institute notes that in 2011 the International Agency for Research on Cancer classified radio-frequency fields as Group 2B, "possibly carcinogenic", on limited and inconsistent epidemiological evidence; this is not the same as proving cancer. Countries set specific absorption rate (SAR) limits for phones, 1.6 W/kg under the U.S. standard and about 2 W/kg under the ICNIRP guideline referenced in the European Union, and handsets must work within those limits.[4][6]

已发表的大规模队列研究没有给出日常使用手机与脑肿瘤之间存在确定因果关系的结论；证据缺口主要来自长周期、高剂量暴露的追踪。基站天线的功率密度随距离快速下降，近场与远场的暴露水平差别很大。通话时手机贴近头部，数据下载时手机常放在桌上或包里，实际暴露位置和时长也不同；免提、耳机和减少弱信号环境下的长时间通话，是降低暴露的直接做法。Large cohort studies published so far have not established a definite causal link between everyday phone use and brain tumours; the evidence gap comes mainly from long-term tracking of high-dose exposure. Power density from a base-station antenna drops rapidly with distance, so near-field and far-field exposure differ greatly. During a call the phone sits close to the head, while during downloads it often rests on a desk or in a bag, so position and duration differ as well. Hands-free mode, earphones and shorter calls in weak-signal areas are direct ways to reduce exposure.[6]

边界：非电离不等于“完全没有热效应”。高功率射频在密闭空间里可以产生热量，工业加热和医疗理疗利用的正是这一点；日常手机的发射功率受网络控制，距离远、占空比低。把“射频无害”说成定论同样不严谨：低概率、长周期的健康效应仍在持续研究。判断这类说法时，应先看频段、功率和暴露时长，再对照官方限值与综述结论，而不是沿用“电器都辐射伤人”的笼统印象。The boundary: non-ionizing does not mean "no heating at all". High-power radio waves can produce heat in an enclosed space, which is exactly what industrial heating and medical diathermy use; an everyday phone's transmit power is network-controlled, short-range and low in duty cycle. Saying "radio frequency is harmless" as a settled verdict is equally too strong, because low-probability, long-term health effects remain under study. Judge such claims by band, power and exposure time, then compare with official limits and review conclusions, rather than the broad idea that all electrical appliances radiate harm.[6]

03平板电视和手机屏幕是怎样逐像素显示出彩色画面的？How do flat TVs and phone screens build a colour picture pixel by pixel?

液晶屏幕靠背光和偏振片控制每个像素的通光与颜色；OLED 则让有机发光二极管自行发光，黑像素直接熄灭。LCD screens use a backlight and polarizers to control light through each pixel, while OLEDs let organic diodes emit their own light and switch black pixels off.

平板显示的基本单元是像素，每个像素再分成红、绿、蓝三个子像素；屏幕控制每个子像素的亮度，三色按不同比例混合出画面颜色。主流技术分两条路线：液晶显示（LCD）本身不发光，靠背光和液晶层控制通光；有机发光二极管（OLED）每个像素自行发光，不需要背光源。The basic unit of a flat display is the pixel, split into red, green and blue sub-pixels. The screen controls the brightness of each sub-pixel, and the three colours blend at different ratios to form the picture. Two mainstream technologies divide this work differently. A liquid-crystal display (LCD) emits no light of its own and uses a backlight and a liquid-crystal layer to control transmitted light; an organic light-emitting diode (OLED) lets each pixel glow on its own, with no backlight.[5]

LCD 结构从背光到眼睛依次是背光源、下偏振片、液晶层、彩色滤色片和上偏振片。偏振片只允许某一方向的电场振动通过；液晶层在电压控制下扭转角度，改变通过光的偏振方向，从而控制像素的明暗。OpenStax 波动光学章节的偏振一节解释了偏振片的起偏与检偏：两片偏振片透光轴平行时通光最大，垂直时几乎遮光。彩色滤色片把白光分成红、绿、蓝子像素，滤色片本身不发光，只是选择透过的波长。From backlight to the eye, an LCD stacks a light source, a lower polarizer, the liquid-crystal layer, a colour filter and an upper polarizer. A polarizer passes only the vibration in one direction. Under voltage control, the liquid-crystal layer twists and changes the polarization of the light passing through, which sets how bright the pixel becomes. The polarization section of the OpenStax wave-optics chapter explains polarizing and analysing: two polarizers pass the most light when their axes are parallel and almost none when they are crossed. The colour filter splits white light into red, green and blue sub-pixels; the filter itself does not emit light, it only selects which wavelengths pass through.[5]

OLED 在两层电极之间夹有机半导体材料；通电后电子与空穴在有机层复合，以光子形式释放能量，不同有机材料发出红、绿或蓝光。因为每个像素独立发光，熄灭的像素就是纯黑，对比度和响应速度优于典型 LCD；OLED 还能做成柔性曲面屏。寿命与烧屏是 OLED 的主要约束：长期静止的高亮图像可能留下残影，因此设备用像素位移和亮度补偿缓解。An OLED sandwiches an organic semiconductor between two electrodes. When current flows, electrons and holes recombine in the organic layer and release energy as photons, with different organic materials emitting red, green or blue light. Because each pixel emits independently, a switched-off pixel is pure black, giving better contrast and response speed than a typical LCD; OLEDs can also be built into flexible curved screens. Lifetime and burn-in are the main constraints: a static bright image held for a long time may leave a ghost, so devices use pixel shifting and brightness compensation to reduce it.[1][5]

屏幕分辨率用每英寸像素数（PPI）衡量，子像素排列方式影响观感；高 PPI 让相邻子像素在正常观看距离下融合成连续颜色。LCD 成本较低、亮度上限高，常见于大尺寸电视；OLED 黑色纯净、轻薄柔性，常见于高端手机与手表。两类屏幕都靠数字驱动电路逐行刷新画面，刷新频率以赫兹（Hz）计，常见 60 Hz、120 Hz，高刷屏在滚动画面时减少拖影。Screen resolution is quoted in pixels per inch (PPI), and the sub-pixel arrangement affects how sharp the image looks; high PPI lets neighbouring sub-pixels merge into a continuous colour at normal viewing distance. LCDs cost less and reach higher peak brightness, which suits large televisions, while OLEDs deliver pure blacks, thin flexible panels and appear in premium phones and watches. Both panels refresh the picture line by line through digital drive electronics; refresh rate is measured in hertz (Hz), commonly 60 Hz or 120 Hz, and a high refresh rate reduces trailing blur during scrolling.[4][5]

边界：屏幕颜色依赖色域与校准，不同设备的红、绿、蓝标准不同，同一图片在两块屏幕上可能有色差。LCD 的背光始终点亮，黑场会有漏光；OLED 的自发光在低亮度下色偏和老化表现与 LCD 不同。把“OLED 一定比 LCD 好”当作通律会忽略尺寸、寿命、亮度和成本；选择时应结合观看距离、使用场景和校准情况判断。The boundary: screen colour depends on colour gamut and calibration, and red, green and blue standards differ between devices, so the same image may look different on two panels. An LCD backlight stays on, so black scenes can leak light, while OLED self-emission behaves differently in low-luminance colour shift and ageing. Treating "OLED is always better than LCD" as a rule ignores size, lifetime, brightness and cost; the choice should weigh viewing distance, use case and calibration.[5]

04手机为什么会发烫？温度升高后为什么要降频？Why does a phone heat up, and why does it throttle as temperature rises?

SoC 功耗转化为焦耳热，被动散热通道有限；结温逼近上限时降低主频与功耗墙，保护芯片电池。SoC power turns into Joule heat over a limited passive cooling path; near the junction limit the clock and power cap drop to protect chip and battery.

长时间打游戏、导航或快充之后，手机背部发烫是常见现象。手机内部没有风扇，热量主要靠机身、屏幕和中框被动传导到外界；轻薄机身把芯片、电池和叠层压得很紧，散热通道有限，高负载时温度上升很快。夏天、边充边玩时发热更明显；手感摸到的只是外壳温度，芯片内部结温通常比外壳高出不少。After long gaming, navigation or fast charging, a warm phone back is common. A phone has no fan inside; heat reaches the outside mainly through passive conduction across the body, screen and midframe. The slim chassis packs chip, battery and layers tightly, so the cooling path is limited and temperature rises fast under heavy load.[7]

发热的来源是功耗。SoC 执行指令消耗电功率，没有转化为有用信号的部分最终变成热；电流通过芯片内阻和线路产生焦耳热，功率关系可以写成 P=IV，内阻上的发热与电流平方成正比。多核并发、屏幕高亮度、射频发射和快充同时工作时，几部分功耗叠加，内部温度明显抬高。影像处理、定位计算和后台同步也占用算力，屏幕常亮和射频发射本身有功耗，快充时电池内部化学反应同样放热。Heat comes from power consumption. The SoC draws electrical power to run instructions, and whatever does not become a useful signal ends as heat; current through internal resistance and traces produces Joule heating, with power P=IV and resistive heat proportional to the square of current. Multi-core bursts, a bright screen, radio transmission and fast charging add up, raising internal temperature noticeably.[7][10]

热量从芯片经导热垫、石墨片或中框传到外壳，再散到周围空气；传导速率和接触面积、材料导热系数以及内外温差有关。机身叠层紧凑、热阻偏大，被动散热能力有限；环境温度高、阳光直射或套上厚保护壳，都会阻碍散热，让内部温度进一步升高。石墨片把芯片热量横向铺开，中框和后盖再把热量散到空气；没有风扇主动抽风，全靠温差自然对流和材料传导。Heat travels from the chip through thermal pads, graphite sheets or the midframe to the housing and then to the surrounding air; conduction rate depends on contact area, thermal conductivity and the temperature difference. The compact stack has fairly high thermal resistance and limited passive cooling; a hot environment, direct sun or a thick case all block dissipation and push internal temperature higher.[8]

芯片有允许的最高结温。温度逼近阈值时，系统会降低 CPU、GPU 主频，限制功耗墙并关闭部分核心，功耗和发热随之下降；表现为游戏掉帧、界面卡顿，这是保护芯片和电池的机制，而不是单纯的软件故障。温度回落后，主频通常会逐步恢复，反复高温则会加速电池老化。降频后如果温度仍下不来，系统还可能进一步限制充电功率或关闭部分功能；长期高温会让电池容量衰减加快、鼓包风险上升。The chip has a maximum allowed junction temperature. As it nears the threshold, the system lowers CPU and GPU clock speeds, caps the power budget and shuts off some cores, so power and heat drop together. This shows as dropped frames or stuttering; it protects chip and battery rather than being a plain software fault. Clock speeds usually recover as temperature falls, while repeated high heat accelerates battery aging.[7][8]

边界要写清：短时温热属于正常工作范围，持续高温才会影响稳定性和寿命。本章不展开具体散热模组设计和各型号温度曲线；异常发热还可能与充电短路、后台持续高负载或电池老化有关，需要结合使用场景判断，不能把所有发烫都归为降频保护。Boundary: brief warmth is normal working range; sustained high heat affects stability and lifespan. This chapter does not cover specific cooling-module designs or per-model temperature curves. Abnormal heat may also come from charging short circuits, persistent background load or an aged battery, so it must be judged against the usage context rather than blamed on throttling alone.[8][10]

05镜头为什么会长霉？清洁镀膜镜头要注意什么？Why does lens fungus grow, and how should coated lenses be cleaned?

霉菌孢子随空气进入镜组，高湿度下在镀膜与玻璃表面繁殖，菌丝侵蚀镀膜；干燥存放、轻柔清洁可延缓，受损镀膜难复原。Spores enter the assembly and grow on coatings and glass at high humidity, hyphae etching the film; dry storage and gentle cleaning help, but damaged coating cannot be restored.

老镜头内部有时会长出蛛网状、苔藓状的霉斑，从镜片边缘或镀膜薄弱处蔓延。霉并不是从玻璃里长出来的，而是空气带入的霉菌孢子在镜组内部定居、萌发并形成菌丝；密封不良、长期潮湿存放的镜头最容易出现。霉斑早期可能只是镜片角落的细小白丝，容易被当成灰尘；发现后应尽快与干燥镜头分开存放，避免孢子扩散到其他镜组。Old lenses sometimes develop spider-web or moss-like patches that spread from lens edges or weak coating spots. The fungus does not grow out of the glass itself; mold spores carried in by air settle inside the assembly, germinate and form hyphae. Poorly sealed lenses stored damp for a long time are most prone.[1]

霉菌孢子无处不在，随空气流通进入镜头接缝和镜组。当相对湿度持续高于约 60%、温度温和，镜片上又有灰尘或有机质残留时，孢子萌发并长出菌丝；水分是霉变的关键条件，干燥环境下孢子可以长期休眠却难以繁殖。镜头长期放在摄影包、抽屉等不通风的地方，梅雨季节或浴室附近湿度高，孢子更容易萌发；镜片上的指纹、油污残留也会提供养分。Mold spores are everywhere and enter lens seams and groups with air movement. When relative humidity stays above about 60%, the temperature is mild, and dust or organic residue sits on the glass, spores germinate and grow hyphae. Moisture is the key condition: in dry air spores can stay dormant for a long time but hardly reproduce.[9]

镜片表面镀有减反射膜，镀膜与玻璃界面存在微小缝隙。菌丝附着后分泌酶和代谢物，侵蚀镀膜并留下斑点；霉斑蔓延后成像反差下降，出现雾状光斑。内部霉斑藏在镜片之间，无法从外部直接擦除，继续扩散会损伤镀膜和玻璃表面。减反射膜只有几百纳米厚，菌丝代谢的酸性物质会逐步腐蚀膜层；一旦膜层被穿透，玻璃表面也可能留下蚀刻痕迹，成像上表现为眩光和反差下降。Lens elements carry anti-reflective coatings, with tiny gaps at the coating-glass interface. Attached hyphae secrete enzymes and metabolites that etch the coating and leave spots; as the patch spreads, image contrast drops and hazy highlights appear. Internal fungus sits between elements and cannot be wiped from outside; continued spread damages coating and glass.[1][5]

外部浮尘用气吹或软毛刷处理，污渍用镜头纸或微湿镜头布沿圆周轻擦，避免干擦划伤镀膜；不要使用成分不明的溶剂，也不要按压镜片。长期存放应放入干燥剂或防潮箱，保持低湿度，先控制水分再谈防霉；已经深入镀膜的霉斑无法靠清洁复原，需要专业检修。清洁前先吹掉浮尘，避免颗粒在擦拭时磨花镀膜；镜头布要保持干净，定期清洗更换，不要用衣角或纸巾代替。External dust is removed with a blower or soft brush; stains are wiped in circles with lens tissue or a lightly moistened cloth, avoiding dry rubbing that scratches the coating. Do not use unknown solvents or press on the element. Long-term storage should use a desiccant or dry cabinet to keep humidity low; moisture control comes first. Coating damage already reached cannot be restored by cleaning and needs professional service.[5][9]

边界要写清：镜头霉变是微生物与湿度问题，和镜头光学设计没有直接关系；本章不展开镜片拆解和镀膜工艺。防霉的关键是控制存放湿度，而不是反复擦拭；不同霉菌对温度和养分的要求有差别，判断霉斑是否停止发展要结合存放环境和定期检查。霉变是否停止发展，要在干燥存放一段时间后对比观察；若霉斑继续扩大，应送专业机构处理，不要自行拆开镜组。Boundary: lens fungus is a moisture-and-microbe issue, unrelated to optical design; this chapter does not cover element disassembly or coating manufacture. Prevention hinges on storage humidity, not repeated wiping. Molds differ in temperature and nutrient needs, and whether a patch has stopped spreading should be judged from storage conditions and regular inspection.[1][9]

06手机怎样实现无线充电？How does a phone charge without a cable?

充电板发射线圈通高频交流产生交变磁场，贴近的接收线圈靠互感感应出电动势，整流稳压后给电池充电，属于近场磁感应。The pad coil carries high-frequency AC to make an alternating field; the receiver coil induces voltage by mutual inductance, rectified and regulated to charge the battery.

手机无线充电不用导线把电路板和充电器连起来，而是靠两只线圈之间的电磁耦合传递能量。充电板里有一只发射线圈，手机背面内置一只接收线圈，两只线圈贴近对齐放置。能量不是靠可见光或无线电信号传过去，而是通过线圈之间的磁场传递，这和有线充电只是输送方式不同，最终进电池的仍是直流电。两只线圈分别固定在充电板和手机内部，对位后轴线大致平行，气隙约 1–5 mm；线圈之间没有金属接触，电能通过磁场这一中介传递，所以称为无线。Phone wireless charging transfers energy through electromagnetic coupling between two coils instead of a wire linking the board and charger. The charging pad holds a transmitter coil, and the phone back holds a receiver coil, placed close and aligned. Energy travels through the magnetic field between coils rather than by light or radio signal; only the delivery method differs from wired charging, and what reaches the battery is still direct current.[11]

工作原理基于互感。发射线圈通入高频交变电流，常见 Qi 标准工作频率约 100–200 kHz，电流不断变化，在线圈周围产生方向和强弱都变化的交变磁场。接收线圈靠近这个磁场时，穿过线圈的磁通随之变化，根据电磁感应，变化磁通会在接收线圈里感应出电动势，也就是把磁场能量又换回电能。The working principle is mutual inductance. The transmitter coil carries high-frequency alternating current; the common Qi standard works around 100 to 200 kHz. As the current changes continuously it produces an alternating magnetic field whose direction and strength vary around the coil. When the receiver coil sits in this field, the flux through it changes, and electromagnetic induction induces a voltage in the receiver coil, converting magnetic energy back into electrical energy.[3][11]

感应出来的是交流，需要处理后才能充电。接收电路先把交流电整流成直流，再稳压到电池所需电压，经过管理电路控制电流后送入手机电池。发射端和接收端之间通常还有通信握手，确认设备到位、识别异物后再开启充电；线圈对齐越好、距离越近，耦合越充分。发射端会检测线圈上的负载变化，判断手机是否放好；接收端还常做过压、过流和温度保护，确认正常后才把电流送进电池。The induced output is alternating current and must be processed before charging. The receiver circuit first rectifies AC to DC, regulates it to the voltage the battery needs, and feeds it through charge management into the phone battery. Transmitter and receiver usually exchange a handshake to confirm the device is in place and identify foreign objects before charging starts; better coil alignment and smaller distance give fuller coupling.[11]

它和手机通话上网的射频通信要区分开。无线充电是近场磁感应，能量集中在两只线圈之间的局部磁场里，随距离快速衰减，气隙通常只有毫米级；射频通信是远场电磁波，把信息带到远处基站。正因为互感随距离衰减快，手机必须放在充电板上对准位置，放偏或垫高都会明显降低效率。This differs from the radio-frequency communication used for calls and internet. Wireless charging is near-field magnetic induction, with energy concentrated in the local field between coils and decaying rapidly with distance, usually a millimeter-scale gap; RF communication is far-field electromagnetic waves carrying information to distant base stations. Because mutual inductance falls off fast with distance, the phone must sit aligned on the pad; being off-center or raised noticeably lowers efficiency.[3][7]

边界要写清：无线充电效率受对齐、距离、线圈损耗和发热影响，一般低于有线直充；线圈之间若有金属异物，会被交变磁场加热，因此充电器带有异物检测。本章只讲互感充电的能量链路，不展开具体芯片方案、充电协议或不同无线充电标准的对比。The boundary matters: wireless charging efficiency depends on alignment, distance, coil losses and heat, and is generally below direct wired charging. A metal foreign object between coils would be heated by the alternating field, so chargers include foreign-object detection. This chapter covers only the mutual-inductance energy chain, not specific chip designs, charging protocols or comparisons between wireless standards.[7][11]
