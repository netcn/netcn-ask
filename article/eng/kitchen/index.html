冰箱、微波炉和对流烤箱都在厨房里“搬热”，只是路径不同：一个用制冷剂把热从箱内泵到背面，一个用微波直接激励食物中的水分子，一个用风扇把热空气吹过食物。这个 Hub 用 8 个问题讲清加热、保温和金属屏蔽，也把 Wi-Fi 与微波炉的剂量差别说清楚。A refrigerator, a microwave oven and a convection oven all move heat in the kitchen, but by different paths: one pumps heat out of the food compartment with a refrigerant, one drives water molecules directly with microwaves, and one blows hot air across the food. Eight questions explain heating, food safety and metal shielding, and separate Wi-Fi from a microwave oven by power and exposure.

01冰箱为什么背面发热、里面却冷？把菜放进去又为什么能保鲜？Why does a refrigerator feel warm at the back and cold inside—and why does chilling food keep it safer?

冰箱把热从冷藏室搬到背面，代价是压缩机做功；冷藏减慢细菌繁殖，却不能杀菌。A refrigerator pumps heat from the food compartment to the back at the cost of compressor work; chilling slows bacterial growth but does not sterilize food.

先给结论：冰箱并不“制造冷”，它只是把热量从冷藏室搬到房间。冷藏室里的蒸发器吸收食物和空气的热，背面或底部的冷凝器把热放给周围空气，所以背面摸起来热、里面却冷。它和空调、热泵一样，是消耗电能、把热从低温处搬到高温处的机器。Start with the conclusion: a refrigerator does not create cold. It moves heat from the food compartment to the room. The evaporator inside absorbs heat from the food and air; the condenser at the back or bottom releases that heat to the surroundings. That is why the back feels warm while the interior stays cold. Like an air conditioner or a heat pump, it uses electrical work to move heat from a colder place to a warmer one.[1][9]

这套循环有四个关键部件：压缩机、冷凝器、节流阀（膨胀阀）和蒸发器。压缩机把低压气态制冷剂压缩，压力和温度一起升高；高温气体进入背面冷凝器，风扇或自然对流把热带走，制冷剂冷凝成液体；节流阀让它突然降压，温度随之下降；低温制冷剂进入箱内蒸发器，吸收冷藏室空气的热量，再变回气体，回到压缩机。如此循环，热就从箱内被“泵”到箱外。这套循环的性能系数（COP）通常在 2–6 之间，意味着搬运的热量可以多于压缩机消耗的电能。The cycle has four key parts: compressor, condenser, expansion valve and evaporator. The compressor squeezes low-pressure refrigerant gas, raising both its pressure and temperature. The hot gas enters the condenser at the back; a fan or natural airflow removes heat, and the refrigerant condenses to a liquid. The expansion valve drops the pressure suddenly, so the refrigerant becomes cold. It then enters the evaporator inside the cabinet, absorbs heat from the food compartment and boils back to gas before returning to the compressor. The heat is pumped out of the box, not destroyed. The coefficient of performance (COP) of a real refrigerator is typically between 2 and 6, meaning it can move more heat than the electrical work it consumes.[2][9]

为什么背面会比室温更热？因为冷凝器不只排出从食物吸来的热，还要排出压缩机做功转化成的热。能量守恒：箱内拿走的热量 Qc 加上电机做功 W，最终都要从背面释放，所以背面排出的是 Qc+W。箱体保温层和门封条把冷室与外界隔开，箱内才能维持 4°C 左右；一旦门封漏气、冷凝器积灰或散热空间被堵，背面会更烫、耗电也会上升。Why is the back hotter than the room? Because the condenser rejects not only the heat taken from the food (Qc) but also the heat equivalent of the compressor's work (W). Energy conservation says Qh = Qc + W, so the back must release both. The insulation and door gaskets keep the cold compartment separated from the room. If a gasket leaks, the condenser is dusty, or the airflow around it is blocked, the back runs hotter and the appliance uses more electricity.[1][2][9]

把菜放进冰箱，目的不是“杀菌”，而是减慢细菌繁殖。USDA 指出，细菌在 40–140°F（约 4–60°C）的“危险温度带”繁殖最快，有的 20 分钟就能翻一倍；冰箱应设在 40°F（约 4°C）或以下。低温只能让多数细菌慢下来，并不能全部杀死，李斯特菌甚至在冷藏温度下仍能缓慢生长；熟食、剩菜和易腐食品要及时冷藏，并注意存放时间。Putting food in the refrigerator is not about killing bacteria; it is about slowing their growth. USDA says bacteria grow fastest between 40 and 140 °F (about 4–60 °C), the “Danger Zone,” with some doubling in as little as 20 minutes; a refrigerator should be set at 40 °F (about 4 °C) or below. Cold storage slows most bacteria but does not kill them all. Listeria, for example, can still multiply slowly at refrigerator temperatures. Leftovers and perishable foods should be chilled promptly and not kept indefinitely.[2]

边界也要说清楚：背面发热是正常现象，但“越热越好”是误解；冷凝器需要清洁和气流，周围不要堆杂物。不是所有冰箱都用同一套压缩循环：吸收式、热电式冰箱原理不同，但“把热从里面搬到外面”这个目标不变。冷藏也不是保险箱：停电、门未关严或温度高于 40°F 超过 2 小时，食物就可能不安全。The boundary matters: a warm back is normal, but “hotter is better” is a misunderstanding. The condenser needs clean coils and airflow, and the surrounding space should not be blocked. Not every refrigerator uses the same cycle—absorption and thermoelectric models work differently—but the goal is still to move heat from inside to outside. Refrigeration is not a safe deposit box either: after a power failure, a loose door, or more than two hours above 40 °F, food may no longer be safe.[1][2][9]

02微波炉和对流烤箱怎样把食物加热？How do microwave ovens and convection ovens heat food?

微波炉让食物中的水分子吸收微波生热；对流烤箱用风扇把热空气吹过食物，路径不同。A microwave drives water molecules in food with electromagnetic energy, while a convection oven blows hot air across the food—two different heat paths.

先给结论：微波炉不是用“热空气”把食物烘热，而是让食物中的水分子直接吸收微波能量，再把这份能量变成热；对流烤箱则相反，它用风扇把已经被加热的空气吹过食物。两条路径都能把食物做熟，但传热方式、上色效果和均匀性不同。Start with the conclusion: a microwave oven does not heat food by blowing hot air. It sends electromagnetic energy into the food, where water molecules absorb it and turn it into heat. A convection oven takes the other path: a fan forces already-heated air across the food. Both can cook, but the heat-transfer route, browning and evenness differ.[3][6]

微波炉的核心是磁控管。它把电能变成 2.45 GHz 左右的微波；2450 MHz 是国际通用的 ISM 加热频段之一，家用微波炉通常工作在这一带。微波进入金属腔体后被腔壁反射，穿过玻璃、纸和多数塑料，却被食物中的水、脂肪等极性分子吸收。水分子随电场来回转动，彼此摩擦，宏观上就是温度上升。微波并不会让食物“从里往外熟”：厚食物外层先被微波加热，内部主要靠热传导慢慢变热，所以中心容易偏冷。The core of a microwave oven is the magnetron. It converts electrical energy into microwaves at about 2.45 GHz; the 2450 MHz band is an ISM allocation, with domestic ovens commonly operating near it. The waves enter a metal cavity, reflect off the walls, pass through glass, paper and many plastics, and are absorbed by water, fat and other polar molecules in food. Water molecules rotate back and forth with the field and rub against their neighbours; macroscopically, the temperature rises. A microwave does not cook a thick food “from the inside out”: the outer layer is heated directly, while the centre warms mainly by conduction, so it can stay cooler.[3][8]

加热不均还有波的因素。腔体内的微波会形成驻波，位置不同，场强不同；转盘、搅拌器或模式搅拌器的作用，就是让食物或波场移动，避免同一块食物总处在弱场区。USDA 提醒，微波烹肉可能留下冷点，细菌会在那里存活；要覆盖食物、烹调中途翻转或搅拌一两次，并用食品温度计在多个位置测量中心温度，别只看“叮”的一声。带壳或带膜的鸡蛋被微波迅速加热时，内部水分汽化成蒸汽，壳与卵膜像密封锅盖把蒸汽封住，内部压力超过承受极限就会爆裂；加热前打破壳、刺破卵膜或留出排气口，才能让蒸汽泄压。Heating is also uneven because the cavity contains standing waves: field strength varies with position. A turntable, stirrer or mode stirrer moves the food or the pattern so that no part stays in a weak spot. USDA warns that microwave-cooked meat can leave cold spots where bacteria survive. Cover the food, rotate or stir it once or twice, and use a food thermometer in several places rather than trusting the beep.When an egg in shell or membrane heats quickly, internal water vaporizes into steam; the shell and membrane seal it like a lid, and once internal pressure exceeds their limit it bursts. Breaking the shell, piercing the membrane or leaving a vent lets steam escape.[3][4]

对流烤箱的路径不同。普通烤箱主要靠上下加热元件辐射和自然对流；厂商的说明指出，对流烤箱加了一个风扇，把热空气强制吹过食物，真正对流（true convection）还在背部风扇周围加了第三个加热元件。流动的空气不断把食物表面那层又冷又湿的边界层吹走，传热更快，表面上色更均匀，多盘同时烤也更容易一致。A convection oven works differently. A conventional oven relies mainly on radiant heat from top and bottom elements and on natural air movement. Manufacturer guidance explains that a convection oven adds a fan that forces hot air across the food; a true convection model also has a third heating element around the fan at the back. The moving air strips away the cool, moist boundary layer at the food surface, so heat transfer is faster, browning is more even, and several racks cook more consistently.[6]

两者的边界值得记住：微波炉快，适合含水多、厚度小的食物，但不容易上色，金属餐具不能放；对流烤箱擅长褐变、酥脆和多层同烤，但预热、能耗和时间通常更高，风太强也会让蛋糕、酥皮等对湿度敏感的食物失水过快。无论哪种，安全都不只看时间：中心温度要达标，微波加热要覆盖、静置、测温，对流烤箱也要避免外焦里生。Remember the boundaries: a microwave is fast for water-rich, thin foods, but it does not brown well and cannot accept metal utensils. A convection oven excels at browning, crispness and multi-rack cooking, but preheating, energy use and time are usually higher, and a strong fan can dry delicate cakes or pastry. In both cases, safety is not just about time: the centre must reach a safe temperature; microwave food needs covering, standing and thermometer checks, and convection cooking must avoid a burnt outside and a raw inside.[3][4][6]

03微波炉为什么用金属做腔体，却不能放金属餐具？Wi‑Fi 又为什么常被说成安全？Why is a microwave oven built from metal but cannot hold metal utensils—and why is Wi‑Fi often called safe?

金属腔体反射微波形成屏蔽，金属餐具却在腔内反射和集中场；Wi‑Fi 低功率非电离，安全是“低于限值”而非零风险。A metal cavity reflects microwaves to form a shield, while metal utensils reflect and concentrate the field inside; Wi-Fi is low-power non-ionizing RF, and safety means below limits, not

先给结论：微波炉用金属做腔体，是因为金属能把微波反射回腔内，形成一道“笼子”；不能把金属餐具放进去，是因为餐具也在腔内，会把微波反射到别处、集中到尖角，造成加热不均甚至损坏。Wi‑Fi 常被说成“安全”，准确说法是：它是低功率、非电离的射频，在合规暴露水平下没有一致的不良健康证据；这不等于“绝对零风险”。Start with the conclusion: a microwave oven is built from metal because metal reflects microwaves back into the cavity, forming a shield. Metal utensils are a different matter: they sit inside the cavity, reflect the waves elsewhere and can concentrate the field at edges, causing uneven heating or damage. Wi-Fi is often called “safe,” but the accurate statement is narrower: it is low-power, non-ionizing radio frequency, and at compliance levels there is no consistent evidence of adverse health effects. That is not the same as absolute zero risk.[3][5][7]

微波炉的金属内壁不是装饰。磁控管产生的微波在腔体里来回反射，最后被食物吸收；门上的金属网、门封和两道互锁把能量关在里面。FDA 的标准把泄漏限制在约 2 英寸处 5 mW/cm²，并要求开门时立即停止产生微波；门铰链、门封损坏就不要继续用。金属腔体在这里是屏蔽层，不是“餐具”。The metal walls are not decoration. Microwaves produced by the magnetron bounce around the cavity until food absorbs them. The door mesh, door seals and two independent interlocks keep the energy inside. FDA limits leakage to about 5 mW/cm² at roughly 2 inches from the surface and requires generation to stop when the door is opened. If hinges, latches or seals are damaged, stop using the oven. The metal cavity is a shield, not a utensil.[3]

金属餐具则是另一回事。FDA 明确说，金属锅、铝箔会反射微波，让食物加热不均，还可能损坏烤箱；尖锐边缘、揉皱的铝箔还可能让电场集中，出现打火。玻璃、陶瓷、纸和部分塑料能让微波通过，所以适合做容器；但容器本身不吸微波，会靠食物变热，部分塑料可能被食物烫熔。最稳妥的做法是看厂商说明书，不要凭“看起来像微波炉专用”猜。Metal utensils are the opposite case. FDA says metal pans and aluminium foil reflect microwaves, cook food unevenly and may damage the oven; sharp edges or crumpled foil can also concentrate the electric field and cause arcing. Glass, ceramic, paper and some plastics pass microwaves, so they are suitable containers—though they are not heated by the field itself and can become hot from the food. Some plastics may melt. Follow the manufacturer's instructions rather than guessing from appearance.[3]

Wi‑Fi 的情况要分开看。Wi‑Fi 和微波炉都使用射频，但 Wi‑Fi 接入点典型功率约 0.1 W，而微波炉把高功率关在一个腔体里；Wi‑Fi 的功率分散到空间，距离一远就迅速衰减。手机射频大致在 450–2700 MHz、峰值功率约 0.1–2 W；ARPANSA 说，Wi‑Fi 设备低功率，现有证据不支持在限值以下出现不良健康效应；WHO 对手机射频的评估也认为，低于组织加热水平的暴露没有一致的不良健康证据，同时把射频列为 2B“可能致癌”，意思是证据有限、不能排除偶然和偏差。Wi-Fi needs a separate frame. Wi-Fi and microwave ovens both use radio frequency, but a Wi-Fi access point typically runs at about 0.1 W, while a microwave oven confines high power in a cavity. Wi-Fi power is spread through space and falls rapidly with distance. Mobile-phone RF spans roughly 450–2700 MHz with peak powers of about 0.1–2 W. ARPANSA says Wi-Fi devices are low-powered and that current evidence does not establish adverse health effects below exposure limits. WHO, assessing mobile-phone RF, found no consistent adverse effects below tissue-heating levels, while IARC classified RF as Group 2B, “possibly carcinogenic”—limited evidence, with chance, bias and confounding not ruled out.[5][7][10]

所以不要把“Wi‑Fi 安全”理解成“微波炉也安全”，也不要把“微波炉危险”理解成“Wi‑Fi 危险”。关键变量是功率、距离、暴露时间和约束方式：微波炉把高功率关在金属腔里，开门或门封损坏才可能泄漏；Wi‑Fi 把低功率散布在环境里，合规设备通常远低于限值。合理的做法是修好坏门封、别在微波炉里放金属、把路由器放在离人稍远的位置；对“绝对安全”或“绝对有害”的口号都保持怀疑。So do not read “Wi-Fi is safe” as “a microwave oven is harmless,” or “microwave ovens are dangerous” as “Wi-Fi is dangerous.” The key variables are power, distance, duration and confinement. A microwave oven keeps high power inside a metal box; leakage matters mainly when the door or seal is damaged. Wi-Fi spreads low power through the environment, and compliant equipment normally sits far below the limits. Sensible precautions are to repair a damaged door seal, keep metal out of the microwave, and place the router a little farther from where people sit—while distrusting absolute slogans on either side.[3][5][7]
