01核能是怎样从原子核里释放出来的？How is nuclear energy released from the atomic nucleus?

重核裂变时质量亏损按 E=mc² 放出能量；一次裂变约 200 MeV，反应堆靠控制中子维持链式反应。Heavy nuclei fission and convert mass defect to energy via E=mc²; one fission releases about 200 MeV, and reactors control neutrons to sustain the chain reaction.

核能来自原子核内的质量亏损。重核裂变或轻核聚变时，产物总质量小于反应物，差额按 E=mc² 换算成释放能量。商用核电靠铀-235（少量钚-239）裂变：中子击中可裂变核，原子核分裂成两个中等质量裂变碎片，放出 2–3 个中子和 gamma 射线，同时把核结合能差以碎片动能和热能释放。这里讲的是裂变，聚变发电目前尚未商用。Nuclear energy comes from mass defect inside the nucleus. When heavy nuclei fission or light ones fuse, the products weigh less than the reactants, and the missing mass converts to energy through E=mc². Commercial nuclear power relies on uranium-235, with some plutonium-239, fission: a neutron strikes a fissile nucleus, which splits into two medium-mass fragments, releases 2–3 neutrons and gamma rays, and turns the nuclear binding-energy difference into fragment kinetic energy and heat. This chapter covers fission; fusion power is not yet commercial.[2][4]

量级上，一次铀-235 裂变约释放 200 MeV，约 3.2×10⁻¹¹ J；1 克铀-235 完全裂变约释放 8×10¹⁰ J，折合两万多千瓦时，能量密度远高于化学燃料。煤燃烧释放的是电子重组的化学能，每个原子的能量差在电子伏特量级；裂变差出约百万倍。反应堆靠控制中子数量维持可控链式反应，燃料浓缩度、慢化剂和临界条件决定反应能否自持。On the numbers, one uranium-235 fission releases about 200 MeV, roughly 3.2×10⁻¹¹ J; one gram of fully fissioned U-235 yields about 8×10¹⁰ J, over twenty thousand kilowatt-hours, an energy density far above chemical fuels. Coal burns through electron rearrangement, an energy difference on the electron-volt scale per atom, about a million times smaller. A reactor keeps the chain reaction controlled by managing the neutron population; enrichment, moderator and critical conditions determine whether the reaction can sustain itself.[1][2]

裂变产物与用途。裂变碎片多为放射性核素，需要屏蔽与乏燃料管理；裂变热先把水加热成蒸汽，推动汽轮发电机，和火电的末段流程相同。OpenStax 化学教材把核反应方程、质量数守恒与结合能作为基础框架；美国能源部核能页面说明反应堆如何把裂变热用于发电。Fission products and use. Most fragments are radioactive nuclides requiring shielding and spent-fuel management; fission heat first boils water into steam that drives a turbine generator, sharing the back end with thermal power plants. The OpenStax chemistry text frames nuclear equations, mass-number conservation and binding energy, while the U.S. Department of Energy nuclear pages explain how reactor fission heat generates electricity.[1][2]

武器装置与反应堆的差别。两者都基于链式反应，差别在于中子增殖速率、材料形态与设计目的：反应堆维持在临界附近的稳定功率，有慢化剂和控制棒随时吸收中子；爆炸装置要求在极短时间内达到超临界并迅速释放能量。历史上两类装置分别使用高浓缩铀和钚材料，材料纯度、构型与组装方式不同；这里只比较物理原理，不展开武器设计细节。Weapons devices and reactors differ. Both use chain reactions, but in neutron growth rate, material form and design purpose: a reactor holds near-critical stable power, with moderator and control rods absorbing neutrons at any time; an explosive device reaches supercriticality in a very short interval and releases energy rapidly. Historical devices used highly enriched uranium and plutonium in different purities, geometries and assemblies; this comparison stays at the physical level and does not cover weapons design detail.[1][2][4]

边界：裂变能来自核力结合能，不是“原子里藏着火”；聚变与裂变是两种不同过程，当前商用发电全部来自裂变。能量密度高不等于单位电量成本低，核电的燃料成本只占一部分，建造、安全与废物管理是主要开销。放射性产物的衰变热与辐射防护，是核能与化石能源最根本的差别。Boundary: fission energy comes from nuclear binding energy, not from fire stored inside the atom; fusion and fission are distinct processes, and all commercial power today uses fission. High energy density does not mean low unit cost: fuel is only part of nuclear expense, with construction, safety and waste management the main outlays. Decay heat from radioactive products and radiation shielding are the most fundamental differences from fossil fuels.[1][2]

02为什么潜艇和航母用核动力，民用交通工具却很少采用？Why do submarines and carriers use nuclear power while civilian vehicles rarely do?

核动力靠高能量密度换取长续航；屏蔽重量、安全监管与扩散成本在小型交通工具上难以摊薄，RTG 则是衰变电池。Nuclear propulsion trades high energy density for endurance; shielding, regulation and proliferation costs are hard to amortize small-scale, while RTGs are decay batteries.

核动力舰艇。潜艇和航空母舰用压水堆把裂变热转化为蒸汽，驱动蒸汽轮机；核动力的优势是能量密度高、不需要频繁补充燃料，潜艇可以长期潜航，航母省去燃油舱和大烟囱。堆芯寿命可达一二十年，航程几乎不受燃料限制。民用车辆和商船很少采用核动力，是因为屏蔽重量、辐射安全、核扩散监管和事故责任成本，在小吨位交通工具上难以摊薄。Nuclear-powered ships. Submarines and aircraft carriers use pressurized-water reactors to turn fission heat into steam for steam turbines. The advantage is high energy density and infrequent refueling: submarines stay submerged for long stretches, and carriers save fuel tanks and a large funnel. Core life can reach one or two decades, with range almost free of fuel limits. Civilian cars and merchant ships rarely use nuclear power because shielding weight, radiation safety, nonproliferation oversight and accident-liability costs are hard to amortize on small vessels.[1][4]

放射性同位素电池与裂变堆不同。放射性同位素热电发生器（RTG）不发生链式反应，而是靠钚-238 等核素衰变放热，经热电元件直接转成电力。它无转动部件、寿命长，功率从几十瓦到几百瓦，用于深空探测器、边远站点，历史上也曾用于心脏起搏器。衰变热功率随半衰期衰减，钚-238 半衰期约 87.7 年；近年讨论的“放射性金刚石电池”仍是把碳-14 等衰变能量封装起来，原理属于同位素衰变，不是微型裂变堆。Radioisotope batteries differ from fission reactors. A radioisotope thermoelectric generator (RTG) runs no chain reaction; it draws decay heat from nuclides such as plutonium-238 and converts it directly to electricity through thermoelectric elements. With no moving parts and a long life, it produces tens to a few hundred watts for deep-space probes and remote sites, and historically for cardiac pacemakers. Decay power falls with half-life; Pu-238 has a half-life of about 87.7 years. The proposed radioactive diamond battery still packages decay energy from carbon-14 or similar nuclides, a decay source rather than a miniature fission reactor.[1][2]

燃料运输与长期工程。铀燃料从矿冶、浓缩到燃料组件，放射性水平逐段变化：低浓铀燃料芯块本身辐射不强，主要危险来自浓缩工艺、乏燃料的高放射性和临界管理。运输靠专用容器、车辆和辐射监测；乏燃料容器设计要承受撞击、火灾和浸水。反应堆退役和大型掩蔽结构是另一类长期工程：切尔诺贝利新安全拱覆盖旧石棺，设计使用年限以数十年到百年计，因为钢混构件在辐射和环境作用下会老化。Fuel transport and long-lived structures. From mining and milling to enrichment and fuel assemblies, radiation levels change stage by stage: low-enriched uranium pellets are only mildly radioactive themselves, while the main hazards lie in enrichment processes, highly radioactive spent fuel and criticality management. Transport uses dedicated casks, vehicles and radiation monitoring; spent-fuel casks must survive impact, fire and immersion. Decommissioning and large containment structures are another long-term engineering problem: the Chernobyl New Safe Confinement covers the old sarcophagus, with a design life measured in decades to a century, because steel and concrete age under radiation and the environment.[1][4]

证据与边界对照。美国能源部核能页面与 OpenStax 教材覆盖反应堆原理；同位素电池属于衰变能利用，与裂变链式反应分开。核动力舰艇的续航优势来自能量密度，代价是堆体重量、屏蔽和维护体系；把“核潜艇能跑几十年”理解成小型核动力可以随便装上汽车，忽略了安全监管和屏蔽重量的工程约束。Evidence and boundary check. DOE nuclear pages and the OpenStax text cover reactor principles; isotope batteries use decay energy and are separate from fission chain reactions. The endurance advantage of naval reactors comes from energy density, at the cost of reactor mass, shielding and maintenance systems. Reading “a submarine runs for decades” as meaning small nuclear power can be put into cars freely ignores the engineering constraints of regulation and shielding weight.[1][2][4]

边界：本类应用都属于工程与监管问题，不是单纯能量问题。民用小型核动力的进展受成本、公众接受度和核不扩散框架限制；放射性同位素材料的获取与使用受严格管控。这里不评估具体电站的事故责任，也不讨论武器扩散路径。Boundary: these applications are engineering and regulatory problems, not pure energy questions. Small civilian nuclear power advances are constrained by cost, public acceptance and nonproliferation frameworks; radioisotope materials are strictly controlled. This chapter does not assign accident liability to specific plants or discuss weapons proliferation paths.[1][2]

03光伏为什么越来越便宜，单块电池的效率极限是多少？Why has PV become cheaper, and what limits a single cell's efficiency?

规模与工艺改进压低组件价格；单结 SQ 极限约 33%，实验室硅电池约 27%，量产组件多为 20–22%。Scale and process improvements cut module prices; the single-junction SQ limit is about 33%, lab silicon cells near 27%, production modules mostly 20–22%.

光伏为什么变便宜。太阳能发电成本下降来自产业链规模、硅料提纯、切片工艺和电池结构改进共同作用：多晶硅产量提升摊薄材料成本，薄硅片减少用料，PERC、TOPCon、HJT 等结构提高效率。过去几十年组件价格随装机规模长期下行，是学习曲线效应，而不是某一种材料的单点突破。Why photovoltaics became cheaper. Falling solar costs come from industrial scale, silicon purification, wafering and cell-structure improvements working together: rising polysilicon output amortizes material cost, thinner wafers cut material use, and PERC, TOPCon and HJT structures raise efficiency. Module prices have declined for decades along with installed capacity as a learning-curve effect, not from one material breakthrough.[3]

效率极限。单结硅电池受 Shockley–Queisser 详细平衡模型限制，理论上限约 33%；在硅材料最优带隙附近，单结上限约 29–30%。实验室硅电池纪录已到 27% 上下，工业组件实际效率多在 20–22%，实验室与量产之间有封装、互连和温度损失。叠层电池用不同带隙材料吸收不同波段，可以突破单结极限，但成本和工艺更复杂。Efficiency limits. A single-junction silicon cell is bounded by the Shockley–Queisser detailed-balance model, with a theoretical ceiling near 33%; around silicon's optimal bandgap the single-junction limit is about 29–30%. Laboratory silicon cells reach records near 27%, while industrial modules run mostly 20–22%, with packaging, interconnection and temperature losses between lab and production. Tandem cells use different bandgaps for separate bands and can exceed the single-junction limit, at higher cost and process complexity.[3][5]

全生命周期。光伏板在制造、运输、安装、运行和回收各阶段都有能耗与排放；运行阶段不燃烧燃料，生命周期碳排放主要来自硅料冶炼、玻璃铝材和运输。随电力清洁度提高和组件寿命延长，单位电量的隐含排放持续下降；回收体系不完善时，报废组件的玻璃、硅片和封装材料会成为固体废弃物问题，需要拆解工艺与材料再利用配套。Life cycle. Panels carry energy use and emissions across manufacturing, transport, installation, operation and recycling; operation burns no fuel, so lifecycle emissions come mainly from silicon smelting, glass and aluminum, and transport. As the grid cleans up and panel lifetimes extend, embodied emissions per kilowatt-hour keep falling; where recycling systems are weak, end-of-life glass, wafers and encapsulation become solid-waste problems.[3][4]

与其他能源对照。OpenStax 大学物理把太阳辐射列为可再生能源的原始来源；美国能源部光伏研究页面给出实验室与量产效率区间；PMC 综述说明单结器件尚未达到 SQ 极限。光伏出力随昼夜、云量和季节波动，需要电网调节或储能配合；单位面积功率受纬度、倾角和遮挡影响，不能把峰值功率当成全天平均输出。Comparison with other sources. The OpenStax university physics text lists solar radiation as the original source of renewable energy; the DOE photovoltaics page gives lab and production efficiency ranges; a PMC review notes single-junction devices have not yet reached the SQ limit. PV output swings with day-night, cloud cover and season, needing grid balancing or storage; power per area depends on latitude, tilt and shading, so peak rating cannot be treated as an all-day average.[3][4][5]

边界：效率百分比是在标准测试条件（1000 W/m²、25°C、AM1.5）下测得，真实场景因温度、积灰和阴影打折。成本下降不意味着零成本，支架、逆变器、土地和并网都计入度电成本；生命周期排放估算随电网电力结构变化，不能用某一地区的制造数据直接代表所有产线。Boundary: efficiency percentages are measured under standard test conditions, 1000 W/m², 25 °C, AM1.5, and real scenes lose ground to temperature, dust and shade. Lower cost does not mean zero cost: racks, inverters, land and grid connection all enter levelized cost; lifecycle emissions estimates shift with grid electricity mix, and one region's manufacturing data cannot represent every production line.[3][5]

04输电线能拉多长，为什么不能随意往电网注入超大功率？How long can transmission lines run, and why cannot huge power be forced into the grid?

电网需实时平衡有功与无功；高压降低线损，美国输配损耗约 5%，电池串并联成阵列，化石储量是动态估算。The grid balances active and reactive power in real time; high voltage cuts line losses near 5% in the U.S., batteries form series-parallel arrays, and fossil reserves are dynamic estimates.

电网实时平衡。发电和用电必须在同一时刻匹配：频率由有功功率平衡决定，电压由无功功率支撑。往电网硬注入超大功率而没有调度、负荷和储能承接，会造成频率越限、电压波动，甚至触发保护切除。输电线路有电阻和电抗，电流通过时发热损耗；EIA 估算美国输配环节损耗约占传输电量的 5% 上下，距离越长、电流越大，线损越高。Real-time grid balance. Generation and consumption must match at every instant: frequency is set by active-power balance, voltage by reactive-power support. Forcing very large power into the grid without dispatch, load or storage to absorb it drives frequency and voltage outside limits and can trigger protective disconnection. Lines carry resistance and reactance, so current heats the conductor and loses energy; EIA estimates U.S. transmission and distribution losses near 5% of transmitted electricity, rising with distance and current.[6][7]

线路能拉多长。没有绝对长度上限，但提高电压等级可以降低电流、减少 I²R 损耗，因此长距离输电用高压、特高压交流或直流。交流距离受同步稳定性和无功限制，直流更适合上千公里点对点送电；线路还要考虑绝缘子、风偏、走廊和地质条件。线路长度本身不是硬约束，经济距离由损耗、造价和两端负荷决定。How long a line can run. There is no absolute length ceiling, but higher voltage lowers current and cuts I²R losses, so long-distance transmission uses high or ultra-high-voltage AC or DC. AC distance is limited by synchronism and reactive power, while DC suits point-to-point delivery over a thousand kilometres; insulators, wind sway, corridors and geology also constrain routing. Length itself is not a hard limit; the economic distance follows losses, capital cost and the loads at both ends.[6][7]

电池阵列与资源。储能电池阵列把成百上千个电芯串并联成模组和电池柜：串联提高电压，并联提高容量。圆柱电芯工艺成熟、一致性好、散热结构规整，适合自动化量产和标准化更换；方形和软包装电芯在能量密度上各有取舍。化石能源剩余量按已探明储量和产量估算，石油储量寿命随勘探、价格和替代能源变化；EIA 的储量与产量数据只能给出当前水平下的粗略年限，不是“还能用多少年”的确定预测。Battery arrays and resources. A storage battery array strings hundreds of cells into modules and cabinets: series raises voltage, parallel raises capacity. Cylindrical cells have mature manufacturing, good consistency and regular cooling structures suited to automated production and standardized replacement; prismatic and pouch cells trade off differently on energy density. Remaining fossil resources are estimated from proven reserves and production, and oil reserve life shifts with exploration, prices and alternatives; EIA reserve and production data give only a rough lifespan at current levels, not a fixed prediction of how many years remain.[6][8]

电网调度与过冬。冬季寒潮同时推高采暖负荷和风电波动，电网调度要在发电备用、联络线通道和需求响应之间平衡；备用容量按峰值负荷和单机故障预留。EIA 电力页面说明发电、输电、配电的基本链条；资源估算章节说明化石燃料储量是动态概念。电池储能在秒到小时尺度平抑波动，跨季节储能仍需其他手段。Grid dispatch and winter operation. Cold waves raise heating load and wind variability at the same time, so dispatch balances generation reserve, interconnection capacity and demand response; reserve margins are set against peak load and single-unit failure. The EIA electricity pages lay out the generation, transmission and distribution chain, and the resource section treats fossil reserves as a dynamic concept. Battery storage smooths second-to-hour fluctuations, while cross-season storage still needs other means.[6][8]

边界：线损百分比随电压等级和电网结构变化，跨国比较不能直接套用；储能循环寿命、温度管理和安全是独立议题。化石能源剩余年限取决于未来产量、新发现和替代速度，不应把储量寿命解读为资源即将枯竭或永不枯竭。电网稳定性是系统问题，单点加大电源或电池不能替代整体调度。Boundary: loss percentages vary with voltage level and grid structure and cannot be copied across countries; storage cycle life, thermal management and safety are separate issues. Remaining fossil years depend on future production, new discoveries and substitution, so reserve life should not be read as imminent exhaustion or permanent plenty. Grid stability is a system problem, and adding a single source or battery cannot replace overall dispatch.[7][8]

05电池为什么会鼓包？析锂、产气和热失控之间是什么关系？Why does a battery swell, and how do lithium plating, gas generation and thermal runaway relate?

SEI 增厚与析锂后电解液产气，压力堆积使电芯鼓包；温升越过临界后进入热失控。SEI thickening and lithium plating precede electrolyte gas release; pressure bulges the cell, and crossing the threshold triggers runaway.

用了较长时间的手机、充电宝外壳鼓起，拆开常看到软包电芯发胀；鼓包是内部产气和结构受压的外在表现，继续使用有漏液、起火的风险。锂离子电池在正常充放电中本就伴随界面反应，鼓包说明内部已经偏离健康状态，需要从电极界面、气体来源和温度演化几个环节看。A long-used phone or power bank may swell, and opening it often shows a bulging pouch cell. Bulging is the visible sign of internal gas generation and mechanical pressure; keeping it in use risks leakage and fire. A Li-ion cell already undergoes interface reactions during normal cycling, so bulging means the internal state has drifted from healthy; the explanation runs through electrode interfaces, gas sources and temperature evolution.[4]

充放电过程中，负极表面会形成一层固体电解质界面膜（SEI），把电解液和负极隔开，稳定界面；这层膜会随循环反复破裂、再生而逐渐增厚，消耗活性锂。过充、低温或大电流充电时，锂离子来不及嵌入石墨层，会在负极表面析出金属锂，也就是析锂；活性锂减少、阻抗上升，电池容量和安全性一起下降。健康的 SEI 薄而致密，允许锂离子通过、阻挡溶剂分子；反复循环后膜层变厚变脆，内阻随之增大。During charging and discharging, a solid electrolyte interphase (SEI) forms on the anode, separating electrolyte from anode and stabilizing the interface. This film cracks and reforms with cycles, thickening gradually and consuming active lithium. Under overcharging, low temperature or fast current, lithium ions cannot intercalate into graphite in time and plate as metallic lithium on the anode surface, called lithium plating; active lithium drops and impedance rises, so capacity and safety decline together.[9]

产气来自界面副反应。SEI 破裂再生、电解液在负极和正极表面分解，会释放氢气、二氧化碳、甲烷等气体；软包电芯铝塑膜刚性低，气体排不出去就在壳内积累，把膜顶起形成肉眼可见的鼓包。圆柱电芯有金属壳体约束，内部产气更多表现为压力上升和安全阀动作，外观不一定明显鼓起。产气不仅来自负极，正极在高温或过充下分解也会贡献气体，具体成分随老化状态变化。Gas comes from side reactions at the interface. SEI cracking and reformation, plus electrolyte decomposition on anode and cathode, release gases such as hydrogen, carbon dioxide and methane. A pouch cell's aluminum-laminate film is not rigid; trapped gas builds up inside and pushes the film outward into a visible bulge. Cylindrical cells are constrained by metal shells, so internal gas more often shows as pressure rise and valve venting rather than an obvious external bulge.[9][10]

温度继续上升会进入热失控。电芯发热使副反应速率加快，温度升到约 80–120 ℃ 区间后，隔膜收缩、电解液与正极分解放热形成正反馈；典型阶段包括界面失效、隔膜破坏、电解液分解和正极释氧。气体先造成变形鼓包，后期才可能泄压、起火；热失控是一组相继触发的放热反应，不是某一个零件单独过热。As temperature keeps rising, the cell enters thermal runaway. Heat speeds up side reactions, and above roughly 80–120 ℃ the separator shrinks while electrolyte and cathode decomposition release heat in a positive feedback loop. Typical stages are interface failure, separator breakdown, electrolyte decomposition and cathode oxygen release. Gas first deforms and bulges the cell; venting or fire may come later. Thermal runaway is a chain of triggered exothermic reactions, not one part overheating alone.[9][10]

边界要写清：鼓包主要由老化、过热、过充和机械损伤引起，本章不展开电池管理系统的保护电路设计。磷酸铁锂、三元等不同化学体系的临界温度和产气成分有差异，不能套用同一温度阈值；鼓包电芯应停止使用并按规范回收，不要穿刺、挤压或继续充电。Boundary: bulging mainly stems from aging, overheating, overcharging and mechanical damage; this chapter does not cover BMS protection-circuit design. LFP, NMC and other chemistries differ in threshold temperature and gas composition, so a single temperature cutoff cannot be applied across them. A bulged cell should be retired and recycled properly: do not puncture, crush or keep charging it.[4][10]

06风力发电机怎样发出电来？How does a wind turbine generate electricity?

风吹过叶片产生升力推动转子转动，经齿轮箱或直驱升速带动发电机，转子在磁场中切割磁感线感应出电流，变流后送入电网。Lift on the blades turns the rotor; a gearbox or direct drive speeds it to the generator, whose rotor cuts field lines and induces current converted to grid power.

风力发电的起点是风的动能。流动的空气携带动能吹向风轮，叶片把这部分动能转化为转轴的转动。和飞机机翼类似，叶片截面做成翼型，气流绕过时上下表面流速不同、产生压力差，形成升力；升力沿圆周方向的分量推动叶轮绕轮毂旋转，风越大，在一定范围内转速和出力越高。Wind power starts from the kinetic energy of moving air. Flow carries kinetic energy toward the rotor, and the blades convert this energy into shaft rotation. Like an aircraft wing, each blade has an airfoil cross-section; as airflow passes, upper and lower surfaces move at different speeds and create a pressure difference that gives lift. The tangential component of this lift turns the rotor around the hub, and within a range, higher wind speed raises rotation and output.[11]

转动要经过传动系统送到发电机。传统结构里，叶轮低转速主轴带动齿轮箱，把转速升到发电机所需的较高转速；直驱结构则去掉齿轮箱，叶轮直接连接低速发电机。机舱内还包含制动、偏航对风、润滑和冷却等辅助系统，让风轮始终对准来风方向并在安全转速内运行。叶片细长并带扭转，是为了在不同半径处都保持合适的迎角；偏航机构让机舱随风向转动，使风轮始终正对来流，吸收更多风能。The rotation passes through a drivetrain to the generator. In a traditional layout, the low-speed main shaft drives a gearbox that raises speed to what the generator needs; direct-drive designs remove the gearbox and connect the rotor straight to a low-speed generator. The nacelle also holds braking, yaw, lubrication and cooling systems that keep the rotor facing the wind and running at a safe speed.[4][11]

发电本身靠电磁感应。发电机转子在磁场中转动，导体切割磁感线，线圈里感应出交流电；风机发出的电频率和电压需要调整，经变流装置整流再逆变成符合电网要求的交流电，通过变压器升压后送入输电网络。整个链条是风的动能→叶轮机械能→转子转动→电能，与光伏直接靠半导体光生伏特的路径不同。风机输出的交流电频率随风速波动，必须经电力电子装置变换成与电网同频同压的交流电，才能稳定并入公共电网。Generation itself relies on electromagnetic induction. The generator rotor turns in a magnetic field, conductors cut field lines, and alternating current is induced in the coils. Frequency and voltage from the turbine need adjustment; a converter rectifies and inverts the output into grid-compatible AC, which a transformer steps up before it enters the transmission network. The chain runs wind kinetic energy to rotor mechanical energy to rotation to electricity, differing from photovoltaic's semiconductor photovoltaic path.[4][11]

风速决定出力区间。风速低于切入风速，约 3–4 m/s，风轮转不起来或出力太小；达到额定风速，约 12–15 m/s 附近，风机达到标称功率；风速过高时需要变桨或制动保护，防止超速损坏。现代陆基风机叶轮直径可达百米级，轮毂高度常为数十米到上百米，容量因子通常约 35–45%，出力随风速和来流条件波动。Wind speed sets the output range. Below the cut-in speed, about 3 to 4 m/s, the rotor cannot turn or produces too little; around the rated wind speed, about 12 to 15 m/s, the turbine reaches its nominal power; at very high speed, pitch control or braking protects against overspeed. Modern land-based turbines have rotor diameters near a hundred metres, hub heights of tens to over a hundred metres, and a capacity factor commonly around 35 to 45 percent, with output fluctuating with wind and inflow conditions.[6][11]

边界要写清：风能出力不稳定、随时间波动，需要电网调节或储能配合平抑。本章只讲风能到电能的转换链条，不展开机位选址、风资源评估、噪声与生态影响，也不讨论具体机型参数和经济性比较；评估一座风电场还要结合当地风况、电网条件和环境约束。The boundary matters: wind output is unstable and fluctuates over time, requiring grid regulation or storage to smooth it. This chapter covers only the wind-to-electricity conversion chain, not site selection, wind-resource assessment, noise or ecological impact, or specific machine parameters and cost comparisons. Evaluating a wind farm also needs local wind conditions, grid conditions and environmental constraints.[6]
