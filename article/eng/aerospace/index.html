01涡桨飞机为什么多用于支线和短途航线？Why are turboprops mostly used on regional and short routes?

低速下螺旋桨排开大质量空气、尾流动能损失小，推进效率高；桨尖接近马赫 1 出现激波，高速区间让给涡扇。At low speed the propeller moves a large air mass with little wake energy, giving high efficiency; near Mach 1 tip shocks limit it, so high speed belongs to turbofans.

螺旋桨靠桨叶把一大团空气低速向后推，获得向前的反作用推力。推进效率取决于发动机把多少功率转化为推进功：低速飞行时，螺旋桨排开的空气质量大、排气速度低，浪费在尾流动能上的功率少，推进效率高。涡桨飞机巡航速度通常在 300–500 km/h 区间，正好利用这一优势；支线航线航程短、起降频繁，对燃油经济性和短跑道性能敏感，涡桨因此占优。A propeller pushes a large mass of air backward at low speed and gets forward thrust by reaction. Propulsive efficiency depends on how much engine power becomes useful propulsive work: at low flight speed the propeller handles a large air mass with a modest exhaust speed, leaving little power wasted as wake kinetic energy, so efficiency is high. Turboprops cruise around 300–500 km/h, exactly where this advantage applies. Short regional routes fly often with take-off and landing, and are sensitive to fuel economy and short-field performance, which favours the turboprop.[4][5]

速度提高后，螺旋桨遇到桨尖激波问题。桨叶旋转时桨尖相对空气的速度是旋转线速度与飞机前进速度的叠加，桨尖马赫数接近 1 时出现压缩性激波，阻力骤增、效率下降并产生噪声。这就是涡桨不适合高速干线飞行的原因：涡扇发动机把风扇包在涵道内，用更小的排气速度匹配高速来流，巡航速度可达 800–900 km/h。两类发动机的效率最优区间不同，不是谁绝对更好。As speed rises, the propeller hits tip-shock problems. Blade-tip speed relative to air combines rotational tip speed with forward flight speed; when the tip Mach number approaches 1, a compressibility shock appears, drag rises sharply, efficiency falls and noise grows. This is why turboprops do not suit high-speed trunk routes: a turbofan encloses its fan in a duct and matches a lower exhaust speed to the high-speed incoming flow, cruising at 800–900 km/h. The two engines have different optimum efficiency ranges rather than one being universally better.[5]

支线短途选择涡桨还有运营层面的理由。涡桨发动机结构相对简单、油耗低，对短跑道和未铺装跑道的适应能力强；1000–1500 km 以内的航线上，爬升和下降阶段占比高，高速巡航的时间收益有限，涡桨的低速效率更划算。航程拉长后，高空高速巡航的燃油与时间优势转向涡扇，干线客机普遍采用高涵道比涡扇。Short regional routes favour turboprops for operational reasons too. The engine is comparatively simple and fuel-efficient, and adapts well to short and unpaved runways. On routes under 1000–1500 km, climb and descent take a large share of the flight, so little time is gained by high-speed cruise and low-speed propeller efficiency pays off. As the route lengthens, high-altitude high-speed cruise gives turbofans the fuel and time advantage, and trunk airliners use high-bypass turbofans.[5][7]

NASA 航空教学材料把推进分解为推力、功率与效率三个量：推力来自动量变化，功率是单位时间做功，推进效率衡量推进功占发动机输出功率的比例。螺旋桨本质是旋转的机翼，桨叶截面同样有迎角和升力，只是升力方向被布置成前进方向。理解桨叶气动与机翼气动是同一套翼型理论，区别只在运动方式和速度范围。NASA aeronautics teaching breaks propulsion into thrust, power and efficiency: thrust comes from momentum change, power is work per unit time, and propulsive efficiency measures propulsive work against engine output. A propeller is a rotating wing: its airfoil sections also have angle of attack and lift, with the lift direction arranged forward. Propeller aerodynamics and wing aerodynamics are the same airfoil theory, differing only in motion and speed range.[4][5]

边界：涡桨效率高是低速区间的结论，不能外推到高速；桨尖速度而非飞机前进速度决定激波临界。涡桨与涡扇的选择还受噪音、维护、载客量和航线距离影响，单纯按油耗排序并不全面。螺旋桨产生推力靠的是空气动量变化，不是“桨叶把空气搅热”，尾流温度变化不是推力的主要来源。Boundary: high turboprop efficiency is a low-speed conclusion that cannot be extrapolated to high speed; the tip speed, not the forward speed, sets the shock threshold. Engine choice also depends on noise, maintenance, seat count and route length, so ranking by fuel burn alone is incomplete. Propeller thrust comes from air momentum change, not from heating the air; wake temperature rise is not the main source of thrust.[5]

02机翼怎样产生升力？伯努利和牛顿两种说法矛盾吗？How does a wing produce lift, and do Bernoulli and Newton contradict each other?

L=½ρv²S·C_L，绕流环量下洗气流，压差与动量反作用等价；迎角超 15°–20° 失速，升阻比约 15–20。L=½ρv²S·C_L; circulation downwashes the flow, and pressure difference equals momentum reaction; past 15°–20° flow separates and stalls, airliner L/D about 15–20.

机翼升力可以用升力公式量化：L=½ρv²S·C_L，其中 ρ 是空气密度，v 是相对来流速度，S 是机翼面积，C_L 是升力系数，随迎角变化。空气绕翼型流动形成环量，机翼把气流向下偏折产生下洗；按牛顿第三定律，气流被向下推，机翼获得向上的反作用力。翼面上下的压力差与下wash反作用是同一现象的两种描述，伯努利压差和牛顿作用反作用并不矛盾。Wing lift is quantified by L=½ρv²S·C_L, where ρ is air density, v the relative incoming speed, S wing area and C_L the lift coefficient, which varies with angle of attack. Flow around the airfoil forms circulation and deflects the airflow downward as downwash; by Newton's third law, the air pushed down reacts on the wing upward. The pressure difference across the wing and the downwash reaction are two descriptions of the same phenomenon, so Bernoulli pressure and Newton action-reaction do not contradict each other.[1][2]

迎角是翼弦与来流的夹角。小迎角下 C_L 随迎角近似线性增大；迎角超过临界值后，上翼面气流分离形成紊流，升力系数骤降、阻力增大，这就是失速。常规机翼的临界迎角大约 15°–20°，具体取决于翼型、后掠角和襟翼状态。低速大迎角起飞着陆时放出襟翼，既增大弯度提高升力，也增加阻力帮助减速。Angle of attack is the angle between the chord line and the incoming flow. At small angles C_L rises roughly linearly with attack; beyond the critical angle the upper-surface flow separates into turbulence, the lift coefficient drops and drag rises: the stall. A conventional wing's critical angle is about 15°–20%, depending on airfoil, sweep and flap setting. At low-speed take-off and landing, flaps increase camber for more lift while adding drag to help slow the aircraft.[2][8]

升阻比 L/D 衡量机翼把阻力换成升力的效率。现代客机巡航升阻比大约 15–20，高效滑翔机可达 30 以上；升阻比越高，维持平飞所需推力越小，巡航油耗越低。升力主要由机翼提供，发动机提供前飞推力；两者分工明确。鸟类拍动翅膀则同时完成两件事：翼面产生升力，拍动的扑翼动作产生前进推力，所以鸟类需要持续扇翅，固定翼飞机不需要扇动机翼。The lift-to-drag ratio L/D measures how efficiently the wing trades drag for lift. A modern airliner cruises at about 15–20, an efficient glider above 30; higher L/D means less thrust holds level flight and lower cruise fuel burn. The wing provides lift and the engine provides forward thrust, a clear division. A bird flapping does both at once: the wing surface makes lift and the flapping motion makes forward thrust, which is why birds keep flapping while a fixed-wing aircraft does not move its wing.[1][2]

气流绕翼型的细节还包括翼尖涡和诱导阻力。机翼下方高压空气绕到上方形成翼尖涡，涡量拖在机后，带来与升力相伴的诱导阻力；展弦比大的机翼（如滑翔机）翼尖涡弱、诱导阻力小。NASA 升力教学页面用环量、下洗和压力分布解释升力来源，把公式 L=½ρv²S·C_L 与气流图像对应起来。理解升力时，“上翼面路程长所以空气跑得快”的通俗说法并不严格，真实升力由绕流环量和动量变化共同决定。Wing details also include tip vortices and induced drag. High-pressure air below the wing spills around the tip to the upper side, forming a tip vortex trailing behind and inducing drag tied to lift; high-aspect-ratio wings such as gliders have weak tip vortices and low induced drag. NASA's lift teaching pages explain lift through circulation, downwash and pressure distribution, matching the L=½ρv²S·C_L formula to the airflow picture. The popular story that upper air must travel farther and therefore speed up is not rigorous; real lift follows flow circulation and momentum change together.[1][3]

边界：升力公式中的 v 是机翼与空气的相对速度，无风条件下等于地速，有风时要按相对气流计算。失速不是发动机停车，而是翼面气流分离，即使速度不低，大迎角下也会失速。伯努利描述压力分布，牛顿描述动量反作用，二者是等价视角；只取一边编造“气流必须同时到达后缘”之类说法是常见误解。Boundary: the v in the lift formula is the relative wing-air speed, equal to ground speed in calm air but computed against relative airflow in wind. A stall is not an engine failure; it is upper-surface flow separation, which can occur at large angle of attack even at decent speed. Bernoulli describes the pressure field and Newton the momentum reaction, equivalent views; claiming air particles must meet at the trailing edge is a common misconception.[1][2]

03民航客机为什么要飞到近万米高空巡航？Why do airliners cruise at altitudes near ten thousand metres?

高空低密度降低阻力，喷气发动机工作更优；但需增压客舱，且受压缩机极限、管制间隔和天气约束，并非越高越省。Thin high-altitude air lowers drag and lets jets run efficiently, but the cabin is pressurized and compressor, traffic and weather limits apply.

民航客机巡航在大约 9–12 km（30000–40000 英尺）高空，主流巡航马赫数 0.8–0.85，对应约 850–900 km/h。高空空气密度低，而阻力 D=½ρv²S·C_D 在其他条件相同时随密度减小而下降；低密度空气同时让喷气发动机在较优的增压比和温度区间工作，燃油效率更好。飞得高可以用较小推力维持高速平飞，这是高空巡航的主要收益。Airliners cruise at about 9–12 km (30,000–40,000 ft), mostly at Mach 0.8–0.85, roughly 850–900 km/h. Air density is low aloft, and drag D=½ρv²S·C_D falls with density when other terms are equal; the thin air also lets jet engines run near a favourable pressure ratio and temperature, improving fuel efficiency. High altitude holds high-speed level flight with less thrust, the main payoff of cruise altitude.[3][4]

高空环境对人和设备都有代价。气压随高度指数下降，9 km 以上外界氧气分压远低于地面，无法直接供人呼吸，客舱必须增压；即使增压，客舱压力也通常相当于 1500–2400 m 海拔。高空温度低、空气干燥，云层和颠簸多在对流层下部，巡航高度常常穿过云层之上，飞行更平稳。机体与发动机还要承受低压、低温和长途循环载荷。The high-altitude environment costs people and equipment. Pressure drops exponentially with altitude; above 9 km the outside oxygen partial pressure is far below ground level and unbreathable, so the cabin is pressurized, though cabin pressure still corresponds to about 1500–2400 m altitude. High altitude is cold and dry; most clouds and turbulence sit in the lower troposphere, so cruise often clears above them for a smoother ride. The airframe and engines also withstand low pressure, low temperature and long fatigue cycles.[3]

为什么不飞得更高？喷气效率在一定高度后受压缩机极限和机体气动限制：空气过稀时发动机需要更大迎风面积才能获得足够空气质量，翼面升力也需要更高速度补偿；再往上进入对流层顶和平流层下部，温度和风切变条件变化。航线高度还受空中交通管制间隔、航路和风带选择约束。跨洋航班常选高空西风带顺风向以节省燃油。Why not fly even higher? Jet efficiency meets compressor limits and airframe aerodynamics after a certain altitude: air too thin demands a larger engine intake for enough mass flow, and the wing needs more speed to compensate for lift. Higher still, near the tropopause and lower stratosphere, temperature and wind-shear conditions change. Cruise levels are also constrained by air-traffic separation, route structure and wind selection; oceanic flights often ride the tailwinds of high-altitude westerlies to save fuel.[3][4]

低速支线飞机飞不到也不必飞到这个高度。涡桨巡航速度低，升力公式中 v 小，需要较密空气维持升力；支线飞机多在 3–7 km 高度活动。NASA 气动与推进资料把高度、密度、速度和发动机工作点放在同一张性能图景里：同一架飞机在不同高度的最优速度、推力和油耗并不相同。Slow regional aircraft cannot and need not reach this altitude. Turboprops cruise slowly; with small v in the lift formula they need denser air for lift, and regional aircraft mostly fly at 3–7 km. NASA aeronautics and propulsion materials place altitude, density, speed and engine operating point in one performance picture: the optimum speed, thrust and fuel burn of one aircraft differ by altitude.[3][7]

边界：高空省油是气动与发动机综合结论，不代表越高越省；超过最优高度后阻力和发动机喘振限制重新抬头。客舱增压保护的是气压与氧气，不能消除低气压环境下的所有健康影响，长时间高空飞行仍需注意补水与活动。巡航高度选择还要避开雷雨、积冰和强烈颠簸区域，气象条件优先于单纯效率。Boundary: fuel saving at altitude is a combined aerodynamic and engine conclusion, not "higher always saves"; above the optimum, drag and engine surge limits return. Cabin pressurization protects pressure and oxygen but does not remove every health effect of low pressure, so long flights still call for hydration and movement. Cruise altitude must also avoid thunderstorms, icing and severe turbulence; weather takes priority over pure efficiency.[3]

04直升机为什么能停在半空中？小鸟为什么能撞坏大型飞机？Why can a helicopter hover, and why can a small bird damage a large aircraft?

旋翼是旋转机翼，悬停 T=W；v_i=√(T/(2ρA))，P_i=T·v_i。鸟撞动能 ½mv² 随速度平方增长。The rotor is a rotating wing; in hover T=W, induced speed v_i=√(T/(2ρA)) and power P_i=T·v_i. Bird-strike energy ½mv² grows with the square of relative speed.

直升机靠旋翼产生升力和推力。旋翼桨叶是旋转的机翼，每片桨叶有自己的翼型和迎角，旋转时在桨盘面积上合成向上的力。悬停时直升机没有前飞速度，旋翼产生的总推力必须恰好等于重力：T=W，才能保持位置不变；推力大于重力就上升，小于重力就下降。与固定翼飞机靠前飞速度流过机翼产生升力不同，旋翼用自身旋转不断把新空气拉过桨叶，所以能在静止空气中悬停。A helicopter produces lift and thrust with its rotor. The rotor blades are rotating wings, each with its own airfoil and angle of attack, and their rotation combines into an upward force over the rotor disk area. In hover the helicopter has no forward speed, so total rotor thrust must equal gravity exactly, T=W, to hold position; thrust above gravity climbs, below descends. Unlike a fixed-wing aircraft, which gets lift from airflow past the wing at forward speed, the rotor pulls fresh air through the blades by spinning, so it can hover in still air.[4][6]

动量理论给出悬停的定量关系。设旋翼盘面积 A、空气密度 ρ，悬停诱导速度（桨盘处空气被向下加速的速度）v_i=√(T/(2ρA))，悬停诱导功率 P_i=T·v_i。旋翼直径越大，盘面积 A 越大，维持同样推力所需诱导速度和功率越小；这也是重型直升机采用大直径主桨的原因。实际功率还要计入桨叶型阻、桨尖损失和传动损失，比理想动量理论值高。Momentum theory gives the hover relation. With disk area A and density ρ, the induced velocity at the disk, the downward air acceleration, is v_i=√(T/(2ρA)), and ideal induced power P_i=T·v_i. A larger rotor diameter raises A, lowering the induced velocity and power needed for the same thrust; this is why heavy helicopters use large main rotors. Real power also includes blade profile drag, tip losses and transmission losses, above the ideal momentum-theory value.[6]

旋翼的控制靠改变桨距。桨叶迎角可以总距同步改变，控制总推力大小；周期变距让不同方位角的桨叶迎角不同，使桨盘倾斜，从而把推力方向朝前、朝后或朝侧方。因此直升机可以垂直起降、悬停、后飞和侧飞，这是固定翼飞机做不到的。旋翼在前飞时还涉及前行桨叶与后行桨叶的速度差，需要挥舞铰等结构平衡升力不对称。Rotor control uses blade pitch. Collective pitch changes all blade angles together to set total thrust; cyclic pitch varies angle by azimuth, tilting the disk so thrust points forward, backward or sideways. This lets a helicopter take off and land vertically, hover, fly backward and sideways, which fixed-wing aircraft cannot do. In forward flight the advancing and retreating blades differ in speed, and flapping hinges balance the lift asymmetry.[6]

小鸟撞坏大型飞机的风险来自动能，而不是鸟的质量大。撞击动能 E_k=½mv²，与相对速度平方成正比：巡航或起飞阶段飞机速度高，即使一只几千克的鸟也携带可观动能，可能击穿风挡或损坏发动机叶片。民航适航要求风挡和发动机通过鸟撞试验，按典型鸟重与速度考核。NASA 旋翼与航空教学材料把旋翼看作旋转机翼，用与固定翼一致的翼型、升力和阻力概念解释悬停与前飞。Bird strikes damage large aircraft through kinetic energy, not through the bird's large mass. Impact energy E_k=½mv² scales with the square of relative speed: at cruise or take-off speed even a bird of a few kilograms carries noticeable energy that can penetrate a windshield or damage engine blades. Airworthiness rules require windshields and engines to pass bird-strike tests at representative bird weight and speed. NASA rotorcraft and aviation materials treat the rotor as a rotating wing, explaining hover and forward flight with the same airfoil, lift and drag concepts as fixed wings.[2][6]

边界：悬停推力等于重力是无升降、无侧移的平衡状态；有风或机动时推力需要分量抵消风扰动。大直径旋翼降低悬停功率，但也带来桨尖速度、重量和振动代价。鸟撞危害随速度平方增长，低速滑行与高速巡航的撞击能量差别很大；把事故简单归因于“鸟太重”忽略了相对速度这一主要变量。Boundary: hover thrust equals gravity in balanced level hover without climb, descent or drift; in wind or manoeuvre, thrust components must cancel disturbance. A large rotor lowers hover power but pays in tip speed, weight and vibration. Strike energy grows with the square of speed, so low-speed taxi and high-speed cruise differ greatly; blaming an accident only on "a heavy bird" misses relative speed, the dominant variable.[6]

05机翼升力到底怎么产生？环量、迎角和伯努利分别起什么作用？How does wing lift actually arise, and what roles do circulation, angle of attack and Bernoulli play?

绕流形成环量并下洗气流，反作用给出升力；等时到达说不成立，压差与动量反作用等价。Circulation around the section and downwash produce reaction lift; equal transit is a myth, pressure and momentum views are equivalent.

机翼升力的核心不是上翼面路程长，而是绕翼型流动形成的环量。气流从前缘分开沿上下表面流向后缘，速度环量 Γ 使上方流速偏快、下方偏慢，机翼同时把空气向下偏转形成下洗。按牛顿第三定律，空气被向下推，机翼获得向上反作用力，这就是升力。升力由 L=½ρv²S·C_L 描述：ρ 为空气密度，v 为相对来流速度，S 为机翼面积，C_L 为随迎角变化的升力系数。Wing lift rests on the circulation around the airfoil rather than on a longer upper path. Air separates at the leading edge and runs along both surfaces to the trailing edge; circulation makes upper flow faster and lower flow slower, while the wing deflects passing air downward as downwash. By Newton's third law, the pushed-down air reacts on the wing upward, and this reaction is lift. Its size follows L=½ρv²S·C_L, where ρ is density, v the relative incoming speed, S the wing area, and C_L the lift coefficient changing with angle of attack.[1][2]

迎角（攻角）是翼弦与来流方向的夹角，是控制升力的主要手段。小迎角下 C_L 随迎角近似线性增大；迎角增大，环量增强、下洗角变大，升力上升。起飞着陆速度低，靠大迎角和襟翼补偿升力；巡航平飞迎角较小。常规机翼临界迎角约 15°–20°，超过后上翼面气流无法贴附，分离成紊流，C_L 骤降、阻力骤增，即失速。失速与发动机停车无关，只看翼面气流是否分离。Angle of attack is the angle between the chord line and the incoming flow, the pilot's main lever on lift. At small angles C_L rises roughly linearly; as the angle grows, circulation strengthens, the downwash angle increases, and lift goes up. At low-speed take-off and landing the aircraft compensates with a large angle of attack and flaps, while level cruise uses a smaller angle. A conventional wing stalls near 15°–20°: beyond that the upper flow cannot stay attached, separates into turbulence, and C_L drops while drag rises sharply. A stall is unrelated to engine running; it depends only on flow separation over the wing.[2][8]

常见说法是上翼面路程长，空气必须更快到达后缘，于是上方压强低产生升力。这个等时到达假设并不成立：真实流动中，上翼面空气往往比下方更早到达后缘。伯努利方程描述定常流中流速与压强的关系，可以解释翼面上下的压差分布，但不能单独推出升力；把升力只说成上方空气跑得快，是把局部现象当成全部原因。完整图像是环量、下洗和动量变化共同决定升力，压差与反作用是同一现象的两种等价描述。A popular story says the upper surface is longer, so air must arrive at the trailing edge faster and a low-pressure region produces lift. This equal-transit assumption does not hold: in real flow upper air often arrives earlier, and the two streams need not meet simultaneously. Bernoulli's equation relates speed and pressure in steady flow and can explain the pressure difference across the section, but it cannot derive lift alone. Calling lift only fast upper air mistakes a local detail for the whole mechanism. Circulation, downwash and momentum change jointly set lift; pressure difference and reaction are two equivalent descriptions of one phenomenon.[1][8]

绕翼型的环量在翼尖处形成翼尖涡，涡量拖在机后，带来与升力相伴的诱导阻力；展弦比大的机翼翼尖涡弱、诱导阻力小，滑翔机因此采用长直机翼。现代客机巡航升阻比大约 15–20，高效滑翔机可达 30 以上；升阻比越高，维持平飞所需推力越小。机翼提供升力、发动机提供前飞推力，两者分工明确：发动机并不把飞机往上推，而是维持速度，让机翼持续产生升力。Circulation closes into tip vortices at the wing tips, trailing behind and inducing drag tied to lift. High-aspect-ratio wings such as glider wings have weak tip vortices and low induced drag. A modern airliner cruises at about 15–20 lift-to-drag, an efficient glider above 30; the higher the ratio, the less thrust holds level flight. The wing supplies lift and the engine supplies forward thrust: the engine does not push the plane upward, it maintains speed so the wing keeps generating lift.[1][2]

边界要写清：升力公式中的 v 是机翼与空气的相对速度，有风时按相对气流计算，不能直接用地速。同一翼型在不同速度、密度和襟翼状态下的升力系数曲线不同，失速迎角也会变化。环量和下洗是连续流动图像，地面效应、襟翼放下和前缘缝翼都会改变绕流；理解升力时，伯努利压差和牛顿动量反作用应当并读，只取一边编造等时到达、气流同时汇合之类说法，都是常见误解。Boundary: the v in the lift formula is the relative wing-air speed, computed against relative airflow in wind, not taken as ground speed. The lift-coefficient curve and stall angle differ with speed, density and flap setting. Ground effect, deployed flaps and leading-edge slats all change the flow field; Bernoulli pressure and Newton momentum reaction should be read together. Accounts assuming equal transit or simultaneous arrival take only one side and are common misconceptions.[1][2][8]

06飞机航线为什么不沿两地之间的直线飞？大圆航线和高空风怎样决定路径？Why do aircraft routes not fly the straight line between two cities, and how do great circles and jet streams set the path?

球面上最短路径是大圆弧线，投影成曲线；航线再随急流顺风与航路管制、天气避让调整。On a sphere the shortest path is a great-circle arc that curves on flat maps; wind, ATC routing and weather avoidance then adjust it.

在平面地图上，两地之间最近的线看起来是直线，但地球是球体，球面上两点之间的最短路径是大圆：两点与地心确定一个平面，平面与球面相交的弧线就是大圆航线。大圆距离的定义是地球表面两点间的最短距离，沿这条弧线飞行航程最短。跨太平洋的航线常常向北绕经高纬度，在平面地图上看似绕远，实际是贴着球面最短弧线走；墨卡托投影把高纬度拉长，大圆航线在图上就弯成一条弧线。On a flat map the shortest line between two cities looks straight, but Earth is a sphere. The shortest path between two surface points is a great circle: the two points and the center define a plane, whose intersection with the sphere is the great-circle arc. Great-circle distance is defined as the shortest distance between two points on the surface, and following that arc minimizes route length. Trans-Pacific flights often swing north through high latitudes; on a flat map this looks like a detour, but it follows the shortest arc over the sphere. Mercator projection stretches high latitudes, so the great-circle route appears as a bent curve on the chart.[9][11]

把球面航线画到平面地图上必然变形。墨卡托投影保持方向和等角航线，但高纬度距离被放大；大圆航线除赤道和经线外，在图上都不是直线。实际航班不会全程沿一条大圆弧线飞行，而是把它拆成一段段可执行的航段，沿规定航路点连接。短距离航线上，大圆与等角航线差别很小；跨洋长航线上，高纬度绕行节省的航程才明显。Mapping a spherical route onto a flat plane always distorts something. The Mercator projection preserves direction and rhumb lines, but it enlarges high-latitude distances; apart from the equator and meridians, great circles are not straight on the chart. Real flights do not ride one great-circle arc end to end. They break it into executable legs linked by designated waypoints. On short routes the great circle and the rhumb line barely differ; over long ocean crossings, the high-latitude detour saves a noticeable amount of distance.[9]

航线选择还要跟着高空风走。对流层上部和平流层下部存在东西向的急流带，高度大约 20000–40000 英尺，风速常达每小时上百公里；极地急流与副热带急流随季节和天气南北摆动。向东飞行时切入急流顺风段，地速增加、油耗下降；向西飞行则选择绕开逆风急流的路径。同一对城市往返航线耗时不同，很多时候不是航程变了，而是顺风与逆风的差别。Route choice also follows high-altitude winds. Narrow east-west jet streams sit in the upper troposphere and lower stratosphere, around 20,000–40,000 ft, with winds often above a hundred kilometers per hour; polar and subtropical jets shift north and south with season and weather. Eastbound flights enter the tailwind part of the jet to raise ground speed and cut fuel burn, while westbound flights choose paths that avoid headwind cores. Two routes between the same pair of cities can take different times mainly because of tailwind versus headwind, not because the distance changed.[9][10]

实际航路受空中交通管制约束。航班沿公布的航路、导航点和高度层飞行，管制员按水平间隔和垂直间隔排序避让；跨洋航线受洋区管制和通信条件限制，不能随意改走最短弧线。绕飞雷雨、积冰和强烈颠簸区域也会使航线偏离几何最短路径。高度层选择同时考虑风、湍流、发动机工作点和载客载货重量，航线与高度是综合权衡后的结果。The actual route is also constrained by air traffic control. Aircraft fly along published airways, navigation points and flight levels, and controllers separate them horizontally and vertically. Oceanic routes are limited by oceanic control and communication conditions, so a shortest arc cannot be taken freely. Avoiding thunderstorms, icing and severe turbulence pushes the route away from the geometric shortest path. Flight-level choice weighs wind, turbulence, engine operating point and payload together; route and altitude are the result of a combined trade-off.[10][11]

边界：大圆航线是几何最短，不等于每次航班实际走的路径；风、管制、天气和机场起降方向都会调整航段。平面地图上的弯曲不代表飞机在绕远，投影变形会放大这种视觉差异。往返航程时间差主要来自高空风而非地球形状变化；选择航路时，省油、安全和管制间隔优先级高于单纯几何距离。Boundary: the great circle is the geometric shortest path, not necessarily the path a given flight actually flies; wind, control, weather and runway direction all adjust the legs. A curve on a flat map does not mean the aircraft is taking a detour, because projection deformation amplifies this visual difference. Round-trip time differences come mostly from high-altitude wind rather than changes in Earth's shape. When choosing a route, fuel economy, safety and separation rules outrank pure geometric distance.[9][10]

07飞机起降时为什么要把舷窗遮光板打开？Why must window shades be raised during takeoff and landing?

起降阶段保留窗外视野，便于机组判断外况选择可用出口，并让眼睛适应外界光线。Keeping shades open in critical phases lets crew judge outside conditions, choose usable exits, and adapt eyes to outside light.

先要澄清：民航客舱的舷窗是双层固定密封结构，飞行中无法打开；通常说的开窗，是指在滑行、起飞和着陆阶段把遮光板升起，让窗外保持可见。这条要求与座椅靠背竖直、小桌板收起、通道无行李属于同一组客舱安全程序，目的是在事故概率最高的阶段缩短应急反应时间。A clarification first: airliner cabin windows are fixed, sealed double structures that cannot be opened in flight. What is usually called opening the window means raising the shade during taxi, takeoff and landing so the outside remains visible. This requirement belongs to the same cabin-safety group as upright seatbacks, stowed tray tables and clear aisles, and its purpose is to shorten emergency response time during the phases when accidents are most likely.[14]

起降是飞行中最关键的阶段。这两个阶段速度低、高度低，飞机同时处在襟翼、起落架和推力状态的切换中，一旦出现发动机故障、冲出跑道、起火或迫降，留给机组判断和撤离的窗口很短，可用高度和时间余量都远小于巡航阶段。适航演示要求座位数超过 44 人的机型在 90 秒内完成全机乘员撤离；这个时间约束决定了任何会拖慢判断和撤离的因素都要在起飞前被排除。Takeoff and landing are the most critical flight phases. The aircraft is low and slow; after an engine failure, runway excursion, fire or forced landing, the window for crew judgment and evacuation is short. Airworthiness demonstrations require aircraft with more than 44 seats to evacuate the full complement within 90 seconds; this time constraint means anything that slows judgment or evacuation must be removed before departure.[13]

升起遮光板是为了观察外况、判断出口是否可用。出现异常停稳后，机组和靠窗乘客需要看清机翼、发动机和窗外有无火焰、烟雾、碎片、积水或障碍物；某个出口外如果有火或障碍物，就应当封闭并引导人员改用其他出口。遮光板放下时，这一观察被延迟，撤离判断会失去最直接的外部依据，而在数秒量级的决策里，延迟本身就是风险。Raising the shade keeps the outside visible so exits can be judged usable. After an abnormal stop, crew and window-seat passengers need to see the wing, engines and outside for flames, smoke, debris, water or obstacles; if fire or obstruction blocks one exit, it should be sealed and passengers directed to others. With the shade down, this observation is delayed and evacuation loses its most direct external evidence, and in decisions measured in seconds, that delay is itself a risk.[12][14]

光线适应与外部互视是另外两条理由。白天或黑夜提前让眼睛接触外界光线，紧急情况下可以减少瞳孔暗适应或亮适应的时间，不至于推开出口后短暂看不清地面；外部救援人员也能透过舷窗观察舱内情况，配合撤离方向和人员分布判断。配套的客舱调光使用同一逻辑：夜间撤离时撤离路径标识在较暗环境中更醒目，机组需要同时掌握内外两侧的光线状态。Light adaptation and mutual visibility are two further reasons. Letting the eyes meet outside light earlier, day or night, reduces dark or light adaptation time in an emergency; outside rescuers can also look through the windows into the cabin to guide evacuation. Cabin dimming follows the same logic: exit-path markings stand out more clearly in a dimmer cabin, and crew need to keep track of lighting on both sides.[14]

不同航司和机型的细节有差别，部分新型机型没有传统遮光板，改用固定透明舷窗或电致变色调光玻璃；程序的核心不是那块板本身，而是在起降关键阶段保留对外部环境的可见性。座椅靠背竖直、行李归位和遮光板升起这些准备动作，都服务于同一个目标：万一需要撤离，人员、通道和出口都能在最短时间内进入可用状态。Details differ across airlines and aircraft types, and some newer types have no conventional shade; the core of the procedure is not the panel itself but preserving visibility of the outside environment during the critical takeoff and landing phases. Seat, luggage and shade preparations all serve one goal: if evacuation becomes necessary, people and exits reach a usable state in the shortest possible time.[12][13]

08飞机舷窗为什么做成圆角椭圆形而不是方形？Why are aircraft windows rounded or oval instead of square?

增压机身每次起降都承受内外压差，方窗直角处曲率突变造成应力集中，反复循环后容易起裂；平滑圆角让应力沿孔边均匀分散。Pressure differences each flight load the fuselage; square corners concentrate stress at sharp jumps, while rounded edges spread it evenly around the cutout.

民航客机巡航在约 10–12 km 高空，外界空气稀薄、气压很低，客舱却要保持相当于海拔约 1.5–2.4 km 的舒适气压。机舱像一只反复充放气的密封圆筒，每次起飞后增压、降落前减压，机身蒙皮和框架都在承受循环载荷。舷窗是在圆筒壳体上开出的孔，开孔本身就会改变周围应力的分布。舱内外压差使蒙皮处于张紧状态，开孔边缘要把绕过孔口的内力重新分配到周围框架上，孔形直接影响这种分配是否均匀。Airliners cruise at about 10 to 12 km, where the outside air is thin and pressure low, while the cabin holds a comfortable pressure equivalent to roughly 1.5 to 2.4 km altitude. The fuselage acts like a sealed cylinder inflated and deflated on every flight; after takeoff it pressurizes and before landing it depressurizes, so skin and frames carry cyclic loads. A window is a hole cut in this cylindrical shell, and the opening changes the stress distribution around it.[15]

材料力学里有应力集中现象。连续受力的构件遇到孔洞、缺口或拐角时，原本均匀传递的内力线会在不连续处拥挤到一起，局部应力明显高于平均水平。方形窗的四个直角是曲率突然改变的位置，孔边应力在尖角处被放大；一旦材料表面存在微小划痕或缺陷，尖角处就容易成为裂纹萌生的起点。Solid mechanics describes this as stress concentration. In a continuously loaded member, internal force lines crowd together at holes, notches or corners, so the local stress rises well above the average level. The four right angles of a square window are places where curvature changes abruptly, and the edge stress amplifies at the sharp corners; where the surface carries a tiny scratch or defect, the corner can become the starting point for a crack.[15][16]

现代舷窗做成带圆角的椭圆形或圆形，正是为了避免这种尖角。平滑连续的轮廓让内力沿孔边逐渐过渡、均匀分散，没有应力突然堆积的点，裂纹不容易从边缘长出来。多层舷窗结构还把受力分散到不同面板上，内层约束气密，外层承受气压差，配合圆角外形共同保证强度。现代舷窗一般由多层透明板材叠合，外层承受压差、内层保持气密，边缘用密封件和框架固定；圆角轮廓贯穿各层，避免任何一层出现尖角。Modern windows are rounded oval or circular precisely to avoid such sharp corners. A smooth continuous outline lets internal forces transition gradually and spread evenly around the edge, with no single point where stress piles up, so cracks are less likely to grow from the rim. A multilayer window also shares the load across panels: inner layers seal the air pressure and outer layers carry the pressure difference, working with the rounded shape to preserve strength.[14][15][16]

历史上有过教训。1950 年代彗星式喷气客机接连发生结构解体事故，事后调查发现，方形舷窗角部在反复增压循环下产生疲劳裂纹，裂纹逐步扩展最终导致机体失效。此后增压客机普遍把窗角改成圆角，开孔边缘也尽量设计成平滑曲线，这一外形要求从此成为机身设计的基本规则。History provides a lesson. In the 1950s the de Havilland Comet jet airliners suffered repeated structural breakups; investigation found that fatigue cracks grew from the square window corners under repeated pressurization, propagated step by step, and eventually failed the fuselage. Pressurized airliners thereafter generally replaced square corners with rounded ones and kept cutout edges as smooth curves, and this shape requirement became a basic rule of fuselage design.[15]

边界要写清：圆角不是强度的唯一保障，材料厚度、止裂带、螺栓孔工艺和整体蒙皮设计共同决定结构安全。本章只讲开孔形状与应力分布的关系，不展开机体疲劳寿命、材料选型和适航审定规范；日常看到的圆角窗，背后是增压循环和应力集中两方面要求的结果。The boundary matters: rounding alone does not guarantee strength; material thickness, crack-stopper bands, fastener-hole workmanship and overall skin design together set structural safety. This chapter covers only the relation between opening shape and stress distribution, not fatigue life, material selection or airworthiness certification. The rounded windows seen every day result from both pressurization cycling and stress-concentration requirements.[16]
