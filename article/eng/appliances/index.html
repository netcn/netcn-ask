01洗衣机和吹风机是怎样工作的？家电电机靠什么带动旋转？How do washers and blow dryers work, and what drives appliance motors to rotate?

通电线圈在磁场中受力矩转动；串激电机高速高转矩用于吹风机，感应电机平稳耐用用于洗衣机。A current coil feels torque in a magnetic field; series motors run fast with high torque for dryers, induction motors run smooth and durable in washers.

洗衣机和吹风机都靠电动机把电能变成旋转机械功。电动机的核心是通电线圈在磁场中受力：电流在磁场里受到安培力，线圈两侧受力方向相反形成力矩，带动转子旋转。家用小电机按结构分成串激电机和感应电机等类型：串激电机转速高、起动转矩大，适合吹风机这类需要高速风叶的设备；感应电机结构简单耐用、运转平稳，常用于洗衣机滚筒和水泵。Washers and blow dryers both use electric motors to turn electrical energy into rotating mechanical work. The motor core is a current-carrying coil in a magnetic field: the current feels an Ampere force, opposite forces on two sides form a torque, and the rotor turns. Household small motors fall into series and induction types: series motors run fast with high starting torque, suiting high-speed fan blades in dryers; induction motors are simple, durable and smooth, used in washer drums and pumps.[1][2]

洗衣机的动力路径是电机带动内筒或波轮旋转。内筒式洗衣机让衣物随筒壁提升到高处后落下，靠摔打和水流揉搓去污；波轮式则靠波轮搅动水流形成涡流，衣物在水流和筒壁之间摩擦。电机正反转交替配合进水、洗涤、漂洗和脱水程序，脱水阶段高速旋转把水从织物中甩出。洗衣机的加热、进水阀和排水泵由程序控制器统一调度，电机只承担旋转动力部分。The washer power path runs from motor to drum or agitator. Drum washers lift clothes up the wall and drop them, cleaning through beating and water flow; agitator washers drive a vortex that rubs clothes against water and the drum. Reversing motor rotation alternates with fill, wash, rinse and spin programs, and the spin stage flings water out of fabric at high speed. Heating, inlet valve and drain pump are scheduled by the controller, while the motor only provides rotation.[1][3]

吹风机的动力与发热是两条相对独立的路径。电机带动风叶高速旋转，把空气从进风口吸入、从出风口吹出；镍铬电热丝在风道中通电发热，气流经过热丝被加热后送出热风。冷风档只开电机、不通电热丝；风温由电热丝功率和风量共同决定，风量大则升温小，风量小则风温高。串激电机体积小、转速高，能在有限机身内提供足够风压。The dryer's power and heating follow two relatively separate paths. The motor spins the fan blade, drawing air through the intake and pushing it out the nozzle; a nickel-chrome heating wire heats in the airflow, and the passing air leaves as warm wind. Cold mode runs the motor only without heating; air temperature depends on heater power and airflow, with larger flow giving less rise and smaller flow giving hotter air. The compact series motor delivers enough pressure at high speed in a small body.[1][2]

OpenStax 大学物理教材在电机章节说明通电线圈在磁场中受力矩转动的原理，在磁学章节描述电流磁场和磁极相互作用；电功率章节给出功率与电压、电流的关系。家电电机的选型本质上是在转速、转矩、噪声、寿命和成本之间权衡，不同设备对转速和转矩的要求差异明显，电机选型需与负载匹配。The OpenStax physics text explains current-coil torque in a magnetic field in the motor chapter, current magnetic fields and pole interaction in the magnet chapter, and power with voltage and current in the power chapter. Motor selection trades speed, torque, noise, life and cost, and devices differ in what rotation they need.[1][2][3]

边界：本章讲家用电机的基本原理和典型动力路径，不展开电机绕组设计、变频控制电路或具体机型参数。电机转速和功率受供电电压、负载和散热影响，电机与负载匹配还影响噪声和寿命；设备异响、过热或漏电应停用检修，不能凭原理自行拆解带电部件。Boundary: this chapter covers basic household motor principles and typical power paths, not winding design, variable-frequency control circuits or model-specific parameters. Speed and power depend on supply voltage, load and cooling; odd noise, overheating or leakage means stop and service, not opening live parts from theory alone.[1][3]

02烘干机是怎样把衣服烘干的？冰箱空调的制冷循环如何工作？How does a clothes dryer dry clothes, and how do refrigerators and air conditioners cool?

电热丝按焦耳热发热，热风对流带走水分；制冷靠蒸发—压缩—冷凝—节流逆循环把热量搬到外界。Heating wire makes Joule heat and warm convection removes moisture; cooling moves heat outside through an evaporation-compression-condensation-throttling reverse cycle.

烘干机靠加热空气和机械翻动把衣物烘干。电热丝通电后按焦耳定律发热，电功率 P=I²R 把电能转化为热量；风机把热空气送入滚筒，湿热空气带走织物中的水分并排出。滚筒持续翻动衣物，让织物各部分轮流接触热风，减少局部烘干不透；排风湿度和温度随烘干进程变化。冷凝式烘干机把湿热空气冷却后凝结水排出，热泵式则回收冷凝热量再加热空气，能耗更低。Dryers remove water from clothes by heating air and tumbling the fabric. The heating wire heats by Joule's law, P = I²R, turning electrical energy into heat; the fan sends warm air into the drum, and damp air carries fabric moisture away and exits. Tumbling rotates the clothes so each part meets hot air in turn and local moisture does not build up; condenser dryers cool damp air to condense water, while heat-pump dryers recover condensation heat to reheat air at lower energy use.[3][6]

冰箱和空调属于逆循环制冷设备。制冷剂在蒸发器低压下蒸发，吸收箱内或室内热量；压缩机把低温低压蒸气压缩成高温高压气体；冷凝器向外界放热凝结成液体；节流装置再把液体降压送回蒸发器，完成蒸发—压缩—冷凝—节流循环。制冷循环把热量从低温处搬到高温处，需要消耗压缩机做功，与自然热量从高温流向低温的方向相反。Refrigerators and air conditioners are reverse-cycle cooling devices. Refrigerant evaporates at low pressure in the evaporator, absorbing heat from the box or room; the compressor squeezes cool low-pressure vapor into hot high-pressure gas; the condenser rejects heat to the outside and condenses to liquid; an expansion device drops the liquid pressure back to the evaporator, closing evaporation, compression, condensation and throttling. The cycle moves heat from cold to hot using compressor work, opposite to the natural hot-to-cold flow.[5][6]

热量传递有传导、对流和辐射三种基本方式。烘干机热风主要靠对流把热量传给织物，电热丝通过对流和辐射加热空气；冰箱箱体隔热材料减少传导和对流，门封条减少冷热空气交换，隔热层厚度和门封密封直接影响冷量损失和能耗。热泵和制冷设备的效率用性能系数衡量，即搬运的热量与输入功之比；环境温度、门封状态和负载量都会影响实际能耗。Heat moves by conduction, convection and radiation. Dryer warm air transfers heat mainly by convection, and the heating wire warms air by convection and radiation; refrigerator insulation reduces conduction and convection, while door seals limit exchange of cold and warm air. Heat pumps and cooling devices are rated by a coefficient of performance, heat moved divided by input work; ambient temperature, seal state and load all affect actual energy use.[5][6]

OpenStax 热力学应用章节描述热泵、冰箱和空调的逆循环与性能系数；热传递章节区分传导、对流和辐射。家电加热与制冷看似相反，都遵循能量守恒：加热是把电能直接变成热，制冷是做功把热量转移，两者都不能无中生有地产生或消灭热量。The OpenStax applied-thermodynamics chapter describes the reverse cycle and COP of heat pumps, fridges and air conditioners; the heat-transfer chapter separates conduction, convection and radiation. Heating and cooling look opposite but both follow conservation: heating turns electricity directly into heat, cooling does work to move heat, and neither creates or destroys heat from nothing.[5][6]

边界：本章讲加热与制冷的基本循环和传热方式，不展开制冷剂种类与环保替代、热泵工况计算或具体烘干程序设置。设备能效受使用习惯、装载量和环境影响，不同烘干方式适用的衣物材质也有差别；加热部件表面高温，制冷设备故障应避免自行拆解管路和制冷剂回路。Boundary: this chapter covers basic heating and cooling cycles and transfer modes, not refrigerant types and replacements, heat-pump load calculations or specific drying programs. Energy use depends on habits, load and environment; heating parts run hot, and cooling faults should not lead to opening refrigerant lines yourself.[3][5]

03搅拌机、洗碗机和咖啡机分别是怎样工作的？How do a blender, a dishwasher and a coffee maker each work?

搅拌机高速刀片剪切破碎，洗碗机喷淋冲刷加洗涤剂分解，咖啡机靠热水与粉层接触萃取。The blender shears with high-speed blades, the dishwasher flushes with sprays and detergent, and the coffee maker extracts by hot water meeting the grounds.

搅拌机是厨房小电里典型的高速剪切设备。电机通过联轴器带动刀座和多层刀片高速旋转，容器内食材被刀齿切削、冲击和涡流带动，从大块逐渐破碎成浆液。刀头形状、转速和容器结构配合决定剪切粗细；转速越高、刀齿越锋利，破碎越细，但硬质食材仍需分次加料，避免电机堵转。搅拌机的加热功能有限，主要动力来自电机，粉碎和搅拌是同一旋转动作的不同程度。The blender is a high-speed shearing kitchen appliance. The motor drives the blade holder and multiple blades through a coupling; food is cut, impacted and carried by the vortex, breaking from chunks into slurry. Blade shape, speed and container structure together set how fine the result; higher speed and sharper blades break finer, but hard food should be added in portions to avoid motor stall. Heating is limited, the main power comes from the motor, and blending and mixing are degrees of the same rotation.[1][3]

洗碗机用机械冲刷加化学分解完成清洁。水泵把水加压送到喷淋臂，臂上喷嘴朝餐具方向喷出高速水流，冲刷掉食物残渣；洗涤剂在热水中分解油污和蛋白质残留，加热提高洗涤剂活性，高温水有助于溶解凝固油污；漂洗阶段用清水冲去洗涤剂，最后靠余热或加热烘干餐具。洗碗机按预洗、主洗、漂洗和烘干编排水温、水量和喷淋节奏，比手洗更集中地用水和热，用水量可因机型和程序不同而变化。Dishwashers clean by mechanical flushing plus chemical breakdown. A pump pressurizes water to spray arms whose nozzles jet high-speed flow at dishes, knocking off residue; detergent breaks grease and protein in hot water, rinse stages wash away detergent, and residual heat or heating dries the dishes. Programs sequence pre-wash, main wash, rinse and drying through water temperature, volume and spray rhythm, concentrating water and heat compared with hand washing; actual use varies by model and program.[3][6]

咖啡机通过热水与咖啡粉接触完成萃取。滴滤式把热水均匀浇在粉层上，靠重力滤过粉床流入壶中；意式机用泵把热水加压穿过粉饼，短时间内萃取出浓缩液。水温、粉粗细、萃取时间和压力共同决定风味；研磨和压粉均匀度影响水流通过粉层的速度。多数家用咖啡机的加热元件把水加热到设定温度，水泵或重力提供水流动力。Coffee makers extract by bringing hot water into contact with grounds. Drip models pour hot water evenly over the bed and gravity filters it into the pot; espresso machines pump hot water under pressure through the puck to extract concentrate in a short time. Water temperature, grind size, extraction time and pressure set the flavor; even grinding and tamping control how fast water passes the bed. Most home makers heat water to set temperature, with a pump or gravity driving flow.[3][6]

OpenStax 电功率章节说明电机、加热元件的功率消耗；热传递章节描述热水与食材之间的传热；简单机械与流体章节涉及刀片剪切和喷淋水流。厨房小电虽然外观不同，工作过程都可拆成动力、加热或流体运动三个要素，按食材和容器配合完成任务。The OpenStax power chapter covers motor and heater power use; the heat-transfer chapter describes hot-water-to-food transfer; the simple-machine and fluid chapters touch blade shear and spray flow. Though kitchen appliances look different, each process breaks into power, heating or fluid motion, matched to food and container.[1][3][6]

边界：本章讲三类厨房小电的基本工作过程，不展开研磨度调校、洗涤剂配方或具体机型选购。设备使用应按说明书装载和加料，装载量和食材硬度影响粉碎与萃取效果，避免空转、超载或接触带电部件；故障停水、漏水或加热异常应停用检修。Boundary: this chapter covers basic processes of three small kitchen devices, not grind adjustment, detergent formulas or model buying. Load and add ingredients per the manual; avoid running empty, overloading or touching live parts; stop and service on abnormal water, leaks or heating.[1][3]

04为什么插线板有两个或三个孔？接地和漏电保护起什么作用？Why do outlets have two or three holes, and what do grounding and residual-current protection do?

两孔接火线零线成回路；三孔另接保护接地，外壳漏电时导向大地，漏保检测火零差值跳闸。Two prongs join live and neutral in a loop; a third adds protective earth that routes chassis leakage to ground, and RCD trips on live-neutral difference.

两孔和三孔插头对应不同的电气连接。两孔插头把火线和零线接入设备，形成电流回路；三孔插头除火线、零线外，还有一根保护接地（PE）线，接到设备金属外壳。保护接地在正常工作时不带电流，只在故障时起作用，不改变设备正常回路。当内部绝缘损坏使外壳带电时，接地导线把外壳电位导向大地，配合断路器或漏电保护器切断电源，降低人体接触外壳时的触电风险。Two-prong and three-prong plugs connect different electrical paths. A two-prong plug joins live and neutral to the device, forming the current loop; a three-prong plug adds a protective earth (PE) wire to the metal chassis. When internal insulation fails and the chassis becomes live, the ground wire pulls chassis potential toward earth, working with breakers or residual-current devices to cut power and reduce shock on contact.[4][7]

触电风险来自电流通过人体。人体电阻随皮肤干湿和接触面积变化，电流达到一定强度会影响肌肉和心脏；安全做法是设备外壳接地、回路有保护器件，并避免湿手操作电器。漏电保护器检测火线与零线电流的差值，一旦有电流经人体或外壳漏入大地，差值超过阈值就快速跳闸；它补充保险丝或断路器只按过流动作的不足。Shock risk comes from current passing through the body. Body resistance varies with wetness and contact area, and current above a level affects muscles and the heart; safe practice grounds chassis, keeps protective devices in the circuit and avoids wet-handed operation. A residual-current device detects the difference between live and neutral current; once some current leaks through a person or chassis to earth, the difference trips quickly. It complements fuses or breakers that act only on overcurrent.[4][7]

家庭电路按功率分配回路。电器功率 P=UI，即电压乘以电流；大功率设备单独走线，导线线径要能承载额定电流，避免线路过热。插座接线规范要求火线、零线和接地位置固定，插头形制匹配不能随意改动；插座、插头的额定电流、线径与导线绝缘都要与负载匹配，老化破损应及时更换。延长线和插线板的总负荷不应超过额定值，多个大功率设备共用一个插座会造成过载发热。Home circuits are split by power. Appliance power P = UI, voltage times current; high-power devices use dedicated wiring, and wire gauge must carry rated current to avoid overheating. Outlet wiring standards fix live, neutral and ground positions, and plug shapes must match without modification; extension cords and strips must stay within rated load, and several high-power devices on one outlet cause overheating.[3][7]

OSHA 电气安全资料说明接地、绝缘、过流保护和触电防护的基本要求；OpenStax 电学危害章节讨论人体电阻、电流路径和电气伤害；电功率章节给出功率、电压和电流关系。插头孔数不是装饰区别，而是电路保护配置的外在表现。OSHA electrical materials cover grounding, insulation, overcurrent protection and shock defense; the OpenStax electrical-hazards chapter discusses body resistance, current path and injury; the power chapter relates power, voltage and current. Prong count is not decorative but an external mark of protective wiring.[3][4][7]

边界：本章讲家用用电安全的基本概念，不展开住宅配电设计、接地线施工或具体漏电保护器选型。电气故障和线路改造应由具备资质的人员处理；不要自行拆开插头、更换线径或绕过保护装置，保护装置动作后应查明原因再复位。出现麻电、打火或焦味应立即断电并检修。Boundary: this chapter covers basic household safety concepts, not residential distribution design, ground-wire installation or RCD selection. Electrical faults and rewiring belong to qualified personnel; do not open plugs, change gauge or bypass protection yourself. On tingling, sparking or burning smell, cut power immediately and service.[4][7]

05门铃是怎样工作的？控制电路怎样用小电流驱动信号和主电路？How does a doorbell work, and how does a control circuit use small current to drive signals and main circuits?

低压按钮接通控制回路，电磁铁吸合衔铁发声；继电器分离控制回路与主电路，信号灯按颜色编码状态。A low-voltage button closes the control loop, the electromagnet pulls the armature to sound; relays separate control and main loops, and lamps encode state by color.

门铃是用小电流控制信号动作的典型家电。传统机械门铃接在降压变压器上，先把市电降到安全低压，变压器把市电隔离成低压，降低门铃回路本身的触电风险；按下按钮接通低压回路，电磁铁线圈通电产生磁力，吸合衔铁敲击铃盖或振动簧片。松开按钮后回路断开，磁力消失，衔铁复位，触点断续动作形成“叮咚”两声。机械触点长期使用可能氧化或磨损，电磁铁把电能转换成磁吸力，再由机械动作发出声音信号。A doorbell is a typical appliance that uses small current to trigger a signal action. The classic mechanical doorbell runs off a step-down transformer that lowers mains to safe low voltage; pressing the button closes the low-voltage circuit, the electromagnet coil energizes and pulls the armature to strike the bell or vibrate the reed. Releasing opens the circuit, magnetism vanishes and the armature resets; intermittent contacts produce the ding-dong. The electromagnet turns electricity into magnetic pull, and the mechanical action gives the sound signal.[1][2]

信号电路与主电路通常分离。门铃、报警和指示灯这类控制信号不需要大电流，用低压小功率回路驱动；大功率主电路由继电器、接触器或开关在另一回路接通。按下按钮只给控制回路一个信号，继电器再用小电流控制主触点闭合，使主电路通断。这样既降低操作侧触电风险，也便于用低电压电子器件发指令。Signal circuits usually separate from main circuits. Door bell, alarm and indicator signals need little current and run on low-voltage low-power loops; high-power main circuits close through relays, contactors or switches in another loop. The button only sends a signal to the control loop, and the relay closes main contacts with small current to switch the main circuit. This lowers shock risk on the operator side and lets low-voltage electronics issue commands.[1][3]

交通和家电中的信号灯靠颜色和位置传递状态。红色表示停止或危险，绿色表示通行或正常，黄色用于警示过渡；灯具、蜂鸣器和显示屏把电路状态转换成人可识别的光声信号，信号编码还包括声音节奏、显示屏文字和指示灯组合，让使用者区分不同状态。现代门铃和报警器还带无线发射，按钮把信号发给室内接收端，不再依赖机械导线连通，但原理仍是“触发—控制—信号输出”。红外遥控器是这类无线信号的常见形式：按键把指令编码成脉冲序列，由红外发光二极管以约 940 nm 的红外光、约 38 kHz 的载波发出，接收端光电二极管解调后再交给执行电路。Traffic and appliance lamps convey state by color and position. Red means stop or danger, green means go or normal, yellow warns transition; lamps, buzzers and displays convert circuit state into recognizable light and sound signals. Modern doorbells and alarms also send wireless signals, the button transmitting to an indoor receiver without mechanical wire, but the principle stays trigger, control, signal output.The infrared remote is a common such wireless signal: a keypad encodes the command into pulses sent by an infrared LED at about 940 nm on a roughly 38 kHz carrier; the receiver photodiode demodulates them and passes the command to the control circuit.[1][2]

Simple as it looks, the doorbell already contains electromagnet, low-voltage isolation, control loop and signal output.[1][2][3]

边界：本章讲电磁铁控制与信号电路的基本原理，不展开无线通信协议、报警系统布线或具体门铃改装。低压变压器和线圈仍可能带电，维修应先断开电源，信号器件的额定电压和触点容量需与回路匹配；无线信号受距离、遮挡和干扰影响，不能替代必要的现场检查。Boundary: this chapter covers electromagnet control and basic signal circuits, not wireless protocols, alarm wiring or specific doorbell modifications. Transformers and coils can still carry live voltage; cut power before repair; wireless signals depend on distance, obstruction and interference and cannot replace site checks.[1][2]
