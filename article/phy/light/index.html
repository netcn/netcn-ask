01无影灯怎样摆脱影子？影子是怎么形成的？激光束会投下影子吗？How does an operating lamp remove shadows, how do shadows form, and does a laser beam cast a shadow?

均匀介质中光沿直线传播，遮挡物后方形成本影与半影；多光源互相补光压淡本影，激光被挡住时同样留下暗区。Light travels straight in a uniform medium; an opaque object leaves umbra and penumbra. Multiple sources lighten the umbra, and a blocked laser leaves a dark area too.

光在均匀介质中沿直线传播。同一介质里光速方向不变，遇到不透明物体时，被挡住的区域在后方留下暗区，这就是影子。光源有一定大小，物体后方正对光源的区域完全无光，是本影；只被部分光源照亮的边缘区域，是半影。影子的形状、大小随光源位置和距离变化，光源越近、物体越大，影子越浓越大。Light travels in straight lines through a uniform medium. Direction stays constant in one medium; when it meets an opaque object, the blocked region behind becomes dark, the shadow. A source with finite size leaves a fully dark umbra directly behind the object and a partial penumbra at the edges. Shadow shape and size shift with source position and distance: a closer, larger source gives a denser, bigger shadow.[1]

无影灯不是让影子消失，而是用多光源把本影压到最小。手术灯由一圈排列的灯珠组成，从不同方向同时照亮手术部位；一个灯珠被器械挡住形成的暗区，被其他方向的灯珠照亮，本影被分散到多个方向，残留的半影也很淡。多光源、大发光面和合理排布，是消除浓重影子的关键；单一强光源反而会留下边界清晰的深影。An operating lamp does not remove shadows but minimizes the umbra with multiple sources. Its ring of bulbs illuminates the surgical site from several directions; the dark area blocked by an instrument under one bulb is lit by bulbs from other directions, so the umbra spreads across directions and the remaining penumbra stays faint. Multiple sources, a large emitting surface and sensible arrangement erase heavy shadows, while a single strong source leaves a sharp deep one.[1]

激光束看起来笔直，是因为激光方向性好、光束发散角小。激光本身不会“投影子”，但激光束被不透明物体挡住时，物体在后方留下激光照明区域的暗区；在有烟尘或水雾的空气里，光束路径被散射显现出来，才看到一条亮线。普通灯泡向四周发光，激光在谐振腔里选出沿轴向传播的光，方向性来自谐振腔对轴向光的选择，因此长距离仍保持细束。A laser beam looks straight because it is highly directional with a small divergence angle. The laser itself does not cast a shadow, but when an opaque object blocks the beam, a dark region appears behind it in the laser-lit area; dust or mist in the air scatters the beam and makes the path visible as a bright line. An ordinary bulb radiates in all directions, while the laser cavity selects axial rays, so the beam stays narrow over long distance.[1][7]

直线传播是几何光学的基本近似。小孔成像、日晷计时、排队看齐和影子方向，都建立在光沿直线走的前提上；光遇到镜面、界面时才改变方向。光在非均匀介质中会弯曲，比如近地面空气受热不均时看到的热浪扭曲，这时直线近似需要修正。影子的浓淡还与环境反射有关，墙面和地面的漫反射会补亮暗区。Straight-line propagation is the basic approximation of geometric optics. Pinhole imaging, sundials, lining up in a queue and shadow direction all rest on light going straight; it changes direction only at mirrors or interfaces. Light bends in non-uniform media, such as heat shimmer over hot ground, where the straight-line approximation needs correction. Shadow density also depends on ambient reflection, and diffuse reflection from walls and floor brightens dark areas.[1][8]

边界：直线传播只在均匀、各向同性介质中成立；遇到界面、衍射尺度或非均匀空气，光线会偏折。无影灯消除的是浓重本影，半影和遮挡仍存在，不可能把所有暗区完全去掉。激光束在空气中可见依赖微粒散射，真空里看不到光束路径。把影子当成物体投出的“实体”，忽略了它只是光被遮挡后的光照分布。The boundary: straight propagation holds in a uniform isotropic medium; at interfaces, diffraction scales or uneven air, rays bend. An operating lamp removes heavy umbra, not all penumbra and occlusion; no arrangement erases every dark area. A laser beam visible in air relies on particle scattering, and the path cannot be seen in vacuum. Treating a shadow as a physical object misses that it is only the illumination pattern left after light is blocked.[1]

02为什么人站在哈哈镜前会变形？平面镜和曲面镜各成什么像？Why does a person look distorted in a funhouse mirror, and what images do plane and curved mirrors form?

反射角等于入射角；平面镜成等大虚像，凹面镜会聚、凸面镜发散，哈哈镜凹凸拼接扭曲人像。Reflection angle equals incidence; a plane mirror forms an equal virtual image, concave converges, convex diverges, and a funhouse mirror twists the figure.

光射到光滑界面上发生反射，反射角等于入射角。入射光线、反射光线和界面法线在同一平面内，两侧关于法线对称。镜面把平行光按同一方向反射出去，形成清晰的镜像；光滑金属镀层反射率高，是镜面成像的常用材料。墙面、纸张这类粗糙表面把光向各个方向漫反射，不形成镜面像。反射定律是平面镜成像、曲面镜和光路可逆的基础。Light reflects off a smooth interface with angle of reflection equal to angle of incidence. Incident ray, reflected ray and surface normal lie in one plane, symmetric about it. A mirror reflects parallel rays in one direction and forms a clear image; rough surfaces like walls and paper scatter light in all directions with no mirror image. The reflection law underlies plane mirrors, curved mirrors and reversibility of light paths.[2]

平面镜成等大、正立、左右相反的虚像。像在镜后与物体到镜面等距的位置，光线进入眼睛后沿反向延长线交于镜后，眼睛以为光从那里发出。穿衣镜、后视镜和潜望镜都利用平面反射；像不是真实光线会聚而成，所以不能用屏承接。改变镜面角度，像的位置随反射方向同步移动。A plane mirror forms an upright virtual image of the same size, reversed left to right. The image sits behind the mirror at the same distance as the object; rays reach the eye and their backward extensions meet behind the mirror, so the eye reads the light as coming from there. Dressing mirrors, rear-view mirrors and periscopes use plane reflection; the image is not formed by converging real rays and cannot be caught on a screen. Tilting the mirror moves the image with the reflected direction.[2][3]

曲面镜把反射光会聚或发散。凹面镜使平行光向焦点会聚，可用于聚光、点火和反射式望远镜；凸面镜把光发散，视野更宽，用于汽车后视镜和路口广角镜。哈哈镜由凹凸不平的曲面拼接而成，不同区域分别放大、压缩或扭曲反射的像，于是人像被拉长、压扁或弯曲。曲面成像的放大率随物距和曲率变化，不能简单按平面镜距离计算。Curved mirrors converge or diverge reflected light. A concave mirror focuses parallel light toward a focal point, used for concentration, ignition and reflecting telescopes; a convex mirror spreads light for a wider field, as in car and road mirrors. A funhouse mirror joins concave and convex patches that enlarge, compress or twist the image in different regions, stretching or bending the person. Curved magnification shifts with object distance and curvature and cannot be computed like a plane mirror.[3]

反射成像的清晰度由表面平整度决定。镜面镀层平整、反射率高，像就清晰；表面变形或氧化，反射光杂乱，像变模糊。金属反射、玻璃背面镀银和水面反射各有损耗与偏差。镜面角度微调会改变反射光方向，像随之移动。光路可逆意味着沿反射光逆向入射，会沿原入射方向返回，这一性质用于校准光路和测距反射器。Image clarity depends on surface flatness. A flat high-reflectivity coating gives a clear image; deformation or oxidation scatters reflection and blurs it. Metal reflection, silvered glass and water reflection each have losses and deviations. Path reversibility means a ray sent back along the reflected direction returns along the original incident path, a property used for optical alignment and ranging reflectors.[2][3]

边界：反射定律在光滑界面成立，漫反射是大量微小镜面各自反射的统计结果，不违反定律。虚像与实像要区分：虚像由光线反向延长形成，不能在屏上呈现。曲面镜成像随曲率和物距改变，同一面镜子近处远处的像可能大小、正倒不同。把“镜中像”当成实物复制，忽略了光线只是被反射、镜后并没有真实物体。The boundary: the reflection law holds at smooth interfaces; diffuse reflection is the statistical sum of many tiny mirrors and does not violate it. Virtual and real images differ: a virtual image comes from backward extensions and cannot be screened. Curved images shift with curvature and object distance, so near and far views may differ in size and orientation. Treating the mirror image as a copied object misses that light is only reflected, with no real body behind the glass.[1][2]

03筷子放在水中为什么像折断了？纸浸透油后为什么变透明？冰块为什么有时发白？Why does a straw look broken in water, why does oiled paper turn transparent, and why does ice sometimes look white?

折射由折射率 n=c/v 与 Snell 定律决定；筷子弯折是视深偏移，油纸和透明冰靠减少界面散射使光透过。Refraction follows n=c/v and Snell's law; the broken straw is apparent-depth offset, and oiled paper and clear ice transmit light by reducing interface scattering.

光从一种介质进入另一种介质时方向偏折，叫折射。偏折程度由两种介质的折射率决定：n=c/v，v 是光在该介质中的速度，c 是真空中光速。水的折射率约 1.33，普通玻璃约 1.5，空气约 1.0003。光进入折射率更大的介质，向法线偏折；从密介质回到疏介质，远离法线。筷子斜插水中，水下部分的光线在水面偏折，眼睛沿反向延长线看过去，筷子像在水面处折断。Light bends when passing from one medium into another: refraction. The amount depends on refractive indices: n=c/v, where v is the speed in that medium and c the vacuum speed. Water index about 1.33, ordinary glass about 1.5, air about 1.0003. Light entering a higher-index medium bends toward the normal; leaving into a lower-index medium it bends away. A straw angled in water: underwater rays bend at the surface, and the eye's backward extension makes it look broken at the line.[4]

折射规律由斯涅尔定律描述：n1·sinθ1=n2·sinθ2。入射角和折射角沿界面法线测量；折射率大的一侧光线更靠近法线。水深看起来比实际浅，也是折射造成的：水底反射的光出水时偏折，视线反向延长后位置被抬高。透镜放大、池水深度判断和海市蜃楼，都与折射和折射率梯度有关。Refraction follows Snell's law: n1·sinθ1=n2·sinθ2. Angles are measured from the interface normal; the higher-index side keeps rays closer to the normal. Pool water looks shallower than it is for the same reason: bottom-reflected light bends on leaving water, and the extended sight line raises the apparent position. Lenses, depth judgment and mirages all relate to refraction and index gradients.[4]

透明与否取决于界面散射多少。纯净冰块内部均匀，光顺利穿过，看起来透明；冻冰时混入气泡和裂隙，气泡与冰的折射率差别很大，界面把光向各方向散射，冰就发白。纸张纤维之间是空气，油渗入后把空气替换成与纤维折射率更接近的液体，界面散射减少，纸就变得更透明。折射率匹配是解释油纸、磨砂纸和冰白现象的主线。Transparency depends on how much interface scattering occurs. A pure ice block is uniform and lets light through, looking clear; bubbles and cracks trapped during freezing have an index very different from ice, and their interfaces scatter light in all directions, turning ice white. Paper fibres hold air; oil replaces it with a liquid closer in index to the fibre, reducing interface scattering and making the paper more transparent. Index matching is the thread for oiled paper, frosted paper and white ice.[1][4]

折射率与光在介质中的速度直接联系。介质 n 越大，光速越慢；不同颜色光在同一介质中 n 略有差别，这是下一章色散的来源。折射光路同样可逆，从水中看岸上物体，视角位置也会偏移。透镜成像、眼睛屈光和眼镜矫正，都按折射率和界面曲率计算光线走向。Refractive index links directly to light speed in the medium. Higher n means slower light; colors differ slightly in n within the same medium, the source of dispersion in the next chapter. Refraction paths are reversible, and an underwater observer sees shore objects shifted in position as well. Lenses, eye optics and prescription correction all trace rays through index and interface curvature.[4][5]

边界：折射发生在两种透明介质的界面上，完全不透明的物体谈不上折射。筷子弯折是视觉位置偏移，筷子本身没有断；把视错觉当成物理断裂会误判。折射率随波长、温度和介质成分变化，1.33、1.5 是常见近似值。气泡造成的发白来自散射，不是冰本身变色；区分折射偏折与界面散射，才能解释透明、半透明和白色三类现象。The boundary: refraction occurs at a transparent-medium interface; an opaque object has no refraction to speak of. The bent straw is an apparent-position offset, not a broken physical straw. Index shifts with wavelength, temperature and composition, and 1.33 or 1.5 are common approximations. Whiteness from bubbles is scattering, not ice changing color; separating refraction bending from interface scattering explains clear, translucent and white appearances.[4]

04白光通过棱镜为什么分成彩色？彩虹里包含哪些人眼看不见的光？物体颜色由什么决定？Why does a prism split white light into colors, what invisible light does a rainbow contain, and what determines object color?

折射率随波长变化，紫光偏折多、红光少；色散分开各色光，物体颜色来自选择反射与散射，红外紫外仍存在但不可见。Index varies with wavelength, violet bending more than red; dispersion separates colors, object color comes from selective reflection and scattering, and IR/UV exist though invisible.

折射率随颜色变化，是色散的根源。同一介质对不同波长光的折射率略有不同：紫光波长最短、折射率最大，红光波长最长、折射率最小。白光通过棱镜时，不同颜色在两个界面偏折程度不同，紫光偏折最多、红光最少，白光被分解成红橙黄绿蓝靛紫的彩色带，这就是色散。彩虹是空中水滴对阳光的折射、反射与色散共同形成的。Refractive index varies with color, which is the root of dispersion. The same medium gives slightly different indices by wavelength: violet, shortest wavelength and highest index; red, longest wavelength and lowest. White light through a prism bends differently by color, violet the most and red the least, splitting white into a red-orange-yellow-green-blue-indigo-violet band: dispersion. A rainbow forms when water drops refract, reflect and disperse sunlight together.[5]

可见光波长约 400–700 nm。波长比红光更长的红外光和比紫光更短的紫外光，人眼看不到，却同样经过色散与折射；彩虹照片里的颜色只是可见部分，水滴分光也会把不可见波段分出。激光、LED 和白炽灯光的颜色组成不同，有的以单色为主，有的是连续光谱叠加。颜色既来自光源光谱，也来自物体对不同波长的吸收和反射。Visible light spans about 400 to 700 nm. Infrared beyond red and ultraviolet beyond violet are invisible yet refract and disperse the same way; colors in a rainbow photo are only the visible part, and drops split the invisible bands too. Laser, LED and incandescent sources differ in color content, some nearly monochromatic, others a continuous spectrum. Color comes from the source spectrum together with an object's absorption and reflection by wavelength.[5][7]

物体颜色是选择反射与散射的结果。白纸反射各种可见光，红布主要反射红光；蓝天是大气分子对短波蓝光的瑞利散射，火星表面偏红则来自氧化铁粉尘对光线的吸收反射。冰块、牛奶和云雾发白，是大量小颗粒对各种波长均匀散射。判断某种颜色，要同时看入射光光谱和物体的反射、吸收、散射特性。Object color is selective reflection and scattering. White paper reflects all visible colors, a red cloth reflects mainly red; the blue sky is Rayleigh scattering of short-wavelength blue by air molecules, and Mars looks red from iron-oxide dust absorbing and reflecting light. Ice, milk and cloud whiten from many small particles scattering all wavelengths evenly. Reading a color needs both the incident spectrum and the object's reflection, absorption and scattering.[1][5]

棱镜、水滴和玻璃珠的分光角度可按折射率曲线估算。紫光与红光在玻璃中的 n 差虽小，经过两个界面和一定厚度后偏折差被放大成可观察的彩带。薄膜肥皂泡的虹彩来自上下表面反射光的干涉，与色散是两类机制；油膜彩色、光盘彩虹也源于波长相关的光程差。色散解释“为什么分开”，干涉解释“为什么有彩色条纹”。Spectrometer angles of prisms, drops and glass beads follow the index curve. The index gap between violet and red in glass is small, but two interfaces and some thickness amplify it into a visible band. Iridescent soap-film colors come from interference of reflections off two surfaces, a different mechanism from dispersion; oil-film and CD colors also follow wavelength-dependent path differences. Dispersion explains why colors separate, interference why colored fringes appear.[5]

边界：色散是 n 随波长变化，不要与白光被棱镜“染色”混为一谈，颜色本来就在白光里。天空蓝色、火星红色各有散射或表面成分原因，不能都归为同一类色散。人眼不可见的红外、紫外仍存在，只是感知不到。薄膜彩色来自干涉而非介质色散；把所有彩色现象都记成棱镜分光，会漏掉吸收、反射和干涉的作用。The boundary: dispersion means n varies with wavelength; it is not the prism 'dyeing' white light, which already contains the colors. Sky blue and Mars red each come from scattering or surface composition, not the same dispersion. Invisible infrared and ultraviolet exist, though not perceived. Film colors arise from interference, not medium dispersion; reducing every colored effect to prism splitting misses absorption, reflection and interference.[5][7]

05光从太阳到地球要多久？什么是光年？无线电波和光是什么关系？光怎样被偏振？How long does sunlight take to reach Earth, what is a light-year, how do radio waves relate to light, and how is light polarized?

真空 c=299,792,458 m/s，阳光 8 分 20 秒到地球；光与无线电波同属电磁波，横波可被偏振片定向。Vacuum c=299,792,458 m/s, sunlight arrives in about 8m20s; light and radio are both electromagnetic transverse waves selectable by a polarizer.

光在真空中以恒定速度传播。真空中光速 c=299,792,458 m/s，约 3.00×10⁸ m/s，这是国际定义的常量，不随光源或观察者运动而改变。太阳光从约 1.5 亿公里外到达地球约需 8 分 20 秒；我们看到的太阳是它八分多钟前的样子。光年是距离单位，指光在一年中走过的路程，约 9.46×10¹² 公里，不是时间单位。Light travels at a constant speed in vacuum. Vacuum light speed c=299,792,458 m/s, about 3.00×10⁸ m/s, a defined constant independent of source or observer. Sunlight from about 150 million kilometres away reaches Earth in roughly 8 minutes 20 seconds; the Sun seen now is its appearance eight-plus minutes ago. A light-year is a distance, the path light covers in one year, about 9.46×10¹² km, not a time unit.[7][8]

光是电磁波的一种。无线电波、微波、红外、可见光、紫外、X 射线和 γ 射线本质相同，都是交变电磁场在空间传播，差别只在频率和波长，满足 c=λf。无线电波波长可达数米到数千米，可见光波长仅数百纳米；不同波段产生方式和穿透能力不同，但在真空中都以 c 传播。望远镜、广播、夜视和医学影像都按同一波谱原理工作在各自波段。Light is one kind of electromagnetic wave. Radio, microwave, infrared, visible, ultraviolet, X-ray and gamma rays are the same thing: oscillating electromagnetic fields propagating in space, differing only in frequency and wavelength through c=λf. Radio waves reach metres to kilometres, visible light only hundreds of nanometres; bands differ in generation and penetration, but all travel at c in vacuum. Telescopes, broadcasting, night vision and medical imaging work on the same spectrum in their own bands.[7]

光波是横波，振动方向垂直于传播方向。普通自然光在各个横向上振动分量均匀；偏振片只允许某一方向的振动通过，把光变成线偏振。反射光在特定角度附近偏振程度增强，偏振太阳镜据此削弱路面和水面的反射眩光。液晶显示、3D 眼镜和应力检测都利用偏振；纵波（如声音）不存在偏振方向。Light is a transverse wave, vibration perpendicular to propagation. Natural light carries even vibration components across directions; a polarizer passes one vibration direction and outputs linearly polarized light. Reflected light grows polarized near a certain angle, and polarized sunglasses cut road and water glare on that basis. Liquid-crystal displays, 3D glasses and stress testing use polarization; longitudinal waves such as sound have no polarization direction.[6][7]

光子是光的能量单元，虽无静止质量却携带能量。光子能量 E=hf，h 为普朗克常量，f 为频率；高频光子能量大，紫外和 X 射线光子能损伤细胞，低频光子能量小。光既有波的干涉、衍射表现，也有光子与物质交换能量的粒子表现，波粒二象性是光学和量子物理的共同基础。A photon is the energy unit of light: though rest-massless, it carries energy. Photon energy E=hf, h the Planck constant, f frequency; high-frequency photons carry more energy, so ultraviolet and X-ray photons can damage cells, low-frequency photons less. Light shows wave effects like interference and diffraction and particle effects exchanging energy with matter; wave-particle duality underlies optics and quantum physics.[7][8]

边界：光速 c 是真空中的上限，介质中光速 v=c/n 更慢；不要把介质中慢下来的光当成光速改变。光年是距离，不能写成时间长度。偏振只对横波成立，声波纵波不能被偏振片选择方向。光子概念是理解光与物质作用的基础。电磁波谱各波段连续衔接，但人眼只感知 400–700 nm；把可见颜色当成光的全部范围，会漏掉红外、紫外等波段。The boundary: c is the vacuum upper limit; in a medium v=c/n is slower, and that slowdown does not change light speed itself. A light-year is distance, not time. Polarization applies to transverse waves only, and longitudinal sound cannot be selected by a polarizer. The electromagnetic spectrum connects continuously, but the eye senses only 400–700 nm; taking visible colors as the whole range misses infrared, ultraviolet and other bands.[6][7]

06光从空气进入水中为什么会偏折？池水为什么看起来比实际浅？镜子里的人为什么左右相反？Why does light bend entering water, why does a pool look shallower than it is, and why does a mirror reverse left and right?

折射来自波速变化与 n=c/v，斯涅尔定律决定偏折方向；视深约为实深的 3/4；平面镜反转前后方向，左右相反是视角约定。Refraction comes from wave-speed change and n=c/v; Snell's law sets the bend; apparent depth is about 3/4 of real depth, and a mirror reverses front-back, read as left-right reversal.

光从一种介质斜射入另一种介质时方向偏折，根子在于两种介质里光速不同。折射率 n=c/v，v 是介质中光速，c 是真空光速；空气 n≈1.0003，水≈1.33，玻璃≈1.5。波前斜到界面时，先进入密介质的一端先减速，另一端仍走得快，波前方向转动，光线向法线偏折；回到疏介质时反过来，光线远离法线。偏折由斯涅尔定律 n1·sinθ1=n2·sinθ2 决定，两角沿界面法线测量。Light bends obliquely between media because wave speed differs. Refractive index n=c/v, v the speed in the medium and c the vacuum speed: air about 1.0003, water about 1.33, glass about 1.5. A wavefront reaching the interface at an angle slows first on the edge entering the denser medium, while the other edge stays faster, so the wavefront rotates and the ray bends toward the normal; leaving into a sparser medium it bends away. Snell's law n1·sinθ1=n2·sinθ2 sets the angle, measured from the interface normal.[4]

池水看起来比实际浅，是同一规律的视觉表现。水底反射光出水时偏折，眼睛沿反向延长线把像抬高；视深 d′=d/n，水 n≈1.33，看起来深度约为实际的四分之三。涉水时容易被这种错觉误导，以为很浅，踩下去才发现没过腰。透镜放大、眼镜矫正、看水里鱼的位置偏移，都是界面折射后的位置重排。A pool looks shallower by the same rule. Bottom-reflected light bends on leaving water, and the eye extends rays backward to raise the image; apparent depth d′=d/n, so water near 1.33 looks about three quarters as deep. Wade in and the illusion makes it seem shallow until it reaches the waist. Lens magnification, eyeglass correction and the displaced position of a fish in water are all position rearrangements after interface refraction.[3][4]

平面镜成等大正立虚像，像在镜后与物体等距。镜面真正反转的是垂直镜面的前后坐标：靠近和远离镜面的一侧被对调，左右上下并没有被物理翻转。人面对镜子时，习惯把镜中像当成转身面对自己的人，按这个转身约定比较方位，左右才显得对调。镜子不是把左耳换到右边，而是前后翻转后，视角约定造成左右相反的观感。A plane mirror forms an upright equal virtual image behind the glass at equal distance. What it reverses is the front-back coordinate normal to the surface: the near and far sides are swapped, while left, right, up and down are not physically flipped. Facing the mirror, people read the image as a person turned around to face them, and by that convention sides appear to exchange. The mirror does not move the left ear to the right; the left-right reading follows the front-back flip plus the viewing convention.[2][3]

觉得镜子里比照片好看，有两类因素。一是熟悉性：平时反复看到镜像，大脑对这个左右版本更熟悉；照片是别人视角的相反版本，略有陌生感，熟悉偏好让镜像更顺眼。二是光照：镜前光线常正面均匀照亮面部，照片的角度、阴影和距离各不相同。需要如实说明，镜中脸是否客观更好看并无统一实验结论，这更多是主观感受与知觉偏差；表情、距离、镜头畸变也会改变观感。Finding yourself better looking in the mirror mixes two factors. One is familiarity: you repeatedly see the mirror version, so the brain knows that left-right arrangement better, while a photo records the opposite viewpoint and feels slightly unfamiliar; mere exposure favors the mirror image. The other is lighting: light in front of a mirror often evenly lights the face, while photos differ in angle, shadow and distance. Honestly, whether the mirror face is objectively more attractive has no unified experimental conclusion; this is mainly subjective perception, and expression, distance and lens distortion also matter.[2][3]

边界：折射只发生在透明介质界面，不透明物体谈不上折射；筷子弯折、池底变浅都是位置偏移，物体本身没动。镜子反转前后，把左右相反当成镜面物理互换会误判。折射率随波长、温度变化，1.33、1.5 是常见近似。镜子更好看不能当成客观测量结论，其中混合了熟悉性、光照和拍摄条件。Boundary: refraction occurs only at a transparent interface; opaque objects have none. The bent straw and raised bottom are apparent-position shifts, not physical movement. A mirror reverses front to back, and treating left-right reversal as a physical swap misreads the optics. Index varies with wavelength and temperature, so 1.33 and 1.5 are approximations. The mirror preference is not an objective measurement; it mixes familiarity, lighting and shooting conditions.[2][4]

07肥皂泡和水面油膜为什么是彩色的？Why are soap bubbles and oil films on water colored?

薄膜上下两个表面反射的两束光叠加，路程差随厚度变化，不同波长在不同厚度处分别加强或抵消，于是显出彩色。Light reflected from the top and bottom film surfaces overlaps; the path difference varies with thickness, so different wavelengths reinforce or cancel at different spots and colors appear.

肥皂泡和水面油膜都隔着一层很薄的透明膜。光线射到膜上时，一部分在膜的上表面直接反射；另一部分穿进薄膜，在膜的下表面，也就是与水或空气接触的界面，反射后再穿出来。这样就得到两束来自同一入射光、但走过路程不同的反射光。两束光频率相同、来自同一光源，满足互相叠加干涉的条件。可见光波长约 400–700 nm，只有薄膜厚度与这一尺度可比时，两束反射光的路程差才落在干涉敏感的范围内。Soap bubbles and oil films on water are covered by a very thin transparent layer. When light reaches the film, part reflects directly off the top surface, while another part travels into the film, reflects off the bottom surface where it meets water or air, and then comes back out. This produces two reflected beams from the same incident light that have traveled different paths. The two beams share the same frequency and source, so they meet the conditions for interference.[9]

两束光相遇时会发生干涉。如果路程差恰好等于某个波长的整数倍，两束波在该波长上同相叠加，亮度加强；如果路程差是半波长的奇数倍，波峰对波谷，相互抵消。路程差由薄膜厚度和光在膜内的折射角度共同决定，膜厚不同，被加强的波长也就不同。反射时在上、下界面还可能发生半波损失，使明暗条件整体平移，但随厚度变化显色的规律不变。The two beams overlap and interfere. If the path difference equals a whole number of wavelengths, the waves add in phase at that wavelength and brighten; if it equals an odd half-wavelength, crest meets trough and they cancel. The path difference is set by film thickness and the refraction angle inside the film, so the wavelength that is reinforced changes with thickness. Reflection at the top and bottom interfaces may also introduce a half-wave loss that shifts the bright-dark pattern overall, but the rule that color varies with thickness stays the same.[5][9]

白光是多种波长的混合。薄膜厚度并不均匀：肥皂泡受气流和重力影响不断变薄、流动，油膜也厚薄不一。在某一厚度位置，波长偏长的红、黄可能被加强；在另一厚度位置，偏短的蓝、绿被加强。不同颜色随厚度分布展开，肉眼就看到泡膜和油膜上一片片流动的彩色条纹，随着膜变薄或人走动，颜色还会不断移动。肥皂膜厚度常只有约 100 nm 到数 μm，水面油膜也多在同一量级；厚度的细小变化就足以改变哪一种颜色被加强。White light mixes many wavelengths. The film thickness is not uniform: airflow and gravity keep the soap film thinning and flowing, and the oil film also varies in depth. At one thickness the longer red and yellow wavelengths may reinforce; at another the shorter blue and green wavelengths reinforce. As these colors spread with the thickness pattern, the eye sees patches of flowing color across the bubble and oil slick, and the colors keep moving as the film thins or the observer walks.[9]

这种颜色和棱镜分光不是一回事。棱镜靠不同颜色折射率不同，把白光在空间上分散开；薄膜彩色靠的是两束反射光的叠加干涉，同一束光自己和自己叠加。肥皂泡越吹越薄，薄膜厚度接近可见光波长甚至更薄时，加强的颜色逐渐变淡；快破时膜面常接近暗黑色，就是因为干涉条件改变、反射光被削弱。光盘背面、镀膜镜片上的彩光也是同一类薄层反射干涉。This color differs from prism dispersion. A prism spreads white light spatially because each color bends by a different amount; film color comes from interference between two reflected beams, the same light interfering with itself. As a soap bubble thins toward the visible wavelength range or below, the reinforced colors fade; near bursting the film often looks nearly dark, because the interference condition changes and reflected light weakens. The sheen on compact discs and coated lenses is the same kind of thin-layer reflection interference.[5][7]

边界要写清：薄膜干涉要求膜厚与光波波长可比。膜太厚时，不同位置、不同次反射的光错开太多，条纹互相混叠，肉眼就看不出彩色；观察角度改变时，光穿过膜的路程随之变化，颜色也会跟着偏移。日常看到的虹彩并不是膜里含有染料，而是结构对光的选择叠加；离开薄膜或油膜之后，这种彩色也就随之消失。The boundary matters: thin-film interference requires the film thickness to be comparable to the wavelength of light. When the film is very thick, reflections from different positions and passes overlap too much, the fringes blur together and no color is visible; changing the viewing angle changes the path through the film, so the colors shift. The everyday iridescence is not a dye in the film but a selective superposition of light by structure; once the film or oil layer is gone, the color disappears with it.[9]
