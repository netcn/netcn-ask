01为什么吹出来的泡泡总是圆的，表面张力和气泡内外压差有什么关系？Why are bubbles round, and how do surface tension and the pressure difference across the film relate?

表面张力把液膜面积压到最小形成球形；拉普拉斯压差与曲率半径成反比。Surface tension minimizes film area into a sphere; Laplace pressure scales inversely with radius of curvature.

吹出来的泡泡总是接近球形，直接原因是液体表面存在表面张力：液膜像一张绷紧的弹性膜，会把表面积收缩到给定体积下的最小值，而相同体积下表面积最小的形状就是球体。肥皂膜有内外两层表面，两侧张力共同把膜拉向最紧凑的形态，所以气泡、水滴在没有外力干扰时都趋向球形。Bubbles come out nearly spherical because liquid surface tension acts like a stretched elastic film: it shrinks the surface area to the minimum possible for a given volume, and the shape with the smallest area for a fixed volume is a sphere. A soap film has two surfaces, and both pull the membrane toward the most compact form, which is why bubbles and droplets tend to be round when nothing disturbs them.[1]

表面张力来自液体分子之间的内聚力：内部分子被四周分子对称吸引，合力接近零；表面分子只受到向内和侧向的拉扯，形成沿液面收缩的趋势。单位长度上的拉力就是表面张力系数，水在常温下约为 0.072 N/m，加入肥皂后膜的稳定性提高，气泡才不至于一拉就破。液膜越薄、杂质越多，局部张力不均就越容易造成破裂。Surface tension arises from cohesion between liquid molecules. A molecule in the bulk is pulled symmetrically from all sides with near-zero net force, while a surface molecule is pulled inward and sideways, producing a shrinking trend along the interface. The force per unit length is the surface tension coefficient; water at room temperature is about 0.072 N/m, and adding soap stabilizes the film so the bubble does not tear at once. Thinner films or more impurities make local tension uneven and favor rupture.[5]

气泡内外存在微小压差，即拉普拉斯压强：曲率半径越小，压差越大，球形液面两侧的压强差与表面张力系数成正比、与半径成反比。肥皂泡有两个表面，压差约为 4γ/R；单个液滴只有一个表面，压差约为 2γ/R。这也解释了为什么极小的气泡内部压强更高，而大气泡在膜变薄后更容易破裂。A small pressure difference exists across the bubble wall, the Laplace pressure: the smaller the radius of curvature, the larger the difference, and the pressure gap across a curved surface scales with tension divided by radius. A soap bubble with two surfaces has a gap near 4γ/R, while a single droplet has one surface and a gap near 2γ/R. This also explains why very small bubbles carry higher internal pressure and why large bubbles break more easily as the film thins.[4]

证据来自肥皂膜实验与毛细管测量：在金属环上形成的液膜会把轻小物体拉向膜面，毛细管中液面上升高度与表面张力直接相关；不同液体的张力系数可由拉脱法或毛细管法测出，温度升高时张力系数通常略有下降。把肥皂膜换成纯水，膜容易破裂，正是因为纯水表面张力高、膜缺乏稳定的双表面结构，液膜在局部变薄后无法维持张力平衡。Soap-film and capillary measurements support the picture: a film on a wire ring pulls light objects toward the membrane, and the rise height in a capillary tube relates directly to surface tension. Tension coefficients for different liquids are measured by pull-off or capillary methods. Pure water films break easily because high tension leaves the membrane without a stable two-surface structure.[1][4]

边界在于：球形是自由液膜在静止、无重力干扰时的平衡形状。气流、振动、重力拉伸和表面杂质都会让气泡变成椭球或不规则形状；表面张力只决定面积最小化的趋势，不能解释气泡为什么能被吹大，也不改变气体本身的压强规律、温度变化和体积变化关系。下一章回到日常体感，看热量究竟通过哪几种方式从身体或物体传走。The sphere is the equilibrium shape only for a free film at rest, without gravity or airflow. Wind, vibration, gravitational stretching and impurities turn bubbles into ellipsoids or irregular shapes; tension only drives area minimization and does not explain why a bubble can be inflated, nor the gas pressure laws themselves. The next chapter turns to everyday sensations and asks how heat actually leaves the body or an object.[4][5]

02扇扇子为什么凉快，摸铁为什么比摸木头冷，衣服和发抖又是怎样帮助保温的？Why does fanning feel cool, why does metal feel colder than wood, and how do clothing and shivering keep us warm?

对流加速蒸发散热，传导速率由热导率决定，衣物靠锁住静止空气隔热，发抖产热。Convection speeds evaporative cooling, conduction rate depends on k, clothing traps insulating air, and shivering generates heat.

扇扇子觉得凉快，不是因为风把温度吹低了，而是空气流动加快了皮肤表面汗液的蒸发与对流换热；摸铁比摸木头冷，是因为铁的热导率远高于木材，把手上的热量更快导走；冬天穿衣服保暖，靠的是织物锁住的静止空气这层低导热介质。三者分别对应对流、传导和隔热三条传热路径，而发抖则是肌肉不自主收缩把化学能转化为热量。Fanning feels cool not because the wind lowers the air temperature, but because moving air speeds up sweat evaporation and convective heat transfer at the skin. Metal feels colder than wood because steel conducts heat away from the hand much faster; winter clothing keeps warm by trapping still air, a low-conductivity layer. These map onto convection, conduction and insulation, while shivering turns chemical energy into heat through involuntary muscle contraction.[2]

热通过三种基本方式传递：传导依靠分子碰撞在固体或静止流体中传递内能，对流靠流体整体流动带走热量，辐射则以电磁波形式在不接触的物体间传递。人体散热主要靠皮肤血管扩张、汗液蒸发和周围空气对流；环境温度低于体温时，身体持续向周围失热，衣物和毛发减少对流与辐射损失。Heat moves in three basic ways. Conduction passes internal energy through molecular collisions in solids or still fluids, convection carries heat by bulk fluid motion, and radiation transfers it between non-touching objects as electromagnetic waves. Human heat loss relies mainly on vasodilation in skin, sweat evaporation and convection to surrounding air; when the environment is below body temperature, the body keeps losing heat outward, and clothing or fur cuts those convective and radiative losses.[3]

材料的导热能力用热导率 k 表示，单位 W/(m·K)。常温下静止空气的 k 约 0.026 W/(m·K)，是很好的隔热层；干燥木材约 0.12 W/(m·K)，钢铁则在 50 至 80 W/(m·K) 量级。所以同一温度下，摸铁时热量从手快速流出，摸木头时流出缓慢，冷暖感受取决于传热速率而不是材料的实际温度。对流换热还受流速、温差和表面形状影响，换热系数 h 越大，单位时间带走的热量越多。A material's conducting ability is thermal conductivity k in W/(m·K). At room temperature, still air is about 0.026 W/(m·K), a good insulator; dry wood is near 0.12 W/(m·K), while steel ranges from 50 to 80 W/(m·K). At the same temperature, heat flows out of the hand rapidly on metal but slowly on wood, so the sensation depends on heat-transfer rate rather than the material's actual temperature. Convection also depends on flow speed, temperature difference and surface shape; a higher convective coefficient h removes more heat per unit time.[5]

证据来自热导率表与传热实验：不同材料在相同温差下的导热速率可由平板导热装置测量；风速提高时皮肤散热曲线明显上升；衣物隔热性能与织物内滞留空气量相关。发抖时骨骼肌高频微收缩消耗 ATP，释放的能量大部分成为热，使产热高于安静状态。Conductivity tables and heat-transfer experiments support this: a flat-plate apparatus measures conduction under matched temperature differences, skin cooling rises with wind speed, and clothing insulation tracks trapped air volume. Shivering uses high-frequency small skeletal-muscle contractions that consume ATP, and most released energy becomes heat, raising output above the resting level.[2][5]

边界：扇子不能降低环境气温，它只加速局部蒸发和对流；当气温接近或高于体温时，对流反而会把热量带给身体，出汗蒸发成为主要散热途径。触感冷不等于物体温度低，金属在凉天感觉更冰是导热快的结果；隔热材料一旦被水浸透，水取代空气成为导热介质，保暖效果会大幅下降。下一章讨论热空气为什么上升，以及烟、火焰和哈气背后的对流与凝结过程。A fan cannot lower ambient temperature; it only accelerates local evaporation and convection. When air approaches or exceeds body temperature, convection adds heat instead, and evaporative sweat becomes the main route. Feeling cold does not mean the object is cold: metal feels icy on a cool day because it conducts fast. Once insulation is soaked, water replaces air as the conducting medium and warmth drops sharply. The next chapter explains why hot air rises and how convection and condensation shape smoke, flames and breath fog.[3][5]

03烟囱的烟和火焰为什么向上冒，冬天嘴里的哈气又为什么会变成白雾？Why do chimney smoke and flame rise, and why does winter breath form a white fog?

加热空气密度变小受浮力上升形成羽流；暖湿哈气遇冷凝结成微米级水滴成雾。Heated low-density air rises by buoyancy as a plume; moist breath condenses into micrometer droplets on cooling.

烟囱里的烟和火焰都向上走，直接原因是被加热的空气膨胀、密度变小，在周围较冷较重的空气中受到向上的浮力；哈气在冬天变成白雾，则是呼出的暖湿气体遇冷后，水蒸气凝结成细小水滴。前者属于对流羽流，后者属于相变凝结，两者常同时出现在同一团热气流里。Smoke and flame rise because heated air expands, becomes less dense and receives an upward buoyant force in cooler, heavier surrounding air. Breath fog in winter appears when warm moist exhaled air cools and water vapor condenses into tiny droplets. The first is a convective plume, the second a phase-change condensation, and they often occur in the same hot airstream.[3]

空气受热后分子间距增大、体积膨胀，单位体积质量减小；根据阿基米德原理，流体中密度较小的物体受到向上的浮力，当浮力超过羽流自身重量时就上升。上升过程中周围冷空气不断从下方补入，形成持续的对流循环。火焰本身是燃烧区加热空气和燃烧产物的可见部分，热羽流带着烟尘继续上升，直到热量扩散、温度与周围接近为止，羽流边缘还会卷入周围空气并逐渐变宽。When air heats up, molecular spacing grows and volume expands, so mass per unit volume falls. By Archimedes' principle, a less dense body in a fluid experiences upward buoyancy; when this exceeds the plume's own weight, it rises. Cold air keeps entering from below to replace it, sustaining a convection loop. The flame is the visible part where combustion heats air and products of combustion, and the hot plume carries smoke upward until heat spreads and temperature matches the surroundings.[1]

冬天口中哈出的气体温度高、含水量大，进入低温环境后饱和蒸气压下降，多余水蒸气凝结成微米级小水滴，悬浮成可见雾团；等水滴蒸发或被吹散，雾就消失。烟囱顶或降落伞顶上的开孔另有作用：排出热空气并稳定气流，避免羽流在设备周围乱窜；降落伞顶孔让部分气流穿过，减少伞面摆动，提高下落稳定性。Winter breath carries warm, high-moisture gas. Entering cold air lowers its saturation vapor pressure, and excess vapor condenses into micrometer-scale droplets that hang as a visible cloud; the fog disappears once droplets evaporate or disperse. Vents at chimney tops or parachute crowns serve a separate purpose: they release hot air and steady the flow so the plume does not swirl around the device. A parachute crown hole lets some air pass through, reducing canopy oscillation and stabilizing descent.[5]

证据来自浮力定律和流场观测：加热空气的密度随温度升高而降低，热羽流的上升速度可由浮力与阻力平衡估算；冷凝实验中，暖湿气流进入冷环境后出现可见水滴，升温或干燥后雾团消散。烟囱排烟效率与温差、抽力和烟道形状相关，顶孔通风设计在飞行器和降落伞工程中经过风洞验证。Buoyancy laws and flow observations support this: heated air density drops with temperature, plume rise speed balances buoyancy against drag, and condensation experiments show visible droplets when warm moist gas meets cold surroundings, with the cloud vanishing on warming or drying. Chimney draft depends on temperature difference, pull and flue shape, and crown-vent designs for aircraft and parachutes are validated in wind tunnels.[3][5]

边界：热空气上升是在重力场中由密度差驱动的，失重或无风密闭环境里对流会显著减弱；火焰向上也依赖重力下的浮力羽流，微重力环境中火焰会接近球形。哈气成雾只在外界足够冷、呼出气体含水量足够高时出现，干燥寒冷天气里雾团很快消散。下一章进入流体压强，解释吸管吸水的极限、大气压为何压不垮身体，以及液体为什么几乎不可压缩。Hot-air rise is driven by density differences in a gravitational field; in weightlessness or a still sealed chamber, convection weakens sharply. Flame shape also relies on gravity-driven buoyancy: in microgravity flames approach a sphere. Breath fog appears only when the outside is cold enough and exhaled moisture high enough, and it dissipates quickly in dry cold air. The next chapter turns to fluid pressure, the suction limit of a straw, why atmospheric pressure does not crush the body, and why liquids are nearly incompressible.[1][5]

04吸管吸水为什么有约十米的极限，大气压为什么压不垮身体，液体真的不可压缩吗？Why is suction limited to about ten meters, why does atmospheric pressure not crush the body, and are liquids truly incompressible?

吸力由大气压驱动，理论水柱约 10.3 米；身体内外压平衡；水体积模量约 2.2 GPa。Suction is atmospheric-pressure driven, theoretical water head about 10.3 m; the body balances internal and external pressure; water bulk modulus about 2.2 GPa.

用吸管吸水并不是嘴把水吸上来，而是嘴降低管内气压，管外的大气压把水压进管里。大气压约为 101.3 kPa，相当于每平方厘米约 1 千克力；理论上靠大气压能支撑的水柱高度约 10.3 米（约 33.9 英尺），这就是普通吸管或离心泵存在吸水极限的原因。超过这个高度，哪怕把管内抽成真空，水也无法继续被压上来。A straw does not pull water upward; the mouth lowers pressure inside the tube, and outside atmospheric pressure pushes water in. Atmospheric pressure is about 101.3 kPa, roughly one kilogram-force per square centimeter, and it can theoretically support a water column near 10.3 meters (about 33.9 feet), which sets the suction limit for ordinary straws and centrifugal pumps. Above this height, even a vacuum in the tube cannot push water higher.[1]

液体压强随深度线性增加：P = ρgh，其中 ρ 是密度，g 是重力加速度，h 是深度。海水密度约 1025 kg/m³，每下潜十米压强增加约一个大气压。人感受不到约 14.7 磅每平方英寸的大气压，是因为身体内部的血液、组织液和肺部气体与外部压强基本平衡，外耳道、鼻窦和体腔内的压力同时向内向外作用，净压强差很小。Liquid pressure rises linearly with depth, P = ρgh, where ρ is density, g gravity and h depth. Seawater density is about 1025 kg/m³, so each ten meters of descent adds roughly one atmosphere. A person does not feel the 14.7 psi atmospheric load because blood, interstitial fluid and lung gas nearly balance the external pressure; pressure in ear canal, sinuses and body cavities acts inward and outward at the same time, leaving a small net difference.[4]

水和多数液体被认为几乎不可压缩，是因为它们的体积模量很大：水的体积模量约 2.2 GPa，要让体积缩小百分之一量级需要数百个大气压。与气体相比，液体分子间距已经很小，继续压缩需要把分子推到排斥力很强的距离，所以加压后密度变化很小；但并非绝对不可压缩，深海高压下水的密度仍会随深度略有增加。Water and most liquids are called nearly incompressible because their bulk modulus is large: water near 2.2 GPa, so shrinking volume by about one percent needs hundreds of atmospheres. Compared with gas, liquid molecules already sit close together, and further compression pushes them into strongly repulsive distances, leaving little density change under pressure. Liquids are not absolutely incompressible, however; deep-sea pressure raises water density slightly with depth.[2]

证据来自压强计测量、流体静力学实验和材料数据：底部开口的容器中液面高度与压强关系由 ρgh 给出；吸力泵的吸水高度受大气压限制，更深的抽水必须把泵放在水下或加压输送。身体内外压平衡可由耳膜在气压变化时的受压感验证，压强不平衡时才会出现胀痛或耳膜鼓胀。Manometer readings, hydrostatic experiments and material data support this: the open-bottom container links liquid height to pressure through ρgh, suction pumps are limited by atmospheric pressure, and deeper pumping needs submerged or pressurized delivery. The body's internal-external balance can be felt when ear pressure shifts with altitude; only an imbalance produces pain or a bulging eardrum.[1][2]

边界：10 米左右的极限是清水在标准大气压、忽略管内真空度损失时的理论值；实际吸管有摩擦、漏气和液体汽化，极限更低。身体不受压垮依赖内部压力与外部持续平衡，外伤、耳部不通或高空低压会打破这种平衡。液体不可压缩是工程近似，在高压、深水或爆炸冲击条件下，体积模量和密度变化必须纳入计算。下一章讨论能量从哪里来、用完后去了哪里，以及温度的下限在哪里。The ten-meter limit is a theoretical figure for clean water at standard atmosphere, ignoring vacuum loss; real straws lose height to friction, leaks and vaporization. The body survives because internal pressure keeps matching the outside, and injury, blocked ear passages or low altitude pressure can break that balance. Incompressibility is an engineering approximation; under high pressure, deep water or blast impact, bulk modulus and density changes must be included. The final chapter asks where energy comes from, where it goes after use, and where the lower temperature limit lies.[2][4]

05能量是什么、从哪里来，用完之后去了哪里，温度的绝对零度又是怎么回事？What is energy and where does it come from, where does it go after use, and what is absolute zero?

能量守恒总量不变，耗散热降低可利用品位；0 K=-273.15 °C 是不可达的理论下限。Total energy is conserved, dispersed heat lowers usable grade; 0 K (-273.15 °C) is an unreachable lower limit.

能量是做功或传热的能力，它不会被凭空创造，也不会真正用完；日常所说的消耗，是把一种形式的能量转化为另一种形式，其中相当一部分最终以热能形式耗散到环境中。烤面包前后质量略有变化，主要是水分蒸发和气体逸出，而不是物质凭空消失；能量守恒与质量守恒分别约束着转换过程的不同侧面。Energy is the capacity to do work or transfer heat. It is neither created from nothing nor truly used up; everyday consumption converts one form into another, and a large fraction ends as heat dispersed into the environment. Bread loses some mass during baking mainly through evaporating water and escaping gas, not through matter vanishing; conservation of energy and conservation of mass constrain different sides of the conversion.[4]

热力学第一定律给出能量守恒：系统内能变化等于传入热量加上对系统所做的功。机械能、电能、化学能、热能和光能可以相互转换，但总量保持不变，计入摩擦损耗和环境散热后账目仍然平衡。热力学第二定律则说明能量转换有方向：耗散成分散热之后，这部分能量难以再集中起来做功，所以可利用的能量品位会下降，这正是机器需要持续燃料、电池需要充电的原因。The first law of thermodynamics states that a system's internal-energy change equals added heat plus work done on it. Mechanical, electrical, chemical, thermal and radiant energy can convert into one another while the total stays constant. The second law gives the direction: once energy disperses as spread-out heat, it is hard to concentrate again for work, so the usable grade declines, which is why machines keep needing fuel and batteries keep needing charge.[1]

绝对零度是温度的理论下限，记为 0 K，等于 -273.15 °C。它对应分子热运动动能最低的状态，但量子力学仍保留零点能；现有技术只能无限接近，不能真正达到。温度越高，分子平均动能越大；热传递本质上是内能从高温区域向低温区域转移，直到温度趋于平衡。Absolute zero is the theoretical lower temperature limit, 0 K or -273.15 °C, corresponding to the lowest thermal-motion energy level, though quantum theory retains zero-point energy. Technology can approach it indefinitely but cannot reach it exactly. Higher temperature means larger average molecular kinetic energy, and heat transfer moves internal energy from hotter to colder regions until temperatures tend to balance.[5]

证据来自量热实验、发动机效率测量和温标定义：燃料燃烧释放的化学能可由热值与做功、散热逐项核对；热机效率永远低于 100%，损失部分主要变成排气和环境散热。低温实验室通过绝热稀释等方法把样品温度压到毫开尔文量级，仍未触及 0 K；水的沸点随压强变化的测量也支持温度与相变的对应关系。Calorimetry, engine-efficiency measurements and temperature-scale definitions support this: chemical energy from fuel checks against heat value, work and losses; heat-engine efficiency stays below 100%, with losses mainly in exhaust and cooling. Low-temperature labs use adiabatic dilution and other methods to reach millikelvin ranges without touching 0 K, and boiling-point measurements versus pressure confirm the temperature-to-phase relationship.[4][5]

边界：能量守恒不意味着转换过程可逆，耗散热难以重新收集利用；说能量用完，指的是可做功的品位下降，而不是能量总量减少。烤面包变轻是水分和挥发物离开面团，剩余固体并没有被销毁。绝对零度是外推的理论极限，不能当作已经达到的实验温度；讨论具体设备时，还必须区分一次能源、二次能源和最终散失的热量。Conservation does not make conversion reversible: dispersed heat is difficult to recapture. Saying energy runs out means its work grade has fallen, not that the total has shrunk. Bread lightens because water and volatiles leave the dough; the remaining solids are not destroyed. Absolute zero is an extrapolated theoretical limit, not a reached experimental temperature, and concrete equipment analysis must distinguish primary energy, secondary energy and finally dispersed heat.[1][5]
