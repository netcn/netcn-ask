01装满水的杯子盖上纸倒立，水为什么不会流出来？水压随深度怎样变化？Why does water stay in an upside-down glass covered with paper, and how does pressure vary with depth?

大气压托住纸片，杯内水压随深度 p=ρgh 增大；杯内水面上方气压低，大气压足以托住水柱。Atmospheric pressure holds the paper, water pressure grows with depth p=ρgh; trapped air above the water is rarefied, so air pressure supports the column.

装满水的杯子盖上硬纸片再倒立，纸片托住整杯水不落下，靠的是大气压。杯内水对纸片向下的压强有限，而纸片下方是整层大气，向上的大气压约 101.3 kPa；只要纸片不漏气、水柱不太高，向上的气压就大于水压加水重，纸片被压在杯口，水不会流出。杯内水面上方若留有少量空气，倒立后气压降低，内外压差更容易托住水柱。A glass full of water, covered with a stiff paper and turned upside down, keeps the water in place because of atmospheric pressure. The water pushes down on the paper with limited pressure, while below the paper sits the whole atmosphere, about 101.3 kPa upward. As long as the paper seals and the column is short, the upward air pressure exceeds water pressure plus the water weight, so the paper stays on the rim and no water spills. A little trapped air above the water expands when inverted, lowering its pressure and making the balance easier to hold.[2]

流体静压强随深度增大。静止液体中深度 h 处的压强 p=ρgh，ρ 是液体密度，g 取约 9.81 m/s²。水的密度约 1000 kg/m³，每下降 10 米，水压约增加一个大气压；水下越深，压强越大，潜水器和堤坝都要按深度设计耐压结构。同一水平面上压强相等，压强差推动流体流动。Static fluid pressure grows with depth. At depth h in a stationary liquid, p=ρgh, where ρ is density and g about 9.81 m/s². Water density is about 1000 kg/m³, so every 10 metres of depth adds roughly one atmosphere of pressure; deeper water presses harder, which is why submersibles and dams are built to withstand depth-dependent loads. Pressure is equal on a horizontal level, and a pressure difference drives flow.[1][2]

倒立水杯演示的是大气压与水压的平衡。标准大气压约能托起约 10.3 米高的水柱（约 760 mm 汞柱），日常杯子十几厘米的水柱远在这个限度以内。马德堡半球、吸盘贴墙、吸管吸水，都是同一原理：内部气压被抽低后，外部大气压把物体压住或把液体压上来。吸盘挤出空气后内部接近低压，大气压把吸盘压在光滑墙面；吸管吸水时先吸出管内空气，液面上方气压降低，大气压把水压入嘴里。The inverted-glass demonstration balances atmospheric pressure against water pressure. Standard atmosphere supports a water column about 10.3 metres high (equivalent to 760 mm of mercury), and an ordinary glass column of a dozen centimetres sits well below that limit. Magdeburg hemispheres, suction cups on walls and drinking through a straw all use the same idea: once internal pressure is lowered, external atmospheric pressure holds the object or pushes the liquid up.[2]

气体压强同样来自流体重量，但空气密度随高度快速减小。近地面空气密度约 1.20 kg/m³，比水小三个量级，所以大气压强主要由整层空气的重量累积而成；高山上气压降低，水的沸点也随之下降。水杯盖纸实验若纸片边缘漏气，内外气压连通，水压就会把纸和水一起压落。Gas pressure also comes from the weight of a fluid, but air density drops rapidly with altitude. Near the ground air density is about 1.20 kg/m³, three orders below water, so atmospheric pressure accumulates from the weight of the whole air column. Pressure falls on high mountains, and the boiling point drops with it. If the paper edge leaks, inside and outside pressures connect, and water pressure pushes the paper and water down together.[1][2]

边界：大气压托住的是纸片和水柱，不是“水被吸住”；杯内必须没有连通大气的缝隙。p=ρgh 适用于静止、均质流体，流动的水中还要叠加动压。把大气压当成一种“吸力”不准确，它本质是气体分子碰撞产生的压强；高度、温度和湿度都会改变实际气压值，气压随海拔升高而降低。The boundary: the atmosphere supports the paper and column; the water is not 'sucked' in. The cup must have no gap open to the air. p=ρgh applies to a stationary homogeneous fluid, while flowing water also carries dynamic pressure. Treating atmospheric pressure as a suction is imprecise: it is really pressure from molecular collisions, and altitude, temperature and humidity all shift the actual value.[2][3]

02气球为什么能飞上去？什么决定物体在水里是浮还是沉？Why does a balloon rise, and what decides whether an object floats or sinks in water?

浮力等于排开流体的重量 ρgV；平均密度小于流体密度就上浮，大于则下沉；氦气密度远小于空气，气球净浮力向上。Buoyancy equals displaced fluid weight ρgV; rise when average density is lower than the fluid, sink otherwise; helium is far lighter than air, so net buoyancy lifts.

气球能飞上去，是因为受到的浮力大于自身重量。浸在流体中的物体受到向上的浮力，大小等于排开流体的重量：Fb=ρfluid·g·V。气球排开体积 V 的空气，空气重量就是浮力；气球本身、外皮和内部气体的总重量向下。飞艇用氦气或热空气提供净升力。浮力大于总重，气球上升；两者相等则悬浮；浮力小于总重，就下沉。A balloon rises because buoyancy exceeds its weight. A body immersed in a fluid receives an upward buoyant force equal to the weight of displaced fluid: Fb=ρfluid·g·V. The balloon displaces a volume V of air, whose weight is the buoyancy, while balloon envelope, payload and internal gas pull downward. If buoyancy exceeds total weight the balloon rises; equal forces hold it suspended; smaller buoyancy makes it sink.[3]

判断浮沉看平均密度。物体整体平均密度小于流体密度，排开同体积流体更重，浮力占优，上浮；平均密度大于流体密度则下沉。氦气在 20 °C、一个大气压下密度约 0.166 kg/m³，空气约 1.20 kg/m³；充氦气球外皮很轻，整体平均密度低于空气，净浮力向上。热气球则靠加热空气降低内部密度，温度越高、内部空气越稀，浮力余量越大。Whether something floats depends on average density. When the body's overall average density is lower than the fluid, the displaced volume weighs more and buoyancy wins; higher density means sinking. Helium at 20 °C and one atmosphere has density about 0.166 kg/m³ against air about 1.20 kg/m³; a light-envelope helium balloon's overall average density sits below air, and net buoyancy points up. A hot-air balloon lowers internal density by heating: the hotter the air, the thinner it is and the larger the buoyancy margin.[1][3]

阿基米德原理给出定量关系。船用钢铁造却能浮，是因为船体内部留出大量空气，排开水的总重量等于船重；把船压进水、水灌入舱内，平均密度升高到超过水，船就沉。鱼通过鱼鳔调节身体体积，改变排开水的体积，从而在不同水层悬浮。同一艘船从淡水驶入海水，海水密度略大，吃水深度会稍浅。密度差是浮力问题的主线，材料本身轻重只是其中一部分。潜艇不靠改变体积，而向压载水舱注水增加自重，重力超过浮力便下潜；再用压缩空气把水排出、减重后上浮。Archimedes' principle gives the quantitative relation. A steel ship floats because its hull encloses lots of air, and the displaced water weighs as much as the ship; if water floods in and raises the average density above water, it sinks. Fish adjust volume through the swim bladder to change displaced volume and hover at different depths. Density difference is the main thread, and the material's own weight is only part of it.A submarine does not change its volume; it fills ballast tanks with water to increase weight, and sinks once weight exceeds buoyancy, then compresses air to push the water out and rise again.[2][3]

气球上升过程中浮力会变化。高空空气密度降低，同样体积排开的空气重量变小；气球表皮膨胀、体积增大，内部气体也在扩张。到一定高度浮力与重量重新平衡，气球不再上升；如果表皮限制膨胀，内压过大还会爆裂。系留气球和飞艇都按目标高度的空气密度设计体积与配重。Buoyancy changes as a balloon climbs. High-altitude air is less dense, so the same volume displaces lighter air; the envelope stretches, volume grows and the gas expands. At some height buoyancy and weight rebalance and the balloon stops rising; if the envelope limits expansion, internal pressure can burst it. Tethered balloons and airships size volume and ballast for the air density at their target altitude.[1]

边界：浮力来自流体压强差，真空环境里没有流体就没有浮力。轻质物体上浮是相对流体密度而言；水中上浮的物体到空气里照样下落。阿基米德原理适用于静止流体，流动水中还会叠加升力和阻力。把“气球轻所以飞”当成完整解释，忽略了排开空气重量这一关键量。The boundary: buoyancy comes from a pressure difference in a fluid; in vacuum there is no fluid and no buoyancy. Something that rises in water still falls in air. Archimedes' principle applies to stationary fluid, while flowing water adds lift and drag. Saying 'the balloon flies because it is light' misses the key quantity, the weight of displaced air.[3]

03为什么能吹出肥皂泡？纸巾浸入水中，水怎样沿纸巾上升？Why can soap bubbles be blown, and how does water climb up a paper towel dipped in water?

内聚力在液面形成表面膜，张力约 0.073 N/m；润湿管壁时毛细上升 h=2γcosθ/(ρgr)，肥皂泡靠液膜包气。Cohesion forms a surface film with tension about 0.073 N/m; wetting adhesion drives capillary rise h=2γcosθ/(ρgr), and a soap bubble wraps air in a film.

肥皂水能吹出泡泡，水会沿纸巾往上爬，都来自液体表面的内聚与附着作用。液体分子间相互吸引，内部分子被周围分子各向拉住，表面分子只受向内和两侧的拉拽，于是液面有收缩成最小面积的趋势，这就是表面张力。20 °C 纯水的表面张力约 0.073 N/m，它让水滴接近球形、让轻小物体能浮在水面。Soap bubbles and water climbing a paper towel both come from cohesion and adhesion at the liquid surface. Liquid molecules attract one another: an interior molecule is pulled in all directions by neighbours, while a surface molecule feels inward and sideways pulls, so the surface tends to shrink to the smallest area — this is surface tension. Pure water at 20 °C has surface tension about 0.073 N/m, which rounds drops toward spheres and lets light objects rest on the surface.[4]

肥皂泡是一层很薄的液膜包住空气。液膜有内外两个表面，表面张力使膜面积尽量缩小，同时膜内空气压力略高于外界，顶住收缩；加入肥皂降低水的表面张力，液膜更容易拉伸、也更不容易破裂，于是能吹出较大的泡泡。肥皂分子一端亲水一端亲油，在膜两侧排列，降低表面张力并增强膜的韧性。吹入的空气越多，膜被撑得越薄，达到一定程度就破裂。A soap bubble is a very thin liquid film wrapping air. The film has two surfaces, and surface tension shrinks its area while the trapped air, slightly above outside pressure, resists the shrink. Soap lowers water's surface tension, so the film stretches more easily and breaks less readily, allowing large bubbles. The more air blown in, the thinner the film until it ruptures.[4]

毛细上升是附着与内聚的配合。水润湿纸张和玻璃，水分子与固体壁的附着作用强于水的内聚，液面在管壁处向上攀附，形成凹弯月面；表面张力沿管壁拉动整段液体上升。细管中毛细高度 h=2γcosθ/(ρgr)，管径 r 越细，升得越高。纸巾由纤维孔隙组成，相当于无数细毛细管，水沿孔隙逐步爬升，湿痕慢慢向上扩散。Capillary rise couples adhesion and cohesion. Water wets paper and glass: adhesion between molecules and the wall beats water cohesion, so the liquid climbs the wall into a concave meniscus, and surface tension pulls the whole column up the tube. In a narrow tube the capillary height is h=2γcosθ/(ρgr): the smaller the radius r, the higher the rise. A paper towel is a bundle of fibre pores acting as countless fine capillaries, so water climbs pore by pore and the wet front spreads upward.[1][4]

表面张力决定了液面对小物体的支承。回形针、昆虫能停在水面，是液面被压出浅凹、表面张力的竖直分量托住重量；一旦刺破表面，物体立即下沉。露珠接近球形也是表面张力收缩表面积的结果。密度比水小的木块浮起靠浮力，而水面昆虫和回形针主要靠表面张力支承，两者机制不同。加肥皂降低表面张力后，回形针会沉入水中。Surface tension supports small objects on the interface. A paper clip or insect can stand on water because the surface dimples and the vertical component of tension holds the weight; pierce the surface and the object sinks at once. A light wooden block floats by buoyancy, while water insects and paper clips rely mainly on surface tension — different mechanisms. Add soap to lower tension and the clip sinks.[4][8]

边界：表面张力只在液气或液液界面起作用；液体内部不存在这种收缩膜。毛细上升高度受管径、接触角和液体黏度限制，不会无限上升。肥皂水改变的是表面张力与膜稳定性，不改变水的密度和浮力。把“水往高处爬”当成违背重力，忽略了管壁附着与表面张力这对界面作用。The boundary: surface tension acts only at a liquid-gas or liquid-liquid interface; there is no shrinking film inside the bulk liquid. Capillary rise is limited by tube radius, contact angle and viscosity and cannot climb forever. Soap changes tension and film stability, not water density or buoyancy. Reading water climbing upward as defying gravity misses the wall adhesion and surface tension at work.[4]

04打开汽水为什么会冒泡泡？水滴进热油锅里为什么会“跳舞”甚至爆溅？Why does soda fizz when opened, and why does water dance or splatter in hot oil?

汽水在加压下溶解 CO₂，开盖降压后气体在成核点析出冒泡；水遇热油迅速汽化膨胀，体积骤增把油溅出。Soda holds CO₂ under pressure; after opening, lower pressure releases gas at nucleation points; water hitting hot oil flashes to vapour and expands, splashing oil.

汽水打开就冒泡，是气体溶解度随压力下降的结果。密封瓶里压强较高，更多二氧化碳溶解在水中；开盖后气压降到大气压，能留在水里的 CO₂ 变少，多余气体在粗糙表面、气泡残留或杂质这些成核点上析出，聚成气泡上浮。摇晃加快了气泡释放，因为扰动把溶解气体带到成核点，也预先形成了小气泡。Soda fizzing on opening follows gas solubility dropping with pressure. Inside the sealed bottle the pressure is high and more carbon dioxide stays dissolved; once opened the pressure falls to atmospheric, less CO₂ can remain, and the excess separates at nucleation sites — rough walls, residual bubbles or impurities — gathering into rising bubbles. Shaking speeds release because it carries dissolved gas to nucleation sites and pre-forms small bubbles.[5]

气体在液体中的溶解服从亨利定律思路：一定温度下，溶解量与上方气体分压成正比。压强降低，平衡溶解量下降，超出部分逸出。温度升高也会降低气体溶解度，所以温汽水比冰镇汽水冒泡更猛；冰镇汽水溶解度高，开盖后析出较少，温度升高时气体平衡溶解量下降，泡沫更容易溢出瓶口。水里原本溶解的空气在加热时也会先析出成小泡，这与沸腾前的细泡同源。Gas dissolves in a liquid along Henry's law: at a fixed temperature the dissolved amount is proportional to the partial pressure above the liquid. Lower pressure lowers the equilibrium dissolved amount, and the excess escapes. Higher temperature also lowers gas solubility, so warm soda fizzes harder than chilled soda; dissolved air comes out as fine bubbles before water boils, the same family of nucleation.[1][5]

水滴进热油后“跳舞”甚至爆溅，是相变和体积骤变。食用油加热到远超水的沸点，水滴落入后表层迅速汽化，水蒸气把水滴和周围油推开；水变成蒸气体积膨胀约上千倍，局部急剧膨胀产生小爆炸，把油滴溅出。油比水轻且不互溶，水滴沉入油底接触高温油面，汽化更剧烈；锅里水多油温上不去，反而不会爆溅。Water droplets dancing or splattering in hot oil come from phase change and sudden volume expansion. Cooking oil heats far above the boiling point of water; when a drop falls in, its surface flashes to vapour, and steam pushes the drop and surrounding oil aside. Water expanding to steam grows about a thousand times in volume, and the local rapid expansion acts like a small burst that flings oil droplets out. Oil is lighter and immiscible, so the drop sinks to the hot oil bottom and vaporizes more violently; plenty of water keeps oil temperature down and prevents splatter.[1][4]

沸腾与冒泡不是一回事。沸腾是液体整体达到沸点、内部持续形成蒸气泡；汽水冒泡是溶解气体在低压下析出，温度并未达到沸点。冷水滴入热油的爆溅是水的快速汽化，和油本身是否沸腾无关。成核点决定气泡大小与快慢：杯壁划痕、糖粒、冰块都能提供成核位置，无缺陷的洁净液体还可能出现过热。Fizzing and boiling are not the same. Boiling is bulk liquid reaching its boiling point and forming vapour bubbles throughout; soda fizz releases dissolved gas at lower pressure without reaching boiling. The hot-oil splatter is rapid water vaporization and does not require the oil to boil. Nucleation sites set bubble size and speed: scratches, sugar grains and ice provide sites, and a flawless clean liquid can superheat.[4][5]

边界：气体溶解度受分压、温度和液体种类共同影响；亨利定律是稀溶液近似。水入油锅的危险来自蒸汽膨胀与油滴飞溅，不是油被点燃；微波炉加热带盖饮料也要留出泄压口，避免内部蒸汽积聚。判断冒泡原因要区分析出的是空气、CO₂ 还是水蒸气；把所有气泡都当成沸腾，会误判温度和安全条件。The boundary: gas solubility depends on partial pressure, temperature and liquid type together; Henry's law is a dilute-solution approximation. Hot-oil danger comes from steam expansion and flying oil droplets, not from oil ignition. Distinguish whether bubbles are air, CO₂ or water vapour; calling every bubble boiling misreads temperature and safety conditions.[5]

05贴着水面扔的石子为什么能弹几下？雷诺数怎样判断水流是层流还是湍流？Why does a stone skip on water, and how does Reynolds number decide whether flow is laminar or turbulent?

Re=ρvL/μ，小 Re 层流平稳，大 Re 湍流紊乱；石子斜击水面靠动压与表面反力弹跳，绕流随 Re 改变。Re=ρvL/μ: low Re gives smooth laminar flow, high Re chaotic turbulence; a skipping stone rides hydrodynamic pressure and surface reaction, with flow changing across Re.

水流平稳还是湍急，可用雷诺数衡量。雷诺数 Re=ρvL/μ，ρ 是流体密度，v 是特征流速，L 是特征长度，μ 是黏度。小雷诺数下黏性力占主导，流体分层流动、互不混杂，是层流；大雷诺数下惯性力占主导，出现旋涡、掺混和脉动，是湍流。圆管中经验分界大致为 Re<2000 层流、Re>4000 湍流，中间为过渡区。Whether a flow runs smooth or turbulent is judged by the Reynolds number. Re=ρvL/μ, where ρ is fluid density, v a characteristic speed, L a characteristic length and μ viscosity. At low Reynolds number viscous force dominates: fluid layers slide past without mixing, the laminar regime. At high Reynolds number inertia dominates, with eddies, mixing and fluctuations, the turbulent regime. In round pipes the empirical divide sits roughly below Re 2000 for laminar and above Re 4000 for turbulent, with a transition in between.[7][8]

打水漂的石子是流动与冲击的结合。石子斜向切入水面时，水被向下向后排开，动压作用在石子底面给出向上的反力；表面张力和水的惯性共同支撑短暂弹跳，石子倾角、速度和入水角度决定弹跳次数。每次接触损失一部分速度和高度，弹跳逐渐变低，最终没入水中。这与船艇滑行、水上飞机离水同属流体动压支承。A skipping stone combines flow and impact. As the stone cuts the water surface obliquely, water is pushed down and back, and dynamic pressure on its underside gives an upward reaction; surface tension and water inertia briefly support the bounce, and angle, speed and entry angle set the number of skips. Each contact loses some speed and height, the bounces get lower, and the stone finally sinks. Boat planing and seaplane takeoff use the same hydrodynamic pressure support.[6][7]

雷诺数决定绕流形态。低速小物体（细针、露珠、昆虫）周围黏性主导，边界层贴附；高速大物体（汽车、飞机）周围惯性主导，尾部出现分离尾流。高尔夫球凹坑通过改变边界层状态减阻，飞机起降时的襟翼也是在不同 Re 下控制附着与分离。水流绕过桥墩、石块时，尾迹里交替脱落旋涡，形成可观察的条纹与晃动。Reynolds number sets the flow around an object. Around slow small objects (a fine needle, dewdrop, insect) viscosity dominates and the boundary layer stays attached; around fast large ones (cars, aircraft) inertia dominates and a separated wake appears behind. Golf dimples reduce drag by changing boundary-layer state, and wing flaps control attachment and separation across flight Re regimes. Flow past bridge piers and stones sheds alternating vortices in the wake, visible as streaks and swaying.[7][8]

日常流动处处是 Re 的区间差别。蜂蜜从勺边缓慢流下，黏度大、Re 很低，是典型层流；打开水龙头急冲、河水绕过石头，Re 高得多，容易出现湍流旋涡。管径越细、流速越低、流体越黏，越容易维持层流；反之管径粗、流速快、流体稀，容易转捩为湍流。判断流动状态不能只看流速，还要把尺度和黏度一起代入 Re。Everyday flows sit at different Re ranges. Honey sliding off a spoon has high viscosity and very low Re, a typical laminar stream; a gushing tap or river around stones runs at much higher Re and easily forms turbulent eddies. Narrow tubes, low speed and viscous fluid favour laminar flow; wide pipes, fast flow and thin fluid trip more easily to turbulence. Judging a flow needs scale and viscosity together in Re, not speed alone.[6][7]

边界：雷诺数是无量纲判据，分界数值随几何形状和入口条件变化；2000、4000 是圆管经验值，不是普适常数。层流与湍流之间存在过渡区，扰动会提前转捩，实测中常用染色示踪观察流线是否掺混。表面张力、浮力和相变在特定问题中可能比 Re 更重要；把所有水流现象都归结为雷诺数，会漏掉界面力和压强差的作用。The boundary: Re is a dimensionless criterion, and its divide shifts with geometry and inlet conditions; 2000 and 4000 are pipe empirical values, not universal constants. A transition zone lies between laminar and turbulent, and disturbances can trigger early transition. Surface tension, buoyancy and phase change may matter more than Re in specific problems; reducing every water phenomenon to Reynolds number misses interface forces and pressure differences.[7][8]
