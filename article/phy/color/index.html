01星星的颜色为什么不一样，星星为什么会眨眼睛？Why do stars have different colors, and why do they twinkle?

恒星颜色对应表面温度：蓝白最热、橙红较冷；闪烁是大气湍流折射，与恒星本身无关。Stellar color tracks surface temperature: blue-white hottest, orange-red cooler; twinkling is atmospheric refraction, not a change in the star.

光的颜色对应波长。可见光谱约 380–750 nm，紫端波长短、红端波长长；恒星近似黑体辐射。温度越高，辐射峰值波长越短，这就是 Wien 位移定律：λmax·T ≈ 2.898×10⁻³ m·K。蓝白星最热，橙红星较冷，太阳表面约 5,778 K，呈黄白色。颜色首先是表面温度的读数，而不是恒星内部温度。Color maps to wavelength. The visible spectrum spans about 380–750 nm, short at the violet end and long at the red; stars radiate approximately like black bodies. The hotter the body, the shorter the wavelength of its peak emission, described by Wien's displacement law: λmax·T ≈ 2.898×10⁻³ m·K. Blue-white stars are hottest, orange-red ones cooler, and the Sun, at about 5,778 K on its surface, appears yellow-white. Color reads surface temperature first, not the core temperature.[1][2]

怎么从颜色估温度。目视比色大致对应：橙红星约 3,000–4,000 K，黄星约 5,000–6,000 K，蓝白星在 10,000 K 以上。参宿四橙红，表面温度约 3,500 K；参宿七蓝白，约 12,000 K。严格做法是用测光波段得到色指数，再换算温度；颜色还会受星际消光、自转和双星叠加影响。同一颗恒星的表面温度与核心温度相差很大，不能把表面色当成内部状态。Estimating temperature from color. Rough visual matching puts orange-red stars near 3,000–4,000 K, yellow stars near 5,000–6,000 K, and blue-white stars above 10,000 K. The rigorous route uses photometric bands to derive a color index and convert it to temperature; interstellar extinction, rotation and binary overlap can shift the appearance. Surface and core temperatures differ greatly, so surface color must not be read as an internal state.[1][2]

闪烁不是恒星在变。恒星本身发光稳定；星光穿过湍流大气时，局部空气密度和折射率不断变化，光路反复偏折，亮度和位置随之抖动，这就是天文视宁度造成的闪烁，视宁度常用角秒量级衡量。靠近地平线时星光穿过的大气更厚，闪烁更强；行星距离近、视张角较大，通常不怎么闪。太空望远镜在大气层之外观测，看到的恒星基本不闪。Twinkling is not the star changing. Stars shine steadily; as their light passes through turbulent air, local density and refractive index keep shifting, bending the path repeatedly so brightness and position wobble, the scintillation astronomers call seeing. Near the horizon the light crosses more air and twinkles more strongly; planets are close and have a larger apparent disk, so they usually do not twinkle much. A space telescope above the atmosphere sees stars that barely flicker.[1][3]

颜色与亮度是两回事。蓝星单位面积辐射更强，但目视亮度还取决于距离、总光度和尘埃消光；天文学用视星等记录目视亮度，用绝对星等比较真实光度，两者需要距离换算。天狼星蓝白且亮，参宿四橙红，不能只凭颜色判断远近或大小。一颗遥远的红星可能很亮，一颗近旁的蓝星也可能显得暗淡。Color and brightness are separate. A blue star radiates more per unit area, but apparent brightness also depends on distance, total luminosity and dust extinction; Sirius is blue-white and bright, Betelgeuse orange-red, and neither distance nor size can be judged from color alone. A distant red star can look bright, and a nearby blue star can look dim.[1][2]

边界：恒星颜色是表面温度的粗略指标，谱线、金属丰度和双星成分会改变表观颜色；闪烁是大气现象，与恒星本身的物理变化无关；目视色觉还受大气散射、观测经验和眼睛适应状态影响。这里不展开赫罗图、恒星演化阶段和光谱型细分。Boundary: stellar color is a rough surface-temperature indicator, while spectral lines, metallicity and binary composition alter the apparent color. Twinkling is an atmospheric effect unrelated to physical change in the star; visual color perception also depends on scattering and observing experience. The Hertzsprung-Russell diagram, stellar evolution stages and spectral-class detail are not covered here.[1][2]

02花和物体为什么有不同颜色，秋天树叶为什么会变色？Why do flowers and objects have different colors, and why do leaves change in autumn?

物体反射特定波长、吸收其余；花色来自花青素与类胡萝卜素，秋叶叶绿素降解后黄橙显露、花青素转红紫。Objects reflect some wavelengths and absorb the rest; flower colors are pigments, and autumn yellow-orange appears as chlorophyll fades.

物体颜色从哪来。不透明物体反射一部分可见光、吸收其余部分，反射光进入眼睛才被感知；可见光约 380–750 nm，被吸收波段的互补色进入视觉。颜料分子里的共轭体系或过渡金属离子选择吸收特定波长，透过或反射互补色；蓝色物体吸收红橙波段、反射蓝光，白色反射全部可见光，黑色吸收全部。颜色不是物体“自带”的标签，而是光源光谱与材料吸收反射的结果。Where material color comes from. An opaque object reflects part of the visible light and absorbs the rest; only the reflected light reaches the eye. Conjugated systems in pigment molecules or transition-metal ions absorb selected wavelengths and transmit or reflect the complementary color: a blue object absorbs red-orange and reflects blue, white reflects all visible light, black absorbs all. Color is not a built-in label but the outcome of source spectrum and material absorption-reflection.[6][7]

花与果实的颜色。花色多由花青素、类胡萝卜素等色素决定，色素与细胞液 pH、共色素作用共同改变色调。植物颜色部分用于吸引传粉者和种子传播者，但花色并非都服务于人眼感知；判断颜色应以反射光谱为准，而不是以人的命名为准。果实成熟时色素组成变化，常与糖分积累和种子传播配合。Flower and fruit colors. Flower colors come mainly from anthocyanins, carotenoids and other pigments, with cell-sap pH and co-pigments shifting the hue. Plant colors partly attract pollinators and seed dispersers, but not every flower color is tuned to human vision; the reflection spectrum, not the human name, is the physical basis. Pigment composition changes as fruit ripens, often alongside sugar accumulation and seed dispersal.[4][6]

秋叶变色。春夏叶绿素含量高，掩盖了类胡萝卜素的黄、橙色；类胡萝卜素包括胡萝卜素和叶黄素。秋季叶绿素降解快于合成，黄橙色显露出来。部分树种在低温和光照条件下合成花青素，酸性细胞液中偏红、偏碱时偏蓝紫，叶片显红紫色；落叶前的养分回收也是季节信号的一部分。同一片叶子上常同时看到绿、黄、橙、红过渡，正反映不同色素此消彼长。Why autumn leaves change. In spring and summer high chlorophyll content masks the yellow and orange of carotenoids; in autumn chlorophyll breaks down faster than it is rebuilt, so yellow and orange show through. Some trees synthesize anthocyanins under cold and light, turning leaves red or purple; nutrient resorption before leaf fall is part of the seasonal signal. A single leaf often shows green, yellow, orange and red together, reflecting pigments trading places.[4][6]

火焰与气体颜色。火焰颜色既有温度贡献：黑体从红到黄再到白蓝随温度升高；也有元素发射谱：钠的燃焰发黄，铜的燃焰偏蓝绿。受热激发的电子跃迁回落时发射特定波长光子，形成特征谱线；激发态原子在低压气体中发光，这与物体靠反射显色是两套机制。同一团火焰中，温度谱和杂质发射谱叠加，不能把火焰颜色只归因于温度。Flames and gases. Flame color has two contributions: temperature drives a blackbody progression from red to yellow to white-blue, while element emission lines add their own hues, sodium burning yellow and copper blue-green. Excited atoms in low-pressure gases emit characteristic lines, a mechanism separate from reflection color. In one flame the thermal spectrum and impurity lines overlap, so flame color cannot be attributed to temperature alone.[3][7]

边界：看到的颜色是光源光谱、物体反射或透射和视觉系统三者共同的结果；同一物体在暖光与冷光下表观色不同。色素名称不等于单一分子；火焰颜色受燃料、温度和杂质共同影响。这里不展开染料化学合成、颜料工业配方和各类色素的分子结构细节。Boundary: perceived color results jointly from source spectrum, object reflection or transmission and the visual system; the same object looks different under warm and cool light. A pigment name is not a single molecule, and flame color depends on fuel, temperature and impurities. Dye synthesis, industrial pigment formulations and molecular structures of each pigment are outside this chapter.[6][7]

03人眼怎样分辨颜色，为什么有人分不出红绿，连衣裙为什么看出不同颜色？How does the eye tell colors apart, why do some people fail red-green, and why does the dress look different?

S/M/L 三类视锥比较差值编码颜色；红绿色盲多为 X 连锁；连衣裙分歧来自光源假设不同的颜色恒常性。S/M/L cones compare differences to encode color; red-green blindness is mostly X-linked; the dress split comes from different light-source assumptions in color constancy.

人眼的三色接收器。视网膜上有视杆细胞负责暗视觉、不分辨颜色，数量约上亿；还有三类视锥细胞，数量约数百万：S 型对短波蓝敏感，M 型对中波绿敏感，L 型对长波红敏。大脑比较三类信号的差值来编码颜色；可见光波段约 380–750 nm。没有这三类接收器的响应曲线，物体反射光再强也无法形成色觉。The eye's three color receivers. The retina holds rods for dim-light vision without color, and three cone types: S cones sensitive to short-wave blue, M cones to mid-wave green, L cones to long-wave red. The brain encodes color by comparing differences among the three signals; visible light spans about 380–750 nm. Without these response curves, reflected light alone cannot produce color vision.[5][6]

色盲怎么回事。红绿色盲多为 X 连锁遗传，M 或 L 视锥缺失，或两类视锥光谱重叠；最常见类型区分红绿困难，红色盲与绿色盲常统称红绿色盲，蓝黄色觉缺陷少见。色盲不等于全色盲，全色盲极罕见，多数患者仍有三色或二色视觉，只是某一通道信息不足。人群中男性红绿色盲比例显著高于女性，这与 X 染色体上相关基因的位置有关。What color blindness means. Red-green color blindness is mostly X-linked: M or L cones are absent, or the two cone spectra overlap. The commonest type makes red and green hard to separate; blue-yellow defects are rarer. Color blindness is not total color blindness: most affected people retain trichromatic or dichromatic vision, just with one channel weak. Red-green defects are far more common in men than women, matching the location of the relevant genes on the X chromosome.[3][5]

为什么人眼对绿色特别敏感。明视条件下光谱灵敏度峰值约在 555 nm 的黄绿色；夜间视杆细胞峰值约 507 nm，明暗视觉之间灵敏度峰值向短波移动，即浦肯野位移。环境中绿光波段信息密集，显示器与照明常按这一灵敏度安排亮度权重。对绿色的“偏爱”是视锥光谱响应与光照演化共同形成的，并不是绿色本身比其他波长更重要。Why the eye seems most sensitive to green. Under bright light the spectral sensitivity peaks near 555 nm, yellow-green; at night rod sensitivity peaks near 507 nm. The green band carries dense environmental information, so displays and lighting arrange brightness weights around this sensitivity. The preference for green arises from cone spectral response and the lighting environment of evolution, not because green wavelengths matter intrinsically more.[3][5]

连衣裙之争。2015 年这张连衣裙照片在社交网络上同时收到白金与蓝黑两类报告，成为研究颜色恒常性的著名案例；分歧来自大脑对光源颜色的假设不同。颜色恒常性会自动扣除环境光照：若把现场假设为暖黄光照，阴影中的布料被解译为蓝黑；假设冷色光照则解译为白金。后续实验改变背景光照后，受试者报告比例随之变化。图像记录的是像素反射比，大脑重建的是表面本色，两者不是同一件事。The dress debate. In one photo some people see white-and-gold and others blue-and-black; the split comes from different assumptions the brain makes about the light source. Color constancy subtracts ambient illumination automatically: assuming warm yellow light, the shaded fabric is read as blue-black; assuming cool light, it reads as white-gold. The image records pixel reflectance, while the brain reconstructs surface color; these are not the same thing.[5][6]

边界：视锥类型划分是简化模型，个体光谱分布存在差异；色盲分型需要临床色觉检查，不能凭一张判断图定论；颜色恒常性研究多以实验室图像和口头报告为依据，效应大小随任务和照明条件变化。这里不展开视觉神经通路、色觉缺陷矫治和颜色命名的跨文化差异。Boundary: cone types are a simplified model, and individual spectra vary. Color-vision classification needs clinical tests and cannot be settled by one Ishihara-style plate; color-constancy studies rely mostly on lab images and verbal reports, with effect sizes varying by task and lighting. Visual neural pathways, color-vision correction and cross-cultural color naming are not covered here.[5][6]

04手机屏幕为什么能显示上百万种颜色，蓝光 LED 为什么更难造？Why can phone screens show millions of colors, and why were blue LEDs harder to make?

RGB 子像素加色混合，8 位/通道约 1,677 万色；氮化镓蓝光 LED 突破获 2014 诺奖。RGB sub-pixels mix additively, 8 bits per channel give about 16.77 million colors; GaN growth and doping held back blue LEDs, honored with the 2014 Nobel Prize.

屏幕怎么混出颜色。LCD 与 OLED 每个像素由红、绿、蓝三种子像素组成；三者亮度按比例混合，通过加色混合生成其他颜色。LCD 液晶本身不发光，靠背光层透过滤色片显色；OLED 像素各自发光。白光可由 RGB 峰值组合得到；屏幕是自发光显示，与靠反射环境光显色的纸张不同。子像素之间靠得很近，在正常观看距离上眼睛把它们融合成单一颜色。How a screen mixes color. Each LCD or OLED pixel has red, green and blue sub-pixels; their brightnesses combine additively to produce other colors. White light can be made from an RGB peak combination; a display emits its own light, unlike paper that reflects ambient light. The sub-pixels sit so close that the eye fuses them into one color at normal viewing distance.[1][3]

位深与百万色。每通道 8 位可表达 2⁸=256 级亮度，三通道组合约 1,677 万种颜色；10 位/通道可达十亿级编码组合，更高位深减少渐变色带，但文件体积与硬件成本上升。显示色域决定能覆盖哪些波长组合，sRGB 是常见基准。“百万色”指可编码的颜色组合数，不等于面板在现实中都能准确还原。Bit depth and millions of colors. Eight bits per channel encode 2⁸=256 brightness levels, and the three channels combine to about 16.77 million colors; higher bit depth reduces gradient banding but raises file size and hardware cost. The color gamut decides which wavelength combinations can be covered, with sRGB a common baseline. Millions of colors means encoded combinations, not that every panel reproduces them accurately in reality.[1][3]

蓝光 LED 为什么难。20 世纪 90 年代红光、绿光 LED 已有高效器件，蓝光波长约 450 nm 量级，半导体材料氮化镓的晶体生长、掺杂和缺陷控制长期受阻；直到 1990 年代末氮化镓蓝光 LED 效率才达到实用水平。赤崎勇、天野浩与中村修二因高效蓝光发光二极管获 2014 年诺贝尔物理学奖。蓝光芯片涂覆黄色荧光粉，把部分蓝光转换为黄光，再混合得到白光。Why the blue LED was hard. In the 1990s efficient red and green LEDs already existed, while gallium nitride crystal growth, doping and defect control for blue semiconductors held out for years; practical GaN blue LEDs arrived only in the late 1990s. Isamu Akasaki, Hiroshi Amano and Shuji Nakamura received the 2014 Nobel Prize in Physics for efficient blue light-emitting diodes. A blue chip coated with yellow phosphor converts part of the blue to yellow, and the mix yields white light.[8]

光源光谱与颜色还原。屏幕自发光光谱由 LED 或背光与滤色片决定，与连续的太阳光谱不同；物体在不同光源下显色性有差异，显色指数 Ra 以满分 100 衡量光源还原表面色的能力。同一面墙在日光、暖灯和冷灯下观感不同，差别来自光源光谱，而不是墙面反射比变了。Source spectrum and color rendering. A display's emitted spectrum comes from its LED or backlight and filters, and differs from the continuous solar spectrum; objects render differently under different sources, and a color-rendering index measures how well a source restores surface colors. The same wall looks different under daylight, warm lamps and cool lamps; the difference lies in source spectrum, not in the wall's reflectance.[1][3]

边界：屏幕颜色是三通道近似，不能覆盖全部可见光颜色；百万色指可编码组合数，实际受面板色域、校准和环境光限制。子像素排列有条纹、菱形等不同方式，近距离放大屏幕可看到 R/G/B 点阵，排列方式影响清晰度与子像素渲染。LED 发光是半导体电致发光，与火焰的原子发射谱不同；显示校准、色域标准和护眼蓝光波段的细节不在本章展开。Boundary: screen color is a three-channel approximation and cannot cover all visible wavelengths; millions of colors means encoded combinations, limited in practice by panel gamut, calibration and ambient light. LED emission is semiconductor electroluminescence, different from atomic line emission in flames; display calibration, gamut standards and blue-light eye-health detail are not covered here.[1][3]
