蓝天、红日与彩虹：大气光学Blue Sky, Red Sunset and Rainbow: Atmospheric Optics

01晴朗天空为什么是蓝色的，而不是紫色或绿色？Why is a clear sky blue rather than violet or green?

空气分子对短波长光的散射按 1/λ⁴ 增强，蓝光被散射到各个方向；人眼对蓝光更敏感，紫光又在高空被吸收。Air molecules scatter short wavelengths as 1/λ⁴, sending blue light in all directions; the eye is more sensitive to blue, and violet is absorbed high in the atmosphere.

晴朗天空的蓝色来自太阳光被空气分子散射。阳光是混合了可见光各波长的白光；穿过大气时，氮气、氧气等分子把一部分光偏转到四面八方，这些散射光从整个天空进入眼睛，天空因此发亮并呈现颜色。散射对波长有强烈偏好：波长越短，散射越强。The blue of a clear sky comes from sunlight scattered by air molecules. Sunlight is white light mixing every visible wavelength; as it passes through the atmosphere, nitrogen and oxygen molecules deflect part of the light in all directions. This scattered light enters the eye from the whole sky, so the sky looks bright and coloured. Scattering has a strong preference for wavelength: the shorter the wavelength, the stronger the scattering.[1][3]

瑞利散射的强度近似与波长的四次方成反比，即 I 正比于 1/λ⁴。取蓝光约 450 nm、红光约 650 nm，散射强度之比约为 (650/450)⁴ ≈ 4.4，蓝光被分子散射的强度是红光的四倍多；紫光波长更短，散射更强。能产生这种散射的粒子必须远小于波长，氮气、氧气分子直径约 0.3–0.4 nm，满足条件。大气光学教材都把这一四次方关系作为蓝天的定量依据。Rayleigh scattering intensity is roughly inversely proportional to the fourth power of wavelength, I proportional to 1/λ⁴. Taking blue at about 450 nm and red at about 650 nm, the scattering ratio is about (650/450)⁴ ≈ 4.4: blue is scattered more than four times as strongly as red, and violet, being shorter, scatters even more strongly. Particles producing this scattering must be much smaller than the wavelength; nitrogen and oxygen molecules are about 0.3–0.4 nm across, which satisfies the condition. Atmospheric optics textbooks use this fourth-power relation as the quantitative basis for the blue sky.[1][3]

紫光散射更强，天空却不呈紫色，原因有三层。太阳光谱在紫光端的能量本身较低；高层大气对紫外与紫光有部分吸收；人眼视锥细胞对蓝光的敏感度高于紫光，接收到的混合信号被大脑判读为蓝色。云滴、尘埃等较大粒子的散射接近米氏散射，波长依赖性弱，因此云雾呈白色或灰色。Violet scatters more strongly, yet the sky does not look violet, for three reasons. The solar spectrum carries less energy at the violet end; the upper atmosphere partly absorbs ultraviolet and violet; and the eye's cones are more sensitive to blue than to violet, so the mixed signal is read as blue. Larger particles such as cloud droplets and dust approach Mie scattering, which depends weakly on wavelength, which is why clouds look white or grey.[2][3]

这一机制可以解释常见现象：高空飞行时天空更深更暗，因为下方空气层更薄、散射光更少；日出前和日落后的霞光也是散射光。用波长关系估算：可见光范围约 380–750 nm，短波段（蓝紫）被优先散射，长波段（红橙）更容易直接穿透。NASA 戈达德研究所的教学材料把蓝天与红日放在同一张散射示意图中，说明两者是同一机制的正反两面。The mechanism explains everyday observations. At altitude the sky looks deeper and darker because the air layer below is thinner and there is less scattered light; dawn and dusk glow are also scattered light. By wavelength, visible light spans about 380–750 nm: short blue-violet wavelengths are scattered first, while long red-orange wavelengths penetrate farther directly. A NASA Goddard teaching diagram places the blue sky and red sun in one scattering picture, showing the two as opposite sides of the same mechanism.[1][2]

边界：蓝天是散射光的颜色，不是大气本身染成蓝色；在没有大气的月球上，天空即使白天也是黑色的。瑞利散射适用于远小于波长的分子，遇到烟尘、海盐颗粒或云滴时要改用米氏散射描述，颜色会偏白偏灰。把“分子越多天空越蓝”绝对化也不成立：污染物和水汽改变粒子大小与数量，既可能加重散射，也可能形成霾白。The boundary: the blue sky is the colour of scattered light, not a dye in the air itself; on the airless Moon the sky is black even in daylight. Rayleigh scattering applies to molecules much smaller than the wavelength; smoke, sea-salt particles or cloud droplets call for a Mie description and tend toward white or grey. It also does not follow that "more molecules means a bluer sky": pollutants and water vapour change particle size and number, enhancing scattering or producing a hazy white.

02为什么正午的天空偏蓝，而夕阳和朝霞却是红色或橙色？Why does the sky look blue at noon but red or orange at sunrise and sunset?

太阳接近地平线时，阳光穿过的大气路径长得多，短波长的蓝紫光沿途被散射掉，剩下长波长的红橙光直射到眼中。Near the horizon sunlight travels a much longer path; blue and violet are scattered away, leaving red and orange direct light.

夕阳偏红不是太阳本身变色，而是阳光到达观察者之前穿过的大气厚度改变了。正午太阳较高，阳光斜穿较薄的大气层；傍晚或清晨太阳贴近地平线，光线要沿近乎水平的方向穿过最厚的一层大气。路径越长，被散射和吸收掉的短波长光越多，直射光里剩下的红橙比例越高。A red sunset does not mean the Sun itself has changed colour; it is the thickness of atmosphere crossed before sunlight reaches the observer that changes. At noon the Sun is high and sunlight crosses a thinner layer at a slant; near evening or dawn it sits on the horizon and travels almost horizontally through the thickest air layer. The longer the path, the more short-wavelength light is scattered and absorbed away, leaving a higher proportion of red and orange in the direct beam.[1]

大气光学用“空气质量”描述光路长度：天顶方向约 1 个大气质量，地平线附近可达 30 个以上大气质量，路径长度放大数十倍。沿长路径，蓝光（约 450 nm）按 1/λ⁴ 被反复散射，不断离开直射光束；红光（约 650 nm）散射弱，穿透距离远。于是直视夕阳时，光束以红橙为主；被空气散射后留在天边的余光，也常呈现橙红到粉紫的层次。NASA 戈达德研究所的散射示意图对比了直射光与散射光的路径。Atmospheric optics describes path length with "air mass": about one air mass toward the zenith and over thirty near the horizon, a path many times longer. Along that long route, blue light (about 450 nm) is repeatedly scattered away at 1/λ⁴ and leaves the direct beam, while red light (about 650 nm) scatters weakly and travels farther. Looking straight at sunset, the beam is therefore mostly red-orange, and the leftover glow scattered along the horizon often shades from orange-red to pink and violet. A NASA Goddard scattering diagram compares direct and scattered light paths.[1][2]

靠近地平线的天空常常更白、更淡，这是米氏散射的贡献。当地近地面集中了水汽、尘埃、海盐和污染物等较大颗粒，它们对各波长的散射差别不大，白光被均匀散射，叠加在瑞利散射的蓝色之上，使天色调白、发灰。空气越干净，地平线附近的蓝色层次越分明；霾重时整片天空偏乳白。The sky near the horizon often looks paler and whiter, which is the contribution of Mie scattering. Close to the ground, larger particles of water vapour, dust, sea salt and pollutants scatter wavelengths almost evenly; this uniform white scatter stacks on the Rayleigh blue and washes the sky toward grey. The cleaner the air, the clearer the blue near the horizon; heavy haze leaves the whole sky milky white.[2]

火山喷发或大规模野火后，细气溶胶进入平流层，日落颜色更红更艳，甚至出现蓝色月亮之类的反常现象，这是气溶胶颗粒尺寸改变了散射类型。观察要点：太阳颜色变化主要看直射光，天空颜色来自散射光；两者由同一套波长依赖关系支配。OpenStax 色散章节讨论棱镜与水滴把白光按波长分开的过程，夕阳的红橙则是散射把短波长从光束中筛除后的结果。After volcanic eruptions or large wildfires, fine aerosols reach the stratosphere and sunsets turn deeper red and more vivid, occasionally producing odd effects such as a blue Moon, as particle size changes the scattering regime. The observation rule: changes in the Sun's colour concern direct light, while sky colour comes from scattered light, both governed by the same wavelength dependence. The OpenStax dispersion chapter discusses how prisms and droplets separate white light by wavelength; sunset red and orange are what remains after scattering filters the short wavelengths out of the beam.[2][4]

边界：长光路解释的是直射光变红，不代表所有晚霞都是干净大气的产物；云的反射、气溶胶成分和视角都会影响颜色。米氏散射与瑞利散射的分界取决于粒子相对波长的尺寸，不能只用“灰尘多就红”概括。晚霞偏红也不能作为空气质量好坏的直接指标，需要结合气溶胶观测判断。The boundary: the long path explains why the direct beam turns red, not why every evening glow proves clean air; cloud reflection, aerosol composition and viewing angle all affect the colour. The line between Mie and Rayleigh scattering depends on particle size relative to wavelength and cannot be summed up as "more dust means redder". A red dusk is not a direct air-quality index; it has to be read together with aerosol measurements.

03彩虹里的颜色是怎样被水滴分开的？为什么红在外、紫在内？How do water droplets separate the colours in a rainbow, and why is red on the outside and violet on the inside?

阳光进入水滴折射色散，内壁反射一次后再出射；红光约 42°、紫光约 40°，主虹红在外、紫在内。Light disperses entering a droplet, reflects once inside, then exits; red near 42° and violet near 40°, red outside the primary bow.

彩虹是阳光被大量水滴折射、反射后形成的彩色圆弧。观察者背对太阳，面前是降雨区或水雾；每颗水滴都把入射阳光重新分配方向，成千上万颗水滴在特定角度把色光送进眼睛。彩虹不是挂在天上的实体物体，而是水滴、阳光和观察者位置共同决定的光学现象。A rainbow is a coloured arc formed when sunlight is refracted and reflected by a large number of water droplets. The observer stands with the Sun behind and rain or spray ahead; every droplet redistributes the incoming sunlight, and thousands of droplets send coloured light into the eye at specific angles. A rainbow is not a solid object hanging in the sky: it is an optical phenomenon fixed by droplets, sunlight and the observer's position together.[5]

光进入球形水滴时先发生折射，水对不同波长的折射率略有差异，紫光折射率大、偏折更多，红光折射率小、偏折较少，白光由此被色散。光线在水滴后内壁反射一次，再从另一侧折射出去。OpenStax 色散章节说明折射率随波长变化是棱镜分色的根源；水滴的几何与此相同，只是增加了一次内反射。总偏折角由水滴几何和折射率共同决定。On entering a spherical droplet the light refracts first. Water's refractive index differs slightly by wavelength: violet has a larger index and bends more, red a smaller index and bends less, which disperses the white light. The ray reflects once at the inner back wall and refracts out through the other side. The OpenStax dispersion chapter shows how wavelength-dependent refractive index underlies prism colour splitting; droplet geometry is the same, with one extra internal reflection added. The total deflection angle is set by droplet geometry and refractive index together.[4][6]

主虹（一次内反射）中，红光出射方向与反日点方向的夹角约 42°，紫光约 40°。眼睛看到的红来自更高处、角度约 42° 的水滴，紫来自更低处、角度约 40° 的水滴，因此主虹红弧在外、紫弧在内。副虹由两次内反射形成，位于约 51°–54° 外侧，颜色顺序相反；两次反射损失更多光强，所以副虹更暗。不同波长的角度差不大，但人眼分辨得出来。In the primary bow (one internal reflection), red leaves at about 42° from the antisolar direction and violet at about 40°. The red reaching the eye comes from higher droplets near 42°, violet from lower droplets near 40°, so the primary bow is red on the outside and violet inside. The secondary bow comes from two internal reflections, outside at about 51°–54° with reversed colour order; two reflections lose more intensity, so it is fainter. The angle spread between wavelengths is small, but the eye can resolve it.[4][6]

彩虹位于以反日点为轴的一个圆锥面上；从地面只能看到上半弧，从飞机或高山上可以看到完整圆环。改变位置，彩虹也跟着移动，因为它始终对应观察者背后的太阳方向。瀑布喷溅、花园水雾和淋浴水珠都能在逆光下形成小彩虹；NASA 科普页面用“背对太阳找雨幕”作为观察条件。水滴大小影响彩虹的鲜艳程度和色带宽度，过大雨滴合并、过小则色散不明显。A rainbow lies on a cone whose axis points to the antisolar point; from the ground only the upper arc shows, while an aircraft or high mountain reveals the full circle. Move and the rainbow moves with you, because it always tracks the Sun behind the observer. Waterfall spray, garden mist and shower droplets all make small rainbows against the light; a NASA explainer states the condition as "face away from the Sun and look toward the rain". Droplet size affects vividness and band width: large droplets merge and very small ones disperse weakly.[4][5]

边界：彩虹里主要是可见光的色散，紫外和红外波段同样被偏折，但人眼看不到；水滴内部多次反射还会形成更淡的更高阶虹。彩虹的角度是对球形水滴的近似，真实雨滴受空气阻力略扁，大水滴的虹角会有微小变化。把彩虹描述成“水滴把光染色”并不准确，水滴本身无色，颜色来自波长相关的折射和观察角度。The boundary: the rainbow is mainly dispersion of visible light; ultraviolet and infrared are deflected too but invisible to the eye, and multiple internal reflections make fainter higher-order bows. The angles approximate spherical droplets, while real raindrops are slightly flattened by air drag, so large drops shift the bow angles a little. It is inaccurate to say droplets dye the light: the droplets are colourless themselves, and the colour comes from wavelength-dependent refraction and the viewing angle.

04极光为什么大多是绿色的？极光是什么粒子造成的？为什么只出现在高纬度夜空？Why are auroras mostly green, what particles cause them, and why do they appear mainly in high-latitude skies?

磁层电子激发高层大气氧原子；绿光 557.7 nm 氧线约 100–250 km，红线 630 nm 在更高层。Precipitating electrons from the magnetosphere excite upper-atmosphere oxygen; green is the 557.7 nm oxygen line at about 100–250 km, red the 630 nm line higher up.

极光来自太阳风与地球磁层的相互作用。太阳向外吹出由质子、电子组成的等离子体流，即太阳风；地球磁场像一个护盾把大部分粒子偏转，夜间磁尾被拉长，其中的电子被加速后沿磁力线沉降到南北极附近的高层大气。高能电子撞击氧原子、氮分子，把它们激发到较高能态；粒子回到基态时放出特定波长的光，发光原理接近霓虹灯。Auroras arise from the interaction of the solar wind with Earth's magnetosphere. The Sun blows out a plasma stream of protons and electrons, the solar wind; Earth's magnetic field acts as a shield that deflects most particles. On the night side the stretched magnetotail accelerates electrons, which precipitate along magnetic field lines into the upper atmosphere near the poles. High-energy electrons strike oxygen atoms and nitrogen molecules, raising them to excited states; as the particles return to ground level they emit light at specific wavelengths, much like a neon tube.[9]

最常见的绿色来自氧原子。沉降电子在约 100–250 km 高度撞击氧原子，受激氧原子回到低能态时发出 557.7 nm（5577 Å）的绿光；这个高度氧原子密度适中、碰撞退激较慢，绿色辐射最强，成为肉眼最常见的极光颜色。在更高的 200–500 km，氧原子发出 630 nm 红线，光更暗，常呈暗红帷幔；氮分子在较低高度贡献蓝紫和紫红的边缘色。颜色随高度和粒子能量分层。The common green comes from oxygen. Around 100–250 km altitude, precipitating electrons collide with oxygen atoms, which on returning to a lower state emit the 557.7 nm (5577 Å) green line. At this height oxygen density is moderate and collisional de-excitation is slow, so green radiation dominates and becomes the most familiar auroral color. Higher up, at 200–500 km, oxygen emits the 630 nm red line, fainter and often seen as dark red curtains; nitrogen molecules contribute blue-violet and purplish-red edges at lower altitude. Colors layer with height and particle energy.[7][8]

极光集中在高纬度的极光卵环带。被加速的粒子沿磁力线沉降，磁力线在磁极附近接入大气，所以极光多出现于南北高纬夜空。太阳风增强、行星际磁场南向时，磁层活动加剧，极光更亮、更活跃，环带也向较低纬度扩展；地磁平静时仍可能有弱极光，只是肉眼不易察觉。Auroras cluster in the high-latitude auroral oval. Accelerated particles follow field lines that enter the atmosphere near the magnetic poles, so displays appear mainly in polar night skies. When the solar wind strengthens and the interplanetary field turns southward, magnetospheric activity rises, the aurora brightens and becomes active, and the oval spreads toward lower latitudes. Even in calm geomagnetic conditions a faint aurora may persist, though it is hard to see with the naked eye.[7][9]

观测极光需要黑暗、晴朗和活跃的地磁条件。高纬度冬季夜空黑暗时间长，是观测极光的主要季节；城市光污染会掩盖暗弱极光，肉眼看到的绿色往往比照片淡，长曝光相机会记录下更多暗弱的红线和结构。极光不是云，不是反射地面灯光，也不是高空燃烧；它是带电粒子与大气原子碰撞激发的发光现象。Seeing an aurora needs a dark, clear sky and active geomagnetic conditions; high-latitude winter nights stay dark for long hours, the main viewing season. Urban light pollution hides faint displays, and the green seen by eye is often paler than in photos; long-exposure cameras record fainter red lines and structures. An aurora is not a cloud, not reflected ground light, and not high-altitude burning; it is light emitted when charged particles collide with atmospheric atoms.[9]

边界：本章讲发光机制与颜色来源，极光的弧、冕、幕等形态随磁场和粒子沉降变化。557.7 nm 与 630 nm 是氧原子发射线，氮分子线位于短波侧；具体颜色混合受高度、太阳风能量和观测条件影响，不能只凭颜色反推精确高度。极光预报依据太阳风速度、行星际磁场和磁层模型，与天气云图是两套系统。Boundary: this chapter covers the emission mechanism and color source; arc, corona and curtain shapes shift with the magnetic field and particle precipitation. The 557.7 nm and 630 nm lines are oxygen transitions, while nitrogen molecular lines lie on the short-wavelength side. The actual color mix depends on altitude, solar-wind energy and observing conditions, so color alone cannot give an exact height. Aurora forecasts use solar-wind speed, interplanetary field and magnetospheric models, a separate system from weather cloud maps.[7][8][9]

05云为什么有白云和乌云之分？Why are some clouds white while others look dark?

云滴对各波长光近似等强散射，薄云透光显白；厚云光在内部反复散射衰减，到达底部所剩无几，就显灰暗。Cloud droplets scatter all wavelengths similarly; thin clouds transmit light and look white, while thick clouds scatter and attenuate light internally, leaving little at the base.

云由大量悬浮的小水滴或冰晶组成。云滴直径多在几到几十微米，比可见光波长大，对红光、蓝光等各种波长的光做米氏散射，而且对各波长的散射强度比较接近。阳光被云滴四面八方散开之后，进入眼睛的光仍混合着各种颜色，所以看上去是白色或灰白色，这是白云的基本来源。Clouds are made of countless suspended water droplets or ice crystals. Droplets are mostly a few to tens of micrometres across, larger than the wavelength of visible light, so they scatter red, blue and other wavelengths by Mie scattering at roughly similar strengths. Sunlight scattered in all directions by the droplets still reaches the eye as a mix of all colors, which is why clouds look white or grayish white; this is the basic origin of white clouds.[2][10]

云显得暗，差别主要在厚度和含水量，而不是水滴本身变黑。薄云或碎云比较稀疏，阳光容易穿过整个云体，云的上下两面都被照亮，底部就亮白。厚云，尤其是发展旺盛的积雨云，垂直厚度可达数千米，水滴又密又多，光线在云内部要经过一次次散射和吸收，能穿透到底部的光越来越少。云的含水量也影响明暗：同样厚度下，单位体积里水滴越多，散射和吸收越强烈，底部就越暗；积雨云正是厚度和含水量同时偏高的情形。Clouds look dark mainly because of thickness and water content, not because the droplets themselves turn black. Thin or broken clouds are sparse and sunlight passes through easily, so both top and base are lit and the base looks bright white. Thick clouds, especially tall cumulonimbus, can reach several kilometres vertically with dense droplets, and light undergoes repeated scattering and absorption inside, leaving little to reach the base.[2][10]

同一片云常常上白下灰。顶部直接接受阳光照射，散射光充足，看起来亮；底部背向阳光，只能靠内部多次散射剩下的光照亮，于是发暗。如果观察者从侧面或下方看，云的厚薄、阳光角度不同，同一团云在不同位置明暗也不一样。乌云并不是云滴染成了黑色，只是光没怎么透下来。The same cloud is often white on top and gray below. The top faces direct sunlight with plenty of scattered light and looks bright; the base faces away from the Sun and is lit only by what remains after multiple internal scatterings, so it darkens. Viewed from the side or below, thickness and solar angle change, and the same cloud looks different from place to place. A dark cloud is not dyed black; little light has simply passed through it.[10]

粒径和疏密也参与决定颜色。水滴越大、云体越密实，散射越强、透射越弱；高层由冰晶组成的卷云比较稀疏，常呈白亮的丝缕状，透光性好。傍晚阳光斜射穿过更厚的大气层，短波长被散射掉，剩下偏红橙的光把云底染成暖色，这与云自身变灰不是同一机制。高空冰晶云由细小冰晶组成，对光的散射更均匀，常呈丝缕状白亮；低空厚积云水滴密集，底部容易发灰发黑。Droplet size and density also set the color. Larger droplets and denser clouds scatter more and transmit less; high cirrus made of sparse ice crystals often looks bright and fibrous with good transmission. At sunset, slanted sunlight travels through more atmosphere, short wavelengths scatter away, and the remaining red-orange light warms the cloud base, which is a different mechanism from a cloud turning gray.[2][3]

边界要写清：云色受太阳高度、背景天空、观察角度和云型共同影响，不能凭颜色单独判断会不会下雨。判断降雨还要结合云的类型、厚度、含水量和天气系统；白亮的云可能在发展中，灰暗的云也未必一定降水。本章只解释明暗差别来自散射和透射，不展开天气预报。The boundary matters: cloud color depends on solar elevation, background sky, viewing angle and cloud type together, so color alone cannot predict rain. Rainfall also depends on cloud type, thickness, water content and the weather system; a bright cloud may still be developing, and a gray cloud may not necessarily precipitate. This chapter explains only how scattering and transmission produce the brightness difference, not weather forecasting.[10]

06月晕是怎样形成的？How does a lunar halo form?

高空卷层云中的六方冰晶折射月光，两次折射的最小偏向角约 22°，大量随机取向冰晶把偏折光集中成月亮周围的光环。Hexagonal ice crystals in high cirrostratus refract moonlight; a minimum deviation near 22°, across many randomly oriented crystals, gathers the bent light into a ring around the Moon.

月晕是出现在月亮周围的淡彩色光环，常常和高空卷层云同时出现。卷层云位于数千米以上的高空，温度远低于 0 °C，云中不是水滴而是大量六方柱状或片状的冰晶。月光穿过这片薄云时，会被这些冰晶折射和反射，大气光学现象就此产生。A lunar halo is a faint colored ring around the Moon, often seen together with high cirrostratus clouds. Cirrostratus sits several kilometres up, well below 0 °C, and contains not droplets but many hexagonal columnar or plate-shaped ice crystals. Moonlight passing through this thin cloud is refracted and reflected by the crystals, producing the atmospheric optical effect.[11]

最常见的是半径约 22° 的晕。光线进入一颗冰晶的一个棱面，折射后在晶体内传播，再从另一个棱面射出，两次折射使光偏离原来方向。六方冰晶相邻棱面的夹角约 60°，在特定入射角下，光线的偏向角有一个最小值，约为 22°；比这个角度更偏折的光较少，偏折到这一角度附近的光相对集中。之所以叫最小偏向角，是因为入射角连续变化时，出射光偏折程度先减小后增大，在某个角度达到极小值；冰晶把大部分光偏折到这个角度附近。The most common form is the halo near 22° in radius. Light enters one face of a crystal, refracts and travels through the ice, then exits another face; the two refractions bend the light away from its original direction. Adjacent faces of a hexagonal crystal meet at about 60°, and at a particular incidence angle the deviation has a minimum near 22°; light bent beyond this angle is scarce, while light bent close to that angle gathers together.[4][11]

天上冰晶成千上万、取向随机，每颗都把月光偏折约 22°。从地面看，所有偏折到同一角度方向的光叠加起来，就在月亮周围形成一个半径约 22° 的亮环。不同波长的光折射角略有差别，环的内侧偏红、外侧偏蓝，所以晕边常常带一点淡彩色，但颜色比彩虹柔和。Thousands of crystals in the sky are randomly oriented, and each bends moonlight by about 22°. From the ground, light bent to the same angular direction by all of them combines into a bright ring of about 22° radius around the Moon. Different wavelengths refract by slightly different angles, so the inner edge leans red and the outer edge blue; the halo often carries a soft tint, gentler than a rainbow.[4][11]

它和彩虹的成因要区分开。彩虹由空气中的水滴折射加内反射形成，弧带在背对太阳的方向；晕由冰晶折射形成，环绕在日月周围。冰晶直径常小于 20 μm，形状规整，取向又分散，所以形成的是圆环而不是水滴那样的弧形彩带。除 22° 晕外，偶尔还能见到更淡、半径约 46° 的大晕，以及日柱、假日等其他冰光学现象。假日是太阳两侧对称的亮斑，日柱是冰晶竖直反射形成的亮带，它们与 22° 晕同属冰晶光学现象，只是冰晶取向和光路不同。Its cause differs from a rainbow. A rainbow forms by refraction plus internal reflection in water drops and appears as an arc opposite the Sun; a halo forms by refraction through ice crystals and rings the Sun or Moon. Crystals are often under 20 μm, regular in shape and randomly oriented, which produces a ring rather than the arc of water drops. Besides the 22° halo, a fainter 46° halo and phenomena such as light pillars or mock suns occasionally appear.[3][4]

边界要写清：晕的出现说明高空已有冰晶云逼近，民间有“晕兆风雨”的经验说法，但这只是天气相关的经验线索，不能单凭一道晕就预报风雨。晕环大小还受冰晶形状、观察角度和云层厚度影响；本章只讲冰晶折射成环的光学机制，不展开天气系统演变。The boundary matters: a halo means high ice clouds are approaching, and folk sayings link halos to coming wind or rain, but this is only an empirical clue and a halo alone cannot forecast weather. Ring size also depends on crystal shape, viewing angle and cloud thickness; this chapter covers only the optical mechanism of refraction into a ring, not the evolution of weather systems.[11]
