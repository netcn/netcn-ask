01水在室温下为什么也会蒸发，而不一定要烧开？Why does water evaporate at room temperature instead of only when boiled?

分子动能分布有高能长尾，液面少数高能分子逸出；蒸发致冷，水汽化潜热约 2260 kJ/kg。A high-energy tail in the kinetic-energy distribution lets surface molecules escape; evaporation cools, and water's latent heat of vaporization is about 2260 kJ/kg.

液体里的分子动能并不整齐划一，而是按统计分布散开：温度越高，平均动能越大，分布整体向高能方向移动。分布有一条高能长尾，液面附近少数动能特别大的分子，足以克服液体表面分子间的吸引而逸出到空气中，这就是蒸发。即使水温远低于沸点，长尾里也总有一些分子达到逸出所需能量，所以室温下的水照样会慢慢变少。Molecular kinetic energies in a liquid are not uniform but spread over a statistical distribution: the higher the temperature, the larger the average energy and the farther the distribution shifts toward high energy. A high-energy tail holds a few molecules near the surface with enough kinetic energy to overcome intermolecular attraction and escape into the air: evaporation. Even well below boiling, the tail always contains some molecules with escape energy, so water at room temperature still slowly disappears.[1]

蒸发是发生在液面的表面过程，与沸腾不同。沸腾是液体内部形成气泡、整体达到沸点后的剧烈汽化；蒸发在任何温度都能进行，只是快慢不同。逸出的是高能分子，留在液体里的分子平均动能随之下降，因此蒸发会带走热量、使液体和邻近表面降温。出汗后皮肤感到凉爽、酒精擦身退热，靠的都是蒸发致冷。Evaporation is a surface process, unlike boiling. Boiling forms bubbles inside the liquid and is vigorous vaporization at the boiling point; evaporation proceeds at any temperature, only faster or slower. Escaping molecules carry high energy away, so the average energy left behind drops, and evaporation cools the liquid and nearby surface. Cool skin after sweating and alcohol rubs rely on this evaporative cooling.[1][2]

水汽化需要吸收潜热。水在沸点附近的汽化潜热约 2260 kJ/kg，即每千克水汽化要吸收约 2.26×10⁶ J 能量，这些能量用于克服分子间结合，而不是升高温度。空气越潮湿，液面上方水汽越接近饱和，逸出分子与返回分子趋于平衡，蒸发就越慢；干燥空气则能容纳更多水汽，蒸发更快。Vaporization absorbs latent heat. Water's latent heat of vaporization near boiling is about 2260 kJ/kg: each kilogram absorbs roughly 2.26×10⁶ J to overcome molecular binding, not to raise temperature. Humid air near saturation balances escaping and returning molecules, slowing evaporation; dry air holds more moisture and evaporates faster.[2][7]

蒸发速率受温度、表面积和通风影响。升温提高高能分子比例，逸出数量增多；扩大液面增加可供逸出的位置；气流把液面附近的水汽带走，降低返回液体的分子比例。晾衣服摊开、放在通风向阳处干得快，正是这三个因素共同作用。蒸发快慢还与液体种类有关，挥发性强的液体分子间结合弱，逸出更快。OpenStax 热学章节把分子动能分布、表面逸出与潜热作为蒸发致冷的定量主线。Evaporation rate depends on temperature, surface area and ventilation. Warmer temperature raises the share of high-energy molecules; a larger surface adds escape sites; airflow sweeps away vapour near the surface and lowers the return rate. Clothes dry faster spread out in sun and wind, the combined effect of all three factors. The OpenStax heat chapters use the kinetic-energy distribution, surface escape and latent heat as the quantitative thread of evaporative cooling.[1][2]

边界：蒸发不是被热“赶跑”的分子，而是动能分布长尾的统计结果；通风加速的是水汽扩散，并不直接降低水温。湿度高时蒸发慢，体感闷热不仅因为温度，也因为汗液难以挥发。把蒸发和沸腾混为一谈会误判条件：前者表面缓慢进行，后者内部气泡、需达到沸点并持续吸热。Boundary: evaporation is not molecules chased away by heat but a statistical result of the energy tail; ventilation speeds vapour diffusion and does not directly lower water temperature. In humidity, slow evaporation makes the air feel muggy not only through temperature but because sweat cannot volatilize. Conflating evaporation and boiling misreads the conditions: the former proceeds slowly at the surface, the latter forms internal bubbles at boiling point with continuous heat input.[1]

02同样 100°C 的水和水蒸气，哪一个更“热”？温度计为什么能测出温度？Which feels hotter, 100°C water or steam, and how does a thermometer read temperature?

蒸汽携带汽化潜热，凝结放热更易烫伤；相变期间温度不变，Q=mcΔT 与 Q=mL 分开计算。Steam carries vaporization latent heat released on condensing, burning worse; phase change keeps temperature constant, with Q=mcΔT and Q=mL computed separately.

温度描述物体热平衡的冷热程度，温度计读数来自测温物质与被测对象达到热平衡后的状态。对理想气体，温度与分子平均动能直接相关；对液体和固体，温度仍是分子热运动剧烈程度的宏观标志。两个接触物体达到热平衡时温度相同，这是接触式温度计能够读数的前提。Temperature describes how hot an object is at thermal equilibrium, and a thermometer reads the state of its sensing material after reaching equilibrium with the object. For an ideal gas temperature ties directly to average molecular kinetic energy; for liquids and solids it remains the macroscopic marker of thermal motion intensity. Two objects in contact read the same temperature at equilibrium, the premise behind contact thermometers.[6]

相变期间温度可以保持不变。冰熔化时吸收的热量用于打破晶体分子间结合，而不是升温；水沸腾时吸热变成水蒸气，在同一压强下液态水与水蒸气可以同处 100°C。但同温度的水和蒸汽携带的能量不同：蒸汽在汽化时吸收过潜热，接触皮肤后凝结放热，因此蒸汽烫伤往往比同温度热水更重。Temperature can stay constant during a phase change. Melting ice absorbs heat to break crystal binding rather than warm up; boiling water absorbs heat into steam, and at one pressure liquid water and vapour can both sit at 100°C. Yet water and steam at the same temperature carry different energy: steam absorbed latent heat during vaporization and releases it on condensing against skin, so steam burns are often worse than equal-temperature hot water.[2]

常用热学数值：液态水比热约 4186 J/(kg·K)，熔化潜热约 334 kJ/kg，汽化潜热约 2260 kJ/kg。升温过程吸热 Q=mcΔT，相变过程吸热 Q=mL，两类公式不能混用。把 1 kg 水从 0°C 加热到 100°C 约需 418.6 kJ，而把 100°C 的 1 kg 水全部汽化还要再吸收约 2260 kJ，潜热远大于升温显热。Typical thermal values: liquid water specific heat about 4186 J/(kg·K), latent heat of fusion about 334 kJ/kg, latent heat of vaporization about 2260 kJ/kg. Warming absorbs Q=mcΔT, a phase change absorbs Q=mL, and the two formulas must not be mixed. Heating 1 kg of water from 0°C to 100°C needs about 418.6 kJ, while vaporizing that kilogram at 100°C absorbs another 2260 kJ: latent heat far exceeds the sensible warming heat.[2][7]

温度计利用随温度变化的物性：液体热膨胀（水银、酒精温度计）、双金属片弯曲、热电偶的塞贝克效应，以及热敏电阻或金属电阻随温度的变化。接触式温度计要与被测对象达到热平衡后读数，接触时间不足会读到尚未平衡的中间值；红外测温则接收物体辐射，不必接触。不同测温原理适用的温度范围和精度不同，NIST 水性质数据库提供比热、潜热等参数核验。Thermometers use properties that vary with temperature: liquid expansion (mercury, alcohol), bimetallic bending, the Seebeck effect in thermocouples, and resistance changes in thermistors or metals. Contact devices read after equilibrium; infrared thermometers receive radiation without contact. Each sensing principle suits a different range and accuracy, and the NIST water properties database supplies specific heat and latent heat values for checking.[6][7]

边界：温度高不等于热量多，热量还取决于质量和热容；一大桶温水含的热量可能多于一小杯热水。100°C 蒸汽比同温水“更烫”，差别在凝结时放出的潜热，而不是温度更高。相变潜热用于改变分子间结合状态，不能当作升温能量；把潜热和显热混入会错误估算加热时间与能耗。测温数值必须连同温标（摄氏、开尔文）一起说明，不同温标的零点不同。Boundary: high temperature does not mean much heat, which also depends on mass and heat capacity; a large tub of warm water can hold more heat than a small cup of hot water. 100°C steam burns worse than equal-temperature water because of latent heat released on condensation, not because its temperature is higher. Latent heat changes molecular binding state and must not be treated as warming energy; mixing latent and sensible heat misestimates heating time and energy.[2]

03保温瓶为什么能长时间保温？风扇为什么让人凉快，它降低了空气温度吗？Why does a thermos stay hot or cold, and why does a fan cool without lowering air temperature?

传导 q=kAΔT/L、对流搬热、辐射 P=εσAT⁴；真空切断传导对流，镀银削弱辐射；风扇只加速对流蒸发。Conduction q=kAΔT/L, convective bulk transport, radiation P=εσAT⁴; vacuum cuts conduction/convection, silvering weakens radiation; a fan speeds convection and evaporation only.

热量通过三条途径传递。传导靠直接接触，分子振动和自由电子把能量从高温处带到低温处，多见于固体；对流靠流体整体流动搬运热量，如水的循环和空气流动；辐射以电磁波形式向外传递，不需要介质，太阳的热就是穿过真空到达地球的。同一环境中三条途径常常同时存在，只是主次不同。Heat travels by three routes. Conduction works through direct contact: molecular vibration and free electrons carry energy from hot to cold, mostly in solids. Convection carries heat by bulk fluid motion, as in water circulation and air currents. Radiation transmits energy by electromagnetic waves without a medium; the Sun's heat reaches Earth through vacuum. The three often act together, differing only in which dominates.[3]

传导可用傅里叶定律量化：热流 q=kAΔT/L，k 是导热系数，A 截面积，L 厚度，ΔT 两侧温差；对应热阻 R=L/(kA)，热阻越大传热越慢。静止空气的 k 约 0.026 W/(m·K)，是很好的隔热体，铜的 k 约 401 W/(m·K)，导热快。保温材料往往靠锁住静止空气来降低传导，例如泡沫、羽绒和织物纤维层；多层织物把空气分割成细小静止气室，避免空气对流把热量带走。Conduction is quantified by Fourier's law: heat flow q=kAΔT/L, with k thermal conductivity, A cross-section, L thickness and ΔT the temperature difference; the corresponding thermal resistance R=L/(kA), larger resistance slowing heat transfer. Still air has k about 0.026 W/(m·K), a good insulator, while copper is about 401 W/(m·K) and conducts fast. Insulating materials trap still air, as foams, down and fabric fibre layers do.[4]

热辐射遵守斯特藩-玻尔兹曼定律：辐射功率 P=εσAT⁴，σ=5.67×10⁻⁸ W/(m²·K⁴)，ε 是发射率，T 为绝对温度。温度越高辐射功率按四次方增长；抛光金属和浅色表面发射率低，辐射散热少。人体在常温下主要发射红外辐射，红外测温与夜视都利用这一波段；高温物体才会发出可见光辐射。Thermal radiation follows the Stefan-Boltzmann law: radiated power P=εσAT⁴, σ=5.67×10⁻⁸ W/(m²·K⁴), ε emissivity and T absolute temperature. Radiation power grows with the fourth power of temperature; polished metal and light surfaces have low emissivity and radiate little. The body mainly emits infrared at room temperature, the band used by infrared thermometers and night vision; only hot objects emit visible radiation.[5]

保温瓶正是分别堵住三条途径。双层玻璃壁之间抽成真空，没有流体和固体接触路径，传导与对流被切断；内壁镀银或抛光成高反射低发射表面，削弱辐射换热；软木或塑料瓶口是热的不良导体，阻断瓶口处的传导对流。多层结构把三条传热途径逐一削弱，使瓶内热水或冰水都能长时间保持温度。A thermos blocks the routes one by one. The double glass wall is evacuated, removing fluid and solid contact paths so conduction and convection are cut; the inner wall is silvered or polished to a reflective low-emissivity surface, weakening radiation; the cork or plastic stopper is a poor conductor, blocking conduction and convection at the mouth. The layered structure weakens each route, keeping hot or cold contents steady for a long time.[3][4][5]

边界：风扇让人凉快是加速对流和汗液蒸发，并不降低空气本身的温度；温度计挂在通风处读数仍接近气温。真空能阻断传导和对流，却不能阻断辐射，所以保温瓶还要镀反射层。表面辐射强弱由发射率决定，颜色深浅只在可见光波段直观，白色并非在所有红外波段都低辐射。保温只是减缓传热速率，不可能完全阻止；环境温差越大，最终散失的热量越多。Boundary: a fan cools by speeding convection and sweat evaporation, not by lowering the air temperature; a thermometer in airflow still reads near the air temperature. Vacuum blocks conduction and convection but not radiation, which is why the thermos needs a reflective lining. Insulation slows the rate of heat transfer and cannot stop it entirely; the larger the temperature difference with the surroundings, the more heat eventually escapes.[3][5]

04玩滑梯时屁股为什么会发热？人体为什么能保持大约 37°C 的体温？Why does sliding down a slide warm the seat, and how does the body hold about 37°C?

摩擦 W=f·s 把机械功转成内能；代谢产热经对流、辐射、汗液蒸发散热，动态平衡维持体温。Friction W=f·s turns mechanical work into internal energy; metabolic heat leaves by convection, radiation and sweat evaporation, a dynamic balance holding body temperature.

摩擦过程中机械功转化为内能。滑梯上臀部与板面摩擦、刹车蹄片摩擦车轮、钻木取火，都是阻力做功把机械能变成热。摩擦做功 W=f·s，f 是摩擦力，s 是相对滑动距离；这部分功最终表现为接触面温度升高。摩擦生热不是摩擦“造出”了温度，而是能量形式从机械能转成内能。Friction converts mechanical work into internal energy. Sliding on a slide, brake pads gripping a wheel, and drilling wood to make fire all turn resistance work into heat. Friction work W=f·s, f the friction force and s the relative sliding distance, ends as a temperature rise at the contact. Friction does not "make" temperature; it changes energy from mechanical motion into internal energy.[1]

按能量守恒，耗散过程中机械能不会消失，而是转化为分子热运动能量。接触面粗糙处互相挤压、剪切，分子振动加剧，局部温度上升；产生的热量在接触的两个物体和周围环境之间分配。摩擦系数、压力和滑动距离决定做功多少，滑动越快越久，转化的热越多。冬天搓手取暖就是用摩擦功提高手部温度。By conservation of energy, dissipative processes do not destroy mechanical work but turn it into molecular thermal motion. Rough contact points compress and shear, intensifying molecular vibration and raising local temperature; the heat distributes between the two bodies and surroundings. Friction coefficient, pressure and sliding distance set the work, and faster or longer sliding makes more heat. Rubbing hands together warms them through this friction work.[1][3]

人体通过代谢产热维持大约 37°C 的体温。食物中的化学能经代谢释放，一部分用于肌肉做功和基础生理活动，其余转化为热；身体再通过皮肤对流、辐射和汗液蒸发把多余热量散出。代谢率随活动量增减，肌肉运动时产热明显上升。产热与散热动态平衡时体温稳定，平衡偏向产热就发热，偏向散热就失温。鸟类正常体温通常在 39–43°C，高于哺乳动物，代谢率和羽毛隔热各有差异。The body maintains about 37°C through metabolic heat production. Chemical energy from food is released by metabolism: part goes to muscle work and basic physiology, the rest becomes heat, and surplus heat leaves by skin convection, radiation and sweat evaporation. Body temperature stays steady when production and loss balance; leaning toward production causes fever, toward loss hypothermia. Birds normally hold 39–43°C, above mammals, reflecting metabolic rate and feather insulation.[3][6]

热平衡被打破会出现中暑或失温。环境高温高湿时对流辐射散热受限，汗液蒸发又受湿度阻碍，产热大于散热，体温升高；寒冷环境中散热超过代谢产热，体温逐步下降。穿衣、避风、补水和调节活动量都是在帮助身体维持产热与散热的平衡。OpenStax 温度与热传递章节把代谢产热、散热途径和热平衡放在同一框架内讨论。Heat balance breaks down into heatstroke or hypothermia. In hot humid surroundings convection and radiation are restricted and evaporation hindered by humidity, so production exceeds loss and temperature rises; in cold, loss exceeds metabolic production and temperature falls. Clothing, shelter from wind, hydration and activity level all help keep production and loss balanced. The OpenStax temperature and heat-transfer chapters frame metabolic production, loss routes and thermal balance together.[3][6]

边界：感到“辣”是辣椒素刺激化学感受器产生的痛觉信号，并不是真正的温度升高；食物的冷热与味觉刺激要分开判断。摩擦生热的本质是机械功转内能，不能说摩擦本身产生温度。人体维持 37°C 是持续调节的动态平衡，不是身体在不断额外发热；外界温度变化时，产热和散热比例需要随之调整。Boundary: the "burn" of spice is a pain signal from capsaicin acting on chemical receptors, not a real temperature rise; taste heat must be judged separately from physical temperature. Friction heating is mechanical work turning into internal energy; one cannot say friction itself produces temperature. Holding 37°C is a continuously regulated dynamic balance, not constant extra heat production; production and loss ratios adjust as the environment changes.[6]

05为什么水能灭火？Why does water put out a fire?

水靠高比热和汽化潜热带走热量，蒸汽膨胀稀释氧气，断开燃烧链条。Water carries heat away through high specific heat and latent vaporization heat, and expanding steam dilutes oxygen, breaking the combustion chain.

灭火先看燃烧靠什么维持。燃烧同时需要可燃物、氧气和达到燃点的热量，链式反应放出的热又把燃料维持在燃点以上；只要切断其中一环，火焰就不能自持。火焰里的燃烧不是燃料单纯接触氧气，而是自由基参与的链式反应：局部高温把燃料分子打断成活泼基团，基团与氧气反应再产生新的基团，反应一旦铺开就自行维持。水灭火的第一条途径是把热量带走，让燃料表面温度降到燃点以下，燃烧链条失去热量来源。Extinguishing a fire starts with what keeps combustion going. A flame needs fuel, oxygen and heat above the ignition temperature at the same time, and the chain reaction releases heat that keeps the fuel above that threshold; cut any link and the flame cannot sustain itself. The flame runs on a radical chain reaction: local heat breaks fuel molecules into reactive groups that react with oxygen and generate new groups, and once spread the reaction maintains itself. Water's first route to extinguish is carrying heat away, dropping the fuel surface below its ignition point so the combustion chain loses its heat source.[3]

吸热能力来自水的高比热和高汽化潜热。液态水的比热约 4.18 kJ/(kg·K)，升温阶段就能吸收大量热量；在 100 °C 汽化时，每千克水还要再吸收约 2260 kJ 的汽化潜热。水喷到火焰和燃料表面，先升温、再汽化，两段吸热把燃料表层温度拉下来；水分子之间氢键较强，升温时要先投入能量打破部分氢键，这也是水比热和汽化潜热都很高的分子层面原因。一旦温度低于燃点，链式反应继续放不出足够的热，火焰就减弱、熄灭。Its heat uptake comes from a high specific heat and a high latent heat of vaporization. Liquid water has a specific heat of about 4.18 kJ/(kg·K), absorbing a large amount as it warms; vaporizing one kilogram at 100 °C takes another about 2260 kJ of latent heat. Water sprayed onto the flame and fuel surface first warms and then vaporizes, and these two heat-absorbing stages pull the fuel surface temperature down. Hydrogen bonds between water molecules are relatively strong, and breaking part of them costs energy, which underlies water's high heat capacity and latent heat. Once it falls below the ignition point, the chain reaction can no longer release enough heat, and the flame weakens and dies.[1][2][7]

第二条途径是蒸汽稀释与热辐射隔阻。1 kg 水变成 100 °C 水蒸气后，体积膨胀约 1600–1700 倍，在火焰周围形成一层蒸汽，排挤、稀释局部氧气浓度；水蒸气还能阻挡一部分火焰反馈给燃料的热辐射，减少燃料继续受热分解。蒸汽层把火焰与周围空气隔开，氧气必须穿过这层蒸汽才能到达燃烧面，供应被进一步削弱。降温与隔氧两条途径叠加，火焰周围的燃烧条件被同时削弱。The second route is steam dilution and radiant shielding. One kilogram of water becomes about 1600 to 1700 times its volume in steam at 100 °C, forming a vapour layer around the flame that displaces and dilutes the local oxygen concentration; the steam also blocks part of the radiant heat fed back from the flame to the fuel, slowing further thermal decomposition. Cooling and oxygen exclusion act together, weakening the burning conditions around the flame at the same time.[2][7]

水并不是对所有火都适用。油类密度比水小，会浮在水面上，浇水后水沉到油层下方，接触不到燃烧面，还可能骤然汽化把燃烧的油滴溅开，反而让火势蔓延；带电设备着火时水会导电，存在触电风险；遇水反应的金属和某些化学品，用水扑救会加剧反应。这类火灾应使用二氧化碳、干粉或与燃料对应的灭火剂。Water does not suit every fire. Oils are less dense than water and float on it; poured water sinks below the oil layer, misses the burning surface, and may vaporize suddenly, splashing burning droplets and spreading the fire. Water conducts electricity, so energized equipment fires carry a shock risk, and reactive metals plus some chemicals react with water and worsen it. Those fires call for carbon dioxide, dry powder or the matching agent for the fuel.[3]

边界需要写清：本章只解释水灭火的物理机制，不构成现场灭火操作指引。判断能不能用水，要看燃料种类、是否带电和周围环境；火势超出初期范围，或伴随有毒烟气、爆炸风险时，应先撤离并呼叫专业消防，不要自行冒险扑救。Boundary: this chapter explains the physical mechanism of water extinguishment, not a field firefighting procedure. Whether water can be used depends on the fuel class, any electrical involvement and the surroundings; once a fire has left its early stage, or gives off toxic smoke or carries explosion risk, people should evacuate first and call professional firefighters rather than fight it themselves.[1][3]
