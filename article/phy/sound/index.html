01如果声音能在真空中传播，宇宙里的爆炸声会有多响？电话怎样把声音传到远处？If sound could travel through space, how loud would explosions be, and how does a telephone carry sound far away?

声音是靠介质接力的机械纵波，真空不传声；20 °C 空气声速约 343 m/s，电话先把声振动转成电信号再还原。Sound is a mechanical longitudinal wave relayed by a medium; vacuum carries none. Air speed about 343 m/s at 20 °C; a phone converts vibration to an electrical signal and back.

声音是机械波，必须靠介质传播。发声体振动时挤压附近的空气分子，被推挤的分子再撞向相邻分子，形成疏密相间的纵波向外扩散；分子本身只在原地附近振动，传播的是振动形式和能量。真空中没有可以被挤压碰撞的介质，振动无法接力传递，所以太空里听不到爆炸声，飞船之间的通讯只能靠电磁波。Sound is a mechanical wave and needs a medium to travel. A vibrating source pushes nearby air molecules, which collide with their neighbours, sending a alternating compression-rarefaction longitudinal wave outward; molecules only oscillate near their own positions, while the vibration pattern and energy travel. A vacuum has no molecules to push and collide, so vibration cannot be handed on: explosions in space cannot be heard, and spacecraft communicate by electromagnetic waves.[1]

声速由介质性质决定。空气中 20 °C 时声速约 343 m/s，温度升高声速略增：对空气这类气体，温度升高使分子平均动能增大、气体弹性模量随温度上升，声速近似与热力学温度的平方根成正比，室温附近每升高 1 °C 声速约增加 0.6 m/s；水中声速约 1480 m/s，固体中更快。声音在不同介质里的速度差别，来自分子间距和弹性：介质越密、弹性越好，振动传得越快。光速约 3×10⁸ m/s，比声速快约八十多万倍，所以打雷时先看见闪电、后听到雷声，按看到闪光到听见雷声的秒数除以 3，可估出雷击距离约多少公里。Sound speed depends on the medium. In air at 20 °C it is about 343 m/s, rising a little with temperature: in gases such as air, higher temperature raises average molecular kinetic energy and the gas elastic modulus, so speed grows roughly with the square root of absolute temperature, about 0.6 m/s per °C near room temperature; in water about 1480 m/s, and faster still in solids. These differences come from molecular spacing and elasticity: a denser, stiffer medium passes vibration more quickly. Light travels about 3×10⁸ m/s, over eight hundred thousand times faster, which is why lightning is seen before thunder; dividing the seconds between flash and sound by three estimates the storm distance in kilometres.[1][2]

声音的传播可以被电话转换和接力。话筒把空气振动变成随声音变化的电信号，沿导线或无线电传到对方，听筒再把电信号还原成空气振动。这条链路里声能先转成电能、再转回声能，传输距离不再受声波在空气中衰减的限制；信号在导线里以接近光速传播，而不是以 343 m/s 跑完全程。扬声器是反向的话筒：音频电流流过磁场中的音圈，音圈往复振动并带动纸盆，推动空气形成声波。Telephones convert and relay sound. A microphone turns air vibration into an electrical signal that varies with the sound, sends it along a wire or radio link, and a receiver turns it back into air vibration. Along this chain acoustic energy becomes electrical energy and then acoustic energy again, so range is no longer limited by air attenuation; the signal travels near light speed in the wire, not at 343 m/s.A loudspeaker is a microphone in reverse: audio current passes through a voice coil in a magnetic field, the coil vibrates back and forth and pushes the cone, which drives the air into sound waves.[1]

声波在传播中会衰减和反射。离声源越远，波面铺开，单位面积能量降低，声音变轻；遇到墙面、山体等障碍物，部分能量被反射形成回声，部分被吸收或绕射。室内吸音材料减少反射，室外开阔地声音向四周散开，同样距离下响度感受不同。声压随距离和吸收逐步下降，可用分贝衡量声级变化。声速、衰减和反射共同决定一个空间里听到的声音。Waves attenuate and reflect as they travel. Farther from the source the wavefront spreads, energy per area drops, and the sound fades; at walls or hills part of the energy reflects as an echo, part is absorbed or diffracted. Indoor absorbers reduce reflection, while open air lets sound spread in all directions, so loudness at the same distance feels different. Speed, attenuation and reflection together shape what a space sounds like.[2][3]

边界：机械声波需要介质，这与电磁波可以在真空中传播不同；不要把引力波、光波也当成声音。声速随温度、湿度和介质成分变化，343 m/s 是 20 °C 干燥空气的近似值。真空不传声是实验事实，若假设真空能传声，需要先改变介质条件，不能直接套用日常空气里的响度经验。The boundary: mechanical sound needs a medium, unlike electromagnetic waves that cross vacuum; do not treat gravitational waves or light as sound. Speed shifts with temperature, humidity and medium, and 343 m/s is an approximation for dry air at 20 °C. Vacuum silence is an experimental fact; assuming sound travels in vacuum changes the medium condition first, so everyday loudness experience cannot be carried over directly.[1][2]

02敲击不同水量的杯子，哪个声音更高？为什么声音有高有低？Which cup sounds higher when tapped, one with more or less water, and why do sounds differ in pitch?

音调由频率决定；水多有效质量大、振动慢、音低。共振在固有频率处放大，Q 值越高峰越窄、延音越长。Pitch depends on frequency; more water means larger mass, slower vibration and lower pitch. Resonance amplifies the natural frequency, and higher Q gives a narrow peak and long sustain.

音调高低由振动频率决定。声源每秒振动的次数叫频率，单位赫兹（Hz）；频率越高，听到的音调越高，频率越低，音调越低。人耳可听范围大致 20 Hz 到 20 kHz，低于 20 Hz 是次声，高于 20 kHz 是超声。音叉、琴弦和声带的振动频率直接对应听到的音高；钢琴中央 C 附近频率约 262 Hz，高八度频率加倍。Pitch is set by vibration frequency. The number of vibrations per second is frequency in hertz (Hz): higher frequency means a higher pitch, lower frequency a lower pitch. Human hearing spans roughly 20 Hz to 20 kHz; below 20 Hz is infrasound, above 20 kHz ultrasound. Tuning forks, strings and vocal folds map directly onto perceived pitch; middle C is about 262 Hz, and one octave up doubles the frequency.[2]

敲击不同水量的杯子，声音高低由杯子和水的共同振动决定。水越多，参与振动的有效质量越大，振动越慢、频率越低，声音越沉闷；水越少，有效质量越小，振动越快，声音越清脆。注意区分敲击杯壁和吹杯口：敲击主要是杯体与水振动，吹杯口主要是杯内空气柱振动，水量增加时空气柱变短，音调反而升高，两种机制结论相反。Striking cups with different water levels: pitch is set by the combined vibration of cup and water. More water means a larger effective vibrating mass, slower vibration, lower frequency and a duller sound; less water means lighter effective mass, faster vibration and a clearer tone. Note the difference between tapping the rim and blowing across the mouth: tapping drives the glass and water, blowing drives the air column, so adding water shortens the column and raises pitch there — the two mechanisms give opposite results.[2][4]

共振让特定频率被放大。物体都有自己的固有频率；外界振动频率接近固有频率时，振幅显著增大，这就是共振。琴弦、管风琴管和音箱箱体都按目标频率设计；空气柱驻波在两端或开口处形成波腹波节，决定可发出的音高。共振峰越窄、能量损失越小，品质因数 Q 越高，声音越纯净延音越长；阻尼大的物体 Q 低，共振峰宽而短促。Resonance amplifies certain frequencies. Every object has a natural frequency; when an external vibration approaches it, amplitude grows markedly. Strings, organ pipes and enclosures are sized for target frequencies, and standing waves in air columns form antinodes and nodes at ends and openings to set pitch. A narrow resonance peak with low energy loss means a high quality factor Q: purer tone, longer sustain. Damped objects have low Q, a broad short peak.[4]

频率与波长通过声速联系：λ=v/f。同一速度下，高频声波波长短，低频声波波长长；低音绕射能力强，能绕过障碍传到拐角，高音直线性强、容易被遮挡。调音时改变弦长、张力或空气质量，就是在改变固有频率；乐器定音、汽车发动机异响和房间驻波，都可以从频率与共振角度分析。Frequency and wavelength link through sound speed: λ=v/f. At fixed speed, high frequency has short wavelength, low frequency long wavelength; bass diffracts around obstacles into corners, while treble travels more straight and is easier to block. Adjusting string length, tension or air mass changes natural frequency; instrument tuning, engine noise and room standing waves all read through frequency and resonance.[2]

边界：音调只由频率决定，响度由振幅决定，两者不要混为一谈；大声不等于高音。杯子实验中敲击与吹气的振动主体不同，结论不能互相套用。共振需要频率接近且有持续能量输入，单纯“物体相似”不会自动共振。人耳对不同频率的敏感程度不同，高音低音都需要相应声压才能被察觉；可听频率范围随年龄和听力状况变化，超声与次声需要专用仪器检测。The boundary: pitch depends only on frequency, loudness on amplitude; loud is not the same as high-pitched. In the cup experiment the vibrating body differs between tapping and blowing, so conclusions do not transfer. Resonance needs a near-matching frequency and sustained energy input; mere similarity does not make things resonate. Audible range varies with age and hearing, and ultrasound or infrasound need instruments to detect.[4][5]

03在回音壁里为什么能听到清晰回音？多个扬声器同步播放，声音为什么会有强弱起伏？Why is a whisper heard clearly in a whispering gallery, and why do synced speakers produce loud and soft spots?

回声是反射波，往返时间 t=2d/v；多波叠加同相加强、反相抵消，路程差决定相长或相消干涉。An echo is a reflected wave with round-trip time t=2d/v; superposition adds in phase and cancels out of phase, path difference setting constructive or destructive interference.

回声是声波遇到障碍物后的反射。声波向前传播，碰到墙面、山崖或建筑表面，一部分能量按反射规律返回，被人耳再次接收。反射面离得越远，回声返回越晚；人耳能分辨回声与原声的间隔约需十分之一秒以上，按声速 343 m/s 算，障碍物至少约 17 米外，回声才不会和原声糊在一起。回音壁利用光滑弧形墙面把声音多次反射，沿墙传播到远处仍保持清晰。An echo is sound reflected off an obstacle. The wave travels forward, hits a wall, hillside or building, and part of the energy returns by the reflection law to be heard again. A farther reflector returns later; the ear separates echo from original only after roughly a tenth of a second, so at 343 m/s the obstacle needs to be about 17 metres or more away before echo and source stop merging. A whispering gallery uses a smooth curved wall to reflect sound repeatedly along the wall, keeping it clear far from the speaker.[2][4]

多个波到达同一地点时发生叠加。两列波同相相遇，振动相加，声音加强，是相长干涉；反相相遇，振动抵消，声音减弱甚至消失，是相消干涉。三个或更多扬声器同步播放同一信号，房间里不同位置到各音箱的路程差不同，相位关系不同，于是出现有的地方响、有的地方轻的干涉图案。这就是为什么同一套音响在房间不同座位听感差别明显。Waves arriving at one point superpose. Two waves in phase add vibration and sound grows: constructive interference. Out of phase they cancel, weakening or silencing the sound: destructive interference. Three or more speakers playing the same signal in sync produce different path lengths to each listener, different phase relations, and a pattern of loud spots and soft spots — the reason one system sounds different from seat to seat.[4]

驻波是干涉的特殊情况。入射波与反射波在有限空间内叠加，形成位置固定的波节和波腹；管乐器、房间和车厢内都可能出现驻波，某些频率被加强，另一些被削弱。回音壁、山谷回声和室内混响的差别，在于反射面距离、形状和吸声条件：单次远反射是清晰回声，多次近反射叠加成长短不一的混响尾巴。Standing waves are a special case of interference. Incident and reflected waves superpose in a finite space into fixed nodes and antinodes; instruments, rooms and car cabins can develop standing waves that strengthen some frequencies and weaken others. Echoes in a gallery, valley and indoor reverberation differ by reflector distance, shape and absorption: a single far reflection is a clear echo, many near reflections blend into a reverberant tail.[2][4]

反射与干涉可以定量估算。回声往返距离 2d，时间 t=2d/v；路程差对应的相位差决定干涉强弱，路程差为波长整数倍时相长，半波长奇数倍时相消。隔声、吸声和扬声器摆位都基于这些规律：吸音材料消耗反射能量，音箱拉开距离或调整角度减少同相叠加，房间对称布置改善干涉分布。Reflection and interference can be estimated quantitatively. Echo round trip is 2d, time t=2d/v; path difference sets phase, constructive at integer wavelengths, destructive at odd half wavelengths. Sound insulation, absorption and speaker placement follow these rules: absorbers consume reflected energy, speakers spaced or angled reduce in-phase buildup, and symmetric room layout evens the interference pattern.[2][3]

边界：回声需要足够大的反射面和足够距离，小房间里反射与原声融合成混响，听不到独立回声。干涉是波的叠加效应，不要求两列波来自同一振动源，但频率必须相同、相位稳定。回音壁的清晰度依赖墙面光滑连续，破损或缝隙会破坏反射路径。把听到的所有“回响”都当成回声，会混淆混响、反射和驻波三种不同现象。The boundary: an echo needs a large reflector and enough distance; in a small room reflections merge with the source into reverberation, with no separate echo. Interference is wave superposition; waves need not share one source, but must match frequency and stable phase. Whispering-gallery clarity depends on a smooth continuous wall, and cracks break the reflection path. Calling every reverberation an echo conflates reverberation, reflection and standing waves.[4]

04为什么录音里自己的声音听起来不像自己？音色由什么决定？Why does your own voice sound unlike you on a recording, and what determines timbre?

音色由谐波频谱与时间包络决定；自己说话经骨传导多传低频，录音走空气传导，回放听感不同。Timbre comes from harmonic spectrum and time envelope; your own voice carries more bass through bone conduction, recordings travel by air, so playback differs.

音色区分相同音高、相同响度的不同声音。每个发声体除基频外还产生若干谐波，谐波的数量、相对强弱和随时间的变化构成频谱；频谱不同，听感上的音色就不同。钢琴和小提琴奏同一个音，基频相同，谐波结构不同，耳朵立刻能分辨。语音也靠频谱特征形成不同元音和辅音，乐器、人声与环境噪声都有自己的频谱指纹。Timbre distinguishes sounds of the same pitch and loudness. Besides the fundamental frequency, each source produces harmonics; their number, relative strength and time variation form a spectrum, and different spectra mean different timbres. Piano and violin play the same note with the same fundamental but different harmonic structure, and the ear tells them apart at once. Speech carries vowels and consonants through spectral features, and voices, instruments and ambient noise each have a spectral fingerprint.[5]

自己听到的声音和录音里不一样，是传导路径不同。日常听自己说话，声音一部分经空气传到耳朵，另一部分通过颅骨和颌骨直接把振动传给内耳，骨传导的低频成分保留更多，音色偏低沉厚重。录音和别人听到的你，主要走空气传导，高频损失和混响不同，于是回放时觉得“不像自己”。堵住耳朵说话时声音更闷，正是骨传导占比变化的直接体验。Your own voice sounds different on a recording because conduction paths differ. In daily hearing part of your speech reaches the ear through air, and part travels through skull and jawbone directly to the inner ear; bone conduction keeps more low-frequency content and sounds deeper. Recordings and what others hear travel mainly by air, with different high-frequency loss and reverberation, so playback feels unlike you. Plugging your ears makes the voice muffler, a direct experience of shifting the bone-conduction share.[3][5]

海螺贴耳听到的“海浪声”不是螺壳里藏着海。海螺腔室对环境噪声起共振和滤波作用，周围环境的低频嗡鸣、血流声和风噪被腔壁选择放大，形成类似海浪的起伏；换不同容器贴耳，听到的嗡嗡声各不相同。老旧乐器音色难以复制，也是因为木材老化、琴箱共鸣和表面状态改变了谐波结构，单凭复制外形无法复原频谱。The 'seaside' sound in a seashell does not come from trapped sea. The shell chamber resonates and filters ambient noise: background hum, blood-flow sound and wind noise are selected and amplified by its walls into a wave-like swell; different containers give different buzz. Old-instrument tone is hard to copy because aged wood, box resonance and surface state alter the harmonic structure, and replicating shape cannot restore the spectrum.[5]

听觉把频谱转换成听感。外耳道、中耳和内耳对不同频率有不同响应，人耳对 2–5 kHz 附近最敏感，低音和高音需要更大声压才被察觉；响度以分贝计量，是相对基准的对数尺度。说话、演唱和乐器制作都围绕音色与频率响应展开，助听器、耳机调音和房间音响设计则是在补偿或塑造频谱。Hearing maps spectrum onto perception. Ear canal, middle ear and inner ear respond differently across frequencies; hearing is most sensitive near 2–5 kHz, while bass and treble need larger pressure to be noticed. Loudness in decibels is a logarithmic scale relative to a reference. Speech, singing and instrument making all revolve around timbre and frequency response; hearing aids, headphone tuning and room audio design compensate or shape that spectrum.[3][5]

边界：音色是谐波频谱与时间包络的综合，不能只靠“声音好听”等主观词概括。骨传导与空气传导的差别是路径不同，不是声音本身改变；录音失真还会叠加设备频响限制。海螺声来自环境噪声被共振筛选，不是壳体发出新的声源。把个人偏好当成本质音色差异，会混淆物理频谱与主观喜好。The boundary: timbre combines harmonic spectrum and time envelope, and cannot be reduced to subjective words like 'nice'. Bone versus air conduction differ in path, not in the sound itself; recording distortion also adds device frequency limits. The shell sound is ambient noise filtered by resonance, not a new source inside. Treating personal preference as an essential timbre difference mixes physical spectrum with subjective taste.[2][5]

05为什么多普勒频移频率才是判断来向的关键？救护车驶过身边声音为什么先高后低？Why is Doppler frequency shift the key to telling direction, and why does an ambulance siren rise then fall as it passes?

相对运动使波长压缩或拉长，频率升降；f′=f(v±vo)/(v∓vs)。响度只反映距离，频移才指示接近或远离。Relative motion compresses or stretches wavelength and shifts frequency; f′=f(v±vo)/(v∓vs). Loudness reflects distance only, shift shows approach or recession.

多普勒效应是声源或听者相对运动时听到的频率变化。声源朝向听者运动，波被挤向前进方向，相邻波峰间距缩短，听到的频率升高、音调变尖；声源远离听者，波被拉开，波长变长，频率降低、音调变低。救护车鸣笛从身边驶过，靠近时声音偏高，擦过后迅速变低，就是最常见的例子。频率变化量由相对速度决定，速度越快，频移越明显。The Doppler effect is the frequency shift heard when source or listener moves relative to each other. Moving toward the listener, the source crowds waves ahead, shortens the spacing between crests, and raises the heard frequency to a sharper pitch; moving away, waves stretch, wavelength grows and frequency drops. An ambulance siren passing by sounds higher while approaching and drops just after it passes — the commonest example. The shift size is set by relative speed: faster motion means a larger shift.[6]

定量关系分声源运动和听者运动。空气中声速 v，声源速度 vs，听者速度 vo，听者听到的频率 f′=f·(v±vo)/(v∓vs)：听者朝向声源取加号，远离取减号；声源朝向听者分母减号，远离分母加号。当相对速度远小于声速时，频移近似与接近或远离的速度成正比。雷达测速、天体光谱红移和医学超声血流测量，都利用同一类频率偏移原理。The quantitative relation separates source and listener motion. With air sound speed v, source speed vs and listener speed vo, the heard frequency is f′=f·(v±vo)/(v∓vs): plus when the listener approaches, minus when recedes; minus in the denominator when the source approaches, plus when it recedes. Well below sound speed the shift is roughly proportional to approach or recede speed. Radar speed traps, cosmological redshift and medical ultrasound blood-flow measurement all use the same class of frequency offset.[2][6]

多普勒频移用频率而非声强来判断运动方向。声音变响可能只是距离变近，响度随距离衰减；只有频率升高或降低，才说明声源在接近或远离。混合车流中不同车辆的鸣笛按各自速度产生不同频移，静止听者可以分辨哪辆车在靠近、哪辆在离开。判断“来向”要把频率变化和响度变化分开，避免把距离变化误当成速度方向。Doppler reads motion direction from frequency, not loudness. Louder may only mean closer, since loudness falls with distance; only rising or falling frequency shows approach or recession. Among mixed traffic, each siren shifts by its own speed, so a stationary listener can tell which vehicle approaches and which leaves. Reading direction needs frequency change separated from loudness change, so distance change is not mistaken for velocity direction.[3][6]

声波与光波都有多普勒效应，机制类比但公式尺度不同。光波不需要介质，红移蓝移由相对速度和相对论修正给出；声波依赖介质，公式中声速相对空气参考系。超声波检测血流时，反射波经历声源与反射体两次运动，频移正比于血流速度，据此可测量血管内流速。日常经验里，火车进站、汽车鸣笛和风吹旗帜的声音变化，都可对照频移方向分析。Both sound and light show Doppler effect, analogous but on different formulas. Light needs no medium, and redshift or blueshift follows relative speed with relativistic correction; sound depends on a medium, and its formula references the air frame. In ultrasound blood-flow measurement the reflected wave undergoes motion at source and reflector twice, and shift is proportional to flow speed to gauge vessel velocity. Trains arriving, car horns and flag sounds in wind can all be checked against shift direction in everyday experience.[2][6]

边界：多普勒效应要求声源与听者之间有相对运动；两者同速同向、距离不变时，频率不发生偏移。高速运动接近或超过声速时公式不再适用，会出现激波和音爆。频移方向取决于相对速度分量，横向运动只产生很小的二阶效应。把所有“声音变了”都归为多普勒，会漏掉反射、混响和环境吸收造成的音色变化。The boundary: Doppler requires relative motion; source and listener moving at the same speed in the same direction keep distance and produce no shift. Near or above sound speed the formula breaks down and shock waves and sonic booms appear. Shift direction follows the radial velocity component, and transverse motion gives only a small second-order effect. Calling every changed sound Doppler misses timbre changes from reflection, reverberation and absorption.[6]

06嗓子为什么会变沙哑？大喊之后声音为什么变粗？Why does a voice go hoarse, and why does shouting make it rougher?

气流吹动声带黏膜波振动发声；用声过度致黏膜水肿、分泌物增多，振动不对称，基频抖动、音色沙哑。Airflow drives the vocal-fold mucosal wave; overuse causes edema and mucus, asymmetric vibration, jittery fundamental and a rough timbre.

嗓音来自声带的周期性振动。声带位于喉部、气管上端，左右各一片，平静呼吸时分开，发声时靠拢闭合。肺呼出气流经过声门，两侧声带在气流冲击和喉部肌肉张力控制下周期性开合，黏膜表面形成波浪式振动，把连续气流切成断续的压力脉冲；脉冲再经咽、鼻腔和口腔这些共鸣腔放大、滤波，成为听到的嗓音。成年男声基频约 100–150 Hz，女声约 180–250 Hz，儿童更高；基频决定音高，谐波结构和共鸣决定音色。The voice comes from periodic vibration of the vocal folds. The folds sit at the top of the trachea in the throat, one on each side, apart during quiet breathing and brought together for phonation. Air exhaled from the lungs passes the glottis, and the two folds open and close cyclically under airflow and laryngeal muscle tension; a mucosal wave rolls over their surface, chopping the steady airflow into intermittent pressure pulses. The pulses are then amplified and filtered by the pharynx, nasal cavity and mouth, the resonance chambers, into the voice heard. Adult male fundamental frequency is about 100–150 Hz, female about 180–250 Hz, higher in children; the fundamental sets pitch, while harmonic structure and resonance set timbre.[1][2]

沙哑的直接原因是声带振动不对称。用声过度后声带黏膜充血、水肿，表面还可能附着分泌物；两侧声带的厚度、质量和张力变得不一致，闭合不全，振动不同步。黏膜波变得不规则，基频出现细微抖动，幅度也起伏不定，气流从闭合缝隙漏出，音色就变粗、漏气、发沙。Hoarseness directly comes from asymmetric fold vibration. After overuse the fold mucosa is congested and swollen, with secretions on the surface; thickness, mass and tension differ between the two sides, closure is incomplete, and vibration falls out of step. The mucosal wave turns irregular, the fundamental jitters slightly and amplitude fluctuates, air leaks through the gap, and the tone grows rough, breathy and hoarse.[1][2]

常见诱因与恢复。长时间大喊、说话过多、熬夜、吸烟、干燥空气和胃酸反流刺激，都会加重黏膜肿胀；频繁清嗓和用力咳嗽会反复撞击声带，同样加重水肿。声带休息、补水、避开刺激后，肿胀通常逐渐消退，声音自行恢复。若持续沙哑超过两三周，或伴吞咽困难、声音嘶哑进行性加重、痰中带血，需要就医检查声带，排除小结、息肉或其他病变。Common triggers and recovery. Long shouting, excessive talking, staying up late, smoking, dry air and acid reflux all worsen mucosal swelling; frequent throat clearing and forceful coughing repeatedly strike the folds and add to the edema. With vocal rest, hydration and avoiding irritants, the swelling usually subsides and the voice returns on its own. Hoarseness lasting more than two or three weeks, or paired with swallowing difficulty, progressive loss of voice or blood-streaked sputum, needs an ENT examination to rule out nodules, polyps or other lesions.[1][4]

共鸣腔改变听感。咽喉、鼻腔和口腔是嗓音的共鸣腔，黏膜肿胀、鼻塞或咽部炎症会改变共振峰位置，使声音发闷、发暗。人耳通过基频、谐波和噪声成分判断音色；沙哑时不规则振动混入大量非周期噪声，听感上就是粗糙、有颗粒感，而不只是音高变低。Resonance chambers change the heard timbre. Throat, nose and mouth are the voice's resonance chambers; mucosal swelling, nasal blockage or pharyngeal inflammation shifts the formants and makes the voice muffled and dark. The ear judges timbre from fundamental, harmonics and noise content; in hoarseness, irregular vibration mixes in much non-periodic noise, which reads as roughness and grain rather than merely a lower pitch.[4][5]

边界：本章讲声带振动与沙哑的物理机制，不替代喉部诊断。一过性沙哑多与用声过度、干燥和刺激有关；持续或反复沙哑要由医生检查声带。把沙哑都归为上火、只靠润喉糖处理，可能延误排查。声音保健的核心是避免用声过度、保持湿润和减少刺激，而不是在肿胀期强行练声。Boundary: this chapter explains the physics of fold vibration and hoarseness, not a substitute for laryngeal diagnosis. Transient hoarseness usually follows overuse, dryness and irritation; persistent or recurrent hoarseness needs a clinician to inspect the folds. Blaming hoarseness on internal heat and relying only on lozenges can delay proper examination. Voice care rests on avoiding overuse, staying hydrated and cutting irritants, not forcing practice while the folds are swollen.[1][5]
