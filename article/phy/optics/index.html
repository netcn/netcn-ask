镜子、透镜与成像Mirrors, Lenses and Imaging

镜子为什么左右颠倒？为什么照镜子能看到自己，镜面又为什么发亮？这篇 Hub 用反射、虚像、透镜像差和眼睛—相机类比，拆解五个常见问题，并区分理想光学模型与真实镜片、眼睛的边界。Why does a mirror seem to reverse left and right? Why can you see yourself in it, and why does it look silver? This Hub uses reflection, virtual images, lens aberrations and the eye–camera analogy to answer five common questions and to separate ideal models from real lenses and eyes.

01镜子为什么左右颠倒，却不让上下颠倒？Why does a mirror seem to reverse left and right but not up and down?

镜子只反转前后，不反转左右；“左右颠倒”是我们想象转身造成的错觉，银亮来自高反射金属膜。A mirror reverses front–back, not left–right; the “left–right swap” comes from imagining a turn, and the silvery look comes from a highly reflective metal film.

镜子并没有把左右交换。平面镜只把垂直于镜面的前后方向翻过来；上下和左右两个方向都保持不变。你面对镜子举起右手，镜中像的右手仍在同一侧；你抬头，像也抬头。我们之所以觉得“左右颠倒”，是因为习惯把镜中像想象成一个转过身来的人，再拿他的左右手和自己比较。这个动作在想象里转了 180°，才制造出“左右交换”的错觉；这也解释了为什么镜中文字会反着读。A mirror does not swap left and right. A plane mirror reverses the component of position perpendicular to its surface — the front–back direction — while leaving left–right and up–down unchanged. If you raise your right hand, the image's right hand stays on the same side; if you look up, the image looks up too. The impression of a left–right swap comes from imagining the image as a person who has turned around to face you, and then comparing that imaginary person's hands with yours. That mental 180° rotation creates the illusion; it also explains why text looks reversed in a mirror.[1]

用几何语言说，把镜面当作 x-y 平面，反射把 z 分量变号：z→−z，x、y 不变。物点和像点对称于镜面，像在镜后等距处；你离镜子一米，像也在一米后面。眼睛把反射光线反向延长，觉得光从镜后发出，于是看到一个虚像。镜子不复制你，它只是改变光线的方向；虚像也不能放在屏幕上承接。In geometric terms, treat the mirror as the x–y plane; reflection changes the sign of z: z→−z, while x and y stay the same. Object and image are symmetric about the mirror, and the image lies the same distance behind it: if you stand one metre in front, the virtual image is one metre behind. The eye extends the reflected rays backwards and perceives light coming from behind the mirror, so we see a virtual image. The mirror does not copy you; it only redirects light, and a virtual image cannot be caught on a screen.[1][2]

为什么照镜子能看到自己？光照到脸上发生漫反射，脸上每一点都把光向各个方向散射；其中一部分射到镜面，按反射定律返回眼睛。平滑镜面让这些光线保持彼此的位置关系，所以眼睛能重建出清晰的像；粗糙表面把光线打散，只剩模糊的光斑。浴室镜起雾时看不清，就是因为水珠把规则反射变成了漫反射。Why can you see yourself? Light diffusely reflected from your face leaves each point in many directions; some of it reaches the mirror and returns to your eye according to the law of reflection. A smooth mirror preserves the spatial relationships among these rays, so the eye can reconstruct a sharp image; a rough surface scatters them into a blur. A bathroom mirror fogged with water droplets stops working for the same reason: the droplets turn specular reflection into diffuse scattering.[1][2]

镜面看起来银亮，不是涂了银色颜料，而是玻璃背面镀了一层高反射金属膜，常见是银或铝，再加保护漆。金属里的自由电子把可见光大部分反射回去，且对不同波长反射率比较平；玻璃只是提供平整表面和保护。背面镜会有玻璃前表面的一次微弱反射，形成重影，望远镜等精密系统常用前表面镜避免这一点。银膜容易氧化，所以保护层和封装同样重要。The mirror looks silvery not because it is painted silver, but because a thin, highly reflective metal film — usually silver or aluminium — is deposited behind glass and protected with paint. Free electrons in the metal reflect most visible light and do so fairly evenly across wavelengths; the glass provides a smooth surface and protection. A back-surface mirror also has a weak reflection from the front glass surface, which can produce a ghost image; precision systems such as telescopes often use front-surface mirrors to avoid it. Silver can oxidise, so the protective coating and sealing matter too.[1][3]

边界：镜子不会把“左”变成“右”，也不会让上下颠倒；真正被反转的是前后。曲面镜、多个镜子、半透半反镜会改变像的大小、位置和方向，不能套用平面镜结论。把“镜子左右颠倒”当口头说法可以，但不能当物理定律；判断镜中关系时，先问“哪条轴垂直于镜面”。The boundary: a mirror does not turn left into right, and does not turn the world upside down; what it reverses is front and back. Curved mirrors, multiple mirrors and beam splitters can change the size, position and orientation of an image, so plane-mirror rules do not automatically apply. "The mirror reverses left and right" is acceptable as everyday shorthand, but not as a law of physics; when in doubt, ask which axis is perpendicular to the mirror.[1][2]

02能不能造出完美的光学透镜？Can we make a perfect optical lens?

不能。实际透镜受球差、色差、衍射和制造公差限制；只能在指定波段、孔径和视场里逼近衍射极限。No. Real lenses are limited by spherical and chromatic aberration, diffraction and manufacturing tolerances; they can only approach the diffraction limit over a specified band, aperture

严格意义上的完美透镜，是在所有波长、所有孔径、所有视场下都无像差、100% 透光、加工零误差。这样的透镜不存在。理想薄透镜只是几何光学的近似；真实镜片由球面或非球面、不同玻璃和镀膜组成，必须在多项指标之间折中。所谓“好镜头”，是在指定用途下把关键误差压到足够小；它要求的是理想化的极限，而不是现实中的工程目标。A perfect lens, in the strict sense, would have zero aberrations at every wavelength, every aperture and every field angle, transmit 100% of the light and be manufactured with zero error. No such lens exists. The ideal thin lens is a geometrical approximation; a real lens is built from spherical or aspherical surfaces, different glasses and coatings, and it must trade one property against another. A "good lens" is one that keeps the important errors small enough for its intended use; it aims at an idealised limit, not at literal perfection.[1][3]

几何像差来自折射面。球面镜片边缘和中心对平行光的聚焦不同，形成球差；斜入射时出现彗差、像散和场曲；不同视场的放大率不一致造成畸变。把这些像差压到很小，需要多片镜片组合、非球面和精确装配，但很难在同一时间对所有视场都做到零。镜头设计因此更像“分配误差”，而不是“消灭误差”；球差在宽孔径下最明显，缩小光圈可以减轻，但会牺牲进光量。Geometrical aberrations arise at refracting surfaces. In a spherical lens, rays at the edge and near the axis do not focus at the same point — spherical aberration. Oblique rays bring coma, astigmatism and field curvature; a magnification that varies across the field produces distortion. Suppressing these errors requires multiple elements, aspherical surfaces and precise assembly, but it is very difficult to make all of them zero across the whole field at once. Lens design is therefore more about distributing error than eliminating it; spherical aberration is most obvious at wide apertures, and stopping down reduces it at the cost of light.[1][2][3]

颜色也会分开。玻璃折射率随波长变化，蓝光通常比红光偏折更强，于是同一物点在不同颜色上聚焦在不同位置，这就是色差。双合透镜用两种色散不同的玻璃互相补偿，可把两种波长拉回同一焦点；复消色差通常能校正三种波长，但整个可见光谱仍无法完全重合。镀膜能减少反射、提高透过率，却不能把色差本身消掉。Colour also separates. The refractive index of glass varies with wavelength; blue light is usually bent more strongly than red, so a single object point focuses at different places for different colours — chromatic aberration. A doublet uses two glasses with different dispersion to compensate, bringing two wavelengths back to a common focus; an apochromat can correct around three. The whole visible spectrum still does not coincide. Coatings can reduce reflections and raise transmission, but they do not remove chromatic aberration itself.[1][3]

即使几何上做到完美，光还会衍射。圆孔把点光源变成艾里斑，两个点靠得太近就无法分辨；分辨率大约受 θ≈1.22λ/D 限制，D 是孔径。增大口径能提高理论分辨率，但大气湍流、材料吸收、杂散光和制造公差会接管限制。天文望远镜要自适应光学，显微镜要用油浸或短波长，都是在绕开这些限制。Even a geometrically perfect lens is limited by diffraction. A circular aperture turns a point source into an Airy pattern, and two points that are too close cannot be resolved; the resolution is roughly limited by θ≈1.22λ/D, where D is the aperture diameter. A larger aperture improves the theoretical resolution, but atmospheric turbulence, material absorption, stray light and manufacturing tolerances take over. Adaptive optics for telescopes, oil immersion or shorter wavelengths for microscopes — these are ways of working around the limits.[1][4]

所以“完美”只能相对于用途定义：在指定波段、孔径和视场里接近衍射极限，已经是很优秀的镜头。手机镜头、显微镜、望远镜和光刻镜头各自优化不同目标，没有一枚镜头能同时满足所有场景。工程上更实际的目标是“在预算内足够好”，而不是追求字面上的完美；这也是为什么复杂镜头往往又大又重。So "perfect" can only be defined relative to a purpose: a lens that approaches the diffraction limit over a specified band, aperture and field is already excellent. Phone lenses, microscope objectives, telescope optics and lithography lenses optimise different things; no single lens satisfies every scenario. The practical engineering goal is "good enough within budget", not literal perfection — which is also why complex lenses tend to be large and heavy.[2][3]

03为什么说眼睛就像照相机？Why is the eye like a camera?

眼睛和相机都用凸透镜把光聚焦到感光面；但眼睛能自动调焦、适应亮度，并由大脑校正。Both eye and camera use a convex lens to focus light onto a photosensitive surface, but the eye focuses and adapts automatically and relies on the brain.

眼睛和相机最明显的共同点，是用一个凸透镜系统把外界光线聚焦到感光面。角膜和晶状体相当于镜头组，虹膜和瞳孔相当于光圈，视网膜相当于传感器。光线在视网膜上形成倒立、左右反转的实像；我们之所以看到正立的世界，是大脑在解释，而不是视网膜上的像本来就是正的。角膜像一块固定镜头，晶状体像可调焦镜片，两者配合。The most obvious similarity between an eye and a camera is that both use a convex lens system to focus light onto a photosensitive surface. The cornea and crystalline lens act as the lens group, the iris and pupil as the aperture, and the retina as the sensor. Light forms an inverted, left–right reversed real image on the retina; we see the world upright because the brain interprets the image, not because the retinal image is already upright. The cornea is like a fixed lens and the crystalline lens like a focusable element, working together.[2][5][6]

聚焦方式不同。相机动镜头组或改变镜片间距；眼睛的角膜提供大部分屈光力，晶状体通过睫状肌改变形状，完成调节。年轻时看近看远都能对焦，年龄增长后晶状体变硬，调节幅度下降，出现老花。近视、远视和散光则是眼球长度、角膜曲率或镜片形状不匹配，和相机“对焦不准”有相似之处，但矫正方法不同；角膜的屈光力约 40 多屈光度，晶状体只做精细调节。Focusing works differently. A camera moves its lens group or changes the spacing between elements; in the eye, the cornea supplies most of the optical power and the ciliary muscle changes the shape of the crystalline lens to fine-tune focus. A young eye can focus near and far; with age the lens stiffens, the amplitude of accommodation falls and presbyopia appears. Myopia, hyperopia and astigmatism come from a mismatch of eye length, corneal curvature or lens shape — somewhat like a camera that is out of focus, but the correction is different; the cornea contributes more than 40 dioptres, while the crystalline lens makes fine adjustments.[5][6]

曝光控制也不同。瞳孔可在约 2–8 毫米范围变化，像相机的光圈；视网膜的感光细胞和神经适应又像自动增益，但眼睛没有相机的固定 ISO。明暗变化时，瞳孔和视网膜适应共同作用；相机的快门可以冻结高速运动，眼睛则靠扫视和大脑整合连续画面。强光下瞳孔缩小，暗处放大，这是最直观的“光圈”类比。Exposure control also differs. The pupil can change over roughly 2–8 mm, acting like a camera aperture; retinal photoreceptors and neural adaptation add something like automatic gain, but the eye has no fixed ISO. In changing light, pupil size and retinal adaptation work together; a camera shutter can freeze fast motion, whereas the eye relies on saccades and the brain integrating a continuous stream. In bright light the pupil constricts and in darkness it dilates — the most intuitive "aperture" analogy.[5][6]

眼睛不是单张照片。两只眼睛有视差，大脑融合成立体感；眼球不停扫视，把不同位置的细节拼起来；盲点被大脑填补。相机可以换镜头、换传感器、长时间曝光，眼睛不能；但眼睛有自适应、色彩恒常和快速目标追踪。相机拍到的是一帧帧图像，眼睛得到的是持续更新的知觉；视野边缘不如中心清晰，也靠大脑和扫视补足。An eye is not a single photograph. Two eyes provide disparity, which the brain fuses into depth; the eyes keep scanning, and the brain stitches details from different positions together; the blind spot is filled in. A camera can change lenses, change sensors or expose for a long time; the eye cannot. But the eye has adaptation, colour constancy and fast target tracking. A camera records frames; the eye receives a continuously updated percept, and peripheral vision is less sharp than central vision, with the brain and scanning making up the difference.[5][6]

边界：把眼睛说成照相机是很好的入门类比，但不能字面等同。近视、远视、散光、老花是屈光或调节问题，矫正需要验光；持续视力变化、视野缺损、闪光感或突然看不清，应及时就医。眼睛的“自动对焦”也不是无限的，长时间近距离用眼会疲劳；验光配镜是矫正屈光，不是治疗所有眼病。The boundary: calling the eye a camera is a useful introduction, but not a literal equivalence. Myopia, hyperopia, astigmatism and presbyopia are refractive or accommodative problems, and correction requires a proper eye examination; persistent changes in vision, field loss, flashes or sudden blurring need medical attention. The eye's "autofocus" is not unlimited either, and prolonged close work causes fatigue; glasses correct refraction, not every eye disease.[5][6]
