01为什么有质量的物体不能达到或超过光速？Why can a massive object not reach or exceed the speed of light?

γ=1/√(1−v²/c²)，接近 c 时能量动量趋于无穷；光子无静止质量才以 c 运动，空间膨胀的视退行不算超光速。γ=1/√(1−v²/c²), so energy and momentum diverge near c; only massless photons travel at c, and expanding-space recession is not faster-than-light motion.

真空光速是一个确定的常数：c=299,792,458 m/s。狭义相对论的两条公设是：物理定律在所有惯性系中等价；真空光速对所有观察者都相同，与光源和观察者的相对运动无关。这意味着不存在“追上光”的惯性系，光速不是普通速度可以叠加的对象。在日常速度下伽利略速度合成近似成立，速度接近光速时必须改用相对论速度合成。The speed of light in vacuum is a fixed constant, c=299,792,458 m/s. Special relativity rests on two postulates: the laws of physics are the same in every inertial frame, and the speed of light in vacuum is the same for all observers, independent of the motion of source or observer. There is no inertial frame that catches up to light, and light speed is not an ordinary velocity to be added by Galilean composition. At everyday speeds Galilean addition works approximately; near light speed the relativistic velocity composition must be used.[1][2]

有质量物体不能达到光速，原因在相对论能量与动量。相对论因子 γ=1/√(1−v²/c²)，相对论动量 p=γmv，总能量 E=γmc²。速度增大时 γ 增长很慢，接近 c 时却急剧发散：v=0.5c 时 γ≈1.15，v=0.9c 时 γ≈2.29，v→c 时 γ→∞。继续加速所需的能量和动量趋于无穷大，而宇宙中没有无穷能源，因此有静止质量的物体只能无限接近 c，不能达到或越过。A massive object cannot reach light speed, as relativistic energy and momentum show. The Lorentz factor is γ=1/√(1−v²/c²), relativistic momentum p=γmv and total energy E=γmc². At low speed γ grows slowly, but it diverges sharply near c: at 0.5c γ≈1.15, at 0.9c γ≈2.29, and as v→c, γ→∞. The energy and momentum needed to keep accelerating tend to infinity, and no source supplies infinite energy, so a body with rest mass can only approach c asymptotically, never reach or cross it.[1][2]

光子没有静止质量，从产生起就以 c 运动；所有无静止质量的粒子在真空中都以光速传播。相对论约束的是物质、能量和信息的传递速度：一个局域信号不能超过 c。时间膨胀和长度收缩是同一套变换的推论：高速运动的钟变慢，沿运动方向的长度缩短，这些效应在粒子加速器和卫星导航修正中被反复验证。Photons have no rest mass and travel at c from creation; every particle without rest mass moves at light speed in vacuum. What relativity constrains is the speed at which matter, energy and information are transmitted: a local signal cannot exceed c. Time dilation and length contraction follow from the same transformation: a moving clock runs slow and a length along the motion shortens, effects checked repeatedly in particle accelerators and in satellite navigation corrections.[1][2]

遥远星系的退行速度可以超过 c，这并不违反相对论。星系被空间本身的膨胀带着彼此远离，并非在空间中做超光速运动；在每个星系附近的局域空间里，它相对邻近物质的速度远低于 c。可观测宇宙的边界来自光在宇宙年龄内能够传播的距离，而不是某个物体突破了光速上限。把“空间膨胀的视退行”和“物体在空间中的运动”混在一起，是超光速误解的主要来源。Distant galaxies can recede faster than c without violating relativity. Space itself expands and carries galaxies apart; they are not moving through space faster than light, and in their local neighborhood each galaxy moves well below c relative to nearby matter. The observable universe ends where light could not travel across the age of the universe, not because something broke the speed limit. Conflating apparent recession from expanding space with motion through space is the main source of faster-than-light misconceptions.[1][2]

边界：相对论禁止的是真空里局域信息超光速，不禁止相速度、波包表观速度或空间膨胀带来的视退行。光在介质中的相速度可以小于 c，带电粒子在介质中超过该介质光速时会产生切伦科夫辐射，这与真空光速上限不矛盾。接近光速时惯性增大表现为加速越来越困难，不能简单说成“质量随速度变大”，现代表述把质量保留为静止不变量。Boundary: relativity forbids local information transfer above c in vacuum, not phase velocity, apparent wave speed or the apparent recession from expanding space. Light's phase speed in a medium can be below c, and a charged particle outrunning that medium's light speed emits Cherenkov radiation, which does not contradict the vacuum limit. Growing inertia near c means acceleration gets harder, not that "mass grows with speed"; the modern treatment keeps mass as the invariant rest quantity.[3]

02量子比特和普通电脑的比特有什么不一样？量子计算机现在已经全面更快了吗？How does a qubit differ from a classical bit, and are quantum computers already faster overall?

量子比特可用复振幅叠加，n 比特有 2ⁿ 个振幅；叠加纠缠带来特定问题优势，但退相干与纠错限制下通用优势尚未实现。A qubit uses amplitude superposition; n qubits carry 2ⁿ amplitudes, giving advantage on structured problems, but decoherence and error correction keep general advantage out of reach.

经典计算机的比特在任一时刻只能取 0 或 1 一个确定值。量子比特可以处于两个基态的叠加态：|ψ⟩=α|0⟩+β|1⟩，其中 α、β 是复振幅，满足 |α|²+|β|²=1。测量时叠加态坍缩到 0 或 1，得到 0 的概率是 |α|²，得到 1 的概率是 |β|²；测量之前不能说它“已经是 0 或已经是 1”。叠加是复振幅的线性组合，不是两个经典状态同时占着。A classical bit holds exactly one definite value, 0 or 1, at any moment. A qubit can sit in a superposition of the two basis states, |ψ⟩=α|0⟩+β|1⟩, where α and β are complex amplitudes with |α|²+|β|²=1. On measurement the state collapses to 0 or 1: the probability of 0 is |α|² and of 1 is |β|². Before measurement it cannot be said to be already 0 or already 1. Superposition is a linear combination of complex amplitudes, not two classical values occupying the state at once.[3][4]

n 个量子比特组成的系统，状态由 2ⁿ 个复振幅同时描述；经典 n 比特只有 2ⁿ 个可能状态之一。纠缠进一步把多比特态绑定：处于纠缠的两个比特不能各自写成独立的单比特态，测量一个就立即确定另一个的测量结果。这种整体表示是量子计算在特定问题上获得指数级表示空间的基础，但读出仍受单次测量概率分布的限制。An n-qubit system is described by 2ⁿ complex amplitudes at once, while a classical n-bit string holds just one of 2ⁿ states. Entanglement further binds multi-qubit states: two entangled qubits cannot be written as independent single-qubit states, and measuring one fixes the measurement outcome of the other. This holistic representation underlies the exponential state space quantum computing exploits on certain problems, although readout is still limited by the probability distribution of a single measurement.[3][4]

算法层面，肖尔 1994 年提出利用量子叠加和纠缠对大数做素因子分解，在经典上困难的问题上显示出量子优势的理论路径；格罗弗算法对无序数据库提供平方级搜索加速。这些优势针对具有特定数学结构的问题，并不是所有任务都能指数提速。通用容错量子计算至今尚未实现，现有设备仍处在含噪声中等规模量子（NISQ）阶段。At the algorithm level, Shor's 1994 factoring algorithm uses superposition and entanglement to factor large numbers, showing a theoretical path to quantum advantage on classically hard problems; Grover's algorithm gives a quadratic speed-up for unordered search. These advantages target specific mathematical structure; not every task speeds up exponentially. General fault-tolerant quantum computing has not been achieved, and present devices remain in the noisy intermediate-scale (NISQ) regime.[3][4]

叠加态非常脆弱：量子比特与环境的任何耦合都会引起退相干，使振幅相位随机化、叠加逐渐衰减为经典混合态。超导等路线把器件冷却到毫开尔文量级以降低热扰动，并需要量子纠错来对抗门错误和环境噪声。当前可实现的比特数量有限、错误率偏高，量子计算机还没有在实用规模的通用问题上全面超越经典超级计算机。Superposition is fragile: any coupling to the environment causes decoherence, randomizing amplitude and phase and decaying the superposition into a classical mixture. Superconducting routes cool devices to millikelvin levels to suppress thermal disturbance, and quantum error correction is needed to counter gate errors and environmental noise. Achievable qubit counts remain limited and error rates relatively high, so quantum computers have not yet beaten classical supercomputers on practical-scale general problems.[3][4]

边界：叠加不等于“同时跑两个经典答案”，纠缠也不能用来超光速传递信息——关联的两端需要经典通信比对结果。量子计算机是特定结构问题的加速器，不替代经典电脑；密码学、化学模拟、优化等方向是预期受益领域，但工程成熟度仍取决于纠错与退相干控制。Boundary: superposition does not mean "running two classical answers at once", and entanglement cannot transmit information faster than light; correlated ends must compare results over a classical channel. A quantum computer is an accelerator for structured problems, not a replacement for classical computers. Cryptography, chemical simulation and optimization are expected beneficiaries, but engineering maturity still depends on error correction and decoherence control.[5]

03电子为什么不能有任意能量？什么是量子隧穿，概率由什么决定？Why can an electron not take arbitrary energies, and what is quantum tunnelling, governed by what?

氢原子能级 E_n=−13.6/n² eV 离散；隧穿概率 T≈e^(−2κL)，随势垒厚度、高度和质量指数下降。Hydrogen levels E_n=−13.6/n² eV are discrete, differences matching photons; tunnelling T≈e^(−2κL) falls exponentially with barrier thickness, height and particle mass.

氢原子的电子只能取一系列离散能级，能量由主量子数 n 决定：E_n=−13.6/n² eV。基态 n=1 对应 −13.6 eV；n=2 约 −3.4 eV，n=3 约 −1.51 eV，n 越大能级越密，n→∞ 时能量趋于 0。电子不能取这些值之间的连续能量，这就是能量量子化：原子光谱是线状而不是连续带。The electron in hydrogen can occupy only a set of discrete levels, given by the principal quantum number n: E_n=−13.6/n² eV. The ground state n=1 is −13.6 eV; n=2 about −3.4 eV, n=3 about −1.51 eV; levels grow denser as n rises and approach 0 as n→∞. The electron cannot take continuous energies between these values, which is energy quantization: atomic spectra are lines, not continuous bands.[5][6]

能级差对应光子的发射与吸收。电子从高能级落到低能级时放出光子，能量 ΔE=hf=hc/λ；反过来吸收一个能量恰好等于能级差的光子才能跃迁到高能级。不同元素的能级结构不同，谱线位置成为识别元素的“指纹”。玻尔模型先给出氢原子能级的正确数值，量子力学再以波函数和概率密度给出更一般的描述。Level differences map to photon emission and absorption. An electron dropping from a higher to a lower level emits a photon with ΔE=hf=hc/λ; conversely, it absorbs a photon whose energy matches the level difference to climb up. Elements differ in level structure, so line positions act as element fingerprints. The Bohr model first gave the correct hydrogen energies, and quantum mechanics later provided the more general description through wave functions and probability density.[5][6]

量子隧穿是经典与量子行为的分水岭。经典粒子能量低于势垒高度 V 时会被弹回；量子粒子的波函数不会在势垒壁处突然归零，而是进入势垒并按 ψ∝e^(−κx) 指数衰减，κ=√(2m(V−E))/ℏ。若势垒有一定厚度 L，穿出概率 T≈e^(−2κL)，随势垒厚度和高度指数下降。势垒越薄、越矮，粒子质量越小，隧穿概率越大。Quantum tunnelling divides classical from quantum behaviour. A classical particle with energy below barrier height V bounces back; a quantum particle's wave function does not vanish abruptly at the barrier wall but enters it and decays as ψ∝e^(−κx), κ=√(2m(V−E))/ℏ. Across a barrier of thickness L, the transmission probability T≈e^(−2κL), falling exponentially with barrier thickness and height. Thinner, shorter barriers and lighter particles raise the tunnelling probability.[5][6]

隧穿有可观测的实例。α 衰变中，α 粒子靠隧穿穿过核表面的库仑势垒逸出；扫描隧穿显微镜利用电子在针尖与样品之间的隧穿电流探测表面原子级起伏，电流对间隙高度极其敏感。电子质量小，隧穿相对容易；质子、重离子质量大，同样势垒下隧穿概率低得多。Tunnelling has observable instances. In alpha decay the alpha particle tunnels through the Coulomb barrier at the nuclear surface to escape; the scanning tunnelling microscope measures atomic-scale surface relief from the tunnelling current between tip and sample, a current extremely sensitive to gap height. Electrons are light and tunnel relatively easily; protons and heavier ions tunnel far less through the same barrier.[5][6]

边界：隧穿概率很小但不为零，它不违反能量守恒，穿出粒子的能量仍由初态决定。宏观物体质量太大，κ 极大，隧穿概率在任何可观测时间内都可忽略。氢原子能级公式只对单电子原子严格成立；多电子原子要考虑电子间屏蔽、库仑排斥和自旋耦合，能级需要更复杂的计算。不同原子的外层电子受屏蔽影响，能级间距与氢原子不同，谱线系也随之变化。Boundary: tunnelling probability is small but nonzero, and it does not violate energy conservation; an escaping particle keeps the initial-state energy. For macroscopic objects κ is so large that tunnelling is negligible over any observable time. The hydrogen level formula is exact only for single-electron atoms; multi-electron atoms need calculations including screening, electron-electron repulsion and spin coupling.[7]

04电子的自旋是像地球自转那样在转吗？Is electron spin like the Earth spinning on its axis?

自旋是 s=1/2 的内禀量子角动量，投影 m_s=±½；按经典小球自转需超光速，不成立，半整数自旋使电子成为费米子。Spin is an intrinsic s=1/2 quantum angular momentum with m_s=±½; a spinning-ball picture needs superluminal speed, and half-integer spin makes the electron a fermion.

电子具有内禀角动量，称为自旋。电子的自旋量子数 s=1/2，自旋角动量大小 S=√(s(s+1))ℏ=√3/2 ℏ；沿任一选定坐标轴的投影只有两个取值，m_s=+½ 或 −½，测量结果是离散的上下两种。自旋是电子的固有属性，和质量、电荷一样属于基本内禀量，不需要借助外部运动来解释。The electron carries an intrinsic angular momentum called spin. Its spin quantum number is s=1/2, and the spin angular momentum magnitude S=√(s(s+1))ℏ=√3/2 ℏ. Along any chosen axis the projection takes only two values, m_s=+½ or −½, and the measurement outcome is discretely up or down. Spin is an intrinsic property of the electron, a basic internal quantity like mass and charge, not explained by external motion.[7][8]

自旋不是经典意义上的自转。如果把电子看成带电小球绕轴旋转，要产生观测到的磁矩，表面切线速度会超过光速，这在物理上不可能。电子也没有可观测的有限大小表面，因此“电子在自转”是错误的图像。自旋带来的角动量和磁矩是量子化的内禀量，方向只能相对测量轴投影，不能按经典刚体处理。Spin is not classical rotation. If the electron were a charged ball spinning on an axis, its surface tangential speed would have to exceed light speed to produce the observed magnetic moment, which is physically impossible. The electron has no observable finite surface, so "the electron is spinning" is the wrong picture. The angular momentum and magnetic moment from spin are quantized intrinsic quantities; their direction is a projection relative to the measurement axis, not a classical rigid-body rotation.[7][8]

1922 年施特恩-格拉赫实验中，银原子束通过非均匀磁场后分裂成两束，而不是形成连续一片，直接证明了角动量投影的量子化。电子自旋磁矩与外磁场相互作用，在不同磁场取向间产生能量差；这一能量差随磁场强度线性变化，是精密测量磁场与频率的基础，磁共振、原子钟等技术都建立在自旋与磁场的相互作用上。In the 1922 Stern-Gerlach experiment a beam of silver atoms split into two beams through a non-uniform magnetic field instead of forming a continuous smear, directly showing that angular momentum projection is quantized. The electron spin magnetic moment interacts with an external field, producing an energy difference between orientations; magnetic resonance and atomic clocks both rely on this spin-field interaction.[7][8]

自旋决定了粒子的统计分类。自旋为半整数（1/2、3/2……）的粒子是费米子，遵守泡利不相容原理：同一个量子态不能容纳两个全同费米子。电子是自旋 1/2 的费米子，不相容原理决定了电子壳层结构和元素周期表。整数自旋的粒子是玻色子，可以聚集在同一量子态，例如光子和玻色-爱因斯坦凝聚体。Spin fixes the particle's statistical class. Particles with half-integer spin (1/2, 3/2...) are fermions obeying the Pauli exclusion principle: one quantum state cannot hold two identical fermions. The electron is a spin-1/2 fermion, and exclusion determines electron shell structure and the periodic table. Particles with integer spin are bosons that can gather in one quantum state, such as photons and a Bose-Einstein condensate.[7][8]

边界：“上自旋/下自旋”是相对测量轴的投影方向，不是电子绕空间某根轴自转。自旋在坐标旋转变换下保持内禀，不会因为转动参考系而消失。原子核也有自旋，这是核磁共振和医学磁共振成像（MRI）的物理基础；核自旋由质子、中子的耦合决定，不等于电子自旋。MRI 正是利用人体氢核在磁场中的能级分裂与射频脉冲响应来成像，自旋状态在测量轴上的投影决定了信号方向。Boundary: "spin up/down" is a projection relative to the measurement axis, not rotation about a spatial axis. Spin stays intrinsic under coordinate rotation and does not disappear when the reference frame turns. Atomic nuclei also have spin, the basis of nuclear magnetic resonance and medical MRI; nuclear spin follows proton-neutron coupling and is not the electron spin.[1]
