01紧急刹车时，为什么站在车厢里的人会向前冲？我们又为什么感觉不到地球在运动？Why do passengers lurch forward when a bus brakes, and why do we not feel the Earth moving?

惯性使物体保持原有速度；车减速而人上身仍按原速前冲，安全带提供减速外力；匀速参考系内部无法判断自身是否在运动。Inertia keeps bodies at their original speed; the bus slows while the torso keeps moving forward, and the belt supplies the braking force; a uniform frame hides its own motion.

惯性是物体保持原有运动状态的倾向：没有外力时，静止的继续静止，做匀速直线运动的继续匀速直线运动。公交车急刹车时车厢减速，乘客的上身因惯性仍保持原来的速度，于是相对车厢向前倾；扶手、安全带和座椅正是用来提供这股把人减速的外力。惯性不是被“甩”出去的力量，而是运动状态来不及改变的表现。Inertia is the tendency of a body to keep its original state of motion: with no external force, a stationary body stays at rest and a body in uniform straight-line motion keeps moving that way. When a bus brakes hard, the carriage slows down while the passenger's torso keeps its original speed, so it lurches forward relative to the floor. Handrails, seatbelts and seats supply the external force that decelerates the person. Inertia is not a force that throws you outward; it is the lag of motion state behind the change.[1]

惯性由质量度量。质量越大，改变速度越难，同样的力产生的加速度越小。平直路面上以约 30 km/h 行驶的车辆制动到停止，若没有扶手和安全带，人体躯干在极短制动时间内仍按原速前冲；制动加速度 a 越大，把人减速所需的制动力 F=ma 也越大。标准重力加速度 g=9.80665 m/s²（NIST CODATA）是衡量下落与制动加速度的基准量。汽车以 60 km/h 行驶时，从踩下刹车到停稳的距离由反应距离与制动距离两段组成；车辆质量越大，同样减速度下需要的制动力越大，制动距离也更长。Inertia is measured by mass. The larger the mass, the harder it is to change speed, and the same force produces a smaller acceleration. A vehicle moving at about 30 km/h on a level road brakes to a stop; without a rail or belt, the torso keeps its original speed during the short braking interval. The larger the braking acceleration a, the larger the force F=ma needed to slow the person down. The standard acceleration of gravity, g=9.80665 m/s² in the NIST CODATA tables, is the reference value for comparing falling and braking accelerations.[1][7]

伽利略的斜面实验和牛顿第一定律确立了惯性概念。日常演示同样直接：桌布被快速抽出而杯盘留在原处，是物体保持静止惯性；汽车起步时人向后仰，是身体保持静止而车厢向前。这些现象里，真正改变运动状态的是摩擦力、座椅力或拉力，惯性本身不提供推力。抽桌布越快、摩擦作用时间越短，杯盘获得的速度增量越小，这正是冲量 FΔt=Δp 在低摩擦、短时间下的结果。Galileo's inclined-plane experiments and Newton's first law established the concept of inertia. Everyday demonstrations work the same way: a tablecloth pulled quickly out from under cups leaves them in place because the dishes keep their rest state; passengers lean back when a car starts because their bodies keep still while the carriage moves forward. In each case the force that changes the motion is friction, a seat force or a pull; inertia itself supplies no push.[1][7]

在匀速运动的车厢内部，无法仅凭车内现象判断车是否在运动。我们感觉不到地球自转，正是因为地面近似一个匀速运动的参考系：自转带来的向心加速度很小，日常物体与地面保持同一速度，相对位置不变。只有在加速、转弯或刹车时，参考系与身体的速度出现差别，惯性才表现为可观察的前倾、后仰或侧倾。Inside a uniformly moving carriage, you cannot tell from local observations whether you are moving. We do not feel the Earth spinning because the ground approximates an inertial frame: the centripetal acceleration from rotation is small, and everyday objects share the ground's speed, so their relative positions stay fixed. Only during acceleration, turning or braking does the frame and the body differ in speed, and inertia appears as a visible lurch forward, backward or sideways.[1]

可测量的量是加速度与力。用制动距离 s 和初速度 v 可估算减速度 a=v²/(2s)；用质量 m 与加速度 a 可算制动力 F=ma。边界要说清：惯性不是一种力，“惯性力”只是在加速参考系里的等效描述；匀速运动时惯性不产生相对运动，只有速度改变时它才显现为前冲或后仰。The measurable quantities are acceleration and force. From braking distance s and initial speed v you can estimate deceleration a=v²/(2s), and from mass m and acceleration a you obtain the braking force F=ma. The boundary: inertia is not a force; an 'inertial force' is only an equivalent description inside an accelerating frame. During uniform motion inertia produces no relative motion; it shows up as lurching only when speed changes.[1][2]

02不计空气阻力时抛体轨迹是什么形状？空气阻力又怎样改变它？高尔夫球为什么要有小坑？What shape is a projectile's trajectory without air drag, how does drag change it, and why do golf balls have dimples?

无阻力时水平匀速、竖直匀加速，轨迹为对称抛物线；阻力与速度反向，射程缩短、轨迹不对称；凹坑促使边界层转捩、减小压差阻力。No drag gives a symmetric parabola: uniform horizontal motion and vertical acceleration; drag shortens range and breaks symmetry; dimples trip the boundary layer to cut pressure drag.

忽略空气阻力时，抛体在水平方向不受力、保持匀速，竖直方向以重力加速度 g 匀加速，两条运动合成一条对称抛物线。出手角相同时，射程由初速度决定；起落点等高、真空条件下，45° 抛射角给出最大射程。地球表面附近 g 取约 9.81 m/s²，水平方向不受力意味着水平速度不随时间改变，竖直位移关于最高点对称。这是理想模型，把空气的作用暂时拿掉，先看清重力单独留下的轨迹。With air drag removed, a projectile has no horizontal force and keeps a uniform horizontal velocity, while gravity accelerates it vertically at g. Combining the two motions gives a symmetric parabola. For a fixed launch speed and equal launch and landing heights, a 45° angle gives the maximum range. This is an ideal model that sets air aside to see the trajectory left by gravity alone.[2]

计入空气阻力后，情况改变。抛体同时受重力 mg 与方向始终与瞬时速度相反的阻力 Fd：低速时阻力近似与速度成正比，高速湍流区近似与速度平方成正比。水平方向没有重力分量，速度因阻力持续衰减，落地前水平速度已明显低于出手速度；上升段与下降段不再对称，轨迹前段陡、后段平，总射程比理想抛物线短。When drag is included, the picture changes. The projectile feels gravity mg and a drag force Fd pointing opposite its instantaneous velocity: at low speed drag is roughly proportional to speed, while in the high-speed turbulent regime it scales with the square of speed. The horizontal direction has no gravity component, so speed keeps decaying under drag, and the horizontal velocity before landing is well below the launch speed. Rising and falling branches are no longer symmetric; the path is steep early and flat late, with a shorter overall range than the ideal parabola.[3]

无阻力轨迹可由 x=v0·cosθ·t、y=v0·sinθ·t−½gt² 求出。实测中高尔夫球表面的凹坑促使边界层提前转捩为湍流，使尾部分离区缩小、压差阻力下降；同尺寸的光滑球在相同初速下射程明显更短。标准大气压下空气密度约 1.2 kg/m³；一颗质量约 46 g 的高尔夫球在 70 m/s 左右的初速下，空气阻力已与重力处于同一量级，不能再当作小修正。凹坑减阻靠的是改变绕流结构，而不是给球额外推力。The drag-free trajectory follows x=v0·cosθ·t and y=v0·sinθ·t−½gt². In measurements, the dimples on a golf ball trip the boundary layer into turbulence early, shrinking the separated wake and lowering pressure drag; a smooth ball of the same size travels noticeably shorter at the same launch speed. Dimples reduce drag by changing the flow structure around the ball, not by adding extra push.[2][3]

45° 最大射程的结论只在真空或阻力可忽略、起落点等高时成立。有风、有升力（回旋镖、羽毛球）或起落点高度不同时，最优角度与轨迹形状都会改变。雨滴、羽毛等轻小物体在空气中很快达到终端速度：重力与阻力平衡后匀速下落，不再继续加速。降落伞靠伞面把迎风面积扩大很多，阻力随面积增大，终端下落速度被压到安全范围，人就能缓慢落地。The 45° maximum-range result holds only in vacuum, or where drag is negligible and launch and landing heights are equal. With wind, lift (boomerangs, shuttlecocks) or a height difference, the optimal angle and trajectory shape both change. Light objects such as raindrops and feathers quickly reach terminal velocity in air: gravity balances drag and they fall at constant speed instead of accelerating further.A parachute greatly enlarges the frontal area, so drag rises with area and the terminal fall speed is held down to a safe, slow descent.[3][4]

可测量的量是阻力系数与射程。阻力系数 Cd 由雷诺数、表面粗糙度和形状共同决定；用实测射程、飞行时间和出手角可以反推实际阻力强弱。高尔夫凹坑是工程上利用边界层转捩减阻的经典例子；把它理解成“凹坑把球推得更远”，则颠倒了因果——凹坑只是减小了空气对球的阻碍。The measurable quantities are drag coefficient and range. The drag coefficient Cd depends on Reynolds number, surface roughness and shape; comparing measured range, flight time and launch angle lets you infer how strong the actual drag is. Golf dimples are a classic engineering use of boundary-layer transition to reduce drag. Reading them as 'dimples push the ball farther' reverses the cause: dimples only weaken the air's resistance.[3]

03为什么旋转的陀螺不会倒？自行车前进时为什么比静止时更容易保持平衡？Why does a spinning top stay upright, and why is a bicycle easier to balance while moving?

角动量 L=Iω 沿自转轴；重力矩改变角动量方向而产生进动，转速越高越稳；自行车稳定还依赖前轮转向几何与重心微调。Angular momentum L=Iω follows the spin axis; gravity torque turns it into precession, so faster spin steadies the top; bicycle balance also needs steering geometry and weight shifts.

高速旋转的物体具有角动量，外力矩改变的是角动量的方向，而不是立刻让它倒下。陀螺倾斜时，重力对接触点产生力矩，使自转轴绕竖直方向进动，表现为轴慢慢画圈，而不是直接翻倒。自行车前进时，车轮的角动量与可转向的前轮几何一起，把车身的稳定姿态维持住。A fast-spinning object carries angular momentum, and an external torque changes the direction of that angular momentum rather than toppling the object at once. When a top tilts, gravity produces a torque about the contact point, so the spin axis precesses around the vertical: the axis traces a slow circle instead of falling straight over. On a moving bicycle, wheel angular momentum together with the steerable front-rod geometry keeps the upright attitude stable.[5]

角动量 L=Iω，方向沿自转轴；转动惯量 I 取决于质量分布，ω 是转速。陀螺倾斜状态下，重力矩 τ=r×mg 垂直于角动量，使 L 的端点沿水平方向转动，这就是进动。转速越高，进动越慢、直立姿态越稳；转速衰减后，力矩才把陀螺拉倒。转动第二定律把力矩写成角动量的变化率：τ=dL/dt。转动惯量对质量分布敏感：质量集中在边缘的圆环比同质量圆盘更难转动，也更难被力矩改变转速。Angular momentum L=Iω points along the spin axis; the moment of inertia I depends on how mass is distributed, and ω is the spin rate. In a tilted top, the gravity torque τ=r×mg is perpendicular to L, so the tip of L rotates horizontally: this is precession. Faster spin means slower precession and a steadier upright attitude; as spin decays, the torque finally pulls the top over. The rotational second law writes torque as the rate of change of angular momentum, τ=dL/dt.[5]

旋转陀螺、翻倒前仍自转的圆盘、行进中稳定而静止时难撑住的自行车，都体现角动量的作用。常见玩具陀螺质量几十克，转速可达每秒数十转；自行车轮直径约 0.6–0.7 m，车速越快车轮角动量越大，对倾倒的抵抗也越强。自行车的稳定还依赖前轮可转向的几何（前叉后倾）和骑车人不断微调重心；车轮陀螺效应是辅助因素，不是唯一原因。静止的自行车没有车轮角动量，平衡只能靠人左右调整重心，很容易倒下。Spinning tops, disks still rotating before they fall, and bicycles stable while moving but hard to hold upright at rest all show angular momentum at work. Bicycle stability also relies on the steerable front fork geometry (rake or trail) and the rider's continuous small weight shifts; wheel gyroscopic effect helps but is not the only cause. A stationary bicycle has no wheel angular momentum, so balance can only come from the rider adjusting the centre of mass, and it tips easily.[4][5]

角动量守恒只在合外力矩为零时严格成立。接触摩擦、空气阻力会慢慢消耗自转；陀螺在完全无摩擦的支点上才会永远保持原轴方向，日常玩具陀螺衰减后必然倒下。高速自旋的物件对倾倒方向有抵抗，但这个抵抗是动态的，转速一低就消失。Angular momentum conservation holds strictly only when the net external torque is zero. Contact friction and air resistance slowly drain the spin; only a top on a perfectly frictionless pivot would keep its original axis forever, and everyday toy tops eventually fall as rotation decays. A fast-spinning object resists tipping dynamically, but that resistance disappears once spin drops.[3][4]

可测量的量是转动惯量、转速和进动角速度。用质量和半径可估算圆盘与圆环的转动惯量差异；进动角速度 Ω=τ/L，力矩一定时角动量越大、进动越慢。判断一个旋转物件稳不稳，不能只看质量，还要看质量分布得离轴多远、转速有多快。The measurable quantities are moment of inertia, spin rate and precession rate. From mass and radius you can estimate how the moments of a disk and a ring differ; the precession rate is Ω=τ/L, so for a fixed torque a larger angular momentum means slower precession. Judging whether a rotating object is stable requires looking not only at mass but at how far that mass sits from the axis and how fast it spins.[4][5]

04抬箱子上楼时，前面的人用力大还是后面的人用力大？When two people carry a box upstairs, who exerts the larger force, the person in front or behind?

刚体平衡要求合力为零、合力矩为零；重心离哪端近，哪端承担的力就大；上楼倾斜时重心水平投影决定前后力臂分配。Balance needs zero net force and torque; the end nearer the centre of mass carries more load, and the horizontal projection of the centre of mass splits the lever arms on stairs.

两人抬一根带箱子的杆件上楼时，箱子重心越靠近哪一端，哪一端承担的力越大。上楼时杆件倾斜，重心在水平方向的投影位置改变了前后力臂的比例，通常后端（低处）更费力。这是刚体静力学问题：在物体不动的前提下，合力为零、合力矩为零，两端支承力由重心位置分配。When two people carry a box on a pole upstairs, whichever end sits nearer the box's centre of mass carries the larger load. On the stairs the pole tilts, and the horizontal projection of the centre of mass changes the split of lever arms; the rear person, at the lower end, usually works harder. This is a rigid-body statics problem: while the object does not move, net force is zero and net torque is zero, so the two support forces follow the centre-of-mass position.[8]

把箱体与抬杆看成杠杆，前后两手是两个施力点，重力作用在重心。以前手为转轴列力矩方程：后手力乘以前后间距，等于重力乘以重心到前手的水平距离。重心靠后，后手的力臂分配更大，后手用力就更大；反过来重心靠前则前手更费力。杆件越硬、两人站位越对称，这个分配越接近力矩计算的结果。Treat the box and pole as a lever, with the two hands as force points and gravity acting at the centre of mass. Taking the front hand as the pivot, torque balance writes the rear hand's force times the hand spacing, equal to gravity times the horizontal distance from the centre of mass to the front hand. When the centre of mass sits behind, the rear arm carries the larger lever arm and works harder; moving it forward shifts the load to the front. Stiffer poles and more symmetric stance make this split match the torque calculation more closely.[8]

水平抬杆时，谁离重物近谁费力；倾斜上楼时，重心的水平投影决定力臂。重物绑扎位置、把手高低、两人脚步先后都会改变前后力的瞬时分配。两人同时抬杆时，手臂倾斜会把一部分力变成竖直分量；杆水平时竖直支承力才直接等于手的施力，倾斜角度越大，沿杆方向的分力需要另作分解。NIST 给出的标准重力 g=9.80665 m/s² 用于把质量换算成重力 mg，是计算支承力时的基准。On a level pole, whoever stands nearer the load works harder; on stairs the horizontal projection of the centre of mass sets the lever arms. How the load is tied, handle height and the timing of the two people's steps all change the instantaneous split. The NIST standard gravity g=9.80665 m/s² converts mass to weight mg, the reference used when computing support forces.[7][8]

以上结论假设杆件刚性、两人同时沿相近方向施力。实际抬箱时手臂角度、箱体变形和脚步晃动都会改变瞬时受力；力矩平衡是静力学结果，一旦起步加速，还要叠加惯性力。两人配合时同步抬落、把重心靠近自己一侧，都是在利用这条力矩分配规律。These results assume a rigid pole and two people pulling in roughly the same direction. In real carrying, arm angles, box deformation and stepping motion change the instantaneous forces; torque balance is a static result, and once walking starts accelerating, inertial force has to be added. Lifting together and keeping the centre of mass close to your own side both use this lever-arm rule.[8]

可测量的量是力臂与两端支承力。设前后手间距 L，重心到前手水平距离 x，则 F前+F后=mg，F前·L=mg·x。若箱体重 100 N、重心距前手 0.6 m、两手间距 1.0 m，则前手支承力约 60 N、后手约 40 N；重心向后移 0.2 m，后手力升到约 60 N，前后互换。给定重心位置与杆长，两端力之差可以定量比较；这也是搬运重物时把重心抬近自己一侧、让对方少费力的原因。The measurable quantities are lever arms and the two support forces. Let L be the hand spacing and x the horizontal distance from the centre of mass to the front hand; then Ffront+Frear=mg and Ffront·L=mg·x. Given the centre-of-mass position and pole length, the force difference between the two ends can be compared quantitatively. This is why moving a heavy load close to yourself leaves the other person with less to carry.[1][8]

05把吹风机装在伞内侧向后吹风，伞会向前飞吗？地面完全无摩擦时，滚动的球还会转动吗？If a blower faces backward inside an umbrella, will the umbrella fly forward? On a frictionless floor, would a rolling ball still spin?

无外力时总动量守恒，向后喷出的流体带来向前反冲；无摩擦时水平切向力消失，球可保持平动，但启动与制动都不再受地面摩擦调节。With no external force momentum conserves, and fluid thrown backward gives forward recoil; on a frictionless floor tangential contact vanishes, so friction cannot start or stop spin.

系统不受外力时总动量守恒。向后抛出或吹出的流体获得向后动量，主体就获得向前的反冲动量。吹风机把空气向后加速，空气对风机施加向前的反作用力，与火箭喷气同类；伞面本身只改变气流方向，真正提供推力的是被加速的空气。能否飞起来，取决于推力与重力、迎面阻力谁大。When no external force acts on a system, total momentum is conserved. Fluid thrown backward acquires backward momentum, and the main body receives equal forward recoil. A blower accelerates air backward, and the air exerts a forward reaction on the fan, the same principle as rocket exhaust; the umbrella canopy only redirects the airflow, while the accelerated air supplies the thrust. Whether it lifts off depends on whether thrust exceeds weight and drag.[6]

动量 p=mv，冲量等于动量变化：FΔt=Δp。反冲装置的推力大小等于单位时间向后喷出的空气质量乘以相对速度，即 F=ṁ·v。火箭排气速度可达每秒数千米，配合大质量流产生巨大推力；家用风扇质量流小、风速低，反冲推力通常只有零点几牛量级。火箭、喷气船、划船拨水都是动量守恒的反冲实例；被排开的水或空气必须计入同一系统，才能说总动量守恒。Momentum is p=mv, and impulse equals the change in momentum: FΔt=Δp. The thrust of a recoil device equals the mass of fluid expelled per unit time times its relative exhaust velocity, F=ṁ·v. Rockets, jet skis and rowing with an oar are all recoil cases of momentum conservation; the displaced water or air must be counted in the same system for total momentum to balance.[6]

无摩擦地面上，接触力只剩法向支承。球与地面之间没有切向摩擦，启动与制动都不能靠地面摩擦完成：球可以保持平动，但也不会因摩擦而改变转动状态。现实地面总有摩擦，球的转动和平动会在摩擦作用下相互转化：打滑时摩擦力先改变转动，进入纯滚动后摩擦趋于零。On a frictionless floor, only normal support remains in contact. With no tangential friction between ball and floor, neither starting nor stopping spin can rely on ground friction: a ball keeps translating, but friction cannot change its rotation. On a real floor friction always exists, and rolling and sliding convert into each other: during skidding friction first changes rotation, and once pure rolling begins friction tends toward zero.[5][6]

动量守恒要求把被吹出的空气计入同一系统；若只看伞体，空气就是外力。小风机推不动大伞，是因为净推力还要扣除迎风阻力与自重；气流速度和空气质量流量有限，反冲推力不足以克服重力。伞面迎风面积大，迎面阻力随速度平方增长；风机转速再高，只要排气量与相对速度有限，向前净推力仍可能被自重和阻力抵消。这类装置的实际升力必须逐项算账，不能凭“向后吹风就会向前飞”的直觉下结论。Momentum conservation counts the expelled air inside the same system; if you look only at the umbrella, the air is an external force. A small fan cannot push a large umbrella because net thrust must also overcome wind drag and its own weight; limited airflow speed and mass flow leave a recoil too weak to beat gravity. Actual lift must be itemized, rather than judged by the intuition that blowing backward automatically flies you forward.[4][5]

可测量的量是质量流、相对速度和动量。用质量与速度可算物体动量，用单位时间排气量乘排气速度可算反冲推力；测量时要分别记录排气质量流、相对速度与净推力，再与自重和阻力逐项比较。边界：反冲在无外力或外力可忽略时严格成立；现实中有空气阻力、摩擦和重力，反冲得到的向前动量会被这些外力逐步消耗。The measurable quantities are mass flow, relative velocity and momentum. Mass and speed give an object's momentum, and exhaust mass flow times exhaust speed gives recoil thrust. The boundary: recoil conservation holds strictly when external forces are absent or negligible; in reality drag, friction and gravity gradually consume the forward momentum gained by recoil.[4][6]

06摩擦为什么会磨出水泡？水泡里的液体是什么？Why does rubbing cause blisters, and what is the fluid inside?

反复剪切使表皮与真皮之间错位分离，组织液渗入形成水泡；摩擦生热、鞋袜挤压和潮湿加重剪切。Repeated shear shifts and separates epidermis from dermis, and interstitial fluid fills the gap into a blister; friction heat, pressure and dampness worsen the shear.

水泡来自摩擦界面的反复剪切。皮肤在鞋袜、手套内部反复滑动时，接触界面产生切向摩擦力；当皮肤表层与下方软组织不能同步移动，两层之间承受反复剪切力。行走、跑步时脚掌反复弯曲和相对滑动，鞋内潮湿使位移增大，剪切在同一局部反复作用，而不是一次性按压。摩擦力大小与接触面粗糙程度和正压力有关；反复滑动比单次按压更易累积损伤，皮肤表层和深层位移不同步时，剪切力集中在两层交界处。Blisters come from repeated shear at the rubbing interface. When skin slides back and forth inside a shoe, boot or glove, tangential friction arises at the contact surface; when the skin surface layer cannot move in step with the soft tissue beneath, the two layers carry repeated shear forces. Walking and running bend and slide the foot repeatedly, and damp footwear increases the displacement, so shear acts over and over at one spot rather than as a single press. Friction depends on surface roughness and normal pressure; repeated sliding accumulates more damage than one press, and when surface and deep layers move out of step, shear concentrates at the boundary between them.[4]

皮肤分层决定了分离发生在哪里。皮肤由表皮、真皮和皮下组织构成，表皮与真皮之间靠基底膜和胶原纤维连接；反复剪切超过连接强度，表皮层与真皮层之间发生分离，形成潜在腔隙。摩擦同时生热，角质层被磨损、变软，进一步削弱表层保护，局部容易在反复错位中裂开。基底膜是表皮与真皮之间的薄层连接结构，真皮内的毛细血管为表皮提供营养；分离发生在表皮下方或表皮真皮交界，而不是把整块皮肤磨穿。Skin layering decides where the separation occurs. Skin consists of epidermis, dermis and subcutaneous tissue, and epidermis joins dermis through a basement membrane and collagen fibers. When repeated shear exceeds the joining strength, epidermis and dermis separate and form a potential gap. Friction also produces heat, wearing and softening the stratum corneum, which further weakens the outer layer and lets repeated displacement split it apart. The basement membrane is a thin joining layer between epidermis and dermis, and dermal capillaries nourish the epidermis; separation occurs under the epidermis or at the dermal-epidermal junction, not by wearing through the whole skin.[3][4]

水泡是渗出液填充分离腔隙的结果。腔隙内毛细血管和组织液渗出，血清样液体主要由水、电解质和少量蛋白质组成，填充分离腔隙，把表皮顶起形成水泡。这层液体起缓冲作用，减少下层组织继续受摩擦；水泡液不是毒液，也不是皮肤"冒水"，而是从组织间隙渗出的液体。The blister forms when exudate fills the gap. Capillaries and interstitial fluid leak into the space; the serum-like fluid, mainly water, electrolytes and a little protein, fills the separated cavity and raises the epidermis into a blister. This liquid cushions the lower tissue against further rubbing. Blister fluid is not poison and not skin sweating water; it is fluid that leaked out of the tissue spaces.[4]

哪些因素会加重。鞋袜过紧、新鞋磨合、长距离徒步、皮肤潮湿出汗都会增加摩擦与剪切；皮肤被汗浸渍后变软，更易分层。赤足或薄袜时摩擦直接作用于足底。新鞋鞋面尚未贴合脚型，局部压强集中；长距离徒步中出汗使鞋袜变软、滑动增大，水泡常在脚跟、脚趾和脚掌侧缘出现。运动中及时调整鞋袜、保持干燥、贴防磨贴，是减少水泡的主要办法；已经发红发热的部位应减少继续摩擦。What makes it worse. Tight footwear, breaking in new shoes, long-distance hiking, damp skin and sweating all raise friction and shear; sweat-softened skin splits more easily. Bare feet or thin socks rub the sole directly. New shoes have uppers that have not conformed to the foot, concentrating local pressure; on long hikes sweat softens footwear and increases sliding, so blisters often appear at the heel, toes and side edges of the sole. During activity, adjusting footwear, staying dry and applying anti-chafing tape are the main preventive measures, and a red hot spot should be spared further rubbing.[1][3]

边界：本章讲摩擦水泡的力学与分层机制。水泡不要随意挑破，完整表皮是天然屏障；已破溃、红肿热痛或有感染迹象需要就医。反复在同一部位起泡，要检查鞋袜贴合度和步态；把水泡归因于上火或体内湿气，不符合剪切分离的力学机制。Boundary: this chapter explains the mechanics and layering of friction blisters. Do not puncture a blister casually; the intact epidermis is a natural barrier. An opened blister, redness, swelling, heat or signs of infection need medical care. Repeated blisters in one spot call for checking footwear fit and gait; blaming blisters on internal heat or dampness in the body does not match the shear-separation mechanism.[1][4]

07为什么重心越低物体越稳定？Why is an object more stable when its center of gravity is lower?

重心越低，倾斜后重力越容易产生回复力矩，重心投影越不容易越出支撑面，稳定角越大。A lower center of gravity gives a larger restoring moment and keeps the weight projection inside the support base through a bigger tilt.

判断一个物体会不会翻倒，先看重力作用线落在什么位置。物体各部分所受重力可以等效到一个重心上，从重心竖直向下画一条重力作用线；支撑面则是各个支撑点在地面上围成的区域。只要这条竖直线还落在支撑面范围以内，重力对支撑边缘产生的就是把物体拉回原位的回复力矩，物体处于稳定状态。一旦倾斜到这条竖直线越出支撑面，重力就变成让物体继续翻倒的翻倒力矩，物体便难以自行恢复。To decide whether an object tips over, first locate where the line of gravity falls. The weight of all parts can be treated as acting at one center of gravity, and a vertical line runs downward from it; the support base is the region enclosed by the contact points on the ground. As long as this line stays inside the base, gravity produces a restoring moment about the support edge that pulls the object back upright, so it is stable. Once the tilt carries the line outside the base, gravity becomes a tipping moment that keeps the object falling, and it cannot recover on its own.[9]

在支撑面大小相同的前提下，重心高低直接决定稳度。重心越低，倾斜同样一个角度时，重心竖直线越不容易越出支撑面，回复力臂也相对更大；要让它翻倒，还需要把重心抬升到支撑边缘上方，克服重力多做功。重心越高，轻微倾斜就可能让投影越出底座，翻倒力矩很快占上风，轻推一下就明显晃动。同样一只箱子，装满重物放在底部比堆在高处稳得多，差别就在整体重心的高低。举例说，底座半宽 0.3 m、重心高 0.5 m 时，tanθ≈0.6，临界倾角约 31°；若把重心降到 0.3 m，同一底座的临界倾角就升到约 45°，稳度明显改善。For the same support base, the height of the center of gravity directly controls stability. The lower the center of gravity, the less easily its vertical line leaves the base during a given tilt, and the larger the restoring arm remains. To tip such an object, the center of gravity must first be raised above the support edge, doing work against gravity. A high center of gravity leaves the base after only a small tilt, the tipping moment quickly dominates, and a light push makes it rock noticeably. The same box sits far more securely with heavy contents at the bottom than stacked high, and the difference is the combined center of gravity.[8][9]

支撑面宽度是另一个独立因素。底座越宽，重心投影越不容易越出边缘；三脚架、宽底座家具、汽车轮距加宽，都是用扩大支撑面来提高稳度。反过来，高脚凳、细杆支架支撑面窄，重心又偏高，就更容易倾倒。重心位置和支撑面要合在一起看，单说其中一项都不足以判断稳度；有些物体重心不高，但底座很窄，照样容易被推倒。The width of the support base is a separate factor. A wider base keeps the weight projection inside the edge for a larger tilt; tripods, broad furniture feet and wider vehicle tracks all raise stability by enlarging the support area. Tall stools and thin pole stands have a narrow base and a high center of gravity, so they tip more easily. Center of gravity and base must be considered together; neither alone determines stability, and some objects with a moderate center but a very narrow base still topple readily.[8]

日常例子随处可见。叠放箱子时重的放下面、轻的放上面，是为了把整体重心压低；汽车把电池和底盘部件布置得很低，过弯时侧倾更小；不倒翁底部配重很重，重心很低，被推倒后重心先升高，松手后重力把它拉回直立位置。搬运高大货物时把重件绑在下层、上层留空，也是同样道理。这些做法的共同点，都是让重心尽量低、尽量靠近支撑面中心。Everyday examples are common. Stacking heavy boxes below light ones keeps the combined center of gravity low; placing batteries and chassis parts low in a car reduces body roll in corners; a roly-poly toy carries heavy weight at its base, so when pushed over its center of gravity rises first, and gravity pulls it back upright on release. Loading heavy cargo on the lower deck and leaving the upper deck light follows the same rule. In each case the goal is to keep the center of gravity low and close to the center of the base.[5][8]

需要划清边界：稳度不只由重心高度决定，还取决于支撑面形状、质量分布和是否受到动态冲击。临界倾角大致满足 tanθ 等于底座半宽除以重心高度，重心越低、底座越宽，θ 越大。本章讲的是静止或缓慢倾斜下的平衡趋势；高速运动中的侧翻还涉及惯性力、轮距和悬挂设计，不能只凭重心高低下结论，移动中的物体还要考虑速度带来的附加影响。The boundary matters: stability depends not only on center-of-gravity height but also on base shape, mass distribution and dynamic shocks. The critical tilt angle roughly satisfies tanθ equal to half the base width over center-of-gravity height; a lower center and wider base give a larger angle. This chapter describes balance at rest or under slow tilt; rollover in fast motion also involves inertia, track width and suspension, so center of gravity alone cannot settle it.[9]

08橡皮筋为什么能拉长又缩回去？Why can a rubber band stretch and then spring back?

橡皮筋里的高分子链平时卷曲缠结，拉伸时被拉直、构象减少而熵降低，松手后链趋向回到高熵卷曲态，产生回弹。Polymer chains in rubber are coiled at rest; stretching aligns them and lowers entropy, so on release the chains return to the more disordered coiled state and pull back.

橡皮筋是硫化交联的高分子材料。橡胶内部由很长的分子链互相连接成网状结构；松弛状态下，这些长链自然卷曲、缠结在一起，弯折点可以出现在很多不同位置，对应大量可能的构象。宏观上橡皮筋松软有弹性，能拉长好几倍，就是这些卷曲链共同表现出来的状态，而不是链本身被抽长。A rubber band is a cross-linked polymer material. Inside the rubber, very long molecular chains are connected into a network. At rest these chains are naturally coiled and tangled, and the kinks can appear in many different positions, giving a large number of possible conformations. The soft, springy feel and the several-fold stretch of the band are the collective behavior of these coiled chains, rather than the chains themselves being pulled longer.[10]

把橡皮筋拉长时，长链被逐渐拉直、沿拉伸方向取向，原本杂乱的缠结被解开。链可以采取的卷曲方式变少，构象数下降，对应的熵，也就是混乱程度，随之降低。分子系统有回到高熵状态的趋势，于是松手后链倾向于重新卷曲、互相缠结，这种趋势在宏观上就表现为橡皮筋往回收缩，把拉它的手往回带。从分子尺度看，松弛态链可以朝任意方向弯折，卷曲组合数随链段数增长；拉伸把这些取向逐一消除，熵的下降正是回弹趋势的来源。When the band is stretched, the long chains are gradually straightened and aligned along the pull direction, and the tangles are opened up. The number of ways the chains can coil decreases, so the number of conformations falls and the entropy, or disorder, drops. A molecular system tends toward a higher-entropy state, so on release the chains return to coiled, tangled arrangements; this tendency appears macroscopically as the band contracting back and pulling against the hand that stretched it.[1][10]

橡胶的回弹和金属弹簧不完全一样。金属弹簧拉伸时主要改变原子之间的距离，回复力来自键能和晶格形变；橡胶主要靠构象熵的变化提供回复力。一个可观察的差别是：拉长橡胶时会放出少量热，回缩时又会从环境吸热；温度升高时橡胶的弹性行为也与普通固体不同，受热反而更容易收缩，这正是熵弹性的特征，和金属受热伸长的方向相反。Rubber recoil differs from a metal spring. In a metal spring, stretching mainly changes interatomic distances and the restoring force comes from bond energy and lattice deformation; rubber supplies its restoring force mainly through conformational entropy. An observable difference is that rubber releases a little heat when stretched and absorbs heat from the surroundings as it contracts; its elastic behavior also changes with temperature in a way ordinary solids do not, shrinking more readily when warmed, which is the signature of entropic elasticity and runs opposite to thermal expansion in metals.[10]

交联网络防止了永久滑移。硫化在链之间形成交联点，把长链连成整体网络；拉伸时链虽然取向，但交联点限制它们彼此滑开，所以松开后网络能回到原来的大致尺寸。如果没有交联，高分子在反复拉伸下会互相黏流、发生不可逆形变，就不是橡皮筋而更像一团软胶；交联密度也决定了橡皮筋是偏软还是偏韧。常见橡皮筋可拉长到原长的 3 倍以上、多在 4–6 倍，卸载后基本回缩；交联密度越高，同样拉力下伸长越小、回弹越干脆。快速拉伸时表面温度可升高约 1–2 °C。The cross-link network prevents permanent slip. Vulcanization forms cross-links between chains that tie them into a whole network; during stretching the chains align, but the cross-links keep them from sliding past one another, so after release the network returns to roughly its original size. Without cross-links the polymer would flow irreversibly under repeated stretching, behaving like soft glue rather than a rubber band; cross-link density also sets whether the band feels soft or tough.[1][8]

本章只讲链构象回弹的机制。橡胶在很大变形范围内的弹性只是近似线性；温度过高链段运动加剧、强度下降，温度过低链段被冻结，材料变硬失去弹性。橡皮筋能拉多长还取决于材料配方、交联密度和截面尺寸，不能把熵弹性理解成所有材料通用的回弹原理；气体、液体和普通金属的弹力来源各有不同。This chapter covers only the conformational recoil mechanism. Rubber elasticity is only approximately linear over large deformations; at high temperature chain motion increases and strength drops, while at low temperature chain segments freeze and the material hardens and loses elasticity. How far a band stretches also depends on formulation, cross-link density and cross-section, so entropic elasticity should not be read as a universal spring principle for every material; gases, liquids and ordinary metals each draw their elastic force from different sources.[10]
