01摩擦为什么会起电？衣物柔顺剂又是怎样防静电的？Why does rubbing produce static charge, and how does fabric softener reduce static?

摩擦使电子在两种材料间转移，得失电子分别带负、正电；导体让电荷流散，绝缘体把电荷留住；柔顺剂在纤维表面形成吸湿导电层。Rubbing transfers electrons, so gained or lost electrons make negative or positive charge; conductors drain it, insulators hold it; softener coats fibres with a moisture layer.

静电来自电荷的转移，而不是“造出”了电。两种材料紧密接触再分开时，外层电子容易在材料间迁移：得到电子的物体带负电，失去电子的带正电。摩擦增大了接触面的有效面积，也把接触点反复拉开，于是起电更明显。电子电荷量的基本单位是元电荷 e≈1.602×10⁻¹⁹ C，宏观带电体带的是这一单位的整数倍。Static electricity comes from charge transfer, not from charge being created. When two materials touch closely and separate, outer electrons can migrate between them: the material that gains electrons charges negative, the one that loses electrons charges positive. Rubbing increases the effective contact area and repeatedly pulls contact points apart, so charging becomes more obvious. The elementary charge is e≈1.602×10⁻¹⁹ C, and any macroscopic charged body carries an integer multiple of this unit.[1]

材料分成导体和绝缘体。金属、大地和人体里有可移动的自由电荷，摩擦产生的电荷会很快流散，不易积累；橡胶、塑料、化纤织物里电荷被束缚在局部，离开接触后仍留在原处，干燥天气下就能保留很久。干燥空气电阻率很高，电荷一旦离开潮湿导体就不易泄放，这也是冬天静电强、梅雨季几乎摸不到静电的原因。冬天脱毛衣听到噼啪声、摸到金属门把被电一下，都是局部积累的电荷通过小放电重新平衡。人体本身是导体，化纤衣物与皮肤、鞋底与地面反复接触分离时，电子转移到人体并在体表分布；干燥天气下鞋底和皮肤表面电阻高，电荷不易经大地泄放，就积累成带静电的人体，手指靠近金属导体时发生尖端放电。Materials split into conductors and insulators. Metals, the ground and the human body hold mobile free charges, so charge produced by rubbing drains away quickly and does not build up. Rubber, plastics and synthetic fibres trap charge locally, which stays in place after contact and can persist in dry weather. The crackle when pulling off a sweater in winter and the small shock from a metal door handle are both local charges rebalancing through a tiny discharge. The body itself is a conductor: when synthetic clothing rubs against skin and footwear repeatedly contacts and separates from the ground, electrons transfer onto the body and spread over its surface; in dry weather the high resistance of soles and skin keeps charge from draining to earth, so the body builds up a net static charge that discharges near a metal conductor.[1][2]

柔顺剂防静电的思路是切断电荷积累。柔顺剂在纤维表面留下一层亲水性薄膜，吸收空气中的微量水汽，使表面电阻下降，摩擦产生的电荷能缓慢导走，而不是在织物上越积越多。它并不“中和”电荷，而是给电荷一条泄漏通路；空气干燥时吸湿层效果变弱，防静电能力随之下降。Fabric softener fights static by blocking charge accumulation. It leaves a hydrophilic film on the fibre surface that absorbs trace moisture from the air, lowers surface resistance and lets rubbed charge leak away slowly instead of piling up on the cloth. It does not neutralize charge; it gives the charge a leakage path. In dry air the moisture layer weakens, and the anti-static effect drops with it.[1]

电荷之间的作用遵守基本规律：同号相斥、异号相吸。带电梳子吸起碎纸片，是纸片近端感应出异号电荷；静电除尘、静电复印也利用感应和吸引。导体上的电荷分布在表面，尖端处电荷密度高，容易发生电晕放电，这也是避雷针把电荷引向尖端的原理。日常静电电压可以很高，但积累电荷量很小，放电能量有限，所以静电火花一般只造成刺痛，不会像电路短路那样持续输出电流。Charge follows a basic rule: like charges repel and opposite charges attract. A charged comb lifts paper scraps because the near side of each scrap induces an opposite charge; electrostatic dust removal and photocopying use the same induction and attraction. Charge on a conductor sits on the surface, and charge density is high at sharp points, where corona discharge easily starts, the same principle that guides lightning rods.[2][7]

边界要写清：静电是电荷的重新分配，总电荷在转移过程中守恒；摩擦本身不产生新电荷。干燥、低温环境绝缘性好，静电更明显；潮湿空气导电，电荷容易泄放。把“摩擦起电”理解成摩擦创造了电，或把静电吸引当成磁性吸引，都是概念上的混淆。The boundary: static is a redistribution of charge, and total charge stays conserved during transfer; rubbing does not create new charge. Dry, cold air insulates well and makes static more obvious, while humid air conducts and lets charge drain. Reading triboelectric charging as charge creation, or static attraction as magnetic attraction, confuses two different mechanisms.[1][7]

02闪电是怎样发生的？云层和地面之间的电是怎样击穿空气的？How does lightning form, and how does electricity break down the air between cloud and ground?

雷暴云内电荷分离形成强电场，电场达到空气击穿阈值（约 3×10⁶ V/m）后空气电离导电，先导与回击构成闪电通道。Charge separation in a thundercloud builds a strong field; when it reaches air breakdown near 3×10⁶ V/m, air ionizes and conducts, and leader plus return stroke form the channel.

闪电是雷暴云中积累的电荷对空气的击穿放电。积雨云内上升气流把冰晶、水滴和过冷水滴反复碰撞摩擦，电荷在云内上下层分离：较重的粒子下沉、较轻的上浮，通常云上部积累正电荷，下部积累负电荷，地面被感应出正电荷。云层与地面之间因此形成一个很大的电场，电压可达数百万到数千万伏。Lightning is a breakdown discharge when charge accumulated in a thundercloud punctures the air. Updraughts in cumulonimbus clouds repeatedly collide ice crystals, water drops and supercooled droplets, separating charge between upper and lower layers: the upper cloud usually carries positive charge, the lower part negative, and the ground beneath induces positive charge. A very large electric field builds between cloud and ground, with voltages reaching millions or tens of millions of volts.[1]

击穿发生在电场强到足以把空气分子电离的时候。干燥空气的击穿场强约 3×10⁶ V/m；云地之间电场达到这一量级后，空气局部变成导电通道，先出现向下伸展的梯级先导，接近地面时地面尖端向上发出迎面先导，两者接通后发生强烈的回击，大电流沿通道流过，形成我们看到的闪电。一次典型云地闪电峰值电流可达数万到十万安，通道温度极高。Breakdown starts when the field is strong enough to ionize air molecules. Dry air breaks down near 3×10⁶ V/m; once the field reaches that order, part of the air becomes a conducting path. A downward stepped leader extends first, and near the ground an upward streamer meets it; when they connect, a strong return stroke carries large current through the channel and produces the visible lightning. A typical cloud-to-ground flash peaks at tens of thousands to a hundred thousand amperes, and the channel becomes extremely hot.[1][7]

证据可以从雷声和放电结构对照。闪电通道把空气瞬间加热到上万摄氏度，空气急剧膨胀产生冲击波，衰减后成为听到的雷声；光跑得远快于声音，看到闪光后按秒数估距，每约 3 秒对应 1 公里。避雷针接地良好时，把先导吸引到针尖，电流沿接地导线流入大地，旁边的建筑与人员避开通道，保护建筑本体不受直击。Evidence matches thunder and discharge structure. The channel heats air to tens of thousands of degrees in an instant, and the rapid expansion sends out a shock wave that decays into thunder. Light travels far faster than sound, so counting seconds after the flash estimates distance: about three seconds per kilometre. Lightning rods and grounding wires guide a possible discharge onto themselves first, protecting the building from a direct strike.[2][7]

雷击的重复与落点有概率特征。同一地点可能被多次击中，尤其高塔、山脊和孤立高处，因为它们更接近先导路径；但“闪电从不击中同一地方两次”是误解。电流在云层与地面之间重新分配电荷，放电结束后云内电荷还会继续积累，下一次雷暴仍可能再次放电。雷暴中还有云间闪电和云内放电，不经过地面，但空气击穿与先导接通的机制相同。Repeat strikes and landing spots are probabilistic. The same site can be hit more than once, especially towers, ridges and isolated high points, because they sit closer to the leader path; the saying that lightning never strikes twice in the same place is a misconception. The current redistributes charge between cloud and ground, and after a strike the cloud keeps accumulating charge, so the next storm can discharge again.[1][2]

边界：闪电是大气中的宏观放电过程，不只是“云带电”这么简单；电荷分离、电场增强、空气击穿、先导接通四个环节缺一不可。室内电器的静电火花与闪电量级不同，原理同为击穿放电，但电流、能量差很多。测量雷暴电场需要专用场强仪，不能凭云的形状判断放电风险。The boundary: lightning is a macroscopic atmospheric discharge, not merely 'the cloud is charged'; charge separation, field strengthening, air breakdown and leader connection are four required stages. Static sparks from indoor electronics share the same breakdown principle but differ enormously in current and energy. Thunderstorm fields need dedicated field meters; cloud shape alone cannot judge strike risk.[7]

03磁铁为什么能吸铁？铁在约 800 °C 附近为什么会失去磁性？Why does a magnet attract iron, and why does iron lose magnetism near 800 °C?

铁磁体由磁畴组成，外场使畴壁移动、磁矩取向一致；加热到居里点（铁约 770 °C）热运动打乱磁畴，磁性消失。Ferromagnets are made of domains; an external field moves domain walls and aligns moments; above the Curie point (iron about 770 °C) thermal motion scrambles domains and magnetism vanishes.

磁铁能吸铁，根源在铁磁材料内部的磁畴。铁、钴、镍这类铁磁体由许多小区域组成，每个区域内原子磁矩方向一致，称为磁畴；未磁化时各畴方向杂乱，宏观磁性抵消。靠近外磁场时，取向有利的磁畴扩张、不利的缩小，畴壁移动，整体磁矩逐渐对齐，材料对外显出磁性，被磁铁吸引。铁钉、回形针靠近磁铁后也能吸起别的铁件，就是内部磁畴被外场带动对齐的结果。A magnet attracts iron because of the magnetic domains inside ferromagnetic materials. Iron, cobalt and nickel are divided into small regions, each with atomic magnetic moments aligned in one direction; an unmagnetized piece has domains pointing every which way, so macroscopic magnetism cancels out. Near an external field, favourable domains expand and unfavourable ones shrink as domain walls move, the net moment aligns, and the material shows magnetism and is pulled toward the magnet.[3]

磁矩来自电子的轨道运动和自旋。在经典图像里，运动电荷形成微小电流环，每个电流环都有自己的磁矩；铁磁材料中相邻原子的自旋通过交换作用耦合，使同一磁畴内磁矩自发平行。磁铁外部的磁感线从 N 极出发回到 S 极，内部则从 S 回到 N，形成闭合回路，磁感应线不会相交。Magnetic moments come from electron orbital motion and spin. In the classical picture, moving charge forms a tiny current loop, and every loop carries its own magnetic moment. In ferromagnets the spins of neighbouring atoms couple through exchange interaction, so moments in one domain are spontaneously parallel. Outside a magnet, field lines run from the north pole to the south; inside they return from south to north, forming closed loops that never cross.[4][7]

温度会破坏磁性。加热到居里点以上，热运动的能量超过磁耦合作用，磁畴结构被打乱，磁矩重新随机排列，材料从铁磁体变为顺磁体，对外不再保留强磁性。纯铁的居里温度约 770 °C（1043 K）；镍约 354 °C，钴约 1121 °C。磁铁被烧红、或长期加热接近居里点后，磁性明显减弱甚至消失，降温后能否恢复取决于是否重新磁化。Temperature destroys magnetism. Above the Curie point, thermal motion exceeds the magnetic coupling, the domain structure scrambles and moments randomize again; the material changes from ferromagnetic to paramagnetic and no longer holds strong magnetism. Pure iron has a Curie temperature near 770 °C (1043 K), nickel about 354 °C and cobalt about 1121 °C. Heating a magnet red hot, or near its Curie point for long enough, weakens or removes its magnetism; recovery on cooling depends on whether it is remagnetized.[3][4]

永磁体的磁性也会随时间和环境缓慢变化。受到剧烈撞击、反复退磁或接近高温时，磁畴取向被打乱，剩磁下降；把铁件长期放在磁铁旁，铁件内部磁畴逐步被定向，也会带上弱磁性。硬磁材料畴壁难移动，剩磁强，适合做永久磁铁；软磁材料容易磁化也容易退磁，适合做电磁铁铁芯。电磁铁则相反：线圈通电时铁芯磁畴对齐产生强磁场，断电后磁性基本消失，磁场强弱可由电流控制。Permanent magnetism also drifts slowly with time and environment. Hard impacts, repeated demagnetization or high temperature scramble domain orientation and lower remanence; leaving an iron piece beside a magnet lets its domains align gradually, so the piece gains weak magnetism. Electromagnets work the other way: current through the coil aligns the core domains into a strong field, which mostly disappears when current stops, and strength is controlled by the current.[4]

边界：磁铁只对铁磁材料有强吸引力，对铜、铝、玻璃、塑料等基本不产生磁力。磁化是磁畴取向的过程，不是铁被“吸进”磁铁内部。判断一块铁是否被磁化，要看它能否吸引铁钉、指示方向或排斥另一块已知磁极；单凭贴在冰箱上不能区分是磁吸引还是胶贴作用。The boundary: magnets attract ferromagnetic materials strongly but exert almost no magnetic force on copper, aluminium, glass or plastic. Magnetization aligns domains; it does not pull iron physically into the magnet. To tell whether an iron piece is magnetized, check whether it attracts nails, points in a direction or repels another known pole; sticking to a fridge alone cannot distinguish magnetic attraction from adhesive mounting.[3]

04干电池为什么能储存电？锂离子电池充放电时离子怎样迁移？How does a dry cell store electricity, and how do lithium ions move during charge and discharge?

电池靠化学反应储能，离子在电解液中迁移；干电池 1.5 V、锂电 3.6–3.7 V，端电压随内阻压降下降。A cell turns chemical energy to potential via electrode reactions and ion migration; dry cells 1.5 V, Li-ion 3.6–3.7 V, terminal voltage drops with load and internal resistance.

电池储存的是化学能，输出时再变成电势能。干电池内部是锌筒负极、碳棒正极，中间隔着糊状物电解液；锌锰干电池的负极就是锌筒本身，正极碳棒周围是二氧化锰去极剂，电解液为氯化铵糊状物。化学反应在两个电极上分别进行，负极释放电子，正极接受电子，外电路接通后电子沿导线流动，形成电流。一节普通锌锰干电池的标称电压约 1.5 V，电压由电极材料的化学势差决定，不是由体积决定。A battery stores chemical energy and converts it into electric potential when it delivers current. A dry cell has a zinc can as the negative electrode and a carbon rod as the positive, separated by a paste electrolyte. Reactions happen at both electrodes: the negative releases electrons, the positive accepts them, and once the external circuit closes electrons flow through the wire as current. A common zinc–manganese dry cell is rated about 1.5 V, a voltage set by the chemical potential difference of the electrode materials, not by its size.[5]

锂离子电池的迁移路径更清楚。充电时外部电源把锂离子从正极材料脱出，穿过电解液和隔膜嵌入负极（石墨层间）；放电时锂离子反向回到正极，电子经外电路做功。单体锂离子电池标称电压约 3.6–3.7 V，能量密度高于干电池；充放循环中离子反复往返，电极材料结构逐渐老化，容量缓慢下降。The lithium-ion path is easier to follow. During charging an external supply pulls lithium ions out of the cathode material, across the electrolyte and separator, and into the graphite anode; on discharge the ions return to the cathode while electrons do work through the external circuit. A single Li-ion cell is rated about 3.6–3.7 V and holds more energy per mass than a dry cell. Ions shuttle back and forth over cycles, the electrode structure ages gradually, and capacity slowly drops.[1][5]

电池的端电压不等于开路电压。电动势（emf）是无电流时两极的电势差；对外供电时，电池内部有内阻 r，电流 I 流过会产生 Ir 压降，端电压 U=emf−Ir。新电池内阻小，带负载时电压下降少；旧电池内阻增大，手电筒一接灯泡就电压塌陷，就是内阻压降占去了大部分电动势。用万用表测开路电压看不出内阻差别，只有接上负载测端电压，新旧电池的差距才明显暴露。Terminal voltage differs from open-circuit voltage. Electromotive force (emf) is the potential difference between poles with no current drawn; under load the cell has internal resistance r, and current I creates an Ir drop, so terminal voltage U=emf−Ir. A fresh cell has low resistance and keeps its voltage under load; an old cell's resistance rises, and a torch bulb collapses the voltage at once, because the internal drop eats most of the emf.[2][5]

化学反应决定电池能放出多少电。容量用安时（Ah）或毫安时（mAh）表示，记录在一定放电条件下能提供的电荷量；实际可用容量随放电电流、温度和老化程度变化。低温下电解液变稠、离子迁移变慢，电池可用容量明显下降；大电流放电时内阻发热也会限制输出。Chemical reactions set how much charge a battery can deliver. Capacity is given in ampere-hours (Ah) or milliampere-hours (mAh), the charge available under a stated discharge condition. Real usable capacity shifts with discharge current, temperature and ageing. In cold weather the electrolyte thickens, ion movement slows and usable capacity drops; high-current discharge also limits output through internal heating.[2]

边界：电池不“存电”成一团静止的电子，储存的是可通过电极反应释放的化学能。干电池一次用完后反应物质耗尽，不能简单充电复原；锂离子电池可以反复嵌脱离子，但过充、过放和高温会加速老化。判断剩余电量不能只看开路电压，带载电压、内阻和循环次数都要一起参考。The boundary: a battery does not hold a static lump of electrons; it stores chemical energy released through electrode reactions. A primary dry cell is consumed after its reactants run out and cannot simply be recharged. Li-ion cells shuttle ions back and forth, but overcharging, deep discharge and high temperature accelerate ageing. Remaining charge cannot be judged from open-circuit voltage alone; loaded voltage, internal resistance and cycle count must be read together.[1][5]

05LC 电路的振荡频率由什么决定？振荡电路怎样发出电磁波？What sets the oscillation frequency of an LC circuit, and how does an oscillator produce electromagnetic waves?

LC 谐振频率 f=1/(2π√LC)，电感电容互换储能；振荡电流加速电荷辐射电磁波，频率波长满足 c=λf。The LC resonant frequency is f=1/(2π√LC), energy swapping between inductor and capacitor; oscillating current accelerates charge and radiates waves per Maxwell, with c=λf.

电感与电容组成的 LC 电路是交流振荡的基本单元。电容先充上电，通过电感线圈放电；电容电压降到零时，线圈的自感作用维持电流继续流动，反过来给电容反向充电；能量在电容的电场能和电感的磁场能之间来回互换，形成周期性振荡。忽略电阻时，振荡频率由电感 L 和电容 C 决定：f=1/(2π√LC)。An inductor and a capacitor form the basic unit of an AC oscillator, the LC circuit. The capacitor charges first and discharges through the coil; when its voltage reaches zero, self-inductance keeps the current flowing and charges the capacitor in reverse. Energy swaps back and forth between the capacitor's electric field and the inductor's magnetic field, giving a periodic oscillation. Neglecting resistance, the oscillation frequency is set by L and C: f=1/(2π√LC).[6]

这个频率可以算。增大电感或电容，周期变长、频率降低；减小 L 或 C，频率升高。收音机选台就是调节可变电容，改变 LC 回路的谐振频率，调谐回路扫过不同频率，信号在谐振点响应最强。电感储存磁场能 ½LI²，电容储存电场能 ½CV²，两者在振荡过程中此消彼长，总电磁能在无损耗时守恒。This frequency can be calculated. Larger inductance or capacitance lengthens the period and lowers the frequency; smaller L or C raises it. A radio tunes stations by adjusting a variable capacitor that changes the resonant frequency of the LC loop, so only the target frequency responds most strongly. The inductor stores magnetic energy ½LI² and the capacitor electric energy ½CV²; the two trade off during oscillation, and total electromagnetic energy is conserved without loss.[6]

振荡电流是加速运动的电荷。按麦克斯韦理论，变化的电场产生磁场，变化的磁场产生电场；振荡电荷与交变电流在周围空间激发相互依存的电磁场，以电磁波形式向外传播。LC 回路本身辐射效率有限，接上天线后，高频振荡电流驱动天线辐射，无线电广播、手机通信都建立在这一机制上。Oscillating current is accelerated charge. In Maxwell's theory, a changing electric field produces a magnetic field and a changing magnetic field produces an electric field; oscillating charge and alternating current excite interdependent fields in the surrounding space, which propagate outward as electromagnetic waves. An LC loop itself radiates weakly; connected to an antenna, the high-frequency oscillation drives the antenna to radiate, and radio broadcasting and mobile communication build on this mechanism.[7]

电磁波在真空中以光速传播，频率与波长满足 c=λf。可见光、无线电波、微波、X 射线都是同一类电磁波，差别只在频率和波长；无线电波波长可达数千米，可见光波长约 400–700 nm，中波广播波长数百米，调频广播约 3 m，传播机制相同而尺度差别很大。电网交流电频率 50 或 60 Hz，附近铁芯在交变磁场中轻微振动，发出同频或倍频的嗡鸣，这是电磁力驱动机械振动，不是电磁波辐射到人耳。Electromagnetic waves travel at light speed in vacuum, and frequency and wavelength satisfy c=λf. Visible light, radio waves, microwaves and X-rays are the same kind of wave, differing only in frequency and wavelength: radio waves can reach thousands of metres, while visible light spans about 400–700 nm. Mains AC runs at 50 or 60 Hz, and nearby iron cores vibrate slightly in the alternating field, producing a hum at that frequency or its harmonic; this is electromagnetic force driving mechanical vibration, not a radio wave reaching the ear.[7][8]

边界：LC 振荡频率是电路谐振频率，不等于电磁波一定向外辐射；辐射还取决于天线尺寸、阻抗匹配和振荡幅度。真实回路总有电阻，振荡会衰减为阻尼振荡，需要持续补充能量才能维持等幅振荡。把电路里的交流嗡鸣当成“听到无线电波”，混淆了机械振动与电磁传播两个层次。The boundary: the LC oscillation frequency is a circuit resonant frequency and does not by itself guarantee radiation; emission also depends on antenna size, impedance matching and oscillation amplitude. A real loop always has resistance, so the oscillation damps out unless energy is supplied continuously to keep it steady. Hearing a mains hum as 'receiving radio waves' confuses mechanical vibration with electromagnetic propagation.[8]

06磁铁为什么有磁力？磁铁加热后为什么会退磁？Why do magnets have magnetic force, and why does heating demagnetize them?

磁畴内电子自旋磁矩同向排列；外场使畴取向一致显宏观磁性，加热到居里温度以上随机化退磁。Domains align electron-spin moments; an external field aligns domains into macroscopic magnetism, and heat above the Curie temperature randomizes them.

磁铁的磁力来自原子内部的电子磁矩。原子里电子的轨道运动和自旋都会产生微小磁矩，其中自旋是铁磁材料磁矩的主要来源。在铁、钴、镍这类铁磁材料里，相邻原子的电子通过交换作用倾向于平行排列，于是材料内部划分成一个个小区域，即磁畴；每个畴内所有磁矩方向一致，像一块微型小磁铁。交换作用是一种量子效应，使相邻电子自旋倾向平行，而不是杂乱取向。A magnet's force comes from electron magnetic moments inside atoms. Electron orbital motion and spin each produce a tiny moment, and spin is the main source of moments in ferromagnetic materials. In iron, cobalt and nickel, exchange interaction favors parallel alignment between neighboring atoms, so the material divides into small regions called domains; within each domain all moments point one way, like a miniature magnet. The exchange interaction is a quantum effect that favors parallel spins between neighboring electrons rather than random orientation.[3][4]

未磁化时各磁畴方向杂乱。不同畴的磁矩互相抵消，整块材料对外不显磁性。靠近外磁场时，与外场同向的畴扩大、反向的畴缩小，畴壁在材料内移动；磁场足够强时，各畴取向整体转向外场方向，材料对外显示出南北极，能吸引铁钉、铁屑等铁磁物体。磁感线从 N 极出发、回到 S 极，在磁体内部闭合，磁极总是成对出现。把一块磁铁切成两段，每段仍各自带有 N 极和 S 极，目前没有发现单独存在的磁单极。Before magnetization the domains point in mixed directions. Their moments cancel, and the bulk shows no external magnetism. Near an external field, domains aligned with the field grow while opposing ones shrink, and domain walls move through the material; at sufficient field strength the domains turn together toward the field direction, and the material shows north and south poles that attract nails and iron filings. Field lines leave the north pole, return to the south, close inside the magnet, and poles always appear in pairs. Cut a magnet in two, and each piece still carries its own north and south pole; isolated magnetic monopoles have not been found.[3][4]

加热会打乱这种有序排列。温度升高使原子热振动加剧，达到居里温度以上时，热运动压倒交换作用，磁畴取向重新变得随机，宏观磁性消失。铁的居里温度约 770 °C（约 1043 K），钴、镍各有自己的数值。冷却后如果没有外磁场引导，材料不会自动恢复强磁；反复敲击、摔打和外加反向磁场也会打乱畴取向，造成退磁。Heating disrupts this order. Rising temperature intensifies atomic vibration; above the Curie temperature thermal motion overwhelms the exchange interaction, domain orientations turn random again, and macroscopic magnetism disappears. Iron's Curie point is about 770 °C, or 1043 K, with cobalt and nickel at their own values. After cooling without a guiding external field the material does not regain strong magnetism on its own; repeated knocking, dropping and reverse fields also scramble domain alignment and demagnetize it.[4][7]

永磁体与电磁铁是两类用法。铁磁材料保留畴取向就成为永磁体；电磁铁靠通电线圈产生磁场，线圈电流产生的磁场使铁芯磁化，断电后软铁铁芯的剩磁很弱。磁场可以穿过真空和纸、木等非磁性材料，但对铁磁体的作用随距离增大快速减弱，两端磁极附近作用最强。Permanent magnets and electromagnets are two uses. A ferromagnetic material that keeps its domain alignment becomes a permanent magnet; an electromagnet relies on a current-carrying coil whose field magnetizes the iron core, and after switch-off the soft-iron core retains little remanence. Magnetic fields pass through vacuum and non-magnetic materials such as paper and wood, but the force on ferromagnets falls off quickly with distance and is strongest near the poles.[3][7]

边界：本章讲铁磁体内部的磁畴与自旋机制；顺磁、抗磁材料没有这样的畴结构，磁矩来源不同，不能套用磁畴模型。居里温度是材料属性，具体数值随材料成分变化。加热退磁是热运动打乱有序排列，不是把磁铁里的磁能烧光；永磁体也会因长期高温、撞击和反向磁场而缓慢减弱磁性。Boundary: this chapter covers domains and spin inside ferromagnets; paramagnetic and diamagnetic materials lack this domain structure and have different moment origins, so the domain model does not apply. The Curie temperature is a material property whose value shifts with composition. Thermal demagnetization means heat scrambles ordered alignment, not that stored magnetic energy is burned away; permanent magnets also weaken slowly through sustained heat, impact and reverse fields.[4][7]

07金属为什么能导电？塑料为什么不导电？纯水为什么不导电，自来水却导电？Why do metals conduct, why does plastic insulate, and why does pure water barely conduct while tap water does?

金属有可自由移动的电子海，外加电压下定向漂移；塑料电子被共价键束缚；纯水几乎无离子，溶解的盐解离出离子才导电。Metals hold a mobile electron sea that drifts under voltage; plastic electrons are bound, and dissolved salt ions carry current in water.

导电的前提是有可自由移动的载流子。电流是电荷在电场作用下的定向移动：金属里的载流子是自由电子，电解质溶液里是正负离子，半导体里是电子和空穴。外加电压在材料内部建立电场，载流子受力漂移，定向移动叠加起来就形成宏观电流。材料导电能力的差别，主要来自载流子数量和移动难易。Conduction needs mobile charge carriers. Electric current is the directed drift of charge under an electric field: in metals the carriers are free electrons, in electrolyte solutions positive and negative ions, and in semiconductors electrons and holes. An applied voltage sets up an internal field, carriers drift under force, and their directed motion sums into a macroscopic current. Differences in conductivity come mainly from carrier count and how easily carriers move.[1][2]

金属是典型导体。金属原子外层电子束缚较弱，脱离原子后形成遍布晶体的自由电子海；晶格正离子固定在原位，电子可以在其间自由移动，所以金属既导电又导热好。铜、铝、银是常见导线材料，电子漂移把电信号和能量从一端传到另一端。金属导电时原子本身不移动，移动的只是外层电子。自由电子海还把热振动迅速传到整块金属，所以金属导热也快。Metals are typical conductors. Outer electrons of metal atoms are weakly bound and detach to form a free electron sea spread through the crystal; lattice positive ions stay in place while electrons move among them, so metals conduct both electricity and heat well. Copper, aluminum and silver are common wire materials, and electron drift carries signals and energy from one end to the other. The atoms do not move during conduction; only outer electrons travel. The free electron sea also conducts thermal vibration quickly through the metal, which is why metals conduct heat well.[2]

塑料、橡胶、玻璃、陶瓷是绝缘体。其中的电子被共价键或离子键紧紧束缚，没有可自由移动的载流子；外加电场下电子只能在原位附近小范围极化，不能形成持续电流，因此常做成导线外层的绝缘层。干燥空气也是绝缘体，只有电压极高、发生击穿时才会导电。绝缘材料受潮后表面形成水膜，绝缘性能会明显下降。Plastic, rubber, glass and ceramics are insulators. Their electrons are tightly bound in covalent or ionic bonds, with no free carriers; under an applied field electrons polarize only in a small local range and cannot sustain a current, which is why these materials form wire insulation. Dry air is an insulator too, conducting only at very high voltage after breakdown. When insulation gets damp, a water film forms on the surface and its insulating performance drops noticeably.[1][2]

水的导电性要看里面溶解了什么。纯水是极弱的电解质，只有极少量 H⁺ 和 OH⁻，电阻率很高，几乎不导电；自来水、海水和汗液溶解了盐类，盐解离出 Na⁺、Cl⁻ 等正负离子，离子在电场中迁移，溶液就能导电。所以电器沾水后漏电风险升高，潮湿皮肤的电阻也更低，触电时电流更容易通过人体。汗液含盐分，手掌出汗后接触电器更危险；蒸馏水这类纯水则接近绝缘体。Water conduction depends on what is dissolved in it. Pure water is a very weak electrolyte with only tiny amounts of H⁺ and OH⁻, high resistivity and almost no conduction; tap water, seawater and sweat hold dissolved salts that dissociate into Na⁺, Cl⁻ and other ions, which migrate in the field and make the solution conductive. Wet appliances therefore carry higher shock risk, and damp skin has lower resistance, so current passes through the body more easily. Sweat holds salts, so sweaty hands contacting appliances are more dangerous; pure water such as distilled water approaches an insulator.[2][5]

边界：本章区分导体、绝缘体和离子导电；半导体介于两者之间，靠掺杂改变载流子浓度。导体与绝缘没有绝对界限，高电压、潮湿、高温和材料老化都会降低绝缘性能。把"水能导电"理解成水分子本身带电是常见误解：导电的是溶解的离子，纯水并不导电。Boundary: this chapter separates conductors, insulators and ionic conduction; semiconductors sit between, and doping changes their carrier concentration. Conduction and insulation are not absolute: high voltage, dampness, heat and aging all reduce insulation performance. Reading "water conducts" as water molecules themselves being charged is a common mistake; the carriers are dissolved ions, and pure water does not conduct.[1][2]

08X 射线是怎样产生和成像的？How are X-rays produced and how do they form an image?

高压电子撞击金属靶辐射出 X 射线；骨骼比软组织吸收多，探测器记录强度差形成影像。High voltage drives electrons into a metal target that emits short-wavelength X-rays; bone absorbs more than soft tissue, and the detector records the intensity contrast.

X 射线和可见光同属电磁波，差别在频率和波长。X 射线波长很短，约 0.01–10 nm，光子能量高，能穿透不少可见光穿不过的物体。日常生活接触到的 X 射线主要来自 X 射线管这类人工装置，而不是太阳光里的主要成分；它属于电离辐射，光子能量足以把原子外层电子打出来。X-rays and visible light are both electromagnetic waves, differing in frequency and wavelength. X-rays are very short, about 0.01 to 10 nm, with high photon energy that penetrates many objects visible light cannot. The X-rays encountered in daily life mainly come from artificial devices such as X-ray tubes rather than being a main component of sunlight; they are ionizing radiation, energetic enough to knock outer electrons out of atoms.[8][9]

产生过程发生在 X 射线管内。管两端加高电压，阴极发出的电子被电场加速，高速射向金属靶，常用钨或钼作靶材。电子撞上靶材后突然减速，部分动能以光子形式放出，这种减速辐射叫轫致辐射；另有电子把靶原子内层电子打出，外层电子回落填补空位时，也会放出能量固定的特征 X 射线。靶材选用高熔点金属，是因为电子撞击会产生大量热；常见管电压约 50–100 kV，轫致辐射的连续谱加上靶材的特征谱线，共同组成 X 射线管输出的射线。Production takes place inside an X-ray tube. A high voltage, often tens of kV, is applied across the tube; electrons emitted at the cathode are accelerated by the field and strike a metal target, commonly tungsten or molybdenum. On impact the electrons decelerate abruptly and part of their kinetic energy leaves as photons, called bremsstrahlung; other electrons knock out inner-shell electrons of target atoms, and outer electrons fall back to fill the vacancy, emitting characteristic X-rays of fixed energy.[9]

成像利用的是穿透与吸收差异。射线穿过人体时，不同组织对 X 射线的吸收程度不同：骨骼含钙，原子序数高、密度大，吸收射线多；肌肉、脂肪等软组织密度低，吸收少。穿过身体后的射线强度分布，就携带了内部组织的密度差别，探测器把这种强度差记录下来，形成影像。不同组织的原子组成和厚度不同，衰减程度随之变化；探测器把穿透强度按位置记录，再转换成灰度图像，密度差越大，对比越清楚。Imaging relies on the difference in transmission and absorption. As rays pass through the body, tissues absorb X-rays to different degrees: bone contains calcium, with high atomic number and density, and absorbs more; muscle and fat are less dense and absorb less. The intensity distribution after passing through the body carries the density differences of internal tissues, and a detector records this intensity difference to form an image.[8][9]

在常见平片上，吸收多的高密度部位到达探测器的射线少，影像偏白；吸收少的软组织区域偏暗。把人体不同厚度、不同密度的组织排列记录在同一张片子上，医生据此观察骨骼形态、异物或异常阴影。X 射线与光成像的根本区别在于：它靠射线穿过物体后的衰减对比，而不是靠物体反射可见光。On a common plain film, dense areas that absorb more deliver fewer rays to the detector and appear whiter, while soft-tissue areas that absorb less look darker. Recording tissues of different thickness and density on one film lets clinicians observe bone shape, foreign objects or abnormal shadows. The basic difference from light imaging is that X-ray pictures rely on attenuation contrast after rays pass through the object, not on reflected visible light.[7][8]
