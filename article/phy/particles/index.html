基本粒子、寿命与暗物质Elementary Particles, Lifetimes and Dark Matter

01标准模型里有哪些基本粒子？夸克还能再分吗？What particles make up the Standard Model, and can quarks be split further?

标准模型分六夸克、六轻子、规范玻色子和希格斯玻色子；目前对撞机探测不到夸克内部结构，原子看似空旷其实被电磁力填满。The Standard Model has six quarks, six leptons, gauge bosons and the Higgs; colliders see no internal quark structure, and an atom feels solid through forces, not hard contact.

已知物质的基本组分由粒子物理标准模型整理。费米子分夸克和轻子两组：六个夸克为上、下、粲、奇、顶、底；六个轻子为电子、μ 子、τ 子和对应的三种中微子。规范玻色子传递相互作用：光子传电磁力，胶子传强作用力，W/Z 玻色子传弱作用力；希格斯玻色子通过希格斯机制赋予基本粒子质量。The known constituents of matter are organized in the Standard Model of particle physics. Fermions split into quarks and leptons: six quarks (up, down, charm, strange, top, bottom) and six leptons (electron, muon, tau, and the three corresponding neutrinos). Gauge bosons carry the forces: the photon electromagnetism, gluons the strong force, W and Z bosons the weak force; the Higgs boson gives elementary particles mass through the Higgs mechanism.[1][7]

质量跨度很大。电子质量约 0.511 MeV/c²，质子约 938.27 MeV/c²；上、下夸克自身质量只有 2–5 MeV/c² 量级，质子质量主要来自胶子场的束缚能而非夸克静质量。粒子数据组（PDG）汇总各粒子的质量、寿命、衰变分支等测量值。OpenStax 粒子物理入门章节按作用力把费米子分成夸克与轻子：夸克参与强相互作用，轻子不参与。Masses span a wide range. The electron is about 0.511 MeV/c² and the proton about 938.27 MeV/c²; the up and down quarks weigh only around 2–5 MeV/c², so most of the proton mass comes from gluon-field binding energy rather than the quarks' rest mass. The Particle Data Group (PDG) compiles measured masses, lifetimes and decay branches for each particle. The OpenStax introduction to particle physics divides fermions by force: quarks take part in the strong interaction, leptons do not.[1][2]

是否存在比夸克更小的组分？高能对撞机把粒子加速到 TeV 级别对撞，探测尺度约 10⁻¹⁹ m 量级，至今没有观测到夸克或轻子内部有更细结构的证据。标准模型把夸克、轻子当作点粒子处理；若未来发现亚结构，需要扩展标准模型。实验上的上限比理论猜测更有分量：没看到分裂，不等于永远不可分，而是目前没有可测的内部层次。Can quarks be split further? High-energy colliders accelerate particles to TeV collisions, probing scales around 10⁻¹⁹ m, and no finer internal structure in quarks or leptons has been observed so far. The Standard Model treats quarks and leptons as point particles; any substructure would require an extension to the model. Experimental limits carry more weight than theoretical speculation: not having seen splitting does not mean infinite divisibility, only that no internal level is measurable today.[2][7]

“原子里大部分是空的”需要准确理解。原子核直径约 10⁻¹⁵ m，原子直径约 10⁻¹⁰ m，体积相差约 10¹⁵ 倍，质量却集中在核上；电子以概率云分布在核外。接触物体时感到的坚硬，来自电子云之间的静电排斥和泡利不相容原理，而不是粒子像小球那样顶住。所以“空”指质量与占据体积的集中，不是中间什么都没有。The claim "an atom is mostly empty" needs careful reading. The nucleus is about 10⁻¹⁵ m across and the atom about 10⁻¹⁰ m, a volume ratio near 10¹⁵, while almost all mass sits in the nucleus; electrons occupy a probability cloud outside it. The hardness felt on contact comes from electrostatic repulsion between electron clouds and the Pauli exclusion principle, not from particles butting like billiard balls. "Empty" here means concentrated mass and volume, not nothing in between.[1][2]

标准模型不是万物理论：它不包含引力，也不解释暗物质、正反物质不对称和中微子质量的起源。模型内粒子分类清楚，但质量参数来自测量而非第一性原理。讨论“最小粒子”时要区分理论模型、实验探测尺度和哲学上的可分性；现有结论是：在对撞机能量范围内，夸克和轻子没有显示内部结构。The Standard Model is not a theory of everything: it excludes gravity and does not explain dark matter, matter-antimatter asymmetry or the origin of neutrino mass. Its particle classification is clear, but mass parameters come from measurement rather than first principles. When discussing the "smallest particle", one should separate theory model, experimental probe scale and philosophical divisibility; the current conclusion is that within collider energies, quarks and leptons show no internal structure.

02质子会不会衰变？不同基本粒子的寿命由什么决定？Will the proton decay, and what sets the lifetime of different elementary particles?

质子寿命下限超 1.6×10³⁴ 年；自由中子约 879 秒、μ 子约 2.2 微秒衰变，短寿命对应宽质量宽度。Proton lifetime lower limit above 1.6×10³⁴ years; free neutron 879 s, muon 2.2 µs; short lifetime means wide mass width.

稳定性是粒子物理的可测量属性，而不是凭直觉判断。质子在标准模型内是最轻的重子，没有允许的衰变通道，因此被当作稳定粒子处理；但大统一理论预言质子可能衰变为更轻的轻子和介子，只是寿命极长。要验证这种预言，只能建造大型探测器长期监视大量质子，等待极稀有的衰变事件。Stability is a measurable property in particle physics, not an intuitive judgement. Within the Standard Model the proton is the lightest baryon and has no allowed decay channel, so it is treated as stable; grand unified theories nonetheless predict the proton may decay into lighter leptons and mesons, with a very long lifetime. To test such predictions, large detectors watch enormous numbers of protons over years for extremely rare decay events.[2][7]

超级神冈探测器（Super-Kamiokande）以数万吨水监测质子衰变，对质子衰变为正电子加 π⁰ 道给出的寿命下限约 1.6×10³⁴ 年，远超过宇宙年龄约 1.38×10¹⁰ 年。作为对照，自由中子寿命约 879.4 秒（约 14.6 分钟），β 衰变为质子、电子和反电子中微子；μ 子寿命约 2.197 µs；电子是最轻的带电轻子，没有可衰变的更轻带电产物。PDG 数据表把这些寿命和测量误差逐项列出。The Super-Kamiokande detector monitors proton decay in tens of thousands of tons of water and sets a lifetime lower limit of about 1.6×10³⁴ years for the proton to positron plus π⁰ channel, far beyond the Universe's age of about 1.38×10¹⁰ years. By comparison, a free neutron lives about 879.4 seconds (roughly 14.6 minutes), beta-decaying into a proton, electron and antineutrino; a muon lives about 2.197 µs; the electron is the lightest charged lepton with no lighter charged final state. The PDG data tables list each lifetime and its measurement uncertainty.[2][6]

寿命与质量宽度通过不确定关系联系：寿命越短，粒子质量分布越宽，Γ 约等于 ħ/τ。衰变是否发生、走哪条道，由守恒律和相互作用强度决定：强作用衰变最快（典型 10⁻²³ 秒），电磁作用次之，弱作用最慢，因此 μ 子、中子这类弱衰变粒子寿命更长。粒子能衰变到更轻、守恒允许的末态就会衰变；找不到这样的末态，就保持稳定。Lifetime and mass width are linked by the uncertainty relation: the shorter the lifetime, the broader the mass distribution, with Γ about ħ/τ. Whether a decay occurs and which channel it follows is set by conservation laws and interaction strength: strong decays are fastest (typically 10⁻²³ s), electromagnetic decays next, weak decays slowest, which is why weakly decaying particles such as the muon and neutron live longer. A particle decays if a lighter, conservation-allowed final state exists; otherwise it stays stable.[2][7]

日常物质由稳定或长寿命粒子构成：质子、中子（核内受束缚的中子寿命可很长）和电子。宇宙中自由中子只能短暂存在，核结合改变了它的稳定性。探测器寻找质子衰变的思路，是在深水下降低宇宙线本底，识别衰变产物的切连科夫光；几十年未观测到事例，不断抬高寿命下限，也约束了各类大统一模型的参数空间。Everyday matter is built from stable or long-lived particles: protons, neutrons (bound inside nuclei, where they can last very long) and electrons. A free neutron in space exists only briefly, and nuclear binding changes its stability. Proton-decay detectors reduce cosmic-ray background deep underground and identify Cherenkov light from decay products; decades without an event keep raising the lifetime limit and constrain the parameter space of grand unified models.[2][6]

“下限”不等于“精确寿命”：目前没有测到质子衰变，只能说在给定衰变道和探测器灵敏度下，寿命高于某个值。不同衰变道的下限不同，不能合并成一个普适数字。弱衰变寿命受耦合常数和相空间影响，不能只看粒子质量大小。把“质子最终会衰变”当成已证实结论不严谨；现状是理论有动机、实验未观测。A "lower limit" is not a precise lifetime: no proton decay has been seen, so one can only say that for a given channel and detector sensitivity the lifetime exceeds a value. Limits differ by channel and cannot be merged into one universal number. Weak-decay lifetime depends on coupling constant and phase space, not only on particle mass. Treating "the proton will eventually decay" as proven is too strong; the current state is theoretical motivation without experimental observation.

03中微子有几种，振荡为什么说明它有质量？中微子是自己的反粒子吗？How many neutrino flavours are there, why does oscillation imply mass, and is a neutrino its own antiparticle?

中微子分电子、μ、τ 三味，振荡要求质量非零；是否为自身反粒子，待无中微子双 β 衰变判定。Three neutrino flavours oscillate, requiring nonzero mass; Dirac or Majorana nature awaits neutrinoless double-beta decay.

中微子是参与弱相互作用的轻子，分电子中微子、μ 中微子、τ 中微子三种味。太阳、反应堆、加速器和大气宇宙线都能产生中微子；它几乎不与物质作用，穿透能力极强。早期标准模型曾把中微子当作无质量粒子，后来的振荡观测改变了这一点。中微子几乎不带电，只参与弱作用和引力作用，因此穿透整颗地球也只发生极少数相互作用，探测需要巨大的靶物质或长基线装置。Neutrinos are leptons that interact through the weak force, in three flavours: electron, muon and tau neutrino. The Sun, reactors, accelerators and atmospheric cosmic rays all produce them; they barely interact with matter and penetrate extremely well. The early Standard Model treated neutrinos as massless, until oscillation observations changed that view.[5]

中微子在传播中会从一种味振荡成另一种味。2015 年诺贝尔物理学奖颁给证实大气中微子与太阳中微子振荡的观测。量子力学上，味本征态是质量本征态的混合；只有不同质量本征态之间存在质量差，传播相位差才会造成味振荡。因此振荡被观测到，就意味着中微子具有非零质量，尽管三种中微子质量之和仍远小于电子质量。In flight, a neutrino oscillates from one flavour to another. The 2015 Nobel Prize in Physics recognised observations confirming atmospheric and solar neutrino oscillation. Quantum mechanically, flavour eigenstates are mixtures of mass eigenstates; only when the mass eigenstates differ in mass can propagation phase differences produce flavour oscillation. So observed oscillation implies nonzero neutrino mass, although the sum of the three neutrino masses remains far below the electron mass.[1][5]

一个悬而未决的问题是：中微子是不是自己的反粒子。若中微子是狄拉克粒子，它与反中微子是两种粒子；若是马约拉纳粒子，中微子本身即反粒子。判别途径是寻找无中微子双 β 衰变：原子核同时衰变放出两个电子而不放中微子，只有当中微子是马约拉纳粒子时这种过程才允许。目前没有可靠观测到该过程，两种可能都保留。An open question is whether a neutrino is its own antiparticle. If it is a Dirac particle, neutrino and antineutrino are two distinct particles; if Majorana, the neutrino is itself the antiparticle. The test is to search for neutrinoless double-beta decay: a nucleus decays emitting two electrons but no neutrino, a process allowed only if the neutrino is Majorana. No such process has been reliably observed yet, so both possibilities remain open.[3][5]

反物质由反粒子组成：反质子带负电、正电子带正电，反氢由反质子和正电子构成。物质与反物质相遇会湮灭为光子等能量形式；CERN 的反氢装置长期囚禁反氢，测量其光谱与相互作用。宇宙最大的谜题之一是正反物质不对称：大爆炸应产生等量正反物质，今天宇宙却以普通物质为主，机制尚未解释。Antimatter is built from antiparticles: the antiproton carries negative charge, the positron positive charge, and antihydrogen combines an antiproton with a positron. Matter and antimatter annihilate into photons and other energy forms; CERN's antihydrogen apparatus traps antihydrogen for long periods to measure its spectrum and interactions. One of cosmology's puzzles is matter-antimatter asymmetry: the Big Bang should have produced equal amounts, yet today's Universe is dominated by ordinary matter, and the mechanism is unexplained.[3]

振荡说明的是质量差和非零质量，没有直接给出每种中微子的绝对质量；质量顺序（正常或倒置）仍在测量。马约拉纳属性一旦证实会改写轻子数守恒的理解，但阴性结果只能压低相关衰变率。反物质研究不支持“反物质可以做燃料”的通俗想象：囚禁和制造反物质的成本极高，湮灭释放的能量远低于投入。Oscillation establishes mass differences and nonzero mass, not the absolute mass of each neutrino; the mass ordering (normal or inverted) is still being measured. A confirmed Majorana nature would rewrite the understanding of lepton-number conservation, but null results only bound the decay rate. Antimatter research does not support the popular idea of antimatter fuel: trapping and producing it costs enormously more than the annihilation energy returned.

04暗物质是什么？我们凭什么断定它存在？What is dark matter, and what evidence tells us it exists?

旋转曲线、引力透镜和 CMB 显示存在不发光的引力物质；普朗克测得暗物质约 26.8%，粒子身份尚未探测到。Rotation curves, lensing and the CMB show unseen gravitating matter; Planck puts dark matter at 26.8%, but the particle is undetected.

暗物质是指不发射或吸收可探测电磁波、却通过引力影响可见物质的物质。它不是黑洞、尘埃或云雾这些普通暗天体的统称；观测要求它主要不参与电磁相互作用。天文学从多个独立方向推断出它的存在，而不是只靠某一张星系图。Dark matter is matter that emits or absorbs no detectable electromagnetic radiation yet affects visible matter through gravity. It is not a catch-all for ordinary dark objects such as black holes, dust or clouds; observations require it to take little part in electromagnetic interactions. Astronomy infers it from several independent directions, not from a single galaxy picture.[4][7][8]

星系外围恒星的轨道速度没有按开普勒规律随距离下降，而是保持平坦。若发光物质就是全部质量，外围恒星应当转得更慢；平坦曲线意味着星系外围包裹着一个看不见的质量晕。星系团的引力透镜、星系团内气体的位力质量也给出同样结论：可见物质只占引力所需质量的一小部分。位力定理给出的星系团总质量远大于发光气体与星系质量之和，两套独立观测指向同一结论。Orbital speeds of stars at a galaxy's outer edge do not decline with distance as Kepler's laws predict; they stay flat. If luminous matter were all the mass, outer stars should orbit more slowly; the flat curve means the galaxy is wrapped in an unseen mass halo. Galaxy-cluster gravitational lensing and the virial mass of intracluster gas give the same conclusion: visible matter is only a small fraction of the mass required by gravity.[4][8]

宇宙成分由宇宙微波背景和膨胀观测约束。欧空局普朗克卫星给出：普通物质约 4.9%，暗物质约 26.8%，暗能量约 68.3%。暗物质候选包括弱相互作用大质量粒子（WIMP）、轴子、惰性中微子等；大质量致密晕天体（MACHO）这类普通天体经过微透镜巡天后基本被排除为主要成分。地下探测器、对撞机和天文巡天正在不同方向寻找直接或间接信号；地下实验屏蔽宇宙线，等待暗物质粒子与靶核发生的稀有碰撞。Cosmic composition is constrained by the cosmic microwave background and expansion observations. ESA's Planck mission measures about 4.9% ordinary matter, 26.8% dark matter and 68.3% dark energy. Dark-matter candidates include weakly interacting massive particles (WIMPs), axions and sterile neutrinos; compact ordinary objects such as MACHOs have largely been ruled out as the main component by microlensing surveys. Underground detectors, colliders and sky surveys search for direct or indirect signals along different routes.[4]

曾经的替代解释包括修改引力定律（如 MOND），它能部分拟合星系旋转曲线，但难以同时解释星系团引力透镜和宇宙微波背景的大尺度结构。目前主流框架是 ΛCDM：冷暗物质加宇宙学常数。暗能量是另一类问题：它驱动宇宙加速膨胀，与暗物质不是同一种东西，不能合并描述。Alternative explanations once included modified gravity (such as MOND), which partly fits galaxy rotation curves but struggles to account for cluster lensing and the large-scale structure of the CMB at the same time. The mainstream framework today is ΛCDM: cold dark matter plus a cosmological constant. Dark energy is a separate problem driving accelerated expansion; it is not the same thing as dark matter and cannot be described together.[4][8]

暗物质的“存在”由引力效应间接证明，但其微观本质未知；直接探测实验尚未给出公认信号。比例数字来自特定宇宙学模型下的拟合，随数据更新会小幅调整。把暗物质说成“外星人能量”或“时空幻象”没有证据支撑；当前能说的是：宇宙中有大量不发光的质量，其粒子身份仍是物理待解问题。The "existence" of dark matter is proven indirectly through gravitational effects, while its microscopic nature remains unknown; direct-detection experiments have produced no accepted signal yet. The percentage figures come from fits within a particular cosmological model and shift slightly as data improve. Calling dark matter "alien energy" or "an illusion of spacetime" has no evidential basis; what can be said is that the Universe holds large amounts of non-luminous mass, and its particle identity remains an open physics problem.
