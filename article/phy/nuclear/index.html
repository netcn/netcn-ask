01为什么铀-235 俘获一个中子后会裂变，链式反应靠什么维持？Why does uranium-235 fission after capturing a neutron, and what sustains the chain reaction?

慢中子俘获 U-235 后重核分裂，放出约 200 MeV 和 2.43 个中子；临界质量由截面、富集度与几何条件决定。A slow neutron captured by U-235 splits the nucleus, releasing about 200 MeV and 2.43 neutrons; critical mass depends on cross section, enrichment and geometry.

铀-235 俘获一个慢中子后会裂成两个中等质量的裂变碎片，同时放出能量和两三个新中子。裂变释放的能量来自质量亏损：产物的总静质量小于原始中子加铀核的静质量，差额按 E=mc² 折算，每次裂变约放出 200 MeV（约 3.2×10⁻¹¹ J）。新中子若再被其他铀-235 核俘获，就能触发下一轮裂变，这就是链式反应；反应堆用慢化剂把快中子慢化成热中子，用控制棒吸收多余中子来维持平稳反应。When uranium-235 captures a slow neutron it splits into two medium-mass fission fragments, releasing energy and two or three new neutrons. The energy comes from a mass defect: the rest mass of the products is smaller than that of the original neutron plus uranium nucleus, and the difference converts through E=mc² into about 200 MeV, roughly 3.2×10⁻¹¹ J, per fission. If the new neutrons are captured by other U-235 nuclei they trigger the next fission, which is the chain reaction. A reactor uses a moderator to slow fast neutrons to thermal energies and control rods to soak up the surplus neutrons and keep the reaction steady.[1][4]

截面描述一个核发生某种反应的概率大小。铀-235 对热中子的裂变截面约 580 barn，1 barn 等于 10⁻²⁸ m²；截面越大，中子撞上并触发裂变的概率越高。天然铀里铀-235 只占约 0.72%，其余是不易热中子裂变的铀-238，所以反应堆燃料要富集到 3%–5%，武器级材料则通常超过 90%。每次裂变平均放出约 2.43 个中子，扣除泄漏和被吸收的部分后，有效倍增系数 k 等于 1 时为临界，大于 1 为超临界，小于 1 链式反应会逐渐熄灭。The cross section describes how likely a nucleus is to undergo a reaction. The thermal-neutron fission cross section of U-235 is about 580 barns, one barn being 10⁻²⁸ m²; a larger cross section means a higher chance the neutron collides and causes fission. Natural uranium is only about 0.72% U-235, the rest being U-238 which does not fission easily with thermal neutrons, so reactor fuel is enriched to 3%–5% and weapons-grade material usually above 90%. Each fission releases on average about 2.43 neutrons. After leakage and parasitic absorption, the effective multiplication factor k equals 1 at criticality, exceeds 1 when supercritical, and falls below 1 when the chain dies out.[1][6]

维持链式反应需要足够多的裂变材料，材料太少时中子泄漏出去的比例太高。裸球临界质量对高富集铀-235 约 50 kg 量级，钚-239 约 10 kg 量级；加上中子反射层、提高富集度或改善几何形状，临界质量都会下降。临界质量不是固定常数，它随截面、密度、反射条件和中子能谱变化：密度越高，核间距越小，中子被及时俘获的概率越大，所需质量越小。A self-sustaining chain reaction needs enough fissile material; if the piece is too small, too many neutrons escape before capture. For a bare sphere the critical mass is on the order of 50 kg for highly enriched U-235 and about 10 kg for Pu-239. A neutron reflector, higher enrichment or a better geometry all lower the required mass. Critical mass is not a fixed constant: it depends on cross section, density, reflection and neutron spectrum. Higher density shrinks the spacing between nuclei, raising timely capture probability and lowering the needed mass.[1][6]

裂变能的利用是可控链式反应：反应堆把每次裂变产生的热导出，加热水或产生蒸汽推动发电设备；瞬发中子在裂变后立即放出，缓发中子在后续衰变中放出，后者让反应堆功率变化有足够时间被调节。原子弹则把两块或多块次临界材料迅速压成超临界状态，在解体前完成多次倍增。OpenStax 核物理章节把临界质量、倍增系数与慢化过程作为裂变的定量主线，NNDC 数据库提供各核素中子截面与衰变数据供核验。Power generation uses a controlled chain reaction: the heat from each fission is carried away to heat water or make steam that drives generators. Prompt neutrons are released immediately after fission, delayed neutrons later in the daughter decays; the delayed fraction gives reactor operators time to adjust power. A bomb assembles several subcritical pieces into a supercritical mass quickly enough for many generations to multiply before the device tears apart. The OpenStax nuclear physics text treats critical mass, multiplication and moderation as the quantitative thread of fission, while the NNDC database supplies verified neutron cross sections and decay data.[1]

边界：裂变不是把原子“打碎成质子中子”，而是重核分裂为中等质量碎片；聚变才是轻核结合成更重的核。截面数值对应特定中子能谱，热中子截面大不代表快中子截面也大。临界质量的数量级随材料和几何条件改变，不能把某个裸球数值套用到反射层包裹的反应堆堆芯上。链式反应能自持的前提是中子平衡，而不是材料简单堆到一起。Boundary: fission does not smash an atom into protons and neutrons; it splits a heavy nucleus into medium-mass fragments, whereas fusion joins light nuclei. Cross section values belong to a specific neutron spectrum, so a large thermal cross section does not imply a large fast-neutron cross section. The bare-sphere critical mass order of magnitude must not be carried over to a reflected reactor core. A self-sustaining reaction rests on neutron balance, not merely on piling material together.[1]

02原子内部到底有多“空”，几乎全部质量集中在哪里？How empty is the interior of an atom, and where is almost all its mass concentrated?

原子半径约 0.1 nm，核半径仅 1–7 fm；99.9% 以上质量集中在核内，核外是电子概率云。The atom is about 0.1 nm across, the nucleus 1–7 fm; over 99.9% of the mass sits in the nucleus, surrounded by an electron cloud.

原子看上去是实心小球，实际上内部绝大部分空间是空的。原子半径约 0.1 nm（10⁻¹⁰ m），而原子核半径只有 1–7 fm（10⁻¹⁵ m）量级；沿半径方向原子核比原子小约一万到十万倍，按体积计算原子核只占原子体积的约 10⁻¹² 到 10⁻¹⁵。把原子放大到足球场大小，原子核大概只是中场附近一颗豌豆，电子则分布在整张看台附近的概率云里。An atom looks like a solid ball, but most of its interior is empty space. The atomic radius is about 0.1 nm (10⁻¹⁰ m), whereas the nucleus spans only 1–7 fm (10⁻¹⁵ m). Along a radius the nucleus is ten thousand to a hundred thousand times smaller, and by volume it occupies roughly 10⁻¹² to 10⁻¹⁵ of the atom. Scale an atom to the size of a football stadium and the nucleus is about a pea near midfield, while the electron occupies a probability cloud over the surrounding stands.[5][6]

原子的质量几乎全部集中在原子核里。质子和中子质量各约为电子质量的 1800 多倍，电子质量只占原子总质量的千分之几；即使是最轻的氢原子，原子核也承担了超过 99.9% 的质量。原子核由质子（决定元素种类）和中子（决定同位素）组成，核外电子通过电磁相互作用被束缚在核周围。原子不显电性，是因为核内正电荷与核外负电荷总量相等。Almost all the atomic mass sits in the nucleus. A proton or neutron is over 1800 times heavier than an electron, and electrons account for only a few thousandths of the atomic mass; even in the lightest hydrogen atom the nucleus holds more than 99.9% of the mass. The nucleus is made of protons, which fix the element, and neutrons, which fix the isotope; electrons are bound around it by the electromagnetic interaction. An atom is electrically neutral because the positive nuclear charge and the negative electron charge balance exactly.[4][5]

卢瑟福 1911 年的金箔实验改变了对原子的认识。高速 α 粒子射向极薄金箔，绝大多数直接穿过，说明原子内部大部分区域没有密集物质；少数 α 粒子大角度偏转，甚至被反弹回来，只能用一个很小、很重、带正电的核来解释。在此之前流行的“葡萄干布丁”模型认为正电荷均匀分布，无法解释大角度散射。这个实验把原子从均匀实心体改成了“核加外层云”的结构。Rutherford's 1911 gold-foil experiment reshaped the idea of the atom. High-speed alpha particles struck a very thin gold foil: most passed straight through, showing that most of the atom holds no dense matter, while a few deflected at large angles or bounced back, which could only be explained by a tiny, heavy, positively charged nucleus. The earlier plum-pudding model spread positive charge uniformly and could not account for large-angle scattering. The experiment replaced a uniformly solid atom with a nucleus plus an outer cloud.[5]

核外电子并不是绕核做圆周运动的小弹珠。量子力学用概率密度（电子云）描述电子位置：靠近原子核的区域概率密度高，远处逐渐变稀；不同量子数对应不同形状的轨道分布。宏观物体由海量原子组成，原子之间看似接触，其实是电子云之间的电磁排斥在起作用；日常所说的“实心”是力学与电磁相互作用的结果，不是物质填满了空间。OpenStax 原子结构章节整理了电子与原子核发现的实验链条，NIST 原子光谱数据则对应核外电子的能级结构。Outer electrons are not tiny marbles circling the nucleus. Quantum mechanics describes their location with a probability density, the electron cloud: density is high near the nucleus and thins outward, and different quantum numbers give different orbital shapes. Macroscopic objects are built from huge numbers of atoms; surfaces that seem to touch are actually kept apart by electromagnetic repulsion between clouds. What we call solidity is the result of mechanical and electromagnetic interactions, not matter filling space. The OpenStax atomic-structure chapter traces the experimental discovery of electrons and the nucleus, and NIST atomic spectral data correspond to the electron level structure.[5]

边界：说原子“很空”是指质量与电荷集中在极小的核内，电子云仍以概率方式填满整个原子范围，不能理解为电子可以在原子内任意穿行。原子核虽然小，却几乎承担全部质量，也决定了元素与同位素身份；化学反应只涉及核外电子，不触动原子核。把行星式轨道当作电子真实轨迹是早期模型的局限，现代描述以概率密度为准。Boundary: calling an atom empty means mass and charge are concentrated in a tiny nucleus; the electron cloud still occupies the whole atomic extent probabilistically, so it does not follow that an electron moves freely inside the atom. Tiny as it is, the nucleus carries nearly all the mass and fixes element and isotope; chemical reactions involve only outer electrons and leave the nucleus untouched. Planetary orbits are an early-model limitation, and the modern description rests on probability density.[5]

03为什么单个原子核何时衰变无法预测，半衰期却能测得很准？Why cannot one predict when a single nucleus decays, yet the half-life can be measured accurately?

衰变是概率量子过程，单核不可预测；大量样本服从指数律，半衰期统计上精确：C-14 约 5730 年。Decay is probabilistic and unpredictable for one nucleus, but a large sample follows the exponential law, so the half-life is statistically precise.

放射性衰变是原子核自发放出 α、β 或 γ 辐射、转变成另一种核素的过程。衰变服从指数规律：剩余核数 N=N₀e^(−λt)，其中 λ 是衰变常数；半衰期 T½=ln2/λ，即一半核发生衰变所需时间。活度 A=λN，单位是贝可勒尔（Bq），每秒一次衰变记为 1 Bq。每过一个半衰期，剩下的核数减半，再下一个半衰期再减半，理论上永远不会精确归零，只是剩余量越来越小。Radioactive decay is the spontaneous emission of alpha, beta or gamma radiation by a nucleus, transforming it into another nuclide. It follows an exponential law: the remaining number is N=N₀e^(−λt), where λ is the decay constant, and the half-life T½=ln2/λ, the time needed for half the nuclei to decay. Activity A=λN is measured in becquerels (Bq), one decay per second being 1 Bq. Each half-life halves what remains, and the next half-life halves it again; in principle it never reaches exactly zero, only grows smaller.[3]

单个原子核什么时候衰变无法预测：放射性是量子过程，只能给出概率。但对数量足够大的样本，统计规律非常精确，半衰期可以测到很高精度。不同核素半衰期相差悬殊：碳-14 约 5730 年，铀-238 约 44.7 亿年，碘-131 约 8.02 天，钴-60 约 5.27 年。半衰期由核内部结构决定，温度、压力、化学状态等外部条件几乎不改变它；改变的只是活度随时间下降的快慢。When an individual nucleus decays cannot be predicted: radioactivity is a quantum process that yields only probabilities. For a sample large enough statistically, however, the law is precise and the half-life can be measured to high accuracy. Nuclides differ enormously: carbon-14 about 5730 years, uranium-238 about 4.47 billion years, iodine-131 about 8.02 days, cobalt-60 about 5.27 years. The half-life is set by the nuclear interior; temperature, pressure and chemical state hardly change it, though they affect how quickly activity falls away.[3][6]

碳-14 测年利用了这种稳定性。活体生物与大气交换碳，体内碳-14 比例保持稳定；死亡后交换停止，剩余碳-14 按 5730 年半衰期减少，测量残留比例即可估算年代。碳-14 方法适用上限大约五六万年，更老的样品需要铀系、钾-氩等长寿命母核。OpenStax 半衰期与活度章节给出 N=N₀e^(−λt)、λ=0.693/T½ 与活度 R=λN 的完整关系，NNDC 收录各核素半衰期、衰变方式与辐射能量。Radiocarbon dating relies on this stability. A living organism exchanges carbon with the atmosphere and keeps a steady C-14 ratio; after death exchange stops and the remaining C-14 declines with its 5730-year half-life, so measuring the residual fraction estimates the age. The method reaches back roughly 50,000–60,000 years; older samples need long-lived parents such as uranium-series or potassium-argon systems. The OpenStax half-life and activity section lays out N=N₀e^(−λt), λ=0.693/T½ and activity R=λN, and NNDC records half-lives, decay modes and radiation energies.[3][6]

衰变放出的能量来自衰变前后的质量亏损。α 衰变中重核放出氦核；β 衰变中中子与质子互相转化并放出电子或正电子；γ 衰变是处于激发态的核以光子形式释放多余能量。α 粒子之所以能穿过库仑势垒逸出，是量子隧穿效应：势垒内波函数指数衰减，仍有小概率出现在核外。半衰期与 α 粒子能量密切相关，能量略高，隧穿概率显著增大，半衰期可短到微秒、长到数十亿年。Decay energy comes from the mass difference before and after. In alpha decay a heavy nucleus emits a helium nucleus; beta decay converts neutron and proton into one another with an electron or positron; gamma decay releases excess energy as a photon from an excited nucleus. An alpha particle escapes the Coulomb barrier through quantum tunnelling: the wave function decays exponentially inside the barrier but still has a small probability of appearing outside. Half-life correlates strongly with alpha energy: slightly higher energy raises tunnelling probability sharply, and half-lives range from microseconds to billions of years.[4][7]

边界：半衰期是大量核的统计量，不能说“一个碳-14 核在 5730 年后衰变”。放射性活度随时间下降，不代表辐射危害线性下降，不同辐射的穿透能力和生物效应差别很大。外部条件不改变半衰期，但会改变样品中核素的化学分布与环境本底，测年时需要校正。衰变链中的子核也会继续衰变，总活度不能只看母核一种核素。Boundary: half-life is a statistical quantity for a large population; one cannot say a single C-14 nucleus decays after 5730 years. Falling activity does not mean radiation hazard falls linearly, since penetration and biological effect differ greatly by radiation type. External conditions do not alter the half-life but do change chemical distribution and background, which dating must correct. Daughter nuclides in a decay chain keep decaying, so total activity cannot be read from the parent alone.[3]

04核废料为什么长期保持放射性，受控聚变目前卡在哪里？Why does nuclear waste stay radioactive so long, and what still holds back controlled fusion?

裂变产物几天到几十年，锕系万年以上；氘氚聚变放约 17.6 MeV，但高温约束、材料与氚自持尚未解决。Fission products live days to decades, actinides millennia; D-T fusion releases 17.6 MeV but confinement, materials and tritium supply remain unresolved.

核废料是裂变反应堆运行和后处理留下的放射性物质，分为低中放废物与高放废物。高放废物主要是乏燃料里的裂变产物和活化锕系核素：裂变产物半衰期从几天到几十年居多，如碘-131 八天、铯-137 约 30 年、锶-90 约 29 年；锕系元素如钚、镎的半衰期可达万年到数十万年量级。短寿命核素衰变快、余热大，长寿命锕系核素需要长期隔离，两者处置思路不同。Nuclear waste is the radioactive material left by reactor operation and reprocessing, split into low- and intermediate-level waste and high-level waste. High-level waste mainly holds fission products and activated actinides in spent fuel: fission products mostly have half-lives from days to decades, such as iodine-131 at eight days, caesium-137 about 30 years and strontium-90 about 29 years; actinides like plutonium and neptunium range from thousands to hundreds of thousands of years. Short-lived nuclides decay quickly and carry strong decay heat, while long-lived actinides need long-term isolation, so the two call for different handling.[6]

处置思路是多重屏障：废物先固化（玻璃固化或陶瓷固化），装入金属容器，再放入稳定地质岩体的深地质处置库，上方覆盖黏土、膨润土和岩层作为缓冲。芬兰 Onkalo 处置库是较早进入运行阶段的深地质库概念；设计要求在万年级别时间内把核素与生物圈隔开。废料刚卸出反应堆时衰变热显著，需要在水池或干式容器中冷却数年到数十年，再转入永久处置。OpenStax 结合裂变与结合能章节说明核素稳定性和能量来源，NNDC 提供各核素半衰期供隔离年限估算。Disposal uses multiple barriers: waste is solidified into glass or ceramic, sealed in metal containers, then placed in a deep geological repository in stable rock, buffered by clay, bentonite and overlying strata. Finland's Onkalo is an early deep-geological facility approaching operation; the design must isolate nuclides from the biosphere over millennia. Freshly discharged fuel carries strong decay heat and is cooled in pools or dry casks for years to decades before permanent disposal. OpenStax chapters on fission and binding energy explain nuclear stability and energy source, and NNDC supplies half-lives for isolation-time estimates.[1]

受控核聚变与裂变相反，是轻核结合成较重核。最容易实现的是氘氚反应：²H+³H→⁴He+n，放出约 17.6 MeV，大部分能量由中子带走。聚变需要克服两个正电荷之间的库仑排斥，等离子体温度要达到约 1 亿 K 量级，并在足够密度下约束足够长时间，才能让聚变功率超过加热功率（Q>1）。现有实验装置仍在追求能量增益与稳态约束，材料承受高中子辐照、氚自持等工程问题尚未完全解决。Controlled fusion is the reverse of fission: light nuclei join into a heavier one. The easiest reaction is deuterium-tritium, ²H+³H→⁴He+n, releasing about 17.6 MeV, most carried by the neutron. Fusion must overcome Coulomb repulsion between two positive charges, so the plasma reaches roughly 100 million K and stays dense and confined long enough for fusion power to exceed heating power, Q>1. Present experiments are still pursuing energy gain and steady confinement; materials under intense neutron bombardment and tritium self-supply remain unresolved engineering problems.[2][4]

地球上曾出现过天然核裂变反应堆。非洲加蓬奥克洛铀矿约 17 亿年前，地下水充当慢化剂，铀-235 丰度当时更高，局部矿层形成自持链式反应并间歇运行了数十万年。这一证据来自残留的裂变产物比例，说明天然条件下也可能出现裂变。聚变则在恒星内部持续发生：太阳核心约 1500 万 K、高压条件下质子链反应释放能量，这是地球阳光的来源。A natural fission reactor once operated on Earth. About 1.7 billion years ago in the Oklo deposit in Gabon, groundwater acted as moderator and the U-235 abundance was higher than today; local ore sustained an intermittent chain reaction for hundreds of thousands of years, evidenced by the residual fission-product ratios. Fusion, by contrast, continues inside stars: the Sun's core, at about 15 million K under high pressure, releases energy through the proton-proton chain, the source of sunlight.[2]

边界：核废料的长期放射性来自核素本身衰变，不会靠“放置”变成无毒，只能随时间降低活度；固化和地质处置是延缓迁移，不是消除核素。聚变废料的活化结构件仍有放射性，但乏燃料里的长寿命锕系负担远小于裂变。说聚变“完全无废料”不准确，工程上比较的是放射性负担与半衰期长短。天然反应堆是远古地质条件下的偶然产物，不能作为现代反应堆设计依据。Boundary: long-term radioactivity comes from nuclide decay itself; storage does not make waste harmless, only lowers activity over time, and solidification plus geological disposal delays migration rather than removing nuclides. Fusion leaves activated structural components radioactive, but its long-lived actinide burden is far smaller than fission's, so calling fusion waste-free is inaccurate; engineers compare burden and half-life length. The natural reactor was a one-off ancient geological accident and is not a modern reactor design basis.[1]
