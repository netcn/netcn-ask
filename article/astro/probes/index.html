01罗塞塔号怎样环绕并登陆 67P 彗星，这次任务改变了我们对彗星的什么认识？How did Rosetta orbit and land on comet 67P, and what did it change about our view of comets?

罗塞塔号首次环绕彗星并释放着陆器，在彗发中检出甘氨酸等有机分子，细化了彗星的碎石堆模型。Rosetta was the first comet orbiter and lander; glycine and other organics in the coma refined the rubble-pile picture of comets.

罗塞塔号是人类第一次环绕一颗彗星并向其表面投放着陆器的任务。它在 2004 年 3 月 2 日发射，经过十年飞行和多次引力借力，于 2014 年 8 月 6 日抵达 67P/丘留莫夫-格拉西缅科彗星附近，进入环绕轨道，11 月 12 日释放菲莱着陆器，2016 年 9 月 30 日受控降落在彗星表面。此前人类对彗星的认识主要来自飞掠拍照和光谱观测，罗塞塔号则把轨道器和着陆器放到彗星身边，长期跟踪它走向近日点的全过程。Rosetta was the first mission to orbit a comet and deploy a lander onto its surface. It launched on 2 March 2004, spent ten years in flight with several gravity assists, and arrived beside comet 67P/Churyumov–Gerasimenko on 6 August 2014. It entered orbit around the nucleus, released the Philae lander on 12 November 2014, and ended its mission with a controlled touchdown on 30 September 2016. Before Rosetta, knowledge of comets came mostly from flyby photographs and remote spectra; Rosetta placed an orbiter and a lander alongside the comet to follow it through its approach toward the Sun.[1]

任务的工程难点在于彗星太小、引力太弱。67P 核体只有几公里宽，没有大气，表面凹凸不平，轨道器不能像环绕行星那样靠大气减速，只能靠发动机逐步调整速度，与彗星以相对速度接近同步的方式伴飞。菲莱着陆器第一次触地后弹起，落到一处背光的岩壁之间，电力很快耗尽，工作时间远短于预期；轨道器则一直留在彗星身边，传回超过 220 GB 数据和十万余张图像，直到任务结束时主动着陆。The engineering challenge was that the comet was tiny and its gravity was weak. The nucleus is only a few kilometres across, has no atmosphere, and an uneven surface, so the orbiter could not use aerobraking the way a planetary probe does. It had to adjust its speed with thrusters and fly in near-synchronism with the comet. Philae bounced after its first touchdown and settled in a shadowed cleft between cliffs, losing power long before its design lifetime. The orbiter stayed alongside instead, returning more than 220 GB of data and over a hundred thousand images before its planned surface touchdown.[1][2]

从图像上看，67P 是一颗双瓣形、表面布满砾石和沟壑的小天体。经典的脏雪球模型把彗星描述成冰与尘埃的混合体，罗塞塔号的观测把这幅画面细化为：核体是一团松散堆积的碎石与冰，表面大部分被尘埃覆盖，冰只在陡壁、坑壁和活动喷流处暴露；靠近太阳时，挥发分受热升华，裹挟尘埃形成彗发和尾。核体的双瓣结构一般被解释为两个较小的团块低速碰撞后并合而成。The images show a two-lobed nucleus covered in boulders, pits and grooves. The classic dirty-snowball model describes a mixture of ice and dust; Rosetta refined that picture into a loose rubble pile of dust and ice. Most of the surface is draped in dust, with ice exposed on steep walls, crater slopes and active jets. As the comet neared the Sun, ices sublimated and carried dust into the coma and tail. The two-lobed shape is generally explained as the merger of two smaller bodies in a slow, gentle collision.[2]

化学成分是这次任务最受关注的部分。轨道器上的 ROSINA 质谱仪在彗发中反复检测到甘氨酸——最简单的氨基酸——以及甲基胺、乙基胺和磷等多种有机分子。甘氨酸是构成蛋白质的原料，磷是 DNA 和细胞膜的关键元素；这说明彗星携带过生命起源所需的部分前体物质。但要注意，这些分子是在彗发气体中测到的，只能说明彗星具备相关化学成分，不能直接证明地球上的生命就是由彗星带来的。Chemistry was the most closely watched result. The ROSINA mass spectrometer on the orbiter repeatedly detected glycine, the simplest amino acid, together with methylamine, ethylamine and phosphorus in the coma. Glycine is a building block of proteins, and phosphorus is essential to DNA and cell membranes, so comets clearly carried some of the prebiotic ingredients life requires. The detection was made in coma gas, however; it shows that comets hold the relevant chemistry, but it does not by itself prove that life on Earth arrived aboard comets.[3]

边界也要写清。罗塞塔号只造访了一颗彗星，不同彗星的成分和结构可能差别很大；它测得的水同位素比例与地球海洋并不完全吻合，因此彗星到底给地球带来了多少水，学界仍在争论。任务没有采样返回，所有成分判断都依赖原位遥感和质谱，精度有限。把一颗彗星的结论推广到所有彗星，或把有机分子等同于生命已出现，都超出了证据；后续采样返回任务才可能回答这些遗留问题。The limits matter. Rosetta visited one comet, and other comets may differ substantially in composition and structure. Its measured water-isotope ratio does not match Earth's ocean exactly, so how much water comets delivered to Earth remains debated. The mission had no sample return, and every chemical conclusion rests on in-situ remote sensing and mass spectrometry with limited precision. Generalising from one comet to all comets, or treating organic molecules as proof that life appeared, goes beyond the evidence; future sample-return missions are what could settle these open questions.[2][3]

02DART 撞击 Dimorphos 怎样改变了它的轨道？为什么这算一次行星防御实验？How did the DART impact change Dimorphos' orbit, and why counts as a planetary-defence test?

动能撞击加上抛射物反冲，把 Dimorphos 公转周期缩短约 33 分钟，首次实测验证小行星偏转可行。The kinetic impact plus ejecta recoil shortened Dimorphos' period by about 33 minutes, the first real test of asteroid deflection.

DART 是人类第一次在真实小行星上测试动能偏转方案。2021 年 11 月 24 日发射，2022 年 9 月 26 日以高速撞上双小行星系统中的 Dimorphos，目的不是摧毁小行星，而是用撞击动量轻轻改变它的轨道。任务成功把 Dimorphos 绕母星 Didymos 的公转周期缩短了约 33 分钟，证明主动偏转近地天体在工程上可行，为未来可能出现的威胁提供了一条可验证的技术路径。DART was the first real-world test of kinetic deflection on an asteroid. It launched on 24 November 2021 and struck Dimorphos, the smaller member of a double-asteroid system, at high speed on 26 September 2022. The goal was not to destroy the asteroid but to nudge its orbit with impact momentum. The mission shortened Dimorphos' orbital period around its parent Didymos by about 33 minutes, showing that actively deflecting a near-Earth object is engineering-feasible and providing a verifiable technical path for a future threat.[4]

目标系统本身没有威胁。Didymos 母星直径约 780 米，卫星 Dimorphos 约 160 米，二者当时都不构成对地球的撞击风险。选择双小行星作为靶场有一个关键好处：偏转效果可以通过公转周期精确测量。撞击只改变 Dimorphos 的轨道速度，幅度只有毫米每秒量级，但轨道周期对速度变化很敏感，地面望远镜靠亮度变化记录公转一圈的时间，即可反推动量转移量。The target system posed no threat. The parent Didymos is about 780 metres across and Dimorphos about 160 metres, and neither was on an Earth-impacting trajectory. A binary system was chosen for a key reason: the deflection can be measured precisely through the orbital period. The impact changes Dimorphos' orbital speed by only millimetres per second, but the period is sensitive to that speed change. Ground telescopes track brightness variations to time each orbit around Didymos, and from that timing the momentum transfer can be inferred.[4]

偏转的物理机制是动量转移，而且不能只算撞击器本身。DART 撞击器把动量交给小行星表面，更重要的是撞击瞬间炸出大量碎屑，碎屑向外喷出时产生反冲，进一步把小行星往相反方向推。动量增强因子 β 衡量这种额外反冲的贡献，DART 观测到的 β 大约在 2.2 到 4.9 之间，意思是碎屑带走的动量是撞击器初始动量的数倍。靶体表面越松散、喷出物越多，反冲贡献越大；这也是为什么小行星的内部结构会影响偏转效率。The mechanism is momentum transfer, and the impactor alone does not account for all of it. DART handed its momentum to the surface, but the more important contribution came from the large ejecta cloud blasted out on impact; outward-flung debris recoils and pushes the asteroid the other way. The momentum-enhancement factor β measures this extra recoil. DART observed β of roughly 2.2 to 4.9, meaning the ejecta carried several times the impactor's initial momentum. A looser surface with more ejecta gives a larger recoil, which is why internal structure changes deflection efficiency.[5]

实测结果可以核对。撞击前 Dimorphos 的公转周期约 11.92 小时，撞击后缩短到约 11.38 小时，变化量为 33.0 ±1.0 分钟；轨道速度减小约 2.7 mm/s。对一颗直径百米级的小行星，这个速度变化看似极小，但行星防御靠的是提前量：如果在威胁天体抵达前数年甚至数十年施加一次小偏转，轨道偏差会随时间不断放大，足以让它错过地球。反过来，临到近前才偏转，同样的动量就远远不够。The measured results can be checked. Before impact Dimorphos orbited Didymos in about 11.92 hours; after impact the period fell to about 11.38 hours, a change of 33.0 ±1.0 minutes, and its orbital speed dropped by about 2.7 mm/s. A speed change this small on a hundred-metre asteroid sounds negligible, but planetary defence works through lead time. A small deflection applied years or decades before arrival lets the orbital miss distance grow over time, enough to miss Earth; the same momentum applied at the last minute would be nowhere near enough.[6]

边界要写清。DART 只在一颗松散碎石堆结构的小天体上做过一次实验；更大、更致密、或接触双星结构的小行星，撞击响应可能完全不同，碎屑分布和反冲方向也更难预测。动能偏转适合提前预警的目标，不适合已经临近的威胁；它不是把小行星炸碎的方案，破碎后的碎片反而可能各自构成风险。真实行星防御还依赖巡天发现、轨道跟踪与国际预警，单次撞击实验只是其中一环。The boundaries matter. DART ran one experiment on a small, rubble-pile body. Larger, denser or contact-binary asteroids may respond very differently, and ejecta distribution and recoil direction are harder to predict. Kinetic deflection suits targets found with years of warning, not threats already close to Earth; it is not a plan to blow an asteroid apart, since fragments could each become separate hazards. Real planetary defence also needs survey discovery, orbit tracking, international warning and a menu of options; one impact test is only one link in that chain.[5][6]

03旅行者号飞了四十多年现在距地球多远？地面靠什么和几十亿公里外的它通信？How far are the Voyagers after four decades, and how does Earth keep communicating with them billions of kilometres away?

双探测器已越过日球层、距地球超 160 亿英里；靠全球三地 DSN 大天线阵接收微弱下行信号。Both Voyagers have crossed the heliopause beyond 16 billion miles; three DSN complexes with large dishes receive their faint downlinks.

旅行者 1 号和 2 号是人类目前仅有的、仍在日球层之外工作的探测器。它们 1977 年发射，先飞掠木星、土星、天王星和海王星，完成外太阳系首次近距离普查，随后继续向星际空间飞行。四十多年后的今天，两台探测器仍在通过无线电向地球回传数据，测量太阳风边界之外的星际介质；它们也是飞得最远、通信距离最长的人造航天器。Voyager 1 and 2 are the only spacecraft still operating outside the heliosphere. Launched in 1977, they first flew past Jupiter, Saturn, Uranus and Neptune, completing the first close reconnaissance of the outer planets, and then kept flying toward interstellar space. More than four decades later both still radio data home, measuring the interstellar medium beyond the solar-wind bubble; they remain the farthest and longest-range human-made spacecraft.[7]

两台探测器现在都已越过日球层。日球层是太阳风吹出的、包裹太阳系的粒子与磁场气泡；旅行者 1 号在 2012 年穿过边界，速度更慢、方向不同的 2 号在 2018 年穿出。它们以每年超过 3 个天文单位的速度向外逃逸，距离地球已经超过 160 亿英里，1 个天文单位约等于 1.5 亿公里。旅行者 1 号是离地球最远的人造物体，两台探测器朝不同方向离开，短期内没有航天器接替。Both have now crossed the heliopause. The heliosphere is the bubble of particles and magnetic fields blown outward by the solar wind. Voyager 1 crossed its boundary in 2012, while the slower, differently directed Voyager 2 crossed in 2018. They are escaping the Solar System at more than three astronomical units per year, and their distance from Earth now exceeds 16 billion miles; one astronomical unit is about 150 million kilometres. Voyager 1 is the most distant human-made object, and the two craft are leaving in different directions, with no successor planned to take their place soon.[7][8]

地面通信靠的是 NASA 深空网（DSN）。DSN 由三个大型天线站组成，沿经度大致相隔 120 度，分别位于美国加州戈尔德斯通、西班牙马德里和澳大利亚堪培拉；地球自转时，一个站目送探测器落下地平线之前，另一个站已经接手跟踪。每个站都有至少四座天线，其中包括一台直径 70 米（230 英尺）的巨型天线，专门接收几十亿英里外极其微弱的下行信号。探测器上的发射机功率很低，信号随距离平方衰减，能被收到靠的是大口径天线、超低噪声接收系统和长时间积分。Ground communication runs through NASA's Deep Space Network. The DSN has three large antenna complexes spaced about 120 degrees apart in longitude: Goldstone in California, Madrid in Spain and Canberra in Australia. As Earth rotates, one complex hands the spacecraft over to the next before it sinks below the horizon. Each site keeps at least four antennas, including one 70-metre (230-foot) dish built to catch the faintest downlinks from tens of billions of miles away. The spacecraft transmitters are low-powered and the signal fades with the square of distance; reception depends on large dishes, ultra-low-noise receivers and long integration times.[9]

为什么这么远还值得继续听。日球层之外的星际介质、磁场方向和带电粒子环境，是任何近地探测器都测不到的；旅行者号的原位测量至今仍在更新我们对太阳系边界的认识。由于放射性同位素电源逐年衰减，工程师只能按预先排好的顺序关闭仪器来省电，旅行者 1 号目前仍有两台科学仪器在工作。探测器还携带金唱片记录地球的声音与图像；1990 年旅行者 1 号回望地球拍下的暗淡蓝点，是最广为人知的影像之一。It is worth listening this far out because the interstellar medium, magnetic field and charged-particle environment beyond the heliosphere cannot be measured by any near-Earth probe. The Voyagers' in-situ measurements still update our picture of the Solar System's edge. As their radioisotope power decays year by year, engineers shut instruments down in a pre-planned order to save power, and Voyager 1 still operates two science instruments. The craft also carry a Golden Record of Earth's sounds, images and greetings; Voyager 1's 1990 Pale Blue Dot portrait of Earth remains the mission's best-known image.[7][8]

边界要写清。无线电信号以光速传播，几十亿英里的距离意味着单程时延以小时计：地面发出的指令要等很久才被执行，探测器回传的也是几小时前的状态。旅行者号没有再加速的推进剂，按目前速度飞到最近的恒星比邻星仍需数万年；它们不是去拜访另一颗恒星，而是在星际空间做长期漂浮测量。日球层边界定义与星际介质分层仍在研究，探测器能工作多久取决于电源和仪器。The limits are clear. Radio signals travel at the speed of light, and at billions of miles the one-way delay runs to hours: a command arrives long after it is sent, and the downlink reports a state that existed hours earlier. The Voyagers carry no propellant for further acceleration, and at their current speed reaching the nearest star, Proxima Centauri, would take tens of millennia. They are not travelling to visit another star; they are long-duration floating measurements in interstellar space. The exact definition of the heliospheric boundary and the layering of the interstellar medium are still under study, and how long the craft keep working depends on how long their power and instruments hold out.[7][9]
