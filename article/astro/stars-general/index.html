01恒星的能量从哪里来？地球上的核聚变最终能成为实用能源吗？Where does a star's energy come from, and could terrestrial fusion ever become a practical power source?

恒星核心氢聚变为氦，质量亏损 0.71% 转为能量；地面聚变仍卡在等离子体约束与材料工程。Stars fuse hydrogen into helium in the core, with 0.71% mass defect turning to energy; terrestrial fusion still faces plasma confinement and materials engineering.

恒星发光的能量来自核心的核聚变，不是烧煤那样的化学燃烧。太阳每秒把约 6 亿吨氢聚变成氦，其中约 400 万吨质量按 E=mc² 转化为能量；每 1 千克氢参与反应，约 0.0071 千克质量变成能量，对应约 6.4×10¹⁴ J。正是这份质量亏损支撑太阳 4×10²⁶ W 的光度，也决定了恒星主序阶段可以稳定发光几十亿年。Stars shine from nuclear fusion in their cores, not from chemical burning like coal. The Sun fuses about 600 million tonnes of hydrogen into helium every second, and roughly four million tonnes of mass convert to energy through E=mc². For each kilogram of hydrogen reacted, about 0.0071 kilograms of mass becomes energy, close to 6.4×10¹⁴ J. That mass defect sustains the Sun's luminosity of 4×10²⁶ W and lets a star burn steadily for billions of years on the main sequence.[1]

太阳大小的恒星走的是质子-质子链。四个氢核先合成氘，氘再结合一个氢核成氦-3，两个氦-3 合成氦-4；中间步骤在核心约 1500 万 K 的高温和高压下完成。第一级氘生成约几秒，氦-3 相遇反应则要约一百万年，整体平衡下来维持核心稳定。质量大于约 1.2 倍太阳质量的恒星，主要走碳氮氧循环，用碳、氮、氧作催化剂完成同样的氢聚氦过程。A Sun-sized star runs the proton–proton chain. Four hydrogen nuclei first make deuterium, deuterium joins another proton to form helium-3, and two helium-3 nuclei combine into helium-4. These steps proceed at the core's roughly 15-million-K temperature and high pressure. The first deuterium step takes seconds, while helium-3 nuclei meet after about a million years; the whole balance keeps the core stable. Stars above about 1.2 solar masses instead use the carbon–nitrogen–oxygen cycle, in which carbon, nitrogen and oxygen act as catalysts for the same hydrogen-to-helium fusion.[1]

为什么化学燃烧和引力收缩都不够。19 世纪曾假设太阳靠煤或木柴燃烧，按质量折算只能维持几千年；开尔文和亥姆霍兹提出引力收缩，外层气体向内坠落、碰撞升温，算下来也只能供约一亿年。地质证据表明地球已有约 40 亿年历史，太阳必须更久，这两条旧路都被时间尺度排除，直到 20 世纪原子核结构和质能关系被弄清，问题才解决。Chemical burning and gravitational contraction cannot do the job. Nineteenth-century models assumed the Sun burned coal or wood, which by mass would last only a few thousand years. Kelvin and Helmholtz proposed gravitational contraction: outer gas falls inward, collisions raise the temperature, and the supply works out to roughly 100 million years. Geological evidence shows Earth is about four billion years old, so the Sun must be older still; both old explanations were ruled out by their timescales until nuclear structure and mass–energy equivalence were understood in the twentieth century.[2]

地球上的核聚变在复制同一件事，但难度完全不同。太阳核心靠巨大引力把氢压到高温高密度；地面装置要用磁约束或惯性约束把等离子体加热到上亿度并维持足够长的能量约束时间。聚变燃料储量丰富，反应不产生长寿命放射性废料，DOE 把可持续燃烧等离子体、材料耐受和燃料循环闭环列为主要攻关方向；目前实验室只在短脉冲下实现过能量增益，并网供电的聚变电站尚未建成。Terrestrial fusion copies the same reaction under far harder conditions. The Sun's gravity compresses hydrogen to high temperature and density; ground devices must heat plasma above a hundred million degrees and hold it long enough with magnetic or inertial confinement. Fusion fuel is abundant and the reaction produces no long-lived radioactive waste, but the U.S. DOE lists sustained burning plasma, material endurance and closing the fuel cycle as the main challenges. Laboratories have achieved energy gain in short pulses only, and a grid-connected fusion plant does not yet exist.[5][6]

边界要写清。恒星聚变靠引力自约束，与地面装置的约束机制不是一回事；太阳核心的反应速率由自身温度和压力调节，人工装置要主动维持等离子体稳定。现有实验距离商用还有材料、能量增益和燃料循环等工程缺口，不能把实验室的单次点火等同于可用电网。不同质量恒星的聚变路径和寿命差别很大，把太阳的质子-质子链套到大质量恒星上并不成立。The limits matter. Stellar fusion is self-confined by gravity, which is not how terrestrial confinement works; the Sun regulates its own reaction rate through core temperature and pressure, while an artificial device must actively stabilise the plasma. Existing experiments still face engineering gaps in materials, net energy gain and fuel recycling, so a single laboratory ignition is not a usable power grid. Fusion paths and lifetimes differ sharply with stellar mass, and applying the solar proton–proton chain to massive stars would be wrong.[1][5]

02物体变热时为什么会发光？灯泡的光也是同样的机制吗？Why does an object glow when heated, and is a light bulb's light produced the same way?

热运动加速带电粒子产生黑体辐射，温度决定峰值波长与总功率；LED 等则是电致发光，不属于热辐射。Thermal motion accelerates charges into blackbody radiation; temperature sets the peak wavelength and total power, while LEDs are electroluminescence, not thermal.

物体变热会发光，是因为热运动让带电粒子不断加速、辐射电磁波，温度越高辐射越强、峰值波长越短。这种由温度决定的辐射叫热辐射，理想模型是黑体：光谱形状只由温度决定，与材料无关。铁块从暗红到橙黄再到白炽，灯丝通电后发亮，太阳表面的白光，背后都是同一条温度—辐射关系。A hot object glows because thermal motion keeps accelerating charged particles, which radiate electromagnetic waves. Higher temperature means stronger radiation and a shorter peak wavelength. This temperature-driven emission is thermal radiation, and the ideal model is a blackbody: the spectrum depends only on temperature, not on the material. Iron moving from dull red to orange to white, a lit filament and the white light of the solar surface all follow the same temperature–radiation relation.[3]

两条规律可以核对。维恩位移定律给出峰值波长与温度成反比：λmax ≈ 3×10⁶ / T（nm·K）；太阳表面约 5800 K，峰值约 500 nm，落在可见光中段。斯特藩-玻尔兹曼定律给出单位面积辐射功率 F = σT⁴，温度升高一倍，辐射功率变为十六倍。所以高温物体不仅颜色变浅，总亮度也增长得极快；恒星表面温度从 2500 K 的红矮星到上万 K 的蓝星，颜色和亮度都由这条曲线拉开。Two laws can be checked. Wien's displacement law puts the peak wavelength inversely proportional to temperature: λmax ≈ 3×10⁶ / T in nm·K. The solar surface near 5800 K peaks around 500 nm, in the middle of the visible band. The Stefan–Boltzmann law gives power per unit area F = σT⁴: doubling the temperature raises radiated power sixteenfold. A hot object therefore does not merely shift colour; its total brightness grows very fast. Stellar surface temperatures from 2,500 K red dwarfs to ten-thousand-K blue stars are separated by this same curve.[3][7]

日常物体的热辐射大多落在红外。室温物体峰值波长约 10 微米，人眼看不见，只能靠红外测温仪感知；加热到约 800 K 开始发出暗红可见光，继续升温依次变橙、变黄、变白。白炽灯丝工作在约 2500 K，峰值仍在红外波段，可见光只占一小部分，这也是老式灯泡效率低的原因：大部分电能变成热而不是光。Most everyday thermal radiation lies in the infrared. A room-temperature object peaks near ten micrometres, invisible to the eye and readable only with an infrared thermometer; near 800 K it begins to emit dull red light, then orange, yellow and white as heating continues. An incandescent filament runs near 2,500 K, still peaking in the infrared, so only a small fraction of its output is visible light. That is why old bulbs are inefficient: most of the electricity becomes heat rather than light.[3]

灯泡的光要分类型。白炽灯和卤素灯靠灯丝高温发光，属于热辐射；荧光灯和 LED 则不是靠把灯丝烧红，而是气体放电或半导体电致发光，电子在能级跃迁时直接发出光子，效率高得多。把所有灯泡都归为热辐射会出错：LED 芯片本身温度不高，它的光来自材料的能带结构，而不是黑体曲线。Light bulbs must be split by type. Incandescent and halogen lamps glow from a hot filament and are thermal radiation; fluorescent tubes and LEDs do not heat a filament red. They use gas discharge or semiconductor electroluminescence, where electrons emit photons directly during energy-level transitions, at much higher efficiency. Calling every bulb thermal radiation would be wrong: an LED chip stays cool, and its light comes from the material's band structure rather than a blackbody curve.[4]

边界要写清。黑体是理想模型，真实物体有发射率差异，颜色还受表面材料和大气吸收影响；热辐射解释的是高温物体发光，不解释萤火虫、荧光棒、夜光材料这类化学发光，也不解释 LED 的电致发光。判断一种光是不是热辐射，关键看它是否由温度决定、光谱是否符合黑体曲线；仅凭“亮不亮”无法区分。The boundary matters. A blackbody is an idealisation; real objects differ in emissivity, and colour is also affected by surface material and atmospheric absorption. Thermal radiation explains glowing hot bodies, not fireflies, glow sticks or phosphorescent materials, which are chemiluminescence, nor LED electroluminescence. To decide whether a light is thermal, check whether it is set by temperature and whether its spectrum follows a blackbody curve; brightness alone cannot tell them apart.[3][4]

03我们看到的恒星是不是它们过去的样子？超新星爆发能实时看到吗？Do we see stars as they were in the past, and can a supernova be watched in real time?

光速有限，100 光年外的光要走 100 年；越远越早，超新星的光也要走完距离才被看到。Light speed is finite: light from 100 light-years away took 100 years to arrive; farther means earlier, and supernova light must cross its distance first.

我们看到的恒星，确实是它们过去的样子。光在真空中速度约 3×10⁸ m/s，虽然是宇宙最快速度，但宇宙距离太大，光走完一段路需要时间；我们此刻收到的光子，是恒星在多年前发出的。一光年是光一年走过的距离，约 9.46×10¹² km；离太阳最近的恒星在数光年之外，它今天的状态要再过几年才能传到地球。The stars we see really are how they looked in the past. Light travels at about 3×10⁸ m/s in a vacuum, the fastest speed in the universe, but cosmic distances are so large that light still needs time to cross them. The photons arriving tonight left their stars years ago. One light-year is the distance light travels in a year, about 9.46×10¹² km; the nearest star beyond the Sun lies a few light-years away, so its present state will not reach Earth for several more years.[4][8]

距离越远，看到的过去越古老。如果一颗恒星距我们 100 光年，今晚看到的光就是它 100 年前发出的；500 光年外的恒星，携来的是 500 年前的消息。仙女座星系距地球约 254 万光年，我们看到的是它两百多万年前的光。天文观测本质上是在按距离翻看宇宙的历史相册，越远的天体越早被看到，望远镜收集到的弱光往往来自更久远的年代。The farther away, the older the view. If a star is 100 light-years off, the light seen tonight left it 100 years ago; a star 500 light-years away carries 500-year-old news. The Andromeda Galaxy, about 2.54 million light-years away, shows light that left more than two million years ago. Astronomical observation is, in effect, turning the pages of a historical album ordered by distance: the farther object is seen earlier, and faint light collected by telescopes often dates from much deeper in the past.[8]

超新星爆发也不能实时看到。大质量恒星死亡时的爆发在几小时到几天内变亮，但光从爆发地点传到地球仍要按距离计算；一颗数百光年外的超新星，我们要等光走完这段距离才在望远镜里看到它。反过来，今天观测到的遥远超新星，爆发发生在很久以前；所谓实时，只对距离很近的天体才有意义。历史上 1054 年被记录到的超新星，今天看到的蟹状星云已是爆发之后的遗迹。A supernova cannot be watched in real time either. A massive star's death brightens over hours to days, but its light still takes the full distance to reach Earth. A supernova hundreds of light-years away is seen only after the light has crossed that gap; conversely, a distant supernova observed today exploded long ago. Real-time viewing is meaningful only for objects very close by. The supernova recorded in 1054 leaves today the Crab Nebula, a remnant long after the explosion itself.[8]

这条时延对探测器通信同样成立。深空探测器发回的图像和数据以光速回传，火星单程约几分钟到十几分钟，更远的探测器要数十小时；地面看到的画面永远是探测器一段时间之前的状态。天体亮度还随距离平方衰减，遥远恒星显得暗，部分原因就是球面波把能量摊到了更大的球面上。The same delay governs probe communication. Images and data from deep-space craft travel home at light speed: one way to Mars takes minutes to over ten minutes, while more distant probes need tens of hours. What the ground sees is always the probe's state from some time earlier. Brightness also falls with the square of distance, so remote stars look dim partly because a spherical wave spreads its energy over a larger surface.[4][9]

边界要写清。日常尺度下光程短到人感觉不到时延，室内看到的就是当下；宇宙学距离上的遥远天体还会因空间膨胀发生红移，观测到的波长和距离不能直接按本地空间换算。看到一颗恒星不代表它此刻仍然存在，但离我们几十、几百光年的恒星大多仍在稳定燃烧；真正需要按光程判断的，是那些已经爆发或死亡的遥远天体。The limits are clear. At everyday scales the light path is too short to notice, so indoors we see the present. At cosmological distances, remote objects are also redshifted by expanding space, and observed wavelength and distance cannot be converted as if in local space. Seeing a star does not prove it still exists, but stars within tens or hundreds of light-years are mostly still burning steadily; light-delay really matters for remote bodies that have already exploded or died.[8][9]

04星星为什么会眨眼睛？为什么白天看不到星星？Why do stars twinkle, and why can't we see stars during the day?

大气湍流折射使星光抖动而闪烁；白天散射阳光形成亮背景，弱星光被掩盖。Atmospheric turbulence refracts starlight into jittery twinkle; daytime scattered sunlight forms a bright background that hides faint stars.

星星眨眼睛，是星光穿过地球大气时被湍流不断折射造成的。大气温度和密度并不均匀，上升下降的气流形成大小不一的湍流团块，折射率随团块起伏；星光在传播途中被一会儿偏左、一会儿偏右地弯折，到达地面时亮度和位置轻微抖动，肉眼看到就是一明一暗的闪烁。恒星距离极远，在天空中是近似点光源，光线集中在极窄的一束里，折射抖动对它影响明显。天文学家把这种抖动称为视宁度，地基望远镜拍到的星像因此被大气模糊成小光斑，大型观测站要用自适应光学校正湍流造成的畸变。Stars twinkle because starlight is refracted repeatedly as it passes through Earth's atmosphere. Temperature and density are not uniform: rising and falling air form turbulent cells of varying refractive index, and light is bent first one way and then another along the path. By the time it reaches the ground, its brightness and apparent position jiggle, which the eye reads as blinking on and off. Stars are so distant that they are nearly point sources, with light concentrated in an extremely narrow beam, so refraction shifts matter a lot. Astronomers call this seeing; it blurs ground-based stellar images into small spots, and large observatories use adaptive optics to correct turbulence distortion.[10]

行星通常不怎么眨眼。太阳系行星离地球近，视角直径虽然肉眼仍看成光点，实际上却是一个很小的面光源，由许多并列的光点组成；不同光点的折射抖动互相平均抵消，整体亮度更稳定。金木水火土五颗肉眼行星看起来光线平稳，只有在低空穿过很厚、很动荡的空气时才会轻微抖动。稳定不闪是辨认行星的一条经验线索，但不是严格判据，大气条件和观测高度都会影响抖动程度。Planets usually do not twinkle. A Solar System planet is close enough to be a tiny extended source, a face made of many adjacent light points rather than one narrow beam; jitter in different points averages out, so the overall brightness stays steadier. The five naked-eye planets look stable, though near the horizon, through thick or turbulent air, they can wobble slightly too. Steady light is an empirical clue for spotting a planet, not a strict criterion, since air conditions and observing altitude both affect the jitter.[4][10]

白天看不到星星，并不是星星消失了。恒星白天仍在原轨道上发光，只是太阳光被大气分子和尘埃散射，把整片天空照成明亮的蓝色背景；人眼在明亮背景上分辨弱目标的对比度有限，暗弱星光的亮度远低于天空背景，被散射光掩盖住。在高空或月球这类没有大气散射的地方，天空背景是黑的，太阳和恒星可以同时被看到，星光不会被一片亮背景冲掉。Stars are not gone during the day; they keep shining on their orbits. Sunlight scattered by air molecules and dust turns the whole sky into a bright blue background. The eye has limited contrast for weak targets against a bright field, and faint starlight sits far below that background, so it is hidden. Above the atmosphere, on the Moon or in space, there is no scattering to lighten the sky: the day sky is black, and the Sun and stars can be seen together, without a bright background washing out the starlight.[9][10]

视亮度同时取决于恒星自身光度和距离，亮度随距离平方衰减，遥远恒星因此显得很弱。天狼星是夜空最亮的恒星，距地球约 8.6 光年，视星等约 −1.46；太阳视星等约 −26.7，只是因为离我们极近，距离约 1.5 亿千米，并不代表它本身光度超群。白天天空背景的亮度远远盖过除太阳、月亮之外的所有天体；日全食时太阳被遮住、背景变暗，才能在白天看到少数较亮的星。Apparent brightness depends on both intrinsic luminosity and distance, falling with the square of distance, which makes remote stars faint. Sirius, the brightest night star, lies about 8.6 light-years away at apparent magnitude about −1.46; the Sun's apparent magnitude near −26.7 reflects closeness to about 150 million kilometers, not exceptional power. The daytime sky background far outshines every body except the Sun and Moon; during a total solar eclipse the background darkens enough that a few bright stars can be spotted in daylight.[4][9]

边界要写清：闪烁是大气效应，与恒星本身的亮度变化无关；变星、超新星的亮度变化来自天体内部，不能用眨眼来解释。城市夜间光污染同样抬高背景亮度，使暗星看不见，原理和白天散射掩盖星光一致。把星星眨眼理解成星星在呼吸、或发光本身不稳，是常见的误解。Boundary: twinkle is an atmospheric effect, not a change in the star itself. Variable stars and supernovae change brightness for reasons inside the object, which cannot be explained as blinking. Urban light pollution raises the night background in the same way, hiding faint stars; the mechanism matches daytime scattering hiding starlight. Reading twinkle as the star breathing or flickering on its own is a common misunderstanding.[4][9]

05太阳为什么会发光发热？恒星的质量怎样决定它的寿命和亮度？Why does the Sun shine, and how does stellar mass set a star's lifetime and brightness?

核心氢聚变为氦释放能量；质量越大燃烧越猛、寿命越短；星等区分光度与视亮度。Core hydrogen fusion releases energy; more massive stars burn fiercer and die sooner; magnitudes separate luminosity from apparent brightness.

太阳发光发热的能源来自核心的核聚变。四个氢核在约 1500 万 K 的高温高压下聚变成氦，过程中约 0.71% 的质量按 E=mc² 转化为能量；太阳每秒把约 6 亿吨氢聚变成氦，其中约 400 万吨质量变成能量，支撑约 4×10²⁶ W 的光度。太阳大小的恒星主要走质子-质子链；质量大于约 1.2 倍太阳质量的恒星主要走碳氮氧循环，用碳、氮、氧作催化剂，完成同样的氢聚氦过程。The Sun's light and heat come from fusion in its core. Four hydrogen nuclei fuse into helium at about 15 million K under high pressure; about 0.71% of the mass turns to energy through E=mc². The Sun fuses roughly 600 million tonnes of hydrogen each second, losing about four million tonnes of mass to energy, which sustains its luminosity of about 4×10²⁶ W. A Sun-sized star runs the proton–proton chain; stars above about 1.2 solar masses mainly use the carbon–nitrogen–oxygen cycle, with carbon, nitrogen and oxygen acting as catalysts for the same hydrogen-to-helium fusion.[1]

质量决定恒星寿命。主序阶段靠烧氢维持引力与辐射的平衡，质量越大，核心压力和温度越高，核反应越快；燃料总量虽然更多，消耗速度却快得多。太阳质量的恒星主序寿命约 100 亿年；约 10 倍太阳质量的大质量恒星燃烧猛烈，主序寿命只有千万年量级；质量不到太阳一半的红矮星反应缓慢，寿命可达数千亿年甚至更长，远超当前宇宙年龄。质量同时决定光谱颜色：热星偏蓝，冷星偏红。Mass sets the lifetime. On the main sequence a star balances gravity and radiation by burning hydrogen; the more massive it is, the hotter and denser its core, and the faster the reaction runs. It has more fuel but consumes it far quicker. A one-solar-mass star spends about ten billion years on the main sequence; a ten-solar-mass star burns its fuel so much faster that its main-sequence lifetime is only tens of millions of years, while red dwarfs below half a solar mass react slowly and can last hundreds of billions of years, longer than the current age of the universe. Mass also sets spectral color: hot stars lean blue, cool ones red.[1][11]

亮度要分清绝对星等和视星等。绝对星等是把恒星放到标准距离 10 秒差距、约 32.6 光年处比较的真实光度；视星等是在地球上实际看到的亮度，同时受光度和距离影响。太阳绝对星等约 4.83，在恒星里只是普通水平；视星等约 −26.7，完全是因为离我们极近。天狼星距地球约 8.6 光年，视星等约 −1.46，是夜空最亮的恒星；距离很远但本身极亮的蓝巨星，视亮度也可能很低。Brightness separates absolute magnitude from apparent magnitude. Absolute magnitude compares true luminosity at a standard distance of 10 parsecs, about 32.6 light-years; apparent magnitude is what we see on Earth, affected by both luminosity and distance. The Sun's absolute magnitude is about 4.83, only ordinary among stars; its apparent magnitude near −26.7 reflects closeness, not exceptional power. Sirius lies about 8.6 light-years away at apparent magnitude −1.46, the brightest night star; a very remote but intrinsically brilliant blue giant can look dim.[11]

天上的星星数量是量级估计。银河系内恒星估计在 1000 亿到 4000 亿颗之间，肉眼可见的只是其中离得近、光度高的一小部分；全天肉眼通常能看到的恒星约几千颗，受光污染影响，城市里只能看到最亮的几百颗。星系之外还有上千亿个星系，每个都包含大量恒星；日常说的星星大多指银河系内的恒星，偶尔也包括肉眼可见的邻近星系。The number of stars is an order-of-magnitude estimate. The Milky Way holds an estimated 100 to 400 billion stars, but the naked eye sees only the nearby, high-luminosity minority: a few thousand across the whole sky under dark conditions, and only the brightest few hundred in a light-polluted city. Beyond the Milky Way lie over a hundred billion galaxies, each packed with stars. What people call stars are mostly Milky Way members, with a few nearby galaxies visible to the eye.[9]

边界要写清：本章讲主序阶段的氢燃烧与质量关系，不展开恒星晚年的红巨星、白矮星、中子星和超新星结局。质量与寿命的趋势是主序阶段的统计规律，双星物质转移、金属丰度等因素会改变具体演化时标；把太阳的质子-质子链套到大质量恒星上并不成立，后者主要走 CNO 循环。Boundary: this chapter covers main-sequence hydrogen burning and the mass relation, not late-life red giants, white dwarfs, neutron stars or supernova endings. The mass–lifetime trend is a main-sequence statistical pattern; binary mass transfer and metallicity change specific evolution timescales. Applying the solar proton–proton chain to massive stars would be wrong, since they mainly run the CNO cycle.[2][9]

06银河系为什么是扁平的盘状？为什么太阳系的行星轨道几乎在同一平面上？Why is the Milky Way a flat disk, and why do planetary orbits lie nearly in one plane?

气体云靠角动量守恒自转，垂直方向碰撞耗散沉降成薄盘；原行星盘共面形成行星轨道。A gas cloud spins by angular momentum conservation and settles vertically through collisions into a thin disk; planets form in a coplanar protoplanetary disk.

银河系是一个盘状星系：中央隆起的核球、扁平的银盘和外围稀疏的银晕，太阳位于银盘内，距银心约 2.6 万光年。银盘直径约 10 万光年，薄盘厚度约 1000 光年，盘内分布旋臂、尘埃带和恒星形成区；恒星沿盘内近圆轨道绕银心转动，银晕里的老年恒星则走高度倾斜的轨道。The Milky Way is a disk galaxy: a central bulging bulge, a flat disk and a sparse outer halo. The Sun sits in the disk, about 26,000 light-years from the center. The disk is roughly 100,000 light-years across, with a thin-disk thickness near 1,000 light-years; it holds spiral arms, dust lanes and star-forming regions. Stars travel near-circular orbits inside the disk, while old halo stars follow highly tilted orbits.[12]

盘状结构来自角动量守恒与气体沉降。原始银河系是一大团缓慢自转的气体云，整体带有一个总角动量；云在引力作用下收缩时，自转方向的角动量无法被耗散掉，自转速度随半径缩小而增大，赤道方向的离心作用抵抗引力收缩。气体分子和云团在垂直盘面方向频繁碰撞，垂直方向的随机运动被碰撞耗散、沉降到赤道面附近；自转方向的运动因为角动量保留下来，于是物质摊成一个薄盘。The disk comes from angular momentum conservation and gas settling. The early Milky Way was a slowly rotating gas cloud with a total angular momentum. As gravity pulled it together, the angular momentum in the spin direction could not be dissipated, and rotation speed rose as the radius shrank; centrifugal spin resisted collapse along the equator. Vertically, gas molecules and clouds collided often, and random vertical motion was dissipated and settled toward the equatorial plane, while spin motion was retained through angular momentum. Matter therefore flattened into a thin disk.[11][12]

恒星在盘内气体中形成。分子云沿盘面分布，坍缩成恒星时继承了盘面的自转方向，所以银盘里的年轻恒星和疏散星团也基本在盘内运动；核球和银晕形成更早，当时气体还没沉降成盘，恒星轨道方向杂乱。这解释了为什么盘内恒星轨道接近共面，而银晕恒星轨道高低交错。Stars form from disk gas. Molecular clouds lie along the plane, and when they collapse into stars they inherit the disk's spin, so young disk stars and open clusters also move within the plane. The bulge and halo formed earlier, before gas settled into a disk, so their stellar orbits run in mixed directions. This is why disk-star orbits are nearly coplanar while halo-star orbits cross at many angles.[1][11]

太阳系的共面是同一机制的缩小版本。太阳形成时周围的原行星盘也是自转气体盘：垂直方向碰撞耗散后摊平，行星在盘内不同半径处由尘埃和星子吸积而成，轨道自然接近同一平面，行星轨道面与黄道面通常只相差几度。少数小天体轨道倾角很大，来自后期引力摄动和轨道散乱；冥王星等海外天体的轨道偏心率和倾角都偏大。The Solar System's coplanarity is the same mechanism at smaller scale. The protoplanetary disk around the forming Sun was also a rotating gas disk: collisions dissipated vertical motion and flattened it, and planets accreted from dust and planetesimals at different radii inside that disk, so their orbits naturally lie near one plane, usually within a few degrees of the ecliptic. A few small bodies have steep inclinations from later gravitational scattering; trans-Neptunian objects such as Pluto show large eccentricity and inclination.[12]

边界要写清：扁平结构是气体耗散系统的结果，球状星团和椭圆星系缺少气体碰撞耗散，恒星轨道杂乱，并不摊成盘。银河系直径、厚度和太阳距离的数值随观测样本与模型不同有一定区间；本章讲盘形成的力学机制，不展开旋臂结构的密度波细节。把银河扁平理解成一个磨盘那样的固体转盘也不准确：盘内不同半径的恒星按各自轨道绕转，角速度并不相同。Boundary: a flat shape results from a dissipative gas system. Globular clusters and elliptical galaxies lack collisional gas dissipation, so their stellar orbits are mixed and do not flatten into a disk. Disk diameter, thickness and the Sun's distance carry some range across observation samples and models; this chapter explains the mechanics of disk formation, not density-wave details of spiral arms. Seeing the Galaxy as a solid turntable would be wrong: stars at different radii follow their own orbits and do not share one angular speed.[1][12]

07夜晚看到的星星都在银河系内吗？银河系有多少颗恒星？星团又是什么？Are the stars we see at night all inside the Milky Way, how many stars does the Galaxy hold, and what is a star cluster?

肉眼恒星基本都在银河内；银河约千亿到四千亿颗恒星；星团是同星云诞生、引力束缚的恒星群。Naked-eye stars are mostly Galactic, with a few bright external galaxies; the Galaxy holds an estimated 100 to 400 billion stars; clusters are bound groups born from one cloud.

夜空中绝大多数肉眼可见的光点都是银河系内的恒星。太阳身处银盘，周围几千光年以内的恒星亮到可以被肉眼分辨；它们沿盘内轨道绕银心转动，构成我们看到的那条银河亮带。肉眼能直接看到的河外天体只有少数极亮的星系，例如仙女座星系 M31、大小麦哲伦云和暗夜条件下的三角座星系，这些不是恒星而是远在银河系之外的恒星集合。把夜空中每个光点都当成银河系内的单颗恒星，会漏掉这些背景星系。The great majority of naked-eye points in the night sky are stars inside the Milky Way. The Sun sits in the disk, and stars within a few thousand light-years shine bright enough to be resolved by the eye; they orbit the center in disk orbits and form the bright band we see as the Milky Way. The only external objects visible directly to the eye are a few very bright galaxies, such as the Andromeda galaxy M31, the Large and Small Magellanic Clouds, and under dark skies the Triangulum Galaxy; these are not single stars but stellar assemblies far outside the Galaxy. Treating every bright point as an in-Galaxy star misses these background galaxies.[12][13]

银河系的恒星总数无法一颗颗清点，只能靠质量和光度模型估算。天文学家由银盘、核球和银晕的质量分布、不同质量恒星的光度比例，反推恒星数量；目前估计约为 1000–4000 亿颗，常用量级是上千亿颗。这一区间宽度来自尘埃遮挡、低质量暗淡恒星难以计数和银晕结构的不确定性。盖亚卫星收录了约十几亿颗恒星的位置和自行，只是这个样本里的一小部分，并非银河系恒星的全部。The total number of Galactic stars cannot be counted one by one; it is estimated from mass and luminosity models. From the mass distribution of disk, bulge and halo and the luminosity proportions of stars of different masses, astronomers infer a count of roughly 100 to 400 billion stars, commonly quoted as over a hundred billion. The wide range comes from dust obscuration, faint low-mass stars that are hard to count, and uncertainty in halo structure. Gaia recorded positions and proper motions for about a billion-plus stars, only a small sample of the whole Galaxy, not its full stellar population.[11][12]

星团是同一团分子云里诞生、又被引力束缚在一起的一群恒星。它们年龄相近、化学成分相近，运动方向也大体一致，用赫罗图可以判断形成年代。星团分两类：疏散星团位于银盘内，成员从几颗到几千颗，结构松散，恒星仍在缓慢散开，昴星团就是典型；球状星团环绕银心分布，成员可达约 10 万–50 万颗，挤在球形区域内，年龄很老，是银河系最早形成的一批恒星。A star cluster is a group of stars born from one molecular cloud and bound together by gravity. They share a similar age and chemical composition and move in roughly the same direction, and their age can be read from the Hertzsprung-Russell diagram. Clusters fall into two classes. Open clusters lie in the disk, holding from a few to a few thousand members in a loose structure that slowly disperses; the Pleiades is a classic example. Globular clusters circle the Galactic center, holding up to hundreds of thousands of stars in a spherical region; they are very old and among the Galaxy's first stellar populations.[11][14]

两类星团的差别来自形成环境和演化历史。疏散星团在盘内气体中形成，附近有气体尘埃，恒星之间引力束缚较弱，经历几亿到几十亿年后逐渐被潮汐力拆散；球状星团在银河系早期形成于致密气体团块中，恒星密集、束缚强，能够稳定存在约 100 多亿年。研究星团的赫罗图顶点和颜色，可以标定恒星演化模型和宇宙距离尺度；星团也是检验恒星质量—年龄关系的天然样本。The two cluster types differ in formation environment and history. Open clusters form in disk gas near dust, with weak mutual gravity, and are gradually torn apart by tides over hundreds of millions to billions of years. Globular clusters formed early in dense gas clumps; their stars are dense and strongly bound, and they can survive for more than ten billion years. Studying the turnoff and colors of cluster Hertzsprung-Russell diagrams calibrates stellar evolution models and the cosmic distance scale; clusters are natural samples for testing mass-age relations.[11][14]

边界要写清：星座和星团不是一回事。星座是视线方向上的二维投影，成员恒星距离和关联各不相同；星团里的恒星才是物理上互相束缚的集团。银河恒星总数是模型估计，不是观测清点值，不同模型给出的区间可能不同；肉眼可见的河外天体数量很少，亮度受暗夜条件影响。把星云、星团和星系混为一谈，是天文观测中常见的概念错位。The boundary matters: constellations and clusters are not the same thing. A constellation is a two-dimensional line-of-sight projection whose stars differ in distance and association; only cluster members are physically bound. The Galactic star count is a model estimate, not an observed tally, and models may give different ranges. The number of naked-eye external galaxies is small and depends on dark-sky conditions. Confusing nebulae, clusters and galaxies is a common conceptual slip in observing the sky.[12][13]
