01地球为什么有春夏秋冬四季？季节是地轴倾斜造成的，还是日地距离造成的？Why does Earth have seasons: axial tilt, or distance from the Sun?

轴倾角 23.4° 决定直射点移动与昼夜长短；轨道偏心率小，日地距离是次要叠加因素。The 23.4° tilt drives subsolar movement and day length; small eccentricity makes Sun-Earth distance a minor overlay.

四季来自地球自转轴相对轨道面的倾斜，而不是地球离太阳远近。地球自转轴与轨道面法线夹角约 23.4°，在公转过程中朝向保持大致不变，于是太阳直射点在南北纬 23.4° 的回归线之间来回移动：北半球倾向太阳时是夏季，背向时是冬季，南半球季节正好相反。回归线和极圈的纬度界线，正是由这个倾角确定的，全球各地的季节节奏都由此派生。Seasons come from the tilt of Earth's axis relative to its orbital plane, not from how close Earth is to the Sun. The axial tilt is about 23.4°, and the axis keeps nearly the same direction through the orbit, so the subsolar point moves between 23.4° north and south. When the north pole leans toward the Sun it is northern summer; when it leans away it is winter, and the southern hemisphere has the opposite season.[1][2][3]

季节的直接成因是阳光入射角和日照时长。倾角大的一侧阳光近于直射，同样一束光落在更小的地面面积上，单位面积接收能量多、白昼长；另一侧阳光斜射，能量摊到更大面积、白昼短。正午太阳高度角随纬度和季节变化，晨昏前后还有一段晨昏蒙影。春分和秋分两天直射赤道，全球昼夜接近等长；夏至冬至对应直射点到达南北回归线的极限位置。The immediate causes are sunlight angle and day length. The tilted side receives near-direct rays, more energy per unit area and longer daylight; the other side gets slanted rays, spread thinner, with shorter days. At the equinoxes the Sun shines over the equator and day and night are nearly equal worldwide; solstices mark the subsolar point reaching the tropic limits.[1][2]

日地距离的影响小得多。地球轨道偏心率约 0.0167，近日点约 1.471 亿千米、在 1 月初，远日点约 1.521 亿千米，恰恰是北半球冬季；如果距离是季节主因，南北半球应同时冷暖。距离变化带来的年总辐射差约几个百分点，远小于倾角造成的入射角和昼长变化，只能叠加在季节趋势上；南半球夏季因距离略近，同纬度平均温度可能略高。Distance to the Sun matters much less. Earth's orbital eccentricity is about 0.0167, and perihelion falls in early January, northern winter; if distance drove seasons, both hemispheres would warm and cool together. The annual radiation change from distance is only a few percent, far smaller than the angle and day-length effects of tilt, and merely overlays the seasonal trend.[1][2]

不同纬度的季节强度不同：赤道附近全年高温、昼夜等长，季节主要表现为季风带来的干湿季；高纬度夏季昼长、冬季夜长，极圈以内出现极昼和极夜；温带地区四季更替最明显。大气环流和洋流会再分配热量，使同一纬度的季节温度并不完全一致。Seasonal strength varies by latitude: near the equator it stays hot with nearly equal days, and seasons are mainly wet and dry; high latitudes have long summer days and long winter nights, with polar day and night inside the polar circles; temperate zones show the clearest four-season cycle. The tilt also sets the latitude boundaries of tropics and polar circles.[1][2]

轴的朝向并非永远固定：受月球和行星引力摄动，地轴存在缓慢进动，约两万六千年扫一圈；倾角本身也在米兰科维奇周期中以数万年到十万年尺度小幅摆动，影响长期气候。短期天气和年际冷暖波动不能归因于轴倾角变化；判断季节成因时，应先区分轨道倾角、日地距离和大气环流三类因素。The axis direction is not fixed forever: lunar and planetary gravity cause slow precession, about a 26,000-year cycle, on top of precession and long-term climate scales. Short-term weather and year-to-year temperature swings cannot be blamed on tilt change. In diagnosing seasons, orbital tilt, Sun-Earth distance and atmospheric circulation must be separated first.[1][2]

02空间站里为什么是微重力而不是零重力？火箭为什么大部分重量是燃料？Why is the station in microgravity rather than zero gravity, and why do rockets carry mostly fuel?

轨道是持续自由落体，残余约 10⁻⁶ g；火箭方程与化学排气速度决定高燃料占比和多级分离。Orbit is continuous free fall with ~10⁻⁶ g residual; the rocket equation and chemical exhaust speed drive the high fuel fraction and staging.

空间站里的“失重”不是没有引力，而是持续自由落体。近地轨道上的引力仍有地面的约九成，飞船和内部物体同时向地球下落，水平轨道速度又让它们始终追着弯曲的地表飞行，于是舱内感觉不到支撑力，进入微重力状态。国际空间站约 400 千米高度上的实验柜就工作在这种环境。残余加速度来自潮汐效应、稀薄大气阻力、姿态控制和设备振动，量级约 10⁻⁶ g，并不是零重力。Weightlessness on a space station is not absence of gravity but continuous free fall. At low orbit gravity is still about 90% of the surface value; the craft and everything inside fall toward Earth together, while horizontal orbital speed keeps them chasing the curved surface, so no support force is felt inside: a microgravity state. Residual acceleration from tides, thin air drag, attitude control and equipment vibration is around 10⁻⁶ g, not zero gravity.[3][6]

近地轨道高度约 400 千米，轨道速度约 7.7 千米每秒，绕地一圈约 90 到 93 分钟。圆轨道的条件是水平速度足够大：物体向下掉落的同时，地表曲率也在向下弯曲，两者匹配就不会落回地面。轨道速度随高度降低而增大，低轨道更快、高轨道更慢。宇航员和飞船在同一轨道状态下，相对运动只由微小的残余力决定。A low orbit sits about 400 kilometers up at roughly 7.7 kilometers per second, completing one lap in about 90 minutes. A circular orbit needs enough horizontal speed: as the object drops, the surface curves downward at the matching rate, so it never lands. Astronaut and craft share the same orbital state, and relative motion comes only from tiny residual forces.[1][3]

火箭入轨需要克服重力和大气阻力，把速度提升到轨道速度，总速度增量约 9.3 到 9.5 千米每秒，其中一部分用于垂直上升克服重力损失，一部分用于水平加速。化学火箭排气速度约 2.5 到 4.5 千米每秒，由齐奥尔科夫斯基火箭方程 Δv = ve·ln(m0/m1) 决定质量比：要求的 Δv 越大，结构和有效载荷之外的燃料占比越高。这就是运载火箭大部分质量是燃料的原因。Reaching orbit requires overcoming gravity and air drag to orbital speed, a total velocity change of about 9.3 to 9.5 kilometers per second. Chemical exhaust speed runs about 2.5 to 4.5 kilometers per second; the Tsiolkovsky rocket equation Δv = ve·ln(m0/m1) sets the mass ratio: the larger the required Δv, the higher the fuel fraction beyond structure and payload. That is why most of a launch vehicle's mass is propellant.[3][6]

单级火箭难以把有效载荷送入轨道，工程上采用多级设计：第一级烧完抛掉空壳，第二级在更轻的质量上继续加速，逐级降低需要的质量比。推进剂常用液氧液氢或液氧煤油，比冲直接对应排气速度。火箭方程对排气速度极其敏感，提高 ve 比单纯增加燃料更有效；化学能上限附近，化学火箭的运载效率受到根本约束。A single stage rarely places a payload in orbit, so rockets are staged: the first stage drops its empty shell after burnout, and the second accelerates a lighter mass, lowering the required mass ratio step by step. The equation is highly sensitive to exhaust speed; raising ve beats adding fuel alone, and near the chemical-energy limit, chemical rockets face a fundamental efficiency constraint.[3][6]

把轨道失重解释成“远离地球所以引力消失”是常见误解。轨道高度只比地球半径小一个数量级，引力衰减平缓；真正的条件是速度和自由落体。火箭燃料占比高也不是设计浪费，而是火箭方程和排气速度限制下的必然结果，多级分离和结构减重都是为了压缩这部分代价。Explaining orbital weightlessness as "gravity fades far from Earth" is a common mistake. Orbit altitude is only an order of magnitude below Earth's radius, and gravity falls off gently; the real condition is speed and free fall. The high fuel fraction is not design waste but a necessary consequence of the rocket equation and exhaust-speed limits; staging and structural weight saving compress that cost.[3][6]

03为什么夜空是黑的？如果太阳突然熄灭，地球要多久才会知道？Why is the night sky dark, and how long would Earth take to notice if the Sun went out?

有限年龄、有限光速与膨胀红移解释奥伯斯佯谬；太阳光行约 8 分 20 秒，熄灭后约 8.3 分钟天空变暗。Finite age, finite light speed and expansion redshift resolve Olbers' paradox; sunlight takes 8m20s, so dimming follows in about 8.3 minutes.

夜空为什么是黑的，曾经是一个严肃的宇宙学问题，即奥伯斯佯谬，开普勒和哈雷先后注意到这一矛盾：如果宇宙静态、无限且永恒存在，恒星均匀分布，沿任意视线最终都应落到某颗恒星表面，整条夜空应该像恒星表面一样明亮，而不是黑暗。实际观测到的背景黑暗，恰恰说明这个静态无限永恒的假设不成立。Why the night sky is dark was once a serious cosmological puzzle, Olbers' paradox: if the universe were static, infinite and eternal, every line of sight would end on some stellar surface, and the whole sky should shine as bright as a star, not stay dark. The observed dark background shows that this static-infinite-eternal assumption fails.[1][2]

第一条解答是宇宙年龄有限。宇宙约 138 亿年历史，可观测宇宙共动半径约 465 亿光年，只有有限距离内天体发出的光有足够时间到达地球；更远恒星的光还在途中，尚未加入我们的视线。恒星本身寿命也有限，太阳级恒星约百亿年，大质量恒星只有数百万年，不可能在无限时间里持续发光照亮所有方向。The first answer is a finite age. The universe is about 13.8 billion years old, so only light from within a finite distance has had time to reach Earth; light from farther stars is still en route and has not entered our line of sight. Stars also have finite lifetimes and cannot shine in every direction for infinite time.[2][6]

第二条解答是空间膨胀。遥远星系的光在传播中被膨胀拉长，频率降低、波长变长，可见光逐步移向红外甚至更不可见的波段；最早可观测的宇宙微波背景辐射红移约 1100，温度从复合期约 3000 K 降到今天 2.725 K。即使光到达地球，也可能落在人眼和普通探测器敏感范围之外；膨胀还在拉大距离，使最遥远区域的光永远无法抵达。The second answer is expanding space. Light from distant galaxies is stretched during travel: frequency drops, wavelength grows, and visible light shifts toward infrared and beyond, possibly outside the sensitivity of eyes and ordinary detectors. Expansion keeps stretching distances, so light from the farthest regions can never arrive.[2][6]

光速有限决定了我们看到的都是过去。日地距离约 1.496×10⁸ 千米，光行约 8 分 20 秒；如果太阳此刻熄灭，地球要等约 8.3 分钟才会看到天空变暗。最近的比邻星距地球约 4.3 光年，看到的是它 4.3 年前的样子；光年是距离单位，不是时间单位。观测遥远星系时，接收到的是它们数十亿年前的样子，“现在”的遥远天体状态无法被即时看到。The finite speed of light means we always see the past. The Sun-Earth distance is about 1.496×10⁸ kilometers, crossed in about 8 minutes 20 seconds; if the Sun went out now, Earth would wait about 8.3 minutes before the sky dimmed. Observing a distant galaxy means seeing it as it was billions of years ago; its present state cannot be seen instantaneously.[1][2]

把夜空黑暗归因于“云或尘埃挡住了星光”并不充分：遮挡物质会被持续照射加热，达到平衡后再辐射出同样强度的光，不能长期压暗背景。现代解释把有限年龄、有限光速、膨胀红移和恒星有限寿命结合起来；对夜空背景亮度的测量，反过来约束宇宙年龄、膨胀历史和恒星形成率，夜空的黑暗本身就是宇宙并非静态永恒的一条可观测证据。Attributing the dark sky to dust clouds blocking starlight is not sufficient: blocked material would be heated by continued irradiation and reradiate at comparable intensity. The modern explanation combines finite age, finite light speed, expansion redshift and finite stellar lifetimes; the dark night sky itself is an observable clue that the universe is neither static nor eternal.[1][6]

04什么是流星和流星雨？星座里的恒星真的聚在一起吗？What are meteors and meteor showers, and are constellation stars physically grouped?

流星体在 70–100 km 大气烧蚀发光；流星雨按辐射点所在星座命名；星座是二维视线投影，恒星距离与关联各不相同。Meteoroids ablate at 70–100 km; showers take the radiant's constellation name; constellations are 2D projections, stars differ in distance and binding.

流星不是星星掉落，而是流星体闯入地球大气的发光现象。流星体大小从尘埃颗粒到小石块不等，来源包括彗星碎屑和小行星碰撞碎块，以每秒十几到几十千米的速度撞入大气层，在约 70 到 100 千米高度剧烈压缩前方空气，空气和流星体表面被加热到白炽状态，烧蚀出一条光迹；较大的成为火流星，有时留下余迹，残余碎片落到地面就是陨石，按成分分为石陨石、铁陨石和石铁陨石。A meteor is not a falling star but the glowing trail of a meteoroid entering Earth's atmosphere. Meteoroids range from dust grains to small rocks, hitting the atmosphere at tens of kilometers per second. Around 70 to 100 kilometers up they compress the air ahead so violently that the air and surface glow white-hot, ablating a luminous trail; larger ones become fireballs, and surviving fragments reach the ground as meteorites.[4][5]

流星雨发生在地球穿过彗星或小行星留下的碎屑带时。大量碎屑轨道相近，同一时段进入大气，透视效应让地面看去光迹从天空同一点辐射出来，这个点称辐射点；流星雨按辐射点所在的星座命名，如 8 月英仙座流星雨、12 月双子座流星雨、11 月狮子座流星雨。彗星抛洒的冰尘碎屑是多数流星雨的物质来源，活动周期与母体回归周期相关。A meteor shower occurs when Earth crosses a debris stream left by a comet or asteroid. Many particles share similar orbits and enter together, so their trails appear to radiate from one sky point, the radiant. Showers are named after the constellation holding the radiant: the Perseids, Geminids, Leonids. Icy dust shed by comets feeds most showers.[4][5]

星座是人类在天球上做的二维连线，不是物理上聚在一起的星团。全天传统与现代划分共 88 个星座；同一星座里的恒星距离可以相差数百上千光年，彼此没有引力束缚，只是投影方向相近。随恒星长期自行，几千年前的星座形状和今天已有可测量差异；星座边界是历史上人为划定的天球区域，恒星用拜耳命名法等编号标注。A constellation is a two-dimensional human drawing on the celestial sphere, not a physically grouped star cluster. Stars in one constellation can be hundreds or thousands of light-years apart, with no gravitational binding; they merely project near one another in the sky. Over long proper motion, ancient constellation shapes differ measurably from today's, and constellation boundaries are historically defined sky regions.[1][4]

猎户座是最容易辨认的冬季星座：三颗亮星排成腰带，左上是红色超巨星参宿四，距离约 500 到 600 光年，右下是蓝色亮星参宿七，距离约 860 光年，周围约 1344 光年外是猎户座大星云恒星形成区。辨认星座靠的是亮星排列图案，而不是恒星之间的实际距离或关联。Orion is the easiest winter constellation to recognize: three bright stars form the belt, with the red supergiant Betelgeuse and blue Rigel above and below, and the Orion Nebula star-forming region nearby. Recognizing a constellation relies on the bright-star pattern, not on real distances or associations among the stars.[1]

小行星和彗星是太阳系残留的小天体：主带内已编号小行星数以万计，岩石金属为主；彗星来自柯伊伯带和奥尔特云，接近太阳时冰升华成彗发和两条彗尾，离子尾沿太阳风方向笔直延伸，尘埃尾受辐射压弯曲，通常都背离太阳方向。流星碎屑与这些小天体的解体直接相关。Asteroids and comets are leftover small bodies of the Solar System: most asteroids orbit in the main belt between Mars and Jupiter, rocky and metallic; comets come from the Kuiper belt and Oort cloud, and near the Sun their ices sublime into a coma and tail. The tail, pushed by solar wind and radiation pressure, usually points away from the Sun. Meteor debris is directly tied to the breakup of these small bodies.[4][5]

05接近光速飞行时星空看起来什么样？极光为什么通常是绿色的？What does the sky look like near light speed, and why are auroras usually green?

光行差把恒星挤向前方、前蓝后红；极光由太阳风粒子撞击大气产生，氧原子 557.7 nm 谱线主导绿色。Aberration crowds stars forward with front-blue/back-red Doppler shifts; auroras form when solar particles hit the air, oxygen 557.7 nm driving green.

接近光速运动时，看到的星空会发生两类相对论变化。光行差效应按速度比 v/c 改变光线入射角，把前方恒星的视位置挤向运动方向，视野中心的天体集中到前方；多普勒效应使前方光蓝移、后方光红移，速度越接近光速，前方越亮、后方越暗，可见光甚至会被移出或推入敏感波段。这些效应在日常低速运动中可以忽略，在近光速旅行的假设场景中决定了窗外景象。Near the speed of light, the visible sky changes through two relativistic effects. Aberration crowds apparent stellar positions toward the direction of motion, concentrating forward objects in the center of view; Doppler effect blueshifts light ahead and redshifts it behind. The closer to light speed, the brighter the front and the darker the back. These effects are negligible at everyday speeds but decide the hypothetical near-light-speed view.[6][8]

极光来自太阳风带电粒子与高层大气的碰撞。太阳风速度约每秒 300 到 800 千米，喷出的电子和质子被地球磁层引导，沿磁力线进入极区，在约 100 到 200 千米高度撞击氧原子和氮分子，把它们激发到高能级，退激发时发出特征波长的光。主导绿色的是氧原子 557.7 纳米谱线，也是肉眼最常见的极光颜色。Auroras arise when solar-wind charged particles collide with the upper atmosphere. Electrons and protons ejected by the Sun follow magnetic field lines into the polar regions and, around 100 to 200 kilometers up, strike oxygen atoms and nitrogen molecules, exciting them; on de-excitation they emit characteristic wavelengths. The dominant green line is the oxygen 557.7-nanometer emission, the aurora color most familiar to the eye.[7][8]

颜色对应高度和成分：更高海拔的氧原子发出 630 纳米左右的红光，氮分子辐射约 427.8 纳米的蓝色和紫色波段；557.7 纳米绿线在较低高度占主导。强磁暴中高能粒子深入或抬升发光层，就会出现红色极光边缘或整片红色光幕。极光集中在围绕磁极的极光椭圆内，太阳活动强烈时向较低纬度扩展。Color follows altitude and composition: higher oxygen emits red near 630 nanometers, while nitrogen molecules radiate blue and violet. During strong magnetic storms energetic particles penetrate deeper or lift the emitting layer, producing red edges or whole red curtains. Auroras concentrate in the auroral oval around the magnetic poles and spread to lower latitudes when solar activity is strong.[7][8]

极光与近光速星空看起来都和光、粒子有关，但机制不同：一个是运动参考系对光方向和频率的几何与多普勒改变，一个是带电粒子激发大气原子分子的辐射，两类现象不能互相套用解释。把极光当成高层大气反射的地面灯光，或把光行差当成普通视角转动，都会漏掉各自的物理机制。Relativistic skies and auroras both involve light and particles, but by different mechanisms: one is the geometric and Doppler change of light direction and frequency in a moving frame; the other is radiation from charged particles exciting atmospheric atoms and molecules. Treating aurora as reflected ground light, or aberration as ordinary turning of the head, misses both mechanisms.[6][7]

从太空生命边界看，木材腐烂需要水分、氧气和微生物共同作用，密闭飞船里的干燥木材缺少这些条件，不会按地球表面的速度分解；在飞船内种菜需要闭合的水、空气和营养循环，还要补光、控温并处理废弃物，而不是把土壤搬上轨道。这类问题属于生命支持与生态工程，不能用地球表面经验直接外推。On the edge of life in space: wood rots through water, oxygen and microbes acting together; dry wood in a sealed cabin does not decompose at surface-Earth speed. Growing plants aboard a craft needs a closed water, air and nutrient cycle, not merely carrying soil to orbit. Such questions belong to life-support and ecological engineering and cannot be extrapolated directly from surface-Earth experience.[7][8]

06为什么光速在任何参考系里都不变？Why is the speed of light the same in every reference frame?

麦克斯韦方程组给出与参考系无关的光速；迈克耳孙-莫雷未测到以太风，爱因斯坦将其列为公设。Maxwell's equations yield a frame-independent light speed; Michelson–Morley found no ether wind, and Einstein made invariance a postulate.

光速不变是狭义相对论的基本公设：在任何惯性参考系中，真空中的光速都约为 3×10⁸ m/s，不随光源和观察者的相对运动改变。这不是日常经验能直接推出的结论。低速运动中，相对速度按伽利略方式叠加，朝光源方向走会觉得光来得更快，背离则更慢；但电磁现象的规律不服从这种叠加，实验测量也不支持。The constancy of light speed is a postulate of special relativity: in every inertial frame the speed of light in vacuum is about 3×10⁸ m/s, unchanged by the relative motion of source or observer. This does not follow from everyday experience. At low speeds, relative velocities add Galilean-style: walking toward a source should make light arrive faster, walking away slower. But the laws of electromagnetism do not follow that addition, and measurements do not support it either.[9]

这条线索来自麦克斯韦方程组。19 世纪电磁学总结出电场和磁场互相激发的方程组，从中可以解出电磁波的传播速度 c = 1/√(ε₀μ₀)，只由真空介电常数 ε₀ 和磁导率 μ₀ 决定；公式里没有出现参考系，也没有出现光源速度。当时物理学家假设存在一种叫以太的光介质，认为光相对以太传播，地球绕太阳运动应穿过以太、测到一股以太风。The clue came from Maxwell's equations. Nineteenth-century electromagnetism summarized mutual generation of electric and magnetic fields into a set of equations, from which the speed of electromagnetic waves follows as c = 1/√(ε₀μ₀), set only by the vacuum permittivity ε₀ and permeability μ₀. The formula contains no reference frame and no source velocity. Physicists then assumed an ether, a light medium through which light propagated; Earth orbiting the Sun should pass through it and show an ether wind.[6][9]

1887 年迈克耳孙-莫雷实验用干涉仪比较垂直两束光的速度，想测出地球穿过以太的漂移；结果两束光没有出现预期的条纹移动，以太风测不到。后续更高精度的实验重复了同样的零结果：光速不随地球运动方向变化，也测不到光介质的相对流动。洛伦兹曾用长度收缩修补以太模型，爱因斯坦则干脆放弃以太，把光速不变作为理论公设。In 1887 the Michelson–Morley experiment used an interferometer to compare light speeds along two perpendicular arms, looking for Earth's drift through the ether. The expected fringe shift did not appear: no ether wind was detected. Later higher-precision experiments repeated the null result: light speed does not vary with Earth's motion direction, and no relative flow of a light medium shows up. Lorentz patched the ether model with length contraction, while Einstein dropped ether entirely and made invariance of light speed a postulate.[9]

光速不变与相对论原理结合，推出时间膨胀、长度收缩、同时性的相对性等结论；低速日常场景里这些效应小到察觉不到，接近光速时才显著。光速是电磁波在真空中的传播速度，不只是光这种东西跑得快；在介质中光的相速度会降低，但真空光速 c 本身是常数。现代定义中，米已由光速定义，c 精确等于 299792458 m/s。Combined with the relativity principle, constant light speed leads to time dilation, length contraction and relativity of simultaneity. At everyday speeds these effects are too small to notice; they become significant only near light speed. Light speed is the propagation speed of electromagnetic waves in vacuum, not merely light being fast; in a medium the phase speed drops, but the vacuum constant c itself stays fixed. By modern definition the meter is derived from light speed: c is exactly 299,792,458 m/s.[6][8]

边界要写清：光速不变指真空中光在惯性系中的传播速度，不排除介质中光速降低、群速度异常等情形；它是经过大量实验检验的公设，而不是从牛顿力学直觉推导出来的。把光速不变误解为光在任何情况下都最慢或最快也不准确：它是局域信息传递和有质量物体速度的上限，引力波在真空中也以 c 传播。Boundary: constancy applies to light in vacuum measured in inertial frames; it does not rule out slower light in media or anomalous group velocities. The postulate is tested by many experiments rather than derived from Newtonian intuition. Misreading it as light always being slowest or fastest is also inaccurate: c is the upper limit for local information transfer and for massive objects, and gravitational waves travel at c in vacuum as well.[8][9]

07为什么有质量的物体不能被加速到光速？Why can't a massive object be accelerated to the speed of light?

洛伦兹因子使总能量随速度趋近光速而趋于无穷；有质量物体需要无穷能量，光子静质量为零。The Lorentz factor drives total energy to infinity near c; a massive object needs infinite energy, while photons have zero rest mass.

有质量的物体无法被加速到光速，是相对论能量关系的直接结果。设静止质量为 m、速度为 v，总能量 E = γmc²，其中洛伦兹因子 γ = 1/√(1−v²/c²)；动能是总能量减去静能 (γ−1)mc²。低速时 γ 接近 1，相对论动能退化为经典的 ½mv²；速度越接近 c，γ 增长越快，每增加一点速度需要的能量越来越大。粒子加速器和宇宙线观测都反复验证了这条能量关系。A massive object cannot be accelerated to light speed, a direct result of the relativistic energy relation. With rest mass m and speed v, total energy is E = γmc², where the Lorentz factor γ = 1/√(1−v²/c²); kinetic energy is total energy minus rest energy, (γ−1)mc². At low speed γ is near 1 and relativistic kinetic energy reduces to the classical ½mv². The closer v gets to c, the faster γ grows, and each extra increment of speed demands more energy than the last.[10]

数值上可以核对：v = 0.5c 时 γ 约 1.15；0.8c 时约 1.67；0.9c 时约 2.29；0.99c 时约 7.09；0.999c 时约 22.4。当 v 趋近 c，根号里的 1−v²/c² 趋于 0，γ 趋于无穷大，把有质量物体加速到 c 需要的功和能量趋于无穷大，现实中任何燃料都提供不了。这也是粒子加速器把质子加速到 99.99% 以上光速却始终达不到 c 的原因。The numbers can be checked: at 0.5c, γ ≈ 1.15; at 0.8c, γ ≈ 1.67; at 0.9c, γ ≈ 2.29; at 0.99c, γ ≈ 7.09; at 0.999c, γ ≈ 22.4. As v approaches c, the term 1−v²/c² approaches 0 and γ diverges to infinity. Accelerating a massive object all the way to c requires work and energy that go to infinity, which no fuel source can supply. This is why particle accelerators push protons above 99.99% of light speed but never reach c.[6][10]

所谓接近光速时质量变大，是通俗说法。严格讲，静止质量 m 是物体内禀不变量；随速度增大而增长的是相对论性总能量和动量，惯性阻力变大表现为难以继续加速。早期文献把 γm 称为动质量，现代教材更多直接写 E=γmc²，避免把质量当成随速度变化的量；动质量这一概念现在已较少使用，容易让人误以为质量本身可以被速度改变。The popular phrase that mass grows near light speed is loose. Strictly, rest mass m is an intrinsic invariant of the object; what grows with speed is relativistic total energy and momentum, and the increased inertia shows up as harder acceleration. Older literature called γm relativistic mass, while modern texts write E=γmc² directly, avoiding treating mass as a speed-dependent quantity.[10]

光子能以光速运动，是因为它静止质量为零。静质量为零的粒子不受 E=γmc² 中质量项的约束，在真空中只能以 c 运动，不能减速到低于光速；有静质量的粒子永远在 c 以下。中微子曾被认为无质量，振荡实验证明它有极小的静质量，因此速度略低于 c，这与上述规律一致。Photons travel at light speed because their rest mass is zero. A zero-rest-mass particle is not bound by the mass term in E=γmc²; in vacuum it can only move at c and cannot slow below it, while particles with nonzero rest mass always stay below c. Neutrinos were once thought massless, but oscillation experiments show a tiny rest mass, so their speed is slightly below c, consistent with the same rule.[6][8]

边界要写清：这条限制说的是物体通过局域加速达到或超过 c，不涉及空间本身膨胀导致的遥远星系退行；也不否定相速度、群速度等可以超过 c 的数学情形，那些不传递信息或实物。把不能超光速理解成科技不够发达、引擎更强就行，是误解：瓶颈是能量与因果结构，不是工程推力大小。Boundary: this limit concerns an object reaching or passing c through local acceleration; it does not cover recession of distant galaxies driven by expanding space, and it does not rule out mathematical cases such as phase or group velocities above c, which carry no information or matter. Reading no faster-than-light as better engines would solve it misses the point: the bottleneck is energy and causal structure, not engineering thrust.[8][10]

08超光速为什么会破坏因果关系？宇宙膨胀超光速算不算超光速运动？Why would faster-than-light motion break causality, and does cosmic expansion count as faster-than-light travel?

超光速信号在某些惯性系中因果时序颠倒；快子只是假想粒子；空间膨胀的退行不是局域运动，不传递信息。An FTL signal reverses cause-effect order in some frames; tachyons are hypothetical; expansion-driven recession is not local motion and carries no information.

因果性要求结果发生在原因之后，而且这个次序在所有惯性参考系中不能颠倒。相对论中，两个事件的时序取决于参考系；如果存在超光速信号连接两个事件，总能找到某个惯性系，在其中信号先到、后发，也就是结果出现在原因之前，形成因果悖论：收到回信之后才发出消息。因此有质量物体和局域信息传递不能超过 c，与能量限制相互印证。Causality requires the effect to follow the cause, with an order that cannot reverse across inertial frames. In relativity the time order of two events depends on the frame; if an FTL signal connected them, one could always find an inertial frame in which the signal arrives before it leaves, so the effect precedes the cause and creates a causal paradox: getting a reply before sending the message. Massive objects and local information transfer therefore cannot exceed c, which agrees with the energy limit.[6][10]

快子是理论上静质量为虚数、速度天然高于 c 的假想粒子。它只是相对论方程允许的数学解，至今没有任何观测证据；即便存在，快子也不能用来向过去发送信息，理论上的不稳定性使它不被视为真实粒子。快子若存在，能量越低速度反而越快，这与普通粒子的直觉正好相反。科幻作品里的超光速通信、曲速航行不属于现有实验物理，需要修改相对论因果结构才能成立。A tachyon is a hypothetical particle with imaginary rest mass, naturally faster than c. It is only a mathematical solution allowed by the relativity equations, with no observational evidence to date; even if it existed, it could not carry messages into the past, and theoretical instability keeps it from being treated as a real particle. FTL communication or warp travel in fiction is not experimental physics; it would require changing the causal structure of relativity.[6]

宇宙膨胀造成的退行速度可以超过光速，但这不算物体在空间中运动超过 c。遥远星系之间的空间本身在膨胀，星系随空间被带走，局域速度仍低于 c；可观测宇宙边缘附近的退行速度大于 c，那里发出的光至今还在向我们传播。哈勃定律描述距离与退行速度的关系，膨胀速率随时标变化，这种超光速退行不违反局域速度上限。Recession driven by cosmic expansion can exceed c, but this is not an object moving through space faster than c. Space itself between distant galaxies expands, carrying galaxies along; their local speed stays below c. Near the edge of the observable universe, recession speed is greater than c, yet light emitted there is still traveling toward us. Hubble's law relates distance to recession speed, and expansion rate changes with cosmic time; such FTL recession does not violate the local speed limit.[11]

量子纠缠也常被说成超光速。两个纠缠粒子测量结果相关，但这种相关不能用来主动发送信息：对一端的测量结果是随机的，另一端只能事后对照数据才看出关联，信息传递仍受光速限制。纠缠不是一根超光速的操纵杆，不能用它遥控或传讯；测量顺序和距离安排不改变这一结论，两端之间不存在可利用的信号通道。Quantum entanglement is often called faster-than-light. Measurement results on two entangled particles are correlated, but the correlation cannot carry an active message: the result on one end is random, and the other side sees the link only afterward by comparing records, so information transfer still respects light speed. Entanglement is not an FTL joystick for remote control or signaling.[6][11]

边界要写清：本章区分三类说法。局域物体加速超光速受能量与因果禁止；数学上的快子、相速度等不构成可观测实体或信息通道；空间膨胀是几何效应，不传递局域信息。把宇宙可以超光速退行当成飞船能超光速飞行的证据，是把空间膨胀和物体运动混为一谈。曲速、虫洞等设想属于另一类时空几何问题，不能作为超光速航行已被允许的证据。Boundary: this chapter separates three claims. Local objects accelerating past c are forbidden by energy and causality; mathematical tachyons and phase velocities form no observable entity or information channel; space expansion is a geometric effect that sends no local information. Treating galaxies receding faster than light as evidence that a ship could fly faster than light confuses expanding space with object motion.[6][10]

09虫洞是怎么形成的？它真的能当宇宙捷径吗？How would a wormhole form, and could it really be a cosmic shortcut?

虫洞是爱因斯坦方程的理论解（爱因斯坦-罗森桥），连接两处时空；可穿越喉道需负能量物质，至今无观测证据。A wormhole is a theoretical Einstein-equation solution, an Einstein–Rosen bridge linking two spacetime regions; a traversable throat needs negative-energy matter, with no evidence yet.

虫洞是广义相对论方程的一类理论解：时空几何可以连接两个相隔遥远的区域，形成一条穿过弯曲时空的喉道，早期解由爱因斯坦和罗森在 1935 年提出，称爱因斯坦-罗森桥。它和黑洞一样，从爱因斯坦引力方程的数学解出发；描述的是时空曲率在某处高度卷曲、两端分别通向不同时空区域的几何结构，不是某条现成的星际隧道。A wormhole is a class of theoretical solution to the equations of general relativity: spacetime geometry can link two widely separated regions through a throat that crosses curved spacetime. An early solution was proposed by Einstein and Rosen in 1935, called the Einstein–Rosen bridge. Like a black hole, it starts from a mathematical solution of Einstein's gravity equations; it describes a geometry where curvature curls sharply and both ends open into different spacetime regions, not an existing interstellar tunnel.[12]

天然形成的喉道不能通行。最简单的史瓦西虫洞解连接两个时空区域，但喉道形成后会瞬时坍缩，任何光或物质都来不及穿过；把它想象成稳定的两扇门对穿，不符合方程解的行为。1988 年莫里斯和索恩提出可穿越虫洞模型：要让喉道张开不坍缩，内部必须有负能量密度的奇异物质撑开几何，普通物质产生的正能量引力只会让喉道闭合。A natural throat is not passable. The simplest Schwarzschild wormhole solution connects two regions, but the throat pinches off instantly after forming; no light or matter can cross in time. Picturing it as two stable doors facing each other does not match the solution's behavior. In 1988 Morris and Thorne proposed a traversable wormhole model: to hold the throat open without collapse, it must be propped by exotic matter with negative energy density; ordinary matter produces positive-energy gravity that only closes the throat.[6][12]

负能量在物理上并非完全陌生：卡西米尔效应中两块靠近的导体板之间存在负能量密度区域，但量级极小、范围受限，远不足以撑开一条宏观虫洞。是否存在足够维持可穿越虫洞的物质和几何条件，目前没有理论定论，更没有工程方案；宏观尺度上维持一条张开的喉道，所需的负能量总量和分布条件都只是理论推测。把虫洞当作现成的交通设施，超出了现有证据范围。Negative energy is not entirely unfamiliar in physics: the Casimir effect produces a region of negative energy density between two nearby conducting plates, but the amount is tiny and limited in range, far from enough to prop open a macroscopic wormhole. Whether the matter and geometry needed for a traversable wormhole exist has no settled theoretical answer, let alone an engineering plan. Treating wormholes as ready-made transport facilities goes beyond current evidence.[13]

至今没有观测到任何虫洞。黑洞已有阴影成像、引力波并合等多条观测证据，虫洞则没有对应的天体观测特征；NASA 等机构也明确区分：黑洞不是虫洞，虫洞不提供通往其他星系或维度的捷径。银河系中心附近是否存在奇特致密天体，需要靠恒星轨道、吸积辐射和引力波等观测约束，不能仅凭几何猜想认定。No wormhole has been observed so far. Black holes have several observational lines, including shadow imaging and gravitational-wave mergers, while wormholes have no matching observational signature. Agencies such as NASA also distinguish clearly: a black hole is not a wormhole, and a wormhole does not offer a shortcut to another galaxy or dimension. Whether exotic compact bodies exist near the Galactic center must be constrained by stellar orbits, accretion radiation and gravitational waves, not by geometric guesswork.[12][13]

边界要写清：本章讲方程解、维持条件与证据现状。虫洞是理论模型，不是已发现的天体；可穿越要求的奇异物质是否真实存在于宏观尺度，仍是开放问题。科幻中的时间旅行、平行宇宙入口不纳入本章结论。判断一条时空几何解能否成为真实天体，标准是存在可检验的观测效应，而不是数学上画得出来。Boundary: this chapter covers the equation solution, the maintenance condition and the current evidence status. A wormhole is a theoretical model, not a discovered object; whether exotic matter needed for traversability exists at macroscopic scale remains an open question. Time travel and parallel-universe entrances in fiction are not part of this conclusion. The standard for a spacetime solution to count as a real body is a testable observational effect, not a drawable mathematical shape.[6][12]
