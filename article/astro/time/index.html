01一天为什么有白天和黑夜？自转一周到底是 23 小时 56 分还是 24 小时？Why are there day and night, and is one rotation 23 hours 56 minutes or 24 hours?

自转朝向太阳为昼、背向为夜；恒星日 23h56m4s 对星空，太阳日 24h 对太阳，日历用太阳日。Rotation faces the Sun for day, away for night; sidereal day 23h56m4s against stars, solar day 24h against the Sun.

白天和黑夜来自地球自转：地球自西向东绕轴转动，从北极上空看呈逆时针方向，朝向太阳的一面被阳光照亮就是昼，背向太阳的一面是夜，昼夜交界的晨昏线随自转扫过地表。春分和秋分两天，晨昏线穿过南北两极，全球昼夜接近等长。地球自转一周的时间有两种计量方式，分别对应遥远恒星和太阳，两者相差约四分钟。Day and night come from Earth's rotation: the planet turns west to east on its axis, the side facing the Sun is lit as day, the side away is night, and the terminator sweeps across the surface. One rotation can be measured against two references, the distant stars and the Sun, and the two differ by about four minutes.[1][7]

恒星日是地球相对遥远恒星真正转一周的周期，以春分点为参考，约 23 小时 56 分 4.1 秒；太阳日是连续两次太阳经过同一子午线的间隔，平均为 24 小时。差别来自地球同时在绕太阳公转：自转一天期间，地球在轨道上前进约 0.986°，地球需要再多转约四分钟，才能让太阳重新对准同一方向。真太阳日受椭圆轨道速度和轴倾角影响，每天长短不一，日常使用的平太阳日是全年平均后的结果。A sidereal day is one true rotation relative to the distant stars, about 23 hours 56 minutes 4.1 seconds. A solar day is the interval between two successive passages of the Sun over the same meridian, averaging 24 hours. The difference arises because Earth also orbits the Sun: during one rotation it advances about 0.986° along its orbit, so it must turn another four minutes to bring the Sun back to the same direction.[1][7]

日常日历按太阳日的 24 小时编排，因为人类作息、昼夜安排和农事都以太阳在天空中的位置为准；恒星日主要用于天文观测和星表定位。两种日长并不恒定：潮汐摩擦让地球自转长期缓慢减慢，日长每世纪约增加 1.7 毫秒；1972 年引入闰秒以来，协调世界时曾多次通过加一秒对齐地球自转，现代则通过闰秒机制协调两者偏差。The everyday calendar follows the 24-hour solar day, because human schedules, daylight routines and farming track the Sun's position in the sky; the sidereal day is used mainly for astronomical observation and star-catalog positioning. Neither day length is perfectly constant: tidal friction slows Earth's rotation slowly over time, and leap seconds coordinate universal time with the rotation deviation.[1][2]

地球自转轴还决定了不同纬度的昼夜长短：赤道附近全年昼夜接近各 12 小时，高纬度地区夏季昼长、冬季夜长，南北极圈（纬度约 66.5°）以内出现极昼和极夜。这部分变化由轴倾角和公转位置共同造成，属于季节问题，而不是自转速度本身的变化；地球轨道偏心率很小，日地距离季节差异远小于轴倾角的影响。The axis also sets day-night length by latitude: near the equator day and night stay close to 12 hours all year, while high latitudes have long summer days and long winter nights, with polar day and night inside the polar circles. That variation comes from axial tilt and orbital position, not from a change in rotation speed itself.[1][7]

把“一天 23 小时 56 分”和“日历 24 小时”当成矛盾，是混淆了两种参考系。恒星日量的是地球相对星空的真实转动，太阳日量的是相对太阳的视运动；历法选择太阳日，是为了让时钟与日出日落保持同步。Treating "one day is 23 hours 56 minutes" and "the calendar uses 24 hours" as a contradiction mixes two reference frames. The sidereal day measures true rotation against the star field; the solar day measures apparent motion against the Sun. The calendar keeps the solar day so clocks stay synchronized with sunrise and sunset.[1][2]

02公历和农历怎样协调年、月、日？闰年和闰月是怎么补上周期差的？How do Gregorian and lunar calendars reconcile year, month and day, and how do leap rules close the cycle gap?

回归年 365.2422 天、朔望月 29.5306 天不可整除；公历四年一闰，农历十九年七闰。The 365.2422-day year and 29.5306-day month do not divide evenly; Gregorian leap every fourth, lunar seven months in nineteen years.

历法要协调三种彼此不可公度的周期：地球自转的日、月球绕地的朔望月、地球绕日的回归年。回归年约 365.2422 平太阳日，朔望月约 29.5306 天；十二个朔望月约 354.37 天，比回归年少约十一天，一年折合约 12.37 个朔望月，不是整数。任何单一周期都不能同时整除另外两个，历法必须用置闰来补齐，本质上是对不可公度周期的整数逼近。A calendar must reconcile three incommensurable cycles: Earth's rotation day, the lunar synodic month, and the tropical year. The tropical year is about 365.2422 mean solar days, the synodic month about 29.5306 days; twelve synodic months give about 354.37 days, roughly eleven days short of the year. No single cycle divides the others evenly, so intercalation is required to close the gap.[1][2]

公历以回归年为基准。平年 365 天，闰年 366 天，二月加一天；闰年规则是四年一闰、百年不闰、四百年再闰：能被 4 整除的年份通常闰年，整百年份需被 400 整除才闰。这套规则把平均年长定为 365.2425 天，与回归年 365.2422 天的偏差积累到千年量级才需要调整。此前使用的儒略历每年 365.25 天偏大约 0.0078 天，十六世纪春分日比古罗马时期提前约十天，由此推动了十六世纪末的公历改革。The Gregorian calendar follows the tropical year. Common years have 365 days, leap years 366 with an extra February day. The leap rule: every fourth year, except century years, which need divisibility by 400. This sets the average year to 365.2425 days, and the deviation from the 365.2422 tropical year only accumulates to a correction on the millennial scale.[1][2]

农历以月相定月：朔日为初一，一个月从一次新月到下一次新月，长度在 29 到 30 天之间，朔的时刻由月球和太阳的黄经位置计算。为了让农历年与回归年对齐，农历采用十九年七闰的置闰规则，把不含中气的月份设为闰月，使春节始终落在冬春之交；不置闰的农历年约 354 天，置闰年约 384 天。The Chinese lunar calendar sets months by phases: new moon marks day one, and a month runs from one new moon to the next, lasting 29 or 30 days. To align the lunar year with the tropical year, it uses a nineteen-year seven-leap-month cycle, inserting an extra month in selected years so the New Year stays between winter and spring. A non-leap lunar year has about 354 days, a leap year about 384.[2][3]

节气是农历中的阳历成分：二十四节气按太阳黄经划分，太阳每沿黄道前进 15° 为一个节气，节气与中气交替排列，与月相无关。因此节气在公历上的日期相对固定，而农历月份随月相摆动；同一套历法同时承载了太阳年和太阴月两套周期，干支纪年则以六十年为一个循环。Solar terms are the solar-calendar component inside the lunar system: the twenty-four terms follow the Sun's ecliptic longitude, one term every 15° along the ecliptic, independent of lunar phase. That is why solar-term dates stay roughly fixed on the Gregorian calendar while lunar months swing with the phases; one calendar carries both solar-year and lunar-month cycles.[1][2]

历法规则是人为约定的近似，不是自然周期本身。不同文明发展过埃及历、儒略历、伊斯兰历等不同体系，取舍在于优先对齐太阳还是月亮；现代公历选择对齐回归年，农历保留月相外观再用闰月补偿年差。Calendar rules are human-made approximations, not the natural cycles themselves. Different civilizations developed Egyptian, Julian, Islamic and other systems, choosing whether to prioritize the Sun or the Moon. The modern Gregorian calendar aligns to the tropical year; the lunar calendar keeps the lunar appearance and compensates the year gap with leap months.[2][3]

03一秒钟现在是怎样定义的？原子钟比机械钟、石英钟准在哪里？How is one second defined today, and how do atomic clocks outperform mechanical and quartz clocks?

秒由铯-133 跃迁 9,192,631,770 个周期定义；喷泉钟不确定度约 10⁻¹⁶，全球钟比对产生 UTC。The second is 9,192,631,770 cesium-133 transition periods; fountain clocks reach ~10⁻¹⁶ uncertainty and global clocks compare into UTC.

时间测量经历了从天体投影到机械振荡、再到原子跃迁的演进。日晷靠太阳投影读时，受天气和季节限制；机械钟用摆轮或擒纵机构把摆动周期传给指针，摆周期受重力、温度和摩擦影响；石英钟利用晶体压电谐振，常见频率 32768 赫兹，即 2 的 15 次方，便于分频计数；原子钟则以原子内部能级跃迁的固定频率作为频率基准，稳定性远高于机械和石英器件。Timekeeping evolved from celestial shadows to mechanical oscillation and then to atomic transitions. Sundials read the Sun's shadow, limited by weather and season; mechanical clocks pass a pendulum or balance-wheel period to the hands through an escapement; quartz clocks use crystal piezoelectric resonance, commonly 32,768 hertz; atomic clocks take the fixed frequency of an internal atomic energy transition as the frequency reference, far more stable than mechanical or quartz devices.[2]

今天国际单位制的秒由原子定义：1 秒是铯-133 原子基态超精细跃迁所辐射周期的 9,192,631,770 倍，对应微波频率约 9.192631770 GHz。这一定义 1967 年由第十三届国际计量大会通过，把时间基准从地球天文运动转移到不受环境影响的量子跃迁上；铯原子束和喷泉装置通过激光冷却把原子团抛起、再探测跃迁频率。The modern SI second is defined atomically: one second equals 9,192,631,770 periods of the radiation from the ground-state hyperfine transition of cesium-133. Adopted into the SI in 1967, this definition moved the time reference away from Earth's astronomical motion to an environment-independent quantum transition. Cesium beam and fountain devices laser-cool a cloud, toss it upward, and probe the transition frequency.[2][4]

铯喷泉钟如 NIST-F2 的频率不确定度在 10⁻¹⁶ 量级，意味着数千万年累积误差不超过一秒；近年发展的锶、镱光钟工作在可见光频段，频率更高，稳定度可达 10⁻¹⁸ 量级，有望成为下一代秒基准。单个时钟的输出还要与全球网络比对：各国守时机构的原子钟通过卫星链路交换数据，国际计量局按权重平均后产生协调世界时 UTC，UTC 不是某一台钟的读数。Cesium fountain clocks such as NIST-F2 reach frequency uncertainty around 10⁻¹⁶, meaning accumulated error stays under one second over tens of millions of years. Optical clocks, working at visible-light frequencies, run higher and more stable and may become the next second standard. A single clock output is compared against a global network: national atomic clocks exchange data over satellite links, and the International Bureau of Weights and Measures combines them into Coordinated Universal Time, UTC.[2][3]

日常使用的北京时间、美国东部时间等都基于地方标准时与 UTC 的偏移；授时信号通过无线电、卫星和网络分发，GNSS 卫星本身就携带星载原子钟广播时间。原子钟不仅用于守时，也是卫星导航、深空测距、甚长基线射电干涉测量和基础物理检验的时间基础设施。Everyday time zones such as Beijing Time or US Eastern Time are local standard offsets from UTC; time signals are distributed by radio, satellite and network. Atomic clocks keep more than official time: they are the time infrastructure for satellite navigation, deep-space ranging, radio interferometry and tests of fundamental physics.[2][3]

把原子钟理解成“更准的机械表”并不准确。它不依靠齿轮和发条，而是测量量子跃迁频率，再由电子计数器把固定振荡周期折算成秒；钟的精度瓶颈来自原子碰撞、热噪声和环境干扰，而不是走针机构的磨损。不同类型原子钟在长期稳定度、复现性和体积上各有取舍，守时、导航和实验室研究选用的型号并不相同。Thinking of an atomic clock as a "more accurate mechanical watch" misses the point. It has no gears or mainspring; it measures a quantum-transition frequency, and an electronic counter converts the fixed oscillation periods into seconds. Its accuracy is limited by atomic collisions, thermal noise and environmental interference, not by wear of a hand-driving mechanism.[2][4]

04卫星定位为什么要做相对论时间修正？两种效应各差多少，不修正会怎样？Why do satellite clocks need relativistic correction, how large are the two effects, and what happens without it?

狭义钟慢 -7.2 μs/天，广义钟快 +45.9 μs/天，净 +38.7 μs/天，不修正每天偏约 11.6 千米。Motion slows the clock -7.2 μs/day, gravity speeds it +45.9 μs/day, net +38.7 μs/day; uncorrected bias reaches about 11.6 km per day.

卫星导航必须同时考虑两种相对论时间效应。狭义相对论指出高速运动的钟走得慢，广义相对论指出处于较弱引力势高处的钟走得快；GPS 卫星在约 20,200 千米轨道上飞行，轨道周期约 12 个恒星时，速度约 3.9 千米每秒，引力比地面弱，两种效应方向相反，必须在星载钟里预先修正。Satellite navigation must account for two relativistic time effects together. Special relativity says a fast-moving clock runs slow; general relativity says a clock higher in a weaker gravitational potential runs fast. GPS satellites fly fast at about 20,000 kilometers in a weaker field than the ground, so the two effects oppose each other and must be pre-corrected in the onboard clock.[5][6]

定量估算可以核对：狭义运动钟慢约 -7.2 微秒每天，广义引力钟快约 +45.9 微秒每天，净效应约 +38.7 微秒每天。如果不做相对论修正，卫星钟每天比地面快约 38.7 微秒；按光速约 3×10⁸ 米每秒换算，这段时间对应的距离约 11.6 千米，定位误差会每天累积十公里量级。The numbers can be checked: special-relativistic motion slows the clock by about -7.2 microseconds per day, general-relativistic gravity speeds it by about +45.9 microseconds per day, for a net of about +38.7 microseconds per day. Without relativistic correction the satellite clock would run about 38.7 microseconds fast daily; at light speed near 3×10⁸ meters per second, that interval corresponds to about 11.6 kilometers, and position error would accumulate to ten kilometers per day.[5][6]

GPS 的定位原理依赖时间同步：每颗卫星广播自身精确时刻和星历，接收机至少收到四颗卫星信号，由信号传播时差解算三维位置和接收机钟差；钟差参数和星历由地面控制段上行注入。纳秒级时间误差直接折算成米级距离误差，因此轨道测量和钟差修正都要进入导航解算；民用单点定位精度通常米级，差分或载波相位测量可以达到厘米级。GPS positioning relies on time synchronization: each satellite broadcasts its own precise time, and a receiver hears at least four signals, solving three-dimensional position and clock offset from travel-time differences. Nanosecond time error converts directly to meter-level distance error, so orbit measurements and clock corrections enter the navigation solution. Single-point civilian positioning is typically meter-level; differential or carrier-phase methods reach centimeter level.[2][6]

深空轨道测量同样靠时间与测距：地面站测量无线电信号往返时延和多普勒频移，结合已知光速定出距离和径向速度；甚长基线干涉测量用多个射电望远镜同时记录信号，靠时间戳对齐观测。定轨精度从千米级到厘米级不等，取决于测距精度、跟踪弧段和力学模型。卫星入轨后的轨道确定，本身就是一次高精度时间同步实验。Deep-space orbit measurements rely on time and ranging as well: ground stations measure round-trip radio delay and Doppler shift, then derive distance and radial speed from the known light speed. Orbit-determination precision ranges from kilometers to centimeters, depending on ranging accuracy, tracking arc and dynamical model. Post-launch orbit determination is itself a high-precision time-synchronization experiment.[2][6]

相对论修正在地面低速、弱场日常活动中可以忽略，手表和手机不需要逐日补偿，但在卫星和深空尺度上不是可选项。早期 GPS 设计阶段曾对是否纳入相对论修正有过争论，现在星载钟和接收机算法都按相对论时标运行，地面控制段还会监测钟差并更新参数；把相对论当成远离现实的理论，会低估它对导航和通信基础设施的约束。Relativistic correction can be ignored in slow, weak-field everyday life, but on the satellite and deep-space scale it is not optional. Early GPS design debated whether to include relativistic correction; today onboard clocks and receiver algorithms run on relativistic time scales. Treating relativity as a theory removed from reality understates how it constrains navigation and communication infrastructure.[5][6]

05时间单位为什么用 60 和 12 进制？时间又为什么只能向前走，不能倒流？Why are time units based on 60 and 12, and why does time flow forward rather than backward?

60 进制源于古巴比伦、便于整除；宏观时间箭头由熵增定义，微观定律可逆但宏观不可逆。The 60-base comes from Babylonian divisibility; the macroscopic arrow follows entropy increase, though microscopic laws are reversible.

时间单位的进制留下了古代计数传统。现代一小时 60 分、一分 60 秒，60 进制可追溯到古巴比伦的计数体系，同一套进制也影响了角度的度分秒；60 能被 2、3、4、5、6、10、12、15、20、30 整除，便于把时间均匀切分。一昼夜分 24 小时、白天黑夜各 12 小时，12 进制与手指计数和黄道十二宫的划分有关；这些单位是人为约定，不是物理定律规定的。Time-unit bases preserve ancient counting traditions. The modern hour of 60 minutes and minute of 60 seconds descend from the Babylonian sexagesimal system; 60 divides evenly by 2, 3, 4, 5, 6, 10, 12, 15, 20 and 30, which makes uniform division easy. A day is split into 24 hours, twelve for day and twelve for night, and the duodecimal base relates to finger counting and the twelve zodiac signs. These units are human conventions, not physical laws.[1][2]

时间为什么单向流动，是物理学的时间箭头问题。微观物理定律对时间反演基本对称，但宏观系统有明确方向：孤立系统的熵，即无序程度，不会减少，热量自发从高温流向低温，冰块在室温融化而不会自行复原。热力学箭头定义了过去与未来，我们的记忆和因果推断也顺着同一方向。Why time flows one way is the physics question of the arrow of time. Microscopic laws are nearly time-reversal symmetric, but macroscopic systems have a clear direction: in an isolated system entropy, or disorder, never decreases; heat flows spontaneously from hot to cold, an ice cube melts at room temperature and does not reassemble on its own. The thermodynamic arrow defines past and future, and our memory and causal reasoning follow the same direction.[2][5]

微观粒子碰撞原则上可逆，但大量粒子组成的系统落在低熵初始状态的概率极低；宇宙早期的低熵状态给了宏观过程一个起点，此后熵增推动演化，这一初始低熵的来源本身仍是宇宙学未解问题。心理上的“先经历后记忆”、生物学的生长衰老、宇宙的膨胀冷却，都与热力学箭头方向一致；理论上的庞加莱回归是极长时标上的统计再现，与日常时间箭头无关。时间反演在宏观尺度上看不到对应的自然过程。Individual particle collisions are in principle reversible, but a many-particle system starting in a low-entropy state is extremely improbable. The universe's low-entropy early state gave macroscopic processes a starting point, after which entropy increase drove evolution. Psychological memory, biological growth and aging, and cosmic expansion and cooling all align with the thermodynamic arrow; no natural macroscopic process shows the reverse.[2][5]

把不同时标放在一起对照，人类有文字的历史约几千年，文明史以万年计，地球年龄约 45.6 亿年，宇宙年龄约 138 亿年。日常秒、分、时对应人体活动尺度，地质年代和宇宙学时标则需要年、百万年、十亿年这样的单位；同一时间概念在不同尺度上用不同单位度量。Placing different timescales side by side: written human history is a few thousand years, civilization on the order of ten thousand years, Earth about 4.56 billion years old, the universe about 13.8 billion. Seconds, minutes and hours fit human activity; geological and cosmic scales need years, millions of years and billions of years. The same time concept is measured in different units at different scales.[1][2]

时间单位可以重新定义和换算，时间箭头却不能靠约定反转。现代秒由原子跃迁定义，与古代日晷读数完全不同，闰秒则用来协调原子时与地球自转；但过去与未来的区分仍由熵增决定。把“时间倒流”当成工程上可以尝试的方向，混淆了单位约定、定律可逆性和宏观不可逆性三个层次。Time units can be redefined and converted, but the arrow of time cannot be reversed by convention. The modern second is defined by atomic transition, utterly different from a sundial reading, yet the distinction between past and future still follows entropy increase. Treating time reversal as an engineering option mixes up three levels: unit convention, law-level reversibility, and macroscopic irreversibility.[2][5]
