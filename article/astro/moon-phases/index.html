01月亮为什么有圆缺？月相是地球影子造成的吗？Why does the Moon have phases, and are they Earth's shadow?

月相是被阳光照亮的半球在轨道上的可见角度，周期 29.5306 天；地球影子落到月球上是月食，与月相不同。Phases are the illuminated hemisphere seen from orbital angles, repeating every 29.5306 days; Earth's shadow on the Moon is an eclipse, not a phase.

月相不是地球影子扫过月亮造成的，而是月球被太阳照亮的半球在不同角度被我们看到的结果。月球自己不发光，靠反射阳光发亮；它绕地球转动，日、地、月三者的夹角不断变化，从地球上看到的被照亮部分比例就周期性改变。一个朔望月为 29.5306 天，从新月到满月再回到新月完成一轮，相邻两次满月相隔约 29.5 天。Phases are not the Earth's shadow sweeping across the Moon; they are the illuminated hemisphere seen from different angles as the Moon orbits. The Moon makes no light of its own and glows only by reflected sunlight. As the Sun-Earth-Moon geometry changes, the fraction facing Earth that we see lit cycles through the month. A synodic month, from full Moon to full Moon, lasts 29.5306 days, with successive full Moons about 29.5 days apart.[3]

几何上，新月时月球在地球与太阳之间，被照亮的一面背对地球，肉眼几乎看不见；上弦月时日、地、月成直角，我们看到西半面发亮；满月时地球位于日月之间，朝向地球的一面全被照亮；下弦月则看到东半面发亮。月球相对恒星转一圈是恒星月，27.3217 天，比朔望月短，因为地球同时在绕太阳公转，月亮要多转一点角度才能回到相同的日地月位置。Geometrically, at new Moon the Moon lies between Earth and Sun, so its lit side faces away from Earth and it is nearly invisible. At first quarter the three bodies form a right angle, and we see the western half lit. At full Moon Earth lies between Sun and Moon, so the Earth-facing side is fully lit, and at last quarter the eastern half shines. The sidereal month, one orbit relative to the stars, is 27.3217 days, shorter than the synodic month because Earth itself moves around the Sun, and the Moon must rotate a little farther to return to the same geometry.[3]

把月相当成地球影子是常见误解。地球影子投到月球上属于月食，只在满月且月球经过轨道交点附近时发生；月相则每月循环，不同时辰月亮的位置和亮面方向都可以预测。从南半球看，亮面朝向与北半球相反；同一轮月相在两地看到的方向是镜像的，南北半球观测者会把弯月看成开口方向相反的两只小船。Treating phases as Earth's shadow is a common mistake. Earth's shadow falling on the Moon is a lunar eclipse, which occurs only near full Moon when the Moon crosses an orbital node; phases repeat every month on a predictable schedule, with the Moon's position and lit orientation changing through the night. From the Southern Hemisphere the lit side appears mirrored relative to the Northern Hemisphere, so a crescent opens in the opposite direction on the two sides of the equator.[3][5]

月相周期是月球运动最基本的观测事实之一。月亮表面反照率并不高，满月之所以显得亮，部分原因是满月附近月表投下的阴影最短，低洼月海被亮背景衬托得更深。相位顺序与月出月落时间一一对应：新月随日出月落，上弦月正午升起、黄昏高悬，满月在日落时升起、午夜高悬。连续一个月记录月亮形状和方位，就能核对这套几何关系。The phase cycle is one of the most basic observations of lunar motion. The Moon's surface albedo is not high; part of the full Moon's brightness comes from the shortest surface shadows near full phase, when dark maria stand out against a bright background. The phase sequence lines up with rise and set times: the new Moon rises at sunrise, first quarter rises at noon and stands high at dusk, and the full Moon rises at sunset and stands high at midnight. Recording shape and position for a month checks this geometry directly.[1][3]

边界：月相由日地月几何决定，与月球自转快慢无关；看到的“弯月”既不是被云遮住，也不是地球影子。云层和大气条件会影响观测亮度，但不改变相位本身。朔望月长度是平均值，因轨道椭圆和扰动，实际间隔会有小幅起伏；把月相盈亏和月食混为一谈，是最常见的概念错误。The boundary: phases follow Sun-Earth-Moon geometry and do not depend on how fast the Moon spins; a crescent is neither cloud-covered nor Earth-shadowed. Clouds and atmosphere affect observed brightness but not the phase itself. The synodic month is an average; orbital ellipse and perturbations make individual intervals vary slightly. Conflating monthly phases with an eclipse is the most common conceptual mistake.[3]

02为什么我们永远只能看到月亮的同一面？Why do we always see only one face of the Moon?

月球自转与公转同步（潮汐锁定），同一面朝向地球；天平动让可见表面约占 59%，背面并非永远黑暗。The Moon is tidally locked with matching spin and orbit, keeping one face toward Earth; libration reveals about 59 percent, and the far side is not permanently dark.

我们永远只看到月球的同一面，是因为月球的自转周期与绕地球公转周期相等，这叫潮汐锁定：它自转一圈的同时也绕地球转一圈，同一面始终朝向地球。月球背面并非不存在，只是从地球无法直接看见；人类直到探测器进入月球轨道，才第一次看到背面全貌。We only ever see one face of the Moon because its rotation period equals its orbital period around Earth, a state called tidal locking: it spins once while orbiting once, so the same hemisphere stays turned toward us. The far side still exists; it simply cannot be seen directly from Earth, and only orbital probes showed its full landscape.[3]

锁定由地球引力长期塑造。地球引力在月球两侧造成潮汐隆起，潮汐摩擦与引力耦合把月球自转逐渐“刹”到与公转同步；月球质心又略偏向地球一侧，使这种朝向形成稳定平衡。天平动让我们能看到稍多于一半的表面，约占总面积的 59%：轨道椭圆使公转速度有快有慢，轨道倾角也带来小幅摆动，背面边缘部分区域会周期性露出来。The lock was shaped over time by Earth's gravity. Earth's pull raises tidal bulges on the Moon, and tidal friction plus gravitational coupling gradually braked the Moon's spin into sync with its orbit. Its centre of mass sits slightly toward Earth, making this orientation a stable balance. Libration lets us see a little more than half the surface, about 59 percent: the elliptical orbit speeds and slows the revolution, and the orbital tilt adds a small wobble, so parts near the far-side edge come into view periodically.[1][3]

背对地球的一面常被误称为“黑暗的一面”，其实它在新月前后同样被阳光照亮，只是那时正面背对太阳、难以从地球观测。背面高地多、月海少，与正面外观差别很大；从背面直接向地球传输信号受阻，绕月与着陆探测需要中继卫星配合。阿波罗与绕月探测器测绘了背面的地形和成分，证实两面在地质上并不对称，月壳厚度也有明显差异。The hemisphere away from Earth is often wrongly called the dark side, but it is lit by sunlight around new Moon just as the near side is lit around full Moon; the near side then faces away from the Sun and is hard to observe from Earth. The far side is mostly highland with fewer maria, and looks very different from the near side; direct signals to Earth are blocked there, so orbiters and landers need a relay satellite. Apollo and orbital surveys mapped far-side topography and composition, confirming the two faces are geologically asymmetric, with markedly different crustal thicknesses.[1][3]

NASA 的潮汐锁定说明和 OpenStax 教材都把同步自转列为月球运动的基本特征。月球目前仍以约 3.8 cm/yr 远离地球；潮汐相互作用同时在减慢地球自转、拉长昼夜，并把角动量从地球自转移到月球轨道。这一过程已由阿波罗反射镜的激光测距直接测量，测距精度达到厘米级，数据还能反演月球内部结构和地球自转变化。NASA's tidal-locking material and the OpenStax textbook both list synchronous rotation as a basic property of lunar motion. The Moon still recedes at about 3.8 cm per year; the same tidal interaction slows Earth's spin, lengthens the day, and transfers angular momentum from Earth's rotation to the lunar orbit, measured directly by laser ranging to Apollo mirrors. The ranging data also constrain the lunar interior and changes in Earth's rotation.[3][4]

边界：潮汐锁定是月球对地球的潮汐同步，不等于月球不自转——它确实在自转，只是自转角速度与公转角速度相同。“月亮好像一直跟着人走”属于视差和距离感受，不在这套潮汐锁定机制内。锁定是引力长期作用的结果，不是有什么力量把月球朝向固定焊死；若未来潮汐环境改变，锁定状态也会随轨道演化缓慢变化。The boundary: tidal locking means the Moon is synchronized to Earth, not that it fails to rotate; it does rotate, but its spin angular velocity matches its orbital angular velocity. The everyday impression that the Moon follows a walking person is a parallax and distance effect, not part of this mechanism. The lock results from long-term gravitational evolution, not from something welding the Moon's orientation in place; if the tidal environment changed, the lock would evolve slowly with the orbit.[3]

03为什么日全食只在很窄的地带看到？日食和月食差在哪里？Why is a total solar eclipse visible only along a narrow band, and how does it differ from a lunar eclipse?

月球本影落地通常仅几十到两百千米宽，形成全食带；月食在满月时半个地球可见，日食只在新月时沿本影带发生。The lunar umbra touches ground in a track tens to two hundred km wide; lunar eclipses are visible from half the globe at full Moon, solar eclipses only along the umbral track at new Moon.

日全食只在月球本影扫过地球的狭窄地带可见。本影是阴影锥形最暗的核心区域，落到地面通常只有几十到两百千米宽，宽度随月球轨道远近和地形变化；本影之外是较淡的半影，那里只能看到日偏食。这就是为什么一次日全食只有沿狭长全食带的观测者能看到，相邻地区可能只看到偏食，再远一些则什么特殊现象都注意不到。A total solar eclipse is visible only along the narrow track where the Moon's umbra touches Earth. The umbra is the dark core of the shadow cone, typically tens to about two hundred kilometres wide on the ground, varying with the Moon's orbital distance and terrain. Outside it lies the softer penumbra, where only a partial eclipse is seen. That is why observers along a narrow totality track see a total eclipse, nearby regions may see only a partial one, and farther away little special change is noticed.[5]

日食发生在新月，月球挡在日地之间；月食发生在满月，地球挡在日月之间。地球本影长约 140 万千米，在月球距离处足够宽，一次月食半个地球都能看到；日食则必须等月球本影带扫到你所在的位置。月球与太阳的视直径相近，是日全食能发生的原因；当月球离地球较远、视直径略小于太阳时，会出现日环食，环食阶段太阳边缘仍有一圈亮光外露。A solar eclipse happens at new Moon, when the Moon passes between Sun and Earth; a lunar eclipse happens at full Moon, when Earth passes between Sun and Moon. Earth's umbra stretches about 1.4 million kilometres, wide enough at lunar distance that half the planet sees a lunar eclipse. A solar eclipse requires the lunar umbral track to sweep over your location. The Sun and Moon have nearly the same apparent diameter, which allows total eclipses; when the Moon is farther away and appears slightly smaller, an annular eclipse leaves a ring of sunlight exposed around the limb.[1][5]

配图是 2017 年 8 月 21 日日全食的钻石环阶段：月球圆盘遮住光球，边缘漏出最后一缕阳光，形成明亮的钻石环与珠状光点，外层日冕在暗背景上展开。日食期间直视未被遮挡的太阳光会损伤视网膜，必须佩戴合格的日食眼镜；只有全食阶段光球被完全遮住时，才能短暂用肉眼观看，偏食和环食阶段都不可裸眼直视，普通墨镜不能替代日食眼镜。The photo shows the diamond-ring phase of the 21 August 2017 total solar eclipse: the lunar disk covers the photosphere, and the last sunlight through limb valleys forms the bright diamond ring and beads, while the outer corona spreads across the dark sky. Looking directly at uncovered sunlight during an eclipse damages the retina, so approved eclipse glasses are required; naked-eye viewing is safe only briefly during totality, when the photosphere is fully hidden, not during partial or annular phases, and ordinary sunglasses do not substitute.[5][7]

OpenStax 教材区分了本影与半影，并比较日食和月食的可见范围；NASA 保存历次日食的全食路径记录。日月食按沙罗周期约 18 年重复出现相似配置，但可见地点并不相同。沙罗周期反映轨道几何的重复，不等于同一地点反复看到同一场日食；一个地点平均要等几百年才会再遇日全食。OpenStax distinguishes umbra from penumbra and compares what solar and lunar eclipses reveal. NASA records the totality tracks of past eclipses. Eclipses repeat in similar configurations on the roughly 18-year saros cycle, but the visible locations differ; the cycle reflects repeating orbital geometry, not the same place seeing the same eclipse twice. A given location typically waits centuries between total solar eclipses.[5][7]

边界：日食不是月亮本身变黑，而是遮挡；月相每月出现，日食月食只在月球轨道交点附近、日月地严格排成一线时发生，一年通常两次到四次。把月相盈亏当成月食，或以为全世界都能看到日全食，都是常见误解。日冕只在光球被遮挡时容易观测，平时它被阳光散射的亮背景掩盖；日珥则是贴在边缘的红色气体突起，与日冕层次不同。The boundary: a solar eclipse is an occultation, not the Moon turning dark. Phases appear every month, but eclipses require alignment near the orbital nodes, usually two to four a year. Treating monthly phases as an eclipse, or assuming a total eclipse is visible worldwide, are common mistakes. The corona is easy to see only when the photosphere is blocked; normally it is hidden behind scattered glare. Prominences are red gas arcs along the limb, a layer distinct from the corona.[5]

04潮汐是怎么形成的？太阳也会引起潮汐吗？What causes tides, and does the Sun also raise tides?

潮汐由月球引力差力形成两个隆起，太阳潮约为其一半；朔望叠加成大潮，上下弦抵消成小潮。Tides arise from lunar differential forces making two bulges; solar tides are about half as strong. Alignment gives spring tides, quadrature neap tides.

潮汐主要由月球引力在地球不同部位产生的差力，也就是潮汐力造成，而不是月球把海水简单“吸起来”。地球朝向月球一侧受到的拉力更强，背向一侧更弱，这种引力差把地球沿地月方向拉出两个隆起：朝向月球和背向月球各一个，地球自转一圈，某地通常经历两次高潮。两个隆起分别对着和背着月球，解释了为什么一天之内往往有两次涨落，两次高潮之间约相隔 12 小时 25 分。Tides are driven mainly by the differential pull of lunar gravity across different parts of Earth, the tidal force, rather than by the Moon simply lifting seawater. The side facing the Moon feels a stronger pull and the far side a weaker one, and this difference distorts Earth into two bulges along the Earth-Moon line, one facing the Moon and one opposite it. As Earth rotates, a given coast usually passes two high tides a day, with the bulges pointing toward and away from the Moon, which explains the twice-daily rise and fall.[1][4]

太阳也会产生潮汐，强度约为月球潮汐的一半。朔月和满月时，日潮与月潮方向相同、相互叠加，潮差更大，称为大潮；上弦和下弦时，日潮与月潮方向垂直，部分抵消，潮差更小，称为小潮。大潮名称与季节无关，指潮水“涌起”，不是春天专属；连续几周记录高潮高度，就能看到大潮小潮随月相起伏。The Sun also raises tides, about half as strong as the lunar tide. At new and full Moon the solar and lunar tides align and reinforce each other, giving larger ranges called spring tides; at first and last quarter they pull at right angles and partly cancel, giving smaller ranges called neap tides. The spring tide name has nothing to do with the season; it means water springs up, not that it belongs to spring. Recording high-water heights over weeks shows the range rising and falling with the phases.[4]

阿波罗宇航员在月面放置了反射镜，激光测距显示月球以 3.8 cm/yr 远离地球。潮汐摩擦把地球自转的能量转移到月球轨道：地球白天缓慢变长，月球轨道缓慢升高，这是同一角动量交换的两端。实际沿海潮位还受海岸形状、水深、河流和气象影响，潮位预报比理想的双隆起模型复杂得多；河口地形还可能把潮差放大成涌潮。Apollo astronauts left reflectors on the lunar surface, and laser ranging shows the Moon receding at 3.8 cm per year. Tidal friction transfers energy from Earth's spin to the lunar orbit: the day lengthens slowly and the orbit rises, the two ends of one angular-momentum exchange. Actual coastal water levels also depend on coastline shape, water depth, rivers and weather, so tide prediction is far more complex than the ideal two-bulge model, and estuary geometry can amplify the range into a tidal bore.[4][8]

潮汐力是引力之差，整体引力按距离平方衰减，潮汐力按距离三次方衰减。太阳质量虽远大于月球，但距离更远，所以月球对地球潮汐的贡献仍占主导。固体地球同样有潮汐形变，最大幅度约二十厘米，海洋潮汐叠加在这种固体潮之上；大气也有潮汐成分，但幅度远小于海洋潮。The tidal force is a difference in gravity. Overall gravity falls off with the square of distance, while tidal force falls with the cube. The Sun is far more massive than the Moon but much farther away, so the Moon remains the dominant tidal contributor. The solid Earth deforms by a tide too, up to about twenty centimetres, and ocean tides ride on top of this solid tide; the atmosphere has a tidal component as well, but a much smaller one.[4]

边界：两次高潮间隔约半天多，以半日潮为主，但各地潮型因海盆形状而异，有半日潮、全日潮和混合潮。潮汐与月相对应，但不是月球直接把某一片海水拉走；把月经、情绪等与潮汐周期挂钩，缺乏可靠的科学证据。潮汐观测还会受风增水、气压和河流径流影响，单次潮位异常不能直接归因于月球位置。The boundary: high tides are separated by about half a day, mainly a semidiurnal pattern, but local tide types vary with basin shape, including semidiurnal, diurnal and mixed tides. Tides follow the Moon's phase but are not the Moon pulling away a patch of seawater directly. Linking menstrual cycles or mood to tidal periods lacks reliable scientific evidence. Observed levels also depend on storm surge, air pressure and river runoff, so one anomalous reading cannot be blamed on the lunar position alone.[8]

05主流理论认为月球是怎么形成的？What does the leading theory say about how the Moon formed?

约 45 亿年前火星大小的 Theia 撞击原始地球，轨道碎屑吸积成月球；同位素相似、小铁核与角动量是主要证据。A Mars-sized Theia struck the young Earth about 4.5 Ga, and orbiting debris accreted into the Moon; isotopic similarity, a small core and angular momentum are the main evidence.

主流的巨撞击理论认为，约 45 亿年前，一颗火星大小、名为 Theia 的天体与原始地球剧烈相撞，飞溅出的碎屑在地球轨道上吸积形成月球。这一模型同时解释月球成分、地球自转较快以及地月系统较大的角动量，是目前证据最集中的起源框架；碰撞发生在太阳系形成后不久，地球尚处于熔融或半熔融状态。The leading giant-impact theory holds that about 4.5 billion years ago, a Mars-sized body named Theia collided violently with the young Earth, and debris flung into orbit accreted to form the Moon. This framework accounts for the Moon's composition, Earth's relatively fast spin and the large angular momentum of the Earth-Moon system, and it concentrates the strongest evidence. The collision happened soon after the Solar System formed, when Earth was still molten or partly molten.[2]

阿波罗带回的月岩与地球地幔在同位素组成上高度相似，而月球铁核较小、岩石缺少挥发性元素；撞击模型把地球外侧被加热的地幔物质抛入轨道，凝聚成月球。Canup 与 Asphaug 2001 年在 Nature 发表的数值模拟显示，火星大小的撞击体可以形成质量与轨道都接近现实月球的碎屑盘，后续研究还在调整撞击体速度、角度和物质混合程度。Apollo lunar samples closely match Earth's mantle in isotopic composition, while the Moon has a small iron core and rocks poor in volatile elements. In the impact model, heated outer mantle material from Earth is thrown into orbit and condenses into the Moon. Numerical simulations by Canup and Asphaug in Nature in 2001 showed that a Mars-sized impactor can produce a debris disk whose mass and orbit resemble the real Moon; later work adjusts impactor speed, angle and mixing.[2][6]

历史上还出现过分裂说、俘获说和同源说，各自在地月角动量或成分匹配上遇到困难。分裂说主张月球从快速自转的地球甩出，难以解释角动量分布；俘获说让一个外来天体恰好进入近圆轨道，概率很低；同源说则难以解释铁核大小和挥发分差异。巨撞击模型本身也在细化撞击体大小、撞击角度、撞击次数等参数；任何新理论都必须同时解释月球低铁含量、同位素相似性和当前轨道特征，不能只解释其中一项。Earlier competing ideas included fission, capture and co-accretion, each struggling with either angular momentum or composition matching. Fission proposed the Moon spun off a fast-spinning Earth but cannot explain the angular-momentum distribution; capture would require a foreign body to settle into a near-circular orbit, which is unlikely; co-accretion struggles with the core size and volatile differences. The giant-impact model itself is still refining impactor size, angle and number of collisions. Any replacement theory must explain the low iron content, isotopic similarity and current orbit together, rather than fitting one observation alone.[2][6]

月球对地球有长期影响：它稳定了地球自转轴倾角，使季节气候相对平稳；它还通过潮汐作用持续塑造地球自转。月球缓慢远离、地球白天缓慢变长，是同一角动量交换的结果，而不是两套独立现象。没有月球的潮汐力矩，地球自转轴的长期稳定会弱得多，这也是地月系统共同演化的一部分。The Moon has long shaped Earth: it stabilizes the axial tilt, keeping seasons and climate relatively steady, and it continues to mould Earth's spin through tides. The slow lunar recession and the lengthening day are two ends of the same angular-momentum exchange, not separate phenomena. Without the lunar tidal torque, Earth's axial tilt would be much less stable over time, part of the co-evolution of the Earth-Moon system.[1][2]

边界：巨撞击是有观测和模拟支持的主流框架，但早期撞击的细节仍在研究，不能说每一个参数都已被完全证实。月球成分与地球相似不等于整颗地球碎片直接形成，铁核偏小和挥发分偏少来自撞击过程中的物质分异。其他假说并未被全部遗忘，只是在解释多组观测时处于下风；未来月球样品和内部探测可能继续修正模型。The boundary: giant impact is the leading framework backed by observations and simulations, but early collision details remain under study, and every parameter cannot be called settled. Similar composition does not mean a whole chunk of Earth formed the Moon directly; the small core and volatile depletion come from material differentiation during the collision. Other hypotheses have not all vanished; they simply fare worse against the full set of observations, and future lunar samples and interior probes may refine the model.[2][6]

06月全食时月亮为什么会变红？为什么月亮好像一直跟着人走？Why does the Moon turn red during a total lunar eclipse, and why does it seem to follow you?

本影里的光经地球大气边缘折射，短波被散射、红橙光进入本影；月亮太远，步行改变不了视线方向。Umbra light bends through Earth's limb; short waves scatter away while red-orange light reaches the Moon, and the great distance keeps its bearing almost unchanged.

月全食时月亮没有被完全熄灭，而是变成暗红或铜红色，颜色来自地球大气边缘的折射与散射。满月时地球位于日月之间，月球进入地球本影；本影里没有直达阳光，但贴着地球边缘穿过厚厚大气层的阳光会被折射弯向阴影锥，落到月面上。空气分子对短波蓝光散射更强，强度大致与波长四次方成反比，蓝紫光在边缘大气中被侧向散射掉，剩下的长波红橙光进入本影照亮月面；这与日出日落偏红是同一套物理。During a total lunar eclipse the Moon is not switched off; it turns dark red or copper-colored because sunlight is bent and filtered by Earth's atmospheric limb. At full Moon the Earth lies between Sun and Moon, and the Moon enters Earth's umbra. No direct sunlight reaches the umbra, but rays skimming the thick atmosphere at Earth's limb are refracted inward toward the shadow cone and land on the lunar surface. Air molecules scatter short blue and violet wavelengths more strongly, roughly as the inverse fourth power of wavelength, so they are scattered sideways; the remaining long red and orange waves enter the umbra and light the Moon, the same physics that reddens sunrises and sunsets.[5][9]

红月亮的明暗和色调并不固定。法国天文学家丹戎提出的丹戎等级把全食月亮分成 L0 到 L4 五级：L0 非常暗，全食中段几乎看不见；L1 暗灰红；L2 深红或铁锈色；L3 明亮砖红；L4 鲜亮铜红偏橙，本影边缘还带一点亮边。大气中的火山灰、尘埃、薄云和水汽越多，被过滤和衰减的光越多，月亮越暗；大规模火山喷发后的几年里，常出现偏暗偏深的红食。月面再把这批红光照回地球，我们看到的就是红月。The brightness and hue of a blood Moon are not fixed. The Danjon scale rates total eclipses from L0 to L4: L0 very dark and nearly invisible at mid-totality, L1 dark gray-red, L2 deep red or rust, L3 bright brick-red, L4 bright copper-red or orange with a bright rim. More volcanic ash, dust, thin cloud and water vapor filter and attenuate the light, making the Moon darker; very dark, deep red eclipses often appear for years after large eruptions. The surface reflects this red light back to Earth, which observers see as a red Moon.[9][10]

地球大气像一圈透镜把阳光折进本影。大气层厚度相对地球半径很薄，但边缘穿过的路径最长；折射使本影里的照亮范围比几何阴影略大，大气投影对月面视角有一定放大。月全食发生在满月且月球经过轨道交点附近，一次月食半个地球可见；全食阶段通常持续几十分钟，比日全食的全食阶段长，因为地球本影在月球距离处足够宽。月食期间肉眼可以直接观看，不需要日食眼镜。Earth's atmosphere acts like a ring of lenses bending sunlight into the umbra. The atmosphere is thin compared with Earth's radius, but the edge path through it is the longest; refraction spreads the illuminated patch slightly wider than the geometric shadow. A total lunar eclipse happens at full Moon near a lunar node, and half the Earth can watch it. Totality usually lasts tens of minutes, longer than a total solar eclipse, because Earth's umbra is wide enough at lunar distance. Lunar eclipses are safe to watch with the naked eye and need no eclipse glasses.[5][7]

另一类日常疑问是为什么走路时月亮好像一直跟着自己。这是视差和距离造成的错觉：月球距离地球约 38.4 万千米，人走几百米甚至几千米，相对这个距离可以忽略，视线方向的改变小到察觉不出；近处的树、房子会从身后滑到前方，月亮的方位角却几乎不动，于是感觉它在跟着走。感觉同速，是因为大脑用近处景物判断运动；月亮在天球上的缓慢移动主要来自地球自转，约每小时 15°。A separate everyday question is why the Moon seems to follow you as you walk. This is a parallax-and-distance illusion: the Moon is about 384,000 kilometers away, so walking a few hundred or thousand meters is negligible next to that distance, and the change in bearing is too small to notice. Nearby trees and houses slide from behind to ahead, while the Moon's azimuth barely moves, so it feels as if it keeps pace. The matching-speed impression comes from judging motion against close objects; the Moon's slow drift mainly reflects Earth's rotation, about 15 degrees per hour.[3]

边界上要区分三件事：月相圆缺是日地月角度造成的每月循环，红月亮是月全食时地球大气过滤折射的结果；月亮跟着人走不是它在平移，而是远距离目标视差极小。红月不等于月亮自己发红光，也不是灾害预兆；大气条件只能改变亮度和色调，不能改变月食的几何时间。把月相盈亏、月食颜色和视差错觉混在一起，是这一类问题里最常见的概念错位。Three ideas should be kept apart. The monthly cycle of phases comes from the changing Sun-Earth-Moon angle, the red color comes from atmospheric filtering and refraction during a total eclipse, and the Moon following you is tiny parallax at a great distance. The red Moon does not emit its own red light and is not an omen; atmospheric conditions only change brightness and hue, not the geometric timing of the eclipse. Mixing these three is the most common slip.[3][5]
