01行星是怎样从一团气体尘埃中形成的？原行星盘上发生了哪些步骤？How do planets form from a cloud of gas and dust, and what happens in the protoplanetary disk?

分子云坍缩成原恒星与旋转盘，尘埃聚成星子，再碰撞吸积成行星，太阳系约 45.6 亿年前成形，主要过程在数千万年内完成。A molecular cloud collapses into a protostar and rotating disk; dust aggregates into planetesimals, which accrete into planets about 4.56 billion years ago.

行星不是预先造好的，而是从绕年轻恒星旋转的气体尘埃盘中逐级聚集而成。太阳系约在 45.6 亿年前由同一团分子云坍缩形成，太阳先在中心点火，盘内物质再一步步长成行星，主要聚集过程在数千万年内完成，剩下的碎片成为小行星、彗星和卫星。这套形成过程在年轻恒星周围的盘结构中可以直接观测到。Planets are not built in advance; they grow step by step from a rotating disk of gas and dust around a young star. The Solar System formed about 4.56 billion years ago from one collapsing molecular cloud: the Sun ignited at the center first, and material in the disk gradually assembled into planets, with leftover debris becoming asteroids, comets and moons. This process can be observed directly in disks around other young stars.[1]

分子云在自身引力或邻近超新星扰动下坍缩，角动量让下落物质摊成一个扁平的旋转盘，中心逐渐聚成原恒星。盘内靠近恒星的一侧温度可达上千开尔文，外侧降到几十开尔文；尘埃先沉降到盘平面形成薄层，微米级颗粒再通过静电碰撞粘成鹅卵石和巨石，千米以上的星子靠彼此引力吸积，最终长成行星胚胎，也就是原行星。The molecular cloud collapses under its own gravity or a nearby shock, and angular momentum spreads the infalling material into a flat rotating disk while a protostar gathers at the center. The inner side of the disk is hot, the outer side cold. Micron-sized dust grains first stick together by static electricity into pebbles and boulders; kilometer-scale planetesimals then accrete one another by gravity, until they grow into planetary embryos.[1][2]

在盘内侧的雪线附近，能凝聚的主要是金属和硅酸盐，吸积出岩质行星；巨行星先攒出大约十个地球质量的固态核，再靠引力吸积周围的氢和氦，成为木星、土星这样的气态巨行星；轨道更外侧的低温区则由水、氨、甲烷冰物质主导，形成天王星、海王星一类冰巨星。没有并入行星的残余物质，留在小行星带、柯伊伯带和彗星轨道上。Inside the snow line, mainly metals and silicates can condense, building rocky planets. A giant planet first gathers a solid core roughly ten Earth masses, then pulls in surrounding hydrogen and helium to become a gas giant like Jupiter or Saturn. Farther out, where ices of water, ammonia and methane dominate, ice giants such as Uranus and Neptune form. Material not incorporated into a planet remains in the asteroid belt, the Kuiper belt and comet orbits.[1][2]

观测证据来自多个方向。红外望远镜在织女星、金牛座 HL 等年轻恒星周围看到盘的间隙和环结构，正是行星正在清出轨道的迹象；ALMA 对邻近原行星盘的高分辨成像也分辨出多条环缝。陨石中的球粒和同位素年龄互相印证，给出太阳系年龄约 45.6 亿年；太阳系行星轨道共面、近圆、大致同向公转，正是盘内物质在同一平面内聚集留下的集体特征。The evidence comes from several directions. Infrared telescopes see gaps and rings in disks around young stars such as Vega and HL Tauri, signs that planets are clearing their orbits. Radiometric dating of meteorites puts the Solar System at about 4.56 billion years old. Solar System planets share nearly coplanar, nearly circular, prograde orbits, a collective signature of assembly inside one disk.[1][3]

这套标准图景能解释太阳系，但不能照搬到所有恒星系统。系外行星里存在热木星、高偏心轨道等例外，说明巨行星会在迁移、散射和轨道不稳定中改写初始位置；盘内最终能否留住大气、在表面形成液态水，还取决于恒星辐射强度和行星所在轨道，形成机制本身并不保证宜居。This standard picture explains the Solar System but cannot be applied to every planetary system. Exoplanet catalogs hold hot Jupiters and highly eccentric orbits, showing that migration, scattering and orbital instability can rewrite initial positions. Whether a disk finally keeps an atmosphere and liquid water on a surface also depends on stellar radiation and orbital location; the formation mechanism by itself does not guarantee habitability.[3][4]

02太阳系行星为什么分成岩质和巨行星两类？最大的木星有多大，金星又为什么最亮？Why do Solar System planets fall into rocky and giant families? How big is Jupiter, and why is Venus the brightest?

内侧四颗岩质行星小而密，外侧四颗巨行星大而轻；木星质量约 318 个地球，金星靠高反照率云层成为最亮行星。The four inner rocky planets are small and dense, the outer giants large and light; Jupiter weighs about 318 Earths, and Venus shines brightest through high-albedo clouds.

八大行星明显分成两族：内圈的水星、金星、地球、火星是岩质行星，直径小、密度高；外圈的木星、土星、天王星、海王星是巨行星，体积大、平均密度低，其中后两颗以冰物质为主。木星是太阳系最大的行星，金星则是夜空中最亮的行星，这两个结论分别由质量测量和反照率测量得到。The eight planets clearly fall into two families. Mercury, Venus, Earth and Mars on the inner side are rocky planets, small in diameter and dense; Jupiter, Saturn, Uranus and Neptune outside are giants, large and lower in average density, with the last two dominated by icy materials. Jupiter is the largest planet in the Solar System, and Venus is the brightest planet in the night sky; these two claims come from mass measurements and albedo measurements respectively.[1][2]

分类由形成位置和成分决定。内侧温度高，氢、氦和冰都留不住，只剩金属与硅酸盐凝聚成固态；外侧寒冷，冰和气体可以被巨行星的核束缚住。岩质行星平均密度约 3.9 到 5.5 克每立方厘米；木星和土星的密度比水还低；天王星、海王星介于两者之间，内部以高压冰流体为主。The division follows formation location and composition. High inner temperatures prevent hydrogen, helium and ices from staying, leaving metals and silicates to condense into solids; far out it is cold enough for ice and gas to be held by a giant planet's core. Rocky planets average about 3.9 to 5.5 grams per cubic centimeter; Jupiter and Saturn are less dense than water, while Uranus and Neptune sit between, their interiors dominated by high-pressure icy fluids.[2]

具体数值可以核对。木星直径约 139,820 千米，质量 1.898×10²⁷ 千克，约合 318 个地球，体积能装下一千三百多个地球；土星质量约 95 个地球，平均密度约 0.687 g/cm³，比水还低；地球质量约 5.97×10²⁴ 千克，平均密度 5.51 g/cm³，火星直径约 6,779 千米。金星反照率约 0.75，浓密的硫酸云把大部分入射阳光反射回太空，加上它距离地球近、相位变化大，最亮时视星等约 -4.9，明显亮于木星。The numbers can be checked. Jupiter's diameter is about 139,820 kilometers and its mass 1.898×10²⁷ kilograms, roughly 318 Earth masses, with volume enough for more than 1,300 Earths. Saturn weighs about 95 Earths, and Earth about 5.97×10²⁴ kilograms. Venus has an albedo near 0.75: thick sulfuric-acid clouds reflect most incoming sunlight back into space, and with its closeness to Earth and changing phase it reaches apparent magnitude about -4.9, brighter than Jupiter.[2][3]

探测器的光谱和密度测量进一步区分了成分：木星、土星以氢和氦为主，天王星、海王星富含水、氨、甲烷冰；金星大气以二氧化碳为主，云层高度约 70 千米，表面气压约 9.2 兆帕，温室效应把表面加热到约 460 °C。木星表面的大红斑是持续数百年的反气旋风暴。行星大小和成分的排序，对应原行星盘内侧到外侧的成分分布。Spectra and density measurements from probes separate the compositions further. Jupiter and Saturn are mostly hydrogen and helium; Uranus and Neptune are rich in water, ammonia and methane ices. Venus has a carbon-dioxide atmosphere with a surface pressure near 9.2 megapascals, and the greenhouse effect heats its surface to about 460 °C. The ordering of planet sizes and compositions matches the inner-to-outer composition gradient of the original disk.[2][3]

这种分类是太阳系经验，不能直接套到所有恒星系统里。巨行星可以迁移到近轨成为热木星，岩质与气态之间还存在次海王星一类过渡体型。金星看起来最亮，是反照率、距离和相位共同作用的结果，并不代表它自身的辐射能量最强；亮度排名也会随它与地球的相对位置变化。This classification comes from Solar System experience and cannot be imposed on every stellar system. Giant planets can migrate inward to become hot Jupiters, and between rocky and gas giants lie transitional types such as sub-Neptunes. Venus looks brightest because albedo, distance and phase act together; it does not emit the most energy itself, and its brightness ranking shifts with its relative position to Earth.[3][4]

03太阳系外的行星看不见细节，天文学家靠哪些方法发现它们？半径谷说明什么？Exoplanets are too faint to see in detail; how do astronomers detect them, and what does the radius gap mean?

凌星、视向速度、直接成像和微引力透镜四类方法互补；统计上 1.5–2 倍地球半径之间存在行星半径谷。Transit, radial-velocity, direct-imaging and microlensing methods complement one another; statistics show a radius gap between 1.5 and 2 Earth radii.

系外行星本身不发光，又淹没在主星的光芒里，探测主要依靠它对恒星造成的可观测影响：行星凌星时挡住一小部分星光，公转牵动恒星来回摆动，少数年轻大行星可以被直接成像，微引力透镜则临时放大背景恒星。NASA 系外行星档案目前已经确认数千颗系外行星，不同方法各自贡献了其中一部分样本。Exoplanets emit no light of their own and are drowned in their host star's glare, so detection relies mainly on the observable effects a planet has on the star: transits block a small fraction of starlight, orbital motion tugs the star back and forth, a few young large planets can be imaged directly, and gravitational microlensing temporarily magnifies a background star. The NASA Exoplanet Archive now lists several thousand confirmed exoplanets, with different methods contributing different parts of the sample.[4][5]

凌星法测量恒星亮度的周期性小幅下降，由行星从恒星圆面前方经过造成。开普勒空间望远镜和凌星系外行星巡天卫星靠长时间连续测光积累样本；光变深度与行星半径平方成正比，行星半径由此推算，轨道周期从相邻两次光变的间隔得到，通常要重复三次以上才确认候选。这种方法要求行星轨道侧对地球，轨道倾角不合适就探测不到。The transit method measures small periodic dips in stellar brightness caused when a planet crosses in front of the stellar disk. The Kepler space telescope and the Transiting Exoplanet Survey Satellite accumulate samples through long continuous photometry; planet radius is derived from the depth of the dip, and orbital period from the interval between dips. The method requires the orbit to be edge-on to Earth; an unsuitable inclination goes undetected.[4]

视向速度法利用多普勒效应：行星引力拉着恒星沿视线方向轻微往返，恒星光谱线随之周期性红移和蓝移，由此得到行星质量的下限。直接成像用日冕仪遮挡恒星本体，只拍摄年轻、高温、距离恒星较远的大行星；微引力透镜借行星质量形成的引力透镜临时放大背景恒星，适合一次性发现较远的冷行星。The radial-velocity method uses the Doppler effect: a planet's gravity tugs the star slightly back and forth along the line of sight, shifting its spectral lines periodically red and blue, from which a lower limit on the planet's mass follows. Direct imaging uses a coronagraph to block the star itself and photographs only young, hot, large planets far from their star. Microlensing uses the planet's mass as a lens to temporarily magnify a background star, suited to one-off finds of distant cold planets.[4][5]

统计样本揭示了一个关键结构：在半径约 1.5 到 2 倍地球半径之间，行星出现概率明显偏低，即半径谷。Fulton 等人 2017 年利用加州-开普勒巡天样本确认这一间隙，解释为光蒸发剥去氢氦大气、或核心质量损失把行星分成小行星与次海王星两支。样本里还有一类轨道周期短于十天的热木星，靠迁移才来到近轨。宜居带指恒星周围允许液态水在表面存在的轨道范围，宽窄由恒星光度决定，随恒星质量移动。Statistical samples reveal a key structure: between about 1.5 and 2 Earth radii the occurrence rate of planets drops noticeably, the radius gap. Fulton and colleagues confirmed this gap in 2017 with the California-Kepler Survey sample, interpreting it as atmospheric photoevaporation or core mass loss splitting planets into small rocky bodies and sub-Neptunes. The habitable zone is the orbital range around a star where liquid water could persist on the surface; its width follows the star's luminosity.[4][8]

每种方法都有选择偏差：凌星只对侧对地球的轨道敏感，视向速度更容易发现近轨大行星，直接成像只适合亮而远的年轻行星。由探测反推的质量和半径都带误差棒，单次探测不等于完整的行星系统。位于宜居带内也不等于宜居，还需要稳定大气、磁场和地质活动配合，表面能否保留液态水要单独评估。Every method carries selection bias: transits favor edge-on orbits, radial velocity finds close-in giants more easily, and direct imaging works only on bright, distant young planets. Masses and radii inferred from detection carry error bars, and a single detection does not equal a complete planetary system. Lying in the habitable zone does not mean habitable: a stable atmosphere, magnetic field and geological activity are also required, and surface water retention must be assessed separately.[4][5]

04地球为什么适合居住？把人类送上火星最难跨过哪些环境难关？What makes Earth habitable, and which environmental hurdles make sending people to Mars so hard?

宜居需要液态水、适宜大气、稳定磁场和温和温度；火星大气稀薄寒冷、无全球磁场，辐射与水保存是主要障碍。Liquid water, air, a magnetic field and mild temperatures make Earth habitable; Mars' thin cold air and radiation make settlement a hard barrier.

地球宜居是多个条件叠加的结果：有液态水海洋、含氧和氮的大气层、适中的表面温度，还有全球性磁场偏转太阳风。火星是离地球最近的候选目的地，但大气、温度和辐射防护条件都差得多，载人登陆与长期居留同时是工程难题和医学难题。判断一颗行星是否宜居，不能只看它在不在宜居带内。Earth's habitability comes from several conditions acting together: liquid-water oceans, an oxygen- and nitrogen-rich atmosphere, a moderate surface temperature, and a global magnetic field that deflects the solar wind. Mars is the nearest candidate destination, but its air, temperature and radiation shielding are far weaker, so landing people and keeping them there remains both an engineering and a medical challenge. Habitability cannot be judged by orbital zone alone.[3][6]

火星表面气压约 0.6 千帕，不到地球的百分之一，大气以二氧化碳为主；平均温度约 -63 °C，昼夜和纬度温差都很大。火星自转周期约 24.6 小时，轴倾角约 25.2°，和地球一样有季节变化，全球尘暴可以在数周内覆盖整颗行星。火星没有全球性偶极磁场，太阳风可以长期剥离高层大气。液态水在当前表面气压下会快速蒸发或冻结，现存水主要以极盖冰和中纬度地下冰的形式保存，表面短期存在的卤水稳定性也有限。Mars surface pressure is about 0.6 kilopascals, under one percent of Earth's, and the atmosphere is mostly carbon dioxide. The mean temperature is about -63 °C, with large day-night and latitude swings. Mars has no global dipole field, so the solar wind can strip its upper atmosphere over time. Under current surface pressure, liquid water evaporates or freezes quickly; existing water is stored mainly as polar-cap ice and mid-latitude subsurface ice, and short-lived brines on the surface remain marginal.[6]

探测器在火星南北极盖层、中纬度地下冰和古河谷三角洲中找到过水活动证据；好奇号在古湖沉积岩中发现过水的迹象和有机分子痕迹，毅力号在耶泽罗撞击坑采集岩芯样本，计划由后续任务带回地球。轨道器与辐射测量显示，火星表面辐射剂量明显高于地球，宇航员每次任务都要面对银河宇宙线和太阳粒子事件；缺乏磁场和厚大气，使表面失去了地球这样的天然屏蔽。Probes found evidence of past water activity in the polar caps, mid-latitude subsurface ice and ancient valley deltas; Curiosity detected water-related signs and organic molecules in ancient lake-bed sediments. Orbiter and radiation measurements show surface radiation doses well above Earth's, so each mission would face galactic cosmic rays and solar particle events; the missing field and thin air remove the natural shielding Earth enjoys.[3][6]

载人任务还要解决密闭生命支持、水和氧气循环、食物补给、低重力对骨骼肌肉的长期影响，以及着陆质量与返航燃料的矛盾。地下洞穴或熔岩管可以部分屏蔽宇宙线和微流星体，但封闭环境中的工程可靠性、心理压力和应急医疗方案都还没有经过长期验证。A crewed mission must also close the loop on life support, water and oxygen recycling, food supply, the long-term effects of low gravity on bone and muscle, and the trade-off between landed mass and return fuel. Underground caves or lava tubes could partially shield against cosmic rays and meteoroids, but engineering reliability, psychological stress and emergency medicine in a closed environment have not been validated over long durations.[3][6]

火星不排除过去存在过、或浅地下仍残存微生物，但宜居性判断针对表面和浅地下环境；把火星大气改造成适合人类呼吸的状态，目前没有可行的工程方案。地球的宜居性也不是永恒背景：板块运动、磁场和大气都受长期地质活动与恒星演化制约，把地球条件当成默认起点，会高估其他行星的可居住程度。Mars cannot be ruled out as a place where microbes once lived or persist deep underground, but habitability judgments concern the surface and shallow subsurface; terraforming the Martian air into something breathable has no viable engineering plan today. Earth's habitability is not a permanent backdrop either: plate tectonics, the magnetic field and the atmosphere all evolve with geology and the star. Treating Earth's conditions as the default overstates how habitable other planets really are.[2][6]

05卫星和环是怎么来的？行星还能和别的天体共享轨道吗，探测器怎样结束任务？Where do moons and rings come from? Can a planet share its orbit with other bodies, and how do probes end their missions?

卫星可由大碰撞、捕获或共轨吸积形成；环是细碎冰岩；特洛伊天体在拉格朗日点共轨，探测器可受控坠入巨行星大气。Moons form by giant impact, capture or co-accretion; rings are fine ice and rock debris; Trojans share an orbit at Lagrange points, and probes can be guided into a giant planet's atmosphere.

行星系统不止行星本身：大小卫星、行星环、小行星和共轨天体都围绕太阳或伴随行星运行。卫星来源主要有三类：巨大碰撞喷出物再聚集形成、捕获路过天体、与行星一起从盘中吸积长大；月球的主流解释是约 45 亿年前一颗火星大小天体与原始地球相撞，喷出物在轨道上重新聚集。环则是被潮汐力撕碎、绕行星铺开的细碎冰岩物质。不同来源的卫星，轨道规则性和成分差别明显。A planetary system is more than its planets: it includes large and small moons, rings, asteroids and co-orbital bodies, all orbiting the Sun or accompanying a planet. Moons form through three main routes: re-assembly of debris from a giant impact, capture of a passing body, or co-accretion with the planet from the disk. Rings are fine ice-and-rock debris torn apart by tides and spread around the planet. Moons of different origins differ noticeably in orbital regularity and composition.[1][3]

土星环主要由水冰碎块与少量岩石组成，颗粒从尘粒到房屋大小不等；环面相对厚度只有约十米到百米，整体却延伸数十万公里。环受卫星引力束缚和雕刻，分出多条分缝与窄环；环物质在陨石撞击和微流星轰击下会缓慢落到行星上，环的年龄估计存在争议，可能比太阳系年轻。木星、天王星、海王星也有暗淡环系，成分更偏岩石与冰尘。土星自转轴相对轨道面倾斜约 26.7°，环面跟着倾斜；地球绕日过程中视线会周期性与环面平行，约每 14 到 15 年出现一次侧视，环被压成一条细线，薄到几乎看不见，并非环真的消失了。Saturn's rings consist mainly of water-ice fragments with a little rock, ranging in size from dust grains to house-sized boulders. The ring plane is only about ten to a hundred meters thick, yet the system stretches hundreds of thousands of kilometers. Satellite gravity binds and sculpts the rings into gaps and narrow strands; Jupiter, Uranus and Neptune also have faint ring systems, more rocky and dusty in composition. Saturn's axis tilts about 26.7° to its orbit, and the ring plane tilts with it; as Earth orbits, our line of sight periodically lies in the ring plane, roughly every 14 to 15 years, so the rings are seen edge-on as a thin line that almost vanishes. They have not actually disappeared.[1][3]

同一轨道上还可以存在其他天体。拉格朗日 L4、L5 点附近的特洛伊小行星与行星共享轨道，保持约 60° 的夹角；木星特洛伊小行星已知数以千计，地球也有临时特洛伊小行星 2010 TK7。两颗行星交换轨道、共用一颗卫星需要极特殊的初始条件，这类构型通常在大行星长期引力摄动下并不稳定。Other bodies can share an orbit. Trojan asteroids near the Lagrange L4 and L5 points ride the same orbit as a planet, holding about a 60-degree angular separation; Earth also has a temporary Trojan, 2010 TK7. Two planets swapping orbits or sharing one moon requires highly special initial conditions, and such configurations are usually unstable under long-term gravitational perturbation.[3]

探测器任务为这套系统提供了直接测量。卡西尼号 2004 年进入土星轨道，环绕探测十三年，期间测量了土卫二冰下海洋的喷羽和土卫六表面的甲烷湖，2017 年 9 月 15 日受控坠入土星大气层，以避免污染可能存在冰卫星海洋的环境；旅行者号、伽利略号的飞掠和轨道测量给出了卫星成分、轨道参数与环结构数据。Probe missions supply direct measurements of this system. Cassini entered Saturn orbit in 2004, circled for thirteen years, and on 15 September 2017 was guided into Saturn's atmosphere to avoid contaminating icy moons that may hold oceans. Flybys and orbiters such as Voyager and Galileo measured moon compositions, orbital parameters and ring structure.[3][7]

卫星形成机制按大小和位置分野：小而不规则的卫星多为捕获，规则大卫星多为共轨吸积或碰撞产物；环会在潮汐作用和碎屑沉降下逐渐老化，不是永恒结构。共轨天体受长期引力摄动影响，临时伙伴可能在百万年尺度上漂移，或被甩出原有轨道，把稳定共轨当成永久关系会低估动力学演化的作用。Moon-forming routes split by size and location: small irregular moons are mostly captured, while regular large moons form by co-accretion or impact. Rings age under tidal forces and debris deposition; they are not permanent structures. Co-orbital bodies are subject to long-term perturbation, and temporary companions can drift or be ejected over million-year timescales. Treating a stable shared orbit as a permanent relation underestimates the role of dynamical evolution.[1][3]

06行星为什么大多是球形的？小行星为什么形状不规则？Why are planets nearly spherical, and why do asteroids keep irregular shapes?

自重压倒材料强度后天体趋向等势面成球；小天体靠结构强度维持棱角，自转使大天体赤道略鼓。Once self-gravity beats material strength, a body tends toward an equipotential sphere; small bodies keep corners through structural strength, and spin bulges the equator.

行星看起来接近球形，根源是引力把物质拉向质心。一颗天体的每个部分都受到指向整体重心的引力，在长期自重作用下，岩石、冰和金属会像非常黏稠的流体一样缓慢流动；表面任何高出质心的部分，都会被自身重量压向低处，直到各处表面到质心的距离大致相等，形成接近等势面的形状。这个状态叫流体静力平衡：引力把物质收拢，材料强度只能在小尺度上维持局部起伏。Planets look nearly spherical because gravity pulls material toward the center of mass. Every part of a body feels gravity pointing toward the overall center, and over geological time rock, ice and metal flow like a very viscous fluid. Any surface that sits farther from the center is pressed downward by its own weight, until the surface sits at roughly equal distance from the center of mass, forming an equipotential shape. This state is hydrostatic equilibrium: gravity gathers material, while material strength can only hold local irregularities on small scales.[1][2]

能不能成球，取决于自重与材料强度的对比。天体越大，质量增长比表面积快得多，自重很快超过岩石或冰维持棱角的能力；一般直径达到约 500 千米以上的天体，多数能在地质时间尺度上进入流体静力平衡，冰质天体更容易变形，岩质天体门槛略高。太阳系里谷神星直径约 940 千米，已经被压成近球形；灶神星直径约 525 千米，仍保留不规则棱角和撞击坑结构。更小的小行星、彗星核则由材料强度支撑，形状千奇百怪。Whether a body becomes round depends on the balance between self-gravity and material strength. As a body grows, mass rises much faster than surface area, so self-gravity quickly exceeds the ability of rock or ice to keep sharp corners. Bodies above roughly 500 kilometers in diameter usually reach hydrostatic equilibrium over geological time; icy bodies deform more easily, while rocky bodies have a somewhat higher threshold. Ceres, about 940 kilometers across, has been pressed nearly spherical, while Vesta, about 525 kilometers across, still keeps irregular edges and cratered structure. Smaller asteroids and comet nuclei stay supported by material strength and keep odd shapes.[2]

成球之后也不是完美正球体。自转产生的离心作用把赤道向外甩，行星在赤道方向略鼓、两极略扁；地球扁率约 1/298，赤道半径比极半径大约 21 千米。木星、土星自转更快，扁率更明显，土星外观上能看出椭圆轮廓。卫星潮汐形变、内部密度分异也会让形状偏离理想球体；这些偏差叠加在近球形的大轮廓上，不改变流体静力平衡的总体结论。Even after becoming round, a body is not a perfect sphere. Rotation produces centrifugal effects that push the equator outward, so planets bulge at the equator and flatten at the poles. Earth's flattening is about 1/298, with the equatorial radius about 21 kilometers longer than the polar radius. Jupiter and Saturn spin faster and are more obviously oblate, and Saturn's outline looks elliptical to the eye. Tidal deformation and internal density differentiation also pull the shape away from an ideal sphere; these deviations ride on the near-spherical outline without changing the equilibrium conclusion.[2][3]

这套机制可以对照两类天体理解。大行星、矮行星质量足够大，引力压倒材料强度，外形由平衡面决定；小天体质量小，岩石和金属的结构强度足以抵抗自重，撞击、碰撞和引力捕获留下的不规则形状得以保留。探测器拍摄的小行星表面布满巨石和碎石堆，正是小天体没有被自重压平的证据；行星表面的山脉、盆地则是在平衡面之上叠加的局部起伏，高度相对行星半径很小。The mechanism can be read off two classes of bodies. Large planets and dwarf planets have enough mass for gravity to overwhelm material strength, so their shape follows the equipotential surface; small bodies have little mass, and the structural strength of rock and metal resists self-gravity, preserving irregular shapes left by impacts, collisions and capture. Probe images of asteroid surfaces covered in boulders and rubble piles are direct evidence that small bodies were not flattened by their own gravity. Mountains and basins on a planet are local relief added on top of the equilibrium surface, small relative to the planetary radius.[2][3]

边界上要注意：流体静力平衡是按长期地质时间尺度说的，不等于天体表面光滑；地球有高山深海，仍属于平衡态。成球门槛不是一条精确的硬线，成分、温度、内部是否熔融都会影响；把直径阈值当成所有材料通用的固定数值，会高估规则性。球形也不是行星独有的标志，矮行星谷神星、冰卫星在足够大时同样接近球形，判断行星身份还要结合轨道清空等其他条件。A boundary point: hydrostatic equilibrium applies over long geological time and does not mean a smooth surface; Earth has high mountains and deep oceans yet remains in equilibrium. The rounding threshold is not a precise hard line: composition, temperature and whether the interior is molten all matter, so treating one diameter value as universal overstates the rule. Roundness is not unique to planets: the dwarf planet Ceres and large icy moons approach spheres when big enough, and classifying a body as a planet also requires other conditions such as clearing its orbit.[1][2]

07行星轨道为什么是椭圆形？小行星会不会撞上地球？Why are planetary orbits elliptical, and could an asteroid hit Earth?

轨道为椭圆，太阳系近圆来自盘内碰撞耗散；近地天体巡天拟合轨道、按都灵指数分级，2024 YR4 已排除重大风险。Orbits are ellipses; near-circularity follows disk damping. Near-Earth bodies are surveyed, fitted and Torino-graded; 2024 YR4 is ruled out as a major impact.

行星轨道是椭圆而不是正圆，这是开普勒第一定律的结论：太阳位于椭圆的一个焦点上，行星到太阳的距离在公转中周期性变化。太阳系八大行星的轨道偏心率大多很小，看起来接近圆，这是原行星盘内物质反复碰撞、相互摄动把初始偏心率逐渐耗散的结果；系外行星里则有高偏心轨道，说明迁移和散射可以重新拉开轨道形状。轨道平面也大致共面，倾角很小，同样来自盘内聚集的初始几何。Planetary orbits are ellipses rather than perfect circles, Kepler's first law: the Sun sits at one focus of the ellipse, and the planet's distance to the Sun changes periodically through the orbit. The eight Solar System planets have small eccentricities and look nearly circular, because repeated collisions and mutual perturbations inside the protoplanetary disk gradually damped the initial eccentricities. Exoplanet catalogs hold highly eccentric orbits, showing that migration and scattering can pull orbital shapes apart again. The orbits are also nearly coplanar with small inclinations, another signature of assembly inside one disk.[1]

小行星会不会撞上地球，要靠持续巡天和轨道拟合来判断。ATLAS、卡特林等巡天望远镜监测夜空，发现新的小天体后，后续观测不断补充位置数据，轨道员用引力模型拟合出未来几百年的轨道，再评估与地球轨道的接近程度。近地天体按大小和撞击概率分级，都灵指数把风险分成 0 到 10 级：0 级表示无危险，1 级需关注，3 级以上才表示有明确的潜在撞击威胁；分级随观测数据更新，可能上调也可能下调。Whether an asteroid could hit Earth is judged by continuous sky surveys and orbit fitting. Surveys such as ATLAS and Catalina monitor the night sky; once a new small body appears, follow-up observations add position data, and orbit analysts fit a gravitational model that predicts the trajectory over the next centuries, then assess close approaches to Earth. Near-Earth objects are graded by size and impact probability; the Torino Scale runs from 0 to 10: level 0 means no hazard, level 1 deserves attention, and level 3 and above signals a clear potential threat. The grade updates as observations arrive and can rise or fall.[1][10]

2024 YR4 是近年被反复评估的案例。它在 2024 年 12 月 27 日由 ATLAS 巡天发现，估计直径约 53 到 67 米，属于会在大气层中解体、但较大碎片仍可能落地的量级。2025 年 1 月 27 日，轨道拟合一度把它列为都灵 3 级，公众关注度升高；随着更多观测纳入，轨道不确定性收窄，后续评估排除了 2032 年 12 月 22 日掠近时的重大撞击风险。这类小天体发现时通常观测弧段很短，概率估计会随新数据明显变化。2024 YR4 is a recent case assessed repeatedly. It was discovered by the ATLAS survey on 27 December 2024 and estimated at about 53 to 67 meters across, large enough to break up in the atmosphere while possible fragments still reach the ground. On 27 January 2025, an orbit fit briefly placed it at Torino level 3, raising public attention; as more observations narrowed the uncertainty, later assessments ruled out a major impact during its close pass on 22 December 2032. Such small bodies usually have short observation arcs when found, so probability estimates shift noticeably with new data.[9][10]

命名和物理性质是两回事。Jupiter 这一名称来自罗马神话中的众神之王；中文古称木星为岁星，因它约十二年绕天一周、对应十二地支，后来五行配属归入木，木星之名由此沿用。中文五星名称来自古代天文与五行体系，并不描述行星的成分或颜色；水星并不真的全是水，火星偏红也不是因为火。了解命名来源有助于区分历史文化称呼与现代行星物理分类。Naming and physical properties are separate matters. Jupiter takes its name from the king of the Roman gods; in Chinese the planet was historically called Suixing, the Year Star, because it completes a cycle about every twelve years matching the twelve Earthly Branches, and later the five-element scheme assigned it to wood, giving the Chinese name. The five-planet names come from ancient astronomy and the five-element scheme, and do not describe composition or color: Mercury is not all water, and Mars' redness is not due to fire. Tracing the naming history helps keep cultural labels apart from modern physical classification.[3]

边界上要分清：轨道椭圆是引力二体问题的标准解，近圆只是太阳系盘内耗散后的特例；撞击风险评估依赖轨道观测精度，单次概率估计不等于必然事件，也不等于已经排除。都灵指数衡量的是给定观测下的潜在威胁，会随数据更新；把一颗小天体的短期关注度当成长期定论，或把神话命名当成物理描述，都是常见错位。近地天体监测仍在持续，新发现的小天体需要长期跟踪才能给出稳定结论。Boundary points: the elliptical orbit is the standard two-body gravitational solution, and near-circularity is a special case after damping in the Solar System disk. Impact risk assessment depends on orbital precision; a single probability estimate is neither a certain event nor a settled exclusion. The Torino Scale measures potential threat under current observations and updates with new data. Treating a small body's short-term attention as a long-term verdict, or reading a mythological name as a physical description, are both common slips. Near-Earth monitoring continues, and newly found small bodies need long tracking before stable conclusions follow.[1][3]

08地球绕太阳跑得多快？这么快我们为什么一点都感觉不到？How fast does Earth orbit the Sun, and why don't we feel that speed?

地球公转约 29.8 km/s；轨道近匀速、缺少加速度参照，大气随地球共动，人体因此无感。Earth orbits at about 29.8 km/s (about 107,000 km/h); near-uniform circular motion lacks an acceleration cue and the atmosphere co-moves.

地球绕太阳的平均轨道速度约 29.8 千米每秒，折合每小时约 10.7 万千米。这个速度来自引力提供的向心力：太阳引力把地球束缚在椭圆轨道上，地球以切向速度不断绕转，既不掉向太阳，也不飞离。轨道速度随日地距离略有变化，近日点快一点，远日点慢一点，全年平均下来约 29.8 千米每秒。对比地面交通工具，民航客机约 250 米每秒，地球公转比客机快一百多倍。Earth's average orbital speed around the Sun is about 29.8 kilometers per second, or roughly 107,000 kilometers per hour. This speed comes from the centripetal force supplied by gravity: the Sun's gravity holds Earth in an elliptical orbit, and Earth keeps circling at a tangential speed, neither falling toward the Sun nor flying away. Orbital speed changes slightly with distance from the Sun, a little faster at perihelion and slower at aphelion, averaging about 29.8 kilometers per second over the year. A jetliner cruises near 250 meters per second, so Earth orbits more than a hundred times faster than a plane.[1][11]

人感觉不到这种高速，首先因为运动近乎匀速。人体主要通过加速度——起步、刹车、转弯、颠簸——感知运动状态；如果速度大小和方向都几乎不变，前庭和皮肤压力感受器得不到明显刺激，就难以判断自己是否在动。地球轨道偏心率很小，近似圆轨道，一年里速度和方向变化平缓，没有剧烈加速或减速，身体自然把这套匀速运动当成静止背景。People do not feel this high speed first because the motion is nearly uniform. The body mainly senses motion through acceleration—starting, braking, turning, bumping. If speed and direction stay almost constant, the vestibular system and skin-pressure receptors receive little stimulus, and it becomes hard to tell whether one is moving. Earth's orbit has a small eccentricity and is nearly circular; speed and direction change gently through the year, with no violent acceleration or deceleration, so the body treats this steady motion as a stationary background.[1][3]

其次，周围环境和人一起在运动。地球带着大气、海洋、地面建筑和室内空气一同公转，没有相对空气或地面的迎面气流作为速度参照；你扔出的物体、桌上的杯子，都和地球处于同一轨道速度之下，惯性让它们随地球一起走。封闭船舱里无法靠日常物体判断船速，地球上也是同理：缺少外部参照物时，匀速运动本身不产生可感力。Second, the surroundings and the person move together. Earth carries its atmosphere, oceans, buildings and indoor air along in the same orbit; there is no oncoming airflow relative to air or ground to serve as a speed reference. Objects you throw and the cup on the table share Earth's orbital velocity, and inertia carries them along with the planet. Inside a closed cabin one cannot judge the ship's speed from everyday objects, and Earth works the same way: without external reference, uniform motion itself produces no perceptible force.[11][12]

能察觉到的是相对运动和长期变化。恒星、行星在天空中的位置随地球公转缓慢改变，季节更替就是轨道运动的结果；地面测量到的科里奥利效应、昼夜交替来自自转而非公转速度本身。要直接感知 29.8 千米每秒的公转，需要对比遥远天体的视运动，而不是身体的本体感觉。地球轨道并非永远不变，长期受行星摄动和潮汐作用缓慢演变，但在人的一生尺度上，速度变化小到无法察觉。What we notice are relative motion and long-term change. The positions of stars and planets shift slowly in the sky as Earth orbits, and the seasons are a result of that orbital motion. The Coriolis effect measured on the ground and day-night cycles come from rotation rather than from the orbital speed itself. To sense the 29.8-kilometer-per-second orbit directly, one must compare apparent motions of distant bodies, not rely on bodily proprioception. Earth's orbit is not fixed forever; planetary perturbations and tides slowly reshape it over long periods, but on a human lifetime scale the speed change is too small to detect.[1][3]

边界要写清：感觉不到速度不等于地球没有在高速运动；匀速与加速在人体感知上是两回事。近圆轨道的“平稳”是天体尺度上的近似，地球仍在持续改变运动方向，只是这个转弯半径约 1.5 亿千米，曲率小到身体无法识别。把“无感”理解成“地球静止”，是把自身参照系当成了宇宙参照系。The boundary matters: not feeling speed does not mean Earth is not moving fast; uniform motion and acceleration are different things to the body. The smoothness of a near-circular orbit is an approximation on the celestial scale; Earth keeps changing direction all the time, but the turning radius is about 150 million kilometers, a curvature too small for the body to recognize. Reading "no sensation" as "Earth is stationary" mistakes one's own reference frame for the cosmic one.[1][11]
