01彗星为什么会从星际空间飞来？小行星会飞进太阳吗？最大的彗星有多大？撞击坑为什么总是圆的？Why do comets arrive from interstellar space, do asteroids fly into the Sun, how large is the largest comet, and why are impact craters so round?

星际彗星沿双曲线过境；掠日彗星多被撕裂；最大彗核约 128 公里；撞击坑因径向抛射而近圆。Interstellar comets pass on hyperbolic paths; sungrazers are torn apart; the largest nucleus is about 128 km; craters are round because ejecta expands radially.

星际彗星不是“从另一颗恒星旁边直接飞来的雪球”，而是来自太阳系之外、轨道不再被太阳引力束缚的彗星。它沿双曲线或近似双曲线穿过太阳系，通常只来一次；2I/Borisov 是第一个被确认的星际彗星，2019 年由业余天文学家 Gennady Borisov 发现。它和 2017 年的 'Oumuamua 一样，都是“星际访客”，但前者有明亮的彗发和彗尾，后者更像一块岩石。An interstellar comet is not a snowball delivered straight from another star; it is a comet from outside the solar system whose orbit is no longer bound to the Sun. It passes through on a hyperbolic or nearly hyperbolic path, usually only once. 2I/Borisov is the first confirmed interstellar comet, discovered in 2019 by amateur astronomer Gennady Borisov. Like 2017's 'Oumuamua, it is an interstellar visitor, but Borisov had a bright coma and tail while 'Oumuamua looked more like a rock.[5]

证据首先来自轨道。Borisov 的轨道是双曲线，速度约 177,000 公里/小时；它不受太阳系束缚，2019 年 12 月 7 日以约日地距离两倍的近日点掠过太阳，随后飞回星际空间。Hubble 在 2019 年 10 月 12 日拍到它的尘埃彗发，说明它含有挥发性冰；它也不是从另一个星系“瞄准”地球，而是恰好穿过太阳系。判断星际天体不能只看速度，还要看轨道能量是否足以让它离开。The evidence begins with the orbit. Borisov's path is hyperbolic and its speed is about 177,000 kilometres per hour; it is not bound to the Sun. It passed perihelion on 7 December 2019 at about twice the Earth-Sun distance and then headed back into interstellar space. Hubble photographed its dust coma on 12 October 2019, showing volatile ices; it did not aim at Earth from another galaxy, it simply crossed the solar system. Speed alone does not identify an interstellar object; the orbital energy must also be enough to let it leave.[5]

“Hubble 发现的最大彗星”通常指 C/2014 UN271（Bernardinelli-Bernstein）。Hubble 在 2022 年 1 月 8 日拍摄后，把彗核直径估计为约 128 公里（80 英里），约为普通彗核的 50 倍，质量约 500 万亿吨；它来自 Oort Cloud，2031 年近日点仍比土星轨道更远，不会撞地球。这里的“最大”指固体彗核，不是彗尾长度。The 'largest comet Hubble has discovered' usually means C/2014 UN271 (Bernardinelli-Bernstein). After Hubble observed it on 8 January 2022, the nucleus was estimated at about 128 kilometres (80 miles) across, roughly 50 times a typical comet nucleus, with a mass of about 500 trillion tons. It comes from the Oort Cloud; its 2031 perihelion stays beyond Saturn's orbit, so it will not hit Earth. 'Largest' here means the solid nucleus, not the tail.[4]

小行星会不会飞进太阳？要区分“撞进去”和“掠过”。SOHO 在 2016 年 8 月看到一颗 Kreutz 族掠日彗星以近 130 万英里/小时冲向太阳，它并没有直接落入太阳，而是在近日点附近被撕裂、蒸发；大多数掠日彗星都如此。小行星也可能被引力扰动到极低近日点，但太阳附近的观测样本以彗星为主，不能把所有小行星都写成“会掉进太阳”。太阳的引力会把它们加速，潮汐力和太阳辐射才是近距离的破坏者。Do asteroids fly into the Sun? Separate 'fall in' from 'graze'. On 3–4 August 2016 SOHO saw a Kreutz sungrazer comet moving at nearly 1.3 million miles per hour; it did not simply fall into the Sun but was torn apart and vaporized near perihelion, as most sungrazers are. An asteroid can also be perturbed onto a very low perihelion, but the observed sample near the Sun is dominated by comets; do not turn every asteroid into a Sun-diver. Solar gravity accelerates them; tides and radiation do the damage near the Sun.[6]

撞击坑为什么近似对称？因为撞击瞬间的爆炸把岩石和抛射物向四面八方推开，坑的圆形来自径向膨胀，而不是撞击体本身的形状；LPI 指出，几乎没有什么撞击体是球形的。低角度撞击（小于 20°）会把抛射物压成不对称的“蝴蝶形”，预先存在的地形也会让坑变不规则；小坑是碗形，大坑会出现中央峰和阶地。亚利桑那流星坑直径约 1.2 公里，就是约 5 万年前一颗约 50 米宽的铁质撞击体留下的简单坑。Why are impact craters roughly symmetrical? Because the explosion at impact pushes rock and ejecta outward in all directions; the circular shape comes from radial expansion, not from the impactor's shape, and LPI notes that almost no impactors are spherical. A very low-angle impact (less than 20 degrees) squashes the ejecta into an asymmetric butterfly pattern, and pre-existing topography can make the crater irregular; small craters are bowl-shaped, while large ones develop central peaks and terraces. Arizona's Meteor Crater, about 1.2 kilometres across, is a simple crater left about 50,000 years ago by an iron impactor roughly 50 metres wide.[7]

02水星那么热，为什么极地还有水冰？天王星为什么看起来格外平滑？我们又如何知道海王星外有冥王星大小的天体？Why does Mercury have polar water ice, why does Uranus look unusually smooth, and how do we know there are Pluto-size objects beyond Neptune?

水星极区永久阴影坑保存水冰；天王星 1986 年只露出雾霾面；柯伊伯带藏着冥王星大小天体。Mercury's permanently shadowed polar craters trap water ice; Uranus hid behind haze in 1986; the Kuiper Belt holds Pluto-size worlds.

水星上有水，但主要是水冰，不是液态水。水星自转轴倾角很小，极区一些撞击坑的底部永远见不到阳光，温度长期极低；1991 年 Arecibo 雷达就在极区看到亮斑，后来 MESSENGER 的影像确认这些亮斑都落在永久阴影坑内。冰在部分坑底直接暴露，在更多地方则埋在比水星表面其他物质更暗的覆盖层下。雷达亮斑、阴影地形和探测器成像指向同一结论，但“有冰”不等于“有可饮用的液态水”。Mercury has water, but mostly as water ice, not liquid water. Mercury's rotation axis is only slightly tilted, so the floors of some polar craters never see sunlight and stay extremely cold. In 1991 the Arecibo radar saw bright patches near the poles; MESSENGER images later confirmed that these patches lie inside permanently shadowed craters. In some craters the ice is exposed at the surface; in most it lies beneath a layer darker than the rest of Mercury's surface. Radar, shadowed terrain and spacecraft imaging point to the same conclusion, but 'ice' is not 'drinkable liquid water'.[1]

为什么冰能留下来？水星几乎没有大气，热量难以通过大气循环；极区永久阴影坑像“冷阱”，水分子一旦进入就很难再获得足够能量逃逸。MESSENGER 的中子谱仪还探测到极区氢的富集，和雷达亮斑、阴影区重合，构成多条独立证据。需要注意，这些冰可能来自彗星、富挥发分小行星的撞击，也可能是水星内部释放的；具体来源仍有争议，不能把“发现冰”说成“发现生命”。Why does the ice survive? Mercury has almost no atmosphere, so heat cannot be carried by an atmospheric circulation; polar permanently shadowed craters act as cold traps, and a water molecule that enters one rarely gains enough energy to escape. MESSENGER's neutron spectrometer also found hydrogen enrichment near the poles, overlapping the radar patches and shadowed regions, which gives independent evidence. The ice may have been delivered by comets or volatile-rich asteroids, or released from Mercury's interior; the exact mix is still debated, and finding ice is not finding life.[1]

天王星看起来“平滑”，最直接的原因是 1986 年旅行者 2 号只看到了它的一面。那时天王星的一个极点几乎正对太阳，探测器看到的是被高层雾霾罩住的蓝绿色球体，云带对比很弱；NASA 把这次观测形容为“台球般光滑”。天王星自转轴相对轨道面倾斜约 90°，极端季节使照明和天气随时间剧烈变化；一个半球长期受光、另一个长期黑暗，云的形成也会跟着改变。Uranus looks 'smooth' most directly because Voyager 2 saw only one face of it in 1986. At that time one pole was pointed almost straight at the Sun, and the spacecraft saw a blue-green ball wrapped in high haze, with very little cloud-band contrast; NASA described the view as billiard-ball smooth. Uranus's axis is tilted by roughly 90 degrees to its orbital plane, so extreme seasons change the lighting and the weather over time; one hemisphere stays sunlit while the other stays dark, and cloud formation follows.[2]

后来 Hubble 在 2000 年代初看到天王星的赤道朝向太阳，云带、亮云和暗斑开始出现；Webb 继续追踪这些季节变化。也就是说，天王星并非真的没有云，而是 Voyager 2 恰好赶上了它最“安静”的一面。把一张 1986 年的照片当成天王星的全部，会误以为它比木星、土星更“光滑”；实际上，观测波段、雾霾和看的角度都会改变对比度。Later, Hubble in the early 2000s saw Uranus with its equator pointed toward the Sun, and bands, bright clouds and a dark spot appeared; Webb is continuing to track these seasonal changes. Uranus is not genuinely cloud-free; Voyager 2 simply caught it on a quiet face. Treating a single 1986 photograph as the whole planet makes it seem smoother than Jupiter or Saturn, when observing band, haze and viewing angle all change the contrast.[2]

海王星外的冥王星大小天体，又是怎么知道的？Kuiper Belt 是海王星轨道外的甜甜圈状冰体带，估计有数百万个天体，其中数十万个直径超过 100 公里；冥王星直径约 2,377 公里，阋神星约 2,400 公里，两者大小相当。发现靠巡天观测、掩星和 Hubble 跟拍：先看到缓慢移动的点，再测轨道、亮度和掩星持续时间，才能估算大小。“冥王星大小”不是正式分类，2006 年 IAU 把冥王星、阋神星等归为矮行星；其总质量不到地球的 10%，不是另一颗行星。How do we know there are Pluto-size objects beyond Neptune? The Kuiper Belt is a doughnut-shaped belt of icy bodies beyond Neptune, with an estimated millions of objects, including hundreds of thousands more than 100 kilometres across. Pluto is about 2,377 kilometres across and Eris about 2,400 kilometres, so the two are comparable in size. Discoveries rely on sky surveys, occultations and Hubble follow-up: a slowly moving point is found, then its orbit, brightness and the duration of an occultation constrain its size. 'Pluto-size' is not a formal class; in 2006 the IAU made Pluto and Eris dwarf planets, and the Belt's total mass is less than 10% of Earth's, so it is not another planet.[3][12][13]

03太阳系绕着银河系转，为什么星座看起来还是老样子？TRAPPIST-1 又特别在哪里？If the Solar System orbits the Galaxy, why do the constellations still look familiar, and what makes TRAPPIST-1 special?

太阳绕银心 2.3 亿年，恒星自行很小；TRAPPIST-1 有 7 颗地球大小行星，3 颗在宜居带。The Sun orbits the Galaxy every 230 million years while stellar motion is slow; TRAPPIST-1 has seven Earth-size planets, three in the habitable zone.

太阳系确实绕银河系中心转，但星空在人的一生中看起来几乎不变。太阳绕银心一圈约 2.3 亿年，速度约 72 万公里/小时；相比之下，人类文明只有几千年，恒星在天空中的相对位移非常小。星座是视线方向上的投影，不是固定的实体，所以“太阳系在动”并不等于“今晚的北斗七星会立刻变形”。The Solar System does orbit the centre of the Milky Way, but the sky looks nearly unchanged during a human lifetime. The Sun takes about 230 million years to complete one orbit at roughly 720,000 kilometres per hour; human civilisation is only a few thousand years old, so the relative displacement of stars is tiny. Constellations are projections along lines of sight, not fixed physical objects, so 'the Solar System is moving' does not mean 'the Big Dipper will visibly deform tonight'.[9][10]

更关键的是，邻近恒星也在绕银河系运动，而且方向大致相同。北斗七星中五颗星属于同一个移动星群，距离约 80–83 光年，另外两颗分别约 104 和 124 光年；它们只是恰好从地球看过去排成勺子。恒星有“自行”，但角速度很小，几千年的变化肉眼很难察觉；把时间拉到 5 万年，勺形才会明显变形。星座的“熟悉感”来自我们只活了宇宙时间的一瞬，而不是恒星真的静止。More importantly, nearby stars are also orbiting the Galaxy, and in broadly similar directions. Five of the seven Big Dipper stars belong to one moving group at about 80–83 light-years, while the other two are about 104 and 124 light-years away; they only happen to line up as a dipper from Earth. Stars have proper motion, but their angular speed is small, so a few thousand years barely changes the pattern; over 50,000 years the dipper's shape becomes noticeably different. The familiarity comes from our snapshot of cosmic time, not from stationary stars.[10]

历史记录也支持这一点：古代天文学家看到的星座与今天相似，是因为我们只截取了宇宙时间的一瞬。地球自转轴还有进动，约 2.6 万年一圈，北极星会换人；但这不是“星座消失”，而是天极方向改变。太阳系绕银心的运动本身不会快速打乱星座，真正改变星空的是恒星各自的相对运动。AMNH 把北斗七星的距离和运动拆开讲，正好说明“看起来在一起”和“物理上在一起”是两回事。History supports this: ancient astronomers saw constellations similar to ours because we occupy only an instant of cosmic time. Earth's axis also precesses, completing a cycle in about 26,000 years, so the pole star changes; that is a change in the celestial pole, not the disappearance of constellations. The Solar System's galactic orbit does not quickly scramble the sky; the real changes come from the stars' own relative motions. AMNH's explainer separates the Big Dipper's distances from its motion, showing that 'looking together' and 'physically together' are different things.[10]

TRAPPIST-1 是另一类“系统”：它是一颗约 40 光年外的超冷红矮星，周围有 7 颗地球大小的岩质行星，2017 年由 Spitzer 等观测确认；其中 3 颗位于宜居带，所有行星的轨道都比水星轨道更靠近母星。它们可能被潮汐锁定，一面永远朝星，另一面永夜；红矮星又容易爆发耀斑，这些条件会让“宜居”变得复杂。TRAPPIST-1 is another kind of system: an ultra-cool red dwarf about 40 light-years away, with seven Earth-size rocky planets confirmed in 2017 by Spitzer and other observatories. Three are in the habitable zone, and all of their orbits are closer to the star than Mercury is to the Sun. They may be tidally locked, with one face permanently day and the other permanent night; red dwarfs also flare, which complicates the idea of habitability.[8]

它之所以有趣，不是因为“已经发现外星生命”，而是因为它提供了一个可以反复检验的实验室。Hubble 的观测表明至少 3 颗行星没有蓬松的富氢大气；密度分析显示这些行星比按地球成分模型预期的约低 8%，说明它们的水、铁、硅比例可能与地球不同。JWST 正在研究它们的大气，但“宜居带”只是液态水可能存在的温度区间，不是生命证据；红矮星的耀斑和潮汐锁定也会给宜居性带来麻烦。把 TRAPPIST-1 当成“七个地球”会过度承诺，它更像七个待查的岩石世界。It is interesting not because 'alien life has been found', but because it is a laboratory that can be tested repeatedly. Hubble observations show that at least three planets lack puffy hydrogen-rich atmospheres; density analysis suggests the planets are about 8% less dense than a Earth-composition model would predict, so their water, iron and silicon mix may differ from Earth's. JWST is studying their atmospheres, but a 'habitable zone' is only a temperature range where liquid water could exist, not evidence of life; flares and tidal locking can also make habitability harder. Calling TRAPPIST-1 'seven Earths' overpromises; it is seven rocky worlds still to be examined.[8][11]

04为什么会有流星？为什么流星是绿色的，又拖着一条尾巴？What causes meteors, why are some green, and why do they leave a trail?

彗星尘埃或小行星碎片闯入大气，压缩加热烧蚀发光；颜色来自元素谱线与高层氧发光，尾巴是电离轨迹与尘埃尾迹。Comet dust or asteroid fragments enter the air, compress and ablate into light; color comes from elemental lines and high-altitude oxygen, and the trail is an ionized track plus dust.

流星不是天上的星星掉下来，而是太阳系里的小碎屑闯入地球大气造成的发光现象。这些碎屑叫流星体，大小从尘粒到小石块不等，多数来自彗星沿轨道留下的尘埃，也有一部分来自小行星碰撞抛射的碎片；它们沿近椭圆轨道绕太阳运行，地球在轨道上穿行时，碎屑以每秒十几到七十多千米的相对速度撞上大气层。只有进入大气并烧出光迹的才叫流星；没烧尽落到地面的部分称为陨石，在太空中运行时则仍是流星体。A meteor is not a star falling from the sky; it is the light trail made when a small Solar System particle enters Earth's atmosphere. These particles are meteoroids, ranging from dust grains to small rocks, mostly debris left along a comet's orbit, with some fragments from asteroid collisions. They orbit the Sun on near-elliptical paths; as Earth crosses those paths, particles strike the atmosphere at relative speeds of roughly 11 to 72 kilometers per second. Only the atmospheric glow is a meteor; whatever survives to reach the ground is a meteorite, and in space the object remains a meteoroid.[11][14]

发光来自高速压缩和烧蚀。流星体在约 70 到 100 千米高度进入大气，速度远高于声速，前方空气被急剧压缩而升温，表面物质被加热汽化、剥蚀，溅出的原子和电离气体发光；碎屑本身在这几秒钟内大部分烧尽。NASA 估计每天约有 44 吨流星物质落到地球，多数是肉眼不可见的微小颗粒。火流星比金星还亮，属于较大碎屑在大气中解体；流星雨则是地球穿过彗星留下的尘埃带，同一辐射点出现多条轨迹。The light comes from rapid compression and ablation. Meteoroids enter the atmosphere around 70 to 100 kilometers high, far above sound speed; the air ahead is compressed sharply and heats up, surface material vaporizes and erodes, and the ejected atoms and ionized gas glow. Most of the particle burns away within a few seconds. NASA estimates about 44 tonnes of meteoritic material reaches Earth each day, most of it tiny grains invisible to the eye. A fireball is a brighter-than-Venus meteor from a larger fragment breaking apart; a shower appears when Earth crosses a comet's dust trail, with multiple paths radiating from one point.[7][14]

流星的颜色由烧蚀物质和被激发的空气成分共同决定。不同元素在高温下发出各自的特征谱线：镁约 518 纳米偏蓝绿，钠约 589 纳米偏橙黄，铁偏黄，钙偏紫；高层大气中的氧在约 110 千米以上可发出 557.7 纳米的绿色光，形成常在尾迹中停留较久的绿色余晖。看到绿色，既可能是碎屑中的镁，也可能是高层氧原子发光；颜色还受碎屑成分、速度和高度影响，不能单凭颜色反推物质种类。Meteor color comes from the ablated material together with the excited air. Each element emits its own spectral lines at high temperature: magnesium near 518 nanometers appears blue-green, sodium near 589 nanometers orange-yellow, iron yellow, and calcium violet. High-altitude oxygen above about 110 kilometers can emit green light at 557.7 nanometers, producing a green afterglow that lingers in the trail. Green may come from magnesium in the particle or from oxygen atoms high up; color also depends on composition, speed and altitude, so it alone cannot identify the material.[11][14]

流星的尾巴是碎屑留下的电离轨迹和尘埃尾迹。烧蚀出的金属原子和尘埃沿轨迹散开，被电离的气体在夜空中短暂发亮，尘埃则反射阳光；尾迹顺着碎屑运动方向延伸，余辉可维持数十秒到一分钟。辐射点是透视效果：同一尘埃带的碎屑平行进入大气，轨迹反向延长线汇聚到天球上一点，并不真的从那个点发出。流星体沿黄道面附近轨道运行，所以流星雨多在固定季节出现。The meteor tail is an ionized track and a dust trail left by the particle. Ablated metal atoms and dust spread along the path; ionized gas glows briefly in the night, while dust reflects sunlight. The trail extends along the particle's direction of motion, and the afterglow can last tens of seconds to a minute. The radiant point is a perspective effect: particles from one dust band enter in parallel, and their trails extended backward converge on one point on the sky, rather than actually emanating from it. Meteoroids orbit near the ecliptic plane, so showers recur in fixed seasons.[3][11]

边界上要分清：流星、流星体、陨石是同一物质在不同阶段的称呼；发光发生在大气中，不是碎屑本身在燃烧起火。绿色不代表流星“含绿物质”，尾巴也不是彗星那种长期存在的彗尾。流星体绝大多数太小，不会造成地面损害；真正需要监测的是直径更大的近地小行星，其风险评估见行星章节。单次流星颜色和亮度受成分、速度、高度与大气条件影响，不能用一条光迹判断碎屑来源或撞击威胁。Boundary: meteoroid, meteor and meteorite are the same material at different stages; the glow happens in the atmosphere, not because the particle catches fire. Green does not mean the meteor contains a green substance, and the trail is not a long-lived comet tail. Most meteoroids are too small to harm the ground; the larger near-Earth asteroids that need monitoring are assessed in the planets chapter. A single trail's color and brightness depend on composition, speed, altitude and atmospheric conditions, so one streak cannot identify the particle's origin or impact threat.[7][14]

05彗星尾巴为什么总是背向太阳？Why does a comet's tail always point away from the Sun?

太阳风把带电气体直吹成离子尾，太阳光压把尘埃推成弯尘尾，两尾都背离太阳。The solar wind sweeps ionized gas into a straight ion tail and sunlight pressure pushes dust into a curved dust tail, both opposite the Sun.

彗星靠近太阳时才有尾巴。远处轨道上的彗核是冰、尘埃和岩石冻结成的小天体，温度极低，外表灰暗，看不出尾迹。运行到距离太阳约 3 个天文单位以内，阳光加热彗核，表面的水冰、二氧化碳冰和其他挥发性物质直接升华为气体，裹挟尘埃向外扩散，在核外形成一团稀薄朦胧的大气，叫彗发。彗发直径可达到数万到数十万公里，比彗核大上千倍；这团气体和尘埃就是后来尾巴的原料。A comet develops a tail only when it nears the Sun. Far out in its orbit, the nucleus is a frozen body of ice, dust and rock, very cold and dark, with no visible trail. Once it comes within about three astronomical units of the Sun, sunlight warms the nucleus and surface ices such as water ice and carbon dioxide sublimate directly into gas, carrying dust outward into a thin, hazy atmosphere called the coma. The coma can reach tens of thousands to hundreds of thousands of kilometres across, over a thousand times the nucleus, and this gas and dust is the raw material of the later tail.[14]

尾巴分两条，推力来源不同。太阳不断向外抛出高速带电粒子流，即太阳风，风速约 300–800 km/s。太阳风撞击彗发里被紫外线电离的气体离子，把它们顺着粒子流动的方向吹走，形成一条细而直、偏蓝白色的离子尾（气体尾）。离子质量小、带电荷，完全跟随太阳磁场和太阳风的走向，所以离子尾紧贴背日方向，很少弯曲。There are two tails, driven by different forces. The Sun continuously ejects a fast stream of charged particles known as the solar wind, at about 300 to 800 km/s. When this wind hits gas ions ionized by ultraviolet light in the coma, it sweeps them along the particle flow and forms a narrow, straight, bluish-white ion tail, or gas tail. Ions are light and charged, so they follow the solar magnetic field and wind closely; the ion tail therefore hugs the Sun-opposite direction and barely curves.[9][15]

另一条是尘埃尾。彗发里的尘埃颗粒直径多在 1 μm 上下，太阳光的辐射压对这些微小颗粒施加向外的推力。光压把尘埃推离彗核，但尘埃质量比离子大，又保留了彗星自身绕日运动的轨道速度，于是尘埃沿彗星轨道后方缓缓散开，形成一条较宽、略带弯曲、反射阳光发亮的尘埃尾。两条尾在天空中方向接近，有时从某个观察角度看去还会张开成不同角度。The other tail is the dust tail. Dust grains in the coma are mostly about one micrometre across, and solar radiation pressure exerts an outward push on these tiny particles. Light pressure pushes dust away from the nucleus, but the grains are heavier than ions and keep the orbital velocity of the comet itself, so they slowly trail behind along the comet's orbit, forming a wider, gently curved dust tail that shines by reflecting sunlight. The two tails point roughly the same way, though from some viewing angles they fan apart at different angles.[15]

所以无论彗星朝哪个方向运动，尾巴都指向背向太阳的一侧。彗星接近太阳时，尾巴在它身后；掉头远离太阳时，尾巴反而跑到彗星前进方向的前方，因为推尾的力量来自太阳，而不是飞行惯性。离子尾被太阳风直吹，尘埃尾被光压推开，二者都以太阳为中心向外；这也解释了为什么彗星越靠近太阳尾巴越明显，远离之后气体重新冻结、尾巴逐渐消散。Thus, whatever direction the comet travels, its tails point away from the Sun. While approaching, the tail lies behind the comet; after turning away from the Sun, the tail actually leads the comet's motion, because the force pushing the tail comes from the Sun rather than flight inertia. The ion tail is blown straight by the solar wind and the dust tail is pushed by light pressure; both radiate outward from the Sun. This also explains why tails grow more obvious near the Sun and fade as gas refreezes farther out.[11][15]

边界要写清：彗尾不是彗星拖在身后的烟，也不是被彗星速度甩出去的。离子尾和尘埃尾成分、受力和形状都不同，天气图上看到的弯曲程度还与颗粒大小、太阳风强弱和观察角度有关。彗核本身质量很小，喷出的气体和尘埃只是表层一小部分；多次回归后彗核物质逐渐损耗，结构松散的彗星最终可能瓦解成一串尘埃碎粒。The boundary matters: a comet tail is not smoke dragged behind the comet, nor material thrown off by the comet's speed. The ion tail and dust tail differ in composition, force and shape, and how curved they look also depends on grain size, solar wind strength and viewing angle. The nucleus itself has little mass, and the ejected gas and dust are only a small part of its surface material; after many returns the nucleus wears down, and a loosely bound comet may finally break apart into a stream of dust fragments.[11]
