01我们能看到宇宙的“边界”吗？可观测宇宙到底有多大？Can we see the edge of the universe, and how large is the observable universe?

宇宙年龄约 138 亿年，可观测宇宙共动半径约 465 亿光年；CMB 是观测上最早的一堵墙。The universe is about 13.8 billion years old; the observable radius is about 46.5 billion light-years, and the CMB is the earliest observable wall.

我们看不到宇宙的实体边界，只能看到“可观测宇宙”：光从宇宙诞生以来有足够时间到达地球的范围。宇宙年龄约 138 亿年，但可观测宇宙的共动半径约 465 亿光年；两个数字不矛盾，因为光传播期间空间本身一直在膨胀，把发光天体推到了更远的位置。We cannot see a physical edge of the universe, only the observable universe: the region from which light has had time to reach Earth since the beginning. The universe is about 13.8 billion years old, yet the comoving radius of the observable universe is about 46.5 billion light-years. The two figures do not contradict each other, because space itself kept expanding while the light traveled, pushing the emitting body farther away.[1][6]

最远能看到的光来自复合时期。大爆炸后约 38 万年，电子与原子核结合成中性原子，光子不再被自由电子频繁散射，宇宙变得透明；这批最早的光就是宇宙微波背景辐射，对应红移约 1100。复合时光子温度约 3000 K，今天 CMB 温度降到 2.725 K，波长被红移从可见光拉到微波波段。在它之前，宇宙是不透明的等离子体，没有任何光可以直接穿出，构成了观测上的一堵“墙”。The farthest light we can see comes from the epoch of recombination. About 380,000 years after the Big Bang, electrons combined with nuclei into neutral atoms, photons stopped scattering off free electrons so often, and the universe became transparent. That earliest light is the cosmic microwave background, at a redshift near 1100. Before it, the universe was an opaque plasma, a wall no direct light can pass through.[1][7]

距离层级可以逐级核对：日地距离约 1.5 亿千米，海王星轨道约 30 天文单位；太阳系处在直径约 10 万光年的银河系中；银河系属于约五十个星系组成的本星系群，再往上是室女座超星系团；可观测宇宙包含的星系近年估计达到万亿量级，半径约 465 亿光年。每一级尺度都由恒星测距、造父变星和红移距离阶梯逐级标定。The distance ladder can be checked level by level: the Sun-Earth distance is about 150 million kilometers, Neptune's orbit about 30 astronomical units; the Solar System sits in a Milky Way about 100,000 light-years across; the Milky Way belongs to the Local Group, then the Virgo Supercluster; the observable universe holds hundreds of billions of galaxies within a 46.5-billion-light-year radius. Each scale is calibrated by stellar distances, Cepheid variables and the redshift distance ladder.[1][5]

视界之外的天体并不是不存在，只是它们发出的光至今来不及到达地球；随着时间推移，更远区域的光会陆续抵达，可观测宇宙还在缓慢扩大。与此同时，空间膨胀让遥远星系所在区域的退行速度可以超过光速，这并不违反相对论——限制的是物体在空间中的运动速度，不是空间本身的拉伸。本星系群、银河系与仙女座受引力束缚，并不参与整体膨胀。Objects beyond the horizon are not nonexistent; their light simply has not had time to reach Earth. As time passes, light from farther regions arrives, so the observable universe slowly grows. Meanwhile, expansion lets distant galaxies recede faster than light, which does not violate relativity: relativity limits motion through space, not the stretching of space itself.[1][6]

把“看不到边界”误当成“宇宙有个外壳”是常见误解。可观测宇宙是一个以观测者为中心的球形范围，换一个星系，中心和边界也随之平移；宇宙整体是否有限、是否闭合，仍由空间曲率测量和大尺度结构观测共同约束，不能凭一张深空照片下结论。Treating "no visible edge" as "the universe has a shell" is a common mistake. The observable universe is a sphere centered on the observer; move to another galaxy and the center and boundary shift with it. Whether the universe as a whole is finite or closed is still constrained by curvature measurements and large-scale structure, not by a single deep-field photograph.[6][7]

02宇宙为什么在膨胀，又为什么没有中心？哈勃常数的两个测量值差在哪里？Why is the universe expanding with no center, and where do the two Hubble constant measurements differ?

空间拉伸驱动距离相关退行；CMB 67.4±0.5 对本地阶梯 73.0±1.0，差距约 5σ。Space stretching drives distance-dependent recession; CMB fit gives 67.4±0.5 and the local ladder 73.0±1.0 km/s/Mpc, a gap near 5σ.

宇宙膨胀不是星系在静态空间里飞散，而是空间本身在拉伸。哈勃 1929 年由河外星系红移与距离的相关关系发现这一规律，哈勃定律给出退行速度与距离的线性关系 v = H0·d：距离越远的星系，红移越大、退行越快，H0 单位为千米每秒每百万秒差距，1 Mpc 约合 326 万光年。膨胀没有中心，也没有边缘；把葡萄干嵌进发酵面团，每颗葡萄干看到其他葡萄干都在远离自己，星系之间的距离随面团膨胀而增大。Cosmic expansion is not galaxies flying apart through static space; space itself stretches. Hubble's law gives a linear relation between recession speed and distance, v = H0·d: the farther a galaxy, the larger its redshift and the faster it recedes. Expansion has no center and no edge. In a rising dough with raisins, every raisin sees the others move away; distances between galaxies grow as the dough expands.[2][6]

红移是膨胀留下的直接标记。遥远星系的光谱线向红端移动，波长被空间拉伸；1998 年通过 Ia 型超新星测距，天文学家发现宇宙膨胀在最近几十亿年其实在加速，这一发现把暗能量推到了宇宙学舞台中心。本星系群内部受引力束缚，不会随膨胀散开；哈勃定律描述的是大尺度平均行为。膨胀是大尺度上引力与暗能量的平均效果；在原子内部，电磁力把电子和原子核固定在稳定距离上，在行星、恒星和星系内部，引力同样远强于膨胀的拉伸，这些束缚系统的尺度不会被宇宙膨胀拉开。光源靠近观测者时，谱线则向蓝端移动，称为蓝移。Redshift is the direct marker of expansion. Spectral lines of distant galaxies shift toward the red, their wavelengths stretched by space. In 1998, distance measurements of Type Ia supernovae showed that expansion has actually been accelerating over the last few billion years, a finding that put dark energy at the center of cosmology. The Local Group is gravitationally bound and does not expand apart; Hubble's law describes the large-scale average. Expansion is a large-scale average between gravity and dark energy; inside an atom the electromagnetic force holds nucleus and electrons at fixed distances, and inside planets, stars and galaxies gravity likewise overwhelms the stretching, so bound systems do not expand with the universe. When a source approaches, its spectral lines shift toward the blue, a blueshift.[2][6]

今天最集中的争议是哈勃常数 H0 的数值。普朗克卫星测量宇宙微波背景，结合 ΛCDM 宇宙学模型拟合出早期宇宙对应的 H0 约 67.4 ± 0.5 千米每秒每百万秒差距；本地距离阶梯用造父变星定标、再由 Ia 超新星延伸到远距，SH0ES 团队测得约 73.0 ± 1.0 千米每秒每百万秒差距。两组结果相差约 5.6 个单位、约五个标准差，称为哈勃张力。The sharpest current debate is the value of the Hubble constant H0. The Planck satellite measured the cosmic microwave background and, fitting the early universe within the ΛCDM model, obtained H0 about 67.4 ± 0.5 km/s/Mpc. The local distance ladder, calibrated with Cepheid variables and extended by Type Ia supernovae, gives about 73.0 ± 1.0 km/s/Mpc from the SH0ES team. The gap is about five standard deviations and is called the Hubble tension.[7][8]

两套方法测量的是不同时标：CMB 反映大爆炸后 38 万年的早期状态，再由模型外推到今天；本地阶梯直接测量邻近天体的当前距离与速度。差异可能来自测距标定误差、尘埃与恒星演化模型，也可能指向模型之外的新物理，例如早期暗能量或修正引力；目前两种解释都没有被证实。The two methods probe different epochs: the CMB reflects the early state 380,000 years after the Big Bang and is extrapolated to today by a model; the local ladder measures nearby distances and velocities directly. The difference may come from distance calibration errors, dust and stellar-evolution models, or from new physics beyond the model, such as early dark energy or modified gravity; neither explanation is confirmed yet.[2][8]

把哈勃张力简单归为“某组数据算错了”过于草率。误差棒已经包含测量统计与部分系统误差，分歧却仍超出联合误差范围；同样，把它当作宇宙模型已经被推翻的证据也为时过早。检验方向包括更多造父变星、红巨星末端距离标尺、强引力透镜时间延迟和引力波标准烛光等独立渠道。Writing off the Hubble tension as "one dataset miscalculated" is too quick. The error bars already include statistical and some systematic uncertainty, yet the disagreement exceeds the combined range. Equally, treating it as proof that the cosmological model has been overturned is premature. Tests rely on independent channels: more Cepheids, the red-giant-branch tip distance scale, strong-lens time delays and gravitational-wave standard sirens.[2][8]

03宇宙主要由什么构成？暗物质和暗能量各有什么证据，宇宙最后会怎样？What is the universe made of? What evidence supports dark matter and dark energy, and how might it end?

普通物质约 5%、暗物质约 27%、暗能量约 68%；暗物质靠引力证据，暗能量由加速膨胀推断。About 5% ordinary matter, 27% dark matter, 68% dark energy; dark matter rests on gravity evidence, dark energy on accelerated expansion.

宇宙的主要成分不是我们熟悉的普通物质。按普朗克卫星 2018 年的测量，物质密度参数 Ωm 约 0.315，其中暗能量对应 ΩΛ 约 0.685；换算成能量预算，约 68% 是暗能量，约 27% 是暗物质，普通重子物质只占约 5%；恒星、行星和我们自己都属于这 5%。辐射和中微子在今天的能量预算中占比很小，但在早期宇宙中地位完全不同。The main component of the universe is not the ordinary matter we know. Planck measurements put about 68% in dark energy, about 27% in dark matter, and only about 5% in ordinary matter; stars, planets and ourselves belong to that 5%. Radiation and neutrinos contribute little to today's energy budget but played a very different role in the early universe.[3][7]

暗物质不发光、不吸收或反射光，也不参与电磁相互作用，只通过引力表现自己。证据来自多条独立线索：旋涡星系的旋转曲线在可见恒星之外仍然平坦，说明外围有看不见的质量；星系团的引力透镜质量分布远大于可见星系质量之和，团内热气体的 X 射线观测也指向缺失质量；子弹星系团的碰撞观测把引力中心与发光物质分开，支持暗物质存在。候选粒子包括弱相互作用大质量粒子、轴子等，尚未被直接探测到。Dark matter emits no light and neither absorbs nor reflects it; it reveals itself through gravity alone. Evidence comes from independent lines: rotation curves of spiral galaxies stay flat beyond the visible stars, implying unseen mass at the outskirts; the lensing mass of galaxy clusters far exceeds the sum of visible galaxies; the Bullet Cluster collision separates the gravitational center from the luminous matter, supporting dark matter. Candidates include weakly interacting massive particles and axions, none detected directly yet.[3][6]

暗能量是驱动加速膨胀的成分，性质比暗物质更不清楚。它可以被写成宇宙学常数，对应真空本身的能量密度；也可能是随时间演化的动力学场。观测上它表现为一种产生排斥效应的能量，让宇宙膨胀在约五十亿年前由减速转为加速。暗能量不与普通物质聚集成团，而是均匀分布在空间中。Dark energy drives accelerated expansion and is even less understood in nature. It can be written as a cosmological constant, the energy density of the vacuum itself, or as a dynamical field evolving over time. Observationally it acts as a repulsive energy that turned expansion from deceleration to acceleration about five billion years ago. Dark energy does not clump like ordinary matter; it is spread uniformly through space.[3][6]

宇宙结局取决于暗能量如何演化。如果暗能量保持宇宙学常数形式，状态方程参数 w ≈ -1，膨胀将持续下去，星系陆续退行出可观测范围，恒星燃尽后宇宙走向冷寂；如果暗能量随时间增强、w 小于 -1，可能出现大撕裂，星系、恒星乃至原子被膨胀撕开；若暗能量减弱或引力重新占优，也存在收缩反弹的可能，但现有观测更支持持续膨胀。The fate of the universe depends on how dark energy evolves. If it stays a cosmological constant, expansion continues, galaxies recede out of the observable range, and after stars burn out the universe drifts toward cold silence. If dark energy strengthens over time, a Big Rip could tear apart galaxies, stars and even atoms. If it weakens or gravity regains the upper hand, a contracting rebound remains possible, though current observations favor continued expansion.[3][6]

需要区分证据强度：暗物质有旋转曲线、透镜和碰撞系统等多条观测支撑，暗能量主要由加速膨胀和 CMB 大尺度结构间接推断；两者都还没有在实验室中被直接确认。把暗物质和暗能量混为一谈，或把它们当成“尚未解释的空缺”，都会低估各自独立的观测基础和待解决的理论问题。Evidence strength must be kept separate: dark matter rests on rotation curves, lensing and colliding systems, while dark energy is inferred mainly from accelerated expansion and CMB large-scale structure. Neither has been confirmed directly in a laboratory. Conflating dark matter and dark energy, or treating both as unexplained gaps, understates their independent observational bases and the theoretical problems still open.[3][7]

04星系有哪些类型？我们在银河系内部，怎样测绘出自己所在的星系？What galaxy types exist, and how do we map the Milky Way from inside it?

旋涡、椭圆、不规则星系；银河系是棒旋星系，靠射电 21 cm、红外和盖亚星表在盘内重建结构。Spiral, elliptical and irregular galaxies; the Milky Way is a barred spiral reconstructed from 21-cm radio, infrared and Gaia catalogs.

星系是恒星、气体、尘埃和暗物质在引力束缚下组成的庞大系统，按形态主要分为旋涡星系、椭圆星系和不规则星系。银河系是一个棒旋星系，直径约 10 万光年，太阳位于距银心约 2.6 万光年的银盘内侧，绕银心公转一周约 2.3 亿年；银心藏有质量约 400 万倍太阳的超大质量黑洞人马座 A*，旋臂由尘埃带和年轻蓝色恒星勾勒。我们身处盘内，看不到银河系的全貌，只能靠多波段观测把它重建出来。太阳绕银心的轨道速度约 220 到 230 千米每秒；从北天极方向看地球沿逆时针自转，从银极方向俯视，银河系盘内恒星也沿大体相同的方向绕银心转动，这种同向继承了原星云坍缩时保留的角动量。银河系本身也在运动：它随本星系群朝仙女座方向靠近，并相对宇宙微波背景有每秒数百千米的本动速度。Galaxies are vast systems of stars, gas, dust and dark matter bound by gravity, divided mainly into spirals, ellipticals and irregulars. The Milky Way is a barred spiral about 100,000 light-years across; the Sun sits inside the disk about 26,000 light-years from the center, completing one orbit in about 230 million years. Because we are inside the disk, we cannot see the Milky Way whole and must reconstruct it from multi-wavelength observations. The Sun orbits the Galactic center at about 220 to 230 kilometers per second; viewed from the north ecliptic pole, Earth spins counterclockwise, and seen from the north Galactic pole, disk stars orbit the center in roughly the same sense, a common direction inherited from the angular momentum retained as the parent cloud collapsed. The Galaxy itself is also moving: it approaches Andromeda within the Local Group and has a peculiar velocity of several hundred kilometers per second relative to the cosmic microwave background.[4][5]

测绘银河系的难点是尘埃遮挡。银盘内的星际尘埃在可见光波段吸收和散射星光，银心方向尤其严重；射电波段的中性氢 21 厘米谱线可以穿透尘埃，描绘旋臂的气体分布；红外观测追踪被尘埃包裹的恒星；盖亚卫星第三期星表收录约 18 亿颗源，测量位置、距离和自行，把银盘结构和星族运动定量化。Mapping the Galaxy is hard because of dust. Interstellar dust in the disk absorbs and scatters visible starlight, especially toward the center. The 21-centimeter radio line of neutral hydrogen penetrates the dust and traces the gas in spiral arms; infrared observations follow stars hidden in dust; the Gaia satellite measured positions, distances and proper motions for more than a billion stars, quantifying disk structure and stellar motions.[5][6]

从外部看，银河系与仙女座星系是本星系群中两个最大的旋涡星系，周围伴随数十个矮星系，仙女座距离约 254 万光年。两者正在相互靠近，预计约 40 亿年后并合成一个椭圆状星系；由于恒星间距远大于恒星半径，并合时恒星之间几乎不会直接碰撞。星系并合是宇宙中星系增长的主要方式之一，小星系被大星系俘获、撕裂，恒星流环绕主星系分布。From outside, the Milky Way and Andromeda are the two largest spirals in the Local Group, accompanied by dozens of dwarf galaxies. The two are approaching each other and are expected to merge into an elliptical-like galaxy in about four billion years. Galaxy mergers are a main route of growth: small galaxies are captured and torn apart, leaving stellar streams around the main galaxy.[4][6]

星系在宇宙中的分布也提供了结构证据。红移巡天显示星系沿纤维状结构排列，纤维之间是巨大空洞；星系团是纤维交汇的节点。这类大尺度结构由暗物质引力骨架引导，普通物质在骨架上冷却形成恒星和星系；在更大的拉尼亚凯亚超星系团划分中，银河系只是边缘区域的一个成员。Galaxy distribution also provides structural evidence. Redshift surveys show galaxies arranged along filamentary structures with huge voids between them; clusters sit at filament intersections. This large-scale structure follows the gravitational scaffold of dark matter, on which ordinary matter cools to form stars and galaxies.[4][6]

“给银河系拍一张全景照片”严格来说做不到：我们只能在盘内测量各个方向的恒星与气体，再由模型拼出外形。地面拍摄的银河拱只是我们所在旋臂方向上的一条亮带，不是银河系俯视图；把照片里的亮带当成银盘全貌，会误判旋臂结构和银心位置。Strictly speaking, a full panoramic photograph of the Milky Way cannot be taken: we measure stars and gas from inside the disk in every direction and model the overall shape. A ground-based Milky Way arch is only a bright band in the direction of our own arm, not a top-down view of the disk; mistaking that band for the whole disk misjudges the spiral arms and the center.[5][6]

05大爆炸之后宇宙经历了哪些阶段？类星体和宇宙线从哪里来，我们找到地外生命了吗？What stages followed the Big Bang? Where do quasars and cosmic rays come from, and have we found extraterrestrial life?

复合期后是黑暗时代，第一代恒星点亮并完成再电离；类星体由超大质量黑洞驱动，地外生命目前尚未确认。Recombination was followed by a dark age, then the first stars and reionization; quasars are powered by supermassive black holes, and life is unconfirmed.

宇宙早期不是一片静止的黑暗，而是一条有明确阶段的时间线。大爆炸后约 38 万年复合，光子脱耦形成宇宙微波背景；此后恒星尚未点亮，宇宙进入只有中性氢和氦的黑暗时代；第一代大质量恒星约在大爆炸后一到两亿年间形成，发出的紫外辐射把周围气体重新电离，即再电离时期，再电离大约在红移 z≈6、即大爆炸后约十亿年时完成；再往后星系、星团和今天的大尺度结构逐步成形。The early universe was not static darkness but a timeline with clear stages. Recombination at about 380,000 years let photons decouple into the cosmic microwave background; after that, before stars lit up, the universe entered a dark age of neutral hydrogen and helium. The first massive stars formed between roughly 100 and 200 million years after the Big Bang, their ultraviolet radiation re-ionizing the surrounding gas in the epoch of reionization; galaxies, clusters and today's large-scale structure then took shape.[1][2]

第一代恒星被称为第三星族星，几乎不含金属，质量可能很大、寿命很短，以超新星结束生命，把重元素抛回星际介质。今天看到的星系是多代恒星和并合的产物；最遥远的星系候选体由詹姆斯·韦布望远镜在红移十以上的区间发现，用于约束早期星系形成的时间和效率。The first stars are called Population III stars, nearly metal-free, possibly very massive and short-lived, ending as supernovae that ejected heavy elements back into the interstellar medium. The galaxies we see today are products of many stellar generations and mergers. The most distant galaxy candidates found by the James Webb Space Telescope at redshifts above ten constrain when early galaxies formed and how efficiently.[1][6]

宇宙中最亮的持续光源是类星体。它们由星系中心的超大质量黑洞驱动：周围气体形成吸积盘，摩擦和引力压缩把物质加热到高温，在小尺度上发出超过整个星系的辐射，亮时光度可达 10⁴⁷ erg/s 量级，吸积辐射效率高于恒星内部核反应；喷流沿黑洞轴向两侧延伸数十万光年。活动星系核是早期宇宙中重元素和能量再分配的重要环节。已知类星体都在数亿到数十亿光年外，辐射被距离稀释，不会直接影响地球气候或生命；银心人马座 A* 现在是宁静活动核，并非活跃类星体。The brightest persistent sources in the universe are quasars. They are powered by supermassive black holes at galaxy centers: surrounding gas forms an accretion disk, friction and gravitational compression heat the material to high temperature, and on a tiny scale it radiates more than an entire galaxy. Jets extend hundreds of thousands of light-years along the black-hole axis. Active galactic nuclei were key to redistributing heavy elements and energy in the early universe. Known quasars lie hundreds of millions to billions of light-years away, so their radiation is diluted by distance and has no direct effect on Earth's climate or life; Sgr A* at the center is now a quiet nucleus, not an active quasar.[2][6]

高能宇宙线是另一类极端现象：能量最高的带电粒子来自太阳系之外，源候选包括超新星遗迹、脉冲星风星云和活动星系核。它们在磁场中偏转，方向记录困难，成分和能谱仍在观测中；宇宙线与星系化学演化、星际介质加热直接相关。加速主要靠激波扩散加速：超新星遗迹激波和脉冲星风云磁场反复反射带电粒子，每穿一次激波获得一点能量；高能宇宙线电离星际气体、改变分子云化学，抵达行星时辐射高层大气与电子设备。High-energy cosmic rays are another extreme phenomenon: the most energetic charged particles come from outside the Solar System, with candidates including supernova remnants, pulsar-wind nebulae and active galactic nuclei. They are deflected by magnetic fields, making direction records hard, and their composition and energy spectrum remain under observation. Cosmic rays are directly tied to galactic chemical evolution and interstellar-medium heating. Acceleration mainly proceeds by diffusive shock acceleration: the expanding shock of a supernova remnant and the pulsar-wind-nebula field bounce charged particles back and forth, adding energy on each crossing. High-energy cosmic rays ionize interstellar gas, alter molecular-cloud chemistry, and expose a planet's upper atmosphere and electronics to radiation.[2][6]

关于地外生命，目前的可靠结论是“尚未发现”。SETI 项目监听射电和光学信号，搜寻技术文明留下的特征；德雷克方程把恒星、行星、生命和文明概率串成框架，但各参数取值范围很宽，不能给出确定结论；所谓费米悖论只是对“为什么沉默”的提问，本身不构成生命不存在的证据。宜居行星数量增加不等于存在生命，更不等于存在可通信的文明；生命起源的化学步骤在地球上如何完成，本身仍是未解决的科学问题。On extraterrestrial life, the reliable conclusion so far is "not yet found." SETI projects listen for radio and optical signals that a technological civilization might leave behind. The Drake equation chains together stars, planets, life and civilization probabilities, but its parameters span wide ranges and yield no firm conclusion. More habitable planets do not imply life, let alone communicable civilizations; how the chemical steps of life's origin completed on Earth remains an open scientific question.[1][6]

06夜空为什么是黑的？如果星星无限多，天空不该被照亮吗？Why is the night sky black? Should an infinity of stars not light it up?

奥伯斯佯谬：有限年龄、有限可观测范围、恒星寿命有限与膨胀红移，共同切断视线必落恒星的推理。Olbers' paradox: finite age, finite observable range, stellar lifetimes and expansion redshift break the sightline-to-star reasoning.

夜空为什么是黑的，看似常识，历史上却是一道著名的宇宙学佯谬，即奥伯斯佯谬。如果宇宙空间无限、恒星均匀分布，而且存在了无限久，那么沿任意视线向外延伸，最终都应当落在某颗恒星的表面；层层叠加的星光会把整片天空照得和太阳表面一样亮。真实夜空却大部分是黑的，这个矛盾说明“无限、静态、永恒”的前提至少有一条不成立。Why the night sky is black looks obvious, but historically it is a famous cosmological puzzle, Olbers' paradox. If space were infinite, stars uniformly distributed, and the universe existed forever, every line of sight outward would eventually hit a stellar surface; stacked starlight would make the whole sky as bright as the Sun's surface. The real sky is mostly dark, so at least one of those infinite, static, eternal premises fails.[1][6]

第一条限制是宇宙年龄有限。宇宙约在 138 亿年前起源，光以有限速度传播，只有从可观测范围内发出、时间足够到达地球的光才能被我们看见；视界之外的恒星光还在路上，视线在它们之前就终止于尚未照亮的区域。可观测宇宙共动半径约 465 亿光年，这个范围之外的天体并不因看不见而不存在，只是光来不及抵达，夜空因此保留大片黑暗。The first limit is the finite age of the universe. It began about 13.8 billion years ago, and light travels at a finite speed, so only light emitted within the observable range and given enough time to reach Earth can be seen. Light from beyond the horizon is still on its way, and lines of sight end in regions not yet lit. The comoving observable radius is about 46.5 billion light-years; bodies outside are not nonexistent, but their light has not arrived, leaving large dark patches in the sky.[1]

第二条限制是恒星寿命有限。恒星只在主序阶段发光几十亿到上百亿年，前身星形成之前、燃料烧尽之后都不发光；即使宇宙更老，每一条视线也不会永远被发光恒星占据。星际尘埃确实会吸收星光，但它吸收的能量会以热辐射再释放出来，长期静态宇宙里尘埃终究会被加热到与恒星相当的温度，不能靠遮挡把星光永久藏起来。The second limit is the finite lifetime of stars. A star shines only during its main sequence, for several to ten billion years; before it formed and after its fuel runs out, it emits no light. Even in an older universe, no sightline would stay occupied by a glowing star forever. Interstellar dust does absorb starlight, but the absorbed energy is re-emitted as thermal radiation; in an eternal static universe the dust would eventually heat to stellar temperatures and could not hide the light permanently.[1][6]

第三条限制是空间在膨胀。遥远星系的退行把光谱线向红端移动，波长被拉长；足够远的星光红移到肉眼不可见的红外甚至更长波段，可见光波段上就显得更暗。膨胀还在不断把星系推出可观测范围，今天看到的夜空亮度是有限年龄、有限恒星寿命与膨胀红移共同作用的结果，而不是宇宙从一开始就均匀照亮。The third limit is expanding space. Receding distant galaxies shift spectral lines toward the red as wavelengths stretch; starlight from far enough away redshifts into invisible infrared or longer bands and looks darker in visible light. Expansion also pushes galaxies out of the observable range over time. Today's sky brightness results from finite age, finite stellar lifetimes and redshift together, not from a uniformly illuminated universe from the start.[2][6]

边界上要区分：夜空发黑不是因为星星太少，也不是尘埃挡住了大部分星光；有限年龄、有限可观测范围、有限寿命和膨胀红移共同切断了“视线必落恒星”的推理。奥伯斯佯谬是对宇宙模型的反证，而不是对恒星分布的直接测量；各因素贡献多少，仍由 CMB、星系计数和膨胀模型共同约束。把黑暗天空直接读成“宇宙没有边界”，或读成“恒星很稀少”，都越过了观测能支持的结论。Boundary: the dark sky does not mean there are too few stars, nor that dust blocks most starlight. Finite age, finite observable range, finite lifetimes and redshift together break the reasoning that every sightline must hit a star. Olbers' paradox disproves a model premise rather than directly measuring star distribution; how much each factor contributes remains constrained by the CMB, galaxy counts and expansion models. Reading the dark sky as proof that the universe has no boundary, or that stars are scarce, goes beyond what observations support.[1][2]

07时间膨胀公式为什么要用光的往返路程？单程为什么不行？Why does the time-dilation formula use a round-trip light path, and why not one way?

往返让发射与接收在同一地点、由同一时钟读数；单程需要先约定同时性，而同时性在相对论中是相对的。A round trip keeps emission and reception at one place read by one clock; a one-way leg needs a simultaneity convention, and simultaneity is relative.

狭义相对论里的时间膨胀公式，通常用光钟思想实验推导：光在上下两面镜子之间来回反射，每往返一次算作一个滴答。静止在钟所在参考系里的观测者看到光垂直上下，单程距离为镜面间距 D，往返时间 2D/c；地面观测者看到钟随飞船运动，光走的是两条斜向路径，往返路程更长，而光速在两个参考系中都等于 c，于是同样的滴答在地面看来间隔被拉长，这就是时间膨胀。In special relativity, the time-dilation formula is usually derived from a light-clock thought experiment: light bounces back and forth between two mirrors, and each round trip counts one tick. An observer at rest with the clock sees light go straight up and down, one-way distance D, round-trip time 2D/c. The ground observer sees the clock move with the spacecraft, so the light travels two diagonal paths with a longer total length; since light speed equals c in both frames, the same tick takes longer to the ground observer, which is time dilation.[9]

公式取往返路程，而不是只算单程，是为了让发射和接收发生在同一地点。光钟从下镜面发出、回到下镜面，发射与接收由同一只时钟读数，这段固有时间 Δτ 可以直接测量；若只算单程，光到达上镜面的事件发生在另一个位置，要把两地时钟的读数对齐，就必须先约定同时性。而在相对论里同时性是相对的，不同惯性系对“两地同时”的判断不一致，单程光信号无法用同一只时钟闭环测量。The formula uses a round trip rather than a one-way leg so emission and reception happen at the same place. The light leaves the lower mirror and returns to it, so emission and reception are read by one clock, and this proper time Δτ can be measured directly. A one-way leg reaches the upper mirror at another location; aligning readings from two clocks requires a simultaneity convention first. In relativity simultaneity is relative, different inertial frames disagree on what is simultaneous at two places, and a one-way signal cannot be closed-loop measured by a single clock.[9][10]

几何关系由勾股定理给出。运动钟在时间 Δt 内移动 vΔt，光沿斜边走过 cΔt，竖直间距 D 不变；两个参考系中的垂直距离相同，于是 (cΔt)² = (vΔt)² + (cΔτ)²，整理得到 Δt = γΔτ，洛伦兹因子 γ = 1/√(1−v²/c²)。v 远小于 c 时 γ 接近 1，地面日常运动看不出差别；速度越接近光速，时间拉长越明显。公式里的往返路径正是为了在垂直方向上保留一个两参考系共同认可的长度。The geometry follows the Pythagorean theorem. In time Δt the moving clock travels vΔt, light travels the hypotenuse cΔt, and the vertical spacing D stays fixed; transverse distance is the same in both frames, so (cΔt)² = (vΔt)² + (cΔτ)², giving Δt = γΔτ with γ = 1/√(1−v²/c²). When v is much smaller than c, γ is near 1 and everyday motion shows no difference; closer to light speed, time stretches more. The round-trip path keeps one vertical length that both frames agree on.[9]

这套推导有实验支撑。宇宙线产生的 μ 子在静止时半衰期约 1.52 微秒，按经典预期很难从高层大气到达地面；但地面探测到的 μ 子数量明显更多，正是运动 μ 子的时间被拉长、寿命在地面参考系中变长的结果。原子钟环球飞行、卫星导航时钟修正也都检验了时间膨胀效应；光速不变公设是整个推导的前提，往返光钟只是把这一公设变成可计算的几何关系。The derivation has experimental support. Muons produced by cosmic rays have a half-life of about 1.52 microseconds at rest, so classically few should reach the ground from the upper atmosphere; detectors find more muons than expected because moving muons have dilated time, their lifetimes lengthened in the ground frame. Atomic-clock round-the-world flights and satellite navigation clock corrections also test time dilation. The constant-speed-of-light postulate is the premise; the round-trip light clock turns that postulate into a calculable geometry.[6][10]

边界上要分清：时间膨胀是相对运动参考系之间的测量效应，不是时钟机械变慢，也不是钟出了故障；公式比较的是两个惯性系中对同一过程的时间读数。往返约定来自同时性的相对性，并不代表光在单程上速度不同，单程光速测量本身依赖时钟同步约定。把时间膨胀当成“运动物体寿命主观变长”，或把往返路线当成光的实际现实轨迹，都会误读推导；μ 子、原子钟和导航卫星的测量才是公式成立的观测依据。Boundary: time dilation is a measurement effect between relatively moving frames, not a clock mechanically slowing down or malfunctioning; the formula compares readings of one process in two inertial frames. The round-trip convention comes from relativity of simultaneity and does not mean one-way light speed differs; measuring one-way speed itself relies on a clock-sync convention. Treating dilation as subjective lengthening of a moving object's lifetime, or reading the round trip as the actual real-world path of light, misreads the derivation. Muons, atomic clocks and navigation satellites are the observational basis for the formula.[6][10]
