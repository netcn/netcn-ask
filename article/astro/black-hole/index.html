01黑洞是什么？看不到本体，凭什么断定它存在？What is a black hole, and how can we confirm one exists without seeing its body?

黑洞是视界内侧引力连光都逃不脱的致密天体；靠吸积盘 X 射线、恒星轨道、引力波与阴影成像间接确认。A black hole traps even light beneath its event horizon; evidence comes from accretion X-rays, stellar orbits, gravitational waves and shadow imaging.

黑洞是一团被压缩到极小空间的极高密度物质，强到其边界——事件视界——内侧的引力连光都无法逃脱。它不是一个黑色的洞，也不是通往别处的隧道；事件视界是一条把黑洞全部物质包在内部的边界。黑洞本身不发光也不反光，望远镜看不到它本体，只能通过它对周围气体、恒星和光线的影响来间接判断，这也是研究它们长期间接、近来才有直接成像的原因。A black hole is a concentration of matter packed into a very small space, dense enough that gravity beneath its boundary, the event horizon, traps even light. It is not a black pit or a tunnel to somewhere else; the horizon is a boundary containing all the matter that makes the hole. The hole itself emits and reflects no light, so telescopes cannot see it directly; it is inferred from how it affects nearby gas, stars and light. That is why the evidence stayed indirect for so long and direct imaging came only recently.[1][8]

困住光的门槛是逃逸速度。任何天体都有逃逸速度：地球表面约 11 km/s，物体要达到这个速度才能挣脱地球引力。黑洞内部的逃逸速度超过光速，而光速是宇宙速度上限，因此视界以内的光、物质和信息都跑不出来。视界大小只由内部质量决定，即史瓦西半径 Rs = 2GM/c²；把太阳压成黑洞，视界半径只有约 3 公里；把地球压成黑洞，半径要缩到约 1 厘米，比一颗葡萄还小。The threshold that traps light is escape velocity. Every body has one: on Earth it is about 11 km/s, the speed needed to break free of gravity. Inside a black hole the escape velocity exceeds the speed of light, the universe's speed limit, so light, matter and information cannot leave the horizon. Horizon size is set only by enclosed mass through the Schwarzschild radius Rs = 2GM/c². Compressed into a black hole, the Sun's horizon would be only about 3 km across; an Earth-mass black hole would shrink to a radius near 1 cm, smaller than a grape.[1][9]

我们看到的“黑洞照片”其实是阴影，不是盘面本身。事件视界望远镜把全球多台射电望远镜组成等效口径，2019 年公布 M87 中心黑洞的图像：一圈亮环围着中央暗斑，暗斑是视界投在周围发光物质上的阴影；2022 年又公布银河系中心 Sgr A* 的同类图像。亮环来自被加热到高温、绕视界高速旋转的吸积气体，光从黑洞旁弯过，形成我们看到的环状结构，环内暗区才对应视界所在位置。The "black hole photographs" we have are shadows, not the disk itself. The Event Horizon Telescope linked radio dishes worldwide into an equivalent aperture and in 2019 released an image of the M87 central black hole: a bright ring around a dark central patch, the shadow the horizon casts against glowing matter around it. A similar image of Sagittarius A* at the Milky Way's centre followed in 2022. The bright ring comes from hot gas orbiting fast near the horizon; light bends past the hole to form the ring we see, and the dark interior marks where the horizon lies.[4][5]

在直接成像之前，证据主要来自周边效应。密近双星里如果一颗不可见天体的质量超过约 3 倍太阳质量，伴星物质被剥离后在周围形成高温吸积盘，发出强 X 射线，这是恒星级黑洞的典型线索；银河系中心多颗恒星的轨道被一个不可见质量牵引，对应约 400 万倍太阳质量的 Sgr A*；2015 年 LIGO 探测到两个恒星级黑洞并合产生的引力波，从另一条独立途径证实了这类天体存在。Before direct imaging, evidence came from effects on surroundings. In close binary systems, if an unseen companion exceeds about three solar masses, its companion star is stripped and feeds a hot accretion disk that emits strong X-rays, the classic signature of a stellar-mass black hole. Orbits of stars at the Milky Way's centre are pulled by an unseen mass equal to about four million solar masses, Sgr A*. In 2015 LIGO detected gravitational waves from the merger of two stellar-mass black holes, confirming such objects through an independent route.[2][3][8]

边界要写清。阴影大小不等于黑洞物理表面；吸积盘、喷流和背景透镜效应都出现在黑洞附近，不能把亮环误当成视界本身。间接证据给出的是质量和引力效应，黑洞内部结构仍无法直接观测；不同观测手段互相印证，结论才比较可靠。仅凭一道暗斑或一条 X 射线源，还不足以单独断定黑洞身份，需要排除白矮星、中子星等致密天体的可能。The boundary matters. Shadow size is not the physical surface of the hole; accretion disks, jets and background lensing can all appear nearby, and the bright ring must not be mistaken for the horizon itself. Indirect evidence yields mass and gravitational effects, while the interior cannot be observed directly. Conclusions hold only when the different observing methods agree; a single dark patch or one X-ray source cannot identify a black hole on its own, and white dwarfs, neutron stars and other compact objects must first be ruled out.[1][2]

02黑洞是怎么形成的？恒星级、中等质量和超大质量差多少？How do black holes form, and how do stellar-mass, intermediate and supermassive holes differ?

大质量恒星核心超 3 倍太阳质量时坍缩成黑洞；按质量分恒星级、中等质量、超大质量三档，与宿主星系共同演化。Massive stars whose core exceeds three solar masses collapse into holes; three mass classes range from stellar-mass to supermassive, evolving with their host galaxies.

恒星级黑洞来自大质量恒星的死亡。恒星一生靠核心聚变支撑引力，燃料耗尽后核心失去向外的压力支撑；外层物质坠落、在核心反弹，抛出为超新星遗迹。如果残留核心质量超过约 3 倍太阳质量，没有任何已知压力能顶住引力，核心继续坍缩，逃逸速度最终超过光速，就形成黑洞。质量不超过约 10 倍太阳质量的恒星，结局通常是白矮星，而不是黑洞；超新星能否留下黑洞还取决于金属丰度和自转。Stellar-mass black holes are born from dying massive stars. A star spends its life supported against gravity by core fusion; once fuel runs out, the core loses outward pressure, the outer layers fall inward and bounce, and are expelled as a supernova remnant. If the remaining core exceeds about three solar masses, no known pressure can resist gravity. It keeps collapsing until escape velocity exceeds light speed, and a black hole forms. Stars up to roughly ten solar masses usually end as white dwarfs rather than holes; metallicity and spin also decide whether a supernova leaves one behind.[2][9]

按质量，黑洞大致分三档。恒星级黑洞约几倍到几十倍太阳质量，由大质量恒星坍缩而成，已知最轻的约 3.8 倍太阳质量；中等质量黑洞估计在几十到几千倍太阳质量之间，候选多来自球状星团和亮 X 射线源，证据仍在积累；超大质量黑洞位于星系中心，质量从几百万到几十亿倍太阳质量，银河系中心的 Sgr A* 约 400 万倍太阳质量，观测到最重的 TON 618 约 660 亿倍太阳质量。Black holes fall into three broad mass classes. Stellar-mass holes are a few to tens of solar masses, from massive-star collapse, and the lightest known is about 3.8 solar masses. Intermediate-mass holes are estimated at tens to thousands of solar masses; candidates come from globular clusters and bright X-ray sources, and evidence is still accumulating. Supermassive holes sit at galaxy centres, from millions to billions of solar masses: Sgr A* at the Milky Way's centre is about four million solar masses, and the heaviest observed, TON 618, about 66 billion.[2][8]

超大质量黑洞和宿主星系共同演化。观测发现大多数旋涡和椭圆星系中心都有超大质量黑洞，其质量常与星系核球的恒星质量成比例；黑洞吸积周围气体时释放的能量会反馈影响恒星形成。早期宇宙中已存在质量很大的黑洞，它们来不及从恒星级黑洞慢慢长大，因此中等质量前身天体的起源仍是研究焦点。星系并非绕着黑洞“形成”，而是黑洞与宿主星系在并合和吸积过程中互相影响。Supermassive black holes evolve together with their host galaxies. Most spiral and elliptical galaxies host one at the centre, and its mass tends to scale with the stellar mass of the host bulge. Energy released as the hole accretes gas feeds back on star formation. Very massive black holes already existed in the early universe, too early to grow from stellar-mass seeds, so the origin of their intermediate-mass precursors remains a research focus. Galaxies did not form orbiting a black hole; hole and host instead influence each other through mergers and accretion.[2][9]

黑洞也会“吃”周围物质，但不是无限填塞。气体和恒星靠近时先形成吸积盘，摩擦加热到高温后一部分能量以辐射和喷流形式带走；物质越过视界才真正落入。如果供料过快，辐射压会把一部分气体推回去，所谓“堵住”更多是吸积率上限的问题。双黑洞并合后质量相加，引力波事件给出了恒星级黑洞质量分布的直接样本，也说明黑洞可以靠吞食同类继续长大。Black holes do consume surrounding matter, but not by plugging up indefinitely. Gas and stars approaching first form an accretion disk; friction heats it, and some energy leaves as radiation and jets, while matter crosses the horizon only afterwards. If the supply arrives too fast, radiation pressure pushes some gas back out, so the "clogging" is more an upper limit on accretion rate. Merging holes add their masses, and gravitational-wave events give direct samples of the stellar-mass mass distribution, showing that holes can keep growing by eating other holes.[2][3]

边界要写清。质量分级是按现有观测归纳的统计分类，边界并不严格；中等质量黑洞候选尚未全部确认，第一代恒星形成的原初黑洞仍是假说。超新星能否留下黑洞取决于核心质量、金属丰度和自转，不是所有大质量恒星都变成黑洞。把星系运动简单归因于中心黑洞会出错：星系质量主要由恒星、气体和暗物质构成，黑洞只占很小一部分；离黑洞足够远时，它的引力和同质量普通天体没有区别。The boundary matters. The mass classes are statistical groupings from current observations, with no sharp dividing lines; not every intermediate-mass candidate is confirmed, and primordial black holes from the first stars remain a hypothesis. Whether a supernova leaves a hole depends on core mass, metallicity and spin, so not every massive star becomes one. Attributing all galaxy motion to the central hole would be wrong: galaxy mass is mainly stars, gas and dark matter, and the hole accounts for only a small fraction. Far enough away, its gravity matches that of any ordinary body of the same mass.[2][8]

03奇点密度无限大，广义相对论还成立吗？黑洞没有大小怎么旋转？Does general relativity hold at an infinitely dense singularity, and how can a size-less black hole spin?

GR 预言奇点处物理量发散，需量子引力；旋转黑洞用克尔解描述，奇点为环，自旋继承前身角动量。GR predicts divergent quantities at the singularity, needing quantum gravity; rotating holes follow the Kerr solution, with a ring singularity and progenitor angular momentum.

广义相对论预言，越过视界之后，所有物质会继续坍缩到一个奇点：密度趋向无穷大、体积趋向零的位置。史瓦西解描述不旋转的黑洞，中心是一个点；在奇点附近，广义相对论给出的曲率趋向发散，物理量失去通常意义。这并不代表那里真的存在一个密度无穷大的小球，而是说明现有理论在这个尺度上失效，需要尚未完成的量子引力理论来描述，甚至不能确定奇点是否真实出现。General relativity predicts that beyond the horizon all matter keeps collapsing to a singularity: a place where density tends to infinity and volume to zero. The Schwarzschild solution describes a non-rotating black hole with a point at the centre; near that point the curvature predicted by relativity diverges, and physical quantities lose their ordinary meaning. This does not mean a real ball of infinite density sits there; it shows that the current theory breaks down at that scale and needs an unfinished quantum-gravity theory, and whether singularities actually occur is still unknown.[1]

旋转黑洞的图像不同。恒星和气体云本身带着角动量，坍缩时角动量守恒，形成的黑洞通常在旋转；克尔解描述旋转黑洞，奇点不再是一个点，而是一环。黑洞的“大小”指视界范围，旋转表现为视界周围时空被拖拽着一起转动，并不要求奇点本身有体积。观测上最快的恒星级黑洞 GRS 1915+105 每秒自转超过一千次，说明自旋可以非常接近理论上限；所有黑洞都带有自旋这一项基本属性。A rotating black hole looks different. Stars and gas clouds carry angular momentum, and conservation as they collapse means the formed hole usually spins. The Kerr solution describes a rotating black hole, where the singularity is a ring rather than a point. The black hole's "size" means the horizon region; spin appears as spacetime near the horizon dragged into rotation, without requiring the singularity to have volume. The fastest observed stellar-mass hole, GRS 1915+105, spins over a thousand times per second, close to the theoretical upper limit, and spin is one of the basic properties every black hole carries.[8][9]

物质和反物质形成的黑洞在经典意义上没有区别。广义相对论里黑洞由质量、电荷和自旋三个量刻画，形成它的是物质还是反物质并不留下标记；反物质黑洞与同质量物质黑洞遵循同样的时空几何。落入黑洞的物质细节也在视界外无法区分，这是“黑洞无毛”结论的一部分。量子层面信息是否完全丢失，仍是尚未解决的黑洞信息佯谬；霍金后期也提出，信息可能以某种方式留在视界附近的辐射中。A black hole made of matter and one made of antimatter are classically indistinguishable. In general relativity a hole is characterised by mass, charge and spin alone; whether matter or antimatter built it leaves no marker. Matter and antimatter holes of equal mass follow the same spacetime geometry, and details of what fell in cannot be told apart outside the horizon, part of the no-hair conclusion. Whether information is truly lost at the quantum level remains the unresolved information paradox; Hawking later proposed that information might remain in some form in radiation near the horizon.[1]

外部观测者看不到内部坍缩过程。在远处看来，落向黑洞的物体信号不断红移、变暗，仿佛冻结在视界附近；真正穿过视界是局部事件，外部无法直接见证。潮汐力在小质量黑洞附近极强，会把靠近的天体横向挤压、纵向拉长，像面条一样撕碎；超大质量黑洞视界处潮汐力相对温和，穿越瞬间的体感可能并不剧烈，但进入视界后仍无法返回。An outside observer cannot watch the internal collapse. From afar, signals from an infalling object keep redshifting and dimming, as if frozen near the horizon; crossing the horizon is a local event that cannot be witnessed from outside. Tidal forces are extreme near small black holes, squeezing approaching bodies sideways and stretching them vertically like noodles; near a supermassive hole the tidal force at the horizon is milder, so the crossing may feel undramatic, but return is still impossible once inside.[1][8]

边界要写清。奇点、环结构和无毛定理都是广义相对论在理想模型下的结论，内部是否被量子效应抹平还没有观测验证；自旋速度和视界形状依赖质量与自转参数，不能把旋转黑洞套进史瓦西点模型。量子引力、信息佯谬和原初黑洞等问题超出了现有观测能力，目前只能在理论框架内讨论；把数学发散当成物理上真实的无穷大，是常见误读，观测上我们掌握的只是视界外的引力效应。The boundary matters. Singularities, ring structure and the no-hair theorem are conclusions of general relativity under idealised models; whether quantum effects smooth out the interior has no observational test yet. Spin rate and horizon shape depend on mass and rotation parameters, and a rotating hole cannot be forced into the Schwarzschild point model. Quantum gravity, the information paradox and primordial black holes lie beyond present observational reach and can only be discussed inside theory. Treating mathematical divergence as a real physical infinity is a common misreading; observationally we know only the gravitational effects outside the horizon.[1]

04黑洞怎么把光困住？它最终会蒸发消失吗？How does a black hole trap light, and will it eventually evaporate away?

质量弯曲时空使光线落入视界；霍金辐射温度与质量成反比，小黑洞蒸发快，恒星级黑洞寿命约 10⁶⁷ 年。Mass bends spacetime so light falls past the horizon; Hawking temperature scales inversely with mass, small holes evaporate fast, stellar holes live ~10^67 years.

黑洞困住光的方式是弯曲时空。牛顿式理解是“光被引力拉住”，广义相对论的描述是质量把周围时空掰弯，光线沿弯曲时空的最短路径行走；当轨迹弯到视界以内，就不再有向外的路径。视界半径由质量决定，光子在视界外附近形成所谓光子球，靠近的背景光被弯折、放大或复制，这就是引力透镜；吸积盘背面的光也会被弯到黑洞上下方，形成观测到的弧形结构。A black hole traps light by curving spacetime. The Newtonian picture says light is pulled by gravity; general relativity describes mass bending the surrounding spacetime, and light follows the shortest path through that curved geometry. When the path bends inside the horizon, no outward route remains. Horizon radius is set by mass, and nearby light forms a photon sphere; background light close by is bent, magnified or duplicated as gravitational lensing, and light from the far side of the accretion disk wraps above and below the hole into the arcs observers see.[1][8]

光不是唯一被影响的对象。靠近黑洞的气体被潮汐力和摩擦加热到百万度以上，在 X 射线波段发光；绕转气体因多普勒效应一侧偏蓝、一侧偏红。望远镜靠这些周边辐射和透镜效应推断黑洞质量与位置，而不是直接看见本体。地球附近目前已知最近的黑洞 Gaia BH1 约 1500 光年，质量远低于威胁尺度，现有观测没有发现临近地球的危险黑洞；距离够远时，黑洞引力与同质量普通天体并无不同。Light is not the only thing affected. Gas approaching the hole is heated past a million degrees by tides and friction and glows in X-rays; orbiting gas shifts blue on one side and red on the other through the Doppler effect. Telescopes infer mass and position from this surrounding emission and lensing rather than seeing the hole itself. The nearest known black hole, Gaia BH1, is about 1,500 light-years away, far below any threatening scale, and no dangerous close black hole has been observed. Far enough away, its gravity matches that of any ordinary body of the same mass.[1][3][8]

1974 年霍金提出，黑洞并非完全不发光。视界附近的量子涨落产生粒子对，其中一个落入黑洞、另一个带走能量，远处看来就像黑洞在向外辐射；这就是霍金辐射。辐射温度与黑洞质量成反比：质量越小越热、蒸发越快；恒星级黑洞温度远低于宇宙背景，蒸发极其缓慢，按现有理论寿命约 10⁶⁷ 年，在宇宙年龄尺度上近似稳定，远长于当前宇宙年龄。In 1974 Hawking proposed that black holes are not completely dark. Quantum fluctuations near the horizon produce particle pairs; one falls in while the other carries energy away, so from afar the hole looks as if it radiates. This is Hawking radiation. Its temperature is inversely proportional to black hole mass: smaller holes are hotter and evaporate faster. A stellar-mass hole is far colder than the cosmic background and evaporates extremely slowly, with a theoretical lifetime near 10^67 years, effectively stable on cosmic timescales and far longer than the current age of the universe.[7][8]

蒸发终点仍是开放问题。小质量原初黑洞如果存在，可能已在近期蒸发并留下爆发信号；大黑洞则要等宇宙背景温度降到自身辐射温度以下，才开始净损失质量。随着质量下降，温度升高，最后阶段可能以一次爆发结束。霍金辐射至今没有被直接观测到，地面实验室的类比模型提供了旁证，但天体黑洞的辐射太弱，现阶段只能作为理论预言，不能当作已确认的现象。The evaporation endpoint remains an open question. Small primordial black holes, if they exist, may have evaporated recently and left burst signals; large holes start losing mass net only after the background cools below their own radiation temperature. As mass drops, temperature rises, and the final stage may end in a burst. Hawking radiation has never been directly observed; analogue models in ground labs offer indirect support, but astrophysical hole radiation is too weak for now, so it stays a theoretical prediction rather than a confirmed phenomenon.[7]

边界要写清。“黑洞会死亡”目前是理论推演，不是已观测到的事实；蒸发速率、终点残骸和信息归宿都依赖尚未验证的量子引力模型。坠入黑洞的体验因质量而异：小黑洞先撕碎再吞噬，超大质量黑洞视界处潮汐温和；但一旦穿过视界，任何物体都无法把信息送回外部。把霍金辐射当成黑洞周围常见的发光现象，或据此说黑洞很快会消失，都超出了现有证据；能观测到的仍是吸积盘、喷流和透镜效应。The boundary matters. "Black holes die" is currently theory, not an observed fact; evaporation rate, endpoint remnant and information fate all depend on untested quantum-gravity models. The fall-in experience varies with mass: a small hole tears the body apart first, while a supermassive horizon has mild tides, but once the horizon is crossed no object can send information back out. Treating Hawking radiation as a common glow around black holes, or claiming holes will soon vanish, goes beyond the evidence; what observers actually record are accretion disks, jets and lensing effects.[1][8]

05物质怎样绕着致密天体堆成吸积盘？黑洞喷流又为什么成对沿转轴喷出？How does matter form an accretion disk around a compact object, and why do black holes launch paired jets along their spin axis?

伴星物质溢出洛希瓣后靠角动量摊成高温吸积盘，盘内磁场沿转轴准直，把电离物质双向喷出近光速喷流。Overflowing companion matter flattens into a hot accretion disk by angular momentum; its magnetic field collimates ionized gas into two near-light-speed jets.

吸积盘是物质在引力牵引下绕致密天体旋转、摊成的扁平气体盘。致密天体可以是白矮星、中子星或黑洞；在密近双星里，伴星演化膨胀后充满自己的洛希瓣，外层气体越过引力分界流向另一颗星。被捕获的物质不会径直落向中心，因为它带着原轨道的角动量：靠近中心时转动加快，离心作用把物质推向垂直转轴的平面，于是气体在赤道方向铺开成盘，而不是堆成一团。盘的内缘离致密天体很近，物质沿螺旋线一圈圈向内转移。An accretion disk is a flat gas disk in which matter orbits a compact object under gravity and spreads into a plane. The compact object may be a white dwarf, a neutron star or a black hole. In a close binary, the companion swells and fills its Roche lobe, so its outer gas crosses the gravitational boundary toward the other star. Captured matter does not fall straight in because it carries the angular momentum of its original orbit: as it nears the center it spins faster, and centrifugal effect pushes it toward the plane perpendicular to the spin axis. The gas spreads along the equator into a disk and spirals inward from the inner edge.[1][10]

盘内高温来自摩擦、压缩和引力释放。相邻轨道的气体转速不同，内圈比外圈快，层与层之间的黏滞摩擦把一部分轨道能量转化为热；气体不断向内沉降，引力势能释放也加热盘面。吸积盘内侧温度可达约 100 万–1000 万开，在 X 射线波段发出强辐射，这正是 X 射线双星和活动星系核最亮的部分。对超大质量黑洞周围的盘而言，被加热的气体在很小尺度上发出的辐射可以超过整个宿主星系。The disk heats up through friction, compression and released gravity. Neighboring orbits rotate at different speeds, the inner ring faster than the outer one, and viscous friction between layers turns some orbital energy into heat; as gas settles inward, released gravitational potential energy also warms the surface. The inner disk can reach millions to tens of millions of kelvin and shines brightly in X-rays, the brightest part of X-ray binaries and active galactic nuclei. Around a supermassive black hole, heated gas can radiate more than its entire host galaxy on a tiny scale.[1][8]

喷流是从盘和黑洞转轴方向射出的两束高速电离物质。吸积气体携带磁场，盘内摩擦和较差自转把磁场缠绕、放大；磁力线沿黑洞自转轴方向竖起，成为一条准直的通道。被加速的电离气体沿这条通道向转轴两侧喷出，形成方向相反、几乎对称的一对喷流，而不是向四面八方散开。喷流速度可接近光速，沿轴向延伸约 10 万–100 万光年，把能量和物质送回星际空间。Jets are two beams of fast ionized matter launched along the spin axis of the disk and black hole. The accreting gas carries magnetic fields, and disk friction plus differential rotation wind and amplify those fields; field lines stand up along the rotation axis to form a collimated channel. Accelerated ionized gas flows out along this channel on both sides, producing a nearly symmetric pair of opposite jets rather than a spray in all directions. Jet speeds can approach the speed of light, extending hundreds of thousands or even millions of light-years along the axis.[9][11]

喷流的能量来源和准直细节仍在研究中。主流模型认为，旋转黑洞与盘磁场耦合，通过布兰登福德—赞杰克机制从黑洞自转中提取能量；另一类布兰登福德—里斯机制则把盘本身的旋转能转化为喷流动能。观测上，喷流的强度、张角和速度随黑洞质量、吸积率和磁场强度变化；活动星系核的喷流扫过周围气体，会在星系际空间激发出射电瓣和冲击波。The energy source and collimation details of jets are still under study. In leading models, a spinning black hole couples to the disk magnetic field and extracts rotational energy through the Blandford-Znajek mechanism; a second route, the Blandford-Rees mechanism, converts the disk's own rotation into jet kinetic energy. Observed jet strength, opening angle and speed vary with black-hole mass, accretion rate and magnetic field.[9][11]

边界要分清：吸积盘在视界之外，不是黑洞的一部分；喷流也不是从视界内部钻出来的，而是视界外的盘和磁场加工出来的。双星里的吸积盘围绕的可能是中子星或白矮星，不能看到盘和 X 射线就断定是黑洞；致密天体身份要靠轨道质量、光谱和后续观测综合判断。盘物质最终是否落入视界、以多快速度落入，取决于轨道参数和磁场结构，不能用一条统一曲线概括。The boundary matters: the accretion disk lies outside the event horizon and is not part of the black hole; jets do not emerge from inside the horizon but are processed by the external disk and magnetic field. A disk in a binary may surround a neutron star or white dwarf, so a disk and X-rays alone cannot identify a black hole; its identity is judged from orbital mass, spectra and follow-up observations together. Whether and how fast disk matter crosses the horizon depends on orbital parameters and magnetic structure, and cannot be summarized by one universal curve.[1][8][10]
