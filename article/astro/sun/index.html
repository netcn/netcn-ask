01太阳为什么能一直发光发热，它的燃料会用完吗？Why does the Sun keep shining, and will its fuel ever run out?

太阳核心每秒把约 6 亿吨氢聚变成氦，约 400 万吨质量转化为能量，主序阶段还剩约 50 亿年。Every second the core fuses about 600 million tonnes of hydrogen into helium, turning some 4 million tonnes of mass into energy; about 5 billion years remain on the main sequence.

太阳靠核心的核聚变发光，而不是像煤那样化学燃烧。每秒钟约有 6 亿吨氢在核心聚变成氦，其中约 400 万吨质量按 E=mc² 转化为能量，以光和热的形式向外辐射。按目前的消耗速度，太阳已经燃烧约 46 亿年，主序阶段还能维持约 50 亿年，之后才会离开氢燃烧阶段。它的“燃料”是原子核里的质量，烧掉的是氢核，留下的灰烬主要是氦核。The Sun shines by nuclear fusion in its core, not by burning fuel the way coal burns in a fire. Every second, about 600 million tonnes of hydrogen fuse into helium, and roughly 4 million tonnes of that mass are converted into energy through E=mc², radiated outward as light and heat. At this rate the Sun has already burned for about 4.6 billion years and can stay on the hydrogen-burning stage for another roughly 5 billion years. Its fuel is nuclear mass: hydrogen nuclei are consumed, and the main ash is helium nuclei.[1][6][7]

聚变只在核心发生。太阳核心温度约 1500 万 K，压强极高，氢核也就是质子，靠量子隧穿克服彼此的静电斥力，走质子-质子链：两个质子先聚成氘，氘再加一个质子变成氦-3，两个氦-3 合成氦-4，同时放出伽马射线和中微子。OpenStax 教材给出，太阳几乎全部能量产生在核心周围约 15 万千米范围内，核心约占内部半径的两成。四个氢核的质量比一个氦核大约多出 0.7%，这部分质量亏损就是辐射能的来源。Fusion happens only in the core. The core temperature is about 15 million kelvin and the pressure is enormous, so hydrogen nuclei, or protons, tunnel through their mutual electric repulsion along the proton-proton chain. Two protons first make deuterium, deuterium adds another proton to make helium-3, and two helium-3 nuclei combine into helium-4, releasing gamma rays and neutrinos. OpenStax notes that nearly all the Sun's energy is generated within about 150,000 kilometres of the centre, with the core occupying about a fifth of the internal radius. Four hydrogen nuclei weigh about 0.7 percent more than one helium nucleus, and that mass deficit supplies the radiated energy.[3][6]

这些数字可以核对。太阳光度约 3.83×10²⁶ W，每秒把约 6 亿吨氢变成氦、约 400 万吨质量转化为能量。Bethe 1939 年在 Physical Review 上提出恒星产能的核反应链，后来因此获得诺贝尔物理学奖。地下实验室测到来自太阳的中微子，直接证实核心正在发生聚变；早期测到的中微子数量偏少，后来由中微子振荡解释，而不是太阳模型本身出错。The numbers can be checked. The solar luminosity is about 3.83×10²⁶ watts; each second about 600 million tonnes of hydrogen become helium and about 4 million tonnes of mass turn into energy. Bethe proposed the nuclear reaction chains that power stars in Physical Review in 1939, work that later earned him a Nobel Prize. Neutrinos detected in underground laboratories come from the solar core and confirm that fusion is underway. The early deficit of measured neutrinos was later explained by neutrino oscillation, not by a flawed solar model.[6][7]

为什么还能烧 50 亿年？太阳质量约 1.99×10³⁰ kg，虽然每秒损失约 400 万吨质量，但相对总质量微不足道；核心可用的氢只占太阳质量的一小部分，而且只有核心区域的高温高压能触发聚变。主序阶段总寿命约 100 亿年，太阳目前处于中年。核心氢耗尽后，它会先膨胀成红巨星，外层抛射成行星状星云，核心留下白矮星；那时地球是否会被吞没，不同模型给出的结果仍有不确定性。Why can it keep going for another 5 billion years? The Sun's mass is about 1.99×10³⁰ kilograms, so losing 4 million tonnes per second is negligible against that total. Only a fraction of the mass is usable core hydrogen, and only the hot, high-pressure core can trigger fusion. The main-sequence lifetime is about 10 billion years, so the Sun is middle-aged. After core hydrogen runs out it swells into a red giant, sheds its outer layers as a planetary nebula, and leaves a white dwarf; whether Earth would be engulfed remains uncertain across models.[3][6]

边界要写清：聚变不是在整个太阳里同时发生，只在核心；太阳也不会像煤一样烧完变黑，它的晚年是膨胀、抛层、留下致密残骸。把“太阳燃料快用完了”当成迫在眉睫的危机是误解，50 亿年的尺度远超人类文明史。太阳每秒损失的质量主要以辐射和太阳风形式离开，质量损失率随演化阶段变化，不能用化学反应的燃烧效率来类比。The boundary matters: fusion does not occur throughout the whole Sun at once, only in the core. The Sun will not simply burn down to a black lump; its old age means expansion, mass shedding and a dense remnant. Treating the fuel supply as an imminent crisis misreads the timescale, which stretches 5 billion years beyond all of recorded human history. Mass leaves mainly as radiation and the solar wind, the loss rate changes with evolutionary stage, and chemical combustion efficiency is the wrong analogy.[6]

02太阳离地球有多远？阳光飞到地球要多久？How far is the Sun from Earth, and how long does sunlight take to reach us?

日地平均距离约 1.496 亿千米（1 AU），光速下光行约 8.3 分钟，我们看到的是 8.3 分钟前的太阳。The average Earth-Sun distance is about 149.6 million km (1 AU); light takes about 8.3 minutes, so we see the Sun as it was 8.3 minutes ago.

太阳与地球的平均距离约 1.496 亿千米，这个距离被定义为一个天文单位（AU）。阳光以每秒约 299,792 千米的光速穿过这段距离，需要约 499 秒，也就是 8.3 分钟。所以你看到的太阳，是它 8.3 分钟之前的样子；如果太阳此刻突然消失，地球上要再过约 8.3 分钟才会暗下来，引力的变化也按同样的速度传播，不会瞬间传到地球。The average distance between the Sun and Earth is about 149.6 million kilometres, and this distance is defined as one astronomical unit, or AU. Sunlight travels at about 299,792 kilometres per second and takes about 499 seconds, or 8.3 minutes, to cross that gap. The Sun you see is what it looked like 8.3 minutes ago; if it vanished this instant, Earth would stay lit for another 8.3 minutes, and the change in gravity would arrive at the same speed rather than acting across space instantly.[1][2]

距离是怎么测出来的？历史上用金星凌日的几何三角测量给出早期数值，现代则通过雷达测距和航天器遥测把天文单位校准到千米级。太阳离我们很远，但它的角直径约半度，相当于伸直手臂看一颗黄豆的大小；地球直径约 1.27 万千米，太阳直径约 139 万千米，大约是地球的 109 倍。距离远近决定了到达地球的太阳辐射强度：平方反比律下，离太阳越远，单位面积收到的能量越低。The distance was not guessed. Early measurements used the geometry of Venus transits for a triangulation estimate, while modern radar ranging and spacecraft telemetry calibrate the astronomical unit to kilometre precision. The Sun is far away, yet its angular diameter is about half a degree, roughly the size of a bean held at arm's length. Earth is about 12,700 kilometres across, while the Sun's diameter is about 1.39 million kilometres, around 109 times Earth's diameter. Distance sets the radiation flux reaching us: under the inverse-square law, each doubling of distance quarters the energy per unit area.[2][3]

你在皮肤上感到的“太阳晒人”，本质是太阳辐射中可见光、红外波段的能量被身体吸收后转化为热。地球轨道上的太阳常数约 1361 W/m²，经过大气吸收和散射后，地面正午晴天的辐照度通常只有一千瓦每平方米上下。OpenStax 与 NASA 的太阳档案都给出日地距离、光度和辐射通量这组配套数据；阳光从核心产生后，先要在辐射层和对流层里反复碰撞、辗转数十万年才能到达光球，再用 8.3 分钟飞到地球。What you feel on your skin when the Sun beats down is absorbed energy from its visible and infrared radiation converted into heat. The solar constant above Earth's atmosphere is about 1361 W/m², and after atmospheric absorption and scattering a clear midday ground level is usually around one kilowatt per square metre. OpenStax and the NASA Sun facts sheet list the distance, luminosity and flux as a matched set. Light produced in the core first bounces through the radiative and convective zones, taking tens of thousands of years to reach the photosphere, and only then crosses the 8.3-minute journey to Earth.[2][3]

“如果太阳突然消失”是一个思想实验，用来区分光信号、引力信号和轨道运动的传播速度。按广义相对论，引力扰动以光速传播，地球不会立刻脱离轨道，而是在约 8.3 分钟后才沿切线方向飞出。真实情况下太阳不会瞬间消失，它的光度和轨道都在缓慢变化；这类假想实验只能帮助理解信号传播速度，不能当作真实预报。The thought experiment in which the Sun suddenly disappears separates the speeds of light signals, gravitational signals and orbital motion. In general relativity a gravitational disturbance travels at light speed, so Earth would not leave its orbit instantly; about 8.3 minutes later it would move off along a tangent. In reality the Sun cannot vanish in an instant, and its luminosity and orbit change only slowly. Such a scenario illustrates signal speed; it is not a real forecast.[1]

边界：8.3 分钟是平均距离对应的光行时间。地球轨道是椭圆，近日点距离约 1.471 亿千米，远日点约 1.521 亿千米，光行时间在约 8.1 到 8.5 分钟之间浮动。大气折射会改变光线方向，但不会把光行时间缩短到可以忽略。把“看到太阳现在的位置”当成实时对准，是常见误解：我们看到的方向也是 8.3 分钟前太阳的位置。The boundary: 8.3 minutes is the travel time at the average distance. Earth's orbit is elliptical, with a perihelion near 147.1 million kilometres and an aphelion near 152.1 million kilometres, so light travel time ranges from about 8.1 to 8.5 minutes. Atmospheric refraction bends the rays but does not shorten the travel time to anything negligible. Treating the Sun's apparent position as its present position is a common mistake: the direction you see is also where the Sun was 8.3 minutes ago.[2]

03太阳是个大火球吗？它有没有固体表面？Is the Sun a ball of fire, and does it have a solid surface?

太阳是电离等离子体，从核心、辐射层、对流层到光球、色球、日冕分层，没有硬边界。The Sun is ionized plasma layered into core, radiative zone, convective zone, photosphere, chromosphere and corona, with no hard boundary.

太阳不是一个可以站上去的大火球，也没有固体表面。它是一团由等离子体构成的恒星，从内到外依次是核心、辐射层、对流层、光球、色球和日冕；我们平时看到的明亮圆盘是光球，只是太阳大气最底层的一层，再往“表面”之外仍有稀薄的气体延伸。所谓太阳边缘，是气体密度降到肉眼和望远镜难以分辨的位置，而不是岩石或液体那样的硬边界。The Sun is not a fiery ball you could stand on, and it has no solid surface. It is a plasma star layered from the centre outward into core, radiative zone, convective zone, photosphere, chromosphere and corona. The bright disk we normally see is the photosphere, only the lowest layer of the solar atmosphere; beyond that edge thin gas still extends into space. The so-called limb is where gas density drops below what eyes and telescopes can distinguish, not a hard boundary like rock or liquid.[2][3]

内部三层分工明确。核心约占半径两成，温度约 1500 万 K，聚变在这里发生；辐射层从约两成半径延伸到约七成半径，能量靠光子与物质反复碰撞向外传递；对流层约 20 万千米深，能量靠热对流像烧开的水一样翻腾到表层。再往外是大气层：光球温度约 5770 K，是我们测量太阳光谱和视直径的层面；色球薄而暗红，平时被光球光芒盖住；日冕是最外层的稀薄高温大气，只有日全食时容易用肉眼看到。The three internal layers divide the work. The core occupies about a fifth of the radius, sits near 15 million kelvin, and hosts fusion. The radiative zone runs from about 20 to 70 percent of the radius, where energy travels through repeated collisions between photons and matter. The convective zone, about 200,000 kilometres deep, carries energy outward by boiling convection like heated water. Above these lie the atmosphere: the photosphere near 5770 K, where spectra and apparent diameter are measured; the thin, reddish chromosphere, hidden in normal glare; and the corona, the outermost tenuous hot atmosphere easiest to see during a total eclipse.[3]

成分上，太阳质量约 73% 是氢、25% 是氦，其余约两成质量份额之外的重元素只占约 2%。平均密度约 1.41 g/cm³，比水重，但核心密度可以达到每立方厘米上百克，外层却越来越稀薄。常被问起的“日冕为什么比光球热”仍是未完全解决的问题：光球约 5770 K，日冕却超过 100 万 K，主流假说把加热归因于磁场波和纳米耀斑释放的能量，具体份额仍在观测和模拟中检验。By composition about 73 percent of the Sun's mass is hydrogen and 25 percent helium, with heavy elements near 2 percent. The mean density is about 1.41 g/cm³, heavier than water, while the core reaches well over a hundred grams per cubic centimetre and the outer layers grow steadily thinner. A frequently asked puzzle is why the corona is hotter than the surface: the photosphere sits near 5770 K while the corona exceeds a million kelvin. Leading models attribute heating to magnetic waves and nanoflare energy release, and the exact share is still being tested by observations and simulations.[2][3]

与地球对照，太阳直径约 139 万千米，是地球的 109 倍；体积可以装下约 130 万个地球；质量约 1.99×10³⁰ kg，占太阳系总质量的约 99.86%。它没有固态地壳，表层的米粒组织是对流单元在光球上的投影，尺度约上千千米，不断生灭。望远镜看到的黑子、耀斑都发生在光球和低层大气，日冕物质抛射则把气体和磁场送进行行星际空间。Compared with Earth, the Sun's diameter is about 1.39 million kilometres, 109 times Earth's; its volume would hold about 1.3 million Earths, and its mass near 1.99×10³⁰ kg accounts for roughly 99.86 percent of the Solar System. There is no solid crust. Granulation visible on the photosphere is the projection of convective cells, each about a thousand kilometres across and continually forming and dissolving. Sunspots and flares occur in the photosphere and lower atmosphere, while coronal mass ejections push gas and magnetic fields out into interplanetary space.[2][3]

边界：把太阳描述成“气态球”并不准确，高温下物质是电离的等离子体，既不是普通气体也不是液体。结构分层是按能量传输方式和观测高度划分的，层与层之间没有截然的界面。日冕高温是观测事实，但加热机制属于尚未定论的前沿；把任何一种单一假说当成标准答案，都会超出证据能支持的范围。The boundary: calling the Sun a gas ball is imprecise, because at these temperatures the matter is ionized plasma, neither ordinary gas nor liquid. The layers are defined by how energy travels and by observing height, with no sharp interface between them. The coronal temperature is an observed fact, but its heating mechanism remains an open frontier; treating any single hypothesis as settled would go beyond what the evidence supports.[3][4]

04太阳黑子为什么不黑？驱动太阳磁周期的原因是什么？Why are sunspots not truly black, and what drives the solar magnetic cycle?

黑子是强磁场抑制对流形成的低温区，比光球暗但本身仍很亮；较差自转与对流缠绕磁场，形成约 11 年活动周期。Spots are cool zones where strong fields suppress convection; dimmer than the photosphere but still bright, they follow an 11-year magnetic cycle.

太阳黑子看起来黑，并不是真的黑，也不是太阳上破了洞。它们是光球上温度较低、辐射较弱的区域：周围光球约 5770 K，黑子本影通常只有 3000 到 4500 K，对比之下显得暗。如果把单个黑子从太阳背景里挪出来，它本身会比满月还亮；只是和旁边灼热的光球放在一起，亮度差让它看上去发黑。黑子由强磁场穿过光球、抑制对流形成，磁场把本该从内部升上来的热量挡在表层之下。Sunspots look black, but they are not truly black and they are not holes in the Sun. They are cooler, weaker-radiating patches on the photosphere: the surrounding photosphere is near 5770 K, while the dark umbra of a spot runs roughly 3000 to 4500 K, so it appears dark by contrast. Taken out of the solar background, a single spot would glow brighter than the full Moon; next to the hot photosphere the brightness difference makes it look black. Strong magnetic fields pierce the photosphere and suppress convection, holding heat that should rise from below back down under the surface layer.[3][8]

磁场是理解黑子的钥匙。黑子常成对出现，分别对应磁力线穿出和穿回光球的位置，两个黑子的磁极相反。强磁场改变了等离子体的运动和能量传输，让局部表面降温；黑子越大、磁场越强，所在的活动区越容易伴随耀斑和日冕物质抛射。NASA 的太阳观测把黑子定义为活动区可见的成分，复杂磁场是太阳爆发的来源。Magnetic fields are the key to spots. Sunspots often appear in pairs, marking where magnetic loops emerge through and return into the photosphere, with the two spots carrying opposite magnetic polarity. The strong field changes plasma motion and energy transport, cooling the local surface. Larger spots with stronger fields sit in active regions that more often produce flares and coronal mass ejections. NASA's solar observations describe spots as the visible component of active regions, whose complex magnetic fields drive solar eruptions.[4][8]

黑子数量并非恒定，而是沿约 11 年的太阳活动周期增减：活动极小期黑子稀少，活动极大期成群出现，纬度分布还会随周期推移形成蝴蝶图。磁场极性每隔一个周期反转一次，完整的磁场轮回约 22 年，叫海尔周期。驱动这台发电机的是太阳较差自转与内部对流：赤道转得比极区快，把磁场线缠绕、拉伸、放大，再上浮到表面形成活动区。具体的发电机方程和表面观测之间仍有细节需要模型补足。The number of spots is not constant: it rises and falls on the roughly 11-year solar cycle. Near solar minimum spots are scarce; near maximum they appear in groups, and their latitudinal drift traces the butterfly diagram. Magnetic polarity flips every other cycle, so the full magnetic round trip takes about 22 years, known as the Hale cycle. The driver is the solar dynamo, built from differential rotation and internal convection: the equator spins faster than the poles, winding, stretching and amplifying magnetic field lines before they rise to the surface as active regions. Details between dynamo equations and surface observations still need model work.[4][8]

黑子与空间天气直接相关。活动区集中的时段，耀斑和日冕物质抛射更频繁，高能粒子和带电云团可能干扰卫星通信、导航信号和地面电网。历史上蒙德极小期即 17 世纪一段长期黑子稀少的时段，与北半球一段偏冷气候相吻合，但太阳辐照变化与气候之间的定量关系仍在研究。日常看到的“太阳上有黑点”，多数就是可见光波段记录到的黑子群。Spots connect directly to space weather. During active periods flares and coronal mass ejections are more frequent, and energetic particles and charged clouds can disrupt satellite communications, navigation signals and ground power grids. The Maunder Minimum, a long stretch of few sunspots in the seventeenth century, coincided with a cool spell in the Northern Hemisphere, though the quantitative link between solar irradiance and climate is still studied. The dark dots people see on the Sun in white-light images are mostly recorded spot groups.[4][8]

边界：黑子低温是相对光球而言，不是低温天体；黑子数量的 11 年周期是统计平均，每个周期的强度和持续时间都有起伏，不能预测成固定时刻表。磁周期由较差自转和对流驱动，但太阳内部的自转剖面依靠日震学反演，存在观测不确定性。把黑子当成太阳熄灭的预兆，或把黑子直接等同于地球天气，都是证据不足的说法。The boundary: spot coolness is relative to the photosphere, not a cold body. The 11-year cycle is a statistical average; each cycle varies in strength and duration and cannot be scheduled like a timetable. The magnetic cycle is driven by differential rotation and convection, but the internal rotation profile is inferred from helioseismology and carries observational uncertainty. Treating spots as omens of the Sun dying, or equating them directly with Earth's weather, claims more than the evidence supports.[3][4]
