01科学方法是什么？一个假说要满足什么条件才进入科学检验？What is the scientific method, and what makes a hypothesis eligible for scientific testing?

观察—假说—预测—检验—修正的循环；假说必须可检验、可证伪，结果可被独立重复。An observation-hypothesis-prediction-test-revision loop; a hypothesis must be testable and falsifiable, and results independently reproducible.

科学方法是一套把观察变成可检验解释的流程：先记录现象，提出可以被验证或推翻的假说，从假说推出明确预测，再用实验或观测去检验，最后根据结果支持、修正或放弃假说。它不保证一次得到真理，而是让结论可以被反复复查和纠错；生物学、心理学和物理学的具体做法不同，都遵循同一套逻辑骨架。The scientific method is a routine that turns observations into testable explanations: record a phenomenon, propose a hypothesis that can be verified or overturned, derive a clear prediction, test it by experiment or observation, then support, revise or discard the hypothesis based on results. It does not guarantee truth on the first pass; it makes conclusions open to repeated review and correction. Biology, psychology and physics use different concrete procedures, but share the same logical skeleton.[1][3]

流程可以拆成五步。观察阶段记录谁、在什么条件下、看到了什么，避免把印象当成事实；假说阶段给出试探性解释，必须包含可检验的预测，比如改变某变量后结果应如何变化；检验阶段设计对照，控制无关变量，重复测量；分析阶段用统计判断结果是否超出随机波动；修正阶段回到假说，要么加强证据，要么更换解释。The flow splits into five steps. Observation records who saw what under which conditions, so impressions are not treated as facts. Hypothesis offers a tentative explanation with testable predictions, such as how a result should change when a variable moves. Test designs a control, holds unrelated variables fixed and repeats measurements. Analysis uses statistics to judge whether results exceed random fluctuation. Revision returns to the hypothesis: strengthen the evidence or replace the explanation.[1][2]

可证伪是科学假说的门槛。波普尔提出，一个命题如果原则上找不到可能推翻它的观测结果，就不属于科学检验范围；占星式解释无论出现什么证据都能自圆其说，因此不可证伪。可证伪不等于已经被证伪，而是承认存在一组反例可以让假说失效。OpenStax 的生物章节把假说定义为可检验的、解释有限范围现象的推测，理论则在假说之上整合更广的证据。Falsifiability is the entry ticket for a scientific hypothesis. Popper argued that if no possible observation could disprove a proposition, it lies outside scientific testing; an astrological account that absorbs any evidence without changing is unfalsifiable. Falsifiable does not mean already falsified; it means a defined set of counterexamples could invalidate the claim. The OpenStax biology chapter defines a hypothesis as a testable conjecture explaining a limited range of phenomena, while a theory integrates wider evidence above the hypothesis level.[1][3]

实际研究中流程常常循环往复。实验受样本量、仪器精度和混杂因素限制，结果只能在当前证据下被支持；同行重复同一实验是检验结论是否稳健的关键一步。不同学科工具不同：自然科学多用受控实验，历史与天文类学科多用观测对比，但判断标准一致——预测能否被独立复核，证据和结论是否分开陈述。In practice the cycle loops back on itself. Experiments are limited by sample size, instrument precision and confounding factors, so results are only supported under current evidence; independent replication is the key check on robustness. Tools differ by field: controlled experiments dominate natural science, while history and astronomy rely on observational comparison, but the standard is the same: whether predictions are independently reproducible and whether evidence and conclusions are stated separately.[1][2]

边界：科学方法不回答价值判断、审美和信仰问题；它给出的是当前证据下最稳妥的解释，而不是永恒定论。一个假说被多次检验支持，也仍可能在新证据下被修正；统计显著不等于因果成立，相关关系需要机制解释。方法流程本身可以被讨论和改进，但可检验、可复核、承认错误这几条核心要求不变。The boundary: the scientific method does not answer value judgments, aesthetics or belief questions; it gives the most reliable explanation under current evidence, not a final truth. A hypothesis supported by many tests may still be revised under new evidence, and statistical significance does not establish causation; correlation needs a mechanism. The workflow itself can be debated and improved, but the core requirements of testability, reproducibility and admitting error stay in place.[2][3]

02科学里的事实、假说、理论和定律有什么区别？为什么“只是个理论”是误解？How do fact, hypothesis, theory and law differ in science, and why is just a theory a misunderstanding?

按证据积累分层：事实是反复确认的观察，理论是整合大量证据的成体系解释，不等于猜测。Layers by evidence: a fact is a confirmed observation, a theory is a systematic explanation over wide evidence, not a guess.

科学话语里的事实、假说、理论和定律是不同层级的概念，不能按日常用词互换。事实是经过反复确认的观察结果，例如水在标准大气压下 100°C 沸腾；假说是对有限现象的试探性解释；理论是整合了大量事实、定律和已检验假说的成体系解释；定律通常描述在特定条件下重复出现的定量关系。日常说“这只是个理论”，把理论当成猜测，和科学含义正好相反。Fact, hypothesis, theory and law are distinct levels in scientific language and cannot be swapped by everyday usage. A fact is an observation confirmed repeatedly, such as water boiling at 100°C at standard pressure. A hypothesis is a tentative explanation for a limited phenomenon. A theory is a systematic explanation integrating many facts, laws and tested hypotheses. A law usually describes a repeated quantitative relation under stated conditions. Saying in daily speech that something is just a theory treats theory as a guess, the opposite of the scientific meaning.[1][2]

层级之间是证据积累关系，不是尊卑关系。假说提出后通过检验逐步获得支持，多个相关假说被整合进同一框架，才上升为理论；理论并不因为名称里带“理论”就低于事实。进化论、原子论、细胞论都是理论，它们解释的范围极广、预测被反复验证；被推翻的不是理论本身，而是理论中与新证据冲突的具体部分。The levels relate by evidence accumulation, not rank. A hypothesis gains support through testing, and several related hypotheses merge into one framework before becoming a theory. A theory is not lower than a fact because it carries the name theory. Evolution, atomic theory and cell theory are theories: their scope is broad and their predictions are repeatedly verified. What is overturned is the part conflicting with new evidence, not the whole theory.[2][5]

OpenStax 心理学章节把这一分层用于研究实践：研究者先提出假说，设计操作化定义，测量变量，再判断数据是否支持假说；事实层面记录测量值，理论层面解释机制。同一个现象在不同证据阶段可以被陈述为事实、假说或理论，混淆层级会导致争论错位——把未检验的假说当事实传播，或把成熟理论贬低为猜想，都是常见的话语错误。The OpenStax psychology chapter applies this layering to research practice: a researcher states a hypothesis, defines operational variables, measures them, then judges whether data support it. The fact level records measured values; the theory level explains mechanism. The same phenomenon can be stated as fact, hypothesis or theory at different evidence stages, and mixing levels causes mismatched arguments: passing an untested hypothesis off as fact, or dismissing a mature theory as a guess, are both common wording errors.[2][5]

证据如何进入这个分层有明确路径。可重复的观测和实验结果成为事实素材；多个独立团队得到一致结果后，相关解释才进入理论框架；定量关系被反复验证后写成定律。模型和类比是帮助推理的工具，其适用范围有限，不能直接当成事实陈述。科学陈述通常标注证据强度和适用条件，而不是用绝对化措辞打包。Evidence enters this layering along a defined path. Repeated observations and experimental results become factual material; after independent teams agree, the related explanations enter a theoretical framework; quantitative relations verified repeatedly are written as laws. Models and analogies are reasoning aids with limited scope and cannot be stated as facts directly. Scientific statements usually mark evidence strength and conditions of applicability rather than wrapping claims in absolute wording.[1][5]

边界：科学中没有“最终答案”的位置标签。一个理论可以被新理论扩展或替代，例如牛顿力学在高速强引力条件下被相对论修正，但牛顿理论在其适用范围内仍然可靠；事实也可能因测量手段改进而被细化。网上流传的“事实”常常只是个人主张或未经复核的说法，科学意义上的事实需要可重复的观测依据；层级标签本身不保证结论永远正确。The boundary: science has no final-answer label. A theory can be extended or replaced by a newer one: Newtonian mechanics is corrected by relativity at high speed and strong gravity, yet remains reliable in its range. Facts can also be refined as measurement improves. A so-called fact circulating online is often a personal claim or unchecked statement; a scientific fact needs repeatable observational support. The level label itself does not guarantee the conclusion stays correct forever.[1][2]

03同行评议怎么把关论文？科学共识是怎么形成的，边界在哪里？How does peer review gate papers, how does scientific consensus form, and where are its limits?

编辑与独立专家评议方法和证据，不为结论背书；共识来自重复与证据积累，可随新证据改变。Editors and independent experts review methods and evidence without endorsing conclusions; consensus grows from replication and evidence, and can change.

同行评议是科研论文发表前的质量把关流程：作者投稿，编辑先初审是否符合范围和基本规范，再把稿件交给独立于研究团队的专家，专家审查方法、数据、结论和文献，提出支持、修改后发表或拒稿意见。它评估的是研究是否达到该领域的证据标准，而不是替结论背书；通过评议不等于论文正确，未通过也不等于观点错误。Peer review is the quality gate before a research paper is published: the author submits, an editor first screens scope and basic standards, then sends the manuscript to experts independent of the team. Reviewers examine methods, data, conclusions and literature, and advise accept, revise or reject. It judges whether the study meets the field's evidence standard; it does not endorse the conclusion. Passing review does not mean the paper is correct, and failing it does not mean the viewpoint is wrong.[4][5]

评议机制分不同形式。单盲评议隐藏作者身份或隐藏评议人身份，双盲两者都隐藏；公开评议在小部分期刊开放过程记录。编辑可能邀请两到三位评审，意见回到作者手中做返修，作者逐条回应质疑。自然期刊政策说明，评议人责任是指出方法缺陷、统计问题和证据缺口，最终判断由编辑综合；学术出版中还存在评议疲劳、小圈子偏袒等已知局限，需要透明流程和后续撤稿机制纠正。Review takes different forms. Single-blind hides either author or reviewer identity, double-blind hides both, and open review shares the process record at a small number of journals. An editor may invite two or three reviewers; comments return to the author for revision, with point-by-point responses. Nature's journal policy states reviewers flag method flaws, statistics problems and evidence gaps, while editors make the final call. Known limits remain: review fatigue, in-group favoritism, corrected by transparent process and later retraction.[4][5]

科学共识不是投票结果，而是证据积累形成的集体判断。独立团队重复实验、综述汇总证据、后续研究逐步收敛后，多数研究者接受同一解释；共识可以随新证据改变，板块构造从大陆漂移假说到全球接受用了几十年。共识不等于绝对真理，但它是当前证据下最稳妥的工作假设；争议往往出现在证据不足或新范式尚未完成整合的阶段。Scientific consensus is not a vote but a collective judgment formed by evidence accumulation. As independent teams repeat experiments, reviews summarize evidence and follow-up research converges, most researchers accept one explanation; consensus can change with new evidence. Plate tectonics took decades from continental drift hypothesis to global acceptance. Consensus is not absolute truth, but the most reliable working assumption under current evidence; disputes usually sit where evidence is thin or a new paradigm has not finished integrating.[4][5]

以物理定律统一为例：标准模型已经把电磁力、弱力和强力纳入同一框架，引力尚未与之统一；量子引力、大统一理论目前仍是活跃的研究假说，而不是成熟理论。这类前沿问题上，同行评议把关的是论证和数学推演是否自洽，实验检验还需要未来观测。研究人员会明确区分已被验证的部分和待检验的推测，公众讨论中则容易把假说写成定论。Physics unification is an example. The Standard Model already brings electromagnetism, the weak force and the strong force into one framework, while gravity has not joined them. Quantum gravity and grand unification remain active research hypotheses, not mature theories. On such frontiers peer review checks whether argument and mathematics are self-consistent; experimental tests await future observations. Researchers separate verified parts from untested speculation, while public discussion often writes hypotheses into settled claims.[5][6]

边界：同行评议不能发现所有造假和错误，重复发表和后续撤稿说明把关存在漏网；共识形成需要时间，新兴领域短期可能被主流忽视。评议制度本身也在随开放科学、预印本和数据公开而调整。科学不要求个人放弃判断，而是要求主张接受公开证据和独立复核；不能被检验、不能被引用追溯的说法，即使包装成研究结论，也不进入科学共识。The boundary: peer review cannot catch every fraud or error, and repeated publication plus later retraction shows slips get through. Consensus takes time, and young fields may be ignored by the mainstream for a while. Review itself keeps changing with open science, preprints and data sharing. Science does not ask individuals to drop judgment; it requires claims to face public evidence and independent review. A statement that cannot be tested or traced to a cited record, even packaged as research, does not enter scientific consensus.[4][5]

04科学和伪科学到底怎么区分？为什么有些说法看起来有道理，却不属于科学？How are science and pseudoscience distinguished, and why do plausible-looking claims fail to qualify as science?

可检验性要求可推翻的预测；证据要看效应量与对照设计；独立重复和预注册保证稳健性，不可检验的说法不进入科学共识。Testability demands falsifiable predictions; evidence needs controls and effect sizes; replication and preregistration secure robustness, and untestable claims stay out of consensus.

区分科学与伪科学，第一道判据是可检验性：主张必须推出可与观测对照的明确预测，原则上存在可推翻它的证据。波普尔的证伪标准指出，找不到冲突观察结果的命题不属于科学检验范围；什么结果都能自圆其说的解释不可证伪。可检验不等于已被证伪，而是承认存在一组反例可让主张失效。可检验性还要操作化：变量怎么测、在什么条件下观察、预期结果是什么，都须说清楚，否则无法重复核查。The first criterion separating science from pseudoscience is testability: a claim must yield clear predictions matched to observation or experiment, and evidence that could in principle overturn it must exist. Popper's falsification standard holds that a proposition with no possible conflicting observation lies outside scientific testing; an account absorbing any outcome is unfalsifiable. Falsifiable does not mean already falsified; it means a defined set of counterexamples could invalidate the claim. Testability also needs operational definitions: how the variable is measured, under what conditions, and what the expected result looks like, stated clearly enough for independent review.[7]

第二道判据是证据强度与效应量。科学陈述不只看方向是否显著，还要看效应多大、置信区间多宽、样本是否充分；统计显著不等于实际有意义，很小的效应在大样本下也能通过检验。设计需要对照组、控制混杂、明确纳入排除标准，并报告效应量而非只报成败。效应量过小、依赖特殊筛选条件、在样本间忽高忽低的主张，即使有个别正向案例，也达不到证据门槛；个案不能替代对照研究。The second criterion is evidence strength and effect size. A scientific statement asks not only whether the direction is significant, but how large the effect is, how wide the confidence interval, and whether the sample is adequate; significance does not equal practical importance, and a tiny effect can pass tests in a large sample. Design needs a control group, confounder control, clear inclusion and exclusion criteria, and reported effect sizes rather than success or failure alone. Small effects, narrow screening conditions, or effects swinging across samples fail the evidence threshold even with a few positive cases; an anecdote cannot replace a controlled study.[7][8]

第三道判据是可重复性与预注册。独立团队按公开方法、数据和流程重复实验，方向量级一致才算稳健；2016 年《自然》对约 1500 名研究者的调查显示，多数认为若干领域可重复性下降，心理学、医学随后开展大规模重复研究。预注册在收数前公开假说、样本量和分析方案，减少事后挑结果、调假设；数据代码可追溯，是复核前提。重复失败不自动证明造假，但要求重审方法、样本和统计处理。The third criterion is reproducibility and preregistration. When independent teams repeat under public methods, data and workflow and obtain consistent direction and magnitude, the result counts as robust. A 2016 Nature survey of about 1,500 researchers found most believed reproducibility had weakened in several fields, and psychology and medicine later ran large replication studies. Preregistration publishes hypothesis, sample size and analysis plan before data collection, leaving less room for cherry-picking or post-hoc adjustment; traceable data, code and workflow are prerequisites for review. Replication failure does not prove fraud automatically, but calls for re-examining methods, sampling and statistics.[7][8]

历史案例可作对照。1989 年 Fleischmann 与 Pons 宣布室温冷聚变超额放热，多个团队重复未果，多数未观测到相应放热，后续指出方法与测量缺陷；年末主流科学界已不再把该主张当作已确立现象。顺势疗法主张反复稀释振荡后留下物质记忆，常用稀释超阿伏伽德罗分子极限，制剂通常已不含原始分子；对照试验未见区别于安慰剂的可靠疗效。这些案例属证据更新，不是对从业者群体的道德评价。Historical cases provide an objective comparison. In 1989 Fleischmann and Pons announced excess heat from room-temperature cold fusion; other teams tried to repeat it, most did not observe corresponding excess heat, and follow-up identified methodological flaws and measurement problems. By late 1989 the mainstream community no longer treated the claim as established. Homeopathy claims repeated dilution and succussion leave a substance memory; common dilutions far exceed the Avogadro molecular limit, so preparations usually contain no original molecule, and trials find no reliable effect distinct from placebo at active doses. These are evidence-updating episodes, not moral judgments of practitioners.[9]

伪科学容易泛滥：给出不可证伪的解释，反例归因于条件不对；选择性展示成功案例，忽略失败与重复失败；借用科学术语和机构外观，却不接受公开检验与独立重复；情绪诉求强于证据。辨识时依次问：预测是什么、怎么测量、谁独立重复过、效应量多大、是否预注册、反例如何处理。科学不要求放弃判断，而要求主张接受公开证据与独立复核；不可检验、无可引用记录的说法，包装成研究结论也不进入共识。Pseudoscience spreads through several mechanisms. It offers unfalsifiable explanations, so counterexamples are blamed on wrong conditions; it selectively shows successes while ignoring failures and failed replications; it borrows scientific terminology and appearance without public testing or independent repetition; and emotional appeals to health, security and belonging outweigh evidence. To sort a claim, ask: what prediction, how measured, who replicated it, how large the effect, was the plan preregistered, how are counterexamples handled. Science requires claims to face public evidence and independent review; an untestable statement with no citable record, even packaged as research, does not enter consensus.[7][8]
