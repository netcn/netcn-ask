01Wi-Fi 是什么？它怎样把设备接入互联网，信号为什么传不远？What is Wi-Fi, how does it connect devices to the internet, and why does signal range stay limited?

Wi-Fi 经 802.11 无线接入路由器，互联网是 IP 包交换网络；信号随距离衰减并被墙体吸收反射。Wi-Fi joins a router over 802.11 radio; the internet is an IP packet-switched network, and signal attenuates with distance and through walls.

Wi-Fi 是设备通过无线电波接入局域网的标准族，对应 IEEE 802.11 系列规范。手机、笔记本等无线终端与接入点（路由器）交换射频信号，接入点再通过网线连到本地局域网，由家用路由器分配内网地址，最后经运营商线路接入互联网。Wi-Fi 本身只解决最后一段无线连接，不是互联网本身。Wi-Fi is the standards family that joins devices to a local area network by radio, corresponding to the IEEE 802.11 specification series. Phones and laptops exchange radio signals with an access point, the router links to the wired local network, the home router assigns private addresses, and the line through the service provider reaches the internet. Wi-Fi solves only this last wireless segment; it is not the internet itself.[1][2]

互联网是一张由网络互联而成的分布式包交换网络。数据被切成带地址的数据包，按 IP 地址逐跳路由，不同包可以走不同路径，到达后再由 TCP 按顺序重组为可靠流。RFC 791 定义网际协议，RFC 793 定义传输控制协议；IP 负责寻址和转发，TCP 负责连接、序号、确认和重传。The internet is a distributed packet-switched network of networks. Data are split into addressed packets, routed hop by hop by IP address, different packets may follow different paths, and TCP reassembles them into a reliable stream after arrival. RFC 791 defines the Internet Protocol and RFC 793 the Transmission Control Protocol: IP handles addressing and forwarding, TCP handles connection, sequence numbers, acknowledgment and retransmission.[2][3]

Wi-Fi 信号为什么传不远，有物理原因。2.4 GHz 和 5 GHz 频段的无线电波随距离衰减，墙壁、家具和人体会吸收或反射信号；频率越高绕射能力越差，5 GHz 穿墙弱于 2.4 GHz，但带宽更高。接入点发射功率和天线增益有限，覆盖范围通常几十米，室内多墙体环境需要多个接入点补盲。Why Wi-Fi signals do not travel far has physical reasons. Radio waves in the 2.4 GHz and 5 GHz bands attenuate with distance, and walls, furniture and bodies absorb or reflect them; higher frequency bends less, so 5 GHz passes walls worse than 2.4 GHz but carries higher bandwidth. Access-point transmit power and antenna gain are limited, indoor range usually runs tens of meters, and wall-heavy rooms need several access points to fill gaps.[1][2]

实际组网围绕这些机制展开。路由器把无线接入、局域网交换和拨号上网合并在一台设备里；运营商提供公网 IP，家用设备经地址转换共享出口。信号弱时调整接入点位置、减少遮挡、增加 Mesh 节点比单纯换高功率设备更有效；频段选择在覆盖和速率之间权衡，2.4 GHz 覆盖远、干扰多，5 GHz 速率高、距离短。Real networks build on these mechanisms. A home router combines wireless access, LAN switching and dial-up uplink in one box; the provider supplies a public IP, and home devices share the exit through address translation. When signal is weak, moving the access point, cutting obstruction or adding mesh nodes helps more than simply buying a high-power device. Band choice trades range for speed: 2.4 GHz reaches farther with more interference, 5 GHz is faster but shorter.[1][3]

边界：Wi-Fi 覆盖范围受建筑物和环境影响，标称距离不能直接当成室内可达距离；无线信号可被监听，家用网络需要加密和强口令。互联网没有单一中心控制，路由策略由各网络自治域协商，端到端连通不保证所有内容可达。IP 地址会动态分配，TCP 的可靠传输建立在 IP 不可靠投递之上；网络故障要分段排查无线、路由和运营商链路。The boundary: Wi-Fi range depends on buildings and environment, so a rated distance cannot be treated as guaranteed indoor reach; wireless can be eavesdropped, so home networks need encryption and strong passwords. The internet has no single central control, routing negotiated among autonomous networks, and end-to-end connectivity does not guarantee every content reaches every user. IP addresses are often assigned dynamically, and TCP's reliable stream sits on top of IP's unreliable delivery; network faults are checked segment by segment across wireless, router and provider link.[2][3]

02二维码是怎么工作的？缺角或被遮挡为什么仍能扫描？How does a QR code work, and why can it still scan after a corner is torn or covered?

数据编入矩阵模块，Reed-Solomon 校验码修复局部错误；L/M/Q/H 可恢复约 7/15/25/30% 面积。Data are encoded in matrix modules, Reed-Solomon parity repairs local errors; L/M/Q/H restore about 7/15/25/30 percent of area.

二维码是把数据按矩阵模块编码的图形标准，三个角上的定位图案帮助扫码器快速对准和旋转校正。扫码器先识别定位图案确定模块网格和旋转角度，再读取格式信息选定掩码和纠错等级。版本 1 为 21×21 模块，随版本增大到 177×177；数据模块之外加入纠错码，即使局部缺损、遮挡或反光，扫码器仍能恢复原始数据。A QR code is a matrix-module graphic standard for encoding data; finder patterns at three corners help scanners align and correct rotation. Version 1 has 21 by 21 modules, growing to 177 by 177 at higher versions. Beyond data modules, error-correction codewords are written in, so scanners can recover the original data even where part is damaged, blocked or reflecting glare.[5][8]

纠错用的是 Reed-Solomon 编码：把数据看作有限域上的多项式，额外计算一组校验码字一并写入图形；读取时根据校验码定位并修复出错的码字。Reed 与 Solomon 1960 年的论文给出这一类多项式码的理论基础。二维码纠错分 L、M、Q、H 四级，分别可恢复约 7%、15%、25%、30% 的码字面积；级别越高冗余越多，能存的有效数据越少。The correction uses Reed-Solomon coding: data are treated as a polynomial over a finite field, and an extra set of parity codewords is computed and written into the graphic; on reading, parity codewords locate and repair erroneous codewords. The 1960 paper by Reed and Solomon laid the theory for this class of polynomial codes. QR correction has four levels, L, M, Q and H, restoring about 7, 15, 25 and 30 percent of codeword area; higher levels add more redundancy and hold less effective data.[4][5]

缺损可恢复的前提是错误分布在纠错能力内。二维码被撕掉一角、沾污或被标志遮挡时，只要受损面积不超过所选等级上限，校验码就能重建数据；超过上限则部分模块无法判读，图形需要重印。定位图案、时序序列和格式信息保证扫码器先确定坐标，再按模块网格解码。版本越高模块越多，能容纳的数据量越大，但对打印尺寸和清晰度的要求也越高。Recoverable damage stays within correction capacity. When a corner is torn, stained or covered by a logo, parity codewords rebuild the data as long as the damaged area does not exceed the level's limit; past that, some modules cannot be read and the code must be reprinted. Finder patterns, timing sequences and format information let the scanner fix coordinates before decoding the module grid.[5][8]

纠错码不只用于二维码。深空通信链路损耗大、误码率高，冗余编码是数据能传回地面的前提之一；光盘、磁盘和条形码也用类似编码容忍局部介质缺陷。思路一致：发送端按规则加入校验位，接收端用校验位定位错误并修复。编码效率和纠错能力需要权衡，冗余越高抗损越强，单位载荷越低。Error-correcting codes appear beyond QR codes. Deep-space links, optical discs, magnetic storage and bar codes use similar redundancy to fight transmission and storage errors. The idea is the same: the sender adds parity bits by rule, and the receiver locates and repairs errors from them. Coding efficiency and correction ability trade off: more redundancy resists damage better but lowers payload per symbol.[4]

边界：纠错能力有上限，不能把二维码当成任意破损仍可扫描的图形；纠错等级是编码时选定的，事后无法提高。扫码成功还依赖图像清晰度、光照和对准角度，纠错只解决局部缺损，不解决模糊和畸变。标准图形本身不加密，里面的内容可读可改，敏感信息不能只靠二维码保护。The boundary: correction has a ceiling, so a QR code is not scannable after any amount of damage. The correction level is chosen at encoding time and cannot be raised afterward. A successful scan also needs sharp image, lighting and alignment; correction handles local defects, not blur or distortion. The graphic itself is not encrypted, its content readable and editable, so sensitive information cannot rely on a QR code alone.[5][8]

03公钥和私钥有什么区别？数字签名怎么验证，为什么私钥不能公开？How do public and private keys differ, how are digital signatures verified, and why must the private key stay secret?

私钥签名解密并保密，公钥验证加密并公开；地址是公钥哈希，泄露私钥即可被冒充。The private key signs and decrypts in secret; the public key verifies and encrypts openly; an address hashes the public key, and leaking the key enables impersonation.

公钥密码用一对数学上相关的密钥替代共享口令：公钥可以公开，私钥必须保密。与把秘密写在一张共享纸条上不同，公钥体系不需要双方事先见面交换密钥。他人用公钥加密的数据只有对应私钥能解开；反过来，持有者用私钥生成签名，任何人都能用公钥验证签名来自该私钥。RFC 8032 定义的 EdDSA 就是一类椭圆曲线数字签名算法。Public-key cryptography replaces a shared password with a mathematically linked keypair: the public key may be published, the private key must stay secret. Data encrypted by others with the public key can be decrypted only by the matching private key; conversely, the holder signs with the private key, and anyone can verify with the public key that the signature came from that key. RFC 8032 defines EdDSA, one family of elliptic-curve digital signature algorithms.[6][7]

签名和验证是两个方向。私钥持有者对消息哈希做签名，验证方用公钥检查签名是否与消息和公钥匹配；这证明消息确实由对应私钥签署且未被篡改，但不公开私钥。比特币白皮书采用同一思路：公钥经哈希得到地址，交易由私钥签名，网络用公钥验证，地址本身不暴露私钥。Signing and verification run in opposite directions. The private-key holder signs a message hash; the verifier uses the public key to check whether the signature matches the message and key. This proves the message was signed by the matching private key and was not tampered with, without exposing the private key. The Bitcoin whitepaper uses the same idea: the public key is hashed into an address, transactions are signed by the private key, the network verifies with the public key, and the address does not reveal the private key.[6][7]

与对称加密对照，差别在密钥分配。对称加密双方共享同一把密钥，加密解密都用它，难点是如何安全交换密钥；公钥体系不需要事先共享秘密，公钥可以公开分发，私钥从不离开持有者。混合加密的典型流程是：一方生成随机会话密钥，用对方公钥加密会话密钥发出，双方再用会话密钥做对称加密；这样既避开公钥算法的速度短板，又解决密钥分发问题。Compared with symmetric encryption, the difference is key distribution. Symmetric cryptography shares one key between both sides, used for encryption and decryption, and the hard part is exchanging it safely. A public-key system needs no prior shared secret: the public key can be distributed openly while the private key never leaves the holder. Real systems mix the two: public keys negotiate a session key, and later data are encrypted fast with a symmetric cipher.[3][6]

私钥的安全边界很明确。私钥等同于身份凭证：泄露后他人可以冒充持有者签名或解密；丢失私钥则无法再证明所有权，没有第三方可以重置。因此私钥应离线保存或存入硬件安全模块，备份要防丢失也防泄露；助记词、硬件密钥和多签方案都是围绕私钥保管设计的，任何索要私钥或助记词的第三方都应视为风险信号。公钥可以公开派生，地址只是公钥的哈希形式，公开地址不会危及私钥。The private-key boundary is clear. A private key equals an identity credential: if it leaks, others can sign or decrypt as the holder; if it is lost, ownership can no longer be proved, and no third party can reset it. Private keys should be kept offline or in a hardware security module, with backups guarded against both loss and exposure. The public key can be derived openly, and an address is only a hash of the public key; publishing the address does not endanger the private key.[6][7]

边界：公钥密码依赖数学难题的计算难度，不是绝对不可破解；量子计算进展可能改变部分算法的安全性。签名验证只证明密钥对应关系，不证明签署人的现实身份，身份信任还需要证书体系或线下核对。算法实现随机数不足、密钥长度不够或侧信道泄露都会削弱强度；密码体系的安全要结合协议、实现和使用流程整体评估。The boundary: public-key security rests on computational hardness of mathematical problems, not absolute unbreakability, and quantum progress may change the safety of some algorithms. A signature verifies the key relationship, not the signer's real-world identity; identity trust still needs a certificate system or offline checking. Weak randomness, short keys or side-channel leaks in implementation all reduce strength. Cryptographic security is assessed across protocol, implementation and usage together.[3][6]

04为什么手机明明支持 5G，状态栏却显示 4G？5G 信号是怎么回落的？Why does a 5G-capable phone show 4G, and how does 5G signal fall back?

NSA 双连接锚定 4G 核心网，SA 直连 5G 核心；覆盖不足或未开通时回落 LTE，SOS 仅可紧急呼叫。NSA dual connectivity anchors on the 4G core while SA attaches to the 5G core; poor band coverage or missing SA falls back to LTE, and SOS allows emergency calls only.

手机状态栏显示 5G 或 4G，反映的是当前接入的无线制式与可用网络，不等于手机本身不支持 5G。5G 新空口（NR）可以叠加在既有 LTE 网络之上：非独立组网（NSA）模式下，5G 基站与 4G 基站双连接，控制面锚定在 4G 核心网，5G 无线只负责高速数据；独立组网（SA）则直接接入 5G 核心网。当 5G 信号不可用、手机回到 LTE 覆盖范围，或当地运营商尚未开通 SA 时，状态栏就显示 4G，这是制式间的回落，不是故障。The 5G or 4G label in the status bar reflects the radio system and available network currently attached, not whether the handset itself supports 5G. 5G New Radio (NR) can be layered over the existing LTE network. In non-standalone (NSA) mode the 5G base station dual-connects to the 4G station, the control plane anchors on the 4G core, and the 5G radio carries high-speed data; standalone (SA) mode connects directly to a 5G core. When 5G is unavailable, the handset returns to LTE coverage, or the local operator has not launched SA, the bar shows 4G. This is an inter-system fallback, not a fault.[9]

5G 覆盖范围由频段决定。低频频段绕射和穿墙好、覆盖半径大；中频 n78（约 3.5 GHz）是城市与郊区主力频段，宏站覆盖通常在数百米到一公里量级，明显短于低频 4G；毫米波频段带宽大但覆盖更近、易被遮挡。频率越高，绕射能力越差，基站密度需要越高。室内、地下和小区边缘区域最先失去 5G 信号，手机便回落到覆盖更好的 4G 制式维持数据连接。5G coverage depends on the frequency band. Low bands diffract and penetrate walls well with a large radius; the mid-band n78, around 3.5 GHz, is the workhorse for cities and suburbs, with macro cells typically covering several hundred meters to about a kilometer, clearly shorter than low-band 4G. Millimeter-wave bands offer large bandwidth but reach even closer and are easily blocked. The higher the frequency, the poorer the diffraction, and the denser the base-station deployment must be. Indoors, underground and at cell edges, 5G is lost first, and the phone falls back to the better-covered 4G system to hold the data connection.[9][10]

语音通话还受组网方式影响。SA 5G 核心网下语音通常走基于 IMS 的 VoNR；NSA 或未部署 5G 语音时，通话会回落到 LTE 的 VoLTE，必要时再向 2G/3G 电路域回落（CSFB）。这就是部分手机上网显示 5G、打电话时却切到 4G 或更老制式的原因。双连接架构下，数据可以同时使用 4G 与 5G 资源，状态栏标注的制式只代表当前主用连接。Voice calls also depend on the deployment mode. Under a 5G SA core, voice usually runs over IMS-based VoNR; with NSA or no 5G voice deployed, calls fall back to VoLTE over LTE, and when needed further to 2G/3G circuit-switched fallback (CSFB). This explains why some phones show 5G for data yet switch to 4G or an older system on a call. In dual connectivity, data can use 4G and 5G resources at once, and the labeled system only marks the current primary connection.[9][12]

状态栏的 SOS 或 SOS only 是另一类标识：手机没有接入所属运营商的正常网络，但仍可通过其他可用网络拨打紧急电话；No Service 则表示当前区域没有可接入的蜂窝信号。紧急呼叫不依赖签约网络，只要有任一运营商覆盖并支持紧急呼叫即可发起。信号格数只反映接收强度，不能直接换算成速率；同一位置 5G 信号弱时，回落 4G 反而比勉强维持弱 5G 更稳定。SOS or SOS only is a different kind of indicator: the phone has not attached to its operator's normal network, but emergency calls can still go through another available network. No Service means no cellular signal can be reached in the area. Emergency calling does not rely on a subscription; any covering operator that supports emergency calls can carry it. Signal bars show received strength only, not a direct speed reading; where 5G is weak, falling back to 4G can be more stable than holding a marginal 5G link.[11][12]

边界：显示 4G 可能是 NSA 架构、频段覆盖不足、SA 未开通、终端兼容或遮挡等多种原因，不能单凭图标判断手机损坏。NSA 与 SA 的演进由运营商网络建设决定，终端支持的频段与载波组合也会影响能否搜到 5G。评估覆盖要结合基站分布、频段和室内外环境；紧急呼叫能力受当地法规与网络支持限制，正常通话和数据服务仍需有效签约覆盖。Boundary: showing 4G can stem from NSA architecture, mid-band coverage gaps, unavailable SA, terminal compatibility or obstruction, so the icon alone cannot prove a broken phone. NSA and SA evolution follows operator network build-out, and the bands and carrier combinations a handset supports affect whether 5G can be found. Coverage assessment must combine base-station distribution, band and indoor/outdoor context. Emergency-call capability is limited by local regulation and network support; ordinary calls and data still require valid subscribed coverage.[10][11]
