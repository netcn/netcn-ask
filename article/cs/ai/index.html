从“我不是机器人”复选框到硅芯片的缩放极限：这篇 Hub 用两个问题拆开验证码的风险评分、AI 攻防、隐私与无障碍代价，以及登纳德缩放结束后的量子隧穿、发热、光刻和封装路线。它不把复选框当成身份证明，也不把摩尔定律当成物理定律。From the “I'm not a robot” checkbox to the limits of silicon scaling, this Hub uses two questions to unpack CAPTCHA risk scoring, the AI arms race, privacy and accessibility costs, and the quantum tunnelling, heat, lithography and packaging problems that followed the end of Dennard scaling. 01为什么勾一下“我不是机器人”就能挡住机器人？Why does a single “I'm not a robot” checkbox stop bots?

复选框只是触发器；后台用浏览器行为、网络与历史信号算风险分，低风险放行，高风险加试。The checkbox is only a trigger: browser behaviour, network context and history feed a risk score; low risk passes, high risk gets a challenge.

先给结论：那个方框不是传统意义上的复选框，而是一个触发器。你点下去之后，页面会向 Google 的 reCAPTCHA 服务提交一串信号，由后台判断你是人还是脚本；风险低就直接发一个通过令牌，风险高才弹出九宫格图片或声音题。多数人只看到“勾一下”，少数人被加试，差别在点击前后的痕迹。Start with the conclusion: that box is not a conventional checkbox but a trigger. When you click it, the page sends a bundle of signals to Google's reCAPTCHA service, which decides in the background whether you are a person or a script. Low risk earns a pass token; higher risk brings an image grid or an audio puzzle. Most ordinary users see only a click, a minority see an extra challenge; the difference lies not in the click itself but in the traces left before and after it.[1][2][3]

Google 的公开文档把 v2 的产物称为 g-recaptcha-response 令牌，站点再把令牌送到后端验证；v3 则直接给每次请求一个 0.0–1.0 的分数，1.0 表示很像正常互动，0.0 表示很像机器人，站点自己定阈值。W3C 的说明更具体：v2 的“伪复选框”会收集鼠标和键盘操作、页面 CSS、浏览器语言、安装的插件、JavaScript 对象以及 Google 在过去六个月放置的 Cookie。Google 不公开权重，“只看鼠标轨迹”只是外部推断。Google's public documentation calls the v2 result a g-recaptcha-response token, which the site sends to its backend for verification. v3 instead returns a score from 0.0 to 1.0 for each request: 1.0 looks like normal interaction, 0.0 looks like a bot, and the site chooses its own threshold. W3C is more specific: the v2 “pseudo-checkbox” collected mouse and keyboard actions, page CSS, browser language, installed plug-ins, JavaScript objects and Google cookies placed over the previous six months. Google does not publish the weight of each signal, so any claim that it “only looks at mouse paths” is an outside inference.[1][2][4]

为什么简单的点击能筛掉大量脚本？因为廉价自动化往往在环境上露馅：没有正常的浏览器历史、请求头顺序不对、点击间隔精确得不像人、同一个 IP 在短时间内提交成百上千次。人的动作有抖动和减速，机器可以模仿，但模仿得越像，成本越高。复选框把这道成本抬到“需要维护一套浏览器环境”的水平，拦住的多数是低投入的批量脚本，而不是有目标的攻击者。Why can a simple click filter so many scripts? Cheap automation often gives itself away in its environment: no ordinary browser history, unusual header order, clicks timed too precisely, or one IP submitting hundreds of requests in seconds. Human motion has jitter and deceleration; machines can imitate it, but the more convincingly they do so, the more it costs. The checkbox raises the cost to “maintain a browser environment”, which stops most low-effort bulk scripts rather than targeted attackers.[1][3][5]

证据也支持“它不是靠点击本身”。Google v2 文档显示，验证结果是一个令牌；v3 文档显示，分数是一个连续值，且令牌两分钟后过期。Cloudflare 的 Turnstile 更进一步：它可以完全不显示 CAPTCHA，只在后台跑计算题、探测浏览器 API 和行为信号；它的“Managed”模式才根据风险决定是否显示复选框。W3C 还记录，传统 CAPTCHA 平均要花用户约 32 秒，这也是网站愿意换成无感方案的原因之一。The evidence supports “not the click itself”. Google's v2 documentation shows that the result is a token; its v3 documentation shows a continuous score and a token that expires after two minutes. Cloudflare Turnstile goes further: it can show no CAPTCHA at all, running computational puzzles, browser-API probes and behavioural checks in the background; only its “Managed” widget decides whether to show a checkbox based on risk. W3C also records that a traditional CAPTCHA takes a user about 32 seconds on average, one reason sites move to invisible checks.[1][2][3][4]

边界要写清楚：复选框不证明“你是人”，只说明“这次会话的风险低”。共享 IP、无痕模式、禁用 Cookie、使用屏幕阅读器或键盘导航，都可能被误判；反过来，租用住宅代理、模拟真人点击的农场也能绕过一部分。它适合挡垃圾注册和爬虫，不适合单独保护支付、改密或管理员操作。把复选框、限流、服务端校验和二次验证叠起来，才是可用的防线。Draw the boundary clearly: the checkbox does not prove “you are human”; it says “this session looks low-risk”. Shared IPs, incognito mode, blocked cookies, screen readers and keyboard navigation can all trigger false positives; conversely, residential proxies and click farms that simulate real people can bypass part of the check. It is suitable for blocking junk registrations and scrapers, not for protecting payments, password changes or admin actions on its own. Stack the checkbox with rate limits, server-side validation and two-factor authentication to make a usable defence.[2][3][4][5]

02验证码还能挡住 AI 吗？“我不是机器人”的边界在哪里？Can CAPTCHAs still stop AI, and where does “I'm not a robot” stop working?

它能提高自动化成本，却不是可靠的人机证明；图像题已被 AI 大量攻破，还带来隐私与无障碍代价。It raises the cost of automation but is not reliable proof of humanity: image puzzles are widely broken by AI, and it carries privacy and accessibility costs.

不能把它当成可靠的人机证明。CAPTCHA 的原名是“完全自动化公开图灵测试”，它从一开始就是一场成本竞赛：防御方让题目对人容易、对机器难，攻击方则用算力和数据把“难”一点点磨平。今天的问题不是“能不能挡住所有机器人”，而是“把自动化成本抬到多高、误伤多少真人、收集多少数据”。It cannot serve as a reliable proof of humanity. CAPTCHA stands for “Completely Automated Public Turing test to tell Computers and Humans Apart”, and it has been a cost contest from the start: defenders make a task easy for people and hard for machines, while attackers grind that hardness down with compute and data. The question today is not “can it stop every bot” but “how high a cost does it impose, how many real people does it misclassify, and how much data does it collect”.[4][5][6]

图像题是最明显的缺口。2016 年哥伦比亚大学团队用深度学习的语义标注攻击 reCAPTCHA 图像题，报告在约 19 秒内解出 70.78% 的挑战；后续研究继续用更强的检测模型、视觉语言模型和代理框架刷高成功率。攻击者不一定自己训练模型：市面上的打码平台把题目转发给真人或模型，按次收费；也有研究用强化学习或模仿学习，让代理在反复尝试中学会点击。Image puzzles are the clearest gap. In 2016 a Columbia University team used deep-learning semantic labelling against reCAPTCHA image challenges and reported solving 70.78% of them in about 19 seconds; later work used stronger detectors, vision-language models and agent frameworks to push success rates higher. Attackers need not train a model: commercial solving services forward puzzles to people or models for a fee, and some research uses reinforcement or imitation learning so an agent learns to click through repeated attempts.[4][5][6]

防御方因此把战场从“出题”移到“判断上下文”：v3 不给题，只给分数；Turnstile 用 proof-of-work、浏览器 API 探测和行为特征，在后台决定放行、加试还是拦截。这样对大多数用户更顺滑，也让攻击者必须同时伪造网络、浏览器和行为。但道高一尺魔高一丈：无头浏览器、住宅代理、真实用户点击农场，都能把“像人”这件事拆成可购买的服务。Defenders therefore moved the battlefield from “setting puzzles” to “judging context”: v3 gives no puzzle, only a score; Turnstile uses proof-of-work, browser-API probes and behavioural signals to decide whether to pass, challenge or block. This is smoother for most users and forces attackers to fake network, browser and behaviour at once. But the arms race continues: headless browsers, residential proxies and real-user click farms turn “looks human” into a purchasable service.[2][3][5]

代价不只在安全。W3C 指出，交互式 CAPTCHA 会把盲人、视障、阅读障碍、听障和认知障碍用户挡在门外；声音题同样可以被语音识别攻破。v2 的伪复选框还收集大量个人数据，W3C 明确把它列为隐私换取便利的例子。无感方案对无障碍更友好，但它把判断转移到第三方，仍然存在误判、申诉困难和数据集中问题。The cost is not only security. W3C notes that interactive CAPTCHAs shut out blind, low-vision, dyslexic, deaf, hard-of-hearing and cognitively impaired users; audio puzzles can also be broken by speech recognition. The v2 pseudo-checkbox collected large amounts of personal data, which W3C cites as an example of privacy traded for convenience. Invisible checks are friendlier for accessibility, but they move the judgment to a third party, with false positives, appeal problems and data concentration.[3][4][6]

所以边界是：CAPTCHA 只能作为一层减速带，不能作为身份认证。对高价值操作，应该用密码管理器、通行密钥、双因素认证、设备绑定、速率限制和服务端异常检测；对普通表单，可以用蜜罐字段、时间戳和 IP 信誉，减少无谓的题。若网站只用复选框就放行付款或改密，那不是“验证了人”，而是把风险推给了一个不透明的分数。So the boundary is: CAPTCHA is one layer of friction, not authentication. For high-value actions, use password managers, passkeys, two-factor authentication, device binding, rate limits and server-side anomaly detection; for ordinary forms, honeypot fields, timestamps and IP reputation can reduce pointless puzzles. If a site lets a checkbox alone authorise a payment or password change, it has not “verified a human”; it has handed the risk to an opaque score.[2][3][4][5]

03芯片越做越小，硅会不会撞上物理极限？As microchips shrink, will silicon hit hard physical limits?

会，但不是某一纳米的硬墙：量子隧穿、漏电、发热、光刻和成本一起收紧，行业转向 3D、新材料与专用架构。Yes, but not as a single nanometre wall: tunnelling, leakage, heat, lithography and cost tighten together, pushing the industry to 3D, new materials and specialised architectures.

会撞上限制，但不是一条写着“1 纳米”的硬墙。摩尔定律从来不是物理定律，而是“单位面积晶体管数量大约每两年翻倍”的产业观察；登纳德缩放定律也曾承诺：晶体管变小，电压和电容一起降，功耗密度大致不变。这个承诺在 2005 年前后失效，因为栅极氧化层薄到量子隧穿不可忽视，电压不能再按比例下降，功耗密度开始上升，主频竞赛随之停下。It will hit limits, but not a hard wall labelled “1 nanometre”. Moore's law was never a law of physics; it was an industry observation that transistor count per unit area doubles roughly every two years. Dennard scaling once promised that shrinking transistors would lower voltage and capacitance together, keeping power density roughly constant. That promise failed around 2005: gate oxides became thin enough for quantum tunnelling to matter, voltage could no longer scale down proportionally, power density rose, and the clock-frequency race stalled.[7][10]

机制可以拆成四本账。第一本是量子账：栅极越薄，电子越容易“穿墙”漏过去，关不干净就费电。第二本是热账：单位面积晶体管越多，开关越快，产生的热越集中，散热跟不上就降频。第三本是光刻账：光有衍射极限，ASML 的 EUV 用 13.5 纳米波长，High NA 把数值孔径做到 0.55，分辨率约 8 纳米，但每缩小一步，设备、掩膜和工艺窗口都更贵。第四本是互连账：线越细，电阻和电容的相对影响越大，信号延迟和噪声更难管。The mechanism can be split into four accounts. The quantum account: thinner gates let electrons tunnel through, so the device leaks when it should be off. The heat account: more transistors per area switching faster concentrate heat, and if cooling cannot keep up, the chip throttles. The lithography account: light has a diffraction limit; ASML's EUV uses 13.5 nm light, and High NA raises the numerical aperture to 0.55 with about 8 nm resolution, but every step costs more in tools, masks and process window. The interconnect account: thinner wires make resistance and capacitance relatively more important, worsening delay and noise.[7][8][10]

产业没有停在“等死”。平面晶体管换成 FinFET，再往 Gate-All-Around（环绕栅极）走；光刻从 DUV 走到 EUV；封装从单颗芯片走到 chiplet、2.5D/3D 堆叠；架构从拼主频转到多核、专用加速器和内存层次。IEEE 的 IRDS 路线图和一份课程综述把 2005 年后的路线概括为：晶体管密度还能涨，但单线程性能不再跟着涨，收益要靠并行、封装和材料。The industry did not wait for the wall. Planar transistors became FinFETs and are moving toward Gate-All-Around; lithography moved from DUV to EUV; packaging moved from a single die to chiplets and 2.5D/3D stacks; architecture moved from clock speed to multiple cores, specialised accelerators and memory hierarchies. IEEE's IRDS roadmap and a course review summarise the post-2005 road: transistor density can still grow, but single-thread performance no longer follows, so gains must come from parallelism, packaging and materials.[6][8][9][10]

这也解释了为什么“制程数字”越来越像营销名。厂商标的 3 纳米、2 纳米，并不等于某个物理尺寸真是 3 纳米；它更像一代工艺的代号，背后是晶体管结构、金属间距、SRAM 密度和良率的组合。真正决定芯片性能的，还有功耗预算、散热、内存带宽和软件。把“更小”直接等同于“更快”，是登纳德时代留下的错觉。This also explains why process numbers look more like brands. A vendor's “3 nm” or “2 nm” is not a physical dimension that is literally 3 or 2 nanometres; it is a generation label combining transistor structure, metal pitch, SRAM density and yield. Power budget, cooling, memory bandwidth and software also decide real performance. Equating “smaller” with “faster” is a habit left over from the Dennard era.[7][8][9]

边界：硅原子本身有大小，晶体管不可能无限缩小；到那一步，量子隧穿、统计波动和制造公差会让“确定性开关”变得困难。但“极限”不是某一天突然关门，而是成本曲线越来越陡，产业转向新材料（碳化硅、氮化镓、二维材料）、新架构（光子、存算一体）和更先进的封装。短期内硅仍是主力；长期看，不是“硅会不会消失”，而是“哪些功能值得继续用硅，哪些要换路线”。The boundary: silicon atoms have a finite size, so transistors cannot shrink forever; near that scale, tunnelling, statistical variation and manufacturing tolerance make a deterministic switch difficult. But the limit is not a door that slams shut one day; it is a cost curve that gets steeper, pushing the industry toward new materials (silicon carbide, gallium nitride, two-dimensional materials), new architectures (photonic and compute-in-memory) and advanced packaging. Silicon remains the workhorse for now; the long-run question is not whether silicon disappears, but which functions are worth keeping on silicon and which need another road.[6][7][9][10]

04AI 训练和推理为什么都要用 GPU？矩阵乘并行、显存带宽和显存墙各起什么作用？Why do AI training and inference rely on GPUs, and what roles do matrix-multiply parallelism, memory bandwidth and the memory wall play?

GPU 成千线程并行切分矩阵乘瓦片，HBM 数百 GB/s–TB/s 带宽供给数据；容量、带宽、互连与散热共同约束速度。GPUs split matrix multiplies into tiles across thousands of threads, fed by HBM bandwidth in hundreds of GB/s to TB/s; capacity, bandwidth, interconnect and cooling jointly bound speed.

AI 模型的核心计算是大规模矩阵乘法。神经网络一层把一组输入向量与权重矩阵相乘再累加，参数动辄数十亿到数千亿量级，单次前向或反向传播要完成海量乘加运算。CPU 为少数高性能串行核心设计，擅长分支、调度和复杂控制；面对同一套算术同时作用在大量数据元素上的任务，串行核心数量不足。GPU 原本为图形渲染设计，内部有成百上千个相对简单的计算核心，可以把输出矩阵切成许多小块，由不同线程块并行处理，这正是矩阵乘最需要的并行度。The core computation of an AI model is large-scale matrix multiplication. One neural layer multiplies a set of input vectors by the weight matrix and accumulates the result; with parameters in the billions to hundreds of billions, a single forward or backward pass performs a vast number of multiply-accumulate operations. CPUs are designed around a few high-performance serial cores that excel at branches, scheduling and complex control, and they lack enough serial cores for one arithmetic operation applied simultaneously to many data elements. GPUs, originally built for graphics rendering, contain hundreds or thousands of relatively simple cores. They split the output matrix into many small tiles handled by different thread blocks in parallel, exactly the parallelism matrix multiplication needs.[7][11]

CUDA 等编程模型把计算映射到成千上万个并发线程：矩阵分块后，每个线程块负责一块瓦片，块内线程协同完成局部乘加，再汇总到全局结果。GPU 适合数据并行：同一操作重复作用于大量数据元素，线程之间控制流简单、分支少。模型训练中反向传播需要对海量样本重复这套过程，并行规模越大，单步迭代耗时越短；CPU 串行执行同样规模的矩阵乘，时间会拉长几个数量级。Programming models such as CUDA map the work onto tens of thousands of concurrent threads: after blocking the matrix, each thread block owns one tile, its threads cooperate on the local multiply-accumulate, and results are reduced to the global output. GPUs suit data parallelism: the same operation repeats over many elements, threads share a simple control flow and few branches. In training, back propagation repeats this process over huge numbers of samples; the larger the parallel scale, the shorter each iteration, while a CPU running the same matrix multiply serially takes orders of magnitude longer.[11][12]

并行算力之外，真正的瓶颈常常是数据搬运。GPU 配备高带宽显存（HBM），通过紧邻计算核心的堆叠内存提供数百 GB/s 到 TB/s 量级的带宽；相比之下，普通内存条带宽低一个量级。矩阵乘反复读写权重和激活值，如果数据要在显存与主机内存之间来回搬运，计算核心会等待数据，这就是显存墙。模型参数越大、批次越大，对显存容量和带宽的压力越高；显存装不下时，只能分层加载，速率随之下降。Beyond parallel compute, the real bottleneck is often data movement. GPUs use high-bandwidth memory (HBM), stacked close to the compute cores, delivering bandwidth in the hundreds of GB/s to TB/s range; ordinary system memory is about an order of magnitude lower. Matrix multiplication repeatedly reads and writes weights and activations, and if data must shuttle between VRAM and host memory, the cores wait on data, the memory wall. Larger parameters and batches raise pressure on both capacity and bandwidth; when VRAM cannot hold the model, data must be loaded in layers and the rate drops.[12]

GPU 集群还需要高速互连在多卡之间同步梯度和参数，散热与供电也是约束。大规模模型训练单次运行可达兆瓦时（MWh）量级的耗电，推理阶段则按每次请求的算力和访问量持续消耗能耗；能效比与带宽利用率和峰值算力同样重要。专用 AI 加速器、张量核心和稀疏化计算，都是围绕数据并行与内存带宽两条主线做的优化，而不是把通用 CPU 主频继续提高。GPU clusters also need high-speed interconnects to synchronize gradients and parameters across cards, and cooling and power supply are constraints too. A large training run can consume on the order of megawatt-hours (MWh), while inference keeps spending energy per request according to compute and memory access. Energy efficiency and bandwidth utilization matter as much as peak compute. Dedicated AI accelerators, tensor cores and sparsity all optimize along data parallelism and memory bandwidth rather than pushing general CPU clock speeds higher.[7][12]

边界：GPU 的优势来自数据并行，不擅长串行依赖强、分支复杂的通用任务；显存容量、带宽、互连和散热共同决定实际训练与推理速度，不能只看峰值浮点算力。不同模型结构、精度（FP16/FP32 等）和软件栈下，硬件利用率差别很大。矩阵乘并行度随矩阵规模变化，小批量或小模型上 GPU 的相对优势会被数据搬运开销抵消；选型要结合模型规模、延迟目标和能耗预算。Boundary: the GPU advantage comes from data parallelism, not from serial tasks with strong dependencies and complex branches. VRAM capacity, bandwidth, interconnect and cooling jointly set real training and inference speed, so peak floating-point performance alone is not enough. Hardware utilization differs widely across model structures, precision such as FP16/FP32 and software stacks. Matrix-multiply parallelism scales with matrix size; on small batches or small models the GPU advantage is offset by data-movement overhead, and selection must weigh model size, latency targets and energy budget.[7][11][12]

05AI 为什么会一本正经地编造信息？幻觉是概率生成和分布外推造成的吗？Why does AI confidently invent information, and are hallucinations caused by probabilistic generation and distribution extrapolation?

模型逐词预测下一个 token，损失函数无真实性约束；证据不足时按语言模式补全，属概率风险而非故意说谎。The model predicts the next token word by word with no truthfulness in the loss; weak evidence leads to pattern completion, a probabilistic risk rather than a deliberate lie.

大语言模型不是在陈述自己知道的事实，而是在逐词预测下一个 token。训练时模型最大化在给定前文下出现真实下一个词的概率分布；生成时按这个分布采样或贪心选择下一个词，再把结果接回前文重复。目标函数衡量的是语言接续的概率拟合，训练损失里并没有专门的真实性约束。因此模型输出的是看起来最像训练语料的连贯文本，而不是经过事实核查的断言；一段流畅回答中混入错误信息，是这种概率生成机制的固有风险，不是模型在故意说谎。A large language model does not state facts it knows; it predicts the next token word by word. During training the model maximizes the probability distribution over the true next word given the preceding context; at generation it samples or greedily picks the next word from that distribution, appends it, and repeats. The objective measures how well language continuation fits the data, and the training loss contains no dedicated truthfulness constraint. The model therefore outputs fluent text that looks most like its training corpus, not a fact-checked assertion. Wrong information mixed into a smooth answer is an inherent risk of this probabilistic generation, not a deliberate lie by the model.[13][14]

模型对常见话题、语料里反复出现的事实预测较稳；遇到罕见事实、最新事件或训练分布之外的组合，证据不足时概率分布变得平坦，高熵采样更容易选中弱证据的 token。训练数据本身的缺口、过时内容和互相矛盾之处，会被模型学成语料倾向；分布外推时，模型按语言模式补全句子，而不是检索到可靠来源。对话上下文持续变化，话题漂移后输入进一步偏离训练分布，幻觉风险随之累积。Predictions are steadier for common topics and facts that recur in the corpus. For rare facts, recent events or combinations outside the training distribution, evidence is thin, the probability distribution flattens, and high-entropy sampling more often selects weakly supported tokens. Gaps, outdated material and contradictions in the training data are learned as corpus tendencies; when extrapolating outside the distribution, the model completes sentences by language pattern rather than retrieving a reliable source. As conversational context keeps changing and topics drift, the input moves further from the training distribution, and hallucination risk accumulates.[13][15]

研究通常把幻觉分为事实性与忠实性两类：前者指与世界事实不符的内容，后者指与给定上下文或指令不一致的内容。采样策略、提示方式、系统温度和上下文长度都会影响出错概率；偏好流畅和确定性答案的评测目标，可能在统计上抬高编造倾向。模型对自身置信度的表达并不等于事实概率，引用格式、人名编号和渠道细节可以生成得很自然，却同样可能是虚构的。Research usually divides hallucination into factual and faithful types: factual content contradicts real-world facts, while faithful content disagrees with the given context or instruction. Sampling strategy, prompting, system temperature and context length all affect the error probability; evaluation goals that favor fluent, confident answers can statistically raise the tendency to fabricate. The model's expression of confidence is not a factual probability, and citation formats, names, numbers and channel details can look natural while still being invented.[14][15]

工程上可以检索增强、引用可核验来源、降低采样随机性、约束输出范围，并在关键环节加入人工或外部校验。这些方法降低概率性错误的发生率，但不能从机制上消除：只要模型按分布预测下一个词，分布边缘的弱证据场景就仍可能产生错误陈述。不同研究对幻觉成因的权重判断仍有分歧，数据质量、架构目标、推理时检索与采样策略各自贡献多少，仍在持续研究中。Engineering can add retrieval augmentation, verifiable source citations, lower sampling randomness, constrained output ranges, and human or external checks at key steps. These methods reduce the rate of probabilistic errors but cannot remove them by mechanism: as long as the model predicts the next word from a distribution, weakly supported scenarios at the distribution edge can still produce false statements. Studies still disagree on the relative weight of causes, and how much data quality, architecture objective, inference-time retrieval and sampling each contribute remains under active research.[13][15]

边界：幻觉是概率生成与分布外推带来的内容风险，描述为 AI 主动欺骗或隐瞒并不准确；模型没有意图和信念，错误来自统计拟合与证据缺口。不能用模型语气自信与否判断对错，也不能把训练截止后的信息当成已知。涉及医疗、法律、财务等事实结论时，应回到一手来源核对；模型适合起草、归纳和解释，最终事实责任仍由使用方承担。Boundary: hallucination is a content risk from probabilistic generation and distribution extrapolation; describing it as deliberate AI deception or concealment is inaccurate. The model has no intention or belief, and errors come from statistical fitting and evidence gaps. Confident tone cannot be used to judge correctness, and information after the training cutoff must not be treated as known. For medical, legal or financial conclusions, verify against primary sources; the model suits drafting, summarizing and explaining, and final factual responsibility stays with the user.[13][14][15]
