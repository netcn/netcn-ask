算法与计算Algorithms and Computation

从二进制开关到推荐系统，再到计算极限与随机性：六个问题串起算法怎样把输入变成输出，以及它为什么会被数据、目标和物理条件限制。这个 Hub 不把算法当成魔法，也不把摩尔定律当成自然法则。From binary switches to recommender systems, and on to computational limits and randomness, six questions trace how algorithms turn inputs into outputs—and why data, objectives and physical constraints keep them bounded. This Hub treats neither algorithms as magic nor Moore's law as a law of nature.

01计算器为什么用二进制？算法又到底是什么？Why do calculators use binary, and what exactly is a computer algorithm?

二进制把数变成开关状态，算法把状态按有限步骤变成结果；两者都不是自然法则，而是工程取舍。Binary turns numbers into switch states, and an algorithm turns those states into a result in finite, definite steps; neither is a law of nature, only an engineering choice.

先给结论：计算器内部用二进制，不是因为十进制“算得不准”，而是因为电子电路最擅长区分两种稳定状态；算法则是把输入变成输出的一组有限、明确、可执行的步骤。你按下“123”，机器先把它编码成一串 0 和 1，再由加法器按位运算，最后转回十进制显示。二进制的优点是电路简单、抗噪声；算法则是这套硬件上被反复调用的一套规则。Start with the conclusion: a calculator uses binary not because decimal arithmetic is wrong, but because electronic circuits are best at distinguishing two stable states. An algorithm is a finite, unambiguous, executable procedure that turns inputs into outputs. When you press “123”, the machine encodes it as a string of 0s and 1s, adds it bit by bit, then converts the result back to decimal for display. Binary keeps the circuit simple and noise-tolerant; the algorithm is the rulebook repeatedly applied on top of that hardware.[1][2]

二进制是逢二进一，每一位的权重是 2 的幂：1、2、4、8、16……例如 1010 表示 8+2=10。一个非负整数可以写成唯一的二进制展开，加减乘除都能分解成取反、进位、移位等开关动作。十进制当然也能做机器，但要在一条电路上稳定区分 0 到 9 十个电压台阶；台阶越多，容差越小，温度漂移和噪声越容易把 8 读成 9。二进制只留高、低两个区，工程上更抗造。Binary is base 2: each position is a power of two—1, 2, 4, 8, 16—so 1010 means 8 + 2 = 10. Every non-negative integer has a unique binary expansion, and arithmetic decomposes into switching actions such as inversion, carry and shift. Decimal machines are possible, but a circuit must distinguish ten voltage levels; more levels mean tighter tolerances, and heat or noise can turn an 8 into a 9. Binary keeps only a high and a low band, which is easier to build reliably.[1][2]

“算法”不是某一台计算器的品牌，也不是一段具体代码。按 Knuth 的经典定义，它要有穷性：有限步后停止；确定性：每一步没有歧义；还要有输入、输出和有效性：每一步都能机械执行。做菜谱、欧几里得求最大公约数、排序，都是算法；同一算法可以用 Python、C 或纸笔实现，程序只是它在某种语言里的一个具体化身。An algorithm is not a calculator brand or a particular piece of code. In Knuth’s classic account it needs finiteness (it stops after a finite number of steps), definiteness (no ambiguous step), input, output and effectiveness (each step can be carried out mechanically). A recipe, Euclid’s greatest-common-divisor method and a sorting procedure are algorithms; the same algorithm can be written in Python, C or on paper, and a program is only one concrete embodiment.[1][11]

把两者接起来看：二进制解决“机器用什么符号表示数”，算法解决“机器按什么顺序搬动这些符号”。计算器里的加法器并不理解“十进制”，它只看见高低电平；十进制的键盘和屏幕是人机接口，中间的编码、运算、进位和显示由算法与电路共同完成。Babbage 的差分机、20 世纪的机械计算器都证明，十进制机器并非不可能，只是更贵、更慢、更易出错。Put the two together: binary decides what symbols represent numbers; the algorithm decides in what order the machine moves those symbols. The adder inside a calculator does not “understand” decimal; it sees only high and low levels. The decimal keypad and display are human interfaces, while encoding, arithmetic, carry and display are shared work of circuits and algorithms. Babbage’s Difference Engine and twentieth-century mechanical calculators show that decimal machines are not impossible—only more expensive, slower and more error-prone.[1][2]

边界也要说清楚：二进制不是“自然法则”，量子比特、多值逻辑和模拟电路都在尝试别的表示；算法也不是“绝对正确”，它依赖模型、输入和精度。浮点数有舍入误差，递归可能栈溢出，并行算法还会遇到竞态。计算器算得准，是因为它把问题限制在有限精度和明确规则内，不是因为它掌握了数学的全部。The boundary matters: binary is not a law of nature; quantum bits, multi-valued logic and analog circuits all try other representations, and an algorithm is not “absolutely correct” because it depends on models, inputs and precision. Floating point rounds, recursion can overflow the stack, and parallel algorithms meet race conditions. A calculator is accurate because it restricts the problem to finite precision and explicit rules, not because it has captured all of mathematics.[1][2][11]

02算法推荐怎样造出“信息茧房”，又怎样把偏见带进新闻？How do recommendation algorithms create filter bubbles and bring bias into the news?

推荐系统用行为数据猜点击，再用曝光反过来塑造行为；气泡与偏见由此被放大，但厚度因平台而异。Recommenders guess clicks from behaviour and then shape behaviour through exposure; bubbles and bias are amplified this way, but their thickness varies by platform.

先给结论：推荐系统不是把“最好的内容”直接递给你，而是先猜你会不会点、会不会看完、会不会互动，再按这个分数排序。它把你困在“信息茧房”里，靠的是反馈回路：你点过什么，系统就多给你什么；多给什么，你就更可能点什么。偏见则从数据、目标和标签三个入口混进来，最后表现为你看到的新闻越来越像你想看的，而另一些议题越来越难出现。Start with the conclusion: a recommender does not hand you “the best content”; it first guesses whether you will click, finish or engage, then ranks by that score. It keeps you in a filter bubble through a feedback loop: what you click shapes what you are shown, and what you are shown shapes what you click. Bias enters through data, objectives and labels, and shows up as news that increasingly resembles what you already wanted—while other issues become harder to see.[3][4]

协同过滤、向量召回和排序模型各有分工：先从上亿条行为里找“和你相似的人还看过什么”，再把候选内容压成向量做粗筛，最后用点击率、停留时长、关注转化等目标精排。问题在于，这些目标不是“公共价值”的中立代理。停留时长会奖励标题党，互动率会奖励愤怒，历史点击会放大已经流行的话题；系统越优化这些指标，越容易把“可预测的你”锁进一个窄口袋。Collaborative filtering, vector recall and ranking models divide the work: first find “what people like you also read” among billions of behaviours, then compress candidates into vectors for coarse recall, then rank them by click-through, dwell time and follow conversion. The problem is that these objectives are not neutral proxies for public value. Dwell time rewards clickbait, engagement rewards outrage, and historical clicks amplify what is already popular; the more the system optimizes those metrics, the more it locks a predictable you into a narrow pocket.[3][4]

“信息茧房”与“回声室”常被混用，但证据并不均匀。Pariser 在 2011 年用“过滤气泡”描述个性化筛选如何把公共议题切成私人版本；2021 年 PNAS 的跨国研究分析了 1 亿多条内容，发现 Facebook 和 Twitter 上的同质化聚合更明显，而 Reddit 和 Gab 的信息扩散并不都朝同温层收窄。也就是说，平台架构、话题和用户行为共同决定气泡的厚薄，不能把所有责任都推给“算法”两个字。“Filter bubble” and “echo chamber” are often conflated, but the evidence is uneven. Pariser used “filter bubble” in 2011 to describe how personalization cuts public issues into private versions. A 2021 PNAS study of more than 100 million content items found stronger homophilic clustering on Facebook and Twitter, while information diffusion on Reddit and Gab did not always narrow toward like-minded users. Platform architecture, topic and user behaviour jointly set the thickness of the bubble; not all of it can be blamed on “the algorithm”.[4]

新闻里的偏见更隐蔽。ProPublica 2016 年对 COMPAS 风险评估的调查发现，黑人被告被错误标成“高风险”的比例约为 44.9%，白人被告约为 23.5%；白人被告被错误标成“低风险”的比例约为 47.7%，黑人被告约为 28.0%。模型没有把种族当输入，但逮捕记录、社区、就业等代理变量把历史不平等带了进来。新闻推荐同理：训练数据里的曝光偏差、编辑目标里的“增长优先”、标签体系里的“重要/不重要”，都会改变谁被看见。Bias in news is subtler. ProPublica’s 2016 investigation of the COMPAS risk tool found that Black defendants were falsely labelled high risk about 44.9% of the time, versus 23.5% for white defendants; white defendants were falsely labelled low risk about 47.7% of the time, versus 28.0% for Black defendants. Race was not an input, but proxies such as arrest records, neighbourhood and employment carried historical inequality inside. News recommendation works the same way: exposure bias in training data, “growth first” editorial objectives and labels of “important/unimportant” all change who gets seen.[5]

边界是：个性化不必然等于窄化，用户也会主动搜索、取关和跨平台迁移；气泡不是“洗脑机器”，它更像一层逐渐变厚的过滤纸。可验证的治理方向是让目标可审计：到底优化什么；让数据可追溯：标签从哪来；给用户可操作的调节项，并对高风险场景做独立评估。把“算法”当成唯一反派，会漏掉真正的权力：谁定义目标，谁拥有数据，谁决定什么算“好新闻”。The boundary: personalization is not automatically narrowing; users also search, unfollow and migrate across platforms, and a bubble is less a “brainwashing machine” than a slowly thickening filter paper. A testable governance direction is to make objectives auditable (what exactly is optimized), data traceable (where labels come from), user controls usable, and high-risk uses independently evaluated. Treating “the algorithm” as the only villain misses the real power: who defines the objective, who owns the data, and who decides what counts as good news.[3][4][5]

03传统计算撞上了哪些墙？随机数真能被算法找到吗？What limits conventional computing, and can an algorithm be found for randomness?

物理、计算、工程三堵墙同时逼近；真正的随机数不能由确定性算法算出，算法只能扩展物理熵。Physical, computational and engineering walls close in together; a truly random number cannot be computed by a deterministic algorithm, which can only expand physical entropy.

先给结论：传统计算的极限不是一堵墙，而是三堵不同的墙。物理墙是能量、散热和光刻精度；计算墙是有些问题根本没有通用算法，或已知算法在输入变大时爆炸；工程墙是成本、良率和内存带宽。随机性则站在另一侧：真正的随机数不能由确定性算法“算出来”，算法能产生的是伪随机序列，它的不可预测性取决于种子和熵源。Start with the conclusion: the limits of conventional computing are not one wall but three. The physical wall is energy, heat and lithographic precision; the computational wall is that some problems have no general algorithm, or that known algorithms explode as inputs grow; the engineering wall is cost, yield and memory bandwidth. Randomness sits on the other side: a truly random number cannot be “calculated” by a deterministic algorithm. What algorithms produce are pseudorandom sequences whose unpredictability depends on a seed and an entropy source.[2][6][7]

物理上，芯片缩小曾靠登纳德缩放让电压和尺寸一起降，功耗密度大致不变；2000 年代中期以后这条曲线断了，主频不再逐年猛涨，多核和专用加速器接棒。Nature 2016 年报道，14 纳米晶体管已经比多数病毒小，逼近 2 到 3 纳米时电路特征只有约 10 个原子宽，电子的量子不确定性会让开关变得不可靠；同时，每代光刻机都更贵，经济账先于物理账算不过来。Physically, shrinking chips once relied on Dennard scaling: voltage and dimensions fell together, so power density stayed roughly flat. That curve broke in the mid-2000s; clock speeds stopped climbing every year, and multicore and specialized accelerators took over. Nature reported in 2016 that 14-nanometre transistors were already smaller than most viruses, and at 2–3 nanometres circuit features would be about 10 atoms wide, where quantum uncertainty makes switching unreliable. Meanwhile, each lithography generation cost more; the economic ledger ran out before the physical one.[2][6]

计算上，图灵在 1936 年证明停机问题是不可判定的：不存在一个通用算法，能对任意程序与输入都正确判断它会不会停。这不是“还没发明出来”，而是逻辑上的边界。Church–Turing 论题进一步说，任何有限手段可计算的问题，都能由图灵机计算；因此不可计算性不是某台机器的性能问题。实践中还有 NP 难问题：验证一个解很快，但找解没有已知的多项式算法。Computationally, Turing proved in 1936 that the halting problem is undecidable: no general algorithm can correctly decide, for every program and input, whether it will stop. This is not “not invented yet” but a logical boundary. The Church–Turing thesis goes further: anything computable by finite means can be computed by a Turing machine, so uncomputability is not a performance problem of one machine. In practice there are also NP-hard problems: checking a solution is fast, but finding one has no known polynomial algorithm.[8][11]

随机数更微妙。NIST SP 800-90A 明确把“确定性随机比特生成器”定义为用算法计算比特，输入种子和熵源；只要种子已知，输出就可复现。真正的熵来自硬件噪声、量子过程等物理熵源，算法只能把它扩展、混合、做健康检测，不能凭空制造。Li 与 Vitányi 的 Kolmogorov 复杂度把“随机”定义为“没有比自身更短的描述”：一个真正随机的串不可压缩，而“这个串最短的描述是什么”本身是不可计算的。Randomness is subtler. NIST SP 800-90A explicitly defines a deterministic random bit generator as computing bits with an algorithm, fed by a seed and an entropy source; if the seed is known, the output is reproducible. True entropy comes from physical sources such as hardware noise and quantum processes. An algorithm can expand, mix and health-test that entropy, but cannot conjure it from nothing. Li and Vitányi’s Kolmogorov complexity defines “random” as “having no description shorter than itself”: a truly random string is incompressible, and “what is the shortest description of this string?” is itself uncomputable.[9][10]

边界：极限不等于终点。专用芯片、芯粒封装、3D 堆叠、光计算和量子计算都在绕开旧路径；量子计算机对特定问题有优势，但不会让所有算法都变快。随机性也有层次：统计随机、密码学伪随机、物理随机不是一回事。把“传统计算到头了”理解成“所有计算都停了”，会错过真正的变化：从单一通用 CPU 转向针对任务定制的系统。The boundary: limits are not the end. Specialized chips, chiplet packaging, 3D stacking, optical computing and quantum computing all bypass parts of the old path. A quantum computer has advantages for specific problems, but it will not speed up every algorithm. Randomness also has layers: statistical randomness, cryptographic pseudorandomness and physical randomness are not the same thing. Reading “conventional computing is over” as “all computing stops” misses the real shift—from one general-purpose CPU to systems customized for the task.[6][7][9][10]
