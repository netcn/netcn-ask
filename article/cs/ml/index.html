01什么是人工智能？它和通用人工智能、机器学习是什么关系？What is artificial intelligence, and how does it relate to general AI and machine learning?

AI 研究让机器完成类智能任务；当前多为狭义 AI，AGI 未实现；机器学习是从数据归纳模式的一条实现路径。AI studies machine tasks needing intelligence; today's systems are mostly narrow, AGI is unrealized, and machine learning induces patterns from data.

人工智能（AI）是研究如何让机器完成通常被认为需要人类智能的任务的领域。它覆盖感知、推理、学习、规划、语言处理和决策等一组能力；日常产品中说的 AI，多数是在特定任务上表现出类似智能行为的系统。判断一个系统是否属于 AI，要看它解决的问题和所用方法，而不是看界面上是否标注了“智能”二字。Artificial intelligence is the field of studying how machines can perform tasks usually thought to require human intelligence. It covers perception, reasoning, learning, planning, language processing and decision-making; most AI in everyday products is a system showing intelligent-like behavior on a specific task. Whether a system counts as AI depends on the problem it solves and the methods used, not on whether the interface labels it intelligent.[1]

需要区分狭义人工智能与通用人工智能。狭义 AI 面向单一或有限任务，如下棋、图像识别、语音转写、机器翻译、内容推荐，在划定范围内可以达到甚至超过人类水平。通用人工智能（AGI）指能够跨领域自主学习、迁移知识并处理陌生任务的系统，目前尚未实现。媒体上“AI 什么都能做”的说法，往往把狭义任务能力外推成了通用能力。Narrow AI and general AI must be separated. Narrow AI targets a single or limited task: playing a game, recognizing images, transcribing speech, translating text or recommending content, and can match or exceed humans within that scope. Artificial general intelligence, which learns across domains, transfers knowledge and handles unfamiliar tasks autonomously, has not been achieved. Claims that AI can do anything often extrapolate narrow task ability into general ability.[1][3]

实现路径主要有两条。符号主义路线把知识和推理规则显式编码进系统，适合规则清晰、可形式化的问题；机器学习路线不从人手写规则，而是从数据中归纳模式，再对新输入做预测。深度学习和大模型是机器学习当前的主流形式，通过多层表示学习从原始数据中提取特征；实际系统常常把规则、检索和学习模型组合在一起。There are two main implementation paths. The symbolic route encodes knowledge and reasoning rules explicitly, suiting clear, formalizable problems. The machine-learning route does not hand-write rules but induces patterns from data and predicts new inputs. Deep learning and large models are the current mainstream form of machine learning, extracting features from raw data through layered representation learning; real systems often combine rules, retrieval and learned models.[1][3]

AI 的能力边界来自它的工作方式。现代模型主要在训练数据的统计分布内做模式匹配和插值，并不真正理解语义或因果；面对训练分布之外的输入，表现可能明显下降，也会生成看似合理但与事实不符的内容。感知和语言任务可以做得很好，不等于系统具备意图、信念或对世界的完整认识。AI's limits follow from how it works. Modern models mainly match and interpolate patterns within the statistical distribution of training data, without truly understanding semantics or causation. Outside the training distribution performance can drop sharply, and the system can produce fluent but factually wrong content. Doing well on perception or language tasks does not mean the system has intentions, beliefs or a complete model of the world.[1][5]

边界：“智能”本身在心理学、哲学和工程中没有唯一定义，AI 的范围也随技术发展不断调整。演示demo 展示的是受控条件下的最佳表现，不能当作通用能力证明；判断一项 AI 能力应看任务定义、测试数据和失败案例，而不是宣传口径。把某一类模型的局限推广到所有 AI，或把某次成功推广到所有场景，都需要谨慎。The boundary: intelligence itself has no single definition across psychology, philosophy and engineering, and AI's scope keeps shifting with technology. A demo shows best behavior under controlled conditions, not proof of general ability; judging an AI capability should look at task definition, test data and failure cases rather than marketing. Generalizing one model's limits to all AI, or one success to every scenario, calls for caution.[3][5]

02机器学习怎样从数据中训练出一个可用的模型？How does machine learning train a usable model from data?

数据划分为训练、验证、测试集，模型在训练集优化损失、验证集调参，测试集衡量泛化；过拟合是记住细节而非规律。Data split into train, validation and test sets; the model optimizes loss on train, tunes on validation, and the test set measures generalization; overfitting memorizes details.

机器学习的基本流程可以分成数据、划分、训练、验证和测试几个环节。先采集并清洗数据，再按比例划分为训练集、验证集和测试集；模型在训练集上通过优化损失函数调整参数，在验证集上比较不同结构和超参数，最后用从未参与训练的测试集评估表现。测试集只在最终评估时使用，避免选型过程把测试信息泄漏进模型。The basic machine-learning pipeline splits into data, partitioning, training, validation and testing. Data are collected and cleaned, then divided into training, validation and test sets. The model adjusts parameters on the training set by optimizing a loss function, compares structures and hyperparameters on the validation set, and is finally evaluated on a test set never seen during training. The test set is used only for final evaluation so selection does not leak test information into the model.[2]

按学习方式，常见范式有监督学习、无监督学习和强化学习。监督学习使用带标签的样本学习输入到输出的映射，如分类和回归；无监督学习在没有标签的数据中寻找聚类或结构；强化学习通过与环境交互获得奖励反馈，逐步调整策略。不同范式的数据需求、反馈信号和评估方式不同，但都遵循“从样本中归纳规律、对新数据做判断”的基本思路。By learning paradigm, there are supervised, unsupervised and reinforcement learning. Supervised learning maps inputs to outputs using labeled samples, as in classification and regression; unsupervised learning finds clusters or structure without labels; reinforcement learning adjusts a policy through reward feedback from interacting with an environment. The paradigms differ in data needs, feedback signals and evaluation, but all induce regularities from samples and judge new data.[2][7]

过拟合是机器学习最典型的失败模式之一。模型把训练样本的细节和噪声也记住，训练集表现很好，遇到新数据却明显变差；泛化能力才是实际目标。缓解手段包括增加数据、降低模型复杂度、正则化、交叉验证和 early stopping；判断模型好坏不能只看训练分数，要看留出数据上的稳定表现。Overfitting is one of the most typical failure modes. The model memorizes details and noise of training samples, scores well on training data but performs poorly on new data; generalization is the actual goal. Remedies include more data, lower complexity, regularization, cross-validation and early stopping; model quality cannot be judged from training score alone but from stable performance on held-out data.[2][7]

与传统编程相比，机器学习改变了知识写入的方式。传统程序把规则和判断条件逐条写进代码；机器学习模型的参数从数据中学得，人提供的是数据、目标函数和模型结构，而不是每一条具体规则。表示学习让模型自动从原始输入中提取层次特征，减少了人工设计特征的工作量，但也使模型决策更难逐项追溯。Compared with traditional programming, machine learning changes how knowledge is written in. Traditional programs encode rules and conditions line by line; a learned model's parameters come from data, while people supply data, objective and model structure rather than each concrete rule. Representation learning extracts layered features from raw input automatically, reducing manual feature engineering, but also makes model decisions harder to trace item by item.[2][7]

边界：数据质量和代表性往往决定模型上限，偏差和缺失会被模型放大；测试集必须与训练数据保持隔离，否则评估结果偏乐观。不同任务范式的流程不能互相套用，强化学习的奖励设计、无监督学习的结构判断各有特殊问题。讨论一个模型时，应说明数据来源、划分方式和评估指标，而不是只报告单一准确率数字。The boundary: data quality and representativeness often set the model ceiling, and bias or missingness get amplified; the test set must stay isolated from training, or evaluation turns over-optimistic. Paradigm pipelines do not transfer to one another: reward design in reinforcement learning and structure detection in unsupervised learning have their own issues. Discussing a model should state data source, partition and metrics rather than report a single accuracy number.[2][8]

03AI 会取代哪些人的工作？哪些任务更难被自动化？Which jobs will AI replace, and which tasks are harder to automate?

规则明确、可量化的重复任务先被辅助；情境判断、共情与灵巧操作短期难替代；自动化先改变任务，岗位净效应受转型与制度影响。Clear, quantifiable repetitive tasks are assisted first; judgment, empathy and dexterity stay hard; automation changes tasks first, and net effects depend on transition.

自动化首先影响的是岗位中的任务，而不是整个职业。规则明确、重复程度高、容易编码和度量的工作环节，最先被软件或机器辅助甚至替代；数据录入、标准化分拣、常规质检和固定流程的报表处理都属于这一类。判断一个任务能否被自动化，要看规则是否清晰、反馈是否容易量化、是否需要处理非结构化情境，而不是看职业头衔本身。Automation first affects tasks within jobs, not whole occupations. Work steps with clear rules, high repetition and easy coding and measurement are the first to be assisted or replaced by software or machines: data entry, standardized sorting, routine inspection and fixed-flow report processing. Whether a task can be automated depends on rule clarity, quantifiable feedback and whether unstructured situations must be handled, not on the job title.[6]

短期内难以自动化的环节，集中在需要情境判断、社交共情和手工灵巧度的工作。面对模糊信息权衡多种目标、理解他人意图与情绪、在物理世界中灵活操作不规则物体，这些任务目前仍依赖人的经验和适应能力。AI 更多先作为工具进入这些岗位，改变任务分工和技能要求，而不是一次性把整个职业取消。Harder-to-automate work concentrates on contextual judgment, social empathy and manual dexterity. Weighing multiple goals under ambiguous information, reading others' intentions and emotions, and flexibly manipulating irregular objects in the physical world still rely on human experience and adaptation. AI mostly enters these jobs as a tool first, changing task division and skill requirements rather than canceling the occupation at once.[3][5]

技术史表明，自动化会改变岗位结构，也会创造新的岗位类型。旧任务减少的同时，系统维护、数据标注、流程设计和人机协作等新需求出现；净就业效应取决于技能转换速度、教育培训、制度安排和技术部署节奏。新岗位的技能要求与旧岗位并不自动对接，劳动者需要重新学习和适应。不同行业采用 AI 的速度差异很大，不能用统一时间表推断某个职业的存亡。Technology history shows automation reshapes job structure and creates new job types. As old tasks shrink, new needs appear in system maintenance, data labeling, process design and human-machine collaboration; the net employment effect depends on reskilling speed, education, institutions and deployment pace. Industries adopt AI at very different speeds, so a uniform timeline cannot predict whether an occupation survives.[5][6]

关于 AI 与就业的研究多为情景估计，预测区间相当宽。岗位结构受技术能力、成本、监管、消费者偏好和企业组织方式共同影响；斯坦福 HAI 的年度 AI 指数等跟踪报告也强调，采用率和岗位变化需要按行业、地区和技能分层观察。把研究中的自动化概率直接读成“某年某职业消失”，是对证据强度的过度解读。Research on AI and employment is mostly scenario estimation with fairly wide prediction intervals. Job structure depends on technical capability, cost, regulation, consumer preferences and firm organization; trackers such as Stanford HAI's annual AI Index also stress that adoption and job change must be observed by industry, region and skill level. Reading automation probabilities as "an occupation disappears in a certain year" overstates the evidence.[3][6]

边界：本节不预测具体年份谁会失业，也不判断某个职业整体安全。AI 工具可能先改变工作内容和技能组合，就业冲击在不同群体间分布不均，转型成本和不平等需要教育、培训和社会保障应对。讨论就业影响应区分任务、职业和行业三个层次，引用预测时注明假设和区间，而不是给出单点结论。The boundary: this section does not predict who loses work in which year, or judge an occupation as safe overall. AI tools may first change work content and skill mix, and employment shocks are unevenly distributed across groups; transition costs and inequality call for education, training and social protection. Employment discussion should separate task, occupation and industry, and cite assumptions and ranges instead of a single-point conclusion.[5][6]

04让象棋或围棋 AI 自己和自己下棋，会发生什么？What happens when you let a chess or Go AI play against itself?

自我对弈凭胜负反馈更新策略并逐步变强；AlphaGo 先学棋谱再对弈，AlphaZero 纯自我对弈提升水平。Self-play updates policy from win-loss feedback, and replayed experience strengthens it; AlphaGo pretrained on human games then self-played, while AlphaZero learns pure self-play.

让棋类 AI 自己和自己下棋，是强化学习自我对弈的典型方式。两个模型副本互相对局，根据胜负和局面变化更新策略；初始策略可能很弱，但随着对局积累，经验被反馈回模型，水平逐步提高。与依赖人类对局数据的训练相比，自我对弈可以持续产生新的局面和对手变化，不受现有棋谱水平的限制。Letting a game AI play against itself is a typical reinforcement-learning self-play setup. Two model copies compete, and the policy updates from wins, losses and position changes. The initial policy may be weak, but as games accumulate, experience feeds back into the model and strength rises gradually. Compared with training on human game records, self-play keeps generating new positions and opponent variations without being limited by existing master-level play.[2][4]

2016 年发表在 Nature 上的 AlphaGo 是这一路线的代表。它先用人类棋谱做监督预训练，再通过强化学习和自我对弈改进策略网络与价值网络，在对弈中选择胜率更高的着法；后续的 AlphaZero 进一步去掉人类棋谱，仅凭规则和自我对弈从随机水平训练到超越人类。论文记录了网络结构、对局机制和对弈结果，是验证自我对弈有效性的公开证据。AlphaGo, published in Nature in 2016, represents this route. It first pretrained on human games, then improved its policy and value networks through reinforcement learning and self-play, choosing moves with higher winning prospects. The later AlphaZero removed human records and trained from random play using only rules and self-play, reaching beyond human level. The paper reports network structure, self-play mechanism and match results, providing public evidence for self-play effectiveness.[4]

自我对弈之所以能突破人类棋谱的局限，在于它生成的局面和反馈规模远超人工整理的对局库。策略在与不同版本自己的较量中反复试错，发现人类棋手较少采用的着法；价值估计随对局数据更新，逐步学会判断复杂局面的优劣。这一过程把“下棋水平”转化为可优化的策略和评估函数，而不是单纯记忆已知对局。Self-play breaks the limits of human game records because the positions and feedback it generates far exceed curated databases. The policy experiments repeatedly against different versions of itself and finds moves human players rarely use; value estimates update with game data and gradually judge complex positions. This turns playing strength into optimizable policy and evaluation functions rather than memorizing known games.[4][7]

边界很明确：自我对弈成立的前提是规则固定、胜负可判定、局面状态可表示。棋类有清晰规则和终局判定，开放世界中的多目标、模糊反馈任务不能照搬这一框架。棋类水平高也不等于通用智能：模型在棋盘内优化胜率，并不因此获得跨领域推理能力。自我对弈的收敛速度和最终水平还受计算资源、奖励设计和搜索算法影响。The boundary is clear: self-play requires fixed rules, decidable wins and representable states. Board games have clear rules and terminal judgments; open-world tasks with multiple goals and fuzzy feedback cannot borrow the same framework. Strong board play is not general intelligence: the model optimizes win rate inside the board and does not thereby gain cross-domain reasoning. Convergence speed and final strength also depend on compute, reward design and search algorithm.[2][4]

边界还包括评估口径。棋类 AI 的强弱通常以固定规则下的Elo 等级分和标准赛事成绩衡量，不同版本、不同规则和时间限制之间不能直接比较。自我对弈产生的经验是否迁移到其他游戏，需要单独实验验证；把棋类 AI 的成功外推为“AI 能靠自我对局学会任何任务”，超出了论文和实验支持的范围。Evaluation scope adds another boundary. Board-game AI strength is usually measured by Elo rating under fixed rules and standard tournament results; versions, rules and time limits cannot be compared directly. Whether self-play experience transfers to other games needs separate experiments. Extrapolating board-game success to "AI can learn any task by self-play" goes beyond what the papers and experiments support.[4][7]

05AI 生成的图像为什么会呈现出那种样子？Why do AI-generated images look the way they do?

扩散模型从噪声按训练分布逐步去噪；瑕疵源于统计相关而非物理理解，数据分布、架构与采样共同决定观感。Diffusion models denoise from noise along the training distribution; flaws come from statistical correlation, not physical understanding, and data, architecture and sampling shape the look.

AI 生成的图像有自己一套可辨认的观感，主要来自生成模型的工作机制。当前主流扩散模型从纯高斯噪声出发，按训练数据上学到的分布一步步去噪，把随机噪声逐渐变成结构化图像；训练集中出现的物体、构图、色调和风格，决定了模型能生成什么内容。模型没有先验地见过某个具体场景，而是在学习大量样本后重建出符合统计规律的画面。AI-generated images have a recognizable look that mainly comes from how generative models work. Mainstream diffusion models start from pure Gaussian noise and denoise step by step according to the distribution learned from training data, turning random noise into structured images. Objects, compositions, colors and styles seen in training determine what the model can produce. It has not seen a specific scene beforehand; after learning many samples it rebuilds an image matching statistical regularities.[8]

除扩散模型外，图像生成还有对抗生成网络（GAN）和自回归生成等路径：GAN 用生成器与判别器相互对抗训练，自回归模型逐块或逐像素预测下一部分。不同架构在细节稳定性、采样速度和可控性上各有取舍；现代高质量图像模型以扩散路线最为常见，其去噪过程的数学描述可追溯到 Ho 等人 2020 年的扩散模型工作。Beyond diffusion, image generation includes GANs and autoregressive approaches: a GAN trains generator and discriminator against each other, while an autoregressive model predicts the next patch or pixel step by step. Architectures trade off detail stability, sampling speed and controllability; modern high-quality image models most often follow the diffusion route, whose denoising formulation traces to Ho et al.'s 2020 diffusion model work.[7][8]

AI 图像常见的瑕疵也与机制有关。手指数量和关节、文字拼写、物体透视和物理关系容易出错，因为模型学到的是图像之间的统计相关性，而不是人体解剖、文字规则或物理定律；采样过程带随机性，同一提示词会得到不同变体。画面局部看似合理，细节处却违反现实约束，正是统计模式匹配与真实理解之间差距的体现。Common AI-image flaws are mechanism-related. Finger count and joints, text spelling, perspective and physical relationships are error-prone because the model learns statistical correlations among images rather than anatomy, writing rules or physical laws. Sampling carries randomness, so one prompt yields different variants. Locally plausible images that violate real-world constraints in details show the gap between statistical pattern matching and genuine understanding.[5][8]

一张 AI 图像的观感由训练数据分布、模型架构和采样过程共同决定，并不代表系统理解了画面中的场景。训练数据的来源、版权归属和偏见会影响生成结果；生成图像不能直接当作事实证据，深度伪造和内容标识需要配套治理。把模型输出当作“相机记录”或客观事实，是对生成机制的误解。The look of an AI image is jointly shaped by training-data distribution, model architecture and sampling process; it does not mean the system understands the depicted scene. Data sources, copyright and bias affect outputs, and generated images cannot be treated as factual evidence; deepfakes and content labeling need accompanying governance. Taking model output as a camera record or objective fact misreads the generation mechanism.[5][8]

边界：图像质量随模型版本和训练规模持续进步，早期瑕疵不能一概套用到最新系统；不同任务的生成可控性差异很大。判断一张 AI 图是否可信，要结合来源、训练数据说明、是否经过编辑和具体用途，而不是只看清晰度。生成式模型的能力边界、版权责任和社会影响，需要结合技术细节与规范框架一起评估。The boundary: image quality keeps improving with model versions and training scale, and early flaws cannot be generalized to the latest systems; controllability differs greatly across tasks. Whether an AI image is trustworthy depends on source, training-data statement, editing and intended use, not sharpness alone. The capability limits, copyright responsibility and social impact of generative models should be assessed together with technical detail and regulatory frameworks.[5][7]
