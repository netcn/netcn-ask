01素数为什么有无穷多个，科赫雪花又为什么周长无限？Why are there infinitely many primes, and why does the Koch snowflake have infinite perimeter?

欧几里得用“乘积加一”不断造出新素数；科赫雪花每步把边长缩为 1/3、段数乘 4，周长发散而面积收敛。Euclid keeps producing a new prime from 'product plus one'; the Koch snowflake shrinks each segment to one third while quadrupling their number, so perimeter diverges and area converges.

结论先行：素数不是“多到数不完”这么简单，欧几里得的证明给出的是一个可重复的构造：任意给定有限个素数，总能再找到一个不在名单里的素数。科赫雪花则展示另一种“无限”：边界长度可以无限增长，围住的面积却收敛到一个有限值。两者其实都在提醒我们，“无限”不是一个大数，而是对过程或集合的描述。Start with the conclusion: the infinitude of primes is not just “too many to count”. Euclid's proof gives a repeatable construction: given any finite list of primes, one can always find another prime outside the list. The Koch snowflake shows a different kind of infinity: its boundary length can grow without bound while the area it encloses converges to a finite value. Both remind us that “infinity” is not a very large number but a description of a process or a set.[1][3]

设已知素数只有 p₁、p₂、…、pₙ。令 N = p₁p₂…pₙ + 1。N 比 1 大，所以必有素因子 q。q 不可能是名单里的任何一个 pᵢ，因为 N 除以 pᵢ 余 1，而 pᵢ 整除乘积。于是 q 是新素数，与“名单已包含全部素数”矛盾。欧几里得原文用线段和“测尽”的语言，但核心就是这个“乘积加一”。注意 N 本身不一定是素数：2×3×5×7+1=211 是素数，而 2×3×5×7×11×13+1=30031=59×509；证明只需要 N 的某个素因子。Suppose the only known primes are p₁, p₂, …, pₙ. Let N = p₁p₂…pₙ + 1. Since N is greater than 1, it has a prime factor q. q cannot be any pᵢ on the list: N leaves remainder 1 when divided by pᵢ, while pᵢ divides the product. So q is a new prime, contradicting the claim that the list contains every prime. Euclid's original text speaks in terms of line segments and “measuring”, but the core is this “product plus one”.[1]

科赫雪花从等边三角形开始。每一步把每条边的中间三分之一换成两个同样长的新边，形成一个小凸起。第 n 步后每条原边变成 4ⁿ 段，每段长度是原来的 1/3ⁿ；总边数是 3·4ⁿ，周长 Pₙ = 3s(4/3)ⁿ。因为 4/3 > 1，n 增大时 Pₙ 无界，极限是无穷。The Koch snowflake starts with an equilateral triangle. At each step, the middle third of every side is replaced by two new segments of the same length, forming a small bump. After n steps, each original side has become 4ⁿ segments, each of length 1/3ⁿ of the original; the total number of sides is 3·4ⁿ, and the perimeter is Pₙ = 3s(4/3)ⁿ. Because 4/3 > 1, Pₙ grows without bound as n increases, and its limit is infinite.[2]

面积却另有算法。每一步新增的小三角形数量按 4 倍增长，单个面积按 1/9 缩小，新增总面积构成等比级数，公比 4/9 < 1，所以总和收敛。若初始三角形面积为 A₀，极限面积是 A₀ + (A₀/3)(1 + 4/9 + …) = 8A₀/5。边界长度发散，面积却只有原来的 1.6 倍；科赫曲线的分形维数是 log4/log3 ≈ 1.262。The area follows a different rule. At each step the number of new small triangles grows by a factor of 4, while each individual area shrinks by a factor of 9, so the added area forms a geometric series with ratio 4/9 < 1 and converges. If the initial triangle has area A₀, the limiting area is A₀ + (A₀/3)(1 + 4/9 + …) = 8A₀/5. The boundary length diverges, yet the area is only 1.6 times the original; the fractal dimension of the Koch curve is log4/log3 ≈ 1.262.[2]

这里的“无限”至少有两种用法。素数集合是无限的，但可以按顺序一个一个数出来，属于可数无限；科赫边界的“长度无限”说的是欧氏长度这个量没有上界，不是边界点数比自然数多。把它当成“某个很大的数”会失去重点：证明和极限描述的都是“无论走多远，下一步仍然存在、仍然更长”。分形边界还说明，长度不是描述形状的唯一尺度。“Infinity” is used here in at least two ways. The set of primes is infinite, but its elements can be listed one after another; it is countably infinite. The Koch boundary's “infinite length” says that its Euclidean length has no upper bound, not that it has more points than the natural numbers. Treating it as “some very large number” misses the point: both the proof and the limit describe a process that never reaches a final step. The fractal boundary also shows that length is not the only measure of a shape.[2][3]

02把 37 的倍数轮换数字，为什么仍然能被 37 整除？有限域里为什么不能除以零？Why does rotating the digits of a multiple of 37 preserve divisibility, and why can't one divide by zero in a finite field?

37 的轮换不变性来自 1000≡1 (mod 37) 与 999=27×37；有限域中 0 没有倒数。The 37 rotation property follows from 1000≡1 (mod 37) and 999=27×37; a finite field excludes zero from its multiplicative group, so zero has no inverse.

37 的这个性质不是巧合。三位数 abc 可以写成 100a+10b+c；轮换后是 100b+10c+a。因为 999 = 27×37，所以 1000 ≡ 1 (mod 37)。于是 10N 与轮换数 R 只差 999a，而 999a 一定是 37 的倍数；若 37 | N，则 37 | 10N，也就能推出 37 | R。The 37 property is not a coincidence. A three-digit number abc can be written as 100a+10b+c; after rotation it becomes 100b+10c+a. Because 999 = 27×37, we have 1000 ≡ 1 (mod 37). Thus 10N and the rotated number R differ by 999a, which is certainly a multiple of 37; if 37 | N, then 37 | 10N, and it follows that 37 | R.[5][11]

用例子看更清楚：259 = 37×7，轮换成 592 = 37×16；148 = 37×4，轮换成 481 = 37×13。只要原数确实是三位数，轮换不改变“是否被 37 整除”。如果轮换后出现前导零，比如 370→703，仍然成立；但 037 不是三位数，不能直接套。Examples make this clearer: 259 = 37×7 rotates to 592 = 37×16; 148 = 37×4 rotates to 481 = 37×13. As long as the original number really has three digits, rotation does not change whether it is divisible by 37. If the rotation produces a leading zero, as in 370→703, the property still holds; but 037 is not a three-digit number and cannot be used directly.[5][11]

这套推理的关键是模运算：10N−R = 999a，而 999 含因子 37。它不需要知道 a、b、c 的具体值，只需要知道 1000 与 1 同余。换成别的除数，比如 7 或 11，条件会变；37 的特殊性来自 999 的分解，不是数字本身有魔力。37×3=111、37×27=999，111 到 999 的九个三位重数也都是 37 的倍数；轮换性质只是这条同余链的一个分支。The key is modular arithmetic: 10N−R = 999a, and 999 contains the factor 37. The argument does not need the individual values of a, b and c; it only needs 1000 ≡ 1. Change the divisor to 7 or 11 and the conditions change; the special role of 37 comes from the factorization of 999, not from magic in the digits.[5]

再看“有限的数域”。有限域（finite field，也叫伽罗瓦域）是一个只有有限个元素、但加、减、乘、除（除数非零）都封闭的代数结构。F₅ = {0,1,2,3,4}，模 5 运算；非零元素 1、2、3、4 都有倒数：1↔1，2↔3，3↔2，4↔4。0 不在乘法群里，所以没有倒数。这五个元素已经足够展示域的公理，不需要把数扩展到无穷。Now consider a “finite field”. A finite field (also called a Galois field) is an algebraic structure with finitely many elements in which addition, subtraction, multiplication and division by nonzero elements are all closed. F₅ = {0,1,2,3,4} with arithmetic modulo 5; every nonzero element 1, 2, 3, 4 has an inverse: 1↔1, 2↔3, 3↔2, 4↔4. Zero is not in the multiplicative group, so it has no inverse.[4][10]

为什么 0 不能有倒数？假设 0 的倒数是 x，则 0·x = 1。但在任何环里 0·x = 0（因为 0·x = (0+0)·x = 0·x+0·x，移项得 0·x = 0），于是 0 = 1，这与域至少有两个元素矛盾。所以“除以 0”不是得到一个很大的数，而是根本没有满足 0·x=1 的 x。反过来，Z/6Z 这样的环里有 2·3 = 0，但 2、3 都不是 0，这不是域；有限域排除了这种零因子。计算器遇到 1/0 报错，而不是给出一个数，也是同一个道理。Why can zero not have an inverse? Suppose the inverse of 0 were x, so 0·x = 1. But in any ring 0·x = 0 (because 0·x = (0+0)·x = 0·x+0·x, and subtracting 0·x gives 0·x = 0), so 0 = 1, contradicting the fact that a field has at least two elements. Therefore “division by zero” does not produce a very large number; there is simply no x satisfying 0·x=1. Conversely, a ring such as Z/6Z has 2·3 = 0 with neither factor zero; that is not a field, and finite fields exclude such zero divisors.[4][10]

03蝴蝶效应和蝴蝶有什么关系？一杯水里的分子，为什么也在讲“尺度”？What does the butterfly effect have to do with a butterfly, and why are the molecules in a glass of water also about scale?

蝴蝶效应是微小初值误差被指数放大的比喻；指定水分子几乎不可能，至少共享一个却接近必然。The butterfly effect is a metaphor for tiny initial errors amplified exponentially; the huge contrast between molecules in a glass and on Earth makes one specified molecule nearly

蝴蝶效应不是“蝴蝶扇翅膀直接造出龙卷风”。洛伦茨 1963 年研究大气对流方程时发现，初始条件的极小差异会让两条轨迹很快分开；1972 年的演讲把它概括成“巴西一只蝴蝶扇动翅膀，会不会在得克萨斯引发龙卷风”。蝴蝶只是微小扰动的比喻，不是因果链上的那只手。The butterfly effect is not “a butterfly's wingbeat directly creates a tornado”. When Lorenz studied equations of atmospheric convection in 1963, he found that extremely small differences in initial conditions could make two trajectories diverge quickly; his 1972 talk summarized this as “does the flap of a butterfly's wings in Brazil set off a tornado in Texas?” The butterfly is a metaphor for a tiny perturbation, not a hand in the causal chain.[6][7]

混沌系统的方程仍是确定的，没有随机项；问题在于误差的指数放大。若初始误差是 δ₀，线性化后 |δ(t)| ≈ |δ₀|·exp(λt)，λ 为正的 Lyapunov 指数。误差每固定一段时间翻一倍，预报时效就少一截；中等尺度天气的误差大约每 3 天翻一倍，业务天气预报因此用集合预报，跑许多个略有差异的初值，用概率而不是单条轨迹说话。洛伦茨吸引子给出一个有界的相空间结构，但其中的轨迹对初值极其敏感，不能靠一条线预测终点。The equations of a chaotic system are still deterministic, with no random term; the problem is the exponential growth of error. If the initial error is δ₀, linearization gives |δ(t)| ≈ |δ₀|·exp(λt), where λ is a positive Lyapunov exponent. Every time the error doubles, the useful forecast horizon shrinks; operational weather forecasting therefore uses ensembles, running many slightly different initial states and speaking in probabilities rather than a single trajectory.[6][7]

另一件看似无关的事是“喝过同一杯水”。地球水总量约 1.386×10⁹ km³，即 1.386×10²¹ L；按 18 g/mol 估算，约 4.6×10⁴⁶ 个水分子。一杯 250 mL 水约 8.3×10²⁴ 个分子；指定一个分子，它在某一杯里的概率约 1.8×10⁻²²，几乎可以忽略。Another apparently unrelated question is “have I drunk the same water twice?”. Earth holds about 1.386×10⁹ km³ of water, or 1.386×10²¹ L; at 18 g/mol that is about 4.6×10⁴⁶ water molecules. A 250 mL glass contains about 8.3×10²⁴ molecules; for one specified molecule, the chance that it is in a particular glass is about 1.8×10⁻²², essentially negligible.[8][9]

但问“两杯水有没有至少一个共同分子”，就是生日问题：把 n 个分子放进 m 个位置，期望重叠约 n²/m；这里 n = 8.3×10²⁴，m = 4.6×10⁴⁶，n²/m ≈ 1500，几乎必然有重叠。更准确地说，P(至少一个) ≈ 1−exp(−n²/2m)，指数项小到可以忽略。于是“任意指定分子”几乎不可能，“至少共享一个”却接近 100%。But asking whether two glasses share at least one molecule is a birthday problem: placing n molecules into m slots gives an expected overlap of about n²/m; here n = 8.3×10²⁴ and m = 4.6×10⁴⁶, so n²/m ≈ 1500, and an overlap is almost certain. More precisely, P(at least one) ≈ 1−exp(−n²/2m), and the exponential term is negligible. Thus “one specified molecule” is almost impossible, while “at least one shared molecule” is close to 100%.[8][9]

边界在于“同一个分子”并不是一个完好的身份。水分子会解离、质子交换，氢键不断重组；环境里还有同位素差异。长时间尺度上，说“某个氢原子曾经在某杯水里”比说“整个分子原样重逢”更合适。蝴蝶效应的比喻也一样：它说明长期精确预报受限，不等于天气没有规律，更不等于一次微小动作必然导致某个特定的结果。The boundary is that “the same molecule” is not a durable identity. Water molecules dissociate, exchange protons and constantly rearrange hydrogen bonds; isotopes add further variation. On long time scales it is more accurate to say “some hydrogen atom was once in that glass” than “the whole molecule was reunited”. The butterfly effect is similar: it shows that long-range precise prediction is limited, not that weather is lawless, and not that one tiny action must cause one particular outcome.[6][9]
