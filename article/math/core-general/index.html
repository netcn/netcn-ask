01为什么乘以 0 得 0，除以 0 却不允许？Why does multiplication by zero give zero, while division by zero is not allowed?

分配律推出 a·0=0；除以 0 时非零数无解、0/0 不唯一，强行赋值会破坏消去律。The distributive law gives a·0=0; division by zero has no solution for nonzero a and is non-unique for 0/0, and forcing a value breaks cancellation.

任何数乘以 0 都得 0，这是算术公理的直接推论，而不是一条人为规定。利用加法分配律：a·0 = a·(0+0) = a·0 + a·0；两边同时减去 a·0，左边剩下 0，右边剩下 0，于是 a·0 = 0。这个推导对实数、整数和有理数都成立，0 与任何数相乘都把乘积压到加法单位元上。理解了这一点，再看除以 0 为什么不允许，就会顺理成章。Multiplying any number by zero gives zero, a direct consequence of arithmetic axioms rather than an arbitrary rule. Using the distributive law: a·0 = a·(0+0) = a·0 + a·0; subtracting a·0 from both sides leaves 0 = a·0. This derivation holds for real numbers, integers and rationals: multiplication by zero collapses the product to the additive identity. With that established, why division by zero is forbidden follows naturally.[1]

除法是乘法的逆运算：a ÷ b = x 的意思是找到唯一的 x，使 b·x = a。令 b = 0，先看 a ≠ 0 的情形：需要 0·x = a，但上一段已经证明 0·x = 0，不可能等于非零的 a，因此这样的 x 不存在，商无解。再看 0 ÷ 0：需要 0·x = 0，而任何 x 都满足这个等式，x 不唯一，无法指定一个确定的商。两种情况都不能给出唯一、确定的结果，所以标准算术中把除以 0 留作无定义。Division is the inverse of multiplication: a ÷ b = x means finding the unique x such that b·x = a. Set b = 0. When a ≠ 0, we need 0·x = a, but 0·x = 0 as shown above and cannot equal a nonzero a, so no such x exists and the quotient has no solution. For 0/0, we need 0·x = 0, and every x satisfies it, so x is not unique and no definite quotient can be assigned. Neither case yields a unique, determinate result, which is why standard arithmetic leaves division by zero undefined.[1]

如果强行给 0/0 赋一个值，算术定律会立刻出现矛盾。假设规定 0/0 = 1，那么一方面 2·0/0 = 2·1 = 2，另一方面 2·0 = 0，于是 2·0/0 = 0/0 = 1，推出 2 = 1。同理，给非零数除以 0 规定一个无穷大符号，也会破坏加法、乘法的封闭性和消去律。数学选择保持运算体系自洽，而不是为了让表达式“看起来完整”去牺牲基本定律。Forcing a value onto 0/0 breaks arithmetic laws immediately. Suppose 0/0 = 1: then 2·0/0 = 2·1 = 2, but 2·0 = 0, so 2·0/0 = 0/0 = 1, yielding 2 = 1. Likewise assigning an infinity symbol to a nonzero number divided by zero destroys closure and cancellation in addition and multiplication. Mathematics keeps the system self-consistent rather than sacrificing basic laws so expressions look complete.[1][6]

不同数学结构对除法的处理并不相同。在模运算中，除以一个数等价于乘以它的乘法逆元；与模数互质的数才有逆元，否则同样不能相除。在极限语境里，0/0 被称为不定式：分子分母同时趋于 0 的分式可以通过取极限得到有限值、无穷大或再次发散，但这是两个函数的变化趋势之比，并不是在实数中真正除以了 0。非标准分析中的无穷小量是另一套形式化对象，也不等于普通实数 0。Different mathematical structures handle division differently. In modular arithmetic, dividing by a number means multiplying by its multiplicative inverse; only numbers coprime to the modulus have an inverse, and otherwise division is equally impossible. In limits, 0/0 is an indeterminate form: a fraction whose numerator and denominator both tend to zero can have a finite limit, diverge to infinity, or diverge again, but this compares the trends of two functions rather than truly dividing by zero in the reals. Infinitesimals in nonstandard analysis are a separate formal object, not the ordinary real zero.[1][7]

边界：无定义是标准实数算术下的结论，不能把“除以 0 无意义”推广到所有代数系统。不定式有确定极限需要具体函数和趋近方式，不能凭直觉写 1/0 = ∞；无穷大在不同结构中含义不同。讨论除法时应先说明数系和运算规则，再判断某个商是否存在、是否唯一。The boundary: undefined is a conclusion within standard real arithmetic and cannot be generalized to every algebraic system. An indeterminate form has a definite limit only for specific functions and approach paths; one cannot intuitively write 1/0 = ∞, and infinity means different things in different structures. Discussing division should first state the number system and rules before judging whether a quotient exists and is unique.[6][7]

02蒙提霍尔问题：换门中奖率真的更高吗？The Monty Hall problem: does switching doors really raise the win probability?

主持人知道位置并必开羊门时，换门 2/3、不换 1/3；随机开门则两门各 1/2，规则前提决定结论。When the host knows and must open a goat door, switching wins 2/3 versus 1/3; random opening leaves 1/2 each, so rules determine the result.

蒙提霍尔问题的结论是：换门把中奖概率从 1/3 提高到 2/3，不换则保持 1/3。节目有三扇门，一辆车藏在其中一扇后，另外两扇后是羊；参赛者先选一扇，主持人知道车的位置，再从剩下两扇中打开一扇有羊的门，然后问参赛者是否换到最后那扇未开的门。直觉上剩下两扇门似乎各占一半，但概率并不对称：主持人的开门动作携带了信息。The Monty Hall conclusion: switching doors raises the win probability from 1/3 to 2/3, while staying keeps 1/3. Three doors hide a car behind one and goats behind the other two. The contestant picks one; the host, who knows where the car is, opens another door with a goat, and asks whether to switch to the last unopened door. Intuition says the two remaining doors should be even, but the probabilities are not symmetric: the host's action carries information.[2]

可以用枚举验证。不失一般性，假设车在 1 号门后。参赛者最初选 1 号，主持人打开 2 或 3 号，换门后得到羊；最初选 2 号，主持人只能打开 3 号，换门得到车；最初选 3 号，主持人打开 2 号，换门得到车。三种等可能的初始选择中，换门赢下两种，概率 2/3。换个角度：初始选中车的概率是 1/3，车在另外两扇门后的合计概率是 2/3；主持人排除其中一只羊后，剩下那扇门继承了原来两扇门合计的 2/3 概率。Enumeration verifies it. Without loss of generality the car is behind door 1. If the contestant first picks door 1, the host opens 2 or 3 and switching loses; picking door 2, the host must open 3 and switching wins; picking door 3, the host opens 2 and switching wins. Of three equally likely initial picks, switching wins two, probability 2/3. Equivalently, the initial pick has 1/3 chance of the car, and the other two doors together hold 2/3; after the host removes one goat, the remaining door inherits that combined 2/3.[2]

结论成立依赖一条关键规则：主持人必须开门，而且他知道车在哪、只会打开有羊的门。如果主持人只是随机推开剩下两扇中的一扇，碰巧露出羊，那么条件概率会变成另一回事：在“露出的是羊”这一条件下，参赛者原门与剩余门各为 1/2。也就是说，是主持人的知识和固定策略把概率集中到了未换的门上；随机开门不提供这种信息，换门不再有优势。The result depends on a key rule: the host must open a door, knows where the car is, and opens only a goat door. If the host instead opens one of the two remaining doors at random and happens to show a goat, the conditional probability changes: given that the revealed door has a goat, the original door and the remaining door are each 1/2. The host's knowledge and fixed strategy concentrate probability on the unswitched door; random opening supplies no such information, and switching gains no advantage.[2][7]

这道题反直觉，是因为人们容易把“剩下两个选项”当成“两个等概率选项”。概率取决于每个选项背后的条件信息，而不是选项的个数。类似的直觉偏差在抽卡、抽签和贝叶斯更新中反复出现：已知条件改变后，旧概率需要按条件重新分配，不能直接平均到剩余选项上。The puzzle feels counterintuitive because people treat two remaining options as two equally likely options. Probability depends on the conditional information behind each option, not on the number of options. The same bias recurs in draws, lotteries and Bayesian updating: after conditions change, old probabilities must be redistributed conditionally rather than averaged evenly over remaining options.[2][3]

边界：2/3 是大量重复试验下的频率倾向，单局比赛仍可能选错，概率不保证单次结果。结论严格对应题目规则：主持人是否总开门、是否总给换门机会、参赛者选择顺序都会改变概率计算。把节目策略换成随机开门、多辆车或多扇门，需要重新建模，不能直接套用 2/3 这个数字。The boundary: 2/3 is a frequency tendency over many repeated trials; a single round can still be wrong, and probability does not guarantee one outcome. The conclusion strictly matches the stated rules: whether the host always opens, always offers the switch, and the order of picks all change the calculation. Random opening, multiple cars or more doors require a new model; the 2/3 figure cannot be carried over directly.[2][7]

03为什么杯子水桶是圆的，蜂巢却是六边形？Why are cups and buckets round, while honeycombs use hexagons?

圆在等周条件下面积最大、应力均匀；六边形在等面积密铺中总周长最小，目标不同最优形状不同。The circle maximizes area at fixed perimeter with even stress; the hexagon minimizes total perimeter in equal-area tiling, and goals differ.

杯子、水桶做成圆形，首先是因为圆形截面在工程上最省事。相同周长下，圆围成的面积最大，这是等周问题的结论；圆形容器用料省、容量大，内壁没有棱角，应力分布均匀，也容易倾倒、刷洗和密封。圆形截面在受压时把力沿周向均匀分散，不像方形容器那样在转角处出现应力集中，这也是储罐、管道和锅具多取圆形的原因。Cups and buckets are round first because a circular cross-section is the most economical engineering shape. Among plane figures with a fixed perimeter, the circle encloses the largest area, the isoperimetric result; round vessels use little material for large capacity, have no sharp corners, distribute stress evenly, and are easy to pour from, clean and seal. Under pressure a round section spreads force around the circumference instead of concentrating it at corners, which is why tanks, pipes and pans are mostly round.[3]

车轮做成圆形，利用的是圆上各点到圆心距离相等这一性质。圆心就是车轴位置，轮子滚动时轴到地面的距离始终等于半径，车身高度保持不变，不会随转动上下颠簸。若换成方形或多边形，顶点与边到中心的距离交替变化，车轴会周期性起落，滚动变成一连串磕碰。圆形把“滚动”转化为平稳的平移，这是其他形状难以同时兼顾的。Wheels are circles because every point on a circle is the same distance from its center. The center is the axle position; as the wheel rolls, axle-to-ground distance stays equal to the radius, so the body height does not rise and fall. A square or polygon alternates between vertices and edges at different distances from the center, making the axle rise and fall periodically and turning rolling into a series of bumps. The circle converts rolling into smooth translation, which other shapes cannot match as easily.[3]

平面密铺中，正六边形是另一种效率最优的形状。正三角形、正方形和正六边形都能不留空隙地铺满平面；在所有等面积格子的平面分割里，六边形分割的总周长最小，这就是 1999 年由 Hales 证明的蜂窝猜想。蜂巢用最少的蜂蜡围成等面积巢室，正是这一效率的体现；六边形内角 120°，相邻格子共用边，单位面积边界长度低于三角形和正方形方案。In plane tiling, the regular hexagon is another efficiency-optimal shape. Equilateral triangles, squares and regular hexagons all tile the plane without gaps; among planar partitions into equal-area cells, the hexagonal partition has the smallest total perimeter, the honeycomb conjecture proved by Hales in 1999. Honeycombs enclose equal-area cells with the least wax, exactly this efficiency: hexagon angles are 120°, neighboring cells share edges, and boundary length per unit area is lower than triangle or square schemes.[4]

形状选择从来不是单目标优化。容器除了容量和应力，还要考虑堆放、堆叠、握持和包装；圆形桶容易滚动，方形箱更便于码放。车轮除了平稳滚动，还要配合路面、承重和转向。所谓“最有效”必须先说明目标：是周长对面积、应力分布、滚动平稳，还是空间利用率；目标不同，最优形状也不同。Shape choice is never a single-objective optimization. Besides capacity and stress, vessels must stack, grip and package; round barrels roll easily while square boxes pack better. Wheels must match road surface, load and steering. Most efficient must first specify the target: perimeter-to-area, stress distribution, rolling smoothness or space utilization; different targets give different optimal shapes.[3][4]

边界：等周结论针对平面固定周长下的面积最大；三维容器的最优形状还受开口、壁厚和制造工艺约束。蜂窝猜想证明的是理想平面分割，真实蜂巢受蜂群行为、巢础和材料强度影响。把某一种形状的优势推广到所有用途并不稳妥，工程选型要把力学、工艺和使用场景放在一起权衡。The boundary: the isoperimetric result concerns maximum area for a fixed planar perimeter; three-dimensional optimal shapes also depend on opening, wall thickness and manufacturing. The honeycomb conjecture proves an ideal planar partition, while real combs are shaped by bee behavior, foundation and material strength. One shape's advantage cannot be generalized to every use; engineering selection weighs mechanics, process and application together.[4][5]

04黄金比例为什么会出现在自然界？哪些对应是真的？Why does the golden ratio appear in nature, and which correspondences are real?

φ=(1+√5)/2，斐波那契相邻比值收敛于它；叶序角度有机制解释，贝壳人脸等比例说法多为附会。φ=(1+√5)/2 and Fibonacci ratios converge to it; phyllotaxis angles have a mechanism, while shell and face ratio claims are often overstated.

黄金比例 φ 是一个确定的代数数：φ = (1+√5)/2 ≈ 1.6180339887，它满足方程 φ² = φ + 1。把一条线段分成两段，使整段与较长段之比等于较长段与较短段之比，这个比值就是 φ。黄金矩形切去一个以短边为边长的正方形后，剩下的矩形与原矩形相似；正五边形的对角线与边长之比同样等于 φ。这些几何性质可以严格推导，不依赖审美判断。The golden ratio φ is a definite algebraic number: φ = (1+√5)/2 ≈ 1.6180339887, satisfying φ² = φ + 1. Split a segment so the whole-to-longer ratio equals longer-to-shorter; that ratio is φ. Cutting a golden rectangle into a square on its short side leaves a rectangle similar to the original, and the diagonal-to-side ratio of a regular pentagon is also φ. These geometric properties are derivable rigorously, independent of taste.[5]

斐波那契数列与 φ 的联系是可验证的极限关系。数列从 1, 1 开始，每项等于前两项之和：1, 1, 2, 3, 5, 8, 13, 21……相邻两项之比依次为 1/1=1，2/1=2，3/2=1.5，5/3≈1.667，8/5=1.6，13/8=1.625，21/13≈1.615，随项数增加趋向 φ。也就是说，斐波那契数是逼近 φ 的离散序列，而不是 φ 先验地决定了数列。The link with the Fibonacci sequence is a verifiable limit relation. Starting 1, 1, each term is the sum of the previous two: 1, 1, 2, 3, 5, 8, 13, 21... Consecutive ratios run 1/1=1, 2/1=2, 3/2=1.5, 5/3≈1.667, 8/5=1.6, 13/8=1.625, 21/13≈1.615, approaching φ as terms grow. Fibonacci numbers are a discrete sequence approximating φ, not something φ dictated in advance.[5]

植物叶序中确实能看到与斐波那契数相关的排列。叶片围绕茎秆的夹角常接近 137.5°，即 360°(1−1/φ)，这种排列让叶片尽量少互相遮挡，种子在花盘上以螺旋方式填充时也对应相邻斐波那契数。但需要区分：叶序角度有生长机制和效率解释，而“贝壳、人脸、名画比例符合黄金比例”的大量说法缺乏稳定证据，测量方式和选样往往事后调整到接近 1.618。Plant phyllotaxis does show arrangements tied to Fibonacci numbers. The angle between successive leaves on a stem is often near 137.5°, or 360°(1−1/φ), so leaves shade one another as little as possible, and seeds fill a disk in spirals matching adjacent Fibonacci numbers. But a distinction is needed: phyllotaxis angles have growth and efficiency explanations, while many claims that shells, faces or famous paintings follow the golden ratio lack stable evidence; measurement and sampling are often adjusted afterward toward 1.618.[3][5]

φ 本身是无理数，不能写成两个整数之比；它与美学偏好之间的关联属于经验和文化议题，不是数学定理。数学能证明的是 φ 的代数性质、几何作图和数列收敛，不能证明某张脸、某座建筑因此更美。把自然界中的螺旋、分节和比例一律归因于黄金比例，属于把相关现象套进一个固定数字。φ itself is irrational and cannot be written as a ratio of integers; its link with aesthetic preference is an empirical and cultural question, not a theorem. Mathematics can prove φ's algebraic properties, geometric constructions and sequence convergence, but cannot prove that a face or building is therefore more beautiful. Attributing every natural spiral, segment and proportion to the golden ratio means forcing correlated phenomena into one fixed number.[5][6]

边界：数列比值趋向 φ 的收敛速度和误差可以按斐波那契通项估计；自然现象是否对应 φ，需要测量数据和机制模型，不能只看外观相似。不同文化和历史时期的审美比例并不统一，黄金比例的流行部分来自后世附会。讨论自然中的黄金比例时，应先给出测量定义和样本范围，再判断相关性是否成立。The boundary: convergence speed and error of sequence ratios toward φ can be estimated from the Fibonacci closed form; whether a natural phenomenon matches φ needs measurement data and a mechanism model, not visual resemblance alone. Aesthetic proportions vary across cultures and periods, and the golden ratio's popularity partly comes from later reinterpretation. Discussing golden ratio in nature should first define measurement and sample range before judging whether the correlation holds.[3][5]

05π 在别的进制下能写成有理数吗？无穷到底是多大的数？Can pi be rational in another base, and how large a number is infinity?

无理性是数的性质，与进制无关；无穷不是实数，按基数分为可数与不可数，发散序列不等于无穷集合。Irrationality is a number property, base-independent; infinity is not a real number, split into countable and uncountable, and a divergent sequence is not an infinite set.

π 是无理数，这一属性与使用哪种进制无关。无理数指不能写成两个整数之比 p/q 的数；进制只是记数符号的选择，十进制、二进制或十六进制改变的是数字写法，不改变数本身。π 在十进制下小数位不循环、不终止，在任何有理数进制下同样不会循环终止。一个有理数 p/q 在进制 b 下是否有限或循环，取决于分母与 b 的素因子关系；无理数在所有进制中都不具备这种表示。Pi is irrational, and this property does not depend on the number base used. An irrational number cannot be written as a ratio of integers p/q; a base is only a choice of numeral notation, and decimal, binary or hexadecimal change the written form, not the number itself. Pi's digits neither repeat nor terminate in decimal, and they do not repeat or terminate in any rational base. Whether a rational p/q has a finite or repeating expansion in base b depends on the prime factors of the denominator and b; an irrational has no such representation in any base.[6]

数学史上证明 π 无理经历了较长过程：18 世纪 Lambert 证明 π 不是有理数，1882 年 Lindemann 进一步证明 π 是超越数，即不是任何整系数多项式的根。把“π 在十进制里无限不循环”误解成“换一种进制就能写成分数”，混淆了数的性质与记数形式。同理，√2 的无理性也不随进制改变；进制换算只重写表示，不把无理数变成有理数。Proving pi irrational took centuries: Lambert showed in the 18th century that pi is not rational, and Lindemann proved in 1882 that pi is transcendental, the root of no polynomial with integer coefficients. Misreading pi's infinite nonrepeating decimal as a fraction in another base confuses number properties with notation. The irrationality of sqrt(2) likewise does not change with base; conversion rewrites the representation and does not turn an irrational into a rational.[6][7]

无穷不是一个可以直接放进实数轴的普通大小。自然数 1, 2, 3……构成可数无穷集合；整数和有理数同样可数，可以按某种顺序一一列出。实数集合不可数，Cantor 对角线论证说明无法把全体实数与自然数一一对应；这两类无穷的基数不同，记为 ℵ₀ 与连续统基数。“无限大的空间”在数学中要由集合、度量和基数来描述，而不是当成一个最大的数。Infinity is not an ordinary size that can be placed on the real number line. The natural numbers 1, 2, 3... form a countably infinite set; integers and rationals are countable too, listable one-to-one in some order. The real numbers are uncountable: Cantor's diagonal argument shows they cannot be paired one-to-one with the naturals, and these two infinities have different cardinalities, ℵ₀ and the continuum cardinality. An infinitely large space in mathematics must be described by sets, metrics and cardinality, not treated as a largest number.[7][8]

日常语言里的“越来越大”和数学中的无穷有区别。趋于无穷的序列是在有限项内不断增大、上不封顶，序列本身仍是有限项的延伸；无穷集合则是整体对象，其大小由基数刻画。把发散过程说成“等于无穷”，在严格表述中需要区分极限状态和集合本身。π 的小数位无限延伸，也不等于它的数字序列已经被穷尽或包含任意指定信息。Everyday "getting larger and larger" differs from mathematical infinity. A sequence diverging to infinity keeps growing without ceiling over finite terms, and the sequence itself is still an extension of finite terms; an infinite set is a whole object whose size is described by cardinality. Saying a divergent process equals infinity needs a strict distinction between limiting state and the set itself. Pi's decimal digits extend infinitely, but that does not mean the digit sequence has been exhausted or encodes any specified information.[7]

边界：无理与超越是不同强度的结论，π 超越意味着比无理更强；进制分析只在有理数基底下讨论，非整数底数的表示规则另有定义。无穷的层级在集合论公理下展开，不同公理体系对连续统基数的判断可以不同。讨论“无限”和“无理”时，应先明确是在说表示形式、数值性质还是集合规模，不能用日常直觉替代定义。The boundary: irrational and transcendental are conclusions of different strength; pi being transcendental is stronger than being irrational. Base analysis applies to rational bases, while representations in non-integer bases follow separate rules. Levels of infinity unfold under set-theory axioms, and axiom systems can differ on the continuum cardinality. When discussing infinity and irrationality, one should first say whether the topic is representation, numerical property or set size, and not replace definitions with everyday intuition.[6][8]

06为什么规定先乘除后加减？结合律和分配律怎样决定算式的运算顺序？Why does multiplication and division come before addition and subtraction, and how do associative and distributive laws set the order?

分配律要求乘法先绑定各项；优先级消除歧义，结合/交换律规定可重排范围，表达式解析为语法树。The distributive law binds terms before addition; precedence removes ambiguity, associativity and commutativity set rearrangeable ranges, and the expression parses into a syntax tree.

先乘除后加减不是方便记忆的人为规定，而是代数结构的要求。数系里乘法对加法满足分配律 a(b+c)=ab+ac；若规定先算加法，这个展开式就无法按统一顺序读出。运算优先级本质上是让表达式的语法结构与代数定律对齐：乘法把若干份相同的量先合并，再与其他部分做加减，分配律才能在不改变结果的前提下展开和合并同类项。先乘除后加减，就是这条对齐约定的算术表层。Multiplying and dividing before adding and subtracting is not a convenient arbitrary rule; it follows from algebraic structure. In number systems, multiplication distributes over addition, a(b+c)=ab+ac. If addition were evaluated first, this expansion could not be read in one consistent order. Operator precedence aligns the expression's syntactic structure with algebraic laws: multiplication combines several equal shares first, then those combined parts are added or subtracted against the rest, so the distributive law can expand and collect like terms without changing the result. Multiplication before addition is the arithmetic surface of this alignment.[10]

优先级可用具体算式对照。3+2×4 按约定先算 2×4=8，再加 3 得 11；若先算加法，(3+2)×4=20，两个结果对应不同语义。括号显式改变分组：括号内先算，括号外再按优先级进行。减法和除法不满足结合律，8−4−2 从左到右得 2，写成 8−(4−2) 得 6；12÷6÷2=1，而 12÷(6÷2)=4。约定从左到右处理同级运算，正是为消除这类歧义；没有括号时，顺序不是随便挑的。Precedence can be checked against concrete expressions. Under the convention, 3+2×4 first computes 2×4=8, then adds 3 to get 11; if addition came first, (3+2)×4=20, and the two results carry different meanings. Parentheses explicitly change grouping: their contents are evaluated first, then the rest by precedence. Subtraction and division are not associative: 8−4−2 evaluates left to right as 2, while 8−(4−2)=6; likewise 12÷6÷2=1 but 12÷(6÷2)=4. The left-to-right rule for same-level operations removes exactly this ambiguity; without parentheses the order is not a free choice.[9][10]

结合律与交换律规定运算如何重排。加法结合律 a+(b+c)=(a+b)+c，乘法结合律 a·(b·c)=(a·b)·c，说明同级运算的分组不改变结果；交换律 a+b=b+a、a·b=b·a 说明同级运算顺序可调换。减法和除法没有结合律，交换后结果改变；分配律是跨级运算唯一的桥梁，要求乘法先绑定括号内各项，再与外部相加。理解这些定律，优先级就不必死记，每条规则对应一条可验证的代数性质。Associative and commutative laws specify how operations may be rearranged. Associativity a+(b+c)=(a+b)+c and a·(b·c)=(a·b)·c leaves same-level grouping unchanged; commutativity a+b=b+a and a·b=b·a lets order within a level swap. Subtraction and division have no associative law, and swapping them changes the result. The distributive law is the only bridge between levels: multiplication binds each parenthesized term first, then adds externally. Once these laws are understood, precedence need not be memorized as a chant; each rule matches a verifiable algebraic property.[10][11]

表达式在计算机和形式化处理中解析为语法树。运算符是内部节点，数字和变量是叶子；优先级决定哪个运算符先成为子树的根，括号直接指定树的分组。3+2×4 的树是加号挂着叶子 3 和乘法子树 2×4；(3+2)×4 的树是乘号挂着加法子树和叶子 4。求值从叶子向上逐层进行，先算子树再算父节点。编译器、计算器和数学排版都按同一套语法树规则解析；算错顺序往往是分组理解错，而非算术本身出错。Computing and formal treatment parse an expression into a syntax tree. Operators are internal nodes, numbers and variables are leaves; precedence decides which operator first becomes the root of a subtree, and parentheses directly specify the grouping. The tree for 3+2×4 has the plus node holding leaf 3 and the multiplication subtree 2×4; the tree for (3+2)×4 has the multiplication node holding the addition subtree and leaf 4. Evaluation proceeds upward from the leaves: a subtree is computed before its parent. Compilers, calculators and typesetting use the same tree rules; an order mistake usually means the grouping was misunderstood, not an arithmetic failure.[9][11]

边界：优先级约定服务于标准算术与代数，不同书写体系或编程语言可能有细节差异，例如幂运算结合方向有的定为从右到左；进入模运算、矩阵运算或函数复合时，规则要按对应结构重新确认。除零、无穷等问题属于数系定义层面，不在本章范围。优先级保证同一表达式在同一规则下有唯一解析；离开具体数系和记号约定，不能直接套用先乘除后加减。Boundary: precedence conventions serve standard arithmetic and algebra, and notation details differ across traditions or programming languages; for example, some languages bind exponentiation from right to left. Modular arithmetic, matrix operations and function composition follow their own structures and must be checked separately. Division by zero and infinity belong to number-system definitions and are outside this chapter. Precedence guarantees one unique parse for one expression under one rule; outside the specific number system and notation, multiply before add cannot be carried over directly.[9][10][11]

07数论到底有什么用？素数和模运算怎样变成现实工具？What is number theory good for, and how do primes and modular arithmetic become practical tools?

数论研究整数的整除与同余，其单向难度支撑公钥密码、校验码与编码可靠性。Number theory studies divisibility and congruence; its one-way difficulty underpins public-key crypto, check digits and coding reliability.

数论研究整数的结构：素数、整除关系、最大公约数和同余。它常被看成最纯的数学分支，讨论的问题表面上和日常无关，现代信息安全却几乎建立在它的结论之上。理解数论的用处，关键是区分两类问题的难度不对称：找两个大素数并相乘相对容易，反过来把一个大乘积分解回素数却非常困难，密码协议利用的正是这种单向性。Number theory studies the structure of integers: primes, divisibility, greatest common divisors and congruences. Often seen as the purest branch of mathematics, its questions look unrelated to daily life, yet modern information security rests almost entirely on its results. The key to its usefulness is an asymmetry in difficulty: finding two large primes and multiplying them is comparatively easy, while factoring a large product back into primes is very hard, and cryptographic protocols exploit exactly this one-way property.[12]

素数在整数中越往后越稀疏，但无穷无尽。素数定理给出渐近估计：不超过 x 的素数个数 π(x) 约等于 x/ln x；例如在 10 万附近，一个随机整数是素数的概率大约是 1/ln(10⁵)，即约 1/11。这一定理由 Hadamard 与 de la Vallée Poussin 在 1896 年分别证明，它描述素数分布的整体趋势，不保证某个具体数字是否为素数。Primes grow sparser further along the integers, yet they never run out. The prime number theorem gives the asymptotic estimate that the number of primes not exceeding x, π(x), is approximately x over ln x; near 100,000, the chance that a random integer is prime is about 1 over ln(100,000), roughly 1 in 11. Proved independently by Hadamard and de la Vallée Poussin in 1896, the theorem describes the overall trend of prime distribution, not whether any specific number is prime.[13]

RSA 公钥密码是这种不对称最直接的应用。选两个大素数 p、q 相乘得到模数 n，乘积可以公开作为公钥；分解 n 得到 p、q 是推导私钥的关键步骤，而在模数达到 2048 位乃至 4096 位时，现有经典计算机上的分解成本高到不可行。网银登录、网站证书和加密通信中的身份认证，都依赖大素数乘积难以分解这一性质。RSA public-key cryptography is the most direct application of this asymmetry. Two large primes p and q are multiplied into a modulus n, and the product can be published as the public key; factoring n back into p and q is the key step to deriving the private key, and with moduli at 2048 or even 4096 bits, factorization on current classical computers is prohibitively expensive. Online banking, website certificates and encrypted identity verification all rely on the difficulty of factoring a large product of primes.[14]

同余和整除性还进入日常编码。ISBN 书号、银行卡号用模运算设计校验位，录入时换位、输错一位都能被校验码发现；余数、互素和最大公约数是这些算法的公共语言。数论因此不只是证明定理的学问，它把可除性结构变成可计算的可靠性工具，让编号、传输和签名在出错和被攻击时仍可核对。Congruence and divisibility also enter everyday coding. ISBN book numbers and bank card numbers use modular arithmetic to design check digits, so transposition or a single mistyped digit is detected; remainders, coprimality and greatest common divisors are the common language of these algorithms. Number theory is therefore not only theorem-proving: it turns divisibility structure into computable reliability tools, letting numbering, transmission and signatures be checked when errors occur or attacks are attempted.[12][14]

边界要说清楚。RSA 的安全性基于大整数分解在现有算力下的经验难度，这一难度没有被证明为绝对不可破解；量子计算在原理上具备加速分解的潜力，后量子密码正是在应对这一变化。数论的价值也不止于应用：理解素数分布、同余结构和整数的深层秩序，本身就是数学认知的一部分，应用常常是在纯理论推进多年之后才出现。The boundary should be stated. RSA security rests on the empirical difficulty of factoring large integers with current computing power; this difficulty has not been proved absolutely unbreakable. Quantum computing holds theoretical potential to speed factorization, and post-quantum cryptography responds to that prospect. Number theory's value also exceeds applications: understanding prime distribution, congruence structure and the deeper order of integers is part of mathematical cognition itself, and applications often appear only years after pure theory advances.[13][14]

08为什么会有负数？Why do negative numbers exist?

为了表示亏欠、零下、反向这类相反意义的量，数系在 0 左侧对称扩展出负数。Negative numbers extend the number line left of zero to represent opposite quantities such as debt, below zero and reverse direction.

自然数只够表示有多少。清点人数、分苹果、量长度时，用 1、2、3 这样的正数就够了；但生活中还有方向相反、性质相对的量：欠别人的钱和存进账户的钱方向相反，零下温度低于 0 °C，向左走和向右走相对。如果只允许用大于等于 0 的数，这些相反意义无法区分，于是数系向 0 的另一侧扩展，引入负数。Natural numbers only say how many. Counting people, dividing apples or measuring length works with positive numbers such as 1, 2, 3; but life also contains quantities of opposite direction or kind: money owed runs opposite to money deposited, a temperature below zero °C, and walking left opposite to walking right. If only numbers greater than or equal to zero were allowed, these opposite meanings could not be told apart, so the number system was extended to the other side of zero, and negative numbers were introduced.[1]

负数表示与正数相反意义的量。记账时收入记正、亏欠记负，−50 元表示欠出或透支 50 元；温度计上 −3 °C 表示比 0 °C 还低 3 度；位移里规定向右为正，向左 4 米就记 −4 米。同一个量选哪一方为正可以约定，相反的一方就用负号标出。负数不是更小的零，而是带方向或相对意义的数，负号说明它站在约定正方向的反面。A negative number represents a quantity opposite in meaning to a positive one. In bookkeeping income is positive and debt negative, so −50 yuan means owing or overdrawn by 50 yuan; on a thermometer −3 °C means three degrees below 0 °C; in motion, with right chosen as positive, moving left 4 metres is recorded as −4 metres. Which side is chosen positive is a convention, and the opposite side is marked with a minus sign. A negative number is not a smaller zero; it is a number with direction or relative meaning, and the minus sign marks the opposite of the chosen positive direction.[15]

在数轴上，负数排在 0 的左侧。规定一个原点 0、向右为正方向后，正数依次排在右边，负数对称地排在左边；−a 是 a 的相反数，二者到 0 的距离相等，方向相反，且 a 与 −a 相加等于 0。0 本身既不是正数也不是负数。把数从自然数扩到整数后，减法在任何两个数之间都能进行，例如 3 减 5 不再不够减，结果记为 −2；这是引入负数最直接的代数理由。On the number line, negative numbers sit to the left of zero. With an origin 0 and right chosen as the positive direction, positives line up on the right and negatives symmetrically on the left; −a is the opposite of a, the same distance from zero in the opposite direction, and a plus −a equals zero. Zero itself is neither positive nor negative. Once numbers are extended from natural numbers to integers, subtraction can be carried out between any two numbers: 3 minus 5 is no longer impossible and is recorded as −2, which is the most direct algebraic reason for introducing negatives.[1][15]

扩展要保持原有运算规则成立。引入负数后，加法、乘法和分配律等基本性质需要仍然适用：一个数减去另一个数等于加上它的相反数；同号两数相乘得正、异号得负，正是为了让分配律在带符号的数上继续成立。可以用欠债模型理解：去掉一笔债务相当于增加资产，所以负负得正与日常直觉一致。负数与正数、0 一起构成整数，再扩展到有理数和实数。The extension must keep the existing rules of operation valid. After negatives are introduced, basic properties such as addition, multiplication and the distributive law still need to hold: subtracting a number equals adding its opposite; multiplying same-sign numbers gives a positive and opposite-sign numbers a negative, precisely so the distributive law keeps working with signed numbers. A debt model helps: cancelling a debt is like adding assets, so negative times negative gives positive, matching everyday intuition. Negatives together with positives and zero form the integers, which then extend to rational and real numbers.[10]

边界：负数是数学上为表达相反意义和闭合运算而建立的数，不是自然界里单独看得见的物体；具体记正记负取决于事先约定的方向。不同文明引入负数的历史路径不同，中国古代用红筹表正、黑筹表负表示收支。本章只说明负数产生的动机和基本位置，不展开方程、不等式或负数指数等后续内容。The boundary: negative numbers are mathematical objects built to express opposite meanings and closed operations, not separate visible objects in nature; whether something is marked positive or negative depends on a previously agreed direction. Different civilizations arrived at negatives along different historical paths; in ancient China red counting rods marked income and black rods marked expenditure. This chapter explains only the motive and basic position of negatives, and does not cover equations, inequalities or negative exponents.[1]
