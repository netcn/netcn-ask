01蒲丰投针怎样估出 π，随机游走又为什么会让赌徒破产？How does Buffon's needle estimate π, and why does a random walk lead to gambler's ruin?

随机抛针数相交频率可估 π；对称随机游走碰上资金边界，就变成破产或翻倍。Count needle-line crossings to estimate π; a symmetric random walk that hits a bankroll boundary becomes ruin or doubling.

蒲丰投针的核心不是“针有灵性”，而是把一次随机抛掷压缩成一个可数的几何事件：针是否跨过平行线。大量独立重复后，相交频率会稳定在某个只含 L、D 与 π 的概率上；把公式倒过来，π 就从频率里被估出来。随机游走则是另一条路：每一步左右各一半，位置是许多小随机量之和；它不承诺“马上回到起点”，但在一维和二维的对称情形里，返回概率为 1，而资金有限的赌徒几乎总会在某个边界先停下。Buffon's needle is not about a magic needle. It turns one random toss into a countable geometric event: does the needle cross a line? After many independent tosses, the crossing frequency settles at a probability containing L, D and π; invert the formula and π can be estimated from the frequency. A random walk is a different route: each step is a coin flip, so position is a sum of many small random displacements. It does not promise an immediate return, but in the symmetric one- and two-dimensional cases the return probability is 1, and a gambler with finite capital almost surely hits a boundary.[1][2]

设平行线间距为 D，针长 L≤D，针中点到最近直线的距离 y 在 [0,D/2] 均匀，针与线夹角 θ 在 [0,π/2] 均匀。针跨线当且仅当 y≤(L/2)sinθ。把 (y,θ) 看成矩形里的均匀点，有利区域面积是 L/2，矩形面积是 πD/4，所以 P=2L/(πD)。取 L=D/2，P=1/π；抛 N 次、记 X 次相交，估计 π≈N/X。它收敛得慢：误差大致按 1/√N 缩小，所以“抛一万次得 3.14”只是演示，不是高精度算法。Let the line spacing be D and the needle length L≤D. The midpoint distance y to the nearest line is uniform on [0,D/2], and the angle θ is uniform on [0,π/2]. The needle crosses exactly when y≤(L/2)sinθ. Treat (y,θ) as a uniform point in a rectangle: the favorable area is L/2, the rectangle area is πD/4, so P=2L/(πD). With L=D/2, P=1/π; after N tosses and X crossings, π≈N/X. Convergence is slow, roughly 1/√N, so ten thousand tosses is a demonstration, not a precision algorithm.[1]

一维对称随机游走中，第 n 步后的位置是 n 个 ±1 的和，期望 0，方差 n，典型距离约 √n；这不是“越走越远”，而是扩散。二维方格上的对称随机游走以概率 1 访问每个格点并返回原点；三维则不同，返回原点的概率只有约 0.3405。维度改变的不是“随机性”，而是路径可绕开的空间有多少：一维和二维太窄，迟早撞回；三维给了足够的岔路，可能永远不回来。In a one-dimensional symmetric walk, position after n steps is a sum of ±1: mean 0, variance n, typical distance about √n. This is diffusion, not steady travel. On a two-dimensional square lattice, the symmetric walk visits every site and returns to the origin with probability 1; in three dimensions the return probability is only about 0.3405. What changes with dimension is not the randomness but the room to go around: one and two dimensions are narrow enough that paths keep running into the origin; three dimensions offer enough detours to escape forever.[2][3][4][5]

把资金看成位置：从 i 元出发，每局公平地赢 1 或输 1，总资金 N，碰到 0 或 N 就停。公平游戏里，先到 N 的概率是 i/N，先破产的概率是 (N-i)/N；期望局数是 i(N-i)。所以不是“运气差才破产”，而是资金少的一方天然更容易先撞到下边界；赌场资金远大于单个赌客，哪怕每局公平，长期也占优，更不用说规则略偏向庄家。若每局不是五五开，破产概率还会更高。Model a bankroll as a position: start at i, win or lose 1 with equal probability, total N, stop at 0 or N. In a fair game the probability of reaching N first is i/N, the probability of ruin is (N-i)/N, and expected duration is i(N-i). Ruin is not merely bad luck: the smaller bankroll is closer to its lower boundary. A casino with far more capital than any patron therefore has a structural advantage even in a fair game, and a house edge makes it worse. If the game is not 50–50, ruin probability rises further.[6]

两个实验都要求“独立、均匀、可重复”。针必须真随机落下，不能按结果摆；随机游走的左右步必须独立同分布，不能“输了就加倍”改变下一步分布。现实中的赌场有上限、抽水与不连续下注，现实中的搜索也不是纯随机；这些都会让模型偏离。投针给出的是 π 的一个统计估计，不是证明；随机游走给出的是边界条件下的概率，不是对某个人的命运预言。Both experiments require independence, uniformity and repeatability. The needle must really fall at random, not be placed to produce a result; the walk's steps must be independent and identically distributed, not changed by a betting system. Real casinos have table limits, rake and discontinuous bets; real searches are not purely random. Buffon's needle gives a statistical estimate of π, not a proof; the random walk gives probabilities under boundary conditions, not a prophecy about one person.[1][6]

02在游乐园找人、集齐扑克牌、抛一百万次硬币：随机搜索的三种尺度How long does random search take—for a friend in a park, a full deck of cards, or exactly half heads in a million coin flips?

双方都动通常比一方等待更快；集齐 52 张牌平均约 236 天；一百万次恰好 50 万正面的概率约 0.08%。Two movers usually meet sooner than one waiter; a full 52-card deck takes about 236 days; exactly 500,000 heads in a million flips has probability about 0.08%.

三个问题看似无关，其实都在问“随机事件要重复多久才凑齐目标”。游乐园里两个人随机走动，是一个二维随机游走相遇问题；每天捡一张牌、直到 52 种牌都出现，是收集 coupon 问题；抛一百万次硬币、数正面是否刚好 50 万，是二项分布的中心点概率。它们的答案分别落在“相遇时间”“收集时间”“精确命中”三种尺度上，共同点是：平均等待可以算，但单次结果极不确定。These three questions look unrelated, but all ask how long random repetition takes to hit a target. Two people roaming a park form a two-dimensional random-walk meeting problem; finding one new card each day until all 52 kinds appear is a coupon-collector problem; flipping a million coins and asking for exactly 500,000 heads is the central-point probability of a binomial distribution. The answers sit at three scales—meeting time, collection time and exact-hit probability—and share one feature: average waiting can be calculated, while a single outcome remains highly uncertain.[7][8][9][10]

若两个人都按随机方向逐格移动，相遇是相对位置在做二维随机游走；若一个人站着、另一个随机走，只有后者在动。emptypipes 的模拟给出：8×8 网格上，两人都动平均约 53 步，一人等待约 73 步；16×16 上分别是 257 步与 453 步。网格越大，差距越明显。直觉是“站着守株待兔”缩小了搜索面积，但随机搜索不是系统扫描：移动的一方能不断把新位置纳入样本，两个都在动则相对扩散更快，第一次撞见的等待反而更短。If both people move randomly on a grid, their relative position performs a two-dimensional random walk; if one stands still, only the other moves. The emptypipes simulation reports about 53 steps for two movers versus 73 for one waiter on an 8×8 grid, and 257 versus 453 on 16×16. The larger the grid, the wider the gap. Waiting seems to shrink the search area, but random search is not a systematic scan: a mover keeps adding new positions to the sample, and when both move, relative diffusion is faster, so the first encounter often comes sooner.[7]

集齐 52 张牌、每次独立均匀抽到一种，期望天数是 52H₅₂，H₅₂=1+1/2+…+1/52≈4.538，乘起来约 236 天。这个式子来自线性期望：收集到 k 种后，抽到新一种的概率是 (52-k)/52，等待时间期望 52/(52-k)；把 k=0 到 51 的等待相加，就是 52H₅₂。末端最慢：最后一张平均要等 52 天，倒数第二张平均 26 天。所谓“怎么还差一张”不是运气特别差，而是收集末端的概率必然变慢。Collecting 52 cards with replacement, each uniformly random, has expected time 52H_52; H_52=1+1/2+...+1/52≈4.538, so the expectation is about 236 days. Linearity of expectation gives the formula: after k kinds are collected, a new kind appears with probability (52-k)/52, so the expected wait is 52/(52-k); summing k=0 to 51 gives 52H_52. The end is slowest: the last new card takes 52 days on average, the next-to-last 26. 'Still one card short' is not unusually bad luck; the tail is mathematically slow.[8]

抛 n=1,000,000 次公平硬币，正面数 X 服从二项分布，均值 500,000，标准差 √(npq)=√250,000=500。恰好 500,000 个正面的概率约为 1/(σ√(2π))≈1/(500×2.5066)≈7.98×10⁻⁴，也就是约 0.08%。注意“恰好一半”很窄；但“接近一半”很宽：落在 500,000±500 内的概率约 68%，落在 ±1000 内约 95%。Flip a fair coin n=1,000,000 times. The number of heads X is binomial with mean 500,000 and standard deviation √(npq)=√250,000=500. The probability of exactly 500,000 heads is about 1/(σ√(2π))≈1/(500×2.5066)≈7.98×10⁻⁴, or 0.08%, about 1 in 1,253. 'Exactly half' is narrow, but 'near half' is wide: about 68% of outcomes fall within ±500 of 500,000, and about 95% within ±1000. A deviation of a few hundred from half is normal; exact equality is the rare case.[9][10]

这些数字都建立在独立、均匀、同分布上。游客会沿道路、摊位和人群移动，系统搜索可能更有效；牌堆有偏、或每天只捡固定区域，52H₅₂ 就不成立；硬币也不绝对公平，2025 年 35 万次翻转估计同一面朝上偏差约 50.8%，起始面随机时正面总偏差可忽略。模型是基准，不是保证：先确认随机机制成立，再谈平均等待。All these numbers assume independent, uniform, identically distributed events. Park visitors do not walk truly at random; they follow paths, stalls and crowds, and a systematic search may beat random search. Card collection fails if the deck is biased or the daily find is confined to one area. Coins are not perfectly fair: a 2025 study of 350,757 flips estimated a same-side bias of about 50.8%, although with a randomized starting side the overall heads bias was negligible. The model is a baseline, not a guarantee; first ask whether the random mechanism really holds, then ask how long the average wait is.[7][8][9]

03彩票号码越整齐就越不可能中奖吗？Does a neatly ordered lottery ticket become less likely to win?

不是。01-02-03-04-05-06 与任何一组号码等概率；觉得它“太整齐”而不选，是代表性启发式。No. 01-02-03-04-05-06 has the same chance as any other combination; avoiding it because it looks 'too ordered' is the representativeness heuristic.

在 49 选 6、顺序不计的彩票里，每一组六个号码都是一个组合，组合总数是 C(49,6)=49!/(6!43!)=13,983,816。01-02-03-04-05-06 只是其中一个组合，和 03-17-22-31-40-48 完全等概率；开奖机不会因为号码连续就“避开”它。人觉得它不可能，不是因为概率更低，而是因为它太像“人为排好的”，不像我们心里那团杂乱的随机。In a 49-number, choose-six lottery where order does not matter, every six-number set is one combination. The total is C(49,6)=49!/(6!43!)=13,983,816. The ticket 01-02-03-04-05-06 is one of them and has exactly the same probability as 03-17-22-31-40-48; the drawing machine does not avoid consecutive numbers. The feeling that it cannot happen comes not from lower probability but from its looking 'too arranged', unlike the messy random image in our heads.[11][13][14]

如果彩票是“从 49 个球里不放回抽 6 个”，每个具体组合的概率都是 1/13,983,816；若彩票还要求顺序或另有特别号，样本空间会变，但“某一组具体号码”仍然只是其中一个结果。连号、同尾数、全偶数、全小数这些“模式”会包含很多组合，所以“出现某种模式”的概率可以很高；但“出现这一组”的概率不会因为模式而被抬高或压低。把“模式常见”误读成“这一组更可能”，是把集合概率和单个结果概率混在一起。If the lottery draws six balls without replacement from 49, each specific combination has probability 1/13,983,816. If the game requires order or an extra ball, the sample space changes, but a specific ticket is still one outcome. Patterns such as consecutive numbers, same endings, all even or all low contain many combinations, so the probability of 'some pattern' can be high; the probability of 'this exact set' is neither raised nor lowered by the pattern. Confusing set probability with single-outcome probability creates the illusion.[11][13]

心理学把这种判断叫代表性启发式：人们用“这个结果有多像随机过程”来代替真正的概率计算。01-02-03-04-05-06 看起来太有秩序，像人为挑选，于是被判为“不像随机”；而 03-17-22-31-40-48 看起来参差，就被认为“更像随机”。但随机序列本来就会包含局部簇、连号、重复和空档；真正的随机不等于“均匀铺开”。这种直觉在识别人为造假时有用，在判断公平抽奖时却会误导。Psychologists call this the representativeness heuristic: people substitute 'how much this result resembles a random process' for an actual probability calculation. 01-02-03-04-05-06 looks too orderly, like a human choice, so it is judged 'not random'; 03-17-22-31-40-48 looks jagged and is judged 'more random'. But random sequences do contain clusters, runs, repeats and gaps; truly random is not the same as evenly spread. The intuition is useful for spotting fabricated data, but misleading for judging a fair draw.[14]

避开“整齐号码”不会提高中奖概率，却可能改变奖金分享。若某个号码组合被大量彩民选中，中奖后要分给更多人，单注奖金可能被摊薄；反过来，选择别人少选的组合，不能让你更容易中，但可能让你中时拿得更多。这是“期望奖金”的优化，不是“中奖概率”的优化。把两者混为一谈，就会以为自己在“提高运气”，其实只是在改变分摊人数。Avoiding 'ordered' numbers does not raise the chance of winning, but it can change how a prize is shared. If a combination is played by many people, a win is split among more winners and the individual share can shrink; choosing a less popular combination cannot make you more likely to win, but it can make a win pay more. That is an optimization of expected payout, not of win probability. Mixing the two makes a player feel as if luck is being improved, when only the number of sharers is being changed.[11][12]

边界也很重要。不同彩票的号码范围、特别号、开奖轮次和奖金规则不同；“顺序”在有些玩法里确实有意义，算术要跟着实际规则走。代表性启发式是稳健的心理现象，但不等于每个彩民都这样想，也不等于“整齐号码”一定没人选——有些人偏偏会选。稳妥的结论很窄：在公平抽奖里，某一组具体组合与其他组等概率；“看起来有序”是人的感受，不是球的性质。The boundaries matter. Lotteries differ in number range, bonus balls, number of draws and prize rules; 'order' may matter in some games, and the arithmetic must match the actual rules. The psychological effect is robust, but it is not a claim that every player uses the same heuristic or that every 'ordered' ticket is unpopular—some players deliberately choose them. The safe conclusion is narrow: for a fair draw, one specific combination is as likely as any other; the feeling of order is a fact about human perception, not about the balls.[11][12][14]

04为什么条件概率更新后先验会被推翻？概率为 0 的事件为什么仍可能发生？Why does Bayesian updating revise the prior, and how can a zero-probability event still occur?

贝叶斯按先验与似然更新后验；忽略基础率会高估阳性患病概率；连续分布单点概率为 0 仍可能发生。Bayes updates the posterior from prior and likelihood; ignoring the base rate inflates post-test probability; a continuous point has zero probability yet can occur.

概率反直觉，常因为新信息进来后概率要按条件重新分配，而非把旧概率平均摊到剩余选项。贝叶斯更新写成 P(H|E)=P(H)P(E|H)/P(E)：先验 P(H) 是看到证据前的判断，似然 P(E|H) 是假说成立时出现证据的概率，分母 P(E) 是证据在所有情形下的总概率。证据出现后，后验由先验与似然共同决定；证据区分度越高，更新幅度越大。条件一变，样本空间就变，旧概率不能直接沿用。Probability feels counterintuitive mainly because new information redistributes probabilities conditionally, rather than averaging old probabilities over the remaining options. Bayes' rule writes P(H|E)=P(H)P(E|H)/P(E): the prior P(H) is the judgment before evidence, the likelihood P(E|H) is how often the evidence appears if the hypothesis holds, and P(E) is its total probability across all cases. The posterior is set jointly by prior and likelihood, and more discriminating evidence produces a larger update. Once the condition changes, the sample space changes, and old probabilities cannot be carried over.[15]

基础率谬误是最常见的更新偏差：人盯着检测灵敏度，忽略事件在人群中的先验比例。经典筛查例子：某病患病率约 1%，灵敏度约 80%，健康者假阳性率约 9.6%。检出阳性后真正患病的概率不是 80%，而是 0.01×0.80/（0.01×0.80+0.99×0.096）≈0.078，约 7.8%。一百个阳性里真正患病的不到八个，其余多为假阳性。直觉把检测准当成阳性即患病，漏掉 99% 的健康基数；基础率越低误报占比越高。The base-rate fallacy is the common update bias: people focus on test sensitivity and ignore how rare the event is in the population. In a classic screening example, prevalence is about 1%, sensitivity about 80%, and the false-positive rate among healthy people about 9.6%. For a random positive result, true disease probability is not 80% but 0.01×0.80/(0.01×0.80+0.99×0.096)≈0.078, about 7.8%. Fewer than eight in a hundred positives are true cases; the rest are healthy people hit by false alarms. Intuition reads an accurate test as positive means diseased, missing the 99% healthy base.[15][16]

概率用乘法，来自事件的依赖结构。两个独立事件同时发生，P(A∩B)=P(A)P(B)；不独立时写成 P(A∩B)=P(A)P(B|A)，第二步乘 A 发生条件下 B 的条件概率。多环节路径要每环都成功，整体概率是各环节概率相乘，环节越多衰减越快：五环各 90% 连乘约 0.9⁵≈0.59，十环只剩约 0.35。加法用于互斥事件并集，乘法用于事件交集，对应不同集合运算。Multiplication follows from event dependency. For independent events, P(A∩B)=P(A)P(B); otherwise the joint is P(A∩B)=P(A)P(B|A), multiplying by the conditional probability of B given A. A chain succeeds only if every link holds, so its overall probability multiplies the link probabilities, decaying fast as links grow: five links at 90% each give about 0.9^5≈0.59, and ten leave about 0.35. Addition applies to the union of mutually exclusive events, multiplication to the intersection.[15]

另一类反直觉来自连续分布：概率为 0 的事件仍可能发生。在 [0,1] 区间均匀取实数，落到任意指定点的概率是点长度除以区间长度，0/1=0，但该点确实可能被取到。单点在连续区间测度为 0，概率 0 表示长度、面积或体积可忽略，不等于逻辑上不可能。反过来概率为 1 也不保证必然发生：避开某指定点的概率是 1，那个点仍可能出现。把离散模型里零概率即不可能的直觉搬到连续模型，正是困惑根源。The other counterintuitive case is the continuous distribution: an event can have probability zero and still occur. Pick a real number uniformly from [0,1]; landing on any one specified point has probability point length over interval length, 0/1=0, yet that point can still be drawn. A single point has measure zero, so probability zero means length, area or volume is negligible, not logical impossibility. Conversely, probability one does not guarantee certainty: the chance of avoiding a point is one, but that point can appear.[17]

边界取决于模型假设。贝叶斯更新要求先验与似然有明确定义，先验影响后验，证据不足时结论对先验敏感；7.8% 只是这个参数组合的结果，不能套到别的筛查。乘法以独立性或明确条件依赖为前提，事件有关联时直接相乘会偏差；零概率结论基于理想实数模型，现实测量有精度和离散化。概率回答给定模型下的倾向，不是单次结果保证。The boundary depends on model assumptions. Bayesian updating needs a defined prior and likelihood; the prior affects the posterior, and thin evidence makes the result sensitive to it. The 7.8% figure belongs to this parameter combination and cannot be copied to other screenings. Multiplication assumes independence or a stated dependency; correlated events make direct multiplication under- or overestimate, and the zero-probability result rests on an ideal real-number model. Probability states a tendency under a given model, not a guarantee for one outcome.[15][16][17]

05抽签有先有后，对每个人都公平吗？Is drawing lots fair whether you go first or later?

不放回抽签时，n 个签里 k 个中奖，随机排列中每个位置地位对称，中签概率都是 k/n，先后顺序不影响边际机会。In drawing lots without replacement, among n tickets k win; random permutation makes every position symmetric, so each has probability k/n and order does not change the marginal chance.

抽签常见于分名额、排顺序、抽代表等场合。假设有 n 个签，其中 k 个是中奖签，其余为空签，签物外观相同、抽取过程随机、抽后不放回。直觉上有人担心先抽的人占走好签，后抽的人吃亏；要判断公不公平，需要看每个位置在随机安排下的中签概率。例如 10 个签里有 2 个中奖，每个位置的中签概率都是 2/10，与排在第几个抽无关。Drawing lots is used to allocate slots, set order or pick representatives. Suppose there are n tickets, k of them winning and the rest blank, identical in appearance, drawn randomly and not replaced. Intuition sometimes worries that early drawers take the good tickets and later ones lose out; fairness must be judged by the winning probability at each position under a random arrangement.[17][18]

从排列角度看，抽签等价于把 n 个签随机排成一列，再按先后顺序依次取走。在一个均匀随机排列里，第 1 位、第 2 位直到第 n 位地位完全对称，没有哪个位置天然更有利。任意一个位置取到中奖签的概率，都等于中奖签占全部签的比例，即 k/n；先抽走一个签，只是改变了剩下签的构成，并不破坏这种位置对称。可以把 n 个签的所有排列看成等可能的样本空间，每个签在每个位置出现的次数相同；因此任意位置抽到中奖签的比例，都等于中奖签在总体中的占比。In permutation terms, drawing lots is equivalent to arranging the n tickets in a random line and taking them in order. In a uniform random permutation, positions 1 through n are completely symmetric, and none is naturally favored. The probability that any position holds a winning ticket equals the fraction of winning tickets, k/n; taking one ticket first changes only the composition left behind and does not break this positional symmetry.[17][18]

用条件概率可以验证第 2 个人。若第 1 人已中签，剩下 k−1 个中奖签、n−1 个签，第 2 人中签概率为 (k−1)/(n−1)；若第 1 人未中，剩下 k 个中奖签，概率为 k/(n−1)。把两种情况按第 1 人的概率加权，第 2 人的中签概率仍回到 k/n。后面每一位都可以这样递推，结果始终相同。Conditional probability verifies the second drawer. If the first drew a winner, k−1 winning tickets remain among n−1, so the second probability is (k−1)/(n−1); if the first missed, k winners remain, giving k/(n−1). Weighting these two cases by the first drawer's probabilities brings the second drawer's chance back to k/n. Every later position can be derived the same way, with the same result.[13][18]

公平有前提：签物必须相同，抽取必须随机，过程不放回且在结果揭晓前不偷看、不传递信息。如果先抽的人当场公开结果，或者签有记号、可以被辨认，后面人的条件概率就会改变，先后顺序才可能带来差别。密封同时抽取、或抽完统一揭晓，正是为了保住这种对称性。常见的抽签、抓阄、摇号在数学上都属于这种不放回随机抽取；只要过程随机、规则对所有人一致，先后手在概率上就没有优劣之分。Fairness has preconditions: tickets must be identical, the draw random, without replacement, and without peeking or passing on information before results are revealed. If an early drawer announces the result on the spot, or tickets are marked and recognizable, later conditional probabilities change, and order can then make a difference. Sealed simultaneous draws or revealing all results afterward preserve this symmetry.[17]

边界要写清：抽签公平指的是机会均等，不保证每个人抽到的结果相同；概率为 k/n 是长期和整体意义上的期望，单次抽签仍有偶然性。先后顺序在规则公平的前提下不影响边际概率，但如果规则允许偷看、调换或信息泄露，公平性就要重新评估，不能套用对称结论。The boundary matters: fair drawing means equal opportunity, not identical results for everyone; probability k/n is a long-run and overall expectation, and a single draw still carries chance. Under fair rules order does not change the marginal probability, but if peeking, swapping or information leakage is allowed, fairness must be reassessed and the symmetry conclusion no longer applies.[18]

06为什么会有幸存者偏差？Why does survivorship bias happen?

只统计到幸存下来的样本，被淘汰者不在数据里，结论建立在残缺样本上；补齐失败案例和基础率后才能接近总体。Only surviving samples reach the data while failures are absent, so conclusions rest on a truncated sample; adding failed cases and base rates brings the picture closer to the population.

幸存者偏差是一种由样本选择造成的判断偏差。我们能观察到的往往是经过一道筛选后留下来的对象：现存的企业、长寿的品牌、传世的作品、返航的飞机；那些倒闭、失传、损毁或没能留下来的同类，已经不在统计视野里。只拿幸存者做分析，看到的并不是总体，而是总体中通过筛选的那一部分。假设 100 家同类企业里只有 20 家存活下来，只研究这 20 家，就等于漏掉了 80 家失败样本。这类偏差之所以隐蔽，是因为幸存者通常更容易被记录和接触：现存企业有公开资料，返航飞机被检查，而失败案例往往不留完整档案。Survivorship bias is a judgment distortion caused by sample selection. What we observe are often objects that passed a filter: surviving companies, long-lived brands, surviving works that remain, and aircraft that returned; the firms that failed, works lost to time, and machines destroyed or otherwise missing are no longer in the statistical view. Analyzing only survivors does not examine the population, only the subset that passed the filter.[19][20]

经典例子来自二战飞机防护。统计返航轰炸机身上的弹孔，发现机翼和机身弹孔多、发动机和座舱弹孔少，有人据此主张给弹孔多的部位加固。分析者却指出，真正该加固的是返航飞机上弹孔少的部位：中弹在发动机和座舱的飞机没能返航，已经不在样本里。只看幸存者的弹孔分布，会把致命部位误当成安全部位。A classic example comes from wartime aircraft protection. Surveying bullet holes on bombers that returned, analysts found many on wings and fuselage but few on engines and cockpits, and some argued to armor the holed areas. The counterargument: the few-holed spots on returned planes are exactly where armor should go, because planes hit in engines or cockpits did not return and were absent from the sample. Reading only survivor hole distribution mistakes fatal areas for safe ones.[19]

生活和商业中类似场景很多。只研究现存成功企业的共性，容易忽略同样具备这些特征却倒闭的公司；只看长寿品牌的经验，看不到一批中途消失的品牌；基金业绩排名常只展示仍在存续的基金，已清盘的差基金被移出样本。把幸存者的共同特征当成成功原因，就容易得出片面结论。看长寿书籍、传世建筑或老牌产品时也要留意，同时代消失的同类没有进入视野；只归纳现存者的共同点，容易把偶然存活当成必然优势。Similar scenes appear in daily life and business. Studying only surviving successful companies easily ignores firms with the same traits that still went bankrupt; looking only at long-lived brands misses a batch that disappeared midway; fund rankings often show only funds still alive, and liquidated poor funds leave the sample. Treating shared survivor traits as the cause of success tends to give one-sided conclusions.[19][20]

它与基础率谬误、选择偏差属于相近的一族问题。样本不是从总体中随机抽取，而是经过存活这道筛选，存活本身与结果相关；如果再忽略失败案例的基础数量，成功概率和原因都会被高估。纠正方法是先明确总体是谁、分母在哪，尽量纳入失败和退出的案例，再结合基础率比较幸存与淘汰两组。It belongs with base-rate fallacy and selection bias as a nearby family. The sample is not drawn randomly from the population but filtered by survival, which itself correlates with outcome; if the base count of failed cases is also ignored, success probability and causes are overestimated. The correction is to define the population and its denominator first, include failed and exited cases where possible, then compare surviving and dropped groups against base rates.[16][20]

边界要写清：识别偏差不等于幸存者经验毫无价值，而是不能只用幸存者下因果结论。补充失败样本有时受数据可得性限制，需要结合记录来源和时间范围判断；本章只解释偏差如何由样本缺失产生，不展开具体行业的统计建模或投资决策方法。The boundary matters: spotting the bias does not mean survivor experience is worthless, only that causal conclusions cannot rest on survivors alone. Adding failed samples is sometimes limited by data availability, so records and time range must be judged; this chapter explains how the bias arises from missing samples, not statistical modeling or investment decisions in any specific industry.[16][19]
