01铁为什么会生锈，怎样才能防止钢铁生锈？不锈钢为什么不容易生锈？Why does iron rust, how can steel be protected, and why does stainless steel resist rusting?

生锈是水膜参与的电化学腐蚀；涂层、镀锌和铬钝化膜分别阻断回路不同环节，不锈钢在氯离子环境中仍可能点蚀。Rusting is electrochemical corrosion driven by a moisture film; coatings, galvanizing and chromium passivation block different parts of the circuit, and stainless still pits in chloride.

铁生锈是一个需要水和氧气共同参与的电化学腐蚀过程，而不是干燥状态下铁的简单变质。干燥空气中的铁可以长期保存；暴露在潮湿环境中的钢铁表面会凝结一层薄水膜，溶解空气中的二氧化碳和盐分后形成导电薄膜，使铁表面不同区域分别充当阳极和阴极，构成无数微小原电池。阳极处铁原子失去电子变成亚铁离子进入水膜，阴极处氧气在水膜中得到电子生成氢氧根，电子通过金属本体在两极之间流动，腐蚀由此持续进行。Iron rusting is an electrochemical process that needs both water and oxygen, not a simple decay of dry metal. Iron kept in dry air lasts a long time, but steel exposed to moisture develops a thin water film that dissolves carbon dioxide and salts to become a conducting electrolyte. Different spots on the surface then act as anode and cathode in countless tiny galvanic cells. At the anode iron atoms lose electrons and enter the film as ferrous ions; at the cathode oxygen gains electrons and forms hydroxide ions, while electrons travel through the metal, so corrosion keeps going.[1]

阳极反应可写成 Fe → Fe²⁺ + 2e⁻，标准还原电位约为 −0.44 V；阴极在中性或弱碱性条件下为 O₂ + 2H₂O + 4e⁻ → 4OH⁻。生成的亚铁离子进一步被氧化为三价铁，再与水结合成疏松的水合氧化铁 Fe₂O₃·nH₂O，也就是常见的红褐色铁锈。铁锈结构多孔，不能像致密氧化膜那样隔绝空气和水，水分与氧继续渗入内层，因此锈蚀会向钢铁深部发展，直到构件截面被削弱。The anode half-reaction is Fe → Fe²⁺ + 2e⁻, with a standard reduction potential near −0.44 V; in neutral or mildly alkaline conditions the cathode reaction is O₂ + 2H₂O + 4e⁻ → 4OH⁻. The ferrous ions oxidize further to iron(III), combine with water into porous hydrated iron oxide Fe₂O₃·nH₂O, the familiar reddish-brown rust. Rust is porous and cannot seal the metal like a dense oxide film, so moisture and oxygen keep reaching the inner layer and corrosion progresses deeper until the section weakens.[1][8]

常见的防锈思路对应阻断电化学回路的不同环节。涂层防护用油漆、油脂或搪瓷把金属与水、氧隔开；镀锌层利用锌比铁更负的电位（Zn²⁺/Zn 约 −0.76 V）作牺牲阳极，即使镀层局部破损，锌仍优先腐蚀而保护铁。不锈钢则依靠合金化：含铬量达到约 10.5% 以上时，表面迅速形成一层致密的氧化铬钝化膜，把基体与水膜隔开，膜层破损后还能在含氧环境中自行修复。Common anti-rust strategies block different parts of this circuit. Paints, oils and enamels separate the metal from water and oxygen. A zinc coating acts as a sacrificial anode because zinc is more electronegative than iron, so even where the layer is scratched zinc corrodes first and protects the iron. Stainless steel works by alloying: above about 10.5% chromium, a dense chromium oxide passivation film forms on the surface, separates the substrate from the water film, and can regrow in oxygen after minor damage.[1][2]

不锈钢并非永不生锈。在含氯离子的环境（海水、除冰盐、汗水）中，钝化膜容易发生点蚀，局部坑洞向内加深；涂层一旦被划伤或起泡，水分沿界面渗入，会在漆膜下发生隐蔽腐蚀。镀锌层随使用逐渐消耗，牺牲保护能力也随之下降。判断一种防护是否有效，要看它阻断的是水膜、氧供应、电通路还是活性阳极，而不是只看表面是否发亮。Stainless steel does not mean rust-proof. In chloride environments such as seawater, de-icing salt or sweat, the passivation film can break down into localized pitting that deepens inward. Once paint is scratched or blistered, moisture travels along the interface and hidden corrosion spreads under the film. A galvanized layer depletes over time and its sacrificial protection fades with it. Whether a protection works depends on which part of the circuit it blocks: water film, oxygen supply, electrical path or active anode.[1][8]

边界：锈蚀速率受湿度、温度、电解质浓度和合金成分共同影响，实验室电位数据对应标准状态，实际环境中的腐蚀电流需要现场测量。把某种钢材的耐蚀表现直接推广到所有工况并不稳妥；不锈钢的等级、表面处理和暴露介质不同，结果差异可以很大。防锈方案应按使用环境选择涂层、镀层或耐蚀合金，并定期检查膜层完整性。The boundary: corrosion rate depends on humidity, temperature, electrolyte concentration and alloy composition. Standard potentials apply to reference conditions, while real corrosion currents need on-site measurement. The performance of one steel in one setting cannot be generalized to every use case; stainless grade, surface finish and exposure medium change the outcome substantially. Protection should be matched to the environment, with coating, plating or corrosion-resistant alloy chosen deliberately and inspected over time.[1][8]

02为什么食盐溶于水却不溶于汽油？极性与溶解能力是什么关系？Why does table salt dissolve in water but not in gasoline, and how does polarity relate to solubility?

极性水靠离子-偶极作用水合 Na⁺ 与 Cl⁻，补偿晶格能；非极性汽油无法溶剂化离子，相似相溶是定性启发而非定量判据。Polar water hydrates Na⁺ and Cl⁻ through ion-dipole forces that repay lattice energy; nonpolar gasoline cannot solvate ions, and like dissolves like is a qualitative guide.

食盐溶于水而不溶于汽油，根源在于溶质与溶剂分子之间的极性匹配。水是强极性分子，氧端带部分负电、氢端带部分正电；汽油主要由非极性烃类组成，分子电荷分布均匀。氯化钠晶体由 Na⁺ 和 Cl⁻ 靠离子键交替排列而成，要把它拆开，溶剂分子必须能用强的静电作用把离子包围并带走，这就是溶解能否发生的基本判据。Table salt dissolves in water but not in gasoline because of the polarity match between solute and solvent. Water is a strongly polar molecule with a partially negative oxygen end and partially positive hydrogen ends, while gasoline consists mostly of nonpolar hydrocarbons with evenly distributed charge. A sodium chloride crystal holds Na⁺ and Cl⁻ in an alternating ionic lattice; for it to dissolve, solvent molecules must surround and carry the ions away through strong electrostatic interactions, and that is the basic criterion for dissolution.[2]

水分子与离子之间形成离子-偶极作用。晶体表面的 Na⁺ 被水分子氧端环绕，Cl⁻ 被氢端环绕，这些水合作用释放的水合能如果足以补偿拆开晶格所需的晶格能，晶体就会逐层解离进入溶液。20 °C 时氯化钠在水中的溶解度约为每 100 g 水 36 g；而汽油分子没有永久偶极，无法对离子形成稳定的溶剂化壳层，晶格能得不到补偿，食盐在其中几乎不溶。Water molecules form ion-dipole interactions with the ions. Na⁺ at the crystal surface is wrapped by the oxygen ends of water, and Cl⁻ by the hydrogen ends. If the hydration energy released by this solvation compensates the lattice energy needed to separate the ions, the crystal peels layer by layer into solution. At 20 °C sodium chloride dissolves to about 36 g per 100 g of water. Gasoline molecules have no permanent dipole, cannot form a stable solvation shell around ions, and do not repay the lattice energy, so salt is nearly insoluble there.[2][3]

相似相溶是一条经验规则：极性溶质易溶于极性溶剂，非极性溶质易溶于非极性溶剂。油脂属于非极性物质，在水中难以分散，却能溶于汽油这类烃类溶剂；乙醇分子既有羟基极性端又有烃基，因此既能与水互溶，也能溶解不少有机物质。判断溶解方向时，除了极性，还要看溶质内部作用力、溶剂化能和温度对平衡的影响，而不是只凭“亲油亲水”的直觉。Like dissolves like is an empirical rule: polar solutes tend to dissolve in polar solvents, nonpolar solutes in nonpolar solvents. Fats and oils are nonpolar, hard to disperse in water but soluble in hydrocarbon solvents such as gasoline. Ethanol carries both a polar hydroxyl end and a hydrocarbon chain, so it mixes with water and also dissolves many organic substances. Beyond polarity, whether dissolution works also depends on internal solute forces, solvation energy and temperature, not just an intuitive oil-versus-water instinct.[2][3]

溶解是一个动态平衡。固体溶质进入溶液的同时，溶液中的离子也会重新回到晶体表面；达到饱和时两个方向的速率相等。搅拌、粉碎和升温可以加快溶解或提高溶解度，但不能改变“极性不匹配就难以溶剂化”这一基本限制。气体和液体溶质的溶解还受分压影响，这与固体溶解度的规律并不完全相同。Dissolution is a dynamic equilibrium. Solute leaves the crystal for the solution while ions in solution return to the crystal surface; at saturation the two rates match. Stirring, grinding and heating can speed dissolving or raise solubility, but they cannot remove the basic limitation that a polarity mismatch resists solvation. Gaseous and liquid solutes are also affected by partial pressure, which differs from the rules for solid solubility.[2][3]

边界：相似相溶只是定性启发，不能替代溶解焓、晶格能和溶剂化自由能的定量比较。有些极性液体之间只能部分互溶，离子化合物在极性溶剂中的溶解度也受晶格结构强烈影响；温度、浓度和共存离子都会改变实测溶解度。描述某一种物质的溶解能力时，应给出溶剂、温度和浓度范围，而不能把“溶于水”推广到所有极性液体。The boundary: like dissolves like is a qualitative guide, not a substitute for quantitative comparison of dissolution enthalpy, lattice energy and solvation free energy. Some polar liquids are only partially miscible, and ionic solubility in polar solvents is strongly shaped by lattice structure; temperature, concentration and coexisting ions all change measured solubility. When describing how well one substance dissolves, state the solvent, temperature and concentration range instead of generalizing soluble in water to every polar liquid.[3][8]

03为什么往溶液里加盐会降低它的冰点？凝固点降低由什么决定？Why does adding salt lower a solution's freezing point, and what determines freezing-point depression?

依数性只看粒子数：ΔTf = Kf·m·i，水的 Kf≈1.86；NaCl 解离 i≈2，除冰有低共熔温度下限。Colligative properties depend on particle count: ΔTf = Kf·m·i, Kf for water ≈1.86; NaCl has i≈2, and de-icing has a eutectic floor.

往溶液里加盐会降低冰点，这是依数性的典型表现：凝固点下降只取决于溶液中溶质粒子的数量，而与粒子的种类关系不大。纯水在 0 °C 结冰；加入可溶盐后，溶质分子或离子占据一部分液面和晶体生长界面，使单位体积内可结成冰的水分子减少，冰与溶液之间的平衡向融化方向移动，需要更低温度才能重新达到凝固平衡。Adding salt lowers the freezing point of a solution, a classic colligative property: the depression depends on the number of solute particles rather than on what those particles are. Pure water freezes at 0 °C. After soluble salt is added, solute molecules or ions occupy part of the liquid surface and crystal-growth interface, leaving fewer water molecules available to form ice, so the ice-solution equilibrium shifts toward melting, and a lower temperature is needed to restore freezing equilibrium.[4]

定量关系为 ΔTf = Kf · m · i。其中 Kf 是溶剂的凝固点降低常数，水的 Kf 约为 1.86 °C·kg/mol；m 是溶质的质量摩尔浓度；i 是范特霍夫因子，反映溶质解离出的粒子数。氯化钠在水中解离为 Na⁺ 和 Cl⁻，稀溶液中 i 接近 2，因此 1 mol/kg 的 NaCl 溶液理想凝固点下降约 3.72 °C；不解离的糖 i 接近 1，同样浓度只下降约 1.86 °C。The quantitative relation is ΔTf = Kf · m · i. Kf is the solvent freezing-point depression constant; for water Kf is about 1.86 °C·kg/mol. m is molality, and i is the van't Hoff factor reflecting how many particles the solute produces. Sodium chloride dissociates into Na⁺ and Cl⁻, so i approaches 2 in dilute solution; an ideal 1 mol/kg NaCl solution freezes about 3.72 °C lower. A non-dissociating sugar has i near 1 and depresses the freezing point by only about 1.86 °C at the same concentration.[4]

道路除冰正是利用这一原理。撒在冰雪上的盐溶解后形成浓溶液，降低冰点，使冰在低于 0 °C 的温度下融化；盐水本身又难以重新结冰。但这一作用有下限：NaCl–水体系的低共熔温度约为 −21 °C，气温低于该温度后加盐不再有效。盐也只能融化已经存在的冰，无法阻止路面再次结霜，过量使用还会腐蚀混凝土、车辆构件并污染土壤和水体。Road de-icing uses exactly this principle. Salt spread on snow and ice dissolves into a concentrated solution that lowers the freezing point, melting ice below 0 °C, and the brine itself resists refreezing. The effect has a floor: the NaCl-water eutectic temperature is about −21 °C, below which salt no longer helps. Salt only melts ice already present, cannot prevent re-frosting, and overuse corrodes concrete and vehicle parts while polluting soil and water.[3][4]

凝固点降低与沸点升高、蒸气压下降、渗透压属于同一类依数性质，都由溶质粒子数驱动。配制防冻液、测定溶质摩尔质量、解释海水比河水更难结冰，都可以从粒子数和稀溶液平衡入手。理解 i 值很关键：电解质解离越充分，相同质量的盐带来的粒子越多，依数效应越强；浓溶液中离子相互作用增强，i 会偏离理想值。Freezing-point depression belongs with boiling-point elevation, vapor-pressure lowering and osmotic pressure as colligative properties driven by particle count. Antifreeze formulation, molar-mass determination and the observation that seawater freezes below river water all start from particle number and dilute-solution equilibrium. The factor i matters: fuller dissociation produces more particles per mass and a stronger effect, while in concentrated solutions ion interactions push i away from its ideal value.[2][4]

边界：ΔTf 公式是稀溶液近似，浓度较高时偏差明显；不同盐的解离度、溶解度和吸潮能力不同，除冰效率不能只按化学式比较。凝固点下降描述的是溶剂结冰平衡，并不改变溶质本身的熔点。实际除冰还受雪量、路面温度和撒布方式影响，引用温度区间时应注明盐种与浓度，不能把理想公式的数值当作道路现场的实测效果。The boundary: the ΔTf formula is a dilute-solution approximation that deviates at high concentration. Different salts differ in dissociation, solubility and moisture uptake, so de-icing efficiency cannot be compared from the formula alone. Freezing-point depression describes the solvent's freezing equilibrium and does not change the solute's own melting point. Real de-icing also depends on snow load, road temperature and spreading method; temperature ranges should cite salt type and concentration rather than treating ideal formula values as field measurements.[3][4]

04高 pH 的碱为什么危险？酸雨是怎么形成的，后来怎么样了？Why is a high-pH base dangerous, how does acid rain form, and what happened to it afterward?

强碱皂化油脂、水解蛋白质并深层渗透；酸雨由 SO₂、NOx 氧化成硫酸硝酸，1980 年代后排放控制使多数地区沉降下降。Strong alkali saponifies oils and hydrolyzes protein deeply; acid rain forms from SO₂ and NOx oxidized to acids, and post-1980s controls lowered deposition widely.

高 pH 的碱液之所以危险，关键在于它对皮肤和组织的作用方式。按 Brønsted–Lowry 定义，酸给出质子 H⁺，碱接受质子；pH 表示氢离子活度的负对数，pH 大于 7 为碱性，数值越高碱性越强。氢氧化钠、氢氧化钾这类强碱在水中完全解离出 OH⁻，能够皂化皮肤表面的油脂保护膜，破坏角质层结构，并使蛋白质水解、溶解，组织随之液化。与酸灼伤常形成结痂阻挡进一步侵蚀不同，碱可以穿透更深，损伤范围和深度在接触后仍继续发展。A high-pH alkaline solution is dangerous mainly because of how it acts on skin and tissue. In the Brønsted–Lowry definition an acid donates proton H⁺ and a base accepts one; pH is the negative logarithm of hydrogen-ion activity, above 7 alkaline, with stronger base at higher values. Strong bases such as sodium or potassium hydroxide fully dissociate into OH⁻ in water. They saponify the protective oil film on skin, break down the stratum corneum, and hydrolyze and dissolve proteins until tissue liquefies. Unlike acid burns, which often form a crust that blocks further attack, alkali can penetrate deeper, and injury keeps widening and deepening after contact.[5]

酸与碱在常见反应中表现出不同行为。酸能与活泼金属、碳酸盐和指示剂反应，稀酸接触皮肤后疼痛出现快、表面易结痂；碱则优先破坏油脂和蛋白质，渗透力强，早期外观可能不严重，内部损伤却在加重。判断危险程度要看碱的种类、浓度、接触时间和是否及时冲洗：浓碱溅到皮肤后应立即用大量流动清水冲洗，并去除沾染衣物，拖延会显著加深损伤。Acids and bases behave differently in common reactions. Acids react with active metals, carbonates and indicators; dilute acid on skin causes early pain and tends to surface scabbing. Bases first attack oils and proteins, penetrate strongly, and may look mild at first while internal damage worsens. Severity depends on base type, concentration, contact time and whether rinsing is prompt: splashed strong alkali should be flushed immediately with plenty of running water and contaminated clothing removed, because delay deepens the injury markedly.[5][8]

酸雨是酸碱化学在区域环境中的典型案例。燃烧含硫燃料和机动车、工业排放的氮氧化物进入大气后，被氧化成硫酸和硝酸，随降水落下；未受污染的雨水因溶解二氧化碳本身呈弱酸性，pH 约 5.6，低于这一数值的降水才常被称为酸雨。酸雨会溶解石灰石与大理石建筑材料，损伤植物叶片，降低水体 pH，影响鱼类和水生生物繁殖。Acid rain is a classic regional case of acid-base chemistry in the environment. Sulfur-bearing fuels plus nitrogen oxides from vehicles and industry oxidize in the atmosphere into sulfuric and nitric acids, which fall with precipitation. Unpolluted rain is already mildly acidic from dissolved carbon dioxide, near pH 5.6; precipitation below that value is usually called acid rain. It dissolves limestone and marble structures, damages plant leaves, lowers water-body pH and impairs fish and aquatic reproduction.[5][7]

20 世纪 80 年代的酸雨担忧推动了排放控制。北美和欧洲通过烟气脱硫、氮氧化物减排和燃油标准收紧，雨后硫酸盐沉降明显下降，部分湖泊和森林逐步恢复；但在仍以高硫煤为主、工业排放治理不足的地区，酸沉降问题并未消失。所谓酸雨的对应物并不是普遍存在的“碱雨”，自然扬尘和碱性颗粒物可局部中和酸性降水，却不构成大范围的碱性沉降事件。The acid rain concern of the 1980s drove emission controls. Flue-gas desulfurization, nitrogen-oxide cuts and tighter fuel standards in North America and Europe reduced sulfate deposition afterward, and some lakes and forests recovered gradually. Where high-sulfur coal and weak industrial controls persist, acid deposition has not disappeared. The counterpart to acid rain is not a widespread alkaline rain; natural dust and alkaline particles can locally neutralize acidic precipitation but do not produce large-scale alkaline deposition events.[7]

边界：pH 衡量的是氢离子活度而非碱的总浓度，浓溶液与稀溶液的危险性不能只按 pH 数值排列。碱灼伤的轻重取决于浓度、接触时间、冲洗是否及时和组织部位；酸雨酸度受本地排放、大气传输和缓冲物质共同影响，单点降水 pH 不能代表区域长期趋势。治理成效需要多年监测数据支持，不能凭单次降水或局部景观下结论。The boundary: pH measures hydrogen-ion activity, not total base concentration, so danger cannot be ranked by pH alone. Alkali injury depends on concentration, contact time, prompt rinsing and body site. Acid-rain acidity reflects local emissions, atmospheric transport and buffering materials together, and a single rain pH cannot represent a long-term regional trend. Recovery claims need multi-year monitoring; conclusions should not be drawn from one shower or a local landscape view.[5][7]

05地壳中含量最多的元素有哪些？丰度按什么排序，锂会不会用完？Which elements are most abundant in the crust, how is the ranking ordered, and will lithium run out?

地壳丰度：氧 46.60%、硅 27.72%、铝 8.13%、铁 5.00%；储量是经济可采概念，不等于固定地质存量。By crustal mass: oxygen 46.60%, silicon 27.72%, aluminum 8.13%, iron 5.00%; reserves are economically recoverable, not a fixed geological stock.

地壳中的元素并非平均分布，而是按丰度形成清晰的排序。按质量百分比计，美国地质调查局的估算中氧约占 46.60%，硅约 27.72%，铝约 8.13%，铁约 5.00%，其后依次为钙、钠、钾、镁等。地壳岩石以硅酸盐和氧化物为主，因此氧和硅合计占去大半；金属元素多以化合态存在于矿物中，单质状态在自然界并不常见。这一排序是认识矿物、矿石和工业原料来源的基础数据。Elements are not evenly distributed in the crust; their abundances form a clear ranking. By weight, USGS estimates put oxygen at about 46.60 percent, silicon at 27.72 percent, aluminum at 8.13 percent and iron at 5.00 percent, followed by calcium, sodium, potassium and magnesium. Crustal rocks are dominated by silicates and oxides, so oxygen and silicon together make up most of the crust. Metals mostly occur combined in minerals rather than as native metal, and this ranking is the baseline data for understanding ores and industrial raw materials.[6]

放到宇宙尺度，元素丰度排序完全不同。按质量计，氢约占四分之三，氦约占近四分之一，二者源于恒星核合成与宇宙演化；碳、氧、氮等元素由恒星内部聚变产生，再经超新星爆发抛入星际空间。地球地壳的丰度受行星分异、岩浆活动和风化沉积改造，不能直接代表宇宙组成。同一种元素在不同地质圈——地壳、地幔、地核——中的含量差别也很大，例如铁在地核中占比很高。On a cosmic scale the ranking is very different. By mass, hydrogen makes up about three quarters and helium nearly a quarter, shaped by stellar nucleosynthesis and cosmic evolution. Carbon, oxygen and nitrogen are built by fusion inside stars and scattered into interstellar space by supernovae. Crustal abundance on Earth has been reshaped by planetary differentiation, magmatism and weathering, so it cannot represent cosmic composition. The same element also differs greatly between crust, mantle and core; iron, for example, is highly concentrated in the core.[6][8]

元素的确认与提取依赖化学和物理方法的进步。早期元素通过矿石的化学分解和重量分析识别；19 世纪光谱分析使稀有元素得以被发现；现代则依靠 X 射线荧光、质谱和实验室合成鉴定新的人造元素。一种元素是否被称为“发现”，要看分离出纯物质或建立明确谱线的时间，不同文献对早期混合矿石中的元素认定有时存在争议。Identifying and isolating elements advanced with chemical and physical methods. Early elements were recognized through chemical decomposition of ores and gravimetric analysis; nineteenth-century spectroscopy made rare elements detectable, and modern work relies on X-ray fluorescence, mass spectrometry and laboratory synthesis for new synthetic elements. Whether an element counts as discovered depends on isolating a pure substance or establishing a clear spectral line, and older sources sometimes disagree over elements first seen in mixed ores.[8]

以锂为例，“锂会耗尽”的说法需要区分储量与资源。锂主要赋存于盐湖卤水、伟晶岩锂辉石和黏土中；储量指当前经济技术条件下可采出的部分，资源量则包括尚未经济开采的潜在总量。电池需求增长会推动勘探和新工艺开发，回收利用也在扩大，因此判断供应前景要看探明储量、提取成本、替代材料和回收比例，而不是宣布某一年份资源必然用完。With lithium, claims that it will run out require a distinction between reserves and resources. Lithium occurs mainly in brine lakes, spodumene pegmatites and clays. Reserves are what can be produced economically under current technology, while resources include potential tonnage not yet economical to mine. Rising battery demand drives exploration and new extraction processes, and recycling is expanding, so supply prospects depend on proven reserves, extraction cost, substitute materials and recycling rates rather than a fixed year when the metal must be gone.[3][6]

边界：丰度数值随参考层位变化，上地壳、整体地壳和地幔的估算并不相同；USGS 等机构的数据会随新采样和分析方法修订。储量是经济概念而非固定地质存量，价格和技术变化都会改变可采边界。元素发现年份取决于认定标准，早期文献与现代确认可能相差多年。引用丰度排序时应注明数据来源和层位，不能把地壳百分比直接当作地球整体或宇宙的组成比例。The boundary: abundance figures depend on the reference layer, and estimates for upper crust, whole crust and mantle differ. USGS and similar figures are revised as sampling and analytical methods improve. Reserves are an economic concept, not a fixed geological stock; price and technology shift what counts as recoverable. Discovery year depends on the recognition standard, and early records may differ from modern confirmation. When citing an abundance ranking, note the source and layer rather than treating crustal percentages as the composition of the whole Earth or the universe.[6][8]

06酸为什么有腐蚀性？pH 和氧化还原分别在其中起什么作用？Why are acids corrosive, and what roles do pH and redox play?

酸在水中释放氢离子，低 pH 促进金属溶解与有机组织水解；强氧化剂另经电子转移夺电子腐蚀。Acids release hydrogen ions in water; low pH promotes metal dissolution and organic hydrolysis, while oxidizers strip electrons through redox.

酸为什么能腐蚀金属、灼伤皮肤，是日常最直观的化学问题。腐蚀性并不是某一种单一反应，而是酸对金属、氧化物、有机物和生物组织产生破坏作用的统称；不同酸、不同浓度下，起主导作用的机制并不相同，要看它是靠氢离子还是靠氧化性起作用。Why acids corrode metals and burn skin is one of the most immediate chemical questions. Corrosiveness is not a single reaction: it is a general term for the damage acids inflict on metals, oxides, organic matter and living tissue. With different acids and concentrations, the leading mechanism varies, depending on whether hydrogen ions or oxidizing power does the work.[5]

酸在水中电离出氢离子，在水溶液里以水合氢离子 H₃O⁺ 形式存在；酸碱度用 pH 表示，pH = −log[H⁺]，pH 越小、氢离子浓度越高，酸性越强。胃酸 pH 约 1.5–3.5，酸雨 pH 低于 5.6。氢离子可以和活泼金属反应放出氢气，和金属氧化物、氢氧化物反应生成盐和水，原本保护金属的氧化层被溶解后，基体更容易继续被侵蚀。食醋是弱酸，电池液这类强酸在低浓度区间也有明显酸味，但不能凭味道判断浓度高低。Acids dissociate in water and release hydrogen ions, which exist as hydronium H₃O⁺ in solution. Acidity is expressed as pH, pH = −log[H⁺]; the lower the pH, the higher the hydrogen-ion concentration and the stronger the acid. Stomach acid runs about pH 1.5–3.5, and acid rain stays below pH 5.6. Hydrogen ions react with active metals to release hydrogen gas, and react with oxides and hydroxides to form salts and water; once a protective oxide layer dissolves, the underlying metal erodes more easily.[5][7][10]

对皮肤、蛋白质和碳水化合物这类有机物，低 pH 环境会催化水解反应，把长链分子的共价键切断；蛋白质发生变性，组织被破坏。灼伤程度不仅取决于酸度，还和浓度、接触时间、温度以及组织含水量有关；同样是酸，稀酸短时间接触可能只是刺激，浓酸长时间停留会造成更深的损伤。For skin, proteins and carbohydrates, a low-pH environment catalyzes hydrolysis and breaks the covalent bonds of long-chain molecules; proteins denature and tissue is damaged. Burn severity depends not only on acidity but also on concentration, contact time, temperature and tissue water content. A dilute acid touched briefly may only sting, while a concentrated acid left in contact longer causes deeper injury.[5][8]

除了氢离子，氧化还原是另一类腐蚀机制。盐酸主要靠氢离子起作用，而硝酸、浓硫酸还具有氧化性，中心元素或酸根在反应中得到电子，把金属氧化成高价盐，过程比单纯酸溶更剧烈。一种材料是否被腐蚀，是溶液 pH、氧化电位和金属本身性质共同决定的电极反应结果；电位越正的氧化剂，越容易从金属表面夺走电子。镁、锌等较活泼的金属容易被氧化溶解，铜、金等不活泼金属则需要电位更高的氧化剂才能发生反应。Beyond hydrogen ions, redox is a second corrosion mechanism. Hydrochloric acid acts mainly through hydrogen ions, but nitric acid and concentrated sulfuric acid are also oxidizers: the central element or anion gains electrons and oxidizes the metal into higher-valent salts, a more violent process than plain acid dissolution. Whether a material corrodes results from the combined effects of pH, oxidation potential and the metal itself; a more positive oxidizing potential strips electrons from the metal surface more readily.[8][9]

边界要写清：酸的腐蚀性不等于强酸都会冒烟，外观气味不能判断危险性；弱酸在高浓度或长时间接触下同样可能造成损伤。本章不展开工业酸洗配方和具体防护规程，碱、盐类对材料的腐蚀另有机制，不能套用酸的结论。遇到不明酸液，应按化学品安全规范处置，而不是凭经验中和。Boundary: an acid's corrosiveness does not mean every strong acid fumes in air, and looks or smell cannot judge hazard; a weak acid at high concentration, or left in contact long enough, can still cause damage. This chapter does not cover industrial pickling formulas or specific protection procedures; alkali and salt corrosion follow other mechanisms and must not be treated by analogy with acids. An unknown acid should be handled under chemical-safety rules rather than neutralized by intuition.[9][10]
