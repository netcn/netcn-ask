01肥皂为什么能洗掉油污，单用清水却不行？洗洁精的泡泡起什么作用？Why does soap remove grease while plain water cannot, and what role do detergent bubbles play?

表面活性剂双亲分子插入油滴围成胶束，乳化分散后随水冲走；泡沫多少不等于去污力，硬水易生成皂垢。Amphiphilic surfactants insert into oil drops, form micelles and emulsify grease for rinsing; foam volume is not cleaning power, and hard water forms scum.

肥皂和洗洁精能洗掉油污而单用清水不行，关键在于表面活性剂分子的双亲结构。水分子之间靠氢键相互吸引，表面张力较高，难以铺展并包裹疏油性的油脂；油污与水的界面能很高，清水冲过只能把油滴带走很少一部分。肥皂或洗洁精分子一端是亲水的离子或极性基团，另一端是疏水的碳氢长链，这种结构使它们能够同时接触水和油，在界面上降低界面张力。Soap and dish detergent remove grease where plain water fails because surfactant molecules have an amphiphilic structure. Water molecules attract one another through hydrogen bonds, giving high surface tension that makes it hard to spread over and coat oily grease. The oil-water interface carries high energy, so plain running water carries away only a little of the grease. Soap or detergent molecules carry a hydrophilic ionic or polar head at one end and a hydrophobic hydrocarbon tail at the other, which lets them touch both water and oil and lower interfacial tension.[1]

去污过程可以分成几步。表面活性剂先吸附在油污水滴界面，疏水尾部插入油滴内部，亲水头部留在水相；随着分子不断聚集，多个表面活性剂把油滴围成微小的胶束，油核被疏水尾包围，头部朝外与水结合。原本漂浮或粘附在餐具、织物上的油脂被乳化分散成细小乳浊液，不再重新聚集成大油滴，随漂洗水流离开表面。The cleaning process runs in stages. Surfactant first adsorbs at the oil-water interface, the hydrophobic tail inserts into the oil drop and the hydrophilic head stays in the water. As more molecules gather, several surfactants surround the drop into a tiny micelle, with the oil core wrapped by tails and heads facing outward toward the water. Grease floating on or sticking to dishes and fabric is emulsified into a fine emulsion, cannot regroup into large drops, and leaves the surface with the rinse water.[1][7]

洗洁精产生大量泡沫，同样来自表面活性剂降低表面张力的作用。空气进入液体被液膜包裹成气泡，表面活性剂在膜两侧排列，使泡沫能够暂时稳定存在。但泡沫多少并不等于去污能力：丰富的泡沫主要改善观感和携带污物的观感，真正决定清洁效果的是表面活性剂种类、浓度、水温、浸泡时间和漂洗程度，低泡配方同样可以洗得干净。The rich foam of dish detergent also comes from the surfactant lowering surface tension. Air entering the liquid is wrapped in a liquid film, and surfactant arranges on both sides of the film so bubbles stay stable for a while. Foam volume, however, is not equal to cleaning power: plenty of foam mostly improves appearance and the look of carried soil. Actual cleaning depends on surfactant type and concentration, water temperature, soaking time and rinsing; low-foam formulas can clean just as well.[1][3]

硬水会影响肥皂效果。水中的钙、镁离子与肥皂中的脂肪酸根结合，生成不溶于水的皂垢沉淀，既消耗肥皂又在衣物和餐具上留痕。合成洗衣粉和洗洁精配方中加入螯合剂或分散成分，就是为了络合钙镁离子、减少皂垢生成。洗衣液、餐洗液的差异主要在表面活性剂搭配、酶制剂和助洗剂，而不是单纯靠“泡沫多”判断优劣。Hard water affects soap performance. Calcium and magnesium ions combine with fatty acid anions in soap to form insoluble soap scum, consuming soap and leaving marks on fabric and dishes. Synthetic laundry and dish formulas include chelating or dispersing ingredients that bind calcium and magnesium to reduce scum. The difference between laundry liquids and dish liquids lies mainly in surfactant blends, enzymes and builders, not in judging quality by foam alone.[1][7]

边界：表面活性剂的去污能力受油污种类、水温和水质硬度影响，不能把某一种配方的效果推广到所有污渍。胶束形成需要达到一定浓度，过低浓度下乳化不充分；泡沫稳定性还受温度和机械搅拌影响。清洁剂残留、手肤刺激和环境降解属于另一组问题，应按产品说明使用并充分漂洗，而不是只凭去油强弱做判断。The boundary: surfactant performance depends on soil type, water temperature and hardness, so one formula's result cannot be generalized to every stain. Micelles form only above a certain concentration; below it emulsification is incomplete, and foam stability also changes with temperature and mechanical agitation. Residue, skin irritation and environmental biodegradability are separate issues; follow product directions and rinse thoroughly instead of judging a cleaner by grease-cutting strength alone.[1][3]

02为什么生鸡蛋煮熟后会变硬？米饭是怎样由米变成饭的？Why does a raw egg harden when cooked, and how does rice turn from grain to cooked rice?

加热使蛋白质展开交联成凝胶，淀粉颗粒吸水膨胀糊化；两者都需水和热，过度加热会让质地变老。Heat unfolds and cross-links proteins into a gel, while starch granules swell and gelatinize; both need water and heat, and overheating toughens texture.

生鸡蛋煮熟后由液态变成硬凝胶，核心变化是蛋白质受热变性。生蛋白和蛋黄中的蛋白质以折叠的空间结构存在，疏水部分藏在内部；加热提供的能量破坏维持构象的氢键、二硫键和疏水作用，蛋白质链展开，原本藏在内部的基团暴露出来。展开的蛋白质分子之间重新交联，连成连续的网状结构，把水分锁在网眼之中，蛋液就从可流动的溶胶变成紧实的凝胶。A raw egg turns into a firm gel when cooked, and the core change is heat-induced protein denaturation. In raw white and yolk, proteins hold a folded spatial structure with hydrophobic parts tucked inside. Heat breaks the hydrogen bonds, disulfide bonds and hydrophobic forces that maintain the conformation, protein chains unfold, and groups once hidden inside become exposed. Unfolded molecules then cross-link into a continuous network that traps water in its meshes, so the runny sol sets into a firm gel.[2]

米饭由硬米变成软糯米饭，主要靠淀粉糊化。大米中的淀粉以颗粒形式存在，内部是有序的结晶区；加水加热时，淀粉颗粒吸收水分膨胀，结晶区逐渐松弛融化，直链淀粉从颗粒中溶出，颗粒破裂并相互黏连。淀粉分子吸水后形成黏稠的凝胶体系，米粒由硬脆变得柔软有弹性；这个过程需要足够的水分和温度，水太少或火候不足都会夹生。Raw rice turning into soft cooked rice relies mainly on starch gelatinization. Starch in the grain exists as granules with ordered crystalline regions. As water and heat are applied, granules absorb water and swell, crystalline regions relax and melt, amylose leaches out of the granules, and granules rupture and stick together. The starch forms a viscous gel system, and the hard brittle grain becomes soft and springy; this needs enough water and temperature, and too little water or heat leaves it undercooked.[3][7]

加热改变食物的两条路径都依赖水和热的配合。蛋白质变性关注的是分子构象被破坏后重新交联，淀粉糊化关注的是颗粒吸水膨胀、结晶融化；二者都不是简单的“烤熟烘干”。不同蛋白质的变性温度不同，蛋清在约 60 °C 以上逐步凝固，蛋黄凝固温度略低；淀粉在常温水中几乎不糊化，需要接近沸点的温度和持续吸水。Both paths of heat-induced food change rely on water and heat together. Protein denaturation concerns conformation being disrupted and then re-cross-linked, while starch gelatinization concerns granules swelling, crystallites melting and sticking; neither is simple drying over heat. Different proteins denature at different temperatures: egg white sets above about 60 °C, yolk slightly lower, and starch barely gelatinizes in cold water, needing near-boiling temperature and continued water uptake.[2][7]

烹饪中常见的色泽和香气来自另一类反应。美拉德反应是还原糖与氨基酸在加热下发生的复杂缩合，产生金黄褐色和香气物质；焦糖化则是糖单独受热分解。它们与蛋白质变性、淀粉糊化并行发生，但机制不同：变性和糊化改变质地，美拉德与焦化改变颜色和风味。理解这些区别，才能解释为什么同样加热，鸡蛋凝固、米饭变软、肉香上色是各自不同的过程。Color and aroma in cooking come from another class of reactions. The Maillard reaction is a complex condensation between reducing sugars and amino acids under heat, producing golden-brown color and aroma compounds; caramelization is sugar decomposing on its own. They run alongside denaturation and gelatinization but differ in mechanism: denaturation and gelatinization change texture, while Maillard and caramelization change color and flavor. Distinguishing these explains why heating sets egg, softens rice and browns meat through separate processes.[2][3]

边界：蛋白质变性通常不可逆，放凉后也不会恢复成生蛋液；过度加热会让水分过度析出，蛋羹变老发柴。淀粉糊化需要充足水分，水分不足时即使温度够高，米粒也不能完全软烂；不同品种大米的直链淀粉含量不同，口感软硬有别。变性和糊化程度受加热时间、温度和配比影响，引用温度时应注明食材与条件，不能把某个凝固点当作所有蛋白质的统一数值。The boundary: protein denaturation is usually irreversible and will not return to raw liquid on cooling; overheating drives out too much water and makes the egg tough and rubbery. Starch gelatinization needs ample water; without it the grain cannot fully soften even at high temperature, and rice varieties differ in amylose content and tenderness. Denaturation and gelatinization depend on heating time, temperature and ratio; temperature figures should cite the ingredient and condition rather than treating one setting point as universal for all proteins.[2][7]

03香蕉为什么放久了会变黑？这种变色还能不能吃？Why does a banana darken as it ages, and is browned fruit still safe to eat?

PPO 在破损组织中把多酚氧化成醌再聚合成黑色素；低温隔氧可减缓，变色本身不等于变质，需结合气味质地判断。PPO oxidizes polyphenols to quinones that polymerize into melanin in damaged tissue; cold and oxygen exclusion slow it, and color alone does not mean spoilage.

香蕉放久了表皮变黑，主要是酶促褐变在起作用。香蕉果肉和果皮中的多酚类物质在多酚氧化酶（PPO）催化下接触氧气，先被氧化成醌类中间体，醌再进一步聚合形成褐色甚至黑色的黑色素多聚物。果皮出现褐斑，说明局部细胞已经破损，酶、底物和氧三者相遇，褐变反应启动。这与切开的苹果、土豆放久变色属于同一类机制。Bananas darken with age mainly through enzymatic browning. Polyphenols in the flesh and peel are oxidized by polyphenol oxidase (PPO) when they meet oxygen, first into quinone intermediates that then polymerize into brown or black melanin-like polymers. Brown spots on the peel mean local cells have broken so enzyme, substrate and oxygen come together and the browning reaction starts. The same mechanism turns cut apples and potatoes brown over time.[2]

成熟和机械损伤会加速这一过程。香蕉采收后继续后熟，乙烯调节呼吸高峰，淀粉逐渐转化为糖，果皮组织衰老、细胞壁强度下降；搬运挤压造成的碰伤破坏细胞分隔，使原本区隔在不同细胞器中的酶与多酚接触。随着成熟度提高，果皮叶绿素分解、底色转黄，褐色斑点随之扩大，外观由黄转暗。Ripening and mechanical damage speed this up. Bananas continue to ripen after harvest; ethylene regulates the respiratory climacteric, starch converts to sugar, peel tissue ages and cell-wall strength drops. Bruising from handling breaks cell compartments, bringing enzymes and polyphenols that were separated in different organelles into contact. As ripening advances, peel chlorophyll breaks down, the background turns yellow, and brown spots spread until the peel darkens overall.[2][3]

日常条件可以从几个环节减缓褐变。低温降低酶活性和呼吸速率，密封隔氧减少氧气供应，酸性环境抑制 PPO 活性，柠檬汁中的抗坏血酸还能还原已生成的醌类。家庭保存时把香蕉与其他释放乙烯的水果分开、避免挤压，能延缓表皮斑点出现；但已经变褐的果肉若伴随软烂、发酵味或霉变，就不再适合食用。Ordinary storage can slow browning at several points. Low temperature lowers enzyme activity and respiration; sealing limits oxygen; an acidic environment inhibits PPO, and ascorbic acid in lemon juice reduces quinones already formed. Keeping bananas away from other ethylene-releasing fruit and avoiding bruising delays peel spotting. Flesh that has turned brown alongside softening, fermented odor or mold is no longer suitable to eat.[3][7]

需要区分酶促褐变与加热引起的变色。酶促褐变由生物酶在常温下催化，发生在破损或成熟组织中；美拉德反应和焦糖化需要加热，产生的是烹饪色泽与香气。二者都让食物变褐，但反应路径、温度条件和风味产物不同。看到食物变色就判断变质并不准确：香蕉皮斑点、苹果切口浅褐可能只是酶促氧化，质地和气味未坏时仍可食用。Enzymatic browning should be separated from heat-driven color changes. Enzymatic browning is catalyzed by biological enzymes at ambient temperature in damaged or ripening tissue; Maillard reaction and caramelization require heat and produce cooked color and aroma. Both brown food, but their pathways, temperature conditions and flavor products differ. Color change alone does not mean spoilage: peel spots or light browning on a cut apple may be enzymatic oxidation, and the food can still be eaten if texture and smell are intact.[2][7]

边界：褐变速度受品种、成熟度、温度、氧气和机械损伤程度影响，不能凭斑点多少单独判断安全性。酶促褐变只改变色泽和部分风味，不必然产生毒素；但微生物引起的腐败往往伴随软烂、异味和霉斑，需要另行判断。家庭储存条件差异很大，引用抑制方法时应注明温度、隔氧和处理方式，不能把某一种保鲜手段推广到所有果蔬。The boundary: browning rate depends on variety, ripeness, temperature, oxygen and mechanical damage, so spot count alone cannot judge safety. Enzymatic browning changes color and some flavor without necessarily producing toxins, while microbial decay usually comes with softening, off-odor and mold that must be judged separately. Home storage varies widely; preservation advice should state temperature, oxygen control and treatment rather than generalize one method to every fruit or vegetable.[3][7]

04奶酪为什么能陈化那么久？蜂蜜为什么几乎不会坏？Why can cheese age so long, and why does honey hardly ever spoil?

奶酪靠受控微生物分解蛋白脂肪并在低水分高盐下成熟；蜂蜜 aw 0.56–0.62、高糖高酸抑制微生物，吸水后仍可能发酵。Cheese ripens under controlled microbes in low moisture and salt; honey's aw 0.56–0.62, high sugar and acid suppress microbes, but it can ferment if wet.

奶酪能长期陈化而多数食物很快变质，依靠的是受控微生物活动与水分、盐分、酸度的配合。制作奶酪时先通过凝乳和排乳清把大部分水分去掉，再由乳酸菌和后续发酵微生物分解蛋白质与脂肪：蛋白质分解出氨基酸和肽，脂肪分解出脂肪酸，进一步反应生成酯类、硫化物等香气物质。陈化在温度、湿度受控的窖房中进行，腐败菌受低水分、高盐和酸性环境抑制，风味则在数月到数年间缓慢积累。Cheese can age for a long time while most food spoils quickly, thanks to controlled microbial activity together with water, salt and acidity. Cheesemaking first removes much of the water through curdling and whey drainage. Lactic acid bacteria and later ripening microbes then break down proteins and fats: proteins yield amino acids and peptides, fats yield fatty acids, and further reactions produce esters, sulfur compounds and other aroma substances. Aging proceeds in temperature- and humidity-controlled cellars; spoilage organisms are held back by low moisture, salt and acidity, while flavor accumulates slowly over months or years.[1][3]

蜂蜜长期不易坏，原因集中在低水分活度、高糖和酸性环境。综述数据显示成熟蜂蜜的水分活度 aw 约在 0.56 到 0.62 之间，pH 约 3.9；高浓度糖产生高渗透压，细菌和真菌细胞中的水分被吸出，难以生长繁殖。蜂蜜中的葡萄糖氧化酶还能缓慢生成少量过氧化氢，配合酸性环境形成额外抑制。密封、干燥存放时，蜂蜜可以存放多年而不发生明显腐败，结晶只是糖的物理析出，并不代表变质。食盐同理：纯 NaCl 是无机矿物，高盐把水分活度压到约 0.90 以下，细胞失水后难以生长，纯盐本身不腐败。Honey keeps for so long because of low water activity, high sugar and acidity. Review data put ripe honey's water activity aw at about 0.56 to 0.62 and pH near 3.9. Concentrated sugar creates high osmotic pressure that draws water out of bacterial and fungal cells and blocks growth. Glucose oxidase in honey also slowly produces a little hydrogen peroxide, adding inhibition alongside the acidic environment. Sealed and kept dry, honey can sit for years without obvious spoilage; crystallization is just sugar separating physically and does not mean it has gone bad. Salt follows the same route: pure NaCl is inorganic, and high salt depresses water activity below about 0.90, plasmolyzing cells so plain salt does not spoil.[4][11]

发酵和陈化本质上是利用可控微生物改造原料。菌种、温湿度和水分被限定在有利于目标微生物的范围内，蛋白质、脂肪和糖类被分解或转化，风味物质富集；它与腐败的区别不在“有没有微生物”，而在菌种是否受控、条件是否卫生、水分和酸度是否把杂菌压住。奶酪、酸奶、腌制品和发酵饮品都遵循这一思路，只是微生物群落和工艺参数不同。Fermentation and aging are, at their core, controlled microbes transforming the raw material. Strains, temperature, humidity and moisture are kept in a range favorable to target microbes, while proteins, fats and sugars are broken down or converted and flavor compounds concentrate. The difference from spoilage is not whether microbes are present, but whether the strains are controlled, conditions are sanitary, and moisture and acidity suppress undesirable organisms. Cheese, yogurt, cured foods and fermented drinks follow this logic with different communities and parameters.[1][3]

保存条件一旦失控，这两套机制都会失效。蜂蜜如果敞开存放、吸收环境水分，含水量上升后耐渗酵母可能引起发酵起泡；陈化奶酪若温湿度不当或卫生不足，杂菌和霉菌会在表面和内部生长。低水分活度、高渗透压和酸度提供的是抑制而非无菌保证，食物仍然可能受包装、污染和储存条件影响。食盐受潮易结块，加碘盐的碘在光照、高温、潮湿下缓慢挥发，保质期主要对应这类品质变化。If storage conditions fail, both mechanisms break down. Honey left open absorbs moisture; once water content rises, osmotolerant yeasts can cause foamy fermentation. Cheese aging with wrong temperature, humidity or hygiene allows unwanted bacteria and molds to grow on the surface and inside. Low water activity, high osmotic pressure and acidity provide inhibition rather than sterility, and food remains exposed to packaging, contamination and storage conditions. Salt is hygroscopic and cakes when damp; iodide volatilizes slowly under light, heat and humidity, so a best-by date tracks quality changes rather than decay of NaCl.[3][4][11]

边界：蜂蜜的 aw 和 pH 随花种、产地和成熟度变化，综述数值是典型区间而非固定常数；“蜂蜜永不变质”指密封干燥条件下不易腐败，吸水后仍可能发酵。奶酪陈化时间与风味、安全性的关系取决于工艺卫生和成熟条件，不能把某种奶酪的陈化年限推广到所有品种。判断保存是否成功要结合水分、酸度、气味和微生物状态，而不是只看存放时间长短。The boundary: honey's aw and pH vary with blossom source, origin and ripeness, and review figures are typical ranges, not fixed constants. Honey never spoils means it resists spoilage when sealed and dry; after absorbing water it can still ferment. The link between cheese aging time, flavor and safety depends on process hygiene and ripening conditions, so one cheese's aging span cannot be generalized to every variety. Whether preservation succeeds depends on moisture, acidity, odor and microbial state, not storage duration alone.[3][4]

05土豆发芽后为什么不能吃？不粘锅涂层刮花后为什么更要注意？Why should sprouted potatoes be avoided, and why does a scratched non-stick coating need attention?

发芽青皮土豆含茄碱等糖苷生物碱，数 mg/kg 即可中毒；PTFE 约 360 °C 以上热解，风险主要来自空烧过热。Sprouted green potatoes carry solanine glycoalkaloids toxic at several mg per kg; PTFE pyrolyzes above about 360 °C, with risk mainly from overheating rather than fine scratches.

土豆发芽或表皮变绿后不宜食用，风险来自糖苷生物碱。土豆中的 α-茄碱（α-solanine）和 α-卡茄碱（α-chaconine）属于甾体生物碱，在芽眼、幼芽和见光变绿的皮层中含量显著升高；正常块茎含量较低，发芽和光照刺激合成后，苦味明显增强。这类物质刺激胃肠道，也可作用于神经系统，误食较多时出现恶心、呕吐、腹痛、腹泻、乏力等症状，严重时影响心率和意识。Sprouted or green-skinned potatoes should not be eaten because of glycoalkaloids. Alpha-solanine and alpha-chaconine in potatoes are steroidal glycoalkaloids whose levels rise sharply in eyes, young sprouts and light-greened skin. Normal tubers are low in these compounds, but sprouting and light stimulate synthesis, and the bitter taste becomes obvious. They irritate the digestive tract and can also affect the nervous system; eating enough causes nausea, vomiting, abdominal pain, diarrhea and weakness, with severe cases affecting heart rate and consciousness.[5][8]

剂量决定风险程度。德国联邦风险评估研究所等机构提示，发青和强烈发芽的块茎应当剔除；文献中每公斤体重 3–6 mg 糖苷生物碱被视为可能致死的剂量范围。家庭处理时，仅把芽挖掉并不安全，因为毒素已扩散到周围组织；表皮大面积发绿、芽长较多的土豆建议整体丢弃，未发芽且仅有少量青皮的情况也应削去厚皮并去除芽眼，出现苦味就不要再吃。Dose sets the level of risk. Germany's Federal Institute for Risk Assessment advises discarding green and strongly sprouted tubers; the literature puts a potentially lethal range at 3 to 6 mg of glycoalkaloids per kilogram of body weight. At home, digging out only the sprout is not safe because the toxin has spread into surrounding tissue. Potatoes with broad green skin or long sprouts should be discarded whole; those without sprouts but with small green patches need thick peeling and eye removal, and any bitter taste means they should not be eaten.[5][8]

不粘锅涂层以聚四氟乙烯（PTFE）为主。按厂商与风险评估机构的数据，涂层在约 260 °C 以下外观和性能保持稳定；明显热分解通常要到约 350–360 °C 以上，超过这一温度会释放聚合物烟雾，吸入后可能引起类似流感的聚合物烟雾热。日常煎炒、水煮的油温多在分解温度以下，真正需要避免的是空锅长时间干烧、高温空烤和把锅留在灶上无人看管。Non-stick coatings are mainly polytetrafluoroethylene, PTFE. According to manufacturers and risk-assessment bodies, the coating keeps its appearance and performance below about 260 °C; noticeable decomposition usually appears above about 350 to 360 °C, above which polymer fumes are released and inhalation can cause polymer fume fever resembling influenza. Ordinary frying, sautéing and boiling stay below decomposition temperature; what to avoid is leaving an empty pan to dry-burn, empty baking at high heat, or leaving a pan unattended on the stove.[6]

涂层刮花后需要注意的重点与毒性判断要分开看。细微划痕本身不意味着涂层已经大量释放有害物质；但磨损加重后，涂层可能成片脱落，食物接触金属基底，碎屑混入菜肴。使用时避免金属铲、钢丝球和骤冷骤热，发现涂层大面积起皮、起泡或开裂后更换锅具，比纠结划痕本身更有实际意义。A scratched coating should be assessed separately from toxicity. Fine scratches do not by themselves mean large amounts of harmful material are being released, but as wear worsens the coating can peel in flakes, food contacts the metal base and crumbs enter the dish. Avoid metal spatulas, steel wool and rapid temperature shocks; replace the pan when large areas blister, peel or crack, which matters more than worrying about the first scratch.[5][6]

边界：龙葵素含量受光照、发芽程度和品种影响，不同土豆差异较大，不能凭外观精确估算剂量；轻度摄入与严重中毒之间存在剂量区间，出现持续呕吐、头晕或肢体无力应及时就医。PTFE 的分解温度随配方和受热时间变化，安全结论针对正常家用烹饪；空锅过热、鸟类宠物对聚合物烟雾敏感等情形需要单独提示。厨房风险应按食材状态、温度和产品说明综合判断，不能把单一物质的毒性简单套用到所有烹饪场景。The boundary: solanine levels vary with light, sprouting and variety, so appearance cannot estimate dose precisely; there is a dose range between mild intake and serious poisoning, and persistent vomiting, dizziness or limb weakness warrants medical care. PTFE decomposition temperature varies with formulation and heating time, and safety conclusions apply to normal home cooking; empty-pan overheating and the special sensitivity of pet birds to polymer fumes need separate mention. Kitchen risk should be judged from ingredient state, temperature and product instructions rather than applying one substance's toxicity to every cooking scenario.[5][6]

06小苏打为什么能除臭？食物为什么会变质，一氧化碳为什么会中毒？Why does baking soda deodorize, and what lie behind food spoilage and carbon monoxide poisoning?

小苏打靠酸碱中和与多孔吸附减轻异味；变质取决于水分活度与微生物生长；CO 与血红蛋白亲和力远高于氧气，造成缺氧。Baking soda neutralizes acids and adsorbs odors; spoilage depends on water activity and microbial growth; CO binds hemoglobin far more tightly than oxygen, starving tissues.

小苏打（碳酸氢钠 NaHCO₃）常被放在冰箱、鞋柜里除味。许多难闻气味来自酸性挥发物，碳酸氢根可以和酸反应生成盐和二氧化碳，把酸性气味物质中和掉；粉末本身多孔，有一定吸附作用，能把一部分气味分子留在表面。需要注意，小苏打是缓和的除味剂，不是杀菌神器，它不能分解所有异味，对碱性气味作用有限，也替代不了清洁和通风。Baking soda, sodium bicarbonate NaHCO₃, is often kept in fridges and shoe closets to remove odors. Many unpleasant smells come from acidic volatiles; bicarbonate reacts with acids to form salts and carbon dioxide, neutralizing acidic odor compounds. The powder is also porous and adsorbs some odor molecules onto its surface. It is a mild deodorizer, not a sterilizer: it cannot break down every odor, works less on alkaline smells, and cannot replace cleaning and ventilation.[3][10]

食物为什么会变质，主要看微生物能不能长起来。细菌、酵母和霉菌在适宜温度、水分和营养条件下繁殖，同时伴随脂肪氧化、酶解和变色。衡量水分是否可被利用，常用水分活度 aw，它是食物中自由水蒸气压与同温纯水蒸气压之比；多数生鲜食品 aw 在 0.95 以上，足以支持细菌生长，糖、盐、干燥和冷冻可以降低 aw 或抑制繁殖速度。Why food spoils depends mainly on whether microorganisms can grow. Bacteria, yeasts and molds reproduce under suitable temperature, moisture and nutrients, alongside fat oxidation, enzymatic breakdown and discoloration. Whether moisture is available is measured by water activity aw, the ratio of the vapor pressure of free water in the food to that of pure water at the same temperature. Most fresh foods sit above aw 0.95, enough to support bacterial growth; sugar, salt, drying and freezing lower aw or slow reproduction.[4][10]

微生物在食物里通常经历适应期、对数增长期、稳定期和衰亡期。冷藏只能减慢代谢和繁殖，并不能杀灭已有微生物；加热可以杀死大部分繁殖体，细菌芽孢则需要更高的温度或更长时间才能灭活。保质期是在特定包装和储存条件下的估计时间，开封后水分、温度和接触环境改变，变质会加快，不能照搬原包装上的日期。Microbes in food usually pass through a lag phase, a logarithmic growth phase, a stationary phase and a decline phase. Refrigeration only slows metabolism and reproduction and does not kill existing microbes; heating kills most vegetative cells, while bacterial spores need higher temperature or longer time to inactivate. Shelf life is an estimate under particular packaging and storage conditions; once opened, moisture, temperature and contact change, spoilage speeds up, and the original date on the package no longer applies.[3][4]

一氧化碳中毒是另一类完全不同的机制。CO 是含碳燃料不完全燃烧的产物，无色、无味、无刺激性，靠嗅觉根本察觉不到。它与血红蛋白的亲和力约为氧气的 200–250 倍，抢先结合血红蛋白形成碳氧血红蛋白，氧气难以结合和释放，全身组织因此缺氧；早期症状像感冒，头痛、头晕、乏力，高浓度暴露可致昏迷甚至死亡。Carbon monoxide poisoning follows a different mechanism altogether. CO forms from incomplete combustion of carbon-based fuels and is colorless, odorless and non-irritating, so smell cannot detect it. Its affinity for hemoglobin is about 200–250 times that of oxygen; it binds first to form carboxyhemoglobin, leaving oxygen little chance to bind and be released, so tissues throughout the body are starved of oxygen. Early symptoms resemble a cold: headache, dizziness and weakness, and high-concentration exposure can cause coma or death.[9]

边界要写清：除臭、食物变质和 CO 中毒是三条不同机制，不能互相套用。小苏打除味靠中和与吸附，不能当消毒剂使用；判断食物能否食用不能只凭气味，霉烂、变色和异常质地都应警惕；CO 无色无味，预防要靠通风、定期检查灶具热水器和安装一氧化碳报警器，不能靠闻味道发现。Boundary: deodorizing, food spoilage and CO poisoning are three distinct mechanisms and must not be mixed. Baking soda works through neutralization and adsorption, not disinfection; whether food is safe cannot be judged by smell alone, and mold, discoloration or abnormal texture are warning signs. CO cannot be smelled, so prevention relies on ventilation, regular checks of stoves and water heaters, and a CO alarm rather than the nose.[3][9]

07水壶里的水垢是怎么形成的？How does limescale form in a kettle?

硬水中的钙镁碳酸氢盐受热分解，放出二氧化碳并析出难溶碳酸盐，沉积在壶壁和加热元件上。Bicarbonates of calcium and magnesium in hard water decompose on heating, release CO2 and precipitate insoluble carbonates that deposit on walls and heating elements.

水垢的来源是水中溶解的矿物质。不少地区的自来水属于硬水，含有溶解状态的钙离子和镁离子，主要以碳酸氢钙、碳酸氢镁的形式存在。这些碳酸氢盐溶于水，平时肉眼看不到；水流经过壶壁、管道和加热元件时，溶解的离子随水温升高发生化学变化，才在固体表面析出固体沉积。地下水在流经岩层时溶解岩石中的碳酸盐矿物，雨水含二氧化碳形成弱酸，帮助矿物溶解；不同地区水质软硬差别，主要由岩层和水源决定。Limescale comes from minerals dissolved in water. Tap water in many regions is hard, containing dissolved calcium and magnesium ions, mainly as calcium and magnesium bicarbonates. These bicarbonates dissolve invisibly; as water passes kettle walls, pipes and heating elements, the dissolved ions change chemically with rising temperature and deposit as solid material on the solid surface.[7][12]

受热是析出的直接原因。碳酸氢钙在加热条件下分解为碳酸钙、二氧化碳和水；碳酸氢镁也发生类似分解。碳酸钙和碳酸镁在水中的溶解度很低，温度升高后进一步下降，难以继续留在溶液里，便以细小固体颗粒析出。析出颗粒附着在粗糙的壶壁和温度最高的加热元件表面，逐层堆积，逐渐形成白色或灰白色的硬质水垢。垢层常先出现在加热管和壶底这些与高温接触最紧密的位置，因为局部温度最高，碳酸盐最先达到过饱和并形成晶核。Heating is the direct cause of precipitation. On heating, calcium bicarbonate decomposes into calcium carbonate, carbon dioxide and water; magnesium bicarbonate behaves similarly. Calcium and magnesium carbonates have very low solubility in water, and solubility drops further as temperature rises, so they cannot stay in solution and separate as fine solid particles. The particles attach to rough kettle walls and the hottest heating elements, layer by layer, gradually building a white or gray-white hard scale.[7][12]

这一类由煮沸去除的硬度叫暂时硬度。钙镁碳酸氢盐受热沉淀后，水中可溶的钙镁离子减少；反复烧水后壶底和加热管上积累的垢层越来越厚，导热变差，加热时能耗升高。水垢主要成分是碳酸盐，遇酸会反应并放出二氧化碳，表面出现气泡，这也是酸性物质可以溶解垢层的化学依据。垢层致密程度不一，初期松散易脱落，反复受热后变得坚硬；除垢时观察到的气泡，正是碳酸盐与酸反应重新生成的二氧化碳。This hardness removed by boiling is called temporary hardness. After the bicarbonates precipitate on boiling, soluble calcium and magnesium ions in the water decrease. Repeated boiling thickens the deposit on the kettle bottom and heating tube, worsening heat conduction and raising energy use. Scale is mainly carbonate; it reacts with acid and releases carbon dioxide, showing bubbles on the surface, which is the chemical basis for acid dissolving the layer.[7][12]

水垢和皂垢不是同一种东西。皂垢是肥皂中的脂肪酸与钙镁离子结合生成的不溶性皂类，多附着在衣物、浴缸和皮肤上；水垢是矿物质受热析出的无机碳酸盐，主要出现在加热过的水器内壁。两者都与硬水离子有关，但生成反应和产物成分不同，外观和附着位置也有区别。肥皂本身是脂肪酸盐，与硬水离子反应后起泡和清洁能力下降；日常看到的白色浮渣多属皂垢，而壶内硬质结壳属水垢。Limescale is not the same as soap scum. Soap scum is insoluble soap formed when fatty acids in soap bind calcium and magnesium ions, clinging to clothing, tubs and skin; limescale is inorganic carbonate precipitated by heating, mainly on the inner walls of heated water appliances. Both relate to hard-water ions, but the reaction and product differ, as do appearance and attachment locations.[1][7]

边界：水垢本身不是有毒污染物，厚垢主要影响导热和清洁；本章讲矿物质溶解—析出的一般化学机制，不提供除垢剂配方、电器保养或饮用水硬度是否达标的判断。水质成分因地而异，长期饮水与管路问题应结合当地检测数据评估。本章不评价除垢食品或偏方是否安全有效，也不把水垢描述为结石或直接的健康威胁。Boundary: scale itself is not a toxic contaminant; a thick layer mainly affects conduction and cleaning. This chapter explains the general chemistry of mineral dissolution and precipitation, not descaling-agent recipes, appliance maintenance or a judgment on whether drinking-water hardness meets a standard. Water composition varies by location, and long-term drinking-water or pipeline issues should be assessed against local test data.[1][12]
