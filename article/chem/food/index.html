01酸奶是怎么做出来的，和牛奶有什么不同？How is yogurt made, and how does it differ from milk?

选定乳酸菌把乳糖转为乳酸，pH 降到约 4.6 时酪蛋白聚连成凝胶；受控发酵不同于腐败变质。Selected cultures turn lactose into lactic acid; near pH 4.6 casein aggregates into a gel, and controlled fermentation differs from spoilage.

酸奶不是放坏了的牛奶，而是用选定的乳酸菌在受控条件下发酵乳类得到的发酵乳制品。典型工艺先把奶加热灭菌并均质，降温到约 40–45 °C 后接入嗜热链球菌和保加利亚乳杆菌，在恒温环境中保温数小时；菌种把乳糖转化为乳酸，酸度升高、蛋白质凝固，乳状液逐渐变成稠厚的凝胶。Yogurt is not spoiled milk; it is a fermented dairy product made by letting selected lactic acid bacteria act on milk under controlled conditions. Milk is first heated to reduce competing microbes and homogenized, then cooled to about 40–45 °C and inoculated with Streptococcus thermophilus and Lactobacillus delbrueckii subsp. bulgaricus. Held at a steady temperature for several hours, the cultures convert lactose into lactic acid; acidity rises, proteins set, and the fluid milk thickens into a gel.[1][5]

两种菌之间存在协同关系：保加利亚乳杆菌先分解乳蛋白，释放小肽和氨基酸供嗜热链球菌生长；链球菌产生的甲酸、叶酸等生长因子又反过来促进乳杆菌繁殖。发酵的核心反应是乳糖经同型乳酸发酵生成乳酸，乳糖含量随发酵下降，乳酸积累使 pH 从鲜奶的约 6.6 逐步降到 4.6 附近。The two starter bacteria cooperate. L. bulgaricus breaks down milk proteins into peptides and amino acids that feed S. thermophilus, while S. thermophilus produces formate and folate that support the lactobacillus. The central reaction is homolactic fermentation: lactose is converted to lactic acid, lactose content falls, and pH drops from about 6.6 in fresh milk toward 4.6.[1][6]

牛奶中的酪蛋白以胶束形式分散在水相中，表面由 κ-酪蛋白保护层维持稳定；当 pH 降到酪蛋白等电点附近约 4.6 时，胶束表面电荷减少、保护层被酸破坏，酪蛋白束相互靠近并聚连成三维网络，把水相和脂肪球包裹在网格里，液体奶因此凝固成酸奶的凝胶结构。这一过程与加酸点制豆腐的原理相近，只是酸由菌种缓慢产生。Casein in milk exists as micelles kept apart by a kappa-casein surface layer. Near the casein isoelectric point around pH 4.6, surface charge drops and that protective layer breaks down; casein bundles then aggregate into a three-dimensional network that traps water and fat globules, turning the liquid into a gel. The mechanism resembles acid-coagulated tofu, except the acid is produced slowly by bacteria rather than added at once.[2][6]

从成分看，酸奶保留了牛奶中的蛋白质、钙和脂肪，部分乳糖被转化为乳酸，乳糖含量比鲜奶略低；鲜奶蛋白质约 3.2–3.5%，发酵后剩余碳水以乳酸和少量残留乳糖形式存在。未经过二次加热的产品在冷链中保持活菌。市售酸奶常额外加糖、果酱或增稠剂，质地和酸度会随配方变化。USDA 食品成分数据库可以核对不同品类奶与酸奶的蛋白质、脂肪和碳水含量。Yogurt keeps the protein, calcium and fat of milk, while part of the lactose has been turned into lactic acid, so its lactose load is somewhat lower than fresh milk. Live cultures remain in products that have not been reheated after fermentation. Commercial yogurts often add sugar, fruit preserves or stabilizers, which change texture and acidity; the USDA FoodData Central database lists protein, fat and carbohydrate values for milk and yogurt categories.[5]

需要区分受控发酵与腐败变质：变质奶由杂菌在不受控温度下繁殖，常伴随异味、结块和致病菌风险；酸奶使用明确菌种、规定温度和卫生条件。酸奶并非完全不含乳糖，严重乳糖不耐受者仍可能出现不适；经过巴氏二次加热的酸奶不再含活菌；植物基酸奶改用其他菌种或酶体系凝固，机制与乳基酸奶并不完全相同。Controlled fermentation differs from spoilage. Spoiled milk develops when mixed unwanted microbes multiply at uncontrolled temperatures, often with off-flavors, curdling and pathogen risk; yogurt uses defined cultures, a set temperature and hygienic conditions. Yogurt still contains some lactose, so people with severe lactose intolerance may still react; heat-treated yogurt has no live cultures; plant-based yogurts use other cultures or enzymes and do not follow the same dairy pathway.[2][6]

02奶酪上的孔是怎么来的？Where do the holes in cheese come from?

瑞士型干酪的眼孔来自丙酸杆菌发酵乳酸产生的 CO₂，在弹性凝乳内膨胀后被固定成型。Swiss-type eyes come from CO₂ released when propionibacteria ferment lactate; bubbles expand in elastic curd and are locked into place.

奶酪上的圆孔并不是搅拌时留下的气泡，而是第二次发酵产生的二氧化碳在弹性凝乳内部撑开后留下的空腔。瑞士型干酪在凝乳切割、压榨排出乳清之后进入成熟阶段，特定丙酸杆菌在温暖环境中继续分解乳酸，生成丙酸、乙酸和二氧化碳；气体被包裹在半凝固的酪蛋白网络里，缓慢膨胀成直径一到数厘米的眼孔。The round holes in cheese are not bubbles left by stirring; they are cavities opened inside an elastic curd by carbon dioxide from a second fermentation. After cutting and pressing the curd to drain whey, Swiss-style cheeses enter a ripening stage in which specific propionic acid bacteria keep breaking down lactate in a warm room, producing propionic acid, acetic acid and carbon dioxide. Trapped inside the half-set casein network, the gas slowly expands into eyes one to several centimeters across.[2]

参与成孔的主要是费氏丙酸杆菌这类微生物。它们以凝乳中的乳酸为底物，经代谢生成丙酸、乙酸和二氧化碳；丙酸和乙酸带来干酪特有的坚果样香气，二氧化碳则负责形成气孔。成熟初期把干酪放在约 20–24 °C 的温暖房间中，菌群活动和气体生成最活跃，之后转入低温储存，气孔边界被弹性凝乳固定下来。The main hole-forming organism is Propionibacterium freudenreichii. It uses lactate in the curd as substrate and produces propionic acid, acetic acid and carbon dioxide; the two acids give the cheese its nutty aroma, while the gas makes the cavities. Early ripening at about 20–24 °C keeps bacterial activity and gas production high; subsequent cold storage locks the hole boundaries into the elastic curd.[6]

凝乳的弹性决定气孔能否成型。酪蛋白网络在成熟中逐步变得柔韧，气体排出受阻时就在内部形成球形空腔，孔壁被气体压力拉伸变薄，成熟后形成光滑的内壁；如果凝乳过硬、水分不足或菌群数量偏少，气泡长不大，干酪就少孔甚至无孔。气孔大小、分布和数量因此受成熟温度、含水率、盐度和菌种比例共同约束。Curd elasticity decides whether holes can form. As the casein network ripens it becomes supple; when gas cannot escape, it forms spherical cavities internally. If the curd is too hard, moisture is too low, or bacteria are too few, bubbles cannot grow large and the cheese stays little-eyed or blind. Hole size, distribution and number are therefore constrained together by ripening temperature, moisture, salt content and starter ratio.[2][5]

不同干酪的孔洞来源并不相同。蓝纹干酪切面的青灰色纹路来自接种的娄地青霉菌丝在凝乳缝隙中生长，是霉菌组织而不是气体孔；有些半硬质干酪的小空洞只是切割压榨时留下的机械缝隙，边缘不规则，与圆形气体孔容易区分。把可见孔洞一概归因于发酵起泡，会混淆气体孔、霉菌纹和机械缝隙三种结构。Holes in different cheeses have different origins. The blue-gray veins of blue cheese are threads of inoculated Penicillium roqueforti growing through curd cracks; they are mold tissue, not gas cavities. Tiny voids in some semi-hard cheeses are merely mechanical gaps left by cutting and pressing. Attributing every visible opening to fermented gas would mix up gas holes, mold veins and mechanical fissures.[2][6]

这一机制的边界也要说清：并非所有奶酪都有孔，孔的大小不等于奶酪品质；丙酸发酵只解释瑞士型干酪的大眼孔。干酪成熟还涉及脂肪分解、蛋白水解和霉菌或酵母的表面作用，香气和质地是多条代谢路径叠加的结果；奶源季节和产地的微生物背景不同，也会影响成熟表现。市售再制干酪经过加热乳化，不再保留原生气孔结构。The limits matter: not every cheese has holes, and hole size does not equal quality. Propionic fermentation explains the large eyes of Swiss-type cheeses only. Ripening also involves fat breakdown, proteolysis and surface mold or yeast action, so aroma and texture emerge from several overlapping pathways. Processed cheese, heated and emulsified after manufacture, no longer retains its native eye structure.[5][6]

03煎炸烘烤时食物表面为什么会变褐变香？丙烯酰胺又是怎样生成的？Why does food brown and develop aroma when fried or baked, and how does acrylamide form?

美拉德反应在约 140–165 °C 低水分下由还原糖与氨基酸生成褐变物与香气；丙烯酰胺来自天冬酰胺与还原糖。The Maillard reaction above 140–165 °C under low moisture makes brown color and aroma from sugar and amino acids; acrylamide comes from asparagine and reducing sugars.

食物表面在烘烤、煎炸后变成棕褐色并散发出香气，主要来自美拉德反应：还原糖与蛋白质中的游离氨基在加热条件下发生一系列缩合、重排和降解，生成棕色的类黑精物质和数百种挥发性香气分子。这是一个不需要酶参与的非酶褐变，常见于面包 crust、烤肉、烘焙坚果和咖啡豆焙炒过程；牛奶加热后表面结出的奶皮，则属于另一类物理与蛋白变性现象。The brown surface and roasted aroma of baked or fried food come mainly from the Maillard reaction. Reducing sugars and free amino groups in proteins undergo condensation, rearrangement and degradation under heat, producing brown melanoidins and hundreds of volatile aroma compounds. This is a non-enzymatic browning that needs no enzyme, and it appears on bread crust, roasted meat, baked nuts and roasted coffee beans. The skin that forms on heated milk belongs to a different, physical and protein-denaturation process.[7]

反应有明确的温度与条件区间。美拉德反应在低水分、表面受热时显著加快，焙烤煎炸温度通常落在约 140–165 °C；碱性环境中游离氨基更多，反应被促进，酸性条件则延缓反应。水分活度偏高时反应变慢，因此水煮、蒸煮由于温度不超过约 100 °C、表面含水多，褐变明显弱于焙烤和煎炸。焦糖化是另一条路径：蔗糖等糖类在约 160 °C 以上单独受热分解变色，不需要氨基酸参与。The reaction has clear temperature and condition ranges. It speeds up under low moisture and surface heating, typical baking and frying temperatures around 140–165 °C. Alkaline conditions leave more free amino groups and accelerate the reaction, while acidity slows it. High water activity slows the reaction, which is why boiling and steaming, held below about 100 °C with a wet surface, brown much less than roasting and frying. Caramelization is a separate route: sugars decompose and color above about 160 °C without any amino acid present.[1][7]

丙烯酰胺是高温加工淀粉类食物时受关注的副产物。它主要由游离天冬酰胺与还原糖（葡萄糖、果糖等）在低水分、温度超过约 120 °C 时经美拉德中间步骤反应生成，在薯片、炸薯条、烘烤面点和烘焙咖啡中检出相对较多；EFSA 持续评估膳食暴露量。反应前驱物的种类、加热温度和时间共同决定生成量，并非所有高温食物都会产生同等水平。Acrylamide is a by-product monitored in high-temperature starchy foods. It forms mainly from free asparagine and reducing sugars such as glucose or fructose, through Maillard intermediates under low moisture above about 120 °C; it is found more in potato chips, fries, baked dough products and roasted coffee. EFSA keeps assessing dietary exposure. Precursor types, heating temperature and time jointly set the amount formed, and not every heated food carries the same level.[4]

奶皮的形成与褐变是两回事。加热牛奶时表面水分先蒸发，浓缩的酪蛋白与受热变性的乳清蛋白在液面聚成蛋白膜，乳脂球随之浮到表面，共同形成一层淡黄色薄膜；它是界面蛋白与脂肪的物理富集和变性产物，并不依赖美拉德反应。继续加热到更高温度、表面脱水足够时，美拉德反应才会在膜上叠加褐色。Milk skin and browning are different phenomena. When milk is heated, surface water evaporates first; concentrated casein and heat-denatured whey proteins gather into a film at the surface, and fat globules rise with them to form a pale yellow membrane. That film is a physical enrichment and denaturation layer of interfacial protein and fat, not a Maillard product. Only at higher heat and sufficient surface dehydration does Maillard browning add brown color onto that film.[2]

边界上还要区分几种褐变：切开的苹果、土豆放久变色是多酚氧化酶催化的酶促褐变，底物是多酚类物质；油炸食品的褐变同时包含美拉德、焦糖化和局部脱水焦化。丙烯酰胺含量受原料品种、预处理和工艺影响很大，家庭蒸煮、炖焖因温度受限生成较少；现有证据支持通过工艺条件控制暴露量，但本章只解释生成路径，不展开膳食建议。Several browning pathways should be kept apart. Sliced apple or potato darkens through enzymatic browning catalyzed by polyphenol oxidase acting on polyphenols; fried food surfaces combine Maillard reaction, caramelization and local dehydration charring. Acrylamide levels vary widely with cultivar, pretreatment and process; home boiling and stewing generate little because temperature stays low. Evidence supports controlling exposure through processing conditions, but this chapter explains the formation routes only and does not extend to dietary advice.[4][7]

04为什么糖水煮干后糖还在？泡泡糖又凭什么能吹出泡泡？Where does the sugar go when syrup boils dry, and what lets bubble gum blow bubbles?

蔗糖溶解后经过饱和重新结晶，高温则焦糖化；吹泡来自胶基弹性体的高分子拉伸与成膜。Sucrose dissolves and recrystallizes from supersaturation, or caramelizes at high heat; bubble blowing rests on elastomer stretching and film formation in the gum base.

糖水熬干后糖并没有凭空消失：蔗糖先溶解在水里形成溶液，随着水分蒸发、浓度升高，溶液到达过饱和状态，蔗糖再以晶体析出；如果继续升温超过约 160 °C，蔗糖会发生焦糖化分解，留下的就不再是原来的晶体。也就是说，糖从糖水中重新出现的形态，取决于熬煮终点的温度、浓度和是否搅拌。When a sugar syrup boils dry, the sugar has not vanished. Sucrose first dissolves in water; as water evaporates and concentration rises, the solution becomes supersaturated and sucrose recrystallizes. If heating continues above about 160 °C, sucrose undergoes caramelization and decomposition, and what remains is no longer the original crystals. The form in which sugar reappears therefore depends on final temperature, concentration and agitation.[3]

蔗糖的溶解是物理过程。它在水中的溶解度随温度升高而增大，20 °C 时约可溶解 204 g/100 g 水；加热熬糖时溶解度上升，更多糖进入溶液。水分蒸发后溶液过饱和，若缓慢降温、分子有规则排列，就析出蔗糖晶体；若快速冷却并加入玉米糖浆等干扰结晶，糖会变成无定形的玻璃态硬糖，而不是规则晶粒，搅拌和添加的糖浆都会阻碍蔗糖分子有序排列。Sugar dissolution is a physical process. Sucrose solubility rises with temperature; at 20 °C about 204 g dissolves in 100 g of water. During sugar boiling, higher temperature lets more sugar enter solution. After water evaporates and the solution is supersaturated, slow cooling lets molecules arrange into crystals; fast cooling with interferents such as corn syrup produces an amorphous glassy hard candy instead of regular grains.[3]

吹泡泡能力则来自口香糖中的胶基高分子。胶基以弹性体聚合物为骨架，早期天然来源是树胶，现代产品多用聚醋酸乙烯酯、橡胶类合成弹性体，再配合树脂、蜡和增塑剂调节软硬。弹性体被拉伸时分子链展开并回弹，配合成膜性好的配方就能把空气包裹成稳定薄膜；这与糖的溶解结晶是两套完全不同的物理体系。Bubble-blowing ability comes from the polymer gum base in chewing gum. The base uses elastomer polymers as its skeleton; early natural gums came from tree resin, while modern products use polyvinyl acetate, synthetic rubber-like elastomers, resins, waxes and plasticizers to tune firmness. When stretched, elastomer chains uncoil and recoil; a film-forming formulation then traps air into a stable membrane. This is a completely different physical system from sugar dissolution and crystallization.[2]

泡泡糖与口香糖的差别主要在配方比例。泡泡糖的弹性体比例更高、胶质更坚韧，拉伸时膜不易破裂，可以吹成较大的泡；普通口香糖胶基更软、弹性较低，侧重长时间咀嚼的口感和香味释放。表面的甜味剂、香精随咀嚼逐渐溶解或挥发，胶基本身不被消化道酶分解，最终随粪便排出。The difference between bubble gum and regular chewing gum lies mainly in formulation ratio. Bubble gum carries a higher elastomer proportion and a tougher base; its film resists rupture while stretched, so a large bubble can form. Ordinary chewing gum base is softer and less elastic, built for prolonged chewing and flavor release. Surface sweeteners and flavorings dissolve or evaporate during chewing; the gum base itself is not digested by intestinal enzymes and leaves the body in feces.[2]

需要说明边界：糖的溶解可逆，但焦糖化和热分解是化学变化，产物已经不是蔗糖；胶基能否吹泡还受温度、湿度和咀嚼软化程度影响。并非所有“胶”食品都用同一类高分子，天然树胶、植物多糖胶和合成弹性体的力学行为不同。把糖的结晶行为和胶基弹性混为一谈，就无法解释为什么同样甜味的糖和胶基在受热、咀嚼时表现完全不同。The boundary should be stated clearly: sugar dissolution is reversible, but caramelization and thermal decomposition are chemical changes whose products are no longer sucrose. Whether a gum base blows bubbles also depends on temperature, humidity and softening during chewing. Not every chewy food polymer uses the same class of material; natural tree gums, plant polysaccharide gums and synthetic elastomers behave differently mechanically. Mixing sugar crystallization with gum-base elasticity would leave unexplained why equally sweet sugar and gum base respond so differently to heat and chewing.[3]

05为什么花椰菜、西兰花这类十字花科蔬菜会发苦？Why are brassica vegetables such as cauliflower and broccoli bitter?

苦味来自硫代葡萄糖苷经黑芥子酶水解的产物，并由个体苦味受体敏感度调节。Bitterness comes from glucosinolate hydrolysis products acted on by myrosinase, modulated by individual bitter-receptor sensitivity.

花椰菜、西兰花、甘蓝这类十字花科蔬菜的苦味，主要来自硫代葡萄糖苷（简称硫苷，GSL）及其酶解产物。硫苷是植物的次生代谢物，用来抵御虫害和微生物；已鉴定的硫苷超过 130 种，不同蔬菜所含的种类和比例不同，鲜重下含量多在数十 mg/100g 量级，品种间可达数倍差异。它们本身味道并不强烈，苦味主要在植物组织被破坏、酶解发生之后才显现，这也是完整生嚼与切开放置后风味不同的原因。The bitterness of cauliflower, broccoli and cabbage, all brassica vegetables, comes mainly from glucosinolates (GSLs) and their hydrolysis products. Glucosinolates are secondary plant metabolites that defend against insects and microbes; more than 130 have been identified, and species and proportions differ across vegetables, with fresh-weight content mostly in the tens of mg per 100 g range and several-fold variation across varieties. The compounds themselves are not strongly bitter; the bitterness appears mainly after plant tissue is damaged and hydrolysis occurs, which is why chewing whole raw florets differs from the taste after cutting and resting.[8]

酶解发生在细胞区室被打破之后。完整细胞里，硫苷与黑芥子酶分隔在不同部位；切割、咀嚼或碾磨造成组织损伤后，酶与底物接触，硫苷被水解，脱去葡萄糖后形成不稳定中间体，再按 pH 条件生成异硫氰酸酯、硫氰酸盐、腈类等产物。这些含硫产物正是冲味和苦味的主要来源，中性 pH 下更偏向异硫氰酸酯，酸性条件下腈类比例上升。Hydrolysis begins when cell compartments break open. In intact cells, glucosinolates and myrosinase are kept in separate locations; cutting, chewing or grinding damages the tissue, the enzyme meets its substrate, and the glucosinolate is hydrolyzed. After glucose is removed, an unstable intermediate forms and, depending on pH, yields isothiocyanates, thiocyanates or nitriles. These sulfur-containing products are the main source of pungency and bitterness; neutral pH favors isothiocyanates, while acidic conditions raise the share of nitriles.[8][9]

人能否尝出这份苦味，还取决于舌上的苦味受体。人类 TAS2R 受体家族有 20 余种，识别疏水的苦味分子，识别阈值通常很低；其中部分受体的基因敏感度存在遗传差异，对苯硫脲（PTC）和丙基硫氧嘧啶（PROP）尝起来苦或无味，人群中分成尝味者与非尝味者。这解释了为什么同一颗花椰菜，有人觉得明显发苦，有人只觉得清淡。Whether a person tastes this bitterness also depends on bitter receptors on the tongue. The human TAS2R family has more than 20 members that recognize hydrophobic bitter molecules, usually at low thresholds; genetic sensitivity varies for some receptors, so people divide into tasters and non-tasters for phenylthiocarbamide (PTC) and propylthiouracil (PROP). This explains why the same cauliflower tastes clearly bitter to one person and mild to another.[8]

硫苷含量随品种、部位和成熟度变化，叶片和花球之间并不一致；加热会使黑芥子酶失活，改变后续水解产物的构成，长时间高温处理还会让部分含硫产物挥发或降解。本章只说明苦味从哪里来、受哪些条件影响，不展开具体烹饪做法；同一蔬菜在不同处理下苦味轻重不同，是酶活性、产物留存和受体敏感度三者叠加的结果。Glucosinolate content varies by variety, plant part and ripeness, and leaves differ from florets. Heating inactivates myrosinase and changes the mix of hydrolysis products; prolonged high heat volatilizes or degrades some sulfur compounds. This chapter explains where bitterness comes from and which conditions affect it, not cooking procedures; bitterness after different treatments reflects the combined effects of enzyme activity, product retention and receptor sensitivity.[5][9]

边界需要写明。苦味不等于有毒，十字花科的硫苷产物在研究中与多种健康效应相关，但结论依赖摄入量、加工方式和个体差异，不能把苦味直接等同于保健价值。个体对苦味的接受度受遗传、早期饮食经验和反复接触共同影响，偏好差异是正常的感官差别，而不是对错之分。The boundary matters. Bitterness is not poison, and brassica glucosinolate products are linked in research to several health effects, but conclusions depend on intake, processing and individual differences, so bitterness cannot be equated with health value. Acceptance of bitterness is shaped by genetics, early dietary experience and repeated exposure, and preference differences are ordinary sensory variation rather than right or wrong.[8][9]

06薄荷吃到嘴里为什么会觉得凉？Why does mint feel cold in the mouth?

薄荷醇激活口腔里感知冷的 TRPM8 通道，在没降温的情况下向大脑发送冷觉信号。Menthol activates the TRPM8 cold channel in the mouth, sending a cold signal to the brain without lowering temperature.

薄荷的凉不是把口腔温度降低了。吃薄荷糖、含薄荷茶或抹清凉油时，口腔和咽喉感到一阵清凉，但测下来局部温度（口腔约 37 °C）并没有明显下降。这种凉感来自一条化学感官通路：食物分子直接作用于黏膜神经末梢上的温度受体，让神经系统误以为遇到了冷刺激。类似地，辣椒的辣是激活热觉受体，薄荷则是激活冷觉受体，二者都是化学物质调制温度感觉。The coolness of mint does not mean the mouth has been cooled down. Eating a mint, sipping mint tea or applying a cooling balm gives the mouth and throat a refreshing chill, yet local temperature does not drop measurably. This cool feeling comes from a chemical sensory pathway: food molecules act directly on temperature receptors at mucosal nerve endings, making the nervous system think it has met a cold stimulus. Similarly, chilli activates a heat receptor while mint activates a cold receptor; both are chemicals modulating temperature sensation.[8]

关键受体是 TRPM8 通道。TRPM8 是一种允许钙离子通过的离子通道，分布在口腔、咽喉和皮肤的冷觉神经末梢上。正常情况下，温度降到约 25–28 °C 以下时通道更容易开放，冷觉信号上传；薄荷醇（menthol）分子可以结合到通道的特定位点，在常温下把它激活，让通道在并不冷的时候也开放。结构研究显示薄荷醇与低温结合的是同一类冷觉通道，只是触发方式不同。The key receptor is the TRPM8 channel. TRPM8 is a calcium-permeable ion channel found on cold-sensing nerve endings in the mouth, throat and skin. Normally the channel opens more readily below about 25 to 28 °C, and the cold signal is sent upward; menthol molecules can bind to a specific site on the channel and activate it at room temperature, so it opens even when it is not cold. Structural work shows menthol and low temperature act on the same kind of cold channel, only through different triggers.[10]

通道开放后产生冷觉信号。薄荷醇结合使 TRPM8 开放，钙离子内流，神经末梢去极化，动作信号沿冷觉纤维传到脑干和大脑，大脑按既有通路把它解读为凉。所以人感到的清凉是受体被化学激活的结果，口腔里的空气、唾液和食物温度并没有真的变低。浓度较低时以清凉感为主，浓度过高或长时间接触，同一受体还可能出现冷觉敏感或脱敏，感觉因人而异。When the channel opens it produces a cold signal. Menthol binding opens TRPM8, calcium flows in, the nerve ending depolarizes, and an action signal travels along cold fibres to the brainstem and brain, which reads it through the usual pathway as cool. The refreshing feeling is thus the result of chemical receptor activation; the temperature of the air, saliva and food in the mouth has not really dropped. At low concentration the sensation is mainly cool; at very high concentration or with prolonged contact the same receptor may show cold sensitivity or desensitization, and the feeling varies between people.[10]

薄荷醇容易挥发，凉感扩散到鼻腔和咽喉。它分子较小、挥发性强，随呼吸和唾液接触上颚、咽喉和鼻腔黏膜，于是凉感不只在舌头；薄荷糖在口中化开时，挥发成分还会刺激呼吸道的冷觉末梢，产生通透感。牙膏、漱口水和外用清凉制剂加入薄荷醇，利用的也是这条 TRPM8 通路；它和真正通过蒸发带走热量的降温是两回事，出汗蒸发那种降温才改变温度。Menthol is volatile, so the coolness spreads to the nose and throat. Its small molecules evaporate readily and contact the palate, throat and nasal mucosa with breath and saliva, so the coolness is not confined to the tongue; as a mint dissolves, volatile compounds also stimulate cold endings in the airway, giving a clear-open feeling. Toothpaste, mouthwash and topical cooling preparations add menthol through this same TRPM8 pathway; this differs from real cooling that removes heat by evaporation, and only evaporative sweating actually changes temperature.[3][10]

边界：薄荷凉是受体层面的冷觉模拟，不等于物理降温，也不能据此判断食物热量低。TRPM8 还受电压、渗透压等因素影响，个体对薄荷醇的敏感度差别很大；有口腔黏膜破损、过敏或特殊基础情况时，强烈清凉刺激可能不适，本章只解释味觉与受体机制，不提供用药、剂量或治疗建议。The boundary: mint coolness is a receptor-level simulation of cold, not physical cooling, and it cannot show that a food is low in calories. TRPM8 is also influenced by voltage and osmotic pressure, and individual sensitivity to menthol varies greatly; with broken oral mucosa, allergy or special underlying conditions, strong cooling stimulation may be uncomfortable. This chapter explains taste and receptor mechanisms only, and gives no medication, dosage or treatment advice.[8]

07味精为什么会有鲜味？Why does monosodium glutamate taste savory?

味精是谷氨酸的钠盐，舌面味蕾上的 TAS1R1/TAS1R3 受体识别谷氨酸，产生第五种基本味鲜味。MSG is the sodium salt of glutamate; the TAS1R1/TAS1R3 receptors on tongue taste buds detect glutamate and give the fifth basic taste, umami.

鲜味是被确认的第五种基本味，和甜、酸、苦、咸并列。它的主要刺激物是谷氨酸，一种氨基酸；味精的化学成分为谷氨酸钠，即谷氨酸与钠离子形成的盐，溶解后释放出谷氨酸离子。肉汤、海带、成熟奶酪和发酵食物里天然含有游离氨基酸，尝起来的醇厚鲜味与味精同源。蛋白质分解或发酵后以游离态释放，游离氨基酸才能被受体直接识别，完整食物蛋白并不会直接产生鲜味。Umami is the recognized fifth basic taste, alongside sweet, sour, bitter and salty. Its main stimulus is glutamate, an amino acid; monosodium glutamate is the sodium salt of glutamate, releasing glutamate ions when dissolved. Broth, kelp, aged cheese and fermented foods naturally contain free amino acids, and their rounded savory taste shares the same source as MSG.[4][11]

鲜味由舌面味蕾上的特定受体识别。味觉细胞成群包在味蕾里，细胞表面的 TAS1R1 与TAS1R3 两个蛋白组成异二聚体，结合谷氨酸后触发细胞内信号通路，释放神经递质，向神经末梢发送信号；这组受体专门响应氨基酸，而不是分辨食物里的整块蛋白质。受体被激活后，信号经面神经、舌咽神经传到大脑，被解读为鲜美、厚实的味道。Umami is detected by specific receptors on tongue taste buds. TAS1R1 and TAS1R3 proteins form a heterodimer on taste cell surfaces; binding glutamate sends a signal to the nerve ending. This receptor pair responds to amino acids rather than recognizing whole dietary proteins. Once activated, the signal travels through facial and glossopharyngeal nerves to the brain, which reads it as a savory, full-bodied taste.[8][11]

核苷酸能增强鲜味。呈味核苷酸（如 IMP、GMP）本身味道不强，但与谷氨酸同时存在时会结合在受体的另一结合位点，稳定受体活化状态，提高响应强度，产生协同增效；这也是肉汤、香菇和肉类搭配时鲜味更突出的原因之一。鲜味信号大致提示食物富含氨基酸和蛋白质，帮助判断食物的营养价值。Nucleotides intensify umami. Flavor nucleotides such as IMP and GMP have little taste on their own, but together with glutamate they strengthen the receptor response through synergy; this is one reason broths, mushrooms and meat pairings taste more savory. The umami signal roughly indicates that food is rich in amino acids and protein, helping judge nutritional value.[4][11]

鲜味有自己的味觉特征，不是咸味或肉香的混合。它在口腔里停留较久，口感圆润回甘；不同人对谷氨酸和核苷酸的敏感度存在差异，长期饮食经验也会改变对鲜味强度的判断，食物成熟度、烹调方式同样会改变游离氨基酸含量。咸味来自钠离子本身，鲜味来自氨基酸与受体的结合，二者是两套不同的味觉通路；菜肴的肉香还来自嗅觉和口腔三叉神经，不只靠味觉。Umami has its own taste character rather than being a mix of saltiness and meat aroma. It lingers in the mouth and rounds out the palate; sensitivity to glutamate and nucleotides varies between people, and ripeness and cooking change free amino acid content. Saltiness comes from sodium itself, while umami comes from amino acid binding to receptors, so the two run on different taste pathways.[8][11]

边界：味精不是兴奋提神物质，也不是典型过敏原，本章不把它描述成健脑提神或有害成分；讲呈味物质与受体机制，不给出添加剂量、人群禁忌或健康功效结论。食物是否适合个人，还要结合整体饮食和身体状况判断，不能凭单一成分下结论。Boundary: MSG is not a stimulant nor a typical allergen. This chapter explains tastant and receptor mechanism, not dosage, population restrictions or health claims. Whether a food suits an individual depends on overall diet and health, not on a single ingredient alone.[4][8]

08食物的涩味是怎么来的？Where does food astringency come from?

鞣质等多酚与唾液富脯氨酸蛋白结合沉淀，唾液润滑性下降，口腔产生干涩发皱的触感。Polyphenols such as tannins bind and precipitate salivary proline-rich proteins, lowering lubrication and giving a dry rough mouthfeel.

涩味主要不是味觉受体直接尝出来的，而是一种口腔触感。未熟水果、浓茶、红酒和坚果皮里含有大量多酚，其中能引起涩感的一类常称鞣质（单宁），分可水解鞣质和缩合鞣质，后者常来自果皮、种皮和叶片。它们进入口腔后与唾液中的蛋白质结合，形成不溶性复合物沉淀；唾液原本在黏膜表面形成润滑薄层，蛋白被结合后润滑性下降，舌面和口腔就觉得干涩、发紧、微微发皱，这种干燥感在吞咽后还可短暂停留。Astringency is mainly a mouthfeel rather than a taste picked up directly by taste receptors. Unripe fruit, strong tea, red wine and nut skins contain many polyphenols; the group causing astringency is often called tannins. In the mouth they bind salivary proteins and form insoluble precipitates. Saliva normally forms a lubricating film on the mucosa; once proteins are bound, lubrication drops, and the tongue and mouth feel dry, tight and slightly rough.[8][12]

唾液里的富脯氨酸蛋白是结合鞣质的主要对象。这类蛋白富含脯氨酸，结构上有适合多酚附着的疏水区域；多酚的多个酚羟基与蛋白交联，促使蛋白聚集沉淀。结合越强，唾液覆盖度下降越多，舌头在黏膜上滑动时摩擦系数升高，干涩粗糙感越明显，这是物理摩擦而非味觉。除蛋白沉淀外，多酚还可能刺激口腔里的化学感觉神经，参与那种微麻、收敛的感觉。Proline-rich salivary proteins are the main target of tannins. Rich in proline, these proteins carry hydrophobic regions suited to polyphenol attachment; several phenolic hydroxyl groups cross-link with the protein and drive aggregation and precipitation. The stronger the binding, the less saliva coverage remains, and the drier and rougher the sensation. Polyphenols may also stimulate oral chemosensory nerves, contributing the faintly numbing, drawing feeling.[3][12]

涩感强弱受食物成分和状态影响。未熟果实单宁含量高，成熟过程中部分单宁分解聚合，涩感下降；浸泡时间、水温、发酵程度、熟化处理都会改变可溶多酚的量。牛奶、富含蛋白质的食物能先与多酚结合，因此搭配后涩感显得缓和，这是口腔蛋白竞争结合的结果。不同食物的涩感成分和浓度差别很大，不能一概而论。Astringency varies with food composition and state. Unripe fruit holds high tannin levels, and as it ripens some tannins decompose or polymerize, reducing astringency. Steeping time, water temperature, fermentation and food pairing all change soluble polyphenol amounts. Milk and protein-rich foods bind polyphenols first, which softens astringency by competing for binding in the mouth.[8][12]

涩味和酸味、苦味需要区分。酸味来自氢离子刺激味觉受体，苦味由多种苦味受体识别；涩感主要来自蛋白沉淀造成的润滑变化和触觉反馈，作用方式不同。多酚也是植物防御取食的化学物质，未熟果实高涩可以减少种子未成熟前被采食。同一食物可以同时有酸、苦、涩，例如未熟果子既酸又涩，几种感觉在口中叠加，但机制各自独立。Astringency differs from sourness and bitterness. Sourness comes from hydrogen ions acting on taste receptors, and bitterness is recognized by multiple bitter receptors; astringency mainly comes from lubrication loss after protein precipitation and tactile feedback. One food can show sour, bitter and astringent notes together, as in an unripe fruit, but the mechanisms are independent.[3][8]

边界：正常食物的涩感是短暂的口腔触感，不等于黏膜损伤；本章讲多酚与唾液蛋白作用的一般机制，不评价多酚是否抗氧化或伤胃，也不提供去涩处理配方、饮食禁忌或胃肠疾病建议。若进食后出现持续口腔不适或过敏反应，应结合具体食物和身体情况另行评估。Boundary: the astringency of ordinary food is a brief mouthfeel, not mucosal damage. This chapter explains the general mechanism of polyphenol-salivary protein interaction, not de-astringency recipes, dietary restrictions or gastrointestinal advice. Persistent oral discomfort or an allergic reaction after eating should be assessed against the specific food and individual condition.[3][12]

09发面馒头里的小孔是怎么来的？Where do the holes in steamed bread come from?

酵母发酵分解糖产生二氧化碳，面筋网络把气泡锁住，受热膨胀定型，就形成馒头的蜂窝小孔。Yeast ferments sugar into carbon dioxide, the gluten network traps bubbles, and heat sets the expanded cells into the honeycomb crumb.

馒头里的孔洞来自发酵产生的气体，而不是揉面时拌进去的空气。面团中的酵母菌在适宜温度下分解糖类，进行发酵，产生二氧化碳和少量乙醇，释放能量供自身繁殖；面团里的糖一部分来自面粉淀粉的少量分解，一部分来自添加的糖。气体在面团内部形成微小气泡，使面团体积膨胀、质地变松。酵母活性受温度和糖量影响，温度过低发酵缓慢，温度过高酵母则会受到抑制。The holes in steamed bread come from gas produced by fermentation, not from air mixed in during kneading. Yeast in the dough breaks down sugars at a suitable temperature and ferments them into carbon dioxide and a small amount of ethanol. The gas forms tiny bubbles inside the dough, expanding it and softening its texture. Yeast activity depends on temperature and sugar supply: too low a temperature slows fermentation, while excessive heat suppresses the yeast.[2][13]

气泡能留在面团里，靠的是面筋网络。小麦粉中的麦谷蛋白和醇溶蛋白加水揉制后水合、交联，形成有弹性和延伸性的面筋，连成一张连续的网状结构；这层膜包裹住一个个气体细胞，既能被气体撑大又不会轻易撕裂，不让二氧化碳直接跑掉。揉面充分、面筋延展均匀时，气泡壁薄而不断，面团才能发起来；缺少面筋的无麸质面团锁不住气体，成品组织偏紧实。Bubbles stay in the dough thanks to the gluten network. Glutenin and gliadin in wheat flour, after water and kneading, form an elastic gluten connected into a continuous mesh. This film wraps each gas cell and keeps carbon dioxide from escaping directly. When kneading is thorough and gluten stretches evenly, bubble walls stay thin and unbroken, so the dough can rise; gluten-free dough without this network cannot hold gas and turns out dense.[2][13]

醒发阶段气泡不断长大。酵母持续产气，气体在已有小气泡内扩散并使其膨大；面团随之上浮变软，内部形成大小不一的气室。气室大小与分布影响成品孔隙，揉面均匀则气泡细密，局部揉不足则气孔偏大。醒发过度会使气泡壁破裂、气体合并，成品出现大孔洞或塌陷；醒发不足则气室细小，口感偏实。Bubbles grow during proofing. Yeast keeps producing gas, which diffuses into existing small bubbles and enlarges them; the dough rises and softens, forming gas cells of different sizes. Over-proofing bursts bubble walls and merges gas, giving large holes or collapse; under-proofing leaves fine cells and a dense crumb.[3][13]

加热把膨起的结构固定下来。上笼蒸制或入炉烘烤时，面团升温，气体进一步膨胀，面筋受热凝固定型，淀粉吸水糊化支撑起细胞骨架；乙醇和少量芳香物挥发，形成馒头特有的松软组织。冷却后气体散去，原来被气体撑开的位置留下蜂窝状小孔。馒头的松软程度，取决于产气多少、面筋持气能力和受热定型是否配合。Heating sets the expanded structure. During steaming or baking, the dough warms, gas expands further, gluten coagulates and starch gelatinizes to support the cell skeleton. After cooling, the gas leaves, and the positions opened by gas remain as honeycomb holes. Softness depends on how much gas is produced, how well gluten holds it, and how heat sets the structure together.[2][3]

边界：馒头小孔是发酵气体与面筋持气共同作用的结果，不是人为注射气泡；本章讲面团发酵和凝胶结构的一般机制，不列糖油盐比例、发酵时长或家庭制作步骤建议。不同发酵面食的组织还受脂肪、糖和盐比例影响；发酵失败多与酵母活性、温度或揉面不足有关，需要结合具体操作判断。Boundary: steamed bread holes result from fermented gas and gluten gas retention, not injected bubbles. This chapter explains general dough fermentation and gel structure, not recipe ratios, proofing times or home cooking steps. Failed rising usually relates to yeast activity, temperature or insufficient kneading and should be judged from the specific operation.[2][13]
