01玻璃真是用沙子做的吗？它为什么透明而金属不透明？Is glass really made from sand, and why is it transparent while most metals are opaque?

玻璃以石英砂为主料，纯碱降熔点、石灰石稳定，快速冷却成非晶态；可见光越过其禁带，金属自由电子则反射吸收光。Quartz-sand glass with soda ash and limestone, quenched amorphous; visible light misses its band gap while metal free electrons reflect and absorb it.

普通钠钙玻璃的主料确实是石英砂，主要成分二氧化硅（SiO₂）。配合纯碱（碳酸钠）降低熔融温度，石灰石（碳酸钙）提高化学稳定性和耐水性；混合料在约 1500 °C 熔炉中熔成均一熔体，再快速成型、冷却。冷却速度快到原子来不及排列成晶体，玻璃就成为非晶态固体，有时被称为过冷液体。The main ingredient of ordinary soda-lime glass is indeed quartz sand, mostly silicon dioxide (SiO₂). Soda ash (sodium carbonate) lowers the melting temperature, and limestone (calcium carbonate) improves chemical stability and water resistance; the batch melts into a uniform liquid in a furnace near 1500 °C, then is formed and cooled quickly. If cooling is fast enough that atoms cannot arrange into a crystal, the glass ends up as an amorphous solid, sometimes called a supercooled liquid.[1]

晶体有规则重复的晶格和明确熔点，非晶材料原子排列短程有序、长程无序，X 射线衍射给出弥散环而非锐线。OpenStax 化学固体章节把固体分为晶态与非晶态：玻璃、塑料、松香属于非晶，金属与盐类晶体属于晶态。玻璃没有晶粒边界，因此各向同性、透明均匀；但非晶态不是稳定平衡态，长期尺度下仍可能缓慢析晶。非晶材料没有固定熔点，而是在一段温度范围内逐渐软化。Crystals have a regularly repeated lattice and a definite melting point; amorphous materials are ordered over short ranges but disordered over long ones, giving diffuse X-ray rings rather than sharp lines. The OpenStax chemistry solids chapter divides solids into crystalline and amorphous: glass, plastics and rosin are amorphous, metals and salt crystals are crystalline. Glass has no grain boundaries, so it is isotropic and optically uniform; the amorphous state is not an equilibrium one and may slowly crystallize over very long times.[1][2]

玻璃透明源于电子结构。可见光光子能量约 1.6–3.1 eV，二氧化硅禁带宽度约 9 eV，可见光不足以把电子激发到导带，光子不被吸收而穿过。金属不同：价带与导带重叠，存在大量自由电子，入射光被反射或在表面附近吸收，因此多数金属不透明并呈金属光泽。掺杂或厚度改变会影响透过率，有色玻璃靠金属离子吸收特定波长。Glass transparency comes from its electronic structure. Visible photons carry about 1.6–3.1 eV, while silica's band gap is about 9 eV, so visible light cannot excite an electron into the conduction band and passes through without absorption. Metals differ: their valence and conduction bands overlap and hold many free electrons that reflect or absorb light near the surface, which is why most metals are opaque and lustrous. Dopants and thickness change transmission, and coloured glass absorbs selected wavelengths through metal ions.[2][4]

石英砂纯度、纯碱与石灰石比例决定玻璃的膨胀系数、耐化学性和加工温度。硼硅酸盐玻璃（耐热玻璃）减少钠、加入氧化硼，热膨胀系数低，骤冷骤热不易裂。平板玻璃经浮法成型，表面平整；钢化玻璃通过淬火形成表面压应力，破碎时成钝角颗粒。从沙子到玻璃的关键步骤不是熔化本身，而是控制冷却路径跳过结晶。Sand purity and the proportions of soda ash and limestone set the expansion coefficient, chemical resistance and working temperature. Borosilicate heat-resistant glass reduces sodium and adds boron oxide, giving a low expansion coefficient that resists thermal shock. Flat glass is formed by the float process for a smooth surface, and tempered glass develops surface compressive stress through quenching, breaking into blunt granules. The key step from sand to glass is not the melting itself but controlling the cooling path so crystallization is skipped.[1][2]

“玻璃是沙子做的”只对普通硅酸盐玻璃成立；有机玻璃是聚合物，金属玻璃是合金非晶态，成分与工艺不同。透明也不是玻璃独有：某些晶体、陶瓷和薄膜在薄到一定程度时也透光。把非晶态说成“液体”只在结构类比意义上成立，室温玻璃的流变极其缓慢，日常使用中视为固体。"Glass is made from sand" holds only for ordinary silicate glass: acrylic glass is a polymer and metallic glass is an amorphous alloy, with different compositions and processes. Transparency is not unique to glass either; some crystals, ceramics and films transmit light when thin enough. Calling an amorphous material a "liquid" works only as a structural analogy: room-temperature glass flows extremely slowly and is treated as a solid in everyday use.

02两块玻璃中间沾了水，为什么会很难分开？Why are two glass slides hard to pull apart when there is a film of water between them?

水膜在玻璃间形成凹液面，毛细压力低于大气压；外界大气压把两片玻璃压紧，接触面积越大、水膜越薄，吸附越牢。The water film forms a concave meniscus whose capillary pressure sits below atmospheric; air pressure presses the slides together, and the grip grows with contact area and a thinner film.

两块干净玻璃之间夹一层水后难分开，主要不是水的黏接力本身，而是水膜把空气挤出后，大气压把两片玻璃压住。玻璃表面亲水，水在表面铺展成连续薄膜；膜内液面弯曲形成凹液面，毛细作用使膜内压强低于外界大气压。这个压差就是把玻璃压在一起的力。玻璃越平整，水膜越连续，负压作用越明显。Two clean glass slides with a water film between them are hard to pull apart not mainly because water sticks, but because the film expels the air and atmospheric pressure presses the slides together. Glass is hydrophilic, so water spreads into a continuous film; the liquid surface curves into a concave meniscus, and capillarity makes the pressure inside the film lower than outside air pressure. That pressure difference is the force holding the glass together.[3]

毛细压差由拉普拉斯公式描述：Δp = γ(1/R₁ + 1/R₂)，γ 为表面张力，R 为液面曲率半径。20 °C 水的表面张力约 0.072 N/m。水膜越薄、液面曲率半径越小，压差越大；作用在整片玻璃上的合力为压差乘以接触面积。同样水膜，大面积玻璃比小载玻片更难拉开，就是因为受力面积大。The capillary pressure difference follows the Laplace equation: Δp = γ(1/R₁ + 1/R₂), where γ is surface tension and R are the meniscus radii of curvature. At 20 °C water's surface tension is about 0.072 N/m. A thinner film with smaller radii gives a larger pressure difference, and the total force on the glass is the pressure difference times contact area. With the same film, a large pane is harder to separate than a small slide simply because the loaded area is larger.[1][3]

OpenStax 流体静力学章节把这类现象归为内聚与附着：水分子之间靠氢键内聚，水与玻璃之间靠附着润湿。附着良好才能形成连续水膜并排出空气；如果表面疏水（如涂蜡或油污），水珠收缩成滴，水膜不连续，毛细负压就建立不起来。沾水后玻璃之间的摩擦力也随之增大，推拉时更滑不动。洗涤剂中的表面活性剂替代部分水分子降低界面张力，也会让水膜更容易滑动。The OpenStax fluid-statics chapter groups this under cohesion and adhesion: water molecules cohere through hydrogen bonds, and water wets glass through adhesion. Good adhesion is needed to form a continuous film and push out air; on a hydrophobic surface (wax or grease), water beads up, the film breaks, and the capillary underpressure cannot form. Wetting also raises friction between the slides, making them harder to slide apart.[2][3]

生活中同类机制随处可见：湿纸张贴在一起难揭开，两块湿瓷砖贴合紧密，吸盘靠排出空气后的负压吸附。拉开湿玻璃的办法是先从边缘让空气进入水膜，破坏连续负压层；一旦气泡进入，两侧压强平衡，两片玻璃即可滑动分开。水的温度、含表面活性剂（洗涤剂）会降低 γ，从而减小毛细压差。载玻片之间夹水后，边缘先进入空气的一侧最先松开，整片玻璃随后分离。The same mechanism appears in everyday life: wet papers stick together, wet tiles bond tightly, and suction cups grip on the underpressure after air is expelled. To separate wet glass, let air enter the film from an edge and break the continuous underpressure layer; once a bubble enters, pressures balance and the slides slide apart. Water temperature and surfactants such as detergent lower γ and reduce the capillary pressure difference.[3]

湿玻璃的吸附力来自气压差与润湿，不是水分子把玻璃“胶合”在一起；水蒸发后膜破裂，吸附基本消失。压差大小受曲率半径、表面张力和面积共同控制，不能只用“水有黏性”解释。若玻璃表面粗糙或有油污，水膜不连续，这一效应会明显减弱。The grip of wet glass comes from pressure difference and wetting, not from water gluing the glass; once the water evaporates and the film breaks, the adhesion largely disappears. The pressure difference is set together by curvature radius, surface tension and area, and cannot be explained by "water is sticky" alone. A rough or greasy surface breaks the film and weakens this effect noticeably.[1][2]

03塑料只有约八十年历史，以前人怎么保存食物？塑料为什么能在环境里存留很久？Plastics are only about eighty years old; how was food kept before, and why do plastics persist so long in the environment?

塑料是单体聚合成的长链高分子；早年靠干燥、腌制、发酵、冷藏保存食物。C–C 主链耐久，老化碎裂成 <5 mm 微塑料。Plastics are polymer chains; before them food was dried, salted, fermented and cooled. The C–C backbone lasts, ageing into microplastics under 5 mm.

“塑料约八十年历史”指现代通用塑料进入日常消费的时段。最早的合成塑料酚醛树脂 1907 年问世，低密度聚乙烯 1933 年发明，聚对苯二甲酸乙二醇酯（PET）在 1941 年取得专利；二战后聚乙烯、聚丙烯、PVC 等才大量进入包装和日用品。在此之前，食物保存依靠干燥、腌制、烟熏、发酵、糖渍、冷藏和冰藏，容器用陶瓷、玻璃、木材、金属和天然纤维。The "about eighty years" figure refers to the period in which modern commodity plastics entered everyday consumption. The first synthetic plastic, Bakelite, appeared in 1907; low-density polyethylene was invented in 1933, and PET was patented in 1941; only after World War II did polyethylene, polypropylene and PVC enter packaging and household goods at large scale. Before that, food was kept by drying, salting, smoking, fermenting, sugaring, cooling and ice storage, in containers of pottery, glass, wood, metal and natural fibres.[1][4]

塑料属于聚合物：小分子单体通过加成或缩聚连成数千到数十万个重复单元的长链。聚乙烯由乙烯（CH₂=CH₂）加聚而成，主链是 C–C 单键；链之间缠绕、结晶度和添加剂决定硬度、韧性和耐热性。OpenStax 有机化学高分子章节按热塑性、纤维、弹性体和热固性树脂分类：热塑性塑料加热可反复成型，热固性树脂交联后不再熔化。Plastics are polymers: small monomers link by addition or condensation into long chains of thousands to hundreds of thousands of repeat units. Polyethylene polymerizes from ethylene (CH₂=CH₂) on a C–C single-bond backbone; chain entanglement, crystallinity and additives set hardness, toughness and heat resistance. The OpenStax organic-chemistry polymer chapter groups materials into thermoplastics, fibres, elastomers and thermosets: thermoplastics can be remoulded on heating, while cross-linked thermosets no longer melt.[4]

塑料耐久来自主链结构：C–C 键和 C–H 键在常温下不易被水解或氧化，微生物缺乏分解这类合成链的酶。环境中主要靠紫外光引发光氧化，使链断裂、材料变脆，再经机械磨损破碎；这一过程以年到百年计。进入环境的大块塑料先碎裂，再形成微塑料——欧盟与研究机构通常把粒径小于 5 mm 的塑料颗粒归入微塑料，更细的称纳米塑料。Plastic durability comes from the backbone: C–C and C–H bonds resist hydrolysis and oxidation at room temperature, and microbes lack enzymes to break such synthetic chains. In the environment, ultraviolet light drives photo-oxidation that breaks chains and embrittles the material, before mechanical wear fragments it; this takes years to centuries. Large pieces fragment first, then form microplastics, generally defined as plastic particles below 5 mm, with finer pieces called nanoplastics.[4][5]

微塑料分两类：原生微塑料是直接造小的颗粒，如工业磨砂珠和塑料颗粒原料；次生微塑料由大块包装、轮胎磨损、合成织物洗涤脱落和渔具碎片破碎而来。欧盟环境署资料把轮胎、纺织纤维、包装和废弃渔具列为主要来源方向。食物保存方式的改变也与塑料相关：薄膜和容器隔绝水分与氧气，延长了加工食品保质期，但丢弃后的残留随之进入水、土壤和空气。Microplastics split into two groups: primary ones are manufactured small, such as abrasive beads and raw plastic pellets; secondary ones fragment from packaging, tyre wear, fibres shed when synthetic textiles are washed, and fishing-gear debris. European Environment Agency materials list tyres, textile fibres, packaging and abandoned gear among the main source directions. Food preservation also changed with plastics: films and containers block moisture and oxygen and extend processed-food shelf life, while discarded residue reaches water, soil and air.[4][5]

塑料“耐用”不等于永不降解：配方、添加剂和暴露条件决定老化速度，可降解塑料在特定堆肥条件下才较快分解。历史上并非没有合成材料，橡胶、虫胶、纤维素衍生物都早于现代塑料；八十年是指石油基通用塑料的普及期。微塑料的环境影响仍在研究中，不能把所有塑料残留一概说成同一风险。"Durable" does not mean never degrading: formulation, additives and exposure conditions set the ageing rate, and biodegradable plastics break down quickly only under specific composting conditions. History did lack no synthetic materials: rubber, shellac and cellulose derivatives predate modern plastics, and eighty years marks the spread of petroleum-based commodity plastics. The environmental effects of microplastics remain under study, and all plastic residue should not be treated as one identical risk.

04为什么有些材料越变形越强？金属、陶瓷和金刚石的强度各由什么决定？Why do some materials grow stronger under deformation, and what sets the strength of metals, ceramics and diamond?

金属位错滑移，冷加工困住位错而硬化；陶瓷强键但受裂纹控制而脆；金刚石最硬，熔岩温度不足以熔化它。Metals work-harden by trapped dislocations; ceramics are strong but flaw-brittle; diamond is hardest and lava cannot melt it.

材料强度不是单一数字，而要看抗拉、抗压、硬度和韧性。金属、陶瓷和金刚石的键合方式不同，变形与断裂的机制也不同。理解强度要先看原子之间靠什么键结合，再看载荷下位错、裂纹如何运动。Material strength is not a single number: it depends on tensile and compressive strength, hardness and toughness. Metals, ceramics and diamond are bonded differently, so their deformation and fracture mechanisms differ. To understand strength, first look at the bonds between atoms, then at how dislocations and cracks move under load.[2][6]

金属晶体中原子规则排列，塑性变形主要靠位错在晶面之间滑移。冷加工（反复弯折、轧制、拉伸）使位错大量增殖并相互缠结，滑移受阻，材料变硬变强，同时塑性下降，这就是加工硬化；退火加热让位错重新排列，强度回落。合金中溶质原子钉扎位错，产生固溶强化。金属强度还受晶粒尺寸影响，细晶界面阻碍位错运动，可同时提高强度与韧性。OpenStax 金属章节描述代表性金属的提取与合金化基础。In a metal crystal atoms are regularly arranged, and plastic deformation mainly proceeds by dislocations slipping between planes. Cold work — repeated bending, rolling, drawing — multiplies and tangles dislocations, blocks slip, and makes the material harder and stronger while reducing ductility; this is work hardening. Annealing reheats the material so dislocations rearrange and strength drops back. Solute atoms pin dislocations in alloys, producing solid-solution strengthening; the OpenStax metals chapter covers extraction and alloying basics for representative metals.[2][6]

陶瓷由离子键或共价键结合，键强高、硬度大、耐高温，但位错滑移困难，断裂由表面和内部裂纹控制。细小裂纹在拉伸尖端产生应力集中，扩展后发生脆性断裂；陶瓷装甲靠高硬度磨钝、侵蚀穿甲弹，自身往往碎裂而不是弯曲。晶粒细化可以同时提高强度和韧性，但陶瓷在受拉时对缺陷极为敏感。陶瓷装甲、刀具和耐磨涂层利用的正是这种高硬度与耐高温特性。Ceramics are held by ionic or covalent bonds: strong, hard and heat-resistant, but dislocation slip is difficult, and fracture is controlled by surface and internal cracks. A small crack produces stress concentration at its tensile tip and propagates into brittle fracture; ceramic armour blunts and erodes penetrators through high hardness, though the armour itself often shatters rather than bending. Grain refinement can raise both strength and toughness, but ceramics are extremely sensitive to defects in tension.[1][2]

金刚石是碳的高压同素异形体，C–C 共价键三维网络使其成为已知最硬的天然材料。金刚石在常压下约 3900 K 量级升华，不经过液态；玄武岩熔岩温度约 1100–1200 °C，低于金刚石的热稳定与熔化条件，单纯熔岩不能熔化金刚石。需要注意：在有氧环境中金刚石约 800 °C 即可氧化燃烧；隔绝氧气时它能承受更高温度。Diamond is a high-pressure allotrope of carbon; its three-dimensional C–C covalent network makes it the hardest known natural material. At ordinary pressure diamond sublimates around 3900 K rather than passing through a liquid, and basalt lava at about 1100–1200 °C stays below its thermal and melting conditions, so lava alone cannot melt it. Note that in an oxygen atmosphere diamond oxidizes and burns near 800 °C; without oxygen it withstands higher temperatures.[1][2]

强度高不等于韧性好：陶瓷和金刚石耐磨抗压，但受冲击易裂；金属强度可能较低却能塑性变形吸收能量。合金化、晶粒尺寸和缺陷状态都改变实测强度，不能只按材料种类排名。金刚石“不熔化”是在常压与隔绝氧气条件下的结论，在高压氧环境或特定催化剂中行为不同。High strength does not mean good toughness: ceramics and diamond resist wear and compression but crack under impact, while a metal may be lower in strength yet absorb energy by plastic deformation. Alloying, grain size and defect state all change measured strength, so materials cannot be ranked by category alone. Diamond "does not melt" under ordinary pressure and oxygen-free conditions; its behaviour differs in high-pressure oxygen or with specific catalysts.

05塑料为什么不导电？塑料难降解和微塑料吸附又是什么原因？Why are plastics insulators, and what explains their persistence and microplastic adsorption?

共价长链束缚电子、能带间隙大，没有自由载流子；长链难被生物分解，粒径越小比表面积越大、吸附越强。Covalent chains bind electrons with a large band gap and no free carriers; chains resist biodegradation, and smaller particles carry more surface area and adsorb more strongly.

电线外皮、插座外壳多用塑料，脱开的塑料本身不会导电。材料导电需要可移动的载流子，也就是能自由流动的电子或离子；常见干燥塑料里两者都缺乏，所以属于绝缘体。要理解塑料为什么不导电，需要从它的分子链结构和电子所处的能带位置入手，而不是只看材料是不是塑料。Wire insulation and socket housings are usually plastic, and a loose piece of plastic itself does not conduct. Conduction needs mobile carriers, electrons or ions that can flow freely; common dry plastic lacks both, so it is an insulator. Why plastic does not conduct must be understood from its chain structure and where its electrons sit in the energy bands, not just from the fact that it is plastic.[7]

塑料由单体通过共价键聚成长链，价电子参与成键后被束缚在键和链上；高分子材料的能带间隙较大，绝缘体通常在 3 eV 量级以上，常温下电子难以被热激发越过间隙进入导带。链与链之间靠范德华力堆积，没有金属里那种可以自由移动的电子气，外加电压时电荷只能在局部做微小偏移，不能形成持续电流。聚乙烯、聚丙烯等常见塑料的主链都是碳碳相连的长链结构，侧基只是氢或甲基，链内没有可自由移动的离子基团。Plastics are long chains polymerized from monomers through covalent bonds; valence electrons are bound in the bonds and chains once they participate. Polymer band gaps are large, typically above the 3 eV range for insulators, so at room temperature electrons are hardly excited across the gap into the conduction band. Chains stack by van der Waals forces without the free electron gas of metals; under an applied voltage charges only shift locally and cannot form a steady current.[1][7]

增塑剂、填料会改善塑料的韧性和加工性能，但一般不提供自由电子；干燥塑料的表面电阻很高，只有在表面受潮、吸附导电杂质或加入导电填料后，表面电导才会上升。也正因为绝缘又容易积累静电，塑料制品摩擦后常常带电吸附灰尘，这和体内能否导电是两回事；撕胶带、梳头发时听到的静电声，也是同一类电荷积累现象。Plasticizers and fillers improve toughness and processing but generally provide no free electrons. Dry plastic has very high surface resistance, and surface conduction rises only when the surface absorbs moisture, picks up conductive impurities or is loaded with conductive filler. Precisely because it insulates and accumulates static charge, plastic attracts dust after rubbing; that static behavior is a separate matter from internal conduction.[1][4]

塑料在环境中难降解，和长链碳碳键稳定、自然界缺少对应的酶有关；光氧化只能缓慢把大链打碎，形成肉眼难见的微塑料。颗粒粒径越小，单位质量的比表面积越大，表面越容易吸附重金属和有机污染物，也更容易被浮游生物、鱼类等摄取。吸附强弱同时受材质、表面老化程度和水体化学条件影响，不是只由粒径决定。Plastic persists in the environment because its stable C–C chains and the absence of matching enzymes in nature resist breakdown; photo-oxidation slowly breaks large chains into invisible microplastics. The smaller the particle, the larger its surface area per unit mass, the more readily it adsorbs heavy metals and organic pollutants, and the easier it is for plankton and fish to ingest. Adsorption also depends on material, surface aging and water chemistry, not particle size alone.[4][5]

边界要写清：塑料不导电不等于任何情况下都安全，潮湿、老化和高压都会使绝缘性能下降；掺杂的共轭高分子属于导电聚合物，是例外。微塑料的吸附量随粒径和环境变化，本章给的是趋势关系，不提供定量阈值；具体材料的绝缘等级要看配方、厚度和测试条件，不能一概而论。Boundary: plastic insulation does not mean safety under every condition; dampness, aging and high voltage all lower insulating performance. Doped conjugated polymers are conducting plastics and an exception. Microplastic adsorption varies with particle size and environment; this chapter gives a trend, not a quantitative threshold. An exact insulation rating depends on formulation, thickness and test conditions, and cannot be generalized.[1][5]
