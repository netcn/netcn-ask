元素与周期表Elements and the Periodic Table

从超重元素、宇宙来源到铁磁性与血液：这篇 Hub 用 7 个问题说明周期表不是一张静态名单，而是核稳定性、元素丰度和材料性质共同写成的“地图”。它区分天然元素、合成元素、经济储量与生物替代，避免把“稀有”“耗尽”和“不能用铁”混为一谈。From superheavy elements and cosmic origins to ferromagnetism and blood, this Hub uses seven questions to show that the periodic table is not a static list but a map written by nuclear stability, elemental abundance and material properties. It separates natural elements, synthetic elements, economic reserves and biological substitutes.

01为什么还要找新元素？周期表底部有什么值得研究？Why keep looking for new heavy elements? What is worth studying at the bottom of the periodic table?

周期表底部有镧系、锕系等真实材料，也有一次只合成几个原子的超重元素；前者有用，后者主要检验核结构理论。The bottom of the table holds real materials such as lanthanides and actinides, and superheavy elements made a few atoms at a time; the former are useful, the latter mainly test nuclear

先把“周期表底部”拆成两件事：表中的镧系、锕系，以及比铀更重的合成元素。前者已经是材料：钕、镝进入永磁体，铕、铽进入荧光粉，铈进入抛光和催化，铀、钚关系核燃料；后者多半一次只合成几个原子，半衰期常短到毫秒。把两类元素混在一起，就会把“找新元素”误当成“找新材料”。Start by separating two things at the bottom of the table: the lanthanides and actinides, and synthetic elements heavier than uranium. The former are already materials: neodymium and dysprosium go into permanent magnets, europium and terbium into phosphors, cerium into polishing powders and catalysts, while uranium and plutonium are tied to nuclear fuel. The latter are usually made a few atoms at a time, with half-lives often as short as milliseconds. Mixing the two makes “finding new elements” look like “finding new materials”.[1][4]

为什么还要找？因为元素存在的边界，就是核结构的边界。质子都带正电，彼此排斥；强核力只在极短距离内把核子拉住。要增加质子，通常也得增加中子，让核力“稀释”排斥，所以核素图上的稳定带向中子过剩方向弯曲。理论还预测，在 Z≈114、N≈184 附近可能出现“稳定岛”，那里的核可能比周围超重核更长寿，但现有实验还没有到达岛心。Why keep looking? Because the boundary of element existence is the boundary of nuclear structure. Protons are positively charged and repel one another; the strong force binds nucleons only over extremely short distances. Adding protons usually requires adding neutrons to dilute that repulsion, so the band of stable nuclides bends toward neutron-rich nuclei. Theory also predicts a possible “island of stability” near Z≈114 and N≈184, where nuclei might live longer than their neighbours; experiments have not yet reached its centre.[1][7][12]

合成路线不是“把元素烧出来”，而是把轻离子加速到接近光速，去撞重靶。以 ²⁴⁹Cf 靶和 ⁴⁸Ca 束流为例，两个核融合后蒸发几个中子，可得到 ²⁹⁴Og；每次实验只产生极少量原子，探测器靠一连串衰变信号识别。GSI 在达姆施塔特发现 Rg 等元素，Dubna 与 LLNL 等机构合作报告过 118 号元素；2016 年 IUPAC 确认并命名 Oganesson，周期表第七周期才完整。The synthesis route is not “burning elements” but accelerating light ions close to light speed and firing them at a heavy target. With a ²⁴⁹Cf target and a ⁴⁸Ca beam, the two nuclei fuse and evaporate a few neutrons, leaving ²⁹⁴Og; each experiment produces only a handful of atoms, identified through a chain of decays. GSI in Darmstadt discovered Rg and other elements, and Dubna, LLNL and partners reported element 118; in 2016 IUPAC confirmed and named oganesson, completing the seventh period.[1][10]

周期表底部也不只有“新元素”。4f 和 5f 电子层带来特殊磁性、发光和配位行为：钕铁硼磁体靠钕、镝等稀土；铕、铽用于荧光粉；铈用于抛光粉和催化剂；锕系中的铀、钚用于核燃料，部分同位素用于核医学。超重元素则更像核物理的试验台：它们验证壳模型、检验相对论效应对电子轨道的影响，也推动加速器、靶材和探测器技术。The bottom of the table is not only about new elements. The 4f and 5f shells produce distinctive magnetism, luminescence and coordination behaviour: neodymium–iron–boron magnets rely on neodymium and dysprosium; europium and terbium serve in phosphors; cerium in polishing powders and catalysts; uranium and plutonium in nuclear fuel, with some isotopes used in nuclear medicine. Superheavy elements are more like test beds for nuclear physics: they probe the shell model, relativistic effects on electron orbitals, and the technology of accelerators, targets and detectors.[1][4]

边界：稳定岛仍是理论预测，现有超重核没有进入岛心；一次几个原子的样品无法称量，也无法做常规化学实验，性质多靠衰变链和计算推断。新元素不等于新材料，发现它不会立刻带来磁体或电池。周期表底部的价值，一半是检验核物理，一半是理解 4f/5f 材料；把这两件事混为一谈，就会高估超重元素的实用意义。The boundary: the island of stability is still a prediction, and existing superheavy nuclei have not reached its centre; a sample of a few atoms cannot be weighed or used in ordinary chemical experiments, so its properties are inferred from decay chains and calculations. A new element is not a new material, and its discovery does not immediately produce a magnet or a battery. The value of the bottom of the table is half nuclear physics, half understanding 4f/5f materials; conflating the two overstates the practical importance of superheavy elements.[1][7][12]

02地球上的元素是巧合吗？为什么只有 92 种天然元素，哪些会耗尽？Is Earth’s element inventory a coincidence? Why only 92 natural elements, and which ones could run out?

元素来自大爆炸、恒星聚变和中子捕获；92 种指 Z=1–92，丰度极不均匀。“耗尽”多是可采储量变紧，不是原子消失。Elements came from the Big Bang, stellar fusion and neutron capture; the 92 are Z=1–92, unevenly abundant. “Running out” usually means economic reserves tighten, not that atoms vanish.

地球并不是“所有元素都齐备”，而是有 92 种 Z=1–92 的天然元素，而且丰度极不均匀。氧、硅、铝、铁等地壳主元素占了大头，金、铂、铼等只以痕量存在；锝和钷没有稳定同位素，天然样品中几乎找不到；镎、钚只在铀矿石里以极微量存在。所谓“元素都存在”，更准确的说法是：地球上能找到 Z≤92 的元素，但很多只是痕量。Earth does not contain “every element” in equal measure. It has 92 natural elements with Z=1–92, and their abundances are wildly uneven. Crustal majors such as oxygen, silicon, aluminium and iron dominate; gold, platinum and rhenium occur only in traces; technetium and promethium have no stable isotopes and are essentially absent from natural samples; neptunium and plutonium occur only in tiny amounts in uranium ores. “All elements are present” is better stated as: elements up to Z=92 can be found on Earth, but many only in traces.[4][7]

这些元素不是地球自己“造”的。大爆炸后的几分钟只合成了氢、氦和少量锂；恒星核心把氢烧成氦，再逐级聚变成碳、氧、硅，直到铁。铁核的结合能最高，铁以上的聚变不再释放能量，反而要吸收能量；所以普通恒星不能靠继续聚变造出更重的元素。铁峰不是“宇宙停止造元素”，而是“聚变这条路线走到头了”。Earth did not make these elements itself. The first few minutes after the Big Bang produced only hydrogen, helium and a little lithium; stellar cores burned hydrogen to helium and then fused progressively to carbon, oxygen, silicon and iron. Iron has the highest nuclear binding energy per nucleon, so fusion beyond iron no longer releases energy — it absorbs it. Ordinary stars therefore cannot continue fusing their way to heavier elements. The iron peak is not “the universe stopped making elements”; it is where the fusion road ends.[1][7]

比铁更重的元素主要靠中子捕获。慢中子捕获（s 过程）发生在渐近巨星支，原子核一步步加中子、偶尔 β 衰变，走到锶、钡、铅附近；快中子捕获（r 过程）需要超新星或中子星并合的极端环境，能在极短时间内把核推到金、铂、铀。超新星遗迹 W49B 的 X 射线图像就展示了硅、硫、铁等元素的分布；这些观测和核合成模型互相校验。Elements heavier than iron are built mainly by neutron capture. The slow process (s-process) operates in asymptotic giant branch stars, adding neutrons one at a time with occasional beta decays, reaching strontium, barium and lead; the rapid process (r-process) needs extreme environments such as supernovae or neutron-star mergers and can push nuclei to gold, platinum and uranium in seconds. The X-ray image of supernova remnant W49B maps silicon, sulphur and iron, providing a check on nucleosynthesis models.[1][5][7]

“元素会不会用完”要分清原子和储量。原子不会消失，只是从矿石、卤水或气氛中变得难以经济提取。氦从天然气中副产，供应受天然气田和液化设施限制；铟、镓、碲多是锌、铝、铜冶炼的副产品，产量跟着主金属走；稀土和锂的储量并不算少，但矿山、分离和加工高度集中。USGS 2025 年估计，2024 年全球稀土矿产量约 39 万吨 REO，中国约 27 万吨、美国约 4.5 万吨，世界储量超过 9,000 万吨。“Will elements run out?” must separate atoms from reserves. Atoms do not disappear; they become harder to extract economically from ores, brines or the atmosphere. Helium is a by-product of natural gas, so supply depends on gas fields and liquefaction; indium, gallium and tellurium are mostly by-products of zinc, aluminium and copper smelting, so their output follows the host metals; rare earths and lithium are not scarce in the crust, but mining, separation and processing are concentrated. USGS estimates for 2025 put global rare-earth mine production in 2024 at about 390,000 tonnes REO, with China about 270,000 tonnes and the United States about 45,000 tonnes; world reserves exceed 90 million tonnes.[6]

边界：储量（reserve）不是资源（resource），更不是地壳总量；价格、技术、环保和勘探都会改变可采数字。稀土并不“稀有”，难在分离和供应链；锂的储量也随新项目增加。所谓“耗尽”的后果，通常是价格上升、替代材料、回收和供应集中，而不是某一天原子突然消失。把地球化学、经济储量和环境约束分开，才能判断哪些元素真的紧张。The boundary: a reserve is not a resource, and neither is the total crustal inventory; prices, technology, environmental rules and exploration all change what can be mined. Rare earths are not “rare”; the difficulty is separation and supply chains, and lithium reserves grow with new projects. “Running out” usually brings higher prices, substitution, recycling and supply concentration rather than the sudden disappearance of atoms. Keeping geochemistry, economic reserves and environmental limits separate is how to judge which elements are genuinely tight.[6][7]

03铁为什么最容易被磁铁吸引？什么元素能替代血液中的铁？Why is iron so strongly attracted to magnets? What elements can replace iron in blood?

铁并不是“宇宙中最强的磁铁”，但它是日常材料里最容易被磁铁吸引的金属之一。钴、镍同样有铁磁性，某些合金的磁性能还更强；铁的优势是饱和磁化强度高、储量较大、加工成熟。铁被吸引，不是因为“含铁”这个标签，而是因为内部磁畴在外磁场中转向，整体磁场与外场同向，产生很强的吸引力。Iron is not “the strongest magnet in the universe”, but it is one of the easiest everyday metals for a magnet to attract. Cobalt and nickel are also ferromagnetic, and some alloys have stronger magnetic properties; iron’s advantages are high saturation magnetisation, large reserves and mature processing. Iron is attracted not because of a “contains iron” label but because its magnetic domains turn in an external field, making the overall field align with the applied field and produce a strong pull.[3]

机制在 3d 电子。铁原子的 3d 轨道有未成对电子，相邻原子的电子自旋通过交换作用平行排列；材料分成许多磁畴，未磁化时方向杂乱，宏观磁性抵消。外磁场让磁畴转向、长大，磁场增强；加热到约 770°C（居里温度）以上，热运动破坏有序排列，铁失去铁磁性。磁畴不是永久的“小磁铁”，它们会随外场和温度改变。The mechanism lies in the 3d electrons. Iron atoms have unpaired 3d electrons, and neighbouring spins align parallel through exchange coupling; the material splits into magnetic domains whose directions are random when unmagnetised, cancelling macroscopically. An external field turns and grows the domains, strengthening the field; above about 770 °C (the Curie temperature), thermal motion destroys the order and iron loses ferromagnetism. Domains are not permanent “little magnets”; they change with field and temperature.[3][11]

血液里的铁做的是另一件事：血红素中的 Fe²⁺ 被卟啉环和组氨酸配位，能可逆地结合 O₂，在肺里装载、在组织里释放。铁的多价态和配位环境使它适合做电子转移和气体运输；铜也做类似的事，但方式不同。血蓝蛋白用铜，见于部分节肢动物和软体动物；少数海洋生物用钒结合蛋白。它们不是“把铁换掉还能照常运行”的通用方案。Iron in blood does a different job: Fe²⁺ in haem is coordinated by a porphyrin ring and a histidine, allowing reversible O₂ binding — loading in the lungs and release in tissues. Iron’s multiple oxidation states and coordination environment suit electron transfer and gas transport; copper can do related chemistry, but differently. Hemocyanin uses copper in some arthropods and molluscs; a few marine organisms use vanadium-binding proteins. They are not a general “swap iron out and carry on” solution.[2][8][9]

为什么人类不能直接换？血红蛋白的四聚体、协同结合和红细胞寿命，都是围绕铁建立的；换元素会改变氧化还原电位、配位几何和毒性。铜、钒在无脊椎动物中有各自的循环系统，但脊椎动物没有已知的“无铁血红蛋白”替代。缺铁会导致贫血，铁过量又会沉积在肝脏、心脏和胰腺；所以“补铁”不是越多越好。Why can’t humans simply switch? Haemoglobin’s tetramer, cooperative binding and red-cell lifespan are built around iron; changing the element would alter redox potentials, coordination geometry and toxicity. Copper and vanadium have their own circulatory roles in invertebrates, but vertebrates have no known “iron-free haemoglobin” substitute. Iron deficiency causes anaemia, while iron excess deposits in the liver, heart and pancreas; “more iron” is not always better.[8][9]

边界：“最吸引”是通俗说法，不是严格排名；磁铁吸引的是铁磁体，铝、铜、金不会被吸引。居里温度是铁磁到顺磁的转变点，不是熔点。生物替代也不是元素周期表上的简单替换：铜蓝蛋白参与铜运输，血蓝蛋白结合 O₂，但它们的结构和生理条件与血红蛋白不同。把磁性、营养和毒性分开，才能回答“铁能不能被替代”。The boundary: “most attracted” is a popular phrase, not a strict ranking; magnets attract ferromagnets, while aluminium, copper and gold are not attracted. The Curie temperature is a ferromagnetic-to-paramagnetic transition, not a melting point. Biological substitution is not a simple swap on the periodic table: caeruloplasmin traffics copper, hemocyanin binds O₂, but their structures and physiological conditions differ from haemoglobin. Keeping magnetism, nutrition and toxicity separate is how to answer whether iron can be replaced.[2][8][11]
