01什么是水循环？水在海洋、陆地和大气之间怎样交换？What is the water cycle, and how does water exchange among ocean, land and atmosphere?

蒸发、输送、凝结、降水与径流闭合循环，太阳能驱动，大气水汽约十天更新。Evaporation, transport, condensation, precipitation and runoff close the cycle, solar-driven, with atmospheric moisture renewing in about ten days.

水循环是水在海洋、陆地、大气和生物之间通过蒸发、输送、凝结、降水和径流不断交换的过程，太阳能是主要驱动。地球表面约 71% 被水覆盖，海洋储存了绝大部分液态水，陆地降水最终通过河流、地下水或蒸腾回到大气，形成闭合的全球循环；海洋蒸发量最大，陆地蒸发和蒸腾补充大气水汽，全球循环把不同纬度和海陆的水连接起来。The water cycle is the continuous exchange of water among ocean, land, atmosphere and biosphere through evaporation, transport, condensation, precipitation and runoff, driven mainly by solar energy. About 71 percent of Earth's surface is covered by water, the ocean stores most liquid water, and land precipitation eventually returns to the atmosphere through rivers, groundwater or transpiration in a closed global cycle.[1]

太阳辐射加热海面和地表，液态水变为水汽进入大气；水汽随大气环流被输送到内陆和高空。温度越高空气能容纳的水汽越多，达到饱和后继续凝结成云。植物蒸腾和土壤蒸发也参与陆地水分返回大气，把土壤和生物体内的水分送回大气圈；水汽在上升和输送过程中遇冷凝结，风把湿润空气带到远离海洋的内陆，输送距离决定内陆水汽补给上限。Solar radiation heats the sea surface and land, turning liquid water into vapor that enters the atmosphere; vapor is carried inland and aloft by atmospheric circulation. Warmer air holds more moisture, and once saturated it condenses into clouds. Plant transpiration and soil evaporation also return land water to the atmosphere, moving moisture from soil and organisms back to the air.[1][2]

水汽在凝结核上凝结成小水滴或凝华成冰晶，聚集成云；云滴增长到空气托不住时形成雨、雪、雹等降水。降水落到陆地后形成地表径流汇入河湖，或下渗补充地下水，部分被植物吸收后再蒸腾，剩余水量最终沿河道回到海洋；地表径流汇入河网后最终入海，下渗的地下水缓慢补给河流和泉眼，植被截留的降水部分直接蒸发返回。Water vapor condenses onto condensation nuclei into droplets or deposits as ice crystals, gathering into clouds; when drops grow too heavy for the air to support, precipitation falls as rain, snow or hail. On land, precipitation forms surface runoff into rivers and lakes, infiltrates to recharge groundwater, or is taken up by plants and transpired, with the remainder returning to the sea along river channels.[1][3]

不同环节停留时间差别很大，大气水汽约十天左右更新一次，海洋和深层地下水停留时间长得多。人类用水、灌溉和水库调节改变局部径流和蒸发节奏，但不改变全球水循环的基本框架，只是在局部重新分配可利用水量；水库和灌溉改变的是局部径流时序与地表蒸发，不增加全球淡水总量，跨区域调水同样只是重新分配。Residence time differs sharply across stages: atmospheric moisture renews in about ten days, while ocean water and deep groundwater stay much longer. Human water use, irrigation and reservoirs alter local runoff and evaporation timing but do not change the global cycle's basic framework, only redistributing usable water locally.[1]

降水量和径流分布受地形、纬度和大气环流影响，区域旱涝不能只看全球平均；循环中的水总量基本稳定，但可利用淡水在时空上分布不均。把某一地区的干旱归因于单一环节，会忽略水汽来源、地形阻挡和长期水平衡等条件，需要结合降水、蒸发和径流综合判断；长期干旱要看多年降水、蒸发和地下水储量平衡，单次少雨年份不等于气候性干旱，丰枯交替需要序列比较。Rainfall and runoff distribution depend on terrain, latitude and circulation, so regional drought and flood cannot be judged from global averages alone; total water in the cycle stays roughly stable, but usable fresh water is unevenly distributed in time and space. Attributing a regional drought to one stage ignores moisture sources, terrain blocking and the long-term water balance, which must be assessed together.[1][3]

02为什么会有云和雨？雪花为什么长成六角扁平的形状？Why do clouds and rain form, and why are snowflakes six-sided and flat?

水汽在凝结核上成云，云滴碰并成雨；六方冰晶沿六向生长为毫米级枝晶。Vapor forms clouds on nuclei, drops coalesce into rain; hexagonal ice grows six-directionally into millimeter dendrites.

云是水汽在凝结核上凝结或凝华形成的可见水滴、冰晶集合体；雨是云滴碰并增长后降落的液态水，雪是冰晶在云层中生长后落下的固态降水。三者同属水汽相变的不同阶段，温度和云内上升气流决定最终形态，同一团水汽在不同温度条件下可以形成不同降水类型；相变方向由云层温度决定，零度以上多形成雨滴，零度以下冰晶可直接以雪的形式落下。A cloud is a visible collection of droplets or ice crystals formed when vapor condenses or deposits on nuclei; rain is liquid water falling after cloud drops collide and grow, and snow is solid precipitation from ice crystals growing inside the cloud. All three are stages of water phase change, and temperature plus updraft determine the final form; the same moisture can produce different precipitation under different temperatures.[2][3]

湿润空气上升冷却，达到饱和后，水汽在尘埃、海盐等凝结核上形成微米级小水滴；大量水滴散射光线形成可见云体。云底高度和形态随稳定度、湿度和上升方式变化，积云、层云和卷云对应不同大气条件，高空卷云主要由冰晶构成；凝结核可以是海盐颗粒、土壤尘埃或污染物微粒，洁净空气中缺少核时凝结会被推迟。Moist air rises and cools, reaches saturation, and vapor forms micron-scale droplets on dust, sea salt and other nuclei; many droplets scatter light into a visible cloud. Cloud base height and shape vary with stability, humidity and lifting mode: cumulus, stratus and cirrus correspond to different atmospheric conditions, and high cirrus is mainly ice crystals.[2]

云滴通过碰撞合并和吸附水汽长大，当重量超过上升气流托举能力时降落为雨。暖云以碰并为主，冷云中冰晶与过冷水滴共存，冰晶增长后融化或直接落下。雨滴大小与云厚、上升气流和水汽供给相关，对流旺盛的云更容易形成大雨滴和强降水；冷云中冰晶通过贝吉龙过程从过冷水滴获取水汽增长，融化层高度决定地面看到的是雨还是雪。Cloud drops grow by collision, coalescence and vapor uptake, and fall as rain when their weight exceeds updraft support. Warm clouds rely mainly on coalescence; cold clouds mix ice crystals and supercooled droplets, which grow and then melt or fall directly. Droplet size relates to cloud thickness, updraft and moisture supply, and vigorous convection produces larger drops and heavier rain.[3]

雪花由冰晶生长而成，冰的晶体结构为六方对称，分支沿六个方向延伸，因此常见六角形枝晶；温度和湿度决定枝片是扁平片状还是柱状。雪花多为毫米级，照片中 4 到 5 毫米的晶体已属较大个体，扁平形态来自冰晶在特定温湿度区间的生长方式，不是随意长成的形状；枝晶的臂长、分支密度随生长时的温度和湿度变化，晶体在下落过程中继续与水汽作用改变外形。Snowflakes grow from ice crystals; the ice lattice is hexagonal, so branches extend along six directions, giving the familiar six-pointed dendrite. Temperature and humidity decide whether plates are flat flakes or columns. Snowflakes are mostly millimeter scale, and a 4–5 mm crystal in photos is already large; the flat shape follows crystal growth in a particular temperature-humidity range rather than random form.[3][4]

云的形态和降水类型受局地温度层结影响，同一片云在不同高度可能产生雨或雪；雪花形状受生长路径影响，没有两片完全相同的晶体是统计观察，不是物理定律。人工增雨等干预只能在具备水汽和上升条件时起作用，无法在干燥稳定的大气中凭空制造降水；人工干预需要雷达和探空确认云内具备过冷水滴或冰晶条件，干燥晴空下播撒催化剂不会产生降水。Cloud shape and precipitation type depend on the local temperature profile, and one cloud may produce rain at one level and snow at another; snowflake shape follows its growth path, and no two identical crystals is a statistical observation, not a physical law. Weather modification works only where moisture and lifting already exist and cannot manufacture precipitation in dry, stable air.[2][4]

03塔里木盆地为什么那么干？夏天高山上为什么还有雪？Why is the Tarim Basin so dry, and why does snow persist on high mountains in summer?

迎风坡抬落成雨、背风下沉成雨影；直减率约 6.5 °C/千米，高山上部仍低于冰点。Windward lifting rains out moisture, lee descent forms a rain shadow; lapse rate about 6.5 °C/km keeps peaks below freezing.

山地迎风坡迫使湿润空气抬升冷却、成云致雨，背风坡空气下沉增温、相对湿度下降，形成雨影区；深居内陆又被山脉环绕的盆地，水汽输送距离远、补给少，气候干旱。气温随海拔升高按约 6.5 °C 每千米的直减率下降，这一速率是中纬度对流层的平均近似值；盆地干旱还与周围高山阻挡西风和季风气流有关，外来水汽在到达盆地前已在外侧山坡降落。On a mountain's windward slope, moist air is forced upward, cools and forms rain; on the leeward side air descends and warms, lowering relative humidity into a rain shadow. A basin deep inland and ringed by mountains receives little moisture because transport distance is long and supply is scarce. Temperature drops with altitude at an average lapse rate of about 6.5 °C per kilometer, an approximate mid-latitude troposphere value.[5][6]

暖湿气流遇到山脉被迫沿迎风坡上升，气压降低、体积膨胀、温度下降，水汽达到饱和后凝结降水；迎风坡中高海拔地带降水最多，山顶和背风坡减少。地形雨的强度取决于气流湿度、坡度和抬升高度，气流越干燥，抬升后能释放的降水越少；抬升高度越高，空气冷却越充分，迎风坡山腰常形成常年多雨带，山顶因水汽减少降水反而下降。Warm moist air hitting a mountain rises along the windward slope; pressure falls, the air expands and cools, and vapor saturates and condenses into precipitation. Rainfall peaks at mid-to-high windward elevations and decreases near the ridge and on the lee side. Orographic rain intensity depends on air humidity, slope and lifting height; drier air releases less precipitation after lifting.[6]

气流越过山脊后在背风坡下沉，下沉过程中压缩增温，相对湿度降低，难以成云致雨，形成干燥的雨影带。盆地周围山脉阻挡外来水汽，盆地内部蒸发旺盛但降水补给不足，土壤和植被呈干旱特征，地下水补给也受周边山区融水限制；下沉空气增温后相对湿度降低，吸收地面水分，使背风坡土壤更干、植被更稀疏，焚风即这种干热下沉风。After crossing the ridge, air descends the leeward side, compresses and warms, so relative humidity falls and clouds and rain are hard to form, producing a dry rain-shadow belt. Mountains around the basin block incoming moisture; inside the basin evaporation is strong but precipitation supply is insufficient, leaving dry soils and vegetation, and groundwater recharge depends on mountain meltwater.[3][6]

高海拔气温低，降水以雪的形式保存，积累超过消融形成常年积雪和冰川；雪线高度随纬度、降水和坡向变化。夏天低海拔气温高于冰点，高山上部仍可低于零度，积雪得以保留，这是气温直减率和海拔共同作用的结果，低山夏季融化而高山积雪不化；雪线以上积雪逐年积累压实成冰，冰川沿山谷缓慢下移，消融与积累的平衡决定冰川进退。At high altitude temperatures are low, precipitation falls as snow, and where accumulation exceeds ablation, permanent snow and glaciers form; the snowline varies with latitude, precipitation and slope aspect. In summer low elevations are above freezing while upper mountains stay below zero, so snow persists through the combined lapse-rate and altitude effect, melting at lower hills but surviving on high peaks.[3][5]

干旱程度是降水、蒸发、径流和地下水长期平衡的结果，不能只凭距海远近判断；雨影效应在高大山脉两侧最明显，低矮丘陵的阻挡作用有限。山地冰川和积雪储量还受气候变化长期影响，年际降雪波动不等于长期趋势，单次丰雪或枯雪都需要放在多年序列中比较；干旱盆地的地下水多来自周边山地降水和融雪，过度开采会消耗长期储存，恢复需要多年补给周期。Aridity results from the long-term balance of precipitation, evaporation, runoff and groundwater, not distance to the sea alone; rain shadow is strongest beside high mountain ranges, while low hills offer limited blocking. Glacier and snow reserves also respond to long-term climate change, and year-to-year snow fluctuation is not a long-term trend; single rich or poor snow years must be compared within a multi-year series.[5][6]

04为什么会有风？吹风为什么让人凉快，风降低了气温吗？Why does wind blow, why does it feel cool, and does wind lower the air temperature?

气压梯度驱动空气流动；凉快来自边界层替换和汗液蒸发吸热，气温读数不变。Pressure gradient drives airflow; coolness comes from boundary-layer replacement and evaporative heat loss, not a lower thermometer reading.

风是空气在气压梯度驱动下从高压向低压的水平运动，地转偏向力使其方向偏转；人感到凉快，主要靠风带走皮肤附近暖湿空气并加速汗液蒸发，蒸发吸收潜热，而不是风本身降低了气温。温度计在通风处读数变化很小，体感降温来自散热效率提高；风的方向和速度由气压场、地转偏向力和摩擦力共同决定，近地面风受地形和建筑摩擦减速。Wind is the horizontal movement of air driven by a pressure gradient from high to low pressure, deflected by the Coriolis effect. The cooling sensation comes mainly from wind carrying away the warm, moist air near the skin and speeding sweat evaporation; evaporation absorbs latent heat, rather than wind lowering the air temperature itself. A thermometer in moving air changes little, and the felt cooling reflects improved heat loss.[1][2][8]

两地气压差异形成梯度，空气由高压流向低压；等压线越密梯度越大，风速越高。海陆温差、地形起伏和大气环流都会造成局地气压差，白天海风、夜晚陆风就是局地热力环流，山坡与谷地之间也存在昼夜反向的山谷风；白天陆地升温快于海面形成海风，夜晚反向形成陆风，山谷风在山坡与谷地之间随昼夜切换方向。A pressure difference between two places forms a gradient, and air flows from high to low pressure; closer isobars mean a stronger gradient and higher wind speed. Land-sea contrast, terrain and circulation all create local pressure differences: daytime sea breezes and nighttime land breezes are local thermal circulations, and mountain-valley winds reverse between day and night on slopes.[8]

汗液在皮肤表面蒸发时吸收汽化潜热，把体表热量带走；空气流动加快汗液更新和扩散，干燥空气比潮湿空气更容易承接水汽。湿度高时蒸发变慢，即使有风也难以通过出汗有效降温，闷热环境中体表热量容易累积；干燥皮肤表面汗液容易散开，潮湿环境中汗液贴在皮肤上，汽化速度受限，散热效率明显下降。Sweat evaporating from the skin absorbs latent heat and carries away body heat; airflow refreshes and disperses the sweat, and dry air accepts moisture more readily than humid air. When humidity is high evaporation slows, so even wind cannot cool effectively through sweating, and body heat accumulates in muggy conditions.[1][8]

风不断替换皮肤周围静止的暖空气边界层，使体表对流散热增强，风寒指数据此估计人体体感温度；气温越低、风速越大，体感比实际气温更冷。这一效应改变的是散热速度，不是温度计读数，风寒效应在寒冷干燥环境中表现最明显；风寒指数把风速换算成等效降温温度，只在有风且气温低于约 10 °C 时参考意义较明显。Wind continuously replaces the still warm boundary layer around the skin, strengthening convective heat loss; wind chill estimates the felt temperature from this effect. The colder the air and the stronger the wind, the colder it feels relative to the actual reading. This effect changes heat-loss speed, not the thermometer value, and wind chill is most obvious in cold dry air.[1][8]

风速和体感受湿度、衣着、辐射和个体代谢影响，风寒指数只适用于人体暴露皮肤；高湿环境下蒸发散热受限，高温高湿组合比干热更危险。地形阻挡和建筑物会改变局地风向风速，大范围气压场不能直接套到小环境，街道和庭院的风况需要单独观测；城市热岛和水体附近的小环流会改变局地风况，预报大范围天气时不能忽略下垫面的摩擦与加热差异。Wind speed and comfort depend on humidity, clothing, radiation and individual metabolism, and wind chill applies only to exposed skin; in high humidity evaporative cooling is limited, so hot humid conditions are more dangerous than dry heat. Terrain and buildings alter local wind direction and speed, so large-scale pressure fields cannot be applied directly to small settings, where street and courtyard winds need separate observation.[8]

05飓风怎样形成并快速增强？现代天气预报比过去进步在哪里？How do hurricanes form and rapidly intensify, and how has modern forecasting advanced?

需 26.5 °C 以上暖水、科氏力和弱风切变；数值模式与卫星使路径预报误差持续下降。Warm water above 26.5 °C, Coriolis force and weak shear are required; models and satellites keep cutting track error.

飓风是暖洋面上发展的强热带气旋，形成需要海表温度约 26.5 °C 以上、一定深度暖水、足够地转偏向力和弱风切变；快速增强发生在暖水和低层水汽供给充足时。现代预报依赖数值模式同化卫星、雷达和探空观测，预报技能比几十年前明显提高，但强度和突发增强仍是难点；风暴在暖洋面获得能量，离开暖水或登陆后水汽和潜热供给切断，强度通常快速减弱。A hurricane is an intense tropical cyclone developing over a warm ocean; formation requires sea-surface temperature above about 26.5 °C, warm water to some depth, sufficient Coriolis deflection and weak wind shear. Rapid intensification occurs when warm water and low-level moisture supply are ample. Modern forecasts assimilate satellite, radar and sounding data into numerical models, and forecast skill has clearly improved over decades, though intensity and sudden strengthening remain difficult.[7][8]

暖洋面蒸发提供水汽和潜热，水汽凝结释放热量加热上层大气，使气压进一步降低、周围空气加速流入；旋转由科里奥利效应启动，赤道附近地转偏向力弱，难以形成气旋。风切变过大则会吹散对流结构，抑制发展，垂直风切变是判断风暴能否组织起来的关键条件之一；低层辐合把暖湿空气卷入中心，高层辐散把云顶水汽向外排出，上下配合维持气旋结构。Warm ocean evaporation supplies moisture and latent heat; condensation releases heat that warms the upper atmosphere, lowering pressure further and drawing surrounding air inward faster. Rotation starts from the Coriolis effect, which is weak near the equator, so cyclones rarely form there. Excessive wind shear tears apart convective structure and suppresses development, making vertical shear a key condition for storm organization.[7]

当风暴经过厚度较大的暖水层，低层暖湿空气持续补给，眼墙对流加强，中心气压快速下降，风速在短时间内跃升。海面温度、上层海洋热含量和环境风切变共同决定增强速度，单次风暴的路径和强度受多种因素调制，暖水层深度不足时增强会被迅速限制；海洋上层热含量比表层水温更能预示增强潜力，暖水层浅薄时风暴经过后容易减弱。When a storm crosses a thick warm-water layer, low-level warm moist air keeps feeding it, eyewall convection strengthens, central pressure falls quickly, and wind speed jumps in a short time. Sea temperature, upper-ocean heat content and environmental shear jointly set intensification rate; a single storm's track and intensity are modulated by many factors, and insufficient warm-water depth caps strengthening rapidly.[3][7]

现代预报依赖数值模式同化卫星、雷达、探空和浮标观测，结合集合预报估计不确定性；几十年前主要依赖天气图和单站观测，对内部结构和快速增强判断有限。热带气旋路径预报误差随卫星和模式改进持续下降，强度预报受内部对流和海洋耦合观测不足限制，进步相对较慢；集合预报给出多条可能路径和强度区间，帮助判断不确定性范围，而不是给出单一确定结果。大气是混沌系统，初始观测的微小误差在非线性演化中被指数放大，预报时效越长不确定性越大；观测站点与卫星密度有限，云、对流等小尺度过程在网格之间被简化为参数化项，模式本身也存在结构误差。Modern forecasts assimilate satellite, radar, sounding and buoy data into numerical models, using ensemble forecasts to estimate uncertainty; decades ago reliance was on weather maps and single-station observations, with limited grasp of internal structure and rapid intensification. Track forecast errors for tropical cyclones keep dropping with better satellites and models, while intensity forecasts advance more slowly because internal convection and ocean-coupling observations remain limited. The atmosphere is chaotic: tiny errors in the initial state grow exponentially under nonlinear evolution, so uncertainty rises with forecast lead time. Observations are sparse, small-scale convection is parameterized between grid points, and the model itself carries structural error.[7][8][11]

预报技能在路径上进步明显，强度、暴雨落区和突发增强仍有不确定性；历史个例不能直接套用到下一次风暴，海温变化和气候背景会改变风暴频率与强度分布。预警时间和疏散决策还受地形、人口和基础设施影响，预报结论需要结合本地承灾条件解读；气候变暖改变海温和大气水汽含量，长期趋势影响风暴强度分布，但单次风暴归因需要区分自然变率。Forecast skill has improved clearly on track, while intensity, rainfall location and sudden strengthening retain uncertainty; past cases cannot be copied to the next storm, and changing sea temperatures and climate background alter cyclone frequency and intensity distribution. Lead time and evacuation decisions also depend on terrain, population and infrastructure, so forecasts must be read against local exposure conditions.[7][8]

06雨为什么砸不死人？雨滴落地到底有多快？Why do raindrops not hurt people, and how fast do they reach the ground?

雨滴终端速度约 6–9 m/s，质量毫克级、落地动能小；液态变形卸力，衣物皮肤缓冲。Drops reach a 6–9 m/s terminal velocity at milligram mass; liquid deformation and clothing and skin cushion the impact.

雨滴从云中落到地面，速度并不会一直加快。雨滴受两个力：重力把它向下拉，空气阻力随速度增大而增大；下落初期重力大于阻力，雨滴加速，阻力同步上升，二者很快平衡，此后雨滴以恒定的终端速度下落。常见直径 2 到 5 毫米的雨滴，终端速度大约在 6 到 9 米每秒；换算成时速约 22 到 32 公里，看着不快，却已是雨滴在空气中能达到的稳定速度。直径小于 1 毫米的毛毛雨滴，终端速度只有每秒一两米左右，飘落得更慢。A raindrop does not keep accelerating all the way from the cloud to the ground. Two forces act on it: gravity pulls it down, while air drag grows with speed. Early in the fall gravity exceeds drag and the drop speeds up; as drag rises to match gravity, the two balance and the drop continues at a steady terminal velocity. A typical raindrop two to five millimeters across reaches about six to nine meters per second, roughly twenty-two to thirty-two kilometers per hour, which is the stable speed a drop can hold in air. Fine drizzle drops under one millimeter fall at only one or two meters per second and drift down much more slowly.[3]

真正决定砸不砸得疼的，不是速度而是质量和动能。1 毫米雨滴质量约 0.5 毫克，5 毫米大雨滴也只有约 60 毫克上下，落到人体上的动能远小于一颗小石子。雨滴直径超过约 5 到 6 毫米时，底部被空气阻力压成扁平甚至碗状，表面张力已经裹不住，水滴会在下落途中破碎成若干小雨滴；这也是为什么自然界几乎看不到拳头大的雨块，大雨滴并不是越大越危险，而是越大越不稳定。What determines whether a drop hurts is not speed alone but mass and kinetic energy. A one-millimeter drop weighs about half a milligram, and even a five-millimeter large drop is only around sixty milligrams, so the energy delivered to a person is far below that of a small stone. Beyond about five to six millimeters, air drag flattens the drop into a pancake or shallow bowl, surface tension can no longer hold it, and it breaks apart into smaller drops mid-fall. This is why nature never produces fist-sized rain blocks: larger drops are not more dangerous but more unstable, fragmenting before they reach the ground.[3][8]

落到人身上时还有多层缓冲。头发、衣物和皮肤先分散接触面积，柔软的表面让雨滴变形摊开，冲击被吸收在很短的距离内；雨点是液态，撞击后立刻散开，不像冰雹那样保持硬实体。雨滴打在脸上主要是凉和湿的感觉，不会造成外伤；只有伴随强风的暴雨，水平速度被风加大，斜砸在暴露皮肤上才会有刺痛感，那是风赋予的动能，不是雨滴自身的重量。Several buffers absorb the impact once drops reach a person. Hair, clothing and skin spread the contact area, and soft surfaces let the drop deform and spread out, absorbing the blow over a short distance. Raindrops are liquid and splash apart on impact, unlike hailstones that stay hard and solid. Hitting the face, a drop feels mainly cool and wet and does not cause injury; only rain driven sideways by strong wind carries added horizontal speed, and that stinging sensation comes from the wind-supplied energy rather than the drop's own weight.[3][8]

对照冰雹更容易看清差别。冰雹是冻结的实体，直径可达一厘米以上甚至更大，终端速度可达每秒十几到二十多米，质量和硬度都高得多，砸到人和作物上可以造成损伤；雨是液态软滴，在同样速度下冲击被液体形变卸掉。夏季雷雨中看似来势凶猛的瓢泼大雨，是大量雨滴在短时间内集中落下，单滴能量仍然有限，真正的破坏力来自持续积水和雷电大风，而不是雨点本身的撞击。Comparing with hail makes the difference clear. Hail is a frozen solid, often over a centimeter across and sometimes larger, falling at ten to twenty-plus meters per second with far greater mass and hardness, enough to damage people and crops. Rain is a soft liquid drop whose deformation dissipates the impact at the same speed. A heavy summer thunderstorm looks fierce because many drops fall in a short time, but each drop still carries limited energy; real damage comes from accumulated flooding, lightning and strong wind, not from the impact of individual drops.[2][3]

边界要分清：雨滴速度和大小受云厚、上升气流与温度层结影响，不同地点测得的终端速度有区间差别，不能把 6 到 9 米每秒当成所有雨滴的固定值。雨滴砸不死人是日常条件下的结论；高速飞行器遇到的雨滴撞击、暴雨中的能见度与积水风险是另外的问题。判断一场降水的危害，要看总量、强度、持续时间和伴随的大风雷电，而不是单看雨点落点。The boundary matters: drop speed and size depend on cloud thickness, updrafts and the temperature profile, so terminal velocities vary within a range rather than one fixed value of six to nine meters per second. Raindrops are harmless in everyday conditions; droplet impact on high-speed aircraft, plus visibility loss and flooding in heavy rain, are separate problems. Judging a storm's hazard means looking at total amount, intensity, duration and accompanying wind and lightning, not the landing speed of single drops.[3][8]

07云为什么不会掉下来？雨为什么是斜着下的？Why do clouds stay aloft, and why does rain fall at an angle?

10 μm 云滴终端速度仅 0.01–0.03 m/s，被上升气流托住；雨滴受水平风拖曳形成斜落轨迹。10 μm droplets settle at 0.01–0.03 m/s and ride updrafts; raindrops gain horizontal wind speed and fall on slanted tracks.

云看起来悬在高空不会掉，是因为组成云的水滴和冰晶极其微小。云滴半径通常只有约 10 微米，也就是 0.01 毫米，比雨滴小两个数量级；这样小的颗粒终端速度只有每秒 0.01 到 0.03 米，几乎被云内缓慢的上升气流托住。上升气流哪怕只有每秒几厘米，也足以让云滴停留在空中；云并不是一整块实心棉花，而是大量悬浮微粒的集合，整体被上升气流和层结稳定的空气维持在云底高度。A cloud seems to hang in the sky without falling because its drops and ice crystals are extremely small. A typical cloud droplet has a radius of about ten micrometers, or 0.01 millimeters, two orders of magnitude smaller than a raindrop; such a tiny particle reaches a terminal speed of only 0.01 to 0.03 meters per second, easily held up by weak updrafts inside the cloud. Even an updraft of a few centimeters per second keeps the droplets aloft. A cloud is not a solid block of cotton but a suspension of countless particles, maintained near its cloud base by rising air and a stable temperature profile.[2]

云滴要变成能落到地面的雨，得先长大。云滴通过碰撞合并和吸附水汽，直径长到大约 100 微米以上，终端速度超过上升气流的托举能力，才开始明显沉降；在下落过程中继续碰并，长成毫米级雨滴。暖云以碰并增长为主，冷云中冰晶与过冷水滴共存，冰晶通过贝吉龙过程获取水汽。云底高度从几百米到数千米不等，云滴在这个厚度内一边长大一边下落，一旦雨滴形成，托不住的水就以降水形式离开云体。Before cloud droplets can become rain reaching the ground, they must grow. Through collision, coalescence and vapor uptake, a droplet grows past roughly 100 micrometers, its terminal speed outruns the updraft, and it begins to settle; falling further, it keeps colliding and merges into millimeter-sized raindrops. Warm clouds grow mainly by coalescence, while cold clouds mix ice crystals with supercooled droplets, and crystals take up vapor through the Bergeron process. Cloud bases range from a few hundred meters to several kilometers; within that depth droplets grow and fall, and once drops form, water leaves the cloud as precipitation.[2][3]

雨落到地面看起来常常是斜的，主要是风在拖曳雨滴。雨滴离开云底后，除了向下的终端速度，还会被水平风带着获得水平速度；不同高度的风速和风向并不相同，这种垂直风切变使雨滴在下落路径上不断被风推移。地面上看到的雨线方向，是水平风速与竖直下落速度合成的结果：风越大，雨线越偏向水平方向；无风时雨滴轨迹接近垂直。城市街道两侧楼群还会扰乱局地风向，同一场雨在不同街区的倾斜角度都可能不一样。Rain often reaches the ground at an angle because wind drags the drops sideways. After leaving the cloud, a drop carries its downward terminal speed but also gains horizontal speed from the wind; wind speed and direction differ with height, and this vertical wind shear keeps displacing drops along their fall path. The rainline seen from the ground results from combining horizontal wind speed with vertical fall speed: stronger wind tilts the rain toward the horizontal, while calm air keeps tracks nearly vertical. Street canyons between buildings disturb local wind, so the same storm can show different slant angles in neighboring blocks.[3][8]

云能悬浮、雨会斜落，本质上都是颗粒大小与气流力量之间的平衡。微小云滴的重力弱，空气阻力和上升气流足以抵消它的重量；雨滴长大以后重力占优，向下沉降，而水平方向的风又给它一个侧向分量。观察云的形态也能辅助判断气流：积云顶部向上发展说明上升气流较强，层云平缓铺展对应稳定弱风环境，雨幕被风拉成斜条则反映低空风场的强度与方向。Cloud suspension and slanted rain both reflect a balance between particle size and air motion. Tiny droplets have weak weight, so drag and updraft offset their gravity; once drops grow, gravity dominates and they settle downward, while horizontal wind adds a sideways component. Cloud shapes also hint at the airflow: rising cumulus tops signal strong updrafts, flat stratus layers mark stable weak-wind conditions, and rain curtains pulled into slanted strips reflect low-level wind strength and direction.[2][8]

边界：云不是永远不会掉，云滴持续增长后就以雨雪形式离开云体；消散的云则是水滴蒸发回到水汽状态。雨的倾角随高度风场变化，不能用地面风向直接推断云底风向。云的悬浮高度和降水类型还受温度层结、地形抬升和水汽供给影响，单看一块云的外形不能断定会不会下雨；人工干预降水也需要云内已有过冷水滴或冰晶条件，干燥稳定的空气中无法凭空造雨。The boundary: clouds do not stay aloft forever; droplets keep growing and leave as rain or snow, while dissipating clouds re-evaporate droplets back into vapor. Rain tilt follows the wind profile with height, so surface wind cannot be read directly as cloud-base wind. Suspension height and precipitation type also depend on the temperature profile, terrain lifting and moisture supply, and a single cloud shape cannot determine whether rain will fall. Weather modification likewise requires existing supercooled droplets or ice crystals; dry stable air cannot produce rain on demand.[3][8]

08天气为什么越来越热？城市夏天为什么比过去更难熬？Why does the weather keep getting hotter, and why are urban summers harder than before?

温室气体增强辐射强迫，CO₂ 倍增约 3.7 W/m²，热浪频次上升；城市热岛夜间可比郊区高 2–5 ℃。Greenhouse gases strengthen radiative forcing; doubled CO2 adds about 3.7 W/m², heat waves rise, and urban nights run 2–5 °C warmer.

近年夏天越来越难熬，长期背景是全球地表气温在上升。太阳短波辐射穿过大气到达地表，地表升温后向外发出长波辐射；水汽、二氧化碳、甲烷等温室气体会吸收部分长波辐射并向下再辐射，使低层大气和地表保持比无温室时更高的温度。人类活动把地质时期埋藏的碳以化石燃料形式释放回大气，大气二氧化碳浓度从工业革命前约 280 ppm 升到 420 ppm 以上，温室效应被持续增强。辐射强迫的估算中，二氧化碳浓度倍增对应的额外辐射强迫约 3.7 W/m²。Recent summers feel increasingly harsh against a long-term background of rising global surface temperatures. Solar shortwave radiation crosses the atmosphere to the surface, which warms and emits longwave radiation back upward; greenhouse gases such as water vapor, carbon dioxide and methane absorb part of that longwave radiation and reradiate some downward, keeping the lower atmosphere and surface warmer than they would be otherwise. Human activity returns geologically stored carbon to the atmosphere through fossil fuels, lifting carbon dioxide from about 280 parts per million before the Industrial Revolution to above 420. In radiative forcing estimates, a doubling of carbon dioxide corresponds to about 3.7 watts per square meter of additional forcing.[9]

增温不是均匀地让每一天都变热，而是改变高温事件的出现频率和强度。气候系统观测显示，全球陆地平均气温上升的同时，热浪的次数、持续天数和峰值温度都在增加；海洋吸收了大部分多余热量，海表温度和海洋热含量随之上升，为强对流和热带气旋提供更多能量基础。年际之间还叠加厄尔尼诺、大气环流波动等自然变率，某一个夏天偏热不能单独归因于长期趋势，但多年序列里高温日数上升的方向是清楚的。Warming does not make every day uniformly hotter; it changes the frequency and intensity of heat events. Observations show that as land averages rise, heat waves occur more often, last longer and peak higher; the ocean absorbs most excess heat, raising sea-surface temperature and ocean heat content, which supplies more energy for convection and tropical cyclones. Year to year, natural variability such as El Niño and circulation oscillations overlays the trend, so one hot summer alone cannot be attributed to long-term warming, but the rise in hot days across multi-year series is clear.[9][10]

城市里的热感受还被城市热岛放大。沥青路面、混凝土建筑白天吸收太阳辐射，夜间缓慢放热；密集建筑阻挡通风，汽车、空调和工业排热进一步补充热量。监测表明，城区夜间气温可比周边郊区高 2 到 5 ℃，老城区和高密度街区尤为明显。绿化树木遮阳蒸腾、水体与通风廊道可以缓解局部高温；热岛是下垫面和人为排热共同造成的局地现象，与全球增暖背景叠加，但尺度和成因并不相同。City heat is amplified further by the urban heat island. Asphalt and concrete absorb solar radiation by day and release it slowly at night; dense buildings block ventilation, and cars, air conditioners and industrial waste heat add more warmth. Measurements show nighttime urban temperatures can run two to five degrees Celsius above surrounding suburbs, especially in old and high-density districts. Tree shade and transpiration, water bodies and ventilation corridors relieve local heat; the heat island is a local effect of surface cover and anthropogenic heat, layered on top of global warming but different in scale and cause.[10]

体感温度还要受湿度和风速调制。气温相同的情况下，空气湿度大时汗液蒸发变慢，身体散热效率下降，闷热感比干热更强；无风环境又会减少体表边界层替换。所以夏天觉得这么热，是背景增温、局地热岛、湿度风速和个体代谢共同作用的结果。理解这些环节，有助于区分哪些是长期气候趋势，哪些是当天天气条件造成的波动。Felt temperature is also modified by humidity and wind. At the same air temperature, high humidity slows sweat evaporation and lowers heat-loss efficiency, making muggy heat feel worse than dry heat; calm air further reduces replacement of the skin boundary layer. Feeling so hot on a given summer day therefore combines background warming, local heat island, humidity, wind and individual metabolism. Separating long-term climate trend from the daily weather fluctuation is what these mechanisms help clarify.[9][10]

边界：单次高温天气的成因需要结合环流形势、海温背景和城市布局判断，不能把每一场热浪都直接等同于温室效应；气候归因研究也是在统计意义上估计人类活动对事件概率的影响。增温幅度在不同地区和季节差别很大，城市规划、用水调度和健康预警需要本地多年观测支撑。本章讲增暖的机制、量级与局地放大因素，不展开具体减排路径、适应工程设计或城市热环境数值模拟。The boundary: a single hot spell must be explained with circulation patterns, sea-temperature background and urban layout; not every heat wave can be equated directly with the greenhouse effect, and climate attribution studies estimate the human influence on event probability in a statistical sense. Warming varies widely by region and season, and urban planning, water management and health warnings need local multi-year observations. This chapter covers warming mechanisms, magnitudes and local amplification, not specific mitigation pathways, adaptation engineering or urban thermal modeling.[8][10]

09雨后空气为什么特别清新？Why does the air smell so fresh after rain?

雨水冲刷掉悬浮尘粒，土壤中的土臭素和植物油脂被雨滴溅起，加上降温增湿，空气显得清新。Rain washes out suspended dust, raindrops kick up soil geosmin and plant oils, and cooler moist air feels fresh.

雨后的清新感首先来自空气被雨水清洗。雨滴在下落过程中碰撞、俘获空气中悬浮的灰尘、花粉、烟尘和气溶胶颗粒，把它们带到地面；悬浮颗粒减少后，干热天气里那种闷浊气味随之减弱。降雨结束时近地面空气里的颗粒物浓度通常比降雨前低，呼吸时鼻腔感受到的刺激也更轻，这是空气显得干净的物理基础。雨滴在云下和近地面穿行时不断合并增大，下落扫过的气层越厚，清除悬浮颗粒的时间越长。The fresh feeling after rain begins with the air being washed clean. Falling raindrops collide with and capture suspended dust, pollen, soot and aerosol particles, carrying them to the ground. As particle load drops, the stale, heavy smell of hot dry weather weakens. Near-ground particle concentration is usually lower right after a shower than before it, so the nose meets less irritation; this physical removal is the basis for the air feeling clean.[2][3]

泥土气息主要来自土壤微生物产物。土壤中的放线菌在分解有机质时会释放一种名为土臭素的化合物；干燥时它留在土壤和岩石表面，雨滴砸向干土和裸岩时溅起细小飞沫，把土臭素和植物析出的油脂一起带进低空空气。这种被称作潮土油的泥土芳香，在久旱初雨时最明显，也是很多人把雨后气味和泥土联系起来的原因。The earthy scent comes mainly from soil microorganisms. Actinomycetes in the soil release a compound called geosmin while decomposing organic matter. When dry, it stays on soil and rock surfaces; raindrops striking dry earth and bare rock splash tiny droplets that carry geosmin and exuded plant oils into the low air. This earthy aroma, known as petrichor, is strongest after a long dry spell, and it is why many people associate the post-rain smell with soil.[3][12]

雷电还会带入少量臭氧。闪电的高温把空气中的氧分子拆开，部分原子重新结合成臭氧；下沉气流把低空少量臭氧带到地面附近，带来一种略带金属感的清爽气味。臭氧浓度通常很低，并不是雨后气味的主体；只有在雷暴近距离发生时，这种刺激性气味才容易被察觉。Lightning also brings in small amounts of ozone. The high temperature of a flash splits oxygen molecules, and some atoms recombine into ozone; downdrafts carry a small amount low to the ground, adding a slightly metallic crispness. Ozone concentration is usually low and is not the main post-rain scent; this sharp note is easy to notice only when a thunderstorm passes close by.[2][12]

温度和湿度改变了体感。降雨同时冷却近地面层，空气降温后密度增大，汗液蒸发节奏变慢，鼻腔和咽喉黏膜保持湿润；相比干燥多尘的空气，凉润的空气更容易被感受为清新。不同地面覆盖会改变气味来源：林地雨后多树脂与腐殖质气息，农田雨后偏土壤与青草气息，城市路面则主要是灰尘被洗净后的寡淡味道。湿度升高还会让气味分子更容易到达嗅觉受体，而干燥空气里的粉尘刺激感更容易被放大。Temperature and humidity shape the body's sensation. Rain cools the near-ground layer, denser air slows sweat evaporation, and the lining of the nose and throat stays moist; compared with dry, dusty air, cool moist air is easier to read as fresh. Ground cover changes the scent source: after rain, woodland carries resin and humus notes, farmland leans toward soil and grass, and city pavement mostly carries the plain smell of washed dust.[2][3]

边界：雨后清新并不等于空气富含所谓负氧离子，也不能据此判断空气质量达到某种健康标准；气味强弱还受地面植被、土壤湿度和下风方向影响。本章讲降雨清洗、气味释放与温湿度体感的一般机制，不展开空气质量等级预报、过敏人群防护或室内空气净化建议。Boundary: post-rain freshness does not mean the air is rich in so-called negative ions, nor can it be taken as a health-grade air quality mark. Scent strength also depends on vegetation, soil moisture and wind direction. This chapter explains the general mechanisms of rain scavenging, odor release and temperature-humidity sensation; it does not cover air quality index forecasts, protection for allergy sufferers, or indoor air purification advice.[2][12]
