01地震波为什么能帮我们“看”到地球内部？How do seismic waves let us see inside the Earth?

P 波穿固液、S 波仅穿固体，波速界面反演地壳、地幔与内外核；科拉钻孔仅约 12.26 km。P waves cross solids and liquids, S waves only solids; velocity interfaces reveal crust, mantle and core, while the Kola borehole reaches only 12.26 km.

地震波是地球内部的听诊器。地震发生时产生体波，纵波（P 波）可在固体和液体中传播，速度较快；横波（S 波）只能通过固体，在液态外核中断。不同深度岩石密度、弹性和温度变化使波速改变，波速在界面反射和折射；台网记录到时和路径，反推出莫霍面、核幔边界和内外核分界面。人类直接钻取的样本只到地壳很浅的位置，深部结构主要靠地震波速剖面推断。Seismic waves are the stethoscope of Earth's interior. An earthquake sends out body waves: primary (P) waves travel through solids and liquids and arrive first, while secondary (S) waves pass only through solids and stop in the liquid outer core. Rock density, elasticity and temperature change with depth, altering wave speed and causing reflection and refraction at boundaries; station networks use arrival times and paths to infer the Moho, the core-mantle boundary and the inner-outer core divide. Direct drilling samples only a shallow crust, and deep structure is inferred mainly from seismic velocity profiles.[1][2]

分层结构。地壳大陆部分平均约 30–50 km 厚，山区可更厚；大洋地壳约 5–10 km。地幔从莫霍面延伸到约 2,900 km 深，占地球体积约八成以上，由铁镁硅酸盐岩石组成，固态但可缓慢塑性流动。外核为液态铁镍，约 2,900–5,150 km；内核半径约 1,220 km，虽温度更高，在极高压力下为固态铁镍。S 波不能穿过外核，是外核液态的关键证据。Layered structure. Continental crust averages about 30–50 km thick, thicker under mountains; oceanic crust is roughly 5–10 km. The mantle runs from the Moho to about 2,900 km, the largest layer by volume, made of iron-magnesium silicate rock that is solid but flows plastically over time. The outer core, about 2,900–5,150 km deep, is liquid iron-nickel; the inner core, though hotter, is solid iron-nickel under extreme pressure. S waves not crossing the outer core are key evidence that it is liquid.[1][2]

内部如何运行。外核液态金属对流产生地磁场；地幔热对流带动岩石圈板块缓慢移动，板块在洋中脊新生、在俯冲带消亡，形成地震、火山和山脉。地球内部热量来自形成时的余热和放射性衰变；温度随深度升高，但岩石强度、相态和压力共同决定物质是刚性还是可流动。How the interior works. Convection of the liquid metallic outer core generates the magnetic field; mantle convection carries lithospheric plates slowly, which are created at mid-ocean ridges and consumed in subduction zones, producing earthquakes, volcanoes and mountains. Internal heat comes from residual formation heat and radioactive decay; temperature rises with depth, but rock strength, phase and pressure together decide whether material is rigid or flowable.[1][3]

人类能钻多深。苏联科拉超深钻孔钻到约 12.26 km，只穿透地壳上部；钻孔内温度随深度升高，地表往下每公里约升高二十多摄氏度，岩石在高温下塑性增强，钻探套管和钻头难以继续。相比 6,371 km 半径，这一深度不足千分之二。地壳之下的信息来自地震波、陨石成分参照和实验室高压矿物模拟，而不是直接取样。How deep can humans drill? The Kola Superdeep Borehole reached about 12.26 km, penetrating only the upper crust. Deeper down temperature rises, rock becomes plastic under heat, and casing and bits struggle to continue. Against the 6,371 km radius, this depth is less than two per mille. Information below the crust comes from seismic waves, meteorite composition references and high-pressure mineral experiments rather than direct sampling.[1][3]

边界：地震波反演给出的是波速结构，成分和状态需结合岩石学与高压实验约束；地幔对流速率每年厘米级，与地表板块运动对应但不能直接观测。莫霍面深度、核幔边界性质在不同区域有变化。这里不展开地震预警、板块边界分类和地球化学分异的细节。Boundary: seismic inversion yields velocity structure, while composition and state need petrological and high-pressure constraints. Mantle convection moves centimetres per year and corresponds to plate motion but cannot be observed directly. Moho depth and core-mantle boundary properties vary by region. Earthquake early warning, plate-boundary classification and geochemical differentiation are not covered here.[1][2]

02水和冰川怎样雕塑地表，张家界峰林是怎么形成的？How do water and glaciers sculpt the land, and how did the Zhangjiajie peaks form?

流水下切侧蚀，冰川磨蚀拔蚀成 U 形谷，差异风化沿节理切出峰林；侵蚀搬运沉积再成岩。Rivers incise and erode sideways, glaciers abrade and pluck U-valleys, differential weathering cuts peaks along joints; erosion, transport, deposition and lithification close the loop.

水是地表最活跃的雕塑家。降水形成地表径流，顺着坡度汇集，冲刷河床、搬运泥沙；河流在坡度陡时下切，在平缓处侧蚀和堆积，并向源头方向后退溯源侵蚀，形成峡谷、阶地和冲积平原。水流携带的碎屑像砂纸一样磨蚀基岩，溶蚀作用还会把可溶性岩石带走。水系长期改造地形，但切割速率受岩性、降水和构造抬升共同控制。Water is the most active sculptor of the land surface. Rainfall forms runoff that gathers along slopes, scouring riverbeds and carrying sediment: rivers cut downward on steep grades, erode sideways and deposit on gentle ones, forming canyons, terraces and alluvial plains. Entrained debris grinds bedrock like sandpaper, and dissolution carries away soluble rock. Drainages remodel terrain over long periods, but incision rates depend jointly on rock type, rainfall and tectonic uplift.[3][7]

冰川是会移动的岩石碎屑搬运工。冰川沿山谷向下流动，底部冻结的岩屑磨蚀床基（磨蚀），把岩石磨成岩粉并刻出擦痕，同时把岩石拔起带走（拔蚀）；刨出 U 形谷、冰斗、角峰和刃脊。与河流形成的 V 形谷不同，冰川谷底宽平、两侧陡直。冰期时大陆冰川覆盖大片陆地，铲平丘陵、搬运漂砾；现代山地冰川仍在改造高山地形。冰川地质资料把磨蚀和拔蚀列为冰川侵蚀的两种基本过程。Glaciers are moving carriers of rock debris. A glacier flows down its valley: rock frozen into its base abrades the bedrock (abrasion) and plucks blocks loose to carry away (plucking), carving U-shaped valleys, cirques, horns and arêtes. Unlike river-carved V-valleys, glacial valleys have broad flat floors and steep sides. During ice ages continental glaciers covered large landmasses, leveling hills and dropping erratics; modern mountain glaciers still reshape high terrain. Glacial geology references list abrasion and plucking as the two basic erosion processes.[4]

峰林与岩溶。张家界一类峰林由层状石英砂岩经流水沿裂隙下切、重力崩塌和差异风化形成：砂岩抗侵蚀能力强，层理与垂直节理共同控制柱体走向，坚硬岩层保留成柱，软弱层被剥蚀，长期切割后形成高差可达数百米的林立峭壁。可溶性碳酸盐岩地区则发育溶洞、峰丛和洼地，化学溶蚀叠加机械侵蚀。地貌是气候、岩性和构造在长时间尺度上的共同产物。Peak forests and karst. Peak forests like Zhangjiajie form from layered quartz sandstone cut along joints by running water, gravity collapse and differential weathering: resistant beds remain as pillars while weaker layers are stripped away, and long incision leaves clustered cliffs. In soluble carbonate rock, caves, peak clusters and depressions develop as chemical dissolution overlays mechanical erosion. Landforms are the joint product of climate, lithology and tectonics over long timescales.[3][4]

搬运与沉积闭环。侵蚀产生的泥沙被河流、冰川和风搬运，在河口堆积成三角洲，在湖盆和浅海形成层状沉积；沉积物压实成岩，再经抬升暴露，进入下一轮侵蚀。生物地球化学循环把碳和矿物在岩石圈、水圈和生物之间的循环作为框架；地表侵蚀与深部抬升通过板块构造相互耦合。地貌由两类营力共同塑造：内营力靠板块碰撞、断块抬升和火山活动造出山脉、高原与盆地的骨架；外营力由流水、冰川、风和风化作用把骨架侵蚀雕塑成峡谷、峰林与阶地，再把碎屑搬运堆积到低处。Transport and sedimentation loop. Eroded sediment is carried by rivers, glaciers and wind, then deposited at estuaries, lake basins and the seafloor; sediments compact into rock, are uplifted and exposed, and enter the next erosion cycle. Biogeochemical cycles frame how carbon and minerals circulate among lithosphere, hydrosphere and life; surface erosion and deep uplift are coupled through plate tectonics. Landforms are shaped by two sets of forces. Internal forces use plate collision, fault-block uplift and volcanism to build the skeleton of mountains, plateaus and basins; external forces of running water, glaciers, wind and weathering erode that skeleton into valleys, peaks and terraces, then carry debris to lower ground.[3][7]

边界：把某一地貌完全归因于水或冰都不准确。峡谷可能先有构造抬升，流水再下切；峰林形态受节理和岩性控制。现代地貌是地质历史叠加的结果，无法用单一过程解释。短期洪水和长期侵蚀的量级差别很大，这里不展开流域水文模型和沉积物预算。Boundary: attributing any landform to water or ice alone is inaccurate. A canyon may first be tectonically uplifted before a river incises it; peak shape is controlled by joints and rock type. Modern terrain is a stack of geological history and cannot be explained by one process. Short-term floods and long-term erosion differ greatly in scale; watershed hydrology models and sediment budgets are outside this chapter.[3][4]

03地球为什么会有海陆之分，岛屿和海岸线是怎么形成的？Why does Earth have land and ocean, and how do islands and coastlines form?

大陆与大洋地壳差异形成海陆；岛屿分火山、珊瑚、大陆和冲积成因；海岸线是分形曲线，长度随尺度变化。Continental and oceanic crust differ to form land and sea; islands are volcanic, coral, continental or alluvial; coastlines are fractal and length varies with scale.

海陆之分。地球表面约七成被海洋覆盖；大陆地壳密度低、厚度大，海拔较高，大洋地壳密度高、厚度薄，被海水充填形成洋盆。板块运动和海平面升降改变海陆分布：俯冲、裂谷和造山运动抬升陆地，冰期—间冰期冰川消长改变海平面。陆地与洋盆的基本格局在板块构造框架下形成。Land and sea. About seven-tenths of Earth's surface is ocean. Continental crust is less dense and thicker, standing higher; oceanic crust is denser and thinner, filled by seawater to form basins. Plate motion and sea-level change redistribute land and sea: subduction, rifting and orogeny raise land, while glacial-interglacial ice growth and melt shift sea level. The basic land-ocean pattern forms within the plate-tectonic framework.[3]

岛屿怎么形成。海洋岛屿有多种成因：火山岛由洋中脊或热点火山喷出物堆积露出海面；珊瑚岛（环礁）在火山岛沉降过程中珊瑚礁向上生长，最后火山沉入海下、只留环状礁体；大陆岛曾与大陆相连，陆桥沉没后分离；冲积岛由河口泥沙堆积形成。岛屿大小、地形和生态与成因类型对应。How islands form. Ocean islands have several origins: volcanic islands rise from material piled up at mid-ocean ridges or hotspots; coral atolls grow upward as a volcanic island subsides; continental islands were once joined to a mainland and separated when a land bridge sank; alluvial islands build from estuary sediment. Island size, terrain and ecology correspond to their origin type.[3][5]

海岸线有多长。海岸线是分形曲线：测量尺度越细，计入的海湾、岬角和潮沟越多，长度越长，因此没有唯一的绝对长度。潮涨潮落之间的潮间带本身就在移动，岸线位置随水位基准变化。不同国家用不同比例尺和基准定义海岸长度，数字不能直接比较。岩石岸、沙质岸、泥质岸和河口湿地在波浪、潮汐、沉积物和抬升沉降作用下形态各异；海岸测绘资料说明海岸长度受潮位基准和制图尺度影响。How long is a coastline? A coastline is a fractal curve: the finer the measurement scale, the more bays, headlands and tidal creeks are counted, and the longer it gets, so there is no single absolute length. Countries define coast length with different scales and datums, and figures cannot be compared directly. Rocky, sandy, muddy and estuarine wetland shores look different under waves, tides, sediment supply and uplift or subsidence; coastal survey materials note that length depends on tidal datum and mapping scale.[2][5]

陆地高程与湖泊。死谷、死海等地低于海平面，是构造洼地加上蒸发量大于补给形成的低地；若周围存在通向海洋的低通道，海水会灌入，封闭洼地则靠蒸发维持湖面。湖泊深度和面积受补给、蒸发、渗漏和盆地形态控制，湖面高程随季节补给与蒸发波动，不存在由面积唯一决定的最小深度。海岸和湖岸都在波浪、沉积物和水位变化中持续后退或推进。Elevation and lakes. Sites such as Death Valley and the Dead Sea lie below sea level: tectonic depressions where evaporation exceeds supply. If a low channel to the ocean exists, seawater floods in; enclosed basins hold their lakes by evaporation. Lake depth and area depend on inflow, evaporation, seepage and basin shape, and no minimum depth follows uniquely from area. Shorelines of both seas and lakes retreat or advance under waves, sediment and water-level change.[3][5]

边界：海岸类型划分是理想化归纳，同一岸段可同时受波浪、潮流和人类活动影响；海岸线长度数字依赖制图尺度，不能当作固定物理常数。岛屿成因分类之间有过渡类型，抬升、沉降和海平面变化使岸线位置不断移动。这里不展开海岸工程防护和海平面上升的具体情景预测。Boundary: coastal type divisions are idealized summaries, and one stretch can be shaped by waves, tides and human activity together. Coastline length depends on mapping scale and cannot be treated as a fixed physical constant. Island origin types have transitions, and uplift, subsidence and sea-level change keep shorelines moving. Coastal engineering protection and specific sea-level-rise scenarios are not covered here.[2][5]

04海洋吸收了多少变暖热量，古气候与甲烷水合物风险是怎样的？How much warming heat does the ocean absorb, and what are paleoclimate and hydrate risks?

多余热量主要进入海洋；石炭纪埋碳成煤，水合物本世纪影响尚不可检测，地球水主要来自小行星体。Most excess heat enters the ocean; Carboniferous swamps buried carbon into coal, hydrate effects are undetectable this century, and Earth's water came mainly from asteroids.

海洋存住了大部分多余热量。全球变暖的多余能量绝大部分（IPCC 估计约九成以上）进入海洋：上层海洋升温最快，热量逐步向深层输送；气候资料指出海洋热含量是地球能量收支的关键指标，比大气温度更能累积长期变暖信号。海洋吸热也使海水膨胀，贡献海平面上升；海洋同时吸收一部分二氧化碳，但酸化随之而来。The ocean stores most excess heat. The extra energy of global warming enters the ocean chiefly: the upper ocean warms fastest, and heat gradually transfers to depth. Climate materials identify ocean heat content as a key measure of Earth's energy budget, accumulating the long-term warming signal better than air temperature. Ocean heat uptake also expands seawater and contributes to sea-level rise; the ocean absorbs some CO₂ as well, but acidification follows.[6]

地质历史中的气候。石炭纪广泛发育森林沼泽，大量植物碳被埋藏形成煤层，长期降低大气 CO₂、改变氧气水平；盘古大陆聚合时内陆广阔、气候干旱，与海岸湿润带形成对比。地质记录显示气候随板块位置、大气成分和轨道周期变化，温度和海平面在长时间尺度上波动；生物地球化学循环说明碳在岩石、海洋和大气之间的交换。Climate in geological history. The Carboniferous held extensive forest swamps, where large amounts of plant carbon were buried into coal, lowering atmospheric CO₂ over time and shifting oxygen levels. When Pangaea assembled, its broad interior was arid, contrasting with humid coastal belts. The geological record shows climate shifting with plate position, atmospheric composition and orbital cycles, with temperature and sea level varying over long timescales; biogeochemical cycles describe carbon exchange among rock, ocean and atmosphere.[7]

温室风险与甲烷水合物。甲烷水合物（可燃冰）在低温高压的陆坡和海底沉积中稳定赋存；升温可能使其分解，甲烷在百年尺度上的增温潜势约为二氧化碳的数十倍。IPCC 第六次评估认为本世纪内水合物分解释放对全球温度的影响尚不可检测，更大规模的释放需数千年尺度；“甲烷枪”式快速失控是假说情景，不是当前预测。综述研究区分了浅海排放与深部水合物储库的不同行为。Greenhouse risk and methane hydrate. Methane hydrate (combustible ice) is stable in cold, high-pressure continental-slope and seafloor sediments; warming may decompose it, and methane is a potent greenhouse gas. The IPCC Sixth Assessment finds hydrate release this century has no detectable global-temperature effect, while larger release operates on millennial timescales; the clathrate-gun rapid runaway is a hypothetical scenario, not a current projection. Reviews separate shallow coastal emissions from deep hydrate reservoirs.[6][7]

水从哪里来。同位素证据显示地球水主要来自陨石状小行星体，彗星贡献比例有限；早期太阳系冰质星子撞击和行星吸积过程带来挥发分。大气中的氦等轻气体逃逸到太空，但地球水圈总体在板内循环与外来源之间维持动态平衡。NASA 海洋世界资料汇总了小行星与彗星水冰证据。Where Earth's water came from. Isotopic evidence points to carbonaceous asteroid-like planetesimals as the main source of Earth's water, with a limited cometary contribution; icy planetesimals and planetary accretion in the early solar system delivered volatiles. Light gases such as helium escape the atmosphere to space, but the ocean keeps a dynamic balance between internal cycling and external sources. NASA Ocean Worlds materials summarize asteroid and comet water-ice evidence.[7][8]

边界：古气候估算来自冰芯、沉积和化石代用指标，时间分辨率和不确定性因记录而异。海洋热含量数据随观测深度和年代修正调整；水合物风险评估区分本世纪与千年尺度。失控温室是金星式极端情景，地球当前气候系统距该边界的距离仍在研究中，不能把假说当成近期事件预测。Boundary: paleoclimate estimates rest on ice cores, sediments and fossil proxies, with time resolution and uncertainty varying by record. Ocean heat-content data shift with observation depth and dating corrections; hydrate risk assessments separate this-century from millennial scales. Runaway greenhouse is a Venus-style extreme scenario, and how far the current Earth system sits from that boundary remains under study; a hypothesis cannot be treated as a near-term event prediction.[6][7]

05为什么湖泊会缩小？Why do lakes shrink?

湖泊是流域水量收支的平衡点；补给长期小于蒸发、出流、渗漏与取水，湖面便下降，气候、引水和淤积是主要驱动。A lake balances watershed inflow against evaporation, outflow, seepage and withdrawal; sustained imbalance lowers the level, driven by climate, diversion and sedimentation.

湖泊是流域水量收支的平衡点。汇入湖泊的水有三类补给：直接降落在湖面的降水、从四周河流沟谷汇入的径流，以及地下水补给；湖水的损失同样有三条路径：水面蒸发、经出流河流排出，以及向湖底和湖岸渗漏。湖面高程和面积就随收支对比升降：补给大于损失时湖面上涨，损失长期大于补给时湖面下降、水域缩小。没有出流河道的终端湖最敏感，因为它只能靠蒸发消耗水量，收支稍有失衡就直接反映在湖面。A lake is the balance point of its watershed water budget. Water enters a lake as precipitation falling directly on the surface, runoff gathering through surrounding streams and valleys, and groundwater inflow. Water leaves through three paths: open-water evaporation, discharge through an outlet river, and seepage into the bed and shores. Lake level and area rise or fall with this balance: when inflow exceeds losses the level climbs, and when losses stay above inflow the level drops and the water body shrinks. Terminal lakes without an outlet are most sensitive, because they can only lose water by evaporation, so any imbalance shows up directly on the surface.[9]

气候偏干或变暖，会从两头压缩湖泊的水量：降水减少直接减少补给，温度升高加大水面蒸发和植被蒸腾，积雪融水补给也随暖季提前而改变。连续多年干旱叠加高温，入湖径流减少、蒸发加大，湖泊就持续退缩。高纬地区还可能因冻土融化、土层排水变好，湖水向地下渗漏增多，使部分湖泊干涸。气候驱动的退缩通常是多年尺度的趋势，与季节丰枯波动不同。A drier or warmer climate compresses lake water from both sides: less precipitation cuts inflow, while higher temperature raises evaporation and plant transpiration, and snowmelt supply shifts as the warm season arrives earlier. Years of drought stacked on high temperature reduce inflow and raise evaporation, so the lake retreats steadily. At high latitudes, thawing permafrost and better-drained soils can increase groundwater leakage and dry out some lakes. Climate-driven shrinkage is a multi-year trend, distinct from seasonal wet-and-dry fluctuation.[6][10]

人类活动在入湖环节截走水量：河流引水灌溉、工业与生活取水、滨湖抽取地下水，都会减少实际入湖补给；上游筑坝蓄水、围垦造地也会改变湖盆和来水。在干旱半干旱区，终端湖依赖的河流被大量引水后，湖面可在几十年内大幅下降，裸露的湖床成为盐尘来源。区分自然干旱与人为取水，要看流域内取水规模与入湖径流的长期记录。Human activity intercepts water before it reaches the lake: river diversion for irrigation, industrial and domestic withdrawal, and pumping groundwater near the shore all reduce actual inflow; upstream dams and land reclamation also alter the basin and incoming water. In arid and semi-arid regions, once a terminal lake's river is heavily diverted, the level can drop sharply within decades, and the exposed bed becomes a source of salt dust. Separating natural drought from human withdrawal requires long records of abstraction and inflow across the watershed.[9][10]

另一种缩小来自湖盆被充填。河流携带的泥沙和有机质在入湖处沉积，三角洲不断向湖中推进，湖底逐渐抬高，库容被压缩；这类缩小主要是容积变浅，水面面积未必同步下降。长期淤积加上水生植物扩张，湖泊可逐步演化为沼泽、草甸。泥沙淤积速率取决于流域侵蚀强度、植被覆盖和水库拦沙情况。Another kind of shrinkage comes from basin filling. Sediment and organic matter carried by rivers settle near the inlet, the delta advances into the lake, the bed rises gradually and storage capacity is compressed. This kind of shrinkage mainly makes the lake shallower; surface area need not fall at the same pace. Over long periods, combined with expanding aquatic plants, a lake can turn into marsh and meadow. Sedimentation rate depends on watershed erosion, vegetation cover and sediment trapped behind reservoirs.[9]

实例上，北美大盆地的终端湖在过去约 150 年间水面缩减最高可达约九成，驱动因素包括农业与城市用水、干旱和变暖气候；大盐湖、咸海等萎缩后盐度升高、栖息地退化。边界：季节涨落是正常波动，不能把一年的低水位当作长期萎缩；湖泊面积还受盆地形态、风场和测站基准影响。本章讲水量平衡与退缩机制，不展开流域水资源调度模型、地下水数值模拟或具体湖泊的管理方案。In practice, terminal lakes of the Great Basin have lost up to about 90 percent of their area over the past roughly 150 years, driven by agricultural and municipal water use, drought and a warming climate; after shrinkage, lakes such as the Great Salt Lake grew saltier and lost habitat. Boundary: seasonal rise and fall is normal fluctuation, and a single low-water year must not be read as long-term shrinkage; lake area also depends on basin shape, wind and gauge datum. This chapter covers the water budget and retreat mechanisms, not watershed water-resource scheduling models, groundwater numerical simulation or specific lake management plans.[10]
