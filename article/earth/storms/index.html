01闪电是怎么形成的？为什么先看见闪电、后听到雷声，雷声为什么轰隆隆？How does lightning form, why do we see it before hearing thunder, and why does thunder rumble?

云中电荷击穿空气形成放电通道；光速约 30 万 km/s、声速约 340 m/s，通道形状与反射让雷声隆隆。Charge separation breaks down air into a discharge channel; light runs near 300,000 km/s while sound moves 340 m/s, and channel shape plus reflection makes thunder rumble.

闪电是积雨云内部或云地之间的放电通道：云中冰晶与冰雹碰撞摩擦分离电荷，正负电荷分别集中在云的不同部位，当电场强度击穿空气绝缘，就形成一条直径约几厘米、长度 2–5 公里的放电通道，峰值温度可达约 3 万 K，约为太阳表面温度的五倍。雷声是通道内空气被瞬间加热、剧烈膨胀产生的冲击波，随后以声波形式在空气中传播。Lightning is a discharge channel inside a cumulonimbus cloud or between cloud and ground. Collisions between ice crystals and graupel separate charges, positive and negative charge gather in different parts of the cloud, and when the electric field breaks down air insulation a channel forms: a few centimeters across, 2–5 km long, peaking near 30,000 K, roughly five times the Sun's surface temperature. Thunder is the shock wave produced when the channel heats air instantly and that air expands violently, then travels outward as sound.[2][3]

先看见闪电后听见雷声，不是闪电慢，而是光速和声速差了约九十万倍。光在空气中速度接近 30 万 km/s，几公里外几乎瞬时到达；声音在 20°C 空气中约 340 m/s，每公里大约 3 秒。数看到闪光到听见雷声的秒数再除以 3，就能估算距离大致几公里；闪电如果在头顶，光和声几乎同时到，雷声会响得非常近。You see the bolt before you hear the thunder because light and sound speeds differ by about a factor of 900,000, not because lightning is slow. Light in air travels close to 300,000 km/s and arrives almost instantly from kilometers away; sound at 20°C moves about 340 m/s, roughly three seconds per kilometer. Counting seconds between flash and clap and dividing by three estimates distance in kilometers; overhead lightning arrives nearly together, so the thunder sounds very close.[1][2]

雷声轰隆隆、长短不一，来自通道的几何形状与声音传播。闪电通道不是笔直一条，而是分叉、弯折的折线，不同位置的振动传到听者距离不同，先后错开形成持续的隆隆声；声波遇到山体、云层和建筑物反射折射，低频成分传得更远，高频很快衰减，所以近处雷声清脆炸裂，远处雷声低沉滚动。一次云间闪电还可能被周围地形反复反射，让隆隆声持续十几秒。Rumbled, rolling thunder comes from the channel's shape and how sound spreads. A lightning channel is not straight but a forked, bent line, so vibrations from different points reach you at slightly different times and stretch into a long rumble. Sound reflects and refracts off hills, clouds and buildings; low frequencies travel farther while high frequencies fade fast, so nearby thunder cracks sharply and distant thunder rolls low. Intra-cloud bolts can bounce around terrain and keep rumbling for over ten seconds.[2][3]

这些机制对应实际观测和安全规则。NWS 的雷电安全资料指出，雷击可以打在离降雨区很远的地方，所谓晴天霹雳并非错觉；雷声听不到不代表没有雷击风险。闪电通道电流可达几万到十几万安培，主要危害是直击、接触电压、跨步电压和雷击后的次生火灾，室外人员应在坚固建筑或全封闭车辆内躲避，不要在树下、开阔高地和水边停留。These mechanisms match real observations and safety rules. NWS lightning safety notes that strikes can hit far outside the rain shaft, so a bolt from the blue is no illusion. Channel current reaches tens of thousands to over a hundred thousand amperes; main hazards are direct strike, contact voltage, step voltage and secondary fire. People outdoors should shelter in a solid building or fully enclosed vehicle, avoiding trees, open high ground and water.[1]

边界：闪电定位网可以事后记录雷击位置和强度，但雷暴内部的起电机制仍有未解决的细节；能估算距离，不能预测下一道闪电何时落在哪里。雷声大小受距离、地形和气象条件影响，不能直接换算成雷击能量；听到雷声只说明雷暴在附近，具体威胁还要结合预警和雷达回波判断。The boundary: lightning networks record strike location and strength afterward, but charging inside thunderstorms still has unresolved details. Distance can be estimated, but where the next bolt lands cannot be predicted. Thunder loudness depends on distance, terrain and weather, so it cannot be converted directly into strike energy. Hearing thunder means the storm is nearby; the actual threat still calls for warnings and radar echoes.[1][2]

02龙卷是怎么从超级单体雷暴中拧出来的？强度怎么分级？How does a tornado twist out of a supercell thunderstorm, and how is its intensity graded?

风切变把低层空气扭成旋转管，上升气流抬斜成中气旋，下沉气流拉到地面；EF0–EF5 按破坏反推风速。Shear twists low air into a rotating tube, the updraft tilts it into a mesocyclone, and downdraft pulls it to the ground; EF0–EF5 infers wind from damage.

龙卷是从超级单体雷暴底部垂到地面的狭窄旋转气柱，形成需要风切变、旋转上升气流和下沉气流三者配合。风随高度的风向风速差异把低层空气扭成水平旋转管，雷暴上升气流把旋转管抬斜成竖直涡旋，形成直径几公里的中气旋；后部下沉气流把涡旋拉向地面，当漏斗云接触地面，就成为龙卷。A tornado is a narrow rotating column hanging from a supercell thunderstorm base to the ground, formed when wind shear, a rotating updraft and a downdraft work together. Changes in wind direction and speed with height twist low-level air into a horizontal rotating tube; the storm updraft tilts that tube upright into a mesocyclone a few kilometers across. Rear-flank downdraft pulls the vortex toward the ground, and when the funnel touches down it becomes a tornado.[2][4]

机制分三步看。第一，低层暖湿空气被强上升气流吸入，环境风切变使上升气流整体旋转；第二，旋转被拉伸，角动量守恒让涡旋越转越快，和花样滑冰收臂加速同理；第三，后侧下沉气流把中气旋底部带到地面附近，地面沙尘被卷起，标志漏斗接地。超级单体之所以能维持强旋转，是因为上升气流被风切变倾斜，降水不直接压垮上升气流，旋转和上升得以共存。The mechanism runs in three steps. First, warm moist low air is pulled into a strong updraft, and environmental shear rotates the whole updraft. Second, the rotation is stretched and conserved angular momentum spins the vortex faster, like a skater pulling arms inward. Third, the rear downdraft carries the mesocyclone base near the ground, and dust rising from the surface marks touchdown. A supercell keeps its strong rotation because shear tilts the updraft so precipitation does not collapse it, letting rotation and ascent coexist.[4]

观测上，龙卷强度按 EF 分级（EF0–EF5），依据造成的破坏反推风速：EF0 约 105–137 km/h，EF5 超过 322 km/h；最强龙卷的地面风速可接近或超过 480 km/h。2011 年美国乔普林龙卷达到 EF5，是近代损失最严重的龙卷之一。NSSL 的资料显示，美国大平原每年龙卷数量最多，但全世界其他陆地区域的中纬度雷暴也能产生龙卷；多数龙卷直径几十到几百米，维持时间几分钟到几十分钟。Observed intensity uses the EF scale, EF0 to EF5, inferred from damage: EF0 runs about 105–137 km/h and EF5 exceeds 322 km/h; the strongest tornadoes can approach or pass 480 km/h near the ground. The 2011 Joplin tornado in the United States reached EF5 and ranks among the costliest modern tornadoes. NSSL notes that the U.S. Great Plains report the most tornadoes each year, though mid-latitude storms elsewhere can produce them too; most are tens to a few hundred meters wide and last minutes to tens of minutes.[4][5]

应用在预警和避险。多普勒雷达能识别中气旋和钩状回波，气象部门据此发布龙卷预警；预警时间通常只有几分钟到十几分钟。龙卷经过时房屋的薄弱面会被内外气压差和风压破坏，避险选择地下室或坚固建筑的内走廊，远离窗户；车辆不是可靠避风所，应离开低洼易被淹的路线，不要驾车跟随或追逐龙卷。This feeds warning and safety practice. Doppler radar spots mesocyclones and hook echoes, and forecasters issue tornado warnings on that evidence; lead time is usually minutes to a dozen minutes. During a passage, weak walls can fail from pressure difference and wind pressure. Seek a basement or interior hallway of a sturdy building, away from windows; cars are not reliable shelters, and drivers should leave low flood-prone routes rather than chase the storm.[5]

边界：雷达中气旋不等于一定接地龙卷，旋转涡旋在云下消散的情况很常见；EF 等级是事后按破坏痕迹反推，不等于直接测到的地面风速。龙卷路径的精确位置和持续时间提前量有限，现有模式难以给出定点定时的预报；照片和视频中的漏斗如果没有接触地面，严格说只是漏斗云，是否成龙卷要看地面是否卷起碎屑。The boundary: a radar mesocyclone does not guarantee a ground-touching tornado, and many vortices dissipate below the cloud. The EF grade is reconstructed afterward from damage rather than a measured surface wind. Precise path and timing ahead of time remain limited, and models cannot issue site-specific fixed-time forecasts. A funnel that never reaches the ground is strictly a funnel cloud; whether it is a tornado depends on debris lifted at the surface.[4][5]

03台风或飓风的风眼为什么风平浪静？热带气旋怎么命名、按什么排名？Why is the cyclone eye calm, and how are tropical cyclones named and ranked?

风眼是下沉增暖的中心，最强风在眼墙；WMO 轮换命名，破坏性名字退役，强度按 Saffir-Simpson 1–5 级。The eye is a sinking warming center; strongest winds sit in the eyewall. WMO rotates names, retires destructive ones, and ranks Category 1–5.

台风和飓风是同一类热带气旋，只是生成海域不同：西北太平洋称台风，大西洋和东北太平洋称飓风。它们在暖洋面上发展，中心风眼下沉气流增暖干燥，所以风眼内风平浪静；最强的风集中在环绕风眼的眼墙，对流猛烈、雨最大。风眼平静不是风暴消失，而是最成熟阶段的结构标志。Typhoons and hurricanes are the same class of tropical cyclone, named by basin: typhoon in the northwest Pacific, hurricane in the Atlantic and northeast Pacific. They develop over warm ocean water. The central eye has sinking, warming, drying air, so winds there are calm; the strongest winds cluster in the eyewall ring around it, with the fiercest convection and heaviest rain. A calm eye does not mean the storm has ended; it marks a mature stage of structure.[3][7]

结构由暖洋面和气压差驱动。海水表层温度约 26.5°C 以上时，暖湿空气上升，水汽凝结释放潜热加热中心空气，中心气压降低，周围空气加速流入补充；地转偏向力让流入气流旋转，北半球逆时针、南半球顺时针。风眼内空气下沉增温，形成晴朗少云的中心；眼墙是上升气流最强的环状区域，水汽在此凝结成浓密积雨云。Structure is driven by the warm sea surface and pressure gradient. Above a sea-surface temperature near 26.5°C, warm moist air rises, condensation releases latent heat that warms the core, central pressure falls, and surrounding air rushes in to replace it. Coriolis rotation spins the inflow: counterclockwise in the Northern Hemisphere, clockwise in the Southern. Air sinks and warms inside the eye, forming a clear center; the eyewall is the ring of strongest updraft, where moisture condenses into dense cumulonimbus.[7]

命名和分级有固定规则。世界气象组织维护各海域轮换的命名名单，破坏性强的风暴名字会被退役替换；等级按持续风速分 Saffir-Simpson 1–5 级。2019 年飓风多里安在巴哈马附近达到峰值持续风速约 185 mph（约 298 km/h），属五级；2003 年飓风伊莎贝尔从国际空间站可见清晰风眼，峰值曾为五级。风速、中心气压和移速共同决定登陆后的破坏范围。Naming and ranking follow fixed rules. The World Meteorological Organization keeps rotating name lists per basin, and names of destructive storms are retired and replaced; intensity uses the Saffir-Simpson scale from Category 1 to 5. Hurricane Dorian in 2019 peaked near the Bahamas at about 185 mph (roughly 298 km/h) sustained, a Category 5. Hurricane Isabel in 2003 showed a clear eye from the International Space Station and peaked as a Category 5. Wind speed, central pressure and forward speed together set the damage footprint after landfall.[6][7]

监测和应用围绕眼墙与外围雨带展开。卫星云图跟踪风眼形状和眼墙闭合程度，雷达和飞机探测测量风速气压；预报关注路径、强度和风暴潮。风眼过境时短暂平静容易让人误判风暴结束，实际上另一侧眼墙会反向扫过；沿海地区的撤离安排依据路径概率、风暴潮高度和预报登陆时间，而不是单看风速排名。Monitoring follows eyewall and outer rain bands. Satellite imagery tracks eye shape and eyewall closure; radar and reconnaissance flights measure wind and pressure. Forecasts focus on track, intensity and storm surge. The brief calm when the eye passes can be mistaken for the storm's end, but the opposite eyewall will sweep back through. Coastal evacuations rely on track probability, surge height and forecast landfall time, not the wind-speed rank alone.[3][6]

边界：热带气旋强度预报存在系统性不确定性，路径外的雨带也能造成强风暴雨；海温、风切变和上层涡旋都会改变发展。风眼平静只说明下沉区经过头顶，不代表风切或威胁解除。命名是管理便利，不按破坏力排序；把历史最强个例的强度套到下一场风暴上，会高估或低估实际风险。The boundary: tropical cyclone intensity forecasts carry systematic uncertainty, and outer rain bands away from the eye can still bring strong wind and heavy rain. Sea temperature, wind shear and upper-level vortices all alter development. A calm overhead eye only means the sinking zone has passed; it does not clear shear or threat. Naming is administrative convenience, not a destruction ranking, and projecting the strongest historical case onto the next storm over- or under-estimates the real risk.[6][7]

04冰雹是怎样在云里形成的？How does hail form inside a storm cloud?

冰核在强上升气流中被过冷水滴反复撞冻，升降循环包出冰层，托不住时坠落成雹。A frozen nucleus is repeatedly coated by supercooled droplets in a strong updraft; cycling through layers wraps ice shells until it falls.

冰雹是强对流云中反复冻结长大的冰粒。它形成于积雨云上升气流强盛的区域：云体上部温度低于 0 °C，水滴仍保持液态的过冷却状态；一颗细小冰粒或冻滴作为核，过冷水滴碰到它就冻结附上去，冰核开始长大。普通雨滴在云内只是碰并聚集，冰雹则要经历多次冻结和再冻结，因此质地更硬、结构分层。过冷水滴在缺少冻结核时可以低于 0 °C 仍不结冰，一旦碰到冰质表面就迅速释放潜热冻结附着。Hail consists of ice pellets that grow by repeated freezing inside a strong convective cloud. It forms where the updraft of a cumulonimbus cloud is vigorous: the upper cloud is below 0 °C, yet droplets remain liquid in a supercooled state. A tiny ice particle or frozen drop acts as a nucleus, and supercooled droplets freeze onto it on contact, so the nucleus begins to grow. An ordinary raindrop only coalesces inside the cloud, whereas hail undergoes repeated freezing and refreezing, making it harder and layered.[3][8]

升降循环决定分层结构。强上升气流把冰粒托到云体上部，过冷水滴不断撞冻在表面；当冰粒长到较重、或进入下降气流，它穿过云中不同温度层，表面部分融化再重新冻结。每绕云内气流一圈，外面就包一层冰，切开的冰雹可见透明与不透明相间的同心圈层，圈层数量对应它在云内经历的上升下降次数。温度较低、水滴迅速冻结时形成不透明的疏松冰层；温度接近0 °C、水膜慢慢铺开时冻结出较透明的冰层，两种圈层交替出现。Up-and-down cycles determine the layered structure. A strong updraft lifts the pellet into the upper cloud, where supercooled droplets keep freezing onto its surface; when the pellet grows heavier or enters a downdraft, it crosses temperature layers, partially melts, and refreezes. Each trip around the internal airflow wraps another shell of ice. A cut-open hailstone shows alternating clear and opaque concentric rings, and the number of rings corresponds roughly to the number of ascent and descent cycles it survived.[2][8]

冰雹大小与上升气流强度直接相关。能托住冰雹的气流越强，循环次数越多，冰雹可以长到直径数厘米；普通小雹粒直径约 5 mm 上下，较大的冰雹可超过 2 cm。冰粒最终重量超过上升气流的托举力时，便坠落到地面，这就是降雹；气流托举不足时，冰粒在云中较早落下，个头通常更小。Hail size is directly tied to updraft strength. The stronger the current that supports the pellet, the more cycles it survives, and the hailstone can reach several centimetres in diameter. Ordinary small pellets are around 5 mm across, while larger hail can exceed 2 cm. When the pellet's final weight exceeds the lifting force of the updraft, it falls to the ground as hail; if the lift is weak, the pellet drops earlier and is usually smaller.[3][8]

成雹需要不稳定的对流环境。冰雹多出现在冷暖空气剧烈交汇、云体高耸发展的雷暴中；高空冷空气叠在暖湿气流上方，层结不稳定，对流发展旺盛。不同雹粒在云内走过的路径不同，落地时大小和圈层结构也有差别；夏季午后到傍晚发展起来的强雷暴，最容易伴随冰雹出现。云内还要有充足的过冷水滴供应，冰粒才具备长大的物质基础；缺水的云只能产生细小冰粒。Hail needs an unstable convective setting. It is most common in thunderstorms where cold and warm air meet sharply and the cloud towers high; cold air aloft sits over warm moist air, the stratification is unstable, and convection grows vigorously. Pellets follow different paths inside the cloud, so hail reaching the ground varies in size and ring structure. Strong thunderstorms developing in the afternoon and evening of summer days are most likely to produce hail.[2][3]

边界：冰雹与霰、冻雨不是同一过程。霰是松脆的小冰粒，冻雨是落地后才冻结的过冷水滴，冰雹则在云内反复冻结长大。本章讲云内成雹的一般机制，不预报具体雷暴的落雹时间和地点，也不评估冰雹灾情、农作物损失或防护工程标准。Boundary: hail is not the same process as graupel or freezing rain. Graupel is a crumbly small ice particle, freezing rain is supercooled water that freezes only after landing, while hail freezes and grows repeatedly inside the cloud. This chapter explains the general in-cloud hail mechanism; it does not forecast when and where a specific storm will drop hail, and it does not assess hail damage, crop loss, or protection engineering standards.[2][8]
