01地震是什么？断层突然错动为什么会释放出这么大的能量？What is an earthquake, and why does slip on a fault release so much energy?

板块在断层上突然错动，积累的弹性应变以地震波形式释放；震级用矩震级 Mw 表示。Plates slip suddenly on a fault and release stored elastic strain as seismic waves; size is reported as moment magnitude Mw.

地震是岩石圈板块沿断层突然错动，把积累的弹性应变能以地震波形式释放出来的过程。板块边界的断层被摩擦锁住，两侧岩石持续变形，应变超过断层强度后发生破裂，能量在几秒到几十秒内传到四周。全球绝大多数地震集中在环太平洋地震带、洋中脊和大陆碰撞带上，震级用矩震级 Mw 表示，2011 年日本东北地震为 Mw 9.0，2008 年汶川地震为 Mw 7.9。An earthquake occurs when lithospheric plates slip suddenly on a fault, releasing accumulated elastic strain as seismic waves. Fault friction locks the two sides together while rock deforms around them; once strain exceeds fault strength, rupture begins and the energy spreads outward in seconds to tens of seconds. Most earthquakes concentrate along the Pacific Ring of Fire, mid-ocean ridges and continental collision belts. Size is reported on the moment magnitude scale, Mw: the 2011 Tohoku, Japan earthquake was Mw 9.0, and the 2008 Wenchuan earthquake was Mw 7.9.[1][4]

机制上，弹性回跳模型解释了应变如何释放。板块运动推动断层两盘缓慢相对滑动，断层面上的凹凸和摩擦把两侧岩石锁在一起，岩石像被弯曲的弹簧一样积蓄弹性应变；当应力超过摩擦强度，断层某一段突然破裂，破裂沿断层面向两侧扩展，两盘回弹到错位位置，储存的应变能一部分变成摩擦热和岩石破裂能，大部分以 P 波、S 波和面波的形式向四周辐射。P 波是压缩纵波，速度最快，能穿过固体和液体；S 波是剪切横波，速度约为 P 波的六成，只能在固体中传播。The mechanism is described by the elastic rebound model. Plate motion pushes the two sides of a fault past each other, but roughness and friction lock them in place, so the rock bends like a spring storing elastic strain. When stress overcomes fault strength, a segment ruptures, the crack spreads along the fault plane, and the two sides rebound to their offset positions. Part of the stored energy becomes frictional heat and fracture work, while most radiates away as P waves, S waves and surface waves. P waves are compressive longitudinal waves, the fastest of the set, and travel through both solid and liquid; S waves are transverse shear waves, roughly sixty percent of the P-wave speed, and move only through solid rock.[1][5]

USGS 的震级记录把这一过程量化：矩震级 Mw 由地震矩换算而来，地震矩正比于断层破裂面积、错距和岩石刚度。震级每增加一级，释放的能量约增大 32 倍，Mw 9.0 的能量约为 Mw 7.9 的 20 倍以上。IRIS 的台网记录显示，环太平洋带集中了全球约八成的浅源大地震，洋中脊则以较小的正断层地震为主；深源地震可深到约 670 公里，与俯冲板块下沉有关。OpenStax 把这些分布归为洋脊张裂、俯冲挤压和平移走滑三种板块边界。USGS magnitude records put numbers on this process. Moment magnitude Mw is derived from the seismic moment, which scales with rupture area, slip and rock stiffness. Each whole magnitude step releases about thirty-two times more energy, so an Mw 9.0 event carries more than twenty times the energy of an Mw 7.9 event. IRIS network records show that about eighty percent of large shallow earthquakes cluster around the Pacific margin, while mid-ocean ridges produce mostly smaller normal-fault quakes; deep events reach roughly 670 km, tied to sinking subducting slabs. OpenStax groups these patterns under three boundary types: ridges, subduction zones and strike-slip transforms.[2][4][6]

这些观测支撑了日常的地震监测与风险评估。全球和区域地震台网通过 P 波、S 波到达时间差定位震源深度与位置，地形变测量和卫星干涉雷达监视断层的慢滑移与应变积累。工程上按震级和场地条件估计地震动强度，抗震设计依据地面运动谱，而不是单一震级数字。断层测绘圈定地表破裂带，判断道路、水坝和居住区是否压在活动断层上。These observations support routine monitoring and hazard assessment. Regional and global seismic networks locate depth and position from the arrival-time gap between P and S waves, while surveying and satellite interferometry track slow slip and strain buildup. Engineers estimate ground motion from magnitude and local site conditions, and seismic design codes respond to response spectra rather than a single magnitude number. Fault mapping also traces surface rupture zones, showing whether new roads, dams or housing sit on an active fault.[1][5]

边界要写清：现有科学还不能精确预报地震发生的年月日和时刻，能做到的是长期地震概率评估和临震前的快速警报。注水采气、水库蓄水等人类活动可以触发小震，但不会制造出独立于构造应力之外的大地震。并非所有地面震动都是构造地震，矿山塌陷、爆破、滑坡也会记录到震动信号；余震是主破裂后断层继续调整的产物，其分布和衰减有统计规律，但单条余震序列的规模仍难提前锁定。The boundary matters: science cannot yet predict the exact day, month or hour of an earthquake. What it can provide are long-term probability estimates and short-warning seconds before strong shaking arrives. Fluid injection from gas and oil operations, and reservoir filling, can trigger small events, but they cannot create a large quake independent of tectonic stress. Not every recorded shake is tectonic: mine collapses, blasts and landslides also produce signals. Aftershocks are the fault adjusting after the main rupture; their decay follows statistical patterns, though the size of any single aftershock sequence remains hard to pin down in advance.[1][2]

02火山的爆发式喷发和溢流式喷发有什么不同？由什么决定？How do explosive and effusive eruptions differ, and what controls the style?

岩浆黏度和气体含量决定风格：黏稠富气爆发，稀薄贫气溢流；同一火山也可能切换。Magma viscosity and gas content set the style: viscous gas-rich magma explodes, fluid magma effuses, and a volcano can switch.

火山喷发分爆发式和溢流式两类，差别主要由岩浆的黏度和气体含量决定。黏稠、富含气体的岩浆在上升中气体难以逸出，压力迅速累积，冲破地表时形成火山灰柱、火山碎屑和火山炸弹，属于爆发式；稀薄、气体容易逸出的岩浆则顺着地形平缓漫流，属于溢流式。同一座火山在不同时期可以切换风格，判断一次喷发属于哪一类，要看岩浆成分、含水量和喷发通道形态，不能只看火山锥的外形。喷发柱内碎屑碰撞摩擦起电、电荷分离，击穿空气时放出火山闪电。Volcanic eruptions split into explosive and effusive styles, and the difference comes down to magma viscosity and gas content. Viscous, gas-rich magma traps volatiles as it rises, pressure builds fast, and the break at the surface throws ash columns, pyroclastic material and volcanic bombs skyward: that is the explosive style. Fluid magma whose gas escapes easily instead pours downslope in quiet lava flows: the effusive style. The same volcano can switch between the two styles over its history, so classifying an eruption means checking composition, water content and conduit shape rather than judging from the cone's outline. In the plume, colliding ash fragments charge and separate; the field breaking down the air produces volcanic lightning.[3][7][9]

根源在地幔熔融与岩浆上升过程。地幔局部熔融形成岩浆，密度小于围岩后缓慢上涌，在地壳浅部形成岩浆房；岩浆中溶解的水、二氧化碳等挥发分随压力降低而脱气，气泡体积可膨胀数十倍。岩浆黏度主要受二氧化硅含量控制：玄武质岩浆二氧化硅约 45–55%，黏度低、流动性好；流纹质岩浆二氧化硅超过 68%，黏稠得像蜂蜜，气泡被牢牢困住。黏度越高、气体越多，破裂时能量越集中，喷发就越猛烈。The root process is mantle melting and magma ascent. Local melting in the mantle produces magma that, being less dense than surrounding rock, rises slowly and pools in shallow crustal magma chambers. Water, carbon dioxide and other volatiles dissolved in the magma come out of solution as pressure drops, and gas bubbles can expand by tens of times. Viscosity is controlled mainly by silica: basaltic magma runs about 45–55 percent silica, low in viscosity and fluid; rhyolitic magma above 68 percent silica is honey-thick and traps bubbles tightly. The higher the viscosity and gas fraction, the more concentrated the energy at rupture, and the more violent the eruption.[4][7]

实例可以对照着看。1980 年 5 月 18 日美国圣海伦斯火山爆发式喷发，北侧山坡先发生崩落，减压后水蒸汽和气体猛烈冲出，火山灰柱升到约 19 公里，碎屑流覆盖周边山谷；夏威夷基拉韦厄火山 2018 年的喷发则以溢流式为主，裂隙涌出的玄武质熔岩沿平原流动，掩埋公路和房屋，却没有大规模灰柱。2022 年 1 月汤加洪阿哈阿帕伊岛火山在浅水海域爆发，海水与岩浆接触产生强烈水蒸汽爆炸，柱云冲到平流层。USGS 按喷出物体积和柱高把喷发分为 VEI 0 到 8 级。Real cases make the contrast clear. On May 18, 1980, Mount St. Helens in Washington erupted explosively: the north flank collapsed first, released pressure, and steam and gas blasted outward in a column that reached about 19 km, with pyroclastic flows sweeping the adjacent valleys. The 2018 eruption of Kīlauea in Hawaii was mainly effusive: basaltic lava poured from fissures across the coastal plain, burying roads and homes but producing no large ash column. The Hunga Tonga–Hunga Ha'apai eruption in January 2022, over shallow seawater, mixed magma with water for a strong steam explosion whose plume reached the stratosphere. USGS ranks eruptions from VEI 0 to 8 by ejected volume and column height.[3][7]

监测和防灾围绕这两类差别展开。火山地震、地面形变和气体成分变化被用来判断岩浆房是否增压；熔岩流速度慢、路线可预测，主要威胁是掩埋财产；火山灰可随风飘到数百公里外，影响航班和呼吸系统；碎屑流沿沟谷高速下冲，是爆发式喷发中最致命的现象。应急部门根据喷发类型划定撤离区，机场和电厂则单独评估灰灾风险。Monitoring and preparedness follow these differences. Volcanic earthquakes, ground deformation and gas chemistry track whether a magma chamber is pressurizing. Lava flows move slowly along predictable paths and threaten property mainly by burial; ash can drift hundreds of kilometers, disrupting flights and breathing; pyroclastic flows race down valleys and are the deadliest product of explosive eruptions. Agencies map evacuation zones by eruption style, while airports and power plants assess ash hazards separately.[3]

边界：喷发风格不是火山的固定标签。冰岛的火山既可以有裂隙溢流，也可以产生爆发式火山灰云；水下和冰下火山的能量被水或冰吸收，观测到的强度与陆上喷发不能直接比较。VEI 是喷发结束后的事后分级，不能用来预报下一次喷发的规模；把一次火山锥形状当成未来喷发方式的依据，会漏掉从溢流转为爆发的可能。The boundary: eruption style is not a fixed label for a volcano. Icelandic systems can show both fissure effusion and explosive ash clouds, and subaqueous or subglacial volcanoes absorb energy in water or ice, so their measured intensity cannot be compared directly with land eruptions. VEI is a retrospective grade assigned after the event; it cannot forecast the size of the next eruption. Treating cone shape as a guide to future behavior misses the possibility of a shift from effusive to explosive activity.[3][7]

03地震波有哪几种？岩浆冷却后形成什么岩石，喷发强度怎么分级？What seismic waves exist, what rock does cooled lava form, and how is eruption intensity graded?

P 波穿液固、S 波只穿固体；岩浆冷却为火成岩，喷出岩分玄武岩、安山岩、流纹岩；VEI 事后分级。P waves cross solids and liquids while S waves stay in solids; cooled magma forms igneous rock, and eruptions are graded afterward on the VEI scale.

地震波分 P 波、S 波和面波三类，岩浆冷却后形成火成岩，喷发强度则用 VEI 事后分级。P 波是最快的压缩波，S 波是较慢的横波，面波沿地表传播、衰减慢但晃动大。波在不同介质中的速度变化被用来反演地球内部结构；岩浆喷出地表或侵入地下缓慢冷却，分别形成喷出岩和侵入岩，常见喷出岩有玄武岩、安山岩和流纹岩。Seismic waves fall into three classes: P waves, S waves and surface waves. Magma cooled into rock forms igneous rock, and eruption intensity is graded afterward on the VEI scale. P waves are the fastest compressional waves, S waves are slower transverse waves, and surface waves travel along the ground, decaying slowly but shaking strongly. How wave speed changes with material lets scientists invert for Earth's interior structure. Magma that reaches the surface or intrudes underground and cools forms extrusive and intrusive igneous rocks; common extrusive types are basalt, andesite and rhyolite.[5][7]

波速机制很直接：弹性波在刚度越大、密度越低的介质中传播越快。P 波可以在固体和液体中传播，S 波依赖固体的剪切刚度，穿过液态外核时消失；地震学家正是根据 S 波影区推断外核为液态。多台台站记录到的波走时被反演成三维波速模型，也就是地震层析成像，用来寻找俯冲板块、岩浆房和异常低速带。The wave-speed mechanism is straightforward: elastic waves travel faster through stiffer, less dense material. P waves move through solids and liquids, while S waves depend on solid shear stiffness and vanish when they cross the liquid outer core. Seismologists inferred a liquid outer core from the S-wave shadow zone. Travel times recorded at many stations are inverted into three-dimensional velocity models, called seismic tomography, which locate subducting slabs, magma chambers and anomalous low-velocity zones.[4][5]

岩石分类对应岩浆成分与冷却速度。喷出岩浆在空气中快速冷却，矿物颗粒细小甚至形成玻璃质：低硅的玄武岩常见于洋中脊和夏威夷，中硅安山岩多见于俯冲带火山，高硅流纹岩对应爆发式喷发。侵入岩浆在地下缓慢冷却，颗粒粗大，成分相同的侵入岩分别是辉长岩、闪长岩和花岗岩。VEI 按喷出物体积分级：VEI 5 喷出约 1 立方公里以上火山碎屑，VEI 6 超过 10 立方公里，喷发柱可超过 25 公里；达到 VEI 6 以上的大型喷发常留下破火山口，火山锥塌陷填补岩浆房空位。Rock classification tracks magma composition and cooling rate. Extruded magma cools quickly in air, so mineral grains are fine or glassy: low-silica basalt dominates mid-ocean ridges and Hawaii, intermediate andesite appears at subduction volcanoes, and high-silica rhyolite pairs with explosive eruptions. Intrusive magma cools slowly underground with coarse grains; the matching intrusive rocks are gabbro, diorite and granite. VEI grades ejected volume: VEI 5 ejects roughly one cubic kilometer or more of pyroclastic material, VEI 6 more than ten cubic kilometers, and columns can top 25 km. Large VEI 6-plus eruptions often leave a caldera as the cone collapses into the emptied magma chamber.[6][7]

这些工具在火山监测和资源勘探中都在用。地震波速异常被用来圈定地下岩浆房的位置和规模，钻孔和台网联合测量波速随深度的变化；岩石学家通过薄片中的矿物组合判断岩浆来源和演化路径。对超级火山这类低概率事件，研究重点是识别过去喷发的堆积层和岩浆房状态，而不是给出近期喷发的确定日期。These tools serve both volcano monitoring and resource exploration. Seismic velocity anomalies outline the position and size of underground magma chambers, while boreholes and networks jointly measure how velocity changes with depth. Petrologists read mineral assemblages in thin sections to trace magma source and evolution. For low-probability supervolcano events, research concentrates on identifying past eruption deposits and magma-chamber state rather than fixing a near-term eruption date.[4][5]

边界：波速模型受台站分布和反演假设限制，低速带不一定就是液态岩浆；同一成分的岩石冷却速度不同，颗粒粗细可以差很多，分类不能只看颜色。VEI 衡量的是单次喷发的产物规模，不代表火山的危险性排名；把 VEI 6 以上事件的地质历史外推成近期时间表，是把百年尺度的观测和万年尺度的事件混在一起。The boundary: velocity models are limited by station coverage and inversion assumptions, and a low-velocity zone is not necessarily liquid magma. Rocks of the same composition can differ widely in grain size depending on cooling rate, so classification cannot rely on color alone. VEI measures one eruption's product volume, not a volcano's overall danger ranking. Extrapolating the geological record of VEI 6-plus events into a near-term schedule mixes centennial observation with events spaced over millennia.[5][7]

04地震为什么会发生？为什么先上下摇、再左右摇？板块为什么会移动？Why do earthquakes happen, why does shaking arrive up-and-down first, and why do plates move?

板块年移 2–10 cm，断层锁住积应变后破裂回弹；P 波先到上下颠，S 波后到左右晃，短期日期不可预报。Plates move 2–10 cm/yr; locked faults rupture and rebound. P waves arrive first with vertical jolt, S waves later with shear shake; exact dates cannot be predicted.

岩石圈被分割成若干板块，在软流层之上缓慢运动，动力来自地幔对流、重力滑移和板块冷却下沉，速度大约每年 2 到 10 厘米，和指甲生长相近。板块边界相互挤压、拉张或错动，断层被摩擦锁住，两侧岩石不能自由滑动，却被持续推着变形，弹性应变逐年积累。当应力超过断层面摩擦强度或岩石强度，断层某一段突然破裂，两盘回弹到错位位置，应变能以地震波形式向四周释放，这就是构造地震。The lithosphere is split into plates drifting slowly over the asthenosphere, driven by mantle convection, gravitational sliding and cooled slab sinking, at roughly two to ten centimeters per year, about fingernail growth speed. At boundaries where plates push, collide or slide past, faults are locked by friction; rock on either side cannot move freely yet keeps deforming, storing elastic strain year after year. When stress exceeds fault friction or rock strength, a segment ruptures, the sides rebound to offset positions, and stored strain radiates outward as seismic waves: a tectonic earthquake.[1][4]

地震波到达地面有先后，所以人先感到上下颠、再感到左右晃。P 波是压缩纵波，像弹簧伸缩一样推动岩石沿传播方向振动，地壳中速度约 5 到 8 km/s，能穿过固体和液体，最先到达；S 波是剪切横波，振动方向与传播方向垂直，速度约 3 到 4.5 km/s，只能在固体中传播，晚到一步。P 波上下颠簸通常较弱，S 波和随后的面波振幅大、周期长，左右摇晃对房屋破坏更明显；震中距越远，P 波与 S 波到达时间差越大，台网据此定位震源位置。Waves arrive in sequence, which is why people feel vertical jolting first and side-to-side shaking afterward. P waves are compressive longitudinal waves that push rock along travel direction like a spring, at about five to eight kilometers per second in the crust, crossing solids and liquids and arriving first. S waves are transverse shear waves vibrating perpendicular to travel, at about three to four and a half kilometers per second, moving only through solid rock and arriving later. The P-wave jolt is usually weak, while S waves and later surface waves carry larger amplitude and longer period, and their side-to-side motion damages buildings more clearly; the farther the station, the larger the P–S gap used to locate source and depth.[1][5]

同一条活动断层不是每年均匀错动，而是锁住、积累、破裂、再锁住的循环。两次大地震之间的复发间隔从几十年到数千年不等，取决于板块推挤速率、断层带强度和历史错距；古地震探槽揭露地层破裂痕迹，估计过去几千年的地震序列。主震之后断层周边应力重新调整，产生余震，余震频次随时间衰减，但单次余震的最大规模难以提前锁定。An active fault does not slip evenly each year but cycles through locking, strain buildup, rupture and renewed locking. Recurrence intervals between large events range from decades to millennia, depending on convergence rate, fault-zone strength and historical slip; paleoseismic trenches expose rupture traces to reconstruct sequences over thousands of years. After a main shock, surrounding stress readjusts into aftershocks whose frequency decays over time, though the largest single aftershock stays hard to fix in advance.[1][4]

现有科学还不能预报地震发生的具体年月日和时刻。地下水变化、动物行为、前震等所谓前兆在统计上并不可靠：同一类现象在无震时期也会出现，前震与主震的判别至今没有稳定指标。地震学家能做的是长期概率评估，给出某条断层未来几十年发生一定震级地震的可能性区间；以及临震前快速警报，利用 P 波先到、S 波后到的几秒到几十秒差，在强晃动到达前通知避险。把概率预报说成确定日期，超出当前证据范围。Science cannot yet predict the exact day, month or hour of an earthquake. Groundwater changes, animal behavior and foreshocks have been watched as precursors, but none proves statistically reliable: the same signals appear in quiet periods, and discriminating foreshocks from ordinary swarms has no stable indicator today. What seismology provides is long-term probability assessment, such as the chance of a given magnitude over coming decades, plus short-warning alerts using the seconds to tens of seconds between early P waves and later S waves to notify people before strong shaking arrives. Calling a probability estimate a fixed date goes beyond current evidence.[1][5]

边界：并非所有地面震动都是构造地震，矿山塌陷、爆破、滑坡、水库蓄水和注水采气也会记录到震动信号，后者可触发小震，但不会制造独立于构造应力之外的大地震。滑动速率、复发间隔和概率估计受观测年限限制，短记录外推到长期存在不确定性；不同区域断层类型、深度和介质差别很大，一条断层的规律不能直接套用。本章讲板块运动、波序与预报边界，不展开抗震设计或地震区划细节。The boundary: not every recorded shake is tectonic. Mine collapses, blasts, landslides, reservoir filling and fluid injection also produce signals; these can trigger small events but cannot create a large quake independent of tectonic stress. Slip rates, recurrence intervals and probabilities are limited by observation length, and short records carry uncertainty when extrapolated long term. Fault type, depth and rock differ by region, so one fault's pattern cannot be transferred to another. This chapter covers plate motion, wave order and prediction limits, not structural seismic design or detailed hazard zoning.[1][5]

05海啸来临前为什么会退潮？火山灰为什么让土壤变肥沃？Why does the sea retreat before a tsunami, and why does volcanic ash fertilize soil?

海底地震抬升海水形成长波，近岸浅水减速堆高，波谷先到则异常退潮；火山灰风化后释放养分，长期培肥。Submarine quakes lift water into long waves that slow and pile up near shore; a leading trough causes abnormal retreat. Weathered ash releases nutrients and builds soil over time.

海底大地震错动断层时，可能把整块海底抬升或下沉数米，上方大片海水被同步顶起或拉低，形成波长上百公里的长波，这就是海啸的起点。海啸在深水中传播很快，浅水波速约等于 √(gh)，g 为重力加速度、h 为水深；深水几千米时波速可达每小时数百公里，但波高通常只有几十厘米，远洋船只几乎察觉不到。海啸不是一道浪，而是一整列长周期波浪，先后多个波峰间隔可达数十分钟到一两个小时。When a large submarine earthquake slips a fault, it can lift or drop the seafloor by several meters, pushing or lowering a broad slab of overlying water and generating a wave train with wavelengths of a hundred kilometers or more: that is the start of a tsunami. In deep water a tsunami travels fast; shallow-water wave speed equals √(gh), with g gravity and h water depth, so at several kilometers depth it can reach hundreds of kilometers per hour, though wave height is usually only tens of centimeters and open-ocean ships barely notice. A tsunami is not one wave but a whole series of long-period waves, successive peaks spaced tens of minutes to an hour or two apart.[8]

传到近岸后，水深变浅，波速随水深减小而下降，后面的波追赶上来，波高被堆高；如果最先到达岸边的是波谷，海水会先大幅退离海岸，露出平时淹没的滩涂和礁石，随后波峰才汹涌涌回。这种异常退潮是明确的危险信号，不能当成捡贝壳的机会；退潮与涨潮的先后取决于海啸波形和岸线地形，有些地点先看到增水，有些先看到退水。河口、海湾地形还会放大潮位，使海啸灾害在局部岸段显著加重。Near the shore, water shallows, wave speed drops with depth, following waves catch up, and wave height piles up. If a trough reaches the coast first, seawater pulls far offshore, exposing beaches and reefs normally submerged, before the crest surges back. This abnormal retreat is a clear danger sign, not an opportunity to collect shells. Whether a site sees retreat or first rise depends on the tsunami waveform and coastal geometry; estuary and bay shapes can amplify the water level and make the hazard heavier along local shorelines.[3][8]

火山喷发是岩浆房中的岩浆带着溶解气体上升，压力降低后挥发分脱气、气泡膨胀，冲破地表的过程；岩浆黏度和气体含量决定爆发还是溢流。喷发抛出的火山灰是细小的岩石和玻璃碎屑，可随风飘到数百公里外，短期影响航班、呼吸和电力设施。火山灰沉降覆盖地表后，经过风化分解，会释放钾、磷、镁、钙等矿物养分；火山母质形成的土壤通常疏松、排水良好，保水保肥能力较强，长期来看有利于农作物生长。A volcanic eruption occurs when magma carrying dissolved gas rises from the chamber; as pressure drops, volatiles come out of solution, bubbles expand and break through the surface, with viscosity and gas content setting explosive or effusive style. Ejected ash consists of fine rock and glass fragments that can drift hundreds of kilometers, affecting flights, breathing and power lines in the short term. After ash settles and weathers, it releases mineral nutrients such as potassium, phosphorus, magnesium and calcium; soils formed on volcanic parent material are usually loose and well drained, with decent water and nutrient retention, supporting crops over the long term.[3][7]

火山灰让土壤肥沃是有时间尺度的。新鲜落下的灰层短期会掩埋植被、堵塞孔隙、改变水体酸碱度，灰中细颗粒和氟硫成分还可能影响牲畜和水源；只有经过风化、淋溶和生物作用，矿物颗粒分解出可利用养分，火山土才进入肥沃阶段。火山活动频繁的地区，人口往往聚集在火山周边耕作，正是因为长期土壤条件好，但同时也承担喷发、碎屑流和火山灰的风险；肥沃与危险来自同一套火山过程。Ash fertility works on a time scale. A fresh ash layer buries vegetation, clogs pores and shifts water chemistry in the short term, and fine particles plus fluorine and sulfur compounds can affect livestock and water sources. Only after weathering, leaching and biological action break down mineral grains into available nutrients does volcanic soil enter its fertile phase. Densely populated farmland around active volcanoes often reflects this long-term soil advantage, while the same communities carry the risk of eruptions, pyroclastic flows and ash fall; fertility and hazard come from the same volcanic process.[3][7]

边界：海啸由海底地震、滑坡、火山喷发或陨石撞击都可能触发，并非所有海底地震都会产生海啸，震级、断层位移方向和海水深度共同决定规模。火山灰的养分含量随岩浆成分和风化程度变化，不能笼统说所有火山土都高产；新鲜火山灰的健康和设施风险不能被长期肥力抵消。本章讲海啸传播与近岸增水、火山灰沉降与土壤改良的机制和时间边界，不展开海啸预警系统布设、火山灾害区划或具体农田施肥方案。The boundary: tsunamis can be triggered by submarine earthquakes, landslides, volcanic eruptions or impacts, and not every submarine quake produces one; magnitude, fault slip direction and water depth jointly set the size. Ash nutrient content varies with magma composition and weathering, so not all volcanic soils are equally productive, and the short-term health and infrastructure risk of fresh ash cannot be offset by long-term fertility. This chapter covers tsunami propagation, ash deposition and soil limits, not warning deployment, hazard zoning or fertilization plans.[3][8]
