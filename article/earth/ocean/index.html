01海水为什么是咸的？平均盐度大约是多少？Why is seawater salty, and what is its average salinity?

开阔大洋平均盐度约 35‰；盐分由河流风化输入与洋壳交换累积，蒸发留盐、降水径流补回淡水。The open ocean averages near 35 parts per thousand; salts accumulate through weathering runoff and crustal exchange, evaporation leaves salts behind and precipitation returns fresh water.

海水是咸的，世界开阔大洋的平均盐度约为 35‰，即每千克海水中约含 35 克溶解盐；其中氯化钠约占溶解盐类的四分之三，氯离子和钠离子是含量最高的两种离子。这个均值在大洋之间差别不大，表层受降水、径流和蒸发影响略有升降，近岸河口区可以明显偏低。海水并不只有咸味，镁盐和硫酸盐带来苦味，因此尝起来又咸又涩；盐度衡量的是全部溶解无机盐，而不是单独的氯化钠浓度。Seawater is salty, and the open ocean averages about 35 parts per thousand, meaning roughly 35 grams of dissolved salts in each kilogram of seawater. Sodium chloride makes up about three quarters of those dissolved salts, with chloride and sodium the two most abundant ions. The mean varies little between major oceans; the surface shifts slightly with rainfall, runoff and evaporation, and can drop noticeably near estuaries. Seawater is not simply salty: magnesium salts and sulfates add bitterness, which is why it tastes both salty and astringent. Salinity measures all dissolved inorganic salts, not sodium chloride alone.[1][2]

海盐主要来自两条长期输入路径。陆地岩石经风化剥蚀后，河流把溶解的钠、钙、镁、硫酸盐等离子持续带入海洋；海底热液喷口和火山活动则在海水与洋壳之间交换矿物离子，把一部分物质送回海水。河流每年输入的溶解物总量很大，但海水在地质时间尺度上保持大致稳定：蒸发只把纯水变成水汽离开海洋，盐分留在海里，降水、融冰和径流又把淡水补回表层，输入、停留和埋藏之间形成长期平衡。Sea salts enter mainly through two long-term pathways. Weathering of continental rocks lets rivers carry dissolved sodium, calcium, magnesium and sulfate ions continuously to the ocean; hydrothermal vents and volcanic activity exchange mineral ions between seawater and the ocean crust. Rivers deliver large amounts of dissolved material each year, but seawater stays roughly stable on geological time scales: evaporation lifts pure water as vapor and leaves salts behind, while precipitation, melting ice and runoff return fresh water to the surface, balancing input, residence and burial over the long term.[1][2]

海水中主要溶解离子的比例相当稳定：氯离子约 19‰，钠离子约 10.5‰，其余为镁、硫、钙、钾等成分。盐度历史上用氯度滴定换算，现代以实用盐度单位表示，数值与千分比接近。温度、盐度和压力共同决定海水密度，进而决定海水的分层与下沉运动；河口区淡水密度小、浮在咸水之上，会形成明显的盐度跃层，两种水体之间混合需要时间和湍流帮助。The proportions of major dissolved ions are fairly constant: chloride near 19 parts per thousand, sodium near 10.5, with magnesium, sulfur, calcium and potassium making up the rest. Salinity was once converted from chloride titration, while modern practice uses the practical salinity scale, close to parts per thousand. Temperature, salinity and pressure jointly set seawater density, which in turn controls stratification and sinking. In estuaries, lower-density fresh water floats on saltier water and forms a halocline; the two layers take time and turbulence to mix.[1][2]

NOAA 海洋服务页面给出世界大洋平均盐度约 35‰，并说明盐分由河流搬运与洋壳交换长期累积。不同海区的盐度差异与当地降水—蒸发平衡有关：副热带蒸发旺盛的海区表层偏咸，赤道和高纬降水较多、融冰补给较多的海区偏淡。北极和南极的海冰形成时把盐分排出冰体，冰本身是淡水；冰融化后回到海中，会在一段时间内降低表层盐度。NOAA Ocean Service states the open-ocean average near 35 parts per thousand and explains that salts accumulate through river transport and crustal exchange. Regional salinity follows the local precipitation–evaporation balance: subtropical zones with strong evaporation are saltier near the surface, while equatorial and high-latitude zones with more rain and melting ice are fresher. Sea ice at the poles expels salt as it forms, so the ice itself is fresh; when it melts back into the sea, it lowers surface salinity for a period.[1][2][8]

边界要写清：盐度是平均概念，不能把所有海域都当成 35‰，近岸、河口和高纬融冰区差异显著。海水含盐总量也不是一成不变，长期地质尺度上，一部分盐分通过沉积埋藏、生物钙化和海相蒸发岩离开海水。本章讲盐度的来源、成分与收支，不展开具体海区的盐度季节预报或河口盐度数值模拟。Boundary: salinity is an average, and no single region should be assumed to sit at 35 parts per thousand; nearshore, estuarine and polar melt zones differ markedly. Total dissolved salt is not fixed either: on geological time scales, some salts leave seawater through sediment burial, biological calcification and marine evaporite deposits. This chapter covers the source, composition and budget of salinity, not regional seasonal forecasts or estuarine numerical models.[1][2]

02海水按温度和密度可以分成哪几层？深层海水为什么不是 4 °C？What temperature and density layers divide the ocean, and why is deep water not at 4 °C?

表层混合层、温跃层与深层冷水三层；海水含盐使密度峰值低于冰点，深层水温多在 0–3 °C。Surface mixed layer, thermocline and deep cold layer; salts push the density maximum below freezing, so deep water mostly sits at 0–3 °C.

海水并不是上下均匀的一盆水，而是按温度和密度分成三层。最上层是混合层，受风、波浪和日照搅动，温度较高、盐度均匀，厚度从几十米到几百米不等；中间是温跃层，温度随深度快速下降，密度随之增大；最下层是深层冷水，覆盖大洋底部，水温长期维持在接近冰点但不冻结的低温范围。表层暖、底层冷的结构，是太阳辐射只能加热表层、而冷水密度更大下沉所致。Seawater is not one uniform body but three layers ordered by temperature and density. The uppermost mixed layer is stirred by wind, waves and sunlight: it is warmer and fairly uniform in salinity, tens to a few hundred metres thick. The middle thermocline drops rapidly in temperature with depth, and density rises with it. The deepest cold layer covers the ocean floor and stays near freezing without solidifying. Warm surface over cold deep water follows because solar radiation heats only the top, while denser cold water sinks.[3][4]

很多人以为深海温度接近 4 °C 的水密度峰值，实际并非如此。纯水在 4 °C 左右密度最大，但海水含有盐分，密度峰值被压低到冰点以下；深层大洋水温通常在 0–3 °C 之间，盐度高、密度大，缓缓沿洋底流动。压力随深度近似每下降 10 米增加约一个大气压，万米海沟处压力可超过一千个大气压，但海水本身的可压缩性很小，深度压力主要作用于气体空腔和人体，而不是把水体整体压垮。Many people expect deep-sea temperatures near the 4 °C density peak of pure water, but that is not the case. Pure water is densest around 4 °C, yet dissolved salts push that density maximum below the freezing point; deep-ocean water typically sits between 0 and 3 °C, salty and dense, moving slowly along the seabed. Pressure rises by roughly one atmosphere per ten metres of depth, exceeding a thousand atmospheres in the deepest trenches, but seawater itself compresses little; depth pressure acts mainly on gas cavities and organisms rather than crushing the water body as a whole.[2][3]

分层由温度、盐度和压力共同决定，密度小的水浮在密度大的水之上。副热带和高纬海区表层水在冬季冷却、增密后下沉，形成深层水团，沿着洋底缓慢流向其他海区，这就是温盐环流的深部支路。跃层越强，上下水体越难交换；季节变化时，表层降温或径流冲淡水，都会改变垂直密度剖面，进而影响营养盐从深层向表层的输送。Stratification follows temperature, salinity and pressure together, with less dense water floating above denser water. Where surface water cools and gains density in winter, in subtropical or high-latitude zones, it sinks and forms deep water masses that creep along the seafloor toward other basins: the deep branch of the thermohaline circulation. A stronger thermocline slows vertical exchange; seasonal cooling or fresh runoff changes the vertical density profile and therefore the transport of nutrients from deep to surface.[3][4]

NOAA 海洋服务页面把海洋温度剖面分为表层混合层、温跃层和深层冷水带，并说明温跃层是温度随深度急剧变化的过渡区；深海页面补充了无光、高压、低温和食物稀少的环境特征。不同海区的分层强度不同：热带海区表层温暖、温跃层稳定，高纬海区表层温度本就偏低，垂直混合可以一直深入到较深位置。NOAA Ocean Service divides the temperature profile into a surface mixed layer, a thermocline and a deep cold layer, describing the thermocline as the transition where temperature drops sharply with depth; its deep-sea page adds the dark, high-pressure, low-temperature, food-poor environment. Stratification strength varies by region: tropical seas hold warm surfaces with a stable thermocline, while high-latitude surface water is already cold and can mix deeply.[3][4]

边界：三层划分是教学上的简化，真实海洋中还存在中层水、底层水等多个水团，分层位置和厚度随季节、纬度和海流变化。温度低不等于密度一定大，盐度也会改变密度；仅凭“深海很冷”不能推断某一海区的水团运动。本章讲垂直结构与密度分层，不展开声速剖面、海洋声学或具体水团的环流数值模拟。Boundary: the three-layer scheme is a teaching simplification; the real ocean contains intermediate and bottom water masses, and layer position and thickness shift with season, latitude and currents. Low temperature alone does not mean high density, because salinity also changes density, and “the deep sea is cold” cannot by itself predict water-mass motion. This chapter covers vertical structure and density stratification, not sound-speed profiles, ocean acoustics or numerical water-mass simulations.[2][3]

03洋流和潮汐分别由什么驱动？两者在尺度上怎样分工？What drives ocean currents and tides, and how do the two differ in scale?

表层流由盛行风与科里奥利效应推动，深层流由密度差驱动，潮汐来自日月引力差叠加地球自转。Surface currents follow winds and the Coriolis effect, deep currents follow density differences, and tides arise from lunar-solar gravitational gradients plus Earth's rotation.

洋流和潮汐是两类不同尺度的海水运动，驱动力并不相同。表层洋流主要由盛行风摩擦海面推动，在地转偏向力和海陆轮廓约束下形成大尺度环流圈：副热带大洋环流在北半球顺时针、南半球逆时针。深层洋流则由密度差驱动，冷水和高盐水团下沉后沿洋底缓慢流动，构成跨洋盆的温盐环流，把热量、盐分和溶解气体在全球范围内重新分配。Ocean currents and tides are motions on different scales, driven by different mechanisms. Surface currents are mainly pushed by prevailing winds dragging on the sea surface; constrained by the Coriolis effect and coastlines, they form large circulation gyres, clockwise in the subtropical Northern Hemisphere and counterclockwise in the Southern. Deep currents are driven by density differences: cold, salty water masses sink and creep along the seabed, forming the inter-basin thermohaline circulation that redistributes heat, salt and dissolved gases worldwide.[3][5]

潮汐来自月球和太阳的引力在地球上不同位置的差异，而不是简单的“月亮把水吸上去”。地球朝向月球一侧和背向月球一侧受到的引潮力方向不同，加上地球自转，沿岸海面每天出现两次涨落；太阳引潮力约为月球的一半，朔望时两者叠加形成大潮，上下弦时相互抵消一部分形成小潮。河口、海湾地形会放大潮差，狭窄喇叭口和浅滩可以把常规潮汐放大成明显的涌潮。Tides come from the gradient of lunar and solar gravity across the Earth, not from the moon simply pulling water upward. The tidal force differs on the side facing the moon and the opposite side; together with Earth's rotation, this produces twice-daily rises and falls along coasts. The solar tidal force is about half the lunar one: at new and full moon the two add up to spring tides, and at quarter moons they partly cancel into neap tides. Estuary and bay geometry amplifies tidal range, and narrow funnel-shaped inlets with shallows can turn ordinary tides into bores.[5][6]

表层流和深流配合，形成全球海洋的输送网络。表层环流从低纬携带热量向高纬运动，深层水在高纬下沉后回到低纬深处，完成多年到千年尺度的环路；上升流区把深层营养盐带到表层，支撑浮游植物和渔场。潮汐则是近岸最稳定的周期性运动，除了水位涨落，还带动沿岸流、潮流通道泥沙搬运和河口盐度锋面移动。Surface and deep currents together form the global transport network. Surface circulation carries heat from low to high latitudes, while deep water sinks at high latitudes and returns at depth to the tropics on a years-to-millennia loop. Upwelling zones bring deep nutrients to the surface and support phytoplankton and fisheries. Tides remain the steadiest nearshore motion: besides water level, they drive coastal currents, sediment transport through tidal channels and the movement of estuarine salinity fronts.[5][6]

NOAA 海洋服务页面分别介绍了表层洋流、温盐环流和潮汐的基本成因：表面流受风场和科里奥利效应支配，密度流受温度盐度控制，潮汐由日月引力差和地球自转共同产生。这些页面同时说明，洋流输送的不仅是水，还有热量、碳和营养盐；潮汐周期则受天文位置与地形共同调制。NOAA Ocean Service explains surface currents, thermohaline flow and tides separately: surface flow follows wind fields and the Coriolis effect, density flow follows temperature and salinity, and tides combine lunar and solar gravitational gradients with Earth's rotation. The pages note that currents transport not only water but heat, carbon and nutrients, while tidal cycles are modulated by astronomical position and coastal geometry.[5][6]

边界：洋流方向和流速受风场变化、涡旋和局地地形影响，不能把大尺度环流圈当成静止不变的管道。潮汐高度是天文引力与海岸地形、水深、河流径流叠加的结果，同一潮周期在不同岸段差别很大。本章讲运动的成因与尺度分工，不展开具体航线的海流预报、潮流能资源评估或风暴潮数值模型。Boundary: current direction and speed shift with wind changes, eddies and local topography, so a large gyre should not be read as a fixed pipeline. Tidal height results from gravitational astronomy combined with coastal shape, depth and river runoff, so the same tidal cycle differs along adjacent shorelines. This chapter covers causes and scale division, not route-specific current forecasts, tidal-energy resource assessment or storm-surge numerical models.[5][6]

04人类测绘了多少比例的海底？深海是什么样的环境？How much of the seafloor has been mapped, and what is the deep-sea environment like?

高分辨率多波束测绘覆盖比例有限，其余靠卫星高度反演；深海无光、高压、低温、食物稀少。High-resolution multibeam coverage remains limited and the rest relies on satellite altimetry; the deep sea is dark, high-pressure, cold and food-poor.

人类对海底的测绘还很不完整。早期海图靠绳索测深，只能在少量测点之间推断地形；现代船载多波束声呐同时发射扇形声波束，测量回波时间换算出海底高程，再拼接成水深图。即便如此，覆盖全球洋底的高分辨率多波束测绘比例仍然有限，大片深海海底只靠卫星海面高度反演和稀疏测点估计，分辨率不足以辨认小尺度地貌。海底测绘是海洋科学、航海安全和资源管理的基础工作。Humans have mapped the seafloor only incompletely. Early charts used rope soundings, inferring terrain between sparse points; modern shipboard multibeam sonar fires fan-shaped acoustic beams, converts echo travel time to seabed elevation, and stitches depth charts together. Even so, the share of the global seafloor covered by high-resolution multibeam mapping remains limited, and large deep areas are estimated from satellite sea-surface-height inversion and sparse soundings at resolutions too coarse to show small features. Seafloor mapping remains a base work for ocean science, navigation safety and resource management.[4][7]

深海环境与表层差异极大。阳光穿透深度有限，真光层以下几乎没有光线，光合作用停止；生物依赖表层沉降的有机碎屑，食物稀少。水温长期偏低，盐度偏高，压力随深度持续增大。热液喷口和冷泉等局部环境例外，化学能支撑了不依赖阳光的生态系统；海沟、海山和深海平原构成了地球表面最大面积的陆地之外地貌单元。常被引用的“只探明 5%”一类数字口径不一：有时指高分辨率声呐测绘覆盖的洋底比例，有时指已记录物种或水体剖面的比例，不同口径不能混用；近年无人潜水器与科考船仍在持续下潜和测绘，海洋探测并没有停止。The deep sea differs sharply from the surface. Light penetrates only to a limited depth; below the photic zone there is almost no light, photosynthesis stops, and animals rely on organic debris sinking from above, so food is scarce. Water stays cold and salty, and pressure keeps rising with depth. Local exceptions such as hydrothermal vents and cold seeps host ecosystems driven by chemical energy rather than sunlight; trenches, seamounts and abyssal plains make up the largest landless geomorphic area on Earth's surface.Often-quoted figures such as five percent explored mix different definitions: the number may refer to the share of seafloor mapped at high-resolution sonar, to recorded marine species, or to sampled water-column profiles, and these calibers cannot be compared directly; unmanned vehicles and research vessels keep diving and surveying, so ocean exploration has not stopped.[4][7]

探测工具包括载人深潜器、遥控潜水器和自主水下航行器。载人深潜器可抵达万米海沟但航程有限；遥控潜水器通过缆绳连接母船，能长时间作业并传回影像；自主航行器按预设航线测绘声呐剖面。科考船投放的浮标、潜标和锚系长期记录温度、盐度和流速，卫星则从海面反演环流和海面高度，多种观测手段拼出目前最接近完整的海洋图景。Exploration tools include manned submersibles, remotely operated vehicles and autonomous underwater vehicles. Manned submersibles reach ten-kilometre trenches but have limited range; ROVs tethered to a mother ship work for long hours and transmit images; AUVs follow programmed routes to survey sonar profiles. Moorings, buoys and floats record temperature, salinity and current over long periods, while satellites infer circulation and sea-surface height from above, and the combined observations give today's most complete picture of the ocean.[4][7]

NOAA 海洋探索页面说明，目前只有一小部分海底被高分辨率测绘过，深海仍有大量未勘查区域；深海页面补充了无光、高压、低温和食物受限的环境约束。多波束声呐、卫星高度计和水下航行器各自覆盖不同尺度，测绘比例随技术投入逐步提高，但完全绘制海底地图仍需要长期全球观测计划。NOAA's ocean exploration page notes that only a small fraction of the seabed has been mapped at high resolution, leaving large deep-sea areas unsurveyed; its deep-sea page adds the constraints of darkness, high pressure, low temperature and limited food. Multibeam sonar, satellite altimetry and underwater vehicles cover different scales, and mapping share rises gradually with technical investment, but a complete seafloor chart still needs a long-term global observing programme.[4][5][7]

边界：卫星海面高度反演反映的是海底地形对海面重力的间接影响，分辨率低于船载声呐；“深海探索不足”不等于深海完全未知，主要环流、水团和大型地貌已有长期观测。本章讲测绘手段与深海环境，不展开具体海沟的地质成因、极端生物分类或深海采矿评估。Boundary: satellite altimetry reflects the indirect gravity signature of seabed terrain and resolves less detail than shipborne sonar; “underexplored deep sea” does not mean the deep ocean is entirely unknown, since major circulation, water masses and large landforms have long been observed. This chapter covers mapping methods and deep-sea environment, not trench geology, extreme-biology taxonomy or deep-sea mining assessment.[4][7]

05为什么不直接煮沸海水制取淡水？反渗透脱盐是怎么回事？Why not boil seawater directly for fresh water, and how does reverse osmosis desalination work?

蒸馏分离彻底但汽化能耗高；反渗透以高压驱动半透膜截留盐分，能耗较低，需预处理并处理浓盐水排放。Distillation separates thoroughly but costs much heat; reverse osmosis pushes fresh water through a membrane under pressure, using less energy, with pretreatment and brine to manage.

海水淡化是把海水中的盐和水分开、获得可利用淡水的过程。最简单的思路是煮沸蒸馏：加热海水使水汽化，再把蒸汽冷凝成淡水，盐分留在残余浓水中。蒸馏分离彻底，但把水加热到沸腾、汽化需要消耗大量热能，单位淡水能耗高；在化石能源或廉价余热充足的地区可行，在普通城市供水尺度上往往竞争不过其他水源。Desalination separates salt and water in the sea to produce usable fresh water. The simplest idea is distillation: heat seawater until water vaporizes, condense the vapor into fresh water, and leave salts in the concentrated brine. Distillation separates thoroughly, but boiling and vaporizing water costs large amounts of heat per unit of fresh water; it works where fossil energy or cheap waste heat is plentiful, but often cannot compete with other sources at ordinary urban water-supply scale.[2][8]

现代大型海水淡化以反渗透为主流。高压泵把海水加压到超过渗透压，使淡水透过半透膜而盐分被截留，膜两侧的选择透过性代替相变完成分离。反渗透不需要把水烧开，能耗主要花在高压泵送和预处理上；膜组件成排布置，进水在多层膜面之间错流过滤，浓盐水被排回海洋。与蒸馏相比，反渗透能耗更低、启动更快，但需要预处理去除悬浮物，膜材料会老化结垢，需要定期更换。Large modern desalination relies mainly on reverse osmosis. High-pressure pumps push seawater above its osmotic pressure so that fresh water crosses a semipermeable membrane while salts are retained; selective permeability across the membrane replaces a phase change to complete separation. RO does not boil the water: energy goes mainly into high-pressure pumping and pretreatment. Membrane modules are arranged in banks, feed water flows tangentially across layers of membrane, and concentrated brine is discharged back to the sea. Compared with distillation, RO uses less energy and starts faster, but needs pretreatment to remove suspended matter, and membrane materials age and foul, requiring periodic replacement.[2][8]

淡化水的成本和边界不只在车间内部。原水可以是海水，也可以是微咸水；浓盐水盐度高、体积不小，排海后需要稀释扩散，避免在局部造成盐度升高。取水和排放口会影响近岸生态，预处理药剂和膜清洗废液也需要管控。长期看，淡化是缺水地区的补充水源，不能替代节水、水循环和本地水资源保护；能耗越高，电力结构越决定淡化水的碳足迹。Cost and limits do not end inside the plant. Feed water can be seawater or brackish water; the high-salinity brine is substantial in volume and must be diluted and dispersed at the outfall to avoid local salinity rises. Intakes and discharges affect nearshore ecology, and pretreatment chemicals and membrane-cleaning waste need management. Over the long term, desalination is a supplementary source in water-scarce regions and cannot replace conservation, recycling and local water protection; the more energy-intensive the process, the more the power grid's mix sets the desalinated water's carbon footprint.[2][8]

NOAA 海洋服务页面介绍了海水淡化的两类基本工艺：蒸馏靠相变把水蒸发再冷凝，反渗透靠压力驱动半透膜分离盐分；盐度页面说明海水平均含盐约 35‰，淡化产品水的盐度则需降到饮用水标准以内。工艺选择取决于能源价格、原水盐度、场地条件和排放环境，沿海干旱地区常用反渗透，有低价余热或核电站配套的场合也会采用蒸馏。NOAA Ocean Service describes two basic desalination routes: distillation uses a phase change to evaporate and condense water, while reverse osmosis drives salt separation through a pressure-driven semipermeable membrane. Its salinity page notes seawater averages about 35 parts per thousand, while product fresh water must drop within drinking-water limits. Process choice depends on energy prices, feed salinity, site conditions and discharge environment: arid coasts favor RO, while cheap waste heat or nuclear-plant co-location can favor distillation.[1][2][8]

边界：淡化解决的是盐分分离，不自动解决水源的全部问题；淡化厂的取水、浓盐水排放和能耗都要纳入区域水资源评估。蒸馏和反渗透不是互相取代的唯一路线，还有电渗析等用于微咸水的工艺。本章讲淡化原理、能耗与环境边界，不评估具体水厂的投资回报、水质标准细则或膜材料专利技术。Boundary: desalination solves salt separation and does not automatically settle every water-supply problem; intake, brine discharge and energy use must be included in regional water assessment. Distillation and RO are not the only routes, and electrodialysis among other processes serves brackish water. This chapter covers desalination principles, energy use and environmental limits, not plant-level investment returns, detailed drinking-water standards or membrane-material patents.[2][8]

06水为什么在 4 ℃ 时最重？冰为什么能浮在水面上？Why is water heaviest at 4 °C, and why does ice float on water?

4 ℃ 纯水密度最大；氢键围成开放六方晶格，冰密度约 0.917 g/cm³，膨胀约 9% 浮起。Pure water peaks near 1.000 g/cm³ at 4 °C; hydrogen bonds form an open hexagonal lattice, ice is 0.917 g/cm³ and expands about 9 percent to float.

绝大多数物质都是冷缩热胀，温度越低密度越大，水在 0 到 4 ℃ 区间却反过来：纯水在约 4 ℃（更精确为 3.98 ℃）时密度最大，约 1.000 g/cm³；温度再降到冰点，密度反而变小，结成的冰浮在水面。水的这种反常膨胀来自分子间的氢键：液态水中分子不断运动、短暂成键又断开，分子排列挤得较紧；温度降低时分子热运动减弱，靠得更近，密度上升；到 4 ℃ 附近分子间距达到最小，继续降温，氢键开始把分子固定成规则结构，间距重新撑开。Most substances contract and grow denser as they cool, but water reverses that rule between 0 and 4 degrees Celsius. Pure water reaches its maximum density, about 1.000 grams per cubic centimeter, near 4 degrees, more precisely 3.98; below that toward freezing, density drops and the resulting ice floats. This anomalous expansion comes from hydrogen bonds: in liquid water molecules move constantly, forming and breaking short-lived bonds and packing fairly tightly; as temperature falls thermal motion weakens and molecules draw closer, raising density to the minimum spacing near 4 degrees. Colder still, hydrogen bonds lock molecules into an ordered arrangement that spreads the spacing back apart.[9]

结冰时，每个水分子通过氢键与周围四个水分子连接，形成开放的六方晶格，环与环之间留有空腔。这套规则结构把分子间距拉大，同质量的水变成冰后体积膨胀约 9%，冰的密度约 0.917 g/cm³，所以冰块约有 9% 的体积露出水面。和液态水相比，冰晶里的分子不是更密而是更整齐；正是开放晶格的空隙，让冰比水轻。冰晶的六方对称也决定了雪花沿六个方向生长，与这一氢键骨架直接相关。On freezing, each water molecule bonds through hydrogen bonds to four neighbors in an open hexagonal lattice, leaving cavities between rings. This regular structure widens molecular spacing: the same mass of water expands about 9 percent into ice, whose density is about 0.917 grams per cubic centimeter, so roughly 9 percent of an ice block sits above water. In the crystal molecules are not denser but more neatly arranged; the open gaps are what make ice lighter than water. The hexagonal symmetry also sets the six-direction growth of snowflakes from the same hydrogen-bond framework.[3][9]

水的密度反常决定了冬季湖泊的结冰次序。深秋表层水冷却，密度增大后下沉，下层水被抬升交换，整湖水温逐步降到接近 4 ℃；继续降温时，低于 4 ℃ 的表层水密度反而变小，停留在最上层，进一步冷却到 0 ℃ 后在湖面结冰。冰层隔开冷空气，减少下层热量散失，冰下水温维持在 0 到 4 ℃ 之间，鱼类和水生生物得以在冰下越冬。如果冰比水重，湖面结的冰会沉到湖底，湖泊从底向上冻结，温带多数水体无法保留液态水下环境。Density anomaly controls how lakes freeze in winter. In late autumn surface water cools, grows denser and sinks, lifting and mixing deeper water until the whole lake nears 4 degrees; colder water below 4 degrees becomes less dense and stays on top, cooling to 0 degrees and freezing at the surface. The ice sheet seals off cold air and slows heat loss, keeping water beneath at 0 to 4 degrees, so fish and aquatic life survive the winter under the ice. If ice were denser than water, surface ice would sink to the bottom and lakes would freeze upward from the bed, leaving little liquid habitat in temperate waters.[2][9]

海水因为含有盐分，密度曲线与纯水不同：盐度越高，密度越大，密度最大的温度被压到冰点以下，所以大洋深层冷水温度多在 0 到 3 ℃，而不是停在 4 ℃。压力随深度增大也会提高密度，但海水本身可压缩性很小，深度压力主要作用于气体空腔和生物体，不会把水体整体压垮。比较密度时要同时看温度、盐度和压力，单说冷就下沉，在含盐水体里并不成立。Seawater differs because dissolved salts shift the density curve: higher salinity raises density and pushes the temperature of maximum density below the freezing point, which is why deep-ocean cold water sits mostly at 0 to 3 degrees rather than near 4. Pressure rises with depth and also raises density, but seawater compresses little; deep pressure acts mainly on gas cavities and organisms rather than crushing the water body as a whole. Comparing density means reading temperature, salinity and pressure together; cold alone does not guarantee sinking in salty water.[3][9]

边界：4 ℃ 密度最大是纯水在常压下的规律，日常饮用水、湖水和海水都要结合盐分与深度修正。氢键结构在融冰过程中逐步瓦解，刚融化的水在短时间内仍保留部分松散排列，微观结构比教科书描述更复杂。本章讲水的密度反常、冰晶结构与湖泊越冬分层，不展开水体热力学数值模型、结冰过程的成核条件或工程防冻设计。The boundary: maximum density at 4 degrees applies to pure water at ordinary pressure, and drinking water, lakes and seawater need salinity and depth corrections. As ice melts, its bond structure breaks down gradually, and freshly melted water retains some loose arrangement for a time, so the microstructure is more complex than textbook sketches. This chapter covers density anomaly, ice crystal structure and overwinter lake layering, not numerical water thermodynamics, freezing nucleation conditions or engineering frost protection.[2][9]

07为什么海洋是蓝色的？Why is the ocean blue?

纯水优先吸收红橙黄长波，蓝光穿透最深并返回眼睛；分子与颗粒散射为辅，泥沙叶绿素可使近岸偏绿。Pure water absorbs red, orange and yellow first; blue penetrates deepest and returns to the eye; scattering is secondary, sediment and chlorophyll tint nearshore water green.

海洋看起来是蓝色，主要不是因为它反射天空，而是纯水对可见光有选择性吸收。阳光由红、橙、黄、绿、蓝、靛、紫各色组成，波长约 400–700 nm；水分子对长波的红、橙、黄光吸收最强，这些颜色在水面下几米内就被吸收掉，剩下穿透最深的蓝、紫光进入水中再返回水面，被人眼接收，海水于是呈现蓝色。晴朗蓝天会在平静海面投下一层反光，但阴天或背阴处的清澈海水仍偏蓝，说明天空反射只是叠加因素，不是主因。The ocean looks blue mainly because pure water absorbs visible light selectively, not because it reflects the sky. Sunlight mixes red, orange, yellow, green, blue, indigo and violet wavelengths spanning about 400 to 700 nanometres. Water molecules absorb long-wavelength red, orange and yellow most strongly; those colours disappear within a few metres below the surface, while blue and violet light penetrate deepest, travel back out of the water and reach the eye, so seawater appears blue. A clear blue sky adds a reflection on calm water, but clear water still looks blue under overcast or shaded conditions, which shows that sky reflection is only an added factor, not the main cause.[10]

吸收之外，水中分子和悬浮颗粒还会散射光线。短波蓝光更容易被散射，这一点与天空呈蓝的道理相似；但在海水里，散射的贡献弱于选择性吸收。清澈大洋水的蓝光可深入数十米，红光通常在数米内就基本消失；近岸水体若含较多浮游植物（叶绿素）、泥沙和有机碎屑，绿光被反射得多，海水就偏绿、黄甚至褐色。水色因此是吸收、散射和水中成分共同决定的结果。Besides absorption, water molecules and suspended particles scatter light. Short-wavelength blue scatters more easily, similar to why the sky is blue, but in seawater scattering contributes less than selective absorption. In clear open-ocean water blue light can reach several tens of metres, while red light mostly vanishes within a few metres. Nearshore water rich in phytoplankton, sediment and organic debris reflects more green and looks green, yellow or even brown. Water colour is therefore set jointly by absorption, scattering and what the water contains.[10][11]

天空呈蓝主要靠大气分子对短波的瑞利散射，散射强度随波长变短而快速上升；海洋呈蓝则以水的吸收光谱为主导，散射为辅。两者都涉及蓝光，机制却不同：天空没有一层被阳光穿透再吸收的液体，而海水是一层有厚度、随深度改变成分的介质。理解这一区别，也能解释为什么深海潜水时红色物体很快显灰变黑——周围已经没有红光可供反射。The sky is blue mainly through Rayleigh scattering of short waves by air molecules, and scattering rises quickly as wavelength shortens. The ocean is blue chiefly through the absorption spectrum of water, with scattering in a secondary role. Both involve blue light, but the mechanisms differ: the sky has no liquid layer that sunlight penetrates and absorbs, whereas seawater is a thick medium whose composition changes with depth. This contrast also explains why red objects turn grey or black on deep dives: no red light remains around them to reflect.[11]

随深度增加，颜色逐层消失：最先不见的是红、橙，其次是黄、绿，蓝光在深层也会衰减。阳光在约 200 m 以下就很微弱，到约 1,000 m 基本无光；深海生物多呈黑色或红色，正是利用了深水缺少红光的环境。潜水器和水下灯光必须自带全光谱照明，否则镜头里只剩偏蓝的单色画面。Colours vanish layer by layer with depth: red and orange go first, then yellow and green, and blue itself decays at depth. Below about 200 metres sunlight is already faint, and near 1,000 metres almost no light remains. Many deep-sea animals are black or red, taking advantage of the red-light-poor deep environment. Submersibles and underwater lights must carry their own full-spectrum illumination; otherwise the camera records only a blue-toned monochrome scene.[4][11]

边界：水色会受悬浮泥沙、浮游植物、水底质地、水深和天气影响，不能用一句“海水本来就是蓝的”概括。卫星水色遥感通过离水辐射反演叶绿素和悬浮物浓度，属于另一套定量方法；本章只讲可见光在水中的吸收与散射机制，不展开遥感反演算法、水体光学分类或具体海域的水色预报。Boundary: water colour depends on suspended sediment, phytoplankton, seabed material, depth and weather, so it cannot be reduced to seawater being blue by nature. Satellite ocean-colour remote sensing derives chlorophyll and suspended-matter concentrations from water-leaving radiation, which is a separate quantitative method. This chapter covers absorption and scattering of visible light in water only; it does not cover remote-sensing retrieval algorithms, optical water-type classification or site-specific water-colour forecasts.[10]
